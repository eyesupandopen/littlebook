6 Ngonda ya ntete, 2025

Kikanda kia Melekisedeke: Mvwatu Waviokeswa, Kuvingila, ye Luvutulwalu

Tuka kwa Yahweh, I MONA Nene:

Wá Mambu ma Yahweh Nzambi a mvu ya mvu mpe yambula yavana ndwenga kwa nkwa ndwenga vovo nwina. Mpe wau inuvene mu bakisa Kikanda kia Melekisedeke, nkutu kikanda kina Klisto Yesu yatumbwa Nganga-Nzambi a Nene, nkutu landila kikanda kia Melekisedeke yakwele mvu! Kansi wau i diau vo mu masumu ma wantu, nzila zame zavilakanwa, mpe mawokela mayingi mabembwa, mpe tombe kiayingi ye nzila zakonda longwa zayala nza mu kuma kia luvunu lwa awana babwa. Mpe diadi mu kuma kia luvunu lwa lukanu ye awana bavavanga fika kieleka mu nzila zau zakonda lunungu mpe mu luvunu lwau lwa mpova muna nza yabakama mu luvukiku lwau lwampwena luwizidi mu nza yamvimba. Nkutu luvukiku mu ndambu luna luyindulanga vo moyo wawonso watuka mu big bang mosi ya kiyindu yavewa mu longa wantu luvunu lwa awana babwa ana bavavanga sweka Yahweh, I MONA Nene, tuka mu nza ina yavanga mu koko kwame.

Mpe I MONA Mfumu a Mpeve, mpe I MONA Mbuta a Lumbu ndiona kieleka, kieleka i yandi wakala vitila lumbu yavangwa mpe I MONA ndiona wavanga mbasi za nlongo zina zasonama: Yandi okitulanga mpeve se anati a nsangu zandi, ye asadi andi se nlaku a tiya, nkutu vava nsema wavangwa mpe wavaikiswa tuka mu lusalu lwa ntima ame a mvu ya mvu luna lusalanga mambu mawonso landila lukanu lwa luzolo lwame. Kadi mu kieleka yazaya vo Adami ovanga sumu mu luzolo nkutu vitila Adami kavanga sumu mpe vo lufwasu lukuluka tuka ku zulu mu wantu a awana babwa. Dianu vo, yakanikina nkubika ame a mvu ya mvu mu luvuluzu lwawonso lwa Adami lwasiwa mvimba mu ndose a Yesu Klisto ndiona mpe i Yeshua HaMashiach mu Luwawanu lwandi Lwampa lwa lunungu ye kieleka!

Mpe landila fwaniswa ye kifwani kia Adami a nsuka wantu bavuluzwa mu kiese ye luvuvamu lwa mvu ya mvu, kuna belungisa mu zola makani ma mvu ya mvu ma Yahweh, I MONA Nene. Mu kieleka, nsema ame wavangwa mpe wavangwa mu zola kimana nsema ame wiza kwa mono mu zola mu mawonso inavene mu zola kwame kwa yau. Mpe kimana nsema ame wadiata koko mu koko ye mono mu makani mame ma mvu ya mvu mu ndungisilu a luzolo lwame lwa mvu ya mvu, mpe yau mpe bakala ye kiese kia mvimba, luvuvamu, ye moyo a mvu ya mvu nkutu wau bafidilwa mu luvuvamu mu koko kwa Mvungudi ame a Nene, Yesu wa Nazaleti, lembi yandi muntu kalendi finama Yahweh ko, I MONA Nene, diaka ko, kadi wau i yasamuna.

Mpe awana babwa bamwangisanga luvunu lwau vana ndose a nsema ame ye nluta vo nza wau yazala ye luvukiku lwau, muna, balekelanga mu vukika. Nkutu luvukiku luna luwizidi mvimba mu kuma kia mbumba a umpumbulu ina yavuka mu kizalu kia Noa mu nzila a zayi wa luvunu wa awana babwa una bavewa kwa muntu a kinsuni mu lufwasu lwandi lwawonso. Diambu ko, kadi itumanga awonso ana bena ye kiese mu kondwa lunungu luvukiku lwampwena kimana yau awonso bafundiswa ana bamenganga zayi wa kieleka mu vava luvunu lwa awana babwa. Kansi wau i diau, vo muntu a kinsuni mu kondwa zayi ovavanga fundisa diambu diandi dia lunungu lwa yandi kibeni vana ndose ame a mvu ya mvu ye ya lunungu, dianu vo, nubakisa vo ke vena nsuni ko walungiswa vana ndose ame mu kuma kia lufwasu mu menga meno mu kuma kia sumu mu nsuni. Kadi moyo a nsuni wina mu menga!

Mu kuma kiaki mwana wa Yahweh wasengomonwa kwa wantu a nza, kimana mu kimenga kia luvuluzu kia mwana meme a Nzambi, Adami wavuluzwa mu luvuluzu lwawonso tuka mu lufundisu lwandi lukwizanga kwa awonso ana belemvokelanga luwawanu lwame lwa luvuvamu kati kwa mono ye wantu ndiona i Yesu wa Nazaleti ndiona wakitulwa mpeve ivananga moyo mu ngolo za koko kwame. Mpe diaka, mu Nkosi a Kanda dia Yuda, ndiona itumanga mu nza mu lunungu mu fundisa ye nwana vita mpe wau nsema uvutulwa mu ngolo tuka mu moko ma akwa nkolami ana bena ntangwa zawonso akolami mpe bavavanga ningamesa luzolo lwame lwa mvu ya mvu, luna lulendi ningameswa ko yakwele mvu, kadi nani wina ko olendi telama vana ndose a koko kwame kwa ngolo? Mpe mu lutangu lwau, ye mu mbi au, ye mu makani mau, ye mu mayele mau, ye nkutu mu tekinolozi yau yakonda mfunu (ina bekuyisanisanga) awana babwa bafwaswa mvimba mu kimenga kia mwana meme a Nzambi, ndiona wavana menga mandi makonda sumu se Nganga-Nzambi a Nene yakwele mvu landila Kikanda kia Melekisedeke mu bulemvo bwawonso kwa ndiona wamtuma, nkutu Yahweh, I MONA Nene ye a Mvu ya Mvu!

Mpe kintinu kiandi ke kikala ye nsuka ko, kadi mu lunungu lwawonso ye nlongo a kieleka wasumba nza mu menga mandi ma luvuluzu kimana luyalu lwa lunungu lwiza vana nza mu ntangwa andi mpe kimana twabaka kiese kia lumbu kia vundu (kadi mvu funda ye Yahweh i nze lumbu kimosi ye lumbu kimosi nze mvu funda vana ndose ame), nkutu lumbu kia sabata kia vundu vitila nioka ina yankulu yalošwa yakwele mvu mu tiya mu mpasi za mvimba ye za mvu ya mvu lembi yindulwa diaka mpe vitila lufundisu lwa kiti kia kimfumu kiampembe lwakubikwa mu vangama. Mpe i mu kisalu kia nganga-nzambi a nene kia Kikanda kia Melekisedeke vo Adami wavuluzwa tuka mu lufwasu mu nzila a Yesu wa Nazaleti ndiona wavana kimenga kiandi kia mvu ya mvu mu mpasi zayingi mu futa nkanikinu a Mose kimana nsiku miame mia mvu ya mvu mialungiswa muna nsema ame a mvu ya mvu uyalwanga mu mpila yalunga mu luvuluzu lwa lunungu.

Mpe kieleka kikwiza vangama vo ivanga zulu diampa ye nza yampa kadi zulu diantete, ye nza yantete ivila, mpe ke vekala diaka mbu ko; ngatu vekala muntu olendi kunutokanesa yakwele mvu. Mpe kikwiza vangama mu kinsalukisa wau vo asundi ame bevwa mambu mawonso, mpe ikala Nzambi au, mpe bekala bana bame. Kansi awana bena ye wonga ye akwa kondwa kwikizi, ye akwa mangemi ye avondi, ye akwa zumba, ye ndoki, ye asambidi a teke, ye akwa luvunu awonso bekala ye ndambu au mu dizanga, dina diokanga ye tiya ye sufele, dina i lufwa lwa zole.

Dianu vo, nani nufwete mona wonga bana bame, kadi nukitulwa bana ba Nzambi amoyo mu mavangu ma Yeshua HaMashiach ndiona mpe i Yesu Klisto, nkutu yandi ndiona yavana ngolo vioka awonso mu nsema ame, katula kaka mu kiti kiame kia kimfumu i nsikidisanga wisa kiame, dianu vo nani olendi telama vana ndose a koko kwa ngolo kwa Klisto Yesu, Mfumu wafuluka mpe watomboka? Kieleka, kieleka, nze una yakala mpe, nze una wina, mpe wau i kikala yakwele mvu ye yakwele mvu vo Yahweh ye Yeshua HaMashiach bena mosi mu kintwadi ye lukanu mpe imuvene ngolo za mvimba mu lunungu lwawonso mpe i yandi ndiona yavana wisa vana vu kia zulu kiamvimba, mpe oyalanga mu bulemvo bwalunga mu luzolo lwame lwa mvu ya mvu. Mpe wau inuvene mu bakisa luvukiku luna luwizidi vana nza, nkutu luvunu lwa awana babwa ana bavavanga sweka kieleka kia luvuluzu lweno ye kieleka kia Mfumu eno a mvu ya mvu, Klisto Yesu!

Dianu vo, nubakisa vo alongi eno a Jesuit batwasa mpofo mu ntima mieno mu sweka kieleka kia nsema ame mpe mu sweka lusansu lweno lwa kieleka tuka kwa yeno, kimana nwadiatila mu tombe mu lungisa zolela yau yambi, nkutu kimana mayembe mame mayikama yau mu vwa kwau kwa mvu ya mvu kwa tiya ye sufele kadi wau i lukanu lwau. Mpe wau i diau vo Yahweh, I MONA Nene, kasisidi kuyisisa lembi mbangi ko. Mpe ye konso luwawanu inavene, mpe ye konso nkanda inavene mpe yatonda, kisisi kuyisisa lembi mbangi a kieleka ya masonuku ko ye lutwadisu luvewanga mu ndinga za ambikudi ame a nkulu, ana yatumba. Mpe yavewa mpe mu ndinga za ambikudi a Luwawanu Lwampa ana Yeshua HaMashiach katumba ana bekunsadila se anganga-nzambi andi a ngolo ye atinu!

Kadi wau i Kikanda kia Melekisedeke, nkutu kikanda kia mvu ya mvu kiavewa kwa wantu, muna luyalu luna Adami kafwete yala vana nza lwakala lwa yala kwa ndiona watumbwa nganga-nzambi a nene, ntotila, ye mbikudi! Kadi wau i (mpe i) Kikanda kia Melekisedeke kiavewa kwa wantu, nkutu kimana wantu bakala ye luyalu lwa lunungu nkutu kana una vo nioka ina yankulu yavukika Eva mu mayele mandi. Mpe wau mpe Kikanda kia Melekisedeke kiakwamanana, nkutu kiakala vana Adami tuka kuna lubantiku kwa ndiona kiavewa, kansi mu fwa bafwa kuna nsuka mpe kikanda kia Melekisedeke kiaviokeswa mu ntangwa, mu lumbu tuka Adami te ye Yakobo.

Mpe wau mu Nza ina yakala vitila kizalu kiampwena Kikanda kia Melekisedeke kiavioka tuka Adami, kwa Kenani, kwa Enosi, kwa Enoki, kwa Metusela, kwa Noa mpe wau i Kikanda kia Melekisedeke kiavioka mu lumbu vitila kizalu kiampwena. Kansi, alongi eno a Jesuit basonika diaka masonuku, mpe bavanga mayele mayingi mu sweka kieleka kia lusansu lwa kieleka lwa Adami nkutu wau bavavanga kitula wantu se mfututu yakonda mfunu mu nsema au wa luvunu una watuka mu big bang kimosi mu ntama au ya kiyindu.

Kadi ba Jesuit basamunanga Yesu wankaka, nkutu Yesu ndiona wavangwa mu kifwani kia Nimirolodi tuka Babilone, ndiona wakala nyadi antete a mpila andi landila kizalu. Mpe kana una vo ayadi ame a lunungu batumbwa tuka nkulu mu Kikanda kia Melekisedeke bavana wantu lunungu vana fulu kia sumu, mpasi, lufwa, ye tumbu kia mvu ya mvu. Muntu a kinsuni kansi osolanga sumu, mpasi, lufwa, ye tumbu kia mvu ya mvu. Kansi, awonso mu nsema ame ana bavewa luzolo lwa kimpwanza bena ye kimpwanza kia kuyisolela yau kibeni kana vo bezinga kwa Yahweh mu nzila a Klisto Yesu, yovo mu ndambu yankaka basola tumbu kiau kia mvu ya mvu mu kuma kia kiese kia sumu mu ntangwa yakufi. Mpe wau yasonama vo awonso tuka mu nza ina yakala katula kaka mioyo nana bavuluzwa amoyo mu kuma kia Noa, ndiona wakala muntu a lunungu ndiona wayala nza landila Kikanda kia Melekisedeke mu ntangwa kadi mu fwa wafwa.

Dianu vo, nubakisa nzila za awana babwa, vo bavavanga ye ntima miau miawonso mu fisa, vuna, ye sweka kieleka tuka kwa mayembe mame, nkutu awana inatumba mu moyo a mvu ya mvu. Mpe wau i diau vo wau nkubika ame a luvuluzu kwa Adami yasengomonwa malembe-malembe, yatumanga masonuku manlongo mu mpeve a mbikudulu ina yakala vana yau, mpe nkutu mpeve a mbikudulu ina yawutwa kuna nsuka mu bana ba Nzambi mu luwutuku lwampa luvewanga mu Luwawanu Lwampa lwa Yeshua HaMashiach, nkutu mu yandi ndiona iyangalelanga ntangwa zawonso. Mpe wau yasengomona nkubika ame malembe-malembe, yavana mpe masonuku manlongo malembe-malembe, mpe Klisto Yesu wavana masonuku manlongo matambuka tuka mu koko kwame, nkutu yandi ndiona osalanga mambu mawonso landila lukanu lwa luzolo lwame.

Mpe wau awana babwa basalanga mu fwa kieleka, nkutu mu mpila yafwanana nze una bakifwasa yau kibeni yakwele mvu ye yakwele mvu. Kadi basonika diaka masonuku, bavana ba Bibila ya longoka ya luvunu, bavana ba lexicons ye concordances ya luvunu muna ayingi bavukikwa ye nsasa zambi muna, nze vo Yahweh kalendi vana asekodi afwana ko mu sekola Diambu diame kadi Diambu diame kilendi tololwa ko ngatu swekwa, kana vo bavavanga. Kieleka, i nkembo a Yahweh mu sweka diambu ye luzitu lwa Atinu mu vavulula dio! Dianu vo, inukanikini mu vavulula kieleka kia Yahweh mu ndose a Yeshua HaMashiach!

Ozevo nubakisa vo awana basadilanga nkembi ndiona wampwena, nkutu alongi eno a Jesuit, basola nkubika yasolwa kiambote ya luvunu luvukikanga muna balekelanga mu vukika. Basweka mbangi zayingi zanlongo mpe basonika diaka ba Bibila zayingi landila masonuku ma luvunu mpe bavanga luvunu lwayingi mu kuma kia kieleka kia nsema ame. Mpe mu zayi wame wantete ye lufundisu lwame yavana nzila kwa awana babwa mu vanga wau, kadi kala vo masonuku mame manlongo malundwa kiambote ye keka nkutu nze una yakanikina ye yekola, uzowa wau ke wadi vangama ko. Kansi wau i nzila za muntu a sumu mpe wau i nzila za awana babwa ana bafilanga wantu mu nzila zambi mu zolela yau yambi ya tiya. Kansi, Diambu diame kilendi tololwa ko mpe kilungisa diambu dina kiatumwa mu lungisa.

Mpe wau yasonama mu Yaseli vo landila kizalu kiampwena mvwatu a Melekisedeke wasikila vana Noa mpe kuna nsuka waviokeswa kwa Semi ndiona wakala wa sambanu mu ndandani mu vwata mvwatu mpe mu ntangwa kikanda kia Melekisedeke kiaviokeswa kwa Abarama ndiona mu ntangwa wakitulwa Se dia makanda mayingi, mpe kwa avingidi andi a nsilu nsilu mia vwa mikwendanga mu ndungisilu kadi mwana a muntu a mpika kekala mvingidi a nsilu ko kadi nsuni ye menga ke vilendi vwa nsilu mia Kintinu kia Nzambi ko kansi asongi ana bezinganga mu kwikizi, nkutu nze una Abarama wa kwikizi kazinga mu lumbu yankulu.

Dianu vo, inuvene mu bakisa nsasa a Melekisedeke ina isongele vo Ntotila a Lunungu. Mpe wau etymology isekolwanga tuka Kiyibelu mu Kingelesi kuna Melek isongele vo Ntotila ye Zedek isongele vo lunungu ye nsasa yavewa ye yakanwa vo Ntotila a Lunungu. Mpe wau atinu aba a lunungu bayala nsema ame, nkutu wantu a lunungu ana bavingila Mesia au, nkutu yandi ndiona owutuka tuka mu kanda dia Yuda.

Mpe ebu yasonama mu Ayibelu mu koko kwa Paulu (ndiona wakala nyadi a mbumba za Klisto Yesu) mpe wau yasonika: Kadi Melekisedeke ndioyo wakala Ntotila a Salemi, Nganga-Nzambi a Nzambi a mpaikilu, ndiona wawanana ye Abarama, vava kavutuka tuka mu lufwasu lwa Atinu, mpe wamsambula: Kwa ndiona mpe Abarama kavana ndambu a kumi ya mambu mawonso: ndiona wantete mu nsasa i Ntotila a Lunungu: landila diadi, yandi mpe i Ntotila a Salemi, i sia vo, Ntotila a luvuvamu, kondwa se, kondwa ngudi, kondwa kanda, mpe kena ye lubantiku lwa lumbu yandi ko, ngatu nsuka a moyo: kansi wafwaniswa ye Mwana a Nzambi, mpe okwamanananga Nganga-Nzambi yakwele mvu. Mpe wau mvwatu a Melekisedeke waviokeswa kwa Abarama wa kwikizi mu lulundilu lwandi nkutu kikanda kialundwa mpe kiakanwa mu kuma kia Yesu Klisto ndiona mpe i Yeshua HaMashiach.

Kansi, ayingi mu yeno bakwikila luvunu lwa alongi eno a Jesuit ana bavana mpofo kwa ayingi mu yeno ye malongi mambi vo verse zazi zivovelanga Yesu Klisto yandi kibeni mpe ke Kikanda kia Melekisedeke kiau kibeni ko. Kadi pwelele, Yesu wa Nazaleti wina ye Se, nkutu Yahweh I MONA Nene, mpe pwelele Yesu wa Nazaleti wina ye ngudi, nkutu Malia ndiona wakala tuka Nazaleti a Ngaliliya ndiona wavova vo yambula yavangama kwa mono landila luzolo lwaku, nkutu mbandu a mvu ya mvu ya kwikizi yavewa kimana akwa kwikizi bazaya nzila za kwikizi zina bediatilanga. Kadi pwelele Yesu wa Nazaleti wina ye kanda dina yandi i mvuluzi a kanda, ekuma nulembi zitisilanga Mambu mame mapwelele mavewa mu masonuku makanwa mu kunufila kiambote tuka mu luvunu lwau? Mpe kansi ayingi mu yeno bekwikilanga luvunu lwalu vioka pwelele ye sikididi kia Mambu mame.

Kansi, wau yasonama vo Semi wasikila ye kwikizi kwa Noa, kadi Kami wabembola luwawanu lwa Noa mvimba mpe wavilakana nzila za Yahweh mpe Kami ye atekelo ayingi a Noa bavava tunga mbanza au yampwena ye nzo nda wau kintinu kia Nimirolodi kiakonda lunungu kiatelama mu yala nza mu lumbu yina mu kondwa lunungu, landila nzila za awana babwa ana kasadila, kana una vo yasala nkumbu miayingi mu vana Nimirolodi nzila mu vutuka kwa mono mu luviluku lwa ntima, nzila ina kamenga yakwele mvu ye yakwele mvu.

Kansi wau yasonama pwelele mu Yaseli mu kuma kia Abarama ye Semi ndiona waviokesa mvwatu kwa Abarama mu lufwasu lwandi lwa Kedorolaomere ye vu kiandi: Mpe vava kavutuka tuka mu wanda atinu aba, yandi ye wantu andi bavioka mu ndimba a Sidimi kuna atinu banwana vita kintwadi. Mpe Bela ntotila a Sodoma, ye nsadisa a wantu andi ana bakala yandi, bavaika tuka mu mawulu ma nteke muna babwa, mu wanana ye Abarama ye wantu andi. Mpe Adonizedeke (yovo Melekisedeke) ntotila a Yelusalemi, yandi mosi wakala Semi, wavaika ye wantu andi mu wanana ye Abarama ye wantu andi, ye mampa ye vinyo, mpe basikila kintwadi mu ndimba a Melek. Mpe Adonizedeke (yovo Melekisedeke) wasambula Abarama, mpe Abarama wamvana ndambu a kumi tuka mu mawonso mana kanata tuka mu sanzu dia mbeni zandi, kadi Adonizedeke (yovo Melekisedeke) wakala nganga-nzambi vana ndose a Nzambi.

Kansi wau yasonama mu Yaseli: Mpe lumbu ya Sala yakala mvu nkama mosi ye makumole ye nsambwadi mpe wafwa, mpe Abarama wavanga dilu diampwena ye dia zitu, mpe wavanga fu ya dilu mu lumbu nsambwadi. Mpe awonso bazinganga mu nsi bafiaulwisa Abarama ye Isaki mwan'andi mu kuma kia Sala. Mpe vava lumbu ya dilu diau yavioka Abarama watuma Isaki mwan'andi, mpe wenda ku nzo a Semi ye Ebele, mu longoka nzila za Mfumu ye malongi mandi, mpe Abarama wasikila kuna mvu tatu. Ebu wau mvwatu a Melekisedeke wavioka kwa Isaki ndiona waviokesa vwa dia mwana antete kwa nleke, kadi Esau wamenga mambu ma Nzambi mpe wateka vwa diandi mu kuma kia dilonga dia madia: Nkutu nzila ina Yahweh katonda yakwele mvu.

Kansi i wau yasonama muna nkand'a Yasere mu diambu dia Kimfumu kia Melekisedeke: Ye vava kiavioka, Selake mwan'a Arafakasadi wafwa muna mvu wau, wina vo mvu wa kumi ye nana wa zingu kia Yakobo ye Esau; ye lumbu yawonso yazinga Selake i mvu nkama iya ye makumatatu ye tatu, i bosi wafwa. Muna ntangwa yayina, Isaki watuma mwan'andi anleke Yakobo kuna nzo a Seme ye Ebere, ye walongoka malongi ma Mfumu, ye Yakobo wasala muna nzo a Seme ye Ebere mu mvu makumatatu ye zole, ye Esau wa mpangi andi kayenda ko, kadi kazolele kwenda ko, ye wasala muna nzo a se diandi muna nsi a Kenani.

Kansi i wau yasonama muna Yasere mu diambu dia Kimfumu kia Melekisedeke, vo muna ntangwa yina Mono, Yave wa Nene MONO INA, yasengomona kwa Yakobo vo kimfumu kia Melekisedeke kifwete kambulwa mu salu tatu, e ngyelele ye Mambu masonama muna Yasere kimana wazaya e ludi kia Mambu mame: Ye Rubeni wafwila ngudi andi Leya kimpala mu diambu diadi, ye wazala ye makasi, ye watelama muna makasi mandi ye wayenda kota muna saba kia Biliya ye wakatula mfulu a se diandi. Muna ntangwa yayina, e ndambu a kimbuta, kumosi ye salu ya kintinu ye kinganga, yakatulwa kwa wana ba Rubeni, kadi wasafula mfulu a se diandi, ye kimbuta kiavewa kwa Yosefe, e salu kia kintinu kwa Yuda, ye kinganga kwa Levi, kadi Rubeni wasafula mfulu a se diandi.

Ye i wau mpe yasonama vo e mbebe zina muna Kimfumu kia Melekisedeke zasikidiswa mu diambu dia luludiku lwa Luwawanu lwa Mose, muna Luwawanu lwandi lwankulu lwavaikisa salu tatu yaswaswana mu diambu dia salu yayi yavauka, i dianu vo Isaele yakala ye kanda dia Ntinu, ye yakala ye Salu kia kinganga kia Levi, ye yakala ye sikola ya ambikudi, nkutu sikola yatezaneswa ye sikola ya ambikudi ankulu, tuka muna lumbu yavitila kizalu.

Ozevo e mbebe ye nkubika za Nganga, Ntinu, ye Mbikudi zawonso zaviokela muna Isaele mun'owu wa mbandu zau zasonama muna masonuku mavauka ye muna ntangwa yina, kwayiza muntu mosi nkumbu andi Yoane ona wawutwa kwa Zakaliya ona wakala wa kanda dia kinganga kia Sadoke. Ye i dianu vo diafwana kwa Yoane wa Mvubi mu lungisa unsongi wawonso vava kavuba mwan'a meme a Nzambi muna nkoko a Yodani kuna kwavubilwa Yesu, ye wavaika vana maza. Ye tala, e zulu diaziuka kwa yandi, ye Yoane wamona mpeve a Nzambi ikulumukanga nze eyembe, ye iningamene vana ntandu andi. Ye tala, ndinga yatuka kuna zulu, ivovele vo, Ndioyu i Mwan'ame anzolwa, ona iyangalelanga kikilu.

Ye i wau mpe Yesu wa Nasiyale watumbwa se nganga yakwele mvu mun'owu wa Kimfumu kia Melekisedeke ye salu yawonso yavutulwiswa muna nsi a mvwatu a kinganga kiaki kiakwele mvu, owau ye yakwele mvu.

Kadi e Kimfumu kia Melekisedeke i kimfumu kia kinganga, kina vo e mvwatu andi ke uvioka ko tuka kwa Yeshua HaMashiach, kadi ofulukidi kimana kafwa diaka ko!

Kadi e Kimfumu kia Melekisedeke i kimfumu kia Ntinu mu diambu dia luyalu lwansongi lwa nsema ame.

Kadi e Kimfumu kia Melekisedeke i kimfumu kia Ambikudi ana bevovanga ye kulonga lusansu, nzila, ye kusentu kwa makani malunga ma Yave mavewa muna ndose a Yesu Klisto.

MONO I YHVH, nkutu MONO INA wa Nene ye ntumini Mambu mama muna ntumwa ye ntaudi ame ye muna ntumwa ye ntaudi ame Mambu mama matuminu. Kieleka, kwa ntumwa ye ntaudi ame ona osadilanga muna nsi a Klisto Yesu wa Mfumu andi akwele mvu ye muna nzila a mpeve a ungunza Yave ye Klisto Yesu basengomwene kwa ntumwa ye ntaudi eto e Nsangu Zambote Zakwele Mvu mu diambu dia lulemvo ye luvuluzu lwa Mambu mama mawonso matuminu. Ye Mambu ma Nsangu Zambote Zakwele Mvu masolola meso, matu, ye ntima mia awonso ana matuminu.

MONO I YHVH, nkutu ndiona wavanga, wasema ye kutuma Mwan'ame mosi kaka wawutwa, nkutu Yeshua HaMashiach ona i Yesu wa Masiya mu diambu dia luvuluzu lwa awonso ana bawutwa kwa Adami ye batambulwila lukuulu lwalunga avo awana babokelwa bavana mvutu kwa mvuluzi au vava kebokelanga. Ozevo, nubakula vo i Mono, YHVH, MONO INA wa Nene ona osengomonanga Yesu wa Nasiyale kwa awana bavila, kimana Mvungudi au a Mfumu kasolola ye kuvutulwisa mameme mame mawonso mavila muna malundilu mame ma luvuvamu ye tininu yakwele mvu ye yakwele mvu.

MONO I YHVH, nkutu ndiona wakambula kinganga kiakwele mvu kia Melekisedeke mu salu tatu yaswaswana ya Ntinu, Nganga, ye Mbikudi muna nsi a Yakobo muna lumbu yandi.

MONO I YHVH, nkutu ndiona wavutulwisa Kinganga kiakwele mvu kia Melekisedeke muna Klisto Yesu, ona wasonamena vo: Kadi kiamoneka kieleka, vo Mfumu eno watuka muna Yuda, mu diambu dia kanda diadi Mose kavova diambu ko, mu diambu dia Kinganga. Ye i diambu diamoneka kieleka diaka, kadi mun'owu wa fwaniswa kia Melekisedeke, mwatelama Nganga wankaka, ona kavangwa nganga mun'owu wa nsiku a nkanikinu a nitu wankulu ko, kansi mun'owu wa nkuma a moyo ulembi fwa. Kadi yandi otele umbangi vo, Ngeye u Nganga yakwele mvu, mun'owu wa kimfumu kia Melekisedeke. Kadi o nkanikinu wavitila, ukatwilu, mu diambu dia lutovoko lwandi, ye ulembi mfunu.

Kadi o Nsiku ke wavanga diambu dialunga ko, kansi e ntwasa a vuvu kisundidi wamvanga walunga, muna yau tufinamanga kwa Yave. Ye wau vo ke konda ndofi ko (kadi awaya bavangwa Nganga kondwa ndofi: Kansi ndioyu wavangwa ye ndofi kwa ndiona wamvovesa vo, Yave odidi ndofi, ye kevilwa ntima ko, Ngeye u Nganga yakwele mvu, mun'owu wa kimfumu kia Melekisedeke) Muna diadi Yesu wavangwa se nkuwa a Luwawanu lusundidi. Ye vana vena yau ayingi bavangwa Nganga, kadi ke balenda kwamanana ko, mu diambu dia lufwa lwau. Kansi muntu ndioyu, wau vo okwamanananga yakwele mvu, wina ye Kinganga, kina ke kilendi vioka kwa muntu wankaka ko. I dianu vo, olenda mpe kuvuluzila emvimba awana bekwizanga kwa Nzambi muna yandi, wau vo ozinganga yakwele mvu, mu kubalombela.

Ozevo, inuvene nwabakula e Kimfumu Kiakwele Mvu kia Melekisedeke ye ludi kia Yeshua HaMashiach, ona mpe i Yesu wa Klisto kadi muna nsi a mvwatu andi akwele mvu wa nganga ambuta mun'owu wa Kimfumu kia Melekisedeke Klisto Yesu oyadika ye kuvia vu kiangolo kia atinu ye anganga muna nsi a Kimfumu kia Melekisedeke ye muna diadi e Kintinu kia Zulu kiyadwa kwa koko kwakwele mvu kwa Mfumu au Akwele Mvu a Unsongi, muna nsi andi beyala emvimba muna unsongi ye vauka kwakieleka yakwele mvu, kadi mu diambu dia Kintinu kia Klisto Yesu, ke kina ye nsuka ko.

Sela!
