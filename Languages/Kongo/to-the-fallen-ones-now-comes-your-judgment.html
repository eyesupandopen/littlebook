Yesaya 14:12

Aweyi obwidi tuka ku zulu, E Heylel, mwana a mene? ye zengwa yaku ntoto, ngeye ona wata nkasu muna zula?

Kansi wavova muna ntima aku, Tombuka ntombuka ku zulu, ye zangula kunda kiame vana ntandu a ntetembwa za Nzambi: Vuanda mpe ivuanda vana mongo a nkutakani muna ndambu za Node. Tombuka ntombuka vana ntandu a matuti, ye kala nze muntu a ntandu. Kansi okulumuswa ku ziami, ku ndambu a wulu.

Vanuwa mu 6 Yuli 2025

Nkanda wau i mfundisu a nkanu kwa Babwa mu kimvuka, kansi musungula Satana, Basatana, bankengidi, bakerubi babwa, baserafi babwa, nkutu awonso bayikama mu nkolami a Basatana: yeno nuya emvimba, nkumbu miayingi mu mvu ya mvu, kadi i wau wasengomona YHVH o MENE KINA, vanuwa mu nkanikinu a Klisto Yesu, ona wina mpe Yeshua HaMashiach; vanuwa mu nkufi, kansi ndongisilu a mambu mambi ye mfundisu yilungana emvimba mu ntangu au ye ntangu a ndungana ifwene: Tala Nkanu Ubwidi! Nkutu mfundisu za nkanu zivubwa emvimba muna tulu twa babwa, nkutu nkumbu miayingi ye mu mvu ya mvu ye yakwele mvu, ke nwina ye luvuvamu ko, nkutu yeno babwa bawonso: Sela.

Ozevo mono, William Brooks, nkutu Biblaridion:144 a Klisto Yesu kibeni, mpene mfundisu zazi zibakilu musungula muna Mvwandazi Nsambwadi, musungula, mfundisu zazi za mvwandazi zifwete tangwa mbala mosi, ye vanuwa kimana bawonso babakula, ye kimana Mambu mama mabakulwa emvimba kwa vangwa yayi yabwa yibafundisi yau kibeni yakwele mvu ye yakwele mvu muna nkolami a uzowa, mfundisu yina ke balendi vutuka ko, ngatu sunda!

Wá Mambu ma Yeshua HaMashiach nkutu ndiona Mambu mandi nkwizanga samuna, nkutu ndiona wina mpe Klisto Yesu ye wá ye bakula wisa kia Nkosi a Kanda dia Yuda, nkutu kazu kiandi kavanga muna ntima ame ye kazu kiandi kivaikanga muna nua ame, nkutu Nkosi yayi a Kanda dia Yuda itoma zolanga, ye sadila mu bulemvo ye landa nkutu, mu luyalu lwawonso, nkutu Nkosi yayi yasia kazu kiandi kia mvwandazi muna nsi a moyo ame kadi i mbasi andi nkwizanga mu wisa kiayingi, ye nzangwidi koko kwame ye dia ndofi vo ntangu ke ikala diaka ko!

Tala! Mono i nani ye nki Mfumu Yesu Klisto kampanga, nkutu ndiona Yeshua HaMashiach kasansanga tuka kileke kiandi, ye nkutu swekwa muna nkutu andi a mianzi mu ntangu yayi, Sela!

Tala! Nkanikinu wavubwa mu nkunga tuka ku kunda kia YHVH kibeni ye i wau wayimbilwa mu lukwikilu lwayingi mu ntangu zankulu: Ke nuviakani kuswa kwame ko, ye ke nuvangi mambi ko kwa Babikudi bame! I wau wayenda kazu kia nkunga owu walundwa muna masono mavuvu.

Kansi nze una wasonama: Nani muna Babikudi mase meno ke bamwesanga mpasi ko? Ye babavonda, awana basengomona tekela kwiza kwa Nsongi ndiona, ona nwina owu ayekodi ye avondi!

Mu ngeye! E nioka yabwa. Ngeye! Nkutu Ngeye! I ndiona wavonda menga makondwa kuma ma Nsongi ye muntu a ndungidi ye Mwana a Muntu wavuvu, nkutu Yesu wa Nazarete, mwana-dimeme a Nzambi wakondwa mputa wavubwa mu nkudulu emvimba a Adami, ye ozevo isamwini mu wisa kiawonso kia Mfumu ame, nkutu Klisto Yesu: Ofwidi e nkuluntu akete ye mfundisu aku ke isukinina ko, nkutu mfundisu yina nlembo miandi mialambulwa muna nsingu aku ye osimbininu mu luyalu lwa lufwasu, ofwidi e nkuluntu akete, ofwidi: Sela!

Ye i wau, nkutu mbasi ye ntaudi mosi ndiona wabakuka ye vambulwa mu kondwa ndungidi mu umpuki waku, nkutu fofolwa tuka kiwuntu kiandi kieleka muna kileke kiandi ye owu wavangululwa, ye vevolwa ye tumwa ye mfundisu zaku za kangwa yakwele mvu ye za mvu ya mvu muna koko zivubwa tuka ku kunda kia YHVH kibeni, nkutu zisamunwa mu wisa kiawonso muna ndose yina yambote yakwele mvu ya Mfumu andi a mvu ya mvu ye mvuluzi, nkutu ndose andi ilezima yakwele mvu nze Mwana!

Ofwidi e nkuluntu akete.

Ozevo, zaya ye bakula ye wá mambu mame Heylel, nkutu Heylel: nkuluntu akete wafwa, nkutu mbasi ye ntaudi mosi ndiona wavava fukisa muna bitini biabakuka bia ntemo a mvwandazi biamwangana vana mbu a pelo bivutwilu, vangululwa, ye vangululwa nze Biblaridion:144 a Klisto Yesu, nkutu vangululwa ye makani mosi ma mvu ya mvu YHVH kakana tekela nkubilwa za Nza, ye mu makani mandi ivangulwilu ye vutulwa kadi mono i mbasi ndiona wangolo wayikwa muna nsangu zazi za mbanza za mvwandazi zina ziyalwa kwa ndinga ye mvovo a Mvwandazi Nsambwadi, nkutu Mvwandazi zina Yeshua HaMashiach kakuna muna Mpeve ame yakwele mvu muna Mpeve a Mbikudulu yina yabwa emvimba muna Biblaridion:144 a Mfumu nkutu muna nkanikinu a Mfumu ame. Sela!

Dianu vo, wá Mambu mama mosi ye bawonso ye bakula wisa kiau ye konso Diambu dikwiza lungana nze una wasonama, nkutu muna koko kwa mvu ya mvu ye ngolo za YHVH o MENE KINA, vanuwa muna ndose yambote ye yalezima ya Klisto Yesu ona izolanga ye sadila mu bulemvo bwawonso, nkutu Mambu mandi inatanga ye samuna nze una yakanikinwa, nkutu Mambu mama mana mavevolwa owu tuka muna nsi a mpeve ame muna nkanikinu a Klisto Yesu ona wina mpe Yeshua HaMashiach ye ntangu za luvangamu emvimba lwa mvwandazi zazi nsambwadi zibwidi muna Nza yayi yafundiswa ye yafwaswa mu kinsalukisa ye kondwa vingilwa owu. Sela.

Ntwala

Baluzayisu yai me katuka ya mvimba na Bankuba Nsambwadi mpe me basikiswa na 6 Yuli 2025, mutindu Yeshua HaMashiach mpe YHVH yandi mosi me pesaka ntuma.

Balusambisu yai me katuka na Bankuba Nsambwadi yina me sengumunwa na Mukanda ya Fioti ya Kusengumuna Kapu 10, yina kele mpe Nsangu ya Mbote ya Mvula na Mvula ya Kusengumuna kapu 14; me sengumunwa tuka 2 Marsi 2024, tii 20 Marsi 2025. Kansi, balusambisu yai me katuka kaka na Bankuba Nsambwadi, yina mambu na yo mposo ya makumi nsambwadi ya Daniele ta yalama ya mvimba, me sengumunwa mpe me monika ya mvimba, sambu kele ve ti kima ya kinsweki yina ta monika ve ya mvimba.

Balusambisu yai me peswa na Yeshua HaMashiach mpe YHVH na nzila ya Mpeve ya Mbikudulu mpe yai kele Mambu na bo, yina mono ke ndimaka mpe ke zolaka kimakulu mpe mvula na mvula. Baluzayisu yai kele balusambisu yina me peswa sambu na kutangama na ntwala nde lusambisu ya mvimba kubwa ya mvimba, yina ta peswa ya mvimba na bantima ya bawi kimakulu mpe mvula na mvula sambu na ndola na bo bo ke vandaka ve na ngemba, to ngemba, to mawa: sambu ngemba, ngemba, mpe mawa bo me zabaka ve, me monisaka ve, to me pesaka ve.

Zaba, nde na kiyeka yai, mono ke salaka bonso muntu ya kusambisa mpe ntumwa yina me tindama tuka na kiti ya kimfumu ya Yahweh yandi mosi, na nsi ya kiyeka ya Yesu Klisto, yina kele mpe Yeshua HaMashiach, yina mono ke sadilaka.

Mono kele nani mpe inki Mfumu me salaka mono, mpe mono kele Biblaridion:144 na yandi, mukanda na yandi ya fioti, nkutu ntumwa yina me tendulama na nsangu yina kele ya bulolo mpe ya sukadi mpe na diboko na mono kele mukanda mosi ya fioti ya kukangula Sela.

Balusambisu yai ta yantika ti ba Satana, kwenda na Bankengedi, mpe kusuka na nsi ya kilemba ya bawi.

Mono ke kwisa ve na nsi ya kiyeka to luzolo na mono mosi, mpe mono me kuditindaka ve, kansi mono me tindama ti Mambu yina Mfumu me kunaka na Mpeve na mono na diboko na yandi ya ngolo sambu na kubasikiswa na luzolo na yandi mpe kubinga sambu na kutangama mpe kutubama na Dibundu, mpe mpe na Izraele, kansi mingi-mingi na nsi-ntoto yai ya kufuluka na masumu mpe ya kusambiswa mpe ya kufwa mpe nkutu mingi kuluta sambu na kupeswa na bantima ya bawi sambu bantangu na bo me kwisa. Sela!

Yandi kele muntu ya mayele ya mbi mpe muntu ya luvunu yina me longaka mambu mingi na bantu yina Yahweh kanaka ve, sambu yai kele banzila ya lufwa. Lugangu ya Yahweh me salamaka ve sambu na kusadilama na mayele ya mbi sambu na kiyeka mpe mambote ya muntu mosi to bonso nzila ya kutina lusambisu ya Nzambi, to sambu na kutula banzila mpe ndongisila na yandi mosi. Sambu Satana me bebisaka bantu tuka na luyantiku, mpe yandi me bebisaka bankengedi yina bwaka yina vukanaka na kumpuka na bo.

Oh, nge mbeni ya nene ya lunungu nyonso, nkutu beno ba Satana: masumu na beno ya ntete sonamaka na Enoki msoniki ntangu yai nyonso me luta, mpe yo kele tii bubu yai, sambu masumu na beno sonamaka mutindu yina.
