Kwa Minkwikizi mu Klisto: Luwutuka Tuka ku Zulu: Ntangu ya Luwutuku Lweno Lwamona Lwakuka Yina Fwana

Wa Wa, Mambu ma Yeshua HaMashiach ye fukama ntima mieno mu ndinga ya Mambu mami ma Nlongo ye bakisa ntangu yina luzingaka bazolwa bami, kadi ka vena muntu ko mu beno wazinga ntangu zazi, ngatu mambu mama mana makubamene ntangu yayi mu nkanu wa kintulumukina ye lufwasu mu Babilone ya Kinsweki, nkutu Nsi zazi za Kimvuka (United States), mu basiswa mu kintulumukina ye lembi vingilwa ntangu yayi mu ndinga ya mpungi yina ikubamene mu sika mu kintulumukina ye lembi vingilwa ntangu yayi. Nkutu mu ndinga ya kazu kiakina kiangolo kadi ntangu ifwene bazolwa bami, ye ntangu zazi zina yeno bonso bwazayiswa ye silwa nsilu. Yina kuluvukisa kwa mono kibeni mu kintulumukina ye lembi vingilwa ntangu yayi, mu wanana yame mu mupepe bonso bwasilwa nsilu, dianu vo inkanikini: LUKUBAMA!

Lukubama beno kibeni Nkento ame a Nkwelolo, kadi ntangu ya nkudulu eno yakuka ikwizanga kwa beno ye ngolo zazo ye kuvingila kwina fwana! Dianu vo, lwiza mvimba kwa Klisto Yesu ye luyambulula beno kibeni mvimba kwa Mfumu eno a Mvu ya Mvu ya Ndungidi, mpasi vo kieleka kieleka, yalenda kulukukula mvimba mu masumu meno mazo ye yalenda kuluvana lenda kia sunda mambu mazo mu ntsundilu anene ya nza yayi yazala ye masumu, bonso bwa yasunda mambu mazo ma nza yayi yazala ye masumu mu ntsundilu anene.

Ye bu mpasi zazi za nsuka za luwutuku zikuma ngolo, lubakisa vo zifinamene manisa, nkutu mu ntangu yina yakikilu yina nsungi yayi ya Kairos ya Harpazo yina lungana bu mwana wa nlongo kewutuka diaka wamoyo mu zulu dia mazulu, nkutu wavaikiswa ye wutuka wamoyo wa mvu ya mvu ye walembi bola kimakulu mu suku dia kiti kia kimfumu kia Adonai-YHVH, bonso bwazayiswa ye silwa nsilu ntete mu Mambu ma ntumwa ame angolo ye nsadi, nkutu mu moko mandi Mambu mama mavanwa ye tindwa mu nzayisilu ye mu nkubukilu diaka ye kubika ndumba zami zangangu, mpasi vo beno bazo lwavambulwa ye ndumba zazo zazowa zina zisevaka ye lembi wa Mambu mami, ye mpasi vo yalenda kuluvukisa mvimba ye nsendo eno wamvimba wina wakubama mvimba mu beno bazo mu moko mami, wakubama mu beno mu vanwa tuka kwa mono kwa beno mu ndungisilu ya nsilu wa mfutu wa ntete wa kima kiasumbwa.

Kansi, ola mosi ye bulu ifinamene ye ina fwana ye vena ye ndinga zayingi zalembi nlongo zina zivovaka ye wungunukanga buzowa bwampamba! Kieleka, kieleka, ndinga zayingi zaluvunu zikazanga ngolo ntangu yayi, mu vana mambu maluvunu mu makanga mu bayina bena ye matu mavwanga. Kieleka, kieleka ba Yezabele babi bayikwa makanga bonso mpunda ya mfinda mu nsungi andi ibokelanga bavioki bandi ba nkuya, ye mu nsungi andi nani lenda kumvioka lembi tumpa mu mabungu mama mabalulwa, bayina nkanu au kulembalalanga ko.

Kansi mutindu mosi mpe mpeve zazi za Yezabele zina zibikulanga mambu maluvunu ma luvukiku ye luvunu, nkutu mambu ma kimpala mana bantu bayingi bazolanga vana fulu kia Mambu mami mavelela ma Ndungidi mavanwa tuka mu ntima wa Adonai-YHVH yandi kibeni, mu luse lwa Klisto Yesu, yina wina mpe Yeshua HaMashiach! Kansi, bika mpambulwa yayi yasikila kadi itwadiswanga mu mbele ya mpeve ame, kaka kulubikila beno kibeni ko mu bwa ku ndambu ya kimama ya mbele ame evo lwayikama mu nkanu miau, bu lwabembola mu kuvambula beno kibeni mvimba mu nkanikinu ame!

LUKEBISWA MVIMBA NTANGU YAYI!

Dianu vo, inkanikini beno bazo, luvaika tuka mu kati kwau nkangu ame MPASI VO LULEMBI yikama mu nkanu miau ye ikebisi mbala zayingi, ye diaka ntangu yayi mu vaika ye kuvambula beno kibeni mvimba tuka mu Babilone ya Kinsweki, kadi nkanu wa ndumba yina yanene ukubamene ntangu yayi mu mwangana mu nza yamvimba bu ola mosi ye bulu ikubamene mu basiswa ye nkuya wangolo ye makasi: Kaka, kaka ntangu yina, ye kaka mu ntangu yina yakikilu yina yasilwa mu mvu mialuta kwa Adonai-YHVH, Mono Ngina Wanene Yandi Kibeni! Dianu vo, lulembi zimbisa kivuvu mu kuvingila kweno kadi bayingi mu beno balembane mu kuvingila kweno, kadi inkanikini beno vo Lukala ye kiese, ye LULEMBI ZIMBISA KIVUVU!

Lwiza kwa mono mvimba bazolwa bami, lwiza mvimba kwa mono lembi boma ye ntima mieno mvimba mu kuvingila kwamvimba ye luse lwaziuka mvimba, lembi simba kima. Tala!
