Ubuhamya Bubiri: Isirayeli
Ibisharira n'Ibiryohereye
3 Nzeri 2024

Nzanye Ibihangange ngo Bisohoze Uburakari Bwanjye!

Dore, baraje! Nzanye ibihangange ngo bisohoze uburakari bwanjye! Umva amagambo yanjye, yemwe abatuye isi, ndetse n'amagambo ya Yahweh, Imana ikomeye ihoraho. Kuko nk'uko byari mu minsi ya Nowa, niko bizaba: Kandi hari ibihangange mu isi muri iyo minsi, ndetse no mu minsi ya Yaredi kugeza mu minsi ya Nowa byarakozwe kandi byazereraga mu isi mu gukiranirwa gukomeye. Kandi nk'uko byahoze, niko bizaba, kandi ibihangange bizagaruka. Mu minsi ya Yaredi abarinzi bamanukiye ku musozi wa Herumoni, bose hamwe ari magana abiri mu minsi ya Yaredi bamanutse bava mu ijuru hejuru. Kandi bihuje mu ndahiro z'imivumo, ndetse n'indahiro yabo bihurijemo yahindutse umuvumo w'iteka ku bose bayiboshyweho kandi batazigera bacika, nuko bashuka kandi bayobya abantu mu byaha bikomeye.

Abarinzi bashukishijwe na Satani, bakurikije irari ry'imitima yabo, bagwa mu bishuko bava mu mimerere yabo ya mbere y'icyubahiro, kandi bari bafite ubugingo buhoraho mu ijuru, kuko ari ko baremwe. Nyamara baraguye, ndetse nk'inyenyeri zigwa niko kugwa kwabo kwabaye, kandi bifatanije na Satani iteka ryose. Nuko Satani ishuka abagore babyemeye ibigisha gushuka abarinzi bakurikije irari ry'imitima yabo, kandi bamwe mu bagore biha abarinzi ku bushake, kandi urubanza rwabo bazarwikorera. Abandi batwawe ku ngufu kuko abarinzi bigaruriye abayobozi babo b'isi ba kera, nuko bagenda batwara abagore ku ngufu babakura ku bagabo babo n'imiryango yabo, nuko abarinzi batwara abo bahisemo bose ngo babe abagore babo, bahohotera benshi, kandi muri uko kwihuza kwanduye havutse ibihangange, ndetse ibihangange bikomeye bya kera. Kuko abarinzi barushijeho kwangiza imiterere y'ingirabuzimafatizo y'abantu. Nuko bacumura ku nyamaswa, n'inyoni, n'amafi ndetse n'ubwoko bwose bw'inyama barabivangavanga, ndetse mu kwigomeka kubushake kandi kwuzuye urwango ku mipaka nashyizeho, jyewe Yahweh, Imana Ikomeye Ihoraho! Kuva mu ntangiriro naremye isi buri mbuto ikurikije ubwoko bwayo, nuko imipaka irarengwa itari ikwiye na rimwe kuvogerwa, kandi kurenga imipaka kamere yashyizweho na Yahweh ni ukuzana ibisazi, imibabaro, n'urupfu ku byaremwe bya Yahweh, kandi ababikora bose bazacirwa urubanza. Ubuzima bwaremwe nk'urusobe rwuzuzanya, aho ubuzima bwose bwunze ubumwe mu mahoro n'ubwumvikane, aho urukundo rutegeka umunsi kandi bose bagashaka icyiza cy'abandi atari icyabo gusa. Ndetse n'ibyaremwe aho nta rari n'ibyifuzo bibi byo kwigaragaza mu rupfu n'irimbuka, kandi niko bizaba iteka n'iteka ryose, kandi ibihe byo gusohozwa biri hano.

Nuko abamarayika bakuru bane bo mu ijuru, ari bo Mikayeli, Uriyeli, Rafayeli, na Gabriyeli, barega ibyo bagenzi babo ba mbere bari bakoze mu isi, nuko urubanza ruratangazwa kandi rusohorezwa vuba ku babigizemo uruhare bose ndetse no ku isi ubwayo, kuko isi yariho icyo gihe yari yononekaye rwose. Abanefilimi, ndetse n'abana b'abarinzi, bategetswe kurwana hagati yabo kugeza ku rupfu mu ntambara nyinshi zikomeye hagati y'amoko ahanganye, ibyo byatewe n'uko nabakuyemo amahoro, nuko bararwana kugeza ku rupfu baricana mu gihe ba se (abarinzi) bahatiwe kureba irimbuka ry'ibizira byabo bari baremeye kurimbura abantu. Kuva aho, abarinzi baboshywe mu gihano n'urubanza kandi batandukanywa hagati yabo mu bihe mirongo irindwi. Kandi nk'uko nanjye nagombaga kureba ibyaremwe byanjye nakundaga cyane birimburwa n'ibizira bibi, niko nateye ibyo biremwa bibi kwicana ubwabyo nk'igihano kuri ba se, kandi niko nishyuye, kuko nkiranuka mu manza zanjye iteka n'iteka ryose, kandi ndi Imana y'imanza zikiranuka zidanirwa, cyangwa ngo zitinde gusohozwa.

Nuko nzana umwuzure ukomeye mu isi, kandi umwuzure wari igikorwa cy'urugomo mu minsi ya Nowa. Amasoko y'imuhengeri hakomeye yarasadutse kandi amazi asendera n'ingufu ava mu isi ubwayo, n'ingingimira z'ijuru zirakingurwa, bituma amazi yo hejuru y'isanzure yuzura isi, ndetse asumba imisozi miremire cyane, kandi aroha ubuzima bwose bwo ku isi, bose uretse abantu umunani barokotse ari bazima mu nkuge, kuko Nowa yabonetse akiranuka imbere y'amaso yanjye. Kandi kubera iyo mpamvu nafashe Nowa mutegeka kubaka inkuge nk'uko amabwiriza yatanzwe n'ukuboko kwanjye ari. Kandi muzanira inyamaswa zari kurokoka ari nzima nazo, nuko Nowa aba indahemuka mu gukora ibyo yategetswe byose, kandi umwuzure uraza nk'uko byahanuwe kandi bigatangazwa, kuko icyo ntangaje, jyewe Yahweh Imana ikomeye ihoraho, ntawe ushobora kugihindura cyangwa ngo akibuze na gato!

Nuko Henoki, umwanditsi wa kera, asobanura iherezo ry'ibi bihangange atya, kuko jyewe, ndetse Yahweh, nabitangaje nti: Kandi noneho, ibihangange, byabyawe n'imyuka n'umubiri, bizitwa imyuka mibi mu isi, kandi mu isi niho hazaba ubuturo bwabyo. Imyuka mibi yavuye mu mibiri yabyo; kuko byabyawe n'abantu, ku Barinzi bera niho hari intangiriro n'inkomoko yabyo ya mbere; bizaba imyuka mibi mu isi, kandi bizitwa imyuka mibi. Naho imyuka yo mu ijuru, mu ijuru niho hazaba ubuturo bwayo, ariko imyuka y'isi yavukiye mu isi, mu isi niho hazaba ubuturo bwayo. Kandi imyuka y'ibihangange irababaza, irakandamiza, irarimbura, iragaba ibitero, irarwana, kandi igakora irimbuka mu isi, igateza ibibazo: ntirya ibyo kurya, ariko nyamara irasonza kandi ikagira inyota, kandi igateza ibyaha. Kandi iyi myuka izahagurukira abana b'abantu n'abagore, kuko ari bo yavuyemo.

Nuko mu gihe gikwiye, Nowa agera ku Musozi wa Ararati, aho inkuge yaruhukiye uko amazi yagabanukaga. Kandi nasezeranye na Nowa, nuko mu gihe runaka abuzukuru ba Nowa batangira gutandukana n'isezerano nagiranye na Nowa, nuko imyuka mibi itangira kubagabaho ibitero. Nuko iyo myuka, yari myinshi, yibasira abana b'abahungu ba Nowa, nuko Nowa arabatakambira, nanjye mboha ibice icyenda bya cumi by'iyo myuka mibi mu kuzimu, aho yabitse kuva icyo gihe kugeza uyu munsi. Nuko ndategeka, nuko Nowa yigishwa imiti yabo yose, n'ibishuko arabyigishwa, kugira ngo Nowa abakize akoresheje ibyatsi byo mu isi. Nuko igice kimwe cya cumi cy'iyo myuka cyemererwa kuguma kidegemvya, kugira ngo bayobye mu gukiranirwa abana b'abantu bazifuza ibinyoma byabo. Nuko imyuka isigaye yaboshywe ngo izacirwe urubanza mu kuzimu izarekurwa mu bihe by'amakuba ya Yakobo, ubwo ikuzimu hazakingurwa rwose kandi ibice icyenda bya cumi byabo biboshwe bizarekurwa ngo bikore imigenzo yabyo mibi kandi y'umwanda nk'igice cy'urubanza rw'umuntu kamere ruri hano, kandi bizakwira mu isi mu buryo butunguranye kandi n'irimbuka rikomeye!

Nuko Kanani, umwuzukuru wa Nowa uwo Nowa yavumye kubera gukiranirwa kwe, akora icyaha gikomeye (nanone) akoresheje ubumenyi bw'abamarayika baguye, kandi mu nzira za Satani yagiyemo ku bushake, izo yayobowemo ku bushake, kandi akora ibindi bizira nyuma y'umwuzure wa Nowa. Kandi ibyo bizira byuzuye mu bihugu bya Shemu byatanzweho umugabane w'umurage, ibyo Kanani wavumwe yigaruriye kandi akiyibira, nuko bita igihugu cye yibye igihugu cya Kanani. Benshi mu rubyaro rwe rw'ibizira bari ibiremwa byahinduwe imiterere y'ingirabuzimafatizo byuzuye rwose mu bihugu by'isezerano, ibyo natumyeho Mose ngo akoranye abana ba Isirayeli abakure mu bihugu bya Egiputa ngo batware umurage wabo bawukure ku bizira. Nuko Yosuwa akora intambara ikomeye kuri ibyo bizira nk'uko byanditswe mu byanditswe byera, kandi bambura ibyo bizira umurage wabo wemewe, nuko byandikwa nk'igicucu cy'ibizaza. Kuko mu buryo nk'ubwo umwana wanjye, Yeshua HaMashiach, uwo nagize Umwami w'Abami n'Umutware w'Abatware, azaza gutwara umurage we ku bigaruriye ibitari ibyabo n'abanyazi, kuko ukwigarurira kwabo kunyuranyije n'amategeko. Si abami kandi nta bwami bafite, ahubwo inyanja nini yaka umuriro n'amazuku niyo murage wabo w'iteka.

Nyamara, Isirayeli ntiyabaye indahemuka rwose mu muhamagaro wayo, kandi ibisigazwa by'ibihangange byemerewe kuguma. Ariko ndetse no mu kutumvira kwabo narabujije ibyo bizira kandi ndimbura byinshi muri byo mu isi kuva icyo gihe. Kandi nirukaniye byinshi muri byo munsi y'ubutaka, kandi byinshi muri byo byagiye mu bihugu bitazwi hakurya y'urubura aho abantu batazerera bisanzuye. Sobanukirwa ko abantu badasobanukiwe isi baremweho kandi babaho, kuko abantu bigaburira ibinyoma by'ibihimbano bigurishwa nk'ukuri, bipfunyitswe bundi bushya nk'uburezi, nuko abantu ku bushake baguma mu bujiji bw'uko k'ijambo ry'Imana ijuru ryabayeho kera, n'isi ihagaze ivuye mu mazi kandi iri mu mazi: Ibyo byatumye isi ya kera irimbuka irengewe n'amazi: Ariko ijuru n'isi bya none, byabikiwe umuriro n'iryo jambo, birindirwa umunsi w'urubanza n'irimbuka ry'abatubaha Imana.

Nuko mu gihe gikwiye nkura ibihangange mu isi mu mbabazi zanjye n'ubuntu bwanjye n'icyifuzo cyanjye cyo gukiza abantu irimbuka, kandi ibisigazwa byabyo bikwirakwijwe hirya no hino ku isi kandi byuzuye mu byaremwe byanjye nk'ubuhamya bw'uko byabayeho. Ndetse n'ibisigazwa by'imijyi yabyo ya kera biracyahagaze nk'ubuhamya bw'uko byigeze gutegeka ubwami bw'amoko mu isi mu gihe cyabyo bita icyahizabu, ndetse no mu minsi ya Nowa mbere y'umwuzure. Amatongo yabyo y'amabuye manini akwirakwijwe hirya no hino nk'ubuhamya bubishinja, kandi ukubaho kw'ayo matongo guhagaze nk'ubuhamya bushunja ibinyoma bya Satani ubwe, ushakisha abigiranye umwete guhisha ukuri kugira ngo abone uko agaba igitero cye gitunguranye rwose. Nuko mbwira abafite amatwi yo kumva n'amaso yo kureba n'umutima wo kwiga ibizaba mbere y'uko biba, kugira ngo imitima yabo itabacika intege mu gutungurwa no kutizera, ahubwo kugira ngo bamenye ukuri kandi bitegure ibizaza kugira ngo bakizwe, ndetse n'abambaza izina rya Yesu Kristo bose kugeza ku iherezo ryabo.

Nuko byaratangajwe kandi byasohoye mu bihe byuzuye, ko narekuye abarinzi mbakura mu mabohero yabo aho bari baboshywe mu mibabaro n'ibihano, baboshywe batandukanyijwe. Nuko barekurwa nyuma y'uko ibihe byo kubohwa kwabo byuzuye: ndetse ibihe mirongo irindwi. Amateka ntashobora guhisha rwose irekurwa ryabo, kandi intambara nyinshi n'ibiza n'ibindi byabaye ku isi yose hamwe n'irekurwa ryabo, kandi biroshye mu burasirazuba, kandi bifatanije n'ukwigomeka kwa Satani, rwose, nanone, kuko niko biboshye iteka ryose mu ndahiro yabo ivumwe, kandi mu nyanja y'umuriro niho indahiro zabo zizasohozwa iteka ryose, kuko babohewe ku gihano n'umurage wa Satani iteka ryose.

Nuko biteguye mu gihe bahawe kandi mu buryo bufashwe mu yubile nyinshi ubu. Nta gushidikanya bongeye gukora ibizira byabo, kandi mu ibanga baritegura kandi bategereje ikurwaho ry'ubabuza ubwo bazagerageza nanone kurenga imipaka nabashyiriyeho, kuko bahora bigomeka. Kandi nubwo baboshywe ngo basohoze imigambi ya El Shaddai, kuko ndi Imana Ishoborabyose, bazashaka kongera gushyiraho igihe cyabo cya zahabu no kwitwarira isi, ibyo bikaba bidashoboka; kuko isi ari iy'Uwiteka n'ibiyuzuye byose, kandi icyangombwa cy'umutungo w'isi cyaguzwe n'umwana wanjye, Yeshua HaMashiach, kandi mu maboko ye akomeye niyo gihawe nk'umurage w'iteka, kandi azawusangira rwose n'abizerwa, abahamagawe, abatoranyijwe, n'abacunguwe.

Nuko Satani n'abarinzi biteguye, kandi bategereje n'amatsiko ikurwaho ry'ubabuza, kuko muri uko kukurwaho ari bwo bwami bwabo burekurwa rwose, kandi isi izongera kuba nk'uko yari imeze mu minsi ya Nowa. Dore! Ntanze itegeko, kandi ndabazanye: ibihangange biraje gusohoza uburakari bwanjye, byishima icyo gihe kimwe kandi bitukana, kandi nzabyemerera kurekurwa mu isi nanone kugira ngo nsohoze imigambi yanjye. Bizacika vuba bive aho byihishe, kandi byihishe mu mijyi yabyo munsi y'ubutaka, kandi byihishe inyuma y'urubura ruzahindurwa vuba, kandi mu isi y'abantu bizemererwa gutera, gusahura, no kwigarurira, kandi Ikigongwe kizabayobora kuko ari igice cy'ubwami bwacyo. Ariko mu gihe gito gusa, ndetse icyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli, icyumweru cy'imyaka irindwi bazarekurwa aho buri munsi ari umwaka, nuko hazaba imyaka irindwi. Kandi bizarimbura byinshi, kandi imitima y'abantu izabacika intege kubera isura yabyo iteye ubwoba n'ingano yabyo nini ugereranyije n'umuntu kamere.

Dore, NDI Ndi we ukomeye, watumye Amagambo yanjye ku mugaragu wanjye, kandi binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye amagambo yanjye yoherejwe. Nuko mbabwira ibintu mbere y'uko biba kugira ngo abantu banjye babe biteguye kandi basobanukirwe ibizaza, no kugira ubushishozi kuko ibi bintu bizaba vuba. Kandi abambaza izina rya Yeshua HaMashiach bose, ndetse Yesu Kristo mu rurimi rw'icyongereza, bazakizwa.

Nuko uburakari bukomeye n'urubanza rukomeye nk'uko byasezeranyijwe kera ubu byiteguye kuza rwose ku isi, kandi ibihe ni ubu, niko Imana Ikomeye Ihoraho ivuga! Mu gihe cyose cy'ubuntu ubutumwa bwiza bw'agakiza n'ibanga rikomeye ry'Imana (ryahishuriwe Intumwa Pawulo) byuzuye isi kandi byageze ku mpera z'isi. Kandi nubwo benshi, benshi, benshi bakijijwe, ndetse n'abaje kuri Yesu w'i Nazareti bose bihannye rwose, bakamugira Umwami wabo w'iteka, ibihe byo gusohozwa byageze kuko abantu bongeye kwanga umuremyi wabo. Kandi jyewe, El Shaddai, ndetse Imana Ishoborabyose, ndi we wabujije ibibi byinshi kugira ngo abaragwa b'agakiza bagere ku bugingo buhoraho muri Kristo Yesu, Umwami wabo wazamutse mu ijuru. Nuko none ntangaje ibi bintu mbere y'uko biba, kuko ndi Yahweh, NDI WE Ukomeye! Nuko mubabwire ko NDI WE UKOMEYE atangaza yivuye inyuma ko mu gukoranya abana banjye bizerwa ku mwana wanjye, ubwo bazazurwa ngo bamusanganire mu kirere kandi uko ubabuza akurwaho, ko ubwami bwa Satani bwose buzarekurwa ku isi kandi isi izahindurwa.

Ndabivuga mu rubanza ku batuye isi ko nzarekura ubwami bwa Satani bwose, kandi ibizira byabo byose bizakwira mu isi, kandi iyo myuka yose naboshye mu kuzimu mu minsi ya Nowa izarekurwa mu isi, kuko ibihe byo gutandukanya kwa nyuma n'urubanza rwa nyuma byageze ku isi, kandi umunsi w'urubanza rukomeye washyizweho. Nuko bizaba ko ubwami bwa Satani buzahabwa gutegeka isi, kuko mu butabera bwanjye, abantu bifuje cyane gutegekwa na Satani, ndetse bakizera ibinyoma bye by'uko ari imana nziza n'umutangamucyo, ku buryo noneho nzaha abantu ibyo bifuza cyane.

Kandi abaguye barekuwe rwose ku bw'urubanza rwabo, kandi bongeye gukora ibizira byabo. Kandi babohewe ku isi hamwe n'ikigongwe, kuko niko gihe cyabo kiri, kandi baziyoberanya nk'abanyamahanga, ndetse ibiremwa byiza biturutse mu zindi si. Nyamara jyewe, Yahweh Imana Ikomeye Ihoraho, ndahiye ku izina ryanjye bwite, kuko nta rindi rikomeye narahiriraho, ko nta si zizenguruka mu binyoma mu isanzure ryaguka ry'ubusa, ahubwo hejuru y'isi hari ijuru, ndetse ijuru ry'amajuru ari ryo juru ryo hejuru cyane, aho Imana Isumbabyose yicaye mu busugire bw'iteka ku ntebe yayo y'ubwami kandi nta gishobora kwihisha amaso yanjye.

Dore! Bamaze kwimenyekanisha, kandi ibintu byabo n'imibumbe yabo biri hose, kandi isi irahinduka vuba cyane ubu. Kandi nk'uko umuntu kamere ubu yanze amasezerano yanjye y'amahoro, ndetse n'umwana wanjye w'ikinege, Yesu w'i Nazareti, niko nanjye ubu nyanze umuntu kamere kandi nzamureka ategekwe rwose n'abanzi be, kandi bararikira irimbuka rye. Nyamara, kwanga kwanjye ntabwo kwuzuye cyangwa ngo kube ukwa nyuma, kuko umukumbi munini uzakizwa mu gihe cy'amakuba ya Yakobo, ndetse n'abambaza izina ry'Umwami bose, ndetse Yesu Kristo, kugeza ku iherezo. Kuko agakiza mu gihe cy'amakuba ya Yakobo kadashobora kuboneka mu yindi nzira. Nuko gutandukanya kwa nyuma kuzatangira, kandi abo nageneye ubugingo buhoraho bose kuva mbere y'uko imfatiro z'isi zishyirwaho bazagera ku bugingo buhoraho, kandi nzabazurira mu bugingo bushya, kandi bazagira ingororano ikomeye.

Nuko mwumve amagambo yanjye kandi mwerekeze imitima yanyu ku magambo yanjye y'umuburo n'itangazo, kuko ibihe by'abanyamahanga ubu byarangiye, kandi uburakari n'urubanza n'ihishurirwa rikomeye ubu biri ku isi, kuko jyewe, ndetse El Shaddai, ndetse Imana Ishoborabyose, ndi we wicaye hejuru, kandi nta gihishwe amaso yanjye, kandi ubu nzakora kugira ngo nkize rwose abaragwa b'agakiza bose uko mbatandukanya n'abagenewe irimbuka ry'iteka. Nuko ingano zizatandukanywa n'urumamfu iteka ryose, kandi abari mu gice cy'ukwigomeka kwa Satani bose bazahabwa urubanza, kandi ibihe by'urubanza rwabo byashyiriweho gukwira mu isi.

Mwumve Amagambo yanjye kandi musobanukirwe ubukana bw'ibizaza, kuko umunsi w'Uwiteka ari umunsi w'umwijima, kandi ni nde ushobora guhagarara?

Jyewe, Yahweh, Imana Ikomeye Ihoraho, ntangaje ibi bintu mbere y'uko biba, kandi nta gihe gisigaye. Ushaka wese naze, naze anywe ku buntu ku mazi y'ubugingo buhoraho, kandi ubutumire bwanjye ku bugingo buhoraho buracyahari, kandi abambaza izina ry'Umwami bose kugeza ku iherezo bazakizwa.

Jyewe, Yahweh, Imana Ikomeye Ihoraho, nohereje aya Magambo binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye, kandi binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye aya Magambo yoherejwe!

Ibihe ni ubu!
