Ubuhamya Bwombi: Isirayeli
Ibisharira N'ibiryohereye
15 Nyakanga 2024

Amahitamo ni Ayawe!

Tega amatwi amagambo y'umukiza wawe, Mwami n'Umwami wanjye Bana banjye kandi mutege amatwi Amagambo y'Umwami wanyu, ndetse na Yesu Kristo ukiranuka, kuko ndi umwana wo gukiranuka kandi nkazamuka mu bwiza bwanjye, kandi mwe mwakuze nk'inyana zo mu kiraro mu mahoro n'umutekano ugereranyije kandi nabarereye intego yo mu ijuru igiye guhishurwa byuzuye muri mwe, abizerwa banjye n'abatoranyijwe. Dore! Ndi ugerageza imitima y'abantu kugira ngo mumenye neza niba koko muri mu kwizera, cyangwa niba mwarayobejwe mukatwarwa n'ibyaha byanyu hamwe n'ubuyobe bukomeye bw'iki gihe bwigaruriye Isi.

Kuko ndi uwo Yahweh yasangiye intebe ye y'ubwami, ndetse na Kristo Yesu Umwami wazutse kandi uhawe ububasha, kandi ndi Umwami w'Abami n'Umwami w'abami kandi dore Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda iraza mu bicu kwakira abizerwa banjye kuri njye kugira ngo bajyanwe mu ijuru mu bukwe bw'Umwana w'Intama, kuko Umugeni wanjye yiteguye. Kandi amatara ye yuzuye, kandi yakase intambi zayo, kandi ategereje kugaragara kwanjye nk'isugi y'umunyabwenge, ndetse n'abo nejeje byuzuye kandi bakaba badafite inenge imbere y'intebe y'Imana. Ndetse n'abatatwawe mu buhendanyi na Baraba, kuko Baraba azana kwigomeka kw'urugomo, intambara n'amakuba, kuko ubu ari umutwe umwe mu mitwe myinshi ikorera hamwe, ariko azahaguruka nka Mesiya w'ikinyoma, kandi yatoranyijwe n'abantu bo mu gihugu ngo ategeke isinagogi ya Satani. Kuko ari umuragwa w'uwo ba se batoranyije, kandi barimo kwitegura kumwakira nk'Umwami wabo.

Ariko wowe, yewe Torero rya Lawodikiya, noneho haje ibihe byawe byo kurimbuka n'urubanza. Kuko mwihitiyemo undi mukiza nk'Umwami wanyu, ndetse n'uwo kuza kwe kubanzirizwa n'ibimenyetso n'ibitangaza by'ibinyoma kandi ubu wabayoboye mu burozi n'ubupfumu. Ndi Yeshua HaMashiach kandi nabaguze amaraso yanjye bwite y'impongano, atagira icyaha, kandi ndi uwo kubakiza, no kubarema bushya mu mwuka wanjye, kuko nahinduwe umwuka utanga ubuzima kuko ari ko Yahweh yategetse uburenganzira bwose ku Mwana we, ndetse n'uwo ari we mperuka y'umwana w'intama w'Imana hamwe n'Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda! Kandi mu gukiranuka nzaca urubanza nkorere intambara umwana w'intama w'Imana n'Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda byuzurijwe muri njye, Kristo Yesu, ndetse Yeshua HaMashiach kuko ari ko ndi!

Nzuzuza agakiza kanyu, yewe Torero rya Lawodikiya, ariko ntimwiteguye kwinjira mu Bwami bwanjye muri iki gihe kuko mwatandukiriye intego yanyu imwe muri Kristo! Nzaberereka ikosa ryanyu, kandi mufite rwose amahirwe yo kwihana ibyaha byose, ariko ntimugifite igihe gisigaye cyo kujya impaka n'ijwi ry'Umwami wanyu, kuko iki cyaha gikomeye ari kimwe mu byinshi Satani yakoresheje kugira ngo ababohe ntimuze kuri njye byuzuye mu kwihana kugira ngo mbeze byuzuye mu maraso yanjye y'impongano mbakureho ibyaha byanyu.

Dore! Ibihe by'abanyamahanga birarangiye kandi igiti cy'umwelayo w'ishyamba cyatewe muri bo, kandi kigabana na bo umuzi n'umubyibuho w'igiti cy'Umwelayo, ubu kigiye gutemwa. Kuko mwimye amatwi Amagambo ya Kristo Yesu, kuko nababwiye nkerura nti ntimwirarire ku mashami yaguye mu kutizera: ariko niba mwirasiye, ntimutwaye umuzi, ahubwo umuzi ni wo ubatwaye. Ndi Umuzi wa Yese, kandi ndi uwo uhagaze nk'urumuri rw'ibyiringiro ibihe byose, ndetse n'uzana agakiza n'inzira za Yahweh ku isi kandi nzategeka umurage wanjye ku ntebe ya Dawidi mu gukiranuka kandi nabaguze isi mu maraso yanjye atagira icyaha kandi nzatwara ibyo ari ibyanjye ku ngufu mu maboko y'abigarurira, ndetse n'abaguye.

Ndi uwahaye isi ubuntu n'imbabazi, kuko igihe cyose cy'ubuntu nagihawe na Yahweh kugira ngo abanyamahanga bakizwe. Kuko imigambi ya Yahweh muri Isirayeli yari buri gihe y'uko abanyamahanga bahabwa umugisha kandi bakabona agakiza binyuze mu kumvira kwa Isirayeli. Ariko uko igihe cyahitaga babaye abibone, n'abirasi, kandi batemwe mu giti cy'umwelayo w'umwimerere mu kutizera. Ariko mwe, Torero ryanjye rya Lawodikiya, ndetse namwe muri urugero rw'ubu bwibone, kutizera, n'ubuhumyi byerekanwe n'amashami y'umwimerere yatemwe mu kutizera, kandi ubu muzatemwa mu giti cy'umwelayo w'umwimerere. Kandi ubu ngiye gusoza umurimo wanjye ku giti cy'umwelayo w'umwimerere, ndetse Isirayeli mwene wacu n'abantu banjye, ndetse n'abo natoranyije bose ngo banyemere nk'Umwami wabo kugira ngo mbareme bushya nk'umuntu umwe mushya kandi kugira ngo ibanga ry'Imana rirangire, ndetse n'iherezo ry'imirimo yanjye y'impongano y'agakiza.

Kandi mwe, Torero ryanjye rya Lawodikiya, mwasize urukundo rwanyu rwa mbere kandi muri mu maboko y'umushukanyi mwarahiye kubera indahemuka. Mwihitiyemo Baraba mushya ndetse nk'Umwami wanyu kandi mwanze Yesu w'i Nazareti nk'Umwami wanyu kandi muri mu maboko y'undi mukiza w'ikinyoma. Ndetse n'uwiyita umukiza wa Babuloni y'ibanga, uvuga kandi agakora nk'umwe muri mwe, ariko akavuga nk'ikiyoka. Kuko Babuloni y'ibanga ibaho mu iraha no mu burumbuke bwinshi bw'ibintu, ibinezeza n'ubutunzi.

Kandi Babuloni y'ibanga yasinyeje isi yose umwanda w'ubusambanyi buri mu gikombe cyayo cya zahabu, ariko uko niko inzira z'abaguye zimeze, batanga umwanda mubi cyane, kandi muwunywa mubishaka kandi munezerewe cyane kuko mwahumwe amaso n'igikombe cya zahabu ku mwanda ukabije munywa mu buryo bw'umwanda. Kuko n'Itorero ryanjye rya Lawodikiya ritagiteranira ku byanditswe byanjye byera ahubwo mu makoraniro ya Baraba, uwo mwiringira. Kandi bityo muhitamo umukiza mushya kuko mukunda isi n'ibintu byo mu isi kandi ntimushobora kwiyumvisha igitekerezo cy'uko mwabura ibyiza byanyu by'agaciro, keretse nzabaka ibinezeza byose mu burakari bwanjye bugenda bwegereza, kandi n'ubu igihombo cyanyu kiriyongera!

Kuko muri impumyi kandi mwarayobye mu bwibone bwanyu ko Yahweh yatanga undi mukiza utari njye, Yesu w'i Nazareti, kuko ndi urubyaro rwasezeranyijwe rw'umugore kandi Dani ni ishashi y'intare itazakomokamo Mesiya n'umwe. Munyumve kandi munyumve neza, yewe torero rya Lawodikiya, kuko ubu ari ukwihana kwanyu mu byaha by'imitima yanyu cyangwa ubu ni igihe nzabasigira umuriro w'utunganya kandi abambaza izina ry'Umwami bose kugeza ku iherezo bazakizwa kandi benshi batuye izina ryanjye batizera bazagwa mu rwobo, batesheje agaciro amahirwe yabo ku ishusho yo kubaha Imana yihakanye imbaraga zayo: Banya Lawodikiya banjye, ndi imbaraga zayo kandi muri njye Yahweh yashyize uburenganzira bwose, kandi mwaranyihakanye, Umwami n'umukiza wanyu, kandi nzabaruka mbakure mu kanwa kanjye keretse mwihannye ubu!

Mu gihe cyanjye cy'ubuntu, agakiza kakwirakwiriye mu mahanga, kandi ishyanga ku rindi ryasangiraga kandi rikabona ku muzi w'igiti cy'umwelayo w'umwimerere. Kandi ubu, amahanga ntazongera kuba urumuri rw'agakiza kandi igihe cyabo mu Mwana cyazanywe no kwanga mwene wacu n'abantu kuko basakuje mu kutizera bati tubohorere Baraba! Ariko uko amabanga ya Yahweh yahishuwe n'intumwa zanjye zera n'abahanuzi, narabujije Baraba sinamurekura byuzuye, ahubwo naretse gusa ibanga ryo gukiranirwa rikomeza. Kandi ubu ibihe byageze aho nzarekura Baraba byuzuye kuko umukumirizi wanjye akuweho kandi uko abizerwa banjye, abahamagawe, n'abatoranyijwe bajyanwa mu ijuru aho nabateguriye ubuturo bwabo buhoraho, kandi bazambikwa imyenda myiza yera itagira inenge idashobora kwanduzwa cyangwa ngo ihabwe inenge.

Mbese mwahumwe amaso cyane ku isi yanyu, kandi mupfa amatwi ku buryo mutacyumva ijwi ryanjye yewe Torero rya Lawodikiya? Mwizeye ibinyoma byazanywe n'ibanga ryo gukiranirwa, kandi mwemereye umusemburo w'uburozi muri Bibiliya zanyu zangiritse byazanye ruswa yuzuye mu Matorero yanyu yanduje imitima yanyu bwite n'imigani mu kutizera kuyobya. Mwemereye ibinyoma by'abapfumu b'ibihe kubaroga n'inyandiko z'ibinyoma n'amagambo y'ibinyoma. Ntimugisobanukirwa ibyanditswe, kandi mwiyikoreye amakuba akomeye azabazaho gitungura. Buri Jambo ry'Imana riraboneye kandi rigenewe kumvira ko kwizera no kubaka abaryakira. Musobanukirwe Amagambo y'Amagambo yanjye kandi musobanukirwe ibihe mubayemo.

Kuko nategetse Yohana Uhishurirwa kwandika ati: Kandi mbona inyamaswa izamuka iva mu nyanja, ifite imitwe irindwi, n'amahembe icumi, kandi ku mahembe yayo hari amakamba icumi, n'amazina yo gutuka Imana ku mitwe yayo.

Kandi nanone Yohana Uhishurirwa yaranditse ati: Mbona umugore yicaye ku nyamaswa y'ibara ry'umuhengeri, yuzuye amazina yo gutuka Imana, ifite imitwe irindwi, n'amahembe icumi. Kandi uwo mugore yari yambaye imyenda y'umuhengeri n'itukura, atatsweho zahabu, n'amabuye y'agaciro n'imaragarita, afite igikombe cya zahabu mu ntoki ze, cyuzuye ibizira n'umwanda w'ubusambanyi bwe.

Kandi nanone: Kandi amahembe icumi wabonye kuri ya nyamaswa, azanga maraya, amuhindure umusaka, amwambike ubusa, arye inyama ze, amutwike n'umuriro. Kuko Imana yashyize mu mitima yabo gukora ibyo ishaka, no guhuza inama, no guha inyamaswa ubwami bwabo, kugeza aho amagambo y'Imana azasohorera. Kandi uwo mugore wabonye, ni wa Murwa mukuru utegeka abami bo mu isi.

Bana banjye, musobanukirwe ko inyamaswa ishobora kwerekeza ku bantu ndetse no ku bwami, mwige kugira ngo mumenye. Inyamaswa itwaye maraya izarimbura maraya kandi ntizongera kugandukira maraya, kuko yategetse abami bo mu isi mu busambanyi bwayo. Bityo inyamaswa izazamuka iva mu nyanja izabyara Mahdi wabo bategereje, ariko izarimbura rwose Babuloni y'Ibanga, kuko Imana yagabije maraya ukomeye mu maboko y'abanzi be basanzwe bamwanga urunuka.

Mbere y'uko inyamaswa iva mu nyanja n'inyamaswa iva mu isi bizamuka byuzuye mu Bwami bw'ikiyoka, hari abategetsi benshi bunze ubumwe mu ntego yo guha ikiyoka imbaraga zabo n'ubutegetsi bwabo, kuko Imana yabishyize mu mitima yabo ngo babikore. Kandi bazarimbura Babuloni y'ibanga. Mbese mutekereza ko mwamenye neza ibyanditswe byanjye byera kuko mwizeye abanyatewolojiya banyu b'abanyabinyoma? Musobanukirwe kandi mwumve Amagambo y'Umwami wanyu!

Nanone, nategetse Yohana Uhishurirwa kwandika ati: Kandi inyamaswa nabonye, yasaga n'ingwe, n'amaguru yayo ameze nk'ay'idubu, n'akanwa kayo nk'akanwa k'intare: kandi ikiyoka kiyiha imbaraga zacyo n'intebe yacyo y'ubwami, n'ubutware bukomeye. Kandi mbona umwe mu mitwe yayo usa n'uwakomeretse bikabije, ariko uruguma rwayo rwica rurakira, nuko isi yose itangazwa n'iyo nyamaswa irayikurikira.

Bana banjye, amagambo ya Yahweh afite ubujyakuzimu bwinshi, nta muntu numwe ushobora kumenya byose. Inyamaswa ivugwa ni ubwami buzazamuka buvuye mu irimbuka rya maraya, kandi mu gahunda gashaje hazazamuka gahunda nshya! Ihagarariye inyamaswa yo mu nyanja, kuko izazamuka nka Mahdi wabo, kandi ihagarariye imiterere y'ubwami azategekesha inyanja. Kuki mutubaha amagambo y'Ijambo ryanjye? Ntivuga ko inyamaswa yo mu nyanja ubwayo yakomeretse, kuko ni uwuhe muntu ufite imitwe irindwi mu by'ukuri? Ariko umwe mu mitwe yayo usa n'uwakomeretse bikabije. Yemwe bapfu, n'abatinda mu mitima kwizera ibyo Abahanuzi bavuze byose! USA N'UWAKOMERETSE bikabije. Amagambo "usa n'uwakomeretse" yagakwiye kubahagarika, ariko ntimukizera n'ijambo ryanjye rito, kandi ndi Ijambo ryabaye umubiri. Mbese ntibigaragara kuri mwe, USA N'UWAKOMERETSE, ni imvugo ya kera? Mbese ntibigaragara kuri mwe ko "usa" ari imvugo y'icyifuzo? Mbese ntibigaragara kuri mwe ko ari ukugabanya kwa, nk'aho byari bimeze bityo?

Ariko oya, mwizera ibinyoma by'ikiyoka, kuko Baraba avuga nk'ikiyoka, ariko si Baraba, ahubwo ni inyamaswa iva mu isi. Kandi noneho isi yose izatangazwa n'inyamaswa kubera ibimenyetso byayo n'ibitangaza by'ibinyoma, kandi izakora ibindi bimenyetso n'ibitangaza byinshi by'ibinyoma, kandi ntiyazamutse byuzuye kuko maraya ataratwikwa n'umuriro, ariko ubu ahishuwe mu gihe cye. Avuga nk'ikiyoka, ati "Imana yonyine niyo yamukijije uruguma rwo mu mutwe" kandi iki ni ikinyoma gituka Imana. Yahweh, Imana ikomeye ihoraho, yantumye Yesu w'i Nazareti nk'umukiza w'isi. Jyewe na Data turi umwe mu bumwe n'intego kandi Yahweh ntiyakijije Baraba kandi nanjye, Kristo Yesu sinamukijije. Mwarashutswe kandi mufatwa mutegwa mu bushukanyi bukomeye, kandi ndababwira nti mwihane mugaruke ku rukundo rwanyu rwa mbere kandi mubikore ubu, kuko nta gihe gihari!

Kuko ndi ugerageza imitima, kandi ndi ugerageza abo mpamagara bose ku gakiza, kuko neza bose mu muriro w'utunganya! Dore! Ndi uwicara nk'utunganya kandi weza ifeza: kandi neza abana ba Yahweh, nkabasukura nka zahabu n'ifeza, kugira ngo batambire Umwami ituro mu gukiranuka. Mbese ntimusobanukirwa ko nzabagira abatambyi n'abami imbere ya Data? Nubwo impano yanjye y'umwuka wera, ibatuma muvuka ubwa kabiri ku mbuto zibora ari ntamakemwa kandi idakeneye gutunganywa, mukomoka kuri Adamu mu maraso kandi mugomba kwemerera byuzuye umurimo wanjye gukorwa mu buzima bwanyu, keretse niba imbuto yanjye yaraguye mu butaka bw'amabuye.

Ubu haje ibihe by'urubanza kandi ibihe by'ubuntu birarangiye. Nubwo benshi bazakoba amagambo yanjye bagatera umugongo mu kutizera, ibizavamo byamaze kumenywa na Yahweh. Kuko Yahweh buri gihe yereka abantu inzira zabo zangiritse kandi agacyaha abantu ibyaha, azi mu rukundo rwe n'ubwenge buhoraho ko azakiza bamwe. Kandi nk'uko Yahweh yinangiye umutima wa Farawo, niko azanangira imitima ya benshi bumva ariko ntibatege amatwi. Yahweh azagerageza umuntu inshuro nyinshi. Kandi bamwe bazaba nka Farawo kandi bazanangira imitima yabo kurushaho mu kutizera ariko bamwe bazava mu nzira za Farawo bazamuke mu buzima buhoraho muri Kristo Yesu, ariko binyuze mu muriro w'utunganya.

Musobanukirwe ko abahitamo Baraba bazigira abafatanyacyaha mu byaha bya Baraba, kandi abanga kwambaza izina ryanjye kugeza ku iherezo bose bazacirwaho iteka kuko bishimiye gukiranirwa. Kuko umukiza wanyu mushya atari umukiza ahubwo ni inyamaswa y'inkazi. Kandi gahunda ye n'ubwami bwe bigizwe n'abagirira nabi abana. Mbese ntimusobanukirwa inzira z'Ikiyoka? Kuko abakurikira inzira z'ikiyoka bahiga abana. Batamba abana kuri Moloki, bashaka isoko yabo y'ubusore mu maraso y'abana batotejwe, basambanya abana ku bw'irari ryabo rirwaye, kandi bakora ibyo gukubita urusoro mu nda kugira ngo bice abana bato mbere yo kuvuka, kandi babayoboye mu ibanga mu nzira zabo, kuko uko niko inzira z'abayobozi ba Babuloni y'Ibanga zimeze ndetse n'ubwami bw'ikiyoka. Kandi ubu haje urubanza rw'Imana kuri ibi bintu n'ibindi byinshi. Kuko iyo mutabona ibi bintu ntimuba mufite icyaha, ariko ubu murabona kandi icyaha cyanyu kiragumaho.

Mbese ntimwasomye? Uku niko Umwami avuga, Ijwi ryumvikanye mu ijuru, umuborogo n'ukurira gukomeye. Rasheli, aririra abana be, yanze guhumurizwa ku bw'abana be, kuko batakiriho. Kuko mwananiwe inshingano mwahawe, kandi mwahawe amabanga yanjye kandi mwahagaze nk'urumuri rw'agakiza n'ikimenyetso cy'ibyiringiro. Ariko ubu mwifatanyije n'abo naboherereje guhagarara imbere. Kandi bityo ubu haje ibihe nzabasigira ejo hazaza hanyu.

Munyumve kandi munyumve neza mwese abakwanga ijwi rya Yeshua HaMashiach, ndetse n'ijwi rya Kristo Yesu: nta gihe mufite! Ndabagira inama yo kunyumva mu rukundo rwanjye kuri mwe bana banjye kandi mwihane ubu! Kuko umuriro w'utunganya ugiye kubagwaho byuzuye, kandi uzeza igihugu ubwami bwose bw'ikiyoka kandi isi ntizongera kugandukira inzira za Satani ukundi kandi ubwami bwe ntibuzongera kuzamuka ukundi, kuko ndi Umwami w'Abami n'Umwami w'Abami, kandi nzarimbura rwose abaguye. Abanga kwihana bose bazagira uruhare rwabo mu nyanja y'umuriro kandi bazasangira igihano cya Satani n'abamarayika be muri iyo nyanja nini y'amazuku yaka. Uko niko, abihana bose bava mu cyaha bakambaza izina ryanjye kugeza ku iherezo bazakizwa kandi bahabwe ingororano ikomeye ihoraho muri njye.

Dore! Ubu ni igihe cyo gukoranya abizerwa, kandi ubu nzasigira abanga kuri Baraba, kuko bakoze amahitamo yabo.

Ndi umwana wo gukiranuka kandi nkazamuka mu bwiza bwanjye.

Dore! Jyewe Yeshua HaMashiach nohereje aya magambo binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye, kandi binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye aya magambo yoherejwe!

Abadahindukira ngo bave kuri uyu mesiya w'ikinyoma bazabona agakiza mu muriro w'utunganya, cyangwa bagwe mu rwobo ibihe byose: Amahitamo ni ayawe!
