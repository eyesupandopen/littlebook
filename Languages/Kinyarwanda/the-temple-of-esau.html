Ubuhamya Bwiri: Isirayeli
Ibisharira Biryoshye
23 Ugushyingo 2024

Urusengero rwa Esawu

Umva, umva! Yewe Isirayeli ijwi rya Yeshua HaMashiach, ndetse n'umwana w'ikinege wa Yahweh, NDI WE ukomeye! Kugira ngo nkuburire ku bintu bizaza, kandi kugira ngo urusheho kumenya umutego Barabasi azatega kugira ngo udafatwa mu mutego n'abatizera; ibyo bintu byanditswe mbere igice mu Ivanjiri y'Iteka. Nyamara nje kubabwira umutego igitangangurirwa gitega mu gihugu cy'isezerano no kubisobanura neza kugira ngo mutarimburwa n'imigambi yabo. Kandi nifuza gutunganya inzira zanyu no kubagumisha mu nzira zo gukiranuka, ndetse n'inzira za Yahweh, inzira ze zibaho mu buryo bwuzuye muri Yeshua HaMashiach Mesiya wanyu w'ukuri wumvira iteka ubushake bwa Yahweh. Kandi kugira ngo mukomeze ikirenge cyanyu muri iyo nzira ifunganye kandi igororotse, iyo nzakomeza ibirenge byanyu bishinze niba mukomeje kunkurikira mu kwizera kumvira. Kuko umukiranutsi azabeshwaho no kwizera!

Sobanukirwa rero ko igihe kigeze, nk'uko byatangajwe kandi bikamenyeshwa aho nzateranyiriza mu buryo butunguranye abizerwa muri Kristo mu ijuru baba abazima cyangwa abapfuye; kandi uko abera bizerwa batwarwa mu buryo butunguranye kurimbuka kuzatungura Babuloni y'Ibanga yicaye ku mazi menshi, ari yo bantu, n'amateraniro, n'amahanga, n'indimi. Kandi uko ivu ry'intambara rigurumana n'imiterere y'amahanga y'ubu isenywa kandi igahuzwa kugira ngo ihuze n'imigambi yabo, gahunda y'inyamaswa ya nyuma y'uwaguye izazamuka yuzuye.

Nyamara, inyamaswa yo mu nyanja n'inyamaswa yo mu isi byakinnye uruhare rw'abungushumba b'inyangamugayo baje kuragira intama zazimiye za Babuloni y'Ibanga bafite amasezerano menshi y'ubusa; ndetse n'amasezerano y'ibinyoma amaherezo azana urupfu n'irimbuka. Kandi biyoberanya nk'abanzi inyuma y'isura y'umusaza w'igikoresho wihishe inyuma yabo kandi uwo mukoresho ari aho kugira ngo aryozwe ibyaha byabo; kandi bagenzura buri ntambwe ye bakamwandikira buri jambo. Kuko uko kuyoba gukomeye gutangwa mu rubanza kuri Babuloni y'Ibanga; ko ukuyoba gukomeye kugomba kumufata wese kuko yatwikiriye isi mu binyoma bye afite igikombe cya zahabu mu ntoki ze, cyuzuye ibizira n'imyanda y'ubusambanyi bwe.

Kandi nyamara izo nyamaswa ebyiri mbi zasahuye Babuloni y'Ibanga, ndetse na mbere y'uko irimbuka ryayo riza kandi zatwaye imbaraga zayo za gisirikare n'ubutunzi ku bw'imigambi y'ikiyoka muri gahunda yabo y'inyamaswa izaza. Kuko intambara izaza izarimbura Babuloni y'Ibanga, kandi koko izarimburwa mu isaha imwe, kuko ari ko byatangajwe na Yahweh, ndetse NDI WE Ukomeye. Nyamara, ibi ni ku bw'umugambi kandi byashyizwe mu mugambi wa Yahweh w'agakiza (ko abanzi be bagomba kumvira ijwi rye) bityo urubanza rwa Maraya ukomeye ruratangazwa, kandi rwanditswe, kandi niko bizakorwa iteka ryose, kandi ntazigera yongera kuzuka. Ndetse n'ubwo bimeze bityo, bazatanya isi kandi hazabaho gutigisa gukomeye, kandi bateguye irimbuka rye kugira ngo bakusanye umutungo bararikira utabye mu isi. Ndetse n'umutungo bakeneye kugira ngo bahe imbaraga kandi bagure ikoranabuhanga ry'uwaguye; ndetse n'aho isi ubu itangajwe kandi irogezwe mu kuyoba kwayo gukomeye, igumishwa mu bubata bw'inshuro zabo n'amayeri yabo aho bakangurwa kandi bakagenzurwa.

Kandi mu bantu bazasigara nyuma y'irimbuka rizaza rya Babuloni y'Ibanga; inyamaswa yo mu isi n'inyamaswa yo mu nyanja bazanye ingabo z'abateye n'abicanyi mu mipaka yayo umubare wabo umeze nk'icyorezo gikomeye cy'inzige, kandi bazarekurwa ku kimenyetso cyabo! Kuko izi ari ingabo z'inyamaswa zakoranyirijwe kurimbura Babuloni y'Ibanga. Benshi muri aba baturuka mu bihugu by'ikiyoka no mu bihugu by'idubu, kandi bazakora nk'uko Babuloni y'Ibanga yabakoreye mu ibanga mu myaka ya yubile yashize: Bazica buri mugabo, umugore, n'umwana nta mbabazi utajyanye n'imigambi yabo kandi benshi bazemererwa gukomeza kubaho bazahatirwa kujya mu nkambi zabo z'abacakara gukora mu birombe byabo kugeza bapfuye. Kandi bazakora cyane kugeza ku mwuka wabo wa nyuma; kuko ibyo Babuloni y'Ibanga yakoreye abandi mu ibanga byose ubu bizamukorerwa ku mugaragaro mu rubanza n'igihano. Kuko Babuloni y'Ibanga yasinze amaraso y'abera, n'amaraso y'abamaritiri ba Yesu yiciye mu bwinshi mu bihugu by'amahanga; mu gihe cyose yivuga ko we ubwe akorera Yesu w'i Nazareti. Ariko mu by'ukuri akorera abaguye kandi imigambi ye ubu yararangiye, kandi bararikira irimbuka rye kugira ngo bahishire ibyaha byabo kandi kugira ngo abungushumba babo babi bagaragare nk'abanyabuntu nibazamuka kugira ngo bahishire umutego wabo.

Nyamara, ntibashobora kwihisha Yahweh, cyangwa Yeshua HaMashiach kandi ibyaha byabo byanditswe neza mu ijuru kandi buri cyaha cyanditswe mu buryo burambuye hamwe n'ibitekerezo n'imigambi y'imitima yabo yanduye. Kandi inyamaswa yo mu nyanja n'inyamaswa yo mu isi ni ibizira bibi byaremewe kurimbura muntu. Izi nyamaswa mbi ni abasambanya abana, kandi bakusanya kandi bagakoresha imibiri y'abana bato bishe kugira ngo bakore ibizira byabo bityo ibyo barimbura byose bitangwa ho igitambo ku mana yabo. Kuko batwaye ihema rya Moloki, n'inyenyeri y'imana yabo Remfani, ibishushanyo bakoze, kugira ngo babiramye, kandi bakoze byinshi cyane bizahishurwa byuzuye mu bihe bikwiye byo guhishurwa kuko buri banga rizajya ahagaragara.

Uko niko bizaba ko inyamaswa yo mu isi izasa n'izuka nk'aho ivuye mu mva; ndetse yivuga ko yazutse mu bapfuye, ndetse no mu mutima w'isi kuko urupfu rutashoboraga kumugumana! Kandi azagira umuzuko we w'ikinyoma azane amahoro y'ikinyoma ku isi kugira ngo arangize intambara yabo, kandi azagaragara ko aje gukiza muntu. Kandi abaguye baziyereka muntu nk'abafasha beza baruta cyane mu bwenge kandi bateye imbere mu nzira z'ikoranabuhanga, ubuvuzi, n'amahoro.

Bazakora ibimenyetso n'ibitangaza byinshi by'ibinyoma, kandi bazavuga ko abakoranyirijwe muri Kristo mu ikoraniro rikomeye bakajyanwa mu mutekano w'ijuru mbere y'uko amakuba akomeye agwa; ko ndetse n'abo batwawe ku ngufu n'umwanzi wiyita uwo mu isanzure. Ndetse n'ingabo z'abanyamahanga zangiza zizaza kongera kurimbura muntu, kandi bazashishikariza umuntu kamere kwifatanya nabo kugira ngo yikize ubu bubi batekereza mu isanzure ryabo ry'ikinyoma ry'isi zizenguruka mu kinyoma mu cyuho cyaguka cy'ubusa.

Ndetse n'imiterere y'isi y'ikinyoma abaguye batekereje kandi bagakoresha mbere mu gihe cyabo cya zahabu cya kera, icyo gihe cya zahabu cyarangiye mu maboko ya Yahweh mu mwuzure mukomeye mu bihe bya Nowa, ibyo byanditswe byanditswe mu byanditswe byera bya kera. Kandi ibyo binyoma bizajugunywa hasi kuko ikinyoma cyabo gikomeye kizashyirwa ahagaragara wese kugira ngo bose babone aba biyita abo batari bo kuko ari ibiremwa byaguye biva mu ijuru byamaze kwiyanduza mu bwigomeke butagira umumaro ku gukiranuka kwa Yahweh ndetse kugira ngo baragwe irimbuka ryabo biremeye mu murage wabo w'amazuku yaka umuriro batazigera bacika.

Kandi bityo inyamaswa yo mu isi izazamuka isa n'iyazutse mu bapfuye kandi ifite ibimenyetso n'ibitangaza byinshi by'ibinyoma izagaragara, ndetse n'ishami ryayo ry'umunzenze mu ntoki. Kandi izazamuka nk'umwana w'intama ufite amahembe abiri uko izamuka mu mbaraga zose n'ubutware bw'ikiyoka ndetse n'inyamaswa yo mu nyanja, ndetse n'uwo watoranyijwe n'ikiyoka n'inyamaswa yo mu isi kugira ngo ategeke amahanga kugira ngo ategeke inyanja y'amahanga hakurikijwe amategeko yabo ya Nowahidi atuka Imana kandi arimbura imbaga. Nyamara ibihugu bye bibiri ntibizigera biba igisubizo kuko ndi we uzahuza Isirayeli iteka ryose ntizigera yongera kugabanywamo ubwami bubiri.

Kandi bityo bazakoresha amategeko yabo mabi n'ingaruka zikubiye muri Nowahidi kugira ngo barwanye abera kandi babaneshe, keretse umuntu wese wambaza izina ry'Umwami kugeza ku iherezo azakizwa, ndetse n'abambaza Yesu w'i Nazareti nk'Umwami wabo ukiza uzabakiza rwose niba badacogora mu makuba yabo. Kandi bityo inyamaswa yo mu nyanja izarekurwa ku bisigaye by'amahanga bazaba bararimbuye, kandi mu ivu ryayo hari amahanga bigeze gutegeka mu buriganya bukomeye. Kandi bazagaragara nk'inyoni nini ya fenigisi izamuka mu ivu n'ibirimi by'umuriro by'intambara bigurumana biva mu buriganya bwabo. Kandi hazaba intambara ikomeye, ndetse n'ikomeye cyane mu ntambara eshatu nk'izo bateje kandi bakayobora; kugira ngo ikiyoka kizamuke cyuzuye mu gihe cyacyo n'inyamaswa zacyo mbi, ziteye ishozi izategekesha igihe gito, ntizongere gutegeka ukundi.

Kuko ibanga ry'ubugome ryagiye rikora mu bihe byose; aho maraya mukomeye wicaye ku misozi irindwi, ndetse na maraya mukomeye utukura w'ibitutsi uramya umwamikazi w'ijuru kandi wigisha undi Yesu nk'igice cy'ubutatu bwabo bwa gipagani. Ndetse n'imizi yabo ishinze muri Babuloni ya kera aho Nimurodi yatangiriye imigani ye n'umwamikazi w'ijuru. Uwo murage n'abatambyi n'imirongo y'amaraso bikomoka ku baguye bategekaga Babuloni ya kera n'ubutatu bwabo butagatifu bakoresha bashinyagurira ubumana bw'ukuri, n'urubyaro rwasezeranyijwe rw'umugore. Iyi migenzo n'imihango ya gipagani byiganje ku isi mu ibanga rikomeye aho abatambyi batangijwe b'abaguye baramya izuba mu ibanga ariko bakavuga ko ari Umuryango wa Yesu; kandi niko itorero ryabo ry'ikinyoma kandi rituka Imana ryakoresheje igitugu n'itoteza ku isi mu minsi ibanga ry'ubugome ryakwirakwiraga mu ibanga. Ku bw'ibyo, irimbuka ryabo ntiritinda kandi ibihe byabo byageze!

Nyamara izi nyamaswa ebyiri mbi, ndetse n'izikora ubu zifatanyije kugira ngo zirimbure rwose Babuloni y'ibanga, ndetse n'inyamaswa yo mu nyanja n'inyamaswa yo mu isi; ndetse n'aba bombi babaye imitwe itegeka ya Babuloni y'Ibanga kandi bategekanye amasezerano menshi akomeye kandi y'ubusa aho aba bungushumba babi baragiye intama zazimiye za Babuloni y'Ibanga basezeranya ubutunzi bw'ikinyoma n'ubukire ku bo baragira. Kandi mu ntoki zabo no mu migambi yabo bateguye rwose kandi bazasohoza ibyahawe mu ntoki z'ikiyoka kugira ngo kibikore.

Kuko Babuloni y'Ibanga, ndetse na maraya mukomeye wicaye ku mazi menshi ni ishyanga ryakozwe kugira ngo izi nyamaswa ebyiri mbi zishobore kuzamuka mu ivu ryayo mu bihe byazo byo guhishurwa. Ndetse n'ishyanga ry'abasambanyi, ndetse n'ishyanga ry'ubusambanyi, ishyanga koko ari maraya mukomeye wiyita umukurikira w'ukuri wa Yesu w'i Nazareti. Ariko mu by'ukuri ni ishyanga ryubatswe n'abubatsi bigobotoye ku Rutare rw'Urufatiro rw'ukuri, ndetse n'iryo buye rikomeye ry'imfuruka abatizera basitaraho, ndetse na Yesu w'i Nazareti ari we Yeshua HaMashiach. Kandi bityo aba bubatsi babi bigobotoye ku gakiza kandi biyegurira rya ziba ry'umuriro aho inyo yabo idapfa banga ibuye rikuru ry'imfuruka n'urutare rwo gusitaraho kandi ntibazacika!

Nyamara abaguye bahora bafite intego zimwe mu ntekerezo kandi imigambi yabo n'intego zabo biguma ari bimwe. Kuko bagambiriye kurimbura rwose Adamu ku isi, kandi bagambiriye rwose kurimbura Yakobo munsi y'izuba ndetse kugira ngo bakomeze kuguma nk'abimukira n'abigarurira isi gukiranuka kugira ngo bashobore kugira igihe cyabo cya zahabu bararikira. Ariko musobanukirwe ko igihe cyabo cya zahabu cyari igihe Adamu yarengerwaga kandi agategekwa n'ubwami bw'isi yose bw'ibizira bibi byaremewe kurimbura rwose muntu, kandi ibizira byabo bizagaruka! Ariko nta gihe cya zahabu bazagira ahubwo ibyiringiro byamenetse n'urubanza rukomeye uko ubwami bw'abaguye burimburwa rwose imbere y'amaso yabo n'ukuboko kwanjye gukomeye!

Kandi icyo bita igihe cyabo cya zahabu cyari igihe umurongo w'abakiranutsi wa Seti wihishaga mu butayu kure y'umuryango wabo mubi mu isi yariho icyo gihe. Ndetse kugira ngo umurongo w'amaraso wa Seti ugume uboneye utandujwe n'ingirabuzimafatizo kandi urubyaro rwasezeranyijwe rw'umugore (ari rwo ndi rwo), rushobore kuza nk'uko byasezeranyijwe kera na Yahweh ubwe. Dore! Yahweh yatangaje ko azazana Abagabo banini kugira ngo basohoze umujinya we, kandi niko bizaba. Nyamara, niko ubwami bwabo bubi buzazamuka kandi bugategeka isi yose kandi bazazana ibibi byinshi, kandi ibizira byinshi bizarekurwa biva hakurya y'urubura no mu mutima w'isi kandi ikuzimu hazakingurwa.

Hazabaho ibibi bikomeye ku isi kandi nubwo bazazamuka bakazana amahoro y'ikinyoma; intambara izahora itegeka ubutegetsi bwabo kuko amahoro agomba gukurwa ku isi mu rubanza mu gukuraho uwo nkomanga. Kandi amahoro ntibazigera bayagira kuko Yahweh yatangaje kera ko abaguye nta mahoro bafite kuko bahemukiye muntu mu irari ryabo ribi n'urwango rw'ibintu byose byaremwe na Yahweh. Kandi bazashyiraho amahoro y'ikinyoma mu gihugu kandi inyamaswa yo mu isi izaza rwose mu mbaraga zayo, kandi izategeka nk'umwana w'intama ufite amahembe abiri uvuga nk'ikiyoka.

Kuko umwana w'intama wa Esawu, ndetse n'inyamaswa yo mu isi ari we kandi Barabasi, yatoranyijwe nk'umwubatsi wabo w'urusengero rw'inyamaswa kandi atangwa nk'umwana w'intama w'ikiyoka kuri Moshiach wabo w'ikinyoma n'umwami wiyita we. Koko, koko, azahuza Esawu na Ashikenazi n'abifatanya nabo bose munsi y'ibendera ry'idini ba Se batari bazi kandi inzira zabo za Talimudi zizategeka isi. Bazakandamiza amahanga munsi ya Nowahidi kandi bazategeka amahanga n'ubugome bukomeye; kandi inyamaswa yo mu isi izatuma bose, abato n'abakuru, abakire n'abakene, abidegemvya n'abacakara bahabwa ikimenyetso cyayo mu ntoki zabo z'iburyo cyangwa mu ruhanga rwabo kugira ngo hatagira umuntu ushobora kugura cyangwa kugurisha, keretse ufite icyo kimenyetso, cyangwa izina ry'inyamaswa, cyangwa umubare w'izina ryayo. Kuko azana kurumwa n'inzoka nta muntu n'umwe ushobora gukira kandi ababifatanyamo bose bazaba barihinduye iteka ryose iryo hinduka ntibazababarirwa, kandi bazavanga icyuma n'ibumba, ariko ntibazafatana. Icyagoramye ntigishobora kugororwa kandi abigiramya bose muri ubwo buryo bazagira umugabane wabo muri rya ziba rikomeye ry'amazuku yaka umuriro utazima aho inyo yabo idapfa.

Bityo inyamaswa yo mu isi ari we kandi muhanuzi wabo w'ikinyoma na Moshiach w'ikinyoma azakora amatangazo ye kandi azubaka urusengero rwe kuko yatumwe nk'umwubatsi w'urusengero rw'inyamaswa wiyubakiye iminara myinshi mu bwami bwe yitiriwe izina rye, kandi azivugaho mu kwishimira gukomeye ndetse yivuga ko we ubwe ari Imana. Kuko aje gutanga amasezerano menshi akomeye adafitiye imbaraga zo gusohoza, kandi aje kuvuga ibintu byinshi bikomeye kandi bituka Imana, kandi niko azakora kuko niko yahawe gusohoza. Kuko ari umwubatsi n'uteza imbere wiyubakiye iminara myinshi n'ahantu ho kuruhukira, kandi koko yicaye hejuru y'ubwami bwe bwite yiyeguriye mu cyubahiro cye; kuko ahora yishimagiza kuko niko inzira z'ikiyoka ari we Se w'ukuri zimeze.

Uko urusengero rwe rwubakwa kandi rukarangizwa mu birori bikomeye n'amasezerano menshi yo gutuka Imana n'ibinyoma, niko azavuga amagambo menshi y'ibinyoma mu mirimo ye nk'umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma kandi ingoma ye izaba iy'ubugome n'ubunyamaswa. Nyamara, azatega umutego we ariko ni uwuhe mutezi utega umutego inyoni ireba? Mu bwihisho bukomeye atega umutego we kandi n'isezerano rikomeye azasezeranya kwegurira urusengero rwe abantu bo mu gihugu kandi n'ibyishimo byinshi Esawu na Ashikenazi baziyizihiza iruhande rw'umwana w'intama wa Esawu, kandi baziyoberanya nk'abari kugarura inzira za Torah, ariko bazana inzira z'urupfu zanditswe mu gitabo cyabo cy'urupfu rutuka Imana, ndetse na Talimudi yabo. Dore! Inyamaswa yo mu isi ni umwicanyi n'umuyobozi w'ubwigomeke kuri Yahweh mu nzira za Barabasi. Azagambanira abo bose aha amasezerano ye akomeye kandi nta kwicuza ukugambana kwe kuzaba kandi n'urwango rukomeye yarazwe na Se, umwanzi uwo aboshywe n'irari rye mu gukiranirwa ngo abisohoze.

Nyamara uko niko kamere y'igitagangurirwa cyaboheye isi yose mu rwebuya rwacyo. Igitagangurirwa cyicara kigategereza ko umuhigo wacyo ugwa mu rwebuya rwacyo maze kigahita gitangira gukora kugira ngo gifate, kigagaze kandi gihambire umuhigo wacyo kugira ngo kibone uko kigaburira buhoro buhoro ku buzima bw'imbohe zacyo; uko zikomeza kumirwa buhoro buhoro n'ikibi zafashwemo. Kandi nyamara imbaga y'abantu bazaturuka hafi na kure ku ijwi ry'umuhamagaro we; kandi ubutumire bwo kwegurira no kweza urusengero rwe buzumvikana ku isi yose kandi benshi banga Mesiya wabo w'ukuri bazafatwa mu mutego we kandi barimbuke. Nyamara ntibizagaragara nk'umutego kuri abo yaroze, ahubwo nk'ububyutse bwa Mose buyobowe na Sanhedrini yabo kandi butegekwa n'imigenzo n'ubwenge bwa Esawu bikubiye mu gitabo cyabo gituka Imana kidakwiriye. N'itsinda ry'abatambyi b'ibinyoma batari aba Lewi kuko Lewi aboneka byuzuye mu Mutambyi Mukuru wa Melikisedeki, kuko Jyewe, Yeshua HaMashiach ndi Umutambyi wanyu Mukuru iteka ryose mu buryo bwa Melikisedeki, kuko uko niko Yahweh yavuze.

Nyamara nk'uko nigishije ukuri mu gihe cyanjye ubwo nazaga mu banjye nk'Umwana w'Intama w'Imana; ubwo nanzwe n'abene gihugu banjye, nyamara nigishije ukuri uko kundi, ku bw'ibyo, Yewe Isirayeli ntiwitwaza. Nubwo, mu rukundo rwanjye kuri mwe kandi hakurikijwe amasezerano n'ibihango bya kera, nzanywe no kubabwira nanone kugira ngo Yakobo atagwa mu maboko ya Esawu.

Kuko byanditswe mu byanditswe byera ngo: Nuko rero nimubona ikizira cy'umurimbuzi, cyavuzwe n'Umuhanuzi Daniyeli, gihagaze ahera, (usoma, asobanukirwe). Icyo gihe abari i Yudaya, bahungire mu misozi. Uri hejuru y'inzu, ntamanuke, ngo agire icyo akura mu nzu ye: Kandi uri mu murima, ntasubire inyuma ngo atware imyenda ye. Kandi ishyano rizabona abatwite, n'abonsa muri iyo minsi. Ariko musenge kugira ngo guhunga kwanyu kutazaba mu itumba, cyangwa ku isabato: Kuko icyo gihe hazabaho amakuba akomeye, atigeze kubaho uhereye ku kuremwa kw'isi kugeza ubu, oya, kandi ntazigera abaho. Kandi iyo minsi iyadahenurwa, nta muntu n'umwe wari gukira: ariko ku bw'intore, iyo minsi izahenurwa.

Nk'uko byanditswe, niko bizakorwa kandi Barabasi azatega umutego we azaba yahishe neza. Kuko abaguye bashaka kurimbura burundu ubwoko bwa Adamu ku isi. Ku bw'ibyo, bazarwanya abera kandi babaneshe, kandi Esawu hamwe na Ashikenazi bashaka gutega Yakobo, kugira ngo Yakobo agwe ku gikomere cyo mu mutwe bashaka ko kirangiza Yakobo. Mwumve ko iyo bitaba ubuntu n'imbabazi bya Yahweh wiyemeje mu rukundo rwe rw'iteka kuri Isirayeli mu masezerano menshi n'ibihango bya kera gukiza no gucungura Yakobo mu maboko y'abanzi be; mwese mwari kurimbukira mu maboko y'abatagatifu batatu! Ariko nyamara, Yahweh ni uwizerwa iteka ku isezerano ryose yigeze gutanga! Kandi Jyewe, Yeshua HaMashiach ndetse n'uwaguze agakiza kanyu binyuze mu gitambo cyanjye kibabaza ndi uwizerwa kugira ngo nsohoze iteka ryose buri sezerano ry'igihango cyose cyakozwe, kandi by'ukuri buri sezerano rizabaho iteka ryose bityo nta sezerano na rimwe rya Yahweh cyangwa Yeshua HaMashiach rizagwa hasi ridashohojwe ahubwo yose azasohozwa byuzuye, iteka ryose.

Ku bw'ibyo, nzaza gutabara Isirayeli, ndetse n'abakomoka kuri Yakobo bazagandukira ingoma yanjye nka Mesiya wabo mu mugambi w'agakiza w'iteka wa Yahweh uwo Yahweh yatanze ku bw'agakiza ka Yakobo; kuko by'ukuri, by'ukuri, iyo iminsi idahanurwa nta muntu wari gukira. Bityo rero, amakuba akomeye cyane azabaho, ndetse n'amakuba atigeze abaho ku isi mbere, kandi by'ukuri, atazigera abaho ukundi. Dore! Aba ni Umugeni wanjye, kandi bazankurikira aho nzajya hose. Dore! Nzabagira abanyambaraga, kandi nta na kimwe cyigeze kiboneka ku isi gishobora kugereranywa n'ubushobozi nzabambika. Bazayoborwa atekanye kure y'uburakari bwuzuye bw'umujinya w'Imana buri mu nzabya zirindwi; kuko muri izi nzabya harimo ubukana bw'umujinya w'Imana bugomba gusukwa ku ntebe y'inyamaswa no kubabaza ubwami bw'Ikiyoka, kuko igihano cyabo ari gikomeye. Kandi 144,000 bazayobora Yakobo atekanye; bazabakiza ibikomere byabo; bazabatabara kandi babahe ibyiringiro bikomeye byo kwambaza izina ry'Umwami, ndetse kugeza ku iherezo niba bikenewe. Kuko iyo ntagabanya iminsi, nta muntu wari gukira. Kandi niko bazarangiza ibyo nzabayoboramo.
