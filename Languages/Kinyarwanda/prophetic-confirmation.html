28 Ukwakira 2024

Iyemezwa ry'Ubuhanuzi rya J. Leland Earls

Nimwumve amagambo yanjye, bwoko bwanjye; mutege amatwi ibyo mbamenyesha muri iyi saha. Ese sinkuza aho muri n'ubu nk'intumwa ikomeye ngo mbahishurire ibyahishwe mu bihe n'ibisekuruza byinshi? Kuko n'ubu nahishurira abafite amatwi yumva ibyo nateguriye umubiri wanjye muri ibi bihe by'imperuka. Ese mutekereza ko nzabasiga mudafite icyerekezo n'ubumenyi bw'imigambi yanjye kuri mwe muri ibi bihe by'imperuka? Ese mutekereza ko ntabasha gutoranya intumwa zihariye nanyuzamo ubutumwa bwanjye n'umugambi wanjye kuri mwe ku iherezo ry'ibintu byose? Nimwumve amagambo yanjye: Nongere mbivuge, mutege amatwi ibyo mbabwira muri iyi saha. Kuko ubu mfite agatabo mu ntoki zanjye, kandi ndimo kumenyesha ibikubiye muri aka gatabo abagaragu banjye nitoranyirije.

Kandi se kavuga iki? Kavuga gatya. Bwoko bwanjye, ndaza aho muri binyuze mu butumwa bwanjye n'imigambi by'ibihe by'imperuka, bizaba iherezo ry'ibyo nabateguriye byose ndetse no mu bisekuruza n'ibihe byinshi. Kandi nzabageza mu bwuzuzanye bw'ibyo nateguye, ndetse n'ubwuzuzanye bw'ibyo narangirije ku musaraba ubwo nabameniraga amaraso y'ubuzima bwanjye, kugira ngo mbahamirize Isezerano Rihoraho, ndetse n'isezerano ry'ubutungane ku bwoko bwanjye; isezerano rikubiyemo ubwuzuzanye bwose bw'ubwiza bwanjye n'imbaraga ku bateguriwe by'umwihariko kuryakira.

Kuko mbabwira nti bake, yego, abasigaye, bazategurirwa kwakira iri hishurirwa n'umugambi by'ibihe by'imperuka, ibyo ndimo kumenyesha ubu. Kuko rikomeye cyane ku buryo ritakwakirwa keretse n'itsinda ryateguwe, ndetse n'"umukumbi muto," uzakira ubwuzuzanye bw'isezerano. Kuko muzagendera muri iyi minsi y'imperuka mu bwuzuzanye bw'ibyo nabateguriye. Kuko n'ubu ndabaha ihishurirwa ry'ibyari bifatanishijwe ikimenyetso, byemejwe, kandi bigirwa ibiboneka ndetse byiteguye ubwo amaraso y'ubuzima bwanjye yamenekaga i Kaluvari. Ese ntabwo byanditswe ko navuze amagambo ngo, "Birarangiye"? Ni iki cyarangiye? Ubwuzuzanye bw'isezerano ryanjye bwararangiye; ndetse n'icyo gihe bwakozweho byuzuye kugira ngo habeho gucungurwa kwuzuye mu mbaraga zose z'umwanzi, no kwinjira byuzuye mu byo niteguye kandi mbasha gutanga byose, hakubiyemo no gukuraho urupfu. Yego, imbaraga zose zo kunesha imirimo yose y'umwanzi, n'ubwiza bwose bwateguwe ndetse uhereye ku kuremwa kw'isi ku bateguriwe kwakira no kwinjira.

Ariko se ntabwo ubu byanditswe ko bagabanyirije ubushobozi Uwera wa Isirayeli? Bana banjye, NDI Uwera wa Isirayeli! Ariko abantu barangabanyirije. Binangiye imitima yabo ngo bizere ibinyoma bityo bananirwa kwakira byuzuye ibyo nateguye byose nkabishyiraho ikimenyetso, nkabihamya, ku musaraba w'i Kaluvari. Yego, ndababwira ko ibintu byose byari byiteguye icyo gihe. Ariko se ni he nashoboraga kubona ubwoko bwiteguye—bwari bwateguriwe kwizera byuzuye? Yego, nta n'umwe wabonetse, uretse abameze nka Yosuwa na Kalebu. Ufite amatwi, niyumve! Kuko n'aha mbona ko amatwi yanyu aremerewe, kandi sinshobora kubahishurira byuzuye muri iki gihe icyo ibi bisobanura, ariko muzabisobanukirwa iminsi myinshi itarashira.

Ariko noneho ngiye guhishura ibingana n'ibyo mwiteguye kwakira. Kuko agatabo gakubiyemo byinshi by'uyu munsi n'iki gihe. Ariko ngomba kubihishura gake gake, kuko n'ubu benshi muri mwe mumeze nk'impinja zikimara gucuka ku mata kandi ntimwiteguye ibiryo bikomeye. Abandi bazasobanukirwa byinshi mu byo mvuga. Bityo rero reka mbategure kandi nkebere imitima yanyu rwose kugira ngo mwakire ubu butumwa bw'ibihe by'imperuka. Kuko uhawe byinshi, asabwa byinshi. Ese mwamenya ibikubiye muri aka gatabo? Ngaho nimutegure imitima yanyu kwakira. Kuko muzabihishurirwa gake gake uko mwiteguye kwakira.

Kuko n'ubu ndimo ntegura ubwoko buzahabwa rwose igihugu cyateguwe—buzambuka umuhengeri wa Yorodani—buzaseka imbaraga z'ikiyoka—ndetse na wa wundi ukuza kwe kuri kumwe n'ibimenyetso n'ibitangaza by'ibinyoma kandi imikorere ye ikaba imeze nk'amazi asuma ya Yorodani. Nimutekereze kuri ibi, bwoko bwanjye! Ese ntabwo byanditswe ko ikiyoka kizaza nk'umwuzure ukomeye w'urupfu n'irimbuka? Kandi se icyo kiyoka ntikizaza ku bwoko bwanjye n'umwuzure w'ibinyoma n'ibishuko muri uyu munsi?

Ariko ntimuzaneshwa, kuko nzatumwa amazi yigabanya mu gihe mwambuka ku bw'ukuboko kwanjye gukomeye ngo muhabwe igihugu mwateguriwe. Kuko by'ukuri, iki ni igihugu cyiza. Kandi ubwoko bwanjye bwararaririye mu butayu bw'irimbuka ry'umwuka igihe gihagije. Ndababwira nti 'mirongo ine y'ikigeragezo' yararangiye, kandi ku nshuro ya kabiri ubwoko bwanjye buraza mu Gihugu cy'Isezerano. N'ubu, ndimo ndabategura kwambuka mu mbaraga z'umwuka. Nimwumve kandi mutekereze ku byo mbabwira. Kuko agatabo nfashe mu ntoki zanjye gakubiyemo amabanga n'amayobera yose nshaka ko mumenya muri iyi minsi.

Yemwe bwoko bwanjye, ese sinategereje n'amatsiko menshi iyi saha ngo mbahishurire ibyahishwe? Ese iyi si yo saha amateka yose yategereje? Ese ibi si byo byavuzwe n'abahanuzi, nubwo bo ubwabo batabisobanukiwe? Yemwe bwoko bwanjye, ese mwatekereje ku cyubahiro n'ikuzo mwahawe byo kubaho muri iyi saha no kugira uruhare muri uyu murimo w'ibihe by'imperuka? Yemwe, iyaba mwakangukaga mu bitotsi byanyu ngo murebe ubwiza bwateguriwe iyi saha. Kuki muvuga mu mitima yanyu ngo igihe ntikiragera? Kuki mutegereza ntimwinjire n'umutima wanyu wose n'ubugingo bwanyu bwose? Ndababwira nti, UBU NI CYO GIHE CYO KUNSHAKANA UMUTIMA WANYU WOSE, kugira ngo mbavugishe mu cyumba cy'imbere cy'umutima wanyu kandi mbamenyeshe inama yanjye y'iyi saha. Ntimwumve abashaka kubaca intege no kubahuma amaso ku byo mvuga.

Bari he abafite ubutwari bwo KUGENDA hejuru y'amazi y'ingorane n'ibigeragezo bakaza BAGANA AHO NDI MU KWIZERA, bazi ko mbasha kubaramira no kubashyigikira muri iyi saha n'iteka ryose, kugira ngo mugende mutsinze hejuru y'amazi y'amakuba n'imidugararo, azagerageza abantu bose muri uyu munsi? Nimugende, ndabivuga, mutumbiriye kuri njye mudakabakaba, mutareba iburyo cyangwa ibumoso; mutareba ababakikije cyangwa amazi asuma yashaka kubaroha.

Kuko mbabwira ko mutazigera mugera ku muhamagaro n'ikanguka by'ibihe by'imperuka keretse mwemeye mu kwizera kuva mu bwato bw'iraha n'umutekano byanyu by'ubu mukinjira mu MUGEZI W'UMUGAMBI WANJYE W'IMANA, mutareka ngo imiraba y'ugushidikanya n'ubwoba ibaneshe, cyangwa ngo mureke abahitamo kuguma mu bwato bababuze. Ariko NIZE BWOKO BWANJYE, kuko nzaba mfite abatinyuka kugenda hejuru y'amazi y'ibitarigeze bikorwa mbere, bakansanganira uko nza aho muri mu mbaraga n'ubwiza muri uyu munsi.

Ese ntibabonye ko wari umubiri ugenda hejuru y'amazi atari umwuka nk'uko babitekerezaga? Kandi se Petero, mu mubiri we, ntiyagenze hejuru y'amazi? Nuko rero, bwoko bwanjye, si umwuka gusa ahubwo ni UMUBIRI W'INYAMA uzazanwa aho ndi muri uyu munsi ngo ukore ibitangaza hejuru y'amazi y'urujijo n'umubabaro by'abantu. Ese ntimuri UMUBIRI wa Kristo; ese ntimuri inyama zanjye n'amagufa yanjye? Uko ni ko muzaba umwe nanjye muri uyu munsi; uko ni ko tuzahuzwa HEJURU y'amazi ngo tujye mbere muri uyu munsi, ni ko Uwiteka avuga. Kandi uko ni ko muzaba mu minsi iri imbere, ni ko Uwiteka avuga, UMUBIRI W'ABANESHAYO—abanesheje kwanduzwa kw'amarangamutima kose kw'ibyobo by'amazi by'umwanzi kandi bakaba bagenda batsinze mu mbaraga zinesha.

Bwoko bwanjye, nimwumve amagambo yanjye! Ese aside zo mu gifu si ishusho y'imikorere yanjye hagati yanyu? Ese mushobora gukura intungamubiri mu bitari umutsima no mu bidahaza? Ndababwira nti, imikorere y'Umwuka Wanjye ni ngombwa kuri mwe kugira ngo mwakire ibitanga ubuzima. Ese ntabwo mbasha gufata iby'ingenzi mu Ijambo ryanjye ngatuma byinjira mu miterere yanyu kugira ngo mwakire izo ntungamubiri mukeneye? Ariko kuki mugaburirwa ibyo, bitari ijambo ryanjye? Kuki mushaka ibyo muzi ko bitazahaza imiterere yanyu y'imbere?

Ndababwira nti, nimutanshaka byimazeyo ngo mureke Umwuka wanjye uhe ubuzima ibyo mushyira mu mubiri wanyu, muzisanga mu mimerere y'umwuka isa n'ivugwa hano. Imikorere y'umwuka wanjye ni ngombwa. Naho ubundi, murafata gusa ibyapfuye kandi bitazagira icyo bikora ngo bizanire ubuzima bw'umwuka imiterere yanyu y'imbere. Nuko rero, mwitondere ibyo musoma, kuko ari ukuri ku ishusho y'ibiri kuba ku bwoko bwanjye uyu munsi. Kuko bidashoboka, mu mimerere y'ubu y'isi y'itorero, ko nkora binyuze mu Mwuka wanjye ku buryo Ijambo ryanjye rizatanga ubuzima n'ubuzima bwiza no gucungurwa ku bakeneye bose. Nuko rero, nongere mbivuge, nimunshake kandi mureke mbahe ibikenewe kugira ngo mubeho ubuzima bw'umwuka bufite intego kandi bwuzuye, ni ko Umwami Yesu Kristo avuga.

Ubutumwa bw'Ubuhanuzi Bwatanzwe na J. Leland Earls 1923-1991, mu gihe runaka mu isonga ry'Intambara y'Ubutita, yabaye ahagana mu 1962. Bwakuwe ku rubuga rukurikira: Intumwa Ikomeye n'Agatabo


Ibitecyerezo bya William Brooks:

Inshuro ebyiri zose, navugishije ushinzwe uru rubuga ucunga inyandiko za Bwana Earls ariko nta gisubizo, bityo kuri iyi ngingo nshobora gusa kwemeza ko atazongera gusubiza. Uretse ibyo, nta kindi mfite cyo kuvuga ku bantu bose bafashe inshingano zo gucunga inyandiko ze, kandi ibi rwose si ukugaya imirimo y'ubuhanuzi Bwana Earls yagezeho kandi amagambo ye akaba agihagaze n'uyu munsi. Uwiteka ni we mucamanza wacu twese, bityo ni kuri Kristo duhagarara cyangwa tugwa, kandi Kristo abasha kuduhagarika!

Gusa, kugira ngo mbisobanure neza, urubuga natanzeho umurongo rufite inyigisho n'imyemerere myinshi ntasangiye nabo cyangwa ngo nshyigikire, ndetse sinemeranya na bimwe mu byanditswe nk'uko Bibiliya ibivuga. Byinshi muri ibi bintu mvuga ntibyanditswe na Bwana Earls, ariko uko uwabyanditse yaba ari we wese, ujye ugira umwete wo kwishyira Imana nk'ushimwa, umukozi udakwiriye kugira ipfunwe, ukwiriranya neza ijambo ry'ukuri.

Kandi ndafata uyu mwanya ngo nongere mbivuge. Icyo umuntu wese yavuga igihe avuga ko avugira Imana, mu buhanuzi cyangwa ukundi, ujye ujyana ayo magambo mu byanditswe kandi cyane cyane ku Mwami ngo yemeze kandi atange gusobanukirwa. Nta kamaro ko kumbaza ukuri kw'umuhanuzi uyu n'uyu. Sinzasubiza kuko sinzafatwa mu mutego wo kuvuga nabi umuhanuzi w'ukuri w'Imana uwo ari we wese, ndetse nta n'ubwo mfite igihe cyo gukuraho ibitekerezo byanjye ngo nemeze imirimo y'undi muntu. Turi ba nde bo gucira urubanza umugaragu w'undi muntu? Ku shebuja wabo ni ho bahagarara cyangwa bagwa, bityo dukorera Umwami wacu Yesu Kristo. Nuko rero, Uwiteka ni we wemeza cyangwa ahakana ukuri kw'umuntu wese uvuga ko avuga mu izina ry'Uwiteka. Niba Uwiteka ahisemo kuvugira mu muntu, ndabizeza ko bafitanye umubano ukomeye na Kristo, kandi amuziho byinshi kuruta ibyo bo ubwabo biyiziho. Mubijyane ku Mwami!

Wenda, ushobora kuvuga uti, William, icyo ushaka kuvuga muri ibi byose ni iki? Icyo nasubiza ni uko Umwami Yesu Kristo yaduteguriye inzira yo kwakira Agatabo a.k.a. Ubutumwa Bwiza Bw'Iteka, atari mu byanditswe gusa, ari byo by'ingenzi cyane, ahubwo no mu myaka nka 60 ishize mu kanwa ka J. Leland Earls. Ku bwanjye, nshimira ubudahemuka bwe, kandi nshimira cyane Kristo Yesu, Umwami wacu wazutse, kuba yarakoreye muri uyu mugabo ngo amuhe aya magambo aduha umugisha mu mbaraga nyinshi uyu munsi. Nshimira Umwami Yesu Kristo kuba yaranhishuriye, ngo mbasangize mwese, Agatabo k'Ibyahishuwe Igice cya 10/Ubutumwa Bwiza Bw'Iteka bw'Ibyahishuwe Igice cya 14. Shalom Shalom!
