Dore! Amahitamo ni Ayanyu | Inyamaswa Zihishuwe | Amagambo y'Ubuhanuzi Aratangajwe

Tegera amatwi amagambo y'umukiza wanyu, Umwami n'Umugenga bana banjye kandi mutege amatwi Amagambo y'Umwami wanyu, ari we Yesu Kristo ukiranuka, kuko ndi umwana wo gukiranuka kandi nkazamuka mu bwiza bwanjye, kandi mwe mwakuze nk'inyana zo mu kiraro mu mahoro n'umutekano ugereranyije kandi nabarereye intego yo mu ijuru igiye guhishurwa byuzuye muri mwe, abizerwa banjye n'abatoranyijwe. Dore! Ndi ugerageza imitima y'abantu kugira ngo mumenye neza niba koko muri mu kwizera, cyangwa niba mwarayobejwe mugatwarwa n'ibyaha byanyu hamwe n'ubuyobe bukomeye bw'iki gihe bwigaruriye Isi.

Kuko ndi uwo Yahweh yasangiye intebe ye y'ubwami, ari we Kristo Yesu wazutse kandi wahawe ububasha, kandi ndi Umwami w'abami n'Umutware w'abatware kandi dore Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda ije mu bicu kwakira abizerwa banjye kuri njye kugira ngo bajyanwe mu ijuru mu bukwe bw'Umwana w'Intama, kuko Umugeni wanjye yiteguye. Kandi amatara ye yuzuye, kandi yakase intambi zayo, kandi ategereje kugaragara kwanjye nk'isugi y'umunyabwenge, ndetse n'abo nejeje byuzuye kandi bakaba badafite inenge imbere y'intebe y'Imana. Ndetse n'abatatwawe mu buhendanyi na Baraba, kuko Baraba azana kwigomeka kw'urugomo, intambara n'amakuba, kuko ubu ari umutwe umwe mu mitwe myinshi ikorera hamwe, ariko azabyuka ari Mesiya w'ikinyoma, kandi yatoranyijwe n'abantu bo mu gihugu ngo ategeke isinagogi ya Satani. Kuko ari umuragwa w'uwo ba se batoranyije, kandi barimo kwitegura kumwakira nk'Umwami wabo.

Ariko wowe, yewe Torero rya Lawodikiya, noneho haje ibihe byawe byo kurimbuka n'urubanza. Kuko mwihitiyemo undi mukiza nk'Umwami wanyu, ndetse n'uwo kuza kwe kubanzirizwa n'ibimenyetso n'ibitangaza by'ibinyoma kandi ubu wabayoboye mu bupfumu n'uburozi. Ndi Yeshua HaMashiach kandi nabaguze amaraso yanjye bwite y'impongano, atagira icyaha, kandi ndi uwo kubakiza, no kubarema bushya mu mwuka wanjye, kuko nahinduwe umwuka utanga ubuzima kuko ari ko Yahweh yategetse ububasha bwose ku Mwana we, ndetse n'uwo ari we mperuka y'umwana w'intama w'Imana hamwe n'Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda! Kandi mu gukiranuka nzaca urubanza nkurwane kuko umwana w'intama w'Imana n'Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda byuzurijwe muri njye, Kristo Yesu, ndetse Yeshua HaMashiach kuko ari ko ndi!

Nzarangiza agakiza kanyu, yewe Torero rya Lawodikiya, ariko ntimwiteguye kwinjira mu Bwami bwanjye muri iki gihe kuko mwatannye mu ntego yanyu imwe muri Kristo! Nzaberereka ikosa ryanyu, kandi mufite rwose amahirwe yo kwihana ibyaha byose, ariko ntimugifite igihe na gito cyo kujya impaka n'ijwi ry'Umwami wanyu, kuko iki cyaha gikomeye ari kimwe mu byinshi Satani yakoresheje kugira ngo ababohe ntimuze kuri njye byuzuye mu kwihana kugira ngo mbeze byuzuye mu maraso yanjye y'impongano mbakureho ibyaha byanyu.

Dore! Ibihe by'Abanyamahanga birarangiye kandi igiti cy'umunzenze cy'ishyamba cyatewe muri bo, kandi kigasangira na bo umuzi n'umubyibuho by'igiti cy'Umunzenze, ubu kigiye gutemwa. Kuko mwirengagije Amagambo ya Kristo Yesu, kuko nababwiye nkereranya nti ntimwirarire ku mashami yaguye mu kutizera: ariko niba mwirasiye, ntimutwaye umuzi, ahubwo umuzi ni wo ubatwaye. Ndi Umuzi wa Yese, kandi ndi uhagaze nk'urumuri rw'ibyiringiro ibihe byose, ndetse n'uzana agakiza n'inzira za Yahweh ku isi kandi nzategeka umurage wanjye ntegekera ku ntebe ya Dawidi mu gukiranuka kandi naguze isi mu maraso yanjye atagira icyaha kandi nzatwara ku ngufu ibyo ari ibyanjye mu maboko y'abigarurira, ndetse n'abaguye.

Ndi uwahaye isi ubuntu n'imbabazi, kuko igihe cyose cy'ubuntu nagihawe na Yahweh kugira ngo Abanyamahanga bakizwe. Kuko imigambi ya Yahweh muri Isirayeli yari buri gihe y'uko Abanyamahanga bahabwa umugisha kandi bakabona agakiza binyuze mu kumvira kwa Isirayeli. Ariko uko igihe cyahitaga babaye abibone, n'abirasi, kandi batemwe mu giti cy'umunzenze kavukire mu kutizera. Ariko mwe, Torero ryanjye rya Lawodikiya, ndetse namwe muri urugero rw'ubu bwibone, kutizera, n'ubuhumyi byagaragajwe n'amashami kavukire yatemwe mu kutizera, kandi ubu muzatemwa mu giti cy'umunzenze kavukire. Kandi ubu ngiye gusoza umurimo wanjye ku giti cy'umunzenze kavukire, ndetse Isirayeli mwene wacu n'abantu banjye, ndetse n'abo natoranyije bose ngo banyemere nk'Umwami wabo kugira ngo mbareme bushya nk'umuntu umwe mushya kandi kugira ngo ibanga ry'Imana rirangire, ndetse n'iherezo ry'imirimo yanjye y'impongano y'agakiza.

Kandi mwe, Torero ryanjye rya Lawodikiya, mwasize urukundo rwanyu rwa mbere kandi muri mu maboko y'umushukanyi mwarahiye kubera indahemuka. Mwarihitiyemo Baraba mushya ndetse nk'Umwami wanyu kandi mwanze Yesu w'i Nazareti nk'Umwami wanyu kandi muri mu maboko y'undi mukiza w'ikinyoma. Ndetse n'uwiyita umukiza wa Babuloni y'ibanga, uvuga kandi agakora nk'umwe muri mwe, ariko akavuga nk'ikiyoka. Kuko Babuloni y'ibanga ibaho mu iraha no mu butunzi bwinshi bw'iby'isi, ibinezeza n'ubukire.

Kandi Babuloni y'ibanga yasinyeje isi yose umwanda w'ubusambanyi bwayo buri mu gikombe cyayo cya zahabu, ariko uko ni ko inzira z'abaguye ziri, batanga umwanda mubi cyane, kandi muwunywa mubishaka kandi munezerewe cyane kuko mwahumwe amaso n'igikombe cya zahabu ntimubone umwanda ukabije munywa mu buryo bwanduye. Kuko n'Itorero ryanjye rya Lawodikiya ritagiteranira ku byanditswe byanjye byera ahubwo mu makoraniro ya Baraba, uwo mwiringira. Kandi bityo muhitamo umukiza mushya kuko mukunda isi n'ibintu byo mu isi kandi ntimushobora kwiyumvisha igitekerezo cy'uko mwabura ibyokurya byanyu by'agaciro, keretse nzabaka ibinezeza byose mu burakari bwanjye bwegereje, kandi n'ubu igihombo cyanyu kiriyongera!

Kuko muri impumyi kandi mwarayobye mu bwibone bwanyu bw'uko Yahweh yatanga undi mukiza utari njye, Yesu w'i Nazareti, kuko ndi urubyaro rwasezeranyijwe rw'umugore kandi Dani ni ikibwana cy'intare nta Mesiya uzamukomokaho. Munyumve kandi munyumve neza, yewe torero rya Lawodikiya, kuko ubu ari ukwihana kwanyu mu byaha by'imitima yanyu cyangwa ubu ni igihe nzabasigira umuriro w'utunganya kandi abambaza izina ry'Umwami bose kugeza ku iherezo bazakizwa kandi benshi batuye izina ryanjye batizera bazagwa mu kurimbuka, batesheje agaciro amahirwe yabo ku ishusho yo kubaha Imana ariko bahakana imbaraga zayo: Banya Lawodikiya banjye, ndi imbaraga zayo kandi muri njye Yahweh yashyize ububasha bwose, kandi mwaranyihakanye, Umwami n'umukiza wanyu, kandi nzabaruka mbakure mu kanwa kanjye keretse mwihannye ubu!

Mu gihe cyanjye cy'ubuntu, agakiza kakwirakwiriye mu mahanga, kandi ishyanga ku rindi ryasangiraga kandi rikabona ku muzi w'igiti cy'umunzenze kavukire. Kandi ubu, amahanga ntazongera kuba urumuri rw'agakiza kandi igihe cyabo mu Mwana cyazanywe no kwanga mwene wacu n'abantu banjye kuko basakuje mu kutizera bati tubohurire Baraba! Ariko uko amabanga ya Yahweh yahishuwe n'intumwa zanjye zera n'abahanuzi, nakumiriye Baraba sinamurekura byuzuye, ahubwo nemereye gusa ibanga ryo gukiranirwa gukomeza. Kandi ubu ibihe byageze aho nzarekura Baraba byuzuye kuko unkumira akuweho kandi uko abizerwa banjye, abahamagawe, n'abatoranyijwe bajyanwa mu ijuru aho nateguye ubuturo bwabo buhoraho, kandi bazambikwa imyenda yera itagira inenge idashobora kwanduzwa cyangwa ngo igire ikizinga.

Ese mwahumye amaso ku isi yanyu bigeze aho, kandi mupfa amatwi ku buryo mutacyumva ijwi ryanjye yewe Torero rya Lawodikiya? Mwizeye ibinyoma byazanywe n'ibanga ryo gukiranirwa, kandi mwemereye umusemburo w'uburozi muri Bibiliya zanyu zangiritse byazanye kwangirika kwuzuye mu Matorero yanyu byanduje imitima yanyu n'imigani mu kutizera kuyobya. Mwemereye ibinyoma by'abapfumu b'ibihe kubaroga n'inyandiko z'ibinyoma n'amagambo y'ibinyoma. Ntimugisobanukirwa ibyanditswe, kandi mwiyongereyeho amakuba akomeye azabazaho gitungura. Buri Jambo ry'Imana riraboneye kandi rigenewe kumvira k'ukwizera no kubaka abaryakira. Musobanukirwe Amagambo y'Amagambo yanjye kandi musobanukirwe ibihe mubayemo. Kuko nategetse Yohana Uhishurirwa kwandika: Kandi nabonye inyamaswa izamuka iva mu nyanja, ifite imitwe irindwi, n'amahembe icumi, kandi ku mahembe yayo hari amakamba icumi, n'amazina yo gutuka Imana ku mitwe yayo.

Kandi nanone Yohana Uhishurirwa yaranditse: Nabonye umugore yicaye ku nyamaswa y'ibara ry'umuhengeri, yuzuye amazina yo gutuka Imana, ifite imitwe irindwi, n'amahembe icumi. Kandi uwo mugore yari yambaye imyenda y'umuhengeri n'itukura, kandi atatsweho zahabu, n'amabuye y'agaciro n'amasaro, afite igikombe cya zahabu mu ntoki ze, cyuzuye ibizira n'umwanda w'ubusambanyi bwe.

Kandi nanone: Kandi amahembe icumi wabonye kuri ya nyamaswa, azanga maraya, kandi azamuhindura umusaka, n'uwambaye ubusa, kandi azarya inyama ze, amutwike n'umuriro. Kuko Imana yashyize mu mitima yabo gukora ibyo ishaka, no guhuza inama, no guha inyamaswa ubwami bwabo, kugeza aho amagambo y'Imana azasohorera. Kandi uwo mugore, uwo wabonye, ni wa Murwa mukuru utegeka abami b'isi.

Bana banjye, musobanukirwe ko inyamaswa ishobora kwerekeza ku bantu ndetse no ku bwami, mwige kugira ngo mumenye. Inyamaswa itwaye maraya izarimbura maraya kandi ntizongera kugandukira maraya, kuko yategetse abami b'isi mu busambanyi bwayo. Bityo inyamaswa izazamuka iva mu nyanja izabyara Mahdi wabo bategereje, ariko izarimbura rwose Babuloni y'Ibanga, kuko Imana yagabije maraya ukomeye mu maboko y'abanzi be basanzwe bamwanga urunuka.

Mbere y'uko inyamaswa iva mu nyanja n'inyamaswa iva mu isi bizamuka byuzuye mu Bwami bw'ikiyoka, hari abategetsi benshi bunze ubumwe mu ntego yo guha ikiyoka imbaraga zabo n'ubutegetsi bwabo, kuko Imana yabishyize mu mitima yabo ngo babikore. Kandi bazarimbura Babuloni y'ibanga. Ese mutekereza ko mumenye neza ibyanditswe byanjye byera kuko mwizeye abanyatewolojiya banyu b'abanyabinyoma? Musobanukirwe kandi mwumve Amagambo y'Umwami wanyu!

Nanone, nategetse Yohana Uhishurirwa kwandika: Kandi inyamaswa nabonye, yasaga n'ingwe, n'amaguru yayo ameze nk'ay'idubu, n'akanwa kayo nk'akanwa k'intare: kandi ikiyoka kiyiha imbaraga zacyo n'intebe yacyo y'ubwami, n'ubutware bukomeye. Kandi nabonye umwe mu mitwe yayo usa n'uwakomeretse bikabije kugeza gupfa, ariko uruguma rwayo rwica rurakira, nuko isi yose itangazwa n'iyo nyamaswa iyikurikira.

Bana banjye, amagambo ya Yahweh afite ubujyakuzimu bwinshi, nta muntu numwe ushobora kuyamenya yose. Inyamaswa ivugwa ni ubwami buzazamuka buvuye mu irimbuka rya maraya, kandi mu gahunda gashaje hazazamuka gahunda nshya! Ihagarariye inyamaswa yo mu nyanja, kuko azazamuka nka Mahdi wabo, kandi ihagarariye imiterere y'ubwami azategekanya inyanja. Kuki mutubaha amagambo y'Ijambo ryanjye? Ntivuga ko inyamaswa yo mu nyanja ubwayo yakomeretse, kuko ni uwuhe muntu ufite imitwe irindwi mu by'ukuri? Ariko umwe mu mitwe yayo usa n'uwakomeretse bikabije kugeza gupfa. Yemwe bapfu, n'abatinda mu mitima kwizera ibyo Abahanuzi bavuze byose! USA N'UWAKOMERETSE kugeza gupfa. Amagambo "usa n'u-" yagombye kubahagarika, ariko ntimukizera n'amagambo yanjye mato, kandi ndi Ijambo ryabaye umubiri. Ese ntibigaragara kuri mwe, USA N'U-, ni imvugo y'igihe kirekire? Ese ntibigaragara kuri mwe ko "usa" ari imvugo y'icyifuzo? Ese ntibigaragara kuri mwe ko ari ukugabanya kwa, nk'aho byari bimeze bityo?

Ariko oya, mwizera ibinyoma by'ikiyoka, kuko Baraba avuga nk'ikiyoka, ariko si Baraba, ahubwo ni inyamaswa iva mu isi. Kandi bityo ubu isi yose izatangazwa n'inyamaswa kubera ibimenyetso byayo n'ibitangaza by'ibinyoma, kandi izakora ibindi bimenyetso n'ibitangaza by'ibinyoma byinshi, kandi ntiyazamutse byuzuye kuko maraya ataratwikwa n'umuriro, ariko ubu ahishuwe mu gihe cye. Avuga nk'ikiyoka, ati "Imana yonyine niyo yamukijije uruguma rwo ku mutwe" kandi iki ni ikinyoma gituka Imana. Yahweh, Imana ikomeye ihoraho, yantumye Yesu w'i Nazareti nk'umukiza w'isi. Njye na Data turi umwe mu bumwe n'intego kandi Yahweh ntiyakijije Baraba kandi nanjye, Kristo Yesu, sinamukijije. Mwarashutswe kandi mufatwa mu mutego w'ubushukanyi bukomeye, kandi ndababwira nti mwihane mugaruke ku rukundo rwanyu rwa mbere kandi mubikore ubu, kuko nta gihe gihari!

Kuko ndi ugerageza imitima, kandi ndi ugerageza abo mpamagara bose ku gakiza, kuko neza bose mu muriro w'utunganya! Dore! Ndi uwicara nk'utunganya kandi weza ifeza: kandi neza abana ba Yahweh, nkabasukura nka zahabu n'ifeza, kugira ngo batambire Umwami ituro mu gukiranuka. Ese ntimusobanukirwa ko nzabagira abatambyi n'abami imbere ya Data? Nubwo impano yanjye y'umwuka wera, ituma muvuka ubwa kabiri ku mbuto zidashira ari ntamakemwa kandi idakeneye gutunganywa, mukomoka kuri Adamu mu maraso kandi mugomba kwemera byuzuye ko umurimo wanjye ukorwa mu buzima bwanyu, keretse niba imbuto yanjye yaraguye mu butaka bw'amabuye.

Ubu haje ibihe by'urubanza kandi ibihe by'ubuntu birarangiye. Nubwo benshi bazakoba amagambo yanjye bagatera umugongo mu kutizera, ibizavamo byamaze kumenywa na Yahweh. Kuko Yahweh buri gihe yereka abantu inzira zabo zangiritse kandi agacyaha abantu ibyaha, azi mu rukundo rwe n'ubwenge bwe buhoraho ko azakiza bamwe. Kandi nk'uko Yahweh yinangiye umutima wa Farawo, ni ko azanangira imitima ya benshi bumva ariko ntibatege amatwi. Yahweh azagerageza umuntu inshuro nyinshi. Kandi bamwe bazamera nka Farawo kandi bazanangira imitima yabo kurushaho mu kutizera ariko bamwe bazava mu nzira za Farawo bazamuke mu buzima buhoraho muri Kristo Yesu, ariko binyuze mu muriro w'utunganya.

Musobanukirwe ko abahitamo Baraba bazigira abafatanyacyaha mu byaha bya Baraba, kandi abanga kwambaza izina ryanjye kugeza ku iherezo bose bazacirwaho iteka kuko bishimiye gukiranirwa. Kuko umukiza wanyu mushya atari umukiza ahubwo ni inyamaswa y'inkazi. Kandi gahunda ye n'ubwami bwe bigizwe n'abagirira nabi abana. Ese ntimusobanukirwa inzira z'Ikiyoka? Kuko abakurikira inzira z'ikiyoka bahiga abana. Batamba abana kuri Moloki, bashakisha isoko yabo y'ubusore mu maraso y'abana batanze ishavu, basambanya abana ku bw'irari ryabo rirwaye, kandi bakora ibyo gukubita urusoro mu nda kugira ngo bice abana bato mbere y'uko bavuka, kandi babayoboye mu ibanga mu nzira zabo, kuko uko ni ko inzira z'abayobozi ba Babuloni y'Ibanga ziri ndetse n'ubwami bw'ikiyoka. Kandi ubu haje urubanza rw'Imana kuri ibi bintu n'ibindi byinshi. Kuko iyo mutaba mwarabonye ibi bintu ntimuba mufite icyaha, ariko ubu murabona kandi icyaha cyanyu kigumyeho.

Ese ntimwasomye? Uku ni ko Umwami avuga, Ijwi ryumvikanye mu ijuru, umuborogo n'ukurira gusharira. Rasheli, aririra abana be, yanze guhozezwa ku bw'abana be, kuko batakiriho. Kuko mwananiwe inshingano mwahawe, kandi mwahawe amabanga yanjye kandi mwahagaze nk'urumuri rw'agakiza n'ikimenyetso cy'ibyiringiro. Ariko ubu mwifatanyije n'abo nabatumyeho ngo muhangane nabo. Kandi bityo ubu haje ibihe nzabasigira ejo hazaza hanyu.

Munyumve kandi munyumve neza mwese mwanga ijwi rya Yeshua HaMashiach, ndetse n'ijwi rya Kristo Yesu: ntimufite igihe! Ndabagira inama yo kunyumva mu rukundo rwanjye kuri mwe bana banjye kandi mwihane ubu! Kuko umuriro w'utunganya ugiye kubagwaho byuzuye, kandi uzeza igihugu ubwami bwose bw'ikiyoka kandi isi ntizongera kugandukira inzira za Satani ukundi kandi ubwami bwe ntibuzongera kuzamuka ukundi, kuko ndi Umwami w'abami n'Umutware w'abatware, kandi nzarimbura rwose abaguye. Abanga kwihana bose bazagira uruhare rwabo mu nyanja y'umuriro kandi bazasangira igihano cya Satani n'abamarayika be muri iyo nyanja nini y'amazuku yaka. Kandi, abihana bose bava mu cyaha bakambaza izina ryanjye kugeza ku iherezo bazakizwa kandi bahabwe ingororano ikomeye ihoraho muri njye.

Dore! Ubu ni igihe cyo gukoranya abizerwa, kandi ubu nzasigira abanga kuri Baraba, kuko bakoze amahitamo yabo.

Kandi benshi muri Babuloni y'Ibanga, ndetse n'abemera ibinyoma bye bagakurikira amagambo ye meza n'imvugo ze nziza batwawe kandi bagushijwe mu mutego w'amahoro y'ikinyoma aho bibwira ko ubuzima bwabo bw'iraha n'ibyaha buzakomeza aho uyu munsi umeze nk'ejo kandi ejo hazaba hameze nk'uyu munsi Kandi benshi bazavuza induru, baboroge, kandi baririre ko ibyiringiro byabo ubu bibakuweho Kandi bazarwanya benegihugu babo kugeza gupfa kandi icyo gihe cyose igitero cyabo gitunguranye kizaba cyarekuwe byuzuye, ndetse n'igihe umukumirizi wanjye akuwe mu isi.
