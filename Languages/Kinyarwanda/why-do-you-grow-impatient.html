Ibisharira N'ibiryohereye
5 Nyakanga 2024

Kuki Mutakaza Kwihangana?

Nimuze, mwunamishe imitima yanyu n'amatwi yanyu mwumve Amagambo y'Umwami wanyu, Itorero ryanjye ndetse n'Umugeni wanjye. Kuki mukuweho mu buryo butunguranye ku byiringiro byanyu byo kuboneka kwanjye, nk'aho ntazaza? Nk'aho amagambo ya Yahweh ashobora guhinduka? Ese Imana ikomeye ihoraho, Yahweh, yibagiwe amasezerano yayo? Ese nibagiwe amagambo yanjye n'amasezerano yanjye? Ni ukuri, ni ukuri, ndavuga nti oya! Kandi abashyira ubupfu kuri Yahweh, Imana ikomeye ihoraho, no kuri njye, Yeshua HaMashiach, Umwami Ukomeye w'abami n'Umwami w'abami, bakwiye kwihana ako kanya. Musabe imbabazi bana banjye kandi mukomeze kwitegura kuko nzaboneka gitungura, kandi muzatangazwa n'ukuntu igikorwa gikomeye gikurikira mu mugambi wa Yahweh wo gucungura abantu kizasohora vuba, kuko kizaba gitunguranye kandi mu buryo butari bwitezwe kuko ari ko byatangajwe. Kuko bizaba ko nzazura abapfiriye muri Kristo mbere naho mwebwe abazima basigaye muzatwarwa gusanganira njye mu bicu, kandi bizaba mu kanya nk'ako guhumbya.

Kuki mutakaza kwihangana? Kuki bamwe muri mwe bigira nk'aho ibintu byasubiye uko byahoze? Biragaragara ko nta gusubira mu nzira za kera, kuko isi iriho ubu izahura n'amakuba akomeye kandi izahinduka iteka ryose, kandi izarushaho guhinduka mu gihe cy'amakuba ya Yakobo kandi isi izaba nk'uko byari mu minsi ya Nowa. Igihe ubuza azakurwaho nta kizabuza imitwe y'abakozi b'ibibi, ndetse n'ingabo z'abaguye bazagaba igitero ku byanjye byose, kuko Yahweh yahaye ikiyoka ububasha bwo kurwanya abera no kubanesha. Benshi bazaza kuri njye binyuze mu kurimbuka kw'imibiri yabo, ndetse n'imibiri bakunda kandi ikibaboshye. Kuko umuntu wese uzambaza izina ry'Umwami azakizwa, kandi umugaragu ntaruta shebuja.

Nubwo natanze ubuhungiro bw'irimbuka rigiye kuza ku bakiranutsi, benshi ntibiteguye ariko bazakizwa kandi bakore umurimo ukomeye mu Bwami bwanjye bugiye kuza, kuko nzategekera ku ntebe y'ubwami ya Dawidi kandi nsangiye intebe y'ubwami ya Data, kandi muzasangira nanjye intebe y'ubwami yanjye mwa bakiranutsi banjye. Kuri ba bandi batiteguye bazababazwa cyane ku bw'izina ryanjye, kandi bazababazwa nk'uko njye, Yesu w'i Nazareti, nababajwe kugira ngo nshikamishe agakiza k'abantu kandi binyuze mu kubambwa kwanjye nasize urugero rwo kumvira kugeza ku gupfa kugira ngo bose barukurikize. Kandi binyuze mu makuba akomeye imbaga nyamwinshi izakizwa kandi ihabwe ingororano ikomeye muri njye Kristo Yesu, ndetse banakorere Yahweh mu rusengero rwe. Ingororano ikomeye itegereje bose bambaza izina ryanjye, kandi abambaza izina ryanjye kugeza ku iherezo bazakizwa, ndetse n'abadakunda amagara yabo kugeza ku gupfa. Kuko iyi si irimo kurimbuka kandi izarimburwa kandi ihanwe byimazeyo mbere y'uko Ubwami bwanjye bw'ubukiranutsi bushyirwaho ku isi.

Nuko rero ntimucike intege Bana banjye, kuko amasezerano yanjye ari yego na amena kandi nzatwara umugeni wanjye mu ijuru nk'uko byasezeranijwe, kandi nta muntu n'umwe watoranyirijwe agakiza k'iteka na Yahweh mbere y'uko imfatiro z'isi zishyirwaho, uzashikuzwa mu maboko yanjye y'iteka kandi agakiza kanyu karashikamye muri njye, Kristo Yesu, gusa mugume mu gukiranuka kandi mugume muri Kristo kugeza ku iherezo. Ibi byahoze ari ibisabwa na Yahweh, ko umuntu asangwa ari umukiranutsi mu nzira ze. Kandi njye, Yeshua HaMashiach, nzanye inzira za Yahweh kandi abanyigiraho biga inzira za Yahweh, kuko ndi umwe mu bumwe n'umugambi na Data. Vuba aha muzaba umwe natwe, mbese nti byanditswe ko mugizwe igice cy'Ubumana bw'iteka, kuko uwo ari wo murage wanyu kandi iyo ni yo ngororano yanjye ku bana b'Imana b'abakiranutsi basanzwe ari abakiranutsi muri njye. Kuko muri njye, Kristo Yesu, ari ho kuzura k'Ubumana kose kuba mu buryo bw'umubiri, kandi muzasangira umurage wanjye ku bw'ubukiranutsi bwanyu, kandi muzasangira nanjye intebe y'ubwami nk'Abami n'Abatambyi kandi Ubwami bwanjye ntibugira iherezo.

Nuko rero mushikame ku bwanjye muri aya masegonda ya nyuma y'ibihe, kandi mwumve ko igihe cyarangiye. Yego, igihe cyarangiye kugira ngo Babuloni y'Amayobera yihane. Ibihe byanjye by'ubuntu n'imbabazi byarangiye, nubwo imbabazi n'ubuntu biri kumwe nanjye iteka. Noneho haje urubanza rukomeye ku isi, no ku mahanga ndetse n'igihugu. Ariko urubanza ntiruzagwa ngo rurimbure abazima bose, ahubwo ruzagwa kandi rwikube. Urubanza ku rundi, ikimenyetso ku kindi, impanda ku yindi, urwabya ku rundi, ishyano ku rindi, n'icyago ku kindi. Kandi nk'igisubizo abantu benshi, benshi, benshi bazaza ku gakiza muri njye, Yesu w'i Nazareti, kuko ndi Umwana w'Intama wa Pasika utunganye w'ibihe byose, kandi nta kindi gitambo gikenewe. Ariko kuri wowe, Mugeni wanjye w'umukiranutsi, ibihe byo gutegereza byarangiye.

Nta gukererwa kuzongera kubaho, kandi nta gutegereza kuzongera, kandi muzagera ku ngororano yanyu yuzuye n'umurage muri njye. Kandi nubwo bamwe bazahura n'igihombo, ijisho ntiryabonye cyangwa ngo ugutwi kumve, cyangwa ngo byinjire mu mutima w'abantu, ibyo Yahweh yateguriye abamukunda. Kandi nuko rero bakunzi banjye b'abakiranutsi, mwagaragaje urukundo rwanyu kuri njye, Kristo Yesu, n'urukundo rwanyu kuri Yahweh kuko mwumviye amategeko yanjye binyuze mu kwizera muri uyu munsi mubi. Nubwo mutambonye n'amaso yanyu, cyangwa ngo murebe ubwiza bw'iteka bwa Yahweh n'amaso yanyu mwagumye kuba abakiranutsi muri njye kugeza ku iherezo, niyo mpamvu hari ingororano ikomeye mubitsemo mu ijuru, nuko ntimucike intege!

Mukomeze kwihangana, Bana banjye, kuko ndimo kwegereza. Ntimwumve abakobanyi babakoba kuko gukoba kwabo vuba cyane kuzahinduka agahinda gakomeye, kandi benshi bakoba bazaceceka kuko ubuzima bwabo buri ku iherezo. Kuko uko niko imanza zigiye kuza ziri. Kuri ba bandi banze amaboko yanjye arambuye kandi bagafata amaraso yanjye y'impongano nk'ikintu kidakwiriye kandi bakibona nk'abadakwiriye ubuzima bw'iteka mu Bwami bwanjye bugiye kuza, ndetse nabo bazazungura umwijima n'urupfu bakunze cyane kandi bifuje kandi benshi bazagwa babishaka mu rupfu rwa kabiri.

Kuko mu rukundo rukomeye rwa Data, ndetse Yahweh Imana ihoraho kuko nta wuhwanye na we, ntazahata n'umwe mu byaremwe bye kumukurikira no kumukunda. Bose bahawe ubushake bwisanzuye kandi bose bazahabwa ingororano y'amahitamo yabo. Kuko uko niko amategeko ya Yahweh ari. Abakunda ibibi, bakunda ibinezeza by'icyaha kandi bagakunda ibibi ibyaha byabo biteza abandi bababazwa barengana ku bw'irari ryabo n'inzira zabo zigoramye bazazungura inzira zabo mbi zigaruke mu bituza byabo kandi niko igihano cyabo kizaba kandi buri cyaha kizishyurwa iteka ryose. Ariko kuri ba bandi bemera amasezerano y'amahoro ya Yahweh, ndetse nanjye Yesu w'i Nazareti, bazazungura ubuzima bw'iteka n'amahoro n'ingororano n'urukundo n'ibyishimo by'iteka mu maso yanjye no mu maso ha Yahweh ubwe, kuko vuba aha ubuturo bwa Yahweh buzaba mu bantu kuko ari ko byanditswe. Kandi ndimo kwegereza kugira ngo mbatware mu cyumba cy'intebe y'ubwami cya Yahweh ubwe, aho mwamaze kwicara, kandi amakamba yanyu arabategereje.

Ntimwizere ibinyoma byabo bana banjye, ndaje kubatwara, gusa mushikame irindi segonda cyangwa abiri, kuko kuboneka kwanjye kuri hafi kandi kuzaba isegonda iryo ari ryo ryose noneho, kandi nzaruta ibyo mwari mwiteze. Mwitegure kandi mwitunganye mu maraso yanjye y'impongano. Mukomeze ubuzima bwanyu bwo kwihana kandi mukomeze kubamba inzira zanyu z'umubiri, kuko atari izanjye kandi nta kintu kibi kizinjira mu Bwami bwanjye kandi nta kintu kibi kizahagarara imbere ya Yahweh. Ndi igitambo cyanyu cy'iteka kandi nabagize abagira inenge cyangwa umunkanyari kandi nta bizinga biri ku bakiranutsi banjye kuko amaraso yanjye akomeza kubatunganya uko mwatura ibyaha byanyu mu kwihana, nubwo umubiri wanyu wangiritse byimazeyo.

Nimuze kuri njye n'umutima ufunguye kandi nzabereka mu rukundo intege nke zanyu kugira ngo mushobore kunyatura ibyaha byanyu, kandi mu kubikora nzabeza kandi nzakomeza kubagira bazima nk'uko muri icyaremwe gishya muri njye, Kristo Yesu. Kandi ni ukuri, ni ukuri bose bashyiriweho ikimenyetso n'umwuka wanjye wera w'isezerano, yaba Umuyahudi cyangwa Umunyamahanga, (yaba imbata cyangwa uwigenga) bazaremwa bundi bushya byuzuye nk'umuntu umwe mushya muri njye Kristo Yesu kandi bazahagarara mu mudendezo wahawe ubwiza iteka ryose! Kuko ndi umwuka utanga ubuzima, kuko ari ko Data yangize. Kandi ndi uwo NDI UKOMEYE yangize iteka ryose. Kandi noneho namwe muzagirwa nkanjye mu mubiri wahawe ubwiza, umubiri waremwe mu ngororano uzagaragaza ibyo mwagezeho mu guhagarara kwanyu ku bwanjye muri uyu munsi mubi. Nta na kimwe mu byo mwakoze kizabura kugororerwa: Kuko Imana atari gukiranirwa, ngo yibagirwe umurimo wanyu, n'imirimo y'urukundo, mweretse Izina ryayo, kuko mwakoreye Abera, kandi mukomeza kubakorera.

Kamere yanyu nyakuri ihishe muri njye, ndetse umukiza wanyu na Mesiya. Iteka ryose muzabana nanjye, kandi muzaba muhuje intekerezo n'umutima nanjye Yeshua HaMashiach ndetse na Yahweh, kuko El Shaddai Ukomeye, ndetse Imana Ishoborabyose izaba byose muri byose kuko imigambi yayo izasohorezwa muri mwe, mwebwe bahamagawe banjye, batoranyijwe kandi b'abakiranutsi. Ndaje kubatwara, Mugeni wanjye, mwiteguye? Ese mufite amavuta mu matara yanyu? Ese mwiogeje byuzuye mu maraso yanjye y'impongano? Ese mwaretse koza ibirenge by'abandi?

Mube maso! Murebe! Mwumve! Mutegereze kuboneka kwanjye kandi muterane inkunga kuko Satani yahisha kuboneka kwanjye iyo ikintu nk'icyo kiza kuba gishoboka. Mundebereho, umutangizi n'usoza kwizera kwanyu, kuko mu kanya nk'ako guhumbya muzahindurwa kandi muremwe nkanjye iteka ryose. Mwumve Amagambo yanjye kandi mwitegure kuboneka kwanjye kandi hejuru ya byose mwumvire biturutse mu Rukundo rwa Yahweh no kuri njye kugira ngo mukomeze Ijambo ryanjye ryera mu mutima utunganye, mumenya ko gucungurwa kwanyu kutakiri hafi ahubwo kwageze.

Ndi umwe mu bumwe n'umugambi na Data, kandi vuba aha muzaba umwe natwe.

Ndi Umwana w'Intama wa Pasika utunganye w'ibihe byose.

Ndimo kwegereza. Ntimwumve abakobanyi babakoba.

Ndaje kubatwara, gusa mushikame irindi segonda cyangwa abiri.

Ndi igitambo cyanyu cy'iteka.

Ndi umwuka utanga ubuzima, kuko ari ko Data yangize.

Ndi uwo NDI UKOMEYE yangize iteka ryose.
