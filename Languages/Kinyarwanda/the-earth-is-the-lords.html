Ubuhamya Bubiri: Itorero
Mu Mirimo ya Yohana Umubatiza
17 Werurwe 2024

Isi ni iy'Uwiteka!

Isi n'ibiyuzuye byose ni iby'Uwiteka: Nubwo umunyagitugu n'umujura yishyiriyeho uburenganzira bwe, ashobora gusa gukora ibyo Data yahaye ukuboko kwe gusohoza mu mugambi we wera wo gucungura muntu. Ntimumumatinye! Agomba gukomeza igihe gito, kuko isaha ye yageze; ariko ntimumumatinye, abakiranutsi banjye, abizerwa banjye; ntimumumatinye! Ese sinabahaye kumunesha? Amakuba ya Yakobo si ay'abizerwa banjye, kandi abangezeho mu bihe byo guhembuka bazashyirwa hejuru cyane mu cyumba cy'intebe y'ubwami y'Imana. Yego, bwoko bwanjye bwizerwa, mubikiwe ingororano ikomeye kubera ubudahemuka bwanyu mwangaragarije mu budahemuka.

Nabagize abami n'abatambyi banjye mu Bwami bwanjye buzaza n'imbere ya Data, nuko rero mukomere, bana banjye, muri iyi minota ya nyuma y'isaha ya nyuma. Ukuboneka kwanjye kuri hafi, kandi gucungurwa kwanyu kuri hafi nk'umwuka wanyu ukurikira, nuko rero muhagarare mumbereye abizerwa, muzi ko ndi imbaraga zanyu n'agakiza kanyu kandi ko mushyiriweho ikimenyetso muri njye, kuko nabaguze amaraso yanjye ubwo nabatangiraga ubuzima bwanjye kugira ngo mwiyunge mu rukundo na Data wo mu ijuru. Ntegerezanyije amatsiko umunsi, kandi Data ategerezanyije amatsiko umunsi, muzahagarara imbere ye mu mibiri mbafitiye mu gucungurwa kwanyu kwuzuye, kandi ntihazabaho ukundi umuvumo ku batoranyijwe banjye.

Yewe muntu, uri inde wowe ushaka kwigarurira umurage wanjye? Ntiwizi ko naguze isi n'ibiyirimo byose n'amaraso yanjye bwite atagira icyaha? Isi n'ibiyuzuye byose ni iby'Uwiteka, kandi gukiranuka kuje ku isi! Nubwo warimbura isi ukayigira ubuturo bwiraye bw'ibizira byawe biteye ishozi, nubwo warahirira kuyoboka umwanzi wanjye w'iteka ugashaka umubabaro wawe bwite kubera inyungu z'igihe gito zitesheje agaciro, ntiwumva ko urwanya ubugingo bwawe bwite uko wibikira umujinya uzaba ku munsi w'umujinya, ubwo nzaha buri muntu wese ibikwiriye imirimo ye.

Yewe muntu w'umupfapfa, wibwira ko imana zawe zizagukiza, kandi nta ngororano zigufitiye, kandi urashutswe kandi ushuka abandi mu buyobe bwawe. Imana zawe z'amabuye ntizishobora kuvuga; ibigirwamana byawe mu mitima yanyu ntibishobora kumva; ntibishobora kugukiza, yewe musenga ibigirwamana! Ibyiza kuri wowe byari kuba kutavuka, kuko umujinya w'Imana ukugumaho kubera inzira zawe z'umutima unangiye. Nubwo akanwa kawe kabaye imva irangaye, karuka ibintu bikomeye birwanya Umwami w'abami, amagambo yawe azakugarukaho, kandi ibyifuzo byawe bibi bizakwihinduka mu gihano cyawe cy'iteka kigutegereje. Iyaba wari warampindukiriye ukemera agakiza kaguha kuva mu mimerere yawe y'ikugwa ya none. Iyaba wari waratuje izina ryanjye gusa mu minwa yawe ibeshya. Ariko oya, wakunze ibinyoma byawe, kandi wakunze ubuyobe bwawe, kandi wagurishije ubugingo bwawe ku kurimbuka kw'iteka kubera ubuvunguka bw'umugati budashobora kugutunga; kuko ndi umugati w'ubugingo! Amakuba yawe azakuzaho gitungura kandi nta muti. Nzatabamura ishyanga ryawe; nzarimbura ubucuruzi bwawe n'ubukungu bwawe; mu kanya gato nzakwambura ubutunzi bwawe; mu by'ukuri, nzakwambura ibyishimo byose mu kanya gato, kandi ubukene bwawe n'umubabaro wawe bizagutsemba. Umujinya w'Umwana w'Intama wegereje, ubwo kwihorera kwanjye Data yampaye kuzashyirwa mu bikorwa, kuko nkwiriye kubumbura ibitabo no gukuraho ibimenyetso.

Wibeshye utekereza ko kwihangana kwanjye no gutinda kwanjye ari intege nke no kwirengagiza. Mwavuze mu mitima yanyu muti: Uwiteka ari he? Ni umugani wa kera gusa. Twize byinshi cyane kuruta inzira zikiza z'Uwiteka; mu bumenyi bwanjye, ndi hejuru, kuko ikoranabuhanga ryanjye rifite imbaraga kuruta igitabo cya kera cy'ibivuguruzanya n'imigani. Mu by'ukuri, benshi muri mwe ntibanemera ko nigeze nza mbere na mbere ngo menne amaraso yanjye acungura ku byaha n'agakiza ka muntu. Ibyiza ni uko mutari mwarigeze muvuka, kuko umujinya mwibikiye ku bugingo bwanyu uzabazaho nta muti, kandi muzamenya neza ibyaha byanyu, kandi umubabaro wanyu ukabije no kwirara bizaba uwo mwashakanye. Muzarya umwanda wanyu bwite, kandi inzira zanyu zanduye zizahorana namwe, uko muzatabwa mu mbuto z'umujinya mu bikorwa byanyu bibi mwakoze mu bubi bukomeye murwanya izina ryanjye. Nimumve imbere; sinabamenye!

Ni nde muri mwe uvuga ibinyoma mu izina ryanjye? Muvuga ko munzi, ko mumpagarariye; bamwe muri mwe banatekereza ko mushobora kuntegeka gukora ibyo mushaka no kwizanira inyungu z'iby'umubiri binyuze mu mirimo yanyu myiza, itari ikindi uretse ubushwambagara bwanduye. Mushobora mute kumvugira kandi mutanzi? Mushobora mute kumvugira kandi ntabahaye amagambo? Mushobora mute kumvugira ku bwoko bwanjye kandi ntabatumye? Oya, mwaritunye, mucuruza ibicuruzwa byanyu byangiritse kubera inyungu zitesheje agaciro z'umururumba no kugira ngo abantu babaramye mwifuza cyane. Wa mupfapfa we! Mve imbere, kuko ntabwo nigeze nkumenya!

Mwa bahanuzi b'ibinyoma mwe, mumbwira ko ndi Umwami mu izina gusa; mwa bahanuzi b'ibinyoma mwe, muvuga ibinyoma bikomeye murwanya ubugingo bwanyu; muzarimbuka rwose, kandi nzabaruka mbakure mu kanwa kanjye. Nuko rero, mwihane! Mungarukire nanjye nzabagarukira. Nimuve mu bigirwamana byanyu kandi muve mu madini yanyu yavumwe maze muze aho ndi mungire Umwami wanyu! Iyaba mwemeraga agakiza kanjye; nyamara mukomeza mu byaha byanyu kandi mukisobanura ku byaha byanyu mu gihe musubiramo Ijambo ryanjye ryera mu mutima ubusa, umutima wuzuye ubusambanyi n'ubusihanyi uko mwirukira ku bigirwamana byanyu. Ni nde uzabakiza umujinya wanjye ubwo mwayobeje ubwoko bwanjye kubera inyungu zanyu zitesheje agaciro? Iyo mungira Umwami nk'uko iminwa yanyu ibeshya yabivuze, mba narabakijije ibyaha byanyu nkabagira bazima muri njye. Mba naravuye ibikomere umubi abatera. Mba narabahaye inzira n'intego nkabohereza ku isi mufite iminwa yuzuye imigisha n'ukuri uko mwari kuyobora ubwoko bwanjye kuri njye.

Ntimutekereze ko nkeneye ubufasha bwanyu, kuko ndi Umwami w'ukuri w'ijuru n'isi, kandi ndi Umwami w'abami n'Umutware w'abatware. Abo natoranyije mu murimo bahawe icyubahiro gikomeye cyo kumvugira no kwita ku mukumbi wanjye, icyubahiro nzaha ingororano z'ubutunzi bw'iteka; nyamara, mwanguranye verisiyo y'ikinyoma ya Kristo. Mwihangiye undi Yesu; mwakunze umwuka wa antikristo, kandi mwakoze icyaha cy'iteka ku bugingo bwanyu mu kuvuga ko ntari mu Itorero ryanjye bwite naguza amaraso yanjye bwite, icyaha mutazakira. Mu by'ukuri, ibi byose n'ibindi bizabazaho mu mujinya wanjye keretse mwicishije bugufi imbere yanjye kandi mukangira Umwami by'ukuri mwihana mwambaye ibigunira kandi mwiyisize ivu. Ntimushake impamvu zo kwirengagiza amagambo yanjye uko mukina n'ibisobanuro by'imvugo yanjye! Mwihane! Mwicare mu bigunira no mu ivu muririre gukiranirwa kwanyu, nanjye nzabakira kuri njye mbagarurire ingororano yanyu nari guha undi.

Ndi Uwiteka ukiranuka.

Ndi umukiza w'isi, ndetse Umwami w'abami n'Umutware w'abatware.

Ndi ugenda hagati y'ibitereko by'amatabaza birindwi kandi utegeka abamarayika barindwi b'amatorero arindwi.

Ndi umwana w'intama wishwe uhereye ku kuremwa kw'isi, kandi muri njye harimo ubugingo buhoraho.

Mfite imfunguzo z'urupfu n'ikuzimu, kandi Data yasangiye nanjye intebe ye y'ubwami, kugira ngo nsangire intebe yanjye y'ubwami n'abizerwa banjye, abahamagawe, n'abatoranyijwe. Ngendana n'ubwoko bwanjye kandi mvugana n'ubwoko bwanjye, kandi ndi umwungeri w'ukuri uragira uwo mukumbi Data yampaye. Muri imboni y'ijisho ryanjye, bizerwa banjye, kandi muri imboni y'ijisho rya Data, kandi kuri iki gihe n'isaha mwagaruriwe gukiranuka n'ubwera bw'ukuri.

Isaha iregereje, kandi mukwiye kureka imirimo yanyu bwite mugategereza ukuboneka kwanjye, kuko kugiye kuza ku isi uko mbakoranya mbakuye mu mfuruka enye z'isi. Ni ukuri, ni ukuri, nzakoranyiriza intama zanjye z'ukuri mu kigega cyanjye mbere y'uko imiyaga ije izarimbura iyi si mbi ya none, kuko ubwami bwa Satani buzashwanyuzwa bukarimburwa rwose kugira ngo buharire inzira Uwiteka ukiranuka uzategeka isi ari ku ntebe y'ubwami ya Dawidi mu gukiranuka n'ubwera bw'ukuri, kandi nzaha imbaraga zikomeye umugeni wanjye mu bwami buzaza, kuko ari abami n'abatambyi banjye, kandi bazategeka mu gukiranuka n'ubwera bw'ukuri, kuko ingororano yanjye iri mu kuboko kwanjye ku bizerwa.

Ndi imbaraga zanyu n'agakiza kanyu, kandi mushyiriweho ikimenyetso muri njye.

Ndi umugati w'ubugingo!

Nkwiriye kubumbura ibitabo no gukuraho ibimenyetso.

Ndi umwami w'ukuri w'ijuru n'isi.

Ndi umwami w'abami.

Ndi Uwiteka ukiranuka.

Ndi umukiza w'isi, Umwami w'abami n'Umutware w'abatware.

Ndi ugenda hagati y'ibitereko by'amatabaza birindwi kandi utegeka abamarayika barindwi b'amatorero arindwi.

Ndi umwana w'intama wishwe uhereye ku kuremwa kw'isi.

Ndi umwungeri w'ukuri uragira uwo mukumbi Data yampaye.
