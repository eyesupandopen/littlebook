Ubuhamya Bubiri: Isirayeli
Ibisharira Bivanze n'Ibiryoshye
27 Nyakanga 2024

Wange Ikimenyetso cy'Inyamaswa!

Umva ijwi ry'ubwenge kandi upfukame wumve ijwi rizima rya Yeshua HaMashiach, kuko ndi Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda, kandi ndi Umwami w'abami n'Umutware w'abatware! El Shaddai, Imana Ishoborabyose, yaranshyize hejuru cyane impa izina risumba ayandi mazina yose. Kugira ngo mu Izina rya Yesu amavi yose apfukame, ay'ibyo mu ijuru, n'ibyo mu isi, n'ibyo munsi y'isi: Kandi indimi zose ziature ko Yesu Kristo ari Umwami, ngo Imana Data w'Imicyo ahabwe icyubahiro, ari we Yahweh, Imana ihoraho kandi y'iteka, kuko nta wuhwanye na Data!

Umva ijwi ry'Umwami wawe kandi wumve ibintu byamaze gutangira n'ibigiye kuba vuba, kuko dore ndi uvuga ibizaba nk'aho byamaze kuba kuko rwose Umwami wanyu ukomeye, ari njye Kristo Yesu usumba byose nta cyo nzakora, keretse mbishishuriye abagaragu banjye b'Abahanuzi. Kuki mwumvira amajwi menshi avuguruzanya, kandi kuki muzuyaza hagati y'ibitekerezo bibiri? Sinavuze ko abahanuzi banjye bavugira hamwe kandi batazigera bavuguruza Amagambo yanjye yera? Kuko ndi Jambo wigize umubiri ubana namwe binyuze mu mwuka wanjye wera wavukiye muri mwe iteka ryose, kuko buri bugingo bwese bwongeye kuvuka bushyirwaho ikimenyetso cy'uwo mwuka wera w'isezerano ari rwo rubuto rwanjye rudashira kandi ikimenyetso cy'Imana Nzima ntigishobora kumenwa. Kandi umunsi wo gucungurwa kw'ibyarobanuwe uregereje kandi gucungurwa kwanyu kwuzuye kuri hano.

Uku ni ko Uwiteka Nyiringabo avuga, ntimwumve amagambo y'abahanuzi babahanurira, bakabigisha iby'ubusa: bavuga iyerekwa ryo mu mitima yabo, ritavuye mu kanwa k'Uwiteka. Nimuze aho ndi bana banjye kuko gusigwa mwahawe nanjye, kuguma muri mwe: kandi ntimukeneye ko hagira umuntu ubigisha: Ahubwo, nk'uko uko gusigwa kubigisha byose, kandi kukaba ari ukuri, kutari ikinyoma: kandi nk'uko kwabigishije, muzaguma muri njye. Bana banjye mugume muri njye kandi mugereranye amagambo y'umuntu wese uvuga ko amvugira n'ibyanditswe kandi munsabe kubyemeza mu mwuka wanyu, wenda mwakumva ijwi ryanjye kuko benshi muri mwe munangiye amatwi, kandi benshi muri mwe mukunda amagambo y'ibinyoma abaryohwera mu matwi kugira ngo mugaragare nk'abanyabwenge ariko ahubwo mugakurikira amagambo y'ibinyoma y'abapfapfa biha agaciro.

Dore! Ndavuga iby'ibihe by'amakuba ya Yakobo yiteguye kugwira isi yose. Kuko urubanza rwamaze gutangira kandi ntirushobora guhagarikwa, kuko icyo Yahweh ategetse nta wushobora kugihindura! Amarira menshi, menshi narayarije ku rubanza rwa Babuloni y'Ibanga kandi nohereje abahanuzi banjye mu myaka menshi, menshi kugeza ubu ndetse nohereje abanyamahanga baturutse mu bihugu by'amahanga kugira ngo batange ubuhamya kandi bahanure iby'imanza zosatiriye ariko ubu zikaba ziri hano.

Kuko ari ko bizaba kuko ari ko byanditswe: Amahembe icumi wabonye ku nyamaswa, azanga maraya, amuhindure umusaka, amwambike ubusa, arye inyama ze, amutwike n'umuriro. Kuko Imana ishyize mu mitima yabo gukora ibyo ishaka, no guhuza inama, no guha inyamaswa ubwami bwabo, kugeza aho amagambo y'Imana azasohorera. Kandi wa mugore wabonye, ni wa mudugudu ukomeye utegeka abami bo mu isi.

Umva amagambo yanjye, urubanza rwa Babuloni y'Ibanga rwamaze gutangira. Amahembe icumi yateguye igitero cyayo gitunguranye kandi igitero cyayo kiregereje kandi kiri ku muryango wanyu. Kuko n'itangazamakuru ryanyu rishaka kubasinziriza kandi benshi bicaye mu mwijima bahumye batabona ukuri bavuga bati: Nicaye ndi Umwamikazi kandi sindi umupfakazi kandi sinzabona umuborogo. Ejo hazaba nka none, kuko none ari nk'ejo hashize. Uko yihesheje icyubahiro, ikiberaho mu binezeza, abe ari ko muyiha kubabazwa no kuboroga: Ni cyo gituma ibyago byayo bizaza ku munsi umwe, urupfu, n'umuborogo, n'inzara, kandi izatwikwa n'umuriro: kuko Yahweh uyicira urubanza, ari Imana Ishoborabyose kandi nta wuyiruta kandi nta gishobora guhagarika ukuboko gukomeye kwa El Shaddai!

Kandi bityo izuba rizarasa vuba ku munsi w'amakuba mutiteze, kandi uwo munsi uri hafi kuruta uko mubitekereza. Kandi uwo munsi uzazana kurimbuka gusesuye kuri Babuloni y'Ibanga, kuko yaciriweho urubanza rwikubye kabiri. Ibyaha byayo n'ubusambanyi bwayo ni byinshi kandi igihano cyayo ntigitinza kandi igihano gikomeye kizagwa ku batarihana ndetse n'Itorero ryanjye ryizerwa ndetse n'Umugeni wanjye wizerwa bazakoranyirizwa aho ndi mu buryo butunguranye mu bicu kandi bakizwe umujinya, ndetse n'amakuba akomeye y'icyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli. Babuloni Ikomeye Iraguye, Iraguye! Umva amagambo yanjye kuko mvuga nk'aho byamaze kuba kuko nta kizahindura, gikerereze, kibangamiye, cyangwa ngo gihagarike imanza za Yahweh kugwira isi.

Mu isaha imwe izatwikwa n'umuriro, imwe mu midugudu yayo izashya, imwe mu midugudu yayo izarengerwa n'umwuzure ukaze, iminara yayo izagwa kandi imenyagurike mo ibice byinshi, ibiraro byayo bizagwa, kandi hazabaho gutigisa gukomeye. Isi izatanyagurika mu buryo bukaze kandi ubutaka bwinshi buzarengerwa n'amazi akaze. Inkombe zayo zizatwikirwa ziturutse iburasirazuba n'iburengerazuba n'imiraba minini kandi hagati hazamuke imiraba yihuta no gutigisa gukomeye. Ibisigaye bizaba umusaka kandi ntizigera yongera kubyuka, kuko iri shyanga ry'ama leta menshi n'imidugudu myinshi ntirizigera ryongera kubyuka kandi kugwa kwaryo kuzaba gukomeye! Kuko isi ari iy'Uwiteka n'ibyiyuzuyemo byose, kandi ibice byose by'ingaruka zayo mbi bizahanagurwa iteka ryose mu mahanga mu gihe cy'icyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli, kuko ari ko nzeza isi mu mujinya wanjye. Jyewe, Kristo Yesu, Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda, natangaje aya magambo, kandi imyiteguro yo kuyasohoza yamaze gutangira kandi izasohora gitunguranye mu isaha imwe.

Kandi bityo intambara izaduka ku isi yose nk'igice cy'igitero gitunguranye kuri Babuloni y'Ibanga, kuko ibi byateguwe kandi bihuza inama mu myaka ya yubile myinshi kugeza ubu, kuko ibanga ryo gukiranirwa ryakoraga kuva mu bihe bya kera kandi rizuzuzwa byuzuye igihe ubuza azaba akuweho ubwo abizerwa banjye bazaba bajyanywe mu ijuru. Intambara izagwa ku isi yose kandi izaza ku bantu bose n'amahanga yose, kuko iyi ari intambara ya gatatu nk'iyo, kuko abaguye bashaka gusenya gahunda za kera z'isi no gusimbuza byose gahunda yabo nshya, ndetse na gahunda y'Ikig айoka. Inyamaswa yo mu nyanja n'inyamaswa yo mu isi zizazamuka mu burakari bukomeye, kandi zizarwanya abera, kuko zahawe kunesha abera. Uko ni ko bizaba ko abadahara amagara yabo bose kandi bakambaza izina ry'Umwami kugeza ku iherezo bazakizwa mu byishimo by'iteka mu iteka ry'iteka ryose. Kandi jyewe Yesu w'i Nazareti nasize urugero rw'iteka rwo kumvira kugeza ku rupfu, kuko kumvira kwanjye Yahweh kwari ndetse n'urupfu rwo ku musaraba ubwo naba barizwaga kandi nishyura ibyaha by'abantu nk'umwana w'intama w'Imana kandi dore ndaza nk'Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda kandi sinzahagarikwa cyangwa ngo mbangamirwe!

Kandi bityo abahanuzi banjye babiri ari bo biti by'iminzenze bibiri n'ibitereko by'amatara bibiri bihagarara imbere y'Imana y'isi bazahanura amezi 42 bambaye ibigunira, kuko bajyanywe mu ijuru mu bihe bya kera. Kandi umuntu nashaka kubagirira nabi, umuriro uzava mu kanwa kabo, utwike abanzi babo: kandi umuntu nashaka kubagirira nabi, ni ko akwiriye kwicwa. Abo bafite ubutware bwo gukinga ijuru, kugira ngo imvura itagwa mu minsi y'ubuhanuzi bwabo: kandi bafite ubutware ku mazi bwo kuyahindura amaraso, no guteza isi ibyago byose, uko bashatse kose.

Ubuhamya bwabo ni ubw'iteka, kandi bazatangaza ubutumwa bwiza bw'iteka, kandi bazavuga imigambi ya Yahweh y'ibihe byose, kandi bazamvugira jyewe Kristo Yesu, kandi amabanga menshi bazayahishura kandi bazahindura imitima ya benshi bayerekeza kuri Data w'Imicyo udahinduka cyangwa ngo agire igicucu cyo guhindukira. Bazantangira ubuhamya jyewe Yeshua HaMashiach kuko ari jyewe Yahweh yohereje muri Isirayeli kugira ngo nsohoze amasezerano yahawe ba Se kandi ndi Mesiya w'ukuri ubu n'iteka ryose kandi ubwami bwanjye ntibugira iherezo! Kuko ni ibihe byihe byigeze kubaho aho Yahweh yisize adafite ubuhamya? NTIHAKIGEZE habaho igihe Yahweh yisize adafite ubuhamya, kandi abatoranyirijwe agakiza k'iteka bose bazaza gukizwa mu buzima bw'iteka n'ibyishimo by'iteka, kuko isohora ry'ibintu byose ryamaze gutangira.

Kandi bityo inyamaswa yo mu nyanja izazamuka hamwe n'inyamaswa yo mu isi, ari na we muhanuzi w'ibinyoma. Kandi izabumbura akanwa kayo ivuga ibitutsi bikomeye kuri Yahweh kugira ngo ituke izina rye n'ihema rye n'ababa mu ijuru. Kandi izahabwa kurwanya abera no kubanesha kandi izahabwa ubutware bwo gutegeka imiryango yose, n'indimi zose, n'amahanga yose kuko izayobora isi. Inyamaswa yo mu isi izategeka n'igihugu nka Moshiach wabo w'ibinyoma kandi izaba umwubatsi wabo w'urusengero. Inyamaswa yo mu nyanja izategeka hakurikijwe amategeko yabo y'ibitutsi ya Nowa kuko bashaka kurandura ikintu cyose gifitanye isano n'Imana y'ukuri n'umwana wayo w'ikinege Yesu Kristo ku maso y'isi.

Inyamaswa yo mu isi izatuma isi iramya inyamaswa, yakize uruguma rwayo rwishe. Kandi izakora ibitangaza bikomeye kugira ngo iyobye abatuye mu isi, kuko izemererwa gukora ibimenyetso n'ibitangaza byinshi by'ibinyoma, kandi izamanura umuriro mu isi uva mu ijuru mu maso y'abantu kandi izatuma abantu bose bayitangarira. Ariko ni umwanzi, kandi yishyira hejuru y'icyitwa Imana cyose, cyangwa igisengwa: kugira ngo yicare mu Rusengero rw'Imana nk'Imana, yiyerekana ko ari Imana.

Kandi izakora igishushanyo cy'inyamaswa kandi izatuma bose baramya igishushanyo izakora. Kandi izemererwa guha umwuka igishushanyo cy'inyamaswa kugira ngo igishushanyo cy'inyamaswa kivuge kandi izatuma abatazaramya igishushanyo cy'inyamaswa bose bicwa. Kandi izategeka bose, abakomeye n'aboroheje, abakire n'abakene, ab'umudendezo n'abagaragu, gushyirwaho ikimenyetso cyayo ku kuboko kwabo kw'iburyo cyangwa mu ruhanga rwabo.

Kandi bityo ndongera kubwira abatuye mu isi kugira ngo bose bumve:

UMUNTU WESE ARAMUTSE ARAMIJE INYAMASWA N'IGISHUSHANYO CYAYO, AGAHYRWAKO IKIMENYETSO CYAYO MU RUHANGA RWE, CYANGWA KU KUBOKO KWE, UWO NA WE AZANYWA KU NZOGA Y'UMUJINYA W'IMANA, YASUTSWE IDAFUNGUSHE MU GIKOMBE CY'UMUJINYA WAYO, KANDI AZABABARIZWA MU MURIRO N'AMAZUKU, IMBERE Y'ABAMARAYIKA BERA, N'IMBERE Y'UMWANA W'INTAMA: KANDI UMWEKA W'UKUBABABARIZWA KWABO UZACUMBA ITEKA RYOSE. KANDI NTA RUKIRUHUKO BAZAGIRA KU MANYWA NA NIJORO, ABARAMYA INYAMASWA N'IGISHUSHANYO CYAYO, N'UMUNTU WESE USHYIRWAHO IKIMENYETSO CY'IZINA RYAYO.

Isi iriho ubu ntizongera kubaho ukundi, kandi ubwami bwa Deregoni buzahanurwa burundu, uko ubwami bwa Satani bugana ku iherezo ryabwo, kuko ari abakodesha batari abami. Kandi iyi ni yo si benshi bazisangamo, ndetse n'abarokotse amakuba n'intambara bigiye kuza. Ntimwakire ikimenyetso bantu banjye, kuko bazaza batanga amahoro, bazaza batanga umutekano, bazababwira ko ari ku bw'ibyiza byanyu. Bazateza ibyo bita ibyorezo kuko bamaze gupanga kwemeza isi ko irwaye. Kandi amoko yose y'indwara z'ibyorezo n'ibiza bizagwira kuko izo ari zo ngaruka z'abagendera ku mafarashi bagiye kuza kandi bazagendaho!

Bazatanga ubuzima n'agakiza mu bihano by'Imana, mu gihe umuntu yagakwiye kwihana ibyaha bye. Bazatanga uburenganzira bwo kwinjira mu muryango wabo mu bihano biteye ubwoba bigiye kugwira isi. Kuko njyewe Kristo Yesu nzababwira mbere y'uko biba kugira ngo mumenye ukuri kw'amagambo yanjye kandi kugira ngo mubashe kunyura mu mazi akaze benshi bazisangamo, ndetse n'abafite ubutwari bwo kwambaza izina ryanjye kugeza ku iherezo, kuko ari abaraganwa agakiza k'iteka. Kuko abambaza Yesu w'i Nazareti nk'Umwami wabo bose, ndetse n'abambaza izina ryanjye rikomeye bose ntibakunde amagara yabo kugeza ku gupfa, bazakizwa bahabwe ubugingo buhoraho.

Ntimwasomye se? Aba ni bo bavuye mu makuba akomeye, bameshe amakanzu yabo, bayejesha amaraso y'Umwana w'Intama. Ni cyo gituma bari imbere y'intebe y'Imana, bakayikorera ku manywa na nijoro mu Rusengero rwayo: kandi uwicaye ku Ntebe azabana na bo. Ntibazongera gusonza, cyangwa kugira inyota ukundi, kandi izuba ntirizabica, cyangwa icyocyere cyose. Kuko Umwana w'Intama, uri hagati y'intebe y'ubwami, azabaragira, akabayobora ku masoko y'amazi y'ubugingo: kandi Imana izahanagura amarira yose ku maso yabo.

Icyakora, nubwo isinzi ry'abantu benshi rizarokokera mu cyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli kandi agakiza kakazagera kuri Isirayeli kuko abasigaye bazampfukamira njyewe Yeshua HaMashiach, (Kuko ndi Mesiya w'ukuri uwo Umwana w'Intama w'Imana azaharira umwanya Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda, kandi byombi bizuzuzwa mu bwumvikane bw'iteka muri njye, kuko muri njye Kristo Yesu Intare izabana mu mahoro n'umwana w'intama.) bibabaje, kandi nta mpamvu, benshi mu batuye isi bazatangazwa kandi bakurikire abo bategetsi b'inyamaswa, kuko bazashukashuka benshi. Binyuze mu burozi bwabo n'ubupfumu bwabo, bazashuka isi kwemera uburozi bwabo, kandi bazaha uburozi burundu abakunda isi binyuze mu burozi bwabo, kuko ikimenyetso cy'inyamaswa kizaba cyiyoberanyije nk'umuti. Ndetse n'ubumara bw'inzoka uburyo bwabo bwaramaze gushyirwa ahagaragara no kugeragezwa, kandi abantu benshi bamaze gutegurwa kwemera ikimenyetso cyabo kandi igikorwa cyabo cy'imbuto y'intambara cyaragenze neza.

Kandi bityo uburyo bwabo bwiteguye igihe nyacyo, kandi bateguye amanota yabo mbonezamubano kandi bose bazagenzurwa ababa mu mijyi yabo no mu muryango wabo. Kuko bazasabwa kwakira ikimenyetso cy'inyamaswa kugira ngo bitabire umuryango wabo wa gisitani, ndetse n'ubwami bwa Deregoni. Abazakira icyo kimenyetso bazahindurwa burundu. Imiterere yabo y'ingirabuzimafatizo izahindurwa, kuko bazivanga n'imbuto y'abantu: ariko ntibazafatana, nk'uko icyuma kidafatana n'ibumba. Ikimenyetso cyabo cy'inyamaswa, ndetse n'ubumara bw'inzoka, kizarimbura burundu abazakira uburozi bwabo bose kandi nta buhungiro buhari.

Ndetse n'abazakira icyo kimenyetso bose bazahindurwa burundu, kandi bazisanga ari igice cy'uburyo bwabo, kandi bazahuzwa n'ikoranabuhanga kandi ubwenge bwabo ntibuzaba ukundi ubwabo. Bazagenzurwa kandi bazaba nk'abapfuye bagenda, bazaba abagaragu b'iteka b'ubwenge rusange bw'uburyo bwabo, ndetse n'ikoranabuhanga ryabo kuko bazabohwa hamwe n'urusobe rw'ibintu bizaza. Bazatakaza ubushake bwabo bwite, kandi ntibazagira ibyiyumvo kandi ikintu cyose cy'Imana kizabasibwamo burundu kuko bazihindura mu mimerere itagishobora gucungurwa, kandi baziyunga burundu n'abaguye kandi bazaba aba deregoni iteka ryose kandi bazajya mu nyanja nini y'umuriro iteka ryose. Kandi uko ni ko iherezo bihitiyemo rizaba.

Kuko iherezo ry'amahitamo yabo ridashobora guhindurwa. Kandi ibyorezo byabo n'ibisebe n'indwara bizaba byinshi, kandi nubwo bazasezeranywa ubuzima n'amahoro, ubuzima n'amahoro ntibazigera babigira. Kuko Satani aharanira gukoresha uburozi bwe kugira ngo arwanye umujinya n'ibihano by'Imana no guha uburozi no kwica abantu benshi, ariko nta ruhuka bazagira kandi abazifatanya mu burozi bwe ntibazigera bacika. Abazakira icyo kimenyetso bazabagirana munsi y'urumuri rwabo rwijimye, kandi amatara yabo azakikiza imihanda y'imijyi yabo bateguriye abagaragu babo. Mumenye ko aba bazaba abavumwe bagenda badafite imbabazi kandi badashobora gukira, kuko aba ari bo amazina yabo atanditswe mu Gitabo cy'Ubugingo cy'Umwana w'Intama, watambwe uhereye ku kuremwa kw'isi. Kandi nta gakiza kazigera kaboneka muri bo cyangwa mu muryango wabo, kuko kugendana na bo no kwinjira mu muryango wabo bizasaba byanze bikunze ikimenyetso cy'inyamaswa.

Abatangabuhamya banjye babiri bazatanga ubuhamya kugeza igihe cy'ubuhamya bwabo kirangiye kuko bahawe amezi 42. Kandi bityo inyamaswa izamuka iva ikuzimu izahabwa kurwana na bo, kandi izabanesha, ibice, ndetse n'ibiti byanjye by'imyelayo bibiri, n'ibitereko by'amatara bibiri nibamara gutanga ubuhamya bwabo. Kandi imirambo yabo izaguma mu muhanda w'umujyi mukuru, mu buryo bw'umwuka witwa Sodoma na Egiputa, aho Umwami wanyu na we yabambwe. Kandi abanyabyaha bazakora ibirori bikomeye kandi bahane impano kubera urupfu rwabo. Kandi nyuma y'iminsi itatu n'igice Umwuka w'ubugingo uva ku Mana uzabinjiramo: kandi bazahagarara ku maguru yabo, kandi ubwoba bukomeye buzateba ku bababonye bose. Kandi bumvise ijwi rikomeye riva mu ijuru, ribabwira riti, Nimuzamuke hano. Kandi bazamutse mu ijuru mu gicu, kandi abanzi babo bazabareba bazamuka mu ijuru mu bugingo buhoraho kandi bazahindurwa iteka ryose. Kandi marayika wa karindwi azavuza impanda, kandi hazumvikana amajwi akomeye mu ijuru, avuga ati, Ubwami bw'iyi si buhindutse ubwami bw'Umwami wacu, n'ubwa Kristo we, kandi azategeka iteka ryose.

(Hahirwa abapfa bapfiriye mu Mwami, uhereye none, yego, ni ko Umwuka avuga, kugira ngo baruhuke imiruho yabo, kandi imirimo yabo irabakurikira.)

Kandi bityo amakuba akomeye kuruta ayandi yose azatangira, nubwo amakuba azaba ahoraho mu cyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli. Kandi urusengero rwabo ruzuzura kandi ikizira kirimbura kigizwe na Moshiach wabo w'ikinyoma. Kandi azasezerana isezerano na benshi mu cyumweru kimwe: kandi hagati mu cyumweru azahagarika igitambo n'ituro, kandi kubera gukwira kw'ibizira, azahindura aho hantu umusaka, kugeza ubwo iherezo ryategetswe rizasukwa ku warimbuwe.

Ibi bizaba ibihe byijimye cyane bana banjye, kuko njyewe Yeshua HaMashiach mbabwira ibi bintu mbere y'uko biba, kandi byanditswe nk'umuburo kuri bose. Kandi bityo abantu banjye 144,000 bazatabara abari bagiye kurimbuka kandi bambaza izina ryanjye. Nzabayobora intambwe ku yindi, kandi bazankorera imirimo myinshi ikomeye. Kuko aba ari bo bazatwarwa mu ikoraniro ry'abera basanga njyewe, Kristo Yesu, mu kirere ku ijwi ry'impanda ya nyuma kandi impanda igiye kuvuga.

Aba ni abizerwa banjye, ndetse n'abo bitabonetsemo uburiganya, kuko biyejeje nk'isugi itunganye mu maraso y'Umwana w'Intama natambye iteka ryose nk'umutambyi mukuru wabo, kuko ndi umutambyi mukuru iteka ryose mu buryo bwa Melikisedeki. Aba ni umugeni wanjye, kandi bazankurikira aho nzajya hose. Dore! Nzabagira abanyambaraga, kandi nta kintu na kimwe cyigeze kiboneka ku isi gishobora kugereranywa n'ubushobozi nzabambika. Bazambikwa imyenda yera, itunganye y'igitare kandi ubushobozi bwabo buzagaragaza ibyo bagezeho kandi bazagira ubushobozi bukomeye kuko ari abizerwa bahamagawe kandi batoranyijwe ndetse na mbere y'iremwabuhanga ry'isi na Yahweh ku bw'iyi migambi.

Abantu banjye 144,000 bazamanuka ku Musozi wa Siyoni kandi nzabayobora. Bazatwara abo mbayoboraho mu butayu kugira ngo bahishwe umwijima w'icuraburindi n'itotezwa ry'ubwami bw'abanzi. Bazayoborwa mu mutekano kure y'ibyorezo birindwi kuko muri ibi bihano harimo ubukana bw'umujinya w'Imana buzasukwa ku ntebe y'inyamaswa no kubabaza ubwami bwa deregoni kuko igihano cyabo ari gikomeye. Kandi ba 144,000 bazabayobora mu mutekano, bazabakiza ibikomere byabo, bazabatabara kandi babahe ibyiringiro bikomeye byo kwambaza izina ry'Umwami ndetse kugeza ku iherezo niba bikenewe. Kuko iyo ntagabanya iyo minsi, nta muntu n'umwe wari gusigara ari muzima. Kandi bityo bazasoza ibyo nzabayoboramo.

Kandi igihe nikigera bityo bizaba, ndetse nk'uko byanditswe kandi nk'uko byanditswe bityo bikorwe: Kandi mbona ijuru rikinguye, mbona ifarashi yera, kandi uyihetsaho yitwa uwo kwizerwa n'uw'ukuri, kandi aca imanza zitabera akarwana intambara gukiranuka. Amaso ye yari ameze nk'ibirimi by'umuriro, kandi ku mutwe we hari amakamba menshi, kandi yari afite izina ryanditswe, nta muntu numwe urizi keretse we ubwe. Kandi yari yambaye umwenda winitswe mu maraso, kandi izina rye yitwa, Jambo ry'Imana. Kandi ingabo zo mu ijuru zamukurikiye ku mafarashi yera, zambaye imyenda y'igitare, yera kandi itunganye. Kandi mu kanwa ke havamo inkota ityaye, kugira ngo ayikubite amahanga: kandi azayategekesha inkoni y'icyuma: kandi asarura mu rwengero rw'ubukana n'umujinya by'Imana Ishoborabyose. Kandi ku mwenda we, no ku kibero cye yari afite izina ryanditswe ngo, Umwami w'Abami, n'Umutware w'Abatware.

Kandi bityo njyewe, ndetse Yesu w'i Nazareti, ndetse Umwana w'Intama w'Imana, ndetse Kristo Yesu ari we kandi Ntari yo mu Muryango wa Yuda nzaza guca imanza no kurwanya Deregoni kandi ubwami bwayo bwose buzamenagurwa burundu ntibuzongera kubyuka ukundi. Kandi inyamaswa n'umuhanuzi w'ikinyoma bazajugunywa ari bazima mu nyanja y'umuriro kandi Satani azabohwa n'umunyururu munini ikuzimu imyaka igihumbi kandi bityo ingoma yanjye y'imyaka igihumbi izatangira kandi mu gukiranuka ni ko gutegeka kwanjye kuzaba. Dore! Nzanye umugeni wanjye n'ingabo z'abami n'abatambyi nzafatanya na bo gutegeka isi mu gukiranuka kandi nzategekesha amahanga inkoni y'icyuma. Bityo mugire ibyiringiro bikomeye bana banjye kuko iherezo ry'ibintu byose riri hafi kandi iki gihe cy'imyaka irindwi y'amakuba akomeye, ndetse amakuba ya Yakobo kizakurikirwa n'ingoma yanjye yo gukiranuka kandi Yahweh azaba byose muri byose.

Kandi mbona ijuru rishya, n'isi nshya: kuko ijuru rya mbere, n'isi ya mbere byari byashize, kandi inyanja yari itakiriho. Kandi njyewe Yohana mbona Umurwa wera, Yerusalemu nshya imanuka iva ku Mana mu ijuru, iteguwe nk'umugeni urimbishirijwe umugabo we. Kandi numva ijwi rikomeye riva mu ijuru, rivuga riti, Dore, Ihema ry'Imana riri kumwe n'abantu, kandi izabana na bo, kandi bazaba abantu bayo, kandi Imana ubwayo izabana na bo, kandi ibe Imana yabo.

Njyewe Yeshua HaMashiach nohereje aya Magambo binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye, kandi binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye aya Magambo yoherejwe!
