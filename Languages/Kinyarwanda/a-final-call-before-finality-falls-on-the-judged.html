Umuhamagaro wa Nyuma Mbere y'uko Imperuka Igwa ku Baciriwe Urubanza

Dore! Ndi Yehova, NDI WE NDI WE Ukomeye, kandi nje kohereza Amagambo yanjye y'urukundo n'umuhamagaro wa nyuma ku bazahura n'imperuka y'urubanza nibaramuka banze umuhamagaro wanjye wa nyuma. Nubwo, abambaza izina rya Yesu bose kugeza ku iherezo bazabona agakiza gakomeye kandi kw'iteka n'ingororano ikomeye, abantu benshi cyane bazacirwa urubanza kubera ibyaha byabo no kwinangira imitima. Kuko, Ndi we wemeza abakiranirwa kwihana, kandi abanga kwihana nkomeza kubemeza kugeza ubwo imitima yabo inangiwe rwose bagatwarwa mu rubanza.

Ku bw'ibyo, nje gutanga ubu butumwa mu rukundo rwanjye mbakunda, kandi nohereze ubuntu bwanjye mudakwiriye ku isi yaciriwe urubanza nanone mu gushaka kwanjye koroshya imitima yanyu yinangiye ngo mwemere agakiza; cyangwa ngo mu rwango rwanyu no kwigomeka musigare mu mimerere yo kwanga burundu, mweze kandi mwiteguye urubanza rwa nyuma. Musobanukirwe, nta gihe gisigaye mu bihe byanjye by'ubuntu n'imbabazi cyo kwihana ubuzima bwose kubera imigambi yanjye y'agakiza ku bantu bose bazagera ku gakiza kuzuye mu bihe byabo no mu byiciro byabo ku bazemera urukundo rwanjye kuri bo. Ku bw'ibyo, ibihe byanyu ni ubu kandi umuhamagaro wa nyuma urimo koherezwa ubu. Sela.

Nimwumve, Yemwe Nimwumve ijwi ry'Imana Ikomeye y'Iteka yo Gukiranuka, ari we Yehova, NDI WE NDI WE Ukomeye, ari na we Mana y'Iteka yo Gukiranuka kandi musobanukirwe igisobanuro cy'imvugo yanjye, kandi mumenye ko ijwi ryanjye rimeze nk'amajwi y'amazi menshi asuma kandi y'ubugingo, kandi musobanukirwe ko ijwi ryanjye rihinda nk'amajwi y'inkuba nyinshi, (ndetse n'inkuba z'inkuba) mu burakari bwanjye bukiranuka ku batuye isi bayituyemo mu kutumvira gukomeye no mu gukiranirwa gukomeye n'icyaha.

Dore! NDI UWONDIWE, ndetse NDI WE NDI WE Ukomeye, kandi naremye ijuru n'isi n'ibyuzuyemo byose, kandi mu mugambi wanjye wo gukiranuka naremye ijuru n'isi bitunganye mu nzira zanjye zo gutunganya, ndetse mbirema mu butungane bw'ubwumvikane no gukiranuka aho ibyo naremye byose byagombaga kubaho mu butungane bw'ubwumvikane n'urukundo no gukorerana mu rukundo. Kandi mu rukundo bakunda Data wabo n'umuremyi wabo, bumviraga ijwi ryanjye n'amategeko yanjye ku buzima bwabo kugira ngo koko bisobanukirwe uko baremwe kandi basobanukirwe n'inzira zanjye. Nyamara, gukiranirwa kwabonetse muri Heyileli, ndetse n'uwaguye ava mu butungane bw'icyubahiro, ndetse n'uwaremwe mu bwiza bukomeye kandi butangaje, gusa kugira ngo agwe ku isi, ateshejwe agaciro mu cyaha n'umwijima kugira ngo ature mu ishusho y'inzoka zavumwe kubera kutumvira kw'Abaserafi. Sela.

Kubera ukuri kw'iteka, inzira zanjye zisumba inzira z'umuntu, kandi Ndi we wajyanye intebe yanjye y'ubwami n'ibihumbi icumi incuro ibihumbi icumi, ndetse n'umubare utabarika w'abamarayika barusha abandi ubwenge, imbaraga, n'ububasha, kandi bakunda Yehova kandi bakunda kwiga inzira za Yehova, NDI WE NDI WE Ukomeye mu kumvira gushyushye kandi kwizerwa iteka: Sela.

Mu by'ukuri, mu kuri gukomeye kandi kw'iteka, nabaremye bafite imbaraga n'icyubahiro kandi mbambika ubwiza burabagirana, no gukiranuka kw'iteka, n'icyubahiro gihoraho. Mu by'ukuri, mu kuri gukomeye kandi kw'iteka, ntibigeze babura icyo bakeneye cyangwa bashaka ndetse no mu tuntu duto cyane na rimwe; kandi bankorera njye n'ibyo naremye byose mu mitima yabo y'urukundo bakunda Data wabo n'umuremyi wabo, ari we Elohim, mu kumvira kwabo Kristo Yesu.

(Ndetse n'Uwo, Yehova, NDI WE NDI WE Ukomeye kandi Uhoraho yashyize ibintu byose mu ijuru no mu isi mu maboko ye akomeye kandi ahoraho kandi yumvira.)

Ku bw'ibyo mumenye ukuri kw'iteka ko Kristo Yesu ari Umwami w'abami n'Umutware w'abatware kandi ni we uzazana amahoro mu ijuru ndetse no mu isi kandi ni we uzagarura mu kuboko kwanjye kw'icyubahiro (mu rukundo akunda Se n'Umuremyi we, Elohim, ndetse na NDI WE NDI WE Ukomeye) Ubwami bweguriwe ku iherezo ry'ibintu byose ubwo amaboko ye akomeye azaba arangije ibintu byose byanditswe kandi byahanuwe ku byerekeye Umwami w'Iteka wo Gukiranuka! Ndetse n'ubwo uwo Mwana w'Umuntu azaba arangije ibintu byose mu mbaraga zikomeye, no mu rukundo rurushijeho gukomera. Ndetse n'icyo gihe, nzaba ndangije mu mbaraga zanjye binyuze mu maboko ye akomeye n'igitambo ibintu byose byatanzwe mu migambi yanjye y'iteka n'amabanga kugira ngo abisohoze.

Ku bw'ibyo, ndabaha gusobanukirwa, ko Ndi we ushobora mu mbaraga zose n'ubusuguge gusobanukirwa by'ukuri, gusohoza, kumenya byuzuye, no kugenzura buri kantu kose kerekeye buri gice cy'ibyo naremye uko cyaba kingana kose cyangwa kigaragara nk'igikomeye kugeza ku kantu gato cyane, kandi nta gishobora guhishwa amaso yanjye! Ndi we ushobora by'ukuri gusohoza ibintu byose ako kanya mu mbaraga zanjye n'ububasha bw'iteka. Nyamara, ndi na we ukora ibintu byose akurikije inama y'ubushake bwanjye bwite kandi udakeneye umujyanama, ku bw'ibyo, imigambi yanjye n'amabanga bizasohora byuzuye muri ibi bihe byo gusohozwa kwabyo, ndetse n'ibihe bya UBU.

Nyamara, mu rukundo rwanjye ku byo naremye n'icyifuzo cyanjye cy'abana n'umuryango, ndema ibiremwa n'abantu byihariye (bafite ubushake bwisanzuye) ku bw'imigambi yanjye y'iteka yihariye ihabwa abumvira mu murimo ukomeye n'icyubahiro, kandi mbaha imbaraga zikwiranye na byo. Ku bw'ibyo, ndabagira inama yo kwiga inzira zanjye mu gakiza cyangwa mugahitamo ibirimi by'umuriro byo kurimbuka iteka ryose. Ariko mumenye, Yewe muntu waguye, ufite amahitamo ubu mu buryo butunguranye azahinduka iteka ryose akaba wari ufite amahitamo, kandi abazaba barahisemo ikibi mu mperuka ubwo imperuka izaza, ntibazigera bacika imanza zabo iteka n'iteka ryose. Sela.

Uri inde, Yewe muntu waguye, ndetse nawe wifatanyije n'ukwigomeka kutagira ishingiro kandi kw'ubupfu n'ubusazi kw'abaguye mu gukiranirwa kose, umururumba, irari, n'ubugome kugira ngo mwishyire hejuru mu bwibone murwanya Imana Ikomeye kandi y'Iteka yabaremeye imigambi yanjye n'ibinezeza byanjye?

Uri inde, Yewe muntu waguye ku buryo mu bujiji bwawe n'irari ryawe watinyuka kubangurira agatsinsino Imana ikomeye kandi y'iteka y'Ijuru ry'Amajuru (ndetse n'UWO waguhaye ubuzima) mu nzira za Satani? Ku bw'ibyo, musobanukirwe imigambi yanjye n'icyo nshaka kuko ndimo kohereza aya Magambo KURI MWE nk'umuhamagaro wanjye wa nyuma kuri benshi muri mwe mutekereza mu bupfu ko mufite igihe mudafite, kandi ndetse ndabaha amagambo y'umuburo mu rukundo rwanjye rudashira mbakunda mbibutsa nanone amatangazo y'urubanza n'amakuba yamaze gutangwa mwirengagije byuzuye yakozwe na Kristo Yesu ku ntebe y'ubwami n'imbaraga bya Yehova, kandi yanatangajwe na Yehova ubwe, ndetse na NDI WE NDI WE Ukomeye!

(Ntimusobanukiwe ko amatangazo yanjye adashobora na rimwe guhindurwa?)

Nyamara, mwirengagiza ndetse n'imvugo y'inkuba zirindwi nahishuriye, kandi ngategeka Yohana Umunyahishurwa kuzifatanisha ikimenyetso mu gihe cye kugeza mu bihe bikwiriye byo guhishurwa ibyo bihe bikaba ari ubu. (Kandi bizakomeza kugeza ubwo ubuhamya bw'abahamya banjye babiri burangiriye mu rubanza rwa nyuma, mbere gato y'uko umubabaro ukomeye kuruta iyindi yose ugwa byuzuye ku bakiranirwa, Sela.) Ndetse izo nkuba zavuzwe na Yeshua HaMashiach na Yehova, NDI WE NDI WE Ukomeye, biturutse ku ntebe yanjye y'ubwami ikomeye yo gutangaza n'urubanza, kandi ubu bikaba bitangwa n'ukuboko kw'intumwa yanjye ikomeye nkohererezaho aya Magambo y'urukundo n'imiburo ikomeye cyane kandi iteye ubwoba, ndetse kugira ngo umuntu wese ushobora kuza aze, mbere y'uko urubanza rugwa byuzuye kandi hakaba nta gihe gisigaye!

Ku bw'ibyo, niba mwifuza gucika urubanza rwanyu no kurimbuka kw'iteka, ndabategeka kugira Yesu Umwami wanyu byuzuye kandi mudashidikanya. Kuko imanza zanjye zirimo kugwa, kandi zizagwa ku wo zoherejweho wese, ndetse n'izo manza zimwe zo gucirwaho iteka ziriho burundu mu mperuka yazo ndetse iteka n'iteka ryose. Ku bw'ibyo, ndagira inama abataratura Yesu Kristo nk'Umwami baturutse ku mutima wuzuye ukwizera kubikora ubu, bamenya ko Ndi we wamuzuye mu bapfuye mu mbaraga zikomeye cyane kugira ngo atazongera gupfa ukundi kandi akomeze mu murage we iteka n'iteka ryose. Nyamara, abenshi cyane bazagwa mu rubanza rukomeye kandi rw'iteka neza nk'uko byanditswe mu byanditswe Byera bya kera n'ibyanditswe Byera by'ubu.

Ariko nje gutanga imiburo yanjye uko byagenda kose, ndetse na nyuma y'uko amatangazo y'urubanza rwanyu rwa nyuma atangajwe kandi akaba yamaze gutangira. Kuko inzira zanjye ari inzira z'ubuntu n'imbabazi n'urukundo n'ukuri kw'iteka, nifuza ko nta n'umwe warimbuka ahubwo ko abantu bose bagera ku gakiza k'iteka no kugarurwa, nubwo abenshi bazanga icyo mbaha bagahitamo umubabaro wabo w'iteka n'urubanza mu cyimbo cyabyo. Nubwo, nje kuburira abantu, nta miburo itangwa izahindura imanza zanjye, cyangwa igihe cyazo, cyangwa ibizazivamo, cyangwa imigambi yanjye y'iteka yo gucungura umuganura wanjye, ndetse nkabashikuzamo kugira ngo basohoze umurage wabo w'iteka mbere y'uko amakuba agwa. Sela.

Kandi nuko nohereza ubu butumwa bw'urukundo n'umuburo mu rukundo rwanjye ku muntu wa kamere mubi, ndetse no muri urwo rukundo rumwe nohereza ubutumwa bw'urukundo n'umuburo ku bana banjye bwite bemeye Yesu Kristo mu kumvira; kuko mvubira imvura abakiranuka n'abakiranirwa kimwe. Kubera urukundo mbakunda, nje gutanga umuhamagaro wanjye wa nyuma mbere y'uko amakuba atera aturutse ku ntebe yanjye bwite y'ubwami y'imbaraga z'iteka, urubanza, no gutangaza aho ijwi ryanjye rihinda nk'inkuba rikomeza guhinda mu burakari bukomeye kandi bukiranuka. Ndetse n'ubwo bimeze bityo, kuko MWANZE kunyumva nubwo nababuriye nkababurira mu yubile nyinshi, nyinshi ubu! Nimunyumve kandi munyumve neza kuko ibihe byanyu byo kurimbuka kw'iteka bizoherezwa kugira ngo bibohe mu rubanza abakunda kandi bakabeshya bose, n'abanezezwa no gukiranirwa, kandi nimunyangira muri ibi bihe by'ubuntu, muzagwa mu rubanza rw'iteka kuko nta yandi mahitamo ahari.

Nyamara, mu bwibone bwanyu buhumye bw'imitima yinangiye, mwanga umuntu umwe nohereje ku bw'agakiza kanyu kuzuye, ari we Yesu w'i Nazareti, ndetse uwo Mwana wicisha bugufi n'umugaragu wa Yehova nagize umwuka utanga ubuzima, ndetse nkamugira uwo hasi ho gato ya Yehova ubwe mu kuzuka kwanjye mu bapfuye no guhabwa icyubahiro kwa Kristo Yesu! Kuko Ndi usumba byose kandi nkaba umutegetsi w'ikirenga ku byo naremye byose n'ukuri kw'iteka kandi kudahinduka, Ndi we wohereza imvura ku bakiranuka n'abakiranirwa kimwe mu rukundo rwanjye ku byo naremye, kandi mu nzira zanjye nta kurobanura ku butoni kubaho mu manza zanjye zikiranuka ku byo naremye.

Ku bw'ibyo, musobanukirwe ko kurobanura ku butoni ari imwe mu nzira nyinshi zanduye z'abaguye, bityo nje kuburira ndetse n'umunyabyaha mubi cyane mu batuye isi mbere y'uko urubanza rwabo rubageraho byuzuye, ndetse kugira ngo bashobore gucika mu masegonda make asigaye kandi yihuta y'ubuntu mbere y'uko amakuba atera. Bityo ndagira inama abumva cyangwa basoma aya Magambo bose guhindukirira Yesu w'i Nazareti ako kanya, badashidikanya na gato kuko ku mbaga nyamwinshi hazaba nta yandi mahirwe, kuko mu byaha byabo bibi no gusuzugura byuzuye ibintu byose Byera, bazagwa mu rubanza rw'iteka, ndetse barindirwe mu mubabaro aho bazategereza urwo rubanza rw'intebe y'ubwami yera nini, aho batazahagarara ahubwo bazabohwa bagashyikirizwa ibirimi by'umuriro by'igihano aho ibyaha byabo bizatangwa mu birimi by'umuriro ku bw'ingororano yuzuye y'ubutabera iteka ryose.

Nyamara, wowe, Yewe muntu waguye, wakwicara mu nzira zawe zo gukiranirwa, ndetse uneshejwe byuzuye n'abaguye mu bujiji bwawe n'ubuhumyi buterwa n'ibinyoma byabo, ndetse ibinyoma ukunda kuko ukunda inzira z'ibinyoma byawe. Ku bw'ibyo, nohereje mu isi kuyoba gukomeye kandi gukanganye kugira ngo mwese mucirweho iteka mwe munezezwa no gukiranirwa mukanga Yesu w'i Nazareti, ari we Yeshua HaMashiach. Kandi uko mukabakaba mu bujiji mu nzira zanyu zihumye kandi zinangiye, uko mwicara mu mwijima mu kutita ku bintu n'icyaha gukomeye, uko mugenda mu busa mwibikira umujinya mukomeye n'ingaruka z'iteka mu kutumvira kwanyu, nzakomeza nzamure ijwi ryanjye, ndetse nohereza aya Magambo nk'ubuhamya bushina abazanga, ndetse mu byiringiro by'uko mushobora guhindukirira Kristo Yesu, kugira ngo mbakize urubanza rw'abaguye: Sela.

Yewe muntu wa Kamere, ndetse namwe mwese bana ba Adamu badacunguwe kandi batihana, ndetse namwe mwese mhana mu isi mu rukundo rwanjye mbakunda! Nyamara mwizeye ibinyoma by'ubupfu cyane mwahawe nk'uburezi mu gucengerwamo kwanyu n'abaguye. Ndetse n'uburezi bw'urupfu butangirwa mu bigo byanyu byononekaye kandi byonona by'urupfu aho inzira z'abasodoma ubu zitegeka uko bashyiraho ikimenyetso kandi batoza abana banyu bwite kugira ngo bashobore kwigabiza abere mu irari ryabo riboze. Nyamara, mwatwawe cyane n'ibinezeza by'icyaha, kandi muremerewe cyane n'umururumba wanyu wo kwirundaho ubutunzi bwinshi, ku buryo mutamba abana banyu bwite ku bushake mu nzira za Baali ku bw'umuvunguka w'umugati wanduye. Ku bw'ibyo, nemereye abaguye kuroga ibyo kurya byanyu, kandi mbahaye uburumbuke bwanyu bwuzuye ndetse kugira ngo ikiyoka, inyamaswa iva mu nyanja, n'inyamaswa iva mu isi bishobore gusohoza imigambi yanjye, kuko ndetse n'abanzi banjye bumvira ijwi ryanjye.

Kuko ibyo mwita uburezi bwanyu ari ibihimbano kandi byononekaye ku bushake kandi ibi ni ukubera ko mwanga ubumenyi bw'ukuri n'inzira bya Elohim umuremyi wanyu, mugahitamo ahubwo gutwarwa n'indorerwamo zanyu z'umukara mufata mu ntoki mu budahemuka uko muva ku kirahure mujya ku kindi mu bujiji bw'umwijima mwinshi. Nyamara mwarengewe byuzuye kandi muhumwa n'ibinezeza bihendutse kandi bihita uko ubwenge bwanyu buhindurwa mu butabire ku bushake binyuze mu byuma mwibohashya mu cyaha. Kandi bityo ubumenyi bw'ibihimbano butera ubwibone kandi ubwibone bubanziriza kurimbuka gukomeye. Sobanukirwa, Yewe Muntu, ko ubumenyi bwawe budashobora kugukiza imanza zanjye, kandi urubanza rw'abaguye ubu rwiteguye kugwa. Nyamara, wowe, Yewe muntu wa Kamere uri umupfu byuzuye ku buryo udasobanukirwa ko abaguye babacengejemo ibinyoma byabo, ndetse kugira ngo batere abantu benshi bashoboka kwifatanya nabo mu murage wabo w'amazuku yaka, aboze, kandi yibirindura. Ndetse n'igihano gikaze cyakorewe umwanzi n'abamarayika be, atari Adamu. Sela.

Nyamara, ku bwemera bwanyu bw'ubushake mwirata cyane mu byo mwagezeho by'isi bitagira agaciro aho mukusanya mu mururumba impapuro zanyu mwiyoberanya ko zitagira agaciro n'imibare yanyu mwiyoberanya ko itagira agaciro kuri konti z'ikoranabuhanga zitagira agaciro muzakwa mu buryo butunguranye mu rubanza ubu, ndetse kuko izi ari inzira za Esawu wagurishije umurage we w'uburiza ku bw'umuvunguka w'umugati. Nyamara, ku bwemera bwanyu bw'ubushake mwemerera abaguye kubaka amafaranga bakoresheje iterabwoba ry'imbaraga kugira ngo bishyure gucengerwamo kw'abana banyu bwite mu nzira z'urupfu. Ndetse kugira ngo abacengerwamo muri ubwo bujiji n'ubuhumyi bose bakorere abaguye ku bushake mu gihe batekereza ko bakorera Imana ubwayo igikorwa cyiza gikomeye mu byaha byabo, nk'aho Yehova yagombaga kwigira ku ruhande akava mu byo yaremye n'ubusuguge bwe kubera ubwibone bwabo butagira agaciro, bw'icyaha. Ndetse n'ikintu kidashoboka kidashobora na rimwe kubaho, kuko NDI WE.

Kandi mu nzira zanyu zinangiye zo gukiranirwa mwatakaje ndetse n'amatwi yanyu kandi muhagaze muri ibipfamatwi, ibiragi, n'impumyi mu nzira zanyu zo gukiranirwa zabagabanyirije mu bubata nk'umugaragu w'icyaha kugira ngo muhaze irari ry'abaguye, abo mukorera mu nzira zanyu zitagira ukwizera. Nyamara, umugaragu ntaguma mu nzu iteka ryose naramuka ahisemo mu kumvira kwinjira mu muryango umwe rukumbi ugana mu ijuru nzigera nkingurira muntu: Yesu Kristo ari na we, Yesu Mesiya, witwa kandi Yeshua HaMashiach, ari we muryango mukomeye kandi umwe rukumbi ukinguye w'agakiza uzigera ukingurwa n'ukuboko kwanjye ku bw'abizerwa bakiranuka muri Kristo Yesu. Sela!

NDI WE, kandi nohereje aya Magambo y'urukundo ku baciriweho iteka binyuze ku ntumwa yanjye n'umugaragu wanjye, kandi binyuze ku ntumwa yanjye n'umugaragu wanjye nohereje aya Magambo ku bazacirwa urubanza vuba.

Musobanukirwe mwese abumva aya Magambo, ko iri atari itangazo rya nyuma ry'urubanza, rizana imperuka ku muntu wa kamere. Ahubwo, ubu butumwa bukora mu kuburira abatumvira n'abakiranirwa ba Babuloni y'Ibanga ndetse n'abo aya Magambo yohererejwe bose, (kandi bamwe bazagera ku gakiza muri aya masegonda ya nyuma) ariko kandi nk'ubuhamya bwa nyuma bw'umuburo butangwa mbere y'uko urubanza rugwa byuzuye kuri Babuloni y'Ibanga.

Musobanukirwe, ko Amagambo yanjye azabona amaso, amatwi, n'imitima by'abo yohererejwe, kandi nta mbaraga z'abaguye, ibizira byabo, cyangwa ikoranabuhanga ryabo ritagira agaciro kandi ry'ubujiji bishobora guhagarika ibyo NDI WE NDI WE Ukomeye agambiriye gusohoza.

Kandi nuko nshyize umukono ku rwandiko rwanjye bwite rw'urukundo ku baciriwe urubanza mu byiringiro by'uko binyuze mu kumvira Amagambo yanjye yoherejwe mu rukundo rwanjye kuri bo bazacika imanza zabo mbere y'uko ukuboko kwa nyuma gukubita iryo segonda rya nyuma.

Kandi nuko nshyize umukono ku rwandiko rwanjye rw'urukundo mu Rukundo rukomeye n'Ubutware bw'Iteka!
