Ubuhamya Bubiri: Isirayeli
Ibisharira N'ibiryohereye
7 Ugushyingo 2024

Umwana w'Intama ufite Amahembe Abiri

Kuva kuri YHWH: NDI UWONDI Ukomeye!

Dore! Ikiyobe gikomeye cyavuye muri Babuloni y'Ibanga kandi cyafashe isi mu maboko yacyo yica! Nimuze mwumve ikiyobe gikomeye, mwa batuye isi mwe, kandi musobanukirwe ibihe murimo, kuko ikiyobe gikomeye kiri hano isi ishimishwa nacyo kuko isi yabo ari urubyiniro n'abayobozi babo bakaba abakinnyi, ndetse n'abiyoberanya biyoberanya. Nuko rero ubu ngiye guhishura ukuri ku byerekeye iserukiramuco ryabo bakoresha baroga rubanda.

Mwirinde! Ikiyobe gikomeye cyabajeho n'ibimenyetso n'ibitangaza byinshi by'ibinyoma kandi iki kiyobe gikomeye kigamije mu buryo bukomeye kurimbura Yakobo, ndetse n'imigambi Esawu yagize ibanga mu bihe byose, ariko ubu ikaba igiye guhishurwa no gushyirwa ahagaragara ngo bose bayibone. Kandi nk'uko umwana wanjye, Yesu w'i Nazareti, yabwiye ba Se muri Yudaya mu bihe bya Yubile byashize: Mukomoka kuri so Satani kandi irari rya so ni ryo mushaka gukora. Nuko rero ndaje kuri wowe, Yewe Isirayeli, kugira ngo udatwarwa n'iki kiyobe gikomeye, kuko ni wowe wibasiwe (nubwo isi izatitira) kuko ni nde mutego utega umutego inyoni ireba?

Kandi ni njye, Yahweh ndetse NDI UWONDI Ukomeye, uje kuburira ubwoko bwanjye binyuze muri aya Magambo nahishuye kandi nkoherereza intumwa yanjye n'umugaragu wanjye watorewe iyi mirimo. Nuko rero ndaje ku bo mu miryango baba abari hafi cyangwa abari kure, baba abari mu gihugu cyangwa abatataniye mu mahanga, kandi nzakoranyiriza abasigaye banjye bose kuri Mesiya wabo akaba n'Umushumba Mukuru, Yeshua HaMashiach.

Kuko ari we uzayobora Isirayeli mu mutekano mu maboko y'abanjye 144,000 bakomeye kuko Yesu w'i Nazareti namugize Umwami w'abami n'Umutware w'abatware! Kandi ni we uzategeka isi mu gukiranuka mu gihe cy'ingoma ye y'imyaka igihumbi ari ku ntebe y'ubwami ya Dawidi muri Isirayeli yarazwe. Kuko ari ko nasezeranyije ba So kandi NDI uwo wuzuza buri sezerano ntanga inshuro nyinshi mu gukiranuka kwanjye kw'iteka kugira ngo byose bibe yego na amena kandi NDI Imana Ihoraho yo Gukiranuka idacogora!

Nyamara, bimeze bityo ko ikiyoka kizabyuka byuzuye kandi inyamaswa yo mu nyanja yarihishe kugira ngo izabyuke byuzuye mu bo izobora, kandi izabyuka ivuye mu ivu mu gihe cyayo cyagenwe. Yasahuye kandi itegura irimbuka ryuzuye rya Babuloni y'Ibanga, ndetse ikorana bya hafi n'inyamaswa yo mu isi kugira ngo bategure kandi bashyire mu bikorwa irimbuka ryabo, kandi niko mbahaye kubigeraho. Kandi isi yaroshywe na maraya mukuru kandi ntishobora kongera kwiyumvisha isi itagira igitugu cya maraya mukuru yakandamije abantu bo ku isi kandi yonona irema ryanjye n'umwanda w'ubusambanyi bwe ndetse ahawe isi avuye muri icyo gikombe cya zahabu giteye ishozi kiri mu maboko ye.

Nuko rero, ndaje kubaburira, Yewe Isirayeli, ku kiyobe gikomeye kigiye gushyira isi mu ntambara yeruye n'akaduruvayo, ndetse n'ikiyobe bitoje bakoresheje ikoranabuhanga ryabo kuko bose ari abiyoberanya biyoberanya, kandi mu kiyobe cyabo umuhanuzi wabo w'ibinyoma azinjira mu gihugu nka Moshiach w'ibinyoma wa Esawu.

Kandi Esawu ariyoberanya akiyisha muri Isirayeli, ndetse akiyita Isirayeli mu gihe bashaka kwivanga muri Isirayeli, ndetse bahisha kamere yabo nyakuri mu bo biyoberanyamo. (Ni urumamfu mu ngano kandi Umwami w'Ibisarurwa azabarandura byuzuye.) Nuko rero ikiyoka kirabyuka kuko ari isaha yacyo, kandi Kristo Yesu azakura mu buryo butunguranye abizerwa muri Kristo ku isi, (abazima n'abapfuye) hanyuma urubanza ruzagera byuzuye kuri Babuloni y'Ibanga ndetse wa maraya mukuru utukura wahanuwe na Yohana Uhishurirwa muri izi yubile nyinshi zashize.

Kandi NDI Umukuru w'Iminsi, ndetse Umwami w'Imyuka kandi ndahamya, kugira ngo bose basobanukirwe imvugo yanjye: Icyabayeho kizasubira kubaho: kandi icyakozwe, kizasubira gukorwa, kandi nta gishya munsi y'izuba. Nuko rero bimeze bityo ko abaguye bakoze kugira ngo barimbure Adamu ku isi kuva mu ntangiriro kugira ngo bayitwarire, ndetse n'ubusitani bwanjye bw'ubugingo aho nkorera kandi nita ku bugingo bw'abantu kugira ngo mbaroore mu kubora muri Kristo Yesu uwo naremye nka Adamu wa nyuma. Kandi ni muri Mesiya wawe, Yewe Isirayeli, Isirayeli y'Imana izakoranyirizwa hamwe byuzuye kandi icungurwe nk'umuntu mushya umwe waremwe mu ishusho y'Umwami n'Umucunguzi wawe, waguze gucungurwa kwawe mu maraso ye bwite y'impongano.

Nuko rero sobanukirwa Yewe Isirayeli ko Yesu w'i Nazareti ari Yeshua HaMashiach ari we kandi Kristo Yesu (sobanukirwa ishimangirwa ry'amazina ye kuko agakiza kawe kuzuye) nuko wumve Amagambo ye mu kumvira ku bw'ubugingo bwawe bwite, kuko ntimugifite igihe cyo kujya impaka n'Umuremyi wanyu cyangwa ngo mwange Mesiya wanyu w'ukuri.

Kandi byanditswe nk'uko nabitangaje, nuko rero natumye Yeshua HaMashiach ahishurira intumwa ye Pawulo ibimwerekeyeho: Icyatumye Elohim imushyira hejuru cyane, ikamuha izina riruta amazina yose, Kugira ngo kw'izina rya Yesu amavi yose apfukame, ay'ibyo mu ijuru, n'ay'ibyo mu isi, n'ay'ibyo munsi y'isi, Kandi indimi zose ziature ko Yesu Kristo ari Umwami, ngo Imana Data wa twese ihimbazwe. Nuko rero, njye YHWH, NDI UWONDI Ukomeye, nongeye kwemeza ukuri kw'aya Magambo kugira ngo ukwizera kwanyu kudateguzwa.

Ikiyobe gikomeye kiri hano kandi benshi bazagifatiwemo kandi benshi bazagwa mu birimi by'umuriro, ndetse n'abo bose amazina yabo atigeze yandikwa mu Gitabo cy'Ubugingo cy'Umwana w'Intama kuva mu ntangiriro. Kuko, njye El Shaddai, NDI uzi iherezo uhereye mu ntangiriro kandi nubwo, mpa buri bugingo amahirwe yo kubaho ubuzima bukiranuka imbere yanjye, nzi abazahitemo kuzuza ibyaha byabo byuzuye mu bazaza kuri Kristo kugira ngo baremwe bundi bushya mu gukiranuka kandi imanza zanjye ziratunganye mu gukiranuka kwanjye.

Ariko wowe Yewe Isirayeli, nta gukiranuka ufite kuko wanze Imana Ihoraho yo Gukiranuka kuko wanga Umwana wanjye w'ikinege natumye ngo abababarire ibyaha nk'umwana w'intama w'Imana, nuko rero, ibyaha byawe biragumaho. Nuko rero ndakugira inama, Isirayeli, umva Amagambo ya NDI UWONDI Ukomeye muri iki gihe cya nyuma kandi wemere igitambo cyanjye!

Yego, Isirayeli, nagutanzeho igitambo gitunganye kuko Adamu yononekaye mu butungane kandi ubugingo bw'umubiri buri mu maraso. Nuko rero icyaha gihita mu maraso ari yo bugingo bw'umubiri kiva kuri Data kijya ku rubyaro rwe ku buryo baba abagabo cyangwa abagore baragwa kubora kuko Adamu yononekaye iteka ryose. Mu mbabazi zanjye z'iteka n'urukundo rwanjye natanze ibyo bidashoboka ko ikindi kiremwa cyose kibikora: Naremye kandi ntanga Umwana wanjye w'ikinege, uwo Mose ategeka ko mumwumvira, ku bw'ibyaha by'abantu bose, baba Abayudaya cyangwa abanyamahanga, baba ababata cyangwa abidegemvya.

Nyamara bimeze bityo Isirayeli ko abanzi bawe bararikira irimbuka ryawe kuko bifuza kugira igihe cyabo cya zahabu nanone, ndetse n'igihe cya zahabu batazigera basubirana kuko aho kugira igihe cyabo cya zahabu cyuzuye ibyaha byo ku bushake mbere y'umwuzure aho ibizira by'amoko yose byagendagendaga, ahubwo bazagira urubanza rukomeye n'igihano uko ubwami bwabo burimburwa byuzuye. Kandi vuba aha bizaba ko inyamaswa n'umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma bazajugunywa ari bazima mu nyanja yaka umuriro n'amazuku kandi Satani azabohwa imyaka igihumbi mu bubabare n'ubwoba bwinshi bw'umurage we uje mu birimi by'umuriro by'iteka by'igihano aho inyo yabo idapfa.

Kandi ikiyoka kiboshywe ku isi kugira ngo ntikizongere kwinjira mu ijuru ukundi ahubwo bigumishijwe munsi y'ikirere biboshywe ku isi ku bw'urubanza bararikiye, kandi ikiyoka gifite umujinya mwinshi mu gutsindwa kwacyo, kuko gitsinzwe byuzuye mu maboko ya Yeshua HaMashiach ari we rubuto rwasezeranyijwe rwa nyina w'abazima bose, kuko ari ko nasezeranyije Adamu na Eva mu rukundo mbakunda. Nuko rero yemerewe gusa kuzuza imigambi yanjye y'iteka mbere y'uko yoherezwa iteka mu birimi by'umuriro, kandi nzashyiraho ikimenyetso, kandi ntibazibukwa cyangwa ngo bavugwe kuko niko hazaza hawe h'isezerano hameze aho ubohowe abagukandamiza iteka ryose kandi mu mahoro y'iteka kandi atunganye uzabaho nupfukamira Mesiya wawe w'ukuri, ndetse Yeshua HaMashiach, kuko nta yindi nzira ihari!

Nuko rero ubu ngiye kubaha gusobanukirwa ibintu bigiye kuba, kandi rwose umutego watezwe n'umutega ugiye guhishurwa, ndetse n'umutego watezwe n'ikiyoka gishaka gufata inuma zanjye z'agaciro, ndetse n'ubwoko bwanjye bwihariye niherereje. Kandi NDI uwo watumye inyoni ya Nowa igaruka ifite ikibabi cy'umunzenze mu kanwa kayo, kandi nanjye NDI uwo wohereje amasezerano yanjye y'amahoro y'iteka mu maso ha Yesu Kristo, Mesiya wawe wazutse Yewe Isirayeli!

Nuko rero, sobanukirwa ibihe ubayemo. Isinagogi ya Satani igendana n'ibimenyetso n'ibitangaza byinshi by'ibinyoma, kandi barwana bakoresheje ikiyobe. Bakora imihango yabo ku mugaragaro, yitwikiriye nk'imyidagaduro n'amakuru n'amakoraniro ya politiki. Barangije igikorwa cyabo cyo guhitamo, aho Esawu ubu yahisemo umwana w'intama we mu Isinagogi ya Satani ku bw'ikiyobe cyabo gituka Imana bashaka gukoresha batega imitego. Ariko inyamaswa yo mu isi si umwana w'intama w'Imana basuzugura mu bupfu no gutuka Imana, ahubwo umuhanuzi wabo w'ibinyoma bihitiyemo ufite amahembe abiri nk'umwana w'intama, ariko avuga nk'ikiyoka.

Nuko rero natumye Umwana wanjye, Yesu Kristo Ukiranuka, ahishura muri The Choice is Yours (Hitamo) igihe Baraba yakiraga igikomere ku mutwe nk'aho cyari icyo gupfa kandi icyo gikorwa cyari gifite ibisobanuro byinshi atahishuye icyo gihe, ariko byari muri iri koraniro, ndetse n'umuhango we wo guhitamo aho bavuga ko, Yahweh ubwe yamutoboye ugutwi amugira umugaragu wanjye w'imana. Nuko rero, ndarahira izina ryanjye, ndetse YHWH NDI UWONDI Ukomeye, ko ntamugize umugaragu wanjye, cyangwa ngo ntange undi Mesiya uwo ari we wese uretse Umwana wanjye w'ikinege, ndetse Yeshua HaMashiach, ari we mwana w'intama w'Imana wenyine nzohereza. Nuko rero igerageza ryo kumwica ryakoreshejwe nk'ikimwe mu bimenyetso n'ibitangaza byinshi by'ibinyoma aho uyu mwiyoberanya yiyoberanya. Kandi kuri uyu mwiyoberanya si uwa kamere yanjye cyangwa umukumbi wanjye kandi ni umwanzi wanjye.

Nyamara, benshi muri Babuloni y'Ibanga biyitaga ko Yesu Kristo ari Umwami wabo ubu bataye Umwami wabo bakurikira Baraba, uvuga nk'ikiyoka nyamara akaba umwana w'intama ufite amahembe abiri. Kandi nyamara benshi mu bagize Baraba Umwami wabo bazasigirwa umuriro w'umutunganya kuko agakiza kabo katuzuye byuzuye kuko batabaga kuri Kristo byuzuye n'imitima yuzuye ukwizera n'ukwihana ahubwo n'irari ubutumwa bwiza bw'iterambere bubyara. Kuko Isinagogi ya Satani yononnye ibyanditswe byabo byera ku bwumvikane bwabo kandi ubu babaye Abaziyoni. Kandi nyamara benshi muri izo bugingo, bazahabwa icyubahiro cyo kuba abahowe Imana ku bw'agakiza kabo niba bahisemo kwemera agakiza bambaza izina ry'Umwami wabo Yesu Kristo kugeza ku iherezo, kandi nzabakira iteka ryose kandi imibabaro yabo ntizagereranywa n'ubwiza buzabahishurirwamo.

Nuko rero, uyu moshiach w'ibinyoma aragenda akabwiriza mu gihe avuga nk'ikiyoka kandi ariyoberanya akavuga ibintu byinshi bikomeye kandi bituka Imana ndetse na mbere y'uko abyuka byuzuye, kandi azavuga ko azabyuka. Sobanukirwa bwoko bwanjye ko uyu muhanuzi w'ibinyoma akaba n'inyamaswa yo mu isi ikorera Isinagogi ya Satani ubu yatoranyijwe nk'umwana w'intama wabo. Kandi bizaba ko umutwe we uzagaragara nk'uwakomeretse bikomeye, ariko igikomere cye cyishe cyarakize, kandi isi yose izatangara ikurikire inyamaswa. Nuko rero uyu mutekamutwe azabyuka.

Mu kubyuka kwe azavuga ko yitangiye nk'umwana w'intama w'Imana azanira amahoro Isirayeli nk'inuma yabo ibazanira ikibabi cy'umunzenze! Kandi atari Isirayeli gusa, ahubwo n'isi ubwayo kandi bizagaragara ko ubu yagarutse kuzana amahoro, ariko inzira z'amahoro ntazizi kandi mu rubanza rwa Babuloni y'Ibanga intambara yeruye izaduka. Kuko umwuka w'ibinyoma w'ihumure uturutse ku kiyoka wicaye ku isi, kandi nohereje ukuyoba gukomeye kugira ngo abishimira gukiranirwa bose bazemere ikinyoma kugira ngo bose bacirwe urubanza bishimira gukiranirwa. Kuko igihe bazavuga bati amahoro n'umutekano, nibwo irimbuka ritunguranye rizabageraho kandi ntibazarokoka.

Kandi benshi muri Babuloni y'Ibanga, ndetse n'abemera ibinyoma bye bagakurikira amagambo ye meza n'imvugo ze nziza batwawe kandi bafatwa n'amahoro y'ibinyoma aho batekereza ko ubuzima bwabo buryoshye kandi bw'ibyaha buzakomeza aho uyu munsi ari nk'ejo kandi ejo hazaba nk'uyu munsi kandi benshi bazavuza induru, borere, kandi barire ko ibyiringiro byabo ubu bibakuweho, kandi bazarwanya benegihugu babo kugeza ku gupfa kandi icyo gihe cyose igitero cyabo gitunguranye cyuzuye kizaba cyarekuwe byuzuye, ndetse n'uko umukumiri wanjye akuwe ku isi kandi Babuloni y'Ibanga izagwa ku gihano cyikubye kabiri mu isaha imwe uko amakuba menshi azagwa.

Kandi Isinagogi ya Satani izagira intambara yabo bakoresheje mu kwica inzirakarengane mu kiyobe cyabo kugira ngo bashotore kandi abaramya kibe zabo z'umukara baziyushya akuya mu bwicanyi, ndetse n'ubwicanyi buzanywe nkana n'ikiyobe gikomeye kandi bazabyuka bavuye mu ivu. Kandi abaguye baziyerekana byuzuye baha abantu ubufasha busa n'ubwiza ariko inzira zabo n'ikoranabuhanga ryabo bigamije kugusha bose mu murage wabo mu birimi by'ibihano, batazigera bacika kandi aho bazahabwa inshuro nyinshi ku BURI cyaha cyose bakoze kandi ubutabera buzakorwa. Ariko ni nde mutego utega umutego inyoni ireba?

Nuko rero ndabaha gusobanukirwa, ko urubanza rwa Babuloni y'Ibanga rwateguwe kandi biteguye. Ariko mbere uyu mwana w'intama w'ibinyoma ufite amahembe abiri aziyoberanya nk'umunyakuri mu gihe mu by'ukuri ari umushumba urwaye ubuheri, umwe mu mahembe abiri yo gutegeka, ndetse ihembe rito rya Daniyeli. Kandi azagaragara ko yagerageje gukiza Babuloni y'Ibanga yeza ruswa yayo ariko mu by'ukuri ni umwangizi mukomeye wateze benshi ubu azasukura kandi ashinje ruswa ye yakoresheje akura benshi ibyabo abashyira mu nsi ye. Kandi ubu ibyo byose bidahuye n'imigambi yabo, cyangwa abashobora gutanga ubuhamya bw'imbonankubone bw'ububi bwabo bazakurwaho n'amaboko y'uyu mushumba urwaye ubuheri kandi bashinjwe ibyaha bagikora kandi bayoboramo abandi, kandi ntibazarokoka.

Kandi, wumve ko benshi bafatanya mu byaha byabo kandi bameze nk'urumamfu nka ba bungushumba banduye bazajya mu bwihisho bwabo bihishe mu nda y'isi bahunga umujinya uzaza, kandi bazarimbura ibimenyetso byose by'ubuzima babayemo muri Babuloni y'Ibanga mu buriganya bukomeye, kandi ntibazacika umujinya uzaza! Nyamara, ibyaha byabo bizashyirwa ahagaragara mu buryo bw'uburyarya n'uwo muhanuzi w'ibinyoma kandi azagaragara nk'umukiranutsi n'intungane, ndetse nk'umuntu watumwe n'Imana gukiza Isirayeli bityo umutego ube witeguye gukora. Kandi nyamara, buri gikorwa cyabo cyose nacyanditse mu bitabo byo mu ijuru, kandi nibuka neza buri kantu kose kandi nzi ibyaha byabo byose nanjye nzabishyira ahagaragara kandi bazishyura iteka ryose ibyaha byabo biteye ishozi.

Bityo inyamaswa yo mu nyanja irimo kwitegura kuzamuka nka Mahadi wabo naho inyamaswa yo mu isi irimo kwitegura kuzamuka nk'umwana w'intama w'ikinyoma kuko ni uwuhe mwana w'intama ugira amahembe abiri kandi ukavuga nk'ikiyoka. Nyamara Esawu yiteguye kumwakira mu gihugu ashaka kwiba kandi bazarandurwa burundu n'ukuboko gukomeye kwa Yeshua HaMashiach nahaye ububasha, ndetse nkasangira intebe yanjye y'ubwami n'Umwana wanjye nkunda kandi azamenagura burundu umutwe w'inzoka n'abanzi be bose bazagirwa intebe y'ibirenge bye kuko ari abaguye banze gupfukama ku itegeko ryanjye mu kwigomeka kwabo kandi ni bo bagabye igitero ku byaremwe byanjye mu buhendanyi n'ubugome kandi bazabibazwa iteka ryose.

Bityo ndahiye izina ryanjye bwite nanone ko, Jyewe YHVH NDI WE NDI Ukomeye, ndahiye ko Mesiya wenyine nigeze nohereza muri Isirayeli kandi nzohereza by'ukuri ari umwana wanjye w'ikinege, Yeshua HaMashiach uwo ba So banze kandi namwe mukaba mumwanga kubera ibinyoma byabo mwarazwe biturutse mu mitima yabo yinangiye byanditswe muri Talimudi yanyu ituka Imana. Bityo nje aho muri mu rukundo mbakunda Yemwe Isirayeli kugira ngo mbahishurire byuzuye umutego mwatezwe na Esawu ugishaka kurimbura Yakobo.

Inyamaswa yo mu isi yabaroshyemo nk'umuhanuzi wanyu w'ikinyoma kandi azazamuka aze aho muri nk'umwana w'intama wanyu w'igitambo witangiye Isirayeli kandi aziyita Moshiach wanyu w'ukuri. Nyamara mu by'ukuri yandujwe na Esawu kandi nta Moshiach uzigera akomoka muri Dani. Koko rero, aje kurimbura Yakobo ariko umutego atega uzafata abamukurikira babishaka bityo bazatwarwa iteka ryose n'ibinyoma bye kandi bazemera ikimenyetso cye azashaka guhatira isi kugira ngo ayigire imbata kandi ayirimbure.

Bityo ndababwira nti, Yemwe Isirayeli, mupfukame mwumvira Mesiya wanyu w'ukuri waje aho muri nk'umwana w'intama w'Imana, uwo ba So banze kubera Esawu. Mupfukame imbere y'Umwami w'abami n'Umutware w'abatware kandi mwemere agakiza kanjye kugira ngo mbagungure mu muvumo w'amategeko kuko nta muntu n'umwe ukiranuka unzaho binyuze kuri Mose, keretse umuntu Kristo Yesu nahinduye iteka ryose, kandi mu buryo bw'umubiri ntakimenywa. Kuko, namuhaye icyubahiro nk'uko Farawo yahaye icyubahiro Yozefu bagasangira intebe ye y'ubwami, keretse ko mu ntebe y'ubwami yari akomeye kuruta Yozefu kandi akagumana ubutware bwe.

Kuko:

NDI uwo uhereye kera kose kugeza iteka ryose kandi ntawuruta NDI WE NDI Ukomeye!

NDI uwo wohereje aya Magambo ku bwoko bwanjye nkunda kugira ngo nze aho muri kandi mbakureho ubuhumyi bwanyu bw'igice ubwo imitima yanyu izaba ihindukiye byuzuye kuri Mesiya wanyu! Ndetse Mesiya wanyu w'ukuri, Kristo Yesu Umwami wazutse kandi wahawe icyubahiro!

NDI Imana y'Iteka ryose yo Gukiranuka itananiwa, kandi ndabaza nti, mbese hari ikintu cyananira ukuboko kwanjye gukomeye kugikora?

Nuko rero ndabinginga, Yemwe Isirayeli, ngo mwumvire ijwi rya YHVH ndetse Elohimu wanyu wari umwungushumba wa ba So binyuze mu kuboko k'umugaragu wanjye Mose narokoye igihe yakuwe mu mazi ya Nili akajyanwa mu mutekano no gutegurirwa ubwami mu nzu ya Farawo uwo yatsinze mu kumvira YHVH!

NDI we, ndetse YHVH NDI WE NDI Ukomeye, urahira izina ryanjye bwite kandi nemeza byuzuye mu kuri kw'iteka ko Yeshua HaMashiach yashohoje amategeko ya Mose nka Pasika itunganye y'ibihe byose natanze mu buryo butunganye n'ukuboko kwanjye bwite, bityo nta kindi gitambo kigikenewe.

NDI YHVH, NDI WE NDI Ukomeye, kandi ndahiye izina ryanjye bwite ko ari jye wazuye Yesu w'i Nazareti mu bapfuye nyuma y'uko yitangiye nk'umwana w'intama w'Imana, ndetse igitambo nemeye ibihe byose n'iteka ryose ku bwo gucungurwa kwa Adamu nk'umuntu mushya muri Kristo Yesu ari we Yeshua HaMashiach Mesiya wanyu w'ukuri!

Kandi NDI we wemeza ukuri kw'aya Magambo nohereje mu Butumwa Bwiza bw'Iteka bwahishuwe nanjye ubwanjye, na Yeshua HaMashiach ku ntumwa yacu ikomeye yateguwe na Yeshua HaMashiach mu bihana byinshi no kwiyeza ku bw'imigambi yanjye y'iteka kandi mu budahemuka yagarukiye Yeshua HaMashiach bityo arasubizwa!

Bityo ndabatumira bwoko bwanjye, ndetse Isirayeli nkunda urukundo rw'iteka, kwifatanya n'Isirayeli y'Imana no kuza kuri Yahweh, ndetse Imana ya Aburahamu, n'iya Isaka, n'iya Yakobo binyuze kuri Yeshua HaMashiach kugira ngo mukoranirizwe iteka mu Isirayeli y'Imana kandi mungarukire binyuze kuri Mesiya wanyu w'ukuri, ndetse Yeshua HaMashiach. Kandi nzabakira iwanjye mbasubize umurage wanyu n'ingororano biri mu kuboko gukomeye kwa Kristo Yesu.

Nta rindi zina ryatanzwe abantu bashobora kwegereramo YHVH, NDI WE NDI Ukomeye kuko ari ko natangaje, nuko rero musome umwana kugira ngo atarakara mukarimburirwa mu nzira.

Dore! Ibihe ni ibi kandi isarura ry'umuganura rirageze kandi bazakoranirizwa ku Mwami wabo kandi bazahabwa icyubahiro ku bw'imigambi yanjye y'iteka.

NDI UKO NDI kandi NDI YHVH nuko rero mwumvire ijwi ryanjye ry'iteka kandi muze kuri Data w'Imicyo wamaze gutegura inzira yanyu.
