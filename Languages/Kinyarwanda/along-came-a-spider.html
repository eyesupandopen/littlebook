Ubuhamya Bwibiri: Isirayeli
Ibisharira Bityoshye
25 Ugushyingo 2024

Hanyuma Haza Igitagangurirwa

Nimwumve, nimwumve, Magambo yanjye Yewe Isirayeli, mwunamishe imitima yanyu mu kumvira ijwi ryanjye, mwakire mu bugwaneza Amagambo ya Yeshua HaMashiach kugira ngo binyuze mu Magambo y'Ubutumwa Bwiza bw'Iteka mubone agakiza kandi mugere ku bugingo buhoraho muri Mesiya wanyu w'ukuri ubarwanirira by'ukuri mu rukundo. Nuko rero nje kubaburira, ndetse n'aba Yakobo n'abasoma bose bakemera amagambo y'Ubutumwa Bwiza bw'Iteka mu kwizera gukiza kugira ngo bizere Yeshua HaMashiach ari we Yesu Kristo, kandi bizere Yahweh NDI UWONDI Ukomeye, uwo mu mutima we Ubutumwa Bwiza bw'Iteka bwahishwe kugeza mu bihe byo guhishurwa kwabwo, ari na cyo cyatumye Yohana Uhishurirwa afatanisha ibimenyetso amajwi y'inkuba indwi yumvise. Nuko rero nje kubaburira iby'igitagangurirwa cyaboheye urushundura rwacyo kigafata isi mu maboko yacyo; ndetse kigaburirwa n'amaraso y'abandi, kugira ngo mwirinde inshundura zacyo nyinshi ntimufatwe mu maboko yacyo yica.

Nuko rero, mwunamishe imitima yanyu mu kumvira kandi mwumve amagambo ya Mesiya wanyu w'ukuri, ari we Yeshua HaMashiach! Kuko mu by'ukuri nje gutuma umunyabwenge muri mwe arushaho kugira ubwenge kugira ngo mumenye ibihe murimo kandi musobanukirwe abanzi banyu na kamere yabo n'inzira zabo z'ibinyoma; kuko bagenda mu ibanga rikomeye no mu bwihisho kandi barwanisha uburiganya. Kuko bose ari indyarya ziyoberanya, kandi igitagangurirwa kigenda mu ibanga rikomeye no mu bwihisho kugeza igihe umuhigo wacyo ufatiwe mu rushundura rwacyo, urwo igitagangurirwa kizahora gikoresha mu migambi yacyo mibisha.

Nuko rero, nje kubaburira iby'igitagangurirwa no kubereka inzira zacyo kugira ngo mwirinde ubumara bwacyo kandi mwikure mu maboko yacyo agagaza kandi yica. Ariko se ni ikihe gitagangurirwa cyigeze kigira ubwami n'ishyanga? Kandi se ni ikihe gitagangurirwa cyigeze cyifatanya n'ihuriro ry'abaguye, ndetse kigakora uruhare rwacyo mu byaha by'ubugome byishimye? Kandi se ni ikihe gitagangurirwa cyigeze cyiyitirira imyirondoro y'abafashwe mu rushundura rwacyo? Nyamara uko ni ko igitagangurirwa nje kubaburira kimeze, kandi mu by'ukuri, igitagangurirwa ni kimwe mu bice byinshi by'ubwami bw'abanzi kandi ni igitagangurirwa cy'ubumara kandi cyica by'ukuri.

Kuko igitagangurirwa kibohera urushundura rwacyo mu bwihisho n'uburiganya. Ndetse n'urushundura rw'uburiganya n'urushundura rw'imyenda ni rwo igitagangurirwa kibohera, kandi urushundura rwacyo ubu ruzingiye isi yose mu maboko yacyo y'ubwihisho. Tekereza ku gitagangurirwa mu miterere yacyo karemano, kibohera urushundura rwacyo mu bwihisho ahantu gishobora gufatira umuhigo wacyo ndetse n'igihe umuhigo wacyo uguye mu rushundura rwacyo utabizi. Kandi uko uwo cyafashe arwana ngo yikure mu rushundura yafatiwemo atabizi akarushaho kwiboha, igitagangurirwa gihita gitangira gukora kikamutera ubumara bwacyo mu gihe kizingira umuhigo wacyo mu rushundura rwacyo. Hanyuma igitagangurirwa kibika umuhigo wacyo ku bw'imigambi yacyo kandi kikagaburirwa buhoro buhoro n'uwo cyafashe kugeza nta gisigaye, kigasiga uwo cyafashe ari umusaka rwose kugeza ubwo uwo cyafashe atakibaho.

Nyamara uko ni ko inzira z'igitagangurirwa karemano cyaremewe imigambi ya Yahweh zimeze, keretse ni nde muri mwe wigeze abona igitagangurirwa gikora gifatanyije n'ubwami n'ishyanga ry'ibindi bitagangurirwa; nyamara abaguye bahurije hamwe ibitagangurirwa byabo muri ibi bihe by'imperuka. Nuko rero nje kubaburira iby'ubwoko bukomoka kuri Yafeti, wabyaye Gomeri, naho kuri Gomeri hakomoka Ashikenazi, naho kuri Ashikenazi hakomoka ishyanga kuko Ashikenazi ari mu rutonde rw'amahanga mu byiciro bya kera ndetse no mu minsi abantu babagaho munsi y'isezerano rya Nowa, isezerano abantu baretse; nyamara hariho Aburamu wizerwa washakaga inzira za Yahweh igihe isi yongeraga kwigomeka ku muremyi wayo. Nyamara nubwo, umuntu wa kamere yanze isezerano rya Yahweh; Yahweh ni uwo kwizerwa mu masezerano ye uko byagenda kose. Kandi nta sezerano na rimwe mu yo Yahweh yagiranye na Nowa (wari umukiranutsi imbere ya Yahweh) ryigeze rigwa hasi ridashohojwe kandi yose azaba yego na amena iteka ryose kuko Yahweh ahora ari uwo kwizerwa mu buryo bwuzuye!

Nyuma y'uko isi irengerwa n'umwuzure mu minsi ya Nowa, isi yagabanyijwe hakoreshejwe ubufindo bw'umurage mu bahungu ba Nowa mu minsi ya Pelegi, nuko Yafeti ajya mu murage we mu bihugu byo mu majyaruguru. Kandi abantu bari bafite ururimi rumwe, nuko abahungu ba Hamu bakoranira mu gihugu cyabaye Babuloni. Kandi ibikomangoma bya Nimurodi bijya inama hamwe na Puti, Misirayimu, Kushi, na Kanani n'imiryango yabo kugira ngo bubake umujyi wabo n'umunara ukomeye muri wo ndetse n'umunara muremure kugira ngo bashobore kurwanya ijuru kandi kugira ngo bikoranyirize hamwe kugira ngo biyubakire izina mu kwigomeka ku itegeko rya Yahweh ryo kororoka no kugwira kugira ngo bongere kuzuza isi. Ariko uko ni ko inzira z'umuntu wa kamere utagira ukwizera zimeze, we mu irari rye n'ubwibone bwe akorera icyaha bityo agakorera Se w'icyaha; ari we satani, binyuze mu kutumvira kwa muntu kuri Yahweh bitewe n'icyaha kiri mu mubiri w'umuntu wa kamere.

Kandi mu rubyaro rwa Nimurodi rwakuriye mu gukiranirwa havugwa ko mu banyabyaha hava ibyaha byinshi. Nuko Nimurodi yanduza isi mu nzira z'abaguye kandi mu bapfumu be n'abaraguzi be ni ho havuye amashule y'amabanga ya kera aho abapfumu b'abaguye bigishaga amabanga yabo y'ubusa ku bazajugunywa mu muriro iteka ryose mu byaha bya ba Satani, kandi bazabiyungaho mu murage wabo w'iteka. Kandi Nimurodi yazamutse mu bwami bwa mbere nyuma y'umwuzure wa Nowa, ndetse aho Nimurodi yategekaga isi mu gukiranirwa; ndetse n'ubutegetsi abaguye bashaka kwiharira byuzuye ku isi, nyamara nta gihe cya zahabu bazagira ahubwo bazagira urubanza rukomeye.

Nuko Yahweh mu rubanza rwe atuma isi yiyasa imira kimwe cya gatatu cy'umunara wabo, n'umuriro umanuka mu ijuru utwika ikindi kimwe cya gatatu cy'umunara, naho kimwe cya gatatu cyemererwa gusigara nk'urwibutso rwo kubarwanya. Ni cyo cyatumye witwa Babeli, kuko ari ho Yahweh yasobanyirije indimi z'isi yose: kandi aho ni ho Yahweh yabatatanyirije ku isi yose. Kandi amahanga menshi n'amoko menshi yanze kandi yibagirwa Yahweh winjiye mu isezerano na Nowa, nuko bibagirwa isezerano Nowa yagiranye na Yahweh hakurikijwe umurage wabo kandi hakurikijwe indimi bavugaga bibagirwa isezerano rya Yahweh na Nowa, nuko bahindukirira inzira z'abaguye kuko nta yandi mahitamo. (Nyamara Aburamu yabaye Aburahamu kandi umukiranutsi azabeshwaho no kwizera!)

I Babuloni Nimurodi yakoze ibibi byinshi ndetse akora ibishushanyo bisengwa byinshi yigira imana kandi akora ubutatu bwe butubaha Imana, ndetse ubutatu budahera, bugamije gukoba ubumana bw'ukuri n'urubyaro rwasezeranyijwe rw'umugore no kuyobya abana b'abantu mu gukiranirwa. Kandi uko ni ko Nimurodi yashinze amashule ye yitwa ay'amabanga uko ubumenyi bw'abaguye bwongeraga gukwirakwira no gutwikira isi kandi abantu bongera guhindura ukuri kwa Yahweh ikinyoma basenga kandi bakorera ibyaremwe kuruta umuremyi uhimbazwa iteka ryose, Amena!

Kandi uko abantu batatanyijwe bava i Babuloni ni ko n'ubumenyi bw'ibinyoma n'inzira z'abaguye byatatanyijwe hamwe na bo, ndetse n'imihango yabo yose iteye ishozi kandi itubaha Imana y'abarya inyama n'amaraso n'abanywa amaraso ya Adamu aho ubugingo bw'umubiri buba; ndetse n'imigenzo yononekaye rwose kandi iteye ishozi Yahweh yabujije rimwe, bose, n'iteka ryose kuko Adamu yaremwe mu ishusho ya Yahweh kandi ubugingo bw'umubiri buri mu maraso. Nyamara bakomeza mu nzira zabo ziteye ishozi kandi zitubaha Imana kugeza n'uyu munsi, kandi imigenzo yabo n'imihango yabo byararindwe mu basenga abadayimoni bakunamira ibishushanyo bisengwa bitavuga kandi izi ni zo nzira z'igitagangurirwa, nyamara igitagangurirwa kiyoberanya nk'aho ari Yakobo w'umukiranutsi uwo nta mugabane cyangwa umurage gifiteho.

Nuko inzira z'ibibi zishinga imizi mu nzira z'abantu b'amahanga ukundi. Kandi kuri Gomeri hakomoka Ashikenazi naho kuri Ashikenazi mu gihe runaka hakomoka ishyanga rikomeye ry'abantu babaye abimukira babaga kandi bakazerera mu kibaya cya Uburayi na Aziya n'inkengero zacyo ndetse n'aho bubatse ubwami bwabo. Ndetse n'ubwami bwabo, bwakuriye mu gukiranirwa kandi bugwa mu maboko y'abanzi babo uko byagenda kose. Ndetse n'abanzi babo babagabyeho igitero kubera gukiranirwa kwabo kandi aho kwemera Yesu w'i Nazareti no kuva mu byaha ahubwo biyoberanya nka Yakobo, kandi muri Yakobo ntiwigeze ubamo umuryango wa Ashikenazi wa Yafeti. Nuko rero banze Yesu w'i Nazareti kuko bakomoka kuri se satani kandi irari rye ni ryo babohewe gukora iteka ryose.

Kandi mu bantu ba Ashikenazi bafite inzira mbi kandi z'uburiganya havuye abantu, keretse mu bwoko bwa Adamu ntibari bo byuzuye, ahubwo urumamfu rubi rwabibwe mu ngano za Yahweh bizononnye iteka ryose mu gukiranirwa. Ndetse n'ababyawe na se satani kuko imbuto ya satani isigaye, nubwo nzabarandura rwose nkabajugunya mu itanura nk'uko Yahweh yabivuze. Kandi kuri Ashikenazi hakomoka igitagangurirwa ariko igitagangurirwa karemano ntabwo cyari cyo, ahubwo ishyanga ry'ibitagangurirwa biteye ishozi ndetse byahinduwe biva mu ishusho ya Yahweh umuremyi wabyo, bibohewe iteka ryose ku irari rya Se satani uwo bazakorera irari rye. Nuko ibitagangurirwa biratatana, ndetse uko bahungaga amaboko y'abanzi b'ugukiranirwa bahagaze barwanya inzira z'igitagangurirwa. Nuko Ashikenazi yanga Yeshua HaMashiach, uwo nta mugabane cyangwa umurage bafiteho.

Nuko mu by'ukuri abantu bayobejwe n'abaguye kuva Adamu yagwa ava mu mimerere ye itagira icyaha yaremewemo mu busitani bwa Edeni kandi kwangirika gukwirakwira ku isi yose binyuze mu muryango wa Kayini. Uko kwangirika abarinzi baguye barushijeho kwangiza igihe bamanukaga ku Musozi wa Herumoni mu minsi ya Yaredi. Nuko rero, Yahweh yoza kwangirika kwabo ku maso y'isi muri wa mwuzure ukomeye mu minsi ya Nowa nyamara abaguye bongera gushyiraho inzira zabo nyuma y'umwuzure kuko uko ni ko Yahweh yemereye abantu kwihitiramo. Kuko by'ukuri, by'ukuri, Yahweh yahaye ibyo yaremye byose ubushake bwisanzuye mu byaremwe bye umudendezo wo guhitamo niba bazabaho mu nzira za Yahweh cyangwa bagahitamo gukorera ba Satani mu kwigomeka kwabo uko kwigomeka kuri hafi kurangira. Kuko njyewe, ndetse Yeshua HaMashiach, ndi we uzamenagura umutwe w'inzoka, ndetse n'uzarimbura rwose ubwami bwayo ntibuzongere kubyuka ukundi.

Uko ni ko bimeze kandi uko ni ko kuri nigishije ku byerekeye ubwami bwo mu ijuru igihe nazaga muri Isirayeli nk'Umwana w'Intama w'Imana mu gihe cyanjye mu mubiri i Yudaya. Kuko bityo, byanditswe ngo: Ubwami bwo mu ijuru bugereranywa n'umuntu wabibye imbuto nziza mu murima we. Ariko abantu basinziriye, umwanzi we araza, abiba urumamfu mu ngano, arigendera. Kandi amababi amaze gushibuka, akera imbuto, urumamfu na rwo ruragaragara. Nuko abagaragu b'nyir'urugo baraza, baramubwira bati, Databuja, ntiwabibye imbuto nziza mu murima wawe? None se urumamfu rwaturutse he? Arababwira ati, Umuntu w'umugome ni we wabikoze. Nuko abagaragu baramubwira bati, None se urashaka ko tugenda tukarurandura? Ariko arababwira ati, Oya, kugira ngo mutarandurana n'ingano mu gihe murandura urumamfu. Mureke bikurane byombi kugeza mu isarura, kandi mu gihe cy'isarura nzabwira abasaruzi nti, Mubanze muteranye urumamfu, muruhambire mu miba kugira ngo rutwikwe: ariko ingano muzihunike mu kigega cyanjye.

Ndetse bityo kuko ingano zunama mu kumvira mu gihe urumamfu ruhagarara rwemye mu kwigomeka kubi. Sela!

Nyamara uko ni ko ibihe byo gukoranya byageze, kandi nzakoranyiriza ingano zanjye mu kigega cya Yahweh kuko igihe cyo gusarura umuganura kigeze. Kandi nuko bizasohora ko nzarandura rwose urumamfu nkaruboha, kandi ruzajugunywa mu muriro mu rubanza rwarwo. Nuko rero musobanukirwe isi mubamo ndetse n'intambara mwavukiyemo. Ndetse n'intambara y'imbuto yarwanwe kuva mu busitani bwa Edeni aho Yahweh yatangarije intambara ku nzoka n'abaguye kubera gukiranirwa kwabo no kwangiza ubusitani bwa Yahweh. Iyo ntambara izarangira mu gutsindwa kudasubirwaho ku bigometse bose muri ubwo buryo hamwe n'abaguye kandi umurage wabo w'amazuku yaka umuriro urabategereje.

Nuko Yahweh aratangaza ati: Nzashyira urwango hagati yawe n'umugore no hagati y'urubyaro rwawe n'urubyaro rwe, kandi ruzagukomeretsa agatsinsino nawe uzarukomeretsa umutwe. Kandi uko ni ko agatsinsino kanjye kakomeretse mu kubambwa kwanjye igihe nitangaga nk'Ipasika itunganye nk'Umwana w'Intama w'Imana ku bushake mu maboko y'abanzi banjye b'abagome. Kandi ndi urubyaro rwasezeranyijwe rw'umugore ruzamenagura rwose umutwe wa Satani kandi nzagira abanzi banjye intebe y'ibirenge byanjye, kuko ndi Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda kandi mu gukiranuka ndaje guca urubanza no kurwana!

Nuko rero, musobanukirwe ko intambara y'imbuto yatsinzwe byuzuye muri Kristo Yesu ndetse nk'uko nategetse Petero kwandika iyi yubile myinshi ishize: Mwarabyaye ubwa kabiri, ntimubyawe n'imbuto zibora, ahubwo mubyawe n'izitabora, binyuze mu ijambo ry'Imana rizima kandi rihoraho iteka ryose. Kandi nanone nategetse Yohana Uhishurirwa kwandika mu rwandiko rwe ati: Umuntu wese wabyawe n'Imana, ntakora icyaha: kuko imbuto yayo iguma muri we, kandi ntashobora gukora icyaha, kuko yabyawe n'Imana. Muri ibi abana b'Imana baragaragara, n'abana ba satani: umuntu wese udakora gukiranuka, si uw'Imana, cyangwa udakunda mwene se. Nuko rero, abashyizweho ikimenyetso muri Kristo bose ntibashobora kwangizwa muri ubwo buryo kandi ntibashobora gukora icyaha aho bahinduwe bava ku kimenyetso cy'Imana nzima, ndetse Yahweh uwo barimo. Nuko rero, ntimwemere ko ubwo bupfu bubahagarika umutima ko ababyawe ubwa kabiri n'umwuka wanjye wera w'ubwana kandi bagashyirwaho ikimenyetso cya Yahweh bashobora gukurwa mu kuboko kwanjye gukomeye mu buryo ubwo ari bwo bwose. Nuko rero, ku bageze ku gakiza agakiza kanyu kararinzwe.

Nuko rero nzagaragaza inzira z'igitagangurirwa, kandi nzabihishura byose, nubwo ibitagangurirwa bikunda kugenda mu bwihisho bukomeye, nzashyira ahagaragara inzira zabyo. Kuko ibi bitagangurirwa byahindutse ari byo byiyitirira imyirondoro y'abo bipangira kandi bikica. Ibi ni ibitagangurirwa bitungwa n'ubugome bukomeye kandi bikunda gutoteza umuhigo wabyo nta yindi mpamvu usibye guhaza irari rya Se bakorera kandi bakaba ari urubyaro rwe. Ibi bitagangurirwa byahindutse mvugaho ni abambuzi, n'abajura, n'abicanyi ndetse n'abiyita abo batari bo (kandi bafite ibyaha byinshi cyane) batabasha guhaga kandi bararikira urupfu rwa Adamu kugira ngo babone ibihe byabo bya zahabu iteka ryose hamwe n'abaguye. Nuko rero ibitagangurirwa byakwiriye ku isi yose kandi biboha inshundura zabyo mu bwihisho n'ibanga rikomeye.

Ndetse n'urushundura runini rw'imyenda kuko igitagangurirwa kigubwa neza kandi cyikungahaje ku zahabu cyabonye binyuze mu nyungu z'akarengane. Igitagangurirwa cyihishe mu bantu kandi cyiyoberanya nka Yakobo kandi bakoze ukwiyoberanya kwemeza. Kuko igitagangurirwa kidashobora kwigaragaza by'ukuri nk'igitagangurirwa kibi, cyahindutse; kuko ni uwuhe mutego mutego utegwa imbere y'inyoni? Ariko igitagangurirwa kiriyoberanya kigahindura isura yacyo n'imigenzereze yacyo kugira ngo cyivange n'abo gishaka kugaburiraho. Kandi uko igitagangurirwa gitangira kuboha urushundura rwacyo mu bantu baretse igitagangurirwa kikabona umwanya wo kuboha mu ishyanga ryabo batabizi; igitagangurirwa gihita kizana ibindi bitagangurirwa biza kwifatanya na cyo mu bikorwa byacyo bibi byo kwigarurira cyangwa binyuze mu rundi rwiri ruva mu isaho y'amagi y'igitagangurirwa byiruka kujya kuboha inshundura zabyo no gukomeza uruziga rw'urupfu. Uko ni ko Adamu aboshye kandi azingazingiwe n'ibi bikoko bibi. Kandi buhoro buhoro n'ibyishimo byinshi bigusha mu mutego kandi bikagaburira kuri Adamu kandi n'ubugome bwinshi n'inzika igitagangurirwa gishaka isi yose kuri cyo ubwacyo na ba Se ba ba se bafite irari baboshyeho.

Kuko mu buriganya bwabyo ibitagangurirwa by'Ashikenazi byiyunze na Esawu, binyuze muri Talimudi kuko mu myaka ya yubile myinshi ishize bemeje Talimudi nk'umutware n'umutegetsi wabo, nyamara si abayoboke b'ukuri ba Mose, cyangwa ngo bakurikire inzira ya Yeshua HaMashiach ariko barigira nk'abakurikira. Kandi abanyishe bari aba Esawu kandi binyuze mu bwenge bw'ikinyoma bwa Esawu, ndetse n'imigenzo yabo ituka Imana y'abantu bashyira hejuru y'inzira za Yahweh, banduje Yakobo kandi bagusha mu mutego mwene se wa Esawu inshuro nyinshi. Ndetse n'ubwo nababwiye mu gihe cyanjye mu mubiri nk'Umwana w'Intama w'Imana nti: Mukomoka kuri so umubisha, kandi irari rya so ni ryo mushaka gukora: yari umwicanyi uhereye mbere na mbere, kandi ntiyagumye mu kuri, kuko nta kuri kumurimo. Iyo avuga ikinyoma, avuga ibye: kuko ari umunyabinyoma, kandi ni se wacyo.

Nyamara igitagangurirwa nticyigeze kireka inzira z'abaguye kandi igitagangurirwa kiracyakora kandi cyishimira ibikorwa byacyo bibi. Kuko igitagangurirwa gihiga abana cyazingazingiye nk'umuhigo wacyo kandi kinywa amaraso yabo ku bana bato gitoteza mu maboko yacyo. Kuko igitagangurirwa gisohoza irari ryacyo riteye isesemi ku bana bato gishimuta mu ishyanga ryabo mu bwihisho bukomeye uko abana bato bafatwa mu rushundura rwacyo. Kandi ni ikihe gitagangurirwa cyabayeho kiboha urushundura nyamara kigacukura mu butaka. Kuko urushundura rw'igitagangurirwa rugera n'ikuzimu kandi urushundura rwacyo rutwikira ahantu hanini kandi igitagangurirwa gishaka ubwami n'ubutegetsi bw'isi yose. Nuko rero igitagangurirwa kigenda mu buriganya bukomeye kuko binyuze mu buriganya barwana.

Kandi nyamara aba biyita abo batari bo biyunze n'abaguye iteka ryose kandi bakaba ari urubyaro rwabo by'ukuri; biyita Yakobo kandi biyitiriye mu buryo bw'ikinyoma igihugu cy'isezerano, icyo badafiteho mugabane cyangwa umurage ariko ni abiyita abo batari bo. Kandi mu nzira zabo ni ho hava umutego inyamaswa yo mu isi izatega Yakobo kuko binyuze muri Talimudi ya Esawu Ashikenazi yiyunze mu masezerano na Esawu, kandi bazakomeza kwiyunga binyuze mu mwana w'intama w'ikiyoka, ndetse n'umwana w'intama wabo w'ikinyoma ufite amahembe abiri. Ndetse n'umwana w'intama udasanzwe aho ihembe rimwe ritegeka inyanja ukurikije amategeko ya Nowa n'irindi hembe ritegeka igihugu cy'isezerano mu gutuka Imana gukomeye n'uburiganya kuko ari ihembe rito rya Daniyeli, kandi uko ni ko isi izategekwa n'ubutegetsi bwabo igihe gito. Kuko bazahuza imbaraga zose n'udutsiko twose tw'ibibi munsi y'ubutegetsi bwabo, kandi bazabakoranyiriza hamwe kugira ngo barwane kuri uwo munsi ukomeye, aho bose bazajugunywa hasi ku mbaraga iteka ryose kandi bakajya mu gihano n'urubanza rw'iteka ryose.

Kandi nyamara inzira z'igitagangurirwa ni inzira zo kwaka ruswa, no kwambura, n'ubwicanyi, na ruswa n'inzira nyinshi, nyinshi mbi igitagangurirwa cyigisha kandi kibamo kuko igitagangurirwa gihora gishaka gukora ibindi bitagangurirwa bikubye kabiri abana b'ikuzimu nka byo ubwabyo. Nuko rero ibitagangurirwa birabyina kandi bikishimira jenoside yabyo ku bantu bo mu gihugu, ndetse bibyina bifatanye amaboko mu gihe bibyina mu byishimo ku rupfu n'irimbuka ry'abo byishe benshi muri bo bakaba ari abagore n'abana n'impinja; kandi uko ni ko irimbuka ryabo ritinda. Nyamara bafashe abayobozi b'isi kandi bagusha mu mutego abami b'isi binyuze mu bwumvikane no mu kwigarurira, kandi hafi ya bose bigaruriye isi yose mu nshundura zabo nyinshi kandi mu nshundura zabo isi iraboshye kandi ishyikirizwa amaboko y'ikiyoka kugira ngo icirwe urubanza n'irimbuka ryageze hafi ya ryose. Kandi nyamara igitagangurirwa gitaka cyane mu buribwe uko kigaburira ku muhigo wacyo ndetse kugira ngo gihore kigaragara nk'umukene utotezwa, mu gihe mu by'ukuri ari igitagangurirwa gitoteza kandi kikagirira nabi Adamu.

Nuko rero, nimutege amatwi ijwi ryanjye bene wacu, ndetse namwe mwese musoma aya magambo kandi mwitandukanye n'igitagangurirwa n'inshundura zacyo, kuko iyo umuntu wese ahindukiye akava mu rimbuka ryabo akambaza izina ry'Umwami, ari we Yesu w'i Nazareti ari we kandi Yeshua HaMashiach ari we Mesiya w'ukuri wa Isirayeli; ni ukuri, ni ukuri uwo muntu azakizwa.

Kuko igitagangurirwa ari Isinagogi ya Satani, ndetse cyavukiye mu muryango wa Ashikenazi wa Yafeti badafite mugabane cyangwa umurage mu murage wa Shemu, kandi cyane cyane uwa Yakobo ukiranuka uwo nzarokora nkamusigira asigaye akomeye.

Nuko rero, nimubavemo kandi mwitandukanye kandi muhindukirire Yeshua HaMashiach kugira ngo munyigireho kandi kugira ngo mwige kubaho mu nzira zikiranuka za Yahweh uwo ndi mu nzira ze kandi uwo ndi Jambo wigize umubiri, kuko Mose ari muzima byuzuye muri Yeshua HaMashiach kandi mu Isezerano Rishya ryanjye harimo amasezerano yose n'ibihango bya kera, amasezerano azasohora byuzuye kandi mu buryo bwuzuye mu ngoma yanjye y'imyaka igihumbi n'Ubwami nzashyiraho mu gukiranuka ku isi.

Ndi Yeshua HaMashiach ari we Mesiya w'ukuri wa Isirayeli, kandi ni ukuri ndi Umwami wanyu, Umwami, Umutambyi Mukuru, na Mesiya iteka ryose kandi Ubwami bwanjye n'ubutegetsi ntibizagira iherezo.

Ndi uje kuburira bene wacu kuri ubu buriganya bukomeye n'irimbuka byamize isi mu rushundura rwabyo kuko ibitagangurirwa byaboheye inshundura zabyo ku isi yose ubu.

Ndi Kristo Yesu, kandi nohereje aya Magambo binyuze ku ntumwa yanjye n'umugaragu wanjye kandi binyuze ku ntumwa yanjye n'umugaragu wanjye aya magambo yoherejwe.

Ndi Yesu Kristo, ari we kandi Yeshua HaMashiach wahishuye Amagambo y'Ubutumwa Bwiza Bw'Iteka hamwe na Yahweh ubwe mu bihe byo guhishura ari byo by'ubu. Ndetse n'ibihe byo guhishura mbere y'uko amakuba ya Yakobo atangira byuzuye kugira ngo Yakobo abashe gukizwa.

Nuko rero, musobanukirwe uburemere bw'Amagambo yanjye kandi ntimube abagayi ahubwo mwizere gusa ubuhamya bwanjye kandi mwumve Amagambo yanjye kandi muve mu kutizera kwinangiye no kwigomeka kuko ubu ari ibihe byo kugarukira Yahweh binyuze kuri Yeshua HaMashiach ndetse no mu bihe by'amakuba ibyo bihe byageze.

Kandi abaza bose kuri Mesiya wabo w'Iteka bakanyakira n'Isezerano Rishya ryanjye bazabona agakiza mu byiciro byabo. Abambaza izina ry'Umwami Yesu Kristo mu cyubahiro cyo guhara amagara yabo bazazurwa mu muzuko w'abakiranutsi kandi uburemere bw'ingororano yabo y'iteka buzaruta kure imihati yabo n'amakuba yabo ibyo nzabaha imbaraga zo kunesha nk'uko Yahweh yampaye imbaraga zo kunesha.

Kandi abo muri Yakobo nzabisha mu butayu mbakiza ibihe by'umubabaro ukomeye utarigeze kubaho; nyuma yaho bazatwarwa ari bazima mu Bwami bwanjye bw'imyaka igihumbi ndetse kugira ngo bororoke kandi bagwire, kandi tuzabaho mu nzira za Yahweh kuko mu gukiranuka isi izategekwa, kandi nta kintu kibi kizayinjiramo.

Kandi ni njye, Yeshua HaMashiach utangaza kuzura kw'ibihe kugira ngo isi ibyare abasinziriye muri Kristo no gukiza abazima kandi basigaye mu kuza kwanjye kandi basanzwe ari abizerwa muri Kristo, kuko ni ukuri, ni ukuri ibihe ni ubu. Sela!
