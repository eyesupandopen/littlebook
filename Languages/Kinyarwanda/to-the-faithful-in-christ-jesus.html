Ubuhamya Bubiri: Isirayeli
Ibisharira N'ibiryohereye
25 Ukwakira 2024

Ku Bizerwa muri Kristo Yesu!

Tega amatwi ijwi ry'Umwami wawe uhoraho, ndetse Yeshua HaMashiach, kuko ari jye waguhamagariye ubwiza n'ingeso nziza binyuze mu kuvuka gushya aho washyizweho ikimenyetso n'umwuka wanjye wera w'isezerano. Yego, nabagize abana b'Imana ikomeye ihoraho, ndetse Yahweh ubwe, kandi nta n'umwe uhwanye na we! Ku bw'ibyo, mwumve, bakunzi banjye ko muri mu bo nashyizeho ikimenyetso kugeza kuri wa munsi w'incungu aho muzahagarara rwose mu gukiranuka nzabambika kandi ingororano nyinshi benshi bazazitwara ku bw'ubudahemuka bwabo, nubwo bamwe bazahura n'igihombo.

Mukomeze gukurikira cyane mugana ku ntego, ku bw'igihembo cyo guhamagarwa kwo hejuru kw'Imana muri Kristo Yesu, kandi kugira ngo mubone ingororano yanyu yuzuye ku nzira nateguriye ibirenge byanyu, ndetse n'inzira z'umurimo aho nayoboye intambwe zanyu mu kunkorera ku isi mu bihe byanjye by'ubuntu. Kandi muri umurimo wanjye, waremewe muri Kristo Yesu gukora imirimo myiza, iyo Imana yateguye kera, kugira ngo muyigenderemo.

Kandi niko biri koko, bakunzi banjye, ko ari jye wabahaye umugambi uhoraho muri jye, Umwami wanyu uhoraho. Ni jye wunze ubumwe bwuzuye n'umugambi na Yahweh, ndetse na NDIHO ukomeye, none se niba Imana iri mu ruhande rwanyu, bakunzi banjye, ni inde ushobora kubarwanya? Kuki mwemera kandi mugaha ibitekerezo byanyu ibintu byoroshye kandi by'ubusabusa bishaka kubayobya kubakura mu kuboko kwanjye gukomeye guhoraho, uko kuyobya kukaba kudashoboka niba mugumishije ijisho ryanyu riri hamwe rinyerekeyeho, Kristo Yesu Umwami wanyu wazamutse!

Kandi nuko nkomeza kwigisha kugeza n'uyu munsi kugira ngo abafite amatwi yo kumva bumvire ngo ntimwibikire ubutunzi mu isi, aho inyenzi n'ingese zibwangiza, kandi aho abajura bamena bakiba. Ahubwo mwibikire ubutunzi mu ijuru, aho inyenzi cyangwa ingese zitabwangiza, kandi aho abajura batamena, ngo bibe. Kuko aho ubutunzi bwawe buri, ari ho n'umutima wawe uzaba. Umucyo w'umubiri ni ijisho: Nuko rero niba ijisho ryawe riri hamwe, umubiri wawe wose uzaba wuzuye umucyo.

Bakunzi banjye, ntimwumva umugani wanjye? Ubutunzi bwanyu ni ibitekerezo byanyu, nuko rero ndabayobora kugumisha ibitekerezo byanyu ku bintu byo hejuru kugira ngo mu kugenda kwanyu mube mugaragaza kandi mugendera mu mirimo myiza nateguriye ibirenge byanyu gukurikira n'umutima umwe. Ku bw'ibyo, natumye Pawulo yandikira Abakorinto ati: Ariko ndatinya ko, nk'uko inzoka yashutse Eva mu buriganya bwayo, ari ko n'imitima yanyu yabononwa ikava mu gukiranuka gushyitse kuri muri Kristo.

Ku bw'ibyo, mushyire imitima yanyu ku bintu byo hejuru, atari ku bintu byo mu isi. Kuko mwapfuye, kandi ubugingo bwanyu buhishanywe nanjye mu Mana. Ubwo jye, Kristo Yesu, uri ubugingo bwanyu, nzerekanwa, namwe muzerekananwa nanjye mu bwiza. Nuko rero, bakunzi banjye, mwumve ko muri umucyo w'isi, ndetse n'abana bose ba Data w'Imicyo! Ku bw'ibyo, mugumishe ibitekerezo byanyu byose kuri jye, Umwami wanyu w'Abami uhoraho n'Umwami w'abami kandi nzakora cyane mu mwuka wanyu kugira ngo umucyo wanjye uhoraho umurike uturutse mu mubiri wanjye muri urugingo rwawo, bityo inzabya zanjye z'ibumba zimurike kandi zerekane ubwiza bw'Imana ihoraho mu maso ha Yesu Kristo!

Kandi nuko ndabaza umugeni wanjye, kandi nuko ndabaza umubiri wanjye, kandi nuko ndabaza abizerwa banjye muri Kristo, kuki mwemerera abanzi banyu kubarangaza no kugabanya ibitekerezo byanyu ku bwiza bwanjye n'icyubahiro cyanjye? Mwumve ko abaguye bumva neza ko incungu yanyu yuzuye iri hafi nk'umwuka wanyu utaha, nuko rero bashaka kwiba ibyishimo byanyu kuko bazi ko bidashoboka kubakura mu maboko yanjye ahoraho, nubwo bagerageza.

Ku bw'ibyo, ntimugatinye ababarwanya na gato, icyo kuri bo kikaba ikimenyetso kigaragara cyo kurimbuka, ariko kuri mwe kikaba icy'agakiza, kandi icyo gituruka ku Mana. Ntimukerekeze ibitekerezo byanyu ku rusaku rw'abaguye, bakunzi banjye, ntimugahindukirire iburyo cyangwa ibumoso. Ahubwo mugume muri iyo nzira igororotse kandi ifunganye, ndetse inzira y'agakiza aho munyigiraho jye Yesu w'i Nazareti kuko ari jye ubigisha inzira z'agakiza aho mukizwa rwose uko mwejejwe mu maraso yanjye y'impongano mu byaha byose kugira ngo mubashe kubaho mu gukiranuka kw'Imana muri Kristo muri mwe ibyiringiro by'ubwiza!

Mwumve ko uko mungendera, Satani abagabaho ibitero ashaka guhagarika imbaraga zanyu no kubayobereza mu mirimo ye y'uburiganya y'ubusa. Ndetse n'ubutumwa bwiza bw'imibereho bw'uburiganya bwibagirwa kwihana no kubabarirwa ibyaha bugashyigikira imirimo myiza. Nyamara niko biri, ko kugaburira abashonje, kwambika abambaye ubusa, gucumbikira abatagira aho baba n'ibindi byinshi ari imirimo myiza ikwiriye guhaza ibyifuzwa bihari.

Ariko jye, Mwana w'Intama w'Imana sinatambwe rimwe, rwose, kandi iteka ryose nk'igitambo cya nyuma cy'icyaha kuri icyo giti kigadabye i Kaluvari kugira ngo nishyure imirimo myiza, cyangwa ngo Yahweh anzure mu bapfuye kugira ngo mbe utanga ibyiza by'iyi si ku babikeneye, ahubwo namanitswe ku giti mvusha igitonyanga cyanjye cya nyuma cy'amaraso mu bubabare bukomeye mu cyimbo no kwishyura icyaha n'ingaruka z'ibyaha by'abanyabyaha.

Jye, Kristo Yesu, nabacunguye mu muvumo w'Amategeko, mpinduwe umuvumo ku bwanyu, (kuko byanditswe ngo, havumwe umuntu wese umanitswe ku giti) kandi nyamara icyo cyari igiciro kitari icyanjye kwishyura, kuko jye, Yesu w'i Nazareti, ndi we w'ubuziranenge buhagarara kugeza n'uyu munsi! Kandi nta cyaha ngira ibihe byose n'iteka ryose kuko buri gihe nkora ibyo Data ashaka. Ku bw'ibyo, muruhukire mu mirimo nabahaye gukora kandi mwikorere umugogo wanjye rwose, kuko umugogo wanjye utaruhije, n'umutwaro wanjye woroshye.

Ibihe by'urubanza rukomeye byaje ku isi yose, ndetse n'ibihe by'amakuba ya Yakobo. Nyamara mbere y'uko amakuba ya Yakobo aza rwose ku isi birakwiye ko abizerwa banjye bazashikuzwa umujinya ugiye kuza kandi jye, Kristo Yesu, ndi we uje mu bicu n'ijwi rya marayika mukuru n'impanda y'Imana kandi abapfiriye muri Kristo bazabanza kuzuka naho mwe abazima basigaye muzaterurirwa mu bicu hamwe na bo maze mu cyumba cy'intebe y'ubwami cya Yahweh So uhoraho ni ho muzajya noneho.

Kandi nuko umunsi ubagezeho, bana banjye, ndetse n'abizerwa bose muri Kristo Yesu. Ku bw'ibyo, musige amaganya y'iyi si yaguye inyuma kandi mwerekeze ibitekerezo byanyu byose rwose ku Mwami wanyu uhoraho, ndetse Yeshua HaMashiach. Yego, ibitekerezo byanyu byose bitagabanyije kuko umwanzi ashaka uwo aconshomera kandi nk'intare yivuga azerera n'ivuga rye ry'ubusa ariko mwumve ko ari umwanzi watsinzwe bakunzi banjye kuko namutsinze rwose kandi intsinzi yuzuye ni iyanjye rwose kuva ku magambo yanjye ya nyuma navugiye mu mubiri kuri icyo giti kigadabye: Birarangiye!

Kandi nuko bimeze ubu ndakora rwose ku bw'incungu yanyu yuzuye kandi nzuzuza uwo murimo utunganye muri buri wese muri mwe aho muzahita muremwa bundi bushya rwose nk'icyaremwe gishya kandi gitunganye kandi kitagira icyaha. Ndetse icyaremwe gitunganye icyo kubora kidashobora kwangiza, icyo ubwiza bwacyo butazigera buhagarara, kandi icyo kumurika kwacyo kutazigera gushira. Kuko muzahindurwa nkanjye, Kristo Yesu, kuko ndi Adamu wa nyuma, kandi ndi umwuka utanga ubugingo ukanaba umugaba w'agakiza kanyu.

Ku bw'ibyo, mwite ku bintu by'umwuka! Ntimukemere ko imyambi yaka umuriro y'umugome yotsa inzira yayo mu butunzi bwanyu. Mwumve ko Satani yashaka gusenya ibitekerezo byanyu byerekeye ku Mwami wanyu uhoraho n'ubutunzi bwe bw'ubusa kandi bunuka inshuro nyinshi buba uburiganya bwemeza bw'ukuri. Nuko rero mwange ibitekerezo bye n'imigambi ye kandi mwamagane inzira ze zose z'ubwigomeke kuko ize ari inzira z'urupfu kandi rwose ashaka gusangira umurage we muri iyo miriro ishyushye cyane y'amazuku anuka n'umuntu wese uhitamo iryo ziba ry'umuriro rihoraho, ariko iryo herezo ntirishoboka kuri mwe bakunzi banjye kandi nabashyizeho ikimenyetso iteka ryose n'ikimenyetso gihoraho cy'Imana ihoraho.

Nuko rero mugume muri jye kugira ngo ntonyange inzira zanyu, kandi kugira ngo mbereke neza imitego Satani yashaka gutega abizerwa banjye. Mugume muri jye, bakunzi banjye, kuko muri Kristo nta mutego ushobora gutegwa kandi umutego w'umutezi ntushobora gutegwa n'uburiganya cyangwa imbaraga. Nuko rero, murinde ibitekerezo byanyu kandi mugumishe imitwe yanyu n'imitima yanyu byuzuye Amagambo yanjye yera! (Kuko intwaro z'intambara yacu atari iz'umubiri, ahubwo zifite imbaraga binyuze ku Mana zo gusenya ibihome.) Dushyira hasi ibitekerezo, n'ikintu cyose kishyira hejuru kirwanya kumenya Imana, kandi dufata mpiri buri gitekerezo ngo cyumvire Kristo.

Kuko tudakirana n'abafite amaraso n'umubiri, ahubwo dukirana n'abatware, n'abafite ubushobozi, n'abategeka iyi si y'umwijima, n'imyuka mibi y'ahantu ho mu ijuru. Ku bw'iyo mpamvu mutware intwaro zose z'Imana, kugira ngo mubashe gukomera ku munsi mubi, kandi murangije byose, muhagarare mushikamye.

Yego, muhagarare mushikamye mu Mwami wanyu uhoraho bana banjye bato kuko ibihe bibagezeho. Nubwo benshi muri mwe bashaka gusubira mu mimerere yo gusinzira, ndetse nk'uko amajwi y'iyi si ahumuriza mu binyoma ashaka kubasinziriza mu kinyoma gikomeye. Ntimugwe mu burozi bwabo, kandi mutere umugongo uburozi bwabo n'amarozi yabo. Mutere umugongo ibyo bita imyidagaduro yabo, kuko bidakwiriye ko abana bera b'Imana isumbaho byose ba Kristo Yesu rwose bidagadurwa n'icyaha ukundi.

Nemeje mu bizerwa banjye ukuba hafi n'ukuri kwuzuye kwo kuza kwanjye kwegereje gukoranyiriza abizerwa banjye mu mutekano w'ijuru. Ndetse nahaye inzozi, iyerekwa, n'amagambo menshi abana bato bakundwa cyane. Bakunzi banjye, iki gikorwa kirimo kwisuka rwose mu nzira yanyu kandi kibagezeho kandi kiri hafi kuruta uko mwiyumvisha. Ku bw'ibyo mwitegure. Mukomeze muve mu byaha byose kandi munyature ibyaha byanyu rwose kandi mwaturirane ibicumuro byanyu.

Nashyizeho ikimenyetso abizerwa banjye muri uwo mwuka wera w'isezerano kandi nuko natanze amavuta y'amatabaza yanyu, ndetse n'abahawe ku buntu impano yanjye ihoraho y'agakiza n'ubugingo buhoraho n'umurage uhoraho muri Kristo Yesu Umwami w'Abami uhoraho n'Umwami w'abami! Murebe mu ijuru bakunzi banjye kandi ku bumvira kandi bategereje mu budahemuka kuza kwanjye bazabona n'amaso yabo ubwiza bwo kuza kwanjye, kuko iki kintu kitazakorwa mu ibanga kandi ntibishoboka ko Satani ahisha ubwiza bwo kuza kwanjye, nubwo azabeshya agashaka gutwikira kuza kwanjye n'abanyamahanga b'ibinyoma n'intambara y'isi.

Ku bw'ibyo, muntegereze mwihanganye kandi mukore rwose imirimo nabahaye kurangiza kugira ngo murangize rwose inzira zanyu z'umurimo mu mugambi wanjye uhoraho w'agakiza ku buzima bwa buri wese muri mwe. Mwumve bakunzi banjye ko ubutabera bwa Yahweh n'ingororano n'umurage n'ibihembo bitabarwa nk'uko umuntu kamere abara kuko umuntu kamere akiranirwa kandi ntatabera. Buri wese muri mwe yahawe imirimo yo gukora kugira ngo muteze imbere imigambi yanjye y'agakiza kandi jye, Kristo Yesu nzaca urubanza rw'imirimo yanyu mwakoze mu kwitanga no gukorera Umwami wanyu uhoraho.

Mwumve ko mwese muzagororerwa cyane ku bw'imirimo yanyu y'ubudahemuka y'umurimo mwakoze mu budahemuka mu izina ryanjye ryera. Kandi, jye, Kristo Yesu ndi we ureba buri murimo ukorwa n'abizerwa muri Kristo n'imigambi y'umutima kandi nzabagororera rwose. Uko narebaga abakire bashyira amaturo yabo mu isanduku y'amaturo nabonye abakire, bashyira amaturo yabo mu isanduku y'amaturo. Kandi nabonye n'umupfakazi umwe w'umukene ashyiramo ibiceri bibiri: Kandi ndavuga nti, Ni ukuri ndababwira nti, uyu mupfakazi w'umukene ashyizemo byinshi kuruta bose.

Kandi nuko ndaza aho muri bakunzi banjye, ndetse hano muri ibi bihe bya nyuma byihuta mbere y'uko mbakoranyiriza kuri jye kandi ndabatera inkunga mwese ku bw'urukundo ruhoraho rwa Yahweh ubwe guhagarara mushikamye no kureba mu kirere kuko incungu yanyu ibagezeho. Kandi buri nkubiri y'amakuba iziyongera mu mbaraga kandi kurimbuka kuziyongera uko buri nkubiri yikubita ku isi. Isezerano ryo kubyara ntirihagarara ubu kandi isi igomba kubyara umwana wayo kuko mu isi hari abana ba Adamu ubu babaye abana b'Imana ndetse binyuze kuri Adamu wa nyuma. Kandi mu buryo butunguranye bose bazahindurwa iteka ryose n'iteka ryose mu mudendezo w'ubwiza bw'ubutungane kandi bazambikwa iteka ryose mu bwiza buhoraho kandi bafite ejo hazaza hahoraho n'umugambi uhoraho mu migambi ihoraho ya Yahweh.

Mwumve ko icyo kiyoka kinini gitukura cyemerewe gutegura urubuga kandi cyimurira ibikoresho byacyo mu mwanya ku kibaho cy'isi. Mwumve ko ibintu byamaze gutangira kandi ko icyerekezo cy'isi cy'ubu kidashobora gukomeza kandi rwose kizavamo amakuba akomeye kandi atunguranye kandi byinshi muri ibi bintu birimo kwihuza bizahurira hamwe mu guhurira gutunganye.

Kuko uko niko igihe cy'imigambi ya Yahweh kiri, kandi buri Yod n'ihwa rya Yod bizahurira hamwe mu ishyirwa mu bikorwa ritunganye n'ubwumvikane, kuko ikintu cyose Yahweh ashyizeho ukuboko kwe kivamo ubutungane buhoraho no gukiranuka guhoraho kuko ndetse n'abahitamo kubora n'ibirimi by'umuriro bihoraho by'igihano bacirwa urubanza kandi bagahanwa mu gukiranuka no mu butungane.

Kandi nuko ndavuga nti bakunzi banjye, mugumishe intambi zanyu zikase kandi mwibungabunge mu isuku yo hejuru mu maraso yanjye akiranuka kandi yeza y'impongano. Mugumishe intambi zanyu zikase mukomeza kwigumisha mu isuku y'ibitekerezo ibyanditswe byanjye bihoraho bizana ku Mana ihoraho, ndetse Yahweh ubwe ni we wohereje Ijambo rye akabakiza.

Ni jye waguhamagariye ubwiza n'ingeso nziza.

Ni jye wabahaye umugambi uhoraho muri jye, Umwami wanyu uhoraho.

Ni jye wunze ubumwe bwuzuye n'umugambi na Yahweh, ndetse na NDIHO ukomeye.

Ni jye ubigisha inzira z'agakiza aho mukizwa rwose.

Nta cyaha ngira ibihe byose n'iteka ryose kuko buri gihe nkora ibyo Data ashaka.

Ndi Adamu wa nyuma.

Ndi umwuka utanga ubugingo ukanaba umugaba w'agakiza kanyu.

Kandi ni jye wohereza amagambo y'ubutumwa bwiza bwanjye buhoraho binyuze ku ntumwa yanjye nahagurukije kandi ngaha inshingano kuri iyi mpamvu nyine kandi binyuze ku ntumwa yanjye amagambo y'ubutumwa bwiza bwanjye buhoraho yoherejwe.

Ni ukuri, ni ukuri, ni jye Yahweh yasangiye intebe ye y'ubwami kugeza abanzi banjye bagizwe intebe y'ibirenge byanjye, ndetse kugeza nsubije ibyaremwe byacunguwe rwose mu maboko y'Imana ihoraho yo gukiranuka, kugira ngo rwose Yahweh abe byose muri byose kandi kugira ngo byose bibe yego na amena.

Kandi ni jye, Yeshua HaMashiach wohereje amagambo y'ubutumwa bwiza buhoraho ku bw'ukumvira kw'abaragwa bose b'ubugingo buhoraho kandi aya magambo azabungabungwa binyuze mu makuba n'imanza bigiye kuza kandi aya magambo azabona amaso, amatwi, n'imitima y'abo yohererejwe. Ni ukuri, ndaza vuba. Amena. Ubuntu bw'Umwami uhoraho Yesu Kristo busukwe cyane ku bizerwa bose muri Kristo, AMENA na Amena.
