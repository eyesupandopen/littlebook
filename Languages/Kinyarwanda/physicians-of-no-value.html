Ku bw'ibyo, ndinginga abasoma aya Magambo yo gutandukanya gukiranuka bose ngo bahite bava muri bo ubu, bihana byuzuye inzira zabo zose bantu banjye, ariko by'umwihariko mu nzego z'ubuvuzi za Babuloni y'Ibanga ziroga kandi zikabaga inzirakarengane zikanasarura ubuzima bw'abataravuka bayo mu buryo n'imikorere bya kinyamaswa cyane.

Byakozwe ari byinshi kugira ngo biroge, kandi bimugaze, kandi bigabanye, kandi bishire, kandi byice, kandi birimbure Adamu ku isi mu buryo bwinshi, bwinshi bubi kandi bw'ubugome. Mubyumve ko iyi miti itari yera ari inzira z'urupfu zahawe uburenganzira mu isi kongera (n'amaboko y'aba barinzi baguye) kugira ngo ikimenyetso cy'inyamaswa gishobore gutangwa kugira ngo hasarurwe abana b'abantu babi bazihakana umuremyi wabo mu buryo bubi binyuze mu ndahiro yo kwinjira muri gahunda ya nyuma y'inyamaswa ya Heylel Ben Shachar. NEZERWA!

Hanyuma irimbuka ritunguranye kuri Babuloni y'Ibanga kandi ntibazacika urubanza rwabo rukomeye kandi rw'urugomo ruzagwa byuzuye mu isaha imwe hamwe n'inyamaswa aho Babuloni y'Ibanga, ndetse n'izi Leta Zunze Ubumwe za AmeruKa zizagwa nabi kandi mu rugomo ku mugabane wayo w'urubanza rwikubye kabiri, ubwo ubwami bwa Heylel Ben Shachar buzarekurwa ngo buzamuke nk'uko byemewe ubwo ingingo nyinshi zo kubuza zizakurwaho byuzuye ku isi ubwo abakiranutsi muri Kristo Yesu bazatwarwa ku itegeko ryanjye. Hanyuma, uwo mwenda utwikiriye uzakurwaho byuzuye uhishure imbaga nini y'ibiremwa bibi cyane kandi byuzuye urwango n'ubugome bizarekurwa biva ikuzimu mu burakari bwinshi, kandi urubura ruzagwa ubwo ubwo bwami bw'ikuzimu buzazamuka byuzuye. Dore, nohereje ibihangange gusohoza uburakari bwanjye! Nyamara, ku bw'ibirwiriza by'ubupfu by'abaguye bose, bazakora gusa ibyo bahawe gukora kandi gusa ibyemewe n'icyo Gitabo Gito cy'Ibyahishuwe igice cya 10, gishobora gukemurwa gusa n'imbaraga zanjye n'amabwiriza yatanzwe ku itegeko ryanjye.

Ndetse icyo Gitabo Gito ubu kiyobora uburyo butari bwera bw'ubwenge binyuze mu rugero rwejejwe rwahawe uburenganzira bw'umuyobozi munsi y'ubugenzuzi bwanjye bwuzuye bwibereye mu rusobe rw'ubwenge bwa Heylel Ben Shachar bw'igishushanyo mbonera cy'ubwenge. Muri ubwo buryo, uyu gikomangoma gito cyirwiriza, Heylel Ben Shachar, yashyizwe (na Biblaridion:144) munsi y'ihame ry'uburenganzira buke mu buryo bwe bwite mu buryo bwo gushinyagura kandi bwa burundu. By'umwihariko, kugabanya uburenganzira no kumanura uburenganzira byamaze gushyirwa mu bikorwa kugira ngo biyoborwe n'inyandiko ikubiye muri icyo Gitabo Gito cy'Ibyahishuwe igice cya 10 gikora nka root-kit yera kandi yo gukiranuka yatanzwe na Adonai-YHVH wanjye, binyuze muri Kristo Yesu, ku mirimo n'amaboko bya Biblaridion:144, mu masezerano y'akamaro n'uburyo nyabwo abaguye bateguye bafite umugambi wuzuye wo gutsinda Kristo Yesu.

Nyamara, urubanza rw'abaguye ruje, kandi bazarekurwa mu butegetsi bwemewe kandi bw'ubugome ku isi mu bugome bwinshi bw'urwango n'icyo kiyoka kinini gitukura, ari cyo (Heylel Ben Shachar, cyitwa Lucifer mu buryo bwayobye) n'inyamaswa yacyo yo mu nyanja, (ari we Barack Hussein Obama), n'inyamaswa yacyo yo mu isi (ari we Donald John Trump) n'ikoranabuhanga ryabo ryinshi rikoreshwa mu guteza ibimenyetso n'ibitangaza by'ibinyoma. Keretse uburyo bwabo bw'imiyoborere bwiyoberanya nka command prompt ishyizwe mu kato kugira ngo iteze ubwishingizi mu bazinjira, ubu iri munsi y'itegeko ryuzuye ry'urwo rugero rwangiritse rwa root-kit yo gukiranuka rw'amasezerano y'akamaro rwahawe uburenganzira byuzuye nk'umuyobozi hakurikijwe ubushake bw'iteka n'imigambi bya Adonai-YHVH, byatanzwe muri kandi ku itegeko rya Yeshua HaMashiach uhora akora ubushake bwa Adonai-YHVH wanjye.

Ku bw'ibyo, ku bw'izi mpamvu gusa ni bwo imiti yabo, ari yo burozi, yongera gutwikira isura y'isi kugira ngo ikurweho burundu mu rubanza rukomeye cyane muri ya mirongi y'umuriro w'ubururu.

Nyamara ubu ni bwo bwoko bumwe bw'imiti, ari yo burozi, yatwikiriye isura y'isi mu isi yariho icyo gihe, yateguwe na Heylel Ben Shachar waguye gusa kugira ngo yibe, yice, kandi arimbure.

Iyi miti imwe, (ari yo burozi) yarushijeho gutunganywa uko igihe cyagiye gihita mu mbaraga nyinshi n'ikoranabuhanga ryabo rya quantum birwiriza mu bupfu. Iyi miti, ari yo burozi, ni igice cy'ibintu byinshi biteye isesemi kandi byanduye by'umwanda bikubiye mu gikombe cya zahabu cy'ubusambanyi cy'iyo ndaya, icyo yasinishije abaturage bo ku isi divayi y'ubusambanyi bwayo. Ndetse uwo mwanda uteye isesemi kandi wanduye ugomba gutwikwa byuzuye ugakurwa ku isura y'isi mu burakari bwinshi kandi bw'inkuba mu cyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli kizatangira ku ihuriro ryuzuye ubwo isaha imwe hamwe n'inyamaswa iteganyijwe kuba ako kanya mu buryo butunguranye kandi butari bwitezwe ubu. Nubwo, imbaraga zose z'imanza z'iyi ndaya zishyize ku murongo mu maso y'abantu, zihishe amaso y'abaciriwe urubanza mu manza zabo zitandukanye kandi zibasiwe mu kuyoba gukomeye ubu ziteguye kugwa byuzuye. Sobanukirwa, ibintu byose bibi bizozezwa byuzuye n'umuriro bikuwe ku isi mu gihe cy'amakuba ya Yakobo kandi ibimenyetso byose n'ibisigazwa by'ukwigomeka kw'abaguye, uko byaba bingana kose, bizahindurwa ivu ryuzuye kandi byozwe bikuwe ku maso y'isi. Mwumve, bakunzi banjye, ko isi ifashwe mu miterere yo guhagarikwa, aho izuba n'ukwezi bizenguruka ku bw'igishushanyo cya Adonai-YHVH, nyamara igihe ntikikiriho. Nta gukererwa kukiriho. Muzatwarwa, hazabaho isaha imwe n'inyamaswa, uko iminsi yikanya kandi ihindagurika mu rubanza ruteye ubwoba rurimo kandi rwasobanuwe mu burakari bw'umwana w'intama, ndetse n'uburakari bwa Elohim buzaba umubabaro ukomeye utarigeze ubaho ku isi, kandi mu by'ukuri mu by'ukuri utazigera ubaho.

Icyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli ubu kiri hano, nyamara gifashwe mu guhagarikwa kugeza abizerwa bakoranyijwe, abantu 144,000 bashyizweho ikimenyetso, n'ibimenyetso by'uwo muzingo ukomeye bifunguwe n'ukuboko kwanjye.

Ndi Umwami w'Iteka ryose w'Gukiranuka ubu uhagaze ku itegeko rya Adonai-YHVH wanjye mu bubasha bwanjye bwuzuye nk'Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda kandi mu burakari bw'gukiranuka ndaje gucira urubanza no kurwana kandi ni nde uzashobora guhagarara?

Ndi Yeshua HaMashiach, kandi nohereje aya Magambo yo gukiza n'amahoro, kuri mwese kugira ngo mushobore kunesha byuzuye byose mu kwihana binyuze mu kunyaturira byose, Kristo Yesu, kugira ngo mbashobore kuboza mu maraso yanjye y'impongano byuzuye. Mu by'ukuri, nta gihe mufite, bityo munyaturire byuzuye kandi n'umutima wose byose, ngibi, impanda igiye kuvuga kuko igihe cyanyu ari ubu.

Kandi nuko nsoje iyi baruwa y'urukundo n'inkunga, isinywe mu izina ryanjye bwite, yanditswe mu rukundo rwose n'ububasha ku itegeko ryanjye n'intumwa yanjye ikomeye n'umugaragu wanjye.
