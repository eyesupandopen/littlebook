15 Ukuboza 2024

Ku Bizerwa muri Kristo: Ntimutangare!

Ntimutangare, bavandimwe banjye, ntimutangare, nubwo iyi si ibanga. Mumenye ko isi yanyanze mbere yuko ibanga, bakunzi banjye, kandi umugaragu ntaruta shebuja. Nuko rero, musobanukirwe ibihe murimo kandi murusheho kwegera Yeshua HaMashiach mu mitima yanyu, kuko ndabahamagara ngo murusheho kunyegera mwa torero ryanjye, mubiri wanjye, ndetse na mugeni wanjye, kandi ndabahamagara ngo murusheho kwegera muri ibi bihe mbere yuko muzamurwa mu ijuru kugira ngo muneshe byose muri Kristo.

Kandi gutya mu buryo butunguranye kandi butitezwe muzatwarwa mu ijuru, kuko nzaza mu isaha mudatekereza. Dore! Nzaza nk'umujura nijoro! Ariko nk'uko iminsi ya Nowa yari iri, niko no kuza k'Umwana w'umuntu kuzaba. Kuko nk'uko mu minsi yabanjirije umwuzure, bararyaga bakanywa, bararongoraga, bashyingirwa, kugeza umunsi Nowa yinjiriye mu Nkuge, ntibamenya na mba, kugeza ubwo umwuzure waje, ukabatwara bose, niko no kuza k'Umwana w'umuntu kuzaba.

Nuko rero nabagiriye inama bakunzi banjye, yo kuva mu by'iyi si, mumenye ko kugenda kwanyu muva muri iyi si mbi kuri hafi. Nyamara, niko bimeze ko mukiriho, kandi mugomba gukomeza kugeza igihe mutazaba mukiriho, kuko nimutwarwa mu ijuru gitungura n'ukuboko kwanjye gukomeye kw'agakiza ingendo zanyu zizarangira kandi ubuzima bwanyu ku isi buzaba burangiye icyo gihe. Nuko rero ndabagira inama bana banjye bato, mubeho mwumvira ijwi ry'Umwami n'Umukiza wanyu, ari we Kristo Yesu Umwami wanyu wazamutse kandi nzabaha kunesha rwose!

Nuko rero, uko mubaho muhe byose kuri njye mu kwiyemeza no mu ntego z'imitima yanyu kuko ndi igikwiye cyanyu muri byose. Ntimutekereze kuvuga mu mitima yanyu ko mwagize icyo mugeraho kandi mukagubwa neza ku bw'imbaraga z'amaboko yanyu, cyangwa ku bw'impamyabumenyi z'ibigo benshi muri mwe baha agaciro mu bujiji kuruta kumenya Yahweh, ndetse n'ibinyoma mwagurishijwe nk'ukuri mu burezi bwanyu bw'ibinyoma bwubatswe ku binyoma gusa, nyamara mwirata mu bujiji ubushobozi bwanyu mwibwira ko mwabukuyeho. Kandi rero ndababwira nanone, ngo muve hagati yabo kandi mwitandukanye muri byose, cyangwa muzasangira mu manza zabo n'ibyago byabo mu gihe ibyo bitari ngombwa kuko ibihe byose bya none ndabahamagara kandi ndabinginga ngo muze kuri njye rwose mu rukundo rwanjye mbakunda bana banjye. Nuko rero muve mu bwibone butagira umumaro ubumenyi buzana!

Nuko rero, ndabaha gusobanukirwa ko icyo mwungutse cyose muri ubu buzima ku byerekeye ibyiza by'iyi si gitangwa n'ineza n'ubushake bwiza n'ubuntu bwa Yahweh, NDI UWITEKA Ukomeye, wifuza mbere ya byose ko mwagubwa neza kandi mukagira amagara mazima nk'uko ubugingo bwanyu bugubwa neza. Nyamara, ntimube abapfapfa ngo mutekereze ko mwiyubatse cyangwa ko mu byubahiro byanyu by'isi mwemerwa imbere ya Yahweh, NDI UWITEKA Ukomeye cyangwa Yesu Kristo, kuko nta mubiri n'umwe utsindishirizwa imbere yacu. Nuko rero musobanukirwe ko abishyira hejuru muri ubwo buryo bazahura n'igihombo gikomeye kubera ubwibone bwabo, bubanziriza irimbuka rikomeye.

Ku bwanyu, ndabinginga ngo muve muri iyi si n'imyifatire yayo yose itubaha Imana yo kwiyita abakiranutsi, kwiyubaka, kwitsindishiriza kw'ibinyoma kutubaha Imana no kwirata k'umubiri. Kuko, ukuri, ukuri, iyo ubuntu bwa Yahweh, NDI UWITEKA Ukomeye, butasukwa mu rukundo rwe ruhoraho kuri mwe, ndetse n'aho urukundo rwe ruhoraho n'umucyo we byamuritse mu mitima yanyu no mu buzima bwanyu mu maso ha Yesu Kristo: mwese mwari kuba nka Sodoma na Gomora kandi ntimwari kugira umuzi cyangwa ishami, nuko rero kwirata kwanyu kube muri Kristo. Icyakora, iryo herezo si iryanyu bakunzi banjye, nuko rero, mu rukundo rwanyu kuri njye mwumvire ijwi ryanjye kugira ngo muneshe byose kuko nimwumvira ijwi ryanjye, nzabaha kunesha!

Niko bimeze, kandi niko bizaba, ko ku banesha banjye hari ingororano nyinshi, ndetse no ku banesheje nk'uko nanesheje, kandi ingororano yabo izaba ikomeye cyane. Nyamara, benshi mu bizerwa banjye muri Kristo barashukwa kandi bagasinzirizwa, nk'abasubira mu bapfuye! Ndetse n'imbere y'amategeko yanjye nahaye Pawulo mu myaka ya Yubile myinshi ishize mu rwandiko rwe yandikiye Abefeso, nuko arandika, kandi nanjye ndabibutsa bakunzi banjye: Nicyo gituma avuga ati, kanguka wowe usinziriye, kandi uzuke mu bapfuye, na Kristo azaguha umucyo. Nuko rero mwirinde kugenda mutitonze, mutari nk'abapfapfa, ahubwo nk'abanyabwenge, mucungura igihe: kuko iminsi ari mibi. Nuko rero, ntimube abaswa, ahubwo musobanukirwe icyo ubushake bw'Umwami ari cyo.

Ese musobanukiwe ubushake bw'Umwami wanyu, bana banjye bato? Ese nicyo mwerekejemo ibitekerezo byose? Cyangwa mwikorera mu budahemuka bw'imitima yanyu, mushaka ubutunzi bwinshi bwo kongera mu bubiko bwanyu buri munsi? Ese mwahuje imitima yanyu mu gukorera ibigo, cyangwa amasosiyete y'abantu b'abanyabyaha ku bw'inyungu mbi, ndetse no mu gihe amafaranga abantu birundanyaho kandi babika mu byaha vuba aha azatangazwa ko nta gaciro agira imbere y'amaso yabo? Cyangwa mwakomeje kumvira ijwi ryanjye kandi musuzuma witonze buri Jambo mbaha ku bw'agakiza kanyu? Nimukomeza kumvira ijwi ryanjye nzabaha kunesha byose, nuko rero, ntimucike intege!

Ndacyabinginga, abizerwa bose muri Kristo n'abazaba abizerwa muri Kristo bose gukomeza kugarukira kuri njye kuko ndabatumira ngo murusheho kunyegera kugira ngo ubusabane bwuzuye bw'urukundo rwanjye kuri mwe bubakomeze kandi bubatunge muri byose, nuko rero, ntimutinye, kandi ntimute igihe mwicira urubanza. Nuko rero, mukomeze gushyuha mu rukundo rwanyu no mu kwiyemeza kw'imitima yanyu ku Mwami wanyu Yesu Kristo, bana banjye bato, kuko nta gihe kigihari cyo guta! Kandi impanda nivuga muzaba muri mu bakobwa b'abapfapfa basigirwa umuriro w'utunganya, cyangwa muzasangwa mu bakobwa b'abanyabwenge bazatwarwa mu ijuru n'umukwe wabo isaha n'isaha guhera ubu. Muzaba abahe kandi muzahagarara he ubwo nzaboneka gitungura isaha n'isaha guhera ubu?

Ese muzanyemerera mbaha amavuta y'amatabaza yanyu kugira ngo muzahagarare ku ntebe yanjye y'imanza? Ese muzatunganya intambi zanyu neza mwumvira amabwiriza yanjye? Cyangwa muzahitamo mu bujiji gukomeza kwihagije muhagaze mu gukiranuka kwanyu mwigejejeho kandi mukomeze guhagarara mu bakobwa b'abapfapfa? Ndetse n'igihe nabateguriye ibyiza, muramutse mwumviye ijwi ryanjye rwose. Nuko rero, ndabagira inama bizerwa banjye, mukomeze kuza kuri njye mu rukundo rwose no kumvira kugira ngo mbashyire hejuru cyane mu ijuru ryo hejuru kugira ngo murabagirane nk'inyenyeri zo mu isanzure ku bw'ibyo mwakoze mwumvira mu gukorera Umwami wanyu Uhoraho wo Gukiranuka!

Nuko rero, ndabaza mwese abazi ijwi ryanjye, ndetse namwe mwahamagawe mu mazina, kandi mwambaza mu izina ryanjye: Ese mwakangutse rwose? Ese amaso yanyu ahumutse rwose ku isi nk'uko iri uyu munsi? Musobanukirwe ko isi mubamo ubu atari imwe n'iyo mbere y'amezi atandatu ashize, kandi isi ikomeza guhinduka cyane imbere y'amaso yanyu, nubwo benshi muri mwe bagishakisha impamvu zo kwirengagiza ibimenyetso n'ibitangaza mu kutizera bashaka gusubira gusinzira. Nuko rero, mwisuzume ubwanyu n'imigambi yanyu n'Umwami wanyu uhoraho kugira ngo nyobore intambwe zanyu kandi mbarinde amakuba mu rukundo rwanjye n'ubuntu bwanjye.

Niko bimeze, ibinyoma by'ikiyoka ubu byuzuye isi kuruta uko byari bimeze mbere yuko ikiyoka kijugunywa hasi; kuko ikiyoka cyajugunywe hasi, cyajugunywe ku isi kandi kiboshywe munsi y'isanzure kugira ngo gicirwe urubanza, kandi kizi ko gisigaranye igihe gito n'umujinya wacyo ni mwinshi! Kandi kije gukora intambara kandi intambara zacyo zizaba nini, nyamara nta gukomera iyo nzoka ya kera izigera igira uretse amazuku yaka kandi yibirindura yaka imiriro ishyushye cyane y'igihano.

Nuko rero nje aho muri bakunzi banjye kubatera inkunga yo gukomeza kwegera umutima wanjye binyuze mu kumvira Amagambo yanjye, kandi ku nkuta z'umutima wanjye buri zina ryanyu ryuzuye ryanditswemo mu rukundo rwanjye kuri mwe, ruhoraho kandi rw'iteka. Yego, mu rukundo rwanjye kuri mwe, (Ndabazi mwese neza kuruta uko mwiyizi) Ndabakomeza hafi y'umutima wanjye ibihe byose kandi cyane cyane mu bihe byanyu by'amakuba n'ibigeragezo muhura nabyo byanze bikunze. (Nakusanyije buri rira ryanyu mu icupa ryanjye ry'amarira kugira ngo nzabagororere kuri buri rimo) Kandi mu ntambara zikomeje muri iyi ntambara y'imbuto, inesherezwa muri Kristo kandi irwanya abaguye; musobanukirwe, ko intambara zanyu zinesherezwa rwose mu Mwami wanyu uhoraho! Nuko rero, muruhukire mu Mwami wanyu uhoraho, ari we Kristo Yesu kandi mureke imirimo yanyu y'ubusa kandi muruhukiremo rwose, kandi murangize imirimo yo gukiranuka nabahaye gukora kugira ngo muneshe abanzi banyu kandi nzabaha Isabato! Sela!

Nuko rero, musobanukirwe iminsi mubamo kuko nta n'umwe muri mwe wigeze abaho muri iyi minsi yaje ku isi muri iki gihe cya nyuma, nubwo igaragara mu buryo bushukana nk'isa n'ibyo mwamenye. Kuko isi iri mu nzibacyuho kandi koko isi irahindurwa, kandi ukuri, ukuri isi izakomeza guhinduka ku ngufu hakurikijwe imigambi n'ibishushanyo bya Yahweh.

Musobanukirwe ko igihe cy'inzibacyuho ikomeye cyaje ku isi, ndetse n'igihe isi irimo gutunganywa no gusubirwamo n'ibikorwa n'amakuba n'imanza bizaza mu isubizwaho ry'isi yahozeho mu minsi ya Nowa! Kandi ibikorwa n'imanza bizaba nk'uko byahanuwe n'abahanuzi ba kera bigomba kuba rwose mu gihe cy'amakuba ya Yakobo. Nuko rero musobanukirwe, ko imanza nyinshi zatangiye kugwa ndetse no muri iki gihe cy'inzibacyuho kuko byaba bidashoboka mu buryo bw'imikorere ko ibintu biba mu bundi buryo.

Kandi niko byanditswe, kandi niko bizakorwa, ubwo nzateranya abizerwa muri Kristo vuba aha kandi gitungura ubwo ubuza azakurwaho, kandi urubura ruzagwa, n'ibimenyetso bizafungurwa, n'impanda zizavuga! Ko abahamya banjye babiri bazahaguruka bazategeka umuriro kumanuka mu ijuru, bazakubita isi ibyago by'amoko yose uko babishatse kose, kandi bazatuma imvura itagwa ku isi mu minsi y'ubuhanuzi bwabo ku itegeko ryabo, kandi nyamara benshi bazanga kwihana! Musobanukirwe ko bazahagarara barwanya ikiyoka, n'inyamaswa n'umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma kandi niba ubushake bw'umuntu uwo ari we wese ari ukubagirira nabi uwo muntu agomba kugirirwa nabi muri ubwo buryo. Niba ubushake bw'umuntu uwo ari we wese ari ukubica uwo muntu agomba kwicwa muri ubwo buryo. Ibi ni ibitereko byanjye by'amatabaza bibiri n'ibiti byanjye by'iminzenze bibiri kandi ibintu byinshi, byinshi bazabihishura mu bihe byo guhishurwa kwabo kandi ubuhamya bwabo buzandikwa iteka ryose.

Ibi ni ibihe mubamo, bana banjye bato, kandi ibi ni ibihe mugomba gukomeza kwihanganira murusheho kwegera mu bushake bwanjye ku buzima bwanyu kuko ariho hari umutekano wanyu n'agakiza kanyu. Musobanukirwe, ko mwakuze nk'inyana zo mu kiraro mu bihe byanjye by'ubuntu n'imbabazi byatanzwe ku bw'agakiza k'imiryango yazimiye yatataniye mu mahanga bityo, amahanga akaba yarahawe umugisha kandi asukwaho urukundo, impuhwe, n'ubuntu kandi benshi bakabona agakiza binyuze muri byo. Ariko ubu ibihe byahindutse cyane, kandi ndabategeka kugendana ubwenge BUKOMEYE, kandi ko muba abanyabwenge nk'inzoka n'abaziranenge nk'inuma kuko ibi bihe biteje akaga mu buryo bushukana.

Kuko ikiyoka ubu kiboshywe ku isi kandi abaguye barushaho gukomera uko isegonda rishize, nyamara nta bushobozi na buke bafite bwo guhagarara imbere y'ijwi ryanjye rikomeye n'imbaraga n'ubushobozi byuzuye! Nuko rero, ndabacyaha kandi nkababuza ku bwanyu kuruta uko mushobora kubisobanukirwa muri iki gihe kandi nashyize ibintu byose by'ubwami bw'abanzi munsi y'ibirenge byanyu kugira ngo mubonyore niba bikenewe. Nuko rero, ntimubatinye kuko nanesheje byose, ndetse n'ubwami bwose bwa Satani; kandi namwe muzanesha muri ubwo buryo, nimuguma ari abizerwa ku Magambo yanjye y'amabwiriza natanze kandi ntanga ngo muyumvire.

Ntimutangare, bakunzi banjye, ndetse n'igihe abaguye biyerekana ku isi, ntimutangare na gato! Kuko iminsi ya Nowa yaje! Ndetse n'iminsi aho ba Satani n'abamarayika babo bajugunywa ku isi, ntibazigere basubira mu ijuru aho ubu birukanywe iteka mu gutsindwa kudasubirwaho. Ndetse n'iminsi aho abarinzi ubu barekuwe mu magereza yabo aho bari baboshywe kandi batandukanijwe umwe n'undi mu bubabare bukomeye ku bw'ibyaha byabo biteye ishozi bakoze kuri Yahweh n'ibyaremwe bye mu minsi ya kera kandi mu gutsindwa kudasubirwaho baboshywe kubera ukwigomeke kwabo n'indahiro barahiye gusohoza. Nyamara, indahiro zabo zizasohozwa iteka ryose ubwo bazabohwa bakajugunywa mu miriro y'igihano muri rya ziba rinini ry'amazuku yaka aho inyo yabo idapfa.

Ndetse n'iminsi, nanone, igaruka nk'uko byari mu minsi ya Nowa ubwo abarinzi bamanukaga ku Musozi wa Herumoni mu minsi ya Yaredi. Ndetse n'iminsi yagarutse aho abaguye ubu bakorana bya hafi n'abami b'isi n'abayobozi babo b'isi bihishe inyuma bafatanyije mu migambi yabo bagambiriye kuzana irimbuka rikomeye n'amakuba n'intambara vuba aha ubuza akurwaho, ubwo ububi bwabo buzaba butagikumirwa rwose. Yego, barekuwe ku isi, kandi ubuza nakurwaho ubwami bwose bwa Satani buzarekurwa ubwo urubura ruzagwa, n'ikuzimu hagafungurwa, n'amarembo yabo akazongera gukora. Dore! Nzanye abagabo barebare kugira ngo basohoze umujinya wanjye!

Ntimutangare, bakunzi banjye, ndetse n'igihe abaguye bamurikira ikirere munsi y'isanzure n'amatara yabo, n'imibumbe, nk'uko ubu biyerekana mu mashusho bafata n'imashini zabo ubu zikaba ziguruka mu bwisanzure kandi bazamutse guteza ibishuko byinshi n'urujijo ku maso y'isi n'ikoranabuhanga ryabo, ndetse no mu gihe cy'isoza ry'ibise, bigomba gusohozwa gitungura kandi bititezwe mu makuba vuba aha. Kandi nuko umuntu wa kamere atangara kandi agakekakeka kandi agashakisha impamvu zo kwirengagiza iby'umwuka ubu byongeye kuba ibya kamere nanone.

Yego, abantu mu kutizera kwabo birengagiza imbaraga za Yahweh zerekanwe kugira ngo abantu bakangukire gukiranuka kandi babone agakiza. Aho guha Yahweh icyubahiro ku bw'imirimo y'intoki ze n'ibimenyetso byari bigenewe kubaburira ku rubanza rwegereje; abantu ahubwo biha icyubahiro ubwabo n'ikoranabuhanga ryabo ritagira umumaro bakuye ku baguye: bityo urubanza rukaze kandi rukomeye ruraje! Ntimugire uruhare cyangwa umugabane mu mitekerereze yabo itagira kwizera kandi mukomeze guhimbaza Yahweh ku bw'imirimo ye y'agakiza yatanzwe mu migambi ye y'iteka y'agakiza ka Adamu mu maso ha Yesu Kristo, ari we Adamu wa nyuma.

Nuko rero ndabaha gusobanukirwa: naho haba ikiremwa cyose giteza impinduka mu ijuru cyangwa mu isi, cyangwa impinduka mu kirere cy'isi, cyangwa uwo ari we wese uhindura irema rya Yahweh mu buryo ubwo ari bwo bwose: Ndabategeka kumenya neza ko Yahweh ari umutegetsi w'ikirenga ku byaremwe bye kandi ko ikintu cyose kiba kiba gusa ku ruhushya rwa Yahweh, kandi abizi neza, ndetse no kugira ngo asohoze imigambi ye y'iteka y'agakiza k'umwana we w'ikirara Adamu. Musobanukirwe ko Yahweh yemerera abakiranirwa, abinangiye imitima, n'abatumvira kubaho mu buzima bwabo bw'ibyaha kugira ngo buzuze ibyaha byabo mu buryo bwuzuye kandi urubanza rwabo rwegereje ntibazarucika, keretse baje kuri Yesu Kristo mu kwihana kwuzuye mu gihe hakiri igihe.

N'ubwo bimeze bityo, Yahweh yemerera abakiranirwa gukora ibyaha kugira ngo mu rubanza bazishyurwe mu buryo bwuzuye bahabwa ingororano yuzuye y'ibikorwa byabo bibi ku mitwe yabo ubwabo iteka ryose n'iteka ryose mu birimi by'igihano, ndetse n'iyo icyo kintu kitari ngombwa kandi cyashoboraga kwirindwa mu gakiza binyuze mu kumvira gukiranuka. Mu buntu bwa Yahweh, n'imbabazi ze mu mugambi we w'iteka w'agakiza kuri Adamu; Yahweh yifuza ko nta muntu n'umwe warimbukira mu birimi by'umuriro, nyamara, abantu bose bahawe ubushake bwisanzuye bwo guhitamo ikibi, cyangwa guhitamo gukiranuka mu maso ha Yesu Kristo uzarema bushya abaza mu gakiza bose nk'umuntu umwe mushya, kandi bazabaho iteka ryose.

Kandi bityo abaguye bazarekurwa mu buryo bwuzuye mu rubanza ku batuye isi batumvira banga izina rya Yeshua HaMashiach ari we Yesu Kristo! Ku bw'ibyo, musobanukirwe ko abanga Yesu Kristo nk'Umwami bose, cyangwa ababaho mu kutizera no mu kutumvira amategeko yanjye bazasigara munsi y'ubutegetsi bwabo bw'igitugu n'ubugome aho bagambiriye guhanagura Adamu burundu kandi kinyamaswa ku buso bw'isi; mu migambi yabo yateguwe igihe kirekire aho bagambiriye kwiba isi mu maboko yanjye akomeye, ndetse n'imigambi badashobora kugeraho mu busazi bwabo bwo kwigomeka.

Nyamara, abantu bakomeza kugenda nkana bagana mu mutego wabo, ndetse n'igihe basobanura ibimenyetso bahawe by'urubanza rwabo rwegereje bakanga kwizera ko ruje mu mitekerereze yabo itagira kwizera ituruka ku burezi bwabo bw'ibinyoma aho ubwenge bwabo buhumwe amaso rwose ku isi nyakuri babamo, ndetse iyo si yaremwe na Yahweh NDIHO Ukomeye, uwo abantu bashaka guhakana kubaho kwe mu burezi bwabo bw'ibinyoma bwateguwe n'Abayezuwiti bwo kwiga barwanya kwiga. Niba mbabwiye iby'isi ntimwizere, muzizera mute, nimbabwira iby'ijuru? Kuko nta muntu wazamutse mu ijuru, keretse uwo wavuye mu ijuru, ari we Mwana w'umuntu uri mu ijuru. Ese muzicisha bugufi imitima yanyu mwizere Amagambo y'Umwana w'umuntu? Cyangwa muzagundira ibigirwamana by'imitima yanyu n'ibinyoma by'abapfapfa bize bavuga barwanya Amagambo yatanzwe na Yahweh mu maso ha Yesu Kristo?

Mu mitekerereze y'abantu itagira kwizera bashaka mu bupfapfa gutanga ibisubizo by'ibinyoma ku bimenyetso n'ibitangaza by'ibinyoma by'abaguye. Ndetse n'ibisubizo bitangwa n'abaguye ubwabo rimwe na rimwe ku banga Amagambo ya Adonai Yahweh bose, ayo Magambo akaba yaratanzwe kugira ngo yumvirwe binyuze mu nzira y'ukuri kandi nzima, iyo nzira akaba ari njye, Kristo Yesu rwose. Ku bw'ibyo, ntimwifatanye na bo mu mitekerereze yabo itagira kwizera na gato. Kandi bityo ndabaha gusobanukirwa, isi ni iya El Shaddai wayihaye Yeshua HaMashiach ari we Mwami w'abami n'Umutware w'abatware, ku bw'ibyo kandi mu by'ukuri, isi ni iy'Uwiteka n'ibiyuzuye! Kandi NTA KIREMWA NA KIMWE gishobora kuvana umurage wanjye mu maboko yanjye akomeye, kandi ndaje gufata umurage wanjye ku mbaraga mbukuye ku bigaruriye ibitari ibyabo bazajya mu rubanza rw'iteka mu buryo bwuzuye urwo batazigera bacika. Kandi nyamara bagundira mu bupfapfa ibyiringiro byabo by'ibinyoma ko mu bwinshi bwabo barekuwe bose bashobora gutsinda Yeshua HaMashiach, ndetse ibyiringiro nzamenagura rwose mu mbaraga z'ubushobozi bwanjye nahawe na Adonai Yahweh ubwe, uwo basangiye intebe ye y'ubwami na Yesu Kristo.

Ntimugwe mu buriganya bwabo bakunzi banjye kandi ntimutsindwe n'ubuyobe bukomeye n'urubanza rutari urwanyu. Kuko mwakuze nk'inyana zo mu kiraro mu bihe by'amahoro n'umutekano ugereranyije aho uwakumiraga yakumiraga kandi ubuntu n'imbabazi byiganje ku isi n'ubwo ibitotezo byari byinshi. Kandi noneho amahoro n'umutekano byagabanutse ku isi kandi hafi ya byose byarashize, n'ubwo uwakumiraga agikumira. Kandi abaguye bakomeza imigambi yabo n'intego zabo aho bashaka gushyiraho igihe cyabo cya zahabu cyasubukuwe, nyamara bazabona gusa urubanza rukaze kandi rukomeye.

Ku bw'ibyo, ntimwigeze munyura muri ibi bihe mbere bakunzi banjye kandi nzakomeza kubayobora intambwe ku yindi mbaganisha hafi y'umurongo wanyu wo gusoza aho ntegereje kandi mbashyigikiye mwambuka imirongo yo gusoza y'imirimo yanyu yo gukorera yatanzwe n'ukuboko kwanjye ku bw'icyubahiro cyanyu, agakiza, n'ingororano! Ku bw'ibyo, ntimubatinye mugeni wanjye kuko muri abaneshi banjye, ntutinye mubiri wanjye kuko ingororano ikomeye ari iyanyu kandi abantu banjye 144,000 bazakora imirimo ikomeye ku isi kuruta n'iyo nakoze mu bihe byanjye by'umurimo ku isi nk'umwana w'intama w'Imana igihe Ponsiyo Pilato yari umutegetsi wa Yudaya!

Kuko nk'uko byari bimeze mu minsi yabanjirije umwuzure, bararyaga bakanywa, bararongoraga, kandi bashyingiraga, kugeza ku munsi Nowa yinjiriye mu Nkuge, kandi ntibagira icyo bamenya, kugeza ubwo umwuzure waje, ukabatwara bose, niko n'ukuza k'Umwana w'umuntu kuzaba. Icyo gihe babiri bazaba bari mu murima, umwe azatwarwa, undi asigare. Abagore babiri bazaba basya ku rusyo: umwe azatwarwa, undi asigare. Mube maso rero: kuko mutazi isaha shobuja azaziraho. Ibi mubimenye neza, ko iyaba nyiri urugo yari azi igihe umujura azazira, yari kuba maso, ntiyemere ko inzu ye icukurwa. Nuko rero namwe mwitegure: kuko mu isaha mudatekereza, Umwana w'umuntu azaza.

Ni nde rero mugaragu wizerwa kandi w'umunyabwenge, uwo shobuja yashyizeho kuba umutware w'abo mu rugo rwe, kugira ngo abahe ibyo kurya mu gihe cyabyo? Hahirwa uwo mugaragu, uwo shobuja naza, azasanga abikora atyo. Ndababwira ukuri yuko, azamugira umutware w'ibintu bye byose. Ariko niba uwo mugaragu mubi azavuga mu mutima we ati, databuja atinze kuza, agatangira gukubita bagenzi be, no kurya, no kunywana n'abasinzi, shebuja w'uwo mugaragu azaza ku munsi, atamutegerejeho, no mu isaha atazi, amuce kabiri, amuhe umugabane we hamwe n'indyarya: aho ni ho hazaba kurira, no guhekenya amenyo.

Kandi bityo ndababurira nanone, kuko abo nkunda bose ndababurira, kandi abo nkunda bose ndabacyaha, kandi abo nkunda bose ndabahana kandi abo nkunda bose ndabakubita inshuro nyinshi kuko Yahweh abafata nk'abana, kandi ni uwuhe Mubyeyi udahana abana be, cyangwa se ntimuri abana ahubwo muri ibibyarwa n'ibicibwa. Ku bw'ibyo, mwihanganire igihano cyanjye kandi mwumve Amagambo yanjye yo gucyaha no guhana kugira ngo mubashe gutsinda abaguye muri byose muri ubu buzima aho bashaka kubatega, ndetse kugira ngo mutazagera ku murage wanyu wuzuye mbafitiye mu kuboko kwanjye gukomeye. Ndetse n'umurage mudashobora gutakaza niba mwumviye amategeko yanjye mu buryo bwuzuye mugomba kwitondera mu rukundo rwanyu kuri Yahweh, ndetse no kuri Yeshua HaMashiach.

Musobanukirwe urukundo rwanjye kuri mwe torero ryanjye, mubiri wanjye, ndetse na mugeni wanjye kandi mumenye ko ndikubahamagara ngo munyegere kurushaho kuko vuba aha nzamanuka mvuye mu ijuru n'ijwi rya marayika mukuru n'impanda ya Yahweh, kandi impanda izavuga n'iryo jwi rizarangurura kandi umunsi, igihe, n'isaha byo kuboneka kwanjye byamaze gutangazwa mu ijuru kandi biri hafi cyane gusohozwa ku isi, ndetse mu buryo butunguranye kandi butari bwitezwe nk'uko byasezeranijwe. Kandi abifuza kandi bagakunda kuboneka kwanjye bose bazabona ingororano ikomeye ndetse bahabwe ikamba ryo gukiranuka ku bw'ibyiringiro byabo kuko abafite ibyiringiro nk'ibyo ari abapfuye rwose ku by'iyi si mbi y'iki gihe, kandi bazaba baratsinze rwose ku bw'imbaraga, ubushobozi, no gukiranuka by'Umwami wabo w'Iteka w'Icyubahiro!

Ndi Yesu w'i Nazareti, ubahamagara ngo munyegere kurushaho torero ryanjye, mubiri wanjye, ndetse na mugeni wanjye.

Ndi Yesu Kristo, ubahamagara ngo munyegere kurushaho muri ibi bihe mbere gato y'uko mwinjizwa mu ngororano z'umurage wanyu nzabaha vuba aha mu ijuru nyuma y'uko mutwawe.

Ndi Kristo Yesu, mu by'ukuri, mu by'ukuri ubahesha gukwira muri byose, ku bw'ibyo muruhukire mu mirimo yanjye no gukiranuka kandi munsabe mu kwicisha bugufi kandi muve mu nzira zose zo kwirata zo kwihagije, kuko mugiye kwifatanya rwose n'Umukwe wanyu uzabaha iteka ryose ubwinshi n'ubutunzi bw'ibintu byose mu murage wanyu muri Kristo.

Ndi Yeshua HaMashiach, ubahamagara kandi ubinginga ngo muze kuri njye rwose mu rukundo rwanjye kuri mwe bana banjye, kugira ngo agakiza kanyu kuzuye gasohozwe rwose muri ibi bihe byo gucungurwa kwuzuye ku bizerwa muri Kristo, baba abaruhukiye mu mutima w'isi uko basinziriye mu mahoro mu gukiranuka, cyangwa baba abazima kandi basigaye ku buso bw'isi mu kuboneka kwanjye, igihe cyabo kikaba kigeze.

Ku bw'ibyo, musobanukirwe akamaro n'ubwihutirwe bw'ibihe kuko nta gihe gisigaye cyo kutumvira kandi mu by'ukuri kigeze hafi rwose ubu, ko mu kuboneka kwanjye uwizerwa muri Kristo azatwarwa mu mutekano mu ijuru aho ingororano ikomeye izaba iyabo kandi bazaba barokowe umujinya uje, n'urubanza rugiye kugwira isi mu makuba akomeye. Kandi bityo mu buryo nk'ubwo mu kuboneka kwanjye utari uwizerwa kandi utari muri Kristo azasigirwa umuriro w'utunganya, aho bazakizwa nibambaza izina rya Yesu Umwami wabo kugeza ku iherezo ryabo kandi nta yandi mahitamo asigaye ku bo mu mahanga banze agakiza kabo kuzuye mu gihe cyo kuboneka kwanjye.

Kandi niko bimeze, bakunzi banjye ko nje kubabwira nanone, ngo ntimutambikire ibumoso, cyangwa iburyo ahubwo mukomeze ibirenge byanyu bishikamye kuri iyo nzira igororotse kandi ifunganye aho ntera abagwaneza n'abiyoroshya mu mutima kugira intsinzi yuzuye uko batsinda abaguye ku bw'imbaraga zanjye zikomeye, no gukiranuka nahaye ku buntu abumvira. Ku bw'ibyo, mugumise amaso yanyu atumbiriye mu ntego imwe ku Mwami wanyu w'Iteka wo Gukiranuka kugira ngo mugere ku ngororano yanyu yuzuye kandi kugira ngo muruhukire rwose mu mahoro n'ihumure nzana uko mbamisha amahoro n'ihumure byanjye mu rukundo rwanjye kuri mwe. Kugira ngo ndetse n'imbere y'amakuba n'ibigeragezo mubashe guhagarara mukomeye iteka ryose mu kumeneka no kubabazwa kwanyu uko muruhukira mu mbaraga zanjye zidashobora gutsindwa.

Ndi Yeshua HaMashiach, hamwe na Yahweh, NDIHO Ukomeye, twohereje Amagambo y'Ubutumwa Bwiza bw'Iteka binyuze ku ntumwa yanjye ikomeye n'umugaragu wanditse mu budahemuka kandi agasoma aya Magambo ku bw'ukumvira n'agakiza by'abo aya Magambo yoherejweho bose. Kandi binyuze ku ntumwa yanjye ikomeye n'umugaragu ni ho Amagambo y'Ubutumwa Bwiza bw'Iteka yoherejwe. Mu by'ukuri, mu by'ukuri, Amagambo y'Ubutumwa Bwiza bw'Iteka azabungabungwa mu makuba n'imanza bije bigiye guturika hirya no hino ku isi. Musobanukirwe ko andi buhamya azatangwa kandi akandikwa mu bitabo byo mu ijuru kandi Amagambo ya Adonai Yahweh azuzura isi mu gukiranuka kose no kwera nyakuri!

Ndaje vuba cyane ku bizerwa muri Kristo, kandi mu by'ukuri ibihe byabo bigeze! Ibihe aho umuganura w'isarura uzahura n'isohozwa ry'isezerano n'ingororano mu maso ha Yahweh ubwe (ndetse n'akanya Yahweh yifuje kuva mu bihe bya kera), na Yeshua HaMashiach n'umutwe w'abamarayika batabarika abo haba ibyishimo mu maso habo hakomeye iyo umuntu umwe gusa abonye agakiza k'iteka. Nyamara, niko bizaba ubwo nzahamagara abizerwa muri Kristo mu rugo ku ngororano yabo y'iteka ko bazafata ibyicaro byabo mu miryango kuko ari Isirayeli y'Imana kandi bazuzuza imirongo y'amatsinda yabo kandi umutwe mugari w'abami n'abatambyi nzawimika kandi nyweze ku bw'umurimo w'iteka mu bwami bwanjye bw'iteka, butagira iherezo.

Ndi Yeshua HaMashiach, ari we kandi Mwami w'Isarura, kandi mu by'ukuri, mu by'ukuri ibihe by'isarura ry'umuganura byageze, n'igihe cy'isarura ry'umuganura ku bw'ingororano mu buryo nk'ubwo nacyo cyageze.

Ibihe ni ubu. Sela!
