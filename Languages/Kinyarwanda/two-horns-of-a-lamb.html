Amahembe Abiri y'Umwana w'Intama

Dore, ndaje kubereka amabanga y'ibi bihe by'imperuka byageze ku isi mbere y'uko amakuba ya Yakobo yisuka ku isi yose. Nimutege amatwi yanyu mu kwicisha bugufi mwumve Amagambo ya Yeshua HaMashiach kugira ngo musobanukirwe ibihe murimo, ndetse n'ibihe byuzuyemo ibishuko bikomeye. Nyamara, uko niko ibihe murimo bimeze kandi niko ibihe by'urubanza byageze ku isi yose bimeze. Dore! Nimwumve Amagambo ya Kristo Yesu bakunzi banjye, kandi mwumve Amagambo yanjye nohereje kugira ngo mpumurize imitima yanyu kandi mubahe gusobanukirwa ibintu byinshi murimo kubona byugarije isi, kandi kugira ngo mukomeze gukurikiza itegeko ryanjye ry'iteka ryo kutagira ubwoba! Kubera ukuri kw'iteka, amakuba yageze ku isi n'imanza zateguriwe abakiranirwa, n'abakobanyi, n'abanga amasezerano y'amahoro ya Yahweh ntabwo byagenewe abakiranutsi n'abizerwa n'abemeye amasezerano y'amahoro ya Yahweh kuko ari ko byanditswe kandi niko bizakorwa.

Nuko rero, bakunzi banjye, nimundeke mbahumurize mu bihe by'amakuba kugira ngo mubone icyo muha abakene kandi kugira ngo mukunde Yahweh n'umutima wanyu wose, ubugingo, ubwenge n'imbaraga zanyu zose kandi kugira ngo mukunde bagenzi banyu nk'uko mwikunda, kuko ni ukuri, ni ukuri, ababaho muri ubwo buryo bose bagaragaza inzira za Yahweh mwanyigiyeho, Umwami wanyu w'Iteka ryose w'Gukiranuka, kuko niko ndi! Nuko rero ndabasaba gukebwa imitima yanyu mu kumvira no kwemera Amagambo yanjye y'ukuri ari na yo Magambo y'ihumure kugira ngo mugire amahoro mu bihe by'amakuba, ndetse n'igihe ibise by'imperuka bizagera ku ndunduro yabyo abizerwa muri Kristo baremwa bundi bushya kandi bavukira mu ijuru mu ishusho ya Kristo Yesu ari we Adamu wa nyuma.

Kandi mu kuri kw'iteka ibihe byo gucungurwa byuzuye byageze kandi gucungurwa no gukizwa byuzuye by'abizerwa muri Kristo bigiye kuba mu buryo butunguranye kandi butitezwe ubu, kandi umunsi, igihe, n'isaha ubu bizwi mu ijuru, kandi ingabo zo mu ijuru zihagaze ziteguye zitegereje urwo rusaku rukomeye, zitegereje kuvuga kw'impanda ya nyuma kuko impanda yatanzwe kandi ibihe ni ubu. Ndetse n'akanya gato aho gucungurwa kwasezeranyijwe n'agakiza kadasubirwaho bizasohora mu kanya nk'ako guhumbya mu butungane butangaje kandi ntihazabaho gutinda ukundi. Ndetse n'akanya ko gusohozwa byuzuye aho abizerwa bose muri Kristo mu bihe byanjye by'ubuntu guhera ku munsi wa Pentekote muri yubile zose zashize, ubwo Yahweh yampaga uwo mwuka wera w'ubwana nasutse kuri bo ubwo bari bahuje umutima mu mwanya umwe. Ubwo mu buryo butunguranye humvikanaga urusaku ruvuye mu ijuru, nk'umuyaga uhuha cyane, rwuzura inzu yose bari bicayemo. Kandi haboneka indimi zigabanyijemo kabiri, nk'umuriro, zihagarara kuri buri wese muri bo. Kandi bose buzuzwa umwuka wera, batangira kuvuga mu zindi ndimi, nk'uko umwuka wabahaga kuvuga. Kandi uko niko impinja zanjye muri Kristo zari zikivuka bundi bushya zahawe kuvuga neza mu ndimi nabo ubwabo batumvaga ubwo bavugaga bashize amanga imirimo itangaje ya Yahweh!

Kandi uko niko kuvuka kw'Itorero ryanjye kwagenze kandi niko amasezerano arireba ameze, ko ni ukuri, ni ukuri, amarembo y'ikuzimu atazanesha Itorero ryanjye ry'ukuri kandi ntabwo azigera arinesha! Nubwo benshi bazagwa mu buryarya butagira ukwizera bwaturutse mu mitima yabo itagira ukwizera ubwo batungaga agatoki Yesu nk'Umwami mu kutizera, ndetse bakanga kwizera ko nazuwe mu bapfuye koko! Ndetse no kwatura Yesu nk'Umwami, aho batigeze bihana ibyaha cyangwa ngo bareke inzira zabo mbi, ahubwo bagahitamo ubuhumyi bwazanywe n'imigani y'Abayesuwiti yononnye Ubukristo ikabugira udutsiko tw'amadini, kandi mu kuri kw'iteka Kristo ntiyigeze agabanywa, kandi Yahweh ntiyigeze akobwa! Nuko rero, musobanukirwe ko umwuka w'ubwana wimwa abegera Yesu w'i Nazareti bafite ukwatura k'uburyarya kandi kutagira ukwizera. Nuko rero ndabaza nti, ukwatura kw'ukwizera gukiza gushobora gute kuva ku minwa ifatanye n'imitima itagira ukwizera? Nyamara, ayo niyo makuba y'ibihe byanyu ko umuntu wa kamere afite amahitamo yo kwakira gucungurwa byuzuye no kubabarirwa ibyaha byuzuye ariko akabyanga, agahitamo ikibi n'urubanza rwe rw'iteka. Kandi ibi ni ukubera ko abantu ba kamere benshi, ndetse n'abantu badafite umwuka wanjye wera w'ubwana, banga kwemera ko bari mu mimerere y'icyaha n'iyangirika, bityo bagahagarara mu bwibone bavuga ko imirimo yabo y'ubusa ari yo ikiranuka, ndetse n'ibyo bavuga bitazashobora guhagarara mu manza zizaza, cyangwa ngo byemerwe imbere yanjye kuko nta muntu n'umwe ushobora kuza kuri Yahweh, NDI HO Ukomeye, keretse anyuze kuri njye, Yesu w'i Nazareti!

Urubanza ruraguye! Urubanza ruraguye! Urubanza ruraguye! Dore! Urubanza ruguye kuri Babuloni y'Ibanga, nk'uko byasezeranyijwe, ndetse no kubera ko imiburo yanjye myinshi yirengagijwe, ndetse no kubera ko Amagambo yanjye yoherejwe n'intumwa zanjye n'abahanuzi kugira ngo abahisemo urubanza n'irimbuka ryabo mu kwigomeka no kutumvira bakizwe yajugunywe nk'imyanda itagira agaciro. Uko niko byanditswe, kandi niko bigiye gusohora: Kandi amahembe icumi wabonye ku nyamaswa, azanga maraya, amuhindure umusaka, amwambike ubusa, arye inyama ze, amutwike n'umuriro. Kuko Imana ishyize mu mitima yabo gukora ibyo ishaka, no guhuza inama, no guha inyamaswa ubwami bwabo, kugeza aho amagambo y'Imana azasohorera.

Kandi niko bigiye gusohora ko urubanza rugwa kuri Babuloni y'Ibanga, kandi ibinezeza byayo biyikurwaho mu rubanza. Kandi mu gihe gito ubu bigiye gusohora byuzuye nk'uko byanditswe na Yohana Uhishurirwa wanditse mu budahemuka Amagambo yatanzwe n'ukuboko kwanjye: Kuko mu isaha imwe ubutunzi bungana butyo buhindutse umusaka. Nuko rero, ubutunzi n'ibinezeza birimo gukurwa ku isi kandi ntibizagaruka kugeza ubwo ubwami bwanjye bw'imyaka igihumbi buzaba buje kandi ibi bikozwe mu rubanza n'igihano ku bararikiye ibyo byiza bafite intego y'uko bashobora kwihana baramutse bahindukiye bafite ukwizera kwuzuye mu mitima yabo kuri Yesu w'i Nazareti bambaza izina ryanjye bakigomwa ubuzima bwabo kugira ngo babone agakiza mu isi yaciriwe urubanza kandi igiye gupfa izarimbuka byuzuye kandi igacirwa urubanza kandi nta n'umwe uzarokoka keretse binyuze mu buntu n'imbabazi bya Yahweh bitangirwa mu maso ha Kristo Yesu Umwami w'Gukiranuka wazutse akazamuka mu ijuru!

Nyamara, Yahweh ni uwizerwa kuko yagiranye isezerano na Nowa ko atazigera yongera kurimbura isi mu buryo butunguranye n'umwuzure ukundi, ahubwo ko isi izatwarwa n'umuriro mu rubanza rwa nyuma rugiye kugwa byuzuye ruhera mu nzu y'Imana. Kuko ndetse no muri ibi bihe by'urubanza rwa nyuma, ubuntu n'imbabazi n'agakiza bizabonwa n'imbaga y'abantu benshi bazabona amakosa y'inzira zabo kandi bakambaza izina rya Yesu kugira ngo bakizwe; no kuri Isirayeli izemera Yeshua HaMashiach nka Mesiya wabo w'ukuri kandi benshi bazahabwa kwinjira mu bwami bwanjye bw'imyaka igihumbi aho gukiranuka gusa kuzaboneka. Kuko ndi Umwami w'Gukiranuka kandi mu gukiranuka nza guca urubanza no kurwana intambara!

Nuko rero, musobanukirwe ko Yahweh ari umuriro ukongora, kandi ndi Kristo Yesu wicaye mfite imivuba mu ntoki nkongeza imiriro yo kweza kugira ngo ubutunzi bwa Yahweh (ari bwo bwoko bwe) bwezwe inkamba zose kugira ngo bazahagarare imbere y'Umwami wabo n'Umukiza w'iteka bambaye atari imirimo yabo y'ubusa yo gukiranirwa, ahubwo bazahagarara imbere yanjye bambaye imyenda yera y'igitare, itagira inenge kuko bemeye gukiranuka kwatanzwe n'ukuboko kwanjye mu Isezerano Rishya nzoresha ntegeka isi mu gukiranuka, ndetse Isezerano ry'ubutungane ritazigera rihagarara. Kandi ku bemeye agakiza muri ubu buryo bizavugwa kuri bo ko ni ukuri batakunze ubuzima bwabo kugeza ku gupfa ahubwo bazaba barahisemo kuva muri iyi si mbi mu cyubahiro cyangwa ubwitange, ndetse mu cyubahiro cyabo nitanze nk'umwana w'intama w'Imana kugira ngo abahisemo Yesu Kristo nk'umukiza wabo bose bazabeho iteka mu butungane imbere ya Yahweh NDI HO Ukomeye! Nuko rero, musobanukirwe ko umugaragu ataruta shebuja.

Kandi uko niko bimeze ko urubanza rutangirira mu nzu y'Imana kandi amakuba ya Yakobo agiye kwisuka ku isi yose, vuba aha abizerwa muri Kristo nibamara gukoranirizwa hamwe, hanyuma ibimenyetso bizafungurwa, n'impanda zizavuga kandi umujinya w'Umwana w'Intama uzagwa byuzuye ku isi kuko Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda, umuzi wa Dawidi, yanesheje kugira ngo ifungure igitabo, no gufungura ibimenyetso byacyo birindwi mu kumvira gukiranuka ubushake n'umugambi w'iteka w'agakiza watanzwe na Yahweh mu Ibanga Rikomeye ry'Imana ku ba Adamu bazemera agakiza ko gukiranuka no ku rubanza rw'abanyabyaha bazakurwa ku isi mu manza zikiranuka zizaza.

Kuko mu kuri iyaba abatware b'iyi si bari basobanukiwe Ibanga Rikomeye ry'Imana nahishuriye intumwa Pawulo ubwo namwigishaga, kandi akandika neza kugira ngo abezwa na ryo bose bumvire gukiranuka: ko abatware b'iyi si batari kuzigera babamba Umwami w'icyubahiro iyaba bari basobanukiwe Ibanga Rikomeye ry'Imana. Kuko mu kwitanga kwanjye nta cyaha mfite mu maboko yabo yuzuye urwango, mu rwango rwabo rwa kinyamaswa batambye batabizi umwana w'intama w'Imana ari we maraso atariho urubanza! Bityo bacungura Adamu byuzuye ku bazahitamo bose kwambaza izina ryanjye kugira ngo bakizwe, bityo bagacika iyangirika ry'iteka mu maraso ya Adamu ryazanywe n'inzoka mu Busitani bwa Edeni icyo gihe cyose gishize. Nyamara niko bimeze, ko ba Satani mu irari ryabo ry'urwango n'ubujiji bafashije kuzana agakiza ka Adamu bashaka kurimbura ku isi! Keretse ko baneshejwe byuzuye n'ibikorwa byabo mu bujiji bwabo bw'ubwenge bw'iteka bwa Yahweh, ndetse NDI HO Ukomeye, bashatse kuneshesha amayeri, ndetse ikintu kidashoboka kitazigera kiba kuko Yahweh afatira abanyamayeri mu rubanza rw'iteka batazigera bacika.

Nuko rero, musobanukirwe ko ari njye ukongeza imiriro yo kweza kugira ngo abazaza kuri Yahweh mu maso ha Yesu Kristo bose babone agakiza byuzuye kandi baze kuri njye byuzuye kugira ngo baremwe bundi bushya byuzuye mu bihe byabo bikwiye no mu rutonde rwabo. Kandi uko niko bimeze ko igihe cyageze cyo gukoranya abizerwa muri Kristo, ndetse n'umuganura w'abapfuye, ndetse n'isarura rikomeye rya sayiri rigiye kuba mu buryo butunguranye ubu kuko ibihe by'isarura byageze mu busitani bw'ubuntu bwa Yahweh aho isarura rikomeye kandi rikiranuka rizakoranirizwa mu bubiko bwa Yahweh kandi umurama uzabohwa kandi utwikwe iteka ryose mu miriro idazima y'igihano, niko Umwami w'Isarura avuga!

Uko niko bimeze, mu gihe cyo kwandika ibi gukoranya gukomeye kw'abizerwa muri Kristo ntikuraba kandi benshi mu bizerwa banjye barwana no kutabura ibyiringiro byo kugaragara kw'Umwami wabo kwegereje binyuze mu kutihangana n'ibishuko byinshi. Nuko rero, njyewe, Kristo Yesu, ndabaha gusobanukirwa ko gukoranya abizerwa muri Kristo byamaze gutangira kandi ntibishobora guhagarikwa cyangwa ngo bitinzwe, kandi nuko musobanukirwe ko ibyatangajwe kandi bigashyirwa mu bikorwa mu ntebe y'ubwami n'imbaraga za Yahweh bidashobora guhindurwa, cyangwa ngo bihindurwe na gato kandi bizasohora neza nk'uko byahanuwe kandi mu gihe cyiza cyane cyatoranyijwe na Yahweh ubwe, kuko icyo Yahweh yagambiriye cyose kizasohora mu butungane bwuzuye kuko uko niko icyubahiro cya Yahweh kimeze, NDI HO Ukomeye!

Nimusohoke muri bo kandi mwitandukanye, kandi nuko ndabongera kubaburira kuko igihe kigeze cyo kuva mu maganya y'iyi si mbi kuko kugenda kwanyu kwegereje. Ndabagira inama yo kubaho igihe cyanyu gisigaye, gihita vuba mu gukiranuka kugira ngo mube mwiteguye ijwi ry'Umwami wanyu kugira ngo mbategeke gukorera bagenzi banyu mugomba gukunda, ndetse mu buryo bumwe mukunda mwebwe ubwanyu kugira ngo mubibe imbuto zo gukiranuka mu mitima yabo kandi muzane abasigaye inyuma mu Nkuge mbere y'uko urwo rugi runini rufunguye mu ijuru rwikubita rugafungwa mu rubanza. Ndabagira inama yo kubaho igihe gisigaye mu gukiranuka kugira ngo mube mwiteguye ijwi ry'Umwami wanyu kandi kugira ngo mwumve urwo rusaku rukomeye ubwo impanda ya nyuma izavuga kugira ngo ikoranye abizerwa muri Kristo. Kandi kugaragara kwanjye kuzabonwa kandi kumenyekane ku isi yose kuko nk'uko umurabyo uva iburasirazuba, ukabonekera iburengerazuba, niko no kuza k'Umwana w'umuntu kuzaba. Nyamara niko bizaba ubwo niyerekaga Pawulo mu nzira ijya i Damasiko ko abemerwa mu ijuru bazumva impanda kandi bagasobanukirwa ijwi ryayo kandi benshi bazabona icyubahiro cyanjye cyisuka mu kirere. Nyamara, benshi ntibazashobora gutandukanya urwo rusaku rukomeye kandi ntibasobanukirwe ijwi ritanzwe na rwo, bityo bazasigara.

Nuko ndabaha gusobanukirwa ko mu gihe cyo kugaragara kwanjye umucyo w'icyubahiro cyanjye uzuzura ijuru kandi icyubahiro cyanjye kizabonwa kandi isi izamenya kandi isobanukirwe mu kanya gato ko Kristo Yesu yagaragaye kuko abizerwa muri Kristo bazatwarwa mu buryo butunguranye mu mutekano w'ijuru aho bazahagarara imbere y'Umwami wabo, ndetse baremwe bundi bushya mu gukiranuka kw'iteka ryose! Kandi abasigaye inyuma bari bizeye neza ko bazemerwa mu kugaragara kwanjye bazarira, baboroge, kandi bagwe mu makuba akomeye kandi asharira. Ndetse n'ubwo, uburemere bw'imimerere yabo n'ahazaza habo bubagwaho. Hanyuma benshi muri bo baziyemeza byuzuye kwambaza izina ryanjye uko byagenda kose no kwambaza izina ryanjye kugeza ku iherezo, kandi bazazurirwa mu cyubahiro kidashobora kuvugwa kuko uburemere bw'ingororano yabo y'iteka budakwiriye kugereranywa n'imibabaro yagaragaye muri iyi si mbi, niko Umwami wanyu w'iteka wamanitswe ku giti ku bushake bwe kugira ngo mukizwe avuga.

Ndetse n'ubwo, nzabashyiramo umwiyemezo udashobora kuvunika wo kwihanganira ibigeragezo n'ibizamini bizaba byageze byuzuye ku isi, ndetse n'ibigeragezo n'ibizamini bigikumiriwe kandi bifatanishijwe ikimenyetso. Nyamara, bazakora imirimo myinshi ikomeye yo gukorera no gukunda bagenzi babo muri ibi bihe kandi nzabaha ibitangaza byinshi bikomeye kugira ngo bakizwe kandi benshi bazihanganira ibigeragezo n'ibyubahiro byo kwitangira ukwizera muri ibi bihe, bityo babone umurage n'ingororano bifite agaciro k'iteka bitarinjira byuzuye mu mitima y'abantu muri iki gihe. Kandi nuko mbwira abazasoma aya Magambo y'Ubutumwa Bwiza Bw'Iteka bose kutareba ibumoso cyangwa iburyo ahubwo mugumise amaso yanyu kuri Yesu Kristo uzabaha imbaraga zo kwihangana kugira ngo abandi benshi bashobore gukizwa binyuze ku rugero rwanyu n'ubuhamya bwanyu.

Nyamara, niko bizaba ko ikiyoka, inyamaswa yo mu nyanja, n'inyamaswa yo mu isi (ari na we muhanuzi w'ibinyoma) bose bazazamuka mu gihe cyabo, ndetse n'igihe bazarekurwa n'ubakumira kugira ngo bakore ibyo bahawe gusohoza mu gihe gito bafite, kandi ntibazigera bazamuka ukundi muri ubwo buryo. Kandi mu kinyoma gikomeye azavuga rwose ko yazutse mu bapfuye, kuko inyamaswa yo mu isi izigira nk'iyakomeretse ku mutwe kandi amakuba akomeye azakurikiraho nk'ingaruka, ndetse nk'uko byahanuwe mbere na Yahweh mu Mwana w'Intama Ufite Amahembe Abiri. Musobanukirwe ko bazihisha mu gihe cy'intambara yabo y'amakuba barimo kurwana ubu izarekurwa byuzuye ubwo ubakumira azakurwaho kandi intambara yabo izasohozwa n'irimbuka ryose intambara yabo izateza. Kuko mu kavuyo kenshi ari ho gahunda yabo nshya izazamuka kandi inyamaswa yo mu isi izabeshya ivuge ko yazutse mu bapfuye kugira ngo ikize Isirayeli kandi yeze isi ikureho ikibi, ndetse n'ikibi bo ubwabo bazazana byuzuye.

Nyamara rero ndabaha gusobanukirwa ibishuko izi nyamaswa mbi zizana kugira ngo mutabigwamo. Nuko rero, mumenye ko abaguye borora abantu nk'uko umworozi yorora inka. Bavanga kandi bagahuza bagakora ibizira byabo kandi bakiyita imana kubera kuvanga imbuto zitagakwiye kuvangwa, kuko Yahweh yaremye ubuzima nk'uko amoko yabyo ari kandi buri gihe byari biteganyijwe ko Yahweh yaremye inyamaswa zo mu isi nk'uko amoko yazo ari, n'amatungo nk'uko amoko yayo ari kugira ngo abungabunge ubuziranenge n'ubwiza bwa buri kiremwa Yahweh yaremye mu rukundo rwe rukiranuka ku byo yaremye. Nyamara, abaguye bacumura ku byaremwe byose bya Yahweh kuko bashaka kuvanga, guhuza, no gukora kopi z'amoko ku yandi, ndetse kugira ngo biyite imana, keretse ko bose ari abiyita bo batari bo.

Ku bw'ibyo, mumenye ko abaguye ari abashaka kwivanga n'imbuto z'abantu ariko ntibazafatana, nk'uko icyuma kitavangwa n'ibumba. Mumenye ko abaguye bakoze ikoranabuhanga ryinshi ry'ibinyoma n'amayeri byo kugenzura no kuyobya abatuye isi, ndetse n'abishimira gukiranirwa bose. Mumenye ko bakoze ibizira byinshi byakorewe kurimbura umuntu wa kamere ku isi. Mumenye ko abarinzi bongeye gukora ibizira byabo, kandi mumenye ko abaguye bize kwigana no gukora kopi y'ubuzima bwahumetswe mu mazuru y'abantu kuko abantu baremwe mu ishusho ya Yahweh.

Ku bw'ibyo, mumenye ko ikizira cyose cyakozwe n'abaguye kitakozwe mu ishusho ya Yahweh ahubwo ari ukwigana ubuzima, kandi ntigikozwe byuzuye mu bwoko bwa Adamu bityo rero, ntigishobora gucungurwa cyangwa ngo kibone agakiza kandi kigamije kurimbura rwose Adamu n'ibizira byose bikozwe muri ubwo buryo bigenewe gusohoza irari rya ba Se byanze bikunze. Icyagoramye ntigishobora kugororwa kandi ntibazagira umuzi cyangwa ishami kandi bose bazajya mu rubanza rw'iteka kubera ibibi bakoze.

Mumenye kandi, ko inyamaswa yo mu isi ije gutegeka ifite ubumara bwayo mu ntoki, ndetse na rwa kurumwa n'inzoka kw'iteka ari rwo kimenyetso cy'inyamaswa. Mumenye ko umuntu wese ukomoka kuri Adamu, yaba umugabo cyangwa umugore, uzemera ku bushake gufata ikimenyetso cy'inyamaswa azahindurwa iteka ryose ave mu ishusho ya Yahweh iteka ryose, kandi bazaba abacakara baboshywe n'ubushake bw'intekerezo z'ikivunge kuko intekerezo zabo zizahuzwa n'urusobe rw'ibintu ruzabaho, ndetse na interineti yakorewe abadayimoni n'abamarayika baguye kugira ngo bayigarurire kandi bayigenzure. Iki? Mwatekerezaga ko ikoranabuhanga ryabo ryari iryo kugirira neza umuntu kandi ko abantu bakoze iri koranabuhanga binyuze mu gukorakora mu igaraji? Mwange ibinyoma byabo bakundwa banjye kandi ntimugwe mu mutego w'imigani yabo idafite ishingiro.

Nuko rero bizaba ko abazafata ikimenyetso cyabo bose bazahuzwa n'ikoranabuhanga ryabo kandi bazaba ihuriro mu rusobe rwabo kandi bazagenzurwa n'ikoranabuhanga ryabo n'imirongo yabo kandi bazaba abavumwe bagenda badashobora kuzabona agakiza mu mimerere yabo yahinduwe kandi abaziyunga bose ku ikoranabuhanga ryabo n'impinduka zabo baboshywe iteka ku manza zabo, ntibazigera babivamo, kandi bazagira umugabane wabo mu murage w'abaguye w'amazuku yaka iteka ryose.

Nyamara, abaguye bakomeza mu busazi bwabo no mu gusara kwabo kuko bashaka gucika isanzure baboshyweho kugira ngo bacirwe urubanza. Bakomeza gukora ibizira byabo kandi bafite ikoranabuhanga ryinshi umuntu wa kamere ataramenya. Kandi nk'uko byari mu minsi ya kera umuntu wa kamere azakomeza gusenga abaguye kubera ikoranabuhanga ryabo n'amayeri yabo. Ibyinshi mu bizira byabo bikorwa ndetse nk'uko byari mu minsi ya Nowa binyuze mu busambanyi hagati y'abamarayika baguye n'abagore bakuwe mu bana b'abantu.
