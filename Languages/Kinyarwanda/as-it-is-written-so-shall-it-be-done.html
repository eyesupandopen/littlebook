Ubuhamya Bubiri: Isirayeli
Ibisharira N'ibiryohereye
7 Nzeri 2024

Uko byanditswe, niko bizakorwa!

Nimwikoranye mwumve amagambo y'Umwami wanyu, ndetse na Yeshua HaMashiach, bana banjye, kuko ndi Umwami w'abami n'Umutware w'abatware, kandi mu gukiranuka nzaza gucira imanza no kurwana. Nuko rero mwumve ibihe murimo n'ibizaba, mutege amatwi ijwi ryanjye, mwumve amagambo y'Umwami wanyu uhoraho, ndetse na Kristo Yesu wazutse kandi agahabwa ikuzo! Ubu ngiye kuvuga ibizaba mu cyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli kandi nzaringaniza inzira zanyu, kandi nzabaha ubuntu bwanjye bukiza binyuze ku bantu banjye 144,000. Kuko baje gukiza ubwoko bwanjye kurimbuka, kandi baje guhisha ubwoko bwanjye umujinya, ndetse n'umujinya w'Imana wagenewe abaguye n'abifatanya nabo iteka ryose n'ubwami bwabo binyuze mu kwakira ikimenyetso cyabo, ndetse n'iryo ryinyo ry'inzoka y'inyamaswa ritagira umuti.

Kandi niko bimeze, nzazamura Umugeni wanjye mu ijuru, kandi nzashyiraho ikimenyetso ku bantu banjye 144,000 bo mu miryango ikurikira, ndetse 12,000 kuri buri muryango ukurikira: Yuda, Rubeni, Gadi, asheri, Nafutali, Manase, Simeyoni, Lewi, Isakari, Zabuloni, Yozefu, na Benyamini. Nuko rero mwumve ijwi ryanjye!

Kandi bazaba imbaraga zidashobora guhagarikwa, ndetse n'imbaraga zidashobora kumeneka. Kuko bafite ubugingo buhoraho, butashobora kubora. Bafite imibiri idashobora kurimbuka, kuko nta kurimbuka gushobora gufata abacunguwe; kuko bafite intsinzi ku rupfu no kurimbuka iteka ryose. Bazaremwa bundi bushya nk'abana b'umucyo, kandi bazambikwa ikuzo rihoraho, kandi bazagira ubushobozi bwihariye n'impano byanditswe muri bo bizagaragaza ibyo bagezeho bikubiyemo ubushobozi nzarema muri bo. Kuko njye, Kristo Yesu, ndi umutunganya wicaye afite umwuka mu ntoki, atwika umuriro w'iyezwa, kugira ngo mbareme mwese bundi bushya muri njye, Kristo Yesu Umwami wanyu wazamutse n'Umukiza; kandi uzanesha wese, azaragwa byose.

Nuko rero mbahaye gusobanukirwa ibizaba vuba, kandi bimwe bizaba bikurikirana mu gihe ibindi bizaba icyarimwe, ariko nzabitondeka kugira ngo mumenye ibihe murimo, kandi kugira ngo mwebwe abera banjye bo mu makuba mutagushwa mu buyobe bukomeye bwatwikiriye isi kandi benshi bazashukwa bacirwe urubanza. Kuko abaguye baziyerekana kandi bazabeshya kenshi, kandi bazatanga ubuzima ariko nta bushobozi bafite bwo gutanga ubuzima, kandi ntibashobora no gutanga ubuzima; ariko buri jambo bavuga rishingiye ku binyoma biyobora ku rupfu rwa kabiri, kandi kurimbuka kw'iteka niko kuzaboneka ku bakurikira inzira z'abaguye n'abemera ibinyoma byabo byanduye nk'ukuri. Ariko uko niko gucirwaho iteka kwaje mu isi, ko abantu bakunze umwijima kuruta umucyo kandi abantu bakunze ibinyoma kuruta ukuri, kuko abantu banyanze, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndetse na Yesu Kristo, bagahitamo umukiza w'ikinyoma; nyamara ukuri kuri muri Kristo wenyine, naho abandi bose ni abanyabinyoma n'abatekamutwe.

Nuko rero ibizaba ni ibi bikurikira, kandi bimwe bizaba icyarimwe ibindi bibe bikurikirana kandi bikenewe kugira ngo ibindi bibeho. Kandi ibintu byinshi ntibyanditswe ariko bizaba mu gihe cyabyo. Nuko rero icyo bamwe bita kuzamurwa mu ijuru, cyangwa gukoranyirizwa hamwe kw'abari muri Kristo bose basanga Umwami wabo, nicyo gikorwa cya mbere kizatera ibindi bikorwa byose mu gihe cy'amakuba ya Yakobo, nanone cyitwa icyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli, kuko kizaba icyumweru cy'imyaka irindwi, aho buri munsi ari umwaka. Nuko rero abizerwa mu Itorero ryanjye, ari ryo Mubiri wanjye n'Umugeni wanjye, bazatwarwa ubwo njye, Kristo Yesu, nzamanuka mvuye mu ijuru n'ijwi rirenga, n'ijwi rya marayika mukuru n'impanda y'Imana. Kuko impanda y'imperuka izavuga, icyo gihe abapfiriye muri Kristo bazazurwa mu bugingo bushya; hanyuma mwebwe abazaba bakiriho bazasigara bazazamurwa mu bicu hamwe nabo, kandi bizaba nk'uko byanditswe, kandi mu buryo bwatanzwe bizaba mu kanya nk'ako guhumbya.

Nuko rero musobanukirwe, mwa bizerwa banjye, ndetse n'abo nkunda urukundo ruhoraho kandi rw'iteka, ko muzisanga gitungura mu cyumba cy'intebe y'ubwami cya Yahweh ubwe, aho nzabamurikira Data w'imicyo mudafite ikizinga cyangwa umunkanyari cyangwa ikindi nk'icyo, kandi muri umucyo w'isi. Kandi ako kanya muzamenya nk'uko mumenywe, kandi muzambona, Yeshua HaMashiach, kandi muzabona Yahweh, Imana Ihoraho Ikomeye, n'amaso yanyu ku nshuro ya mbere, kandi muzatangazwa cyane n'ibyo muzabona n'ikuzo muzaba mwambitswe. Kandi iteka ryose muzaba mwunze ubumwe na Data, n'Umwana, kandi tuzabana iteka ryose.

Mu gihe cyo gukoranya abera, ubuza azakurwaho, kandi urubanza ruzagwira inzu y'Imana wese. Kandi abanyemereye kubatunganya mu muriro w'umutunganya bazahagarara kuko bejejwe mu maraso y'umwana w'intama kandi bazahagarara mu gukiranuka nabahaye, kuko ndi Umwami Ukiranuka uri umwuka utanga ubugingo. Kandi abanze ijwi ryanjye bakinangira ku bushake bwanjye bakanga kuva mu byaha ngo baze mu gukiranuka bazasigirwa gutunganywa kurushaho mu gihe cy'amakuba ya Yakobo, kandi amakoraniro menshi azabona agakiza k'iteka, ingororano, n'ikuzo, kandi bazabaho iteka kandi ikuzo rikomeye rizaba iryabo iteka ryose. Kandi niko n'amatorero y'ibinyoma asigaye azarimburwa ubwo Ikigagaza, inyamaswa iva mu nyanja, n'inyamaswa iva mu isi bizihuta kugira ngo birwanye abera basigaye kandi bizabanesha. Kandi niko bizaba.

Igihe ubuza azakurwaho, Babuloni y'Ibanga izarimburwa nk'uko byanditswe. Ndetse mu isaha imwe iherezo ryayo rizaba irya burundu, kandi amakuba akomeye n'irimbuka bizatsemba isi, kandi hazabaho gutigisa gukomeye, kandi bizagaragara ku isi ko umunsi w'Umwami wageze, nubwo abaguye bazabeshya bakiyerekana nk'abanyamahanga beza, kandi bazagerageza guhisha ukuboneka kwanjye ku bizerwa mu ntambara n'igitero cy'ibinyoma cy'abanyamahanga. Nuko rero ntimwizere ibinyoma byabo, bana banjye, kuko ibinyoma byabo bigamije kubayobora mu kurimbuka kw'iteka kwagenewe abaguye, nuko rero ntimwifatanye nabo.

Nuko rero ibimenyetso bizafungurwa n'abapanda mafarashi bane bazarekurwa ku isi, kandi abantu benshi cyane bazarimbuka kubera ibyo. Kandi mu bwami bwa Babuloni y'Ibanga bwicaye ku mazi menshi, ndetse n'aba bazarimburwa kandi bicwe, kandi iminyago izakorera gahunda y'inyamaswa izamuka kandi ya nyuma; kandi ni iya nyuma kuko njye, Kristo Yesu, nzarimbura burundu kandi nsenye ubwami bwa Satani, ntibuzongera kuzamuka ukundi. Nuko rero isi izatangazwa n'inyamaswa, kandi benshi bazifatanya n'ubwami bwabo bwa satani. Kandi kamere mbi mu bantu, yazanywe n'icyaha cya Adamu igihe Eva yafataga ku nzoka akarya, izaba idakumiriwe na gato, kandi abantu bazagira ubusazi bwuzuye mu mitima yabo, kandi benshi bazifatanya n'inyamaswa iteka ryose bemeye kandi bafashe ikimenyetso cy'inyamaswa. Ntimufate ikimenyetso cyayo, kuko abazagifata bose bazajugunywa ari bazima mu nyanja y'umuriro n'amazuku, yaka ibirimi by'umuriro bishyushye cyane by'ubururu.

Mu ivu ry'intambara niho ubwami bwabo bwa nyuma buzazamuka, ndetse no mu ivu rya gahunda ishaje, kuko mu kavuyo bateza hazazamuka gahunda yabo nshya, ndetse na gahunda y'Ikigagaza. Kandi niko bazashaka guhuza isi yose munsi y'ibendera ryabo, kandi bazahuza amadini yose y'isi kandi bazanyihakana burundu, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndetse na Yesu Kristo, utazemererwa kuba muri bo. Kandi ukuri, ukuri, sinigeze ngira uruhare cyangwa umugabane muri bo. Kandi niko bazakora bihuta cyane kandi n'ubugome bukomeye mu ntambara yabo ku bera, kandi isi izahindukirana burundu abera bo mu makuba, kandi abazanesha bose bambaza izina ryanjye kugeza ku iherezo bazahabwa ikuzo iteka ryose mu ngororano y'iteka n'ubwiza buhoraho, kandi ingororano yabo ni nini, kandi abazanesha bazaragwa byose.

Uko umuryango wabo mushya uzamuka, niko n'ibiti byanjye by'iminzenze bibiri bizazamuka, ndetse n'ibitereko byanjye by'amatara bibiri bihagarara imbere y'Imana y'isi. Bazahabwa ubushobozi bukomeye, kandi bazahanura amezi 42 bambaye ibigunira; kandi umuntu nashaka kubagirira nabi, umuriro uzava mu kanwa kabo, utsembe abanzi babo: kandi umuntu nashaka kubagirira nabi, agomba kwicwa atyo. Aba bazagira ubushobozi bwo gufunga ijuru, kugira ngo imvura itagwa mu minsi y'ubuhanuzi bwabo: kandi bazagira ubushobozi ku mazi bwo kuyahindura amaraso, no gukubita isi ibyago byose, uko bashatse kose. Kandi urwango rw'isi kuri bo ruzaba rukomeye. Kandi abazahagarara barwanya ibiti byanjye by'iminzenze bibiri bazaba inyamaswa n'umuhanuzi w'ikinyoma, kandi umwe azatunga urutoki ku wundi, kandi baziyikuza, kandi bazavuga ibintu byinshi byo gutuka Imana, kandi bazakora ibimenyetso n'ibitangaza byinshi by'ibinyoma mu maso y'abantu.

Izi nyamaswa zizakora ibitangaza bikomeye kugira ngo ziyobye abatuye mu isi, kuko zizemererwa gukora ibimenyetso n'ibitangaza byinshi by'ibinyoma, kandi zizategeka mu mbaraga nk'izo. Inyamaswa iva mu isi izatuma umuriro umanuka mu isi mu maso y'abantu, kandi izatuma abantu bose bayitangarira n'ibimenyetso n'ibitangaza byinshi by'ibinyoma. Kandi izakora igishushanyo cy'inyamaswa kandi izatuma bose baramya icyo gishushanyo izakora. Kandi izemererwa guha umwuka igishushanyo cy'inyamaswa kugira ngo igishushanyo cy'inyamaswa kivuge kandi izatuma abatazaramya igishushanyo cy'inyamaswa bose bicwa. Kandi izatuma bose, abakire n'abakene, abigenga n'abacakara, bahabwa ikimenyetso cyayo ku kuboko kwabo kw'iburyo cyangwa mu ruhanga rwabo.

UMUNTU WESE NARAMYA INYAMASWA N'IGISHUSHANYO CYAYO, KANDI AGAHABWA IKIMENYETSO CYAYO MU RUHANGA RWE, CYANGWA KU KUBOKO KWE, UWO NAWE AZANYWA KU MVINYO Y'UMUJINYA W'IMANA, YASUTSWE IDAFUNGWANZE MU GIKOMBE CY'UBURAKARI BWAYO, KANDI AZABABARIZWA MU MURIRO N'AMAZUKU, IMBERE Y'ABAMARAYIKA BERA, N'IMBERE Y'UMWANA W'INTAMA: KANDI UMWOTSI W'UMUBABAARO WABO UZAMUKA ITEKA RYOSE. KANDI NTIBAZAGIRA IKIRUHUKO KU MANYWA NA NIJORO, ABARAMYA INYAMASWA N'IGISHUSHANYO CYAYO, N'UMUNTU WESE WAKIRA IKIMENYETSO CY'IZINA RYAYO.

Kandi muri iki gihe urusengero rwabo ruzubakwa, ndetse n'igihe igitambo cy'inyamaswa kitazaba kigikenewe kuko ndi Umwana w'Intama w'Imana kandi nitanze ho igitambo ku byaha by'abantu nk'umutambyi mukuru iteka ryose mu buryo bwa Melikisedeki. Impongano yakorewe ibyaha by'abantu rimwe, bose, n'iteka ryose mu maraso yanjye y'impongano, atagira icyaha. Nuko rero bimeze bityo, nta gitambo cy'ibyaha kikiriho.

Nuko rero igihe ibiti byanjye by'iminzenze bibiri bizaba birangije ubuhamya bwabyo ku iherezo ry'amezi 42, inyamaswa izamuka iva mu rwobo ruzimu izabirwanya kandi izica ibitereko byanjye by'amatara bibiri, kuko ariko yahawe kubikora. Kandi imibiri yabo izaryama ipfuye iminsi itatu n'igice mu mihanda, ndetse n'igihe isi yishimira kandi ihanahana impano ku rupfu rwabo. Nuko rero ijwi rikomeye rizava mu ijuru rivuge riti, Nimuzamuke hano! Kandi bazazurwa mu bugingo bushya, kandi bazakirwa mu ijuru mu maso y'abanzi babo.

Kandi bazarangiza urusengero rwabo kandi umutego uzategwa, kuko ni uwuhe mutego utegwa imbere y'inyoni? Kuko inyamaswa iva mu isi ari yo itega umutego mu gihugu, izashuka benshi mu ruzundura rwayo rw'ibinyoma, kandi izagambanira abantu bo mu gihugu. Kandi uwo muntu w'icyaha azahishurwa, ndetse n'umwana wo kurimbuka, Uri umwanzi, kandi akiyishyira hejuru y'icyitwa Imana cyose, cyangwa igisengwa: kugira ngo yicare nk'Imana mu Rusengero rw'Imana, yiyerekana ko ari Imana. Kandi urusengero rwabo ruzarangira, kandi ikizira cy'umurimbuzi kizakorwa na Moshiach wabo w'ikinyoma. Kandi azasezerana isezerano na benshi mu cyumweru kimwe: kandi hagati mu cyumweru azahagarika igitambo n'ituro, kandi kubera gukwira kw'ibizira; azahindura umusaka, ndetse kugeza igihe iherezo ryagenwe rizasukirwa ku musaka.

Nuko rero amakuba akomeye cyane azabaho, ndetse n'amakuba atarigeze abaho ku isi mbere, kandi mu by'ukuri, atazongera kubaho ukundi. Dore! Njye, ndetse na Yeshuah HaMashiach, sinzasiga abantu banjye mu kurimbuka gutunguranye, kandi nzakiza Isirayeli, ndetse na rya shami rikomeye rizemera nk'Mesiya w'ukuri, kuko niko Yahweh yandemye. Nuko rero abantu banjye 144,000 bazatabara abari bagiye kurimbuka kandi bambaza izina ryanjye. Nzabobora intambwe ku yindi, kandi abantu banjye 144,000 bazankorera imirimo myinshi ikomeye.

Kuko aba ari bo bazamurwa mu ikoraniro ry'abera basanga njyewe, Kristo Yesu, mu kirere ku ijwi ry'impanda y'imperuka, kandi impanda igiye kuvuga. Aba ni abizerwa banjye, ndetse na ba bandi batabonetsemo uburiganya, kuko biyejeje nk'isugi iboneye mu maraso y'Umwana w'Intama, ayo natanze iteka ryose nk'umutambyi wabo mukuru, kuko ndi umutambyi mukuru iteka ryose mu buryo bwa Melikisedeki. Aba ni Umugeni wanjye, kandi bazankurikira aho nzajya hose. Dore! Nzabahindura abanyambaraga, kandi nta na kimwe cyigeze kibonwa ku isi gishobora kugereranywa n'ubushobozi nzabambika. Bazambikwa imyenda y'igitare myiza, yera, itagira inenge, kandi ubushobozi bwabo buzagaragaza ibyo bagezeho, kandi bazagira ubushobozi bukomeye; kuko ari abizerwa, bahamagawe, kandi batoranyijwe, ndetse na mbere y'ishingwa ry'isi na Yehova ku bw'iyi migambi.

Abantu banjye 144.000 bazamanuka ku Musozi wa Siyoni, kandi nzabayobora. Bazajyana abo nzabayoboraho mu butayu kugira ngo bahishwe umwijima w'icuraburindi n'itotezwa by'ubwami bw'abanzi. Bazayoborwa ahatekanye barindwe ibyago birindwi, kuko muri izi manza ari ho ubukana bw'umujinya w'Imana buzasukwa ku ntebe y'inyamaswa no kubabaza ubwami bw'Ikiyoka, kuko igihano cyabo ari gikomeye. Kandi ba 144.000 bazabayobora ahatekanye; bazabakiza ibikomere byabo; bazabatabara kandi babahe ibyiringiro bikomeye byo kwambaza izina ry'Uwiteka, ndetse kugeza ku iherezo niba bikenewe. Kuko iyo ntagabanya iyo minsi, nta muntu n'umwe wari kurokoka. Kandi gutyo bazasoza ibyo nzabayoboramo.

Kandi nk'uko ba batatu banduye bagenda kugira ngo barimbure abanyemera nka Mesiya wabo, ni ko n'umujinya wuzuye w'Imana uzasohorezwa ku mitwe yabo no ku bwami bwabo bwose; ni ukuri, ni ukuri, inzabya zirindwi zujuye umujinya w'Imana; kandi urubanza rukomeye n'umubabaro biri mu nzabya zabo, kandi bizaba nk'uko byanditswe:

Marayika wa mbere azasuka urwabya rwe ku isi, kandi abakira ikimenyetso cyabo cyanduye bazafatwa n'ibisebe bibi kandi bibabaza cyane bitewe n'urwabya marayika wa mbere azasuka.

Kandi marayika wa kabiri azasuka urwabya rwe, nuko inyanja zizahinduka nk'amaraso y'umuntu upfuye, kandi ibyaremwe byose bizima byo mu nyanja bizapfa.

Kandi marayika wa gatatu azasuka urwabya rwe, bitume imigezi n'amasoko bihinduka amaraso, kuko bavushije amaraso y'abera n'abahanuzi, bityo bikaba bikwiriye ko bahabwa amaraso yo kunywa.

Ni ukuri ni ukuri, marayika wa kane azasuka urwabya rwe ku zuba, kandi azahabwa kubabaza abantu n'ubushyuhe bw'umuriro, kandi abantu bazatwikwa n'ubushyuhe bavuge amagambo akomeye yo gutuka Imana yo mu ijuru.

Kandi marayika wa gatanu azasuka urwabya rwe ku ntebe y'inyamaswa, nuko ubwami bwayo buzarushaho kuba umwijima, ufite ubukana bukomeye, kandi bazihekenya indimi zabo kubera umubabaro.

Kandi marayika wa gatandatu azasuka urwabya rwe ku ruzi runini Ufurate, nuko amazi yaho azakama, kugira ngo inzira y'Abami bo Iburasirazuba itegurwe.

Kandi marayika wa karindwi azasuka urwabya rwe mu kirere: nuko hazumvikana ijwi rirenga rivuye mu Rusengero rwo mu ijuru ku ntebe y'ubwami, riti: Birarangiye. Kandi hazabaho amajwi, no guhinda kw'inkuba, n'imirabyo, kandi hazabaho umutingito ukomeye w'isi, utarigeze kubaho uhereye igihe abantu babereye ku isi, umutingito ukomeye cyane utyo. Kandi umujyi mukuru uzagabanywamo ibice bitatu, n'imijyi y'amahanga izagwa. Kandi uko ni ko ibyo bintu bizaba.

Bityo rero mwumve, bana banjye, ko Yehova azahisha abe ndetse no mu gihe cy'amakuba ya Yakobo, ndetse no mu gihe cy'umubabaro mwinshi hazabaho abasigaye bazarokoka kandi bazaguma amahoro mu butayu, ndetse n'ahantu hateguwe n'Imana ubwayo, kugira ngo bahabugaburirire iminsi igihumbi na magana abiri na mirongo itandatu. Kandi nta muntu n'umwe wari kurokoka iyo iyo minsi itagabanywa. Kandi abaguye bazikoranira kugira ngo barwane n'Umwami w'abami n'Umutware w'abatware, kandi uko ni ko byanditswe kandi uko ni ko bizakorwa.

Kandi mbona ijuru rikinguye, nuko mbona ifarashi y'umweru, n'uyicayeho yitwa Uwizerwa n'Umunyakuri, kandi aca imanza zitabera akarwana intambara mu gukiranuka. Amaso ye yari ameze nk'ibirimi by'umuriro, kandi ku mutwe we hari amakamba menshi, kandi yari afite izina ryanditswe, nta muntu numwe urizi keretse we ubwe. Kandi yari yambaye umwenda winitswe mu maraso, kandi izina rye yitwa, Jambo ry'Imana. Kandi ingabo zo mu ijuru zamukurikiraga ziri ku mafarashi y'umweru, zambaye imyenda y'igitare myiza, yera kandi iboneye. Kandi mu kanwa ke havamo inkota ityaye, kugira ngo ayikubite amahanga: kandi azayategeza inkoni y'icyuma: kandi asarura mu rwengero rw'ubukana n'umujinya by'Imana Ishoborabyose. Kandi ku mwenda we, no ku kibero cye afite izina ryanditswe ngo, UMWAMI W'ABAMI, N'UMUTWARE W'ABATWARE.

Kandi gutyo marayika wanjye azamanuka ava mu ijuru, afite urufunguzo rw'ikuzimu n'umunyururu munini aho azabohera Satani ikuzimu imyaka igihumbi kandi ashyireho ikimenyetso kugira ngo atazongera kuyobya amahanga ukundi kugeza imyaka igihumbi ishize. Ku iherezo ry'ingoma yanjye y'imyaka igihumbi, Satani azabohurwa ave ikuzimu mu gihe gito.

Dore! Nk'uko Yohana Uhishurirwa yabyanditse, ni ko bizaba: Kandi mbona intebe z'ubwami, n'abazicayeho, bahabwa guca imanza: kandi mbona ubugingo bw'abaciwe imitwe kubera guhamya Yesu, no kubera ijambo ry'Imana, n'abatararamije inyamaswa, cyangwa igishushanyo cyayo, kandi batigeze bashyirwaho ikimenyetso cyayo mu ruhanga rwabo, cyangwa ku maboko yabo; nuko barabaho bima ingoma hamwe na Kristo imyaka igihumbi. Ariko abandi bapfuye ntibongeye kubaho kugeza aho imyaka igihumbi ishiriye. Uku ni ko kuzuka kwa mbere. Hahirwa kandi ni uwera ufite umugabane mu kuzuka kwa mbere: kuri bene abo urupfu rwa kabiri ntirubafiteho ububasha, ahubwo bazaba Abatambyi b'Imana, n'aba Kristo, kandi bazima ingoma hamwe na we imyaka igihumbi.

Kandi ku iherezo ry'ingoma yanjye y'imyaka igihumbi Satani azabohurwa ave mu nzu y'imbohe ye mu gihe gito kugira ngo abakoranye nk'umusenyi wo ku nyanja. Kandi azasohoka ajye kuyobya amahanga ari mu mfuruka enye z'isi, Gogi na Magogi, kugira ngo abakoranyirize hamwe ku rugamba: umubare wabo ungana n'umusenyi wo ku nyanja. Kandi bazazamuka bakwira isi yose, bagota inkambi y'abera, n'umujyi ukundwa: nuko umuriro uzamanuka uva ku Mana mu ijuru, urabatwika. Kandi umwanzi wabayobeje azajugunywa mu nyanja y'umuriro n'amazuku, aho inyamaswa n'umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma bazaba bari, kandi bazababazwa ku manywa na nijoro iteka ryose.

Kandi gutyo hazabaho kuzuka kw'abakiranirwa, kuko ari ko byanditswe na Yohana Uhishurirwa: Kandi mbona intebe y'ubwami yera nini, n'uyicayeho, uwo isi n'ijuru byahungaga mu maso he; kandi ntihaboneka aho biba. Kandi mbona abapfuye, aboroheje n'abakomeye, bahagaze imbere y'Imana; nuko ibitabo birabumburwa: n'ikindi gitabo kirabumburwa, ari cyo Gitabo cy'Ubugingo: kandi abapfuye bacirwa imanza z'ibyanditswe muri ibyo bitabo, hakurikijwe imirimo yabo. Kandi inyanja igarura abapfuye bo muri yo; n'urupfu n'ikuzimu bigarura abapfuye bo muri byo: kandi bacirwa imanza umuntu wese akurikije imirimo ye. Kandi urupfu n'ikuzimu bijugunywa mu nyanja y'umuriro. Uru ni rwo rupfu rwa kabiri. Kandi umuntu wese utarabonetse yanditswe mu Gitabo cy'Ubugingo yajugunywe mu nyanja y'umuriro.

Kandi uko ni ko byanditswe kandi uko ni ko bizakorwa, ko hazabaho gukoranyirizwa hamwe aho abapfiriye muri Kristo bazazurwa n'abazima basigaye bazazamurwa hamwe na bo mu bicu, kandi ibi bizaba mbere gato y'itangira ry'amakuba ya Yakobo. Hanyuma ku iherezo ry'amakuba ya Yakobo hazabaho kuzuka kw'abakiranutsi, kugizwe n'abera bo mu gihe cy'umubabaro mwinshi n'abo mu Isezerano rya Kera bapfuye bafite ukwizera, barabayeho bakurikije amasezerano. Kandi nk'uko byandikiwe Daniyeli, ni ko bizaba: Ariko genda mu nzira yawe kugeza ku iherezo: kuko uzaruhuka, kandi uzahagarara mu mugabane wawe ku iherezo ry'iminsi. Kandi gutyo iherezo ry'iminsi ni isozwa ry'icyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli no mu itangira ry'ingoma yanjye y'imyaka igihumbi.

Kandi ku iherezo ry'ingoma yanjye y'imyaka igihumbi na nyuma y'uko Satani ajugunywa mu murage we wa nyuma muri iyo nyanja y'umuriro yaka iteka, ni ko kuzuka kw'abakiranirwa kuzabaho, ndetse n'urwo rubanza rw'intebe y'ubwami yera nini ruzabaho, kandi umuntu wese izina rye ritazaboneka ryanditswe mu Gitabo cy'Ubugingo azajugunywa mu nyanja y'umuriro.

Kandi gutyo njyewe, ndetse Yesu w'i Nazareti, ndetse Umwana w'Intama w'Imana, ndetse Kristo Yesu, ari we n'Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda, nzaza guca imanza no kurwanya Ikiyoka, kandi ubwami bwacyo bwose buzashenjagurwa rwose, ntibuzongera kubyuka ukundi. Kandi inyamaswa n'umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma bazajugunywa ari bazima mu nyanja y'umuriro, kandi Satani azabohwa n'umunyururu munini ikuzimu imyaka igihumbi, kandi gutyo ingoma yanjye y'imyaka igihumbi izatangira, kandi mu gukiranuka ni ho ntegekera. Dore! Nzanye Umugeni wanjye n'ingabo z'abami n'abatambyi nzategekana na bo isi mu gukiranuka, kandi nzategeza amahanga inkoni y'icyuma. Bityo mugire ibyiringiro bikomeye, bana banjye, kuko isozwa ry'ibintu byose riri hafi, kandi iki gihe cy'imyaka irindwi cy'amakuba akomeye, ndetse amakuba ya Yakobo, kizakurikirwa n'ingoma yanjye ikiranuka, kandi Yehova azaba byose muri byose.

Kandi gutyo mbahaye ibyiringiro bikomeye, bana banjye bato, ndetse kugira ngo mushyire imitima yanyu ku byishimo byabashyiriwe imbere kuko imibabaro y'amakuba ya Yakobo itagereranywa kandi ntikwiriye no kuvugwa ugereranyije n'ibyishimo by'iteka byabashyiriwe imbere. Dore! Byanditswe na Yohana Uhishurirwa ngo: Aba ni bo bavuye mu mubabaro mwinshi, kandi bameshe amakanzu yabo, bayejesha amaraso y'Umwana w'Intama. Ni cyo gituma bari imbere y'intebe y'ubwami y'Imana, bakayikorera ku manywa na nijoro mu Rusengero rwayo: kandi uwicaye ku Ntebe y'Ubwami azabana na bo. Ntibazongera gusonza, cyangwa kugira inyota ukundi, kandi izuba ntirizabatwika, cyangwa ubushyuhe bwose. Kuko Umwana w'Intama, uri hagati y'intebe y'ubwami, azabagaburira, kandi azabayobora ku masoko y'amazi y'ubugingo: kandi Imana izahanagura amarira yose ku maso yabo. Bazaba Abatambyi b'Imana, n'aba Kristo, kandi bazima ingoma hamwe na we imyaka igihumbi.

Kandi uko ni ko ibyiringiro byanyu biri, bakundwa banjye, kugira ngo mwihangane kugeza ku iherezo, kandi ibyanyu ni ibyiringiro by'iteka, kuko Eli Shaddai, Imana Ishoborabyose, izarema ijuru rishya n'isi nshya ibamo gukiranuka, kandi muzahabwa kwinjira muri uwo mujyi wera wateguwe kandi utatswe na Yehova ubwe nk'umugeni uteguriwe umukwe, kandi muzarya ku giti cy'ubugingo. Kandi nk'uko byanditswe ngo: Unesha, azaragwa byose, kandi nzaba Imana ye, na we azaba umwana wanjye.

Dore! Ndi Yeshua HaMashiach, kandi nohereje amagambo yanjye binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye, kandi binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye Amagambo yanjye yoherejwe.

Dore! Ubutumwa bwiza bw'iteka ryose bwahishuwe kandi bwoherezwa mu gihe cyabwo gikwiriye, kandi aya magambo azabungabungwa, kandi azagera ku maso, amatwi, n'imitima by'abo yohererejwe bose.

Dore! Mukomeze kurinda kwanyu kandi ntimuve mu byimbo byanyu, bantu banjye, kuko ibihe byageze.

Dore! Ibihe ni ubu!
