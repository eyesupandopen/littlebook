Uburyo bwo Kwakira Ubuhanuzi: Uko Nakira Ubutumwa bw'Ubuhanuzi

Ku wa 2 Werurwe 2024, Umwami Yesu Kristo yatangiye kumpa ubutumwa bw'ubuhanuzi bwo gusangiza abantu ku byerekeye kugaragara kwe kwegereje ku rwa Torero rye ndetse n'izindi ngingo. Ubu buhanuzi bwaje kuri njye mu buryo Bibiliya isobanura nk'igihe "ukuboko k'Uwiteka" kuba ku muntu. Kuri njye, ni ibyiyumvo byimbitse by'uburemere mu mwuka wanjye, bitajya bishira kugeza ndangije ubutumwa. Kristo ashyira amagambo make cyangwa interuro mu ntekerezo zanjye ntashobora kwikuramo kandi ntazibagirwa, kandi kenshi niyoherereza iyo nteruro muri mesaje kugira ngo nzayibuke. Iyo nicaye ngatangira kwandika, Umwami anyereka Amagambo mu mwuka wanjye uko nyandika: Yego, hari igihe mbona amagambo imbonankubone, kandi kenshi numva amagambo uko nyandika, atari mu matwi ahubwo mu mwuka wanjye, kandi byumvikana mu buryo budashidikanywaho.

Iyo nkoze ikosa nkandika ibitekerezo byanjye, amagambo arahagarara kugeza mpanaguye ibyo nongeyemo. Iyo nishidikanyijeho ngasiba interuro Kristo yashakaga kugumishamo, amagambo arahagarara kugeza nsubije interuro uko yari imeze, kandi iyo nteruro ntiva mu ntekerezo zanjye kugeza mbikoze. Muri iyi minsi, Umwami yampaye ubutumwa bwimbitse cyane bw'inyigisho, kandi yanjyanye mu byanditswe byihariye. Ashyira interuro mu mwuka wanjye nkayandika; hanyuma ngashakisha iyo nteruro muri Bibiliya yanjye, nkasanga iyo nteruro iri mu gice cy'ibyanditswe. Icyo gihe anyereka ibyanditswe ngomba gusubiramo, nkabishyira mu nkuru, hanyuma amagambo agakomeza ako kanya. Ndiratangazwa n'uko Amagambo Umwami ampa ahuza neza n'ibyanditswe ampa ngo mbisubiremo, kandi ibyanditswe nsubiramo bijyana n'inkuru inshuro nyinshi mu buryo ntabasha kwikorera ubwanjye. Ntabwo nigeze nkora ikintu nk'iki mu buzima bwanjye, kugeza ubu. Ubusanzwe simenya ubutumwa bwuzuye kugeza nsubiyemo ngakora isuzuma ry'imyandikire n'utwatuzo. Icyo gihe nzongera mbusome, kandi Umwami azansaba gukora impinduka nto, wenda kongeramo interuro isobanura, wenda gusiba ijambo nkarisimbuza irindi, n'ibindi. Hanyuma uburemere buragenda nkamenya ko ndangije.

Nyamuneka zirikana ko muri ubu butumwa bwose bw'ubuhanuzi, ntabwo nigeze mpindura ikintu na kimwe ku ngingo, habe na rimwe; kandi ntabwo higeze habaho impinduka nini ku bikubiyemo, habe na rimwe. Ubuhanuzi burebure ubusanzwe butwara nk'isaha imwe cyangwa munsi yayo mu kubwandika, kandi igikorwa cyo kubunonosora gishobora kongeraho indi saha imwe cyangwa abiri. Ngenzura byose nifashishije ibyanditswe kugira ngo menye neza ko nta kintu nangije mu kumva kwanjye kwa kimuntu. Kugeza ubu ntabyo, ariko kugira ngo mbisobanure neza: Menshi muri aya Magambo arenze imyumvire yanjye iyo nyandika, kandi nkenera kujya mu byanditswe kugira ngo nige byinshi nshingiye ku byo nakiriye. Nta na rimwe ubutumwa bwigeze buvuguruzanya n'ibyanditswe, nubwo bugerageza imyumvire yanjye y'ibyanditswe, kandi nanjye ndi umunyeshuri. Ubu, ndi umukozi w'Imana kandi mfite imyaka igera kuri 30 mu byanditswe kandi nakoze inyigisho nyinshi, amabwiriza, n'ibindi. Reka mvuge ibi nshingiye ku myaka yanjye y'ubunararibonye: Ntibishoboka ko nandika ikintu cy'uru rwego n'ubusobanuro bwimbitse muri icyo gihe gito ku mbaraga zanjye n'imyumvire yanjye. Bintwara iminsi myinshi, rimwe na rimwe icyumweru cyangwa irenga, kugira ngo ntegure inyigisho ndende, isobanuye neza, kandi ibi si kimwe, habe na gato. Ibi si ibyanjye kandi birenze imyumvire yanjye. Umwuka w'ubuhanuzi wamanukiye, kuko Ubuhamya bwa Yesu ari wo mwuka w'ubuhanuzi.
