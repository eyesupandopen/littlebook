Ubuhamya Bubiri: Isirayeli
Ibisharira N'ibiryohereye
31 Nyakanga 2024

Mwebwe abita umwijima urumuri, n'urumuri mukarwita umwijima, mugushije ishyano!

Nimwumve amagambo y'Umwami n'Umukiza wanyu, mwa Torero ryanjye, ndetse nawe Mugeni wanjye, kuko ari jye uha abanyabwenge ubwenge, kugira ngo abanyabwenge muri mwe barusheho kugira ubwenge. Kandi ndi umucuzi watsa umuriro weza, nuko rero nimwumve amagambo yanjye mwese, kandi mupfukamire ubutumire bwanjye bw'ubugingo buhoraho kandi mushake ubwenge kuko ubwenge ari bwo bw'ingenzi, nuko rero mushake ubwenge. Nuko nimumbiyegurire n'umutima ukunze n'amaboko ashabutse kandi mupfukame kandi mukomeze kungira, Yesu w'i Nazareti, Umwami wanyu uhoraho kuko nkomeje guhamagara abaraganwa b'ubugingo buhoraho bose, nubwo bamwe banangiye amatwi.

Ariko mwange ubwenge bw'iyi si butagira icyo bumara, kuko ubwenge bw'isi buturuka ku batware b'isi bose vuba aha bazabohwa imyaka igihumbi ikuzimu mu bubabare n'igihano cy'inzira zabo. Kuko n'ubwo bazaba bari mu minyururu iremereye bazabohwamo vuba, bazasobanukirwa ibizababaho, kandi bazatitira banahinde umushyitsi imyaka igihumbi nk'iteka ryabo ryagenwe muri rya ziba ryaka umuriro n'amazuku byaka mu birimi by'umuriro bishyushye cyane by'ubururu bwa kobaliti kuko ari ko Yahweh yateguye kandi ni ko umurage wabo ubategereje.

Kuko uko ari ko kwifuza kw'imitima yabo ku bana b'Imana ihoraho, niko Yahweh azagarura kwifuza kw'imitima yabo mu bituza byabo, bityo bazahora bashya iteka ryose mu bihe by'ibihe. Kuko by'umwihariko abaguye bazahabwa ibihwanye n'inzira zabo kuko ari bo ba se b'icyaha, ishyano, gukiranirwa, no kwigomeka kandi ibikorwa byabo by'ikizira bazabigarurirwa iteka ryose. Kandi bazamenya kandi bishyure byuzuye buri cyaha cyose bakoze inshuro nyinshi cyane kandi igihano cyabo kizahoraho, kuko ari ko byabonetse mu mitima yabo kandi ibikorwa byabo ni ibyo kwangwa iteka ryose. Kuko uko ari ko nzira za Yahweh, uburyo umuntu acira undi urubanza, niko nawe azacirwa urubanza kandi uko niko iherezo n'igihano cy'abahitamo kurimbuka kwabo aho guhitamo ubugingo buhoraho muri jye Yesu w'i Nazareti kuko ndi Umwana w'Intama w'Imana.

Kuko umuntu wese w'umupfapfa bihagije kugira ngo yigomeke ku gahunda y'irema, ndetse n'amategeko kamere ya Yahweh waremye ubuzima nk'urusobe rwuzuzanya, azasangira nabo igihano cyabo. Kwigomeka ku mategeko y'ubuzima ni ukuzanira abandi urupfu n'irimbukiro, kandi ibibi bikomeye n'ingaruka zikomeye bikorwa n'abigomeka bose muri ubwo buryo kandi bagakurikira irari ryabo bwite byangiza abandi. Kwishimira ukwigomeka kw'abaguye ni ukwifatanya n'inzira zabo kandi ababikora bose bazasangira iteka umurage wabo mu birimi by'umuriro bihoraho, kandi nta yandi mahitamo ahari.

Kuko kuva kera na kare abantu bahawe ubushake bwisanzuye kandi Adamu yashutswe na Eva wakiriye ku nzoka yamushutse mu buriganya bwayo. Nuko Adamu na Eva bahitamo urupfu nk'ingaruka z'icyaha, kuko abantu baremwe kimwe n'abamarayika, kugira ngo bakomeze kuba abera kandi bakiranuka, kandi urupfu, rurimbura byose, ntirwari kubabasha.

Kandi bityo kumenya icyiza n'ikibi byangije abariye bose kuva icyo gihe. Kandi intambara y'imbuto yararushijeho gukara kuva mu Itangiriro n'imbuto yasezeranijwe y'umugore, mu kurimbuka kwinshi, kandi muri iyi ntambara nta cyasizwe n'ingaruka zayo. Kugeza n'uyu munsi irema ryose rirashinyiriza kandi rinihira mu bubabare na n'ubu kuko irema naryo rizabaturwa mu bubata bwo kubora rikagezwa mu mudendezo w'ubwiza bw'abana b'Imana, kandi gucungurwa ntigutinza! Ariko mumenye bana banjye ko kugeza n'uyu munsi intambara y'imbuto yatsinzwe byuzuye muri jye, Kristo Yesu, Kuko mwabyawe ubwa kabiri n'imbuto itabora kandi mu Mwana w'Intama w'Imana niho habonetse impongano y'ibyaha by'abantu rimwe rizima. Kandi ku bw'imbaraga zitagereranywa za Yahweh, urupfu rwatsinzwe iteka ryose mu kuzuka kwanjye mu bapfuye, kuko bidashoboka ko urupfu rumbasha kuko ntagira icyaha kandi ibihembo by'icyaha ni urupfu.

Nyamara nishyuye ikiguzi kitatekerezwa ku bwo gucungurwa no kubaturwa kwanyu, kandi abana b'Imana ihoraho isaha n'isaha bazisanga bahagaze mu cyumba cy'intebe y'ubwami y'Imana bambaye ubugingo buhoraho iteka ryose. Uko niko biri kandi niko bizaba, ko umuntu wese wambaza izina ry'Umwami azakizwa iteka ryose, nibakomeza kuba abizerwa mu kwambaza izina ryanjye kugeza ku iherezo. Kuko abaguye ari abanzi batsinzwe, kandi noneho haje ibihe byo kurimbuka gukomeye ku bwami bwabo n'igihano cyuzuye uko umujinya w'Umwana w'Intama n'umujinya w'Imana bisukwa mu minsi iri imbere. Kuko umunsi w'Umwami ugeze, nuko rero nimwumve ijwi ryanjye mbere y'uko urugi rw'Inkuge rufungwa.

Nimwumve amagambo yanjye kandi musobanukirwe imvugo yanjye. Kuko mvuga ibintu biri kuba ubu ndetse n'ikinyoma gikomeye cyayobya n'intore niba zihisemo kuziba amatwi yazo ku ijwi ryanjye. Ubu ngiye kuvuga ku ngurube z'ishyamba zishaka kugaragara nk'aho zitari mu biraro zashyizwemo n'umukumirizi wanjye. Ndetse n'umutambagiro n'amatsinda y'ingurube ateza imbere irimbuka uko bukeye n'uko bwije byegereza isi intambara yeruye, n'amakuba yuzuye, kuko ari ko zahawe gukora kandi Imana yabishyize mu mitima yazo kugira ngo zisohoze ubushake bwayo kuko n'abanzi ba Yahweh bumvira ijwi rye.

Ingurube zigaragura mu mwanda wazo, zishakisha mu byondo, kandi ntizigera zihaga, ahubwo zizamira icyo zibonye cyose uko zishakisha n'amazuru yazo mu butaka kuko ari iz'ubutaka, kandi zihumye ku bindi byose uretse irari ry'inda yazo n'irari rya se zizakora. Kuko uko ari ko abayobozi b'igihugu b'ubu bameze, kandi bataka mu bubabare uko bakubita inzirakarengane zabo. Kuko nta gishobora guhaza byuzuye izi ngurube, kandi ziroze byuzuye imbere bitewe n'ibyo zirya, kuko zose zihumanye kandi ari urumamfu nyarwo kandi niko kamere yazo iri.

Aya manyamaswa mabi ni ibiti byaboze kandi bitagira imbuto, byapfuye kabiri, kandi byaranduranywe n'imizi. Ntibizigera bigira ubugingo buhoraho, kandi inzira zabyo ni inzira z'urupfu n'irimbukiro bihoraho, ndetse n'irimbukiro rihoraho mu murage wa ba Se muri rya ziba rinini ry'umuriro w'ubururu. Kamere yabyo ni ukwigarurira, kandi byiyegereza abantu mbere bishaka ubwugamo uko byitaka nk'inzirakarengane, ndetse bishinja abandi urwango bikorera amahanga. Bitaka mu bubabare uko bikubita abo bishaka kurimbura kugira ngo bigaragare nk'ibifite ishingiro mu nzira zabyo mbi.

Kuko aya manyamaswa y'inkazi adashobora kugirirwa impuhwe kuko inzira zayo ari izo kurimbuka kandi aya azakora irari rya Se umusembwa kuko irari rye ari ryo byishimo byayo kandi niko yuzuzwa. Mwebwe abishimira ikibi kandi abita umwijima urumuri n'urumuri umwijima, mugushije ishyano. Kuko inzira zabo ari inzira z'irimbukiro, kandi mu muriro uhoraho ntibazigera bawucika. Musobanukirwe bana banjye ko isaha n'isaha ubu ingurube zizabohurwa mu biraro zashyizwemo n'umukumirizi. Kuko uko umukumirizi akurwaho, izi ngurube zisa n'izororerwa mu rugo kandi zitagira icyo zitwara ziyita intore z'Imana zizahinduka iz'ishyamba ozikimara kurekurwa mu ntoki zikomeye z'umukumirizi, kuko niko kamere yazo nyakuri iri.

Zizatera ibihugu byinshi kandi zizamira ibyo zibonye byose. Zizatungwa n'urupfu n'irimbuka ry'abandi, kuko niko kamere yazo iri. Zazerereye ahantu henshi, kandi zifite amategeko yazo n'imiterere y'imibanire, kuko Yahweh aha n'ingurube urwego rw'ubwenge n'imikorere, kuko inzira za Yahweh zitunganye kandi zikiranuka. Izi ngurube ntizigira kwicuza, ntizigira impuhwe, kandi ntizigira ikibazo cyo kurya abapfuye ndetse no guhaza irari ryazo, kandi zizanarya abazima niba bihuye n'intego yazo. Dore isaha irageze aho ingurube zitazaba zigikumiriwe kandi nizirekurwa, zizarya igihugu, zizamira ibyiza by'igihugu, zizarimbura amahoro byuzuye mu isi kandi zizagaburirwa zinigaragure mu kumena amaraso kw'intambara yeruye. Kuko n'abashyushyarugamba banyu babi baririmba inzira zazo, kandi inzira zazo si ibanga.

Izi ngurube z'ishyamba zaremewe kurimbura no kumira umwanda w'igihugu kugira ngo zeze isi ku bw'intego za Yahweh, hanyuma zizabohwa zikugwe mu muriro uhoraho iteka ryose. Kuko ibyaha byazo ari bikomeye, kandi ibyaha byazo ni byinshi. Ariko musobanukirwe ko izi ngurube zagumishijwe mu kiraro cyazo mu bihe byagenwe kandi nyinshi ziyita ibikoresho byera by'ubwami bwazo buzamuka. Kandi ubu ni cyo gihe cyo kurekurwa kwazo, kandi ubu zizerekana kamere yazo nyakuri zihishe mu myambaro isa n'iy'abakiranutsi, kandi kibe y'umukara ya Saturne iri hagati mu nyenyeri yazo ndetse no hejuru y'amaso. Zishaka kwitandukanya kuko zihisemo kuba iz'Imana bwite. Ariko nta muntu uza kuri Yahweh keretse anyuze kuri jye, Yesu w'i Nazareti, uwo ba Se banze kandi abangize Umwami ni bo Isirayeli nyakuri y'Imana kandi bazaremwa bundi bushya byuzuye muri jye nk'umuntu umwe mushya, yaba Umuyahudi cyangwa Umunyamahanga, yaba imbata cyangwa uwigenga. Uru rumamfu rw'Abedomu ruzashyirwa ahagaragara byuzuye nk'abatekamutwe bari bo kandi bazarandurwa byuzuye mu gihugu no mu isi yose mu rubanza rukomeye n'igihano gikomeye.

Ariko mbere y'uko zikurwaho, zizemererwa inyota yazo y'amaraso uko kamere yazo nyakuri ihishurirwa bose ngo bayibone, kuko ubu zihisha mu bintu bisa n'iby'idini. Zitunga imyanda, zitunga abapfuye, zitera kandi zikamira abazima kuko inyota yazo y'amaraso ari nini kandi idashobora guhazwa! Kandi icyo gihe cyose ziyita izera, ziyita iz'igihugu kandi ari abaraganwa bemewe, kandi ziyita ko atari abatera kuko indiri yazo kamere ari ubutayu atari igihugu cy'isezerano. Kandi uko niko kamere y'ingurube iri kuko ozikimara gusubizwa mu ishyamba kamere yazo nyakuri y'ishyamba ishyirwa ahagaragara byuzuye. Nyamara, ziyita ko zibaho mu myirondoro y'abo zishaka kwigarurira kandi zizarandurwa byuzuye mu gihugu kandi ntizigira umuzi cyangwa ishami kuko ari ko Yahweh yasezeranye. Nuko rero musobanukirwe ko izi ngurube zigaragara nk'abayobozi beza b'igihugu cy'isezerano mu gihe mu by'ukuri ari inyamaswa z'inkazi zibeshwaho na jenoside kandi zizashaka byuzuye kurimbura ikintu cyose cyanjye, Kristo Yesu, kandi zizanagambanira ab'Igihugu bakurikira amategeko yabo ya Talimudi mu rimbukiro nyamara asigaye y'igihugu azaza kuri jye, Yeshua HaMashiach, kuko nzanakiza Yakobo!

Mwebwe abita umwijima urumuri n'urumuri umwijima, mugushije ishyano! Ndetse n'abatera Yahweh, na Yeshua HaMashiach batera abana b'Imana Ihoraho. Kuko ubwo mwabikoreye umwe muri aba bavandimwe banjye boroheje mwabinkoreye, kandi guhora ni ukwanjye, niko Umwami uhoraho avuga, kandi nzishyura. Nuko rero nimuhumuke, bana banjye, kandi mwange abazamura inyenyeri ya Remfani ku ibendera ryabo! Kuko kwifatanya nabo ari ukuba mu ihema rya Moloki no gupfukamira Bafometi yabo! Sinababwiye kubara umubare w'inyamaswa kandi uwo mubare ugaragazwa n'ishema muri icyo gishushanyo biremeye kugira ngo basenge imana yabo y'inyenyeri, kuko hagati mu nyenyeri yabo hari kibe yabo! Musobanukirwe uyu mugani, kuko inguni esheshatu ziyobora kuri mpandeshatu esheshatu zikora hegizagone kandi imbere hagakwiramo kibe yabo, nuko rero mubare umubare w'inyamaswa kandi mureke kwizera ibinyoma by'Abedomu kuko basenga abaguye kandi imana yabo ni ikiyoka.

Kandi kuva mu minsi ya Nowa, ndetse na nyuma y'umwuzure, ibyenda bya cumi by'imyuka itagira imibiri y'Abanefili byakumiriwe ikuzimu, kuko Nowa yasengeye abuzukuru be batewe nabo kandi bakabanesha. Nuko Yahweh yubaha icyifuzo cya Nowa aboha ababi cyane n'abateye ishozi cyane, kandi ibyenda bya cumi by'umubare wabo byagumishijwe ikuzimu kuva icyo gihe. Nimunyumve kandi munyumve neza, Bana banjye, kandi musobanukirwe ijwi ry'Umwami wanyu. Mu ikoraniro ry'abizerwa mu Torero ryanjye ndetse n'Umugeni wanjye kuri jye mu bicu umukumirizi azakurwaho, kandi ikuzimu hateguriwe gufungurwa byuzuye.

Ikibi ntikizongera gukumirwa, kuko Yahweh yakumiriye byimazeyo ubuzuye bw'ubwami bwa Satani kugira ngo abaraganwa b'agakiza gahoraho bakizwe, ndetse n'abangize Umwami wabo mu bihe byanjye by'ubuntu n'imbabazi. Kubera ko abantu ubu banyanze, ikibi ntikizongera gukumirwa ku bantu. Musobanukirwe ko bose, ndetse n'imbaga nini y'iyi myuka bose bazarekurwa, ndetse n'umubare munini cyane uzarengera isi n'ikibi kidakumiriwe kandi kitavugwa. Kuko Enoki umwanditsi yanditse kuri ibi ati: Kandi imyuka y'ibihangange irababaza, irakandamiza, irarimbura, iratera, irarwana, kandi igakora irimbuka mu isi, kandi igateza ibibazo: ntibafata ibyo kurya, ariko nyamara barasonza kandi bakagira inyota, kandi bagateza ibyaha. Kandi iyi myuka izahagurukira abana b'abantu n'abagore, kuko bakomotse muri bo.

Ntimwasomye mu ijambo ryanjye ryera aho nanyuze mu gihugu cy'Abagadareni? Kuko nirukanye imyuka muri babiri bari bayitewe, nuko nemerera iyi myuka mibi kwinjira mu mukumbi w'ingurube wari hafi aho. Musobanukirwe ko ubuzuye bw'ubwami bw'Ikiyoka bugiye kurekurwa igihe gito, kugira ngo birukane abagenewe ubugingo buhoraho mu maboko yanjye akiza! Kuko iyi myuka ifunzwe izarekurwa ngo yifatanye n'ubwami bwa Satani byuzuye ku maso y'isi. Kandi bazinjira mu ngurube nanone. Kuko barwana n'abantu kandi banesha abantu kuko irari ryabo ridashobora guhazwa kandi iyi ni yo myuka iha imbaraga ingurube kandi ikorana n'ingurube kandi izi ngurube z'ishyamba ni ikinyabiziga cyabo.

Si bo gusa ahubwo n'abazashyirwaho ikimenyetso cyabo cy'inyamaswa bose bazaba ibinyabiziga byemeye by'iyi myuka izarwanya abantu nanone. Kuko bazongera kumira umuntu kamere muri ibi bihe bya nyuma, kuko ari ko byagenwe kuba muri ibi bihe by'imperuka. Kandi iherezo ryamaze gutangira kandi riraza byuzuye vuba cyane uko amakuba ya Yakobo akwira mu gihugu. Isezerano ryo kubyara riri hafi cyane ubu ntirihagarara kandi Yahweh yakoze ibimenyetso byinshi mu isi n'ibimenyetso n'ibitangaza byinshi mu isanzure no mu ijuru no mu isi, nyamara benshi baracyasobanura ibi bimenyetso mu ntekerezo zitagira ukwizera.

Dore! Aho kuza kwihana, imbaga y'abantu ndetse ishimishwa n'imikumbi y'udukoko twibasiye benshi no mu bihugu byinshi hirya no hino ku isi. Baseka kandi bakinisha imiburo yatanzwe na Yahweh ku isi kuko Imana ihoraho izahaguruka mu buturo bwayo kandi ikigaragaza kandi abaguye barahinda umushyitsi kuko ibihe byabo bigeze, kandi bafashwe n'ubwoba buhoraho. Kuko mwa baguye mwe mwatekereje ko mushobora gufata Imana ihoraho mu buriganya, mu gihe ikintu nk'icyo kidashoboka. Kuko ni ryari inzabya z'igisebo zahagurutse ngo zirwane kandi zifate umubumbyi mu buriganya? Ariko ubwo ni bwo busazi n'ubwibone bw'abaguye, kandi ntibazacika itanura ryabo rihora ryaka.

Nuko rero musobanukirwe ibihe mubayemo kandi mugume muri jye, Torero ryanjye, ndetse nawe Mugeni wanjye! Kuko gucungurwa kwanyu kwuzuye n'umurage wanyu wuzuye bitazatinda, kandi ntimuzasigara mu ngurube kuko mwizeye izina ryanjye muri iyi minsi y'ubuntu n'imbabazi, bityo muzagira ubuntu n'imbabazi bihoraho iteka ryose. Kandi abavandimwe banyu bazabiyungaho mu iteka ryose no mu Bwami bwanjye buza bw'igikiranuka, ndetse n'abiteguye kugaragara kwanjye bose.

Kuko ndi umutunganya watsa umuriro weza kugira ngo neze ubutunzi bwa Yahweh, nkura inkamba, kandi ndi uwicara nk'umucuzi ufite umuvuba mu ntoki watsa amakara kandi uconga akanacura ibyuma by'agaciro bya Yahweh kugira ngo atange imbaraga zihoraho kandi zitagira iherezo. Imbaga nini, ndetse n'imbaga y'amambaga, izaza kuri jye inyuze mu makuba ya Yakobo, kandi bazasarura ingororano ikomeye, kandi ni abaraganwa b'agakiza gahoraho kandi bazajya mu byishimo bihoraho kandi Isirayeli izakizwa! Nuko rero ntimutinye bana banjye, kandi ntimuhangayikire abanyu mukunda kandi munyizere jye Kristo Yesu, kuko nshobora gukiza byimazeyo abambaza izina ryanjye bose, kandi nta n'umwe wagenewe ubugingo buhoraho ushobora gushikuzwa mu kuboko kwanjye, kandi nzakiza benshi mu muriro uhoraho, ndetse mbakure mu birimi by'umuriro.

Jyewe Yeshua HaMashiach nohereje aya magambo binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye, kandi binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye aya magambo yoherejwe!

Ndi uha abanyabwenge ubwenge, kugira ngo umunyabwenge arusheho kugira ubwenge.

Ndi Umwana w'Intama w'Imana uje nk'Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda.

Ntagira icyaha, nyamara nishyuye ikiguzi kitatekerezwa ku bwo gucungurwa no kubaturwa kwanyu.

Ndi uwicara nk'umucuzi uconga akanacura ibyuma by'agaciro by'ubutunzi bwa Yahweh.

Nshobora gukiza byimazeyo abambaza izina ryanjye bose, kandi nta n'umwe wagenewe ubugingo buhoraho ushobora gushikuzwa mu kuboko kwanjye.

Mube maso mutegereze kugaragara kwanjye, kuko ibihe bigeze. Igihe gisigaye gitanzwe gusa kugira ngo gikorere intego za Yahweh bana banjye, nuko rero ntimwemere gusinzirizwa nanone, ndetse mwima amatwi amagambo yanjye y'ubwenge n'imiburo myinshi.

Umunyabwenge abona icyago akiyisha, naho abapfapfa barakomeza, bakabona ishyano. Sinabahamagariye ubwenge bwa Yahweh buhoraho?

Kandi ndi uzabahisha aho mutari kuzigera mwihisha ubwanyu, kuko nzabahisha mu cyumba cy'intebe y'ubwami cya Yahweh ndetse no mu ijuru ry'amajuru aho muzinjira mu byishimo by'Umwami wanyu kandi ibyumba by'ibirori byo mu ijuru bizabishimira mwese, kuko vuba kuruta uko mubitekereza muzabonesha amaso yanyu ubwiza bw'icyubahiro, buhoraho bubategereje mu murage wanyu. Mukomeze kuba mu rukundo rwanjye, kuko mukundwa cyane na Data kandi muri imboni y'ijisho ryanjye, abo ntakorwa n'isoni zo kwita abavandimwe banjye.

Jyewe Yeshua HaMashiach mvuze kandi nohereje aya magambo bityo yanditswe kandi Ubutumwa Bwiza Buhoraho bwahishuwe mu gihe cyabwo.
