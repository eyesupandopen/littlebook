Inzibacyuho ku Bizerwa muri Kristo, Hanyuma Kurimbuka Gutunguranye ku Isi

Dore! Impinduka zikomeye kandi zifite imbaraga zirimo kuba, kandi ihererekanya ryenda kurangira, ndetse n'igihe isi ivanwa mu Bwami bwo mu Ijuru aho isi itegekwa munsi y'Isezerano Rishya kandi ikibi gikumirwa kugira ngo kitajya mu Bwami bw'Ikiyoka, ibyo bararikira. Nk'uko byabaye mu bihe byanjye by'ubuntu n'imbabazi ku isi byatangiye kuri wa munsi mukuru wa Pentekote muri za Yubile nyinshi zishize, ubu ntibizongera kubaho ukundi. Ndetse n'ihererekanya riva mu Isezerano Rishya ryanjye, rijya ku butegetsi bw'Ikiyoka, inyamaswa iva mu nyanja, n'inyamaswa iva mu isi ari na we muhanuzi wabo w'ibinyoma, ariko Isezerano Rishya ryanjye ntirizigera rihinduka ubusa kuko mu by'ukuri, mu by'ukuri, Isezerano Rishya ryanjye ni Isezerano ry'iteka, ndetse n'Isezerano Yahweh yemeye ibihe byose n'iteka ryose, Sela.

Dore! Ihererekanyabubasha kuva mu Bwami bwo mu Ijuru kujya ku butegetsi bwa nyuma bw'inyamaswa, butazigera bugira ubundi bubusimbura. Dore! Ndi Yeshua HaMashiach kandi nza kuzana inkuru nziza y'amahoro n'ibyishimo ku bizerwa muri Kristo, ndetse kugira ngo ibyishimo byanyu byuzure, kandi ntimwihebe mu byo mwiteze kandi ntimutakaze icyerekezo cy'ibyiringiro byanyu, bigiye guhishurwa mu buryo butunguranye ubwo nzagaragara mu bicu mu buryo butunguranye ubu, kandi muzatwarwa. Kuko, nza gushikukuza abizerwa muri Kristo mbajyana mu ijuru, kandi ni nde ushobora guhagarika ukuboko kwanjye gukomeye? Ndavuga nti ni nde?!

Kuko nza mfite imbaraga n'ubutware bwuzuye bw'intebe y'ubwami ya Yahweh ubwe kandi nza gutwara umutungo wanjye niguze amaraso yanjye bwite acungura, ndetse n'amaraso yanjye atagira icyaha natanze ku bushake mu mbaraga zikomeye n'urukundo rudashira ku bw'ibyaha by'isi. Ndetse kugira ngo ibyaha bya Adamu bibe kuri Yesu w'i Nazareti, ndetse uwo Yohana Umubatiza yagaragaje akoresheje Amagambo ngo "Dore! Umwana w'Intama w'Imana, ukuraho ibyaha by'isi!"

Uko niko biri, ko mu by'ukuri, mu by'ukuri, nishyuye igihano cya Mose ku bose bemeye Yesu w'i Nazareti nk'amasezerano yabo y'amahoro hagati yabo na Yahweh, kuko Adamu ari umwana w'ikirara wasuzuguye Yahweh maze akayoboka umukumbi w'ingurube wa Satani nk'abagaragu b'icyaha. Nyamara, Amagambo yanjye aguma ari ay'ukuri kuko umugaragu w'icyaha ataguma mu nsi y'Ubwami bwa Satani niba bemeye kandi bakatura Yesu nk'Umwami. Mu by'ukuri, mu by'ukuri, umugaragu ntaguma mu nzu y'icyaha iteka ryose niba bahisemo kwemera agakiza k'iteka muri Yesu Kristo kuko nta rindi zina rizigera ritangwa ku bw'agakiza ka Adamu, kandi abaguye ntibazigera bababarirwa.

Nyamara, abanga ubutumire bwanjye n'abihinduye bose mu nzira z'i Kanani n'abazashyirwaho ikimenyetso cy'inyamaswa bose bazaguma mu nzu y'icyaha iteka ryose kuko abahinduka abana b'icyaha bazaguma munsi y'imbaraga z'angirika ry'imiterere y'inzoka iteka ryose, kandi abakora ibitutsi bose ku izina rya Yahweh, uwo Adamu yaremwe, ntibazigera bababarirwa, mu by'ukuri icyigize kigoramye ntigishobora kugororwa. Ku bw'ibyo, mu butabera butunganye bwa Yahweh abakora ibyaha byose by'abaguye kandi bagahindura imiterere yabo bwite bakoze icyaha cyica, nk'uko nategetse Yohana Uhishurirwa kwandika mu nzandiko ze ku bizerwa muri Kristo. Kandi abanga amaraso yera kandi atagira icyaha ya Yesu w'i Nazareti bose nk'ikintu kidakwiriye bazifatanya n'abaguye mu murage wabo w'amazuku yaka umuriro, kuko mu by'ukuri, mu by'ukuri gucungurwa kwanyu kwantwaye ikiguzi gikomeye naba niteguye kongera kwishyura, nubwo ikintu nk'icyo kidakenewe iteka ryose.

Kuki mwihebye, bakunzi banjye? Kuki ishyaka ryanyu rigabanuka? Ese nshobora kubaza umugeni wicara yijimye mu gahinda, ndetse n'igihe umukwe yegereje? Ndetse n'igihe umukwe wanyu yatangaje ko yegereje kuza, nyamara benshi muri mwe mufite agahinda mu mutima nk'aho nabanze? Nyamara, uko niko bimeze ko abari hafi cyane mu gihe cyo gukoraniriza hamwe gukomeye kw'abizerwa muri Kristo mu buryo runaka bemeye gutakaza ibyishimo byabo, ndetse no kumva impamvu zitagira ukwizera z'abahanuzi n'abakozi b'ibinyoma, no kwemerera imyambi yaka umuriro gukora ibyari mu ntekerezo zabo byasenya ukwizera kwabo niba bidazimijwe n'Amagambo yanjye n'umwuka! Kuko Amagambo yanjye, ni umwuka, kandi ni ubuzima ku bagendera mu kwizera! Ku bw'ibyo, ntimwiyemerere kwibagirwa ko hari ikamba ryo gukiranuka ryabikiwe abakunda kuboneka kwanjye bose, gusa ntimurekure amakamba yanyu bakunzi banjye, kuko Satani ashaka kubambura umurage wanyu.

Kuki umugeni wanjye yijimye mu maso kubera agahinda no gucika intege, nk'aho yatereranywe n'umukwe utizerwa? Ese, njye Kristo Yesu, nigeze mbaha Amagambo atizerwa, cyangwa nigeze mbabeshya, bakunzi banjye? Oya, mu by'ukuri ndi Umwami Wizerwa Iteka kandi w'Iteka ryose w'Gukiranuka uwo buri gihe ari yego na amena kuko ndi we amasezerano yose ya Yahweh na Yeshuah HaMashiach asohoreramo iteka ryose. Ku bw'ibyo, musobanukirwe imiterere y'impinduka igiye gukorwa muri mwe, ndetse namwe mbwira, ndetse n'abizerwa muri Kristo! Ndetse n'itorero ryanjye, umubiri wanjye, ndetse n'Umugeni wanjye, uwo nyoherereza aya Magambo yo guhumuriza! Nuko rero mwishime!

Benshi muri mwe bafata kuvuka ubwa kabiri nk'imbaraga zo gutsinda ibyaha byanyu, kandi mu by'ukuri ndabaha imbaraga zo gutsinda ibyaha byanyu kugira ngo mube abatsinzi kandi mutsinde muri byose, icyakora kuvuka ubwa kabiri birenze gutsinda ibyaha. Nubwo nashyizeho ikimenyetso ku bizerwa muri Kristo, ndetse n'icyo kimenyetso kidashobora kumeneka cya Yahweh ubwe, ndetse n'uwo mwuka w'ubwana ubwawo atari ukwuzura ko kuvuka ubwa kabiri, ahubwo ari avansi y'umutungo waguze w'igucungurwa ryabo ryuzuye ryegereje kandi ryasezeranyijwe. Nk'uko byanditswe, Uwo namwe mwiringiye, mumaze kumva ijambo ry'ukuri, ari ryo Butumwa Bwiza bw'agakiza kanyu, uwo kandi mumaze kumwizera, mwahawe ikimenyetso cy'umwuka wera w'isezerano, Ari we ngwate y'umurage wacu, kugeza ubwo abacunguwe bazabona umudendezo wabo, kugira ngo ubwiza bwe bushimgwe.

Ku bw'ibyo, ikimenyetso cy'Imana nzima kigumaho kandi gishikamye ku bo nashyizeho ikimenyetso bose, kandi mushobora kwiyita abavutse ubwa kabiri, kandi mu by'ukuri nabahaye imbaraga z'imiterere yanjye nshya kugira ngo mugende mukiranuka kandi mutunganye mu kumvira kose k'ukwizera uko mugenda mu mwuka, kuko umukiranutsi azabeshwaho no kwizera! Ku bw'ibyo, musobanukirwe ko nubwo mufite ukwizera kose, n'ibigaragaza imbaraga byose, ndetse n'impano zose n'ubushobozi bitangwa muri uwo mwuka w'ubwana mwahawe igihe mwashyirwagaho ikimenyetso, imbaraga zose n'ubwiza bwo kuvuka ubwa kabiri ntibizamenyekana kugeza ubwo mu buryo butunguranye ku isi mutazaba muhari!

Kuko, nza kubatwara mu ijuru, ndetse n'aho muzatangazwa n'ubwiza muzaremwamo bushya kandi mukambikwa. Muzahagarara mu cyumba cy'intebe y'ubwami cya Yahweh aho nzerekana Urumuri rw'Isi kuri Data w'Urumuri kandi muzabona kandi muzamenya kandi muzumva kandi muzasobanukirwa. Kandi uko muhagaze mu bwiza bukomeye imbere y'intebe y'ubwami y'ubwiza bw'iteka, muzarabagirana nk'inyenyeri mu isanzure ry'ijuru, kandi muzagira imiterere yaka. Kandi umumarayika azashyira ikimenyetso ku 144,000 banjye mu ruhanga rwabo kandi ubushobozi bwinshi, ubuhanga, n'imbaraga muzabigira mwese nk'uko ubudahemuka bwanyu bwabaye. Kuko muri uwo mwanya mwese muzaba iteka ryose igice cy'Ubumana bw'iteka iteka ryose, ntimuzigera muvaho. Sela.

Kandi icyo gihe ubwo gukoraniriza hamwe abizerwa muri Kristo ku Mwami wabo w'Iteka ryose w'Gukiranuka bibaye mu buryo butunguranye kandi butari bwitezwe ubu, ndetse n'abumva ijwi ry'iyo mpanda ya nyuma bose, n'abumva Amagambo ngo "Zamuka hano" bazisanga mu buryo butunguranye kandi butari bwitezwe bavutse ubwa kabiri ndetse nk'ikiremwa gishya kandi gitunganye kandi kitagira icyaha kandi cyahawe ubwiza, cyakozwe ndetse nkurikije Umwami wanyu w'Iteka ryose w'Gukiranuka; kuko ndi Adamu wa Nyuma wahinduwe umwuka utanga ubuzima kandi agakiza kanyu kuzuye kari ku muryango wanyu.

Ku bw'ibyo, nje kubabwira nanone ngo mwitegure kandi mugire ibyiringiro byanyu byuzuye byo gucungurwa kwuzuye imbere mu ntekerezo zanyu n'imitima yanyu, kuko mu by'ukuri ibihe byanyu byageze. Ku bw'ibyo, mwitegure muva mu byaha byose n'ishusho yose y'ikibi. Mukomeze muve muri iyi si yuzuye ibyaha kandi yaciriwe urubanza kugira ngo ntimusangire ku byago byayo n'imanza zayo na gato.

Mwature ibyaha byanyu kuri njye, Kristo Yesu, kandi mumbaze ku byerekeye ubuzima bwanyu kugira ngo mbereke ibyaha byanyu n'amakosa yanyu wenda mutazi, ndetse n'Urukundo rwa Yahweh rutwikira ibyaha byinshi kuko bidashoboka ko mwabyatura byose. Mutunganye ibyanyu kandi musobanukirwe ko ubuzima bwanyu kuri iyi si mu mubiri w'icyaha burangiye kandi ntimuzongera kugira amahitamo yavumwe yo gukurikira imiterere y'icyaha isaba kwitabwaho kwanyu no kumvira mu gukiranirwa kose, ahubwo muzavuka ubwa kabiri, muvuke muturutse hejuru, ndetse muvuke muri bazima bushya iteka ryose ntimuzongera gukora icyaha kandi ntimuzongera gupfa ukundi, kandi ni njye Kristo Yesu ubitangaza gutyo kandi muzambikwa gukiranuka kwuzuye, mumbuwe iteka kubora kw'icyaha iteka ryose.

Ndi Kristo Yesu, kandi nohereje aya Magambo binyuze ku ntumwa yanjye n'umugaragu wanjye kandi binyuze ku ntumwa yanjye n'umugaragu wanjye aya Magambo yoherejwe, ndetse kugira ngo itorero ryanjye, umubiri wanjye, ndetse n'umugeni wanjye bazahagarara bashikamye mu byiringiro byuzuye kandi bishimishije byo gucungurwa kwabo kwuzuye kugiye gukwira isi yose ndetse nk'uko umurabyo uva iburasirazuba ukagera iburengerazuba kandi mwese muzahindurwa mu kanya nk'ako guhumbya!

Hanyuma bizaba, nk'uko byahanuwe, ko kurimbuka gutunguranye kuzagwira Babuloni y'Ibanga n'inkombe zayo zizatwikirwa, izacikamo kabiri hagati kuva mu kigobe kugeza mu biyaga, umuriro mukomeye uzagwa n'ibisasu byabo bizaterwa, ndetse Umumarayika wanjye ukomeye azajugunya ibuye rikomeye mu nyanja kandi umujyi mukuru wa Babuloni y'Ibanga ntuzarokoka, cyangwa umujyi w'Abamarayika kuko mu by'ukuri, mu by'ukuri, imijyi yayo y'ibyaha izashya kandi yubikwe. Ingabo z'inyamaswa zizarekurwa kandi zizahiga buri muntu kandi zizica benshi, kandi bafite amabwiriza yo kurimbura abadahuye n'intego zabo bose, cyangwa abashobora guhaguruka bakabarwanya undi munsi. Uko niko intambara yabo ya gatatu y'isi izaba.

Gutingita gukomeye n'intambara yeruye bizarimbura amahanga menshi n'imijyi n'imidugudu kandi isi izacikamo ibice ahantu henshi, kandi ukumira azakurwaho mu buryo butunguranye, n'urubura ruzagwa, n'ibizira byabo bizarekurwa n'ikuzimu hazafungurwa n'ubwami bwuzuye bwa Satani buzarekurwa kandi bwishyire hamwe mu kibi mu ntambara yabo n'abera aho bazabatsinda ndetse nk'uko byanditswe. Kandi urubanza rukomeye ruzagwa mu buryo butunguranye, ndetse nk'uko byaburiwe, kandi bigatangazwa kandi niko bizaba ko urubanza rukomeye ruzagwa ku isi yose iyo ihererekanya rirangiye. Kandi ibi kuko umuntu kamere yifuza abaguye kandi yifuza ikibi cyabo n'umwijima wabo, ku bw'ibyo, ikibi n'umwijima ubu umuntu kamere azabigira kandi ubuhungiro bwe bwonyine ni ukwambaza izina rya Yesu kugeza ku iherezo rye, kuko abigometse ba Adamu ubu bazahabwa byuzuye ba Satani bazarimbura. Nyamara, nzaza gukiza asigaye akomeye ava muri Yakobo mu maboko y'144,000 banjye bakomeye! Sela!

Ndi, Kristo Yesu, kandi natangaje mbere ikoraniro ry'abizerwa muri Kristo ku Mwami wabo w'Iteka kandi w'Iteka ryose, ndetse no mu Butumwa Bwiza bw'Agakiza n'Ibanga ry'Imana n'Ubutumwa Bwiza bw'Iteka ryose kandi nza gushimangira ukuri kw'ibyo natangaje ndetse n'ibyo Yahweh yatangaje, kuko ibyatangajwe byose mu ntebe y'ubwami n'imbaraga bya Yahweh bizasohora neza nk'uko byatangajwe kandi ntihazabaho gutinda ukundi.

Ndi Umwami wanyu w'Iteka ryose w'Gukiranuka, kandi nza gucyaha abo nkunda bose, kuko bidakwiriye ko uwo ari we wese mu bizerwa muri Kristo, ndetse n'Umugeni wanjye bwite watsinze byose nk'uko natsinze yagakwiye kwicara yihebye nk'aho ibyiringiro byanyu byo gucungurwa mu kuvuka ubwa kabiri kwuzuye mu ijuru ry'amajuru byahagaritswe cyangwa byatinze cyangwa ko wenda Umwami wanyu yatinze mu nzira mu gukiranirwa. Imana ibirinde ikintu kidashoboka kitazigera kiba!

Kuko ibintu nk'ibyo bidashoboka, nubwo Satani yashaka gusenya ukwizera kwanyu n'imyambi ye yaka umuriro, ku bw'ibyo, muzimye imyambi ye n'ingabo y'ukwizera, (ndetse n'ubushobozi bwanjye bwose nabahaye) kandi muhagarare mu byiringiro byuzuye kandi bishimishije byo kuboneka kwanjye. Ntimuhangayikishwe n'abashyiraho amatariki n'amajwi menshi avuga kandi yitonganya, ahubwo murebe Umwami wanyu w'Abami n'Umwami w'Abami wegereje kandi uzakora neza nk'uko byatangajwe mu ntebe y'ubwami ya Yahweh.

Nuko rero ndabaza, ni nde ushobora guhagarika ukuboko kwanjye gukomeye ngo kudashikukuza abo nkunda kandi nashyizeho ikimenyetso cy'umwuka wera w'isezerano kugira ngo muzane uburinganire mu ijuru kandi muzane gukiranuka ku isi ubwo muzaza gutegekana n'Umwami wanyu w'Abami n'Umwami w'Abami, ndetse n'ubutegetsi bukomeye munsi y'Umutambyi Mukuru iteka ryose mu buryo bwa Melikisedeki? Sela!

Dore, ndi hafi. Ku bw'ibyo, mube maso mu byiringiro byuzuye kuko abizerwa muri Kristo batazasigirwa umuriro w'utunganya mu gucika intege gukomeye. Mu by'ukuri, mwatsinze. Sela.
