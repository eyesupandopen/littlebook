Igihe Cyawe cya NONAHA

NONAHA haje Yeshua HaMashiach kukubwira impamvu z'ibyo nakoze, kugira ngo wumve neza Amagambo yanjye wumve impamvu zatumye iki gihe cya NONAHA kigeraho! Kugira ngo wumve ingaruka zizagwa mu isaha imwe n'inyamaswa mu buryo butunguranye kandi butari bwitezwe muri uku kurangira kwa NONAHA, kandi kugira ngo witegure mu buryo bwuzuye ihuza rizahirima kuri iyi si mu rubanza rw'umuriro mu gihe abizerwa muri Kristo Yesu bazagenda ako kanya. Selah.

Erekeza umutima wawe WOSE, ubugingo, ubwenge, n'imbaraga mu kumvira Kristo Yesu NONAHA kuko ibi bise birangira gitungura NONAHA. Aho uzatwarwa gitungura kandi utabiteganyije nonaha, ukajyanwa mu cyumba cy'intebe y'ubwami cya Adonai-YHVH.

Uko niko bimeze, Jyewe Kristo Yesu, nje kubabwira ibyanyu mwebwe abizerwa banjye muri Kristo Yesu kugira ngo musobanukirwe imigambi yanyu ya Adonai-YHVH natumye mbatandukanya n'iyi si igoramye kandi yanduye mbere y'uko urubanza rugwa.

Muri itorero ryanjye, umubiri wanjye, ndetse n'umugeni wanjye: Bityo, musobanukirwe uwo muri we muri Kristo Yesu. Kuko mbategetse mwese NONAHA, gushyira ubwenge bwanyu bwose ku bintu byo hejuru, atari ku bintu byo mu isi, bizahindurwa umuyonga mu rubanza. Kuko mwapfuye, kandi ubugingo bwanyu buhishe hamwe na Kristo Yesu muri Adonai-YHVH. Ubwo Kristo Yesu, ari we bugingo bwanyu, azaboneka, namwe muzabonekana na we mu bwiza.

Musobanukirwe mwa bizerwa mwese muri Kristo Yesu ko nategetse MWESE kuva mu mirimo ya kamere no kuva mu nzira za Babuloni y'Ibanga zayigejeje ku rubanza rwayo rw'umuriro rwikubye kabiri, ruzagera no ku yandi mahanga y'indaya hakurikijwe uko ubucuruzi bwayo bwabaye.

Nyamara benshi muri mwe mwinangiye mwanga amabwiriza yanjye, asobanurwa mu magambo y'iki gihe nonaha atari mu ndimi zihinduwe, nyamara mukomeza kwicara mu mwijima mu kutumvira kweruye nk'aho Amagambo yanjye ari ayo guhitamo mu nzira zanyu z'akazuyazi. Nta rwitwazo mufite.

Aho, aho gukorera Umwami Yesu Kristo waguze ubugingo bwanyu mu kuboko kw'abanzi banyu b'iteka, mwakoresheje gukizwa kwanyu nk'umwanya wa kamere kandi mu buryo bwikora muguma mu bucakara bw'abaguye mu mirimo ya kamere mu buhakanyi bw'uburyarya uko nyine.

Musobanukirwe, mwese abigaburira kuri izi nshuro zidasera mu byaha byanyu bya kamere mu bishuko bya dopamine bifitanye isano n'imikino, ko kubera kutumvira kwanyu no gutuka kwinangira umutima kuri Kristo Yesu, mutazinjira mu Bwami bwo mu Ijuru muri iki gihe! Kandi ibi si ukubera igikorwa cyo gukina umukino, ahubwo ni imikino mukina. Mwirinde imirimo ya kamere uko byagenda kose, mwige kugira ngo mumenye.

Ahubwo muzisanga mwambuwe umurage kandi mwatanzwe burundu mu mirimo ya kamere mu gihe cy'amakuba ya Yakobo. Iyo mirimo ya kamere mugomba kuyihana kandi mukaguma mu mimerere yo kwihana no kwitegura nk'uko mwabitegetswe kugira ngo nshobore kubacungura byuzuye mu rubanza rukaze rutagenewe abumvira ari bo bizerwa muri Kristo Yesu. Ahubwo ku batumvira n'abinangiye imitima NONAHA banga kwinginga kwanjye kwa nyuma bagwa muri uru rubanza uko nyine. Selah.

Musobanukirwe, mushobora gukoresha ibi bikoresho by'ingenzi mu muryango wanyu waguye ku bw'imigambi yo gukiranuka no gukira no kuba bazima no gusoma ubuhamya bwanjye binyuze mu Magambo Yanjye ari muri aka gatabo gafunguye nafunguye mu kuboko kw'intumwa yanjye n'umugaragu wanjye. Kugira ngo mugendere imbere yanjye mutunganye mu kumvira kwuje urukundo. Ibintu byose mu rugero! Muve mu byaha bya kamere! Suko mwagabanya ibyaha byanyu bya kamere, nubwo, benshi muri mwe bakora ibyo ngibyo. Mwirinde ishusho yose y'ikibi kandi mugerageze byose, mugundire ibyiza. Abakiranirwa ntibazaragwa ubwami bwa Adonai-YHVH, muburiwe byuzuye NONAHA.

Gukina ubwabyo si icyaha; ndetse n'igitekerezo cyabyo. Icyakora, kwemera kubushake imirimo ya kamere mu gihe abandi bakora ibyaha byabo mu gihe mushimishwa n'ibikorwa byabo ni kimwe no gukora ibyo byaha ubwanyu. Naho inzira y'umwanda yaba iyihe: Yaba televiziyo, ibitabo, tabuleti, telefone, n'ibindi nk'ibyo: Ndababwira nweruye: Abigaburira umwanda bose bazasigara mu banyamwanda, kandi ibyo birasezeranyijwe NONAHA.

Musobanukirwe, buri wese muri mwe wavuye mu Itorero rya Lawodikiya ashaka kubarwa mu bizerwa muri Kristo Yesu, nk'uko mukwiye. Mugomba kuva mu nzira zanyu z'akazuyazi zo kutumvira igice Amagambo yanjye kandi mugatanga umutima wanyu wose ubugingo ubwenge n'imbaraga mu bisobanuro byimbitse kugira ngo mukomezwe mu bizerwa muri Kristo Yesu. Kuko bidahagije kuva mu matorero yanyu ya Lawodikiya no guhindura aho muri kugira ngo mutangire ubuzima bushya na/cyangwa umwirondoro mudafite kwihana kwuzuye kw'ibyaha byanyu byose, ntimushobora gutangira ubuzima bushya kuko izi ari inzira z'abaguye. Hagize uwo muri mwe wibwira muri we mu gukiranirwa kwe ko nshobora gutamba iyi ngororano ku bw'iki cyaha, noneho by'ukuri ntimwiteguye kwinjira mu Bwami bwo mu Ijuru muri iki gihe.

Musobanukirwe: Mwese abatizerwa muri Kristo Yesu musoma aya Magambo: Cyangwa muze aho ndi NONAHA, kandi mugume mu kwihana kwuzuye, aho mwemera ibyaha byanyu imbere yanjye kandi mugasaba imbabazi kandi mukabireka ako kanya cyangwa muzasigirwa umuriro w'utunganya. Ndabategeka kuguma mu budahemuka munsi y'amaraso yanjye y'impongano azabeza byuzuye, bitabaye ibyo, mwirengagize Amagambo yanjye kandi mugume munsi y'ubutware bw'izi nshuro z'ibyaha kandi muhangane n'urubanza rw'abanyabyaha rutabagenewe.

Benshi muri mwe mukunda ibikoresho byanyu cyane ku buryo mutakimenya Kristo Yesu ndetse no kunyerekezaho ibitekerezo byanyu ku bw'agakiza kanyu. Bamwe muri mwe mwemerera ibi bikoresho bibeshya kubabwira ko Amagambo yanjye yera yatanzwe muri aka Gatabo Gato k'imanza zikiranuka ari ukuri igice, cyangwa ibinyoma byeruye bibabohera mu burozi bwabyo n'uburiganya aho intiti imwe itavuga rumwe n'indi, aho benshi muri mwe atari intiti none mwabimenya mute? Cyangwa ko niyo mwaba muri intiti mu ntiti, hari izindi ntiti nyinshi zitavuga rumwe, bityo, ni inde washobora kumenya ukuri. Kandi bityo, Amagambo "bahora biga ariko ntibashobore kugera ku kumenya ukuri" ahagaze mu gucirwaho iteka ry'iteka ku bose bakunda gukora no kubaho mu binyoma byabo, cyangwa ibinyoma by'abandi uko nyine.

Mwumve Amagambo yanjye, nta ngororano z'igice zihari kandi nta mishahara igabanyije kandi nta konti zo kuzigama mu bwami bwo mu Ijuru kuko ibi bintu byakuweho iteka ryose kuri mwe NONAHA mu gucungurwa kwanyu kwuzuye. Byongeye kandi, musobanukirwe, ku iherezo ry'icyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli ibi bintu bizaba byarahagaze mu rubanza rw'umuriro rwatanzwe na Adonai-YHVH ku itegeko rya Kristo Yesu iteka ryose.

Ikizaba ku batizerwa kiroroshye: Ntibazatwarwa mu murage w'iteka NONAHA ahubwo bazasigara hamwe n'abaciriwe urubanza kugira ngo bigaragaze ko ari abizerwa mu guhara amagara ku bw'izina ryanjye. Nibasanga bakwiye iri cyubahiro. Nyamara, ku bahawe iri cyubahiro bose muzabona agakiza nimwanga gucika intege. Bityo ndabinginga mwese mu rukundo rwanjye ruhora rwaka kuri mwe: Muve byuzuye mu kutumvira kwose NONAHA kandi mureke ako kanya kwirengagiza amabwiriza yanjye ya rhema muri aka gatabo gafunguye kugira ngo muve byuzuye mu nzira za Babuloni y'Ibanga, musome kandi musobanukirwe Amagambo yanjye kandi munsabe kubereka ubuzima bwanyu, ndabakunda iteka n'iteka ryose!

Muri Itorero RYANJYE: Ekklesia yanjye yahamagawe iteraniye hamwe kandi ishyizweho ikimenyetso ku bw'imigambi yanjye muri ibi bihe by'ubuntu byarangiye NONAHA. Abizerwa muri Kristo Yesu bakuwe muri Isirayeli guhera i Yudaya mu minsi ya Ponsiyo Pilato, hanyuma no mu mahanga yose kimwe ko by'ukuri ayo mashami y'igiti cy'umunzenze cyo gasozi NONAHA agomba guterwa ku giti cy'umunzenze cya kamere iteka n'iteka ryose. Aho mutewe muri Isirayeli ya Adonai-YHVH ku itegeko rya Adonai-YHVH Ubwe, bikozwe n'amaboko yanjye akomeye kuko abaneshi banjye bizerwa NONAHA bagaragaye ko ari abizerwa muri Kristo Yesu, MWISHIME!

Mugume muri NJYE, bakunzi banjye, kandi ntimwemerere amaboko yanyu kuguma yanduye naramuka yanduye. Mwature ibyaha byanyu NONAHA, ndi hano kumwe namwe. Nzabibakura kandi mbeze byuzuye.

Uko niko bimeze, Jyewe Yeshua HaMashiach, nje kubabwira impamvu z'ingaruka z'igihe cyanjye cy'impamvu z'ibyo nakoze, kugira ngo murye aka Gatabo Gato gafunguye nafunguye mu kuboko kw'intumwa yanjye n'umugaragu wanjye, ndetse Biblaridion:144, nubwo karyoshye mu kanwa kandi gasharira mu nda. Kugira ngo musobanukirwe ubushake bwanjye ku buzima bwanyu mu nzira zanyu zose, kandi kugira ngo musobanukirwe iki gihe cya nyuma cyahagaritswe cya NONAHA cyatanzwe kugira ngo mwese muve byuzuye mu byaha bya kamere, cyangwa muzaguma mu muriro wanjye w'utunganya kugira ngo mwambaze izina ryanjye kugeza ku iherezo, cyangwa muzagwa mu rubanza rw'iteka mu nyanja y'umuriro nimuhakana byuzuye. Kuko nta rundi ruhererekane rw'amahitamo ruhari, ni ibi.

Biblaridion:144 ni umugabo ukiranuka ku bw'igikiranuko cyanjye cyonyine cyatanzwe mu mwuka wanjye weza kandi mu gihano cyinshi no kwiyeza mu gukiranuka kw'inzira ZANJYE za Adonai-YHVH we, byatanzwe n'amaboko ya Se Yeshua. Kandi muri izi nzira, asangwa ari uwizerwa kandi n'umuneshi ukomeye utaranesheje Heylel Ben Shachar wenyine, (wamugabyeho igitero imbonankubone), ahubwo n'aba barinzi baguye batiyeguriye Imana bahawe umudendezo bakamugabaho igitero na bo. Nyamara mu buntu bwanjye n'imbabazi zanjye n'umuriro wanjye w'utunganya Biblaridion:144 yaguye inshuro zirindwi, wongeyeho inshuro zirindwi zindi, nyamara, aracyagaruka mu budahemuka guhagarara ku maguru ye akomeye kurushaho ku itegeko ryanjye, kandi yiyemeje mu mutima mu kumvira kwose gukiranuka ntiyitege gutorwa mu byimbo, nyamara agendera mu kwizera kwuzuye kuko njyewe nenyine mumuha binyuze mu maboko y'abandi.

Jyewe, Kristo Yesu, ari we kandi Yeshua HaMashiach natandukanyije Biblaridion:144 n'umurimo wanjye wera mu Itorero ryanjye aho namushyize kugira ngo mwigishe inzira zanjye n'uko yakorera bagenzi be by'ukuri mu rukundo, abababarira aho kubagabaho igitero iyo alter yatewe kandi by'ukuri nta alters zisigaye muri Biblaridion:144 ahubwo ni umuhamya muzima muri byose. Namwigishije kandi uko yicara ku meza y'ifunguro ryemewe ndetse no kugera ku bo hasi cyane mu tuyira tw'inyuma. Hanyuma naramufashe ndamubahisha mwese kugira ngo nshobore kumweza mu muriro wanjye w'utunganya mu byo yakorewe byose no kumweza mu byaha bye byari byinshi kandi bikabije.

Musobanukirwe ko ibyaha yaguyemo byatewe n'ibyo yakorewe mu buryo bunyuranyije n'amategeko we na mushiki we mu buryo bw'ubutabire n'amaboko y'aba barinzi baguye bakiri abana mbere y'imyaka yo kubazwa ibyo bakora. Ibi bintu yabikorewe mu buryo bunyuranyije n'amategeko muri gahunda z'ibanga kandi zatoranyijwe z'ibikorwa bya psychologique zashyizwe muri aya mashuri y'amayobera yiswe nabi uburezi rusange n'aba barinzi batiyeguriye Imana, ibyo bigo by'umwanda bikaba bikwirakwijwe muri Babuloni y'Ibanga. Yahawe ibiyobyabwenge byinshi mu busore bwe kandi ahatirwa, hanyuma aremera kandi yitabira imyitozo yabo ku bw'ubwumvikane bwe bw'ubusore bw'ubupfu. Aho yabonye imbabazi kubera ubusore bwe no gusenya umuryango we biturutse ku ngaruka mbi zo hanze.

Mu by'ukuri yahawe aba barinzi batiyeguriye Imana na se wizeye ibinyoma byabo ko azagirwa umugabo mwiza kandi watsinze munsi y'ubwitonzi bwabo kandi bakazakora nka mukuru we kugira ngo bamusubize mu muryango kandi akazabona amanota meza nk'igisubizo. Bamugabanyijemo uburyo bwinshi bwemewe muri uru ruhererekane rw'ibintu kandi bamutoza mu buhanzi bwinshi bw'ubupfumu n'ubuhanzi bwinshi bw'imirwano byagabanyijwe ubukana kandi bipfundikirwa mu gukiranuka ku bw'imigambi yanjye.

Nyamara, Adonai-YHVH yari azi neza uru ruhererekane rw'ibintu mbere y'ishingwa ry'isi kandi aho gukura Biblaridion:144 mu maboko yabo ahubwo yamwemereye kwihanganira neza kandi gusa ibihuye n'imigambi yanjye nyayo ya Adonai-YHVH muri Yeshua HaMashiach kugira ngo akomeze kandi ahure uru rwabya rukomeye rw'urubanza n'intambara. Nyamara, Jyewe, Kristo Yesu nihishe ababigizemo uruhare bose nyamara ngumana na we igihe cyose. Nyamara, yagizwe umuntu uteje akaga kandi ushoboye umurimo we yagenewe kuri Heylel Ben Shachar, yazaniwe n'aba barinzi baguye bavuzwe muri ubu buhamya. Mu rukundo rwanjye kuri we namwujuje urukundo n'impuhwe n'uburinzi kandi ndamukiza, ndetse naremye bundi bushya ubwenge bwe mu rukundo rwanjye kuri we. By'ukuri, ni umwungeri wizerwa wanyeretse Ijambo rye kuko yagaragaje ko nshobora by'ukuri kumwiringira nanone.

Bityo ibi byemerewe kubereka bose ko bidashoboka kuri uwo ari we wese muri bo gukura UMUNTU UWO ARI WE WOSE mu maboko yanjye AKOMEYE kandi no kugira ngo Biblaridion:144 asangire mu mibabaro yanjye kandi yababaye cyane kandi yababaye igihe kirekire, nyamara aracyahagaze mu kumvira iruhande rwanjye ntiyitege ikintu na kimwe mu byimbo.

Ku bw'ibyo, byaremewe ko atozwa mu bucece kandi mu ibanga mu nzira zabo z'amayeri n'imirwano, nyamara arinzwe n'ukuboko kwanjye kuko nashyizeho ikimenyetso Biblaridion:144 mu busore bwe mbere y'uko yinjira mu burezi rusange, aho we na mushiki we bamenyekanye kandi bakibasirwa muri aya mashuri y'amayobera ku bw'icyiswe resonance yabo yahawe impano, mu by'ukuri cyari ikimenyetso cy'umwuka wera cyashyizwe ku buzima bwabo n'ukuboko kwanjye ndetse n'ubushobozi bwa kamere bwateguwe na Adonai-YHVH muri izi nzabya zombi, zatoranyijwe kandi zibikiwe imigambi yanjye. Kandi bityo, aba barinzi baguye bamwemeye bishimye kandi ntibitangiriye itama mu mbaraga zabo zo kumena icyo kimenyetso cyera cyo gukiranuka namushyizeho. Ku bw'imbaraga zabo zose bose bananiwe bikabije nk'uko bigaragazwa n'aka gatabo gafunguye n'ibizakorwa n'amaboko ya Biblaridion:144 mu rubanza rw'aba barinzi baguye ari rwo rwa NONAHA.

Musobanukirwe ko yaturutse mu muryango wibasiwe ibyo bikaba bimenyerewe mu bihugu bya AmeruKa, kandi benshi muri mwe muturuka muri iyi mimerere imwe.

Mushiki we, wahohotewe mu busore bwe mbere y'imyaka yo kubazwa ibyo akora azahagarara mu nzu yanjye kubera ibintu yihanganiye mu busore bwe mu maboko y'aba barinzi baguye bamwe no ku byo yahatirijwe mu bujiji mu busore bwe byamukomerekeje cyane ku buryo atigeze akira, ku buryo atongeye kwita ku kubaho kuko yiyitiriraga urupfu rw'umwana we wamukuweho mu buryo burenganya mu cyaha.

Ku bw'ibyo, nababariye bose baje aho ndi muri iki kibazo bashaka imbabazi munsi y'amaraso yanjye y'impongano, kuko ku musaraba nishyuriye ibyaha aba barinzi baguye bari guteza ndetse n'ibintu byose bakunda gukora n'ibitaramo byabo, n'ibintu bihindura imitekerereze.

Sobanukirwa ko ubuntu n'imbabazi bigaragazwa muri ubu buryo kugira ngo hatagira n'umwe ucira urubanza ku bw'imigambi ya Adonai-YHVH keretse niba urubanza rubagenewe ku bw'inangire y'imitima yabo. Kandi mu gukiranuka kwuzuye n'ubutabera bwuzuye nta kurobanura ku butoni kubaho muri ibi byemezo, ahubwo urubanza rutunganye rutangwa na Adonai-YHVH rushingiye ku mwana w'intama wa Adonai-YHVH ruzuzuzwa byimazeyo n'Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda. Ku bw'ibyo, humurizwa cyane ku bw'abakundwa bawe wenda utekereza ko bazimiye ariko mu by'ukuri bashyizweho ikimenyetso, basinziriye mu isi kugira ngo bazurwe mbere yanyu mwebwe abazima basigaye. Selah.

Ku bw'ibyo, azahagarara mu nzu yanjye uko byagenda kose kuko mu byago bye no mu bihano bye yihannye byimazeyo mu minota ye ya nyuma kandi muri ibyo bikorwa hamwe n'ibitekerezo n'imigambi y'ubuzima bw'umutima arababariwe kandi azasohoza imigambi ye y'iteka ryose. Yari azi neza ko ndi kumwe na we kuko namuhishuriye kamere yanjye y'umucyo kugira ngo muhoze kandi mumenyeshe ko aruhuka neza ko yari yarengewe kandi ahabwa uburozi n'abantu babi boherejwe na ba barinzi baguye bagomba kugwa mu rubanza rw'iteka ku iherezo ry'icyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli kandi ibihe byabo byageze koko.

Nuko rero, sobanukirwa kandi uhumurizwe cyane kuko nzababarira uwo nzababarira kuko inzira zanjye ari inzira za Adonai-YHVH wanjye uhora akungahaye ku mbabazi n'ubuntu kandi uhora ahagaze kugira ngo azamure abaneshejwe. Ndetse n'abo mu mitima yabo no mu ntekerezo zabo badashaka ikindi kitari ukunesha ibintu byose, nyamara ubuzima bwabo bugasa n'ubucibwa bugufi. Nyamara, ntibishoboka gukura uwo ari we wese muri mwe mu maboko yanjye uko ibintu byaba bimeze kose.

Sobanukirwa uwo ndi we: Ndi umwana w'intama wa Adonai-YHVH kandi bose uretse abana b'abatumvira bashobora kwihana kuko umugaragu ataguma mu nzu y'icyaha iteka ryose ahubwo umwana ni we uhaguma. Ku bw'iyi mpamvu, Umwana wa Adonai-YHVH yarahishuwe kugira ngo mbohore byimazeyo kandi nrimbure buri kimenyetso cyose ku maso y'isi imirimo yose ya Heylel Ben Shachar. Kandi nuko bimeze: Ku mbaraga z'umwuka wanjye wera w'isezerano, uwo mbaremeramo mwese, nsenya imirimo ya Heylel Ben Shachar mu buzima bwanyu mu buryo ubwo ari bwo bwose mwagabwaho igitero kandi mukabohwa.

Ku bw'ibyo, sobanukirwa, ko njyewe Yesu w'i Nazareti ari na we mwana w'intama wa Adonai-YHVH naje gukiza abanyabyaha abo benshi muri mwe muri abakuru babo. Kandi nuko bimeze, ko Adonai-YHVH yahisemo kubagira mwese ingero mu mbabazi ze n'ubuntu bwe yashutse ku isi ku bwinshi binyuze muri Kristo Yesu, ari we Yeshua HaMashiach. Kandi nuko, ntoranya kandi nkazamura ingero rimwe na rimwe mu bakiranutsi banjye kugira ngo mwese mumenye kugira neza kw'iteka rya Adonai-YHVH, ndetse na El Shaddai Ukomeye!

Kandi nuko bimeze, abana banjye b'ibirara bagarukiye Adonai-YHVH wanyu mu kumvira kwuje urukundo kandi baguma ari abizerwa mu murimo uwo ari wo wose cyangwa imirimo mwahawe n'amaboko yanjye. Ku bw'ibyo, NONAHA nzabazamura mwese nbereye mu cyumba cy'intebe y'ubwami cya Adonai-YHVH!

Ese musobanukiwe ubukuru bw'uku kuzamurwa?

MWABISOBANUKIRWA MUTE?

Kandi ibi ni ukubera ko bitarinjira byimazeyo mu mitima y'abantu ibintu mwateguriwe kandi bibategereje mu maboko yanjye, mukwiye kwishima cyane aho kureba kalendari zanyu kuko ndi ku isi yamaze kugera muri iki gihe cya nonaha.

Ku bw'ukwihangana kwanyu n'ubudahemuka, muzahembwa bikomeye kandi mu buryo butunganye kandi mu tuntu duto duto ku bw'umurimo wose wakozwe mu gukorera Kristo Yesu ku rwego rwo hejuru ku buryo nta yindi nzira yo hejuru yo kugaragaza uku kuzamurwa no gushimira. Bityo rero, sobanukirwa ko ingororano n'inshingano byo ku rwego rwo hejuru rwo mu Ijuru ari ibyanyu iteka ryose NONAHA. Kandi nuko, ndabategeka mwese, mwishimire abazamurwa imbere yanyu mu murimo. Ku bw'ibyo, mugume mwiteguye kandi mwihishe uku kurimbuka kugiye kuza mubona kwishyira ku murongo ku mugaragaro kandi ibisobanuro birambuye bizasohora neza byatangajwe ubu muri aka Gatabo Gato ko Gukiranuka kandi ndategeka bose abo aya Magambo azageraho kwibiza muri aya Magambo yatunganirijwe gushaka amaso, amatwi n'imitima y'abo yohererejwe bose.

Kandi bityo, nje muri iki gihe cya UBU kugira ngo mube mwiteguye kwinjira mu Ijuru kandi bityo ndabategeka mwese kujya mu byumba byanyu by'Abageni kandi musabane nanjye muri ubu buheruka. Kuko UBU ari bwo mutwarwa, kandi UBU ni ho dufite akanya kenshi ko kugira ngo muve rwose mu byaha byanyu kugira ngo mushobore kunesha muri byose kandi muhabwe ingororano yanyu yuzuye iturutse mu kuboko kwanjye kwiteguye.

Ndi Kristo Yesu ari we kandi Yeshua HaMashiach kandi nohereje aya Magambo binyuze ku ntumwa yanjye y'inyambaraga n'umugaragu wanjye kandi binyuze ku ntumwa yanjye y'inyambaraga n'umugaragu wanjye aya Magambo yoherejwe.

Ndetse kugira ngo mugire ibyiringiro bikomeye by'ingororano iturutse mu kuboko kwanjye ku byo mwakoze byose mu budahemuka mu murimo. Kandi kugira ngo murusheho kubona no kumva imbabazi zanjye zizahabwa uwo zizahabwa ku itegeko rya Adonai-YHVH. Ko nta muntu n'umwe washyizweho ikimenyetso n'ukuboko kwa Kristo Yesu ushobora kuzigera ashimutwa cyangwa ngo hagire n'umwe muri mwe ushobora gukurwa mu maboko yanjye. Ibyo ntibishoboka rwose. Kandi bityo nje kwerekana ibi bintu mu muntu wa Biblaridion:144 na we werekanywe mu muntu wa Shaul, uwo igiti cy'umunzenze cy'ishyamba cyita Pawulo, uzicara iburyo bwanjye kuko yahawe ukuboko kw'iburyo kw'ubusabane, kandi ibyo ni byo bigaragazwa n'iyo nteruro muri Bibiliya zanyu.

Mugume mwiteguye kandi mugume munsi y'amaraso yanjye y'impongano muri byose no mu buryo bwose. Shyira ibitekerezo byawe byose kuri njye ubu kandi wumve ko ugomba kuva muri iyi si mu mutima no mu ntego kuko izagwa rwose kandi mu buryo butunguranye mu isaha imwe hamwe n'inyamaswa kandi igihe ni UBU.

Bishyizweho umukono ku itegeko ryanjye n'Ukuboko kwa Biblaridion:144 mu buryo ntegeka.
