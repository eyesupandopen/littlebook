Umuhamya Wabiri: Isirayeli
Ibisharira N'ibiryohereye
Ku ya 5 Nzeri 2024

Inshoberamahanga y'Ubutatu!

Nimwumve ijwi rya Yeshua HaMashiach, bakunzi banjye, kandi muze aho ndi mbahumurize kandi mubone ikiruhuko mu rukundo mbakunda, bana banjye bato. Kuko mwabaye abizerwa mu bintu bike, none nzabagira abatware b'ibintu byinshi ubwo mwinjira mu byishimo by'Umwami wanyu. Urubanza si urwanyu, mwa bizerwa banjye; ndetse n'abari muri Kristo bose n'abasinziriye muri Kristo bose bazazurirwa ubugingo bushya mu kanya nk'ako guhumbya. Kuko mu by'ukuri, nzamanuka mvuye mu ijuru n'ijwi rirenga, n'ijwi rya Marayika mukuru, n'impanda y'Imana: kandi abapfiriye muri Kristo bazazurirwa ubugingo budashira kandi namwe abazaba bakiriho bazasigara bazazamurwa hamwe na bo mu bicu bityo muzabana n'Umwami wanyu iteka ryose. Muzahita muhindurwa mu kanya gato muve mu miterere yanyu yaguye mube icyaremwe cyacunguwe byuzuye kitabora, ndetse muremwe bushya nk'umuntu umwe mushya, kandi uko ndi, ni ko namwe muzaba; kandi nk'uko byanditswe, ni ko bizakorwa mu buryo butunguranye.

Kuki mureka imitima yanyu ihagarika, bana banjye? Kuki mureka amagambo y'umurezi abahagarika imitima mukeka ko nta kuzamurwa mu bicu kuzabaho, cyangwa ko mudakwiriye ubuntu bwanjye bukiza? Ndabagira inama, bana banjye, yo gukomeza kugendera mu kwizera mudashingiye ku byo mureba; kandi ni jye wabagize abakwiriye, ndetse n'abari muri Kristo bose kandi bafite isano ikiza n'Umwami wabo ukiranuka, nuko rero ntimugashidikanye! Nubwo, mutambonye n'amaso yanyu y'umubiri, muzi neza ko ndi Umwami wanyu muzima n'umukiza, kandi muri jye ni ho hari ukuzura k'ubugingo n'agakiza. Ni ukuri, ni ukuri, unyizera ntazasonza cyangwa ngo agire inyota ukundi.

Kuko ndi umutsima w'ubugingo, kandi abawurya bose ntibazasonza ukundi; kandi ni jye masoko y'amazi y'ubugingo atemba mu burumbuke bwose n'ubugingo bushya, kandi uyanyweyeho ntazagira inyota ukundi. Ariko mwese muzagirwa bazima mu buzima bwiza bwuzuye, ubusabane bwuzuye, n'ubumwe bwuzuye na Yahweh, n'Umwami wanyu muzima n'umukiza, kuko ndi Kristo Yesu, wabaguze amaraso yanjye y'impongano, kandi mu by'ukuri muri ab'agaciro kenshi. Kandi uko ni ko kwishyura kw'agaciro kenshi kwatanzwe nkitanga iteka ryose nk'umwana w'intama w'Imana, nuko ntanga igitambo cyanjye ku bwanyu nk'umutambyi mukuru iteka ryose mu buryo bwa Melikisedeki kandi nishyura incungu yanyu mu maraso yanjye atagira icyaha.

Kandi bityo ni ko bimeze iteka ryose ko nta kindi gitambo cy'ibyaha gikenewe, kuko niba amaraso y'amapfizi n'ay'ihene, n'ivu ry'ishashi, bimishijwe ku banduye, byeza ku byerekeye kweza umubiri, Nkanswe amaraso ya Kristo wituye Imana binyuze mu Mwuka w'iteka adafite inenge, azeza imitima yanyu imirimo ipfuye, kugira ngo mukorere Imana nzima? Kandi ibyo igitambo cy'inyamaswa kitashoboraga gukora, amaraso yanjye y'impongano yashoboye kubikora rimwe rizima, kuko natanze impongano y'ibyaha by'abantu rimwe rizima, ndetse no ku bafata icyemezo cyo kwakira agakiza mu izina ryanjye bose, kuko nta rindi zina ryatanzwe munsi y'ijuru abantu bakizirwamo. Ntimukizere ibinyoma byabo rero, kuko ikiyoka, inyamaswa, n'umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma bazaduka, kandi bazashaka guhuza amadini yose y'ibinyoma yo mu rwego rw'isi munsi y'ibendera rimwe, keretse Yesu Kristo ntazemererwa kuba muri bo, haba mu izina cyangwa mu bikorwa, kandi bahawe kurwanya abera no kubanesha. Ni ukuri, ni ukuri, nta mugabane cyangwa uruhare mfitanye na bo, kandi ni jye uzabarimbura rwose, kuko nza guca urubanza no kurwana intambara mu gukiranuka.

Kandi none ndabwira abazaza aho ndi mu gihe cy'amakuba ya Yakobo, ndetse n'abera bo mu gihe cy'umubabaro mwinshi bazahabwa ingororano ikomeye n'umurage w'iteka mu Mwami wabo n'Umuhanuzi wabo w'iteka, ari we Yeshua HaMashiach. Kandi bityo byanditswe nk'ukuri kw'iteka, kandi bityo mvuga iteka ryose ko mugomba kwinjira mu irembo rifunganye: kuko irembo ari rigari, n'inzira ijyana kurimbuka ari ngari: kandi abayinyuramo ni benshi, Kuko irembo rifunganye, n'inzira ijyana mu bugingo ifunganye, kandi abayibona ni bake. Nuko rero ndabategeka, bana banjye bato, kuguma mu nzira igororotse kandi ifunganye no kwirinda kurimbuka kw'abapfapfa, ndetse n'abatarigeze bandikwa mu Gitabo cy'Ubugingo uhereye ku kuremwa kw'isi, kuko bakunda inzira zo kurimbuka, kandi bakunda gukiranirwa, kandi bakunda kuyoba gukomeye kuruta gukiranuka n'ukuri.

Kuko baziruka hirya no hino, kandi intambara yeruye igiye kuza ku isi, kandi kurimbuka kweruye kurugarije kandi kuri hafi; akaga, integuza y'amage ya 911, kandi umunsi urushaho kwegereza kandi ubari hejuru. Kandi mu ivu ry'intambara bazabyuka, kandi mu ivu ry'intambara baziyereka abantu nanone, kandi abantu bazatangazwa n'abaguye mu bwoba bwinshi kubera amayeri yabo barogesheje abazimiye uhereye kera. Bazahuza amadini yabo y'ibinyoma, kandi bazababwira ibinyoma byinshi bivuzwe mu kanwa k'imyuka ibeshya ya antikristo, kuko ba antikristo benshi badutse mu isi.

Antikristo ni iki, bana banjye? Antikristo ni urwanya Kristo, umukiza w'ukuri na Mesiya, kandi akaba mu mwanya wa Kristo; kuko ndi Yeshua HaMashiach, kandi nta wundi ubaho. Kandi bityo ibinyoma byabo byamaze gukwira isi yose kuko bateguye imbaga y'abantu kumvira ubutatu budakiranuka, ari byo kiyoka, inyamaswa ivuye mu nyanja, n'inyamaswa ivuye mu isi, ari na yo muhanuzi wabo w'ibinyoma. Ariko se Yohana yanditse iki kuri iyi ngingo mu rwandiko rwe rwa mbere? Sinabandikiye kuko mutazi ukuri: ahubwo ni ukubera ko mukuzi, kandi ko nta kinyoma gituruka mu kuri. Umubeshyi ni nde, keretse uhakana ko Yesu ari Kristo? Uwo ni we Antikristo, uhakana Data, n'Umwana. Umuntu wese uhakana Umwana, uwo ntafite Data: ariko uwemera Umwana, afite na Data.

Kandi bityo bimeze, ko jye, Yesu w'i Nazareti, ndi inzira, ukuri, n'ubugingo; kandi nta n'umwe uza kuri Yahweh keretse anyuzeho. Ni jye muryango wonyine, kandi ni jye nzira yonyine ijya kuri Yahweh, Imana ikomeye y'iteka; kuko bityo Yahweh yabitangaje kandi bityo Yahweh yabiteguye kandi abifataho icyemezo mbere y'ishingwa ry'isi mu kumenya kwe kw'iteka n'ubwenge bwe. Ni nde muri mwe ushobora kugira inama Yahweh, Imana ikomeye y'iteka? Nubwo inzovu inshuro inzovu zikikije intebe ye y'ubwami, ntakeneye umujyanama; kandi ni Yahweh, Imana ikomeye y'iteka, ukora ibintu byose akurikije inama y'ubushake bwe kugira ngo mube ishimwe ry'ubwiza bwe mwebwe ababanje kwizera Kristo.

Nuko rero mwumve ibishuko bigiye kuza byamaze kugera ku isi. Kuko abakozi ba Satani bavuga barwanya ukuri, kandi bararangurura mu mahanga kandi bavuga cyane ko amadini yose y'isi ayobora ku mana imwe uko ukwizera kwabo kwaba kumeze kose, uko imyizerere yabo yaba imeze kose, ko andi mazina yose yatanzwe n'abaguye ari ukuri kandi ayobora ku mana y'ukuri, ariko ayobora ku kiyoka cyonyine. Ni ukuri, ni ukuri ndababwira nti, ntimukizere ibinyoma byabo, kandi ntimukanabifurize ihirwe abizera ubwo bupfapfa. Kuko irembo rijya kurimbuka ari rigari, rijya muri rya ziba rinini ry'umuriro waka, kandi Satani ashaka gusunika abantu benshi ashoboye mu nzira ye ijya kurimbuka kw'iteka, kuko uwo ari wo murage we, kandi urwo ni rwo rwango abangira mwese. Ntimukizere ibinyoma byabo byo guhuza amadini, kandi muhunge ubumwe bwabo budakiranuka, kuko iherezo ryabyo ari ibirimi by'umuriro bishyushye cyane by'ubururu mu gihe amazuku yaka agurumana iteka ryose, kandi nta n'umwe uzarokoka mu baboshywe bakajugunywa mu muriro.

Mwumve, bana banjye, ko amazina yabo yose ayobora ku mana imwe, ari yo Satani, kuko atari imana ahubwo ari abiyita bo. Kandi bityo bazazamura amabendera yabo y'ubudasa, kandi bazavuga ko amazina menshi ayobora ku mana imwe. Kandi ku ruhande rumwe bavuga ukuri kuko inzira nyinshi ziyobora ku mana y'iyi si, yaciriwe urubanza kandi igihano cyayo no kurimbuka kwayo ntibitinda. Kandi ku rundi ruhande, babeshya isi kandi bashaka gushukashuka abazakurikira inzira zabo zo kurimbuka bose ngo binjire mu muryango wabo mushya uzabyuka mu ivu ry'intambara uzasaba ikimenyetso kugira ngo winjiremo.

Ntimugafate ikimenyetso cyabo, kuko abagifata bose baciriweho iteka ryose kandi bahinduwe iteka ryose. Kandi babeshya bikabije ko amazina yose ayobora ku Mana y'ukuri, ari yo Yahweh, Imana y'iteka ya Isirayeli, kuko Yahweh yitoranyirije Yakobo kugira ngo mube ubwoko bwe bwite. Kandi bityo Satani ahora ashaka kwigana inzira z'Imana imwe y'ukuri, ari yo El Shaddai, Imana Ishoborabyose! Kandi bityo yakoze amadini menshi y'ibinyoma yagenewe kwigana inzira za Yahweh, Imana y'iteka ryose, kandi uko kwizera kw'ibinyoma kose kurangirira mu kurimbuka kw'iteka ku baguye muri uwo mutego. Kuko ni nde mutego utega umutego inyoni ireba?

Kandi bityo bamaze gutanga imigisha kuri bose, ndetse n'amadini yose, kandi bamaze gushaka guhuza amadini yose munsi y'amabendera yabo yo kumvikana kw'amadini, kuko bityo ari ko inzira zabo zo kuyobya imitekerereze zigerwaho. Ku ruhande rumwe batanga igitekerezo; ku rundi ruhande batanga ikigirwanya; kandi mu makimbirane bagenzura bazana ihuriro ryabyo, kuko bashaka guhuza imico yose, amahanga, n'imiryango n'indimi mu muryango wabo wa gisitani kugira ngo barimbure abakurikira inzira zabo zo kurimbuka bose. Kandi bityo mwumve ko, igoramye kidashobora kugororwa, kandi amagambo yabo meza n'imvugo zabo nziza byagenewe gukurura amarangamutima y'abantu n'irari n'impuhwe z'ibinyoma bazita kwera. Mwumve ko umuntu ubwe yononekaye rwose kandi adashobora kugorora inzira ye bwite, kandi nta cyiza kiri mu muntu, ahubwo umuntu yononwe no kutumvira kwa Adamu kandi cyane cyane n'ibinyoma n'amayeri by'inzoka, iyo nzamenagura umutwe wayo rwose, kuko Yahweh yatangaje uhereye mbere na mbere iherezo ry'iyo nzoka ibeshya igaburirwa umukungugu w'isi.

Uko ni ko byabaye, uko ni ko bimeze, kandi uko ni ko bizaba, ko nta gishya kiri munsi y'izuba. Kandi uko isi iva mu buntu ijya mu rubanza, amakuba atunguranye agiye kugwa, kandi bizaba mu buryo butunguranye. Nuko rero ngira inama abasoma aya magambo bose kumva ko aya magambo atabagezeho ku bw'impanuka, ahubwo yoherejwe na Yahweh ubwe binyuze ku mwana we w'ikinege, ari we Yeshuah HaMashiach, avuzwe mu ijwi rimwe n'abagaragu banjye b'abahanuzi. Kandi ni jye, Yesu w'i Nazareti, ari we Yeshua HaMashiach, wishyuye ikiguzi cy'iteka cy'agakiza kanyu n'agakiza k'abazaza mu bugingo buhoraho bose mu rukundo rwanjye rudashira kandi rw'iteka Yahweh yandemeye gusohoza. Ndababwira kimwe n'icyo navuze uhereye mbere na mbere: ko jye, Yesu Kristo, ari we Yeshua HaMashiach, ndi inzira, ukuri, n'ubugingo, kandi nta muntu uza kuri Data keretse anyuzeho; kandi nta rindi zina ryatanzwe mu isi, na rimwe, abantu bagomba gukizirwamo, kandi andi mazina yose uko yaba yigana neza kose ntayobora ku Mana y'ukuri, ari yo Se w'Imicyo udatindiganya cyangwa ngo agire igicucu cyo guhinduka. Nuko rero mwumve, bana banjye bato, ko muri umucyo w'isi Yahweh yabyaye, ndetse akabarema bushya muri jye, Kristo Yesu Umwami ukiranuka w'iteka, kandi nahinduwe umwuka utanga ubugingo.

Dore, ndabereka inshoberamahanga izaha ubwenge abanyabwenge kandi ikarinda abapfapfa n'abadasobanukiwe kwifatanya n'abajya kurimbuka mu nzira zabo. Gatatu, kabiri, kamwe: kandi mu madini menshi y'isi hari atatu yiganje azayobora ku w'ikinyoma udakiranuka, kandi ayo matatu agaragara nk'abanzi kandi yarwanye intambara nyinshi. Nyamara, muri ayo matatu, abiri ni inshuti mu ibanga, kandi rimwe rizakoresha irindi, kandi yombi ayobora ku kurimbuka. Kuko muri ayo matatu, abiri azahindukirana yivuye inyuma rimwe, ari ryo ryera banga ariko ryabanesheje rwose. Kandi bityo uwera azazana urubanza mu nzu y'Imana mbere, kuko urubanza rutangirira mu nzu y'Imana kandi umunsi w'urubanza wageze.

Nubwo uwera yanesheje, abiri muri ayo matatu azahindukira kugira ngo arimbure iryo rimwe, ariko uko kurimbuka ntigushoboka, kandi iryo rimwe ryamaze kubona intsinzi yuzuye. Ariko uko ni ko kuyobya kuzaba ko abiri muri ayo matatu azahindukirana iryo rimwe kandi agashinja ibinyoma mu kanwa k'abatangabuhamya b'ibinyoma benshi, kandi bazarwanya abera babaneshe. Kandi bakwiza mu isi ibinyoma byinshi bituka Imana, ariko iryo rimwe, ari ryo ryera ry'Imana, ryamaze kubanesha kandi rizamenagura umutwe wabo rwose. Ariko ayo mabiri azagaragara nk'ayanesheje iryo rimwe ashaka kurimbura, kandi muri ayo mabiri azarwanya iryo rimwe, nubwo kurimbuka kwabo kwa nyuma kudatinda. Kandi bityo umutego uzategerwa isi, ko mu rugomo rwinshi n'intambara bazihuza ubwabo kugira ngo gatatu, kabiri, kamwe bibe kabiri, kamwe, gatatu kuko bazihuza ubwabo mu idini rimwe ry'ikinyoma riyobowe n'ubutatu budakiranuka, ari byo kiyoka, inyamaswa ivuye mu nyanja, n'inyamaswa ivuye mu isi, ari na yo muhanuzi wabo w'ibinyoma.

Nuko rero mbereke igisubizo cy'ihurizo ryanjye ry'ibya gatatu. Ibyo uko ari gatatu ni amadini atatu akomeye yiganje mu isi ya none; kandi muri ayo uko ari atatu, rimwe gusa ni ryo ry'ukuri, ari ryo ryera ry'Imana; kuko jyewe, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndi uwera w'Imana wanesheje umubi, akaba n'Umwami w'abami n'Umutware w'abatware. Sinshobora kuneshwa, kandi nahawe izina risumba amazina yose, kugira ngo mw'izina rya Yesu Kristo, ari we Yeshua HaMashiach (kuko ndi umukiza w'abanyamahanga n'Abayuda bose), amavi yose apfukame, mu ijuru no mu isi ndetse no munsi y'isi. Nuko rero amadini abiri y'ibinyoma agaragara nk'arwanya irindi ariko yashatse kwiyegereza iryo rimwe, kandi koko Satani yiganye itorero ryanjye ry'ukuri akoresheje indaya y'ikinyoma isingiza kandi igaramya umwamikazi w'ijuru. Nuko bashaka ihuriro ry'ikinyoma kandi bavangavanga ibinyoma byinshi mu kuri, keretse ko ibinyoma n'ukuri bitivanga: uba ufite ikinyoma cyangwa ukagira ukuri: nta yandi mahitamo ahari.

Kandi nk'uko itorero ryanjye ry'ukuri rizamurwa mu ijuru n'uzitira agakurwaho, ni ko n'itorero ry'ikinyoma rizarimburwa, bityo iryo rimwe rigakurwaho burundu. Amadini abiri asigaye azazamuka mu ntsinzi y'ikinyoma, kandi azagira amahoro y'ikinyoma, n'ubumwe bw'ikinyoma azashaka kugira ngo ahuze abazimiye, ndetse atange ubugingo buhoraho, ubuzima buzira umuze, n'umunsi mushya ikiyoka kizatanga. Kandi azahuza ayo madini yombi ayagire idini ba se batigeze bamenya ariko rizagaragara nk'irisa kuko muntu atazi amateka ye y'ukuri. Kandi binyuze muri uko kwihuza kw'irimbuka bazajya mu muriro utazima, kuko ababiyungaho bose ari abishimira gukiranirwa, kandi si ibyo gusa, ahubwo n'abakunda kandi bakizera bagakora ndetse bakaramya ibinyoma kuko inzira zabo ari iz'ikinyoma n'ubugome kandi inzira zabo ziyobora ku rupfu rwa kabiri.

Nuko bazayobya isi, izabatangarira, iti: Ni nde uhwanye n'inyamaswa? Kandi inyamaswa imwe izahabwa ububasha bw'indi nyamaswa, n'inyamaswa yo mu nyanja izabategeka nk'uko ubutegetsi bwayo buri, n'inyamaswa yo mu isi izategeka igihugu nk'uko ubutegetsi bwayo buri, kandi bazategeka isi mu gukiranirwa. Kandi muri ayo abiri asigaye, azihuza abe rimwe. Keretse ko, inzira zabo ari urupfu, kandi aho ni ho umutego utegwa, kuko ni nde mutego w'inyoni utega umutego inyoni ireba? Abakurikira inzira zabo z'ubumwe bw'amadini bose bazahuzwa, gusa mu murage w'abaguye; kandi abiyunga bose binyuze mu kurumwa n'inzoka yabo bazasangira umurage w'uwaguye muri rya ziba rinini ryaka umuriro n'amazuku aho bazashya iteka ryose n'inyo yabo ntigwe. Nuko rero ubumwe bwabo bw'amadini bwuzuyemo imitego ihishe mubuhanga, kuko ni nde mutego w'inyoni utega umutego inyoni ireba?

Nuko rero ngira inama abasoma aya magambo bose n'abafite amaso yo kureba n'amatwi yo kumva n'umutima wo gusobanukirwa, ko agakiza n'ubugingo buhoraho n'Imana y'ukuri, ari we Yahweh ubwe, biboneka mu rindi zina iryo ari ryo ryose keretse mu izina ry'Umwana w'ikinege w'Imana, ari we Yesu Kristo, ari na we Yeshua HaMashiach; kandi ndi umukiza w'abantu bose, ndetse n'urubyaro rwa Adamu urwo amaraso y'abantu bose basangiye byuzuye. Kandi mu rurimi urwo ari rwo rwose, no mu ishyanga iryo ari ryo ryose, bose bashobora kuza ku buntu bakakira impano yanjye y'agakiza k'iteka bambaza izina ryanjye ryera kugeza ku iherezo. Kuko ndi Umwami n'umukiza wanyu, gusa ntimuzigere mureka izina ryanjye riva ku minwa yanyu, kandi nzabakiza rwose.

Ntimwifatanye n'ubumwe bwabo bw'amadini, kuko batazi icyo baramya, kandi batwawe n'ibinyoma bizabayobora kurimbuka kwabo kw'iteka. Kandi nuko agakiza ntabwo kaboneka mu rindi zina, kandi nta rindi zina ryatanzwe abantu bashobora gukirizwamo kandi ngo abantu begere Yahweh, kuko udafite Umwana adafite na Data. Nuko rero nzashyigikira kandi nkomeze abambaza izina ryanjye ryera bose kugeza ku iherezo. Kandi muzankorera ibitangaza bikomeye, kandi mbatumye gukunda Uwiteka Imana yanyu n'umutima wanyu wose, n'ubugingo bwanyu bwose, n'ubwenge bwanyu bwose, n'imbaraga zanyu zose no gukunda mugenzi wanyu nk'uko mwikunda no gufashanya no gukundana mu bihe bibi cyane bigiye kugwira isi.

Nuko gatatu, kabiri, rimwe, aho abiri azahindukirira rimwe, n'abiri asigaye azihuza abe rimwe kandi azategekwa na batatu banduye, bazategeka abantu amazina yabo atigeze yandikwa mu Gitabo cy'Ubugingo uhereye mbere y'ishingwa ry'isi. Gusa ntimwifatanye nabo, bera banjye; kuko ibyiza ari ukubura ubugingo bwanyu kugira ngo mububone kuruta gukunda ubugingo bwanyu gusa kugira ngo mububure iteka mu kurimbuka guhoraho. Dore! Gutandukanya kwa nyuma kugiye gutangira kandi ibihe ni ibi. Ntimubatinye, bana banjye bato, ahubwo mwishingikirize ku Mwami n'umukiza wanyu uhoraho. Ndi umufasha uhoraho mu bihe by'amage, kandi nzongera nkize imbaga y'amateraniro mu gihe cy'amakuba ya Yakobo, kandi nzongera nkize abasigaye ba Isirayeli, kandi nta n'umwe ushobora kubakura mu maboko yanjye.

Musobanukirwe ko ibyiringiro byanyu ari ibyiringiro by'iteka, kandi sinabasize mudafite ibyiringiro, kandi nabasigiye icyitegererezo gihoraho. Kuko byanditswe kuri njye mu Baheburayo ngo: Dutumbira Yesu wenyine, ni we Banze n'Usohoza kwizera kwacu, uwo kuko yashimishijwe n'ibyishimo byamushyizwe imbere yihanganiye umusaraba, ntiyita ku isoni zawo, yicara iburyo bw'intebe y'Imana. Nuko rero musobanukirwe ko icyitegererezo cyanjye cyo kumvira cyumvikana nk'icyitegererezo cyo gukomeza kwizera kwanyu kugira ngo mwihangane kugeza ku iherezo, nk'uko nanjye nihanganye kugeza ku iherezo ubwo navugaga amagambo ya nyuma ku musaraba nti: Birarangiye. Nuko Imana inzuza mu bapfuye kuko bitashobokaga ko urupfu rumfata, kuko amaraso yanjye ari amaraso atariho urubanza.

Nuko rero mbahaye ibyiringiro bikomeye, bana banjye bato, ndetse kugira ngo mushyire intekerezo zanyu ku byishimo byabashyizwe imbere kuko imibabaro y'amakuba ya Yakobo itagereranywa kandi ntiyemerewe kuvugwa ugereranyije n'ibyishimo by'iteka byabashyizwe imbere. Dore! Byanditswe na Yohana Uhishurirwa ngo: Aba ni abavuye mu makuba akomeye, bameshe amakanzu yabo, bayejesha amaraso y'Umwana w'Intama. Ni cyo gituma bari imbere y'intebe y'Imana, bakayikorera ku manywa na nijoro mu rusengero rwayo: kandi uwicaye ku ntebe azabana nabo. Ntibazongera gusonza, kandi ntibazongera kugira inyota, izuba ntirizabica, cyangwa icyokere cyose. Kuko Umwana w'Intama uri hagati y'intebe azabaragira, akabayobora ku masoko y'amazi y'ubugingo: kandi Imana izahanagura amarira yose ku maso yabo. Bazaba abatambyi b'Imana n'aba Kristo, kandi bazimana na we imyaka igihumbi.

Kandi uko ni ko ibyiringiro byanyu biri, bakundwa banjye, kugira ngo mwihangane kugeza ku iherezo. Kandi ibyanyu ni ibyiringiro by'iteka kuko El Shaddai, Imana Ishoborabyose, izarema ijuru rishya n'isi nshya ibamo gukiranuka, kandi muzahabwa uburenganzira bwo kwinjira muri uwo mudugudu wera wateguwe kandi utatswe na Yahweh ubwe nk'umugeni warimbishirijwe umukwe we, kandi muzarya ku giti cy'ubugingo. Kandi nk'uko byanditswe ngo: Unesha azaragwa byose, nanjye nzaba Imana ye, na we azaba umwana wanjye.

Jyewe, Yeshua HaMashiach, ni njye wohereje amagambo yanjye binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye, kandi binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye amagambo yanjye yoherejwe kugira ngo abasoma bose bumvire.

Kandi nahishuye ubutumwa bwiza bw'iteka, kuko ibihe byo kubuhishura ari ibi.

Ndi uje gutwara abari muri Kristo kandi nzakiza abizerwa mu itorero ryanjye mbakure mu muriro w'utunganya n'umujinya uzaza.

Ndi utandukanya ingano n'urumamfu, kandi nta n'umwe muri mwe ushobora gukurwa mu kuboko kwanjye.

Muhagarare ku bwanjye, bera banjye bo mu makuba, kandi muze aho ndi, yewe Isirayeli, kuko ndi Yeshua HaMashiach, Mesiya wanyu w'ukuri, kandi mbahaye ibyiringiro bikomeye n'imbaraga zikomeye. Kandi benshi muri mwe bazankorera imirimo ikomeye, kandi nta n'umwe muri mwe uzabura kugororerwa, kuko n'utuntu duto cyane mukora mu bihe by'amakuba akomeye tuzana ingororano y'iteka, ubwiza bwabyo imibabaro yanyu idakwiriye kuvugwa; nuko rero mwambaze izina ryanjye rikomeye, bantu banjye! Agakiza n'ingororano ikomeye biri mu kuboko kwanjye ku banesha, kuko uko ari ko Yeshua HaMashiach asezeranya abizerwa.

Dore, ibihe ni ibi!
