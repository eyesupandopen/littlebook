Ubuhamya Bubiri: Itorero
Mu Mirimo y'Intumwa Pawulo
8 Gicurasi 2024

Umva Amagambo Yanjye y'Ihumure Kandi Wumve Amagambo Yanjye y'Imbaraga!

Umva amagambo y'Umwami, ndetse n'amagambo ya Yeshua HaMashiach. Ngwino aho ndi, Mugeni wanjye; ngwino aho ndi witeguye, kuko ndaje kugutwara. Ruhukira muri jye, bana banjye bato, kandi mwicare ku birenge byanjye. Mwumve amagambo yanjye y'ihumure, kandi mwumve amagambo yanjye y'imbaraga, kuko nabahaye umufasha kandi muri uwo mufasha harimo imbaraga zikomeye. Munyumve kandi mwizere amagambo yanjye, mucishe bugufi ubwibone bwanyu kugira ngo mwumve amajwi y'intumwa zanjye, kuko zicisha bugufi mu mutima. Sinaboherereje abakomeye b'iyi si; sinaboherereje abafite ubutunzi bwinshi n'icyubahiro (nubwo biyoberanya). Oya, naboherereje abo isi yajugunye mu gasuzuguro kuko bamvugira kandi umwuka wanjye uri muri bo kandi ntushobora gukurwamo.

Ntimutangazwe n'uko isi ibanga, kuko isi yabanje kunyanga, kandi umugaragu ntaruta shebuja. Niba baranyanze, namwe bazabanga. Ariko muri ba nde ku buryo mwatinya icyo umuntu yabakorera? Muri ba nde ku buryo mwakwicara mu mwijima no mu bwoba kandi narabayoboye kuri Data akaba na So, ndetse Imana ikomeye ihoraho, kuko nta yindi ibaho! Jyewe, ndetse na Kristo Yesu, nabicaje mu byo mu ijuru, kandi abari mu ijuru bose bategereje cyane bafite ibyiringiro byinshi ukuza kwawe vuba, Mugeni wanjye.

Kuko ndetse n'Imana ikomeye kandi ihoraho, ndetse na Data, ndetse na Yahweh ubwe (uhoraho iteka ryose) agutegereje cyane! Kuko vuba cyane uzagaragara imbere y'intebe ye y'ubwami mu bwiza bwinshi, kandi mu cyumba cye cy'intebe y'ubwami uzahicara, kuko uzagabana ku ntebe yanjye y'ubwami. Abamarayika nabo bagutegereje bishimye, bana banjye, kuko habaho ibyishimo imbere yabo iyo umunyabyaha umwe yihannye. Kuko bimeze bityo: Ahubwo mwageze ku musozi wa Siyoni, no ku murwa w'Imana ihoraho, Yerusalemu yo mu ijuru, no mu iteraniro ry'abamarayika batabarika. Ndetse na bamwe muri abo bamarayika ni abakozi b'abazaragwa agakiza, kandi ibyo biragukubiyemo, Mugeni wanjye. Kandi jyewe, ndetse na Kristo Yesu, ndetse nanjye nifuza cyane ukubaho kwawe imbere yanjye. Kuko ndagukunda kandi nkakwifuza, kandi vuba aha tuzahuzwa iteka ryose n'ubukwe bw'Umwana w'Intama. Ndi Umukwe wawe wifuza Umugeni wanjye, kandi ndaje kugutwara! Naguteguriye ibintu byinshi, kandi ubutunzi bwinshi n'ingororano z'ubudahemuka biragutegereje. Vuba aha uzagera ku murage wawe wuzuye muri jye, kandi uzagira iteka ryose ryo kubona ubukuru n'ubwiza bw'ibyo nkoranyiriza kuri jye kugira ngo mbisohoze, n'icyo nzagukoramo, kuko uri icyaremwe gishya, umuntu mushya, waremewe muri jye! Kandi uzankorera ibintu byinshi, byinshi cyane, atari mu Bwami bwanjye bw'Gukiranuka gusa aho nzategeka isi mu gukiranuka umunsi umwe! Ariko cyane cyane mu ijuru rishya n'isi nshya ubukuru bwawe buzabagirana, kandi uzabana nanjye iteka ryose. Kuko umunsi umwe ku Mwami umeze nk'imyaka igihumbi, n'imyaka igihumbi nk'umunsi umwe. Mfite ikibazo mbabaza, bakundwa banjye, ndetse nanjye, Yesu Kristo, Umwami w'abami n'Umutware w'abatware! Munyumve kandi mutekereze ku magambo yanjye, kuko amagambo y'Umwami ari amagambo atunganye: nk'ifeza yatunganyirijwe mu ruganda rw'ibumba, igatunganywa karindwi. Kuki mwitesha agaciro? Ndabyumva ko mwitonda kandi mucisha bugufi mu mutima, kandi ibyo biva kamere nshya naremye muri mwe, kuko umuntu wa kamere ahora yibona. Ariko kuki mwibona nk'abatagira agaciro, mwibaza impamvu Imana yakwifuza gukorana namwe, n'impamvu nabaha agakiza kandi hari abandi bakandida beza? Kuki mwicara aho mwihebye, mwicira urubanza iyo mukoze icyaha? Kuki mutikunkumura ivumbi? (Ivumbi rizaba ibyokurya by'inzoka.) Haguruka! Byuka! Kanguka! Yego, mwese muzakora icyaha kuva mu gitondo kugeza nimugoroba. Ese ibi ni ibintu Yahweh yirengagije mu kumenya kwe kwa mbere? None se ni iki? Ese Imana yibagiwe ko muzakora icyaha nyuma y'agakiza? Imana ibirinde: yego, Imana ibe inyakuri, ariko umuntu wese abe umunyabinyoma! Kuko mu mugambi wayo w'incungu, ndetse n'imigambi yayo inyerekeyeho, ndetse n'ibanga rikomeye ry'Imana, ndetse n'ibyo byose byaboshywe hamwe mu buntu n'imbabazi; mwemere gusa ubuntu bwanjye n'imbabazi zanjye, kuko benshi muri mwe mwihana mwibabaza.

Bana banjye, nishyuye ikiguzi cy'ibyaha byanyu. Iyo mugendeye mu gukiranuka no mu bunyangamugayo mugakora icyaha kuko umubiri wanyu wangiritse, mumbwire icyo cyaha gusa kandi mwiyuhagire mu maraso yanjye acungura, kuko ndi incungu yanyu kandi narabacunguye. Abacunguwe n'Umwami nibabivuge: abo yacunguye mu kuboko kw'umwanzi. Nta cyo mwakora ngo mwongere ku mirimo yanjye yarangiye, kuko imirimo yarangiye neza ikeneye iki kindi? Igihe navugaga ku musaraba mu mwuka wanjye wa nyuma muri iyo nzu y'itaka ritukura, nari nambaye icyo gihe: Birarangiye, nta cyo nasize ntagikoze. Kandi iyo Imana yemeye igitambo cyarangiye, gitunganye, ntihagire ugerageza kongeraho ikintu na kimwe; kuko iyo ari imirimo y'ubusa, ndetse n'ubwibone bwanyu bwihishe n'ubusa bwanyu, kuko mu isi hari ubusa bwinshi. Ubusa bw'ubusa, niko Umubwiriza avuga: ubusa bw'ubusa, byose ni ubusa. Kuko ubusa buvuga, kandi bukababeshya, buvuga ko mutari beza bihagije kandi igitambo cyanjye kituzuye.

Ku bw'ibyo, mugomba gukora ikindi kintu kugira ngo mwerekane ko mukwiriye iyo mwihannye imbere yanjye. Ndababwira ukuri, imbuto zikwiriye ukwihana ziremewe, ariko si ukwicira urubanza, si ukwiyanga, si imitekerereze y'ukwiheba yitsinda. Kuko iyo mukoze iyi mirimo y'ubusa y'umubiri, muba mwita ukuri ikinyoma mutabizi kandi mugerageza guhuza umubiri wanyu n'umwuka. Bana banjye, ikibyarwa n'umubiri ni umubiri kandi ikibyarwa n'umwuka ni umwuka. Kuko umubiri urarikira ibirwanya umwuka, n'umwuka ukararikira ibirwanya umubiri: kandi ibyo birahabanye: ku buryo mudashobora gukora ibyo mwifuza. Kuko ku bw'igitambo cyanjye mwiyunze nanjye, Umwami wanyu Yesu Kristo, kandi nabayunze n'Imana, kuko iryo ari ryo banga rikomeye ry'Imana muri mo uruhare. Kuki mushidikanya amagambo yanjye? Bwira umubiri wawe uceceke, kandi ucecekeshe amajwi arwanya inyigisho yanjye nzima! Kuko nabateguriye ubukuru. Nabahaye imbaraga mu mwuka wanjye w'ubwana aho mutakamba muti, ABBA, Data. Nabahaye imbaraga n'ubushobozi bwo kunkorera ibintu bikomeye; kandi hejuru ya byose, nabatumye muri jye, ndetse no mu nzandiko zanjye z'urukundo ku Mugeni wanjye. Ndetse n'inzandiko nategetse Pawulo kwandikira Itorero ryanjye zisobanura ibi mu mvugo yumvikana kugira ngo mubyumve kandi mubyizere. Kuko nabwiye Itorero ryanjye ry'i Korinto binyuze kuri Pawulo nti iki? Ku bw'ibyo, umuntu wese iyo ari muri Kristo, aba ari icyaremwe gishya: ibya kera biba bishize, dore, byose biba bishya. Kandi byose biva ku Mana, iyatwiyunze na yo binyuze kuri Yesu Kristo, ikaduha umurimo w'ubwiyunge. Bana banjye, iyo mugendeye mu mwuka wanjye kandi mukaguma ari indahemuka ku mategeko yanjye, ndabarema bundi bushya muri jye.

Kuko umuntu wa kera w'itaka ritukura arimbuka, kandi Umuyahudi n'Umunyamahanga bazagirwa umuntu umwe mushya muri jye, ndetse binyuze mu mwuka wanjye w'ubwana, kuko nzakiza bamwe muri bose. Noneho, niba hari umuntu udafite umwuka wa Kristo, uwo si uwe. Uwo namwe mwamwizeye, mwashyizweho ikimenyetso cy'uwo mwuka wera w'isezerano, Ni wo ngwate y'umurage wacu, kugeza ku gucungurwa kw'ibyo yaguze, kugira ngo ubwiza bwe bushimgwe. Nuko rero mugendere mu bugingo bushya kandi mwumve ko vuba cyane ibyo naguze bizahabwa byose (mufite avansi y'umwuka wanjye), kandi muzambikwa imibiri mishya, imibiri itangirika, imibiri ifite imbaraga mu ngano n'ubushobozi, ndetse n'amazu akwiriye umwami! Kuko muzaba abami n'abatambyi imbere y'Imana yacu. Nuko rero nabayunze n'Imana, kandi nta rwango rukiriho, kandi nabahaye umurimo w'ubwiyunge. Oya, ariko kuki nakora ikintu nk'icyo, mushobora kubaza? Umva kandi ndakubwira.

Kandi nuko Abakorinto bakomeza ubuhamya bwabo: Ni ukuvuga ko Imana yari muri Kristo, yiyunga n'isi, ntiyababaraho ibyaha byabo, kandi yaduhaye ijambo ry'ubwiyunge. Noneho rero turi ba Ambasaderi ba Kristo, nk'aho Imana ibingingira muri twe; turabinginga mu cyimbo cya Kristo, ngo mwiyunge n'Imana. Bana banjye, muri ba ambasaderi banjye, kandi mufite umwanya wubahwa cyane, kuko mumpagarariye. Kuko kuba ambasaderi wanjye ni uguhagararira Umwami w'abami n'Umutware w'abatware mu isi igiye kurimbuka, kandi ndi umutambyi mukuru iteka ryose mu buryo bwa Melikisedeki. Nuko rero, munyumve: Nabagize ba ambasaderi banjye. Nabayunze n'Imana Ishoborabyose, ndetse na El Shaddai, kuko imigambi yayo izakorwa! Nabahaye ijambo ry'ubwiyunge, ndetse n'amagambo y'ubuhanga akwiriye ba ambasaderi banjye, kuko Ijambo ry'Imana rihoraho iteka ryose. Kandi nahaye buri wese muri mwe umurimo w'ubwiyunge. Ibi bivuze ko nahaye uburenganzira buri bugingo bwabyawe bwa kabiri n'umwuka wanjye w'imbaraga nk'abakozi banjye b'imbaraga, kandi umwanzi arabatinya cyane kandi azabahunga iyo mugendeye mu mbaraga zanjye, nubwo azagerageza kubongorera amagambo y'ibinyoma ndetse agatontoma nk'intare. Ariko umunwa we unuka nta menyo ufite, kuko yamenwe n'imbaraga z'umuzuko wanjye, kandi ntazakira. Mwirengagize iyo ntare itagira imenyo, kandi mwibande ku ijwi ryanjye, ndetse n'Amagambo y'Ijambo ryanjye.

Muri uyu mwanya ukomeye w'ubutware n'imbaraga (kuko mugendera mu izina ryanjye kandi izina ryanjye riruta amazina yose, kandi Yahweh wenyine ni we mukuru!) Nabashyizeho nk'abakozi banjye na ba ambasaderi banjye, kandi nabateguye kandi mbaha ibikoresho kandi mbambika imbaraga zikomeye! Bana banjye, ntimwumva amategeko y'Amagambo yanjye, kuko abungeri baguye bahaye akazi abakozi b'i Lawodikiya ngo bababeshye ku byerekeye imbaraga n'impano byaremwe muri mwe mu kuvuka gushya. Kuko Pawulo yasobanuye ubukuru bw'imbaraga zatanzwe mu mwuka w'ubwana byaremwe muri mwe. Kuko yabwiye Abakorinto ati: Ku byerekeye iby'umwuka, bene Data, sinshaka ko mutabimenya. Kandi ukundi: Hariho impano zitandukanye, ni ukuri, ariko umwuka ni umwe. Kandi ukundi: Ariko kwiyerekana k'Umwuka guhabwa buri muntu wese, kugira ngo bose bafashwe. Ntimube injiji mu by'umwuka; mwumve itandukaniro hagati y'impano no kwiyerekana, kuko atari amagambo amwe mu nyandiko.

Natanze impano y'umwuka wera, kandi muri iyo mpano harimo kwiyerekana. Kuko Ijambo ryanjye rivuga iki: Ariko kwiyerekana k'umwuka, guhabwa buri muntu wese kugira ngo bose bafashwe.

Abakozi banyu b'akazuyaze b'i Lawodikiya basubiyemo ibinyoma Satani yavuze mu bihe byose, ibinyoma byo kubemeza ko nta mbaraga mufite, n'ibinyoma byo kugerageza guhisha imbaraga zanyu nyakuri muri jye, kuko umwuka wanyu ari Kristo muri mwe, ibyiringiro by'ubwiza! Nuko rero kwiyerekana kwanjye k'umwuka kurutonde rwakozwe gutya: ijambo ry'ubwenge, ijambo ryo kumenya, kwizera, impano zo gukiza, gukora ibitangaza, guhanura, kurobanura imyuka, indimi zitandukanye, gusobanura indimi. Mbere y'uko dukomeza, mushyire hasi ibitabo byanyu by'ibisobanuro by'isi n'inkoranyamagambo z'isi n'ibitabo by'isi, kandi mureke kubabeshya ngo basobanure bundi bushya ibyanditswe ku ntego za Satani.

Muri buri mwuka muri buri muntu hari ubu bushobozi bwo kwerekana imbaraga ziva mu ijuru! Ariko ibyo byose bikorwa n'uwo mwuka umwe, agabanya buri muntu wese uko ashaka. Bana banjye, kuki mwatekereza ko izi ari impano kandi ibyanditswe bivuga ko ari kwiyerekana? Barababeshye; musome amagambo yanjye kandi mwizere amagambo abasobanuzi banjye bahisemo, kuko nari kumwe nabo. Mufite ubu bushobozi bwose, kandi niba mushaka kubyerekana, nzabikorera muri mwe kugira ngo nkure imbohe mu kuboko kwa Satani nzigeze mu mucyo w'ubwiza bw'iteka ryose. Mwirengagize umuntu wese uvuga ibirwanya aya magambo, kandi mwiyigishe mu Ijambo ryanjye. Mureke guhangayikishwa n'uko ibi bintu bikora, kuko bidakora binyuze mu magambo y'ubuhanga n'ibitekerezo byiza. Bikora hakurikijwe ikenewe, ku byifuzo by'umuntu ugomba kugira ubushake bwo gukora ubushake bwanjye, kandi cyane cyane binyuze mu kunyizera. Kuko nzakora imirimo ikomeye mu maboko yanyu, kandi nabatumye, mugeni wanjye, kuko ubu ari ubutumwa bwa nyuma.

Natanze impano y'umwuka wera, kandi muri iyo mpano harimo izindi mpano nyinshi, kuko Abefeso bavuga iki: Ariko buri wese muri twe yahawe ubuntu, hakurikijwe urugero rw'impano ya Kristo. Nicyo gituma avuga ati: Azamutse mu ijuru, yajyanye imbohe, aha abantu impano. Kandi ukundi mu Baroma: Kuko mvuga, binyuze mu buntu nahawe, kuri buri muntu wese uri muri mwe, ngo ntiyitekerezeho ibirenze ibyo akwiriye gutekereza, ahubwo atekereze neza, nk'uko Imana yahaye buri muntu urugero rwo kwizera. Nuko rero mwumve ko mukwiriye kwitekerezaho cyane kuko mwaremwe bundi bushya mu gukiranuka no mu kwera nyakuri ndetse no kwerekana ubwiza bw'Imana ubwayo, ndetse na Data w'imicyo, kuko muri umucyo w'isi. Ariko mutekereze neza, kuko ntimuruta abavandimwe banyu na bashiki banyu babyawe n'umwuka umwe namwe: Kuko mwese mufite ubu bushobozi, kandi mwese mufite izi mpano.

Kuko nahaye abantu impano: Kandi yahaye bamwe kuba intumwa: na bamwe abahanuzi: na bamwe ababwirizabutumwa: na bamwe abungeri, n'abigisha: Kugira ngo abera batunganywe, bakore umurimo wo kugabura, kugira ngo umubiri wa Kristo wubakwe. Hariho imirimo myinshi nahaye Umugeni wanjye, kandi ni iyanyu niba mubyifuza. Munsabe kandi nzakora iyi mirimo muri mwe, ariko mugomba kugira icyifuzo. Nuko rero mwifuze cyane impano ziruta izindi: Kandi nyamara ndabereka inzira iruta izindi. Iyo nzira iruta izindi, bana banjye, ni urukundo rw'Imana rudatsindwa. Mukwiriye kwifuza cyane impano ziruta izindi, kuko nabahaye imbaraga. Ariko gukorera mu rukundo rw'Imana, gutanga ibitambo by'iminwa yanyu n'ishimwe ry'umutima wanyu wizerwa mu rukundo, biruta byose. Mutange ku buntu nk'uko mwahawe ku buntu. Mugendere muri iyi mbaraga kuko mukunda Imana kandi mukankunda, Yesu Kristo, kandi mutange ku buntu ibyo mwahawe byose, kandi mugende, mudashidikanya na gato.

Mugeni wanjye, nuko rero nabatumye gusohoza iyi mirimo, kandi vuba aha amasomo yanyu azarangira, kandi nimakoranyiriza kuri jye mu bicu, muzamenya, nk'uko namenye ku musaraba, kandi muzashobora kuvuga (nk'uko nigeze kuvuga): Birarangiye. Vuba aha muzinjira mu byishimo by'Umwami wanyu!

Ndaje kugutwara vuba cyane.

Ndifuza cyane ukubaho kwawe imbere yanjye.

Ndi Kristo Yesu kandi nakuremye bundi bushya muri jye.

Ndi umutambyi mukuru iteka ryose mu buryo bwa Melikisedeki. Ndi uwo wahaye abantu impano.

Ndi uwo waguhaye umurimo n'amagambo y'ubwiyunge.

Ndi uwo wagutumye nka ba ambasaderi banjye bizerwa.

Ndi uwo Ndi we, nuko rero ubabwire uti Ndi uwo Ndi we arantumye.

NDI UWO NDI WE arantumye kuri mwe.

Ndi uwo ugutuma muri iki gihe cya nyuma.
