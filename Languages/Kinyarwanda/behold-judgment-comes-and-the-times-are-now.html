Ubuhamya Bubiri: Itorero
Mu Mirimo ya Yohana Umubishuzi
4 Kamena 2024

Dore! Urubanza Ruraje kandi Ibihe ni Ubu!

Ibihe byo gusohozwa byageze, bana banjye bato, ndetse n'isohozwa ry'ibintu byose birebana n'icyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli. Kuko icyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli ari icyumweru cy'imyaka aho buri munsi ari umwaka. Icyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli kigiye gusohozwa kandi kizatagira ubwo abizerwa mu Itorero ryanjye bazazamurwa mu bicu gusanganira njye. Impanda y'imperuka izavuga kandi n'ijwi rirenga rya marayika mukuru abapfiriye muri Kristo bazabanza kuzuka, naho mwebwe abazaba bakiriho basigaye muzazamuranwa na bo mu bicu, bityo muzabana nanjye iteka ryose, ndetse na Kristo Yesu, kuko ndi Umunyanazareti wazutse wapfiriye ibyaha rimwe rizima kandi utazongera gupfa ukundi. Kuko ubwami bwanjye ari ubw'iteka ryose kugeza iteka ryose, kandi ubwami bwanjye ntibuzagira iherezo, kuko Yahweh ari ko yabivuze.

Ubwo abizerwa banjye bazajyanwa mu ijuru, ni ko n'ukomakoma azakurwaho, bityo ni ko itangiriro ry'icyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli rizatangira. Benshi muri mwe mugorwa n'igitekerezo cyanjye, Yeshua HaMashiach, cyo kuzamura abizerwa banjye mbakiza urubanza rutabagenewe, nyamara nzabaha kubyumva, nimuza aho ndi mwicishije bugufi nk'umwana muto, ndetse n'ubuziranenge n'amatsiko by'umwana muto. Kuko muri abana banjye bato, ndetse abana banjye bato barebera mu ndorerwamo ibijiji kandi bahanura igice. Kandi nuko ndababwira nti, mwicishe bugufi mwihane ubwibone bwanyu butagira umumaro, kuko ari icyaha, kandi muze aho ndi mwige kandi mwemere ibyanditswe byera. Kwanga igice icyo ari cyo cyose cy'ibyanditswe ni icyaha, kuko buri Jambo ry'Imana ritunganye kandi rigomba kwakirwa mu kubaha no kumvira, kandi njye, Yesu Umunyanazareti, ndi ijambo ryabaye umubiri.

Gukoranyiriza abera banjye aho ndi mu bicu ryari ibanga rihishe mu ibanga rikomeye ry'Imana. Bityo ryari ibanga mu rindi banga kandi niyo mpamvu ritahishuwe kugeza ubwo ryahishuriwe Intumwa Pawulo mu nzandiko ze. Ni we wahishuriwe ibanga rikomeye ry'Imana, kuko Pawulo na we yari igisonga cy'amabanga y'Imana, kuko ari ko namutumye. Kandi nuko mumenye ko guhakana ibyanditswe kuri iki gikorwa ari ukumpakana no kuba mu bakobwa b'abapfu.

Kandi nuko nzabasobanurira ibintu byinshi bigiye kubaho, ndetse nkomeza imirimo nahaye Yohana Umubishuzi, kuko ubuhamya bw'ibyo mpishura bwandikiwe guhisha mu gihe buhishura. Kandi nuko ubu nzababwira ibintu bitaraba kugira ngo mwitegure iminsi iri imbere. Kuko njye, Kristo Yesu, nzaha abantu banjye guhagarara mu mirimo aho mbatuma kugira ngo murangize rwose imirimo myinshi ikomeye mfite ngo muyikore.

Uko gukoranyirizwa hamwe kuba, ukomakoma arakurwaho, kandi kurimbuka gukomeye kuzagwira isi, mu buryo butunguranye kandi mu kanya gato. Kuko uko ukomakoma akurwaho, nta kizaba gisigaye cyo kubuza ibibi mu isi n'irari n'ibyifuzo bibi by'abantu. Intambara ikomeye izaduka mu isi, kandi umuvandimwe azongera guhagurukira kwica umuvandimwe we, kandi kurimbuka kwabo kuzaba gukomeye. Amahanga menshi azagwa kuko Babuloni y'Ibanga, ndetse ya ndaya ikomeye yicaye ku mazi menshi, izahindurwa umusaka mu isaha imwe.

Uwo mudugudu mukomeye, ndetse iryo shyanga rya Leta Zunze Ubumwe nyinshi, rizarimburwa rwose; kuko ryikwije umugabane wikubye kabiri w'urubanza, kandi rirabikwiye. Kuko ryabayeho mu iraha mu cyaha gikomeye; ryicaye mu bwibone bukomeye ryibwira ko ritazigera rigwa kandi ko ibintu bizakomeza, ejo nk'uyu munsi, n'ejo hazaza bikazakomeza uko byari biri ntibihinduke. Kandi indaya ikomeye yononnye isi n'igikombe cya zahabu kiri mu ntoki zayo, cyuzuye ibizira n'imyanda y'ubusambanyi bwayo. Kandi nuko urubanza rwayo ruzagwa gitumo. Umuriro mukomeye uzagwira imijyi yayo uko ibisasu bizagwa; umutingito mukomeye uzaba, kandi iryo shyanga rizigabamo kabiri hagati. Inkombe zaryo zizarengerwa n'amazi menshi, kandi ntirizongera kubyuka ukundi, kuko ibyaha byaryo ari bikomeye n'urubanza rwaryo rukaba rukomeye.

Uko intambara yaduka mu gihugu n'abagendera ku mafarashi bagasohoza imanza zabo mu isi, umubabaro mukomeye no kurimbuka bizaza ku isi. Isi iriho ubu ntizongera kubaho ukundi; ubwo ukomakoma azakurwaho, buri kimwe cyose kizaba gitandukanye. Hazabaho urupfu rukomeye, ibyorezo, inzara ikomeye, n'akarengane gakomeye; nyamara abanjye 144,000 bazarindwa umujinya n'urubanza, kuko bazaba bafite ikimenyetso cy'Imana Ihoraho, ndetse Yahweh, mu gahanga kabo; kuko ari ko bazashyirwaho ikimenyetso, kandi nohereza abamarayika banjye kubashyiraho ikimenyetso. Kandi nuko akavuyo gakomeye no kugarariza amategeko bizategeka uwo munsi. Amezi 42 ya mbere azaba ibihe by'itotezwa rikomeye n'amakuba akomeye, ariko umubabaro mukomeye uzaba mu mezi 42 ya kabiri: kuko hari amezi 84 mu minsi irindwi y'imyaka y'umubabaro wa Yakobo. Kandi nuko igice cya kabiri ni umubabaro mukomeye, ariko umubabaro n'itotezwa mu gihugu bizaba biteye ubwoba ku batuye isi igihe cyose.

Kuko yaba njye, Kristo Yesu Umwami wazamutse, cyangwa Yahweh, Imana ikomeye y'iteka (kuko nta wuhwanye na Yo), nta n'umwe wishimira imanza n'amakuba bigiye kuza. Data ntiyaremye abantu mu rukundo kugira ngo abacire urubanza kandi abarimbure. Nyamara, Satani yononnye ubusitani bw'ubuntu bw'Imana, kandi ubu kwangirika kuzasukurwa kugira ngo gukiranuka gutegeke. Kandi nuko nta yandi mahitamo y'agakiza ku banze guhamagara kwanjye kw'agakiza mu gihe cy'ubuntu. Abantu bagomba gukanguka mu bitotsi byabo no mu binezeza by'icyaha, kuko nta gihe kigihari kandi ubu ni cyo gihe cyo guhitamo iteka, cyangwa amahitamo azakorwa mu rubanza ku banyanga banga guhitamo. Amakuba menshi n'imanza zigiye kuza bizakangurira benshi agakiza k'iteka, ndetse bibashikuje mu miriro y'iteka; kuko benshi basinze ibinezeza by'icyaha, kandi nuko ibinezeza bizakurwaho. Ntabwo bazongera kugira ibyishimo muri iyi minsi, kandi kurokoka bizaba ari iby'iminota ku minota mu bihe bimwe, kandi akaga kenshi kazagwira.

Uko ni ko bizaba ko abizerwa banjye n'abatoranyijwe mu Itorero ryanjye bazahishwa kure y'ibihe by'umubabaro wa Yakobo mu ijuru. Kandi imirimo yabo izacirwa urubanza n'umuriro, kandi uwo imirimo ye izaguma azahabwa ingororano naho uwo imirimo ye izashya azahomba, ariko we ubwe azakizwa, nk'ukuwe mu muriro. Bazafata imyanya yabo mu nzego bateguriwe mu ijuru. Kuko njye, Yeshua HaMashiach, nkwiriye kwenda igitabo no gufungura ibimenyetso byacyo, kandi mu gufungura ibimenyetso abagendera ku mafarashi barabohurwa. Kandi bazagenda mu isi kunesha, kurwana intambara, guteza ibyorezo n'urupfu rukomeye. Impanda indwi zizavuga, ibyago biraje, kandi inzabya indwi z'umujinya w'Imana zizasuka mu isi mu buryo bwuzuye.

Intambara y'isi igiye kuza ntizamara imyaka, kuko Satani azi ko asigaranye igihe gito, nyamara intambara izahora ihari muri ibi bihe by'amakuba. Mu matongo n'amahano by'intambara hazazamuka inyamaswa yo mu nyanja, n'inyamaswa yo mu isi, ari na we muhanuzi w'ibinyoma. Aba bombi bazakorana, nk'uko basanzwe bakorana mu ibanga kugira ngo barimbure Babuloni y'Ibanga. Kandi amahembe icumi, azanga indaya, ayigire umusaka, ayambike ubusa, arye inyama zayo, ayitwike n'umuriro. Kuko Imana ishyize mu mitima yabo gusohoza ibyo ishaka, no guhuza inama, no guha inyamaswa ubwami bwabo, kugeza aho amagambo y'Imana azasohozerezwa. (Kandi nuko abagendera ku mafarashi bazagendera.)

Bazazamuka batanga amahoro, umutekano, no kubaha Imana kw'ibinyoma. Bazaca ikintu cyose gifitanye isano nanjye, Yesu Kristo, mu isi yose, kandi bazarwanya abera babaneshe, kuko babihawe mu ntoki zabo kubikora. Abo bita abanyamahanga babo bazigaragaza kandi bazaha abantu ubufasha bwabo, ariko aba ni abamarayika baguye bazana kuyobya gukomeye. Dore, nzanye ibihangange gusohoza umujinya wanjye, kandi uko ni ko Yahweh yavuze. Nk'uko byari mu minsi ya Nowa, ni ko bizaba ku bamarayika baguye ba kera; ndetse n'abo bita imana za kera (si imana) bazashaka kubaka igihe cyabo cya zahabu mu isi, kandi barekuriwe urubanza, ariko nta gihe cya zahabu bazagira. Ibizira byabo bizarekurwa, kuko ukomakoma azaba yagiye kandi akuweho rwose, kandi nta kizababuza. Abakunda amagara yabo bazayabura iteka, kuko bazasabwa ikimenyetso kugira ngo bitabire umuryango wabo mushya, ndetse n'itegeko ryabo rishya. Kandi abazashyirwaho ikimenyetso cy'inyamaswa bose bazagira umugabane wabo mu kiyaga cy'umuriro.

Aba 144,000 banjye ntibatumwe kuba mu mijyi yabo, aho basabwa gufata ikimenyetso kugira ngo babeho. Ahubwo bazatumwa hirya no hino kandi bazakoranyiriza benshi aho bari kandi bite kuri benshi kandi bazafasha kuzana imbaga nyamwinshi mu gakiza aho ndi, Yeshua HaMashiach (mu rurimi urwo ari rwo rwose): abazambaza izina ryanjye bose kugeza ku iherezo bazakizwa. Imana izahanagura amarira yose ku maso yabo; kandi urupfu ntiruzabaho ukundi, cyangwa umuborogo, cyangwa kurira, cyangwa kubabara ntibizabaho ukundi: kuko ibya mbere bishize. Nk'uko byari mu minsi ya Nowa, abubaha Imana bazaba hanze y'umuryango wabo mubi w'abamarayika baguye, kuko uwabo uzaba ari umuryango w'icyaha uyobora mu kurimbuka kw'iteka mu miriro itazima; nuko ntimuzifatanye na bo kuko amahitamo ari ay'iteka. Kandi nuko aba 144,000 banjye bazazana agakiza n'ubufasha kuri benshi.

Bazakora ibimenyetso bikomeye, ibitangaza, n'imirimo itangaza, kandi nzabayobora intambwe ku yindi, kandi nzayobora inzira zabo ku bo mbatumaho. Kandi nzakoranyiriza intama zanjye aho bari kugira ngo ziyoborwe mu mutekano. Kandi uwo ari we wese udakunda amagara ye kugeza ku iherezo azakizwa. Kandi abanyabwenge bazaka nk'umucyo w'isanzure; n'abahindurira benshi ku gukiranuka bazaka nk'inyenyeri iteka ryose.

Inyamaswa yo mu nyanja izazamuka kandi izakorera imigambi mibi y'abategeka igihugu cy'isezerano mu cyaha gikomeye, ndetse isinagogi ya Satani. Izategeka amahanga kandi izavuga ibitutsi byinshi, kandi izakora ibimenyetso n'ibitangaza by'ibinyoma bikomeye, kandi baramije ikiyoka cyahaye inyamaswa ubushobozi, kandi baramije inyamaswa, bati, Ni nde uhwanye n'iyi nyamaswa? Ni nde ushobora kuyirwanya? Isinagogi ya Satani izakora ibishoboka byose kugira ngo irimbure umuntu wese wambaza izina ryanjye, kandi inyamaswa n'umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma bazashyira mu bikorwa amategeko ya Nowa ku mahanga ku ngufu kandi bazagaragara nk'aho bari kweza igihugu bakuramo ibibi. Ariko ahubwo bazarwanya abera babaneshe, kandi inyamaswa yo mu nyanja ihawe gukomeza amezi 42.

Inyamaswa yo mu isi na yo izazamuka ivuye mu ivu. Izaba ifite ubushobozi bwose bw'inyamaswa ya mbere, kandi izakora ibimenyetso n'ibitangaza by'ibinyoma byinshi imbere y'inyamaswa ya mbere, kandi izakora igishushanyo cy'inyamaswa, kandi umwuka uzemererwa gutuma igishushanyo cy'inyamaswa kivuga, kivuga ko umuntu wese utazapfukama ngo aramye igishushanyo cy'inyamaswa ya mbere agomba kwicwa.

Kuko inyamaswa yo mu isi bazayimika ho umwami w'ibihugu by'isezerano, kandi izaba Moshiach wabo bategereje igihe kirekire, kandi izubaka urusengero rwabo (nubwo Yahweh atayitumye), kandi uko ni ko bizaba. Bazayakira nk'umutegetsi mukuru, ugira neza, umuhanuzi, n'umwami. Kandi inyamaswa yo mu isi (ndetse n'umuhanuzi wabo w'ibinyoma) izatuma bose, abato n'abakuru, abakire n'abakene, abidegembya n'abagaragu, bashyirwaho ikimenyetso ku kuboko kwabo kw'iburyo cyangwa mu gahanga kabo, Kandi kugira ngo hatagira umuntu numwe ushobora kugura cyangwa kugurisha, keretse ufite icyo kimenyetso, cyangwa izina ry'inyamaswa, cyangwa umubare w'izina ryayo. Bateguye isi kwemera ikimenyetso, kandi uburyo bwatunganijwe mu gihe cy'icyo bise icyorezo cyabo.

(Byanditswe mu Byahishuwe ngo: Kandi marayika wa gatatu abakurikira, avuga ijwi rirenga ati, Umuntu wese naramya inyamaswa n'igishushanyo cyayo, agashyirwaho ikimenyetso cyayo mu gahanga ke, cyangwa ku kuboko kwe, Uwo na we azanywa ku nzoga y'umujinya w'Imana, isutswe idafunguye mu gikombe cy'umujinya wayo; kandi azababazwa n'umuriro n'amazuku imbere y'abamarayika bera, n'imbere y'Umwana w'Intama: Kandi umwotsi wo kubabazwa kwabo ucumba iteka ryose: kandi ntibaruhuka ku manywa na nijoro, abaramya inyamaswa n'igishushanyo cyayo, n'umuntu wese ushyirwaho ikimenyetso cy'izina ryayo.)

Mu mezi 42 ya mbere, nzaha ubushobozi abahamya banjye babiri, kandi bazahanura iminsi igihumbi na magana abiri na mirongo itandatu, bambaye ibigunira. Ibi ni ibiti by'iminzenze bibiri, n'ibitereko by'amatara bibiri bihagaze imbere y'Imana y'isi. Kandi niba hari umuntu ushaka kubagirira nabi, umuriro uva mu kanwa kabo, ukarya abanzi babo: kandi niba hari umuntu ushaka kubagirira nabi, agomba kwicwa atyo. Aba bafite ubushobozi bwo gukinga ijuru, kugira ngo imvura itagwa mu minsi y'ubuhanuzi bwabo: kandi bafite ubushobozi ku mazi bwo kuyahindura amaraso, no guteza isi ibyago byose, uko bashatse kose. Kandi nibamara gusohoza ubuhamya bwabo, inyamaswa izamuka iva mu kuzimu izabarwanya, ibaneshe, ibice.

Inyamaswa yo mu isi, ndetse n'umuhanuzi wabo w'ibinyoma, izana rwose ikizira kirimbura. Izatuma igitambo cya buri munsi gihagarara kandi ishyire ikizira kirimbura mu cyimbo cyacyo. Kandi nuko narabivuze kandi nuko Matayo yabyanditse: Nuko rero nimubona ikizira kirimbura, cyavuzwe na Daniyeli umuhanuzi, gihagaze ahera (usoma, abyumve), Icyo gihe abari i Yudaya bazahungire ku misozi: Uri hejuru y'inzu ntazamanuke ngo agire icyo akura mu nzu ye: Kandi uri mu murima ntazasubire inyuma ngo atware imyenda ye. Kandi ishyano rizabona abatwite, n'abonsa muri iyo minsi! Ariko musenge kugira ngo guhunga kwanyu kutazaba mu itumba, cyangwa ku isabato: Kuko icyo gihe hazabaho umubabaro mukomeye, utarigeze kubaho uhereye ku kuremwa kw'isi kugeza ubu, oya, kandi ntuzigera ubaho.

(Hahirwa abapfa bapfiriye mu Mwami uhereye none: Yego, ni ko Umwuka avuga, kugira ngo baruhuke imirimo yabo; kandi imirimo yabo irabakurikira.)

Kandi nuko nzarinda benshi kandi mpishe benshi mu butayu mbakiza umubabaro mukomeye, kuko iyo iyo minsi itagabanywa, nta muntu n'umwe wari kurokoka. Hanyuma inyamaswa n'umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma bazakoranya abami b'isi n'abari kumwe na bo bose bava mu bwami bw'ikiyoka kuri uwo munsi mukomeye w'intambara. Kandi nuko natumye Yohana Umubishuzi yandika: Kandi mbona ijuru rikinguye, dore ifarashi y'umweru; n'uyicayeho yitwa Uwizerwa n'Ukwuri, kandi aca imanza zitabera akarwana intambara mu gukiranuka. Amaso ye yari nk'ikirimi cy'umuriro, kandi ku mutwe we hari amakamba menshi; kandi yari afite izina ryanditswe, ritazwi n'umuntu numwe, keretse we ubwe. Kandi yari yambaye umwenda winitswe mu maraso: kandi izina rye yitwa Ijambo ry'Imana. Kandi ingabo zo mu ijuru zamukurikiye ku mafarashi y'umweru, zambaye imyenda y'igitare myiza, yera kandi itunganye. Kandi mu kanwa ke havamo inkota ityaye, kugira ngo ayikubite amahanga: kandi azayategeka n'inkoni y'icyuma: kandi asibanganya mu rwengero rw'umujinya w'inkazi w'Imana Ishoborabyose.

Kandi inyamaswa n'umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma bombi bazafatwa mpiri bajugunywe mu kiyaga cy'umuriro waka amazuku. Kandi abasigaye bishwe n'inkota y'uwicaye ku ifarashi, iyo nkota yavaga mu kanwa ke: kandi inyoni zose zihaga inyama zabo. Kandi mbona intebe z'ubwami, n'abazicayeho, bahabwa guca imanza: kandi mbona ubugingo bw'abaciwe imitwe kubera ubuhamya bwa Yesu, no kubera ijambo ry'Imana, n'abatararamije inyamaswa, cyangwa igishushanyo cyayo, kandi batashyizweho ikimenyetso cyayo mu gahanga kabo, cyangwa mu ntoki zabo; kandi barabayeho bima ingoma hamwe na Kristo imyaka igihumbi.

Kandi nuko marayika wanjye azamanuka ava mu ijuru, afite urufunguzo rw'ikuzimu n'umunyururu mukomeye azabohesha Satani ikuzimu imyaka igihumbi kandi ashyireho ikimenyetso kugira ngo atazongera kuyobya amahanga ukundi kugeza imyaka igihumbi ishize. Ku iherezo ry'ingoma yanjye y'imyaka igihumbi, Satani azabohurwa ave ikuzimu mu gihe gito.

Azasohoka ajye kuyobya amahanga ari mu mfuruka enye z'isi, Gogi na Magogi kugira ngo abakoranyirize hamwe bangana n'umusenyi wo ku nyanja, kandi azagaba igitero ku nkambi y'abera agote umudugudu wera, kandi umuriro uzamanuka uva kuri Yahweh ubatwike rwose. Kandi umwanzi wabayobeje azabohwa ajugunywe muzima muri cya kiyaga gikomeye cy'umuriro, cyaka amazuku mu birimi by'umuriro by'ubururu bishyushye cyane. Kandi hazabaho kwihorera gukomeye kw'ibibi kandi ubutabera bukomeye buzasohozwa ku mutwe wa Satani ubwe, abamarayika be baguye, n'abifatanyije mu kwigomeka kwabo bose; kandi ntibazongera kubyuka ukundi, kandi bazibagirana rwose; kandi ntibazongera kwibukwa ukundi.

Hanyuma Yahweh azarema ijuru rishya n'isi nshya, kandi azatura mu bantu, kandi mu mahoro n'ubwumvikane ni ko bizaba iteka ryose, kandi ntihazongera kubaho igihe cy'amakuba ukundi, kandi ntibazongera gukora icyaha ukundi, haba binyuze mu bwibone cyangwa kutubaha Imana, kandi iminsi yabo yose izaba ihiriwe.

Ndi Umunyanazareti wazutse wapfiriye ibyaha rimwe rizima kandi utazongera gupfa ukundi.

Ndi Yeshua, ndetse Mesiya wanyu w'ukuri, uwo Yahweh yatumye gusohoza amasezerano yahawe ba Data.

Ndi uwo amaso ye ari nk'ikirimi cy'umuriro, kandi ku mutwe wanjye hari amakamba menshi.

Ndi Umwami w'abami n'Umutware w'abatware, kandi mu gukiranuka nje guca imanza no kurwana intambara.

Ndi uwo uzayobora kandi agatwara aba 144,000 banjye, kandi bazaririmba indirimbo nshya nta muntu numwe ushobora kwiga.

Ndi uwo uzanesha ubwami bwa Satani, kandi nzamumena umutwe.

Ndi uwo Yahweh yatumye kuzana agakiza ku bizera bose, kandi igihe cyanjye kigeze. Dore! Urubanza ruraje kandi ibihe ni ubu.
