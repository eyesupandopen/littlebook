8 Ukwakira 2024

Nimuze Rwose ku Mwami Wanyu Uhoraho!

Nimwumve, nimwumve! Nimwumve ijwi ry'Umwami wanyu kandi mwumve amagambo ya Yeshua HaMashiach kandi mwunamishe imitima yanyu mu kumvira ubushake bw'Umwami wanyu kandi mureke amagambo yanjye ahumurize imitima yanyu. Rero reka mbabaze ikibazo? Ese murumva? Umwana w'Umuntu naza, mbese azasanga kwizera mu isi? Ni ukuri, ni ukuri, nzasanga kwizera mu bizerwa banjye nzajyana mu mutekano w'ijuru kubera ubudahemuka bwabo, ariko abenshi mu bari mu isi nta kwizera bagira. Kandi niyo mpamvu nkomeza kubaburira bakunzi banjye ngo muve mu byaha by'abanyabyaha, kandi muve mu nzira zabo mube abantu bitandukanyije nabo, kugira ngo mutazagabana ku bihano byabo.

Nyamara, niko bimeze, abantu benshi banyigiraho uburyarya bakoresheje iminwa yabo mu gihe imitima yabo iri kure yanjye. Kuri uwo munsi benshi bazambwira bati Mwami Mwami, nanjye nzababwira nti, nimumbemo sinigeze mbamenya! Nimwumve ko bidahagije kumbwira ngo ndi Umwami mu mutima utagira kwizera, ahubwo abangira Umwami by'ukuri ni abihana bakareka ibyaha byabo kandi bagashaka kubaho gukiranuka imbere yanjye. Mbese ntimuzi ko jye, Yesu w'i Nazareti, nahitamo umutima wanyu wose mu rukundo no kwitanga kuruta ibitambo by'iminwa yanyu? Yego, nshaka imbabazi kuruta ibitambo, kuko igice kimwe ari binyuze mu mbabazi zanjye, ukudatanga umujinya ukwiye, ko urukundo rwanjye rwiyerekana, ndetse n'urukundo rwa Yahweh Imana ikomeye ihoraho! Nyamara amagambo yanjye y'umuburo yumvwa n'abankunda kandi bakitabira mu kwihana ku bw'urukundo mbakunda nk'uko niyeretse kuri bo ku bw'agakiza kabo. Kandi niko bimeze, urukundo rwanjye rwakirwa mu bo imitima yabo yorohereye ijwi ryanjye kandi, mu by'ukuri, ku bankunda bose n'abo nigishije kunyigiraho ndetse n'abo nyobora mu nzira zanjye z'agakiza.

Kuki mureka imitima yanyu igahagarika, bana banjye bato? Mbese intumwa n'abahanuzi banjye ntibaburiye iby'uyu munsi kuva mu ntangiriro y'ibihe? Mbese abahanuzi ba kera ntibahanuye iby'icyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli kije mu rubanza rukomeye ku isi? Ku bw'ibyo, nimwumve, muba mu minsi ibanziriza amakuba ya Yakobo yisuka rwose ku isi kandi muri iyi minsi amakuba menshi, ibimenyetso, ibitangaza, n'imanza byarahanuwe kandi ubu byarekuwe, none kuki mutangara? Iyi ni imiburo ya nyuma ku rugero ruto y'ibizaza ku isi yose ubwo abizerwa banjye bazaba bakuwe mu isi basanga mu ijuru uko mbakoranya nk'inyana zo mu kiraro, kandi bazabona umutekano usesuye no kuzuzwa uko bahagaze mu bihe byabo bya mbere bavutse ubwa kabiri mu buzima bw'iteka ryose, aho batazarimbuka, cyangwa ngo bababare, cyangwa ngo barware, ahubwo bazagira ibyishimo byuzuye n'ubwumvikane bwuzuye na Yahweh, ndetse nanjye Yeshua HaMashiach kandi nzabaha ingororano ikomeye n'umurage, kuko mfite ingororano yabo mu kuboko kwanjye gukomeye.

Benshi mu bizerwa banjye bandujwe n'inyigisho z'ibinyoma z'ubutumwa bw'uburumbuke bubwirizwa mu buryo buyobya n'abahanuzi n'abigisha b'ibinyoma babeshya ubwoko bwanjye, bababwira ko amakuba atazigera abageraho igihe cyose batekereza ibyiza. Ayo mariba atagira amazi abwiriza undi Yesu kandi ntibamenye, Yeshua HaMashiach, kandi sinigeze mbatuma. Buri gihe mugereranye amagambo y'abavuga ko bamvugira n'ibyanditswe kandi aho bavuguruza ibyanditswe niho bavuga bava mu mitima yabo ku bw'inyungu zabo z'umururumba.

Ni ukuri, ni ukuri hari abakozi benshi b'ibinyoma babibye ibinyoma mu matorero yanjye kandi ubutumwa bw'ibinyoma bw'uburumbuke buyobora mu nzira ngari y'irimbukiro aho amarembo y'ikuzimu azakinguka cyane ku bakurikira iryo ryanja ry'umuriro ryaka iteka ryaka ibirimi bishyushye cyane by'ubururu bwa kobaliti byateguriwe abaguye n'ababakurikira, bityo ntimukagwe mu binyoma byabo! Sinvuga ku bahanuzi banjye bizerwa wenda bibeshya ijambo ku bwanjye, cyangwa abakora ikosa mu budahemuka bakikosora. Nimwumve ko nta n'umwe mu bahanuzi banjye uhanura byuzuye, ahubwo abahanuzi banjye bose bahanura igice kandi ntibazigera bavuguruza ibyanditswe iyo bamvugiye by'ukuri, Kristo Yesu, kuko ndi ijambo ry'Imana ryabaye umubiri ribana namwe, nubwo mu mubiri ntakimenywa.

Bana banjye bato ntimukabeho mushukwa kuko buri gihe muri iyi si habaho itotezwa n'umubabaro, cyane cyane ubu ku iherezo ry'ubwami bwa Satani uko abaguye baza mu rubanza n'uko bitegura gukora ibyo bemerewe gusohoza mu rubanza rwabo kuko n'abanzi ba Yahweh bumvira ijwi rye. Mbese ntimwasomye mu Baroma aho nigishije Pawulo mu nzira zanjye? Ni inde uzadutandukanya n'urukundo rwa Kristo? Mbese ni amakuba, cyangwa ibyago, cyangwa kurenganywa, cyangwa inzara, cyangwa kwambara ubusa, cyangwa ibyago, cyangwa inkota? (Nk'uko byanditswe ngo, Ku bwawe twicwa umunsi wose, twashyizwe mu rwego rw'intama zo kubagwa.) Oya, muri ibyo byose turushaho kunesha, ku bw'uwadukunze. Kuko menye neza yuko naho rwaba urupfu, cyangwa ubugingo, cyangwa abamarayika, cyangwa abategeka, cyangwa ubushobozi, cyangwa ibiriho, cyangwa ibizaza, cyangwa uburebure bw'igihagararo, cyangwa uburebure bw'ikuzimu, cyangwa ikindi cyaremwe cyose, bitazabasha kudutandukanya n'urukundo rw'Imana, ruri muri Kristo Yesu Umwami wacu.

Kandi bityo abatoranyijwe banjye bagiye bababazwa byinshi ku bw'izina ryanjye kandi habayeho umubare munini w'abamartiri mu gihe cyanjye cy'ubuntu batanze ubuzima bwabo mu gukorera mu guteza imbere ubutumwa bwiza bw'agakiza, ndetse n'ubutumwa bwiza bwakijije imitima yanyu binyuze mu kwizera Yesu w'i Nazareti kuko igice kimwe byari binyuze mu mirimo y'abamartiri banjye ubutumwa bwiza bw'agakiza bwakwiriye isi. Nyamara, benshi muri mwe mwandujwe n'umusemburo w'inyigisho z'ibinyoma zitandukanye zo kuvuga no kwiyitirira byakwiriye mu Itorero ryanjye rya Lawodikiya.

Izi nyigisho mbi si izanjye kandi ibitekerezo byanyu nta cyo bigeraho hanze yanyu keretse muvuze amagambo yanjye ku minwa yo kwizera igihe ntanga ibimenyetso, ibitangaza, n'imirimo itangaje ngo bikorwe n'amaboko yanyu. Bityo ntimushobora kwiyumvisha ko muvuye mu byago n'itotezwa, kandi abahamagawe, abatoranyijwe n'abizerwa banjye ntibasonewe amakuba. Ni he mu Ijambo ry'Imana havuga ko umwizera wese atazigera ababazwa n'amakuba, cyangwa ibyago, cyangwa kurenganywa, cyangwa inzara, cyangwa kwambara ubusa, cyangwa ibyago, cyangwa inkota? Ahubwo bimwe cyangwa ibi byose byijejwe ababaho mu budahemuka ku bwanjye bose, kandi bityo ndabagira inama yo kwamagana amagambo yabo kuko amagambo yabo ari imigani yahimbwe n'ubwenge yoherejwe n'abaguye kugira ngo bahirike kwizera kw'abatoranyijwe niba ibyo bishoboka. Icyakora, nimwumve ko abizerwa banjye bose bankurikira n'umutima wabo wose bakizwa umujinya w'Umwana w'Intama n'umujinya w'Imana kandi bazajyanwa mu mutekano mu cyumba cy'intebe y'ubwami cya Yahweh mbere y'uko urubanza rwisuka rwose ku isi.

Kandi bityo bana banjye benshi muri mwe muzahura n'amakuba atandukanye n'itotezwa bizanwa n'abaguye kandi benshi bazatakaza inyungu z'iyi si muri uku gusoza mbere y'uko nkoranya abizerwa banjye. Uko buri segonda rihita, kugaragara kwanjye kurushaho kwegereza. Bityo ndabatera inkunga, bakunzi banjye, kumpagararira no kugundira urukundo rwanjye nk'imbaraga ibasunikira kwihanganira ibikenewe kugira ngo muzanire agakiza abazimiye uko mubayobora mu Nkuge mbere y'uko amakuba atera.

Nimwumve icyo nahaye Pawulo ngo yandikire Abakolosayi: None nishimira imibabaro yanjye mbabazwa ku bwanyu, kandi nuzuza ibibura ku mibabaro ya Kristo mu mubiri wanjye ku bw'umubiri we, ari ryo torero, iryo nahinduriwe umukozi waryo nk'uko Imana yabitegetse, itegeko nahawe ku bwanyu, ryo gusohoza ijambo ry'Imana. Niko bimeze, bakunzi banjye, ko iyo mubabajwe ku bw'izina ryanjye, muba mukomeje kandi musangiye imibabaro yanjye nihanganiye i Kaluvari. Nubwo, ntasize na kimwe kidakozwe mu gakiza kanyu, kandi ndi Pasika yuzuye kandi itunganye y'ibihe byose, nahamagaye buri wese muri mwe kwihanganira byinshi ku bw'izina ryanjye, bityo mubibare nk'ibyishimo byose! Kandi mumenye bana banjye ko ijisho ritabonye, n'ugutwi kutumvise, ndetse bitigeze binjira mu mitima y'abantu ibyo Imana yateguriye abayikunda. Bityo nimwiruke, nimwiruke kugira ngo mubihabwe! Kuko Imana itakiranirwa, ngo yibagirwe umurimo wanyu, n'imirimo y'urukundo, mwerekanye ku bw'Izina ryayo, kuko mwakoreye Abera, kandi mukibakorera.

Benshi muri mwe mwayobejwe mu byo mwari mwiteze ku buryo mutakibona ko kenshi abahamagawe, abatoranyijwe, n'abizerwa banjye bihangangira ku bushake ingorane, gukozwa isoni, igihombo, n'ibindi byinshi cyane kandi iherezo ry'ukwihangana kwabo mu mibabaro ya Kristo ni agakiza ku bazimiye n'ingororano ikomeye n'igihembo ku bitanga muri ubwo buryo. Mbese ntimwasomye mu Byakozwe n'Intumwa aho Pawulo na Silasi bihanganiye ingorane nyinshi i Makedoniya, bakubitwa bikomeye kandi bagashyirwa mu nzu y'imbohe mu buryo burenganya bagashyirwa mu mbago i Filipi?

Nyamara nohereje umutingito kugira ngo mbakure mu minyururu yabo, bityo umurinzi w'inzu y'imbohe y'i Filipi n'abo mu rugo rwe babona agakiza binyuze mu kwihangana kwa Pawulo na Silasi. Kandi kuri benshi mu bankurikira, bazahura n'ingorane n'itotezwa n'amakuba. Ariko muri ibi byose murushaho kunesha kandi ndabarwanirira bana banjye, niba gusa mugumye kumvira ijwi ryanjye kandi mugakurikira ubushake bwanjye n'umutima wanyu wose kandi nzabaha kunesha byose niba gusa mugumye kuba abizerwa ku ijwi ryanjye kandi mukandeka nkayobora intambwe zanyu, kuko agakiza kanyu kari mu kumvira umukiza wanyu uhoraho.

Kandi bityo ndabagira inama, bakunzi banjye, yo kumpagararira no guha urukundo rwanjye isi igiye gupfa kandi iciriweho iteka ihagaze mu minyururu uko urubanza rugiye kwisuka rwose. Mbese muzanyemerera mbafashe kunesha gucika intege kugira ngo mugumane ibyiringiro byanyu mu bwenge? Mbese mwibagiwe amagambo yanjye, bakunzi banjye? Kandi noneho hagumaho kwizera, ibyiringiro n'urukundo, ariko ikiruta ibyo ni urukundo. Kandi bityo ndabatuma gukunda abazimiye, kuko ari kugira neza kw'Imana kuyobora abantu mu kwihana kenshi.

Ku bw'ibyo, nimunkorere mu miryango yanyu, musengere abatakaje byose kuko mu gihe bagihumeka bishoboka ko gutakaza iyi si bizatuma bunguka iteka ryose. Mubwirize abanyabyaha kandi muzanire agakiza abazimiye kandi mwibuke ko muri ba ambasaderi banjye bakunzi banjye kandi musohoze umuhamagaro wanyu muri jye kandi mugume mushikamye muri uku gusoza kw'ibihe. Bityo ndabatuma gukunda abaturanyi banyu no guha ibyo kurya n'ubufasha ababikeneye. Ndetse n'igikombe cy'amazi cyangwa isahani y'ibiryo, mutange icyo mushobora cyose gufasha abababara mudashidikanya na gato. Kandi niba bibayeho ko mubabarana n'abazimiye noneho mubakunde rwose kandi mwerekane ubuhamya bwanjye n'imbaraga kandi mumvugire ku minwa yo kwizera biva mu mutima wuzuye kwizera, ibyiringiro, n'urukundo!

Kandi bityo ndababaza, mbese mutinya gufasha abanyabyaha? Mbese ntimwumva ko ubwo natumwaga gusa ku ntama zazimiye z'inzu ya Isirayeli namaranye igihe cyanjye n'abakoresha b'ikoro n'abanyabyaha? Mbese mwibagiwe ko namwe mwigeze kubaho mu buzima bw'ibyaha by'akarande? Mbese mwibagiwe ko ari jye muntu wenyine utagira icyaha ndetse n'umwana w'intama w'Imana? Bityo abacunguwe b'Uwiteka nibabivuge abo nacunguye mbakuye mu kuboko kw'umwanzi. Nimukunde, mukunde, mukunde kugeza ku mwuka wanyu wa nyuma kuko byiza gupfa mukorera mu rukundo rwa Kristo kuruta kwikanya mu bwoba ku mihati idafite ishingiro y'ibintu bya kamere bidakomeye kandi bikennye, ibyo nanesheje rwose ngaha intsinzi abangira Umwami wabo uhoraho bose. Bityo ntimukunde ubuzima bwanyu kugeza ku iherezo kandi mukurikire urugero rwanjye rwo gukorera mudatinya imbere y'ikibi n'urupfu byaneshejwe rwose n'urukundo ruhoraho rw'Imana ikomeye ihoraho, ndetse El Shaddai!

Ntimutinye, kuko ndi kumwe namwe: ntimugire ubwoba, kuko ndi Imana yanyu: nzabakomeza, kandi mbafashe, kandi nzabashyigikira n'ukuboko kw'iburyo kw'o gukiranuka kwanjye. Bityo nimuze kuri jye bana banjye kandi muruhukire mu rukundo mbakunda kandi nzahumuriza imitima yanyu kandi mkize ibikomere byanyu kandi nzabakomeza mu kumeneka kwanyu kugira ngo mubone icyo guha ababakeneye kuko ari ko mwatumwe. Kuko ari mwe ba ambasaderi banjye batatoranyijwe gusa ngo bahamagarirwe ubwiza bw'Ubwami bwanjye, ahubwo no kubabazwa ku bw'izina ryanjye.

Ni ukuri, ni ukuri, muri abafatanyabikorwa mu mibabaro yanjye, mukurikire gusa urugero rwanjye kandi ntimucike intege mu isaha yanyu yo kwiheba. Kuko ndi kumwe namwe muri buri ntambwe y'inzira, kandi ni jye, Umwami Yesu Kristo weza imitima yanyu mu muriro wanjye utunganya, nubwo muri inzabya z'ibumba gusa mu maboko y'umubumbyi, imiriro yeza izatuma mu maso hanyu hakomera nk'urutare kugira ngo mudashobora kuvunika mu mbaraga zanjye nyamara mukiri mu ntege nke z'umubiri wanyu. Nyamara, niko bimeze ko mu ntege nke z'umubiri w'icyaha ariho urukundo rwanjye rutunganirizwa muri mwe uko mbigereza hafi yanjye kandi nkabategurira kurushaho imigambi yanyu y'iteka. Ku bw'ibyo, ni ikintu gikwiye ko abacunguwe bababazwa ku bw'izina ryanjye kuko natanze ubugingo bwanjye bwose mu gitambo kibabaza kugira ngo mubone kubaho imbere ya Yahweh, ndetse Imana ishobora byose ikomeye. Nimwumve ko mu bigeragezo byanyu n'imibabaro yanyu mutari kuzanira gusa ubuntu bwanjye bukiza ababukeneye ahubwo murimo no gusohoza agakiza kanyu bwite mutinya kandi muhinda umushyitsi kugira ngo mbageze ku ngororano yanyu yuzuye kandi bityo ibyiringiro byanyu bigiye gusohozwa!

Ahari mwumvise iby'ukwihangana kwa Yobu? Uko Yobu yari umugabo wizerwa wageze mu bihe bikomeye kandi akagabwaho igitero na Satani mu buryo burenganya. Ahari mwumvise iherezo rya Yobu kuko Yobu atari umukiranirwa waneshejwe n'ikibi, ahubwo yari umukiranutsi wabonye imbabazi waneshesheje ikibi icyiza binyuze mu gukurikiza amagambo ya Yehova no kuguma mu gukiranuka ndetse n'imbere y'itotezwa ribi. Nuko Yehova asubiza Yobu ibikubye kabiri by'ibyo yari yanyazwe byose mu buryo burenganya, kandi Yehova yitura Yobu ku bw'amakuba ye n'itotezwa. Dore, twita abahirwe abihangana. Mwumvise iby'ukwihangana kwa Yobu, kandi mumenye iherezo Umwami Imana yamuhaye. Kuko Umwami Imana ari inyambabazi cyane n'impuwe.

Nuko rero haguma kwizera, ibyiringiro, n'urukundo, ariko ikiruta byose ni urukundo. Bityo rero, bakundwa banjye, mumenye ko ku bizerwa ibyiringiro bigiye gusohora byuzuye ubwo muzashimutirwa mu mutekano w'ijuru mukurwa mu manza ziri kuza. Nimwumve ijwi ryanjye kandi mwumve urukundo mbakunda mwa bahamagawe banjye, abizerwa, n'abatoranyijwe, ndetse n'umubiri wanjye n'umugeni wanjye! Nuko rero, muruhukire mu rukundo rwanjye kuko ndetse no mu makuba akomeye cyane urukundo rwanjye ruguma rukomeye kandi ntiruneshwa kuko ndi umwe mu bumwe n'umugambi na Data, kandi ni urukundo rw'iteka rwa Yehova mpaha abazaragwa ubugingo buhoraho bose n'abaza kuri Yesu w'i Nazareti bose gushaka agakiza.

Mumenye ko nta muntu n'umwe ushobora kuza kuri Yehova keretse anyuze kuri njye, Kristo Yesu. Kandi nuko rero urukundo rwa Yehova ntiruneshwa kuko Yehova adashobora kuneshwa, bityo nanjye sinneshwa, kuko nanesheje. Kandi ndi Umwami w'agakiza uhoraho, ndetse n'uwo Yehova yagize Umwami w'abami n'Umutware w'abatware, kandi yampaye izina riruta amazina yose kugira ngo mu izina rya Yesu Kristo amavi yose apfukame mu ijuru no mu isi no munsi y'isi. Mumenye ko abaguye babatinya bakundwa banjye kandi ubwoba bwinshi bufata imitima yabo ku bw'urubanza rwabo rwegereje rutazatinda.

Ndabategeka kandi ndabatera inkunga, bana banjye, ngo mujyane urukundo rwanjye muri iyi si irwaye kandi igiye gupfa n'iciriweho iteka muri iki gihe cya nyuma mbere y'uko amakuba agwa byuzuye. Ndababwira nti mukize abarwayi, muzure abapfuye, mukize ababembe, mwirukane abadayimoni kuko unyizera imirimo nakoze ku isi na we azayikora ndetse n'imirimo isumba iyo kuko ngiye kwa Data. Mugende mu rukundo rwinshi no kwicisha bugufi, kandi muhure n'abafite ibibazo mu mbaraga za Kristo nzaha imbaraga zikomeye muri mwe. Kuko ari muri mwe, nzabya zanjye z'ibumba, imbaraga z'ubwiza bw'Imana muri Kristo muri mwe ziba. Muvugishe ibibazo muhura na byo mu kwizera. Mugire kwizera muri Yehova, no kwizera muri Yeshua HaMashiach kandi mubwire uwo musozi muti KUKA WIKUBITE MU NYANJA! Kandi uko mubikora mu kwizera, nzaha ibimenyetso byinshi, ibitangaza n'imirimo ikomeye gukorwa n'amaboko y'abizerwa.

Mugire ubutwari kandi mukomere, kuko ndi Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda ubarwanirira. Ndabinginga kandi ndabahata ku bw'urukundo rw'Imana ruri muri Kristo Yesu Umwami wanyu kwihangana mubikunze ku bw'izina ryanjye no kugarura abazimiye mu rugo kwa Data ndetse no muri iki gihe cya nyuma. Ndabinginga batoranyijwe banjye mutange urugero kuko muri abizerwa banjye n'abatoranyijwe naremeye mu makuba kugira ngo mu ntege nke no kumeneka k'umubiri wanyu mushobore kugaragaza imbaraga z'Imana ikomeye ihoraho, ndetse na Yehova ubwe uko mugenda mu murimo n'ubutware bukomeye ku bo mbohereje gukorera.

Ndi kumwe namwe muri buri ntambwe yose.

Ndi umwe mu bumwe n'umugambi na Data.

Ndi Umwami w'agakiza uhoraho.

Ndi uwo Yehova yagize Umwami w'abami n'Umutware w'abatware.

Ndi Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda ubarwanirira.

Njye, Yeshua HaMashiach, nohereje aya magambo binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye, kandi binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye amagambo yanjye yoherejwe ku bw'ukumvira no gutera inkunga abazabona aya magambo bose. Kandi Amagambo yanjye azabona amaso, amatwi, n'imitima by'abo yohererejwe. Muhagarare mushikamye bakundwa banjye kuko ingororano yanyu iri ku muryango wanyu, gusa ntimucike intege. Muruhukire mu rukundo rwanjye kandi muruhukire mu mirimo yanjye, mumaze kureka iyanyu bwite kandi none ni cyo gihe cy'incungu yanyu kandi none ni cyo gihe cy'agakiza kanyu kuzuye, kandi ndaje kubatwara bakundwa banjye. Nimunyumve kandi mwumve amagambo yanjye, sinzabasiga cyangwa ngo mbatatererane bizerwa banjye muri Kristo, ndetse n'abumvira ijwi ryanjye bose. Murebe mu kirere, kuko kuboneka kwanjye kugiye gutunguka mu kirere mu gihe n'isaha mudatekereza. Ndaje kubatwara bizerwa banjye, mwiteguye?
