Ndi uwo n'icyo Umwami yangize, kandi ndi Biblaridion ye:144, agatabo ke, ndetse n'intumwa ivugwa n'ubutumwa burura kandi buryohereye kandi mu ntoki zanjye hari agatabo gafunguye Sela.
