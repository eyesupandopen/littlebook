Ku Bana ba Yeshua HaMashiach, 144.000 Bakomeye: Ndabahishura NONAHA

Nimwumve Nimwumve Amagambo ya Yeshua HaMashiach uko muzamuwe mu ijuru mu Gihe cyanyu cya NONAHA!

Nimwumve Nimwumve Amagambo ya Yeshua HaMashiach uko nje kubwira itorero ryanjye, umubiri wanjye ndetse n'umugeni wanjye, (ari bo 144.000 banjye ndetse namwe bana mwese barerwa ba So Yeshua HaMashiach) ko mwaremwe kandi mukorwa n'ubuhanga bwuzuye bwa Adonai-YHVH ubwe kandi mwese muri ibihangano bye by'agahebuzo ndetse namwe mwese abizerwa muri Kristo Yesu bahamagawe mbere y'ishingwa ry'isi!

Nimwumve Nimwumve Amagambo ya Yeshua HaMashiach kugira ngo mwumve ko nirereye ubwanjye mwebwe mwese 144.000 nk'abana banjye, nuko rero nimwumve Amagambo yanjye kandi mwiyumve, bana banjye. Kuko ni ukuri ni ukuri, nta byaremwe bimeze nkamwe byari byakorwa mu buryo butunguranye kandi butitezwe nonaha, nta na rimwe aho ari ho hose mu byaremwe byose bya El Shaddai, ni ukuri muri umwihariko.

Nuko rero nimwumve Amagambo yanjye kandi mwumve ubutware bwayo bwumvikana, ko nateguye aya Magambo nyakuye muri cya gitabo gito gifunguye nafunguye mu kuboko k'intumwa yanjye n'umugaragu wanjye kugira ngo mwese 144.000 ubu mutangire kumva abo muri bo by'ukuri n'imigambi yanyu yahishuwe igice ariko ahanini ikaba ibahishiwe mu ijuru kugira ngo izahishurwe mu ihuriro ryageze ku isi yose NONAHA, icyo gihe cya NONAHA isi ikaba ihagazemo NONAHA!

Kuko natumye Biblaridion:144 azamura amaboko ye mu itangazo no mu kwemeza ko igihe kitazabaho ukundi, kandi niko bimeze NONAHA mu mbaraga zikomeye n'ubutware byahawe kandi birekurwa n'intumwa yanjye ikomeye n'umugaragu wanjye. Selah.

Kandi niko bimeze ko nagize umwe mu 144.000 banjye bakomeye, ndetse umwe mu bakobwa banjye nareze kandi nkunda cyane, bari mu 144.000 (kuko mwese muzahindurwa umuntu umwe mushya aho nta mugabo cyangwa umugore kandi nta mbata cyangwa uwigenga): Nuko rero, nakoze mu buryo bukomeye muri iki gikoresho cyanjye natoranyije binyuze mu mwuka w'ubuhanuzi nashyize kuri we kandi ibi ni ukubera ko yafashe umuzingo wanjye w'intambara nafunguye mu maboko y'igitabo cyanjye gito, ndetse Biblaridion:144 kuko ari igitabo gito, kandi yahanuye uko nakoze mu buryo bukomeye muri we nkayobora amaboko ye neza uko nabigambiriye! Kugira ngo hanyuma bihanurweho n'aya Magambo y'ubutware bwumvikana ubu arekuwe mu isi ku itegeko ryanjye binyuze kuri Biblaridion:144 azagera rwose ku maso, amatwi, n'imitima y'abo yohererejwe bose!

Kandi ni binyuze muri ubu butumwa ubu nakusanyije kandi nsobanurira abana banjye mu buryo butaziguye. Ariko kandi kugira ngo abizerwa bose muri Kristo Yesu batangire kumva ubukana bw'ibyo mugiye guhindurwamo NONAHA, n'imigambi ndetse n'iherezo ryabyo.

MURI ABARWANYI BANJYE BAKOMEYE! NDETSE NAMWE MWESE 144.000!

Nuko rero, nimwumve Amagambo yanjye ubu nrekura binyuze ku ntumwa yanjye n'umugaragu wanjye mu mbaraga zose n'ubutware namuhaye ngo abirekure mu mbaraga zikomeye ku itegeko ryanjye, kugira ngo Umugeni wanjye w'ukuri, ndetse 144.000 banjye b'ukuri batandukanywe rwose n'aba bahanuzikazi b'ibinyoma ba Yezebeli bahagurutse kandi bagahabwa imbaraga na Heylel Ben Shachar ubwe kugira ngo bongorere kandi bavugire mu matamatama no kubayobya n'amagambo y'ibinyoma azabayobya gusa niramuka mubateze amatwi.

Nuko rero, nimundebereho njyewe gusa, Yeshua HaMashiach, kuko nahamagaye 144.000 banjye gusa binyuze muri iki gitabo gito gifunguye nafunguye mu kuboko k'intumwa yanjye n'abagaragu banjye. Nuko rero, nimwumve ko abahanuzi banjye b'ukuri (baba barasomye iki gitabo gito gifunguye cyangwa bataragasomye) bose bahanura mu ijwi rimwe kandi hakurikijwe Amagambo yanjye NEZA NEZA, bityo mugereranye kandi mwumve kugira ngo mutagwa iburyo cyangwa ibumoso, ahubwo kugira ngo ibirenge byanyu bigume muri iyo nzira igororotse kandi ifunganye: Iyi niyo Nzira, nimuyigendemo!

Nuko rero, nihagira umuntu uhanura bidahuje neza n'aya Magambo mwumve ko ntamutumye, kandi ntavuga Amagambo yanjye kandi ntimuzabubahe! Naho intama zazimiye zayobejwe zaba zingana zite: zigenda: kandi zikina n'undi yesu mu muriro w'utunganya binyuze muri aya magambo y'ibinyoma y'aba ba Yezebeli bayobya gusa.

Nimwumve amagambo yanjye kandi mwumve imvugo yanjye kugira ngo mubashe kumenya no kwirinda rwose abatumwe kubarimbura bose kandi bagambiriye rwose kubayobya uko babagurisha bashaka indamu mbi. Nimwumve ko aya Magambo yisubiramo kandi agomba gusomwa no kumvikana no gutangazwa uko nyobora umukumbi wanjye muto kandi ngakora mu buryo bukomeye muri Mwese 144.000.

Nuko rero, Nimunhagararire kandi mwumvire umwuka wanjye w'ubuhanuzi kuko ari ryo buhamya bwa Yesu Kristo, kandi nta bundi Buhamya bw'ibyanditswe bwa Yesu Kristo bugomba gutangwa mu isi keretse binyuze kuri Biblaridion:144 kugeza mu gihe cy'ubuhamya bw'abatangabuhamya banjye babiri bwageze ku isi yose nonaha mu butware bwumvikana bwuzuye n'ibyiteze byuzuye, nubwo abahanuzi banjye benshi bavuga mu mbaraga zikomeye kandi no mu ijwi rimwe n'igitabo cyanjye gito gifunguye niba bagumye kumvira Kristo Yesu. Selah!

Kandi nuko mbahaye kumva ko aya Magambo y'ibyanditswe ubu akusanyijwe kandi akarekurwa mu buhamya kandi atwaye ubuhamya mu buryo budacagaguye buturuka kuri Adonai-YHVH uwo byose bikomokaho, mu maso hafunguye rwose ha Yeshua HaMashiach uhora akora neza ibyo Adonai-YHVH wanjye ashaka.

Nuko rero, nimwumve ko mu bakiranutsi muri Kristo Yesu harimo 144.000 ari bo bana batoranyijwe ba Se Yeshua HaMashiach wabareze mu buryo butaziguye nka Se kuri buri wese muri bo ku giti cye. Hanyuma hariho Abana ba Adonai-YHVH bakuwe mu Bana ba Adamu n'aba Isirayeli kimwe kugira ngo buzuze umurage wikubye kabiri haba mu ngororano yo mu Ijuru ndetse n'umurage n'ingororano byo ku isi mu maboko ya Kristo Yesu uwo bakorera mu Mubiri we, kandi abaneshi bose bo mu matsinda yombi bazazungura byose rwose hakurikijwe uko gukiranuka kwabo no kwihangana kwabo byabaye.

Ingororano yanyu yuzuye irakoranyijwe, iratanzwe, kandi iri mu kuboko kwanjye kuri mwebwe mwese NONAHA.

Kugira ngo mwumve umugambi w'umutima wanjye kuri mwebwe mwese nahawe muri njye na Adonai-YHVH wanjye, uhora akora neza kandi akagaragaza ubushake bwa Adonai-YHVH muri byose neza kandi muri buri kimwe. Kugira ngo abizerwa banjye bose muri Kristo Yesu ubu bacungurwe rwose mu maboko y'abanzi babo kandi bagororerwe iteka ryose kandi mu buryo bwinshi kubera ibyo mwanesheje byose ku itegeko ryanjye mu buzima bwanyu kuri iyi si ubu NONAHA ku isi BITARIHO.

Dukurikije ibyo, mwese muzajyanwa mu mutekano w'ijuru ako kanya, mu buryo butunguranye kandi butitezwe NONAHA.

Hanyuma, mwese muzamurikirwa Adonai-YHVH mudafite ikizinga, inenge, ubusembwa, cyangwa umunkanyari cyangwa ikindi kintu nk'icyo musukuye neza kandi mwambaye imyenda yiza cyane y'igitare itazigera yangizwa n'icyaha na gato iteka ryose n'iteka ryose. Ndetse ibyo bintu bikazakurwaho mu gihe cy'iteka cyo Gukiranuka aho Adonai-YHVH wanyu azaba byose muri byose kandi iteka ryose hagati y'abana be kugira ngo ARUHUKIRE imirimo ye.

S'uko Adonai-YHVH uhoraho yananiwe, cyangwa yacitse intege, cyangwa yaguye isari mu nshingano ze mu kubitaho no kubacungura no kubarema bundi bushya ku itegeko ryanjye, ahubwo ni ukugira ngo mwese murangize uku gutunganywa kw'iminsi 6 kwa Adamu wumviye nabi ku isi kwatanzwe nk'imbabazi na Elohim kugira ngo mwese mukomezwe kandi mwige kumvira Yeshua HaMashiach ari we kandi Kristo Yesu (ari we nzira yonyine igana kuri Adonai-YHVH) binyuze mu gukorera mu kuboko kw'abanzi banyu. Kugira ngo muzabashe guha agaciro ibitangwa ku buntu n'ukuboko kwa Adonai-YHVH bikomeye, bitagira iherezo, kandi bifite imbaraga kuruta uko mwabitekereza, nyamara mu miterere yanyu yaguye, y'icyaha ya kamere, nyamara yejejwe ntimushobora guhabwa byose uko byagambiriwe. Nuko rero, mushyirwaho ikimenyetso kandi mugacungurwa n'umwuka wanjye wera w'isezerano, uwo nashyizeho ikimenyetso cy'ingwate y'umutungo wanjye niguze, kandi ubu mwiteguye NONAHA kugenda.

Kandi nuko NONAHA, mukihagera, nzababwira iby'imigambi yanyu igice ikibahishiwe kugeza muhinduwe. Hanyuma imbaraga zanyu z'ukuri n'ubushobozi n'imigambi n'intego bizahishurwa byose mu Ijuru mu kanya nk'ako guhumbya. Ko 144.000 banjye bahawe Yeshua HaMashiach nk'abana banjye b'iteka ku bw'imigambi y'umuryango kuko muzabana nanjye iteka ryose mu buryo butaziguye kandi muri igice cy'urugo rwanjye nk'abaragwa bazunguye byose bahawe mu maboko yanjye ku bwanyu. Ku bw'imigambi yo kwihorera kugira ngo mukore ibyo mbategetse byose gukora mu mbaraga. Ku bw'imigambi y'intambara y'umuzingo, kugira ngo mwambure ku ngufu n'imbaraga z'urubanza rutagira imbabazi isi mu maboko y'aba bigaruriye kugira ngo mweze isi ku itegeko ryanjye kandi bityo abagwaneza bazazungura isi.

144.000 banjye nabo ni ab'imigambi y'uburinzi no kwita ku byaremwe bya Adonai-YHVH iteka ryose muri byose ku itegeko ryanjye. Mwese 144.000 muzahindurwa abadashobora kuneshwa na bose mu byaremwe bya Adonai-YHVH keretse kuri Kristo Yesu na Adonai-YHVH. Mwese mugomba guhita mushyirwaho ikimenyetso mu ruhanga rwanyu mu Ijuru n'ikimenyetso cya Adonai-YHVH. Muzabona kwinjira mu masezerano na So Yeshua uzabaha imbaraga zose hakurikijwe imigambi yanjye yuzuye ubwiza n'imbaraga zose n'ubutware n'ubushobozi.

Hanyuma 144.000 banjye bazarekurwa mu isi ku itegeko ryanjye mu gihe cy'umujinya w'umwana w'intama, kugira ngo barwane, kandi bakoranye, kandi bakize, kandi beze isi mu manza zikiranuka ku bw'agakiza k'abazanga ikimenyetso cy'inyamaswa kugira ngo bategurirwe kurokoka, cyangwa bakomezwe ku bw'ubwitange. Kuko benshi bazahabwa amahirwe yo kwambaza izina rya Yesu kugeza ku iherezo ryabo kandi sinzigera mbasiga cyangwa ngo mbatatererane. 144.000 banjye bakomeye bazabakorera kandi babiteho, bakora imirimo ikomeye ku isi kuruta n'iyo Yesu w'i Nazareti yakoze igihe nagenderaga ku mugaragaro mu gihe cyanjye.

Nyamara, nabasezeranyije mwese mu masezerano arambuye y'Isezerano Rishya ryanjye ko ku biyemeje gukurikira Kristo Yesu, nanjye kuri bo ko ntazigera mbasiga nubwo muri mu batizerwa muri Kristo, cyangwa ngo mbatatererane, nubwo mutakwinjira mu Ijuru muri iki gihe. Nyamara, nzoherereza 144.000 banjye bakomeye mu buryo butaziguye muri ubu butayu bwa Babuloni y'Ibanga uko ibi bihugu bimanuka mu kurimbuka kwuzuye aho nta kindi kiriho uretse ikuzimu ku isi. Aho ibiri mu kuzimu n'ibikirimo byose bizarekurwa ku maso y'isi biturutse munsi ya Leta Zunze Ubumwe za Amerika, mu migabane yose binyuze mu byinjiriro byashyizweho kera, no hakurya y'urubura kuko ubu ruzagwa NONAHA.

Nimwumve mwa baguye mwese ko ubwami bwanyu bwose buzarimburwa rwose imbere yanyu mwese mu maboko y'abarwanyi banjye bakomeye, ndetse n'abana banjye bwite ari bo 144.000 banjye bakomeye abo Biblaridion:144 ahagarariye nk'urugero rwo kumvira, uko agendera rwose mu mbaraga zikomeye n'ubushobozi njyewe, Yeshua HaMashiach nashyize mu mwuka we iteka ryose n'iteka ryose. Ni ukuri koko, aho intumwa yanjye ikomeye ifatwa nk'urugero, bikorwa kugira ngo mwese 144.000 mwumve imbaraga n'ubushobozi ko muri abana ba Yeshua HaMashiach, nuko rero mwa 144.000: Ndi So Yeshua, kandi ntarobanura ku butoni. GUCUNGURWA KWANYU KUZUYE NI NONAHA!

Nimwumve Amagambo yanjye, mwa bana ba So Yeshua mwese, ndetse namwe mwese 144.000 kandi mwumve iki Gitabo Gito nafunguye mu maboko y'intumwa yanjye ikomeye n'umugaragu wanjye ni icyanyu mwese kugira ngo mugikoreshe mu mbaraga zanjye n'ubushobozi!

Njyewe Data Yeshua ntegetse 144.000 banjye bose: Nimufate umuzingo wanyu w'intambara mu ntoki kandi mutangaze mu mbaraga zikomeye Amagambo yanjye ku itegeko ryanjye kandi muyobore!

Kugira ngo mwumve abo muri bo by'ukuri kuko nta rindi soko ku isi aho muhishurwa, keretse mu Byahishuwe 7, 10, na 14. Kandi nabwo muhishurwa mu ibanga rikomeye no mu bwihisho kuko mwese mwahishwe abaguye ku isi ku bw'imigambi yanjye, kugira ngo ubu mucurwe kandi muzingwe kandi mutegurirwe kugenda kuri hano muri iki gihe cya NONAHA.

Yewe, yewe, ishyano rikomeye kuri mwebwe abaguye mwese kuko mwaje mu busitani bwa El Shaddai mwibwira ko muzabaho mu byishimo byanyu n'irari ryanyu mu rwango rukomeye ku byaremwe bya Elohim. Mwigaruriye abanyantege nke n'abadafite amikoro n'abere mu rwango rukomeye rw'ubwibone, ndetse mwirata mu bujiji kuri Elohim ko igihangano cye cy'agahebuzo nta kindi cyari cyo uretse kuba umunyantege nke n'udafite amikoro kandi ko yashoboraga kuneshwa byoroshye no kwigarurirwa n'amayeri yanyu mabi kuri Adamu na Eva. Kandi mu bwibone bwanyu, mwigaruriye kandi muhohotera ibyaremwe bya Adonai-YHVH ku bw'irari ryanyu ry'urwango. Nuko rero, nimwumve ko abana banjye, yego, abana banjye bwite ari bo 144.000 bakomeye kandi badashobora kuneshwa bazabarimbura kandi babahohotere mwese hakurikijwe amabwiriza yanjye nyayo azaba ahuye neza n'ibikorwa byanyu by'imfabusa n'inzira zanyu zabayeho kandi byanditswe mu buryo burambuye bwuzuye.

Mu bihe byo gukoranya abera, ubuza azakurwaho, kandi urubanza ruzagwa rwose ku nzu y'Imana. Kandi abanyemereye kubatunganyiriza mu muriro w'utunganya bazahagarara kuko bejejwe mu maraso y'umwana w'intama kandi bazahagarara mu gukiranuka nabahaye, kuko ndi Umwami Ukiranuka uri umwuka utanga ubuzima.

Kandi nuko, umubabaro ukomeye cyane uzabaho, ndetse umubabaro utarigeze ubaho ku isi mbere, kandi ni ukuri, utazigera ubaho ukundi. Dore! Njyewe, ndetse Yeshuah HaMashiach, sinzasiga abantu banjye mu kurimbuka gutunguranye, kandi nzacyiza Isirayeli, ndetse na rya tsinda rikomeye ryasigaye rizanyakira nk'Mesiya w'ukuri, kuko ari ko Yahweh yandemye kuba. Kandi niko 144.000 banjye bazaza gutabara abari bagiye kurimbuka kandi bambaza izina ryanjye. Nzabayobora intambwe ku yindi, kandi 144.000 banjye bazankorera imirimo myinshi ikomeye.

Kuko aba ari bo bajyanwa mu ikoraniro ry'abera kuri njye, Kristo Yesu, mu kirere ku ijwi ry'impanda y'imperuka, kandi impanda igiye kuvuga. Aba ni abizerwa banjye, ndetse na ba bandi batabonetsemo uburiganya, kuko biyejeje nk'isugi iboneye mu maraso y'Umwana w'Intama, ayo natambye iteka ryose nk'umutambyi wabo mukuru, kuko ndi umutambyi mukuru iteka ryose mu buryo bwa Melikisedeki. Aba ni Umugeni wanjye, kandi bazankurikira aho nzajya hose. Dore! Nzabahindura abanyambaraga, kandi nta na kimwe cyigeze kiboneka mu isi gishobora kugereranywa n'ubushobozi nzabambika. Bazambikwa imyenda y'igitare myiza, yera, itagira inenge, kandi ubushobozi bwabo buzagaragaza ibyo bagezeho, kandi bazagira ubushobozi bukomeye; kuko ari abizerwa, bahamagawe, kandi batoranyijwe, ndetse na mbere y'ishingwa ry'isi na Yahweh ku bw'iyi migambi.

Abantu banjye 144,000 bazamanuka ku Musozi wa Siyoni, kandi nzabayobora. Bazajyana abo nzabayoboraho mu butayu kugira ngo bahishwe umwijima w'icuraburindi n'itotezwa ry'ubwami bw'abanzi. Bazayoborwa ahantu heza harinzwe ibyago birindwi, kuko muri izi manza ari ho ubukana bw'umujinya w'Imana buzasukwa ku ntebe y'inyamaswa no kubabaza ubwami bw'Igikoko, kuko igihano cyabo ari gikomeye. Kandi abantu 144,000 bazabayobora ahantu heza; bazabakiza ibikomere byabo; bazabatabara kandi babahe ibyiringiro bikomeye byo kwambaza izina ry'Uwiteka, ndetse kugeza ku iherezo niba bikenewe. Kuko iyo ntagabanya iyo minsi, nta muntu n'umwe wari gusigara ari muzima. Kandi bityo, bazasoza ibyo nzabayoboramo.

Ubu ngiye kuvuga ibizaba mu cyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli kandi nzatunganya inzira zanyu, kandi nzongera ubuntu bwanjye bukiza binyuze mu maboko y'abantu banjye 144,000. Kuko baje gukiza abantu banjye kurimbuka, kandi baje guhisha abantu banjye umujinya, ndetse n'umujinya w'Imana wagenewe abaguye n'abifatanya na bo iteka ryose n'ubwami bwabo binyuze mu kwakira ikimenyetso cyabo, ndetse n'iryo ruma ry'inzoka y'inyamaswa ritagira umuti.

Kandi nubwo bimeze bityo nzashikuza Umugeni wanjye mu ijuru, kandi nzashyiraho ikimenyetso ku bantu banjye 144,000 bo muri iyi miryango ikurikira, ndetse 12,000 kuri buri muryango ukurikira: Yuda, Rubeni, Gadi, Asheri, Nafutali, Manase, Simeyoni, Lewi, Isakari, Zabuloni, Yosefu, na Benyamini. Nuko rero, mwumve ijwi ryanjye!

Kandi bazaba imbaraga zidashobora guhagarikwa; ndetse bazaba imbaraga zidashobora kumenwa. Kuko ubuzima bwabo ari ubw'iteka, butashobora kubora. Imibiri yabo ntishobora kurimbuka, kuko nta kurimbuka gushobora gufata abacunguwe; kuko intsinzi ku rupfu no kurimbuka bayifite iteka ryose. Bazaremwa bundi bushya nk'abana b'umucyo, kandi bazambikwa ubwiza bw'iteka, kandi bazagira ubushobozi n'impano byihariye byanditswe mu miterere yabo bizagaragaza ibyo bagezeho bikubiyemo kandi bigaragaza ubushobozi nzarema muri bo. Kuko njye, Kristo Yesu, ndi umutunganyije wicara afite umuvuba mu ntoki, akongeza imiriro yo kweza, kugira ngo mbareme mwese bundi bushya muri njye, Kristo Yesu Umwami wanyu wazamutse mu ijuru n'Umukiza; kandi uzanesha wese, na bo bazaragwa byose.

Mumenye bana banjye ko abizerwa bose muri Kristo Yesu bari itorero rye, umubiri we, ndetse n'umugeni we batoranyijwe by'umwihariko muri Kristo Yesu mbere y'ishingwa ry'isi. Nubwo benshi basobanura aya Magambo bakoresheje amagambo y'ubupfu nka "abatoni cyane n'abahawe umugisha by'ukuri" kugira ngo bahe ihumure umubiri iyo bivuzwe nta kwizera kandi nta gusobanukirwa ukuri kwanjye kw'iteka, uwo si wo mugambi wuzuye ariko rwose ni ukuri ku bizerwa muri Kristo ku bw'ukumvira kwabo no kwihana.

Mumenye ko, imvugo ngo "batoranyijwe muri we mbere y'ishingwa ry'isi" yerekana ko Adonayi-YHVH wanyu yatoranyije ubwe kandi akayobora mu mbaraga zose z'ubushake n'igishushanyo cy'umugambi utunganye, atigeze ahutaza ubushake bwa buri muntu na gato, binyuze mu kubumba, gukora, no kurema bundi bushya muri Kristo Yesu buri wese muri mwe mu gusobanukirwa kwuzuye n'uburenganzira bwuzuye bw'ubuhanzi n'igishushanyo mu kwitondera buri kantu kose kuri buri wese muri mwe byatwaye ibihe byose kuva kuri Adamu na Eva kugeza ubu kugira ngo mbisohoze mu buhanga bwanjye.
