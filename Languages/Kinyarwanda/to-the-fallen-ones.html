Mu Mirimo ya Yohana Umuhishuzi

Inkuba ya 1 n'iya 2

Ku Baguye: Ubu Haje Urubanza Rwanyu!

Kuva kuri Yeshua HaMashiach:

Mwa bahamagawe banjye, batoranyijwe, kandi bizerwa, mwumve Amagambo y'Umwami wanyu muzima n'umukiza, ari we Yeshua Mesiya! Amahanga mutuyemo n'amateka mwigishijwe n'abarezi banyu b'aba Yezuwiti ni ishusho y'ikinyoma gikomeye, cyenda guhishurwa byuzuye kandi cyamaze gutangira, aho abaguye bazagaragara nk'abanyamahanga beza ariko mu by'ukuri ari abamarayika baguye babi. Icyabayeho ni iki? Ni cyo kizabaho: kandi icyakozwe ni iki? Icyo kizakorwa: kandi nta kintu gishya munsi y'izuba.

Satani yatanze ubumenyi bwe bwitwa ko buteye imbere kuva mu ntangiriro, ndetse n'ubumenyi bwe bw'icyiza n'ikibi. Ni umushukanyi mukuru n'umunyabinyoma wigishije abantu ibintu byinshi Yahweh atari yagennye, kuko izi ari inzira z'urupfu. Ibyaremwe bya Yahweh ntibyaremewe gukoreshwa mu gushaka ububasha n'inyungu z'umuntu ku giti cye cyangwa nk'uburyo bwo guhunga urubanza rw'Imana, cyangwa gushyiraho inzira n'amategeko by'umuntu. Kuko Satani yononnye abantu kuva mu ntangiriro, kandi yononnye abarinzi baguye bifatanyije mu kwigomeka kwabo.

Yewe mwanzi mukuru w'ugukiranuka kose, ndetse namwe ba Satani: ibyaha byanyu bya mbere byanditswe na Henoki umwanditsi icyo gihe cyose, kandi bihari kugeza n'uyu munsi, kuko ibyaha byanyu byanditswe bityo. Kandi nuko rero nincamake, kuko mwakoze ibi bintu, kandi mwakoze n'ibindi byinshi: Mwayobeje abana b'Imana bose, mubamanura ku isi, mubayobesha binyuze mu bakobwa b'abantu. Mwahaye abana b'Imana bera inama mbi, murabayobya kugira ngo banduze imibiri yabo n'abakobwa b'abantu. Mweretse abana b'abantu ibikomere byose by'urupfu, muyobya Eva, mwereka abana b'abantu intwaro z'urupfu: ingabo n'ikoti ry'icyuma, n'inkota y'intambara, n'intwaro zose z'urupfu ku bana b'abantu. Kandi bivuye mu kuboko kwanyu byibasiye abatura ku isi kuva uwo munsi kugeza iteka ryose.

Mwa ba Satani mwigishije abana b'abantu ibisharira n'ibiryohereye, kandi mwabigishije amabanga yose y'ubwenge bwabo. Mwigishije abantu kwandika mukoresheje wino n'urupapuro, bityo benshi bakoze ibyaha kuva kera kugeza kera n'uyu munsi. Kuko abantu bataremewe iyo ntego, yo kwemeza ukwizera kwabo kwiza n'ikaramu na wino. Kuko abantu baremwe kimwe n'abamarayika, kugira ngo bakomeze kuba abera n'abakiranutsi; kandi urupfu, rurimbura byose, ntirwari kubafata, ariko binyuze muri ubu bumenyi bararimbuka, kandi binyuze muri ubu bubasha buratsemba abantu.

Mwa ba Satani mweretse abana b'abantu gukubita kubi kwose kw'imyuka n'amadayimoni, no gukubita urusoro mu nda, kugira ngo ruvemo, no gukubita ubugingo, kurumwa n'inzoka, no gukubita kubaho binyuze mu bushyuhe bwa saa sita, umwana w'inzoka witwa Tabââ'ĕt. Kuko mwasabye na Mikayeli kubereka izina ryihishe, kugira ngo murivuge mu ndahiro, kugira ngo abo bahinde umushyitsi imbere y'iryo zina n'indahiro bahishuriye abana b'abantu ibyari mu ibanga byose.

Kuko mwatumye abarinzi bifatanya mu kwigomeka kwanyu uko mwakomezaga kwangiza abantu. Kandi nuko ibyaha by'abarinzi byanditswe na Henoki umwanditsi, kandi nincamake mu buryo bukurikira: Bigishije abantu gukora inkota, n'ibyuma, n'ingabo, n'ibyuma bikingira igituza, babamenyesha ibyuma byo mu isi n'ubuhanga bwo kubikora, n'ibikomo, n'imitako, n'ikoreshwa rya antimuani, no gukora ibitsike byiza, n'amabuye y'agaciro y'amoko yose, n'amabara yose. Bigishije uburozi, no gukata imizi, gukemura uburozi, kuragurisha inyenyeri, amatsinda y'inyenyeri, ubumenyi bw'ibicu, ibimenyetso by'isi, ibimenyetso by'izuba, n'inzira y'ukwezi.

Kandi nuko binyuze mu kwangiza kwanyu n'ibinyoma, mwanduje ibyaremwe bya Yahweh n'inzira zanyu mbi n'ibizira bituka Imana. Mwayobeje abantu kandi mukora ibyaha kuri Yahweh, kandi mwarenganyije abantu, ndetse mukora ibyaha ku nyoni, n'inyamaswa, n'ibikururuka, n'amafi, kandi ibyaha byanyu bizana urubanza rukomeye mutazakira. Mwanduje ibyaremwe bya Yahweh n'ikoranabuhanga ryanyu n'uburiganya bwanyu, kandi mwatumye abantu batangazwa n'amayeri yanyu n'ibinyoma. Kuko Yahweh yaberetse gusa amabanga y'ubusa y'ibyaremwe bye kandi abika amabanga ye y'agaciro kenshi mu mutima we kure y'ubugambanyi bwanyu.

Mwa babi mwe mwigishije inzira zanyu z'ubusa ku bantu uko bororokaga ku maso y'isi, kandi mwazanye amakuba akomeye n'irimbuka binyuze mu migambi yanyu mbi n'ikoranabuhanga. Kuko mutazanywe n'ikindi uretse kwiba, kwica, no kurimbura. Mwigishije uburiganya bw'ikirere, kuko muri umutware w'ububasha bw'ikirere, kuko muriganya aithēr. Kuko igihe Kayini yakoraga icyaha, yubatse umujyi; kandi mwigishije abantu amabanga yanyu y'ubusa n'uburiganya. Iyi mijyi yakoreshejwe mu kugenzura no kuriganya no kuzana irimbuka ry'abantu, nk'uko mwayikoresheje mu gukora ibizira byanyu by'irimbuka.

Mwatumye ishyanga rihagurukira irindi shyanga mu ntambara n'amakimbirane adashira, kandi mwatumye abantu banjye bakora ibyaha, kandi Yahweh yatatanyirije Imiryango ya Isirayeli mu miyaga ine. Nta kibazo, umugambi wanyu mubi wateje imbere imigambi ya Yahweh, kuko binyuze mu gutatana kwabo hazakorwa gukoranya intama zisigaye. Kandi nzohereza abahamya banjye babiri guhanura iminsi 1260 bambaye ibigunira. Mu mbaraga zanyu zose zo kurimbura abana ba Yahweh mwatsinzwe bikomeye, kandi ntimwashoboraga kubona ko imbaraga zanyu zateje imbere imigambi ya Yahweh, kuko n'abanzi be bumvira ijwi rye.

Kuko mwatoteje kandi mugatesha umutwe imiryango 12 ku isi yose uko mwashakaga kuyirimbura. Mwibye abana ba ba nyina, mutwara abana mu miryango mubimurira hirya no hino ku isi, hanyuma murababeshya mutuma bizera imigani y'aba Yezuwiti ku byerekeye amateka yabo nyakuri, imigambi, n'inkomoko. Nta kibazo, mwatsinzwe, kuko njyewe, Yeshua HaMashiach, nzabahuza byuzuye n'amateka yabo yashize, ishyanga n'umurage nyakuri, kandi nzaha abazaragwa agakiza bose kuba umuntu mushya umwe, ndetse muri njye, Kristo Yesu.

Binyuze mu mayeri n'irimbuka rikomeye, mwahishe neza ikoranabuhanga ryanyu, ndetse mubeshya ko abantu bo hambere bari abinjiji kandi nta koranabuhanga bari bafite. Kuko mweretse aba kera amabanga yanyu y'ubusa, kandi nta kintu gishya munsi y'izuba. Kandi nuko muhisha ukuri ku byerekeye uburiganya bwanyu bw'ibyaremwe, ndetse mubihisha abo mwagashutse n'ikinyoma cyanyu gikomeye. Mwongeye kuzana amayeri yanyu y'ubumaji nanone, kuko mwateguye urubuga kugira ngo mwiyoberanye nk'abanyamahanga. Kandi binyuze mu gitero cyanyu cy'abanyamahanga b'ibinyoma muzatuma bose batangazwa n'ibimenyetso byanyu by'ibinyoma n'ibitangaza, ndetse n'abo amazina yabo atanditswe mu Gitabo cy'Ubugingo kuva ku kuremwa kw'isi. Ariko njyewe, Yeshua HaMashiach, nzabashyira ahagaragara byuzuye, kandi buri banga ryihishe rizajya ahagaragara.

Dore! Nzatwara abizerwa banjye mu ijuru, kuko nje gutwara Umugeni wanjye kandi sinzahagarikwa. Kandi njyewe, Yeshua HaMashiach, nje guca urubanza no kurwana intambara no kuzana urubanza rukomeye ku isi, kandi ntimuzarokoka.

Njyewe, Yeshua HaMashiach, Umwami w'abami n'Umutware w'abatware, nohereje aya magambo binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye, kandi binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye amagambo yanjye yoherejwe, kandi byose bizasohora.

Kuva kuri Yahweh, Imana Ikomeye Ihoraho:

Yewe, mwa ba Satani, mwashutse ibyaremwe byanjye gukurikira ibimenyetso byanyu by'ibinyoma n'ibitangaza, kandi umujinya wanjye urabaka kuri mwe. Abantu banjye bazatandukanywa byuzuye n'urumamfu rwanyu mwabibye mu ngano zanjye mu busitani bwanjye bw'ubuntu; kandi Umwana wanjye, Kristo Yesu, azakoranya ingano zanjye mu kigega cyanjye, kandi nuko Umwami w'isarura azakoranya, abohere kandi atwike urumamfu rwanyu. Urubanza rukomeye ruri kuri mwe, kandi ntimuzarokoka ukuboko kwanjye. Kuva mu ntangiriro, mwiyeretse kandi mwihisha mu bana banjye, ndetse mwangiza abato banjye kuva mu ntangiriro cyane n'ibinyoma byanyu n'uburiganya.

Ntimuri imana, n'abakurikiye ikinyoma cyanyu n'ibitekerezo by'insinzi na bo si imana. Ntimushobora kurema, kuko mubuze ububasha, kandi sinigeze mbarema n'ubwo bushobozi kuko nari nzi ko muzigomeka mbere y'uko muremwa; kandi mu butabera bwanjye n'urukundo rwanjye nabaremye mu gukiranuka, kandi mwari batunganye ku munsi mwaremweho. Mu bwibone bwanyu bw'ubusa mwirata ubukuru bwanyu, ariko nta bukuru mufite, kandi nzabashyira ahagaragara byuzuye nk'abatekamutwe muri bo. Nzabashyira imbere y'Abami, kandi bazababona n'ukuntu nta bubasha mufite. Umwana wanjye, Yeshua HaMashiach, yabatsinze byuzuye i Kaluvari, kandi nta byiringiro mufite.

Mwemererwa gukora gusa ibyo nemeye, kuko muri umwanzi watsinzwe. Mwibye icyangombwa cy'isi mu buriganya bwanyu n'ibinyoma kuri Adamu, kandi none umwana wanjye w'ikinege, ari we Yeshua HaMashiach, yabatsinze mu rukundo rwe kuri njye n'ibyaremwe byanjye; kandi isi ni iye, ndetse Umwami w'abami n'Umutware w'abatware, kuko nabitangaje. Kuko yaguze isi n'abantu n'amaraso ye atagira icyaha, kandi abahisemo ubugingo bazacungurwa. Imirimo yanyu yose y'ubusa izasenywa, kandi abahisemo ubugingo bose bazasohoka mu binyoma byanyu n'uburiganya binjire mu bugingo bushya, kandi nzaha abizerwa ubwiza buruta ubwo mwari mufite kera. Kuko mwatsinzwe, yewe batware b'isi banyantege nke kandi b'abasabirizi, ndetse na buri mutware mu nzego zanyu yatsinzwe hamwe namwe, yewe nzoka yikurura, y'ikinyoma.

None hageze igihe cy'Urubanza rwanyu n'irimbuka ryuzuye ry'ubwami bwanyu, ndetse ubwami mwubatse mu mbaraga zanyu zo kugerageza kumpisha, ndetse Yahweh, ku byaremwe byanjye. Ntimuzarokoka. Ntimuzongera kwinjira mu ijuru, kuko mwajugunywe ku isi ntimuzigere mugaruka, kandi umwanya wanyu mu ijuru ntukiriho. Muboshywe ku isi mwararikiye, itari iyanyu ngo muyifate. Buri wese mu mirimo yanyu uzasenywa mu buzima bw'abo mwagerageje kurimbura (no ku isi ubwayo); bazatura iteka mu mahoro n'umutekano, kandi bazagira ibyishimo byuzuye, kandi nzatura mu bantu kandi nzaba Imana yabo.

Ariko wowe, yewe wikurura, nta bubasha ufite mu maso yanjye, kandi nta cyo wakora ngo unhagarike. Mu minsi itandatu kuva kugwa k'umuntu, kuko umunsi umwe ari imyaka igihumbi mu maso yanjye, nta cyo mwagezeho uretse urubanza rwanyu, kuvumwa, kurimbuka, n'umubabaro kuri mwe n'ababakurikira bose. Kuko muzabohwa kandi mujugunywe muri rya ziba rinini ryaka umuriro aho nabakoreye ibirimi by'umuriro bishyushye cyane, ndetse ibirimi by'umuriro by'ubururu bwiza, kandi muzashya iteka, kandi nzabitura buri gikorwa kibi cyose mwigeze gukora, inshuro nyinshi. Mwishe imbaga nyinshi ku mbaga, kandi muzishyura buri bugingo bwose bwatakaye, kandi ntimuzigera murokoka. Muzamenya ibyaha byose mwakoze, kandi ntimuzigera murokoka urubanza, kandi muzababazwa iteka kubera ibyaha mwakoze. Muzamenya neza ibyaha byanyu n'ibicumuro byanyu mu mubabaro wanyu, kandi abo mwanga bazabatsinda bwa nyuma. Kandi abakiranirwa bose mwigeze gushuka gukora ibyaha no kwiyemeza kwifatanya mu kwigomeka kwanyu, ndetse n'abo amazina yabo atigeze yandikwa mu Gitabo cy'Ubugingo, bazasangira umubabaro wanyu. Mwese muzishyura buri cyaha cyose mwakoze, kuko mwakoze icyaha kuri Yahweh no ku byaremwe byanjye, kandi nzishyura. Kuko mu butabera bwanjye nzabitura ibyaha byanyu, ibikorwa, n'inzira mbi byuzuye mu bituza byanyu inshuro nyinshi kandi iteka ryose. Kandi njyewe, Yahweh, ndetse Imana ikomeye ihoraho, ndahiye ibi bintu mu izina ryanjye bwite, kuko nta wukomeye kuruta uwo nshobora kurahirira.

Muzakorera imigambi yanjye, kandi mu rwango rwanyu n'irimbuka muzazana intore zanjye kuri njye; kuko nzabayobora mu kuboko kwa Yeshua HaMashiach n'ingabo ze z'abagabo b'abanyambaraga, kuko bazakoranya intama zanjye zisigaye. (Dore! Abantu 144,000 boherejwe na Yeshua HaMashiach bazakora imirimo ikomeye imbere y'ikibi cyanyu giteye ishozi.) Kandi nta n'umwe nageneye ubugingo buhoraho uzagwa mu migambi yanyu, kuko batashobora gukurwa mu kuboko kwanjye, kandi nta n'ushobora kubakura mu maboko y'Umwana wanjye: Kuko namuhaye izina riruta amazina yose, kandi aje mu bubasha bwa Yahweh ubwe, kuko nasangiye intebe yanjye y'ubwami na we. Kandi azamenagura umutwe wanyu byuzuye. Mwatsinzwe, mutware muto, kandi igihe cyanyu kigeze.

Njyewe, Yahweh, Imana ikomeye ihoraho, nohereje aya magambo binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye, kandi binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye amagambo yanjye yoherejwe, kandi azasohora. Kuko Yahweh, Imana ikomeye ihoraho, yabitangaje bityo, kandi nta gishobora kwica amatangazo yanjye. NDI El Shaddai!
