Ku 144,000: Ku Bw'Iki Gihe Ni Bwo Mwahamagariwe Kandi Mushyirwaho Ikimenyetso

Muhagarare ku bwanjye kandi mukurikire urugero rwanjye, kuko Abaheburayo bavuga iki ku byerekeye guhagarara kwanjye ku bwa Yahweh? We kubw'ibyishimo byashyizwe imbere ye, yihanganiye umusaraba, ntiyita ku isoni, kandi yicaye iburyo bw'intebe y'Imana. Bityo mu rukundo rwanjye ku Data, rudashobora kumeneka, no mu kumvira kwanjye kubw'urukundo, no mu rukundo nkunda abantu n'icyifuzo cyanjye cyo kwitanga ngo mbakize, nihanganiye igihano gikomeye, umubabaro, n'imibabaro kandi nabikoze mbikunze, mfite ibyishimo mu mutima wanjye. Nari nzi ko binyuze mu gitambo cyanjye n'urupfu rwanjye, benshi bazagirwa abakiranutsi kandi bakaba bazima ku bw'imbaraga z'umuzuko wanjye kuko Yahweh ubwe yanzuye mu bapfuye, kuko bidashoboka ko urupfu rwamfata. Kandi muri izo mbaraga, namwe muzazurirwa mu bugingo bushya, kuko nishyuye ikiguzi ntanga amaraso yanjye atagira icyaha nk'incungu kuri bose bazizera izina ryanjye, kandi umuntu wese uzambaza izina ry'Umwami azakizwa, badakunda amagara yabo kugeza ku gupfa!

Kandi none ndabasaba, abizerwa banjye n'abatoranyijwe, ndetse n'abavandimwe banjye, guhagarara ku bwanjye kandi ntimwisubireho. Kuko navuze neza mu rwandiko rwanjye ku Baheburayo nti: Ariko umukiranutsi azabeshwaho no kwizera: ariko nasubira inyuma, umutima wanjye ntuzamwishimira.

Mubyumve neza, kandi mwongere mubisomerwe, kandi ntimusubire muri Torah, kuko Torah yubahirizwa no kunyizera; bityo, umukiranutsi azabeshwaho no kwizera. Mwitiranwa nanjye, kandi ni njye wabagize abakiranutsi mu mirimo yanjye y'impongano no mu maraso yanjye atagira icyaha.

Benshi muri mwe mugorwa n'igitekerezo cy'uko nafashe umwanya wanyu mu gucirwaho iteka ku giti kubera ibyaha ntakoze, kandi nabikoze mbikunze kandi nishimye kugira ngo nzane agakiza ku muntu kamere wangiritse, kugira ngo mbacungure kuri Yahweh, ndetse Imana yanyu na So, ndetse na Se w'Imicyo utagira guhinduka cyangwa igicucu cyo guhindukira. Kuko Yahweh adahinduka; ni wa wundi ejo, uyu munsi, n'iteka ryose!

Ni yo Mana ba sokuruza banyu bumvikanyeho mu isezerano, ndetse no ku Musozi wa Sinayi ubwo Mose yahabwaga amategeko na Yahweh ubwe. Kandi nubwo amategeko ari ayera, n'itegeko rikaba iryera, kandi rikiranuka, kandi ryiza, abantu ntibashoboraga kubaho bakurikije amahame yaryo kuko amategeko yatangiwe kwemeza icyaha mu mubiri, kandi mwese muvuka mupfuye mu byaha n'ibicumuro kuko icyaha kiri mu mubiri wanyu. Torah ntiyatangiwe abakiranutsi ahubwo yatangiwe abakiranirwa, kugira ngo icyaha kigaragare nk'icyaha mu mubiri. Torah yatanzwe nk'umurezi wanyu kugira ngo ibazane kuri njye, ndetse Kristo Yesu, Umwami w'Abami n'Umwami w'abami wazamutse!

Kandi bityo nishyizeho ingaruka z'icyaha ku bushake, kuko ari ko navukiye gucungura abantu mu muvumo w'amategeko. Kuko amategeko atari avumwe; yego, amategeko narekwe kandi umuntu wese avumwe uvuma amategeko ya Mose, kuko ntaje gukuraho amategeko, ahubwo naje gushyiraho amategeko ndetse no kurenza amategeko ya Mose. Mutekereza ko ari ugutuka Imana?

Ubuhamya bw'Abaheburayo buhagarara hagati yacu: Kuko niba amaraso y'amapfizi n'ay'ihene, n'ivu ry'ishashi rishanyagurirwa ku bahumanye, ryeza kugeza ku kweza umubiri: Amaraso ya Kristo, we wituye Imana binyuze mu Mwuka w'iteka adafite inenge, azarushaho gukura imirimo ipfuye mu mitima yanyu kugira ngo mukorere Imana nzima?

Kuko mukunda Mose (nk'uko mukunda), ndabagira inama yo kumvira amabwiriza ya Mose mu rukundo; kuko yavuze ati: Yahweh Elohim wanyu azabahagurukiriza Umuhanuzi muri mwe, mu bavandimwe banyu, umeze nkanjye; muzamwumvira. Nimwumve rero ijwi rya Yeshua HaMashiach! Mwejejweho icyaha n'ibyaha byose mukora binyuze mu maraso yanjye y'impongano atagira icyaha natuye Yahweh nk'incungu y'ubugingo bwanyu, kandi nabacunguye mu kuboko kw'umwanzi. Ntimugakore icyaha! Kandi niba umuntu akoze icyaha, mwature ibyaha byanyu kuri njye, kuko ari njye ubeza mu maraso yanjye y'impongano.

Ntabwo natangiye ibyaha byanyu n'ingaruka zabyo impongano n'ihene y'igitambo, cyangwa ngo ntange umwana w'intama w'umwaka wa mbere; kuko ndi Umwana w'Intama w'Imana w'umwaka wa mbere, kandi ni njye wituye ntagira inenge cyangwa ikizinga, ndetse ndi uwera, utunganye, kandi wejejwe kuri Yahweh ubwe (ndetse na Data) ku bw'ibyaha byanyu rimwe rizima, ndetse n'ibihe byose. Natangiye ibyaha byanyu impongano mu maraso yanjye atagira icyaha; bityo, ntihakiriho ikindi gitambo cy'ibyaha. Kandi kunyanga, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndetse n'amasezerano y'amahoro atunganye ya Yahweh ku bantu, ni ukwanga Yahweh no kwifatanya n'ubwigomeke bwa Satani, kuko Satani yigometse ku gahunda y'Imana kuva mu ntangiriro.

Kuko nabwiye Abaheburayo nti: Kuko niba dukora icyaha nkana tumaze kumenya ukuri, ntihakiriho ikindi gitambo cy'ibyaha. Bityo gukora icyaha nkana wanga amaraso yanjye y'impongano, bityo ukanga amasezerano y'amahoro yoherejwe na Yahweh, ni bibi cyane, kandi ntihakiriho ikindi gitambo. Kuko byanditswe mu Baheburayo ngo: Uwasuzuguye amategeko ya Mose yapfaga nta mbabazi abihamijwe n'abatangabuhamya babiri cyangwa batatu: Mutekereza ko azakwiriye igihano kirushijeho gukomera, uwasuzuguye Umwana w'Imana, agafata amaraso y'isezerano, ayo yejejweho, nk'ikintu gihumanye, kandi agasuzugura Umwuka w'ubuntu?

Bityo ndabashishikariza mwese kwemera igitambo cyanjye no kumva ko nta bindi bitambo bikenewe kandi hariho Mesiya umwe w'ukuri, ndetse nanjye, Yeshua HaMashiach. Kandi byanditswe kuri njye ngo: Ariko uyu mugabo, amaze gutanga igitambo kimwe cy'ibyaha iteka ryose, yicaye iburyo bw'Imana; Kuva ubwo ategereje kugeza aho abanzi be bazagirwa intebe y'ibirenge bye. Kuko ku gitambo kimwe yatunganije iteka ryose abejejwe.

Bityo mwumve ko nabejeje mu buryo bwose n'ibice byose birebana n'icyaha n'ingaruka zacyo. Mwejejwe muri njye; mugizwe abakiranutsi muri njye; mugizwe gukiranuka kw'Imana muri njye; kandi nabacunguye byuzuye kuri njye, ndetse nanjye, Yeshua HaMashiach, uwo Yahweh yohereje nka Mesiya wanyu. Kandi bityo mwumve ko nta Batambyi Bakuru b'Abalewi bakiriho, kandi Ubutambyi bwa Aroni bwaje kandi buragenda, kuko bwari igicucu cy'ibizaza. Kuko ndi umutambyi mukuru wanyu iteka ryose mu buryo bwa Melikisedeki.

Kuko amategeko nta cyo yatunganije: ahubwo yari intangiriro y'ibyiringiro byiza, ibyo byiringiro ni byo bituma twegera Yahweh. Kandi na none: Byari bikwiriye ko tugira Umutambyi Mukuru nk'uwo wera, utagira inenge, utanduye, utandukanijwe n'abanyabyaha, kandi ushyizwe hejuru y'ijuru. Kandi na none: Kuko amategeko agira abantu abatambyi, bafite intege nke: ariko ijambo ry'indahiro ryaje nyuma y'amategeko, rigira umwana umutambyi, utunganye iteka ryose. Ntimwasomye ibyo ibyanditswe bivuga kuri njye? Yahweh yarahiye kandi ntazicuza: Uri Umutambyi iteka ryose mu buryo bwa Melikisedeki.

Mwumve ko nta kibura mu gitambo cyanjye, kandi mutekanye muri njye, Kristo Yesu. Torah yubahirizwa no kunyizera, Yeshua HaMashiach, kandi ntibishoboka ko mwica iryo ari ryo ryose mu mategeko y'Imana mugendera muri njye, Kristo Yesu. Kuko mugendera mu mategeko y'Umwuka w'ubugingo, kandi nababohoye mu mategeko y'icyaha n'urupfu. Bityo munyizere, kuko umukiranutsi azabeshwaho no kwizera. Mfite imirimo myinshi ikomeye nabahamagariye, kandi muzagira uruhare mu kuzana agakiza kuri benshi. Hari ibyiringiro bikomeye n'ingororano mwabikiwe mu ijuru kubera guhagarara kwanyu no gukiranuka kwanyu.

Kuko mbohereje, mushyizweho ikimenyetso mu ruhanga rwanyu n'ikimenyetso cya Yahweh ubwe, mu isi izabanga cyane, kandi ntibitangaje, kuko isi yabanje kunyanga cyane, kandi umugaragu ntaruta shebuja. Muri abatangabuhamya banjye n'abahanuzi n'abakiza banjye n'abatanga banjye. Mbohereje mu mwijima w'icuraburindi utigeze ubaho kugira ngo utwikire irema rya Yahweh. Ku bw'igihe nk'iki mwarahamagawe, muratoranywa, kandi mushyirwaho ikimenyetso, kandi ibi bihe bizaba iby'ubugome n'ibibi cyane isi itigeze ibona, ndetse bibi kuruta iminsi ya Nowa. Hagati yabo ndabohereje, kandi ndababwira nti ntimutinye! Ntimutinye iterabwoba ryabo; ntimutinye ibyo umuntu ashobora kubakorera; ntimutinye mu maso habo; ntimutinye imyuka ibaha imbaraga; ntimutinye inyamaswa n'umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma; kandi ntimutinye Satani ubwe: Ndetse na cya kiyoka kinini. Kuko muzakandagira buri gice cy'ubwami bwa Satani, kandi nta kintu na kimwe kizabahagarika niba mugumye kuba abizerwa ku ijwi ryanjye kandi mukareka nkabayobora intambwe ku yindi. Kuko mu by'ukuri, muri abakozi bagenzi banjye muri iki gihe cy'amakuba ya Yakobo, kandi nabahaye imbaraga.

Muri abanyambaraga n'abakomeye, kandi mbohereje mu bugwaneza no kwicisha bugufi. Mbohereje kugira ngo mwubahirize amategeko binyuze mu kunyizera kwanyu, bityo mukunde Yahweh n'umutima wanyu wose, ubugingo, ubwenge, n'imbaraga, kandi mukunde mugenzi wanyu nk'uko mwikunda. Mugenzi wawe ni nde? Abakeneye ubufasha bwanyu bose mboherejeho, kandi sinbabangamiye n'imipaka y'ibihugu. Mugende muragire umukumbi wanjye, kuko ndi Umushumba Mukuru ufite ingororano yanyu mu kuboko kwanjye kubera gukiranuka. Mugende mwite ku mukumbi wanjye, mwite ku bikomere byabo, mubagaburire, mubakize, mubayobore, kandi hejuru ya byose mubazane kuri njye; kuko ubwanyu nta cyo muri cyo, ariko muri njye, muri abagabo banjye b'Imana bakomeye, b'abanyambaraga. Mubigishe kwambaza izina ryanjye kugeza ku iherezo, kuko uzabikora wese azakizwa.

Mugumise amaso yanyu ku ngororano n'ibyishimo byashyizwe imbere yanyu, kandi mukomeze imbere mugana ku ntego y'ingororano yo guhamagarwa kwo hejuru kw'Imana muri Kristo Yesu. Kuko ingororano yanyu izaba nini, kandi muzatangazwa n'ibyo mwabikiwe mu ijuru. Kandi ingororano yanjye si iy'igihe gito ahubwo ni iy'iteka, kandi ingororano zanyu ntizizarimbuka ahubwo zizaba umurage w'iteka mu gusohoza amasezerano Yahweh yahaye ba sokuruza; buri yod cyangwa ihwa rya yod rizasohozwa. Ntimuzamenya ubunini bw'imirimo yanyu kugeza kuri uwo munsi w'ingororano, ariko Ibyanditswe birasobanutse ku byerekeye imbuto z'imirimo yanyu.

Kuko Yohana Umuhishuzi yanditse mu budahemuka ibyo namweretse: Hanyuma y'ibyo mbona, dore umutwe munini w'abantu, utashoboraga kubarwa n'umuntu, bo mu mahanga yose n'imiryango, n'abantu, n'indimi, bahagaze imbere y'intebe y'Imana, n'imbere y'Umwana w'Intama, bambaye amakanzu maremare yera, n'amashami y'imikindo mu ntoki zabo. Kandi bavuga n'ijwi rirenga, bati, Agakiza kava ku Mana yacu, yicaye ku ntebe y'Imana, no ku Mwana w'Intama. Kuko umutwe munini w'abantu muzawufasha gukizwa no gukorera, kandi ingororano zanyu zo gukiranuka zizaba nini.

Kandi muzankurikira aho nzajya hose, kandi muri umuganura waguzwe mu bantu, kandi muzaririmba indirimbo nshya imbere y'intebe y'Imana mwe gusa mushobora kwiga. Muzanategekana nanjye mu Bwami bwanjye imyaka igihumbi kandi muzabana nanjye iteka ryose mu buzima bw'iteka. Kuko ibyishimo byanyu ari iby'iteka, kuko muzabana nanjye mu Bwami bwanjye ku isi uko ntegeka isi mu gukiranuka ku ntebe ya Dawidi, kandi Ubwami bwanjye ntibugira iherezo. Kandi nta kintu kibi kizinjiramo, kandi ntihazabaho amakuba ukundi, kandi ubwanjye ni ubwami bw'ugukiranuka aho umwana w'intama n'intare bizaryamana mu mahoro. Kandi ku iherezo ry'ingoma yanjye y'imyaka igihumbi, Satani azabohurwa igihe gito kugira ngo akusanye igitero cye cya nyuma, kandi umuriro uzamanuka uva kuri Yahweh ubarimbure rwose. Satani azajugunywa mu nyanja y'umuriro, ntazongera kubyuka ukundi, kandi Yahweh azarema ijuru rishya n'isi nshya, kandi azatura mu bantu, bityo muzatura muri Yerusalemu Nshya, ndetse uwo mudugudu wera waremwe na Yahweh ubwe. Kandi Yahweh ubwe yavuze ati: Unesha azaragwa byose; kandi nzaba Imana ye, na we azaba umwana wanjye.
