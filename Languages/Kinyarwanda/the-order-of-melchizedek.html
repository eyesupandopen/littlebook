Ibisharira N'ibiryohereye
6 Mutarama 2025

Ubutambyi bwa Melikisedeki: Guhererekanya Umwambaro, Gukererwa, no Kongera Gushyirwaho

Biturutse kuri Yahweh, NDI WE NDI Ukomeye:

Nimwumve Amagambo ya Yahweh Imana ikomeye ihoraho kandi mundeke mpe ubwenge abanyabwenge muri mwe. Nuko rero mbahaye gusobanukirwa Ubutambyi bwa Melikisedeki, ubutambyi Yesu Kristo yimikiwemo kuba Umutambyi Mukuru, yewe no mu butambyi bwa Melikisedeki iteka ryose! Nyamara bimeze bityo ko kubera ibyaha by'abantu, inzira zanjye zibagiranye, kandi amasezerano menshi yatawe, n'umwijima mwinshi n'inzira zitigishijwe byabuditse mu isi kubera ibinyoma by'abaguye. Kandi ibi ni ukubera ibinyoma byigambiriwe n'abashaka guhisha ukuri mu nzira zabo zo gukiranirwa no mu binyoma byabo by'amahimbano byatumye isi itwarwa n'ikinyoma cyabo gikomeye cyaje ku isi yose. Yewe n'ikinyoma igice kimwe cyibwira ko ubuzima bwose bwaturutse ku iturika rinini ryihimbirwe ryatanzwe kugira ngo ryigishe abantu ibinyoma by'uwaguye ugerageza guhisha Yahweh, NDI WE NDI Ukomeye, mu isi naremye n'ukuboko kwanjye.

Kandi NDI Umwami w'Imyuka, kandi NDI Umusaza w'Ibihe ni ukuri, ni ukuri ni we wabagaho mbere y'uko iminsi iremwa kandi NDI we waremye abamarayika bera abo byanditsweho ngo: Agira imyuka intumwa ze, n'abakozi be ibirimi by'umuriro, ndetse n'igihe irema ryaremwaga rikanazanwa rivanywe mu butunzi bw'umutima wanjye uhoraho ukora byose ukurikije inama y'ubushake bwanjye. Kuko mu by'ukuri nari nzi ko Adamu azakora icyaha abishaka ndetse na mbere y'uko Adamu akora icyaha kandi ko kubora kuzagwa kuvuye mu ijuru mu ishusho y'abaguye. Ku bw'ibyo, nateguye umugambi wanjye uhoraho w'incungu yuzuye ya Adamu ishyizwe rwose mu maso ya Yesu Kristo ari we kandi Yeshua HaMashiach mu Isezerano Rye Rishya ryo gukiranuka n'ukuri!

Kandi mu ishusho n'imiterere ya Adamu wa nyuma abantu bazacungurwa binjizwe mu byishimo n'amahoro bihoraho, aho bazasohoza mu rukundo imigambi ihoraho ya Yahweh, NDI WE NDI Ukomeye. Mu by'ukuri, irema ryanjye ryaremwe kandi rikorwa mu rukundo kugira ngo ibyo naremye bishobore kuza aho ndi mu rukundo ku bw'ibyo nabahaye byose mu rukundo mbakunda. Kandi kugira ngo irema ryanjye rishobore kugendana nanjye mu migambi yanjye ihoraho mu gusohoza ubushake bwanjye buhoraho, kandi na bo bazagira ibyishimo byuzuye kandi bitunganye, amahoro, n'ubugingo buhoraho ndetse n'igihe bayoborwa mu mahoro n'ukuboko k'Umwungeri wanjye Mukuru, Yesu w'i Nazareti, uwo nta muntu numwe ushobora kwegera Yahweh, NDI WE NDI Ukomeye, atamufite, kuko ari ko nabitangaje.

Kandi abaguye bakwirakwije ibinyoma byabo mu maso y'irema ryanjye ku buryo isi ubu yuzuye ibishuko byabo, ibyo bakoresha bota igico kugira ngo bayobye. Ndetse n'ibishuko byaje byuzuye biturutse ku ibanga ryo gukiranirwa ryarokotse umwuzure wa Nowa binyuze mu bumenyi bw'amahimbano bw'abaguye bahaye umuntu wa kamere kugira ngo arimbuke rwose kandi byuzuye. Nta kibazo, kuko nohereza abishimira gukiranirwa bose ubuyobe bukomeye kugira ngo bose bacirwe urubanza banga kumenya ukuri bagahitamo ibinyoma by'amahimbano by'abaguye. Nyamara bimeze bityo, ko umuntu wa kamere mu bujiji ashaka kwiregura mu gukiranuka yihangiye imbere y'amaso yanjye ahozaho kandi akiranuka, ku bw'ibyo, musobanukirwe ko nta mubiri n'umwe utsindishirizwa imbere yanjye kubera kubora kuri mu maraso yanyu bitewe n'icyaha mu mubiri. Kuko ubugingo bw'umubiri buri mu maraso!

Ku bw'iyi mpamvu umwana wa Yahweh yagaragarijwe abatuye isi, kugira ngo binyuze mu gitambo gicungura cy'umwana w'intama w'Imana, Adamu acungurwe mu gakiza kuzuye akurwe mu rubanza rwe rwegereje ku bwa bose bumvira amasezerano yanjye y'amahoro hagati yanjye n'abantu ari we Yesu w'i Nazareti wagizwe umwuka utanga ubuzima n'imbaraga z'ukuboko kwanjye. Kandi na none, binyuze ku Ntare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda, uwo nohereza ku isi mu gukiranuka kugira ngo acire urubanza kandi arwane intambara bityo irema rizamburwa ku ngufu mu maboko y'abigaruriye ibitari ibyabo bahora bigomeka kandi bashaka guhagarika ubushake bwanjye buhoraho, guhagarikwa kwabwo kukaba kudashoboka iteka ryose, kuko ni inde ushobora guhagarara imbere y'ukuboko kwanjye gukomeye? Kandi mu bwinshi bwabo, no mu bubi bwabo, no mu migambi yabo, no mu buriganya bwabo, ndetse no mu ikoranabuhanga ryabo ritagira umumaro (iryo birata) abaguye batsinzwe rwose n'igitambo cy'umwana w'intama w'Imana, watanze amaraso ye atagira icyaha nk'Umutambyi Mukuru iteka ryose mu butambyi bwa Melikisedeki mu kumvira kose uwamutumye, ari we Yahweh, NDI WE NDI Ukomeye kandi Uhoraho!

Kandi ubwami bwe ntibuzigera bugira iherezo, kuko mu gukiranuka kose n'ukwera nyakuri yaguze isi mu maraso ye bwite acungura kugira ngo ingoma ikiranuka ize ku isi mu gihe cyayo kandi kugira ngo dushobore kwishimira umunsi w'ikiruhuko (kuko imyaka igihumbi kuri Yahweh ari nk'umunsi umwe n'umunsi umwe nk'imyaka igihumbi imbere yanjye), ndetse n'ikiruhuko cy'iminsi y'isabato mbere y'uko ya nzoka ya kera ijugunywa iteka ryose mu birimi by'umuriro mu bubabare bwuzuye kandi buhoraho butazigera bwibukwa ukundi na mbere y'uko urubanza rw'intebe y'era nini rutegekwa kuba. Kandi ni binyuze mu murimo w'umutambyi mukuru w'Ubutambyi bwa Melikisedeki ko Adamu yacunguwe mu kubora binyuze kuri Yesu w'i Nazareti watanze igitambo cye gihoraho mu mibabaro ikomeye kugira ngo yishyure igihano cya Mose kugira ngo amategeko yanjye ahozaho ashobore guhaza bityo irema ryanjye rihoraho ritegekwe neza mu ncungu ikiranuka.

Kandi mu by'ukuri bizasohora ko nzarema ijuru rishya n'isi nshya kuko ijuru rya mbere, n'isi ya mbere bizashira, kandi inyanja ntizabaho ukundi; haba ndetse n'uzabatesha umutwe iteka ryose. Kandi bizasohora mu buryo butunguranye ubu ku buryo abaneshi banjye bazaragwa byose, kandi nzaba Imana yabo, na bo bazaba abana banjye. Ariko abanyabwoba n'abatizera, n'abakora ibizira n'abicanyi, n'abasambanyi, n'abarozi, n'abasenga ibishushanyo, n'abanyabinyoma bose bazagira umugabane wabo mu nyanja yaka umuriro n'amazuku, ari rwo rupfu rwa kabiri.

Ku bw'ibyo, ni nde mukwiye gutinya bana banjye, kuko mwagizwe abana b'Imana ihoraho binyuze mu mirimo ya Yeshua HaMashiach ari we kandi Yesu Kristo, ndetse n'uwo nahaye imbaraga hejuru ya byose mu irema ryanjye, keretse mu ntebe yanjye y'ubwami ni ho ngumana ubutware bwanjye, ku bw'ibyo ni nde ushobora guhagarara imbere y'ukuboko gukomeye kwa Yesu Kristo, Umwami wazutse kandi akazamuka mu ijuru? Ni ukuri, ni ukuri, nk'uko byahoze kandi, nk'uko biri, kandi ni ko bizaba iteka ryose kandi ibihe byose ko Yahweh na Yeshua HaMashiach ari umwe mu bumwe n'umugambi kandi namuhaye imbaraga zose mu gukiranuka kose kandi ni we nahaye gutegeka ingabo zose zo mu ijuru, kandi ategeka mu kumvira gutunganye ubushake bwanjye buhoraho. Nuko rero mbahaye gusobanukirwa amayeri yaje ku isi, ndetse n'ibinyoma by'abaguye bashaka guhisha ukuri kw'agakiza kanyu n'ukuri kw'Umwami wanyu uhoraho, Yesu Kristo!

Ku bw'ibyo, musobanukirwe ko abigisha banyu b'Abayesuwiti bateye ubuhumyi kugwa ku mitima yanyu mu guhisha ukuri kw'irema ryanjye no mu kubahisha amateka yanyu nyakuri, kugira ngo muzerere mu mwijima musohoza irari ryabo ribi, ndetse kugira ngo inuma zanjye zifatanye na bo mu murage wabo uhoraho w'amazuku yaka kandi yibirindura kuko uwo ari wo mugambi wabo. Kandi bimeze bityo ko Yahweh, NDI WE NDI Ukomeye, ntabwo nigeze nisiga ntagira ubuhamya. Kandi muri buri sezerano natanze, no muri buri testamenti natanze kandi nkemera, ntabwo nigeze nisiga ntagira ubuhamya bwumvikana bw'ibyanditswe n'amabwiriza yatanzwe n'amajwi y'abahanuzi banjye ba kera, abo nimikaga. Kandi byatanzwe n'amajwi y'abahanuzi b'Isezerano Rishya Yeshua HaMashiach yimitse bazamukorera nk'abatambyi be n'abami be bakomeye!

Kuko uko ari ko Ubutambyi bwa Melikisedeki bumeze, ndetse n'ubutambyi buhoraho bwahawe abantu, bityo ubutegetsi Adamu yagombaga gukoresha ku isi bwari ubwo gutegekwa n'uwimitswe kuba umutambyi mukuru, umwami, n'umuhanuzi! Kuko uko ari ko Ubutambyi bwa Melikisedeki bwahawe (kandi buhabwa) abantu, ndetse kugira ngo abantu bagire ubutegetsi bukiranuka nubwo ya nzoka ya kera yashutse Eva mu buriganya bwayo. Kandi n'Ubutambyi bwa Melikisedeki bwakomeje kubaho, ndetse buba kuri Adamu kuva mu ntangiriro uwo bwahawe, nyamara mu gupfa amaherezo barapfuye kandi ubutambyi bwa Melikisedeki buhererekanywa imbere mu gihe runaka, mu minsi kuva kuri Adamu kugeza kuri Yakobo.

Kandi bityo mu Isi yariho icyo gihe mbere y'umwuzure mukuru Ubutambyi bwa Melikisedeki bwavuye kuri Adamu, bujya kuri Kenani, kuri Enoshi, kuri Henoki, kuri Metusela, kuri Nowa kandi bityo ni ko Ubutambyi bwa Melikisedeki bwahererekanyijwe mu minsi mbere y'umwuzure mukuru. Nyamara, abarezi banyu b'Abayesuwiti banduye ibyanditswe, kandi bakora amayeri menshi kugira ngo bahishire ukuri kw'amateka nyakuri ya Adamu ndetse n'igihe bagerageza kugira abantu agahu k'umukungugu kadasobanutse mu isanzure ryabo ry'ikinyoma bivugwa ko ryaturutse ku iturika rinini ahantu runaka mu gihe cyabo cyahise bihimbiye.

Kuko Abayesuwiti babwiriza undi Yesu, ndetse na Yesu waremwe mu ishusho ya Nimurodi w'i Babuloni, wari umutegetsi wa mbere w'ubwoko bwe nyuma y'umwuzure. Kandi nubwo abayobozi banjye bakiranuka bimitswe kera munsi y'Ubutambyi bwa Melikisedeki bahaye abantu gukiranuka aho kubaha icyaha, kubabara, urupfu, n'igihano gihoraho. Umuntu wa kamere nyamara akenshi ahitamo icyaha, kubabara, urupfu, n'igihano gihoraho. Nyamara, bose mu irema ryanjye bahawe ubushake bwisanzuye bafite uburenganzira bwo kwihitiramo niba bazabaho kuri Yahweh binyuze kuri Yesu Kristo, cyangwa bagahitamo igihano cyabo gihoraho ku bw'ibinezeza by'icyaha mu gihe gito. Kandi bityo byaranditswe ko bose bo mu isi yariho icyo gihe uretse ubugingo umunani barokowe ari bazima ku bwa Nowa, wari umuntu ukiranuka wategetse isi akurikije Ubutambyi bwa Melikisedeki mu gihe runaka kuko mu gupfa yarapfuye.

Ku bw'ibyo, musobanukirwe inzira z'abaguye, ko n'imitima yabo yose bashaka kugoreka, kubeshya, no guhisha ukuri inuma zanjye, ndetse n'abo natoranyirije ubugingo buhoraho. Kandi bimeze bityo ko uko umugambi wanjye w'agakiza kuri Adamu wahishurwaga buhoro buhoro, nohereje inyandiko zera ku bw'umwuka w'ubuhanuzi wababayeho, ndetse n'umwuka w'ubuhanuzi waje kuvukira mu bana b'Imana binyuze mu kuvuka gushya kwatanzwe mu Isezerano Rishya rya Yeshua HaMashiach, ndetse no muri we nshimishwa na we iteka. Kandi uko nahishuraga umugambi wanjye buhoro buhoro, natanze n'inyandiko zera buhoro buhoro, kandi Yesu Kristo yatanze inyandiko zera zakiriwe mu kuboko kwanjye bwite, ndetse n'ukora byose akurikije inama y'ubushake bwanjye bwite.

Kandi bityo ni ko abaguye bakora kugira ngo bonone ukuri, ndetse mu buryo busa n'uko biyononnye ubwabo iteka ryose n'ibihe byose. Kuko banduye ibyanditswe, batanze Bibiliya z'icyigisho z'ibinyoma, batanze inkoranyamagambo n'ibitabo by'ishakiro by'ibinyoma bityo benshi barayobywa n'ibisobanuro byanduye binyuze muri byo, nk'aho Yahweh adashobora gutanga abasemuzi bashoboye bo gusemura Ijambo ryanjye kuko Ijambo ryanjye ridashobora kuvunwa cyangwa guhishwa, nubwo bagerageza. Mu by'ukuri, ni icyubahiro cya Yahweh guhisha ikintu n'icyubahiro cy'Abami kugishakashaka! Ku bw'ibyo, ndabategeka gushakashaka ukuri kwa Yahweh mu maso ya Yeshua HaMashiach!

Nuko rero musobanukirwe ko abakorera iyo maraya ikomeye, ndetse n'abigisha banyu b'Abayesuwiti, bahisemo urusobe rwatoranyijwe witonze rw'ibinyoma bifitanye isano rya kure bityo bota igico kugira ngo bayobye. Bahishe ubuhamya bwinshi bwera kandi banduye Bibiliya nyinshi bishingiye ku nyandiko z'ibinyoma kandi bakoze ibinyoma byinshi by'amahimbano ku byerekeye ukuri kw'irema ryanjye. Kandi mu kumenya kwanjye kwa mbere no mu manza zanjye nemereye uwaguye kubikora, kuko iyo inyandiko zanjye zera zirindwa neza kandi zigakomeza nk'uko byategetswe kandi byabikijwe, ubupfu nk'ubwo ntibwari kuzigera bugerwaho. Ariko uko ni ko inzira z'umuntu w'umunyabyaha zimeze kandi uko ni ko inzira z'abaguye bayobya abantu mu rari rwabo rubi ruhora rwaka zimeze. Nyamara, Ijambo ryanjye ntirishobora kuvunwa kandi rizasohoza icyo ryatumwe gusohoza.

Kandi bityo byanditswe mu gitabo cya Yasheri ko nyuma y'umwuzure mukuru umwambaro wa Melikisedeki wagumye kuri Nowa kandi amaherezo wimurirwa kuri Semu wari uwa gatandatu mu gukurikirana mu kwambara umwambaro kandi mu gihe runaka ubutambyi bwa Melikisedeki bwimuriwe kuri Aburamu uwo mu gihe runaka wabaye Se w'amahanga menshi, kandi ku baragwa be b'isezerano ni ho amasezerano y'umurage ajya kugira ngo asohozwe kuko umwana w'umuja atazaragwa amasezerano kuko umubiri n'amaraso bidashobora kuragwa amasezerano y'Ubwami bw'Imana ahubwo abakiranutsi babeshwaho no kwizera, ndetse nk'uko Aburahamu wizerwa yabayeho mu minsi ya kera.

Ku bw'ibyo, mbahaye gusobanukirwa igisobanuro cya Melikisedeki bisobanura Umwami wo Gukiranuka. Kandi bityo inkomoko y'ijambo ni Igiheburayo cyahinduwe mu Cyongereza aho Melek bisobanura Umwami n'aho Zedek bisobanura gukiranuka n'igisobanuro cyatanzwe kandi cyari kigambiriwe kikaba Umwami wo Gukiranuka. Kandi bityo aba Bami bakiranuka bategetse irema ryanjye, ndetse n'abantu bakiranuka bategereje Mesiya wabo, ndetse n'uzavuka mu muryango wa Yuda.

Kandi nk'uko byanditswe mu Baheburayo n'ukuboko kwa Pawulo (wari igisonga cy'amabanga ya Yesu Kristo) kandi bityo yaranditse ati: Kuko uwo Melikisedeki yari Umwami w'i Salemu, Umutambyi w'Imana isumbaho byose, wasanganiriye Aburahamu, ubwo yagarukaga avuye kwica Abami, akamuha umugisha: Uwo Aburahamu yahaye n'icyacumi cya byose: ubanza gusobanurwa ngo Umwami wo Gukiranuka: hanyuma y'ibyo, ni na we Mwami w'i Salemu, ni ukuvuga ngo, Umwami w'amahoro, utagira se, utagira nyina, utagira abo muryango umwe, kandi ntagira itangiriro ry'iminsi ye, cyangwa iherezo ry'ubugingo: ahubwo ashushanywa n'Umwana w'Imana, kandi agumaho ari Umutambyi iteka ryose. Kandi bityo umwambaro wa Melikisedeki wahererekanyijwe kuri Aburahamu wizerwa mu kurindwa kwawo ndetse n'ubutambyi bwarinzwe kandi bugenewe Yesu Kristo ari we kandi Yeshua HaMashiach.

Nyamara, benshi muri mwe mwizeye ibinyoma by'abigisha banyu b'Abayesuwiti babahumye amaso bakoresheje inyigisho zirwanya izindi nyigisho ko iyi mirongo ivuga kuri Yesu Kristo ubwe aho kuvuga ku Rwego rwa Melikisedeki ubwarwo. Kuko bigaragara neza ko, Yesu w'i Nazareti afite Data, ari we Yehova NDI UWONDI Ukomeye, kandi bigaragara neza ko Yesu w'i Nazareti afite nyina, ari we Mariya w'i Nazareti y'i Galilaya wavuze ati bimbere uko ubishaka, ndetse akaba urugero rw'iteka rw'ukwizera rwatanzwe kugira ngo abizerwa bamenye inzira z'ukwizera bagenderamo. Kuko bigaragara neza ko Yesu w'i Nazareti afite abo bafitanye isano abera umucunguzi w'umuryango, kuki mutubaha Amagambo yanjye asobanutse yatanzwe mu byanditswe yagenewe kubayobora mu mutekano abakura mu binyoma byabo by'ibihimbano? Nyamara benshi muri mwe mwizera ibyo binyoma kuruta ukuri n'ubusobanuro bw'Amagambo yanjye.

Nyamara, niko byanditswe ko Semu yakomeje kuba indahemuka kuri Nowa, kuko Hamu yaretse isezerano rya Nowa rwose akibagirwa inzira za Yehova kandi Hamu n'abuzukuru benshi ba Nowa bagerageje kubaka umujyi wabo ukomeye n'umunara ubwo ubwami bwa Nimurodi bukiranirwa bwazamukaga kugira ngo butegeke isi muri iyo minsi mu gukiranirwa, hakurikijwe inzira z'abaguye yagenderagamo, nubwo nakoze kenshi kugira ngo mpe Nimurodi amahitamo yo kugaruka aho ndi mu kwihana, amahitamo yanze iteka ryose.

Nyamara niko byanditswe neza mu gitabo cya Yasheri ku byerekeye Aburamu na Semu wahaye Aburamu inshingano ubwo yicaga Kedorulawomeri n'ingabo ze: Kandi agarutse avuye kwica abo bami, we n'abantu be banyuze mu kibaya cya Sidimu aho abami bari barwaniye. Kandi Bera umwami w'i Sodomu, n'abandi bantu be bari kumwe na we, bavuye mu byobo by'isayo bari baguyemo, basanganira Aburamu n'abantu be. Kandi Adonizedeki (cyangwa Melikisedeki) umwami w'i Yerusalemu, ari we Semu, asohokana n'abantu be gusanganira Aburamu n'abantu be, afite umugati na divayi, baguma hamwe mu kibaya cya Meleki. Kandi Adonizedeki (cyangwa Melikisedeki) aha Aburamu umugisha, nuko Aburamu amuha kimwe cya cumi cy'ibyo yari yakuye mu minyago y'abanzi be byose, kuko Adonizedeki (cyangwa Melikisedeki) yari umutambyi imbere y'Imana.

Nyamara niko byanditswe muri Yasheri: Kandi iminsi ya Sara yari imyaka ijana na makumyabiri n'indwi nuko arapfa, kandi Aburahamu amugirira icyunamo gikomeye kandi kirekire, amukorera imihango y'icyunamo iminsi irindwi. Kandi abaturage bose bo mu gihugu bahumuriza Aburahamu na Isaka umuhungu we ku bwa Sara. Kandi iminsi y'icyunamo cyabo ishize Aburahamu yohereza umuhungu we Isaka, ajya mu nzu ya Semu na Eberi, kwiga inzira z'Uwiteka n'amabwiriza ye, kandi Aburahamu agumayo imyaka itatu. Uko niko inshingano za Melikisedeki zahawe Isaka wampaye uburenganzira bw'imfura umwana muto, kuko Esawu yasuzuguye iby'Imana akagurisha uburenganzira bwe bw'imfura isahani y'isupu: Ndetse ni icyemezo Yehova yemeye iteka ryose.

Nyamara niko byanditswe muri Yasheri ku byerekeye Urwego rwa Melikisedeki: Kandi nyuma y'ibyo Selahi mwene Arupakisadi arapfa muri uwo mwaka, ari wo mwaka wa cumi n'umunani w'ubuzima bwa Yakobo na Esawu; kandi iminsi yose Selahi yabayeho yari imyaka magana ane na mirongo itatu n'itatu nuko arapfa. Icyo gihe Isaka yohereje umuhungu we muto Yakobo mu nzu ya Semu na Eberi, yiga amabwiriza y'Uwiteka, kandi Yakobo aguma mu nzu ya Semu na Eberi imyaka mirongo itatu n'ibiri, kandi Esawu mwene se ntiyagenda, kuko atashakaga kugenda, aguma mu nzu ya se mu gihugu cy'i Kanani.

Nyamara niko byanditswe muri Yasheri ku byerekeye Urwego rwa Melikisedeki, ko mu gihe cyanjye, Yehova NDI UWONDI Ukomeye, nahishuriye Yakobo ko urwego rwa Melikisedeki rwagombaga kugabanywamo imirimo itatu ndetse hakurikijwe Amagambo yanditswe muri Yasheri kugira ngo mumenye ukuri kw'Amagambo yanjye: Kandi Rubeni yagiriye ishyari nyina Leya kubera ibyo, yuzura umujinya, ahagurukana umujinya we aragenda yinjira mu ihema rya Biluha nuko akuramo uburiri bwa se. Icyo gihe umugabane w'uburenganzira bw'imfura, hamwe n'imirimo y'ubwami n'ubutambyi, byakuwe ku bana ba Rubeni, kuko yari yahumanije uburiri bwa se, kandi uburenganzira bw'imfura buhabwa Yozefu, umurimo w'ubwami uhabwa Yuda, naho ubutambyi buhabwa Lewi, kuko Rubeni yari yahumanije uburiri bwa se.

Kandi niko byari bimeze kandi niko byanditswe ko inshingano zikubiye mu Rwego rwa Melikisedeki zashyizwe ku ruhande kugira ngo hashyirweho icyerekezo cy'Isezerano rya Mose, iryo Sezerano rya Kera ryatanze imirimo itatu itandukanye kandi yihariye kuri izo nshingano zera, bityo Isirayeli yagize umurongo w'Abami, igira n'Umurimo w'ubutambyi bw'Abalewi, igira n'ishuri ry'abahanuzi, ndetse n'ishuri ryakozwe mu ishusho y'ishuri ry'abahanuzi ba kera, kuva mu minsi mbere y'umwuzure.

Bityo inshingano n'amabwiriza by'Umutambyi, Umwami, n'Umuhanuzi byose byanyuze muri Isirayeli hakurikijwe ibisekuru byabo byanditswe mu byanditswe byera kandi mu gihe cyabyo, haza umugabo witwa Yohana wabyawe na Zekariya wari uwo mu muryango w'abatambyi ba Sadoki. Kandi bityo byari bikwiriye ko Yohana Umubatiza asohoza gukiranuka kose ubwo yabatizaga umwana w'intama w'Imana mu ruzi rwa Yorodani aho Yesu yabatijwe, agahita ava mu mazi. Kandi dore, ijuru riramukingukira, Yohana abona umwuka w'Imana amanuka asa n'inuma, amujyaho. Kandi dore, ijwi riva mu ijuru, riti, Uyu ni Umwana wanjye nkunda, nkamwishimira cyane.

Kandi bityo Yesu w'i Nazareti yimitswe kuba umutambyi iteka ryose mu Rwego rwa Melikisedeki kandi imirimo yose yagarujwe munsi y'inshingano z'ubu butambyi bw'iteka, haba none n'iteka ryose.

Kuko Urwego rwa Melikisedeki ari urwego rw'abatambyi, urwo nshingano rutazava kuri Yeshua HaMashiach, kuko yazutse kugira ngo atazongera gupfa ukundi!

Kuko Urwego rwa Melikisedeki ari urwego rw'Abami rwo gutegeka mu gukiranuka ibyo naremye.

Kuko Urwego rwa Melikisedeki ari urwego rw'Abahanuzi bavuga kandi bakigisha amateka, inzira, n'ahazaza h'imigambi ya Yehova yo gutunganya yatanzwe mu maso ha Yesu Kristo.

NDI YHVH, ndetse NDI UWONDI Ukomeye kandi nohereje aya Magambo binyuze ku ntumwa yanjye n'umugaragu wanjye kandi binyuze ku ntumwa yanjye n'umugaragu wanjye aya Magambo yoherejwe. Mu by'ukuri, ku ntumwa yanjye n'umugaragu wanjye ukorera munsi ya Kristo Yesu Umwami we w'iteka kandi binyuze mu mwuka w'ubuhanuzi Yehova na Kristo Yesu bahishuriye intumwa yanjye n'umugaragu wanjye Ubutumwa Bwiza bw'Iteka kugira ngo abantu bose bumvire kandi bakizwe aya Magambo yoherejwe. Kandi Amagambo y'Ubutumwa Bwiza bw'Iteka azagera ku maso, amatwi, n'imitima y'abo yohererejwe bose.

NDI YHVH, ndetse n'uwaremye, akora kandi akohereza Umwana wanjye w'ikinege, ari we Yeshua HaMashiach ari we Yesu Mesiya kugira ngo ababyawe na Adamu bose bakizwe kandi bemere gucungurwa byuzuye niba abahamagawe bitabye umukiza wabo iyo abahamagaye. Ku bw'ibyo, mwumve ko ari njye, YHVH, NDI UWONDI Ukomeye uhishurira Yesu w'i Nazareti abazimiye, ndetse kugira ngo Umwungeri wabo Mukuru ashobore gushaka no kugarura intama zanjye zazimiye zose mu bigega byanjye by'umutekano n'ubuhungiro iteka n'iteka ryose.

NDI YHVH, ndetse n'uwagabanyije ubutambyi bw'iteka bwa Melikisedeki mu mirimo itatu itandukanye kandi yihariye y'Umwami, Umutambyi, n'Umuhanuzi munsi ya Yakobo mu bihe bye.

NDI YHVH, ndetse n'uwagaruye Ubutambyi bw'iteka bwa Melikisedeki muri Kristo Yesu, uwo byanditsweho ngo: Kuko bigaragara neza ko, Umwami wanyu yakomotse muri Yuda, umuryango Mose atagize icyo avugaho, ku byerekeye Ubutambyi. Kandi ni ikintu kigaragara neza kurushaho, kuko nyuma y'ishusho ya Melikisedeki, hadutse undi Mutambyi, utagizwe umutambyi hakurikijwe amategeko y'itegeko rya kamere rya kera, ahubwo hakurikijwe imbaraga z'ubuzima butagira iherezo. Kuko ahamya atya, Uri Umutambyi iteka ryose, mu rwego rwa Melikisedeki. Kuko itegeko ryabanjirije, ryakuweho, kubera intege nke zaryo, no kutagira umumaro kwaryo.

Kuko Amategeko nta cyo yatunganije rwose, ahubwo kuzana ibyiringiro byiza kurushaho byaratunganije, ibyo bituma twegera Yehova. Kandi kubera ko bitabaye nta ndahiro (kuko aba bagizwe Abatambyi nta ndahiro: Ariko uyu yagizwe we n'indahiro y'uwamubwiye ati, Yehova yarahiye, kandi ntazicuza, Uri Umutambyi iteka ryose, mu rwego rwa Melikisedeki) Kubera ibyo Yesu yagizwe umwishingizi w'Isezerano ryiza kurushaho. Kandi muri bo benshi bagizwe Abatambyi, kuko batashoboraga gukomeza, kubera urupfu rwabo. Ariko uyu mugabo, kuko akomeza iteka ryose, afite Ubutambyi, budashobora kuva ku wundi bujya ku wundi. Ku bw'ibyo, ashobora no gukiza rwose abegera Imana binyuze kuri we, kuko ahoraho iteka, kugira ngo abasabire.

Ku bw'ibyo, mbahaye kumva Urwego rw'Iteka rwa Melikisedeki n'ukuri kwa Yeshua HaMashiach, ari we kandi Yesu Kristo kuko munsi y'inshingano ye y'iteka y'umutambyi mukuru mu Rwego rwa Melikisedeki Kristo Yesu azimika kandi yeze umutwe ukomeye w'abami n'abatambyi mu Rwego rwa Melikisedeki kandi bityo Ubwami bwo mu Ijuru buzategekwa n'ukuboko kw'iteka kw'Umwami wabo w'Iteka wo Gukiranuka, uwo bazategeka munsi ye mu gukiranuka no mu kwera nyakuri iteka ryose, kuko ku byerekeye Ubwami bwa Kristo Yesu, nta herezo ribaho.
