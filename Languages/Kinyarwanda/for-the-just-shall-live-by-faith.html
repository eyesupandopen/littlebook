Ubuhamya Bubiri: Itorero
Mu Mirimo y'Intumwa Pawulo
Mata 27, 2024

Kuko umukiranutsi azabeshwaho no kwizera!

Kuko umukiranutsi azabeshwaho no kwizera! Kanguka, kandi ukangure abavandimwe bawe, torero ryanjye ry'i Lawodikiya ry'akazuyazi, ryibagiwe amabanga yanjye; kuko isaha yakuze kandi igihe kigiye gushira. Nimuze aho ndi, bana banjye, mufite amaboko ashabashabye n'umutima ufunguye (kuko amaboko yanjye n'umutima wanjye bibafunguriwe); nimuze aho ndi mufite umutima woroshye kandi witeguye kwakira amagambo yanjye. Kuko se umuvandimwe wanjye Yakobo yavuze iki? Nuko rero mwiyambure imyanda yose n'igisa n'igicumuro cyose, mwakirane ubugwaneza ijambo ryatewe muri mwe, ribasha gukiza ubugingo bwanyu. Bana banjye, mumbere abigishwa, kuko ndi umugwaneza kandi noroheje mu mutima, kandi mwakirane ubugwaneza Ijambo ryatewe, nashyize hagati yanyu kugira ngo mugire amaso areba n'amatwi yumva. Muhindukirize imitima yanyu kuri jye, kuko ndi inzira, ukuri, n'ubugingo, kandi nta wujya kwa Data keretse anyuzeho, kuko uko ari ko ubushake bwa Data bugaragazwa neza, kandi niko yabigennye.

Kuki mureka imitima yanyu igahagarika umutima, bana banjye? Kuki mukomeza gutera umugongo amagambo yanjye mukayobya abandi mu ikosa ryanyu? Kuko mwe, torero ryanjye ry'i Lawodikiya, mwibagiwe abo muri bo muri jye, Kristo Yesu, kandi mwatakaje amabanga yanjye, nubwo nayabashinze. Kuko ndi Umwami wabaguze amaraso yanjye bwite; ndi Umwami w'abami n'Umutware w'abatware warazwe isi na Data, kandi nzasangira umurage wanjye n'abizerwa n'abatoranyijwe; nzasangira umurage wanjye namwe, Mugeni wanjye! Ntimutinye rero, kuko nubwo muri abakene, muri abakire muri jye, kuko Data yashyize byose mu maboko yanjye. Yewe, torero ry'i Lawodikiya, wahindutse umugore utagira kwizera w'ibwumvikane bucye.

Oya, ntimwita ku byokurya bikomeye by'ijambo ryanjye, kandi ntimunkurikira n'umutima wanyu wose; ntimwasomye mu Migani? Kujijuka kuzana igikundiro, ariko inzira y'abarakare iraruhije. Kandi inzira z'ibwumvikane mwahisemo ziraruhije; inzira zanyu zitagira kwizera si izanjye, kandi mugerageza kugendera mu nzira nabahaye mufite ukuguru kumwe mu nzira ukundi mu mwobo, nyamara mukagira ubushizi bw'amanga bwo kundenganya, jye Yeshua HaMashiach, iyo muguye mu mwobo nk'uko mwayobejwe. Kuko urugendo rwanyu rurimo kugwa kwinshi, kandi ibyiringiro byanjye kuri mwe ni uko kugwa kwanyu kuba nk'ukugwa kw'abakiranutsi: Kuko umukiranutsi agwa karindwi akongera kubyuka: ariko abanyabyaha bazagwa mu byago.

Sinabahamagariye kugwa mu byago, bana banjye, ndetse nawe, torero ryanjye ry'i Lawodikiya, kuko ntarabatererana mu bihe by'ibigeragezo. Kuko dore! Ndacyahagaze ku rugi nkomanga kuko ntazigera nifatanya n'inzira zanyu z'akazuyazi, z'ibwumvikane, ahubwo ndabahamagarira gukiranuka. Ndabahamagarira guhagarara mu gukiranuka, kuko abungeri bayobye babashutse, babayobora mu nzira za Balamu; kandi benshi muri mwe bahanura ibinyoma babitewe n'imyuka ya Yezebeli, ushaka gupfobya amagambo y'abahanuzi banjye b'ukuri kandi yifuza kwica abahanuzi nejeje muri jye, kuko ari jye wahaye abantu impano. Kuko nandikiye Abagalatiya igihe basitaraga nk'uko musitara: Ndibaza, ukuntu mwikuye vuba ku wabahamagaye mu buntu bwa Kristo, mukajya mu rundi Butumwa Bwiza: Rutari urundi. Yego, ongera ubusome, kuko nta rundi Butumwa Bwiza buhari kandi nta rindi zina ryatanzwe abantu bakizwamo keretse iryanjye, Yesu Kristo. Kuki muyobywa mukava kuri jye, bana banjye bato? Ese sinabaha ibyo mukeneye kandi nkabitaho, kuko ndi umwungeri mwiza uhara ubugingo bwe ku bw'intama. Ariko amagambo ntavuze, ayo mwifuza cyane kandi mugakurikira, arabaryoharyohya mu matwi; kuko ibyo byiyumviro byabaye urukundo rwanyu rwa mbere mu nzira zanyu z'ubwibone; kuko mwibikiye ibinyoma nk'aho ari ukuri, kandi mwatakaje gushishoza kose.

Mwibagiwe amateka y'ibanze y'itorero? Mwabaye abanebwe bo kwiga? Ese iyi ngingo ntiyabaryoharyohya mu matwi bihagije ngo mwumve amagambo yanjye kandi muyizere? Kuko umuntu atazatungwa n'umutsima gusa ahubwo azatungwa n'ijambo RYOSE riva mu kanwa k'Imana! Itorero ryanjye ryavutse ku munsi wa Pentekote kandi byanditswe mu Byakozwe n'Intumwa kugira ngo mubigenzure. Kuko itorero ryanjye ryatangiriye muri Isirayeli, i Yudaya, nubwo batumvaga amagambo navugiye muri Petero kuri uwo munsi w'icyubahiro ubwo nasukaga umwuka wera ku batoranyijwe banjye, ubwo bavugaga mu zindi ndimi nk'uko umwuka yabahaga kuvuga! Kuko Petero yabwiye iki imbaga y'abantu i Yerusalemu uwo munsi ubwo yamaburiraga? Kuko isezerano ari iryanyu, n'abana banyu, n'abari kure bose, ndetse n'abo Umwami Imana yacu izahamagara bose. Bana banjye, muri ba bandi bari kure, kuko benshi muri mwe atari aba Isirayeli ku mubiri, ariko nabakuye mu mahanga mu buntu bwanjye kugira ngo muhagarare mu mbabazi zanjye, kuko mwahamagawe.

Ntimwasomye? Ariko noneho muri Kristo Yesu, mwebwe abari kure kera, mwigijwe hafi n'amaraso ya Kristo. Ariko mu mazu yanyu yo gusengeramo yanduye mwitirira izina ryanjye mu bitutsi (nubwo ntazayinjiramo ahubwo mpagarara ku rugi nkomanga), mutekereza ko mwahawe amategeko ya Mose aturutse kuri jye, kandi mu bwibone bwanyu ntimwumva ko Mose yari uwo muri Isirayeli, kandi ko Isirayeli ari yo yahawe Torah ngo iyitondere, inshingano bananiwe; kuko nta muntu n'umwe ushobora kwitondera amategeko ya Mose kubera kwangirika inzoka yateje mu mubiri wanyu, kuko ubumenyi bwayo buri gihe bwangiza, kandi ibihembo by'ibyaha ni urupfu. Kuko Abakorinto bahamya amagambo yanjye: Uwadushoboje kuba abakozi b'Isezerano Rishya, ritari iry'inyuguti, ahubwo iry'umwuka: kuko inyuguti yica, ariko umwuka utanga ubugingo. Mwahamagariwe kugendera mu mwuka, nubwo benshi muri mwe bifuza mu buryo bwayobye inyuguti y'amategeko, atari ayanyu, ahubwo yuzurijwe muri jye.

Munsubize iki: Ese umwuka yaje kuri mwe binyuze mu kuzuza Torah kwanyu? Cyangwa yaje binyuze mu kuzuza Torah kwanjye gukiranuka nka Pasika yanyu itunganye? Kuko ubuhamya bw'Abagalatiya butaka buti iki? Iki gusa ni cyo nshaka kubigiraho, mwahawe umwuka, ku bw'imirimo y'Amategeko, cyangwa ku bwo kumva kw'ukwizera? Kandi Pawulo yongeye kubwira Abagalatiya ati: Sinshaka gupfobya ubuntu bw'Imana: kuko niba gukiranuka kuzanwa n'Amategeko, ubwo Kristo yapfiriye ubusa. Ntimwumva ko mu ntangiriro y'ibihe hariho itorero ry'i Yudaya n'itorero ry'Abanyamahanga? Nubwo itorero ryanjye ryavukiye i Yudaya, Pawulo yabwiye Abagalatiya kandi narabirinze aho: Kuko uwakoreye muri Petero mu butumwa bw'abakebwe, ni we wakoreye muri jye ku Banyamahanga. Kuko mbere nohereje Petero ku Banyamahanga, kandi byanditswe mu Byakozwe n'Intumwa ubwo nabwiraga Petero nti: Ibyo Imana yejeje, ntubyite ibihumanye. Kuko ubwo noherezaga Petero kwa Koruneliyo, ubuhamya bukurikira bwaranditswe: Kandi abakebwe bizeraga, baratangaye, abazanye na Petero bose, kuko no ku Banyamahanga hasutsweho impano y'Umwuka Wera. Ndababaza, mwa Banyalawodikiya mwe, ese Koruneliyo yakiriye agakiza binyuze mu gukurikira Torah (kuko yari umuntu wubaha Imana)? Cyangwa yakiriye isezerano binyuze mu kwizera? Abakorinto bafite ijwi kuri iyi ngingo ubwo Pawulo yabandikiraga ati: Ari na ryo mukirizwa, niba mugumya kwibuka ibyo nababwirije, keretse niba mwarizeye ubusa. Agakiza kazanywe no kubwiriza kw'ukwizera ntabwo ari inyuguti y'amategeko, kuko kwishyira munsi y'amategeko ya Mose, ndetse no kwizera kwanyu kuba kubaye imfabusa.

Nuko rero Abaroma baravuga bati: Ariko noneho gukiranuka kw'Imana kugaragajwe hatariho Amategeko, guhamywa n'Amategeko n'Abahanuzi. Ndetse gukiranuka kw'Imana, kuzanwa no kwizera Yesu Kristo ku bose, no kuri bose bizera: kuko nta tandukaniro: Kuko bose bakoze ibyaha, ntibashyikira ubwiza bw'Imana. Kuko nabwiye itorero ryanjye mu Bagalatiya aho Pawulo yanditse yumvira ati: Kuko muri Kristo Yesu, gukebwa nta cyo kumaze, cyangwa kudakebwa, ahubwo kwizera gukorera mu rukundo. Kandi Pawulo yongeye kwandikira Abagalatiya ati: Kuko muri Kristo Yesu gukebwa nta cyo kumaze cyangwa kudakebwa, ahubwo icyaremwe gishya. Bityo agakiza ntigatangwa n'imirimo y'amategeko, kandi agakiza kanyu ntabwo kabeshwaho cyangwa ngo kagerweho n'imirimo y'amategeko. Kuko Abaroma bandikiwe bati: Dore, nshyize i Siyoni ibuye ryo gusitaraho, n'urutare rwo kugusha: kandi umwizera wese, ntazakorwa n'isoni. Bana banjye, ndi iryo buye ryo gusitaraho n'urutare rwo kugusha, kuko ndi Yesu w'i Nazareti, umwana w'intama w'Imana nyakuri, kandi nujuje amategeko.

Yewe, mwa b'i Lawodikiya mwe, mutekereza ko ari mwe mwe nyine musitara kuri iyi ngingo? Ndavuga nti oya. Kuko mushobora gushyira amabaruwa yanjye mu byiciro bibiri by'ibanze, keretse ko yose yandikiwe umuntu umwe mushya muri Kristo Yesu. Kuko amabaruwa yanjye yandikiwe amatorero y'Abanyamahanga, kandi amwe yandikiwe amatorero y'i Yudaya, bityo Petero yanditse ku byerekeye guhishurirwa kw'Ibanga nahishuriye Pawulo: Nk'uko no mu nzandiko ze zose, avuga muri zo iby'ibyo bintu, birimo ibintu bimwe biruhije kumva, ibyo abaswa n'abahindagurika bagoreka, nk'uko bagoreka n'ibindi Byanditswe, ngo barimbuke.

Bityo ibibazo byanyu si bishya, kuko nta kigeragezo cyabagezeho kitari rusange ku bantu: ariko Imana ni iyo kwizerwa, itazabakundira kugeragezwa ibiruta ibyo mushobora; ahubwo hamwe n'ikigeragezo izabaha n'uburyo bwo kurokoka, kugira ngo mubashe kucyihanganira. Ese muzahitamo inzira yo kurokoka Imana yabahaye mu buntu bwanjye n'imbabazi zanjye kugira ngo murokoke abashaka kubambura ingororano yanyu babatera kugwa mu buntu? Ese muzanga amagambo yanjye muhitemo Abayudaiza bavuga mu bujiji ko mugomba kwitondera Torah kugira ngo mukizwe? Ese muri abapfapfa ku buryo mutekereza ko, mwaratangiye itegeko ry'ubugingo mu mwuka wa Yesu Kristo, noneho mweguriwe itegeko ry'icyaha n'urupfu? Kuko se amategeko bimeze bite? Bana banjye, ndababwira. Ntimwanzure mu bupfapfa ko ndi ijwi ry'ikinyoma ribayobora mu ikosa, kuko benshi muri mwe mutazi ijwi ryanjye kandi ntimunzi, nubwo mbahamagara! Ntimutekereze ko naje gukuraho amategeko cyangwa abahanuzi. Sinaje gukuraho, ahubwo naje kuzuza. Kuko ndababwira ukuri nti, Kugeza aho ijuru n'isi bizashirira, nta nyuguti imwe cyangwa agace k'inyuguti, bizavaho mu mategeko, kugeza aho byose bizuzurizwa.

Ndi uwujuje amategeko ya Mose, kandi amabwiriza yayo yitonderwa mu kumvira amagambo yanjye y'ubuntu n'imbabazi mu nzandiko zanjye no binyuze mu kwizera Yesu Kristo. Mutekereza mu bujiji ko kubera ko ndi ishyirwa mu bikorwa ry'amategeko nshyigikira umuntu uwo ari we wese kwica amategeko y'Imana, yaba amategeko ya Mose cyangwa amategeko mbonezamuco y'Imana. Kuko nabwiye iki abatuye i Korinto? Torero ryanjye ry'i Lawodikiya, iryo nkunda, si mwe ba mbere bahanganye n'iki kigeragezo, bityo natumye Pawulo yandika ati: Uwadushoboje kuba abakozi b'Isezerano Rishya, ritari iry'inyuguti, ahubwo iry'umwuka: kuko inyuguti yica, ariko umwuka utanga ubugingo. Ariko niba umurimo w'urupfu wanditswe, kandi ukabazwa mu mabuye, wari ufite ubwiza, ku buryo abana ba Isirayeli batashoboraga gutumbira mu maso ha Mose, kubera ubwiza bwo mu maso he, ubwiza bwagombaga gukurwaho: Umurimo w'umwuka, ntuzarushaho kugira ubwiza? Ntimwibeshye, bana banjye, inyuguti yica kubera icyaha kiri mu mubiri wanyu gicirwa urubanza n'iyo nyuguti, ndetse n'amategeko ya Mose: Bityo amategeko ni ayera, n'itegeko ni iryera kandi rirakiranuka kandi ni ryiza. Ese icyo cyiza, cyambereye urupfu? Imana ibirinde. Ahubwo icyaha, kugira ngo kigaragare ko ari icyaha, cyankoreyemo urupfu binyuze mu cyiza: kugira ngo icyaha binyuze mu Itegeko kibe kibi cyane. Bityo natumye Pawulo abwira Abagalatiya ati: Sinshaka gupfobya ubuntu bw'Imana: kuko niba gukiranuka kuzanwa n'Amategeko, ubwo Kristo yapfiriye ubusa. Bana banjye, mwarayobejwe, kandi narababwiye ko divayi nshya igomba gushyirwa mu mivure nshya, nuko rero muhagarare mushikamye mu buntu jye, Kristo Yesu, nababohoreyemo.

Bana banjye, ntimushobora kwica amategeko y'Imana iyo mugendera mu mwuka, kuko bidashoboka. Mwubatswe ku rufatiro rw'intumwa n'abahanuzi, Yesu Kristo ubwe akaba ibuye rikuru ry'imfuruka. Kandi Abaheburayo babwiwe bati: Kuko nzababarira gukiranirwa kwabo, n'ibyaha byabo n'ibicumuro byabo sinzabyibuka ukundi. Mu kuvuga ngo, isezerano rishya, yagize irya mbere irishaje. Noneho igishaje kandi kigenda gisaza kiba kigiye kuvaho. Bana banjye, gukiranuka ntikuzanwa no kwitondera amategeko, kuko ari impano yakirwa no kwizera jye, Kristo Yesu. Kandi kubera ko mwahinduwe abakiranutsi muri jye, mwabatuwe ku itegeko ry'icyaha n'urupfu.

Kuko ari igitutsi kwanzura ko kugendera mu mwuka w'ubugingo ari icyaha mu buryo runaka, kuko umuntu wese wizera ibyo afata igitambo cyanjye ku bw'ibyaha by'abantu nk'ikintu kidahumanye. Kuko nkuruta Mose, kuko Mose ubwe avuga ati: Umwami Imana yanyu izabahagurukiriza Umuhanuzi muri mwe, mu bavandimwe banyu, umeze nkanjye, ni we muzumvira. Nimwumve ijwi ryanjye, bana, kandi mwumve imvugo yanjye, kuko ntabagize abagome, kuko abana ba Satani ari abagome, atari abana b'Imana! Kuko mu Baroma Pawulo yanditse neza ati: Noneho rero nta gucirwaho iteka ku bari muri Kristo Yesu, batagendera ku mubiri, ahubwo bagendera ku mwuka. Kuko itegeko ry'umwuka w'ubugingo, muri Kristo Yesu, ryambatuwe ku itegeko ry'icyaha n'urupfu.

Kuko icyo amategeko atashoboraga gukora, kuko yari intege nke kubera umubiri, Imana yohereje Umwana wayo bwite, mu ishusho y'umubiri w'icyaha, kandi ku bw'icyaha icira icyaha urubanza mu mubiri: Kugira ngo gukiranuka kw'amategeko kuzuzwe muri twe, tutagendera ku mubiri, ahubwo tugendera ku mwuka. Kandi Abaroma bongera kuvuga bati: Kugira ngo nerekane, ndavuga, muri iki gihe gukiranuka kwe: kugira ngo abe umukiranutsi, kandi atsindishirize uwizera Yesu. Kwirata kuri he rero? Kwarakuweho. Ku bw'irihe Tegeko? Ry'imirimo? Oya: ahubwo ku bw'Itegeko ryo kwizera. Bityo rero, twanzuye ko, umuntu atsindishirizwa no kwizera, hatariho imirimo y'Amategeko. Kuko navuze iki: Kuko muri ryo gukiranuka kw'Imana guhishurwa kuva mu kwizera kujya mu kwizera; nk'uko byanditswe ngo, Umukiranutsi azabeshwaho no kwizera.

Nyamara benshi muri mwe mwikoreza bagenzi banyu imitwaro idakwiye kuko mwizeye abahanuzi ba Satani boherejwe kubatega mutekereza ko mugomba kwitondera Torah nk'ibisabwa kugira ngo mukizwe kandi mubeshweho n'agakiza; kandi bamwe muri mwe bavuga mu bitutsi ko nujuje amategeko binyuze mu kwitamba kwanjye kugira ngo ubu mushobore kwitondera Torah. Ku ruhande rumwe, muri mu kuri, kuko kugendera mu itegeko ry'umwuka w'ubugingo ari ukwitondera Torah mu buryo butunganye binyuze mu kwifatanya nanjye; kuko itorero ryanjye ryubatswe rikomeye mu gukiranuka no kwera nyakuri kuri jye, Kristo Yesu, ibuye rikuru ry'imfuruka. Mwitondera Torah binyuze mu kumvira ukwizera kuri muri jye. Kuki muhitamo ijwi ry'Abayudaiza aho guhitamo ijwi ryanjye ryera? Kuki mwikoreza abavandimwe banyu imitwaro idakwiye, kuko ntimwasomye? Kuko namaze kubwira Abakolosayi nti: Nuko rero ntihakagire umuntu ubacira urubanza ku byo murya, cyangwa ibyo munywa, cyangwa ku byerekeye umunsi Mukuru uwo ari wo wese, cyangwa ukwezi Gushya, cyangwa iminsi y'Isabato Biri igicucu cy'ibizaza, ariko umubiri ni uwa Kristo.

Bana banjye, sincira amategeko urubanza; ngaragaza gusa ibigaragara ko kubera icyaha kiri mu mubiri wanyu mudashobora na rimwe kwitondera Torah keretse binyuze mu kwizera jye, kuko umukiranutsi azabeshwaho no kwizera! Kwizera jye ni iki, mushobora kubaza? Kwizera jye byubatswe ku kwiringira amagambo yanjye no kumva ko mwifatanyije nanjye ku nzego zose, kuko muri itorero ryanjye nuhagiye mu maraso yanjye bwite atagira icyaha; kuko kubabarirwa ibyaha bidashoboka kugerwaho binyuze mu bitambo by'amatungo. Munyizere, kuko binyuze mu kwizera jye Torah yitonderwa, binyuze mu kwizera jye amategeko yuzurizwa muri mwe, kandi muri abakiranutsi, mwatsindishirijwe, kandi mwejejwe! Igihe nazukiraga ubugingo bushya, namwe mwazutse; kandi igihe nazamukaga mu ijuru iburyo bwa Data, namwe mwicajwe mu byo mu ijuru. Kuko Abefeso bahamya bati: Ariko Imana ikungahaye ku mbabazi, ku bw'urukundo rwayo rwinshi yadukunze, Ndetse n'ubwo twari dupfuye mu byaha, yaduhinduranye bazima na Kristo (mwaheshejwe agakiza n'ubuntu), Kandi yatuzuranye na we, itwicazanya na we mu byo mu ijuru muri Kristo Yesu. Mugomba kumva ko amategeko ya Mose atari isezerano ryanyu, kuko iryanyu ari isezerano rishya ryemejwe mu maraso yanjye kandi ryishyurwa n'imibabaro yanjye.

Nuko rero mwumvire Ukwizera, bantu banjye, kandi ntimukomeze kuba akazuyazi. Muhitemo ubugingo muri jye, Yesu Kristo, kuko ari ko mwahamagawe. Mukore imirimo myinshi ikomeye nabahaye, ndetse no muri uyu munota wa nyuma. Ndashaka ko mwumva ko mutamfite, nta cyo mushobora gukora, kandi mwitondera ibice byose bya Torah binyuze mu kwizera jye. Byongeye kandi, Abayudaiza b'izina ryera buzuye mu itorero ryanjye, bikoreza imitwaro idakwiye abashaka agakiza n'abari abanjye. Data yampaye izina riruta amazina yose, kugira ngo mu izina rya Yesu Kristo amavi yose apfukame. Mutekereza ko nakwikoreza umutwaro udakwiye amahanga avuga indimi nyinshi gukoresha izina ryanjye, Yeshua HaMashiach, mu Giheburayo gusa? Munyumve kandi munyumve neza, kuko ndaje, kandi nzabaza ibazwa abatumvira n'abashaka kubahagarika umutima.
