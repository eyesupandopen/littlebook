1 Gashyantare 2025

Ikoraniro Rikomeye ry'Abizerwa muri Kristo

Umva Amagambo y'Umwami wawe Uhoraho, itorero ryanjye, umubiri wanjye, ndetse n'umugeni wanjye, kandi mutege amatwi mu kumvira Amagambo ya Yeshua HaMashiach. Mwumve ijwi ryanjye kandi muze ku Mwami wanyu n'imitima yiteguye kwakira Amagambo yanjye, kugira ngo ihumure ribazeho mu buryo bwuzuye, kuko ibihe by'ihumure n'incungu yuzuye bibagezeho bakunzi banjye, nuko rero mwitegure!

Uko biri, Jyewe Yesu w'i Nazareti, ari we kandi Yeshua HaMashiach mu rurimi rw'Igiheburayo, ndaje ngo mbategure bakunzi banjye kuko nta gihe gisigaye kandi ikoraniro ry'abizerwa muri Kristo rigiye gutangira mu buryo butunguranye kandi butitezwe none. Nuko rero ntimutinye bakunzi banjye, kandi mumenye ko imanza z'abantu banze imiburo yanjye atari izanyu, kandi muzakizwa rwose umujinya ugiye kuza! Ni ukuri, ni ukuri, kuko mwizeye kandi mukakira agakiza n'imbabazi z'ibyaha mu kuvuka gushya hakurikijwe Isezerano Rishya ryanjye mu bihe by'ubuntu n'imbabazi, mukijijwe rwose umujinya ugiye kuza!

Nyamara, niko bimeze, ko imitima myinshi idahama kandi itari inyakuri ivuga ibinyoma mu buryarya kandi ikagoreka Amagambo yanjye ngo yizanire urubanza n'irimbuka, abantu binyuze mu buriganya no kwirata k'umubiri bavuga ko ari imbaraga zikomeye za Yahweh, kandi bakayobya abumva bose n'abumvira ibinyoma byabo bivuye mu mitima yuzuye uburiganya. Nuko rero, mumenye amasezerano yanjye nahishuriye Pawulo ubwo namwigishaga Ibanga Rikomeye ry'Imana ryahishuwe byuzuye mu nzandiko ze, ibanga abenshi muri mwe mutarasobanukirwa. Nyamara, yatanze ubuhamya kandi ahamya iby'umujinya wa Yahweh: Ariko Imana yerekanye urukundo rwayo idukunda, ubwo Kristo yadupfiraga tukiri abanyabyaha. None rero, ubwo tumaze gutsindishirizwa n'amaraso ye, nkanswe tutazakizwa umujinya ku bwe. Kuko ubwo twari abanzi, twiyunze n'Imana ku bwo gupfa k'Umwana wayo, nkanswe ubwo tumaze kwiyunga, tutazakizwa n'ubugingo bwe!

Yemwe abafite kwizera guke, nzabihanganira kugeza ryari, bakunzi banjye? Nuko rero ndaje ngo mbahumurize kuko ntazigera mbasiga cyangwa ngo mbahane, ndetse namwe mwese muri muri Kristo, kandi ni ukuri nzabana namwe iteka ryose kuko muzabana nanjye iteka kandi muzaba nkanjye iteka. Sela.

Nuko rero, mumenye ko abashaka kubaho bubaha Imana bose bazarenganywa mu buryo bumwe cyangwa ubundi, kandi ya ntare itagira amenyo iracyazerera ishaka uwo yaconshomera, nuko rero muyirwanye rwose izabahunga. Icyakora, amarembo y'ikuzimu ntazanesha Itorero ryanjye cyangwa abizerwa mu Itorero ryanjye bumvira ijwi ryanjye kandi mpa imbaraga zikomeye binyuze mu mwuka wanjye w'Ubwami waremwe muri bose bangize Umwami bafite umutima wuzuye kwizera, kandi mushyiriweho ikimenyetso cy'Imana Ihoraho yo Gukiranuka, ari we Yahweh NDI UWONDI Ukomeye, kandi icyo kimenyetso ntigishobora kumenwa kandi nta n'ubwo mushobora gushikuzwa mu maboko yanjye, nubwo babeshya!

Nuko rero, mumenye ko mukijijwe umujinya, kandi mukijijwe umujinya wose. Nuko mumenye ko umujinya w'Umwana w'Intama utagenewe abumvira, ahubwo ari uwo gutangiza urubanza rwa nyuma ku baguye n'abifatanya nabo bose cyangwa abagumana nabo, no kuzanira agakiza ka nyuma abatumvira n'abarinangiye bazumvira Ubutumwa Bwiza Buhoraho mu bihe byabo by'amakuba n'ibyago kuko birengagije imiburo yanjye mu bihe by'imiburo ubu byarangiye, kandi mu mwanya w'imiburo, ubu hasigaye amatangazo!! Nyamara, abizerwa bose muri Kristo, ndetse n'abana bose ba Yahweh barerwa byuzuye nk'abana b'iteka kandi ubu bakaba baratewe ku giti cy'Umunzenze karemano cya Isirayeli y'Imana aho ukwuzura kose kuba, ntibagengwa n'umujinya w'Umwana w'Intama, cyangwa umujinya wa Yahweh, Sela!

Kuko umujinya w'Umwana w'Intama uza mbere kandi uzagwa nabi kandi uremereye ku batumvira n'abahemu bo mu nzu y'Imana, baba aba Isirayeli cyangwa amahanga. Kuko iyi ari imbabazi yatanzwe mu masezerano ya kera ikomeza gusezeranywa kandi yashyiriweho gusohozwa ibihe byose n'iteka ryose mu Isezerano Rishya ryanjye ryo gusohozwa kwuzuye! Umujinya w'Umwana w'Intama uza ubwo mfungura ibimenyetso ku muzingo wanjye w'umurage, ndetse n'icyangombwa cy'isi ndetse n'umurage wanjye ukomeye nahawe mu maboko yanjye akomeye na Yahweh Ubwe ngo mbusangire n'abaneshi banjye kuko nanesheje muri byose! Abahamya babiri bazahaguruka mu gihe cyabo kandi bazakora ibitangaza bikomeye, kandi bazahishura amabanga menshi, kandi bazatanga ubuhamya bwinshi bwa kera mu bihe byabo byagenwe byo guhishurwa, kandi bazategeka ibyago byinshi, ibitangaza, n'ibimenyetso binyuze mu Magambo n'amategeko y'imitima yabo yuzuye kwizera n'akanwa kabo kuzuye imbaraga! Sela.

Kandi, muri iki gihe byahawe ikiyoka, inyamaswa iva mu nyanja, n'umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma kurwanya abera no kubanesha. Nyamara, abadakunda amagara yabo kugeza ku gupfa n'abambaza izina rya Yesu bose kugeza ku iherezo ryabo bazabona agakiza k'iteka n'umurage ukomeye w'iteka uburemere bwawo budashobora kugereranywa n'imibabaro bazihanganira ku bw'izina ryanjye. Kandi Jyewe, Yeshua HaMashiach, nzayobora abantu banjye 144,000 bakomeye ngo batabare Yakobo, ndetse na rya shami rikomeye rizemera ko ndi Mesiya wabo, ndetse n'abava muri Isirayeli bose bakomoka mu mirongo y'amaraso y'isezerano kandi bazatandukanywa bajyanwe ari bazima mu butayu ahantu bateguriwe na Yahweh aho bazaguma bihishwe mu mutekano kure y'umubabaro ukomeye uzigera uba ku isi, kandi ni ukuri ibihe by'amakuba ntibizigera byongera kuba ku isi ukundi, kuko niko Yahweh, NDI UWONDI Ukomeye, yabitangaje!

Nuko rero, mumenye Amagambo yanjye kandi mwumve ijwi ryanjye bakunzi banjye, kandi mumenye ko mukijijwe umujinya ugiye kuza, nubwo mwese mukiri ku isi kandi mwese mukaba mugirwana intambara nyinshi zikomeje muri ibi bihe bya nyuma by'intambara y'imbuto yo mu Itangiriro 3:15 igikomeje kugeza n'uyu munsi hagati ya Yahweh, n'ijuru, na satani bigaruriye abantu babi ku isi ngo bakore ibyo bashaka binyuze mu gukunda amafaranga n'ibinyoma byinshi biyobya kandi bitanga amasezerano amaherezo biyobora mu nyanja y'umuriro aho inyo yabo idapfa. Nyamara, narabanesheje kandi intambara zose zizahagarara ku mbaraga zikomeye n'ukuboko gukomeye kw'Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda, ubwo nzaza mu gukiranuka guca imanza no kurwana! Sela.

Yego, mukijijwe umujinya kandi amarembo y'ikuzimu ntazabanesha! Nuko rero, mwitange kuri Kristo Yesu Umwami wanyu w'iteka, ari we wabacunguye mu kuboko kw'umwanzi, yego mwitange ku Mwami wanyu kuko ibihe byo kumurikwa kwanyu mu ijuru byegereje. Ndetse n'igihe cy'ibyiringiro by'iteka cyari gihishwe mu mutima wa Yahweh ubu cyahishuwe byuzuye, ndetse n'igihe isezerano ry'iteka n'ibyiringiro bihura n'isohozwa no kumurikwa kuko, Jyewe, Kristo Yesu, ndetse n'Umwami wanyu Uhoraho wo Gukiranuka nzamurika abizerwa muri Kristo imbere ya Yahweh, NDI UWONDI Ukomeye, mu cyumba cye cy'intebe y'ubwami mu ijuru ry'amajuru.

Kandi NZAMURIKA abumvira, badafite ikizinga cyangwa inenge, cyangwa ikizinga, cyangwa ikindi kintu nk'icyo ahubwo nzabambika mwese byuzuye imyenda yera, itagira inenge y'igitare kandi niko amazu yanyu azaguma iteka ryose, kuko imyenda, imyambaro, n'amazu byose byerekeza ku mibiri yanyu yazutse kandi itunganye ku buryo butunguranye ubu muzisanga muhagazemo iteka ryose ubwo nzarema bundi bushya abizerwa muri Kristo mu kanya nk'ako guhumbya kuri urwo rusaku rukomeye rwa Marayika mukuru kandi iryo jwi ryiza ry'impanda y'iherezo rizavuga, rihagarika ku mugaragaro ibihe byanjye by'Ubuntu n'imbabazi, bizashyira iherezo ryuzuye ku bihe by'abanyamahanga, nubwo, abambaza izina rya Yesu w'i Nazareti bose kugeza ku iherezo bazakizwa rwose.

Nuko rero ndaje ngo nshyire ahagaragara ababateza amakuba, ndetse n'abo bose benshi muri mwe mugiha agaciro amagambo yabo y'uburozi, ndetse n'amagambo y'ibinyoma yatanzwe mu buriganya buvuye mu mitima mibi iyoborwa n'irari ry'imibiri yabo. Ndetse n'abagoreka Amagambo atunganye y'ubutumwa bwiza bwanjye n'imigani n'inkuru zidashira ari ibinyoma, bigenewe rwose kurimbura abakwizera ayo magambo atagira kwizera bose. Bakunzi banjye, mushyire ibitekerezo byanyu byose kuri njye n'Ijambo ryanjye kuko ari njye gukomera kw'ibihe byanyu n'imbaraga z'agakiza, kandi ni nde washobora guhagarara imbere y'Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda!?

Dore! Narinze Amagambo yanjye atunganye mu murongo w'Inyandiko za Byzantine kandi Jyewe Yeshua HaMashiach, hamwe na Adonai Yahweh, twahishuye Amagambo yacu mu Butumwa Bwiza Buhoraho, ndetse no muri cya Gitabo Gito cyo Gukiranuka cyongeye gushyiraho ukuri kwinshi mu bizerwa muri Kristo, nuko rero, mugereranye mubyitondeye buri jambo rivuye ku muntu uwo ari we wese uvuga ko avugira Yeshua HaMashiach, cyangwa Yahweh, NDI UWONDI Ukomeye n'Amagambo yanjye atunganye ari mu byanditswe kandi mwige kugira ngo mwiyerekane ko mwemewe!

Kuko ubuhanuzi bwose bwatanzwe na Yeshua HaMashiach cyangwa Adonai Yahweh, ntibuzigera buvuguruza ukuri kwashyizweho kwahishuwe mu bihe bya kera cyangwa mu bihe bya none. Mwamagane ibinyoma byabo kandi mumenye ko abizerwa muri Kristo batazihanganira umwijima w'icuraburindi nk'ibyago byo mu Misiri, kandi ntibazaba bari ku isi ubwo ikuzimu hazakingurwa, kandi Itorero ryanjye ntirizaneshwa n'amarembo y'ikuzimu, ngo bihishe mu mazu yabo nk'ibigwari bidafite imbaraga ku bibi! Mumenye, gutuka Imana kw'abavuga ibi bintu bose ntikuzabura guhembwa ku banga kwihana ubwo bupfu bose.

Mumenye kandi, abantu 144,000 batoranyijwe na Yahweh mbere y'ishingwa ry'isi ku bw'igihe nk'iki. Babumbirwe mu makuba kandi bose bafite ubushobozi n'imbaraga zihariye. Bazatwarwa mu kuzamurwa kw'abizerwa ariko bashyizwe ukwabo ku bw'imigambi ya Yeshua HaMashiach kandi bazashyirwaho ikimenyetso mu ruhanga rwabo mu ijuru. Baza nyuma ku isi mu gihe cy'umubabaro ukomeye ngo bayobore Isirayeli mu mutekano. Ariko mbere batwarwa gushyingiranwa n'Umukwe wabo kuko ari Umugeni wanjye, abahamagawe, abatoranyijwe, abizerwa, n'abanyakuri. Ndetse n'abo Jyewe, Kristo Yesu, nagize abera nk'isugi mu maraso yanjye y'impongano. Nuko rero, umuntu wese uvuga ibinyuranye n'aya Magambo ntanzi, kandi ntavugira njye! Sela!

Nuko rero mumenye, ko amarembo y'ikuzimu ntazanesha abizerwa muri Kristo ari bo Torero ryanjye ry'ukuri kuko niko byanditswe kandi bigasezeranywa kera, kandi niko bikyanditswe kandi bigasezeranywa mu bihe bya none, kuko Amagambo ya Yahweh na Yeshua HaMashiach ari amagambo atunganye nk'ifeza, yageragejwe mu ruganda rwo ku isi, atunganyijwe karindwi. Yego, abaguye nta mbaraga bafite ku bizerwa muri Kristo kandi nzaha abizerwa banjye gukandagira munsi y'ibirenge byabo ikintu cyose cy'ubwami bw'abanzi, ndetse no gukandagira abashaka guhagarika ibidashobora guhagarikwa.

Nuko rero, ndabategeka kugira kwizera mu Mwami wanyu Uhoraho kandi mwemerere Imana Ihoraho yo Gukiranuka kubuzuza imbaraga z'imbere imbere y'Umwami wanyu, Kristo Yesu! Mugende mufite kwizera gukomeye muri Yeshuah HaMashiach, ari we Yesu w'i Nazareti kuko nabahaye imbaraga kugira ngo mukandagire munsi y'ibirenge byanyu ibintu byose by'ubwami bw'abanzi. Nuko rero, mumenye, ko ndetse n'abamarayika baguye bari munsi y'ibirenge byanjye mu gutsindwa kwuzuye, kandi muramutse muhuye na Satani ubwe mwakenera gusa gusubiza nk'uko Mikayeli yasubije ubwo yahariraga umubiri wa Mose, avuga mu kwizera gukomeye ati, Umwami agucyahe Satani! Kandi nzacyaha, kandi mbohe uwo ari we wese mu baguye washaka kubagirira nabi cyangwa kubabuza gukora ubushake bw'uwabatumye, muramutse mukomeje kugenda mu budahemuka mu kwizera uko muvuga Amagambo nzabaha mu kwizera.

Nuko rero ndabategeka bakunzi banjye kwitegura, kuko kugenda kwanyu kwegereje! Kandi ndaje ngo mbaburire kuguma mutandukanye na Babuloni y'Ibanga muri byose no mu buryo bwinshi uko nzabayobora ku bw'ukwezwa no gutandukanywa kwanyu. Mwumve Amagambo y'Ubutumwa Bwiza Buhoraho kandi mwemerere ukuri kwanjye kose kwahishuwe muri ibi bihe byo guhishurwa kuba itara ry'ibirenge byanyu kugira ngo inzira yanyu imurikirwe, kuko hari inzira imwe gusa, ndetse n'iyo nzira ifunganye kandi ntoya iyobora ku bugingo buhoraho muri Kristo Yesu Umwami wanyu w'iteka!

Mwitegure, mugeni wanjye kandi mungureho amavuta kugira ngo amatara yanyu yuzuzwe! Amavuta yanjye aboneka mu Magambo yanjye rukundo rwanjye, ndetse no mu Magambo nyine murimo gusoma ubu kandi nabahaye amagambo menshi yo kubahumuriza, kubarinda, no kubayobora mu gukiranuka kose aho uwo mubi atabakoraho!

Mwitegure, bakunzi banjye, kuko buri gihe ari igihe cyo gukata intambi zanyu, kandi muzakata intambi zanyu binyuze mu kwihana ibyaha no kwera imbuto z'umwuka uko munesha imirimo y'umubiri binyuze mu mwuka wanjye wera wavukiye muri mwe byuzuye, kandi udashobora gukurwaho iyo umaze gushinga imizi mu mitima y'abantu mu kwera kose no gukiranuka. Mukomeze imitima yanyu buri gihe yoroshye ku rukundo rwanjye n'amabwiriza, kugira ngo murindwe rwose kandi mukingirwe ibibi byose mu kiganza cy'amaboko yanjye y'iteka.

Mwitegure, Yemwe Bizerwa muri Kristo, kandi mumenye ukwegera kw'isaha ya nyuma ubwo ikoraniro ry'abizerwa muri Kristo rizaba, ribatwara mwese mu mutekano wuzuye n'ingororano by'ijuru ubu byiteguye gukwira isi mu kuboneka kwanjye kw'icyubahiro. Uko icyubahiro cyanjye kimurika nk'umurabyo uva iburasirazuba ukagera iburengerazuba, benshi muri mwe bazabona ukumirabagirana kwo kuza kwanjye.

Hanyuma, ni ukuri, ni ukuri, ubwo urusaku rukomeye rwa Marayika mukuru ruzumvikana ku isi yose, uko iryo jwi rikomeye ry'impanda rizavuzwa ku isi yose, abapfiriye muri Kristo bazavuka bava mu isi n'imbaraga n'ubushobozi bikomeye; hanyuma mwebwe abazaba bakiriho basigaye muzazamurwa hamwe nabo gusanganira Umwami wanyu Uhoraho wo Gukiranuka mu kirere, kandi muzahita mwisanga mwambaye icyubahiro cyuzuye na kamere nziza cyane kandi y'icyubahiro, muhagaze mu ijuru ry'amajuru. Muzabona byuzuye, muzumva byuzuye, muzamenya nk'uko namwe mumenywe, kandi benshi muri mwe bazongera guhura n'ababo bakunda kandi bose bazanezerwa!

Mwebwe abazaba bakiriho basigaye muzasobanukirwa ibirori muzisangamo kandi muzumva Amagambo ngo "Zamuka Hano!" kandi muzumva mukuruwe n'imbaraga kandi vuba hejuru mu kumirabagirana kw'icyubahiro cyanjye kandi ibise by'ivuka bizahagarara uko abana b'iteka ba Yahweh bavukira mu ijuru aho bazahagarara mu cyubahiro gikomeye n'ingororano y'iteka hakurikijwe uko ubudahemuka bwabo bwabaye.

Ni Jyewe, Kristo Yesu, Umwami Uhoraho wo Gukiranuka wohereje aya Magambo binyuze ku ntumwa yanjye n'umugaragu wanjye ukomeye, kandi binyuze ku ntumwa yanjye n'umugaragu wanjye ukomeye Amagambo yanjye yoherejwe, kugira ngo abizerwa muri Kristo bategurwe byuzuye kandi bategereze ukwegera kw'Umukwe wabo, ubu wegereye umugeni we! Sela.

Mwitegure, Yemwe Bizerwa muri Kristo, kuko isaha y'incungu yanyu yuzuye igiye kubatwara mu buryo butunguranye kandi butitezwe ikabakura mu makuba yose azagwa ku isi mu rubanza rukomeye kandi ruteye ubwoba kuko ibihe by'urubanza byageze kandi bigomba gusohozwa, gusa mwitandukanye byuzuye muri byose mu ijambo no mu gikorwa n'abatagira kwizera kugira ngo mutazagabana mu manza zabo, cyangwa ngo mugabane mu byago byabo.

Mwitegure, Mugeni wanjye, kandi muze kuri njye byuzuye mu kwihana kwuzuye kandi muve mu bibi byose kugira ngo amatara yanyu yuzure kandi intambi zanyu zikatwe kandi kugira ngo mutegurirwe Umukwe wanyu uza rwose kandi isaha y'urugendo rwanjye yegereje gusoza! Sela!

Ku itorero ryanjye, umubiri wanjye, ndetse n'umugeni wanjye, ndavuga nti ibihe by'incungu yanyu yuzuye byageze, nuko rero, muntegereze kandi mwitegure kandi mukomeze ubuhamya nbahaye kuko ubu buhamya buzababeshaho kandi bukabakurinda uwo mubi uza rwose kubarwanya n'urwango rukomeye n'umujinya utabagenewe. Mumenye, ko ncyaha kandi nzitira abaguye kuruta uko uwo ari we wese muri mwe abitekereza kandi barazitiwe kugeza ubwo umuzitizi wanjye akuweho. Nuko rero, mwitandukanye n'imbaga yaciriwe urubanza kandi ntimwifatanye mu makoraniro yabo kuko bamaze kuzana akaduruvayo n'irimbuka ku bazahabwa byuzuye urubanza mu buryo butunguranye uko umuzitizi wanjye akuweho. Nuko rero, ntimutinye ibintu bavuga, cyangwa ngo mutinye ibintu bakora kandi ntimutinye ibintu bateganya kandi birata ko bazakora rwose, kuko ibi bintu bitagenewe abizerwa muri Kristo, nuko rero, ntimubatinye.

Ni nde washobora guhagarara imbere y'Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda n'umujinya wuzuye n'imbaraga z'ubushobozi bwanjye!? Mwumve Amagambo yanjye kandi munyumve neza, ndetse namwe mwese abaguye uhereye ku muto kugeza ku mukuru. Jyewe, Yesu Kristo, ndetse na Kristo Yesu, ni njyewe utangaza mu ntebe y'ubwami n'imbaraga za Yahweh, NDI UWONDI Ukomeye, ko ari njyewe uje, kandi nzigarurira byuzuye abacunguwe byuzuye n'amaraso yanjye y'impongano kandi bashyizweho ikimenyetso cy'Imana ihoraho, ari we Yahweh, NDI UWONDI Ukomeye, kandi ibihe byabo byageze. Nuko rero, sinzahakanwa kandi imbaraga zanyu zo kundwanya zizagwira byuzuye ku mitwe y'abo mwohereza kugerageza. Ni nde muri mwe washobora guhagarara imbere y'imbaraga zanjye n'ubushobozi bwanjye!? Ni nde!?

Ubushake bwa Yahweh, NDI UWONDI Bukorwe iteka ryose, rimwe, bose n'iteka ryose! Dore, ndegereje, kandi abashaka kubangamira bose bazarengerwa mu gutsindwa kwuzuye n'urubanza batazacika, iteka n'iteka ryose. Sela!


Ku Bizerwa muri Kristo, nongeye kuvuga nti:

Mwitegure mu kumvira kose n'urukundo,

Yeshua HaMashiach, Umwami Uhoraho wo Gukiranuka.
