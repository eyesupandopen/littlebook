Ubuhamya Bubiri: Itorero
Mu Mirimo y'Intumwa Pawulo
21 Mata 2024

Munyumve mu Rukundo Rwange Kuri Mwe Bana Bange!

(Mu rwego rw'ubu butumwa bw'ubuhanuzi, "Umwedomo" byerekeza ku bantu bakurikije imigenzo y'Abanyakanani yo guhindura imiterere y'ingirabuzimafatizo, amarozi, gutamba abana, kurya abantu, n'ibindi. Esawu mu by'ukuri yarongoye abagore babiri b'Abahiti, ari bo Banyakanani, kandi atwara umugore wa gatatu mu nzu ya Ishimayeli. Kurongora abagore b'Abanyakanani kwa Esawu bigaragaza kutumvira no kwifatanya n'abandi ndetse n'iyanduzwa ry'ingirabuzimafatizo, kuko Kanani yari yarahinduye ingirabuzimafatizo mu buryo runaka. Ijambo Umwedomo rikoreshwa hano ku bantu bagikora iyo mihango ya gisitani, kandi iri zina rishobora gukoreshwa ku bwoko cyangwa ishyanga iryo ari ryo ryose rihuje n'ibyo. Umwami Herode yari Umwedomo, ndetse n'Abafarisayo benshi, kandi Yesu yabise abana ba Satani, ibyo bikaba byerekana urubyaro. Ariko rero, mumenye ko ijambo Umwedomo nk'uko rikoreshwa muri ubu butumwa riterekeza ku muntu uwo ari we wese bitewe n'ubwoko bwe cyangwa ubwenegihugu bwe. Ni ikirango gikoreshwa mu kwerekana abakora mu ibanga imihango y'ubupfumu y'Abanyakanani cyangwa abakomoka kuri iyo miryango kandi bakaba batataniye mu mahanga yose.)

— William Brooks


Nimwumve amagambo yange, Torero ryange, kandi mutege amatwi ubwenge n'ukuri, kuko dore: Amagambo yange ni ubwenge n'ukuri. Ubwenge burangurura mu nzira, kandi ukwiyumvisha gushaka kuganira namwe; mbese none murampa amatwi yanyu mu kumvira mubishaka? Kuko Njyewe, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndimo ndarangurura ngo numvwe n'Umugeni wange, ndetse n'abari mu mubiri wa Kristo bose. Nimwumve amagambo yange, niko Umwami ukomeye kandi wizerwa avuga, ndetse n'uwo bwami bwe butari ubw'iyi si, kuko ubwami bwange ari ubwami bwo mu ijuru. Uko bimeze mu bwami bwange, nk'uko bimeze mu ijuru, niko bizaba ku isi; kuko nzazana gukiranuka ku isi, kandi nta kintu kibi kizinjira mu marembo yange, kuko ubwami bwange ari ubwami bwo gukiranuka aho intare izaryamana n'umwana w'intama mu mahoro n'umutekano. Benshi muri mwe, Mugeni wange, mwabaye abibone muri iyi minsi y'ubwenge bwiyongereye, kandi ibi ntibitunguranye, kuko Ijambo ryange rivuga ngo iki: Kuko ubwenge butera kwihimbaza! Ariko urukundo rurubaka. None nimunyumve mu rukundo rwange kuri mwe, bana bange!

Nimwumve amagambo yange, kuko ngiye kuvuga ku Bedomo bari muri mwe, kuko benshi muri mwe mutazi abo mvuga. Nzavuga ku biyambika uruhu rw'intama ariko ari amasega aryana, kuko biyoberanya bakagendagenda muri mwe. Nzavuga ku isinagogi ya Satani n'abantu bose binjiye mu byaremwe bya Data, batumwe na Satani ubwe, kuko ari urubyaro rw'inzoka nyirizina. Yewe, mwe guhagarika amatwi yanyu ngo mupfuke amaso yanyu kuko mutemeranya n'amagambo yange, yewe, bato bange bahumye kandi bafite ubumara ku bushake, ahubwo mupfukame mu kwicisha bugufi kandi mutege amatwi amagambo yange, kuko kwibona kubanziriza kurimbuka kandi umutima w'ubwibone ubanziriza kugwa.

Mumenye, bato bange, ko amagambo ari mu Ijambo ryange ry'Imana ari ukuri, nyayo, kandi yizerwa: niyo mwaba mutumva aya magambo mugagerageza gusobanura ukuri nyako, mwibwira muti: Oya, ibyo si ukuri nyako ahubwo ni imvugo y'ikigereranyo. Oya, ibyo ntabwo byandikiwe njyewe ahubwo byandikiwe undi muntu. Oya, ibyo ni imigani ya kera gusa n'uburyo bw'ubuvanganzo. Oya, ibyo ni inkuru z'ibihe byashize gusa kandi ntibigomba gufatwa uko byakabaye. Wa muswa we! Wemerera abungeri bayobye kuvuga ko Ijambo ry'Imana nyirizina, amagambo azana agakiza kawe, amagambo ya Yahweh ubwe atari ukuri ahubwo ashobora gusobanurwa mu buryo butagira ukwizera? Yewe, nimuve mu nzira zanyu zitagira ukwizera, bantu bange, kandi muze aho ndi n'umutima ufunguye n'ubwenge bwambaye kwicisha bugufi.

Ntimwasomye se? Ese Itangiriro rivuga iki kuri iki kibazo ubwo Yahweh yahangabanyaga inzoka kubera ubugome bwayo bw'uburiganya yakoze mu Busitani muri iyi Yubile myinshi ishize? Kandi nzashyira urwango hagati yawe n'umugore, no hagati y'urubyaro rwawe n'urubyaro rwe: ruzagukubita ku mutwe, nawe uzarukubita ku gatsinsino. Muratekereza ko aya magambo ari ayo kwihimbira n'ikigereranyo, bana bange? Mwizera amagambo atagira ukwizera y'umucyo yahumye ubwenge bwanyu ku magambo yange muri iki gihe cyose noneho? Ibinyoma byemerwa muri siyansi yanyu, kuko bihindurwa ibihimbano n'ibinyoma byabo. Ibinyoma byemerwa mu bumenyi bwanyu bw'isanzure, kuko bihindurwa ibihimbano n'ibinyoma byabo. Ibinyoma byemerwa mu mateka yanyu, kuko mwibagiwe abo muri bo, bato bange, kandi mwatandukanijwe n'amateka yanyu nyakuri mukabaho mu binyoma by'umwanzi mu bujiji bukabije bw'isi nyakuri mubamo.

Nk'uko rwose ndi urubyaro rwasezeranijwe rw'umugore, yego, wa mugore utangaje mu byanditswe (uwo bamwe basenga mu buryo bw'icyaha) wavuze ati, Bibe kuri njye nk'uko ubushake bwawe buri, uwo nanyuzemo nza muri iyi si. Navukiye ndetse kuri Mariya i Betelehemu y'i Yudaya nrerwa nk'umwana w'umubaji, nrerwa na Yozefu wari uwo mu muryango wa Dawidi, ubwo atashakaga kumukoza isoni mu ruhame (kuko yamukundaga cyane), kuko yabonetse atwite inda y'Umwuka Wera. Nk'uko ndi urubyaro rwasezeranijwe rwatumwe n'Imana, niko na Satani afite urubyaro. Si uko ashobora kurema ikintu icyo ari cyo cyose ubwe, kuko ari umusundura n'umujura, ariko yakoze ibizira kugira ngo murimbuke.

Kuki ibi bibagora kubyizera? Mwumva ibinyoma bitangwa nk'ukuri, mwatesheje agaciro ibintu by'ibanze cyane, kuko nababwiye iby'isi, ntimwizere: muzizera mute ninbabwira iby'ijuru? Ntimwasomye amagambo ya Yahweh kubw'umugaragu we wizerwa Yobu? Wari he ubwo nashyiragaho imfatiro z'isi? Bivuge, niba ufite ubwenge. Ni nde washyizeho ibipimo byayo, niba ubizi? Cyangwa ni nde wayigeresheje umugozi? Imfatiro zayo zishimangiwe kuki? Cyangwa ni nde washyizeho ibuye ryayo rikuru ry'imfuruka? Ubwo inyenyeri zo mu gitondo zaririmbiraga hamwe, n'abana b'Imana bose bakarangurura amajwi y'ibyishimo. Nimwongere musome icyo gice cya nyuma, bana bange,

"abana b'Imana baranguruye amajwi y'ibyishimo" — yego, abamarayika bari bahari mu irema, kandi bazi amabanga menshi y'ijuru atari ay'uko abantu bamenya, kuko abantu bashakishije imyaka myinshi mu irari ryabo ry'ubutegetsi n'icyubahiro.

Ubu ni ubumenyi bw'abamarayika baguye, bana bange, ntimwasomye se? Cyangwa muracyizera ibinyoma by'Abedomo bababibye muri mwe? Ariko Itangiriro rivuga iki kuri ibi? Muri iyo minsi hariho ibihangange mu isi: ndetse na nyuma y'ibyo, ubwo abana b'Imana basangaga abakobwa b'abantu, bakababyarira abana; abo babaye abagabo b'abanyambaraga, bo hambere, abagabo b'ibirangirire. Benshi muri mwe munyicira kwihangana mwizera umugani w'ikinyoma w'uko abana b'Imana bari abakomoka kuri Seti, kandi ko barongoye abakobwa babi b'abantu bakabyara ibihangange mu gukora ibibi. Abantu babaye babi kuva bagwa mu cyaha, kandi uko abantu babi bakororoka kose, urubyaro rwabo ntiruzaba ibihangange binini cyane. Oya. Uwo ni umugani ugamije kuyobya, kuko igice cy'intsinzi ya Satani giterwa n'ibanga rye ry'uburiganya, kandi yaratsinzwe, bityo intsinzi ye y'igihe gito yarangiye mu gutsindwa gukomeye kandi ntazacika inyanja ye. Ntimumumtinye, ahubwo mwumve amagambo y'Ijambo ryange mu rwego rwayo rwa Bibiliya, kandi ntimube abaswa n'abajiji b'ukuri.

Abana b'Imana ni abarinzi muri uru rwego, abamarayika bari bashinzwe gufasha abantu ariko aho kubikora baretse ubuturo bwabo bwa mbere, kuko bararikiye abakobwa b'abantu. Kuko nabwiye Yuda gutangaza iki mu izina ryange? Kandi abamarayika batarinze ubuturo bwabo bwa mbere, ahubwo bakareka aho babaga, yabarindiriye mu minyururu y'iteka mu mwijima, kugeza ku rubanza rwo ku munsi ukomeye. Nimwongere mubisome kandi ntimube abantu batagira ukwizera, ahubwo mwizere gusa. Abana b'Imana ni abamarayika baguye biyandurishije abagore; benshi muri aba bagore barafashwe barakoreshwa nabi, nubwo bamwe muri bo babikoze ku bushake kandi bazikorera urubanza rwabo.

Urubyaro babyaye mu kwiyanduza kwabo, yego, ubu bumwe butagatifu bwabyaye ibihangange, ibyaremwe bibi byakorewe kurimbuka kwanyu. Mwihemukira cyane mwishingikiriza gusa ku gitabo cyanyu cy'amategeko 66. Hariho ibitabo byinshi by'amategeko kandi igitabo cyanyu cy'amategeko 66 cy'Abaporotesitanti ntigikubiyemo ubuhanuzi bwose bwatanzwe ariko gikubiyemo ibintu byose byerekeye ubuzima no kubaha Imana muri njye Kristo Yesu. Musome ibitabo bya 1 Enoki na Yasheri na Yubile. Si ibyanditswe kuri mwe ariko rwose ni ibitabo byiza byo gusoma bisabwa kandi byavuzwe mu byanditswe mukunda. Ntimugengwe no gutekereza ko ibitabo byemewe mu gitabo runaka cy'amategeko ari bwo buhanuzi bwose bwatanzwe. Munyigireho.

Nyamara, Imana yarimbuye isi yariho icyo gihe, kuko inshuti yange Petero ivuga iti: Kuko ibi babiyobewe ku bushake, yuko kera ijuru ryabayeho ku bw'ijambo ry'Imana, n'isi ikabaho ivuye mu mazi kandi ihagaze mu mazi, Ibyo ni byo byatumye isi ya kera irengerwa n'amazi, ikarimbuka. Kandi mu minsi ya Nowa Data yohereje umwuzure ukomeye warimbuye ibizira byabo byose bibi, kuko bakoze ibizira byinshi byazanye kurimbuka gukomeye ku isi. Bishe gahunda kamere y'ubuzima kandi bazana guhungabana muri DNA nyirizina y'abantu, kandi umuntu yavanzwe n'inyamaswa n'inyamaswa ivangwa n'ifi n'ubwoko bumwe bw'inyamaswa buvangwa n'ubundi bwoko bw'inyamaswa kugeza ubwo ibizira byifuzwaga byagezweho. Hanyuma ibizira byarimbuye abantu. bityo Data arabarimbura, arokora ubugingo umunani. Nowa yabonye ubutoni imbere y'Imana, kandi Nowa n'umuryango we ni bo bonyine batari bononekaye ku isi mu guhindura kwabo ingirabuzimafatizo, kuko Nowa yari umukiranutsi.

Musome amagambo yange kandi musobanukirwe isi yanyu, kuko benshi muri mwe mwizera ibinyoma by'umucyo w'ikinyoma wangiye ikintu cyose cy'umwuka kandi kigakora kugira ngo gihindure rubanda injiji ku binyoma byabo bitagira ukwizera by'uko ibintu byo ku isi ari byo byonyine bifite agaciro. Ntimwumva se, bangiye Imana mu magambo yabo atagira ukwizera? Babayobeje nkana bakoresheje kwiga kurwanya kwiga: mwige kugira ngo musobanukirwe iyi nyigisho y'Abayezuwiti.

Ariko Itangiriro rivuga iki kuri ibi? Muri iyo minsi hariho Ibihangange mu isi: ndetse na nyuma y'ibyo.

Nyuma y'iyihe minsi, murabaza? Nyuma y'iminsi ya Nowa. Kuko nyuma y'umwuzure isi yagabanyijwe hakoreshejwe ubufindo bw'umurage, kandi mu minsi ya Pelegi isi yagabanyijwe mu murage hagati y'abana ba Nowa, Shemu, Hamu na Yafeti. Ku bw'intego zacu tuzakurikira umurongo wa Hamu, kuko kuri Hamu hakomotse imiryango y'abavumwe, ndetse na Nimurodi ukomoka kuri Kushi na Egiputa cyangwa Mizirayimu, n'Abanyakanani babi bakomoka kuri Kanani mubi. Kuko Kanani ari we Nowa yavumye. Kuko Kanani yasize umurage we maze ashinga umujyi wa Tiro ku cyabaye inkombe ya Foyinike kandi kiganisha kuri Sidoni aho Yezebeli yakomotse. Yabikoze kugira ngo abe hafi y'Umusozi wa Herumoni, aho abarinzi bamanukiye kandi aho nahinduriwe isura.

Kanani yabonye ibyo abarinzi basize inyuma, kandi yakoresheje ubumenyi bwabo bw'abamarayika baguye mu marozi akomeye, kandi imihango myinshi mibi yakozwe n'Umanyakanani, kuko banatambaga abana babo bwite. Bari babi cyane kandi bakwirakwiriye hose ku buryo yashoboye kwanduza igihugu cya Kanani n'ibizira bye bishya yari yakoze, aho Satani yakoreraga kubera ibyaha bye. Nk'uko Data yakuye Abaheburayo mu bunyage bwa Egiputa, niko yabahaye inshingano yo kwambura umurage wabo aya moko mabi y'ibihangange. (Ese muracyanga kwizera Amagambo yange?) Ntimwasomye inkuru mibi yatanzwe n'abatasi icumi mu cumi na babiri boherejwe mu gihugu cya Kanani na Mose kugira ngo bagitase? Kuko Yosuwa na Kalebu ari bo bonyine bavuze amagambo y'ukwizera, ariko inkuru mibi yatanzwe yari ukuri nyako, keretse ko yari inkuru y'icyaha, itagira ukwizera yihakanye amagambo ya Data mu cyimbo cy'ibyo babonye n'amaso yabo, kuko Imana iruta amaso y'umuntu uwo ari we wese, bato bange. Musome inkuru mu Gutegeka kwa Kabiri: Ariko abagabo bazamukanye na we, baravuga bati, Ntidushobora kuzamuka ngo dutere ba bantu, kuko baturusha amaboko. Kandi bazaniye abana ba Isirayeli inkuru mibi y'igihugu batase, bavuga bati, Igihugu twanyuzemo, kugira ngo tugitase, ni igihugu kirya abagituye, kandi abantu bose twakibonyemo, ni abantu barebare cyane. Kandi twabonye ibihangange, bene Anaki, bakomoka ku bihangange: kandi twari tumeze nk'inzige mu maso yacu, niko twari tumeze no mu maso yabo.

Nuko Yosuwa yeza igihugu ibyo bizira bibi, kandi ntibari ibyo mwakwita abantu; bari ibizira by'imvange byakorewe kurimbura umurongo wa Kristo, kuko Satani yifuzaga cyane guhagarika kuza kwange kwa mbere. Nyamara, abana ba Isirayeli ntibasohoje mu budahemuka inshingano Data yabahaye yo kwaka umurage wabo abanyarugomo bawigaruriye. Byinshi muri ibi byaremwe byaryaga abantu; ntimusomye se aho igihugu cyariye abagituye? Kuko Abacamanza batubwira iki kuri ibi: Kandi Uwiteka yari kumwe na Yuda, yirukana abari batuye mu misozi, ariko ntiyashobora kwirukana abari batuye mu kibaya, kuko bari bafite amagare y'ibyuma.

Kandi Abacamanza bongera kuvuga bati: Kandi Manase ntiyirukanye abari batuye i Betisheyani, n'imidugudu yaho, cyangwa Taanaki n'imidugudu yaho, cyangwa abari batuye i Dori, n'imidugudu yaho, cyangwa abari batuye i Ibuyamu, n'imidugudu yaho, cyangwa abari batuye i Megido, n'imidugudu yaho: ariko Abanyakanani bashakaga gutura muri icyo gihugu. Kandi byabayeho ubwo Isirayeli yari ikomeye, bashyira Abanyakanani ku buhake, ariko ntibabirukana burundu. Kuko Kubara kuvuga iki kuri ibi: Ariko nimutirukana abaturage b'igihugu imbere yanyu, bizaba ko abo muzareka bagasigara muri bo, bazaba ibikwasi mu maso yanyu, n'amahwa mu mbavu zanyu, kandi bazabagirira nabi mu gihugu mutuyemo.

Nuko ibyanditswe birasohora kandi amoko y'Abanyakanani aracyagirira nabi abana b'Imana kugeza n'uyu munsi. Bivanze mu byaremwe by'Imana kandi bihishe mu maso y'abantu, ndetse bafata umuco n'imigenzo by'amahanga babamo. Ariko iherezo ni rimwe buri gihe: Barimbura nkana amahanga babamo (Imana yabagize inzererezi n'abashyitsi mu isi). Bavuze ibinyoma bikomeye kandi bagurisha ibyo binyoma nk'ukuri. Bigaruriye ibihugu byanyu, kandi ntimunababona, kuko mwahumwe amaso ku nzira zabo kuko benshi muri mwe mwumvikanye nabo, ndetse n'isinagogi ya Satani.

Mugaruke ku Ijambo ryange ryera kandi mwihunze amagambo n'inzira by'Abedomo. Mureke gushyira hejuru amagambo y'icyaha hejuru y'Ijambo Ryera cyane rya Data. Kuko benshi muri aba Banyakanani babaye Abidumeya uko igihe cyahitaga kandi babaga hafi ya Yuda. Ubwo Yohana Hirukano yigaruriraga Idumeya, yahinduye Abidumeya abayoboke b'idini rya Kiyahudi, kandi Yudaya yavotse mu guhuza Yuda na Idumeya. Kandi bityo nahagaze mbahangana muri Isirayeli mu myaka nka 2000 ishize, kandi guhagarara kwange kwanditswe mu mavanjiri kugira ngo mugende nk'uko nagendaga. Amagambo yabo yagoretse Tora, kandi imigenzo yabo y'imvugo n'imihango y'ubupfumu byo muri Babuloni byihishe mu gihugu cyange, kuko igihugu cyange cyafashwe n'abiyitirira, Isinagogi ya Satani nyirizina.

Kuva mu Itangiriro 3:15 habayeho intambara y'urubyaro hagati y'Imana na Satani kandi urubyaro rwa Satani ubwe rurwanira ubutegetsi bw'ikirenga, ruzaba rudafite. Natsindiye intsinzi, bana bange, kandi nabahaye ububasha, bana bange, bwo kurwanya uburyo bwose bw'ikibi, kandi nta kintu na kimwe kizabahagarika; kuko Njyewe, Yesu Kristo, natsinze ubwami bw'umwanzi. Muhagarare mubarange kandi mureke guteza imbere inzira zabo mbi. Mube mu maso yabo kuko bigize nk'aho basa namwe neza. Ntimwizera umugani wange w'ingano n'urumamfu? Undi mugani yawubaciriye, avuga ati; Ubwami bwo mu ijuru bugereranywa n'umuntu wabibye imbuto nziza mu murima we: Ariko abantu basinziriye, umwanzi we araza abiba urumamfu mu ngano, arigendera. Mube mu maso yabo kandi mwitandukanye n'isinagogi ya Satani bavuga ko ari Abayuda ariko bakabeshya atari bo. Kuko Isirayeli yatataniye mu Banyamahanga, kandi ntitumwe ahandi keretse ku ntama zazimiye zo mu nzu ya Isirayeli; bityo ibihe by'Abanyamahanga byenda kurangira, kandi nakoze umuntu umwe mushya muri njye mbakuye muri babiri, bityo nzana amahoro.

Bana banjye, mbahaye incamake y'amateka yanyu, kuko amateka y'ikiremwamuntu akomoka kuri Nowa kugeza kuri Adamu, kandi Imana yaremye amahanga yose mu maraso amwe. Bana banjye, igihe kigiye kurangira, ariko ibyo ntibivuga ko umurimo wanyu urangiye. Ndacyahamagara bana banjye ngo bampagararire kandi bankorere. Kuko nabagize abakozi bashoboye b'Isezerano Rishya mu maraso yanjye. Ntimwasomye aho nabwiye abanyoboka ko nibabwira uyu musozi ngo uvanweho utabwe mu nyanja, kandi ntibashidikanye, bazabona ibyo bategetse? Ndabagira inama yo gusubira inyuma umurongo umwe, kuko iyo nkuru itangira ivuga ngo mugire kwizera Imana (kuko ku Mana nta kidashoboka) kandi nta cyo mushobora gukora ntabarimo, bana banjye bato.

Benshi muri mwe mwigishijwe n'Abedomu ibinyoma bibayobora mu nzira zabo. Ariko niba mwizera Imana, muzategeka ibidashoboka, kandi muzakenera gukora ibyo gusa mu murimo mbatumyemo. Ndabatumye, bana banjye, gukoranya intama zisigaye, kuko benshi bazaza aho ndi muri iyi minota ya nyuma kandi sinzabahakana, ahubwo ndacyabashaka n'ishyaka ryinshi. Mufungure amakanwa yanyu mu kwizera kandi mumpagararire, mukorere abana banjye.

Nababwiye uko mukoresha imbaraga zanjye, ariko mwanga amabwiriza yanjye, bityo ndongera kubwira abibagirwa muri mwe. Kuki musaba umwuka mu bwoba ngo uve mu muntu awubamo, mudidimanga kandi mukoresha izina ryanjye, Yesu Kristo, mu buryo butarimo kwizera? Ntimugakoreshe izina ryanjye mu busa! Mutegeke imyuka mu izina ryanjye mukoresheje kwizera gukomeye kandi mwizere amagambo nzabaha, nuko imyuka mibi izagenda ku bw'imbaraga zanjye binyuze muri mwe!

Kuki musaba indwara kugenda mufite ubwoba mu gihe mwagakwiye kuvugisha indwara mwizeye mu izina ryanjye kandi mugategeka ibidashoboka mu izina ryanjye, mugakiza abarwayi. Mubwire uwo musozi muti, kuha hano utabwe mu nyanja, mukize abarwayi, mwirukane imyuka, muzure abapfuye kandi mutegeke ibidashoboka, kuko ari ko nabashoboje.

Ntimwasomye ko abambere bazaba aba nyuma, n'aba nyuma bakaba abambere? Mumenye, bana banjye, ko ndi gukora mu mbaraga ziruta izo nakoresheje mu kinyejana cya mbere, kandi nasutse umwuka wanjye mu rugero rwuzuye ku isi yose. Ni nde muri mwe ufite ubutwari bwo kumpagararira? Bwo kumvugira amagambo mbaha? Ni nde muri mwe ufite kwizera kwo kudasubizwa inyuma n'inkuru mbi ahubwo agahangana n'ikibi imbonankubone? Kuko uri muri mwe ari mukuru kuruta uri mu isi. Ntimukabe abagaragu b'Abanyakanani, kuko bavumwe ngo babe abagaragu banyu. Byongeye kandi, ntimugaheranwe n'ibisekuruza by'amahanga, kuko natumwe mu mahanga yose kugira ngo nkize bamwe, kandi mu kubikora, ndi gukiza Isirayeli nyakuri, kuko yirukanywe mu mahanga, kandi mwese muri Isirayeli nyakuri y'Imana!

Mumpagararire kandi mumvugire, kuko igihe kigiye kurangira. Ese muzaba bamwe mu bazegera intebe yanjye y'imanza bakoranyije ibiti, ibyatsi, n'ibikenyeri bitegura uwo munsi ukomeye wegereje? Cyangwa muzantura zahabu, ifeza, n'amabuye y'agaciro, kuko ubwoko bwanjye ari ubutunzi bwanjye. Mubohore imbohe kandi mukorane nanjye muri iyi minota ya nyuma. Kandi nta gushidikanya, ibanga ry'ubumana ni rikuru: Imana yagaragariye mu mubiri, itsindishirizwa mu Mwuka, ibonwa n'abamarayika, ibwirizwa mu banyamahanga, yizerwa mu isi, izamurwa mu cyubahiro.

Bana banjye, ndi Jambo wigize umubiri, kandi jyewe na Data turi umwe. Mu buryo bumwe, mube umwe natwe binyuze mu kunyumvira kwanyu, kuko vuba cyane muzaba nkanjye kandi muzamurwe mu cyubahiro, kuko ndi umuganura w'abazutse mu bapfuye, kandi nzabamurikira itorero ryiza cyane, ridafite ikizinga, cyangwa umunkanyari, cyangwa ikindi nk'icyo; ahubwo ngo ribe iryera kandi ridafite inenge. Igihe kigiye kurangira, nuko rero ntimushidikanye. Mukorane nanjye muri iyi minota ya nyuma, kandi ntimushidikanye gufungura amakanwa yanyu. Nzabazana aho muri, nuko rero mukore imirimo yanjye mureke iyanyu. Nzakora imirimo myinshi ikomeye binyuze mu maboko yanyu, gusa ntimushidikanye!
