Ubuhamya Bubiri: Itorero
Mu Mirimo y'Intumwa Pawulo
2 Gicurasi 2024

Nziza Ukwizera Kwanyu, niko Umwami Ukomeye avuga!

Dore! Jyewe, ndetse na jyewe, Kristo Yesu Umwami wazutse n'umukiza w'isi, mfite ikibazo nkubaza, mugeni wanjye. Ese uzansubiza mu by'ukuri? Ese uzashobora kumpa igisubizo gikwiye inyandiko y'ukuri? Nuko rero, ndakubaza nti: Umwana w'umuntu naza, mwibwira yuko azasanga ukwizera mu isi? Biragaragara ko iki ari ikibazo kidakeneye igisubizo, ariko iyo uza guhagarara ku muhamagaro wanjye ngo usubize, cyaba ikihe? Ese wambwira iby'ukwizera kwawe n'ukuntu ari igitangaza? Ese uzambwira ibitekerezo byawe byiza, aho wibwira mu bujiji ko utegeka isi binyuze mu bitekerezo byawe? Ntimubona ko amahame y'ibanze mu kwizera kwanjye, ndetse n'ukwizera kwa Yesu Kristo, ndetse n'ayo mahame y'ibanze yiganywe kandi agasobanurwa bundi bushya nk'ibinyoma? Ariko mu bujiji muvuga muti: "Yewe, Imana ntiyigera yemera ko ibyo biba!" Kuri ibyo ndababaza nti, muzi Imana neza mu rugero rungana iki? Ese muri abajyanama bayo? Ese muzahagarara mutegeke Yahweh? Ese muzahagarara muntegeke, jyewe Yeshua HaMashiach, gukora ibyo mushaka? Nyamara benshi muri mwe bagerageza gukora ibyo mu bujiji, kuko mwashutswe mu buryo bubi cyane.

Mwemereye abahanuzi b'ibinyoma, ndetse n'urumamfu Satani yabibye mu matorero ye y'ibinyoma (atari ayanjye), aba bahanuzi b'ibinyoma umuvandimwe wanjye Yuda yavuzeho ubwo nahumekeraga amagambo yanjye mu mutima we kandi nkagera umwuka we muri we mu guhumekerwa kwera: Bari ishyano, kuko bagendeye mu nzira ya Kayini, kandi birukiye mu ikosa rya Balamu bashaka ibihembo, kandi barimbuwe no kugandamura kwa Kora. Aba banyabinyoma bagendagenda ku mugaragaro hagati yanyu, kandi benshi muri mwe barabakurikira, bibwira ko ari njye mukurikira, kuko ari inyiganano, kandi bagerageza kwigana ijwi ryanjye kugira ngo abaswa mu ntama zanjye babakurikire mu ikosa rikomeye, iryo barimo. Kuko se ikindi Yuda yavuze kuri aba bantu b'imburamumaro ni iki? Aba ni inenge mu minsi mikuru yanyu y'urukundo, iyo basangira namwe, bigaburira nta bwoba: ni ibicu bitagira amazi, bitwarwa n'imiyaga, ibiti byumye imbuto, bitagira imbuto, bipfuye kabiri, biranduranwe n'imizi. Imiraba y'inyanja yivumbagatanya, izamura ifuro ry'isoni zabo, inyenyeri zizerera, izabikiwe umwijima w'icuraburindi iteka ryose.

Ndabizi ko inyiganano yemeza bamwe muri mwe, kuko bamwe muri mwe mutanzi by'ukuri ahubwo mugerageza gusenga Yahweh no kunkurikira, Umwami wanyu Yesu Kristo, binyuze mu mahame y'ubutumwa bwiza bw'iterambere. Ntimwibeshye: Ubutumwa bwiza bw'iterambere ni ikinyoma, kandi sinigeze nitanga nk'umwana w'intama wanyu wa Pasika kugira ngo mwirundanyireho ubutunzi: Kuko gukunda impiya ari umuzi w'ibibi byose: izo bamwe bararikiye, bayobye bava mu byo kwizera, bihandisha imibabaro myinshi. Benshi muri mwe mwakurikiye bumwe mu butumwa bwiza bwinshi bw'inyiganano, ariko nta bundi butumwa bwiza buhari. Benshi muri mwe mwakurikiye umwe muri ba Kristo benshi b'inyiganano, ariko nta wundi Kristo uhari uretse njye, Yeshua HaMashiach. Ndababwira nweruye, mwarashutswe, kandi benshi muri mwe mwaguranye ukwizera kw'ukuri kwa Yesu Kristo ukwizera kw'ikinyoma. Ukwizera kw'ikinyoma n'ukwizera kubeshya aho ijambo ukwizera ubwaryo (ese sinabahaye Ijambo ry'Ukwizera ry'ukuri?) ritagisobanura kwiringira, kwemera, no gushikama muri Yahweh, So, no muri njye, Yesu Kristo Umwami wanyu, ahubwo ryasobanuwe bundi bushya rihinduka amakabyankuru y'imburamumaro n'imigani n'ibitekerezo by'ubupfumu. Mwemereye imburamumaro kubayobya, kandi amatorero yanyu yinjiwemo n'abasenga Satani, urumamfu nyirizina umubi yabibye hagati yanyu.

Aya makabyankuru yibanda ku bitekerezo byiza n'ibibi, kandi mwibwira mu bujiji ko icyo mutekerejeho igihe kirekire cyose kizaba, nk'aho Imana yashyize imbaraga zayo n'ubutoni bwayo ku bafite ukwizera kw'ikinyoma kuruta abandi, aho mutegeka ibyaremwe binyuze mu bitekerezo byanyu. Ntimwumva ko abakora ibyo bintu by'ubusa bose bafatwa bagategwa kandi bakagirwa imbohe z'irari ryabo n'ibyo binyoma, ndetse bakabohwa ku gutsindwa, kuko mukurikira inzira za Satani mu bujiji? Mumenye ko ari abamarayika baguye, yego, ba Satani n'abarinzi bifatanyije nabo, bigishije abantu uburozi, guca imizi, amarozi, n'ubupfumu; kandi abantu benshi babi binjiye mu itorero ryanjye rya Lawodikiya kandi batwaye benshi nk'iminyago bizeye ibinyoma byabo.

Ibi byabaye kuko benshi mu itorero ryanjye rya Lawodikiya banga guhagarara ngo barwane mu gukiranuka kugira ngo munesheshe ikibi icyiza! Reka mbafashe kumva ukuri. Nta n'umwe muri mwe ufite imbaraga n'ubushobozi bwe bwite, kuko ari mu ntege nke zanyu iyo amaherezo mumenye ubushobozi buke bw'imihati yanyu. Hanyuma, inshuro nyinshi, mwumva ko iyo munyishingikirijeho, Kristo Yesu, kandi mugakurikira amabwiriza yanjye mu nzira nabahitamo, ko munyishingikirizaho n'imbaraga zanjye kandi mukanyizera byuzuye. Kuko nzabereka mwese mu buryo bwanjye, iyo mugumye kugwa neza ku ijwi ryanjye kandi mugahindukira iyo mbabwiye kubikora, ko mu mihati yanyu n'imbaraga zanyu muri ubusa uretse akabumbabumba k'umusenyi mu butayu, kayobye n'imiyaga, kandi sinapfiriye gukiza umusenyi ahubwo itaka ritukura rya Adamu, kuko ndi Adamu wa nyuma, kandi ndi umwuka utanga ubuzima. Nyamara, muri njye Kristo Yesu, muri abanyambaraga kandi ntimuhagarikwa, kuko mufite ubu butunzi mu nzabya z'ibumba, kandi nzabaha ibitangaza byinshi, ibimenyetso, ibitangaza n'ibindi nk'ibyo mu maboko yanyu iyo mufite ukwizera gushingiye ku mibanire muri njye, Yesu Kristo, aho kwiyizera ubwanyu. Nyamuneka munyemereye mbayobore, kandi mureke ibinyoma by'abaguye, kuko binyuze mu bikorwa byabo umujinya ukomeye ubabikiwe ku munsi w'umujinya, aho iherezo ryabo rikongorerwa mu muriro n'amazuku yaka.

Mumenye uko uku gushukwa kwagezweho, kuko mu Yubile za vuba aha habayeho kumurikirwa kw'ikinyoma aho abo sosiyete yanyu ifata nk'abakomeye banze Imana bashyigikira imitekerereze yabo y'ubusa. Bakoze ndetse n'ikigirwamana cy'imitekerereze mu gihe cy'impinduramatwara yabo; ese ntimuzi amateka? Aya mariba atagira amazi hamwe na filozofiye zabo zononekaye kandi z'ubusa banze Imana kandi bigisha mu buryo bwayobye ko Imana yari indorerezi gusa itita ku bantu by'ukuri ahubwo itanga byose iri kure kandi ikareka abantu bakabyishakira, ubutoni bw'Imana bukajya ku mupfumu ukomeye kuruta abandi muri bo. Hanyuma, uko igihe cyahitaga, ubugingo bwinshi budahamye, ndetse n'urumamfu rwa Satani nyirizina, rwinjiye mu itorero ryanjye rya Lawodikiya kugira ngo basohoze ibinyoma byabo mu bihe n'ibihe bikwiye. Babinjiyeho n'amagambo meza n'imvugo nziza kandi bigisha imbaraga z'ibitekerezo by'ubupfumu ku Mugeni wanjye, kandi benshi mu Mubiri wanjye babakurikiye bafite umururumba, kuko umururumba wabo wari ubyukijwe.

Aba bana babi ba Satani bivanze rwihishwa uburozi mu nyigisho zanjye kandi bageze ku ntego yo kwanduza Ijambo ryanjye ryera n'umusemburo, uretse ko Ijambo ryanjye ridashobora kwanduzwa n'ibihimbano byabo, kuko ibinyoma byabo bishingiye ku binyoma. Ntimwumva ko umusemburo muke usembura irobe ryose? Ntimwumva ko umusemburo wa Herode n'umusemburo w'Abafarisayo atari zo nzira zonyine zo kwangirika? Kuko mwarozwe n'umusemburo w'ubupfumu bwa Gikristo! Nuko rero, MWIHANE! Kuko nabwiye iki Abakorinto binyuze mu kuboko kw'Intumwa Pawulo? Nuko rero mwikuremo umusemburo wa kera, kugira ngo mube irobe rishya, nk'uko mutagira umusemburo. Kuko na Kristo Pasika yacu yatambwe ku bwacu. Mwumve Amagambo yanjye! Nuko rero izi nyamaswa z'inkazi, aya mariba atagira amazi, ndetse n'urumamfu rw'imburamumaro kandi rwica rwarabwirije kandi rutangiza amadini yabo y'ibinyoma ya Gikristo, ndetse n'amatsinda y'ibanga, biba benshi mu babakurikira babakura mu Itorero ry'ukuri, aho nitaho Umugeni wanjye mu rukundo n'ubuntu n'imbabazi, babakura kuri njye babajyana mu mazu y'ibinyoma yo gusengeramo yeguriwe Bayali rwihishwa, kuko ishyanga ryanyu ari Babuloni y'Ibanga!

Nk'aho ibi bitari bibi bihagije, abapfumu benshi bakomeye kandi b'abakire batanze amafaranga menshi kandi bakoresha imbaraga nyinshi bashyira ibinyoma byabo muri Bibiliya z'ibinyoma. Bazanye inyandiko za Alegizandiriya, inyandiko za ginositiki, bituma Itorero ryanjye riba iry'imitima ibiri riva ku cyerekezo kimwe cy'inyandiko narokoye i Antiyokiya, ndetse na Bibiliya zimwe na zimwe zifashishwa aho ibi binyoma byongewe mu mpera. Ibi bitabo byahise byinjizwa mu mashuri yanyu ya tewolojiya yononekaye kandi biba Bibiliya z'ibanze zo kwiga kuri aba bahanuzi b'ibinyoma babwirije ibinyoma kuva icyo gihe, kandi bazishyura byuzuye. Kuva icyo gihe, ibihugu byanyu byuzuye amatorero y'ibinyoma ayobowe n'amasega aryana, kandi n'ubu intama zanjye nyinshi zibwira mu buryo bwayobye ko zishobora kugendana n'amasega. Amatorero yanyu y'ibinyoma ni amazu yo gutuka Imana; ni amazu y'imyidagaduro aho baririmbira Satani ishimwe imbere yanyu, kandi mukabura ubushishozi bw'umwuka bwo kubona. Hariho abami benshi mu bwami bwa Satani, kandi babaririmbira bati Mwami, Mwami, uretse ko nibambwira bati Mwami, Mwami, nzavuga nti, Nimumve imbere, mwa nkozi z'ibibi mwe kuko ntabwo nigeze mbamenya.

Kugira ngo ibintu bibe bibi kurushaho, bafashe ubutumwa bwiza bw'iterambere butuka Imana kandi barabuhindura kurushaho binjiza imitekerereze yo kwifasha, kandi navuze iki ku byerekeye ibyo bintu: Mwirinde hatagira umuntu ubanyagisha ubwenge bw'abantu n'ibihendo by'ubusa, bikurikiza imigenzo y'abantu, n'imihango y'isi, bidakurikiza Kristo. Aya matorero y'ibinyoma arononekaye nta gaciro ko gucungura afite, kandi menshi yashinzwe n'abubatsi babi banyigobotoye, ibuye rikuru ry'imfuruka, kandi inyubako zabo zose zizagwa mu musenyi wimuka. Aba ni abubatsi batuka Imana n'abacukuzi b'amabuye b'ibinyoma n'abafundi b'ibinyoma banze ibuye rikuru ry'imfuruka, kandi nta kuri kubarimo! Berekana ndetse n'ibimenyetso byabo mu mazu yanyu mwita ayo gusengeramo, aho babashinyagurira ku mugaragaro. Mwumve Umugani mbafitiye, kuko ari uw'ukuri kandi uzazanira ubugingo bwanyu kumurikirwa kw'ukuri: Umuntu w'imburamumaro, umuntu mubi, agendana akanwa k'ubugome. Acinya icyicyo, avugisha amaguru ye, yigishisha intoki ze.

Ubugome buri mu mutima we, ashinga inama z'ibibi iteka, abiba amacakubiri. Kuko uko ari ko bari kandi uko niko bakora imbere y'amaso yanyu, ariko ntimushobora kubona, kuko mwahumishijwe mu nyigisho zabo z'ibinyoma n'inzira zabo z'ubugome.

Bana banjye, nimubavemo kandi mwitandukanye nabo; kuko Kristo ahuriye he na Beliya. Ntimugafatanye n'imirimo y'umwijima itagira imbuto, kandi mwitandukanye nabo rwose. Kuko mwe, torero ryanjye rya Lawodikiya, ndetse namwe muri akazuyaze kandi ntimugira umugongo, kandi izi si inzira zanjye na gato, kuko nirukanye abavunjayi mu Rusengero mu ishyaka ryanjye rya Yahweh. Nimumgarukire, Yesu Kristo, umwungeri w'ukuri n'umuganga w'ubugingo bwanyu, kandi munyigireho. Kuko amagambo meza yo kwatura n'ibitekerezo byiza by'imburamumaro atari ibyanjye, ahubwo ni ibinyoma mwongorewe n'intumwa z'ibinyoma, ndetse n'abakozi ba Satani nyirizina bihinduye abakozi bo gukiranuka. Nimumbwire, bana banjye bato, kuko igihe cyarangiye, kandi mwibwira mu bujiji ko mufite igihe cyose mu isi, kandi mufite iminota mike gusa yo kwihana no kungarukira mu kwicisha bugufi kugira ngo nshobore kubategurira umwanya ku meza yanjye kandi kugira ngo nshobore kubakingurira urugi mu ijuru kugira ngo mwinjire mu byishimo by'Umwami wanyu!

Kuko benshi muri mwe bibwira ko muzakoranyirizwa kuri njye mugihe mubaho muri ibi binyoma kandi mugihe murya umwanda abahanuzi b'ibinyoma babagaburira, batuka Imana mu izina ryanjye. Mumenye ko ntabatumye kandi amagambo yabo atari ayanjye, kandi niba mutabavuyemo, muzagomba kwigaragaza kuri njye mu bigeragezo byinshi no kugeragezwa mu gihe cy'amakuba ya Yakobo, yaje ku isi yose kandi agiye kurekurwa! Mundebere mu mwuka wanyu; mwibande, kuko mushobora kumva ibise mu mwuka wanyu, kuko ndabahamagara mu rugo kandi Umugeni wanjye agiye kubyara. Dore, mbasigiye ibaruwa naboherereje, mwa Banyalawodikiya banjye, kuko muri aya magambo hamurikiwe inzira yo kungarukira; kandi niba mwumvira gusa, nzabasubiza ingororano yanyu, kandi muzasangira nanjye ku meza yanjye mu ijuru nateguriye Umugeni wanjye.

Kandi wandikire marayika w'itorero ry'i Lawodikiya uti: Uku niko (Amena) umugabo wo kwizerwa kandi w'ukuri avuga, ishingiro ry'ibyo Imana yaremye. Nzi imirimo yawe, yuko udakonje kandi ntushyuhe. Icyampa ugakonja cyangwa ugashyuha. Nuko rero kuko uri akazuyaze, udakonje kandi ntushyuhe. Nzakuruka mu kanwa kanjye: kuko uvuga uti ndi umutunzi kandi ntunganiwe, nta cyo nkennye, kandi ntumenye ko uri umutindi n'uwo kubabarirwa, n'umukene, n'impumyi n'uwambaye ubusa. Ndagugira inama yo kungura izahabu yatunganirijwe mu muriro, kugira ngo ube umutunzi, n'imyenda yera, kugira ngo wambare, isoni z'ubwambure bwawe ntizigaragare, kandi usige amaso yawe umuti w'amaso, kugira ngo ubone. Abo nkunda bose ndabacyaha nkabahana. Nuko rero ugire ishyaka, wihane. Dore, mpagaze ku rugi ndakomanga. Umuntu niyumva ijwi ryanjye agakingura urugi, nzinjira iwe, nsangire na we, na we asangire nanjye. Unesha nzamuha kwicarana nanjye ku ntebe yanjye y'ubwami, nk'uko nanjye nanesheje nkicarana na Data ku ntebe ye y'ubwami.

Dore!

Ndi ugendagenda hagati y'ibitereko by'amatara birindwi, nuko rero nimumgarukire, kugira ngo ntabakura igitereko cy'itara ryanyu.

Ndi umwungeri mwiza witangiye umukumbi.

Ndi uzategeka isi ari ku ntebe y'ubwami ya Dawidi kandi nzakubita amahanga inkoni y'icyuma.

Ndi ukwiriye kwenda igitabo no kumena ibimenyetso byacyo.

Ndi utegeka marayika uzamuka aturuka iburasirazuba, ufite ikimenyetso cy'Imana ihoraho, kuko bazashyira ikimenyetso ku 144,000 banjye, ndetse n'abagaragu b'Imana ihoraho.

Ndi uzahagarara ku Musozi Siyoni ndi kumwe n'144,000 banjye, kandi bazaririmba indirimbo nshya nta muntu numwe ushobora kwiga.

Ndi Umwami w'abami n'Umutware w'abatware, kandi nje kurwanya abami b'isi, kuko igihe cyabo kigeze.

Ndi uje gutwara Umugeni wanjye, kuko kuboneka kwanjye kuri hafi. Mwitegure, Mugeni wanjye!
