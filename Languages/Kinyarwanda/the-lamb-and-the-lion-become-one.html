Ubuhamya Bwiri: Isirayeli
Ibisharira N'ibiryoshye
20 Ugushyingo 2024

Umwana w'Intama n'Intare Biba Umwe!

Nimuze kuri Yeshuwa Hamashiya, mwene wacu, kandi mwumve umurimo w'umucunguzi wanyu, kuko ndi umucunguzi wanyu. Kuko ari jye uzacungura asigaye ba Yakobo kugira ngo babeho imbere ya NDIHO ukomeye, ari we Yehova, Imana ikomeye ihoraho kandi y'iteka. Kandi ingoma yanjye n'ubwami bwanjye ntibizagira iherezo, nuko rero, mwumve Amagambo y'Umwami wanyu w'Isezerano uhoraho, ari we Yeshuwa Hamashiya, ari we Yesu Kristo, ari we Kristo Yesu, kuko ari ko NDIHO ukomeye yangize iteka ryose.

Kandi koko Yehova yampaye izina risumba amazina yose kugira ngo mu izina rya Yesu amavi yose apfukame, mu ijuru no mu isi ndetse no munsi y'isi. Kuko ndi we Yehova yahaye ibintu byose mu ijuru no mu isi mu maboko yanjye akomeye kugira ngo ibintu byose bisohore. Nuko rero, Yewe Isirayeli, umva ijwi ryanjye mu kumvira kwuje urukundo mu gusubiza urukundo nakweretse mpfa mu cyimbo cyawe mu bubabare bukomeye butari ubwanjye kwihanganira. Nyamara, mu rukundo rwanjye kuri wowe, nafashe umwanya wawe kugira ngo ngucungure mu gihano cya Mose, icyo gihano kikaba kidashoboka ko mwe ubwanyu mwikyura kubera icyaha kiri mu mubiri wanyu. Ndetse koko no gucungura Adamu ni cyo cyatumye nkorwa mu isi.

Kuko Mose ari umukiranutsi kandi ari uwera kandi Mose abaho by'ukuri muri Mesiya wanyu w'ukuri n'Umwami w'iteka wo Gukiranuka, kuko ari ko ndi. Kandi ndi we witondeye amategeko ya Mose mu rukundo rwanjye no kumvira Yehova wanyoboye mu rukundo rwe rudashira ku rubyaro rwa Adamu rwarenganyijwe kandi rugatsindwa n'abaguye, nuko imbabazi n'ubuntu bitangwa ku bwinshi kugira ngo bihagarike ubukana bwose bw'urubanza n'umujinya kugira ngo gutandukanya kwuzuye kandi kwa nyuma gushoboke hagati y'ingano za Yehova n'urumamfu rw'abaguye, bityo abaragwa bose b'ubugingo buhoraho muri Kristo Yesu bazacungurwa no guhitamo kwabo kwiyegurira Yeshuwa Hamashiya, ari we Kristo Yesu, akaba na Yesu w'i Nazareti.

Kandi mu mahanga ni ho Yehova yantumye, kandi mu gukorera imiryango yazimiye ya Isirayeli ni ho nagiye kuko nanzwe na mwene wacu mu kutizera, ndetse n'abatambyi bakuru n'Urukiko rw'Ikirenga bakorera mu ibanga Ikigongwe kuko ari aba Esawu wifatanyije mu rwango rwe ku muvandimwe we Yakobo kuri Ashikenazi wa Yafeti, kugira ngo bikoranyirize ibintu byanjye kandi kugira ngo bahe Yakobo igikomere cya nyuma mu mutwe icyo Yehova atazemerera, ndetse n'igikorwa azabuza n'ukuboko kwanjye gukomeye kandi kwumvira iteka. Nuko rero, mwene wacu, muganduke mu kumvira ijwi ryanjye kugira ngo mubeho imbere yanjye, kandi kugira ngo mubeho imbere ya Yehova uwo nta muntu numwe ushobora kwegera adafite Yesu Kristo kuko ari ko Yehova yahisemo mu mugambi we w'iteka wo gucungura n'ubwenge. Nuko mwumve ko mudafite Mesiya wanyu w'ukuri, ari we Yeshuwa Hamashiya muguma mupfuye mu byaha n'ibicumuro ibyo nanesheje igihe nishyuraga byuzuye igihano cya Mose ibihe byose no ku bose bahisemo kwemera igitambo cyanjye.

Kandi nuko Mose abaho muri Yeshuwa Hamashiya, kandi ndi we Mose yategetse kumvira kwanyu kwuzuye, nuko mwumve amagambo yanjye kandi mukebwe imitima yanyu n'amatwi yanyu kugira ngo mwumve imvugo yanjye. Kuko imitima yanyu yabyibushye mu kutumvira, kandi mwujuje amatwi yanyu ubwenge bwa Esawu kandi mwanduza imitima yanyu n'ibinyoma byabo bituka Imana bya Talimudi. Mwumve ko Mose atunganye kandi ari umukiranutsi kandi ari uwera kandi niba hari umuntu wita amategeko ya Mose avumwe, uwo muntu navumwe kubera gutuka Imana kwe, ndetse n'amategeko ya Mose yavumye. Nuko rero, ndabaha kumva ko amategeko yatanzwe kugira ngo ahamye icyaha mu mubiri, kuko aho nta mategeko ari icyaha ntikibarwa hakurikijwe amategeko, nubwo icyaha kiba mu mubiri uko byagenda kose. Nuko rero, ku mirimo y'amategeko ya Mose nta mubiri uzatsindisirizwa imbere ya Yehova: kuko mu mategeko ya Mose ari ho hava kumenya icyaha.

Nyamara, nk'uko nategetse Pawulo kwandikira Itorero ryanjye mu gihe cyanjye cy'Ubuntu ko ubu gukiranuka, kw'Imana kwahishuwe nta mategeko ya Mose, guhamywa n'amategeko n'Abahanuzi. Yewe, abahanuzi banjye ba mbere baguye bava ku mugabane wanyu wa mbere wahawe ishyanga ryanyu ryahoze rikomeye na Yehova ubwe kuko mwagombaga kuba ubwoko bwihariye butandukanye n'amahanga! Nyamara mwaranyanze kimwe na ba So mu bwigomeke bwanyu bw'imitima inangiye ku migambi n'imigambi y'iteka y'Imana Ishoborabyose, ari yo El Shaddai. Kandi nuko mu kwanga kwanyu Amagambo yanjye yatanzwe mu butumwa bwiza bw'agakiza igihe nahishuraga ibanga rikomeye ry'Imana ku ntumwa zanjye zera n'abahanuzi mwabaye nk'ukeneye amata, kandi ntimushobora kugogora inyama zikomeye kuko mukiri impinja zidafite ukumva kwuzuye, nubwo mwibona mu bwibone nk'abanyabwenge n'urumuri ariko mwahumuriwe mu binyoma n'abaguye kugira ngo muyobye abantu mu nzira zanyu zitize.

Nuko rero, mwumve, mwene wacu, ko umukiranutsi azabeshwaho no kwizera kandi ko ku mirimo y'amategeko nta mubiri uzatsindisirizwa! Byanditswe mu byanditswe byera byatanzwe n'amaboko ya Pawulo, wari koko Umubenyamini wumvira wahoze atumvira kandi ari intagondwa mu kwanga Yesu w'i Nazareti, ndetse kimwe namwe: Nuko rero abizera, bahabwa umugisha hamwe na Aburahamu wizera. Kuko abari ab'imirimo y'amategeko bose, bari munsi y'umuvumo w'amategeko: kuko byanditswe ngo, havumwe umuntu wese udakomeza mu bintu byose, byanditswe mu gitabo cy'Amategeko, kubikora.

Nyamara, kubera ubugome, uburiganya, n'amayeri y'inzoka ku munsi Adamu yaguye ava hejuru; icyaha cyategetse mu mubiri kuva icyo gihe kandi ishusho yononekaye ya Adamu si urugo rwanyu, kandi ntimugenewe kumara iteka mu mazu y'ibumba ayo mazu asubira mu mukungugu w'isi aho imiterere yanyu yakuwe. Nuko rero, Yehova yangize umwuka utanga ubuzima, kandi ndi Adamu wa nyuma. Kandi ndi we waremwe nk'icyitegererezo cy'ubugingo buhoraho kugira ngo mwese muremwe bundi bushya mu bwuzuzanye n'ubumwe, ndetse nk'umuntu umwe mushya. Kuko hatazabaho amashusho abiri y'abantu iteka ryose ahubwo imwe kuko ishusho ya Adamu izarimbuka iteka ryose hamwe n'ukubora kuri mu mubiri wononekaye.

Nuko rero, mwumve ko abaguye babahisha ukuri mu buhumyi bwanyu bw'igice bwa none n'ubwibone, ndetse n'ukuri kuzababatura rwose mu mudendezo nshaka kubaha. Nuko ndabaha kumva, Yewe Isirayeli, (uwo nkunda) ko nta muntu utsindisirizwa n'imirimo y'amategeko ya Mose imbere ya Yehova, biragaragara: kuko umukiranutsi azabeshwaho no kwizera. Kandi amategeko si ayo kwizera: ahubwo umuntu uzakora ibyo bintu, azabibeshwaho. Kandi jye, Yeshuwa Hamashiya, nabacunguye mu muvumo w'amategeko, mpinduwe umuvumo ku bwanyu, kuko byanditswe ngo, havumwe umuntu wese umanitswe ku giti. Nuko rero, Yewe Isirayeli, emera igitambo cyanjye kugira ngo wowe n'urubyaro rwawe mubeho kandi kugira ngo mubone kwinjira mu bwami bwanjye bw'imyaka igihumbi butangwa nk'ikiruhuko cy'isabato mu migambi y'iteka ya Yehova yo gucungura abantu, kuko imyaka igihumbi imeze nk'umunsi umwe n'umunsi umwe nk'imyaka igihumbi imbere ya Yehova, uwo mufitanye gahunda, Yewe Isirayeli.

Nuko rero, muve mu bababeshya kandi mwitandukanye n'icyo gitabo cy'ibinyoma gituka Imana cyoherejwe n'abaguye kugira ngo kibashuke kandi kibabohe mu ikosa kugira ngo murimbuke rwose. Muve mu binyoma by'inzoka kandi muve hagati yabo, kandi mwitandukanye, Yewe Isirayeli, nanjye nzabakira kuri jye kandi mbarinde muzima mu kurimbuka kw'icyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli, ndetse n'amakuba ya Yakobo aje kweza isi mu maboko y'inkozi z'ibibi mu rubanza rukomeye kandi rwo gukiranuka.

Kuko ibihe byo gutandukanya bije rwose ku isi aho Yakobo, uwo murage we ari uw'isezerano ku bana b'isezerano babeshwaho no kwizera, azatandukanywa rwose kandi iteka ryose n'umuvandimwe we utumvira, ari we Esawu ushaka kwiba ibyo yagurishije mu kutizera kuko nta cyubahiro cyangwa kwizera yari afite mu masezerano yizerwa yahawe mu kwizera na Yehova kuri Aburahamu wizera, ahubwo yitaye cyane ku bintu by'isi aba umusambanyi n'umuntu uhumanye wagurishije umurage we w'iteka n'uburenganzira bwe bw'imfura ku gice cy'umugati.

Nuko ndabaha kumva ejo hazaza mbahamagarira gusangira kandi aho nzabarindira mu mutekano w'imbaraga zanjye niba muzagandukira ingoma yanjye n'ubutware bwanjye, kuko mu kubikora nzabagarura rwose kuri Yehova ndetse na Data w'Imicyo uwo nta guhindagurika cyangwa igicucu cyo guhindukira bimubaho. Kandi nuko byanditswe mu masezerano ya kera kandi niko bizaba mwene wacu uwo ndi umucunguzi we!

Kandi nuko bizasohora ku iherezo ry'amakuba ya Yakobo ko nzashyiraho ubwami bwanjye mu gukiranuka ku isi, kandi nta kintu kibi kizabwinjiramo. Mwumve ko nzazana iherezo ry'iteka ku bwami bwa Satani kandi ikigongwe ntikizongera kuzamuka ngo gitegeke kandi inyamaswa yo mu nyanja n'inyamaswa yo mu isi zizajugunywa nzima muri rya ziba rinini ry'umuriro w'amazuku yaka, yibirindura yaka ibirimi bishyushye cyane by'ubururu kandi muri iryo ziba rinuka ry'igihano bazajugunywamo iteka ryose, aho inyo yabo idapfa kuko ari ko bakwiriye.

Satani ubwe azafatwa abohwe mu munyururu munini w'ububabare n'igihano aho azategereza mu bwoba n'umubabaro by'ukurimbuka kwe kwegereje, uwo murage azasangira n'abamukurikira bose. Koko, koko, mu kuzimu azaguma afunzwe kandi aboshye mu gihe cy'imyaka igihumbi, ku iherezo ry'icyo gihe azabohurwa kugira ngo akoranye abazaba barigometse bakifatanya n'inzira ze, ndetse n'ubwigomeke bwe bwa nyuma kugira ngo ikibi kirimburwe iteka ryose mu iherezo ry'urubanza. Kandi ku iherezo ry'igihe cye gito ubwo azaba yabakoranyije hamwe nk'umusenyi wo ku nyanja ku nkambi y'abera ku bugari bw'isi, umuriro uzamanuka uva kuri Yehova mu ijuru uzabatsemba rwose kandi ubamire bose, kandi Satani azabohwa iteka ryose ajugunywe mu murage we w'iteka muri iyo miriro y'iteka yaka y'igihano kandi urwo rubanza rw'intebe y'era nini ruzatangira.

Kandi muri iryo ziba ryaka iteka ry'igihano ryaka ibirimi bishyushye cyane by'ububabare, ndetse mu guhobera kwaryo kw'iteka kwaka Satani azishyura rwose buri cyaha cya nyuma yigeze gukora inshuro nyinshi, kandi buri gikorwa kibi cyanditswe neza mu ijuru kandi kizatangwa mu miriro y'igihano nk'umurage we w'iteka. Koko, buri kiremwa cyakurikiye kandi kikiyegurira impamvu ye kizahura n'igihano kimwe kandi ntibazababarirwa kuko batagaragaje imbabazi, nuko rero nta mbabazi bazahabwa iteka ryose. Kandi nk'uko bagiriye abandi, kandi nk'uko bangije irema rya Yehova niko bizishyurwa ku mitwe yabo, ndetse nk'uko byasezeranijwe kera kuko nta sezerano na rimwe rya Yehova rizigeze rigwa hasi ridasohoye. Koko, koko abanga icyifuzo cyanjye cy'agakiza bose bazajugunywa mu miriro kandi nta kurobanura ku butoni.

Nyamara, iherezo ry'abaguye bahisemo kandi bakikorera mu bwigomeke bwabo bubi kuri Yehova n'irema rye si iryawe mwene wacu, niba uzanyemera nka Mesiya wawe. Oya, ahubwo, hari ingororano nyinshi n'imigisha byabitswe kuri wowe uzabona rwose mu bwami bwanjye nzategeka mu gukiranuka kw'iteka. Hazabaho Umwami w'Abami n'Umutware w'Abatware umwe uzategeka isi mu gukiranuka kw'iteka, ndetse no muri Yerusalemu, ndetse no ku ntebe ya Dawidi iyo ndi umuragwa w'iteka! Kandi mu mugeni wanjye wizerwa nziyegurira mu isezerano ry'iteka nzambika ikamba kandi neze umutwe munini w'Abami n'Abatambyi hakurikijwe uko ubudahemuka bwabo bwabaye kandi bazategeka mu gukiranuka mu bwuzuzanye bwuzuye n'Umwami wabo w'Abami n'Umutware w'Abatware kandi tuzategeka nk'umwe.

Kandi abantu bo mu gihugu n'abantu bo mu mahanga bazacura inkota zabo mo amasuka kandi amahanga ntazongera kurwana hagati yayo, kandi abantu bo ku isi ntibaziga kurwana no guhangana mu bwami bwanjye bwera. Ndetse nk'uko byanditswe n'abahanuzi ba kera: Isega na ryo rizabana n'umwana w'intama, kandi ingwe izaryama hamwe n'umwana w'ihene, n'inyana, n'intare, n'itungo rishishe hamwe, kandi umwana muto azabayobora. Kandi inka n'idubu bizagaburirwa: ibyana byabyo bizaryama hamwe: kandi intare izarya ubwatsi nk'ikimasa. Kandi umwana wonka azakinira ku mwobo w'impiri, kandi umwana ucukijwe azashyira ukuboko kwe ku mwobo w'incira. Hanyuma nta numwe uzababaza cyangwa ngo arimbure mu musozi wanjye wera wose: kuko isi izuzura kumenya Uwiteka, nk'uko amazi atwikira inyanja.

Kandi kuri uwo munsi hazabaho amazi y'ubugingo asohoka muri Yerusalemu, igice cyayo kigana ku nyanja y'Iburasirazuba, n'igice cyayo kigana ku nyanja ya kure, kandi bizaba, mu mpeshyi no mu itumba. Kandi Uwiteka azaba Umwami ku isi yose: kuri uwo munsi hazabaho Uwiteka umwe, n'Izina rye rizaba rimwe. Igihugu cyose kizahinduka nk'ikibaya kuva i Geba kugeza i Rimoni, mu Majyepfo ya Yerusalemu, kandi kizamurwa, gituwe mu mwanya wacyo: kuva ku irembo rya Benyamini kugeza ku mwanya w'irembo rya mbere, kugeza ku irembo ry'inguni, no kuva ku munara wa Hananiyeli, kugeza ku rwengero rw'Umwami. Kandi abantu bazagituramo, kandi nta kurimbuka kuzongera kubaho, ahubwo Yerusalemu izaturwa amahoro.

Nuko mwumve, Yewe Isirayeli ibyo nzakorera abankunda bose mu kumvira amategeko yanjye y'iteka y'ubugingo n'amahoro ayo mbategeka. Nuko mwumve ko gukunda Yehova ari ukwitondera amategeko ye kandi amategeko ya Yehova ategeka kumvira Umwana wa Yehova, uwo ndi we: nuko rero, musome Umwana kugira ngo ntaraka mukarimbukira mu nzira.

Kuko, ubwami bwanjye ari ubwami aho imigambi yanjye y'iteka izahurira hamwe mu isozwa no mu ishyirwa mu bikorwa ryuzuye uko umwana w'intama azaba umwe n'intare.

Kuko, ndi Umwana w'Intama w'Imana, watangiwe ibyaha by'abantu, uwo amaraso ye ari igitambo cy'impongano cy'iteka, binyuze muri cyo abakiranirwa bezwa bagasa n'urubura mu mweze wera.

Kuko, ndi Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda ukurwanirira Yewe Isirayeli, kandi uzarimbura rwose abanzi bawe nk'uko byanditswe kuri njye; guhora ni ukwanjye kandi ni njye uzishyura, ni ko Umwami w'abami n'Umutware w'abatware avuga!

Nuko rero, musobanukirwe imigambi yanjye n'uwo Yehova yatumye ngo abacungure kandi hariho ibice byinshi by'imigambi yanjye y'agakiza. Kandi ni njye usumba byose, ndetse n'uwaroboye agakiza kanyu iteka ryose niba muhisemo kwemera ukuboko kwanjye kw'urukundo mukemera n'isezerano ryanjye rishya.

Kuko ari binyuze mu isezerano ryanjye rishya nashimangiye mu maraso yanjye bwite, ari ryo Sezerano ryanjye Rishya ubu isi izategekeshwa gukiranuka! Kandi kumenya Yehova kuzatwikira isi n'ubwiza bwanjye buzamurika buvuye i Yerusalemu kugira ngo amahanga yikoranire yinjire mu marembo yera y'uwo mudugudu wera kugira ngo yige ibya Yehova kandi abesheho mu nzira ze ku isi.

Mu bwami bwanjye ntihazongera kubaho uruhinja ruzarama iminsi mike, cyangwa umusaza utazuzuza iminsi ye: kuko umwana azapfa afite imyaka ijana: ariko umunyabyaha w'imyaka ijana azavumwa. Kandi hazabaho ko ubaho akageza ku myaka ijana azaba ameze nk'uruhinja.

Ndetse n'ubwo bimeze bityo, isi izasubizwa mu bwiza bwayo bwa mbere mbere y'uko abaguye banduza isi n'ibizira byabo ubwo bamanukaga ku Musozi wa Herumoni kugira ngo barahire indahiro yabo ivumwe kugira ngo babeho mu irari ryabo mu cyaha gikomeye no mu kwigomeka ku mategeko y'ubuzima baremewemo mu buryo bwuzuye. Ntibazaba bari mu bwami bwanjye; kandi isi ntizategekwa mu rugomo rwuzuye urwango aho abakomeye ari bo barokoka, kuko ubwo bucucu ari ukugoreka amategeko ya Yehova ari byo Satani ahora ashakisha kugoreka amategeko ya Yehova kugira ngo kurimbuka kutegeke.

Kandi ni njye, Yeshua HaMashiach uzategeka amahanga n'inkoni y'icyuma binyuze mu maboko y'Abami n'Abatambyi benshi nzambika amakamba nkabimikira umurimo usumba yose, kandi ni bo mbuto z'umuganura z'abapfuye, kandi ntibazapfa ntibazongera gupfa ukundi kandi ingororano yabo ntigira iherezo, kuko baje aho ndi ku bushake bwabo mu minsi y'ubuntu nkabakoraniriza aho ndi mu mubiri wanjye waremwe bundi bushya nk'umuntu umwe mushya baba Abayuda cyangwa Abanyamahanga, baba imbata cyangwa abidegemvya. Bazahabwa ubushobozi bwinshi n'ububasha n'imico bahawe kubera ubudahemuka bwabo kuko ari abaneshi banjye banesheje byose ku bw'amaraso y'umwana w'intama.

Kuko ari Umwana w'Intama w'Imana wanesheje abaguye i Kaluvari iteka ryose kandi ni Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda izagandura rwose ibyaremwe byose kuri Yehova, ndetse kugira ngo Yehova abe byose muri byose ubwo nzamumurikira ibyaremwe byaganduwe kuko ngomba gutegeka kugeza ubwo abanzi banjye bose bagizwe intebe y'ibirenge byanjye, kandi ni Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda izasiribanga buri gice cyose cy'ubwami bwa Satani munsi y'ibirenge byanjye bikomeye kandi by'iteka.

Nuko rero, ndi uwo umwana w'intama azungira ubumwe mu mahoro yuzuye n'ubumwe n'intare kuko imigambi yanjye y'iteka izakorwa iteka ryose kandi nta kintu na kimwe cyangwa umuntu n'umwe ushobora guhagarara imbere ya Yeshua HaMashiach kandi ibihe byanjye byageze aho nzihishurira abatuye isi. Dore, ndazana n'ibicu, kandi buri jisho rizambona: yego, ndetse n'abancumise: kandi amoko yose yo ku isi azandirira, bibe bityo, Amen.

Ndi Yeshua HaMashiach ugiye kwihishurira isi kandi isi yose izamenya ko hariho Imana mu ijuru kandi inzira za Yehova zizuzura isi mu gukiranuka kwera.

Ndi Kristo Yesu, Umwami wanyu wazamutse mu ijuru kandi wazutse, Umwami, Umutambyi Mukuru, na Mesiya!

Ndi uwo watumye Amagambo yanjye yera binyuze ku ntumwa yanjye n'umugaragu wanjye kandi binyuze ku ntumwa yanjye n'umugaragu wanjye Amagambo yanjye yera yoherejwe! Kuko intumwa yanjye ikomeye yasubiye mu muhamagaro wayo w'iteka mu budahemuka nyuma y'igihano kinini, ukwihana, no kwiyeza mu maraso yanjye y'impongano; kandi namwakiriye iwanjye mu rukundo rwanjye ku bihana abo ari bo bose baza aho ndi bafite umutima umenetse. Kandi intumwa yanjye nayihishuriye Igitabo Gitoya Yohana Umubishuzi yategetswe gufatanya ikimenyetso kugira ngo kizahishurwe mu gihe cyacyo, icyo gitabo kikaba kirimo ijwi ry'inkuba indwi ndetse byose bikaba ari Ubutumwa Bwiza bw'Iteka kandi yatangaje Amagambo yanjye mu budahemuka nk'uko yategetswe, ubuhamya Yohana Umubishuzi yanditse kandi yahamije.

Ndi uwo utarobanura ku butoni, ndetse Yeshua HaMashiach, kandi uwo ari we wese n'abaza aho ndi bose baturutse mu muryango wa Adamu muri ubwo buryo nzabakira, keretse abafata ikimenyetso cy'inyamaswa mu bupfu, cyangwa abihindura muri ubwo buryo. Ni ukuri, ni ukuri abaza aho ndi bose muri ubwo buryo bahindukirira kubaho ubuzima bukiranuka bazagororerwa iteka ryose kandi uburemere bw'ingororano yanyu ni ubw'iteka.

Kandi bimeze bityo muryango wanjye, ko agakiza ari impano y'ubugingo buhoraho, nanjye Yeshua HaMashiach nakoreye binyuze mu kumvira kwanjye gukomeye amategeko ya Mose no mu gitambo cyanjye cy'iteka cyanjye ubwanjye nk'Umwana w'Intama w'Imana utagira icyaha kandi wuzuye. Nuko rero, abaza aho ndi bose mu kwihana kwuzuye no kwicisha bugufi no kumvira nzabagarura aho ndi no kuri Yehova kandi nzabaha imirimo myinshi yo gukiranuka Yehova yashaka ko basohoza mu migambi ye ihoraho kandi y'iteka. Nuko rero, impano y'ubugingo buhoraho iri mu maboko yanjye ku basaba bose mu bugwaneza no kwihana kandi baza aho ndi mu bihe byatangiwe kwihana.

Ndi Yeshua HaMashiach, kandi ndabakunda benewacu kandi ndaje kubatabara kandi nzakiza abumvira muri mwe kugeza ku mpera, nuko rero mwumvire Amagambo ya NDIHO ukomeye mu kumvira Mesiya wanyu w'ukuri, uwo umwana w'intama agendana agatoki ku kandi n'intare, kuko ni ukuri, ndi we.

Dore! Agakiza ka Isirayeli kari hafi kandi umwana w'intama aririmbana mu ijwi rimwe n'intare yivuga mu burinzi bukomeye ku byana byayo, nuko rero, mwitegure Yewe Isirayeli kuko gucungurwa kwanyu kuje.

Munyumve mu rukundo rwanjye kuri mwe, benewacu, kuko Mesiya wanyu w'ukuri ari hafi!
