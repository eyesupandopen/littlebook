Mu Mirimo y'Intumwa Pawulo

Inkuba ya 7

Nyumva Kandi Unyumve Neza!

Nyumva kandi unyumve neza, mwa bahanuzi b'ibinyoma mugoreka Ijambo ryanjye ryera, kuko mutari abanjye, kandi ntimufite umwuka wanjye wera, kandi mwagabye igitero ku Mugeni wanjye, ari ryo Torero ryanjye nahamagaye! Umva kandi witondere umuburo wanjye, yewe ishyanga ritagira kwizera, rigoramye kandi ryangiritse, kuko muzabazwa buri gikorwa kibi cyose! Umva amagambo yanjye y'umuburo, mwebwe abayobya Itorero ryanjye, kuko uburakari bwanjye bubagurumanira, kuko ndi Umwana w'Intama w'Imana! Kuko benshi muri mwe muzihisha mu masenga, no mu bitare byo ku misozi. Kandi muzabwira imisozi n'ibitare muti, mutugwire, muduhishe amaso y'uwicaye ku ntebe y'ubwami, n'uburakari bw'Umwana w'Intama: Kuko umunsi ukomeye w'uburakari bwe ugeze, kandi ni nde uzabasha guhagarara?

Kuko isi izabona kuboneka kwanjye, kandi umutingito ukomeye uzaza kuri bose, kandi kurimbuka gutunguranye kuzagera ku isi ubwo Umugeni wanjye azatwarwa mu mutekano. Kuko nzavanaho ubuza, kandi ikibi cyose kizaba kidakumiriwe rwose, kandi urubanza rukomeye ruzamanurwa ruvuye mu ijuru, nk'uko byanditswe muri cya gitabo gikomeye cy'ibihishurwa byanjye, ari cyo gitabo cy'Ibyahishuwe! Nuko rero ntegetse urubanza kuri mwe, mwese abanduza Itorero ryanjye: iminsi yanyu irabaze, kandi vuba aha imitima yanyu izabatererana ubwo uburakari bwanjye buzahishurwa. Mwa bahanuzi b'ibinyoma b'imburamumaro binjiye mu Torero ryanjye rwihishwa, mwigishije ubupfumu buvanze n'ububisha hamwe n'Ijambo ryanjye ryera n'inzira zanjye zera, kandi muzabishyura.

Mwatumye abato banjye bacumura kandi bafatwa mu byago bikomeye, ku buryo nzabibishyura incuro ijana mu rubanza rwanjye kuri mwe. Mwasobanuye ukundi amagambo mu byanditswe byanjye byera, mwiganye ibyanditswe byanjye byera mubyandika bundi bushya kandi muhindura amagambo y'Imana Ihoraho, ndetse n'amagambo ya Yehova, kandi muzabishyura iteka ryose. Mwinjije inyandiko zangiritse mu Torero ryanjye kimwe n'uko ba sokuruza banyu b'imburamumaro baroze amariba kandi bakaroza ibyokurya byahawe gutunga abantu, kandi muzabishyura iteka ryose. Buri jambo ryose ry'imfabusa mwavuze, buri giceri cya nyuma cy'indamu mbi mubika kugeza murimbutse, buri gitekerezo kibi cyose mwigeze kugira, buri ntego mbi yose mwigeze gukurikira bizabagaragarizwa, kandi muzabishyura, ndetse kugeza ku giceri cya nyuma; kandi ntimuzabasha na gato guhagarara imbere yanjye, kandi muzajya mu mwanya wanyu mu muriro Data yateguriye Umubi n'abamarayika be.

Ariko mwumve ko mutari igitangaza kandi nta bitangaza bibarimo, kuko muri intumwa z'ibinyoma, abakozi bariganya, bihindura intumwa za Kristo. Kandi ntibitangaje, kuko Satani ubwe yihindura marayika w'umucyo. Nuko rero, si igitangaza niba abakozi be nabo bihindura nk'abakozi bo gukiranuka, iherezo ryabo rizaba rihwanye n'imirimo yabo. Kuko ari ko nandikiye Abakorinto!

Muzakomeza imirimo yanyu mwuzuza ibyaha byanyu kugeza ku ndunduro kandi mwuzuza iteka ryose ibihano byanyu n'umubabaro wanyu, kuko uburakari bwanjye bubagurumanira, kandi muzasanga ba so mu muriro. Kuko muri urumamfu mu ngano, kandi nzabarandura kugeza ku ndunduro; kuko Data, ari we Yehova, yavuze ko mutazigera mugira umuzi cyangwa umurage, kuko imbuto yanyu ari iy'umubi, kandi mwese muri abicanyi n'abanyabinyoma. Kuko nabwiye iki ba so i Yudaya muri iyi Yubile nyinshi zashize? Mukomoka kuri so umuzimu, kandi irari rya so ni ryo mushaka gukora: yari umwicanyi uhereye mbere na mbere, kandi ntiyagumye mu kuri, kuko nta kuri kumurimo. Iyo avuga ikinyoma, avuga ibye: kuko ari umunyabinyoma, kandi ni se wacyo.

Mumenye ibi, mwese abavukiye mu gutuka Imana, ndetse no gutuka Yehova ubwo ba so b'imburamumaro bageragezaga guhindura ishusho y'Imana, iyo Adamu yaremwemo, ikaba ibizira byakorewe kurimbura abantu, iminsi yanyu yenda gushira. Kuko ari nde mu bahanuzi ba so batatoteje? Kandi bishe abahanuye mbere iby'ukuza k'Umukiranutsi; uwo mwebwe ubu mwabaye abagambanyi n'abicanyi be. Ntimuzarokoka, uko mwakwibira ikuzimu kose ngo mwihishe uburakari bwanjye; uburakari bwanjye buzababona, kuko nta gishobora guhishwa amaso yanjye, kandi isi ni iyanjye n'ibiyuzuye, kuko nayiguze amaraso yanjye bwite ba sokuruza banyu bamennye batuka Imana, ndetse bashyiraho kashe y'iherezo ryanyu mu muriro. Ndetse nanjye, Yesu Kristo, uwazamutse mu ijuru kandi agahabwa icyubahiro, ndaje kubareba, kandi ntimuzarokoka.

Nimumve, yewe mwa baguye mwe, ndetse namwe mwese mwanteye icumu, kuko mwibwiye muti, twatsinze Yehova kandi twishe umwana we yatumye kuturwanya ngo yigarurire isi, kandi turatsinze iteka ryose, kuko Yeshua yapfuye, nuko mwishima mu bupfu bwanyu! Mwa bapfu b'abibone batagira ukwiyumvisha! Ntimwasobanukiwe ko Imana yari kunzura, ibohoye ububabare bw'urupfu: kuko bitari bishoboka ko mfatwa narwo. Mwakomerekeje agatsinsino kanjye gusa, kandi nta mbaraga mumfiteho, kandi ntimwasobanukiwe ko Isumbabyose, ari we muremyi wanyu, Yehova, yaberetse gusa amabanga y'imburamumaro!

Kuko yari azi ko muzamuhinduka we n'ibyaremwe bye kandi mukagerageza gutwara ibyo yanteguriye, Umwami Yesu Kristo. Abagwaneza banjye bazaragwa isi, kuko isi ni iyanjye n'ibiyuzuye. Kuko mwigishije abantu ubumenyi bubujijwe; mwigishije intambara; mwayobeje abagore mu kurimbisha ingohe zabo basiga amarangi mu maso kugira ngo mubakoreremo irari ryanyu; mwangije Ubusitani bw'Imana mutera urumamfu mu ngano, kandi buri rumamfu mwateye rwose ruzarandurwa, rubohwe, kandi rujugunywe mu muriro hamwe namwe! Kuko mwatsinzwe ubwo nitangaga ku bwanjye kugira ngo abantu bakizwe, kandi Imana ikanzura mu bapfuye ngo sinzongere gupfa ukundi!

Ariko mwari abapfu cyane ntimwasobanukirwa ko natanga ubuzima bwanjye kubera urukundo rw'Imana yaremye muri njye, kuko ndi umukiza w'isi! Urukundo rwa Yehova ntirushobora gutsindwa, kuko Yehova adatsindwa, kandi nta hantu na hamwe mu byaremwe hari ikiremwa nka we, kuko ari Isumbabyose, kandi ubwiza bwe n'icyubahiro cye bifite imbaraga zose uhereye iteka kugeza iteka! Kuko nababwiye nti, "icyo ukora, gikore vuba!" Mu bujiji bwanyu n'ubwibone ntimwasobanukiwe ko nemeye gufatwa n'abakozi banyu b'imburamumaro bakoze igikorwa kibi cyo kumena amaraso y'umwere! Mu bujiji bwanyu bw'ubwibone, ntimwamenye ko nari mfite imitwe cumi n'ibiri y'abamarayika ntegeka, kuko igihe cyose nari gushobora kubahagarika. Ariko se ubundi ubushake bwa Yehova bwari gusohora bute, uretse binyuze mu rukundo rwanjye no kumvira Data? Kuko urukundo rwa Yehova nta n'umwe warutsinda! Oya, haba mu ijuru cyangwa mu isi, haba mu buryo ubwo ari bwo bwose cyangwa iteka ryose!

Mwa baguye mwe, ndetse namwe batware b'iyi si yaguye, mwa ba Satani mwe, mwese muzashya mu muriro udashira kubera urwango rwanyu rutari urwera n'inzira zanyu mbi cyane zo kutumvira no kurimbura. Nk'uko mwabikoreye umwe muri aba batoya, abavandimwe banjye, ni njye mwabikoreye. Yewe mwa barinzi baguye, ndetse namwe bamarayika batumvira mwataye umwanya wanyu wa mbere, nta mahoro mufite! Muzajugunywa mu muriro mutazigera muvamo, kandi muzashya rwose. Urumamfu rwanyu rwose rw'ikizira rutari urwera mwateye murenga imbibi za Yehova mu guhindura imiterere y'ingirabuzimafatizo hagati y'umuntu na marayika, hagati y'umuntu n'inyamaswa, hagati y'umuntu n'ibiremwa byo mu nyanja, hagati y'inyamaswa z'amoko atandukanye, ndetse n'ikizira cyose mwakoze kizajugunywa mu muriro hamwe namwe, kuko nta mahoro cyangwa imbabazi mufite!

Mwakurikiye ba Satani bagerageje kurimbura ibyaremwe bya Yehova, ndetse kugeza n'aho munyica, njyewe Yesu Kristo, ubwo nitangaga mu maboko ye mbikunze nk'Ipasika itunganye y'isi, bityo ngashyiraho kashe y'incungu y'abantu kandi ngashyiraho kashe y'iherezo ryanyu iteka ryose mu miriro mikomeye itazigera izima! Muzishyura rwose ibyaha byanyu byose n'ibizira byanyu, kandi buri gikorwa kibi cyose mwigeze gukora kizabagarukaho incuro ijana, kuko bose bazahemberwa ibihwanye n'imirimo yabo. Kandi inzira zanyu mbi zose zizishyurwa ku mitwe yanyu inshuro nyinshi, kuko mwibwiye ko mushobora kuntsinda, kandi mu bwibone bwanyu muracyatekereza ko mushobora gutsinda kandi mwaratsinzwe rwose.

Umva amagambo yanjye, yewe nzoka ya kera, kuko urubanza rwawe ruri kuri wowe kandi mbona ubwoba bwawe, kuko utinya ejo hawe hazaza kuruta ikindi cyose, yewe ntare y'imburamumaro itagira amenyo! Guhora ni ukwanjye, kandi nzishyura, kandi ibihe byawe byageze. Uburakari bw'Imana n'uburakari bw'umwana w'intama bugiye kurekurwa ubwo nzamenagura ibimenyetso birindwi, ubwo abamarayika birindwi batumwe kuvuza impanda z'imanza, kandi abamarayika birindwi batumwe bafite ibyago birindwi bya nyuma, kandi bizarekurwa, kuko muri byo ari ho huzuye uburakari bw'Imana. Ibyago byinshi bizabageraho mwese, ubwo nzahana rwose kandi nsenye ubwami bwanyu. Umwami wanyu ukomeye nzamutsinda kandi umuhanuzi wanyu w'ikinyoma nzamutsinda, kandi bazajugunywa ari bazima mu nyanja y'umuriro yaka amazuku; kandi aho bazabategereza ngo mubasange, kandi aho muzahora mugoswe n'umuriro n'amazuku, kandi urubanza rwanyu rugiye gutangira!

Mugeni wanjye, witinya! Amagambo yanjye y'urubanza si ayawe ahubwo ni ayo kuguhumuriza, kuko nagukijije uburakari bugiye kurekurwa, kandi nzaguhisha mu mutekano n'ingororano ikomeye, kandi uzabana nanjye iteka ryose. Kuko nagutoranyije, kuko naguhamagaye, kandi uri ubutunzi bwanjye bukomeye cyane, kandi uri ubutunzi bukomeye cyane bwa Yehova; ndetse uri isaruti y'igiciro cyinshi, kuko nagutanzeho byose. Ngira inama Umugeni wanjye gushaka ubushishozi no kuza aho ndi gushaka ubwenge, kuko nzakwigisha. Kuko bamwe muri mwe bakurikiye zimwe mu nzira z'aba bahanuzi b'ibinyoma; nimugaruke gusa mwihana ku Mwami wanyu, nanjye nzabakira.

Kuko babashutse bakoresheje ubupfumu bwa Gikristo, kandi mwizeye ibinyoma byinshi mwagurishijwe, ndetse bababwira ko ari ukuri, ariko ni ibinyoma by'ibizira. Aya mariba adafite amazi yagerageje kubankura, kandi ibyo ntibishoboka, nuko rero ntimutinye, kuko ntazahakanwa! Nzakomeza kubabohora kugira ngo mubohoke by'ukuri, kandi sinzacecekeshwa! Ndabarwanirira mu buryo bwinshi cyane mutazi. Kuko mwese mwashyizweho ikimenyetso mbere y'uko muvuka, kuko Data yabamenye ndetse mukiri mu nda za ba nyoko, kandi yategereje buri wese muri mwe igihe mudashobora kwiyumvisha, kandi mwahoranye umwanya rwagati mu mutima we. Mwashyizweho ikimenyetso mukivuka, kandi abamarayika banjye babarinze ku itegeko ryanjye kandi barabarinze barabakingira, kuko ari ko batumwa. Ese ibi birabatangaza? Ese muzumva icyo Abaheburayo bavuga? Ariko se ni nde mu Bamarayika yigeze abwira ati, icara iburyo bwanjye, kugeza aho nzashyirira abanzi bawe munsi y'ibirenge byawe? Ese bose si imyuka ikora umurimo, itumwa gukorera abazaragwa agakiza?

Mugeni wanjye, muri abaragwa b'agakiza muri njye, Yesu Kristo. Mwahamagariwe muri njye; mwarinzwe muri njye kandi murindwa nanjye; mwahawe amahirwe yo kumva kubwiriza kw'ukwizera kunyerekeyeho kugira ngo mungire Umwami wanyu, niyo mpamvu nabashyizeho kashe y'uwo mwuka wera w'isezerano. Mumenye ko kashe idashobora kumenwa, kandi mugiye gucungurwa byuzuye kugeza iteka ryose, kuko nta gitekerezo mufite cy'ubwuzuye bw'umurimo w'irema ngiye gusohoza muri wowe, Mugeni wanjye! Mumenye ko abaguye n'urumamfu rwabo babagirira ishyari, kandi banga icyo Yehova akunda cyose, kandi nzabahorera rwose ku mitwe yabo, kuko ari njye uzakomeretsa umutwe w'iyo nzoka ya kera, kuko byatangajwe, kandi icyo Yehova atangaje ntigishobora guhinduka.

Nta n'umwe ushobora kubantandukanya; nta bwinshi bw'ikibi bushobora kumena iyo kashe; muri abanjye kandi muri aba Data, kuko ibyo Data ampa byose nzabisubiza Imana kugira ngo tube byose muri byose! Kandi yampaye ibintu byose mu ijuru no mu isi, uretse ku ntebe ye y'ubwami aranduta!

Kandi njyewe, Yesu Kristo, ndabatangariza aya magambo, kandi ni njye, Yesu Kristo, ucira urubanza abanzi banyu, kandi nzabisohoza kugeza ku giceri cya nyuma. Mugeni wanjye, urukundo rwanjye kuri wowe ntiruzima, kandi naguteguriye umwanya, kuko mu nzu ya Data hari amazu menshi! Nuko rero vuba cyane muzashyingiranwa nanjye iteka ryose, kandi uko ni ko muzahorana nanjye, kandi amaherezo muzasobanukirwa ubukomeye bw'intego yanyu muri njye mwaremewe gusohoza!

Kuko muri njye, Yehova yabahaye ejo hazaza n'intego, ndetse n'intego y'ibihe igiye kubahishurirwa, kuko ndaje! Kandi sinzihakanwa. Mutegereze kuboneka kwanjye. Mutegereze ubwiza bw'ukuza kwanjye, kuko bugiye kurasa ku isi ubwo nzabonekera mu bicu n'ijwi riranguruye, n'ijwi rya marayika mukuru, n'impanda y'Imana, kuko mbabwira ukuri ko ndetse n'abamarayika bategereje iyo mpanda igiye kuvuga, kandi biteguye! Munyitegure kandi murebe, murinde, mwitegereze nk'uko mwabitegetswe; kuko vuba aha muzambona n'ubwiza bwanjye Data yanyambitse, kuko ari ko nzabambika, kuko muzaba nkanjye, kandi muzambona uko ndi.

Ndi Umwana w'Intama w'Imana!

Ndaje gusohoza uburakari ku bwami bwose bwa Satani, kandi ntibazarokoka.

Ndi umukiza w'Isi!

Ndi Yesu Kristo kandi guhora ni ukwanjye kandi nzishyura!

Ndi uzamenagura umutwe w'inzoka, kuko Imana Ishoborabyose yabivugiye! Ngiye guhishurirwa mu bicu ku bw'Umugeni wanjye, uwo nzatwara.

Ndi uje ku ifarashi yera.

Ndi uwitwa uwizerwa n'uw'ukuri, kandi mu gukiranuka nzaca urubanza nteze intambara.

Ndaje!
