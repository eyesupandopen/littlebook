Mu Mirimo ya Yohana Umuhishuzi
2 Kamena 2024

Ku 144,000: Mwange Ibinyoma Byabo

Mutege amatwi ijwi ryanjye kandi mwumve ubwenge bwanjye, kuko ubwenge burangurura mu mayira: mbese muzumva ijwi ryanjye? Ndi Yeshua HaMashiach, ndetse umwana wa Yehova, waje mu mubiri kandi nkaba umwana w'intama w'Imana ukuraho ibyaha by'isi. Mwumve ko isi mubamo atari ishusho y'ikinyoma abarezi banyu b'aba-Jesuit bashushanyije kugira ngo babayobye. Benshi muri mwe mwigishijwe ibinyoma by'aba-Jesuit, ndetse mwiga kurwanya kwiga. Iyi ntambara ku kuri yashyiriweho kubumba imitima y'abantu ngo igandukire Satani kandi igerageza gukuraho no kwigarurira ubushake bw'umuntu kugira ngo umuntu akurikire buhumyi inyigisho ze.

Amateka y'ukuri ntashobora kumvikana binyuze mu binyoma by'abafilozofi b'aba-Jesuit, kuko ari abanyabinyoma bacuruza ibinyoma mu buriganya bukomeye. Ibyanditswe birasobanutse kuri iyi ngingo, kandi Abaroma batangaza ukuri kuvuzwe neza: Kuko umujinya w'Imana uhishurwa uva mu ijuru, uje ku kutubaha Imana kose no gukiranirwa kw'abantu, bapfukirana ukuri mu gukiranirwa kwabo.

Ese mwaretse ayo magambo agera mu mitima yanyu? Abantu ku bushake bapfukirana ukuri mu kutubaha Imana no gukiranirwa, kandi umujinya wa Yehova uri ku babikora. Nuko rero uburezi bwanyu bwahinduwe ibihimbano n'ibinyoma byabo.

Bakoze ibi kugira ngo bategure inzira y'ikinyoma gikomeye kigiye kuza, kandi kugira ngo ubwami bwa Satani bubohorwe ngo butegeke mu buriganya bukomeye n'ibinyoma. Ibinyoma bababwiye mu burezi bikora kugira ngo bihumye amaso ya rubanda ku kuri: Nta bivejuru (aliens) bibaho, ahubwo ni abamarayika baguye berekeza mu nyanja y'umuriro kugira ngo babohwe kandi batwikwe iteka ryose. Ntimwizere ibinyoma byabo ahubwo muhungire kuri njye, Kristo Yesu, kuko muri njye ari ho haba ukuri kose kuzababatura. Ndababwira ibi bintu kugira ngo mutazayobywa hamwe n'abazifatanya na Satani bakakira ikimenyetso cy'inyamaswa kigiye kuza.

Amateka yumvikana binyuze mu gukurikira ukuri kwashyizweho mu Byanditswe no mu bitabo bitari ibya Bibiliya nka 1 Enoki, Yasheri, Yubile, n'ibindi, uhereye mu ntangiriro. Kuko aba bantu batubaha Imana n'ibinyoma bakoresha kugira ngo bahishire ukuri ni igice gusa cy'intambara ikomeye, ndetse intambara y'imbuto hagati ya Yehova na Satani ikomeje kugeza n'uyu munota, nyamara yatsinzwe iteka ryose binyuze mu mirimo yanjye y'impongano. Kuko intambara yatangajwe na Yehova kuri ya nzoka ya kera kubera ishyari ryayo, ndetse uhereye mu ntangiriro y'irema ry'umuntu ubwo Satani yageragezaga kurimbura Adamu na Eva. Yehova yabwiye inzoka mu itangazo rye ati: Nzashyira urwango hagati yawe n'uyu mugore, no hagati y'urubyaro rwawe n'urubyaro rwe: ruzagukubita ku mutwe, nawe uzarukubita ku gatsinsino. Nuko intambara z'imbuto zikomeza uko kuva icyo gihe.

Mu Busitani bwa Edeni, Satani yabashije kwanduza urubyaro rwa Adamu, aciraho iteka Adamu munsi y'imbaraga no kwangirika kw'icyaha. (Muri ibi umwana w'Imana yarabonekeye, kugira ngo asenye imirimo ya Satani.) Kuko Satani yakoresheje ubumenyi bwe bwabozwe bw'icyiza n'ikibi kugira ngo yanduze umuntu byimazeyo, kandi ku bumvira ijwi ryanjye, uko kwangirika gukurwaho muri njye, Kristo Yesu, uwo muri we muri icyaremwe gishya kidashobora kwangirika na rimwe. Imbuto yanjye ntishobora kwangirika, kuko Yehova yayigize idashobora kwangirika, kandi binyuze mu kuvuka gushya nashyizeho ikimenyetso ku batoranyijwe banjye n'abizerwa ndetse na wa mwuka wera w'isezerano!

Yohana mu rwandiko rwe rwa mbere atangaza ukuri kw'iteka: Umuntu wese wabyawe n'Imana ntakora icyaha: kuko imbuto yayo iguma muri we, kandi ntashobora gukora icyaha, kuko yabyawe n'Imana.

Mu gihe gito nyuma yo kugwa kwa Edeni, aho Adamu yakoze icyaha akava mu butungane akajya mu kwangirika, abarinzi bamanukiye ku Musozi wa Herumoni mu minsi ya Yaredi. Kuko bose biyemeje binyuze mu ndahiro zabo bwite, ndetse indahiro yabo itagatifu aho biyemeje iteka ryose ku cyaha n'igihano cy'iteka. Bakoze ibi kugira ngo bose bishakire abagore mu bo bahisemo bose, bityo bahindura gahunda ya kamere yabo.

Baje ku muntu batanga ubumenyi bwabo buboze bw'abamarayika baguye, kuko bifatanije n'ubwigomeke bwa Satani.

Bigishije abantu gukora inkota, n'ibyuma, n'ingabo, n'ibyuma bikingira igituza, kandi babamenyesha ibyuma byo mu isi n'ubuhanga bwo kubikora, n'ibikomo, n'imitako, n'imikoreshereze ya antimoni, no gukora ibitsike byiza, n'amabuye y'agaciro y'amoko yose, n'amabara yose. Bigishije uburozi, no gukata imizi, gukemura uburozi, kuragurisha inyenyeri, amatsinda y'inyenyeri, ubumenyi bw'ibicu, ibimenyetso by'isi, ibimenyetso by'izuba, n'ingendo z'ukwezi.

Nuko abarinzi baguye batwara abagore babo basambana nabo, nuko baratwita bababyarira ibihangange binini. Kandi mu gihe runaka iyi mbuto itagatifu yamize ibyari ku isi byose, n'ibyahawe umuntu byose, kandi amaherezo imira abantu benshi uko bashoboraga. Kuko bari abanywi b'amaraso n'abarya abantu, kandi bakoze ibibi byinshi mu maso ya Yehova, kuko ubuzima bw'umubiri buri mu maraso kandi umuntu yaremwe mu ishusho ya Yehova. Barakuze barororoka kandi amaherezo bashyiraho ubwami bwabo ku isi yose. Dore! Amatongo y'inyubako zabo nini aracyari ku isi nk'umuhamya ubashinja. Bategetse isi muri ibyo bihe, kandi baremewe kurimbura umuntu. Nuko ibizira by'amoko yose birakorwa, kuko ibyanditswe byera ndetse bitangaza uko byakozwe.

Nk'aho bitari bihagije ko abantu bararikira umubiri udasanzwe, bagasambana n'abamarayika baguye, kandi bakabyara ibihangange, kubera abarinzi bose bakoze ibyaha ku nyoni, n'inyamaswa, n'ibikururuka, n'amafi. Uhereye mu ntangiriro y'irema, Yehova yategetse buri bwoko bw'ubuzima ukwabwo. Ariko abarinzi babi bivanzemo n'irema, ndetse bavangavanga ibyaremwe by'ubwoko bumwe n'ibyaremwe by'ubwoko busa kugira ngo bazane ukudahama kw'ingirabuzimafatizo no guhindura irema rya mbere rya Yehova ry'ubutungane bwuzuye. Iki gikorwa cyarasubiwemo, bavangavanga ibyaremwe bisa by'ubwoko bumwe n'ibyaremwe bisa by'ubundi bwoko kugeza bageze ku cyo bashakaga. Nuko isi yariho icyo gihe, yanduzwa n'ibizira byinshi bibi byakoze ibyaha bikomeye kuri Yehova, umuntu, n'irema ryose. Mwumve, abaguye bashaka isi yabo bwite, ndetse isi y'umwijima itandukanye rwose na Yehova, kandi bazaragwa ibyo gusa mu nyanja y'umuriro yateguriwe na Yehova ubwe.

Nuko iminsi ya Nowa iragera kandi isi yariho icyo gihe, ihagaze mu mazi no mu mazi, irengerwa n'amazi irarimbuka. Kuko amasoko y'imuhengeri hakomeye yamenetse, biteza urugomo rukomeye ubwo isi yasatagurwaga n'imbaraga zikomeye z'amazi y'urugomo, n'amadirishya y'ijuru arakingurwa, yemerera amazi yo hejuru y'isanzure (no munsi y'isi) gutwikira no kurimbura isi yose.

Nuko Enoki umwanditsi asobanura iherezo ry'ibi bihangange gutya, kuko Yehova yabivuze: Kandi none, ibihangange, byakomotse ku myuka n'umubiri, bizitwa imyuka mibi ku isi, kandi ku isi ni ho hazaba ubuturo bwabyo. Imyuka mibi yavuye mu mibiri yabyo; kuko byabyawe n'abantu, ku barinzi bera ni ho hari intangiriro n'inkomoko yabyo ya mbere; bizaba imyuka mibi ku isi, kandi imyuka mibi ni ko bizitwa. Naho imyuka yo mu ijuru, mu ijuru ni ho hazaba ubuturo bwayo, ariko naho imyuka y'isi yavukiye ku isi, ku isi ni ho hazaba ubuturo bwayo. Kandi imyuka y'ibihangange irababaza, irakandamiza, irarimbura, iratera, irarwana, kandi ikora irimbura ku isi, igateza ibibazo: ntirya ibyo kurya, ariko nyamara irasonza kandi ikagira inyota, igateza ibyaha. Kandi iyi myuka izahagurukira abana b'abantu n'abagore, kuko yavuye muri bo. Mwumve ko ibyenda bya cumi by'iyi myuka mibi biri ikuzimu kubera ubusabe n'isengesho rya Nowa, kandi umuntu yahanganye n'icya cumi cyayo gusa kuva icyo gihe. Ibyo bigiye guhinduka ubwo ikuzimu hazakingurwa kandi yose ikarekurwa.

Nuko isi, n'ibizira byose by'abarinzi, birarimburwa. Abarinzi ubwabo babohewe mu manza zabo zitandukanye mu bihe mirongo irindwi, kugira ngo bazarekurirwe urubanza mu gihe cy'urubanza rukomeye. (Kuko urubanza rukomeye rugiye gutangira.) Icyakora, Nowa yabonye ubuntu mu maso ya Yehova kuko yari umukiranutsi, kandi umuryango we Yehova yawurokoye ari abantu umunani bose hamwe, hamwe n'ibindi byaremwe byinshi mu Nkuge. Nowa yirinze we n'umuryango we kwivanga mu ngirabuzimafatizo mbere y'umwuzure. Ariko umuhungu we Hamu n'urubyaro rwe rubi amaherezo bari kuzanga Nowa ndetse na Yehova ubwe (uwabaremye akabarokora), bifatanya n'ubwigomeke bwa Satani.

Amagambo yo mu Itangiriro 6 yujujwe n'ibyaha by'urubyaro rwa Nowa, ndetse umurongo wa Hamu. Hariho ibihangange mu isi muri iyo minsi: ndetse na nyuma y'ibyo. "Iyo minsi" yari iminsi kuva kuri Yaredi kugeza kuri Nowa, kandi "ibihangange mu isi" byari urubyaro rw'abarinzi n'abakobwa b'abantu mbere y'umwuzure. "Nyuma y'ibyo" ni nyuma y'iminsi ya Nowa, ubwo ingirabuzimafatizo z'umuntu zongeye kwanduzwa n'ubumenyi bw'abamarayika baguye. Ndabasaba kutongera kwizera ikinyoma cy'inyigisho itari yo ya Seti ahubwo mwizere amagambo y'ibyanditswe byera, kuko aya magambo ari ukuri.

Igihe isi yagabanywaga na tombola mu bahungu ba Nowa (Shemu, Hamu, na Yafeti), umugabane wa Hamu wari uwo kuragwa ibihugu byo mu majyepfo, nuko biba ibye. Icyakora, Kanani yararikiye igice cy'umurage wa Shemu yimukira mu bihugu bitari ibye kwigarurira. Kuko Kanani ari we Nowa yavumye. Kuko Kanani yasize umurage we ashinga umujyi wa Tiro ku cyabaye inkombe ya Foyinike kigana i Sidoni, aho Yezebeli akomoka. Yabikoze kugira ngo abe hafi y'Umusozi wa Herumoni, aho abarinzi bamanukiye kandi aho nahinduriwe ishusho.

Kanani yabonye ibyo abarinzi basize inyuma mbere y'umwuzure wa Nowa, kuko bashakaga kubungabunga ubwigomeke bwabo ndetse na nyuma y'umwuzure, kuko bashushanyije byinshi mu bitare n'amabuye. Kanani yakoresheje ubumenyi bwabo bw'abamarayika baguye mu burozi bukomeye, kandi imihango myinshi mibi yakozwe n'Abanyakanani, kuko ndetse batambaga abana babo bwite. Bari babi cyane kandi bakwirakwiriye ku buryo yabashije kwanduza igihugu cya Kanani n'ibizira bye bishya, aho Satani yakoreraga kubera ibyaha bye.

Nuko amoko y'ibihangange bya nyuma y'umwuzure, ndetse amoko mabi y'Abanyakanani, akomoka ku byaha n'ibicumuro n'uburozi bya Kanani. Mu gihugu Kanani yibye Shemu haje Abaheti, n'Abahivi, n'Abaperizi, n'Abagirigashi, n'Abamori, n'Abayebusi. Bari abantu bateye ubwoba, ndetse Abarefayimu. Hari amoko menshi kuruta ayavuzwe, ariko aba bateye ubwoba bari bategereje mu gihugu cy'isezerano mu gikorwa cyo kwica abarinze umurongo wa Kristo, ndetse ba sokuruza banyu b'Abisirayeli n'ababyeyi bari Abaheburayo.

Yosuwa yinjiye mu gihugu cy'isezerano kugira ngo yegukane ibyahawe Shemu mu buryo bwemewe na tombola n'igihugu cyari icya Isirayeli mu buryo bwemewe n'isezerano rya Yehova ubwe. Ariko abana ba Isirayeli ntibashohoje byuzuye amategeko ya Yehova yo kutagira urubingo na rumwe basiga ruzima, kuko bari babi kandi batari abantu, kandi imigenzo yabo yari mibi, kandi bakoze ibibi byinshi mu maso ya Yehova, kuko iyo yari kamere yabo. Nuko Yehova abwira Isirayeli nk'uko byanditswe mu byanditswe: Ariko nimutirukana abaturage b'icyo gihugu imbere yanyu, nuko bizaba ko abo muzareka bagasigara muri bo, bazaba amahwa mu maso yanyu, n'amahwa mu mbavu zanyu, kandi bazabababaza mu gihugu mutuyemo. Kandi niko byagenze.

Amateka mu gice yerekana ikwirakwizwa n'igenda ry'ibi bizira, ndetse urubingo rwatewe na Satani ubwe, kuko babanye n'ingano kuva mu minsi ya kera. Bashyizeho imico myinshi bakoresheje ikoranabuhanga ryabo n'ubumenyi bw'abamarayika baguye; ni abubatsi b'abahanga, ni abarozi, benshi muri bo bafite ubushobozi budasanzwe, kandi bafitanye isano na se Umwanzi, ndetse basangiye imiterere ye n'irari rye. Uru rubingo rwinjiye muri buri gice cy'umuryango, kandi si bose ari ibihangange. Benshi basa n'abantu, keretse ko ari ibyaremwe bivange byahinduwe iteka ryose kuva ku bishushanyo bya Yehova mu irema kandi ntibazacungurwa. Ntimubatinye, kuko uri muri mwe aruta uri mu isi.

Uru rubingo rushaka gutwara ibyagenewe abana b'Imana binyuze mu murage n'isezerano. Nta masezerano ashobora kubaho nabo, kuko ari aba se Umwanzi, kandi irari rya se bazarikora. Yari umwicanyi uhereye mu ntangiriro, kandi iyo avuga ikinyoma avuga ibye bwite, kuko ari umunyabinyoma kandi ni se wacyo. Bazahindura rwose, banduze, barimbure, bice, basahure, banyage, kandi bakore ibibi byabo byose ku kintu cyose cy'Imana bararikiye. Nta mahoro bafite, kandi kamere yabo yaboze rwose. Bahinduye isi, kandi bazashyiraho ubwami bwabo isaha imwe nk'uko ikiyoka gifite ingoma yacyo ku isi. Nyamara bashaka kongera gushyiraho igihe cyabo cya zahabu, ariko nta gihe cya zahabu bazagira uretse urubanza rukomeye, kandi ntibazarokoka.

Uru rubingo rubi rwanduje ubumenyi, kwiga, amateka, idini, siyansi, kandi bakomeje kwanduza. Bakwirakwije ikoranabuhanga ry'abamarayika baguye kandi nta gishya munsi y'izuba. Uko ibihe byuzuye, batwikiriye isi yose ibinyoma, kandi isi yose iri mu mubi. Binyuze mu kumurikirwa kwabo, urubingo rw'aba-Jesuit rwanduje rwose umuryango kandi ruyobora umuntu mu nyanja y'ibinyoma, aho ubuhungiro bwonyine ari ukwihana no kwanga ibyaha byose n'ibinyoma. (Kuko Roma yaremye na Isilamu; mwige kugira ngo mumenye.) Ubumenyi bwabo bw'abamarayika baguye n'ibinyoma bihagaze birwanya amateka y'ibyanditswe ahubwo bigatanga imigani idafite ishingiro ihumya intekerezo n'imitima y'abantu. Nuko rero, muve hagati yabo kandi mwitandukanye.

Isi y'uyu munsi ni aho abantu benshi cyane bibagiwe muri rusange kubera inyigisho z'aba-Jesuit. Benshi muri mwe mwibagiwe atari gusa amasezerano n'inzira za Yehova, ahubwo mwibagiwe rwose abo muri bo n'abo ba sokuruza banyu bari bo by'ukuri. Ibi byakozwe ku bushake binyuze mu ntambara, ibinyoma, n'ibikorwa byo kongera gutuza abantu mu myaka yashize byatatanyije benshi mu mfuruka enye z'isi. Kuri ibi birengagiza ku bushake ko bidashoboka guhisha ikintu icyo ari cyo cyose mu maso ya Yehova; kandi njye, Yeshua HaMashiach, nzi abanjye n'abo mpamagarira agakiza.

Bamwe mu batatanye bari imfubyi kandi bakuwe mu miryango yabo binyuze mu ntambara cyangwa gushimutwa. Izi roho zakuze ku mateka y'ibinyoma, ndetse zibeshywa n'ibitekerezo byiza by'ahashize h'ikinyoma, nuko ukuyoba gushinga imizi cyane mu mitima y'abantu, kandi benshi bahumwe amaso iteka n'ibinyoma byemewe nk'ukuri. Ariko ku bahindukirira njye, Yeshua HaMashiach, muzamenya ukuri kandi ukuri kuzababatura. Umuryango wanyu w'ubu wubatswe ku matongo n'ivu by'imico y'abaguye ba kera, kandi imbaraga nyinshi zashyizwe mu guhisha ukuri. Icyabayeho kizongera kubaho, kandi nta gishya munsi y'izuba.

Nta gitambo cyanjye cy'impongano n'itangwa ryanjye ry'agakiza, mwese mwari kuba mwararimbutse. Nuko rero muze kuri njye, Yeshua HaMashiach, kandi nzabayobora mu kuri kose kandi nzabigisha amateka yanyu y'ukuri. Nzabahuza n'inkomoko yanyu y'ukuri, kuko benshi muri mwe mukomoka mu gihugu, nubwo agakiza ari ak'amahanga yose. Nzabigisha mu nzira z'ubutungane, kandi muzabaho mu gukiranuka binyuze mu kwizera njye, Yesu Kristo, kandi muzabaho iteka ryose. Nta kibanga kitazajya ahagaragara, kandi byose bizashyirwa ahagaragara rwose, kugira ngo ukuri kumenywe na bose.

Nuko rero mureke kwishimira ibinyoma uko mwibera mu kwirara, kuko ibihe byo kwirara byarangiye. Ibihe byo guhishura biri hafi. Kuko dore! Nk'uko byari mu minsi ya Nowa, niko bizaba. Satani yakoresheje urusobe rwe rw'ibinyoma kugira ngo ategure urubuga rw'ibinyoma bye bikomeye by'imperuka, kandi igice kinini cy'isi kizakurikira ibinyoma bye. Kuko ubwami bwose bwa Satani buzabohorwa buve ikuzimu, kandi ibihangange bizongera kubana n'abantu, kuko bije gusohoza umujinya wa Yehova.

Satani avuga ibinyoma bikomeye, kandi aba baguye bazavuga ko bavuye mu zindi si kandi ko bateye imbere kuruta umuntu mu myaka ibihumbi byinshi. Bazaza batanga ubumenyi busa n'ubwiza, kandi baziyambika ineza y'ikinyoma. Ariko aba ni ab'ubwami bwa Satani, nuko ntimuzagwe mu binyoma. Ntimuzakurikire inzira z'isi, kuko isi izatangazwa n'ubwami bwa Satani, ndetse ivuga iti, ni inde uhwanye n'inyamaswa?

Nzabahisha, nzabarinda, nzabaha imbaraga, nzabayobora intambwe ku yindi, kandi nzasenya rwose umwenda w'ibinyoma watwikiriye isi mu buriganya. Njye, ndetse Yeshua HaMashiach, nzabaha imbaraga kandi ntume mukora ibitangaza byinshi bikomeye muri iki gihe cy'amakuba ya Yakobo. Nuko ndababwira ibi bintu, kugira ngo ukuri kubabature, kandi kugira ngo mubature by'ukuri kandi ntimugwe mu kinyoma cyabo gikomeye. Kuko imfatiro zacyo ari ibinyoma, uburiganya, n'ibinyoma byakwirakwijwe n'urubyaro rw'inzoka.

Ndi inzira, ukuri, n'ubugingo; nta muntu uza kwa Data keretse anyuzeho.

Ndi uwabahamagariye ubwiza n'ingeso nziza.

Ndi uzategeka iteka ryose nk'Umwami w'abami n'Umutware w'abatware.

Ndi Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda.

Ndi utegeka ingabo zo mu ijuru.

Ndi uzabonekera mu bicu kugira ngo nkoranye Itorero ryanjye.

Ndi ufite imfunguzo z'urupfu n'ikuzimu, kandi nzaca urubanza mu gukiranuka.

Ndi uwo buri ivi rizapfukamira kandi buri rurimi ruzatura.

Ndi uwo NDIHO Ukomeye yatumye, nuko mwumve amagambo yanjye.

Dore! Ndababwira ibi bintu atari ukubaha isomo ry'amateka ryuzuye ahubwo ari ukubayobora intambwe imwe yisumbuyeho kugana ku kuri. Mu gihe runaka, byose bizajya ahagaragara kandi ubwami bw'ikiyoka buzashyirwa ahagaragara, kuko njye, Yeshua HaMashiach, mbitangaje, kandi ndi uwo Yehova yatumye.
