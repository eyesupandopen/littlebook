Kugira ngo wumve umuryango wawe nyakuri n'uko wabayeho n'uwo umuhungu wawe ari we n'impamvu namukijije nkamurinda ari muzima n'icyo byari bigamije.

Nubwo uza kwandika ibi unyuze mu marira menshi ndagusaba gusa gukomeza kwandika, kuko numva ukuntu ibi bikugoye kuko nabanje nawe muri buri ntambwe wateye kugira ngo unyihanganire mu byo nagutumye gukora, kugira ngo ngukomeze kandi nkuyobore. Kandi ibi bishingiye ku kwizera gusa, atari ku gisekuru cyawe. Kuko abana bo kwizera baragwa amasezerano yahawe ba data, atari abana b'umubiri. Nyamara, umenye ko narinze amaraso y'umuryango wanjye wa hafi, yego, abavandimwe banjye na bashiki banjye nk'uko itegeko rya Adonayi-YHVH riri kugira ngo mu by'ukuri, amaraso yanjye akomeze kubaho kandi warahishwe unatatanyirizwa mu mahanga kugira ngo nkurinde kandi nkugumane uri muzima. Naho ubundi, Heylel Ben Shachar yari kunyica wese iyo ntaguhisha muri ubwo buryo, kandi nta kurobanura ku butoni muri ibi bikorwa.

Kandi ibi byaremewe kugira ngo ugaragare nk'uwatsinzwe ku banzi bawe gusa kugira ngo uhaguruke ukomeye mu kwizera gukomeye kugira ngo bose bamenye ko nta n'umwe ushoka gukura n'umwe mu bato banjye mu maboko yanjye akomeye kandi yahawe imbaraga na Adonayi-YHVH cyane cyane abato baza kuri njye mu nzirakarengane yabo nk'uko wabikoze kenshi mu busore bwawe no mu guhamagarwa kwawe ku gicaniro aho nagushyizeho ikimenyetso burundu ku bw'imigambi yanjye, nziza akarengane kari kwemererwa kugira ngo gukomeze umwanzuro wawe bityo ubu ntushobora kumeneka ku itegeko ryanjye n'imbaraga zose z'inkuba indwi nagushyizemo iteka ryose.

Umva mu gihano cyanjye no gukubitwa kwanjye kwatanzwe mu rukundo rwinshi nyamara kugatangwa mu buryo bukomeye ku bw'agakiza kawe abanzi bawe bibwiye mu bujiji ko urupfu rwawe rwegereje biturutse ku kwangirika kw'ingingo witeje mu bwigomeke bwawe butagira ishingiro bwatewe n'ibyiciro byinshi bya gahunda bagushyizemo utabizi mu busore bwawe. Nyamara ibi byaremewe kugira ngo wumve neza inzira zabo kandi ucurirwe iteka ryose kubarwanya mu bwumvikane bwuzuye n'ubushake n'imigambi bya Adonayi-YHVH na Yeshua HaMashiach, kugira ngo umenye abanzi bawe neza kandi wumve kamere mbi y'ibi biremwa kugira ngo umpagararire mu kwiyemeza kwuzuye kandi ubashyire ahagaragara mu buhamya, ndetse no gukoresha amayeri yabo n'imyitozo yabo kubarwanya mu rubanza rwabo ruri hano rwuzuye.

Ndetse n'aba barinzi baguye na Heylel Ben Shachar ubwe ubu wamutsinze n'imbaraga zidahagarikwa kandi z'urugomo ziri muri izo nkuba indwi zirekuwe mu isi ubu. Ndetse n'ibi biremwa bibi uzazanira ubutabera bukomeye, ingororano, n'umujinya bizatangwa nta mbabazi kandi mu buryo bumwe bakoze ku bandi.

Si uko nemeye cyangwa ngo nifatanye mu byaha byawe, nanga urunuka mu isesmidwa rikomeye (kimwe n'uko ubikora ubu mu kwuzura kwawe) ahubwo ko nari kukurinda kandi nkagukiza uri muzima ku batumwe kukwica, mu ntego zabo n'amayeri yabo nagukijije uri muzima mu buntu bwanjye urukundo impuhwe n'imbabazi kuri wowe Biblaridion:144.
