Ubuhamya Bubiri: Itorero
Mu Mirimo ya Petero
19 Mata 2024
Inkuba ya 5 n'iya 6

Abakiranirwa Ntibazaragwa Ubwami bw'Imana!

Kuva kuri Yeshuwa Hamashiya:

Mwana wanjye, nushyikira amagambo yanjye, ukabika amategeko yanjye muri wowe; kugira ngo utege ugutwi ubwenge, kandi uhugurire umutima wawe kujijuka. Ntimunangire imitima yanyu, nk'uko byari mu gushotorana, ku munsi w'ibigeragezo mu butayu: kandi mumenye ko jyewe, Umwami Yesu Kristo, mporana namwe kandi mpora mu buzima bwanyu uko munkomeyeho n'agakiza kanjye, kuko ndi umutwe w'Umubiri, kandi ndi Umwami wanyu wizerwa, Mugeni wanjye nkunda. Kuko nabateguriye ahantu n'ahazaza, kandi hari ingororano ikomeye ku bahamagawe banjye, bizerwa, kandi batoranyijwe.

Sinabahamagariye igihombo n'igihano cyangwa ngo mbahamagarire isoni no kwicira urubanza; ntimwasomye ko nta rubanza ruri ku bari muri Kristo Yesu, badakurikiza ibya kamere y'umubiri ahubwo bakurikiza iby'umwuka? Bityo mwizere amagambo yanjye! Sinabahamagariye kubaho mu gicucu cy'ibyaha byanyu bya kera, cyangwa kubaho mu gicucu cy'ibikorwa byanyu byo mu busore, ahubwo nabahamagariye gupfa ku nzira zanyu za kera z'ibyaha no kwambara ubuzima bushya muri njye uko mwiyambura kamere yanyu y'umubiri, uko mwiyambura ibyaha byanyu mukagendera mu buzima bushya muri njye. Dore! Nkora byose bishya! Ese ibi si byo byiringiro byanyu?

Bana banjye, nabakuye mu kwangirika kw'iyi si y'ubu igenda ipfa, kandi nabakuye mu mbaraga z'umwijima mbimurira mu bwami bwanjye, ubwami bw'Imana, buyobowe nanjye, Umwami w'abami n'Umutware w'abatware, uwo Yehova ari Imana, kuko nta yindi iriho. Nabahamagariye ubwiza n'ingeso nziza muri njye, kandi nabakuye mu bwami bwa Satani, ubwami bw'umwijima, mbazana mu Bwami bwanjye, aharangwa umucyo! Nuko rero, kanguka wowe usinziriye, uzuke mu bapfuye, nanjye, Yesu Kristo, nzaguha umucyo.

Ntimwasomye mu nzandiko noherereje Mugeni wanjye, izo naboherereje, mwebwe Torero ryanjye bwite? Ntimuzi yuko abakiranirwa batazaragwa ubwami bw'Imana? Ntimwishuke: abasambanyi, cyangwa abasenga ibishushanyo, cyangwa abasambakazi, cyangwa ibitingwa, cyangwa abagabo bendana, Cyangwa abajura, cyangwa abifuza, cyangwa abasinzi, cyangwa abatukana, cyangwa abanyazi, ntibazaragwa ubwami bw'Imana. Kandi uko ni ko bamwe muri mwe mwari muri: ariko mwaruhagiwe, ariko mwejejwe, ariko mwatsindishirijwe mu Izina ry'Umwami Yesu, no mu Mwuka w'Imana yacu.

Ntimwumva amabwiriza yanjye, yewe torero ry'akazuyaze rya Lawodikiya? Sinabahamagariye gusubira mu kamere yanyu y'umubiri, cyangwa ngo mbahamagarire gukomeza mu nzira zanyu zo gukiranirwa. Mutekereze, kandi muzirikane; uwibwira ko ahagaze, yirinde atagwa! Mwiyirinde ubwanyu, n'inyigisho: mukomeze muri byo: kuko nubikora, uzikiza wowe ubwawe, n'abakumva. Mwumve, bana banjye b'akazuyaze, ko mudashobora kubaho ubuzima bwo kuringaniza hagati y'icyiza n'ikibi, mwibwira ko mutunganye imbere yanjye, mu gihe muhitamo ikibi gito mu bibi bibiri mu mihango yanyu y'amatora ntemera cyangwa ngo nshyigikire.

Mumuvemo, bantu banjye, kuko NTAGIZE igisubizo cya politiki ntanga, kandi rwose sinabahaye umuyobozi w'isi ngo yongere agire ikintu icyo ari cyo cyose gikomeye. Mwarashutswe, kandi murimo gutegurwa gukurikira no kwemerera umwana wo kurimbuka ugenda hagati yanyu ku mugaragaro, nubwo atarahishurwa neza kugeza ubu. Ariko mwebwe musaba kurobanura muzamenya uwo ari we, kugira ngo mwirinde ibishuko bye. Oya, kuringaniza si ibyanyu, kuko muri abana banjye n'abavandimwe, kandi sinatsindiye agakiza kanyu ntanga ubuzima bwanjye nk'incungu ya benshi binyuze mu kuringaniza na Satani; kuko kuringaniza ari ukwifatanya n'ingabo zabo. Nuko rero ntimwibeshye: Cyangwa muhagarara ku ruhande rwanjye mu gukiranuka no kwera nyakuri, cyangwa mwifatanyije n'ingabo za Satani mu bwami bwe bupfa, nuko MWIHANE!

Mwunguka iki mu kuringaniza n'ikibi? Ntimwumva ko mugomba kuneshesha ikibi icyiza? Nuko muhagarare mushikamye muri njye kandi mureke kwivumbagatanya nk'ifi ivuye mu mazi. Benshi muri mwe baracyatwarwa n'imiyaga yose y'inyigisho n'uburiganya bw'abantu, ibyo bakoresha babarindiriye kugira ngo babashuke. Jyewe, Kristo Yesu, navuze, kandi sinzacecekeshwa. Benshi muri mwe mwiyanduje ubwanyu n'itorero ryanjye, kandi ndababaza nti: muzahagarara hagati y'ibitekerezo bibiri kugeza ryari? Niba Umwami ari Imana, mumukurikire: ariko niba ari Bayali, mumukurikire. Mu magambo make, ntimushobora kuringaniza ngo muhitemo ubwoko ubwo ari bwo bwose bw'ikibi ngo mwibwire ko muhagaze ku ruhande rwanjye; iyo muhisemo ikibi, muhitamo inkomoko y'icyo kibi nk'umwami wanyu n'umutware wanyu mukaba abagaragu b'icyaha. Ntimuzi yuko uwo mwihaye kuba abagaragu bo kumwumvira, muri abagaragu b'uwo mwumvira: haba iby'icyaha kizana urupfu, cyangwa ibyo kumvira bizana gukiranuka?

Munyumve kandi munyumve neza, Torero ryanjye; mumenye ko mpagaze nzinutswe imigenzo yogeye mu gihugu cyanyu no ku isi hose, iyo mwemeye mukayireka ikinjira mu Torero ryanjye kugira ngo ibabaze abana banjye. Nk'uko byari mu minsi ya Nowa, ni ko bizaba. Iminsi ya Nowa yari igihe isi yategekwaga n'ibizira bibi byakozwe n'abamarayika baguye, urubyaro rwabo bwite, urubyaro rw'ikibi rwakorewe kurimbuka kwanyu kwuzuye: kandi bazagaruka, kuko dore, nzazana ibihangange kugira ngo bisohoze umujinya wanjye. Ibi babigezeho bakuraho kandi bahutaza imbibi kamere Data yashyizeho kugira ngo amahoro no gukiranuka biganze, ariko mu gukuraho no kwambuka mu buryo bw'ubwiyandarike imbibi zigaragara muri kamere ubwayo, izo Data yashyizeho, benshi muri mwe mwiyandujije binyuze mu kwemera ikibi cyogeye mu bihe byanyu. Ikintu cyabayeho, ni cyo kizabaho: kandi igikozwe, ni cyo kizakorwa; kandi nta kintu gishya kiri munsi y'izuba. Ntimwasomye, yewe mwa bafite ukwizera guke mwe? Gukuraho imbibi z'Imana ubwayo bitera ubusazi, n'indwara, n'urujijo rwa byose. Benshi muri mwe bishimira indwara zo mu mutwe, bibwira ko umugabo ashobora kuba umugore n'umugore akaba umugabo, kandi muyobora abana banyu bwite mu buyobe bwanyu. Benshi muri mwe bitabira amatorero ayobowe n'abakozi b'ibinyoma b'abatinganyi beruye kandi birarira mu ndwara yabo, kandi mukibwira ko bamvugira? Mumenye ibi: niba muri muri iyo mimerere y'icyaha, nzabarekurira irari murarikira cyane, kandi muzasarura imbuto z'inzira zanyu zanduye.

Dore! Ku bw'ibyo, Imana yabaheye imyifuzo iteye isoni: kuko n'abagore babo bahinduye imikoreshereze ya kamere bayigira inyuranye na kamere: Kandi n'abagabo na bo, bareka imikoreshereze ya kamere y'umugore, bashyuhirana mu rari rwabo hagati yabo, abagabo bakorana n'abagabo ibiteye isoni, kandi bihabwa ingororano ikwiriye kuyoba kwabo. Ariko oya, ntibihagije kuyoborwa n'ibitingwa n'ibinyoma byabyo; mwemerera abana banyu bwite kwangizwa ndetse no gukatwa ibice by'umubiri imbere yanjye.

Hari ingaruka zikomeye zibitse ku bw'ibi bikorwa, kandi ntibizabura guhanwa. Kandi nyamara mukomeza mu busazi bwanyu, kandi nimutihana, nzabaruka mbakure mu kanwa kanjye. Nimuze aho ndi mu kwihana, mwicare mu magunira no mu ivu, kugira ngo menye ukuri ko kwihana kwanyu kandi muririre kandi muborogere ibyaha byanyu kugira ngo mutazishyura ikiguzi mu rubanza. Benshi muri mwe muri abibone cyane kuko mugerageza kungira Umwami mu gihe mugifite umutima utizera, kuko umutima ari wo umuntu yizeza akabarwaho gukiranuka. Nuko rero mugire ukwizera kugira ngo mukizwe; ukwizera kwanyu kuzerekanwa kuri bose binyuze mu kwihakana icyaha no kuva mu nzira zanyu zanduye. Abanyabyaha bagenda impande zose, iyo abantu babi cyane bashyizwe hejuru. Ariko muracyemera inzira z'abatinganyi, kandi mufasha kuzana ikibi gikomeye mu gihugu, kugeza n'aho mutora abahohotera abana ngo bayobore ibihugu byanyu. Mubura ndetse n'urwego rw'ibanze rwo kurobanura, kandi ntimukurikiza inzira yanjye; kandi nimukomeza, nzabarekurira rwose imyifuzo yanyu iteye isoni kugira ngo umubiri urimbuke kugira ngo umwuka ukizwe.

Reka mbahumure amaso, mwebwe abiyobagiza ku bushake kandi b'impumyi, mwebwe mwicara mu mwanda no mu mwijima, mwivuruguta mu byondo. Ntimushobora kwemerera ikibi ngo mwibwire ko munkorera, kuko narwanyije ikibi n'ibishuko kugeza igitonyanga cya nyuma cy'amaraso kimenetse giturutse kuri njye kijya ku butaka butukura, kuko ndi Adamu wa nyuma kandi nahinduwe umwuka utanga ubuzima. Benshi muri mwe mwahumwe amaso n'amagambo meza n'imbwirwaruhame nziza kandi mwemerera byimazeyo abagizi ba nabi n'abantu babi bahiga abana. Bahohotera abana, iyicarubozo abana ku bw'irari ryabo ribi: Kuko ari ko umujinya w'Imana uza kuri Babuloni y'Amayobera, ariko benshi muri mwe mukomeza kwemerera izo nyamaswa z'inkazi zashyiriweho umuriro w'iteka utazigera uzima. Mumuvemo, bantu banjye, cyangwa muzasangira ibyago bye n'urubanza ruteye ubwoba rumuzaho; kandi narababuriye. Kuko Kristo ahuza ate na Beliya? Nimusangira ibinyoma bye, muzasangira ibyago bye; nuko rero mwitandukanye rwose mu magambo no mu bikorwa, kandi ntimwemeranye na bo na gato. Ntimukabeho ubuzima bwo kwirara, bwangiritse; kuko nzahana ababikora bose. Mwibwira ko kubera ko mufite ubuzima bw'iteka mudashobora guhura n'igihombo gikomeye n'ingaruka? Nzabambura ingororano zanyu nzihe abandi, kandi muzaba aboroheje cyane mu bwami bwo mu ijuru nimukomeza, kuko ari ko Yeshuwa Hamashiya, Umukwe, abwira Mugeni wanjye!

Kuva kuri Yehova, Imana Ikomeye Ihoraho:

Yemwe, mwa bateshwa agaciro mwe bangiza irema ryanjye, mwa bateshwa agaciro mwe mwigurishije kuri Satani, mwa bateshwa agaciro mwe mwagize Satani Imana yanyu, mwa bateshwa agaciro mwe mwanga umwana wanjye Yesu Kristo n'agakiza natanze mu izina rye, mwa bateshwa agaciro mwe mwemereye abana banyu bwite gushya mu muriro wa Moloki. Nzabaha isi mwifuza, kandi nabateguriye isi ntarimo, isi itarimo umwana wanjye Kristo Yesu, isi ifite inyanja nini y'umuriro n'amazuku byagenewe mwebwe gusa.

Nzabaha umwijima mwifuza cyane, kuko umucyo wanjye utazaba uhari, kandi muzamara iteka ryose mutandukanijwe nanjye, Yehova, Imana ikomeye y'icyubahiro, iyo ubwiza bwayo n'umucyo wayo nta mubiri ushobora kureba kandi iriho uhereye kera kose kugeza iteka ryose. Mwa bagome mwe, babi mwe mwarimbuye irema ryanjye uhereye mu ntangiriro mu rari rwanyu n'urwango n'ishyari, mumenye ko mwiyikoreje igihano gikomeye n'umubabaro mutazigera mukira kandi no ku bayobejwe bose n'ibinyoma byanyu bakanga Yesu Kristo, bakanga guhindukira bava mu binyoma byanyu baza mu kuri kwanjye.

Mwibwira ko kugoreka inzira zanjye no gukuraho imbibi zanjye ari ikintu cyoroheje, ariko muzumva umujinya wanjye ozakanya ikirimi cy'umuriro cya mbere kibakozeho, kandi muzatwikirwa n'umuriro n'amazuku mutazigera mucika. Byongeye kandi, mwishe, mwasambanyije ku gahato, mwasahuye, mwahohoteye, mwababaje, mwishe, mwarimbuye, kandi mwifatanyije mu byaha bikomeye cyane, ndetse mwifuza umubiri udasanzwe; kandi mwifatanyije n'abamarayika baguye, nuko rero nzabahuza n'inyanja y'umuriro nateguriye Umubi n'abamarayika be, kandi ntimuzacika. Nta mahoro mufite.

Nzabashyiraho ubutabera, kuko ndi Imana y'ubutabera, kandi ndi Imana yo guhorera. Nyamara, muzahagarara imbere yanjye mu bwibone, mwiburanira, mwibwira ko nzasubira inyuma imbere y'inzira zanyu za gisitani zo kuvuga ngo: kora icyo ushaka, ni ryo rizaba itegeko ryose. Nta mahoro mufite. Nzishyura, kandi muvuga ko ndi Imana y'urukundo (kandi ndi yo), ariko noneho mukitega ko ntazashyira ubutabera ku banduje irema ryanjye, ku bakoze iyicarubozo bakica umwana wanjye w'ikinege ubwo yitangaga nk'incungu ya bose; yego, mwitega ko ntazabashyiraho ubutabera ku bw'inzira zanyu ziteshwa agaciro mu gihe mwishe, mugakora iyicarubozo, kandi mugakomeretsa inzirakarengane kandi mwicishije inzara abana banjye ku bw'inyungu zanyu bwite.

Mu by'ukuri, Satani ubwe yarimbura ibyanjye byose ku bw'inyungu ze bwite z'umururumba, kandi n'ubwo bimeze bityo mugerageza kunshinja ubupfu mu manza zanjye zikiranuka iyo nje kurengera abanjye. Iminsi yanyu yo kuyobya abana banjye igiye kurangira. Dore! Inyamaswa yanyu n'umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma bazarimburwa rwose, kandi nzabajugunya ari bazima muri ya nyanja nini yaka umuriro n'amazuku; bazabategereza ngo mwifatanye na bo mu murage wanyu.

Urubanza rukomeye ruri kuri wowe, yewe gikomangoma cy'umwijima; ubwami bwawe n'abagukurikira bose bigiye kurimburwa rwose. Kuko mutari imana, kandi ntimushobora kwikiza ubwanyu buteshwa agaciro mu kuboko kwanjye, kandi nzababoha mu mwijima w'icuraburindi mwifuza cyane, ndetse no mu kuzimu kwimbitse cyane nzababohisha urunigi rukomeye mudashobora guca. Yego, mu kuzimu muzabayo umunsi umwe, kuko umunsi umwe ku Mwami ari nk'imyaka igihumbi, n'imyaka igihumbi nk'umunsi umwe. Kandi n'ubwo bimeze bityo nzababohura igihe gito kugira ngo mushuke kandi mukoranye aba nyuma b'abo bantu b'ibizira babakurikira n'inzira zanyu. Kandi nyuma nzabatwika mwese n'umuriro, n'intebe z'imanza zizashyirwaho, kandi nzabacira urubanza mwese inkozi z'ibibi, kandi muzajya mu gihano cy'iteka kure y'amaso y'Umwami. Muzashya imbere y'abamarayika n'imbere y'Umwana w'intama, kandi bazabareba mu mubabaro n'igihano, kuko mwabahemukiye bose, kandi muzishyura byuzuye ibyaha byanyu iteka ryose.

Hanyuma muzashyirwaho ikimenyetso muri gereza yanyu, kandi muzibagirana, kandi ntimuzigera mwongera kwibukwa, kuko ibya mbere bizashira kandi byibagirane. Kuko dore, nzarema ijuru rishya n'isi nshya mubarizwamo gukiranuka; nanjye, Yehova, Imana ikomeye ihoraho y'imana zose, kuko nta wundi umeze nkanjye, dore, nzagira byose bishya, kuko jyewe, Yehova mbivuze! Yehova, Imana ikomeye ihoraho, azashyira intebe ye y'ubwami ku isi, kandi ubuturo bwanjye buzaba mu bantu, kandi ntimuzigera mwongera kubaho, yemwe mwa bateshwa agaciro mwe, kandi muzashya iteka muri gereza yanyu yibagiranye y'umubabaro mudashobora gucika, kuko nzashyiraho ikimenyetso kandi nta n'umwe ushobora kugica.

Nyamara, benshi muri mwe bumvikana na Satani banshinja ubupfu bwo kwifatira ubutabera no guhorera mu maboko yanjye kugira ngo nshyikirize imiriro abigize abakwiriye igihano cy'iteka. Mwumve amagambo yanjye, yemwe batuye isi: Umwana wanjye araje, kandi aje n'uguhorera gukomeye, umujinya, n'uburakari ku bw'ibibi byakozwe n'amaboko yanyu. Ibihe by'urubanza, ndetse n'urubanza rw'umunsi mukuru wa Kristo rugiye guturika ku isi hose, kandi nta muti uzabaho ku babi, nta muti kuri Satani, kandi nta muti ku bafatanyije na we bose, kuko bose bazajya mu kurimbuka kw'iteka, kandi nzabasubiza ibyo bagiriye inzirakarengane byose.

Ku bw'ibyo, nzabashyiraho ibyifuzo by'imitima yabo mibi n'imirimo yabo mibi yose ibagarukeho iteka ryose. Murebe umujinya wanjye ubira kandi mumenye ko ibihe byo gusubizaho byose biri hafi, kandi nta mibare isigaye ku masaha yo mu ijuru, kuko imibare yose yaguye iva ku isura y'isaha, kuko igihe kigeze! Ngira inama abasoma aya magambo bose kwirukira ku mwana wanjye Yesu Kristo mu kwihana bakamugira Umwami; kuko nta n'umwe uzaza aho ndi keretse binyuze muri Yesu Kristo, kuko nta rindi zina natanze ryo gukirizwamo, kandi ibigirwamana byanyu byose bizashya. Haracyari igihe cy'agakiza, kandi umuntu wese uzambaza izina ry'Umwami kugeza ku iherezo azakizwa. Nuko rero mwirukire kuri Yesu Kristo mu gihe mugishobora, kuko umunsi w'Umwami ugeze, kandi nzarekura Umwana wanjye, n'umujinya w'Umwana w'intama uzashira. Musome Umwana kugira ngo arakara, mukarimbukira mu nzira, ubwo umujinya we uzaba ucanwe ho gato: Hahirwa abamwiringira bose.


Ibyo kwifashisha: Yesaya 13:1 mu Busebutuwaginta: Ndabategeka, kandi ndabazana: ibihangange biraje kugira ngo bisohoze umujinya wanjye, byishima icyarimwe kandi bitukana.
