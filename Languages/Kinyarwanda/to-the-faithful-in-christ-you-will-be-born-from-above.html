Ku Bizerwa muri Kristo: Muzavuka Gitangaza: Igihe cyo Kuvuka Kwanyu Bushya Bwuzuye Kirageze

Nimwumve Nimwumve, Amagambo ya Yeshua HaMashiach kandi mwunamishe imitima yanyu ku ijwi ry'Amagambo yanjye Yera kandi musobanukirwe ibihe murimo bakunzi banjye, kuko nta n'umwe muri mwe wigeze abaho muri ibi bihe, ndetse n'ibi bikorwa ubu bitondekanyijwe kugira ngo Babuloni y'Ibanga icirwe urubanza rutunguranye kandi rwihuse ndetse no kurimbuka, ndetse n'izi Leta Zunze Ubumwe, bigiye kurekurwa mu buryo butunguranye kandi butitezwe ubu ku ijwi ry'iryo tumba ry'impanda riteganyijwe kuvuga mu buryo butunguranye kandi butitezwe ubu. Ndetse no ku ijwi ry'iyo nduru ikomeye kuko ibihe birageze bakunzi banjye, kandi ibi bihe bibagezeho nk'uko byatangajwe kandi bigasezeranywa. Nzabakoranyiriza aho ndi mu buryo butunguranye kandi butitezwe ubu, kugira ngo musanganire mu kirere nk'uko byasezeranyijwe, bityo ndabategeka nti: MUBE MASO!

Mwitegure Mugeni wanjye, kuko isaha y'igicunguro cyanyu cyuzuye ibagana ifite imbaraga zose kandi ibyitezwe birageze! Ku bw'ibyo, nimuze byuzuye kuri Kristo Yesu kandi mwicuze byuzuye ku Mwami wanyu w'Iteka ryose wo Gukiranuka, kugira ngo ni ukuri ni ukuri, mbeze byuzuye ibyaha byanyu byose kandi kugira ngo nzatume munesha ibintu byose mu ntsinzi ikomeye y'iyi si yuzuye ibyaha, nk'uko nanesheje ibintu byose by'iyi si yuzuye ibyaha mu ntsinzi ikomeye.

Kandi uko ibi bise bya nyuma byiyongera, musobanukirwe ko byegereje kurangira, ndetse no kuri uwo mwanya nyawo aho iki gihe cya Kairos cya Harpazo kizasozwa ubwo umwana wera azongera kuvuka ari muzima mu ijuru ry'amajuru, ndetse akabyarwa kandi akavuka ari muzima iteka ryose kandi adashobora kubora iteka ryose mu cyumba cy'intebe y'ubwami cya Adonai-YHVH, nk'uko byatangajwe kandi bigasezeranywa mbere n'Amagambo y'intumwa yanjye ikomeye n'umugaragu wanjye, ndetse uwo ari we wabaye igikoresho cyo gutanga no kohereza aya Magambo mu itangazo no mu rindi tegura no kwitegura kw'abakobwa banjye b'abanyabwenge, kugira ngo mwese mutandukanywe n'abo bakobwa b'abapfu bakwena kandi bakirengagiza Amagambo yanjye, kandi kugira ngo mbahuze byuzuye n'ingororano yanyu yuzuye yateguriwe mwese byuzuye mu maboko yanjye, yateguriwe ko nyibaha mu gusohoza isezerano ry'ingwate y'umutungo waguzwe.

Nyamara, isaha imwe hamwe n'inyamaswa iregereje kandi irageze kandi hari amajwi menshi mabi yongorera kandi avuga ubupfu bwinshi! Ni ukuri, ni ukuri, amajwi menshi y'ibinyoma ubu arataka cyane, atanga amagambo y'ibinyoma mu butayu ku bafite amatwi yishimagura. Ni ukuri, ni ukuri aba Yezebeli bamenyereye ubutayu nk'indogobe y'ishyamba mu gihe cyayo ihamagara abahisi bayo b'abadayimoni, kandi mu gihe cyayo ni inde ushobora kuyinyuraho adasimbukiye muri izo nzabya zicuramye, zifite urubanza rutazatinda.

Nyamara uko niko iyi myuka ya Yezebeli imeze ihanura amagambo y'ibinyoma by'uburiganya n'ikinyoma, ndetse n'amagambo y'inyiganano benshi bahitamo aho guhitamo Amagambo yanjye atunganye yo Gukiranuka yatanzwe aturutse mu mutima wa Adonai-YHVH ubwe, mu maso ha Kristo Yesu, ari we kandi Yeshua HaMashiach! Nyamara, reka uku gutandukana kugumeho kuko guterwa n'inkota y'umwuka wanjye, gusa ntimwemere kugwa mu ruhande rw'ibumoso rw'inkota yanjye cyangwa muzagira uruhare mu manza zabo, kuko mwanze kwitandukanya byuzuye ku itegeko ryanjye!

Selah.

Kandi ibi ni ukubera ko benshi muri mwe muhitamo amajwi menshi yanduye kandi yanduza yatanzwe n'abahanuzi b'ibinyoma boherejwe guhumanya abakobwa banjye b'abanyabwenge bagishaka kuguma mu bakobwa b'abapfu, nubwo nshaka kubatandukanya byuzuye n'amagambo yabo y'ibinyoma yatanzwe kugira ngo arangaze kandi agabanye imitima yanyu ku ijwi ryanjye ryumvikana ndetse n'Amagambo yanjye atunganye y'Umwami wanyu w'Iteka ryose wo Gukiranuka, kandi ndegera kugira ngo mbakoranye Mugeni wanjye n'abashyitsi bacu bityo ntimucike intege, kandi mwizere Amagambo yanjye Bakunzi banjye kandi muruhukire mu rukundo rwanjye kuri mwe kuko umutwaro wanjye woroshye kandi umugogo wanjye utaremereye.

Nimuze kuri njye byuzuye bakunzi banjye, nimuze kuri njye byuzuye mudafite ubwoba kandi n'imitima yanyu yose mu byitego byuzuye kandi mufite isura ifunguye byuzuye, ntimugire icyo musiga inyuma. Dore! Mbona byuzuye imbere mu mitima yanyu n'ubwenge bwanyu, kandi nsobanukirwa imikorere y'imbere y'ubwenge bwanyu ndetse n'ubwoba bwanyu bwinshi budafite ishingiro, bityo ndababwira na none nti ntimutinye!

Kandi se ni nde wundi mukwiye gutinya bakunzi banjye? Mu kuri kw'iteka mbafashe mwese cyane mu biganza byanjye, ku buryo nta kiremwa na kimwe mu bwami bwose bw'abaguye, (oya, ndetse na Heylel Ben Shachar ubwe cya kiyoka kinini cya kera), ushobora no kurekura gusa ukugumya kwanjye kw'iteka mbarindisha byuzuye! Kuko nta n'umwe mu byaremwe byose bya Adonai-YHVH ushobora kubakura mu maboko yanjye akomeye y'imbaraga n'ububasha bw'iteka.

Ni ukuri, ni ukuri, nta n'umwe ushobora guhagarara abwanya namwe iyo mukomeje kumvira Umwami wanyu w'Iteka ryose wo Gukiranuka, bityo ndababaza na none, ni nde mukwiye gutinya? NDAVUGA NDE!

Nimwumve uwo ndi we, bakundwa banjye, kuko ndi umwana w'intama wa Adonai-YHVH, ndetse n'umwana w'intama utagira icyaha iteka ryose kandi utagira inenge, ikizinga, cyangwa ubusembwa. Kandi mu mbaraga zanjye z'ubushake n'urukundo rudashobora kumeneka kuri mwe bakundwa banjye bizerwa, natanze ubuzima bwanjye mu gitambo kibabaza kubera ibyaha byanyu, ndetse bityo, kugira ngo ncungure abantu byuzuye mu kuboko kwa Heylel Ben Shachar, ndetse n'uwo mwana w'umwami muto watsinzwe. Kandi mu nzira ze z'ubunyamaswa, Heylel Ben Shachar yatambye umwana w'intama wa Adonai-YHVH mu rwango rwe rw'ubunyamaswa kandi rudafite ubwenge n'irari ry'ibintu byose byiza. Kandi mu kubikora bityo Adamu yacunguwe byuzuye n'amaraso yanjye atagira icyaha, kandi Adonai-YHVH yanzuye mu bapfuye, iteka ryose kugira ngo ntapfa ukundi kuko bidashoboka ko urupfu rumfata.

Nyamara niko bimeze, ko niyeretse Adonai-YHVH nk'Umutambyi Mukuru iteka ryose mu buryo bwa Melikisedeki kandi Adonai-YHVH yemeye igitambo cyanjye cyane ku buryo yahinduye Yesu w'i Nazareti umwuka utanga ubuzima uzabarema mwese bushya mu buryo butunguranye kandi butitezwe ubu kuri iyo nduru ikomeye kandi iryo tumba ry'impanda rikomeye riteganyijwe kuvuga mu buryo butunguranye kandi butitezwe ubu!

Aho nzabarema bushya nk'umuntu umwe mushya, aho nta Muyahudi cyangwa umunyamahanga: nta mbata cyangwa uwigenga: nta mugabo cyangwa umugore: kuko mwese muri umwe muri Kristo Yesu, ari we kandi Yeshua HaMashiach! Kandi kubera ko muri aba Kristo Yesu, noneho muri urubyaro rwa Aburahamu, n'abaragwa binyuze mu isezerano kandi babatuwe mu gukiranuka iteka ryose mu maboko y'abanzi banyu muri Kristo Yesu Umwami wanyu w'iteka, Umwami, Umutambyi Mukuru, na Mesiya, ku bw'ibyo gucungurwa kwanyu kuruzuye, kuruzuye, kuruzuye rwose!

Yego, gucungurwa kwanyu kuruzuye kandi kurarangiye ku buryo mubyukuri nta kintu na kimwe mubura kandi mwese mwaguzwe igiciro kidashobora kugereranywa iteka ryose! Bityo ndabaza, ni nde muri mwe ushobora gutanga amaraso atagira icyaha kugira ngo atangire ibyaha bya Adamu, wakoze icyaha igihe yari atagira icyaha? Nyamara So wo mu ijuru, ndetse Adonai-YHVH, NDIHO Ukomeye yatanze umwana we w'ikinege rimwe, bose kandi iteka ryose kugira ngo atangire ibyaha byose by'abantu!

Ku bw'ibyo, mwaguzwe igiciro, bityo nimumuhimbaze Adonai-YHVH bakunzi banjye kuko yamaze gutanga itegeko ryo kubakoranya byuzuye kuri icyo gihe cyiza nyawo, bityo ndabategeka nti: MUNEZERWE!

Nyamara, benshi muri mwe ntibasobanukirwa ubukana bw'iki gikorwa, ahubwo batekereza ko gucungurwa kwanyu ari cyo kintu cyonyine kigomba kugerwaho cy'ingenzi. Nyamara niko bimeze, ko benshi muri mwe ahubwo bibanda gusa ku isozwa ry'ikoranywa ryanyu aho muzahagarara mucunguwe byuzuye kandi mwahinduwe byuzuye iteka ryose mu ijuru ry'amajuru ubwo iryo tumba ry'impanda rizavuga, kuko rigiye kuvuga!

Kandi nubwo aya Magambo atanzwe nk'inkunga, ijwi ry'Abaheburayo rihagarara nk'umuhamya ushinja benshi muri mwe: Kuko nubwo mwari mukwiriye kuba abigisha ubu, dore musigaye mukeneye uwongera kubigisha ishingiro ry'ibanze ry'ijambo ry'Imana: kandi mwahindutse abakeneye amata, atari ibyo kurya bikomeye.


Rero ndabyongera, neza nk'uko byavuzwe kugira ngo Amagambo yanjye akomezwe:

Kandi nubwo aya Magambo atanzwe nk'inkunga, ijwi ry'Abaheburayo rihagarara nk'umuhamya ushinja benshi muri mwe: Kuko nubwo mwari mukwiriye kuba abigisha ubu, dore musigaye mukeneye uwongera kubigisha ishingiro ry'ibanze ry'ijambo ry'Imana: kandi mwahindutse abakeneye amata, atari ibyo kurya bikomeye.

Benshi muri mwe mwibagiwe amahame y'ibanze y'ibyavuzwe n'Imana ko mutegetswe kugumana Amagambo yanjye y'isezerano mu bwenge kandi mukibanda gusa kandi byuzuye kuri Kristo Yesu, ari we kandi Yeshua HaMashiach mu mutima umwe no kwitegura kw'ubwenge. Ko mwahindura ubwenge bwanyu n'imitima yanyu kandi mukagumana Amagambo yanjye y'isezerano akomeye kandi byuzuye mu bwenge bwanyu n'imitima yanyu natanze binyuze mu mukumbi wanjye muto wamenyesheje kandi ugatangaza kandi ugategura abantu banjye kuri iki Gitabo Gito ari cyo Ubutumwa Bwiza Bw'Iteka ryose bwahishuwe igice, nafunguye mu maboko y'intumwa yanjye n'abagaragu banjye, nk'uko byasezeranyijwe na Yohana Uhishurirwa muri yubile zose zashize ndetse n'igihe amagambo y'inkuba indwi yafungwaga kandi agahagarikwa mu Byahishuwe igice cya 10. Nyamara, benshi muri mwe nkunda, mu bupfu mwanga Amagambo yanjye mugahitamo abanyatewolojiya banyu b'abanyabinyoma, ibinyoma byabo bizahagarikwa mu rubanza rutunguranye. Ku bw'ibyo, nimubasohokemo kandi mwitandukanye muri byose kugira ngo mutagira uruhare mu rubanza rwabo.

Kandi ibi ni ukubera ko amagambo y'inkuba indwi ari ishyirwa mu bikorwa nyaryo ry'ibikorwa byatangajwe muri Apokalupsis ya Yesu Kristo, ndetse n'icyo gitabo gikomeye cyahishwe cya kera: Ibyahishuwe bya Yesu Kristo; cyatanzwe nkana igice kandi nkana kiduzuye. Ndetse bityo, kugira ngo abantu baze kuri Yesu Kristo Umucamanza Ukiranuka n'Umuvugizi kugira ngo nce urubanza rw'ubuzima bwanyu mu gukiranuka, ndetse nkuraho ibyaha byanyu niba hari uwakumvira mu kwizera n'urukundo kugira ngo yumvire amategeko yatanzwe n'ijwi ryanjye.

Nuko rero, mwumve ko inkuba indwi zihishuwe, kandi zikangutse, kandi ubu zikaba zikora ku buryo zidashobora guhagarikwa cyangwa ngo zikome mu nkokora na gato. Nuko murusheho kumva icyo ibi bisobanura bakundwa banjye, kuko nta gukererwa gukiriho, kandi njyewe, Kristo Yesu, ndatangaza mu ishusho y'intumwa yanjye ikomeye n'umugaragu wanjye, ko igihe kitazongera kubaho ukundi!

Nubwo, mu rubanza rwabo bamburwa ubumuntu bwose kandi bakamenyekana gusa nk'amasatani, kandi aba baserafi babi bazabohwa, bajugunywe mu miriro ishyushye cyane y'ubururu kandi igihano cyabo ni cyo gikomeye cyane mu baciriwe urubanza bose, bazahabwa imiriro y'igihano, babohewe iteka ryose kugira ngo bahabwe ingororano yuzuye n'urubanza rw'umuriro iteka ryose batazigera bacika, cyangwa ngo baruhuke na gato.

Nyamara, nzabahishura kuko bashaka kwihisha kuko bose ari abiyoberanya biyoberanya, kandi nta banga na rimwe ritazajya ahagaragara.
