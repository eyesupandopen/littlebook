Keretse ko, mu buryo butunguranye ku isi mutazaba mukihari, kandi mu mimerere yaguye ntimuzongera kubamo ukundi, kuko gucungurwa kwanyu kutakiri hafi, ahubwo kwabagezeho rwose. Ku bw'ibyo, mugume mwiteguye.

Ndavugisha abizerwa kandi ndasaba imitima yanyu kumvira amagambo yanjye byuzuye.

Sinkyimenywa mu buryo bw'umubiri ukundi.

Ndi umwuka utanga ubuzima.

Ndi Yeshua HaMashiach.

Ndi Umwami w'abami n'Umutware w'abatware.

Ndi umutambyi mukuru wanyu w'iteka ryose mu buryo bwa Melikisedeki.

Ndi uwo NDIHO Ukomeye yatumye kugira ngo byose bisohore.

Nohereje aya magambo binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye, kandi binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye aya magambo yoherejwe.

Ngira inama abumva aya magambo bose kumvira byuzuye amabwiriza yanjye no kumenya ko ubu ari cyo gihe kandi mu buryo butunguranye isohozwa rizaza byuzuye. Ndasaba imitima yuzuye y'abizerwa banjye, nuko rero mukomeze kwibatura mu maraso yanjye y'impongano kandi munyature ibyaha byanyu kugira ngo mbeze rwose mu maraso yanjye y'impongano. Ntimushukwe n'ibyiyumvo byanyu, kuko Babuloni y'Ibanga ihumye kandi ari igipfamatwi kandi yirengagiza nkana urubanza rwayo rwegereje, rwamaze gutangira kandi rudashobora guhagarikwa. Ku bw'ibyo, ntimugabane mu buhumyi bwayo kugira ngo mutazababazwa n'ibyago byayo, kandi mwitandukanye na bo kugira ngo mube mwiteguye rwose ukuboneka k'Umwami wanyu w'iteka. Dore, Mugeni wanjye! Umukwe araje, nuko rero mugumane amatabaza yanyu yuzuye, intambi zikase kandi zaka, mu byiringiro bikomeye by'Umwami wanyu uje, kuko ibihe byo gukoranya ni ibi, kandi ibi ni byo bihe byo gukoranya.
