1 Mutarama 2025

Ndi Umwami w'Iteka ryose wo Gukiranuka!

Dore! Nimwumve Amagambo ya Yesu Kristo kandi mugandure ibitekerezo byanyu, mukebwe imitima n'amatwi yanyu mu kumvira Amagambo ya Yeshua HaMashiach, nanjye nzaha ubwenge abanyabwenge kugira ngo umunyabwenge muri mwe yige kubaha Uwiteka kandi mumenye gutigisa imbere y'Uwiteka Nyiringabo, kuko uko ni ko Yahweh, NDI HO Ukomeye yandemye.

Uri inde, wa muntu w'umubiri we, ngo ugerageze kurwanya Yahweh, NDI HO Ukomeye, we Muvandimwe w'Iminsi, we Mwami w'Imyuka? Uri inde wa muntu w'umubiri we ngo wishyire hejuru mu kwibona no kwirata kuri Yesu w'i Nazareti ari we Kristo Yesu, we ufite izina risumba ayandi mazina yose, we Yahweh, NDI HO Ukomeye, yasangije intebe ye y'ubwami, we washyizwe hejuru ya byose uretse Yahweh ubwe kugeza ubwo abanzi banjye bazagirwa intebe y'ibirenge byanjye? Kandi ubwami bwanjye n'ingoma yanjye ntibizagira iherezo kandi nzashyikiriza Ubwami bwagandujwe Data w'Imicyo kugira ngo Adonai Yahweh abe byose muri byose!

Ese ikintu cyaremwe cyabaza uwakiremye kiti, kuki wandemye utya? Ese ibumba ryabumbwemo inzabya z'icyubahiro ryabaza umubumbyi riti, kuki wandemye utya? Kuki wampaye icyubahiro kingana gitya? Ndashaka kuba ikindi kintu kandi ahubwo nzabona icyubahiro cyabo! Ese hari umubumbyi utarimbura icyaremwe kibi gishaka kwigomeka ku kuboko kwe ndetse gishaka no kurimbura umubumbyi, hamwe n'ibindi byaremwe by'umubumbyi; nk'aho ibyo byashoboka?

Nyamara uko ni ko kugenda gushyize cyera kandi kw'abasazi kw'abaguye bigometse ku muremyi wabo binyuze mu kureka inshingano zabo n'imirimo yera kandi y'icyubahiro bahawe gusohoza mu gukiranuka, ndetse n'imigambi bahawe na Yahweh ubwe ku munsi yaremye abana b'Imana. Ndetse n'abamarayika bera Yahweh yaremye mbere y'uko arema isi, abo byanditsweho muri Yobu igihe yaremaga isi: Inyenyeri zo mu gitondo zaririmbiraga hamwe, n'abana b'Imana bose barangururaga amajwi y'ibyishimo! Kandi koko baranguruye amajwi y'ibyishimo ku bitangaza n'ibitangaza babonye mu maboko ya Yahweh igihe yaremaga isi. Kandi uko ni ko abamarayika baguye bari bameze, ndetse n'abana b'Imana baremwe mbere bakagirwa abanyacyubahiro n'abanyambaraga ku bw'imigambi yabo kandi Yahweh yari yababambitse icyubahiro abaha no kurusha abandi ubwenge kugira ngo bakunde Yahweh mu kumvira ijwi rye mu buryo bwuzuye kandi koko bari bahawe umwanya wa mbere w'icyubahiro baremewe mbere, kugeza ubwo bigometse mu irari ry'imitima yabo maze gukiranirwa kukabonekamo.

Kandi uko ni ko ba Satani bigometse mu ntangiriro ku muremyi wabo, ndetse n'abahoze bagose intebe y'ubwami ya Yahweh, ndetse n'intebe ye y'ubwami ubu igoswe n'ibihumbi icumi inshuro ibihumbi icumi kandi n'ubu Yahweh ni umutegetsi w'ikirenga kandi ntakeneye umujyanama. Nyamara uko ni ko byanditswe kuri iyi kiyoka kinini gitukura, gifite imitwe irindwi, n'amahembe icumi, n'amakamba arindwi ku mitwe yacyo ko umurizo wacyo wakuruye kimwe cya gatatu cy'inyenyeri zo mu ijuru, ukabijugunya ku isi: kandi uko ni ko ba Satani ndetse na kimwe cya gatatu cy'abamarayika bakurikiye ukwigomeka kwabo bajugunywe ku isi, ndetse no mu minsi igihe kwangirika kw'abamarayika kwagwaga kuva mu ijuru mu cyaha no kwigomeka, maze bahinduka umwijima w'icuraburindi nyuma y'ibyaha byabo, kandi uko ni ko bameze kugeza n'uyu munsi.

Kandi uko ni ko inzoka yegereye nyina w'abazima bose mu Busitani bwa Edeni mu bihe byashize, maze ishuka Eva kugira ngo arye ku kintu Yahweh yari yababujije kurya, kuko ku munsi bari kurya ku giti cyo kumenya icyiza n'ikibi, ndetse uwo munsi bari gupfa byanze bikunze. Nyamara, Eva yakiriye ibyo inzoka yamuhaye kuko yari yashutswe n'uburiganya bwayo maze ararya, kandi aha umugabo we uru rubuto rubujijwe maze Adamu ararya abishaka kandi mu gupfa barapfuye nk'uko byari byarasezeranyijwe, kandi abantu bose bashyizwe munsi y'urupfu kuva icyo gihe kubera icyaha cyabonetse mu maraso ye. Kuko Adamu yangiritse iteka ryose mu cyaha kandi ku bw'iyo mpamvu ni bwo Umwana w'Umuntu yagaragajwe kugira ngo nsenye imirimo ya Satani kandi nzane gucungurwa kuri Adamu waguye kuko ndi Adamu wa nyuma uwo Yahweh yagize umwuka utanga ubuzima. Ni ukuri, ni ukuri, nzongera ndeme abemera agakiza kanjye bose mbagire ibyaremwe bishya kandi bikiranuka kuko uko ni ko Isezerano Rishya ryanjye rizana.

Bityo Adamu na Eva birukanywe mu Busitani bwa Edeni, kandi bahinduwe bavanwa muri kamere nziza y'umucyo baremwemo mu cyubahiro cyuzuye na Yahweh ubwe, kandi bashyirwa munsi y'icyaha n'ibibi kizana bazerera ku isi mu gihano cyabo. Kandi mu minsi ya Yaredi ni bwo Abarinzi bamanutse ku Musozi wa Herumoni, ndetse n'abatwawe n'irari ry'imitima yabo kuko bararikiye abakobwa b'abantu, ndetse bifuza kubabyaraho urubyaro kandi mu irari ry'imitima yabo bashukwamo na ba Satani bihuje mu mivumo y'ubwumvikane, ndetse no mu ndahiro yabo itari yera yaje guhinduka umuvumo w'iteka uzasohorezwa muri rya ziba rinini ry'amazuku yaka umuriro aho urunyo rwabo rudapfa.

Kandi uko ni ko abana b'Imana binjiye ku bakobwa b'abantu, maze bihitiramo abagore mu bo bashakaga bose. Bamwe mu bagore bagiye babishaka, ndetse biyegurira byuzuye ibyaha by'abaguye (bazikorera urubanza rwabo), naho abandi batwarwa ku ngufu kandi bahohoterwa bikomeye n'Abarinzi mu irari ryabo ribi. Kandi uko ni ko isi yari imeze icyo gihe, ndetse n'isi yarimbuwe mu mwuzure ukomeye wabaye mu minsi ya Nowa wasenywe kubera ibizira bibi byakozwe n'abarinzi, bari baremewe kurimbura abantu ndetse kugira ngo abantu batazigera bigarurira isi abaguye bararikiye kandi ubu bakaba babohewe mu rubanza.

Kuko bihitiyemo kurimbuka kwabo no kubabazwa iteka mu cyimbo cy'ibyo bararikiye bitari ibyabo byo gufata, kandi ibyo bintu bafashe, kandi n'ubu baracyashaka kubifata ku ngufu. Nyamara, ibikorwa byabo n'imigambi yabo byose bizahinduka ubusa, kandi ntibazigera basubirana icyo bita igihe cyabo cya zahabu, ahubwo bazabona urubanza rukomeye kandi rukaze rw'iteka. Kuko mu kuri kudahinduka: Isi ni iy'Uwiteka n'ibiyuzuye byose, kandi ndi Umwami w'Iteka ryose wo Gukiranuka waguze isi mu maraso yanjye bwite ancungura, kandi nje gufata ku ngufu ibyo mfitiye uburenganzira, kandi ni inde uzashobora guhagarara imbere y'imbaraga zanjye zikomeye?

Nk'uko byanditswe kuri njye ni ko bizakorwa: Dore, Intare yo mu muryango wa Yuda, umuzi wa Dawidi, yanesheje kugira ngo ibumbure igitabo, kandi imene ibimenyetso byacyo birindwi. Nuko rero, mwumve neza ko ari njye ukwiriye kumena ibimenyetso kuko icyangombwa cy'isi ari icyanjye! Kandi nk'uko byanditswe kuri njye ni ko bizakorwa kuko nkwiriye kwenda igitabo, no kumena ibimenyetso byacyo: kuko nishwe, kandi ncunguye abazaza mu gakiza k'iteka kuri Yahweh bose mu maraso yanjye atagira icyaha, kandi nabacunguye mbakuye mu miryango yose, n'indimi zose, n'amoko yose, n'amahanga yose: kandi nta kiremwa na kimwe gishobora gukura n'umwe muri mwe mu maboko yanjye y'iteka! Ku bw'ibyo, ni nde mukwiye gutinya?

Bityo abagore batwawe n'abana b'Imana, (ndetse n'abarinzi baguye) barasamye maze bababyarira abagabo banini b'ibizira bari babi kugeza mu ndiba z'imitima yabo. Kandi mu bunini bwabo n'imbaraga zabo ni byo biringiraga kandi bakabyirata, nyamara mu rubanza rwa Yahweh kuri bo kubera ibibi bakoreye abantu yaboheye imyuka yabo ku isi mu gihano kuko baremwe ku isi mu ishusho y'umubiri w'icyaha kandi imyuka yabo yari iy'iteka muri kamere yahawe na ba Se abo bagomba gusohoza irari ryabo. Kandi uko abo ba Nefilimi bapfaga mu manza zabo zivuye kuri Yahweh ubwe, imyuka yabo yakuwe mu mibiri yabo mu rupfu rwabo, maze ikomeza kubaho, ibohewe ku isi mu gihano. Nuko rero, barasonza kandi bakagira inyota ariko ntibashobora gufata ibyo kurya cyangwa amazi. Mu gihe kera bari bafite imibiri minini kandi y'imbaraga bakoreshaga kugira ngo bategeke abatuye isi, bahinduwe abagira intege nke keretse gusa bashoboye kwigarurira uwo babamo nk'inyonzi z'abadayimoni, kuko uko ari ko bahindutse. Mwumve ko kuva mu minsi ya Nowa kubera kwinginga kwa Nowa ko ibyenda bya cumi byabo byagumishijwe ikuzimu ariko vuba aha bigiye kurekurwa ku isi mu rubanza.

Nyamara, uko ni ko kwigomeka kw'abaguye kwabaye kuva ku munsi wa mbere gukiranirwa kwabonekaga mu mitima yabo. Uko gusara n'ubusazi byujuje imitima y'abaguye mu kwigomeka kwabo; hamwe n'umuntu w'umubiri, wifatanyije na bo mu kwanga kuva mu mimerere ye yaguye kandi y'icyaha: ko bose bashaka kuba ikindi kintu gitandukanye n'icyo Yahweh ateganya mu butungane bwose bwo gukiranuka. Aho kumvira umuremyi wabo wari kuzabaha gusohozwa kwuzuye, ibyishimo, umunezero, n'amahoro; wari kuzahora abaha ibyo bakeneye byose mu buryo bwuzuye mu rukundo rwe ku byaremwe bye, ahubwo bashatse kandi bakurikira irari ry'imitima yabo kandi basaba isi yabo bwite idafite Yahweh, aho bashobora kubaho mu irari ry'imitima yabo kandi bakishimira kwigomeka kwabo mu binezeza by'icyaha byangiza ibyaremwe bya Yahweh. Ndetse n'ibyifuzo by'icyaha n'ibibi batazigera bageraho cyangwa ngo babisohoze.

Uko ni ko umuntu w'umubiri yifatanyije mu kwigomeka kwabo uretse ko umuntu w'umubiri avukira mu bujiji n'umwijima by'imigambi ye nyakuri n'icyubahiro gishoboka Yahweh yasezeranyije abumvira kandi umuntu w'umubiri yakurikiye abishaka inzira z'abaguye, nubwo, abaguye biyemeje kurimbura Adamu, ibyo Adamu ayobewe abishaka mu irari rye, ndetse n'ibyo yiboheramo mu byiringiro by'ubupfu ku binyoma byabo byinshi kandi bibi. Ndetse binyuze muri ibyo binyoma, benshi bazayoborwa mu nyanja y'umuriro mu gihe icyo gihano kitari cyabateguriwe.

Nyamara, uko ni ko uku kwigomeka kwakomeje mu bihe byose kugeza ubu, ndetse n'ukwigomeka kugaragarira mu ntambara y'imbuto hagati y'ibyaremwe bikiranuka bya Yahweh n'ibizira bidakiranuka byakozwe n'impinduka z'ingirabuzimafatizo z'ababi, kandi si imana nubwo biyoberanya. Kandi uko ni ko ibihe ubamo bimeze, wa muntu we, wigomeka mu kutumvira kwuzuye urwango uva mu nzira z'umuremyi we, ndetse wifatanya mu bujiji n'abiyemeje byuzuye urupfu rwawe n'irimbuka ryawe ry'iteka ibyo bararikira.

Kandi uko ni ko ntanze itangazo ry'umunsi, njyewe Yeshua HaMashiach, ndatangaza ko umunsi w'Uwiteka ugeze kandi ugiye kugwa ku isi mu rubanza rukomeye rw'urugomo kandi amakuba menshi ubu yiteguye kurekurwa ku isi, ndetse n'amakuba n'imanza byahanuwe kera ibihe byabyo bikaba bigeze. Ku bw'ibyo, mwumve neza uwo ndi we utanga iri tangazo mu mbaraga n'intebe y'ubwami ya Yahweh ubwe, kuko ndi we ukwiriye kwenda igitabo no kugifungura, no kumena ibimenyetso byacyo, kandi nzakora ibyo ngibyo neza, kandi ni inde ushobora guhagarika ukuboko kwanjye gukomeye?!

Umva, wa muntu w'umubiri we, ndetse nawe wanga umuremyi wawe urunuka, ko ibihe byawe bigeze. Kandi urukundo rwinshi n'ubuntu n'impuhwe byatanzwe muri ibi bihe by'urubanza, kuko abambaza izina rya Yesu w'i Nazareti bose kugeza ku iherezo ryabo bazakizwa kandi uburemere bw'ingororano yabo ntibukwiriye kugereranywa n'imibabaro bazihanganira ku bw'izina ryanjye. Umva, wa muntu w'umubiri we, ko nzayobora intwari zanjye 144,000 mu gucungura Yakobo kandi nzabika asigaye akomeye ya Isirayeli bazakomeza kubaho mu Bwami bwanjye bw'imyaka igihumbi mu gunganya hateguwe na Yahweh mu butayu, ndetse kugira ngo bororoke kandi bagwire kandi bishimire umubyibuho w'igihugu mu bwami bwanjye bw'imyaka igihumbi bugiye kuza vuba aha ku isi mu gusohoza isezerano, igihango, n'isezerano rishya.

Uko ni ko bizagendera umuntu w'umubiri, ko bazemera umwana w'intama w'Imana nk'umukiza wabo cyangwa bazaba bihitiyemo urubanza rwabo rw'iteka muri ya nyanja y'umuriro ihora yaka kuko nta yandi mahitamo ahari, kandi nta handi hantu bashobora kujya kuko Yahweh atazigera yemerera ikibi kwigarurira no kurimbura ibyaremwe bye kandi iminsi yabo yo kwigomeka babishaka iri kugera ku iherezo.

Ndi umwana w'intama w'Imana, watangiwe ibyaha by'isi.

Ndi Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda, kandi mu gukiranuka nje guca urubanza no kurwana intambara.

Ndi we uwo umwana w'intama w'Imana n'Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda byunze ubumwe byuzuye mu migambi yanjye y'iteka n'ingoma nk'Umwami w'Abami n'Umutware w'Abatware, nuko rero ni nde ushobora guhagarara imbere ya Yeshua HaMashiach ari we wera wa Adonai, ndetse n'uwo Yahweh yatumye gusohoza urubanza rw'iteka ku baguye n'abahitamo bose kwifatanya n'inzira zabo.

Nuko rero ngira inama abizerwa muri Kristo gukomeza kuba abizerwa kuko umunsi w'incungu yanyu wageze kandi uri hafi yanyu. Murebe gusa ku Mwami wanyu Uhoraho wo Gukiranuka kuko abaguye bujuje isi umwuzure w'ibinyoma n'uburiganya byagenewe kuyobya n'intore niba ibyo byashoboka. Nuko rero, mumenye ko abahanuzi b'ibinyoma benshi boherejwe bahanura mu nzira za Balamu kandi batwawe n'imyuka ya Yezebeli kandi bavuga binyuze mu myuka ya anti-Kristo bigisha undi Yesu wiyitirira kandi akarwanya Yesu Kristo ari we Yeshua HaMashiach mu mitima y'abayobejwe. Abatwawe muri uko kuyobya bose bazacirwa urubanza rwabo nibanga kwihana inzira zabo igihe bahanganye n'Umwami wabo mu kanwa k'abizerwa muri Kristo. Ni ukuri, ni ukuri, bamwe mu bahanuzi b'ibinyoma ni abanyakuri kandi barayobejwe kandi bashobora kugerwaho n'ukuri kuvugiwe ku minwa y'urukundo, ariko ukuri gusa ni ko kuzababatura kandi ukuri kweruye kw'ibyaha byabo n'ubuhanuzi bw'ibinyoma kugira ngo koko bashobore kwigarura mu kwihana.

Hanyuma hari abahanura bashaka amafaranga, n'indamu mbi, no kugira ngo bemerwe kandi bitabweho no kwishimira byuzuye ibyiza by'iyi si mbi. Naho yaba ari nde uhanura ibinyoma, mubacyashe kandi mwamagane Amagambo yabo, ndetse n'Amagambo yateguwe n'abaguye kugira ngo bagwishe intore mu mutego w'ibinyoma niba bishoboka. Kuki mwiruka hirya no hino, Torero ryanjye ryizerwa? Ese sinabategetse kandi nkabagira inama nkanabaha imbaraga zo kuba abaneshi banjye? Ndetse n'abanesha buri gice cy'ubwami bw'abanzi nk'uko nanjye nanesheje? Nuko rero, muze kuri Kristo Yesu kandi nzahamya mu mitima yanyu abahanuzi banjye b'ukuri kuko abahanuzi banjye b'ukuri bavugira hamwe n'umutima w'Umwami wabo ari we Jambo wigize umubiri. Nuko rero, Kristo Yesu ntashobora na rimwe kuvuguruza ibyanditswe, kuko ari ko ndi!

Ku bibwira mu bwibone ko bampagarariye ariko bakaba batwawe mu binyoma: Kuki mwiruka hirya no hino, mushaka umuntu wo kubashimisha amatwi ndetse mugashaka abapfumu batongera kandi bavugira mu matama bazababwira ibyo mushaka kumva gusa kugira ngo bakongeze irari mu mitima yanyu mibi mu gihe muhunga ukwihana mugana mu maboko y'abayobya boherejwe kuyobya. Mwiruka hirya no hino kuko mwima amatwi ijwi rya Yeshua HaMashiach, kuko mwanga ukuri kwababatura, kuko mwanga kunyigiraho mufata Ijambo rya Yahweh nk'urugero rwanyu rwo hejuru kandi rwonyine. Nuko rero, ngira inama abibwira bose ko bahanurira Kristo Yesu, ndetse n'abibwira bose ko bavugira Yahweh, NDI HO Ukomeye kwitonda kugira ngo mutagwa mu mwijima w'iteka n'urubanza ruhoraho kuko abifatanya n'abaguye bose muri ubwo buryo kandi bakanga kwihana amazina yabo azahanagurwa mu gitabo cy'ubugingo nibanga umuriro w'utunganya bazarekerwamo nibakomeza mu byaha byabo, kuko nta gihe kikiriho.

Nuko rero ngira inama kandi nkaburira abo nkunda kugira ngo birinde ubuyobe bukomeye butabagenewe nibagendera mu kumvira Umwami wabo, kandi nuko ngira inama nkanaburira abo nkunda kugira ngo batagushwa mu mutego w'ubuyobe bukomeye ndetse n'ubu buri kwigarurira isi. Ngira inama abazumva aya Magambo bose kuva mu byaha no kuva ku bapfumu batongera kandi bavugira mu matama no gukura imyanya yanyu ku meza ya Yezebeli no kwanga ingororano za Balamu. Mumenye ko umuntu wese uwo ari we wese ubuhanuzi bwe buhanura burwanya ibyanditswe aba avuga ibinyoma mu buryarya. Mumenye ko haba Yahweh, NDI HO Ukomeye, cyangwa Yeshua HaMashiach (ari we kandi Kristo Yesu) batazigera bavuguruza ibyanditswe. Nuko rero, abarwanya ibyanditswe barwanya Yahweh NDI HO Ukomeye kandi bakarwanya na Yeshua HaMashiach ari we kandi Kristo Yesu.

Mumenye neza uwo ndi we n'uwo Yahweh yandemye kuba we. Ndi Ijambo rya Yahweh ryigize umubiri utuye muri mwe bakundwa banjye, kandi sinshobora kwivuguruza cyangwa ngo mbeshye, kuko ntagira icyaha kandi nzahora ntagira icyaha. Mumenye Data w'Imicyo utabamo guhindagurika cyangwa igicucu cyo guhinduka, ndetse n'uwo mufitanye gahunda! Mumenye ko bidashoboka ko Adonai Yahweh yivuguruza kuko bidashoboka ko Adonai Yahweh abeshya. Nyamara, kuki mutegeka amatwi yanyu mu bupfapfa ku bahita rwihishwa nijoro? Ese muzakora uburaya bakundwa banjye, mu gihe ahubwo mugomba kuba abizerwa kandi bumvira Umwami umwe, ari we mukiza wanyu w'iteka, ndetse n'uzanywe no gutwara abizerwa muri Kristo mu ijuru isegonda iryo ari ryo ryose none aha?

Nuko rero mumenye, ko amarembo y'ikuzimu atazanesha abizerwa muri Kristo bari Itorero ryanjye ry'ukuri kuko ari ko byanditswe kandi bigasezeranywa kera, kandi niko bikyanditswe kandi bigasezeranywa mu bihe bya none, kuko Amagambo ya Yahweh na Yeshua HaMashiach ari amagambo atunganye nk'ifeza, yatunganirijwe mu ruganda rw'isi, agasukurwa karindwi. Yego, abaguye nta mbaraga bafite ku bizerwa muri Kristo kandi nzaha abizerwa banjye gukandagira munsi y'ibirenge byabo igice icyo ari cyo cyose cy'ubwami bw'abanzi, ndetse no gukandagira abashaka guhagarika ibidahagarikwa.

Ndi Kristo Yesu kandi ni nde ushobora guhagarara arwanya imbaraga zanjye n'ubushobozi nahawe na Yahweh ubwe!

Mumenye ko jyewe na Data turi umwe mu bumwe n'intego, kandi mpora nkora ibyo Data ashaka! Nuko rero, nta muntu n'umwe ushobora kumpagarara imbere, cyangwa ngo habeho ikiremwa icyo ari cyo cyose mu baguye gishobora kunesha, haba mu buhanga, cyangwa ku mbaraga, cyangwa mu mubare, cyangwa mu ikoranabuhanga ryabo ritagira agaciro! Kandi ndi uwo wemeje iteka ukuneshwa kwabo kw'iteka i Kaluvari ubwo navugaga Amagambo nti, birarangiye. Mumenye ko mwitirirwa byuzuye Kristo Yesu kandi ndatuma muhagarara mufite imbaraga mu mbaraga zanjye n'ubushobozi! Nuko rero, ni nde ushobora kubarwanya bakundwa banjye? Ni nde mugomba gutinya? Nimwumve Amagambo yanjye bana banjye kandi mumenye ko ntazigera mbareka cyangwa ngo mbatererane mu kuboko kw'abanzi banyu kuko mwabanesheje kandi mukaba mwarumviye ibigeragezo byinshi n'ibihano byatanzwe n'ukuboko kwanjye gukiza!

Ni nde muri mwe ugomba kwihisha nk'ibigwari mu mazu yanyu? Ni nde muri mwe ugomba guceceka imbere y'ibinyoma byabo bibi? Ni nde muri mwe ugomba kugandukira iyo ntare itagira amenyo urubanza rwayo rutazatinda? Nimwumve ukwivuga kw'Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda ihagaze kugira ngo ibarwanirire kandi mumenye imigambi yanjye muri ibi bihe by'imperuka mbere y'uko amakuba ya Yakobo atera! Kuki mwihisha mufite ubwoba imbere y'abanzi banyu baneshejwe? Mundebereho mu kwizera kandi nzabaha Amagambo yo kuvuga mubarwanya kugira ngo mubabohe, kandi mubirukane, kandi mucecekeshe abashaka kubakandamiza bashaka mu bupfapfa kubabohera mu rubanza rwabo binyuze mu binyoma byabo by'impimbano bashaka kugushamo inuma zanjye.

Nuko rero, ndabategeka kugira ukwizera mu Mwami wanyu Uhoraho kandi mwemerere Imana Ihoraho yo Gukiranuka kubuzuza imbaraga z'imbere imbere y'Umwami wanyu, Kristo Yesu! Mugende mufite ukwizera gukomeye muri Yeshuah HaMashiach, ari we Yesu w'i Nazareti kuko nabahaye imbaraga kugira ngo mukandagire munsi y'ibirenge byanyu ibice byose by'ubwami bw'abanzi. Nuko rero, mumenye, ko ndetse n'abamarayika baguye bari munsi y'ibirenge byanjye mu kuneshwa gukomeye, kandi muramutse muhuye na Satani ubwe mwakenera gusa gusubiza nk'uko Mikayeli yasubije ubwo yahariraga umubiri wa Mose, avuga mu kwizera gukomeye ati, Umwami agucyahe Satani! Kandi nzacyaha, mbohe uwo ari we wese mu baguye wagerageza kubagirira nabi cyangwa kubabuza gukora ibyo uwabatumye ashaka, nimukomeza kugenda mu budahemuka mu kwizera mu gihe muvuga Amagambo nzabaha mu kwizera. Nuko rero, mugire ukwizera mu mitima yanyu kandi mubwire uyu musozi muti kuha! Kandi uzatabwa mu nyanja ku mbaraga zanjye zikomeye. Bari he abagenda hejuru y'amazi nka Petero amaso yabo ayerekeje kuri jye? Bari he abafite ubutwari bwo guhagarara no gutangaza ukuri imbere y'ibibazo bisa n'ibidashoboka? Yego! Ni nde muri mwe uzaba Yosuwa na Kalebu banjye? Muhagarare kandi mwiyerekane imbere y'Umwami wanyu utegeka kumvira kwanyu mu murimo!

Nuko rero, mumenye imbaraga mugomba kugenderamo, bizerwa banjye muri Kristo, kandi umuzitizi wanjye arazitira kugeza ubwo abizerwa muri Kristo bazatwarwa icyo gihe nta muzitizi uzaba uhari wo kuzitira abaguye ukundi. Igihe muzatwarwa mu ijuru mu buryo butunguranye none Itorero ryanjye, umubiri wanjye, ndetse n'umugeni wanjye; abaguye bazarekurwa byuzuye nta nkomyi mu rubanza kuri Babuloni y'Ibanga kandi nyuma yaho gato, ibimenyetso bizafungurwa, n'impanda zizavuga, kandi mu gihe gikwiye inzáo z'umujinya wa Yahweh zizasuka. Kuko umunsi w'Umwami wageze ku isi kandi ibihe by'amakuba ya Yakobo byageze, kandi ni nde uzashobora guhagarara?

Nuko rero, bizaba mu buryo butunguranye ko gutandukanya kwa nyuma kuzatangira. Kandi abatoranyirijwe agakiza k'iteka bazambaza izina rya Yesu w'i Nazareti kugeza ku iherezo, kandi abo muri Isirayeli bemeye Yeshua HaMashiach nka Mesiya wabo w'ukuri bazatwarwa mu butayu kandi bahishwe umujinya wa Yahweh n'intambara y'uwo munsi mukuru n'ikiyoka, inyamaswa, n'umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma. Kandi mu mahoro n'umutekano ugereranyije bazarindwa ibibi kandi bazagaburirwa banitabweho aho bahishwe kandi ntibazaboneka.

Ndi Yesu Kristo uhora ahamagara abizerwa muri Kristo kurushaho kwegera mu isano ikiza n'Umwami wabo n'umucunguzi wabo witita by'ukuri ku mitima yabo.

Ndi Yeshua HaMashiach kandi ndahamagara Yakobo kwemera nka Mesiya wabo w'ukuri, kuko nta wundi Mesiya Yahweh azigera yohereza.

Ndi uwo umwana w'intama w'Imana n'Intare yo mu muryango wa Yuda bunze ubumwe iteka ryose kuko ndi Umwami wanyu n'umucunguzi wanyu kandi mu gukiranuka nzanywe no guca urubanza no kurwana, kandi ni nde ushobora guhagarara arwanya imbaraga z'ubushobozi bwanjye!

Ndi ushyigikira kandi agakomeza abizerwa muri Kristo, ndetse kugira ngo imigambi ya Yahweh na Yeshua HaMashiach ibeho mu buzima bwabo n'imirimo yabo kandi kugira ngo bakandagire buri gice cy'ubwami bw'abanzi munsi y'ibirenge byabo niba bikenewe!

Ndi uzacira urubanza umuntu uwo ari we wese wigeze avuga ko amvugira nk'abahanuzi banjye bera, kandi ku bahanuzi banjye b'ukuri umwuka w'ubuhanuzi uba muri bo kandi bavugira hamwe, kandi cyane cyane n'Umwami wabo ubaha Amagambo yo kuvuga kuko umwuka w'ubuhanuzi ari ubuhamya bwa Yesu. Kandi abahanuzi banjye b'ukuri bazahabwa ingororano z'iteka zagenewe abahanuzi bizerwa bizerwa kandi b'ukuri batanga Amagambo y'Umwami wabo kandi bazahabwa icyubahiro kinini n'inshingano ubwo nzabimika nkanabaha kuba abami n'abatambyi kugira ngo bakorere munsi y'Umwami wabo w'Abami n'Umutware w'Abatware!

Ndi uzatuma abahanuzi b'ibinyoma bose bagwa mu guceceka gusesuye mu manza zabo kandi bazishyura igihano gikomeye kubera kuyobya abakiranutsi no kwifatanya mu kwigomeka kw'abaguye, no gutekereza ko ari ikintu cyoroheje kuvuga ibinyoma mu buryarya bavuga ko amagambo yabo y'ibinyoma yatanzwe n'Ijambo ryigize umubiri, kuko ni ukuri ndi we! Kandi abavuga ibinyoma bose muri ubwo buryo bazahura n'urubanza rw'iteka, igihombo, n'igihano nibanga kwihana inzira zabo mbi. Kandi ku banga kwihana bagakomeza muri byo bazagira umugabane wabo muri ya nyanja y'umuriro yaka iteka kandi ishyushye cyane aho inyo yabo idapfa.

Nuko rero, muhagarare mudakuka bizerwa banjye nkunda kandi nzacungura byuzuye mu buryo butunguranye! Mumenye ko nzatuma munesha byuzuye nimukomeza kumvira ijwi ryanjye, kandi ku banesheje ndabategeka gukomeza mu mirimo nabahaye gukora kandi mwirinde mu isuku yose muhora mwishyira mu mimerere yo kwihana kugira ngo mbashe kubasukura byuzuye iteka ryose mu maraso yanjye ahongerera ibyaha.

Ndi we, hamwe na Yahweh, NDI HO Ukomeye, wahishuriye Amagambo y'Ubutumwa Bwiza Bw'iteka intumwa yacu n'umugaragu wacu, kandi binyuze ku ntumwa yacu n'umugaragu wacu Amagambo y'Ubutumwa Bwiza Bw'iteka yahishuriwe kumvirwa mu bo yohererejwe bose! Amagambo y'Ubutumwa Bwiza Bw'iteka azabona amaso, amatwi, n'imitima y'abo yohererejwe bose! Sela!

Ndaje kubatwara bakundwa banjye, mu buryo butunguranye none, nuko rero mugumane imitima yanyu yuzuye yerekeje kuri Kristo Yesu Umwami wanyu. Sela!
