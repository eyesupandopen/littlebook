Ubuhamya Bubiri: Isirayeli
Ibisharira Biryohereye
Kuwa 3 Ugushyingo 2024

Yewe Isirayeli, Ndi Kuguhamagara!

Biturutse kuri Yehova, Imana Ikomeye Ihoraho:

Nimwumve Amagambo ya Yehova, NDIHO ukomeye kandi mutekereze ku kibazo cyanjye. Kuki imbeba zihunga inzu igiye kugwa, bana banjye? Igisubizo kigaragara ni ukugira ngo zirokoke, keretse imbeba zihunga Babuloni y'Ibanga zibikorera kugira ngo zihishe ibyaha byazo, kandi kugira ngo ziyisahure byuzuye bityo zibone ubutegetsi bugufi nzihaye. Kuko umuntu wa kamere akunda umwijima, noneho ngiye kumuha umwijima akunda cyane. Nyamara, nanditse (kandi nzakomeza kwandika) buri gikorwa cya buri kiremwa mu byo naremye gifite umudendezo wo guhitamo kuko uhawe byinshi asabwa byinshi.

Kandi nta gihishe amaso yanjye, nta gihishe kureba kwanjye, kandi NDI ubona, wandika, kandi wibuka BURI gikorwa kibi cyose cyakozwe mu byo naremye. Kandi nta gishobora kuba ntabizi ngo mbyemere! Imbere yanjye, ibitekerezo byose biba byambaye ubusa. Imbere yanjye buri gitekerezo cy'umutima kiba cyambaye ubusa kandi nta kintu cyangwa umuntu waremwe cyangwa wakozwe utari munsi y'ukuboko kwanjye gukomeye, kuko ndi Imana Ihoraho yo gukiranuka mufitanye gahunda, Yewe Isirayeli kandi ndakubona byuzuye, nubwo uhitamo kunsuguzugura mu buhumyi bw'igice kubera kwinangira kw'imitima yanyu.

Kuko, ubwo nazaga aho muri mu ishusho ya Mesiya umwe rukumbi nigeze nohereza kandi Mesiya umwe rukumbi nzohereza, nanzwe byuzuye na ba So. Kuko, NDI uwaremye Yeshua HaMashiach mu mubiri kugira ngo abamwambaza mu izina rye rikomeye bose bakizwe, uwo mwanga mu bujiji ndetse mu bwibone no gutuka Imana mukamwita ikigirwamana cy'abanyamahanga (goyim). Yewe Isirayeli, benshi muri mwe bifatanyije n'Isinagogi ya Satani kandi ubu mutegereje n'amatsiko Moshiach wanyu w'ikinyoma Esawu yateguriye kurimbura Yakobo.

Nuko rero nimwumve ijwi rya NDIHO UKOMEYE kuko ijwi ryanjye rihoraho, rimeze nk'inkuba riri kuguhamagara muri ibi bihe bya nyuma kandi nongeye kohereza Umwana wanjye aho muri kugira ngo acungure Yakobo n'abasigaye bemeye Umwana wanjye Uhoraho, Yeshua HaMashiach, bazakizwa n'ukuboko kwe gukomeye kandi nta yandi mahitamo y'agakiza ahari. Kandi nta yandi mahitamo cyangwa izina rizahabwa agakiza kuko namuhaye izina risumba amazina yose, kugira ngo mu izina rya Yesu w'i Nazareti amavi yose apfukame. Kandi ku banga, bazahatirwa gupfukamira Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda uwo nohereza ku isi kugira ngo aciye imanza kandi arwane intambara mu gukiranuka kuhoraho, kandi ni we uzashyira ibyo naremye byose munsi y'ubutware bukiranuka kandi azategekesha inkoni y'icyuma kandi urubanza, ubuntu, n'imbabazi bibana na we iteka. Mumenye ko inzira y'irimbukiro ari ngari kandi amazina menshi n'imana z'ibinyoma bishaka kubajyana mu murage wabyo w'inyanja y'umuriro yaka iteka, yaka amazuku n'ibirimi by'umuriro bishyushye cyane by'igihano.

Yewe Isirayeli, uburyo wamennye umutima wa Yehova mu kutumvira kwawe kandi umutima wanjye uracyakumenekera kuva mu bihe bya kera by'iteka aho nari nzi mu kumenya kwanjye kw'imbere ko mu mugambi wanjye w'agakiza ba So bazanga Yesu w'i Nazareti bagahitamo Baraba, wari umwicanyi n'umuyobozi w'imyigaragambyo y'ubugome mu bihugu icyo gihe. Nuko rero natumye yihindukirira abanyamahanga, na bo baramwumva mu gihe runaka, nyamara ubuhumyi bw'igice bwabaye kuri Isirayeli kugira ngo abasigaye ba Isirayeli bashobore kugera ku gakiza mu gihe gikwiye. Mumenye ko benshi bo muri Yudaya bamugize Umwami wabo kandi bagera ku gakiza k'iteka muri Isirayeli y'Imana kandi bazabana n'umwana wanjye iteka ryose. Kandi NDI uwohereje Yesu w'i Nazareti mu mahanga kugira ngo acungure bene wanyu batataniye mu mahanga kubera kwigomeka kwabo, ndetse n'abatari bafite ibyiringiro n'abenshi bo mu mahanga bageze ku gakiza k'iteka kandi ubu batewe ku mashami y'imiryango iteka ryose. Nuko rero menya Yewe Isirayeli ko icyo NDIHO Uhoraho yejeje utagomba kucyita igisanzwe cyangwa igihumanye!

Ariko wowe Yewe Isirayeli, wanze kandi wica umwana wanjye Yesu Kristo umugabiza mu maboko y'abantu b'abanyabyaha bari biyemeje kumurekura mu nzirakarengane ye. Ariko se umugambi wanjye w'agakiza k'iteka wari gusohora ute keretse ibisabwa n'amategeko y'iteka kandi adahinduka ya Yehova byujujwe, amategeko nashyiriyeho gahunda ikiranuka y'ibyo naremye byose kugira ngo biyoborwe mu butabera bwuzuye. Nuko rero Yesu w'i Nazareti yitanze nk'Umwana w'Intama w'Imana nk'Umutambyi Mukuru wanyu w'iteka ryose mu buryo bwa Melikisedeki kugira ngo yishyure igihano cyategetswe na Mose ku byaha n'ibicumuro by'abantu kandi acungure abantu byuzuye mu muvumo w'amategeko. Kuko Mose yaje nk'umurezi wa Isirayeli kugira ngo atware kandi arindire Isirayeli Yeshua HaMashiach kandi yishyuye byuzuye incungu yanyu, kandi nubwo muri abanyeshuri bigomeka abasigaye bazitabira igihano cyanjye ndetse n'amakuba abazaho mu cyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli kiri ku muryango wanyu kandi vuba aha kizakubita inzugi zanyu kugeza ku mahitamo y'iteka.

Nuko rero mumenye uwo mwanze, Yewe Isirayeli, uwo nkunda urukundo rw'iteka, kuko muri abo nasezeranyije ba So mu masezerano menshi mu bihe byashize ko muri ibi bihe by'urubanza nzabarinda ku bwabo, kandi ndi ya Mana Ihoraho yo Gukiranuka idahinduka! Kandi NDI UWIBUKA kwinginga kwinshi kwakozwe na ba So ku bwanyu kandi buri kwinginga ndakwibuka ijambo ku rindi kandi nzubahiriza icyifuzo cyabo ku bemeye kandi bumvira muri mwe. Kandi na n'ubu Umwana wanjye, uwo mwanga mu bujiji, arabatakambira mu rukundo rwe rw'iteka ku bene wabo kuko Yeshua HaMashiach ari umucunguzi wa Isirayeli, ndetse ni we waguze isi mu maraso ye y'impongano kandi yababohoye mu maboko y'umwanzi niba muhisemo kubyemera. Nubwo, benshi muri mwe barazwe urukundo rwa Egiputa na ba So, ntimwemere kugwa iteka ryose mu butayu bw'abatagira kwizera.

Kuko Yeshua HaMashiach ari we uzakiza abasigaye ba Yakobo bazaza kuri we byuzuye, ndetse no mu bigeragezo by'icyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli. Nuko rero mumenye ibihe murimo kandi mukanguke mu buhumyi bwanyu bw'igice ku itegeko ryanjye nimuhindukirira Yeshua HaMashiach, uwo nohereje ku bw'agakiza kanyu nka Mesiya wanyu (igihe muzaba mutagihumishijwe n'ukwinangira kw'imitima yanyu). Kuko muzahindukirira umwana wo gukiranuka na we akabakira, kandi azabajyana atekanye abahishe kurimbuka kwanyu mu maboko ya Moshiach w'ikinyoma wa Esawu.

Nuko rero mumenye uwo mufitanye gahunda kuko NDI Imana y'amasezerano menshi, kandi NDI Imana y'amasezerano menshi, kandi NDI Imana Ihoraho yo Gukiranuka ije yongera guhamagara ubwoko bwanjye ngo bugarukire umwungeri wabo mukuru nohereje ku bw'agakiza ka Adamu. Kandi hari ibice byinshi by'uwo ndi we, kandi buri zina rifite igisobanuro cyaryo ku byerekeye Imana Ihoraho. Nuko rero munyumve kandi munyumve neza kandi mumenye Yehova, ubahamagarira kugaruka kuri njye kugira ngo mubone agakiza. Nuko rero ndabibutsa kandi ndabigisha nk'impinja, mu gihe ari mwe mwagakwiye kuba abigisha, Yewe Isirayeli! Nuko rero mwige iby'Imana yanyu Ihoraho yo Gukiranuka!

Kandi mfite andi mazina menshi n'ibiranga, nuko rero nimwumve ijwi ryanjye rihinda nk'inkuba mu ijuru, Yewe Isirayeli, kuko NDI Imana ya ba So musenga mu kinyoma mu kwinangira.

NDI Imana ya Aburahamu mwataye!

NDI Imana ya Yakobo ndetse uwo mwasize mukajya mu maboko y'undi, kandi mwirukiye ku mana zanyu z'ibinyoma ndetse mwishimira kwigomeka kwanyu. Menya Isirayeli ko uri mu maboko y'Isinagogi ya Satani ndetse n'abavuga ko ari aba Isirayeli bakabeshya kandi atari bo.

Umva ijwi ryanjye Yewe Isirayeli, kandi uze kuri Yeshua HaMashiach muri ibi bihe bya nyuma kuko ndaguhamagara mu rukundo rwanjye ngukunda kandi ndakongerera ubuntu n'imbabazi zanjye muri iki gihe. Ariko uri ishyanga ryinangiye kandi kwinangira n'imitima yinangiye ya ba So mwayigize iyanyu, kandi mwanze Imana ya ba So iyo musenga mu buryo butamenyerewe kandi bw'ikinyoma kuko ubwoko bwanjye burimbuwe no kubura ubwenge, nuko rero muze kuri Yeshua HaMashiach mumwigiho kugira ngo mubone agakiza mu kuboko kwe gukomeye.

Yewe Isirayeli, uburyo umutima wanjye ukumenekera kuko nagufashe nk'igihingwa cyoroshye mu butayu bwumye bwiteguye kurimbuka no kuma iteka ryose, nguha ubuzima kandi nkugira ubwoko bwanjye bwite. Nagiye mu masezerano menshi na ba So, ndetse n'amasezerano batashoboraga gukomeza kubera icyaha no kononekara biri mu maraso ya Adamu. Ariko NDI Uwiteka Imana yawe yibuka kandi yakomeje amasezerano yanjye kandi nzuzuza buri sezerano ryose nigeze kugirana n'abantu ndetse no mu maso ya Yeshua HaMashiach, ari we mwana wanjye nkunda Yesu Kristo.

Yewe Isirayeli, uzaguma utagira kwizera kandi utumvira? Ese uzifatanya n'abanzi bawe iteka mu gihano cyabo cy'iteka? Kandi nuko mwabaye abatumvira kandi mwagendeye mu buhumyi bw'igice, kandi nabatwaye ishyanga ryanyu kandi ntimukiyoborwa na Yahweh Imana Ikomeye Ihoraho. Ariko mu kwigomeka kwanyu muyoborwa n'ukuboko kw'abanzi banyu biyoberanya bakigira Yakobo kandi mu by'ukuri ari Esawu. Musobanukirwe ko abanga Yeshua HaMashiach bose bateye umugongo Yahweh bakagana imana z'ibinyoma no kutizera kandi benshi bararozwe kandi baboshye ku nzu zabo z'umukara basenga mu bujiji mu kutizera. Nuko musobanukirwe, abayoborwa n'ikinyoma cya Moshiach wabo cyangwa Mahdi wabo w'ikinyoma mu madini yabo y'ibinyoma atagira kwizera atayobora kuri Yahweh cyangwa ku bugingo buhoraho, bazabona kurimbuka kw'iteka ku bose bahisemo kugumamo.

Yewe Isirayeli, ngwino kuri njye binyuze mu mwana wanjye Yeshua HaMashiach ari we Yesu w'i Nazareti, uwo mwanga nk'uko ba So babigenje mbere yanyu. Gusa ntimurimbukire mu byaha bya ba So kandi mwemere urukundo rwanjye n'igitambo natanze ku bwanyu, ndetse icyo gitambo Adamu wese yahawe umugisha kandi benshi bo mu bihugu no mu mahanga baje ku Mwami wabo bihana kandi bahawe umugisha kandi bacunguwe iteka ryose. Kandi musobanukirwe ko NDI we washyize ibintu byose muri Kristo Yesu wazutse afite imbaraga, kandi agahindura amaboko kuko NDI we wamugize umwuka utanga ubuzima kandi ku ngoma ye n'ubwami bwe ntibizagira iherezo!

Ku bw'ibyo, amasezerano yanjye yose kuva kuri Adamu kugeza kuri Mose, ndetse n'Isezerano rya Mose ubu ryashyizweho kugira ngo risohorezere mu maso ya Yesu Kristo binyuze mu Isezerano Rishya ryanjye ari ryo Sezerano Rishya ryanjye ryatanzwe kugira ngo ibintu byose bisohore ndetse kuva kuri Adamu kugeza kuri Kristo. Kuko ababona ubugingo buhoraho bagomba kuganduka no gupfukamira umwana wanjye w'ikinege, uwo nishimira iteka. Nuko muze kuri njye mu maso ya Yeshua HaMashiach uwo mu maraso ye atagira icyaha yemeje Isezerano rye Rishya, ari ryo Sezerano Rishya, iryo nemeye byuzuye kandi nateganyije mbere y'uko imfatiro z'isi zishyirwaho. Kandi nzohereza abanjye 144,000, bayobowe na Yeshua HaMashiach, ku bw'agakiza kanyu kandi bazabahisha mu gunguru nateguriye mwe kandi aho muzitabwaho kandi mukizwe muzima kuri Esawu utegura kurimbuka kwanyu.

Nyamara Esawu, ukandamiza ubwoko bwanjye, ari guta Babuloni y'Ibanga kuko bagambiriye gutwikira ibyaha byabo. Icyaha imbeba zita inzu igwa zitariho urubanza. Ku bw'ibyo, bazagerageza guhisha ibyaha byabo ndetse n'ibitiyo byabo by'ibyaha bakoresheje batwikira ibyaha byabo kandi nzabashyira ahagaragara, niko Uwiteka Nyiringabo avuga. Kuko basahuye Babuloni y'Ibanga byuzuye nk'uko nemeye ko biba mu mugabane wanjye wikubye kabiri w'urubanza kuri iyo maraya ikomeye! Kandi nuko bikoranyirije imbaraga za gisirikare zose za Babuloni y'Ibanga kandi bazakomeza gusahura kugeza Babuloni y'Ibanga irimbuwe mu isaha imwe kandi ntizongera kubyuka ukundi. Musobanukirwe ko nta ntwaro yacuzwe cyangwa yaremewe kubarwanya izagira icyo igeraho niba mukomeje kwambaza Mesiya wanyu w'ukuri, ndetse Yeshua HaMashiach!

Nuko musobanukirwe ko Isinagogi ya Satani ifite ishusho yo kubaha Imana mu isura yabo y'inyuma, ariko bakomoka kuri Se umubisha kandi irari rya Se ni ryo bazakora kuko Esawu yononnye urubyaro rwe iteka ryose. Uru rubingira rubi, ndetse n'abayobozi banyu basa n'abakiranutsi ni urubingira rubi kandi benshi ni abahohotera abana bahiga abakiri bato kandi bayobye mu byaha bya Sodomu na Gomora, kandi bahiga abana, kandi si abungushumba b'imico myiza ahubwo ni abafite ubuheri. Kandi ikiyoka ari we Se ni we ubayobora, ndetse ni we watanze abungushumba babiri b'ubuheri kugira ngo bategeke kandi baragire isi mu gukiranirwa. Ndetse n'inyamaswa yo mu nyanja izazamuka nka Mahdi wabo n'inyamaswa yo mu isi, ari na we Moshiach wabo w'ikinyoma kandi azaza gutegeka igihugu kandi mu by'ukuri bazategeka isi mu gukiranirwa.

Kandi ikizinga cy'ubuheri bwabo cyanduza kamere yabo yose, kandi agakiza ntibashobora kukageraho. Kuko abihindura mu buryo bw'ingirabuzimafatizo muri ubwo buryo cyangwa ababyawe n'abaguye nta gakiza bafite kuko baremwe mu ishusho y'ikiyoka kandi iyo shusho bayifite iherezo. Icyagoramye ntigishobora kugororwa, kandi buri kintu cyose giteye ishozi cy'abaguye gishobora gusa gukora ibibi kuko baremwe kugira ngo bazane kurimbuka kw'abantu, kandi bari kuba barabigezeho iyo ntabahagarika mu minsi ya Nowa.

Kandi nyamara bazongera kubyuka, kuko nzazana ibihangange kugira ngo nsohoze umujinya wanjye! Kandi Isinagogi ya Satani irakora kugira ngo ihishe ibimenyetso byose by'abo bari bo by'ukuri mbere y'uko amakuba atera kandi ikinyoma cyabo kigahurirana n'ibyago kuko abaguye bemerewe kurimbura Babuloni y'Ibanga. Nyamara buri cyaha kizatangwa byuzuye mu bituza byabo kandi ku bw'ibyo, ntibishoboka kwihisha Yahweh n'umwana wanjye Yeshua HaMashiach kandi umujinya wacu uzababona ndetse n'ubwo bagerageza kwihisha mu mutima w'isi. Nta n'umwe ugengwa n'urubanza rukomeye ushobora gucika inyanja y'umuriro, keretse abanditswe mu gitabo cy'ubugingo cy'umwana w'intama kandi nta n'umwe ushobora gucika ibyaha byabo keretse abo agakiza kagenewe ndetse n'abemera amasezerano yanjye y'amahoro. N'ubwo barimbura amahanga angahe kandi n'ubwo bakwihisha kure mu isi, ntibazacika kwihorera n'umujinya bibikiye kandi nzabishyura byuzuye niko Imana Ihoraho ivuga.

Kuko nzahindura isi nyikuye mu miterere yayo y'ubu nyishyire mu bwiza bwayo bw'umwimerere, kandi abacunguwe b'isi bazagendayo mu mahoro. Kandi nuko ibiriho, bizahindurwa bibe ibizaba, kandi ntibazacika, ndetse n'abasigaye mu rubanza aho amakuba n'ibyago byinshi bizaba, kandi urubura ruzayenga, kandi amahanga azahinduka, kandi isi izasubizwa mu bwiza bwayo bwa mbere. Ubwo bwiza abaguye bahinduyemo kubora kugira ngo bahuze n'irari ryabo.

Kandi ndi Yahweh, Imana Ikomeye Ihoraho, ndetse n'uwaremye ijuru n'isi, ndetse n'uwo kuva kera kose kugeza iteka ryose, kandi ku bushake bwanjye n'inama yanjye navuze ibintu biriho ndetse n'ibitari biriho. Nuko rero umva ijwi ryanjye, Yewe Isirayeli, ndetse namwe mwese mushaka kwirengagiza Amagambo yanjye mu kwinangira kw'imitima ikomeye. Urubanza rugiye kuza byuzuye ku isi kandi nzashyira ahagaragara Esawu wese uwo ari we by'ukuri, kandi nzashyira ahagaragara byuzuye ba Se abo irari ryabo baboshywe iteka.

Dore! Abo kwa Esawu bavuga gukiranuka kwabo, bibwira mu bujiji ko ari ubwoko bwanjye bwatoranyijwe, kandi Moshiach wabo w'ikinyoma, ndetse n'iryo hembe rito, azavuga ko we ubwe, ari Imana! (Nta wuhwanye na Yahweh, Imana Ikomeye Ihoraho kandi Ishoborabyose!) Kandi abaguye ntibashobora kwikura mu maboko yanjye ashobora byose, kandi Yeshua HaMashiach azamenagura umutwe wa Satani rwose nk'uko nasezeranyije Adamu. Nta bujyakuzimu mu isi aho bashobora kwihisha, n'ubwo bagerageza, kandi ubu babohewe ku isi ndetse no munsi y'ikirere icyo badashobora gucika. Nta yindi myirondoro bashobora gufata aho ntababona uko bari by'ukuri. Nta mashusho bashobora gufata aho ntabamenya ngo mbabone byuzuye, kugeza mu mitima yabo yanduye. Kandi buri wese mu baguye kuva ku muto kugeza ku mukuru n'abakurikira inzira zabo mbi bose bazagira umurage wabo w'iteka muri iyo miriro y'igihano muri iyo nyanja yaka umuriro iteka aho inyo yabo idapfa.

Nuko rero nimufate amaganya, Yewe Isirayeli, kuri mwe mwinangira mwanga ubutumire bwanjye bw'ubugingo buhoraho, ndetse n'abazahitamo gukurikira abaguye bose n'abazifatanya n'ikiyoka bose n'abakomoka kuri Adamu bose bari bafite amahitamo y'agakiza ariko bagahitamo ibyaha byabo bizabayobora ku rupfu rwa kabiri niba batambaje izina rya Yeshua HaMashiach ku bw'agakiza.

Nuko nzasobanura amahitamo musigaranye ubu. Mwemera Mesiya wanyu w'ukuri, Yeshua HaMashiach nohereje, kandi muzakizwa mu ngororano z'iteka n'ubwiza, ibyo ibigeragezo byanyu n'itotezwa bidakwiriye kugereranywa. Cyangwa niba hari ukomeza cyangwa yifatanya n'ikiyoka mu gahunda yabo nshya, muzaba mwanze ubugingo buhoraho ahubwo mwemeye byuzuye kuvumwa kwanyu ku bw'ibyaha by'umubiri, kandi muzikorera urubanza rwanyu mu buryo bukwiye igihe mwingingwaga gucika binyuze mu kumvira, ibyo muzaba mwanze.

Umuntu wese n'abantu bose bazashyirwaho ikimenyetso cy'inyamaswa, bazaba bahinduye iteka ryose ishusho ya Yahweh baremwemo, bibasiga mu mimerere idashobora gucungurwa. Ku bw'ibyo, ndabinginga ngo muhitemo ubuzima kugira ngo mubone kubaho iteka ryose mu rukundo n'imigisha by'umuremyi wanyu bigaragarira byuzuye mu maso ya Yesu Kristo, ndetse Yeshua HaMashiach! Bitabaye ibyo, muzifatanya n'abaguye mu murage wabo w'amazuku yaka muri iyo nyanja y'umuriro y'iteka, aho igihano no kwishyurwa by'ibyaha ari byo by'iteka ryose muzigera mumenya.

Musobanukirwe, ko umugambi wanjye atari ukuba umugome cyangwa urenganya irema ryanjye, ariko amategeko yanjye y'ubutabera ntahinduka kandi ntashobora guhindurwa. Kandi nuko irema riniha kandi rintakambira cyane munsi y'umutwaro w'ibyaha by'abaguye n'ibyaha by'abantu, kandi ibihe byo kweza no gukura ibibi mu isi mu rubanza byageze; n'ubwo imbuto zo kwigomeka zizasigara ariko ubwami bw'abanzi ntibuzongera kubyuka ukundi. N'ubwo Satani azagerageza igitero cye cya nyuma mu gihe cye gito mbere y'uko ajugunywa iteka mu miriro kuko atari umwami, nyamara intebe ye y'ubwami izaba imiriro idashobora kuzima y'ubutungane bushyushye cyane, kandi ntibazagira imbabazi.

Kuko amategeko yanjye y'iteka ategeka iki ku batumvira, nuko mwibuke ko ibihembo by'ibyaha ari urupfu, kandi nuko Adamu yakoze icyaha arangiza ubuzima bwe mu bihembo by'ibyaha, ari ryo rupfu. Nyamara ibitambo byoswa n'ibitambo bya kera ntibyari bihagije kugira ngo bicungure byuzuye umwana wanjye w'ikirara, kandi nuko umuntu utunganye utagira icyaha ni we gitambo cyonyine cyemerwa kuko Adamu yari atunganye kandi atagira icyaha igihe yakoraga icyaha.
