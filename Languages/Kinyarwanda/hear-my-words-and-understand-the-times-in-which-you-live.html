Ubuhamya Bubiri: Itorero
Mu Mirimo ya Yohana Umuhishuzi
23 Gicurasi 2024

Umva Amagambo Yanjye Kandi Wumve Ibihe Urubamo!

Umva Amagambo yanjye, bantu banjye, ndetse n'abo mpamagara bose, kandi musobanukirwe ibihe murimo. Kuko isi muzi ubu izavaho, kandi mu cyimbo cyayo hazasigara amatongo menshi, ndetse n'ivu n'ibisigazwa bya Babuloni y'Ibanga, kuko intambara ije. Kuko uko nzamura Umugeni wanjye mu ijuru, amakuba akomeye azagwira Babuloni y'Ibanga, kandi izarimburwa mu isaha imwe, kandi mu isaha imwe izarimburwa, kandi nta gishobora guhindura iherezo ryayo, kuko Yehova ari ko yabivuze.

Kuri wowe, Babuloni y'Ibanga, wanze ijwi ryanjye kandi unshyira ku ruhande, njyewe Yesu Kristo Ukiranuka, ukurikira ibinezeza by'icyaha. Kuko n'imiburo ikomeye yatanzwe hejuru y'igihugu cyawe mu ijuru yashyiriweho gusa gushimisha imitima yanyu itizera n'amaso yanyu yuzuye irari, kandi kuri wowe wari undi munsi usanzwe mu gihe wabagaho mu byaha byawe bikugeza ku kurimbuka. Wagombaga kwihana!

Kuko mwahindutse ishyanga ry'abasenga ibishushanyo, ishyanga ry'abashoza intambara, ishyanga rigaburirwa ibinyoma kandi rikunda ibishuko byaryo, ishyanga ryujuje isi ibizira byanyu; kandi amahanga anywa ku gikombe cyanyu cya zahabu cyuzuye ibizira n'umwanda w'ubusambanyi bwanyu.

Ku bw'ibyo, ndakurwanya, kandi kurimbuka kwawe kugeze kandi kwatangiye, kuko wirengagije guhamagara kwa Yehova kwatanzwe binyuze muri njye, Yeshua HaMashiach, kandi nari kugukiza. Kandi naririye inzira zanyu zinangiye, ariko ntimurashaka kureka ibibi; bityo nabateye umugongo, kandi ibihe by'abanyamahanga bigiye kurangira burundu ubwo nzamura abizerwa mu Itorero ryanjye ku ijwi ry'impanda.

Ariko muri ishyanga riboshye mu minyururu, kandi muzarimburwa rwose, kandi ntimuzongera kubyuka. Uko Umugeni wanjye atwarwa, isi izatangazwa cyane n'ibyo yabonye, kuko ibi bitazaba mu ibanga, ahubwo ku mugaragaro, kugira ngo isi yose imenye ko hari Imana mu ijuru kandi ko yantumye, Kristo Yesu, gushaka zahabu, ifeza, n'amabuye y'agaciro byo mu isi. Kuko ubutunzi bukomeye bwa Data, ndetse n'amasaro y'agaciro kenshi, bizakurwa mu rubanza no mu kurimbuka bigiye kuza.

Kuko Umugeni wanjye akijijwe umujinya ku bw'igitambo cyanjye, kandi nzatabara abizerwa n'abasanzwe ari abizerwa muri Kristo mu gihe cyo kuboneka kwanjye n'abapfuye bose ari abizerwa muri Kristo, ndetse bashyizweho ikimenyetso nanjye, Kristo Yesu, kuko nahinduwe umwuka utanga ubuzima. Kandi nzazura abapfiriye muri Kristo, n'abazaba bakiriho basigaye bazazamuranwa na bo gusanganira njyewe mu bicu. Bityo musobanukirwe ko ibi ari ukugororera abizerwa no gusohoza ubukwe bw'Umukwe n'Umugeni wanjye, kuko tuzahuzwa iteka mu isezerano kandi ntibazigera bava aho ndi. Kandi ni njye uzabagira abami n'abatambyi imbere ya Data.

Kandi gutyo bazava mu isi, kandi gutyo isi izabibona, kandi benshi bazatangazwa cyane. Kuko igice kimwe cy'impamvu y'ibi ari ugutera kwihana mu basigaye inyuma, ndetse n'abanze guhamagara kwanjye mu gihe cy'ubuntu, ndetse na benshi batumvise bihagije ngo bizere bazakizwa mu gihe cy'amakuba ya Yakobo, cyane cyane abo mu gihugu cy'isezerano.

Kuko ndaguhamagara, yewe Isirayeli: mbese witeguye kumva ijwi rya Mesiya wawe w'ukuri, Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda? Kuko ijwi rya Mesiya wawe w'ukuri rihamagara, kandi ni njye uzategekera ku ntebe ya Dawidi mu murwa wera, ndetse njyewe, Yeshua HaMashiach; gusa ntiwirengagize amagambo yo guhamagara kwanjye! Kandi gutyo igihe cy'ubuntu, ndetse n'ibihe byo guhembuka, ubu birimo birangira burundu. Kandi uko kurangira kuzagerwaho byuzuye ubwo nzakoranyiriza abizerwa b'Itorero ryanjye aho ndi mu bicu n'igihe kugenda kwabo kuzatigisa isi yose.

Bizatigisa isi by'ukuri, kandi gutigisa gukomeye kuzarimbura Babuloni y'Ibanga, kandi izatanyagurwa hagati uko abera banjye bagenda. Izatwikwa rwose n'umuriro uva mu kirere uko ibisasu bizagwa. Imyenda yayo izatwikirwa n'amazi aturuka iburasirazuba n'iburengerazuba! Kuko marayika ukomeye azaterura ibuye rimeze nk'urusyo runini, arijugunye mu nyanja, ati: Uku ni ko ururembo rukomeye Babuloni ruzatabwa hasi n'imbaraga nyinshi, kandi ntiruzongera kuboneka ukundi. Kuko ari ko byanditswe kandi ari ko bizakorwa!

Kuko urubanza rwayo rukomeye, kandi urubanza rwatangiriye mu nzu y'Imana. Amatorero yayo yaguye azatsembwa, kuko naciriye urubanza ayo yose atari ayanjye, ndetse n'amatorero yose y'ibinyoma avuga ibinyoma bikomeye mu buryarya. Babuloni y'Ibanga, iyaba wari warampindukiriye, Yesu Kristo, ukava mu nzira zawe mbi, kuko nari kugukiza, kandi haba njyewe cyangwa Yehova nta we unezezwa n'imanza zigiye gusukwa.

Ariko nubwo bimeze bityo, imanza zizagwa nk'uko byavuzwe, kandi urwo rubanza ruzaba rukomeye kandi rw'iteka. Kuko nari narahindukiriye abanyamahanga mu gihe runaka, kandi ubu nzatera umugongo abanyamahanga mpindukirire umukumbi nakomokamo, ari wo Isirayeli. Nubwo umuntu wese uzambaza izina ry'Umwami kugeza ku iherezo azakizwa, yaba Umuyahudi cyangwa Umunyamahanga, kuko agakiza kataboshywe n'imipaka y'amahanga.

Yewe Babuloni y'Ibanga, ibiraro byawe bizagwa; imihanda yawe izacurika, yicike, kandi ntibe nyabagendwa; imiyoboro yawe y'ibicuruzwa yamaze gusenyuka kandi vuba izaba umusaka. Nta biribwa bizakugezwaho, kuko nta muntu uzagutabara, kandi uzaba wenyine mu isaha yawe yo kuba umusaka, yewe maraya ukomeye. Nta byishimo uzagira, kandi abazarokoka bazisanga barwana gusa kugira ngo babone amazi yo kunywa. Inzara izatwikira igihugu. Kandi ibyorezo bikomeye bizakugwira, ndetse n'inyamaswa zizagutera, kuko ibihe byageze.

Ingabo zitukura guverinoma yawe y'abagambanyi yemereye kwinjira mu mipaka yawe zizica buri mugabo, umugore, n'umwana utajyanye n'imigambi yabo. Kuko bazica kandi bagire abacakara abo bazabona bose. Ni ingabo z'inyamaswa zakoranyijwe zivuye mu mfuruka enye z'isi ku bw'iyi mpamvu, kandi inyamaswa yihishe n'umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma wihishe barabakoranyije.

Nta mpuhwe bafite, nta mbabazi, nta buntu, nta kwicuza, kandi banga urunuka abaturage ba Babuloni y'Ibanga kubera ibyaha biteye ubwoba abayobozi banyu bakoreye mu bihugu byabo. Kandi icyo gihe cyose benshi muri mwe mwafanaga intambara zanyu zikiranirwa uko mwarebaga mwishimye kuri za televiziyo zanyu, uko mwishimiraga cyane ibyaha by'abayobozi banyu.

Mwahaye uburenganzira abayobozi banyu abo mwemeje ibyaha byabo binyuze mu matora, bityo muzasangira urubanza rwabo, kuko mwigize abakwiriye umugabane wikubye kabiri w'urubanza, kandi ruzabageraho gitungumo.

Kandi gutyo Umugeni wanjye azagaragara imbere y'intebe yanjye y'imanza, kandi bamwe bazahomba, bamwe bazatwara ingororano zikomeye, kandi bose bazahabwa byuzuye avansi bamaze guhabwa: Kuko nzabaha imibiri mishya, itagira icyaha itazigera ibora! Kandi gutyo ibimenyetso bizafungurwa, kuko nubwo amakuba n'umubabaro byagwiriye mu isi mu myaka myinshi ubu, si umujinya w'Imana cyangwa umujinya w'Umwana w'Intama.

Benshi muri mwe mutekereza mu bujiji ko mugomba kwihanganira umujinya w'Imana, mwibwira mu bwibone ko mushobora kwihanganira ibigiye kuza. Musobanukirwe ko nakijije Itorero ryanjye umujinya, kuko naje ku banjye no ku banyamahanga mu gihe cy'ubuntu nk'amasezerano y'amahoro, ntanga amahoro n'ubwiyunge kuri Data w'Imicyo, ndetse El Shaddai! Kandi abo nakijije bemeye ku bushake bwabo ubuntu bwanjye ntabereye, bityo mukurikire urugero rwabo!

Kandi nzashyiraho ikimenyetso ku 144,000 banjye bo mu miryango ikurikira, ndetse 12,000 kuri buri muryango ukurikira: Yuda, Rubeni, Gadi, Asheri, Nafutali, Manase, Simeyoni, Lewi, Isakari, Zabuloni, Yozefu, na Benyamini. Bityo mwumve ijwi ryanjye!

Kandi muzankorera imirimo myinshi ikomeye, kandi muzayobora benshi ku gakiza mubakuye mu isi ipfa kandi y'urugomo. Kuko Satani yamaze gutegura ikinyoma gikomeye kandi azakora ibishoboka byose ngo abeshye, atwikire, kandi ahishe ikoraniro rikomeye mu bicu rigiye kuba. Ibinyoma bye byamaze gushinga imizi mu ntekerezo zanyu no mu mitima yanyu binyuze mu burezi bwanyu bw'Abayezuwiti buhakana Data kandi bwabagejejweho n'abaguye. Bahinduye ibintu byose ibihimbano, ndetse bayobora benshi muri mwe kwizera ko hejuru y'isi hari umwanya utagira iherezo w'imibumbe n'ubundi buzima, kandi benshi bazaba abaswa ku buryo bazamira ibyo binyoma. Ndetse n'abo amazina yabo atigeze yandikwa mu Gitabo cy'Ubugingo, kuko bazemera ikimenyetso cyabo.

Mumenye, bantu banjye, kandi mwumve umuburo wanjye. Nta buzima buri ku yindi mibumbe! Mwige kugira ngo mumenye, kuko imibumbe ari inyenyeri zizerera zashyiriweho gutangaza imirimo y'intoki za Yehova mu isanzure munsi y'ikirere. Aba ntibazaba abanyamahanga baturuka mu zindi si zizenguruka mu binyoma; icyakora, si abo mu isi, kuko ari abamarayika baguye bo mu ijuru, kandi bakoze ibizira byinshi. Ntimubizere!

Bityo mu ivu ry'ugendera ku ifarashi no mu kurimbuka gukomeye inyamaswa n'umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma bazazamuka, kuko ibanga ryo gukiranirwa ryamaze gutangira gukora. Kuko Babuloni y'Ibanga ishyizwe mu maboko yabo n'ihuriro ririmo kuremwa, ndetse amahembe icumi azayarimbura, kuko ari abami icumi baha byose ikiyoka, inyamaswa, n'umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma. Kuko numara gukuraho ubuza, bazazamuka byuzuye nk'uko Ibyanditswe bivuga; kuko mu kavuyo hazamuka gahunda yabo nshya, ndetse gahunda y'Ikiyoka, kuko ari we mbaraga zabo n'ubutware bwabo, kuko ari ko yabihawe mu buryo bufite imipaka mu gihe gito cyane.

Ikiyoka gifite umujinya mwinshi kandi kizakora mu mwuka wa Hamani kugira ngo kigire ibya Kristo byose ibitemewe n'amategeko, kandi umuntu wese wifatanya n'izina ryanjye, ndetse Yesu Kristo, azahigwa yicwe. Kandi abadakunda amagara yabo kugeza ku gupfa bazakizwa kandi bagire ingororano ikomeye. Bityo mugumane intekerezo n'umutima wanyu ku byishimo byashyizwe imbere yanyu, kuko muri ubwo buryo nizeye agakiza kanyu mu mubabaro ukomeye wari uwanyu, keretse ko nemeye gufata igihano cyanyu ku bushake kandi nishyura byuzuye ibyaha by'abantu rimwe rizima, ndetse n'ibihe byose.

Inyamaswa izakora yihuse, kuko izakomeza amezi 42 ivuga ibintu bikomeye byo gutuka Imana. Izarwanya abera, kandi ihawe kubanesha. Izaca imitwe benshi. Izakora ibimenyetso n'ibitangaza byinshi by'ibinyoma, kandi mu ivu hazazamuka gahunda yayo nshya. Kandi umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma azamutunga urutoki amushime kandi atume bose baramya igishushanyo cye, kuko ibi byanditswe neza mu Ibyanditswe. Kandi gutyo inyamaswa izasukura isi ikureho ikintu cyose cyiza, cyera, kandi cy'agaciro. Nyamara, nzahisha abizerwa banjye, kandi bazakora ibitangaza byinshi, kandi benshi bazakizwa.

Ariko umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma, ndetse inyamaswa iva mu isi, izatuma bose, aboroheje n'abakomeye, abakire n'abakene, abidegembya n'ababoshye, bashyirwaho ikimenyetso ku kuboko kwabo kw'iburyo cyangwa mu ruhanga rwabo: Kandi ko nta muntu numwe ushobora kugura cyangwa kugurisha, keretse ufite icyo kimenyetso, cyangwa izina ry'iyo nyamaswa, cyangwa umubare w'izina ryayo. Bityo musobanukirwe ko kwitabira sosiyete yabo nshya y'ibinyoma bizasaba ikimenyetso, kandi abafata ikimenyetso bose ntibazigera bababarirwa. Kandi bazagira umugabane wabo muri rya ziba rinini ryaka umuriro n'amazuku iteka ryose, kandi gutyo ni ko umwotsi w'umubabaro wabo uzazamuka.

Abahamya banjye babiri bazahaguruka, kandi bazahanura bambaye ibigunira! Ibi ni ibiti by'iminzenze bibiri, n'ibitereko by'amatara bibiri bihagarara imbere y'Imana y'isi. Kandi nta n'umwe uzashobora kubarwanya, kuko bazahanura amezi 42. Kandi niba hari umuntu ushaka kubagirira nabi, umuriro uva mu kanwa kabo, ukongora abanzi babo: kandi niba hari umuntu ushaka kubagirira nabi, agomba kwicwa atyo. Aba bafite ububasha bwo gukinga ijuru, kugira ngo imvura itagwa mu minsi y'ubuhanuzi bwabo: kandi bafite ububasha ku mazi bwo kuyahindura amaraso, no guteza isi ibyago byose, uko bashatse kose.

Kandi nibamara kurangiza ubuhamya bwabo, inyamaswa izamuka iva mu kuzimu izabarwanya, ibaneshe, kandi ibice. Kandi bazasiga imibiri yabo mu mihanda iminsi itatu n'igice, kandi isi yose izishima, hanyuma bazazurwa bazamurwe mu ijuru.

Umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma yamaze kwiyegereza abo mu gihugu ngo batekereze ko ari Mesiya wabo n'umwami wabo. Ni umwubatsi w'urusengero rw'ibinyoma kandi akomoka mu muryango wa Dani, kandi si Kirusi. Azaha inkunga yose inyamaswa ya mbere izamuka iva mu nyanja. Kuko inyamaswa ya mbere izashyira mu bikorwa ku ngufu amategeko ya Nowa ku mahanga mu gukiranirwa gukomeye, kandi umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma azategekera ku ntebe y'ubwami atari iyayo, kuko ari iyanjye, ndetse Yeshua HaMashiach. Kandi gutyo urusengero ruzubakwa, kandi umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma azakora ibimenyetso n'ibitangaza byinshi by'ibinyoma, kuko ahawe imbaraga kimwe n'inyamaswa ya mbere, bityo bombi bazakorana n'intekerezo imwe n'umugambi umwe, ndetse n'intekerezo n'imigambi bya cya kiyoka kinini cy'ikinyoma, ariko nta gaciro afite kandi nta menyo afite, bityo ntimumumutinye!

Kuko azi ko nje kurimbura ubwami bwe, kuko atari umwami ahubwo ari umwanzi watsinzwe rwose, udafite ibyiringiro na mba. Bityo ntimumumutinye kandi mugumane intekerezo zanyu kuri njye. Kuko dore, mbabwiye ibi bintu bitaraba kugira ngo munyizere kandi mwizere Data wantumye, ndetse Yehova Imana ikomeye ihoraho, ndetse nanjye umwana we, Yeshua HaMashiach, uzasohoza amasezerano yahawe ba sogokuruza.

Kandi gutyo ku iherezo ry'amezi 42 ya mbere ikizira cy'umurimbuzi kizaba binyuze ku muhanuzi w'ibinyoma, ndetse inyamaswa izamuka iva mu isi. Mu mezi 42 ya kabiri, ndetse muri icyo gihe ni bwo amakuba akomeye kuruta ayandi yose azaba n'ikizira cy'umurimbuzi, kandi aziyita Imana! Benshi bazamenagurwa kandi benshi bazarimbuka, ariko abumvira ijwi ryanjye bazajyanwa ahantu mu butayu aho bazahishwa kandi barindwe mu gihe runaka, kuko ibi bizaba ibihe by'ubugome bukabije, kandi ibi bihe by'ibigeragezo n'ibizamini bizaba bikomeye cyane. Hanyuma muzabona ijuru rikingutse, kandi muzambona, Yesu Kristo, nyoboye ingabo zikomeye zo mu ijuru zivuye mu ijuru, kandi izaba ari ingabo idasa n'iyindi, na rimwe! Kuko Umugeni wanjye azaba ari kumwe nanjye. Kandi nzamenagura inyamaswa n'umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma n'ingabo zabo zose n'ibizira byabo byose bakoze mu buryo buteye ishozi, kandi gutyo bazarimburwa ndetse n'umucyo wo kuza kwanjye n'inkota ityaye ifite ubugi bubiri iva mu kanwa kanjye, kandi ntibazahagarara ahubwo bazarimburwa rwose. Kandi inyamaswa n'umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma bazajugunywa ari bazima muri rya ziba ryaka umuriro n'amazuku, kandi ntibazigera barekurwa mu mubabaro wabo, kuko bigize ababikwiriye.

Kandi icyo gihe ni bwo umuzuko w'abakiranutsi uzaba, ubwo abera ba kera n'abera bo mu makuba bazazurwa mu bapfuye bajye mu buzima bushya, kandi ntibazongera gupfa ukundi, kandi nzabaha ingororano ikomeye, imeze nk'itari yinjira mu mitima y'abantu muri iki gihe. Kuko ndi Umwami w'imbabazi n'Umwami w'imbabazi, kandi ndi kuzura kw'Ubumana mu mubiri.

Kandi gutyo ingoma yanjye y'imyaka igihumbi ku isi izatangira, kandi gukiranuka kuzategeka ku isi, kandi nzategeka amahanga n'inkoni y'icyuma, kandi isi izasabwa n'inzira za Yehova. Kuko nje n'imbaga y'abami n'abatambyi, kandi bazategekera ku ntebe yanjye y'ubwami, kandi bazategeka mu gukiranuka. Kandi intare izaryamana n'umwana w'intama.

Ndabahamagara ngo musobanukirwe ibihe murimo.

Ndi umwuka utanga ubuzima, kuko ari ko nahinduwe.

Ni njye uzabagira abami n'abatambyi imbere ya Data.

Ndaguhamagara, yewe Isirayeli.

Nje kurimbura ubwami bwa Satani, kuko atari umwami.

Ndi Umwami w'imbabazi n'Umwami w'imbabazi.

Ndi kuzura kw'Ubumana mu mubiri.

Nje n'imbaga y'abami n'abatambyi, kandi bazategekera ku ntebe yanjye y'ubwami, kandi bazategeka mu gukiranuka.

Ndabahamagara; gusa ntimwirengagize guhamagara kwanjye!
