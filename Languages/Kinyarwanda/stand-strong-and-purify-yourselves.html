18 Werurwe 2024

Muhagarare Mushikamye Kandi Mwihumanure

Umwami wizerwa aravuga ku batoranyijwe be n'abizerwa ati: Muhagarare mushikamye kandi mwihumanure mureka kandi mwatura ibyaha byanyu. Nimumuvemo, bantu banjye, kandi mwitandukanye n'ibyaha bye kugira ngo mutazagabana ku rubanza rwe n'ibyago bye. Murabaza muti ni inde? Ese si Babuloni Ikomeye, uciriwe urubanza? Ese si indaya ikomeye yasinze amaraso y'abera? Nzabaha umutekano n'amahoro, kandi nzabayobora mu bwugamo mu nzu ya Data kugeza igihe kirangiye.

Ndi Umwami Yesu Kristo, wabahamagariye ubwiza n'ingeso nziza, ntimugacike intege mu gukora neza. Nabahaye ijwi ryo kumvugira no gutangaza ukuza kwanjye vuba. Nyamara benshi mu Itorero ryanjye batakaje ijwi ryabo kandi babaye abatari abizerwa mu byiringiro byanyu Data yashyize mu Ijambo rye. Ntimube abatari abizerwa ahubwo mwizere gusa. Mushake ibimenyetso byo kuza kwanjye, nk'uko Data yabitanagaje mu ijuru kandi bimaze kumenyekana cyane. Kuki mukiri abanyabukererwe mu kwizera? Mungirire icyizere, bana banjye, kuko mbatabara; yego, ntemera buri shami ritera imbuto muri mwe. Mugirire icyizere Ubwami bwanjye kuri mwe kandi mukomeze agakiza kanyu mukorera agakiza kanyu mutinya kandi mutitira. Mungarukire mu isengesho kandi munsabe kubereka ibyaha byanyu kugira ngo mubinyature kandi mubitsinde binyuze mu maraso yanjye. Ntimutinye rero, kuko agakiza kanyu kari hafi; yego, isegonda rya nyuma ryenda kurangiza uruziga rwaryo rwa nyuma mu gihe cyanjye cy'ubuntu.

Mungarukire kandi muze aho nteguye umwanya ku meza yanjye kandi nkabategurira ubwugamo mu nzu ya Data kugira ngo muhunge tsunami y'ibyago igiye kuza. Byegereye abatuye isi, kandi urubanza rukomeye rugiye kugwa ku isi. Ntimukareke izina ryanjye! Ndabizi ko murushye muri iyi saha ya nyuma, ariko muruhukire muri njye kandi mubone imbaraga muri njye kandi mutangaze ukuza kwanjye vuba. Sinatanze umunsi cyangwa isaha ngo mutegereze, kuko mwakubaka ubuzima bwanyu bwose kuri icyo gikorwa. Ariko ntimwibeshye: itariki n'isaha bizwi mu ijuru kuri iyi ngingo, kandi abamarayika banjye biteguye, bategereje ijwi ry'impanda ubwo nzateranya abasinziriye mu isi mbere, n'abazima basigaye bazabiyungaho ngo bansanganire mu kirere, kandi nzabajyana ku murage wanyu Data yahishe muri njye. Gusa ntimugacike intege.

Bizaba mu kanya nk'ako guhumbya, ubwo benshi muri mwe muzaba muri mu mirimo yanyu y'umunsi, kandi ako kanya muzahagarara imbere yanjye mbamurikira Data; kandi twifuza uwo munsi nk'uko twembi twifuza ukuhaba kwanyu. Nshobora rwose kugaragara nkabwiriza benshi, kandi ni inde wavuga ko ntabikoze rimwe na rimwe? Ese muzatangaza icyo Umwami ashobora gukora? Cyangwa muzagabanya ubushobozi bwanjye n'Ubwami bwanjye kubera kubura kwizera kwanyu? Ntibishoboka! Icyakora, nabagize abakozi bagenzi banjye kandi mbashyira mu murimo Data yampaye gukora, kandi mumenye ko ari icyubahiro n'ibyishimo nabahaye ku bw'agakiza kanyu, ingororano, n'ubuzima bwuzuye.

Nk'uko mwakoreye abandi mu izina ryanjye, ese nanjye sinabakoreye? Mutange ku buntu kandi ntimwiharire ubumenyi bw'ukuza kwanjye, nubwo benshi bazarwanya kandi mugafatwa nk'abasazi. Ibinyoma by'umwanzi bizayenga imbere y'amagambo yanyu yuzuye imbaraga mu gihe muvuga mu izina ryanjye kandi mugatangaza kugaragara kwanjye n'urubanza rugiye kuza, kuko no muri aya masegonda ya nyuma bamwe bazaza ku gakiza. Benshi ntibaritegura kandi bazanga amagambo yanyu; mubakunde uko byagenda kose, kuko umukumbi munini uzakizwa mu bigeragezo bikomeye n'amakuba, kandi mumenye ko amagambo yanyu ashobora kuba urumuri n'ibyiringiro mu bigeragezo bashobora guhura na byo.

Yemwe abatuye isi banze umuhamagaro wanjye kandi mwikura ku mpano yanjye y'agakiza n'ibyo izana byose: Urubanza rukomeye rwatangajwe ku isi kandi ruri hafi yanyu, nubwo mwibeshya, muti, "Ndifitiye imigambi mikomeye kandi ejo hazaba hameze nk'uyu munsi!" Wa mupfapfa we! Ntimunasobanukirwa ihinduka ry'ibihe, kuko ibirere bitangaza ubukwe bw'umugeni wanjye nanjye mu ijuru, kandi ibirere biva ku buntu bijya ku rubanza imbere y'amaso yanyu. Ibyago bikomeye bizabageraho vuba, kandi benshi bazapfira mu byaha byabo. Si ubushake bwanjye ko murimbuka, ariko mwanze igitambo cyanjye kitagira icyaha kuri mwe cyashoboraga kubakiza rwose.

Isi muzi uyu munsi izashira imbere y'amaso yanyu, kandi ibyishimo byose by'icyaha muri ubu buzima ntibizongera kuba inshuti zanyu; ahubwo muzahigwa n'ingabo z'abanzi banyu bifuza urupfu rwanyu kandi bararikira imitungo yanyu. Hazabaho gutigisa gukomeye munsi y'ibirenge byanyu, kandi isi iziyasa, kandi benshi bazagwa mu isi. Abayobozi banyu bamaze kubagambanira, nubwo mukibareba mu bupfapfa, mugerageza kumenya uko muzatora. Imunara yanyu izagwa, n'ibiraro byanyu bizahirima, n'ibikorwaremezo byanyu bizashira imbere y'amaso yanyu, kandi nta kizongera kuba uko cyari kiri.

Muzahungira he ko mwanze Umwami wera, njyewe, Umwami Yesu Kristo? Ni inde uzabakiza ubu mu gihe cyanyu cy'urubanza? Oya, ariko muravuga muti, Ntikwiriye urubanza, ushatse kuvuga iki? Ese ntimwanze Umuremyi wanyu ubwo yantumaga kuri mwe ku bw'ubuntu bwe bukiza, ubuntu bukiza mwibwira ko mudakwiriye kubona? Kuki mwanze? Ni irihe kosa nabakoreye? Natanze igitonyanga cyanjye cya nyuma cy'amaraso mu bubabare bukomeye n'agahinda kugira ngo mubone kubaho imbere yanjye, nyamara ntimurizera abahamya banjye natumye mu izina ryanjye.

Ndetse no mu kubaburira ku byago byanyu byegereje, yaba njyewe cyangwa Data nta byishimo dufite mu bigiye kurekurwa. Ariko nk'uko mwishimiye icyaha, ubu muzamizwa bunguri n'ingaruka mwibikiye. Nk'uko mwararikiye amafaranga, imitungo, n'icyubahiro cy'amafaranga, nta kindi mwakoze uretse kwirundaho umujinya ku meza yanyu, kandi muzasangira amakuba n'agahinda. Nzabaha umwijima mukunda cyane. Umwijima uzagwa mu gihugu cyanyu kandi uzatwikira isi, umwijima w'icuraburindi ku buryo izuba rya saa sita rizasa n'irizimijwe. Murambaza muti, ni ikihe cyaha mfite gituma uzana umujinya wawe; ese ntitwakoze imirimo myinshi ikomeye mu izina ryawe?

Ni ukuri, mwakoze imirimo myinshi, ariko si imirimo nabahaye; oya, mwabaye abahanuzi b'ibinyoma, muhanura amagambo ava mu mubiri wanyu ugoramye. Oya, mukora imirimo yanyu ku bw'icyubahiro cyanyu, kuko mwagize inda yanyu imana yanyu. Mushyira amagambo meza n'imvugo nziza z'abantu babi hejuru y'Ijambo ryanjye ryera. Ese sindi Ijambo ryabaye umubiri? Mwashoboraga kundya nk'umugati w'ubuzima mukarokoka kurimbuka, ntimuzongere gusonza ukundi. Mwashoboraga kunywa ku migezi y'amazi y'ubuzima atemba ava muri njye; yego, mwashoboraga kunywa ku mwuka wanjye ntimuzongere kugira inyota ukundi. Ariko mu kunyanga kwanyu, muzajya mu nzara ikomeye n'umubabaro, nubwo kugeza kuri uyu munota mugitekereza ko bitazabageraho mu kanya gato. Ubukungu bwanyu burarimbutse, kandi ubutunzi mwararikiye buzaba nta gaciro bufite ako kanya. Mwashyize ukwizera kwanyu mu bategetsi b'uyu mwijima w'iki gihe kandi mwishimiye ibinyoma bya Satani kuruta urumuri rw'ukuri kwanjye guhora guhari.

Ku bw'ibyo, muzashaka umwanya ku meza yabo, gusa musange nta mwanya wabateguriwe. Bazaba bafite byinshi bibikiye, kandi muzabona kwinjira gusa mufashe ikimenyetso cy'inyamaswa. Nubwo inyamaswa itarahishurwa neza mu gihe cyayo, nayiburiyeho kandi nyimenyesha abizerwa banjye kugira ngo batayobywa n'amagambo yayo meza n'imvugo nziza, nubwo muri iki gihe nta bwami ifite, byibuze kugeza ubu. Ntimufate ikimenyetso cyayo, kuko ababikora bose bazagabana n'inyamaswa n'umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma mu nyanja y'umuriro, kandi umwotsi w'ububabare bwabo uzahora ucumba.

Mwaraburiwe, kandi nubwo ngishaka kubakiza kurimbuka kwanyu, muracyanga umuhamagaro wanjye mwinangiye. Ku bw'ibyo, nzabaha imbuto z'inzira zanyu.

Muzahura n'inzara ikomeye, intambara ikomeye, kurimbuka gukomeye. Ingabo zitukura zizahiga, zibabaze, kandi zirimbure benshi muri mwe; kandi bizaba ko muzagoswa n'akaga no kurimbuka. Udatwawe ho imbohe mu bubabare n'urupfu ruzakurikiraho azagwa ku nkota; utagwa ku nkota azahura n'inyamaswa z'inkazi, zizica benshi; utarimbuka n'inyamaswa z'inkazi azicwa n'inzara, kandi inzira z'urupfu zizagwira mu gihugu.

Amakuba akomeye, ibyago, kurimbuka bizabageraho ako kanya, kandi imiraba ikomeye y'inyanja izaza ku gihugu cyanyu. Ntimuzongera kuba igihugu gikomeye ukundi, nyamara abasigaye bazakizwa. Umuntu wese uzambaza izina ry'Umwami azakizwa, ariko ukunda ubuzima bwe kugeza ku iherezo azagira uruhare rwe mu nyanja y'umuriro, kandi muzagabana mu bubabare n'igihano cya Satani kuko mwagundiriye isi ye igiye gupfa kugeza ku mwuka wanyu wa nyuma w'inangire, kandi muzarimburanwa n'iyi si igiye gupfa rwose. Mungarukire hakiri igihe kugira ngo mbakize mwe n'imiryango yanyu ibyago bigiye kuza. Nk'uko umukumbi munini wakiriye agakiza ku munsi wa Pentekote imyaka myinshi ishize mu minota mike ya mbere y'iki gihe, ni ko n'umukumbi munini uzakizwa mu minota mike ya nyuma y'iki gihe, kandi ubutumire bwanjye buracyahari mu gihe gito cyane gisigaye.

Ikibi kigiye kuza ntikizubaha abagore cyangwa abana cyangwa abageze mu za bukuru cyangwa abarwayi. Oya, icyo kibi kizamira byose gishobora mu kurimbuka gukomeye n'agahinda. Mumungire hakiri igihe, kuko isaha yenda guhagarara, kandi ntihazongera kubaho igihe ukundi. Satani agiye kujugunywa mu isi vuba, kandi ubwami bwe buzarekurwa byuzuye ku isi yanyu, kandi afite umujinya mwinshi kuko azi ko asigaranye igihe gito. Mungarukire mugishobora kugira ngo muhunge ikibi kigiye kuza.

Ndi Umwami w'abami n'Umutware w'abatware.

Ndi ugendagenda hagati y'ibitereko by'amatara birindwi kandi ntegeka abamarayika barindwi b'amatorero arindwi.

Ndi utegeka ingabo zo mu ijuru, nk'uko Data yashyize ubutegetsi bwazo mu maboko yanjye.

Ndi Ijambo ryabaye umubiri rikaba muri mwe.

Ndi umuzi wa Yesayi, kandi ubwami bwanjye ntibugira iherezo.

Ndi umwana w'Imana waje mu mubiri ngo atangire ibyaha bya bose impongano, iyaba gusa bose babyemeraga.

Abemeye ubuntu bwanjye kandi bakaba abizerwa bazategekana nanjye mu bwami bwanjye kandi bagabane ku ntebe yanjye y'ubwami, kuko nabagize abami n'abatambyi imbere y'Imana yacu. Hari ingororano ikomeye kuri mwe ibitswe mu ijuru; gusa muhagarare mushikamye kandi mwizerwa mu minota mike isigaye.
