Ibisharira N'ibiryohereye
6 Ugushyingo 2024

Ubwo Bazavuga Bati Amahoro N'umutekano!

Biturutse kuri Yeshua HaMashiach ari we Kristo Yesu:

Nimwumve Amagambo ya Yeshua HaMashiach, uwo Yahweh yatumye nk'Mesiya wanyu, Umwami, Umwami w'abami, n'Umutambyi Mukuru iteka ryose mu buryo bwa Melikisedeki kandi mwunamishe imitima yanyu mu kumvira Amagambo yanjye kuko nje kuburira abizerwa mu Itorero ryanjye, ndetse n'Umugeni wanjye, (n'abafite amatwi yo kumva bose baba abari hafi cyangwa kure) nanone ku buyobe bukomeye buriho ubu bwamaze gukwira ku isi yose, buzaba bubi kurushaho ubwo uzitira azakurwaho vuba kandi gitungumo! Sinavuze nti ni uwuhe mutezi w'imitego utega umutego inyoni ireba? Yemwe abafite ukwizera guke!

Nuko rero ndabaha gusobanukirwa nanone ibyo nigishije bene wacu ubwo nazaga muri Isirayeli nk'Umwana w'Intama w'Imana, nuko nimwumve Amagambo yanjye! Kuko hazaduka ba Kristo b'ibinyoma, n'abahanuzi b'ibinyoma, bazakora ibimenyetso n'ibitangaza bikomeye, kugira ngo, iyaba byashobokaga, bayobye n'intore. Dore, mbibabwiye mbere. Nuko nibababwira bati: Dore, ari mu butayu, ntimuzasohoke: Dore, ari mu byumba by'ibanga, ntimuzabyizere. Kuko nk'uko umurabyo urabira iburasirazuba, ukabonekera n'iburengerazuba, ni ko no kuza k'Umwana w'Umuntu kuzaba! Nuko ndabaha gusobanukirwa ko kugaragara kwanjye kuri hafi kandi kuje gitungumo n'imbaraga, kandi sinzangirwa umutungo wanjye naguze amaraso yanjye bwite, ndetse n'umugeni wanjye wizerwa n'umubiri wanjye, ndetse n'itorero ryanjye ryizerwa nkunda urukundo ruhoraho! Muri abanjye!

Kandi nuko bimeze, bakundwa banjye, ko ba Kristo b'ibinyoma badutse kandi babaye benshi. Ariko ubu ibihe by'ikiyoka byageze kandi amakuba ya Yakobo yageze kandi abasigaye bose bakomoka kuri Adamu ku isi bagomba gufata umwanzuro w'iteka. Kuko ikiyoka cyahawe gutegeka isi hamwe n'abungeri bacyo babiri babi, ndetse n'inyamaswa ivuye mu nyanja n'inyamaswa ivuye mu isi, ari na we muhanuzi wabo w'ibinyoma. Ese muzirinda kugwa mu buyobe bukomeye mwumvira ijwi ryanjye? Ese mwagerageza gukurikira isi mu buyobe bwayo n'ibinyoma byayo mukarimbuka rwose binyuze mu kutumvira ijwi ryanjye, ndetse n'igihe nje kubaburira ku buyobe bukomeye buriho ubu bwamaze kwigarurira isi? Ese mwemereye imitima yanyu n'ukwizera kwanyu kunyeganyezwa n'ubuyobe bukomeye muhabwa nk'ibyiringiro by'ibinyoma?

Kandi nuko bimeze ko nta gishobora guhindura amatangazo y'urubanza yatanzwe na Yahweh ubwe kuri Babuloni y'Ibanga, ndetse n'urubanza rwashyizwe mu maboko yanjye akomeye ngo ndurekure ubwo abizerwa bazaba bakoranyijwe kandi uzitira akuweho, kandi bizaba gitungumo nk'uko byasezeranijwe! Ndetse n'izo manza zatangajwe mu kuburira abaturage ba Babuloni y'Ibanga, umuburo birengagije rwose ubwo bakobaga batizera. Ndetse n'imiburo yanjye myinshi, myinshi yatanzwe mu kanwa k'abahanuzi banjye benshi batangaje aya Magambo y'umuburo mu budahemuka mu myaka myinshi cyane ubu. Iyo miburo nahagaritse kandi mu mwanya wayo ubu hakaba hari amatangazo, ndetse n'amatangazo ya Yahweh, n'aya Yeshua HaMashiach, ndetse ayo matangazo akaba adashoboka ko ikiremwa icyo ari cyo cyose kiyahagarika cyangwa ngo kiyahindure. Kuko ibyatangarijwe mu mbaraga n'intebe y'ubwami ya Yahweh nta muntu n'umwe ushobora kubihindura, kuko uko ni ko inzira z'Imana Ihoraho yo Gukiranuka ziri, ikora byose ikurikije inama y'ubushake bwayo.

Nuko rero ndababwira mwa bizerwa banjye, nk'uko nabwiye Simoni Petero mu isaha y'igitambo cyanjye nti: Simoni, Simoni, dore, Satani yabasabye, kugira ngo abagosore nk'ingano. Ariko ndakwingingiye, kugira ngo ukwizera kwawe kudacogora: nuko numara guhinduka, ukomeze bene wanyu. Nuko nje aho muri mu gukererwa kw'iyi saha y'imperuka (kuko isaha igiye kurangira) kubaburira mu buryo nk'ubwo kugira ngo mwikomeze mu nzira kandi ndabategeka mwebwe murabagirana nk'urumuri rw'Isi gukomeza bene wanyu no gukomezanya n'aya Magambo mu kwizera kwuje urukundo no kumvira kugira ngo mwese mushobore kwirinda no kwikomeza mu nzira, nuko mwumvire ijwi ryanjye kandi mugendere muri yo.

Dore! Handitswe ngo: Kandi ni cyo gituma Imana izaboherereza ubuyobe bukomeye, kugira ngo bizere ikinyoma: kugira ngo bacirwe urubanza bose batizeye ukuri, ahubwo bakishimira gukiranirwa. Ese muzagwa muri ubu buyobe butari ubwanyu, bakundwa banjye? Ni ukuri, ni ukuri, ndabwira itorero ryanjye nti, ese muzirukira mu maboko y'aba bayobya? Ndetse n'abatumwe ngo babarimbure n'ikiyoka ubwacyo? Nuko rero, bakundwa banjye, mukomeze muve hagati yabo kandi mwitandukanye! Ntimufatanye mu bikorwa byabo bibi n'ibyo biteze kuko benshi batwawe n'uburozi bwabo n'ubupfumu bwabo, kandi uwo bahisemo ubu avuga nk'ikiyoka, ndetse atanga amasezerano akomeye n'amagambo akomeye yiyerekeye kandi benshi bazakurikira inzira ze zangiza kandi benshi bazayobywa kandi bafatwe mu mutego, nuko muhindukirire ijwi ryanjye kandi mwumve amagambo yanjye kandi munyirukireho bakundwa banjye, ntimuhindukirire ibumoso, cyangwa iburyo kandi nzabarinda amahoro mu kiganza cy'amaboko yanjye y'iteka aho nta n'umwe ushobora kubakura mu ntoki zanjye zikomeye.

Nuko rero kuki mwemerera imitima yanyu kubahagarika umutima, mwa bizerwa banjye kandi b'ukuri, ndetse n'abo nkunda urukundo ruhoraho, ndetse n'abo bose ikiyoka gishaka gufatira mu mutego w'ubuyobe n'urubanza rutari urwanyu. Nuko rero, ntimwifanye n'ubuyobe bwabo kandi muve hagati yabo kandi mwitandukanye! Kandi ku banyu bihindukiriye bava mu buyobe bukomeye mu buryo bukwiye, ndabategeka kutabisubiramo na gato. Dore! Yaba Yahweh, Imana Ikomeye Ihoraho, cyangwa Yeshua HaMashiach ntibazohereza cyangwa ngo babe barohereje ibisubizo bya politiki cyangwa abategetsi b'isi kugira ngo bakemure ibibazo by'isi muri iki gihe. Ndetse n'ibibazo bije mu rubanza ku bari ku isi biturutse kuri Yahweh ubwe, urubanza nta n'umwe ushobora kurokoka, keretse abizerwa muri Kristo Yesu. Musobanukirwe neza ibyo nategetse Pawulo kwandikira Abefeso mu rwandiko nategetse ko anyandikira mu izina ryanjye ku itorero ryanjye nkunda:

Hanyuma, bene wacu, mukomerere mu Mwami, no mu mbaraga z'ubushobozi bwe. Mwambare intwaro zose z'Imana, kugira ngo mushobore guhagarara mudatsinzwe n'ibitero by'umwanzi. Kuko tudakirana n'abafite amaraso n'umubiri, ahubwo dukirana n'abatware, n'abafite ubushobozi, n'abategetsi b'iyi si y'umwijima, n'imyuka mibi iri mu byo mu ijuru. Nuko musobanukirwe bakundwa banjye ko amagambo yanjye adahinduka, nuko rero kuki jye, Kristo Yesu Umwami wanyu wazamutse, naba naraboherereje umutegetsi w'isi, cyangwa umwami wahagarika imanza zizaza? Yahweh, uwo nuzuza ubushake bwe mu buryo bwuzuye kandi bidashoboka ko yabeshya, yashobora ate kwivuguruza mu migambi ye y'iteka nahawe yose ngo nyuzuze?

Kuko abategetsi b'isi bose bahaye ikiyoka ubwumvikane bwabo n'imbaraga zabo, kuko ari ko bahawe ngo babisohoze kuko n'abanzi ba Yahweh bumvira ijwi rye kandi bazasohoza ubushake bwe bahawe muri iki gihe gito cyane kigiye kugwira isi n'umujinya mwinshi! Kuko ibihe by'umujinya w'Umwana w'Intama n'umujinya w'Imana Ishoborabyose byageze kandi nta gishobora guhagarika, cyangwa ngo kibangamire na gato ibigiye kurekurwa vuba aha nyuma y'uko nkoranyirije abizerwa banjye mu ijuru. Nuko ndarangurura! Ni nde ushobora guhagarara imbere y'ubushake bw'iteka bwa Yahweh, NDIHO ukomeye?!

Nyamara, musobanukirwe ko mu gihe cyanjye cy'ubuntu nategetse Itorero ryanjye gusabira abategetsi babo b'isi, nzi ko abategetsi babo b'isi bari babi kamere kandi kugira ngo binyuze mu kwinginga kw'abera ikibi kizitirwe kurushaho (kandi uzitira wanjye aracyazitira), kandi aba bategetsi bashoboraga guhitamo ibyagirira akamaro n'imibereho myiza abakunda Imana, kandi uko ni ko gihe cyanjye cy'ubuntu cyari kimeze, nubwo ibitotezo byari byinshi. Kandi nubwo, isengesho rivuye ku mutima ry'umukiranutsi rigira umumaro mwinshi, musobanukirwe ko nta bategetsi b'isi batanzwe ngo bakize isi kugeza igihe nzazira n'ingabo zikomeye z'Abami n'Abatambyi b'iteka bazategeka isi mu gukiranuka ku itegeko ryanjye kuko ndi Umwami w'abami n'Umutware w'abatware kandi ndi Umutambyi Mukuru iteka ryose mu buryo bwa Melikisedeki.

Musobanukirwe neza kandi mudaciye ku ruhande, nta bategetsi b'isi bashobora kuzana amahoro ku isi. Nta bategetsi b'isi bashobora kuzana umutekano ku isi. Nta bategetsi b'isi bashobora kugarura uburumbuke ku isi. Nubwo, bazasezeranya amahoro n'umutekano n'uburumbuke bwagarutse, musobanukirwe ko badafite imbaraga zo gutanga ibyo basezeranya, nubwo bazaha ibintu by'isi bitagira agaciro abemera ikimenyetso cy'inyamaswa, ndetse n'ibihembo byo gukiranirwa bizabakurwaho rwose mu rubanza! Nuko ntimubakurikire kuko umuntu wese n'abazabakurikira bose amaherezo bazasabwa kwemera ikimenyetso cy'inyamaswa kandi ababikora bahabwa iteka ryose mu muriro w'igihano udashira, w'iteka kandi isi n'ibintu byayo bitagira agaciro birashira. Kandi ikiyoka kizamuka rwose n'inyamaswa zacyo, kandi inyamaswa ivuye mu isi yateguye ubumara bwayo butangwa n' kurumwa kw'inzoka kugira ngo itere ubumara bwayo kandi icyuma kizavangwa n'ibumba, ariko ntibizafatana.

Kandi nuko byanditswe kandi byandikwa neza mu buhamya bw'ihishurirwa ryanjye, ndetse n'Ibyahishuwe bya Yesu Kristo kugira ngo bose basobanukirwe abafite amaso yo kureba n'amatwi yo kumva, n'umutima woroshye wo gusobanukirwa, nuko Yohana arandika ati: Kandi marayika arambwira ati, Ni iki gitumye utangara? Ndakubwira ibanga ry'umugore, n'iry'inyamaswa imuhetse, ifite imitwe irindwi n'amahembe icumi. Inyamaswa wabonye yahozeho, kandi ntiriho; kandi izazamuka ive mu kuzimu, ijye kurimbuka: kandi abatura ku isi bazatangara amazina yabo atanditswe mu gitabo cy'ubugingo uhereye ku kuremwa kw'isi, ubwo bazabona inyamaswa yahozeho, kandi ntiriho, nyamara ikaba iriho.

Nuko rero, bana banjye bato mwakire inyigisho kandi ndabaha gusobanukirwa nubwo ubwami bw'ikiyoka budashobora kuzamuka rwose (kuko uzitira azazitira kugeza ubwo uzitira atazongera kuzitira, ndetse n'igihe abizerwa muri Kristo bazatwarwa mu ijuru) abaguye bemerewe gutegura igitero cyabo gitunguranye kandi Imana yoherereza abatura ku isi mu kutumvira ubuyobe bukomeye. Ariko ibi bintu ntibigenewe mwebwe bizerwa banjye kandi batoranyijwe, kandi jye, Yeshua HaMashiach ndatangaza kandi ndahamya uku kuri n'aya magambo nohereje binyuze ku ntumwa yanjye n'umugaragu wanjye ngo asomerwe ihumure n'ukumvira by'abo aya magambo yose agenewe. Nuko ndabaza abanyu bahitamo ubu buyobe aho guhitamo ukuri kwanjye kw'iteka, kuki mutangara? Ese muzakomeza guhitamo ibinyoma by'abungeri babi aho guhitamo ukuri n'ihumure umwuka w'ubuhanuzi uzana?

Nuko rero, ndabaha gusobanukirwa ko aba bungeri babiri babi bariho kandi batariho nyamara BAZABAHO RWOSE, batarazamuka rwose, kandi bazazamuka rwose bave mu ivu n'amatongo by'ubutegetsi bwa kera kandi ubutegetsi bwabo bushya buzazamuka rwose! Ubu bihitiyemo Moshiach wabo w'ikinyoma, ndetse na Baraba, kandi azubaka urusengero rwabo, kandi inyamaswa ivuye mu nyanja iracyakoresha igikoresho cyayo gishaje kandi bazatsinda, kandi bazazana irimbuka ryuzuye rya Babuloni y'Ibanga, kuko barwana bakoresheje uburiganya kandi uko ni ko kamere yabo iri.

Kandi batumwe n'igitagangurirwa cyateye ibihugu, kandi ni igitagangurirwa cyamuhisemo ngo ayobore ibihugu ubwo bizaba byazamutse rwose. Kuko ari Isinagogi ya Satani yafashe umwanzuro wayo igahitamo Moshiach wabo mushya w'ikinyoma kandi Babuloni y'Ibanga yongeye guha ikiyoka ubwumvikane bwayo, nuko bazashyirwa rwose mu maboko y'ikiyoka kibaha imbaraga zo gusohoza umujinya n'urubanza, urubanza ndetse rwoherejwe nk'umugabane wikubye kabiri uturutse kuri Yahweh ubwe, urubanza rudatinda kandi rwegereje nuko bihitiyemo irimbuka ryabo bwite.

Kandi nuko bimeze ko Babuloni y'Ibanga ubu itangaza ko ejo hazaza hayo hazaba hatarimo urubanza rwa Yahweh ubwo bashaka gucika bakoba mu nzira zabo zitarimo ukwizera. Kuko Babuloni y'Ibanga iboshywe rwose mu ruti rw'igitagangurirwa, kandi ntishobora gucika, kandi Babuloni y'Ibanga iri mu minyururu itegereje urubanza rwayo rwa nyuma. Kandi bazakomeza kwangiza, kwiba, kwica, gusahura no gutwara ku ngufu uburumbuke bwayo n'urubyaro rwayo kuko ari ko bahawe ngo babisohoze kandi hazasigara ubutayu aho bazasahura isi kurushaho bashaka umutungo bifuza cyane.

Kandi hanyuma irimbuka ritunguranye rizaba mu buryo butunguranye, kandi mu isaha imwe azarimburwa. Ndongera kuvuga nti, intambara izaduka mu buryo butunguranye ku isi yose, kandi umuriro uzagwa, kandi bazohereza ibisasu byinshi bya misile kugira ngo bamurimbure. Umujyi we ukomeye cyane uzashya kandi imiraba minini y'irimbuka izagera ku nkombe ze z'iburasirazuba n'iburengerazuba. Isi izatigisa cyane, kandi azacikamo kabiri hagati kandi aho hantu hari heza cyane kandi hakize, hazasigara amazi azamugabamo kabiri, kandi azagwa rwose mu gihano cye cyikubye kabiri. Ndetse n'igihano bihitiyemo ubwabo ubwo birengagizaga ijwi ryanjye nohereje binyuze mu ntumwa zanjye n'abahanuzi n'abagaragu banjye abakomeye n'aboroheje: ni ukuri yirengagije bose kugeza arimbutse kandi ntazongera kubyuka ukundi. Kandi bityo isi ishyizwe mu maboko y'ikiyoka kandi kizaha ubutegetsi bwacyo bwo kuzimu abungushumba bacyo babi cyazamuye ku bw'imigambi yacyo bwite kandi bononekaye iteka ryose.

Nuko rero, ntimugwe mu buriganya bwabo inyamaswa yo ku butaka izana kuko ivuga nk'ikiyoka. Ntimugakurikire abo muri Babuloni y'Ibanga bategereje urubanza rwabo, kuko urubanza rwayo ruzaza mu isaha imwe kandi mu buryo butunguranye ntibeho! Kuko abashoboraga gukurikira Yesu w'i Nazareti nk'Umwami wabo ubu bashukishijwe kwinjira mu maboko y'umushukanyi kuko bakunda kubaho mu buyobe bwabo, batekereza ko uko bimeze uyu munsi, ari ko byari bimeze ejo, kandi rwose uyu mwami ukomeye azongera angire ukomeye! Ariko, wowe Babuloni y'Ibanga, ntuzongera gukomera ukundi kandi ibyaha byawe biragumaho kandi ntuzacika urubanza rwawe ruri kuri wowe.

Kandi ni jye Yeshua HaMashiach, ndetse Umwami w'abami n'Umutware w'abatware utaratanze umwami n'umwe ku isi muri iki gihe kuko nta bundi butegetsi Yahweh yashyizeho, uretse Ubwami bwo mu Ijuru, ubwo jye Yeshua HaMashiach ndi Umwami w'abami n'Umutware w'abatware! Nuko rero mwumve ko abiyeguriye uyu mushukanyi bose bagata Umwami n'umukiza wabo Yesu Kristo bazasigirwa umuriro w'utunganya kuko muri mu maboko ya Kristo w'ikinyoma, ari we kandi Moshiach wabo w'ikinyoma. Kandi bityo gutandukanya kwa nyuma kuje mu gihe cyanjye cy'ubuntu kandi benshi barashutswe batwarwa nk'iminyago n'inyamaswa yo ku butaka. Kandi nubwo, benshi bazava mu buriganya bwayo bakajya mu bugingo buhoraho bizasaba amakuba ya Yakobo kugira ngo bibakure mu buyobe bukomeye niba birengagije umuhamagaro wanjye wo kwihana ako kanya muri aya masegonda ya nyuma.

Kandi bityo ndabaha kumva ko Yahweh yatanze impanda ya nyuma ngo ivuge, ndetse impanda yanjye ya nyuma mu gihe cyanjye cy'ubuntu kandi impanda izavuga. Kuko igihe kigeze kandi kiri kuri mwe bakundwa banjye, aho nzamanuka mvuye mu ijuru n'ijwi rirenga, n'ijwi rya marayika mukuru n'impanda y'Imana, kandi abapfiriye muri Kristo bazabanza kuzuka kandi namwe abari muri Kristo muzabasanga mu kirere kandi bityo muzabana nanjye iteka bakundwa banjye kandi sinzasiga abizerwa banjye mu muriro w'utunganya kuko gutunganywa kwabo nkurangije mu rukundo rwanjye mbakunda kuko baje aho ndi ku bushake bwabo ndetse n'igihe nahishurirwaga kuri bo.

Nuko rero mwumve bakundwa banjye ko isaha igeze kugira ngo ikiyoka kizamuke byuzuye kandi kugira ngo intambara n'urubanza bize byuzuye kandi kugira ngo inyamaswa yo mu nyanja n'inyamaswa yo ku butaka zizamuke byuzuye hamwe n'ikiyoka cyazo. Iyo ntagabanya iminsi nta muntu n'umwe wari gusigara ari muzima, kandi bityo igitero cyabo gitunguranye kizaza byuzuye ku isi kandi bazarimbura rwose Babuloni y'Ibanga. Nuko rero ntimugwe mu buyobe bwabo n'ibinyoma byabo mu gihe bishimira ubwabo bakavuga ku mugaragaro imbere y'imanza za Yahweh n'umujinya w'Umwana w'Intama bati “amahoro n'umutekano” “amahoro n'umutekano”! Kuko ndi Yeshua HaMashiach uzakura rwose amahoro n'umutekano ku isi kugeza ku mpera y'icyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli ubwo nzashyiraho ubwami bwanjye byuzuye ku isi. Nuko rero, ntimugwe mu buyobe bwabo.

Kandi ni jye wohereza aya Magambo binyuze mu ntumwa yanjye n'umugaragu wanjye kandi binyuze mu ntumwa yanjye n'umugaragu wanjye aya Magambo yoherejwe.

Dore! Igihe kigeze kugira ngo Umwami wanyu amurikishe ubwiza bwe n'icyubahiro cye mu kirere kugira ngo bose babibone kandi nzateranya abizerwa banjye bose muri Kristo ndetse n'abo ku mpera z'isi mu gihe nzabonekera mu kirere kugira ngo nteranye abizerwa muri Kristo kandi kuboneka kwanjye kuzagaragara mu kirere nk'uko umurabyo uva iburasirazuba, ukagaragara kugeza iburengerazuba, kandi sinzahakanwa!

Ndi Yeshua HaMashiach uzaza gucira imanza no kurwana intambara ku isi kandi ubwami bwanjye ntibuzagira iherezo.

Ndi uwo NDIHO ukomeye yangize kandi ibihe birageze.

Ndi Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda kandi ibihe byanjye birageze.

Byose bizaba iteka ryose, Yego na Amen!

Bivuye kuri Yahweh Imana ikomeye y'Iteka:

Nimwumve, Nimwumve amagambo y'Imana Ihoraho kandi y'Iteka! Ibihe by'amakuba ya Yakobo bije ku isi kandi ibihe by'isarura birageze. Hanyuma mu buryo butunguranye kandi butitezwe umwana wanjye, Kristo Yesu, ari we kandi Yeshua HaMashiach azabonekera mu bicu kugira ngo abapfuye n'abazima muri Kristo bazuke bave mu isi bambaye ukudapfa no kutabora kugira ngo bahagarare baremwe bushya mu iteka ryose ndetse n'igihe bazahagarara mu cyumba cyanjye bwite cy'intebe y'ubwami, ndetse n'igihe nifuje kandi nategereje mu bihe by'iteka byahise.

Nuko rero, mwitondere Yeshua HaMashiach kuko natanze itegeko kandi icyo gihe rwose kiregereje kandi umunsi n'igihe nyacyo ubu bizwi mu ijuru, nubwo bitarahishurwa byuzuye ku isi. Kandi bityo ibihe by'iryo jwi rya nyuma ry'impanda birageze kandi umuganura uzateranywa kandi umukwe aziyunga mu isezerano ry'iteka n'umugeni we wizerwa kandi bazasohoza imigambi yanjye y'iteka kandi Isezerano Rishya rya Yeshua HaMashiach rizashyirwaho byuzuye iteka ryose, kuko amasezerano yanjye yose ari Yego na Amen!

Abizerwa muri Kristo bazahagarara imbere y'intebe y'imanza y'Umwami wabo kandi bahabwe ibikwiriye imirimo yabo yaba myiza cyangwa mibi ariko bose bafite ubugingo buhoraho. Kandi benshi bazatwara ingororano ikomeye y'ibintu bitigeze byinjira mu mutima w'abantu byuzuye ibyo nabateguriye. Kuko Kristo Yesu azabagira Abami n'Abatambyi imbere y'intebe yanjye y'ubwami kandi bazaragwa byuzuye intebe zabo z'ubwami n'ubutware biva kuri Yeshua HaMashiach mu murage we namuhaye kugira ngo asangire n'abizerwa, n'abahamagawe, n'abagaragaye ko ari abanyakuri, kandi b'ukuri.
