Ubuhamya Bwombi: Itorero
Mu Mirimo y'Intumwa Pawulo
4 Gicurasi 2024

Muzabona ishyano mwa bungeri bayobye!

Muzabona ishyano, mwa bungeri bayobye, kuko mwateye amatorero yanyu y'ibinyoma mu bihugu byose, kandi ntimumvugira, cyangwa ngo mbatume mu mirimo mwihesheje. Kandi mwagize amafaranga Imana yanyu, kuko mwiringira indamu mbi, kandi mwigishije ubwoko bwanjye ko inyungu z'umubiri ari ukubaha Imana. Muzabona ishyano, mwa bungeri bayobye, kuko amagambo ya Yeremiya abasobanura neza: Indogobe yo mu ishyamba imenyereye ubutayu, ihumeka umuyaga uko yishakiye; mu gihe cyayo ni nde wayisubiza inyuma? Kuko ubusambanyi bwanyu n'ubusukanyi bwanyu ari iby'ibibi, kuko mwahisemo ba Satani nk'imana yanyu, kandi ibyo bikomangoma nta gaciro bifite si imana. Muri abakozi b'ibinyoma biyoberanya nk'abakozi bo gukiranuka, kandi ntibitangaje, kuko Satani yiyoberanya nk'umumarayika w'umucyo.

Muzabona ishyano, mwa bungeri bayobye, kuko mwateye amatorero yahinduye amazi yanjye aryoshye y'ubugingo nk'umuravumba usharira, ayo muha abo nahamagaye, amazi y'akazuyaze yanduye asharira mu nda. Ndetse n'amazi yanyu asharira atangirwa mu mariba atobetse, kandi nta mbaraga mufite, nubwo mwirata imbaraga nyinshi. Mwanduje ibihugu n'inyigisho z'ibinyoma kandi mwubaka amatorero yanyu y'ibinyoma nka gereza z'umwuka zangijwe na guverinoma yanyu, kuko mwagurishije umukumbi wanjye mu kuboko k'umwanzi; nuko rero, nimuyisohokemo none, bwoko bwanjye! Amatorero yanyu y'ibinyoma nta gaciro akoreshwa nk'ibigo by'igenzura aho muzazana urupfu n'irimbuka ku basigaye inyuma, kandi muzabishyura.

Mwagize Remfani Imana yanyu. Mukorera Bayali. Mutambira abana Moloki. Mwarangiritse rwose. Muri amariba adafite amazi, inzabya zirimo ubusa zikwiriye kurimbuka, kuko umurage wanyu ari amazuku yaka umuriro, kandi muzaba muri rya ziba rinini hamwe na so, Umubi. Muri intumwa z'ibinyoma, abahanuzi b'ibinyoma, abavugabutumwa b'ibinyoma, abashumba b'ibinyoma, n'abigisha b'ibinyoma; kandi abo muhindurira mu nzira zanyu zangiza mubahindura abana b'ikuzimu inshuro ebyiri kubarusha, kuko muri ab'ubwangizi bwangiza rwose. Kuko Sitefano yagaragaje mbere inzira zanyu ubwo yavugaga ibya ba sokuruza banyu, ndetse n'urumamfu rwayogoje Isirayeli, kuko yavuze ati: Yego, mwajyanye ihema rya Moloki, n'inyenyeri y'Imana yanyu Remfani, ibishushanyo mwakoze, kugira ngo mubiramye. Mwa bungeri bayobye ndetse muzamura mudafite isoni inyenyeri ya Remfani iyo mwitiranya mu bujiji na Dawidi, sogokuruza wanjye; kuko njyewe, Yesu Kristo, ari ko nkomoka, kandi nzategekera ku ntebe ya Dawidi mu gukiranuka. Kuko ari njye uhawe ubutegetsi nk'ubwo, kuko nzategeka isi mu gukiranuka!

Muzabona ishyano, mwa bungeri bayobye, nzabishyura byimazeyo ku bw'amagambo yanyu yose mabi, kandi inzira zanyu zose mbi zizagaruka ku mitwe yanyu iteka ryose. Kuko muzifatanya n'Umubi n'abamarayika be mu murage wabo uturuka ku Mana Isumbabyose; ndetse na Yehova yabateguriye iziba rinini ry'umuriro udashira. Mwimye agakiza abagashakaga, ariko mumenye ko nzabakiza kandi muzabishyura. Mwimye umwuka wanjye abari kwatura izina ryanjye mubahe amagambo y'ibinyoma yo kuvuga n'amasengesho y'ubupfu, y'ubusa kandi asubirwamo adakiza. Nimunyumve, bwoko bwanjye: agakiza kazana no kwatura Yesu nk'Umwami wanyu no kwizera ko Imana yamuzuye mu bapfuye, kandi mu isano ikiza nanjye gusa ni ho mushobora gukizwa; kuko nima ikimenyetso cy'Imana abatura izina ryanjye bafite umutima utizera, kuko ngenzura imitima n'impyiko.

Mwibwira mu bwibone ko kubera ko munyita Umwami imbere y'iteraniro ryanyu mwagize imbohe ko mbazi? Nzabwira kuri uwo munsi nti, nimumve imbere, mwa nkozi z'ibibi mwe, kuko ntabwo nigeze mbamenya! Mwigisha ibinyoma mu cyimbo cy'ukuri, kandi muyobya abanshakashaka, njye Mwami Yesu Kristo, mukabajyana mu butumwa bwiza bwangiritse, bw'ubupfu, kuko nta bundi butumwa bwiza buhari: Kuko inkuru nziza iri muri njye, Kristo Yesu! Amagambo yanyu y'ubusa agiye kurangira, kandi umujinya wanjye urabariho cyane, kandi ntimuzarokoka. Mbona buri gikorwa cyo gutuka Imana mukora; numva buri jambo ryo gutuka Imana muvuga mu izina ryanjye, kuko muri abiyoberanya. Mwiyoberanya ko muvuga amagambo yanjye, nk'uko mwiyoberanya ko nabatumye n'inkuru zanyu z'ibihimbano n'ibitangaza byanyu by'ibihimbano n'imikino yanyu yo gukiza y'ibihimbano, kuko muri abiyoberanya!

Muzabona ishyano, mwa bungeri bayobye, kuko mwiba abari kuza aho ndi, mubabwira ibinyoma ko basabwa n'Imana kubaha amafaranga. Mugoreka Ibyanditswe Byera byanjye mu bujiji, mubwira abo mwayobeje ko bagomba kubaha icyacumi. Kuko nabwiye Pawulo iki igihe yandikiraga ab'i Korinto: Ariko ndavuga nti, Ubiba gake, azasarura gake: n'ubiba kenshi, azasarura kenshi. Umuntu wese atange nk'uko abigambiriye mu mutima we; atinuba, cyangwa asa n'ushyizweho agahato: kuko Imana ikunda utanga yishimye. Ariko oya, mwigisha ibinyoma mu cyimbo cy'ukuri kandi mwiba ubwoko bwanjye, ndetse mwohereza amafaranga mukusanya mu migambi ya Satani, kandi muzabishyura, kuko uburakari bwanjye bubariho cyane kuko muyobora abantu banshaka, njye Yesu Kristo, mukabakura ku gakiza mukabajyana kurimbuka. Ariko mumenye ibi, mwa bungeri bayobye, ntimuzigera mukura n'umwe muri aba mu maboko yanjye, kuko mu bo Data yampaye bose, natakaje umwe gusa, umwana wo kurimbuka, ari we Yuda, kuko yahisemo kurimbuka kwe aho guhitamo ubugingo muri njye! Imirimo yanyu n'imihati yanyu ni imfabusa, yewe bungeri bayobye; mwibikiye urubanza rukomeye n'umujinya. Kuko navuze iby'inzira za ba sokuruza banyu, kandi ni mwe murya ingo z'abapfakazi kandi mukitwaza gusenga amasengesho maremare: MUZAHABWA igihano kirushijeho gukomera!

Muzabona ishyano, mwa bungeri bayobye, kuko mwayoboye ubwoko bwanjye gushyigikira guverinoma z'isi zitari izanjye, nubwo isi ari iyanjye. Yewe, mwa bungeri bayobye, mwereka ubwoko bwanjye abayobozi b'isi b'Umubi, mubabwira ko batumwe n'Imana. Muyobya abantu mukabatekereza ko ntazabaha umugisha keretse bahaye umugisha abakora jenoside ku banyantege nke. Ariko navuze iki mu byanditswe ku byerekeye mwe, kuko ibi atari bishya, kandi benshi baje mbere yanyu mu buriganya, nubwo muri abapfu bihagije ngo mutekereze ko muri umwimerere. Nimunyumve, bana banjye, kuko ndi gushyira ahagaragara ubu bubi ku bwanyu. Kuko ibyanditswe byanjye bivuga biti: Muri ab'Imana, bana bato, kandi mwabanesheje: kuko uri muri mwe aruta uri mu isi. Bo ni ab'isi: ni cyo gituma bavuga iby'isi, kandi isi ikabumva. Inyigisho zanyu z'iby'isi, imigenzo yanyu y'iby'isi, ubugambanyi bwanyu bw'iby'isi na Satani, byose bizagera ku iherezo ritunguranye, kandi ntimuzongera kubyuka. Ariko mwe, Mugeni wanjye, muri abanjye, kandi aya magambo si ayanyu, kuko muzinjira mu byishimo by'Umwami wanyu, ari njye, Kristo Yesu.

Ndabwira none amateraniro y'amatorero y'ibinyoma ayobowe n'abungeri bayobye, ndetse namwe, iteraniro ryanjye rya Lawodikiya. Nzi imirimo yawe, ko utari uw'ubukonje cyangwa uw'ubushyuhe; nakwifuje ko uba uw'ubukonje cyangwa uw'ubushyuhe. Kuko Pawulo yandikiye Timoteyo ibyawe n'abandi nkawe: Kuko igihe kizaza ubwo batazihanganira inyigisho nzima, ahubwo bakurikije irari ryabo baziyongerera abigisha, bafite amatwi abarya: Kandi bazakura amatwi yabo ku kuri, bayahindurire ku migani y'ibihimbano. Benshi muri mwe bitabira aya matorero y'ibinyoma kubera igitutu cy'abandi no kwiyoberanya ko muri kunshakashaka, njye Kristo Yesu, ibyo mutari byo rwose.

Mwifuza gushimishwa, kuko gushimishwa ari cyo cyifuzo cyanyu, kandi gushimishwa kwabaye ikigirwamana cyanyu! Kandi ntimuzanyumva, njye Yesu Kristo, cyangwa ngo mwumve n'umwe mu ntumwa zanjye keretse amatwi yanyu ashimishijwe, ariko amagambo yanjye ntashimisha amatwi! Mwa materaniro yayobye: Nimunyumve kandi munyumve neza, kuko navuze mu gitabo gikomeye cy'Ibyahishuwe nti: Kandi numva irindi jwi riva mu ijuru, rivuga riti, Nimuyisohokemo, bwoko bwanjye, kugira ngo mutagira uruhare mu byaha byayo, kandi ngo mutakira ibyago byayo: Kuko ibyaha byayo byageze mu ijuru, kandi Imana yibutse gukiranirwa kwayo. Nimuyisohokemo, cyangwa muzasangira ibyago byayo n'urubanza rwayo, kuko igihe kigeze. Ku ba Lawodikiya banjye: Nimuve mu matorero yanyu y'ibinyoma none, kuko igihe kiri hafi, kandi ndababurira, nubwo benshi muri mwe muri abanebwe cyane ngo mukore ku magambo yanjye.

Ntimushidikanye ku muburo wanjye, kuko nahaye Yohana kwandika iki mu rwandiko rwe rwa kabiri? Umuntu wese ucumuza ntagume mu nyigisho ya Kristo, nta Mana afite: uguma mu nyigisho ya Kristo, uwo afite Data n'Umwana. Nihagira uza aho muri, atazanye iyo nyigisho, ntimuzamwakire mu nzu yanyu, cyangwa ngo mumwifurize amahoro y'Imana. Kuko umwifuriza amahoro y'Imana, aba afatanije na we imirimo ye mibi. Ariko mwe ba Lawodikiya muri abatumvira cyane ku buryo mwima amatwi aya magambo kandi aho kubifuriza amahoro y'Imana, ibyo ubwabyo ari bibi bihagije, mujya kure mu bibi mukabatumira mu mazu yanyu kandi mukabaha uburenganzira ku bana banyu, kandi muri mu makosa rwose imbere yanjye, nuko rero NIMWIHANE!

Kuko mu by'ukuri, mwakinguriye imiryango yanyu isi n'inzira zayo; mwinjije amasega, ndetse n'abo hasi cyane, kugira ngo barye abana banjye, kandi nimutihana, muzabishyura. Nimwihane cyangwa nzabatera, kuko musangiye imirimo yabo mibi, kandi ntimunyumvira, njye Kristo Yesu, ndetse n'uzabambura ingororano yanyu akayiha undi niba mukomeza kunyima amatwi. Kuko ubuntu bwanjye n'imbabazi zanjye bigiye kurangira, kandi umujinya uzaguma mu mwanya wabyo, nubwo ubuntu n'imbabazi bihorana nanjye, kandi umuntu wese uzambaza izina ry'Umwami kugeza ku iherezo azakizwa. Kuko mudashobora kugirana ubusabane n'imirimo y'umwijima itagira umumaro, ahubwo mugomba kuyihana.

Ariko muri abaswa cyane ngo mutekereze ko kubera ko umuntu abeshya kandi agakoresha izina ryanjye ryera mu buryo bwo gutuka Imana ku mirimo yabo itari iyera ko batumwe nanjye kandi bavuga amagambo yanjye. Nimwumve amagambo yanjye, kuko muzabamenyera ku mbuto zabo! Nimusubire ku Jambo ryanjye ryera ry'Imana kandi munyigireho, kuko mutanzi kandi ijwi ryanjye ni iry'amahanga kuri mwe, kandi nta Data mufite kuko mudafite Umwana. Nimungarukire mu kwihana, nanjye nzabakira. Mwicare mu magunira n'ivu mu mitima yanyu kandi mwicishe bugufi kugira ngo mbashyire hejuru! Nabahamagariye kunesha, kandi none ni ryo shanswe ryanyu rya nyuma ryo kunesha aba bungeri bayobye n'inzira zabo mbi; kuko ndaje, kandi nzanye umujinya mwinshi kuri Babuloni y'Ibanga.

Igihe ni igikundiro mutagifite. Ntimushobora gukomeza kuba abanebwe, cyangwa muzisanga mu makuba ya Yakobo, ubwo nari kubategurira umwanya ku meza yanjye nkabashyingira nk'umugeni wanjye, ariko muri mu maboko y'undi mu buhemu, ari we Satani! Nuko rero nimuze aho ndi kugira ngo mbasubize ingororano yanyu. Ntibihagije kugana ku miryango isohoka mu kwizinukwa; mugomba kwihana ubu bugome kandi mugahindura inzira zanyu kandi mukanyatura ibi byaha, nanjye nzabeza mu maraso yanjye y'impongano kandi mbahanagureho amabara mu isuku yanjye itunganye, kuko ndi umwera kandi imyenda yanjye yera nk'urubura rushya. Nimungarukire kandi muzabana nanjye iteka ryose; nimusohoke kandi muze mu maboko yanjye arambuye, kuko ndi hano ngo mbabwire ko nta gihe mufite cyo guta.

Ku Mugeni wanjye wizerwa, wumvise ijwi ryanjye kandi agakurikira inzira zanjye: Kuri mwe ndavuga nti, nimukomeze mu budahemuka bwanyu kuri njye, kandi niba hari n'umwe muri mwe ufite ikirenge mu muryango w'itorero rya Lawodikiya, ndabategetse kugikura mu muryango kandi mureke umuryango ufungwe! Kuko mudashobora gukora ibyo Umwami wanyu adashaka! Ntimwasomye urwandiko rwanjye nandikiye itorero rya Lawodikiya mu Byahishuwe? Dore, mpagaze ku muryango, ndakomanga: umuntu wese numva ijwi ryanjye, agakingura umuryango, nzinjira iwe, nzasangira na we, na we asangire nanjye. Mumenye ko, ntavuze ko nzinjira mu Itorero rya Lawodikiya, ariko niba umuntu uwo ari we wese mu itorero rya Lawodikiya yumvise ijwi ryanjye agakingura umuryango, nzinjira nsangire na we, atari itorero rye rya mbere ryayobye, nubwo nabo ndikubabahamagara niba gusa bakwita. Mugeni wanjye, umugaragu ntaruta shebuja, nuko rero mukurikire inzira zanjye.

Mugeni wanjye, ndenda kugera aho ngo mbazamure mu ijuru, nuko rero nimukomeze kwitegura kandi mukomeze gutangaza kuza kwanjye. Kuko natumye benshi muri mwe mu murimo wa Yohana Umubatiza ngo batangaze kuza kwanjye kandi bahamagare abantu kwihana. Natumye benshi muri mwe mu murimo wa Petero ngo muragire kandi mugaburire umukumbi. Natumye bamwe muri mwe nk'ibisonga by'ibanga rikomeye ry'Imana, mu murimo w'Intumwa Pawulo (n'abandi bisonga by'ibanga). Natumye benshi muri mwe mu murimo wa Filipo Umubwirizabutumwa, nuko rero mubwirize ijambo; mugire umwete mu gihe gikwiriye n'ikitari cyo; mwemeze, mucyahe, muhugure mufite kwihangana kose n'inyigisho. Natumye bamwe muri mwe mu murimo w'Intumwa Yohana, wari n'umushumba kandi akita ku mukumbi n'urukundo, ubwuzu, n'impuhwe, ndetse agakiza ibikomere intama zanjye zatewe n'umubi. Ntimubishidikanye, kuko nabatumye mu mirimo yabo; kuko igihe kiri hafi, kuko ngiye gushyingiranwa n'Umugeni wanjye, kuko ndaje kubatwara, bakunzi banjye! Nimurangize isiganwa ryanyu, kuko ndi hafi. Igihe kirashize.

Ndi Yesu w'i Nazareti.

Ndi uzategekera ku ntebe ya Dawidi, kuko ari ko nkomoka.

Ndi gushyira ahagaragara ubu bubi, nubwo Satani ashaka ko buguma ari ibanga.

Ndababurira, nubwo benshi muri mwe muri abanebwe cyane ngo mukore ku magambo yanjye.

Ndaje, kandi nzanye umujinya mwinshi kuri Babuloni y'Ibanga.

Ndi umwera kandi imyenda yanjye yera nk'urubura rushya.

Ndi hano ngo mbabwire ko nta gihe mufite cyo guta.

Ndi guhamagara, cyane cyane mwe, Itorero ryanjye rya Lawodikiya.

Ndenda kugera aho ngo mbazamure mu ijuru.

Ngiye gushyingiranwa n'Umugeni wanjye.

Ndaje kubatwara, bakunzi banjye!

Ndi hafi kandi ndabakunda cyane; muri rwagati mu mutima wanjye.

Ndi ibyiringiro byanyu n'agakiza kanyu, ndetse n'Umwami w'abami n'Umutware w'abatware.

Ndi Yeshua HaMashiach.
