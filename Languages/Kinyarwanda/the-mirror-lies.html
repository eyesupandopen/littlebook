Ubuhamya Bubiri: Isirayeli
Ibisharira Biryohereye
5 Ukuboza 2024

Indorerwamo Irabeshya!

Ndorerwamo, ndorerwamo ku rukuta, mbwira iby'isi aho za ecran (screens) zuzuye zitwara imitima ya bose! Binyuze mu gusubiramo ibinyoma bivugwa kenshi, n'amashusho abeshya adahagarara bityo mu buriganya bukomeye babumba imitima yabo n'imyidagaduro y'imihango kugira ngo intama ziyobejwe n'ibitekerezo byabo! Ndetse n'ubwo bimeze bityo, batwawe n'irari ry'umubiri, n'irari ry'amaso n'ibyiratwa by'ubu bugingo bikongezwa kandi bigashyuhwa mu bumenyi bw'ibinyoma butangwa ku bw'icyitwa uburezi aho ibinyoma byabo bigurishwa mu buhendanyi. Bityo, amatwi araryoharyohwa kandi agahozwa n'amajwi asubirwamo kugira ngo abibone n'abapfapfa bayoborwe kandi bategekwe. Kandi mu gihe runaka basanze binyuze mu ndirimbo z'abana zigenzura ubwenge imyitwarire ya muntu ihinduka nk'uko babishaka kandi akabohwa mu mbaraga zabo!

Nyamara uko niko isi mubamo imeze, Yewe Isirayeli, ndetse n'isi iri mu Mubi rwose, ndetse n'isi yafashwe mpiri kandi igatwarwa rwose n'urusobe rw'ibinyoma bitangwa kugira ngo bategure imbaga y'abantu ku nkuru zabo z'ubwicanyi zitangwa ubu zidasiba kugira ngo barimbure Adamu. Indorerwamo, indorerwamo ziri hose, ariko mu kureba kw'indorerwamo ukuri ntikurimo, kuko ukuri kutabonwa n'amaso ahugiyeho kuko indorerwamo zabo ari inyaburiganya mu kuboha ukuri kw'ibinyoma kwakozwe n'ikoranabuhanga ry'abaguye ryagenewe kugira abantu abagaragu b'ibinyoma byabo kugira ngo Adamu apfe iteka ryose.

Indorerwamo z'umukara, ziri hose kandi zashyizweho kugira ngo zerekanwe aho amaso yose ashobora kureba, kugira ngo indorerwamo zivuge ibinyoma bimwe bihuje n'umugambi mubisha, kugira ngo umuntu wa kamere ashimishwe n'icyaha n'urupfu kandi arokwe n'uburozi bwinshi kandi bukomeye bupfunyitse nk'ubwenge n'iterambere ariko mu by'ukuri biteza urupfu n'irimbuka kuko nta kuri kuri mu mashusho abeshya batanga. Indorerwamo z'umukara, ziri hose kandi zifatwa mu ntoki aho ishusho ku yindi yubaka ubusazi n'ubuyobe bukomeye mu gihugu, aho ukuri kudatandukanywa n'ibinyoma, ibyo abishimira gukiranirwa bose batazibagirwa ahubwo bazapfa iteka mu birimi by'umuriro w'irimbuka ry'iteka ndetse n'igihano babohewe iteka kandi nta hantu na hamwe bashobora kwirukira cyangwa kwihisha aho bashobora kubona ubuhungiro bw'urubanza.

Yewe, bumve baririmba! Tega amatwi wumve isinzi ry'amajwi y'inyoni z'ibishushungwe (mockingbirds) aririmbira hamwe, ahuje n'amashusho abeshya indorerwamo zabo z'umukara zihora zitanga. Baririmba begera cyane indirimbo z'inyoni z'ukuri ariko mu by'ukuri iyabo ni indirimbo y'inyiganano ivuga ibinyoma kugira ngo amajwi yabo ahuze n'ubwiyorobetsi mu buriganya bwabo bityo bashaka guhisha. Kandi nyamara izi nyoni z'ibishushungwe zibeshya nta ndirimbo zazo bwite zigira ahubwo zigana kandi zigasubiramo indirimbo z'inyoni z'ukuri zitaririmbye cyangwa ngo zisobanukirwe amagambo ari mu ndirimbo ziririmba, bityo amashusho yerekanywe ku ndorerwamo z'umukara ari zo za ecran (screens) arahuzwa kugira ngo ibinyoma bisubirwamo kandi imbaga y'abantu igaragare bityo. Ndetse n'imyumvire mibi n'ukuri kubeshya aho isi yose ifatiwe mu binyoma by'abaguye, ndetse n'ukuri kubeshya kwaje ku isi gusa kugira ngo kwiyongere uko nkuraho uwo nshyiraho imipaka yanjye ubwo nkoranyiriza abizerwa muri Kristo mu mutekano w'ijuru mu buryo butunguranye kandi butitezwe ubu.

Nuko rero, umva amagambo ya Yeshua HaMashiach kandi usobanukirwe isi ubamo n'uburiganya bwarekewe gufata isi mu maboko yayo abeshya. Ndetse n'uburiganya butangwa mu rubanza aho abaguye barekewe gutegeka, kandi ibyo ni ukubera ko umuntu wa kamere yifuza kwifatanya na ba Satani mu kwigomeka kwabo ku bw'ibihembo byo gukiranirwa, kugira ngo ashimishwe no gukiranirwa kugeza arimbutse, bityo umuntu wa kamere ubu azatangwa mu maboko y'abanzi be, nyamara nubwo bimeze bityo isinzi ry'abantu rizakizwa binyuze mu muriro w'utunganya. Kandi gutandukanya gukomeye kandi kwuzuye kuzabaho mu gihe cy'amakuba ya Yakobo, ndetse no gutandukanya aho asigaye ya Isirayeli abohorwa mu bubata bwa Esawu kandi akabohorerwa mu bwami bwanjye bw'imyaka igihumbi bugiye gushyirwaho vuba aha ku isi!

Nyamara, umuntu wa kamere ahitamo ukuri kubeshya kw'abaguye aho bashaka guhisha Yahweh (ibyo guhishwa bidashoboka), nyamara uko niko ubuyobe bukomeye banyanyagije ku isi aho abanga Yesu w'i Nazareti bose, ndetse na Yeshua HaMashiach, babohwa na bwo. Nuko rero, umva Amagambo ya Yeshua HaMashiach, kugira ngo usobanukirwe isi ubamo n'ubuyobe bukomeye bwafashe isi mu maboko yayo. Sobanukirwa, Yewe Isirayeli, ko jyewe, Yeshua HaMashiach ari we na Kristo Yesu ndi we uzakuraho ubuhumyi bwawe bw'igice ubwo uzahindura imitima yanyu mu kumvira kwuzuye ijwi ryanjye, rivugiwe ku minwa y'abizerwa babeshwaho no kwizera Umwana wa Yahweh, kuko umukiranutsi azabeshwaho no kwizera!

Icyabayeho ni iki? Icyo, nicyo kizabaho: kandi icyakozwe ni iki? Icyo nicyo kizongera gukorwa: kandi nta kintu gishya kiri munsi y'izuba. Hari ikintu umuntu yavuga ati: Dore iki ni gishya? Cyabayeho kera mu bihe byatubanjirije. Nta rwibutso rw'icyo gihe cya mbere, kandi nta rwibutso rw'igihe cyanyuma kizabaho, ku bazaza nyuma. Ni ukuri, ni ukuri, hazaza igihe abaguye batazibukwa, cyangwa ngo baze mu ntekerezo kandi amabanga yabo abeshya azahagarara iteka ryose ubwo bazajugunywa mu birimi by'umuriro.

Nuko rero, sobanukirwa ibinyoma bya kera byahawe abantu n'abaguye mu isi yariho mbere y'umwuzure ukomeye wa Nowa, ndetse ibyo binyoma bimwe byongeye gupfunyikwa kandi bikoreshwa mu kurimbura abantu nanone. Nuko rero sobanukirwa, abaguye bazana ikoranabuhanga ryabo n'uburiganya bwabo n'ibimenyetso n'ibitangaza bibeshya bakoresheje mu kuroga no kurimbura abantu mu minsi ya Yaredi. Kandi isi izasubira uko yari imeze mu minsi ya Nowa kandi niko amakuba ya Yakobo azaba, ndetse n'igihe ku isi gitegekwa n'abanzi ba Yahweh. Ndetse n'icyo kiyoka kinini cya kera ubu cyabohewe ku isi ku bw'urubanza, kandi ibizira byinshi bizarekurwa mu isi y'abantu; ndetse nk'uko Yahweh yabitangaje ko azazana ibihangange kugira ngo asohoze umujinya we.

Kuko ibi babiyobewe ku bushake, yuko kera ijuru ryabayeho ku bw'ijambo ry'Imana, n'isi ivuye mu mazi kandi ihagaze mu mazi, ibyo byatumye isi ya kera irengerwa n'amazi irarimbuka. Ariko ijuru n'isi by'ubu, byabikiwe umuriro ku bw'iryo Jambo, bitegereje umunsi w'amateka n'irimbuka ry'abatubaha Imana.

Ikibabaje, imbaga nyamwinshi ku isi ziyobagiza ku bushake ko isi ya kera Yahweh yayirengeje amazi mu buryo bukomeye ikarimbuka kubera ba Nefilimi n'ibizira byabo byinshi; ndetse no kubera abaguye biyizeza ko ari imana ubwo bononaga inzira z'abantu ku isi n'amabanga yabo y'ubusa bigishije abantu kugira ngo barimbuke. Nuko rero umva ukuri, kandi usobanukirwe Amagambo yanjye, kandi wumve ijwi rya Yeshua HaMashiach kugira ngo usobanukirwe ukuri kandi kugira ngo utagushwa mu mutego w'ikoranabuhanga ryabo kandi kugira ngo udahindurwa n'ikimenyetso cy'inyamaswa ukaba ikizira cyabikiwe ibirimi by'igihano iteka ryose.

Kuko ikoranabuhanga ryabo ari ibyakozwe mbere, kandi ubu biriho, kandi bizaza byuzuye nanone mu gihe cya nyuma cy'imperuka. Nta gushidikanya, ikoranabuhanga ryabo rizahishurwa byuzuye uko bakomeza gushyiraho gahunda yabo ya nyuma y'inyamaswa, hamwe n'ibimenyetso n'ibitangaza bibeshya byose bihabwa abarimbuka. Kandi nuko ikiyoka kizazamuka kugira ngo gitegeke isi mu buriganya bukomeye binyuze mu mwana w'intama ufite amahembe abiri.

Kandi abaguye baziyerekana nk'ibiremwa byiza bivuye mu zindi si zizenguruka zibeshya mu isanzure ryabo ribeshya ry'isi zizenguruka zibeshya zakoreshejwe mu minsi ya kera. Sobanukirwa ko hejuru y'isi hari ijuru n'isanzure ndetse n'ijuru ry'amajuru aho Yahweh yicaye ku ntebe ye y'ubwami, kandi ni ukuri, ni ukuri Yahweh ategeka byose mu byaremwe bye byose ari we wenyine mu ntangiriro yaremye akurikije inama y'ubushake bwe bwite kandi ku bw'imigambi ye y'iteka.

Nuko rero, yiga kugira ngo usobanukirwe isi yawe uko iri by'ukuri kandi usobanukirwe ko imibumbe ari inyenyeri zizerera zatanzweho ibimenyetso n'ibihe kandi atari izindi si zituwe n'ibindi binyabuzima, nuko rero, yamagana ibinyoma by'abaguye. Aba bita abanyamahanga (aliens) ni abigometse kuri Yahweh uhereye mu ntangiriro kandi ni abanyamahanga mu buryo bw'uko inkomoko yabo ari mu ijuru ku munsi baremwe mu gutunganya gukiranuka na Yahweh ubwe. Nyamara, mu kwigomeka kwabo baguye bava mu gutunganya kwabo kw'umwimerere, kandi bihinduye ubwabo iteka ryose, kandi ntibazacika. Nyamara umuntu wa kamere ahitamo ibi binyoma kugira ngo asohoze irari ry'umubiri we wibora mu gukiranirwa kose n'ubugome, kandi abagenda muri iyi nzira ngari y'irimbuka bose bazagwa mu birimi by'umuriro w'iteka bityo.

Kandi nuko bazaboneka, biyoberanya nk'abitaye ku bibazo by'abantu kandi baziyerekana byuzuye ubwo uwo nshyiraho imipaka azakurwaho uko Itorero ryanjye rikoranyirizwa aho ndi mu bicu. Bazaboneka bavuga ko baje kurinda abantu igitero no gukiza abantu intambara kandi bazaza n'ibinyoma byinshi bipfunyitse neza kandi byumvikana neza. Icyakora, amahembe icumi afite inama imwe kandi azaha inyamaswa imbaraga n'ubutware bwayo! Kandi amahembe icumi ni yo azanga maraya, akamugira umusaka akamwambura ubusa, akarya inyama ze, akamutwikisha umuriro mu ntambara yabo ya gatatu y'isi igiye gukwira isi yose. Kandi nyamara bazaboneka bafite ikoranabuhanga ryabo mu ntoki, ariko bazana gusa ibisubizo bibeshya n'inzira zibeshya z'ibinyoma n'uburiganya bitanga urubanza rw'iteka n'irimbuka ku bifatanya mu kwigomeka kwabo bose.

Nyamara, nuko bazazamuka, ndetse n'inyamaswa yo mu nyanja n'inyamaswa yo mu isi, kandi bazazamuka nk'umwana w'intama ufite amahembe abiri uvuga nk'ikiyoka, ndetse n'Umwana w'Intama w'Ikiyoka Esawu yihitiyemo kugira ngo ategeke isi mu gukiranirwa. Ndetse n'ubutegetsi aho amahanga ategekwa n'igitugu na Ashkenaz binyuze muri Noahide n'inyamaswa yo mu nyanja n'inyamaswa yo mu isi itegeka igihugu n'isi binyuze muri Talmud ya Esawu mu buriganya bukomeye uko atega umutego we kuri Yakobo.

Sobanukirwa ko inyamaswa yo mu nyanja yishyize kure ya Babuloni y'Ibanga kugira ngo yishyire kure y'ibyaha byayo ari byinshi, kandi bazanduza izina rye kandi base nk'abamushinja. Ariko sobanukirwa ko inyamaswa yo mu nyanja n'inyamaswa yo mu isi baturuka mu miryango imwe y'ibizira kandi bakorana intoki mu ntoki, ndetse n'itsinda ry'inyamaswa ebyiri, kugira ngo bakorere kandi bahaze imigambi y'irari ry'ikiyoka. Inyamaswa yo mu nyanja izazamuka mu gihe cyayo kandi izazamuka kugira ngo ibe umurwanyi wabo wasezeranyijwe, ndetse na Mahdi wabo bategereje ubu. Kandi azahisha kamere ye nyakuri n'amateka ye n'ibyaha bye, nubwo imbaraga nk'izo ari iz'ubusa kuko buri cyaha yigeze gukora cyanditswe neza mu ijuru. Nubwo, azashuka abamukurikira ku bw'amasezerano ye y'ubusa.

Kandi uko bazamuka bava mu ivu ry'intambara, bazifatanya ku mugaragaro n'ikiyoka n'abaguye abo basanzwe ari bo, kandi bazihutira kwishyiraho mu bwami bwabo bwa nyuma, ubwo bamaze gushyiraho urufatiro rwabwo, ndetse na mbere y'uko Babuloni y'Ibanga irimburwa binyuze mu ibanga ryo gukiranirwa. Nyamara, bazashaka kubaka ibyamaze kubaho uko abaguye bashaka gushyiraho igihe cyabo gishya cya zahabu, ndetse n'igihe cya zahabu batazigera bagira ahubwo urubanza rukomeye. Nyamara, bazazamuka mu mbaraga nyinshi kandi inyamaswa imwe izatunga urutoki ku yindi kandi bazahamya umwe ku wundi n'undi ku wundi kandi bazavuga ibintu byinshi bikomeye kandi bituka Imana kandi bazazana ibimenyetso n'ibitangaza byinshi bibeshya.

Nyamara, nuko bazazamuka nk'uko byanditswe. Kandi Umwana w'Intama ufite amahembe abiri azakorana intoki mu ntoki n'ikiyoka kandi bazahuza ibintu byose bibe bimwe mu muryango wabo mushya. Bazahuza inzego zabo z'imari, bazahuza za guverinoma zabo, bazahuza ibintu byose by'umuryango wabo mushya hamwe kandi bishyire hamwe nk'umwe muri gahunda yabo nshya. Bazahuza amadini yabo y'amabanga atuka Imana ya kera mu idini rishya rizasa nk'idini rya ba Se basenga za cube zabo z'umukara, nyamara bazahuza amadini yabo mu rindi dini ribeshya ba Se batari bazi ariko za cube zabo zizagumaho. (Nyamara, ntazita ku Mana ya ba se, cyangwa ku byifuzo by'abagore, cyangwa ngo yite ku Mana iyo ari yo yose: kuko aziyishyira hejuru ya byose).

Sobanukirwa icyo bazakora, Yewe Isirayeli, kuko bazarwanya abera bakabanesha, kandi abambaza izina rya Yesu w'i Nazareti ari we Yeshua HaMashiach kugeza ku iherezo bose bazakizwa, kuko ari ko byasezeranijwe kandi byanditswe. Bazashinja ibyaha byabo abambaza izina rya Yesu kandi Baraba azagambanira abera ba Babuloni y'Ibanga kuko benshi muri bo beguriye Amatorero yabo inyamaswa yo mu isi batabizi, itaraza mu gihe cy'iyandikwa ry'ibi. Kandi yigize Umwami wabo igurisha Bibiliya mu izina ryayo, nyamara yiyemerera ko atari umukirisitu, nubwo benshi bizera mu bujiji ko ari we.

Kandi uko isi igenda ita umurongo ikagwa mu ntambara ikomeye, n'ibyorezo, n'inzara, n'akarengane n'urupfu bazashyira amakosa y'ibibazo byabo ku bintu byose bya Kristo ibyo bo ubwabo bateje kandi bazavuga ko ibivejuru byatwaye abakiranutsi bateraniye muri Kristo, nyamara mu by'ukuri nzaba nabateranyirije mu cyumba cy'intebe y'ubwami cya Yahweh ku bw'ubukwe bw'umukwe n'umugeni we kugira ngo bahabwe umurage wabo kandi bahabwe ikuzo iteka ryose. Nyamara ku isi bazavuga ibinyoma byinshi kandi bazahimba ibintu byinshi cyane ku ndorerwamo zabo z'umukara bakoresheje amayeri n'ikoranabuhanga by'abaguye kandi bazerekana ibimenyetso n'ibitangaza byinshi by'ibinyoma ku madirishya yabo rubanda ruzahita rwizera.

Sobanukirwa ko ababa mu mijyi yabo n'abashyirwaho ikimenyetso cy'inyamaswa bazahindurwa iteka ryose kandi bazavanga icyuma n'ibumba ariko ntibazafatana. Abashyirwaho ikimenyetso cyabo bazahindurwa kandi bazaba abagaragu b'imitekerereze yabo ihuriweho n'urusobe rw'ibintu ruzabaho kandi bazaba mu bubata bwo gukora ubushake bw'ikiyoka, icyo bazaba baribohoyeho iteka ryose kuko bazaba barihinduye bava mu ishusho ya Yahweh baremwe mu ishusho yayo. Kandi abakora ibyo bintu bose ntibazigera bababarirwa, kuko igoramye kidashobora kugororwa.

Bityo sobanukirwa ko umuryango wabo mushya uzaba wubakiye rwose ku buriganya n'ibinyoma kandi abaturage bo ku isi bazatandukanywa rwose mu buyobe n'isi nyakuri n'ukuri bazaba babamo. Isi yose izabohwa mu buriganya bukomeye kandi rubanda ruzatangazwa n'inyamaswa ruvuga ruti, ni inde uhwanye n'inyamaswa kandi ni inde ushobora kuyirwanya? Kandi abantu benshi, benshi cyane bazagwa mu muriro baguye mu binyoma byabo ndetse n'abo bantu amazina yabo atigeze yandikwa mu gitabo cy'ubugingo uhereye ku kuremwa kw'isi bazagwa iteka ryose kuko bazaba banze Yahweh rwose binyuze mu kwanga Yesu Kristo ari we Yeshua HaMashiach.

Uku kwanga kuzaba ukwa nyuma kandi nta mbabazi bazagira kuko babaze Amagambo y'agakiza yari kubakiza rwose nk'ikintu kidakwiriye kandi bagafata ukuri kw'Imana nk'ikinyoma, ndetse n'icyaha batazikuramo. Ariko uko ni ko gutandukanya kwa nyuma kw'ibihe kuzaba ku buryo nta kintu kibi kizinjira mu Bwami bwanjye bw'imyaka igihumbi keretse abaza kuri Yeshua HaMashiach ari we Yesu Kristo bihannye mu bihe by'agakiza kuko nta muntu n'umwe ushobora kuza kuri Yahweh mu rindi zina cyangwa ngo habeho iyindi nzira, kuko ndi inzira y'ukuri kandi nzima.

Ibi ni byo bihe ubayemo, Yewe Isirayeli, kandi ntushobora kuba mu mijyi yabo kandi ntushobora kwifatanya n'umuryango wabo kuko kwifatanya na bo bisaba ikimenyetso kandi abashyirwaho ikimenyetso cy'inyamaswa bose ntibazigera bababarirwa. Nyamara, muntu yatojwe binyuze mu burezi bwe bw'ibinyoma buhora buhabwa agaciro, ndetse n'imyitozo igurishwa nk'uburezi kandi rubanda rutoje bityo, kandi bazemera ibinyoma byabo kandi umubare munini w'abantu basanzwe bazifatanya n'abaguye iteka ryose.

Nyamara uko ni ko bizaba, ko abemera ikinyoma ku bushake kandi bakishimira gukiranirwa bose bazabohwa mu gihano cy'iteka ryose kubera kwifatanya n'abaguye, kandi abifatanya n'abaguye bose bazasangira rwose umurage wabo utangwa nta mbabazi kuko batagaragaje imbabazi ahubwo urwango rw'agashinyaguro gusa ku bintu byose bya Yahweh.

Ndi Kristo Yesu ari we Yesu Kristo ari we Yeshua HaMashiach kandi nza nk'intare yo mu muryango wa Yuda kandi nza guca urubanza no kurwana mu gukiranuka.

Ndi uzarimbura ubuyobe bukomeye n'ukuri kw'ibinyoma by'abaguye, kandi mu mudendezo w'ubwiza bw'ukuri no gukiranuka ibyaremwe bizashyikirizwa umudendezo w'ubwiza bw'abana ba Yahweh.

Ndi uhamagara aba Isirayeli, ndetse na Adamu wese ku gakiza kugira ngo biyunge rwose na Yahweh binyuze mu maraso y'umwana w'intama, kuko ari ko ndi.

Ndi Kristo Yesu Umwami wanyu wazamutse mu ijuru kandi wazutse kandi nahinduwe umwuka utanga ubuzima kugira ngo abaza aho ndi bose bafite umutima ufunguye n'amaso akeye bashobore kuza rwose ku gakiza mu Mwami wabo w'iteka!

Ndi Yeshua HaMashiach uwo hamwe na Yahweh NDI UWO NDI WE Ukomeye twohereje Amagambo y'Ubutumwa Bwiza bw'Iteka binyuze ku ntumwa yanjye ikomeye n'umugaragu wanesheje ibintu byose aza rwose ku Mwami we n'Umukiza we w'iteka mu kwicisha bugufi no kwihana kubabaje kandi binyuze ku ntumwa yanjye ikomeye n'umugaragu Amagambo y'Ubutumwa Bwiza bw'Iteka yoherejwe kugira ngo aboherejwe aya Magambo bose bayumvire.

Ku bw'ibyo, sobanukirwa ibihe by'ubugambanyi ubayemo aho isi yose izatwarwa mu busazi n'ubuyobe kandi ikibi ntikizaba kigikumiriwe rwose nyamara umati munini, munini cyane uzabona agakiza k'iteka wambaza izina ry'Umwami wabo Yesu kugeza ku iherezo. Kandi mu buryo nk'ubwo njyewe, Yeshua HaMashiach ari we Mesiya nyakuri wa Yakobo, nzarokora asigaye akomeye ya Isirayeli azatwarwa agahabwa kwinjira mu Bwami bwanjye bw'imyaka igihumbi aho buri sezerano rizaba yego na amena!

Nuko rero, Yewe Isirayeli, yanga ikoranabuhanga ryabo kandi ntukomeze kurogwa n'indorerwamo zabo z'umukara, ndetse n'amadirishya yabo aho abantu ubu baboshywe uko bamara ubuzima bwabo mu busa bava ku idirishya bajya ku rindi uko batwarwa kandi bashimishwa n'icyaha. Ndetse n'ukuri kw'impimbano gusa nk'ukuri ariko mu by'ukuri, ari inkuru y'ikinyoma kandi nta kindi uretse imigani yabohewe hamwe mu buhanga igamije kurimbura muntu.

Ku bw'ibyo, nimubavemo kandi mwitandukanye na bo mu bintu byose. Mwihumanure mu maraso yanjye y'impongano munyaturira ibyaha byanyu byose kandi mwihumanure mu ijambo ryanjye ryera, ndetse n'Amagambo ya Yahweh, kuko ndi Ijambo ryabaye umubiri ribaho kandi ribana namwe kandi mu by'ukuri, muri mwe binyuze mu mwuka wanjye w'isezerano niba mwumviye Amagambo yanjye.

Ndabakunda bene wacu, kandi mu by'ukuri ndabarwanirira kandi nzabakiza rwose niba gusa mudacitse intege mu byago byanyu ahubwo mukaruhukira mu mbaraga zanjye n'ubushobozi bwanjye, kuko nza mu bubasha bwose bwa Yahweh NDI UWO NDI WE Ukomeye wasangiye intebe ye y'ubwami kugeza abanzi banjye bagizwe intebe y'ibirenge byanjye kandi ingoma yanjye ntigira iherezo kandi nzakiza abumvira rwose mu rukundo rw'iteka n'agakiza kandi muzongera kuba ubwoko bwihariye, Yewe Isirayeli, Sela na Amena!
