Ibanga ry'Agatabo Gato

Kuva kuri Yeshua HaMashiach:

Dore, ibanga ry'agatabo gato! Kuko abamarayika barindwi bohereza inkuba zabo, kandi inkuba zavuze amajwi yazo. Ni byo banga ry'agatabo gato, kuko agatabo gato kari gafatanijwe ikimenyetso. Kuko ijwi ryavuye mu ijuru, ribwira Yohana Umubishuzi riti: Fatanisha ikimenyetso ibyo inkuba indwi zavuze, kandi ntubyandike. Dore, wariye agatabo gato, kuko gahishuwe, kandi kari karyoshye nk'ubuki mu matwi yawe igihe wasinziraga, ariko karashaririye mu nda yawe bigukangura mu iyerekwa; kandi dore, wakangutse mu bitotsi byawe! Dore, ibanga ry'agatabo gato rije guhishurwa muri iki gihe, kuko ari ryo banga rya nyuma rihishurwa mu gihe cyanjye cy'ubuntu, ndetse agatabo gato k'ubusharire buryoshye. Kuko amajwi y'inkuba agiye guhishurwa, kandi none ni cyo gihe cyo guhishura, kuko ibanga ry'agatabo gato ari rikuru, nubwo ari agatabo gato. Kuko ako gatabo gato kahishuriwe Yohana Umubishuzi, kandi none nzakaguhishurira, kuko guhishurwa kwako kuri hafi. Umva ijwi ryanjye, mugeni wanjye, kandi untege amatwi, njyewe Kristo Yesu, kuko igihe kigeze. Kuko nta kindi gihe gisigaye.

Ndi umwana w'Imana waje mu mubiri, kandi njyewe, Yeshua HaMashiach, nohereje aya magambo.

Kuva kuri Yahweh, Imana ihoraho:

Ijwi ry'inkuba indwi RIRAVUGA! Mwitegure, yemwe mahanga, kuko urubanza rukomeye rubariho, kandi amagambo y'inkuba indwi arahishuwe. Tega amatwi wumve amagambo y'ibanga ry'Agatabo Gato. Kuko imizingo izakurwaho ibimenyetso, kandi gukuraho ibimenyetso bije ku isi yose, ndetse n'urubanza rukomeye n'amakuba. Kuko ibyaha by'isi ari umunuko mu mazuru yanjye, kandi njyewe, ndetse Yahweh, nzeza isi, kandi nzasenya buri kintu cyose kibi; kandi umwana wanjye, uwo nohereza, azategeka mu gukiranuka ku isi, kandi amahoro menshi azaba ku bakiranutsi narindiye umunsi mubi. Kandi nzaha amahoro menshi abambaza izina rya Yesu Kristo bose, ndetse Yeshua HaMashiach, kuko abambaza izina ry'Uwiteka bose bazakizwa. Kandi mu mahoro n'umutekano nzabayobora, kandi mu gukiranuka nzabambika, kandi mu kwera nyakuri bazampimbaza; kuko nabahaye ubwiza, kandi bazagira ubuzima bushya.

Nuko rero nzanye ubutabera bwanjye n'umujinya wanjye kugira ngo niture abakora ibibi mu nzira zabo zanduye, bityo umuntu wese unyanga agahitamo ibibi, nabe mubi na none, kandi umujinya w'Imana ugume kuri we! Kandi umuntu wese uzaba umukiranutsi n'abareka ibibi bose uko bambaza izina ry'Uwiteka bazagira ingororano ikomeye; bityo nibagire imbuto z'amahoro zo gukiranuka, kandi bazagira umurage ukomeye mu mwana wanjye, Kristo Yesu, ufite ingororano yabo mu ntoki ze. Bityo umuntu wese uzaba mubi, nabe mubi na none. Kandi umuntu wese uhindukirira gukiranuka akambaza izina ry'Uwiteka, nabe umukiranutsi na none, kuko umunsi uzabigaragaza. Dore, ikintu nzakubwira mbere yuko kiba, kuko ubuhanuzi bugiye gusohora, kandi nta gukererwa kuzabaho ukundi. Kuko ibihe by'ubuntu n'imbabazi bigiye kurangira, kandi igihe cy'umujinya mwinshi kigiye kurekurwa ku isi, kandi nta gihe nk'iki cyigeze kubaho ku isi, ndetse no mu minsi ya Nowa, kandi nta gihe nk'icyo kizongera kubaho ukundi. Kuko njyewe, ndetse njyewe, narahiye mu izina ryanjye kandi natangaje ibi bintu. Icyo njyewe, ndetse Yahweh Imana ihoraho, ntangaje ntigishobora guhinduka kandi kizasohora. Kuko mu minsi y'umarayika wa karindwi, ubwo azatangira kuvuza impanda, ndetse ibanga ry'Imana rizaba rirangiye.

Ndi NDI UWONDI, bityo ubabwire ko NDI UWONDI ukomeye yohereje aya magambo, ndetse Yahweh, Imana ikomeye kandi ihoraho!

Ubuhamya n'Ibyabaye, bya William Brooks

Kandi noneho kugira ngo twumve neza igice cy'ubu butumwa aho Yeshua HaMashiach yavuze ati, "Dore, wariye agatabo gato, kuko gahishuwe, kandi kari karyoshye nk'ubuki mu matwi yawe igihe wasinziraga, ariko karashaririye mu nda yawe bigukangura mu iyerekwa; kandi dore, wakangutse mu bitotsi byawe." Mu gihe gishize mu kwezi gushize cyangwa se, sinzi itariki nyayo, ariko nari nasinziriye mu gicuku, maze nkangukira mu iyerekwa mvuye mu bitotsi byanjye (cyangwa yari inzozi zigaragara cyane kandi nakangutse mu ntangiriro). Ijwi ryasomaga amagambo mu bwenge bwanjye no mu mwuka wanjye, kandi nari nzi ko ari ijwi ry'Umwami, kandi yasomaga mu matwi yanjye amagambo ntashoboraga kumva kuko amagambo yisukaga adahagarara. Ariko hamwe n'amagambo haje amahoro menshi yantujeho rwose. Amagambo yari aryoshye cyane mu matwi yanjye, kandi nari nduhutse. Hanyuma mu buryo butunguranye, navuye muri iyo mimerere mfite ikibazo gikomeye cyo kugarura ibyo mu gifu, igifu cyanjye cyari cyizingazinze, maze mbyuka vuba mu gitanda, niga kandi nkorora. Namanutse hasi nywa amazi menshi, byafashijeho gato ariko ntibyatuje igifu cyanjye. Nari maso igihe kirekire nyuma y'ibyo, ariko sinari nzi icyo ibi byose bisobanura.

Kandi ku buhamya, aho Yahweh avuga ati, "Ijwi ry'inkuba indwi RIRAVUGA!" Ku ya 7 Gicurasi 2024, nagize umunsi ugoranye cyane, maze ntaha njya kuryama kare cyane. Nari naryamye nkererewe cyane ijoro ryabanje kuko ntabashaga gusinzira, ibintu bikunze kumbaho. Hari umuyaga mubi urimo imvura nyinshi igihe naryamaga, ariko imirabyo n'inkuba byose byari kure cyane. Naryamye ngo nsinzire ahagana saa moya z'uwo mugoroba, ari byo kare kuri njye, maze nsinzira mu byo natekerezaga ko ari ibitotsi. Sinzi niba narigeze nsinzira by'ukuri, ariko ninjiye mu iyerekwa cyangwa inzozi zigaragara cyane, kandi numvaga cyane umuyaga wari urimo kuba. Amagambo n'amajwi byazaga kuri njye bivuye mu byasaga n'ibyerekezo bitandukanye, kandi byari ahantu hose. Sinibuka ibikubiye mu majwi atandukanye, uretse rimwe. Ryari ijwi ryumvikana cyane, kandi andi majwi yose yaracecetse. Ijwi ryaravuze riti, "Ijwi ry'inkuba indwi RIRAVUGA!" Igihe ayo magambo yavugwaga, nahise nkangurwa n'igikuba cy'inkuba cyumvikana cyane nigeze numva. Nicaye aho mu gitanda cyanjye, ntangaye rwose.

Igihe nabisangizaga umugore wanjye umunsi umwe cyangwa ibiri nyuma yaho, twari ku ibaraza ryacu ry'imbere. Yamfashe ukuboko anyobora iruhande rw'inzu tugana mu gikari. Yambwiye ko we n'umuhungu wacu bari mu cyumba cy'uruganiriro igihe icyo gikuba cy'inkuba cyakubitaga. Umurabyo wari wamuritse kandi utigisa icyumba cy'uruganiriro. Biragaragara ko, yari yakubise mu gace kegereye igikari cyacu, ari gito cyane, kuko tuba mu mujyi. Nyamara nta bwangizi twabonye aho ari ho hose. Nagiye mbona ibikurikira igihe inkuba ikubise isi cyangwa ikintu ku isi mu buryo bukomeye, kandi isiga ubusahuzi ku rwego rumwe cyangwa urundi. Yabonye umurabyo, kandi twembi twumvise igikuba cy'inkuba cyumvikana cyane, nyamara nta bwangizi na bumwe bwabonetse aho ari ho hose.


12 Kamena 2024

Umwami yampishuriye neza icyo uyu murimo ari cyo, kandi wiswe utya. Nakiriye ibikubiye mu Gatabo Gato kari mu ntoki z'Mumarayika ukomeye mu Byahishuwe 10, wambaye igicu kandi umukororombya wari ku mutwe we n'isura ye yari nk'izuba n'ibirenge bye nk'inkingi z'umuriro. Ibi bihagarariye ibihe n'amasezerano bijyanye, byose bihurira mu isura ya Yesu Kristo, umwana w'ikinege wa Yahweh, n'ishusho y'Imana ihoraho.

Umukororombya ni ikimenyetso cyatanzwe mu isezerano rya Nowa Yahweh yagiranye na Nowa nyuma y'umwuzure. Igicu n'inkingi z'umuriro bihagarariye isezerano ry'Imana na Mose. Isura ya Yeshua HaMashiach irabagirana nk'izuba kandi ni isezerano rishya. Ubu butumwa burindwi bukubiyemo urubanza ku baguye n'abandi kuva mbere y'umwuzure wa Nowa kugeza ku mperuka, mu byo bikiri imbere guhera ku itariki y'uyu munsi.
