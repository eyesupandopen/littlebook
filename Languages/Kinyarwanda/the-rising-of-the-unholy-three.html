Umuhamya w'Ikabiri: Isirayeli
Ikibishya n'Ikijyoshya
13 Nyakanga 2024

Kuzamuka kw'Abatatu Babi!

Kandi rero igihe kigeze ku iherezo kandi ukuza kwanjye kuregereje! Ubu ngiye kubabwira uko ibintu bigiye kuba mu minsi iri imbere, nuko rero mutege amatwi mwumve ijwi rya Mesiya wanyu, ari we Yeshua HaMashiach, ndetse n'amagambo y'Umwami w'abami, Mesiya, n'umutambyi mukuru iteka ryose mu buryo bwa Melikisedeki. Wenda ubwibone bw'igihugu cyanyu buzahuma imitima yanyu ntimumbone, yewe Babuloni y'Ibanga: ndetse n'izi Leta Zunze Ubumwe. Kuko mwibwira ko mwicaye nk'umwamikazi kandi nta byago bizabagwira, kandi mukibwira muti, Sindi umupfakazi! Nyamara kurimbuka kwanyu kuzaza mu isaha imwe kandi ntimuzongera kubyuka ukundi cyangwa ngo ubuyobozi bwanyu bwongere guteza ibyago isi ya Yahweh ukundi, kuko umugabane wikubye kabiri w'urubanza watangajwe na Yahweh, kandi icyo Yahweh atangaje ntawe ushobora kugihindura.

Tekereza, niba ubishaka, isi itagira Ubukristo n'amahanga ya gikristo. Ntihazongera kubaho Amatorero kandi Ubukristo buzaba bitemewe n'amategeko ku isi yose kandi igihe cy'Itorero kigeze ku iherezo ndetse n'igihe cyanjye cy'ubuntu cyatangajwe n'intumwa zanjye n'abahanuzi mu kinyejana cya mbere, kandi babibye imbuto aho mushobora kubona ubwugamo muri ibi bihe by'amakuba, ndetse n'Ijambo ryanjye ryera n'amabanga y'Imana! Ni ngombwa gusobanura ibi bintu hakoreshejwe ururimi nyarwo rwakoreshejwe na Yohana Uhishurirwa, kuko yavuze ati: Nuko mpagarara ku musenyi w'inyanja: mbona inyamaswa izamuka iva mu nyanja, ifite imitwe irindwi, n'amahembe icumi, no ku mahembe yayo amakamba icumi, no ku mitwe yayo, izina ryo gutuka Imana. Kandi ukundi: Nuko mbona indi nyamaswa izamuka iva mu isi, kandi yari ifite amahembe abiri nk'ay'umwana w'intama, ivuga nk'ikiyoka. Kandi ukundi: ikiyoka kiyiha imbaraga zacyo, n'intebe yacyo, n'ubutware bukomeye. Kandi uko niko kuzamuka kw'abatatu babi kuzaba, inyamaswa yo mu nyanja, inyamaswa yo mu isi ari nayo muhanuzi w'ibinyoma na Moshiach w'ibinyoma, n'ikiyoka kiyobora bose.

Nta wundi anti-Kristo mukuru ubaho, nubwo imyuka ya anti-Kristo yasohotse mu isi ikayobya benshi, ahubwo inyamaswa ebyiri n'ikiyoka. Imwe yo gutegeka inyanja, cyangwa amahanga y'abapagani, n'indi yo gutegeka ibihugu byera, ikomoka mu muryango wa Dani kandi ikaba ari umwubatsi w'urusengero w'inyamaswa. Inyamaswa yo mu nyanja izategeka amahanga, cyangwa goyim binyuze mu mategeko ya Nowa, kandi yamaze gushyirwaho, kandi yombi arwanya Kristo muri kamere yayo. Antikristo arandwanya, njyewe Yesu Kristo kandi akaba mu cyimbo cya Yesu Kristo. Kuko dore! Ibanga ryo gukiranirwa rigeze kure kandi Satani ategereje kugenda kwanyu, Torero ryanjye, ndetse n'Umugeni wanjye. Azategeka binyuze mu idini rihuriweho, kandi azavuga ko ari Mahdi wabo. Guhuza amadini kuzabahuza, munsi y'ikinyoma cy'uko inzira zose zigana ku mana imwe. Uretse, njyewe Yesu Kristo Mesiya w'ukuri ntazemerwa, kandi nta ruhare mbagiramo kandi kurimbuka kwabo ntigutinda. Bityo guhuza amadini kwabo kuzabageza ku muryango wabo w'inyamaswa aho ikimenyetso kizahatirwa amahanga kugira ngo yinjire mu muryango wabo. Ntimuzafate ikimenyetso, kuko uzagifata azasangira inyanja y'umuriro n'inyamaswa n'umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma. Kandi abazafata ikimenyetso bose bazahindurwa iteka ryose mu miterere yabo y'ingirabuzimafatizo. Ntimwasomye? Kandi aho wabonye icyuma kivanze n'ibumba, bazivanga n'urubyaro rw'abantu: ariko ntibazafatana, nk'uko icyuma kitivanga n'ibumba. Abazafata ikimenyetso bazaba abacakara b'ubutegetsi buzamuka kandi bazaba abacakara b'intekerezo zihuriweho zizabaho biturutse ku kuvanga umubiri n'ikoranabuhanga.

Inyanja igereranya amahanga y'abapagani. Amategeko y'ibinyoma ya Nowa yamaze kuvuga ko Ubukristo ari ugusenga ibishushanyo kandi ateganya gucwa umutwe nk'igihano. Ayo mategeko ariho kandi azashyirwa mu bikorwa nta mbabazi. Kandi abazambaza izina ry'Umwami kugeza ku iherezo bazakizwa, kuko byanditswe ngo: Kandi Imana izahanagura amarira yose ku maso yabo: kandi urupfu ntiruzabaho ukundi, cyangwa umuborogo, cyangwa kurira, cyangwa kubabara ntikuzabaho ukundi: kuko ibya mbere bishize. Kandi uwari wicaye ku ntebe y'ubwami, aravuga ati, Dore, byose ndabihindura bishya. Kandi arambwira ati, andika: kuko aya magambo ari ay'ukuri kandi yo kwizerwa. Kandi uko niko ejo hanyu hazaba, mwa bera banjye bo mu makuba, kuko nzakiza Yakobo n'abazambaza izina ryanjye rikomeye mu rurimi rwose bazambazamo.

Inyamaswa yo mu isi, ari nayo muhanuzi w'ibinyoma izategeka Isirayeli, kandi izategeka Isi iri mu gihugu cy'isezerano, ariko atari ubwoko bw'Imana, atari Isirayeli y'Imana kuko muri Isirayeli y'Imana, ndetse n'abana b'Isumbabyose bashyizweho ikimenyetso cy'Imana Isumbabyose, kandi ikimenyetso cya Yahweh ntigishobora kumenwa. Kandi imbaga nyamwinshi izava mu Makuba ya Yakobo kandi izafata imyanya yayo ikwiye muri Isirayeli y'Imana, kuko Umuyahudi n'Umunyamahanga bazakizwa kandi bazaba umuntu mushya umwe kuko nzabaremamo bundi bushya muri njye mu muzuko w'abakiranutsi.

Bana banjye, Isinagogi ya Satani yateguye ubutegetsi bwayo kandi nta kwicuza bagira, muri inyamaswa z'amaguru abiri kuri bo, kuko bakurikira Talimudi ya Babuloni ituka Imana, igitabo gituruka kuri Satani atari kuri Yahweh, kuko Isirayeli yateye umugongo Yahweh kera ubwo banyangaga, njyewe Yeshua w'i Nazareti, kandi nzakiza ubwoko bwanjye. Aba ni bwa bwoko bw'urumamfu nabwiye ba Se mbabwira nti: Mukomoka kuri so umubisha, kandi irari rya so niryo mushaka gukora: yari umwicanyi uhereye mbere na mbere, kandi ntiyagumye mu kuri, kuko nta kuri kumurimo. Iyo avuga ikinyoma, avuga ibye: kuko ari umunyabinyoma, kandi ni se wacyo. Musobanukirwe Bana banjye ko Dani ari icyana cy'intare kandi Dani nta gice afite mu 144,000. Uru rumamfu rutegereje n'amatsiko umwubatsi warwo w'urusengero, kandi bamaze kumwemera nk'umwami wabo. Inyamaswa yo mu isi niyo izana ikimenyetso cy'inyamaswa. Ndetse n'igikorwa cy'imbuto y'intambara, ubu cyageragejwe kandi ginozwa kugira ngo gitangwe kandi gishyirwe mu bikorwa binyuze mu rupfu ku bazabyanga. Isi igereranya igihugu cy'isezerano.

Bana banjye, mureke kwizera ibinyoma by'abatamvugira, kuko ntabatumye. Abahanuzi banjye b'ukuri bavugira hamwe, kandi ni benshi, kandi ntibavuguruzanya, cyangwa ngo bavuguruze ibyanditswe, kuko ndi Ijambo ryabaye umubiri. Izi nyamaswa ebyiri mbi ntizihabwa imbaraga zazo n'umubyeyi w'itorero wangiritse, ahubwo Ikiyoka ubwacyo, kuko Ikiyoka ari umubisha. Musobanukirwe ko Ubukristo butazagira uruhare na ruto kuko amatorero yose y'ibinyoma acirwa urubanza kandi akarimbuka kuko urubanza rutangirira mu nzu y'Imana, kandi Itorero ryanjye ry'ukuri, ndetse n'Abizerwa banjye bahamagawe kandi batoranyijwe bazajyanwa mu ijuru guhabwa ingororano zabo no gutangira iteka ryabo. Kandi ikiyoka gihawe gutegeka, kandi abanyamahanga bazaboneka, ariko si abanyamahanga ahubwo ni abamarayika baguye kandi Ikiyoka nicyo mutwe wabo. Benshi muri bo baboheshejwe iminyururu kuva mu minsi ya Nowa ariko ubu barekuriwe urubanza rwabo rwa nyuma rudasinzira.

Inyamaswa yo mu nyanja, ndetse n'uwo izina rye rihindurwa mu Cyongereza riva mu Giheburayo: Umurabyo ugwa uva mu ijuru: yiga kugira ngo amenye. Kuko atifuza abagore, cyangwa ngo yubahe imana iyo ari yo yose: kuko aziyishyira hejuru ya byose. Ikiyoka inyamaswa yo mu nyanja, n'inyamaswa yo mu isi bizazamuka biva mu ivu nk'intwari kubera kurimbuka kwa Babuloni y'Ibanga, maraya ukomeye, kandi bose bakorera hamwe. Banga Amerika n'ikintu cyose gifitanye isano n'izina rya Yesu Kristo, ariko bazapfukama uko byagenda kose mu rubanza rwabo, urwo batazigera bikuramo. Bityo, ibyanditswe ntibishobora kumvikana mu miterere y'amahanga y'ubu n'isi ubwayo. Kuko Yahweh arimo ategura kuzana umutingito ukomeye ku isi mu rubanza kandi imipaka izongera gushushanywa. Iyi ni intambara yabo ya nyuma yo kurimbura gahunda ya kera, no kuzana gahunda ya Satani uzategeka abantu binyuze ku nyamaswa yo mu nyanja n'inyamaswa yo mu isi ari nayo muhanuzi w'ibinyoma. Amahembe icumi, ari bo bami icumi bazahabwa ububasha bwo gutegeka isaha imwe, kuko ari ko babihawe, bazaha ububasha bwabo n'ubwumvikane bwabo ubu butatu bw'inyamaswa.

Dore! Ngiye kubaha urutonde rw'ibanze rw'ibizaba kugira ngo mumenye ibihe murimo. Bimwe muri ibi bintu bizaba bikurikirana, kandi bimwe bizabera rimwe, kandi ni ibi bikurikira!

  • Babuloni y'Ibanga irarimbuka uko intambara y'isi yose ikwira ku isi, kandi n'ubu barimo gutegura ingabo zabo kandi barategura igitero gihujwe neza kuri Babuloni y'Ibanga. Ntimwizere abayobozi banyu, cyangwa itangazamakuru, kuko ari urubuga rw'abanyabinyoma bari mu gice cy'icyo gitero.
  • Inyamaswa yo mu nyanja, n'inyamaswa yo mu isi, n'ikiyoka bizazamuka biva mu ivu ry'intambara.
  • Izi nyamaswa ebyiri mbi zizahagarara zirwanya abahamya 2 bazahanura amezi 42, icyo gihe bakazicwa kandi bakazamuka mu ijuru mu minsi itatu n'igice kandi uko niko kuzuka kwabo kuzaba.
  • Uko urusengero rwubakwa, intambara ku bera ikomeza amezi 42 kandi ikimenyetso cy'inyamaswa gishyirwaho, gitangwa kandi gishyirwa mu bikorwa ku gihano cy'urupfu. Hanyuma ihinduka, ubwo ikizira cy'umurimbuzi kizakorwa kandi inyamaswa yo mu isi izashaka kwica abazaza kuri njye bose, ndetse na Yeshua HaMashiach muri iki gihe.
  • Inyamaswa yo mu isi yigira Imana kandi yihinduka ku bantu kugira ngo ibice, kuko igambanira abantu mu buryo bumwe na Antiochus Epiphanes.
  • Kandi ndareba, mbona Umwana w'Intama ahagaze ku musozi Siyoni, ari kumwe n'abantu ibihumbi ijana na mirongo ine n'ane, bafite Izina rya Se ryanditswe mu ruhanga rwabo. (Hahirwa abapfa bapfira mu Mwami, uhereye none, yego, niko Umwuka avuga, kugira ngo baruhuke imiruho yabo, kandi imirimo yabo irabakurikira.)
  • 144,000 banjye bazaha ubwugamo ubwoko bwanjye kandi babajyane ahantu h'umutekano kugira ngo barokoke umuyaga uko igihugu gihagurwa gukiranirwa kose, ndetse n'urumamfu rubi. Kuko iyo ntagabanya iyo minsi nta mubiri wari kurokoka, nuko rero nzaha ubwugamo benshi mu butayu. Ibi bizaba igice kugira ngo asigaye mu bantu akizwe.
  • 144,000 baza ku isi bambaye imyenda nzabadodera by'umwihariko, kandi ntishobora gucika kandi bazaba abanyambaraga n'abakomeye kandi nta kintu cy'umwanzi kizabanesha, bityo imigambi ya Yahweh izasohozwa.

Ndi Yeshua HaMashiach kandi nohereje aya magambo binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye, kandi binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye amagambo yanjye yoherejwe.

Murebe mu kirere Torero ryanjye ryizerwa kuko ndegereje kandi nzabajyana mu nzu ya Data aho nabateguriye umwanya kandi muzategekana nanjye nk'Abami n'Abatambyi, ubwami bwanjye bukaba butagira iherezo, nuko rero mwihumurize aya magambo.
