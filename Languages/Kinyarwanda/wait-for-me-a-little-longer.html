Ubuhamya Bubiri: Itorero
Mu Mirimo ya Yohana Umubatiza
23 Werurwe 2024

Muntegerezeho gato: Sinzatinda

Muntegerezeho gato, bana banjye, kandi sinzatinda. Sinzigera mbareka cyangwa ngo mbatatererane, kuko muri imboni y'ijisho rya Data ndetse n'imboni y'ijisho ryanjye. Muri Umugeni wanjye, kandi ni uwuhe mukwe usiga umugeni we mu rubanza rw'abanyabyaha?

Mumenye ko jyewe, Umwami Yesu Kristo, nzabatura ubwoko bwanjye. Ntimwasomye mu Byanditswe Byera? Data ntiyazamuye Enoki mu ijuru? Data ntiyazamuye Eliya mu ijuru? Data ntiyakuye abana ba Isirayeli muri Egiputa? Ntiyakuye Loti i Sodoma mbere y'uko umuriro ugwa kuri iyo mijyi, ukayirimbura yose? Nkanswe umugeni wanjye nacunguje amaraso yanjye, sinzamukura mu maboko y'urubanza rugiye kugera kuri iyi si? Muzageza ryari kwitirira ubupfu jyewe, Umwami wanyu, na Data, Imana yanyu?

Nabahaye ubugingo buhoraho, kandi hari ingororano ikomeye ibikiwe abizerwa mu ijuru, nuko rero ntimutinye, kuko umurage wanyu ugiye gusohora. Ndi Umwungeri ukiranuka ukiza umukumbi amasega aryana. Ayo masega vuba aha azumva ngo, "Nimumve imbere, mwa nkozi z'ibibi mwe; sinigeze mbamenya, nubwo muvuga muti, Mwami, Mwami!" Ndi Umwungeri wizerwa utaratakaje n'umwe mu bo Data yampaye, keretse umwana wo kurimbuka wangambaniye ku biceri mirongo itatu by'ifeza kandi ibyanditswe byasohorejwe mu gicumuro cye no kwanga kwe. Ni ayahe masomo arimo kuri mwe, bana banjye, keretse y'uko mudashobora gutakarira mu kuboko kwanjye kandi nta n'umwe ushobora kubanyaga?

Ntimutinye urubanza rw'inkozi z'ibibi, mwa bizerwa banjye, kandi mumenye ko umujinya wa So utazabakoraho. Ni uwuhe mubyeyi muri mwe waha n'umwe mu bana be mu maboko y'umwanzi? Nubwo icyo ari icyaha gikunze kubaho mu bantu bo ku isi, nta gicucu cyo guhinduka kiba kuri Se w'imicyo! Nuko rero ntimutinye kandi mugire amahoro. Mureke imirimo yanyu bwite muruhukire muri jye. Mukurikire imirimo yanjye kandi mwishimire kuruhukira muri jye, Umwami Yesu Kristo, witangiye kuba igitambo cy'ibyaha bya bose rimwe rizima.

Yemwe bana b'isi, ibyaha byanyu biri imbere yanjye, kandi mwibikiye umujinya n'urubanza bikomeye! Aho kwibika umurage w'imigisha, mwibikiye umujinya ku wundi kuri uyu munsi ukomeye ugiye kubatungura. Yemwe, amagambo yanyu y'imfabusa mwavuze, muti, "Umwami ari he; ni umugani gusa utigeze ubaho kandi ejo hazaba hameze nk'uyu munsi n'ejo bundi." Urubanza rwanyu ruzabatungura, kandi ibintu bizihuta cyane ku buryo muzarimburirwa mu mibabaro. Amagambo yanyu mabi n'inzira zanyu mbi bizabazanira irimbuka rikomeye mu kanya gato. Ibyo mukunda byose muzabinyagwa, kandi ubutunzi bwanyu bwose buzayoyoka mu maboko yanyu kuko nabihaye mu maboko y'ababanga, kuko mwanyanze mpora ubusa.

Umujinya w'Umwana w'Intama ugiye kubasukwaho, kandi ibise byegereanye cyane ubu, byegereanye ku buryo mwibyitiranya n'umujinya w'Umwana w'Intama. Menya, wa muntu w'umupfu we, ko mu kwihangana kwanjye kwinshi ndikubaburira ku irimbuka ryanyu, ariko muracyakoba mu kutizera. Vuba cyane nzakura uwo mukumirizi hagati yanyu, kandi nzakura Umugeni wanjye hagati yanyu, maze irimbuka ryuzuye rizabatungura. Satani yimuye ingabo ze n'ibikoresho bye abishyira mu myanya, yiteguye kubasukaho uburakari bwe n'umujinya we wose, kandi nabagabije mu maboko ye. Ishyanga ryanyu rikomeye ryaraciweho umurongo kandi rishyirwaho ikimenyetso cyo kurimbuka rwose; iyaba mwumvaga amagambo ngo, Babuloni y'Amayobera. Nyamara haracyari igihe cyo kwihana, ariko benshi muri mwe ntibazabikora mu nzira zanyu z'inangieramutima z'ubuyobe n'ubugome.

Urubanza rwanyu ruzabazaho mu buryo bubiri bukomeye: ruturutse imbere n'inyuma. Indaya ikomeye izatanyagurwa; izarigiswa mu mazi kandi itwikwe n'umuriro itwikirwe n'ibyorezo n'inzara. Yego, wa muntu w'isi we, ni nde uzakuvana mu kuboko kwanjye? Nta n'umwe uje kugukiza. Irimbuka ryawe riri hafi. Ingabo zitukura zinjiye mu gihugu cyanyu imbere y'amaso yanyu, kandi ntimwabyitayeho na gato igihe cyose mwari mufite ibibashimisha kandi mukungahaye ku butunzi bw'igihe gito butigeze buba ubwanyu na mbere hose, kuko isi n'ibiyuzuye ari iby'Uwiteka. Ntimwishyiriyeho kashe y'irimbuka ryanyu gusa, ahubwo mwagurishije abana banyu n'abuzukuru banyu ku irimbuka ryuzuye, kandi muracyakoba. Urubyaro rwanyu rurarimbuka. Ntimuzakoba igihe kirekire kuko inzira zanyu zose mbi kandi zangiritse zizabarengera mu mubabaro, iyicarubozo, n'irimbuka, nubwo imbaga nyamwinshi izatunganywa binyuze mu makuba.

Abazahindukirira bose bakambaza mu izina ryanjye kandi ntibakunde amagara yabo kugeza ku gupfa, nzabagororera iteka ryose! Ntibazongera kubabara ukundi, ariko umubabaro wanyu ntuzigera urangira, mwa babi mwe. Ariko wowe, muntu wa kamere wanga Imana, washoje intambara ku Muremyi wawe ukararikira kumpigomekaho no ku nzira zanjye. Irimbuka ryawe rizatungurana kandi nta gutabarwa. Mwahindutse ishyanga ry'indaya, kandi mbega ukuntu bikwiriye ko amahanga yanyu yitwa indaya mu Byanditswe Byera. Ingabo zizarimbura ibisigaye ku ishyanga ryanyu ryahoze ari rikomeye kandi ry'igihangange, kandi kweza gukomeye kuzaza mu gihugu, n'igihano gikomeye kizabarimbura kubera inzira zanyu mbi.

Nabatwaye byose kubera inzira zanyu z'ubugome n'inangieramutima; kandi nubwo nishyuriye ibyaha byanyu, mwanze icyo nabahaye, muhitamo kubyiyishyurira ubwanyu, kandi muzabyicuza iteka ryose. Igihe umukumirizi azakurwaho, irimbuka ry'ako kanya rizaba inshuti yanyu; kandi mwatambye abana banyu ku iherezo rimwe n'iryanyu, nubwo benshi bazakizwa. Ndetse na bamwe mu bana banyu bazampindukirira, kandi nzabakira mu bwiza, nubwo mwabambujije, kandi muzajya mu kurimbuka muri rya ziba rinini ritegereje Umubi n'abamarayika be.

Bana banjye bato, ntimutinye. Mukomeze guhagarara mwizerwa kuri jye hano muri uyu munota wa nyuma, yego, umunota wanyu wa nyuma muri iyi si igiye gushira. Narabahamagaye, narabatoranyije, narabitoranyirije kubera urukundo munkunda n'ubudahemuka bwanyu, kandi ubutunzi bukomeye bubategereje mu nzu ya Data. Muzajyanwa ahatekanye n'ingororano ikomeye; nubwo bamwe bazahura n'igihombo, intore zanjye zose zizakizwa byuzuye kandi kugeza ku mperuka.

Mumpange amaso, mugirane ubusabane nanjye; ndi hafi yanyu kuruta umwuka wanyu ukurikira. Mumenye, ntore zanjye, ko mbarwanirira; kandi nubwo itotezwa n'amakuba byiyongera, muzakizwa umujinya. Nuko rero, mwitunganirize mu kuva muri iyi si no kwanga inzira zayo mbi. Mukarabe ibyaha byayo mu ntoki zanyu kandi muve mu gukiranirwa kwayo, kuko umuntu wese uri mu buriri bw'indaya azababara. Mwiyuhagire mu maraso yanjye y'impongano kandi mwiyuhagire byuzuye. Dore, nabashyizeho ikimenyetso cya Data, kandi nzabacungura byuzuye mbareme bundi bushya mu ishusho yanjye. Yego, muzaba abantu bashya kandi muri icyaremwe gishya muri jye, kandi vuba aha hazaba ijuru rishya n'isi nshya, kuko Data ari guhindura byose bishya. Nuko rero muhagarare mushikamye kandi mumenye ko urukundo rwanjye ari umuriro ugurumana, kandi muri ubutunzi bukomeye kandi mwifuzwa cyane. Vuba aha muzaba muri kumwe nanjye.

Ndi Umwami wanyu muzima n'umukiza, Yesu Kristo.

Ndi umucamanza ukiranuka upima byose ku munzani.

Ndi umuzi wa Yesayi uzicara ku ntebe y'ubwami ya Dawidi mu Bwami bwanjye bugiye kuza.

Ndi umwana w'Imana waje mu mubiri akabambwa ku musaraba kubera ibyaha byanyu, afata umwanya wanyu mu rubanza.

Ndi umwana w'umuntu wahanuwe kera.

Ndi ugendagenda hagati y'ibitereko by'amatabaza birindwi.

Ndi umucyo w'isi.

Ndi umutsima w'ubugingo.

Ndi uwo mu nda ye hatemba imigezi y'amazi y'ubugingo.

Ndi uwabahinduye bashya akabarema bundi bushya.

Mukomeze gushikama, bizerwa banjye, kuko gucungurwa kwanyu kuri hafi kuruta uko mubitekereza.
