Ubuhamya Bubiri: Itorero
Mu Mirimo y'Intumwa Pawulo
Mata 30, 2024

Imana ni Urukundo!

Bana banjye, nimwumve kamere yanyu nyakuri, kuko iyanyu ari kamere nyayo mwahawe n'Imana muri jye, Kristo Yesu, kuko Imana ari urukundo! Nyamara, urukundo rw'Imana ntirumeze nk'urukundo rw'umuntu nk'uko inzira z'Imana atari inzira z'abantu; ariko mwiga inzira z'Imana muri jye, kuko ndi umwigisha wanyu n'umwungeri wanyu. Kuko Imana yakunze abari mu isi cyane, byatumye itanga Umwana wayo w'ikinege: kugira ngo umwizera wese atarimbuka, ahubwo ahabwe ubugingo buhoraho. Kandi muri icyo gikorwa cy'Imana cyo kuntanga, Yesu Kristo, umwana wayo w'ikinege ku bw'ibyaha by'abantu, urukundo rwayo rugaragarira mu bumwe bwuzuye muri jye. Kuko nkomoka kuri Data kandi nkagira kamere imwe n'umwuka umwe, kuko Imana ari umwuka, kandi abayisenga bakwiriye kuyisengera mu mwuka no mu kuri, kuko jye na Data turi umwe mu mwuka, mu bumwe, no mu ntego.

Mumenye ko urukundo rw'Imana rudasa n'urukundo rw'umuntu, kuko urukundo rw'umuntu akenshi ruba rushingiye ku byo abona, kandi umuntu waguye akunda kugaragaza urukundo rwe mu gutanga no kwakira ibyiza, kandi urukundo rw'umuntu rwirebaho kugira ngo rugirire akamaro urukunda, kuko uko niko urukundo rw'umuntu rumeze. Urukundo rw'Imana kenshi rugaragarira mu gutanga ku buntu kugira ngo undi agirirwe neza, no gukorera mugenzi wawe nta gihembo rimwe na rimwe, no gusura abapfakazi, gukiza abarwayi, no kubaka Umubiri wa Kristo. Nuko rero ndabasaba, bana banjye, kunyigiraho jye Kristo Yesu, kuko umbonye aba abonye Data, kandi nubwo mutarambonesha amaso yanyu, vuba cyane muzambona.

Kuko hahirwa utarabonye ariko akizera, kandi mwamboneye mu mwuka no mu kuri, kandi mwamboneye mu nzira zanjye no mu rukundo nohereza ndetse no mu mirimo abakozi banjye babakorera ku bwanyu. Nuko rero muranzi kandi mwarambonye, kandi iyo mugendera mu mwuka wa Kristo wavukiye imbere muri mwe, muba mugendera mu rukundo by'ukuri, kuko iyo ni yo kamere yanjye nabahaye, kandi niko Data wampaye kuri kamere ye ameze: Nimurebe urukundo ruhebuje Data yadukunze, rwatumye twitwa abana b'Imana: ni cyo gituma ab'isi batatumenya, kuko batamumenye. Kandi nanone mu rwandiko rwa mbere rwa Yohana: Muri ibi ni ho urukundo ruri, si uko twebwe twakunze Imana, ahubwo ni uko yo yadukunze, igatuma Umwana wayo kuba impongano y'ibyaha byacu. Bakundwa, ubwo Imana yadukunze ityo, natwe dukwiriye gukundana.

Bana banjye bato, muri abana b'Imana ihoraho, ari yo Yehova, kuko nk'uko Yehova yateye ubusitani mu burasirazuba muri Edeni, yabibye imbuto z'umwuka w'ubwana muri mwe, kuko ndi umurinzi w'ubusitani witabita ku mitima yanyu. Kuko mwaremwe bundi bushya muri jye, kandi mwaremewe mu rukundo, kandi urukundo rw'Imana ruri muri kamere yanyu. Mumenye ko gucungurwa kwanyu kwuzuye kuri ku muryango ubwo muzamenya nk'uko namwe mwamenywe. Mugeni wanjye, mfite byinshi byo kubasobanurira no kubigisha no kubahishurira byerekeye mwebwe ubwanyu n'impamvu mwejejwe kugira ngo muzisohoze, kuko byari ibanga mu mutima wa Data mbere y'irema, kandi vuba muzabyumva.

Nuko rero ni nde uzabadutandukanya n'urukundo rw'Imana? Mbese ni amakuba, cyangwa ibyago, cyangwa kurenganywa, cyangwa inzara, cyangwa kwambara ubusa, cyangwa ibyago, cyangwa inkota? Oya, muri jye, Kristo Yesu, muri abaneshi kuruta, kandi urukundo rwanjye rurimo kuzuzwa muri mwe, Mugeni wanjye, kuko ibihe byegereje kandi vuba muzabimenya. Urukundo rwa Data ntiruneshwa, kuko anesha byose, kuko Imana ari urukundo, kandi urukundo ni kamere ya Yehova kuko Imana ari urukundo. Rimwe mu mayeri menshi ya Satani akoresha kugira ngo abayobye ni ukugaragaza urukundo nk'urunyantegenke, rutagira umurongo, rwikunda kandi rw'ubusa: Kuko izo ari zo nzira z'umuntu wa kamere.

Mu by'ukuri, Satani ari mu kwihakana, kuko ari we wahinduwe ubusa, kandi urukundo rwa Data ntirushobora kuneshwa, kuko nta wuhwanye na we! Muri jye ni ho urukundo rwe rwatunganirijwe mu mubiri, kuko ku bw'urukundo nkunda Data n'urwo mbakunda, Mugeni wanjye, ni bwo nitanze ho incungu itagira icyaha; kuko mukundwa cyane. Kandi mumenye ko muri imboni y'ijisho rya Yehova, kandi mwe, Mugeni wanjye, muri imboni y'ijisho ryanjye, kandi mu rukundo nabacunguye mu kuboko k'umwanzi mu buryo bwuzuye, nta kibura, mu maraso yanjye y'impongano. Abacunguwe n'Uwiteka nibabivuge, abo yacunguye mu kuboko k'umwanzi!

Bana banjye, ubwo Imana ibakunda cyane, mukwiriye kwemera urukundo rwayo no kwemera amagambo yayo aberekeyeho hamwe n'agakiza gakomeye Data yakoze mu gitambo cyanjye nka Pasika yanyu itunganye. Benshi muri mwe ntimwumva iherezo ry'irema nabaremye muri jye, Kristo Yesu, no mu mwuka naremye muri mwe, kuko uwo mwuka ufite imbaraga, kandi ni urukundo; kuko ari kamere ya Data, kuko Yehova ari urukundo. Dore, nta wufite urukundo ruruta uru, rwo gushyirira inshuti ze ubugingo bwe. Kandi nashyiriyeho inshuti zanjye ubugingo bwanjye, kandi mu kubikora nabagize abavandimwe banjye kandi nabagize abana b'Imana: Nimurebe urukundo Data yaduhaye, rwatumye twitwa abana b'Imana: ni cyo gituma iyi si itabamenya, kuko itamumenye.

Nimwumve Amagambo yanjye nahaye Yohana mu rwandiko rwe rwa mbere, kandi muyumve neza. Yehova yabahaye ubuntu buhebuje imbere y'umurezi n'imbere y'ababaciraho iteka, kandi yagize ibirego byabo iby'ubusa kuri mwe, kuko mwaguzwe amaraso yanjye atagira icyaha, kandi mwagizwe abere ku birego byose mu murimo wanjye w'impongano. Ntimwasomye ibyo nabwiye Abaroma mu bihe bya Yubile byinshi byashize? Ni nde uzarega intore z'Imana? Imana ni yo itsindishiriza: Ni nde ucira ho iteka? Kristo ni we wapfuye, ndetse akaba yarazutse, uri iburyo bw'Imana, udusabira. Ntimwumvise ubuhamya bw'Abaheburayo, aho ijwi ryabo rihamya iby'ubutambyi bwanjye budahinduka? Nicyo gituma abasha gukiza rwose abegera Imana ku bwe, kuko ahoraho iteka ngo abasabire.

Nimwumve aya magambo, kandi mureke acengere mu mizi y'ubugingo bwanyu, kandi mwumve ijwi nahaye itorero ry'i Roma: Kuko uwo utigeze kumenya icyaha, Imana yamuhinduye icyaha ku bwacu, kugira ngo muri we duhinduke gukiranuka kw'Imana. Ese aya magambo arabatangaje cyane? Ese muziyemerera kubyumva, cyangwa muzaziba amatwi yanyu mu migenzo yanyu y'ubusa n'ubuhamya bw'ibinyoma bw'ubusa mukomeza, bwatanzwe n'abahanuzi b'ibinyoma b'ubusa bagamije kubayobya? Ese biragoye cyane kwemera ko mwagizwe gukiranuka kw'Imana muri jye, Kristo Yesu?

Nkeneye ko mutuZa rwose mu ntekerezo zanyu no mu mitima yanyu mukareka ijwi ryanjye rigatuza umwuka wanyu kandi mukumva amagambo yanjye. Nimworoshye imitima yanyu kandi musenye ubwibone bwanyu; kuko mfite icyo nkubwira kizakubatura mu bubata niba wemera amagambo yihariye ngiye kuguha nanone. Kuko ijwi ry'Abaroma ribavugisha uko musoma, nuko musome kandi mwumve; kandi mu kumva, mwemere ukuri kw'igihagararo cyanyu muri jye, Kristo Yesu. Kandi amagambo yanjye ni aya: Nuko rero noneho nta teka rizacirwaho abari muri Kristo Yesu, batagenda bakurikiza kamere, ahubwo bakurikiza umwuka. Kuko itegeko ry'umwuka w'ubugingo, muri Kristo Yesu, ryambatuye ku itegeko ry'icyaha n'urupfu. Ntimureke amaso yanyu anyura ku magambo gusa, ahubwo musome buri jambo buhoro buhoro kandi mwizeye, munyizera, kuko navuze aya magambo kuri mwe, bana banjye; kandi jye, Kristo Yesu, nababohoye gucirwaho iteka kose muri jye. Ntimwumva ko mwagizwe gukiranuka kw'Imana? Nimunyumve kandi mwumve ijwi ryanjye kandi mwite ku magambo yanjye: Ni nde ubaciraho iteka? Kristo ni we wapfuye, ndetse akaba yarazutse, uri iburyo bw'Imana, udusabira.

Bana banjye, sinabihamagariye mu gukiranuka no mu kwera nyakuri kugira ngo mbacireho iteka, kandi mugomba kumva ukuri: Ntimufite uburenganzira bwo kwiciraho iteka, kandi benshi muri mwe murabikora, kandi ubuzima bwanyu bubabazwa n'uku kwiyanga umwanzi yabashyizemo. Nimundeke nce iyi minyururu y'ibinyoma y'umwanzi, kandi mwumve amagambo yanjye kandi muyizere, kuko ari ukuri, kandi yandikiwe mwe kugira ngo mube abigenga by'ukuri muri jye. Kuko nababohoye gucirwaho iteka kose, kandi umwanzi n'umurezi wa bene Data ajugunywe hasi: Noneho agakiza karaje, n'imbaraga, n'ubwami bw'Imana yacu, n'ubutware bwa Kristo wayo: kuko umurezi wa bene Data ajugunywe hasi, wabaregaga imbere y'Imana yacu ku manywa na nijoro.

Mumenye, bana banjye, ko muri abakiranutsi, kandi kwiciraho iteka ni ukwirengagiza amagambo yanjye Data yabahaye; nuko rero ntimube abantu batizera, ahubwo mugirire icyizere kinini amagambo yanjye, kuko ari ukuri, kandi nta buhamya bw'ukuri kuruta ubu bwatanzwe. Mumenye itandukaniro riri hagati yo kwiciraho iteka n'umwuka wanjye ukorera muri mwe kugira ngo ubemeze ibyaha; kuko nabwiye Yohana iki mu rwandiko rwe rwa mbere: Kuko imitima yacu nitudaciraho iteka, Imana iruta imitima yacu, kandi izi byose. Bakundwa, imitima yacu nitudaciraho iteka, tuba dufite gushira amanga imbere y'Imana. Ntimwumva ko umwuka wanjye naremye muri buri wese muri mwe, Mugeni wanjye, mu by'ukuri ubemeza ibyaha byanyu na nyuma y'uko muvuka ubwa kabiri muri jye, Kristo Yesu, Umwami wanyu?

Ntimwasomye ubuhamya bwanjye bwanditswe mu butumwa bwiza bwa Yohana bwerekeye umufasha? Kandi naza, azatsinda ab'isi ku bw'icyaha, no ku bw'gukiranuka, no ku bw'amateka. Ku bw'icyaha, kuko batanyizera. Ku bw'gukiranuka, kuko njya kwa Data, kandi ntimuzongera kumbona. Ku bw'amateka, kuko umutware w'iyi si aciriweho iteka. Bana banjye, umufasha yabemeje ibyaha, kandi mwarampindukiriye, kandi mu kuvuka gushya no mu mbaraga z'umwuka naremye muri mwe, ndetse na wa mwuka w'ubwana, ndetse na Kristo muri mwe ibyiringiro by'ubwiza, nabagize gukiranuka kw'Imana muri jye. Nuko rero, ntimukemezwa ibyaha, ahubwo mwemezwa gukiranuka iyo mukoze icyaha, ndetse n'iyo musubiye mu nzira zanyu za kera, kuko inzoka ikunda kuyobya abantu banjye hanyuma ikazana ibirego imbere ya Data n'imbere yanjye by'imirimo yayo itubaha Imana, kandi ndi umuvugizi wanyu.

Kuko nanone, ubuhamya bwa Yohana mu rwandiko rwe rwa mbere buvuga kuri iyi ngingo: Bana banjye bato, mbandikiye ibi kugira ngo mudakora icyaha. Kandi umuntu nakora icyaha, dufite umuvugizi kuri Data, ni we Yesu Kristo ukiranuka: Kandi ni we mpongano y'ibyaha byacu: kandi si ibyacu gusa, ahubwo n'iby'isi yose. Bana banjye, ndi umuvugizi wanyu, kandi muri abakiranutsi muri jye, kandi muri abakiranutsi iteka ryose, kuko gukiranuka kwavukiye muri kamere yanyu. Nuko rero mugire umutima woroshye kuri jye, kandi nzabereka ibyaha byanyu n'amakosa yanyu, ndetse n'ingeso zanyu mbi n'inzira zanyu mbi za kera rimwe na rimwe mwoshwa gusubiramo. Kuko iyo umutima wawe uguciriyeho iteka, mpindukirira kandi wature ibyaha byawe, kuko ibyanditswe bivuga iki: Ariko nitugenda mu mucyo, nk'uko na we ari mu mucyo, tuba dufite ubusabane twese, kandi amaraso ya Yesu Kristo Umwana we atwezaho ibyaha byose. Nituvuga ko nta cyaha dufite, tuba twishutse, kandi ukuri ntikuba kuri muri twe. Nitwatūra ibyaha byacu, ni uwo kwizerwa kandi akiranukira kutubabarira ibyaha byacu, no kutwezaho gukiranirwa kose.

Bana banjye, nabaciriye urubanza rwo gukiranuka muri jye, kandi Yehova yabagize abere ku birego byose muri jye; kuki mutiyemerera kwizera amagambo yanjye y'ubuntu n'urukundo? Kuko urukundo rw'Imana rugaragarira muri yo ndetse n'urukundo nkunda Mugeni wanjye ndetse n'isi yose, kuko ushaka wese yaza, gusa naze mbere y'uko urugi rufungwa kuko igihe kigeze. Mungarukire buri munsi kandi mwiyeze mu maraso yanjye y'impongano, kuko igitambo cyanjye gitunganye kandi ni icy'igihe cyose: Kuko Abaheburayo bavuga neza ibyanjye: Uwo, ubwo ari kurabagirana kw'ubwiza bwayo, n'ishusho yayo bwite, kandi akomeza byose n'ijambo ry'imbaraga ze, amaze kwiyuhagira ibyaha byacu, yicaye iburyo bw'Ikirenga mu byo hejuru. Bana banjye, mumenye ko umufasha yanatsinze isi ku bw'amateka, kuko umutware w'iyi si yaciriweho iteka, kandi igihano cye aragikwiriye. Ariko kugeza kuri uyu munota ashaka kubamanura mu gucirwaho iteka kwe akwiriye, nuko aza kubaciraho iteka; nuko rero ntimwumve amagambo ye kandi mwamagane ibinyoma bye.

Bana banjye bato, mugendere mu rukundo rw'Imana muri jye, Kristo Yesu umwami wanyu. Mukunde Data kandi munkunde mwitondera amategeko yanjye, kuko Yohana wa Mbere avuga iki? Uvuga ati: Ndamuzi, ntiyitondere amategeko ye, ni umunyabinyoma, kandi ukuri ntikuri muri we. Ariko uwitondera ijambo rye, muri we ni ho urukundo rw'Imana rwatunganirijwe by'ukuri: ibyo ni byo bitumenyesha ko turi muri we. Uvuga ko aguma muri we, akwiriye kugenda nk'uko yagendaga. Ndabategeka, Mugeni wanjye, gukomeza kugenda nk'uko nagendaga no kwitondera amategeko yanjye, kuko nahoraga nkora ibyo Data ashaka, kandi namwe mukwiriye guharanira guhora mukora ibyo nshaka, kuko ibyo nshaka ari byo Yehova ashaka. Mu kwitondera amategeko yanjye muzagomba guhindukirira bene Data, kuko Yohana wa Mbere avuga iki? Iki ni cyo kitumenyesha ko dukunda abana b'Imana, iyo dukunze Imana tukitondera amategeko yayo. Kuko gukunda Imana ari uku, ari uko twitondera amategeko yayo, kandi amategeko yayo ntaruhije. Nimwumve ijwi ryanjye kandi mukurikize amagambo yanjye kugeza ku iherezo ryo kumvira, kandi mukomeze gukunda bene wanyu no gukorera mu rukundo, atari ku bw'inyungu mbi; kuko ingororano yanyu iri mu maboko yanjye, atari mu maboko y'abo mukorera.

Ntihakagire igikorwa ku bw'amahari cyangwa kwifuriza icyubahiro cy'ubusa; ahubwo mu kwicisha bugufi mu ntekerezo umuntu wese yibwire ko mugenzi we amuruta.

Ndi umwigisha wanyu n'umwungeri wanyu.

Nkomoka kuri Data kandi mfite kamere imwe n'umwuka umwe.

Ndi gukiranuka kw'Imana, kandi nabagize gukiranuka kw'Imana.

Ndi uwo utsindishirizwa mu nzira ze.

Ntagira icyaha, kandi nabatsindishirije muri jye, Kristo Yesu.

Ndi uwejejwe iteka ryose, kuko Ubwami bw'Imana ari ubwanjye, kandi nabejesheje umwuka w'ubwana.

Ndi uwatanze igitonyanga cye cya nyuma cy'amaraso ku musaraba kugira ngo mbezeho icyaha cyose, kuko nta cyaha kiri mu mwuka w'ubwana nabahaye.

Ndi umuvugizi wanyu.

Ndi umwunganizi wanyu.

Mube maso, Mugeni wanjye, kuko ibihe byo gusanwa byegereje, kandi kugaragara kwanjye kuri hafi kuruta uko mubitekereza.
