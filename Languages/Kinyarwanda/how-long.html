Kugeza ryari?

Ijambo ry'Uwiteka ryatanzwe na Yesu Kristo.

Kugeza ryari, kugeza ryari, abantu banjye bazirengagiza guhamagara kwanjye? Kugeza ryari bazagundira ibyaha byabo? Kugeza ryari bazabaho mu binezeza by'icyaha? Mwihane. Mwihane. Mwihane! Mungarukire n'imitima yanyu yose. Mugarukire Uwiteka Imana yanyu no mu maboko yanjye arambuye!

Ku Mugeni wanjye: Nabahaye ubugingo, nyamara ubugingo bwanyu buhishe muri njye; kandi ubwo nzaboneka, namwe muzaboneka mu bugingo nabahaye. Mwaherewe ubuntu, nyamara byantwaye ikiguzi kinini kugira ngo mbacungure. Ese muzakoresha ubuntu bwanjye nk'igihe cyo gusinzira? Cyangwa muzakoresha ubuntu bwanjye nk'igihe cyo kwiyeza binyuze mu mbaraga z'amaraso yanjye nabameneye i Kaluvari? Ese muzemera ubutumire bwanjye bwo kubwiriza isi igiye kurimbuka no gukiza benshi muri bene wanyu? Cyangwa muzirengagiza ubutumire bwanjye ahubwo muhitemo ibinezeza by'icyaha mu gihe gito gisigaye?

Kuri bose, ndavuga nti: Mungarukire n'imitima yanyu yose; mwihane n'ubugingo bwanyu bwose! Mushake mu maso hanjye kandi mwemere imbabazi zanjye, kuko umunsi w'Uwiteka wegereje, nk'uko naburiye abantu banjye. Sinzaceceka, kandi ndabashishikariza kubumbura amakanwa yanyu kugira ngo mukize abo mukunda n'abo nzabazanira. Mwibuke urukundo rwanyu rwa mbere, kandi muze aho ndi mbere y'uko impanda ivuga.
