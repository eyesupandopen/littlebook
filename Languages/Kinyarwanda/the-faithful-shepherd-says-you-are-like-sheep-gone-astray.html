Ubuhamya Bwiriwe: Itorero
Mu Mirimo ya Petero
17 Mata 2024

Umwungeri Wizerwa Aravuga Ati: Mumeze nk'Intama Zayobye!

Yemwe bantu banjye, mwese mumeze nk'intama zayobye, kandi buri wese muri mwe yihinduriye mu nzira ze bwite, inzira ntabaremeye, ariko mukurikira inzira z'abahanuzi b'ibinyoma babeshya kandi bakarahira mu izina ryanjye ryera, kandi ntibazabura guhanwa. Ndabihanangiriza cyane, bantu banjye, kandi ndahamagara ijuru n'isi ngo byandike amagambo yanjye: NTIMUGAFATE amagambo y'ubuhanuzi ntanze, jyewe Umwami Yesu Kristo, nk'ikintu kidakwiriye; ahubwo, mugerageze buri jambo ntanga. Nimwanga, ni icyaha kibabazwaho.

Ndababwira mbere y'uko nkomeza, benshi muri mwe bazaziba amatwi yabo kandi bahumirize amaso ku magambo ngiye gutanga, kandi ndabagira inama yo kudatinya. Benshi muri mwe bazagwa mu gitutu cy'inshuti n'imiryango, igitutu cy'amatorero y'ibinyoma n'ibigo by'isi, batekereza ko ishuri rimpagarariye mu gihe ishuri ry'isi ari ubumenyi bw'ikinyoma bwa Satani ubwe, kandi ubumenyi bwe bw'icyiza n'ikibi buzanira urupfu buri gihe, kuko ari ko yateguye amagambo ye ngo abigereho. Ntimube mu banga amagambo yanjye, ahubwo mwumve n'ubugingo bwanyu bwose kandi mutekereze ku byo nzavuga, kuko amagambo yanjye ari ubuzima, kandi azatuma guhamagarwa no gutoranywa kwanyu gukomera.

Ni he mu Jambo ryanjye ryera mubona ko hariho ikintu kitwa Siyonisime ya Gikristo? Ntimwumva ko abayobozi babo ari isinagogi ya Satani? Yego, hari abinjiye muri Isirayeli batari aba Isirayeli kandi bari mu isinagogi ya Satani, kandi barabeshya, bavuga ko ari Abayuda ariko atari bo! Mwebwe, Torero ryanjye, muri Isirayeli y'Imana, kandi ntimuri igisimbura, cyangwa ngo ibi bibe tewolojiya y'igisimbura, ahubwo ni iJambo ryanjye ryera. Nimwirebere ubwanyu! Oya! Sinvuga nabi abanjye, nubwo hari abari muri mwe bishyira hejuru mu bwibone bwabo bazavuga ibyo.

Murebe igisekuru cyanjye, kuko navutse ku babyeyi, kandi amamuko yanjye ni mazima; kandi niba hari umuntu muri mwe ugira ishyaka rya Isirayeli, ndamurusha, kuko nabatanzeho ubuzima bwanjye! Mama n'abavandimwe banjye ni bande? Kuko umuntu wese ukora ibyo Imana ishaka ari we mama n'abavandimwe banjye, ntimwasomye? Ndabatoranyiriza: icyiza n'ikibi, kuko benshi muri mwe mubura ubushishozi nyabwo bw'umwuka kandi mwahumishijwe n'ibinyoma by'ababi. Ntimwasomye ko Imana isohoza inzira zayo rimwe na rimwe binyuze mu maboko y'abanzi bayo, kuko n'abanzi bayo bumvira ijwi ryayo? Yego, Isirayeli yongeye gushyirwaho mu 1948, kandi nkunda benshi mu gihugu kandi nzabatabara; ariko Isirayeli yongeye gushyirwaho n'abantu babi, kandi abayobozi babo bafite imigambi mibi y'ibanga. Si ibyanyu, bana banjye, kugira uruhare muri iki gice cy'imirimo ya Data muri iki gihe, nubwo muzagira uruhare mu gihe kizaza mu Bwami bwanjye; ariko ni inshingano yanjye gukiza Yakobo, si iyanyu!

Mwayobejwe n'amagambo meza n'imvugo nziza kandi mwaguye mu mutego w'uburiganya bukomeye. Mwaroshywe n'imyuka ya Yezebeli, ikorera mu nzira za Balamu. Mumenye ko nzavunagura ububasha bwabo kuri mwe kandi nkingure amaso yanyu n'amatwi yanyu niba mumpindukiriye mwicishije bugufi kandi mwiteguye kuza ku Jambo ryanjye no kunyigiraho mudafite ibitekerezo byanyu byabanje mwize kuri iyi myuka y'ibinyoma ihanura binyuze mu bahanuzi bayo b'ibinyoma. Imyuka ya Yezebeli ivuga ibinyoma bikomeye mu bahanuzi bayo b'ibinyoma, ndetse n'ibimenyetso n'ibitangaza by'ibinyoma, kandi ihanurira inyungu z'umururumba mu mwuka wa Balamu, ibi byinjiye mu matorero yanyu, kandi murya umwanda uva mu maboko yabo.

Mwabeshywe n'isinagogi ya Satani mu masezerano y'ibinyoma na bo, isinagogi ya Satani. Bamwe mu Torero ryanjye ntibizerwa ku magambo yanjye n'amategeko yanjye kuko bizeye ibinyoma byavuzwe mu izina ryanjye, basangira ubuhumyi bwabo, ibyo bikaba bikwiriye. Ntimukemere gusangwa muri bo nza; sinavuze nti, nimuyisohokemo kandi mwitandukanye, kuko Kristo ahuriye he na Beliya? Mwoze icyondo mu maso yanyu mu maraso yanjye y'impongano kandi muyafugure kugira ngo mubone urumuri nyarwo nzabaha, kuko amaso yanyu atwikiriwe n'ibyondo, kandi ntimushobora kuyafungura ntabarimo.

Nk'uko byanditswe, Imana yabahaye umwuka w'ibitotsi, amaso y'uko batabona, n'amatwi y'uko batumva kugeza n'uyu munsi, kandi binyuze mu magambo meza n'imvugo nziza mwifatanyije mu buhumyi bwabo, atari ukuba impumyi ku migambi yanjye kuri Isirayeli gusa ahubwo mwahumishijwe no ku guhamagarwa kwanyu nyako muri njye, Kristo Yesu, Umwami wanyu! Niba muri impumyi, ntimwisanzuye ngo musome kandi mwumve amagambo yanjye, kandi ntimugendera mu mwuka. Ku bw'ibyo, mukurikire amagambo yanjye, bantu banjye; kuko nubwo mwamenye mu buryo bw'umubiri, mu buryo bw'umubiri ntimukimenya. Kuko nahinduwe umwuka utanga ubuzima, wahaye umwuka w'ubuzima Isirayeli y'Imana, ndetse n'umwuka wo kuba abana utuma mutaka muti ABBA, Data!

None Umwami ni uwo Mwuka: kandi aho Umwuka w'Umwami ari, hari umudendezo; ku bw'ibyo, muhagarare mushikamye mu mudendezo Kristo yababohoyemo kandi ntimwongere kubohwa n'umugogo w'uburetwa. Mutegere amatwi yanyu kandi muhindure imitima yanyu ngo mwumve iJambo ryanjye, kandi ntimuryange! Umwuka utanga ubuzima; umubiri nta cyo umaze. Amagambo mbabwira ni umwuka kandi ni ubuzima. Ku bw'ibyo, ntimwishushanye na Isirayeli mu buryo bw'umubiri, kandi musohoke mu binyoma byayo bya politiki, ahubwo mwishushanye n'umwuka wanjye waremwe mu ishusho y'Imana!

Ntimwumva? Ibinyoma byabo byemerewe gutegura inzira y'umwana wo kurimbuka, kugira ngo bose bacirwe urubanza bishimira gukiranirwa, amazina yabo atanditswe mu Gitabo cy'Ubuzima uhereye ku kuremwa kw'isi. Mwumve amagambo yanjye yo gucyaha kandi ntimunangire imitima yanyu, kuko amagambo yanjye azabakiza amakuba agiye kuza kandi azabatandukanya n'abandwanya kandi banyanze mu myaka igera ku 2000 ubu. Kandi nubwo ubuhumyi bwabayeho igice kuri Isirayeli, abasigaye ba Yakobo bazampindukirira kandi bemere gukiranuka kutava mu mategeko ahubwo mu kwizera njye. Mweze ibitekerezo byanyu kandi mwikunkumure amagambo y'ibinyoma y'ibirura byabongoreye mu matwi yanyu mu binyoma, ndetse bivuga ibinyoma mu izina ryanjye, kuko ntabatumye.

Hari ikibi gikomeye hejuru y'igihugu cyanjye, kandi nzaza gutabara. Hari ikibi gikomeye kuri Yakobo kuko bemeye kuyobywa n'abanyabinyoma b'Abedomu. Ntimwizeye inkuru yanjye y'uko isinagogi ya Satani ibaho kandi ari impamo? Mumenye ko bagendagenda hagati yanyu! Ntimwumva ko impumyi idashobora kuyobora impumyi, kandi Isirayeli ihumishijwe igice kugeza ubwo umubare wuzuye w'Abanyamahanga uzinjira? Nzakiza abasigaye bizerwa ba Yakobo, kandi ndashyiraho ikimenyetso ku 144,000. Kandi bazankorera imirimo ikomeye, kandi nzabatuma hirya no hino mu gihe cy'amakuba ya Yakobo, kandi bazakiza benshi, kandi bazakora imirimo myinshi ikomeye, kandi nzabayobora ngo bayobore abandi, kuko nta yindi nzira ijya kwa Data uretse binyuze muri njye, Umwami Yesu Kristo, Mesiya banze.

Ariko mwebwe, yewe Mukristo wo muri iki gihe, mwa bana banjye b'akazuyaze, mutekereza ko muzi byinshi kuko mwasomye inyandiko zabo zazanywe rwihishwa hagati yanyu. Kandi mwizeye inkuru yabo y'ibinyoma, inkuru y'ibinyoma iva mu isinagogi ya Satani y'Abedomu, agatsiko kubaka urusengero, abasenzi nyabo Satani yatumye ngo bigarurire isi. Kandi bayobora ishyanga mwakuramya mu cyimbo cya Data niba mukurikira imigani yabo yahimbwe n'ubwenge, biyita ubwoko bwatoranyijwe bw'Imana kandi bakabemeza kubaha umugisha mu gihe bazabarimbura mu mahirwe ya mbere. Mumenye ko ntatunze igisubizo cya politiki, kandi za guverinoma z'isi zikorera Satani, waziguze ibihembo byo gukiranirwa.

Ntimwasomye kandi ntimwumvise ko ari njye gisubizo cyonyine, kandi ndi Umwami w'umurwanyi watumwe na Yahweh ubwe ngo yigarurire ubwami mu gukiranuka kandi akureho gutegeka n'ubutware bwose bwa Satani? Yego, nzavunagura ubutegetsi bwe, ntazigere yongera gukira, kandi nzakomeretsa umutwe we w'ibinyoma nk'uko byahanuwe, kandi azashya iteka ryose mu birimi by'umuriro by'ubururu bishyushye cyane, kandi azoga mu mazuku yaka muri rya ziba rinini kuri uwo munsi mukuru, kandi ntazigere akira ahubwo azarimbuka kandi ashye iteka. Mumenye ko nta bugome butabera mu butabera bwa Data, ahubwo ni ingororano isanzwe y'ibikorwa bya Satani ubwe n'ibyifuzo bye ku bana ba Data n'irema, bityo Data azamuha ibyo abifuriza mu butabera bwe.

Ntimwasomye? Umwami azabasubiza ati, Ndababwira ukuri yuko, Ubwo mwabikoreye umwe muri bene Data aba boroheje bari hanyuma y'abandi, ari jye mwabikoreye. Kuki mukurikira abayobozi ba politiki b'ibinyoma nk'aho ari njye mukurikira? Nzababwira impamvu kugira ngo mwumve neza: Benshi muri mwe mukurikira ibinyoma muri Bibiliya zanyu z'ifashayobora ntiyanditse, kandi sinatumye abakusanyije inyandiko zitwa iza Siyonisime ya Gikristo. Nta kintu nk'icyo kibaho cya Siyonisime ya Gikristo, kuko ntari umwibone w'Umuyuda. Iyo mba Umuziyonisiti, sinari kuzigera mpindukirira Abanyamahanga, kandi amahanga yanyakiriye igihe gito. Oya, bakoresha Siyonisime ngo bazane umwana wo kurimbuka n'urusengero rwabo bazubaka mu bujiji mu izina rya Data, kandi basuzugura Data kuko banyanga, Yesu Kristo. Suko naje mu banjye gusa bakanyanga, ahubwo benshi muri bo baracyanyanga kugeza n'uyu munsi. Ariko mwebwe, yewe torero rya Lawodikiya, mutekereza ko mushobora kwihutisha isohozwa ry'ubuhanuzi mukurikira ubusazi bwabo; kuko isinagogi ya Satani ishaka isi yabo bwite, isi idafite mwe, bana banjye. Sinabakijije kubavaho?

Mumenye ko Satani yagerageje kunyiba umurage wanjye, kuko isi ari iy'Uwiteka n'ibiyuzuye. Satani ashaka isi idafite Imana, isi yigenga ku Mana, isi idafite njye, ibuye rikuru ry'imfuruka, n'isi aho abakomeye barokoka mu gihe barimbura isi yose uretse urubyaro rwa Satani ubwe, rwo barirwo. Isirayeli yinjiwemo mu myaka ibihumbi ubu n'inyandiko zabo z'i Babuloni (yego, bashyize mu nyandiko imigenzo y'imvugo yahanahanwe) zicira urubanza njye, Umwami Yesu Kristo, ibyo bikaba bidashoboka kugeraho kuko ntazihakanwa. Kandi isi igiye kumva umujinya wanjye n'imbaraga zanjye, kuko nkuruta bose uretse Data, kandi ntibishoboka kumpagarika.

Nategereje mu kwihangana no kwihanganira ibibabaza, nemera ko izina ryanjye ritukwa n'igitambo cyanjye cy'impongano gifatwa nk'ikintu kidakwiriye kugira ngo Umugeni wanjye akizwe, Umugeni nyirizina wari uhishwe mu bihe byashize mu ibanga rikomeye ry'Imana, ibanga nahishuriye byuzuye Intumwa Pawulo. Kandi mushobora gusoma inyandiko ze ngo mwumve, kuko ativugiye ubwe, ahubwo yatangaje ubutumwa bwiza bw'Ibanga rikomeye ry'Imana namuhaye ngo amhishurire. Nta n'umwe uzatwara ibyanjye, kuko nabishyuye amaraso yanjye bwite y'impongano, kandi nzitura inkozi z'ibibi.

Satani ashaka isi idafite mwe, bana banjye, ariko benshi muri mwe muha amafaranga yanyu mu bujiji ngo mushyigikire isinagogi ya Satani n'imigambi yabo mibi mu gihugu cyanjye no ku basigaye b'ishyanga rya Isirayeli, ishyanga nzategekeramo isi ntekeye ku ntebe ya Dawidi mu bwami bwanjye bugiye guhishurwa vuba. Mushyigikira urupfu rwanyu bwite, mutekereza ko muteza imbere ubutumwa bwiza. Kuki mudashobora kumva ko urupfu n'irimbuka n'abayobozi b'abicanyi ATARI abanjye ahubwo ari urubyaro rwa Satani ubwe; yego, se wabo ni Umwanzi, Se w'Ibinyoma, Satani ubwe. Mumenye ko ari umwanzi watsinzwe, kandi azi afite ubwoba ko iminsi ye ari mike, kandi atinya ejo he hazaza kuruta ikindi cyose, kuko azi ko ntakizigera kigabanya igihano cye, kuko Imana ubwayo ibitangaza! Bana banjye, ntimushobora gukurikira Satani ngo munkorere. Kuko nta muntu ushobora gukorera abami babiri: kuko yakwanga umwe, agakunda undi; cyangwa akaguma kuri umwe, agasuzugura undi. Bityo muhitemo uwo muzakorera, kuko ntabwo nigeze ntegeka Itorero ryanjye kugira aho rihurira n'icyitwa Siyonisime ya Gikristo, kandi uwo mutwe si uwanjye, ahubwo ni uwa Satani, kandi awukoresha ngo azane umwana wo kurimbuka. Benshi muri mwe mwatakaje igisobanuro cy'amagambo yanjye, mutekereza ko "anti" bisobanura kurwanya byimazeyo gusa nta kindi. Oya, umuntu w'icyaha yoherejwe mu buhanga mu cyimbo cyanjye ku bantu banyanze, kandi nyamara nzakiza Yakobo binyuze mu makuba akomeye, kandi sinzeza igihugu cyanjye cy'umurage gusa, ahubwo nzeza isi yose ibibi n'ibyaha. Mwumve ko nzakiza Yakobo ku bw'amasezerano Data yahaye ba sekuruza babo, kandi si ku bw'agaciro cyangwa gukiranuka kwabo bwite, kuko nta mubiri ukiranuka imbere yanjye.

Ndabagira inama yo gushyira hasi inyandiko z'abatamvugira, kandi mube abanyabwenge, bana banjye; kuko benshi muri mwe muri abaswa ku buryo mudashobora gutandukanya ibinyoma n'ukuri. Kuko ntimunfata nk'umutwe wanyu ahubwo mufata amagambo avugwa n'abahanuzi b'ibinyoma ba Yezebeli, bakorera mu nzira za Balamu, bakurikira ingororano zo gukiranirwa. Kuko Ijambo ry'Imana ari rizima kandi rifite imbaraga, kandi riratyaye kuruta inkota yose y'amugi abiri, rihinguranya kugeza aho rigabanya ubugingo n'umwuka, n'ingingo n'umusokoro, kandi rimenya ibitekerezo n'imigambi y'umutima. Mugaruke ku Jambo ryanjye ryera ry'Imana, kuko Data aririnda kugeza n'uyu munsi.

Mwumve uburyo bwanjye, bantu banjye, kandi munyigireho. Sinatoranyije ijwi rimwe ngo rihagararire ibyanditswe byanjye, kandi sinatoranyije ijwi rya kera cyane ngo ritegeke amagambo yanjye kandi ryemeze ukuri kwayo. Oya, mu nzira za Data (Data ntaba akeneye umujyanama mu migambi ye) no mu bwenge bwa Data yatoranyije amajwi menshi mu bihe byinshi, ahantu, n'imico byemeranya. Murebe kuri Byzance, bana banjye, kandi ntimushidikanye ku magambo yanjye, kandi mwibike mu nyandiko narinze, kandi mwicishe bugufi ku magambo yanjye. Ntimwategetswe kwiga ngo mwiyerekane ko mwemewe? Kuko abana b'iyi si mu gisekuru cyabo barusha ubwenge abana b'umucyo: Kuko ari abanyabwenge mu bibi n'uburiganya, kandi nta kindi bakora uretse kwiba, kwica, no kurimbura.

Data yarinze ijambo rye ryera ry'Imana, kandi riri imbere yanyu, ariko muraryanga kugira ngo mubone uko muhaza irari ryanyu ry'ubunebwe mukoresheje inyandiko zoroshye gusoma, inyandiko zangiritse ntatume cyangwa ngo nandike. Kuki mutera umugongo aya magambo? Mu bujiji bwanyu bw'ubwana, ntimusobanukirwa ko Satani yigana ndetse n'Ibyanditswe Byera. Abikora kimwe n'uko umutekamutwe utari inyangamugayo yiba akuramo impeta z'umunyururu imwe imwe kugira ngo umunyururu ube mugufi ntawe ubimenye. Ese imbibi ntizimurwa buhoro buhoro? Kuko ni uwuhe mutego w'umutezi utegwa imbere y'inyoni? Yahinduye amagambo, akuramo ibice byose n'imirongo, yongeramo amagambo, kandi ibyo byose yabikoze mu gushaka guhuza amadini yose mu mugambi w'amadini yose kugira ngo agandure bose munsi ya Kristo we w'ikinyoma, ugendagenda hagati yanyu ku mugaragaro, ugiye guhishurwa vuba.

Nzajugunya inyamaswa n'umuhanuzi w'ikinyoma ari bazima mu nyanja yaka umuriro n'amazuku kugira ngo bagere ku murage wabo, umurage Data yateguriye Umwanzi n'abamarayika be. Kuko azabaha isi yabo bwite n'isi ijyanye n'irari ryabo ribi babifuriza, bana banjye. Ese ntimwize cyangwa mwibagiwe ko udutsiko tw'abagnositike twahinduye inzandiko zanjye bakimara kuzibona? Kandi mutekereza ko bidashobora kuba mu bihe byanyu, ubwo ubumenyi bwiyongereye?

Mushakishe ijambo ryanjye ryera, kandi mwizere ko nshoboye gutanga abasemuzi b'inyangamugayo. Mwizere amagambo natanze; mwizere amagambo y'Ijambo ry'Imana. Ntimube abana ngo mutekereze ko inyandiko iyo ari yo yose itunganye; ese sinababwiye kwiga kugira ngo mwiyerekane ko mwemewe? Ariko bantu banjye, iyo mwiga, mureka abanzi b'umusaraba bagasobanura amagambo yanjye kugira ngo muyobywe mu gutanga uruhushya ku isinagogi ya Satani. Mu bujiji mutanga uruhushya rwanyu n'ukwemera kugaragara kuri Satani n'abana be bigaruriye ibihugu byanjye byera, ariko mu gihe gito gusa, kandi amakuba ya Yakobo azabahanagura mu gihugu, kandi abagwaneza bazaragwa isi. Munyizere, Ijambo ryabaye umubiri, kugira ngo mbabwire ukuri; kandi ukuri kuri mu Byanditswe Byanjye Byera, kuko bimvuga.

Ese muzanga amagambo yanjye, bantu banjye? Ese muzahitamo gusebya intumwa natumye? Kuko natumye benshi! Benshi muri mwe bazakomeza mu busazi bwanyu, ntimufate n'igihe cyo kugenzura amagambo y'ubu buhanuzi, kandi muzabikora mwiyangiriza no gutakaza ingororano. Uwitambika nakurwaho n'Umugeni wanjye agatwarwa, bazihuta cyane kwica ikintu cyose n'umuntu wese w'Umukristo. Bazakura Ubukristo ku isi iyo babishobora, kandi bazatoteza banice abera banjye mu buryo bwinshi, kandi abambaza izina ry'Uwiteka bazakizwa.

Mungarukire none, kandi mweze imitima yanyu mu magambo yabo y'ibinyoma. Mwiyikoreje amagambo y'urupfu n'irimbuka mu bujiji bwuzuye bw'amateka yanyu. Ndabashishikariza kwiga amateka y'ibitabo ntananditse mushyira hejuru y'amagambo yanjye. Mwige abo abanditsi bari bo by'ukuri, kuko banditsemo ibinyoma bikomeye mu bitabo byabo, kuko muzabamenyera ku mbuto zabo. Byinshi muri ibi bitabo byagizwe ihame mu mashuri yanyu ya tewolojiya; mubishakishe, kuko ari ukuri! Niba muzagira umwete muri ibi bintu mukangumaho, nanjye nzabayobora mu kuri kose, kandi amaherezo muzasobanukirwa ibanga ry'ubumana murimo. Ariko ndetse n'ibanga rikomeye ryagizwe ubusa mu ntekerezo zanyu n'imitima yanyu n'ibinyoma byabo; iryo banga rikomeye ni umuntu umwe mushya wakuwe mu Bayahudi no mu Banyamahanga kimwe, aho nta rukuta rwo gutandukanya rukiriho, kuko urwo rukuta rwavanyweho muri njye.

Benshi muri mwe mufatira ku murongo w'Isezerano rya Kera wakuwe mu rwego rwawo: mutekereza mu bujiji ko mugomba gushyigikira abasazi b'abajenosideri kugira ngo mbaha umugisha. Nha umugisha abitiriwe izina ryanjye, kandi nubwo ngusha imvura ku bakiranutsi n'abakiranirwa kimwe, imigisha yose iri muri njye, Kristo Yesu Umwami wanyu, Umwami w'abami w'ukuri n'Umwami w'ukuri w'abami. Nuko rero, mungarukire mwihana, kuko mwafashe ibinyoma bya Yezebeli nk'ukuri kw'ivanjiri, kandi mwiyikoreje umujinya mu kubikora; kuko nzaha n'abana banjye ibikwiriye imirimo yabo, nubwo ntazabacira urubanza ku byaha byanyu.

Ndi Umwana w'Intama w'Imana watoranyijwe mbere y'ishingwa ry'Isi.

Ndi Umwami w'umurwanyi watumwe na Yahweh.

Ndi uwo ijuru rigomba kwakira kugeza mu bihe byo gusubizaho ibintu byose.

Ndi uwabatijwe na Yohana Umubatiza ubwo umwuka wamanukaga nk'inuma ku ijwi rya Data ubwe.

Ndi uwaje mu banjye akangwa, umugabo w'imbabare wishyuye ikiguzi gihanitse ku bwanyu mwese.

Ndi mukuru kuri bose uretse Data, Yahweh.

Ndi Yeshua HaMashiach. uzwi kandi nka Yesu Kristo. na ιησου χριστου mu Kigiriki no mu zindi ndimi nyinshi zose zindanga nka Yesu Kristo cyangwa Kristo Yesu, kuko ndi Umwami!

Vuba cyane uzambona imbonankubone, Mugeni wanjye, kandi uzamenya nk'uko umenywe. Nuko rero, ndaguhamagara ngo uze aho ndi; nuko nturwanye amagambo yanjye, kandi wumve ijwi ryanjye, kuko ubugingo n'agakiza bibonwa n'abumvira.
