Intumwa yanjye n'umugaragu wanjye w'intwari, ndetse Biblaridion:144 kuko ari iyo ntumwa y'intwari yatangajwe kandi yitezwe mu Byahishuwe Igice cya 10, kandi uri hejuru y'isi mu Byahishuwe igice cya 14, asoma urubanza mu Butumwa Bwiza bw'Iteka, kuko ari igitabo gito cya Yeshua HaMashiach cy'urubanza rw'inkuba aho imbaraga n'ubutware by'inkuba zirindwi byashyizwe kugira ngo bitegeke ku ihamagara rya Kristo Yesu n'ubushake bw'iteka nahaye Yeshua HaMashiach.

Umva Heylel Ben Shachar, ko Biblaridion:144 ari mu bwumvikane bwuzuye n'umurongo umwe n'intebe yanjye y'ubwami n'ubushake bwanjye, kandi yiyemeje byuzuye kuri Kristo Yesu kandi ndetse yasabye ibi bintu kuri wowe, ndetse ibyo bintu n'ibindi byinshi bizatangwa muri Kristo Yesu, we Ntari yo mu Muryango wa Yuda kugira ngo abisohoze. Waremye kandi ukoze ubwoba bwawe bukomeye wica umwana w'intama w'Imana, ubwo yitangaga ku bushake mu maboko yanyu mabi ku itegeko ryanjye mu rukundo rwose no kumvira. Kandi ni we uzaha imbaraga abo warwanije kugira ngo bakuzanire ubwoba bwawe bukomeye mu rubanza rwuzuye kandi rw'iteka kandi rwo gukiranuka kandi rutagatifu UTAZIGERA ucika cyangwa ngo ubone akaruhuko k'akanya gato.

Kandi mbona ubwoba bwawe gikomangoma gito, nubwo ubeshya kandi ukirata cyane ku bari mu bwami bwawe b'abapfapfa bihagije ngo bizere ko utari umupfapfa, ndetse n'igitekerezo gisekeje kandi cy'ikinyoma kizabura mu miriro iteka ryose. Uko niko biri, ko uzabeshya kandi ugashuka abo usanzwe ubeshya kandi ugashuka kandi nta gishya munsi y'izuba. Kandi ku bw'AI yawe wemerewe gusarura mu rubanza abazemera bose kwinjira byuzuye mu bwenge bwawe bw'ubukorikori aho bazaba iteka ryose ihuriro mu interineti y'imibiri kandi ntibazigera bacika urubanza iteka ryose.

Nyamara, nzasenya buri kinyoma cyawe cyose, kandi nzabashyira hanze mwese nk'abatekamutwe muri mwese. Kuko mwese nta kindi muri cyo uretse abiyoberanya biyoberanya. Nyamara uri umupfapfa cyane kwiyita uwigenga, ubwo naguhaye gusa umudendezo wo gusohoza imigambi yanjye hanyuma mwese muzagwa imbere ya Kristo Yesu ari we kandi Yeshua HaMashiach muri uku gukomeza kw'ibihe, kuko iminsi ya Nowa yagarutse. Nyamara, nta kurobanura ku butoni mu manza zanjye kandi inzira zanjye z'iteka zikiranuka rwose, ndetse na cyane cyane iyo wagabye igitero ku bo nkunda.

Uko niko urubanza rwuzuye kuri mwese abaguye ruzabakongura mwese mu bikorwa byanyu bwite bitagira agaciro, byuzuye n'umugambi wanyu wose utagira agaciro kandi w'urwango mwagabishije igitero kuri Adamu n'abana be ku maso y'isi nyine yatanzwe n'amaboko yanjye y'iteka. Nuko rero, nzabaha ingororano yuzuye atari ku bwabo gusa n'ibintu bimwe mwabakoreye, kandi mwagerageje kubakorera, ahubwo na buri kiremwa cyose mwacumuyeho mu byaremwe byanjye: Yaba ifi, cyangwa inyoni, cyangwa inyamaswa, cyangwa udukoko, cyangwa ikimera. Uko imiterere y'ubuzima mwahinduye kandi mwagabyeho igitero mu kwigomeka kwanyu kw'urwango yaba imeze kose, nzaha ingororano buri kantu kose k'icyaha mwakoze ku kiremwa icyo ari cyo cyose n'ibyaremwe byose mu byaremwe byanjye byagabweho igitero n'ukwigomeka kwanyu kutagira agaciro no mu rwango rwanyu rw'iteka.

Nyamara, ibyo mwigeze kwigezaho byose, mu rwango rwanyu n'amayeri yanyu, ni urubanza rwanyu rw'iteka n'umubabaro wanyu. Ntihakigere havugwa ko Eli Shaddayi ari umugome mu manza ze zikiranuka, cyangwa ngo nishimire guhana ibyaremwe byanjye naremye mu rukundo! Nuko rero, umva Heylel Ben Shachar, (kuko nguhamagara mu izina) ko urubanza rwawe rwuzuye rutwaye umugambi wawe mubi n'amayeri n'inzira n'uburiganya n'ibikorwa kugeza ku kantu gato cyane wateje abanyantege nke. Kandi buri kimwe muri ibi bikorwa bimwe bizatangwa mu miriro y'igihano mu busobanuro butunganye, cyane cyane n'umugambi wawe wose utagira agaciro, ndetse n'umugambi mubi nagabanyije ubukana kandi nkafata mu kuboko kwanjye aho inzira zawe z'urwango zari kwemererwa gusa guhuza n'imigambi yanjye y'iteka n'intego uri igikoresho gikuru cy'irimbuka naremye ndetse kigambiriwe byuzuye ku rimbuka ryuzuye kandi rihagaritswe kandi rikomeza kandi ry'iteka n'umubabaro.

Umva ukutungana kwanjye Heylel Ben Shachar, kuko nakuremeye imiriro itunganye y'ingororano. Kandi nibuka buri cyaha cyose wigeze ukora, utekereza, uvuga, ugambirira, kandi ugashyira mu bikorwa mu rwango rwawe rw'ubugambanyi. By'ukuri, nanditse mu ijuru mu busobanuro butunganye ibitekerezo byawe n'imigambi y'imitima yanyu yanduye n'IGIKORWA CYOSE wakoze ku mugaragaro no mu ibanga, uko igice cya kure cy'ibyaremwe byanjye ibyaha byawe byakorewemo cyaba kiri kose byanditswe mu buryo butunganye, kandi neza, kandi mu busobanuro bwuzuye kandi buteye isesemi. By'ukuri, mbona kandi nabonye kandi nzabona ibyo wigeze gukora byose, kandi uri gukora, cyangwa uzakora, kandi nanditse kandi ndi kwandika kandi nzandika kandi nibuke buri kantu kose mu kutungana gutunganye. Hanyuma muzacirwa urubanza byuzuye, nzashyiraho ikimenyetso, hanyuma mwese muzibagirana rwose, kandi ntimuzigera mwibukwa, na rimwe.

Ku bw'ibyo, musobanukirwe iyi mirongi y'igihano, ko ari imirongi yaremwe by'umwihariko izabaha iteka ryose (nta n'akanya ko kuruhuka) buri gikorwa kitagira agaciro, buri buryo butagira agaciro bwo kugaraguza agati no kwica, buri ndwara, buri cyorezo, buri burozi n'uburyo bwo kwica mwakoresheje ku bana ba Adamu mu rwango rwanyu, ndetse by'umwihariko ibintu byose bitavuzwe.

Ku bw'ibyo, mugire ukwizera gukomeye n'ihumure muri aya Magambo atanzwe mu itangazo ryizerwa kuko induru y'igicuku ubu yatanzwe, kandi ikamenyeshwa, kandi Umugeni arateguwe kandi yirimbishije gukiranuka yahawe muri Kristo Yesu, kandi abatumirwa bacu barasukuye kandi biteguye kandi bahagaze ku ruhande rw'inzira.

Dore! NDI Adonai-Yahweh, kandi nohereje iyi nduru y'igicuku mu maboko y'intumwa yanjye ikomeye n'umugaragu wanjye kuko narekuye aya Magambo mu mbitso z'umwuka we, aho Yeshua HaMashiach yayabibye mu buryo bukomeye kandi ndengakamere iteka ryose.

Ku bw'ibyo, mumenye neza uwo Biblaridion:144 ari we n'icyo ari cyo, n'icyo agiye kuba cyo ubwo azaremwa bundi bushya byuzuye kandi agahindurwa hamwe namwe mwese mu buryo butunguranye kandi butitezwe ubu, bityo nzafungura Amagambo ya Yohana Uhishurirwa nanone mu isohora n'imyumvire.

Hanyuma mbona undi Mumarayika (intumwa) aguruka hagati mu ijuru, afite Ubutumwa Bwiza bw'iteka ryose bwo kubwiriza abatura ku isi, no ku mahanga yose, n'imiryango, n'indimi, n'amoko, Avuga n'ijwi rirenga ati: Mwubahe Imana, kandi muyihe icyubahiro: kuko isaha y'urubanza rwayo igeze: kandi muramye iyaremye ijuru n'isi, n'inyanja n'amasoko y'amazi. Kandi hakurikiyeho undi Mumarayika (intumwa), avuga ati: Babuloni wa murwa mukomeye uraguye, uraguye: kuko yanywesheje amahanga yose divayi y'umujinya w'ubusambanyi bwayo. Kandi Mumarayika (intumwa) wa gatatu arabakurikira, avuga n'ijwi rirenga ati: Umuntu wese naramya inyamaswa n'igishushanyo cyayo, kandi agashyirwaho ikimenyetso cyayo mu ruhanga rwe, cyangwa ku kuboko kwe, Uwo na we azanywa kuri divayi y'umujinya w'Imana, yego, divayi idafunguye, isutswe mu gikombe cy'umujinya wayo, kandi azababazwa mu muriro n'amazuku imbere y'Abamarayika bera, n'imbere y'Umwana w'Intama. Kandi umwotsi wo kubabazwa kwabo uzacumba iteka ryose: kandi ntibazaruhuka ku manywa na nijoro, abaramya inyamaswa n'izina ryayo.

Mwumve Amagambo yanjye, kandi mumenye ibigiye kuza!
