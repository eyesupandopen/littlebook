Imanza Zitangajwe
16 Kamena 2025

Dore! Incamake y'Imanza Zitangajwe kuri Babuloni y'Ibanga Mbere y'uko Urubanza Rugwa Rwose

Imanza zikurikira zaturutse mu Gitabo Gito cy'Ibyahishuwe Igice cya 10, ari na ryo Vanjiri y'Iteka. Urubanza kuri Babuloni y'Ibanga rumaze igihe rutangajwe mu myaka n'ayubile byinshi. Ibikurikira ni Imanza zitangajwe zakuwe mu Gitabo Gito zizasohozwa rwose mu buryo butunguranye kandi butitezwe ubu. Muri uru rwego, nkora nk'intumwa y'ubutabera yoherejwe gutangaza imanza zivuye ku ntebe y'ubwami ya Yahweh, zitangiwe mu maso ya Yesu Kristo, uwo nkorera.

Bimenyekane ko, uyu munsi ndetse na tariki ya 16 Kamena 2025 ku Kalendari ya Gregory (ari na yo kalendari ikoreshwa na Babuloni y'Ibanga) ko Umwami wanjye wazamutse mu ijuru Kristo Yesu, ndetse Yeshua HaMashiach antegeka gusoma izi manza kuri Babuloni y'Ibanga, ndetse na Leta Zunze Ubumwe za Amerika, kugira ngo umuntu wese ashobore kwihana mu gihe hakiri akanya gato k'igihe gishira, kandi kugira ngo urubanza rutangazwe mbere y'uko umuriro ugwa. Selah!

Uku gutangaza ntikurimo byose kuko izi atari imanza nshya ahubwo zasohotse zose kuva tariki ya 2 Werurwe 2024, kugeza tariki ya 20 Werurwe 2025. Nyinshi muri izi zivuye kuri Yeshua HaMashiach, hamwe n'igice kimwe kivuye kuri Yahweh, NDI UWONDI Ukomeye. Ubu ngiye gutangaza.

1Dore! Urubanza Ruraje Kandi Ibihe Ni Ubu!4 Kamena 2024 (Ijwi: Yeshua HaMashiach)

Uko gukoranirahamwe kuba, ubuza akurwaho, kandi kurimbuka gukomeye kuzagwira isi, mu buryo butunguranye kandi mu kanya gato. Kuko uko ubuza akurwaho, nta kizasigara ngo kibuze ibibi mu isi n'irari n'ibyifuzo bibi by'abantu. Intambara ikomeye izaduka ku isi, kandi umuvandimwe azongera ahagurukire kwica umuvandimwe we, kandi kurimbuka kwabo kuzaba gukomeye. Amahanga menshi azagwa kuko Babuloni y'Amayobera, ndetse na maraya ukomeye wicaye ku mazi menshi, azahindurwa umusaka mu isaha imwe. Uwo mudugudu mukuru, ndetse n'iryo shyanga rya Leta Zunze Ubumwe nyinshi, rizarimburwa rwose; kuko yakoreye umugabane wikubye kabiri w'urubanza, kandi arabikwiriye.

Kuko yabayeho mu byishimo mu cyaha gikomeye; yicaye mu bwibone bukomeye yibwira ko adashobora kugwa kandi ko ibintu bizakomeza, ejo nk'uyu munsi, n'ejo hazaza biba uko kandi bidahinduka. Kandi maraya ukomeye yononnye isi n'igikombe cya zahabu kiri mu ntoki ze, cyuzuye ibizira n'imyanda y'ubusambanyi bwe. Kandi bityo urubanza rwe ruzagwa gitumo. Umuriro ukomeye uzagwira imijyi ye uko ibisasu bigwa; umutingito ukomeye uzaba, kandi iryo shyanga rizigabamo kabiri hagati. Inkombe zaryo zizarengerwa n'amazi menshi, kandi ntirizongera kubyuka ukundi, kuko ibyaha byaryo ari bikomeye n'urubanza rwaryo rukaba rukomeye.

2Umva Amagambo Yanjye Kandi Wumve Ibihe Ubamo!23 Gicurasi 2024 (Ijwi: Yeshua HaMashiach)

Umva Amagambo yanjye, bwoko bwanjye, ndetse n'abo mpamagara bose, kandi mwumve ibihe mubamo. Kuko isi muzi ubu izareka kubaho, kandi mu mwanya wayo hazasigara ubutayu bwinshi, ndetse n'ivu n'amatongo bya Babuloni y'Amayobera, kuko intambara ije. Kuko uko nzamura Umugeni wanjye mu ijuru, amakuba akomeye azagwira Babuloni y'Amayobera, kandi azarimburwa mu isaha imwe, kandi mu isaha imwe azarimburwa, kandi nta gishobora guhindura iherezo rye, kuko Yahweh yabivuze. Kuri wowe, Babuloni y'Ibanga, wanze ijwi ryanjye kandi unshyira ku ruhande, njyewe Yesu Kristo Ukiranuka, kubera ibyishimo by'icyaha. Kuko n'imiburo ikomeye yatanzwe ku ishyanga ryawe mu ijuru yabaye gusa iyo gutanga imyidagaduro ku mitima yanyu itagira kwizera n'amaso yanyu yuzuye irari, kandi kuri mwe wari undi munsi usanzwe uko mwabayeho mu byishimo mu byaha byanyu bibaganisha ku kurimbuka kwanyu. Mwagombye kuba mwarihannye! Kuko mwabaye ishyanga ry'abasenga ibishushanyo, ishyanga ry'abashoza intambara, ishyanga ryigaburira ibinyoma kandi rikunda ibishuko byaryo, ishyanga ryujuje isi ibizira byaryo; kandi amahanga anywa ku gikombe cyanyu cya zahabu cyuzuye ibizira n'imyanda y'ubusambanyi bwanyu.

Kandi bityo iryo herezo rizagera rwose ubwo nzakoranyiriza abakiranutsi b'Itorero ryanjye aho ndi mu bicu kandi ubwo kugenda kwabo kuzatigisa isi yose. Bizatigisa isi by'ukuri, kandi umutingito ukomeye uzagwira Babuloni y'Ibanga, kandi izatanyagurika hagati uko abera banjye bagenda. Izatwikwa rwose n'umuriro uva mu kirere uko ibisasu bizagwa. Imyenda yayo izatwikirwa n'amazi aturutse iburasirazuba n'iburengerazuba! Kuko marayika ukomeye azafata ibuye rimeze nk'urusyo rukomeye, arijugunye mu nyanja, ati, Uku ni ko uwo mudugudu mukuru Babuloni uzajugunywa hasi n'imbaraga nyinshi, kandi ntuzongera kuboneka ukundi. Kuko ari ko byanditswe kandi ari ko bizakorwa! Kuko urubanza rwayo ari rukomeye, kandi urubanza rwatangiriye mu nzu y'Imana. Amatorero yayo yaguye azarimburwa, kuko naciriye urubanza ayo yose atari ayanjye, ndetse n'amatorero yose y'ibinyoma avuga ibinyoma bikomeye mu buryarya. Babuloni y'Amayobera, iyaba wari warampindukiye, Yesu Kristo, ukava mu nzira zawe mbi, kuko nari kugukiza, kandi yaba njyewe cyangwa Yahweh nta n'umwe wishimira imanza zigiye kurekurwa.

Yewe, Babuloni y'Ibanga, ibiraro byawe bizagwa; imihanda yawe izacurika, icike, kandi ntizabasha kugendwa; imiyoboro yawe y'ibikoresho yamaze gutangira gusenyuka kandi vuba izaba umusaka. Nta biribwa bizakugezwaho, kuko nta muntu uzaza kugutabara, kandi uzaba wenyine mu isaha yawe y'umusaka, yewe, maraya ukomeye. Ntabwo uzagira ibyishimo, kandi abazarokoka bazisanga barwana gusa kugira ngo babone amazi yo kunywa. Inzara izagwira igihugu. Kandi icyorezo gikomeye kizakugwira, ndetse n'inyamaswa zizagutera, kuko ibihe byageze.

Ingabo zitukura guverinoma yawe y'abagambanyi yemereye kwinjira mu mipaka yawe zizica buri mugabo, umugore, n'umwana wese udakwiranye n'imigambi yabo. Kuko bazica kandi bagire abacakara abo bazabona bose. Ni ingabo z'inyamaswa zakoranyijwe zivuye mu mfuruka enye z'isi ku bw'iyi mpamvu, kandi inyamaswa yihishe n'umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma wihishe barazikoranyije.

Nta mpuhwe bagira, nta mbabazi, nta buntu, nta kwicuza, kandi banga rwose abaturage ba Babuloni y'Amayobera kubera ibyaha biteye ubwoba abayobozi banyu bakoreye mu bihugu byabo. Kandi icyo gihe cyose benshi muri mwe mwishimiraga intambara zanyu zidakiranuka uko mwarebaga mwishimye kuri za televiziyo zanyu, uko mwishimiraga cyane ibyaha by'abayobozi banyu. Mwahaye uruhushya abayobozi banyu abo mwashyigikiye ibyaha byabo binyuze mu matora, bityo muzasangira mu rubanza rwabo, kuko mwagize abakwiriye umugabane wikubye kabiri w'urubanza, kandi ruzabagwira gitumo. Kandi bityo Umugeni wanjye azagaragara ku ntebe yanjye y'urubanza, kandi bamwe bazahura n'igihombo, bamwe bazatwara ingororano ikomeye, kandi bose bazahabwa byuzuye avansi bamaze guhabwa: Kuko nzabaha imibiri mishya, itagira icyaha itazigera ibora!

3Ku Itorero Ryanjye, Umubiri Wanjye, Ndetse n'Umugeni Wanjye: Umva Amagambo y'Umwami Wawe!13 Gicurasi 2024 (Ijwi: Yeshua HaMashiach)

Bazagera ku rubyiniro rw'isi mu mbaraga zikomeye, kandi ihuriro ry'abami icumi rizategeka. Ndetse n'ubu, ihuriro ryamaze gutegura ingabo zaryo mu ibanga ku bw'igitero cy'ibanga kuri Babuloni y'Ibanga. Bamaze kwishyira mu buryo bw'ibanga mu kwitegura, kandi rwose bahuje ibikorwa by'igitero, kandi bateye ku mugaragaro Babuloni y'Ibanga hakurikijwe urubanza rwatangajwe n'Imana rugiye kuyigwira, urwo itazigera yongera kubyuka. Kuko gukiranirwa kwayo kwageze imbere y'Isumbabyose, n'ibyaha byayo imbere y'intebe y'ubwami ikomeye y'ijuru, ndetse imbere y'Imana ikomeye y'iteka, kuko nta yindi ikomeye kuruta; kandi yaciriwe urubanza.

Nzanye umujinya ukomeye kuri Babuloni y'Ibanga. Igihe ni igikundiro mutagifite. Ntimushobora gukomeza kuba abanebwe, cyangwa muzisanga mu makuba ya Yakobo, ubwo nari kubategurira umwanya ku meza yanjye nkabashyingira nk'umugeni wanjye, ariko muri mu maboko y'undi mu buhemu, ndetse na Satani! Nuko rero muze aho ndi kugira ngo nshobore kubasubiza ingororano yanyu. Ntibihagije gusohoka mu burakari; mugomba kwihana ubu bugome mugahindura inzira zanyu kandi mukantura ibi byaha, nanjye nzaboza mu maraso yanjye y'impongano mpanagure ibizinga kuri mwe mu kubonera kwanjye gutunganye, kuko ndi umwera n'imyenda yanjye ikaba yera nk'urubura rushya. Mungarukire kandi muzabana nanjye iteka; nimusohoke muze mu maboko yanjye arambuye, kuko ndi hano ngo mbabwire ko nta gihe mufite cyo guta.

5Abakiranirwa Ntibazaragwa Ubwami bw'Imana! (Inkuba ya 5 n'iya 6)19 Mata 2024 (Ijwi: Yeshua HaMashiach, Umutwe wikubye kabiri mu Butumwa)

Reka mfungure amaso yanyu, mwe mwirengagiza kubishaka kandi mwe mufite ubuhumyi, mwe mwicaye mu mwanda no mu mwijima, mwivuruguta mu byondo. Ntimushobora gutanga uruhushya rwanyu ku kibi ngo mwibwire ko muri kunkorera, kuko narwanyije ikibi n'ibishuko kugeza igitonyanga cya nyuma cy'amaraso kimenetse kiva kuri njye kijya ku butaka butukura, kuko ndi Adamu wa nyuma kandi nahinduwe umwuka utanga ubugingo. Benshi muri mwe mwahumwe amaso n'amagambo meza n'imvugo nziza kandi mutanga uruhushya rwanyu rwose ku bagizi ba nabi n'abantu babi bahiga abana. Basambanya abana, bica urubozo abana ku bw'irari ryabo ribi: Kuko ari ko umujinya w'Imana uza kuri Babuloni y'Amayobera, ariko benshi muri mwe mukomeza gutanga uruhushya rwanyu kuri izi nyamaswa z'inkazi zashyiriweho umuriro w'iteka w'ikuzimu utazigera uzima. Nimuyisohokemo, bwoko bwanjye, cyangwa muzasangira mu byago byayo n'urubanza ruteye ubwoba ruyijeho; kandi nababuriye. Kuko Kristo ahuriye he na Beliyari? Nimusangira mu binyoma byayo, muzasangira mu byago byayo; nuko rero mwitandukanye rwose mu magambo no mu bikorwa, kandi ntimwemeranye na bo na gato. Ntimukabeho ubuzima bw'uburangare, bwiyandarika; kuko nzahana ababikora bose. Mwibwira ko kubera ko mufite ubugingo buhoraho mudashobora guhura n'igihombo gikomeye n'ingaruka? Nzambura ingororano zanyu nzitange, kandi muzaba abato mu bwami bwo mu ijuru nimukomeza, kuko ari ko Yeshua HaMashiach, Umukwe, abwira Umugeni wanjye!

6Mulinzi, Kanguka!Mata 5, 2024 (Ijwi: Yeshua HaMashiach)

Ndaje, kandi sinzaceceka, n'umujinya wanjye uragurumana kuri Babuloni y'Ibanga, yego, Babuloni y'Ibanga igurisha abana bato nk'ibicuruzwa mu maboko y'abantu babi, izahagarikwa. Ibyiza byari kuba ko batigeze bavuka kuruta kuvuka bagakora ibyaha by'ubugome n'ubugizi bwa nabi ku bana. Ibyiza byari kuba ko bahambirwa urusyo mu ijosi, bakajugunywa mu nyanja; ariko babikiwe umuriro utazima, kandi ubutabera buzaba vuba, kuko umujinya w'Umwana w'Intama ugiye gutwikira isi yose n'amakuba ya Yakobo akazaduka igihe ubuza azaba akuweho. Ariko wowe, yewe Babuloni y'Ibanga ikomeye, wanduje umuheto wa Data w'amabara menshi yahaye Nowa n'abantu bose nk'isezerano ry'uko atazongera kurimbura isi yose n'amazi ukundi, kandi azakomeza ijambo rye. Ariko wowe, yewe Babuloni y'Amayobera, uzarimburwa mu isaha imwe.

Umuriro ukomeye uzakuza; umutingito ukomeye uzagushyira hasi; iminara yawe izagwa; ibikorwaremezo byawe bizahinduka amatongo, n'imiraba ikomeye izarengera inkombe zawe, kandi nzakwitura hakurikijwe imirimo yawe, n'urubanza rukomeye ruri kuri wowe. Kuko wigeze kuba urumuri rw'ibyiringiro n'urumuri rw'isi mu gihe gito, ariko wahisemo umururumba, ubwikunde, na ruswa nk'inshuti zawe magara, yewe maraya ukomeye! Ninde uzagukiza njyewe n'umujinya wanjye n'uburakari bwanjye bugiye kukugeraho bugurumana? Uhagaze uboheshejwe iminyururu, utegereje urubanza rwawe rwa nyuma, ruzagaragara kugira ngo isi yose irubone, n'ubwoba bukomeye buzagera kuri benshi.

Dore! Amahanga menshi Data yayahiritse mu rubanza, kuko ashyiraho umwe agahanura undi, kuko Yahweh ari we Mutegetsi w'Ikirenga kandi ntawuruta Imana ikomeye ihoraho ihagararira kandi igakiza abayo mu muriro wabikiwe ababi. Dore! Amahanga yari agize ubwami bukomeye aracyariho, nubwo yagabanutse, ariko yakomeje kubaho. Ariko wowe, yewe maraya ukomeye, wigize indaya ku bakunzi benshi kubera umururumba mubi n'ibinezeza by'icyaha. Wayobeje abantu banjye bakava kuri njye ku isi yose kubera umwanda wahaye amahanga ngo arye kandi anywe, kandi uzajugunywa hasi rwose, kandi ntuzongera kubaho ukundi. Kandi mu ivu ryawe nta shyanga rikomeye rizabyuka, nyamara abasigaye bazakizwa. Ukuboko kw'umwanzi kuri mu majosi yanyu, kandi nzanye ibishingwe mu mipaka yanyu. Bazasahura, banyage, kandi batware ibisigaye.

Ninde uzagutabara? Kuko wihakanye umuturanyi wawe, kandi nta bwoba bw'Uwiteka buri mu mitima yanyu. Wavuze uti, Sindi umupfakazi, nyamara uri umupfakazi kandi uri impumyi n'igipfamatwi imbere yanjye. Nta gakiza kuri wowe kubera ubusambanyi bwawe wakoze n'isi; niyo mpamvu, nzakurimbura rwose. Ibyenda kuba bizaba byihuse cyane; hanyuma umwuzure ukomeye uzatwikira imyenda yawe. Umuriro uzakongora rwose kandi uzeza inkozi z'ibibi mu gihugu, n'imbohe zanjye zizabohurwa, kandi nta gishobora guhagarika ibigiye kuba. Ndi Umwami Yesu Kristo, kandi ndabibabwira bitaraba, kandi ndatangaza ibigiye kuba nk'aho byamaze kuba, kuko aya magambo ari ay'ukuri, kandi nta gishobora kuyahindura, kuko ubuhanuzi buzuzuzwa. Mukomere, bantu banjye, kuko nzatwara umugeni wanjye; kuko mufitiye imigambi n'intego mu bwami bwanjye bugiye kuza.

7Ese Sinakugize Umukiranutsi?Mata 1, 2024 (Ijwi: Yeshua HaMashiach)

Abatazitandukanya na we bazasangira urubanza rwe, none se kuki wahitamo urupfu aho guhitamo ubugingo naguhaye, bwatangiwe amaraso yanjye atagira icyaha? Intambara igiye kuza rwose mu gihugu cyanyu n'ishyanga ryanyu, kandi nubwo yamaze gutangira — yego, intambara itatangajwe barwana namwe — vuba izaduka ku isi yose uko ibimenyetso bifungurwa. Intambara ikomeye n'itotezwa rikomeye bizaza ku isi kuko bazagerageza kurimbura ikintu cyose cy'Imana na Kristo wayo kuko amaherezo bazabatangazaho intambara ntagatifu ku mugaragaro. Bazafata benshi, kandi benshi bazatwarwa, n'abazakizwa bazaba bafite amahitamo amwe gusa: kwihangana kugeza ku iherezo no kudakunda amagara yabo kugeza ku iherezo no kwambaza izina ryanjye kugeza ku iherezo.

Abayobozi banyu bwite barabagambaniye kandi bazatanga benshi ho igitambo ku mubi mu ntambara zigiye kurota vuba. Ishyanga ryanyu rizagabwaho igitero riturutse imbere n'inyuma. Ingabo zitukura zamaze kugera hano, kandi zitegereje amabwiriza yazo; kuko zitegura igitero gitunguranye, kandi benshi bazatwarwa. Ingabo z'umubi zizatwara byose mu gihugu zibyigira, no kubabanga. Bazasahura ubutaka bwanyu, ingo zanyu, urubyaro rwanyu; n'ubuzima bwinshi buzabura mu kutizera. Intambara iraturuka hanze kuko amahanga menshi yishyize hamwe ngo abarwanye; kandi bafite uruhushya rw'abayobozi banyu, n'abayobozi banyu bakorera urupfu rwanyu. Bashaka isi yabo bwite, isi yeguriwe Satani aho bashobora gukurikira byimazeyo ibibi bibabamo, kandi nta kizabuza irari ryabo ribi, kuko ubuza azaba yagiye.

Bazatwika rwose Babuloni y'Ibanga n'umuriro, n'uzarokoka inkongi y'umuriro azicwa n'inzara; uzarokoka inzara azicwa n'icyorezo; uzarokoka inkota azamanikwa ku giti kubera izina ryanjye, kandi benshi bazacibwa imitwe nk'uko Ijambo ryanjye Ryera ribivuga. Kandi abazicwa bazira izina ryanjye bazabona ingororano ikomeye, kandi nzahanagura amarira yose ku maso yabo. Mumenye, bana banjye, ko ibyiza byose n'ibinezeza by'icyaha abantu babi bibikira bizayoyoka mu kirere, n'amakuba n'umubabaro bizafata ibibanza byabyo. Umwijima uzatwikira igihugu, kandi hazabaho inzara y'Ijambo ryanjye, n'ijwi ry'umugeni n'ijwi ry'umukwe ntibizongera kumvikana muri we ukundi.

8Reka Ikimenyetso cy'Inyamaswa!Nyakanga 27, 2024 (Ijwi: Yeshua HaMashiach)

Umva ijwi ry'ubwenge kandi upfukame wumve ijwi rizima rya Yeshua HaMashiach, kuko ndi Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda, kandi ndi Umwami w'Abami n'Umutware w'Abatware! El Shaddai, ndetse n'Imana Ishoborabyose, yaranshyize hejuru cyane impa izina riruta amazina yose. Kugira ngo mu Izina rya Yesu amavi yose apfukame, ay'ibyo mu ijuru, n'ibyo mu isi, n'ibyo munsi y'isi: Kandi indimi zose ziature, ko Yesu Kristo ari Umwami, ngo Imana Data w'Imicyo ihabwe icyubahiro, ndetse Yahweh, Imana ihoraho kandi y'iteka, kuko nta wuhwanye na Data!

Umva ijwi ry'Umwami wawe kandi wumve ibintu byamaze gutangira n'ibintu bigiye kuba vuba, kuko dore ndi uvuga ibizaba nk'aho byamaze kuba kuko rwose Umwami wanyu ukomeye, ndetse nanjye Kristo Yesu usumba byose nta cyo nzakora, keretse mbishishuriye abagaragu banjye b'Abahanuzi. Kuki mwumvira amajwi menshi avuguruzanya, kandi kuki muzuyaza hagati y'ibitekerezo bibiri? Ese sinavuze ko abahanuzi banjye bavugira hamwe kandi batazigera bavuguruza Amagambo yanjye yera? Kuko ndi Ijambo ryabaye umubiri ribana namwe binyuze mu mwuka wanjye wera wavukiye muri mwe iteka ryose, kuko buri bugingo bwose bwongeye kuvuka bushushanyijweho uwo mwuka wera w'isezerano ari ryo mbuto yanjye itabora n'ikimenyetso cy'Imana Nzima kidashobora kumenwa. Kandi umunsi wo gucungurwa kw'ibyaraguzwe uregereje n'ugucungurwa kwanyu kwuzuye kuri hano.

Kuko ubwo bazavuga bati amahoro n'umutekano, ni bwo kurimbuka kuzabatungura kandi ntibazarokoka.

Kandi benshi muri Babuloni y'Ibanga, ndetse n'abizera ibinyoma bye bagakurikira amagambo ye meza n'imvugo ze nziza batwawe kandi bagushijwe mu mutego w'amahoro y'ikinyoma aho bibwira ko ubuzima bwabo buryoshye kandi bw'ibyaha buzakomeza aho uyu munsi umeze nk'ejo hashize n'ejo hazaza hakazaba nka none kandi benshi bazavuza induru, borere, kandi baboroge ko ibyiringiro byabo ubu bibakuweho, kandi bazarwana na bene wabo kugeza bapfuye kandi icyo gihe cyose igitero cyabo gitunguranye kizaba cyarekuwe byuzuye, ndetse n'igihe uwo wanjye ubuza azaba akuwe mu isi kandi Babuloni y'Ibanga izagwa ku gice gikubye kabiri cy'urubanza mu isaha imwe mu gihe amakuba menshi azaba agwa.
