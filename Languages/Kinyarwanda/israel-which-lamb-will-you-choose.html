Ubuhamya Bubiri: Isirayeli
Ibisharira N'ibiryohereye
13 Ugushyingo 2024

Yewe Isirayeli! Ni uwuhe Mwana w'Intama uzahitamo?

Umva Amagambo ya Yehova NDI UWONDI Ukomeye kandi wirukire ku ijwi ry'Amagambo yanjye kugira ngo uhabwe ubwenge kandi n'umunyabwenge muri mwe arurusheho kuba umunyabwenge.

Dore! Nje kubabwira ibizaba kugira ngo mumenye gukomera kw'Amagambo yanjye kandi kugira ngo mugere ku gakiza kanyu kuzuye muri Yeshua HaMashiach, ari we koko Mesiya wanyu w'ukuri, Umwami, Umutegetsi, n'Umutambyi Mukuru iteka ryose mu buryo bwa Melikisedeki.

Nuko rero, ndategeka ko mumwumva n'umutima wanyu wose kandi mugakeba imitima yanyu mu kumwumvira! Kuko, ni Yeshua HaMashiach, ari we Yesu Kristo mu rurimi rw'Icyongereza watangiwe atari ukugira ngo akize Isirayeli gusa, ahubwo n'agakiza k'urubyaro rwose rwa Adamu kuko abazambaza izina ry'Umwami bose kugeza ku iherezo bazakizwa baba Abisirayeli cyangwa ab'amahanga, kandi Yeshua HaMashiach azarokora abasigaye ba Isirayeli ndetse no mu rubyaro rwa Adamu bazakomeza mu bwami bw'imyaka igihumbi.

Kandi nuko bimeze, ko natanze ibyo urubyaro rwa Adamu rutashoboraga kwitangira ku bw'agakiza karwo kuko urubyaro rwa Adamu rwashoboraga gusa kubona incungu yuzuye mu maraso y'umwana w'intama. Kuko ari jye, Yehova, NDI UWONDI Ukomeye watanze Pasika yanyu itunganye rimwe gusa, ubu, n'iteka ryose kandi ibihe byose igitambo cye cyaremewe, nuko rero, nta kindi gitambo gikenewe, Yewe Isirayeli.

Nk'uko byanditswe mu Baheburayo, nanjye ndongera kubabwira nti: Kuko niba amaraso y'amapfizi n'ay'ihene, n'ivu ry'ishashi rishanyagurirwa ku bahumanye, byeza kugira ngo umubiri uhumanuke: Amaraso ya Kristo, we wituye Imana atagira inenge binyuze mu Mwuka w'iteka, azarushaho gukura imirimo ipfuye mu mitima yanyu, kugira ngo mukorere Imana ihoraho? Nuko ndabagira inama, Yewe Isirayeli, mwemere igitambo cyanjye kuko nta kindi gitambo cy'ibyaha gisigaye!

Nuko rero wumve Isirayeli, ko bidashoboka ko umuntu uwo ari we wese yegera Imana Ihoraho yo Gukiranuka binyuze kuri Mose ukundi, kuko, nagize Yeshua HaMashiach inzira, ukuri, n'ubugingo ku buryo nta muntu numwe ushobora kuza kuri Yehova keretse binyuze kuri Yeshua HaMashiach!

Kandi nategetse ko mwumvira ijwi rye, ndetse no binyuze muri Tora aho ijwi rya Mose ubwe ritaka kugeza n'uyu munsi nk'umutangabuhamya ubashinja kandi mu nzira zanyu z'ubujiji n'izitagira ukwizera aho kumvira ahubwo mushaka mu bujiji kongera kubaka urusengero rwubatswe n'abantu, kuko mwayobejwe na Esawu! Kuko mwanze urusengero rwubatswe n'umuremyi wanyu, ndetse urusengero rutubatswe n'intoki, ndetse urusengero rwubatswe na Yehova ubwe ku bw'Isirayeli y'Imana urwo Yesu Kristo ari ibuye rikuru ry'imfuruka. Nuko rero, mureke imirimo yanyu bwite kandi muruhukire mu mirimo ya Mesiya wanyu w'ukuri nanjye nzabaha Isabato.

Nuko ndabategeka Yewe Isirayeli kandi ni jye watanze Amagambo yavuzwe na Mose ku ba Data banyu kuri Sinayi, ayo Magambo ndategeka ko muyumvira iteka n'iteka ryose ndetse no mu kutumvira kwanyu kw'inangira n'umutima unangiye. Nuko Mose aravuga ku bw'ukumvira kwa bose ati: Uwiteka Imana yanyu izabahagurukiriza Umuhanuzi umeze nkanjye, muri mwe, ndetse no mu bavandimwe banyu: ni we muzumvira!

Naho Mose yari umuhuza w'Isezerano rya mbere (ryanditswe n'urutoki rwanjye kuri Sinayi) ryatanzwe kugira ngo ritsindishe icyaha mu mubiri, iryo Sezerano ubu rirabagirana mu maso ha Yesu Kristo, ari we Yeshua HaMashiach Umwami wanyu w'ukuri na Mesiya! Kandi nyamara Amategeko ya Mose ni igicucu cy'ibizaza ibyo bikaba byarasohoreye muri Kristo Yesu kandi namugize umwuka utanga ubugingo kandi mu mubiri Yesu w'i Nazareti ntakimenywa ukundi!

Kandi nk'uko byakozwe n'ukuboko kwanjye gukomeye, ndetse urutoki rwa YHVH NDI UWONDI ukomeye, nanditse amategeko yanjye ku bisate by'amabuye kuri Sinayi aho nahaye Mose amategeko yanjye kugira ngo Isirayeli ibeho imbere yanjye. Nyamara, icyahawe Isirayeli kuri Sinayi cyari ahubwo ugucirwaho iteka kw'icyaha mu mubiri kugira ngo Isirayeli ibone umurezi uyigeza kuri Mesiya wayo w'ukuri, ari we Yeshua HaMashiach uwo natumye ku bw'agakiza kanyu kuzuye.

Kandi nuko bimeze, amategeko ya Mose ni meza, kandi aratunganye, kandi ni matagatifu kandi niba hari umuntu wavuma amategeko ya Mose nacirwe urubanza na yo mu bukana bwose buteganijwe munsi y'umuvumo n'igihano cy'amategeko ya Mose. Nyamara icyiza n'igitunganye n'icyera cyazanye urupfu ku baciriweho iteka na cyo n'icyaha mu mubiri, kuko aho nta mategeko ari icyaha ntikibarwaho, nuko rero amategeko ya Mose yatanzwe kugira ngo ahishure kandi atsindishe icyaha mu mubiri.

Nyamara ni ku bw'umwuka w'amategeko atari inyuguti abantu babona imbabazi mu rukundo rwanjye kuri bo, kuko inyuguti y'amategeko izana ubukana (Ntimwasomye ko nshaka imbabazi kuruta ibitambo?) nuko mu rukundo rwanjye ku bana ba Isirayeli abo nteganya gukiza niba mwumviye Yeshua HaMashiach, nategetse igitambo kuko hatabayeho kumena amaraso nta kubabarirwa ibyaha kubaho. Nuko ku bw'ukuboko kurinda kwa Mose ubwoko bwanjye bwararinzwe kugeza kuri Kristo, nubwo benshi muri mwe mwahindukiye imana z'ibinyoma kandi mugakunda cubes zanyu z'umukara, ibyo bintu bikaba biteye ishozi rwose mu maso yanjye, Yewe Isirayeli, nuko rero muveho nonaha!

Nuko wumve uburakari bwanjye kuri wowe Yewe Isirayeli, kuko NDI YHVH, NDI UWONDI ukomeye ndetse ni we Mana Ihoraho Ishoborabyose yo Gukiranuka itananiwe kandi nta kintu na kimwe kinaniye ukuboko kwanjye gukomeye gukora. Kandi ni jye, El Shaddai, Imana Ishoborabyose yaremye ibyaremwe mu bwenge bwanjye bw'iteka kandi ku bw'imbaraga zikomeye z'umwuka wanjye ibintu byose bifatanyirijwe hamwe mu kumvira ijwi ryanjye.

NDI Elohim wanyu, uwo ibihumbi 10,000 inshuro 10,000 bikikije intebe yanjye y'ubwami, ndetse n'ingabo zikomeye z'Abamarayika z'imyuka ikorera izo ntumwa zikaba ari ibirimi by'umuriro birusha abandi ubwenge kandi byaremwe mu buryo buhebuje kandi butunganye nk'uko nabaremye mu rukundo rwanjye kuri bo! Kandi mu rukundo rwabo kuri jye, bumvira byimazeyo ubushake bwanjye bw'iteka kandi nahaye Yeshua HaMashiach gutegeka bose.

Nyamara, jye YHVH NDI UWONDI Ukomeye, ndetse ukikijwe n'ingabo z'Abamarayika zitabarika NTA MUJYANAMA nkeneye mu bwenge bwanjye bw'iteka kandi mu byo naremye ndi umutegetsi w'ikirenga kandi nkora ibintu BYOSE nk'uko inama y'ubushake bwanjye bwite iri, kandi mu banyabwenge bakomeye b'iteka ryose nta n'umwe urusha ubwenge NDI UWONDI ukomeye! Nuko rero umve ijwi ryanjye kandi ucecekeshe indimi zanyu niba muhisemo kwemera ubugingo buhoraho kuruta ibirimi by'umuriro bitazima by'igihano Yewe Isirayeli kuko uko ari ko guhitamo buri muntu wese agomba gukora.

Kandi mu bwenge bwanjye bw'iteka natumye Yesu w'i Nazareti muri Isirayeli, ndetse n'igihe Yudaya yategekwaga na Ponsiyo Pilato, ndetse n'igihe abatambyi bakuru n'Urukiko rw'Abayahudi bamuregaga ibinyoma kandi ku bw'amaboko mabi umwana w'intama w'Imana yishwe kandi abambwa ku musaraba kugira ngo umubiri w'icyaha uboha Adamu urimburwe muri bo bose babaho mu kumvira binyuze mu kwizera, kuko umukiranutsi azabeshwaho no kwizera! Kandi NDI we, ndetse El Shaddai (Imana Ishoborabyose) washyize ibintu byose mu ijuru no mu isi mu maboko akomeye ya Kristo Yesu, ari we Yeshua HaMashiach kandi ni we Ntaramaboko yo mu Muryango wa Yuda uwo NOHEREZA gucira urubanza no kurwana intambara no ku bw'agakiza k'abizerwa n'abumvira. Nuko rero, umve Amagambo yanjye mu kumvira Mesiya wanyu w'ukuri, kuko nta wundi uhari!

Nuko wumve uburakari bwanjye kuri wowe Yewe Isirayeli ko mu buhumyi bwawe bw'igice warazwe na ba So bafite imitima inangiye, abo ijwi ryabo rya Talimudi mwubaha kuruta Elohim wanyu. Yego, mufata imigenzo y'abiyita abanyabwenge banyu n'abahanga mu gaciro gakomeye kuruta Amagambo ya NDI UWONDI Ukomeye, ari we muremyi w'iteka kandi uhoraho w'ibintu byose byaremwe! Kuko muri YHVH ari ho ibintu byose byaremewe, ibiri mu ijuru, n'ibiri mu isi, ibiboneka n'ibitaboneka: byaba intebe z'ubwami cyangwa ubutegetsi, cyangwa ubukuru, cyangwa imbaraga, ibintu byose byaremwe nanjye, kandi ku bwanjye, Kandi NDI mbere y'ibintu byose, kandi muri YHVH ibintu byose bibeshwaho.

Nyamara, mutekereza ko muri abajyanama banjye kandi mu bujiji mwiyita ubwoko bwanjye bwatoranyijwe mu gihe mwanga Amagambo yanjye mu kutumvira kutagira ukwizera? Yewe Isirayeli mwahindukiye ibigirwamana bitavuga by'umutima n'amagambo y'ibinyoma ya Esawu uwo inama ze muhitamo kuruta Imana Ihoraho yo Gukiranuka, ndetse Elohim wanyu! Kandi mudandabirana mu buhumyi bw'igice kuko muri Mose imitima yanyu itwikiriwe. Nyamara, igihe umutima wanyu uzahindukirira Mesiya wanyu w'ukuri, igitambaro kizakurwaho kandi aho kuba impumyi z'igice muzabona!

Uri inde, Yewe Isirayeli, ku buryo mwakwigira abajyanama ba Yehova? Nyamara aho kumva Amagambo ya Yehova yatanzwe mu maso ha Yeshua HaMashiach, mwizeye ibinyoma byahererekanyijwe mu migenzo y'abakuru banyu bari mu bubata bwa Esawu, abo binyuze mu nyandiko zabo mwiyeretse Esawu mu buryo bumwe kandi kuri Esawu muraboshwe! Kandi nanditse nweruye kugira ngo Mose ahe ba So kuri Sinayi: Ikintu cyose mbategetse, mwitondere kugikora: ntimuzacyongeraho, cyangwa ngo mugabanyeho. Kandi nanone nabitegetse kwandikwa mu bwenge bwa Salomo, ubwo bwenge buhagarara iteka: Buri jambo ry'Imana riraboneye: ni ingabo ikingira abamwiringira. Ntukongere ku magambo ye, kugira ngo atakugaya, ukaboneka ko uri umunyabinyoma.

Yewe Isirayeli, iyaba mwunamiraga imitima yanyu mu kumvira Amagambo yatanzwe ku bw'agakiza kanyu n'ibyishimo by'iteka, iyaba mwongeraga kumvira Amagambo y'umuremyi wanyu! Kandi NDI we wazabagarura kandi akabazana mu mukumbi w'iteka uri munsi y'ukuboko gukomeye k'Umushumba wanjye Mukuru uwo nahaye ibintu byose! Ndetse Yeshua HaMashiach, uragira Isirayeli y'ukuri y'Imana mu buryo butunganye mu rukundo rwe rwumvira ijwi ryanjye, ndetse urugero musuzugura. Kuko Yeshua HaMashiach ari we muntu wenyine wigeze yumvira byimazeyo ubushake bwa YHVH, NDI UWONDI ukomeye uwo nishimira iteka, nuko rero namugize umwuka utanga ubugingo nuko mumwumve uwo natumye ku bwanyu kuko nta wundi uhari.

Kandi mwinangiye imitima yanyu ikomera nk'Ibuye ry'Urufatiro ryafashwe mpiri n'Isinagogi ya Satani kuko mwanze amagambo y'inama yanjye yatanzwe mu maso ha Kristo Yesu, uwo mwanga mu bujiji, bityo mukanga ibyiringiro byanyu byonyine byo kugarurwa n'agakiza iteka n'iteka ryose. Nuko muhagaze muri impumyi kandi mwambaye ubusa kandi mufite isoni mu cyaha cyanyu, mutekereza mu bujiji ko mwabaye abajyanama ba YHVH, NDI UWONDI Ukomeye!

Nuko mbaha kumva mu buhumyi bwanyu bw'igice ko mwatsikiye ku ibuye ryanjye rikuru ry'imfuruka abubatsi banyu banze, ndetse ibuye ryanjye ryo kugusha. Kuko Yeshua HaMashiach ari ibuye rikuru ry'imfuruka ndetse ibuye ry'urufatiro ry'ukuri, ndetse Yesu w'i Nazareti uwo mwanga mu kutumvira kw'inangira nubwo nabatoranyije ubwanjye nk'ubwoko bwihariye kandi umutima wanjye uravunika.

Umva, umva, Yewe Isirayeli kandi wumve ko NDI we wandika amategeko yanjye y'iteka, ndetse ikimenyetso cy'ubushake bwanjye bw'iteka mu mugambi wanjye w'iteka w'incungu nakoze nk'uko inama y'ubushake bwanjye bwite bw'iteka kandi butunganye iri mu bisate by'amabuye ku bw'ukumvira kwa Isirayeli kuri Sinayi! Nuko rero, NDI we uzandika amategeko yanjye ku bisate by'inyama by'imitima yanyu kuko mu rukundo rwanjye kuri mwe, n'agatonyanga gato k'imbaraga zanjye gashobora kwandika amategeko yanjye ku bisate byanyu by'amabuye kandi ku bw'ubuntu bwanjye n'imbabazi bigaragarira mu maso ha Yeshua HaMashiach nzamenagura ibisate byanyu by'amabuye bibe ibumba ry'umubumbyi kugira ngo mwongere kuremwa iteka mu gukiranuka n'ukwera kw'ukuri nk'uko narahiye mu masezerano yanjye kuri ba So ko nzakora.

Nuko rero, Yewe Isirayeli, ndabategeka kumvira ijwi rya Yeshua HaMashiach kuko nta gakiza kaboneka mu rindi zina ryatanzwe uretse Yesu Kristo Umukiranutsi, uwo mwita mu bujiji ikigirwamana cy'abanyamahanga kuko muri mu bubata bwa Esawu!

Nyamara nuko byanditswe kandi nuko byagenze: Kuva kuri Aburahamu kugeza kuri Isaka, igihe Yakobo na Esawu bamuvukagaho, ukuboko kwa Yakobo kwafashe bwa mbere agatsinsino ka Esawu. Kuko Esawu ari iherezo ry'isi, kandi Yakobo ni intangiriro y'iyikurikira. Ukuboko kw'umuntu kuri hagati y'agatsinsino n'ukuboko. Nyamara nuko bimeze ko ubu ngiye gukora kugira ngo ntandukanye Esawu na Yakobo kuko Esawu ari Isinagogi ya Satani ari bo byatsi bibi byateye mu gihugu cy'isezerano nanone, kandi Isirayeli yongeye guterwa n'Abanyakanani.

Kandi nk'uko byanditswe ni ko bizakorwa, ni ko NDI UWO NDI WE Uhoraho avuga: Dore umunsi uraje, uzatwika nk'itanura, kandi abibone bose, yego n'abakora ibibi bose bazaba nk'ibishingwe: kandi umunsi uje, uzabatwika, ni ko Uwiteka Nyiringabo avuga, ku buryo utazabasigira umuzi cyangwa ishami. Ariko kuri mwe mwubaha Izina ryanjye, Umwana wo Gukiranuka azabarasira afite gukiza mu mababa ye, kandi muzasohoka mukure nk'inyana zo mu kiraro.

Kandi nk'uko Yeshua HaMashiach akoranya abizerwa mu itorero, ari ryo mubiri we, n'umugeni we ubu nzazana Yeshua HaMashiach kugira ngo atandukanye bwa nyuma Esawu na Yakobo, kandi Yakobo azakizwa n'ubwo yanyura mu bigeragezo by'icyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli. Ku bw'ibyo, ntihakabeho umusambanyi, cyangwa umuntu uhumanye nka Esawu, wagurishije umugabane we w'imfura ku isahani imwe y'ibiryo. Kuko muzi yuko nyuma yaho, ubwo yashakaga kuragwa umugisha, yanzwe: kuko atabonye aho yihana, n'ubwo yashatse uwo mugisha arira.

Bityo rero muntu ari hagati y'ukuboko kwa Yakobo n'agatsinsino ka Esawu, keretse ko Esawu ashaka guha Yakobo igikomere cyo mu mutwe cya burundu, icyo ntazemerera. Kandi bimeze bityo, ko abaturage bose bo mu isi basigaye mu makuba ya Yakobo kugira ngo bihanganire umuriro w'utunganya bazaba aba Yakobo binyuze kuri Yesu w'i Nazareti, abo ni bo bana b'umurage, cyangwa bazangwa hamwe na Esawu wagurishije umugabane we w'imfura ku gice cy'umugati. Ku bw'ibyo, umuntu wa kamere azambaza izina rya Yesu w'i Nazareti kugeza ku iherezo rye kugira ngo azazurirwe muri Isirayeli y'Imana cyangwa na we azagurisha amahirwe ye n'umurage we w'iteka ku gice cy'umugati gitanzwe n'ukuboko kwa Baraba, kuko Baraba azayobora Isinagogi ya Satani kandi ni we mwana w'intama w'ikiyoka.

Kandi nyamara ni ko umutego uzategwa mu gihugu kugira ngo ufate Yakobo burundu kandi Baraba azana ibishuko bikomeye mu ngoma ye kandi azakora ibibi byinshi haba ku butaka no mu nyanja. Nyamara, ni ko bizaba ko uko imiriro y'intambara yaka kandi igurumana Baraba azazamuka ndetse yivuga ko yazutse kugira ngo asohoze imigambi y'Imana kandi azavuga ibintu byinshi byo gutuka Imana n'ibibi. Kandi azazana ishami rye ry'umunzenze kandi azivuga ko ari Nowa, ndetse muzima kugira ngo akize Isi urubanza rwagenewe abantu babi.

Kuko inyamaswa yo mu isi ari yo muhanuzi wabo w'ibinyoma izana igisubizo cyayo kuko ari umwana w'intama ufite amahembe abiri uvuga nk'ikiyoka. Kandi ingoma ye izaba ifite kamere ebyiri kuko azashaka guhuza abantu bose munsi y'ingoma y'ikiyoka, ari we wa mwanzi wa kera ubwe. Kuko Baraba atazaza gutegeka wenyine n'ubwo azavuga ko we ubwe ari Imana ariko azana isezerano ryo gucikamo ibice aho amahembe abiri azategeka inyanja n'isi, kandi muri ayo abiri rimwe ni ihembe rito rya Daniyeli. Nyamara nasezeranyije ko Isirayeli itazongera gucikamo ibice kandi mu bihugu bye bibiri ntihazigera habaho igisubizo ariko Yeshua HaMashiach azahuza Isirayeli yuzuye kandi iteka ryose.

(Nyamara ni ko bizasohora bakundwa banjye bityo mwumve icyo nzongera gukora binyuze kuri Yeshua HaMashiach: Nzafata abana ba Isirayeli mbakure mu bapagani, aho bagiye hose, kandi nzabakoranya impande zose, mbazane mu gihugu cyabo. Kandi nzabagira ubwoko bumwe mu gihugu, ku misozi ya Isirayeli, kandi umwami umwe azaba umwami wabo bose: kandi ntibazongera kuba amoko abiri, cyangwa ngo bongere gucikamo ibice bibiri uhereye none. Kandi ntibazongera kwiyanduza ibigirwamana byabo, cyangwa ibizira byabo, cyangwa ibicumuro byabo byose: ariko nzabakiza mbakure mu mazu yabo yose, aho bacumuriye, kandi nzabeza: bityo bazaba ubwoko bwanjye, nanjye nzaba Imana yabo.)

Iyindi ni inyamaswa yo mu nyanja iyo byanditsweho ngo: Kuko atifuza abagore, cyangwa ngo yite ku mana iyo ari yo yose: kuko aziyishyira hejuru ya byose. Ndababaza none ngo murebe niba umugabo aramukwa? Nyamara umugore we agaragara nk'umugore ufite abana, n'ubwo atari abana be. Kandi inyamaswa yo mu nyanja izazamuka yunze ubumwe byuzuye mu mugambi n'inyamaswa yo mu isi kandi bigira nk'abanzi bagaragara nk'abatandukanye mu bitekerezo n'idini.

Ariko barabeshya kandi barigira kandi se wabo ni cya kiyoka cyakoze izo nyamaswa ebyiri mbi ku bw'imigambi yacyo mibisha, ndetse no kurimbura Adamu kugira ngo cyibe isi iteka ryose ibe iyacyo kandi ntibishoboka ko cyasohoza imigambi yacyo mibisha kuko isi ari iy'Uwiteka n'ibiyuzuye byose, ni ko YHVH, NDI UWO NDI WE Uhoraho atangaza!

Kuko Baraba azana leta ebyiri zizahuzwa munsi y'ikiyoka kandi bazagira amahoro y'ibinyoma kuko basezeranya ubugingo bw'iteka n'ubuzima n'amahoro niba gusa mwemeye gufata ikimenyetso cyabo mukifatanya nabo uko bavanga icyuma n'ibumba ariko ntibazafatana. Mwumve, mwebwe mwese mwatekereza kwifatanya iteka ryose n'abaguye binyuze ku kimenyetso cyabo ko bazababwira ibinyoma byinshi bavuga ko ari umuti w'igitangaza uzatanga ubuzima n'ubuzima bwiza kandi utangaje cyane mu miterere yawo!

Ariko abazafata uburozi bwabo bose bazihindura iteka ryose ndetse impinduka itagira igaruriro. Kuko gutuka ishusho ya YHVH iyo Adamu yaremweho ari ukwifatanya n'ibyaha by'abaguye bigira nk'imana kandi atari zo, kandi ababikora bose nta mbabazi bafite kandi nta mahoro bafite kandi muri rya ziba ry'umuriro ridazima ni ho bazajya iteka ryose kuko ari ko bazaba bahisemo.

Nyamara, uko batatu badakiranuka bazamuka ni ko n'abatangabuhamya banjye babiri bazazamuka, kandi bazahanura iminsi 1260 bambaye ibigunira kandi bazatanga ubuhamya bwinshi. Bazaza muri Isirayeli kandi bahamye Imana Ihoraho yo Gukiranuka kandi bazahamya cyane Yeshua HaMashiach ndetse Yesu w'i Nazareti uwo nta muntu numwe ushobora kuza kuri El Shaddai atamunyuzeho! Nyamara, batatu badakiranuka bazatangaza ko Yesu Kristo ari ikivume kandi bazaca inyandiko zera izo Yeshua ari Jambo ryabaye umubiri. Nyamara ibyo bashaka guca ni byo bizamenagura imitwe yabo mu gutsindwa kw'iteka.

Ku bw'ibyo, mu minsi y'ubuhamya bwabo niba hari umuntu ushaka kubagirira nabi umuriro uzava mu kanwa kabo utwike abanzi babo: kuko niba hari umuntu ushaka kubagirira nabi bagomba kugirirwa nabi muri ubwo buryo kandi niba hari umuntu ushaka kubica bagomba kwicwa muri ubwo buryo. Aba bafite ububasha bwo gufunga ijuru, kugira ngo imvura itagwa mu minsi y'ubuhanuzi bwabo, kandi bafite ububasha ku mazi bwo kuyahindura amaraso, no gukubita isi ibyago by'amoko yose, uko bashatse kose. Kandi ni ko iminsi y'ubuhamya bwabo izaba.

Kandi bazavuga ibintu byinshi kandi bazamamaza Ubutumwa Bwiza Bw'iteka ndetse ubwo bwahishuwe mu gihe cyabwo kugira ngo ibintu byose bisohore mu maso ha Yesu Kristo. Kandi bazatanga ubuhamya bwabo kandi bazahishura ibintu byinshi mu minsi y'ubuhamya bwabo kandi byose bizandikwa iteka ryose. Kandi ku iherezo ry'iminsi yabo bagomba kwicwa kandi imibiri yabo izaryama mu muhanda w'umujyi mukuru, mu buryo bw'umwuka witwa Sodoma na Egiputa, aho Umwami wanyu na we yabambwe kandi bazishimira cyane kandi bahererekanye impano ku rupfu rwabo. Kandi ku iherezo ry'iminsi itatu n'igice bazumva ijwi rikomeye rivuga ngo nimuzamuke hano kandi bazazurwa ari bazima iteka ryose kandi batwarwe mu ijuru bityo ubuhamya bwabo buzaba burangiye.

Bityo rero mwumve ibishuko byageze ku muryango wanyu Isirayeli kandi mwumve amahitamo mugomba gukora. Muzemera Baraba nk'Moshiach wanyu w'ibinyoma kandi mufate ikimenyetso cye bityo mwifatanye na Esawu uko mugurisha umugabane wanyu w'imfura ku gice cy'umugati iteka ryose, cyangwa muzacisha bugufi imitima yanyu kandi mwumvire ijwi rya Yahweh Imana ikomeye y'iteka ndetse NDI UWO NDI WE Uhoraho mu maso ha Yeshua HaMashiach ari we Kristo Yesu uwo nazuye mu bapfuye akazamuka mu ijuru, kandi nkamuha ikuzo mu bwiza bw'iteka kandi ingoma ye n'Ubwami bwe ntibizagira iherezo, kandi nta muntu ushobora kwegera YHVH mu rindi zina iryo ari ryo ryose ryatanzwe haba mu ijuru cyangwa mu isi cyangwa munsi y'isi ibihe byose n'iteka ryose. Kuko Muri Kristo bose bazahindurwa bazima kandi baremwe iteka nk'umuntu umwe mushya iteka ryose kuko Kristo Yesu ari Adamu wa nyuma.

Kandi bazarwanya abera kandi bazabanesha kuko bahawe ububasha bwo kubikora. Ariko ntibazanesha Isirayeli, oya abasigaye ba Isirayeli bazakizwa nibahindukirira Yeshua HaMashiach n'imitima yabo yose.

Ku bw'ibyo umva Amagambo yanjye Yewe Isirayeli kugira ngo ubeho imbere yanjye kandi nzabakoranyiriza kuri njye munsi y'ubutware bukomeye bwa Mesiya wanyu w'ukuri, ndetse Yeshua HaMashiach. Kandi byanditswe ngo: Nyamara ntibishoboka ko ijambo ry'Imana ritagira icyo rikora: kuko bose atari Isirayeli, abakomoka kuri Isirayeli: Kandi bose atari abana, kuko ari urubyaro rwa Aburahamu: ariko, Muri Isaka ni ho urubyaro rwawe ruzitirirwa: Ni ukuvuga ngo, abana b'umubiri, si bo bana b'Imana: ariko abana b'isezerano, ni bo babarwa nk'urubyaro.

NDI YHVH, NDI UWO NDI WE Uhoraho kandi ndabahamagara Isirayeli kugira ngo abasigaye bizerwa baze kuri Yeshua HaMashiach kugira ngo abakize umutego Baraba ari gukora kugira ngo atege mu gihugu ku bw'irimbuka rya nyuma rya Yakobo, ndetse irimbuka ntazemerera.

Ndi uwo wohereza aya Magambo binyuze mu ijwi ry'urangurura mu butayu, wacunguwe mu bapagani na Kristo Yesu kandi agatoranywa ku bw'imigambi yacu y'iteka, kandi kuri Yeshua HaMashiach ni ho akorera YHVH, NDI UWO NDI WE Uhoraho, kuko nta muntu ushobora kuza kuri njye mu rindi zina, ndetse na Mose wanjye nkunda wenyine, kuko Mose asaba kumvira Yesu w'i Nazareti!

Ndi uwo uzakuraho ubuhumyi bw'igice kuri Isirayeli ihumye ku Isezerano Rishya n'igitambaro cya Mose kigipfutse imitima yabo mu buhumyi bw'igice. Kandi Isirayeli yose yambaza Yeshua HaMashiach izongera kubona kandi izabona byuzuye, ndetse mu maso ha Yesu Kristo, Mesiya wanyu w'iteka!

Ndi Imana Ishoborabyose ya Aburahamu, n'iya Isaka, n'iya Yakobo kandi nje gusohoza amasezerano yanjye yose inshuro nyinshi kandi nzabikora byuzuye binyuze mu mirimo y'iteka y'Umwana wanjye nkunda uwo ntegeka ubudahemuka bwanyu bwuzuye, ukwizera, urukundo, no kumvira, Yewe Isirayeli keretse niba muteganya kugurisha umugabane wanyu w'imfura, mwifatanya na Esawu mu buryo bwikora muri ya miriro y'iteka y'igihano n'ingororano y'ubutabera.

Umva, Yewe Isirayeli ko isi izamenya ko hari Imana muri Yakobo kandi ko Imana ya Yakobo ari Yahweh iteka ryose, ndetse NDI UWO NDI WE Uhoraho, kandi ni muri Kristo Yesu Isirayeli y'Imana izakoranywa kandi nzabakoranyiriza kuri njye nk'uko nasezeranyije. Ko baba hafi cyangwa kure, cyangwa baba Abayuda cyangwa abapagani ko bose bashobora gukoranywa muri Kristo ari bo baragwa b'ubugingo bw'iteka kandi ari bo bana b'ukuri b'isezerano kuko abana b'isezerano ari abakiranutsi babeshwaho no kwizera.

Kandi ni ko bizaba kandi YHVH ni ko yatangaje kandi byose bizaba yego na Amen na Amen na AMEN!
