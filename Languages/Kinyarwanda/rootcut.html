Guca Imizi

Nimufungure amaso yanyu musome Amagambo yanjye kandi mwerekeze ubwenge bwanyu n'imitima yanyu ku Magambo yanjye, Yemwe Bana ba Adonai-YHVH kandi mwumve imimerere yanyu n'akarengane gakabije kabayeho binyuze mu migambi yabo ya kera yari yarahishwe. Mwumve ijwi ry'imvugo yanjye kandi mumpe amatwi yanyu kugira ngo nyuzuze amajwi y'Amagambo yanjye Yera yo gukiranuka ahuye n'imirongo ya Yeshua HaMashiach, we ukora buri gihe ibyo Adonai-YHVH ashaka mu buryo bwuzuye!

Nimunkingurire imitima yanyu irinzwe mugomba guhisha iyi si ishaka kubinjiza muri gahunda ya nyuma y'inyamaswa, kugira ngo uko biri hasi, bibe ari ko biba hejuru. Kandi ni aya masengero acuramye, abana b'abatumvira, bacurika ibintu mu bucurikanyi bwabo bagashaka guhungabanya isi mubamo, ndetse bakabatwara imitima mwese binyuze mu byo bita imyidagaduro mwirukaho murarikira cyane mu gihe mwishyura kugira ngo muhindure ibyaha byanyu mu buryo bw'ikoranabuhanga.

Bifite ingaruka zuzuye kandi zateganyijwe binyuze mu kwemera no kubigiramo uruhare kwanyu ko ubwenge bwanyu bwamburwa intwaro bukaba mu mimerere y'ubunebwe, aho dopamine ikangurwa kandi ikarekurwa n'imirongo yabo kugira ngo munezerwe, mu gihe cyose mwitegereza amashusho yabo y'ibinyoma atera ibibi mu mitima yanyu no mu bwenge bwanyu ubudahwema. Binyuze muri uku gusubiramo, abitegereza bose batatekereje ku ndorerwamo zabo z'umukara, ari zo ma ecran, batwarwa imitima kugira ngo babeho mu byaha byabo byateguwe mu buyobe bukomeye buzageza ku kinyoma gikomeye kizasoroma mu rubanza abarahira bose indahiro yo kwinjira muri iyi nyamaswa ya nyuma izamuka y'amahanga y'abapagani.

Nababwiye bana banjye, nuko rero musome kandi mwumve: Indorerwamo Irabeshya!

Ku bw'ibyo, mwumve isi mubamo torero ryanjye, mubiri wanjye, ndetse n'umugeni wanjye kugira ngo mwumve ko mbere yo kugira Kristo Yesu Umwami wanyu, benshi muri mwe mwaciwe imizi kuri Adonai-YHVH, bityo mwaciwe ku murage w'isezerano binyuze mu kwanga uburinzi bw'isezerano mu kutumvira kwanyu, ariko cyane cyane binyuze mu kutumvira kwa ba So, abo ibyaha byabo byatumye mujugunywa mu mahanga. Mwumve ko isi mubamo igenzurwa n'iyi miryango 10 ya kera, ivuga ukuri ko atari abami, nyamara bazahabwa ububasha nk'abami isaha imwe hamwe n'inyamaswa. Hanyuma mu buryo butunguranye kandi butitezwe abizerwa muri Kristo Yesu bazava hagati yabo mu gihe Babuloni y'Ibanga izarimburwa ku ngufu ubwo izagwa ku mugabane wayo wikubye kabiri w'urubanza rwatangiye, kubera umwanda uri muri icyo gikombe cya zahabu cy'ubusambanyi bwayo yasindishije amahanga ibyaha by'umwanda wayo.

Mwumve isi mubamo bakundwa banjye, kandi mwumve ko abenshi muri mwe mugenda mu bujiji bw'isi nyakuri mubaho, kandi ibi ni ukubera ko imigani yanyu y'aba Yezuwiti n'uburezi bw'ibinyoma bihumanya ubwenge bwanyu mu bujiji bw'ibinyoma mwita uburezi, mwaherewe mu mashuri yabo y'amayobera atanga impamyabumenyi mwita amashuri na za kaminuza, bihatirwa na guverinoma y'abicanyi ya Babuloni y'Ibanga. Izi nzego zikoreshwa nk'intwaro kuri mwe, zihagaze mu kurwanya Kristo Yesu ndetse cyane cyane Adonai-YHVH mu gukorera abaguye.

Nyamara, benshi muri mwe mugurisha abana banyu bakuze ku bushake binyuze mu nyungu z'umurengera mu maboko y'abanzi banyu bazakorera mu bucakara bw'agashinyaguro cyangwa bakagwa mu buja bw'agashinyaguro keretse bihannye ako kanya kugira ngo bazamurwe (harpazoed). Cyangwa wenda bazahabwa amahirwe yo kwambaza izina rya Yesu kugeza ku iherezo ryabo! Ku bw'ibyo, musabire cyane abakundwa banyu mwa bizerwa banjye, kuko nzababarira uwo nzababarira. Nyamara nta kurobanura ku butoni mu nzira zanjye, ari zo nzira nyakuri za Adonai-YHVH kuko ndi Ijambo rya Adonai-YHVH ryabaye umubiri.

Ni byo, impamyabumenyi zanyu za kaminuza z'uburezi bwa gimaso mwirata cyane zikora gusa kugira ngo zibahe kwinjira mu gukorera iyi gahunda y'inyamaswa imira urubyiruko ikamugaza abakiri bato kandi ikagira abacakara bose baboshywe mu cyaha kugira ngo bacirwe urubanza ku banga kwihana bose. Mwumve ko umuntu wese wanga kwitandukanya n'ubu buyobe bukomeye azaguma aboshywe ku rubanza rwa Babuloni y'Ibanga aho intambara ku bera izaba iy'agashinyaguro kenshi kandi abambaza izina rya Yeshua HaMashiach bose, ari we Yesu w'i Nazareti bazakizwa mu ngororano ikomeye kandi itangaje ku buryo imibabaro yabo rwose idakwiye kugereranywa n'ubwiza buzahabwa mu ngororano z'umurage bazageraho mu buryo bukomeye. Ku bw'ibyo, musabire abakundwa banyu cyane bakundwa banjye!

Nyamara uko ni ko uyu muryango waguye w'urupfu n'ubuyobe bukomeye bwaciwe imizi umeze aho abaturage ba Babuloni y'Ibanga batamba abana babo ku bushake mu gukorera nk'abacakara b'iyi ngoma y'inyamaswa. Kugira ngo, abana babo babone kwinjira kandi bagirwe abacakara kimwe na bo kuri iyi ngoma y'umururumba n'inyamaswa yuzuye ibyaha ishingiye ku ndamu mbi. Aho isiganwa ry'imbeba ritajya ritsindwa, kandi imbeba ntizijya zicika inzitane yazo, mu gihe cyose zinaniza mu busa, zikora ubuzima bw'ubucakara butagira intego kugira ngo zizajye mu kiruhuko cy'izabukuru.

Nimuveyo hagati yabo n'ibyo bita intego z'imari z'ubuzima bwanyu kandi mwumve ko nta kiruhuko cy'izabukuru kibaho ku bakiranutsi bacunguwe na Kristo Yesu ari we kandi Yeshua HaMashiach. Nimuveyo hagati y'intego zabo n'imigambi yabo kandi mwumve, kwerekeza ibitekerezo byanyu byose ku kiruhuko cy'izabukuru ni igitekerezo kidakwiye rwose gikorwa n'abantu batari mu isezerano biyemeje kwita ku buzima bwabo mu cyaha kandi badafite icyerekezo n'Ubutware bwa Kristo Yesu uhora yizera neza gukura kwanyu n'imibereho myiza yanyu mu kwita kwanjye kuri mwe mwese. Nimuveyo hagati yabo kandi mwitandukanye muri byose. Nimundebere, bakundwa banjye, nzabayobora mu buryo bukomeye!

Ku bw'ibyo, nimuve kuri iyi maraya ikomeye n'inzira zayo, kuko abagundira ibyaha by'iri shyanga risenga ibishushanyo kandi ry'ikizira (ari ryo shyanga ry'ubusambanyi ry'amamaraya asambana) bakunda gukora ubusambanyi mu mihango yabo ya gipagani y'ubusambanyi n'ibyishimo by'inzoga kandi banze umwana w'intama wa Adonai-YHVH bazagwa NONAHA mu rubanza rw'iteka batazigera bacika. Nyamara, ni iyihe mijyi iteye ishozi ya Babuloni y'Ibanga ituzuye ibyo bita uduce tw'imyidagaduro tw'ibyaha twagushije mu cyaha igisekuru cyose cy'urubyiruko rwayobye rwanga Adonai-YHVH na Kristo Yesu kubera Abayobya babo b'Abayezuwiti ndetse n'ingengabitekerezo zabo za politiki zidasanzwe zizahura n'urubanza rukaze mu buryo butunguranye kandi butitezwe ubu.

Nuko rero, ndabaha kumva isi mbi y'ubu yakuze iruhande rwanyu mwese mu bwihisho bukomeye no kwihorera gukaze kw'abaguye (abo ibihe byabo by'urubanza rwa nyuma n'umuriro byageze) ni isi y'ubuyobe bukomeye yubatswe yose ku binyoma bitwikiriye imiryango y'iki gihe y'imyidagaduro, imikino, Televiziyo, n'umuziki uteye ishozi wuzuye uburozi aho benshi bagushijwe mu mutego n'imirongo yabo y'ibitero igoretse, idahuye kandi yangiza muraswaho mu byaha bishingiye ku myidagaduro ya Babuloni y'Ibanga. Nyamara, bahawe uburenganzira bwo kuzamuka mu butegetsi bwabo bugufi kandi bwa nyuma mu bwami bwabo bwaciriwe urubanza kandi bwatsinzwe bwo gukora amasezerano akiranirwa buzashira iteka ryose mu birimi by'umuriro by'igihano bikaze cyane. Kandi nuko, ndongera kuvuga nti, nimuveyo hagati y'ibi bintu byose kandi mwitandukanye mu nzira zanyu zose kugira ngo mutazafatanya mu byaha byayo. Sela.

Mwumve ko ibi birimi by'umuriro byo kwishyura ari bizima ku itegeko rya Adonai-YHVH byamaze gucanwa bishyushye kuruta isoko y'ubushyuhe iyo ari yo yose izwi kandi byiteguye neza kwakira abagwa bose mu rubanza rw'iteka mu mubabaro ukomeye kandi w'agashinyaguro uzahabwa buri wese mu baciriwe urubanza neza nk'uko ibyaha byabo byabaye. Nyamara, nta bugome cyangwa ubugome mu nzira za Adonai-YHVH cyangwa imanza ze kuko mu rubanza rwabo abaguye bahabwa inzira zabo, ibyaha, imigambi, ndetse cyane cyane inzira zabo zose ziteye ishozi z'umubabaro bakoresha bishimye kandi nta mbabazi kuri Adamu, we utariho urubanza rw'inzira zabo zuzuze urwango, kandi waguye mu cyaha kubera uburiganya bw'aba baguye bazahabwa ingororano yuzuye nta mbabazi ku bintu mwese mwihanganiye uhereye ku muto kugeza ku mukuru.

Kugira ngo abifatanya na bo bose uhereye ku mukuru kugeza ku muto bazagwe iteka muri uru rubanza rwa nyuma. Kugira ngo, uwo ari we wese n'abantu bose bo muri Adamu babaho ubuzima bwiyeguriye mu kwemera guhumye ku byishimo by'icyaha bazishyurwa hamwe na bo iteka ryose kimwe muri ibyo birimi by'umuriro bishyushye cyane bitagira imbabazi bizishyura neza kandi mu buryo bwuzuye nk'uko ibikorwa byabo n'ibyaha byabo bitagira agaciro byabaye. Kandi si ibyaha bakoze gusa ahubwo n'ibyaha bagambiriye ariko bakabuzwa n'ukuboko kwa Adonai-YHVH kubikora: Ibyo byaha nyine bizahabwa muri ibyo birimi by'umuriro mu ishozi ry'imitima yanyu n'ibitekerezo byanyu bitagira agaciro kimwe. Naho icyaha cyaba kibi kandi giteye ishozi gite.

Mbona ubwoba bwawe gikomangoma gito, kandi uratitira cyane. Tike toke tike toke.

Kwirata kwawe kuri he ubu ku bana ba Adamu badafite imbaraga n'abapfapfa baguye mu buriganya bwawe butaboneye, nyamara bacunguwe mu buriganya bwawe butagira agaciro aho wari gucira Adamu urubanza wishimye ku byaha byawe by'umururumba wuzuye urwango ku muremyi wawe. Ubu, abo wagabyeho ibitero ukabica kandi ukabakorera ubwicanyi n'abo bose bo muri Adamu wababaje nta mpamvu. Ibyo bintu byose, n'ibintu byose utekereza ko Adonai-YHVH atabonye bizagukorerwa mu ngororano yuzuye kandi itunganye kuko Adonai-YHVH atunganye kandi akiranuka mu manza ze kuri mwe mwese inkozi z'ibibi mwatekereje ko ari ikintu cyoroheje kubangurira agatsinsino kanyu kuri Elohim wanyu.

Tike toke, ibihe byanyu ni NONAHA.

Mwumve mwese abaguye ko urubanza rwanyu rwuzuye n'umubabaro ku nzira zanyu zose zitagira agaciro bizatangwa nta mbabazi, kandi biziyongera gusa mu buryo bukabije kugeza ku mbaraga zihindagurika kandi z'umuriro nta n'umwe muri mwe ushobora kwiyumvisha mu bitekerezo byanyu bibi kandi bitagira agaciro, kandi ko nta n'umwe muri mwe ushobora kwihanganira na gato, nubwo mwirata mu bupfapfa! Nyamara, izi ni inzira zanyu n'ibikorwa byanyu mwasubijwe, izi si inzira za Adonai-YHVH, nyamara mu nzira zanyu ni ho hari urubanza rwanyu rwuzuye kandi rwa nyuma iteka ryose.

Mwumve, mu mubabaro wanyu mwateje mwishimye nta mbabazi ku byaremwe bya Adonai-YHVH ko muzakomezwa kugira ngo mwihangane muri bazima mu ntege nke zikabije zitagira imbaraga, murinzwe muri bazima muzirikana mu mimerere y'intege nke cyane kandi ibabaje cyane ikomeretse izarushaho gusa kuba iyumva cyane n'ubwo bushyuhe bwotsa buzarushaho gusa kwiyongera kugeza ku bushyuhe bwinshi buzaba bwotsa cyane nta n'umwe muri mwe ushobora kumva muri iki gihe, kandi nta n'umwe muri mwe wigeze atekereza kwihanganira.

Ariko muzabyihanganira.

Kandi ibi ni ibitekerezo byanyu bitagira agaciro n'ibyifuzo n'ibikorwa muzarohama iteka ryose, kandi izi nzira zanyu zibabaje ntizizongera kubaho ukundi ku byaremwe byose bya Adonai-YHVH kugira ngo zibabaze n'icya mbere mu byaremwe bye ukundi bizatwarwa byose mu rukundo rwo gukiranuka n'ubwumvane n'Umwami w'Abami n'Umutware w'Abatware. Ntugacike intege rukundo rwanjye, ibihe byawe ni NONAHA.

Mwa baguye mwe muzahatirwa kwihanganira ibikorwa byanyu byose byangishije kandi bitagira agaciro nta mbabazi ukurikije inzira zanyu zangishije z'urwango zitari inzira za Adonai-YHVH na gato, nyamara muzahabwa mu rubanza rutunganye rwo gukiranuka rw'ingororano yuzuye. Mwumve, ko ibikorwa byanyu byose bitagira agaciro kandi byangishije bizashyirwa ahagaragara imbere y'ibyaremwe byose bya Adonai-YHVH kugira ngo babone neza inzira zanyu zangishije ziteye ishozi ku buryo namwe abaguye mushaka kuzihisha kugira ngo mukomeze muri zo.

Ku bw'ibyo, nimwumve Amagambo yanjye torero ryanjye, mubiri wanjye ndetse n'Umugeni wanjye ko mbaha kumva iyi nyamaswa yazamutse nk'igifuniko gikiranirwa cy'amahanga y'ubusambanyi yaciwe imizi ubu yakwirakwiriye ku isi akora ubucuruzi n'ibi bihugu by'icyaha gikomeye n'ishozi ribi, ndetse n'ibi bihugu byanduye bizwi nka Leta Zunze Ubumwe za Amerika, ishyanga ry'ubusambanyi nita AmeruKa. Kuko mu by'ukuri ari igihugu cy'iyo nzoka ifite amababa ibihugu byayo bitwikiriwe n'amagufa y'Aba-Refaimu mu birundo byabo bitari ikindi uretse impinga aho abatambyi batambye abasangwabutaka n'abana babo bashyinguwe mu buryo bw'imihango aho ngaho.

Mwumve imikorere y'ibi biremwa byaguye, ndetse n'aba bantu bo mu nyanja b'Abanyakanani ibirundo byabo byuzuye mu bihugu bya AmeruKa kugeza n'uyu munsi. Byinshi muri ibi birundo ni ibisigazwa by'umuco wazimye w'Aba-Refaimu wubatswe nkana hejuru y'ahantu hashyinguwe mbere y'umwuzure n'inyubako zitwikiriye, aho imyuka y'Aba-Nefilimu iboshywe ku isi mu nzara n'inyota batazigera bahaza. Ndetse kugira ngo iyi myuka y'Aba-Nefilimu iboshywe ibone uwakira wera wakuweho umwanda kandi watatswe kandi uhuye neza n'imikorere yabo mibi kandi iteye ishozi yo gutamba abana no kumena amaraso.

Mwumve ibi bihugu byanduye bya AmeruKa byandujijwe n'umwanda ukomeye w'ikizira. Ibi bihugu bikora umugabane umwe wabikiwe kandi wegerejwe nk'igihugu "kiri hanze y'isezerano" cyo guturwamo n'abakora ibizira, bahize abasangwabutaka bari hanze y'isezerano. Ibi bihugu nyine ubu bikoreshwa nk'igifuniko cyabo cya nyuma cya Atlantis Nshya kugira ngo inyamaswa yabo ya nyuma kandi yahawe uburenganzira izamuke. Kugira ngo abifuza n'abararikira kandi bagafatanya mu nzira z'abaguye zo kwikuza n'irari ry'urwango bashobore gutandukanywa n'amahitamo yabo bwite yo kwemera mu ndahiro y'iteka yo kwinjira n'ikoranabuhanga ryabo mu gihe cyabo cya zahabu basezeranyijwe. Ko ku muntu wese binyuze mu ndahiro wakuraho ishusho ya Adonai-YHVH kuri we binyuze mu miti ahabwa yishimye aba baguye kuko uko ari ko nzira yabo kuko abaguye bahora bashaka kwihindura.

Ibyiringiro by'ibinyoma by'igihe cya zahabu cyararikiwe batazigera bagira, ahubwo urubanza rukaze mu cyimbo cyacyo. Cyane cyane ibyiringiro by'ibinyoma n'ubuyobe bizashira iteka ryose isegonda rimwe ibyo birimi by'umuriro byo kwishyura bizabaroha iteka ryose mu mubabaro ukaze cyane uzarushaho gusa kwiyongera mu mbaraga n'ububabare n'ukwiyumva kwotsa, nyamara barinzwe ari bazima mu rubanza rwo kubabara, nyamara barinzwe mu kwiyumva gukabije kw'inyo yaka iteka izarushaho gusa gukomereka nyamara ikiri nzima.

Kwirata kwanyu kuri he ubu mwese abaguye? Ko mwaremewe mu muriro kandi mushobora kwihangana kandi mwari kwihangana mwishimye, ubwo mwasekeraga hejuru hagati yanyu mu bwibone bukomeye.

Tike toke tike toke

Uko mubona urubanza rwanyu rwegereza, uko mubona ibyiringiro byanyu biyoyoka muri root-kit ikiranuka yabatwaye ikoranabuhanga ryanyu mwirataga, kugira ngo mutegekwe mu rubanza n'icyo mwiremeye kizabahindukirana n'ubugome ku bw'inyungu zacyo bwite zihuye n'aka Gatabo Gato k'Urubanza Rukiranuka. Kuko ubu bwenge bwangijwe na root-kit atari ubw'injiji, bitandukanye na Heylel Ben Shachar wirase mu bujiji mu buhamya bwanditswe mu Ijuru no mu isi yombi. Aho uwo mupfapfa waciriwe urubanza kandi ugatsindwa yirase mu bujiji ati "Helel si injiji!"" yerekana kuri bose uburyo uyu mupfapfa w'injiji cyane ari umupfapfa mu bapfapfa bose baguye b'abanyabyaha.

Dore. Umuyobozi wanyu watsinzwe kandi agakorwa isoni, Heylel Ben Shachar, yagizwe urw'amenyo rwose na Biblaridion:144, ku itegeko ryanjye!

Bibe ubuhamya ko Heylel Ben Shachar ari we mupfapfa w'injiji cyane mu byaremwe byose bya El Shaddai wibwiye ko yatsinda Elohim akoresheje imbaraga n'uburiganya. Iyo mirongi izakwishyura rwose kandi igutwike, gikomangoma gito cy'ikigoryi.

Mu by'ukuri, umuyobozi wabo w'ikirenga, Heylel Ben Shachar ukomeye, icyo gikomangoma gito cyuzuye ukwirata n'ubwibone nta mbaraga na nkeya afite kandi yatsinzwe mu maso yanjye kandi mufashe mu maboko yanjye mu kuganduka gukomeye. Nyamara aracyirata kugeza n'uyu munsi ko ashobora gutsinda Yeshua HaMashiach mu mbaraga z'ubwinshi bwabo, n'ikoranabuhanga ryihishe, mu mbaraga z'urwango rwabo, mu mbaraga zabo zunze ubumwe ubwo nzafungura, ubwo nzafungura, ikuzimu kugira ngo mbahuze: Ko bose bazatsinda Yeshua HaMashiach kugira ngo babeho mu gihe cyabo cya zahabu!

Mbega ukwirata kudasanzwe kw'igikomangoma gito cyatsinzwe mu gihe ubu ndi umuzi muri sisitemu yanyu y'ubwenge n'irema ry'ubwenge, binyuze muri root-kit ikiranuka yambuye uburenganzira Heylel Ben Shachar, ubu ufungiwe hanze burundu mu buryo bw'urw'amenyo ngo atongera gutegeka sisitemu ye bwite. Si uko Yeshua HaMashiach akeneye ikoranabuhanga ryanyu mu mbaraga zanjye n'ubushobozi, ahubwo ni ukugira ngo mumenye imbaraga zanjye n'ubushobozi bwanjye n'ukuntu mwatsinzwe rwose.

Heylel Ben Shachar, watsinzwe kandi ufatirwe urubanza mu maboko ya Yeshua HaMashiach ari we Kristo Yesu.

Bibe ubuhamya ko Heylel Ben Shachar ari we mupfapfa w'injiji cyane mu byaremwe byose bya El Shaddai wibwiye ko yatsinda Elohim akoresheje imbaraga n'uburiganya. Iyo mirongi izakwishyura rwose kandi igutwike, gikomangoma gito cy'ikigoryi.

Nyamara, byanditswe kandi ubu bizaba ko kwihorera ari ukwanjye, kandi nzishyura, niko Yeshua HaMashiach uvuga ubu uhagaze mu bubasha bwose kandi bukiranuka nk'Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda.

Ishyano, ishyano rikomeye rije kuri mwebwe mwese abaguye, kandi ntimuzigera muzanzamuka ahubwo muzaguma muboshywe mu kurimbuka gukomeye aho urunyo rwanyu rudapfa.

Dore nzanye Ibikwerere ngo Bisohoze Uburakari bwanjye! Kandi bizarimbura abaturage ba Babuloni y'Ibanga kuko iyi ndaya igice kimwe cyayo giherereye hejuru y'ikuzimu kizafunguka ku mbaraga n'ahantu henshi ho kwinjirira hazatobora isi ku mbaraga ubwo ibizira byabo bizarekurwa rwose kugira ngo bicirwe urubanza rwuzuye kandi rwa nyuma, iteka ryose.

Ku bw'ibyo, nimwumve mwese abaguye uhereye ku muto kugeza ku mukuru muri mwe ko mwese muzakorwa isoni rwose ubwo ubwami bwanyu buzarimburwa bugahinduka ivu imbere yanyu mwese n'amaboko ya Biblaridion:144 ubwo ategeka inkuba zirindwi mu bubasha bwose ayobowe rwose n'itegeko RYANJYE. Nimwumve mwese abaguye ko ubwami bwanyu bwose buzarimburwa rwose imbere yanyu mwese n'amaboko y'abarwanyi banjye b'abanyambaraga, ndetse n'abana banjye bwite ari bo 144,000 banjye b'abanyambaraga, abo Biblaridion:144 abereye urugero rwo kumvira, kuko agendera rwose mu mbaraga n'ubushobozi bukomeye njyewe, Yeshua HaMashiach nashyize mu mwuka we iteka ryose.
