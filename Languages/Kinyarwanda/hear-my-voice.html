Ubuhamya Bubiri Umuhamya: Itorero
Mu Mirimo ya Yohana Umubatiza
14 Werurwe 2024

Umva Ijwi Ryanjye!

Ijambo ry'Uwiteka ryangezeho. Nimwumve ijwi ryanjye, bana banjye bato, kandi muze mu maboko yanjye arambuye. Mumenye ko urukundo mbakunda rudashobora kuvunika kandi rudashobora kuneshwa, kuko ari urukundo rwa Data kuko yahaye abana be kamere ye, kandi muri njye ni ho kuzura kose kuba. Nabahamagaye mu mazina yanyu kandi nabatumiye guhagarara imbere yanjye. Intama zanjye zumva ijwi ryanjye, kuko zizi ijwi ry'umwungeri wazo. Ntimurumva ijwi ryanjye? Mbese mwazibye amatwi yanyu ngo ntimwumve ijwi ryanjye? Ndangurura ijwi mu mayira no mu tuyira; nasuye na za gereza zanyu nzanira agakiza abahohotewe n'abibagiranye.

Yewe muntu, kuki ureka Umwami wawe? Kuki wayobye inzira zanjye? Nyamara, uzavuga uti, Sinayobye inzira zawe; ndi uwo Uwiteka yahamagaye. Ni ukuri, narahamagaye, kandi mwaritabyemo igihe gito, igihe cy'ubusore n'ibyishimo byinshi. Bamwe muri mwe bajyanywe aho amasega aba, kandi benshi muri mwe bayobejwe n'amagambo meza, imbwirwaruhame nziza, n'indimi zibeshya zigiye gucecekeshwa vuba.

Ibuka urukundo rwawe rwa mbere, kandi wihane; nture ibyaha byawe imbere yanjye, kuko mpora niteguye kweza ibyaha byawe mu maraso yanjye y'impongano.

Nimwumve ijwi ryanjye, bana banjye, kuko amajwi menshi yumvikana kimwe kandi ba Kristo b'ibinyoma benshi bagerageza kwigana ijwi ryanjye. Ni ukuri, nita ku itorero ryanjye kandi nkariragira n'ukuboko kwanjye, kugira ngo nzabamurikire Imana muri abakobwa basugi kandi mwambaye imyenda myiza y'igitare, ibizinga by'ibyaha byanyu byababariwe, byakuweho, kandi byibagiranye, kuko nabijugunye mu kuzimu kwimbitse, ngo bitazongera kuzamuka ukundi. Nyamara, benshi muri mwe mwarangajwe ndetse mwatwawe n'iyi si mbi ya none. Babagabyeho ibitero bakoresheje uburozi bwabo binyuze mu kurumwa n'inzoka, ariko mumenye ko naho inzoka yabaruma, nta cyago kizabageraho, kuko ikimenyetso cya Data kidashobora kumenwa. Mwerekeze amaso yanyu kuri njye, umwana we w'ikinege, uwo yatanze abikunze ngo abe impongano y'ibyaha byanyu kugira ngo twongere guhura, ntituzongere gutandukana ukundi. Reka mbakize; reka mbakize rwose kandi nkureho inkovu zanyu kugira ngo mube bazima kandi mutunganye imbere yanjye. Mukurikire inzira zanjye kandi mukurikire Ijambo ryanjye, kuko:

Ndi Ijambo ryabaye umubiri ribana namwe.

Ndi umutsima w'ubugingo, kandi uwurya wese ntazongera gusonza ukundi.

Ndi uwo ubwami bwe butari ubw'iyi si, kandi ingororano yanjye iri kumwe nanjye ku bizerwa n'abatoranyijwe banjye.

Ndi ugendagenda hagati y'ibitereko by'amatara birindwi kandi ntegeka abamarayika barindwi b'Itorero.

Ndi Uwera w'Imana.

Ndi umuvugizi wanyu.

Ndi ugerageza imitima y'abantu.

Mpora niteguye kweza ibyaha byanyu mu maraso yanjye y'impongano.

Ndi umucamanza utabera.

Ndi Kristo nyakuri wavuzwe uhereye mbere na mbere.

Ndi umwana w'Imana waje mu mubiri.

Ndi uwatanze amaraso yanjye atagira icyaha ku banyabyaha, kugira ngo baragwe ubugingo buhoraho.

Ndi umwana w'Imana waje mu mubiri.

Ndi umwungeri mwiza.

Ndi uwaje mu banjye ariko abanjye baranyanga.

Ndi Kristo nyakuri, watoranyijwe na Data uhereye ku kuremwa kw'isi.

Ndi uwo ndi we.

Bana banjye, ndababwira ukuri ko naramuriye ukuboko kwanjye isi igiye kurimbuka mu gihe cya za Yubile nyinshi kugeza ubu, kandi ukuboko kwanjye ntikuzahora kurambuwe mu mbabazi n'ubuntu, nubwo imbabazi n'ubuntu bihora biri kumwe nanjye. Nahamagaye benshi, kandi benshi baranze. Icyakora, abo nahamagaye nkabatoranya nkabagira abera nkabuhagira nkabasukura nkabakiza, abo ngabo bazahagarara imbere y'intebe yanjye y'imanza kandi batware ingororano ikomeye kubera gukiranuka kwabo.

Nubwo mfite imirimo myinshi mbabikiye ngo muyikore, kandi nubwo mwankoreye byinshi njye n'izina ryanjye, nifuza umutima wanyu wose kuruta imirimo yanyu. Nifuza ko mumpindukirira mukareka ibyaha byanyu. Nimuze mumbere abera, kuko Umwami wanyu yiteguye n'ukuboko kwanjye gukiza kurambuye kugira ngo mbagire abera nk'urubura rushya. Nubwo amaboko yanjye yatewe imisumari, amaraso yanjye y'impongano yamenetse avamo no mu mubiri wanjye kugeza ku gitonyanga cya nyuma. Natanze ibyanjye byose kugira ngo mubone kubaho imbere ya Data iteka ryose.

Ni ukuri, sinabaye impongano y'icyaha kimwe gusa; ni ukuri, sinabaye impongano y'ibyaha bike gusa; ni ukuri, sinabaye impongano y'ibyaha byinshi gusa, ahubwo nabaye impongano y'ibyaha byose ibihe byose niba gusa muzanyitaba igihe nemeza imitima yanyu binyuze mu mwuka wanjye wera. Ntimwumva ko nta cyifuzo mfite cyo kubaciraho iteka? Hariho ufite icyo cyifuzo kandi urarikira kurimbuka kwanyu akabarega ku manywa na nijoro imbere ya Data, nubwo ndi umuvugizi wanyu. Yajugunywe hasi kandi yaratsinzwe; nyamara, kuki mushyira umujura mu mwanya wo hejuru mu mitima yanyu kandi mfite intsinzi yuzuye kandi ishyitse kuri we no ku bwami bwe bugiye guhinduka ubusa vuba? Mutuze kandi mugire amahoro kandi muze aho ndi n'umutima wicisha bugufi. Ntimuzi yuko iyaba mwiciraga imanza ubwanyu, mutari kuzacirwaho iteka? Munsabe mbereke ubuzima bwanyu, kuko nzabahishurira ibintu byinshi kugira ngo mwature amakosa yanyu kandi mukizwe rwose.

Ntimutinye, bana banjye bato, kuko atari njye ubaciraho iteka. Nimwumve umutima nama wanyu kandi ntimwirengagize ijwi ryanjye mwitwaje impamvu. Iyi si igiye kujugunywa hasi, kandi uko iyi si ihinduka iteka, muzazamurwa mu ijuru hejuru, kugira ngo mbamurikire Data, kugira ngo mubone umurage wanyu kandi muhabwe ingororano zanyu zo gukiranuka. Nifuje cyane umunsi tuzabonana mu ijuru kugira ngo amaherezo muhishurirwe ubuzima bwanyu nyakuri kuko mwacitse ubu buzima bw'igihe gito bw'ibibi, ibyaha, n'ubugome. Ndi ugerageza imitima y'abantu, atari ukugira ngo mbacireho iteka, ahubwo kugira ngo binyuze mu muriro w'utunganya mwezwe muve mu nzira zanyu za kera kandi mumenye inzira zanjye z'urukundo, umucyo, ibyishimo, amahoro, kwihangana, n'ubugwaneza.

Nimwumve gucyaha kwanjye kandi mwemere igihano cyanjye kuko So abafata nk'abana, kandi ni uwuhe mwana Se akunda adacyaha ngo amuhane. Igihano cyanjye cyera imbuto z'amahoro zo gukiranuka, kuko igihano cyanjye ari ukubasukura rwose mu byaha byanyu niba gusa muzumva amagambo yanjye mugakurikira ijwi ryanjye. Nimugende, muhagarare, kandi muvuge amagambo yose y'ubu buzima. Bamwe bazakizwa muri iyi saha ya nyuma, kandi mu izina rya Yesu Kristo gusa, izina ryanjye rikomeye ririmo agakiza, kuko nta rindi zina ritangirwamo agakiza. Izina ryanjye rikomeye rihagaze bwuma, ribategereje ngo mubanzanire, kugira ngo mmurikire Data abihana nk'uruhinja ruvutse vuba rwinjijwe mu buzima bw'iteka. Ndabakunda cyane, kandi nk'uko Data yampaye, nzaha abizerwa n'abatoranyijwe banjye.

Ndi umwana w'Imana waje mu mubiri, ntagira icyaha kandi ndi umwere iteka ryose. Kandi, ndi uwatanze amaraso yanjye atagira icyaha ku banyabyaha kugira ngo baragwe ubugingo buhoraho. Mbese mwebwe, bizerwa n'abatoranyijwe banjye, ntimuri mu baragwa b'ubwiza buhoraho? Kuki mugihagaze mucecetse nk'abategereje amagambo akwiye. Nimuvuge! Mufungure akanwa kanyu muvuge, kuko nzayobora imvugo yanyu kugira ngo mufashe abahamagawe banjye bagibabara, kandi benshi bazakomeza kwitaba. Mwifatanye nanjye mu murimo wanjye muri iyi saha ya nyuma mbere y'uko ntitiza imfatiro z'isi nkayisatura ahantu henshi n'urubanza rukomeye rugatwikira isi. Nzabasubiza iyi si mu Bwami bwanjye buzaza, ariko mbere na mbere ubwami bw'ababi buzaburizwamo kandi bucirweho iteka ryo kubohwa mu rwobo ruzimu, kandi bazi ko inyanja y'umuriro waka cyane aho bazamara iteka ryose ibategereje.

Nzarimbura rwose intebe zabo z'ubutegetsi; nzarimbura rwose ubutware bwabo bwose; nzabatwika rwose kugeza hasi n'umuriro udashobora kuzima, kuko nanjye, Umwami wizerwa, nabibabwiye mbere y'uko biba. Mu nzu ya Data hari amazu menshi, kandi icyubahiro n'ubwiza byinshi birabategereje. Nuko rero, ntihakagire ubambura ingororano yanyu kandi mukomeze kumbera abizerwa. Mukomeze kwerekeza amaso yanyu kuri njye gusa, Umwami uhoraho, ndetse n'Umwami w'icyubahiro. Ndi umwungeri mwiza; ndi Kristo nyakuri watoranyijwe na Data uhereye ku kuremwa kw'isi. Ndi uwaje mu banjye ariko abanjye baranyanga, nubwo binyuze mu kugeragezwa gukomeye Yakobo azakizwa n'ukuboko kwanjye gukomeye. Isohozwa ry'ibintu byose riri hafi, kandi iherezo ry'umutware w'iyi si riri hafi. Nubwo azabohurwa igihe gito, urubanza rwe rw'iteka rurakurikira, urwo adashobora kuzigera akurwamo kandi ntihazabaho umuti. Nimungarukire, muvugane nanjye, munkorere umurimo, kuko Data yampaye byose, kandi ingororano yanyu mbabikiye ibitswe mu ijuru ku bizerwa n'abatoranyijwe banjye kandi ni ihamye nk'ikimenyetso cya Data nashyizeho kashe. Hasigaye iminota mike gusa y'igihe cy'ubuntu, kandi urubanza rwanjye ruzagwa gitungura uko umujinya w'Umwana w'Intama uza ku isi. Mutegure imitima yanyu kandi mwitegure, kuko igihe kiri hafi.
