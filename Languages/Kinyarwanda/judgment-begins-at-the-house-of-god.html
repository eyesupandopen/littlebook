Ibisharira Biryoshye
10 Ukuboza 2024

Urubanza Rutangirira mu Nzu y'Imana!

Nimwumve amagambo ya Yeshua HaMashiach bene wacu kugira ngo musobanukirwe inzira zanjye kandi kugira ngo muze kuri Mesiya wanyu w'ukuri kuko ndabahamagara, Yewe Isirayeli, mbese muracyumva ijwi ryanjye? Nuko rero, mutege amatwi yanyu mu kumvira kandi mukebwe imitima yanyu, kugira ngo umunyabwenge muri mwe arushijeho kuba umunyabwenge niba bumviye inyigisho zanjye kandi bakiyoroshya ku mabwiriza yanjye. Kuko nje kubabwira iby'abazashyirwaho ikimenyetso cy'inyamaswa, kugira ngo musobanukirwe itangiriro ryabo n'iherezo ryabo, kandi kugira ngo mudafatwa mu mutego ngo mutwarwe na byo.

Nuko rero, musobanukirwe ibihe murimo kandi musobanukirwe gutandukanya kwa nyuma kugiye gutangira vuba aha ubwo abizerwa banjye muri Kristo bazakoranyirizwa aho ndi mu bicu mu buryo butunguranye kandi butitezwe ubu. Uko niko biri kandi niko bizaba ko nzamanuka gitungura kandi ntabyitezwe mvuye mu ijuru nza mu bicu gukoranya abizerwa muri Kristo ubwo impanda y'Imana izavuga n'ijwi rirenga n'ijwi rya marayika mukuru. Hanyuma abapfiriye muri Kristo bazazuka mbere naho abazima basigaye bazakoranyirizwa mu bicu gusanganira Umwami wabo uhoraho mu kirere kandi gutyo bazatwarwa mu mutekano w'ijuru kandi bazahorana nawe iteka ryose bahindurwe gusa n'Umwami wabo uhoraho wo gukiranuka!

Nyamara, kuboneka kwanjye ntikuzakorwa mu ibanga nk'uko Bibiliya z'ikinyoma zigisha mu kwibeshya, cyangwa ngo hazabeho ibihe by'ikinyoma bizamanuka ku isi bitandukanya Isirayeli n'Itorero ryanjye ry'ukuri, kuko abizerwa muri Kristo Yesu ari bo Isirayeli y'Imana kandi bazafata imyanya yabo mu byiciro n'imiryango mu ijuru kandi ni umuntu umwe mushya. Uko niko byanditswe mu rwandiko rwa Pawulo rwandikiwe Abagalatiya: Kuko muri Kristo Yesu gukebwa nta cyo kumaze, cyangwa kudakebwa, ahubwo icy'ingenzi ni ukuba icyaremwe gishya. Kandi abazagendera kuri iryo tegeko bose, amahoro n'imbabazi bibe kuri bo, no ku Isirayeli y'Imana. Nuko rero, musobanukirwe ko ari jye uzatuma Isirayeli itazongera kuba ubwami bwigabanyijemo kabiri iteka ryose. Nuko rero, mwange ibinyoma by'abapfumu b'abaziyoni n'abigisha iby'ibihe kandi mwemere ukuri kwa Yeshua HaMashiach!

Bityo ndabaha gusobanukirwa ko ibihe n'izamurwa ry'ibanga ari ibitekerezo byahimbwe na Satani aho bashaka kugusha mu mutego aboroheje no gutwara abagwaneza n'abiyoroshya mu mutima bakoresheje amayeri n'imigani yahimbwe n'inkuru z'ibinyoma. Nyamara, kuboneka kwanjye kuzaba nk'uko byanditswe kandi kuzabonwa na bose kandi isi izamenya ko Kristo Yesu yabonetse nk'uko yasezeranyije abera be bizerwa, ndetse asiga abakazuyazi kugira ngo imibiri yabo y'icyaha yabafatiye mu cyaha irimburwe kandi niko bizakorwa.

Nyamara, abizerwa muri Kristo bazakoranywa nk'uko byanditswe mu byanditswe byera basange Umwami wabo uhoraho! Kandi sinzigera na rimwe nkwimwa umutungo wanjye niguze, kandi nzashikukuza ku mbaraga abanjye bose bizerwa, yaba abasinziriye mu nda y'isi cyangwa abari mu bazima ku maso y'isi: Bazakoranyirizwa aho ndi mu kirere kimwe. Nyamara, abaguye bazabeshya n'igitero cyabo cy'ibinyoma cy'ibivejuru aho bazavuga ko ibivejuru byatwaye abakoranyirijwe muri Kristo kandi intambara ikomeye izaduka ku isi, kandi abaguye bazabeshya inshuro nyinshi, gusa ntimuzizere ijambo na rimwe bavuga. Kuko gukoranya abizerwa muri Kristo bitangwa nk'ibyiringiro n'icyemezo cy'uko kugaruka kwanjye kwa kabiri kuri hafi kuza ku iherezo ry'icyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli, icyo kikaba ari icyumweru cy'imyaka irindwi aho buri munsi w'icyumweru ubarwa nk'umwaka.

Nyamara, abakazuyazi ntibazakomeza kuba abakazuyazi kuko ibihe byo gutandukanya kwa nyuma bizaba bidakumiriwe ubwo ukumira kwanjye kuzakurwaho kandi urubura rukagwa. Kandi muri ubwo buryo abakazuyazi bazafata ibyemezo byabo, aho bamwe bazashyuha cyane mu kwiyemeza kwabo no mu byizerwa byabo ku Mwami wabo Yesu Kristo, naho abandi bazakonja nk'urubura rugiye kugwira mu nyanja, bahitamo kurimbuka kwabo kw'iteka.

Nyamara, Jyewe, Kristo Yesu, nzakongeza umwiyemezo udashobora gutsindwa muri bo bose bahisemo ubugingo buhoraho muri Yesu w'i Nazareti kandi nzaba narabahaye umwiyemezo udashobora kuvunika wo kwambaza Umwami wabo Yesu kugeza ku iherezo, kandi gutyo bazagera ku ngororano yabo y'iteka aho imibabaro idakwiriye kugereranywa n'uburemere bw'iteka n'ubwiza bw'ingororano nyinshi z'iteka zihabwa abanesheje; kandi nzatuma abaneshi banjye banesha nk'Umwami wabo, ndetse n'igihe nanesheje abaguye mu buryo nk'ubwo mu gihe cy'igitambo cyanjye nk'Umwana w'Intama w'Imana.

Kandi niko bizaba, ko benshi bashoboraga guha imitima yabo Umwami wabo Yesu Kristo kugeza ku iherezo ahubwo bazakonja nk'urubura ku Mwami wabo uhoraho kandi mu buryo bubabaje bazatera umugongo ubugingo buhoraho bajye mu muriro w'iteka w'igihano kuko bazaba baranze Yesu w'i Nazareti byuzuye kubera ibinezeza by'icyaha kandi bazahitamo kubabazwa kwabo kw'iteka mu gihe bashoboraga guhitamo ibyishimo by'iteka imbere ya Yahweh iteka ryose. Kandi benshi bazaba barigeze kugira amahitamo y'iteka yo kuba Abana b'Imana iteka ryose ahubwo bazatera umugongo bifatanye n'abavumwe bagenda bazagwa iteka mu rupfu rwa kabiri.

Nyamara, bose bafite uburenganzira bwo guhitamo, nuko rero bamwe bazahitamo mu bwenge kandi benshi bazahitamo mu bupfu. Nuko rero mwinjirire mu irembo rifunganye: kuko irembo ari rigari, n'inzira ijyana kurimbuka ari ngari: kandi abayinyuramo ni benshi. Kuko irembo rifunganye, n'inzira ijyana mu bugingo ari ntoya, kandi abayibona ni bake ijyana iteka mu buzima bwo gukiranuka kudashira kandi nta yandi mahitamo ahari. Nuko rero, muhitemo ubugingo kugira ngo mwe n'urubyaro rwanyu mubone kubaho!

Nyamara, nje kubaburira, Yewe Isirayeli, kugira ngo bose bumve amabwiriza yanjye kandi bite ku miburo yanjye ndetse n'abari hafi bose n'abari kure bose batataniye mu mahanga kuko amahitamo amwe yo gutandukanya kwa nyuma asigariye abakomoka kuri Adamu bose batuye ku isi uko ishyanga, cyangwa umuryango, cyangwa ururimi bavukiyemo byaba biri kose. Kandi niko bizaba ko ubwo abizerwa banjye muri Kristo bazakurwa ku isi bakajyanwa mu ijuru kugira ngo bahishwe umujinya uzaza kandi bagororerwe byuzuye kubera gusoza isiganwa ryabo mu kumvira Kristo Yesu Umwami wabo wazutse akazamuka mu ijuru; ko isi izapfukamiswa mu kutizera kutagira ukwizera mu kuboneka kwanjye no kurimbuka gutunguranye kuzakurikira.

Ndetse n'ubwo bimeze bityo, kuko nzerekana ubwiza bwanjye ntabyitezwe mu kuboneka kwanjye gutunguranye mu bicu kandi umucyo wanjye uzamurikira ikirere kandi bose bazabona ubwiza bw'Umwami bwuzuye ibirere mu mucyo w'ubwiza kandi benshi bazatwarwa gitungura, kandi isi izadandabirana inyeganyege kandi ihinde umushyitsi imbere ya Kristo Yesu, uwo mu mucyo we benshi bazashaka guhisha amaso yabo. Kuko nk'uko umurabyo urabira iburasirazuba, ukabonekera n'iburengerazuba, niko no kuza k'Umwana w'umuntu kuzaba.

Kandi imbaga nyamwinshi, ndetse n'abizerwa muri Kristo, bazabura gitungura kandi ntabyitezwe nta gace basize ku isi, kuko ubuzima bwabo ku isi mu mubiri wangirika burangiye kandi isiganwa ryabo bazaba bararishoje. Nuko rero, bazatwarwa mu bwiza bw'ijuru, aho bazahabwa ubwiza iteka ryose kandi bagororerwe iteka ryose. Dore! Bazahindurwa gusa n'Umwami wabo uhoraho wo Gukiranuka, kandi bazambikwa imyenda yera y'igitare itagira inenge idashobora kwanduzwa cyangwa kwangirika. Bazahabwa ingororano nyinshi kubera ubudahemuka bwabo n'imirimo yo gukiranuka bakoze mu izina ryanjye, ndetse n'imirimo nabahaye gukora ku bw'agakiza kabo, inyungu, n'icyubahiro.

Kandi benshi bazatwara ingororano zimeze nk'izitarinjira byuzuye mu mitima y'abantu muri iki gihe. Nubwo, bamwe bazahomba ubwo imirimo yabo izatwikwa n'umuriro w'utunganya kugira ngo umurama wose ukurwe mu buzima bwabo mu isuku y'iteka kandi kugira ngo ikizinga cy'imirimo mibi kibaveho iteka ryose. Musobanukirwe, ko nta n'umwe muri mwe wagendeye imbere yanjye mu buryo butunganye, nuko rero, bamwe bazahomba, bamwe bazahomba kandi bahabwe ingororano, kandi bamwe bazahabwa ingororano ari bo baneshi banjye bazaba baranesheje nk'uko nanesheje abaguye kandi nicaye iburyo bwa Yahweh ubwe, ndetse na NDIHO ukomeye kandi uhoraho. Nyamara, abazatwarwa n'abazakoranywa bose bazagira ubugingo buhoraho iteka ryose kandi bazabaho ubuzima bwo gukiranuka bw'iteka ryose kuko ari ko bagenewe.

Mu buryo nk'ubwo, nk'uko abizerwa batwarwa, niko n'abasigaye inyuma bazasobanukirwa uburemere bw'ibibazo byabo kandi imbaga nyamwinshi izemera ukuri n'ibigeragezo bagomba kwihanganira, kandi benshi bazarira kandi baboroge mu kwihana kandi benshi bazabaho ubuzima bwo kwihana bw'urukundo n'ibitambo, kandi benshi bazabura ubuzima bwabo mu rukundo no gukorera bagenzi babo bazaba bari mu bihe bikomeye na bo kandi imbaga nyamwinshi izaza aho ndi yambaza izina rya Yesu Kristo ari we Mwami wabo w'iteka kugeza ku iherezo!

Kandi isi izadandabirana inyeganyege, kandi kurimbuka gukomeye kuzaduka ku isi, nk'intambara itunguranye n'icyorezo, n'akarengane, n'inzara n'urupfu bizateza kurimbuka gukomeye ku isi kandi isi izahindurwa imiterere yayo y'ubu n'imitingito myinshi ikomeye kandi benshi bazarimbuka gitungura kandi nta muti. Kandi nyamara abandi benshi bazasigara ari bazima kandi bazarokoka amakuba agiye kuza kandi benshi bazasigirwa ibigeragezo by'umubabaro wa Yakobo byaje ku isi kugerageza abatuye ku isi, ndetse no gukiza abazaza mu gakiza muri Yeshua HaMashiach bose.

Nuko rero, musobanukirwe ko hariho isarura ryinshi kuri Adamu. Isarura ry'umuganura wa sayiri ni iry'abizerwa banjye muri Kristo, ndetse n'abagenze mu budahemuka binyuze mu mwuka wanjye mu bihe byanjye by'ubuntu n'imbabazi bose ndetse n'abejeje imbuto z'umwuka mu buzima bwabo binyuze mu kumvira Amagambo yanjye ubwo bagenderaga mu mwuka wanjye wo kuba abana aho bashyiriweho ikimenyetso kugeza ku munsi ukomeye wo gucungurwa. Aba ni abizerwa muri Kristo kandi ni n'abaneshi banjye n'umugeni wanjye n'abanjye 144,000 nzohereza gukiza Yakobo ari muzima mu kuboko kwa Ashkenazi na Esawu.

Hanyuma hazatangira isarura ry'ingano aho ingano zeze mu mirima kandi zikunama mu kumvira kubera uburemere bw'imbuto zera, naho urumamfu rutagira imbuto ruzahagarara rwemye mu busuzuguro kandi guhura gukomeye kuzabaho, kandi ingano zanjye zizakoranyirizwa mu bugingo buhoraho naho urumamfu ruzahambirwa mu miba, rubohwe, kandi rutwikirwe mu muriro w'iteka w'igihano. Nuko rero musobanukirwe ko abazanga gusarurwa mu kumvira mu guhura kwanjye bose bazifatanya n'uru rumamfu rubi mu murage w'abaguye w'ibirimi by'umuriro by'iteka byakorewe by'umwihariko abatumvira n'abigomeke, kandi ntibazarokoka. Kandi niko abatemera n'abatagira ukwizera bazagenda banze amasezerano y'amahoro ya Yahweh, ari we Yesu w'i Nazareti wari kubakiza rwose.

Uko niko iherezo ry'ubwami bwa Satani rizaba, bene wacu, ndetse n'igihe cyo gutandukanya n'isarura kibika abakiranutsi iteka ryose kandi kigaha abakiranirwa igihano cy'iteka mu murage w'abaguye, ndetse n'iherezo ryabikiwe abakomeza mu kutumvira bose n'abakomeza mu kwigomeka kwa Satani, n'Abarinzi, n'abakurikira inzira zabo mbi bose. Abo bazarwanya Umwana w'Intama, kandi Umwana w'Intama azabanesha: kuko ari Umwami w'abami, n'Umutware w'abatware: n'abari ku ruhande rwe, bahamagawe, kandi batoranyijwe, kandi bizerwa.

Nuko rero, musobanukirwe ko mu ivu ry'intambara bazazamuka, ndetse n'inyamaswa yo mu nyanja, inyamaswa yo mu isi, n'ikiyoka! Bazazamuka mu ivu ryo kurimbuka gukomeye bo ubwabo bateje. Nyamara, bazashyira amakosa yo kurimbuka kwabo bateje ku bivejuru by'abanzi, n'Abakristo, n'ibintu byose byerekeye Yesu w'i Nazareti, kandi gutyo Kristo azagirwa ikivume mu bwami bwabo bwa Satani. Ni ukuri, ni ukuri, Jyewe, Kristo Yesu nta mugabane cyangwa uruhare mfitanye na bo na gato, nyamara abazambaza izina ry'Umwami wabo Yesu kugeza ku iherezo bose bazakizwa.

Nuko bazarwanya abera kandi bazabanesha n'ubugome bukomeye buzabasubiraho nta mbabazi iteka ryose. Ndetse n'ubwo bimeze bityo, imbaga nyamwinshi y'amateraniro izakizwa kurimbuka kw'iteka binyuze mu kurimbuka kw'imibiri yabo y'icyaha yabagize imbata z'icyaha. Kandi gutyo nzakura ibinezeza byose ku isi kandi nzavuna amarozi n'ubupfumu umubi yabashyizeho kugira ngo bashobore gukanguka mu bitotsi byabo babeho mu gukiranuka imbere yanjye ubwo bazaba bavuye mu cyaha mu kwihana kwuzuye no kwanga ubwami bwa Satani, ubwo mbere bashyigikiraga mu kwemera mu kutumvira.

Nyamara, uko ni ko abaguye baziyerekana biyita ibivejuru byo mu yindi si y'ibinyoma yizengurutsa itabaho mu buryo bumwe n'uko abatoza babo b'Abayezuwiti batoje rubanda binyuze mu kwiga kurwanya ukuri. Kugira ngo ibinyoma by'abaguye byizerwe mu kwaya gukomeye n'abishimira gukiranirwa. Kandi uko ni ko urubuga ruteguriwe abo bayobya ngo biyerekane kuko bose ari abiyoberanya biyita icyo batari cyo.

Baziyita abafasha beza baje gukiza Adamu kurimbuka kwe, uko kurimbuka mu by'ukuri, bazaba biyerekanye kugira ngo babifashemo mu bwihisho bukomeye no mu buriganya bwose ndetse n'ibimenyetso n'ibitangaza byinshi by'ibinyoma. Kuko bazazana ikoranabuhanga ryabo n'amayeri yabo n'ibizira byabo kandi ikuzimu hazakingurwa rwose. Ikibi ntikizongera gukumirwa kandi ubwoko bwose bw'ikibi buzategeka isi mu gihe cy'amakuba ya Yakobo. Ndetse kuko umuntu wa kamere yifuza ikibi n'umwijima cyane, ku buryo Yahweh azarekurira umuntu wa kamere rwose mu bwami bwa Satani kugira ngo benshi bashobore gukizwa, ndetse nk'abakuwe mu muriro. Kandi ibi ni ukubera ko umuntu ari mubi n'imirimo ye ari mibi, icyo kibi umuntu agikunda mu kwigomeka kwe ari kumwe na Satani. Ku bw'ibyo, abantu bazarekurirwa Satani rwose, we wamaze gutegura iherezo ry'iteka rya Adamu, nubwo imigambi yabo izahinduka ubusa kandi bakazabona gusa urubanza rukomeye kandi ruteye ubwoba kuko ari rwo bakwiriye.

Bityo bazagaragara bafite ibisubizo n'ikoranabuhanga ryabo mu ntoki kandi bazagaragara biyita ibivejuru byo mu yindi si y'ibinyoma itabaho. Kandi Baraba azahaguruka, ndetse n'inyamaswa yo mu isi kandi izahaguruka ifite kurumwa n'inzoka mu ntoki. Kandi izategeka bose, abato n'abakuru, abakire n'abakene, abidegemvya n'abaretwa, gushyirwaho ikimenyetso ku kuboko kwabo kw'iburyo cyangwa mu ruhanga rwabo. Kandi kugira ngo hatagira umuntu numwe ushobora kugura cyangwa kugurisha, keretse ufite icyo kimenyetso cyangwa izina ry'iyo nyamaswa cyangwa umubare w'izina ryayo. Hano hari ubwenge. Ufite ubwenge, abare umubare w'inyamaswa: kuko ari umubare w'umuntu, kandi umubare we ni magana atandatu na mirongo itandatu na gatandatu.

Ku bw'ibyo, musobanukirwe ibyanditswe n'ibyo Umwuka wa Yahweh atangaza yeruye muri ibi bihe by'imperuka bene wacu kugira ngo musobanukirwe akaga k'ibihe murimo: Umuntu wese naramuka aramije inyamaswa n'igishushanyo cyayo, agashyirwaho ikimenyetso cyayo mu ruhanga rwe, cyangwa ku kuboko kwe, uwo na we azanywa ku nzoga y'umujinya w'Imana, yego, inzoga ivanze idafunguye, isutswe mu gikombe cy'umujinya wayo, kandi azababazwa mu muriro n'amazuku imbere y'Abamarayika bera, n'imbere y'Umwana w'Intama. Kandi umwotsi wo kubabazwa kwabo uzacumba iteka ryose: kandi ntibazaruhuka ku manywa na nijoro, abaramya inyamaswa n'izina ryayo.

Nyamara, bazahaguruka uko byagenda kose, ndetse n'inyamaswa yo mu nyanja n'inyamaswa yo mu isi, n'ikiyoka kandi bazahaguruka mu butegetsi bwabo butagatifu kandi bazategeka isi mu gukiranirwa n'ibibi kandi nta kizakumirwa mu byifuzo by'imitima yabo mibi. Kandi bityo bazasezeranya ibintu byinshi bikomeye kandi by'imbaraga. Kandi bazatwikira banarimbishe kurumwa n'inzoka mu buriganya bukomeye kandi bafite ibinyoma byinshi n'amasezerano y'ibinyoma bazabwira abantu ku byerekeye ubu burozi ku buryo nta muntu numwe ushobora kubucika namara kubufata. Ku bw'ibyo, abazafata ikimenyetso cy'inyamaswa bose bazaba bihinduye iteka ryose, ndetse impinduka badashobora gucika kuko igoramye idashobora kugororwa.

Kandi bityo bazasezeranya ubugingo buhoraho, kandi bazasezeranya ubuzima bwiza butunganye no kutagira indwara kandi bazasezeranya ubwumvikane butunganye binyuze mu ikoranabuhanga ryabo bazavuga ko rigamije amahoro, n'ubuzima bwiza no gukiza. Kandi abazafata kurumwa n'inzoka bose bazahindurwa iteka ryose bave mu ishusho ya Yahweh baremweyo, bityo bihindure abavumwe bagenda bazaba abacakara iteka ryose by'irari ry'ikiyoka. Kuko mu by'ukuri ikimenyetso cy'inyamaswa gitangirwa kurimbuka kwa Adamu kugira ngo abaguye bashobore kugira igihe cyabo cya zahabu ku isi bonyine hamwe n'ibizira byabo kandi badafite Adamu banga. Ndetse imigambi idashoboka gushyirwa mu bikorwa kuko nta gihe cya zahabu bazagira ahubwo bazabona urubanza rukomeye kandi ruteye ubwoba.

Ni ukuri, ni ukuri, mu kwemera kurumwa n'inzoka bazaba batanze ubushake bwabo bwisanzuye bagandukira ikiyoka kuko bazivanga n'urubyaro rw'abantu: ariko ntibazafatana, nk'uko icyuma kidashobora kuvangwa n'ibumba. Nyamara baziyihindura uko byagenda kose binyuze mu kurumwa n'inzoka n'ikoranabuhanga ririmo. Kandi bazisanga bometse ku rusobe rw'ibintu by'ikoranabuhanga bizaza, ndetse aho bazagenzura bakanayobya abacakara babo kugira ngo bakore ibyo ikiyoka gishaka mu kumvira uko bagendera mu ntekerezo imwe y'agatsiko k'abazombi badashobora gucika kandi abazaba bafashwe muri uwo mutego bose bazagenzurwa n'imirongo yabo n'amashusho yabo mu buryo bwuzuye.

Nyamara, ikiyoka kizi ko gisigaranye igihe gito kandi mu by'ukuri, igihe gito kandi cyagabanyijwe ni cyo gusa ikiyoka gifite. Abemera kurumwa n'inzoka, ndetse n'ikimenyetso cy'inyamaswa bazisanga bagambaniwe vuba cyane kandi bazasobanukirwa vuba cyane ko bagambaniwe n'amasezerano menshi y'ibinyoma, nyamara bazaba abacakara bo gukora irari ry'ikiyoka kandi bazakora ibibi byinshi kuko bazaba bagenzurwa kandi bategekwa n'ikoranabuhanga ryabo ku buryo gucika bizaba bidashoboka.

Kandi uko ni ko ubuzima bw'abanga Yesu Kristo muri ubwo buryo buzaba kuko kwinjira mu muryango wabo bizasaba gufata ikimenyetso cyabo cy'inyamaswa kandi ababikora bose ntibazigera bababarirwa kandi ntibazacika. Kuko igihe cyo kugira ngo abakiranutsi batandukanywe rwose n'abakiranirwa kigeze, uko gukiranirwa abakiranutsi bakuzinukwa. Kandi ni igihe cy'uko abatizera, n'abatagira kwizera binjira rwose mu nzego z'abigomeke kuko abanga Yeshua HaMashiach bose nta yandi mahitamo bafite kuko abanga n'abanga guhitamo bose, ndetse na byo binyuze mu kutumvira kwabo k'ubwigomeke no kwanga kwabo bazaba bahisemo byanze bikunze ya nyanja y'umuriro yaka iteka ryose irimo amazuku agurumana.

Bityo bizaba ko abafashe kurumwa n'inzoka bakiyegurira guhindurwa kw'ingirabuzimafatizo bazaba igice cyuzuye cy'urusobe rwabo rw'ibintu by'ikoranabuhanga bizaza kandi bazaba bishyize mu maboko y'abashaka kubarimbura rwose, ndetse n'abafata Adamu nk'inyamaswa ishobora kwinjirirwa. Nyamara, umuryango wabo mushya uzaba uwo kuyoba gukomeye n'ibinyoma aho ukuri kutazaboneka.
