Ku Baguye: Noneho Urubanza Rwanyu Ruraje!Inkuba ya 1 n'iya 2 (6 Kamena 2024)

1Kuva kuri Yeshua HaMashiach: Inkuba ya Mbere!

Mwa bahamagawe banjye, batoranyijwe, kandi bizerwa, mwumve Amagambo y'Umwami wanyu muzima n'umukiza, ndetse Yeshua Mesiya! Amahanga mutuyemo n'amateka mwigishijwe n'abarezi banyu b'aba Yezuwiti ni ishusho y'ikinyoma gikomeye, guhishurwa kwacyo kwuzuye kuregereje kandi kwamaze gutangira, aho abaguye bazagaragara nk'ibivejuru byiza ariko mu by'ukuri ari abamarayika baguye babi. Icyabayeho ni iki? Ni cyo kizabaho: kandi icyakozwe ni iki? Icyo kizakorwa: kandi nta kintu gishya munsi y'izuba.

Satani yatanze ubumenyi bwe bwitwa ko buteye imbere kuva mu ntangiriro, ndetse n'ubumenyi bwe bw'icyiza n'ikibi. Ni umushukanyi mukuru n'umunyabinyoma wigishije abantu ibintu byinshi Yahweh atari yagennye, kuko izo ari inzira z'urupfu. Ibyaremwe bya Yahweh ntibyaremewe gukoreshwa mu gushaka ububasha n'inyungu by'umuntu ku giti cye cyangwa nk'uburyo bwo guhunga urubanza rw'Imana, cyangwa gushyiraho inzira n'amategeko by'umuntu. Kuko Satani yononnye abantu kuva mu ntangiriro, kandi yononnye abarinzi baguye bifatanyije mu kwigomeka kwabo.

Yewe mwanzi mukuru w'igikiranuka cyose, ndetse namwe ba Satani: ibyaha byanyu bya mbere byanditswe na Henoki umwanditsi icyo gihe cyose gishize, kandi bihari kugeza n'uyu munsi, kuko ibyaha byanyu byanditswe bityo. Kandi nuko rero nincamake, kuko mwakoze ibi bintu, kandi mwakoze n'ibindi byinshi: Mwayobeje abana b'Imana bose, mubamanura ku isi, mubayobesha binyuze mu bakobwa b'abantu. Mwahaye abana b'Imana bera inama mbi, murabayobya kugira ngo banduze imibiri yabo n'abakobwa b'abantu. Mweretse abana b'abantu ibikomere byose by'urupfu, muyobya Eva, mwereka abana b'abantu intwaro z'urupfu: ingabo n'ikoti ry'icyuma, n'inkota y'intambara, n'intwaro zose z'urupfu ku bana b'abantu. Kandi bivuye mu kuboko kwanyu byibasiye abatuye mu isi kuva uwo munsi n'iteka ryose.

Mwa ba Satani mwigishije abana b'abantu ibisharira n'ibiryohereye, kandi mubigisha amabanga yose y'ubwenge bwabo. Mwigishije abantu kwandikisha wino n'urupapuro, bityo benshi baracumura kuva kera kugeza kera n'uyu munsi. Kuko abantu bataremewe iyo mpamvu, kugira ngo bemeze kwizera kwabo kwiza bakoresheje ikaramu na wino. Kuko abantu baremwe kimwe n'abamarayika, kugira ngo bakomeze kuba abera n'abakiranutsi; kandi urupfu, rurimbura byose, ntirwari kubafata, ariko binyuze muri ubu bumenyi bararimbuka, kandi binyuze muri ubu bubasha buratsemba abantu.

Mwa ba Satani mweretse abana b'abantu gukubita kubi kwose kw'imyuka n'abadayimoni, no gukubita urusoro mu nda, kugira ngo ruvemo, no gukubita ubugingo, kurumwa n'inzoka, no gukubita kubaho binyuze mu bushyuhe bwa saa sita, umwana w'inzoka witwa Tabââ'ĕt. Kuko mwasabye na Mikayeli kubereka izina ryihishe, kugira ngo murivuge mu ndahiro, kugira ngo abo bahinde umushyitsi imbere y'iryo zina n'indahiro bahishuriye abana b'abantu ibyari mu ibanga byose.

Kuko mwatumye abarinzi bifatanya mu kwigomeka kwanyu uko mwakomezaga kwangiza abantu. Kandi nuko ibyaha by'abarinzi byanditswe bityo na Henoki umwanditsi, kandi nincamake mu buryo bukurikira: Bigishije abantu gukora inkota, n'ibyuma, n'ingabo, n'ibyuma bikingira igituza, babamenyesha ibyuma byo mu isi n'ubuhanga bwo kubikora, n'ibikomo, n'imitako, n'ikoreshwa rya antimuani, no gukura ibitsike, n'amabuye y'agaciro y'amoko yose, n'amabara yose. Bigishije uburozi, no gukata imizi, gukemura uburozi, kuragurisha inyenyeri, amatsinda y'inyenyeri, ubumenyi bw'ibicu, ibimenyetso by'isi, ibimenyetso by'izuba, n'inzira y'ukwezi.

Kandi nuko binyuze mu kwangiza kwanyu n'ibinyoma byanyu, mwanduje ibyaremwe bya Yahweh n'inzira zanyu mbi n'ibizira bituka Imana. Mwayobeje abantu kandi mucumura kuri Yahweh, kandi mwarenganyije abantu, ndetse mucumura no ku nyoni, n'inyamaswa, n'ibikururuka, n'amafi, kandi ibyaha byanyu bizana urubanza rukomeye mutazakira. Mwanduje ibyaremwe bya Yahweh n'ikoranabuhanga ryanyu n'amayeri yanyu, kandi mwatumye abantu batangazwa n'amayeri yanyu n'ibinyoma byanyu. Kuko Yahweh yaberetse gusa amabanga y'ubusa y'ibyaremwe bye kandi abika amabanga ye y'agaciro kenshi mu mutima we kure y'ubuhemu bwanyu.

Mwa babi mwe mwigishije inzira zanyu z'ubusa ku bantu uko bororokaga ku isi, kandi mwazanye amakuba akomeye n'irimbuka binyuze mu migambi yanyu mbi n'ikoranabuhanga. Kuko mutazanywe n'ikindi uretse kwiba, kwica, no kurimbura. Mwigishije kuyobora ikirere, kuko muri umutware w'ububasha bw'ikirere, kuko muyobora aithēr. Kuko igihe Kayini yacumuraga, yubatse umujyi; kandi mwigishije abantu amabanga yanyu y'ubusa n'amayeri yanyu. Iyi mijyi yakoreshejwe mu kugenzura no kuyobora no kuzana irimbuka ry'abantu, uko mwayikoresheje mu gukora ibizira byanyu by'irimbuka.

Mwatumye ishyanga rihagurukira irindi shyanga mu ntambara n'amakimbirane adashira, kandi mwatumye ubwoko bwanjye bucumura, kandi Yahweh yatatanyije Imiryango ya Isirayeli mu miyaga ine. Nta kibazo, umugambi wanyu mubi wateje imbere imigambi ya Yahweh, kuko mu gutatana kwabo ari bwo gukoranya intama zisigaye kuzagerwaho. Kandi nzohereza abatangabuhamya banjye babiri guhanura iminsi 1260 bambaye ibigunira. Mu mbaraga zanyu zose zo kurimbura abana ba Yahweh mwatsinzwe bikomeye, kandi ntimwashoboraga kubona ko imbaraga zanyu zateje imbere imigambi ya Yahweh, kuko n'abanzi be bumvira ijwi rye.

Kuko mwatoteje kandi mugatesha umutwe imiryango 12 ku isi yose uko mwashakaga kuyirimbura. Mwibye abana ba ba nyina, mutwara abana mu miryango mubimurira hirya no hino ku isi, hanyuma murababeshya mutuma bizera imigani y'aba Yezuwiti ku byerekeye amateka yabo nyakuri, imigambi, n'inkomoko yabo. Nta kibazo, mwatsinzwe, kuko njyewe, Yeshua HaMashiach, nzabahuza byuzuye n'amateka yabo, ishyanga n'umurage wabo nyakuri, kandi nzaha abazaragwa agakiza bose kuba umuntu mushya umwe, ndetse muri njye, Kristo Yesu.

Binyuze mu mayeri n'irimbuka rikomeye, mwahishe neza ikoranabuhanga ryanyu, ndetse mubeshya ko abantu bo hambere bari abinjiji kandi nta koranabuhanga bari bafite. Kuko mweretse aba kera amabanga yanyu y'ubusa, kandi nta kintu gishya munsi y'izuba. Kandi nuko muhisha ukuri ku byerekeye amayeri yanyu ku byaremwe, ndetse mubihisha abo mwagashutse n'ikinyoma cyanyu gikomeye. Mwongeye kuzana amayeri yanyu y'ubumaji nanone, uko mwateguye urubuga kugira ngo mwiyoberanye nk'ibivejuru. Kandi binyuze mu gitero cyanyu cy'ibivejuru by'ibihimbano muzatuma bose batangazwa n'ibimenyetso byanyu by'ibinyoma n'ibitangaza, ndetse n'abo amazina yabo atanditswe mu Gitabo cy'Ubugingo kuva ku kuremwa kw'isi. Ariko njyewe, Yeshua HaMashiach, nzabashyira ahagaragara byimazeyo, kandi buri banga ryihishe rizajya ahagaragara.

Dore! Nzatwara abizerwa banjye mu ijuru, kuko nje gutwara Umugeni wanjye kandi sinzahagarikwa. Kandi njyewe, Yeshua HaMashiach, nje guca urubanza no kurwana intambara no kuzana urubanza rukomeye ku isi, kandi ntimuzarokoka.

Njyewe, Yeshua HaMashiach, Umwami w'Abami n'Umutware w'Abatware, nohereje aya magambo binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye, kandi binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye amagambo yanjye yoherejwe, kandi byose bizasohora.

2Kuva kuri Yahweh, Imana Ikomeye Ihoraho: Inkuba ya Kabiri!

Yewe mwa ba Satani, mwashutse ibyaremwe byanjye gukurikira ibimenyetso byanyu by'ibinyoma n'ibitangaza, kandi umujinya wanjye urabaka. Ubwoko bwanjye buzatandukanywa byimazeyo n'urumamfu rwanyu mwabibye mu ngano zanjye mu busitani bwanjye bw'ubuntu; kandi Umwana wanjye, Kristo Yesu, azakoranya ingano zanjye mu kigega cyanjye, kandi nuko Umwami w'isarura azakoranya, abohere kandi atwike urumamfu rwanyu. Urubanza rukomeye ruri kuri mwe, kandi ntimuzarokoka ukuboko kwanjye. Kuva mu ntangiriro, mwakambakambye kandi mwihisha mu bana banjye, ndetse mwangiza abato banjye kuva mu ntangiriro cyane n'ibinyoma byanyu n'ubushukanyi.

Ntimuri imana, kandi n'abakurikiye ibinyoma byanyu n'ibishuko by'intsinzi na bo si imana. Ntimushobora kurema, kuko mubuze ububasha, kandi sinigeze mbaremana ubwo bushobozi kuko nari nzi ko muzigomeka mbere y'uko muremwa; kandi mu butabera bwanjye n'urukundo rwanjye nabaremye mu gukiranuka, kandi mwari batunganye ku munsi mwaremweho. Mu bwibone bwanyu bw'ubusa mwirata gukomera kwanyu, ariko nta gukomera mufite, kandi nzabashyira ahagaragara byimazeyo nk'abatekamutwe muri bo. Nzabashyira imbere y'Abami, kandi bazababona n'ukuntu nta bubasha mufite. Umwana wanjye, Yeshua HaMashiach, yabanetshe byimazeyo i Kaluvari, kandi nta byiringiro mufite.

Mwemererwa gukora gusa ibyo nemeye, kuko muri umwanzi watsinzwe. Mwibye icyangombwa cy'isi mu mayeri yanyu n'ibinyoma kuri Adamu, kandi none umwana wanjye w'ikinege, ndetse Yeshua HaMashiach, yabanetshe mu rukundo rwe kuri njye n'ibyaremwe byanjye; kandi isi ni iye, ndetse Umwami w'Abami n'Umutware w'Abatware, kuko nabitangaje. Kuko yaguze isi n'abantu n'amaraso ye atagira icyaha, kandi abahisemo ubugingo bazacungurwa. Imirimo yanyu yose y'ubusa izasenywa, kandi abahisemo ubugingo bose bazasohoka mu binyoma byanyu n'ubushukanyi binjire mu bugingo bushya, kandi nzaha abizerwa ubwiza buruta ubwo mwari mufite kera. Kuko mwatsinzwe, yewe batware b'isi banyantege nke kandi b'abasabirizi, ndetse na buri mutware mu nzego zanyu yatsindanywe namwe, yewe nzoka ikambakamba, inyabinyoma.

Noneho hageze igihe cy'Urubanza rwanyu n'irimbuka ryimazeyo ry'ubwami bwanyu, ndetse ubwami mwubatse mu mbaraga zanyu zo kugerageza kumpisha, ndetse Yahweh, ku byaremwe byanjye. Ntimuzarokoka. Ntimuzongera kwinjira mu ijuru ukundi, kuko mwajugunywe ku isi mutazigera mugaruka, kandi umwanya wanyu ntukiri mu ijuru. Muboshywe ku isi mwararikiye, itari iyanyu ngo muyifate. Buri murimo wanyu wose uzasenywa mu buzima bw'abo mwagerageje kurimbura (no ku isi ubwayo); bazatura iteka mu mahoro n'umutekano, kandi bazagira ibyishimo byuzuye, kandi nzatura mu bantu kandi nzaba Imana yabo.

Ariko wowe, yewe ukambakamba, nta bubasha ufite mu maso yanjye, kandi nta cyo wakora ngo umpagarike. Mu minsi itandatu kuva kugwa k'umuntu, kuko umunsi umwe ari imyaka igihumbi mu maso yanjye, nta cyo mwagezeho uretse urubanza rwanyu bwite, kuvumwa, kurimbuka, n'umubabaro kuri mwe n'ababakurikira bose. Kuko muzabohwa kandi mukajugunywa muri rya ziba rinini ryaka umuriro aho nabakoreye ibirimi by'umuriro bishyushye cyane, ndetse ibirimi by'umuriro by'ubururu byiza, kandi muzashya iteka, kandi nzabitura buri murimo mubi wose mwigeze gukora, inshuro nyinshi. Mwishe imbaga nyamwinshi ku zindi, kandi muzishyura buri bugingo bwose bwatakaye, kandi ntimuzigera murokoka. Muzamenya ibyaha byose mwakoze, kandi ntimuzigera murokoka urubanza, kandi muzababazwa iteka kubera ibyaha mwakoze. Muzamenya neza ibyaha byanyu n'amakosa yanyu mu mubabaro wanyu, kandi abo mwanga bazabatsinda bya burundu. Kandi abakiranirwa bose mwigeze gushuka ngo bacumure kandi biyemeze kwifatanya mu kwigomeka kwanyu, ndetse n'abo amazina yabo atigeze yandikwa mu Gitabo cy'Ubugingo, bazasangira namwe umubabaro wanyu. Mwese muzishyura buri cyaha cyose mwakoze, kuko mwacumuye kuri Yahweh no ku byaremwe byanjye, kandi nzishyura. Kuko mu butabera bwanjye nzitura ibyaha byanyu bwite, ibikorwa, n'inzira mbi byimazeyo mu bituza byanyu inshuro nyinshi kandi iteka ryose. Kandi njyewe, Yahweh, ndetse Imana ikomeye ihoraho, ndahiye ibi bintu ku izina ryanjye bwite, kuko nta wukomeye kuruta uwo nshobora kurahirira.

Muzakorera imigambi yanjye, kandi mu rwango rwanyu n'irimbuka muzazana abatoranyijwe banjye kuri njye; kuko nzabayobora mu kuboko kwa Yeshua HaMashiach n'ingabo ze z'abagabo b'abanyambaraga, kuko bazakoranya intama zanjye zisigaye. (Dore! Abantu 144,000 boherejwe na Yeshua HaMashiach bazakora imirimo ikomeye imbere y'ikibi cyanyu kizira.) Kandi nta n'umwe nageneye ubugingo buhoraho uzagwa mu migambi yanyu, kuko batashobora gukurwa mu kuboko kwanjye, kandi nta n'ushobora kubakura mu maboko y'Umwana wanjye: Kuko namuhaye izina riruta amazina yose, kandi aje mu bubasha bwa Yahweh ubwe, kuko nasangiye intebe yanjye y'ubwami na we. Kandi azamenagura umutwe wanyu byimazeyo. Mwatsinzwe, gatware gato, kandi igihe cyanyu kigeze.

Njyewe, Yahweh, Imana ikomeye ihoraho, nohereje aya magambo binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye, kandi binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye amagambo yanjye yoherejwe, kandi azasohora. Kuko Yahweh, Imana ikomeye ihoraho, yabitangaje bityo, kandi nta gishobora kumena amatangazo yanjye. NDI El Shaddai!


Ibihe ByagezeImirabyo 3 & 4 (16 Gicurasi 2024)

3Kuva kuri Yeshua HaMashiach: Umurabyo wa Gatatu

Dore! Ibihe byo gutandukanya byegereje ku musozo, kandi kugaragara kwanjye kwenda kugera ubu. Kuri hafi cyane ndetse n'abamarayika n'ingabo zo mu ijuru bari ku ijwi ry'impanda. Naramabuye amaboko yanjye ku isi igiye gupfa mu bihe byinshi bya yubile ubu, kandi benshi bemeye amasezerano y'amahoro Data, Yahweh, yoherereje abantu, ndetse nanjye, Kristo Yesu, kuko ndi amahoro yanyu. Nyamara abandi benshi banze icyifuzo cyanjye n'ubuntu bwa Data n'imbabazi byasutswe ku isi.

Abashyizweho ikimenyetso n'abacunguwe ni abatoranyijwe kuva ku kuremwa kw'isi. Kuko Data yabamenye kuva kera kose kugeza kera kose, kandi mwahoze mu rwagati rw'umutima we, nk'uko nanjye nahoze mu rwagati rw'umutima we, kuko njyewe, Yesu w'i Nazareti, ndi umwana w'ikinege wa Yahweh. Kandi nyamara binyuze mu mirimo yanjye yuzuye nk'Pasika itunganye no binyuze mu kumvira k'umwana w'ikinege, abana b'Imana benshi, benshi bongeye kuvuka mu buzima bw'iteka ryose, ndetse bakuwe mu bana b'abantu, kuko ndi Umwana w'Umuntu!

Ibi bihe by'ubuntu byari ibanga rihishe mu mutima wa Yahweh atabwiye umuntu numwe kugeza ubwo arihishuriye, kandi narihishuriye intumwa zanjye n'abahanuzi banjye bera kandi narinze Ijambo ryanjye ryera ku matwi n'imitima y'abatoranyijwe n'abizerwa. Kuko mwe, Mugeni wanjye, muri igice cy'ibanga rikomeye ry'Imana, kandi ubuzima bwanyu (buhishe muri njye) bugiye guhishurwa. Kandi nzaha ingororano abizerwa ku mirimo yabo yose y'ubudahemuka bakoze mu kwizera gukomeye kuri njye, Yesu Kristo.

Kandi gutyo nakijije imbaga nyamwinshi binyuze mu maraso yanjye y'impongano, kuko amaraso yanjye atagiraga icyaha kandi kugeza n'uyu munsi ntagira icyaha, kuko ntazigera nkora icyaha. Ababayeho mu buzima bwabo mu budahemuka muri Kristo bazabona ingororano ikomeye, kandi bazaka nk'inyenyeri mu ijuru, kandi ubwiza bukomeye ni ubwabo bwo kugira, kuko batishyize hejuru nk'aho bari abakomeye. Ahubwo bampaye icyubahiro, Yeshua HaMashiach, kandi nzabamurikira Data badafite ikizinga cyangwa umunkanyari, kandi imyenda yabo izaba yera de nk'urubura kandi ntibazigera banduzwa. Kandi bose bazabaho ntibazongere gukora icyaha ukundi, haba binyuze mu kutubaha Imana cyangwa binyuze mu bwibone. Kandi bazahabwa icyubahiro muri njye.

Kuko ndi umwungeri mukuru, kandi nayoboye umukumbi wanjye mu rwuri rwiza kugeza mu bihe by'iherezo, byihuta biza ku isi, kuko nta mucanga usigaye wo guca mu kirahure. Kandi nzaha ingororano bose, uhereye ku muto kugeza ku mukuru, mu buryo mudashobora kumva muri iki gihe; ariko uko kumva kuri ku muryango wanyu, kuko muzatwarwa mu ijuru vuba cyane. Kandi gutyo ni ko Mugeni wanjye azatwarwa mu bwiza, aho nzaca urubanza rw'imirimo ye kandi ngacungura byuzuye umugabo, umugore, n'umwana wa nyuma. Nzabaha ingororano ikomeye ndetse no ku tuntu duto twakozwe mu kunkorera. Kuko Imana itari gukiranirwa, ngo yibagirwe umurimo wanyu n'imirimo y'urukundo, mwagaragarije Izina ryayo, kuko mwakoreye Abera, kandi mukomeza kubakorera.

Witinya, Mugeni wanjye, kuko Imana itacira imanza nk'uko umuntu aca imanza, kuko umuntu akiranirwa kandi arobanura ku butoni. Umuntu aha icyubahiro abo umuntu abona ko bakomeye, kandi umuntu wa kamere akenshi yirengagiza kwicisha bugufi n'ingeso nziza agashyigikira abibone n'abiyemera. Kuko umuntu wa kamere ataka kugira ngo abarwe nk'umukiranutsi imbere y'Imana, ariko umuntu wa kamere ntashobora gutsinda kamere ye y'icyaha kandi yanga kwemera ko yononekaye, ndetse akihakana Umwami w'Abami, wamukiza.

Kandi gutyo ubwibone bubanziriza kurimbuka, n'umutima wibona ubanziriza kugwa. Aho kugira ngo bunamire imitima yabo ku magambo yanjye n'ubwenge n'urukundo nasutse ku isi, umuntu wa kamere ahubwo ahitamo gukurikira irari rye n'ibyifuzo bye, kuko inzira z'isi zateguwe na Satani kugira ngo agerageze kandi akongeze ibyo byifuzo, ndetse n'ingororano zo gukiranirwa. Kandi gutyo ibihe by'Abanyamahanga byegereje ku musozo, kandi ni igihe cy'amakuba ya Yakobo; kuko nzacungura Isirayeli, ndetse n'intore zanjye zizerwa, bazaza kuri njye bihana ibyaha byabo n'ibyaha bya ba se banyihakanye. Kandi nk'uko byanditswe, bibe bityo.

Yewe, muntu wa kamere, kuki ugihitamo ibyaha byawe kandi ukirengagiza ubuntu bwanjye n'agakiza kanjye nk'aho ari ikintu cyoroheje? Haba Yahweh cyangwa njyewe, Kristo Yesu, nta byishimo dufite mu manza zije n'ubwoba bukomeye bugiye kurekurwa vuba. Ariko Imana ntiyahisemo iki gice cy'umugambi wayo kugira ngo ibe inkoramaraso ku byo yaremye: kuko Imana ari urukundo, kandi Imana ni umucyo, kandi muri yo nta mwijima na muke ubamo. Amakuba akomeye aje yateguwe kugira ngo ahe abantu amahirwe ya nyuma mbere y'uko biba bicyererewe kuri bo. Imbaga nyamwinshi izakizwa ivanywe mu makuba, ndetse n'abakomeza izina ryanjye kugeza ku mwuka wabo wa nyuma kandi ntibakunde ubuzima bwabo kugeza ku iherezo.

Muntu wa kamere, uri intagondwa cyane, kandi ntiwumva, kandi wanga mu bujiji ubuntu bwanjye, kandi ndacyasenga ntya: Data, bababarire, kuko batazi icyo bakora. Kuri ba bandi basigaye mu makuba akomeye, ndetse n'abanze icyifuzo cyanjye cy'amahoro, benshi bazabura bajye mu kurimbuka, kuko banze Umwami Yesu Kristo. Nyamara, benshi bazakizwa, ndetse n'abambaza izina ry'Umwami bose bazakizwa. Kandi gutyo ibigeragezo by'umuriro bigiye gutangira, kuko urubanza rwegereje.

Urubanza rukomeye ruje ku bwami bwa Satani! Kandi wowe, muntu wa kamere, ndetse no mu kutizera kwawe kw'intagondwa, wanga ndetse n'icyifuzo cyoroshye cyane. Kuko mu bihe byanjye by'ubuntu, agakiza kari ubuntu kandi koroshye, kandi icyo umuntu yagombaga gukora cyose kwari ukwatura Yesu nk'Umwami no kwizera ko Imana yamuzuye mu bapfuye. Kandi ubu hazabaho inzara y'ibintu byiza byose mu gihugu, ndetse n'Ijambo ryanjye Ryera. Kuko mwese mwashyizweho ikimenyetso mbere y'uko muvuka, kuko Data yabamenye ndetse mukiri mu nda za ba nyoko, kandi yategereje buri wese muri mwe igihe mudashobora kwiyumvisha, kandi mwahoze iteka ku mutima we. Mwashyizweho ikimenyetso mukivuka, kandi abamarayika banjye babarinze ku itegeko ryanjye kandi barabarinze barabakingira, kuko ari ko boherezwa. Ese ibi birabatangaza? Muzumva se icyo Abaheburayo bavuga? Ariko se ni uwuhe mu Bamarayika yigeze abwira ati, icara iburyo bwanjye, kugeza aho nzagirira abanzi bawe intebe y'ibirenge byawe? Ese bose si imyuka ikora umurimo, yoherejwe gukorera abazaragwa agakiza?

Mugeni wanjye, muri abaragwa b'agakiza muri njye, Yesu Kristo. Mwahamagariwe muri njye; mwarinzwe muri njye kandi murindwa nanjye; mwahawe amahirwe yo kumva kubwiriza kw'ukwizera kunyerekeyeho kugira ngo mungire Umwami wanyu, niyo mpamvu nabashyizeho ikimenyetso cy'uwo mwuka wera w'isezerano. Mumenye ko ikimenyetso kidashobora kumenwa, kandi mugiye gucungurwa rwose kugeza iteka ryose, kuko mutazi ubwuzuye bw'umurimo w'irema ngiye gusohoza muri mwe, Mugeni wanjye! Mumenye ko abaguye n'urumamfu rwabo babagirira ishyari, kandi banga icyo Yehova akunda cyose, kandi nzabahorera rwose ku mitwe yabo, kuko ari njye uzajanjagura umutwe w'iyo nzoka ya kera, kuko byatangajwe, kandi icyo Yehova atangaje ntigishobora guhinduka.

Nta muntu n'umwe ushobora kubatandukanya nanjye; nta bubisha na bumwe bushobora kumena icyo kimenyetso; muri abanjye kandi muri aba Data, kuko ibyo Data ampa byose nzabisubiza Imana kugira ngo tube byose muri byose! Kandi yampaye ibintu byose mu ijuru no mu isi, uretse ku ntebe ye y'ubwami aranduta!

Kandi njyewe, Kristo Yesu, mbabwiye aya magambo, kandi ni njyewe, Yesu Kristo, ucira urubanza abanzi banyu, kandi nzabisohoza kugeza ku giceri cya nyuma. Mugeni wanjye, urukundo mbakunda ntiruzima, kandi nabateguriye umwanya, kuko mu nzu ya Data hari amazu menshi! Nuko rero vuba cyane muzashyingiranwa nanjye iteka ryose, kandi uko ni ko muzahorana nanjye, kandi amaherezo muzasobanukirwa ubukomeye bw'intego yanyu muri njye mwaremewe gusohoza!

Kuko muri njye, Yehova yabahaye ejo hazaza n'intego, ndetse n'intego y'ibihe igiye kubahishurirwa, kuko ndaje! Kandi sinzahakanwa. Mutegereze kuboneka kwanjye. Mutegereze ubwiza bw'ukuza kwanjye, kuko bugiye kugaragara ku isi ubwo nzabonekera mu bicu n'ijwi rirenga, n'ijwi rya marayika mukuru, n'impanda y'Imana, kuko mbabwira ukuri ko ndetse n'abamarayika bategereje iyo mpanda igiye kuvuga, kandi biteguye! Munyitegure kandi murebe, murinde, mwitegereze nk'uko mwabitegetswe; kuko vuba muzambona n'ubwiza bwanjye Data yanyambitse, kuko ari ko nzabambika, kuko muzaba nkanjye, kandi muzambona uko ndi.

Ndi Umwana w'Intama w'Imana!

Ndaje gusohoza umujinya ku bwami bwa Satani bwose, kandi ntibazacika.

Ndi umukiza w'Isi!

Ndi Kristo Yesu kandi kwihorera ni ukwanjye nzishyura!

Ndi uzajanjagura umutwe w'inzoka, kuko Imana Ishoborabyose yabivugiye! Ngiye guhishurirwa mu bicu nzanwe n'Umugeni wanjye, uwo nzajyana.

Ndi uje ku ifarashi yera.

Ndi uwitwa uwo kwizerwa n'ukuri, kandi mu gukiranuka nzaca urubanza nteze intambara.

Ndaje!
