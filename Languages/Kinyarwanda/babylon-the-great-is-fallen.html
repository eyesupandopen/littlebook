Ubuhamya Bubiri: Isirayeli
Ibisharira Biryoshye
18 Kanama 2024

Babuloni Ikomeye Iraguye, Iraguye!

Mutege imitima yanyu amagambo yanjye kandi mwumve imvugo yanjye, kandi mureke amagambo yanjye yinjire mu mitima yanyu: kuko ndetse n'abizerwa mu Itorero ryanjye ndetse n'Umugeni wanjye bahura n'ibishuko byo kwisobanura no kutizera. Munyumve kandi munyumve neza kandi mutege amatwi yanyu kugira ngo musobanukirwe ibihe murimo, kuko nta n'umwe muri mwe wigeze abaho mu bihe by'ubu byibasiye isi mu rubanza. Kuko urubanza n'amakuba biri hano kandi byatangiye, nubwo ibi ari ibise byo kubyara gusa, ubu bidahagarara kandi bigenewe kuburira ababaho batabizi. Nyamara, ndetse no mu ntore zanjye n'abizerwa harimo abahitamo ubuhumyi aho kubona ukuri kandi bagahitamo kumva urusaku aho kumva amabwiriza asobanutse n'ihumure Amagambo y'Uwiteka azana.

Kuko jyewe, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndetse na Yesu Kristo mu rurimi rw'icyongereza, ndi we wohereza aya magambo binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye nahagurukije nkamutuma kandi nkamuhisha mu mutana wanjye mu gihe nk'iki. Kandi ntimutangare kuko uko niko inzira zanjye ziri, kuko jyewe Kristo Yesu, ndi we ushobora guhagurutsa intumwa, abahanuzi, ababwirizabutumwa, abashumba, n'abigisha mu bantu benshi badafite ibyemezo by'iyi si yaguye cyangwa ngo babe mu bifatanyije mu masezerano y'amadini na ba minisitiri ba Satani bagaragara nk'abakozi bo gukiranuka. Kandi ntimutangare kuko Satani ubwe yihindura nka marayika w'umucyo, bityo rero si igitangaza kuba abakozi be bihindura nk'aho ari abakozi bo gukiranuka, kuko rwose bashaka guhuza bose munsi y'ibendera ryabo rimwe ry'isi yose.

Kandi nk'uko inshuti yanjye Yohana Umubatiza yavuze mu myaka myinshi ishize ubwo yatunganyaga inzira nazagendamo: Ntimutekereze kuvuga mu mitima yanyu muti, Dufite Aburahamu nk'data: kuko ndababwira nti, Imana ishobora guhagurutsa abana ba Aburahamu muri aya mabuye. Kandi noneho ishoka ishyizwe ku muzi w'ibiti: bityo igiti cyose kitera imbuto nziza kiratemwa, kigajugunywa mu muriro.

Musome kandi musobanukirwe kandi musabe ubwenge, bana banjye bato, kandi mushyire mu bikorwa aya magambo mu bihe byanyu. Kuko ndababwira nweruye ko ntagize abakomeye b'iyi si intumwa n'abahanuzi banjye, cyangwa ngo nsabe ibyemezo by'isi, Dogiteri uyu na Pasiteri uriya, ndetse n'amazina abantu biha kugira ngo bagaragare nk'abubaha Imana. Kuko umuntu waguye ahora ashaka guhimbaza umubiri we mu bikorwa by'isi, ndetse agashaka guhagarara akiranuka imbere y'Imana ubwayo mu mubiri wangiritse nta muntu n'umwe ushobora kweza, keretse binyuze kuri jyewe Yesu w'i Nazareti. Nuko rero ndabaza nti ni ibihe byemezo by'isi nari mfite mu gihe cy'umurimo wanjye i Yudaya, ubwo natumwaga gusa ku ntama zazimiye z'inzu ya Isirayeli? Ese ntiyari igisitaza ku batizera mu baturage banjye ubwo babazaga bati ubu bwenge n'imirimo ikomeye uyu mugabo abikura he? Uyu si mwene umubaji?

Kandi nk'uko byanditswe niko byuzujwe: Ariko Imana yatoranyije iby'ubupfu by'isi kugira ngo ikoze isoni abanyabwenge; kandi Imana yatoranyije ibinyantege nke by'isi kugira ngo ikoze isoni ibikomeye; N'ibiciriritse by'isi, n'ibihinyurwa, Imana yarabitoranyije, yego, n'ibitariho, kugira ngo ihindure ubusa ibiriho: Kugira ngo nta mubiri n'umwe wirarira imbere yayo. Nuko rero mwumve Amagambo yanjye yo kwizera avugirwa ku minwa y'abizerwa banjye kandi navuze mbere, kandi noneho ndongera kuvuga ko abahanuzi banjye, ari benshi, batavuguruza ibyanditswe byera, cyangwa ngo bamvuguruze, jyewe Kristo Yesu Umwami wazamutse!

Musobanukirwe, yemwe abafite kwizera guke, ko abahanuzi banjye bavugira hamwe, kandi bavuga Amagambo mbaha. Ese ntimuzi ijwi ry'Umwami wanyu? Ese Amagambo yanjye ni amamahanga kuri mwe, bana banjye bato? Ese mutinya ukuri? Kuko ndi Jambo ry'Imana ryabaye umubiri utuye hagati yanyu kandi ndi we watanze ku mwuka wanjye ukomeye nasutse ku mubiri wose muri ibi bihe bya nyuma nk'uko byasezeranijwe. Kuko byanditswe ngo: abahungu banyu n'abakobwa banyu bazahanura, n'abasore banyu bazabona iyerekwa, n'abasaza banyu bazarota inzozi: Kandi ku bagaragu banjye n'abaja banjye nzasuka ku Mwuka wanjye muri iyo minsi; kandi bazahanura: Kandi nzerekana ibitangaza mu ijuru hejuru, n'ibimenyetso mu isi hasi; amaraso, n'umuriro, n'umwotsi: Izuba rizahinduka umwijima, n'ukwezi guhinduke amaraso, mbere y'uko umunsi ukomeye kandi w'icyubahiro w'Uwiteka uza.

Nuko rero mwumve amagambo yanjye kandi mwemere Amagambo yanjye kuko ari ukuri kandi byose bizasohora, ndetse na buri yod na buri mwambi wa yod bizasohora kuko buri Jambo ry'Imana ritangirwa kumvira kandi buri Jambo ry'Imana rizasohora mu buryo bwuzuye nta kibura, kuko inzira za Yahweh ziratunganye kandi icyo ashyizeho ukuboko kwe gukora gitanga ugutunganya gutangaje. Ese ntimuzi inzira za So, bana banjye bato?

Nuko rero mwumve ijwi ryanjye, mwa bahamagawe bizerwa kandi batoranyijwe, ndetse n'Itorero ryanjye ndetse n'Umugeni wanjye! Ese muzakomeza kwirengagiza amagambo yanjye kandi mutere umugongo ijwi ryanjye muhitamo ibinyoma by'isi? Ese muzakurikira ijwi ry'abaguye mu gihe mumara ubuzima bwanyu mureba gusa kuri shene mudatekereza? Ndabinginga, mwumve ijwi ryanjye kandi ndababwira nti, kuki imitima yanyu ibahagarika umutima? Ese ntimusobanukiwe ko ndi we wicaye mfite umuvuba mu ntoki nkongeza inkongi z'umuriro wo kweza kugira ngo nkureho inkamba mu byuma by'agaciro mu butunzi bwinshi bw'agaciro kenshi bwa Yahweh?

Kandi niko imirimo y'amaboko yanjye itanga mu mitima yanyu n'ubuzima bwanyu bakunzi banjye: kuko ndi we ukuraho kutizera, ndi we ukuraho inzira zanyu zo kutizera, ndi we ubeza rwose uburyo bwose bwo kwangirika kw'umubiri n'ibibi uko muza aho ndi mukatura ibyaha byanyu n'amakosa yanyu kugira ngo nshobore kubakiza rwose mu maraso yanjye y'impongano kugira ngo mushobore guhagarara mudafite umugayo imbere y'intebe y'ubwami ya Yahweh ubwo nzabamurikira Data imbonankubone mu gihe cyanyu kizaza cya vuba cyane.

Nyamara, benshi muri mwe bashaka gusubira mu buzima bwanyu bwa kera iyo ntagize ubushake bwa Yahweh nk'uko igihe cyanyu n'ibyo mwari mwiteze biri. Kuko niko bimeze, ndetse n'abigishwa banjye ba hafi n'intumwa basubiye kuroba nyuma yo kubambwa kwanjye no kuzuka kwanjye, nyamara nabagize abarobyi b'abantu. Kandi nuko nza kubakoranya nanone ku ijwi ryanjye, kandi rwose ntimusobanukiwe akaga kabakikije muri iyi si mbi ya none. Kandi nuko mbahamagarira aho ndi muri ibi bihe bya nyuma kugira ngo muze rwose aho ndi kandi muve rwose mu cyaha no kutizera, kuko ndi we wabahamagariye ingeso nziza n'ukuri, nubwo benshi muri mwe bagihitamo gusubira muri Egiputa.

Nuko rero ndabagira inama yo gushyira ku mutima amasomo yanditswe kera atanga umuburo n'ubwenge ku banyabwenge. Kuko babwiye Mose bati: Ese ni uko nta mva zari ziri muri Egiputa, watuvanyeho kugira ngo dupfire mu butayu? Kuki watugiriye utya, kutuvana muri Egiputa? Kuki mugikumbura iminsi ya kera? Ese muracyaroshwe no kubaho mu byishimo by'icyaha? Kuki mushaka kuguma mu bakobwa b'abapfu mu gihe mpagaze hejuru yanyu mbaha ukuboko ngo muhagarare ku maguru yanyu, kuko mwaguye mutabizi kandi muri mu mukungugu w'isi, kuko umukungugu uzaba ibyo kurya by'inzoka. Nuko rero byuke wowe usinziriye kandi wikunkumure umukungugu w'isi kandi uhaguruke mu bwiza bw'ijuru kandi wizere amagambo yanjye yo kwizera abizerwa bavuze ku minwa yo kwizera!

Nuko rero musobanukirwe ibirimo kuba ubu kandi musobanukirwe ibihe murimo, kuko benshi bemeye gusinzirizwa kandi bizeye amagambo yo kutizera ya Satani uko yongorera ibinyoma byumvikana neza byayobya ndetse n'intore niba zihisemo kwizera ibinyoma bye. Musobanukirwe ko Satani, ndetse n'umwanzi uyobya isi yose, ubu yajugunywe hasi avanywe mu ijuru kandi aboshywe ku isi ku bw'urubanza rwegereje rudatinza.

Nuko rero hariho akaruhuko aho abaguye bemerewe kwimura amabuye yabo mu kibuga cya shishi cy'isi, n'akaruhuko aho ubugingo bwa nyuma busigaye ubu burimo kwinjira mu Nkuge y'ubuzima bw'iteka muri jyewe Kristo Yesu Umwami wabo wazutse n'Umukiza. Nyamara, benshi muri mwe mu ntekerezo zanyu zo kutizera mwanzura ko Amagambo yanjye ari ibinyoma kandi mu bupfu mwanzura ko mufite igihe cyo gusubira mu buzima bwanyu bwa kera bw'icyaha n'ibyishimo. Keretse, mu buryo butunguranye, abizerwa bazatwarwa mu buhungiro mu cyumba cy'intebe y'ubwami cya Yahweh ubwe mu ngororano ikomeye n'ubwiza bw'iteka n'umurage w'iteka, kandi abizerwa bose bazahindurwa iteka ryose.

Ese muzaguma mu bizerwa? Ese mufite ukwicisha bugufi ko kubona kutizera kwanyu bwite no kwihana kugira ngo mushobore kwinjira mu nzego z'abizerwa? Niba muhisemo kubona, jyewe Kristo Yesu, nzabereka. Nuko rero kuki murambirwa gukora neza? Ese mwafashe icyemezo cy'uko abo mbazanaho n'abo mbazanira badakwiriye ubuzima bw'iteka? Nuko rero ndabagira inama yo kwerekana imbabazi n'ubuntu bimwe ku bagenzi banyu naberetse muri aka karuhuko ka nyuma mbere y'uko kurimbuka kumanuka. Kandi mu kubikora muzakiza benshi, kuko mukiri ba ambasaderi banjye. Nuko rero mufungure amakanwa yanyu mu kwizera, mudashidikanya na gato, kandi nzabahumekera amagambo yo kuvuga.

Nuko rero bana banjye bato ndetse n'abizerwa banjye barahagarika umutima kandi ntibihangana, ndetse no muri aka karuhuko ka nyuma mbere y'uko Itorero ryanjye n'Umugeni wanjye batwarwa mu ijuru. Nyamara, nk'uko byari bimeze mu butayu ubwo Mose yahuraga na Yahweh kuri Sinayi abana bahagurukiye kurya no kunywa no gukina, kandi biremeye inyana ya zahabu yo kuramya mu kimbo cy'Imana nzima, iyo batari bemerewe kubona ubwiza bwayo ariko bahagaze hafi bakabona ubwiza bw'ukuhaba kw'Imana ikomeye y'iteka kuri Sinayi! Kandi mu buryo bumwe nk'uko Mose yasabiye abana, nanjye niko nsabira abazungura bose b'agakiza k'iteka, kugira ngo abahamagawe bose bashobore kuza rwose ku gakiza no kwihana bava mu nzira zabo mbi kandi zo kutizera. Musubire ku ijwi ryanjye kandi mugume mu byambu nabahaye, kandi nzabarinda urubanza rutari urwanyu. Ese sinababwiye ngo muve hagati yabo kandi mwitandukanye? Mwumvire ijwi ryanjye!

Nuko rero urubanza ubu ruragwa ku isi kandi urubanza rwateguriwe kera abatizera n'abanangiye imitima, ndetse n'abakomeza ku bushake cyangwa mu bujiji kwigomeka kwa Satani. Kuko abantu benshi, benshi, benshi bazahitamo ku bushake inkongi z'umuriro zigenewe umwanzi n'abamarayika be aho kuva mu cyaha no kuza mu buzima bw'iteka bambaza Yesu w'i Nazareti ku bw'agakiza kabo. Kandi niko bizaba, kuko El Shaddai, ndetse n'Imana Ishoborabyose, yahaye abantu ubushake bwisanzuye kugira ngo abantu bashobore guhitamo kubaho mu gukiranuka n'amahoro imbere ya Yahweh, cyangwa guhitamo kurimbuka kw'iteka muri rya ziba rikomeye ry'umuriro w'ubururu ryakorewe abaguye aho urunyo rwabo rudapfa. Kandi ibihe by'urubanza ni ubu.

Nuko rero mbwira abazumva bose iby'ibintu bizaba mbere y'uko biba. Ibi bintu si ibanga ahubwo byatangajwe n'abahanuzi banjye n'abagaragu banjye mu myaka myinshi ubu, ndetse n'abo natumye bose kuri Babuloni y'Ibanga. Kuko ibihe by'Urubanza rw'iyo maraya ikomeye biri hano, kandi inyamaswa imutwaye ubu izamurimbura rwose. Kuko Imana yashyize mu mitima y'amahembe icumi gukora ubushake bwayo, kandi banga maraya, kandi bazakomeza kumugira umusaka n'uwambaye ubusa, kandi bazarya inyama ze, kandi bakomeze kumutwika n'umuriro. Kandi niko Babuloni y'ibanga izagirwa umusaka rwose kandi ishyirwe ku mugaragaro mu isoni kugira ngo bose babibone.

Imigi ye yahoze ari mikomeye yari ingero z'akazi katoroshye n'umusaruro n'uburumbuke bwa El Shaddai ubu yicaye mu matongo n'urubyaro rwe rwarabaswe n'ibiyobyabwenge kandi rubaho mu mwanda mu mihanda ye iteye ishozi. Kandi agizwe uwambaye ubusa uko ubwirinzi bwe bwimurirwa kure ndetse n'ubutunzi bwe n'imbaraga ze bihabwa mu maboko y'abanzi be b'Abedomu biyita inshuti. Maraya ukomeye warwanyije amahanga yose yatumwe kwigarurira ubu araboshye, kandi nta bwirinzi afite kandi yiteguye kwigarurirwa rwose. Kandi umubiri wose uzarimburwa mu mubabaro kandi abazafatwa bose bazatoranywa kandi abashobora gukorera imigambi y'inyamaswa bazatwarwa mu bucakara n'abasigaye bazicwa, nubwo abasigaye bazabaho. Kandi arashya kandi hari inkongi nyinshi z'umuriro zisanzwe mu mipaka ye no mu bihugu by'abaturanyi be bo mu majyaruguru basangira ibyago bye. Nyamara ibi ni ibise byo kubyara gusa kandi si imanza za nyuma ahubwo ni umuburo kuri bose w'irimbuka ryabo ryegereje. Kuko mu buryo butunguranye bizaba, kandi mu buryo butunguranye bizabaho. Bizabaho gusa kandi ndetse n'igihe nshikuje abizerwa banjye ku isi mbajyana mu mutekano kandi nzakoranya mu buryo butunguranye ingano za Yahweh mu kigega cye cy'iteka, kuko ibihe by'isarura biri hano. Nyamara si isarura rya nyuma, ahubwo ni irya mbere mu menshi, kandi nuko urubanza rutangirira mu nzu y'Imana.

Babuloni y'Ibanga, ibihe byawe byageze, kandi iminsi yawe irarangiye. Jyewe, Yesu w'i Nazareti, narize amarira menshi ku irimbuka ryawe ryegereje kandi nohereje ibimenyetso hejuru, n'ibitangaza mu kirere, n'ibimenyetso mu isi ndetse n'amakuba menshi atunguranye abaho mu gihe nyacyo no hagati ye kandi n'ubundi Babuloni y'Ibanga iziba amatwi yayo mu kwigomeka kwinangiye, kandi noneho izarya rwose inzira zayo zo kwigomeka. Nuko rero musobanukirwe, ko uko nkoranya mu buryo butunguranye abizerwa mu mutekano n'ingororano, niko irimbuka ritunguranye rizagwa kuri Babuloni y'ibanga uko ubuza akurwaho rwose. Mwumve amagambo yanjye kandi musobanukirwe ijwi ryanjye kuko ubuza ari cyo kintu cyonyine kibuza irari ryangiza ry'abaguye kugeza ubwo ubuza atazongera kubuza kandi agakurwa hagati.

Hanyuma irimbuka ritunguranye rizagera ku isi yose. Imirongo y'urugamba iraciwe, kandi bateguye intambara yabo ya gatatu y'isi, ndetse n'intambara yabo yo guhirika rwose inzira za kera n'amategeko ya kera. Nuko rero umuriro uzagwa mu buryo butunguranye kuri Babuloni y'Ibanga kandi imigi yayo izarimburwa. Umuriro uzagwa mu buryo butunguranye uko misile zigwa mu bihugu byayo, ziyisiga ihiriye kandi yambaye ubusa. Nyamara imigi yayo myinshi ku nkombe zayo z'ibyaha izagwa mu mazi azaza mu buryo butunguranye kandi akagwa ku nkombe zayo yuzura imbere mu gihugu ku bilometero byinshi, byinshi kandi ibi bizaba biturutse iburasirazuba n'iburengerazuba. Isi izatigisa mu buryo butunguranye bidasubirwaho, kandi irimbuka rikomeye rizagera ku bihugu bya Babuloni y'ibanga, kandi ibihugu bizangizwa kandi bihindurwe. Ibiraro byayo bizagwa, kandi imihanda yayo izaba idashobora kugendwa, kandi ubutayu n'amatongo bizasigara. Umunuko w'urupfu uzatwikira ibihugu byayo kandi ibyorezo n'indwara bizuzura ibihugu byayo kandi izaba idashobora kwifasha rwose.

Kandi uko iki gitero cya nyuma kiba, imitwe y'Ingabo Zitukura izarekurwa rwose ku baturage basigaye mu gihugu. Bazica abazitambika bose kandi bazica benshi batazitambika. Kuko abantu ba Babuloni y'ibanga bagurishijwe mu bucakara n'imyenda y'imisozi yafashe, kuko binyuze mu nyungu abaguye bakora kugira ngo barimbure abantu benshi. Kandi nk'uko abantu bayo biyemeje kandi bagasinya ubuzima bwabo ku bw'inyungu z'ibintu n'ibyishimo by'icyaha, niko n'abo bahitamo kugira abacakara bazakorera abo babereyemo imyenda mu mirimo y'agahato kandi ikimenyetso kizashyirwa ku mbaraga ku barokotse bose, gusa ntimufate ikimenyetso.

Kandi bityo isi iriho ubu, izasimburwa mu buryo butunguranye n'ivu ry'amatongo n'ibisigazwa by'umuryango wahoze ukomeye. Kandi inzara izagwira mu bihugu, kuko Babuloni y'ibanga yateje inzara mu bihugu byinshi kandi ubu inzara izaba umushyitsi wayo ku meza, kuko uko yatanze ari ko izahabwa kabiri. Amazi azaba ikintu gishakishwa cyane, kandi amazi menshi azahindurwa asharira kubera irimbuka n'imiriro y'intambara ndetse n'ibyorezo by'abatashyinguwe bizanduza igihugu. Kandi benshi bazababara bidasabwa kandi barimbuke mu rubanza rw'ibyaha byabo, nubwo ibyo bitari ngombwa kuri bo ariko abazasigara mu baciriwe urubanza nabo ubwabo bazacirwa urubanza, nyamara umuntu wese uzambaza izina ry'Umwami wabo kugeza ku iherezo azakizwa, ariko bazagomba kwihangana kugeza ku mwuka wabo wa nyuma. Kuko ubudahemuka kugeza ku iherezo buri gihe bwabaye ibisabwa ku bizerwa, ndetse n'abana ba Aburahamu ba Yehova ubwe ari bo bana b'isezerano, kuko umukiranutsi azabeshwaho no kwizera.

Kandi bityo Babuloni y'ibanga izacikamo kabiri hagati kandi amazi azisuka aho hantu hahoze ari ubutaka bwumutse burumbuka, ndetse n'ibihugu byo hagati bizarengerwa n'amazi kugeza hagati. Kandi bityo imbabazi n'ubuntu by'Imana bizakurwa rwose ku isi ubwo uzitira azakurwaho mu buryo butunguranye kandi irimbuka rizashyingiranwa n'irimbuka, kuko ibi ari ibihe by'irimbuka aho ubwami bwa Satani buzacirwa urubanza rwose kandi bukarimburwa rwose, ariko mbere na mbere bizakorera imigambi ya Yehova mu gutsemba rwose Babuloni y'ibanga kandi bityo izatwikwa rwose kandi itsembwe mu miriro myinshi yaka izagota kandi igatangira kweza igihugu. Kandi bityo irimbuka rya Babuloni y'ibanga rizasohora mu isaha imwe, kuko nk'uko byanditswe ari ko bizakorwa, kandi izahabwa umugabane wikubye kabiri w'urubanza. Kandi nk'uko byanditswe, ni ko bizakorwa.

Kuko byanditswe kera na Yohana Uhishurirwa: Nagiye mbona marayika wundi amanuka ava mu ijuru, afite ubushobozi bukomeye, ku buryo isi yamurikiwe n'ubwiza bwe, Kandi arangurura ijwi rirenga, ati, Iguye, iguye, Babuloni wa mudugudu mukuru, kandi ihindutse icumbi ry'abadayimoni, n'indiri y'imyuka mibi yose, n'akazu k'inyoni zose zihumanye kandi zangwa. Kuko amahanga yose yanyoye ku mvinyo y'umujinya w'ubusambanyi bwayo, kandi abami b'isi basambanye na yo, n'abacuruzi b'isi bakize kubera ubwinshi bw'ibinezeza byayo.

Kandi numvise irindi jwi riva mu ijuru rivuga riti, nimuyisohokemo, bantu banjye, kugira ngo mutaba abafatanyacyaha b'ibyaha byayo, kandi kugira ngo mutazabona ku byago byayo. Kuko ibyaha byayo byageze mu ijuru, kandi Imana yibutse gukiranirwa kwayo. Muyiture, nk'uko yabituye, kandi muyihe kabiri hakurikijwe imirimo yayo: kandi mu gikombe yabahaye, muyuzurizemo kabiri. Nk'uko yihesheje icyubahiro, ikabaho mu binezeza, abe ari ko muyiha kubabazwa n'agahinda: kuko ivuga mu mutima wayo iti, Nicaye ndi umwamikazi, kandi sindi umupfakazi, kandi sinzigeze mbona umuborogo. Nuko rero, ibyago byayo bizaza ku munsi umwe, urupfu, n'agahinda, n'inzara, kandi izatwikwa n'umuriro: kuko Imana iyicira urubanza, ari Umwami ukomeye!

Kandi bityo iri shyanga ryahoze rikomeye ry'ama leta n'imijyi myinshi rizagwa gitumo, kandi marayika w'irimbuka ubu aguruka hejuru y'umujyi waryo mukuru kandi munini cyane uri ku mazi y'iburasirazuba kandi ishema ryaryo n'ibyishimo n'ishyari ry'isi bizarimburwa rwose ntibizongera kubyuka ukundi. Kandi bityo irimbuka ryaryo rizasangirwa n'abaryifatanyije na ryo bose, kuko ari maraya mukuru wicaye ku mazi menshi ari yo moko n'amateraniro n'amahanga n'indimi.

Kuko urubanza rwabo rutazatinda, kandi abami b'isi bose bifatanyije n'amahembe cumi mu gitero cyabo gitunguranye. Kuko abayobozi babo bakorera abaguye babikunze, kuko uko ni ko abategetsi b'isi bose bameze. Nta beza bahari, oya, n'umwe. Aba bayobozi bakoze imyaka myinshi kugira ngo iki gitero gishoboke kandi bagambaniye ibihugu byabo boherejwemo kugira ngo babishuke. Kandi abantu babakurikiye babikunze mu binezeza bazagwa mu miriro y'intambara, ibyorezo, urupfu n'irimbuka. Kandi ibi bintu byiteguye kurekurwa gitumo byuzuye ku isi. Kandi bityo amahanga yose yiyita aya gikristo azagwa ku nkota kuko urubanza rutangirira mu nzu y'Imana. Kandi nk'uko byari bimeze mu minsi ya kera ubwo Isirayeli yasigaga Data w'Imicyo igakorera izindi mana, zo kamere yazo atari imana, ni ko n'aba bazatangwa mu maboko y'abanzi babo, nk'uko imiryango cumi n'ibiri yacitsemo ibice igatsindwa kera kubera kugambanira amasezerano kandi bikazanwa n'umuvumo w'amategeko ku batumvira n'abinangiye imitima.

Kandi mu ivu ry'intambara yeruye n'irimbuka hazaduka gahunda ya nyuma y'inyamaswa, kuko ikiyoka cyamaze gutegura gahunda y'inyamaswa ngo izamuke, ndetse n'abifatanya n'abami cumi bazaha ubushobozi bwabo bwose inyamaswa iva mu nyanja n'inyamaswa iva mu butaka ari we muhanuzi wabo w'ibinyoma na Moshiach w'ibinyoma. Inyamaswa iva mu nyanja izazamuka nka Mahdi wabo, kandi ihabwa ubushobozi bwo kurwanya abera no kubanesha. Kandi bityo inyamaswa iva mu butaka isangira ubushobozi bumwe n'inyamaswa iva mu nyanja, kandi ni yo rya Hembe Rito ryahanuwe kera na Daniyeli.

Kandi bityo izo nyamaswa zombi zizahabwa ubushobozi bwose na cya kiyoka kinini gitukura kandi zizazamuka byuzuye kandi zizanesha kandi zigende zinesha, kandi bazavuga bati: Ni nde uhwanye n'inyamaswa? Kandi ni nde ushobora kurwanya inyamaswa? Kandi bityo bose bazasabwa gushyirwaho ikimenyetso cyayo ku bashaka kwifatanya nabo mu muryango wabo wa gisitani. Kandi ubusazi buzapfukira isi yose, kuko uzitira atazaba akihari ngo yemeze abantu ibyaha no kuzitira irari ryabo n'ibyifuzo bibi. Kandi bityo ikibi cyuzuye, kitazitiwe cyiteguye kurekurwa ku isi kandi ikuzimu hazakingurwa rwose, kuko abatuye ku isi banze Yehova, kuko banze njyewe, Yesu w'i Nazareti woherejwe ngo abakize. Ariko bibarishije ko badakwiriye ubugingo buhoraho kandi banga Yehova ubwo Yehova yabazagaho mu rukundo, ubugwaneza, ubuntu, n'imbabazi akabaha amasezerano y'amahoro ahoraho ku bantu. Amasezerano y'amahoro ubu banze. Nyamara, umuntu wese uzambaza izina ry'Umwami kugeza ku iherezo azakizwa.

Kandi nubwo ibihe by'urubanza, irimbuka rikomeye, intambara, inzara, ibyorezo n'ibindi nk'ibyo bizarekurwa byuzuye ku isi, iki cyumweru cya nyuma ndetse n'icyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli cyatangiwe agakiza ka Yakobo kandi njyewe, Yeshua HaMashiach nzabika abasigaye ba Isirayeli bazanyakira nka Mesiya wabo w'ukuri kandi ukwiriye kuko, uko ni ko naremwe, nkorwa, kandi noherezwa n'Imana Ishoborabyose mu guha icyubahiro amasezerano yakozwe mu masezerano n'isezerano ku ba Se.

Kuko Yehova ari Imana ikomeye ihoraho yicaye mu ijuru kandi ubu izakora kugira ngo yiyereke ibyo yaremye kandi njyewe, Kristo Yesu, ndi Umwami w'Abami uhoraho kandi Umwami w'Abami w'ubwami butagira iherezo. Kandi nzohereza abahanuzi banjye babiri, ndetse n'ibitereko byanjye by'amatabaza bibiri bizahanura, kandi nzohereza abanjye 144,000 kugira ngo bakize abazima abo boherejweho mu butayu ahantu hatoranyijwe na Yehova kandi bazagaburirwa kandi bitabweho na Yehova. Kandi Imana ihoraho yabivuze kera. Kuko Yehova ari we uvuga ibintu bitaraba nk'aho byamaze kuba kuko uko ni ko inzira z'Imana Ihoraho zimeze yampaye Ubutumwa Bwiza Buhoraho ngo butangazwe ku isi yose kugira ngo agakiza gahoraho kagere ku bazambaza izina ryanjye rikomeye, rihoraho bose.

Njyewe Yeshua HaMashiach, ndetse Kristo Yesu, nohereje aya magambo binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye kandi binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye aya magambo yoherejwe.

Ndi Jambo ry'Imana ryabaye umubiri ribana namwe.

Ndi uwicaye afite umuvuba mu ntoki akongeza imiriro yo kweza.

Ndi ukuraho kutizera.

Ndi ukuraho inzira zanyu zitarimo kwizera.

Ndi uweze rwose ubwoko bwose bw'ikiboreze cy'umubiri.

Ndi uwabahamagariye ingeso nziza n'ukuri.

Nbahagaze hejuru, mbaha ukuboko ngo mwongere muhagarare ku maguru yanyu. Ese muzemera icyo mbaha?

Kandi mumenye ko ndi we Imana ikomeye ihoraho kandi y'iteka yahaye ibintu byose mu maboko yanjye akomeye kandi yampaye izina risumba amazina yose, kugira ngo mu izina rya Yesu amavi yose apfukame mu ijuru, mu isi no munsi y'isi. Kandi ndi we Ntaramakenga yo mu muryango wa Yuda kandi mu gukiranuka ndaza gucira urubanza no kurwana no gusohoza urubanza ku baguye, kandi isaha y'urubanza ubu irageze. Kandi bityo ngira inama abatuye ku isi gukurikira amagambo ya Yehova no Gusoma Umwana, kugira ngo ntaraka, mukarimbukira mu nzira, ubwo umujinya wanjye uzaba ucanwe ho gato.

Hahirwa abamwiringira bose, ndetse Yeshua HaMashiach n'abambaza izina ryanjye bose kugeza ku iherezo bazakizwa, ndetse n'abaragwa b'agakiza bose uhereye mbere y'uko imfatiro z'isi zishyirwaho.
