Ubuhamya bubiri Umuhamya: Itorero
Mu Mirimo ya Yohana Umubatiza
25 Werurwe 2024

Munyizere

Nimwumve ijwi ryanjye, bana banjye, kandi munyizere, njyewe Umwami Yesu Kristo. Nabahamagariye ubwiza n'ingeso nziza no guhagarara imbere yanjye mwejejwe mu nzira zanyu n'amaraso yanjye y'impongano, kuko amaraso y'umwana w'intama yoza imyanda yose. Mwejejwe muri njye. Mumenye, bana banjye, ko nta gucirwaho iteka ku bari muri njye, ni ukuvuga, muri Kristo. Kwifatanya nanjye n'imigambi yanjye ni ukugirana ubusabane na Data, Yahweh, nanjye, umwana we Yeshua. Ndi Uwiteka wabahamagaye mu mazina yanyu akabaha ubutumire bw'ubugingo buhoraho aho hazaba hatakiriho amakuba, cyangwa umubabaro, cyangwa agahinda; kuko nzahanagura amarira yose ku maso yanyu mu gakiza kanjye kuri mwe. Ese natangira umurimo sinwusoze? Ese Imana y'ijuru n'isi yasiga ikintu kidakozwe cyangwa ngo yice amasezerano n'ijambo ryayo? Ntimwasomye ko, bidashoboka ko Imana ibeshya? Ni ukuri, buri sezerano rizasohora kandi ryuzuzwe, nuko rero mumenye ko gucungurwa kwanyu kwegereje — atari ugucungurwa kw'igice, ahubwo ugucungurwa kwuzuye nta gisigaye kidakozwe kandi nta kibura.

Ibi byose byarasohoye, kandi ibidashoboka byabaye muri njye kuko nanesheje urupfu kugira ngo ntapfa ukundi. Mumenye ko buri murimo mubi Satani yagerageje kubaka mu buzima bwanyu nzawusenya. Yego, nzarimbura imirimo yose ya Satani; kuko ibyo ari byo byatumye nigaragaza. Nabahaye kuba bazima, kandi nzabakiza rwose imibiri yanyu yononekaye, kandi muzaba nkanjye. Mugenzure Ijambo ryanjye, mwumve Amagambo yanjye, muyagereranye n'Ibyanditswe, kandi mumenye ko gucungurwa kwanyu kwuzuye kuri hafi nk'umwuka wanyu ukurikira kandi kuzaba mu kanya nk'ako guhumbya. Muhagarare mushikamye ku bwanjye; mumvugire. Ntimukagire icyo mupfana n'abasenga ibishushanyo n'abasambanyi binjiye mu Itorero ryanjye rwihishwa kandi bagerageza kuyobya abana banjye bato. Uburakari bwanjye buri kuri bo no ku muntu wese ugaba igitero ku bwoko bwanjye kugira ngo abayobye.

Kuko bidashoboka ko mwashikuzwa mu kuboko kwanjye, kandi abo nagiriye imbabazi ni abangize Umwami wabo binyuze mu kwatura izina ryanjye rikomeye, Yesu Kristo, nk'Umwami. Sinzabatenguha, kandi ntibyigeze binjira mu mutima w'umuntu ibintu bibategereje, kuko nzabajyana vuba. Ntimukemere ko amagambo mabi ahagarika imitima yanyu, bana banjye, kuko mwakijijwe uburakari bw'Imana, kandi abizerwa banjye ntibazanyura mu gihe cy'imyaka irindwi y'uburakari igiye gusukwa. Imitima yanyu nibe mu mahoro kandi muruhukire muri njye, ndetse no mu makuba yanyu n'ibitotezo muruhukire muri njye, nanjye nzabaha amahoro adashobora gusobanurwa. Imbabazi zanjye n'ubuntu bwanjye bihoraho iteka, kandi nishyuye ikiguzi cy'ibyaha byanyu, nuko rero mukomeze kubivamo. Mbona imvururu munyuramo. Numva amagambo yose akomeye mubwirwa n'abavugwa kuri mwe. Byongeye kandi, ndabakomeza mu ntege nke zanyu, kandi nabahaye ku mwuka wanjye, kandi muri abanyambaraga n'abakomeye muri njye. Mu mbaraga zanyu muzatsindwa, nuko rero muruhukire mu mbaraga zanjye, kuko Data yanshyize hejuru y'ibindi byose.

Nzihoreera ku banyabyaha vuba cyane, bana banjye, nuko rero ntimutinye. Uku kwihorera si ukwanyu, kandi ntikugenewe abana b'Imana ahubwo ni ukw'abanzi bayo bagiriye nabi ikiremwamuntu n'abantu babi bica kandi bagasahura abaturanyi babo n'abavandimwe babo.

Yego, abantu babi benshi bifatanyije na Satani kubera inyungu z'umururumba, kandi bafite umugabane wabo n'ingororano yabo mu nyanja y'umuriro yateguriwe Umwanzi n'abamarayika be. Ese mwese ntimuri ubwoko bumwe, ubwoko bwa muntu? Nyamara, mumenye ko uburakari bukomeye bugiye gusukwa kandi ijuru rigiye guhishurirwa isi. Buri gikorwa cyaranditswe. Buri jambo ryose ry'imfabusa ryavuzwe ryaranditswe. Nta banga rishobora kumpishwa, kandi nta hantu ho kwihisha amaso yanjye. Nubwo abanyabyaha bacukura imyobo mu isi kugira ngo bahunge uburakari bwanjye bwegereje, bazahura n'imperuka yabo bagerageza kunyihisha.

Nzishyura, kandi nzihoreera nshyireho ubutabera ku barenganijwe. Mfite inkota ityaye y'amugi abiri iva mu kanwa kanjye, kandi nta kintu cyangwa umuntu numwe ushobora kumpagarika, kuko ndi Umwami w'abami n'Umutware w'abatware. Ni nde washobora kubatandukanya n'urukundo rw'Imana ruri muri njye? Sinishyuye ibyaha byanyu ngo mbaha ubugingo buhoraho kugira ngo mbasukaho uburakari no gucirwaho iteka, mwa bizerwa banjye. Nta kintu cyangwa umuntu numwe ushobora kudutandukanya, kandi vuba aha muzareba Data nanjye n'amaso yanyu. Nubwo mwubatse icyizere gikomeye no kunyizera, mutarigeze mundebesha amaso yanyu, vuba aha muzabona, kandi muzamenya nk'uko muzwi. Ni nde ubahagarika umutima? Ese ntazahinduka ubusa n'imirimo ye mibi ikamugarukaho incuro ebyiri? Abantu babi bibikiye uburakari bukomeye, yego, uburakari bukomeye kubera guhohotera ibyaremwe bya Data, kandi nzishyura, niko Uwiteka avuga.

Muhagarare imbere yanjye kandi mukomeze kwitandukanya n'iyi si mbi, igiye kurimbuka kandi mwemere ko umurimo wanjye w'impongano uboza, ubejesha rwose. Dore! Ndahindura byose bishya, kandi ibyaha byanyu n'ibicumuro byanyu sinzabyibuka ukundi. Nuko rero muve mu mirimo yanyu bwite mwakire imirimo yanjye, kuko mu kubikora muzazana ubuzima bwuzuye mu bugingo bwanyu. Ntimutinye abanyabyaha! Kuko vuba aha imirimo yabo izatumuka nk'umukungugu imbere y'umuyaga ukomeye, kandi nzagira byose byiza kandi bishya. Hari ingororano ikomeye ku bizerwa banjye n'abatoranyijwe; kandi vuba, yego, vuba cyane muzasarura ingororano z'imirimo yanyu niba mudacogoye.

Muhagarare ku bwanjye kugeza ku iherezo, kuko utakaza ubugingo bwe muri njye azabubona kandi azasarura ibyishimo bikomeye n'agakiza no kunezerwa iteka ryose imbere ya Data. Nabagize abami n'abatambyi imbere ya Data, kuko ndi umutambyi mukuru uhoraho iteka ryose mu buryo bwa Melisedeki. Muhagarare mushikamye mu buntu bwanjye kandi mumenye ko vuba cyane urugi ruzakingwa, nk'uko Imana yakingiranye Nowa n'ubutunzi bwe mu Nkuge. Uko niko nanjye nzabakoranyiriza aho ndi mu ijuru. Hari urugi rukinguye nta muntu ushobora gukingura cyangwa gukinga, kandi nzabakira iwanjye, Mugeni wanjye. Nuko rero muntegereze kuko igihe cyegereje kurangira.

Ndi Uwiteka wizerwa.

Ndi inzira, ukuri, n'ubugingo. Ndi umutwe w'umubiri.

Ndi umukwe w'umugeni, kandi ndavuga nti ngwino!

Ndi udapfa ukundi, kuko bidashoboka ko urupfu rumfata.

Ndi uwabahamagariye ubwiza, ingeso nziza, n'ukuri.

Ndi Umwami Yesu Kristo, umwana w'Imana wigize umuntu wapfiriye ibyaha byanyu.

Ndi uwazutse ku munsi wa gatatu kugira ngo ntapfa ukundi.

Ndi uwo Imana yakiriye mu ijuru ubwo intumwa zanjye n'abigishwa banjye bandebaga nzamuka mu bicu.

Ndi umutambyi mukuru uhoraho iteka ryose mu buryo bwa Melisedeki.
