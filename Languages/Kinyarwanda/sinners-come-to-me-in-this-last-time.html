Ubuhamya Bubiri: Itorero
Mu Mirimo y'Intumwa Pawulo
10 Gicurasi 2024

Banyabyaha! Nimuze Kuri Njye Muri Iki Gihe Cya Nyuma!

Nimwumve Amagambo yanjye, ndetse n'Amagambo ya Yesu w'i Nazareti, kuko nje aho muri mu mwuka w'urukundo, impuhwe, kwicisha bugufi, ubuntu, ndetse cyane cyane imbabazi. Kuko vuba aha ubuntu bwanjye n'imbabazi bizasimburwa n'amakuba akomeye, ndetse n'amakuba ya Yakobo, kandi iki gihe cyose cy'imyaka irindwi kizaba kidasanzwe ku byo isi yigeze kubona, ndetse na mbere y'irema. Bizatangirana n'irimbuka ritunguranye n'itotezwa bizagenda byiyongera. Ku bw'ibyo, nje bwa nyuma ku banyabyaha batarihana mu buntu bwanjye n'imbabazi. Kuko hasigaye akanya gato ko kwihana kwanyu niba muza kuri njye, Yesu Kristo, mukanyatura nk'Umwami wanyu kandi mukizera mu mitima yanyu ko Imana yanzuye mu bapfuye. Narambuye amaboko yanjye mu buntu n'imbabazi ku isi igiye kurimbuka mu gihe cyose cy'ubuntu ubu, kandi benshi bangize Umwami wabo, ndetse n'abo Umwami Imana yacu yahamagaye kandi igatoranya mbere y'ishingwa ry'isi kugira ngo bahishurirwe muri njye, Yesu Kristo Umwami ukomeye: Kuko ndi Umwami w'abami n'Umutware w'abatware.

Kandi nahamagaye abatoranyijwe, n'abaye abizerwa bose mu gakiza kanjye. Benshi barakijijwe! Mukijijwe iki, murabaza? Mukijijwe urubanza n'irimbuka bigiye gusukwa kuri iyi si, imana zayo, abategetsi bayo, ibikomangoma byayo, n'abakurikira Satani bose, kuko ari we mana y'iyi si kandi urubanza rwe rugeze. Ndetse mukijijwe urubanza rw'intebe y'era nini n'inyanja y'umuriro yateguriwe Umwanzi n'abamarayika be. Urubanza rwamaze gucirwa abategetsi b'uyu mwijima w'iki gihe, kandi vuba aha ruzasohora rwuzuye, kuko isi izategekwa n'ukuboko kwanjye mu gukiranuka, kuko igihe cyanjye cyo guhishurwa kigeze, kandi isi ni iyanjye n'ibyiyuzuyemo byose, ndetse nanjye, Yeshua HaMashiach!

Urubanza ruraje kandi ruri hano ariko nturuzura. Kandi benshi muri mwe baracyatekereza ko bafite igihe cyo kwishora mu byaha byabo no gushaka guhaza irari ryanyu ry'ibyaha rituruka mu mubiri wanyu wangiritse. Mumenye ko nta cyifuzo mfite cyo gucira urubanza no guciraho iteka uwo ari we wese muri mwe, kuko natanze ubuzima bwanjye mu rukundo rwanjye kuri mwe no kwishyura byuzuye ibyaha byanyu mu bubabare bukomeye n'umubabaro ku musaraba! Kuko nta cyaha na kimwe kitishyuwe byuzuye n'igitambo cyanjye cy'impongano nka Pasika itunganye. Ndi Umwana w'Intama w'Imana ukuraho ibyaha by'isi. Kandi sinaje kubaciraho iteka, ahubwo ndi hano kugira ngo mbakize. Isi nk'uko muyizi igiye guhinduka cyane ku buryo mutazamenya ibizazuka mu ivu, kuko igice kinini cy'isi kigiye gushya mu muriro no kurimbuka mu buryo bwinshi mu rubanza rwo gukiranuka rwa Yahweh. Kandi ishyano rikomeye rigiye kuva muri iryo vu.

Kuko umutware w'iyi si aciriwe urubanza, kandi urubanza rwe ruzasohora rwuzuye. Kuko Satani yakoze ibibi byinshi kandi inzira ze ni mbi cyane, kandi aza gusa kwiba, kwica, no kurimbura. Yangije abantu byuzuye kandi mu buryo butandukanye, kandi ashaka kurimbura ibyo Imana ikunda byose. Kuko Imana yakunze abari mu isi cyane, byatumye itanga Umwana wayo w'ikinege kugira ngo umwizera wese atarimbuka, ahubwo ahabwe ubugingo buhoraho. Bityo mumenye byuzuye ko ndi umwana w'intama wa Pasika wa nyuma w'ibihe byose (ntihazabaho undi), kandi nishyuye ibyaha byose. Satani yangije imiterere y'abantu kuva mu busitani bwa Edeni, ubwo yashukaga Adamu na Eva kwemera imbuto ze ziboze no kwizera ubumenyi bwe butagira agaciro, kuko ubumenyi bwe buzana urupfu ku baburya bose. Kandi ku bw'iyo mpamvu Imana Ishoborabyose yahaye irema ryayo amasezerano y'amahoro, ndetse nanjye, Yesu Kristo.

Kuko ijwi ry'Abefeso rivuga iki, ubwo Pawulo yabandikiraga ku byerekeye imirimo yanjye yuzuye (kuko navuze mu mwuka wanjye wa nyuma ku musaraba nti, "Birarangiye")! Ariko noneho, muri Kristo Yesu, mwebwe abari kure kera mwigijwe hafi n'amaraso ya Kristo.

Kuko ari we mahoro yacu, waduhinduye twembi umwe, kandi akagusha urukuta rwari urugabano hagati yacu. Nimwumve ijwi ryanjye. Ndi amahoro yanyu, kuko amategeko yaciriyeho iteka icyaha mu mubiri; ndetse n'umubiri wanyu n'amaraso byangiritse mu cyaha, ndetse byanduye kuva muri Edeni. Njye, Yesu Kristo, mu gitambo cyanjye cy'impongano i Kaluvari nagushije urukuta rwo hagati rwatandukanyaga Umuyahudi n'Umunyamahanga, mbahindura bombi umuntu mushya umwe, ndetse mbarema bundi bushya mu gukiranuka no kwera nyakuri ku bemeye amahoro bose banyatura nk'Umwami wabo wazutse!

Ariko hari ibindi biva mu Befeso (nk'aho ibi bidatangaje bihagije), bityo Abefeso barakomeza: kandi akuraho binyuze mu mubiri we impamvu y'urwango (ni ukuvuga, itegeko ry'amategeko ari mu mategeko yanditswe) kugira ngo ahindure babiri umuntu mushya umwe muri we, bityo akora amahoro: kandi yiyunge bombi n'Imana mu mubiri umwe binyuze ku musaraba we, kandi yica urwango binyuze muri byo. Nyumva, yewe muntu wanze umuhamagaro wanjye: Ndaguhamagara none aha muri aya masegonda ya nyuma, kandi igihe kigiye gushira! Nanyakire nk'igitambo cyanyu cy'amahoro, kuko mbaha ku buntu agakiza n'incungu mu mibiri yanyu yangiritse, ndetse n'ubugingo buhoraho muri njye, Kristo Yesu. Ntimwibeshye: Buri mpano nziza, na buri mpano itunganye biva hejuru, kandi bimanuka biva kuri Data w'imicyo, udatakaza cyangwa ngo agire igicucu cyo guhinduka.

Nimwumve ijwi ryanjye kandi mumenye ko ikintu cyose cyiza mu buzima bwanyu, ikintu cyose gifite agaciro n'ingeso nziza, biva kuri Data kandi bitangwa na Data w'Imicyo kugira ngo mugire ubuzima bwuzuye kandi muze kuri njye, Umwami Yesu, nk'umukiza wanyu bwite. Kandi kugira ngo mumenye ko hariho ubugingo buhoraho kuri mwe niba mwemeye gusa. Ese mwiyanga cyane ku buryo mwiteguye gutanga ibyo mukunda byose mu buzima byatanzwe na Data kugira ngo muhaze irari ryanyu ryikunda? Ese ibyaha byanyu ni ingenzi cyane kuri mwe ku buryo mwahisemo gucirwaho iteka ry'iteka na Satani aho guhitamo kugira neza kw'Imana n'ubuzima bushya muri njye, Kristo Yesu? Ese ibyaha byanyu ni ingenzi cyane kuri mwe?

Ndaguhamagara, yewe muntu wa kamere, kandi ndagutumira mu mahoro n'ubugingo buhoraho, ubuzima butagira indwara n'icyaha, ndetse n'ubuzima butagira indwara n'agahinda, ubuzima butagira umubabaro aho mutazongera kureba abo mukunda bababara; kuko ibya mbere bizashira vuba cyane. Nta gihe kigihari cyo guhaza ibyaha byanyu. Kuko benshi muri mwe bafashe ubuntu bw'Imana uko butari kandi bizera ibinyoma by'umujura, ushaka kubiba ubuzima bwanyu kugira ngo mufatanye na we mu gihano cye cy'iteka. Ntimwibeshye. Kunyanga, Yesu w'i Nazareti (ndetse n'uwitanze ku bw'ibyaha byanyu n'agakiza), ni ukwanga ubuzima no guhitamo iteka urupfu rwa kabiri urwo Satani n'abamarayika be baciriweho iteka: Kuko badafite imbabazi, kandi ntibazaruhuka, kandi badafite amahoro. Kandi inyanja y'umuriro yateguriwe Umwanzi n'abamarayika be.

Kwanga igitambo cyanjye ni ukwanga ubugingo buhoraho no kwanga Imana byuzuye. Ku bw'ibyo, ntihazabaho imbabazi, ubuntu, urukundo, umucyo, cyangwa amahoro ahubwo kwicuza gusa, umwijima, n'umubabaro aho muzasarura imbuto ziboze z'umubiri wanyu iteka ryose. Ese ntimubona ukuntu ibi ari ubupfu? Nishyuye ibyaha byanyu mu maraso yanjye y'impongano! Ariko mwanze inzira zanjye, nubwo ndikubuhamagara nonaha kandi kuri benshi bwa nyuma. Mwanze urukundo rwanjye, nubwo umutima wanjye ucyibabaza ku bwanyu n'imiryango yanyu, kandi nabakiza byuzuye niba mwihannye gusa mugareka ibyaha byanyu. Ese ntimwumva ko nabanje kubabara nk'umunyabyaha wo hasi cyane mu gitambo cyanjye cy'impongano kugira ngo mbazamure ku Mana mu gakiza? Kuko nazamuwe ku musaraba kugira ngo nkize ubugingo bwanyu kandi mbayunge na Data, kandi nahoranye ntagira icyaha, bityo mwemere amaraso yanjye atagira icyaha!

Kuko Ibyanditswe bivuga iki? Kuko rwose mwumvise ko Imana yakunze abari mu isi cyane byatumye itanga Umwana wayo w'ikinege: kugira ngo umwizera wese, atarimbuka, ahubwo ahabwe ubugingo buhoraho. Nzi ko aya magambo asanzwe kuri mwe, kuko ndetse n'abashimisha banyu babagumisha mu bubata bwa Satani bakoresheje nabi amagambo yanjye mu buryo bwo gutuka Imana. Ariko mwakora neza gukomeza gusoma, bityo ndabinginze nimwumve ijwi ryanjye! Kuko Imana itatumye Umwana wayo mu isi gucira isi urubanza: ahubwo kugira ngo isi ikizwe binyuze kuri we. Umwizera, ntacirwaho iteka: ariko utamwizera, amaze gucirwaho iteka, kuko atizeye Izina ry'Umwana w'ikinege w'Imana.

Ese imvugo yanjye ntisobanutse bihagije? Ese amagambo yanjye ntiyumvikana byoroshye? Niba munyanze, Umwami Yesu, Umwana w'Intama w'Imana, muzifatanya burundu n'umwanzi wanjye, ndetse n'abashinzwe kumena amaraso y'abere: Kuko kunyanga ni ukwifatanya n'ukwigomeka kwa Satani, kuko Umwanzi yakoze icyaha kuva mu ntangiriro. Kandi uru ni rwo rubanza, uko umucyo waje mu isi, abantu bagakunda umwijima kuruta umucyo, kuko imirimo yabo yari mibi. Kandi uko ni ko ukuri kuberekeyeho, yewe muntu wa kamere, ndetse n'abamuri mwe banga umwuka wanjye w'ubuzima, kandi ku bw'ibi byose ndacyabahamagara, nubwo uyu ari umuhamagaro wa nyuma mbere y'uko urubanza rutangira. Kuko nta gihe kigihari. Ese ibyaha byanyu ni ingenzi cyane kuri mwe ku buryo mwamara iteka mu mwijima wo hanze n'igihano muri iyo nyanja nini y'umuriro yateguriwe Umwanzi n'abamarayika be? Kuko Imana itabifuriza iki gihano kandi yantumye nk'igitambo cyayo cy'amahoro kugira ngo mbayunge na yo binyuze mu maraso yanjye y'impongano.

Nimwumve amagambo yanjye kandi mwemere amahoro yanjye. Ku bumva aya magambo bose, mumenye ko njye, Yesu w'i Nazareti, ndabahamagara; kandi sinita ku byaha bibaboha kuko nabyishyuye byose. Mumenye ko gufata ikimenyetso cy'inyamaswa bitazababarirwa, kandi ndagerageza kubakiza iki gishuko (nubwo ikimenyetso kitaragera hano); kuko benshi bazabura ubuzima bwabo mu mubabaro kubera kwanga. Ariko mumenye ibi bikurikira: Ntimushobora kumanuka cyane ku buryo ntabasha kubageraho; gusa ntimufate ikimenyetso. Ndetse n'abibone ntibashobora kwishyira hejuru mu cyaha cyane ku buryo ntabasha kubamanura buhoro mu gakiza no kubashyiraho ikimenyetso cy'umwuka wanjye kugira ngo mbashyire hejuru! Kuko nishyuye ibyaha byose by'abantu mu bihe byose, kandi nta na kimwe nasize nkoze. Hari ibyaha byinshi, ariko nzahamagara bamwe muri mwe by'umwihariko ku ngeso zanyu mbi. Mumenye ko nzabababarira ibyaha byanyu byose atari ibi gusa, kandi nzabeza byuzuye mu maraso yanjye ku byaha byanyu byose.

Ku munywi w'inzoga n'umunyabiyobyabwenge, ndavuga nti: Nimumhindukirire none aha mu kanya k'iteka ko gusobanukirwa kandi mwumve amagambo yanjye, kuko niba mwumva mu kwizera, amagambo yanjye azabazanira umucyo w'icyubahiro w'agakiza. Amagambo yanjye azaboha abadayimoni babatera kunywa no gukoresha ibiyobyabwenge, nubwo mwabagira inshuti zanyu. Mbaha gusobanukirwa kw'igitangaza kugira ngo muhitemo icyo muzagira iteka ryose: Ubugingo buhoraho hamwe nanjye, Yesu Kristo, mu bumwe butunganye n'Imana ubwayo? Cyangwa gucirwaho iteka ry'iteka mu muriro utazima? Ese icyaha mufashe mu ntoki zanyu murarikira, ndetse n'uburwayi bwanyu bw'inzoga n'ibiyobyabwenge, ni ingenzi cyane ku buryo mwahisemo kurimbuka, ndetse mugateza iherezo ryanyu ku bo mukunda babakurikira aho kunkurikira?

Ntimuri hasi cyane ku buryo ntabasha kubazamura, kandi nifuza cyane kubababarira, ariko mugomba kwitaba ijwi ryanjye kandi mukava mu nzira zanyu mu kwihana. Mumenye inzoga zanyu kandi mureke kwiha uburozi, kandi muze kuri njye mu kwihana, nanjye nzabakiza. Nta gihe cyo guta; ntimunyihorere ngo mwijande mu businzi kandi mwibagirwe amagambo yanjye, kuko amagambo yanyu bwite yo kwanga azandikwa mu ijuru nk'umuhamya ubashinja ku munsi w'urubanza, kandi ntimuzarokoka!

Ku mwicanyi, ndavuga nti: Hagarika ako kanya urugomo rwawe rwo kwica abaremwe mu ishusho y'Imana, ndetse na mugenzi wawe, kuko iki ari icyaha gikomeye. Ni ukuri, kandi mu kuri kudahinduka, mwiyishe ubwanyu muri buri buzima mwatwaye, kandi ntamfite nta muti w'igihano cyanyu kigenda kiza. Ariko ndetse n'ubwicanyi bwanyu bwishyuwe byuzuye mu gitambo cyanjye, kuko nta na kimwe nasize nkoze. Nerekanye ubuntu bwanjye mu buzima bw'Intumwa ikomeye Pawulo nk'umuhamya wo gutera inkunga, yemwe bicanyi.

Kuko na Sawuli yari yarayobejwe kandi yerekeza ku rubanza rukomeye mu nzira ijya i Damasiko, aho namukijije we ubwe. Pawulo yatoteje, ababaza, kandi yemera ndetse akora ubwicanyi ku Itorero ryanjye ryera! Kuko abibyiyemerera mu Ibyakozwe n'Intumwa ati: Kandi natoteje ab'iyo nzira kugeza gupfa, nabohera abagabo n'abagore kubashyira mu nzu y'imbohe. Nyamara yemeye ituro ryanjye ry'ubugingo buhoraho maze angira, Yesu Kristo, Umwami we kandi ankorera mu budahemuka nk'icyitegererezo cyo kwihana kuri bose, nanjye mushyira hejuru cyane kubw'ukumvira kwe. Nishyuriye inzira zanyu z'ubwicanyi niba muzazihana mukangarukira mu kwihana nyakuri, kuko nimubikora ubu, nzabakira mu buntu n'imbabazi. Ntimuyobe; nta gihe cyo guta gisigaye.

Ku basuzuguritse kurusha abandi bose, ndavuga nti: Ndetse nabaciriye inzira yo kuva mu busazi bwanyu n'urujijo, kuko nabarekeye irari ryanyu ribi nk'uko mu Abaroma havuga ngo: Ni cyo cyatumye Imana ibareka ngo bakurikize irari ry'isoni nke: kuko n'abagore babo bakoresheje imibiri yabo ibinyuranye n'ibyaremwe: Kandi n'abagabo na bo ni uko, bareka gukoresha abagore ibyaremwe, bashyuhirana mu irari ryabo ubwabo, abagabo bakoranirana n'abandi bagabo ibiteye isoni, bakibonera ingororano ikwiriye kuyoba kwabo.

Nimuveyo mu byaha byanyu by'umwanda, kandi muve mu bwibone bwanyu butagira umumaro, mureke gutuka ikimenyetso cyahawe Nowa na buri kiremwa cyose kizima, ari cyo umukororombya wigaragaza mu bicu by'imvura. Umukororombya ni uw'uko isi imenya ko Imana itazongera kurimbuza isi amazi ukundi, nubwo urubanza rw'umuriro rwegereje. Nimuze aho ndi mwihane, muhindure imigenzereze yanyu kandi mwambaze izina ryanjye, munyatureho kuba Umwami mubikuye ku mutima wuzuye kwizera kuzuka kwanjye, nanjye nzabakira. Mwihute! Kuko nta gihe mufite cyo gushidikanya, kuko urubanza rugiye gusukwa. Nta gihe cyo guta gisigaye.

Nimwumve amagambo yanjye kandi muze aho ndi, kuko ntanezezwa n'urubanza n'irimbuka bigiye kuza, ndetse na Yehova ntabyishimira, oya, ndetse n'Imana Ishoborabyose! Kuko Adamu yaremwe mu rukundo rwa Data no mu butungane mu gukiranuka n'ubwera nyakuri. Imana yamuhaye ubuzima bwuzuye nta na kimwe abuze. Nuko Satani agaba igitero ku byaremwe maze yanduza imibiri yanyu ndetse na kamere yanyu ku buryo muyoba mukiri mu nda, kuko icyaha kiri mu maraso yanyu: Ubuzima bw'umubiri buri mu maraso. Nyamara Imana, mu mbabazi zayo nyinshi, kuko imbabazi zayo zihoraho iteka ryose, ndetse Imana Ishoborabyose yampaye, njye mwana wayo w'ikinege, nk'igitambo gitunganye kizabakiza rwose niba gusa muzaza aho ndi mwihana ibyaha byanyu. Zzaburizamo imirimo ya Satani mu buzima bwanyu niba muzaza aho ndi. Ndabinginze nimuze, kuko amaboko yanjye atazakomeza kuramburwa igihe kirekire!

Hasigaye igihe gito cyane, kandi amakuba ya Yakobo agiye kugwirira isi yose, kandi imitima y'abantu izabavamo kubera ubukana bw'ibigiye kuza. Satani yateguye ibizira byinshi azarekura, kuko kwanga umuhamagaro wanjye ari uguhitamo ubwami bwa Satani bw'urupfu n'irimbuka; nanjye nzabarekera amahitamo yanyu, kandi nzabaha umwijima n'umubabaro mukunda cyane, kandi muzarya ku mbuto z'inzira zanyu keretse mwihannye. Kuko ibi ari byo, uyu ni wo muhamagaro wa nyuma mbere y'uko urubanza ruza vuba. Ndetse n'abagaragu banjye n'abakozi banjye barataka kugeza ku mpera z'isi muri uyu mwanya, gusa bazatakambira agakiza kanyu kugeza ubwo bazashikuzwa bakansanga mu bicu ubwo nzaza gutwara umugeni wanjye mu kirere vuba aha, kandi ibi bizaba mu buryo butunguranye, nubwo mbabwira nweruye mbere y'uko biba. Hanyuma umuhamagaro wanjye wa nyuma w'ubuntu n'imbabazi uzaba urangiye. Kandi ubwo nzashikuza umugeni wanjye nkamujyana atekanye, irimbuka ritunguranye rizagwirira isi, kandi benshi muri mwe bumva aya magambo ntibazarokoka. Bamwe muri mwe batekereza mu bupfu ko mushobora gukomeza gukoresha nabi ubuntu bwanjye kandi ko buzakomeza iteka, ariko ukuri kubabaje ni uko ubuntu buzahinduka urubanza, kandi bizaba mu buryo butunguranye, kandi benshi ntibazarokoka.

Kuko abatazicwa n'iri rimbuka ritunguranye rigiye gusukwa kuri Babuloni y'Ibanga bazisanga mu isi batigeze barota ko ishoboka, isi irimo urupfu n'irimbuka bihoraho, kuko Satani azatsemba mu buryo buteguye ikintu cyose gifitanye isano nanjye, Yesu Kristo, kandi azabikora nta mbabazi. Hazabaho kubabazwa mu gihugu cyose n'ubugome ndengakamere, kandi kuza aho ndi muri iki gihe cy'urubanza n'ikigeragezo bizaba ari byo bigoye cyane; ariko umuntu wese uzambaza izina ry'Umwami azakizwa, kandi bazagomba kwihangana kugeza ku iherezo, kandi benshi bazacibwa imitwe, kandi benshi bazapfa mu mibabaro ikomeye.

Ariko na bwo, Imana izahanagura amarira yose ku maso yabo, kuko Yohana Umuhishuzi yabatangarije ati: Aba ni bo bavuye mu makuba akomeye, bamesa amakanzu yabo, bayejesha amaraso y'Umwana w'Intama. Ni cyo gituma bari imbere y'intebe y'Imana, bakayikorera ku manywa na nijoro mu Rusengero rwayo: kandi uwicaye ku Ntebe y'Ubwami azabana na bo. Ntibazongera gusonza, kandi ntibazongera kugira inyota, izuba ntirizabica, cyangwa icyokere cyose. Kuko Umwana w'Intama uri hagati y'intebe y'ubwami azabaragira, akabayobora ku masoko y'amazi y'ubugingo: kandi Imana izahanagura amarira yose ku maso yabo.

Njye, ndetse nanjye, Kristo Yesu, Umwami wazutse akazamuka mu ijuru, ndetse na Mesiya nyakuri: Ndabahamagara, kandi ibyiringiro byanjye n'icyo ntegereje ni uko mwemera ituro ryanjye ry'amahoro mukaza mu gakiza kanjye kugira ngo muzabane nanjye iteka ryose mu gituza cya Data! Gusa ntimwange umuhamagaro wanjye, kuko ntazahamagara igihe kirekire, kandi ntizongera guhamagara mu buntu n'imbabazi muri ubu buryo ukundi, kuko igihe cyarangiye (nubwo ubuntu n'imbabazi bihorana nanjye iteka). Ndabinginze nimuze aho ndi kugira ngo mbakize muri aya masegonda ya nyuma y'ubuntu. Kuko ntimutandukanye n'abizeye mu ntangiriro y'igihe cyanjye cy'ubuntu, kandi agakiza kanjye n'ingororano bizaba ibyanyu niba mwumviye ijwi ryanjye nonaha. Nonaha ni cyo gihe cy'agakiza! Nimwihane muze aho ndi mwicishije bugufi. Mungire Umwami wanyu kandi mumenye ko ndiho iteka ryose, kuko Imana yanzuye mu bapfuye kugira ngo ntazongera gupfa ukundi. Niba mwemeye ubutumire bwanjye, muzaba munesheje, kandi muzahabwa ingororano z'unesha:

Dore mpagaze ku rugi ndakomanga: umuntu niyumva ijwi ryanjye agakingura urugi, nzinjira iwe, nsangire na we, na we asangire nanjye. Unesha, nzamuha kwicarana nanjye ku ntebe yanjye y'ubwami, nk'uko nanjye nanesheje nkicarana na Data ku ntebe ye y'ubwami. Musome ibi kenshi gashoboka, kuko mbahaye amahirwe ya nyuma y'agakiza ku bw'ubuntu. Gusa ntimwange umuhamagaro wanjye, kuko nta gihe kigihari cy'ibyo bintu. Kuri benshi muri mwe, kunyanga bizaba ibya burundu kandi bihoraho, kandi ntimuzakira cyangwa ngo murokoke urubanza rugiye kuza, kuko irimbuka ritunguranye ribariho. Kandi Umwuka n'Umugeni baravuga bati: Ngwino. Kandi uwumva navuge ati: Ngwino. Kandi ufite inyota naze. Kandi ushaka wese, ajyane amazi y'ubugingo ku buntu.

Kandi dusoza: Ukiranirwa, nakomeze akiranirwe: n'uwanduye, nakomeze yandure: n'ukiranuka, nakomeze akiranuke: n'uwera, nakomeze yezwe. Kandi dore, ndaza vuba, nzitwaza ingororano yanjye, kugira ngo ngororere umuntu wese ibikwiriye ibyo yakoze. Bibe bityo.

Ndahamagara abanyabyaha mu mwuka w'urukundo rwanjye.

Ndi Umwami w'abami n'Umutware w'abatware.

Ndi Umwana w'Intama w'Imana ukuraho ibyaha by'isi.

Ndi hano kugira ngo mbakize.

Ndi umwana w'intama wa Pasika wa nyuma.

Ndi ituro ryanyu ry'amahoro.

Ndabahamagara ubu muri iki gihe cya nyuma.

Ndabaha agakiza n'incungu.

Ndabahamagarira ubu kwihana no kubabarirwa ibyaha.

Ndabatumira mu mahoro n'ubugingo buhoraho.

Ndiracyabahamagara.
