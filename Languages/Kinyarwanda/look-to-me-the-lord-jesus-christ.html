Ubuhamya Bubiri: Itorero
Mu Mirimo ya Yohana Umubatiza
28 Werurwe 2024

Nimumbange amaso, jyewe Umwami Yesu Kristo!

Bana banjye, nimufungure amaso yanyu mundebe, jyewe Umwami Yesu Kristo. Nimukanguke mu bitotsi mugaruke aho ndi; mugaruke aho ndi, kuko amaboko yanjye aramburiwe mwe, intore zanjye. Vuba aha muraba muri kumwe nanjye, kuko isaha igeze ku munota wa nyuma kandi ibihe by'ubuntu byegereje kurangira, kandi nzabishyitsa. Nimukanguke mu bitotsi, kandi nzavuna amarozi y'iyi si ku maso yanyu. Murire kandi muboroge ku bw'ibyaha byanyu no kwanga kuza aho ndi, kandi mumenye ko mbahamagara kuri uyu munota wa nyuma. Ndabahamagara ngo mugaruke aho ndi, urukundo rwanyu rwa mbere, nubwo mwashyize abandi mu mitima yanyu.

Mubahunge bose, kuko ari abatekamutwe; yego, ni amasega yambaye uruhu rw'intama, kandi mwasinziriye mu maboko yabo mabi. Ntimusinzire, bana banjye, ahubwo nimukanguke muntegereze, kuko ibimenyetso byanjye byamaze kugaragara mu kirere kandi nzaza nk'umujura nijoro. Nzahasanga iki nimgaruka gutwara Itorero ryanjye? Ese nzahasanga kwizera? Yewe, torero ry'i Lawodikiya, uri akazuyazi, kandi nakwifuje ko uba ubukonje cyangwa ubushyuhe. Nimungarukire nanone, nanjye nzabeza mbakure mu bitotsi byanyu, kandi nzomora ibikomere mwatewe n'ababi. Nta cyifuzo mfite cyo kubaruka, nuko rero nimungarukire nanone, nanjye nzabakira. Mumenye ko urukundo mbakunda rudashira kandi ruhora rukomeye rudahinduka.

Nta wundi utari jyewe ushobora kubakiza, nta wundi utari jyewe ushobora kubakiza ngo abakize indwara, nta wundi utari jyewe ushobora kubarinda no kubaha ubugingo buhoraho, nta wundi utari jyewe ushobora kubakiza ibyaha byanyu, kuko nta rindi zina ryatanzwe abantu bashobora gukirizwamo. Ndi ibyiringiro byanyu bihoraho wabaremye bundi bushya mu kuvuka kwanjye gushya nabasutseho. Mumenye ko muri abana ba Data, kandi mufite umurage ukomeye ku bw'ubudahemuka bwanyu. Nyamara, nubwo nzaca urubanza rw'imirimo yanyu kugira ngo muhembwe cyangwa muhombe, mwavuye mu gukiranirwa mwinjira mu bugingo buhoraho binyuze mu kunyizera no mu Bwami bwanjye buhoraho. Dore! Nabagize abami n'abatambyi imbere ya Data, kuko ndi umutambyi mukuru iteka ryose mu buryo bwa Melikisedeki.

Ni inde wakwihandagaza ngo abarwanye? Kuko kurwanya bene Data ari ukundurwanya, kandi nzishyura, niko Umwami ukomeye w'iteka w'icyubahiro wo mu ijuru avuga! Ibintu byose mu ijuru no mu isi byashyizwe mu maboko yanjye, nuko rero mumenye ko mfite ubutware bukuru, kandi nta n'umwe unduta uretse Data, wanshyize hejuru ya byose. None se ni inde mwatinya? Ese gutotezwa n'amakuba ntibituma murushaho gukomera muri jye? Ese siko bimeze ko iyo imbaraga zanyu zibabanye nke, mba ndi aho ngaho kugira ngo mbakomeze mu ntege nke zanyu?

Nuko rero nimuhagarare mushikamye kandi mukomere muri jye! Kuki mushidikanya amagambo yanjye? Ahubwo, nimunyizere kandi muvuge amagambo mbaha, kuko aya magambo akomeye kandi afite imbaraga niba gusa muvuganye kwizera. Muvuge mufite icyizere n'ubutware, kandi mutegeke ibintu by'ubutindi mu izina rya Yesu Kristo, izina ryanjye rikomeye risumba yose. Vuba aha muzambonesha amaso yanyu, kandi ukuhaba kwanyu mu ijuru kwifuzwa cyane nanjye na Data, yego, ndetse na Yeshua na Yahweh. Muri ubutunzi bukomeye bwa Data, kandi ntimugereranywa. Kuko ahandi ni he hatangwa amaraso atagira icyaha ku bw'ibyaha byanyu uretse muri jye?

Yemwe ababi mwicara mukishimira ibibi, mwibwira ko mufite intsinzi ikomeye. Nzavuruga imigambi yanyu mibi, kandi muzashobora gukora gusa ibyo Data yagennye ko mushobora gukora mu rwego rwo guteza imbere imigambi ye. Muri abanyantege nke n'abatindi kandi mwatsinzwe rwose. Yewe, muntu wa kamere, kuki ugihitamo inzira mbi z'ababi bakuyobya mu buryo bubi? Ese ibinezeza by'icyaha bikwiriye gutandukanywa iteka n'icyubahiro cy'Imana ihoraho? Ni he muzabona ubugingo hanze yanjye? Ni inde uzabatabara uretse uwo mukomeza kwanga? Ese ubuntu bwanjye n'imbabazi zanjye ntibyakumiriye ukuboko k'umwanzi?

Ikintu cyose cyiza mufite cyamanutse gituruka kuri Se w'imicyo, udahinduka cyangwa ngo agire igicucu cyo kwihinduriza. Ariko oya, mwanze ubuntu bwanjye kandi mukoresha imbabazi zanjye kugira ngo mwishimishe kandi mwinezeze mu gukiranirwa kwanyu. Dore! Kurimbuka gukomeye kuzabatungura. Mwagambaniwe n'abayobozi banyu bwite, bacura imigambi yo kubarimbura kandi baretse ingabo zitukura zinjira. Kurimbuka n'ibibi bikomeye bizabazaho gitumo, kandi bizaza ubwo nzakura umugeni wanjye hagati y'inzira zanyu mbi. Hanyuma kurimbuka gutunguranye kuzabatega, kuko ni uwuhe mutego utegwa imbere y'inyoni? Oya, umutego uhishe neza witeguye gukora, kandi bizaba ubwo uwo nzitira azakurwaho, kandi ntimuzarokoka.

Hanyuma imitima yanyu izashonga muri mwe kandi imbaraga zanyu zizabashiramo ubwo muzabonesha amaso yanyu ibiremwa bigiye kurekurwa biva mu kuzimu ngo bibagereho. Nzafungura ikuzimu, kuko mfite ubutware naho; kuko mfite imfunguzo z'urupfu n'ikuzimu, kandi nta wushobora kuzimnyaga. Iyo muza gusobanukirwa ko nta cyo mutunze ku isi, kandi byose ari impano y'ubuntu n'imbabazi, kuko urukundo rw'Imana ruyobora abantu ku kwihana. Ariko oya, murundanya umururumba w'ibyiza by'Imana kuri mwe ubwanyu kandi mukica abaturanyi banyu aho kubakunda nkuko mwikunda, nkuko nabategetse. Imana yakunze abari mu isi cyane byatumye itanga umwana wayo w'ikinege, Yesu w'i Nazareti, kugira ngo abiyunge na yo mu rukundo binyuze muri jye.

Ariko mu bwibone bwanyu n'inzira z'isi, mwinangiye imitima yanyu kuri jye n'agakiza kanjye. Ku bw'ibyo, kurimbuka gukomeye kuzaza ku gihugu cyanyu mu isaha imwe, kandi Babuloni y'ibanga ntizongera kubaho. Yego, Babuloni ikomeye, amabanga yawe azashyirwa ahagaragara bose bayabone, kandi abakugumaho bazahura n'urubanza rumwe.

Ku bw'ibyo rero, yewe muntu wanga Imana, icishe bugufi uze aho ndi none! Isaha yawe yo gukenera ubufasha ni none. Nungarukire; ndakwinginga, kandi ndaguhamagara mu izina! Sohoka muri iyi si igiye gupfa, kuko hari agakiza muri jye niba gusa wemeye.

Nta byishimo mfite mu rubanza rwanyu, kandi Data yifuza ko mubaho imbere ye, kandi mu rukundo rwe yahaye ibyaremwe bye amahitamo: Nshyize imbere yanyu uyu munsi ubugingo n'urupfu, nuko rero, muhitemo ubugingo kugira ngo mwe n'urubyaro rwanyu mubaho! Nuko rero nimumbange amaso bitaraba iby'ubusa kandi mumenye ko iyi si mbi mugundiriye izarimburwa rwose, kandi bizaba binyuze mu burakari bw'Umwana w'Intama. Ese Imana ntizacira urubanza inkozi z'ibibi zishe abana bayo, zamugaje kandi zihohotera abere, zishe umwana w'ikinege w'Imana? Nubwo natanze ubugingo bwanjye ku bushake ku bw'anyu kandi nta muntu wanyatse ubugingo bwanjye, hari urubanza rukomeye n'ubutabera bigomba gukorwa nanjye. Kuko naguze isi mu maraso yanjye n'igitambo cy'impongano, kandi ni iyanjye. Nzashyiraho ubwami bwo gukiranuka n'ubwera nyakuri, aho intare n'umwana w'intama bizaryamana, aho nta kintu kibi kizinjira. Ku bw'ibyo, nimuve mu byaha byanyu, kuko ndabakunda, kandi ndabahamagara n'umwuka wanjye wa nyuma mbere yuko akabuye k'umusenyi ka nyuma kagwa. Kandi byegereje.

Umwuka n'Umugeni baravuga bati, ngwino!

Ndabahamagara kuri uyu munota wa nyuma.

Ndabahamagara ngo mugaruke aho ndi.

Ndi hano kugira ngo mbakomeze mu ntege nke zanyu.
