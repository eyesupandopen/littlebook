Ubuhamya Bubiri: Itorero
Mu Mirimo y'Intumwa Pawulo
15 Gicurasi 2024

Dore, Ingororano yanjye iri kumwe nanjye!

Ku Mugeni wanjye wizerwa, nkunda, wankunze byimazeyo n'umutima we wose. Muri imboni y'ijisho ryanjye, kandi vuba nzaza kubatwara, vuba kuruta uko mubitekereza. Ku bw'ubudahemuka bwanyu, hari ingororano ikomeye mu ijuru mwateguriwe. Dore! Ijisho ritigeze ribona, cyangwa ugutwi ngo kumve, ndetse bitigeze byinjira mu mutima w'umuntu, ibyo Imana yateguriye abayikunda. Uko niko ukuri gutangaje kuberekeyeho, rukundo rwanjye, kuko muzasangira umurage wanjye nk'uko ubudahemuka bwanyu buzabihamya ku ntebe yanjye y'urubanza, ndetse na ya Bema ikomeye, aho nzaca urubanza rw'imirimo ya buri wese mu bo nahamagaye, natoranyije kandi bizerwa.

Ku bafite imirimo izashya, bazahomba, ariko bo ubwabo bazakizwa nk'abakuwe mu muriro; naho abafite imirimo izagumaho, bazahabwa ubutunzi bwinshi, kuko uwo munsi uzabigaragaza vuba. Kuko ibi atari igitekerezo cy'amahanga kuri mwe, ahubwo ni icyo mwize mu buhamya bw'Abakorinto mu rwandiko rwa kabiri rwa Pawulo: Kuko twese dukwiriye kuzagaragara imbere y'intebe y'urubanza ya Kristo, kugira ngo umuntu wese ahabwe ibikwiriye ibyo yakoze akiri mu mubiri, byaba byiza cyangwa bibi. Nuko rero, kuko tuzi igitinyiro cy'Umwami, twemeza abantu; ariko tugaragara imbere y'Imana, kandi nizeye ko tugaragara no mu mitima yanyu.

Kuko umutima nama wanyu uzahamanya n'amagambo yanyu ubwo muzaba musobanura ibyanyu imbere y'Umucamanza ukiranuka, ari we njye, Yesu Kristo. Bamwe bazahagarara imbere yanjye berekane imirimo yabo bwite izatwikwa n'umuriro w'utunganya bizabaviramo isoni n'igihombo. Bamwe bazahagarara imbere yanjye batunganye, biyogosheje mu maraso yanjye y'impongano, aho muzanyereka imirimo nabahaye ngo mukore. Kuko kwinjira mu buruhukiro bwanjye ari ukuruhuka imirimo yanyu bwite. Ku bwaruhukiye mu mirimo yanjye hari ingororano ikomeye ku bizerwa. Ariko mwese muzakizwa, kandi nta n'umwe muri mwe wagendeye imbere yanjye atunganye rwose, bityo uwo munsi uzabigaragaza. Ntimutinye, nzabagororera byimazeyo, kuko imirimo yose ikomeye mwankoreye mu ibanga izagaragazwa, bityo uwo munsi uzabigaragaza.

Kuki imitima yanyu ihagarikwa n'aya magambo? Ntimwasomye mu rwandiko rwa mbere rwa Yohana, aho namutegetse kwandika? Kuko niba imitima yacu iducira urubanza, Imana iruta imitima yacu, kandi izi byose. Bakundwa, niba imitima yacu itaducira urubanza, tuba dufite gushira amanga imbere y'Imana. Kandi: Niba tuvuga ko nta cyaha dufite, tuba twishuka, kandi ukuri ntikuba kuri muri twe. Niba twatura ibyaha byacu, ni yo yo kwizerwa kandi ikiranukira kutubabarira ibyaha byacu, no kutwezaho gukiranirwa kose. Nuko rero, mumbwire ibyaha byanyu buri munsi kandi kenshi uko mubishaka, kandi mwiyuhagire mu maraso yanjye y'impongano.

Kuko ndi umuvugizi wanyu, kandi mwaguzwe igitambo cyanjye nka Pasika itunganye kugira ngo nkuraho ibyaha, no gukora ubwiyunge bw'ibicumuro, no kuzana gukiranuka kw'iteka. Dore, Mugeni wanjye! Ibihe byo gusubizwa ibintu byose biri hafi, kuko Petero yahanuye iby'iki gihe ubwo yakizaga umuntu ku Irembo ry'Urusengero Ryiza: Kandi azatuma Yesu Kristo, uwo mwabwirijwe mbere. Uwo ijuru rikwiriye kwakira, kugeza mu bihe byo gusubizwa ibintu byose. Kandi noneho haje ibihe byo gusubizwa, Mugeni wanjye mwiza, kuko muri abera imbere yanjye kandi mwejejweho gukiranirwa kose mu maraso y'Umwana w'Intama, kuko ndi Umwana w'Intama w'Imana! Nuko rero, mwature ibyaha byanyu mu budahemuka.

Bityo urubanza, isubizwa, igihombo, n'ingororano ikomeye byose bitangirira mu nzu y'Imana. Kuko vuba aha, muzakoranyirizwa mu bicu gusanganira njye mu kirere, kuko nzamanuka mvuye mu ijuru n'ijwi rirenga, n'ijwi rya marayika mukuru, kandi impanda y'imperuka izavuga! Mukomeze kwitegereza ikirere kandi murebe, kuko Imana yakoze ibimenyetso byinshi bikomeye mu ijuru, ndetse itangaza ubukwe bw'umukwe n'umugeni we, ndetse n'isezerano ry'iteka hagati yanjye n'umugeni wanjye, rigiye kuba vuba. Nuko mwature ibyaha byanyu kandi munsabe kubereka ibyaha mwibagiwe kugira ngo nabyo mubyature; kandi hejuru ya byose, muhagarare mushikamye mu rukundo rw'Imana, kuko urukundo rw'Imana rutwikira ibyaha byinshi. Ntimutinye rero, kuko bidashoboka ko mubyatura byose.

Ku by'urubanza: kuko mwavuye mu rupfu mukajya mu bugingo muri njye, Umwami wanyu Yesu Kristo wazutse. Kuko natanze ubugingo bwanjye; ntawabuntwaye. Nabutanze ku bushake kandi nabikora ukundi bibaye ngombwa. Ariko nta mpamvu y'ikintu nk'icyo, kuko nabwiye Abaheburayo nti: Kuko bidashoboka ko amaraso y'amapfizi n'ay'ihene akuraho ibyaha. Ariko uyu mugabo, amaze gutamba igitambo kimwe cy'ibyaha iteka ryose, yicaye iburyo bw'Imana. Mugeni wanjye, sinzabacira urubanza nk'uko ibyaha byanyu biri; kuko abari muri Kristo bose batsindishirijwe, yego bejejwe, ndetse bagizwe abakiranutsi muri njye, Kristo Yesu, uzabaho kandi agategeka iteka ryose, kuko ubwami bwanjye butagira iherezo. Bityo mwagizwe abere ku byaha byanyu; nuko rero, ntimuzaba mu rubanza rw'abanyabyaha ahubwo mu rubanza rw'abakiranutsi, kuko urubanza rutangirira mu nzu y'Imana.

Ku by'isubizwa: kuko nzishyura, niko Uwiteka avuga. Benshi muri mwe basize amazu, ibihugu, imiryango kandi bigomwa ibintu byinshi, byinshi; kandi benshi mu Mugeni wanjye ndetse batanze ubuzima bwabo, ni Abahowe Imana banjye. Bityo hazabaho isubizwa ry'ibintu byigomwe ndetse n'ibyatakaye. Kuko iherezo rya Yobu ryabaye irihe: Kandi Uwiteka akura Yobu mu bunyage, ubwo yasabiraga inshuti ze: kandi Uwiteka aha Yobu ibikubye kabiri ibyo yari afite mbere. Kandi ijwi rya Yakobo mwene data naryo rigira riti: Dore, twita abahirwe abihangana. Mwumvise iby'ukwihangana kwa Yobu, kandi mwabonye iherezo ry'Umwami: ko Umwami agira imbabazi nyinshi n'impuhwe. Kandi niko So ari, kandi niko ndi, kuko ndi uwo NDI WE ukomeye!

Ku by'igihombo: kuko benshi bazahomba, kuko navuze ku byerekeye intebe yanjye y'urubanza ku Bakorinto: Kuko twese dukwiriye kuzagaragara imbere y'intebe y'urubanza ya Kristo, kugira ngo umuntu wese ahabwe ibikwiriye ibyo yakoze akiri mu mubiri, byaba byiza cyangwa bibi. Kandi nanone Abakorinto barahamya: Umurimo w'umuntu wese uzagaragazwa. Kuko uwo munsi uzabigaragaza, kuko bizahishurwa n'umuriro, kandi umuriro uzagerageza umurimo w'umuntu wese uko umeze. Niba umurimo w'umuntu wese uzashya, azahomba: ariko we ubwe azakizwa: nyamara nk'ukuwe mu muriro.

Ku by'ingororano ikomeye: kuko Abakorinto bavuga nanone: Ijisho ritigeze ribona, cyangwa ugutwi ngo kumve, ndetse bitigeze byinjira mu mutima w'umuntu, ibyo Imana yateguriye abayikunda. Bizerwa banjye kandi batoranyijwe, ntimushobora kumva ibirori bikomeye n'iminsi mikuru mwateguriwe na Data mu ijuru; kuko muje mu birori, kandi ubutunzi bukomeye bwabitswe ku bwanyu mu ijuru. (Kuko mutashye iwanjye, Kristo Yesu, na Data, Yehova, Imana ihoraho.) Kandi nanone: niba umurimo w'umuntu wese agumyeho, uwo yubatseho, azahabwa ingororano. Mfite ingororano nyinshi zo gutanga, kandi umurage wanjye uri kumwe nanjye; ntimutinye rero, kuko muzasangira umurage wanjye; kandi aho ndi, niko namwe muzaba, kuko ibihe byo gutandukana byarangiye.

Amakamba y'ingororano n'ubutware arabategereje. Nashyize amwe mu byanditswe: ikamba ritangirika, ikamba ryo kwishima, ikamba ryo gukiranuka, ikamba ry'ubwiza, n'ikamba ry'ubugingo.

Ku birukanse isiganwa, ndetse n'inzira z'umurimo nabashyiriyeho, bazahabwa ikamba ritangirika: kuko muzaba mwaragandukirije umwuka wanjye, kandi muzaba mwaritoje nk'uko umukinnyi wese yabikora. Ku batsinze isiganwa mu buryo bwemewe n'amategeko ni ikamba ritangirika.

Hariho ikamba ryo kwishima. Kuko ibyiringiro byacu ni ibiki, cyangwa ibyishimo, cyangwa ikamba ryo kwishima? Mbese si mwe imbere y'Umwami wacu Yesu Kristo mu kuza kwe? Kuko, muri ubwiza bwacu n'ibyishimo. Kandi bityo tuzagira ibyishimo byinshi, kandi mwese muzaba mu bwiza kandi muhabwe ubwiza muri njye! Ku bakoze mu budahemuka kugira ngo bahindure kandi bazamure bene wanyu ni ikamba ryo kwishima.

Hariho ikamba ryo gukiranuka ku barangiza isiganwa ryabo no ku bakunda kuboneka kwanjye. Nuko mukomeze kureba hejuru, kuko ndi hafi kandi mfite ibyiringiro bikomeye byo kuboneka kwanjye. Kuko ibyiringiro bikomeye byo kuboneka kwanjye nabyo bizana ingororano ikomeye. Ku bafite ibyiringiro bikomeye byo kuboneka kwanjye ni ikamba ryo gukiranuka.

Hariho ikamba ry'ubwiza ku bagaburiye umukumbi w'Imana. Kuko Petero yanditse ati: mugaburire umukumbi w'Imana uri muri mwe, muwurinda, mutabikora ku gahato, ahubwo mubikunze: mutabikoreye indamu mbi, ahubwo mubikunze. Ku batigize abami ku murage w'Imana kandi batatewe ishyaka n'indamu mbi kandi bankoreye mu budahemuka mu kwicisha bugufi n'umutima ukwiye w'urukundo ni ikamba ry'ubwiza.

Hariho ikamba ry'ubugingo nzaha abahowe Imana banjye, ku bababazwa n'amakuba n'ibitotezo ku bw'izina ryanjye no ku bihanganiye amayeri n'imigambi ya Satani. Ku banesheje ibi bintu mu budahemuka ni ikamba ry'ubugingo.

Muzahindurwa nkanjye, Adamu wa nyuma kandi muzaba nkanjye, kandi muzambona uko ndi, kandi niko namwe muzaba. Dore! Nzaremamo imibiri mishya ku bwanyu idasa n'ikintu cyose mushobora gutekereza. Reba inyandiko mu byanditswe aho nabonekeye abizerwa banjye mbere yo kuzamurwa mu ijuru. Nzadoda uyu mubiri by'umwihariko kuri mwe, kandi uzaba ufite ubushobozi bwanyu, ibyo mwagezeho, ibyo mwakoze; kandi muzatangara cyane.

Ntimwumva icyo nategetse Pawulo kwandikira Abakorinto? Hariho n'imibiri yo mu ijuru, n'imibiri yo mu isi: Ariko ubwiza bw'iyo mu ijuru ni ukundi, n'ubwiza bw'iyo mu isi ni ukundi. Hariho ubwiza bw'izuba, n'ubundi bwiza bw'ukwezi, n'ubundi bwiza bw'inyenyeri: kuko inyenyeri imwe itandukanye n'indi mu bwiza. Uko niko no kuzuka kw'abapfuye kuri: bibibwa mu kubora, bizurwa mu kutabora. Bana banjye, muzagira ubushobozi bwinshi mutigeze murota. Nubwo buri wese muri mwe azaba atandukanye n'undi, mwese muzagendera muri njye mu buryo butunganye, kuko nzabagira abami n'abatambyi imbere y'Imana yacu!

Hazabaho ibintu byinshi mutazongera kuremerwa nabyo. Ntihazabaho indwara ukundi, nta cyaha ukundi kuri mwe, nta rupfu ukundi kuri mwe, nta bwoba ukundi, nta guhangayika; kuko mwese muzaremwa bundi bushya mu buryo butunganye muri njye, Kristo Yesu Umwami. Nuko ndababwira nti, Mugeni wanjye, mube abaneshi, kuko ingororano z'unesha nzazitanga, kandi birenze ibyo mutekereza n'inzozi zanyu. Kuko igiti cy'ubugingo, cyeraga imbuto z'amoko cumi n'abiri, cyeraga imbuto zacyo buri kwezi: n'amababi y'icyo giti yari ayo gukiza amahanga. Kandi ntihazabaho umuvumo ukundi, ahubwo intebe y'Imana n'iy'Umwana w'Intama izaba irimo, kandi abagaragu bayo bazayikorera. Kandi bazabona mu maso hayo, n'izina ryayo rizaba mu ruhanga rwabo. Kandi ntihazabaho ijoro aho ngaho, kandi ntibakeneye itara, cyangwa urumuri rw'izuba, kuko Umwami Imana ibaha urumuri, kandi bazategeka iteka ryose. Ntimutinye rero, Mugeni wanjye wizerwa, ingororano ikomeye igiye gusohora kuri mwe vuba. Kuko Imana itakiranirwa, ngo yibagirwe umurimo wanyu n'imiruho y'urukundo, mwerekanye ku bw'Izina ryayo, kuko mwakoreye Abera, kandi mukibakorera.

Ndaje kubatwara, vuba kuruta uko mubitekereza.

Ndi umuvugizi wanyu.

Ndi we kandi niko namwe muzaba, kuko muzambona uko ndi.

Ndaje, kandi ingororano yanjye iri kumwe nanjye ku bizerwa.

Ndi uwo uzabaha umurage wanyu byuzuye, kuko niko nabiherewe.

Ndi uwo uzabaha imibiri mishya yo gusimbura umubiri w'icyaha.

Ndi we, kuko ndi uwo NDI WE ukomeye!

Mushake kuboneka kwanjye mu bicu, kuko ibimenyetso bizantangaza mbere y'uko umucyo wanjye utamurura ikirere. Njye, Yeshua HaMashiach, nohereje aya Magambo.
