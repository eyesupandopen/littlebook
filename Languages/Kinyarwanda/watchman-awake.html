Ubuhamya Bubiri: Itorero
Mu Mirimo ya Yohana Umubatiza
5 Mata 2024

Murinzi Kanguka!

Murinzi, kanguka, ukenyeze ukuri, kandi wambare icyuma gikingira igituza cyo gukiranuka!

Murinzi, kanguka, mwambare ingofero y'agakiza n'inkota y'Umwuka, ari yo Jambo ry'Imana!

Murinzi, kanguka, mukwete mu birenge byanyu kwitegura kuvuga Ubutumwa Bwiza bw'amahoro!

Bura abantu banjye kandi ntuhishe ururimi rwawe, kuko narubohoye! Rwanira abantu banjye kandi ubure intore zanjye, kuko ingororano y'umuhanuzi uzayihabwa n'uvuga amagambo nzaguha. KANGUKA! Ese sinabwiye abantu banjye gutegereza kuza kwanjye? Ese ibihe n'ibihe ntibigaragara kandi biri kuri mwe? Nuko rero, mutegereze kugaragara kwanjye, kuko kuri hafi. Murebe ibimenyetso n'ibitangaza mu kirere, kuko Data yateguye inzira zabyo uhereye mbere na mbere, kandi umunsi warashyizweho. Mutegereze umucyo wanjye mu kirere kuko impanda igiye kuvuga; dore, narabivuze.

Bantu banjye, musuzume Ijambo ryanjye kandi murebe mu Byanditswe Byera byanjye kugira ngo mwumve ko nzajyana umugeni wanjye mukamukura mu rubanza rw'abakora ibibi. Nzarinda umugeni wanjye n'umubiri wanjye; kuko ni nde muntu wigeze wanga umubiri we, ahubwo arawugaburira akawukuyakuya? Murebe, mwitonde, mwitegereze ibihe, kandi mumenye ko ibihe byageze kandi umunsi uzatungurana nk'izuba rikomeye rirashe gitungura mu kirere. Sinzihakanwa, kandi sinzahagarikwa. Nubwo umujura azagerageza, azivuga nk'intare; ariko intare itagira amenyo niyo gusa ari. Ntazanhagarika, kandi sinzangirwa umutungo wanjye, kuko naguze isi cyane cyane abantu banjye mu maraso yanjye atagira icyaha. Kandi izina ryanjye riri hejuru ya byose uretse irya Data, umuruta; kandi sinzihakanwa, sinzitambirwa, cyangwa ngo mpagarikwe.

Ndaje, kandi sinzaceceka, kandi uburakari bwanjye burakaze kuri Babuloni y'Ibanga, yego, Babuloni y'Ibanga icuruza abana bato ikabagira ibicuruzwa mu maboko y'abantu babi, bazahagarikwa. Byari kuba byiza iyo batavuka kuriko kuvuka bagakora ibyaha by'ubugome n'ibibabaza ku bana. Byari kuba byiza iyo urusyo rumanikwa mu majosi yabo, bakajugunywa mu nyanja; ariko babikiwe umuriro utazima, kandi ubutabera buzubahirizwa vuba, kuko umujinya w'Umwana w'Intama ugiye gutwikira isi yose kandi amakuba ya Yakobo azaduka igihe ubuza azaba akuweho.

Mwikomeze, bantu banjye, kandi muhagarare bwuma muri njye n'umutima umwe n'intego imwe. Mureke iby'isi mwifuje, kandi mungire, njye Mwami Yesu Kristo, icyifuzo cyanyu; kuko ndi umunara wanyu ukomeye, urutare rw'ubwirinzi, n'umurinzi w'inyuma. Ni nde uzabatera ushobora guhangana n'ukuboko kwanjye gukomeye? Intore zanjye n'abizerwa banjye bari mu maboko yanjye arambuye, kandi ntawe ushobora kubankura. Muri abanjye, kandi mutekanye muri njye; nuko rero muruhukire muri njye muve mu mirimo yanyu, kuko ibihe byo kwishyura bizaza gitungura. Igihembo cy'urubanza ku bakora ibibi kiri hano, kandi igihembo cy'abizerwa banjye kiri ku ntore zanjye, kuko nzabagororera umurage wabo, kuko nabagize abakwiriye.

Yewe kiyoka gitesha agaciro, kitagira amenyo; wa nzoka ya kera: igihe cyawe cyageze kandi urubanza ruri kuri wowe. Nubwo wibwira mu bwibone bwawe ko uzatsinda, nzatwara byose wakusanyirije mu maboko yawe y'ubujura, kandi abagukurikira mu mururumba wabo w'irari bazasangira umurage wawe mu nyanja y'umuriro, aho buri wese azagororerwa hakurikijwe imirimo yabo inshuro nyinshi. Umujinya w'Imana uzagumaho iteka ryose, kandi ntuzigera uruhuka.

Igihano gikomeye kizaba umuhoza wawe, kandi umwotsi w'amazuku yaka uzahora mu mazuru yawe iteka, kandi uzishyura iteka ryose. Kandi umwotsi w'umubabaro wawe uzacumba iteka, kandi abagukurikira bose uhereye ku muto kugeza ku mukuru bazagira umurage wabo n'ingororano hamwe nawe mu birimi by'umuriro byera by'ubururu bizagutwikira iteka kuko uzabohwa ukajugunywa mu iherezo ryawe. Hazabaho kurira no guhekenya amenyo kwinshi. Kandi mwibwiraga ko mushobora guhangana na Yehova na Kristo we, nyamara muzahindurwa ubusa uretse agasuzuguro k'iteka mu mubabaro wanyu utagira iherezo, kandi muzabikwa iteka ryose. Kandi ntimuzigera mwongera kugirira nabi ibyo naremye, kandi muzibagirana burundu, kandi ntimuzongera kwibukwa.

Nuko rero bantu banjye, mugeni wanjye wizerwa nkunda cyane, ni iki mufite cyo gutinya? Umujinya wanjye n'umujinya wa So si uwanyu kandi ntuzabageraho, kandi ntimuzigera muba mu kaga k'urupfu rwa kabiri. Ntimutinye rero kuko nzihimura byuzuye ku babarwanya, kuko guhora ari ukwanjye, niko Uwiteka avuga! Ntimuzi ko muzacira abamarayika urubanza? Ntimuzi ko muzacira isi urubanza? Nuko rero mwiyeze muri njye, kuko amaraso yanjye y'impongano ari yo yonyine azabagira abakwiriye guhagarara imbere yanjye mu bwiza. Muhishe ijambo ryanjye mu mitima yanyu, kandi mugumye inyandiko zanjye mu ruhanga rwanyu, kandi muruhukire mu magambo yanjye ya kera; kuko ari ubuzima kuri mwe, kandi ubuzima bwanyu buhishe muri njye, kandi nzabuhishura!

Yewe Babuloni y'Ibanga, iyaba wari wasobanukiwe igitambo cy'amahoro Data yakoherereje, kandi none ukaba wanyanze, njye Yesu w'i Nazareti, kandi byabaye nk'uko ibyanditswe byera bivuga ko abantu babi n'abayobya bazagenda barushaho kuba babi. Ariko wowe, yewe Babuloni y'Ibanga ikomeye, wanduje umuheto wa Data w'amabangi menshi yahaye Nowa n'abantu bose nk'isezerano ko atazigera yongera kurimbura isi yose n'amazi, kandi azakomeza ijambo rye. Ariko wowe, yewe Babuloni y'Amayobera, uzarimburwa mu isaha imwe.

Umuriro mwinshi uzakuza, umutingito ukomeye uzagushyira hasi; iminara yawe izagwa; ibikorwaremezo byawe bizabura mu matongo, kandi imuhengeri ikomeye izarengera inkombe zawe, kandi nzagororera ukurikije imirimo yawe, kandi urubanza rukomeye ruri kuri wowe. Kuko wigeze kuba urumuri rw'ibyiringiro n'urumuri rw'isi mu gihe gito, ariko wahisemo umururumba, ubwikunde, na ruswa nk'abafatanyabikorwa bawe, yewe maraya mukuru! Ni nde uzagukiza njye n'uburakari bwanjye n'umujinya wanjye wegereje ugukongera? Uhagaze mu minyururu, utegereje urubanza rwawe rwa nyuma, ruzagaragara kugira ngo isi irubone, kandi ubwoba bwinshi buzaba kuri benshi.

Dore! Amahanga menshi Data yayarimbuye mu rubanza, kuko ashyiraho rimwe agakuraho irindi, kuko Yehova ari umutegetsi w'ikirenga kandi ntawuruta Imana ikomeye ihoraho ihagararira kandi igakiza abayo mu muriro wabikiwe ababi. Dore! Amahanga yari agize amakompanyi akomeye aracyariho, nubwo mu buryo bugabanutse, ariko yakomeje kubaho. Ariko wowe, yewe maraya mukuru, wisambanyije n'abakunzi benshi kubera umururumba mubi n'ibinezeza by'icyaha. Wayobeje abantu banjye bava kuri njye ku isi yose kubera umwanda wahaye amahanga ngo arye kandi anywe, kandi uzajugunywa hasi burundu, kandi ntuzongera kubaho ukundi. Kandi mu ivu ryawe ishyanga rikomeye ntirizabyuka, nyamara abasigaye bazakizwa. Ukuboko kw'umwanzi kuri mu majosi yanyu, kandi nazanye ibishingwe mu mipaka yanyu. Bazasahura, banyage, kandi batware ibisigaye. Ni nde uzagutabara?

Kuko wihakanye umuturanyi wawe, kandi nta bwoba bw'Uwiteka buri mu mitima yanyu. Wavuze uti, Sindi umupfakazi, nyamara uri umupfakazi kandi uri impumyi n'igipfamatwi imbere yanjye. Nta gakiza kuri wowe kubera ubusambanyi bwawe wakoze n'isi; nuko rero, nzagushyira mu kurimbuka gukomeye. Ibyenda kuba bizaba byihuse cyane; hanyuma umwuzure ukomeye uzatwikira amajipo yawe. Umuriro uzagutwika burundu kandi uzeza abakora ibibi mu gihugu, kandi imbohe zanjye zizabohurwa, kandi nta gishobora guhagarika ibigiye kuba. Ndi Umwami Yesu Kristo, kandi ndabibabwira mbere yuko biba, kandi ntangaza ibigiye kuba nk'aho byamaze kuba, kuko aya magambo ari ay'ukuri, kandi nta gishobora kuyahindura, kuko ubuhanuzi buzuzuzwa.

Muhagarare bwuma, bantu banjye, kuko nzajyana umugeni wanjye; kuko mufitiye imigambi n'intego mu bwami bwanjye buzaza. Azahora ari kumwe nanjye, kandi ntazigera amva iruhande. Ndamukunda kandi ndamuha agaciro mu rukundo rwuzuye n'impuhwe. Nzabagira abakomeye mu bwiza bwanjye, kandi muzakora imirimo ikomeye mu murage wanyu uzaza, ntimutinye rero. Mumenye ko umutima wanjye umenetse, kandi mbabajwe n'abantu banjye batiteguye. Bamwe bazasigara inyuma kuko imana y'iyi si yahumye imitima yabo, kandi benshi ntibiteguye. Ntimutinye, nubwo muzanyihanganira byinshi, mwese muzakizwa mwe mufite ikimenyetso cy'Imana ihoraho. Nzabaha ibyo mukeneye niba mutihakana izina ryanjye, niba, kandi mukankorera mu murimo nabahaye.

Muzakora imirimo ikomeye niba gusa munsabye kubatuma. Narabahamagaye kandi ndabakunda cyane, kandi abambaza izina ryanjye bakihangana kugeza ku iherezo bazakizwa kandi bazurwe mu bwiza bukomeye kandi bagororerwe iteka ryose kubera guhagarara kwanyu, kuko ndabakunda kandi nabivugiye amatwi yanyu. Hariho isarura ryinshi kandi buri ryo mu gihe cyaryo, ntimutinye rero; kuko ibihe bikomeye bihora bihabwa abagabo bakomeye b'Imana ngo baragire umukumbi, kandi narabahamagaye. Abamarayika banjye bazashyiraho ikimenyetso ku 144,000, kandi bamwe muri mwe bazabafasha mu mirimo yabo n'inshingano nabahaye. Ntimutinye, kuko nzakora ibimenyetso byinshi, ibitangaza, n'imirimo ikomeye mu maboko yanyu, nuko rero muruhukire mu rukundo rwanjye kandi muruhukire mu mbaraga zanjye; kuko ndi kumwe namwe kandi sinzigera mbasiga cyangwa ngo mbahane.

Muzamenya ko najyanye umugeni wanjye, kandi nzaba nkoranyirije abizerwa kuri njye kugira ngo nteze kwihana kuri benshi mu isi igiye kurimbuka. Nuko rero mureke isi ihite kandi ntimusangire mu makuba yayo n'iherezo ryayo kuko atari ibyanyu niba muzakomeza kweza Uwiteka mu mitima yanyu kandi mukabaho mwitandukanyije kubera njye. Kuko ibibi bikomeye byegereje, kandi ntibyigeze bizamuka muri ubu buryo, kandi ntibizigera byongera kuzamuka, bana banjye bato, kuko narabivuze. Muhumurizwe n'inzira nabateguriye, kandi mukomeze ibirenge byanyu mu nzira. Nzaba kumwe namwe kandi nzabaha amahoro asumba ukumenya kose, kandi muzagira amahoro mu isi y'akaduruvayo, amahoro azabarinda niba mudateshutse ngo mujye iburyo cyangwa ibumoso.

Ntimufate ikimenyetso, kandi ntimwizere ibinyoma. Bazatanga amahoro n'ubuzima n'umutekano ku bafata ikimenyetso, kandi bazababwira ko mwinjiye mu isi nshya y'amahoro n'uburumbuke n'umutekano. Ntimwizere ibinyoma by'inzoka, kuko umwami wabo atazakomeza ijambo rye. Uwo muntu w'icyaha afite inshingano imwe gusa, ari yo: kurimbura.

Nta kuri kumurimo, kandi Satani ubwe azagenzura imikoba ye. Nta mahoro azava kuri we, ahubwo buri jambo rizaba ikinyoma gihumuriza, cyagenewe kuyobora isi mu kurimbuka gukomeye. Kandi iyo ntagabanya iminsi, nta mubiri wari gukizwa ikiboko azazana mu gihugu, kuko azahabwa imbaraga zo gusohoza imigambi yanjye mu gihe gito. Hanyuma azabohwa mu minyururu iremereye y'umwijima mu kuzimu kugira ngo abohurwe igihe gito ngo ayobye isi bwa nyuma. Namara kubakoranyiriza kuri we, bazagaba igitero kuri uwo mudugudu mukomeye, kandi umuriro uva ku Mana uzabarimbura bose. Hanyuma urubanza rwa nyuma, yego, urubanza rw'intebe y'ubwami yera rukomeye ruzabageraho bose, kandi bazaba badafite umuti. Ndababwira ibi bintu kugira ngo mbaha ibyiringiro kandi ntegure abantu banjye, kuko Ibanga Rikomeye ry'Imana rizagezwa ku iherezo.

Ndi inzira, ukuri, n'ubugingo.

Ndi Umwami w'abami n'Umutware w'abatware.

Ndi ukwiriye gufungura ibimenyetso, kandi ibihe byageze.

Ndi ugerageza imikoba y'intore zanjye kugira ngo mbeze kandi mbasukure mu maraso yanjye y'impongano.

Ndi Umwana w'Imana waje mu mubiri, abambwa, kandi azuka ku munsi wa gatatu, abonwa na benshi, kandi yakirwa mu ijuru imbere y'amaso yabo.

Ndi umwana wa Dawidi, kandi nzakoranyiriza abasigaye ba Yakobo munsi y'amaboko yanjye y'ihumure n'imbaraga.

Ndi uzasohoza ubushake bwa Data bwose.

Ngiye kugira abanzi banjye intebe y'ibirenge byanjye, kandi ishyano ni iry'abampuranya, kuko umujinya w'Umwana w'Intama ari mukuru.

Ndi uzategeka ibyaremwe bya Data kandi ngashyira byose mu kuboko kwe, kugira ngo Imana ibe byose muri byose.

Ndi uwo ubwami bwe buhoraho iteka ryose, kandi nzaha intore zanjye kurya ku giti cy'ubugingo no kunywa ku migezi y'amazi y'ubugingo, kandi ntimuzongera gusonza cyangwa kugira inyota ukundi, kandi nzahanagura amarira yose ku maso yanyu.

Njye, Uwiteka, narabivuze, kandi nzabikora. Igihe cyageze, nuko rero mutegereze kugaragara kwanjye, kuko nta gishobora guhagarika ukuboko kwanjye gukomeye.
