Ibisharira N'ibiryohereye
29 Ukwakira 2024

Igihe cyo Gusohora Kirageze: Yego na Amen

Kuva kuri Yeshua HaMashiach:

Uwo Mose yategetse Isirayeli kumva no kumwumvira.

Tega amatwi yawe wumve amagambo ya Yeshua HaMashiach muri ibi bihe bya nyuma, kandi wunamishe imitima yanyu mu kumvira kandi mwemerere imitima yanyu kwakira amabwiriza yanjye. Kuko amagambo yanjye ari ubuzima ku bayabona kandi azana amagara mazima n'isezerano, ndetse no mu bihe by'amakuba akomeye. Bityo musobanukirwe ko amakuba akomeye yageze kandi ibyago byegereje, kandi ibihe ni ubu. Ni ukuri, ni ukuri mu kuri kw'iteka ni njyewe, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndetse n'Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda uje gucira imanza no kurwana, kandi musobanukirwe ko nta gihishe amaso yanjye, cyangwa ngo hagire igihishwa amaso ya Yahweh kandi mbona akarengane na jenoside bikorerwa abaturage b'igihugu n'abateye baturutse mu Isinagogi ya Satani kandi nzishyura niko Uwiteka Ukiranuka avuga!

Kuko mu by'ukuri, mu myaka myinshi ya yubile abaguye bateje imbere ibanga ryabo ryo gukiranirwa, ndetse no mu bihe by'ubu aho imigambi yabo yiteguye gusohora mu buryo bwuzuye. Ariko musobanukirwe ko bemerewe gusa gusohoza imigambi ya Yahweh mu migambi ye y'iteka n'iteka y'agakiza kugira ngo abantu bacungurwe byuzuye. Kuko umuntu wa kamere yasinzirijwe mu buriganya bukomeye no kwirara gukomeye.

Bityo rero abaguye bakoresha abantu kugira ngo bateze imbere irimbuka ry'abantu mu gihe bita umucyo umwijima n'umwijima umucyo. Kandi niko bimeze no ku Isinagogi ya Satani bateye ibihugu by'isezerano kandi nzabarandurana n'imizi yabo yose. Kuko Isinagogi ya Satani ishaka kwiyitirira amasezerano ya Yahweh yanditswe kandi agahamirizwa ba Data bo hambere. Isinagogi ya Satani yuzuyemo urumamfu rubi abaguye babibye mu ngano zanjye kandi igihe cyo kubarandura kirageze kandi niko byanditswe kandi niko bizakorwa: Kuko dore, umunsi uje uzatwika nk'itanura, kandi abibone bose, yego, n'abakora ibibi bose, bazaba ibishingwe, kandi umunsi uje, uzabatwika, niko Uwiteka nyiringabo avuga, kandi ntuzabasigira umuzi cyangwa ishami.

Nyamara, ibyo byagezweho n'abaguye ku buryo igitagangurirwa cyaboshye urushundura rwacyo ku isi yose ubu, kandi binyuze mu buriganya bararwana. Kandi ubu ni cyo gihe cyo kuzamuka kwabo kandi ubutegetsi bwabo bugufi butari ubwera buri hafi. Kuko Yahweh mu butabera bwe azaha umuntu wa kamere ibyo yifuza mu bujiji bwe mu gushakisha ibinezeza by'icyaha. Bityo umwijima n'ibibi umuntu wa kamere yifuza umuntu wa kamere ubu azabibona. Nyamara, niba Ubutumwa Bwiza bwanjye buhishe, buhishe abazarimbuka. Abo imana y'iyi si yahumye imitima yabo batizera, kugira ngo umucyo w'Ubutumwa Bwiza bwa Kristo, ari we shusho y'Imana, utabamurikira. Bityo umuntu wa kamere mu bujiji bwe yifatanyije n'igitagangurirwa mu buriganya bwacyo, kuko binyuze mu buriganya bararwana.

Ku bw'ibyo, El Shaddai, aboherereza kuyoba gukomeye kugira ngo bizere ibinyoma byakozwe n'abaguye, kugira ngo bacirwe ho iteka bose batizeye ukuri, ahubwo bakishimira gukiranirwa. Nyamara mu rubanza rwa Yahweh n'imbabazi ze zihoraho, ibinezeza ubu bizakurwa mu isi kandi ibyiza umuntu wa kamere yifuza cyane kwirundaho bizakurwa mu isi. Bityo ndetse n'ibinezeza by'icyaha bizakurwa mu isi, ku buryo abantu bazasara bashaka guhaza irari ry'imitima yabo. Icyakora, Yahweh ntiyitamo ibyo bikorwa kugira ngo agirire nabi ibyaremwe bye ahubwo ni ukugira ngo ahane inkozi z'ibibi kandi akangure abari kwanga gukanguka mu bitotsi byabo kuko binyuze mu binezeza by'icyaha n'ibihembo byo gukiranirwa babyibushye kandi bahuma. Aho guhabwa urubanza rwuzuye kandi rutunguranye umuntu wa kamere ahabwa ubuntu kuko umuntu wa kamere yaneshejwe n'abaguye.

Yego, mu buntu n'imbabazi bya Yahweh no mu mugambi we w'iteka Yahweh ashaka gukangura umuntu wa kamere mu byaha no guhamagarira umuntu wa kamere gukiranuka. Muri uko guhamagara benshi bitabiriye ku bushake agakiza kanjye mu bihe byanjye by'ubuntu binyuze mu Butumwa Bwiza bw'Agakiza. Muri ibyo bihe, agakiza kari impano y'ubuntu aho umuntu yashoboraga kungira Umwami kandi akagira igihe cyo gukorera agakiza ke afite ubwoba no guhinda umushyitsi uko baza kuri njye byuzuye ndetse bava mu byaha byuzuye. Kandi niko bimeze ko ibihe byanjye by'ubuntu bigeze ku iherezo ryabyo, nubwo ubuntu n'imbabazi bihorana nanjye. Kandi vuba aha bizasohora ko ibihe byo gutegereza bitazongera kubaho kandi ko ubwami bw'iyi si buzaba ubwami bw'Umwami wanyu, n'ubwa Kristo we, kandi nzategeka iteka ryose hamwe n'abizerwa mu murage wacu w'iteka nzasangira byuzuye n'abaneshi banjye.

Kandi imbaga nyamwinshi izaza kuri njye mu gihe cy'amakuba ya Yakobo kugira ngo mbakize iteka ryose. Kandi abazambaza izina ry'Umwami bose kugeza ku mperuka bazakizwa, uko ururimi cyangwa ishyanga baturukamo byaba biri kose. Kandi ndakora kugira ngo nkize Isirayeli, kandi ndahamagara bene wacu nyakuri bayobejwe n'amadini y'ibinyoma kandi bakandamijwe n'Isinagogi ya Satani, kandi bazabona agakiza k'iteka kandi nzongera nkize Yakobo uwo agakiza ke kazagerwaho binyuze mu muriro w'utunganya. Ariko musobanukirwe ko ibihe byo kuramba no kugubwa neza mu by'umubiri ku isi byageze ku iherezo kugeza ubwo Ubwami bwanjye buzashyirwaho vuba, kandi isi iboshywe mu minyururu itegereje imanza zayo za nyuma zizasuka mu burakari bukomeye. Dore! Uburakari bw'Umwana w'Intama buza mbere, kandi amakuba akomeye azisuka ku isi nk'umwuzure w'irimbuka ryuzuye, kandi benshi bazarimbuka.

Nyamara, ibibazo bikomeye n'amakuba bizarekurwa ubwo ibimenyetso bizaba bimenwe n'uburakari bukomeye, n'ibyago byinshi n'indwara z'ibyorezo, n'inzara, n'amapfa, n'intambara, n'akarengane, n'urupfu n'ibindi byinshi bizaza byuzuye ku isi; nyamara imbaga y'imbaga nyamwinshi izakanguka iva mu nzira zayo z'akazuyaze. Kandi bazaba mu gusharira gukomeye n'umubabaro w'ubugingo ubwo bazabona ukuri kudasubirwaho kw'ibyanditswe n'ukuntu babuze amahirwe yabo y'agakiza mu ntege nke zabo no gucika uburakari n'urubanza mu ikoraniro ry'Itorero ryanjye ryizerwa kuri njye. Nyamara mu makuba yabo asharira n'umubabaro nzabashyiramo umwiyemezo udashobora kuneshwa kandi nzabikururira kuri njye byuzuye, ndetse n'abaza kuri njye bose mu mibabaro n'amakuba no binyuze mu bigeragezo byo guhorwa Imana. Kandi bazagira ingororano ikomeye, ingororano ikomeye ku buryo amakuba yabo ari imbere adakwiriye kugereranywa n'ubwiza bw'iteka buzahishurwa byuzuye muri bo.

Uko niko byanditswe kandi niko bizasohora kugeza ku mperuka: Aba ni bo bavuye mu makuba akomeye, kandi bameshe amakanzu yabo, bayejesha amaraso y'Umwana w'Intama. Ni cyo gituma bari imbere y'intebe y'Imana, bakayikorera ku manywa na nijoro mu Rusengero rwayo: kandi uwicaye ku Ntebe azabana na bo. Ntibazongera gusonza, cyangwa kugira inyota ukundi, kandi izuba ntirizabavaho, cyangwa ubushyuhe ubwo ari bwo bwose. Kuko Umwana w'Intama, uri hagati y'intebe y'ubwami, azabagaburira, kandi azabayobora ku masoko y'amazi y'ubugingo: kandi Imana izahanagura amarira yose ku maso yabo.

Bityo igitagangurirwa cyaboshye urushundura rwacyo ku isi yose mu buriganya bukomeye, iterabwoba, kwaka ruswa no mu buryo bwinshi ubumara bw'igitagangurirwa bukora ibibi byabwo binyuze mu nzira zacyo z'uburiganya n'ubumara. Kuko cyaboshye urushundura rw'uburiganya kandi cyaboshye urushundura rw'imyenda ku buryo isi ubu iboshywe byuzuye mu maboko y'igitagangurirwa kandi yamugajwe n'ubumara bwacyo. Igitagangurirwa cyagendanye ubwihisho bukomeye n'uburiganya binyuze mu ibanga ryo gukiranirwa, kandi isi ubu iri byuzuye mu rushundura rwacyo. Kandi mu buriganya bukomeye aba biyitirira ibyo batari byo (bakozwe n'abaguye) bateye ibihugu nk'inzige. Ndetse nk'uko igitagangurirwa kibika amagi yacyo kugeza ubwo imbaga y'abana bacyo ibohoka ikiruka igenda kuboha inshundura zayo iruhande rwacyo. Kandi niko isinagogi ya Satani yafashe isi yaba ku ngufu no mu mayeri.

Kandi uko igihe cyagiye gihita binyuze mu mayeri menshi n'imigambi mibisha bigize abakomeye, kuko babihawe na Yahweh mu migambi ye y'iteka y'agakiza kugira ngo babisohoze. Kuko umuntu wa kamere ahumwe n'ibinyoma byabo kandi akabakurikira mu bujiji kugeza ku irimbuka rye. Iyo migambi mvuga izagera ku ndunduro yayo mu gutandukanya kwa nyuma hagati y'ingano n'urumamfu no hagati y'abiyemeje byuzuye n'abakunda ibyaha byabo kandi bagahitamo ku bushake ibirimi by'umuriro mu rukundo rwabo rubi n'irimbuka ryabo bwite, kandi bazicuza iteka amahitamo yabo bahisemo kwihuza kugira ngo bahuze n'imigambi y'igitagangurirwa. Bityo inzáo z'umujinya w'uburakari bw'Imana zizasuka byuzuye.

Musobanukirwe ko igitagangurirwa ari Isinagogi ya Satani ubu yiteguye kwigarurira isi muri gahunda yabo ya nyuma y'inyamaswa uwo mutegetsi nzashyiraho iherezo ry'iteka kandi irimbuka ryabo n'urubanza rwabo ntibitinda. Musobanukirwe, bakundwa banjye, ko Isinagogi ya Satani bose ari abiyitirira ibyo batari byo. Ni urumamfu rw'Abedomu abo ba sekuruza babo nahanganye nabo nk'umwana w'intama w'Imana ubwo nitangaga ku bushake kugira ngo nishyure ibyaha by'abantu. Bityo narabashyize ahagaragara, kandi niko nababwiye imbona nkubone mu maso yabo mabi: Mukomoka kuri so umusebanya, kandi irari rya so naryo muzarikora: yari umwicanyi uhereye mbere na mbere, kandi ntiyagumye mu kuri, kuko nta kuri kumurimo. Iyo avuga ikinyoma, avuga ibye: kuko ari umunyabinyoma, na se wacyo.

Bityo kuva bagaba igitero cyabo cya mbere mu gihugu cy'isezerano kitari icyabo kugira ngo bacyiyitirire, batoteje, bica urubozo, kandi bakora jenoside yeruye ku baturage bemewe b'ibihugu by'isezerano. Biyitirira kuba abaragwa bemewe b'agakiza, ndetse bakoresha nabi ibice by'amategeko ya Mose mu idini ryabo ry'ikinyoma rya Talimudi. Kandi binyuze muri ubwo buryo bahumye benshi batekereza ko bumvira ubushake bwanjye batekereza ko nzaha umugisha uwo ari we wese uha umugisha uru rumamfu kandi nzavuma uwo ari we wese uvuma uru rumamfu. Bityo benshi mu bantu banjye mu bujiji bifatanya n'Isinagogi ya Satani ku bwumvikane kandi ndabategeka rwose kwihana byuzuye ibinyoma byabo mwahisemo ku bw'ukuyoba kwanyu.

Bana banjye bato musobanukirwe ko njyewe, Yesu Kristo, ntabwo nigeze nandika Bibiliya z'icyigisho ariko izi zikoreshwa nk'ibikoresho by'uburiganya n'ibinyoma bitangwa mu mpera z'urupapuro kugira ngo basobanure ukuri. Kandi abaguye bahinduye amagambo yanjye muri Bibiliya z'ibinyoma, ku bw'ibyo, murebe umurongo w'inyandiko za Byzantine, ndetse na Textus Receptus aho narinze Amagambo yanjye. Ku bw'ibyo, muze kuri njye bakundwa banjye kandi munyigireho kandi nzabatwara byuzuye mu kuri kw'ibinyoma byabo by'ubu niba muzacisha bugufi imitima yanyu ku ijwi ryanjye ritangwa n'abamvugira.

Musobanukirwe ko ari njyewe, Yesu w'i Nazareti, wahanganye n'aba biyitirira ibyo batari byo kubera imigenzo yabo yo mu kanwa bashyize hejuru y'Amagambo atunganye ya Yahweh yahawe Mose. Kuko Amategeko ya Mose ari ayera, kandi akiranuka, kandi atunganye iteka ryose kandi hagize umuntu uvuma Amategeko ya Mose uwo muntu na we azavumwa. Kuko njyewe, Yesu w'i Nazareti, nasohoje Amategeko ya Mose ubwo natangaga amaraso yanjye atagira icyaha i Kaluvari ku bw'ibyaha bya Adamu (n'abamukomokaho bose) kandi Amategeko ya Mose yubahirizwa byuzuye n'abakundwa banjye bagendera mu kwizera muri njye, Yesu Kristo, Umwami wabo wazamutse kandi uhoraho.

Bityo rero, menya ko bidashoboka kwica Amategeko ya Mose iyo umuntu agendera mu kwizera nk'uko mbarongora binyuze mu mwuka wanjye naremye muri bo iteka ryose. Nyamara, abagendera mu mubiri bose baracumura kuko Mose yaje gucira urubanza icyaha mu mubiri atanga iryo tegeko ry'iteka ry'ibyishimo ryanditswe n'urutoki rwa Yahweh ubwe. Kandi nta mubiri n'umwe ukiranuka imbere yanjye cyangwa imbere y'Imana Ihoraho yo Gukiranuka. Nuko rero, mwature ibyaha byanyu n'intege nke zanyu imbere yanjye, jye Mwana w'Intama w'Imana uri umutambyi mukuru wanyu w'iteka ryose mu buryo bwa Melikisedeki, nanjye nzabeza rwose mu maraso yanjye y'impongano yamenewe kweza abaza aho ndi bose ngo bakizwe.

Ni nde wumvise ibintu nk'ibi? Ni nde wabonye ibintu nk'ibi? Mbese isi yabyarwa mu munsi umwe? Cyangwa ishyanga ryavuka icyarimwe? Nyamara niko bimeze kuko ishyanga rya Isirayeli ryarongeye kubaho nk'ishyanga binyuze mu itangazo ry'urumamfu, kandi ni Imana ubwayo yemereye abanzi bayo kwiyoberanya mu bujiji mu kinyoma cyabo ko ari Yakobo atari Esawu, kuko Esawu ari we wagurishije umugabane we w'uburiza ku muto. Kandi bityo igitagangurirwa mu bujiji no mu bwibone gitekereza ko kizazana igihe cy'iteka cya zahabu ku isi kuko iryo ari ryo rari rya ba Se bazakora kandi abaguye ni bo ba Se ndetse na ba Satani basangiye kamere.

Kandi nyamara bateye ibihugu ku mbaraga nyinshi kuko babihawe ngo babikore kugira ngo bakangure abasinziriye, ndetse n'abari mu buhumyi bw'igice bazitaba umuhamagaro wa Mesiya wabo, ari we Yeshua Mesiya w'Ukuri. Kandi mumenye ko mbona buri gikorwa kibi bakora, kandi mbona buri karengane bagirira abarengana, kandi nzishyura niko Umwami w'Iteka Yahweh yagize Mesiya, Umwami, Umutambyi mukuru, n'Umwami avuga! Buri gikorwa kibi cyanditswe neza mu ijuru, kandi bazishyurwa iteka ryose ndetse no kugeza ku ndunduro mu murage wabo w'iteka mu birimi by'umuriro bishyushye cyane by'ubururu bwijimye mu mwijima w'icuraburindi iteka ryose.

Kandi mbona Rasheli aririra abana be mu gihe bababazwa na jenoside n'akarengane k'Isinagogi ya Satani kandi nzishyura, niko Umwami w'abami n'Umutware w'abatware avuga kandi nzakiza asigaye ya Isirayeli ari mazima mu cyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli, ari cyo gihe cy'amakuba ya Yakobo. Mu mugambi w'iteka wa Yahweh, yumva ko benshi batazigera bava mu byaha ngo bajye mu gukiranuka baretse ibyishimo n'ibinyoma by'icyaha. Bityo aho kuzana urubanza rwihuse, Yahweh yahisemo kuzana urubanza ku rundi kugira ngo ibintu byiyongere kandi bigende biba bibi kurushaho. Kandi urubanza ruziyongera muri ubwo buryo abazahitamo kunyambaza mu makuba yabo n'ibyago byabo ni bo nzakiza kugeza ku ndunduro, kandi bazayoborwa atekanye.

Kandi bityo imigambi yabo izahishurwa ubu. Kuko nzayobora abanjye 144,000 kumanuka ku Musozi Siyoni, kandi bazankurikira intambwe ku yindi uko bayobora abasigaye banjye ba Yakobo atekanye mu butayu aho Imana yabateguriye umwanya w'umutekano wabo, kandi bazagaburirwa banitabweho kandi ikiyoka, inyamaswa, n'umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma ntibazabakora, cyangwa ngo bababone. Ariko umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma azazana ikizira cy'umurimbuzi kandi urumamfu rwinshi ruzakurwa mu gihugu, kandi nzahisha abasigaye banjye kure y'intambara y'uwo munsi ukomeye, ari wo munsi wo kugaruka kwanjye uwo munsi Yahweh yashyizeho mu bihe byashize by'iteka kandi washyizweho iteka ryose mu ngengabihe ye y'agakiza kandi uwo munsi uriko uregera kuko byanditswe.

Nuko mbona ijuru rikinguye, mbona ifarashi y'umweru, n'uyicayeho yitwa Uwo kwizerwa n'Ukwuri, kandi aca imanza zitabera akarwana intambara mu gukiranuka. Amaso ye yari ameze nk'ibirimi by'umuriro, kandi ku mutwe we hari amakamba menshi, kandi yari afite izina ryanditswe, ritazwi n'umuntu wese keretse we ubwe. Kandi yari yambaye umwenda winitse mu maraso, kandi izina rye yitwa, Jambo ry'Imana. Kandi ingabo zo mu ijuru zamukurikiye ku mafarashi y'umweru, zambaye imyenda y'igitare myiza, yera kandi itunganye. Kandi mu kanwa ke havamo inkota ityaye, kugira ngo ayikubite amahanga: kandi azayategekesha inkoni y'icyuma: kandi asibanganya urwengero rw'ubukana n'umujinya by'Imana Ishoborabyose. Kandi ku mwenda we, no ku kibero cye yari afite izina ryanditswe ngo, UMWAMI W'ABAMI, N'UMUTWARE W'ABATWARE.

Bityo bizasohora ko nzaza gufata umurage wanjye ku mbaraga mu maboko y'abanyazi n'abigarurira iby'abandi igihe cyabo kigeze ku ndunduro y'iteka, kuko ubwami bwa Satani butazongera kubyuka ukundi muri ubwo buryo. Kandi nzeza isi nyikuremo gukiranuka guke kose, kandi Satani azabohwa imyaka igihumbi mu bubabare n'ubwoba bwinshi bw'umurage we w'iteka wegereje muri uwo muriro w'amazuku unuka waka ibirimi bishyushye cyane by'igihano.

Ndetse n'ibyo birimi byaremewe kwishyura buri gikorwa kibi bakoze kuva igihe baremewe bagahitamo gukiranuka guke. Kandi buri cyaha cyose cy'abaguye kizishyurwa byuzuye kuri buri wese muri bo, ndetse na buri cyaha bakoze mu bugome bwinshi bakoze kuri Yahweh ubwe n'ibyaremwe bye byo bononnye. Kandi bityo uko baboshwe bakajugunywa ari bazima mu birimi bishyushye cyane kandi byera cyane by'igihano aho umuriro uzaka ubururu bushyushye cyane n'amabara y'umweru ashyushye cyane y'ubutungane. Kandi muri ibyo birimi by'igihano bazishyurwa rwose ibikorwa byabo byose bitagira umumaro kandi biteye ishozi bizahabwa byuzuye mu bituza byabo inshuro nyinshi iteka ryose.

Kuko mu rubanza rwabo nta mahoro bagira!

Kuko mu rubanza rwabo batazigera baruhuka!

Kuko bambuye abarengana ikiruhuko!

Kuko amahoro batayamenye!

Kuko batagaragaje imbabazi!

Kandi bazishyurwa byuzuye mu rubanza rutunganye kandi rw'iteka rwa Yahweh rutazatinda.

Kandi bityo ingoma yanjye y'imyaka igihumbi izaza ku isi byuzuye ku iherezo ry'amakuba ya Yakobo, kandi izaba ingoma yo gukiranuka. Muri iki gihe abakiranutsi bose bazazurwa, kandi buri wese azaba yazutse mu gihe cye no mu byiciro by'isarura ryabo. Abizerwa muri Kristo Yesu bazaba bakiriho n'abasinziriye muri Kristo bazashimutirwa mu ijuru mu kanya nk'ako guhumbya, kandi bazagororerwa cyane kandi bamwe bazahomba, ariko bose bazahabwa ubugingo byuzuye kandi iteka ryose.

Hanyuma ku iherezo ry'amakuba ya Yakobo hazabaho umuzuko w'abakiranutsi aho nzazura abera b'umubabaro mwinshi bishwe bazira izina ryanjye kandi nzazura mbaha ubugingo bw'iteka abapfuye bafite ukwizera munsi y'amasezerano menshi ya Yahweh ndetse n'abashizemo umwuka wabo wa nyuma ku isi kuva kuri Adamu kugeza ku ivuka ry'Itorero ryanjye ku munsi wa Pentekote.

Nyamara bityo bizasohora bakunzi banjye rero mumenye ibyo nzongera gukora: Nzafata abana ba Isirayeli mbakure mu bapagani, aho bagiye hose, kandi nzabakoranyiriza impande zose, mbazane mu gihugu cyabo. Kandi nzabagira ubwoko bumwe mu gihugu, ku misozi ya Isirayeli, kandi umwami umwe azaba umwami wabo bose: kandi ntibazongera kuba amoko abiri ukundi, cyangwa ngo bongere kugabanywamo ubwami bubiri ukundi. Kandi ntibazongera kwanduzwa n'ibigirwamana byabo ukundi, cyangwa n'ibizira byabo, cyangwa n'ibicumuro byabo byose: ariko nzabakiza mbakure mu buturo bwabo bwose, aho bacumuriye, kandi nzabeza: bityo bazaba ubwoko bwanjye, nanjye nzaba Imana yabo.

Kandi tuzishimira umubyimba w'igihugu kandi isega izabyagira hamwe n'umwana w'intama uko amahoro aza ku isi yose uko isi isubizwa mu gukiranuka ku bwiza bwayo bwateganyijwe.
