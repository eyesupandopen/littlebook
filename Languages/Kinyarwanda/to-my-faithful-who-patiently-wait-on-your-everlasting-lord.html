Ku Bizerwa Bange Bategereza Biyumanganya Umwami Wanyu Uhoraho!

Umva amagambo y'Umwami wanyu uhoraho, ari we Yeshua HaMashiach kandi muze aho ndi n'imitima yanyu yiteguye kwakira no kwitega ingororano ziva mu kuboko kwanjye, kuko koko igihe ni iki. Ndetse na cya gihe cyategerejwe cyane ubwo nzamanuka mvuye mu ijuru n'ijwi rirenga, n'ijwi rya marayika mukuru n'impanda y'Imana (kuko iyo mpanda izavuga) n'abapfiriye muri Kristo bazazurwa badashobora kubora kandi namwe abazaba bakiriho basigaye muzazamurwa mu bicu aho muzambikwa kudapfa kandi uko niko muzabana, kandi mumeze nk', Umwami wanyu uhoraho iteka ryose.

Ndi Umwami w'abami n'Umutware w'abatware, kandi ni njye waguze isi mu maraso yanjye y'impongano. Koko, koko, nishyuye ikiguzi cy'ibyaha by'abantu kugira ngo abantu biyunge na Yahweh, kuko njyewe, Yesu w'i Nazareti ndi amasezerano yanyu y'amahoro atuma abantu biyunga n'Imana, niyo mpamvu natumye Pawulo yandikira Abakorinto ati: Noneho rero turi intumwa za Kristo: nk'aho Imana ibingingira muri twe, turabinginga mu cyimbo cya Kristo, ngo mwiyunge n'Imana. Kuko yamuhinduye icyaha ku bwacu, we utigeze amenya icyaha, kugira ngo duhinduke gukiranuka kw'Imana muri we.

Nuko rero, bana banjye, nabahinduye gukiranuka kw'Imana binyuze muri Kristo uri muri mwe ibyiringiro by'ubwiza, ndetse na wa mwuka w'ubwana buhoraho utuma mutaka muti ABBA, Data bityo mukavuka ubwa kabiri nk'abana b'Imana bahoraho kandi mukaba mushyiriweho ikimenyetso n'uwo mwuka wera w'isezerano. Mwumve ko ikimenyetso cya Yahweh kidashobora kumenwa, cyangwa ngo gihindurwe, kandi abashyizweho ikimenyetso bose bafite umutekano w'iteka, bityo mwumve ko ibyo Yahweh yanditse kandi agatangaza ntawe ushobora kubihindura. None se ni nde ushobora kubakura mu maboko yanjye? Ni nde ushobora kunesha Imana ikomeye ihoraho, ari yo Yahweh, ari we Data w'Imicyo? None se kuki muhangayika kandi mukiganyira ko nzabata? Nyamara se, ni nde muri mwe wata abana be bwite, nk'aho ikintu nk'icyo ari ikintu cyoroheje imbere y'Umwami n'Umukiza wanyu, ari we Yesu w'i Nazareti.

Koko, koko, impano no guhamagara kw'Imana ntibisubirwaho kandi abahawe impano y'ubugingo buhoraho binyuze mu kwatura Yesu nk'Umwami mu gihe cyose biringira ko Imana yamuzuye mu bapfuye bahabwa ubugingo buhoraho kandi bahindurwa gukiranuka kw'Imana muri Kristo Yesu Umwami wabo. None niba umuntu wese yatura uko kwatura abikuye mu mutima utagira ukwizera, mufatira ikimenyetso cy'Imana nzima kugira ngo mbikururireho byuzuye kugira ngo bagere ku kwihana ibyaha byuzuye, hato wenda imbuto yanjye itaba yaraguye mu butaka bw'amabuye. None niba umuntu wese adafite umwuka wa Kristo, si uwe. Ni njye ugerageza kandi ugerageza imitima y'abantu kugira ngo bejejwe mu muriro wanjye weza kuko ndi we wicara afite umuvuba mu ntoki akongeza ibirimi by'umuriro weza kuko ndi umucuzi uvugutira amakara kugira ngo mugere ku murage wanyu wuzuye muri njye, Kristo Yesu, bityo mugume mwicishije bugufi ku mihati yanjye kandi ntimurwanye gukurura k'uwo mwuka w'ubwana wavukiye muri mwe uzana gukiranuka no kwera nyakuri. Nuko rero ndabinginga bana banjye mugendere mu mwuka kugira ngo mudasohoza irari ry'umubiri.

Nyamara, benshi muri mwe mureka imitima yanyu ikabahagarika umutima bidasobanutse ko ikimenyetso cy'Imana gishobora kumenwa n'ubupfu bw'umuntu. Ndababaza, bakunzi banjye? Ese El Shaddai mu kumenya kwe mbere yirengagije ukuri ko yari antumye, Yesu w'i Nazareti, gucungura abanyabyaha bari kuzongera gukora icyaha nyuma y'agakiza? Ese Yahweh, Imana ikomeye ihoraho idakeneye umujyanama nubwo inzovu inshuro inzovu zikikije intebe yayo y'ubwami, kandi ikora ibintu byose ikurikije inama y'ubushake bwayo ntiyumvise ukudatungana kwa Adamu wangiritse, ndetse n'abantu bose bavutswe mu gitaka gitukura basangiye amaraso ya Adamu?

Oya bana banjye, ntibishoboka ko Yahweh atakumva ibyo bintu kandi yarabateganyirije mu buryo bwose kandi agatanga uburyo bwose kugira ngo gucungurwa kwanyu kwuzuye kubazeho byuzuye bakunzi banjye. Nuko rero mugendere mu mategeko yo kwizera no binyuze mu mwuka wanjye wavukiye muri mwe kandi mugendere mu mwuka w'ubugingo muri Kristo Yesu. Kuko muri Kristo Yesu gukebwa nta cyo kumaze, cyangwa kudakebwa, ahubwo icyaremwe gishya. Kandi abagendera kuri iryo tegeko bose, amahoro abe kuri bo, n'imbabazi, no kuri Isirayeli y'Imana. Ntimwumva se ko umukiranutsi azabeshwaho no kwizera? Nuko mwumve ko nta n'umwe mu bizerwa banjye utunganye muri iki gihe, oya, kugeza igihe bazacungurirwa byuzuye. Kandi abacunguwe bose bashyizweho ikimenyetso n'uwo mwuka wera w'isezerano bose bazakora icyaha, haba babizi cyangwa batabizi, umunsi wose. Nuko rero, mwiyuhagire mu maraso yanjye y'impongano buri munsi. Muze aho ndi kandi mukongeze umubano wanyu nanjye kandi muwukomeze, bakunzi banjye. Mumbwire ibyaha byanyu buri munsi, kandi mugume mwicishije bugufi ku ijwi ryanjye rito rituje iyo nje aho muri kubemeza iby'igukiranuka. Namaze kubegera kandi ndi aho ngaho imbere yanyu mbatera inkunga kandi nkorera inzira zanyu kugira ngo mushobore kugendera muri iyo nzira igororotse kandi ifunganye mwahamagariwe kugenderamo. Kandi mukomeze kwitega uwo munsi wo gucungurwa kwanyu kwuzuye aho muzahagarara mutunganye imbere ya Yahweh ubwe mwambaye gukiranuka nzabaha byuzuye kuko ari njye, Kristo Yesu, uzabambika imyenda yera nziza itagira ikizinga, inenge, cyangwa umunkanyari cyangwa ikindi kintu nk'icyo.

Nuko rero, mwumve bana banjye bato, ko urubanza rwaje ku isi, ndetse na rwa rubanza rukomeye rwavuzwe kera n'abahanuzi ba kera ndetse n'igihe cyarwo cyo gusohozwa kikaba kigeze. Isezerano ryo kubyara ntirihagarara ku isi ubu kuko urubanza ruri hano kandi abacunguwe banjye bazagera ku gucungurwa kwabo kwuzuye. Kandi niko ibintu bizaza, inkubiri ku nkubiri. Buri nkubiri ikomeye kandi ifite ubukana mu kurimbura kwayo kuruta iyabanje kugeza ubwo mu buryo butunguranye mujyanywe ahantu heza kandi mu buryo butunguranye Babuloni y'Ibanga ikaba itakiriho. Buri nkubiri izana urubanza kandi buri nkubiri yikubita hasi cyane kandi ikavuga cyane kuruta iyabanje. Kandi niko imanza zanjye n'imiburo yanjye ku isi bimeze, kuko nza nk'intare yo mu muryango wa Yuda kandi mu gukiranuka nza guca urubanza no kurwana.

Kandi niko bimeze ko mbere y'uko amakuba ya nyuma atera bizaba ku itegeko ryanjye ko abizerwa banjye basinziriye muri Kristo bazazurwa mu mva zabo kandi abizerwa banjye bakiriho basigaye bazakoranywa hamwe nabo kandi mu ijuru ry'amajuru muzajyayo, aho mwese muzahagarara imbere y'intebe y'urubanza ikomeye ya Kristo kugira ngo mwese muhabwe kubw'ibyakozwe mu mubiri. Abajyanywe bose kwegera Bema yanjye ntibazayo gucirwa urubanza kubera ibyaha byabo kuko mwemeye kubabarirwa ibyaha mu gihe cy'umunsi w'umuntu w'umunyabyaha ndetse no muri uyu munsi ubwo umuntu w'umunyabyaha ategeka isi y'ubwo butegetsi buri ku iherezo ryabwo. Kuko isi ari iyanjye, Kristo Yesu, n'ibyuzuyemo byose, kandi ndegereje kandi nzatwara ku ngufu umurage wanjye ku bigaruriye kandi urubanza rwabo rwuzuye ntirutinda. Bakunzi banjye, mwumve ko mwavuye mu rupfu mukajya mu bugingo buhoraho muri njye, Kristo Yesu Umwami wanyu. Kandi ni njye wabogeje byuzuye ibyaha mu maraso yanjye y'impongano kuko ndi Pasika yanyu itunganye wituye Yahweh Imana ikomeye ihoraho, rimwe, bose, n'iteka ryose kubw'ibyaha by'abantu: nuko rero, ntihakiriho igitambo cy'ibyaha.

Nuko rero, abana b'Imana bazegera intebe yanjye y'urubanza kugira ngo nce urubanza rw'imirimo yabo kugira ngo bose bahabwe kubyo bagezeho, byaba byiza cyangwa bibi. Kandi niko mwese muzazana ibitambo by'ubuzima bwanyu bwacunguwe ku isi mwatuye mu gukorera Umwami wanyu uhoraho. Nk'uko byanditswe, niko bizakorwa, nk'uko byanditswe: Kuko nta wundi mushingi umuntu ashobora gushyiraho, uretse uwashyizweho, ari wo Yesu Kristo. Kandi niba umuntu yubaka kuri uwo mushingi, zahabu, ifeza, amabuye y'agaciro, imbaho, ibyatsi, cyangwa ibikenyeri, Umurimo w'umuntu wese uzagaragara: kuko umunsi uzawerekana, kuko uzahishurwa n'umuriro: kandi umuriro uzagerageza umurimo w'umuntu wese uko umeze. Niba umurimo w'umuntu wese, yubatseho, ugumaho, azahabwa ibihembo. Niba umurimo w'umuntu wese uhiye, azahomba, ariko we ubwe azakizwa: nyamara nk'aho ari mu muriro.

Niko bizaba bakunzi banjye ko nzabagororera byuzuye kubw'umurimo wanyu kuri njye kandi ibintu byatwitswe nk'umurama n'imiriro yanjye y'urubanza muzahomba kandi kubw'ibyo bintu bihagarara imbere y'imiriro yanjye y'urubanza, ndetse n'imirimo nabaheye gusohoza, ingororano ikomeye izaba iyanyu. Kuko byanditswe ngo: Ijisho ntiryabonye, n'ugutwi ntikwumvise, kandi ntibyigeze byinjira mu mutima w'umuntu, ibyo Imana yateguriye abayikunda. Kandi niko kamere y'umurage wanyu n'ingororano bimeze, ko nta muntu n'umwe ku isi wumva byuzuye ubwiza n'ikuzo by'umurage w'abana b'Imana bahoraho bavutswe ubwa kabiri bakurikije umwuka wa Kristo, bityo mukavuka ubwa kabiri nk'abana b'Imana iteka ryose.

Nuko mwumve bakunzi banjye ko umunsi wo gucungurwa kwanyu kwuzuye uri hafi kuruta uko mubitekereza, kandi urabegera cyane muri iyi segonda kandi uwo munsi ugiye gusohozwa vuba, vuba kuruta uko mubyiteze. Mwumve ko nta gihe gisigaye cyo kuburira, nta gihe gisigaye cyo kwinginga, nta gihe gisigaye cyo kujya impaka na Babuloni y'ibanga kuko urubanza rwayo ari ntakuka kandi rudashobora guhindurwa. Oya, ibihe byayo byo kwihana byarapfushijwe ubusa, kandi yirengagije byuzuye amajwi y'abahanuzi banjye n'intumwa zanjye ivuga mu mutima wayo mu gihe yashinyaguriraga amagambo yanjye iti “Nicaye nk'umwamikazi kandi sindi umupfakazi, ejo hazaba nk'uyu munsi kandi uyu munsi ni kimwe n'ejo hashize.”

Nyamara mu isaha imwe amakuba yayo azayigwaho byuzuye, kandi ntizongera kubyuka ukundi. Ariko mu cyimbo cy'ishyanga ryahoze rikomeye ryubahaga inzira za Yahweh kandi aho abantu bakurikiraga Yesu w'i Nazareti kandi ntibakorwe n'isoni zo gukunda abaturanyi babo hazasigara ubutayu aho Babuloni y'Ibanga izarimburwa mu isaha imwe kuko Umwami Imana iyicira urubanza ikomeye. Kandi uko ayo makuba yegereza cyane bakunzi banjye, niko no gucungurwa kwanyu kwuzuye, agakiza, ingororano, n'ibyishimo by'iteka byegereza, nuko rero ntimuhangayikishwe n'urubanza rwegereje rw'abanyabyaha rutari urwanyu niba muvuye mu byaha byanyu byuzuye.

Kandi niko uko guhumbya kw'ijisho kwegereza aho mu isegonda rimwe muzisanga muhagaze mu cyumba cy'intebe y'ubwami cya Yahweh ubwe aho intebe zanyu z'ubwami zitatse kandi aho mwicaye nk'abami n'abatambyi bahoraho kubw'umurage w'Umwami wanyu uhoraho, bityo muzategeka amahanga n'inkoni y'icyuma kuko niko nahawe, kandi niko nzaha abanesha. Abanesha banjye ni ba nde? Ndetse n'abavuye mu cyaha byuzuye binyuze mu kwihana kwuzuye no kwanga ibyaha byabo. Kandi ni njye Kristo Yesu wanesheje amayeri ya Satani byuzuye n'iteka ryose, musiga ari umwanzi wenyine kandi watsinzwe byuzuye uwo ibirimi by'umuriro w'iteka by'igihano bikongejwe kandi byiteguye kwakira, kuko niko umurage wabo umeze kandi niko bakwiriye.

Nuko rero, nateguye inzira ikomeye ku bampa byuzuye kugira ngo baneshe byuzuye amayeri ya Satani mu buryo bumwe nk'uko nabikoze. Nuko muze aho ndi byuzuye kugira ngo mbayobore muri iyo nzira igororotse kandi ifunganye igana ku gakiza kanyu kuzuye. Mwumve ijwi ryanjye mwebwe mwanyereye mukagwa mu nzira kuko umukiranutsi agwa karindwi, akongera kubyuka, nuko muhaguruke Yemwe bagabo b'Imana kandi muze aho ndi byuzuye kugira ngo nongere mbahuze n'ingororano yanyu mbogeza mu maraso yanjye y'impongano kugira ngo muhagarare mwambaye byuzuye gukiranuka kwanjye. Mwumve ko abanesha banjye bazaragwa ibintu byose kandi nta kintu cyiza bazimwa. Kandi niko abanesha banjye, bazahora baka, ndetse nk'umucyo w'isanzure: kandi mwebwe mwahinduriye benshi ku gukiranuka, muzaka nk'inyenyeri, iteka ryose. Nuko rero ubu mumenye ibi bana banjye ko ubu ari igihe cyo gusiga amaganya y'iyi si kandi mukaruhukira muri njye kandi mukaguma muri abera nk'isugi mu maraso yanjye y'impongano.

Nuko rero, bakunzi banjye muhagarare mushikamye imbere y'amakuba yegereje, kuko mubona uwo munsi uteye ubwoba wegereza, kandi ni njye wahumuye amaso y'abicisha bugufi mu mutima kugira ngo mubone ukuri kw'isi yanyu kandi ntimugwe mu buyobe bukomeye butari ubwanyu. Mwumve ko njyewe, Yeshua HaMashiach ndi we utuma impumyi zibona, nuko rero, muze aho ndi kugira ngo nsige amaso yanyu umuti w'amaso kandi kugira ngo mubone igice cy'iyi si mbi uko iri koko iyo mubamo. Nuko ntimutinye urubanza rw'abanyabyaha kuko urwo rubanza rutari urwanyu bakunzi banjye, oya urubanza si urw'abacunguwe kuko mwakijijwe umujinya w'Umwana w'Intama n'umujinya w'Imana, niyo mpamvu muhagarara mushikamye kandi mufite umutekano mu gakiza kanyu bakunzi banjye kuko umunsi uri hafi kandi urabegera cyane uko isegonda rishira.

Yego icyo gihe cyo gucungurwa kwanyu kwuzuye cyabagezeho bakunzi banjye kandi icyo gihe gihoraho kiri hafi cyane kandi kirabegera cyane uko isegonda rishira. Nemeje mu myuka yanyu, nerekanye ibimenyetso hejuru n'ibimenyetso mu isi, nateje ibitangaza mu ijuru ndetse nrekura n'ibiza mu rubanza ku banyabyaha no gukangura abasinziriye kugira ngo bumve ko ubu ari igihe cyo gucungurwa kwuzuye kw'abana b'Imana Ishoborabyose, ndetse n'umugeni wanjye n'umubiri wanjye.

Ntimwifatanye na bo mu nzira zabo zitagira kwizera, kuko bashaka kwiyitirira no gusobanura nabi ibimenyetso n'ibitangaza byinshi byerekanwa mu ijuru, ibyinshi muri byo bikaba byarashushanyijwe n'urutoki rwa Yahweh ubwe. Ese mwibagiwe Itangiriro bakunzi banjye? Kuko byanditswe ngo: Kandi Imana iravuga iti "Mu isanzure ry'ijuru habeho ibyo kuvusha umucyo, kugira ngo bitandukanye amanywa n'ijoro, bibe ibimenyetso n'ibihe, n'iminsi n'imyaka. Kandi bibe ibyo kuvusha umucyo mu isanzure ry'ijuru kugira ngo bimurikire isi": niko byabaye. Nuko rero, musobanukirwe ko Yahweh ategeka byose kandi nta kintu na kimwe cyemererwa mu byaremwe bye atemeye, kandi atazi. Nuko rero, ntimwifatanye na bo mu mitekerereze yabo itagira kwizera y'uko ubu ikiremwamuntu kigenzura kandi kigakoresha ijuru, nubwo ikiremwamuntu kigerageza kunesha Imana kandi imbaraga zacyo zikaba zihinduka ubusa buri gihe.

Nyamara, abaguye bagerageza guhisha ibimenyetso byanjye n'amatangazo menshi Yahweh yashyize mu ijuru y'ubukwe bw'umukwe n'umugeni we. Nuko rero muhumurizwe mu bwiza bw'Imana, mumenye ko icyo abaguye bemererwa gukora cyose gikorera gusa imigambi y'iteka y'Imana ihoraho kuko niko inzira zayo ziri ku buryo n'abanzi bayo bumvira ijwi ryayo. Ariko ntimwibeshye Yahweh ategeka byose kandi nta kiba atemeye mu buntu bwe, imbabazi ze, n'urubanza rwe rw'iteka ruzageraho ku batarihana n'abaguye. Ndetse n'urwo rubanza rukomeye rwavuzwe kera ubu rugiye kugwa ku isi mu buryo bukomeye.

Kandi uwo munsi ubariho bakunzi banjye kandi NDI KUZA KURI MWE! Nubwo abigarurira ibitari ibyabo bashaka guhisha ubwiza bwanjye, sinzasangira ubwiza bwanjye n'undi bityo imbaraga z'ibintu byoroshye kandi bikennye ni imfabusa mu guhisha kuboneka kwa Kristo Yesu ku mugeni wanjye n'umubiri wanjye. Oya, sinshobora kubuzwa cyangwa guhishwa na gato kandi isi yose izamenya ibyo kuboneka kwanjye, nubwo abaguye bazashaka guhisha kuboneka kwanjye binyuze mu ntambara n'igitero cyabo cy'ibinyoma cy'abanyamahanga. Musobanukirwe ko nta banyamahanga baturuka mu zindi si zizenguruka mu binyoma ahubwo ni abaguye birukanywe mu ijuru iteka ryose biyoberanya kuko bose ari abiyoberanya baza gushuka no kuyobya.

Musobanukirwe ko kuboneka kwanjye atari kimwe no kugaruka kwanjye kwa kabiri kandi ni ibanga rihishe mu ibanga rikomeye ry'Imana njye, Yeshua HaMashiach, nahishuriye intumwa yanjye Pawulo. Kugaruka kwanjye kwa kabiri kwamenyekanye kandi gutegerezwa mu bihe byinshi ubu kandi kuzana no kuzuka kw'abakiranutsi. Mu kuboneka kwanjye nza gukoranyiriza aho ndi abera banjye kugira ngo mbahembe kandi nshyingiranwe n'umugeni wanjye kandi nsangire n'abashyitsi banjye b'icyubahiro. Ni mu kuboneka kwanjye nzishikuzira abizerwa baje aho ndi bihannye byuzuye nkabajyana mu mutekano w'ijuru kugeza urubanza ku isi rurangiye, kuko ibihe by'urubanza byageze ku isi yose cyane cyane ku baguye.

Musobanukirwe ko kugaruka kwanjye kwa kabiri ari bwo nzazana n'abacunguwe banjye n'abantu 144,000 banjye, n'ingabo zose zo mu ijuru kurimbura burundu ubwami bwa Satani, irimbuka ubwami bwe butazigera bwiyubakamo. Kandi mu kugaruka kwanjye kwa kabiri bose bazabona kandi bose bazamenya kandi nta gushuka gushobora gushuka umuntu uwo ari we wese kuri iki gikorwa. Satani si umwami kandi abaguye si imana ahubwo ni abiyoberanya biyoberanya. Kandi Satani azabohwa ajugunywe ikuzimu imyaka igihumbi kugira ngo atazongera gushuka amahanga kugeza igihe azabohorerwa igihe gito cye kandi aho azaguma aboshye mu munyururu munini mu bubabare n'ubwoba bukomeye bw'umurage we muri rya ziba ryaka umuriro n'amazuku atazigera acika. Kandi nyuma y'igihe gito cye azajugunywa burundu muri rya ziba ryaka iteka ry'amazuku anuka yaka ibirimi bishyushye cyane by'ubururu. Kandi ni bwo urubanza rw'intebe y'era rukomeye ruzaba, ndetse rwose mbere y'uko Yahweh arema ijuru rishya n'isi nshya, kandi agahindura byose bishya mu isozwa ry'iteka kandi ritunganye.

Kandi niko byanditswe kandi niko bizasohora vuba ko nzamanuka mvuye mu ijuru n'ijwi riranguruye n'ijwi rya marayika mukuru n'impanda y'Imana kandi iryo jwi rikomeye ry'impanda ntiritinda kandi ni impanda ya nyuma y'igihe cyanjye cy'ubuntu uko isi ihindukira guhangana n'urubanza rwayo rwegereje kandi rukomeye. Kandi nzakoranya intore zanjye mu mfuruka enye z'isi, ndetse n'abasinziriye muri Kristo bose, yego bose basinziriye muri Kristo bashyiriweho ikimenyetso cy'uwo mwuka wera w'isezerano. Kandi ibise bidahagarara bizaha inzira isi ibyara abasinziriye, ariko abo bidashoboka ko urupfu rubagumana n'isegonda na rimwe. Kandi namwe abazaba bariho basigaye muzazamurwa mu bicu hamwe na bo gusanganira njye mu bicu kandi mu kanya nk'ako guhumbya muzabona.

Yego, muzabona n'amaso yanyu ubwiza bw'iteka bw'Imana ikomeye ihoraho, ndetse na Data w'Imicyo wifuza kubaho kwanyu kandi wateguriye iki gihe iteka ryose kuko mwahoze mu mutima wa Yahweh. Kandi muzambona, Umukiza wanyu w'iteka kandi uhoraho witangiye wese mu bubabare ubwo nitangaga ho igitambo kugira ngo mubone kubaho imbere ya Data w'Imicyo wabyaye imicyo y'isi. Yego, muzambona n'amaso yanyu, ndetse namwe mwese mwizera Umwami wanyu wahawe ubwiza uwo mutarabona n'amaso yanyu.

Muzabona n'amaso yanyu ubwiza bwanjye bwuzuye Yahweh yampaye mu murage wanjye nzasangira byuzuye n'abaneshi banjye kandi nzahembamo bose baza aho ndi n'imirimo ihangara ikigeragezo cy'umuriro. Ni ukuri, ni ukuri, muzabona n'amaso yanyu ingabo zo mu ijuru, izo benshi boherejwe gukorera abazaragwa agakiza k'iteka. Kandi muzahagarara mu buzima bwuzuye, ndetse muremwe bundi bushya mu butungane bwo mu ijuru kandi muzahagarara rwose mutangazwe n'icyo iherezo ryanyu ry'iteka rizazana n'icyo ubutunzi bw'ubwiza bw'umurage wanjye mu bera busobanura by'ukuri kuri mwe.

Musobanukirwe bakunzi banjye, (ndetse namwe mwese mudakunda amagara yanyu kugeza ku gupfa) ndetse namwe mwavuye mu byaha byanyu mwihannye byuzuye: Iherezo ryanyu ry'iteka ni intangiriro yanyu y'iteka aho muzahagarara mu isegonda ryanyu rya mbere ry'ubuzima bw'iteka kandi nta kizongera kubaboroza na gato. Kuko muzaremwa bundi bushya mutunganye mu butungane bwera kandi ntimuzigera mubora ukundi kuko muzaremwa mudashobora kubora.

Kandi iteka ryose muzakorera Yahweh, kandi iteka ryose muzabana nanjye aho nzajya hose. Kandi nzabasubiza ibyo inzige zariye byose n'ibyo Satani yababye byose. Muzahemberwa byuzuye imirimo yanyu n'imiruho y'urukundo mwerekanye ku izina ryanjye kuko mwakoreye abera kandi mukabakorera. Tuzarya ku byiza by'igihugu mu gihe cy'ingoma yanjye y'imyaka igihumbi aho muzategekana nanjye nk'abami n'abatambyi banjye kandi rizaba itegeko rikiranuka iteka ryose.

Ni njye wabogeje rwose ibyaha mu maraso yanjye y'impongano.

Ni njye Pasika yanyu itunganye wituye Yahweh Imana ikomeye ihoraho.

Ni njye wahumuye amaso y'abiyoroshya mu mutima.

Ni njye mucuzi uvugutira amakara kugira ngo mugere ku murage wanyu wuzuye muri njye, Kristo Yesu.

Kandi amagambo y'ubutumwa bwiza bw'iteka azarindwa mu byago bigiye kuza kandi azabona amaso, amatwi, n'imitima y'abo yohererejwe kuko njye, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndetse Kristo Yesu ntangaza ibi bintu n'ubutware bwuzuye n'imbaraga bya Yahweh ubwe wasangiye intebe ye n'umwana we w'ikinege kugira ngo binyuze mu kuboko kwanjye gukomeye ibyaremwe bishobore gusubizwa iteka ryose umuremyi wabyo uhimbazwa iteka ryose, yego na Amen.
