Ubuhamya Bwombi: Itorero
Mu Mirimo ya Petero
15 Mata 2024

Kuki mwataye amabanga yanjye?

Kuki mwataye amabanga yanjye, yewe torero ry'i Lawodikiya ry'akazuyazi? Kuki mwataye ibanga rikomeye ry'Imana? Mwaguranye amabanga yera cyane ya Data, ndetse na Kristo uri muri mwe ibyiringiro by'ubwiza, kugira ngo mubone indamu mbi, kandi inda yanyu niyo ibabereye imana. Nyamara, muzambwira muti, "Nta kibi kindimo. Sinkurikiye indamu mbi, kuko ndi intungane imbere yawe." Nanjye nkababwira nti: Ese ntimukurikira ubutumwa bwiza bw'ibinyoma bw'ubutunzi bwigishwa mu matorero yanyu y'i Lawodikiya? Mwagurishije ukuri kw'amabanga yera cyane y'Imana kugira ngo mubone inyungu z'isi, kandi ntimukigira ibyiringiro by'ubwiza nifuza cyane ko musobanukirwa, ahubwo mwahinduye ibyiringiro by'ubwiza mo ibyiringiro by'inyungu z'ubutunzi mushaka kwirundanyaho binyuze mu bitekerezo byanyu byanduye. Sinabahamagariye amafaranga no gukunda iby'isi, ahubwo nabahamagariye gukiranuka no kwera nyakuri kuboneka muri jye.

Muvē mu makosa yanyu kandi mungureho umuti w'amaso, kugira ngo musige amaso yanyu mubashe kubona ukuri ko mu Byanditswe nabahaye kugira ngo mukizwe. Mwashutswe ngo mushyigikire za guverinoma z'isi, mwibwira ko kuzishyigikira ari ugushyigikira abatoranyijwe b'Imana. Mukwiye kumva mukoze isoni imbere yanjye kuko mu bujiji bwanyu mudasobanukiwe Isirayeli nyakuri y'Imana iyo ari yo, kandi ntimugisobanukiwe ibanga rikomeye ry'Imana ry'uko Abanyamahanga bagomba kuba abaraganwa umurage, n'ab'umubiri umwe, n'abafatanyije isezerano ryayo muri Kristo, binyuze mu Butumwa Bwiza. Kuko ari jye mahoro yanyu, wahinduye ababiri kuba umwe, kandi nkasenya urukuta rwagabanyaga hagati yanyu.

Mwemeye ko isinagogi ya Satani ibiba umwirondoro wanyu, kandi mwatakarije mu magambo y'abahanuzi b'ibinyoma. Ese ntimusobanukiwe ko muri Isirayeli y'Imana? Abagendera kuri iryo tegeko bose, amahoro n'imbabazi bibe kuri bo, no kuri Isirayeli y'Imana. Yewe torero ry'akazuyazi, ni nde uzabagikiza ubusambanyi bwanyu bwabononnye bukabakura ku mutima umwe uri muri jye, Kristo Yesu Umwami wanyu? Ntimushobora kugundira iyi si igiye kurimbuka, mwibwira ko kubikora ari ukunkorera; kandi mukwiye kwihana ugushyigikira kwanyu guverinoma z'isi mbi, kuko Kristo ahuriye he na Beliyali, kandi ntibankorera ahubwo bakorera Satani. Mujye mu Byanditswe byanjye mushake kamere yanyu nyakuri nahishe mu nzandiko zanjye; kuko mwapfuye, kandi ubugingo bwanyu bukahishwana na Kristo mu Mana. Kandi uwapfuye aba abatuwe ku cyaha, kandi itegeko ry'Umwuka utanga ubugingo ryababaturiye itegeko ry'icyaha n'urupfu.

Nuko rero, umuntu wese iyo ari muri Kristo aba ari icyaremwe gishya: ibya kera biba bishize, dore byose biba bihindutse bishya. Nabaremye muri jye nk'uruhinja ruvutse, ruhumeka umwuka warwo wa mbere mu buzima bw'iteka; kuko ubuzima bw'iteka bwatangiye kuri mwe isegonda mwabyawe ubwa kabiri n'umwuka wanjye, kuko ababiri nabahinduye umuntu umwe mushya, bityo nzana amahoro. Yego, umuntu umwe mushya muri jye ugizwe n'Umuyahudi n'Umunyamahanga mu itorero ryanjye ry'Imana. Abo nahamagariye kugira ubugingo bushya muri jye ni ubwoko bwatoranyijwe bw'Imana, kandi ababyawe n'umwuka wanjye ni abana nyakuri b'Imana, kandi umuntu wese utagira umwuka wa Kristo, ntaba ari uwe.

Kuki mwanga ibice bimwe by'Ibyanditswe, mukabifata nk'ibyanduye kubera ibyo mwita ibitekerezo byiza? Kuki mucamo Ibyanditswe ibice, mwibwira ko Ijambo ry'Imana ryose atari iryo kubigisha, kubakosora, no kubatoza? Ibitekerezo byiza by'ibinyoma byirengagiza ukuri bibahuma amaso ntimubone inzira zanjye n'imigambi yanjye nabateguriye kera. Yego, mukwiye gushyira ibitekerezo byanyu ku biri hejuru, ariko ntimukite ibice by'Ibyanditswe byanjye iby'amakuba n'umwijima kuko mutabifata nk'ibitekerezo byiza. Mwaguye mu mutego w'uburozi bwa gikristo, mukoresha ubumenyi bw'abamarayika baguye bigishije abantu uburozi n'ubupfumu no gukata imizi uhereye kera.

Ubumenyi bwanyu bw'abamarayika baguye ni ikinyoma cyahimbwe n'ubwenge kigamije kugusha ubugingo bwanyu mu mutego binyuze mu magambo meza n'imvugo nziza. Mumenye ko abantu benshi babi mu myaka yashize binjije ubupfumu mu Bukristo, bababwira ko niba mutekereje ku kintu igihe kirekire, kigomba kuba nk'uko mubitekereza. Wa mupfapfa we! Imana ntiyakuremeye kwiyubakira ibishuko byawe bwite bishingiye ku bukristo bwawe bwo kwifasha n'ibitekerezo byiza. Mukurikira undi Yesu kandi mwataye kwizera kuri muri jye, kandi mwataye amagambo yanjye n'amategeko yanjye muhitamo amagambo meza n'imvugo nziza, kandi mwagoteshejwe n'ibirura by'abanyamururumba bibabeshya.

Barababeshya kandi bagakoresha nabi amategeko ya Mose, babwira Abakristo ko bagomba gutanga icyacumi kugira ngo bashyigikire imiryango yabo itubatswe kuri jye, ibuye rikuru ry'imfuruka. Musuzume Ibyanditswe, kandi musobanukirwe ko nk'uko umuntu agambiriye mu mutima we, abe ari ko atanga kandi atange yishimye, kuko Imana ikunda utanga yishimye. Kandi iri ni itegeko ry'ingenzi mu Isezerano Rishya ryemejwe mu maraso yanjye. Icyacumi cyari icyo gushyigikira abatambyi b'Abalewi, nabo bagashyigikira umutambyi mukuru binyuze mu ituro ryo kuzamurwa. Ibyo byakuweho muri jye, kuko ndi umutambyi mukuru iteka ryose mu buryo bwa Melikisedeki kandi nabahinduye abatambyi n'abami imbere yanjye. Ariko mwe, yewe torero ry'akazuyazi, mwahumwe amaso n'iyerekwa ry'imbaraga n'ubushobozi by'ibinyoma, ubushobozi butaboneka muri Kristo.

Nta kintu na kimwe mugenzura binyuze mu bitekerezo byanyu gusa, kandi mumpumwe amaso ntimubona imigambi yanjye nabateguriye. Mwizibye amatwi ngo mutumva inyigisho nzima muhitamo inyigisho z'ibinyoma zibaryoharyohya amatwi. Si ibyo gusa, ahubwo mugaba ibitero ku bavandimwe babaye abanzi banyu kuko bababwira ukuri kuri ibi bintu, kandi mwanze imyumvire y'ab'i Beroya muhitamo amarangamutima yiswe Ubukristo mu buryo bwayobye. Musobanukirwe ibi, ba shebuja bayobye: muzishyurwa mu rubanza kubera kuyobya intama zanjye, kandi guhora ni ukwanjye nanjye nzishyura.

Musobanukirwe ibi, bana banjye: nta kintu na kimwe mugenzura binyuze mu bitekerezo byanyu byiza, kandi ntimuri rukuruzi y'ubutunzi kubera ibitekerezo byanyu byiza. Mwarashutswe kandi mwayobejwe n'abashukanyi benshi. Byongeye kandi, nta kintu mushobora gukora ntabarimo nk'uko nanjye nta kintu nshobora gukora Data atandimo; kuko Imana ari yo iha imbaraga abakiranutsi kandi Data akaba ari we waremye byose, kandi buri wese kugira ngo aheshe icyubahiro izina rikiranuka rya Yehova hejuru ya byose!

Muvē mu mururumba wanyu kandi mureke gukunda amafaranga, kuko gukunda amafaranga ari umuzi w'ibibi byose.

Iyo mufunze amaso yanyu ntimubone ubutumwa bwiza bwanjye bwose mugahitamo gusa ibyo mwita "ibyiza," muba muri mu rwobo hamwe n'abayobozi bahumye babayoboye mwembi kuri iyo ndunduro. Sinabahamagariye kugwa mu rwobo bana banjye, ahubwo nabahamagariye kubaho imbere yanjye mu gukiranuka no kwera nyakuri, nuko rero mwikure mu byondo. Nabahamagariye kureka ibyaha byanyu no kwihana inzira zanyu zikiranirwa. Binyuze gusa mu maraso yanjye y'impongano ni bwo mushobora kwezwa ibyaha byanyu; ibitekerezo byanyu byiza ntibikora gukiranuka, nubwo mwabigize ibigirwamana by'imitima yanyu.

Kuki mwemerera abavandimwe b'ibinyoma n'Abayudaiza kwinjira hagati yanyu kugira ngo batate umudendezo mufite muri jye, Umwami ukiranuka? Iyo muza gusobanukirwa ibanga rikomeye ry'Imana, mwari gusobanukirwa ko Kristo ari iherezo ry'Amategeko kugira ngo uwizera wese ahabwe gukiranuka, Kuko Mose asobanura gukiranuka kuva mu Mategeko, ko umuntu ukora ibyo azabeshwaho na byo. Kuko nongera guhamiriza umuntu wese ukebwa, ko agomba kwitondera amategeko yose. Bite se ku mategeko ya Mose, noneho?

Nujuje ibisabwa n'amategeko kandi ntsindishiriza umuntu binyuze mu kunyizera, kuko nanesheje, kandi nujuje amategeko, nitanga nk'impongano itunganye ya Pasika rimwe rizima; nuko rero, umukiranutsi azabeshwaho no kwizera. Ese ntimwasomye ko ntaje gukuraho amategeko cyangwa abahanuzi ahubwo naje kuzuza amategeko no guha abantu gukiranuka binyuze mu kwifatanya nanjye, Umwami wabo Yesu Kristo.

Ndavuga nti, mugendere mu mwuka, kandi ntimuzakora ibyo kamere irarikira, kandi nta tegeko rihana imbuto z'umwuka. Nuko rero, mwahambanywe nanjye mu mubatizo mu rupfu rwanjye, kugira ngo nk'uko Kristo yazuwe mu bapfuye n'ubwiza bwa Data: abe ari ko namwe mugendera mu bugingo bushya. Binyuze gusa mu mirimo yanjye y'impongano, nk'umwana w'intama w'Imana utagira icyaha, ni bwo umuntu wese ashobora kunesha icyaha akabaturwa ku muvumo w'amategeko; kuko nahinduwe umuvumo ku bwanyu, kuko havumwe umuntu wese umanitswe ku giti.

Mose yasobanuye gukiranuka kuva mu mategeko, Ko umuntu ukora ibyo azabeshwaho na byo. Kuko muri Kristo Yesu gukebwa cyangwa kudakebwa nta cyo bimaze, ahubwo icyaremwe gishya ni cyo gifite agaciro. Kristo ni iherezo ry'amategeko ku wizera wese, nyamara benshi muri mwe bazanga aya magambo kuko mwubaha amagambo y'abapasiteri banyu, ababwiriza, n'abarabi banyu kuruta amagambo y'Umwami n'Umukiza wanyu, Kristo Yesu. Ese ntimusobanukiwe ko mwubatswe ku rufatiro rw'intumwa n'abahanuzi, Yesu Kristo ubwe akaba ibuye rikuru ry'imfuruka? Kuko nta rundi rufatiro umuntu ashobora gushyiraho, uretse urwashyizweho, ari rwo Yesu Kristo; nuko rero, ntimuri munsi y'amategeko ahubwo muri munsi y'ubuntu.

Kuko abizera bashobora kwinjira mu buruhuko bwanjye, kuko uwinjiye mu buruhuko bwanjye na we aba aruhuse imirimo ye, nk'uko Imana yaruhutse iyayo ku munsi wa karindwi. Ese ntimuzi ko amategeko yari umurezi wanyu wo kubageza kuri jye, Kristo Yesu? Nyamara benshi baranyanze banga n'ubuntu bwanjye n'imbabazi zanjye bahitamo imirimo yabo bwite itagira umumaro yo gukiranuka. Ubuhumyi bwageze kuri Isirayeli igice kugeza ubwo umubare wuzuye w'Abanyamahanga uzinjira. Yewe shami ry'igiti cy'umunzenze, ntukirate ku giti cy'umunzenze watewemo, kandi ntukurikire ubuhumyi bwabo; kuko nshobora gukiza Yakobo kandi nzakora ibyo rwose kuko abasigaye bazakizwa.

Nuko rero ntihakagire umuntu ubacira urubanza ku byo murya, cyangwa ibyo munywa, cyangwa ku byerekeye iminsi mikuru, cyangwa imboneko z'ukwezi, cyangwa amasabato. Kuki mushyira umutwaro ku bana banjye, mubabwira ko bagomba kuvuga izina ryanjye mu rurimi runaka rwera? Numva indimi zose kandi ndeba mu mutima, bityo menya iyo abana banjye bambambaje mu rurimi urwo ari rwo rwose bambazamo, kandi mbashyiraho ikimenyetso cya Data nk'uko bikwiye, kandi mbazana mu Itorero ry'Imana. Yaba ba sogokuruza cyangwa mwe ntimushobora kwitondera amategeko, ariko amategeko yuzurizwa muri mwe mu gukiranuka kwanjye; kuko amategeko yose akubiye mu mategeko abiri akomeye: Kunda Uwiteka Imana yawe n'umutima wawe wose, n'ubugingo bwawe bwose, n'ubwenge bwawe bwose, n'imbaraga zawe zose, kandi ukunde mugenzi wawe nk'uko wikunda.

Gukiranuka ntikuva mu mategeko ahubwo kuva mu kunyizera, Umwami Yesu Kristo. Kuko ndi umutambyi mukuru iteka ryose mu buryo bwa Melikisedeki, kandi binyuze mu maraso yanjye y'impongano muratsindishirizwa, kuko ndi Pasika itunganye, kandi ndi umwana w'intama w'Imana. Ntihakagire umuntu ubayobya muri ibi bintu. Amategeko ntiyashyiriweho umukiranutsi, ahubwo yashyiriweho abagome n'abatumvira. Dore! Nahanaguye urwandiko rw'amategeko, rwari rubarwanya, rwari rubangamiye mwe, ndarukuraho, ndarubamba ku musaraba wanjye. Nyamara, muracyifuza kuba imbata z'ibintu bya mbere bidakomeye kandi bikennye. Ibyanditswe bivuga iki: Wirukane umuja n'umuhungu we, kuko tutari abana b'umuja ahubwo turi ab'umugore widegemvya.

Ese sinabivuze nweruye nti: Dore! Ndababwira ibanga, ntituzasinzira twese, ahubwo twese tuzahindurwa, kuko nzamanuka mvuye mu ijuru n'ijwi rirenga, n'ijwi rya marayika mukuru n'impanda y'Imana kandi abapfiriye muri Kristo nibo bazabanza kuzuka, hanyuma mwe muzaba muriho musigaye muzazamuwe mu bicu musanganire jye, Kristo Yesu mu kirere, bityo muzabana nanjye iteka ryose. Yewe torero ry'i Lawodikiya, mwanga ndetse n'amagambo yanjye yumvikana neza nababwiye. Nuko rero, mumenye ko nzabanga ku meza yanjye, mu gihe nifuzaga cyane kubategurira umwanya mu ijuru.

Mutekereza ko ari ikintu cyoroheje kwanga amagambo yanjye no kuyobya abandi mu makosa yanyu. Nuko rero, ubwo mushaka umunsi w'Uwiteka, nzabareka mwihanganire umunsi w'Uwiteka, kuko mwibwira mu bwibone bwanyu ko mushobora guhagarara. Mwanga ukuboneka kwanjye kandi mugakuza amagambo y'abahanuzi b'ibinyoma hejuru y'inyigisho nzima ntanze muri jye. Ese ntimwasomye umugani w'abakobwa icumi b'amasugi? Ndabagira inama yo kwizera amagambo yanjye kandi ntimunyihakane, kugira ngo nanjye ntazabihakana. Muzaba abahe, bana banjye? Ese muzaba mu bakobwa b'abanyabwenge biteguye bagakata intambi zabo bakagira n'amavuta mu matabaza yabo? Cyangwa muzajya mu mwanya w'abakobwa b'abapfapfa, muvuga muti, Mwami, Mwami dukingurire; nanjye nzababwira nti, Simbazi!

Ese ntimwasomye? Ni cyo gituma umugabo azasiga se na nyina, akabana n'umugore we, kandi bombi bazaba umubiri umwe. Iri ni ibanga rikomeye: ariko ndavuga ibyerekeye Kristo n'Itorero. Ese ntimusobanukiwe amagambo y'umukwe, mugeni wanjye? Mugomba kungundira nk'umutwe wanyu mu buryo bumwe umugore agundira umugabo we nk'umutwe we, kuko Umugeni ari Itorero ryanjye n'umubiri wanjye, kandi ni nde mugabo wigeze yanga umubiri we? Nyamara, mwemerera abungeri bayobye kubayobya muri iki kintu, kandi benshi muri mwe muha agaciro amagambo agoretse y'abahanuzi b'ibinyoma bagoretse hejuru y'Ijambo ryanjye Ryera.

Mwashyize kwizera kwanyu mu bantu babi, abantu ntatumye; nyamara barababeshya bakoresheje amagambo meza n'imvugo nziza ntabahaye. Murabakurikira nk'aho ari jye mukurikira. Mwihane! Benshi muri mwe mukurikira abakozi b'ibinyoma bigira nk'abakozi bo gukiranuka. Nta gitangaza rero, kuko Satani yihindura nka marayika w'umucyo, bityo ntibikwiye gutangaza ko n'abakozi be bakora batyo. Benshi muri mwe mwahumwe amaso n'umucyo w'ikinyoma, uturuka ku bakozi b'ibinyoma ntatumye, kandi bavuga amagambo akomeye yibutumbe anyuranye n'inyigisho nzima iri muri jye, Umwami Yesu Kristo.

Mwihagarareho, yewe Bakristo batagira urutirigongo, kandi mumuvēmo muve muri Babuloni y'Ibanga. Nifuzaga ko muba ashyushye cyangwa akonje ntimube akazuyazi, kandi nta cyifuzo mfite cyo kubaruka mu kanwa kanjye. Nuko rero, mugaruke kuri jye, bana banjye, kandi mugaruke ku magambo yanjye. Mungundire nk'umutwe wanyu, kuko ndi Umwami wabapfiriye, kandi mwazutse igihe nazukaga. Kuki mwishingikiriza ku bahanuzi b'ibinyoma? Ntimukeneye ko hagira umuntu ubigisha, ahubwo gusigwa mwahawe nanjye kuguma muri mwe kandi kukabigisha byose, yewe abafite kwizera guke! Muhindukirire jye kandi mugaruke mu nzira ifunganye. Murire, maboroge, kandi mwihane, bana banjye! Abo nkunda bose, ndabacyaha, kugira ngo guhamagarwa kwanyu no gutoranywa kwanyu gukomere.

Mumenye ko mbakunda cyane kandi nifuza umutima wanyu wose kuruta imirimo yanyu. Muze kuri jye n'umutima ufunguye kandi mumpe byose byanyu, nk'uko nanjye nabahaye byose byanjye. Ntimutinye kandi ntimwicire urubanza, kuko nta rubanza ruri ku bari muri Kristo Yesu. Mugaruke gusa kuri jye kandi mwature ibyaha byanyu, nanjye nzabababarira kandi mbagarure mu migambi n'impano nabahaye. Muhagarare mushikamye kandi mukomeye, kuko nzaboneka mu buryo butunguranye vuba cyane, kandi isi izamenya ko nabatwaye, kuko iki kintu kitazakorwa mu rwihisho. Igihe ni gito, bana banjye, kandi vuba cyane igihe ntikizaba kikiriho.

Nshobora gukiza Yakobo kandi nzakora ibyo rwose kuko abasigaye bazakizwa.

Ndi umutambyi mukuru iteka ryose mu buryo bwa Melikisedeki.

Ndi Pasika itunganye.

Ndi umwana w'intama w'Imana.

Ndi Umwami wabapfiriye, ndetse Yeshua HaMashiach.
