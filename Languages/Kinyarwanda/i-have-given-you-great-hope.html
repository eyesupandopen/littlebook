Ubuhamya Bubiri: Itorero
Mu Mirimo y'Intumwa Pawulo
3 Gicurasi 2024

Nabahaye Ibyiringiro Bikomeye!

Nimuze aho ndi mwumve amagambo yanjye, Mugeni wanjye, kuko ndi hano kugira ngo nhoze imitima yanyu, mpumurize imitima yanyu, kandi mbakize muri njye, Kristo Yesu. Ntimucike intege ku bw'amagambo yanjye yo gucyaha, kuko uwo nkunda, ndamuhana, kugira ngo guhamagarwa no gutoranywa kwanyu gukomere muri njye, Umukwe, ari we Kristo Yesu. Bana banjye, mutege amatwi ijwi ryanjye kandi mwumve amagambo yanjye, kuko ntabwo nabahamagariye urubanza no gucirwaho iteka, ahubwo nabagize abakiranutsi muri njye, ari we Mwami wanyu Yesu Kristo. Ntimwumvise ibyo Pawulo yabwiye Abakorinto mu rwandiko rwe rwa mbere? Kandi noneho hagumaho kwizera, ibyiringiro n'urukundo, ibyo uko ari bitatu: ariko ikiruta byose ni urukundo. Kandi bityo urukundo ni rwo rukomeye muri ibyo uko ari bitatu, kuko urukundo rwa Yehova rudashobora gutsindwa. Urukundo rwa Yehova ni rwo rwomeka rukahuza. Ni imbaraga ikomeye itera ishyaka, kandi gutanga ku buntu nk'uko mwahawe ku buntu ni ukwigana Imana!

Benshi muri mwe mwatakaje ibyiringiro, ndetse n'ibyiringiro byanyu muri njye, Yesu Kristo, kuko nabahaye ibyiringiro bikomeye, bana banjye. Mumenye ko umujura yabayobya kandi akabayobya, ariko Imana si iy'umuvurungano, ahubwo ni iy'amahoro nk'uko bimeze mu matorero yose y'abera. Satani yagerageje guhisha amategeko yanjye natanze mu guhishurirwa kwanjye kw'Ibanga Rikomeye ry'Imana ku Ntumwa Pawulo muri iyi Yubile nyinshi zashize. Bana banjye, ibyiringiro byanyu ni cyo gitekerezo kibatera ishyaka ryo kwihanganira ibigeragezo n'amakuba menshi mufite muri ubu buzima. Kuko iyo mutagira ibyiringiro byanyu, mwaba nk'uko Pawulo yabitangarije Abakorinto: Niba muri ubu buzima gusa ari ho twiringiye Kristo, tuba turi abantu bo kugirirwa impuhwe kuruta abandi bose. Ariko noneho Kristo yazutse mu bapfuye, aba umuganura w'abasinziriye. Bana banjye, nabahaye ibyiringiro bikomeye ku bw'ibyiringiro byanyu, kuko navuze nti: twebwe abazaba bakiriho basigaye, tuzazamurwa mu bicu, nk'uko abapfiriye muri Kristo bazabanza kuzuka, kandi twese tuzahindurwa, kuko ari ko muzaba nkanjye iteka ryose kandi ni ko Umugeni wanjye azabana nanjye iteka ryose.

Kuko ibyiringiro byanyu muri Kristo ni ibiki, bana banjye? Ese si isezerano ryo gucungurwa no gusubizwa mu buzima bwuzuye, aho muzabona ijuru n'icyubahiro n'ikuzo bya Yehova n'amaso yanyu? Ese ibyiringiro byanyu si ejo hazaza aho muzankorera mu bwami bwanjye bw'o gukiranuka, kuko mwarabaye abizerwa mu bintu bike, nanjye nzabagira abatware b'ibintu byinshi. Ese ibyiringiro byanyu si isezerano ry'ubugingo buhoraho aho muzarya ku giti cy'ubugingo kandi mukagendera mu mihanda ya Yerusalemu Nshya, aho umucyo wa Yehova n'umucyo wa Yeshua HaMashiach bizahora bimurika mu bwiza bw'ikuzo? Ese ibyiringiro byanyu si ikuzo ry'amakamba menshi n'ingororano ku bw'ubudahemuka bwanyu n'urukundo mukunda njye, Kristo Yesu? Ese ibyiringiro byanyu si ibyiringiro by'ubuzima bwiza bwuzuye, nta ndwara cyangwa uburwayi ukundi? Ese ibyiringiro byanyu si uko nzashyira abanzi banyu munsi y'ibirenge byanyu ndetse nkanatuma isinagogi ya Satani yikubita imbere y'ibirenge byanyu? (Kuko bazamenya ko nabakunze!) Ese ibyiringiro byanyu si iby'ibyishimo bihoraho imbere ya Yehova ubwe? Kuko azatura mu bantu, kandi ntihazongera kubaho ubwami bwigabanyijemo, ahubwo Ubwami bw'Imana gusa, kuko nta wukomeye kuruta Yehova; oya, nta n'umwe ushobora kugereranywa n'ubwiza bw'Imana ikomeye ihoraho, ari we Data!

Mwishimire ejo hazaza hanyu, bana banjye, kuko nabahaye ejo hazaza n'intego yera muri njye, Umwami wanyu urabagirana! Ese ibyiringiro byanyu si ibyiringiro bikomeye aho mukizwa umujinya muri njye? Kuko ibyanyu ari ibyiringiro bikomeye kuruta byose, ibyiringiro byavuzwe n'igikoresho cyanjye natoranyije Intumwa Pawulo, igisonga nyacyo cy'Ibanga Rikomeye ry'Imana? Kuko nahishuriye Pawulo ibyo yandikiye Abakolosayi: Ari ryo banga ryahishwe uhereye kera kose n'ibihe byose, ariko none rikaba ryahishuriwe abera bayo, abo Imana yashatse kumenyesha ubutunzi bw'ubwiza bw'iri banga mu banyamahanga, ari ryo Kristo uri muri mwe, ibyiringiro by'ubwiza. Ibyanyu ni ibyiringiro by'ubwiza bikwiriye umwami, kuko nzabagira abami n'abatambyi imbere y'Imana yacu; kuko ndi umutambyi mukuru iteka ryose mu buryo bwa Melikisedeki, kandi ntihazabaho undi.

Bana banjye bato, ntimutinye ibinyoma by'umwanzi, kuko yabananiza mu busa iyo abishobora; ariko yahuye n'umurusha imbaraga muri njye kandi namutsinze rwose, kandi ndaje gusenya ubwami bwe, kuko natumwe gusenya imirimo ya Satani, kandi nanjye ni ko nabatumye! Inzoka ya kera n'ubwami bwayo bwose nta byiringiro bafite. Bazi ko baciriweho iteka ryo kurimbuka no guhanwa iteka ryose, aho bazashya mu birimi by'umuriro by'ubururu bishyushye cyane mu gihe bazaba boga mu mazuku yaka iteka ryose. Babagiriye ishyari kandi batanga icyo ari cyo cyose ngo babe mwe, bityo ntimwumve amagambo yabo. Munyumve, Mugeni wanjye; Satani azakora ibishoboka byose ngo abakure ku ngororano yanyu muri njye, Kristo Yesu. Azakora ibishoboka byose ngo atwikire ibyiringiro byanyu nabahaye, bityo amayeri ye muri uru rwego inshuro nyinshi ni ugutesha agaciro icyo gitekerezo binyuze mu kwigana ukuri no binyuze mu rurimi rwe rubeshya.

Ntimumutinye, ahubwo muve mu binyoma bye kandi mugumane ibyiringiro byanyu by'ukuri mu ntekerezo, kuko ntimwasomye? Umwuka ubwe ahamanya n'umwuka wacu ko turi abana b'Imana. Kandi niba turi abana, turi n'abaragwa, abaragwa b'Imana, n'abaraganwa na Kristo: niba tubabazanywa na we, kugira ngo duhabwe ubwiza hamwe na we. Kuko nibwira yuko imibabaro y'iki gihe idakwiriye kugereranywa n'ubwiza buzahishurirwa muri twe. Bana banjye, iyaba mwari musobanukiwe ubukomezi bw'icyo bivuze kuba abaraganwa na Kristo, ariko vuba aha muzamenya nk'uko namwe mumenywe. Ntimusobanukiwe ko Imana yampaye ibintu byose mu ijuru no mu isi, kandi umurage wanjye ni wo murage ukomeye? Bana banjye, ntimuzi gusa abo muri bo by'ukuri, kuko kamere yanyu nyakuri ihishe muri njye, kandi ubwo nzaboneka, namwe muzabonekana nanjye mu bwiza!

Ntimutinye, kandi mugume mushikamye mu rukundo rwanyu no kwizera kwanyu, kuko ibyiringiro byanyu bizatuma mugendera mu kwihangana gukomeye; kuko ibyiringiro byanjye n'ibyishimo Data yashyize imbere yanjye byatumye nsohoza iki? Kuko Abaheburayo havuga ngo: Dutumbira Yesu, we Ntangiriro n'Umusozo wo kwizera kwacu, uwo kubw'ibyishimo byashyizwe imbere ye, yihanganiye umusaraba, asuzugura isoni, kandi yicaye iburyo bw'intebe y'Imana. Ese ntabwo nabasigiye icyitegererezo, kandi ntabwo nabaye urugero mukwiye gukurikiza? Ntimusobanukiwe? Ko unyizera atazakora gusa imirimo nakoze mu gihe cyo kuza kwanjye kwa mbere, ahubwo azakora imirimo iruta iyo nakoze? Kuko nagiye kwa Data kandi nabahaye imbaraga kandi mbahagarariye n'imbaraga zanjye zose, kuko nkomeye, kuko ari ko Imana yangize mu kumvira kwanjye.

Bantu banjye, aya magambo akwiye kubatera inkunga no kubaha imbaraga zo guhagarara, kuko nashyize ibyishimo bikomeye imbere yanyu! Ntimwizere ibinyoma by'umwanzi ku byerekeye ibyiringiro byanyu, kuko yagerageza kubasiga nta byiringiro kandi mukandagirwa munsi y'ibirenge, ndetse no mu ntege nke zanyu muhindurwa abakomeye muri njye, Umwami wanyu Yesu Kristo! Ubu ngiye kuvunagura ejo hazaza hanyu, Mugeni wanjye, bityo mutege amatwi amagambo y'akanwa kanjye, kuko ari amagambo yo kwizera, ni amagambo y'urukundo, kandi amagambo yanjye azongera gukongeza ibyiringiro byanyu mu kuri, kuko ibyiringiro bikomeye biri mu kuri nabahaye!

Mbere na mbere, musobanukirwe ko nabahamagaye; nabakijije mu mwuka wanjye w'imbaraga no kuba abana; nabagize icyaremwe gishya muri njye, Kristo Yesu! Ku bw'igitambo cyanjye nka Pasika itunganye, abanyizera bose mu kwizera gukiza guturuka mu mitima yabo nabo bazakizwa umujinya muri njye. Bana banjye, musobanukirwe ko aya magambo ari meza cyane ku bantu benshi, kuko ibyaha byabo bibakubita hasi mu bisebe, gucirwaho iteka, n'isoni, kandi ibi biba kuko inshuro nyinshi banga kwiyuhagira mu maraso yanjye y'impongano, kandi bakirengagiza kwatura ibyaha byabo, kandi bakirengagiza umubano wabo nanjye, umuganwa w'amahoro, kuko agakiza gaturuka mu kwizera gushingiye ku mubano muri njye, Umwami mukuru!

Benshi muri mwe ntimusobanukiwe ko nabatsindishirije, kandi nk'igisubizo cy'uko gutsindishirizwa muri njye, mwakijijwe umujinya, kuko nabwiye Abaroma nti: Noneho rero, ubwo tumaze gutsindishirizwa n'amaraso ye, tuzakizwa umujinya na we rwose. Munsubize ibi, Mugeni wanjye nziye, kuki mwifuza umunsi w'Umwami? Kuki mwumviye abakozi ba Satani biyoberanya nk'abakozi banjye iyo bavuga ko mugomba kwihanganira amakuba ya Yakobo ndetse n'umubabaro mwinshi uba mu gice cya nyuma cy'icyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli? Barababeshya kandi mukabizera. Bishingikiriza kuri kamere yanyu y'icyaha, bababwira ko nta gaciro mufite mu gihe muri isarura ry'igiciro cyinshi; bababwira ko mukwiriye umujinya ukomeye muri njye, kandi barabeshya! Bababwira ko mudakwiye kwitega gukizwa umubabaro, kandi basubiramo nabi Ijambo ryanjye ryera. Kuko mu by'ukuri nabwiye Pawulo ibi bikurikira kugira ngo yigishe Timoteyo: Kurenganywa, imibabaro yambayeho muri Antiyokiya, muri Ikoniyo, muri Lisitira, kurenganywa nihanganiye: ariko muri byose Umwami yarankijije. Yego, n'abashaka kujya bubaha Imana bose muri Kristo Yesu, bazarenganywa. Kandi mu by'ukuri, Satani azakurikirana ababaho mu buzima bwubaha Imana bose, kandi mu kurenganywa kwe kwose nzabakiza nimungarukira mu makuba yanyu kandi mukaguma muri abizerwa mu byiringiro byanyu. Musobanukirwe itandukaniro, kuko Satani yateje urujijo muri iki kibazo: Satani azarenganya abubaha Imana bose, kandi uko ni ukurenganywa; kandi inshuro nyinshi mugomba kwihanganira umujinya we, kuko mu gutsindwa kwe afite uburakari bukomeye. Ariko ntimutinye gutontoma kwe, kuko nigishije Petero, nti: Mwirinde, mube maso: kuko umurenganya wanyu Satani azerera nk'intare itontoma, ashaka uwo aconshomera: Uwo mumurwanye mushikamye mu kwizera, muzi yuko imibabaro nk'iyo isohorezwa ku bene wanyu bari mu isi.

Musobanukirwe, bana banjye, ko umujinya uzaza atari umujinya wa Satani ahubwo ni umujinya w'Imana n'umujinya w'Umwana w'Intama wagenewe Satani. Kuko Satani ari ikigwari cyikurubuye mu byaremwe byose kugira ngo kigerageze kwihisha umujinya yibikiye, yibwira ko yatsinda Yehova muri ibyo bintu; ariko imigambi ya Data ivugwa itya: Databuja, ntiwabibye imbuto nziza mu murima wawe? None urumamfu rwavuye he? Arababwira ati, Umwanzi ni we wabikoze. Abagaragu baramubaza bati, None urashaka ko tugenda tukarurandura? Ariko arababwira ati, Oya: kugira ngo ahari nimurandura urumamfu, mutarurandurana n'amasaka. Mureke bikurane byombi kugeza mu isarura: kandi mu gihe cy'isarura, nzabwira abasaruzi nti, Mubanze muteranye urumamfu, muruhambire mu miba ruzatwikwe: ariko amasaka muyahunitse mu kigega cyanjye. Uko ni ko amasaka azahunikwa mu kigega, ariko urumamfu n'abarubibye bose bakarwuhira bazajugunywa ari bazima muri rya ziba rinini ry'umuriro n'amazuku Data yateguriye Satani n'abamarayika be, ariko nta gice cy'umujinya w'Imana kigenewe mwe, nubwo Satani azagerageza kwikingiriza inyuma yanyu kandi agashaka kubatwara ho iminyago. Ntimutinye, kuko umujinya atari uwanyu. Ndumva imitima myinshi idashikamye igoreka uyu mugani, ibabwira ko muzihanganira umujinya hamwe nabo, ariko babikora birengagiza gahunda isobanutse y'ibyanditswe kuri iki kibazo, kandi bagerageza kubahuma amaso basubiramo nabi umugani wanjye.

Musobanukirwe ko nzabashikukuza nkabajyana mu ijuru, kuko nateguriye Umugeni wanjye ahantu h'umutekano n'ingororano; kuko mu nzu ya Data nzabahisha kandi hari amazu menshi. Bityo yego, nzabateranyiriza hamwe kuri njye mu kuboneka kwanjye, kugiye kuba. Kuko nabwiye Abatesalonike nti: Kuko Umwami ubwe azamanuka ava mu ijuru aranguruye ijwi, n'ijwi rya Marayika mukuru, n'impanda y'Imana: kandi abapfiriye muri Kristo bazabanza kuzuka. Hanyuma twebwe abazaba bakiriho, basigaye, tuzazamuranwa na bo mu bicu, gusanganira Umwami mu kirere: kandi uko ni ko tuzabana n'Umwami iteka ryose. Nuko rero, muhumurizanye n'ayo magambo. Ndababaza, Mugeni wanjye wabeshywe, ese ubu muzanga ibinyoma bya Satani mwihambire ku magambo yanjye kandi muyahumurizwemo kandi muhumurize bene wanyu? Kuko ari ko bigiye kuba vuba, kandi nifuza cyane kubaho kwanyu, kuko mbakunda cyane, kuko ndi Umukwe wanyu.

Nimara gukurwa mu isi, amakuba ya Yakobo azakwira isi yose kandi kurimbuka gukomeye kuzaza gitumo, ariko si ukwanyu, kuko muzatwarwa mu ijuru. Kandi icyo gihe muzahagarara imbere y'intebe yanjye y'imanza kugira ngo mbaha ingororano zikwiranye n'imirimo yanyu. Kandi ku bizerwa hari ingororano ikomeye n'amakamba menshi, kandi muzambikwa inzu nabakoreye, ndetse inzu izaba ikwiranye namwe gusa kandi izaba irimo ibyo mwagezeho byinshi, nzahaho ubushobozi bwinshi, kuko ari ko umubiri mushya mbabikiye umeze. Nta ndwara, nta bubabare, nta gahinda, kandi nta muvumo ukundi, bana banjye, kuko umuvumo uzahindukirira ku mitwe y'abavumwe. Kandi uko ni ko muzaba mu ijuru muri icyo gihe cy'imyaka irindwi, ndetse n'icyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli. Kandi hanyuma, muzagendana nanjye nk'uko ingabo zose zo mu ijuru zizabikora, kuko byahanuwe kandi ni ko bizaba: Kandi mbona ijuru rikinguye, mbona ifarashi y'umweru, kandi uyihetsaho yitwa Uwo kwizerwa n'Ukwuri, kandi aca imanza zitabera akarwana intambara gukiranuka. Amaso ye yari ameze nk'ibirimi by'umuriro, kandi ku mutwe we hari amakamba menshi, kandi yari afite izina ryanditswe, nta muntu numwe urizi keretse we ubwe. Kandi yari yambaye umwenda wibitswe mu maraso, kandi izina rye yitwa, Ijambo ry'Imana. Kandi ingabo zo mu ijuru zamukurikiye ku mafarashi y'umweru, zambaye imyenda y'igitare myiza, yera kandi ikeye.

Bana banjye, ibyanyu ni ejo hazaza h'ubwiza butagereranywa, kandi ntimuzongera gukena ukundi, kuko mwiyeretse kuri njye, kandi ntimukeneye irindi geragezwa; none se kuki mwakenera kwihanganira amakuba ya Yakobo? Kuko intego yayo ari ugukiza Yakobo, kuko abasigaye bazakizwa. Ariko mwizere mudashidikanya, abahakana kuboneka kwanjye mu mbaraga zabo z'ubusa zo gusenya ibyiringiro byanyu by'ukuri, ndetse n'abo bungeri bayobye, bazihanganira amakuba ya Yakobo, kandi bazakizwa nibambaza izina ryanjye kandi bakaba abizerwa kugeza ku iherezo. Ariko ntibazagira umwanya ku meza yanjye, kuko guhakana kuboneka kwanjye ni ukumpakana, njye Mukwe, ubwo nzaza gutwara Umugeni wanjye, kandi ngiye gukora ibyo ngibyo.

Mushake ubwiza bw'ukuboneka kwanjye! Mwumve, kuko muzumva ijwi ryanjye n'ijwi rirenga ry'impanda y'imperuka; kuko nubwo ndi Umwana w'Intama w'Imana, ndaje nk'Intare y'i Yuda, kandi ubwami bwose bwa Satani bwuzuye ubwoba bwanjye, Kristo Yesu, kuko ibihe byabo byageze, kandi ibirimi by'umuriro byabacaniriwe bishyushye. Ariko ntibizabegera, kuko nabateguriye ingororano ikomeye n'umurage ukomeye, Mugeni wanjye. Musome amagambo yanjye mu gitabo gikomeye cy'Ibyahishuwe, kuko nzabereka ishusho y'ejo hazaza hanyu: Kandi iruhande rw'Intebe y'Ubwami hari intebe makumyabiri n'enye, kandi kuri izo ntebe mbona Abakuru makumyabiri na bane bicaye, bambaye imyenda yera, kandi bari bambaye amakamba ya zahabu ku mitwe yabo. (kandi nanone) Kandi ibyo bizima nibiha ubwiza, n'icyubahiro, no gushimira uwicaye ku Ntebe y'Ubwami, uhoraho iteka ryose, Abakuru makumyabiri na bane bikubita imbere y'uwicaye ku Ntebe y'Ubwami, bakaramya uhoraho iteka ryose, bakajugunya amakamba yabo imbere y'Intebe y'Ubwami, bavuga bati, Ukwiriye, Mwami, guhabwa ubwiza, n'icyubahiro, n'imbaraga: kuko ari wowe waremye byose, kandi ku bw'ubushake bwawe biriho, kandi byaremwe.

Bana banjye, ntimutinye ejo hazaza hanyu, ahubwo mundebere mu byiringiro bikomeye, kuko vuba cyane muzaba muri mu ijuru mujugunya amakamba yanyu imbere y'intebe y'Imana isumba byose!

Ndi umucamanza utabera kandi vuba aha muzahabwa ingororano ikomeye ku ntebe yanjye y'imanza.

Ndi Umukwe w'umugeni wanjye.

Ndaje gutwara Umugeni wanjye!

Ndi Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda kandi isi igiye kubisobanukirwa.

Ndi uhawe ubukuru mu ijuru no mu isi, kuko izina ryanjye riruta amazina yose keretse Yehova.

Ndi Umwana w'Imana waje mu mubiri ku bw'ibyaha by'abantu, kandi nazutse ku munsi wa gatatu nzamurwa mu ijuru.

Ndi uzategeka mu gukiranuka mu isi, kandi Umugeni wanjye azategekana nanjye.

Ndi uwabahamagariye ubwiza n'ingeso nziza, bityo ntimucike intege!

Ndi we.
