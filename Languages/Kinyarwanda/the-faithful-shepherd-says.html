Umwungeri Wizerwa Aravuga Ati

Umwungeri wizerwa abwira abizerwa ati: Ndababwira ukuri rwose nti, nimuve mu gukiranirwa kandi ntimugakore icyaha. Kuko ingororano yanjye iri kumwe nanjye, kugira ngo ngororere umuntu wese ibikwiriye ibyo yakoze. Nabahamagaye mu rukundo rwa Data kugira ngo mugire ubugingo, kandi mubugire buhoraho. Nabahamagariye kugendera mu Bwami bwanjye, kunkorera no gutura mu isi mu gukiranuka. Nabahaye ubuntu bwanjye kugira ngo mukurire muri jye, Umwami wanyu Yesu Kristo. Ntimugacike intege mu bihano byanjye, kuko abo nkunda bose mbacyaha, kugira ngo ubuntu bwanjye bukiza bushinge imizi mu mbitso z'ubugingo bwanyu, kugira ngo mukore imirimo ikomeye, imirimo y'imigisha, imirimo yo gukiza, kandi kugira ngo mwera imbuto nyinshi muri jye, bantu banjye.

Kuko Data ari Imana y'urukundo, kandi urukundo rwa Data ntirushobora gutsindwa. Muri jye, Umwami ukiranuka, ni ho hari ukuzura kose Data yampaye. Yashyize byose mu maboko yanjye, kandi nk'uko Farawo yashyize hejuru Yozefu akamuha Misiri yose mu maboko ye, ni ko Data yanshyize hejuru akampa byose mu ijuru no mu isi mu maboko yanjye. Yampaye izina riruta amazina yose, kugira ngo mu izina rya Yesu amavi yose apfukame, mu ijuru no mu isi, kandi indimi zose ziature ko ndi Umwami, mu ijuru no mu isi.

Nabahaye ubugingo buhoraho, kandi nabaciriye inzira yo kurokoka ubutayu no kurokoka uwo munsi mukuru w'umujinya wegereje, nuko rero ntimugacike intege! Ntimukarambirwe gukora neza, ahubwo muhagarare imbere yanjye, nanjye nzakomeza kubiyegereza mu kubaho kwanjye. Mwicaye mu by'ijuru kandi muzagira uruhare ku ntebe yanjye y'ubwami nk'uko nanjye mfite uruhare ku ntebe y'ubwami ya Data. Mfite amakamba menshi n'imigisha myinshi mbabikiye, bantu banjye bizerwa. Muhagarare imbere yanjye kandi mube intungane muri jye. Nimuve mu byaha byanyu kandi mureke ibihe byo guhembuka bihuhe umurama mu buzima bwanyu. Muri jye ni ho hari ubugingo buhoraho, kandi muri jye ni ho hari icyaremwe gishya, umuntu mushya, ndetse na Kristo muri mwe, ibyiringiro by'ubwiza! Nimuve mu bibi kandi muve mu cyaha. Mukomeze kumpamya mu bapagani, kuko hakiri abantu bo gukizwa muri iyi saha ya nyuma. Mukomeze kwamamaza izina ryanjye, kuko nta rindi zina munsi y'ijuru abantu bakirizwamo. Muhumurizwe n'ikiruhuko nabahaye, kandi muture imitwaro yanyu ku birenge byanjye; kuko umutwaro wanjye utaruhije, n'umugogo wanjye woroshye.

Ku bizerwa (abanesheje icyaha n'amakuba binyuze mu maraso yanjye y'impongano) ku bizerwa, ndavuga nti: Mfite ingororano ikomeye mbabikiye mu nzu ya Data. Ibyo ijisho ritigeze kubona, n'ibyo ugutwi kutigeze kumva, ndetse n'ibitigeze kwinjira mu mitima y'abantu ni byo byabateguriwe. Nzabahemba bikomeye amakamba menshi, nuko rero mumhagararire kandi mube abizerwa ku ijwi ryanjye n'amagambo yanjye; kuko muri ayo magambo muzabonamo ubugingo. Mbese sindi Jambo wabaye umubiri akabana namwe?

Ndi umucamanza ukiranuka.

Ndi umwungeri mwiza wungera umukumbi.

Ndi gukira kwanyu, kandi nzabakiza rwose.

Navetswe n'umugore mba umubiri, nubwo nta cyaha kimbarwaho.

Nabambwe ku musaraba ku bw'ibyaha byanyu, kandi mu by'ukuri, nta muntu wanyatse ubugingo bwanjye, ahubwo nabutanze ku bushake ku bw'urukundo nkunda abahungu n'abakobwa ba Data.

Nazuwe mu bapfuye ku munsi wa gatatu, kuko bitari gushoboka ko urupfu rumfata. Data yamennye iminyururu y'urupfu n'imbaraga zo kuzuka kwanjye.

Ndi umuganura w'abapfuye.

Vuba aha muzansanganira mu kirere, kandi ububabare bw'urupfu buzarekura abasinziriye, maze namwe abazaba bakiriho basigaye muzazamuranwa na bo, kandi muzabana nanjye iteka ryose. Ndi Umwana w'Imana, kandi mu kuboko kwanjye harimo ingororano y'abizerwa banjye. Mukomere, bana banjye, kandi ntimugacike intege. Mukomerere mu Mwami no mu mbaraga z'ubushobozi bwanjye, kuko Data wenyine ari we unduta. Numvise amasengesho yanyu, kandi nabakiriye nk'abanjye.

Mwihangane kandi mukomeze kunkorera. Kuko vuba aha muzaba muri mu rugo hamwe nanjye, kandi iteka ryose tuzaba dukora imirimo ya Data. Mukomere kandi mugire ihumure. Ndabizi ko benshi muri mwe mubona amakuba n'ibyago byegereza, kandi benshi muri mwe mwabonye ingabo z'umugome zambuka imipaka yanyu. Ntimubatinye! Ntimubatinye, nubwo babarimbura iyo biza gushoboka, ariko ntibazanywe n'abizerwa banjye, kuko kurokoka gukomeye no gukorakoranya gukomeye kuzakiza abizerwa banjye isaha y'igeragezwa n'ibishuko igiye kuza ku isi yose. Muruhukire mu rukundo rwanjye, mumenye ko ntazigera mbareka cyangwa ngo mbatatererane, nk'uko Data atigeze andeka cyangwa ngo antererane mu isaha yanjye y'igitambo. Kuko agakiza kanyu gafite umutekano muri jye; nuko rero, munyegereze, kuko nzabakomeza kurushaho uko nabikoze. Ibanga ry'ubumana ni rikuru!
