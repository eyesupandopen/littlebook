Ku Bizerwa muri Kristo: Mwitegure Kandi Mube Maso Kugira ngo Mukoranywe, Umukwe Wanyu Yegereje!

Nimwumve, nimwumve Ijambo rya Yeshua HaMashiach kandi mwunamishe imitima yanyu mu kumvira ijwi ry'Amagambo yanjye! Kandi niko bimeze, ko benshi mu bantu banjye bateshutswe bagana iburyo n'abandi benshi bagana ibumoso, ndetse bava kuri Kristo Yesu Umwami wabo, nubwo nje kubakoranyiriza aho ndi niba mukomeza kuba abizerwa ku ijwi ryanjye mu kumvira Amagambo yanjye kose. Kuko, ni ukuri, ni ukuri, ndi Umwami wanyu w'ukuri, Umwami, Umutambyi Mukuru, na Mesiya! Yego, ndabahamagara mwese mu maboko yanjye yuje urukundo kugira ngo mukoranyirizwe aho ndi mu buryo butunguranye kandi butitezwe ubu, ndetse n'abategereza bose mu kumvira no kwizera gusesuye, kandi by'umwihariko abaguye bose muri uku kuyoba kwateguwe na Heylel Ben Shachar kugira ngo ayobye imitima y'abantu basanzwe, kandi asenye ibyiringiro byanyu bikiranuka by'incungu ikiranuka kandi yuzuye, ndetse n'incungu yanyu yuzuye izatungura abizerwa muri Kristo, mu buryo butunguranye kandi butitezwe ubu!

Ndetse n'amagambo y'ibinyoma yavuzwe n'abahanuzi b'ibinyoma bashyiraho amatariki, ndetse bavuga ibinyoma mu izina rya Yesu Kristo, ndetse bavuga ko bamvugira! Nyamara ntibavuga Amagambo yanjye, cyangwa ngo bere imbuto zanjye, kandi ntibanzi, kandi nzabwira abavuga ibyo binyoma bose kuri uwo munsi ukomeye nti: “Nimuva imbere yanjye mwa nkozi z'ibibi mwe, sinigeze mbamenya!” Kandi niko bimeze, ko benshi mu bizerwa muri Kristo ari bo torero ryanjye umubiri wanjye, ndetse n'umugeni wanjye ubu yicaye ababaye mu gucika intege, nubwo Umukwe wabo yegereje nk'uko byatangajwe, bikavugwa kandi bigasezeranywa! Nyamara benshi muri mwe ubu mwicaye mu bucece nk'aho ikoraniro rikomeye ry'abizerwa muri Kristo rishobora guhagarikwa, cyangwa rigakurwaho na Heylel Ben Shachar, kuko ni ukuri, ni ukuri, ni we wateganyije mu binyoma igenda ry'abizerwa muri Kristo kandi benshi mu bizerwa banjye bariye amagambo ye y'ibinyoma bafite umururumba nk'aho ayo magambo y'ibinyoma aturuka kuri Kristo Yesu ubwe! Nimwumve Amagambo yanjye torero ryanjye mubiri wanjye ndetse n'umugeni wanjye! Ntimuzi ijwi ryanjye? Ntimumenya Amagambo yanjye? Kuko nta nyuguti n'imwe, cyangwa agace k'inyuguti kazavaho kugeza igihe byose bizasohorezwa NEZA nk'uko byanditswe kandi bigasezeranywa.

Nyamara niko bimeze, ko benshi muri mwe ubu mwicaye mwihebye, kandi mufite imitima imenetse mu kutumvira kwanyu, ndetse ku buryo ubu mwicaye mwihebye mugandukira amagambo y'abakobanyi n'abashinyaguzi, urubanza rwabo ruzabatungura mu buryo butunguranye kandi butitezwe ubu kandi benshi, benshi muri bo bazagwa mu rubanza rw'iteka, kandi ntibazigera bahabwa icyubahiro cyo kwambaza izina rya Yesu kugeza ku mperuka yabo, kuko agakiza binyuze mu guhara amagara ari amahirwe n'icyubahiro gihabwa abaraganwa b'ubugingo buhoraho kandi bw'iteka bazahabwa ingororano ikomeye ku bw'uguhagarara kwabo, kandi nzabakira iwanjye iteka ryose mu rukundo mbakunda, nyamara Umugeni wanjye wizerwa ni we uzashimwa na bose kuri uwo munsi! Ku bw'ibyo, muhagarare mushikamye Mugeni wanjye kandi mwiyunge ku Magambo yanjye, ndetse n'Amagambo y'Ubutumwa Bwiza Buhoraho nahaye isi kugira ngo mbategure bakundwa banjye, ku bw'ibyo muzirike Amagambo y'ubuhamya bw'intumwa yanjye n'umugaragu wanjye cyane ku mitima yanyu, mumenye ko aya Magambo atunganye aturuka imbonankubone kuri Yahweh, mu maso ha Kristo Yesu, kandi ndayarekura mu gusobanukirwa ku itegeko ryanjye uko nyahishurira neza kandi mu buryo bwuzuye mu mutima w'intumwa yanjye, ndetse n'aho nayabibye, ndetse akabikwa kugira ngo arekurwe ku itegeko ryanjye, kandi ni ukuri Amagambo yanjye atangwa mu kuri kose no gukiranuka. Sela.

Ni ukuri, ni ukuri, nk'uko bimeze: Heylel Ben Shachar, mu rwango rwinshi, ashakisha cyane guhirika ukwizera kw'abantu bose ukwizera kwabo gushobora guhirikwa n'amagambo ye y'ibinyoma n'ibishuko bye by'uburiganya, ndetse n'abantu bose ukwizera kwabo kutari gushikamye byuzuye kuri urwo rutare rw'ukuri rw'urufatiro, ari rwo Kristo Yesu, ahubwo bakerekeza amaso yabo mu kutumvira ku magambo y'ibinyoma y'abantu b'abanyabyaha, batamenye Kristo Yesu cyangwa ngo bankorere na gato, ahubwo bakorera Heylel Ben Shachar w'umunyabinyoma. Kandi ku bw'amagambo yabo y'ibinyoma n'imbuto zabo zo gukiranirwa muzabamenya; ariko benshi mu bizerwa banjye baracyafite amatwi yishimira kumva ibinyoma aho mwifuza ko bayabwira amagambo y'ibinyoma kandi ahumuriza mu binyoma, ndetse n'amagambo y'ibinyoma Kristo Yesu atohereje, cyangwa ngo ashyigikire, kandi sinzigera nohereza ijambo na rimwe ry'ikinyoma rivuguruza ibyanditswe byanjye Byera, cyangwa ngo mvuge ijambo na rimwe rinyuranye n'ibyanditswe byashyizweho natanze, kuko ni ukuri, ni ukuri, ndi Ijambo rya YHVH, NDI HO Ukomeye wigize umubiri, kandi nahinduwe umwuka utanga ubugingo, kandi sinzigera nivuguruza na gato, nubwo babeshya. Ku bw'ibyo, ndabategeka mu bubasha bwose kugumisha Amagambo yanjye cyane mu mitima yanyu mu kwiyemeza gushya kugira ngo mwisubiraho ku bw'ubuhemu bwanyu, ndetse kugira ngo mwese mwizeye aya magambo y'ibinyoma mushobore kwihana no kuyanyaturira, kugira ngo mbeze byuzuye mu maraso yanjye y'impongano kandi kugira ngo mwitegure byuzuye ukuboneka kwanjye kubariho mu buryo butunguranye kandi butitezwe ubu, ndetse mu gihe abandi bose bo mu isi basubira inyuma bava kuri Kristo Yesu, wari kubakiza byuzuye. Ndetse n'igihe basubira inyuma mu kwiyemeza kwuzuye ku ishusho y'umuhowe Imana w'ikinyoma waremwe kandi ugatangwa kugira ngo urange abazabona agakiza binyuze mu muriro utunganya, kuko iyi ari yo ntangiriro y'intambara ku bera igiye kurekurwa byuzuye ku bw'agakiza k'abatumvira bakiri mu bubata bw'icyaha, kugira ngo umubiri ushobore kurimbuka, kugira ngo ni ukuri umwuka ushobore gukizwa, Sela.

Ni ukuri ni ukuri, ndi Kristo Yesu, ndetse Yeshua HaMashiach, kandi nje komora ibikomere by'abatumvira mu bizerwa banjye, ndetse n'abantu bose bakwemera gukura imitima yabo ku Magambo yanjye y'Ubutumwa Bwiza Buhoraho, ndetse n'Amagambo yatanzwe n'intumwa zanjye n'abahanuzi banjye mu ntangiriro y'iki gihe cy'ubuntu, ndetse n'Amagambo y'ukuri yahishuye amabanga yanjye mu bihe byayo byo guhishurwa, ndetse n'Amagambo ari ukuri uyu munsi nk'uko yari ari igihe yatangwaga bwa mbere nanjye mu matwi y'abari bahari, ariko by'umwihariko abigishwa banjye intumwa zanjye zakomotsemo: Mwirinde: mube maso, kandi musenge: kuko mutazi igihe icyo gihe kizabera.

Nyamara benshi muri mwe mwemera amagambo y'ibinyoma avuguruza Amagambo yanjye y'ukuri y'isezerano, ndetse n'amagambo yateguriwe guhirika ukwizera kwanyu binyuze mu kubaka ibyiringiro by'ibinyoma ku itariki ntatangaje, ndetse ibyo byiringiro bikaza gusenyerwa nkana kugira ngo murohame mu byiringiro bitasohoye n'abashaka kubahungabanya, ndetse n'abantu bose baza aho muri mu buhemu bukomeye batanga ibinyoma binyuranye n'ibyanditswe, ndetse n'amagambo y'ibinyoma atangwa n'abavuga ibinyuranye n'Amagambo yanjye, ndetse n'abahanura ibinyoma mu izina rya Yesu.

Nyamara, benshi mu itorero ryanjye umubiri wanjye ndetse n'umugeni wanjye bakomeza kwemerera aba bungeri b'ibinyoma guhirika ibyiringiro byabo by'uko Umukwe wabo yegereje ku munsi bo ubwabo bihitiyemo, ndetse n'ubuhanuzi bw'ibinyoma bunyuranye n'ibyamaze gutangazwa na YHVH, NDI HO Ukomeye, ndetse na Kristo Yesu ku itegeko rya Adonai-YHVH kuko mpora nkora ibyo uwantumye ashaka. Kuko ndi Ijambo ryigize umubiri utuye koko muri mwe ndetse no muri mwe binyuze muri uwo mwuka wera w'ubwana, wuzuza koko imuri z'Isi byuzuye zambitswe imbaraga zivuye mu ijuru! Nyamara benshi muri mwe muhitamo kubushake kwicara mu mwijima ndetse mwima amatwi Amagambo nyine yazimya imyambi yaka umuriro y'umubi, iyo benshi muri mwe muhitamo mu bupfu. Ni ukuri, ni ukuri, ndi uwicaye afite umuvuba mu ntoki ndetse atiza umuriro wo kweza, kugira ngo ni ukuri ni ukuri, mwese mushobore kwitegura ukuboneka k'Umwami wanyu, kuko ndaje kubatwara bakundwa banjye, kandi ni inde ushobora kumbuza? Ndabajije, kandi nongeye kubaza, NTI NI INDE!

Nyamara, niko bimeze. Ko Umugeni wanjye ubu yicaye yijimye mu rujijo, ndetse yaheze ku magambo y'abajujura n'abavugira mu matamatama! Kandi nuko ndabategeka kwanga aya magambo y'ibinyoma, ndetse n'abantu bose bashyira ukwizera kwabo n'icyizere cyabo mu bapfumu b'ibinyoma bajujura kandi bavugira mu matamatama kandi bavuga ibinyuranye n'Amagambo yanjye y'ubugingo, kuko navuze nweruye mu byanditswe, kandi ibyanditswe ntibishobora kuvuguruzwa: Ko nta muntu n'umwe ku isi uzi umunsi, igihe, cyangwa isaha y'ikoraniro rikomeye ry'abizerwa kuri Kristo riteganyijwe kuba mu buryo butunguranye kandi butitezwe ubu. Kandi nabategetse mwese kugumisha ibyiringiro by'imitima yanyu n'intekerezo zanyu byerekeye byuzuye ku Mwami wanyu w'Iteka ryose wo Gukiranuka wegereje rwose kubatwara bakundwa banjye, ndetse n'abakobwa banjye bose b'abanyabwenge batunganyije intambi zabo, kandi bakuzuza amatara yabo amavuta meza y'umwuka wanjye, kugira ngo ni ukuri ni ukuri mushobore kwitegura ukuboneka k'Umukwe wanyu kandi kugira ngo mushobore kuba mwiteguye byuzuye kandi mutegereje mwiteguye hamwe n'abashyitsi bacu. Ndetse n'abahamagawe bose, abatoranyijwe, n'abatumiwe muri ubwo bukwe bukomeye bw'Umukwe n'umugeni we, ndetse uwo munsi nyine ubagwiriye mwese mu buryo butunguranye kandi butitezwe ubu.

None se kuki Umugeni wanjye yicaye yijimye mu gahinda nk'aho Umukwe wanyu yabatengushye akabasiga muhagaze ku ruhande rw'inzira mwanzwe, mwatengushywe kandi mufite imitima imenetse mu byiringiro byanyu? Oya, bizerwa banjye ibi bintu si ibyanyu, ahubwo bizatangwa mu rubanza ku bahemu, n'abantu bose banga ubutumire bw'Umwami wabo w'Iteka ryose wo Gukiranuka bwo kwitegura hakurikijwe amategeko yanjye, mu kumvira kwuje urukundo ku ijwi n'amabwiriza by'Umwami wabo!

Ku bw'ibyo, nje kubibutsa nanone aya Magambo yatanzwe mu bihe bya kera ku Bana b'Isezerano, ndetse Isirayeli hakurikijwe umubiri mu bihe byo guhishurwa kwabo, ndetse n'Amagambo ahagaze kugeza uyu munsi bakundwa banjye, ku bw'ibyo, nimwumve Amagambo y'Umwami wanyu kandi mwakire amabwiriza yanjye mu bugwaneza bwose, kwicisha bugufi, koroshya umutima, no kwitegura kw'intekerezo. Nimwumve Kandi Mwumvire: Kandi niba mutekereza mu mitima yanyu muti, Tuzamenya dute ijambo Uwiteka atavuze? Iyo Umuhanuzi avuze mu Izina ry'Uwiteka, niba icyo kintu kidakurikiyeho cyangwa ngo gisohore, icyo ni cyo kintu Uwiteka atavuze, ahubwo Umuhanuzi yakivuze yishyize imbere: ntimuzamutinye rero.

Nyamara, benshi muri mwe bibagiwe aya Magambo yatanzwe nk'imbibi n'uburinzi ku birura by'inkazi, urubanza rwabyo rwose rutazatinda. Ku bw'ibyo, mugereranye mubyitondeye amagambo y'umuntu wese uvuga ko avugira Kristo Yesu, ari we kandi Yeshua HaMashiach mubyitondeye n'Amagambo yanjye yashyizweho kandi arinzwe mu byanditswe byera, ndetse n'Amagambo yera yatanzwe mu bihe bya kera na Adonai-YHVH, NDI HO Ukomeye, ndetse n'Amagambo yera yatanzwe mu bihe by'ubu na Adonai-YHVH na Kristo Yesu, ari we kandi Yeshua HaMashiach.

Ku bw'ibyo, Bakundwa banjye, nimwumve Amagambo y'Umwami wanyu w'iteka ryose wo Gukiranuka, ndetse n'Amagambo yatanzwe n'amaboko yanjye uko nyahishura mu mutima w'intumwa yanjye y'imbaraga n'umugaragu wanjye kandi ku bw'amaboko yanjye n'ubushake bwanjye aya Magambo arahishurwa mu gusobanukirwa kuva mu mutima w'intumwa yanjye y'imbaraga n'umugaragu wanjye aho njye, Kristo Yesu ntanga aya Magambo yo guhumuriza, ndetse n'Amagambo aturisha kugira ngo ahumurize abizerwa muri Kristo, ndetse n'abakobwa banjye b'abanyabwenge biteguye binyuze muri aya Magambo yatanzwe kugira ngo arekurwe mu isi ku bw'abizerwa muri Kristo mu kumvira kose ndetse kugira ngo mwese mube mwiteguye kandi mutegereje ku ruhande rw'inzira amatara yanyu yuzuye, n'intambi zanyu zitunganijwe, ndetse namwe mwese mwafashe umuriro wanjye mugacana amatara yanyu mu kumvira gukiranuka ku Magambo yanjye, kuko ndabegereye bakundwa banjye.

Kandi niko bimeze, Umukwe aregereye Umugeni we kandi isi mu buryo butunguranye ubu izatwikirwa n'umwijima w'icuraburindi, kuko ntazagabana icyubahiro cyanjye n'undi, kandi uko umwijima w'icuraburindi utwikira isi, icyubahiro n'umucyo by'ukuboneka kwanjye bizamurikira isi ndetse nk'uko umurabyo uturuka iburasirazuba ukagera iburengerazuba, kandi mu buryo butunguranye kandi butitezwe ubu Itorero ryanjye, Umubiri wanjye, Ndetse n'Umugeni wanjye bazisanga bahagaze mu ijuru ry'amajuru, ndetse bahagaze mu cyumba cy'intebe y'ubwami cya YHVH, NDI HO Ukomeye, ndetse n'akanya Adonal-YHVH yategereje kuva mu bihe by'iteka byashize, ndetse n'igihe cya kera cyane ku buryo mudashobora kubyumva muri iki gihe. Kandi mwese mwahishwe mu mbaraga ze n'umutima we utangaje w'urukundo n'umucyo, ndetse n'aho yabarindiye mu ibanga rye rikomeye kuruta ayandi yose ndetse Ibanga Rikomeye rya Yahweh, NDI HO Ukomeye, kandi ni ukuri ni ukuri amajuru ariteguye kandi yamaze kwishimira kuhagera kwanyu gutunguranye ubu, ndetse na mbere y'uko muhagera. Kuko ndetse n'abamarayika bera n'ibiremwa bitangaje byo mu majuru bitegerezanyije amatsiko ukuboneka kwanyu no kuhaba kwanyu bakundwa banjye.

Ku bw'ibyo, muruhutse intekerezo n'imitima yanyu byuzuye mu rukundo mbakunda bana banjye, kandi ntimwemerere amagambo y'ibinyoma yatanzwe n'abahanuzi b'ibinyoma bajujura kandi bavugira mu matamatama kandi bashyiraho amatariki y'ubukwe bwanjye bw'i Galilaya guhirika ukwizera kwanyu, cyangwa ngo abababaze mu mutima nk'aho nzatenguha Umugeni wanjye mwiza witeguye mu kumvira kwuje urukundo ku ijwi ry'Umukwe we. Kandi nuko ndabategeka bakundwa banjye kwerekeza umutima wanyu wose, ubugingo, intekerezo, n'imbaraga ku Magambo y'Umwami wanyu w'Iteka ryose wo Gukiranuka, ndetse n'Amagambo yanjye yera y'ibyanditswe nahishuye muri cya gitabo gito gifunguye nafunguye mu maboko y'intumwa yanjye n'umugaragu wanjye, kuko igitabo gito cy'ibyahishuwe igice cya 10 gikubiyemo amajwi y'inkuba zirindwi ndetse n'ubutumwa bwiza buhoraho igice kimwe, kandi aya ni Amagambo yatanzwe kugira ngo ayobore abantu banjye mu bihe by'isarura by'amakuba ya Yakobo ndetse n'icyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli. Ndetse n'Amagambo yafatanishijwe ikimenyetso kandi akagirwa ibanga ku itegeko rya YHVH, NDI HO Ukomeye, kuko ni ukuri, ni ukuri, amajwi n'ijwi by'inkuba zirindwi biravuga, kandi izi nkuba zirindwi ni itangira ry'igitabo cy'ibyahishuwe, ndetse n'icyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli kizasiga irimbuka ryuzuye kandi rya burundu ry'ubwami bwa Heylel Ben Shachar iteka ryose, kandi isi izategekwa mu Gukiranuka, ndetse no gukiranuka aho nta kintu kibi kizinjira mu bwami bwanjye bw'imyaka igihumbi, bityo muruhukire mu byiringiro bikomeye muri aya Magambo y'ibyiringiro n'isezerano ry'iteka bakundwa banjye.

Ndi Kristo Yesu, kandi nje kuzana aya Magambo y'igihano, gucyaha, n'urukundo ku bumvira, n'abatumvira kugira ngo ni ukuri, ni ukuri, ntimugwe ku ruhande rw'abatumvira rw'inkota yanjye y'umwuka, ndetse kugira ngo muzabarwe ko mukwiriye gukoranywa n'abizerwa mu buryo butunguranye kandi butitezwe ubu. Kuko ndetse no muri iyi minota ya nyuma mbere y'uko urwo rusaku rukomeye rwumvikana, kandi mbere y'uko iryo jwi rikomeye ry'impanda rivuga mu mbaraga kandi mu buryo butunguranye ubu, ndacyatigisa kandi ngateza gutigisa gukomeye ku bantu banjye, ndetse kugira ngo nshobore gutigisa abahemu kugira ngo bashobore kuba abizerwa mu kumvira gukiranuka, cyangwa bagwe mu muriro utunganya mu kutumvira kwabo, ndetse mbere gato y'uko ako kanya ka kairos gahura n'igihe gisanzwe kigiye kubagwira mwese mu buryo butunguranye kandi butitezwe ubu. Ndetse n'igihe isi iririra umukiza wayo w'ikinyoma, ndetse n'umuhowe Imana w'ikinyoma ntatohereje, ahubwo watanzwe na Baraba mu cyimbo cye, nyamara benshi ubu bakurikira uyu muhowe Imana w'ikinyoma, ndetse bayobejwe bava ku ijwi rya Kristo Yesu ari we kandi Yeshua HaMashiach, Sela.

Kandi niko bimeze, ko ntegura abazasigirwa umuriro utunganya byuzuye, ndetse n'abantu bose bazahabwa amahirwe yo kwambaza izina rya Yesu Kristo kugeza ku mperuka yabo, kuko abantu bose badakunda amagara yabo kugeza ku gupfa muri ubwo buryo bazabona agakiza k'iteka ni ukuri ni ukuri, kadashobora kugereranywa n'imibabaro bazihanganira ku bw'izina ryanjye.

Nimwitegure Itorero ryanjye Umubiri wanjye Ndetse n'Umugeni wanjye, mwitegure kandi mube maso hakurikijwe Amagambo yanjye yera n'ibyanditswe by'Ubutumwa Bwiza Buhoraho kandi muntagereze bakundwa banjye kuko ndaje kubatwara kandi ntimuzatenguhwa mu byiringiro byanyu. Ku bw'ibyo, mwakire gucyaha kwanjye n'igihano cyanjye kandi mwumvire Amagambo yanjye n'imiburo yanjye kuko ndaje kubatwara Bakundwa banjye, kandi sinzigera nsiga abizerwa muri Kristo mu muriro utunganya, cyangwa mu rubanza rw'abanyabyaha rutari urwanyu.

Nimwitegure Bakundwa banjye, mu kumvira kose n'ibyiringiro, kandi mwibike mu Magambo y'Ubutumwa Bwiza Buhoraho, kandi muyagumishe mu ntekerezo zanyu n'imitima yanyu kandi musobanukirwe ubutunzi bw'ibyanditswe nahaye isi kugira ngo mbakomeze muri ibi bihe byo guhishurwa, ndetse n'ihishurwa natangiranye n'impanda nyinshi zateguwe mu mukumbi wanjye muto zavuze mu budahemuka, ndetse izo mpanda zateguye inzira y'intumwa yanjye n'umugaragu wanjye uri igikoresho cyatoranyijwe ku Mwami we w'Iteka ryose, ndetse Kristo Yesu, ari we kandi Yeshua HaMashiach, ndetse n'aho mpishura Amagambo yanjye mu gikoresho cyanjye cyashyizwe ukwacyo cyemeranya kandi cyiyemeje byuzuye imigambi nagihaye, kandi ni ku itegeko ryanjye n'imbaraga zanjye intoki ze zibona imfunguzo ndetse zo guhishura aya Magambo yera nashyize muri we kugira ngo ayarekure mu isi bwa mbere, ndetse n'Amagambo yatunganirijwe kubona amaso, amatwi, n'imitima y'abantu bose yohererejwe, Sela.

Ku bw'ibyo, nimwumve ijwi ryanjye muri iri tangazo ryera ritanzwe kugira ngo rikururire abizerwa ku itegeko ryanjye, kandi ribatunganye mwese mu kumvira ijwi ry'iyo mpanda, kugira ngo mushobore gukoranyirizwa aho ndi ku itegeko ryanjye.

Dore! Nshyize umukono kuri iri tangazo n'ibaruwa n'ukuboko kw'intumwa yanjye n'umugaragu wanjye, nyamara ishyizweho umukono mu izina ryanjye bwite mu rukundo rwose n'ububasha,

Yeshua HaMashiach, Umwami w'Iteka ryose wo Gukiranuka.
