Ubuhamya Bubiri: Isirayeli
Ibisharira N'ibiryohereye
10 Ugushyingo 2024

Ikoraniro ry'Abizerwa muri Kristo!

Dore, Dore! Nimwumve ibigiye gusukwa ku isi.

Dore, Dore! Nta gushidikanya umujinya w'umwana w'intama ukuriho wowe Babuloni y'ibanga. Kandi nk'uko njyewe, Yeshua HaMashiach nabiburije, niko bizakorwa!

Dore! Dore! Umunsi ukugezeho yewe Babuloni y'Ibanga kandi umunsi wawe w'urubanza urageze, ndetse n'umunsi uzarekura wa munsi ukomeye wasezeranyijwe kera ari wo munsi w'Uwiteka! Abatuye isi mwebweho ishyano, kuko umujinya ukomeye ugiye kubasukwaho wese, kandi ntimuzarokoka kandi koko, icyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli kirageze.

Nuko rero ngira inama itorero ryanjye, umubiri wanjye ndetse n'umugeni wanjye kwambara gukiranuka kwanjye no kwitegura guhagarara bemye imbere y'Umwami wanyu, ndetse no mu cyumba cy'intebe y'ubwami cya Yahweh, aho nzerekana umuganura ndetse n'abo nzateranya bose basinziriye muri Kristo kandi baruhutse bategereje guhamagara kwanjye, ndetse n'abazima bose bari ku isi bari muri Kristo mu budahemuka mu gihe cyo kuboneka kwanjye. Kandi nk'uko niyerekanye imbere ya Yahweh nk'umuganura w'abazutse, niko namwe mumenyekana hamwe n'Umwami wanyu w'Iteka ryose wo Gukiranuka. Kuko nk'uko najyanywe mu ijuru, niko namwe muzajyanwa mu ijuru, aho nzerekana umuganura wanjye w'isarura udafite ikizinga cyangwa umunkanyari cyangwa ikindi nk'icyo.

Kandi mwese muzahagarara imbere y'intebe yanjye y'urubanza, ndetse n'iyo ntebe ikomeye y'urubanza aho imirimo yanyu izacirwa urubanza kandi bamwe bazagororerwa, abandi bazahomba kandi bamwe bazahura n'igihombo n'ingororano kuko ibiti, ibyatsi, n'ibikenyeri bizatwikwa kuko nta n'umwe muri mwe wagendeye imbere yanjye atunganye nyamara mu rukundo rwa Yahweh muzatangazwa cyane n'ubunini bw'umurage wanyu. Kandi ku baneshi banjye ingororano yanyu ni ikomeye kandi muzaragwa byose nk'uko nabikoze ubwo naneshaga abaguye. Icyakora, abateranirijwe ku Mwami wabo w'Iteka ryose, ndetse na Yeshua HaMashiach bazaza mu bugingo bushya kuko baremwe bundi bushya iteka ryose kandi ntimuzigera mwangirika cyangwa ngo muhinduke mu butungane bubategereje, kuko Yahweh ari ko yabivuze!

Kandi mu buryo butunguranye muzatwarwa, kandi isi izamenya ko nabonetse, kandi ubuza azakurwaho! Kandi mu gihe ubuza wanjye azaba adahari imbaraga zose n'ingaruka z'ikibi bizarekurwa byuzuye ku isi. Kandi ni njyewe, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndetse na Kristo Yesu, utangaza aya Magambo bityo aya Magambo azamenyekana ku isi, kandi azabona amaso, amatwi, n'imitima y'abo yohererejwe.

Kandi niko bimeze, kandi niko bizaba, ariko mu gihe gito cyane ubwami bw'Ikigongwe buzazamuka byuzuye, kuko niko bahawe kugira ngo babisohoze. Kuko ubwo nangwaga na mwene wacu, ndetse na Isirayeli uwo ndi umucunguzi wa mwene wabo, nahindukiye mu mahanga kugira ngo amahanga ahabwe umugisha, nk'uko Yahweh yasezeranyije kera. Kandi Amagambo yanjye yakiriwe n'imbaraga, ndetse no mu itotezwa rikomeye n'ibigeragezo byinshi, kandi amarembo y'ikuzimu ntiyashoboye kunesha Itorero ryanjye. Nyamara, nubwo Itorero ryanjye rikuweho, kandi ubuza wanjye agakuweho ikibi kizigarurira isi byuzuye, kandi ntibazarokoka.

Ndetse n'abishimira gukiranirwa bose kandi biteguye kwanga Yesu w'i Nazareti bagahitamo Baraba kandi ubwo bazavuga bati amahoro n'umutekano, nibwo kurimbuka kuzabatungura, kandi ntibazarokoka. Nyamara Ikigongwe kiziyerekana, kandi gihambiriwe ku isi kandi cyirukanwe mu ijuru iteka ryose ntikizigera kigaruka, kandi gifite umujinya mwinshi kuko kizi ko igihe cyacyo ari kigufi, nyamara gitekereza mu bujiji ko kigishobora kunesha njyewe Yeshua HaMashiach, nubwo nacyibasiye nkanagitsinda burundu i Kaluvari mu mirimo yanjye y'impongano y'agakiza yarangiye na Birarangiye!

Kandi abaguye batekereza ko bashobora gucika urubanza rwabo rwasezeranyijwe kera kuko batinya ejo habo hazaza kuruta ikindi cyose; kuko bumva ko mu bugome bwabo bagaragaje kandi bagaragaza kutagira imbabazi. Kuko bazi ko batagaragaje imbabazi, bityo, nta mbabazi bazagaragarizwa. Bazi neza ko ibikorwa byabo byose byo gukiranirwa n'ibizira bizahabwa iteka ryose inshuro nyinshi muri rwa nyanja nini y'amazuku yaka umuriro aho ibirimi by'igihano bitazigera bizima. Ndetse n'ibirimi byaremwe bizagororera buri murimo mubi bakoze mu bituza byabo, kandi bazishyura byuzuye kandi bazahora mu bubabare bukabije iteka ryose.

Kuko bagabye igitero kuri Yahweh kandi bagatera ubusitani bw'Imana, ndetse nk'uko biyise imana kandi urubanza rwabo rurageze. Nyamara niko bimeze, ko abaguye basinzirije imbaga y'abantu n'ikoranabuhanga ryabo, n'imirongo yabo, ndetse n'ibinezeza by'icyaha, n'ibihembo byo gukiranirwa. Nyamara, niko bimeze ko abantu bakunze umwijima kuruta umucyo, bityo noneho, nzabaha umwijima bifuza cyane.

Kuko ibinezeza byose bigiye gukurwa ku isi kandi ubuzima bw'icyaha n'uburumbuke bukabije ntibuzongera kuboneka ku isi ukundi mu buryo nk'ubwo aho abantu birundaniriza ubutunzi bakirengagiza bagenzi babo mu mururumba wabo no gukiranirwa, ndetse basuzugura abakeneye ubufasha mu gihe babakandagira mu nzira yabo yo kwirundaniriza ubutunzi bwinshi. Umuntu wa kamere yibereye mu binezeza by'icyaha ku buryo atazigera anazamura urutoki ngo afashe abakeneye ubufasha, bityo nanjye sinzazamura urutoki ngo mufashe gucika urubanza rwe kuko umuntu wa kamere ubu ahawe mu maboko y'abanzi be. Kandi niko bizaba ko ibi bintu bitazarokoka icyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli kandi ntibizongera kubaho ukundi mu buryo nk'ubwo mu bwami bwanjye bwo gukiranuka!

Nyamara niko bizaba ko ubwo ubuza azakurwaho ikibi cyihishe mu muntu kubera amayeri y'inzoka ubu kizagenda kidakumiriwe kandi ubusazi buzigarurira isi kuko umuvandimwe azica umuvandimwe we kandi abenegihugu bazarwana n'abenegihugu babo kandi akavuyo gakabije n'intambara bizigarurira isi. Nyamara iki gikorwa ni ukurimbuka n'urubanza rwa Babuloni y'ibanga, kandi ibimenyetso byiteguye gufungurwa, kandi ugenderera ku ifarashi azagendera!

Kandi bityo kugwa gukomeye kurimo kuba nk'uko byahanuwe. Kuko mu gihe cyo kuboneka kwanjye, ubwo benshi bagombaga kuba bategereje kandi biyeza mu Ijambo ryanjye n'umwuka, kandi aho gusohoza agakiza kabo mu maboko yanjye yuje urukundo, ahubwo bataye Umwami wabo, ndetse na Kristo Yesu, bagahitamo Baraba uwo bahaye imitima yabo. Kandi nk'uko Ponsiyo Pilato, wari wiyemeje kundekura mu binyoma byabo by'abafarisayo bidafite ishingiro n'ibirego, yahaye abantu bo mu gihugu amahitamo. Nuko Pilato arababwira ubwo bari bateraniye hamwe, mbese murashaka ko mbabohurira Baraba, cyangwa Yesu witwa Kristo? Nuko baravuga bati, Baraba. Pilato arababwira ati, none se nze kugenza nte Yesu, witwa Kristo? Bose baramubwira bati, nabambwe.

Kandi ibi byabaye bityo kuko Esawu yakoresheje ingaruka ze mbi ku bantu kugira ngo bihitiremo umwicanyi aho guhitamo uwatumwe ngo abakize, ndetse na Yesu w'i Nazareti! Nyamara se igihano cy'amategeko ya Mose cyari gusohozwa gite uretse binyuze mu gitambo cyanjye ubwanjye mu bubabare bukabije nk'umwana w'intama w'Imana, ndetse n'aho nitanze ku bw'ibyaha by'isi nk'umutambyi mukuru iteka ryose mu buryo bwa Melikisedeki. Nyamara, mu kumenya mbere kwa Yahweh yari azi neza ko Isirayeli izanga Mesiya wabo w'ukuri bityo akoresha urwango n'ishyari by'abaguye abibahinduraho, kuko iyo baza kumenya ibanga rikomeye ry'Imana ntibari kuzigera babamba Umwami wanyu w'Iteka ryose w'Icyubahiro!

Nyamara niko bimeze, ko Melikisedeki Umwami w'i Salemu yazanye umugati na divayi: kandi yari Umutambyi w'Imana isumbaho byose. Kandi bityo Aburamu yatanze amaturo ye nk'uko byasabwaga kugira ngo yubahe Yahweh kandi bityo Melikisedeki ni gahunda yo kwizera yari igicucu cy'ibizaza. Kuko nk'uko byanditswe kuri Aburahamu wizerwa wabayeho mu kwizera Imana Isumbaho Byose kandi binyuze mu rugero rwe abakiranutsi bazabeshwaho no kwizera iteka ryose! Kandi bityo abanyamahanga bahawe umugisha kandi bashyirwa mu miryango mu Isirayeli y'Imana, kandi basangira ku masezerano yahawe ba Data mu buryo bwuzuye kuko barerwe na Yahweh ubwe ndetse n'ukuboko kwanjye gukomeye, bityo icyo Imana yejeje ntukacyite igisanzwe cyangwa igihumanye, Yewe Isirayeli!

Nyamara niko bimeze ko umunsi wanjye ugeze. Kandi sinzasangira icyubahiro cyanjye n'undi, kandi Imana yoherereje ubuyobe bukomeye kugira ngo bizere ikinyoma. Kuko abakurikira Baraba babikora kubera irari ryabo kuko bibwira ko bari mu gihe cye cya zahabu yasezeranyije mu by'ukuri kitagenewe bo, nubwo abeshya. Kuko igihe cya zahabu asezeranya gishingiye ku gihe cya zahabu cy'abaguye mu minsi ya Nowa aho abaguye bigaruriye isi mu gukiranirwa! Bityo, iyo Baraba avuze iby'igihe cya zahabu avuga ukuri ku ntego y'imitima yabo, uretse ko bashaka byuzuye kurimbuka kwuzuye kwa Adamu kugira ngo ibizira byabo n'urumamfu byigarurire ubusitani bw'Imana iteka ryose. Kandi niko bimeze, ubu murumva impamvu nta mubiri wari kurokoka ari muzima iyo Yahweh adagabanya iminsi.

Nyamara nta gihe cya zahabu bazagira kuko ndi urubyaro rwasezeranyijwe rw'umugore ruzamenagura burundu umutwe w'inzoka iteka ryose kandi nzamenagura burundu ubwami bwe bwose kandi ntibazahagarara. Bityo mu mwanya w'igihe cyabo cya zahabu bifuza, ahubwo bazagira urubanza rukomeye kandi ubwami bwabo buzarimburwa burundu imbere yabo ntibuzongera kuzamuka ukundi, kandi ibimenyetso bizafungurwa, n'impanda zizavuga, kandi asigaye yanjye azahishwa hanyuma inzabya zifite umujinya wose w'Imana zizasukwa, kandi bazaba mu bubabare bukabije n'agahinda ndetse n'ubwo babona gutsindwa kwabo kwegereza byuzuye.

Bityo, abakurikira Baraba mu bujiji ndetse n'ubwo yigira mu bujiji ko yatumwe na Yahweh, azi neza ko ari uw'ikigongwe ahubwo, kandi uwo yatoranyijwe kuyobora igihugu ndetse n'isi hamwe n'inyamaswa yo mu nyanja. Nyamara niko bimeze, benshi baguye mu maboko y'uyu mwiyemezi bagata Kristo Yesu ubu bazahabwa mu maboko ye ubwo abizerwa bazatwarwa. Kandi hari abanshinja ko nataye Itorero ryanjye mu gihe ahubwo ari abatari muri Kristo, batigeze bagera ku gakiza kuzuye bantaye binyuze mu kwifatanya na Baraba, kandi narababuriye ko bagomba kuguma mu muzabibu kuko mutamfite, nta cyo mushobora gukora.

Nubwo benshi bazarimbuka amazina yabo atigeze yandikwa mu Gitabo cy'Ubugingo cy'Umwana w'Intama kandi bazarimbuka bave ku isi ubwo urubanza rwabo ruzagera, kandi mu buryo butunguranye ibyiringiro byabo bizayoyoka mu kanya gato.

Kuko batari bafite ibyiringiro byo gukiranuka!

Kuko batari bafite ibyiringiro byo gutsindishirizwa!

Kuko batari bafite ibyiringiro byo kwezwa!

Kuko batakaje ibyiringiro byose by'Umwami wabo ubagarukira ndetse nk'uko nasezeranyije mu Isezerano Rishya ryanjye kandi buri sezerano rya Yahweh na Yeshua HaMashiach rizasohozwa ibihe byose n'iteka ryose!

Kuko igihe cyanjye cy'ubuntu n'imbabazi kirangiye, nubwo ubuntu n'imbabazi biri kumwe nanjye iteka ryose, kandi nk'uko byanditswe niko bizakorwa ko haba Yahweh na Yeshua HaMashiach bazagirira imbabazi uwo tuzagirira imbabazi kuko ni nde ushobora guhagarara imbere y'intebe y'ubwami ya Yahweh kuko ari Imana Isumbabyose kandi nta n'umwe uhwanye na we! Bityo rero mwumve ko umujinya wateguwe kandi ugiye gusukwa ku isi nk'uko ibise byanjye byabitangaje kandi bikabihanura, kandi uwo munsi wageze!

Nuko rero ndatangaza kuboneka kwanjye, kandi bizaba nk'uko byanditswe mu byanditswe byera kandi umugeni wanjye azunga ubumwe iteka ryose mu isezerano n'umukwe wabo kandi abazatwarwa mu ijuru bose mu gihe cyo kuboneka kwanjye, baba abazima cyangwa abapfuye, bazaremwa bundi bushya iteka ryose mu bugingo bushya bw'iteka. Kandi abiyeguriye by'ukuri amagambo meza n'imvugo nziza bya Baraba bose bazagwa bave mu ikoraniro ryanjye nk'amababi yumishijwe n'icyaha. Nyamara, benshi bazagwa bazagaruka aho ndi banyuze mu bigeragezo bikomeye n'amakuba kandi benshi muri bo bazambaza izina ryanjye kugeza ku iherezo kandi imbaga y'amateraniro menshi izava mu cyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli yinjire mu bugingo bw'iteka, nubwo abenshi bazashyirwaho ikimenyetso cy'inyamaswa bakagwa mu birimi by'umuriro iteka ryose.

Ku bw'ibyo, Jyewe, Kristo Yesu ndetse Umwami wanyu wazutse akazamuka mu ijuru, ndategeka abizerwa banjye kubavamo rwose no kwitandukanya nabo byuzuye. Mwitegure bakunzi banjye! Ntimukifatanye mu myigaragambyo yabo, mwirinde amakoraniro yabo, kandi mu buryo bwose mwirinde ikibi cyikoranyiriza hamwe munsi y'umwuka w'amahoro w'ikinyoma kuko igitero cyabo rwose cyiteguye. Mwumve bakunzi banjye ko mwaba mwinjira mu mutego wabo niba mwakwifatanya nabo muri ubwo buryo. Bakunzi banjye, igihe kirageze ngo mureke amaganya y'iyi si, kuko ubuzima bwanyu buhishe muri jye, Kristo Yesu, kandi bugiye guhishurwa byuzuye kugira ngo mumenye nk'uko namwe muzwi.

Bityo rero mwumve ikinyoma kibariho bakunzi banjye kandi mwihishe mutegereze kuboneka kwanjye, kuko kuri hafi! Mwumve ikinyoma cyabo, ko umwana w'intama ufite amahembe abiri azigira nk'uwakomeretse mu mutwe, kandi azagaragara nk'uwazutse mu bapfuye agakira igikomere cye cyo mu mutwe ariko ibyo atanga byose nk'igihamya cy'ibirego bye by'ibinyoma ko ari we Moshiach wabo ni ibimenyetso n'ibitangaza by'ibinyoma kandi haba Yahweh NDI WE ukomeye, cyangwa jye, Yeshua HaMashiach ntitwohereje uyu mushukanyi, nubwo yemerewe kuza mu gihe cye cyagenwe kugira ngo ategekane n'inyamaswa yo mu nyanja hamwe n'ikiyoka nk'umutwe wabo kuko ari ko bahawe gusohoza mu mugambi w'iteka wa Yahweh wo gucungura Adamu!

Nuko rero ndababwira bakunzi banjye ngo muntegereze mwihanganye kandi ntimwizere ibinyoma byabo kuko nta muntu cyangwa ikintu na kimwe gishobora guhagarika kuboneka kwanjye ku bizerwa mu Itorero ryanjye, nubwo bazagerageza guhisha iki gikorwa bakoresheje igitero cy'ibinyoma cy'ibivejuru n'intambara y'isi izarutisha intambara zose zabayeho ku isi. Nyamara, intambara yabo izaba ubusa ugereranyije n'urugamba rw'uwo munsi ukomeye aho bazarimburwa rwose ubwo nzaza ku isi ndi kumwe n'ingabo zo mu ijuru, kuko nzaza guca urubanza no kurwana intambara mu gukiranuka!

Jyewe, Yeshua HaMashiach nohereje aya Magambo binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye ari we ntumwa yanjye yizerwa, kandi ni jye, Yeshua HaMashiach wemeza ubu buhamya butangwa mu Butumwa Bwiza Bw'iteka bwahishuwe mu gihe cyabwo.

Ni jye ugiye guhindukirira Isirayeli kugira ngo mbacungure mbakure mu isi nk'umucunguzi wabo wo mu muryango kandi ndabahamagara Yemwe Isirayeli kuko igihe kigeze ngo mwemere Mesiya wanyu w'ukuri, kuko nk'uko uyu mushukanyi azagambanira kandi akarimbura Babuloni y'ibanga, ni nako azagambanira abantu bo mu gihugu mu kizira cye cy'umurimbuzi cyegereje rwose.

Bityo rero mwumve ko bazakururuka bajya mu byobo byabo byo kwihishamo kugira ngo bihishe mu bitare by'isi kandi benshi bazarimburirwamo. Nuko rero ndabaza nti, ni he umuntu uwo ari we wese yakwihisha kugira ngo ahunge amaso yanjye y'iteka, cyangwa ni nde ushobora guhunga amaso ya Yahweh kuko byose bibonwa kandi bizwi kandi icyo ari cyo cyose kiba ku isi cyemererwa kuba haba mu rubanza cyangwa ku bw'agakiza k'abazambaza izina ryanjye kugeza ku iherezo cyangwa ku bemeye ko ndi Mesiya wabo, kandi abakora ibibi bose baziturwa byuzuye.

Kandi ni jye uzaboneka gitungura mu kirere mu bwiza bukomeye kandi bazamenya kandi benshi bazasobanukirwa ko bacikanywe n'iki gikorwa kimwe bari bizeyeho cyane ko bari bakirimo, ariko bakaba bacyimwe kubera ubuzima bwabo bw'ibyaha kuko icyaha kitazigera cyinjira mu ijuru cyangwa ngo gihagarare imbere yanjye.

Dore! Ibihe birageze nuko rero ubu ngiye gukora kugira ngo nkoranye abizerwa.

Murebe! Mube maso! Mwumve! Ese mwiteguye bakunzi banjye? Umunsi urabagezeho.
