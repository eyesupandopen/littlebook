Nimwumve Amagambo Yanjye Kandi Mukoranire ku Ijwi ry'Umwami Wanyu!

Nimwumve amagambo yanjye kandi mukoranire ku ijwi ry'Umwami wanyu, Umwami, Mesiya, n'Umutambyi Mukuru, ari we Yeshua HaMashiach. Kuki mushidikanya ku magambo yanjye n'uko ibintu bizakurikirana, ndetse n'ibisobanuro byimbitse by'amagambo yanjye? Kuko navuze neza ibyerekeye Babuloni y'ibanga n'imperuka yayo yegereje:

Ibyahishuwe 17

[17:13] Abo bahuje inama, kandi baha inyamaswa imbaraga zabo n'ubutware bwabo.

[17:16] Kandi amahembe icumi wabonye kuri ya nyamaswa, azanga ya ndaya, ayigire umusaka, ayambike ubusa, arye inyama zayo, ayitwike ikongoke.

[17:17] Kuko Imana ishyize mu mitima yabo gukora ibyo ishaka, no guhuza inama, no guha inyamaswa ubwami bwabo, kugeza aho amagambo y'Imana azasohorera.

Nyamara benshi muri mwe mubura ubushishozi bukwiye bw'ibyanditswe kandi benshi muri mwe muracyafitiye icyizere umuntu wa kamere. Mwizeye ubushobozi bw'umuntu wa kamere, imbaraga z'umuntu wa kamere, ibisubizo by'umuntu wa kamere, n'ibinyoma by'umuntu wa kamere, ntimwasomye ko abantu bose ari abanyabinyoma bana banjye? Nimunyumve kandi musobanukirwe: Nta gisubizo gitangwa n'umuntu wa kamere kitaganisha kuri dragone, kandi dragone izategeka isi igihe gito numara gukurwaho ubuza, ndetse n'ubu ni yo mana y'iyi si, mbese ibi ntibyanditswe neza mu byanditswe?

Kuki mugishidikanya ku magambo yanjye, bakundwa banjye n'abo nifuza? Mwizere amagambo yanjye kandi musobanukirwe ko umuntu wa kamere nta mbaraga afite, umuntu wa kamere nta bushobozi afite bwo kurwanya Satani, ibisubizo by'umuntu wa kamere birangira ari ubusa kandi bikorera imigambi ya Satani, kandi nta kuri kuba mu muntu wa kamere no mu bayobozi b'isi yanyu. Kuko umuntu wa kamere yononekaye kuva mu ntangiriro mu Busitani bwa Edeni abigizwemo n'inzoka kandi ayoba akiva mu nda ndetse avuka apfuye mu byaha n'ibicumuro, kandi ku bw'iyo mpamvu ni cyo cyatumye njyewe, Yesu w'i Nazareti, nigaragaza kugira ngo nsenye imirimo ya Satani mu bemeye amahoro n'agakiza ntanga.

Dore! Abayobozi b'isi bose bakorera hamwe mu rwihisho, kandi bose bakorera Satani. Ntimufite abayobozi batowe, ahubwo muhabwa amahitamo y'ibinyoma kuko mutigeze muhitamo abo mutekereza ko mutora, ahubwo babazanira kugira ngo mubahe amajwi yanyu kugira ngo babone kwemera kwanyu kandi kwemera kwanyu ni icyaha kuko mudashobora guhitamo ikibi gito ku bushake bwanyu ngo mumbere abahamya icyarimwe, uko ni ukuyoba. Nimwihane! Nimubasohokemo kandi mwitandukanye nabo! Njyewe, Umwami Yesu Kristo mu rurimi rw'Icyongereza na Yeshua HaMashiach mu rurimi rw'Igiheburayo, ntabwo natanze igisubizo cya politiki kandi nta muyobozi w'isi uzabaho kuko ntawe natanze, kandi abizera ibitandukanye n'ibyo barashutswe bikomeye. Abayobozi b'isi bose bakoze agatsiko, kandi ntibazatandukana kuko abayobozi banyu bose bakorera Satani mu rwihisho cyangwa ku mugaragaro. Ese muracyifuza amagambo meza n'imbwirwaruhame nziza? Ntimwabeshywe bihagije, mwarabayeho mu buzima bw'ibinyoma bya gisitani kuva muvutse? Kuki mugishakisha abayobozi b'isi aho gushaka njyewe, Kristo Yesu? Kuko ndi Umwami wanyu, Umwami, Mesiya, n'Umutambyi Mukuru Yahweh yatanze, kandi naguze isi ndetse n'ubuzima bw'abacunguwe mu maraso yanjye atagira icyaha atanga impongano. Kuko nk'Umutambyi Mukuru wanyu, nitanze nk'umwana w'intama wa Pasika wanyu utagira inenge kandi utagira icyaha. Kandi ndi Umwami w'abami n'Umutware w'abatware, kandi nzaza nk'Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda kandi nzategekera ku ntebe y'ubwami ya Dawidi ku isi kandi imigambi y'iteka ya Yahweh y'agakiza n'incungu izasohorera muri njye ubwo Intare izabana n'umwana w'intama mu Bwami bwanjye buzaza n'ingoma itazagira iherezo.

Dore! Ndababwira ikintu mbere y'uko kiba. Babuloni y'Ibanga, ari yo Leta Zunze Ubumwe zahoze ari urumuri rw'isi, iri mu minyururu ndetse ibikiwe urubanza rw'umuriro. Urubanza rwayo no kurimbuka kwayo bizaza gitungura kandi mu isaha imwe izatwikwa n'umuriro, mu isaha imwe izacikamo ibice, mu isaha imwe imyenda yayo izatwikirwa n'amazi aturutse iburasirazuba ndetse n'iburengerazuba. Ingabo zitukura z'inyamaswa zamaze kwinjira ku mipaka yayo kandi zamaze kwitegura zitegereje amabwiriza, kandi zizica buri mugabo, umugore, n'umwana utazakorera imigambi yazo. Kuko inyamaswa n'umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma bagiye guhishurwa bamaze gukorera hamwe kugira ngo barimbure Babuloni y'ibanga, kandi bamaze kugera ku mipaka yayo.

Bombi ni abahoze ari abayobozi ba Babuloni y'Ibanga. Umwe agaragara nk'aho atari we kandi agakoresha umusaza w'igikoresho wataye ubwenge, naho undi akagaragara nk'urenganywa n'uwo musaza w'igikoresho, nyamara ab'icyo gihugu bamutangaje ko ari Kuro wabo kandi bamushaka nk'umwami wabo, avuga ko ari we watoranyijwe kuko dragone yamutoranyije kugira ngo ayobye abo mu gihugu cy'isezerano, kandi azubaka urusengero rwabo, ndetse n'igihe nta mpamvu y'icyo kintu kuko njyewe Yeshua HaMashiach ndi Pasika itunganye kandi mwe muri urusengero rw'Imana ihoraho, ndetse urusengero rutubatswe n'intoki z'abantu ahubwo rwubatswe n'imbaraga za Yahweh ku bw'icyubahiro cye kandi azabaha icyubahiro muri njye Kristo Yesu Umwami wanyu!

Dore! Izi nyamaswa mbi n'ihuriro ryazo ry'amahembe icumi ririmo gukorwa bamaze gutegura irimbuka rya Babuloni y'Ibanga. Kuko biteguye ku isi hose, kandi bateguye ndetse bashyira mu bikorwa ku isi hose isenywa rya nyuma ry'isi nk'uko muyizi kandi mu ivu hazazamuka gahunda yabo nshya, mu kavuyo hazazamuka gahunda yabo ya gisitani kandi ikimenyetso kizashyirwaho ku ngufu kugira ngo winjire mu muryango wabo kandi abantu b'Imana bazaba mu butayu nk'uko byari mu minsi ya Nowa kandi bake cyane ni bo bazarokoka. Kuko inyamaswa n'umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma bazarwanya abera bakabanesha. Aba ni bo bazava mu mubabaro ukomeye, kandi bameshe amakanzu yabo, bayejesha amaraso y'Umwana w'Intama. Ni cyo gituma bari imbere y'intebe y'Imana, bakayikorera ku manywa na nijoro mu Rusengero rwayo: kandi uwicaye ku Ntebe y'Ubwami azabana na bo. Ntibazongera gusonza, cyangwa kugira inyota ukundi, kandi izuba ntirizabica, cyangwa icyokere cyose. Kuko Umwana w'Intama, uri hagati y'intebe y'ubwami, azabagaburira, kandi azabayobora ku masoko y'amazi y'ubugingo: kandi Imana izahanagura amarira yose ku maso yabo.

Dore! Umwanzi araje kandi uko ibintu bizakurikirana ni uku: Ubuza azakomeza kubuza kugeza igihe nzakoraniriza abizerwa mu Itorero ryanjye ngo bansanganire mu bicu mu kuboneka kwanjye ku bw'Itorero ryanjye, ari ryo Mugeni wanjye. Nubwo benshi muri mwe muhakana ibyanditswe bisobanutse kuri iki gikorwa, kizaba, kandi kizamenyekana ku isi hose. Nta kuzamurwa kw'ibanga kuzabaho, kuko ayo ni amagambo y'abahanuzi b'ibinyoma b'Abasiyoni, kandi kuboneka kwanjye ntikuzahakanwa ku isi hose. Bazashaka guhisha kugenda kwanyu bakoresheje intambara n'igitero cy'ibinyoma cy'ibivejuru, ariko nta bivejuru bibaho ahubwo ni abaguye biyoberanya. Igihe Umugeni wanjye azaba ajyanywe mu ijuru kandi ubuza akurwaho irimbuka ritunguranye rizaba ku isi hose kandi amahanga yose yigeze kwiyita aya gikristo azareka kubaho mu buryo abayeho ubu, nyamara hazasigara abantu bazaneshywa kandi bakicwa. Babuloni y'Ibanga izagwa mbere kandi gitungura mu gihe kimwe n'igitero cyabo gitunguranye kizatagira, ariko ntizigera yongera kubyuka ukundi, kuko Yahweh yayitangarije umugabane wikubye kabiri w'urubanza.

Kuko Babuloni y'Ibanga yayobeje amahanga mu cyaha gikomeye n'ibinyoma binyuze mu burozi bwayo n'ubupfumu bwayo ndetse n'imyidagaduro yayo yitwa iy'imihango ndetse n'ubupfumu bwayo buteye ishozi bwitwa imiti nyamara mu by'ukuri ari uburozi bwica. Kandi uko ni ko iherezo ryayo rizaza gitungura kandi nta muti. Kandi hazasigara ubutayu, nubwo abasigaye bazarokoka, kuko umuntu wese uzambaza izina ry'Umwami kandi ntagire ubuzima bwe agaciro kugeza ku iherezo azakizwa.

Nuko rero musobanukirwe ibihe murimo kandi musobanukirwe uko ibintu bizakurikirana kandi musobanukirwe ko nta kintu na kimwe gishobora guhindura ibi bintu, kuko ibyo Yahweh yatangaje nta muntu numwe ushobora kubihindura!

Dore! Ndi Yeshua HaMashiach kandi nohereje aya Magambo binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye, kandi binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye aya Magambo yoherejwe.

Nzaboneka gitungura, witeguye Mugeni wanjye?
