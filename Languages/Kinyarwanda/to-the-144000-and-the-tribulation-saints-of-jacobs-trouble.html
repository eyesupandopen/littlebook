Ku 144,000 n'Abezwa b'Umubabaro mu Makuba ya Yakobo

Ku 144,000 n'abezwa b'umubabaro mu makuba ya Yakobo: Dore! Ndababwira ibintu byinshi bitaraba kugira ngo umunyabwenge muri mwe wumva ijwi ryanjye arirusheho kuba umunyabwenge, kandi kugira ngo mumenye ukuri kw'amagambo yanjye. Kuko Satani azagerageza guhisha kugenda kw'abizerwa mu Itorero ryanjye (kuko nzabatwara mu kugenda kwabo bava mu isi bajya mu ijuru) akoresheje intambara n'ikigaragara nk'ibinyoma cy'abiyita abanyamahanga atari ikindi uretse abaguye biyerekana ku isi mu gukiranirwa hejuru y'ugukiranirwa gushinze imizi mu binyoma byabo. Kuko ari ibishuko.

Babeshye ku byerekeye icyitwa isanzure; kuko nta sanzure ryaguka ry'ubusa gusa, ahubwo hejuru y'isi hari ijuru ry'amajuru, ndetse (ijuru ryo hejuru cyane) aho Yahweh Imana isumba byose yicara ku ntebe yayo, nyamara izatura hamwe n'abantu. Kandi iri hose kandi ni byose muri byose mu Mubiri wa Kristo. Ku isi hari isaro ryayo ry'igiciro cyinshi, ndetse n'abatoranyijwe mu bihe byashize by'iteka mu mutima wa Yahweh ubwe. Kandi isi ni iy'Uwiteka n'ibiyuzuye byose; kuko njye, Yesu w'i Nazareti, naguze isi n'amaraso yanjye bwite acungura.

Mwige imiterere y'isi yanyu y'ukuri (mwige kugira ngo mumenye) kandi mwizere ibisobanuro n'amagambo mu Byanditswe Byera ku byerekeye irema, kuko bimeze nk'uko Yahweh yabisobanuye mu Byanditswe, kandi ntibishoboka ko Yahweh abeshya. Niba, mbabwiye iby'isi, ntimwizere, muzizera mute nimbabwira iby'ijuru? Oya, nta banyamahanga babaho mu buryo ibinyoma byabo bibisobanura, ahubwo ni abamarayika baguye, abigometse, ndetse na Satani n'ubwami bwe babeshye kandi bashuka isi kugira ngo abishimira gukiranirwa bose bacirwe urubanza.

Baboheye imigani myinshi yahimbwe n'ubwenge mu mwenda w'ibinyoma, kandi bagenzuye impande zombi z'inkuru zabo kuva mu ntangiriro uko basunikaga imigambi yabo. Kuko bagenzura igitekerezo n'ikinyuranyo cyacyo, kandi bahuza imirimo yabo mibi intambwe ku yindi, kandi igishuko cyabo ni kinini! Ariko umucyo w'Ibyanditswe Byera ushyira ahagaragara ubugambanyi, nyamara umuntu yanga ubufasha bw'Imana kugeza urubanza ruciwe n'amakuba akaba. Hanyuma abantu bashaka kwihana iyo byamaze kuba iby'ubusa nyuma y'uko urubanza ruciwe, kandi ababaho muri ubwo buryo ntibazacika urubanza rwabo. Kuko icyo Yahweh yavuze nta muntu n'umwe ushobora kugihindura!

Benshi bazagwa mu bishuko bizaza bizaturuka mu ntambara y'isi n'amakuba agiye kugera ku isi. Kuko baziyerekana ku bantu nk'ibiremwa byiza byaturutse mu zindi si zateye imbere kugira ngo basangire ubumenyi bwabo bwiza. Ariko ni ubumenyi bw'icyiza n'ikibi bwahinduriwe isura kugira ngo buhuze n'intego zabo. Bazaba bafite ikoranabuhanga ryo hejuru kandi bazagaragara nk'abateye imbere cyane, kure cyane y'umuntu kamere. Ariko iterambere ryabo riri mu bibi gusa, kandi si abo kuri iyi si ahubwo baremwe na Yahweh mu ntangiriro y'irema mu ijuru. Bari bahari ubwo Yahweh yaremaga isi. Data mu kumenya kwe mbere yari azi ko bazagambana bityo agumisha ibanga ry'umutima we w'agaciro rihishe abo yari azi ko bazazana irimbuka ribi ku byo yaremye.

Ndiracyahamagarira umuntu kamere gukiranuka, ariko umuntu kamere ahubwo ahitamo indwara, icyaha, n'umwijima; bityo nzaha abantu icyo bifuza cyane. Kubera ko umuntu kamere akora ibibi kandi agakunda umwijima, nzamuha umwijima utagira mipaka.

Kuko muri iki gihe, umuntu kamere ahagarara mu buntu bwanjye yibona akoresha imbabazi nk'impamvu yo gukora icyaha ku kindi, aho kwemera amasezerano y'amahoro ya Yahweh, ndetse nanjye, Yeshua HaMashiach, watanze amaraso yanjye atagira icyaha mu kumvira Yahweh nk'ikiguzi cy'ibyaha byose by'abantu ibihe byose. Ntimwibeshye: Ndi Mesiya w'ukuri, n'abandi bose ni inyiganano zihendutse zitagira agaciro.

Satani yigometse nyuma gato y'itangira ry'irema maze yanduza irema rya Data mu buryo butagira isoni, bubi, bw'ubushyamirane, kandi bwangiza; ariko byakozwe mu buhendanyi bukomeye n'ibimenyetso n'ibitangaza by'ibinyoma, ndetse n'ikoranabuhanga ryo hejuru ritangaza ibyiyumvo. Kandi mu rwitwazo basaba gusengwa no kumvira inzira zabo byimazeyo, nyamara inzira zabo buri gihe zizana urupfu n'irimbuka ry'abantu, kuko ari ko zateguwe.

Nta gishya munsi y'izuba, bana banjye. Ibyitwa imana by'amateka ya kera n'urubyaro rwabyo ruzira rwitwa ibigirwamana bizagaruka, kandi nk'uko byari bimeze mu minsi ya Nowa, ni ko bizaba. Kuko babohowe mu minyururu yabo n'umubabaro kugira ngo bahabwe urubanza rwabo rwuzuye, kandi nzishyura, ni ko Umwami Yesu Kristo avuga, kandi Yahweh azabitura kugeza ku ndunduro.

Satani n'abaguye be banze gupfukamira gahunda ya Yahweh ahubwo bagerageza kwiba isi mu bantu binyuze mu gishuko gikomeye. Satani yagerageje no kwiba umurage wanjye kuri Data w'Imicyo! Ariko nta n'umwe ushobora gushikuzwa n'umwe muri mwe mu maboko yanjye, kandi nta n'umwe ushobora kwambura umurage wanjye; kuko utangwa na Yahweh ubwe, kandi uwo ari we wese wanga kumpfukamira, Yesu Kristo, yigomeka kuri gahunda ya Yahweh, yifatanya n'abaguye iteka ryose.

Nuko rero ndababwira nti, ntimwizere ibinyoma bya Copernicus, wari Umujesuwiti n'Umu-Freemason wakoreraga Satani. Yigishije gusa amagambo y'abafilozofe ba gipagani b'Abagereki ba kera. Bityo imiterere y'isi mwemera yatekerejwe n'abasenga izuba n'abapagani ishingiye ku binyoma bya Satani, ndetse n'Abajesuwiti batuka Imana bangije inzego zanyu z'uburezi n'inyigisho zirwanya inyigisho, bafite intego yuzuye y'uko isanzure ryabo ry'ikinyoma rishobora gushyirwa mu bwenge bw'abantu binyuze mu binyoma bipfunyitse nk'uburezi. Ahubwo, gucengezwa amatwara kutagira ubwenge kwarabaye, kandi isi nk'uko iri by'ukuri ntisobanukirwa n'abayituye.

Ubu urubuga rwateguriwe igishuko gikomeye cyane, kandi ibinyoma byamaze kubakwa, kuko imfatiro zabyo ari gukiranirwa n'ubushukanyi bukomeye. Ibi bishuko bishinze imizi mu burezi bwanyu bw'Abajesuwiti. Kuko isi yose ishukwa n'ibinyoma byabo; ndetse n'intore ubwazo zashukwa iyaba byashobokaga. Bityo umutego witeguye gukora, kandi ikintu cyonyine kiwufashe ni ubuza, buzavanwaho mu buryo butunguranye kandi vuba. Hanyuma intambara ikomeye izaduka ku isi yose uko abizerwa banjye bakoranirizwa aho ndi mu ijuru. Hanyuma mu ivu hazazamuka inyamaswa n'umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma, bazaba ishingiro mu gishuko kizaza.

Nuko rero ntimwizere ibinyoma byabo, mwa banyambaraga banjye, kuko muri ubwoko bwihariye kandi nabahamagaye mbashyiraho ikimenyetso cy'Imana Ihoraho, kugira ngo umujinya w'Imana utabakoraho. Nzaboherereza nk'abanyambaraga banjye. Muzaba amajwi yanjye menshi arira mu butayu aho muzoherezwa hose, kandi ndaboherereza gukoranya intama zanjye zazimiye zisigaye.

Kuko imbaga nyamwinshi izava mu makuba ya Yakobo, kandi muzakora ibikorwa by'ubutwari kandi mutegeke ibidashoboka, kuko nzaha ibimenyetso, ibitangaza, n'imirimo itangaje byinshi gukorwa n'amaboko yanyu. Muzakora ibitangaza bikomeye bya kera byanditswe mu Byanditswe Byera, ndetse mu mbaraga za Eliya, uretse ko muzakora imirimo mikuru kandi ikomeye kuruta iyanditswe kuri we kera.

Kuko Ibyanditswe bivuga ko unyizera, Yeshua HaMashiach, atazakora gusa imirimo nakoze ku isi mu murimo wanjye i Yudaya nk'umwana w'intama w'Imana, ahubwo muzakora imirimo ikomeye kuruta iyo nakoze, kuko nzaha ibimenyetso, ibitangaza, n'imirimo itangaje bikomeye mu maboko yanyu uko mutegeka ibidashoboka binyuze mu kunyizera. Nuko rero mundebereho buri gihe. Kandi mugumise amaso yanyu kuri njye, kuko nzabayobora intambwe ku yindi. Kugenda kwanyu kuzaba ukw'ubwenge, kandi muzagira ururimi rw'abanyabwenge, kandi nzaba imbaraga zanyu n'agakiza kanyu, kuko muri njye, Kristo Yesu, ari ho kuzura kose kuba.

Nuko rero ndabagira inama mwese ko vuba cyane amafaranga azata agaciro, kandi ifaranga ryonyine ryemerwa rizasaba ikimenyetso kugira ngo ubone ibyiza by'isi. Ntimuzafate ikimenyetso, kuko byaba byiza gupfa muri ubu buzima (mudakunda ubuzima bwanyu kugeza ku iherezo) kuruta gufata ikimenyetso mukajugunywa mu nyanja y'umuriro y'iteka yateguriwe Umwanzi n'abamarayika be, kuko ibihe byabo byageze; gusa ntimuzifatanye nabo. Kuko bazasezeranya amasezerano asa n'aho ari makuru kandi y'agaciro, ariko amagambo yabo ni ibinyoma, kandi ubumara buri mu magambo yabo, n'amasezerano yabo ni ibinyoma kandi yuzuye ibintu byose byanduye. Ntibazigera bashobora gusohoza amasezerano yabo, kuko urubanza rwabo ruje; nta mahoro bafite, kandi amahoro azakurwa rwose ku isi.

Ikibi cy'abantu kizaba kitagira mipaka rwose; kandi kuri benshi, ubuza namara kuvanwaho, baziyegurira iteka ryose ubusazi n'ikibi. Uretse ko ubaho akoresheje inkota agomba kwicwa n'inkota, kandi bazagwa rwose ibumoso n'iburyo ari benshi, ariko ntibizabageraho uko mugendera njye; gusa ntimukunde ubuzima bwanyu kugeza ku iherezo kandi mukurikire urugero rwanjye rwo gukorera abandi no kwitanga. Kuko ingororano ikomeye izaba iyanyu kubera kumvira n'ubudahemuka.

Bityo irari n'ikibi by'abantu bizaba bitagira mipaka rwose, kandi Umwanzi n'abamarayika be bazaba batagira mipaka rwose, n'ibizira byabo byose bibi bizaba bitagira mipaka rwose, kandi bazaza nk'abafasha ba kera. Igishuko cyabo ni kirekire kandi gifite imizi yimbitse igiye kurandurwa rwose vuba. Si imana, kandi si abagiraneza bateye imbere, ahubwo ni abashukanyi, kandi biyanduje mu buryo bwose. Bityo bazaba batagira mipaka rwose, kandi ubwuzuye bw'ubwami bwa Satani buzabohorwa buve ikuzimu, kuko hazakingurwa.

Ibi bizakorwa kugira ngo byereke umuntu kamere icyo yahisemo kandi bihe amahirwe ya nyuma yo kwihana; kuko ikibi kitazongera guhishwa no gukumirwa, kandi buri banga rizajya ahagaragara. Ndetse n'ibinyoma by'igishuko cyabo gikomeye n'ubushukanyi bukomeye bizashyirwa ahagaragara kugira ngo bose babibone, kuko Yahweh yabivuze mu Byanditswe Byera, kandi icyo Yahweh avuga nta muntu ushobora kugihindura.

Nuko rero gutandukanya kwa nyuma kuzabaho, kandi benshi mu birara nkunda bazasigirwa ibigeragezo n'ibizamini bizaza, ndetse kugira ngo bashobore gukizwa. Kuko nta gihe kigihari, kandi ubuntu bwanjye n'imbabazi zanjye ntibizongera gutangwa mu buryo bumwe, kandi nta gihe kigihari cy'inzira z'akazuyazi. Buri wese azagomba guhitamo; kandi ku banyabwoba n'abatizera bazaba bahisemo Satani n'ubwami bwe mu gihe bashoboraga kumpitamo, ndetse na Yesu Kristo, umwana w'Imana waje mu mubiri kandi nkitanga nk'Ipasika itunganye ku byaha byose by'abantu rimwe rizima. Benshi bazakomeza kwinangira bange ukuboko kwanjye kw'ubufasha mu gihe cy'amakuba akomeye, kuko ubufasha bwanjye ari ubufasha bw'iteka kandi buhoraho bw'ingororano ikomeye, imbabazi, ubuntu, n'urukundo.

Nyamara, benshi mu batuye isi bazizera ibinyoma kandi bazatangazwa n'inyamaswa, bavuga bati, ni nde uhwanye n'inyamaswa? Bazemera bitagoranye kurumwa n'inzoka ubumara bwayo budashobora kuvurwa, kuko buhoraho. Ku muntu guhitamo ikibi mu bihe byo guhitamo bizaba amahitamo ahoraho, kandi abakora ayo mahitamo bazahagarara mu rubanza rukomeye.

Abahitamo ikibi ku bushake bazabazwa buri gitekerezo kibi, buri jambo ribi, na buri gikorwa kibi bakoze nabi ku isi, kuko ibitabo bizabumburwa kandi nta mbabazi zizabaho. Kandi nta muntu n'umwe uzashobora guhagarara mu gukiranuka kwe bwite, kuko umuntu nta gukiranuka kwe bwite agira; kuko inzoka yabanduje kuva mu ntangiriro, kandi iminsi yayo yo kwanduza igiye kurangira. Uko ni ko Yahweh yantumye, umwana we w'ikinege, nk'amasezerano y'amahoro ku muntu kamere, kandi umuntu kamere yanze amahoro ahozaho kuko umuntu kamere akunda ikibi.

Urubanza rukomeye rugiye gusukwa ku bwami bwa Satani. Uru rubanza ruzarushaho kuba rubi, n'ibikorwa bizakorwa mu rubanza bizarushaho kuba bibi. Ishyano rizongerwa ku ishyano n'umubabaro uzongerwa ku mubabaro ku banga bose kwihana ibikorwa byabo bibi. Kuko benshi batazihana imirimo y'amaboko yabo, ngo bareke gusenga abadayimoni, n'ibishushanyo bisengwa by'izahabu, n'ifeza, n'umuringa, n'amabuye, n'ibiti: bitabasha kureba, cyangwa kumva, cyangwa kugenda: Kandi ntibazihana ubwicanyi bwabo, cyangwa uburozi bwabo, cyangwa ubusambanyi bwabo, cyangwa ubujura bwabo.

Kuko abaguye atari imana, ahubwo ni abiyitirira ibinyoma, abigira uko batari, abakinnyi, n'abashukanyi. Bazana urupfu n'irimbuka gusa ku babakurikira, kandi abashutswe bose bazamanjirwa rwose, kuko ibyo bari biteze bizayoyoka muri iyo nyanja nini y'umuriro aho nta byiringiro bihari. Kandi njye, Kristo Yesu, nzasenya rwose ubwami bwa Satani mu rubanza rukomeye, kuko urubanza rukomeye ruzasukwa kuri bo bose, kandi ndi Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda.

Nuko rero buri kimenyetso kizongera urubanza hejuru y'urubanza, kandi buri mpanda izongera urubanza hejuru y'urubanza, buri shyano rizongera ishyano hejuru y'ishyano, kandi kuri buri gikombe hazaza umujinya wuzuye w'Imana, ndetse umujinya hejuru y'umujinya. Ubwami bwa Satani buzababara cyane, kandi ubwami bwe buzarimburwa rwose kandi busenywe burundu.

Kuko bazikoraniriza hamwe kandi bitondekere kurwanya kuza k'Umwami, kuko nzaza rwose. Kuva mu ntangiriro y'amakuba ya Yakobo, hazaba iminsi irindwi y'imyaka, kuko icyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli kizasohora kugira ngo gukiranirwa kurimburwa kandi Isirayeli ikizwe. Kuko nzeza Isirayeli ikibi cyafashe igihugu.

Kuko haba Yahweh cyangwa njye ubwanjye, Yeshua HaMashiach, ntitwohereje cyangwa ngo duhe inshingano abategetsi babo, kuko bategeka mu gukiranirwa kandi jenoside zabo ni ibizira, kandi ubwoko bwanjye bwishyura ikiguzi cy'intambara zabo. Nyamara benshi mu Itorero ryanjye bizera ibinyoma byabo bya Siyoni, bityo mumenye ko ntari Umu-Zionist, kuko ari abahezanguni b'Abayahudi, kandi ni urumamfu, imbuto ya Satani ubwe; kandi nzabarandura mu gihugu, kuko biyitiriye umurage wanjye, kandi nje gufata ibyange. Kuko njye, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndetse nanjye, mfite icyangombwa cy'isi; kuko nayiguze amaraso yanjye atagira icyaha, kandi mfite imfunguzo z'urupfu n'ikuzimu. Kandi nishyuriye ibyaha by'abantu, kandi umuntu wese uzambaza izina ry'Umwami azakizwa.

Bityo isi ni iyanjye n'ibiyuzuye byose, kandi nzeza igihugu ikibi cyabo, n'isinagogi ya Satani izarimburwa rwose, bityo nzazana isezerano rishya ku isi kandi nzategeka mu gukiranirwa. Kuko uko isi irushaho kuba umwijima n'ikibi uko isegonda rishize, kuza kwanjye kwa kabiri kurushaho kwegereza uko isegonda rishize.

Mugire ibyiringiro bikomeye byo kuza kwanjye; kuko nzaza kandi nzatobora ikirere ndi kumwe n'ingabo zo mu ijuru, ndetse ingabo zitandukanye n'izindi zose, kuko ingabo nk'izanjye zitigeze zibaho, ingabo zidashobora gutsindwa zigizwe n'ababaho ubuzima bw'iteka badashobora kwicwa cyangwa gutsindwa. Kuko mpabwa imbaraga na Yahweh ku bw'iyo ntego, kandi nzaba nabahaye imbaraga n'ingororano kubera ubudahemuka bwabo n'urukundo bankunda n'izina ryanjye, ndetse Yesu Kristo! Kuko ukunda kandi agakumbira Yeshua HaMashiach akunda kandi agakumbira Yahweh, kuko nta wusumba Imana ikomeye ihoraho ya Isirayeli! Uhakana Umwana, uwo nta Data afite; kandi ndi Yesu Kristo umwana w'ikinege wa Data witangiye ibyaha bya bose, kandi ndi umutambyi mukuru iteka ryose mu buryo bwa Melikisedeki.

Kandi nzatsinda rwose ikiyoka, inyamaswa, n'umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma. Kuko inyamaswa n'umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma bazajugunywa ari bazima muri iyo nyanja yaka umuriro yaka ibirimi bishyushye cyane by'ubururu bidashobora kuzima kandi nta n'isegonda na rimwe ryo kuruhuka rizabaho. Ariko ibikorwa byabo byose bibi bizabagarukaho iteka ryose. Umubabaro wose bateje bazawishyurwa iteka ryose, kuko Yahweh azishyura buri gikorwa kibi kugeza ku giceri cya nyuma mu bituza byabo bibi, kandi bazishyura iteka ryose ibyaha byabo.

Nuko rero Satani azabohirwa ikuzimu imyaka igihumbi, kuko ari ko byavuzwe na Yahweh. Njye, Yeshua HaMashiach, nzategeka isi mu gukiranuka umunsi umwe. Kuko kuri Yahweh umunsi umwe ni nk'imyaka igihumbi n'imyaka igihumbi ni nk'umunsi umwe. Kandi nzategekera ku ntebe ya Dawidi, kandi muri Isirayeli ni ho ingoma yanjye izaba mu gusohoza amasezerano Yahweh yahaye ba Data, kandi umunsi wanjye uregereje.

Ariko mbere urubanza rugomba kuza ku isi kandi isi izezwa buri kintu kibi cyose. Kuko nta kintu kibi kizemererwa kwinjira mu bwami bwanjye, aho hazaba amahoro n'umutekano nk'uko byasezeranyijwe na Yahweh ubwe. Kandi intare izaryamana n'umwana w'intama. Njye, ndetse nanjye, Yeshua HaMashiach, mvuga amagambo mbere y'uko aba, kugira ngo mumenye ko Imana ya Isirayeli ije gukoranyiriza intama zayo zazimiye z'inzu ya Isirayeli kuri njye, Umushumba Mukuru, kandi nzavunagura amahanga n'inkoni y'icyuma, kandi nzatsinda rwose ibyaremwe na Data mu gukiranuka n'ubwera bw'ukuri. Nta gihe kigihari.

Ndi Yeshua HaMashiach, Mesiya w'ukuri ku bwoko bwanjye Isirayeli.

Ndi we uzategeka isi mu gukiranuka ari ku Ntebe ya Dawidi.

Ndi we uzabonekera mu bicu ari kumwe n'ingabo zo mu ijuru, kuko nzazitegeka.

Ndi we uzakoraniriza hamwe abasigaye banjye nk'imbaga nyamwinshi ivuye mu mubabaro ugiye kuza.

Ndi we ugerageza imitima y'abantu, kandi nzatandukanya intama n'ihene.

Ndi Umwami w'isarura, kandi ibihe by'isarura byageze.

Ndi Umwami w'Abami n'Umutware w'Abatware, kandi ni nde ushobora guhagarara?

Dore umunsi w'Umwami ugiye gutangira ku isi mu makuba n'urubanza. Kuko umunsi mukuru w'umujinya we uje, kandi ni nde uzashobora guhagarara?
