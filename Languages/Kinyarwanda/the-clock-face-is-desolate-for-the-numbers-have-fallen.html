Ubuhamya Bubiri: Isirayeli
Ibisharira N'ibiryohereye
19 Kamena 2024
´

Isura y'Isaha Yabaye Umusaka Kuko Imibare Yaguye

Ese witeguye, Mugeni wanjye? (Kuko ndaje) Ese witeguye? Ese urumva? Ese urareba? Kuko ndaje kugutwara, bityo rero ntucike intege kandi ugire ibyiringiro byinshi n'amatsiko, kuko ubutunzi bwo mu Ijuru butegereje abizerwa banjye kandi ingororano yawe iri mu kuboko kwanjye! Ariko mwebwe abanyanze mu kutizera, ndetse nanjye Yesu w'i Nazareti, muzabona ishyano! Kandi umunsi w'urubanza rwanyu ubagezeho, yewe maraya ukomeye wicaye ku mazi menshi, kandi igihe cyawe cyagenwe kirageze.

Kurimbuka gutunguranye kubagezeho kandi abanzi banyu biteguye, nyamara muracyabaho mu byishimo nubwo muri ishyanga riri mu minyururu imbere yanjye, Kristo Yesu. Igihe cyanyu cyo kuba mu zuba cyabayeho kuko mu gihe runaka ba So bakurikiye Umwana w'Imana kandi bakitondera Amagambo yanjye. Ariko igihe cy'Itorero ryanjye kirarangiye kuko mwagurishije ubutunzi bw'amabanga yanjye, ndetse n'imibereho myiza n'urukundo rw'abantu banjye mwabigize ubucuruzi kugira ngo mubone indamu mbi n'icyubahiro imbere y'abantu.

Mwigurishije mu mururumba mubi n'ubuhemu, kandi mwarantaye, ndetse na Yesu Kristo, wapfiriye ibyaha by'isi. Ariko mwanze ijambo ryanjye, mwanze gucyahwa kwanjye, mwanze inama yanjye, mwanze intumwa zanjye, mwanze abahanuzi banjye, mwanze intumwa zanjye, kandi mwanze njyewe, Yesu w'i Nazareti, bityo mwanze Yehova, kandi none nanjye nzabanga, yewe Babuloni y'ibanga. Kandi urubanza rutangiriye mu Nzu ya Yehova!

Kuko igihe mwari gushobora kugira amahoro n'umutekano, mwahisemo gukurikira buhumyi abangije ishyanga ryanyu. Ndetse n'urumamfu rw'Abedomu rwayogoje ibihugu, kandi rwahagaritse umutima isi kuva umubi yakwirakwiza ibizira bye mu byaremwe bya Yehova.

Mumenye rero, ko njyewe, ndetse na Kristo Yesu, nzarimbura rwose Babuloni y'Ibanga n'umuriro kandi mu isaha imwe kurimbuka kwanyu kuzaza, kuko byanditswe kandi icyo Yehova yavuze nta wushobora kugihindura. Yemwe basenga ibigirwamana b'abapfapfa bo mu gihugu cya Babuloni y'Ibanga, iyaba mwari musobanukiwe ibyanyu. Iyaba mwari gutega amatwi amagambo yanjye kuko nahamagaye, ninginze, ncyashye, nteye hejuru, nerekana ibimenyetso hejuru n'ibimenyetso mu isi ariko aho kwambika imitima yanyu ibigunira, aho kwicara mu ivu ry'ukwihana ahubwo murishimisha nk'aho mureba ibitangaza by'ubumaji.

Muzabona ishyano mwebwe mwishimira ibimenyetso bya Yehova mu isanzure bitangaza urubanza rwanyu, kandi ishyano rikubye kabiri kuri wowe Babuloni y'Ibanga, kuko ufite umugabane wikubye kabiri w'urubanza ugiye kurekurwa, kandi ntuzarokoka cyangwa ngo wiyubake. Babuloni y'Ibanga uri igihugu cy'ibyishimo by'ako kanya, kandi nta bwenge ugira. Washutse amahanga mu nzira zawe z'ibibi n'ubwumvikane buke, kandi wayasindishije umwanda w'ubusambanyi bwawe. Ntimusobanukiwe inzira zanjye, kuko buri wese muri mwe agendera mu nzira ze bwite kuko bakora ibyo bashaka bya gisitani, kora icyo ushaka nicyo tegeko ryabo ryose rya gisitani bityo abagome bahinduka ubusa.

Ntimusobanukiwe inzira zanjye, kuko inzira zanjye ari inzira za Yehova, kandi ni njye nzira ukuri n'ubugingo, kuko nta muntu uza kwa Data keretse anyuzeho kandi niba mwarambonye mwabonye Data. Ariko mwafunze ubwenge bwanyu, mwazibye amatwi yanyu, mwinangiye imitima yanyu, kandi mwabumbuye amakanwa yanyu mu kutizera gutuka Imana, ndetse mutuka amasezerano y'amahoro Yehova yabahaye. Kuko njyewe Yesu Kristo, ndi amahoro yanyu!

Ariko oya, aho kungira Umwami ahubwo mukoresha izina ryanjye ryera, ndetse na Yesu Kristo, mu busa nk'irindi jambo ryo kugaragaza umwanda uri mu mitima yanyu. Ariko ntimushobora guhumanya izina ryanjye, kuko Yehova yampaye izina risumba amazina yose, kugira ngo mu izina rya Yesu Kristo amavi yose apfukame mu ijuru no mu isi. Kandi muzishyura buri jambo ry'imfabusa, kandi ibihe byanyu byageze. Kandi none, kuko mwifungiranye mu masenga yanyu mwibwira ko mushobora kwihisha urubanza rwa Yehova, noneho nzafunga urugi rwinjira mu ijuru ku batizera mu gihe nzamura abizerwa banjye mu ijuru kuko umukwe yegereje.

Mwibwira mu bupfapfa ko byose byasubiye mu buryo busanzwe, kandi ko intambara igiye kuza ishobora kureberwa kuri TV nk'izindi ntambara abayobozi banyu bashoje mu buryo butaboneye. Nk'uko mwishimiye urupfu n'irimbuka ry'andi mahanga, niko n'ishyanga ryanyu rizarimbuka rwose, kandi urupfu rwanyu n'irimbuka ryanyu bizaba indishyi ku barimbuwe mubyemeye.

Mwibwira mu bupfapfa ko mushobora kwitegura umujinya ugiye kuza mukibeshaho mu ishyamba, ahantu hatari umuriro, keretse ko muzarimbuka rwose, mugafatwa, mukicwa, mugahigwa, mukicwa n'inzara, mukarwara, kandi ibyago byinshi bizagwira abanyanga bose Yeshua HaMashiach, kuko kunyanga ari ukwanga Imana ikomeye ihoraho, ndetse na Yehova ubwe kandi nta muti uzabaho. Nimunhungireho rero nonaha!

Ntimusobanukiwe mu bupfapfa ko amahembe icumi yifatanyije n'inyamaswa yo mu isi n'inyamaswa yo mu nyanja zitaragera ku bubasha bwazo bwose, kandi ko ishyanga ryanyu ryahoze rikomeye ryashyizwe mu maboko yabo kugira ngo ririmbuke rwose. (Kandi mu kurimbuka rwose kw'amahanga yanyu inyamaswa zizazamuka!) Kuko Imana yabishyize mu mitima yabo kugira ngo basohoze imigambi yayo.

Mugoswe rwose n'amato y'abanzi banyu, mwemereye za miriyoni na miriyoni z'Ingabo Zitukura, ndetse n'ingabo z'inyamaswa kwinjira mu bihugu byanyu. Mwemereye ko bakora ibikorwa byo kwangiza, bangije imiyoboro yanyu y'ibicuruzwa, bangije ibiribwa byanyu, bangije ubukungu bwanyu, bakoze inkambi zo kwica, gukorera iyicarubozo no kwica uwo ari we wese udakora ibyo bashaka. Musobanukirwe ko n'ubwo bimeze bityo umuntu wese wambaza izina ryanjye, ndetse na Yesu Kristo, kugeza ku iherezo azakizwa. Abafata icyemezo cyabo cya nyuma cyo kwanga bazabaho iteka ryose n'ingaruka zo kutizera mu birimi by'umuriro byateguriwe umubisha n'abamarayika be, kuko nta yandi mahitamo ahari.

Ngira inama abasoma aya magambo bose kunyegera. Mureke impaka zanyu zitagira ishingiro kandi mureke gushyamirana kwanyu kutagira ishingiro hagati yanyu kandi mubwirize ubutumwa bwiza bw'agakiza nk'uko byanditswe mu byanditswe kandi mubwirize ijambo ryanjye, ndetse n'amagambo y'amagambo yanjye muri aya masegonda ya nyuma. Muyabwirize hagati yanyu, kandi muhozaho mwiteguye gufasha ababikeneye. Mureke gucira imanza bene wanyu na bashiki banyu ahubwo mufashanye mu rukundo guhagarara mu buntu n'imbabazi natanze, kuko bihagije.

Mwature kandi mwihane ibyaha byanyu kandi mubikore nonaha, nta gihe mufite! Mungire, Yesu w'i Nazareti, Umwami wanyu, kuko igihe cyarangiye. Nimuze aho ndi nonaha, kuko nta yandi mazina yatanzwe ngo abantu bakizwe, keretse iryanjye, Yesu Kristo. Nimuze aho ndi nonaha kandi muve mu gukiranirwa nanjye nzabakira kuri njye niba gusa muntangaje ko ndi Umwami mu mutima wuzuye ukwizera muzi ko nazutse mu bapfuye kugira ngo ntapfa ukundi. Narambuye amaboko yanjye ku isi igiye gupfa mu bihe byinshi bya Yubile kugeza ubu kandi ibihe by'ubuntu n'imbabazi biri kugana ku musozo, nubwo ubuntu n'imbabazi bihorana nanjye kuko ndi Umwami w'ubuntu.

Kandi none, mutegereje muri aya masegonda ya nyuma, yewe Babuloni y'ibanga. Kuko bizaba mu buryo butunguranye. Mu buryo butunguranye umuriro uzagwa, mu buryo butunguranye amazi azatwikira amajipo yanyu azamuwe hejuru y'amabuno yanyu, yewe maraya ukomeye, mu buryo butunguranye umutingito ukomeye uzabazaho kandi uzabashwanyaguza, mu buryo butunguranye itegeko rizatangwa kandi amamiliyoni azakwira mu gihugu kwica umuntu wese muzima udakora ibyo bashaka. Ntabwo bazagira imbabazi, nta kwicuza bagira, ntibashobora kwingingwa, nta mpuhwe bagira, ahubwo bakorera Satani kandi bazasohoza imigambi bahawe gusohoza, kuko bazasohoza umujinya w'Imana kuri Babuloni y'Ibanga kandi ntizongera kuzuka ukundi.

Dore! Ibihe birageze. Mwihane mugishobora guhumeka, kuko bizaba mu buryo butunguranye. Umve amagambo yanjye kandi musobanukirwe: Ubuza ni ryo mbaraga yonyine ibuza ubu bubi, kandi igihe mwibwira ko mufite ni igihe gusa cyatanzwe cyo gutegura no kwimura ibikoresho mu mwanya wabyo ku kibaho cy'isi kandi ibi bihe bikorera imigambi ya Yehova.

Hanyuma mu buryo butunguranye nzakoranya abizerwa mu Itorero ryanjye ngo bansanganire mu bicu kandi nzatwara Mugeni wanjye mu byumba bye, kuko mu nzu ya Data hari amazu menshi kandi nagiye kubategurira umwanya Mugeni wanjye kandi ijuru ryabateguriwe!

Kandi mu buryo butunguranye, ubwo ubutunzi bw'agaciro bwa Data buzaba bubitswe neza mu bubiko bwo mu ijuru (kuko bizaba mu kanya nk'ako guhumbya) buri nyuguti n'agace kayo bizasohora, ndetse na buri jambo navuze binyuze mu bahanuzi banjye b'ukuri n'intumwa, kuko ari benshi kandi benshi bari muri njye bavugira hamwe kandi bavuga bakurikije ibyanditswe byanjye. Musobanukirwe ibi, abanga Amagambo asobanutse mu byanditswe yerekeye gukoranya Itorero ryanjye ngo rinsanganire mu bicu, abo bamwe bahakana nabo bazahakanwa kandi bafate umwanya wabo ku bushake mu mujinya mu gihe ikintu nk'icyo kitari ngombwa, nyamara ni bangahe muri mwe bazanga ijwi ryanjye mu bwigenge kugira ngo bakurikire ababashimisha amatwi? Nyamara, niko bizaba.

Niba hari uvuga adakurikije amagambo yanjye, kuko njyewe Kristo Yesu, ndi Ijambo ryabaye umubiri, ntimuzanamwifurize amahoro kuko badasobanukirwa neza Amagambo yanjye kuko abahanuzi banjye batazigera bavuguruza ibyanditswe kuko ntashobora kwivuguruza. Ndi amagambo nyayo ya Yehova yabaye umubiri, kandi ndi Yeshua HaMashiach, umwana w'intama w'Imana waje kweza ibyaha by'abantu.

Ndaje nk'Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda kandi umujinya wanjye urashyushye mu isi, kuko mu gukiranuka nca imanza kandi nkarwana intambara. Mwihane yemwe babi bo mu isi kuko kwanga kwanyu kuzaba ukw'iteka. Ku iherezo ry'imyaka irindwi y'amakuba ya Yakobo Isi izaruhuka kuko hazabaho umunsi w'ikiruhuko kuko imyaka igihumbi ari nk'umunsi umwe imbere ya Yehova. Kandi Intare izaryamana n'Umwana w'Intama kuko imigambi yanjye y'Umwana w'Intama w'Imana izasohorezwa n'Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda. Igihe cyarangiye.

Njyewe Yeshua HaMashiach nohereje aya magambo binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye, kandi binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye aya magambo yoherejwe!
