AmeruKa: Yasobanuwe kandi Icirwa Urubanza! Umukwe Ategetse Umugeni we, Witegure: Ndaje Nze Kugutwara!

Umva Amagambo ya Yeshua HaMashiach, ari we Kristo Yesu, kandi wumve imvugo yanjye kugira ngo usobanukirwe inkomoko nyakuri, kamere, n'intego bya Babuloni y'Ibanga. Kuko, mu by'ukuri ari Igihugu cy'Inzoka Ifite Amababa, ndetse na AmeruKa, ndetse n'igihugu cyikurura mu ibanga, kikarya abagituye, kikica inzirakarengane, kandi kigatamba se na nyina ku bwinshi kuva mu bihe bya kera, ndetse n'ugeza mu bihe bya none. Ndetse n'igihugu cyavumwe gihumanya abagituye mu ibanga, no mu bwinshi bwo guhumanya, no kwanduza ibisigazwa by'imihango y'ibitambo by'amaraso n'ubugome byo mu bihe byashize byahambwe hirya no hino muri iri shyanga ryanduye mu birundo byinshi by'ibishushanyo, imihango, gutwikira i Tofeti, n'amaraso y'ibitambo atemba ku buntu. Ndetse ayo maraso yatose ubutaka bwayo aho isi itakambira ubutabera ku maraso yamenwe, kandi n'ubu akimenwa ku bwinshi. Ndetse na byo byo bihugu byanduye bitakambira ijuru cyane kandi mu ijwi riranguruye bisaba ubutabera muri uyu mwanya, ndetse mu gihe isi ihuzagurika cyane nk'umusinzi witegura kugwa, ndetse isaba urubanza rukiranuka rutazatinda rwose.

Nawe Babuloni y'Ibanga, ndetse nawe: Uri umugabane wanduye kandi uhumanye, ndetse n'igihugu cyatuwe bwa nyuma kuva mu minsi ya Nowa, igihugu cyahariwe Heylel Ben Shachar n'inzira z'inzoka, igihugu cyatose amaraso y'inzirakarengane binyuze mu mihango n'ibikorwa by'urubyaro rwanduye rw'impiri n'abahumanya bagiye guhumanya mu bihe bya kera. Ndetse n'igihugu cyabitswe mu ibanga n'amaboko yihishe y'abaguye, ndetse cyabitswe kandi giteganyirijwe kuba Atilantisi yabo Nshya, ndetse n'ishyanga ryakozwe kandi rihariwe kuzamuka kw'inyamaswa iva mu nyanja n'inyamaswa iva mu butaka. Ndetse n'ishyanga ryiyita irya Gikristo, ariko mu by'ukuri rikaba ishyanga ry'abasatanisiti b'ibanga bashinze kandi bibera neza mu nzego zaryo zanduye kandi zihumanya (kuva ku muto kugeza ku mukuru) kandi ni izo nzego ziyoborwa n'amatsinda y'ibanga na yo ubwayo yiyeguriye Heylel Ben Shachar (uwo bita Lusiferi mu kinyoma no mu bujiji), ndetse bakiyita abamurika n'abajijutse mu bujiji bwabo, ndetse no mu kwibeshya kwabo n'ibinyoma byabo aho bo ubwabo babohewe iteka ryose ku ndahiro zabo zanduye, babeshya, nyamara igihe cyose bakomeza kubeshywa n'abo baboheweho iteka nk'abana b'icyaha ku baguye iteka ryose.

Bimenyekane ko, Babuloni y'Ibanga ari Leta Zunze Ubumwe za Amerika, ndetse kera yitwaga Ameru-Ca, Igihugu cy'Inzoka Ifite Amababa, yikuruye kandi yihindura ya maraya ukomeye wicara ku mazi menshi kandi akaba ishyanga ryakozwe, kugira ngo inyamaswa iva mu nyanja n'inyamaswa iva mu butaka zizazamuke zivuye mu ivu ryayo mu bihe byazo byo guhishurwa. Ndetse n'ishyanga ry'abasambanyi, ndetse n'ishyanga ry'ubusambanyi, ishyanga mu by'ukuri ari indaya ikomeye yiyita umukurikira ukiranuka wa Yesu w'i Nazareti; ariko mu by'ukuri rikaba ishyanga ryubatswe n'abatagira Uruhutare nyakuri rw'Urufatiro, ndetse rwa buye rikuru ry'imfuruka abatari abizerwa basitaraho, ari we Yesu w'i Nazareti ari we Yeshua HaMashiach.

Mu by'ukuri, AmeruKa yahindutse izi Leta Zunze Ubumwe z'iki gihe, kandi ni ishyanga ryashinzwe n'amaboko yabo yanduye kandi yihishe, ndetse n'igihugu cy'amatsinda y'ibanga yiyeguriye inzoka mu myambaro y'ibanga; kandi uko imbaraga z'Umusaraba w'Iroza zateye AmeruKa niko n'igifuniko cyabo cya nyuma cya Atilantisi Nshya cyashinzwe. Ndetse n'umugambi wahishwe igihe kirekire wari guhuza no kuvanga ayo madini y'inzoka ku isi yose binyuze mu kurimbura no gutamba benshi mu banyamadini b'iyo mico yaguye, na benshi bo muri iyo mico ubwayo bari kugerageza mu bwibone no mu bupfu guhagarika iyo nzoka ubwayo biyeguriye mu ndahiro nyinshi z'amaraso kugira ngo bayikorere byuzuye, badasobanukiwe kamere y'icyo kiremwa nyakuri biyiboshyeho, nubwo, ibisigazwa by'abo bantu biri muri ibyo bihugu kugeza n'uyu munsi.

Nuko rero uri AmeruKa: Kuko mbwira umugabane uturuka mu majyaruguru ukagera mu majyepfo no kuva iburasirazuba ukagera iburengerazuba ndetse ukaba igihugu cyahariwe ya nzoka ifite amababa ya kera yahumanyije kandi yanduza ibi bihugu ku bw'imigambi yayo, ndetse nk'uko ubwami bwagiye buzamuka bukagwa mu mipaka yayo, ndetse n'ubwami bwinshi butanditswe bw'inzoka, ndetse n'ishyanga ry'inzoka ifite amababa kandi iguruka ryahariwe imiryango iyoborwa n'abagore n'iya demokarasi aho udutsiko tw'abagizi ba nabi twashoboraga gutegeka munsi y'ububasha bwa Heylel Ben Shachar. Byongeye kandi, urubyaro rwe rw'impiri rwahumanyije rwose ibihugu bya Babuloni y'ibanga, ndetse rikaba igihugu cyatuwe ubwo umuntu yazererega ku isi kuva aho Inkuge yaguye ku Musozi wa Ararati icyo gihe cyose gishize. Kuva aho, abantu bategetswe na Yahweh, NDI UWONDI Ukomeye, babohewe munsi y'isezerano rya Nowa gukwira no gutatana no kongera gutuza isi mu gukiranuka kw'isezerano; nubwo urubyaro rwa Hamu rwo rwikoranyirije hamwe mu kutumvira gukomeye kugira ngo rwiheshe izina mu Gihugu cya Shinari, rwibwira ruti: Nimuze, twiyubakire umunara muremure kugira ngo twirinde, kandi kugira ngo mu by'ukuri turwane n'ijuru kuko Nimurodi yigize umuhigi ukomeye imbere y'Uwiteka, ndetse umuhigi ukomeye w'abantu yahigaga nk'imikino.

Nuko abantu baza mu gihugu cy'inzoka ifite amababa baturutse iburengerazuba, bagenda bagana iburasirazuba bambuka ikiraro cy'ubutaka cya Bering nk'uko kizwi muri iki gihe, kandi baturutse mu nyanja baturutse iburasirazuba bagana iburengerazuba bambuka Atilantika, banyuze mu nzira zitandukanye z'ubucuruzi n'ingendo baje muri ibi bihugu byabitswe na Heylel Ben Shachar ku bw'imigambi ye mibisha. Ndetse n'igihugu abaguye bigaruriye byuzuye mu isi yariho icyo gihe, ndetse n'igihugu aho bahambye amabanga menshi bazemererwa gutaburura mu buryo butunguranye kandi butitezwe, ndetse nk'uko umurinzi wanjye akuweho kandi nk'uko ubutegetsi bwabo bugufi bwemerewe ku bw'agakiza ka Yakobo, kandi kugira ngo na bo bagwe mu rubanza rw'umuriro nk'uko uru rubanza rukomeje kuva mu minsi ya Nowa, kugira ngo barimburwe rwose, kuko ibihe by'abaguye byageze ku iherezo koko, kandi ni njye Kristo Yesu, ndetse n'Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda uzamenagura umutwe w'iyi nzoka ya kera mu iherezo ry'urubanza.

Nuko rero Kanani yabonye ibyo abarinzi bahambye, kandi yayobowe n'irari ry'umutima we ubwo yateraga kandi akiba igihugu kitari icye cyo guturamo mu kwica isezerano rya Nowa. Nyamara yararikiye iminsi ya kera yari yararimbuwe muri wa mwuzure mukomeye aho isi yariho icyo gihe yarimbutse bitewe n'uko ba Satani bashutse abarinzi baguye kwivanga n'abagore kugira ngo babyare urubyaro rwabo rwavumwe. Ndetse urwo rubyaro nta kindi ruri cyo uretse ibibyarwa by'indabare n'ibicucu, ndetse n'ikigana cy'ubuzima aho ibizira byabo bibohewe iteka ryose gukora irari ribi kandi ryangiritse rya ba se, kandi gukora irari rya ba se babohewe iteka ryose. Ku bw'ibyo, sobanukirwa ko uru rubingo rubi, ruzira, ibizira byuzuye, nta muzi cyangwa ishami rufite mu ijuru, ku isi, ndetse no munsi y'isi kandi ruzarandurwa rwose, iteka ryose, munsi y'izuba hakurikijwe isezerano rya Adonai-YHVH NDI UWONDI Ukomeye! Kandi bazabohwa batwikirwe muri ya miriro mikomeye yo kwishyura aho urunyo rwabo rudapfa kandi abajugunywe mu miriro bose bazahabwa ibyaha byabo inshuro nyinshi mu bituza byabo bwite mu rubanza rw'umuriro rw'iteka kandi rukiranuka batazigera bacika.

Nuko rero, Heylel Ben Shachar, wa gikomangoma gito cyatsinzwe, ndetse wa mwibone mukomeye n'umuhumanya w'ibiboneye byose, yagiye gutera urubingo rwe mu busitani bwa Yahweh, NDI UWONDI Ukomeye, ndetse bwa busitani bwatewe ubwo Elohim yavugaga ati habeho urumuri nuko urumuri rubaho. Kuko mu ntangiriro, Elohim yaremye ijuru n'isi, kandi isi yari itagira ishusho kandi irimo ubusa ubwo umwijima wazaga hejuru y'imuhengeri. Uwo mwijima wari Heylel Ben Shachar na kimwe cya gatatu cy'abamarayika be bakurikiye ukwigomeke kwe bakajugunywa ku isi mu isoni nyinshi, no gutsindwa. Kandi ni Heylel Ben Shachar, ndetse wa gikomangoma gito cyatsinzwe cyaboroze mu isoni no gutsindwa ubwo cyajugunywaga ku isi kivuye mu ijuru ry'amajuru mu burakari bukomeye, ndetse kibikiwe urubanza rwa nyuma rw'umuriro mu miriro y'uwo munsi mukomeye, ndetse iyo miriro ikaba yongerewe imbaraga n'ibirimi by'umuriro by'ubururu bishyushye cyane kandi biboneye byiteguye kwakira abifatanyije n'ukwigomeka kwa Satani bose kuva ku muto kugeza ku mukuru mu bwami bw'abaguye.

Elohim yaravuze ati "Habeho urumuri nuko urumuri rubaho!" Hanyuma Elohim ashinga, yongera gushinga isi ku itegeko rye gusa, no ku Magambo ye gusa, no ku nama ye gusa niho irema ryakozwe, riremwa, kandi rishyirwaho. Ndetse no ku Magambo ye akomeye kandi y'iteka n'imbaraga n'inama ye gusa. Kuko YHVH, NDI UWONDI Ukomeye, adakeneye umujyanama nubwo intebe ye y'ubwami igoswe n'ibihumbi icumi incuro ibihumbi icumi, kandi nta kiremwa kimeze nk'Imana y'Iteka yo Gukiranuka, nubwo babeshya kandi bakiyoberanya mu buryo bubabaje! Kandi uko niko isi yaremwe n'ibiyirimo byose kandi iyo si yahawe Adamu, kuko umuntu yaremewe isi, kandi isi yaremewe umuntu, mu by'ukuri waremwe mu ishusho y'umuremyi we, ari we YHVH - NDI UWONDI Ukomeye!

Nyamara iyo nzoka y'uburiganya yagabye igitero ku murage wa Adamu mu mayeri yayo, ndetse igitero n'ihutaza byaburijwemo byuzuye i Kaluvari, ubwo njye, Yesu w'i Nazareti, navugaga Amagambo y'iteka nti, "Birarangiye!"; nuko rero, niguze muntu, isi, n'ibiyirimo byose mu maraso yanjye y'impongano. Ku bw'ibyo, isi ni iy'Uwiteka n'ibiyuzuye byose, kandi ndaje nk'Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda guca urubanza no kurwana, nuko rero mwumve ukwivuga kwanjye, yemwe batuye isi kuko ibihe byanyu byageze!

Nuko abaguye bagiye guhumanya no guhindura irema kurikura mu butungane bwaryo bw'umwimerere ryaremwe. Uretse ko, bo ubwabo badashobora kurema kuko YHVH, NDI UWONDI Ukomeye, atigeze abaremana ubwo bubasha cyangwa ubushobozi. Ku bw'ibyo, sobanukirwa ko aba baguye birarira ko ari imana kandi bose ari abiyoberanya biyoberanya. Sela.

Ku bw'ibyo, umva Amagambo ya Yeshua HaMashiach, ndetse Kristo Yesu ubwo nje guhishurira itorero ryanjye, umubiri wanjye, ndetse n'umugeni wanjye ukuri ku byerekeye ibyo bihugu byangiritse kandi byanduye aho benshi muri mwe bavukiye, kandi batuye. Kuko ba So bazerereye isi mu gihano munsi ya Adonai-YHVH, NDI UWONDI Ukomeye, kandi byari mu gihano gikomeye aho Yahweh yajugunye imiryango y'amajyaruguru ya Isirayeli mu mahanga yitwaje urwandiko rwo gusenda. Aho, batatanyirijwe mu mahanga barazerereye kandi bararindwa, kandi barabikwa kuva icyo gihe, kandi benshi muri bo ubu ni igice cya Isirayeli y'Imana, iteka ryose! Ndetse barinzwe n'umurezi wabo, kuko bakomeje amategeko ya Mose ubwo bajyaga mu bihugu by'amajyaruguru nyuma yo kuneshwa mu kutumvira n'amaboko y'Abashuri. Nuko imiryango y'amajyaruguru irasendwa kandi ijugunywa mu mahanga, nyamara yari yaramaze gutatanyirizwa mu bunyage ubwo Abanyababuloni baneshaga kandi bagahana Ubwami bw'Amajyepfo, ndetse n'abatumvira mu ba Data bo mu miryango ya Yuda na Benyamini n'abandi, bakoze Ubwami bwa Yuda mu minsi ya kera. Ndetse ari na byo byabaye Yudaya ubwo Idumeya na Yuda byahuzwaga ku mbaraga z'inkota.

Uko niko intama zazimiye z'Inzu ya Isirayeli zatatanyirijwe mu mahanga bitewe no kutumvira kwazo, nubwo ibisigazwa byagumye mu gihugu cy'isezerano kugeza n'uyu munsi. Ubwami bw'Amajyepfo bwatatanyirijwe mu mahanga y'amajyaruguru, mu bihugu bya Gogi na Magogi, hirya no hino mu Burayi, no muri Amerika niko bagiye. Nuko Abavandimwe babo n'abo mu miryango yabo bo mu Bwami bw'Amajyepfo na bo baneshwa n'Abanyababuloni. Nyamara, baragarutse kubaka urusengero rwa kabiri kandi mu gihe runaka batatanyirizwa mu mahanga mu minsi ya Vespasian na Titus, n'ikindi gihe mu kwigomeka kwayobowe na Simon Bar Kokhba, ubwo abandi bantu banjye benshi batatanyirizwaga mu bihugu byo mu majyepfo n'iburasirazuba bwa Yerusalemu mu majyaruguru ya Afurika no muri Alegizandiriya ya Egiputa n'ahandi hantu henshi bazerereye mu gihano cyabo. Nyamara na bo barinzwe na Mose, nubwo benshi bari mu bubata bwa Esawu, na Ashkenaz, kandi abandi benshi muri bo barashutswe kandi bategereje Mahdi wabo, aho gutegereza Yeshua HaMashiach ari we Mesiya wabo nyakuri, n'umucunguzi wa Isirayeli wasezeranyijwe kera na Adonai-YHVH, Sela.

Nyamara, nk'uko byanditswe, niko biri koko, ko abantu banjye ahanini bari mu bujiji kandi bahumye igice ku byerekeye abo bari bo nyakuri n'inkomoko yabo. Ndetse baciwe ku muzi wa Yahweh, NDI UWONDI Ukomeye, uwo igiti cy'umunzenze kavukire gisangira ku burumbuke bw'umuzi, ndetse uwo muzi ukaba Yehovah-YHVH, NDI UWONDI Ukomeye.

Nyamara, benshi mu bantu banjye bemeye kwangishanya mu bujiji mu binyoma n'urwango kandi bitera ubujiji bwimbitse ku nkomoko yabo nyakuri, ndetse nk'uko bigishijwe kwemera ibinyoma by'uru rubyaro rw'abatambyi b'inzoka mu gihugu icyo ari cyo cyose bashinzemo, ndetse urwo rubyaro rw'abatambyi b'inzoka bakorera Heylel Ben Shachar, wa museraphim waka umuriro kandi waguye ubikiwe imiriro y'igihano cy'iteka n'urubanza iyo miriro ikaba ifite igihano gikaze kuri iyi nzoka mbi yiyanduje mu kwigomeka kudafite ishingiro, ndetse igumishijwe mu mimerere yo gutsindwa no kugwa itazigera yikuramo.

Nuko abenegihugu banjye n'abavandimwe bigishijwe kwangana binyuze mu binyoma byinshi byateguwe kugira ngo byigishe rubanda. Nuko rero byaranditswe ngo: Ibi bintu bitandatu Uwiteka arabyanga: yego, umutima we wanga birindwi: amaso y'ubwibone, ururimi rubeshya, n'amaboko avusha amaraso y'inzirakarengane, umutima ugambirira imigambi mibisha, amaguru yihutira gukora ibibi, umutangabuhamya w'ibinyoma uvuga ibinyoma, n'uteza amakimbirane mu bavandimwe.

Nuko rero, abana b'Ubwami bw'Ijuru bigishijwe mu ibanga, uburiganya, n'urwango rwinshi ko bo ubwabo ari bo bagize abavandimwe babo abacakara mu gihe cy'ubucuruzi bw'abacakara bwambukiranya Atilantika. Uretse ko iki gikorwa cyakozwe na Esawu na Ashkenaz mu gukorera Heylel Ben Shachar. Aho bashinja abana b'Ubwami bw'Ijuru ibyaha by'urubingo rwibye abana kuri ba nyina na ba se, kandi rwibye ba se na ba nyina n'abana mu bihugu bitari ibyabo; hanyuma bakagurishwa mu bucakara muri AmeruKa, (n'ahandi) mu rwango rwinshi kuri uru rubingo n'abaturanyi babo bwite bari imiryango y'abaturanyi barwanaga babagurishije mu maboko y'aba bagabo babi mu bihe bya kera. Nuko rero sobanukirwa ko ibi bikorwa bibi byanditswe neza mu ijuru kandi ubutabera no kwishyura bizagwa mu buryo butunguranye ku batuye isi bagishakisha irimbuka rya Yakobo; ahubwo irimbuka ryabo bwite rizabagwira mu buryo butunguranye kandi butitezwe ubu, Sela.

Nyamara, benshi muri mwe mugumye mu bujiji kuko ibara ry'uruhu rwanyu ritandukanye n'iry'abavandimwe banyu ku bw'ubugeni bwa Yahweh n'igishushanyo cyiza. Nyamara, nta kindi kiremwa ku isi cyiyanga gityo bitewe n'ibinyoma by'inzoka mu byaremwe bya Elohim byose, nyamara abantu banjye bemeye kubohwa n'ubwo bupfu n'ibinyoma bidafite ishingiro by'ubucucu budafite ishingiro byatanzwe n'abaguye ubwabo ku bw'irimbuka ry'abantu banjye.

Mwihane ubwo bupfu nonaha!

Nta gihe mufite!

Nyamara, benshi muri mwe mukomeza kuba injiji ko muri Kristo Yesu nta Muyahudi, cyangwa umunyamahanga, cyangwa imbata, cyangwa uwigenga, ahubwo hariho icyaremwe gishya muri Kristo, ndetse icyaremwe gishya aho ibisekuruza bya kera bya Adamu biseswa iteka ryose binyuze muri uwo mwuka w'Ubwami bw'umwana. Kuko amasezerano yahawe Aburahamu wizerwa azaragwa n'abakiranutsi babeshwaho no kwizera, nk'uko Aburahamu wizerwa yabayeho, ndetse uko kwizera kuyobora byuzuye mu maso ya Yesu Kristo mu kuvuka gushya.

Keretse, benshi muri mwe muhitamo ubujiji mu nzira zanyu zidafite ishingiro zitari izanjye kandi mugomba kwihana byihuse! Ndetse n'ubwo bimeze bityo, kuko nategetse ko mukunda Adonai-YHVH n'umutima wanyu wose, ubugingo, ubwenge, n'imbaraga zanyu zose, kandi mugakunda bagenzi banyu nk'uko mwikunda. Itegeko benshi muri mwe mwanga mu rwango rwanyu rw'amoko murwanya ubwoko bumwe bwa Adamu bwambaye amakote y'uruhu afite amabara atandukanye n'ayanyu mu bwiza bw'irema rya Yahweh. Mwihane!

Kandi niko bimeze, mu minsi ya kera, urubyaro rwa Kanani rwariyononnye rwikomatanya n'abaguye, ndetse no mu minsi ya kera, kandi uko bihumanyije mu cyaha biyeguriye byuzuye abaguye mu gihugu cya Shinari, ndetse no mu gicucu cy'uwo munara munini w'i Babuloni, ndetse uwo munara YHVH, NDI HO Ukomeye, yagushije mu gutsindwa kwabo.

Kuko ari ko byanditswe: Nyuma y'uko isi irengerwa n'umwuzure mu minsi ya Nowa, isi yagabanyijwe n'ubutaka bw'umurage mu bahungu ba Nowa mu minsi ya Pelegi. Kandi nuko Yafeti ajya mu murage we mu bihugu byo mu majyaruguru, kandi abantu bari bafite ururimi rumwe, n'abahungu ba Hamu bateranira mu gihugu cyabaye Babuloni. Kandi abatware ba Nimurodi bajya inama hamwe na Futi, Misirayimu, Kushi, na Kanani n'imiryango yabo kugira ngo bubake umujyi wabo n'umunara ukomeye ndetse umunara muremure kugira ngo barwanye ijuru kandi kugira ngo baterane ngo biheshe izina mu kwigomeka ku itegeko rya Yahweh ryo kororoka no kugwira kugira ngo bongere kuzuza isi. Ariko niko inzira z'umuntu wa kamere utagira kwizera zimeze, we, mu irari rye n'ubwibone akorera icyaha bityo agakorera se w'icyaha ari we satani, binyuze mu kutumvira kwa muntu kuri Yahweh, NDI HO Ukomeye, kubera icyaha mu mubiri w'umuntu wa kamere.

Kandi mu rubyaro rwa Nimurodi rwakuriye mu gukiranirwa: Bivugwa ko mu babi havamo gukiranirwa kwinshi. Kandi nuko Nimurodi yahumanyije isi mu nzira z'abaguye kandi mu bapfumu be n'abarozikazi havuye amashuri yabo y'ibanga ya kera aho abapfumu b'abaguye bigishaga amabanga yabo atagira umumaro ku bazajugunywa mu muriro hamwe nabo iteka ryose mu byaha bya ba Satani, kandi iteka bazifatanya nabo mu murage wabo w'iteka. Kandi Nimurodi yazamutse mu bwami bwa mbere nyuma y'umwuzure wa Nowa, ndetse aho Nimurodi yategetse isi mu gukiranirwa; ndetse ubutegetsi abaguye bashaka kwigabiza byuzuye ku isi, nyamara nta gihe cya zahabu bazagira ahubwo urubanza rukomeye.

Nuko, Yahweh mu rubanza, yatumye isi yiyasa imira kimwe cya gatatu cy'umunara wabo, n'umuriro umanuka mu ijuru utwika ikindi kimwe cya gatatu cy'umunara wabo, n'igice cya gatatu cyemerewe gusigara nk'urwibutso rubarwanya. Ni cyo cyatumye witwa Babuloni, kuko Yahweh ari ho yasobanyirije indimi z'isi yose: kandi aho ni ho Yahweh yabatatanyirije ku isi yose. Atari uko mwihisha mu kintu cyitwa iminsi itatu y’umwijima kitazabaho kandi ari inkuru y’impimbano ya Yezuite ihimbwe ngo ivuge ibinyuranye n’Amagambo ngo “nta wumenya umunsi cyangwa isaha”, kuko kugira ngo habe iminsi itatu ibanziriza umunsi n’isaha bitazwi icyo gihe cyahita kimenyekana uhereye ku itangira rya iyo minsi itatu. Ni ikinyoma n’inkuru y’ubupfapfa itazabaho!

Nuko, mwitegure kandi mwiyitegure bakunzi banjye, kuko ndaje kubatwara. Kandi sinzabasiga mu rubanza rw’abanyabyaha kandi mwese muzatungurwa musange muhagaze mu cyumba cy’intebe ya YHVH, aho ingabo zo mu ijuru zitegereje kubakira mu cyubahiro kinshi, ingororano, ibyishimo, n’ubugingo buhoraho butanzwe byuzuye na Adonai-YHVH, mu maso ya Yesu Kristo, mu mibiri yanyu mishya y’icyubahiro muhita muhagaze mukaba mucunguwe rwose.

Mwiteguye, bakunzi banjye?

Kuko mu kuboko kwanjye, impanda. Impanda isangijwe. Impanda y’icyubahiro. Impanda idasanzwe. Impanda yateguriwe amatwi y’ab’indahemuka, isa idahumanye kandi isukuye. Ndetse impanda itezwe ku murongo w’uruvange rujya gusa n’irwumvwa gusa n’abari muri Kristo, ndetse bose nabateye kumva no kwakira amajwi yayo ahindura ubuzima azabahindura mwese bitunguranye kandi noneho iteka ryose muhindurwe, mutazongera kwangirika cyangwa guhindurwa kuva kuri uku kudatungana gutangaje nzabaremera noneho mu mbaraga zikomeye kandi za nyuma. Kandi muzatangarira iteka ryose igihangano gishya gikomeye muzahindurwamo mu mbaraga zikomeye, zera, z’ubutungane kandi ziremya iteka ryose.

Ameni, bibe uko mu gihe cy’itegeko cyasezeranyijwe cy’akataraboneka.


Byasinywe,

Yeshua HaMashiach, ari we Yesu Kristo
