Ubuhamya Bubiri: Itorero
Mu Mirimo y'Intumwa Pawulo
6 Gicurasi 2024

Ku Bakozi banjye bizerwa: Ndabatumye!

Ku bakozi banjye bizerwa ndetse n'abavuye i Lawodikiya!

Mugeni wanjye, umva ijwi ryanjye unyegere, kugira ngo nkomore ibikomere byawe kandi nguhe imbaraga mu bushobozi bwanjye. Witinya! Uburakari bwanjye ntiburi kuri wowe, kuko nzabwira nti: Wabyitwayemo neza, mugaragu mwiza kandi wizerwa; wabaye uwizerwa mu bintu bike, nzakugira umutware w'ibintu byinshi: injira mu byishimo bya databuja. Kuko mfite amagambo akomeye, amagambo yo guhana, ku batumvira; ariko icyo gihano si wowe kigenewe kuko uri uwizerwa, nubwo ukwiye kwitondera amagambo yanjye, kugira ngo utagwa.

Mugeni wanjye wizerwa, uri imboni y'ijisho ryanjye, kandi nta gucirwaho iteka kuri wowe, kuko uri muri Kristo, kandi wubatse ubuzima bwawe kuri njye, Yesu Kristo, ndetse n'ibuye rikuru ry'imfuruka abubatsi b'ibinyoma banze. Komeza kuba uwizerwa muri njye, kuko ndikuza aho uri mu mbaraga zikomeye, kuko iherezo ry'ibihe riri hafi. Ndagutuma mu mbaraga zikomeye no mu mirimo utigeze witekerezaho, kuko utari uzi ko wavukiye mu ibanga ry'Imana mu mwuka wawe wa mbere, ariko wakuze cyane mu buntu bwanjye, kandi noneho urabona. Kuko ubuntu bwanjye ari ubuntu bukomeye budashobora gutsindwa, kandi imbabazi zanjye zihoraho iteka ryose.

Dore! niko Yeshua HaMashiach avuga. Nyumva kandi wandike amagambo yanjye ku bisate by'inyama by'imitima yanyu. Kuko ndabatumye; yego, ndabatumye aya magambo, kandi abo ntumye bazabona igihamya kidashidikanywaho cy'ubushake bwanjye bukomeye. Ndabatumye mu mirimo y'abakomeye banjye, imirimo batangiye mu myaka ya yubile myinshi ishize, kandi iyo mirimo irakomeza kugeza n'uyu munsi. Kuko igitabo cy'Ibyakozwe n'Intumwa ntikirangira kwandikwa kugeza igihe Umugeni wanjye azaba ari kumwe nanjye. Hanyuma muzasobanukirwa byuzuye ibitabo byo mu ijuru, kuko ibikorwa byanyu byanditswemo, kandi twese tuzishimira ubudahemuka bwanyu imbere ya Yahweh ubwe, kuko yishimana natwe kandi ari muri mwe mwese.

Dore! Ntumye bamwe muri mwe mu murimo wa Pawulo nk'ibisonga by'amabanga yanjye ndetse nk'intumwa mu mahanga. Dore! Ntumye bamwe muri mwe mu mwuka wa Eliya kugira ngo muhangane na Yezebeli, umuhanuzikazi w'ibinyoma, kandi mumpanurire, njye Kristo Yesu! Dore! Ntumye bamwe muri mwe mu murimo wa Filipo umubwirizabutumwa, aho nzabajyana ahantu hatandukanye mufite amagambo y'ubutumwa bwiza bwanjye ku minwa yanyu, ahuje n'ibyo abatarakizwa bakeneye, kuko muzabakiza mubwiriza umusaraba! Dore! Ntumye bamwe muri mwe mu murimo wa Petero, kuko yaragiye umukumbi wanjye akawugaburira ijambo ry'Imana ryuzuye. Kuko abakozi b'abanebwe b'i Lawodikiya bagaburira umukumbi wanjye ibiryo bitagira intungamubiri, ndetse n'amagambo adafite kwizera n'imbaraga. Dore! Nabagize abigisha kugira ngo mwigishe kandi muyobore ubwoko bwanjye mu nzira zo gukiranuka, kuko muzabigisha uko mbayobora! Kuko muzabaragira uko mbayobora! Kuko muzababwiriza ubutumwa uko mbayobora! Kuko abahanuzi banjye bazavuga amagambo mbaha, adahindutse mu busobanuro no mu mutima! Kuko intumwa zanjye zizamurika urumuri rw'ubutumwa bwiza bwanjye bw'ubwiza bw'ibanga rikomeye ry'Imana, kugira ngo igitambaro cya Mose gikurwe ku mitima yanyu. Kuko ubuhamya bw'i Korinto burangurura ijwi! Ariko n'uyu munsi, iyo basoma ibya Mose, icyo gitambaro kiba kiri ku mitima yabo. Nyamara, iyo bahindukiye bagana Umwami, icyo gitambaro gikurwaho. Kandi Umwami ni we Mwuka, kandi aho Umwuka w'Umwami ari, haba hari umudendezo. Ariko twese, tureba ubwiza bw'Umwami mu maso hadatwikiriwe nk'abareba mu ndorerwamo, duhindurwa gusa na we, kuva mu bwiza tujya mu bundi bwiza, nk'abakoze n'Umwuka w'Umwami. Njye, Kristo Yesu, nkuyeho igitambaro cyanyu!

Dore, ndabatumye muri iyi minota ya nyuma mbere y'uko mukoranirizwa aho ndi mu kuza kwanjye. Ndashaka ko mwumva ijwi ryanjye kandi mugakurikiza amabwiriza yanjye, nk'uko mubikora mu budahemuka, Mugeni wanjye wizerwa, kuko muri abizerwa! Ntimugende mu murimo mbatumyemo mu bwenge bwanyu. Ntimugende mu murimo mbatumyemo mu mbaraga zanyu. Ntimugende mu murimo mbatumyemo mu bushobozi bwanyu. Ntimugende mu murimo mbatumyemo mu ntege zanyu. Mwumve amagambo yanjye n'umutima wanyu wose! Kuko njye, ndetse nanjye, Kristo Yesu Umwami wahawe ikuzo, ndababwira nti: Ndi ubwenge bwanyu, kandi ndi imbaraga zanyu, kandi ndi intege zanyu, kandi ndi ubushobozi bwanyu: gusa ntimushidikanye!

Mukore imirimo nzabayobora gukora, kandi mwite ku bantu mboherereje. Ese muzicisha bugufi ku bantu boroheje? Ese muzicarana, mubwirize, mukize, kandi musabane n'abatagira aho baba? Kuko ntumye bamwe muri mwe kuri iyo mpamvu. Ese muzinginga abakozi b'i Lawodikiya mu kwicisha bugufi nk'uko umuntu yinginga Se? Kuko izo ari zo nzira zanjye. Ese muzavuga amagambo mbahaye, nubwo mwaba mutayasobanukiwe neza cyangwa mutayemeranya na yo? (Mwemeze amagambo yanjye mukoresheje ibyanditswe.) Kuko mbatumye gukora ubushake bwanjye, atari ubwanyu. Ese muzaragira umukumbi mu mbaraga zikiza z'umuriro mu mwuka wanyu nzacana muri mwe? (Kandi Umushumba mukuru naza, muzahabwa ikamba ry'ubwiza ridashira.) Mukore imirimo yanjye, Mugeni wanjye, kandi mureke imirimo yanyu, kuko nta gihe gisigaye, kandi amatara agiye kuzima vuba.

Musobanukirwe, ndabatumye mu murimo wanjye, kandi muzabona ingororano ikomeye niba muri abizerwa, kuko mufite ubutunzi bwinshi bwabitswe mu ijuru, kandi amazu menshi mu nzu ya Data yateguriwe mwe. Kuko amakuba ya Yakobo ari iminsi irindwi y'imyaka irindwi, aho buri mwaka ubarwa nk'umunsi. Uko ni ko n'ubukwe bw'Umukwe n'Umugeni we bumeze. Kuko hazaba iminsi irindwi y'imyaka irindwi mu ijuru, aho buri mwaka ubarwa nk'umunsi, kandi muzahorana nanjye iteka, Mugeni wanjye. Hanyuma tuzagendana!

Mugende mu nzira zanyu kandi mugende mu nzira mbayoboramo. Mugandukirane mu rukundo no kwicisha bugufi umwe ku wundi. Mwite ku busitani bwanjye, kuko nateye ubusitani bw'ubugingo buhoraho umubi adashobora kurandura cyangwa ngo aburimbure. Muzarya ku giti cy'ubugingo yananiwe kubabuza, kandi nta bugingo bwuzuye mu nyanja y'umuriro ya Satani, aho agiye, ahubwo hari igihano, kwicuza, isoni, n'umubabaro gusa. Ibyo bintu si ibyanyu, Mugeni wanjye, kandi urupfu rwa kabiri nta mbaraga rubafiteho, kuko mwavuye mu rupfu mujya mu bugingo muri njye, ndetse Yesu Kristo, umukiza wanyu n'Umwami.

Nuko rero mugende aho mbatumye, kandi mukorane n'abantu mboherereje, n'abo mboherereza. Kuko batabagana ku bw'impanuka, ahubwo ni uko namaze gutangira kubayobora aho muri. Mubeho mu ibanga ryanjye kandi mubeho nk'uko babayeho mu Byakozwe n'Intumwa, kuko muri igice cya nyuma cyandikwa uko mumbeshaho, kandi vuba aha muzabibona nimugera mu ijuru nimara kubatwara aho ndi, kuko muri abanjye! Muri umugeni wanjye, Itorero ryanjye ry'ubwiza, iryo nziyegurira, ridafite ikizinga cyangwa umunkanyari, cyangwa ikindi nk'icyo: ahubwo kugira ngo mube abera kandi mudafite umugayo, kuko nabogeje mu maraso yanjye y'impongano!

Ndamuhamagara, Mugeni wanjye, kandi ndabahamagarira mu mirimo mutigeze murota ko mushobora gukora, kuko abakozi b'i Lawodikiya bagerageje kubicaza mu mwijima. Ntimuhunge imbaraga ndikucana muri mwe, kuko ukuboko kw'Umwami, ndetse ukuboko kwanjye kw'umugisha, kuri kuri mwe. Ntimuhunge imirimo yanjye ndikucana mu mwuka wanyu. Kuko nzacana umuriro ukomeye mu mwuka wanyu, Mugeni wanjye, umuriro uzayengurutsa ibintu byose bibadindiza. Ntimutinya! Mugendere mu mbaraga zanjye, kuko abakozi b'i Lawodikiya batagira kwizera bagerageje kubemeza ko mudafite imbaraga, kandi hari ingaruka zikomeye kuri bo nibatihana.

Dore! Ndabatumye mu mirimo aho abandi bananiwe, kandi muzagenda mu mwanya wabo, kuko muri abizerwa: Gusa murimbure ubwibone bwanyu, kugira ngo mutagwa mu mutego w'ikibi nk'uko byabagendekeye. Ntimufate indimi zanyu, kuko nazibohoye. Muvuge amagambo nabahaye, kandi mumbanjirije mu mbaraga zikomeye. Ese mutekereza ko nrobanura ku butoni? Kuko ubu ni igihe cy'ibitangaza bidasanzwe, nk'uko nabyanditse mu Byakozwe n'Intumwa: Kandi Imana yakoreshaga amaboko ya Pawulo ibitangaza bidasanzwe: Ku buryo batwaraga ibitambaro cyangwa amapironi bivuye ku mubiri we bakabishyira abarwayi, indwara zikabavamo, n'imyuka mibi ikabavamo. Mugeni wanjye w'ukwizera gukomeye, nsohorezaho imirimo mbahamagarira, kuko turimo gusoza imigambi yanjye muri mwe, kandi izuba rirarenga ku gihe cyanjye cy'ubuntu, kandi vuba aha ijoro rizahita rigwa ku isi yose, kuko mugiye kugenda. Dore! Njye, Yesu Kristo, nzakora ibitangaza byinshi bikomeye, ndetse n'ibitangaza bidasanzwe binyuze mu maboko yanyu, kuko ubu ni ubutumwa bukomeye bwa nyuma ku Itorero ryanjye mbere y'uko mutwarwa mu bwiza.

Mugende, mu mahoro, kandi nzabayobora. Mumenye ko muzarwanywa cyane n'inshuti, umuryango, ndetse n'abanzi. Ibi ni ibihe byo gutandukanya aho nkorera igiti cyanjye cya elayo nkuramo abahemu n'inzira zabo zitagira kwizera, kuko batiteguye umukwe, kandi bazafata imyanya yabo hamwe n'abakobwa b'abapfu. Kandi ndikweza abizerwa banjye ibyaha byabo kugira ngo bashobore kwera amashami mashya yera imbuto nyinshi. Ntimutinya, mutekereza ko mudakwiriye ibitangaza. Kuko ndabyemera, ubwanyu nta n'umwe ushobora gukora ibitangaza; ariko nzaha ibimenyetso, ibitangaza, n'imirimo ikomeye myinshi mu maboko y'abizerwa banjye. Mugende mubwire imisozi, ivaho itabwe mu nyanja, kandi ntimushidikanye ahubwo mwizere Yahweh, nanjye, Yeshua HaMashiach.

Nta nyundo y'umwuka yo kuzunguza ihari, bityo mureke imbaraga zanyu bwite. Kuko mushobora kugenda hejuru y'amazi niba mugumishije amaso yanyu kuri njye kandi ntimushidikanye kandi ntimurebe ibumoso cyangwa iburyo ahubwo mukagumisha amaso yanyu kuri njye, kandi ntimuzarohama. Mutegeke imyuka, kuko inyoborwa nanjye bityo ikaba iyoborwa namwe. Mutegeke imyuka kugenda; ntimusabe, ahubwo mutegeke! Ntimuyemerere kuvuga, ahubwo muyicecekeshe; ntimuvugane na yo, kuko amagambo yayo ari uburozi; ahubwo muyirukane mu butayu aho idafite inzu, kandi vuba aha izatabwa mu muriro. Mukize abarwayi! Muvugishe indwara mu izina ryanjye, kandi muyibwire igende; ntimutinya, kuko nzabaha amagambo. Ntimutinya! Ntimugomba kuvuga amagambo y'ubuhanga; muvuge gusa amagambo yo kwizera nzashyira mu mitima yanyu kandi ntimushidikanye.

Dore! Mugende ku bwanjye, Mugeni wanjye, mudashidikanya na gato. Ndabatumira mu mirimo ndikurangiza, aho benshi muri mwe mwasanzwe muri abakozi bagenzi banjye. Haracyari imitima yo gukiza, ndetse n'iyo Satani agerageza guhisha. Nzabakura mu ngoyi ze binyuze mu mirimo yanyu myinshi ikomeye n'ibikorwa tuzakorana. Mugende, Mugeni wanjye, mudashidikanya na gato, kuko nabateguye ubuzima bwanyu bwose kuri iyi minota mike y'imbaraga no kumvira, kandi mwatojwe neza nanjye, n'abo natumye kwita ku mitima yanyu.

Mugeni wanjye wizerwa, ndagukunda, kandi uri ibyishimo byanjye n'umunezero wanjye. Ndakumwenyura buri munsi, nubwo utambonesha amaso yawe, kandi uracyagira kwizera. Ndaririra ibikomere byawe, nubwo ndira mu bucece inshuro nyinshi. Numva ugutaka kwawe usaba ubufasha, kandi ndi umufasha uhora uhari mu gihe cy'amage. Ndagukunda kandi ndakwitaho, kandi uri imboni y'ijisho ryanjye, kandi vuba aha tuzaba turi kumwe. Nzi ko unaniwe, kuko wabaye uwizerwa ku Jambo ryanjye n'izina ryanjye, bityo nzaguha imbaraga mu mbaraga z'Imana itagira iherezo kandi ihoraho, ndetse Yahweh, kuko nta wuhwanye na we! Ntimucike intege, kuko nubwo umubiri wanyu waba unaniwe, nzabaha imbaraga mu bushobozi bwanjye, kuko njye, Yeshua HaMashiach, mbitangaje!

Ndikuza aho muri mu mbaraga zikomeye.

Ndabatumye mu mbaraga zikomeye.

Ndamuhamagara, Mugeni wanjye, mu mirimo mutari muzi ko mufite kugeza ubu.

Ntumye bamwe muri mwe gusoza imirimo y'abahemu, kandi muzabona ingororano yabo.

Ndikugabanya abahemu.

Ndikweza abizerwa banjye ibyaha byanyu kugira ngo mwera imbuto nyinshi.

Ndi umufasha uhora uhari mu gihe cy'amage.

Mugende mu nzira yanyu, muhagarare mu Rusengero, kandi mubwire abantu amagambo yose y'ubu bugingo. Kuko muri urusengero rw'Imana ihoraho!
