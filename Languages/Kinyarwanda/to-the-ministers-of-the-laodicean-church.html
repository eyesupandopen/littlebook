Ubuhamya Bubiri: Itorero
Mu Mirimo y'Intumwa Pawulo
6 Gicurasi 2024

Ku Bakozi b'Itorero rya Lawodikiya: Nimwumve Amagambo yanjye!

Mugeni wanjye n'Itorero ryanjye, nimunyumve mu rukundo mbakunda, bana banjye, kandi mutege amatwi imvugo yanjye. Nimwumve Amagambo yanjye, yemwe bakozi banjye b'i Lawodikiya, namwe abo nahaye impano. Kuko nahaye abantu impano, bamwe mbaha kuba intumwa, abandi abahanuzi, abandi ababwirizabutumwa, abandi abungeri, n'abigisha. Nabahaye izi mpano kugira ngo abera batunganirizwe umurimo wo kugabura iby'Imana, no kubaka umubiri wa Kristo. Kuko ni iki nabwiye intumwa zanjye n'abigishwa mu byanditswe by'inkuru nziza ya Yohana: Kandi dore, nzaboherereza isezerano rya Data. Ariko mugume mu murwa wa Yerusalemu, kugeza ubwo muzambikwa imbaraga zivuye mu ijuru.

Kandi mu Byakozwe n'Intumwa zanjye handitswemo isohozwa ry'iri sezerano: Umunsi wa Pentekote usohoye, bose bari hamwe mu mutima umwe. Nuko mu ijuru humvikana gitumo umuriri umeze nk'uw'umuyaga uhuha cyane, wuzura inzu yose bari bicayemo. Haboneka indimi zigabanyijemo kabiri, zimeze nk'umuriro, ururimi rujya ku mutwe w'umuntu wese muri bo. Bose buzuzwa Umwuka Wera, batangira kuvuga izindi ndimi, nk'uko Umwuka yabahaye kuzivuga. Kandi ishingwa ry'Itorero ryanjye ryanditswe mu byanditswe kugira ngo mumenye isohozwa ry'iri sezerano rikomeye ry'inkuru nziza, ko nabujuje imbaraga zivuye mu ijuru, ndetse no mu kuvuka gushya, ndetse no muri njye, Kristo Yesu Umwami wanyu. Kandi ni njye mutwe w'umubiri, ari ryo Torero: ni njye ntangiriro, imfura yo kuzuka mu bapfuye, kugira ngo muri byose mbe uwa mbere: Kuko byashimishije Data ko muri NJYE kuzura kose kuba.

Bakozi banjye b'i Lawodikiya, kuzura kwanjye ntikuba mu matorero yanyu mwiremeye mu ishusho yanyu, kuko mpagaze ku rugi ndakomanga! Bakozi banjye b'i Lawodikiya, sinigwijeho mu bwikunde kuzura kose kwa Data kuri muri njye, ahubwo nasutse umwuka wanjye mbaha imbaraga, kandi nabashyizeho ikimenyetso cy'uwo mwuka wera w'isezerano. Nta kintu na kimwe gishobora kwica icyo kimenyetso, nubwo mushobora kwigira mu buhakanyi niba ari ko muhisemo. Ariko ikimenyetso cya Data, ari we Yahweh Imana ihoraho, ntigishobora kwicwa; kuko bidashoboka kwica ikimenyetso cy'Imana, kandi muri amahoro muri njye niba munyizera, kuko amasezerano yanjye ari yego na amena!

Bakozi banjye b'i Lawodikiya, impano nabahaye zirengagijwe n'imigenzereze yanyu y'akazuyazi, kandi mwazimije umwuka nabahaye, kandi mwanze gufungura impano nabahaye, mureka impano zanjye ziguma zifunze mu kutita no kurengagiza. Iki si ikintu gito cyo gufata uko kibonetse, kuko ari icyaha gikomeye kibabazwaho, kandi ntimwumvira ijwi ryanjye. Ntimwubaha amagambo yanjye, kandi ntimwubaha cyangwa ngo mwigishe inzira zanjye. Kuko ni iki nandikishije Pawulo ku muyobozi ukiri muto w'Itorero ryanjye ryari rikimara gushingwa, ari we Timoteyo: Ntugahinyure impano ikurimo, iyo wahawe binyuze mu buhanuzi, no kurambikwaho ibiganza n'abakuru. Ibyo bintu ubitoze, kandi ubyitangire, kugira ngo kunganirwa kwawe kugaragare muri byose.

Yemwe, iyaba mwarasomye amagambo yanjye mubyumva! Yemwe, iyaba mwibukaga amagambo yanjye mu kwizera, ariko ntimusobanukirwa kandi ntimwizera! Yemwe, iyaba mwumviraga amagambo yanjye, kuko kunyumvira, njye Mwami Yesu Kristo, ari ukumvira isoko y'ubugingo, kandi mu nda zanyu hazatemba imigezi y'amazi y'ubugingo, kuko nabahaye umwuka wanjye. Ariko aho gusoma amagambo yanjye, muhanze amaso za mugaragaza (screens) zanyu mu kutumvira kutagira ubwenge; muva kuri mugaragaza mujya ku yindi, mushimishwa n'icyaha! Kandi muri abapfapfa cyane kuba mutekereza ko kutumvira kutagira ubwenge ari ukwizera gukora muri njye, kandi mwarashutswe. Mwirengagije impano nabahaye, ndetse n'umuntu mushya, ahubwo mwamaze igihe mutoza kamere y'umuntu wanyu wa kera mu busa mushaka guhaza imibiri yanyu.

Aho kwitoza mu by'umwuka, mwitoza mu busa imibiri yanyu yononekaye kandi irimbuka, kuko imyitozo y'umubiri igira umumaro mu gihe gito gusa, kandi imibiri yanyu irarimbuka. Kuko ijwi ryanjye ryumvikana mu bihe byose kuva ku rwandiko rwa Pawulo rwandikiwe Abefeso: Amaze gukuraho mu mubiri we urwango, ari rwo mategeko y'amategeko ari mu mabwiriza, kugira ngo muri we areme umuntu umwe mushya mu babiri, bityo azane amahoro. Nimunyumve, bana banjye, kuko mbakunda kandi nita ku bugingo bwanyu, ndetse namwe banya Lawodikiya banjye b'akazuyazi. Kuko muhitamo kugendera mu muntu wanyu wa kera n'inzira ze, ndetse n'umubiri wanyu wononekaye, kuko muha umubiri wanyu icyubahiro cyose. Ahubwo, mukwiye kwerekeza ubwenge bwanyu n'imitima yanyu kuri njye no ku ijambo ryanjye ryose: Kuko umuntu atazatungwa n'umutsima gusa, ahubwo azatungwa n'ijambo ryose riva mu kanwa k'Imana. Kuko binyuze mu kumvira amagambo yanjye mweruye (ndetse n'Ijambo ry'Imana, kuko ndi Ijambo ryabaye umubiri kandi ntuye hagati yanyu no muri mwe), mugendera mu mwuka ntimugendere mu mubiri.

Kuko nabwiye Itorero ry'i Korinto ibi bikurikira: Ariko twese, tureba ubwiza bw'Umwami mu maso hagaragara nk'abareba mu ndorerwamo, duhindurwa gusa na we, tuva mu bwiza tujya mu bundi bwiza, nk'uko bikorwa n'Umwuka w'Umwami. Bana banjye, inzandiko zanjye ku Itorero ryanjye (ndetse n'inzandiko z'urukundo noherereje Umugeni wanjye) ni indorerwamo y'uko muri by'ukuri, kuko uko muri by'ukuri guhishwe mu ibanga rikomeye ry'Imana. Binyuze mu kumvira amagambo yanjye n'amategeko yanjye muzagendera mu mwuka, kandi ntimuzaha ibyishimo umubiri wanyu wononekaye. Ibyanditswe byose bigomba kubahwa no kwigwa, ariko muri ibanga ryari rihishwe mu Mana kandi ubu ryahishuwe, kandi mugomba kwiga ibyo nahishuriye Pawulo kugira ngo musobanukirwe iremwa rishya ndikubarema muri njye, Kristo Yesu.

Bakozi banjye b'i Lawodikiya, hari ikosa rikomeye rwose muri mwe. Mwatakaje icyubahiro cyose ku magambo yanjye, kandi mwirengagiza impano nabahaye kugeza n'ubwo mutagikiza ahubwo mushyikiriza iteraniro ryanjye imiti (pharmakeia) n'icyuma cy'abaganga; kuko iyo muba munyumvira, nari gutanga ibitangaza bigakorwa n'amaboko yanyu. Ariko mwubaha amagambo y'abungeri b'abahakanyi kuruta amagambo ya Yeshua HaMashiach, kandi iteraniro ryanjye ry'i Lawodikiya rishyura ikiguzi cyo kutumvira kwanyu; kuko mutacyabitaho, ahubwo mubareka bakaborera mu cyaha kidafite imbaraga. Abungeri b'abahakanyi bamenyekana binyuze mu magambo nahaye Yohana mu rwandiko rwe rwa mbere: Bana bato, ni igihe cy'imperuka: kandi nk'uko mwumvise ko Antikristo azaza, na n'ubu hariho ba Antikristo benshi, ari byo bitumenyesha ko ari igihe cy'imperuka. Bavuye muri twe, ariko ntibari abacu: kuko iyo baba abacu, nta gushidikanya baba baragumanye natwe: ariko bavuyemo kugira ngo bigaragare ko bose atari abacu.

Ntimusobanukirwa ko abayoborwa n'imyuka ya antikristo bazana undi Yesu mu mwanya w'umwungeri w'ukuri, ari njye, Kristo Yesu? Kuko aba ari abanzi b'umusaraba wa Kristo, kandi bandwanya, kandi bishyira mu mwanya wanjye, bashaka kwigarurira ubutware bwanjye, ibintu bidashoboka. Aba binjiye mu matorero yanyu, mu mashuri yanyu ya tewolojiya, mu bigo byanyu bitandukanye, kandi bayobora amatorero yanyu binyuze mu kwemera kwanyu gukorera munsi ya 501(c)(3), kuko abari munsi y'iryo sezerano bose bapfukwa iminwa na za leta z'isi. Za 501(c)(3) zanyu zigaragaza umururumba wanyu w'indamu mbi, kuko mwisambanyije munsi y'inzira za Balamu, kandi mutanga amagambo y'ibinyoma kugira ngo mubone amafaranga kugira ngo mwikubire amafaranga yose abana banjye bato babahaye mu busambanyi bwanyu busonewe imisoro! Mwabaye inshuti y'isi, kandi isi izaza guhiga ikintu cyose cyitiriwe izina ryanjye igihe ubuza azaba akuweho. Mubuze kurobanura mu by'umwuka kandi muri impumyi! Mubuze ubwitange bwo kurinda umukumbi aya masega aryana, kuko muha agaciro amazina y'icyubahiro yomekwa ku mazina yanyu, amazina ntabahaye; nyamara murayararikira. Kuko nababwiye kurarikira cyane impano ziruta izindi: Kandi nyamara ndabereka inzira irushaho kuba nziza: inzira irushaho kuba nziza mwanga mu migenzereze yanyu y'ubunebwe n'akazuyazi.

Ntimukibwiriza ibyaha no kwihana ibyaha, ahubwo mubwiriza imigani yumvikana neza itari iyanjye, kandi imigani yanyu igizwe n'amagambo y'ibinyoma kandi iyobora mu nzira z'ibinyoma; kuko ntimuri ikindi uretse ababara inkuru zishimisha amatwi y'abanebwe. Ndavugana namwe, ntumwa zanjye: kuko amaso yanyu ari umwijima, kandi ntimukibona urumuri rwanjye, kandi mutangaza ibinyoma! Ndavugana namwe, bahanuzi banjye: kuko muvugira undi Yesu, kandi mwirengagiza amagambo yanjye, kandi ntimunzi, kandi muhanura iby'ahazaza h'ibinyoma! Ndavugana namwe, babwirizabutumwa banjye: Kuko mubwiriza amahanga inyigisho z'ibinyoma z'abadayimoni, kandi mutuma banywa amazi yanduye, kuko mubuzuza imyuka mibi! Ndavugana namwe, bungeri banjye: Kuko mwita ku irari ryanyu bwite, kandi mwagize inda zanyu imana yanyu n'imyanda yanyu ya filozofiyo yo kwifasha; wa mupfapfa we, ntushobora gukiza intama zanjye n'ururimi rubeshya! Ndavugana namwe, bigisha banjye: Kuko mutakigisha amagambo atunganye y'ubutumwa bwiza bwanjye, ahubwo mwigisha amagambo y'ubwumvikane bw'amadini, kandi hari ikosa rikomeye rwose muri mwe mwese, bakozi banjye b'i Lawodikiya. Mwese musangiye indwara imwe: Ntimukyubaha amategeko ari mu Ijambo ryanjye, kandi ntimukyubaha amagambo ku giti cyayo ari mu byanditswe byanjye.

Mutekereza mu bwibone ko muri abasemuzi kuko mushobora gutoranya ibisobanuro mu nkoranyamagambo z'isi n'ibitabo by'isi bihuza amagambo kugira ngo bihuze n'intego zanyu: Ibitabo ntananditse, kuko nandika ibyanditswe binyuze mu bahanuzi banjye, kuko umwuka w'ubuhanuzi ari ubuhamya bwa Yesu! Abasemuzi bavuga ururimi rumwe gusa, kandi mwabaye urw'amenyo! Ahubwo, mwubaha amagambo y'abungeri b'abahakanyi, ndetse n'indyarya z'urumamfu zita agaciro. Binjiye mu materaniro yanyu kugira ngo biyigise nkamwe (kuko muri ingano nakoranyiriza mu kigega cyanjye niba mugarutse kuri njye). Ariko ahubwo ni urumamfu, bene Data b'ibinyoma babaye abakozi b'ibinyoma babayobya. Ni igihingwa cy'igihimbano kandi cy'uburozi!

Aba babi ntibakiva mu Itorero ryanjye kugira ngo bigaragare, ahubwo murabatumira mukabahata gusangira namwe kandi mukubaha amagambo yabo y'ibinyoma n'indimi zabo zibeshya kuruta amagambo nabaha, ndetse n'amagambo y'Imana Ihoraho, ndetse n'amagambo ya Yahweh! Mucuruza amagambo yanjye kugira ngo mubone icyubahiro cyose n'ishimwe byanjye, njye Mwami wanyu Yesu Kristo. Ndababwira ukuri ko mwabonye ingororano yanyu, kandi nzabambura ingororano yanyu nyihe umuntu wari uwizerwa mu mwanya wanyu. Kuko mutakivugira njye, kandi ngomba kuza mu Itorero ryanjye nk'ijwi ry'urangurura mu butayu, kuko mwamfungiranye urugi mu maso, nubwo nkiri aho mpagaze nkomanga! Isoni nyinshi n'agasuzuguro biri kuri mwe keretse mwihannye!

Niba mwirengagije amagambo yanjye ntimwumve ijwi ryanjye, niba mukomeje mu migenzereze yanyu y'akazuyazi, nzabasigira amakuba ya Yakobo; kuko nababwiye mbere, banya Lawodikiya banjye, nzabaruka mbakure mu kanwa kanjye keretse mwihannye! Kandi nanone navuze ku byerekeye uwo muhanuzikazi w'ibinyoma Yezebeli igihe nandikiraga Tuwatira, kuko ni iki nabwiye Yohana Uhishurirwa?

Nyamara, mfite ibintu bike nkunenga, kuko wihanganira uwo mugore Yezebeli, wiyita umuhanuzikazi, akigisha kandi agashuka abagaragu banjye gusambana, no kurya ibyatambiwe ibishushanyo bisengwa. Kandi namuhaye igihe cyo kwihana ubusambanyi bwe, ntiyihana. Dore, nzamujugunya ku buriri, n'abasambana na we mbashyire mu makuba akomeye, keretse bihannye imirimo yabo.

Ntimwasomye aya magambo? Ese muri abibone ku buryo mutekereza ko Yahweh cyangwa Yeshua HaMashiach barobanura ku butoni? Mutekereza ko nubaha amazina y'icyubahiro mwiha kugira ngo muyamanike hejuru y'imitwe y'abantu banjye, uko mwitegeka nk'abatware ku murage w'Imana?

Pasiteri uyu, Musenyeri uriya; iyo mutirengagiza impano zanjye, impano yanyu iba yarabaciriye inzira imbere y'abakomeye kandi ntimuba mwarakeneye amazina yanyu y'icyubahiro atagira umumaro, kuko mba narabahesheje icyubahiro namwe mukakimpesha, ariko muransuzugura! Hari ikosa rikomeye muri mwe, kandi uburakari bwanjye bubagurumaniraho, kuko mwananiwe inshingano nabahaye ahubwo mukaba abahakanyi, ndetse mukayobora abana banjye bato mu ruzi aho mwabambye amahema yanyu. Muhitamo amazi yanduye yo mu ruzi rwanyu kuruta amazi yanjye meza y'umwuka wera, ayo uzayanywaho wese atazongera kugira inyota ukundi!

Muha abana banjye bato amazi yanduye, mabi mu bidendezi bimenetse, kandi uburakari bwanjye buri kuri mwe! Nzambura ingororano yanyu nyihe undi, kuko nk'uko nabavuzeho: Ni ukuri, mufite ingororano yanyu. Mufite ingororano yanyu y'imyanya mukunda yo kwiyubahisha, kandi mufite ingororano yanyu y'amazina y'icyubahiro atagira umumaro, kandi mufite ingororano yanyu y'indonke mbi, kandi muzabona ingororano y'uburakari bwanjye keretse mwihannye mukangarukira mwicishije bugufi. Kuko ingororano y'uburakari bwanjye ari umujinya, kandi nimunzanira ibiti byanyu, ibyatsi, n'ibikenyeri, bizashya bibe ivu imbere ya bose, kandi muzagaragara ko muri abakozi b'ibinyoma!

Hari ikosa rikomeye muri mwe mwese, ndetse namwe, ntumwa zanjye, bahanuzi, bavugabutumwa, bungeri, n'abigisha! Ntimukigumya mu isano ikiza nanjye, Yesu Kristo, ahubwo mwarandekuye; kuko ndi umutwe w'umubiri, kandi mugomba kunshyira hejuru ya byose! NTIMUSHOBORA kunkorera niba mutanazi ijwi ryanjye. Muri ibipfamatwi, kandi mufite ubumuga bw'amatwi bwa burundu keretse mwihannye, kuko nzakiza amatwi yanyu imyuka y'ibitotsi kugira ngo mwumve ijwi ryanjye, kuko ndikuvugana namwe! Keretse mungize umutwe wanyu kandi mukubaha Amagambo yanjye, nzabasiga muri impumyi no mu ruzi hamwe n'ababayoje. Muri impumyi, kandi mufite ubumuga bw'amaso bwa burundu keretse mwihannye, kuko nakiza amaso yanyu imyuka y'ibitotsi kugira ngo musome amagambo yanjye kandi mubone mu maso hanjye: ariko hagati aho ibitotsi bike, no kwifumbata bike.

Mwahindutse urw'amenyo n'igisebo, kuko inzira zanyu z'akazuyaze zihumanya izina ryanjye ryera. S'uko gusa mutangira, Kristo Yesu, nk'umutwe wanyu (kuko iyo mubikora, mwari kumvugira), ahubwo murushaho kujya mu buhakanyi muhumanya amagambo yanjye yera, kuko ndi Jambo wigize umubiri! Ndababaza, ntimwasomye imiburo yanjye? Kuko ndahamiriza umuntu wese wumva amagambo y'ubuhanuzi bw'iki gitabo, Niba umuntu wese azongera kuri ibi bintu, Imana izamwongeraho ibyago byanditswe muri iki gitabo. Kandi niba umuntu wese azakuraho amagambo y'igitabo cy'ubu buhanuzi, Imana izakuraho umugabane we mu Gitabo cy'Ubugingo, no mu murwa wera, no mu bintu byanditswe muri iki gitabo. Ese muri abapfapfa, ibipfamatwi, n'impumyi ku buryo mutumva ko natanze uwo muburo atari gusa ku gitabo gikomeye cy'ibihishuwe byanjye, Ibyahishuwe! Ahubwo uwo muburo ureba ibyanditswe byera byose! Kuko Imigani yanjye irangurura mu mihanda ku bafite amatwi yo kumva bose: Amagambo yose y'Imana ni meza kandi aratunganye; kuko ari ingabo ikingira abayiringira bose. Ntukagire icyo wongera ku magambo yayo, kugira ngo itagucyaha ukaboneka ko uri umunyabinyoma.

Bakozi banjye b'i Lawodikiya, bagiranye ubucuti n'isi. Bakozi banjye b'i Lawodikiya, bigira ba shebuja ku murage w'Imana, mwiyerekana ko muri abakomeye. Bakozi banjye b'i Lawodikiya, mwirengagije umwuka wanjye naremye muri mwe. Bakozi banjye b'i Lawodikiya, mwiha amazina y'icyubahiro atagira umumaro n'ibirango byo gomeka ku mazina yanyu. Bakozi banjye b'i Lawodikiya: Nimwumve amagambo yanjye kandi munsubize, kuko buri rurimi ruzatura!

Ni inde wabahaye uburenganzira bwo guhindura amagambo yanjye, ndetse n'Ijambo ry'Uwiteka natanze binyuze mu ntumwa zanjye? Inzira zanyu, yemwe bakozi b'i Lawodikiya b'abanebwe, si nshya, kuko akimara gukwirakwizwa amabaruwa yanjye mu Matorero yanjye mashya yanditswe mu Byakozwe n'Intumwa n'ahandi, udutsiko tw'Abagunositike twayobowe n'abahanuzi b'ibinyoma b'Abagunositike twahinduye amagambo yanjye twongera kwandika ibyanditswe, kuko bavuye mu Itorero ryanjye kugira ngo bose bamenye ko bari abungeri b'abahakanyi!

Muzasarura ingororano z'inzira zabo, kuko mwifatanyije nabo, kandi mubaho mu nzira zabo, kandi mugaragaza imyuka mibi ya Yezebeli, umuhanuzikazi w'ikinyoma mwita uwera! Mbona imirimo yanyu mibi, kandi yanditswe mu ijuru mu buryo burambuye ibarwanya! Mugomba kuvuga amagambo mbaha, kandi mugomba kuyavuga mu budakemwa, mu bunyangamugayo, mudahinduye n'ijambo na rimwe ry'ubuhanuzi ubwo ari bwo bwose! Sinivuguruza, kuko nababwiye nti: Ujye ugira umwete wo kwishyira Imana nk'ushimwa, umukozi udakwiriye gukorwa n'isoni, ukwiriranya neza ijambo ry'ukuri. Amagambo menshi yarahinduwe mu byanditswe byanjye mu myaka ishize, kandi niba muziga kandi mukubaha amagambo yanjye natanze, nzabayobora mu kuri kose, kuko ari ko natanze umufasha.

Nzabereka, niba muzumva amajwi menshi nabasigiye. Mukurikire inyandiko natanze muri Antiyokiya, kuko ari amajwi y'abatangabuhamya benshi, kandi abatangabuhamya banjye ntibabeshya kandi ntibavuguruzanya. Murebe kuri Textus Receptus kandi mwirinde Bibiliya z'abahakanyi: mwige kugira ngo musobanukirwe! Ariko mwe, bakozi banjye b'i Lawodikiya, ntimwubaha amagambo yanjye, kandi muhindura amagambo yanjye kugira ngo ahuze n'inyungu zanyu mbi, mwigisha abantu banjye kugira ngo bakomeze mu byaha, ndetse mubayobora kure mu nzira zanyu zanduye! Hari ikosa rikomeye muri mwe, kandi ntimukurikira inzira zanjye, kuko mwibona kandi mufite imitima yinangiye, kandi nzabarekera kurimbuka k'umubiri keretse mwihannye mugahindura inzira zanyu. Kuko jyewe, Yesu Kristo, nohereje aya magambo! Gusa ntimuyirengagize, kandi mwihane mwambaye ibigunira mwiyisize ivu. Mwihane ku mugaragaro imbere y'amatorero yanyu kandi mwature ibyaha byanyu ku bo mwayobeje, kuko nzabaka igitereko cy'amatara cyanyu. Mwihane kandi mugende mutunganye, nanjye nzagarura itara ryanyu ryaka umuriro mushya, kuko ari jye uzacana umuriro w'umwuka wanjye mu bugingo bwanyu.

Mugaruke ku rukundo rwanyu rwa mbere, kuko murankunda, gusa atari mu buryo bw'ubumenyi. Ntimugashidikanye ku magambo yanjye, kuko nzaza vuba kuruta uko mubitekereza, kuko nzaza nk'umujura nijoro. Ndetse no mu ikosa ryanyu, mutekereza ko umujura nijoro ari umuntu winjira mu idirishya rwihishwa nk'umugizi wa nabi. Wa mupfapfa we! Iyaba mwasobanukirwaga inzira z'Abaheburayo. Umujura nijoro yari umutambyi mukuru wazaga ataburijwe kureba niba abatambyi be ari indahemuka mu nshingano zabo z'urusengero, kuko inshingano zimwe zakorwaga ijoro ryose kugeza zirangiye hakurikijwe amategeko. Iyo umutambyi mukuru yasangaga umutambyi ashinzwe asinziriye mu kazi, yamutaga ikara ryaka mu myenda ye, kugira ngo umutambyi yikunkumure imyenda ye yaka umuriro yiruke asohoka mu rusengero yambaye ubusa kandi afite isoni. Ntimuzabe musinziriye nza, cyangwa muzagwa imbere yanjye mwambaye ubusa kandi mufite isoni mu isoni mbi cyane zishoboka, kuko nzabakorwa n'isoni nkabambura ingororano zose. Igihano cyanjye kiri kuri mwe kandi ukuboko kwanjye kuremereye kuri mwe, kandi keretse mwihannye, muzagira amakuba akomeye, akomeye mwari gushobora gucika. Mwihane, kugira ngo nshobore kubategurira umwanya ku meza yanjye, kandi muzifatanya n'Umugeni wanjye mu gushyingiranwa nanjye, Kristo Yesu, Umukwe.

Ndi Jambo wigize umubiri ubana namwe.

Ndi umwungeri mwiza kandi intama zanjye zumva ijwi ryanjye.

Ndi Umwana w'Intama w'Imana woherejwe ku bw'ibyaha by'isi.

Ndi umutambyi mukuru iteka ryose mu buryo bwa Melikisedeki.

Ndaje kandi ingororano yanjye iri kumwe nanjye.

Ndi ugenda hagati y'ibitereko by'amatara birindwi n'abamarayika barindwi.

Ndi umhanuzi wahagurukijwe mu bavandimwe uwo Mose yavugaga: Nimwumve ijwi ryanjye mu kumvira.

Ndi uwahaye abantu impano, nuko rero ntimwirengagize impano natanze.

Ndaje vuba kuruta uko mubitekereza; mwitegure, Mugeni wanjye!

Jyewe, Umwami Yesu Kristo, nohereje aya magambo kandi mbaye ijwi ry'urangurura mu butayu; kuko Itorero ryanjye ry'i Lawodikiya riri akazuyaze kandi vuba aha rizaba ryambaye ubusa kandi rifite isoni keretse bihannye. Mpaze ku rugi ndakomanga mu kanya gato gasigaye! Nta mibare ikiri ku isaha, kuko imibare yaguye; kandi nta mpeke z'umusenyi zikiboneka igihe impeke ya nyuma izaba inyuze mu isaha y'umusenyi, kandi igiye kugwa.
