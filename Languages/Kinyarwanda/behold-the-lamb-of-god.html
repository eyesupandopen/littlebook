Ubuhamya Bubiri: Isirayeli
Ibisharira N'ibiryohereye
16 Ugushyingo 2024

Dore! Umwana w'Intama w'Imana!

Nimwumve Amagambo ya Yeshua HaMashiach kandi mutege amatwi amajwi y'Amagambo yanjye, mukebere imitima yanyu kwakira Mesiya wanyu nyakuri, Yemwe Isirayeli, kandi mwumve imvugo yanjye kugira ngo mube abanyabwenge mu nzira zanyu, kandi kugira ngo munyemerere nyobore ibirenge byanyu mbakure muri iyo nzira ngari y'irimbukiro mukurikira, mbayobore mu nzira ifunganye y'agakiza ari yo nzira zanjye kandi ari yo ndi. Kuko ndi uwo Yahweh yatumye ngo abakize, kandi ndi urubyaro rwasezeranyijwe rw'umugore ruzamenagura umutwe w'inzoka, kandi ndabahamagara ngo muze kuri njye bavandimwe banjye, ndetse n'abavandimwe banjye ndi umucunguzi wabo. Nuko rero ndabategeka gukebwa imitima yanyu no kureka impaka zanyu zitagira umumaro kugira ngo mwera imbuto muri Mesiya wanyu nyakuri, bityo mwumve amabwiriza yanjye atangirwa kumvira kwanyu kugira ngo mubone kubaho imbere ya Yahweh NDI HO Ukomeye, ndetse no kunyakira nka Mesiya wanyu w'iteka ryose uwo mu by'ukuri, mu by'ukuri: Ndi we.

Kandi nk'uko Yahweh yoherereje ba So amategeko ya Mose (amategeko ari umurezi wanyu ugamije kubageza kuri Kristo), ni ko na Yahweh yantumye nk'uko byasezeranyijwe kera ubwo yasezeranyaga agakiza ba So abo mbwira abana babo ubu, ndetse n'abana ba Isirayeli nkunda urukundo rwaka iteka. Nyamara mu bujiji no kutizera nanzwe na ba So nshyirwa mu maboko y'abanyabyaha kugira ngo bankorere n'ubugome bwinshi n'inzika ibyo amategeko yababuzaga gukora; kandi no kugira ngo bagaragare nk'abakiranutsi imbere y'abantu bo mu gihugu ubwo bicaga umwana w'intama w'Imana babyumvikanyeho na ba So, ndetse n'icyaha benshi muri mwe mucyumvikanaho mu bujiji.

Nyamara, wumve Isirayeli ko ntagufashwe ku ngufu n'abanzi banjye n'abanzi banyu. Mwumve, ko ntagufashwe ku ngufu, cyangwa ngo mfatwe n'uburiganya kandi nta ntwaro iremwe ngo indwanye ishobora kugira icyo intwara, kuko ari ko Yahweh yabitangaje kandi icyo Yahweh atangaje nta wushobora kugihindura! Koko, Isirayeli, ndi uwo wabwiye Yuda Isikariyota ubwo nagambanirwaga n'ukuboko kwe ku biceri mirongo itatu by'ifeza, nti icyo ukora, gikore vuba! Kuko igiciro cyagombaga guhabwa nyabuja w'umugaragu wiswe n'imfizi ni cyo buzima bwanjye bwagurishijwe mu maboko y'abancumise, gitanzwe ndetse n'amaboko y'abatambyi bakuru n'Urukiko rwa Sanhedrini abo nahanganye nabo kubera inzira zabo mbi z'uburyarya. Nuko Yuda angambanira mu maboko ya Esawu, ndetse n'uwo wiyitiriye igihugu cy'isezerano nanone ku ngufu, ndetse n'uburenganzira Esawu yagurishije agati k'umugati. Nyamara, nemeye ku bushake bwanjye gufatwa n'abanzi banjye mu gihe nari gushobora gutoroka byoroshye, kandi mu by'ukuri, mu by'ukuri, iyo nsaba Yahweh, yari kumpa itegeko rya legiyoni zirenga cumi n'ebyiri z'Abamarayika, ariko se Ibyanditswe byari gusohozwa bite, nuko birarangira.

Nyamara, ni ko bimeze, ko imigambi yanjye n'inzira zanjye byumviswe nabi n'abavandimwe banjye ndetse n'abo mu ishyanga ryanjye nkunda iteka ryose n'urukundo rwimbitse kandi rwaka rudashobora kuzimywa cyangwa kuneshwa, kuko ndi Urutare rwanyu rw'Urufatiro urwo nta mwubatsi ashobora kubakaho rutarimo, kandi urwo abatizera basitaraho. Kuko byari mu rukundo rwanjye kuri mwe, n'abakomoka kuri Adamu bose baneshejwe n'abaguye, ko nitanze ku bushake bwanjye mu maboko y'abanzi banjye kugira ngo mbacungure mu muvumo w'amategeko.

Kandi iryo ni ryo tegeko ryanjye ry'iteka, ngo mukundane, nk'uko nabakunze. Nta muntu ufite urukundo ruruta uru, rw'uko umuntu ahara amagara ye ku bw'incuti ze, ibyo nakoze ku bushake bwanjye nziko ntamviye ubushake bwa Data muntu yari gushyirwa kure iteka ryose. Kandi ndi umukiza wa Adamu ndetse n'uwo wacunguye muntu mu mimerere ye yo kugwa niba hari umuntu waza kuri njye apfukamye akanyatura nk'Umwami wabo kandi mu by'ukuri ndi Mesiya wanyu nyakuri Yemwe Isirayeli.

Mwumve bavandimwe banjye, ko ntiyise ibyo ntabayeho bitari byaranditswe n'urutoki rwa Yahweh ku bisate by'amabuye kandi bikavugwa mu kanwa k'abagaragu be abahanuzi ngo byandikwe ku bw'ukwizera no kumvira kw'abaragwa b'isezerano, abo muri bo Yemwe Isirayeli, niba mutazinangira imitima yanyu nk'uko ba So bakoze mu butayu mu gushotora kwabo Yahweh, kandi mu butayu ni ho ubuzima bwabo butagira ukwizera bwarangiriye mu busa, kandi bo ubwabo ntibarazwe amasezerano ahubwo abana b'ukwizera ni bo bayarazwe!

Nyamara mu buhumyi bwanyu bw'igice mwizera ibinyoma bya Esawu ko ndi umushukanyi kandi birumvikana ko nanzwe na Esawu kuko Esawu ashaka kwiyitirira umurage yagurishije umuto kandi nta nzira yo kwihana kuri Esawu, nubwo yayishakanye amarira. Kandi ba So ntibumvise imigambi yanjye nyakuri kuko bashakaga umuyobozi w'isi wo guhirika abo biyemeje kugandukira kugira ngo bagumane imyanya yabo mu bantu. Nyamara, natumwe gukiza muntu mu kuboko kw'abaguye abo Adamu yifatanyije nabo mu bujiji mu kutumvira kwe itegeko rya Yahweh kubera amayeri y'inzoka yamushutse we na nyina w'abazima bose ubwo bacumuraga muri Edeni mu ntangiriro. Kandi nubwo amahanga menshi yagiye yiyubaka akagwa, n'abami benshi bagiye biyubaka bakagwa kuva mu bihe by'igitambo cyanjye, Isirayeli iracyari mu bubata bw'abanzi babo kugeza n'uyu munsi kandi ikorera imigambi ya Esawu, ushaka irimbuka ryabo ryose.

Yego, Isirayeli yagumye mu bubata kuko ishyanga ryanyu ryabambuwe mu gihano cyo kumena amaraso y'inzirakarengane, ndetse no kwica ku bushake n'ubugome umuntu utagira icyaha watumwe na Yahweh ngo abacungure. Ndetse nanjye, Yeshua HaMashiach, igihe cyategetswe kigeze, Imana yohereje Umwana wayo wabyawe n'umugore, kandi atwarwa n'amategeko, kugira ngo acungure abatwarwaga n'amategeko. Kandi mu buzima bwanjye butagira icyaha no kumvira ni bwo niyerekanye nk'umwana w'intama w'Imana mu gitambo cy'ububabare n'ubugome ubwo nitangaga nk'umutambyi mukuru iteka ryose mu buryo bwa Melikisedeki.

Kandi ubu butumwa bwararangiye kandi nicaye iburyo bwa Data, ndetse Yahweh, NDI HO ukomeye. Kandi narazwe byose, kandi mu maboko yanjye akomeye ni ho hashyizwe byose mu ijuru no mu isi kugeza igihe abanzi banjye bazagirwa intebe y'ibirenge byanjye ariko Ubwami bwanjye ntibuzigera bugira iherezo! Ubwo Bwami aho Umwami wanyu azategeka buri kwegereza mu mpera y'amakuba ya Yakobo aho nzarimbura ubwami bw'abanzi nkazana urubanza rukomeye n'ingaruka ku baguye kandi bazajya mu gihano kubera ibyaha byabo ari byinshi n'urubanza rwabo rw'iteka urwo rutazigera rugira akaruhuko. Kandi bamwe muri bo bazajya muri rya ziba rinini ry'umuriro n'amazuku yaka mu kuza kwanjye kwa kabiri gutegeka isi ubwo inyamaswa n'umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma bazajugunywamo ari bazima. Kandi ba Satani bazabohwa muri uwo munyururu munini bazabohwamo mu bubabare n'ubwoba bwinshi kuko babohewe mu bwoba bategereje ibibategereje mu murage wabo w'iteka w'igihano iteka ryose. Ndetse n'igihano giteye ubwoba icyo Satani adashobora kuzigera acika aho abohewe akajugunywa mu miriro ishyushye cyane iteka ryose mu mpera y'igihe cye gito.

Kuko, ndi uwo watanzwe ku bw'agakiza ka Yakobo, ndetse n'urubyaro rwasezeranyijwe rw'umugore urwo Adamu yizeye mu byiringiro, kandi azazurirwa mu buzima bushya mu iteka ryose. Kuko ndi uwo watumwe gucungura Adamu, kandi natanze igitonyanga cyanjye cya nyuma cy'amaraso cyamenetse kigacengerera mu gitaka gitukura i Kaluvari, ndetse n'aho navugiye ayo magambo y'iteka ngo Birarangiye. Kandi ni ko bimeze, ko ndi uwo mu rukundo rwanjye kuri Yahweh n'urukundo rwanjye kuri bagenzi banjye nitanze ho igitambo mu bubabare bwinshi, isoni, n'iyicarubozo kandi nanesheje icyaha n'urupfu kandi umunsi uregereje aho buri kintu kibi cyose kitazongera kubaho.

Kandi nabikoze ku bushake bwanjye, ndetse ku bushake ku bw'icyubahiro nashyizwe imbere kandi nabikoze nsuzugura isoni zose zitari izanjye kwikorera. Nyamara mu rukundo rwanjye kuri mwe, nafashe isoni zanyu n'igihano cyanyu ku mugongo wanjye, ndetse n'ubwo banyaguzaga inyama ku magufa yanjye n'ibiboko byabo. Yego, nafashe isoni zanyu n'igihano cyanyu mu ihohoterwa n'urwango rw'abanyabyaha bayoborwaga n'abaguye ubwabo ubwo banyicaga mu bujiji mu cyimbo cyanyu batumva mu nzira zabo z'ubunyamaswa ko mu kwica umwana w'intama w'Imana bari kwishyiriraho iherezo ryabo mu miriro iteka ryose kuko bidashoboka ko urupfu rumfata, kuko ntarigera ncumura na rimwe, habe na rimwe, kandi mu by'ukuri ntabwo nzigera ncumura!

Yego, Isirayeli, nafashe umwanya wanyu mu gihano n'umwanya wa ba So banyanze mu bujiji ku busabe bwa Esawu! Nyamara se mwari gucungurwa mute uretse binyuze mu bugambanyi bwabo butagira ukwizera kuko kugira ngo Adamu acungurwe amaraso atagira icyaha gusa ni yo yashoboraga kuzuza ibisabwa by'igihano byasabwaga n'amategeko y'iteka kandi akiranuka ya Yahweh kuko hatabayeho kumena amaraso nta kubabarirwa ibyaha kubaho. Kandi kuko Adamu yaremwe atagira icyaha kandi atunganye ku munsi yaremweho, ni ko nanjye, Yeshua HaMashiach (ari we mwana w'intama w'Imana) naremwe ntagira inenge kandi ntagira icyaha ku bw'incungu yuzuye ya muntu. Kuko niba amaraso y'amapfizi n'ay'ihene, n'ivu ry'ishashi, bimishijwe ku bahumanye, byeza ku byerekeye kweza umubiri, amaraso ya Kristo we wituye Imana atagira inenge binyuze mu Mwuka w'iteka, azarushaho gukura imirimo ipfuye mu mitima yanyu, kugira ngo mukorere Imana nzima?

Nuko rero ndabaha kumva ko nafashe umwanya wanyu mu gucirwaho iteka n'igihano, kandi mbikora ku bushake bwanjye nta gahato; nubwo nasenze mu busitani bwa Getsemani niba hari indi nzira yaba, ariko atari ubushake bwanjye ahubwo ubwa Yahweh bube. Kandi nuko mu isoni zanjye, najyanywe inyuma y'amarembo y'uwo mudugudu munini, (ndetse Yerusalemu yica abahanuzi) aho natumwe ku banjye, kandi aho ni ho imyenda yanjye yagabanyijwe ubwo bakoraga ubufindo! Yego, hariya i Kaluvari, nyuma y'uko nari namaze kwihanganira amasaha y'igihano kidashira icyo nari umwere ho, ni ho nabambiwe n'amaboko y'abanyabyaha, abo byaha byabo ntagizemo uruhare cyangwa umugabane habe na gato. Nyamara ku bushake bwanjye kandi mbyibwirije naraboshwe nterwa imisumari mu maboko yanjye no mu birenge byanjye ngo mmanikwe kuri icyo giti kigaye mu cyimbo cyanyu, ndetse n'igihano kitari icyanjye kwikorera. Kuko ni ryari byigeze bihabwa umwere kwambara ku bushake mu isoni igiti cy'agasuzuguro mu isoni nyinshi n'ububabare ku bw'agakiza k'abavandimwe be bakiranirwa ngo ababare mu cyimbo cyabo, nyamara mu rukundo rwanjye kuri mwe nakoze ibyo ngibyo. Mwumve ko igihe cyose nari gushobora kubahagarika nsaba Data kandi yari kumpa legiyoni zirenga 12 z'abamarayika ku itegeko ryanjye, nyamara, ntabwo nigeze nsaba ahubwo nahisemo kwihanganira byuzuye kandi ku bushake igihano cyanyu kitari icyanjye kwikorera. Kandi nuko mu rukundo rwanjye narabitangiye ku bwanyu, kandi nabikora ukundi, nubwo ikintu nk'icyo kidakenewe kandi Yahweh yemeye igitambo cyanjye rimwe, byose, n'iteka ryose, bityo ntihakiriho ikindi gitambo cy'ibyaha.

Ku bw'ibyo, Isirayeli, mwumve urukundo, kwihangana, ubuntu n'imbabazi mbereka mu mimerere yanyu y'ubu yo kwigomeka mwanga Mesiya wanyu nyakuri, kandi ndaza kuri mwe muri iki gihe cya nyuma mu rukundo rwanjye kuri mwe rucyari ruzima kandi rudashobora kuneshwa, niba mwakwemera gusa Mesiya wanyu nyakuri. Kuko ndi uwo wafashe umwanya wanyu ku bushake mu gucirwaho iteka, igihano, n'iyicwa ry'abanyabyaha kuko ibihembo by'ibyaha ari urupfu, nyamara nanjye ntagira icyaha iteka ryose, kandi nhora nkora ubushake bwa Data, ndetse n'urugero mwanga mu kutumvira kwanyu ku bushake kuri Yahweh, no mu kwanga kwanyu Mesiya wanyu nyakuri.

Kandi se kuki nanzwe? Ndetse na Ponsiyo Pilato wari Guverineri wa Yudaya icyo gihe yari yiyemeje kundeka kandi yavuze ukuri ku bandegaga, ndetse n'aba Esawu bashishikarije ba So kumusakuza bati turekurire Barabasi! Ndetse n'ubwo basakuzaga hafi mu mwuka umwe bati "Mubambe!", ku byerekeye Pilato kubarekurira Mesiya wabo nyakuri n'amaraso y'inzirakarengane ayo banze. Kuko yari azi, neza, ko ishyari ari ryo ryatumye bangambanira. Kandi ubwo yari yicaye ku ntebe y'urubanza, umugore we yamutumyeho, ati, ntugire icyo ukorana n'uwo mukiranutsi: kuko nabawe cyane uyu munsi mu nzozi kubera we, kandi niko byagenze kandi niko byanditswe mu byanditswe byera niba muzabona ukwizera ko kwizera ubuhamya bwanjye.

Kandi ibyo byose ni ukubera ko ba So bifuje iby'iyi si bagakunda imyanya yabo mu bantu aho bashoboraga kubeshwaho n'ibyacumi n'amaturo y'abantu, bakihagararaho nk'abahanuzi b'ibinyoma biyita abakiranutsi, aho basubiragamo ubwenge bwa Esawu mu matwi y'abantu: ndetse n'ubwenge bw'ibinyoma abakuru babo bahaga agaciro cyane kuruta ijwi n'ubwenge bwa Yahweh, NDI UWONDI ukomeye, uwo banze ubwo bangaga kandi bakibambira Yesu w'i Nazareti.

Uko niko bimeze rero, ko mu kunyanga kwanyu, jyewe Yesu w'i Nazareti, nahindukiriye abanyamahanga, nabo bumva ijwi ryanjye mu gihe runaka kandi benshi, benshi batari bafite ibyiringiro, ubu bageze ku gakiza mu Mwami wabo, ndetse n'imbaga nyamwinshi nzazura ari bazima kugira ngo mbatange imbere ya Yahweh mu ikoraniro rikomeye ry'abera rigiye kuba. Kandi benshi bo muri Yudaya na benshi bari baratataniye mu mahanga bashyizweho ikimenyetso cy'agakiza kuzuye. Kandi mu bavandimwe banyu, ndetse n'abo mu bwami bw'amajyaruguru batoranyirijwe agakiza k'iteka, benshi muri bo ubu bashyizweho ikimenyetso cy'iteka ryose kandi bazazurirwa ubugingo bushya. Ni ukuri, ni ukuri, aba bose basize urugero rwo kwihana kugira ngo murukurikize, niba mwumvira ijwi rya Mesiya w'ukuri wenyine Yahweh azigera yohereza, ari we Yesu w'i Nazareti Mesiya wanyu w'ukuri!

Kuko kubera ibyaha byabo imiryango y'amajyaruguru yatataniye mu mahanga, aho barinzwe kandi bakabikirwa agakiza, ndetse n'ukuboko gukomeye kwa Yahweh. Kandi niko byanditswe n'ukuboko kw'Intumwa yanjye Pawulo ubwo yabwiraga Abaroma mu myaka ya yubile myinshi ishize: Tuzi yuko byose bifatanya kuzanira ibyiza abakunda Imana, ari bo bahamagawe nk'uko yagambiriye. Kuko abo yamenye kera, yabatoranyije kera gushushanywa n'ishusho y'Umwana wayo, kugira ngo abe imfura muri bene se benshi. Kandi abo yatoranyije kera, nabo yarabahamagaye, n'abo yahamagaye, nabo yarabatsindishirije, n'abo yatsindishirije, nabo yabahaye ubwiza. Kandi gutyo muri Isirayeli y'Imana ikoraniro ry'imiryango yazimiye rizuzuzwa, kandi Abisirayeli benshi bageze ku gakiza binyuze mu kwatura nk'Umwami wabo, jyewe Yesu w'i Nazareti, mu gihe cyanjye cy'ubuntu n'agakiza bagezeho.

Nubwo muri iki gihe musuzugura imirimo yanjye, ndetse n'imirimo yanjye y'agakiza nakoze mu mahanga ku itegeko rya Yahweh mu gihe cyanjye cy'ubuntu ubu kigeze ku iherezo ryacyo; nuko rero mwumve ko ibyo Yahweh yejesheje amaraso ya Yeshua HaMashiach, ntimubyite ibizira cyangwa ibihumanye! Kandi jyewe, Yeshua HaMashiach ndabinginga ngo mukurikire inzira z'abavandimwe banyu baba abari hafi cyangwa kure baje aho ndi bihannye byuzuye kugira ngo mbakire kandi mbakiranure na Yahweh, kuko ufite Umwana aba afite na Data ariko udafite Umwana ntabwo afite na Data, kandi uko niko Yahweh yabitangaje!

Kandi niko bimeze ko nongeye guhindukirira bene wacu ndetse nk'uko ntera umugongo amahanga muri iki gihe cya nyuma cy'imperuka kandi gutandukanya kwa nyuma ubu kuzaba mu cyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli, cyageze kugira ngo cyeze isi mu muriro wanjye utunganya kandi Esawu azatandukanywa na Yakobo burundu n'iteka ryose. Kuko Esawu yagurishije umugabane we w'ubumfura, kandi Yahweh yemeye amahitamo ye iteka ryose kugira ngo bibe amahitamo y'iteka, kuko abana b'isezerano ari bo baragwa b'amasezerano atari abana b'umubiri, niba muri aba Kristo, muri urubyaro rwa Aburahamu, n'abaragwa nk'uko isezerano riri. Kandi niko bimeze ko amasezerano y'umurage yatanzwe na Yahweh ubwe yatanzwe mu kwizera ku bo yahawe, kuko umukiranutsi azabeshwaho no kwizera! Kandi nyuma y'uko amasezerano yo kwizerwa atanzwe niko n'amasezerano yagiranye n'ibimenyetso by'isezerano byatanzwe kandi amasezerano ahinduka ibyasezeranijwe binyuze mu kumena amaraso kandi amasezerano yose ya kera n'amasezerano yose ya Yahweh uhereye ku rito kugeza ku rikomeye byashyizwe mu Isezerano Rishya ryanjye ry'isezerano, aho irema rizahindurwa abakiranutsi iteka ryose mu kugarurwa kw'iteka kuri Se w'imicyo, udahinduka kandi ntagire n'igicucu cyo guhinduka.

Nuko rero nje kuri bene wacu mu rukundo no mu kwicisha bugufi, kandi nje aho muri mbikunze mu rukundo mbakunda ntidashaka ko hagira n'umwe muri mwe urimbuka mu kutizera. Kandi ni ukuri, ni ukuri abasigaye bazakizwa ari bazima baturutse kuri Yakobo ndetse no mu gihe cy'amakuba ya Yakobo ari cyo cyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli. Nuko rero, ndabinginga bavandimwe banjye nimwumve ijwi ryanjye kuko iki ari igihe cya nyuma kandi uku ni ugutandukanya kwa nyuma mbere y'uko umujinya usukwa wose kandi mbere y'uko nza kurimbura ubwami bw'abanzi burundu, kuko mu gukiranuka nza nk'Intare yo mu muryango wa Yuda kandi nza guca urubanza no kurwana intambara!

Kandi abazahindukirira bose muri iki gihe, Yewe Isirayeli, ndetse n'abo ntegeka bose bavutse kuri ba Data ba kera ngo bumve ijwi ryanjye, ndetse n'abazambaza izina ryanjye rikomeye bose kugeza ku iherezo baturutse mu mahanga nabo bazakizwa kandi bazakizwa mu byiciro byabo. Amahanga azahabwa icyubahiro gisa n'icyanjye ubwo nitangiraga ibyaha by'abantu, kandi bazicwa urw'abamartiri ubwo bambaza izina ryanjye rikiza kugeza ku iherezo. Kandi bazajya mu bwiza bukomeye iteka ryose kandi ingororano zabo ni iz'iteka ryose kubera guhagarara kwabo, kandi binyuze mu kumvira kwabo bazaba batsinze inzira zabo zo kutizera n'akazuyaze, ndetse nk'abacishijwe mu muriro.

Kandi kuri Isirayeli, bavandimwe banjye, nzaza kubakiza kandi nzaza kubatabara ndi kumwe n'abanjye 144,000 ndetse n'abiyogeje byuzuye nk'abaneshi banjye mu maraso yanjye y'impongano n'acungura ku munsi wabo w'agakiza kandi bazakoranyirizwa aho ndi muri iryo koraniro rikomeye rigiye kuba mu buryo butunguranye ku isi. Kuko nzayobora abanjye 144,000 kumanuka ku Musozi Siyoni, kandi bazankurikira intambwe ku yindi ubwo bayobora abasigaye banjye ba Yakobo ahantu heza mu butayu aho bateguriwe na Yahweh kugira ngo babe amahoro, kandi bazagaburirwa banitabweho kandi ikiyoka, inyamaswa, n'umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma ntibazabakoraho, cyangwa ngo bababone, abo basigaye akaba ari mwe Yewe Isirayeli. Ariko Baraba azazana ikizira cy'umurimbuzi kandi urumamfu rwinshi ruzakurwa mu gihugu, kandi nzabisha abasigaye banjye kure y'intambara y'uwo munsi mukuru, ndetse n'umunsi wo kugaruka kwanjye uwo munsi Yahweh yashyizeho mu bihe bya kera by'iteka kandi washyizweho iteka ryose mu ngengabihe ye y'agakiza kandi uwo munsi wegereje rwose, kuko niko byanditswe kandi niko bizakorwa.

Nuko rero, bene wacu nkunda iteka n'iteka ryose, kandi nyamara ndacyubaha uburenganzira bwanyu mwahawe n'Imana bwo kwihitiramo inzira muzagendamo. Nuko rero ndababaza, ese muzaguma muri iyo nzira ngari igana kurimbuka iyobora mu miriro idazima yagenewe abaguye? Cyangwa, muzumvira ijwi rya Mesiya wanyu w'ukuri, ndetse n'uwatanganye igitonyanga cya nyuma cy'amaraso ye atagira icyaha mu mibabaro n'agahinda kenshi kugira ngo mubeshweho imbere y'Imana yanyu, ndetse na Yahweh NDI UWONDI Ukomeye? Yego, Yahweh warize amarira menshi mu gahinda ke k'intama zazimiye z'inzu ya Isirayeli, ndetse n'abanga ijwi rye n'ijwi ryanjye bose. Ese muzifatanya nanjye binyuze mu kumvira Mesiya wanyu w'ukuri? Cyangwa muzifatanya mubikunze na Baraba uwo Esawu yohereje kugira ngo abarimbure?

Nuko rero mwumve Amagambo yanjye Yewe Isirayeli kandi mwumve amahitamo mugomba gukora kandi mwumve ko ndikubiyegereza kandi nzohereza abatangabuhamya banjye babiri bakomeye kandi ndabategeka kubumva kugira ngo mwakire ubuhamya bwabo kuko bazahamya ibintu byinshi bihishwe byahishwe imitima y'abantu ariko ibyo bazahishura kandi ubuhamya bwabo buzahama iteka n'iteka ryose. Nuko rero, nimwumve ijwi ry'umwana w'intama w'ukuri w'Imana, Yewe Isirayeli, kuko nta gakiza n'incungu mu rindi zina iryo ari ryo ryose ryatanzwe, kandi ni ukuri, ni ukuri nta rindi zina rizigera ritangwa kuri iyi mpamvu, keretse Yeshua HaMashiach ari we kandi Yesu w'i Nazareti nubwo mu mubiri ntakimenywa kandi ndi Kristo Yesu ndetse n'Umwami w'Abami n'Umutware w'Abatware.

Ndi Intare yo mu muryango wa Yuda kandi mu gukiranuka nza guca urubanza no kurwana intambara.
