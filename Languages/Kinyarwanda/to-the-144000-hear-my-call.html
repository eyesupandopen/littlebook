Ubuhamya Bubiri: Itorero
Mu Mirimo ya Yohana Umuhishuzi
20 Gicurasi 2024

Ku bantu 144,000: Nimwumve Umuhamagaro wanjye!

Nimwumve, nimwumve, miryango yanjye cumi n'ibiri, ijwi rya Mesiya wanyu, Umwami, Umwami n'Umutambyi mukuru, ari njye, Yeshua HaMashiach, Umwana w'Umuntu uwo Yehova yazuye mu bapfuye. Kuko:

Ndi Mesiya wanyu w'ukuri, ukomoka ku ntebe y'ubwami ya Dawidi, kandi ni ku ntebe ye nzategekera Igihugu cy'Isezerano ku bwa Yehova, Imana ihoraho, kuko ari ko yasezeranyije ba So. Kandi mwumve ko ndi Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda, kandi nta mwami cyangwa umuhanuzi watanzwe uturutse muri Dani, aho umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma aturuka.

Ndi Umwami wanyu, kuko Yehova yangize Umwami w'abami, kandi nabaguze amaraso yanjye bwite nk'Ipasika yanyu itunganye, yatangiwe ibyaha by'Abanyamahanga n'Abayuda kimwe, kugira ngo mwembi mube umuntu mushya umwe muri njye, ari we Kristo Yesu. Kuko Yehova yampaye izina risumba amazina yose kandi atuma nsangira na We intebe ye y'ubwami kubera kumvira kwanjye. Kandi bityo ubwami bwanjye butangwa nk'urumuri rw'agakiza, kuko nahinduwe umwuka utanga ubuzima, kandi Yehova wenyine ni we unduta, kuko ari Imana ihoraho.

Ndi Umwami wanyu, kuko Yehova yangize Umwami w'abami, kandi nzategeka isi mu gukiranuka. Kandi dore! Nzanye Umugeni wanjye, uzategekana nanjye, kuko nabagize abami n'abatambyi imbere ya Yehova uhoraho, uwo nta wuhwanye na We. Kuko mu ngoma yanjye y'imyaka igihumbi yasezeranyijwe, nzaba mfite ubutware bukuru, kandi nzohereza abami banjye n'abatambyi gutegeka mu murage wabo muri njye; kuko nabaremye bushya muri njye, bakuwe mu Bayuda n'Abanyamahanga kimwe bagahindurwa umuntu mushya umwe, baremwe bushya mu butungane. Ndi umwe mu bumwe n'umugambi na Data, Yehova, kandi ku ntebe ye y'ubwami aranduta.

Ndi umutambyi wanyu mukuru iteka ryose mu buryo bwa Melikisedeki, kandi natuye amaraso yanjye bwite y'impongano mu gitambo ubwo nitangaga nk'Ipasika yanyu itunganye kandi yuzuye. Kuko Abaheburayo batanga ubuhamya bwabo ubwo natumaga Intumwa Pawulo ibabwira mu izina ryanjye (yakomokaga muri Benyamini): Mbese amaraso ya Kristo, wituye Imana binyuze mu Mwuka uhoraho adafite inenge, ntazarushaho kweza imitima yanyu mu mirimo ipfuye kugira ngo mukorere Imana ihoraho? Kandi bityo igitambo cyanjye cyo kwitanga ni icy'icyarimwe kandi cy'iteka ryose, kandi nta wundi Mesiya uhari.

Nubwo isinagogi ya Satani izakira mesiya wabo w'ibinyoma n'umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma ari na we mwubatsi w'urusengero w'inyamaswa (kuko ari inyamaswa ituruka mu isi). Kuko muri urusengero rw'Imana ihoraho, kandi nta mpamvu y'igitambo cy'inyamaswa izongera kubaho ukundi, kuko ndi igitambo cya nyuma, Ipasika itunganye, itagira icyaha, kandi imirimo yanjye iruzuye kandi iratunganye. Ku bw'ibyo, nta mpamvu y'ikintu nk'icyo izongera kubaho, ahubwo ni uko gusa muza aho ndi n'ibyaha byanyu mwihana, nanjye nzabeza imirimo yanyu ipfuye y'umubiri. Kuko mu Baroma handitswe ngo: Kuko itegeko ry'Umwuka w'ubugingo muri Kristo Yesu ryambaturuye mu itegeko ry'icyaha n'urupfu. Kuko ari ko nababohoye muri njye.

Mubyumve, miryango yanjye n'abantu banjye, ko ibihe by'Abanyamahanga bigeze ku musozo. Ibihe by'Abanyamahanga byaje kubera kwinangira kw'imitima ya ba so. Kuko Pawulo yavuze iki mu Byakozwe n'Intumwa kuri icyo kibazo: Byari bikwiriye ko ijambo ry'Imana ribanza kubwirwa mwebwe: ariko kuko muryigijeyo, mukiyibwira ko mudakwiriye ubugingo buhoraho, dore, duhindukiye ku Banyamahanga.

Ndabagira inama, bantu banjye, ntimukore amakosa amwe kandi ntimubeho mu byaha bya ba so, ahubwo mwihane ibyaha byanyu bwite kandi mwihane n'ibyaha bya ba so, maze muze aho ndi nanjye nzabakira, kuko muri abana b'isezerano. Kandi buri nyuguti cyangwa agace kayo bizasohora; kuko ibyasezeranyijwe na Yehova bidashoboka guhinduka, kuko bidashoboka ko Yehova abeshya. Ku bw'ibyo, ndi isohozwa ry'amasezerano menshi, menshi, kuko ndi Mesiya wanyu w'ukuri, ishusho y'Imana ihoraho. Kuko ndi umwana wayo w'ikinege, Umwana w'Intama w'Imana uwo Yohana Umubatiza yagaragaje ibihe byose. Kuko yavuze ati: Dore Umwana w'Intama w'Imana ukuraho icyaha cy'Isi!

Kandi bityo nahindukiye ku mahanga kuko igihugu cyanjye bwite cyanyanze, ndetse na nyuma y'uko mpfiriye ibyaha byabo nkazuka. Kuko nabatumyeho intumwa zanjye n'abahanuzi, kandi benshi barakijijwe. Kuko mu ntangiriro y'igihe cy'ubuntu, nabohereje mu Rusengero no mu masinagogi, kandi benshi barampindukiriye banyemera nk'Umwami wabo. Kandi bityo ndabahamagara ngo mube bazima mu Bwami bwanjye, kuko nabacunguriye Data kugira ngo namwe mube abana be binyuze mu mwuka wanjye nzabashyiraho ikimenyetso. Kandi bityo ibihe by'Abanyamahanga birarangiye, kuko ubu na bo banyanze bagahitamo undi Yesu basenga nk'imana, bityo bakigira abasenga ibishushanyo; kuko Satani ari ko yabashutse. Kuko hariho Imana imwe gusa, Yehova uhoraho, kandi hariho Mesiya umwe w'ukuri, ari njye, Yeshua HaMashiach.

Kuko batakaje amabanga yanjye, birengagiza amayerekwa yanjye n'imigambi y'ibyanditswe byanjye byera. Batakaje icyerekezo cy'umugambi wa Data wo gucungura, kandi banga mu bwibone amagambo yanjye nandikishije ku Baroma. Kuko Pawulo yavuze ati: Ndavuga nti, Mbese Imana yataye abantu bayo? Ntibikabeho. Kuko nanjye ndi Umwisirayeli, wo mu rubyaro rwa Aburahamu, wo mu muryango wa Benyamini. Imana ntiyataye abantu bayo, abo yamenye kera. Kandi na none: None se bimeze bite? Isirayeli ntiyabonye icyo yashakaga; ariko abatoranyijwe barakibonye, naho abandi bahumwa amaso. Kandi bityo gutoranywa kwari gukomeye ku bo natoranyije mu miryango; kuko intumwa zanjye zose zari iz'igihugu n'iz'isezerano, kandi bityo nazohereje mu mahanga, ibyo abayobozi banyu b'idini babeshyera batuka Imana, babita ab'ibinyoma. Abo bayobozi b'ibinyoma n'abungeri b'imburamumaro ndetse bahakana kuzuka kwanjye, kuko nazutse mu bapfuye, kandi sinzongera gupfa ukundi iteka ryose.

Kuko Abaroma bavuga iki kindi kuri iyi ngingo: Dawidi aravuga ati, Ameza yabo ababere umutego, n'ikigoyi, n'igisitaza, n'ingororano: Amaso yabo ahumwe kugira ngo batabona, kandi ubahemberishe imigongo yabo iteka. Ndavuga nti, Mbese basitiye kugira ngo bagwe? Ntibikabeho: ahubwo binyuze mu kugwa kwabo agakiza kageze ku Banyamahanga, kugira ngo babatere ishyari.

Kuko ntashaka, bene Data, ko mutamenya iri banga, kugira ngo mutiyemera; ko guhuma igice kwabaye ku Bisirayeli, kugeza ubwo umubare wuzuye w'Abanyamahanga uzaba winjiye. Kandi bityo ndatangaza ko umubare wuzuye w'Abanyamahanga wageze, kandi amabanga y'Imana azasubizwa Isirayeli kugira ngo bumvire kwizera. Kuko nzongera gukiza Yakobo, kandi asigaye yizerwa azampindukirira mu budahemuka, mu kwizera gukomeye!

Kandi bityo nje kubatera ishyari mu mitima yanyu kuri njye, Yeshua HaMashiach, na Yehova Imana ikomeye ihoraho yantumye, uwo mutagisenga mukurikije ubumenyi, kuko igitambaro cy'ubuhumyi kizatwikira umuntu wese ukurikiza amategeko ya Mose. Kuko nabwiye iki Abakorinto kuri iyi ngingo: Kandi tudakora nka Mose, washyize igitambaro mu maso he, kugira ngo Abisirayeli batitegereza iherezo ry'ibyari bigiye kuvaho: Ariko imitima yabo yarahumye: kuko n'uyu munsi icyo gitambaro kigumaho ntikikurweho mu gusoma Isezerano rya Kera; icyo gitambaro kikaba gikurwaho muri Kristo. Ariko n'uyu munsi, iyo basoma Mose, igitambaro kiba kiri ku mitima yabo.

Nyamara, iyo ihindukiye Umwami, igitambaro gikurwaho.

Kuko nujuje amategeko ya Mose, kuko mwebwe cyangwa ba so mudashobora kugera ku kuyubahiriza mu buryo butunganye kubera icyaha kiri mu maraso yanyu. Kuko njye, Yeshua HaMashiach, nujuje amategeko mu buryo bwuzuye, kandi amategeko yubahirizwa mu buryo butunganye gusa binyuze mu kwizera njye, Yesu Kristo. Mubarwaho gukiranuka binyuze mu kwifatanya kanyu kwuzuye nanjye, kandi bityo umukiranutsi azabeshwaho no kwizera. Kandi bityo nzakuraho igitambaro ku mitima yanyu kugira ngo mushobore kumbona, Yeshua, Mesiya wanyu w'ukuri, kuko ntabataye! Kandi nzira aho muri mu isezerano rishya, atari mu busaza bw'inyuguti. Kuko nemeje isezerano ryanjye rishya mu maraso yanjye bwite, nk'uko ubushake n'amasezerano bya Yehova biri, kugira ngo mucungurwe byuzuye. Kandi nzabayobora ku murage wanyu, kuko amasezerano y'Imana ari yego na amena. Ndetse nta nyuguti cyangwa agace kayo kazabura, kuko byose bigiye gusohora; kandi mu gihe cyabyo, ni ukuri, nzasohoza byose, kuko ari ko natumwe na Yehova kubikora.

Kandi bityo Abanyamahanga bampindutse nk'imbwa isubira ku birutsi byayo, kandi igihe kirageze cyo kubabarekeramo. Kuko mu gihe cyanjye cy'ubuntu, nta muntu n'umwe Data yatoranyirije agakiza wabuze, kandi nta n'umwe ushobora kubakura mu kuboko kwanjye. Kuko abanyatuye, Yesu, nk'Umwami wabo, bafite kwizera mu mitima yabo ko Imana yanzuye mu bapfuye, bashyizweho ikimenyetso cy'umwuka wera w'isezerano, kandi imigambi ya Data yarasohoreye muri njye kuri bo. Kandi bityo bazabana nanjye iteka.

Kuko igiti cy'umunzenze gihishura ukuri. Kandi navuze iki ku giti cy'umunzenze mu rwandiko rwa Pawulo ku Baroma: Kandi niba amwe mu mashami yaravunwe, nawe, uri umunzenze w'ishyamba, ukomekwa muri yo, ugasangira na yo umuzi n'umubyibuho by'igiti cy'umunzenze; Ntiwirarire ku mashami. Ariko niwirarira, umenye ko atari wowe utunze umuzi, ahubwo umuzi ari wo ugutunze.

Kandi bityo bakoze amakosa kandi bafata ibyaha bya ba so maze bampinduka basanga ibishushanyo byabo, kandi ntibazongera kumva ijwi ryanjye. Nubwo nibambaza izina ry'Umwami, bazakizwa abatikundira amagara yabo kugeza ku gupfa.

Kuko ubuhamya bw'Abaroma mu byanditswe buvuga iki kindi nk'umuburo ku mahanga: Uzavuga uti, Amashami yaravunwe, kugira ngo njye nkomekweho. Ni byiza; kubera kutizera yaravunwe, kandi wowe uhagazweho no kwizera. Ntiwibone, ahubwo utinye: Kuko niba Imana itarababariye amashami y'umwimerere, irinde kugira ngo nawe itakubabarira. Dore rero kugira neza n'ubukari by'Imana: ku baguye, ubukari; ariko kuri wowe, kugira neza, niba uguma mu kugira neza kwayo: bitabaye ibyo nawe uzacibwa. Kandi na bo, nibataguma mu kutizera, bazakomekwaho: kuko Imana ishobora kongera kubakomekaho. Kuko niba waraciwe ku giti cy'umunzenze w'ishyamba kamere, ugakomekwa mu buryo bunyuranye na kamere ku giti cy'umunzenze cyiza: nkanswe aya, ari yo mashami y'umwimerere, ntazarushaho gukomekwa ku giti cyayo cy'umunzenze?

Kuko ntashaka, bene Data, ko mutamenya iri banga, kugira ngo mutiyemera; ko guhuma igice kwabaye ku Bisirayeli, kugeza ubwo umubare wuzuye w'Abanyamahanga uzaba winjiye. Kandi bityo Isirayeli yose izakizwa: nk'uko byanditswe ngo, I Siyoni hazaturuka Umukiza, kandi azakuraho kutubaha Imana kuri Yakobo.

Kandi bityo, bene wacu, ibihe by'Abanyamahanga bigeze ku musozo, kandi impinduka nini irimo kuba, aho ubuntu bwanjye buzahita buhinduka urubanza. Kandi n'ubu ibice byemerewe kujya mu mwanya wabyo n'ukuboko kwa Satani, kuko azahagarara, nubwo azamenagurwa rwose, kandi nzasenya ubwami bwe burundu; kuko atari umwami.

Kandi bityo ubu hageze igihe cyo kongera gukomeka amashami y'umwimerere ku giti cy'umunzenze, kuko imigambi ya Yehova yujujwe mu buntu n'imbabazi bidakwiriye byerekanwe ku Banyamahanga (kuko ubuntu n'imbabazi by'Imana bidakwiriye ku muntu wese), kuko n'ubwo buntu n'imbabazi byakoreshejwe mu gushaka intama zazimiye z'inzu ya Isirayeli. Kuko mudakwiriye kuyoberwa amateka yanyu bwite, mu gihe mushyize hasi ibitabo byanyu bya Talimudi by'i Babuloni bituka Imana mukanyigiraho; kuko ntabwo nohereje icyo gitabo, kandi mukeneye kwihana izo nyigisho no kwanga ibinyoma bya Satani. Nimugaruke ku ijambo ryanjye ryera ry'Imana kandi muve mu byaha bya ba so, kuko nzabacungura nimumara kumva ijwi ryanjye.

Kuko ntabwo natumwe keretse ku ntama zazimiye z'inzu ya Isirayeli. Kandi bityo imiryango icumi yatatanyirijwe mu mahanga ivanywe mu bunyage bwayo n'Abashuri icyo gihe cyose gishize. Bazerereye kandi bagenda mu isi nk'abashyitsi n'abimukira. Bavuye muri Ashuri bagiye mu bihugu byo mu majyaruguru, muri Sikitiya, n'i Burayi, ndetse amaherezo no muri Amerika, aho nabahaye ubuturo bw'amahoro mu gihe runaka. Kandi nahamagaye umuntu wese ushobora kuza aturutse mu mahanga ku gakiza kanjye. Ndetse n'Abisirayeli benshi barakijijwe kandi baracunguwe kimwe na benshi bo mu mahanga, abari kuba batarigeze bagira ibyiringiro. Kandi imbaga nyamwinshi yaritabiriye, kandi bityo ibihe by'Abanyamahanga birarangiye, kuko nta gihe kikiriho. Ariko mwumve ko abambaza izina ry'Umwami bose bazakizwa niba ari abizerwa kugeza ku iherezo, kuko nzabakira uko ishyanga bakomokamo ryaba riri kose.

Kandi bityo bimeze, ko amakuba ya Yakobo aje ku gihugu no ku mahanga, kuko igihe kigeze ngo urubanza rukomeye rugwe ku isi. Kandi bityo inyamaswa ku mbaraga z'ikiyoka zizahaguruka; gusa ntimuzafate ikimenyetso cyayo kandi ntimuzagwe mu buriganya bwayo. Kuko yamaze kwiyegereza abategetsi b'igihugu cyanyu, ndetse n'isinagogi ya Satani n'imigenzo yabo ya Kedomu. Kandi yamaze kuvuga ibitutsi bikomeye, kuko irimo gutegurirwa umugambi wayo w'ukuri.

Kuko ikizira cy'umurimbuzi kije. Kuko nategetse Luka wanjye wizerwa kwandika iki ibihe byose: Kandi nimubona Yerusalemu igoswe n'ingabo, muzamenye ko kurimbuka kwayo kwegereje. Icyo gihe abari i Yudaya bazahungire ku misozi; n'abari hagati yayo bazayivemo; n'abari mu byaro ntibazayinjiremo. Kuko iyo ari iminsi yo guhorera, kugira ngo ibyanditswe byose bisohore.

Kandi bityo bizaba mu gihe gikwiriye, kandi nzarinda abanjye, kuko mbohereza nk'abahanuzi banjye, abungeri, abakiza, n'abakozi. Kuko mbahamagariye gukora, ndetse no gusohoza umuhamagaro wanyu w'ukuri mwatangiwe mu isezerano kuri ba so na Yehova Ubwe, kandi ku kuboko kwanjye bizasohora. Kuko Yehova yashyize intebe yanjye ku ntebe ye y'ubwami, kandi nasangiye intebe yanjye y'ubwami n'Umugeni wanjye, abahora bari kumwe nanjye. Kandi bityo nta n'umwe mu isi unduta; nta n'umwe mu bahanuzi banyu b'ibinyoma wagereranywa nanjye, kuko bose bafite intege nke kandi ari abasabirizi kandi batumwe gushuka no kuyobya, kuko ari abungeri b'imburamumaro.

Mumenye, bantu banjye, ko umwanzi yabateze imitego myinshi, bityo mukurikire ubuyobozi bwanjye kandi muzayirinda yose. Kuko vuba aha ibyishimo byose bizakurwa mu isi. Benshi ntibazabona n'umugati, kandi ntibazagira n'ameza yo kuriraho ahubwo bazaba inzererezi mu isi kubera kunyizera.

Ntimwemere gushukwa na manu yabo y'ibinyoma izasaba ikimenyetso cy'inyamaswa kugira ngo mwinjire mu muryango wabo wiyandaritse. Kuko ikimenyetso kitazababarirwa, kandi abazagwa muri uyu mutego bose ntibazabohorwa; kuko ari icy'iteka ku bafata uwo mwanzuro wa nyuma w'iherezo, kuko bazaba bashyizeho ikimenyetso ku iherezo ryabo kandi iryo herezo ni irya nyuma. Bityo ntimushukwe n'icyambo, cyangwa muzisanga mu nshundura kandi mufashwe burundu mu rwasaya rwanyu.

Mbohereje gukoranya umukumbi wanjye, kandi nzabayobora kuri bo nabo mbayobore kuri mwe, kandi muzabaragira ku bwanjye, kuko ndi umwungeri mukuru. Ntimuzahakana ubundi bwoko cyangwa igihugu cyangwa irindi shyanga iryo ari ryo ryose, kuko isezerano rya Yehova rihagaze, kandi abambaza izina ry'Umwami bose bazakizwa. Ntimukabe abinangiye nka ba so, kandi mwicishe bugufi ku ijwi ryanjye. Kuko ba so banyanze, bityo mwature ibyaha bya ba so n'ibyanyu mu kwihana kuri njye, nanjye nzabababarira kandi mbameneho urukundo rwanjye n'ubuntu bwanjye, kuko nzatemesha imigezi y'amazi y'ubugingo iva mu nda yanyu. Nabahaye imbaraga z'ibitangaza bikomeye. Kandi mbohereje kuri bene wanyu kugira ngo babampindukirire, kuko abasigaye bazakizwa; kandi nzakiza Yakobo mu kuboko kw'abanzi babo, kuko nabitangaje kandi bityo bizasohora.

Njye, ndetse Yeshua HaMashiach uwo Yehova yatumye, ndi Mesiya w'ukuri wa Isirayeli n'isi. Ndetse nzohereza abahanuzi banjye babiri, ndetse n'abo Yehova yakuye mu isi mu bihe byabo: kuko uwo mugambi ari wo watumye batwarwa, kandi bityo bazoherezwa. Mfite ibintu byinshi byo kubabwira kandi ibyo bintu bizavugwa, kandi nzabategurira ubutumwa bwanyu, kandi muzankorera imirimo mikuru kandi ikomeye, ndetse n'imirimo mutigeze mutekereza ko ishoboka. Kuko ibihe bibi cyane buri gihe bibyara abagabo bakomeye ba Yehova, ndetse n'abagabo batazavunika, kuko muzaruhukira mu mbaraga zanjye, kandi sinvunika. Kuko ari ko Yehova yangize, kandi ari ko nzabagira. Bityo nimwumve ijwi ryanjye, kuko ndahamagara kandi nzohereza abamarayika banjye gushyira ikimenyetso cya Yehova ku bantu 144,000.

Nzabarinda inyamaswa n'ikizira cyayo; nzabahisha, kandi muzagaburirwa mu ijuru ndetse niyo mwabikenera, bityo ntimutinye. Ntimutinye ku bw'amagara yanyu, kuko ingororano ikomeye ari iyanyu ku bw'umurimo n'ubudahemuka, kandi nzabagororera ku bw'igihagararo cyanyu.

Ndi Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda, kandi muzumva gutontoma kwanjye, kuko ndabahamagara.

Ndi we uzashyira ikimenyetso ku bantu 12,000 bo muri buri muryango usibye Dani na Efurayimu.

Ndi we uzamenagura icyana cy'intare kizaturuka muri Dani.

Ndi we uzatsinda inyamaswa n'umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma, kandi nzabajugunya ari bazima mu nyanja nini y'umuriro.

Ndi we uzayobora abantu banjye mu rwuri rwiza binyuze mu bungeri banjye, kuko ndi umwungeri mukuru.

Ndi we uzasohoza amasezerano yahawe ba so, kuko ari ko natumwe.

Ndi we uzamenagura umutwe wa ba Satani, kuko abatware b'iyi si bazishyura.

Ndi we uzategekera ku ntebe y'ubwami ya Dawidi, kuko ubwami bwanjye butagira iherezo.

Ndi we uje mu bicu, kandi nzaza mu gihugu cyanjye bwite kandi nzafata ubutegetsi bwanjye, kandi sinzahagarikwa.

Njye, ndetse njye, Yeshua HaMashiach, nohereje aya magambo ku bo mpamagara. Bityo nimwumve amagambo yanjye mu kumvira, kandi mumenye ko nzabohora igihugu n'isi yose kandi nzategeka mu gukiranuka. Bityo nimwumve umuhamagaro wanjye, kuko nza aho muri kandi nzabigisha inzira zanjye kandi nzabayobora mu kuri kose. Bityo ntimwange inyandiko zanjye, kuko amagambo y'Imana azasubizwa Isirayeli, kandi isi izamenya ko hari Imana muri Isirayeli, ndetse Yehova, Imana ikomeye ihoraho, kuko nta yindi ihari, kandi nta wuhwanye na Yo. Shalom! Shalom! Bityo mugire amahoro kandi mumererwe neza, kuko nzabakiza mwese rwose abashyira kwizera kwabo muri Yeshua HaMashiach.
