Muzahungira Kuri Nde?

Ijambo ry'Uwiteka ryangezeho.

Umwami Yesu Kristo aravuga ati: Muzahungira kuri nde ngo mucike umujinya wanjye? Ese muzashyira kwizera kwanyu mu bantu babi batumwe kuyobya n'intore niba bishoboka? Ese muzashyira kwizera kwanyu muri gahunda y'inyamaswa y'i Babuloni, ya maraya ukomeye? Ese mwarayobejwe ngo mutekereze ko mushobora kwihitiramo umwami wanyu binyuze mu matora? Ntimwumva yuko Satani ari imana y'iyi si kandi ayobya isi yose? Ese muzashyira kwizera kwanyu mu miryango yashyizweho n'abantu ndetse no mu mazu y'abahanuzi b'ibinyoma batumwe kuyobya intore zanjye? Muzahungira kuri nde? Muzajya he ko nta gishobora guhishwa amaso yanjye?

Muri kubona ababi bitegura kwihisha mu masenga yabo mu isi biyongereye ubwabo ngo bampunge, ariko biyongereye ubusa. Umujinya wanjye uregereje kandi nta muntu n'umwe ushobora guhagarara. Mwambaze izina ryanjye mu gihe kigihari. Data yashyize byose mu maboko yanjye, haba mu ijuru no mu isi, kandi nzasohoza imigambi ye. Nta kintu na kimwe cyangwa umuntu n'umwe ushobora kumpagarika kuko Data yampaye ububasha, kandi sinshobora guhagarikwa. Umujinya w'Umwana w'Intama uri ku rugi rwanyu, nubwo nkomeza guhagarara ku rugi nkomanga. Ndifuza cyane kwinjira ngo nsangire namwe niba mwakingura urugi rwanyu.

Bwoko bwanjye nimukanguke!! Mukanguke!! MUKANGUKE!! Mukangukire gukiranuka kandi ntimukore icyaha. Numvise amasengesho yanyu, kandi nabonye kubabara kw'imitima yanyu n'amakuba yanyu. Nimuze aho ndi n'imitima yanyu yose kandi muve mu byaha. Muve mu byaha by'i Babuloni n'abayobozi bayo; ndababwira nweruye nti muyivemo rwose, ndetse no mu matorero yayo yaguye. Kuko urubanza rw'i Babuloni rwegereje, kandi ruzaza gitungura, ruyisige ari umusaka mu isaha imwe. Kuko ibyaha byayo ari bikuru; niyo mpamvu, urubanza rwayo ari rukuru.

Ubuhungiro bwanyu bwonyine buri mu mutekano w'amaboko yanjye kandi ntawe ushobora kubakura mu maboko yanjye. Ntimukabe akazuyazi, kuko ibihe byo gutandukanya byamaze gutangira. Ku bantu: muzahungira he ubwo nzaba nkoranyije umugeni wanjye mbakuyeho? Ntimwasomye? Ntimwumvise?

Ndifuza cyane kwinjira ngo nsangire namwe niba mwakingura urugi rwanyu.

Ibyahishuwe 18

Kandi umucyo w'itabaza ntuzongera kumurika muri wowe ukundi: n'ijwi ry'umukwe n'umugeni ntirizongera kumvikana muri wowe ukundi: kuko abacuruzi bawe bari abakomeye bo mu isi: kuko amahanga yose yayobejwe n'uburozi bwawe.

Ijwi ry'umukwe n'umugeni ntirizongera kumvikana ukundi kuko nzakoranya umugeni wanjye nkamuhisha mu byumba nateguriye igihe kizaza, kuko mu nzu ya Data harimo amazu menshi.
