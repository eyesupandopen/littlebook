Ubuhamya Bubiri: Isirayeli
Ibisharira n'Ibiryoshye
4 Kanama 2024

Ubutumwa Bwiza Bw'Iteka!

Umva Amagambo ya Yeshua HaMashiach kandi utege amatwi wumvira. Kandi uko ibihe bihinduka n'umunsi ugeze aho amakuba azagwira isi gitumo. Nzakoranyiriza abizerwa banjye aho ndi mu bicu gitumo kandi kurimbuka kuzagwira isi gitumo kandi isi muzi izahinduka iteka ryose, ntizigera isubira muri iyi mimerere yononekaye kandi y'icyaha. Nubwo iherezo ry'ingoma y'icyaha ya Satani rigaragara, uwo munsi ntuzaza byuzuye kugeza igihe imbaraga zose, n'ubutware bw'Ikiyoka bizakurwaho mu kugaruka kwanjye kwa kabiri ubwo nzaza gutegeka isi mu gukiranuka ndi kumwe n'abarwanyi bo mu ijuru, ndetse n'ingabo zikomeye zo mu ijuru zirimo umuganura w'abazutse mu bapfuye kandi nzabikoranyiriza aho ndi.

Kuko abazaba bari kumwe nanjye bahamagawe, batoranyijwe, kandi ni abizerwa n'abanyakuri. Kandi bigaragaje ko ari abizerwa mu bintu bike, bityo nzabagira abategetsi ba byinshi kandi binjira mu byishimo by'Umwami wabo. Bazaba ingabo zidashobora kurimbuka kandi z'iteka zidashobora kuneshwa cyangwa guhagarikwa, kandi bazahonyora munsi y'ibirenge byabo ibice byose by'Ubwami bw'abanzi mu kurimbuka gusesuye no kugandukira ibintu byose bibi. Kuko guhora ari ukwanjye, niko Uwiteka avuga, kandi nzishyura. Kandi njye, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndi Umwami w'abami n'Umutware w'abatware, kandi mu gukiranuka ndaza guca urubanza no kurwana intambara.

Nuko rero mwumve amagambo yanjye kandi musobanukirwe ibyiringiro by'ibyaremwe byose, kuko n'ibyaremwe bitegereje cyane guhishurwa kw'Abana b'Imana! Kuko ibyaremwe byagandukijwe iby'ubusa, bitabishaka, ahubwo kubw'uwabigandukije mu byiringiro: Kuko ibyaremwe ubwabyo nabyo bizabaturwa mu bubata bwo kubora, bikagera mu mudendezo w'ubwiza bw'abana b'Imana. Kandi nanjye, Yesu Kristo, nzabatura ibyaremwe mu gukiranuka kandi nzakiza isi kandi ntegeke mu gukiranuka. Kandi nzakuraho ubutware bwose n'ingoma y'umwanzi, kandi ubwami bwa Satani ntibuzongera kubyuka ukundi.

Nk'uko byanditswe, niko bizaba, kandi niko nategetse Yohana Uhishurirwa kwandika: Kandi mbona ijuru rikinguye, mbona ifarashi yera, kandi uyihetsweho yitwa Uwizigirwa n'Umunyakuri, kandi aca imanza zitabera akarwana intambara mu gukiranuka. Amaso ye yari ameze nk'ibirimi by'umuriro, kandi ku mutwe we hari amakamba menshi, kandi yari afite izina ryanditswe, ritazwi n'umuntu wese keretse we ubwe. Kandi yari yambaye umwenda winitswe mu maraso, kandi izina rye yitwa, Jambo ry'Imana. Kandi ingabo zo mu ijuru zamukurikiye ku mafarashi yera, zambaye imyenda y'ibitare myiza, yera kandi itunganye. Kandi mu kanwa ke havamo inkota ityaye, kugira ngo ayikubite amahanga: kandi azayategeka inkoni y'icyuma: kandi asarura mu rwengero rw'ubukana n'umujinya by'Imana Ishoborabyose. Kandi ku mwenda we, no ku kibero cye yari afite izina ryanditswe ngo, UMWAMI W'ABAMI, N'UMUTWARE W'ABATWARE. Kandi uko niko ibyiringiro byo kugaruka kwanjye kwa kabiri biri, kandi uwo munsi ugenda wegereza uko amasegonda ahita.

Dore! Kuri uwo munsi nzazana abarwanyi banjye kuko uwo ari wo murage wabo, ndetse n'ingabo zitagereranywa, kuko ingabo nk'izanjye zitigeze zibaho, ingabo zidashobora kuneshwa zigizwe n'abacunguwe, ndetse n'abavutse bapfuye mu byaha n'ibicumuro ariko ubu bakazabaho mu buzima bw'iteka kandi badashobora kwicwa cyangwa kuneshwa. Kuko nahawe imbaraga na Yahweh kubw'iyo mpamvu, kandi nzaba narabahaye imbaraga n'ingororano kubw'ubudahemuka bwabo n'urukundo bankunda n'izina ryanjye, ndetse Yesu Kristo! Kuko ukunda kandi agakorera Yeshuah HaMashiach aba akunda kandi akorera Yahweh, kuko nta wusumba Imana ikomeye y'iteka ya Isirayeli! Kuri uwo munsi wegereje muzabona ijuru rikinguye, kandi muzambona, Yesu Kristo, nyoboye ingabo zikomeye zo mu ijuru zivuye mu ijuru, kandi izaba ari ingabo zitagereranywa, iteka ryose! Kuko Umugeni wanjye azaba ari kumwe nanjye.

Kandi nzamenagura rwose inyamaswa n'umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma n'ingabo zabo zose n'ibizira byabo byose bakoze mu buryo buteye ishozi kandi niko bazarimbuka ndetse no kubw'umucyo wo kuza kwanjye n'iyo nkota ityaye amugi abiri iva mu kanwa kanjye, kandi ntibazahagarara ahubwo bazarimbuka rwose. Kandi inyamaswa n'umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma bazajugunywa ari bazima mu nyanja yaka umuriro n'amazuku kandi ntibazigera barekurwa mu bubabare bwabo, kuko bigize abakwiriye ibyo. Ariko mbere isi igomba kugarurwa binyuze mu makuba ya Yakobo kandi benshi bazaza aho ndi, ndetse n'imbaga nyamwinshi n'ubugingo bwose bwambaza izina ry'Umwami kugeza ku iherezo buzakizwa rwose, kandi Isirayeli igomba gukoranywa kuko nzaba nzakiza asigaye muri Yakobo bazaza ku Mesiya wabo w'ukuri kandi w'iteka, kuko ndi Yeshua HaMashiach kandi ndaza nk'uko amasezerano yahawe ba Data yatangajwe kera na Yahweh ubwe, kandi ndi isohozwa ryayo kandi igihe cyanjye kirageze.

Nuko rero ku banze izina ryanjye rikiza, ndetse Yesu w'i Nazareti, ndabagira inama yo kumva amagambo yanjye no gusobanukirwa ibihe murimo kandi mukirukira ijwi ryanjye kandi mukambaza izina ryanjye nonaha kandi mukanyatura nk'Umwami wanyu, kuko nta gihe mufite cyo guta. Nk'uko hariho ababaye abambere mu kuvuka gushya mu ivuka ry'itorero ryanjye ku munsi wa Pentekote nk'uko byanditswe mu byanditswe, niko hazabaho abanyuma bazaza mu kuvuka gushya muri iki gihe cy'ubuntu, ndetse n'abaza mu gakiza bose uko gusuka kwanjye umwuka wera mu gihe cyanjye cy'ubuntu n'imbabazi kugenda kugera ku musozo.

Kandi nuko inzara y'ibintu byose by'Imana izagwa ku isi kandi icyaha ntikizaba kigikumiriwe uko nkuraho ungumya igihe nkoranya Itorero ryanjye. Umuntu wa kamere yanze ijwi ryanjye, umuntu wa kamere yanze ubuntu bwanjye, umuntu wa kamere yanze imbabazi zanjye, umuntu wa kamere yanze ubutumire bwanjye bwo gukiranuka, kandi umuntu wa kamere yaranyanze Yesu w'i Nazareti bityo umuntu wa kamere yanze Yahweh Imana ikomeye y'iteka yantumye, kandi ubu nzaha isi umwijima yifuza cyane.

Ariko musobanukirwe ko benshi bazankorera imirimo ikomeye, n'abatangabuhamya banjye babiri, ndetse n'ibitereko byanjye by'amatara bibiri bizahanura, kandi nzohereza abanjye 144,000 gukiza benshi, nuko ntimutinye! Agakiza kazagerwaho, ndetse binyuze mu kurimbuka kw'umubiri kandi imbaga nyamwinshi izaza mu gakiza muri iki gihe. Kuko mu by'ukuri, Yahweh ntiyigeze kandi ntazigera yisiga adafite ubuhamya!

Ibihe by'Abanyamahanga byeze imbuto nyinshi mu bari kuzaguma badafite ibyiringiro iyo bataba barashyizwe mu masezerano yahawe ba Data, ndetse n'ababeshwaho no kwizera Umwana w'Imana wabakunze akitanga kubw'agakiza kabo. Kuko nk'uko byanditswe: Ukiranuka azabeshwaho no kwizera! Kandi nuko binyuze mu kunyizera, Kristo Yesu, babaye abana bo kwizera, ndetse abana bo kwizera kwa Aburahamu. Kuko Aburahamu yizeye Imana mbere y'uko ahabwa ikimenyetso cyo gukebwa, kandi byamuhwaniye no gukiranuka, nk'uko Mose abihamya! Kandi nuko Aburahamu ni se w'abizera bose n'abatsindishirijwe no kwizera Yesu Kristo Ukiranuka, kuko ndi umukiranutsi n'utsindishiriza abizera izina ryanjye rikomeye bose!

Nyamara benshi bakoresheje ubuntu bwanjye n'imbabazi nk'uruhushya rwo gukora icyaha, ndetse bishimira kandi birata inzira zabo z'icyaha n'irimbuka, ndetse bigisha abana babo kubaho mu nzira zabo z'icyaha bityo ibisekuruza ni bibi mu nzira zabyo kandi inzira zabyo ni izo kwangwa bityo abantu babi n'abayobya barushijeho kuba babi. Ntimuyobe, niba umuntu abiba mu bya kamere uwo muntu azasarura kubora mu bya kamere. Kandi abanga icyifuzo cyanjye cy'ubuzima bw'iteka bose ahubwo bagahitamo kubora kwa Adamu bazasarura imbuto z'uko kubora mu rubanza rw'iteka, kuko urubanza ruri hano kandi ibihe byo guhishura byageze.

Ni ukuri, ni ukuri, ibihe by'urubanza rukomeye byahanuwe kera na Enoki umwanditsi (kuko ubuhamya bwe bwatangiwe iki gisekuruza), byaje ku isi nk'uko byanditswe kandi bose bazabibazwa. Abemeye icyifuzo cyanjye cy'agakiza kandi bagasangwa ari abizerwa muri Kristo mu kuboneka kwanjye bazahindurwa iteka ryose, baba barasinziriye muri Kristo cyangwa baba bariho kandi baguma muri Kristo, abari muri Kristo mu kuboneka kwanjye bazatwarwa mu ijuru ry'amajuru. Kuba muri Kristo ni ukugendera mu mwuka wanjye ukomeye, ndetse n'itegeko ry'umwuka w'ubuzima! Kandi ababayeho mu gukiranuka mbere y'ivuka ry'Itorero ryanjye no ku bera bo mu makuba ni umuzuko w'abakiranutsi, aho abakiranutsi bazazukira mu buzima bushya mu ngororano ikomeye, y'iteka kandi ihoraho. Ku iherezo ry'ingoma yanjye y'imyaka 1000 hazabaho urubanza rw'intebe y'era ikomeye kandi ababayeho bose bakishimira gukiranirwa n'icyaha bazishyurwa hakurikijwe imirimo yabo kandi benshi bazagwa mu rupfu rwa kabiri kuko ariko bahisemo.

Kandi nuko ibihe byo guhishura byageze, kandi ubutumwa bwiza bw'iteka bwahishuwe mu gihe cyabwo ndetse n'agatabo gato kari mu ntoki z'umumarayika wanjye ukomeye ubu karahishuwe kandi kuvuga kw'inkuba indwi kwatangajwe! Kuko uyu murimo ari umurimo w'iteka, kandi ni isongo y'amasezerano n'ibihango bya kera mu masezerano n'ibihango by'ibishya, ndetse Isezerano Rishya ryanjye nashyizeho umukono mu maraso yanjye bwite. Kuko njye Yeshua HaMashiach, ndetse Yesu Kristo Ukiranuka ari we Mwami w'Abami n'Umutware w'Abatware, ndi uwo watanzwe kugira ngo asohoze amasezerano yose n'ibihango byose.

Kuko Yohana Uhishurirwa yanditse ibyo yabonye, nuko arandika: Kandi mbona undi mumarayika ukomeye amanuka ava mu ijuru, yambaye igicu, kandi umukororombya wari ku mutwe we, kandi mu maso he hari hameze nk'izuba, n'ibirenge bye nk'inkingi z'umuriro. Kandi yari afite mu ntoki ze agatabo gato kabumbuye: kandi ashyira ikirenge cye cy'iburyo ku nyanja, n'icy'ibumoso ku butaka. Kandi nanone Yohana Uhishurirwa aratangaza: Kandi mbona undi mumarayika aguruka hagati mu ijuru, afite ubutumwa bwiza bw'iteka, bwo kubwiriza abari mu isi, no ku mahanga yose, n'imiryango, n'indimi, n'amoko, avuga n'ijwi rirenga ati, mwubahe Imana, kandi muyihe icyubahiro, kuko isaha y'urubanza rwayo igeze: kandi muramye iyaremye ijuru n'isi, n'inyanja, n'amasoko y'amazi.

Kandi nuko byanditswe kandi nuko bikozwe. Ubutumwa bwiza bw'iteka bwaje guhishurwa mu gihe cyabwo kandi agatabo gato ntikikiri ibanga. Umumarayika ukomeye wambaye igicu n'umukororombya wari ku mutwe we kandi mu maso he hari hameze nk'izuba n'ibirenge bye nk'inkingi z'umuriro. Umukororombya ni ikimenyetso cyatanzwe mu isezerano rya Nowa Yahweh yagiranye na Nowa nyuma y'umwuzure ko atazigera yongera kurengera isi yose. Igicu n'inkingi z'umuriro bigereranya isezerano ry'Imana na Mose, ryubatswe hejuru y'amasezerano yabanje. Kuko ubwo Mose yari amaze kuvuga amategeko yose ku bantu bose nk'uko amategeko abivuga, yafashe amaraso y'ibimasa n'ay'ihene, n'amazi, n'ubwoya butukura, na ezobu, amisha ku gitabo, no ku bantu bose nuko Isezerano rya Kera rishyirwaho umukono!

Kandi nk'uko byanditswe mu Baheburayo: Kuko niba amaraso y'amapfizi n'ay'ihene, n'ivu ry'ishashi rimishijwe ku bahumanye, ryeza kugira ngo umubiri utunganywe: Amaraso ya Kristo, we wituye Imana adafite inenge binyuze mu Mwuka w'iteka, azarushaho gukiza imitima yanyu imirimo ipfuye kugira ngo mukorere Imana ihoraho? Kandi kubw'iyo mpamvu ni we muhuza w'isezerano rishya, kugira ngo binyuze mu rupfu, kubw'igucungurwa kw'ibicumuro byari mu isezerano rya mbere, abahamagawe bahabwe isezerano ry'umurage w'iteka.

Kandi nuko nk'uko byanditswe niko byashohojwe abizerwa banjye, kandi niko Isezerano Rishya riri. Kandi nk'uko ari El Shaddai wategetse umucyo kumurika mu mwijima mu ntangiriro, mu buryo nk'ubwo Imana Ishoborabyose yamuritse mu mitima yacu kugira ngo itange umucyo w'ubumenyi bw'ubwiza bw'Imana, mu maso ha Yesu Kristo. Nuko rero, mu isezerano ryanjye rishya rifite imbaraga nyinshi, kuko narishyizeho umukono n'amaraso yanjye adafite icyaha, hasohojwe amasezerano yose y'ibihe byose, kuko navuze i Kaluvari mu mwuka wanjye wa nyuma: Birarangiye. Kandi nuko isezerano rishya ryashyizweho umukono n'amaraso yanjye mu Isezerano Rishya!

Kandi nuko muri njye, Kristo Yesu, amasezerano ya kera asohozwa n'ubwiza bugaragara, ndetse mu maso y'umukiza wanyu w'iteka na Mesiya, ndetse njye Yeshuah HaMashiach. Kandi uyu mumarayika Yohana Uhishurirwa asobanura yari afite ikirenge kimwe ku nyanja n'ikirenge kimwe ku butaka kuko agakiza kanjye gafunguriwe abavuye mu nyanja y'amahanga, (Abanyamahanga) n'abavuye mu gihugu cy'isezerano ndetse n'abamize Umwami wabo w'iteka bose, ndetse n'abangana n'abo Umwami Imana yacu izahamagara! Kandi nuko ubutumwa bwanjye bwiza bw'iteka buzabona amaso, amatwi, n'imitima y'abo bwoherejweho bose kandi benshi bazaza mu gakiza kuko buri jambo ry'Imana ari rizima kandi ry'iteka kandi nta jambo na rimwe rya Yahweh rizigera rinanirwa cyangwa ngo rihite kugeza byose bisohojwe. Kuko kuri Yahweh amasezerano yose ari yego na amena kandi yose azasohozwa kandi njye, Kristo Yesu, nitanze mu bubabare bukomeye kugira ngo nshyireho ikimenyetso ku isezerano ryanjye rishya mu maraso yanjye bwite adafite icyaha, rimwe rizima kandi kugeza mu iteka ryose isezerano rishya rizabaho.

Nuko rero mwumve amagambo yanjye kandi musobanukirwe imvugo yanjye, ndetse ijwi ry'Umwami wanyu na Mesiya. Ibihe byageze bana banjye kandi amagambo yanjye yuzuye isi kandi abahanuzi banjye n'abakozi banjye batwikiriye impera z'isi, kandi mu ndimi zose, n'amoko, n'amahanga ubutumwa bwanjye bwiza bw'agakiza bwumvikanye, ndetse kugeza ku mpera z'isi. Kandi nuko bimeze, kandi nuko ntangaza: Inkuru yanjye nziza yubatswe buri gihe ku rufatiro rw'amategeko n'abahanuzi kandi ndi ibuye rikuru ry'imfuruka urusengero rwose rw'Imana ihoraho rwubakiyeho! Bana banjye, muri urusengero rw'Imana ihoraho!

Kandi nuko mbaha gusobanukirwa ko hari ibice byinshi by'ubutumwa bwiza bwa Kristo! Ese muri abapfapfa bihagije ngo mwizere ko hari ubutumwa bwiza bune gusa? Kandi hari n'ibindi bintu byinshi nakoze, ibyo, iyo biza kwandikwa byose, ndatekereza ko n'isi ubwayo itari gukwirwamo ibitabo byari kwandikwa. Kandi nuko hari inkuru nziza yo kuza kwanjye kwa mbere, hari inkuru nziza y'ibanga ry'Imana nahishuriye Pawulo Intumwa, hari ubutumwa bwiza bw'iteka butuyobora ku isongo y'ibintu byose. Kuri Yahweh nta na rimwe habaho iherezo ry'inkuru nziza!

Kandi nuko mbabajije ikibazo? Ese muri abajyanama ba Yahweh? Ese abanyatewolojiya banyu b'abanyabinyoma n'abakozi b'akazuyaze bashobora kugira inama Yahweh? Uri inde, wa muntu we, kugena igihe n'uburyo Yahweh avuga n'ibirimo n'imiterere y'Amagambo ye, cyangwa uwo ahitamo koherereza amagambo ye? Kandi nuko bimeze. Amagambo y'Imana azakomeza gutemba kandi ubuhamya bwose buzarangizwa kandi ibitabo bizandikwa kugeza mu iteka kuko Ijambo ry'Imana ari rizima kandi rihoraho iteka!

Kandi nuko ndababaza, ese Isezerano Rishya rya Kristo Yesu ryasibye Isezerano rya Kera n'ibihango bya Kera? Imana ibirinde! Buri Jambo ry'Imana riraboneye kandi yose azasohozwa ndetse rimwe, yose, n'iteka ryose! Kandi nuko imiterere y'Imana idahinduka iri! Irungu ry'intebe yayo hari inzovu inshuro inzovu nyamara ntikeneye umujyanama. Yahweh, Imana ikomeye y'iteka ikora ibintu byose ikurikije inama y'ubushake bwayo kandi ni Imana y'iteka! Nyamara yahishuye imigambi yayo, ndetse n'ubushake bwayo bw'iteka kandi buhoraho mu byiciro. Kuki Yahweh yakora ikintu nk'icyo mushobora kubaza? Ntimwasomye ibyo Pawulo Intumwa yanditse ubwo namutegekaga kwandika? Ahubwo tuvuga ubwenge bw'Imana mu ibanga, ndetse ubwenge buhishe, Imana yagennye mbere y'isi, kubw'ubwiza bwacu. Ubwo nta n'umwe mu batware b'iyi si yamenye: kuko iyo babumenya, ntibari kubamba Umwami w'ubwiza.

Kandi nuko Imana y'iteka, ndetse Yahweh udakeneye umujyanama kandi mu bwenge bwayo bw'iteka ihishura inzira zayo n'Amagambo yayo mu buryo ihitamo mu gusohoza imigambi yayo y'iteka kandi ihoraho y'incungu ku bintu byose. Musobanukirwe bakundwa banjye, ndetse n'Umugeni wanjye, ko mwabaye mu mutima wa Yahweh kuva mbere y'irema kandi mwahishwe mu buhanga mu mutima wa Data kandi mwahishwe icyo muzaba cyo mu kuboneka kwanjye, kuko mwapfuye kandi ubuzima bwanyu buhishe muri njye Kristo Yesu. Kandi ubwo nzaboneka, namwe muzabonekana nanjye mu bwiza! Bana banjye bato mugiye kuremwa bundi bushya muri njye kandi muzatangazwa n'ibyo Yahweh yateguriye abamukunda. Kuko nk'uko bimeze nta muntu ku isi wasobanukiwe byuzuye neza icyo abana b'Imana bazaba cyo mu gucungurwa kwabo kwuzuye, kandi mugiye kubimenya kuko umunsi ugeze kandi kuboneka kwanjye kuregereje.

Ngira inama umwe kandi ngira inama bose, ndetse Kristo Yesu Umwami wanyu w'iteka! Mwakire Amagambo yanjye kandi mugire ubwenge no gusobanukirwa, kuko kamere yanyu y'ukuri ari ibanga ry'iteka rihishe mu Mana ubwayo rigiye guhishurwa byuzuye. Vuba cyane muzisanga muhagaze mu cyumba cy'intebe y'ubwami cya Yahweh, ndetse Data, kandi ategereje n'amatsiko kandi yifuza cyane ukuhaba kwanyu nonaha, ndetse no mu bihe bya kera by'iteka. Kandi ibyumba binini mu ijuru byateguriwe kuza kwanyu kandi ameza arateguye kandi ubukwe bw'Umukwe n'Umugeni we buri hafi kandi umugeni wanjye yiteguye kandi nzabakira aho ndi kandi iteka ryose bazabana nanjye mu isezerano ry'iteka kandi bazakora imirimo myinshi ikomeye itarinjira mu mitima y'abantu muri iki gihe.

Kandi nuko Yahweh Imana ikomeye y'iteka kandi ihoraho yateguye kandi itunganya ibintu byinshi kubwanyu, kandi njye, Kristo Yesu Umwami washyizwe hejuru kandi wazamutse mfite ingororano yanyu mu ntoki zanjye ndetse no ku bizerwa banjye. Kandi ingabo zo mu ijuru zitegereje n'amatsiko ukuhaba kwanyu kandi mu ngororano yo mu ijuru kandi y'iteka ubu muzajyayo. Nuko ntimutinye kuko ubuzima bwanyu ubu bugiye guhishurwa kandi isegonda iryo ari ryo ryose ubu impanda ya nyuma izavuga, mwiteguye kuboneka kwanjye bana banjye bato? Kuko biri hafi kuruta uko mubitekereza kandi igihe hagati y'icyo gihe n'ubu nta gaciro gifite kandi gikorera imigambi y'iteka ya Yahweh Imana ikomeye y'iteka!

Ntimubizi? cyangwa ntimwabyumvise, ko Imana y'iteka, Uwiteka yaremye impera z'isi? ntacika intege, cyangwa ngo ananirwe kandi nta kurondora ubwenge bwe.

Ndi uvuga ibintu bizaza nk'aho byamaze kuba.

Ndi uwabahamagariye ubuzima bw'iteka kandi akabashyiraho ikimenyetso cy'Imana ihoraho!

Ndi ibuye rikuru ry'imfuruka inyubako yose yubakiyeho.

Ndi Yeshua HaMashiach kandi nzakiza Yakobo!

Ndi umukiranutsi n'utsindishiriza abizera izina ryanjye rikomeye bose!

Dore! Nohereje aya Magambo binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye kandi binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye aya magambo yoherejwe!

Mwigumye mu isuku bizerwa banjye, kandi ntimwemerere amaboko yanyu kwandura! Mukomeze mwiyuhagire mu maraso yanjye y'impongano kugira ngo mushobore guhagarara imbere yanjye muremwe bundi bushya kandi mufite isuku nk'isugi kuko nabuhagiye mu maraso yanjye y'impongano. Murebe mu kirere! Ibimenyetso byanjye bitangwa ku isi yose ubu kandi biri mu ijuru kandi biri ku isi yose kandi ibise ntibihagarara. Gucungurwa kwanyu kwuzuye kuri hano, gusa muhagarare mushikamye kandi muri abizerwa kugeza ku iherezo, kuko iherezo ryanyu ari iry'iteka aho iteka ryose ritegereje!
