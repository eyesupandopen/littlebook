Ibisharira Biryoshye
3 Ukuboza 2024

Amaraso y'Umwana w'Intama wa Yahweh

Nimuze ku Mwami wanyu uhoraho mwumve Amagambo yanjye, mwa bene wacu, kandi mwunamishe imitima yanyu mu kumvira Amagambo ya Yeshua HaMashiach. Kuko jyewe, Yeshua HaMashiach, nzaha ubwenge umunyabwenge muri mwe, Yewe Isirayeli, arusheho kuba umunyabwenge nimwumvira amabwiriza yanjye kugira ngo mubone kwakira ubundi bwenge n'ubumenyi kuko ubwenge n'ubumenyi bizaba gukomera kw'ibihe byanyu n'imbaraga z'agakiza, kubaha Uwiteka ni ryo butunzi bwanyu.

Nuko rero, musobanukirwe ibihe murimo kugira ngo mugendere mu kuri kandi imigambi yanjye y'agakiza isohore rwose muri mwe abahamagawe mu izina ryanjye, ndetse nawe Yewe Isirayeli, nubwo kugeza ubu mutanzi cyangwa ngo mugendere mu nzira zanjye kuko Isirayeli itegekwa na Esawu na Ashkenaz, kandi byaravuzwe neza ko mukuru azakorera muto kandi nta n'umwe hagati ya Esawu na Ashkenaz ufite uburenganzira ku murage w'uburiza bwa Yakobo. Nyamara, Yakobo yanze Yeshua HaMashiach nuko mpindukirira amahanga mu gihe runaka kugira ngo nsohoze ibyo Isirayeli itigeze yumvira ngo ibisohoze byuzuye ko binyuze muri Isirayeli amahanga azahabwa umugisha kandi ko mu miryango yazimiye hazarokoka asigaye.

Nyamara, nimuhindukira mu mitima yanyu mukumvira ijwi ry'Amagambo y'amabwiriza ya Mesiya wanyu w'ukuri muzagarurwa mube ubwoko bwihariye bwa Yahweh, nubwo ba sokuruza banyu bateye umugongo Yahweh kera. Yego, bateye umugongo Yahweh igihe banyangaga, jyewe Yesu w'i Nazareti, ari we Yeshua HaMashiach, ndetse no mu minsi Ponto Pilato yategekaga Yudaya igihe natangwaga n'abatambyi babo bakuru n'urukiko rwa Sanhedrini mu maboko y'abanyabyaha kugira ngo bakore ibyo amategeko yababuzanyije rwose gukora, nyamara mu rwango rwabo kuri njye no ku bintu byose bya Yahweh babikoze babikunze n'igomwa n'ubugome bwinshi. Kandi uko ni ko agakiza ka Adamu gasohozwa n'amaboko y'abanzi be, kugira ngo uwizera wese akambaza izina rya Yesu w'i Nazareti ari we Yeshua HaMashiach azakizwe rwose.

Kandi bimeze bityo kuko ndi umwana w'intama wa Yahweh, ubundi se impongano y'ibyaha by'abantu yari gusohozwa ite keretse amaraso yanjye atagira icyaha amenwe mu gitambo kugira ngo yishyure ikiguzi kidashobora kugereranywa. Musobanukirwe ko nubwo umuntu wa kamere yirata mu bwibone bwinshi ko yigize kandi yihagije muri byose, umuntu wa kamere yaguye mu mimerere y'icyaha n'iteye agahinda yo gukiranirwa n'umugayo akwiriye rwose.

Nyamara, mu buntu bwanjye n'imbabazi no kwitanga kwanjye i Gologota igihe niyerekanye imbere ya Yahweh nk'umwana w'intama w'Imana igihe nitangaga mu bubabare bwinshi uko igitonyanga cyanjye cya nyuma cy'amaraso cyatembaraga i Gologota, kugeza no ku mwuka wanjye wa nyuma igihe navugaga amagambo nti birarangiye. Kandi narahambwe, nuko nzuka ku munsi wa gatatu nk'uko ibyanditswe bivuga; nk'uko Yahweh yasezeranyije ko azabikora, kuko bidashoboka ko urupfu rwari kugumana amaraso atagira icyaha. Kandi Yahweh yaranzuye aho, nyuma yaho gato ninjiye ahera cyane mu ijuru ubwaho, aho niyerekanye nk'umutambyi mukuru iteka ryose imbere ya Yahweh, NDIHO ukomeye, igitambo yemeye iteka ryose. Nuko rero ubwo mufite umutambyi mukuru ukomeye utyo, winjiye mu ijuru, ari we Yesu umwana wa Yahweh, dukomeze kwatura kwacu. Kuko mudafite umutambyi mukuru, Yewe Isirayeli, udashobora kwibabarana natwe mu ntege nke zacu, ahubwo yageragejwe muri byose kimwe natwe, nyamara ntiyagira icyaha.

Nuko rero ndabaza, Yewe Isirayeli, ni iki cyarangiye? Nuko nsubiza nweruye nti: Isezerano ryanjye rishya, ari ryo sezerano rihoraho ndetse n'Isezerano ryanjye Rishya! Kuko isezerano rikomezwa iyo abantu bapfuye: kuko nta mbaraga riba rifite igihe cyose uwarigize akiriho; kandi hafi ibintu byose byezwa n'amaraso nk'uko amategeko abivuga, kandi amaraso atamenwe nta kubabarirwa ibyaha kubaho. Kimwe n'Isezerano rya Kera igihe Mose yari amaze kubwira abantu amategeko yose, nk'uko Amategeko abivuga, yafashe amaraso y'inyana n'ay'ihene, n'amazi n'ubwoya bw'intama bw'umuhengeri na ezobu, abimisha ku gitabo no ku bantu bose, ati: Aya ni amaraso y'isezerano, Imana yabategetse.

Nyamara, isezerano rya Kera, ndetse n'isezerano rya mbere rya Mose ntabwo ryari rigambiriwe kuba iherezo rya gahunda ya Yahweh yo gucungura abantu, cyangwa ngo Isirayeli yari ikwiriye guteranira hamwe mu bwibone, ikora udutsiko twitandukanya mu byiciro byinshi bidakenewe, kandi byica. Ahubwo Isirayeli yari igambiriwe kuba urumuri rw'agakiza ku banyamahanga kugira ngo amahanga abone ko mu Isirayeli hari Imana kandi kugira ngo amahanga yisuke mu Isirayeli kwiga ibya Yahweh nk'uko byanditswe mu bihe bya kera.

Nuko rero, musobanukirwe imiterere y'igihe gito ya Mose nk'uko byakorwaga n'Isirayeli mu bihe mbere y'Isezerano ryanjye Rishya kandi musobanukirwe ko Mose abaho byuzuye muri Mesiya wanyu w'ukuri, ari we wera w'Imana wabayeho mu buryo bwuzuye nk'uko Mose abivuga kandi watanze ubuzima bwe rimwe, rwose, n'iteka ryose kugira ngo yishyure igihano cya Mose ku bwanyu. Nk'uko byanditswe, nuko rero, musobanukirwe Isezerano ryanjye Rishya, Yewe Isirayeli: Kuko Amategeko afite igicucu cy'ibyiza bizaza, atari ishusho nyayo y'ibyo bintu, ntashobora na rimwe kweza abayegera binyuze muri ibyo bitambo, batamba uko umwaka utashye ubudahagarara. Kuko iyo biba bityo ntibari kureka kubitamba, kuko abayegera, bamaze kwezwa rimwe, ntibari kongera kugira umutimanama w'ibyaha? Ariko muri ibyo bitambo, habaho kwibuka ibyaha uko umwaka utashye.

Kuko bidashoboka ko amaraso y'amapfizi n'ihene yakuraho ibyaha. Kandi bimeze bityo bene wacu, ko Mose yashoboraga gusa kweza ibyaha by'igihe gito, nyamara ibyaha byinshi munsi ya Mose byaragumaga aho abakoraga ibyo bintu bacibwaga mu bantu iteka ryose kubera ibyaha byabo. Nuko rero, Mose yari igicucu cy'ibintu bizaza, ibyo bintu bikaba byuzuzwa byuzuye mu gitambo cyanjye nitanze, kuko amaraso yanjye atagira icyaha atangira impongano y'ibyaha byose, rimwe rwose n'iteka ryose, kugira ngo abakomoka kuri Adamu bose babone agakiza binyuze muri byo. Nyamara ku bemwemera ikimenyetso cy'inyamaswa, nta mbabazi bazigera babona.

Kandi niko byanditswe kuri Yeshua HaMashiach, ko binyuze mu buzima bwanjye no kumvira Adamu yashoboraga kugarurwa kuri Elohim, umuremyi we uhoraho kandi ukiranuka. Nuko rero, ubwo aza mu isi, aravuga ati, ibitambo n'amaturo ntiwabishatse: ariko wanyteguriye umubiri. Ibitambo byoswa, n'ibitambo by'ibyaha, ntiwabishimiye. Nuko ndavuga nti, Dore, ndaje (Mu ntangiriro y'igitabo byanditswe kuri njye) kugira ngo nkore ibyo ushaka, Mana. Haruguru, ubwo yavugaga ati, Ibitambo n'amaturo, n'ibitambo byoswa, n'ibitambo by'ibyaha ntiwabishatse, kandi ntiwabishimiye (bitambwa nk'uko Amategeko abivuga) Nuko aravuga ati, Dore, ndaje gukora ibyo ushaka, Mana, akuraho icya mbere, kugira ngo ashyireho icya kabiri. Muri uko gushaka ni ko twejejwe, binyuze mu gutambwa k'umubiri wa Yesu Kristo rimwe rizima. Kandi buri Mutambyi wa Lewi ahagarara buri munsi akora umurimo, kandi kenshi atamba uburyo bumwe bw'igitambo, budashobora na rimwe gukuraho ibyaha: Ariko uyu mugabo amaze gutamba igitambo kimwe cy'ibyaha, yicara iteka iburyo bw'Imana, Kandi uhereye ubwo arategereza, kugeza abanzi be bagizwe intebe y'ibirenge bye. Kuko mu gitambo kimwe yejeje iteka ryose abejejwe binyuze muri cyo.

Kuko Yahweh na we aduhamya: kuko amaze kuvuga mbere ati, Iri ni ryo Sezerano nzasezerana na bo nyuma y'iyo minsi, niko Elohim avuga, Nzashyira amategeko yanjye mu mitima yabo, kandi mu ntekerezo zabo nzayandikamo. Kandi ibyaha byabo no gukiranirwa kwabo sinzabyibuka ukundi. None aho kubabarirwa ibyo bintu biri, nta gitambo cy'ibyaha kikibaho. Kandi muhagaze mu gihe cy'ubujiji bw'igihugu aho ba So kera banze uwo ari we Mwana w'Intama wa Pasika utunganye wa Yahweh ubwe, ndetse aho Yahweh yakunze abari mu isi cyane byatumye atanga umwana we w'ikinege kugira ngo uwizera izina rya Yeshua HaMashiach wese, ari we Yesu Kristo ndetse uwo muntu azakizwe rwose. Nuko rero ndabahamagarira kuva mu bujiji bwanyu n'ubuhumyi bw'igice, Yewe Isirayeli, ndetse mu kwanga no kwamagana rwose ubwenge bwo gutuka Imana bwa Esawu buri muri Talimudi ye yanduye kandi mugaruke ku Magambo atunganye ya Yahweh, ndetse ayo Magambo akaba yarabitswe mu murongo w'Inyandiko za Byzance, ndetse na Textus Receptus nk'uko yabitswe mu nyandiko ya Stephens.

Nuko rero, musobanukirwe icyo kwanga Yeshua HaMashiach iteka ryose bivuze kuri wowe Isirayeli kugira ngo musobanukirwe uburemere bw'uko kwanga. Kuko niba mukora icyaha nkana nyuma y'uko mumaze kwakira no kwemera uko kuri, nta gitambo cy'ibyaha kikibaho, ahubwo habaho gutegereza urubanza ruteye ubwoba, n'umuriro w'inkazi, uzatsemba abanzi. Uwasuzuguraga Amategeko ya Mose, yapfaga nta mbabazi abihamijwe n'abatangabuhamya babiri, cyangwa batatu: Mutekereza ko azakwiriye igihano kirushijeho gukomera kingana iki, ukandagira Umwana w'Imana, kandi agafata amaraso y'Isezerano Rishya nk'ikintu kidakwiriye. Nuko rero, mwemere igitambo cyanjye kandi mwature ibyaha byanyu kuri njye kugira ngo mbababarire kuko ndi uwo kwizerwa kandi nkiranukira kubababarira ibyaha byanyu kugira ngo mwezwe mu maraso yanjye atanga impongano kandi mwaturirane ibyaha byanyu.

Nuko rero, ndaza kuri mwe mu mbabazi zanjye n'ubuntu bwanjye kugira ngo mbakure mu buhumyi bwanyu bw'igice, Yewe Isirayeli, ndetse kugira ngo mubone agakiza binyuze mu kumvira ijwi ryanjye kandi kugira ngo muve mu kwanga kwa ba So kwashyizweho iteka ryose muri Talimudi yanyu yanduye no mu idini ryanyu ryanduye Esawu yakoze kugira ngo murimbuke, ariko ryo mufatiriyeho nk'aho ubwenge bwe bwatanzwe na Yahweh ubwe, atari Satani. Yewe Isirayeli, umva ijwi ryanjye rikiza kandi wemere urukundo rwanjye kuri wowe kandi uze kuri njye n'imitima yanyu yose ifunguye kandi yiteguye kwakira kugira ngo mwuzuzwe ikuzo ryose n'ingeso nziza.

Ndabaha gusobanukirwa imbabazi zisesuye no kugarurwa amaraso yanjye atanga impongano azanira abanyakira bose nk'Umwami wabo ukiza, ndetse nka Mesiya wabo w'ukuri, kuko ari ko ndi. Kandi nk'uko Adamu yaremwe, akabumbwa, kandi akagirwa utagira icyaha no mu butungane bwuzuye kugeza ku munsi yakoze icyaha nkana, niko n'ikiguzi cy'icyaha cya Adamu gisaba amaraso atagira icyaha, ndetse ikintu kidashoboka ku batuye mu isi mu kubora gutanga. Kandi mu rukundo rwa Yahweh yatanze ubutungane nk'ubwo n'amaraso atagira icyaha mu mubiri w'umwana we w'ikinege utangwa mu gitambo cy'ibyaha bya Adamu, nuko rero nta gitambo cy'ibyaha kikibaho.

Ndi uwo witangiye rwose ibyaha bya Adamu waguye.

Ndi uwo witangiye rwose mu mpongano ihoraho kugira ngo abagandukira agakiza k'iteka bose beze binyuze muri byo.

Ndi uwo uri Umutambyi Mukuru wanyu iteka ryose mu Bwoko bwa Melikisedeki, ndetse n'uwo witangiye nka Pasika itunganye rimwe, rwose, n'iteka ryose.

Ndi Yeshua HaMashiach, ndetse n'uwo uri Mesiya wanyu w'ukuri kandi ingoma yanjye ntigira iherezo.

Ndi Kristo Yesu, kandi nohereje Amagambo yanjye binyuze ku ntumwa yanjye n'umugaragu wanjye kandi binyuze ku ntumwa yanjye n'umugaragu wanjye Amagambo yanjye yoherejwe.

Nuko rero, musobanukirwe ko isohozwa ry'ibintu byose ryaje, Yewe Isirayeli, kandi ndabiyerekezaho binyuze mu Butumwa Bwiza Buhoraho butangwa mu bihe bikwiriye byo guhishurwa kandi ibihe byo guhishurwa ni ubu. Kuko kuva mu ntangiriro Yahweh yabwiye inzoka mu busitani mu bihe icyaha cyinjiraga mu isi, Nzashyira urwango hagati yawe n'umugore no hagati y'urubyaro rwawe n'urubyaro rwe, uzamukomeretsa agatsinsino, na we azagukomeretsa umutwe.

Kandi nuko mu gihe cyagenwe Dawidi, igihe yari umusore w'umushumba, wabaye Umwami w'Isirayeli mbere y'uko ubwami bugabanywa; wabaye n'umugabo uhuje n'umutima wa Yahweh, yarengereye Isirayeli kandi yica icyo gihangange cy'Umuhilisitiya, ari we Goliyati w'i Gati wari urumamfu rukomoka ku Barafayimu! Nuko Dawidi umusore w'umushumba ajya ku rugamba na Goliyati w'i Gati ndetse nk'umushumba, afite inkoni, uruhago n'umuhumetso, ajya kurwana n'umuntu w'igihangange w'umurwanyi, ariko Dawidi yagiye ku rugamba mu izina rya Yahweh, uwo Abafilisitiya basuzuguye, kandi Dawidi ahagarara kurwana n'abanzi b'Isirayeli kugira ngo ingabo za Elohim zitsinde.

Uko ibuye ryo mu muhumetso wa Dawidi ryatumye iki gihangange kidakiranuka kigwa hasi cyikubita, Dawidi yakuye inkota y'iki gihangange ubwacyo amuca umutwe. Kandi nk'uko byanditswe kera: Kandi Dawidi yafashe umutwe w'Umuhilisitiya, awuzana i Yerusalemu, ariko ashyira intwaro ze mu ihema rye. Nuko Dawidi ahamba igihanga cya Goliyati w'i Gati inyuma y'inkuta z'umujyi wa Yerusalemu. Nyamara, mu gitambo cyanjye nitanze nk'umwana w'intama w'Imana, najyanywe inyuma y'inkuta z'umujyi kandi aho i Gologota niho nabambwe, ndetse n'ahantu h'igihanga. Kandi uko nabambwaga, bamanitse kuri icyo giti kigដi aho bakomerekeje agatsinsino kanjye n'imisumari yabo igihe bamanikaga kuri icyo giti kigដi. Kandi nuko intsinzi yabonetse aho kuri Adamu igihe agatsinsino kanjye kakomerekwaga aho i Kaluvari aho umutwe wa Satani nawo wamenwe uko amaraso yanjye yatembaraga azana intsinzi ku rubyaro rw'inzoka iteka ryose. Nuko rero, mu buryo bumwe nk'uko umusore w'umushumba yatsinze urubyaro rwa Satani ubwe, niko n'Umushumba mukuru yatsinze Satani iteka ryose mu gitambo cyanjye cy'urukundo natangiye ibyaha bya Adamu.

Nyamara, uko napfaga mu bubabare bwinshi nk'umugizi wa nabi usanzwe (ubwo bugizi bwa nabi nkaba ndi umwere) inyuma y'amarembo y'uwo mujyi wera, amaraso yanjye atagira icyaha yamenetse ava mu mubiri wanjye mu ishusho yuzuye y'umuntu w'umunyabyaha w'igitaka gitukura agera ku ncungu yuzuye. Kandi uko amaraso yanjye yatose igitaka gitukura ahantu h'igihanga aho i Kaluvari niho umutwe w'inzoka wakomerekejwe iteka n'iteka ryose, kandi mu gusohozwa kw'ibintu byose icyo kiyoka gishaje ubwacyo kizabohwa kandi kijugunywe iteka mu muriro w'igihano aho inyo yabo idapfa, kuko uwo ari wo murage we.

Kandi ndi uwo uje kurimbura rwose ubwami bw'abanzi kugira ngo ikiyoka kibohwe kandi gishyirweho ikimenyetso mu kuzimu imyaka igihumbi kugira ngo ingoma yanjye yo gukiranuka mu isi ishyirweho. Ku iherezo ryayo kizabohurwa mu gihe cyacyo gito aho kizakoranya ukwigomeka kwacyo kwa nyuma. Kandi uko bagaba igitero ku nkambi y'abera umuriro uzamanuka uva kuri Yahweh ubwe ubatsembe rwose icyo gihe Satani azajugunywa mu muriro w'iteka waka iteka ryose n'amazuku anuka.

Nuko rero, ndabaha gusobanukirwa, Yewe Isirayeli, igihano cya Mose cyishyuwe rwose n'igitambo cya Yeshua HaMashiach ari we Yesu Kristo mu rurimi rw'Icyongereza ndetse kugira ngo musobanukirwe ukuzura kw'igitambo cyanjye n'ubutungane bwagezweho n'amaraso yanjye atanga impongano iteka yamenwe kandi agatangirwa ibyaha bya Adamu. Musobanukirwe byuzuye, Yewe Isirayeli, ko iyo abaguye baba barasobanukiwe ibanga rya Yahweh, ntibari kuzigera babamba Umwami wanyu w'Ikuzo! Kuko binyuze mu kubambwa kwanjye aho inzoka yakomerekeje agatsinsino kanjye by'igihe gito, umutwe w'inzoka ubu wakomerekejwe iteka ryose, ndetse igikomere itazigera ikira.

Nuko rero, Yewe Isirayeli, umva Amagambo yanjye kandi usobanukirwe urukundo rwanjye kuri wowe kandi ko ndikuguhamagara muri iki gihe cya nyuma. Nuko musobanukirwe imvugo yanjye kuko byanditswe ngo: Imana y'amahoro yazuye mu bapfuye Yeshua HaMashiach, umushumba mukuru w'intama, binyuze mu maraso y'isezerano rihoraho, ibatunganye mu mirimo myiza yose, kugira ngo mukore ibyo ishaka, ikorera muri mwe ibishimwa imbere yayo binyuze muri Yesu Kristo, uhabwa icyubahiro iteka ryose, Amen.
