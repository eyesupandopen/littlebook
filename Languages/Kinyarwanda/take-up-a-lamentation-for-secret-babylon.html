Ubuhamya Bubiri: Isirayeli
Ibisharira Biryoshye
3 Kanama 2024

Fata Indirimbo y'Agahinda kuri Babuloni y'Ibanga!

Umva amagambo yanjye kandi wumve ijwi ry'urangurura mu butayu! Ndahamagara, ndahamagara, mbese muranyumva? Mufunge indimi zanyu zuzuye ibyaha muceceke kandi mupfukame mwihana, mwumve ijwi ryanjye rihoraho mu gihe mugishobora, kuko urukundo mbakunda ari rwinshi, kandi ni jye uha amarira menshi mu muvuniko w'umutima ukomeye ku bizaba ku banga ijwi ryanjye. Kuko n'uri uyu mwanya wa nyuma abasubiza bose batumiriwe mu buzima bw'iteka. Nuko rero munyatureho Umwami wanyu, kuko ndi Yesu w'i Nazareti, kandi ni jye Imana ihoraho yatumye kubatabara mbere y'uko amakuba n'urubanza bibageraho. Kuki mwibona nk'abadakwiriye ubugingo buhoraho? Mbese muzahitamo ibirimi by'umuriro w'irimbukiro aho guhitamo inzuri z'amahoro z'uburumbuke aho muzabona urwuri ruhoraho?

Kuko nk'uko inkozi y'ibibi yabambanywe nanjye i Kaluvari izabana nanjye muri paradizo, nanjye nzabakiza rwose niba mungize Umwami wanyu ubu! NTA GIHE CYO GUTA KIHARI. Kuko napfiriye inyuma y'amarembo y'i Yerusalemu hamwe n'abanyabyaha ubwo nafataga umwanya wanyu mu rubanza, kandi muzakizwa nimungarukira mwihana nonaha. Ndababwira ukuri, ukuri: Aya magambo ntiyageze mu matwi yanyu ku bw'impanuka, kandi vuba aha hazabaho irimbuka rikomeye kandi benshi ntibazarokoka, kandi iteka ryose ukwanga kwabo kuzumvikana mu miriro ihoraho yagenewe abahitamo bose kugundira ibyaha byabo no kuguma mu masezerano n'abaguye.

Kwifatanya n'ubwigomeke bwa Satani, ndetse no mu bujiji buhumye, ni ukugira uruhare mu byaha byabo ku bushake, bityo ababikora bose bazahora bikoreye urubanza rwabo. Mbese muzemera icyo mbaha, muzi ko Imana yanzuye mu bapfuye kugira ngo ntapfa ukundi kandi muzi ko ndi wa Mwami ukomeye w'abami n'Umutware w'abatware? Mwihane mu gihe mugishobora, kuko Yehova yiteguye gukinga urugi rw'inkuge, kandi nta gihe gihari kandi isaha iyo ari yo yose nzazamura abizerwa mu ijuru, ndetse n'abakijijwe umujinya bose mu gitambo cyanjye gikiza iteka ryose. Nuko rero muntangaze, Yesu w'i Nazareti nk'Umwami wanyu kandi mumenye ko ndiho iteka ryose kuko Imana yanzuye mu bapfuye kugira ngo ntapfa ukundi.

Bana banjye, nimutege amatwi kandi mukomeze mupfukame mu kwihana gukomeza kandi mwumve ijwi ry'Umwami wanyu Yesu Kristo, ndetse n'uwo Yehova yatumye nk'umwana w'intama w'Imana. Umva Amagambo yanjye, Torero ryanjye ndetse Mugeni wanjye, Kuko nk'umutambyi wanyu mukuru uhoraho mu buryo bwa Melikisedeki nitanze nk'Pasika yanyu itunganye, ndetse ntanga amaraso yanjye atagira icyaha kugeza ku gitonyanga cya nyuma kugira ngo mucungurwe rwose mu mimerere yanyu yaguye y'ubu kandi gucungurwa kwanyu kwuzuye kuri hano, nuko rero mwishime!

Kandi abanyakiriye bose bakatura izina ryanjye rihoraho nk'Umwami wabo bazagira ubugingo buhoraho. Kuko ndi Yesu w'i Nazareti Umwami wanyu uhoraho, kandi nagize abatuwe izina ryanjye bose muri ibi bihe by'ubuntu n'imbabazi abana b'Imana bahoraho. Dore, urukundo Data yabahaye, kugira ngo mwitwe abana b'Imana: ni cyo gituma isi itabamenya, kuko itamenye jye, Kristo Yesu Umwami w'abami n'Umutware w'abatware. Bakundwa cyane, ubu turi abana b'Imana, ariko ntihiragaragara icyo tuzaba cyo: kandi muzi ko nimboneka, muzaba nk'anjye: kuko muzambona uko ndi.

Bana banjye bato, nimwumve ko ngiye kugaragara isaha iyo ari yo yose kuko kuboneka kwanjye mu bicu kuri hano. Kuko jye, Kristo Yesu, nzamanuka mvuye mu ijuru n'ijwi rirenga, n'ijwi rya Marayika mukuru, n'impanda y'Imana: kandi abapfiriye muri Kristo bazabanza kuzuka. Hanyuma twebwe abazima, basigaye, tuzazamurwa hamwe na bo mu bicu, gusanganira Umwami wanyu uhoraho mu kirere: bityo tuzabana n'Umwami wanyu iteka ryose. Mbese muzareka aya magambo abahumurize, bana banjye? Mbese mwaretse Satani atuma mwihakana aya magambo asobanutse yandikiwe bose ngo bayabone? Mbese muzahitamo kuguma mu baciriwe urubanza mu bwigomeke bwinangiye? Kwihakana iki gikorwa cyanditswe neza mu byanditswe n'ukuboko kw'Intumwa yanjye Pawulo ni ukunyihakana kuko nje gukiza abizerwa umujinya kuko uko ni ko amasezerano ya Yehova yahishuwe mu ibanga rikomeye ry'Imana ryanditswe na Pawulo muri yubile nyinshi zishize.

Ndabagira inama: ntimunyihakane, kandi mwinjire mu nkuge mbere y'uko biba bicyererewe. Kuko abahitamo bose kuguma mu manza ziri imbere bazaza aho ndi binyuze mu guhamagara izina ryanjye kugeza ku iherezo, kandi benshi bazababazwa kugeza ku mwuka wabo wa nyuma. Ariko nyamara bazazukira mu bugingo buhoraho n'ingororano ikomeye n'ibyishimo bihoraho! Kandi bityo abahamagariwe iteka ryose bazagera ku murage wabo uhoraho, kuko nta n'umwe ushobora gushikuzwa n'umuto muri mwe mu maboko yanjye ahoraho. Nuko rero mwumve amagambo yanjye kandi mugume mu ntekerezo imwe iri muri jye, Kristo Yesu, kuko ndi Ijambo ry'Imana ryabaye umubiri kandi mpora mvuga ukuri. Mureke kumvira amajwi menshi atagira ukwizera ahakana Amagambo yanjye cyangwa muzisanga mwasigaye muri bo mu gihe ikintu nk'icyo kidakenewe.

Kuko mwebwe abakijijwe banjye, ndetse namwe ubu muri Abana b'Imana Ihoraho, ndetse n'aba Yehova bwite binyuze mu gitangaza cyo kuvuka ubwa kabiri mwakijijwe iteka ryose kandi mwabyawe ubwa kabiri n'imbuto itabora. Nyamara, muri abashyitsi n'abimukira mu bihugu by'amahanga, kandi benshi muri mwe muri mu mipaka y'iyo ndaya ikomeye, ndetse n'ibihugu biri mu minyururu bitegereje urubanza rwabyo rwuzuye n'irimbuka: Kuko umugabane wikubye kabiri w'urubanza watangajwe kuri Babuloni y'Ibanga kandi uko ni ko bizaba. Kuko ni ryari Yehova atigeze akiza abakiranutsi mu Rubanza, kuko nta kiremwa gishobora kwitirira Yehova ubupfu mu buryo bukwiye, kandi ubuhamya bwa Loti burabihamya kugeza n'uyu munsi. Ndetse no mu bihugu biteye ishozi by'i Sodoma n'i Gomora Yehova yohereje Abamarayika be gukiza uyu mugabo umwe w'umukiranutsi wari wicaye mu marembo y'umujyi w'umwanzi.

Kandi nanjye nzakiza Itorero ryanjye ryizerwa, ndetse n'abo bose nashyizeho ikimenyetso cy'uwo mwuka wera w'isezerano kuva ku munsi wa Pentekote mu ivuka ry'Itorero ryanjye ryanditswe mu buhamya bw'Ibyakozwe n'Intumwa! (Ntimwasomye?) Kandi abapfuye bose ari abizerwa muri Kristo kuva mu ntangiriro y'igihe cy'Itorero ryanjye n'abazima bose basigaye bazakoranyirizwa hamwe mu kanya nk'ako guhumbya kandi iteka ryose bazabana nanjye! Kuko ndi uje gutwara abizerwa banjye, abahamagawe, n'abatoranyijwe, ndetse n'Umubiri wanjye n'Itorero ryanjye ndetse n'Umugeni wanjye. Nzacungura abizerwa, ndetse n'abagendeye mu mwuka wanjye n'abihannye ibyaha byabo bose bakangira Umwami bafite umutima wuzuye ukwizera kuko ari bo Isirayeli y'Imana! Kandi iki gikorwa kigiye kuza kizaba gikomeye kandi ihungabana rizaba rikomeye kandi n'ibimenyetso byinshi ukuza kwanjye kwatangajwe kandi kuracyatangazwa mu ijuru n'ibimenyetso hejuru n'ibimenyetso mu isi hasi. Kandi uko ni ko gukoranya umuganura kuzaba kugira ngo bose babibone kandi batangare!

Kandi ijwi ryanjye riracyumvikana muri iyi si yaguye, ribona amaso, amatwi, n'imitima y'abo ryoherejweho bose. Mbese mwumva ijwi rya Yeshua HaMashiach, mbese mumenya ijwi ryanjye? Mbese muzakira amagambo yanjye? Mbese muranyumva uko mvugisha umutima wanyu? Kuko nk'uko byanditswe: imitima y'aba bantu yabaye ibinure kubera kurya cyane, kandi amatwi yabo atinda kumva, n'amaso yabo barayahumirije, kugira ngo batigera bareba n'amaso yabo, bakumva n'amatwi yabo, bakumva mu mitima yabo, bagahinduka, nanjye nkabakiza. Benshi muri aba basinzirijwe n'amagambo ateye ishozi ariko ahumuriza ya Yesu wundi, ariko mwizere mudashidikanya ko nta wundi uhari.

Kuko mu rurimi urwo ari rwo rwose izina ryanjye rihamagarwamo, jye Yesu w'i Nazareti, ndetse Yeshua HaMashiach, nzumva kandi nzakiza rwose abambaza bose kugeza ku iherezo. Kuko amagambo yanjye y'ubwenge, ndetse n'ubutumwa bwiza buhoraho byahishuwe kandi byumvikana hirya no hino ku isi. Kandi amagambo yanjye abona abo yoherejweho, kuko uko ni ko Amagambo y'Imana Isumbabyose, ndetse Yehova, kuko nta Jambo na rimwe ry'Imana rigaruka ubusa, kandi azasohoza icyo yagenewe kandi hahirwa amaso yanyu, kuko areba: n'amatwi yanyu, kuko yumva n'imitima yanyu yuzuye ukwizera, kandi nujuje urukundo rw'Imana mu mitima yanyu bwite ari rwo rutuma mutaka muti Abba Data! Muri abana b'Imana kandi ntihiragaragara icyo muzaba cyo, ariko nimboneka muzabona byuzuye kandi ubuzima bwanyu buhishe muri jye, Kristo Yesu, kandi ubuzima bwanyu bugiye guhishurwa byuzuye kandi ubu ni cyo gihe cyo guhishurwa.

Dore! Babuloni y'Ibanga yicaye iboshye mu gihe amahembe icumi asoza imyiteguro yayo yo kuyirimbura rwose. Ukuri, ukuri, kuri Babuloni y'Ibanga no mu yubile nyinshi ubu nohereje intumwa zanjye, nohereje abahanuzi banjye, nohereje abavugabutumwa banjye, nohereje abashumba banjye, nohereje abigisha banjye, nohereje abigishwa banjye bizerwa n'abavandimwe kandi nohereje ndetse n'abapfakazi mu bwigunge bwabo, kandi nasutse umwuka wanjye rwose muri iyi minsi ya nyuma y'ubuntu n'imbabazi!

Ariko aho kugira ngo amahanga yicare mu magunira n'ivu ashimishwa n'ibimenyetso n'imiburo itangwa y'urubanza rwegereje uko bafata amafoto na videwo byo kwishimisha kwabo. Banze intumwa zanjye zazanye umucyo w'ubutumwa bwanjye bwiza bw'agakiza, bashinyaguriye kandi baseka abahanuzi banjye, bagabye ibitero ku bavugabutumwa banjye, bangiza abashumba banjye, kandi batoteza banangiza abigisha banjye.

Ingabo z'ikiyoka kinini gitukura zikugose babuloni y'ibanga kandi ziri mu mipaka yawe rwose. Kuko ndetse n'abayobozi bawe b'aba Siyoni b'abagambanyi bakugurishije ku gice cy'umugati ku banzi bawe ndetse no guteza imbere imigambi yabo ya gisitani. Kandi El Shaddai yemereye aba basazi gukomeza, ndetse abishyira mu mitima yabo gusohoza imigambi ye. El Shaddai ugucira urubanza arakomeye, yewe ndaya yambaye umutuku yicaye mu bwibone mu binezeza ndetse n'ubwo wambuwe icyubahiro cyose, wambuwe ibyiza byose, wambuwe ndetse n'inzu zawe kuko nahaye inzu zawe n'ibihugu byawe mu maboko y'abanzi bawe. Ndetse no mu maboko y'inyamaswa n'umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma, kuko bakorana bya hafi kugira ngo bakurimbure rwose inyuma y'isura y'igikoresho gishaje. Kuko inyamaswa igiye kujugunya hasi indaya ikomeye iyo inyamaswa yarambiwe gutwara, kandi imigambi yayo yagezweho. Ku bw'ibyo, izatwikwa rwose, kandi irimbuke rwose mu isaha imwe kandi irimbuka ryayo ntiritinda.

Kuko imirongo yabo y'intambara, ndetse n'imirongo yabo itukura yatanzwe mu buryo bw'uburiganya ngo itarengwa, yashyizweho kandi irengwa kenshi kandi nkana. Ntimwizere ibinyoma byabo, cyangwa impamvu zabo z'ibinyoma kuko bashaka kugaragara nk'abakiranutsi mu ntambara yabo igiye kuza kandi inyamaswa n'umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma batarazamuka byuzuye bazazamuka byuzuye bave mu ivu bavuge ko urubanza rw'Imana rwaguye kugira ngo rukoze imigambi yabo, nuko rero ntimwizere na kimwe mu binyoma byabo bito kandi urubanza rwabo rwa nyuma ntirutinda.

Kuko bazakomeza ndetse bagakora ibyaha bibi kuruta Babuloni y'Ibanga, kandi bafite uruhare mu kuyobya Babuloni y'Ibanga rwose no kuyigeza ku irimbuka ryayo ryuzuye, kuko Imana yabishyize mu mitima yabo gusohoza ubushake bwayo. Bakorana mu bwigunge kandi abagaragara nk'abanzi bose bakorana mu ibanga uko bakina bakurikije inyandiko yabo kandi bagakina imyanya yabo ku rubyiniro rw'isi, kandi bazica abo bose mu nzego zabo bahitamo kutitoza no gukina imyanya yabo neza kugira ngo basohoze imigambi y'ikiyoka. Kuko ikiyoka giha inyamaswa imbaraga zacyo n'intebe yacyo y'ubwami, n'ubutware bukomeye, Kandi amahembe icumi ahabwa ububasha nk'abami isaha imwe hamwe n'inyamaswa kandi aba bafite intekerezo imwe, kandi bazaha inyamaswa imbaraga zabo n'ubushobozi bwabo kuko uko ari ko bahawe na Yehova ngo babisohoze. Nimwitegure bana banjye kandi munyigireho kandi mukurikire inzira zanjye, kuko inzira zanjye ari inzira za Yehova, Imana ikomeye ihoraho!

Nimwumve kandi mwitonde, kuko izi nyamaswa z'abagambanyi n'abami babi ari abo mu nzego z'Ikiyoka bayoboye Babuloni y'Ibanga baturutse mu miryango yabo y'ibanga kuva yatangira. Umuntu wihishira kandi akiyoberanya, ndetse n'umuntu mubi, agendana akanwa kagoramye. Yego biyoberanya nk'abandi kuko bose ari abiyoberanya, kandi bihishe mu mahanga yose urumamfu rwabibwe. Bakora ibimenyetso n'amaso yabo: berekana n'amaguru yabo: bigisha n'intoki zabo. Ibintu by'urukozasoni biri mu mitima yabo: batekereza ibibi igihe cyose, kandi bateza amakimbirane. Ku bw'ibyo, irimbuka ryabo rizaza vuba: bazarimbuka gitumo nta gukira. Ibi bintu bitandatu Uhoraho arabyanga: yego, ubugingo bwe bwanga birindwi: Amaso y'ubwibone, ururimi rubeshya, n'amaboko avusha amaraso y'abarengana, umutima utekereza imigambi mibi, amaguru yihutira kwirukira mu bibi, Umuhamya w'ibinyoma uvuga ibinyoma, n'uteza amakimbirane mu bavandimwe.

Mbese muri impumyi? Mbese mwanga kumenya ibimenyetso byabo kugira ngo mukomeze kwicara mu bujiji bw'ubushake ko abayobozi banyu ari abo mu baguye? Mbese ntimwumva ko ababashimisha ari abo mu baguye? Mbese ntimubona ko badateye nkamwe, kuko benshi bihinduye iteka ryose mu busazi bwabo kandi bamwe ni urumamfu kuva bavuka kandi Umubisha ni we Se? Nyamara muhitamo kwicara mu mwijima ku bushake, kandi mukishimira no kuryoherwa mu byaha byanyu uko mwivuruguta mu byondo n'ingurube zabagejeje ku marembo ya kuzimu aho benshi biyerekana mu bujiji mu bwirasi n'ubwibone. Ni cyo cyatumye mvuga mu rwandiko rwanjye rwandikiwe Efeso ruhawe Pawulo nti: Kanguka wowe usinziriye, kandi uhaguruke mu bapfuye, nanjye, Kristo Yesu, nzaguha umucyo! Nuko rero mwirinde kugenda mutitonda: mutari nk'abapfu, ahubwo nk'abanyabwenge. Kandi bityo nshyira ahagaragara aba bayobozi babi ba Babuloni y'Ibanga ku bahamagawe banjye batoranyijwe kandi bizerwa kugira ngo mutagwa mu mutego w'uburiganya bwabo kandi kugira ngo muve muri yo rwose kandi mutandukane mu nzira zanyu zose kandi ntimusangire ku byago byayo.

Umva amagambo yanjye kandi wumve ibiri kuba ubu, kuko igitero cyabo gitunguranye kirimo kuba. Ubu mvugisha Babuloni y'Ibanga nubwo ari igipfamatwi, ikiragi, n'impumyi kandi iboshye rwose mu minyururu ku bw'urubanza rwayo. Kuko bateguye igitero cyabo gitunguranye mu myaka myinshi ubu, kandi bizeye irimbuka rya Babuloni y'Ibanga binyuze mu nyungu z'ikirenga, n'ubundi buryo bw'uburiganya, kuko ndetse n'inoti zayo z'amafaranga nta kindi ziri cyo uretse isezerano ririmo ubusa ry'imyenda yafashwe n'abayobozi banyu bakorera abavunjayi kandi babuloni y'ibanga igurishwa mu maboko y'abanzi babo binyuze muri ubu buryo bw'uburiganya. Mbese ntimwumva amabanki abika igice n'amafaranga y'impapuro? Mwige kugira ngo mumenye.

Sinabahaye urugero ubwo nirukanaga abavunjayi mu rusengero? Nyamara, aho kubajugunya hanze, mubatungira mu nzu ngo musangire n'imiryango yanyu, kandi mwatanze abana banyu n'abuzukuru banyu nk'ingwate kugira ngo muhaze irari ryanyu ry'umururumba bityo urubyaro rwanyu rwagizwe igicuruzwa kugira ngo rwizeze irimbuka ryanyu kugira ngo mubashe kubaho mu binezeza by'icyaha. Wowe, Babuloni y'Ibanga, wica abataravuka bawe mu byishimo byinshi n'ubwibone kandi abica abarengana bafite urubanza rwabo rwagenwe kandi urubanza rwawe ntirutinda. Nubwo abasigaye bake biruka bazamuka mu Nkuge mbere y'uko El Shaddai akinga urugi, benshi bazisanga basigaye mu rubanza kuko bazahitamo kuguma hanze bashinyagura kandi babaho mu binezeza.

Mu buryo butunguranye, mu isaha imwe kurimbuka kwayo kuzagwa gitumo kandi ntibazarokoka. Kubera ko yitanze mu gukorera abanzi bayo bahoraho ubu bazazana irimbuka ryayo rihoraho kuko Babuloni y'Ibanga itazigera yongera kuzamuka. Yewe Babuloni y'Ibanga, kubera ko wagurishije abana bawe nk'ingwate mu kwishingira imyenda yawe kugira ngo ukomeze mu binezeza by'icyaha (kuko ndetse n'amafaranga yawe ari inyungu z'ikirenga), kubera ko wishe abarengana, kandi wakoze byinshi kurushaho. Ubu nzagushyira rwose mu maboko yabo kandi Yehova ndetse yatangaje umugabane wikubye kabiri w'urubanza kuri wowe babuloni y'ibanga. Kandi benshi mu bana bawe bazoherezwa mu bigo byabo by'imirimo n'ingando z'igororamuco kandi benshi bazajyanwa mu bihugu by'amahanga kuko ikiyoka cyemerewe kwigarurira ibihugu n'abantu banyanze, Yeshua HaMashiach, bagashyigikira Baraba Mesiya wabo w'ibinyoma. Kandi benshi bazafata ikimenyetso cyabo cy'inyamaswa kandi bagurishije ubugingo bwabo n'urubyaro rwabo ku gihano gihoraho kiva imbere y'Imana ihoraho kandi y'iteka ryose ku bw'ibinezeza by'icyaha mu gihe gito.

Fata umuborogo ku bw'abantu ba Babuloni y'Ibanga, ndetse namwe mwicaye mu mwijima no mu bujiji bukabije bw'irimbuka ryanyu ryegereje. Imbere y'amaso yanyu bwite kandi ku bw'ubwumvikane bw'ijwi ryanyu ndetse no binyuze mu kudashishikara kwanyu k'ubunebwe, igihugu cyanyu cyose cyasenywe imbere y'amaso yanyu. Ntimugifite ubushobozi cyangwa umutungo wo kurwana intambara, kuko jyewe, ndetse na Kristo Yesu, nambuye imbaraga zanyu za gisirikare nkazishyira mu maboko y'abanzi banyu. Imijyi yanyu yahoze ikomeye ubu isigaye mu matongo y'ibyaha n'ubukene, ibyo abashyushyarugamba banyu namwe mwirata mu ishema rikomeye. Abedomu bamaze igihe kirekire bimuye inganda zabo mu bihugu byanyu, kandi mubuze ubushobozi bwo gukora mwahoze mufite ndetse n'ibikoresho by'ibanze byo gukora ibyo mukeneye kugira ngo murwane intambara zanyu zidashira.

Kuko muva mu ntambara mujya mu yindi, kandi mwarimbuye rwose ibihugu, iby'amahanga n'iby'imbere mu gihugu. Kuko muri wowe, Babuloni y'Ibanga, havuye icyago cy'Ingabo Zitukura, ndetse gikwirakwiza ku isi yose umusemburo utukura w'Ikiyoka kinini gitukura, kuko mwakoreye abaguye mu ibanga kuva mu ntangiriro kandi mugakomeza kugeza na n'ubu. Babuloni y'Ibanga, utanga ubwumvikane bwawe ku bushake ku bategetsi bawe ba gisitani, bazi neza imigambi yabo kandi bazi neza ko bari mu gikorwa cyo kurimbura rwose igihugu cyawe cyahoze gikomeye ndetse nawe. Kuko uhamwa n'icyaha cy'amaraso y'abera, kandi amaraso y'abishwe bahowe Imana ataka ari mu butaka asaba urubanza rwawe kandi iyo Yehova aza kuba atazagucira urubanza yari gukenera kwihana ku mahanga yaciriye urubanza mu bihe byashize, ndetse n'ayo akora nk'umuburo w'urubanza rwawe rwegereje wirengagiza nkana.

Muri wowe, Babuloni y'Ibanga, havuye irimbuka ryazanywe mu bihugu by'amahanga n'Ingabo Zitukura ubwo Abedomu biyegerezaga abakozi bakoresheje umusemburo wabo w'uburozi, ndetse n'inyandiko yabo y'ibitutsi yatewe inkunga n'Ingabo Itukura. Kuko abambuzi bakomeye bo mu bihe byashize, ndetse n'abayobozi banyu b'inganda bo mu bihe byashize, (ndetse n'abo mwagurishijeho urubyaro rwanyu) ni bo nyabayazana b'icyago cy'ingabo zitukura. Babuloni y'Ibanga n'urusobe rwayo rw'imyenda yateye inkunga kandi ishyigikira Ingabo Zitukura mu bikorwa byazo kuva mu ntangiriro, kandi Babuloni y'Ibanga yabaye igice cy'urusobe rwabo kuva mu ntangiriro ubwo Ingabo Itukura yashingaga imizi ku isi yose. Ndetse n'umwuka w'ikiyoka cy'umuriro wavuye ku Ngabo Itukura, kuko ari bo baguye bagendagenda hagati yanyu.

Muri Babuloni y'ibanga ni ho abayobozi babo b'"impinduramatwara" batojwe mu ibanga kuko yicaye ku mazi menshi, kandi boherejwe mu ibanga mu bihugu by'amahanga, ndetse no muri Gogi na Magogi kandi impinduramatwara zabo zari nini, kandi z'urugomo, kandi z'ubugome ndetse ubu zikwiriye isi yose. Bakwirakwije umusemburo wabo wa gisitani mu bihugu byo mu burasirazuba kandi barimbura rwose ibintu byose byari mu nzira yabo. Bavuye muri Babuloni y'Ibanga kandi babaye icyago ku isi, kandi icyo gihe cyose Babuloni y'ibanga yarabeshye kandi indaya ikomeye yiyise ko imvugira kandi yiyita Umukristu. Uku guhirika ku ngufu gahunda ya kera kwakwiriye isi yose kandi ubu kugeze mu cyiciro cyako cya nyuma, kandi bazemererwa sisitemu yabo, ariko gusa kugira ngo bakorere imigambi y'iteka ya Yehova, kuko mu by'ukuri abaguye barahinda umushyitsi kandi ubwoba bufata imitima yabo mibi kuko bazi ko urubanza rwabo rwa kera rubariho. Kuko wowe Babuloni y'Ibanga mu busambanyi bwawe n'uburaya bwawe, uryamana n'umwanzi.

Dore! Indaya ikomeye, ndetse Babuloni y'Ibanga igihugu kigizwe na leta nyinshi, ni wa mugore yeretswe Yohana Umuhishuzi, ndetse wa mugore wasinze amaraso y'Abera, n'amaraso y'Abahowe Imana banjye kandi urubanza rwawe ruri kuri wowe. Kuko muri ubwo buryo, wabayeho mu iraha mu gihe abayobozi bawe basenyaga ayandi mahanga ubyemeye kandi n'amafaranga yawe ufite icyaha kimwe n'urumamfu rw'Abedomu wagurishijeho urubyaro rwawe ku bw'ibinezeza by'icyaha. Wateye inkunga kandi ushyigikira ndetse uhuza mu ibanga ihirikwa ry'amahanga menshi! Kandi amahanga yose yahiritswe n'ingabo zitukura ashyigikiwe n'Ingabo Itukura yaratoranyijwe, aratotezwa, arakorerwa iyicarubozo, yicishwa inzara kandi yicwa mu buryo bubabaje cyane. Abera bari muri ibyo bihugu mwabishe babahora Imana ndetse za miliyoni zitabarika. Kandi icyo gihe cyose mwarabyinnye kandi mukoresha ubupfumu bwa Satani mu kwishima mutekereza ko bitazigera bibageraho, ndetse n'ibiyobyabwenge mwahawe by'urupfu rwanyu. Babuloni y'Ibanga, watambye igisekuru cyose cy'abana ubu babaye abagabo bagejeje igihe cyo kujya mu gisirikare bari gushobora kukurwanirira ariko aho kubikora ubu baba mu mihanda y'imijyi yawe iteye isesemi kandi babaswe n'ubu bupfumu nta byiringiro kandi babaye ibishushungwe by'abapfuye bagenda kuko ibi biyobyabwenge biborza imibiri yabo nubwo bakiri bazima.

Ubu urubanza rwawe ruje rwuzuye Babuloni y'Ibanga kandi ubaye igitaramo kugira ngo bose barebe, nyamara ukomeza mu bujiji n'ubuhumyi uvuga mu mitima yanyu ko amakuba atazigera akugeraho, nubwo wishimiye ubwo abayobozi bawe bazanaga amakuba ku nkombe z'amahanga atabarika mu ntambara zabo zidafite ubutabera izo mwishimiraga mureba kuri televiziyo. Kandi bityo mwagabanyijwe mu banzi banyu kandi bamaze kwishimira iminyago y'intambara ndetse na mbere y'uko ibisasu bigwa. Kuko gutsindwa kwa Babuloni y'Ibanga kwemejwe kandi gutangwa na Yehova ubwe kandi nta kizahagarika irimbuka ryayo ryuzuye kandi ntizigera yongera kubyuka.
