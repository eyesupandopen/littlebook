AmeruKa: Igihugu cy'Inzoka ifite Amababa / Mwitegure, Ndaje!

Nimwumve Amagambo ya Yeshua HaMashiach, ari we Kristo Yesu, kandi mwumve imvugo yanjye kugira ngo musobanukirwe inkomoko nyakuri, kamere, n'intego bya Babuloni y'Ibanga, kuko mu by'ukuri ari Igihugu cy'inzoka ifite amababa ndetse na AmeruKa, igihugu cyikurura mu bwihisho kikarya abagituye kandi kikica inzirakarengane kigatamba se na nyina mu bwinshi kuva mu bihe bya kera, ndetse n'ugeza mu bihe bya none. Ndetse n'igihugu cyavumwe cyanduza abagituye mu bwihisho no mu bwinshi bw'ibisigazwa byanduza kandi bihumanya by'ibitambo by'amaraso n'ubugome byahambwe hirya no hino muri iri shyanga ryanduye mu birundo byinshi by'ibishushanyo, imihango, n'ibitambo bitwikirwa i Tofeti, n'amaraso atemba ku buntu, ndetse ayo maraso yatose mu butaka bwacyo aho isi itakambira ubutabera ku maraso yamenwe, kandi n'ubu akomeza kumenwa ku bwinshi. Ndetse n'ibyo bihugu byanduye bitakambira ijuru cyane kandi mu ijwi riranguruye bisaba ubutabera muri uyu mwanya, ndetse mu gihe isi ihuzagurika cyane nk'umusinzi witegura kugwa, ndetse isaba urubanza rukiranuka mu by'ukuri rutazatinda.

Nawe Babuloni y'Ibanga, ndetse nawe: Uri umugabane utari uwera kandi wanduye, ndetse n'igihugu cyatuwe bwa nyuma kuva mu minsi ya Nowa, igihugu cyahariwe Heylel Ben Shachar n'inzira z'inzoka, ndetse n'igihugu cyatose amaraso y'inzirakarengane binyuze mu mihango n'imigenzo y'uru rubyaro rw'impiri n'abanduza bagiye kwanduza mu bihe bya kera. Ndetse n'igihugu cyabikiwe mu bwihisho n'amaboko yihishe y'abaguye, ndetse cyabitswe kandi giteganyirizwa kuba Atilantisi yabo Nshya, ndetse n'ishyanga ryakozwe kandi riharirwa kuzamuka kw'inyamaswa iva mu nyanja n'inyamaswa iva mu butaka. Ndetse n'ishyanga ryiyita irya Gikristo, ariko mu by'ukuri rikaba ishyanga ry'abasatanisiti b'ibanga bashinze kandi bibera neza mu nzego zaryo zanduye kandi zanduza kuva ku muto kugeza ku mukuru kandi ni aya mashyirahamwe y'ibanga yiyeguriye Heylel Ben Shachar (uwo bita Lusiferi mu kinyoma no mu bujiji), ndetse mu bujiji bakiyita abamurika kandi bajijutse, ndetse mu buyobe bwabo n'ibishuko byabo aho bo ubwabo biboshye iteka ryose ku ndahiro zabo zitari izera, bashuka, nyamara igihe cyose bashukwa n'abo biboshyeho iteka ryose nk'abana b'icyaha.

Bimenyekane ko Babuloni y'Ibanga ari Leta Zunze Ubumwe za Amerika, ndetse kera yitwaga Ameru-Ca ndetse igihugu cy'inzoka ifite amababa yikuruye kandi yihindura ya maraya ukomeye wicara ku mazi menshi kandi ni ishyanga ryakozwe, kugira ngo inyamaswa iva mu nyanja n'inyamaswa iva mu butaka zizabone kuzamuka zivuye mu ivu ryaryo mu bihe byo guhishurwa kwazo. Ndetse n'ishyanga ry'abasambanyi, ndetse ishyanga ry'ubusambanyi, ishyanga mu by'ukuri ari indaya ikomeye yiyita umukurikira ukiranuka wa Yesu w'i Nazareti. Ariko mu by'ukuri ni ishyanga ryubatswe n'abubatsi batagira Urufatizo rw'Urutare nyakuri, ndetse rwa buye rikuru ry'imfuruka abatari abizerwa basitaraho, ari we Yesu w'i Nazareti ari we kandi Yeshua HaMashiach. Mu by'ukuri, AmeruKa ni ishyanga ryashinzwe n'iryo boko ritari iryera kandi ryihishe, ndetse igihugu cy'amashyirahamwe y'ibanga yiyeguriye inzoka mu myambaro y'ibanga kandi uko imbaraga z'Umusaraba w'Iroza zateye AmeruKa, niko n'igice cya nyuma cya Atilantisi Nshya cyashinzwe, ndetse umugambi wari uhishe igihe kirekire wari guhuza amadini y'inzoka ku isi hose, ndetse ukarimbura kandi ugatamba bose bo mu rwego rw'abatambyi b'iyi mico yaguye, n'iyo mico ubwayo yari kugerageza mu bwibone no mu bupfu guhagarika iyo nzoka ubwayo biyeguriye mu ndahiro nyinshi z'amaraso kugira ngo bayikorere byuzuye, badasobanukiwe kamere y'icyo kiremwa nyakuri biboshyeho.

Uko niko uri AmeruKa, kuko mbwira umugabane kuva mu majyaruguru kugeza mu majyepfo no kuva iburasirazuba kugeza iburengerazuba ndetse ni igihugu cyahariwe iyo nzoka ifite amababa ya kera yahumanyije kandi yanduza ibi bihugu, ndetse n'uko ubwami bwagiye buzamuka kandi bukagwa mu mipaka yacyo, ndetse n'ubwami bwinshi butanditswe bw'inzoka, ndetse n'ishyanga ry'inzoka ifite amababa kandi iguruka ryahariwe imiryango iyoborwa n'abagore na demokarasi aho udutsiko tw'abagizi ba nabi twashoboraga gutegeka munsi y'ububasha bwa Heylel Ben Shachar, n'urubyaro rwe rw'impiri rwanduje ibihugu bya Babuloni y'ibanga iteka ryose ndetse ari cyo gihugu cyatuwe uko abantu bagendagendaga ku isi baturutse aho Inkuge yaguye ku Musozi wa Ararati icyo gihe cyose gishize. Kandi uko abantu bategetswe na Yahweh, NDI UWITEKA Ukomeye, baboshye munsi y'isezerano rya Nowa gukwira no gutatana no kongera gutuza isi mu gukiranuka kw'isezerano; urubyaro rwa Hamu rwo rwikoranyirije hamwe mu kutumvira gukomeye kugira ngo rwiheshe izina mu bihugu bya Shinari, rwibwira ruti: nimuze, twiyubakire umunara ukomeye kugira ngo twirinde, kandi kugira ngo mu by'ukuri turwanye ijuru kuko Nimurodi yigize umuhigi ukomeye imbere y'Uwiteka, ndetse umuhigi ukomeye w'abantu yahigaga nk'imikino.

Uko niko abantu baje mu gihugu cy'inzoka ifite amababa baturutse iburasirazuba bambutse ikiraro cy'ubutaka cya Bering nk'uko kizwi muri iki gihe, kandi baje baturutse mu nyanja iburengerazuba banyuze mu nzira zitandukanye z'ubucuruzi n'ingendo baje muri ibi bihugu byabikiwe na Heylel Ben Shachar ku bw'imigambi ye mibisha, igihugu mu by'ukuri abaguye bigaruriye byuzuye mu isi yariho icyo gihe, ndetse n'igihugu aho bahambye amabanga menshi bazemererwa gutaburura mu buryo butunguranye kandi butitezwe, ndetse n'uko umurinzi wanjye akuweho kandi n'uko ubutegetsi bwabo bugufi bwemerewe ku bw'agakiza ka Yakobo, kandi no kugira ngo nabo bagwe mu rubanza rw'umuriro uko iki kibazo cyemerewe gukomeza kugira ngo barimburwe rwose, kuko ibihe by'abaguye byageze koko kandi ni njye Kristo Yesu, ndetse Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda izamenagura umutwe w'iyi nzoka ya kera mu mperuka y'urubanza.

Uko niko Kanani yabonye ibyo abarinzi bahambye, kandi yayobowe n'irari ry'umutima we ubwo yateraga kandi akiba igihugu kitari icye ngo agituture mu kwica isezerano rya Nowa. Nyamara yararikiye iminsi ya kera yari yararimbuwe muri wa mwuzure mukomeye mu minsi ya kera aho isi yariho icyo gihe yarimbutse bitewe n'uko ba Satani bashutse abarinzi baguye ngo bivange n'abagore kandi babyare urubyaro rwabo ruvumwe, ndetse urwo rubyaro rukaba nta kindi ruri cyo uretse ibibyarwa by'indabakazi n'abantu bataye agaciro, ndetse n'ukwigana ubuzima aho ibizira byabo biboshye iteka ryose gukora irari ribi kandi ryangiritse rya ba se, kandi irari rya ba se bazarikora. Ku bw'ibyo, musobanukirwe ko uru rubingira rubi, ruri ibizira by'ibizira byuzuye, rutagira umuzi cyangwa ishami mu ijuru, ku isi, ndetse no munsi y'isi kandi ruzarandurwa rwose, iteka n'iteka ryose, munsi y'izuba hakurikijwe isezerano rya Adonai-YHVH NDI UWITEKA Ukomeye, kandi bazabohwa banatwikirwe muri iyo miriro mikomeye yo kwishyura aho urunyo rwabo rudapfa kandi abazajugunywa mu miriro bose bazahabwa ibyaha byabo inshuro nyinshi mu bituza byabo bwite mu rubanza rw'umuriro rw'iteka kandi rukiranuka batazigera bacika.

Uko niko Heylel Ben Shachar, wa gikomangoma gito cyatsinzwe, ndetse wa mwibone mukomeye n'uwanduza ibyera byose, yagiye kubiba urubingira rwe mu busitani bwa Yahweh, NDI UWITEKA Ukomeye, ndetse bwa busitani bwatewe ubwo Elohim yavugaga ati habeho urumuri nuko urumuri rubaho. Kuko mu ntangiriro, Elohim yaremye ijuru n'isi, kandi isi yari itagira ishusho kandi irimo ubusa nuko umwijima uza hejuru y'imuhengeri. Uko niko Heylel Ben Shachar na kimwe cya gatatu cy'abamarayika be bakurikiye ukwigomeke kwe bajugunywe ku isi mu isoni, no gutsindwa, kandi ni Heylel Ben Shachar, ndetse icyo gikomangoma gito cyatsinzwe cyabogoje mu isoni no gutsindwa ubwo cyajugunywaga kiva mu ijuru ry'amajuru mu burakari bukomeye, ndetse kibikiwe urubanza rwa nyuma n'urw'umuriro mu miriro y'uwo munsi mukomeye, ndetse iyo miriro ikaba yacanwe cyane n'ibirimi by'umuriro by'ubururu byera cyane, kandi yiteguye kwakira abifatanyije n'ukwigomeke kwa Satani bose kuva ku muto kugeza ku mukuru mu bwami bw'abaguye.

Nuko Elohim aravuga ati habeho urumuri nuko urumuri rubaho, kandi uko niko Elohim yashinze isi yongeye gushyirwaho ku itegeko rye gusa no ku Magambo ye gusa no ku nama ye gusa niho irema ryakozwe, riremwa, kandi rishyirwaho ndetse n'Amagambo ye akomeye kandi y'iteka n'imbaraga n'inama ye gusa kuko YHVH adakeneye umujyanama. Nta kiremwa kimeze nk'Imana Ihoraho yo Gukiranuka, nubwo babeshya kandi bakigira nk'abari bo mu buryo buteye isoni. Uko niko isi yaremwe n'ibiyirimo byose kandi iyo si yahawe Adamu, kuko umuntu yaremewe isi, kandi isi yaremewe umuntu, mu by'ukuri waremwe mu ishusho y'umuremyi we, ari we YHVH - NDI UWITEKA Ukomeye!

Nyamara iyo nzoka y'uburiganya yagabye igitero ku murage wa Adamu mu mayeri yayo, ndetse igitero n'ihutaza byaburijwemo byuzuye i Kaluvari, ubwo njye, Yesu w'i Nazareti, navugaga Amagambo y'iteka ngo birarangiye kandi uko niko niguze muntu, isi, n'ibiyirimo byose mu maraso yanjye y'impongano kandi isi ni iy'Uwiteka n'ibiyuzuye byose, kandi ndaje nk'Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda guca urubanza no kurwana, nuko rero nimwumve ukwivuga kwanjye, yemwe abatuye isi kuko ibihe byanyu byageze!

Uko niko abaguye bagiye kwanduza no guhindura irema kurikura mu butungane bwaryo bw'umwimerere ryaremwemo. Bo ubwabo ntibashobora kurema kuko YHVH atigeze abaremana iyo mbaraga cyangwa ubushobozi. Ku bw'ibyo, musobanukirwe ko aba baguye birarira ko ari imana mu gihe icyo bari cyo cyose ari abiyita ibyo batari byo.

Ku bw'ibyo, nimwumve Amagambo ya Yeshua HaMashiach, ndetse Kristo Yesu uko nje kubwira Itorero ryanjye, Umubiri wanjye, ndetse n'umugeni wanjye iby'ibihugu byangiritse kandi byanduye aho benshi muri mwe bavukiye kandi batuye, uko ba So bagendagendaga ku isi bahanwa na Adonai-YHVH, NDI UWITEKA Ukomeye, ndetse we wirukanye imiryango y'amajyaruguru mu mahanga afite urwandiko rwo gusenda mu ntoki, kandi aho, batatanyirijwe mu mahanga niko barinzwe kandi babungabungwa kuva icyo gihe ndetse n'umurezi wabo, kuko bakomeje amategeko ya Mose uko bagendaga mu bihugu byo mu majyaruguru ubwo batsindwaga mu kutumvira n'amaboko y'Abashuri, nyamara bagatatanywa n'Abanyababuloni, nabo batsinze kandi bahana abatumvira mu ba Data bo mu miryango ya Yuda na Benyamini, ndetse Ubwami bw'Amajyepfo bwa Yuda mu minsi ya kera bwabaye Yudaya ubwo Idumeya na Yuda byahuzwaga ku mbaraga z'inkota.

Uko niko intama zazimiye z'inzu ya Isirayeli zatatanyirijwe mu mahanga mu kutumvira kwazo. Ubwami bw'amajyaruguru bwatatanyirijwe mu mahanga y'amajyaruguru mu bihugu bya Gogi na Magogi, hirya no hino mu Burayi no muri Amerika niko bagiye. Uko niko na Bene wabo n'abo mu miryango yabo bo mu bwami bw'amajyepfo batatanyirijwe hirya no hino mu minsi ya Tito na Vespasiani n'ikindi gihe mu kwigomeka kwayobowe na Simon Bar Kokhba kandi benshi mu bwoko bwanjye barushijeho gutatanyirizwa mu bihugu byo mu majyepfo n'iburasirazuba bwa Yerusalemu mu majyaruguru ya Afurika no muri Alegizandiriya ya Egiputa n'ahandi hantu henshi bagendagenze mu gihano cyabo, nyamara barinzwe na Mose, nubwo benshi bari mu bubata bwa Esawu, na Ashikenazi, kandi benshi muri bo barashutswe kandi bategereje Mahdi wabo, aho gutegereza Yeshua HaMashiach ari we Mesiya nyakuri, n'umucunguzi wa Isirayeli wasezeranyijwe kera na Adonai-YHVH, Sela.

Nyamara, nk'uko byanditswe niko biri koko ko ubwoko bwanjye ahanini buri mu bujiji kandi buhumye igice ku byerekeye abo bari bo nyakuri n'ibiranga umwimerere wabo. Ndetse baciwe ku muzi wa Yahweh, NDI UWITEKA Ukomeye, uwo igiti cy'umwelayo cya kamere gisangira ku gicekuru cy'umuzi, uwo muzi ukaba ari Yehovah-YHVH, NDI UWITEKA Ukomeye.

Nyamara uko niko ubwoko bwanjye bwagizwe abanzi hagati yabo mu bujiji mu binyoma n'urwango n'ubujiji bw'abo bari bo nyakuri, ndetse n'uko bigishijwe kwemera ibinyoma by'uru rubyaro rw'abatambyi b'inzoka mu gihugu icyo ari cyo cyose bashinzemo imizi, ndetse urwo rubyaro rw'abatambyi b'inzoka bakorera Heylel Ben Shachar, ndetse wa muserafi waka umuriro kandi waguye ubikiwe imiriro y'igihano cy'iteka n'urubanza rw'iyo miriro ibikiwe urubanza rukaze cyane kuri iyi nzoka mbi yiyanduje mu kwigomeka kudafite ishingiro, ndetse igumishijwe mu mimerere yo gutsindwa no kugwa itazigera yikuramo.

Uko niko benewacu n'abavandimwe banjye bigishijwe kwangana binyuze mu binyoma byinshi bipfunyitse kugira ngo byigishe rubanda, nyamara niko byanditswe: Ibi bintu bitandatu Uwiteka arabyanga: yego, umutima we wanga urunuka birindwi: amaso y'ubwibone, ururimi rubeshya, n'amaboko avusha amaraso y'inzirakarengane, umutima utekereza imigambi mibisha, amagirenge yihutira kwirukira ibibi, umutangabuhamya w'ibinyoma uvuga ibinyoma, n'uteza amakimbirane hagati y'abavandimwe. Uko niko abana b'ubwami bw'Ijuru bigishijwe mu bwihisho, uburiganya, n'urwango rwinshi ko bo ubwabo ari bo bagize abavandimwe babo abacakara mu nyanja ya Atalantika, ndetse igikorwa cyakozwe na Esawu na Ashikenazi mu gukorera Heylel Ben Shachar, aho bitirira abana b'Ubwami bw'Ijuru ibyaha by'urubingira rwibye abana mu bihugu no kubakura kuri ba nyina na ba se bagurishijwe mu rwango rwinshi n'abaturanyi babo n'abo mu miryango yabo mu maboko y'abantu babi mu bihe bya kera. Ku bw'ibyo musobanukirwe ko ibi bikorwa bibi byanditswe neza mu ijuru kandi ubutabera no kwishyura bizagwa mu buryo butunguranye ku batuye isi bagishaka kurimbuka kwa Yakobo, nubwo ari ukurimbuka kwabo bwite kuzabagwirira mu buryo butunguranye kandi butitezwe none, Sela.

Nyamara, benshi muri mwe muguma mu bujiji kuko ibara ry'uruhu rwanyu ritandukanye n'iry'abavandimwe banyu ku bw'ubugeni bwa Yahweh n'igishushanyo cye cyiza, nyamara nta kindi kiremwa ku isi cyiyanga gityo bitewe n'ibinyoma by'inzoka mu byaremwe bya Elohim byose, nyamara ubwoko bwanjye bwemera kubohwa n'ubwo bupfu n'ibinyoma bidafite ishingiro by'ubucucu butagira ishingiro byatanzwe n'abaguye ubwabo kugira ngo barimbure ubwoko bwanjye. Mwihane ubwo bupfu none, kuko nta gihe mufite!

Nyamara, benshi muri mwe muguma mu bujiji ko muri Kristo Yesu nta Muyuda cyangwa Umunyamahanga, cyangwa imbata, cyangwa uwigenga ahubwo ari icyaremwe gishya muri Kristo, ndetse icyaremwe gishya aho ibisekuruza bya kera bya Adamu biseswa iteka ryose binyuze muri uwo mwuka w'Ubwami bw'Umwana, kuko amasezerano yahawe Aburahamu wizerwa azaragwa n'abakiranuka babeshwaho n'uko kwizera kumwe kwa Aburahamu wizerwa, noneho gutanzwe byuzuye mu maso ha Yesu Kristo mu kuvuka gushya, nyamara benshi muri mwe muhitamo ubujiji mu nzira zanyu zidafite ishingiro zitari izanjye, kandi mugomba kwihana, kuko nabategetse gukunda Imana n'umutima wanyu wose n'uwuzuye, ubugingo, ubwenge, n'imbaraga; no gukunda mugenzi wanyu nk'uko mwikunda. Itegeko benshi muri mwe mwanga mu rwango rwanyu rw'amoko rw'ubwoko bumwe bwa Adamu bafite ibara ritandukanye mu bwiza bw'igishushanyo cya Yahweh. Mwihane!

Uko niko mu minsi ya kera, urubyaro rwa Kanani rwarushijeho kwiyanduza binyuze mu kwivanga n'abaguye, ndetse no mu minsi ya kera, kandi uko biyanduzaga mu cyaha biyeguriye byuzuye abaguye aho ngaho mu bihugu bya Shinari ndetse no mu gicucu cya wa munara mukomeye w'i Babeli, ndetse uwo munara YHVH yahanuye mu gutsindwa. Kuko niko byanditswe: Nyuma y'uko isi irengerwa n'umwuzure mu minsi ya Nowa, isi yagabanyijwe hakoreshejwe ubufindo bw'umurage hagati y'abana ba Nowa mu minsi ya Pelegi, nuko Yafeti ajya mu murage we mu bihugu byo mu majyaruguru. Kandi abantu bari bafite ururimi rumwe, nuko abana ba Hamu bikoranyiriza mu gihugu cyabaye Babuloni. Kandi ibikomangoma bya Nimurodi byajyanye inama na Futi, Mitsirayimu, Kushi, na Kanani n'imiryango yabo kugira ngo bubake umujyi wabo kandi muri wo umunara ukomeye ndetse umunara muremure kugira ngo bashobore kurwanya ijuru kandi kugira ngo bikoranyirize hamwe kugira ngo biheshe izina mu kwigomeka ku itegeko rya Yahweh ryo kororoka no kugwira kugira ngo bongere gutuza isi. Ariko uko niko inzira z'umuntu wa kamere utagira ukwizera ziri, we mu rari rwe n'ubwibone akorera icyaha bityo agakorera Se w'icyaha; ndetse na satani, binyuze mu kutumvira kwa muntu kuri Yahweh bitewe n'icyaha kiri mu mubiri w'umuntu wa kamere.

Kandi kuva ku rubyaro rwa Nimurodi wakuriye mu gukiranirwa havugwa ko mu babi havamo gukiranirwa kwinshi. Kandi uko ni ko Nimurodi yanduje isi mu nzira z'abaguye kandi mu bapfumu be n'abaraguzi be ni ho havuye amashuri y'ibanga ya kera aho abapfumu b'abaguye bigishaga amabanga yabo y'ubusa ku bazajugunywa mu muriro iteka ryose mu byaha bya ba Satani, kandi bazifatanya na bo mu murage wabo w'iteka. Kandi Nimurodi yazamutse mu bwami bwa mbere nyuma y'umwuzure wa Nowa, ndetse aho Nimurodi yategetse isi mu gukiranirwa; ndetse n'ubutegetsi abaguye bashaka kwiharira ku isi yose, nyamara nta gihe cya zahabu bazagira ahubwo bazabona urubanza rukomeye.

Nuko Uwiteka mu rubanza rwe atuma isi yiyasa imira kimwe cya gatatu cy'umunara wabo, kandi umuriro umanuka mu ijuru utwika ikindi kimwe cya gatatu cy'umunara, naho kimwe cya gatatu cyemererwa gusigara nk'urwibutso rwo kubarwanya. Ni cyo cyatumye witwa Babeli, kuko ari ho Uwiteka yashobereye indimi z'isi yose: kandi aho ni ho Uwiteka yabatanirije ku isi yose. Kandi amahanga menshi n'amoko menshi yanze kandi yibagirwa Uwiteka wagiranye isezerano na Nowa, nuko bibagirwa isezerano Nowa yagiranye n'Uwiteka hakurikijwe umurage wabo kandi hakurikijwe indimi bavugaga bibagirwa isezerano ry'Uwiteka na Nowa, nuko bahindukirira inzira z'abaguye kuko nta yandi mahitamo bari bafite. (Nyamara Aburamu yabaye Aburahamu kandi umukiranutsi azabeshwaho no kwizera!)

I Babuloni Nimurodi yakoze ibibi byinshi ubwo yaremagamo ibigirwamana byinshi akiyita imana kandi akarema ubutatu bwe butuka Imana, ndetse n'ubutatu budakwiriye, bugamije gukoba ubumana nyabwo n'urubyaro rw'umugore rwasezeranyijwe no kuyobya abana b'abantu mu gukiranirwa.
