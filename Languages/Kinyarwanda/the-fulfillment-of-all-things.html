Ubuhamya Bubiri: Isirayeli
Ibisharira n'Ibiryoshye
11 Kanama 2024

Isohozwa ry'Ibintu Byose!

Umva Amagambo y'Umwami n'Umukiza wawe uhoraho, ndetse na Mesiya wawe w'ukuri, ari we Yeshua HaMashiach. Umuve kandi wumve Amagambo yanjye bana banjye kuko ubu ngiye kuvuga ku byerekeye isohozwa ry'ibintu byose, kuko isohozwa ryabyo ryegereje. Bityo rero, mwumve, kuko abantu ubu banze amasezerano y'amahoro ya Yahweh kandi bakanga Yesu w'i Nazareti (kuko ari njye masezerano yanyu y'amahoro) ibihe ku isi ubu bizahinduka bive ku buntu bijye ku rubanza, bizahinduka bive ku mbabazi bijye ku makuba, bizahinduka bive ku mahoro bijye ku ntambara, bizahinduka bive ku bugwaneza bijye ku burakari, bizahinduka bibe kwihorera kweruye no kurimbuka gukomeye ku batuye isi. Ndetse n'abaranze bose kungira Umwami no kwemera urukundo rwanjye n'imbabazi zanjye kuri bo, ibyo byari gutuma mbasubiza ku muremyi wabo nkabaha ubugingo buhoraho, ntibazongere gupfa ukundi.

Ariko aho kugira ngo umuntu wa kamere aze aho ndi byuzuye, benshi banze mu bwibone bwigomeka bahitamo kugumana na Satani n'ubwigomeke bwabo kandi mu buryo bwikora bifatanyije na bo haba mu bujiji, cyangwa bazi neza amahitamo yabo. Kandi bityo abahisemo mu bujiji nk'ubwo ubu bazagerwaho n'umujinya w'Imana n'umujinya w'umwana w'intama ugiye gusukwa mu buryo bwuzuye vuba aha. Kandi nubwo benshi muri iki gihe banze, imbaga y'abantu benshi bazaza aho ndi kandi bavuge ko Yesu ari Umwami kandi bazambaza izina rya Mesiya wabo kugeza ku iherezo. Kandi bityo ku bwa Yeshua HaMashiach bazakizwa. Nyamara abandi benshi bazarimbuka, ndetse n'abantu bose amazina yabo atigeze yandikwa mu Gitabo cy'Ubugingo cyishwe uhereye ku kuremwa kw'isi kandi uko niko kuzaba amahitamo yabo.

Kandi bityo mu buryo bumwe nk'uko izo mpinduka zirimo kuba ku isi mu rubanza, amahoro yashinze imizi mu ijuru kandi intambara ubu izarimbura isi rwose. Habaye intambara mu ijuru kandi umurezi wa bene Data ajugunywa hasi, ajugunywa hasi, kandi icyo kiyoka kinini gitukura ubu cyaboshywe ku isi kandi gitegereje urubanza rwacyo kandi urubanza rwacyo ntirutinda. Mu gutsindwa gukomeye azakora imigambi ye mu migambi ihoraho ya Yahweh, kuko n'abanzi be bumvira ijwi rye, kandi bityo ahabwa gusa mu buryo bufite imipaka cyane kandi ubwami bwe buzarimburwa rwose. Dore! Icyo kiyoka cya kera, ndetse n'icyo kiyoka kinini gitukura, ubu cyajugunywe ku isi, kandi gifite umujinya mwinshi kuko kizi ko gifite igihe gito! Kandi bityo njyewe, Yeshua HaMashiach ngira inama buri wese kwirukira ku ijwi ryanjye no kwambaza izina ryanjye rikomeye kugeza ku iherezo kuko abadakunda amagara yabo kugeza ku iherezo bazakangukira mu bwiza buhoraho, ibyishimo, n'ingororano kandi ingororano yabo ni nini! Kuko abambaza izina ryanjye bose kugeza ku iherezo bazakizwa, mu rurimi urwo ari rwo rwose bambaza kandi njyewe Yesu Kristo, ari na we Yeshua HaMashiach, ntangaje aya magambo!

Kandi bityo amasezerano n'indahiro bya kera bizasohora, ndetse n'amasezerano yose y'ibihe byose kuko uko niko ubutumwa bwiza buhoraho bumeze. Kandi uko intambara iganza ku isi, amahoro ubu ashyizweho mu ijuru aho ijuru ubu riteguriwe kwakira abizerwa muri Kristo, ndetse n'abapfuye ari abizerwa muri Kristo, n'abazima basigaye kandi bakabaho ubuzima bwabo muri Kristo. Kuko abagendera mu mategeko y'umwuka w'ubugingo muri Kristo Yesu mu gihe cyanjye cy'ubuntu bazakoranywa kandi bahabwe ingororano yabo ihoraho kandi bazasobanukirwa kandi bazashyirwa mu byiciro byabo kugira ngo basohoze umugambi uhoraho wa Yahweh baremewe bundi bushya muri Kristo. Kandi bityo ubukwe bw'Umukwe n'Umugeni ubu buzaba, kandi hazaba ibirori bikomeye mu ijuru mu bwiza n'ikuzo bihoraho!

Kuko kuva mu ntangiriro, Imana yaremye Adamu, kandi ikura kuri Adamu irema Eva kugira ngo azabe umufasha we uhoraho kandi abe nyina w'abazima bose kandi kugira ngo bororoke bagwire. Bahawe itegeko ko mu biti byose byo mu busitani bwa Edeni, bashobora kurya ku giti cyose usibye igiti cyo kumenya icyiza n'ikibi. Kandi bityo binyuze mu gishuko cy'uburiganya cy'inzoka Eva yarashutswe, maze afata ku rubuto rubujijwe ararya, kandi aha umugabo we Adamu, na we abizi neza ararya bityo icyaha cyangiza muntu kandi mu gupfa bapfuye kuva icyo gihe. Kandi bityo Adamu na Eva ntibigeze basohoza umuhamagaro wabo wo hejuru wo gutegeka no kuganza isi mu gukiranuka no kororoka no kugwira no gukwira isi mu gukiranuka ahubwo bakoze icyaha kandi babaho mu bihembo by'icyaha kugeza ubwo ibyo bihembo byageze ku ndunduro mu rupfu rwabo kandi mu gupfa muntu yapfuye kuva icyo gihe. Kandi mu muzuko w'abakiranutsi bazahabwa ingororano yabo n'ibihembo kuko babaye abizerwa kugeza ku iherezo.

Kandi bityo niko bimeze ko Yahweh, mu kumenya kwe kwa mbere kuhoraho, yamenye mbere y'uko biba ko muntu azihitiramo icyaha n'urupfu kuva mu ntangiriro. Kandi El Shaddai, ndetse no mu rukundo rwe rukomeye akundisha muntu, (n'ibyo yaremye byose), yatanze ubuntu n'imbabazi bitagereranywa ubwo yatangazaga ko azashyira urwango hagati y'urubyaro rwa Satani n'urubyaro rw'umugore. Urubyaro rwa Satani rwakomerekeje agatsinsino kanjye i Kaluvari mu gusohoza Amagambo ya Yahweh kandi ubu ngiye kugenda nanjye nanjagure rwose umutwe w'inzoka kuko uko niko ahazaza he hameze n'abamukurikira bose, kandi nk'uko byanditswe, niko bizakomeza gukorwa kugeza byuzuye.

Nyamara, Imana yahisemo mu bwenge bwayo butagira iherezo guhishura imigambi yayo y'ubuntu, imbabazi, n'amahoro ku bwo gucungurwa kwuzuye kwa muntu mu byiciro, kandi nyamara buri gihe yatanze ibyiringiro bikomeye ku bakiranutsi ko umunsi umwe vuba aha bazazurwa mu bugingo bushya bavuye mu bapfuye ntibazongere gupfa ukundi, kandi bazabaho mu burumbuke n'amahoro iminsi yose y'ubuzima bwabo kandi iyo minsi ntizagira iherezo. Uko ibihe byagiye bihita abizerwa bapfuye bafite ukwizera kwuzuye muri Yahweh ko azasohoza amasezerano ye kandi bapfuye biringira amasezerano ya Yahweh nk'ibyiringiro byabo by'agakiza kandi amasezerano yose ya Yahweh azasohorezwa rwose mu maso ha Kristo Yesu, ndetse nanjye, Umwami n'Umukiza wanyu wazutse. Kandi bityo bizeye Imana kandi byababarirwaho gukiranuka ndetse no ku bizeye bose kandi bakagira ibyiringiro mu rubyaro rw'umugore rwasezeranyijwe.

Kandi bityo umugambi wa Yahweh w'ibihe byose wo gucungura muntu byuzuye wahoze ushingiye kuri njye, Kristo Yesu ndetse Yesu w'i Nazareti ari na we Yeshua HaMashiach kuko ndi Umwami w'Abami wazamutse ari we mwana w'intama w'Imana kandi wazaniye agakiza gahoraho Umuyahudi n'Umunyamahanga kimwe, kandi nzanakiza Yakobo mbakure mu bateye igihugu cy'isezerano kandi kurimbuka kwabo ntigutinda. Ndi we uwo intare izaryamana n'umwana w'intama kuko muri njye intare yo mu muryango wa Yuda n'umwana w'intama w'Imana bizagera ku ntego yuzuye. Kuko ndi intare yo mu muryango wa Yuda kandi ndi Umwana w'intama w'Imana kuko ndi Umwami w'Abami n'Umutware w'Abatware kandi ndi Umutambyi Mukuru iteka ryose mu buryo bwa Melikisedeki.

Kandi bityo Adamu na Eva babayeho mu budahemuka bakomeza imbere y'Uwiteka Imana yabo, kandi barororotse baragwira, nyamara Satani yari gutegura ikindi gitero binyuze mu bwigomeke bw'abarinzi baguye. Bifatanyije n'ubwigomeke bwa Satani kandi kugira ngo bahaze irari ryabo basize ubwiza buhoraho bw'icyiciro cyabo cya mbere, icyo baremewemo, kugira ngo biyandurishe abagore, ndetse n'inyama n'amaraso, kuko bari kuguma mu bwiza buhoraho ariko aho kubikora bahitamo igihano cyabo gihoraho kugira ngo basohoze irari ryabo ku kiguzi n'urupfu rw'ibyaremwe byariho icyo gihe.

Kandi bityo aba bamarayika baguye bigishije umuntu waguye uburyo bwo kwirimbura binyuze mu gisekuru cya Kayini n'ubumenyi bubujijwe n'amayeri bigishije muntu. Biyise imana kandi basaba gusengwa kandi BIHITIYEMO abagore ku ngufu mu bo bahitamo bose, kandi abagore benshi bitanze ku bushake bayobowe na Satani kugira ngo bashuke kandi bareshye abarinzi mu byaha byabo by'irari ibyo biyibohereyemo iteka mu muvumo wabo kuko bagiranye imivumo hagati yabo. Ibi byabaye mu minsi ya Yaredi n'ibizira byabo, ndetse n'Abanefilimi babo bakwiriye isi yose mu bwami bwabo bw'isi yose mu gihe cyabo bise igihe cya zahabu.

Kandi abarinzi n'urubyaro rwabo bangije isi n'ibyari biyirimo byose kandi ihindagurika ry'imiterere y'ingirabuzimafatizo ridasanzwe ry'amoko yose ryarakozwe kandi ryuzura mu isi kuko bakoze icyaha ku bafite inyama bose kandi irari ryabo ntiryagiraga imipaka, kandi biyise imana, ndetse imana zirema ariko mu by'ukuri nta kindi bari cyo usibye abiyita bo bakoze imvange ikabije hagati y'amoko. Kandi bityo isi yarangiritse rwose kandi yategekwaga n'Abanefilimi, bariye abantu n'ibyo abantu batunze. Kandi abasigaye bose batanduye mu gihe cya Nowa (kuko Nowa yari umukiranutsi kandi atunganye kandi yagendanaga na Yahweh kandi akabona ubutoni mu maso ya Yahweh) bari abantu umunani barokotse ari bazima hamwe n'inyamaswa Yahweh yazanye mu Nkuge kugira ngo amoko yazo arokoke. Kandi bityo Imana yateje umwuzure ku isi kandi iyangirika ryose ryo mu isi yariho icyo gihe rirarimbuka, kandi abarinzi nta mahoro bafite, cyangwa imbabazi kandi iteka ryose n'iteka ryose urubanza rwabo rukomeye ruzabaho kuko babikwiriye. Kandi atari abarinzi gusa, ahubwo n'abifatanya bose n'ubwigomeke bwa Satani bazababara muri ubwo buryo kandi bahembwe iteka muri rya ziba rinini ry'umuriro w'amazuku y'ubururu yaka ibirimi bishyushye cyane kandi byera by'igihano.

Nyuma y'umwuzure, Imana yashyizeho isi iriho ubu, ndetse ishyirwaho bwa mbere mu gukiranuka. Kandi Yahweh yagiranye amasezerano na Nowa, aho Yahweh yatumiye Nowa kuzamura gukiranuka ku isi no kororoka no kugwira kugira ngo urubyaro rw'umugore rwasezeranyijwe ruze. Yahweh yasezeranyije kutazongera guteza umwuzure ku isi ukundi kandi nubwo kamere ya muntu ari mbi, Imana yakoranye na muntu kugira ngo irinde kandiihe umugisha imiryango ikiranuka ku isi ari na yo njyewe Yeshua HaMashiach nakomotsemo.

Imana yabahaye umugisha wo kororoka no kugwira no kuzuzanya isi, kandi Imana yasezeranyije umuntu n'inyamaswa ko itazongera kurimbura abafite inyama bose ku isi ikoresheje amazi kandi El Shaddai ntabwo azongera guteza umwuzure ku isi yose ahubwo azarinda isi, kandi arinde muntu n'urubyaro rwe, kandi iyi si imwe irinzwe kuva kuri uwo munsi w'amasezerano aho Data w'Imicyo yashyize umuheto we mu ijuru nk'ikimenyetso n'urwibutso rw'amasezerano ye na Nowa. Kuko ibi babiyobewe ku bushake, ko ku bwa jambo ry'Imana ijuru ryabayeho kera, n'isi ihagaze ivuye mu mazi kandi iri mu mazi: Ibyo byatumye isi yariho icyo gihe, irengerwa n'amazi, irarimbuka: Ariko ijuru n'isi, biriho ubu, ku bwa jambo rimwe bibikiwe, biteganyirijwe umuriro kugeza ku munsi w'urubanza no kurimbuka kw'abantu batubaha Imana.

Kandi bityo Yahweh yazamuye Aburamu kugira ngo abe Aburahamu, kandi yasezeranyije Aburamu ko izina rye ritazongera kuba Aburamu ukundi ahubwo Aburahamu kandi ko azamugira Se w'amahanga menshi kandi ko azaha Aburahamu n'urubyaro rwe igihugu cya Kanani ho gakondo ihoraho kandi ko Yahweh azaba Imana yabo! Kandi bityo Aburahamu yabaye Se w'amahanga menshi, ibyo Yahweh yasezeranyije mu masezerano. Noneho, Aburahamu n'urubyaro rwe ni bo bahawe amasezerano. Ntavuga ati, No ku mbuto, nk'aho ari nyinshi; ahubwo nk'umwe, No ku rubyaro rwawe, ari rwo Kristo. Kandi bityo umukiranutsi azabeshwaho no kwizera!

Kandi bityo abana baguye mu bucakara mu Misiri nyuma y'uko abana b'isezerano bagiyeyo munsi y'uburinzi n'ibimutunga bya Yozefu kuko Yozefu yategekaga mu gukiranuka munsi ya Farawo wari mukuru kuri Yozefu ku ntebe y'ubwami, ariko Yozefu yakoreraga NDIHO ukomeye! Hadutse farawo utari uzi Yozefu, kandi uru rubingira rwakandamije kandi rugerageza kwica umurongo wa Kristo nanone, ndetse n'abana b'isezerano. Kandi Yahweh yazamuye umucunguzi w'Umuheburayo amukuye mu nzu ya Farawo, ndetse n'uwo wakuwe mu mazi kandi akarokorwa ari muzima ku bw'ubuntu bwa Yahweh. Yari Mose ubwe wari kuyobora abana b'isezerano abakura mu bucakara abajyana mu mudendezo w'ubwiza bw'igihugu cy'isezerano, kuko ari abana b'isezerano kandi nzabazura mu bitotsi byabo kandi ngororere abizerwa n'abakiranutsi byinshi, ndetse n'abagumye ari abizerwa kugeza ku iherezo. Kandi bityo Mose yakijije abana ba Isirayeli amaboko y'ababakandamizaga kandi ababakurikiye bose barohama mu nyanja, kuko ni nde ushobora guhagarara imbere ya El Shaddai?

Kandi bityo Yahweh yayoboye Mose n'abana b'isezerano ku Musozi wa Sinayi aho Yahweh yahaye Mose amabwiriza, amategeko n'amabwiriza bikubiye mu mategeko kugira ngo abana bose bumvire. Kandi imigisha n'imivumo byashyiriweho kumvira no kutumvira. Kandi ubwo Yahweh yari amaze guha Mose mu masezerano amategeko akubiye mu mabwiriza yo kumvira, Mose yemeje amasezerano ayagira Isezerano binyuze mu kumena amaraso. Kandi Mose yafashe igice cy'amaraso, ayashyira mu mabesani, n'ikindi gice cy'amaraso akimisha ku Gicaniro. Kandi yafashe igitabo cy'amasezerano, agisomera mu matwi y'abantu: baravuga bati, Ibyo Uwiteka yavuze byose, tuzabikora, kandi tuzumvira. Kandi Mose yafashe amaraso ayaminjira ku bantu, aravuga ati, Dore amaraso y'Isezerano Uwiteka agize namwe, ku byerekeye aya magambo yose. Byongeye kandi, yaminjiye amaraso ku Ihema ry'ibonaniro, no ku bikoresho byose by'Umurimo. Kandi hafi ibintu byose byezwa n'amaraso ku bw'Amategeko: kandi hatabayeho kumena amaraso nta kubabarirwa ibyaha kubaho.

Kandi bityo amateka yikubura nk'uko byanditswe, kandi mu gihe runaka ishyanga rya Isirayeli ryashinzwe rwose, nyamara nta buturo bwa Yahweh bwari buri mu bantu. Kandi Yahweh yazamuye Umwami ukiranuka, ndetse umuntu uhuje n'umutima w'Imana! Mu gihe runaka Yahweh yohereje Natani Umuhanuzi kuri Dawidi amubwira ko n'iminsi yawe isohora, ugasinzirana na ba so, nzazamura urubyaro rwawe nyuma yawe, ruzakomoka mu mara yawe, kandi nzashimangira ubwami bwe. Azubakira izina ryanjye inzu, kandi nzashimangira intebe y'ubwami bwe iteka ryose. Nzaba se, na we azaba umuhungu wanjye. Kandi inzu yawe n'ubwami bwawe bizashimangirwa iteka imbere yawe: intebe yawe y'ubwami izashimangirwa iteka. Dukurikije aya magambo yose, no gukurikiza iri yerekwa ryose, niko Natani yavuganye na Dawidi.

Uko niko muntu yakurikiye Yahweh ava mu isezerano ajya mu rindi yongera isezerano ku rindi ryemejwe n'ibitambo by'amatungo, kandi isezerano ryongewe ku rindi. Nyamara, nubwo muntu yagerageje uko ashoboye, ku bwe ntiyigeze ashobora kugera ku gukiranuka kw'Imana, ndetse n'amategeko akiranuka kandi atunganye ya Mose ayo nkunda kandi nasohoje. Nihagira uwita amategeko ya Mose n'amasezerano menshi ya Yahweh ibivume, avumwe. Kuko amategeko ari meza, akiranuka kandi ari matagatifu, kandi amasezerano yose ya Yahweh ni yego na amena. Nyamara, amategeko yatanzwe nk'umurezi kugira ngo abayobore kuri Kristo. Kuko njyewe, Yesu w'i Nazareti, ndi we Yahweh yatanze ngo asohoze ibyo muntu adashobora kubera icyaha cyagiye gihererekanywa uko ibihe biha ibindi kivuye mu maraso ya Adamu, yononekaye iteka ryose. Kuko icyo amategeko atashoboraga gukora kuko yari afite intege nke bitewe n'umubiri: Imana yaragikoze, yohereza umwana wayo asa n'umubiri w'icyaha.

Nuko mu gihe cyategetswe gisohoye, njyewe Yesu w'i Nazareti, navetswe n'umugore nk'uko amategeko ya Mose abisaba no mu gusohoza amasezerano, kandi ndi urubyaro rw'umugore rwasezeranyijwe rwavuzwe na Yahweh ubwe mu bihe bya kera. Kuko Imana yakunze abari mu isi cyane, byatumye itanga Umwana wayo w'ikinege, kugira ngo umwizera wese atarimbuka, ahubwo ahabwe ubugingo buhoraho. Nuko rero ndi umwana w'ikinege w'Imana, ndetse rwa rubyaro rw'isezerano abahanuzi bahanuye kuva kera kugeza ubu. Kandi ndi we washyiriweho gusohoza byose, kandi muri njye, Kristo Yesu, ibintu byose n'amasezerano yose bizasohora kandi nta sezerano na rimwe rizasigara ridasohoye, ahubwo yose azasohora iteka ryose.

Kuko byanditswe mu Baheburayo aho nategetse Pawulo kwandika ngo: Kuko niba amaraso y'amapfizi n'ay'ihene, n'ivu ry'ishashi biminjiriwe ku bahumanye, byeza umubiri: Amaraso ya Kristo, wituye Imana atagira inenge binyuze mu Mwuka uhoraho, azarushaho gukura imirimo ipfuye mu mitima yanyu, kugira ngo mukorere Imana ihoraho? Kandi ni cyo gituma ari Umuhuza w'Isezerano Rishya, kugira ngo binyuze mu rupfu, rwo gucungura ibicumuro byakozwe mu Isezerano rya mbere, abahamagawe bahabwe isezerano ry'umurage uhoraho.

Nuko rero mwumve ko muri njye, Kristo Yesu, amasezerano ya kera arinzwe kandi amasezerano ya kera arakomejwe n'isezerano rya kera rirarinzwe kandi rigasohora kuko ndi isohozwa ryayo ritunganye. Kandi nyamara haracyari amasezerano agomba gusohozwa kandi ubu ni ibihe byo gusohozwa. Kuko ibihe bimeze bityo, aho nzazura gitunguranye abasinziriye muri Kristo, kandi nzakoranya gitunguranye abazima basigaye ngo bansanganire mu bicu kandi gitunguranye tuzahurira mu cyumba cy'intebe y'ubwami cya Yahweh ubwe. Kuko mwebwe Itorero ryanjye ndetse n'umugeni wanjye namwe muri isohozwa ry'isezerano kandi mwabyawe ubwa kabiri n'imbuto zitabora kandi mwacunguwe n'amasezerano menshi mu Isezerano ryanjye Rishya, ari ryo sezerano rishya rikubiyemo buri sezerano ry'amasezerano ya kera ndetse rikongeraho amasezerano akomeye kandi y'agaciro kenshi abacungurira ubugingo buhoraho n'ingororano ihoraho kandi tuzabana iteka ryose.

Mwumve ko ubuzima mwabayeho mu mubiri, ndetse namwe abizerwa banjye n'abatoranyijwe, ubwo buzima burangira mu gucungurwa kwanyu kwuzuye. Mwumve ko mwifatanyije nanjye byuzuye, Kristo Yesu, mu nzego zose. Igihe nabambirwaga ibyaha byanyu, namwe mwabambanywe nanjye. Igihe nagwaga kuri icyo giti kibi, namwe mwapfanye nanjye. Igihe nazukiraga ubugingo nyuma y'iminsi itatu n'amajoro atatu mu mutima w'isi, namwe mwazukiye ubuzima bushya, kandi igihe nazamukaga mu ijuru ry'amajuru namwe mwazamukanye nanjye nuko mwicara mu by'ijuru. Kuko ndi Pasika yanyu itunganye, kandi ndi umusimbura wanyu wafashe umwanya wanyu mu rubanza n'igihano mwari mukwiriye. Kandi njyewe, Yesu w'i Nazareti, nabacunguye ku bushake mbakura mu muvumo w'amategeko kuko kubera umubiri wanyu w'icyaha ntimushobora na rimwe gusohoza amategeko ku mbaraga zanyu.

Mwumve ko naje ntaje gukuraho amategeko, cyangwa amasezerano, ahubwo naje gusohoza byose mu bihe byo gusohozwa. Nihagira uwita amategeko ya Mose n'amasezerano menshi ya Yahweh ibivume, avumwe. Kuko amategeko ari meza kandi akiranuka kandi ari matagatifu, kandi amasezerano yose ya Yahweh ni yego na amena. Nyamara, amategeko yatanzwe nk'umurezi kugira ngo abayobore kuri Kristo. Kuko njyewe, Yesu w'i Nazareti, ndi we Yahweh yatanze ngo asohoze ibyo muntu adashobora kubera icyaha cyagiye gihererekanywa uko ibihe biha ibindi kivuye mu maraso ya Adamu, yononekaye iteka ryose. Kuko icyo amategeko atashoboraga gukora kuko yari afite intege nke bitewe n'umubiri: Imana yaragikoze, yohereza umwana wayo asa n'umubiri w'icyaha.

Buri sezerano rigiye gusohora byuzuye muri njye Kristo Yesu kandi nzinjira mu isezerano ry'ishyingiranwa n'Umugeni wanjye kugira ngo bazategekanane nanjye mu murage wanjye kandi bazakiza isi bayobowe nanjye kandi bari kumwe nanjye, bagende bategeke nk'abatambyi n'abami mu isi kandi buri sezerano rizasohora kuva kuri Adamu kugeza kuri Mose mu Maso ha Yesu Kristo. Aya ni amasezerano y'ubugingo buhoraho azasohorezwa mu bakiranutsi, ndetse n'abaremwe bundi bushya nk'umuntu umwe mushya muri Kristo Yesu. Kuko bose bazaremwa bundi bushya byuzuye kandi bagororerwe iteka ryose ku mugabane wabo kandi byose bizaba yego na amena. Kuko buri nyuguti ya yodi, na buri kantu kayo bizasohora byuzuye kandi iteka ryose.

Mwumve ko muri urusengero rw'Imana ihoraho, Torero ryanjye ryizerwa kandi nabaremye nk'inzu itubatswe n'intoki kugira ngo mube ubuturo buhoraho bw'Imana ihoraho, ndetse bwa Yahweh So. Kandi nzashyingiranwa n'umugeni wanjye kandi nzabana nabo iteka mu isezerano rihoraho, kugira ngo aho ndi, nabo babe ari ho bari, kandi iteka ryose bazaba nkanjye, Kristo Yesu Umwami wabo uhoraho. Kandi mwumve mwa bizerwa banjye ko ibihe byo gutandukana bigeze ku musozo kandi nta gutandukana kuzongera kubaho hagati ya Yahweh n'abana be akunda kandi nta gutandukana kuzongera kubaho hagati ya Kristo Yesu Umwami wanyu uhoraho n'abo nkunda cyane. Mwumve ko tuzagendera hamwe nk'umuntu umwe kandi nta cyaha cyangwa inkomyi bizabaho. Muzakora imirimo myinshi ikomeye itarigeze inatekerezwa mu mitima y'abantu. Muzaba abami banjye n'abatambyi bakomeye abo mbereye Umwami w'abami n'Umutware w'abatware kandi tuzategeka isi mu gukiranuka.

Kandi abandi benshi bazazurirwa ubugingo ku iherezo ry'icyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli kandi bose bazagororerwa hakurikijwe uko ubwizerwa bwabo bwabaye. Kandi bose bapfuye ari abizerwa bategereje ko Umwami wabo na Mesiya atazasiga ubugingo bwabo mu mva bazakangukira ubuzima bushya no gusubizwa mu buryo buhoraho. Kuko nzabahuza n'ababo bakunda kandi nzabasubiza ibyo inzige zariye kandi nzabasubiza ibyo Satani yabibye mu bihe bari abashyitsi mu isi bategereje gucungurwa kwabo kwuzuye ngo bave muri iyi si yaguye kandi mbi.

Kandi uko niko ubutumwa bwiza buhoraho bumeze kandi iteka ryose ibintu byose bizasohora mu gukiranuka, kwera, n'ukuri. Kuko nta kintu kizinjira mu marembo y'ubwami bwanjye cyanduye cyangwa kibi cyangwa gikunda kandi gihimba ibinyoma ahubwo ingoma yanjye izaba mu kuri no gukiranuka kuhoraho. Nuko rero mwumve ijwi rya Yeshua HaMashiach ndetse namwe mwese abo aya magambo yohererejwe. Umwami wanyu uhoraho ntiyibagiwe amasezerano ya Kera kandi nta masezerano yigeze aba ubusa. Ahubwo yose azasohora kandi koko, menshi yarasohoye nuko rero murebe ibyo bintu mubone ibyiringiro. Kuko nk'uko byanditswe niko bizakorwa kandi uko niko ubutumwa bwiza buhoraho bumeze muri ibi bihe byo gusohozwa.

Njyewe Yeshua HaMashiach nohereje aya Magambo binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye kandi binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye aya magambo yoherejwe.

Ndi urubyaro rw'umugore rwasezeranyijwe.

Ndi amasezerano yanyu y'amahoro.

Ndi Umutware w'abatware wazamutse mu ijuru ari we mwana w'intama w'Imana.

Ndi we uzatuma intare iryamana n'umwana w'intama.

Ndi intare yo mu muryango wa Yuda.

Ndi Umwana w'Intama w'Imana.

Ndi Umwami w'abami n'Umutware w'abatware.

Ndi Umutambyi Mukuru iteka ryose mu buryo bwa Melisedeki.

Nuko rero ndabaha kumva ko isohozwa ry'ibintu byose riri hano kandi ryamaze gutangira. Abo mu mugeni wanjye n'umubiri wanjye bazakorera kandi babeho mu buzima bushya iruhande rw'abazazurwa mu gukiranuka mu muzuko w'abakiranutsi nyuma y'icyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli. Kandi mu gukiranuka n'amahoro tuzabana mu bwumvikane n'ibyaremwe by'Imana kandi hazabaho amahoro arambye kandi ahoraho ku bana b'isezerano, kandi bazakorerana, kandi bazankorera, kandi bazakorera Yahweh kandi mu mahoro n'ubwumvikane ibyaremwe bya Yahweh bizasubizwa uko byahoze.

Kuko haracyariho ikiruhuko cy'abantu b'Imana. Nuko rero uko igihe cyanjye cy'ubuntu kigana ku musozo igihe cya nyuma kiri hafi bana banjye, ndetse na wa munsi wa nyuma ubwo twese tuzaruhuka imirimo yacu n'amakuba yacu, kandi tuzishimira ibyiza by'igihugu uko turuhuka ku munsi w'ikiruhuko ndetse hazabaho umunsi w'Isabato w'ubwumvikane. Habayeho iminsi itandatu y'imirimo, kuko umunsi umwe ku Mwami Imana umeze nk'imyaka igihumbi n'imyaka igihumbi nk'umunsi umwe. Bityo, mu ngoma yanjye y'imyaka igihumbi abizerwa bazamara umunsi w'ikiruhuko hamwe n'Umuremyi wabo, Data, n'Imana Ihoraho mbere y'uko Yahweh arema ijuru rishya n'isi nshya aho gukiranuka kuhoraho kuzatura iteka ryose. Dore, ibihe ni ubu, nuko rero mube maso!
