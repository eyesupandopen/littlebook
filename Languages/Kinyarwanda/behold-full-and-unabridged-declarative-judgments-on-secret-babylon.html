Dore! Imanza z'Itangazo Zuzuye kandi Zidhinnye kuri Babuloni y'Ibanga: Zatangajwe Zuzuye Mbere y'uko Urubanza Rugwa Rwuzuye

Urubanza Rwuzuye kuri Babuloni y'Ibanga: Rwatangajwe ku Cyumweru, 22 Kamena 2025. Ibyavuyemo byose byakuwe mu Gitabo Gito cy'Ibyahishuwe Igice cya 10 cyahishuwe kuva ku ya 2 Werurwe 2024 kugeza ku ya 20 Werurwe 2025. Amagambo yose nahawe na Yeshua HaMashiach na YHVH, NDI WE UKOMEYE binyuze mu Mwuka w'Ubuhanuzi ari ryo hamya rya Yesu Kristo. Selah!

Imanza zikurikira ziva mu Gitabo Gito cy'Ibyahishuwe Igice cya 10, ari na ryo Vanjiri y'Iteka y'Ibyahishuwe Igice cya 14. Urubanza kuri Babuloni y'Ibanga rwatangajwe none mu yubile n'imyaka myinshi mu Gitabo cy'Ibyahishuwe. Icyakora, Inkuba Indwi n'Igitabo Gito byahishuwe kandi biratangazwa none iki gitabo gikomeye kirafunguye mu kuboko kwange no mu maboko y'abasoma iri tangazo bose bava mu Mukumbi Muto, ndetse bahamagawe na Yeshua HaMashiach ku bw'iyi ntego n'iki gihe! Selah!

Zikurikira ni Imanza z'itangazo zakuwe mu Gitabo Gito zizasohozwa rwose mu buryo butunguranye kandi butitezwe none. Muri uru rwego, nkora nk'intumwa y'ubutabera yoherejwe gutangaza imanza ziva ku ntebe y'ubwami ya YHVH, zitangiwe mu maso ha Yesu Kristo, uwo nkorera. Nyabuneka menya ko, zimwe muri izi manza zirimo kuba kandi zimwe zizaba vuba, ariko zose zizasohozwa nk'uko zatangajwe kandi urubanza kuri Babuloni y'Ibanga ruzaba umuburo ku mahanga yose kandi umwotsi w'ibibabaza byayo uzazamuka nk'itangazo rikiranuka ry'imanza zikiranuka za Kristo Yesu, ndetse na YHVH NDI WE UKOMEYE!

Bimenyekane ko, uyu munsi ndetse na 22 Kamena 2025 ku Kalendari ya Gregory (ndetse na kalendari imwe ikoreshwa na Babuloni y'Ibanga) ko Umwami wange Wazamuwe Kristo Yesu, ndetse Yeshua HaMashiach antegeka gusoma izi manza kuri Babuloni y'Ibanga, ndetse na Leta Zunze Ubumwe za Amerika, kugira ngo umuntu wese ashobore kwihana mu gihe hakiri akanya gato k'igihe gishira, kandi kugira ngo urubanza rutangazwe mbere y'uko umuriro ugwa. Selah!

Iri tangazo rirambuye kandi izi si imanza nshya ahubwo zasohotse zuzuye kuva ku ya 2 Werurwe 2024 kugeza ku ya 20 Werurwe 2025. Bityo, ndohereza umuntu wese ku Gitabo Gito cy'Ibyahishuwe igice cya 10 ku mbuga zatanzwe. Nyabuneka menya: Izi manza zikomoka ku Nkuba ya 5 by'umwihariko kandi ubu busomyi buzatangirana n'Abakiranirwa Ntibazaragwa Ubwami bw'Imana, Inkuba za 5 n'iya 6, zatanzwe na Yeshua HaMashiach ndetse na YHVH NDI WE UKOMEYE!

Nyabuneka menya ko, ubu busomyi bukora nk'urubanza rwuzuye rw'itangazo rurwanya Babuloni y'Ibanga rwatanzwe n'Umukumbi Muto mbere y'uko Urubanza rugwa rwuzuye. Ndi intumwa n'umugaragu w'Umwami muri uru rwego kandi ndi umukozi w'ubutabera woherejwe kandi wahawe ububasha na YHVH, mu maso ha Yeshua HaMashiach kandi ni bo nkorera byuzuye mu mirimo yange.

Nyabuneka menya ko, ubu busomyi bukora kandi nk'ubuyobozi bw'ibyabaye bizaba vuba, kandi ibi bikozwe hagamijwe ko abantu b'Umwami, ndetse n'abizerwa muri Kristo, n'Umugeni wa Kristo bazazamurwa mu buryo butunguranye none. Bitanzwe kugira ngo tumenye kandi dushobore gutangaza iherezo uhereye mu ntangiriro, ndetse na mbere y'uko biba. Ibi byabaye bizaba neza nk'uko byatanzwe na Kristo Yesu ndetse n'ukuboko kwa YHVH, NDI WE UKOMEYE, uwo ibintu byose bikomokaho bitanzwe mu izina rikomeye n'ubutware bwa Kristo Yesu, ari we kandi Yeshua HaMashiach.

Ibi byatwaye igihe n'imbaraga kugira ngo bikusanywe, kandi bikora nk'ubusomyi bw'urubanza rucucitse kuri Babuloni y'Ibanga, ndetse n'urutonde rw'ibyabaye kugira ngo Umugeni nyakuri wa Kristo, Abizerwa muri Kristo, n'abantu 144,000 batayobywa n'inyiganano zihendutse. Ni Igitabo Gito kizayobora ibihe by'imperuka binyuze mu Nkuba Indwi zafunguwe none, atari abahanuzi b'ibinyoma biyohereza ubwabo bavuga amagambo adafite ishingiro bava mu mitima yabo. Selah!

Nyabuneka menya: Ibi bigenewe kuba ubuyobozi bwo kwiga ku bizerwa kugira ngo muzamenye ibizaza kandi kugira ngo twese dushobore kuruhukira muri Kristo kandi twishingikirize ku Magambo YE atari amagambo y'abahanuzi b'ibinyoma batamenye Umwami wacu, kandi batamuvugira na gato.

Ubu ngiye gutangaza.

1Abakiranirwa Ntibazaragwa Ubwami bw'Imana!19 Mata 2024
Kuva kuri Yeshua HaMashiach:

Mwana wange, niba uzakira amagambo yange, ugahisha amategeko yange muri wowe; kugira ngo utege ugutwi ubwenge, kandi uhindurire umutima wawe ku gusobanukirwa. Ntimunangire imitima yanyu, nk'uko byagenze mu gushotorana, ku munsi w'ibigeragezo mu butayu: kandi mumenye ko jyewe, Umwami Yesu Kristo, mporana namwe kandi mpora mpari mu buzima bwanyu uko munkomeza n'agakiza kange, kuko ndi umutwe w'Umubiri, kandi ndi Umwami wanyu wizerwa, Mugeni wange nkunda. Kuko nabateguriye ahantu n'ejo hazaza, kandi hari ingororano ikomeye ku bahamagawe bange, n'abizerwa, n'abatoranyijwe.

Sinabahamagariye igihombo n'igihano cyangwa ngo mbahamagarire isoni no kwiciraho urubanza; ntimwasomye ko nta rubanza ruri ku bari muri Kristo Yesu, batagenda bakurikiza kamere, ahubwo bakurikiza umwuka? Bityo mwizere amagambo yange! Sinabahamagariye kubaho mu gicucu cy'ibyaha byanyu bya kera, cyangwa kubaho mu gicucu cy'ibikorwa byanyu by'ubusore, ahubwo nabahamagariye gupfa ku nzira zanyu za kera z'ibyaha no kwakira ubuzima bushya muri jye uko mwiyambura kamere yanyu, uko mwiyambura ibyaha byanyu mukagenda mu buzima bushya muri jye. Dore! Mpindura byose bishya! Ese ibi si byo byiringiro byanyu?

Bana bange, nabakuye mu kwangirika kw'iyi si ya none igenda ipfa, kandi nabakijije imbaraga z'umwijima mbimurira mu bwami bwange, ubwami bw'Imana, buyobowe nanjye, Umwami w'Abami n'Umutware w'Abatware, uwo Yahweh ari Imana yabo, kuko nta yindi. Nabahamagariye ubwiza n'ingeso nziza muri jye, kandi nabakijije ubwami bwa Satani, ubwami bw'umwijima, mbazana mu Bwami bwange, aho hari urumuri! Bityo, kanguka wowe usinziriye, kandi uzuke mu bapfuye, nanjye, Yesu Kristo, nzaguha urumuri.

Ntimwasomye mu nzandiko noherereje Umugeni wange, noherereje mwe, Itorero ryange bwite? Ntimuzi ko abakiranirwa batazaragwa ubwami bw'Imana? Ntimwishuke: abasambanyi, cyangwa abasenga ibishushanyo, cyangwa abahehesi, cyangwa ibitingwa, cyangwa abagabo baryamana n'abandi bagabo, Cyangwa abajura, cyangwa abifuza, cyangwa abasinzi, cyangwa abatukana, cyangwa abanyazi, ntibazaragwa ubwami bw'Imana. Kandi bamwe muri mwe mwari mumeze mutyo: ariko mwaruhagiwe, ariko mwarejejwe, ariko mwatsindishirijwe mu Izina ry'Umwami Yesu, no mu Mwuka w'Imana yacu.

Ntimwumva amabwiriza yange, yewe, torero ry'akazuyaze rya Lawodikiya? Sinabahamagariye gusubira muri kamere yanyu, cyangwa ngo mbahamagarire gukomeza mu nzira zanyu zikiranirwa. Mutekereze, kandi musuzume; uwibwira ko ahagaze, yirinde atagwa! Mwiyirinde ubwanyu, n'inyigisho: mukomeze muri byo: kuko nubikora, uzikiza wowe ubwawe, n'abakumva. Mwumve, bana bange b'akazuyaze, ko mudashobora kubaho ubuzima bwo kuringaniza hagati y'icyiza n'ikibi, mwibwira ko mutunganye imbere yange, iyo muhisemo ikibi gito mu bibi bibiri mu mihango yanyu y'amatora ntemerera cyangwa ngo nshyigikire.

Mumuvemo, bantu bange, kuko NTAGO natanze igisubizo cya politiki, kandi rwose sinabahaye umuyobozi w'isi ngo agire icyo ari cyo cyose gikomeye nanone. Mwarashutswe, kandi murimo gutegurwa gukurikira no kwemerera umwana wo kurimbuka ugenda hagati yanyu ku mugaragaro, nubwo atarahishurwa neza. Ariko mwebwe musaba ubushishozi muzamenya uwo ari we, kugira ngo mushobore kwirinda ibishuko bye. Oya, kuringaniza si ibyanyu, kuko muri abana bange n'abavandimwe, kandi sinatsindiye agakiza kanyu ntanga ubuzima bwange nk'incungu ya benshi binyuze mu kuringaniza na Satani; kuko kuringaniza ari ukwifatanya n'ingabo zabo. Bityo ntimwibeshye: Cyangwa muhagararira jye mu gukiranuka n'ubutungane nyakuri, cyangwa mwifatanyije n'ingabo za Satani mu bwami bwe bupfa, bityo MWIHANE!

Mwunguka iki mu kuringaniza n'ikibi? Ntimwumva ko mugomba kuneshesha ikibi icyiza? Bityo muhagarare mushikamye muri jye kandi mureke guhindagurika nk'ifi ivuye mu mazi. Benshi muri mwe muracyatwarwa n'imiyaga yose y'inyigisho n'uburiganya bw'abantu, bakoresha amayeri yo gushuka. Jye, Kristo Yesu, navuze, kandi sinzacecekeshwa. Benshi muri mwe mwariringanije ndetse n'itorero ryange, kandi ndababaza: muzakomeza gushidikanya hagati y'ibitekerezo bibiri kugeza ryari? Niba Uwiteka ari Imana, mumukurikire: ariko niba ari Bayali, mumukurikire. Mu magambo make, ntimushobora kuringaniza ngo muhitemo ubwoko ubwo ari bwo bwose bw'ikibi mwibwira ko muhagarariye jye; iyo muhisemo ikibi, muhitamo inkomoko y'icyo kibi nk'umwami wanyu n'umutware wanyu mukaba abagaragu b'icyaha. Ntimuzi yuko uwo mwiha kuba abagaragu bo kumwumvira, muri abagaragu b'uwo mwumvira: cyangwa ab'icyaha kizana urupfu, cyangwa ab'ukumvira kuzana gukiranuka?

Munyumve kandi munyumve neza, Torero ryange; mumenye ko mpagaze nzinutswe imigenzo yiganje mu gihugu cyanyu no ku isi hose, mwaretse ikinjira mu Torero ryange ngo ibabaze abana bange. Nk'uko byari mu minsi ya Nowa, ni ko bizaba. Iminsi ya Nowa yari igihe isi yategekwaga n'ibizira bibi byakozwe n'abamarayika baguye, imbuto yabo bwite, imbuto y'ikibi yaremewe kurimbuka kwanyu kwuzuye: kandi bazagaruka, kuko dore, nzanye ibihangange ngo bisohoze umujinya wange. Babigezeho bakuraho kandi bahutaza imipaka kamere Data yashyizeho kugira ngo amahoro no gukiranuka bishobore kuganza, ariko mu gukuraho no kwambuka mu buryo bw'ubwiyandarike imipaka igaragara muri kamere ubwayo, Data yashyizeho, benshi muri mwe mwaranduye binyuze mu kwemerera ikibi cyiganje mu bihe byanyu. Icyabayeho, ni cyo kizabaho: n'icyakozwe, ni cyo kizakorwa; kandi nta kintu gishya kiri munsi y'izuba. Ntimwasomye, yewe, mwa bafite ukwizera guke? Gukuraho imipaka y'Imana ubwayo bitera ubusazi, n'indwara, n'urujijo rw'ibintu byose. Benshi muri mwe mwishimira indwara zo mu mutwe, mwibwira ko umugabo ashobora kuba umugore n'umugore akaba umugabo, kandi muyobora abana banyu bwite mu buyobe bwanyu. Benshi muri mwe mujya mu matorero ayobowe n'abakozi b'ibinyoma b'abatinganyi bweruye kandi birarira mu ndwara yabo, kandi mwibwira ko bamvugira? Mumenye ibi: niba muri muri iyo mimerere y'icyaha, nzabarekurira irari mwifuza cyane, kandi muzasarura imbuto z'inzira zanyu zanduye.

Dore! Ku bw'ibyo, Imana yabaheye irari riteye isoni: kuko n'abagore babo bahinduye imikoreshereze ya kamere bayigira inyuranyije na kamere: Kandi n'abagabo na bo uko, bareka imikoreshereze ya kamere y'umugore, bashyuhirana mu rari rwabo hagati yabo, abagabo bakorana n'abandi bagabo ibiteye isoni, kandi bihabwa ingororano ikwiriye y'ubuyobe bwabo. Ariko oya, ntibihagije kuyoborwa n'ibitingwa n'ibinyoma byabyo; mwemerera abana banyu bwite kwangizwa ndetse no gukatwa imbere yange. Hari ingaruka zikomeye zibikiwe ibi bikorwa, kandi ntibizabura guhanwa. Kandi nyamara mukomeza mu busazi bwanyu, kandi keretse mwihannye, nzabaruka mbakure mu kanwa kange.

Nimuze kuri jye mwihana, kandi mwicare mu magunira n'ivu, kugira ngo menye ukuri kwo kwihana kwanyu kandi murire kandi maboroge ku bw'ibyaha byanyu kugira ngo mutishyura ikiguzi mu rubanza. Benshi muri mwe muri abibone cyane kuko mugerageza kungira Umwami mu gihe mugifite umutima utizera, kuko umutima ari wo umuntu yizeza akabarwaho gukiranuka. Bityo mugire ukwizera kugira ngo mukizwe; ukwizera kwanyu kuzerekanwa kuri bose binyuze mu kwihakana icyaha no kuva mu nzira zanyu zanduye. Ababi bagenda impande zose, iyo abantu babi cyane bashyizwe hejuru. Ariko muracyemerera inzira z'abatinganyi, kandi mufasha kuzana ikibi gikomeye mu gihugu, kugeza n'aho mutorera abahohotera abana kuyobora ibihugu byanyu. Mubura ndetse n'urwego rw'ibanze rw'ubushishozi, kandi ntimukurikiza inzira yange; kandi nimukomeza, nzabarekurira rwose irari ryanyu riteye isoni kugira ngo umubiri urimbuke kugira ngo umwuka ukizwe.

Reka mbahumure amaso, mwebwe b'injiji ku bushake kandi b'impumyi, mwebwe mwicara mu mwanda n'umwijima, mwivuruguta mu byondo. Ntimushobora kwemerera ikibi ngo mwibwire ko munkorera, kuko narwanyije ikibi n'ibishuko kugeza igitonyanga cya nyuma cy'amaraso kimenetse kiva kuri jye kijya ku butaka butukura, kuko ndi Adamu wa nyuma kandi nahinduwe umwuka utanga ubuzima. Benshi muri mwe mwahumwe amaso n'amagambo meza n'imvugo nziza kandi mwemerera byuzuye abagizi ba nabi n'abantu babi bahiga abana. Bahohotera abana, bica urubozo abana ku bw'irari ryabo ribi: Kuko ari ko umujinya w'Imana uza kuri Babuloni y'Ibanga, ariko benshi muri mwe mukomeza kwemerera inyamaswa z'inkazi zashyiriweho imiriro y'iteka y'ikuzimu itazigera izima.

Mumuvemo, bantu bange, cyangwa muzasangira ibyago bye n'urubanza ruteye ubwoba rumuzaho; kandi nababuriye. Kuko Kristo ahuza ate na Beliya? Nimusangira ibinyoma bye, muzasangira ibyago bye; bityo mwitandukanye rwose mu magambo no mu bikorwa, kandi ntimwemeranye na bo na gato. Ntimubeho ubuzima burangaye, buringaniza; kuko nzahana ababikora bose. Mwibwira ko kuko mufite ubuzima bw'iteka mudashobora guhura n'igihombo gikomeye n'ingaruka? Nzambura ingororano zanyu nzihe abandi, kandi muzaba abato cyane mu bwami bwo mu ijuru nimukomeza, kuko ari ko Yeshua HaMashiach, Umukwe, abwira Umugeni wange!


Kuva kuri Yahweh, Imana Ikomeye y'Iteka:

Yewe, mwa bateshwa agaciro mwe mwongiza irema ryange, mwa bateshwa agaciro mwe mwiyigurishije kuri Satani, mwa bateshwa agaciro mwe mwagize Satani Imana yanyu, mwa bateshwa agaciro mwe mwanga umwana wange Yesu Kristo n'agakiza natanze mu izina rye, mwa bateshwa agaciro mwe mwemereye abana banyu bwite gushya mu miriro ya Moloki. Nzabaha isi mwifuza, kandi nabateguriye isi ntarimo, isi idafite umwana wange Kristo Yesu, isi ifite inyanja nini y'umuriro n'amazuku yagenewe mwe gusa.

Nzabaha umwijima mwifuza cyane, kuko umucyo wanjye utazaba uhari, kandi muzamara iteka ryose mutandukanye nanjye, Yehova, Imana ikomeye y'icyubahiro, iyo ubwiza bwayo n'umucyo wayo nta muntu n'umwe wabasha kureba kandi ihoraho iteka ryose. Mwa byigomeke mwe, mwa babi mwe mwarimbuye ibyo naremye uhereye mu ntangiriro mu irari ryanyu n'urwango n'ishyari, mumenye ko mwiyikoreje igihano gikomeye n'umubabaro mutazigera mukira hamwe n'abayobejwe bose n'ibinyoma byanyu bakanga Yesu Kristo, bakanga kureka ibinyoma byanyu ngo bagarukire ukuri kwanjye.

Mutekereza ko kugoreka inzira zanjye no gukuraho imbibi zanjye ari ikintu cyoroheje, ariko muzasobanukirwa umujinya wanjye ikirimi cy'umuriro cya mbere nikibakozaho, kandi muzarohama mu muriro n'amazuku mutazigera mucika. Byongeye kandi, mwishe, mwasambanyije ku gahato, mwasahuye, mwahohoteye, mwakomerekeje, mwarimbuye, kandi mwifatanyije mu byaha bikomeye cyane, ndetse mwifuza n'imibiri idasanzwe; kandi mwifatanyije n'abamarayika baguye, nanjye nzabahuza n'inyanja y'umuriro nateguriye Satani n'abamarayika be, kandi ntimuzacika. Nta mahoro mufite.

Nzabacira urubanza rutabera, kuko ndi Imana y'ubutabera, kandi ndi Imana yihorera. Nyamara, muzahagarara imbere yanjye mu bwibone, mwiburanira, mutekereza ko nzasubira inyuma imbere y'inzira zanyu za gisitani zivuga ngo: kora ibyo ushaka, niyo mategeko yose. Nta mahoro mufite. Nzishyura, kandi muvuga ko ndi Imana y'urukundo (kandi ndi yo), ariko mukitega ko ntazahana abahumanije ibyo naremye, abakoreye iyicarubozo bakica umwana wanjye w'ikinege ubwo yitangaga ngo abe incungu ya bose; yego, mwiteze ko ntazabahana ku bw'inzira zanyu zitagira agaciro ubwo mwishe, mugakorera iyicarubozo, kandi mugakomeretsa abere kandi mugicisha inzara abana banjye bato ku bw'inyungu zanyu bwite.

Koko, Satani ubwe yazarimbura ibyanjye byose ku bw'inyungu ze bwite z'umururumba, kandi n'ubwo bimeze bityo mugerageza kunshinja ubupfu mu manza zanjye zitabera iyo nje kurengera abanjye. Iminsi yanyu yo kuyobya abana banjye bato igiye kurangira. Dore! Inyamaswa yanyu n'umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma bazarimburwa rwose, kandi nzabajugunya ari bazima muri ya nyanja nini yaka umuriro n'amazuku; bazabategereza ngo mwifatanye nabo mu murage wanyu.

Urubanza rukomeye ruri kuri wowe, yewe gikomangoma cy'umwijima; ubwami bwawe n'abagukurikira bose bigiye kurimburwa rwose. Kuko mutari imana, kandi ntimushobora kwikura mu kuboko kwanjye mwa batagira agaciro mwe, kandi nzababohera mu mwijima w'icuraburindi mwifuza cyane, ndetse no mu kuzimu kwimbitse nzababohesha urunigi rukomeye mudashobora guca. Yego, mu kuzimu muzabayo umunsi umwe, kuko umunsi umwe ku Uwiteka umeze nk'imyaka igihumbi, n'imyaka igihumbi imeze nk'umunsi umwe. Kandi n'ubwo bimeze bityo nzababohura igihe gito kugira ngo mugerageze kandi mukoranye aba nyuma b'abo banyabizira babakurikira n'inzira zanyu. Kandi nzabatwikisha umuriro mwese, n'intebe z'imanza zizashyirwaho, kandi nzabacira urubanza mwese inkozi z'ibibi, kandi muzajya mu gihano cy'iteka mu maso h'Uwiteka. Muzashya imbere y'abamarayika n'imbere y'Umwana w'Intama, kandi bazabareba mubabara kandi muhanwa, kuko mwabahemukiye bose, kandi muzishyura byuzuye ibyaha byanyu iteka ryose.

Hanyuma muzashyirwaho ikimenyetso muri gereza yanyu, kandi muzibagirana, kandi ntimuzigera mwibukwa ukundi, kuko ibya mbere bizashira kandi byibagirane. Kuko dore, nzarema ijuru rishya n'isi nshya ibamo gukiranuka; kandi jyewe, Yehova, Imana ikomeye ihoraho y'imana zose, kuko nta wuhwanye nanjye, dore, nzarema byose bibe bishya, kuko jyewe, Yehova mvuze ibyo! Yehova, Imana ikomeye ihoraho, azashyira intebe ye y'ubwami ku isi, kandi ubuturo bwanjye buzaba mu bantu, kandi ntimuzigera mubaho ukundi, yewe mwa batagira agaciro mwe, kandi muzashya iteka muri gereza yanyu yibagiranye y'umubabaro mudashobora gucika, kuko nzashyiraho ikimenyetso kandi nta n'umwe ushobora kugica.

Nyamara, benshi muri mwe bishyira hamwe na Satani banshinja ubupfu bwo kwihanira no kwihorera mu maboko yanjye kugira ngo nshyire mu muriro abigize abakwiriye igihano cy'iteka. Nimwumve amagambo yanjye, yewe batuye isi: Umwana wanjye araje, kandi aje afite kwihorera gukomeye, umujinya, n'uburakari ku bw'ibibi byakozwe n'amaboko yanyu.
