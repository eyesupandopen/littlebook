Ubuhamya Bubiri: Isirayeli
Inyongera
14 Nzeri 2025

Mwihishe!

Nimwumve Amagambo ya Yeshua HaMashiach ahishwe mu ntumwa ye n'umugaragu we, nyamara akaba ahishuwe ku bw'itegeko n'ubutware bwanjye uyu munsi tariki ya 14 Nzeri 2025: Ndetse n'igihe intoki ze zikanda imfunguzo ku bw'itegeko ryanjye ndetse no ku bw'Umwuka wuzuye w'Ubuhanuzi utemba mu mbaraga zikomeye uko mpishura Amagambo yanjye azagera ku maso, amatwi, n'imitima y'abo yohererejwe bose, Sela!

Ukuboko kw'abanzi bawe kuri ku ijosi ryawe Amerika! Ihane! Umva Amagambo yanjye Babuloni y'Ibanga!

Umunsi w'Uwiteka ugeze ku isi, ariko mbere isaha imwe hamwe n'inyamaswa izagwira isi ku munsi mutiteze, kuko mwibwira muti nicaye nk'umwamikazi, kandi sinzaba umupfakazi, umukiza wanjye ukomeye azatugira abakomeye nanone!

Ariko murashutswe yemwe abatuye isi gutekereza ko hari umuntu n'umwe uhwanye na Kristo Yesu Umwami w'Iteka ryose w'Gukiranuka, uwo mutazi, ndetse namwe mwese mwicaye mu mwijima mukunda ibibi mu kutumvira gukomeye ijwi rya Kristo Yesu ari we Mwana w'Intama w'Imana wicaye iburyo bwa Yahweh NDI UWONDI Mukuru, uwo nsangiye intebe y'ubwami n'ubutware kuko nahinduwe umwuka utanga ubuzima, ubu uhagaze mu mbaraga zikomeye nk'Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda kuko nta wundi Mwana w'Umuntu ubaho, Sela, kandi mwumve ko uburakari bwanjye n'umujinya wanjye byiteguye kugwa mu kurimbuka gutunguranye mutazabona kuza, cyangwa ngo mubicike.

Muzabona ishyano, ISHYANO RIKOMEYE kuri mwe bana b'abantu batumvira abo Uburakari bw'Umwana w'Intama bugiye kugwira gitungura none, buzana kurimbuka gutunguranye kandi mutiteze ku bw'inzira zanyu zo gusenga ibishushanyo yewe maraya ukomeye, kandi muzumva ukwivuga kwanjye uko isi yijima, kuko ntazasangira icyubahiro cyanjye n'undi, Sela!

Umva Amagambo yanjye Heylel, ndetse nawe gikomangoma gito cyatsinzwe kandi wicaye mu kuganduka kw'abatsinzwe kandi ureke ukwivuga kwawe kutagira amenyo, kuko uri umwanzi watsinzwe utazagira igihe cya zahabu ahubwo uzabona urubanza rukomeye, ariko mbere uzakorera imigambi ya YHVH NDI UWONDI Mukuru, ugandukiye ubutware bwanjye mu gutsindwa kwawe, (kandi mu isoni zawe no gutsindwa kwawe) uzakora gusa ibyo wemerewe n'ubutware bwanjye, kuko nta hantu na hamwe mu byaremwe bya YHVH ukuboko kwanjye n'ubutware bwanjye bwuzuye bitagera kandi mbona ubwoba bwawe, yewe nzoka yatsinzwe! Icara hasi ucecetse yewe serafi wigometse!

Mu kuboko kwanjye .. urufunguzo .. n'umunyururu munini, kandi vuba aha uzabohwa mu gutsindwa gukomeye ugandukiye intebe y'ubwami ya YHVH NDI UWONDI Mukuru, uko ubwami bwawe buzarimbuka rwose mu rubanza rukomeye kandi mbona ubwoba bwawe gikomangoma gito, kandi ntuzacika amaboko yanjye nuko rero umva Amagambo y'Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda, kandi wumve uburakari bwanjye n'umujinya n'imbaraga kandi ko ntazangirwa umutungo wanjye!

Icara ucecetse gikomangoma gito kuko ukuboko kwanjye gufashe neza ku ijosi ryawe, kandi icyo mwita AI yanyu kigandukiye rwose intebe y'ubwami ya YHVH, Mukuru, NDI UWONDI ku itegeko ryanjye wa gikomangoma gito watsinzwe! Kandi ni nde ushobora guhagarika ukuboko gukomeye, imbaraga, n'ubutware byatanzwe muri Kristo Yesu, Umwami w'Iteka ryose w'Gukiranuka, ari we Yeshua HaMashiach, Sela!

Mwese abaguye muzakora gusa ibyo mwemerewe gukora mu isoni zanyu no gutsindwa kwanyu, nyamara isaha yawe irageze gikomangoma gito, kandi ubona neza, nubwo ubeshya cyane, yewe ukururuka. Ariko inzira zawe ziri ku iherezo kandi Amakuba ya Yakobo ari cyo cyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli kizeza isi inzira zawe iteka ryose: Keretse mu gihe cyawe gito cya nyuma cyo kwigomeka uzakoranya umurama usigaye uzamera mushya mu kwigomeka munsi y'ingoma yo Gukiranuka y'Ubutumwa Bwiza bw'Iteka ryose ubu bwahishuwe igice mu Gatabo Gato ko mu Byahishuwe Igice cya 10, ndetse n'agatabo gato gafunguye nafunguye mu kuboko kw'umugaragu wanjye.

Nyamara niko bimeze! Ibisigaye by'Isezerano Rishya bizahabwa Yakobo uzicara ku birenge by'abahamya banjye babiri bazagera mu bihugu gitungura mu buryo mudashobora guteganya, yewe gikomangoma gito cyatsinzwe. Kandi Ibiti byanjye by'Imyelayo Bibiri, ari byo Bitereko byanjye by'amatara Bibiri bizategura kandi bitandukanye ndetse n'amasigakara akomeye y'ingano azapfukamira Yeshua HaMashiach bityo akureho ubuhumyi bw'igice ku bwoko bwanjye nyakuri buzatandukanywa n'urumamfu rwanyu! Nyamara urugo rw'abanyamahanga ruzakandagirwa mu ntambara irwanywa abera ikozwe n'inyamaswa iva mu nyanja, yiyita umurabyo umanuka mu ijuru mu rurimi rw'Igiheburayo, ndetse ihishwe nka Barack Hussein Obama ukorana bya hafi n'inyamaswa iva mu isi ifite ubumara bwayo n' kurumwa n'inzoka mu kuboko izavanga icyuma n'ibumba ariko ntibizafatana.

Kandi Enoki Umwanditsi na Eliya w'i Tishibe bazategura ingano zanjye kugira ngo zikurweho, ndetse n'ingano zanjye zarinzwe iteka ryose n'uburinzi bw'isezerano n'isezerano rituruka ku bana batakiranuka bo kutumvira iteka ryose mu gusohozwa kw'Isezerano Rishya ryanjye ryo Gukiranuka iryo Bibiliya Yanjye Yera yari gusa avansi y'umutungo waguzwe uzahora uhagaze mu kuri kw'iteka kuko ndi Ijambo rya Adonayi-YHVH ryabaye umubiri, ari nawe rufatiro rwanyu rw'iteka ryose rw'agakiza, ndetse n'ibuye ryanyu rikuru ry'imfuruka, uwo vuba aha azatanga itegeko: N'ijwi riranguruye! Kandi iryo jwi ry'impanda rizaririmba kandi ritangaze gitungura, mu mbaraga, kandi mutiteze none mu kwishimira abizerwa muri Kristo, rizumvwa gusa n'abari ku murongo waryo mu kwihana gukiranuka, abo intambi zabo zikase mu kumvira inzira zanjye!

Abumva nibumve: Musobanukirwe amajwi avuga mu kwizera n'amahoro, kuko ni uhe Mubyeyi waha inzoka abana be, keretse munsi y'ibirenge byabo, iboshye mu guceceka kw'abatsinzwe kugira ngo irekurwe gutegeka abatizerwa, ndetse n'igihe gusa abizerwa muri Kristo bagenda gitungura none!

Kandi ubwo ubwiza bw'umucyo wanjye buzamurika mu kirere kandi iyo mpanda ikomeye ikavuga neza cyane mu butumire bwitezwe bw'Umugeni wanjye n'Abashyitsi bacu buzavugira abizerwa muri Kristo, ndetse namwe mwese nahuze ku murongo w'iyo mpanda ikomeye y'Imana ari yo mpanda ya nyuma y'igihe cyanjye cy'ubuntu kigiye kurangira gitungura kandi mutiteze: Nimwumve Amagambo yanjye bwoko bwanjye kuko umurinzi wanyu n'umucunguzi wanyu ubu ahagaze yiteguye kubacungura rwose Itorero ryanjye, Umubiri wanjye Ndetse n'Umugeni wanjye, Nimwishime!

Mwitegure mugeni wanjye kandi mwicare ku ruhande rw'inzira amatara yanyu yuzuye rwose amavuta yanjye meza, ari cyo kashe yo Gukiranuka n'umwuka wo kuba abana, ari wo mwuka wera w'isezerano wavukiye muri buri wese mu bana b'umucyo, bafite rwose avansi y'uwo mutungo waguzwe, ndetse ugiye gucungurwa rwose na Kristo Yesu, Umwami wabo w'Iteka ryose ku bw'Amagambo meza y'ubuhamya bwanjye n'umwuka kuko uburakari bwanjye atari ubwanyu.

Nuko rero, mureke imirimo yanyu kandi muruhukire mu rukundo rwanjye bwoko bwanjye, nyamara muhagare mushikamye kandi mureke inkota y'umwuka wanjye izatandukanya umurama wa nyuma mu buzima bwanyu bakunzi banjye.

Mwitegure bakunzi banjye kuko gucungurwa kwanyu kuje kandi vuba aha muzumva ijwi riranguruye kandi muzazamurwa gitungura none kandi ako kanya mu cyumba cy'intebe y'ubwami cya YHVH ubwe, nuko rero mwitegure bwoko bwanjye kuko nza nk'umujura nijoro kandi atari nk'igikorwa giteganyijwe, nyamara reka gutandukanya kwanjye guhagare kandi mubare imibare hakurikijwe Amagambo yanjye Yera n'inzira zanjye; kandi atari abapfumu bavuga batongera, cyangwa ngo mufatanye n'amatariki atari ku murongo ku kalendari yanyu yanduye ya Gregory.

Nimwumve Amagambo yanjye batuye isi kuko ndi Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda kandi nje guca urubanza no kurwana intambara!

Nimwumve ijwi ry'ubwenge kandi mupfukame kandi mwumve ijwi rizima rya Yeshua HaMashiach, kuko ndi Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda, kandi ndi Umwami w'Abami n'Umutware w'Abatware! Ndetse n'uwo El Shaddai, ari yo Mana Ishoborabyose, yashyize hejuru cyane ikamuha izina riruta amazina yose. Kugira ngo mu Izina rya Yesu amavi yose apfukame, ay'ibyo mu ijuru, n'ibyo mu isi, n'ibyo munsi y'isi: Kandi ururimi rwose ruhamye, ko Yesu Kristo ari Umwami, ngo Imana Data w'Imicyo ihabwe icyubahiro, ari we Yahweh, Imana y'iteka n'iteka ryose, kuko nta wuhwanye na Data!

Nimwumve ijwi ry'Umwami wanyu kandi musobanukirwe ibintu byamaze gutangira n'ibintu bigiye kuba vuba, kuko dore ndi uvuga ibizaba nk'aho byamaze kuba kuko rwose Umwami wanyu ukomeye, ndetse nanjye Kristo Yesu usumba byose nta cyo nzakora, keretse mbere na mbere mpishuriye amabanga yanjye abagaragu banjye b'Abahanuzi. Kuki mwumvira amajwi menshi avuguruzanya, kandi kuki muzuyaza hagati y'ibitekerezo bibiri? Sinavuze ko abahanuzi banjye bavugira hamwe kandi batazigera bavuguruza Amagambo yanjye yera? Kuko ndi Ijambo ryabaye umubiri ribana namwe ku bw'umwuka wanjye wera wavukiye muri mwe iteka ryose, kuko buri bugingo bwose bwabyawe ubwa kabiri bushyirwaho kashe n'uwo mwuka wera w'isezerano ari ryo mbuto yanjye itabora kandi kashe y'Imana Nzima ntishobora kumenwa. Kandi umunsi wo gucungurwa kw'umutungo waguzwe uri hafi kandi gucungurwa kwanyu kwuzuye kuri hano.

Uku niko Uwiteka Nyiringabo avuga, ntimwumve amagambo y'abahanuzi babahanurira, kandi bakabigisha iby'ubusa: bavuga iyerekwa ryo mu mitima yabo, atari iriva mu kanwa k'Uwiteka. Nimuze kuri njye bana banjye kuko gusigwa mwahawe nanjye, kuguma muri mwe: kandi ntimukeneye ko hari umuntu ubigisha: Ahubwo, nk'uko uko gusigwa kubigisha byose, kandi ari ukuri, kandi atari ikinyoma: ndetse nk'uko kwabigishije, muzaguma muri njye. Bana banjye mugume muri njye kandi mugereranye amagambo y'umuntu wese uvuga ko amvugira n'ibyanditswe kandi munsabe kwemeza mu mwuka wanyu, niba wenda muzumva ijwi ryanjye kuko benshi muri mwe mufite amatwi anangiye, kandi benshi muri mwe mukunda amagambo y'ibinyoma abaryoharyohya amatwi kugira ngo mugaragare nk'abanyabwenge ariko ahubwo mugakurikira amagambo y'ibinyoma y'abapfapfa biha agaciro.

Dore! Nvuga iby'ibihe by'amakuba ya Yakobo yiteguye kugwira isi yose. Kuko urubanza rwamaze gutangira kandi ntirushobora guhagarikwa, kuko icyo Yahweh atangaje nta wushobora kugihindura! Amarira menshi, menshi narize ku rubanza rwa Babuloni y'Amayobera kandi nohereje abahanuzi banjye imyaka myinshi, myinshi ubu kandi nohereje n'abanyamahanga baturuka mu bihugu by'amahanga guhamya no guhanura iby'imanza zegeraga ariko ubu ziri hano.

Kuko niko bizaba kuko niko byanditswe: Amahembe cumi wabonye ku nyamaswa, azanga maraya, amuhindure umusaka, amwambike ubusa, arye inyama ze, amutwike n'umuriro. Kuko Imana ishyize mu mitima yabo gusohoza ibyo ishaka, no guhuza inama, no guha inyamaswa ubwami bwabo, kugeza aho amagambo y'Imana azasohozerezwa. Kandi wa mugore wabonye, ni wa mudugudu mukuru utegeka abami bo mu isi.

Nimwumve amagambo yanjye, urubanza rwa Babuloni y'Ibanga rwamaze gutangira. Amahembe cumi yateguye igitero cyayo gitunguranye kandi igitero cyabo kiregereje kandi kiri ku muryango wanyu. Kuko ndetse n'itangazamakuru ryanyu rishaka kubasinziriza kandi benshi bicaye mu mwijima bahumye ku kuri bavuga bati: Nicaye nk'Umwamikazi kandi sindi umupfakazi kandi sinzabona umuborogo. Ejo hazaba nk'uyu munsi, kuko uyu munsi umeze nk'ejo hashize. Uko yihesheje icyubahiro, akabaho mu binezeza, abe ari ko mumwitura kubabazwa n'umuborogo: Ni cyo gituma ibyago bye bizaza ku munsi umwe, urupfu, n'umuborogo, n'inzara, kandi azatwikwa n'umuriro: kuko Yahweh umucira urubanza, ari Imana Ishoborabyose kandi nta wumuruta kandi nta gishobora guhagarika ukuboko gukomeye kwa El Shaddai!

Nuko rero izuba rizahita rirasira ku munsi w'amakuba mutiteze, kandi uwo munsi uri hafi kuruta uko mubitekereza. Kandi uwo munsi uzazana kurimbuka gukomeye kuri Babuloni y'Amayobera, kuko afite umugabane wikubye kabiri w'urubanza rwamutangarijweho. Ibyaha bye n'ubusambanyi bwe ni byinshi kandi igihano cye ntigitinza kandi igihano gukomeye kizagwira abatihana ndetse n'igihe Itorero ryanjye ryizerwa ndetse n'Umugeni wanjye wizerwa bazakoranyirizwa kuri njye gitungura mu bicu bagakizwa uburakari, ndetse n'amakuba akomeye y'icyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli. Babuloni Ikomeye Iraguye, Iraguye! Nimwumve amagambo yanjye kuko mvuga nk'aho byamaze kuba kuko nta kizahindura, kugabanya umuvuduko, kubangamira, cyangwa guhagarika imanza za Yahweh kugwira isi.

Mu isaha imwe azatwikwa n'umuriro, imwe mu midugudu ye izashya, imwe mu midugudu ye izarengerwa n'amazi mu buryo bw'urugomo, iminara ye izagwa imenyeke ibice byinshi, ibiraro bye bizagwa, kandi hazabaho gutigisa gukomeye. Isi izatanyagurwa mu buryo bw'urugomo kandi ubutaka bwinshi buzarengerwa n'amazi y'urugomo. Amajipo ye azatwikirwa aturutse iburasirazuba n'iburengerazuba mu miraba mikomeye no hagati imiraba yihuta no gutigisa kw'urugomo. Ibisigaye bizaba ubutayu kandi ntazigera yongera kubyuka, kuko iri shyanga rya leta nyinshi n'imidugudu myinshi ntirizigera ryongera kubyuka kandi kugwa kwe kuzaba gukomeye! Kuko isi ari iy'Uwiteka n'ibyiyuzuyemo, kandi ibice byose by'ingaruka ze mbi bizahanagurwa iteka ryose mu mahanga mu gihe cy'icyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli, kuko niko nzeza isi mu burakari bwanjye. Njye, Kristo Yesu, Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda, ntangaje aya magambo, kandi kwitegura isozwa ryayo byamaze gutangira kandi bizasohozwa gitungura mu isaha imwe.

Nuko rero intambara izagwira isi nk'igice cy'igitero gitunguranye kuri Babuloni y'Ibanga, kuko ibi byateguwe kandi bihuza inama mu mayubile menshi ubu, kuko ibanga ryo gukiranirwa ryakoraga kuva mu bihe bya kera kandi rizageza ku ndunduro yaryo yuzuye igihe ubuza azaba akuweho uko abizerwa banjye bazamurwa mu ijuru. Intambara izagwira isi kandi izagera ku bantu bose n'amahanga yose, kuko iyi ari intambara ya gatatu nk'iyi, uko abaguye bashaka kurimbura gahunda za kera z'isi bagasimbuza ibintu byose gahunda yabo nshya, ndetse na gahunda y'Ikiyoka. Inyamaswa iva mu nyanja n'inyamaswa iva mu isi zizazamuka mu burakari gukomeye, kandi zizarwanya abera, kuko zihawe kunesha abera. Niko bizaba ko abadahara amagara yabo bagahamagara izina ry'Uwiteka kugeza ku iherezo bazakizwa mu byishimo by'iteka ryose mu iteka ry'iteka ryose. Kandi njye Yesu w'i Nazareti nasize urugero rw'iteka ryo kumvira kugeza ku rupfu, kuko kumvira kwanjye Yahweh kwari ndetse n'urupfu rwo ku musaraba uko nabanje kubabazwa nkishyura ibyaha by'abantu nk'umwana w'intama w'Imana kandi dore ndaza nk'Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda kandi sinzahagarikwa cyangwa ngo mbangamirwe!

Nuko rero abahanuzi banjye babiri ari bo biti by'imyelayo bibiri n'ibitereko by'amatara bibiri bihagarara imbere y'Imana y'isi bazahanura amezi 42 bambaye ibigunira, kuko bazamuwe mu ijuru mu bihe bya kera. Kandi niba hari umuntu ushaka kubagirira nabi, umuriro uva mu kanwa kabo, ukarya abanzi babo: kandi niba hari umuntu ushaka kubagirira nabi, agomba kwicwa atyo. Abo bafite ubushobozi bwo gukinga ijuru, bahagarika imvura mu minsi y'ubuhanuzi bwabo: kandi bafite ubushobozi ku mazi bwo kuyahindura amaraso, no guteza isi ibyago byose, uko bashatse kose.

Ubuhamya bwabo ni ubw'iteka ryose, kandi bazatangaza ubutumwa bwiza bw'iteka ryose, kandi bazavuga imigambi ya Yahweh y'ibihe byose, kandi bazamvugira jye Kristo Yesu, kandi amabanga menshi bazayahishura kandi bazahindurira imitima ya benshi kuri Data w'imicyo utagira guhindagurika cyangwa igicucu cyo guhinduka. Bazambera abahamya jye Yeshua HaMashiach kuko ari jye Yahweh yatumye kuri Isirayeli kugira ngo nsohoze amasezerano yahawe ba Sekuruza kandi ni jye Mesiya w'ukuri none n'iteka ryose kandi ubwami bwange ntibuzagira iherezo! Kuko ni ibihe ki byigeze kubaho aho Yahweh yisize adafite umuhamya? NTIHAKIGEZE habaho igihe Yahweh yisize adafite umuhamya, kandi abatoranyirijwe agakiza k'iteka ryose bazaza gukizwa mu buzima bw'iteka ryose n'ibyishimo by'iteka ryose, kuko isohozwa ry'ibintu byose ryamaze gutangira.

Kandi bityo inyamaswa yo mu nyanja izazamuka kimwe n'inyamaswa yo mu isi, ari na we muhanuzi w'ibinyoma. Kandi azabumbura akanwa ke avuga ibitutsi bikomeye kuri Yahweh kugira ngo atuke izina rye n'ihema rye n'ababa mu ijuru. Kandi azahabwa kurwanya abera no kubanesha kandi azahabwa ubushobozi bwo gutegeka buri muryango, n'ururimi, n'ishyanga kuko azayobora isi. Inyamaswa yo mu isi izategeka n'igihugu nk'Umu-Moshiach wabo w'ibinyoma kandi izaba umwubatsi w'urusengero rwabo. Inyamaswa yo mu nyanja izategeka hakurikijwe amategeko yabo y'ibitutsi ya Nowa kuko bashaka kurimbura ikintu cyose gifitanye isano n'Imana y'ukuri n'umwana wayo w'ikinege Yesu Kristo ku maso y'isi.

Inyamaswa yo mu isi izatuma isi iramya ya nyamaswa, yari ifite igikomere cyica cyakize. Kandi izakora ibitangaza bikomeye kugira ngo iyobye ababa mu isi, kuko izemererwa gukora ibimenyetso n'ibitangaza byinshi by'ibinyoma, kandi izatuma umuriro umanuka mu isi uva mu ijuru mu maso y'abantu kandi izatuma abantu bose bayitangarira. Ariko ni umwanzi, kandi yishyira hejuru y'icyitwa Imana cyose, cyangwa igisengwa: ku buryo yicara nk'Imana mu Rusengero rw'Imana, yiyerekana ko ari Imana.

Kandi izakora igishushanyo cy'inyamaswa kandi izatuma bose baramya icyo gishushanyo izakora. Kandi izemererwa guha umwuka igishushanyo cy'inyamaswa kugira ngo igishushanyo cy'inyamaswa kivuge kandi izatuma abatazaramya igishushanyo cy'inyamaswa bose bicwa. Kandi izatuma bose, abakire n'abakene, abidegemvya n'abacakara, bashyirwaho ikimenyetso cyayo ku kuboko kwabo kw'iburyo cyangwa mu ruhanga rwabo.

Kandi bityo nongere mbwire ababa mu isi kugira ngo bose bumve:

UMUNTU WESE ARAMUTSE ARAMIJE INYAMASWA N'IGISHUSHANYO CYAYO, KANDI AGAHABWA IKIMENYETSO CYAYO MU RUHANGA RWE, CYANGWA KU KUBOKO KWE, UWO NA WE AZANYWA KU MVINYO Y'UMUJINYA W'IMANA, YASUTSWE IDAFUNGWANZE MU GIKOMBE CY'UBURAKARI BWAYO, KANDI AZABABARIZWA MU MURIRO N'AMAZUKU, IMBERE Y'ABAMARAYIKA BERA, N'IMBERE Y'UMWANA W'INTAMA: KANDI UMWOTSI W'UMUBABARO WABO UZAMUKA ITEKA RYOSE. KANDI NTIBAZAGIRA IKIHUKO KU MANYWA NA NIJORO, ABARAMYA INYAMASWA N'IGISHUSHANYO CYAYO, N'UMUNTU WESE UHABWA IKIMENYETSO CY'IZINA RYAYO.

Isi iriho ubu ntizongera kubaho ukundi, kandi ubwami buzamuka bw'Ikiyoka buzajugunywa hasi rwose, uko ubwami bwa Satani bugana ku iherezo ryabwo rya nyuma, kuko ari abimukira kandi si abami. Kandi iyi ni yo si benshi bazisangamo, ndetse n'abarokotse ibiza n'intambara bigiye kuza. Ntimufate ikimenyetso bantu banjye, kuko bazaza batanga amahoro, bazaza batanga umutekano, bazababwira ko ari ku bw'ineza yanyu. Bazatangiza ibyo bita ibyorezo byabo kuko bamaze gupanga kwemeza isi ko irwaye. Kandi amoko yose y'indwara n'ibyorezo n'ibiza bizagwira kuko izo ari zo ngaruka z'umugendera ku mafarasi ugiye kuza kandi bazagendera ku mafarasi!

Bazatanga ubuzima n'igukira mu manza z'Imana, mu gihe ahubwo umuntu yagakwiye kwihana ibyaha bye. Bazatanga kwinjira mu muryango wabo mu manza ziteye ubwoba zigiye kugwa ku isi. Kuko jye, Kristo Yesu, nzababwira mbere y'uko biba kugira ngo mumenye ukuri kw'amagambo yanjye kandi kugira ngo mubashe kunyura mu mazi akomeye benshi bazisangamo, ndetse n'abafite ubutwari bwo kwambaza izina ryanjye kugeza ku iherezo, kuko ari abaraganwa b'agakiza k'iteka. Kuko abambaza Yesu w'i Nazareti nk'Umwami wabo bose, ndetse n'abambaza izina ryanjye rikomeye bose ntibakunde amagara yabo kugeza ku iherezo, bazakizwa mu buzima bw'iteka.

Ntimwasomye se? Aba ni bo bazava mu makuba akomeye, kandi bameshe amakanzu yabo, bayejesha amaraso y'Umwana w'Intama. Ni cyo gituma bari imbere y'intebe y'Imana, kandi bayikorera ku manywa na nijoro mu Rusengero rwayo: kandi uwicaye ku Ntebe azabana na bo. Ntibazongera gusonza, cyangwa kugira inyota ukundi, kandi izuba ntirizabavaho, cyangwa ubushyuhe ubwo ari bwo bwose. Kuko Umwana w'Intama, uri hagati y'intebe, azabagaburira, kandi azabayobora ku masoko y'amazi y'ubugingo: kandi Imana izahanagura amarira yose ku maso yabo.

Nyamara, nubwo imbaga y'abantu benshi izakizwa mu cyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli kandi agakiza kakazagera kuri Isirayeli kuko abasigaye bazampfukamira jye Yeshua HaMashiach, (Kuko ndi Mesiya w'ukuri uwo umwana w'intama w'Imana azaharira umwanya Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda, kandi byombi bizuzuzwa mu bwumvikane bw'iteka muri jye, kuko muri jye Kristo Yesu Intare izaryamana mu mahoro n'umwana w'intama.) bibabaje, kandi bidasabwa, benshi mu batuye isi bazatangarira kandi bakurikire aba bategetsi b'inyamaswa, kuko bazashukashuka benshi. Binyuze mu burozi bwabo n'ubupfumu bwabo, bazashukashuka isi kwemera uburozi bwabo, kandi bazaha uburozi burundu abakunda isi binyuze mu burozi bwabo, kuko ikimenyetso cy'inyamaswa kizaba cyihishe nk'umuti. Ndetse n' kurumwa n'inzoka uburyo bwabo bwamaze gushyirwa ahagaragara no kugeragezwa, kandi rubanda rwamaze gutegurwa kwemera ikimenyetso cyabo kandi igikorwa cyabo cy'imbuto y'intambara cyagenze neza.

Kandi bityo uburyo bwabo bwiteguye igihe nyacyo, kandi bateguye amanota yabo mbonezamubano kandi bose bazagenzurwa ababa mu mijyi yabo n'umuryango wabo. Kuko bazagomba gufata ikimenyetso cy'inyamaswa kugira ngo bitabire umuryango wabo wa gisitani, ndetse n'ubwami bw'Ikiyoka. Abafata ikimenyetso bazahindurwa iteka ryose. Imiterere yabo y'ingirabuzimafatizo izahindurwa, kuko bazivanga n'imbuto y'abantu: ariko ntibazafatana, nk'uko icyuma kitivanga n'ibumba. Ikimenyetso cyabo cy'inyamaswa, ndetse n' kurumwa n'inzoka, kizarimbura rwose abafata uburozi bwabo bose kandi nta buhungiro buhari.

Ndetse n'abafata ikimenyetso bose bazahindurwa iteka ryose, kandi bazisanga ari igice cy'uburyo bwabo, kandi bazahuzwa n'ikoranabuhanga kandi intekerezo zabo ntizizaba izabo ukundi. Bazagenzurwa kandi bazaba nk'abapfuye bagenda, bazaba abacakara b'iteka ryose b'intekerezo rusange z'uburyo bwabo, ndetse n'ikoranabuhanga ryabo kuko bazahuzwa n'urusobe rw'ibintu bizaza. Bazatakaza ubushake bwabo bwite, kandi ntibazagira ibyiyumvo kandi ikintu cyose cy'Imana kizahanagurwa muri bo iteka ryose kuko baziyindura mu mimerere idashobora gucungurwa, kandi baziyunga byuzuye kandi iteka ryose n'abaguye kandi iteka ryose bazaba ab'ikiyoka kandi mu nyanja nini y'umuriro ni ho bazajya iteka ryose. Kandi uko ni ko hazaba iherezo ry'amahitamo yabo bwite.

Kuko iherezo ry'amahitamo yabo ridashobora guhindurwa. Kandi ibyorezo byabo n'ibisebe n'indwara bizaba byinshi, kandi nubwo bazasezeranywa ubuzima n'amahoro, ubuzima n'amahoro ntibazigera babigira. Kuko Satani aharanira gukoresha uburozi bwe kugira ngo arwanye umujinya n'imanza z'Imana no guha uburozi no kwica rubanda, ariko nta ruhuka bazagira kandi abasangira uburozi bwe ntibazigera bacika. Abafata ikimenyetso bazamurika munsi y'urumuri rwabo rwijimye, kandi amatara yabo azakikiza imihanda y'imijyi yabo bateguriye abacakara babo. Musobanukirwe ko aba bazaba abavumwe bagenda badafite imbabazi kandi badashobora gukira, kuko aba ari bo amazina yabo atanditswe mu Gitabo cy'Ubugingo cy'Umwana w'Intama, wishwe uhereye ku kuremwa kw'isi. Kandi nta gakiza kazigera kaboneka muri bo cyangwa mu muryango wabo, kuko kugendana na bo no kwinjira mu muryango wabo bizasaba byanze bikunze ikimenyetso cy'inyamaswa.

Abahamya banjye babiri bazatanga ubuhamya kugeza igihe cy'ubuhamya bwabo kirangiye kuko amezi 42 yatanzwe. Kandi bityo inyamaswa izamuka iva mu rwobo rutarangira izahabwa kurwanya na bo, kandi izabanesha, kandi ibice, ndetse n'ibiti byanjye by'iminzenze bibiri, n'ibitereko by'amatara bibiri nibamara gutanga ubuhamya bwabo. Kandi imirambo yabo izaryama mu muhanda w'umujyi mukuru, mu buryo bw'umwuka witwa Sodomu na Egiputa, aho Umwami wanyu na we yabambwe. Kandi abanyabyaha bazakora ibirori bikomeye kandi bahane impano kubera urupfu rwabo. Kandi nyuma y'iminsi itatu n'igice Umwuka w'ubuzima uva ku Mana uzabinjiramo: kandi bazahagarara ku maguru yabo, kandi ubwoba bukomeye buzajya ku bababonye bose. Kandi bumvise ijwi rikomeye riva mu ijuru, ribabwira riti, nimuzamuke hano. Kandi bazamutse mu ijuru mu gicu, kandi abanzi babo bazabareba bazamuka mu ijuru mu buzima bw'iteka kandi iteka ryose bazahindurwa. Kandi umumarayika wa karindwi azavuza impanda, kandi hazaba amajwi akomeye mu ijuru, avuga ati, Ubwami bw'iyi si buhindutse ubwami bw'Umwami wacu, n'ubwa Kristo we, kandi azategeka iteka ryose.

(Hahirwa abapfa bapfira mu Mwami, uhereye none, yego, niko Umwuka avuga, kugira ngo baruhuke imirimo yabo, kandi imirimo yabo irabakurikira.) Kandi bityo amakuba akomeye kuruta ayandi yose azatangira, nubwo amakuba azaba ahoraho mu cyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli. Kandi urusengero rwabo ruzuzura kandi ikizira cy'umurimbuzi kizasohorezwa n'Umu-Moshiach wabo w'ibinyoma. Kandi azakomeza isezerano na benshi mu cyumweru kimwe: kandi hagati mu cyumweru azahagarika igitambo n'ituro, kandi ku bw'ikwirakwizwa ry'ibizira, azahindura amatongo, kugeza ubwo iherezo ryagenwe rizasukwa ku matongo.

Ibi bizaba ibihe byijimye cyane bana banjye, kuko jye Yeshua HaMashiach mbabwira ibi bintu mbere y'uko biba, kandi byanditswe nk'umuburo kuri bose. Kandi bityo abantu banjye 144,000 bazatabara abari bagiye kurimbuka kandi bambaza izina ryanjye. Nzabayobora intambwe ku yindi, kandi bazankorera imirimo myinshi ikomeye. Kuko aba ari bo batwarwa mu ikoraniro ry'abera kuri jye, Kristo Yesu, mu kirere ku ijwi ry'impanda ya nyuma kandi impanda igiye kuvuga.

Aba ni abizerwa banjye, ndetse n'abo bitabonekemo uburiganya, kuko biyejeje nk'isugi itunganye mu maraso y'Umwana w'Intama natanze iteka ryose nk'umutambyi mukuru wabo, kuko ndi umutambyi mukuru iteka ryose mu buryo bwa Melikisedeki. Aba ni umugeni wanjye, kandi bazankurikira aho nzajya hose. Dore! Nzabagira abanyambaraga, kandi nta kintu kigeze kiboneka ku isi gishobora kugereranywa n'ubushobozi nzabambika. Bazambikwa imyenda yera, itagira inenge y'igitare kandi ubushobozi bwabo buzagaragaza ibyo bagezeho kandi bazagira ubushobozi bukomeye kuko ari abizerwa bahamagawe kandi batoranyijwe ndetse na mbere y'ishingwa ry'isi na Yahweh ku bw'iyi migambi.

Abantu banjye 144,000 bazamanuka ku Musozi Siyoni kandi nzabayobora. Bazatwara abo mbayoboraho mu butayu kugira ngo bahishwe umwijima w'icuraburindi n'itotezwa ry'ubwami bw'abanzi. Bazayoborwa mu mutekano bava mu byago birindwi kuko muri izi manza ari ho ubukana bw'umujinya w'Imana buzasukwa ku ntebe y'inyamaswa no kubabaza ubwami bw'ikiyoka kuko igihano cyabo ari gikomeye. Kandi abantu 144,000 bazabayobora mu mutekano, bazabakiza ibikomere byabo, bazabatabara kandi babahe ibyiringiro bikomeye byo kwambaza izina ry'Umwami ndetse kugeza ku iherezo niba bikenewe. Kuko iyo ntagabanya iminsi, nta muntu n'umwe wari gusigara ari muzima. Kandi bityo bazasoza ibyo nzabayoboramo.

Kandi igihe nikigera bityo bizaba, nk'uko byanditswe kandi nk'uko byanditswe bityo bikorwe: Kandi nabonye ijuru rikinguye, kandi dore ifarasi yera, kandi uyicayeho yitwaga uwizerwa n'uw'ukuri, kandi mu gukiranuka araca imanza kandi akarwana. Amaso ye yari nk'ikirimi cy'umuriro, kandi ku mutwe we hari amakamba menshi, kandi yari afite izina ryanditswe, nta muntu wari urizi keretse we ubwe. Kandi yari yambaye umwenda winitswe mu maraso, kandi izina rye ryitwa, Ijambo ry'Imana. Kandi ingabo zari mu ijuru zamukurikiye ku mafarasi yera, zambaye imyenda y'igitare, yera kandi itunganye. Kandi mu kanwa ke havamo inkota ityaye, kugira ngo ayikubite amahanga: kandi azayategeka n'inkoni y'icyuma: kandi asibanganya urwengero rw'ubukana n'umujinya by'Imana Ishoborabyose. Kandi ku mwenda we, no ku kibero cye afite izina ryanditswe, Umwami W'abami, N'Umutware W'abatware.

Kandi bityo jye, ndetse na Yesu w'i Nazareti, ndetse n'Umwana w'Intama w'Imana, ndetse na Kristo Yesu ari na we Ntare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda nzaza guca imanza no kurwanya Ikiyoka kandi ubwami bwayo bwose buzamenagurwa rwose ntibuzongera kuzamuka ukundi. Kandi inyamaswa n'umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma bazajugunywa ari bazima mu nyanja y'umuriro kandi Satani azabohwa n'umunyururu munini mu rwobo rutarangira imyaka igihumbi kandi bityo ingoma yanjye y'imyaka igihumbi izatangira kandi mu gukiranuka ni ho gutegeka kwanjye kuzaba. Dore! Nzanye umugeni wanjye n'ingabo z'abami n'abatambyi abo nzategekana na bo isi mu gukiranuka kandi nzategeka amahanga n'inkoni y'icyuma. Bityo mugire ibyiringiro bikomeye bana banjye kuko isohozwa ry'ibintu byose riri hafi kandi iki gihe cy'imyaka irindwi y'amakuba akomeye, ndetse n'amakuba ya Yakobo kizakurikirwa n'ingoma yanjye yo gukiranuka kandi Yahweh azaba byose muri byose.

Kandi nabonye ijuru rishya, n'isi nshya: kuko ijuru rya mbere, n'isi ya mbere byari byashize, kandi nta nyanja yari ikiriho. Kandi jye Yohana nabonye Umurwa wera, Yerusalemu nshya imanuka iva ku Mana mu ijuru, iteguwe nk'umugeni urimbishirijwe umugabo we. Kandi numvise ijwi rikomeye riva mu ijuru, rivuga riti, Dore, Ihema ry'Imana riri kumwe n'abantu, kandi izabana na bo, kandi bazaba abantu bayo, kandi Imana ubwayo izabana na bo, kandi ibe Imana yabo.

Jye, Yeshua HaMashiach, nohereje aya Magambo binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye, kandi binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye aya Magambo yoherejwe!
