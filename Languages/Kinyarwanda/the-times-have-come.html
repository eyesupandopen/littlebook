Ubuhamya Bubiri: Itorero
Mu Mirimo ya Yohana Umuhishuzi
16 Gicurasi 2024
Inkuba ya 3 n'iya 4

Ibihe Byageze!

Kuva kuri Yeshua HaMashiach:

Dore! Ibihe byo gutandukanya byegereje ku musozo, kandi kugaragara kwanjye kwegereje cyane ubu. Ni hafi cyane ndetse n'abamarayika n'ingabo zo mu ijuru bari ku muhamagaro w'impanda. Narambuye amaboko yanjye ku isi igiye kurimbuka mu yubile nyinshi ubu, kandi benshi bemeye amasezerano y'amahoro Data, Yahweh, yoherereje abantu, ndetse nanjye, Yesu Kristo, kuko ndi amahoro yanyu. Nyamara abandi benshi banze icyifuzo cyanjye n'ubuntu bwa Data n'imbabazi byasutswe ku isi.

Abashyizweho ikimenyetso kandi bagacungurwa ni abatoranyijwe kuva ku kuremwa kw'isi. Kuko Data yabamenye kuva kera kugeza iteka ryose, kandi mwahoze mu mutima we, nk'uko nanjye nahoze mu mutima we, kuko njye, Yesu w'i Nazareti, ndi umwana w'ikinege wa Yahweh. Kandi binyuze mu mirimo yanjye yatunganijwe nka Pasika itunganye no kumvira k'umwana w'ikinege, abana b'Imana benshi, benshi bongeye kuvuka mu buzima bw'iteka, ndetse bakuwe mu bana b'abantu, kuko ndi Umwana w'Umuntu!

Ibi bihe by'ubuntu byari ibanga rihishe mu mutima wa Yahweh atabwiye umuntu numwe kugeza ubwo arihishuriye, kandi narihishuriye intumwa zanjye zera n'abahanuzi kandi narinze Ijambo ryanjye ryera ku matwi n'imitima by'abatoranyijwe n'abizerwa. Kuko mwe, Mugeni wanjye, muri igice cy'ibanga rikomeye ry'Imana, kandi ubuzima bwanyu (buhishe muri njye) bugiye guhishurwa. Kandi nzaha ingororano abizerwa ku mirimo yabo yose y'ubudahemuka bakoze mu kwizera gukomeye kuri njye, Yesu Kristo.

Kandi gutyo nakijije imbaga nyamwinshi binyuze mu maraso yanjye y'impongano, kuko amaraso yanjye atagiraga icyaha kandi kugeza n'uyu munsi nta cyaha ngira, kuko ntazigera nkora icyaha. Ababayeho mu buzima bwabo mu budahemuka muri Kristo bazabona ingororano ikomeye, kandi bazaka nk'inyenyeri mu ijuru, kandi icyubahiro gikomeye ni icyabo, kuko batishyize hejuru nk'aho ari abakomeye. Ahubwo bampaye icyubahiro, Yeshua HaMashiach, kandi nzabamurikira Data badafite inenge cyangwa umunkanyari, kandi imyenda yabo izaba yera de kandi ntizigera yanduzwa. Kandi bose bazabaho ntibazongera gukora icyaha ukundi, haba mu kutubaha Imana cyangwa mu bwibone. Kandi bazahabwa icyubahiro muri njye.

Kuko ndi umwungeri mukuru, kandi nayoboye umukumbi wanjye mu rwuri rwiza kugeza mu bihe by'imperuka, byihuta kuza ku isi, kuko nta mucanga usigaye wo guca mu kirahure. Kandi nzaha ingororano bose, uhereye ku muto kugeza ku mukuru, mu buryo mudashobora kumva muri iki gihe; ariko uko kumva kuri ku muryango wanyu, kuko muzatwarwa mu ijuru vuba cyane. Kandi gutyo ni ko Umugeni wanjye azatwarwa mu cyubahiro, aho nzaca urubanza rw'imirimo ye kandi ngacungura byuzuye umugabo, umugore, n'umwana wa nyuma. Nzabaha ingororano ikomeye ndetse no ku tuntu duto twakozwe mu kunkorera. Kuko Imana itakiranirwa, ngo yibagirwe imirimo yanyu n'umuhati w'urukundo, mwerekanye ku Izina ryayo, kuko mwakoreye Abera, kandi mukomeza kubakorera.

Witinya, Mugeni wanjye, kuko Imana idaca urubanza nk'uko umuntu aruca, kuko umuntu akiranirwa kandi arobanura ku butoni. Umuntu aha icyubahiro abo abona ko bakomeye, kandi umuntu wa kamere akenshi yirengagiza kwicisha bugufi n'ingeso nziza agashyigikira abibone n'abiyemera. Kuko umuntu wa kamere ataka kugira ngo abarwe nk'umukiranutsi imbere y'Imana, ariko umuntu wa kamere ntashobora gutsinda kamere ye y'icyaha kandi yanga kwemera ko yononekaye, ndetse akihakana Umwami w'Abami, wamukiza.

Kandi gutyo ubwibone bubanziriza kurimbuka, n'umutima wiyemera ubanziriza kugwa. Aho kugira ngo bunamire imitima yabo ku magambo yanjye n'ubwenge n'urukundo nasutse ku isi, umuntu wa kamere ahitamo gukurikira irari rye n'ibyifuzo bye, kuko inzira z'isi zateguwe na Satani kugira ngo agerageze kandi akongeze ibyo byifuzo, ndetse n'ingororano zo gukiranirwa. Kandi gutyo ibihe by'Abanyamahanga bigiye kurangira, kandi ni igihe cy'umubabaro wa Yakobo; kuko nzacungura Isirayeli, ndetse n'intore zanjye zizerwa, bazaza kuri njye bihana ibyaha byabo n'ibyaha bya ba se banyihakanye. Kandi nk'uko byanditswe, bibe bityo.

Yewe, muntu wa kamere, kuki ugihitamo ibyaha byawe ukiyibagiza ubuntu bwanjye n'agakiza kanjye nk'aho ari ikintu cyoroheje? Haba Yahweh cyangwa njye, Yesu Kristo, nta byishimo dufite mu manza ziri kuza n'ubwoba bukomeye bugiye kurekurwa. Ariko Imana ntiyahisemo iki gice cy'umugambi wayo kugira ngo ibe inyamaswa ku byaremwe byayo: kuko Imana ari urukundo, kandi Imana ni umucyo, kandi muri yo nta mwijima ubamo na gato. Umubabaro mukomeye uri kuza wateguriwe guha abantu amahirwe ya nyuma mbere y'uko biba bicyererwe kuri bo. Imbaga nyamwinshi izakizwa mu mubabaro ukomeye, ndetse n'abakomeza izina ryanjye kugeza ku mwuka wabo wa nyuma kandi ntibakunde ubuzima bwabo kugeza ku iherezo.

Muntu wa kamere, uri intagondwa cyane, kandi ntuzumva, kandi mu bupfu bwawe wanga ubuntu bwanjye, kandi ndacyasenga nti: Data, ubababarire, kuko batazi icyo bakora. Kuko abasigaye mu mubabaro mukomeye, ndetse n'abanze icyifuzo cyanjye cy'amahoro, benshi bazarimbuka, kuko banze Umwami Yesu Kristo. Nyamara, benshi bazakizwa, ndetse n'abambaza izina ry'Umwami bose bazakizwa. Kandi gutyo ibigeragezo by'umuriro bigiye gutangira, kuko urubanza rwegereje.

Urubanza rukomeye ruri kuza ku bwami bwa Satani! Kandi wowe, muntu wa kamere, ndetse no mu kutizera kwawe kwinangiye, wanga ndetse n'icyifuzo cyoroshye. Kuko mu bihe byanjye by'ubuntu, agakiza kari ubuntu kandi koroshye, kandi icyo umuntu yagombaga gukora cyari ukwatura Yesu nk'Umwami no kwizera ko Imana yamuzuye mu bapfuye. Kandi noneho hazabaho inzara y'ibintu byiza byose mu gihugu, ndetse n'Ijambo ryanjye Ryera. Igihe gikomeye cyane cy'umubabaro cyegereje, kandi Satani mu bupfu bwe yitegura guhagarara, ndetse yibwira mu bwibone ko ashobora gutsinda kugira ngo we n'abamarayika be bashobore kugira isi yabo bwite, isi itagira Imana, kuko inzira zabo zigoramye. Ni ukuri, icyigize kigoramye ntigishobora kugororwa, kandi nta mbabazi cyangwa amahoro kuri Satani n'abamarayika be, cyangwa urubyaro rwabo.

Ndi umwana w'Imana waje mu mubiri.

Ndi Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda.

Ndi uzategeka amahanga n'inkoni y'icyuma.

Ndi uzabohora ibimenyetso vuba cyane.

Ndi uje gutwara Umugeni wanjye.

Ndi uzabasanganira mu kirere.

Ndi Ndiwe, kandi impanda igiye kuvuga.

Njye, Yeshua HaMashiach, nohereje aya Magambo.

Kuva kuri Yahweh, Imana Ikomeye Ihoraho:

Noneho haje urubanza rw'Imana ihoraho, ndetse Yahweh, kuko nohereje Umwana wanjye, kandi nta gihe gisigaye. Yemwe, mwa bateshwa agaciro mwe mwibwiraga ko mushobora gutsinda Yahweh, ndetse n'Imana ikomeye ihoraho! Yemwe, mwa bateshwa agaciro mwe mwibwiraga mu bupfu bwanyu ko mushobora gutsinda Umwana wanjye w'ikinege, Yeshua HaMashiach. Mwikururiye umujinya w'iteka, kandi uburakari bwanjye ni bwose kuri mwe, kuko ndi Imana ifuha. Kuko kuva mu ntangiriro mwarwanyije imigambi yanjye, kandi mwarababaje, mwarahagaritse imitima, mwaratoteje, mwarakoreye iyicarubozo, kandi mwarimbuye ibyaremwe byanjye. Nta mahoro mufite.

Munyumve kandi munyumve neza, kuko ntangaza ibizababaho, kandi icyo Yahweh atangaje nta muntu n'umwe ushobora kugihindura, na rimwe, habe na gato. Kandi gutyo urubanza rwanyu ruraje. Nzoherereza Umwana wanjye gukoranya abatoranyijwe n'abizerwa banjye, ndetse n'abana ba Adamu ubu babaye abana b'Imana. Mwaratsinzwe, yewe, nzoka y'imburamumaro, yikurura mu buhemu n'ibinyoma. Kuko mutari imana, kandi ntimushobora kwikura mu kuboko kwanjye. Kuko nzishyura!

Nzabasubizaho buri gikorwa kibi cyose n'ububabare bwose n'imibabaro mwateje abere, kandi muzashya iteka mu birimi by'umuriro bishyushye cyane by'ubururu, n'umwotsi w'uburozi uzaba umwuka wanyu, kandi muzahora mu bubabare bukomeye iteka ryose. Kuko mwatumye ndimbura ibyaremwe byanjye, ndetse n'isi nk'uko yari imeze mbere y'umwuzure wa Nowa, ndetse kubera imbuto yanyu y'imburamumaro mwakoze kugira ngo irimbure abantu; kandi gutyo kubera ibyo bintu, nzabarimburira mu murage wanyu w'umuriro.

Kandi mwagerageje kurimbura isi nyuma y'umwuzure wa Nowa, ndetse mukora ibizira byanyu nanone, ariko ntibizagira umuzi cyangwa ishami; kuko urumamfu rwose ruzarandurwa, rubohwe, kandi rujugunywe mu muriro. Kandi gutyo nzarimbura isi mu rubanza rw'umuriro, kuko nasezeranye kutazongera kurengera isi yose ukundi. Ariko mu muriro urubanza rwanjye ruraje, kuko ndi umuriro ukongora byose, kandi uburakari bwanjye bubira ku banyabyaha. Kandi gutyo kubera ibyo bintu, nzabarimburira mu muriro ushyushye cyane n'amazuku yaka.

Munyumve kandi munyumve neza, kuko ntangaza ibizababaho, ndetse namwe barinzi b'imburamumaro mwasize icyubahiro cyanyu cya mbere. Nta mahoro mufite, kandi nubwo nababohoye mu minyururu yanyu mu gihe gito, nabikoze gusa kugira ngo mukore imigambi yanjye. Hanyuma urubanza rukomeye ruri kuri mwe, kandi nta mahoro mufite. Kuko mwatumye nteza umwuzure ku isi kubera ibizira byanyu. Muri ba nde bo gukina umuremyi n'ibyaremwe bya Yahweh? Muri ba nde bo guhumanya ubusitani bw'Imana n'inzira zanyu zanduye? Mwakoze ibibyarwa byanyu by'ibinyendaro n'ibyangirika mwihumanya n'abagore, kandi bazasangira namwe umurage wanyu w'umuriro. Kuko nabwiye Malaki nti: Kuko dore, umunsi uraje uzaka nk'itanura, n'abibone bose, yego, n'abakora ibyaha bose, bazaba ibishingwe, n'umunsi uje, uzabatwika, niko Uwiteka Nyiringabo avuga, kandi ntuzabasigira umuzi cyangwa ishami. Dore uwo munsi uregereje rwose, kandi mwese muzishyura hakurikijwe inzira zanyu mbi.

Kuko nahaye abagabo abagore kugira ngo batagira icyo babura, abagore bo kubabera inshuti no kubabyarira abana. Ariko mwamanutse ku Musozi wa Herumoni, ndetse musiga icyubahiro cyanyu cya mbere, kandi mwihumanyije n'abagore, kandi nta mahoro cyangwa ikiruhuko mufite. Nk'uko nategetse Enoki umwanditsi kubabwira mu gihe cy'icyaha cyanyu: Nta mahoro mufite: igihano gikomeye cyabasohoyeho cyo kubashyira mu minyururu: Kandi ntimuzihanganirwa cyangwa ngo mwemererwe icyifuzo cyanyu, kubera gukiranirwa mwigishije, no kubera imirimo yose yo kutubaha Imana no gukiranirwa n'icyaha mwerekanye ku bantu. Kandi mu minyururu muzajyayo uko mujugunywe mu nyanja y'umuriro.

Mwangije ibyaremwe byanjye kubera irari ryanyu, ndetse mufata ku ngufu benshi mwahisemo nk'abagore banyu, kandi muzishyura. Mwifatanyije na ba Satani, ndetse n'ibikomangoma byishyize hamwe kundwanya, kandi mwahisemo ibinyoma by'abaserafi. Ku bw'ibyo, muzifatanya nabo mu nyanja nini y'umuriro y'iteka, kandi muzashya iteka, kandi ntimuzigera mubona ikiruhuko. Mwangije ibyaremwe byanjye kandi mwica benshi cyane, kandi ntimwitaye ku buzima naremye. Ku bw'ibyo, sinzabitaho, kandi narabaremye, kandi noneho haje urubanza rwanyu no kurimbuka kwanyu kwanyuma.

Ndabwira ba Satani, abarinzi, abakerubi baguye, abaserafi baguye, ndetse n'abifatanyije n'ukwigomeka kwa ba Satani bose: muzashya rwose, inshuro n'inshuro iteka ryose; kuko nzababoha nkabajugunya mu muriro aho inyo yanyu idapfa, ndetse n'ibirimi by'umuriro byabikiwe mwe mwa bateshwa agaciro mwe bizaka ibirimi bishyushye cyane kubera mwe gusa, kuko mubikwiye. Kuko nzabakorera neza ibyo mwakoreye abandi. Ububabare bwose, agahinda, kwicuza, imibabaro, ndetse na buri gikorwa cyose giteye ishozi bizabagarukaho ibihe byose, kandi ntimuzarokoka. Nzambika ubusa imbere y'amahanga kandi mbashyire ahagaragara nk'abatekamutwe muri bo, kandi bazabwira imana nto (zitari imana): Mbese namwe mwabaye abanyantege nke nkatwe? Mbese mwabaye nkatwe?

Nzabasubizaho rwose inzira zanyu mbi mu bituza byanyu, kandi muzishyura iteka. Muzajugunywa muri bazima mu birimi by'umuriro, kandi muzashya imbere y'Umwana w'Intama, n'abamarayika n'abakiranutsi bose mwarenganyije bazahabwa nanjye ibyikubye inshuro nyinshi ibyo mwabatwaye. Kuko nimwishyurwa ibyaha byanyu, nzaba nahaye abana banjye batoranyijwe kandi bizerwa ingororano ikomeye izaruta ibyo mwatwaye inshuro nyinshi hejuru y'ibikorwa byanyu by'imburamumaro.

Kandi gutyo niko bizaba. Nzarema ijuru rishya n'isi nshya ibamo gukiranuka, kandi abana banjye bazabana nanjye, kandi nzatura mu bantu, kandi gutyo niko bizaba. Ariko muzashya mu nzira zanyu mbi, kandi buri kintu kibi cyose mwakoze kizabagarukaho iteka ryose. Kandi gutyo nzashyiraho ikimenyetso, kandi muzibagirana, kandi ntimuzibukwa. Kandi bose bazatura mu mahoro, kandi ntimuzigera mugira amahoro.

Dore, igihe kirashize, kandi nohereje Umwana wanjye gutwara Umugeni we, ndetse Yesu Kristo Umukiranutsi! Kuko muri we nishimira cyane! Kandi abo yakijije bose akabakiza inzira zanyu z'imburamumaro, ndetse n'abo mwareze ibinyoma, bazaruhukira muri njye kandi bagire amahoro atunganye, ibyishimo, n'urukundo. Kuko nzabagira nkabari, kandi mbakoremo abanyacyubahiro kuruta uko mwari mumeze mbere, kandi nta bwami mufite, kuko mutari umwami.

Kandi ishyano ku batuye isi, kuko uburakari bukomeye buri kuri mwe. Kuko mwanze umuremyi wanyu kandi mukunda urupfu. Kuko kunyanga ari ugukunda urupfu, kandi mu gupfa muzapfa. Uburakari bwanjye burashyushye kuri mwe, kandi nzabambura ibyishimo byose, umunezero wose, ndetse n'ibyo mukeneye by'ibanze ntibizaboneka. Kuko igihe nabasukagaho ubuntu n'imbabazi, mwahisemo gukoresha ubuntu nk'umwanya wo kwifatanya n'abateshwa agaciro, ndetse n'ubwami bwa Satani, mu gihe mwari gushobora kurokoka. Kandi noneho benshi bazarimbuka, amazina yabo atigeze yandikwa mu Gitabo cy'Ubugingo kuva mu ntangiriro. Umwana wanjye, Yesu Kristo, aregereje, kandi azatwara umugeni we mu ijuru, kandi nzakuraho ubuza. Dore! Nohereje ibihangange gusohoza uburakari bwanjye! Mwafashe ubuntu bwanjye n'imbabazi zanjye mu busa kandi mwabayeho mu buzima bw'ibyishimo by'ibyaha, kandi nzishyura.

Kuko igihe mwari gushobora kugira amahoro iteka ryose, ndetse n'ubuzima bw'iteka bw'ibyishimo byuzuye n'umunezero, mwahisemo urupfu rwanyu, kandi nzabaha icyo mwifuza. Mushaka isi y'ibibi n'umwijima; nzabaha neza icyo mwifuza, kandi nzabishyura n'ububabare bwose mwateje abandi mu gihe mwabaga mu byaha bikomeye. Nzabambura ibyishimo byose, kandi muzarya umwanda w'inzira zanyu. Urubanza rukomeye ruri ku isi, kandi nta gukererwa kuzongera kubaho.

Dore! Abambaza izina ry'Umwami bose bazakizwa. Kuko imbaga nyamwinshi izakizwa mu mubabaro ukomeye uri kuza, kandi nzabakira kuri njye. Kuko byanditswe ngo: Aba ni bo bavuye mu mubabaro mukomeye, kandi bameshe amakanzu yabo, bayejesha amaraso y'Umwana w'Intama. Ni cyo gituma bari imbere y'intebe y'Imana, kandi bayikorera ku manywa na nijoro mu Rusengero rwayo: kandi uwicaye ku Ntebe y'Ubwami azatura muri bo. Ntibazongera gusonza, cyangwa kugira inyota ukundi, kandi izuba ntirizabavaho, cyangwa ubushyuhe ubwo ari bwo bwose. Kuko Umwana w'Intama uri hagati y'intebe y'ubwami, azabagaburira, kandi azabayobora ku masoko y'amazi y'ubugingo: kandi Imana izahanagura amarira yose ku maso yabo.

Ndinginga mwese kwambaza izina rya Yesu Kristo, Mesiya w'ukuri, ndetse n'umwana w'ikinege w'Imana, kuko aje. kandi uburakari bw'Umwana w'Intama n'uburakari bwa Yahweh bugiye kurekurwa.

Ndi Yahweh, Imana ikomeye ihoraho.

Ndi El Shaddai, Imana Ishoborabyose.

Ndi Yehova Yire.

Ndi Elohim ukomeye, Imana ya Isirayeli.

Ndi uwohereje umwana we w'ikinege gucungura abazacungurwa.

Ndi uwazuye Kristo Yesu mu bapfuye akamuha izina riruta amazina yose, kugira ngo amavi yose apfukame n'indimi zose zature.

NDI NDIWE Ukomeye, kandi uburakari bwanjye bugiye gusohozwa vuba, kandi nta wundi mukuru, kandi mvuze ibyo.

Njye, Yahweh, Imana ikomeye ihoraho, nohereje amagambo nohereje, kandi nohereje aya magambo. Nohereje umwana wanjye, Yeshua HaMashiach, kandi yohereje amagambo ye ku bizerwa. Igihe kirashize.
