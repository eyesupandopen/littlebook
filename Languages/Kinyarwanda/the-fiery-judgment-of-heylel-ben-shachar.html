7 Ugushyingo 2025

Urubanza rw'Umuriro rwa Heylel Ben Shachar

Nimwumve, nimwumve Amagambo ya Adonayi-YHVH kandi mwiyegereze ijwi ryanjye, kugira ngo mwicare ku birenge byanjye, bana banjye, kandi mwumve Amagambo yanjye ndekura mvanye mu mwuka w'intumwa yanjye ikomeye n'umugaragu wanjye. Amagambo atanzwe mu butware bukomeye mu Mwana wanjye w'ikinege washyizeho kashe aya magambo akomeye mu ntumwa yanjye n'umugaragu wanjye nahaye Kristo Yesu nk'igikoresho cyatoranyijwe, kugira ngo yicare ibumoso bwe mu cyubahiro gikubye kabiri nk'umushumba wuje urukundo n'uburinzi wuzuye impuhwe kuko yarezwe n'Umushumba Mukuru ari we Yeshua HaMashiach kandi akuzuzwa urukundo rwanjye mu mbabazi n'ubuntu bihoraho bitangirwa mu mwana w'intama wa Adonayi-YHVH mu rukundo rwe aho yazinze kandi agacura Biblaridon:144 ubwe, nk'uko yabikoze byuzuye mu bantu be 144,000 bakomeye ari bo bana ba Yeshua HaMashiach yireze ubwe n'ukuboko kwe, kandi nta kurobanura ku butoni mu nzira za Adonayi-YHVH kandi Yeshua HaMashiach ni Ijambo rya Adonayi-YHVH ryabaye umubiri. Bityo rero, nimwumve mwa ba 144,000 mwese: Muri abana ba Data Yeshua, kandi NDI Adonayi-YHVH wanyu uri Data kuri mwese muri Kristo Yesu. Sela.

Bityo rero, nimwumve ubushobozi bwanjye n'ubusuguge bwanjye bw'iteka ko NDI WE ushobora guhagurutsa abarwanyi bakomeye ba Yeshua HaMashiach bo gutegeka, uhagaze ubu mu butware bwuzuye kandi ahawe ubushobozi bwuzuye no koherezwa gukiza abizerwa muri Kristo Yesu nk'Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda ivuye hagati, iteza irimbuka ritunguranye ubwo arekura isaha imwe hamwe n'inyamaswa mu kugenda kwanyu (gitunguranye cyane) icyo gihe yahawe byuzuye mu maboko ye kugira ngo asohoze mu kumvira gukiranuka n'ibyishimo, kandi ni Yeshua HaMashiach uhora akora ibyo Adonayi-YHVH we ashaka.

Kubw'ibyo, Biblaridion:144 na yo ni intwaro ikomeye kandi ifite amashami menshi mu kuboko kwa Yeshua HaMashiach uhuza byuzuye n'imigambi ye nk'uko yahishuwe kugeza ubu n'iteka ryose, unari umukozi w'urubanza rutangazwa kandi rushyirwa mu bikorwa, rwo gukoreshwa ku itegeko ryo gukubita, kandi rwanahawe inshingano yo gutangaza no gushyira mu bikorwa neza nk'uko byatangajwe munsi y'ubutware bwuzuye bw'Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda. Mu by'ukuri, Biblaridion ni inkota iri mu rwubati ifashwe mu kuboko na Yeshua HaMashiach kugira ngo ikurwe mu rwubati byuzuye mu cyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli, ndetse no ku bw'imigambi yanjye y'iteka itarahishurwa ubwo mwese muzakoranyirizwa aho ndi mu kanya nk'ako guhumbya aho nzabahishurira abo muri bo by'ukuri n'imigambi yanyu ubu ihishe muri Kristo Yesu, ariko ubu ikaba ihishuwe igice.

Nimwumve bana banjye ko abizerwa bose muri Kristo Yesu bari itorero rye, umubiri we, ndetse n'umugeni we batoranyijwe by'umwihariko muri Kristo Yesu mbere y'ishingwa ry'isi. Nubwo benshi basobanura aya Magambo n'interuro z'ubupfu nka "abatoniwe cyane n'abahawe umugisha by'ukuri" kugira ngo bahe ihumure umubiri iyo bivuzwe nta kwizera kandi nta kumva ukuri kwanjye kw'iteka, uwo si wo mugambi wuzuye ariko ni ukuri rwose ku bizerwa muri Kristo kubera kumvira kwabo no kwihana.

Nimwumve, interuro "batoranyijwe muri we mbere y'ishingwa ry'isi" yerekana ko Adonayi-YHVH wanyu yatoranyije ubwe kandi akayobora mu mbaraga zose z'ubushake n'igishushanyo cy'umugambi utunganye, nta na rimwe ahutaje ubushake bwa buri muntu na gato, mu kurema, gukora, no kongera kurema muri Kristo Yesu buri wese muri mwe mu buryo bwatoranyijwe n'ubwumvane bwuzuye n'uburenganzira bw'ubugeni n'igishushanyo byuzuye n'ukwitondera utuntu duto duto gutangaje kuri buri wese muri mwe byatwaye ibihe byose kuva kuri Adamu na Eva kugeza ubu kugira ngo mbisohoze mu bunyangamugayo bwanjye.

Muri ibihangano byanjye by'indashyikirwa byaremewe muri Kristo!

Ubu bugeni, ubunyangamugayo n'igishushanyo byabibwe mu ngirabuzimafatizo zanyu aho natoranyije by'ukuri n'ubuhanga n'ubugeni bukomeye buri kantu kose kuri mwese ku bw'imigambi yanjye y'iteka igomba guhishurwa byuzuye mu cyumba cyanjye cy'intebe y'ubwami, ako kanya kandi ku mbaraga ku itegeko ryanjye. Bityo rero, nimwumve ko naremye, nakoze, kandi narema buri wese muri mwe by'umwihariko kandi mu tuntu duto cyane ku buryo mudashobora kwiyumvisha ibyo mugiye guhindurwamo ukurikije imigambi yanjye yihariye kandi yatoranyijwe kuri buri wese muri mwe.

Bityo rero, ntimutinye, Adonayi-YHVH wanyu ntarema ikiremwa kimwe gitandukanye n'ikindi mu buryo bwo kurobanura kugira ngo inyungu yibwirwa yishimirwe mu cyaha cyo kwirata, cyangwa kugira ngo ikiremwa kimwe gitegeke ikindi mu rugomo rw'icyaha n'urwango rw'ubupfu, ahubwo ni ukugira ngo imigambi yanjye y'iteka isohozwe muri mwese. Ku by'ukuri kw'iteka: Ntabwo byari byinjira byuzuye mu mutima w'umuntu mu gihe cy'iyandikwa ry'ibi ibyo muzongera kuremwamo n'imigambi yabyo. Nuko rero, ndabasaba mu rukundo rwanjye mbakunda (mwe naremye kandi ngatoranya kubera kwizera kwanyu, bana banjye bihana mu budahemuka) kugira kwizera kungana n'akabuto ka sinapi gusa kandi mwumve ko kuva ku muto kugeza ku mukuru mu Isirayeli ya Adonayi-YHVH mwese mwaremwe neza kandi mu buryo butangaje kandi mugacurwa mu mbaraga n'ubushobozi bukomeye ku buryo ari Isirayeli ya Adonayi-YHVH izashimwa na bose.

Amahoro abe kuri mwe byuzuye.

Kandi ibi nta kurobanura abantu ku butoni, ahubwo binyuze mu bumenyi bwanjye bw'iteka kandi bukiranuka bushobora byose n'igenzura ryuzuye ry'ibintu byose aho natoranyije buri wese muri mwe muri ubu buryo ku bw'imigambi yanjye y'iteka muri Kristo Yesu. Kuko nari nzi byuzuye mu bihe by'iteka byashize ko buri wese muri mwe, ku giti cye, azaza byuzuye kuri Kristo Yesu mu bihe byanyu, nuko rero narabiteganyije. Bityo rero, nimwumve ko nubwo amabanga ahishe ahishurwa n'ubuhamya n'urugero rw'umurimo muri Biblaridion:144, ibi bikorwa kugira ngo mwese mutangire kumva ubukomezi bw'umurage wanyu n'imigambi y'iteka naremeyemo, ngatoranya, kandi nkabakora mwese binyuze mu gishushanyo cy'ubwenge kugeza ku kantu gato cyane. Nimwishime!

Bityo rero, nimuze byuzuye kuri Yeshua HaMashiach ndetse namwe mwese abizerwa muri Kristo Yesu, kugira ngo muhabwe ingororano yanyu yuzuye ikubye kabiri mu cyubahiro cyuzuye, afite mu kuboko nahaye abanesheje bose nk'uko Kristo Yesu yanesheje. Nimwumve Amagambo yanjye yo kubatera inkunga ndetse namwe mwese 144,000, muri abana barerewe ba Data Yeshua, kandi azasangira byose namwe bana be, banari abaneshi be bizerwa, banesheje ibintu byose nk'uko Yeshua HaMashiach yanesheje. Icyakora, aba si bo baneshi bonyine bazaba baranesheje ibintu byose bibarwanya, kuko benshi, benshi mu bizerwa muri Kristo Yesu batari 144,000 banesheje ibintu byose nk'uko Kristo Yesu yabikoze, kandi ingororano yabo ni nini!

Yego! Hari abaneshi benshi mu Isirayeli ya Adonayi-YHVH kandi nta kurobanura abantu ku butoni kuri Adonayi-YHVH, ndetse by'umwihariko Yeshua HaMaschiach uhora akora neza ubushake bwa Adonayi-YHVH we. Bityo rero, ntimuhangayike kandi ntimwiganyire uko mugerageza kumva ibihishe mu Ijuru bitarinjira byuzuye mu mutima w'umuntu muri iki gihe, kandi by'umwihariko mwirinde kwigereranya hagati yanyu nk'uko bisanzwe mu mimerere yanyu y'ubu yaguye, ariko yejejwe byuzuye muri Kristo Yesu.

Nuko rero nimwumve, mu Bwami bw'Ijuru, umuntu wese ushyirwa hejuru y'undi bikorwa mu butware, na/cyangwa ingororano ku bw'imigambi yanjye gusa, ariko nta na rimwe ari ukugira ngo bahabwe icyubahiro ku buryo undi mu byaremwe byanjye yakwiyumva nk'umunyantege nke, cyangwa ko abashobora gushyirwa hejuru bakwirata mu cyaha hejuru y'undi. Ahubwo, icyubahiro n'urukundo bihabwa mwese byuzuye. Kuko naremye mwese, kandi by'umwihariko abizerwa muri Kristo Yesu kubera kumvira kwanyu muri Kristo Yesu kugira ngo nshobore kongera kubarema mu cyubahiro cyuzuye. Mwe, by'umwihariko, muri ibihangano byanjye by'indashyikirwa byaremewe muri Kristo Yesu ku bw'imirimo myiza kugira ngo muyigendemo mu mbaraga zikomeye ku bw'imigambi y'iteka ya Adonayi-YHVH wanyu, itangirwa muri Kristo Yesu. Nimwishime!

Kandi nuko bimeze ko NDIHO Ukomeye, ndetse na Se w'umukwe, ari we Yeshua HaMashiach yatanze Induru ya Nnijoro ku mugeni n'abashyitsi be, kandi biteguye ku buryo amatara yabo yuzuye amavuta y'umwuka wera kandi intambi zanyu zaciweho imyanda yose kandi amatara yanyu yatsanywe ya miriro yera yakozwe n'ayo mavuta yera y'umwuka wanjye natanze muri Yeshua HaMashiach, kandi yaremye by'umwihariko muri buri wese muri mwe, abizerwa muri Kristo.

Nuko rero, ntera inkunga abizerwa bose muri Kristo Yesu gukomeza mu kumvira kwuzuye kandi kurambuye mu bintu byose ku Mushumba Mukuru wanyu wita kuri buri kantu kose k'ubuzima bwanyu mwese mu buryo buto cyane kandi butunganye. Nimuze byuzuye ku Mwami wanyu w'Iteka w'Gukiranuka nabateganyirije mwese ubwo Yeshua w'i Nazareti yitangaga nk'Ipasika itunganye y'ibihe byose mu maboko y'uwo mwana w'umwami watsinzwe mu kumvira kwose ubushake bwa Adonayi-YHVH. Nimuze byuzuye ubu, mudashidikanya kandi mwumve ko NDIHO Ukomeye abakurura binyuze muri aya Magambo yatunganirijwe gushaka amaso, amatwi, n'imitima y'abo yohererejwe bose.

Nimwumve ibihe mubayemo, kuko igihe cyanjye cy'ubuntu kigeze ku musozo, kugira ngo gihagarikwe gitunguranye kandi mu buryo butari bwitezwe kandi byuzuye ku itegeko rya Yeshua HaMashiach ubwo itegeko rizatangwa n'ijwi riranguruye!

Hanyuma, ijwi rikomeye kandi ryiza ry'impanda, ubwo gitunguranye ku isi muzaba mutakiriho!

Ubwo gitunguranye mu ijuru ry'amajuru muzakirwa mu cyubahiro gikomeye, intsinzi, imbaraga, n'icyubahiro mu murage wanyu w'ibihe wateguriwe kugabanywa abizerwa muri Kristo Yesu. Mu by'ukuri igihe cyari cyitezwe cy'incungu ya Adamu ku bizerwa muri Kristo Yesu kigeze mu Majuru nk'ikindi gihe cyose, kuko Abana banjye b'iteka bagenwe by'umwihariko n'aya Magambo yatanzwe muri Yohana wa Mbere: "Nimurebe urukundo Data yadukunze, rwatumye twitwa abana b'Imana: Ni cyo gituma iyi si itatumenya, kuko itamumenye. Bakundwa, ubu turi abana b'Imana, ariko ntiharahishurwa uko tuzaba: kandi tuzi ko naza: tuzasa na we: kuko tuzamureba uko ari. Kandi umuntu wese ufite ibyo byiringiro muri we yiyeza, nk'uko uwo ari uwera", ubu bagiye kongera kuvuka ari bazima kandi bakongera kuremwa byuzuye mu bwiza bwose, ikuzo, n'icyubahiro ako kanya ku itegeko rya Yeshua HaMashiach namuhaye by'umwihariko kandi UBU muzahagarara gitunguranye kandi mutabiteganyije mu cyumba cyanjye cy'intebe y'ubwami hamwe n'abizerwa bose muri Kristo Yesu.

Harpazo yanyu irageze!

Nuko rero, ntegetse abizerwa bose muri Kristo Yesu gukurikiza aya Magambo mu kumvira gukomeye cyane kugira ngo Kristo Yesu abeze byuzuye munsi y'amaraso ye y'impongano mbere gato y'uko mutwarwa kandi mugahindurwa iteka n'iteka ryose.

Mugume Mwiteguye!

Kuko mugiye kuvukira mu cyumba cyanjye cy'intebe y'ubwami, aho ntegereje n'ibyishimo nka Data witeguye uwo NDI WE, ku bw'ivuka ryuzuye ry'Isirayeli ya Adonayi-YHVH igomba gukoranywa UBU.

Nfite ibi mu ntekerezo, ndaje kugira ngo nfashe buri wese muri mwe kumva imbaraga n'ubusobanuro byakoreshejwe mu bwenge n'ubuhanga bukomeye na Adonayi-YHVH wanyu kugira ngo abahishe kandi abarinde Heylel Ben Shachar wakwica akura ku isi uwo ari we wese mu bana banjye ahawe amahirwe, ariko akabahishura bihagije kugira ngo hanyuma mucurwe mukurikije imigambi yanjye y'iteka muri Kristo Yesu.

Bityo rero, mwarashushanyijwe kandi murarindwa, ariko murahishwa kandi muhishwa mu maso ya bose, ariko muhishurwa bihagije gusa: Kandi byose mu maboko ya Kristo Yesu! Kuko ari we wabakunze kandi akiyitanga, kandi akeza, kandi agatunganya kandi agatsindishiriza Itorero rye rikomeye rigizwe n'abizerwa muri Kristo Yesu. Kuko mu by'ukuri, amarembo y'Ikuzimu ntazigera anesha Itorero ry'ukuri mu cyubahiro n'imbaraga byaryo byose bikomeye. Ndetse iryo Torero rigomba guhindurwa kandi rigahinduka ubu ku bw'imigambi yaryo y'ukuri kandi yari ihishe ubu igiye guhishurwa byuzuye muzamenya byuzuye nk'uko namwe muzwi.

Mwihangane bana banjye, ibihe byanyu ni UBU.

Nyamara, natandukanyije itsinda rimwe ryihariye mu Itorero, ndarigena mbere y'igihe ku bw'imigambi yanjye (nubwo mwese mufite igenwa) kandi ndariha Kristo Yesu. Bityo rero, nimwumve iri tandukaniro kuri mwese 144,000 ndetse n'abasoma aya Magambo bose.

Kristo Yesu mu rukundo rwe kuri mwese, n'urukundo rwe no kumvira Adonayi-YHVH we yahagurukije abana ba Adonayi-YHVH ari bo bizerwa muri Kristo Yesu kandi bashyirwaho kashe n'uwo mwuka wera w'isezerano kandi bashyirwa ukwabo kandi begurirwa iteka ryose kuzuza imyanya yasiwe mu Majuru mu cyubahiro gikubye kabiri, ndetse no kuragwa isi mu cyubahiro gikubye kabiri kuko mwahisemo kwizera ubuhamya bwa Yesu w'i Nazareti muri uyu munsi mubi, ndetse n'uwo mwana w'intama w'Imana ubwo yamuhishurirwaga na Adonayi-YHVH wanyu kandi muhawe amahirwe yo kugira kwizera kungana n'akabuto ka sinapi gusa, mwarizeye! Kandi iyo mbuto ikomeye kandi y'iteka yakuze cyane ubu muri mwese! Ariko ihishurwa byuzuye gusa mu cyumba cyanjye cy'intebe y'ubwami gitunguranye kandi mu buryo butari bwitezwe ubu.

Mubyumve neza, ko abizerwa muri Kristo Yesu ari abana ba Adonai-YHVH, bazakorera Kristo Yesu, ari we Yeshua HaMashiach, mu nshingano zanyu no mu byiciro byanyu nateguye neza kandi nkabigenera buri wese muri mwe. Ingororano yanyu ibagenewe neza cyane kandi izaba iruta iy'undi, gusa hakurikijwe uko ubudahemuka bwanyu bwabaye. Nyamara, ibi ntibibangamira guhabwa ikuzo kwanyu binyuze mu murage, ahubwo ni icyubahiro gihabwa ukwiye icyubahiro kubera ibintu byinshi mwese mwakoze mukorera Kristo Yesu. Kandi mwese muri umwihariko umwe ku wundi, nyamara musa mu byo mwihanganiye kandi mukanesha binyuze mu kuboko gukomeye kwa Kristo Yesu wabafashije mwese.

Kandi, abatoranyijwe mu bizerwa muri Kristo Yesu ni 144,000 bashyizwe ku ruhande by'umwihariko nk'abana barerwa ba Yeshua HaMashiach, batoranyijwe mu Bisirayeli ba Adonai-YHVH by'umwihariko n'ukuboko kwanjye kwatoranyije, n'ubushake bw'iteka kandi butabangamirwa. Aba barwanyi b'umuriro baje gucurirwa mu makuba akomeye kandi binyuze mu bigeragezo n'amakuba byinshi bose bihanganiye mu maboko ya Heylel Ben Shachar, kandi ubu babumbye kandi bacuriwe mu mbaraga n'ubushobozi bukomeye ku buryo bacuriwe byuzuye mu mitima yabo itazigera igambanira imigambi ya Adonai-YHVH igaragarira muri Yeshua HaMashiach, ndetse barinzwe mu rukundo rwa Agape rudakuka kandi rufite imbaraga zose. Selah.

Ku bw'ibyo, mwumve 144,000 banjye, ko mwese mwaremwe mu buryo bukomeye kandi butunganye mu gishushanyo cyuzuye imbaraga n'ubwenge, kandi ku itegeko rya Kristo Yesu, ari we Yeshua HaMashiach, muzasohoza imigambi yacu y'iteka kuri iyi nzoka y'ikuzimu, kandi kugira ngo abaguye bose bacirwe urubanza rwuzuye n'ingororano n'igihano byuzuye bitangwa mu rubanza rw'umuriro rwuzuye kandi rukiranuka kuri uyu gikomangoma muto watsinzwe kandi w'umupfapfa. Ndetse n'ubwo bimeze bityo, mwese 144,000 muri abakozi b'ibi bikorwa bitangwa hakurikijwe ibyanditswe byanditswe neza ngo: Kwihorera ni ukwanjye: Nzishyura, niko Uwiteka avuga. Ku bw'ibyo, muri abakozi bagenwe b'uku kwishyura gukiranuka kw'ingororano ku mitwe y'abaguye bose, ariko cyane cyane uwo gikomangoma muto w'umupfapfa, watsinzwe kandi uhora yirata, Heylel Ben Shachar.

Ndetse n'uwo gikomangoma muto watsinzwe wahawe icyubahiro hejuru ya bagenzi be ku bw'imigambi y'iteka ya Elohim, kandi mu irari rye n'ubwibone bwe ubwo yihaga ikuzo, atekereza ko ibyo namuremeye kuba byose byari iby'ikuzo rye n'imigambi ye byo gukoreshwa ku itegeko rye n'ubushake bwe, nuko agwa mu kwigomeka gukomeye no kutumvira mu buryo bukojeje isoni cyane mu isoni zose n'ubwibone buvunaguritse rwose kandi arinzwe mu mimerere ibabaje cyane kandi yononekaye atazigera akira kandi izarushaho kuba mbi cyane iteka ryose.

Dukurikije ibyo, ubwo Heylel Ben Shachar (n'abakurikiye uyu muserafi bose) bajugunywaga ku ngufu, n'urugomo, kandi mu buribwe bukomeye mu buryo bworoshye n'imbaraga zanjye bavanwa mu Ijuru ry'Amajuru, ubwo bamburwaga ku ngufu ubutware bwose kubera ibyaha byabo biteye ubwoba kandi byagambiriwe kandi byateguwe mbere byakozwe n'ubugome bwose n'urwango rw'ubugambanyi. Ndetse n'ibintu byakuze biva mu mitima yabo bwite kuko Heylel Ben Shachar yanze gutegeka ibitekerezo bye mu kumvira Elohim we, aho kwibanda ku ikuzo n'imbaraga bya Elohim we, kugira ngo mmuhagarike mu mwanya we w'iteka w'umurimo, ubushobozi, imbaraga, n'ikuzo, aho kubikora yibanze ku ikuzo rye ryigaragaza yagerageje kwigarurira ku ngufu n'urugomo nk'irye bwite.

Kandi mu kwibeshya kwe kw'ibyaha kwabayeho ubwo yihaga ikuzo mu cyaha hejuru ya Elohim we wamuremye mu buryo bukomeye n'umugambi w'imbaraga akamwambika ikamba ry'ikuzo n'icyubahiro bikomeye ku munsi yaremeweho hakurikijwe imigambi n'imigambi yanjye, n'ubuhanga n'imbaraga, Heylel Ben Shachar n'abamukurikiye bose bajugunywe n'urugomo bavanwa mu Majuru mbere y'uko bashobora kuzamuka ngo buzuze imyanya yabo y'iteka y'umurimo, ubushobozi, imbaraga, n'ikuzo kuko biyeguriye binyuze mu ndahiro zo kutumvira kw'urwango mu kwigomeka kudafite ishingiro aho kubikora.

Heylel Ben Shachar icyo gihe abigambiriye kandi abiteguye mbere yabibye kwigomeka kwe mu ibanga mu bagenzi be, nk'aho El Shaddai atabonaga ibikorwa bye n'ibitekerezo n'imigambi y'umutima we. Nyamara, nabaretse buzuza ibyaha byabo kugeza ku ndunduro, kugira ngo hanyuma bacirwe urubanza rukomeye cyane mu baciriweho iteka bose bigeze kuremwa kandi bagacirwaho iteka kubera ibyaha byabo by'urwango na Elohim.

Uko niko Isezerano ryanjye ry'Ikuzo ryishwe na Heylel Ben Shachar, ndetse n'isezerano Ryera ryakozwe na Elohim ku Bana ba Elohim benshi no ku byaremwe byinshi kandi bitangaje by'iwabo ari mu Majuru kandi bikikije intebe yanjye y'ubwami ubu nk'umubare utabarika, nyamara ni Adonai-YHVH udakeneye umujyanama kandi ukora ibintu byose akurikije inama y'ubushake BWE bwite.

Mu buryo bumwe n'uko icyaha cyose mu barinzi baguye cyashyizwe kuri Azazeli n'igihano gihuye nacyo kigatangwa mu rubanza, Heylel Ben Shachar ni we nyirabayazana w'icyaha cyose mu byaremwe byanjye nk'isoko y'ubwibone n'agasuzuguro n'icyaha no kwishyira hejuru, kandi mu butabera bwanjye bw'iteka, rwose urubanza rw'uyu muserafi watsinzwe ruzagaragaza ibi bintu mu butungane bwuzuye bw'umuriro n'ikiguzi cya nyuma mu ngororano yuzuye kandi itunganye y'umuriro. Nyamara byikubye mu gukuba n'ubunini bw'ubukana buzajegajega gusa mu buryo bw'urugomo kandi bukiyongera iteka.

Mbona ubwoba bwawe; wa gikomangoma muto watsinzwe.

Kwirata kwawe kuri he ubu gikomangoma muto? Ko waremewe mu muriro kandi iwanyu hakaba ari mu miriro mu byishimo kandi bikaba bikwiye ikiguzi cy'ukwigomeka kwawe, ko washoboraga kwihanganira imiriro y'igihano cyanjye.

Dore! Musobanukirwe ko nahagaritse isi mu gihe cya UBU kandi UBU ntibuzavaho kugeza mu ngororano y'umurage w'iteka kandi imbere y'intebe yanjye y'ubwami mwese muhagaze mwambaye.

NDI El Shaddai! Ndetse n'Imana Ishoborabyose ari na we Mwami w'Imyuka n'Uwa Kera na Kera KANDI nohereje aya Magambo binyuze ku ntumwa yanjye n'umugaragu wanjye binyuze mu Mirimo ya Yeshua HaMashiach kuko yashyize aya Magambo akomeye kandi Yera iteka ryose mu ntumwa yanjye ikomeye n'umugaragu wanjye, uwo imbaraga z'inkuba indwi zizahabwa mu guhura. Selah.
