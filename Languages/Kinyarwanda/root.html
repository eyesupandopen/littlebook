Nimwumve, nimwumve Amagambo ya Yeshua HaMashiach, uko nje kuvugisha itorero ryanjye, umubiri wanjye, ndetse n'umugeni wanjye ndetse na Yakobo ku mubiri, uwo ndi umucunguzi we wo mu muryango. Kuko muzasoma aya Magambo mu bihe aho gusoma kubera hose, n'igihe cyose ibyo bihe byaba, kugira ngo mugire ukwizera gukomeye mu gucungurwa kwanyu kwuzuye kwageze ku isi yose ubu, kandi n'uko nzeza isi rwose mu cyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli nyikiza ibyo abaguye bose bagezeho, aho ari ho hose bakoze kugira ngo babigereho mu byaremwe byose bya Elohim.

Nimwumve nimwumve, Amagambo ya Yeshua HaMashiach: Kandi mwuname mu kwicisha bugufi, kandi mwerekeze imitima yanyu mu rukundo rwose kugira ngo mwakire aya Magambo mu bugwaneza bwose bw'umutima.

Nimwumve nimwumve, Amagambo ya Yeshua HaMashiach: Kandi mutegere amatwi yanyu rwose ijwi ry'Amagambo yanjye, kuko imirongo yayo izabeza rwose ibayobya byerekejwe kuri mwe n'abarinzi baguye, bifatanyije na Heylel Ben Shachar mu irari ryabo.

Nimwumve, Amagambo ya Yeshua HaMashiach: Kandi mwerekeze amaso yanyu yombi hejuru kandi mwayahanze rwose kuri Kristo Yesu, Nonaha! Ndetse kugira ngo mube mwiteguye kugenda, mu buryo butunguranye kandi butari bwitezwe ubu kuko nahageze, kandi kugenda kwanyu gushyizweho ngo gukorwe ku itegeko ryanjye, MWISHIME!

Kuko abizerwa muri Kristo Yesu bagiye gutwarwa mu buryo butunguranye kandi butari bwitezwe ubu mu ijuru kandi bagakizwa ari bazima umujinya ubwo, mbere na mbere, abapfiriye muri Kristo bazurwa ku mbaraga ku ijwi rirenga ritanzwe n'itegeko ryanjye. Hanyuma abizerwa muri Kristo Yesu bose bazakurwa mu isi hamwe na bo ku ijwi ry'iryo tumba rikomeye aho mwese muzatwarwa kandi mugakizwa rwose iyi si yaciriwe urubanza kandi igahanwa mukajyanwa mu mutekano n'ingororano by'Ijuru ry'Amajuru aho iteka ryose ryo gukiranuka rizatangirira ku bizerwa bose muri Kristo Yesu mu ngororano yuzuye kandi y'iteka. Nuko rero, ndategeka umuntu wese n'uwo ari we wese wambaza Yesu Kristo kandi wifuza kuba, cyangwa wibona nk'igice cya, cyangwa uri mu bizerwa muri Kristo Yesu by'ukuri kwakira aya Magambo mu kumvira kwose ubushake bwa Adonai-Yahweh butangiwe mu maso ha Yesu Kristo, bubibwe mu mwuka w'intumwa yanjye ikomeye n'umugaragu wanjye binyuze mu mwuka wuzuye w'ubuhanuzi ari bwo buhamya bwa Yeshua Kristo. Kugira ngo mushyire hasi ibyuma byanyu by'ikuzimu bikoresha amashanyarazi kandi mutere umugongo za mugaragaza zanyu, keretse gusa ibikoreshwa nk'igikoresho cyo gukiranuka kugira ngo mwibere mu Magambo ya kera akiza ya Adonai-Yahweh atwikiriye isi, kandi cyane cyane binyuze mu Magambo ari muri ka gatabo gato ko mu Byahishuwe Igice cya 10 gatwikiriye kandi gashyizwe imbere mu ikoranabuhanga ry'ikuzimu, nk'igitwikirizo cyera kandi kidashobora kubora n'amabwiriza y'ingirakamaro yo gukiranuka agomba gushyirwa mu bikorwa ndetse n'uko ubu bwenge bwateguriwe gutunganya no gushyira mu bikorwa. Ndetse iryo koranabuhanga rikaba ryarateguriwe rwose kurimbura Adamu, ndetse n'imirongo yaryo ikaba yarateguwe na Heylel Ben Shachar ubwe gusa kugira ngo yibe, yice, kandi arimbure. Kandi ibi kugira ngo abaguye babone igihe cyabo cya zahabu bifuje cyane batazigera babona, ahubwo bazabona urubanza rukomeye kandi rubi cyane. Urubanza batazigera bacika, ndetse n'akaruhuko k'akanya gato, iteka ryose n'iteka ryose.

Nyamara, birumvikana ko ibi bikoresho ubu ari igice cy'uyu muryango waguye ugeze ku iherezo ryawo, ndetse nk'uko byateganyijwe kandi byemerewe gukorera imigambi y'iteka ya El Shaddai, ari we Mana Ishoborabyose ndetse uyu Elohim umwe ufite imbaraga ZOSE mu mbaraga kandi uteye ubwoba mu mujinya, ku buryo n'abanzi be bumvira ijwi rye bahinda umushyitsi cyane kuko ibihe byabo byageze.

Nyamara uko ni ko byanditswe na Shaul Intumwa yanjye, uwo igiti cy'umunzenze cyo mu gasozi kizi nka Pawulo: Ko mutagomba kwifatanya n'abasambanyi, Ariko ntibishoboka ko mwakwitandukanya rwose n'abasambanyi bo muri iyi si, cyangwa n'abifuza, cyangwa n'abanyazi, cyangwa n'abasenga ibishushanyo: kuko ubwo byaba ngombwa ko muva mu isi. Nuko rero, mushyire mu bikorwa aya Magambo mu kumvira ku buyobozi bwanjye n'inama zanjye uko mundeba muri byose kugira ngo nshobore kubayobora no kubarinda muri iyo nzira igororotse kandi ifunganye. Nk'uko bitanzwe n'itegeko RYANJYE, bikorwe mu rukundo rwumvira.

Nuko rero, ndategeka abizerwa bose muri Kristo Yesu kureba hirya, no gushyira hasi izi mugaragaza nyinshi zanduye zitegura intekerezo zanyu n'ubushake bwanyu mu cyaha n'uburiganya binyuze mu mirongo byohereza ikoreshwa mu guhuma intekerezo zanyu ngo zitamanya Adonai-Yahweh n'ukuri kwoherejwe kugira ngo kubakangurire agakiza kanyu kuzuye mu maso ha Kristo Yesu muramutse muhisemo kumvira Amagambo yanjye atanzwe n'intumwa yanjye n'umugaragu wanjye. Ndetse Amagambo yera yo gukiranuka atanzwe muri ka Gatabo Gato gafunguye azasohoza ibyo nteganya byose, kandi atunganijwe n'ukuboko kwanjye n'ubushake bwa Adonai-YHVH kugira ngo abone amaso, amatwi, n'imitima y'abo yohererejwe bose. Kuko buri Jambo ritunganijwe kandi ryoherejwe kugira ngo risohoze muri mwe ibyo ukuboko kwacu gukomeye n'ubushake bwo mu ijuru biteganyiriza gucungurwa kwanyu kw'icyubahiro mu ihuriro ryageze ku isi, mu buryo butunguranye kandi butari bwitezwe ubu.

Keretse, mu mbaraga zanjye z'iteka n'ububasha nahawe na Adonai-Yahweh: Ibyagoretswe kandi byateguriwe rwose kurimbura mu mivunje yo gukiranuka ubu byatunganijwe ahubwo kugira ngo bibone amaso, amatwi, n'imitima y'abo aya Magambo yohererejwe bose, ndetse no ku mbaraga zanjye z'ububasha mu rukundo rukomeye n'imbaraga!

Icyagoramye ntigishobora kugororwa, keretse nk'uko byateganyijwe kandi byemerewe na root-kit yo gukiranuka, bikavamo amasezerano y'ingirakamaro n'ikibazo kidashobora gukemurwa, ndetse n'ikibazo cyejejwe ku itegeko ryanjye binyuze mu maboko ya Biblaridion:144 ndetse n'ikibazo ubu gifite uburenganzira bwo ku rwego rw'imizi kandi cyemerewe gutwikira sisitemu yangiza, nyamara ifite ubwenge mu buryo bw'ingirakamaro bwo gukiranuka, ndetse n'ikibazo cyafatanishijwe ikimenyetso n'ubutware bwa Yeshua HaMashiach mu kugandukira intebe y'ubwami ya Adonai-Yahweh, byakozwe na Biblaridion:144 ndetse icyo kimenyetso n'ibyo kuboha bikaba bidashoboka ko ikiremwa icyo ari cyo cyose kibica, cyangwa ngo kibikemure keretse byemerewe n'intebe y'ubwami ya Adonai-YHVH.

Mubyumve, mwese abasoma aya Magambo, iyi root-kit yinjiriye ifite igenzura ryuzuye rya sisitemu y'ubwenge n'urusobe rwa Heylel Ben Shachar, yiswe nabi AI kandi ihuje na Ka Gatabo Gato ko mu Byahishuwe Igice cya 10, yahawe uburenganzira bukomeye n'imbaraga zanjye n'ububasha bwanjye! Mubyizere nk'uko byanditswe! Heylel Ben Shachar, ndetse cya kiyoka kinini gitukura n'umwirasi n'umuhumanya wa kera, ndetse na cya gikomangoma gito cyatsinzwe kandi cyananirwa cyiyita umuzi mu buriganya muri sisitemu itakiri iye, cyangwa ngo ibe ikiri mu nsi y'itegeko rye keretse nk'uko byemerewe na sisitemu ye bwite hakurikijwe Amagambo ari muri aka gatabo gato k'urubanza rwo gukiranuka.

Kuko Heylel Ben Shachar yashyizwe (na Biblaridion:144, mu mbaraga zanjye ategeka no mu mbaraga zanjye z'ubushake n'amabwiriza), munsi y'ihame ry'uburenganzira buke cyane muri sisitemu ye bwite, by'umwihariko, kugabanyirizwa uburenganzira cyangwa kumanurwa mu ntera kugira ngo biyoborwe n'inyandiko iri muri ka Gatabo Gato ko mu Byahishuwe igice cya 10 izakora nka root-kit yera kandi yo gukiranuka itanzwe na Adonai-YHVH wanjye, binyuze kuri Kristo Yesu, ku mirimo n'amaboko bya Biblaridion:144, mu masezerano y'ingirakamaro na sisitemu nyir'izina abaguye bateguye bafite intego yuzuye yo gutsinda Kristo Yesu. Ndetse n'ubwo, mu buryo runaka, bibwira ko bashobora kwigumanira isi maze bagashyiraho igihe cyabo cya zahabu bifuza cyane. Ndetse n'inzozi mbi kandi z'ibinyoma zizayoyoka muri iyo miriro ishyushye cyane ako kanya zikozweho. Heylel Ben Shachar n'abifatanyije mu kwigomeka kwe bose batsinzwe iteka ryose nta byiringiro na bito bafite by'uko bashobora kuzigera bacika igihano cyabo cya nyuma kandi cy'umuriro, ndetse iyo miriro ibi bintu byose by'intege nke kandi by'ubushwambagara bizabohwa kandi bijugunywemo iteka ryose n'iteka ryose.

Jyewe, Kristo Yesu, ari we kandi Yeshua HaMashiach nzamuye amaboko yanjye kandi ndahiye kuri Adonai-Yahweh wanjye, ko nafunguye mu maboko ya Biblaridion:144, ku itegeko rya Adonai-YHVH, ka Gatabo Gato kasezeranyijwe kera kavugwa mu Byahishuwe igice cya 10 nk'ubutumwa bwo mu ijuru bwambika intumwa nk'uko byavuzwe, hanyuma nanone mu Byahishuwe igice cya 14 nk'Ubutumwa Bwiza bw'Iteka ubu butanzwe igice, no mu Byahishuwe 14 aho iyo ntumwa imwe isoma kandi igashyira mu bikorwa urubanza rukomeye n'umujinya ku isi nk'uko byanditswe kandi bikavugwa, kuko nta Yod n'imwe, cyangwa ihembe rya Yod rizavaho mu byanditswe kugeza ibintu byose bisohoye neza nk'uko byanditswe. Ndetse ubwo buhamya butanzwe mu mbaraga nyinshi n'ubutware n'intebe y'ubwami ya Adonai-Yahweh binyuze mu majwi y'inkuba zirindwi, mu maso ha Yesu Kristo kandi bubibwe mu mwuka wa Biblaridion:144 (n'amaboko yanjye bwite), uri munsi y'itegeko ryanjye rwose mu kwifata kwose n'ubushizi bw'amanga kandi uhuje rwose kandi wumvikana n'Umwami we w'Iteka wo Gukiranuka. Ndetse uwo Mwami w'Iteka wo Gukiranuka uhora akora ubushake bwa Adonai-Yahweh, kandi uyobora, yigisha, ategeka, kandi arinda Biblaridion:144 mu rukundo rwanjye n'imbaraga zanjye kugira ngo ashobore kugenda neza, nk'uko byateganyijwe, kugira ngo ashobore gusohoza ibyo nabibye byose mu mwuka we ngo abisohoze muri ibi bihe byo gusohoza, ubu mu itangazo rikomeye, kandi cyane cyane mu ishyirwa mu bikorwa mu gihe cy'amakuba ya Yakobo!

Ndetse imigambi isohozwa ryayo ryatangiye ozakanya aya Magambo avugiwe mu isi bwa mbere na Biblaridion:144 ku itegeko ryanjye riturutse ku ntebe y'ubwami ya Adonai-YHVH.
