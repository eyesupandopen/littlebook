Ubuhamya Bubiri: Itorero
Mu Mirimo ya Petero
12 Mata 2024

Buri banga ryose rizajya ahagaragara!

Ntimwasomye se? Buri banga ryose rizajya ahagaragara. Ntimwasomye se? Buri kintu cyose gihishe kizashyirwa ku mugaragaro. Ntimwasomye se? Imigambi y'imitima yose izaza imbere yanjye. Buri rurimi rwose ruzanyatura, niko Uwiteka Ukiranuka n'Umwami Ukiranuka, utegeka mu gukiranuka avuga. Yemwe bantu banjye, kuki muhitamo ikosa rya Adamu na Eva, abagerageje guhisha isoni zabo mu mirimo yabo kuva mu ntangiriro, kuko badodeje amababi y'umutini bakikorera imyambaro. Nyamara, Imana yabonye ibyabaye kandi yari izi ko bazasitara mbere y'uko ibarema. Kandi se Data yabakoreye iki? YABAKOREYE imyambaro y'impu arabambika biturutse hanze y'imirimo yabo bwite.

Kuki mutekereza ko mushobora kumpisha ibyaha byanyu, bana banjye? Ndababona mwese kandi ndabazi neza kuruta uko mwiyizi. Nimureke kumvira abungeri bayobye bababwiriza undi Yesu; mu by'ukuri mu mariba atobetse baha amazi y'akazuyaze itorero ry'akazuyaze ritwikiriwe n'uburyarya ndetse n'ibyo bita gutekereza neza. Bababeshya n'ubukirisitu bwo kwifasha, bababwira ukuri kuvanywe mu murongo nk'uko Imana ibakunda uko muri. Nubwo urukundo rwa Data rudashira kandi rumeze nk'umuriro wera ku bana be, ubukirisitu nyakuri bubwiriza kwihana no kubabarirwa ibyaha, ibyaha byaturwe mu budakemwa n'umutima uboneye biganisha ku gakiza no kuba muzima. Nyamara, benshi mu Itorero ryanjye bashakira impamvu kandi bakisobanura mu byaha byabo. Bisobanura mu mitekerereze yabo bwite bagashaka urwego rw'ikinyoma, basuzugura bene wabo mu bwibone mu gihe batwikira gukiranirwa kwabo mu kwisobanura kwabo kw'ikinyoma. Ese ubu si bwo bumenyi bwa Satani igihe yahaga urubuto rubujijwe nyina w'abazima bose? Ese iyi si yo mirimo ya kamere?

Ndababaza ikibazo; mwese muzi igisubizo. Ibihembo by'icyaha ni ibiki? Ibyanditswe Byera bivuga ko ibyo bihembo ari ibiki? Ese si urupfu? Nyamara, benshi muri mwe mwumva ko ibyaha byanyu bifite ishingiro kandi ko ari n'igice cyanyu n'ikibagira umwihariko, mu gihe mukoresha nabi ubuntu bwanjye. Mu gupfa, muzapfa. Nubwo mufite ubugingo buhoraho muri njye, kandi nkazabababarira byose sinzabatabanya niba muguma muri njye, mu gupfa muzapfa.

Kuva ku mwuka wanyu wa mbere, murarimbuka kubera kamere y'icyaha ibarimo.

Iyo mukomeje gukora icyaha mugaburira kamere, muzasarura kubora buri gihe, kuko mu gupfa muzapfa. Icyaha kizatsemba buri gice cy'ubuzima bwanyu ndetse n'imibiri yanyu kugeza ku mwuka wanyu wa nyuma niba mukomeje. Si ibyo gusa, ahubwo muzatakaza ingororano yanyu muhagarare mufite isoni mu ijuru imbere y'intebe yanjye y'imanza mu gihe ibiti, ibyatsi, n'ibikenyeri mwibikiye bizashya bigahinduka ubusa imbere yanyu, kandi muzahura n'igihombo kuko nzabaka ingororano yanyu nkayiha undi, kuko ndi umucamanza ukiranuka. Kuki muza aho ndi mwihana hanyuma mugasubira ku birutsi byanyu? Mugeraho iki? Ntimwumva ko mudashobora kunkorera njye na Satani icyarimwe? Niba mukoze icyaha, muri abagaragu b'icyaha kugeza igihe mwejejwe imbere yanjye, kandi buri rurimi ruzatura! Kuki mwifuza gusubira ku bya mbere bidafite imbaraga kandi bikennye? Icyo bizakora cyose ni ugushaka kubica mu gihe bibatwara nk'iminyago.

Mumenye ko imyuka mibi yirata ku bana banjye ishobora gufata. Kuki muhitamo gusiga icyasha amazina yanyu mukigira urw'amenyo? Mwibagiwe urukundo rwanyu rwa mbere, kandi benshi muri mwe mwijanditse mu bupfumu bwa gikirisitu n'ubukirisitu bwo kwifasha, uburyo bwo kwizera butandimo, kuko sinzinjira mu itorero rya Lawodikaya ahubwo mpagaze ku rugi ndakomanga. Ndababwira nweruye, gutekereza neza kwanyu nta cyo gutegeka, kandi mwarayobejwe.

Ese ibinezeza by'icyaha by'igihe gito ni byo by'ingenzi kuri mwe? Ntimwumva ko nzabagororera bikomeye n'ibintu byo mu ijuru ku bw'amakuba n'ibigeragezo byanyu? Nzishyura ibyo mwigomwe ku bwanjye mu izina ryanjye. Nimugaruke ku rukundo rwanyu rwa mbere, bana banjye, muze aho ndi kandi musohoke muri Babuloni. Kuko iminsi ya maraya ukomeye ibaze, kandi nta muti uzamubaho; nuko rero, nimumusohokemo, bantu banjye, mwitandukanye kugira ngo mutazafatanya mu byago bye. Nimuve mu irari no mu mirimo ya kamere. Kuko ibiri mu isi byose, irari rya kamere, n'irari ry'amaso, n'ubwibone bw'ubuzima, bidaturuka kuri Data, ahubwo bituruka mu isi kandi isi irashira.

Muhunge ubusambanyi kandi ntimusangire isoni n'ingaruka zabyo. Bana banjye, ni nk'aho ari mwe mukora icyo gikorwa iyo mukura ibyishimo mu bugome n'ubwiyandarike. Mureke inzira zanyu z'umururumba, kuko nzahana abirundanyiriza ubutunzi, kandi vuba aha amafaranga yanyu azaba nta gaciro agifite. Mwinjire mu mwuka wanjye, kuko umwuka wanjye ari ubugingo n'amahoro. Nimuve mu bibi kandi ntimugakore icyaha! Musarure imbuto z'umwuka muri njye, kuko mbahaye imbaraga zo gutsinda ibyaha byanyu niba muza mwejejwe imbere yanjye. Mutekereza ko mushobora guhisha ikintu amaso yanjye? Nahawe imbaraga zo kubona, kandi njye na Data turi umwe mu bumwe n'intego. Ntibishoboka kwihisha, kandi ntibishoboka kumpisha ibyaha byanyu. Benshi muri mwe nta gitekerezo mufite cy'ukuntu mugaragara nabi iyo mwiyise abakiranutsi nk'aho nta cyaha mugira, nyamara amaso yanyu yaka irari ritwika amayasha yanyu.

Mwumve ko umujura akoresha ingeso mbi nyinshi n'amayeri abagirira nabi. Niba mukurikiza ubushake bwanjye bwo kurwanya no kumpindukirira, ndi umufasha uhora ahari mu gihe cy'amage kandi nzabatabara, nzabarinda, nzabeza kandi nce inzira yo gucika ibigeragezo byinshi muhura na byo, ariko mugomba kurwanya n'ibibariro byose. Nta cyo bivuze iyo muvuze ko musabye imbabazi mugahita musubira ku birutsi byanyu, ariko mwakwibaza mubonye imbwa ikora ibyo, nyamara mu bwibone bwanyu mutekereza ko mutandukanye, bityo ntimwitware nk'imbwa y'inkazi.

Icyaha ni icyaha. Uburyo bwonyine bwo gutsinda icyaha ni amaraso yanjye y'impongano no kuruhukira muri njye. Umwuka nabasutseho uzabaha imbaraga. Naremye kamere nshya muri mwe, kandi niba musabye gusa, nzarema umutima ukeye muri mwe.

Nuko rero mufunge amakanwa yanyu yanduye, mufunge amaso yanyu yuzuye irari mu isengesho, kandi muve mu gukiranirwa, nanjye nzabeza. Yego, nzabeza rwose mube bera nk'urubura rushya iyo mwaturiye kandi mugahinduka mukava mu nzira zanyu zanduye. Abo nkunda bose, ndabacyaha; kandi abo nkunda bose, ndabahana. Ni uwuhe mubyeyi udahana umwana we? Nubwo benshi bakora ibyo gusa, izi si zo nzira zanjye, kandi izi si zo nzira za Data. Kuko Imana ibafata nk'abana, izabahana nk'abana; kuko niba itabahana, ntimuri abana bayo ahubwo muri ibibyarwa.

Ariko muri abana bayo, nuko rero mutege amatwi amagambo yanjye kandi mwihanganire igihano, kandi mwumve imiburo, kuko ubuzima n'ibyishimo bibategereje. Nta gihano kigaragara nk'igishimishije mu gihe cyacyo, ariko kizazana imbuto z'amahoro zo gukiranuka. Muze aho ndi, nanjye nzabeza inzira zanyu za kera, kandi nzabigisha inzira zanjye kugira ngo mugendere mu gukiranuka n'amahoro. Mufashe bene wanyu kandi mube urugero rwo kwihana, kuko mu guhagarara kwanyu muzaca iminyururu myinshi n'ingoyi nyinshi, ndetse n'ingoyi mufashe mu maboko yanyu mukazikomeza. Ndabagira inama yo kuzireka zose. Icyaha kiganisha mu bubata, kandi gihemba urupfu; nuko rero, nimumusohokemo, kugira ngo mutazafatanya mu manza ze.

Ndi uwera, nuko rero mube abera.

Ndi utsindishiriza abana banjye.

Ndi weza abana banjye.

Ndi ugira abana banjye abakiranutsi muri njye.

Ndi ucungura abana banjye mu buntu bwanjye n'imbabazi zanjye.

Ndi uboza mukaba abera mu maraso yanjye atagira icyaha.

Ndi umucamanza ukiranuka.

Igihe ni kigufi, bana banjye, kandi ingendo zanyu ziri hafi kurangira. Ntimutekereze ko gukiranirwa kuzinjira mu bwami bwanjye, cyangwa ko muzahagarara imbere yanjye mu byaha byanyu mugatwara ingororano. Abanesha gusa ni bo bazahagararana nanjye. Muzakizwa, ariko kuki mwakwifuza guhura n'igihombo cy'ubwiza nabateguriye, kuko mudashobora no kumva ibyo mwigomwa ku bw'irari ryanyu bwite.

Muze aho ndi, kuko nkize ku mbabazi.

Muze aho ndi, kuko ubuntu bwanjye buzabaramira.

Muze aho ndi, kuko ndi ugendagenda hagati y'ibitereko by'amatabaza birindwi kandi uzarinda ingororano yanyu niba muje aho ndi mu kwicisha bugufi, mureka ubwibone bwanyu butagira umumaro. Ndabakunda, bana banjye, kandi vuba aha muzaba muri kumwe nanjye. Dore! Mpaze ku rugi ndakomanga mu gihe gito gisigaye. Ndababwira ibi bintu atari ukugira ngo mbacire urubanza, ahubwo kugira ngo mugire amavuta mu matabaza yanyu kandi mube mwiteguye igihe nzazira. Ndaza mu gihe mudatekereza, kandi ndaza vuba kuruta uko mubitekereza. Igihe cyenda gushira.
