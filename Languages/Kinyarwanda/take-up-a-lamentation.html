Ubuhamya Bubiri: Itorero
Mu Mirimo ya Yohana Umubatiza
13 Werurwe 2024

Fata Umuborogo

Amagambo Uwiteka yampaye, ndayatangaza ku mugaragaro: Nimwumve Ijambo ry'Uwiteka!

Nimufate umuborogo; koko, nimufatire umuborogo abagikomeje iyi si yaguye. Kuko bavuze mu mitima yabo bati, "Ni iki cyatugeraho? Nk'uko byahoze kera, bizakomeza iteka; kandi jye n'abanjye tuzakomeza kubaho mu byishimo, kandi tuziyegeranyiriza ibyiza by'iyi si n'ubutunzi, bizadukiza mu gihe cy'amage." Mwavuze mu mitima yanyu muti, "Turi abanyabwenge, kandi turakize; amakuba ntazatugeraho, nk'uko atajya atugeraho, kuko Imana yacu itazareka ishyanga ryacu rigwa; kuko turi abanyabwenge n'abanyambaraga." Nyamara ntimuri abanyabwenge, ahubwo muri abanyabwenge mu byiyumviro byanyu; kandi nahaye abanzi banyu intsinzi hejuru yanyu.

Yewe muntu, ni nde uzakurokora mu isaha yawe y'urubanza yegereye isi yose? Ese abayobozi bawe bazakurokora? Ndavuga nti oya, kuko umwana wo kurimbuka aza mu nzira yanyu kandi yamaze kugendagenda hagati yanyu ku mugaragaro, ayobya benshi n'amagambo meza n'imvugo nziza. Ese amafaranga yawe azakurokora? Oya, ubutunzi bwawe bwose buzahinduka ubusa mu kanya gato, kandi nta ngororano y'imirimo y'ubuzima bwawe wirundanyirije mu mururumba izabaho. Icyampa ntimube mwararetse inzira z'Uwiteka kandi mugakunda mugenzi wanyu mugafasha abakene mu burumbuke bwanyu.

Ariko oya, mwirundanyiriza uburumbuke Imana yabahaye kugira ngo muhaze ubugingo bwanyu. Koko, nahaye benshi muri mwe ibibahagije n'ibyo guha umugisha mugenzi wanyu; ariko oya, mwavuze muti, nzigumishiriza ubu burumbuke bwinshi kuko Imana yampaye umugisha kubera ineza yanjye n'imbaraga zanjye. Dore, mbabwiye ikintu kigitaraba, kugira ngo mu isaha yanyu y'amakuba muzibuke ijwi ryanjye n'amagambo yo mu kanwa kanjye nahumekeye mu bahanuzi banjye. Kuko ntabatumye kuvuga ibyabo, ahubwo mbatuma nk'umuburo kuri mwe, kugira ngo mukize ubugingo bwanyu mumpindukiriye, jyewe Umwami Yesu Kristo, ushobora kubakiza rwose. Ariko mwavuze mu mutima wanyu muti, Agakiza? Ntabwo nkeneye gukizwa, kuko ndi mwiza uko navutse. Nyamara ibyaha byanyu ni umunuko mu mazuru ya Data, kandi igihugu cyaraboze kubera gukiranirwa kwanyu. Ntimuzi yuko mwavutse mupfuye mu byaha n'ibicumuro, byonona kamere yanyu?

Nubwo nabaha kamere nshya, nziza, mufata igitambo cyanjye nk'ikidafite agaciro mugakomeza umubiri wanyu uboze.

Nimufatire umuborogo abantu b'ishyanga ryanyu ryahoze rikomeye banga ijwi ryanjye. Kuko agahinda kenshi n'umubabaro bizabazaho gitungumo. Hazabaho umutingito ukomeye, kandi ingabo zanjye zikomeye zo mu ijuru zizagusha kurimbuka gukomeye kuri maraya ukomeye, kandi ntazongera kubyuka ukundi, nubwo abasigaye bazakizwa. Ndetse no mu makuba yanyu no kurimbuka kwegereje nzaboherereza abahanuzi banjye. Muzabamenyera ku mbuto zabo, amagambo, n'ibitangaza byo gukiza; gukiza kwinshi n'ibitangaza nzabikora binyuze mu maboko yabo. Nyamara, na n'ubu benshi bazarimbuka bapfire mu byaha byabo, ariko abagwaneza bazaragwa isi; kuko umurage wanjye uri kumwe nanjye, witeguye kugabanywa abizerwa. Nzamara igihe kingana iki nkurira ukuboko kwanjye abantu bavuguruza bampagurukira n'inzira zanjye kandi mu bushukanyi bwabo bakiyita abakiranutsi imbere y'Imana mu myambaro yabo yanduye?

Mwihane! Murire kandi muborogere ibyaha byanyu, kandi muntakambire kugira ngo mbakize. Amatorero yanyu menshi yinjiwemo n'amasega aryana ababwiriza amagambo azabageza ku iherezo ryanyu, kandi n'ubundi ntimuzabareka ngo mumpindukirire mufite isura n'umutima bifunguye, kuko nabakiza amakuba yanyu nkarinda abana banyu ingabo z'umubi, zigendagenda mu mihanda yanyu ku mugaragaro kandi zikaba zaratangiye kubiba byinshi. Irari ryabo ntirizahaga kugeza babatwaye byose kandi bakamugaza benshi. Ariko abambaza izina ryanjye bazakizwa nibihangana kugeza ku iherezo.

Kuki munangira imitima yanyu ku Uwiteka Nyiringabo? Kuki mukibeshya imitima yanyu, muti, "Nta makuba ariho; uko byari ejo, ni ko biri uyu munsi, n'ejo hazazana ibisa bityo?" Mwa bakobanyi mwe muzasarura ingororano y'inzira zanyu zitagira kwizera, kandi abanyanga bazarimburwa rwose kandi bagire amakuba menshi. Nimwumve amagambo yanjye, yewe gisekuru cy'abasambanyi n'ababi. Nimwumve amagambo yanjye kandi mutege amatwi yanyu kugira ngo mwumve buri nyuguti nzavuga. Umutingito ukomeye uzatanya ishyanga ryanyu mwubaha cyane, kandi mu mutingito ukomeye hazavamo kurimbuka gukomeye. Nzarimbura imijyi yanyu yahoze ikomeye kandi nzazana n'amazi atwikire inkombe zanyu z'ibyaha; kuko mudakunda Uwiteka ahubwo mukunda ibinezeza kuruta uko mukunda Imana.

Nta byishimo mfite mu rubanza rwanyu, kuko ubuntu bwanjye n'imbabazi zanjye byabagwiririye igihe kirekire ubu. Benshi mu banduje isezerano rya Nowa nabo ubwabo bazatwikirwa n'amazi kubera guhumanya umuheto wera w'amabara menshi Data yatanze nk'ingwate. Umuriro uzabagwaho uturutse mu kuboko kw'abanzi banyu, kandi benshi bazarimbukira mu birimi by'umuriro n'uburozi buzakurikiraho. Igihugu cyanyu kizasatuka, kandi amakuba akomeye azaba ifunguro ryanyu rya nimugoroba, kuko mwanze Umwami w'abami n'Umutware w'abatware.

Umubi yamaze kuzana Ingabo ze Zitukura mu mijyi yanyu, mu makarere, no mu byaro. Bagendagenda hagati yanyu, bacura imigambi yo kubarimbura; kandi bategereje amabwiriza yabo no kugenda k'umukumirizi wanjye, uwo nzarekura vuba. Bazakubura kandi barimbure banigarurire ibizaba bisigaye. S'ibyo gusa, ahubwo bazabyiyitirira, kandi bazatwara benshi muri mwe mu nkambi zabo bamaze gutegura. Benshi bazacibwa imitwe ku bw'izina ryanjye, kandi nzabagororera cyane nibaguma ari abizerwa kugeza ku iherezo. Nta rumuri ruzabaho; hazabaho ibiraro byaguye n'imihanda yacuritse. Ni nde uzabaha ibyo kurya n'amazi igihe ishyanga ryanyu rizaba ryarimbutse ritazongera kubyuka ukundi? Ariko nzakiza abasigaye, kandi abasigaye nzabakiza, kuko mbivuze!

Ntimutinye, bantu banjye n'abizerwa banjye. Nzabakura hagati mu kanya nk'ako guhumbya mbajyane mu nzu ya Data, aho nabateguriye umwanya n'ameza kugira ngo dusangire, bana banjye. Ndakumbuye ukuhaba kwanyu, kandi vuba muzabona isura yanjye n'amaso yanyu, kandi muzabona ingororano yo kunyizera kwanyu kuko mufite kwizera mu mutima wanyu kandi mutarabonye n'amaso yanyu ubwiza bwanjye, cyangwa ubwiza bwa Data, unduta. Kandi nabahamagaye mbatoranya nk'abanjye, kandi ntimuzigera mundeka, kuko muzahorana n'Umwami wanyu n'umucunguzi wanyu. Mumenye ko nzabagororera cyane nkabasukaho imigisha mudashobora gusobanukirwa muri iki gihe. Mukomeze kuba abizerwa kugeza ku iherezo, kuko igihe gito ibi bintu bizasohora; koko, isaha irakuze, kandi ijoro ryamaze kugwa ku isi.

Ndi Umwami w'abami n'Umutware w'abatware, kandi nahawe izina riruta amazina yose, kugira ngo mu izina rya Yesu Kristo amavi yose yo mu ijuru no mu isi apfukame.

Ndi Umuganwa w'Amahoro, kandi hanze yanjye nta mahoro ariho.

Ndi uwabahaye kuruhuka, kugira ngo muruhukire muri njye kandi mubone ubuhungiro mu buntu bwanjye.

Ndi ugendagenda hagati y'ibitereko by'amatara birindwi, utegeka abamarayika ba Data, kandi ibintu byose mu ijuru no mu isi biri mu maboko yanjye, nk'uko Data yabimpaye mu ntsinzi yanjye ku mubi kugeza igihe abanzi banjye bazagirwa intebe y'ibirenge byanjye.

Ndi Umwami wanyu n'umucunguzi wanyu, kandi ndi umwungeri mwiza wambitswe ikamba ry'ubwiza bwose, kandi mfashe ubugingo buhoraho mu maboko yanjye ku bazicisha bugufi bakansaba.

Nimumbwire ko ndi Umwami! Ntimutinye, bana banjye bato, kuko ari ibihe by'imperuka kandi umukumbi munini uzakizwa mu makuba akomeye agiye kuza. Umuntu wese wambaza izina rya Yesu Kristo azakizwa, kandi nzabakiza mbagororere ku bw'ubudahemuka bwabo. Koko, ngendagenda hagati yanyu, kandi ni ikihe kintu gikomeye Uwiteka yateguye atabwiye abahanuzi be mbere yuko kiba? Ndababwira nweruye, nimwumve ijwi ryanjye, kuko hasigaye igihe gito cyane mbere y'ijwi ry'impanda ya nyuma. Ndetse n'abamarayika bahagaze biteguye mu ijuru hejuru, bategereje kuvuga kw'impanda! Gucungurwa kwanyu kwuzuye kuri ku rugugi rwanyu, nuko rero ntimutinye kandi munyihambireho, kuko ubugingo bwanyu buhishe muri njye kandi bugiye guhishurwa.
