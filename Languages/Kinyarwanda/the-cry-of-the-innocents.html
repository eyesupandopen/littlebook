23 Nyakanga 2024

Amarira y'Inzirakarengane!

Mufate indirimbo y'agahinda, murire kandi maboroge ku bw'iyicwa ry'inzirakarengane. Kuko nta cyaha bakoze, nta buzima babayeho, cyangwa ngo babe bakoze icyaha na kimwe, kuko kugenda kwabo imburagihe kwabaye bakiri mu mutekano w'inda za ba nyina, kandi mbere y'uko babona amahirwe yo guhumeka bwa mbere ntibakiriho!

Mufate indirimbo y'agahinda, murire kandi maboroge ku bw'ubuzima bw'inzirakarengane bwatakaye n'amaraso yabo yamenwe atakambira ijuru asaba ubutabera. Dore! Isi irajegajega hirya no hino kandi iratigita uko ubukana bwiyongera, kuko uburemere bw'iyicwa ry'inzirakarengane ari bunini!

Mufate indirimbo y'agahinda ndetse no ku bana ba Rasheli kuko uku ni ko Uwiteka avuga; Ijwi ryumvikaniye i Rama, amaganya n'arira asharira: Rasheli, aririra abana be, yanze guhumurizwa ku bw'abana be, kuko batakiriho. Nubwo mutekereza ibi, nk'ikintu cyoroheje, mumenye ko ibi byaha bitagomba kwirengagizwa kandi uburemere bw'igihano cyabyo ni bunini cyane kandi urubanza ruri ku bakoze ibyo byaha bose bakanga amahoro ntanze!

Nimwumve amagambo yanjye, bwoko bwanjye, kandi mutege imitima yanyu amagambo yanjye mwumve ubwenge bwanjye kuko urubanza atari urwanyu. Mumenye agahinda kenshi n'amarira asharira by'Umwami n'Umukiza wanyu, ndetse Yesu w'i Nazareti! Kuko amarira menshi, menshi, nakomeje kuyarira ku bw'iyicwa ry'inzirakarengane. Nimusohoke mu rwagati rwayo bwoko bwanjye kandi mundeke nsenye burundu imirimo ya Satani mu buzima bwanyu muri ibi, ndetse no muri buri kibazo bana banjye. Kuko umwuka wanjye utakiza kubemeza ibyaha, kuko mwahinduwe gukiranuka kw'Imana muri njye kuko mwemeye impano yanjye y'ubugingo buhoraho n'impano yanjye yo gukiranuka, ibyo mwaheshejweho gukiranuka, ndetse gukiranuka kw'Imana muri njye ku bw'amaraso yanjye y'impongano n'umwuka w'Imana Ihoraho, ndetse umwuka wo kufatwa nk'abana ari na wo mwakashijweho kugeza ku munsi wo gucungurwa, kandi muri icyaremwe gishya muri njye. Nuko rero ntimutinye!

Mwaremewe mu gukiranuka no mu kwera nyakuri kandi iyo umwuka wanjye ubari imbere ubemeje, ni njye Kristo Yesu ubemeza gukiranuka. Yego, ndabemeza gukiranuka kugira ngo mubashe kubaho mukiranuka kuko muri gukiranuka kw'Imana kandi mugomba gukomeza kwica ibyifuzo bya kamere, no kugendera mu mwuka wanjye kuko noneho nta teka rizacirwa abari muri Kristo Yesu batagendera mu bya kamere ahubwo bagendera mu by'umwuka! Nuko rero mumenye ko urubanza atari urwanyu, kuko buri bugingo Yahweh yababaririye muri njye Kristo Yesu butazacirwa urubanza rw'ibyaha ahubwo buzava mu rupfu bujye mu bugingo buhoraho kuko mwemeye Yesu w'i Nazareti, ndetse amasezerano y'amahoro ya Yahweh ku bantu bose n'iteka ryose ku bemeye igitambo cyanjye bose!

Nimuze aho ndi mwature ndetse n'ibyaha mwibagijwe nkana kandi mukangukire gukiranuka ntimukore icyaha. Nimuze aho ndi mwature amakosa yanyu, kugira ngo mboze ibizinga byose mu maraso yanjye y'impongano, kandi kugira ngo imirimo ya Satani ihanagurwe burundu kandi iteka ryose mu buzima bwanyu. Kuki mukomeza kugundira ibyaha byanyu mwinangiye? Kuki mushaka gusinzira ngo muruhuke? Ese mushobora kwihisha Imana Ihoraho iyo mufunze amaso yanyu? Ese mwizirika ku mugani wa Nyahembe mugashaka kwigana uwo mugani? Sinabahamagariye kubaho mu mugani wa Nyahembe cyangwa guhisha imitwe yanyu mu mucanga cyangwa gutwikira ibyaha byanyu mu binezeza no kwibagirwa. Kuko Nyahembe ishyira umutwe wayo mu butaka kugira ngo yite ku magi y'abana bayo bataravuka, atari ukwihisha ukuri. Ariko nabahamagariye guhangana n'ibyaha byo mu gihe cyahise cyanyu no kubinyatura kugira ngo mbakize kandi nsenye imirimo ya Satani mu buzima bwanyu kuko ashaka kubabohera mu byaha bye no kubashinja ubupfu iteka ryose!

Nuko rero, mukomeze kwatura ibyaha byanyu ku mugaragaro n'amakosa yanyu hagati yanyu kandi mumenye ko uko mubikora abanzi banyu bahinda umushyitsi kuko babona ibimenyetso bidashidikanywaho by'agakiza kanyu gakomeza n'ibimenyetso bidashidikanywaho by'irimbuka ryabo ryegereje n'urubanza rw'iteka mu nyanja nini aho ibirimi by'umuriro w'ubururu byera cyane bizaka iteka ryose n'inyo yabo ntigwe! Nuko rero nimubasohokemo kandi mwitandukanye nabo kandi mumenye ko mufite kubabarirwa ibyaha. Uko mukomeza mu mibanire yanyu inzanira agakiza nanjye, Kristo Yesu, nzabeza rwose ibyaha byanyu byose kugira ngo mbigire itorero ryiza cyane ritagira ikizinga cyangwa umunkanyari cyangwa ikindi nk'icyo, ahubwo kugira ngo mube abera kandi mutagira umugayo imbere yanjye mu rukundo. Kandi kugira ngo mwambikwe imyenda y'igitare cyiza kuko uko ari ko hazaza hanyu hafi. Nuko rero mumenye ko muri njye, Kristo Yesu, nta teka rizacirwa cyangwa urubanza kuri mwe bana banjye kandi amagambo yanjye akomeye si ayanyu ahubwo ndabagira inama yo kunyigiraho no kumva uburemere bw'ibyo mbakijije kuko mwacunguwe mu rubanza.

Kandi mumenye ko buri gikorwa cyanditswe mu ijuru kuri buri cyaremwe munsi y'ijuru kigengwa n'urubanza rukuru. Kandi nabonye n'andika amagambo n'imirimo by'abahanuzi b'ibinyoma, ndetse n'abiyita ko bamvugira kandi bakaba barashyigikiye iyicwa ry'inzirakarengane ku bw'inzira zabo zangiza. Kuko aba ari bo mariba atagira amazi, ndetse n'abo busambanyi bwabo bwatumye habaho iyicwa ry'inzirakarengane. Abo, aho kwihana ubusambanyi bwabo n'ubusihanyi bwabo ahubwo bishe abana babo bataravuka kugira ngo bakomeze kugaburirwa n'umukumbi wanjye aho kugaburira umukumbi wanjye, kandi bazahura n'umujinya ukomeye ku bw'ibyaha byabo kuko bidashoboka ko amasega aryana yihisha kuri ruhimbi mu maso yanjye areba iteka, kandi bazikorera urubanza rwabo bose batazihana ibyo bikorwa!

Nuko rero ndakubwira wowe muntu wa kamere, kandi ndakubwira wowe Babuloni y'Ibanga. Kuko uriho urubanza rw'amaraso y'inzirakarengane, ndetse n'abo wakuye mu mutekano w'inda za ba nyina. Wazingiye gukubita urusoro mu kwisobanura kw'ibinyoma n'impamvu zituma igishuko cyawe cyo kwikunda cyo kwica inzirakarengane kiba ikintu gisanzwe nk'ugusura muganga, kandi amavuriro yawe y'ubwicanyi ni umunuko mu mazuru ya Data, n'umunuko mu mazuru yanjye. Kuko amaraso y'inzirakarengane atakambira ijuru aturuka mu isi kugera ku ntebe y'ubwami ya Yahweh mu ijuru ry'amajuru ureba byose, ndetse namwe mwese mushaka kwihisha amaso ye ahoraho. Kandi njye Yeshua HaMashiach noherejwe na Data mu rubanza, kandi ndi Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda, kuko mwanze Umwana w'Intama w'Imana kandi noneho haje urubanza rwanyu. Kandi nzeza rwose igihugu mu nzira zanyu z'ubwicanyi kandi muzarya umwanda w'inzira zanyu mbi, kuko Yahweh yatangaje umugabane wikubye kabiri w'urubanza kuri wowe, yewe Babuloni y'Ibanga.

Kuko mwishe inzirakarengane, mutekereza mu bujiji ko iki gikorwa kibi cyane ari ikintu cyoroheje. Mwagurishije abana banyu bataravuka ku bw'indonke mbi kugira ngo muteze imbere imirimo yanyu, ndetse n'imirimo yanyu mu busa bwose kuko yose izahinduka ubusa n'imihati yanyu y'isi muha agaciro kenshi y'ubusa yose izashira mu rubanza ntizongere kwibukwa. Kuko mwishe mubishaka abana banyu bataravuka mu gitambo mutazi cya Moloki, kandi mwica abataravuka banyu bwite mufite ishema ryinshi. Kandi uko ni ko bizaba, kuko ishema ribanziriza kugwa n'umutima wibona ubanziriza kurimbuka kandi urubanza rwanyu n'irimbuka ntibitinda ahubwo biri kuri mwe kandi byatangiye n'ibise ntibigihagarara ubu.

Ariko aho kwihana, aho kuza aho ndi, Yesu w'i Nazareti mu kwicisha bugufi ubwo nabahaga ubugingo buhoraho no kubabarirwa ibyaha n'ingaruka zikomoka ku rubanza ruhuye nabyo mwahisemo ibyaha byanyu n'igihano cyanyu cy'iteka. Mwiyemeje kandi muhitamo aho guhitamo njye Yesu w'i Nazareti, Baraba, Moshiach wanyu w'ikinyoma. Aho guhitamo Yahweh Imana ikomeye ihoraho, mwahisemo ikiyoka nk'imana yanyu kandi ubu ngiye kubabaha kuko mwifatanyije n'inzira zabo mu kwibona kwanyu n'ishema ryanyu kandi ubu muzarya umwanda w'inzira zanyu bwite.

Mwatambye amaraso y'inzirakarengane ku ziko ry'ibitambo ryo kwinezeza, kandi mwajugunye inusoro zanyu zakubiswe nk'aho ari ibitambaro byanduye bijugunywa mu myanda yo kwa muganga, kandi mwabaze umwuka w'ubugingo uri mu maraso nk'ikintu kidakwiriye kandi urubanza rwanyu ruri kuri mwe. Ariko abanyu bwite, ndetse n'abana banyu bataravuka, ntibari bagezweho n'imyambarire yanyu ihora isaza kandi ubuzima bwabo bwari inkomyi kuri mwe yo kwibiza rwose mu binezeza by'ibyaha.

Mwisobanura kandi mugasaba amaraso y'inzirakarengane mu bitekerezo byanyu bya politiki by'ubwicanyi, kandi mubizi mwifatanyije n'abaguye mu kwigomeka kwabo, kuko ari ba Satani bigishije gukubita urusoro mu nda, ndetse n'uburyo bwanyu bw'ubwicanyi bw'agaciro muterwa ishema no kurinda no kwirata. Nuko rero amaraso y'inzirakarengane atakambira ijuru aturuka mu isi kandi uburemere bw'ibyaha byanyu bwatumye isi idandabirana nk'umuntu wasinze, kandi gutigita kuzaba gukomeye n'ikigusha kizaba gikomeye! Babuloni Ikomeye iraguye, iraguye!

Yewe muntu wa kamere, Yewe Babuloni y'Ibanga, uburyo waguye uva mu buntu n'imbabazi naguhaye utabikwiriye. Kandi nubwo ubuntu bwanjye n'imbabazi atari imfabusa, kuko imigambi ya Yahweh, ndetse n'imigambi ye ihishe mu ibanga rikomeye ry'Imana yashohojwe mu mahanga y'abanyamahanga n'ibihe byabo byarangiye. Kandi ndi we ugiye guhindukira agakiza Yakobo kurimbuka, kuko bazampindukirira nka Mesiya wabo, ndetse n'abo Yahweh ubwe yahamagaye bose kuva mu bihe bya kera by'iteka kugira ngo bagendere imbere ye nk'umuntu umwe mushya muri njye Kristo Yesu, n'ishyanga rimwe rishya kandi ntibizongera kubaho ko Isirayeli y'Imana igabanywa kandi ntihazabaho inyanja ukundi. Ariko mbere ibihugu bigomba kwezwa n'ubwami bwa Satani buzarimburwa burundu, ntibuzongera kubyuka ukundi. Yewe muntu wa kamere w'umupfu, mubi kandi wangiritse, ndetse nawe udafite umwuka wanjye n'uwanze ubugingo buhoraho muri njye Kristo Yesu.

Mumenye ko abana ari umurage w'Uwiteka, kandi byaba byiza ku muntu kutavuka aho kugirira nabi inzirakarengane mbere y'umwuka wabo wa mbere; kugirira nabi n'umwe mu bato banjye ni ishyano kandi bizana igihano cy'iteka. Ariko nubwo bimeze bityo murakomeza. Nubwo nahumekeye benshi guhagarara hanze y'amavuriro yanyu kugira ngo bagerageze kubahagarika kwishyiriraho iherezo ryanyu. Ariko ntimwabishatse, kandi mwanze imiburo yanjye kandi ubu muzababazwa n'urubanza rwanyu.

Mwayobye mujya mu nzira za Baali kandi mwabaye nk'uko Satani yabayoboye, kuko mwarenze ibyiyumvo byose mu nzira zanyu z'ubusa. Kora icyo ushaka ni ryo tegeko ryabo ryose mwagize iryanyu bwite. Mwayobejwe mujya mu nzira z'Abanyakanani, kuko nabo batambaga abana babo, bafite ishema ryinshi n'ibyishimo, nk'uko mukomeza kubikora! Ariko mu kwibona kwanyu mutekereza ko ntaho muhuriye n'abo ba kera b'abapfu n'abatarize kandi inzira zanyu zisumba iz'abo banyamusozi ba kera, nyamara mukora ibyo bakoraga. Ariko mutekereza mu bujiji ko mutandukanye, kandi mushaka gukoresha ubujiji bushaka nk'umwitero, kandi mushaka kwisobanura no guhisha ibyaha byanyu mu burezi bwanyu bw'ibinyoma bwabayoboye mu rubanza. Kuko uburemere bw'amaraso y'inzirakarengane butakambira ijuru na n'ubu kandi busaba ubutabera, kandi Yahweh yumvise gutaka kwabo!

Mwatanze abataravuka banyu mutabizi kugira ngo muteze imbere gahunda y'inyamaswa, kandi abaguye bemeye ibitambo byanyu. Bakoresheje amaraso y'inzirakarengane nk'uko bahora babikora mu gukora ibizira byabo, kandi nk'uko byari bimeze mu minsi ya Nowa ni ko bizaba. Kuko n'uwabuzaga akuweho, nibwo muzumva ubunini n'umubare w'ibizira byabo, kuko bizarekurwa rwose nta nkomyi.

Mwatambye amaraso y'inzirakarengane kandi ibizira bakoze bizabahiga kandi bibakurikire kugeza ku mpera z'isi kuko bahora barya abaturage b'igihugu n'urumamfu rubi rwakozwe n'amaboko y'abaguye ntirubabarirwa ibyaha byarwo n'ibyaha byakorewe Yahweh, kuko umuntu yaremwe mu ishusho ya Yahweh kandi guhumanya ubuzima bw'umubiri buri mu maraso ni icyaha gikomeye kandi ntikizirengagizwa ku banze agakiza kanjye mu bihe byanjye by'ubuntu n'imbabazi. Dore! Nohereje abanyamaboko kugira ngo basohoze umujinya wanjye!

Ubu ngiye kuvugisha abaguye, kandi barumva kandi bahinda umushyitsi! Inzira zanyu zateye irimbuka ryanyu ry'iteka kandi rihoraho kuko iryanyu ari irimbuka rihoraho aho muzasubizwa buri cyaha inshuro nyinshi. Buri buzima mwatwaye mu nzira zanyu mbi z'umururumba n'irari muzabwishyurwa inshuro ijana. Kuko mwakoresheje amaraso y'inzirakarengane mu mihango yanyu, n'ibikorwa, n'imigenzo kandi mwakoze ibizira byinshi ubwo mwigiraga imana! Ariko ntimuri imana kandi ntimushobora kwikura mu kuboko kwanjye, kuko njye Kristo Yesu ndi urubyaro rwasezeranyijwe rw'umugore ruzamenagura umutwe wanyu rwose!

Ubu haje urubanza rwanyu n'irimbuka ryegereje. Kuko mutamennye amaraso y'inzirakarengane gusa, ndetse nanjye Yesu w'i Nazareti, ahubwo muriho urubanza rw'amaraso y'inzirakarengane ndetse kuva ku wa mbere kugeza ku wa nyuma kuko ari mwe mwigishije gukubita urusoro kugira ngo rupfe. Inzira zanyu zizira n'ibikorwa byanyu muzabyishyurwa byose iteka ryose kandi ntimuzigera mucika cyangwa ngo mubone akaruhuko k'akanya gato mu gihano cyanyu cy'iteka. Kuko ari mwe nyabayazana w'urupfu n'irimbuka ry'inzirakarengane n'abakurikije inzira zanyu z'irimbuka bose mu rwango rutihana bazasangira umurage wanyu w'umuriro w'iteka uko mwishyura buri gikorwa cyose giteye ishozi mwakoreye irema rya Yahweh. Kuko ibyo mwakoreye abandi bizashyirwa mu bibirira byanyu inshuro nyinshi ndetse n'iteka ry'iteka ryose kandi ntimuzacika.

Kuko mwagerageje kunyiba umurage wanjye, keretse ko mwatsinzwe kuko mwagerageje ibidashoboka: Mwagerageje kwica amaraso y'inzirakarengane, ndetse nanjye Yesu w'i Nazareti, kuko bidashoboka ko urupfu rumfata. Kuko natanze ubuzima bwanjye mbishaka kandi nta wubuntwaye, ahubwo nashyizeho ubuzima bwanjye mbishaka nka Pasika itunganye y'ibihe byose. Kandi njye, ndetse Kristo Yesu, nzasenya imirimo yanyu yose mibi mu buzima bw'abo mwahohoteye, ndetse n'abaje aho ndi bihana inzira zanyu mbi n'abazambaza izina ryanjye kugeza ku iherezo, kuko umati MUNINI uzarokoka. Ndetse na buri bugingo Yahweh yageneye ubugingo buhoraho kuva ku mishingo y'isi kandi ntimuzashikuzamo n'umwe mu bato muri aba mu maboko yanjye y'iteka, yewe kiyoka cyatsinzwe. Kuko inzirakarengane mwishe zizazukira mu bushya bw'ubugingo buhoraho n'ibyishimo by'iteka mu maso ya Yahweh kandi bazabaho iteka mu byishimo bihoraho kandi bazishimira umubyibuho w'igihugu! Kuko Yahweh yasezeranyije iki?

Dore! Uku ni ko Uwiteka avuga, Ijwi ryumvikaniye hejuru, umuborogo n'arira asharira. Rasheli aririra abana be, yanze guhumurizwa ku bw'abana be, kuko batakiriho. Uku ni ko Uwiteka avuga, reka ijwi ryawe kurira, n'amaso yawe amarira: kuko umurimo wawe uzagororerwa, ni ko Uwiteka avuga, kandi bazagaruka bava mu gihugu cy'umwanzi: Kandi hariho ibyiringiro mu iherezo ryawe, ni ko Uwiteka avuga, ko abana bawe bazagaruka mu mipaka yabo bwite.

Dore! Jyewe, ndetse Yeshua HaMashiach nzazura inzirakarengane zose zazimiye kandi nzabihoremera amaraso yabo ku banyabyaha! Nzabazurira ubugingo buhoraho n'ubuzima bw'abataravuka buzashibuka mu bugingo buhoraho n'ibyishimo by'iteka kandi nzabahuza n'abacunguwe, ndetse n'abo nagiriye imbabazi baje aho ndi bihana kandi nzabasubiza ibyo mwatwaye, mwa baguye mwe. Kandi ibyabo bizaba ibyishimo bihoraho kandi nzasubiza inzirakarengane zishwe mu miryango yazo kandi Yahweh ubwe azaba Se kandi bazafata imyanya yabo mu miryango ihoraho kandi bazahorerwa ndetse no ku mitwe yanyu y'imfabusa!

Dore! Bazagirwa abanyambaraga, n'abanyacyubahiro, kandi inzirakarengane zifite umwanya wihariye mu mutima wa Yahweh n'imigambi ye izasohorezwa mu buzima bwabo bw'iteka kandi iteka bazabagirana imbere ya Se n'Imana yabo kandi bazahabwa icyubahiro iteka muri njye Kristo Yesu uzabazura agasubizaho rwose ubuzima bwabo bwibwe mbere y'uko babona n'amahirwe yo guhumeka.

Nuko rero humura Rasheli, kuko abana bawe bazasubizwaho kandi uruhukire munsi y'ukuboko kwanjye gukomeye guhumuriza umenya ko abato bawe bazabaho iteka ryose kandi ntibazigera barimbuka, kuko njye Yeshua HaMashiach nabitangaje kandi nta gishobora guhindura itangazo ryanjye. Kandi Yahweh Imana ikomeye ihoraho arahaguruka mu burakari n'urubanza kandi urubanza rugiye kugwa!

Kuki mushaka gusubira gusinzira mwa bahamagawe n'abatoranyijwe banjye? Ibyo nasezeranyije nzabisohoza kuko nziye mu mbaraga n'icyubahiro bya Yahweh Imana ikomeye ihoraho kandi ndi umwe mu bumwe n'umugambi na Data, ndetse El Shaddai Imana ikomeye ihoraho kuko nta wuhwanye na we! Mumenye ko kugaragara kwanjye kwegereje kandi ubu kuri ku rugugi rwanyu! Igihe cyarangiye kuri Babuloni y'Ibanga ngo yihane kandi urubanza rwayo rwatangiye. Mu buryo butunguranye ubwo uwabuzaga azaba akuweho n'abantu banjye bakazurwa bagahindurwa ngo bansanganire mu kirere, irimbuka ritunguranye rizagwa ku batubaha Imana kandi ntibazacika n'amaraso y'inzirakarengane y'ibihe byose azahorerwa rwose iteka ryose ku munsi w'urubanza rukuru, nuko rero ubu urubanza rukuru ruzagwa. Muhumurizanye n'aya Magambo, kuko mwakijijwe umujinya mwa Torero ryanjye, ndetse Mugeni wanjye!

Jyewe Yeshua HaMashiach nohereje aya magambo binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye, kandi binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye aya magambo yoherejwe!

Ndigisha abatihana bose kwemera agakiza ku bw'ubuntu mbere y'uko urugi rw'Inkuge rufungwa. Ntimufite igihe, nuko rero mungire Umwami ubu mumenya ko Yahweh yanzuye mu bapfuye!

Kandi abasigaye bose mu muriro w'utunganya ndabagira inama yo kwambaza izina ryanjye kugeza ku iherezo kandi ntimwemere ikimenyetso cyabo, kandi muzakizwa mu byishimo bihoraho!
