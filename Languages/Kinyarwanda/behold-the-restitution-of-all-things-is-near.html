Ubuhamya Bubiri: Isirayeli
Ibisharira N'ibiryohereye
9 Nyakanga 2024

Dore! Kugarura Ibintu Byose Biri Hafi!

Kuva kuri Yeshua Hamashiach:

Ibihe byo kugarura byegereje isi, ariko mbere na mbere ibihe n'irangira ry'urubanza rukomeye bigomba gusohora. Kandi njye, Kristo Yesu, nzaca urubanza rw'isi kuko isi ari iyanjye n'ibiyuzuye byose, kandi niguze isi amaraso yanjye y'impongano i Kaluvari. Mu kuri kudahinduka, natanze buri gitonyanga cy'amaraso yanjye y'umwere yamenewe ibyaha by'abantu. Ku bemeye amahoro yanjye n'ubugingo buhoraho hazabaho amahoro, n'ubugingo buhoraho, kandi inzira zo gukiranuka zizatsinda. Ku banze icyifuzo cyanjye bahisemo inzira z'urupfu, urubanza, n'igihano cy'iteka, kuko isi ishira kandi nta yandi mahitamo ahari. Kandi bityo bazikorera urubanza rwabo kandi buri cyaha, ijambo ribi, igitekerezo kibi, n'igikorwa kibi bizashyirwa ahagaragara kugira ngo babibazwe. Ariko abanga agakiza kanjye ntibazigera babasha guhagarara, kandi ku bw'amahitamo yabo bahisemo inyanja y'umuriro yateguriwe umwanzi n'abamarayika be kandi ikintu nk'icyo ntabwo ari ngombwa, ariko buri wese afite uburenganzira bwo guhitamo. Ndi uje guca urubanza no kurwana intambara, kandi inzira za Yehova zizategeka isi mu gukiranuka mu bwami bwanjye buza. Kuri mwe Torero ryanjye, ndetse n'Umugeni wanjye w'agaciro muzatwarwa mu ijuru, kandi muzahabwa umurage wanyu, kandi muzajya mu myanya yanyu mu byiciro nabateguriye, ndetse n'umwanya wanyu muri Yakobo kuko muri Isirayeli y'Imana.

Kuko muri njye, Kristo Yesu, nta Muyahudi cyangwa Umunyamahanga ahubwo hariho icyaremwe gishya, kandi icyo cyaremwe kizagendera hamwe nk'umuntu umwe mushya. Dore! Muzaba umuntu mushya, kandi muzaba icyaremwe gishya, mbese sinabahaye ingwate y'umwuka wera? Ndetse n'ikimenyetso cy'Imana ihoraho, kidashobora kumenwa? Ni nde muri mwe ushobora guhindura ivuka ry'abana banyu b'umubiri? Ntimushobora kuko imiterere yanyu ihererekanywa uko ibisekuruza bisimburana kandi abana ni abahoraho. Kandi niko bimeze, imbuto ya Yehova yaremwe mu bana b'Imana ntishobora kwangirika, kuko bidashoboka ko mwakurwa mu maboko yanjye. Mbese ntimwasomye? Kuko mwabyawe ubwa kabiri, atari ku mbuto yangirika, ahubwo ku itangirika, binyuze mu ijambo ry'Imana rizima, kandi rihoraho iteka. Bana banjye, mbese amagambo yanjye aratangaje cyane? Mbese mwemereye amajwi y'ibinyoma kubayobya ngo muve mu ntekerezo imwe muri Kristo? Nta kintu na kimwe gishobora gukuraho imbuto ya Yehova iyo imaze kuremwa imbere, kuko impano no guhamagara kw'Imana bitagira ukwicuza. None se kuki mushidikanya ku gakiza kanyu? Nubwo umubiri wanyu uzatuma mukora ibyaha, kandi nkabababarira nkabasukura uko mwatura ibyaha byanyu mwihana, nta kintu na kimwe gishobora gukura abana b'Imana mu muryango w'Imana. Ku babaho mu gukiranirwa imirimo yabo izashya, kandi bazahagarara bambaye ubusa kandi bafite isoni, naho abafite imirimo ihagarara nk'iyokiranuka bazahabwa ingororano ikomeye, ariko abana b'Imana bahabwa agakiza gahoraho. Umuntu wese utagira umwuka wa Kristo, ntabwo ari uwe.

Ku babaho mu buzima bw'ubuhakanyi n'abiyita ko babereyeho Kristo mu izina gusa bazagwa uko Itorero ry'ukuri rizaba rigenda. Kuko ubiba mu mubiri azasarura kubora mu mubiri. Kandi abagerageje kungira Umwami bafite umutima utizera bagakomeza gukora ibyaha bazasigirwa umubabaro wa Yakobo kugira ngo umubiri urimbuke kandi bazakenera kwambaza izina ry'Umwami kugeza ku iherezo, kandi niko bizaba. Abiyobye kugeza ku mwuka wabo wa nyuma ntibapfire muri Kristo kandi ntibigere bagirana umubano w'agakiza nanjye bazahura n'urubanza rwabo. Mbese mutekereza ko Yehova ugenzura imitima ari umupfapfa? Mbese mutekereza ko njye, Kristo Yesu, ntageregeza imitima y'abantu ngo ndebe ukwizera no kunyizera? Ariko niko bizaba, kandi nzabwira benshi banyita Umwami nti: Mumbemo, mwa nkozi z'ibibi mwe, sinigeze mbamenya.

Ariko kuri mwe Torero ryanjye, abahamagawe banjye n'abizerwa n'abatoranyijwe: Ndetse na buri bugingo bwageze ku gakiza nabwo bugirwa umuragwa w'amasezerano yahawe ba Data na Yehova. Kuko byanditswe ko Abanyamahanga bagomba kuba abaraganwa, n'ab'umubiri umwe, n'abasangiye isezerano rye muri Kristo binyuze mu butumwa bwiza, kandi nabateye ku Giti cy'Umunzenze cy'umwimerere. Kandi abaremwe bushya muri njye bose bazagarurwa byuzuye mu butungane no gukiranuka n'ubwera bw'ukuri. Mbese sinabasezeranyije imyenda itagira inenge, bana banjye bato? Kandi nabageneye umurage wanyu mu miryango, kuko muri Isirayeli y'Imana nacunguye mu isi.

Mureke gutekereza mu buryo bw'ibisekuruza bya Adamu, kuko amaraso ya Adamu yangirijwe mu cyaha! Nk'uko byanditswe: umuntu wa mbere Adamu yaremwe ari ubugingo buzima, naho Adamu wa nyuma yaremwe ari umwuka utanga ubuzima. Mbese ntimwasomye? Ntabwo bose ari Isirayeli, bakomoka kuri Isirayeli. Kuko abana b'isezerano ari urubyaro rwa Aburahamu binyuze mu kwizera, kandi nk'uko byanditswe Umukiranutsi azabeshwaho no kwizera. Kandi muri njye, Kristo Yesu, ibintu byose biruzuzwa kuko Intare izaba mu bumwe bwuzuye n'Umwana w'Intama kandi mwitirirwa njye byuzuye, Kristo Yesu Umwami wanyu. Dore! Ariko kuri mwe mutinya Izina ryanjye, umwana wo gukiranuka azabarasira! Ndi umwana wo Gukiranuka, ndetse n'umwana w'ikinege w'Imana isumba byose, Yehova, kuko nta wuhwanye na we, kandi ndi uje gushikuzuza itorero ryanjye mu makuba, ndetse n'umugeni wanjye nkamujyana mu rugo rwe mu ijuru, kandi azabana nanjye iteka. Kuko niko bizaba, nzabahuza n'umuryango wanyu w'ukuri kuko Isirayeli yatatanyirijwe mu mahanga kandi muri Isirayeli y'Imana bazakoranywa, ntibazongera gutatana ukundi, kuko muri Isirayeli y'Imana!

Nuko rero nzatwara Itorero ryanjye mu ijuru, abizerwa banjye, kandi muzabana nanjye iteka. Kuko mwasezeranyijwe umugabo umwe: Yeshua Hamashiach, kandi binyuze mu mwuka wanjye nabagize isugi itanduye kandi neza abizerwa kugira ngo babashe guhagarara bakiranuka mu gitambo cyanjye cy'impongano. Nimutwarwa muzagororerwa ku bw'ubudahemuka bwanyu kandi muzatwara amakamba menshi, nubwo bamwe bazahomba. Muzagororerwa birenze uko mwabitekereza, kuko ijisho ritigeze ribona cyangwa ugutwi ngo kumve cyangwa ngo byinjire mu mutima w'umuntu ibyo Yehova yateguriye abamukunda. Kandi abizerwa, ndetse n'Umugeni wanjye, bazabana nanjye iteka ryose, kandi bazategeka nk'abami n'abatambyi iteka ryose, kuko Ubwami bwanjye butagira iherezo.

Kandi bityo marayika wanjye azazamuka aho muzaba muri kumwe nanjye mu ijuru ashyireho ikimenyetso abantu 12,000 muri buri muryango. Mbese ntimwasomye Amagambo yanjye nahawe Yohana Uhishurirwa? Kandi nabonye undi marayika azamuka aturuka iburasirazuba, afite ikimenyetso cy'Imana ihoraho: asakuza n'ijwi rirenga abwira abamarayika bane, bahawe kugirira nabi isi n'inyanja, ati, ntimugirire nabi isi, cyangwa inyanja cyangwa ibiti, kugeza aho tuzashyirira ikimenyetso mu ruhanga rw'abagaragu b'Imana yacu. Mbese ntimwabonye ijambo kuzamuka? Abamarayika bera bo mu ijuru bamanukira mu isi, kuko urugo rwabo ruri mu ijuru. Kugira ngo umumarayika wera azamuke, aba azamuka mu ijuru. Ni abamarayika baguye bazamuka mu isi bavuye ikuzimu, mbese ntimwubaha Amagambo y'ijambo ryanjye ryera?

Kandi bazashyiraho ikimenyetso ku bantu 12,000 muri buri muryango urondowe: Yuda, Rubeni, Gadi, Asheri, Nafutali, Manase, Simeyoni, Lewi, Isakari, Zebuloni, Yozefu, Benyamini. Kandi bityo abantu banjye 144,000 bazaba bafite ikimenyetso cy'Imana ihoraho mu ruhanga rwabo. Kandi bazahagararana nanjye ku Musozi Siyoni, kandi bazaririmba indirimbo nshya imbere y'intebe y'ubwami, n'imbere y'ibizima bine, n'abakuru: kandi nta muntu numwe washoboraga kwiga iyo ndirimbo keretse ba bantu ibihumbi ijana na mirongo ine na bine, bacunguwe mu isi. Aba ni bo batandujwe n'abagore; kuko ari amasugi. Aba ni bo bakurikira Umwana w'Intama aho ajya hose. Kuko aba bacunguwe mu bantu, babaye umuganura ku Imana no ku Mwana w'Intama. Kandi mu kanwa kabo ntihabonetsemo uburiganya: kuko batagira inenge imbere y'intebe y'ubwami y'Imana.

Abantu 144,000 batoranyijwe na Yehova mbere y'ishingwa ry'isi ku bw'igihe nk'iki. Babumbirwe mu makuba, kandi bazagira ubushobozi n'imbaraga byihariye nzabambika mu myenda yabo mishya nzabaha, kuko imyenda yanyu idoderwa mwe by'umwihariko kandi izaba irimo ibyo mwagezeho n'imico yanyu. Bazatwarwa mu ikoraniro ry'abizerwa ariko bashyiriweho imigambi yanjye, ndetse n'imigambi ya Yehova na Yeshua Hamashiach. Baza mu gihe cy'umubabaro ukomeye kugira ngo bayobore Isirayeli mu mutekano. Ariko mbere batwarwa gushyingiranwa na Kristo kuko ari Umugeni we, abahamagawe, abatoranyijwe, abizerwa, n'ab'ukuri. Ndetse n'abo Kristo yagize abera nk'isugi mu maraso ye y'impongano. Kandi ni mwe mugeni wanjye muzakorera imigambi ya Yehova ku itegeko ryanjye mu bihe by'amakuba akomeye cyane.

Kuko muri umuganura wasaruwe mu mirima y'isarura, kuko ndi Umwami w'isarura, kandi none igihe kirageze ngo mbakoranyirize mu kigega cyanjye kugira ngo muhabwe ingororano y'imirimo yanyu. Kuko abakusanyije ingano bazagira byinshi naho abakusanyije ibyatsi bazahomba uko umurama ushya mu muriro, ariko nyamara bazakizwa. Kandi bityo abakozi banjye n'abantu banjye bazagera ku murage wabo muri njye, Yeshua Hamashiach, kuko ndi Umwami w'Abami n'Umutware w'Abatware kandi sinzacecekeshwa cyangwa ngo mpagarikwe.

Mbese ntabwo byanditswe? Uku niko Umwami IMANA ivuga; Dore, nzavanya abana ba Isirayeli mu bapagani, aho bagiye hose, kandi nzabakoranya impande zose, mbazane mu gihugu cyabo: Kandi nzabagira ishyanga rimwe mu gihugu ku misozi ya Isirayeli; kandi umwami umwe azaba umwami wabo bose: kandi ntibazongera kuba amahanga abiri, cyangwa ngo bagabanywemo ubwami bubiri ukundi: Kandi ntibazongera kwiyanduza ibigirwamana byabo, cyangwa ibintu byabo byangwa cyangwa ibicumuro byabo byose: ariko nzabakiza mbakure mu buturo bwabo bwose, aho bakoreye ibyaha, kandi nzabeza: bityo bazaba abantu banjye, nanjye nzaba Imana yabo.

Njye Yeshua Hamashiach nohereje aya Magambo binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye, kandi binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye aya Magambo yoherejwe! Dore! Ndi uje mu bicu, nuko mwitegure, mwa masugi y'abanyabwenge mwe.

Kuva kuri Yehova Imana Ikomeye Ihoraho:

Mbona ubwoba bwanyu, mwa baguye mwe, kuko noneho haje urubanza rwanyu ndetse n'urubanza rukomeye rwasezeranyijwe kera, n'ibihe by'urubanza rukomeye byatangajwe mu bihe byashize nanjye, Yehova Imana ikomeye ihoraho wavuze urubanza rwanyu n'inkuba zirindwi zivuga amajwi yazo kandi bityo ijwi ry'Imana ishobora byose rizumvikana! Kandi imigambi yanjye izahuzwa, ndetse n'imigambi yanjye y'ibihe byose izuzuzwa mu mwana wanjye Yesu Kristo. Kuko mu muganura haza ingororano z'isarura ku bakozi boherejwe na Yeshua Hamashiach kandi ingano zanjye noneho zizakoranyirizwa mu kigega cyanjye n'itandukanya rya nyuma mu isi rizatangira kandi umubabaro wa Yakobo uri hano.

Dore! Nzatandukanya ijuru hejuru ntitize isi hasi, kandi nzakoranya abaragwa b'agakiza basigaye mu isi, kandi bazagera ku gakiza, kandi bazambaza izina ry'Umwami kugeza ku iherezo, kandi bazankorera mu rusengero rwanjye n'imbaga nyamwinshi kandi ikomeye izakizwa mu cyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli. Njye, ndetse na Yehova, nzasatura isi nkure imisozi mu myanya yayo kandi isi yose izamenya ko El Shaddai yabyutse aturutse hejuru kandi urubanza rukomeye noneho ruzagwira isi ubwo Abizerwa banjye bazaba bakoranyijwe kuko ibicumuro bizarangira, ndetse n'igicumuro cya Yakobo n'ibyaha by'inzu ya Isirayeli.

Mu cyimbo cyabo hazaza ingoma yo gukiranuka, ndetse n'ingoma yo gukiranuka y'umwana wanjye w'ikinege, Yeshua Hamashiach, uwo nishimira iteka. Ni we uzakubita amahanga inkoni y'icyuma kandi izaba ingoma yo gukiranuka, aho Intare izaryamana n'umwana w'intama. Kandi niko bizaba umutambyi mukuru iteka ryose mu buryo bwa Melikisedeki n'Umwami w'Abami n'Umutware w'Abatware baziyunga byuzuye mu mwuzuro w'iteka kandi ingoma ye izahoraho iteka, ndetse Yeshua Hamashiach kuko uwo ari we Yesu Kristo!

Mwitegure mwa batuye mu isi mu gukiranirwa kuko nohereza umwana wanjye gusohoza urubanza rwanjye no kumenagura ubutegetsi bwose bw'abaguye, kuko ibihe byabo byageze kandi urubanza ruzagwira isi biremereye. Kuko mwibikiye umujinya ukomeye uzabageraho ku munsi w'umujinya kandi umunsi w'umujinya ubariho, mwa bateshwa agaciro mwe, kuko mwiyanduje mu bwangizi bukabije, kandi nta mahoro mufite ku bw'inzira zanyu zanduye ziteye ishozi. Ariko mbere y'uko mujya mu murage wanyu w'iteka aho muzarohama mu birimi by'umuriro by'ubururu bishyushye cyane, muzabanza gukorera imigambi yanjye, kuko ndetse n'abanzi banjye bumvira ijwi ryanjye.

Kandi bityo urubanza ruzatangirira mu nzu y'Imana kandi nzohereza umwana wanjye ndetse Kristo Yesu Mesiya wazutse wa Isirayeli n'umukiza w'isi, kandi azakora ibyo nategetse byose nasezeranyije ba Data n'abana kandi byose bizaba yego na amena uko isohozwa ry'amasezerano rizaba.

Kuko ijuru ryateguriwe Abana banjye, ndetse n'abo nategetse bose ku bw'imigambi yanjye mbere y'uko irema ritangira. Kuko narinzi buri wese muri mwe mu bihe by'iteka byashize, kandi nabashyizeho nk'ibanga rihishe mu mutima wanjye nahishuye gusa mu gihe gikwiye mu mugambi wanjye w'iteka w'agakiza. Nta kintu gishobora kuba ntabizi mbere y'uko kiba, kandi Ni Njye, Imana Ishobora Byose, ihamagara ibitariho nk'aho byamaze kubaho. Yemwe, mwa baguye mwe, muzashya rwose mu rubanza rwanyu rwegereje, kandi mwari abapfapfa bihagije ngo mutekereze ko mushobora gufata Yehova mu buriganya, ariko NDI WE ufata abibone mu rubanza, kuko ari abanyabwenge mu kwibona kwabo. Kuko naberetse gusa amabanga atagira agaciro, kuko narinzi ko muzagambanira ibyanjye byose. Mbega ukuntu muri abapfapfa mwa baguye mwe kandi ntimuri abanyabwenge. Ibyo mwagezeho byose ni umubabaro wanyu n'irimbuka rihoraho mu birimi bishyushye cyane, kandi mukoze mu mazuku yaka umuriro, kuko niko inyanja ngari yaka iteka, kandi nzashyiraho ikimenyetso, kandi muzibagirana rwose, kandi ntimuzibukwa.

Ariko ibi siko bizabagendera mwe batoranyijwe, abizerwa banjye, ndetse n'abitirirwa izina ryanjye bose. Ibyanyu ni ejo hazaza h'ibyishimo by'iteka, umunezero w'iteka, umugisha w'iteka kandi ntihazabaho iherezo ry'iminsi kandi ntihazigera habaho umunsi mubi ku binjira muri paradizo. Dore! Nzagarura ibintu byose ndetse mbigire byiza kuruta ubutungane bwanjye bw'umwimerere mbere y'uko kubora kugwa, kuko nzarema ijuru rishya n'isi nshya ibamo gukiranuka kandi nta kintu kizinjiramo kibi cyangwa cyanduye. Kuko hanze hari imbwa, n'abarozi, n'abasambanyi, n'abicanyi, n'abasenga ibigirwamana, n'umuntu wese ukunda kandi agakora ibinyoma, kandi bose bazagira uruhare rwabo muri iyo nyanja yaka umuriro aho inyo yabo idapfa.

Nta cyo gutinya rero mu gihe kizaza cyanyu mwa bahamagawe, batoranyijwe, kandi bizerwa, kandi muzategekana iteka na Yeshua Hamashiach kandi nta iherezo ry'ubwami bwe cyangwa ingoma ye. Kandi njye Imana ikomeye ihoraho, ndetse na Yehova ubwe nzatura mu bantu kandi hazabaho amahoro n'ubutungane bwuzuye ku byaremwe byanjye byose kandi nzahanagura amarira yose ku maso yabo kandi nta muntu numwe uzongera kubyuka uzabateza ibibazo na gato, kuko njye, Yehova, ndetse n'Imana ikomeye ihoraho, ndahiye ibi bintu ku izina ryanjye kuko nta wundi mukuru narahirira. Ariko kuri mwe mutinya izina ryanjye Umwana wo Gukiranuka azabarasira afite gukiza mu mababa ye; kandi muzasohoka, mukure nk'inyana zo mu kiraro.

Njye Yehova, Imana Ikomeye ihoraho, nohereje Amagambo yanjye binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye, kandi binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye Amagambo yanjye yoherejwe.

NDI NDI WE Ukomeye kandi nta wundi uhari!

Musome Umwana kugira ngo atarakara, mukarimburwa mu nzira, ubwo umujinya we uzaba ucanwe ho gato.
