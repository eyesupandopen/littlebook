Ubuhamya Bubiri: Isirayeli
Ibisharira N'ibiryohereye
2 Nzeri 2024

Bakora Intambara Binyuze Mu Buriganya!

Dore! Ni nde mutego utega umutego inyoni ireba? Ariko ni ukuri, ni ukuri, abantu b'igihugu cy'isezerano barategwa umutego, kandi intambara iri mu isi kuko umujinya n'urubanza bigiye kugwa. Nimwikoranyirize ku ijwi rya Yeshua HaMashiach kandi mwumve amagambo yanjye. Dore! Ndababwira ibizaba bitaraba, kugira ngo amagambo yanjye abe umuburo ku bo yohererejwe bose. Kuko uko niko kamere y'amagambo y'ubugingo mvuga iri, kuko jyewe, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndi Ijambo ry'Imana ryigize umubiri, kandi ntuye muri mwe, kuko muri urusengero rw'Imana nzima, kandi ndi umutambyi mukuru iteka ryose mu buryo bwa Melikisedeki.

Ni ukuri, ni ukuri, amagambo yanjye azagera ku maso, amatwi, n'imitima y'abo yohererejwe, kandi azarinzwe mu ntambara n'amakuba bigiye kuza, keretse ko hazabaho ibura ry'ibyanditswe ku isi yose kuko igihe isi yasutsweho amagambo ya Yahweh ku bwinshi, isi yakiriye ayo magambo igihe gito. Ariko noneho isi yanze amagambo ya Yahweh ihitamo ibinyoma by'Ikiyoka, bityo rero Ijambo ry'Imana rizaba iry'agaciro, kandi hazabaho inzara y'Ijambo ry'Imana, kuko uko niko imanza zigiye kuza ziri. Nyamara, ubugingo bwose buzambaza izina ry'Umwami kugeza ku iherezo buzakizwa, ndetse n'abatizirika ku bugingo bwabo kugeza ku iherezo.

Ariko mwumve kandi musobanukirwe amagambo yanjye, kandi mumenye musobanukirwe ko amagambo yanjye azarinzwe, kandi azahishurirwa isi, kandi ibihe byo guhishurwa byageze. Kandi nuko jyewe, Yeshua HaMashiach, nohereje ubutumwa bwiza bw'iteka ryose mu bihe by'amakuba akomeye, ndetse n'amakuba ya Yakobo, kugira ngo imbaga y'abantu benshi bakizwe nk'abakuwe mu muriro. Kandi nuko ubutumwa bwiza bw'iteka ryose bwaje guhishurwa mu gihe cyabwo, kandi ubuhamya buzandikwa, kandi amagambo ya Yahweh azatemba nk'inzuzi z'amazi y'ubugingo, kandi yandikiwe kumvirwa n'abafite amatwi yo kumva n'amaso yo kureba n'umutima wo kwiga bose. Kandi amagambo ya Yahweh ni amagambo y'iteka ryose nta muntu n'umwe ushobora gutakaza, kwangiza, cyangwa gucecekesha, kuko umuntu ubikoze azitakaza we ubwe, nyamara amagambo y'Imana ihoraho ahoraho iteka.

Dore! Ibihe by'amakuba ya Yakobo byageze, kandi ibihe byo guhishura byose byageze, kuko Yahweh yatoranyije iki gihe ngo yihishurire ibyo yaremye, ndetse no mu maso y'umwana we w'ikinege, Yesu Kristo. Kuko ndi Yesu Kristo, ndetse n'Umwami w'abami n'Umutware w'abatware wazamutse, kandi mu maboko yanjye ni ho hashyizwe icyangombwa cy'isi kuko nayiguze amaraso yanjye atagira icyaha, kandi ingororano yanjye iri kumwe nanjye. Kandi nzagabanya umurage wanjye mu bakiranutsi, kandi uneshesha azaragwa byose mu kuboko kwanjye nk'ingororano y'ubudahemuka bwabo, kugira ngo bahore berekana ubwiza bw'iteka bwa Yahweh. Kandi Yahweh azabaha ubwiza, kandi abicishije bugufi mu kwicisha bugufi, kumvira, no kwihana bazashyirwa hejuru mu bwiza bw'iteka kandi bazaka nk'inyenyeri mu isanzure, kandi nuko ubwiza bwabo buzaka iteka ryose. Nuko rero ndabatera inkurugutwi, bakunzi banjye, kumva ijwi ryanjye no kunamisha imitima yanyu mu kumvira.

Munyumve, kandi munyumve neza; intambara irageze, kandi Babuloni y'Ibanga ibohewe kurimbuka; kandi urubanza rwayo n'igihano cyayo ntibitinda ahubwo biri hafi. Kandi uko nshikuje Itorero ryanjye, Umubiri wanjye, ndetse n'Umugeni wanjye mu makuba nkabajyana mu mutekano w'ijuru, ubuza azakurwaho, kandi bazagaba igitero cyabo gitunguranye, kandi igihe cy'igitero cyabo ni ubu. Abami icumi bakoze inama rwihishwa hagati yabo, kandi rwose bateguye amaboko yabo; bariteguye, barateguye, kandi batwikiriye isi mu binyoma byabo, kugira ngo bagaragare nk'abafite ishingiro mu itsembabwoko rya za miriyoni na miriyoni bazagwa mu byago n'ububabare bw'intambara yabo.

Kandi ni isinagogi ya Satani ubwayo igenzura isi ubu, kandi bihisha mu gihugu cy'isezerano nk'uko igitagangurirwa cyihisha kure y'amaso y'umuhigo wacyo kugeza igihe umuhigo uguye mu ruzundura rwacyo. Ni ukuri, ni ukuri, baboshye uruzundura rwabo ku isi yose, kandi bafashe abami b'isi mu nzundura zabo z'uburiganya, kuko binyuze mu buriganya bakora intambara, ndetse n'ubwicanyi, ruswa, iterabwoba, kwambura ku ngufu, n'ibindi byaha byinshi. Kuko inzira zabo zangiritse rwose, kuko Satani ashyira aboroheje cyane n'abangiritse rwose nk'abayobozi b'isi kugira ngo bayoborwe byoroshye kandi bakoreshwe rwose n'urumamfu Satani yabibye mu mirima y'ingano ya Yahweh. Kandi ibihe by'isarura rya nyuma byegereje aho ingano zizakoranyirizwa mu kigega cy'iteka cya Yahweh binyuze mu masarura agiye kuza, kandi urumamfu rwa Satani ruzarandurwa rwose, rubohwe, kandi rujugunywe ruzima muri rwa nyanja y'umuriro w'amazuku yaka ibirimi by'umuriro by'ubururu bishyushye cyane mu murage wa ba se; kandi uko niko ejo habo hazaza hameze, nuko ntimubatinye.

Ariko niko bimeze, igitagangurirwa gitegeka uruzundura rwacyo uko gishaka gufata igenzura ry'isi; kuko isi ishyizwe mu maboko y'ikiyoka mu buryo bufite imipaka mu gihe gito, ndetse n'icyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli, ibyo bihe bikaba ari ubu. Kandi nuko abakiranutsi mu Itorero ryanjye bazajyanwa mu ijuru mu ngororano ikomeye, kandi bazahabwa umurage wabo, kandi umukwe azahura n'umugeni we mu isezerano ry'iteka, kandi bazahorana nanjye aho nzajya hose. Kandi nuko icyangombwa cy'isi mfashe mu maboko yanjye ubu gifatanijwe ibimenyetso kigiye gufungurwa, kandi ibimenyetso bizafungurwa, kandi bazavuza amakanderi yabo, kandi inzabya zizasendwa mu mujinya. Kandi urubanza rukomeye n'irimbuka bizatwikira isi, kandi amakuba azagwa ku isi yose uko intambara ya nyuma yo guhuza amahanga munsi y'igenzura rya Satani ikomeza, kugira ngo inyamaswa n'umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma bazamuke kandi bategeke isi mu bugome bukomeye, kandi niko bizaba. Bazatangaza intambara ku bintu byose by'Imana y'ukuri, kandi Ubukristo buzaba bunyuranyije n'amategeko, kandi inyamaswa zihawe kurwanya abera no kubanesha, kandi benshi bazacibwa imitwe ku bw'izina ryanjye, kandi abazabikora bose bazabona ubugingo bwabo iteka mu ngororano ikomeye n'ubwiza bw'iteka, kandi bazabaho ubuzima bw'iteka bw'amahoro, kandi bazishimira ibyiza by'igihugu.

Nyamara, uko isi ipfukamishwa itagira ukwizera mu kugandukira ikibi cyuzuye binyuze mu ntambara ikomeye, ubugome, n'irimbuka, inyamaswa iva mu nyanja n'inyamaswa iva mu isi zizazamuka byuzuye, nubwo ubu biyoberanya nk'abanzi mu mayeri yabo y'ubu n'uburiganya bwo kurimbura Babuloni y'Ibanga. Musobanukirwe ko se ari umwe, kuko ari aba Satani, ndetse na ba babiri b'abizira ikiyoka cyatoranyije kugira ngo basohoze imigambi yacyo. Kandi nuko inyamaswa iva mu nyanja n'inyamaswa iva mu isi zizazamuka, kandi zizahuza amahoro mu mahanga, keretse ko azaba ari amahoro y'ibinyoma. Musobanukirwe ko nta mahoro ari hanze y'amasezerano y'amahoro yatanzwe na Yahweh ku byaha by'isi, kuko jyewe, Yesu w'i Nazareti, ndi umuganwa w'amahoro kandi nzana amahoro y'iteka, kandi mu gukiranuka nzategeka isi nturutse mu gihugu cy'isezerano ku ntebe y'ubwami ya Dawidi, kuko ndi umuragwa we wemewe n'amategeko.

Kandi nuko bazazamuka. Intambara ya gatatu y'isi izabyutswa hagati y'abapfukamira kibe yabo y'umukara kandi bazakora intambara ku isi uko bashaka kurandura ikintu cyose gifite aho gihuriye n'izina Yesu Kristo. Keretse abapfukamira kibe yabo y'umukara bazashyirwa rwose munsi y'igenzura ry'inyamaswa iva mu nyanja, izabayobora nka Mahdi wabo. Kandi abari mu bihugu by'isezerano bazagira umwami wabo w'ibinyoma, umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma, Moshiach w'ibinyoma, kandi azaba umwubatsi w'urusengero rw'inyamaswa, kandi inyamaswa iva mu isi izabayobora ityo mu buriganya bukomeye. Kandi abambara kibe y'umukara mu ruhanga rwabo bazahuza amahoro n'abapfukamira kibe yabo y'umukara, kandi isi izunga ubumwe uko bakurikira izo nyamaswa kandi bavugana bati, ni nde uhwanye n'inyamaswa? Kandi ni nde ushobora kurwanya inyamaswa? Nyamara jyewe, Kristo Yesu, uri Umwami w'abami n'Umutware w'abatware, nzaza guca urubanza no kurwana, kandi nzarimbura rwose inyamaswa n'umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma, kandi bazajugunywa bazima muri rwa nyanja runini rwaka umuriro n'amazuku aho bazategereza Ikiyoka ngo kiyunge nabo mu gihe cyagenwe.

Kandi nuko bazatanga amasezerano akomeye y'amahoro n'umutekano, kandi bazakoranya abatakaye iteka mu kurimbuka kwabo. Bazasezeranya sosiyete nshya n'ubutegetsi bushya bizasaba ikimenyetso cyabo cy'inyamaswa kugira ngo witabire, nuko ntimuzafate icyo kimenyetso. Binyuze mu kimenyetso cyabo bazasezeranya umuti w'ibyorezo kandi bazasezeranya ubuzima bwiza n'uburamba, kandi baziyumvisha ejo hazaza heza, kandi bazaha amasezerano menshi y'ibinyoma abazarimbuka kubera gufata ikimenyetso cyabo cy'inyamaswa.

Kandi nuko nongera kubwira abatuye mu isi, kugira ngo bose bumve:

UMUNTU WESE NARAMUKA ARAMIJE INYAMASWA N'IGISHUSHANYO CYAYO, KANDI AGAFATA IKIMENYETSO CYAYO MU RUHANGA RWE, CYANGWA KU KUBOKO KWE, UWO NAWE AZANYWA KU MVINYO Y'UMUJINYA W'IMANA, YASUTSWE IDAFUNGWANZE MU GIKOMBE CY'UBURAKARI BWAYO, KANDI AZABABARIZWA MU MURIRO N'AMAZUKU, IMBERE Y'ABAMARAYIKA BERA, N'IMBERE Y'UMWANA W'INTAMA: KANDI UMWOTSI W'UKUBABARIZWA KWABO UZACUMBA ITEKA RYOSE. KANDI NTIBAZAGIRA IKIHUKO KU MANYWA NA NIJORO, ABARAMYA INYAMASWA N'IGISHUSHANYO CYAYO, N'UMUNTU WESE UFATA IKIMENYETSO CY'IZINA RYAYO.

Ariko musobanukirwe, bana banjye, ko sosiyete yabo itazagira amahoro, kandi amasezerano yabo ni ibyambo byo gushukira abatatse mu mutego wabo; kuko ni nde mutego utega umutego inyoni ireba? Nyamara inyoni nyinshi zizafatwa mu mutego wabo, ndetse n'abantu bose amazina yabo atigeze yandikwa mu Gitabo cy'Ubugingo. Musobanukirwe ko abazafata ikimenyetso bose bazahindurwa iteka, kandi bazaba bakoze ituka kuri Yahweh, binyuze mu guhindura DNA yabo bityo bakangiza ishusho y'Imana umuntu yaremwemo, kandi abazabikora bose nta mbabazi bafite, nuko ntimuzafate ikimenyetso cyabo. Ibyiza ni ugutakaza ubuzima bwanyu bwo ku isi kugira ngo mbakize iteka kandi kugira ngo mubone ubugingo bw'iteka muri njye, Yeshua HaMashiach, binyuze mu kwambaza izina ryanjye kugeza ku iherezo kuruta kugerageza mu bujiji gukiza ubuzima bwanyu bwo ku isi mugatakaza amahirwe yose y'ubugingo bw'iteka mu rupfu rwa kabiri rw'ibirimi by'umuriro by'iteka aho inyo yabo idapfa. Nuko rero muhitemo neza!

Isi izazamuka iva mu ivu ry'intambara izaba isi y'ibyorezo, urupfu, inzara, ihumana, akarengane, ubugome, ubusazi, n'umubabaro wuzuye. Ibyishimo byose bizakurwa ku isi, nyamara inyamaswa iva mu isi izahatira ikimenyetso cyayo ku isi, ndetse n' kurumwa n'inzoka, kandi umuntu wese uzabyanga azatakaza ubuzima bwe. Kandi uko niko guhitamo benshi bazagomba gukora kugira ngo babone ubugingo bw'iteka mu gakiza, kandi abazambaza izina ry'Umwami bose kugeza ku iherezo bazakizwa. Nyamara, sosiyete yabo nshya ntizazana amahoro, kandi abazafata ikimenyetso bose bazaba igice cy'intekerezo z'ikivunge, kandi bazavangwa n'ikoranabuhanga, kandi bazaba abagaragu b'ubushake bw'ikiyoka, kandi nicyo bazakorera, kandi muri rwa nyanja runini rw'umuriro niho bazajya, kandi urwo nirwo rupfu rwa kabiri.

Ikiyoka, inyamaswa iva mu nyanja, n'inyamaswa iva mu isi bizayobora ihuriro ry'abami, kandi bazakora intambara ku bantu bose bashaka kubaho mu nzira za Yahweh, kandi bazanshinja, Yeshua HaMashiach, amakuba agwira isi. Mu rubanza no mu kwigomeka gukomeye bazatuka izina ry'Imana yo mu ijuru n'umwana wayo w'ikinege, kandi bazakora ibirego bikomeye by'ibitutsi. Inyamaswa iva mu nyanja n'inyamaswa iva mu isi zizishyira hamwe mu kurwanya abahamya banjye babiri n'ibitereko by'amatabaza byanjye bibiri kandi bazahagarara babarwanya n'ibimenyetso n'ibitangaza byinshi by'ibinyoma, kandi bazavuga amagambo menshi y'ibitutsi. Kandi ubwo abahamya banjye babiri n'ibitereko by'amatabaza bibiri bazaba barangije ubuhamya bwabo, inyamaswa izamuka iva mu kuzimu izabarwanya, kandi izabanesha, ibice. Nyamara mu minsi itatu n'igice bazazuka, kandi ijwi rikomeye riva mu ijuru rizababwira riti, Nimuzamuke hano. Kandi bazazamuka mu ijuru mu gicu, kandi abanzi babo bazabareba.

Kandi bityo inyamaswa ivuye mu isi, ndetse n'uzana ikimenyetso cyayo cy'inyamaswa mu ntoki, ndetse n'uzaba umuhanuzi wabo w'ibinyoma na Mesiya w'ibinyoma, azaza gutegeka igihugu cy'isezerano. Kandi yamaze kwiyegereza abategetsi b'icyo gihugu, ndetse n'abayobozi b'isinagogi ya Satani, kuko asangiye nabo amaraso yabo yanduye, kandi yewe yirata igisekuru cye, kandi Dani ni icyana cy'intare kitazigera gikomokaho Mesiya n'umwe. Kandi bityo azubaka urusengero rwabo mu gihe bashaka mu bujiji kongera gushyiraho amategeko ya Mose, ibyo bikaba byarasohojwe mu butungane bwuzuye mu gitambo cyanjye nitanzeho nk'Umwana w'Intama w'Imana, kuko ari jye witangiye kuba Pasika itunganye ku byaha by'abantu. Ariko se ni uwuhe mutego umutega-nyoni atega inyoni ireba?

Ni ukuri, ni ukuri, bazatega umutego muri Siyoni, kandi bazashaka gufata abo mu gihugu, kuko Satani ashaka kurimbura ubugingo bwose busigaye bw'urubyaro rwa Adamu, ndetse n'ikiremwamuntu; kandi iyo iminsi y'amakuba ya Yakobo itaza kugabanywa, nta muntu n'umwe wari kurokoka. Ariko bityo urubuga ruzategurwa n'urusengero rwabo rwa gatatu, aho ikizira kirimbura kizabera. Kandi bityo naraburiye nti: Nuko rero nimubona ikizira kirimbura, cyavuzwe na Daniyeli Umuhanuzi, gihagaze ahera (usoma, asobanukirwe). Icyo gihe abazaba bari i Yudaya, bazahungire mu misozi. Uzaba ari hejuru y'inzu, ntazamanuke, ngo agire icyo akura mu nzu ye: Kandi uzaba ari mu murima, ntazasubire inyuma kuzana imyenda ye. Kandi bazabona ishyano abatwite, n'abonsa muri iyo minsi. Ariko musenge kugira ngo guhunga kwanyu kuzabe mu gihe cy'imbeho, cyangwa ku munsi w'Isabato: Kuko icyo gihe hazabaho umubabaro mwinshi, utarigeze kubaho uhereye ku kuremwa kw'isi kugeza ubu, oya, kandi ntuzigera ubaho.

Musobanukirwe ko iki gihe kiri hafi kandi kizaba nyuma y'amezi 42 ya mbere y'amakuba ya Yakobo. Mu mezi 42 ya mbere, abatangabuhamya babiri bazahanura, kandi bazubaka urusengero rwabo mu gihe ikintu nk'icyo kitagikenewe; kuko jyewe, Yesu w'i Nazareti, ndi Pasika itunganye ku byaha by'abantu iteka ryose. Nyamara, izi nyamaswa z'inkazi zihagaze zindirwanya, kandi inyamaswa ivuye mu isi izagerageza kwishyira hejuru nka Mesiya wabo w'ibinyoma, kandi uyu ni wo mutego wabo.

Baziyambaza abo mu gihugu bakinyanga, Yeshua HaMashiach, Mesiya wabo w'ukuri, kandi benshi baziruka bakoranire kuri Mesiya wabo w'ibinyoma, nyamara bamukurikira mu bujiji bagana ku kurimbuka kwabo. Kuko inyamaswa ivuye mu isi ari yo itega umutego mu gihugu, kandi izashukashuka benshi ibashyire mu ruzundura rwayo rw'ibinyoma; hanyuma igitagangurirwa kizasimbukira ku muhigo wacyo, kandi izagambanira abantu bo mu gihugu. Ntimwasomye se? Kuko bityo, byanditswe ngo: Ntihakagire umuntu ubeshya na gato: kuko uwo munsi utazaza, keretse habanje kubaho kugwa, kandi uwo mugabo w'icyaha agahishurwa, ndetse umwana wo kurimbuka, Uwo ni umwanzi, kandi azishyira hejuru y'icyitwa Imana cyose, cyangwa igisengwa: kugira ngo yicare nk'Imana mu Rusengero rw'Imana, yiyerekana ko ari Imana. Nuko rero musobanukirwe ko kugwa kwa mbere ari aho nzateranyiriza abizerwa mu Itorero ryanjye aho ndi ngo bansanganire mu kirere kandi icyo gihe ubuza azakurwaho nuko uwo mugabo w'icyaha azahishurwa mu gihe cye.

Ariko se ni ryari Yahweh yigeze arekera abaraganwa agakiza kurimbuka kw'iteka, kandi ndabitangaza ku mugaragaro mbere y'uko biba: Ko jyewe, Yeshua HaMashiach, nzongera gukiza abasigaye ba Yakobo, kandi benshi bo mu gihugu bazanyakira nka Mesiya wabo. Kandi nzabatabara. Dore, byaranditswe ngo: Kandi narebye, mbona Umwana w'Intama ahagaze ku musozi wa Siyoni, ari kumwe n'abantu ibihumbi 144 bafite Izina rya Se ryanditswe mu ruhanga rwabo. Kandi bityo niko bizaba ko nzaza gutabara abasigaye ngo batarimbuka, kandi nzaza n'imbaraga zidashobora guhagarikwa bazagenda intambwe ku yindi uko mbayobora kugira ngo nyobore abana banjye bato atekanye. Wenda ushobora kwibaza abo bantu ibihumbi 144 abo ari bo n'impamvu bankurikira ku Musozi wa Siyoni?

Abo bihumbi 144 batoranyijwe na Yahweh mbere y'ishingwa ry'isi ku bw'iki gihe. Bacuriwe mu makuba, kandi bazagira ubushobozi n'imbaraga byihariye nzabambika mu myenda yabo mishya nzabaha, kuko imyenda yabo idoderwa bo by'umwihariko kandi izaba irimo ibyo bagezeho n'ibiranga kamere yabo. Bazatwarwa mu ikoraniro ry'abizerwa ariko bashyizwe ukwe ku bw'imigambi yanjye, ndetse n'imigambi ya Yahweh na Yeshua HaMashiach. Bazaza mu gihe cy'umubabaro mwinshi kugira ngo bayobore Isirayeli atekanye ariko babanza gutwarwa ngo bashyingiranwe na Kristo, kuko ndi Umukwe, kandi bo ni Umugeni wanjye, abahamagawe, abatoranyijwe, abizerwa, n'ab'ukuri, ndetse n'abo nagize abera nk'isugi mu maraso yanjye y'impongano. Kandi ni wowe, Mugeni wanjye, uzakorera imigambi ya Yahweh ku itegeko ryanjye mu bihe by'amakuba akomeye cyane.

Dore, byaranditswe! Kandi wa mugore ahungira mu butayu, aho afite ahantu hateguwe n'Imana, kugira ngo bamugaburirireyo iminsi igihumbi na magana abiri na mirongo itandatu. Kandi bityo niko bizaba ko nzaha abantu banjye ubuhungiro mu butayu, kuko nzongera gukiza Yakobo mu makuba yabo, kandi benshi bo mu gihugu cy'isezerano bazampindukirira, Yeshua HaMashiach, ari we Mesiya wabo w'ukuri. Kandi nzabakiza umutego bazategerwa, kandi bityo mburira bose mbere y'uko biba ngo birinde uwo mutego kandi ntibabakurikire. Ntimwizere ibimenyetso byabo by'ibinyoma n'ibitangaza, kandi ntimwizere amagambo yabo yo gutuka Imana, ahubwo mwambaze izina ry'Umwami, ndetse Yeshuah HaMashiach, ari we Yesu Kristo mu rurimi rw'Icyongereza, kuko mu rurimi urwo ari rwo rwose umuntu yambazamo izina ryanjye, ndetse bazakizwa nibambaza izina ryanjye kugeza ku iherezo.

Kandi bityo nzanywe no kumenyesha abantu banjye umutego bazategerwa n'umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma uzana ikimenyetso cye cy'inyamaswa mu ntoki. Kandi bityo mbwira abantu banjye nkererayo mbere y'uko biba ibizaba ku isi mbere y'uko biba. Ni ukuri, Umwami Imana ntizagira icyo ikora, itabanje guhishurira abagaragu bayo b'abahanuzi ibanga ryayo. Kandi nzaza gutabara benshi muri Isirayeli bari kuzarimbuka kandi nzabayobora mu butayu ahantu bateguriwe na Yahweh, kandi aho ni ho bazagaburirwa, baragirwe, kandi bitabweho kugeza amakuba arangiye. Kuko bizasohora ku iherezo ry'amakuba ya Yakobo, kandi bityo byaranditswe kandi bityo niko bizakorwa:

Kandi mbona ijuru rikinguye, dore ifarashi y'umweru, kandi uyicayeho yitwa uwo kwizerwa n'uw'ukuri, kandi aca imanza zitabera akarwana intambara gukiranuka. Amaso ye yari ameze nk'ibirimi by'umuriro, kandi ku mutwe we hari amakamba menshi, kandi yari afite izina ryanditswe, nta muntu numwe urizi keretse we ubwe. Kandi yari yambaye umwenda winitswe mu maraso, kandi izina rye yitwa, Jambo ry'Imana. Kandi ingabo zo mu ijuru zamukurikiraga ziri ku mafarashi y'umweru, zambaye imyenda y'igitare cyiza, cyera kandi gikeye. Kandi mu kanwa ke havamo inkota ityaye, kugira ngo ayikubite amahanga: kandi azayategeka n'inkoni y'icyuma: kandi asibanganya urwengero rw'ubukana n'umujinya by'Imana Ishoborabyose. Kandi ku mwenda we, no ku kibero cye afite izina ryanditswe ngo, Umwami w'Abami, N'Umutware w'Abatware.

Ndi Yeshua HaMashiach, kandi nohereje aya Magambo binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye, kandi binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye aya magambo yoherejwe. Kandi jyewe, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndi we wahishuye ubutumwa bwiza bw'iteka mu gihe cyabwo, kandi ibihe byo guhishura ni ubu.

Dore! Ibihe byo guhishura bije ku isi, kandi ndatangaza ko amakuba ya Yakobo, ndetse n'icyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli, kigeze. Mu buryo butunguranye nzashikukuza abizerwa mu isi mbajyane atekanye mu ijuru, kandi mu buryo butunguranye ubuza azakurwaho, kandi mu buryo butunguranye amakuba akomeye, urubanza, n'ibyago bizagwira abatuye isi. Kandi abagenewe ubugingo buhoraho bose bazaza ku gakiza muri jye, Kristo Yesu, Umwami wazutse uhoraho. Kandi ubwami bwa Satani buzahanwa byimazeyo kandi burimbuke, ntibuzigere bwongera kubyuka.

Ndi we uje guca imanza no kurwana intambara, kandi nzanywe nko kuba Intare yo mu muryango wa Yuda, kandi abasigaye ba Yakobo nzabakiza, kandi nzategeka isi mu gukiranuka mu bwami bwanjye bw'imyaka igihumbi, kandi nzanye umutwe munini w'abami n'abatambyi kugira ngo bategeke isi mu gukiranuka ku itegeko ryanjye.

Ndi Yeshua HaMashiach, Umwami w'Abami ukomeye n'Umutware w'Abatware, nuko rero mwumve Amagambo yanjye, kuko ibihe byo gusohozwa kwayo bigeze.
