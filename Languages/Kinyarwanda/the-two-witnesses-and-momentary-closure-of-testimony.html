Ubuhamya Bubiri: Isirayeli
Ibisharira N'ibiryohereye
13 Mutarama 2025

Abahamya Babiri n'Ifungwa ry'Igihe Gito ry'Ubuhamya

Kuva kuri Yahweh, NDI UWONDI Ukomeye:

Dore! Nje kubabwira iby'abahamya banjye babiri, ibitereko byanjye by'amatara bibiri n'ibiti byanjye by'imyelayo bibiri no guha impumyi kubona mpishura amabanga yahishwe isi mu bwenge bwanjye, kugira ngo mumenye mudashidikanya ibizaba n'inzira y'agakiza ishyirwe ahagaragara ku basoma bose kandi bafite ukwizera ko kumvira aya Magambo y'Ubutumwa Bwiza Bw'Iteka.

Dore! NDI Yahweh, uwaremye ijuru n'isi ku bw'imigambi yanjye y'iteka n'icyubahiro, kandi NDI uzahembura irema akarivana mu kubora, kugira ngo inzira zikiranuka za Yahweh zuzuze isi gukiranuka mu maso ha Yesu Kristo. Kandi bityo inzira zanjye zizakwira isi mu Bwami bw'Imyaka Igihumbi bwa Kristo Yesu, ubwami aho nta kintu kibi kizinjira, ubwami aho isi itegekwa mu gukiranuka, kandi amahoro nyakuri no kugarurwa bizuzuza isi, n'abizerwa bo mu bihe byose bazagendamo mu mahoro atunganye n'umutuzo kuko inzira za Yahweh zizatwikira isi mu gukiranuka. Ndetse n'igihe Satani azabohwa mu bubabare n'ubwoba bukabije bw'umurage we uje w'igihano cy'iteka, aho azabona inyamaswa n'umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma bajugunywa mu birimi by'igihano cy'iteka bagashya, kuko bose ari ko bakwiriye, nyamara azasohoza imigambi yanjye y'iteka yo gukiranuka mbere y'uko abohwa ngo ajugunywe mu muriro iteka ryose ntazigere avamo, kandi nzashyiraho ikimenyetso ntibazongera kwibukwa ukundi.

Kandi nzaha (kandi nishimire) ikiruhuko cy'isabato hamwe n'abaneshi banjye bakiranuka n'abemererwa kwinjira mu Bwami bw'Imyaka Igihumbi bwa Kristo Yesu! (Kuko imyaka igihumbi ari nk'umunsi umwe mu maso yanjye n'umunsi umwe nk'imyaka igihumbi kandi imyaka igihumbi ni mileniyumu.) Ndetse n'abazaba barakoranyijwe bakazurwa mu bihe byose, iby'ubu n'ibyahise, muri Kristo Yesu; ndetse na mbere y'uko Satani ajugunywa mu birimi by'umuriro, mbere y'uko igihe cye gito cy'imperuka kiba. Mu gusoza kwayo ya nzoka ya kera izabohwa iteka ryose ijugunywe mu muriro, ntizigere iva mu gihano cyayo kidashira mu birimi bishyushye cyane by'amazuku yaka. Kuko Satani n'abakurikira ibinyoma bye nta mbaraga bafite mu maso yanjye y'iteka! Ni nde wanesha Yahweh, NDI UWONDI Ukomeye, nubwo babeshya bakirata? Cyangwa ni nde wahindura ubushake bwanjye bw'iteka n'amatangazo y'imanza zagenewe kugwa byuzuye ubwo uwo nzitira azakurwaho mu buryo butunguranye ubu ku ijwi ry'impanda yahawe kuvuga mu gihe nyacyo kandi gitunganye nahisemo mu mugambi wanjye w'iteka uhereye kera kose. Ndetse n'igihe kiri hafi cyane kuba aho isezerano rikiranuka rihura n'isohozwa ry'iteka mu bihe bya none.

Kuko NDI, NDI UWONDI Ukomeye, umenya iherezo uhereye mu ntangiriro n'intangiriro uhereye mu iherezo. Ku bw'ibyo, nje guhishura ibice by'umugambi wanjye w'iteka w'agakiza wagumye ari ibanga, ndetse n'ibintu bigihishwe mu Ibanga ry'Imana rigiye gusoza mu gukiranuka kw'iteka. Nk'uko byanditswe na Yohana Uhishurirwa ubwo yandikaga mu budahemuka Amagambo yahawe na Kristo Yesu! Kandi bityo byanditswe ko mu minsi y'ijwi rya Marayika wa karindwi, ubwo azatangira kuvuza impanda, ibanga ry'Imana rizarangira, nk'uko yabibwiye abagaragu be b'Abahanuzi.

Nyamara, benshi bahumishijwe ku kuri kw'aya Magambo n'abapfumu b'ibihe babeshya bahawe ruswa n'ingabo itukura ngo bagoreke inyandiko zanjye zera n'ibinyoma by'ibihe n'ibya Siyoni n'inyigisho z'ibinyoma z'isimburwa, ndetse n'ibinyoma byatanzwe mu buhendanyi n'abaguye binyuze mu bantu babi ngo bayobye imitima y'abiyoroshya n'abacishije make. Kandi bazayobya intore zanjye bwite niba intore zanjye zihisemo kwirengagiza Amagambo yanjye yo gukiranuka mu bwibone zitekereza ko zitashobora kuyobywa kubera gutoranywa kwazo. Nyamara abakora amakosa nk'ayo banga ukuri bagahitamo ibinyoma byabo bazayobywa. Ku bw'ibyo, mwumve bantu banjye ko impanda ya karindwi atari ya mpanda yahawe kuvuga ngo irangize ibihe byanjye by'ubuntu no gukoranya abizerwa muri Kristo, ntimukayobywe!

Nyamara, ukuri kwa Yahweh guhama iteka ryose kandi ntiguhinduka, kandi Ijambo ryanjye ntirishobora kuvunwa, kandi Ijambo ryanjye rizasohoza icyo nrigambiriye ku wo Ijambo ryanjye ryohererejwe wese. Kandi nta n'umwe ushobora guhagarika imigambi yanjye y'iteka! Nta n'ushobora guhagarika Kristo Yesu, uwo umugambi wanjye w'iteka w'agakiza uruhukiyemo byuzuye. Ndetse n'uwo nahaye imbaraga hejuru y'ikiremwa cyose mu irema ryanjye nkamuha gutegeka byuzuye irema ryanjye kugeza ubwo abanzi be bagizwe intebe y'ibirenge bye, icyo gihe azashyikiriza Ubwami bwagandujwe kuri Adonai Yahweh.

Nyamara mu kuri kw'iteka, nta herezo ry'ingoma ikiranuka ya Yeshua HaMashiach, uzategeka mu gukiranuka iteka ryose, ndetse n'umugeni we bazabana iteka ryose mu nshingano zabo z'ubutware n'amatsinda y'imirimo yabo. Kandi ibyo ni ukubera ko bakiriye Yesu w'i Nazareti nk'Umwami wabo w'iteka mu bihe by'ubuntu n'imbabazi, kandi bakamukunda byuzuye mu kumvira Amagambo ye, ndetse ari na yo Magambo bazaba baraneshesheje byuzuye ibintu byose by'ubwami bw'abanzi iteka ryose.

Kuko mu kuri kw'iteka, umwambaro wa Melikisedeki uri ku ntugu z'iteka za Yeshua HaMashiach, ntuzigere uvaho! Kuko iteka ryose Umwami wo Gukiranuka azategeka iteka ryose n'itsinda rye ry'Abatambyi, Abami, n'Abahanuzi bazuzuza isi inzira zikiranuka n'ubumenyi bwa Yahweh, ndetse no mu bihe ubuhanuzi buzahagarara iteka ryose kuko nzatura mu bantu mu ijuru rishya n'isi nshya bigiye kuremwa mu gukiranuka no kwera nyakuri! Ndetse n'irema aho kumenya icyiza n'ikibi bizahagarara iteka ryose kandi gukiranuka n'urukundo n'amahoro gusa ni byo bizaboneka iteka ryose ntibizigere bivaho cyangwa ngo bigabanywe cyangwa byangizwe na gato. Ndetse bityo kuko kubora kuzajugunywa mu birimi by'umuriro iteka ryose.

Nyamara, bimeze bityo ko abaguye batwikiriye kandi bakwirakwiza ibinyoma byabo ku irema ryanjye ndetse bakabitangaza ubutitsa binyuze mu ikoranabuhanga ryabo rya gidayimoni, kugira ngo umuntu wa kamere ategekwe kandi ayoborwe n'imirongo yabo n'ikoranabuhanga rya gidayimoni. Ndetse n'amashusho ategeka ubwenge n'ahindura imyitwarire, inkuru mbi, n'amarozi bitangazwa ku ndorerwamo zabo z'umukara aho benshi bicara mu mwijima batwawe n'amashusho n'ibinyoma byabo. Kandi mu kuri, abaguye baroze imbaga y'abantu n'uburozi n'imiti (pharmakeia) bitangwa nk'imiti ivura, kugira ngo barogwe kandi bicwe na byo. Nyamara, uburozi bwabo n'ikoranabuhanga n'indorerwamo bizangizwa iteka ryose, n'ibiganiro byabo bizahagarara iteka, ibyo bakoresha batereka amarozi yabo kandi bakabyutsa irari n'ibyifuzo by'umuntu wa kamere mu cyaha gikomeye kandi ikoranabuhanga ryabo ritagira umumaro rizahinduka ubusa, ntirizongera kubaho ukundi.

Nyamara, bimeze bityo, ko nemereye abaguye gutwikira irema ryanjye mu binyoma byabo, kugira ngo abazakurikira inzira zabo ziteye ishozi bose bahitemo kuguma mu murage wabo w'iteka w'amazuku yaka kuko bishimiye gukiranirwa bacumura kuri Yahweh, NDI UWONDI Ukomeye, no ku banyantege nke mu irema ryanjye, abo barenganyije. Kandi mu bwenge bwanjye bw'iteka n'urukundo nkunda irema ryanjye, nemereye buri wese uhereye kuri Adamu guhitamo uwo azakorera: niba ari icyaha kiganisha ku rupfu, cyangwa kumvira gukiranuka kugeza ku bugingo buhoraho.

Uko ni ko inzira za Yahweh zimeze, kuko inzira zanjye ari inzira zo gukiranuka, amahoro, n'urukundo! Ndetse n'urukundo rw'iteka kandi rudashira, aho irema ryanjye ryose, rigizwe n'ingabo z'ibyaremwe binyuranye kandi byaremwe mu buryo butangaje by'amoko yose n'imigambi yose, bizabaho mu bwumvikane bukiranuka n'urukundo. Kuko irema ryanjye riteganyijwe nk'urusobe rw'ubuzima bwuzuzanya rutegekwa mu gukiranuka n'urukundo ku bw'inyungu zose n'icyubahiro cya buri kiremwa cyanjye, aho nta kiremwa na kimwe kizigera kibona inyungu ku gihombo cy'ikindi, ahubwo bose bazasangira ku buntu ku burumbuke budashira buri mu bubiko bwanjye bwagenewe inyungu n'icyubahiro cy'abumvira amategeko yanjye akiranuka babikuye ku mutima mu rukundo bakunda Data wabo w'iteka, uwo mu by'ukuri, NDI we!

Nyamara, bimeze bityo, ko NDI NDI UWONDI Ukomeye kandi sinshyigikira cyangwa ngo nemeranye n'ibinyoma byabo, kandi sinzemerera ibinyoma byabo gukomeza. Bityo rero, ndatangaza, ko jyewe, Yahweh, NDI UWONDI Ukomeye uri Imana Ihoraho yo Gukiranuka nzashyira ahagaragara byuzuye buri gice cy'ubwami bw'abanzi bugiye kuzamuka mu gihe gito cyane igongwe rihawe gutegeka, kandi rizaba rifite ubutegetsi bufite imipaka cyane. Kandi nk'uko, nzashyira ahagaragara abaguye bihishe mu irema ryanjye, bagomba kurandurwa no gushyirwa ahagaragara mu isoni zabo z'iteka byuzuye mbere y'uko bajugunywa mu birimi by'igihano iteka ryose, aho inyo yabo idapfa. Kandi bityo itangazo ryanjye rizasohoka, kuko ibyatangajwe mu cyubahiro n'imbaraga by'intebe ya Yahweh, ndetse NDI UWONDI Ukomeye, ntibishobora guhindurwa cyangwa ngo bivuguruzwe, kandi bizasohora neza nk'uko byatangajwe. Sela!

Nyamara, bimeze bityo, ko ntabwo nigeze mpisha abaguye byuzuye, kandi natangiye kubashyira ahagaragara byuzuye kandi umwenda uragenda uba muto. Ndetse n'uwo mwenda wo guhisha uwo nabahishishije amaso y'abantu, n'uzitira azakurwaho ugizwe n'ingingo zinyuranye zo kuzitira zizakurwaho byuzuye ubwo Mikayeli azahabwa itegeko ubwo impanda ya nyuma izavuga mu buryo butunguranye ubu. Kandi bityo urubura ruzagwa, n'isi izahinduka, kandi bazagira intambara yabo ikomeye n'abenshi bazarimbuka binyuze muri yo, nk'uko byanditswe kandi bigahanurwa na Yohana Uhishurirwa mu buhamya bwe bw'iteka. Kandi bityo ndabaha kumva ko mu gihe cyo gukoranya hamwe abizerwa muri Kristo nzambura uwo mwenda wo guhisha igice mu buryo butunguranye kandi n'imbaraga nk'uko natabuye umwenda w'urusengero mo kabiri uhereye hejuru kugeza hasi byaranze iherezo ry'umurimo wa Lewi n'igarurwa ryuzuye rya Melikisedeki. Ndetse kugira ngo nkureho ubuhumyi bw'igice ku bana ba Isirayeli iteka ryose, ntibuzigere bugaruka ku inuma zanjye.

Kandi ibi bizaba mu buryo butunguranye kandi butitezwe kuko isi ititeze ko Kristo Yesu amurikira inkombe n'ibirere byuzuye mu cyubahiro cye cy'iteka muri ibi bihe by'ibise by'imperuka, ndetse bityo, kuko isi mu by'ukuri itagira ukwizera. Kandi mu bangirwa kwinjira mu ijuru ubwo urwo rugi runini ruzakingwa, benshi b'abanyabyaha babi bazarimbuka kandi batwarwe mu rubanza iteka ryose, ndetse n'abanze Yesu w'i Nazareti byuzuye mu gutuka Imana gukomeye n'ubwibone ku muheto wanjye wera, n'abandi benshi bafite ibyaha bingana mu kwigomeka kwabo bazagwa iteka ryose mu rubanza rukomeye kubera ibibi bakoze. Ndetse n'ibyo bibi bakomeza gukora mu kwiyemeza gukomeye, kandi ntibazarokoka.

Mwumve ko abashaka kuvogera imipaka kamere n'amategeko y'irema ryanjye binyuze mu kwambuka iyo mipaka n'amategeko, ndetse bashaka kwihindura ibizira (mu nzira z'abaguye) na bo bazagwa mu rubanza rw'iteka kimwe: Kuko iki ari icyaha kitabarirwa, ndetse no gutuka Yahweh ari we Mwuka Wera, bashaka kwihindura bava mu ishusho ya Yahweh baremwemo mbere. Ku bw'ibyo, mwange imigani yabo y'Abayezuwiti yahinduye ubumana nyabwo mu migani yazanywe na Nimurodi n'ubutatu bwe bwa mbere butari bwera! Kuko ubutatu bwera bwa Data, Umwana, n'umwuka wera bwangijwe buvanwa ku busobanuro bwabwo bw'umwimerere n'intego, nyamara ukuri kwa Yahweh ntigushobora guhishwa n'ibintu byoroshye kandi bikennye nubwo babeshya! Nyamara, Yesu w'i Nazareti ni Yahweh wigize umuntu, kandi umwuka wanjye wera w'ubwana wuzuza byuzuye abizerwa muri Kristo bari mu by'ukuri igice cy'ubumana mu gutegeka kwabo na Kristo Yesu munsi y'Itegeko rya Melikisedeki nk'Abatambyi n'Abami iteka ryose! Ku bw'ibyo, mumenye abo muri bo muri Kristo bana banjye kuko abaguye bashaka guhisha ukuri kuberekeyeho binyuze mu migani yabo y'ibinyoma.

Nyamara, abizerwa muri Kristo barabayeho kandi barapfa kandi baracyagenda ku maso y'isi mu kwizera gukomeye, kandi iki gihe gitangaje kandi cy'icyubahiro ntikizabatungura mu bujiji kuko bazumva kuvuga k'urwo rusaku rukomeye, kandi bazumva ijwi ry'impanda ya nyuma yamaze gutangwa mu ntoki n'amategeko yuzuye yo kuvuga ubwo itegeko rizatangwa na Kristo Yesu, ukora buri gihe ubushake bwa Adonai Yahweh mu buryo butunganye. Abizerwa muri Kristo bazemererwa kwinjira muri urwo rugi runini rwinjira mu ijuru nta muntu n'umwe ku isi ushobora gukinga cyangwa gukingura, baba basinziriye cyangwa bariho ku maso y'isi mu gihe cyo guhamagarwa kwabo!

Kandi amakuba akomeye azagera ku batizerwa batuye Yesu w'i Nazareti bafite umutima utizera, ndetse banga kubaho ubuzima bwo kwihana kuko bakundaga ibyaha byabo. Bizaba ko abahisemo gukomeza mu cyaha, abinyuze mu mitima yigometse kandi inangiye batuye Yesu nk'Umwami ariko bagakomeza mu mirimo ya kamere bazasigirwa umuriro w'utunganya kugira ngo kamere irimbuke, ndetse kugira ngo barokoke ibinezeza by'icyaha kandi barokoke kamere yabo yabatwaye mu rari rwayo. Kandi benshi bazaza ku gakiza binyuze mu kwambaza Yesu w'i Nazareti kugeza ku mwuka wabo wa nyuma, kuko byahoze ari ibisabwa ku bizerwa, ko umuntu asangwa ari uwizerwa kugeza ku mwuka we wa nyuma, niba atari umwe mu bake ugereranyije bazatwarwa ari bazima mu mutekano w'ijuru mu bihe byabo byo kwizera. Sela!

Kandi intambara yabo izarota kandi ikomeze mu gihe cyayo cyagenwe kandi mu ivu ry'irimbuka ryabo bazazamuka. Igongwe, inyamaswa iva mu nyanja n'inyamaswa iva mu isi ari na we muhanuzi wabo w'ibinyoma bazazamuka mu gutuka Imana gukomeye kugira ngo bagire ubutegetsi bwabo bw'ubugome ariko bugufi ku bw'imbabazi. Nyamara, Yahweh, NDI UWONDI Ukomeye, ntiyigeze yisiga adafite ubuhamya, nk'uko bihamywa n'ingabo y'abahamya mu nyandiko zanjye z'iteka kandi zera zarinzwe mu buryo butunganye mu ijuru. Kandi nk'uko bimeze, byinshi mu buhamya bwanjye byatakaye mu bihe no kwirengagizwa n'abahawe inshingano yo kubyitaho. Mwumve ibihe bana banjye kandi mwange ibinyoma byabo by'ibihe by'inyiganano. Mwumve ko irema ritamaze imyaka za miriyoni nk'uko ibinyoma by'abaguye bivuga. Irema rimaze imyaka iri munsi y'ibihumbi bitandatu kandi nagabanyije ibi bihe mu myaka. Habayeho igihe cy'akaduruvayo ugereranyije kubera abaguye banduje isi yariho icyo gihe n'ibizira byabo, ndetse ibyo bizira byarimbuwe byuzuye mu rubanza rwasojwe n'umwuzure ukomeye mu minsi ya Nowa.

Nyamara, Noha yakomeje kubaho mu gihe cy'umwuzure, ndetse arokoka ari muzima mu Nkuge hamwe n'urubyaro rwe (n'inyamaswa n'ibindi byaremwe) byahawe umwanya wo gukomeza kubaho kuko Noha yari umukiranutsi kandi umutegetsi ukiranuka ukurikije Itegeko rya Melikisedeki, uwo umwambaro wari ku ntugu ze kugeza igihe umugaragu wanjye agiye mu kiruhuko cye cy'amahoro kugeza akangutse vuba aha mu gukiranuka. Mu gihe cyagenwe Mose yakuwe muzima mu mazi ya Nili, kandi yazamutse mu mbaraga nyinshi atangiza igihe cyanjye cya Tora, ndetse n'igihe Isirayeli yagirwaga ishyanga rikomeye kandi ry'amaboko ari ryo gicucu cy'ibizaza.

Kandi mu gihe cyagenwe gitunganye kandi nyacyo Yesu w'i Nazareti yavutse ku isugi kandi arazamuka aba umwana w'intama w'Imana, ndetse yitanga ho igitambo cy'impongano y'iteka ku gihano cya Mose, ndetse igitambo nemeye iteka ryose ku bwo kubabarirwa ibyaha iteka ryose, ku bwejejwe na cyo bose, ndetse no mu Isezerano Rishya rye ryo gukiranuka Kristo Yesu yemeje kandi agashyiraho kashe iteka ryose mu maraso ye atagira icyaha, ku bw'ibyo, nta mpamvu ikiriho y'igitambo cy'inyamaswa. Mu by'ukuri, Yesu w'i Nazareti ni ishingiro ryo gukiranuka mu bihe byose, ndetse n'igihe cyahuriranye no gutsindwa kw'abaguye iteka ryose, ndetse n'igihe cyafashwe n'Amagambo y'iteka ngo "BIRARANGIYE!"

Bityo igihe cyanjye cy'ubuntu cyatangijwe kuri uwo munsi wa Pentekote ubwo nahaga Kristo Yesu umwuka wanjye wera w'Ubumwana uwo yasutse ku itegeko ryanjye ku bwo gucungurwa kw'abahitamo bose kuba abana b'Imana batanzwe n'umwuka w'Ubumwana muri ibi bihe. Kandi uko ni ko kuvuka kw'Itorero rya Kristo Yesu ryatangiwe ubuntu n'imbabazi ku banyamahanga, ndetse na mbere y'uko urubanza rukomeye kandi rwa nyuma rugwa. Ndetse n'ibihe by'imbabazi n'ubuntu aho imiryango yazimiye y'inzu ya Isirayeli ishobora gukoranirizwa muri Isirayeli y'Imana, hamwe n'umuntu wese wo mu mahanga wahamagawe kandi akitaba agira Yesu w'i Nazareti Umwami wabo w'iteka. Kandi abantu banjye batatanyijwe mu gihano kandi bazerereye isi nk'abashyitsi mu bihugu by'amahanga, n'abakomoka kuri Adamu bose bemeye agakiza muri ibi bihe bazaba iteka ryose igice cya Isirayeli y'Imana, ntibazigera bava mu mirimo yabo y'iteka bakorera Kristo Yesu Umwami wabo wazamutse!

Saba kumva rero ko isi ya none igeze ku iherezo ryayo ry'iteka kandi iyi si mbi ya none ntizigera yongera kubaho ngo ihangayikishe abo nkunda iteka ryose. Bityo igihe cyanjye cy'ubuntu gikomeza kugeza ku isegonda rya nyuma aho impanda ya nyuma y'igihe cy'ubuntu ivuga yamaze gutangwa ngo ivuzwe. Ku iherezo ry'amakuba ya Yakobo, ari ryo cyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli, ndetse icyumweru cy'imyaka aho buri munsi ari umwaka mu cyumweru cy'iminsi irindwi ni bwo Ubwami bw'Imyaka Igihumbi bwa Yeshua HaMashiach buzashyirwaho ku bwo umunsi w'ikiruhuko ku iherezo ryawo hazaba imyaka ibihumbi birindwi. Ku bw'ibyo, mwumve ko hazaba harabayeho ibihe bitatu by'imyaka ibihumbi bibiri buri kimwe, hamwe n'umunsi w'isabato w'imyaka igihumbi uzasohora mu iherezo ryera kandi rikiranuka aho ibyaremwe byanjye bicungurwa byuzuye mu byaha, ntibizongere gukora icyaha ukundi.

Saba kumva ibihe mubayemo, kuko amabanga n'inzira za Yahweh byatakaye igice kimwe kubera ibyaha by'abantu ku batuye ku isi, bihishwe ndetse n'ibishuko by'abaguye. Kalendari nahaye Enoki umwanditsi iyo abantu bagombaga kumenya neza iminsi, ibihe, n'ibihe by'umwaka yatakaye cyane kubera ukwirengagiza no kwigomeka. Amateka y'abantu yahinduwe mu kwirengagiza n'imigani y'ibinyoma. Inyandiko zera za kera zaratakaye igice kimwe ku isi kubera igihe n'icyaha, kandi mu by'ukuri nemeye ko ibi biba kubera ibyaha by'abantu banjye kugira ngo bagendere mu mwijima niba batakaje inyandiko zanjye zera kugeza bihannye ibyo byaha. Nyamara, Ijambo ryanjye ntirishobora kuvunwa kandi ryararinzwe ijambo ku ijambo mu ijuru, ndetse n'intego y'uko nk'uko biri mu ijuru ari ko bizaba ku isi! Sela!

Ku bw'ibyo, Jyewe, Yahweh, NDI WE WAMBERE UKOMEYE, ndatangaza kandi mmenyesha kuzamuka kw'abahamya banjye babiri, ndetse abantu ba kera bakuwe mu bantu mu bihe byabo kandi barinzwe ari bazima n'ukuboko kwanjye gukomeye. Ndetse n'ubwo bimeze bityo, kuko Yahweh, NDI WE WAMBERE UKOMEYE NTIYIGEZE yisiga adafite umuhamya, kandi nzongera nihe umuhamya utunganye ndetse no muri ibi bihe by'amakuba byiteguye vuba aha gukwira isi mu birimi by'umuriro w'urubanza.
