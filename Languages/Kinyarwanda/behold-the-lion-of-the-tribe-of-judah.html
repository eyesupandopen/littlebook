Ubuhamya Bwiriwe: Isirayeli
Ibisharira N'ibiryohereye
17 Ugushyingo 2024

Dore! Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda!

Zibura amatwi yanyu, kandi mundeke mfungure amaso yanyu, Yewe Isirayeli, kandi mukanguke mu buhumyi bwanyu bw'igice kandi mwumve ijwi rya Mesiya wanyu w'ukuri, ndetse n'Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda, kuko ari ko ndi. Bityo mwumve amagambo ya Yeshua HaMashiach, ndetse n'uwo mugomba kugirana na we, Yewe Isirayeli, kuko ari ko ndi.

Ni ukuri, ni ukuri, mwumve amagambo yanjye bavandimwe banjye kugira ngo musobanukirwe ibihe murimo kandi musobanukirwe ibyiringiro byo guhamagarwa kwanyu kugaruka mu gihugu cy'isezerano, kuko ndi ibyiringiro byo guhamagarwa kwanyu, kandi ndabahamagara, Yewe Isirayeli, kugira ngo musobanukirwe ibizaza ndetse n'umujinya ugiye gusukwa muri ibi bihe murimo. Kandi ndi Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda kandi mu gukiranuka ndaje guca urubanza no kurwana intambara, bityo musobanukirwe ubukana bw'Amagambo yanjye, ndetse n'Amagambo y'uwo mugomba kugirana na we kuko ari ko Yahweh yangize.

Kuko ndi rwa rutare rwo gusitara, ndetse n'ibuye rikomeye ryo gusitara aho abatari abizerwa bose bazasitara, kuko abakiranutsi batakibeshwaho na Mose ahubwo abakiranutsi bazabeshwaho no kwizera! Kuko Mose aba muri Mesiya wanyu w'ukuri ndetse n'uwo witondeye amategeko adashoboka ko Isirayeli iyitondera, ndetse na Adamu wese kuyitondera kubera icyaha mu mubiri wanyu wangiritse. Kandi ni jye wishyuye igihano cya Mose ubwo nitangaga nk'umwana w'intama w'Imana kuri Yahweh mu gitambo cy'impongano cy'iteka kuko igihano cy'abanyabyaha kitari icyanjye ngo ncyishyure, nyamara mu rukundo rwanjye kuri Yahweh, n'urukundo rwanjye ku bantu nishyuye ibihano byanyu byose. Ndetse n'ibihano by'umubabaro mwinshi, agahinda, n'urupfu narabyishyuye byose kandi Yahweh yemeye igitambo cyanjye iteka ryose.

Bityo musobanukirwe, umujinya w'Umwana w'Intama ugiye gufungurwa kandi impanda ziteguye kuvuga, kandi dore, inzáo z'umujinya w'Imana ziregereje kandi ziri ku muryango wanyu, nuko rero, mwumve ijwi ryanjye kugira ngo mwe n'abana banyu mubone kubaho kandi murokoke umutego Baraba akora kugira ngo atege ubwoko bwanjye, ndetse n'umwana w'intama w'ikiyoka watoranyijwe na Esawu nka Moshiach wabo, akaba n'umwubatsi w'urusengero w'inyamaswa. Kandi azazamuka nk'umwana w'intama ufite amahembe abiri, kandi aje kugabanya ibihugu kurushaho kubatuye bafite uburenganzira birukanywe na jenoside yabo, kugira ngo abigabanye mu bimukira baturutse kuri Esawu biyitiriye ibitari ibyabo, n'abateye boherejwe n'ikiyoka ari bo bakomoka kuri Ashikenazi atari Yakobo ariko bakaba bifatanije na Esawu mu migenzo yabo ya Talimudi.

Kandi ni jye Yahweh yohereje nk'umwana w'intama w'Imana, ndetse nk'amasezerano y'amahoro atunganye, ndetse nka Pasika yanyu itunganye ndetse n'igitambo cya nyuma cy'ibyaha by'abantu rimwe, bose, n'iteka ryose kandi nta kindi gitambo cy'ibyaha gisigaye. Kandi naje mu banjye mu rukundo n'amahoro, ndetse n'abanze igitambo cyanjye, nyamara noherejwe nka Pasika itunganye mu kumvira ubushake bwa Yahweh, kandi ndi umwana w'intama w'Imana, uwo Yahweh yatanze mu buryo butunganye ku byaha byanyu, nuko rero mwumve ijwi ryanjye Yewe Isirayeli kandi musobanukirwe ko ntazongera kuza ku isi nk'umwana w'intama w'Imana, kandi amahoro agiye gukurwa ku isi mu rubanza rwaje. Ndetse urwo rubanza rugiye gusukwa gitungura mbere na mbere ku nzu y'Imana kuko urubanza rutangirira ku nzu y'Imana.

Kuko abizerwa muri Kristo bazatwarwa mu ijuru, naho abakazuyazi bazasigirwa umuriro w'utunganya kugira ngo umubiri urimbuke, ndetse kugira ngo umwuka urokoke ariko nk'ukuwe mu muriro. Kandi abantu benshi cyane bazaza kuri Yesu w'i Nazareti baturutse mu mahanga yose n'imiryango n'indimi kandi nzabeza mu gukiranuka kandi nzabakira iwanjye kandi nzabarema bundi bushya mu gukiranuka no kwera kw'ukuri kandi ingororano yabo nta kigereranyo ifite n'imibabaro bazihanganira, kuko uburemere bw'ingororano yabo buzaruta kure ibyo abantu biteze kandi iteka ryose bazabaho mu mahoro atunganye no kwera no gukiranuka kw'ukuri.

Kuko byanditswe kuri bo kandi uko ni ko bizaba: Aba ni bo bavuye mu makuba akomeye, kandi bameshe amakanzu yabo maremare, bayejesha amaraso y'Umwana w'Intama. Ni cyo gituma bari imbere y'intebe y'Imana, kandi bayikorera ku manywa na nijoro mu Rusengero rwayo, kandi uwicaye ku ntebe azabana na bo. Ntibazongera gusonza, cyangwa kugira inyota ukundi, kandi izuba ntirizabavaho, cyangwa icyokere cyose. Kuko Umwana w'Intama, uri hagati y'intebe, azabayobora, kandi azabayobora ku masoko y'amazi y'ubugingo, kandi Imana izahanagura amarira yose ku maso yabo.

Bityo musobanukirwe ko ari jye warangije imirimo yanjye mu banyamahanga kandi ibihe byanjye by'ubuntu n'imbabazi byarangiye, nubwo ubuntu n'imbabazi biri kumwe nanjye iteka, kandi Yahweh ubwe yatangaje ko azagirira imbabazi uwo azagirira imbabazi, bityo ubuntu n'imbabazi bihoraho iteka ryose. Ni ukuri, ni ukuri, nagiye mu banyamahanga ndetse nk'umunyagihugu wanzwe n'abe batwawe na Esawu mu bubata bw'imigenzo ye yo gutuka Imana aho bashaka konona Amagambo y'iteka n'iteka ryose ya Yahweh, uko konona kukaba kudashoboka. Nyamara bihimbiye imyizerere yabo y'ikuzimu mu maboko y'abanditsi babo bashyize imigenzo y'abantu babi mu gitabo bita ko cyera, ariko se igitabo cyo gutuka Imana gishobora gite kuba cyera? Ni ukuri, ni ukuri, ishyano ni iry'abita umwijima umucyo n'umucyo umwijima, kuko urubanza rwabo rutazatinda!

Kandi ibihe byanjye mu banyamahanga byarangiye kuko baguye mu byaha bimwe ba So baguyemo ubwo bangaga umwana w'intama w'Imana uwo Yahweh yaboherereje batabikwiye nk'amasezerano y'amahoro y'iteka ku mahanga, abo umukumbi munini waje mu gakiza. Kandi noneho abashoboraga kuba abana b'isezerano mu Isirayeli y'Imana biraririye ku mashami y'umwimerere ndetse n'igiti cy'Umunzenze cy'umwimerere bometseho mu mwanya w'amashami y'umwimerere yaciwe muri uko kutizera.

Nyamara bakuwe ku giti cy'umunzenze w'ishyamba, ndetse igiti kidafite ibyiringiro cyangwa isezerano kandi muri abo baje kuri jye bakangira Umwami wabo w'iteka w'ugukiranuka mu bihe by'amahoro, ubuntu, n'imbabazi ubu bararezwe mu Isirayeli y'Imana kandi bafite uruhare rwuzuye mu masezerano yahawe ba So kandi benshi muri aba bazankorera iteka nk'umubiri wanjye kandi nzabatwara iwanjye nk'umugeni wanjye kandi nzashyiraho ikimenyetso ku 144,000 banjye biyejesheje amaraso yanjye y'impongano bera nk'amasugi atandujwe na maraya ukomeye. Kandi binyuze mu gakiza k'abanyamahanga amahanga yahawe umugisha kandi koko abo mu miryango yazimiye bumviye ijwi ryanjye baje mu gakiza mu Mwami wabo kandi bazabaho iteka mu miryango mu Isirayeli y'Imana! Bityo musobanukirwe, nziza bamwe muri bose bo muri Adamu aho yaba yarazerereye hose ku isi mu minsi ye yo guhanwa.

Kuko Yahweh yangize umwuka utanga ubuzima, kandi nyuma y'umubiri sinzwi ukundi. Ni ukuri, ni ukuri, ndi uwo ubu waciye igiti cy'umunzenze w'ishyamba kubera kutizera kwabo kandi kuko bishe isezerano ryanjye, ndetse n'Isezerano Rishya ryanjye. Kandi ibyo ni ukubera ko bagomba kugira Umwami umwe, ari we jye, Kristo Yesu Umwami w'abami n'Umutware w'abatware kandi mu butware bwanjye ni ho hari ibyiringiro byabo byonyine. Nyamara bihitiyemo ibyiringiro by'ibinyoma muri Baraba kandi mu bujiji bashyira ibyiringiro byabo mu mwami w'ikinyoma wateye Amatorero yabo n'ibyiringiro by'ibinyoma by'ubutunzi bw'iby'isi, ndetse n'Umukiza w'ikinyoma usezeranya gukiza Babuloni y'Ibanga ariko mu ibanga akorera irimbuka rya Babuloni y'Ibanga. Ndetse n'uwo mwiyemezi ari we nyamaswa yo mu isi izazamuka nka Moshiach w'ikinyoma wa Esawu, ndetse n'umwana w'intama w'ikiyoka uzazamuka afite amahembe abiri. Kandi bityo Itorero ryanjye rya Lawodikaya ryinjiwemo ryarayobye kandi ubu riri kwemera urupfu rwaryo, kuko Baraba azana urupfu gusa, kandi azarimbura rwose kandi atsembe uwo maraya ukomeye, ndetse Babuloni y'Ibanga, uwo urubanza rwe rwaje kubera ububi bwe bukabije n'ubwiyandarike bwe yandurishije isi mu busambanyi bwe.

Bityo bahisemo mu bwigomeke ndetse n'uzana irimbuka ryabo nk'umukiza wabo w'iyobera, kuko Isinagogi ya Satani ari abiyemezi bose biyoberanya. Bityo mwige, Yewe Isirayeli, icyo Baraba agiye gukora kuko yabagurishije mu maboko y'abanzi babo, kandi azihimbira igikomere cye cyo mu mutwe yihishe mu isi mu makuba agiye gutera. Kandi azazamuka mu buriganya bukomeye nka Moshiach w'ikinyoma kuri Isirayeli kuko bazagira intambara yabo, ndetse intambara yabo ya gatatu nk'iyo, kandi izaba imbi cyane muri izo uko ari eshatu. Ndetse bityo, kuko ari ko byanditswe kandi uko ni ko bizakorwa: Kandi nabonye inyamaswa izamuka iva mu nyanja, ifite imitwe irindwi, n'amahembe icumi, kandi ku mahembe yayo hari amakamba icumi, kandi ku mutwe wayo izina ryo gutuka Imana. Kandi inyamaswa nabonye yasaga n'ingwe, n'amaguru yayo nk'ay'idubu, n'akanwa kayo nk'akanwa k'Intare: kandi ikiyoka kiyihaho imbaraga zacyo n'intebe yacyo y'ubwami, n'ubutware bukomeye. Kandi nabonye umwe mu mitwe yayo nk'ukomeretse bikomeye kugeza ku gupfa, ariko igikomere cyayo cyica cyarakize, kandi isi yose itangazwa n'iyo nyamaswa iyikurikira.

Kandi uko ni ko amagambo mu byanditswe ahishura ubugambanyi bwe, ndetse n'Amagambo nahaye Yohana Uhishurirwa muri iyi yubile nyinshi zashize. Kandi bizagaragara ko Baraba azabona igikomere cyo mu mutwe, ariko azabeshya kandi ashuke isi izizera byimazeyo ibimenyetso bye by'ibinyoma n'ibitangaza kuko yabihawe kugira ngo yikururire abishimira gukiranirwa bose mu buyobe bukomeye, kuko ari we mwanduza wabo n'umurimbuzi wabo. Kandi mu maboko ye azana kurumwa n'inzoka, ndetse n'urwo rukingo rw'inyamaswa, kandi bazavanga icyuma n'ibumba nubwo bitazafatana, nyamara uko ni ko umwana w'intama w'ikiyoka azazamuka. Kandi azazamuka hejuru ya gahunda ya nyuma y'inyamaswa y'uwaguye, izaba ubwami bwabo bwa nyuma bwo gutegeka isi baturutse mu gihugu cy'isezerano. Nyamara ntazazamuka wenyine, ahubwo azazamuka hamwe n'inyamaswa yo mu nyanja, kandi bazategeka ihuriro ryabo ry'abami bahaye ububasha bwabo bwose n'ubutware ikiyoka kuko bose basenga ikiyoka kandi ni cyo biyeguriye byimazeyo kuko ari Data wabo, ndetse n'uwabibye urumamfu rwe rubi mu ngano za Yahweh, kandi nzabarandura rwose mbajugunye mu muriro.

Kandi uko ni ko umwana w'intama w'ikiyoka azazamuka mu buriganya bwuzuye kandi ibimenyetso byinshi by'ibinyoma n'ibitangaza azabyerekana kugira ngo yemeze ubuhanuzi bwe bw'ibinyoma, kandi azivugaho ibintu byinshi byo kwirata no gutuka Imana ndetse yiyita ko we ubwe ari Imana. Kandi inyamaswa imwe izatunga urutoki ku yindi kandi bazagira ubutegetsi bufatanyije, ndetse hakurikijwe imigambi y'ikiyoka azategeksha ubwami bwe bubi igihe gito. Kandi bazagira ibintu byose bya Gikristo bitemewe n'amategeko, kandi bazaca inyandiko zera, kandi bahawe mu maboko yabo kurwanya abera no kubanesha, kandi uko ni ko bazabikora. Nyamara bazahuza amadini y'isi kugira ngo bose basenge kibe zabo z'umukara kuko ku birenge bya Satani biyunze mu bwigomeke bwa nyuma kandi ntibazacika ukuboko kwanjye gukomeye, niko Mesiya wanyu w'iteka avuga, ndetse Yeshua HaMashiach!

Nyamara mu rwango rwabo ku bintu byose byerekeye Kristo Yesu bazatuma agakiza kaza ku mukumbi munini w'abo bazagira abahowe Imana, ndetse n'abazambaza izina rya Yesu w'i Nazareti kugeza ku mwuka wabo wa nyuma kandi nzabakomeza mbaha ibyiringiro bikomeye, kandi bazaza kuri jye byimazeyo, ndetse n'abaragwa bose b'agakiza k'iteka, kandi nta n'umwe ushobora kubakura mu maboko yanjye kandi abantu bose amazina yabo yanditswe mu gitabo cy'ubugingo cy'umwana w'intama bazakizwa. Bityo ntimuhangayikire abanyu bakundwa basa n'abazimiye, kuko benshi muri bo bari mu maboko yanjye akomeye kandi nzabazana mu gakiza muri Kristo Yesu kandi mu rurimi urwo ari rwo rwose bambaza izina ryanjye rikomeye nzabumva!

Nyamara, uko ni ko bizaba ko umwana w'intama w'ikinyoma ufite amahembe abiri azazamuka kugira ngo ategeke isi aturutse mu gihugu cy'isezerano Esawu ashaka kwiyitirira nk'icye, ariko ibyo yiyitirira ni ibinyoma kandi umurage wa Yakobo urakomeye iteka ryose, ndetse uwo murage Esawu yagurishije igice cy'umugati. Kuko Esawu yifatanije n'abaguye kandi yifatanije na Ashikenazi bafashe imigenzo n'imikorere by'Umunyakanani mubi nyamara barabeshya bakavuga ko ari aba Isirayeli kandi atari bo. Kuko ari Kanani wararikiye inzira z'abaguye nyuma y'umwuzure ukomeye, bityo ahindura urubyaro rwe iteka ryose mu kuvumwa kw'iteka kuko abihindura mu buryo bw'ingirabuzimafatizo bava mu ishusho y'Imana baremwe mu ishusho yayo nta mbabazi bafite kuko izi ari inzira za Satani Esawu yashyize mu gice mu Talimudi ye yo gutuka Imana aho uburiganya bwe bukomoka.

Kandi bityo Ashikenazi, ukomoka kuri Yafeti, yifatanije na Esawu mu kwiyitirira umurage w'ibinyoma, aho bashaka kwiba umurage wa Yakobo ku bana b'isezerano. Icyakora, imihati nk'iyo ni imfabusa kandi ntizahagarara kuko ndi uwo Yahweh yahaye ibintu byose mu maboko yanjye akomeye kandi ashoboye. Bityo musobanukirwe ko ndi uwarazwe ibintu byose byasezeranijwe Yakobo kandi ndi Mesiya wanyu w'ukuri, kandi mu maboko yanjye ni ho hari ingororano zanyu Yewe Isirayeli kandi mu maboko ya Baraba irimbuka ryanyu ry'iteka rirabategereje, nuko rero ntimwifatanye na we!

Bityo musobanukirwe ko ingororano z'isezerano zahawe ba So ba kera ziri mu maboko yanjye kandi nta n'umwe ushobora kwiba ibyo mfitiye uburenganzira kuko ari ko mbaraga nahawe na NDIHO ukomeye kandi nzaha uyu murage abana b'isezerano, ndetse n'abakiranutsi babeshwaho no kwizera Umwana wa Yahweh, uwabakunze akitanga ku bw'agakiza k'iteka k'abana b'isezerano, nuko rero Yewe Isirayeli mnemere nka Mesiya wanyu kugira ngo mwe n'abana banyu mubone kubaho kandi mugubwe neza mu nzira. Kandi nzabarinda mbabungabunge kugira ngo mugere ku mugabane wanyu wuzuye w'umurage mu bwami bwanjye bw'imyaka igihumbi buri ku muryango wanyu. Bityo mwumve guhamagara kwanjye, Yewe Isirayeli! Kuko ndabahamagara kugira ngo mugandukire ukuboko kwanjye gukomeye kugira ngo namwe mube abana b'isezerano kuko Mose arindwa byimazeyo no kunyizera, Yeshua HaMashiach, nuko rero, muruhukire mu mirimo yanjye kandi mureke iyanyu.

Kandi bityo Ashikenazi yifatanije na Esawu mu kwiyitirira kwe kw'ibinyoma, aho bashaka kwiba umurage wa Yakobo ku bana b'isezerano. Icyakora, imihati nk'iyo ni imfabusa kandi ntizahagarara kuko ndi uwo Yahweh yahaye ibintu byose mu maboko yanjye akomeye kandi ashoboye. Bityo musobanukirwe ko ndi uwarazwe ibintu byose byasezeranijwe Yakobo, kandi ndi Mesiya wanyu w'ukuri, kandi mu maboko yanjye ni ho hari ingororano yanyu Yewe Isirayeli kandi mu maboko ya Baraba irimbuka ryanyu rirabategereje. Nuko rero, musobanukirwe amahitamo mugomba gukora, n'uwo mugomba kugirana na we, kuko ndi uwo woherejwe na Yahweh ku bw'agakiza kanyu, ndetse n'uwo Mose yategetse ko mumwumvira, Yewe bavandimwe banjye batumvira.

Kandi bityo nk'uko byanditswe kuri bo kandi uko ni ko bizasohorezwa n'ukuboko kwanjye gukomeye, kuko Yahweh yahishuriye abahanuzi be ukuri kwa Esawu mu minsi y'ibyaha byabo byabaye byinshi. Kuko byanditswe kera: Kubera ubugome bwawe ku muvandimwe wawe Yakobo, isoni zizagutwikira, kandi uzacibwa iteka ryose. Ubwo wahagararaga hakurya, ku munsi abanyamahanga batwaraga ubutunzi bwe, n'abanyamahanga bakinjira mu marembo ye, bagakora ubufindo kuri Yerusalemu, ndetse nawe wari nk'umwe muri bo.

Nuko rero saba, Yewe Isirayeli, ko gutandukanywa kwa nyuma kwa Yakobo na Esawu kukugezeho, kandi nje kweza igihugu cy'isezerano mu maboko y'abaguye kandi Esawu azarandurwa burundu, kandi kuri Esawu byanditswe ngo: Kandi inzu ya Yakobo izaba umuriro, n'inzu ya Yosefu ibe ikirimi cy'umuriro, n'inzu ya Esawu ibe ibishingwe, kandi bazabikongeza babiconshomere: kandi nta gice cy'inzu ya Esawu kizasigara: kuko Uwiteka abivuze. Nuko rero ab'kwa Esawu basoma Amagambo yanjye basobanukirwe, icyo Yahweh yatangaje ntawe ushobora kugihindura kandi iherezo ryanyu rizaba irya burundu kandi muzarandurwa burundu ntimuzongera kubaho ukundi.

Kuko ndi Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda kandi mu gukiranuka nje guca urubanza no kurwana intambara. Nuko rero umva Amagambo yanjye Yewe Isirayeli kandi mwihishe umunsi ubagezeho. Mwihishe umuntu w'icyaha uza mu nzira yanyu, kuko Baraba aza afite urupfu rw'iteka mu maboko ye rutangwa nk'umuti w'igitangaza usezeranywa mu binyoma gutanga ubugingo buhoraho. Ariko ntibazagira ubuzima bwo kubaho mu rupfu rwa kabiri ahubwo kurimbuka guhoraho mu birimi by'umuriro bishyushye cyane by'igihano aho inyo yabo idapfa.

Ndi we uzamena ibimenyetso ku muzingo wanjye w'umurage, ndetse n'icyangombwa cy'isi niguze amaraso yanjye y'impongano mu kuboko kwa Yahweh ubwe. Kandi nje kurandura abigaruriye ibitari ibyabo n'abo bose abaguye bohereje, ndetse n'abaguye ubwabo n'ibihe byabo birarangiye. Nuko rero saba ibigiye kuza, Isirayeli, kuko bazashyiraho ubwami bwabo kandi bazagaragara nk'abatsinze kandi ibimenyetso n'ibitangaza byinshi by'ibinyoma bazabyerekana kugira ngo bayobye abishimira gukiranirwa bose. Kandi bazatekereza ko bashyizeho intangiriro y'igihe cyabo cya zahabu icyo bifuza gushyiraho, kuko bakomoka kuri Se umubisha, kandi umubisha ni icyo kiyoka kinini, ku bw'ibyo, irari ry'ikiyoka bazarikora.

Nyamara, ntibazagira igihe cya zahabu ahubwo urubanza rukomeye kandi rukarishye gusa, urwo rubanza ruzatangira vuba uko abizerwa banjye muri Kristo bakoranirizwa aho ndi mu bicu uko ngaragara ibyo isi izabona. Nyamara, bazabeshya babwire isi ko abanyamahanga b'abanzi bibye benshi bakabakura ku isi kandi bazagaruka kandi isi igomba kuba yiteguye kurwanya abo biyita abatera. Nyamara mu by'ukuri barimo kwitegura intambara y'uwo munsi ukomeye, ndetse n'umunsi wo kugaruka kwanjye kwa kabiri, ndetse n'umunsi inyoni zo mu kirere zizarya cyane inyama z'abami babo baguye bo ku isi kandi uko ni ko bizaba.

Kandi uko imanza zigwira, uko ibimenyetso bimenwa (abagendera ku mafarashi bazagenda) n'impanda zizavuga kandi bizaba ndetse nk'uko byanditswe na Yohana Uhishurirwa kandi no hakurikijwe Amagambo y'Ubutumwa Bwiza Buhoraho bwahishuwe mu gihe cyabwo kugira ngo Isirayeli yitegurire umunsi w'amakuba yabo kandi yitegurire umunsi w'agakiza kabo, udatinda. Kandi benshi bazatuka Imana yo mu ijuru kubera imanza zigiye kubageraho kandi benshi ntibazihana, kandi abenshi bazafata urukingo rwabo rwa kinyamaswa bivanze n'ikoranabuhanga badasobanukiwe, kuko ikoranabuhanga ryabo ari iryo kurimbura Adamu nuko rero mwihishe Yewe Isirayeli.

Kandi abahanuzi banjye babiri, ndetse n'ibitereko byanjye by'amatabaza bibiri bizahaguruka bihanure ibintu byinshi byahishwe mu mitima y'abantu kandi bazahishura byinshi byatakaye mu nyandiko za kera kandi ukuri kuzamenyekana, kandi abaguye bazashyirwa ahagaragara rwose, kandi Edomu izashyirwa ku mugaragaro aho Esawu yakomotse kandi abishyize hamwe bose mu ihuriro bazashyirwa ahagaragara kandi ukuri kuzamenyekana. Kandi bazatangaza Ubutumwa Bwiza Buhoraho kandi ubuhamya bwabo buzongerwa mu nyandiko zera iteka ryose n'iteka ryose. Kandi nyamara ndetse no mu bihe by'urubanza ubuntu bwanjye butegeka mu buryo bwuzuye, kuko aho kuba ubutabera bwihuse bwaroha igihugu cy'abazima, ubuntu n'umwanya wo kwihana bizatangwa mbere y'uko urubanza rwa nyuma rugenwa ngo rugwe. Ku bw'ibyo, benshi bazaza ku Mwami wabo Uhoraho wo Gukiranuka kandi nzabakira iwanjye mbabike iteka ryose ku bw'imigambi ihoraho ya Yahweh iyo bakirijwe kugira ngo bayisohoze.

Kandi nibamara gutanga ubuhamya bwabo, inyamaswa izamuka iva mu kuzimu, izabarwanya, kandi izabanesha, ibice, kandi imibiri yabo izaryama ipfuye mu mihanda iminsi itatu n'igice. Icyo gihe nikirangira bazazukira ubuzima bushya uko bumva iryo jwi rikomeye riva mu ijuru rizavuga imbere y'abari aho bose ngo Zamuka hano! Kandi bazazurwa batwarwe mu ijuru bose bareba.

Kandi uko urusengero rwuzura umwana w'intama w'ikiyoka azagenda yegurire urusengero kandi nyamara azashyira ikizira cy'umurimbuzi ahera kandi atume ituro rihagarara hagati mu cyumweru aho aziyita Imana yihinduke abantu. Kandi amaraso azatemba cyane, kandi abanze Amagambo yanjye bakifatanya na Baraba, ndetse na benshi cyane muri bo bazicwa ndetse nk'uko byanditswe ngo: Kandi Marayika atembagaza umuhoro we utyaye ku isi, atema imizabibu y'urutoki rw'isi, ayijugunya mu rwengero runini rw'umujinya w'Imana. Kandi urwengero rwarasibanganyijwe inyuma y'umujyi, amaraso ava mu rwengero, agera ku mikoba y'amafarashi, mu ntera ya sitadiyo igihumbi na magana atandatu.

Nuko rero saba umutego mutegurirwa Isirayeli, kandi muve mu nzira za Esawu mujye mu maboko ya Mesiya wanyu w'ukuri kandi nzisigariza abasigaye bazima. Kandi nuko bizaba ko nzaza ku isi ku Musozi Siyoni ndi kumwe n'abanjye 144,000 kandi nzabayobora ahantu mu butayu hateguwe na Yahweh aho muzitabwaho kandi mukarindwa kuba bazima mu mujinya w'uwo munsi ukomeye. Kuko bizaba ndetse nk'uko byanditswe ngo: Abapfa bapfira mu Mwami, barahirwa rwose. Nuko Umwuka aravuga ati: kuko baruhuka imirimo yabo, kandi imirimo yabo irabakurikira.

Kandi uko umukumbi wanjye utwarwa mu murima wabo w'umutekano, umujinya wuzuye w'uburakari bw'Imana uzasukwa wose ku bwami bw'inyamaswa ndetse n'ababa ku isi bose mu gukiranirwa kandi bizaba ndetse nk'uko byanditswe n'umubabaro ukomeye, n'ingaruka mbi zizagwa ku bihisemo kwirwanya n'Intare yo mu Muryango wa Yuda kandi igihano gikomeye n'umubabaro bazabyihanganira kubera inzabya z'umujinya zizasukwa ku isi kandi uko ni ko bizaba.

Nuko rero saba uwo ndi we, Yewe Isirayeli, kuko mutakizi Yahweh, cyangwa ngo mukurikire inzira ze ahubwo musenga mutabizi imana z'ibinyoma ba So batari bazi, ndetse no mu nzira za Esawu wifatanyije n'abaguye n'ihuriro ryishyize hamwe rikikije igitabo cyabo cy'ibinyoma kandi gituka Imana cyane aho bashyizeho amategeko y'imigenzo y'abantu hejuru y'ukuri kw'Amagambo ahoraho ya Yahweh yo gukiranuka bityo basenga kandi bakorera icyaremwe aho gukorera umuremyi, uhimbazwa iteka ryose Yego na Amen!

Ntiwagombaga kwinjira mu irembo ry'abantu banjye ku munsi wo kurimbuka kwabo, cyangwa ngo urebe amakuba yabo ku munsi wo kurimbuka kwabo, cyangwa ngo urambike amaboko ku mutungo wabo ku munsi wo kurimbuka kwabo. Cyangwa ngo uhagarare mu mayirabiri kugira ngo ubace, abagombaga gucika, cyangwa ngo ukingirane abasigaye babo ku munsi w'amakuba. Kuko umunsi w'Umwami URI HANO, no ku banyamahanga bose: nk'uko wakoze, ni ko uzakorwa: ingororano yawe izagaruka ku mutwe wawe.
