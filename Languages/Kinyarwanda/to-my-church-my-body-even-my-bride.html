Ubuhamya Bubiri: Itorero
Mu Mirimo y'Intumwa Pawulo
13 Gicurasi 2024

Ku Itorero ryanjye, Umubiri wanjye, Ndetse n'Umugeni wanjye: Nimwumve Amagambo y'Umwami wanyu!

Ku Itorero ryanjye, Umubiri wanjye, ndetse n'Umugeni wanjye: Ndabasaba gutega amatwi ubwenge bwanjye kandi mwumve ijwi ryanjye, kuko ngiye kuvugana namwe. Ese muranyumva? Nimushyire ku ruhande inyigisho zanyu zinyuranye z'abantu n'ibyo mwita tewolojiya, kandi mwerekeze imitima yanyu ku Byanditswe Byera, ndetse n'ijambo ryanjye ritunganye. Nimushyire ku ruhande inyigisho zanyu zirimo umusemburo, kuko kuvanga Amagambo y'Imana, ndetse n'Ijambo ry'Imana ritunganye, bisembura irobe ryose ry'ifu kandi ntimuba mugifite ibyanditswe. Kuko ndi Ijambo ry'Imana ryigize umubiri, kandi ntuye muri mwe, kandi ndabahamagarira mu rugo rwanyu mu ijuru vuba cyane.

Nimushyire ku ruhande ibitekerezo byanyu, kandi mureke kumva amajwi menshi y'ibinyoma, ndetse n'amajwi atari ayanjye; ahubwo, mugumane Amagambo yanjye mu mitima yanyu kandi muyagereranye n'ibyanditswe kugira ngo mubone kwizera Amagambo yanjye no kunyizera, Jyewe Kristo Yesu. Kuko niba umuntu aje aho muri ntavuge inyigisho z'Umwami, ndetse n'Amagambo y'Imana atunganye, ntimukamwifurize na kabiri keza. Kuko nzabaha ubwenge mu Byanditswe Byera byanjye, ndetse n'Ijambo ry'ubugingo, kandi ubwenge bwanjye ntibwangirika. Nuko rero mwirundanyirize ubutunzi mu ijuru, aho inyenzi n'ingese bitaburya, n'abajura batamena ngo bibemo. Kuko aho ubutunzi bwanyu buri, ari ho n'imitima yanyu izaba. Bana banjye, ndi ubutunzi bwanyu, nuko rero mugumane ibitekerezo byanyu n'imitima yanyu kuri jye gusa mu mutima umwe.

Ntimwasomye Amagambo ahishura iri banga rikomeye ry'Imana? Kuko dufite ubu butunzi mu nzabya z'ibumba kugira ngo imbaraga zikomeye zibonekemo zibe iz'Imana, zitari izacu. Abo Imana yashatse kumenyesha ubutunzi bw'ubwiza bw'iri banga mu banyamahanga, ari ryo Kristo uri muri mwe, ibyiringiro by'ubwiza. Ntimutinye rero, kuko mwabyawe ubwa kabiri n'umwuka wanjye wera; ku bw'ibyo, nimwumve Amagambo y'Ijambo ryanjye. Kuko nzababwira ubwenge buzabahumuriza kandi bukabakuramo kuyoba gukomeye kwabaye mu isi; kuko isi ibohewe urubanza kandi iri mu bwoba n'umubabaro mwinshi.

Ku bw'ibyo, nje kuvugana namwe kugira ngo mpamye mu banyabwenge banyu iby'abayobozi babiri b'isi ubu bagaragara nk'abungeri beza, ndetse n'intama zatoranyijwe mu mukumbi kugira ngo zikore imirimo ya leta, bagaragara nk'abaje kuragira intama zazimiye za Babuloni y'Ibanga ngo bazigeze aheza. Ariko aba bungeri babiri barwaye ibishishi si intama, ahubwo ni inyamaswa mbi ziyoberanyije nk'intama kandi zikomoka mu rundi rugo, ndetse n'urw'ikigongwe cy'umushumba ubwacyo. Ikigongwe cy'umushumba si umushumba w'ukuri w'intama, kuko umukumbi wacyo atari umukumbi ahubwo ni umutwe w'abaguye bavuye mu bwiza bakaba ibintu byose byanduye bikwiriye kurimbuka. Ndavuga ku kinyoma cy'ubu kirimo gukoreshwa mu gutegura inzira y'izamuka ry'abatagatifu batatu. Kuko hariho batatu bahamya mu ijuru, Data, Ijambo, n'Umwuka Wera: kandi aba batatu ni umwe. Kandi hariho abatagatifu batatu bazamuka (ndetse n'igishushanyo gikomeye cya ba batatu bahamya mu ijuru): ikigongwe, inyamaswa, n'umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma (na we akaba inyamaswa). Kandi izi nyamaswa z'inkazi zaremewe kurimbuka, kuko ejo hazaza hazo ari umuriro waka n'amazuku mu nyanja nini y'umuriro yaka ibirimi byera by'ubururu mu mwijima w'icuraburindi iteka ryose.

Ndavuga ku mimerere yabo y'ubu n'ubutegetsi bwabo bw'ubu, kuko batarahishurwa byuzuye, kuko ubuza aracyahari; ariko nzabamenyesha abizerwa banjye, ndetse n'abafite amaso yo kureba n'amatwi yo kumva. Mwumve ko aba bayobya bakora ikinyoma gikomeye na mbere y'uko bahishurwa n'imbaraga zabo z'ukuri, kuko bazahabwa ubutware n'ikigongwe; kuko abuhabwa igihe gito cyane kandi mu buryo bufite imipaka kugira ngo agerageze isi, kandi arwanye abera n'ukubanesha. Kandi nubwo ibanga ryo gukiranirwa rikora cyane ku bw'izi ntego, guhishurwa kwabo ntikuragera ariko kuri hafi.

Kuko n'ubu bagaragara nk'abagabo bakomeye, abagabo b'ineza, abagabo b'ubutwari n'abagiraneza, babaho ubuzima bwo gukorera abandi batizigamye. Ariko ntimukayobywe n'abungeri barwaye ibishishi, kuko bagaragara nk'abasukuye kandi batunganye ku bantu benshi bazabatangariera kandi vuba aha bakazavuga bati: Ni nde uhwanye n'inyamaswa? (Kuko n'ubu bayobya abazimiye, batwawe n'uburozi bwabo.) Ariko ku bafite amaso yo kureba n'amatwi yo kumva, bagaragara uko bari by'ukuri: kuko ari abungeri barwaye ibishishi bahabwa imbaraga zabo n'ikigongwe gikomeye, ndetse na Satani ubwe. Kuko umwe azazamuka ava mu nyanja kandi ubu akorera mu ibanga (inyuma y'ishusho y'umusaza washyizweho) kugira ngo arimbure Babuloni y'Ibanga; naho undi azazamuka ava mu isi kandi yoherejwe mu binyoma ku bari mu Gihugu cy'Isezerano kandi azaba umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma w'isinagogi ya Satani, ndetse na Moshiach wabo w'ibinyoma.

Kuko umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma w'inyamaswa afatanyije n'inyamaswa, itegeka mu ibanga, kandi bombi bakorera hamwe kugira ngo bateze imbere ibanga ryo gukiranirwa, kugira ngo vuba aha bahishurwe; kuko ibihe byabo byageze, nubwo bataragira ubwami. Ntimugwe mu buyobe, kuko umushumba umwe urwaye ibishishi utegeka mu ibanga akoresha umusaza nk'igipupe, mu gihe undi mushumba urwaye ibishishi ugaragara nk'urenganywa n'umusaza akorana bya hafi n'umushumba urwaye ibishishi utegeka mu ibanga. Kuko biyoberanya nk'abanzi muri iki gihe, ariko bose bakomoka mu mukumbi umwe urwaye ibishishi, basohoza imigambi y'ikigongwe.

Ikibabaje ni uko benshi bibwira ko bakijijwe no kwatura batizera ko Yesu ari Umwami bava mu mutima utizera ku minwa y'ukutizera (kuko umutima ari wo umuntu yizeza akabarwaho gukiranuka) batwawe n'aba badayimoni, kandi bazayobya n'intore zanjye niba bishoboka, ndetse n'ababakurikira mu bujiji. Kuko aba bungeri badatunganye, kandi imyitero yabo ikozwe mu bwoya bwanduye kandi burwaye, kuko ari abungeri barwaye ibishishi baragira umukumbi urwaye ibishishi, kandi muzabamenyera ku mbuto zabo.

Si abera kandi ntibera de nk'urubura, nk'intama zanjye; kuko ndi Umwana w'intama utagira inenge cyangwa ikizinga, watoranyijwe mu mukumbi kugira ngo abe Pasika itunganye y'ibihe byose. Nsukura intama zanjye mu maraso yanjye y'impongano, kandi nta bishishi bibonekamo. Nubwo bavuga ko boherejwe n'Imana Ishoborabyose, Imana Ishoborabyose ntiyabohereje. Nubwo abahanuzi benshi b'ibinyoma bamuhamya ibinyoma, sinamwohereje, ahubwo yoherejwe n'ikigongwe. Kuko ndi umutambyi mukuru iteka ryose mu buryo bwa Melikisedeki, kandi nta yindi myana y'intama iriho uretse jyewe, Kristo Yesu.

Agakiza kari mu izina ryanjye gusa, kuko nta rindi zina ryatanzwe mu bantu abantu bakizirwamo uretse iryanjye, Umwami Yesu. Kuko nta muntu n'umwe nteganya ko yakira Amagambo yanjye utazayumva. Niba bakira amagambo yanjye mu kwizera cyangwa niba bayanga batizera ni amahitamo Data yahaye buri wese gukora, kuko Imana itihatira cyangwa ngo ihatire urukundo rwayo ku muntu n'umwe. Kandi nje gusohoza urubanza kuri bose no gucira urubanza abatubaha Imana bose muri bo, ku mirimo yabo yose yo kutubaha Imana bakoze mu buryo bwo kutubaha Imana, no ku magambo yabo yose akomeye, abanyabyaha batubaha Imana bamuvuzeho.

Navuganye namwe mu buryo bwinshi, kandi mu mirimo yanjye myinshi nohereje abahanuzi banjye n'abakozi banjye, kuko igihe cyarangiye. Nuko rero nzavuga ibintu bitahamijwe ku bihe byashize ariko ubu bikaba bihishuwe, kuko ibihe byo guhishurwa kwanjye byageze. Kuko ngiye guhishurwa mvuye mu ijuru, ndetse na jyewe, kuko ndi Mesiya w'ukuri, ndetse Yeshua HaMashiach. Kandi nzavugira mu migani ku bari hanze, ariko abafite amaso yo kureba n'amatwi yo kumva bazasobanukirwa, kuko nzabihamya mu mwuka wanjye waremwe muri bo.

Nje kuvuga amagambo Yohana Uhishurirwa yahawe, kandi nzabasobanurira inzira neza niba mwakira Amagambo yanjye kandi mugacisha bugufi imitima yanyu ku ijwi ryanjye.

Dore! Ndavuga iby'abayobozi babiri bazaza bakorana, kandi bazayobya Isi bajyane benshi mu muriro w'ikuzimu. Ndetse n'abataranditswe mu Gitabo cy'Ubugingo bazabakurikira kandi bazakubitwa n' kurumwa n'inzoka, ibyo isi yaburiwe, ndetse n'ikimenyetso nta muntu numwe uzakira; kuko imiburo ari iya nyuma, kandi ibyo Imana yatangaje ntibishoboka kubihindura.

Aba bayobozi, ndetse n'abungeri barwaye ibishishi, ubu biyoberanya nk'abakozi ba leta ba Babuloni y'Ibanga. Kuko munsi y'imyitero y'abungeri barwaye ibishishi bambara (kuko buri wese yiyoberanya mu buryo bwe), ni inyamaswa z'inkazi irari ryazo ridashobora guhaga, kandi bazasaba bose kubaramya n'ishusho y'inyamaswa n'ikigongwe nk'aho ari Imana; kuko bagiye kurekurwa igihe ubuza azaba akuweho.

Kuko vuba cyane kandi mu buryo butunguranye, ubwo nzateranyiriza Itorero ryanjye kuri jye mu bicu, kurimbuka gutunguranye kuzagera ku isi; kuko bazarimbura Babuloni y'Ibanga, kandi intambara ikomeye izaduka mu gihugu kandi kurimbuka gutunguranye kuzagera ku isi. Abatagatifu batatu bazazamuka bava mu ivu ry'amatongo n'akaduruvayo byasizwe n'abagendera ku mafarashi, bagiye kurekurwa nyuma y'uko Umugeni wanjye atwawe kuri jye mu ijuru. (Kuko icyo gihe ari bwo nzafungura ibimenyetso by'isezerano ryanjye kuko Itorero ryanjye rikizwa umujinya uzaza, kandi nzabatabara.) Bazazamuka bava mu ivu ry'intambara, ibyorezo, inzara, n'akarengane gakomeye, no mu mpfu z'abantu benshi cyane.

Bazatanga amahoro n'umutekano, kandi bazavuga ibinyoma byinshi bazahindura ubwenge bw'Imana, kandi bazavuga ibintu byinshi bituka Imana kandi bivumwe; kandi umwe azatunga urutoki undi, kandi mu ntoki zabo hazaba hari igisubizo cyabo, ndetse n'uburozi bwabo budashobora gukurwaho cyangwa kubabarirwa. Kuko buri wese witanga ku kurumwa n'inzoka, azashyirwaho ikimenyetso iteka ryose. Ariko icyo ni cyo kizaba ikiguzi cyo kwinjira muri sosiyete yabo nshya, ndetse n'itegeko ryabo rishya ryakozwe na Satani n'abamarayika be bakorera urubyaro rwabo rwangwa mu bujiji.

Bazasezeranya sosiyete nshya itagira urupfu n'irimbuka bigiye kuza ku isi yose, ubwo kurimbuka gutunguranye kuzaza mu ikoraniro ry'Itorero ryanjye. (Bashaka kongera gukongeza igihe cyabo cya zahabu Imana yarimbuye mu mwuzure wa Nowa.) Bazatanga amahoro, kuramba, kutagira indwara, ubuzima n'umutekano, kandi bazasezeranya umunsi mushya. Ariko aba bungeri babiri barwaye ibishishi (bagaragara nk'aho baturuka mu mukumbi) bazavuga ko Imana Ishoborabyose yabohereje, kandi isi yose izabatangarira, ariko si abungeri. Ndababaza, ese ikigongwe gishobora kuragira umukumbi? Ni ryari ikigongwe cyigeze kiragira intama, uretse gushyiraho kashe y'irimbuka ryazo ryuzuye no gutandukanywa na Se w'Imicyo, ndetse Yahweh? Kuko inyamaswa n'umuhanuzi w'ibinyoma bazahabwa imbaraga zabo n'ikigongwe, kandi ni abacakara b'irari n'ubushake by'ikigongwe.

Mu gihe cyo guhishurwa kwabo, bazajugunya kwiyoberanya kwabo nk'abakozi ba leta boroheje; n'ubwibone bwose, ndetse n'imyitero yabo irwaye ibishishi y'impu z'intama zirwaye ibishishi na kamere yabo y'ukuri, imbaraga, n'imigambi bizahishurwa. Isi izabatangarira n'inzira zabo, ndetse ibaramye kugeza barimbutse. Bazahagarara ku rubyiniro rw'isi mu mbaraga zikomeye, kandi ihuriro ry'abami icumi rizategeka. N'ubu, ihuriro ryamaze gutegura ingabo zaryo mu ibanga ku gitero cy'ibanga kuri Babuloni y'Ibanga. Biyemeje mu ibanga kwitegura, kandi nta gushidikanya bahuje igitero, kandi bateye Babuloni y'Ibanga ku mugaragaro hakurikijwe urubanza rwatangajwe n'Imana rugiye kuyigwirira, ruzatuma itazongera kuzamuka ukundi.

Kuko gukiranirwa kwayo kwageze imbere y'Isumbabyose, n'ibyaha byayo imbere y'intebe y'ubwami ikomeye y'ijuru, ndetse imbere y'Imana ikomeye ihoraho, kuko nta yindi ikomeye; kandi yaciriwe urubanza. Kuko El Shaddai akoresha ndetse n'amaboko y'abanzi be kugira ngo asohoze imigambi ye kandi ndetse n'abanzi be bumvira ijwi rye; kuko ni nde ushobora guhagarara imbere y'Imana Ishoborabyose? Ntimwibeshye, abungeri babiri barwaye ibishishi ubu bafatanyije inyuma y'ishusho y'igipupe. Batarahishurwa ku Isi, kandi nibahishurwa, isi izabatangarira kandi ibakurikire kugeza barimbutse. Nyamara si intama na gato, ahubwo ni inyamaswa z'inkazi imbere zizahabwa imbaraga zikomeye vuba aha n'ikigongwe, kuko abihabwa mu gihe gito. Kuko bazishyira imbere, kandi mu mwanya w', umushumba mwiza; uretse abafite ubushishozi bw'umwuka bazabona ibishishi, ndetse n'ikibi n'ubwangizi bwuzuye buri muri bo, kuko bafite kamere yanduye kandi ni babi kugeza mu mizi y'ubuzima bwabo. Mwamaze kumenya abo biyita bo, ariko ntimuzi abo bari bo by'ukuri, kandi ndetse bihishe amazina y'ibinyoma, bityo umubare ntushobora kubarwa.

Kuko inyamaswa izazamuka iva mu nyanja kugira ngo itegeke amahanga yamaze kubaho, kandi igaragara ko ubu itariho ariko mu by'ukuri iracyariho, ndetse iyobora amaboko y'igipupe gishaje kitagira ubwenge. Uyu ni we witiriwe mu Giheburayo akoresheje amagambo navuze ubwo nabonaga Satani agwa nka rabarabya ava mu ijuru. Rabarabya igwa iva mu ijuru: mwige kugira ngo mumenye. Kuko atifuza abagore, cyangwa ngo yubahe imana iyo ari yo yose: kuko azishyira hejuru ya byose. Ndababaza ubu kandi murebe niba umugabo aramukwa umwana? Nyamara umugore we agaragara nk'umugore ufite abana, nubwo atari abana be.

Inyamaswa izazamuka iva mu isi mwamaze kuyimenya, kandi benshi muri mwe murayikurikira mu bujiji, ndetse mwamaze kuyobywa mu gihe mwagombye kuba mubwiriza murwanya ikinyoma. Kuko akomoka mu gihugu, ariko mu muryango we nta muntu n'umwe washyizweho kashe mu 144,000. Kandi kuri Dani yavuze ati, Dani ni icyana cy'intare: azasimbuka ava i Bashani, kandi akomoka mu muryango wa Dani, ndetse yishimira amaraso ye, kuko amaraso y'ubururu atembera mu mitsi ye. Azwi nka Kirusi w'iki gihe kandi azaba umwubatsi w'urusengero kugira ngo azane ikizira cy'umurimbuzi mu gihe cye, kandi ubu yamaze kubaka mu Gihugu cy'Isezerano ku isinagogi ya Satani.

Bana banjye bato, mbasigiye ubwenge bwo gutekerezaho, kandi aya si amabanga ukundi ahubwo yahishuwe mu gihe cyayo. Gusa ibihe byabo ntibizahishurwa ku isi kugeza ubuza akuweho, kandi bizaba vuba cyane kuruta uko mubitekereza. Kuko ibi bintu bigiye gusohora. kandi nta gihe gisigaye, kuko imibare yaguye iva ku isura y'isaha. Mugume munyizera, kandi nimara guhamya mu mwuka wanyu umwirondoro wabo, muhindure bene wanyu. Dore, kugaragara kwanjye kurenda gusohozwa.

Ndi Ijambo ry'Imana ryigize umubiri, kandi ntuye muri mwe.

Ndabahamagarira mu rugo rwanyu mu ijuru vuba cyane.

Ndi Umwana w'intama utagira inenge cyangwa ikizinga.

Ndi umutambyi mukuru iteka ryose mu buryo bwa Melikisedeki. Ndi Mesiya w'ukuri, ndetse Yeshua HaMashiach.

Nje gusohoza urubanza kuri bose.

Nje kuvuga amagambo Yohana Uhishurirwa yahawe.

Ndi uwo Ndi we, kuko NDI WE ukomeye yantumye.

Dore, Jyewe, ndetse Yesu Kristo. noherereje aya magambo abizerwa banjye; none nimwumve Amagambo yanjye!
