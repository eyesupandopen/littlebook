Ubuhamya Bwombi: Isirayeli
Ibisharira n'Ibiryoshye
Ku ya 9 Kanama 2024

Mu buryo butunguranye Ibyiringiro byanyu birahishuwe!

Nimwikoranye kandi mwumve ijwi ry'Umwami wanyu, ndetse n'ijwi rya Yesu w'i Nazareti kuko ari jye wituye rimwe, rwose, kandi iteka ryose nk'Umwana w'Intama wa Pasika utunganye kuri Yawe ku bw'ibyaha byanyu, ndetse n'Imana ikomeye ihoraho, nk'umutambyi wanyu mukuru iteka ryose mu buryo bwa Melikisedeki kugira ngo mubabarirwe ibyaha. Nimuze kuri jye, kandi muzane amatwi yanyu n'imitima yanyu kugira ngo mwumvane ukwizera kwuzuye amagambo yanjye yo kwizera! Yego ndabasaba kumva, kandi ntimwumve gusa amajwi y'amagambo yanjye ahubwo mwumvishe imitima yanyu kugira ngo mumenye umugambi wanjye kandi mubone ihumure mu rukundo mbakunda kandi kugira ngo mwakire mu bugwaneza ijambo ryatewe muri mwe, ribasha gukiza ubugingo bwanyu.

Kuko mbwira abizerwa mu Itorero ryanjye, ndetse n'umubiri wanjye, ndetse n'umugeni wanjye ndetse n'abo ngiye kwikoranyiriza mu mutekano wo mu ijuru mu cyumba cy'intebe y'ubwami ya Yawe. Kuko ari jye wita ku bugingo bwanyu kandi nita ku gukura kwanyu gukomeza kugira ngo mukure nk'igihingwa gikiranuka imbere yanjye, kuko ari jye Yawe yatanze nk'umurinzi n'uwita ku busitani bwe bwera cyane bw'ubugingo bw'abacunguwe. Kuko muri isaro ry'igiciro cyinshi kandi mu butunzi bwinshi bwa Yawe muri mu by'agaciro kenshi aho nta buye na rimwe ryasizwe ridahinduwe mu gakiza kanyu kandi nta mbaraga zasizwe zo kubakorera no kubatabara no kubamurikira Yawe, ndetse na Data ushoborabyose nk'isugi itanduye idafite ikizinga, cyangwa umunkanyari, cyangwa ikindi kintu nk'icyo. Nuko rero ntimutinye, kandi ntimwemerere gushidikanya na guke ku Magambo yanjye!

Nuko rero ndababaza, bakundwa banjye, kuki imitima yanyu ibahagarika umutima? Kuki muzunguza imitwe yanyu nk'aho mwikoreye umugayo? Mbese mwemereye ba Satani kwinjira mu mitima yanyu no kongorera amagambo y'ibinyoma y'ihumure avamo kutizera gusa kuko bashaka gushwanyaguza no guca ibyera byose? Mbese muzakira ya nzoka ya kera mu buriganya bwayo buhora bubeshya n'ubushukanyi bwayo buhora bwiyongera yakwinjira mu matwi yanyu amagambo y'ibinyoma yasenya ukwizera kwanyu, nk'uko byagendeye benshi bemereye intekerezo zabo kononwa zikava ku kwibanda gusa kuri jye, Kristo Yesu Umwami wanyu?

Kuko nta wundi uzabitaho, kandi abandi bose ni abiyitirira biyitirira kandi nta wundi Yesu ubaho usibye inyiganano zihendutse n'ibihimbano bitagira agaciro byagenewe kuyobya. Nuko rero ndabasaba kutirengagiza uku kurobanura nshaka kubaha kugira ngo mukomeze guhagarara nk'urumuri rw'umucyo ndetse no muri ibi bihe bya nyuma mbere y'uko isi mwamenye ireka kubaho kandi igahindurwa iteka ryose ikava mu kutubaha Imana kwayo kw'ubu ikajya mu rubanza ruhoraho ruzagwa ubu nk'igihano gihoraho ku batubaha Imana bashaka kwigarurira umurage wanjye. Kuko isi ari iyanjye n'ibiyuzuye byose, kandi ndi Umwami w'abami ukomeye uhoraho n'Umwami w'abami, ndetse n'Umwami wanyu ukiranuka kandi ni jye uzatwara umurage wanjye mu maboko y'abigarurira batagira uburenganzira kandi batari abami kandi batari imana kandi batari abanyamahanga baturutse mu zindi si ahubwo abaguye biyitirira kuko bose ari abiyitirira biyitirira. Kandi iminsi yabo izagirwa iherezo rihoraho mu murage wabo uhoraho mu birimi by'umuriro by'ubururu bishyushye cyane.

Kuko ibise byo kubyara bitagihagarara ubu, kandi hari ibimenyetso byinshi hejuru n'ibimenyetso byinshi mu isi bigaragara iteka kandi bidahagarara. Kandi ibi bitangwa nk'itangazo kandi kugira ngo mugire ibyiringiro, nuko rero ntimubipfobye ngo mubishakire impamvu nk'abakobanyi! Imirimo y'amaboko ya Yawe itangwa n'amaboko y'abamarayika be bakomeye, ndetse igashushanywa n'urutoki rw'Imana ubwayo rimwe na rimwe, ndetse no ku itegeko ryayo rihoraho itangaza ibi bikorwa byo mu ijuru byegereje, kuko umunsi ugeze kandi ubariho bakundwa banjye, nuko rero ntimutinye! Ibihe birageze byo gukoranya abizerwa banjye ngo bamusanganire mu bicu ubwo nzamanuka mvuye mu ijuru n'ijwi rya marayika mukuru n'impanda ikomeye y'Imana, nuko rero mwizere Amagambo yanjye kuko ari yego na amena!

Ni nde kandi ni iki gihari cyabahagarika umutima bana banjye? Mbese mutinya ko umukwe atazagera ngo afate ukuboko kw'umugeni we mu isezerano rihoraho nk'uko byasezeranijwe kera? Mbese Ijambo ry'Imana ryabaye imfabusa? Imana ibirinde! Nk'uko byanditswe kandi bigatangazwa ni ko bizaba rwose ko mu buryo butunguranye nzabakura mu burakari nkabajyana mu mutekano wo mu ijuru aho nagiye kubategurira umwanya, kuko mu nzu ya Data hari amazu menshi. Kandi ni ko muzabana nanjye iteka kandi ni ko muzaba abami n'abatambyi imbere ya Yawe Imana ikomeye ihoraho, kuko ari ko yagambiriye kandi igategura mu buzima bwanyu, kandi ni nde ushobora guhagarika imigambi n'imyiteguro by'Imana ihoraho?

Ndababwira ukuri, nta kintu na kimwe kandi nta n'umwe ushobora kubangamira na gato imigambi ya Eli Shaddai kandi nta n'umwe ushobora guhagarara (ndetse no mu kwigaragambya guto) adacecekeshejwe mu bwoba bw'imbaraga zikomeye, ubwiza no gutitira biba iyo Yawe yinyeganyeje na gato ngo ahaguruke ku ntebe ye y'ubwami ihoraho kandi ijuru n'isi byose bigatitira imbere y'ubwiza bwe bukomeye. Kuko nubwo Yawe yihishe mu bugwaneza, urukundo, n'impuhwe mu minsi myinshi ubu (kuko umunsi umwe umeze nk'imyaka igihumbi imbere y'Imana ihoraho) ubu ni cyo gihe Yawe yahisemo aho aziyereka ibyo yaremye, ubwo bose bazamenya ko hari Imana mu ijuru, kandi bazasobanukirwa rwose ko nk'uko biri hejuru, ari ko bizaba hasi ndetse no mu isi kuko Yawe ari Imana y'ijuru n'isi kandi ategeka ibyo yaremye byose kandi nta wundi uhari usibye we mu mbaraga, ubwiza n'ubushobozi. (Kandi abaguye bafashwe n'ubwoba buhoraho) Nuko rero ntimutinye Bana b'Isumbabyose kuko Imana ikomeye ihoraho, ndetse Yawe ubwe ni we ubarwanirira kandi ni we uzabatabara, akabacungura, kandi agahindura ubuzima bwanyu ubwiza buhoraho mu kanya nk'ako guhumbya.

Nuko rero kuki murambirwa? Mbese mwumva nk'aho Umwami wanyu uhoraho n'umukwe yabataye, mugeni wanjye? Mbese umukiza wanyu uhoraho yataye umubiri we, awusiga udafite umutwe? Imana ibirinde ko ikintu nk'icyo cyatekerezwa. Ni ikihe kintu Yawe yatangarije ibyo yaremye ko cyegereje ntikibe nyuma yaho? Mbese Amagambo ya Nowa yaguye ku butaka bwumagaye nk'amasezerano y'ubusa ubwo yari umubwiriza wo gukiranuka imyaka ijana na makumyabiri mu isi yariho icyo gihe? Oya! Ahubwo mu buryo butunguranye kandi n'imbaraga nyinshi amazi yarekuriwe mu kanya gato kandi azamuka hejuru y'imisozi miremire mu isi mu minsi yakurikiyeho.

Nuko rero ndabagira inama yo kutongera kwifatanya n'abantu b'injiji ku bushake. Bahitamo ubujiji ku bushake kugira ngo birengagize ko ku bw'ijambo ry'Imana ijuru ryabayeho kera, n'isi ihagaze ivuye mu mazi kandi iri mu mazi: Ibyo ni byo byatumye isi ya kera irengerwa n'amazi, ikarimbuka: Ariko ijuru n'isi by'ubu, ku bw'iryo jambo bibikiwe umuriro kugeza ku munsi w'urubanza n'ukurimbuka kw'abatubaha Imana. Nuko rero ndongera gutangaza ko umunsi w'umuriro n'urubanza n'ukurimbuka kw'abatubaha Imana ugeze, nk'uko byasezeranijwe kera, kandi ni ko bizaba mu buryo butunguranye.

Nuko rero ndongera kubabaza, bana banjye bato, Mbese Imana ni umuntu ngo ibeshye? Mbese jye, Yesu Kristo ukiranuka, sinakwizerwa ku buryo nabibagirwa kandi nkabaha ibyiringiro by'ibinyoma? Mbese narabibagiwe ahubwo njya kuroba? Ndababaza bakundwa banjye mbese natanze igitonyanga cyanjye cya nyuma cy'amaraso mu bubabare ku musaraba kugira ngo mbatabare ubwo nabasezeranije amasezerano akomeye kandi y'agaciro kenshi, ndetse n'amasezerano yatanzwe na Yawe ubwe mu ibanga rye rihoraho, ndetse n'ibanga rikomeye ry'Imana?

Imana ibirinde ko ibintu nk'ibyo byatekerezwa nuko rero mumenye ibitekerezo bibi bya Satani abatera, kandi mubyange kandi mubicyaha kandi musimbure uko gukeka gupfuye kandi kubi n'ibyanditswe byera, ndetse n'amasezerano ya Yawe ari yego na amena. Ariko ibihe byose kandi cyane cyane ibihe nk'ibi byaranditswe kandi nuko Pawulo yandikiye Abakorinto mu mwanya wanjye: Ariko ndatinya ko nk'uko inzoka yashutse Eva mu buriganya bwayo, ari ko intekerezo zanyu zayononwa zikava ku gutunganya kuri muri Kristo. Nuko rero ndabagira inama bana banjye, ndetse namwe bakundwa banjye kandi nifuza cyane gukomeza kwibanda gusa kuri jye, n'amagambo yanjye, kuko ndi Ijambo ry'Imana ryabaye umubiri kandi buri Jambo ry'Imana rizasohora rwose, kandi nta na rimwe mu Magambo yanjye mato rizabura gusohora ryavuzwe kandi rikayoborwa na wa Mwuka Wera ukomeye uhoraho, ari we Yawe, Imana ikomeye ihoraho!

Nuko rero ndabagira inama bana banjye, ndetse namwe bakundwa banjye kandi nifuza cyane, ndetse namwe byishimo byanjye n'ikamba ryanjye: muruhukire muri jye kandi muruhukire mu mirimo yanjye kandi mureke iyanyu. Kuko byanditswe ngo: Nuko rero haracyariho uburuhuko bw'abantu b'Imana. Kuko uwinjiye mu buruhuko bwayo, na we aba aruhukiye imirimo ye, nk'uko Imana yaruhukiye iyayo. Nuko rero mugire umwete wo kwinjira muri ubwo buruhuko bwanjye, kugira ngo hatagira umuntu ugwa akurikije urugero rw'uko kutizera. Bana banjye mugire umwete wo kureka imirimo yanyu kandi mukore imirimo mbaha gukora kandi ntimwishingikirize ku buhanga bwanyu ahubwo mu nzira zanyu zose mumenye jye, Kristo Yesu Umwami wanyu wazamutse, kandi muzabona uburuhuko mu misozi mibisi y'inzuri n'umutekano, cyane cyane munsi y'ubwitonzi n'uburinzi bw'ukuboko kwanjye gukomeye, kuko ndi Yeshua HaMashiach ari we n'umushumba wanyu mukuru ugiye kuboneka!

Nuko rero ndababaza, bakundwa banjye, mbese muzasubira kubana n'abakobanyi b'abapfapfa bakobana kandi bashaka kubashukira mu nzira zabo zitarimo ukwizera? Mbese muzirengagiza amagambo yanjye ndetse n'ibimenyetso byinshi, ibitangaza n'imirimo ikomeye bibakikije? Mbese mutekereza mu bupfapfa ko mushobora gukomeza kubaho nk'abatazi amakuba n'urubanza byegereje? Mbese muzasubira kubana n'abaciriwe urubanza bagenda, banga kwihana ahubwo bagahitamo urubanza rukomeye n'igihano? Ndababwira nweruye ko nimugerageza muzisanga nk'abapfapfa bakomeza kurenga imiburo yanjye kandi bagahanwa bidasobanutse nuko rero mwaburiwe.

Musobanukirwe ko isi mubamo ubu atari imwe n'iyo yari iri ndetse no mu byumweru bike bishize, kandi ikomeza guhinduka ku muvuduko wiyongera. Musobanukirwe ko nta n'umwe muri mwe wigeze abaho mu isi iriho ubu, kuko isi mubamo ubu iboshywe mu rubanza kandi nyamara yemerewe gutanga amatangazo y'ubwiza y'ubukwe bugiye kuba mu ijuru aho jye Kristo Yesu nzashyingiranwa iteka n'umugeni wanjye wizerwa kandi ni ko bazabana nanjye iteka. Kandi uko umugeni wanjye n'Itorero ryanjye batwarwa mu buryo butunguranye, ni ko kurimbuka gutunguranye kuzagwa ku bakobanyi bose n'abantu babi babasetse babakoba bati: Ejo hazaba hameze nk'uyu munsi, kandi uyu munsi umeze nk'ejo hashize, kandi nta makuba nzabona kandi sindi umupfakazi ahubwo ndi umwamikazi utegeka ibintu byose! Keretse mu buryo butunguranye kurimbuka kwabo kuzabageraho nk'umugore uri ku bise, kandi ntibazarokoka.

Nuko rero nimubavemo kandi mwitandukanye muri BYOSE no mu nzira zanyu zose. Ntimushobora kugira uruhare cyangwa umugabane na bo cyangwa muzasangira ibyago byabo. Musobanukirwe ko ibintu byatangiye kuba, ndetse n'ibintu bizagira ingaruka ku banyabyaha, ariko nzaha umutekano abizerwa banjye kugira ngo uru rubanza rutazabagiraho ingaruka bana banjye. Kuko ibi bise bidahagarara bigenewe gukangura aba nyuma mu basinziriye no gutandukanya aba nyuma mu bazinjira mu Nkuge mbere y'uko amakuba agwa mu buryo butunguranye kandi igihe kikaba kirangiye. Ntimushobora gukomeza kubaho ubuzima bw'uburangare kandi mugomba kugendera mu bwenge bwinshi nuko rero mwumve imiburo yanjye kandi musobanukirwe ko nabahaye ubwenge bwinshi n'ubuhanga kugira ngo mushobore kumenya ibihe mubamo, ndetse no hagati y'abasinziriye cyane kandi vuba ku makuba yegereje kandi bazababara kubera ibyo.

Nuko rero musobanukirwe ko igihe gisigaye nta gaciro gifite kandi gikora gusa imigambi ya Yawe mbere y'uko urubanza rugwa mu buryo butunguranye. Yego, umwanzi ahawe amahirwe yo gutegura igitero cye kuri Babuloni y'Ibanga kuko ari ko yahawe na Yawe ngo abikore. Ariko musobanukirwe rwose ko Satani ari umwanzi watsinzwe ubohewe ku isi ku bw'urubanza kandi yemerewe gusa guteza imbere imigambi ya Yawe kandi azicara acecetse ategereze ko ubuza akurwaho kandi nta kindi ashobora gukora usibye icyo yemerewe, nuko rero ntimutinye gutontoma kw'iyo ntare itagira amenyo kuko igikomangoma gito kiboshywe mu gutsindwa! Kuko mu by'ukuri, abaguye barabatinya kandi barasobanukiwe kandi bazi ko abo bigeze kugirira nabi bazaba ari bo bazana urubanza n'igihano gikiranuka mu bituza byabo iteka n'iteka ryose. Iki? Mbese ntimuzi ko muzacira abamarayika urubanza?

Nuko rero ndababaza bakundwa banjye, mbese ubu muzanga ubuntu bwanjye n'imbabazi zanjye zihabwa abazimiye, ndetse n'igihe urubanza n'amakuba biteguwe kugwa mu buryo butunguranye? Mbese mwibagiwe imyaka myinshi y'ubuntu n'imbabazi nabahaye ntabikwiriye kugira ngo musohoze ibyaha byanyu mbere yo guhindukirira gukiranuka? Mbese muzahakana abashaka kwinjira mu Nkuge kugira ngo twihuze umwanya mbere? Ndabizera neza kurushaho, bana banjye, nuko rero ndabasaba kwihangana no kwihangana cyane, ndetse na mbere y'uko amakuba agwa mu buryo butunguranye.

Niba hari uwo muri mwe utekereza ko afite impamvu yo kuzana ubutabera kuri Satani n'ubwami bwe, mfite impamvu nyinshi kuruta izigeze kwinjira mu mitima yanyu. Kuko ni nde muri mwe warwanyije Satani, ndetse kugeza ku gitonyanga cyanyu cya nyuma cy'amaraso ubwo mwamanikwaga ku bushake kuri icyo giti kigដi mu bubabare bukomeye. Kuko nikoreye umusaraba wanjye ku bushake kandi nitanga mu ntsinzi ihoraho ubwo narenganywaga mu maboko y'inkozi z'ibibi, ubwo nafashe umwanya wanyu mu rubanza mu bubabare bukomeye inyuma y'amarembo hagati y'abanyabyaha babi cyane. Amaraso yanjye yari aboneye kandi nta kibi cyashoboraga gushyirwa ku rwandiko rwanjye mu buryo bukwiye. Nyamara mu rukundo nkunda Data n'urukundo mbakunda, ntanze ubugingo bwanjye ku bushake kandi nabikora ukundi mu kanya gato, nubwo ikintu nk'icyo kidakenewe. Nuko rero ndetse no mu makuba yanyu y'ubu mukurikize urugero rwanjye rwo kwihangana muzi ko mu buryo butunguranye muzajya mu gukizwa kuhoraho!

Nuko rero ndabasaba gukomeza gushikama kugeza ku iherezo, ndetse n'iherezo aho mutwarwa mu buryo butunguranye mu kanya nk'ako guhumbya mu ijuru mu ngororano ihoraho n'umugambi uhoraho, kandi nyamara abapfiriye muri Kristo bazabanza kuzuka kandi muzabiyungaho mu buryo butunguranye mu byishimo bihoraho. Kuko imigambi ihoraho mwahamagariwe, mugatoranywa, mugashyirwaho ikimenyetso, kandi mukarindwa izasohoza imigambi ihoraho y'Imana isumbabyose kandi byose bigiye guhishurwa muri mwe bana banjye, nuko rero mumurikire nk'urumuri rwanjye ubu muri uku kuruhuka kwa nyuma mbere y'uko amakuba atunguranye akubita isi. Kandi nzabazana kuri mwe kugira ngo mubanzanire, kuko mukiri ba ambasaderi banjye kugeza ubwo mu buryo butunguranye mutwarwa mu ijuru. Kandi mu buryo butunguranye amakuba akomeye azagwa.

Musobanukirwe ko igihe kirangiye kandi nta kintu gishobora gusubiza inyuma ibintu bigiye kuza kandi byishyira ku murongo kurwanya abaturage bo ku isi, kuko urubanza ruzaza rw'abanyabyaha rukomeye. Nuko rero ndabasaba kandi mbahata ku bw'impuhwe, ubuntu n'imbabazi Yawe yaberetse mu maso ha Yesu Kristo ku bw'agakiza kanyu. Mbese mushobora gukomeza kuba abizerwa kugeza ku iherezo? Mbese muziyemerera kwinjira mu buruhuko bwanjye buhoraho mudacitse intege ku munota wa nyuma? Mbese muzerekeza umutima wanyu wose n'ukwitonda kwanyu ku magambo yanjye kandi musobanukirwe ko mu buryo butunguranye bizahinduka iteka kandi mu buryo butunguranye ni ubu.

Nta gihe gisigaye kuri Babuloni y'Ibanga cyo kwihana kandi nta gihe gisigaye mu gihe cyanjye cy'ubuntu n'imbabazi kuko igihe kirangiye. Kandi kuri buri gihe hari igihe n'umugambi kandi mu gusohozwa kw'ibihe gucungurwa gutunguranye kuzagwa ku bacunguwe nuko rero abacunguwe bo mu isi nibabivuge abo yacunguye mu kuboko kw'umwanzi. Kandi ntimwemerere umujura kwiba ibyishimo byanyu kuko ni uwuhe mugeni uririra umunsi w'ubukwe bwe, ndetse n'umunsi w'ubukwe bwiza, bwera nta n'umwe ushobora kwangiza cyangwa guhagarika umutima.

Nuko rero muhumurizwe n'Amagambo yanjye y'ihumure kandi mureke kubaho nk'abajugunywe hasi mu isoni n'icyaha. Kuko mwe mugeni wanjye, muri abo nagize abatagira ikizinga kandi batagira umugayo imbere y'intebe y'ubwami ya Yawe, kandi muri umuganura mu bapfuye. Nubwo abazima muri mwe kandi basigaye batazigera basogongera ku rupfu, mwavutse mupfuye mu byaha n'ibicumuro. Kandi muzaba mwaravuye mu rupfu (urwo mu gupfa mwari gupfa), mukajya mu buzima buhoraho urwo musanzwe mubamo avansi, ndetse n'icyo kimenyetso gikomeye cya Yawe mwashyizweho ikimenyetso cy'uwo munsi utunguranye wo gucungurwa. Kuko mwabyawe ubwa kabiri n'imbuto itabora kandi muri abana b'Imana isumbabyose, ndetse n'abana b'Imana Ishoborabyose!

Nuko rero, muhumurizwe kandi muruhuke kandi mukorane nanjye muri uyu muzenguruko w'intsinzi w'ibihe. Kuko ntabwo higeze habaho igihe ku isi nk'iki kandi ntihazigera habaho igihe nk'iki ukundi. Ndetse n'igihe abana b'Imana bahoraho bategereza babizi umwuka wabo wa mbere w'ubuzima buhoraho nk'impinja z'iteka ryose mu kubaho kuhoraho kw'Imana ihoraho aho bazashyingiranwa n'Umwami wabo uhoraho mu isezerano rihoraho, kandi...mu buryo butunguranye, ni ko bizaba!

Ndi we wituye rimwe, rwose, kandi iteka ryose nk'Umwana w'Intama wa Pasika utunganye.

Ngiye kwikoranyiriza abizerwa mu mutekano wo mu ijuru mu cyumba cy'intebe y'ubwami ya Yawe.

Ndi we wita ku bugingo bwanyu kandi nita ku gukura kwanyu gukomeza kugira ngo mukure nk'igihingwa gikiranuka.

Ndi we Yawe yatanze nk'umurinzi n'uwita ku busitani bwe bwera cyane bw'ubugingo bw'abacunguwe.

Ndi Umwami w'abami ukomeye uhoraho n'Umwami w'abami, ndetse n'Umwami wanyu ukiranuka.

Ndi we uzatwara umurage wanjye mu maboko y'abigarurira batagira uburenganzira.

Ndi Ijambo ry'Imana ryabaye umubiri kandi buri Jambo ry'Imana rizasohora rwose.

Ndi Yeshua HaMashiach ari we n'umushumba wanyu mukuru ugiye kuboneka!

Jye, Yeshua HaMashiach nohereje aya Magambo binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye, kandi binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye aya Magambo yoherejwe!

Nuko rero ndabatera inkunga bakundwa banjye ngo mudacika intege kandi mugume mushikamye kugeza ku iherezo ritunguranye cyane rizashyira iherezo ku gihe cyanyu ku isi mu mimerere yanyu y'ubu. Mu buryo butunguranye, kandi mu kanya nk'ako guhumbya muzisanga muhagaze mu cyumba cy'intebe y'ubwami ya Yawe mu byishimo bihoraho kandi muzasobanukirwa rwose kandi mumenye nk'uko namwe musobanukiwe rwose kandi muzwi. Nuko rero isezerano rizahura n'isohozwa mu byishimo bihoraho n'ubwiza buhoraho bubategereje kandi byose bizasohorezwa muri mwe mu kanya nk'ako guhumbya, kandi mu buryo butunguranye ni ko bizaba. Mugume muri jye bana banjye kuko mbakunda cyane kandi sintejejwe isoni no kubita bene data.
