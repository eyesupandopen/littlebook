Ubuhamya Bubiri Ubuhamya: Itorero
Mu Mirimo ya Petero
10 Mata 2024

Nimuhumure!

Bana banjye bato, nimuhumure; sinanesheje umubi nkabacungura n'amaraso yanjye bwite? Kuki mwacika intege? Kuki mufite intimba mu mutima; mutekereza ko nabibagiwe? Oya, aho kwishingikiriza ku buhanga bwanyu, mushake ubwenge bwanjye, buva mu ijuru; kuko ubwenge bw'Imana ari bwo buzabayobora, ntimukabureke! Ese ubwenge ntibukirangurura ijwi mu mayira? Nuko rero ntimutinye kandi muhumure, kuko nanesheje namwe muzanesha, binyuze mu maraso yanjye bwite. Mureke amahoro yanjye ahumurize imitima yanyu kandi mureke amagambo yanjye aruhure ubugingo bwanyu, kuko ndi kumwe namwe.

Ni nde uzabatandukanya n'urukundo rwanjye? Ese ni amakuba, cyangwa inkota, cyangwa inzara bizabatandukanya? Ndavuga nti oya, kandi murebe mumenye ko mutekanye muri njye. Mumenye ko nabashyizeho ikimenyetso cya Data, kandi icyo kimenyetso ntigishobora kumenwa cyangwa ngo kibakurweho, nuko rero mugume muri njye, Umwami wanyu, imbaraga zanyu, n'umucunguzi wanyu. Dore! Nabaguze amaraso yanjye bwite, nabahamagaye mu mazina yanyu, nabashyizeho ikimenyetso nk'uko kwizera kwanyu mu izina ryanjye kuri, kandi uzabyizera wese azakizwa no kwizera, kuko umukiranutsi azabeshwaho no kwizera! Mukomeze ibyiringiro byanyu kandi mureke bibakomeze, kandi mwongere ukwihangana ku rukundo rwanyu no kwizera kwanyu, kuko mwakijirijwe ibyiringiro. Ibyiringiro bikomeye n'ibyo mutegereje bikomeye ni ibyanyu, kuko nabivuze.

Kuki mwihebye kandi mubabaye? Dore! Naranesheje, kandi vuba aha muzaba muri kumwe nanjye, kandi muzandebesha amaso yanyu, kandi muzamenya nk'uko mumenywe.

Nimurebe! Uko ni ko Umwami ukomeye avuga, Umwami w'imbaraga ari we Mwami w'abami. Nimundebere mu kababaro kanyu no mu byago byanyu. Nzi imitima yanyu, kandi nzi neza ko munkunda kandi mwifuza kubana nanjye nk'uko nanjye nifuza kubana namwe; gusa ntimugacike intege. Munsubize iki, igihe gito gisigaye mu gutegereza kwanyu — ese cyagereranywa n'iteka ryose muzabana nanjye, mugeni wanjye? Oya, imibabaro yanyu n'amakuba mu isi ntibikwiriye kugereranywa n'ubwiza ngiye guhishura muri mwe, kuko muzaba nk'uko ndi, kandi muzambona nk'uko ndi, nuko rero ntimureke imitima yanyu ihagarika umutima. Musobanukirwe ibihe byanyu kandi muhoze mwiteguye, kuko kuboneka kwanjye kuzaba mu buryo butunguranye, kandi kuzaruta ibyo mwari mwiteze.

Dore, bana banjye! Isi izamenya ko mbakunda kandi ko muri abanjye, kandi Satani azazana ubuyobe bukomeye kugira ngo ayobye abazarimbuka, ariko ntibuzabegera. Oya, urubanza si urwanyu, ndetse n'umujinya wanjye si uwanyu. Musobanukirwe, bana banjye, ko muri njye, muri abakiranutsi, atari abakiranutsi mu mirimo yanyu bwite, ahubwo muri abakiranutsi mu gukiranuka nabambitse. Ntimugata amakanzu nabambitse, kandi ntimukazimye umwuka wanjye. Nubwo ari mu bihe by'imperuka, nzasuka umwuka wanjye, kandi abahungu banyu n'abakobwa banyu bazahanura, n'abasaza banyu bazarota inzozi. Haracyari umurimo ugomba gukorwa, kandi igikorwa cyanjye cya nyuma muri ibi bihe by'ubuntu kizaruta umunsi wa Pentekote ubwo nasukaga bwa mbere ku batoranyijwe banjye umwuka nahawe na Data. Imirimo ikomeye iri mu ntoki zanyu kandi hari benshi bagomba gukizwa, nuko rero mukore umurimo wanjye kandi mukorere abana banjye kandi mushake intama zanjye zazimiye. Munsabe nanjye nzabaha inshingano, kuko nabashoboje ubushobozi bukomeye kandi nahaye abantu impano. Ni nde ushobora kubahagarara imbere, nubwo mufite ubu butunzi mu nzabya z'ibumba? Kuki mukinshidikanyaho? Mu ntege nke zanyu ni ho imbaraga zanjye zitunganirizwa muri mwe. Nuko rero, musagambe mu murimo w'Umwami, bana banjye.

Musuzume Ijambo ryanjye Ryera, kuko nk'uko nazuye abapfuye, namwe mushobora gukora mutyo. Mwisige umuti w'amaso mu maso yanyu kugira ngo mubone gukiza gukomeye kwinshi nakoze kugira ngo mbohore imbohe igihe nazaga mu banjye; namwe mushobora gukora mutyo. Sinavuze ko unyizera, azakora imirimo nk'iyo, kandi azakora imirimo iruta iyo kuko ngiye kwa Data ari we So, no ku Mana yanjye ari yo Mana yanyu: Muri abami n'abatambyi imbere yanjye kandi mwicaye mu by'ijuru, none kuki mugishidikanya? Mugire kwizera kungana n'akabuto ka sinapi, kuko ari akabuto gato cyane kabyara igiti kinini, uko ni ko muzaba nimuguma mushikamye muri njye.

Kuki mwitiranya amagambo yanjye kandi mukirengagiza ibisobanuro birambuye mu mvugo yanjye? Sinbeshya: mushobora gukora ibyo nabashinze gusohoza nimuguma kuba abizerwa. Muvuge Ijambo ryanjye n'ubutware bukomeye n'ubushizi bw'amanga. Mubwire iyo misozi muti, Tabwa mu nyanja! Amagambo yanjye ni ukuri n'ubugingo, nuko rero ntimwizere ibinyoma by'abungeri bayobye bavuga ko mudashobora gukora ibyo mbabwira. Mufate isomo. Murashaka gukiza abarwayi? Mutegeke indwara kugenda mu izina ryanjye, kandi muvuge amagambo mufite kwizera muri njye no kwizera mu magambo nzabaha. Mubivuge bibe bityo, kuko ubugingo n'urupfu biri mu mbaraga z'ururimi; mbega ukuntu indimi zanyu zifite imbaraga iyo muvuze imbaraga z'Imana kandi mugategeka ibidashoboka.

Muvuge mushize amanga. Muvuge mwiyizeye. Muvuge mu kwizera, mudashidikanya na gato; kuko nabatumye kandi nkabaha imbaraga. Kuki musaba umwuka kugenda mu izina ryanjye mufite ubwoba ku minwa yanyu? Muwutegeke kugenda mu izina ryanjye n'ubushizi bw'amanga bwose n'icyizere, kuko uri muri mwe aruta uri mu isi. Dore! Imyuka mibi irabatinya kandi igahunga amagambo yanyu, none kuki mutinya ibintu by'intege nke kandi byoroheje byaneshejwe kandi bitegereje umuriro? Ntimutinye, ahubwo mwizere gusa!

Mugende mu izina ryanjye kandi mukore imirimo yanjye ikomeye. Mwibuke urukundo rwanyu rwa mbere kandi muruhukire mu buruhukiro nabahaye. Mureke imirimo yanyu bwite n'imitwaro yanyu, kandi mwikorere umugogo wanjye; kuko umugogo wanjye utaruhije, n'umutwaro wanjye woroshye. Mukore imirimo nabateguriye kandi muyigenderemo, kuko mu kubikora muzakiza benshi mu birimi by'umuriro, kandi imbuto zizagwira mu byanyu. Igihe kiregereje, mugeni wanjye, n'iherezo ry'urugendo rwanyu riri hafi, hafi kuruta uko mubitekereza. Ariko munyumve, bana banjye; munyumve, mugeni wanjye! Musabire abandi mu gihe hakiri igihe. Mukize abarwayi mu gihe Umwana amurikira mu buzima bwanyu, abaha imbaraga zanjye.

Nimushake intama zanjye zazimiye; muzihamagare, kuko hakiriho benshi bo gukiza muri aya masegonda ya nyuma yo guhembuka. Kuko vuba muzagera ku murage wanyu muri jye, kandi muzabona ubuzima bwanyu bwuzuye muri jye; kuko ubugingo bwanyu buhishwe muri jye, kandi ubwo nzaboneka, ni ko n'ubuzima bwuzuye Data yabahaye muri jye buzaboneka. Ndabakunda cyane, bana banjye bato, nuko rero namwe mukunde abazimiye muri ubwo buryo. Ntimubacire urubanza cyangwa ngo musuzugure amakuba yabo, kuko baneshejwe na Satani nk'uko namwe mwari mumeze kera; nuko mwibuke agakiza nabakoreye maze muguhe abagakeneye. Nubwo benshi bazabarwanya, ntimuzahagarare kugeza intama zanjye zikoranyijwe, kuko vuba cyane ingano zanjye zizakoranyirizwa mu kigega cyanjye amahoro. Abahakana kuboneka kwanjye barampakanije kandi bahakanye n'ijambo ryanjye; nuko rero mwikomeze mu ijambo ryanjye kandi mureke kwizera abajura, kuko abajura na bo ari abanyabinyoma.

Ni jye wihanganye kugeza ku mwuka wanjye wa nyuma, ndwanya icyaha n'amaraso yanjye bwite atagira icyaha.

Ni jye wazuwe ku munsi wa gatatu nkazamuka kwa Data.

Ni jye wagendagendaga hagati y'abigishwa banjye n'intumwa iminsi mirongo ine, ndetse nkabonwa n'abavandimwe basaga magana atanu icyarimwe, nkabonwa kandi ngakorwaho na benshi, kuko umwuka utagira inyama n'amaraso.

Ni jye wajyanywe hejuru, bakindeba, mu bicu.

Ni jye ufite umucyo urabagirana kuruta izuba, kuko ngaragaza ubwiza bwa Data.

Ngiye guhishura ubwiza bukomeye muri mwe.

Ndi kumwe namwe iminsi yose, kugeza ku mperuka y'isi.
