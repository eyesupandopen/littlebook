Ubuhamya Bubiri: Isirayeli
Ibisharira Biryoshye
24 Kanama 2024

Igihe ni iki

Igihe kirageze, bana banjye, igihe kirageze. Igihe kirageze cyo gutaha, ubwo impanda ivuga n'itorero ryanjye rigakoranywa, aho Yehova ategereje afite ibyishimo mu mutima we ku bw'ukuza kwanyu gutunguranye. Igihe kirageze cyo gutaha, ubwo ijwi ry'impanda rihamagara abana b'Isumbabyose kujya aho abamarayika baba kandi bagendagenda. Igihe kirageze bana banjye ngo mbakoranye mbakuye mu mfuruka enye z'isi, ndetse n'abasinziriye muri Kristo bose bazabanza kuzuka naho mwebwe abazima basigaye muzazamurwa hamwe na bo mu bicu. Kuko jyewe Kristo Yesu ndi Umwami n'Umukiza wanyu uhoraho, kandi ndabahamagara ngo muze mu rugo rwanyu mu ijuru aho ingororano zanyu zibategereje, aho nabateguriye umwanya mu nzu ya Data, kuko mu nzu ya Data harimo amazu menshi. Ndabahamagara ngo mutahe muhagarare imbere y'Umwami wanyu, kugira ngo bose bahabwe ibikwiye ibyo bakoze bakiri mu mubiri, byaba byiza cyangwa bibi, kugira ngo bose bahabwe n'Umwami ingororano yabo, kandi kugira ngo bose bafate imyanya yabo mu byiciro byo mu ijuru byateguriwe buri wese muri mwe.

Dore! Ndi Yeshua HaMashiach, kandi ndi umukwe wegereje aje gukoranya umugeni wanjye kuko igihe cy'ubukwe bwacu kigeze, aho nzagirana isezerano rihoraho n'abo natoranyije, ndetse n'abiteguye, kandi nabejeje mu maraso yanjye y'impongano kuko baje aho ndi rwose bakampa imitima yabo yose mu rukundo no kwiyemeza. Nubwo batagendaga batunganye, kandi benshi bakaba baranesheje ibyaha bikomeye, biyejeje rwose mu maraso y'umwana w'intama, kandi ubu bahagaze imbere yanjye bejejwe rwose ibyaha byabo kandi baraboneye nk'amasugi atarakoze ubusambanyi kandi bakirinda umubi. Dore! Abaneshi banjye nababariye ibyaha byose nkabasukira amavuta mu matabaza yabo kuko baje aho ndi mu bihe byabo by'amage nkabereka uko bakata intambi zabo kandi narabakijije ndabitandukanya ngo babe abanjye, ndetse n'abo Yehova yatoranyije bose mbere y'ishingwa ry'isi ngo bahagarare nk'umugeni wanjye kandi babane nanjye iteka ryose, kandi bazategekana nanjye nk'Abami n'Abatambyi bahoraho kuko ari ko bahamagawe, bejejwe kandi batoranyirijwe muri jye, Kristo Yesu Umwami wabo wazutse.

Kandi abanjye 144,000 bazahindurwa kandi bashyirweho ikimenyetso ndetse baremwe bundi bushya rwose ku bw'imigambi bahamagariwe kandi bateguriwe gusohoza. Ni ukuri, ni ukuri, buri bugingo bwizeye izina ryanjye rikomeye bukamugira Umwami wabo uhoraho buzahindurwa rwose kandi buremwe bundi bushya nk'icyaremwe gishya, kandi bazarema nkanjye. Kandi ntibazongera gukora icyaha ukundi, haba binyuze mu bwibone cyangwa kutubaha Imana kandi ntibazongera kuvumwa ukundi, kandi indwara cyangwa ububabare, cyangwa urupfu cyangwa agahinda ntibizongera kubageraho ukundi kuko ibya mbere bishize kandi umucyo w'ukuri ubu uramurika. Kandi ni jye, Kristo Yesu, ugiye kwiyereka Yakobo kandi umuntu wese wambaza izina ryanjye rikomeye kugeza ku iherezo azakizwa, ndetse n'abatuye isi bose bagenewe ubugingo buhoraho kandi hazabaho umukumbi munini uzanyakira nk'Mesiya wabo kandi nzabakiza byimazeyo.

Ndaje kubatwara, bakunzi banjye, kandi ibihe ni ubu. Nategereje igihe cyose cy'ubuntu kandi nakijije benshi, kandi benshi bazazurirwa kugira ubugingo bushya muri jye, Kristo Yesu Umwami wabo uhoraho, kandi abapfiriye muri Kristo bazabanza kuzuka, kandi impanda ya nyuma iriteguye ubu. Ibimenyetso byinshi, ibitangaza n'imirimo ikomeye byatwikiriye isi mu bwiza bwabyo kandi ibimenyetso ntibihagarara hirya no hino ku isi ubu bitangaza ukuza kwanjye, bitangaza ubukwe bw'Umwana w'Intama n'umugeni we wizerwa, kandi ibyumba by'ibirori mu ijuru birateguwe kandi biriteguye.

Kandi bimeze bityo, ibirori byo mu ijuru bitegereje abizerwa banjye, abahamagawe, n'abatoranyijwe, kandi ubu ni igihe cyo kuruhuka iteka imirimo yabo bakoze mu isi mu mazu yabo y'ibumba, kandi bakoze mu mubiri nk'abakozi bagenzi banjye kandi bakoranyije benshi bakabanzanira, Umwami Yesu Kristo kugira ngo bahabwe agakiza gahoraho iteka ryose. Kuko nubwo imibiri yabo yangijwe n'icyaha binyuze kuri Satani, nabahaye intego ndabahamagara ngo bave iteka mu busa bari baraciriweho iteka n'icyaha.

Kandi bityo bafite ubu butunzi mu nzabya z'ibumba kugira ngo imbaraga z'Imana zibe kandi ziture muri bo, kandi bakoze imirimo myinshi ikomeye mu izina ryanjye, kandi ni jye wabahaye imbaraga zo kuba abaragwa b'agakiza n'abaragwa b'ubugingo buhoraho kandi narabacunguye mbaha ingwate ihoraho mu kuvuka gushya kandi nabashyizeho ikimenyetso bose n'uwo mwuka wera w'isezerano, ndetse icyo kimenyetso kidashobora kumeneka cy'isezerano. Kandi ubu ni igihe cyo gucungura rwose ibyaguzwe, ndetse n'abamize Umwami bose bafite umutima wuzuye ukwizera bazi ko Yehova yanzuye mu bapfuye ngo sinzongera gupfa ukundi. Ubu ni ibihe byo gucungurwa kwabo kwuzuye aho bazahabwa byuzuye ingwate yabo bahawe nanjye mu kuvuka gushya kwa Kristo muri mwe ibyiringiro by'ubwiza!

Dore! Umva ijwi ryanjye kandi wumve amagambo yanjye kandi umenye ko ejo hazaza hanyu heza hatari mu myaka iri imbere, cyangwa ngo hateganyijwe kugera mu Yubile zizaza, ahubwo kubagezeho bakunzi banjye kandi mu buryo butunguranye mu kanya gato gahoraho mwese muzahindurwa kandi ibihe byo guhindurwa ni ubu. Muzisanga muhinduwe mu buryo butunguranye mu bwiza buhoraho, kandi nzabambika imyenda itagira inenge idashobora kwanduzwa. Mu buryo butunguranye muzisanga muhagaze mu cyumba cy'intebe y'ubwami cya Yehova, ndetse mu bwiza n'ikuzo bihoraho bya Data w'Umucyo, kandi muzasangira iteka ryose kamere ye ihoraho kuko muri abana b'Imana ihoraho, ndetse n'abakuwe muri Adamu bose bagacungurwa bavanywe mu gitaka gitukura.

Kandi yari amaraso yanjye y'impongano yamenwe ku isi i Gologota yemeje agakiza kanyu rimwe, n'iteka ryose ubwo natangaga ubugingo bwanjye n'amagambo ngo "Birarangiye!" Kandi jye Kristo Yesu, ndi Umwami w'Abami wazutse kandi akazamuka iteka ryose kuko Yehova yanzuye mu bapfuye ngo sinzongera gupfa ukundi! Ndi Adamu wa nyuma, kandi ndi umwana w'umuntu wacunguye abana b'abantu bambaje izina ryanjye nkabahindura abana b'Imana bahoraho bazabaho iteka mu bwiza buhoraho n'ikuzo rihoraho kandi iteka ryose Yehova azahabwa icyubahiro muri bo, na bo muri Yehova kuko muri jye, Yeshua HaMashiach ni ho kuzura kose kuba iteka ryose, kandi Yehova azaba byose muri byose kandi iteka ryose tuzagendera hamwe nk'icyaremwe gishya n'umuntu mushya. Kandi mwebwe, abahamagawe banjye batoranyijwe kandi bizerwa, mucunguwe iteka ryose, kandi mugiye kumva ubuzima bwanyu nyabwo kuko ubuzima bwanyu buhoraho. Bityo mwumve ko mwapfuye, kandi ubuzima bwanyu buhishe hamwe nanjye, Kristo Yesu, muri Yehova. Ubwo jye, Kristo Yesu, uri ubuzima bwanyu, nzaboneka, namwe muzabonekana nanjye mu bwiza. Ntimuzi ko kamere yanyu nyakuri ihishe muri jye, Kristo Yesu?

None se kuki mwihebye batoranyijwe banjye, bizerwa banjye, Itorero ryanjye ndetse n'Umugeni wanjye? Ndaje kubatwara kandi dore, ndabegereye. Kandi nzabakoranyiriza aho ndi mu buryo butunguranye. Kandi uko isi yinjira mu rubanza n'intambara, ijuru rizishimira ukuhaba kwanyu kandi hazabaho ibirori bikomeye ubwo abana b'Imana bazaba bunze ubumwe iteka ryose n'Umwami wabo w'Abami n'Umwami w'Abami mu bwiza buhoraho kandi ubugingo bushya buzahabwa bose. Kandi ntihazongera kubaho gutandukana hagati y'abacunguwe n'amaraso y'Umwana w'Intama na Se n'Imana yabo.

Kuko ubu ni cyo gihe Yehova yatoranyije cyo gutangira kwiyereka rwose ibyo yaremye kandi ukwiyereka kwe rwose gutangirira mu nzu y'Imana, kandi agiye kwiyereka rwose abatoranyijwe be n'abizerwa, ndetse n'umuganura, kuko abo nkoranyiriza aho ndi ku ijwi ry'impanda ya nyuma ari umuganura w'abapfuye. Kandi ubu ni igihe cyo kugira ngo bamenye rwose, nk'uko namwe mwese muzwi rwose nanjye na Data. Kandi bityo ntanze amakuru y'ukuza kwanjye kandi ngira inama mwebwe bizerwa banjye gukomeza kwitegura ukuza kwanjye, kuko ubu ni ibihe ubwo urubanza rutangirira mu nzu y'Imana. Kandi mu buryo butunguranye, bizaba.

Dore! Ntimutinye imihengeri igiye kuza benshi muri mwe mubona yikanya: imihengeri y'intambara, imihengeri y'amakuba yegereje, imihengeri y'ibiza byanze bikunze bigiye kuba. Mwumve ko nta kintu na kimwe kizabagiraho ingaruka, nubwo benshi babaho mu bishuko n'intambara y'umwuka ineshwa rwose muri jye, Kristo Yesu. Dore! Igihe cy'ukuza kwanjye ku bizerwa banjye kirageze kandi nk'uko ibise bitahagarara kandi mu buryo butunguranye bazakoranywa kandi bashikuzwe mu rubanza rutari urwabo.

Ndabahamagara ngo muze mu rugo rwanyu mu ijuru aho ingororano zanyu zibategereje.

Ndabahamagara ngo mutahe muhagarare imbere y'Umwami wanyu, kugira ngo muhabwe ibikwiye ibyo mwarangije mu mubiri.

Ndi Yeshua HaMashiach.

Ndi umukwe wegereje aje gukoranya umugeni wanjye kuko umunsi w'ubukwe ugeze.

Ndaje kubatwara, bakunzi banjye, kandi igihe ni ubu.

Ndi uwo wabahaye imbaraga zo kuba abaragwa b'agakiza n'abaragwa b'ubugingo buhoraho.

Ndaje kubatwara ngo mbashikuze mbajyane mu mutekano wo mu ijuru kandi ibihe ni ubu.

Jye Yeshua HaMashiach nohereje aya magambo binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye, kandi binyuze ku mugaragu wanjye aya magambo yoherejwe.

Mugume mwiteguye kandi ntimuve mu myanya yanyu kandi mukomeze kundindira. Ntimube abantu batagira ukwizera, ahubwo mube maso kandi mwumve ko ibimenyetso by'ukuza kwanjye biri ku isi yose, kandi hanyuma mu buryo butunguranye muzahagarara mu kubaho kuhoraho kwa NDIHO ukomeye!

Mwiteguye?
