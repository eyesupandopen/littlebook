Uku ni ko Uwiteka avuga ngo: Ku bw'ibicumuro bitatu bya Isirayeli, ndetse dore ni bine, sinzivumbura, kuko bagurishije umukiranutsi ifeza, n'umukene inkweto. Iriburiro

Iyi nyandiko ni icyemezo cy'urubanza kuri Babuloni y'Ibanga cyatanzwe ku wa 26 Kamena 2025, nk'uko byategetswe na Yeshua HaMashiach na YHVH. Izi manza zakuwe mu Gatabo Gato k'Ibyahishuwe Igice cya 10, ari na ryo Vanjiri y'Iteka ryose y'Ibyahishuwe igice cya 14; byahishuwe kuva ku wa 2 Werurwe 2024, kugeza ku wa 20 Werurwe 2025. Byatanzwe na Yeshua HaMashiach na YHVH binyuze mu Mwuka w'Ubuhanuzi. Aya magambo akora nk'umuburo wo kwihana mbere y'uko urubanza rwuzuye rugera, kandi akora nko kuyobora ku byabaye bigiye kuba mu minsi ya vuba.

Bimenyekane ko, muri uru rwego, nkora nk'uhagarariye urubanza n'intumwa yoherejwe ivuye ku ntebe y'ubwami ya Yahweh ubwe, munsi y'ubutware bwa Yesu Kristo, ari we Yeshua HaMashiach, uwo nkorera. Ndi uwo ndi we n'icyo Umwami yangize, kandi ndi Biblaridion ye, agatabo ke gato, ndetse n'intumwa isobanurwa n'ubutumwa burura kandi buryohereye kandi mu kuboko kwanjye hari agatabo gato gafunguye. Sela. Sinje ku bwanjye cyangwa ku bushake bwanjye, kandi sinikoresheje, ahubwo noherejwe n'Amagambo Umwami yabibye mu Mwuka wanjye n'ukuboko kwe gukomeye kugira ngo arekurwe ku bushake bwe no kumuhamagara kugira ngo asomwe kandi atangazwe ku Itorero, no kuri Isirayeli, ariko no kuri iyi si yuzuye ibyaha kandi yaciriwe urubanza kandi igiye kurimbuka ndetse by'umwihariko ahabwe mu bituza by'abaguye kuko ibihe byabo byageze. Sela!

Incamake

Bimenyekane, ko Babuloni y'Ibanga ari Leta Zunze Ubumwe za Amerika, ndetse na maraya mukuru wicara ku mazi menshi kandi ni ishyanga ryakozwe, kugira ngo inyamaswa yo mu nyanja n'inyamaswa yo mu isi zishobore kuzamuka mu ivu ryayo mu bihe byazo byo guhishurwa. Ndetse n'ishyanga risambana, ndetse n'ishyanga ry'ubusambanyi, ishyanga riri rwose maraya mukuru wiyita umukurikira w'ukuri wa Yesu w'i Nazareti. Ariko mu by'ukuri ni ishyanga ryubatswe n'abubatsi bigobotoye ku Rutare rw'Urufatiro rw'ukuri, ndetse n'iryo buye rikomeye ry'imfuruka abatari abizerwa batsitaraho, ari we Yesu w'i Nazareti ari we Yeshua HaMashiach.

Ibyaha n'Amakosa

Igikorwa cy'Imbuto y'Intambara / Iterambere ry'Ikimenyetso cy'Inyamaswa

  1. Yewe Babuloni y'Ibanga, n'uburozi bwawe n'ubupfumu bwawe washutse isi kwemera uburozi, ndetse n'intwaro y'ibanze kandi yatoranyijwe y'ibinyabuzima barogesheje abakunda isi n'uburozi bwabo kandi bakanga kwihana kuko nari kubakiza!
  2. Mwumve ko Igikorwa cy'Imbuto y'Intambara n' kurumwa n'inzoka cye bitari ikimenyetso cy'inyamaswa, ahubwo ikimenyetso cy'inyamaswa kiziyoberanya nk'umuti kandi ni indahiro yuzuye y'ubudahemuka yo kwemera ikoranabuhanga ryabo rizasiba iteka izina rya Yahweh ku muntu wese ubyemeye.
  3. Ndetse n' kurumwa n'inzoka aho sisitemu yabo yamaze gushyirwa ahagaragara kandi ikageragezwa, kandi imbaga yamaze gutegurwa kwemera ikimenyetso cyabo kandi Igikorwa cyabo cy'Imbuto y'Intambara cyagenze neza.
  4. Kuri wowe Babuloni y'Amayobera, havuye aya makoranabuhanga y'ikuzimu kandi binyuze mu binyoma byawe n'uburiganya bwawe imbaga yararozwe kandi urubanza rwawe ntirutinda.

Kwemera ikibi

  1. Benshi muri mwe muri abibone cyane kuko mugerageza kungira Umwami mu gihe mugifite umutima utizera, kuko umutima ari wo umuntu yizeza kugira ngo akiranuke. Bityo mugire kwizera kugira ngo mukizwe; kwizera kwanyu kuzerekanwa kuri bose binyuze mu kwanga icyaha no kuva mu nzira zanyu zanduye.
  2. Ababi bagenda impande zose, iyo abantu babi cyane bashyizwe hejuru. Ariko muracyemera inzira z'abasodoma, kandi mufasha kuzana ikibi gikomeye mu gihugu, kugeza n'aho mutora abahohotera abana ngo bayobore ibihugu byanyu. Mubura ndetse n'urwego rw'ibanze rwo kurobanura, kandi ntimukurikira inzira yanjye; kandi nimukomeza, nzabarekurira rwose irari ryanyu ribi kugira ngo umubiri urimbuke kugira ngo umwuka ukizwe.
  3. Reka mfungure amaso yanyu, mwe mwirengagiza kubushake kandi mwe mufite ubuhumyi, mwe mwicara mu mwanda n'umwijima, mwivuruguta mu byondo. Ntimushobora kwemera ikibi ngo mutekereze ko munkorera, kuko narwanyije ikibi n'ibishuko kugeza igitonyanga cya nyuma cy'amaraso kimenetse giturutse kuri njye kigwa ku butaka butukura, kuko ndi Adamu wa nyuma kandi nahinduwe umwuka utanga ubuzima.
  4. Benshi muri mwe mwahumwe amaso n'amagambo meza n'imbwirwaruhame nziza kandi mwemera rwose abagizi ba nabi n'abantu babi bahiga abana. Bahohotera abana, biyaraguza abana kubera irari ryabo ribi: Kuko ari ko umujinya w'Imana uza kuri Babuloni y'Amayobera, ariko benshi muri mwe mukomeza kwemera izi nyamaswa z'inkazi zashyiriweho umuriro w'iteka utazigera uzima.
  5. Nimuveyo, bantu banjye, cyangwa muzasangira ibyago byayo n'urubanza rubi ruyizaho; kandi nababuriye. Kuko Kristo afitanye masezerano ki na Beliyali? Nimusangira ibinyoma byayo, muzasangira ibyago byayo; bityo mwitandukanye rwose mu magambo no mu bikorwa, kandi ntimwemeranye na bo na gato. Ntimukabeho ubuzima burangwamo uburangare, bwiyandarika; kuko nzahana ababikora bose. Mutekereza ko kubera ko mufite ubuzima bw'iteka mudashobora guhura n'igihombo gikomeye n'ingaruka? Nzambura ingororano zanyu nzihe abandi, kandi muzaba abato mu bwami bwo mu ijuru nimukomeza, kuko ari ko Yeshua HaMashiach, Umukwe, abwira Umugeni wanjye!
  6. Bimenyekane! Abanzi ba Babuloni y'Ibanga nta mpuhwe bafite, nta mbabazi, nta buntu, nta kwicuza, kandi banga rwose abaturage ba Babuloni y'Amayobera kubera ibyaha bibi yakoreye mu bihugu byabo. Kandi mu gihe icyo ari cyo cyose benshi muri mwe mwishimiye intambara zanyu zidakiranuka mu gihe mwarebaga mushimishijwe kuri televiziyo zanyu, mu gihe mwishimiraga cyane ibyaha by'abayobozi banyu. Mwemereye abayobozi banyu ibyaha byabo mwemeje binyuze mu matora, bityo muzasangira urubanza rwabo, kuko mwihinduye abakwiriye umugabane wikubye kabiri w'urubanza, kandi ruzabatungura.
  7. Dore! Abayobozi bose b'isi bakorera hamwe mu ibanga, kandi bose bakorera Satani. Ntimufite abayobozi batowe, ahubwo muhabwa amahitamo y'ibinyoma kuko mutigeze muhitamo abo mutekereza ko mutora binyuze mu matora, ahubwo babashyirwa imbere kugira ngo mubatore kugira ngo babone kwemera kwanyu kandi kwemera kwanyu ni icyaha kuko mudashobora guhitamo kubushake ikibi gito ngo munshyigikire icyo gihe kimwe, uko ni ukuyoba.
  8. Babuloni y'Ibanga, utanga kubushake kwemera kwawe ku bayobozi bawe ba Satani, bazi neza imigambi yabo kandi bazi neza ko bari mu gikorwa cyo kurimbura rwose ishyanga ryawe ryahoze rikomeye nawe ubwawe.
  9. Kuko wowe Babuloni y'Ibanga mu busambanyi bwawe n'uburaya bwawe, uryamana n'umwanzi.
  10. Kuko muri ubwo buryo, wabayeho mu byishimo mu gihe abayobozi bawe barimburaga andi mahanga ubyemeye.
  11. Kuko muri ubwo buryo, Babuloni y'Ibanga, wishimiye mu gihe abayobozi bawe bazanaga amakuba ku nkombe z'amahanga atabarika mu ntambara zabo zidakiranuka wowe washimishijwe no kureba kuri televiziyo.
  12. Kuko ari Isinagogi ya Satani yakoze amahitamo yayo igahitamo Moshiach wayo mushya w'ikinyoma kandi Babuloni y'Ibanga yongeye guha ikiyoka kwemera kwayo.
  13. Ndetse imbere y'amaso yanyu bwite kandi binyuze mu kwemera kw'ijwi ryanyu no binyuze mu bunebwe bwanyu, ishyanga ryanyu ryose ryasenywe imbere y'amaso yanyu.

Kandi uko ni ko bizakorwa kuko byatangajwe.

Igikombe cya Zahabu: Ubusambanyi n'Uburaya, n'ibindi.

  1. Mumenye ko urubanza ruzagera kuri Babuloni Ikomeye vuba, kandi ruzaba urubanza ruteye ubwoba, ariko rurakwiye. Yewe Amerika, nzokwihanganira kugeza ryari? Wavuye ku ishyanga ry'abera ryabereye urumuri isi iza aho ndi, uhinduka ishyanga ry'indaya zisambana.
  2. Natandukanyije ubwoko bwanjye nawe, kandi gutandukana kurenda kuzura uko iki gihe cy'ubuntu kigana ku musozo. Yewe Amerika, kuki ukurikira umubi, ukemera ruswa ze zita agaciro kunduta, jyewe Kristo uhoraho, mu bwiza bwanjye bwose n'ikuzo. Kandi indaya ikomeye yononnye isi n'igikombe cya zahabu kiri mu kuboko kwayo, cyuzuye ibizira n'umwanda w'ubusambanyi bwayo.
  3. Nyamara wowe, yewe Amerika, wanga iby'umwuka ugasuzugura abaturanyi bawe, ubu ugiye kwihanganira urubanza rukomeye mu gihugu cyawe, kandi Babuloni Ikomeye ntizongera kubaho ukundi. Kwishimisha mu Bibo / Farumakeya - Ibiyobyabwenge / Ibinezeza by'Icyaha

  1. Yewe Amerika: Utekereza mu bujiji ko byose byasubiye mu buryo, kandi ko intambara igiye kuza ishobora kureberwa kuri televiziyo nk'izindi ntambara abayobozi bawe bashoje mu buryo butaboneye. Nk'uko washimishijwe n'urupfu n'irimbuka ry'andi mahanga, niko n'ihanga ryawe rizarimburwa rwose, kandi urupfu rwawe n'irimbuka ryawe bizaba indishyi ku barimbuwe ubyemeye.
  2. Igihe cyawe cyiza cyabayeho kuko mu gihe runaka ba So bakurikiye Umwana w'Imana kandi bakitondera Amagambo yanjye.   3. Kuko Babuloni y'Ibanga yayobeje amahanga mu byaha bikomeye n'ibishuko binyuze mu burozi bwayo n'ubupfumu bwayo ndetse n'imyidagaduro yayo yitwa iy'imihango n'ubupfumu bwayo buteye ishozi bwitiriwe imiti nyamara mu by'ukuri ari uburozi bwica.
  4. Mwiyigurishije mu mururumba mubi n'ubuhemu, kandi mwarantaye, njyewe Yesu Kristo, wapfiriye ibyaha by'isi. Ariko mwanze ijambo ryanjye, mwanze igacyaha cyanjye, mwanze inama yanjye, mwanze intumwa zanjye, mwanze abahanuzi banjye, mwanze intumwa zanjye, kandi mwanze njyewe, Yesu w'i Nazareti.

Iyaba mwari mwateze amatwi amagambo yanjye kuko nahamagaye, naringinze, naratakambye, narahuguye, naravuza induru, nerekana ibimenyetso hejuru n'ibimenyetso mu isi ariko aho kwambika imitima yanyu ibigunira, aho kwicara mu ivu ry'ukwihana ahubwo murishimisha nk'abareba ibitangaza by'amarozi.
  6. Mwatanze mutabizi abana banyu bataravuka kugira ngo muteze imbere gahunda y'inyamaswa, kandi abaguye bemeye ibitambo byanyu. Bakoresheje amaraso y'abaziranenge nk'uko bahora babikora mu gukora ibizira byabo, kandi nk'uko byari mu minsi ya Nowa niko bizaba. Kuko n'uwabuzaga nakuweho, nibwo muzasobanukirwa ubunini n'umubare w'ibizira byabo, kuko bizarekurwa rwose nta nkomyi.

Imibereho y'Ibyaha Inezeza / Ubwibone / Kwinangira Umutima

  1. Kuko yabayeho mu byishimo mu byaha bikomeye; yicaye mu bwibone bwinshi atekereza ko adashobora kugwa kandi ko ibintu bizakomeza, ejo hashize nk'uyu munsi, n'ejo hazaza bimeze uko kandi bidahinduka.
  2. Ariko mu bwibone bwanyu n'inzira z'isi, mwinangiye imitima yanyu kuri njye n'agakiza kanjye.
  3. Yewe Babuloni y'Ibanga, iyaba wari wasobanukiwe igitambo cy'amahoro Data yakoherereje, kandi none waranyanze, njyewe Yesu w'i Nazareti, kandi byabaye nk'uko ibyanditswe byera bivuga ko abantu babi n'abayobya bazagenda barushaho kuba babi.
  4. Kurimbuka gutunguranye kubariho kandi abanzi banyu biteguye, nyamara muracyabaho mu byishimo nubwo muri ihanga riri mu minyururu imbere yanjye, Kristo Yesu.
  5. Babuloni y'Ibanga uri igihugu cyo kwinezeza k'ako kanya, kandi ntimugira ubwenge na busa.
  6. Ahari ubwibone bwanyu bw'igihugu buzahuma imitima yanyu kuri njye, yewe Babuloni y'Ibanga: ndetse n'iyi Leta Zunze Ubumwe. Kuko mutekereza ko mwicaye nk'umwamikazi kandi nta byago bizabagwira, kandi mwibwira muti, Sindi umupfakazi!

  8. Ejo hazaba nk'uyu munsi, kuko uyu munsi umeze nk'ejo hashize. Uko yihaye ikuzo, ikabaho mu byishimo, niko muyiha kubabazwa n'agahinda.
  9. Kuko muri ubu buryo, mwabayeho mu byishimo mu gihe abayobozi banyu barimburaga andi mahanga mubyemeye kandi n'amafaranga yanyu muri abanyabyaha nk'urumamfu rw'Abedomu mwagurishije urubyaro rwanyu ku bw'ibinezeza by'ibyaha.
  10. None urubanza rwanyu ruje rwuzuye Babuloni y'Ibanga kandi muri igitaramo buri wese areba, nyamara murakomeza mu bujiji n'ubuhumyi muvuga mu mitima yanyu ko ibyago bitazigera bibageraho.

Ibyaha ku Baziranenge

  1. Babuloni y'Ibanga icuruza abana bato nk'ibicuruzwa mu maboko y'abantu babi, izahagarikwa. Ibyiza byari kuba ko batigeze bavuka kuruta kuvuka bagakora ibyaha by'ubugome n'ibibabaza ku bana.

Ariko abanyu bwite, ndetse n'abana banyu bataravuka, ntibari bagezweho mu myambarire yanyu ihora ishira kandi ubuzima bwabo bwari inkomyi kuri mwe yo kwibiza rwose mu byishimo by'ibyaha.
  6. Mwisobanura kandi musaba amaraso y'abaziranenge mu myumvire yanyu ya politiki y'ubwicanyi, kandi mubizi mwifatanyije n'abaguye mu kwigomeka kwabo, kuko ari ba Satani bigishije gukubita urusoro mu nda, ndetse n'uburyo bwanyu bw'ubwicanyi bw'agaciro muterwa ishema no kurinda no kwirata. Nuko rero amaraso y'abaziranenge ataka ava mu isi kandi uburemere bw'ibyaha byanyu bwatumye isi idandabirana nk'umusinzi, kandi umutingito uzaba ukomeye kandi kugwa kuzaba gukomeye!   7. Kuko n'itangazamakuru ryanyu rishaka kubasinziriza kandi benshi bicaye mu mwijima bahumye batabona ukuri bavuga bati: Nicaye nk'Umwamikazi kandi sindi umupfakazi kandi sinzabona umuborogo.
  8. Ejo hazaba nk'uyu munsi, kuko uyu munsi umeze nk'ejo hashize. Uko yihaye ikuzo, ikabaho mu byishimo, niko muyiha kubabazwa n'agahinda.
  9. Mwayobejwe mu nzira z'Abanyakanani, kuko nabo batambaga abana babo, bafite ishema n'ibyishimo byinshi, nk'uko mukomeza gukora! Ariko mu bwibone bwanyu mutekereza ko mutandukanye n'abo ba kera b'abapfapfa n'abaswa kandi ko inzira zanyu zisumba iz'abo banyamusozi ba kera, nyamara mukora ibyo.
  10. Kuko uburemere bw'amaraso y'abaziranenge butakambira ijuru na n'ubu kandi busaba ubutabera, kandi Yehova yumvise ugutaka kwabo!

Babuloni y'Ibanga, watambye igisekuru cyose cy'abana ubu babaye abagabo bageze igihe cyo kujya mu gisirikare bari gushobora kukurinda ariko aho kubikora ubu baba mu mihanda y'imijyi yawe iteye ishozi kandi babaswe n'aya marozi nta byiringiro kandi babaye ibishushanyo by'abapfuye bagenda kuko ibi biyobyabwenge biborza imibiri yabo nubwo bakiri bazima.

Inyungu z'Umurengera / Umwenda / Gucuruza Abantu

  1. Ubu ndavugisha Babuloni y'Ibanga nubwo ari igipfamatwi, ikiragi, n'impumyi kandi iboshye rwose mu minyururu ku bw'urubanza rwayo.   2. Ntimusobanukiwe amabanki abika igice n'amafaranga y'impapuro? Mwige kugira ngo mumenye.
  3. Yewe Babuloni y'Ibanga, kuko wagurishije abana bawe nk'ingwate y'imyenda yawe kugira ngo ukomeze mu byishimo by'ibyaha (kuko n'amafaranga yawe ari inyungu z'umurengera), kuko wishe abaziranenge, kandi n'ibindi byinshi wakoze.
