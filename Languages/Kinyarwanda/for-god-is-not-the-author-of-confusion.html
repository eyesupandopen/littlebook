Ubuhamya Bubiri: Itorero
Mu Mirimo ya Petero
Mata 20, 2024

Kuko Imana atari Imana y'umuvurungano!

Kuko Imana atari Imana y'umuvurungano, ahubwo ni iy'amahoro, nk'uko bimeze mu matorero yose y'abera! Musome aya magambo kandi mwumve ijwi ryanjye. Tega amatwi yawe, wumve amagambo y'abanyabwenge, kandi ushyire umutima wawe ku bumenyi bwanjye. Wenda ukwiye gukurikiza urugero rwanjye rwo mu busitani ubwo nasengaga mu ishavu rikomeye nkitura hasi imbere ya Data nsenga kandi nciye bugufi, maze akampa imbaraga zo kwakira igikombe cy'umujinya w'ibyaha byanyu ubwanyu nikoreye ku bwanyu, kuko nshobora kubyihanganira. Ndabwira abibone bo mu Itorero ryanjye, kandi ndabwira amacakubiri menshi ari muri mwe. Ariko kuki ucira urubanza mwene so? Cyangwa kuki uhindura mwene so ubusa? Kuko twese tuzahagarara imbere y'intebe y'imanza ya Kristo. Mbese ntabwo byanditswe mu rwandiko noherereje abakurikira Inzira i Roma? Ariko se Zekariya abivugaho iki: Ariko banze kumva, bikurayo, baziba amatwi yabo ngo batumva. Ntimwasomye Yesaya, bana banjye bato? Kuko abahanuzi ba kera bavuga iki? Kuko Yesaya yongeye kuvuga ati: Kumva muzumva, ariko ntimuzasobanukirwa: kandi kureba muzareba, ariko ntimuzabona.

Bana banjye, naberetse inzira irushaho kuba nziza, ariko benshi muri mwe mwavuye mu buntu kandi ubwibone bwanyu bwatumye mwishyira hejuru birenze urugero. Nyamara, amagambo yanjye nahaye Intumwa Pawulo ngo ayashyire ab'i Galatiya avuga neza ati: Kuko umuntu niyibwira ko ari ikintu, kandi ari nta cyo ari cyo, aba yishutse. Bana banjye bato, nzi ko mufite ishyaka mu mutima ryo kunkorera, kandi nzi ko munkunda, gusa rimwe na rimwe bidahuje n'ubumenyi. Ntimuzi ko Imana irwanya abibone ariko ikaha ubuntu abicisha bugufi? Nuko rero, mwicishe bugufi munsi y'ukuboko gukomeye kw'Imana, kugira ngo izabashyire hejuru mu gihe gikwiriye. Namwe urubyiruko, mugandukire abakuru banyu. Yego, mwese mugandukirane, kandi mwambare kwicisha bugufi. Imana irwanya abibone ariko ikaha ubuntu abicisha bugufi. Ntimufate amagambo yanjye nk'ayasubirwamo, kuko mu kanwa k'abatangabuhamya babiri cyangwa batatu ijambo ryose rizahamwa. Musobanukirwe, bana banjye, ntimushobora kunkorera mukoresheje umubiri wanyu: Kuko ibiri mu isi byose, irari ry'umubiri, n'irari ry'amaso, n'ubwibone bw'ubuzima, bitava kuri Data, ahubwo biva mu isi. Torero ryanjye, mugandukirane mu ntekerezo zambaye kwicisha bugufi, kuko nshobora kuba ari jye utanga amagambo yo kuvuga n'ubushobozi bwo kubigisha, nubwo benshi muri mwe bazashyira ku ruhande ijwi ry'uwicisha bugufi bagahitamo izina n'umuntu runaka. Mube abigana Imana, bana banjye, kuko mu by'ukuri Imana ntirobanura ku butoni.

Dore! Ni icyubahiro cy'Imana guhisha ikintu: ariko icyubahiro cy'Abami ni ugucukumbura ikintu. Musobanukirwe ko nzabagira abami n'abatambyi imbere y'Imana yacu na Data, bityo musobanukirwe amagambo y'abanyabwenge muri mwe: mbese Imana ntiyabahaye amatwi abiri n'umunwa umwe? Ntimwasomye? Buri wese muri mwe afite Zaburi, afite inyigisho, afite ururimi, afite ihishurirwa, afite ubusobanuro: Byose bikorwe kugira ngo byubake! Ariko oya, muraryana kandi muramirana mu bwibone bwanyu; mu bwibone bwanyu mugaba ibitero ku bagerageza kubigisha, kandi mwahindutse abatumva inyigisho. Mujya impaka, muratongana, murajya impaka, kandi murarwana aho kwicisha bugufi ngo musuzume ibyo undi avuga. Ku rundi ruhande, mbese muri abibone cyane ku buryo mutekereza ko kubera ko amagambo asohoka mu minwa yanyu ari ukuri kuko MWE mwayavuze? Mugandukirane. Mufate ururimi rwanyu kandi mujyane amagambo ya bene wanyu mu byanditswe kugira ngo mugerageze byose kandi mugundire ibyiza. Muzibure amatwi yanyu kandi mwumve neza kandi mutekereze, kuko mu kubikora nzabagira abanyabwenge mu kwicisha bugufi kwanyu, bityo ntimukabe abanyabwenge mu maso yanyu. Ngira inama buri wese muri mwe, mwumve amagambo yanjye. Kandi ayo magambo avuga iki? Kuko mvuga, kubw'ubuntu nahawe, kubwira umuntu wese uri muri mwe, kutiyumvaho ibirenze ibyo akwiriye kwiyumvaho, ahubwo atekereze neza, nk'uko Imana yagabanyije buri wese urugero rwo kwizera.

Oya, nimuhagarare, mutekereze ku magambo mmaze kubaha, mufungure Bibiliya zanyu kandi musome aya magambo ukundi. Muyumve kandi muyizere, kuko mugomba kwiyumvaho ibikomeye, kuko nabazuye hamwe nanjye nkabicaza hamwe nanjye mu bintu byo mu ijuru muri jye, Kristo Yesu. Ariko icyo gihe kimwe mugomba gutekereza neza, kuko Imana yahaye buri muntu muri mwe urugero rumwe rwo kwizera, bityo umwuka watanzwe ntabwo ari mukuru cyangwa muto muri bene wanyu, ahubwo buri wese muri mwe yahawe ubuntu, nk'uko urugero rw'impano ya Kristo ruri. Ni cyo gituma avuga ati: Ubwo yazamukaga mu ijuru, yajyanye imbohe, aha abantu impano. Nahaye bamwe kuba intumwa: abandi kuba abahanuzi: abandi kuba ababwirizabutumwa: abandi kuba abungeri, n'abigisha: Kugira ngo abera batunganirizwe umurimo wo kugabura iby'Imana, no kubaka umubiri wa Kristo. Mugandukirane kandi mureke ubwibone bwanyu butagira umumaro kuko buteza amacakubiri muri mwe. Kuki mudasobanukirwa ko hatagomba kubaho amacakubiri muri mwe, bana banjye. Mbese umubiri wa Kristo waragabanyijwe? Nabwiye iki Abakorinto?

Ndabinginga bene Data mu izina ry'Umwami wacu Yesu Kristo, kugira ngo mwese muvuge rumwe, kandi he kubaho amacakubiri muri mwe: ahubwo muhuzwe rwose mu ntekerezo zimwe, no mu guca imanza kumwe. Mbese mwese ntimuri umwe muri jye, Umwami Yesu Kristo? Nyamara mufite amacakubiri menshi muri mwe, kandi muraryana kandi muramirana ku bw'ukutumvikana guto cyane. Mumenye ko mu macakubiri yanyu no gusebanya kwanyu mushimisha Satani, kandi yirata ku busa bw'imbaraga zanyu mukoresha murwana hagati yanyu kuko benshi muri mwe babuze kwicisha bugufi. Kandi amatwi yanyu arazibye ku buryo mudashobora kumva amajwi y'abandi, kandi mwahumirije amaso yanyu, kandi afatanye mu bwibone no kutizera, kuko benshi muri mwe mutekereza ko mufite ukuri kose. Kandi musuzugura amagambo ya bene wanyu, kandi musuzugura amagambo nyine nabahesheje ku bwanyu, kandi mu bwibone bwanyu bubi mubura kwicisha bugufi kw'ibanze ngo mwumve kandi mutekereze. Kuko niba umuntu atumvise ikintu, biba ari ubupfu n'igisebo kuri we. Kandi na none, kuki muhindura bene wanyu ubusa, kuko twese tugomba guhagarara imbere y'intebe y'imanza ya Kristo? Ndabagira inama, Torero ryanjye, yo kwihakana ubwibone bwanyu n'inzira zanyu z'ubwibone mukaza aho ndi nk'umunyeshuri w'Ijambo ry'ubuzima, kuko ndi Ijambo ry'Imana ryabaye umubiri.

Nuko rero mwumve n'ubugingo bwanyu bwose kandi mwite ku majwi y'amatwi yanyu kandi musobanukirwe amagambo yanjye. Nuko musobanukirwe uko ibintu bikurikirana byatanzwe mu Ijambo ryanjye, kuko benshi muri mwe bayobejwe n'amasega aryana mukurikira mu bujiji, kuko mwumviye abungeri bayobye. Muri ba nde bo kubwiriza ko nta gukoranira hamwe kw'Itorero ryanjye kuri jye, ko ntazasanganira Itorero ryanjye mu kirere ubwo nzaza n'ijwi rirenga n'ijwi rya marayika mukuru n'impanda y'Imana? Kuko impanda izavuga kandi abapfiriye muri Kristo bazabanza kuzuka namwe abazaba bakiriho basigaye ntimuzabatanga, kandi mwese muzazamurwa mu bicu gusanganira jye kandi muzabana nanjye iteka ryose. Mbese aya magambo ni meza cyane kuri mwe ngo muyizere? Cyangwa muzizera amagambo y'abungeri bayobye b'Abayuda kuruta ijwi ry'Umwami n'umukiza wanyu, Kristo Yesu? Kuki mudafite kwizera mu Ijambo ryanjye cyangwa mu magambo agize Ijambo ryanjye? Ntimusobanukiwe icyo ibanga rya bibiliya ari cyo cyangwa intego yaryo?

Nababwira: Ibanga ni ikintu kigizwe ibanga ariko cyahanuwe gihishurwa mu gihe gikwiriye. Kandi kuki Data yakora ikintu nk'icyo, murabaza? Ntimwasomye raporo yanjye? Ahubwo tuvuga ubwenge bw'Imana mu ibanga, ndetse n'ubwenge bwahishwe Imana yategetse mbere y'isi, ku bw'icyubahiro cyacu. Ubwo nta n'umwe mu batware b'iyi si yamenye: kuko iyo babumenya, ntabwo baba barabambye Umwami w'icyubahiro. Muri ba nde bo gusuzugura imigambi n'inzira bya Data? Kuko adakeneye umujyanama, nubwo inzovu inshuro inzovu zikikije intebe ye y'ubwami.

Nuko rero muhumurizanye n'amagambo yanjye, kuko mukijijwe umujinya muri jye. Hanyuma, mu nzira zanyu zo kutizera, mujya impaka ku gihe iki gikorwa kizabera. Nubwo bamwe muri mwe bavuga bibeshya ko nta gukorakoranya kuzabaho, benshi muri mwe bajya impaka ku gihe kizabera ugereranyije n'amakuba ya Yakobo. Nzabisobanura neza kuri mwe niba mwumva, nubwo benshi muri mwe bazahakana iki gikorwa binangiye, ndetse bakampakanira kandi bagashyiraho iherezo ryabo ryo gufata umwanya wabo mu bakobwa b'abapfu. Kuko Ijambo ryanjye rivuga iki?

None turabinginga, bene Data, ku byo kuza k'Umwami wacu Yesu Kristo, no gukoranira kwacu kuri we, Kugira ngo ntimukuke imitima vuba, cyangwa ngo muhagarikwe imitima n'umwuka cyangwa n'ijambo cyangwa n'urwandiko, nk'aho byavuye kuri twe, nk'aho umunsi wa Kristo ugeze. Ntihakagire umuntu ubagusha mu ikosa uburyo bwose: kuko uwo munsi utazaza, keretse habanje kubaho kugwa, kandi urya muntu w'icyaha agahishurwa, ari we mwana wo kurimbuka. (Murebe ku bya Byzance, bana banjye, kuko Bibiliya nyinshi zangiritse zavuye mu nyandiko za gnostic, inyandiko z'ibihimbano zatanzwe n'abantu b'abanyabyaha. Ntimusobanukiwe itandukaniro riri hagati y'inyandiko ya bibiliya n'ubwoko bwa Bibiliya?) Hazabaho kugwa mu kwizera mu buhakanyi, kandi abanga kuva mu kwizera bazava mu isi baze kunsanganira mu kirere.

Kuko aha ari ho hari umugani w'abakobwa icumi. Abakobwa batanu b'abanyabwenge baguze amavuta batunganya intambi zabo bari biteguye ubwo umukwe yazaga, naho batanu ntibari biteguye kandi birengagije kugura amavuta, ahubwo bagerageza gufata ku byo abandi bakeneye. Abakobwa batanu biteguye bakomeje kujyana n'umukwe, naho batanu b'abapfu basaba kwinjizwa nyuma babwirwa bati, nimuve aho ndi, sinigeze mbamenya. Ntimuzimye umwuka wanjye muri mwe wemeza aya magambo. Musobanukirwe ko umwana wo kurimbuka adashobora kujya ku butegetsi kandi ubwami bwose bwa Satani ntibushobora kuzamuka buvuye ikuzimu kugeza ubwo ubuza akuweho, kandi kuko mukijijwe umujinya, ubuza azakurwaho ubwo nzakorakoranya Umugeni wanjye kuri jye mu bicu ubwo azansanganira mu kirere.

Hanyuma ku iherezo ry'imyaka irindwi y'amakuba ya Yakobo hazaza kuzuka kw'abakiranutsi. Dore! Kandi mbona intebe z'ubwami, barazicaraho, bahabwa guca imanza: kandi mbona ubugingo bw'abaciwe imitwe babahora ubuhamya bwa Yesu, n'ijambo ry'Imana, n'abatararamije inyamaswa, cyangwa igishushanyo cyayo, cyangwa ngo bashyirweho ikimenyetso cyayo mu ruhanga rwabo, cyangwa mu ntoki zabo; kandi barabayeho bima ingoma hamwe na Kristo imyaka igihumbi. Ariko abandi bapfuye ntibongeye kubaho kugeza ubwo imyaka igihumbi irangiye. Uku ni ko kuzuka kwa mbere. Hahirwa kandi ni uwera ufite umugabane mu kuzuka kwa mbere: kuri bene abo urupfu rwa kabiri ntirubasha kugira imbaraga, ahubwo bazaba Abatambyi b'Imana, n'aba Kristo, kandi bazima ingoma hamwe na we imyaka igihumbi.

Mbese ntimurizera amagambo yanjye? Ni ukuri, benshi muri mwe bazagundira ikosa ryabo binangiye n'amagambo y'abungeri bayobye, kandi benshi muri mwe bashoboraga gucika umujinya w'Imana uza kugerageza isi mu gihe cy'amakuba ya Yakobo, bagahitamo kwihanganira umujinya mwinshi ubwo namaze kubagerageza nkabagerageza, keretse muzanga kugaragara kwanjye mwinangiye, kandi muzagundira umujinya utabagenewe. Sinababwiye ngo mumuvemo kugira ngo mutazafatanya mu byago bye?

Nyamara, hazabaho kuzuka kundi kumwe. Kandi mbona intebe y'ubwami yera nini, n'uyicayeho, isi n'ijuru bihunga mu maso he, ntihaboneka aho biba. Kandi mbona abapfuye, aboroheje n'abakomeye, bahagaze imbere y'Imana: n'ibitabo birabumburwa: n'ikindi gitabo kirabumburwa, ari cyo Gitabo cy'Ubugingo: n'abapfuye bacirwa imanza z'ibyanditswe muri ibyo bitabo, nk'uko imirimo yabo iri. Kandi inyanja igarura abapfuye bari bayirimo: n'urupfu n'ikuzimu bigarura abapfuye bari babirimo: kandi bacirwa imanza buri muntu wese nk'uko imirimo yabo iri, kuko uku ari ko kuzuka kw'abakiranirwa bantu banjye.

Kuki mudasobanukirwa ko gukorakoranya Itorero ryanjye ngo rinsanganire mu bicu mu kugaragara kwanjye bitandukanye no kugaruka kwanjye n'Umugeni wanjye n'ingabo zo mu ijuru? Kuki mudasobanukirwa ko gukorakoranya Itorero ryanjye ari ibanga ryahishuwe n'Intumwa Pawulo mu rwego rw'Ibanga Rikomeye ry'Imana. Bityo ni ibanga riri mu rindi banga, kandi ntabwo ryahishuriwe abana b'abantu byuzuye kugeza ubwo ryahishuriwe Pawulo wabyanditse, kandi narabibarindiye.

Nyamara, hari ingero nyinshi z'Imana ijya ijyana abantu mu ijuru ku mpamvu zihariye. Enoki yaragiye. Eliya yaragiye. Nagiye mu ijuru ubwo intumwa zanjye zarebaga zitangaye. Filipo yakuwe ahantu hamwe ajyanwa ahandi ubwo Data yamutwaraga ku bw'imigambi yanjye. Kandi uko ni ko umugeni wanjye azakorakoranywa kuri jye mu bicu ngo ajyanwe mu ijuru gushyingiranwa nanjye no gukorera imigambi yanjye yo gucungura.

Ntimusobanukiwe ko Umugeni wanjye azaba abami n'abatambyi imbere y'Imana yanjye na Data kandi azategekana nanjye mu bwami bwanjye buza kandi azabana nanjye iteka ryose? Kuki mutinda kwizera amagambo yanjye? Mbese mwararozwe kandi muyobywa n'abungeri bayobye? Nyamara, benshi muri mwe bazaba ubuhanuzi bwuzuza ubwabwo kandi bazishyiraho ikimenyetso cyo kugwa binyuze mu guhakana amategeko yanjye, cyane cyane inyigisho y'Itorero ryanjye rikorakoranywa kuri jye. Mukoze ishyano mwe mushyira iyi nyigisho ku bahanuzi b'ibinyoma mu mateka ya vuba, mutangaza izamurwa ry'ibinyoma, ry'ibanga rihujwe n'izindi nyigisho z'ibinyoma z'ibihe.

Musobanukirwe ko hariho ibihe mu gihe cy'Imana atari ibihe by'imicungire, kandi ntabwo ari igitekerezo kimwe. Mwige kugira ngo mwiyerekane ko mwemewe, kandi mureke kuba abanebwe mu by'ubwenge. Mureke kwishingikiriza no gusoma ubusobanuro bw'abandi bose bw'ibyanditswe, kandi mubeho ku byanditswe: Kuko mudakeneye ko umuntu uwo ari we wese abigisha. Nyamara, benshi muri mwe bazakoba kandi bange inama yanjye. Keretse iki kintu ntabwo kizakorwa mu ibanga kandi Satani azatangiza ikinyoma gikomeye cyo guhisha iki gikorwa kuko kizamenyekana ku isi yose. Gutingita gukomeye kuzabaho, kandi isi yose izahinduka ive ku isi mwamenye, kandi urya muntu w'icyaha azahishurwa kandi azamuke avuye mu ivu ryasizwe n'abapanda amafarasi bane. Ni igihe cyo kwitegura, Mugeni wanjye, kandi mubikore vuba, kuko dore kugaragara kwanjye kuri hafi!

Ndi umwana w'Umuntu wahanuwe na Enoki, uwo Imana yajyanye mu ijuru.

Ndi uwo wahinduye isura kandi akavugana na Mose na Eliya iby'igenda ryanjye, kandi Imana yajyanye Eliya mu ijuru mu gihe cye.

Ndi uwo wajyanywe mu gicu ubwo nakirwaga mu ijuru kugeza mu bihe byo gusubizaho byose.

Ndi uwo watumye Filipo ku nkone y'Umunyetiyopiya mbere y'uko Imana imujyana ahandi hantu ku isi.

Ndi uwo watwaye Pawulo mu ijuru rya gatatu nkamwereka ibintu bitavugwa atari yemerewe gusangiza abandi.

Ndi uwo uzamanuka ava mu ijuru n'ijwi rirenga gukorakoranya Umugeni wanjye ngo ansanganire mu kirere.

Ndi uwo uzazura abatangabuhamya banjye babiri nyuma y'uko bapfuye iminsi itatu n'igice, kandi nzabajyana mu ijuru.

Ndi uwo ari Alufa na Omega, kuko ndi itangiriro n'iherezo, kandi dore! Ndaza vuba kuruta uko mutekereza, nuko mube mwiteguye kunsanganira cyangwa mugwe.

Umuntu wese uzambaza izina ry'Umwami kugeza ku iherezo azakizwa.
