Dore! Ndaje: Ibaruwa y'Urukundo y'Ihumure ku Bizerwa muri Kristo

Nimwumve Amagambo ya Yeshua HaMashiach kandi mwakire Amagambo yanjye y'ihumure mwumve n'Amagambo yanjye y'ibyishimo, kuko gucungurwa kwanyu kwuzuye kuje kandi kuri hafi. Ni ukuri, ni ukuri, ni jye uje gutwara Itorero ryanjye kandi umukwe wanyu ntatinda mugeni wanjye, nuko rero muhagarare mushikamye mu kwizera gukomeye kandi mureke Amagambo yanjye n'imbaraga zanjye n'ubushobozi bibe ugushikama kw'ibihe byanyu n'imbaraga z'agakiza, kandi kubaha Uwiteka ni yo butunzi bwanyu.

Ndetse n'ibihe bibagezeho bakunzi banjye kandi ibihe byo guhishurwa kwanjye kwuzuye birageze. Kandi nzihishurira buri wese mu gihe cye. Nzabanza nihishure byuzuye ku bizerwa muri Kristo mu ijuru, mu buryo butunguranye kandi butitezwe ubu kuri rya jwi rirenga rya Marayika mukuru, kandi iyo mpanda ikomeye izavuga kuko yahawe mu ntoki ngo ivuge ku itegeko ryanjye muri icyo gihe nyacyo cyatunganijwe cyatoranyijwe na Yahweh ubwe mu bihe bya kera by'iteka! Ndetse no mu migambi ye y'iteka n'irahoraho y'agakiza k'urubyaro rwa Adamu kandi ijwi ryayo rizuzura isi n'Abizerwa muri Kristo bazumva ngo "Zamuka hano!" mu buryo butunguranye kandi butitezwe ubu.

Ariko abahemu n'abarinangiye bazasigara inyuma hamwe n'abaguye n'abatizera kugira ngo bambaze izina ryanjye kugeza ku iherezo ryabo, kandi abazabikora bose bazajyanwa mu gakiza k'iteka n'ubwiza, ndetse n'ingororano y'iteka ifite agaciro n'uburemere bitabasha kugereranywa n'imibabaro n'ibigeragezo byo kuri iyi si, ari bito cyane kandi nta gaciro bifite ubigereranyije. Uko ni ko avuga uwababajwe n'igihano cyuzuye cy'ibyaha byanyu mu mubiri wanjye utagira icyaha, mvusha mu mubabaro ukomeye amaraso yanjye atagira icyaha kugeza ku gitonyanga cya nyuma, kandi nta cyo nabimye bakunzi banjye. Nuko rero, nimundebereho, Yesu w'i Nazareti, ndetse n'Umwana w'Intama w'Imana ari we mutangizi n'usohoza kwizera kwanyu, uwo kubw'ibyishimo byashyizwe imbere yanjye, nihanganiye umusaraba, nirengagije isoni, kandi nicaye iburyo bw'intebe y'Imana. Sela!

Ndetse namwe, abizerwa muri Kristo, mwese muzajyanwa mu ijuru ry'amajuru aho nzabamurikira Yahweh ubwe! Kandi muzatangazwa n'ubwiza muzaba mwambaye, kandi muzamenya nk'uko mwamenywe, kandi muzabona nk'uko mwabonwe, kandi muzumva nk'uko mwumviswe n'Imana Ihoraho yo Gukiranuka, ndetse na Yahweh ubwe wibuka buri Jambo muri buri isengesho mwigeze kuvuga ryaba irivuzwe mu ijwi riranguruye n'ingingo zanyu z'itumanaho, cyangwa bucece mu mitima yanyu, kuko Yahweh ari we mugenzuzi w'imitima, kandi ni iki gishobora guhishwa amaso ye? Ni ukuri, ni ukuri, ni iki gishobora guhishwa amaso yanjye? Nuko rero, mumenye ko Data nanjye turi umwe mu bumwe n'umugambi kandi ibyo Data afite byose, yabishyize mu ntoki zanjye ku bw'agakiza ka Adamu, kandi mu by'ukuri agakiza n'iyezwa ry'isi ubwayo, kuko isi ari iyanjye kandi mu gukiranuka ndaje gucira urubanza no kurwanya abaguye bagejeje igihe cyabo, kandi urubanza rwabo rwuzuye ntirutinda kandi ntibazacika.

Nuko rero, ndabaha gusobanukirwa bakunzi banjye ngo munhagararire mushikamye kandi mukomeze mwuzuze amatara yanyu amavuta y'umwuka wanjye nabateguriye kandi mugendere mu mwuka wanjye wo Gukiranuka nk'uko mugenda mu kwizera kuko umukiranutsi azabeshwaho no kwizera, Sela! Kandi kugira ngo mudashukwa ngo mugende muri iyi saha yikereye mu mbaraga z'ubushobozi bwanyu mwuzuza amatara yanyu amavuta yanduye ashingiye ku mirimo yanyu bwite, ahubwo mwuzuze amatara yanyu amavuta atunganye y'umwuka wanjye kugira ngo inzira zanyu zimurikirwe, kuko ni ukuri, ni ukuri, Ijambo ryanjye ni itara ry'ibirenge byanyu. Kandi nabujuje mwese kugeza ku ndunduro umwuka wanjye w'Ubwami kandi nasutse umwuka wanjye muri iyi minsi y'imperuka nk'uko byasezeranijwe na Yoweli mu nyandiko za Kera zahawe ba Data, ari bo ba So ku bw'umurage w'isezerano kuko muri abana b'isezerano, ndetse abana ba Aburahamu Wizerwa, Sela!

Reka mbigishe uko mukata intambi zanyu kuko muri umucyo w'Isi udashobora guhishwa! Ni binyuze mu Magambo yanjye n'umwuka wanjye muhabwa amabwiriza mu gukiranuka, kandi binyuze mu mwuka wanjye w'ubwami mwemezwa gukiranuka, kugira ngo ni ukuri, ni ukuri, mubashe kubaho mu gukiranuka muri byose. Kuko ari binyuze mu mwuka wanjye w'ubwami gukiranuka kwanjye nyakuri gushyirwa muri mwe, aho ni ukuri, ni ukuri mwese mufite amahitamo y'uwo muzumvira, mwaba mwumvira icyaha kiganisha ku rupfu, cyangwa kumvira kugeza ku gukiranuka kugeza ku bugingo buhoraho n'ingororano yuzuye. Nuko rero, mwumvire amabwiriza yanjye yihariye ku buzima bwanyu kandi mwicishe bugufi ku ijwi ryanjye no ku majwi y'abo ntuma kandi bamvugira, kandi ku mbuto zabo muzabamenya, no ku Magambo yabo mbaha ku bw'agakiza kanyu n'imibereho myiza yanyu. Kandi ndabategeka gusohoza agakiza kanyu mutinya kandi muhinda umushyitsi, kandi ndabategeka kutimana ibyiza igihe biri mu mbaraga z'amaboko yanyu kubikora.

Nuko rero, mumenye ko nahaye imbaraga n'ubushobozi benshi muri mwe ku bw'imigambi yanjye y'agakiza kandi ko mukwiye gushyigikirana mu rukundo. Nuko rero, ntimukirundanyaho ibyiza by'umubiri n'iby'amafaranga nabahaye, ndetse mwimana abandi mu nzira za Satani ushaka gushyira ibipimo n'ingero ku byiza bya Yahweh, mu by'ukuri bitagira urugero kuko nta munzani ubaho mu ijuru cyangwa mu isi uhagije gupima ubwinshi bwa Yahweh yateguriye mu maso ha Yesu Kristo ku bamukunda.

Nuko rero, nimubasohokemo byuzuye kandi ntimufatanye nabo mu byaha byabo, ntimukirundanyaho ubwinshi bw'amafaranga n'iby'umubiri bya Yahweh byatanzwe mu maso ha Yesu Kristo nk'aho ari ku mbaraga z'amaboko yanyu mwageze kuri ubu bwinshi ngo bubikwe kandi burundanyirizwe irari ryanyu. Ndetse kugira ngo mwihumurize ko mwiyitayeho mu bwibone n'icyaha, ngo musekere abadafite, ndetse mwishimira imibare iri kuri konti zanyu mu isoni nyinshi.

Nuko rero, mumenye kandi muburirwe ko abakora ibintu nk'ibyo bose uhereye ku mukuru kugeza ku muto, bazahura n'igihombo gikomeye cy'ingororano kandi mu by'ukuri baziyitirira urwego rwo hasi cyane mu bwami bw'ijuru! Kandi ibyo ni ukubera ko ku bw'umururumba wanyu imirimo yanyu izashya ibe ivu ku ntebe yanjye y'imanza aho mwese muzahagarara mukisobanura. Nuko rero, mugabanye ibintu byose ku byo abera bakeneye mu rukundo hagati yanyu kandi ntimwimane ikintu cyiza na kimwe, kuko ni ukuri, ni ukuri, ubwinshi bwanyu bw'iby'umubiri n'amafaranga bwatanzwe n'ukuboko kwanjye ku bw'imigambi yanjye buvuye mu bubiko bwa Yahweh, nuko rero, ntimukirundanyaho ibyiza by'Uwiteka ahubwo mukore ibyo bintu nzabayoboramo intambwe zanyu mu gukorera no gukunda abera.

Nimusohoke mu byaha byanyu bantu banjye, mutazafatanya nabo mu manza zabo, kuko izi ari zo nzira za Babuloni y'Ibanga kandi ibihe by'urubanza rwayo birageze, kandi Adonai Yahweh uyicira urubanza rw'imigabane ibiri arakomeye, nuko rero nimuve mu nzira zayo n'ibyaha byayo bitemewe kandi bibi cyane mu maso yanjye! Sela!

Yego, mu by'ukuri, ngira inama buri wese gukomeza kuva mu byaha byanyu no kwinjira byuzuye mu gukiranuka kwanjye, kandi mundeke mbe igihagije cyanyu muri byose, kuko ni ukuri, ni ukuri, ndi we.

Ndi uje mu bicu ku bizerwa muri Kristo mu buryo butunguranye kandi butitezwe ubu, kuko isi, mu kutizera kwayo, ititeze ko Kristo Yesu azabonekera mu bicu nk'uko byasezeranijwe mu byanditswe. Nyamara, iki gihe ntikizabatungura bakunzi banjye nk'umujura nijoro, niba muguma mwicishije bugufi ku mabwiriza yanjye kandi mugakurikiza amakuru arambuye y'amategeko yanjye n'imitima yanyu yose, mumenye ko ku bw'umwete wanyu n'ubudahemuka bwanyu haza ingororano zikwiye z'umurage w'abizerwa muri Kristo, mu by'ukuri, bitarinjira byuzuye mu mitima y'abantu muri iki gihe. Nuko rero, ntimwemerere Satani kugura ingororano zanyu z'umurage mu nzira za Esawu, wari umuntu w'umuhakanyi n'umusambanyi wagurishije uburenganzira bwe bw'imfura ku isahani y'isupu yajugunywe mu mapfa. Sela!

Nuko rero, mumenye ukwegera mu gihe k'ukuboneka kwanjye n'ukuntu gucungurwa kwanyu kwuzuye kuri hafi, bakunzi banjye. Ni cyo gituma, mbaha gusobanukirwa ko ingabo zo mu ijuru zafashe imyanya yazo mu byiciro byazo n'imitondekanyirize yazo, kandi ziyerekanye ku bw'umurimo, kandi zihagaze ziteguye. Zafashe imyanya yazo, kandi ziteguye! Nuko rero, mumenye, ko mfashe iyo mpanda ikomeye mu ntoki, ko ku itegeko ryanjye abapfiriye muri Kristo bazazurwa badashobora kubora, kandi mwe abazaba bakiriho basigaye muzatwarwa gitungura musanganire nanjye mu bicu kandi uko ni ko mwese muzahindurwa iteka nka Nyagasani wanyu, kandi ku banesheje byuzuye bazahorana nanjye kuko nzabiyegereza mu isezerano nk'abanjye bwite, kandi imirimo myinshi ikomeye muzayikora, kuko umugeni wanjye ari ingabo zikomeye z'Abahanuzi, Abami, n'Abatambyi bazahabwa imbaraga n'ubushobozi bukomeye nk'umugeni wanjye w'umurwanyi, kandi ni nde uzashobora guhagarara imbere y'umugeni wanjye w'abadafa bahawe imbaraga bacunguwe mu isi mu mbaraga zikomeye no gukiranuka, Sela!

Nuko rero, mumenye ko igihe nyacyo cyatoranyijwe na Yahweh mu kumenya kwe kera cyahoze ari umunsi nyawu, igihe n'isaha, keretse ko byari bihishwe mu mutima wa Yahweh wenyine kugeza igihe ibihe bikwiye byo guhishurwa kwabyo bisohoreye mu ijuru ryonyine, kuko iki gihe ntigihishurwa ku isi, nubwo Satani ahagaze yiteguye kumira bunguri abasigaye bose ku isi, nuko rero, abazambaza izina rya Yesu bose kugeza ku iherezo ryabo bazakizwa. Mumenye ko iki gihe nyacyo nacyo kitahawe Satani ariko agomba guhagarara yiteguye kugeza arekuwe ku bw'ingoma ye ngufi nyuma yaho akazabohwa n'umunyururu ukomeye mu kuzimu mu mubabaro n'agashinyaguro aho azasobanukirwa byuzuye umurage we uje.

Kandi uko ni ko nje kubaha gusobanukirwa ibihe murimo kandi mbikora mbaha nanone Amagambo nyayo nahaye Petero ngo abwire imbaga y'abantu kuri urya munsi mukomeye wa Pentekote iyi Yubile myinshi ishize ubu, aho nubwo bimeze bityo, Amagambo ye yumvikana ari ukuri iteka ryose mu isohora ry'iteka n'iteka ryose. Nuko ndababwira ko ibi ari byo, byavuzwe n'Umuhanuzi Yoweli, Kandi mu minsi y'imperuka, ni ko Imana ivuga, Nzasuka ku mwuka wanjye ku bafite umubiri bose, kandi abahungu banyu, n'abakobwa banyu bazahanura, kandi abasore banyu bazabona iyerekwa, n'abasaza banyu bazarota inzozi. Kandi ku bagaragu banjye, no ku baja banjye nzasuka ku Mwuka wanjye muri iyo minsi, kandi bazahanura. Kandi nzerekana ibitangaza mu ijuru hejuru, n'ibimenyetso mu isi hasi, amaraso, n'umuriro, n'imyotsi icumba. Izuba rizahinduka umwijima, n'ukwezi guhinduke amaraso, mbere y'uko urya munsi mukomeye kandi w'icyubahiro w'Uwiteka uza. Kandi bizaba, ko umuntu wese uzambaza Izina ry'Uwiteka, azakizwa.

Nuko rero, ndaje gutangaza aya Magambo asohoye mu matwi yanyu uyu munsi, kandi ko mu buryo butunguranye kandi butitezwe ubu abizerwa muri Kristo bazatwarwa kandi bacungurwe kandi bahindurwe kandi bahabwe ubwiza n'ingororano iteka ryose. Nuko musobanukirwe isohora ritunguranye rigiye kuza gitungura. Ntimutege amatwi abashyiraho amatariki n'Abayuda badasobanukiwe ibyo bigisha, cyangwa ukuri nyako kw'ibyanditswe bagoreka ku bw'igihombo cyabo.

Ni cyo gituma, mbaha gusobanukirwa ko gukoranywa kw'abizerwa muri Kristo kudahujwe by'umwihariko n'umunsi wera uwo ari wo wose, cyangwa iminsi mikuru, cyangwa indi tariki cyangwa igikorwa cyahawe Isirayeli ya kera mu mategeko ya Mose, ndetse iryo tegeko rikaba ryarasohoye byuzuye muri Kristo Yesu Nyagasani wanyu Uhoraho wo Gukiranuka. Nk'uko nababwiye mu bihe byashize ndababwira nanone bakunzi banjye: Nuko rero ntihakagire umuntu ubacira urubanza ku byo murya cyangwa ibyo munywa, cyangwa ku by'umunsi wera, cyangwa ku by'imboneko z'ukwezi, cyangwa ku by'iminsi y'Isabato, Ibyo bikaba ari igicucu cy'ibizaza: ariko umubiri ni uwa Kristo.

Icyakora, musobanukirwe ko iki gikorwa gishobora kuba isegonda iryo ari ryo ryose no ku munsi uwo ari wo wose kandi gishobora rwose kuba ku munsi w'ingenzi, igihe, cyangwa isaha cyangwa ndetse no mu minsi yera, cyangwa iminsi mikuru, cyangwa iminsi y'Isabato, ari igicucu cy'ibizaza! Nuko rero, mutegereze kandi murinde kandi mugire ibyiringiro bikomeye by'ukuboneka kwanjye kuko ibihe byanyu byo gucungurwa kwanyu kwuzuye byageze. Kandi nuko mbaha gusobanukirwa ndetse no ku bw'ukumvira kwanyu, ko mutegereza kandi mukarinda buri munsi (kandi atari gusa ibihe byanyu byo kurinda bikomeye mwishyiriyeho) kandi ko mwitondera cyane aya Magambo mu byiringiro byuzuye by'isohora, kuko nta gutinda kukiriho. Sela!

Ndi Kristo Yesu kandi ndi uwo wohereje aya Magambo binyuze ku ntumwa yanjye n'umugaragu wanjye kandi binyuze ku ntumwa yanjye n'umugaragu wanjye aya Magambo yoherejwe. Ndetse nk'inkunga kugira ngo Itorero ryanjye, umubiri wanjye, ndetse n'Umugeni wanjye bahumurizwe n'ihumure rikomeye kandi kugira ngo musobanukirwe byuzuye ukwegera nyako mu gihe k'iki gikorwa, kugira ngo mudatungurwa n'iki gikorwa kidashidikanywaho ko kizaba gitungura ubu.

Ndi Yeshua HaMashiach ugiye ubu kohereza abahamya banjye babiri n'ibitereko by'amatara yanjye bibiri, ndetse nk'uko byatangajwe mu Butumwa Bwiza Bw'Iteka, kuko Yahweh atigeze yisiga adafite umuhamya, kandi abahamya banjye babiri bazahamya ibihe byose no mu mbonera y'isi n'ijuru, kandi Enoki Umwanditsi azana inyandiko za kera mu ruhago rwe kandi ubuhamya bwabo buzongerwa ku Butumwa Bwiza Bw'Iteka buzahoraho iteka ryose hamwe n'Amagambo yose ya Yahweh azagarurwa ku isi mu butungane nyabwo nk'uko byanditswe mu ijuru, ndetse kugira ngo imirimo ya Satani ibashe gusenyerwa ruriya kandi kugira ngo inzira n'Amagambo bya Yahweh byuzure isi mu gukiranuka ibihe byose n'iteka ryose! Sela.
