Ubuhamya Bubiri: Itorero
Mu Mirimo y'Intumwa Pawulo
24 Mata 2024

Umva Amagambo y'Uwiteka: Ibanga Rikomeye Rihishuwe!

Yeshua HaMashiach abwiye Umugeni we ati: Mwitonde kandi mwumve amagambo yanjye, mwa batoranyijwe banjye, kandi mwumve imvugo yanjye, kuko ibanga ry'ubumana ari rikomeye! Benshi muri mwe ntimusobanukiwe ibanga rikomeye ry'Imana, nyamara riri mu mutima no mu rufatiro rw'abakurikira Inzira, haba mu nyigisho no mu bikorwa: Ni ryo shingiro ry'Ubukristo aho nabaremye muri njye, kandi muri ibyaremwe bishya muri Kristo Yesu, kuko njyewe, Yesu Kristo, ari ko nabaremye.

Kuri benshi muri mwe, ibanga rikomeye ry'Imana ryatakaye mu bihe no mu migenzo ndetse no mu buriganya bwinshi bakoresha babategera kugira ngo babayobye. Ntimutinye, bana banjye, kandi mwumve amagambo yanjye mwumve n'imvugo yanjye, kuko ngiye kubibasobanurira hano muri iyi minota ya nyuma y'ibihe. Musobanukirwe, bana banjye, ko urujijo rwinshi n'ibinyoma byinshi byazanywe mu itorero ryanjye n'amasega aryana n'inyigisho zabo z'ibinyoma. Bigabanyije Ijambo ryanjye (n'Umubiri) mu buryo bw'ibinyoma binyuze mu myizerere y'ibihe (dispensationalism), kandi iki ni ikinyoma kinini cyazanywe n'abubatse amazu yabo ku musenyi, atari kuri njye, ibuye rikuru ry'imfuruka, kuko ntagihari nta muntu numwe ushobora kubaka.

Umusenyi bahitamo kugira ibuye ry'imfuruka ry'inyubako zabo ni Bibiliya zangiritse ziyobora ku bumwe bw'amadini ku bw'imigambi umuntu w'icyaha afite mu mutima we, imigambi yo kugenzura, urupfu n'irimbuka. Umusenyi bakoresheje bubaka ugizwe n'inyandiko zangiritse, inyandiko z'Ikigereki zagiye zihererekanywa zivuye muri Alegizandiriya. Mukurikire inyandiko ziva mu murongo wa Antiyokiya, uwa Byzance, kandi izi ziyobora kuri Textus Receptus, umwanzi ukomeye wa Roma. Nta bihe by'imicungire (dispensations) bibaho, kandi Imana ntiyigabanyijemo, nubwo nzasobanura imicungire nk'uko igaragara mu nzandiko zanjye. Imana yagiye ikorana n'abantu ikoresheje ibihe; mwige kugira ngo mumenye. Bityo musobanukirwe, uko kwigabanya ntikuvuye kuri njye ahubwo kwakozwe kugira ngo kwangize Itorero ryanjye kuva mu ntangiriro.

Ibanga rikomeye ryabahishwe, bana banjye, ariko ntiryahishiriwe, kuko narihishuriye Intumwa Pawulo mu myaka ya Yubile myinshi ishize. Rihishwe binyuze mu binyoma, uburiganya, inyigisho z'ibinyoma zitari izanjye; ariko rihishwe aho buri wese abona, kandi umujura yifuza ko mutazigera mubibona, ariko ni ikintu kinini kigaragara. Kuko ikintu gitera Satani ubwoba cyane ku Itorero ryanjye ni uko azi ko nta bushobozi afite bwo kubagenzura iyo mugendera mu mbaraga zanjye zuzuye no mu mibanire yeruye nanjye. Ku bw'ibyo rero, kuko adashobora kumena ikimenyetso cy'Imana nabashyizeho, kandi ntawe ushobora kubakura mu maboko yanjye cyangwa mu maboko ya Data, ahubwo yagerageza kubanigira mu bukene. Atari ubukene bw'iby'umubiri; Itorero ryanjye ntiryagombye na rimwe kwamamaza ubutumwa bwiza bw'ibinyoma bw'iterambere kuko butari ubwanjye, nubwo benshi muri mwe mwizera ibyo binyoma. Satani abifuriza ubukene bw'umwuka kugira ngo abahishe abo muri bo by'ukuri kandi abashuke mwizere ko muri ibikoresho bidafite imbaraga, byagenewe iteka guhindukiza urundi rushyi no kwiruka nk'ibigwari, ibyo bikaba ari ugukoresha nabi amabwiriza yanjye bikoreshwa mu kubuza abahungu banjye kugendera mu mbaraga zabo nyakuri. Oya, sinabatumye guhindukiza urundi rushyi kugira ngo muhinduke inzirakarengane kandi muneshwe n'ikibi; nabatumye kuneshesha ikibi icyiza no gusenya imirimo ya Satani, kuko ari ko natumwe, kandi ari ko nabatumye. Ariko musobanukirwe: guhorera ni ukwanjye, ntimukihorere, ahubwo muhindukize urundi rushyi igihe bikwiye ku bw'agakiza k'undi no guha umwanya umujinya, kuko ubugingo bwinshi bwakijijwe muri ubu buryo.

Musobanukirwe: babashutse n'ibinyoma byabo, kandi bashyize ibyo binyoma birwanya ukuri kwanjye iruhande rw'ibyanditswe byera. Babikoze mu mpera z'amapaji ya Bibiliya z'icyigisho; babikoze bakoresheje ubusobanuro bw'ibinyoma; babikoze mu buriganya bwo kugoreka ubusobanuro bumwe bw'Ikigereki bushingiye ku muco wa gipagani wa kera aho gushingira ku kuri kw'Ijambo Ryera ry'Imana. Mushobora gukoresha aya masoko rimwe na rimwe, ariko mwitonde cyane kandi mureke kwemerera umuntu wa kamere kugerageza kubasobanurira iby'umwuka; kuko umuntu wa kamere adashobora gusobanukirwa iby'umwuka, kuko ari ubupfu kuri we. Ku bw'ibyo, mumenye ko benshi muri aba batekamutwe batema ibyanditswe nta kwizera bafite mu Ijambo ry'Imana kandi ntibakwiriye umurimo bihayemo kuko badafite umwuka wa Kristo kandi si abanjye; kuko iyo baba abanjye, nari kubayobora mu kuri. Ni njye wabashyizeho ikimenyetso cy'incungu yuzuye mu mbaraga z'umwuka wanjye wera.

Umwami Yesu Kristo abwiye Umugeni we akunda ati: Ndashaka ko musobanukirwa intego yanyu nyakuri, kandi niba mudasobanukiwe ibanga rikomeye nk'uko ribareba, ntimuzagendera mu bwuzuzanye bw'imbaraga nabahamagariye kugenderamo, ahubwo muzacika intege mu mbaraga zanyu nk'uko benshi muri mwe byabagendekeye. Birambabaza kandi bigashengura umutima wanjye kubona abana banjye badasobanukiwe abo bari bo by'ukuri kandi nta cyifuzo bafite cyo gushakashaka. Mugomba guhora mushakashaka amagambo yanjye n'umwete wose; ariko inyigisho z'ibinyoma zituma benshi birara, kuko nta ntungamubiri zirimo. Kuko ari ibinyoma bya Satani, ariko benshi batungwa n'ibiryo byihuse n'ibinyobwa birimo isukari, kandi abenshi babura imbaraga zo kurya ibiryo nabahaye no kunywa ikinyobwa nabahaye, kuko nimubikora ntimuzongera gusonza cyangwa kugira inyota ukundi.

Nuko rero mwumve amagambo yanjye, Mugeni wanjye wiraye; igihe kirageze ngo muhagurukire umuhamagaro wanyu nyakuri kandi mukore imirimo nabateguriye, kandi mumenye ko nzakora ibitangaza byinshi mbinyujije mu maboko yanyu uko mugendera muri njye mu kwizera no mu bumenyi nyakuri. Kuko navuze iki mu murimo wanjye kuri Isirayeli kera nkigendera ku mugaragaro i Yudaya? Kuko inkuru ya Luka ibivugaho iki: Arababwira ati, Mwebweho mwahawe kumenya ibanga ry'ubwami bw'Imana: ariko abari hanze, ibyo byose babibwirwa mu migani. Bityo musobanukirwe, intumwa zanjye zatoranyirijwe kwakira ibanga rikomeye ry'Imana nazihishuriye. Barabyanditse, kandi narinze inyandiko kugira ngo mumenye ukuri kw'ibyo bintu.

Benshi cyane muri mwe bumvira Abayudaiza bashaka kubasubiza munsi ya Tora. Birashimishije kumenya ko iyo bitaba ku bw'ibanga rikomeye ry'Imana, abenshi muri mwe ntimuba muzi Tora; kuko itahawe amahanga y'abanyamahanga ahubwo yahawe Isirayeli ya kera ngo iyitondere, ibe umurezi wabo wo kubanzanira. Kuko ari njye musozo w'amategeko, kandi abagundira amategeko ya Mose n'ishyaka bashaka guhimbaza imibiri yabo no kugendera mu gukiranuka kwabo bwite, kandi babuze gusobanukirwa ko Tora yubahirizwa mu buryo bwuzuye kandi kugeza ku nyuguti binyuze mu kwifatanya kwanyu nanjye. Kuko ari njye wenyine washoboye kwitondera amategeko, kandi njyewe, Yesu w'i Nazareti nasohoje ibisabwa byo gukiranuka by'amategeko. Nemeje ibyo nagezeho mu maraso yanjye bwite, maze mbuhagira mu mazi atemba y'umwuka wanjye uko mwabatirijwe muri njye.

Kuko nategetse Pawulo kubwira iki Abakorinto? Kuko mu Mwuka umwe twese twabatirijwe mu mubiri umwe, twaba Abayuda cyangwa Abanyamahanga, twaba imbata cyangwa ab'umudendezo; kandi twese twahawe kunywa ku Mwuka umwe. Niba mugerageza kwitondera amategeko, muzananirwa kandi muzabura umuhamagaro wanyu, kuko nahurije amategeko mu mategeko abiri akomeye: Kandi ukunde Uwiteka Imana yawe n'umutima wawe wose, n'ubugingo bwawe bwose, n'ubwenge bwawe bwose, n'imbaraga zawe zose: iri ni ryo tegeko rya mbere. N'irya kabiri rihwanye na ryo, ni iri, Ukunde mugenzi wawe nk'uko wikunda. Mumenye, bana banjye, ko nta yandi mategeko aruta aya, kuko muri aya amategeko yubahirizwa.

Musobanukirwe: Ibi byose mbisobanura mu nzandiko noherereje amatorero yanjye mu bihe byashize. Bityo musobanukirwe ko nahishuye ibanga rikomeye ry'Imana, kandi abahanuzi ba kera bashakashatse n'umwete babaza iby'iri banga bari bazi, ariko ntibyari byarahawe bo kubimenya. Ndifuza ko musobanukirwa ko nubwo igifuniko cya Bibiliya zanyu kivuga ngo “Yera,” si iyera ku bw'agaciro kayo bwite, kuko ari igifuniko cy'igitabo gusa. Igikora Bibiliya iyera ni uko ibitabo bahambira bikubiyemo Ibyanditswe Byera byoherejwe n'umwuka ku bana b'Isumbabyose, byoherejwe na Yahweh, Imana ikomeye ihoraho, kuko nta wuhwanye na yo. Nuko rero mwumve amagambo yanjye yera n'ubugingo bwanyu bwose! Kuko Petero yavuze iki kuri mwe, bana banjye? Iyo agakiza abahanuzi babajije, bagashakashaka n'umwete, abahanuye iby'ubuntu bwagombaga kubazaho, bashakashaka kumenya igihe cyangwa uburyo bw'igihe Umwuka wa Kristo wari muri bo yerekanaga, ubwo yahamanyaga mbere y'igihe imibabaro ya Kristo, n'ubwiza bwagombaga gukurikiraho.

Bana banjye, muri kubaho mutabizi mu ngwate y'ubwiza bwagombaga gukurikiraho, kandi umurage wanyu wuzuye ugiye kubahishurirwa muri njye, Kristo Yesu, kuko vuba aha muzashikuzwa kandi muhabwe ubwiza bwuzuye. None se iri banga ni irihe? Kandi murisanga he? Ndababwira, kuko ndi Umwami Yesu Kristo, uragira umukumbi, kandi ndi Umukwe w'Umugeni wanjye, kandi muri kubaho mu ibanga rikomeye ry'Imana, nubwo benshi muri mwe bagendera mu mwijima kuri iyi ngingo. Ntimutinye, nzabaha gusobanukirwa, kandi abanyabwenge n'abicisha bugufi nzabaha ubwenge. Ariko ku bari muri mwe bazirengagiza amagambo yanjye, mubikora mundwanya; kandi muba muri kumwe nanjye cyangwa mundwanya, nta hagati habaho. Kuko mbagira inama mwese yo kunyigiraho, mwumve amagambo yanjye kandi ntimwirengagize ijwi ryanjye, kuko nabahaye ejo hazaza n'intego muri njye. Ndi Umwungeri mwiza: umwungeri mwiza atangira intama ze ubugingo bwe, kandi intama zanjye zumva ijwi ryanjye, nuko rero mureke gukurikira abakozi b'abacanshuro biyoberanya nkanjye!

Dore! Umwuka aravuga ati: Hariho amabanga menshi mu Ibanga Rikomeye ry'Imana! Ndahera ku Ibanga Rikomeye, hanyuma mvuge ku yandi mabanga arimo. Mbere y'uko dukomeza, muhanagure igihu cy'imyizerere y'ibihe (dispensationalist) ku madarubindi yanyu kandi mufungure amaso yanyu murebe: Amavanjiri nk'uko muyazi, imirimo ya Matayo, Mariko, Luka, na Yohana, ni ingenzi kuri bose. Ibi bitabo byerekana isohozwa ryanjye ry'amategeko kugeza ku nyuguti, nta na kimwe gisigaye kidakozwe. Binyerekana mu kuza kwanjye muri Isirayeli. Binyuze mu kumvira amavanjiri ane nk'uko azwi, mwiga kugenda nk'uko nagendaga, kuko ari ko mwategetswe. Nyamara, si yo mavanjiri yonyine; oya, si yo makuru meza yonyine anyerekeyeho.

Kuko nahaye ubutumwa bwiza bw'Ibanga Rikomeye ry'Imana Intumwa Pawulo mu nzandiko ze zigenewe itorero, zoherejwe zivuye kuri njye zijya kuri we kugira ngo abibahishurire. Ese murakoba iki gitekerezo? Mutekereza ko mbwiriza irindi vanjiri kandi ko ndi ijwi ry'ikinyoma? Ese mumenyereye gukurikira amajwi y'abacanshuro ku buryo mutakizi ijwi ryanjye? Ku bazakoba, mukwiye kwita ku ijwi ry'Abagalatiya ubwo Pawulo yavugaga ati: Ariko ndabamenyesha, bene Data, ko Ubutumwa bwiza nababwirije, butari ubw'abantu. Kuko ntabuhawe n'umuntu, haba no kubwigishwa, ahubwo nabuhawe mu guhishurirwa na Yesu Kristo. Pawulo yavuze mu budahemuka ukuri namuhaye, nyamara benshi muri mwe mwirengagiza amagambo namuhaye, ndetse mugapfobya amagambo mu mvugo ngo: Yewe, ibyo ni ibyo Pawulo yanditse gusa, cyangwa, izo ni inzandiko za Pawulo gusa; si amagambo ya Kristo kuko atanditse mu ibara ritukura! Wa mupfu we! Muri mu ikosa; mufungure amaso yanyu murebe umucyo wanjye n'ukuri kwanjye! Nahaye Pawulo ivanjiri y'ibanga. Ese muri abapfu bigeze aho kwizera ko hariho imirimo ine gusa migufi ikubiyemo Ivanjiri yanjye?

Nyamuneka mwumve amagambo ya Kristo Yesu, bana banjye, kuko nzigisha ababishaka. Kuko Ibanga ry'Ubumana ari rikomeye n'ivanjiri irimo, kuko natangaje inkuru nziza y'ibanga. Ariko sinzabasiga n'ijwi ry'umuhamya umwe gusa; ijambo ryose rikomezwe n'akanwa k'abahamya babiri cyangwa batatu. Kuko urwandiko noherereje Abaroma rushaka kubabwira ijambo: Nuko Imana ishobora kubakomeza nk'uko ubutumwa bwiza bwanjye buri, no kubwiriza kwa Yesu Kristo, nk'uko guhishurwa kw'ibanga rya kera ryahishwe uhereye kera kose kuri. Inkuru nziza ya Pawulo ni ihishurwa ry'ibanga ryahishwe uhereye kera kose, kimwe n'uko Matayo, Mariko, Luka, na Yohana ari inkuru nziza yo kuza kwanjye kwa mbere muri Isirayeli.

Ndi Umwami Wizerwa kandi amagambo yanjye ni ukuri, nuko rero muntege amatwi yanyu kandi cyane cyane umutima wanyu, kuko umutima ari wo umuntu yizeza akabarwaho gukiranuka. Ndabasaba kugira ukwizera kungana n'akabuto ka sinapi gusa, kuko iyo mbuto izakura cyane muri mwe mu gihe yitaweho neza. Kuko Pawulo yabwiye iki Abakorinto: Ahubwo tuvuga ubwenge bw'Imana mu ibanga, ari bwo bwenge bwahishwe, ubwo Imana yateguriye kera isi itarabaho, kugira ngo buduheshe ubwiza. Ubwo nta n'umwe mu batware b'iyi si wamenye: kuko iyaba babumenye, ntibaba barabambye Umwami w'ubwiza. Byanditswe mu buryo bwumvikana, bana banjye, kandi ntibikeneye ubusobanuro, ariko ndasobanura ho gato.

Ese murasobanukirwa aya magambo, bana banjye? Ese murasobanukirwa uburyo muri ab'agaciro kenshi kuba Imana yarabashyizeho ijisho ryayo mbere y'irema ry'isi, kandi nk'uko ihoraho, namwe ni ko muri, kuko mwabaye ku isonga mu ntekerezo zayo mbere y'ibihe, kandi yabahaye ubugingo buhoraho muri njye, Kristo Yesu? Ibihe byanyu n'ubugingo bwanyu byateguwe na Yahweh, Imana ikomeye kandi itangaje, iyo umucyo wayo nta mubiri n'umwe ushobora kureba. Iyo Satani amenya imigambi ya Data, ntaba yarambambye, kuko mu kumvira kwanjye kugeza ku rupfu no mu kuzuka kwanjye ari ho huzurijwe imbaraga zamunesheje kandi ararimbutse rwose. Ntiyigeze yifuza itorero rikomeye rihabwa imbaraga n'umwuka wanjye kandi nkoramo imirimo ikomeye binyuze mu maboko yabo.

Ese ibyo birabakora ku mutima? Mureke guhagarika umutima mu ntekerezo zanyu kuko intumwa zanjye zibabwira ibyo mudashaka kumva, kandi muneshe ubwibone bwanyu, kuko nzayobora abicisha bugufi gusa kandi nzarwanya abibone. Mwumve amagambo yanjye kandi mureke acengere mu mizi y'ubugingo bwanyu; kuko nabaremye muri njye, kandi muri abanyambaraga, kandi muri ibitangaza; kuko mwejejwe mu maraso yanjye bwite, ndetse amaraso y'umwana w'intama. Kuko ndi umutwe w'Umubiri wanjye ukorera abantu banjye nkomeje guhamagara imirimo myinshi ikomeye, gusa mugundire Umwami wanyu, Yeshua HaMashiach, mu mutima umwe no mu bumwe bw'intego, kuko ndi Umwami w'abami.

Umuhamya wizerwa, n'imfura yo kuzuka mu bapfuye abwiye abizerwa be n'abatoranyijwe be! Hariho itorero rya kera muri Efeso, kandi kuri bo ni ho hahishuriwe ubwuzuzanye bw'Ibanga Rikomeye ry'Imana mu rwandiko rwoherejwe na Pawulo ruvuye kuri njye, kuko ativugiye ku bwe. Ndasobanura ho gato, ariko mumenye ko ntegereje kumvira kwanyu mu kwizera kuri njye, Yesu w'i Nazareti, kandi uhawe byinshi asabwa byinshi. Mbere y'uko nkomeza, ndashaka ko musuzuma ibi bikurikira bivuye i Kolosayi, kuko noherereje urwandiko Abakolosayi na bo: Kuko mwapfuye, kandi ubugingo bwanyu bukaba buhishanywe na Kristo mu Mana. Kristo, ari we bugingo bwacu, n'erekanwa, namwe muzerekananwa na we mu bwiza.

Bana banjye, mwapfuye igihe napfaga, kandi mwipfira ubwanyu buri munsi mwibwira ko kamere y'umuntu wa kera yapfuye; kuko mwabambanywe nanjye, kandi nta handi hantu na hamwe mu byanditswe muzabona indorerwamo y'abo muri bo by'ukuri, kuko nta kindi gice cy'amavanjiri kibihishura uretse ivanjiri y'Ibanga Rikomeye nahishuriye Itorero ryanjye. Nubwo murebera mu ndorerwamo ibihu, ibyo bigiye guhinduka vuba, kandi muzamenya nk'uko namwe mumenywe. Mwibike mu Ivanjiri y'Ibanga Rikomeye ry'Imana, kuko itorero ryanjye ry'i Lawodikiya ryataye amabanga yanjye, kuko abenshi muri mwe badakunze gusoma inzandiko zanjye zigenewe Itorero kuko abungeri bayobye babayoboye ahandi bashakisha amagambo yanditse mu ibara ritukura. Bana banjye, nzi ko munkunda, nuko rero munkunde mukurikije ubumenyi, kandi ntimugendere mu buriganya.

Nuko rero ndababaza nti, ibaruwa yandikiwe Abefeso ivuga iki? Dore, ibanga rikomeye riri aho ngaho! Yatumenyesheje ibanga ry'ibyo ashaka, nk'uko yabyibwiye akabigambirira muri we, Kugira ngo mu bihe byategetswe bisohoye, ahurize ibintu byose muri Kristo, ibyo mu ijuru n'ibyo mu isi, ndetse no muri we. Nababwiye ibanga ry'ubushake bwa Yehova, iryo yahishe muri we, kuko adakeneye umujyanama. Ntimutekereze ku busobanuro bw'ibihimbano mwize ku bahanuzi b'ibinyoma mu nyandiko zabo z'imfabusa zivuga iby'ibihe, kuko Satani ahindura rwihishwa inyigisho z'ibyanditswe kugira ngo zihuze n'ubushake bwe n'imigambi ye izahinduka ubusa.

Iri banga ryahawe Pawulo, kandi ni we wari igisonga cyashinzwe kurigeza ku Itorero ryanjye, ndetse no kuri mwe niba mufite amatwi yumva n'amaso areba. Kuko ubutumwa bwiza bw'Ibanga Rikomeye bwahawe Pawulo, kandi namutumye ari mu nzira ijya i Damasiko. Mu bihe byategetswe bisohoye, Data azahuriza ibintu byose ibyo mu ijuru n'ibyo mu isi muri njye, Kristo Yesu. Nta kwitandukanya kuzongera kubaho, nta gutandukana hagati y'Imana n'abana bayo, nta rwango hagati y'Umuyahudi n'Umunyamahanga, kuko mwese mwabyawe ubwa kabiri kandi muri ibyaremwe bishya muri njye, Kristo Yesu. Ese aya magambo arabatangaje cyane? Ese amagambo yanjye atuma muyarakarira kuko mwemera ubusobanuro bw'abungeri bayobye?

Munyigireho mwicishije bugufi kugira ngo musobanukirwe. Kandi, mu Befeso Pawulo yanditse amagambo namuhaye: Ni cyo gituma njyewe Pawulo, imbohe ya Kristo Yesu ku bwanyu banyamahanga, Niba mwarumvise iby'umurimo w'ubuntu bw'Imana nahawe ku bwanyu: Yuko nahishuriwe ibanga. Kandi, ntimugwe mu mutego w'amagambo y'abahanuzi b'ibinyoma, kuko umurimo w'ubuntu bw'Imana wari umurimo we ku Itorero, kuko we (n'abandi) batoranyijwe kuba ibisonga by'amabanga y'Imana.

Kandi na none: Iryo mu bindi bihe ritamenyeshejwe abana b'abantu, nk'uko ubu ryahishuriwe intumwa zayo zera n'abahanuzi mu Mwuka, Yuko abanyamahanga bagomba kuba abaraganwa natwe, kandi bo mu mubiri umwe, n'abasangiye isezerano ryayo muri Kristo, kubw'Ubutumwa Bwiza. Yego, musome neza kandi mwongere musome: abanyamahanga bizera izina ryanjye ryera, ari ryo Yesu Kristo, ni abaraganwa natwe kandi bo mu mubiri umwe n'abo muri Isirayeli bangize Umwami, kuko nta tandukaniro riri muri njye.

Kandi na none: Ndetse n'ibanga ryari ryarahishwe uhereye kera kose no mu bihe byose, ariko none rikaba ryahishuriwe abera bayo, Abo Imana yashatse kumenyesha ubutunzi bw'ubwiza bw'iri banga mu banyamahanga, ari ryo Kristo uri muri mwe, ibyiringiro by'ubwiza. Yego, mwabyawe ubwa kabiri n'umwuka wanjye, kandi umwuka wanjye uba mu bana banjye iteka, kuko ari umwuka w'ubwana, ingwate y'umurage wanyu, kandi uwo mwuka ukubiyemo ubushobozi bwose nabahaye, kuko nahaye abantu impano. Mwaremwe mu ishusho yanjye, kandi mwaremwe bushya muri njye; kuko Data yababyariye muri njye, kuko ndi umwuka utanga ubugingo.

Kristo Yesu wazamutse mu ijuru aravuga ati: Bana banjye bato, muhumurizwe cyane muri njye, kuko agakiza kanjye, kamaze kugerwaho binyuze mu kungira Umwami wanyu mu kwizera no kwihana, kadashobora gutakara cyangwa ngo gakurweho; kuko ni nde muri mwe ushobora gukuraho iremwa kamere ry'imiterere yanyu mu bana banyu bwite, nubwo bashobora gutakaza umurage niba mubihakanye. Agakiza ni impano itangwa na Data binyuze muri njye, Yeshua HaMashiach, kandi impano no guhamagara kw'Imana ntibisubirwaho. None, ngiye kubaha ibimenyetso byo gukurikira mu rugendo rwanyu mu mabanga akubiye mu Ibanga rikomeye ry'Imana, nuko rero mwige kugira ngo mwiyerekane ko mwemewe n'Imana, umukozi w'Ijambo, ukoresha neza ijambo ry'ukuri, kugira ngo mutazakorwa n'isoni imbere yanjye mu kuza kwanjye.

Hariho ibanga mu Baroma, urebye guhuma kwa Isirayeli igice. Hariho ibanga ryo gukoranya umugeni wanjye, iryo bamwe bita kuzamurwa mu ijuru, kandi ibyo biri mu Bakorinto (n'ahandi); kuko mu kanya gato, ndetse mu guhumbya, impanda y'imperuka izavuga, kandi kuvuga kwayo kuri hafi yanyu. Hanyuma hariho ibanga ry'umugabo n'umugore baba umubiri umwe, kandi iri ni ibanga rikomeye: ariko ndavuga ibya Kristo n'itorero, kandi ibyo mushobora kubisanga mu Befeso. Kuko nzavaho guhuma kwa Isirayeli nibagarura imitima yabo kuri njye. Kuko nzakoranyiriza Umugeni wanjye kuri njye, kuko ndi Umukwe. Ndi uwo muzashyingiranwa na we, kugira ngo tuzabe umwe iteka muri njye no muri Data, kuko Imana ari byose muri byose. Umutima wanjye uri kuri mwe, bana banjye, kandi vuba aha muzambona imbonankubone; kandi ntimwibeshye, ndabahamagara kuri njye, bakobwa banjye b'abanyabwenge, kandi ndaje kubatwara vuba cyane, kandi nifuza kubabona mwese aho tuzaba turi imbonankubone, kandi muzamenya, nk'uko namwe mumenywe.
