Ubuhamya Bubiri: Itorero
Mu Mirimo ya Yohana Umuhishuzi
25 Gicurasi 2024

Ku 144,000: Nimwumve Amagambo Yanjye n'Ubushishozi!

Nimwumve amagambo yanjye kandi mutege amatwi ubwenge bwanjye, kuko jyewe, Umwami Yesu Kristo, nzavugana namwe nimwumva amagambo yanjye. Kuko nzashyiraho ikimenyetso ku 144,000 banjye; ntimwasomye se? Kandi mbona marayika wundi azamuka aturuka iburasirazuba, afite ikimenyetso cy'Imana ihoraho: avuga ijwi rirenga abwira ba bamarayika bane bahawe kugirira nabi isi n'inyanja, ati ntimugirire nabi isi, cyangwa inyanja cyangwa ibiti, kugeza aho tuzamarira gushyira ikimenyetso mu ruhanga rw'abagaragu b'Imana yacu. Kandi nuko nzohereza abo nzashyiraho ikimenyetso, kandi muzankorera imirimo ikomeye, kandi muzagendera mu mbaraga nyinshi. Ariko ibi ntibizaba nk'ibyo mu gihe cy'ubuntu, aho ababwiriza mu ruhimbi bigishaga bava mu byanditswe byera. (Bamwe bavugaga ibinyoma abandi bankorera imirimo ikomeye mu matorero yabo.) Igihe cy'itorero kigiye kurangira, kandi igihe cyanjye cy'ubuntu kigiye kuzura, nuko ibihe byo kugeragezwa bizatangira, ndetse n'amakuba ya Yakobo.

Kandi nuko nzabatuma kwita ku mukumbi wanjye uzarushaho gutatana no kugeragezwa n'amakuba akurikirana. Kuko iminsi izaba mibi, kandi muzamurika nk'urumuri kandi muhagarare nk'inkingi mu batatanye. Kandi nuko mbatumye gukunda no kwita ku baturanyi banyu. Kuko mwebwe abizerwa ku muhamagaro wanjye muzabaho mu mategeko natanze, atari amategeko ya Mose. Kuko jyewe, Yeshua HaMashiach, nasohoje amategeko ya Mose, kandi Torah yubahirizwa no kunyizera, kuko umukiranutsi azabeshwaho no kwizera.

Kandi nuko bizaba: Ukunde Uwiteka Imana yawe n'umutima wawe wose, n'ubugingo bwawe bwose, n'ubwenge bwawe bwose, n'imbaraga zawe zose, kandi ukunde mugenzi wawe nk'uko wikunda. Kuko mu gukora ibyo muzakiza ubugingo bwanyu kurimbuka ndetse n'abo mbatumyeho. Ibi si ibihe byo kuramba no kugubwa neza, kuko iyo minsi yashize ku isi kugeza aho nzazira gutegeka isi mu gukiranuka. Udakunda ubugingo bwe kugeza ku iherezo azakizwa kandi abeho iteka ryose.

Muriho mu bihe by'intambara, n'umujinya mwinshi, n'ihishurirwa rikomeye ry'ibintu byose. Kuko Imana ikomeye ihoraho, ndetse na Yahweh, itazongera kwihisha isi; kuko izasohoka mu buturo bwayo, kandi irantuma, jyewe Yeshua HaMashiach, gusohoza umujinya wayo no gusohoza amasezerano yahaye ba Data. Ariko mbere na mbere, urubanza ruzagwa, kandi umujinya mwinshi uzagwa ku isi, kandi ntimuzongera kubasha kwifatanya n'umuryango wabo. Kuko kwifatanya n'umuryango wabo uzazamuka uva mu ivu bizasaba buri wese kwakira ikimenyetso cyabo. Nimwifatanya nabo, muzasangira nabo kurimbuka kw'iteka muri rya ziba rinini ry'umuriro, kandi iryo ziba ryateguriwe na ryo Imana kugira ngo rijyemo Umwanzi n'abamarayika be, nuko ntimukifatanye nabo.

Ntimuzabona amahoro mu isi yabo izamuka ahubwo ibibi bidakumiriwe gusa, kuko nzaba nkuyeho ubuza. Kandi niba mutari mu gice cy'imitekerereze yabo, ntimuzagira uruhare nabo, kuko bazica ababavuguruza bose. Kuko ikimenyetso kizahindura iteka abacyemera binyuze mu guhindura DNA yabo, kuko umuntu yaremwe mu ishusho ya Yahweh, nubwo Satani ashaka guhindura iyo shusho mu buryo bwo gutuka Imana bidasubirwaho binyuze mu miti ye igurishwa mu binyoma bikomeye. Bazabasezeranya kuramba, amahoro, no kugubwa neza, ariko ibyo binyoma ni umutego gusa. Kuko iyo ikimenyetso kimaze kwakirwa, nta kizakuraho ibyangijwe, kuko Yahweh yabivuze atyo!

Kuko umugaragu ataruta shebuja, kandi ndabahamagarira imirimo aho nzabayobora intambwe ku yindi, kandi nk'uko byari mu minsi ya Nowa, niko bizaba. Kandi nuko jyewe, Yeshua HaMashiach, nasohoje intego yanjye nk'umwana w'intama w'Imana, kuko natuye amaraso yanjye atagira icyaha kuri Yahweh ku bushake bwanjye bwite no ku bw'urukundo rutunganye rwaka mu mutima wanjye.

Kandi nuko ku bw'ibyishimo byashyizwe imbere yanjye, nihanganiye amakuba akomeye, iyicarubozo, n'umubabaro, (ndetse mu buryo burenganya) kuko nikoreye ingaruka zose z'ibyaha byose by'abantu. Kandi nishyuye buri cyaha, byuzuye, kandi nabikoze mbikunze. Nuko mundebereho, Yesu, umutangizi n'usohoza kwizera kwanyu, we ku bw'ibyishimo byashyizwe imbere ye, yihanganiye umusaraba, agasuzugura isoni, kandi yicaye iburyo bw'intebe y'Imana. Nuko mukurikire urugero rwanjye kugera ku ntsinzi ikomeye, kuko ndetse no mu butayu nzaringaniza inzira yanyu.

Nuko mubyumve, nta mahoro azabaho, kandi mbatumye gukora umurimo mu gihe cy'intambara ikomeye n'amakuba. Kuko ikiyoka kizagaba intambara ku bera kandi kizabanesha, kuko niko cyahawe ububasha bwo kubikora. Kandi abazambaza izina ry'Umwami bazakizwa, kuko abadakunda ubugingo bwabo kugeza ku iherezo bazabona ubugingo bwabo iteka muri jye, Kristo Yesu. Kuko natangiye impongano y'ibyaha by'abantu mu maraso yanjye atagira icyaha, kandi ndi umutambyi mukuru iteka ryose mu buryo bwa Melikisedeki, watuye amaraso yanjye atagira icyaha kuri Yahweh rimwe rizima. Nuko rero, ntimugasubire mu nzira za kera, cyangwa imigenzo yabo, cyangwa Talmud yabo, cyangwa Torah; kuko Torah yubahirizwa byuzuye no kunyizera, Yesu Kristo.

Kandi nuko binyuze mu makuba akomeye, ububabaro bukomeye, intambara zikomeye, abera banjye bazamurika, kandi ingororano yabo izaba ikomeye, kuko ingororano yanjye ari ikomeye. Kuko isezerano rivuga iki: Aba ni bo bavuye mu makuba akomeye, kandi bameshe amakanzu yabo, bayejesha amaraso y'Umwana w'Intama. Ni cyo gituma bari imbere y'intebe y'Imana, kandi bayikorera ku manywa na nijoro mu Rusengero rwayo: kandi uwicaye ku Ntebe azabana nabo. Ntibazongera gusonza, cyangwa kugira inyota ukundi, cyangwa izuba ntirizabavaho, cyangwa ubushyuhe ubwo ari bwo bwose. Kuko Umwana w'Intama, uri hagati y'intebe, azabagaburira, kandi azabayobora ku masoko y'amazi y'ubugingo: kandi Imana izahanagura amarira yose ku maso yabo.

Kandi nuko nzohereza abo nahamagaye. Ndabahamagarira gukunda Imana n'umutima wanyu wose, ubugingo, ubwenge, n'imbaraga, kandi mbatumye gukunda mugenzi wanyu nk'uko mwikunda. Kuko kurokoka kuzava mu magambo atanga ubugingo Data yatanze ngo yumvirwe na bose. Kandi nuko mukunde mugenzi wanyu, mwitaneho, mufashanye kandi mushyigikirane; kuko abantu banjye bazatatana. Bazakorana mu miryango mu butayu, kandi nubwo ubutayu butazaba butekanye, nzaha ubuhungiro abantu banjye kandi nzabaha ibitunga ibyo bakeneye.

Kandi nuko nzohereza abo nahamagaye. Ndabatuma gutegeka ibidashoboka uko mugendera mu rukundo n'imbaraga nyinshi, ndetse n'imbaraga nzabambika. Kuko muzangendera intambwe ku yindi inshuro nyinshi mu kumvira, uko munyishingikirizaho, Kristo Yesu, uzabayobora n'umwuka wanjye; kandi gusigwa mufite kuzatuma mugenda mutsinze. Ariko bizasohora nk'uko ibyanditswe bivuga: Hahirwa abapfa bapfiriye mu Mwami, uhereye none, yego, niko Umwuka avuga, kugira ngo baruhuke imihati yabo, kandi imirimo yabo irabakurikira.

Intambwe ku yindi muzagenda, kandi nzabayobora muri ubwo buryo. Kuko ndi ubuhungiro bwanyu n'igihome, ndetse na Mesiya wanyu w'ukuri uwo muziringira. Kandi nubwo igihumbi bazagwa iruhande rwawe, n'ibihumbi icumi iburyo bwawe: ariko ntibizakwegera. Kuko mushyizweho ikimenyetso cyo gusohoza imigambi yanjye, kandi ingororano ikomeye n'isezerano nabahaye byo kubashyigikira no kubakomeza, kuko mbatumye mu mirimo Data yantegetse, kandi nuko mbatumye nk'abakozi banjye n'abahanuzi.

Nuko uko mugenda gutegeka ibidashoboka, mumenye ko muzategeka byinshi binyuze mu mwuka wavukiye muri mwe. Nuko mutegeke imisozi gukurwaho no kujugunywa mu nyanja, uko mwizera Yahweh. Nuko mutegeke imyuka kugenda uko muvuga amagambo akomeye n'ukwizera gukomeye muri Yahweh. Nuko mutegeke indwara zose n'ibyorezo kugenda uko muvuga amagambo agomba kumvirwa (ndetse n'ibintu byose) uko muvuga amagambo y'imbaraga nyinshi n'ukwizera gukomeye muri Yahweh. Kuko binyuze mu migambi n'intego bya Yahweh ubwe natumwe, kandi muri iyo migambi n'intego ndabatuma, kandi muzankorera imirimo ikomeye. Kuko Yahweh ari byose muri byose binyuze mu mwuka wanjye, uwo nzashyiraho ikimenyetso ku batoranyijwe.

Kuko muri jye, Yeshua HaMashiach, kuzura kwose gutuye, kuko Data yampaye ibintu byose mu ijuru no ku isi, kugira ngo nsohoze byose. Kandi nuko Yahweh ni byose muri byose, kandi mufite uburenganzira bwo kwegera Data n'Umwana we, kuko niko Mose yigishije: Uwiteka Imana yawe izaguhagurukiriza Umuhanuzi hagati muri mwe, mu bavandimwe bawe, umeze nkanjye, uwo ni we muzumvira. Ndi uwo muhanuzi Mose yavuze (ndetse wahagurukijwe mu muryango wa Yuda), kandi ndi byinshi kurushaho; kuko Data yagennye ko jyewe, Yeshua HaMashiach, ngomba kugira ubukuru bwose, kandi muri jye kuzura kwose kugomba gutura. Ndi uwo muhanuzi Mose yavuze, ndetse wazuwe mu bapfuye ku bw'iyo ntego.

Kandi nuko nzabaha ibyo mukeneye n'abo nzabatumaho. Kuko muzaba urumuri, ndetse rwihishe mu maso y'abantu mu isi y'umwijima n'idahamye. Muzabwiriza ibintu byose binyerekeyeho, Umwami wanyu n'umukiza, kuko ndi umwana w'intama w'Imana, ndetse na Pasika itunganye. Nta gakiza kaboneka mu rindi zina uretse iryanjye, ndetse Yesu Kristo Umukiranutsi.

Bamwe muri mwe nzabajyana ahantu hatandukanye, kandi muzaba abavugizi banjye, abahanuzi, n'abakozi. Muzakenera kugerageza imyuka y'abo muhura nabo kandi mugendere mu bwenge bwinshi; kuko isi mwamenye izasimburwa n'isi itagira amategeko y'ubutayu bwinshi, kandi bamwe muri mwe bazatumwa gukorera abari mu butayu, kandi muzumva amabwiriza yanjye, kandi muzakora ibikorwa bikomeye.

Kandi nuko mwumve ko nyuma y'intambara n'irimbuka bizabaho nyuma y'uko nkoranya abizerwa banjye (kandi nkakuraho ubuza), hazabaho akavuyo kenshi n'irimbuka. Abantu baziruka hirya no hino, kandi benshi bazarimbukira mu kutizera kutagira ukwizera, kandi bazahiga bafate kandi bice benshi. Kandi kuri benshi, ijwi rya mbere bazumva ntirizaba iryanjye, nubwo iryo jwi rizatanga agakiza k'ikinyoma. Oya, ndetse n'inyamaswa izazamuka mu mbaraga nyinshi ivuye ku kiyoka izahamagarira benshi mu kinyoma ku iherezo ryabo ry'iteka ritagira ibyiringiro.

Bose baziyoberanya, kuko ari abiyoberanya bakomeye, kandi baziyoberanya ko ari Mesiya w'ukuri, kandi ababakurikira bazavuga ko bahagarariye Mesiya w'ukuri n'Imana y'ukuri; ariko sinabatumye, nuko ntimugakurikire ibimenyetso byabo by'ibyiringiro by'ibinyoma. Kuko bazatanga kwinjira mu muryango wabo mushya kugira ngo bubake isi yabo nshya; ariko kugira ngo winjire, umuntu agomba kwakira ikimenyetso. Kandi ku bakira ikimenyetso, bazahabwa kwinjira mu birimi by'umuriro by'ubururu muri rya ziba rinini ry'umuriro ryaka amazuku. Kuko abakira ikimenyetso bose bazagira uruhare rwabo mu miriro idazima kandi bazaba baguranye ubugingo bw'iteka agace k'umugati mu nzira za Esawu.

Muzakenera kwirengagiza amajwi yose uretse iryanjye kandi muve mu banyanga bose. Bizasaba ubwenge bwinshi, ubuhanga, n'ubwenge bwo kurokoka, kandi nzabaha ururimi rw'abanyabwenge kugira ngo mumenye uko musubiza buri muntu. Kuko nzabasobanurira ijwi ryanjye, nubwo ari ijwi rito, rituje. Muzamenya ijwi ryanjye, kuko nzabahamiriza ijwi ryanjye inshuro nyinshi, nuko ntimukabe abatagira ukwizera ahubwo mugire ukwizera gukomeye mu magambo yanjye y'isezerano.

Nzabigisha byose, kandi muzamenya uko mukora imirimo mbatumye gusohoza. Ijwi ryanjye ni ijwi ritunganye, ndetse ijwi ry'urangurura mu butayu, kandi mvugira mu ijwi ry'urangurura mu butayu; kuko niko batumwe, ndetse n'abahanuzi banjye n'abakozi bagiye imbere kugira ngo batunganye inzira zanyu kandi baringanize inzira zanyu. Nategetse imirimo myinshi ikomeye kuri mwe, kandi muzakora bikomeye.

Nuko rero, mumenye amoko yanjye, abavandimwe, n'abana b'isezerano: isarura ni rinini koko, ariko abakozi ni bake; nuko rero musabe Umwami w'isarura, kugira ngo yohereze abakozi mu isarura rye. Mugende mu nzira zanyu: Dore, mbohereje nk'abana b'intama mu masega. Kuko ndi Umwami w'Isarura, kandi nubwo abakozi bahoze ari bake, hari ingororano ikomeye n'inyungu y'iteka n'icyubahiro ku murimo muzakora. Kuko muzasarura ingororano nyinshi n'imbuto muri jye; gusa ntimugacike intege mu makuba yanyu n'ibibazo. Uku ni ugutandukanya kwa nyuma, kandi abagenewe ubugingo bw'iteka bose mbere y'ishingwa ry'Isi bazaboneka, kandi ntibazigera babura, kuko nta n'umwe ushobora kubakura mu kuboko kwanjye.

Kandi nuko imirimo yanyu izabakurikira mu milele, kandi mu bikorwa byanyu byinshi muzaha icyubahiro Yahweh, wantumye, Yeshua HaMashiach, nka Mesiya wanyu w'ukuri. Kandi binyuze mu byo muzageraho byinshi bikomeye n'amagambo menshi yunzwe y'ukuri muzavuga, muzatangaza kugaruka kwanjye vuba kandi mugende mu mirimo ya Yohana Umubatiza kugira ngo mutegure kuza kw'Umwami: nuko mutangaze kugaruka kwanjye, kuko nzasohoza rwose amagambo yanjye y'isezerano. Kuko ndababwira ukuri ko ku iherezo ry'amakuba ya Yakobo, ndetse ku iherezo ry'icyumweru cya nyuma cy'imyaka, ndetse icyumweru cya mirongo irindwi cya Daniyeli, nzagarukana n'ingabo zo mu ijuru gushyiraho Ubwami bwanjye ku Isi, ndetse Ubwami bw'Imana; kandi ingoma yanjye y'imyaka igihumbi izatangira, ndetse ingoma yo gukiranuka, kwera, n'amahoro uko Intare izaryama hamwe n'Umwana w'Intama!

Ndabahamagarira imirimo aho nzabayobora intambwe ku yindi.

Ndabahamagarira gukunda Imana n'umutima wanyu wose, ubugingo, ubwenge, n'imbaraga, no gukunda mugenzi wanyu nk'uko mwikunda.

Ndabatuma gutegeka ibidashoboka uko mugendera mu rukundo rwinshi n'imbaraga nyinshi.

Ndi ubuhungiro bwanyu n'igihome, ndetse na Mesiya wanyu w'ukuri uwo mwiringira.

Mbatumye mu mirimo Data yantegetse, kandi nuko mbashyiraho nk'abakozi banjye n'abahanuzi.

Ndabatuma, kandi muzankorera imirimo ikomeye.

Ndi uwo muhanuzi Mose yavuze, ndetse wahagurukijwe mu muryango wa Yuda ku bw'iyo ntego.

Ndi byinshi kurushaho, kuko muri jye, Yeshua HaMashiach, kuzura kwose gutuye.

Ndi umwana w'intama w'Imana, ndetse na Pasika itunganye.
