Milandu ndi Zolakwa

Operation War-seed / Chitukuko cha Chizindikiro cha Chilombo

  1. O' Babulo Wachinsinsi, ndi ufiti wanu ndi matsenga anu mwanyenga dziko lapansi kuti lilandire poizoni, ngakhale chida choyambirira ndi chosankha cha chilengedwe chomwe apha nacho omwe amakonda dziko lapansi ndi ufiti wawo ndikukana kulapa pakuti ndikadawachiritsa!
  2. Mvetsetsani kuti Operation War-seed ndi kulumwa kwa njoka yake sikunali chizindikiro cha chilombo, koma chizindikiro cha chilombo chidzabisidwa ngati mankhwala ndipo ndi lumbiro lathunthu la kukhulupirika kuvomereza matekinoloje awo omwe adzafafaniza kwamuyaya dzina la Yahweh kwa uyo amene avomereza.
  3. Ngakhale kulumwa kwa njoka komwe dongosolo lawo lakhazikitsidwa kale ndikuyesedwa, ndipo unyinji wakonzedwa kale kuti ulandire chizindikiro chawo ndipo Operation War-seed yawo inali yopambana.
  4. Kuchokera kwa iwe Babulo Wachinsinsi, kwabwera matekinoloje a gehena awa ndipo ndi mabodza anu ndi chinyengo chanu unyinji wapatsidwa poizoni ndipo chiweruzo chanu sichichedwa.

Kuvomereza zoyipa

  1. Ambiri a inu ndinu odzikuza chifukwa mumayesa kundipanga Mbuye pamene muli ndi mtima wopanda chikhulupiriro, pakuti ndi mtima munthu amakhulupirira mpaka chilungamo. Choncho khalani ndi chikhulupiriro kuti mupulumutsidwe; chikhulupiriro chanu chidzasonyezedwa kwa onse mwa kukana kwanu tchimo ndikutuluka m'njira zanu zonyansa.
  2. Oipa amayenda mbali zonse, pamene anthu onyansa kwambiri akwezedwa. Koma inu mumavomerezabe njira za ogonana amuna kapena akazi okhaokha, ndipo mumathandiza kubweretsa choipa chachikulu m'dzikolo, mpaka kufika poti mumavotera ogwiririra ana kuti atsogolere mayiko anu. Mumasowa ngakhale mlingo woyambirira wa kuzindikira, ndipo simutsatira njira yanga; ndipo ngati mupitiriza, ndidzakuperekani kwathunthu ku zilakolako zanu zonyansa kuti thupi liwonongeke kuti mzimu upulumutsidwe.
  3. Ndiloleni nditsegule maso anu, inu omwe muli osadziwa mofunitsitsa ndi akhungu, inu omwe mumakhala mu dothi ndi mdima, mukugubuduzika m'matope. Simungathe kuvomereza zoyipa ndikuganiza kuti mukunditumikira, pakuti ndinatsutsana ndi zoyipa ndi mayesero mpaka dontho lomaliza la magazi litatuluka mwa ine pa nthaka yofiira, pakuti ndine Adamu womaliza ndipo ndapangidwa kukhala mzimu wopatsa moyo.
  4. Ambiri a inu mwakhala akhungu ndi mawu abwino ndi zolankhula zabwino ndipo mumavomereza kwathunthu zigawenga ndi anthu oyipa omwe amadya ana. Amagwiririra ana, amazunza ana chifukwa cha zilakolako zawo zoyipa: Pakuti kotero kumabwera mkwiyo wa Mulungu pa Babulo Wachinsinsi, koma ambiri a inu mumapitiriza kuvomereza zilombo zankhanza izi zomwe zayikidwa pambali pa moto wa gehena wamuyaya womwe sudzazimitsidwa.
  5. Tulukani mwa iye, anthu anga, kapena mudzagawana naye miliri yake ndi chiweruzo chowopsa chomwe chikubwera pa iye; ndipo ndakuchenjezani. Pakuti Khristu ali ndi chiyanjano chanji ndi Beliyali? Ngati mugawana mabodza ake, mudzagawana miliri yake; choncho dzilekanitseni kwathunthu m'mawu ndi m'zochita, ndipo musagwirizane nawo ngakhale pang'ono. Musakhale moyo wosasamala, wosagwirizana; pakuti ndidzalanga onse amene amatero. Mukuganiza kuti chifukwa muli ndi moyo wosatha simungathe kukumana ndi kutayika kwakukulu ndi zotsatira zake? Ndidzavula mphotho zanu ndikuzipereka, ndipo mudzakhala wamng'ono kwambiri mu ufumu wakumwamba ngati mupitiriza, pakuti watero Yeshua HaMashiach, Mkwati, kwa Mkwatibwi wanga!
  6. Zidziwike! Adani a Babulo Wachinsinsi alibe chifundo, alibe chisomo, alibe chisoni, ndipo amadana kotheratu ndi okhala mu Babulo Wachinsinsi chifukwa cha machimo owopsa omwe anachita m'mayiko awo. Ndipo nthawi yonseyi ambiri a inu munkakondwera ndi nkhondo zanu zopanda chilungamo pamene munkawonera mosangalala pa wailesi yakanema yanu, pamene munkasangalala kwambiri ndi machimo a atsogoleri anu. Mwavomereza atsogoleri anu omwe machimo awo munawavomereza povota, ndipo choncho mudzagawana nawo chiweruzo chawo, pakuti mwadzipanga kukhala oyenera gawo lowirikiza la chiweruzo, ndipo chidzakugwerani mwadzidzidzi.
  7. Taonani! Atsogoleri onse adziko lapansi amagwirira ntchito limodzi kuseri kwa zochitika, ndipo onse amatumikira Satana. Mulibe atsogoleri osankhidwa, koma mwapatsidwa chisankho chonyenga chifukwa simunasankhepo omwe mukuganiza kuti mwawasankha povota, koma amaperekedwa kwa inu kuti muwavotere kuti apeze chilolezo chanu ndipo chilolezo chanu ndi tchimo chifukwa simungathe kusankha mofunitsitsa choyipa chocheperako ndikundiimira nthawi yomweyo, chimenecho ndi chinyengo.
  8. Babulo Wachinsinsi, mumavomereza mofunitsitsa olamulira anu a Satana, omwe amadziwa bwino mapulani awo ndipo amadziwa bwino kuti ali mkati mowononga kwathunthu mtundu wanu womwe unali waukulu ndi inu.
  9. Pakuti iwe Babulo Wachinsinsi mu dama lako ndi chigololo chako, umagona ndi mdani.
  10. Pakuti m'njira yotere, mwakhala moyo wokoma pamene atsogoleri anu anawononga mayiko ena ndi chilolezo chanu.
  11. Pakuti m'njira yotere, Babulo Wachinsinsi, mwakondwera pamene atsogoleri anu anabweretsa tsoka m'mbali mwa mayiko osawerengeka mu nkhondo zawo zopanda chilungamo zomwe munasangalatsidwa nazo pamene munkawonera pa wailesi yakanema.
  12. Chifukwa ndi Sunagoge la Satana lomwe lapanga chisankho chawo ndikusankha Moshiach wawo watsopano wonyenga ndipo Babulo Wachinsinsi waperekanso chilolezo chake kwa chinjoka.
  13. Ngakhale pamaso panu penipeni ndipo mwa chilolezo cha voti yanu ndi kudzera mu ulesi wanu, mtundu wanu wonse waphwasulidwa pamaso panu.
