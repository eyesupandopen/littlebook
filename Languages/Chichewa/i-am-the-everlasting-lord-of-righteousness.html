Ndine Mbuye Wamuyaya wa Chilungamo!

Taonani! Imvani Mawu a Yesu Khristu ndipo perekani maganizo anu ndi kudula mitima yanu ndi makutu anu pomvera Mawu a Yeshua HaMashiach, ndipo ndidzapereka nzeru kwa anzeru kuti munthu wanzeru pakati panu aphunzire kuopa Ambuye ndi kuti muphunzire kunjenjemera pamaso pa Mbuye wa Makamu, pakuti ndi momwe Yahweh, WAMKULU NDINE wandipangira.

Ndinu ndani, inu munthu wamba kuti mukweze chidendene chanu pa Yahweh, WAMKULU NDINE, ngakhale iye amene ali Nkhalamba ya Masiku, ngakhale iye amene ali Mbuye wa Mizimu? Ndinu ndani inu munthu wamba kuti mudzikweze nokha modzikuza motsutsana ndi Yesu wa ku Nazarete amene alinso Khristu Yesu, ngakhale iye amene dzina lake lili pamwamba pa dzina lina lililonse, ngakhale iye amene Yahweh, WAMKULU NDINE, wagawana naye mpando wake wachifumu, ngakhale iye amene wakwezedwa pamwamba pa onse kupatula Yahweh mwiniwake mpaka adani anga atapangidwa chopondapo mapazi anga? Ndipo ufumu wanga ndi ulamuliro wanga ulibe mapeto ndipo ndidzapereka Ufumu wogonjetsedwa kwa Atate wa Zounikira kuti Adonai Yahweh akhale zonse mu zonse!

Kodi chinthu chopangidwa chinganene kwa amene anachipanga, bwanji mwandipanga chonchi? Kodi dongo lomwe lapangidwa kukhala ziwiya zaulemu linganene kwa woumba, bwanji mwandipanga chonchi? Bwanji mwandipatsa ulemu wotere? Ndikufuna kukhala chinthu china ndipo m'malo mwake ndidzakhala ndi ulemu wawo! Kodi woumba aliyense sangawononge cholengedwa choipa chotere chomwe chingapandukire dzanja lake ngakhale kufunafuna imfa ya woumba, pamodzi ndi zolengedwa zina za woumba; ngati kuti chinthu chotere n'chotheka?

Komabe ndi momwe ziliri njira zopusa ndi zamisala za omwe adagwa omwe apandukira mlengi wawo posiya malamulo awo ndi ntchito zoyera ndi zaulemu zomwe anapatsidwa kuti akwaniritse m'chilungamo, ngakhale zolinga zoperekedwa ndi Yahweh mwiniwake pa tsiku lomwe analenga ana a Mulungu. Ngakhale angelo oyera omwe Yahweh anawapanga asanapange dziko lapansi omwe kwalembedwa za iwo m'buku la Yobu pamene anapanga dziko lapansi: Nyenyezi zam'mawa zinayimba pamodzi, ndipo ana onse a Mulungu anafuula mwachimwemwe! Ndipo iwo anafuuladi mwachimwemwe pa zodabwitsa ndi zozizwitsa zomwe anaziona m'manja mwa Yahweh pamene analenga dziko lapansi. Ndipo momwemonso anali angelo ogwa, ngakhale ana a Mulungu omwe poyamba analengedwa ndi kupangidwa aulemerero ndi amphamvu pa zolinga zawo ndipo Yahweh anawaveka ulemu ndi kuwapanga kuti apambane mu nzeru kuti akonde Yahweh pomvera mawu ake kwathunthu ndipo ndithudi anapatsidwa mkhalidwe woyamba waulemerero womwe poyamba analengedwa nawo, mpaka anapanduka m'zilakolako za mitima yawo ndipo mphulupulu inapezeka mwa iwo.

Ndipo motero asatana anapanduka pachiyambi motsutsana ndi mlengi wawo, ngakhale omwe kale anazungulira mpando wachifumu wa Yahweh, ngakhale omwe mpando wawo wachifumu panopa wazunguliridwa ndi zikwi khumi kuwirikiza zikwi khumi ndipo komabe Yahweh ndi wolamulira wamkulu ndipo safuna phungu. Komabe kwalembedwa za chinjoka chachikulu chofiyirachi, chokhala ndi mitu isanu ndi iwiri, ndi nyanga khumi, ndi akorona asanu ndi awiri pamitu yake kuti mchira wake unakoka gawo limodzi mwa magawo atatu a nyenyezi zakumwamba, ndikuziponya padziko lapansi: ndipo motero asatana ndi ngakhale gawo limodzi mwa magawo atatu a angelo omwe anatsatira kupanduka kwawo anaponyedwa pansi padziko lapansi, ngakhale m'masiku omwe chivundi cha angelo chinagwa kuchokera kumwamba mu uchimo ndi kupanduka, ndipo anakhala mdima wandiweyani pambuyo pa machimo awo, ndipo momwemo aliri mpaka lero.

Ndipo njoka inafikira mayi wa amoyo onse m'Munda wa Edene zaka zambiri zapitazo, ndipo inanyenga Hava kuti adye chinthu chomwe Yahweh anawalamula kuti asadye, chifukwa tsiku lomwe adzadya mtengo wodziwitsa zabwino ndi zoipa, ngakhale tsiku lomwelo adzafa ndithu. Ngakhale zili choncho, Hava anatenga kwa njoka popeza ananyengedwa ndi chinyengo chake ndipo anadya, ndipo anapatsa mwamuna wake chipatso choletsedwachi ndipo Adamu anadya mofunitsitsa ndipo pakufa anafadi monga mwa lonjezo, ndipo anthu akhala pansi pa imfa kuyambira pamenepo chifukwa cha uchimo womwe unapezeka m'magazi ake. Chifukwa Adamu waipitsidwa kwamuyaya mu uchimo ndipo pa cholinga ichi Mwana wa Munthu anaonekera kuti ndiwononge ntchito za mdierekezi ndi kubweretsa chiwombolo kwa Adamu wogwa chifukwa ndine Adamu womaliza amene Yahweh wamupanga kukhala mzimu wopatsa moyo. Zoonadi, zoonadi, ndidzalenganso onse omwe amalandira chipulumutso changa kukhala cholengedwa chatsopano ndi cholungama pakuti ndi momwe Chipangano changa Chatsopano chimabweretsera.

Choncho Adamu ndi Hava anathamangitsidwa m'Munda wa Edene, ndipo anasinthidwa kuchoka ku chikhalidwe chowala chodabwitsa chomwe analengedwa nacho mu ulemerero wonse ndi Yahweh mwiniwake, ndipo pansi pa kugonjera ku uchimo ndi zoipa zomwe umabweretsa ayendayenda padziko lapansi m'chilango chawo. Ndipo m'masiku a Yaredi Alonda anatsikira pa Phiri la Herimoni, ngakhale omwe anakopeka ndi zilakolako za mitima yawo chifukwa analakalaka ana aakazi a anthu, ngakhale kufuna ana kwa iwo ndipo m'zilakolako za mitima yawo zomwe anayesedwa nazo ndi asatana anadzimanga okha ku matemberero, ngakhale m'lumbiro lawo losayera lomwe lasanduka temberero lamuyaya lomwe lidzakwaniritsidwa m'nyanja yayikulu yamoto ndi sulufule yomwe mbozi yawo sifa.

Ndipo ana a Mulungu analowa kwa ana aakazi a anthu, ndipo anadzitengera akazi mwa onse omwe anawasankha. Akazi ena anapita mofunitsitsa, ngakhale kudzipereka okha kwathunthu ku machimo a omwe adagwa (omwe adzanyamula chiweruzo chawo), ndipo ena anatengedwa mokakamiza ndipo anazunzidwa mwankhanza ndi Alonda m'zilakolako zawo zoipa. Ndipo momwemo linaliri dziko lomwe linalipo panthawiyo, ngakhale dziko lomwe linawonongedwa mu chigumula chachikulu chomwe chinachitika m'masiku a Nowa chomwe chinawonongedwa chifukwa cha zonyansa zoipa zopangidwa ndi alonda, omwe kwenikweni anapangidwa kuti awononge anthu ngakhale kuti anthu asadzalandire dziko lapansi lomwe omwe adagwa analakalaka ndipo tsopano amangidwa m'chiweruzo.

Chifukwa anasankha chiwonongeko chawo ndi mazunzo amuyaya m'malo mwa zinthu za zilakolako zawo zomwe si zawo kuti azitenge, ndipo zinthu zomwe anazitenga, ndipo akufunabe kuzitenga mokakamiza. Komabe, zochita zawo ndi mapulani awo zonse zidzakhala zachabechabe, ndipo sadzapezanso nthawi yawo yomwe amati ndi yagolide, koma adzakhala ndi chiweruzo chaukali ndi chokhwima ndi chamuyaya m'malo mwake. Pakuti pa choonadi chosasinthika: Dziko lapansi ndi la Ambuye ndi kudzaza kwake, ndipo Ine ndine Mbuye Wamuyaya wa Chilungamo amene anagula dziko lapansi ndi magazi anga owombola, ndipo ndikubwera kudzalandira mokakamiza zomwe zili zanga zoyenera, ndipo ndani angathe kuima motsutsana ndi mphamvu yanga yayikulu?

Monga kwalembedwa za ine motero kudzachitika: Taonani, Mkango wa fuko la Yuda, muzu wa Davide, wapambana kutsegula bukhu, ndi kumasula zisindikizo zake zisanu ndi ziwiri. Chifukwa chake, mvetsetsani kwathunthu kuti ndine amene ndili woyenera kumasula zisindikizo chifukwa chikalata cha dziko lapansi ndi changa! Ndipo monga kwalembedwa za ine motero kudzachitika chifukwa ndine woyenera kutenga bukhu, ndi kutsegula zisindikizo zake: pakuti ndinaphedwa, ndipo ndawombola onse omwe adzabwera ku chipulumutso chamuyaya kwa Yahweh ndi magazi anga opanda uchimo, ndipo ndakuwombolani mwa fuko lililonse, ndi lilime, ndi anthu, ndi mtundu: ndipo palibe cholengedwa chimodzi chomwe chingakwatule aliyense wa inu m'manja mwanga amuyaya! Chifukwa chake, ndani amene muyenera kumuopa?

Choncho akazi otengedwa ndi ana a Mulungu, (ngakhale alonda ogwa) anatenga pakati ndipo anawaberekera zimphona zonyansa zomwe zinali zoipa mpaka pansi pa mitima yawo. Ndipo mu ukulu wawo ndi mphamvu zawo anadalira ndi kudzikuza, komabe m'chiweruzo cha Yahweh pa iwo chifukwa cha zoipa zomwe anachitira anthu anamanga mizimu yawo padziko lapansi m'chilango chifukwa anapangidwa padziko lapansi m'chifanizo cha thupi lauchimo ndipo mizimu yawo inali yamuyaya m'chilengedwe yoperekedwa kuchokera kwa Atate awo omwe zilakolako zawo amangidwa kuti azikwaniritse. Ndipo pamene Anefili awa anafa m'ziweruzo zawo kuchokera kwa Yahweh mwiniwake, mizimu yawo inachotsedwa m'matupi mwawo m'maimfa awo, ndipo anapitiriza, omangidwa padziko lapansi m'chilango. Chifukwa chake, amamva njala ndi ludzu koma sangatenge chakudya kapena madzi. Pamene poyamba anali ndi matupi a zimphona ndi amphamvu omwe ankagwiritsa ntchito kulamulira okhala padziko lapansi, anapangidwa opanda mphamvu pokhapokha ngati angathe kutenga umwini wa wolandira yemwe amakhalamo ngati majeremusi a ziwanda, chifukwa ndi momwe asandukira. Mvetsetsani, kuti kuyambira masiku a Nowa chifukwa cha kupembedzera kwa Nowa kuti magawo asanu ndi anayi mwa magawo khumi a iwo asungidwa kuphompho koma posachedwapa adzamasulidwa padziko lapansi m'chiweruzo.

Ndipo komabe, momwemo mwakhalira kupanduka kwa omwe adagwa kuyambira tsiku lomwe mphulupulu inapezeka m'mitima yawo. Kuti misala ndi wamisala yadzaza mitima ya omwe adagwa m'kupanduka kwawo; pamodzi ndi munthu wamba, yemwe anawaphatikiza pokana kusiya mkhalidwe wake wogwa ndi wauchimo: kuti onse akufuna kukhala chinthu china osati chomwe Yahweh akufuna mu ungwiro wonse wolungama. M'malo momvera mlengi wawo yemwe angawonetsetse kukwaniritsidwa kwawo kwangwiro, chimwemwe, chisangalalo, ndi mtendere; yemwe akanapezera iwo zinthu mwangwiro m'chikondi chake pa cholengedwa chake, m'malo mwake anafunafuna ndi kutsatira zilakolako za mitima yawo ndipo amafuna dziko lawo lopanda Yahweh, komwe angathe kukhala ndi zilakolako za mitima yawo ndi kusangalala m'kupanduka kwawo m'zosangalatsa za uchimo pa ndalama za cholengedwa cha Yahweh. Ngakhale zilakolako zauchimo ndi zoipa zomwe sadzazipeza kapena kuzikwaniritsa.

Mofananamo, munthu wamba wadziphatikiza yekha ku kupanduka kwawo kupatula kuti munthu wamba amabadwa mu umbuli ndi mdima wa zolinga zake zenizeni ndi ulemerero womwe ungakhalepo womwe Yahweh walonjeza omvera ndipo munthu wamba watsatira mofunitsitsa njira za omwe adagwa, ngakhale kuti, omwe adagwa adzipereka ku chiwonongeko cha Adamu, chomwe Adamu ali mbuli mofunitsitsa m'zilakolako zake, ngakhale momwe amadzimangira yekha m'chiyembekezo chopusa ku mabodza awo ambiri ndi oipa. Ngakhale kudzera m'mabodza omwe, ambiri adzatsogoleredwa kunyanja yamoto pamene chilango chimenecho sichinapangidwire iwo.

Komabe, kupanduka kumeneku kwapitirira m'mibadwo yonse tsopano, ngakhale kupanduka komwe kukuchitika mu nkhondo ya mbewu pakati pa zolengedwa zolungama za Yahweh ndi zonyansa zosalungama zopangidwa ndi kusintha kwa majini kwa oipa, ndipo iwo si milungu ngakhale amayerekezera. Ndipo momwemo ziliri nthawi zomwe mukukhala, inu munthu, amene amapanduka m'kusamvera kwa chidani kuchokera ku njira za mlengi wake, ngakhale kuphatikizana mosazindikira ndi omwe adzipereka kwathunthu ku imfa yanu ndi chiwonongeko chamuyaya chomwe amalakalaka.

Ndipo motero ndikulengeza tsikulo, Ine Yeshua HaMashiach, ndikulengeza kuti tsiku la Ambuye lafika ndipo latsala pang'ono kuphulika padziko lapansi m'chiweruzo chachikulu chachiwawa ndipo masoka ambiri tsopano akhazikitsidwa kuti amasulidwe padziko lapansi, ngakhale masoka ndi ziweruzo zoloseredwa kalekale zomwe nthawi zawo zafika tsopano. Chifukwa chake, mvetsetsani kuti ndine ndani amene akupanga chilengezo ichi mu mphamvu ndi mpando wachifumu wa Yahweh mwiniwake, chifukwa ndine amene ndili woyenera kutenga bukhu ndi kulitsegula, ndi kumasula zisindikizo zake, ndipo ndidzachita chimodzimodzi, ndipo ndani amene angaletse dzanja langa lamphamvu?!

Mvetsetsani, inu munthu wamba, ngakhale inu amene mungakane mlengi wanu mwa chidani, kuti nthawi zanu zafika. Ndipo chikondi chambiri ndi chisomo ndi chifundo zaperekedwa m'nthawi izi za chiweruzo, chifukwa onse omwe amayitana pa dzina la Yesu wa ku Nazarete mpaka mapeto awo adzapulumutsidwa ndipo kulemera kwa mphotho yawo sikoyenera kuyerekezedwa ndi masautso awo omwe adzapirira chifukwa cha dzina langa. Mvetsetsani, inu munthu wamba, kuti ndidzatsogolera amphamvu anga 144,000 kuti apulumutse Yakobo ndipo ndidzabisabe otsala amphamvu a Isiraeli omwe adzapitiriza kukhala ndi moyo mu Ufumu wanga wa zaka chikwi pamalo okonzedwa ndi Yahweh m'chipululu, ngakhale kuti akhale obala zipatso ndi kuchulukana ndi kusangalala ndi mafuta a dziko mu ufumu wanga wa zaka chikwi womwe watsala pang'ono kubwera padziko lapansi pokwaniritsa lonjezo, pangano, ndi chipangano.

Zidzakhala choncho kwa munthu wamba, kuti adzalandira mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu ngati mpulumutsi wawo kapena adzakhala atasankha chiweruzo chawo chamuyaya m'nyanja yamoto yoyaka nthawi zonse chifukwa palibe chisankho china, ndipo palibe kwina komwe angapite chifukwa Yahweh sadzalola zoipa kugonjetsa ndi kuwononga cholengedwa chake ndipo masiku awo a kupanduka kofunitsitsa akufika kumapeto.

Ndine mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu, woperekedwa chifukwa cha machimo a dziko lapansi.

Ndine Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda, ndipo m'chilungamo ndikubwera kudzaweruza ndi kupanga nkhondo.

Ndine amene mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu ndi Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda agwirizana kwathunthu m'zolinga zanga zamuyaya ndi kulamulira monga Mfumu ya Mafumu ndi Mbuye wa Ambuye, ndiye ndani amene angaime motsutsana ndi Yeshua HaMashiach amene ali woyera wa Adonai, ngakhale iye amene Yahweh wamutumiza kudzapereka chiweruzo chamuyaya pa omwe adagwa ndi onse omwe asankha kuphatikizana ndi njira zawo.

Ndipo chotero ndikulangiza okhulupirika mwa Khristu kuti apitirizebe kukhala okhulupirika chifukwa tsiku la chiwombolo chanu lafika ndipo lili pafupi nanu. Yang'anani kokha kwa Ambuye wanu Wamuyaya wa Chilungamo chifukwa ogwawo asezera dziko lapansi ndi chigumula chachikulu cha mabodza ndi chinyengo chopangidwa kuti chisocheretse ngakhale osankhidwa ngati kuli kotheka. Chotero, mvetsetsani kuti aneneri onyenga ambiri atumizidwa omwe amanenera m'njira za Balamu ndipo omwe atengedwa ndi mizimu ya Yezebeli ndipo omwe amalankhula ndi mizimu ya wokana Khristu omwe amaphunzitsa Yesu wina yemwe amaima monyenga m'malo mwa ndi kutsutsana ndi Yesu Khristu yemwe ndi Yeshua HaMashiach m'mitima ya osokeretsedwa. Onse omwe atengeka m'chinyengo chotere adzalandira chiweruzo chawo ngati akana kulapa m'njira zawo pamene akutsutsidwa ndi Ambuye wawo m'kamwa mwa okhulupirika mwa Khristu. Zoonadi, zoonadi, aneneri ena onyenga ndi oona mtima ndipo asokeretsedwa ndipo akhoza kufikiridwa ndi choonadi cholankhulidwa pamilomo yachikondi, koma choonadi chokha ndi chomwe chidzawamasule ndipo choonadi chosapita m'mbali chokha cha machimo awo ndi maulosi awo onyenga kuti athe kudzibwezeretsa okha m'kulapa.

Kenako pali omwe amanenera chifukwa cha ndalama, ndi phindu lonyansa, ndi kuti atchuke ndi kutchuka ndikusangalala mokwanira ndi zinthu za dziko loipali. Ziribe kanthu kuti ndi ndani amene amanenera monyenga, mdzudzuleni ndi kukana Mawu awo, ngakhale Mawu omwe apangidwa ndi ogwawo kuti akole ngakhale osankhidwa m'chinyengo ngati kuli kotheka. Chifukwa chiyani mukuthamangathamanga, Mpingo wanga wokhulupirika? Kodi sindinakulamuleni ndi kukulangizani ndi kukupatsani mphamvu kuti mukhale ogonjetsa anga? Ngakhale omwe amagonjetsa mbali iliyonse ya ufumu wa adani mofanana ndi momwe ine ndinagonjetsera? Chotero, bwerani kwa Khristu Yesu ndipo ndidzatsimikizira m'mitima yanu aneneri anga oona chifukwa aneneri anga oona amalankhula mogwirizana ndi mtima wa Ambuye wawo yemwe ndi Mawu wosandulika thupi. Chotero, Khristu Yesu sangatsutsane ndi malemba, chifukwa ndine wotero!

Kwa iwo omwe modzikuza amaganiza kuti akundiimira koma atengeka m'mabodza: Chifukwa chiyani mukuthamangathamanga, kufunafuna wina woti atikitire makutu anu ngakhale kufunafuna afiti omwe amalira ndi kung'ung'udza omwe adzakuuzani ndendende zomwe mukufuna kumva kuti adzutse zilakolako m'mitima yanu yoipa pamene mukuthawa kulapa kupita m'manja mwa onyenga omwe atumizidwa kudzanyenga. Mukuthamangathamanga chifukwa mukunyalanyaza mawu a Yeshua HaMashiach, chifukwa mukukana choonadi chomwe chikanakumasulani, chifukwa mukukana kuphunzira za ine posunga Mawu a Yahweh monga muyezo wanu wapamwamba ndi wokhayo. Chotero, ndikulangiza onse omwe amaganiza kuti amanenera Khristu Yesu, ngakhale onse omwe amaganiza kuti amalankhulira Yahweh, WAMKULU NDINE kuti mudzichenjere nokha kuti mungagwere mu mdima wamuyaya ndi chiweruzo chosatha chifukwa onse omwe amalumikizana ndi ogwawo mofananamo ndikukana kulapa mayina awo adzafafanizidwa m'buku la moyo ngati akana moto wa woyenga womwe adzasiyidwamo ngati apitiriza m'machimo awo, chifukwa palibe nthawi yotsala.

Chotero ndikulangiza ndi kuchenjeza omwe ndimawakonda kuti athe kupewa chinyengo champhamvu chomwe sichinapangidwe kwa iwo ngati ayenda m'kumvera Ambuye wawo, ndipo chotero ndikulangiza ndi kuchenjeza omwe ndimawakonda kuti asakodwe ndi chinyengo chachikulu chomwe ngakhale tsopano chikuphimba dziko lapansi. Ndikulangiza onse omwe adzamva Mawu awa kuti achoke ku zoipa ndikuchoka kwa afiti omwe amalira ndi kung'ung'udza ndikuchotsa malo anu patebulo la Yezebeli ndikukana mphotho za Balamu. Mvetsetsani kuti aliyense amene maulosi ake amanenera zotsutsana ndi malemba akulankhula mabodza mwachiphamaso. Mvetsetsani kuti ngakhale Yahweh, WAMKULU NDINE, kapena Yeshua HaMashiach (yemwenso ndi Khristu Yesu) sadzatsutsana ndi malemba. Chotero, omwe amalankhula zotsutsana ndi malemba amalankhula zotsutsana ndi Yahweh WAMKULU NDINE ndipo amalankhulanso zotsutsana ndi Yeshua HaMashiach yemwenso ndi Khristu Yesu.

Mvetsetsani bwino lomwe kuti ndine ndani ndi yemwe Yahweh anandilenga kuti ndikhale. Ndine Mawu a Yahweh wosandulika thupi yemwe amakhala mwa inu okondedwa anga, ndipo sindingathe kudzitsutsa ndekha kapena kunama, chifukwa ndine wopanda tchimo ndipo ndidzakhala wopanda tchimo nthawi zonse. Mvetsetsani Atate wa Kuwala yemwe mwa Iye mulibe kusinthasintha kapena mthunzi wotembenuka, ngakhale iye yemwe muli naye chita! Mvetsetsani kuti n'kosatheka kwa Adonai Yahweh kudzitsutsa yekha chifukwa n'kosatheka kwa Adonai Yahweh kunama. Komabe, chifukwa chiyani mukupendeketsa makutu anu mopusa kwa omwe amadutsa mwachinyengo usiku? Kodi mudzachita uhule okondedwa anga, pamene m'malo mwake muyenera kukhala okhulupirika ndi omvera kwa Ambuye mmodzi, yemwe ndi mpulumutsi wanu wamuyaya, ngakhale iye yemwe akubwera kudzachotsa okhulupirika mwa Khristu kupita kumwamba mphindi iliyonse tsopano?

Chotero mvetsetsani, kuti zipata za gehena sizidzagonjetsa okhulupirika mwa Khristu omwe ndi Mpingo wanga woona chifukwa chotero kwalembedwa ndi kulonjezedwa kalekale, ndipo chotero kudalembedwabe ndi kulonjezedwa mu nthawi za tsopano, chifukwa Mawu a Yahweh ndi Yeshua HaMashiach ndi mawu oyera ngati siliva, woyesedwa m'ng'anjo ya nthaka, woyengedwa kasanu ndi kawiri. Inde, ogwawo alibe mphamvu pa okhulupirika mwa Khristu ndipo ndidzachititsa okhulupirika anga kupondereza mbali iliyonse ya ufumu wa adani, ngakhale kupondereza omwe angafune kuletsa chosaletsedwa.

Ndine Khristu Yesu ndipo ndani angathe kuima kulimbana ndi mphamvu zanga ndi ukulu wanga womwe ndalandira kuchokera kwa Yahweh mwiniwake!
