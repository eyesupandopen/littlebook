Kwa Okhulupirika mwa Khristu Yesu!

Mverani mawu a Ambuye wanu Wamuyaya, ngakhale Yeshua HaMashiach, chifukwa ndine amene ndakuitanani ku ulemerero ndi ukoma kudzera mu kubadwa kwatsopano komwe mwasindikizidwa ndi mzimu wanga woyera wa lonjezo. Inde, ndakupangani kukhala ana a Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya, ngakhale Yehova mwiniwake, ndipo palibe wina wofanana naye! Chifukwa chake, mvetsetsani, okondedwa anga kuti muli m'gulu la omwe ndawasindikiza ku tsiku la chiwombolo limene mudzaima mokwanira m'chilungamo chomwe ndidzakuvekani ndipo mphoto yambiri ambiri adzaitenga chifukwa cha kukhulupirika kwawo, ngakhale ena adzataya.

Pitirizani kutsata mwamphamvu cholinga, kuti mulandire mphoto ya kuitana kwapamwamba kwa Mulungu mwa Khristu Yesu, ndipo kuti mukwaniritse mphoto yanu yonse pa njira zomwe ndakhazikitsa mapazi anu, ngakhale njira zautumiki pomwe ndayendetsa mapazi anu mu utumiki kwa ine pa dziko lapansi mu nthawi zanga zachisomo. Ndipo inu ndinu ntchito yanga, olengedwa mwa Khristu Yesu kuchita ntchito zabwino, zomwe Mulungu adazikonzeratu, kuti muyende mwa izo.

Ndipo zili choncho ndithu, okondedwa anga, kuti ndine amene ndakupatsani cholinga chamuyaya mwa ine, Ambuye wanu wamuyaya. Ndine amene ndili mu umodzi wangwiro ndi cholinga ndi Yehova, ngakhale NDINE wamkulu, choncho ngati Mulungu ali mbali yanu, okondedwa anga, ndani angakuimireni? Chifukwa chiyani mukugonjera ndi kupereka chidwi chanu ku zinthu zofooka ndi zaumphawi zomwe zikufuna kukusocheretsani kuchoka m'dzanja langa lamphamvu lamuyaya, komwe kuchotsedwa n'kosatheka ngati musunga diso lanu limodzi loyang'ana pa ine, Khristu Yesu Ambuye wanu wokwera kumwamba!

Ndipo choncho ndikuphunzitsabe mpaka lero kuti onse omwe ali ndi makutu akumva amvere kuti musadziunjikire chuma pa dziko lapansi, pamene njenjete ndi dzimbiri zimawononga, ndipo mbala zimaboola, ndi kuba. Koma dziunjikireni chuma kumwamba, komwe njenjete kapena dzimbiri siziwononga, ndipo mbala siziboola, kapena kuba. Pakuti kumene kuli chuma chanu, komweko kudzakhala mtima wanu. Kuwala kwa thupi ndi diso: Chifukwa chake ngati diso lanu lili limodzi, thupi lanu lonse lidzakhala lodzaza ndi kuwala.

Okondedwa anga, kodi simukumvetsa fanizo langa? Chuma chanu ndi maganizo anu, ndipo choncho ndikukulangizani kuti musunge maganizo anu pa zinthu zakumwamba kuti m'kuyenda kwanu mupange ndi kuyenda mu ntchito zabwino zomwe ndakhazikitsa kuti mapazi anu atsate ndi mtima umodzi. Chifukwa chake, ndinapangitsa Paulo kulembera ku Korinto: Koma ndikuwopa kuti monga njoka inanyenga Hava ndi kuchenjera kwake, momwemonso maganizo anu angawonongedwe kuchoka ku kuphweka komwe kuli mwa Khristu.

Chifukwa chake, ikani mitima yanu pa zinthu zakumwamba, osati pa zinthu za pa dziko lapansi. Pakuti mwafa, ndipo moyo wanu wabisika ndi Ine mwa Mulungu. Pamene Ine, Khristu Yesu, amene ndine moyo wanu, ndidzawonekera, pamenepo inunso mudzawonekera pamodzi ndi ine mu ulemerero. Chifukwa chake, okondedwa anga, mvetsetsani kuti ndinu kuwala kwa dziko lapansi, ngakhale onse omwe ali ana a Atate wa Kuwala! Chifukwa chake, ikani chidwi chanu chonse pa ine, Ambuye wanu wamuyaya wa Ambuye ndi Mfumu ya Mafumu ndipo ndidzagwira ntchito mwamphamvu mu mzimu wanu kuti kuwala kwanga kwamuyaya kuwale kuchokera m'thupi langa lomwe inu muli mbali yake, ndipo choncho ziwiya zanga zadothi zikuwala ndi kusonyeza ulemerero wa Mulungu wamuyaya pankhope ya Yesu Khristu!

Ndipo choncho ndikufunsa inu mkwatibwi wanga, ndipo choncho ndikufunsa thupi langa, ndipo choncho ndikufunsa okhulupirika anga mwa Khristu, chifukwa chiyani mukulola adani anu kusokoneza ndi kugawa chidwi chanu kuchoka ku ulemerero wanga ndi ukulu wanga? Mvetsetsani kuti akugwa amamvetsetsa bwino kuti chiwombolo chanu chokwanira chili pafupi ngati mpweya wanu wotsatira, ndipo choncho akufuna kuba chimwemwe chanu chifukwa akudziwa kuti n'zosatheka kukuchotsani m'manja mwanga mwamuyaya, ngakhale amayesa.

Chifukwa chake, musawope adani anu m'pang'ono pomwe, chomwe kwa iwo ndi chizindikiro chowonekera cha chiwonongeko, koma kwa inu cha chipulumutso, ndipo chimenecho cha Mulungu. Musapatutsire chidwi chanu ku phokoso la akugwa, okondedwa anga, kapena kutembenukira kumanja kapena kumanzere. Koma khalani pa njira yowongoka ndi yopapatiza, ngakhale njira ya chipulumutso yomwe mukuphunzira za ine Yesu wa ku Nazarete pakuti ndine amene ndikuphunzitsani njira za chipulumutso zomwe mupulumutsidwa nazo mpaka kumapeto pamene mukuyeretsedwa m'magazi anga owombola ku machimo onse kuti mukhale ndi chilungamo cha Mulungu mwa Khristu mwa inu chiyembekezo cha ulemerero!

Mvetsetsani kuti pamene mukundiyendera, Satana amakuukirani pamene akufuna kuyimitsa khama lanu ndi kukupatutsirani mu ntchito zake zachinyengo zachabechabe. Ngakhale uthenga wabwino wachinyengo wa chikhalidwe womwe umanyalanyaza kulapa ndi kukhululukidwa kwa machimo m'malo mwa ntchito zabwino. Koma zili choncho, kuti kudyetsa anjala, kuveka amaliseche, kupatsa nyumba opanda pokhala ndi zina zambiri ndi ntchito zabwino zoyenera kukwaniritsa zosowa zomwe zilipo.

Koma Ine, Mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu sindinadzipereke kamodzi, konse, ndi kwamuyaya ngati nsembe yomaliza ya tchimo pa mtengo waukali ku Kalvare kuti ndilipire ntchito zabwino, kapena Yehova sanandiukitse kwa akufa kuti ndikhale wopereka katundu wa dziko lino kwa omwe akusowa, koma ndinapachikidwa pa mtengo ndikukhetsa dontho langa lomaliza la magazi mu zowawa zowawa m'malo ndi kulipira tchimo ndi zotsatira za machimo a ochimwa.

Ine, Khristu Yesu, ndakuwombolani ku temberero la Chilamulo, pokhala temberero m'malo mwanu, (pakuti kwalembedwa, wotembereredwa ndi yense wopachikidwa pa mtengo) ndipo komabe umenewo unali mtengo womwe sunali wanga kuti ndilipire, chifukwa Ine, Yesu wa ku Nazarete, ndine amene kusalakwa kwanga kuimabe mpaka lero! Ndipo ndine wopanda tchimo nthawi zonse ndi kwamuyaya chifukwa ndimachita chifuniro cha Atate wanga nthawi zonse. Chifukwa chake, pumulani mu ntchito zomwe ndakupatsani kuti muchite ndipo tengani goli langa pa inu mokwanira, pakuti goli langa ndi lofewa, ndipo katundu wanga ndi wopepuka.

Nthawi zachiweruzo chachikulu zafika pa dziko lonse lapansi, ngakhale nthawi za masautso a Yakobo. Koma masautso a Yakobo asanafike mokwanira pa dziko lapansi ziyenera kukhala kuti okhulupirika anga adzakwatulidwa ku mkwiyo womwe ukubwera ndipo Ine, Khristu Yesu, ndine amene ndikubwera m'mitambo ndi mawu a mngelo wamkulu ndi lipenga la Mulungu ndipo akufa mwa Khristu adzauka kaye ndipo inu omwe muli amoyo ndi otsala mudzatengedwa m'mitambo pamodzi ndi iwo ndipo m'chipinda chachifumu cha Yehova Atate wanu wamuyaya mudzapita tsopano.

Ndipo choncho tsikuli lafika pa inu, ana anga, ngakhale onse omwe ali okhulupirika mwa Khristu Yesu. Chifukwa chake, siyani nkhawa za dziko lapansi logwali m'mbuyo ndipo ikani chidwi chanu chonse pa Ambuye wanu wamuyaya, ngakhale Yeshua HaMashiach. Inde, chidwi chanu chonse chosagawanika chifukwa mdierekezi akufunafuna omwe angawameze ndipo ngati mkango wobangula amayendayenda ndi kubangula kwake kopanda pake koma mvetsetsani kuti iye ndi mdani wogonjetsedwa okondedwa anga chifukwa ndamugonjetsa mokwanira ndipo chigonjetso chokwanira ndi changa mokwanira kuyambira mawu anga omaliza omwe ndinayankhula m'thupi pa mtengo waukaliwo: Zatha!

Ndipo zili choncho kuti tsopano ndikuyenda mokwanira chifukwa cha chiwombolo chanu chokwanira ndipo ndidzamaliza ntchito yangwiro imeneyo mwa aliyense wa inu pomwe mudzalengedwanso nthawi yomweyo ndi mokwanira ngati cholengedwa chatsopano ndi changwiro ndi chopanda tchimo. Ngakhale cholengedwa changwiro chomwe chivundi sichingawononge, chomwe ulemerero wake sudzatha, ndipo kuwala kwake sikudzazilala. Pakuti mudzapangidwa ngati ine, Khristu Yesu, pakuti ndine Adamu womaliza, ndipo ndine mzimu wopereka moyo amenenso ndi mtsogoleri wa chipulumutso chanu.

Chifukwa chake, samalirani zinthu za mzimu! Musalole mivi yamoto ya woyipayo kuwotcha njira yawo mu chuma chanu. Mvetsetsani kuti Satana angafune kuwononga chidwi chanu cha mtima umodzi pa Ambuye wanu wamuyaya ndi chuma chake chachabechabe ndi chowola chomwe nthawi zambiri chimakhala chinyengo chokhutiritsa cha chenicheni. Choncho kanani maganizo ndi malingaliro ake ndipo kanani njira zake zonse zopanduka pakuti zake ndi njira za imfa ndipo ndithudi akufuna kugawana cholowa chake m'malawi otentha kwambiri a sulufule wowola ndi aliyense amene asankha nyanja yamoto yamuyaya, koma mapeto amenewo ndi osatheka kwa inu okondedwa anga ndipo ndakusindikizani kwamuyaya ndi chidindo chamuyaya cha Mulungu wamoyo.

Choncho khalani mwa ine kuti ndiwongole njira zanu, ndipo kuti ndikuwonetseni poyera misampha yomwe Satana angafune kukola okhulupirika anga. Khalani mwa ine, okondedwa anga, pakuti mwa Khristu palibe msampha womwe ungakhazikitsidwe ndipo msampha wa mlenje sungakhazikitsidwe ndi luso kapena mphamvu. Chifukwa chake, londerani maganizo anu ndipo sungani mitu ndi mitima yanu yodzaza ndi Mawu anga oyera! (Pakuti zida za nkhondo yathu sizili zathupi, koma zamphamvu mwa Mulungu kugwetsa malinga.) Kugwetsa maganizo, ndi chinthu chilichonse chokwezeka chomwe chimadzikweza chokha motsutsana ndi chidziwitso cha Mulungu, ndi kugwira ukapolo lingaliro lililonse ku kumvera kwa Khristu.

Pakuti sitilimbana ndi thupi ndi magazi, koma ndi maufumu, ndi mphamvu, ndi olamulira a dziko lapansi, akalonga a mdima wa dziko lino, ndi zoyipa zauzimu, zomwe zili m'malo apamwamba. Chifukwa chake tengani zida zonse za Mulungu, kuti muthe kukaniza m'tsiku loyipa, ndipo mutamaliza zinthu zonse, khalani chilili.

Inde, khalani chilili mwa Ambuye wanu wamuyaya tiana twanga chifukwa nthawi zafika pa inu. Ngakhale ambiri a inu mukufuna kubwerera ku mkhalidwe wa tulo, ngakhale mafunde otonthoza mwachinyengo a dziko lino akufuna kukugonetsani mu chinyengo chachikulu. Musagwe m'matsenga awo, ndipo tembenukani kuchoka ku ufiti ndi malodza awo. Tembenukani kuchoka ku zomwe amati zosangalatsa zawo, chifukwa sikoyenera kuti ana oyera a Mulungu wamkulu omwe ali a Khristu Yesu mokwanira asangalatsidwe ndi tchimo kachiwiri.

Ndatsimikizira mwa okhulupirika anga kuyandikira ndi zenizeni zenizeni za kuwonekera kwanga komwe kukuyandikira kusonkhanitsa okhulupirika anga mu chitetezo cha kumwamba. Ndapereka ngakhale maloto, masomphenya, ndi mawu ambiri kwa tiana tomwe timakondedwa kwambiri. Okondedwa anga, chochitika ichi chikudziponya chokha mokwanira njira yanu ndipo chafika pa inu ndipo chili pafupi kuposa momwe mumadzilolera kulingalira. Chifukwa chake dzekonzekereni ine. Pitirizani kutuluka mu tchimo lonse ndipo kuulula machimo anu kwa ine mokwanira ndipo kuulula zolakwa zanu wina ndi mzake.

Ndindikira okhulupirika anga mu mzimu woyera wa lonjezo ndipo choncho ndapereka mafuta a nyali zanu, ngakhale onse omwe alandira mwaulere kuchokera kwa ine mphatso yanga yamuyaya ya chipulumutso ndi moyo wamuyaya ndi cholowa chamuyaya mwa Khristu Yesu Ambuye wamuyaya wa Ambuye ndi Mfumu ya Mafumu! Yang'anani kumwamba okondedwa anga ndipo kwa omwe akuyang'ana momvera ndi mokhulupirika kuwonekera kwanga adzawona ndi maso awo ulemerero wa kuwonekera kwanga, chifukwa chinthu ichi sichidzachitidwa mwachinsinsi ndipo n'zosatheka kwa Satana kubisa ulemerero wa kuwonekera kwanga, ngakhale adzanama ndi kufuna kuphimba kuwonekera kwanga ndi alendo abodza ndi nkhondo yapadziko lonse.

Chifukwa chake, ndidikireni moleza mtima ndipo kwaniritsani mokwanira ntchito zomwe ndakupatsani kuti mumalize kuti mumalize mokwanira maphunziro anu autumiki mu dongosolo langa lamuyaya la chipulumutso pa moyo wa aliyense wa inu. Mvetsetsani okondedwa anga kuti chilungamo cha Yehova ndi mphoto ndi cholowa ndi malipiro siziwerengedwa monga munthu wachibadwa amawerengera chifukwa munthu wachibadwa ndi wosalungama ndi wopanda chilungamo. Aliyense wa inu wapatsidwa ntchito zoti apange kuti mupititse patsogolo zolinga zanga za chipulumutso ndipo Ine, Khristu Yesu ndidzaweruza ntchito zanu zomwe mwagwira mu nsembe ndi utumiki kwa Ambuye wanu wamuyaya.

Mvetsetsani kuti nonse mudzalandira mphoto yayikulu chifukwa cha ntchito zanu zokhulupirika zautumiki zomwe mwagwira mokhulupirika m'dzina langa loyera. Ndipo, Ine, Khristu Yesu ndine amene ndikuwona ntchito iliyonse yomwe yachitidwa ndi okhulupirika mwa Khristu ndi cholinga cha mtima ndipo ndidzakupatsani mphoto mpaka kumapeto. Pamene ndimayang'ana anthu olemera akuponya mphatso zawo m'chosungira chuma ndinawona anthu olemera, omwe anaponya mphatso zawo m'chosungira chuma. Ndipo ndinawonanso mkazi wamasiye wosauka wina yemwe anaponya mmenemo timakobiri tiwiri: Ndipo ndinati, Zoonadi ndikukuuzani, kuti mkazi wamasiye wosaukayu waponya zambiri kuposa onsewo.

Ndipo choncho ndikubwera kwa inu okondedwa anga, ngakhale pano mu mphindi zomaliza zothamanga izi ndisanakusonkhanitseni kwa ine ndipo ndikukulimbikitsani nonse ndi chikondi chamuyaya cha Yehova mwiniwake kuti mukhale chilili ndi kuyang'ana kumwamba pakuti chiwombolo chanu chafika pa inu. Ndipo funde lililonse la tsoka lidzawonjezeka mu mphamvu ndipo chiwonongeko chidzawonjezeka pamene funde lililonse likugunda pa dziko lapansi. Zowawa zobereka sizikuyima tsopano ndipo dziko lapansi liyenera kubereka mwana wake pakuti pa dziko lapansi pali ana a Adamu omwe tsopano asanduka ana a Mulungu ngakhale kudzera mwa Adamu womaliza. Ndipo mwadzidzidzi onse adzasinthidwa kwamuyaya ndi kwamuyaya kukhala ufulu waulemerero wa ungwiro ndipo adzavekedwa kwamuyaya mu ulemerero wamuyaya ndi wosatha ndipo ali ndi tsogolo lamuyaya ndi cholinga chamuyaya mu mapulani amuyaya a Yehova.

Mvetsetsani kuti chinjoka chachikulu chofiira chaloledwa kukonza bwalo ndipo chikusuntha zidutswa zake m'malo pa bolodi la dziko lapansi. Mvetsetsani kuti zochitika zikuchitika kale ndipo kuti njira yamakono ya dziko lapansi ndi yosatheka kupitiriza ndipo idzatsogolera kotheratu ku tsoka lalikulu ndi ladzidzidzi ndipo zambiri mwa zochitika izi zomwe zikugwirizana zidzabwera pamodzi mu kugwirizana kwangwiro.

Pakuti ndi momwe iliri nthawi ya mapulani a Yehova, ndipo Yod iliyonse ndi munga wa Yod idzabwera pamodzi mu kachitidwe kangwiro ndi kugwirizana, pakuti chilichonse chomwe Yehova akayika dzanja lake chimabweretsa ungwiro wamuyaya ndi chilungamo chamuyaya chifukwa ngakhale omwe asankha chivundi ndi malawi amuyaya a chilango amaweruzidwa ndi kuweruzidwa mwachilungamo ndi mwangwiro.

Ndipo choncho ndikuti okondedwa anga, sungani zingwe za nyali zanu zodulidwa ndipo mudzisunge nokha pa chiyero chapamwamba m'magazi anga olungama ndi oyeretsa owombola. Sungani zingwe za nyali zanu zodulidwa podzisunga nokha mu chiyero cha maganizo chomwe lemba langa lamuyaya limabweretsa pakuti Mulungu wamuyaya, ngakhale Yehova mwiniwake ndi amene watumiza Mawu ake ndi kuwachiritsa.

Ndine amene ndakuitanani ku ulemerero ndi ukoma.

Ndine amene ndakupatsani cholinga chamuyaya mwa ine, Ambuye wanu wamuyaya.

Ndine amene ndili mu umodzi wangwiro ndi cholinga ndi Yehova, ngakhale NDINE wamkulu.

Ndine amene ndikuphunzitsani njira za chipulumutso zomwe mupulumutsidwa nazo mpaka kumapeto.

Ndine wopanda tchimo nthawi zonse ndi kwamuyaya chifukwa ndimachita chifuniro cha Atate wanga nthawi zonse.

Ndine Adamu womaliza.

Ndine mzimu wopereka moyo amenenso ndi mtsogoleri wa chipulumutso chanu.

Ndipo ndine amene ndikutumiza mawu a uthenga wanga wabwino wamuyaya kudzera mwa mthenga wanga amene ndamuukitsa ndi kumutumiza chifukwa cha cholinga ichi ndipo kudzera mwa mthenga wanga mawu a uthenga wanga wabwino wamuyaya atumizidwa.

Zoonadi, zoonadi, ndine amene Yehova wagawana naye mpando wake wachifumu mpaka adani anga atapangidwa chopondapo mapazi anga, ngakhale mpaka nditapereka chilengedwe chowomboledwa mokwanira m'manja mwa Mulungu wamuyaya wa chilungamo, kuti ndithudi Yehova akhale zonse mu zonse ndipo kuti zonse zikhale inde ndi ameni.

Ndipo ndine, Yeshua HaMashiach amene ndatumiza mawu a uthenga wabwino wamuyaya kuti amvere onse omwe ali olowa nyumba a moyo wamuyaya ndipo mawu awa adzasungidwa kudzera mu masautso ndi ziweruzo zomwe zikubwera ndipo mawu awa adzapeza maso, makutu, ndi mitima ya omwe atumizidwa kwa iwo. Ndithudi, ndikubwera msanga. Ameni. Chisomo cha Ambuye wamuyaya Yesu Khristu chitsanulidwa mochuluka pa onse okhulupirika mwa Khristu, AMENI ndi Ameni.
