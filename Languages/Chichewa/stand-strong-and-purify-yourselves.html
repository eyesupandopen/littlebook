Khazikikani Ndipo Mudziyeretse

Ambuye wokhulupirika akuti kwa osankhidwa ndi okhulupirika ake: Khazikikani ndipo mudziyeretse mwa kusiya ndi kuvomereza machimo anu. Tulukani mwa iye, anthu anga, ndipo mukhale osiyana ndi machimo ake kuti musatenge nawo gawo pachiweruzo chake ndi miliri yake. Kodi iye ndani mukufunsa? Kodi si Babulo Wamkulu, amene chiweruzo chake chafika? Kodi si hule lalikulu lomwe laledzera ndi magazi a oyera mtima? Chitetezo ndi mtendere ndidzakupatsani, ndipo ndidzakutsogolerani kumalo obisala m'nyumba ya Atate wanga mpaka nyengoyi itatha.

Ine ndine Ambuye Yesu Khristu, amene ndakuitanani ku ulemerero ndi ukoma, choncho musataye mtima pakuchita zabwino. Ndakupatsani mawu oti mundilankhulire ndi kulengeza za kubwera kwanga posachedwa. Koma ambiri mu Mpingo wanga ataya mawu awo ndipo asanduka opanda chikhulupiriro m'chiyembekezo chawo chomwe Atate wanga anaika kwa inu m'Mawu ake. Musakhale opanda chikhulupiriro koma khulupirirani kokha. Yang'anani zizindikiro za kubwera kwanga, monga Atate wanga walengeza kumwamba ndipo zikudziwika kale bwino. Chifukwa chiyani mukuchedwa kukhulupirira? Ndikhulupirireni, ana anga, pakuti ndikukuthandizani; inde, ndikudulira nthambi iliyonse mwa inu yomwe sibala chipatso. Khalani ndi chidaliro mu Ufumu wanga pa inu ndipo lowani mu chipulumutso changa pamene mukugwirira ntchito chipulumutso chanu ndi mantha ndi kunjenjemera. Bwererani kwa ine m'pemphero ndikundipempha kuti ndikuwonetseni machimo anu kuti muwavomereze kwa ine ndi kuwagonjetsa ndi magazi anga. Choncho musachite mantha, pakuti chipulumutso chanu chili pafupi; inde, mkonono wa masekondi watsala pang'ono kumaliza kuzungulira kwake komaliza mu m'badwo wanga wa chisomo.

Bwererani kwa ine ndipo bwerani kwa ine popeza ndakukonzerani malo patebulo langa ndipo ndakukonzerani malo obisala m'nyumba ya Atate wanga kuti muthawe tsunami ya masoka yomwe ikubwera. Zikuyandikira omwe akukhala pa dziko lapansi, ndipo chiweruzo chachikulu chatsala pang'ono kugwa pa dziko lonse lapansi. Musasiye dzina langa! Ndikudziwa kuti mwatopa mu ola lomalizali, koma pumulani mwa ine ndipo pezani mphamvu mwa ine ndikulengeza kubwera kwanga posachedwa. Sindinapereke tsiku kapena nthawi yoti mudikire, chifukwa mukadamanga miyoyo yanu yonse mozungulira chochitikacho. Koma musanyengedwe: tsiku ndi ola zikudziwika kumwamba panthawiyi, ndipo angelo anga ali okonzeka, akudikira kulira kwa lipenga pamene ndidzasonkhanitsa omwe akugona m'dziko lapansi poyamba, ndipo omwe ali amoyo ndi otsala adzawaphatikiza kukumana nane mumlengalenga, ndipo ndidzakutengerani ku cholowa chanu chomwe Atate wabisa mwa ine. Koma musataye mtima.

Zidzachitika m'kuthwanima kwa diso, pamene ambiri a inu mudzakhala mukuchita zinthu zanu za tsiku ndi tsiku, ndipo nthawi yomweyo mudzaima pamaso panga pamene ndikukuperekani kwa Atate wanga; ndipo ife tikulakalaka tsikulo monga momwe tonse tikulakalakira kukhalapo kwanu. Ndikhoza kuonekera ndikulalikira kwa ambiri, ndipo ndani anganene kuti sindinachite zimenezo nthawi zina? Kodi mudzalengeza zomwe Ambuye angachite? Kapena mudzachepetsa luso langa ndi Ufumu wanga chifukwa cha kusowa kwanu chikhulupiriro? Sizingatheke! Komabe, ndakupangani kukhala antchito anzanga ndikukuphatikizani mu ntchito yomwe Atate wanga anandipatsa kuti ndikwaniritse, ndipo dziwani kuti ndi ulemu ndi chimwemwe chomwe ndakupatsani chifukwa cha chipulumutso chanu, mphoto, ndi umphumphu wanu.

Monga mwatumikira ena m'dzina langa, kodi ine sindinakutumikireni? Perekani mwaulere ndipo musabisale chidziwitso cha kubwera kwanga, ngakhale ambiri adzakana ndipo mudzaonedwa ngati amisala. Mabodza a mdani adzasungunuka pamaso pa mawu anu odzaza ndi mphamvu pamene mukundilankhulira ndikulengeza kuonekera kwanga ndi kulengeza chiweruzo chomwe chikubwera, popeza ngakhale m'masekondi omalizawa ena adzabwera ku chipulumutso. Ambiri sanakonzekerebe ndipo adzakana mawu anu; akondeni ngakhale zili choncho, chifukwa khamu lalikulu lidzapulumutsidwa m'mayesero akulu ndi chisautso, ndipo dziwani kuti mawu anu akhoza kukhala ngati nyali ya kuwala ndi chiyembekezo pa mayesero omwe angakumane nawo.

O, okhala pa dziko lapansi omwe mwakana kuitana kwanga ndikudziyesa osayenera mphatso yanga ya chipulumutso ndi zonse zomwe imabweretsa: Chiweruzo chachikulu chalengezedwa pa dziko lapansi ndipo chatsala pang'ono kukugwerani, ngakhale mukudzinamiza nokha, kuti, "Ndili ndi mapulani akulu a ine mwini ndipo mawa kudzakhala chimodzimodzi ndi lero!" Wopusa iwe! Sumvetsa ngakhale kusintha kwa nyengo, pamene thambo likulengeza ukwati wa mkwatibwi wanga kwa ine kumwamba, ndipo thambo likusintha kuchoka pa chisomo kupita ku chiweruzo pamaso pako. Tsoka lalikulu lidzakugwerani posachedwa, ndipo ambiri adzafa m'machimo awo. Sifuniro langa kuti muwonongeke, koma mukukana nsembe yanga yopanda tchimo yomwe ikanakupulumutsani mpaka kumapeto.

Dziko lomwe mukulidziwa lero lidzazimirira pamaso panu, ndipo zosangalatsa zonse za tchimo m'moyo uno sizidzakhala anzanu; koma mudzathamangitsidwa ndi kusakidwa ndi gulu lankhondo la adani anu omwe akulakalaka imfa yanu ndikulakalaka katundu wanu. Kudzakhala kugwedezeka kwakukulu pansi pa mapazi anu, ndipo dziko lapansi lidzang'ambika, ndipo ambiri adzagwera m'dziko lapansi. Atsogoleri anu akuperekeni kale, ngakhale mukupitiriza kuyang'ana kwa iwo mopusa, kuyesera kudziwa voti yanu. Nsanja zanu zidzagwa, ndipo milatho yanu idzagwa, ndipo zomangamanga zanu zidzazimirira pamaso panu, ndipo palibe chomwe chidzakhala chimodzimodzi.

Mudzathawira kuti pamene mwakana Ambuye woyera, ine, Ambuye Yesu Khristu? Ndani adzakupulumutsani tsopano mu nthawi yanu ya chiweruzo? O, koma mukuti, sindiyenera chiweruzo, mukutanthauza chiyani? Kodi simunakane Mlengi wanu pamene wanditumiza kwa inu chifukwa cha chisomo chanu chopulumutsa, chisomo chopulumutsa chomwe mukudziyesa osayenera kuchilandira? Chifukwa chiyani mwandikana? Ndakuchitirani cholakwa chanji? Ndinapereka dontho langa lomaliza la magazi mu kuzunzika kwakukulu ndi ululu kuti mukhale ndi moyo pamaso panga, koma simukukhulupirirabe mboni zanga zomwe ndazitumiza m'dzina langa.

Ngakhale pokuchenjezani za tsoka lanu lomwe likuyandikira, ine kapena Atate wanga tilibe chimwemwe mu zomwe zatsala pang'ono kumasulidwa. Koma monga mwasangalatsidwa ndi tchimo, tsopano mudzamezedwa amoyo ndi zotsatira zomwe mwadzikundikira nokha. Monga mwalakalaka ndalama, katundu, ndi kutchuka kwachuma, simunachite china koma kuunjika mkwiyo pa tebulo lanu la chakudya, ndipo mudzadya chisautso ndi ululu. Ndidzakupatsani mdima womwe mumaukonda kwambiri. Mdima udzagwa pa dziko lanu ndipo udzaphimba dziko lapansi, mdima wokhuthala kwambiri kotero kuti dzuwa la masana lidzaoneka ngati lazimitsidwa. Mukuti kwa ine, ndili ndi tchimo lanji kuti ndibweretse mkwiyo wanu; kodi sitinachite ntchito zambiri zamphamvu m'dzina lanu?

Zoonadi, mwachita ntchito zambiri, koma si ntchito zomwe ndakupatsani; ayi, mwasanduka mneneri wonyenga, kulosera mawu ochokera m'thupi lanu lopotoka. Ayi, mukuchita ntchito zanu chifukwa cha ulemerero wanu, popeza mwapanga mimba yanu kukhala mulungu wanu. Mumayika mawu abwino ndi zolankhula zokongola za anthu oipa pamwamba pa Mawu anga oyera. Kodi ine sindine Mawu osanduka thupi? Mukanadya mwa ine mkate wa moyo ndikuthawa chiwonongeko, osamvanso njala. Mukanamwa mitsinje ya madzi amoyo yotuluka mwa ine; inde, mukanamwa mzimu wanga ndipo simukanamvanso ludzu. Koma pakundikana kwanu, mudzalowa m'njala yachikulu ndi masautso, ngakhale mpaka mphindi ino mukuganizabe kuti sizidzakugwerani mwadzidzidzi. Chuma chanu chawonongeka, ndipo chuma chomwe mwachilakalaka chidzakhala chopanda phindu nthawi yomweyo. Mwayika chikhulupiriro chanu mwa olamulira a mdima wamakono ndipo mwakonda mabodza a Satana kuposa kuwala kwa choonadi changa chomwe chilipo nthawi zonse.

Choncho, mudzafunafuna malo pa tebulo lawo, koma mudzapeza kuti palibe malo okonzedwa inu. Adzakhala ndi zochuluka zomwe adzikundikira okha, ndipo mudzapeza mwayi pokhapokha mutalandira chizindikiro cha chilombo. Ngakhale chilombocho sichinawululidwe kwenikweni mu nthawi yake, ndachenjeza za iye ndikumudziwitsa kwa okhulupirika anga kuti asanyengedwe ndi mawu ake abwino ndi zolankhula zake zokongola, ngakhale panthawiyi alibe ufumu, osachepera pakali pano. Musatenge chizindikiro chake, chifukwa onse omwe atenga adzakhala ndi gawo lawo pambali pa chilombo ndi mneneri wonyenga m'nyanja yamoto, ndipo utsi wa kuzunzika kwawo udzakwera kwamuyaya.

Mwachenjezedwa, ndipo ngakhale ndikufunabe kukulanditsani ku chiwonongeko chanu, mukukanabe kuitana kwanga mwamakani. Choncho, ndidzakupatsani zipatso za njira zanu.

Mudzakumana ndi njala yachikulu, nkhondo yachikulu, chiwonongeko chachikulu. Magulu ankhondo ofiira adzakuthamangitsani, kukuzunzani, ndi kuwononga ambiri a inu; ndipo zidzachitika kuti mudzazunguliridwa ndi zoopsa ndi chiwonongeko. Uyo amene sanatengedwe ukapolo ku kuzunzika ndi imfa yotsatira adzagwa ndi lupanga; uyo amene sagwa ndi lupanga adzakumana ndi zilombo zakuthengo, zomwe zidzapha ambiri; uyo amene sawonongeka ndi zilombo zakuthengo adzafa ndi njala, ndipo njira za imfa zidzachulukitsidwa m'dzikomo.

Chisautso chachikulu, tsoka, chiwonongeko chidzakugwerani nthawi yomweyo, ndipo mafunde amphamvu a nyanja adzabwera pa mtundu wanu. Simudzakhala mtundu wamphamvu kachiwiri, komabe otsala adzapulumutsidwa. Yense amene aitanira pa dzina la Ambuye adzapulumutsidwa, koma uyo amene akonda moyo wake mpaka mapeto adzakhala ndi gawo lake m'nyanja yamoto, ndipo mudzagawana kuzunzika ndi chilango cha Satana chifukwa mwagwiritsitsa dziko lake lomwe likufa mpaka mpweya wanu womaliza wamakani, ndipo mudzawonongeka ndithu ndi dziko lomwe likufali. Bwererani kwa ine pamene kuli nthawi kuti ndikupulumutseni inu ndi mabanja anu ku tsoka lomwe likubwera. Monga momwe khamu lalikulu lidalandira chipulumutso pa tsiku la Pentekoste zaka zambiri zapitazo mu mphindi zochepa zoyambirira za m'badwo uno, momwemonso khamu lalikulu lidzapulumutsidwa mu mphindi zochepa zomaliza za m'badwo uno, ndipo kuitana kwanga kudakalipo kwa nthawi yochepa chabe.

Choipa chomwe chikubwera sichidzalemekeza amayi kapena ana kapena okalamba kapena odwala. Ayi, choipacho chidzawononga zonse zomwe chingathe mu chiwonongeko chachikulu ndi ululu. Thawirani kwa ine pamene kuli nthawi, popeza wotchi yatsala pang'ono kuyima, ndipo sipadzakhala nthawi inanso. Satana adzaponyedwa pa dziko lapansi posachedwa, ndipo ufumu wake udzamasulidwa mokwanira pa dziko lanu, ndipo ali ndi mkwiyo waukulu chifukwa akudziwa kuti ali ndi nthawi yochepa. Bwererani kwa ine pamene mukanatha kuti muthawe choipa chomwe chikubwera.

Ine ndine Ambuye wa Ambuye ndi Mfumu ya Mafumu.

Ine ndine amene ndikuyenda pakati pa zoyikapo nyali zisanu ndi ziwiri ndikulamulira angelo asanu ndi awiri a mipingo isanu ndi iwiri.

Ine ndine amene ndikulamulira magulu ankhondo akumwamba, monga Atate wanga waika ulamuliro wawo m'manja mwanga.

Ine ndine Mawu osanduka thupi amene ndinakhala pakati panu.

Ine ndine muzu wa Jese, ndipo ufumu wanga ulibe mapeto.

Ine ndine mwana wa Mulungu wobwera m'thupi kudzapereka nsembe yotetezera machimo a onse, ngati onse angavomereze.

Omwe alandira chisomo changa ndipo akhala okhulupirika adzalamulira nane mu ufumu wanga ndikugawana mpando wanga wachifumu, monga ndakupangani kukhala mafumu ndi ansembe pamaso pa Mulungu wathu. Pali mphoto yaikulu yosungidwa kwa inu kumwamba; ingoimani chilili ndi okhulupirika kwa mphindi zochepa chabe.
