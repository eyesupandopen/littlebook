Epulo 24, 2024

Imvani Mawu a Ambuye: Chinsinsi Chachikulu Chavumbulutsidwa!

Yeshua HaMashiach akuti kwa Mkwatibwi wake: Samalani ndipo imvani mawu anga, osankhidwa anga, ndipo mvetsetsani kulankhula kwanga, pakuti chinsinsi cha Umulungu ndi chachikulu! Ambiri a inu mulibe lingaliro la chinsinsi chachikulu cha Mulungu, komabe chili pamtima ndi pamaziko kwa otsatira a Njira, mu chiphunzitso ndi mu machitidwe: Ndiwo maziko a Chikhristu momwe ndakulengerani mwa ine, ndipo ndinu cholengedwa chatsopano mwa Khristu Yesu, pakuti Ine, Yesu Khristu, ndakupangani inu choncho.

Kwa ambiri a inu, chinsinsi chachikulu cha Mulungu chatayika mu nthawi ndi miyambo ndi kuchenjera komwe amabisalira kuti anyenge. Musaope, ana anga, ndipo imvani mawu anga ndi kumva kulankhula kwanga, pakuti ndikufotokozerani pano mu mphindi zomaliza za nthawi ino. Mvetsetsani, ana anga, kuti chisokonezo chambiri ndi mabodza ambiri abweretsedwa mu mpingo wanga ndi mimbulu yolusa ndi ziphunzitso zawo zonyenga. Iwo agawa molakwika Mawu anga (ndi Thupi) kudzera mu chiphunzitso cha magawo a nthawi (dispensationalism), ndipo ili ndi bodza lalikulu lobweretsedwa ndi omwe amanga nyumba zawo pamchenga, osati pa ine, mwala waukulu wapangodya, pakuti popanda ine palibe munthu angamange.

Mchenga womwe amasankha kukhala mwala wapangodya wa nyumba zawo ndi Mabaibulo oipitsidwa omwe amatsogolera ku umodzi wa zipembedzo pazolinga zomwe munthu wauchimo ali nazo mumtima mwake, zolinga zolamulira ndi imfa ndi chiwonongeko. Mchenga womwe anagwiritsa ntchito pomanga umapangidwa ndi malemba oipitsidwa, malemba achigiriki omwe aperekedwa kuchokera ku Alexandria. Tsatirani malemba ochokera ku mizere ya Antiokeya, Byzantine, ndipo awa amatsogolera ku Textus Receptus, mdani wamkulu wa Roma. Palibe magawo a nthawi, ndipo Mulungu sagawidwa, ngakhale ndidzafotokoza za kugawa monga momwe kumapezekera m'makalata anga. Mulungu wachita ndi anthu pogwiritsa ntchito mibadwo; phunzirani kuti mudziwe. Choncho mvetsetsani, magawo awa sakhala ochokera kwa ine koma anapangidwa kuti aipitse Mpingo wanga kuyambira pachiyambi.

Chinsinsi chachikulu chabisidwa kwa inu, ana anga, koma osati kubisidwa kotheratu, pakuti ndinachivumbulutsa kwa Mtumwi Paulo zaka za Yubile zambiri zapitazo. Chabisidwa kudzera m'mabodza, chinyengo, ziphunzitso zolakwika zomwe sizochokera kwa ine; koma chabisidwa poonekera, ndipo mbala imafuna kuti musachizindikire, koma ndi chinthu chachikulu chomwe aliyense akuchinyalanyaza. Pakuti chomwe chimamuopseza Satana kwambiri za Mpingo wanga ndichoti akudziwa kuti alibe mphamvu zokulamulirani pamene mukuyenda mu mphamvu yanga yonse ndi ubale woonekera ndi ine. Choncho, popeza sangathe kuswa chidindo cha Mulungu chomwe ndayika pa inu, ndipo palibe amene angakuchotseni m'manja mwanga kapena m'manja mwa Atate anga, m'malo mwake angayese kukutsamwitsani mu umphawi. Osati umphawi wa zinthu zakuthupi; Mpingo wanga sumayenera kulimbikitsa uthenga wabwino wachitukuko wolakwika chifukwa si wochokera kwa ine, ngakhale ambiri a inu mumakhulupirira mabodza amenewo. Satana amafuna umphawi wauzimu kwa inu kuti athe kukubisirani umunthu wanu weniweni ndi kukukopani kuti mukhulupirire kuti ndinu opanda mphamvu, oikidwiratu kuti nthawi zonse muzipereka tsaya lina ndi kuthawa ngati wamantha, zomwe ndi kugwiritsa ntchito molakwika malangizo anga komwe kumagwiritsidwa ntchito kulepheretsa ana anga kuyenda mu mphamvu yawo yeniyeni. Ayi, sindinakutumizeni kuti mupereke tsaya lina kuti muzunzidwe ndi kugonjetsedwa ndi choipa; ndinakutumizani kuti mugonjetse choipa ndi chabwino ndi kugwetsa ntchito za Mdyerekezi, pakuti momwemo ndinatumizidwa, ndipo momwemo ndakutumizirani. Koma mvetsetsani: kubwezera ndi kwanga, choncho musadzibwezere nokha, koma perekani tsaya lina pamene kuli koyenera kuti wina apulumutsidwe ndi kupereka malo ku mkwiyo, pakuti miyoyo yambiri yapulumutsidwa mwanjira iyi.

Mvetsetsani: akunyengeni ndi mabodza awo, ndipo ayika mabodza awa omwe amatsutsana ndi choonadi changa pafupi ndi malemba oyera. Anachita izi m'mbali mwa Mabaibulo ophunzirira; anachita izi pogwiritsa ntchito ndemanga zolakwika; anachita izi mwachinyengo posintha matanthauzo ena achigiriki omwe amachokera ku chikhalidwe chakale chachikunja osati choonadi cha Mawu Oyera a Mulungu. Mutha kugwiritsa ntchito magwero awa nthawi zina, koma samalani kwambiri ndipo siyani kulola munthu wamthupi kuyesa kukufotokozerani zinthu zauzimu; pakuti munthu wamthupi sangathe kuzindikira zinthu zauzimu, pakuti ndi zopusa kwa iye. Choncho, dziwani kuti ambiri mwa achinyengo awa omwe amapotoza malemba alibe chikhulupiriro m'Mawu a Mulungu ndipo sakuyenerera ntchito yomwe anadzipatsa okha chifukwa alibe mzimu wa Khristu ndipo si ochokera kwa ine; pakuti ngati akanakhala ochokera kwa ine, ndikanawatsogolera m'choonadi. Ndine amene ndakudindani chidindo cha chiwombolo chokwanira mu mphamvu ya mzimu wanga woyera.

Ambuye Yesu Khristu akuti kwa Mkwatibwi wake wokondedwa: Ndikufuna mumvetsetse cholinga chanu chenicheni, ndipo ngati simumvetsetsa chinsinsi chachikulu monga momwe chikukhudzira inu, ndiye kuti simudzayenda mu chidzalo cha mphamvu yomwe ndakuitanirani kuti muyendemo, koma mudzakhala ofooka m'kuyesetsa kwanu monga momwe ambiri a inu mwakhalira. Zimandipweteka ndi kundimvetsa chisoni mumtima mwanga kuona kuti ana anga samamvetsetsa umunthu wawo weniweni ndipo alibe chikhumbo chofufuza. Nthawi zonse muyenera kufufuza mawu anga ndi khama lonse; koma ziphunzitso zonyenga zimapangitsa ambiri kukhala okhutira, pakuti mulibe chakudya mwa izo. Pakuti ndi mabodza a Satana, koma ambiri amakhala ndi moyo pa zakudya zopanda thanzi ndi zakumwa zotsekemera, ndipo ambiri alibe mphamvu zodya chakudya chomwe ndapereka ndi kumwa chakumwa chomwe ndapereka, pakuti pochita izi simudzamvanso njala kapena ludzu.

Choncho imvani mawu anga, Mkwatibwi wanga wokhazikika; ndi nthawi yoti mudzuke ku maitanidwe anu enieni ndikuchita ntchito zomwe ndakukonzerani, ndipo dziwani kuti ndidzachita zozizwitsa zambiri ndi manja anu pamene mukundiyendera m'chikhulupiriro ndi chidziwitso chenicheni. Pakuti ndinati chiyani mu utumiki wanga kwa Isiraeli kalekale pamene ndinkayenda poonekera ku Yudeya? Pakuti nkhani ya Luka pa nkhaniyi ndi yotani: Ndipo anati kwa iwo, Kwa inu, kwapatsidwa kudziwa chinsinsi cha ufumu wa Mulungu: koma kwa iwo omwe ali kunja, zinthu zonsezi zimachitika m'mafanizo. Choncho mvetsetsani, atumwi anga anasankhidwa kuti alandire chinsinsi chachikulu cha Mulungu chomwe ndinawavumbulutsira. Anachilemba, ndipo ndateteza malembawo kuti mudziwe choonadi cha nkhaniyi.

Ambiri a inu mumamvera Ayuda omwe angafune kukubwezerani pansi pa Chilamulo (Torah). Ndi zosangalatsa kudziwa kuti ngati sikunali chifukwa cha chinsinsi chachikulu cha Mulungu, ambiri a inu simukadadziwa za Chilamulo; pakuti sichinaperekedwe kwa mitundu ya anthu akunja koma kwa Isiraeli wakale kuti asunge, kuti chikhale mphunzitsi wawo kuwabweretsa kwa ine. Pakuti ndine kukwaniritsidwa kwa chilamulo, ndipo omwe amamatira mwakhama ku chilamulo cha Mose amafuna kulemekeza thupi lawo ndi kuyenda m'chilungamo chawo, ndipo alibe kumvetsetsa kuti Chilamulo chimasungidwa mokwanira ndi m'malembo kudzera mukudziwika kwanu ndi ine. Pakuti ndine ndekha amene ndinatha kusunga chilamulo, ndipo Ine, Yesu wa ku Nazarete ndakwaniritsa zofunikira zolungama za chilamulo. Ndinatsimikizira zomwe ndakwaniritsa m'magazi anga, ndipo ndinakusambitsani m'madzi oyenda a mzimu wanga pamene mwabatizidwa mwa ine.

Pakuti ndinamuuza Paulo kuti auze chiyani Akorinto? Pakuti ndi Mzimu mmodzi ife tonse tinabatizidwa m'thupi limodzi, kaya tikhale Ayuda kapena Amitundu, kaya tikhale akapolo kapena aufulu; ndipo tonse tamwetsedwa mu Mzimu mmodzi. Ngati mupita kukasunga chilamulo, mudzalephera ndipo mudzaphonya maitanidwe anu, popeza ndafupikitsa chilamulo kukhala malamulo awiri akulu: Ndipo uzikonda Ambuye Mulungu wako ndi mtima wako wonse, ndi moyo wako wonse, ndi nzeru zako zonse, ndi mphamvu zako zonse: ili ndi lamulo loyamba. Ndipo lachiwiri lifanana nalo, lakuti, Uzikonda mnzako ngati udzikonda wekha. Dziwani, ana anga, kuti palibe malamulo akulu kuposa awa, pakuti mwa awa malamulo amasungidwa.

Mvetsetsani: Ndikufotokoza zonsezi m'makalata omwe ndinatumiza ku mipingo yanga nthawi zakale. Choncho mvetsetsani kuti ndavumbulutsa chinsinsi chachikulu cha Mulungu, ndipo aneneri akale anafufuza mwakhama ndi kufunsa za chinsinsi ichi chomwe anachidziwa, koma sichinaperekedwe kwa iwo kuti achidziwe. Ndikukhumba mukanamvetsetsa kuti ngakhale chikuto cha Mabaibulo anu chimati “Woyera,” sichoyera paubwino wake, pakuti ndi chikuto chabe cha bukhu. Chomwe chimapangitsa Baibulo kukhala loyera ndichoti mabukhu omwe amamanga ali ndi Malemba Oyera otumizidwa ndi mzimu kwa ana a Wam'mwambamwamba, otumizidwa ndi Yahweh, Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya, pakuti palibe wina wofanana naye. Choncho imvani mawu anga oyera ndi moyo wanu wonse! Pakuti Petulo ananena chiyani kwa inu, ana anga? Chipulumutso chimene Aneneri anachifunsa, ndi kuchifufuza mwakhama, omwe ananenera za chisomo chomwe chikadabwera kwa inu, kufufuza kuti, kapena ndi nthawi yotani yomwe Mzimu wa Khristu womwe unali mwa iwo unasonyeza, pamene unachitira umboni pasadakhale za masautso a Khristu, ndi ulemerero womwe ukadatsatira.

Ana anga, mukukhala mosazindikira malipiro oyamba a ulemerero womwe uyenera kutsatira, ndipo cholowa chanu chonse chatsala pang'ono kuvumbulutsidwa kwa inu mwa ine, Khristu Yesu, pakuti posachedwa mudzakwatulidwa ndi kupatsidwa ulemerero wonse. Ndiye chinsinsi ichi ndi chiyani? Ndipo mumachipeza kuti? Ndikuuzani, pakuti ndine Ambuye Yesu Khristu, amene amaweta gulu la nkhosa, ndipo ndine Mkwati kwa Mkwatibwi wanga, ndipo mukukhala chinsinsi chachikulu cha Mulungu, ngakhale ambiri a inu mukuyenda mumdima pa nkhani iyi. Musaope, ndikupatsani kumvetsetsa, ndipo kwa anzeru ndi odzichepetsa ndidzapereka nzeru. Koma kwa omwe pakati panu adzanyalanyaza mawu anga, mukutero potsutsana ndi ine; ndipo muli mbali yanga kapena mukunditsutsa, palibe chapakati. Pakuti ndikukulangizani nonse kuti muphunzire kwa ine, imvani mawu anga ndipo musanyalanyaze mawu anga, pakuti ndakupatsani tsogolo ndi cholinga mwa ine. Ndine Mbusa wabwino: Mbusa wabwino amapereka moyo wake chifukwa cha nkhosa, ndipo nkhosa zanga zimamva mawu anga, choncho siyani kutsatira aganyu omwe amadziyesa ine!

Taonani! Mzimu ukuti: Pali zinsinsi zingapo mkati mwa Chinsinsi Chachikulu cha Mulungu! Ndiyamba ndi Chinsinsi Chachikulu, kenako ndidzalankhula za zinsinsi zina mmenemo. Tisanapite patsogolo, pukutani chifunga cha chiphunzitso cha magawo a nthawi m'magalasi anu ndipo tsegulani maso anu kuti muone: Mauthenga Abwino monga momwe mumawadziwira, ntchito za Mateyu, Maliko, Luka, ndi Yohane, ndi zofunika kwa onse. Mabukhu awa amasonyeza kukwaniritsa kwanga chilamulo m'malembo, popanda chosiyidwa. Amandivumbulutsa pakubwera kwanga kwa Isiraeli. Pomvera mauthenga abwino anayi monga momwe amadziwikira, mukuphunzira kuyenda monga momwe ndinayendera, pakuti mwalamulidwa kutero. Komabe, si mauthenga abwino okhawo; ayi, si uthenga wabwino wokhawo wonena za ine.

Pakuti ndinapereka uthenga wabwino wa Chinsinsi Chachikulu cha Mulungu kwa Mtumwi Paulo m'makalata ake kwa mpingo, otumizidwa kuchokera kwa ine kupita kwa iye kuti akuvumbulutsireni. Kodi mudzanyoza lingaliro ili? Kodi mukuganiza kuti ndikulalikira uthenga wabwino wina ndipo kuti ndine mawu onyenga? Kodi mwazolowera kutsatira mawu a aganyu mpaka simukudziwanso mawu anga? Kwa omwe anganyoze, muyenera kumvera mawu a Agalatiya pamene Paulo anati: Koma ndikudziwitsani, abale, kuti Uthenga Wabwino womwe unalalikiridwa ndi ine, si wa munthu. Pakuti sindinaulandire kwa munthu, kapena kuphunzitsidwa, koma mwa vumbulutso la Yesu Khristu. Paulo analankhula mokhulupirika choonadi chomwe ndinamupatsa, komabe ambiri a inu mumanyalanyaza mawu omwe ndinamupatsa, ngakhale kuchepetsa mawuwo ndi zonena monga: O, ndi zomwe Paulo analemba chabe, kapena, izo ndi makalata a Paulo chabe; si mawu a Khristu chifukwa sali ofiira! Wopusa iwe! Walakwa; tsegula maso ako ku kuwala kwanga ndi choonadi! Ndinapatsa Paulo uthenga wabwino wa chinsinsi. Kodi ndinu opusa kwambiri kukhulupirira kuti pali ntchito zinayi zokha zazifupi zomwe zili ndi Uthenga Wabwino wanga?

Chonde imvani mawu a Khristu Yesu, ana anga, pakuti ndidzaphunzitsa ofunitsitsa. Pakuti chinsinsi cha Umulungu ndi chachikulu ndi uthenga wabwino mmenemo, pakuti ndinalengeza uthenga wabwino wa chinsinsi. Koma sindidzakusiyani ndi mawu a mboni imodzi yokha; mawu onse akhazikitsidwe pakamwa pa mboni ziwiri kapena zitatu. Pakuti kalata yomwe ndinatumiza kwa Aroma ikufuna kulankhula nanu: Tsopano kwa iye amene ali ndi mphamvu yokukhazikitsani inu malinga ndi uthenga wabwino wanga, ndi kulalikira kwa Yesu Khristu, malinga ndi vumbulutso la chinsinsi, chomwe chinasungidwa mwachinsinsi chiyambire dziko lapansi. Uthenga wabwino wa Paulo ndi vumbulutso la chinsinsi chosungidwa mwachinsinsi chiyambire dziko lapansi, monga momwe Mateyu, Maliko, Luka, ndi Yohane ali uthenga wabwino wa kubwera kwanga koyamba kwa Isiraeli.

Ndine Ambuye Wokhulupirika ndipo mawu anga ndi oona, choncho ndibwerekeni khutu lanu ndipo makamaka mtima wanu, pakuti ndi mtima munthu amakhulupirira mpaka chilungamo. Ndikukupemphani kuti mukhale ndi chikhulupiriro cha njere ya mpiru chabe, pakuti njereyo idzakula mwamphamvu mwa inu ndi chisamaliro choyenera. Pakuti Paulo ananena chiyani kwa Akorinto: Koma timalankhula nzeru ya Mulungu m'chinsinsi, ngakhale nzeru yobisika yomwe Mulungu anaikonzeratu lisanakhale dziko lapansi, ku ulemerero wathu. Yomwe palibe mmodzi wa mafumu a dziko lino anaizindikira: pakuti akadaidziwa, sakadapachika Ambuye wa ulemerero. Zalembedwa momveka bwino, ana anga, ndipo sizifuna kufotokozedwa, koma ndifotokoza pang'ono.

Kodi mukumvetsetsa mawu awa, ana anga? Kodi mukumvetsetsa momwe muliri amtengo wapatali kuti Mulungu wakhala akukuyang'anani kuyambira lisanengedwe dziko lapansi, ndipo popeza iye ndi wamuyaya, inunso muli choncho, pakuti mwakhala patsogolo pa malingaliro ake kuyambira nthawi isanakhaleko, ndipo wakupatsani moyo wosatha mwa ine, Khristu Yesu? Nthawi zanu ndi miyoyo yanu zakhazikitsidwa ndi Yahweh, Mulungu wamkulu ndi wodabwitsa, yemwe kuwala kwake palibe thupi lingathe kukuona. Satana akadadziwa mapulani a Atate, sakadandipachika, pakuti mukumvera kwanga mpaka imfa ndi m'kuuka kwanga munakwaniritsidwa mphamvu yomwe inamugonjetsa ndipo wawonongeka kotheratu. Sakanatha kufuna mpingo wamphamvu womwe wapatsidwa mphamvu ndi mzimu wanga ndipo womwe ndimachita ntchito zamphamvu ndi manja awo.

Kodi zimenezo zikugwirizana ndi inu? Siyani kudandaula kwanu m'maganizo chifukwa amithenga anga akukuuzani zomwe simukufuna kumva, ndipo gonjetsani kunyada kwanu, pakuti ndidzatsogolera odzichepetsa okha ndipo ndidzatsutsana ndi onyada. Imvani mawu anga ndipo aloleni alowe pansi pa miyoyo yanu; pakuti ndakulengerani mwa ine, ndipo ndinu amphamvu, ndipo ndinu ozizwitsa; pakuti mwayeretsedwa m'magazi anga, ngakhale magazi a mwanawankhosa. Pakuti ndine mutu wa Thupi langa yemwe amachita ntchito zambiri zamphamvu kwa anthu anga omwe ndikuwaitanabe, gwirisisani Ambuye wanu, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndi mtima umodzi ndi umodzi wa cholinga, pakuti ndine Mbuye wa Ambuye.

Mboni yokhulupirika, ndi wobadwa woyamba kuchokera kwa akufa akuti kwa okhulupirika ndi osankhidwa ake! Kunali mpingo wakale ku Efeso, ndipo kwa iwo kunavumbulutsidwa chidzalo cha Chinsinsi Chachikulu cha Mulungu m'kalata yotumizidwa ndi Paulo kuchokera kwa ine, pakuti sanalankhule za iye mwini. Ndifotokoza pang'ono, koma dziwani kuti ndikuyembekezera kumvera kwanu m'chikhulupiriro kwa ine, Yesu wa ku Nazarete, ndipo kwa amene wapatsidwa zambiri kumayembekezeredwa zambiri. Ndisanapitirize, ndikufuna muganizire zotsatirazi kuchokera ku Kolose, pakuti ndinatumiza kalata kwa Akolose komanso: Pakuti mwafa, ndipo moyo wanu wabisika ndi Khristu mwa Mulungu. Pamene Khristu, yemwe ali moyo wathu, adzaonekera, pamenepo inunso mudzaonekera naye mu ulemerero.

Ana anga, munafa pamene ndinafa, ndipo mumafa kwa inu nokha tsiku ndi tsiku poyesa chikhalidwe cha munthu wakale kukhala chakufa; pakuti munapachikidwa pamodzi ndi ine, ndipo palibe malo ena m'malemba momwe mudzapeza galasi la umunthu wanu weniweni, pakuti palibe gawo lina la mauthenga abwino lomwe limavumbulutsa kupatula uthenga wabwino wa Chinsinsi Chachikulu chomwe ndinavumbulutsa kwa Mpingo wanga. Ngakhale mukuona kudzera mugalasi modetsa, izo zatsala pang'ono kusintha, ndipo mudzadziwa monga momwe inunso mukudziwikira. Dzimizeni nokha mu Uthenga Wabwino wa Chinsinsi Chachikulu cha Mulungu, pakuti mpingo wanga wa Laodikaya wataya zinsinsi zanga, pakuti ambiri a inu mwa apo ndi apo mumawerenga makalata anga kwa Mpingo chifukwa abusa osokera anakutsogolerani kwina kufunafuna mawu ofiira. Ana anga, ndikudziwa kuti mumandikonda, choncho ndikondeni malinga ndi chidziwitso, ndipo musayende m'chinyengo.

Choncho ndikukufunsani, kalata yopita kwa Aefeso imati chiyani? Pakuti taonani, chinsinsi chachikulu chili pamenepo! Atatiwululira chinsinsi cha chifuniro chake, malinga ndi kukondwera kwake, komwe anatsimikiza mwa iye yekha, Kuti mu kugawa kwa chidzalo cha nthawi, asonkhanitse pamodzi zinthu zonse mwa Khristu, zomwe zili kumwamba, ndi zomwe zili padziko lapansi, ngakhale mwa iye. Ndakudziwitsani chinsinsi cha chifuniro cha Yahweh, chomwe anachibisa mwa iye yekha, pakuti safuna phungu. Musaganize za tanthauzo lonyenga lomwe munaphunzira kwa aneneri onyenga m'zolemba zawo zopanda phindu zam'mbali zokhudza magawo a nthawi, pakuti Satana amatanthauziranso mwachinyengo malingaliro a malemba kuti agwirizane ndi chifuniro chake ndi mapulani omwe amakhala opanda pake.

Chinsinsi ichi chinaperekedwa kwa Paulo, ndipo anali mdindo wopatsidwa ntchito yochigawa kwa Mpingo wanga, ngakhale kwa inu ngati muli ndi makutu akumva ndi maso akuona. Pakuti uthenga wabwino wa Chinsinsi Chachikulu unaperekedwa kwa Paulo, ndipo ndinamutuma panjira yopita ku Damasiko. Mu chidzalo cha nthawi, Atate adzasonkhanitsa pamodzi zinthu zonse zomwe zili kumwamba ndi zomwe zili padziko lapansi mwa ine, Khristu Yesu. Sipadzakhalanso kugawikana, sipadzakhalanso kulekanitsidwa pakati pa Mulungu ndi ana ake, sipadzakhalanso udani pakati pa Myuda ndi Wamitundu, pakuti nonse mwabadwa mwatsopano ndipo ndinu cholengedwa chatsopano mwa ine, Khristu Yesu. Kodi mawu awa ndi odabwitsa kwambiri kwa inu? Kodi mawu anga amakupangitsani kuwatsutsa chifukwa mumakhulupirira kumasulira kwa abusa osokera kwa iwo?

Phunzirani kwa ine modzichepetsa kuti mumvetse. Ndiponso, mu Aefeso Paulo analemba mawu amene ndinamupatsa: Chifukwa cha ichi ine Paulo, wam'ndende wa Yesu Khristu chifukwa cha inu anthu amitundu, Ngati mwamva za makonzedwe a chisomo cha Mulungu, chimene chinapatsidwa kwa ine chifukwa cha inu: Kuti mwa vumbulutso anandiziwitsa chinsinsi. Ndiponso, musapunthwe ndi mawu a aneneri onama, popeza makonzedwe a chisomo cha Mulungu anali utumiki wake ku Mpingo, pakuti iye (ndi ena) anasankhidwa kukhala adindo a zinsinsi za Mulungu.

Ndiponso: Chimene m'mibadwo ina sichinadziwitsidwe kwa ana a anthu, monga momwe chavumbulutsidwira tsopano kwa Atumwi ake oyera ndi Aneneri mwa Mzimu, Kuti anthu amitundu akhale olowa nyumba anzathu, ndi a thupi limodzi, ndi olandira nawo lonjezo lake mwa Khristu, mwa Uthenga Wabwino. Inde, werengani molondola ndipo werenganinso: anthu amitundu amene akhulupirira m'dzina langa loyera, lomwe ndi Yesu Khristu, ndi olowa nyumba anzathu ndipo ndi a thupi limodzi ndi a Isiraeli amene andipanga ine kukhala Ambuye, pakuti mwa ine mulibe kusiyana.

Ndiponso: Ngakhale chinsinsi chimene chinabisika kuyambira nthawi zakale, ndi m'mibadwo, koma tsopano chaonetsedwa kwa oyera mtima ake, Amene Mulungu anafuna kuwadziwitsa chuma cha ulemerero wa chinsinsi ichi pakati pa anthu amitundu, chomwe ndi Khristu mwa inu, chiyembekezo cha ulemerero. Inde, mwabadwa mwatsopano mwa mzimu wanga, ndipo mzimu wanga umakhala mwa ana anga kosatha, pakuti ndi mzimu wa umwana, chikole cha cholowa chanu, ndipo mzimu umenewo uli ndi maluso onse amene ndakupatsani, pakuti ndapereka mphatso kwa anthu. Mwapangidwa m'chifanizo changa, ndipo mwalengedwa mwatsopano mwa ine; pakuti Atate akubalani mwa ine, pakuti ndine mzimu wopatsa moyo.

Khristu Yesu wokwezedwa akuti: Tiana tanga, tengerani chitonthozo chachikulu mwa ine, pakuti chipulumutso changa, chikapezeka mwa kundipanga ine Ambuye wanu m'chikhulupiriro ndi kulapa, sichingatayike kapena kusinthidwa; pakuti ndani wa inu angasinthe chilengedwe cha makhalidwe anu mwa ana anu, ngakhale angataye cholowa ngati mutawakana. Chipulumutso ndi mphatso yoperekedwa ndi Atate wanga kudzera mwa ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndipo mphatso ndi maitanidwe a Mulungu zilibe chisoni. Tsopano, ndikupatsani zizindikiro zoti mutsatire paulendo wanu wopita kuzinsinsi zomwe zili mu Chinsinsi chachikulu cha Mulungu, choncho phunzirani kuti mudzionetse ovomerezeka kwa Mulungu, wantchito wa Mawu, wogawa bwino mawu a choonadi, kuti musachite manyazi pamaso panga pakubwera kwanga.

Pali chinsinsi mu Aroma, poganizira khungu la Isiraeli mwa mbali ina. Pali chinsinsi cha kusonkhanitsa mkwatibwi wanga, chomwe ena amachitcha kukwatulidwa, ndipo chimenecho chili mu Akorinto (ndi kwina); pakuti m'kamphindi, m'kuthethima kwa diso, lipenga lomaliza lidzalira, ndipo kulira kwake kuli pafupi ndi inu. Kenako pali chinsinsi cha mwamuna ndi mkazi kukhala thupi limodzi, ndipo ichi ndi chinsinsi chachikulu: koma ndilankhula za Khristu ndi mpingo, ndipo mungapeze zimenezo mu Aefeso. Pakuti ndidzachotsa khungu kwa Isiraeli pamene atembenuza mitima yawo kwa ine. Pakuti ndidzasonkhanitsa Mkwatibwi wanga kwa ine, pakuti ndine Mkwati. Ndine amene mudzakwatiwa naye, kuti tikhale amodzi kosatha mwa ine ndi mwa Atate wanga, pakuti Mulungu ndi zonse mu zonse. Mtima wanga uli pa inu, ana anga, ndipo posachedwa mudzandiona maso ndi maso; ndipo musalakwitse, ndikukuitanirani kwa ine, anamwali anga anzeru, ndipo ndikubwera kudzakutengani posachedwapa, ndipo ndikulakalaka kukuonani nonse kumene tidzakhala maso ndi maso, ndipo mudzadziwa, monga momwe inu mukudziwidwira.

Ndine amene ndakusindikizani chizindikiro cha chiombolo chokwanira mu mphamvu ya mzimu wanga woyera. Ndine kukwaniritsidwa kwa chilamulo.
