Kwa Atumiki Anga Okhulupirika: Ndikukutumizani!

Kwa atumiki anga okhulupirika ndi kwa iwo omwe asiya Laodikaya!

Mkwatibwi wanga, imva mawu anga ndipo bwera kwa ine, kuti ndithe kutonthoza mabala ako ndi kukupatsa mphamvu mu ulemerero wanga. Usaope! Mkwiyo wanga suli pa iwe, pakuti ndidzati kwa iwe: Wachita bwino, kapolo wabwino ndi wokhulupirika; wakhala wokhulupirika pa zinthu zochepa, ndidzakukhazika kuti ulamulire zinthu zambiri: lowa m'chimwemwe cha mbuye wako. Pakuti ndili ndi mawu okhwima, mawu a chilango, kwa osamvera; koma chilango chimenecho sichikulunjika kwa iwe chifukwa ndiwe wokhulupirika, ngakhale uyenera kusamalira mawu anga, kuti ungagwe.

Mkwatibwi wanga wokhulupirika, ndiwe mwana wa diso langa, ndipo palibe kutsutsidwa kwa iwe, pakuti uli mwa Khristu, ndipo wamanga moyo wako pa ine, Yesu Khristu, ngakhale mwala wapangodya womwe omanga onyenga awukana. Pitiriza kukhala wokhulupirika mwa ine, pakuti ndikubwera kwa iwe mu mphamvu yayikulu, pakuti kutha kwa nthawi kuli pafupi. Ndikukutumiza mu mphamvu yayikulu ndi m'ntchito zomwe sunaziganizirepo wekha, pakuti sunadziwe kuti unabadwira mu chinsinsi cha Mulungu pa mpweya wako woyamba, koma wakula mwamphamvu m'chisomo changa, ndipo tsopano ukuwona. Pakuti chisomo changa ndi chisomo champhamvu chomwe sichingagonjetsedwe, ndipo chifundo changa n'chosatha mpaka muyaya.

Taona! atero Yeshua HaMashiach. Ndimvere ndipo lemba mawu anga pa magome a mnofu a mitima yanu. Pakuti ndikukutumizani; inde, ndikukutumizani ndi mawu awa, ndipo omwe ndiwatumiza adzalandira chitsimikizo chosatsutsika cha chifuniro changa champhamvu. Ndikukutumizani m'ntchito za akulu anga, ntchito zomwe anayamba zaka zambiri zapitazo, ndipo ntchitozo zikupitirirabe mpaka lero. Pakuti buku la Machitidwe silinamalizidwe kulembedwa mpaka Mkwatibwi wanga ali nane. Pamenepo mudzamvetsa chidzalo chochokera m'mabuku a kumwamba, pakuti ntchito zanu zolembedwa mmenemo, ndipo tonse tidzakondwera chifukwa cha kukhulupirika kwanu pamaso pa Yahweh mwiniwake, pakuti amakondwera nafe ndipo ali mwa inu nonse.

Taona! Ndikutumiza ena a inu mu utumiki wa Paulo monga adindo a zinsinsi zanga ndi monga atumwi kwa mitundu. Taona! Ndikutumiza ena a inu mu mzimu wa Eliya kuti muyime motsutsana ndi Yezebeli, mneneri wamkazi wonyenga, ndi kunenera ine, Khristu Yesu! Taona! Ndikutumiza ena a inu mu ntchito ya Filipi mlaliki, komwe ndidzakutengerani kumalo ndi malo ndi mawu a uthenga wanga wabwino pamilomo yanu, ogwirizana ndi zosowa za osapulumutsidwa, pakuti mudzawapulumutsa polalikira mtanda! Taona! Ndikutumiza ena a inu mu ntchito ya Petro, pakuti anaweta gulu langa ndi kuwadyetsa mawu oyera a Mulungu. Pakuti atumiki aulesi a Laodikaya amadyetsa gulu langa chakudya chopanda thanzi, ngakhale mawu opanda chikhulupirika opanda mphamvu. Taona! Ndakupangani kukhala aphunzitsi kuti muphunzitse ndi kulangiza anthu anga m'njira za chilungamo, pakuti mudzawaphunzitsa monga ndikutsogolerani! Pakuti mudzawaweta monga ndikutsogolerani! Pakuti mudzawalalikira monga ndikutsogolerani! Pakuti aneneri anga adzalankhula mawu omwe ndiwapatsa, osasinthidwa tanthauzo ndi mtima! Pakuti atumwi anga adzaunikira kuwala kwa uthenga wanga wabwino waulemerero wa chinsinsi chachikulu cha Mulungu, kuti chophimba cha Mose chichotsedwe m'mitima yanu. Pakuti mboni ya ku Korinto ikufuula mokweza! Koma ngakhale mpaka lero, pamene Mose awerengedwa, chophimba chili pa mtima wawo. Komabe, pamene chidzatembenukira kwa Ambuye, chophimba chidzachotsedwa. Tsopano Ambuye ndiye mzimuwo, ndipo pamene Mzimu wa Ambuye uli, pali ufulu. Koma ife tonse, ndi nkhope yosaphimbidwa poyang'ana monga m'galasi ulemerero wa Ambuye, tikusinthidwa kukhala chifaniziro chomwecho, kuchokera ku ulemerero kupita ku ulemerero, ngakhale ndi mzimu wa Ambuye. Ine, Khristu Yesu, ndikuchotsa chophimba chanu!

Taona, ndikukutumizani mu mphindi zomaliza izi musanasonkhanitsidwe kwa ine pa kuonekera kwanga. Ndikufuna kuti mumve mawu anga ndi kutsatira malangizo anga, monga momwe mumachitira mokhulupirika, Mkwatibwi wanga wokhulupirika, pakuti ndinu okhulupirika! Musapite mu ntchito yomwe ndikukutumizani mu kumvetsa kwanu. Musapite mu ntchito yomwe ndikukutumizani mu mphamvu yanu. Musapite mu ntchito yomwe ndikukutumizani mu luso lanu. Musapite mu ntchito yomwe ndikukutumizani mu nyonga yanu. Imvani mawu anga ndi mtima wanu wonse! Pakuti Ine, ngakhale Ine, Khristu Yesu Ambuye wolemekezedwa, ndikuti kwa inu: Ndine kumvetsa kwanu, ndipo ndine mphamvu yanu, ndipo ndine nyonga yanu, ndipo ndine luso lanu: kokha musakayikire!

Chitani ntchito zomwe ndidzakutsogolerani kuti mukwaniritse, ndipo samalirani anthu omwe ndikukutumizirani. Kodi mudzadzichepetsa kwa anthu a udindo wotsika? Kodi mudzakhala, kulalikira, kupulumutsa, ndi kuyanjana ndi osowa pokhala? Pakuti ndikutumiza ena a inu pa cholinga chimenecho. Kodi mudzapempha atumiki a Laodikaya modzichepetsa monga momwe munthu angachitire kwa Atate? Pakuti njira zanga zili choncho. Kodi mudzalankhula mawu omwe ndikukupatsani, ngakhale simukuwamvetsa bwino kapena kuvomereza? (Tsimikizirani mawu anga ndi malemba.) Pakuti ndikukutumizani kuti muchite chifuniro changa, osati chanu. Kodi mudzaweta gulu mu mphamvu zochiritsa za moto mu mzimu wanu zomwe ndidzayatsa mwa inu? (Ndipo pamene mbusa wamkulu adzaonekera, mudzalandira korona waulemerero wosafota.) Chitani ntchito zanga, Mkwatibwi wanga, ndipo lekani ntchito zanu, pakuti palibenso nthawi, ndipo nyale zizima posachedwa.

Mvetsetsani, ndikukutumizani mu ntchito yanga, ndipo mudzakolola mphoto yayikulu ngati muli okhulupirika, pakuti muli kale ndi chuma chambiri chosungidwa kumwamba, ndipo nyumba zambiri m'nyumba ya Atate wanga zakonzedwa kwa inu. Pakuti sautso ya Yakobo ndi masiku asanu ndi awiri a zaka zisanu ndi ziwiri, pomwe chaka chilichonse chimawerengedwa ngati tsiku. Ndi momwemonso ukwati wa Mkwati kwa Mkwatibwi wake. Pakuti kudzakhala masiku asanu ndi awiri a zaka zisanu ndi ziwiri kumwamba, pomwe chaka chilichonse chimawerengedwa ngati tsiku, ndipo mudzakhala nane nthawi zonse, Mkwatibwi wanga. Kenako tikukwera!

Pitani njira zanu ndipo pitani njira yomwe ndikukutsogolerani. Mveranani m'chikondi ndi kudzichepetsa wina ndi mzake. Samalirani munda wanga, pakuti ndabzala munda wa moyo wosatha womwe woyipayo sangauzule kapena kuuwononga. Mudzadya kuchokera ku mtengo weniweni wa moyo womwe walephera kukuletsani, ndipo palibe moyo wochuluka m'nyanja yamoto ya Satana, komwe akupita, koma chilango, chisoni, manyazi, ndi mazunzo okha. Zinthu izi sizanu, Mkwatibwi wanga, ndipo imfa yachiwiri ilibe mphamvu pa inu, pakuti mwachoka ku imfa kupita ku moyo mwa ine, ngakhale Yesu Khristu, mpulumutsi ndi Ambuye wanu.

Choncho pitani komwe ndikukutumizani, ndipo gwirani ntchito ndi anthu omwe ndikukutumizirani, ndipo ndikuwatumiza kwa inu. Pakuti sakubwera kwa inu mwangozi, koma chifukwa ndayamba kale kuwatsogolera kwa inu. Khalani chinsinsi changa ndipo khalani monga momwe anachitira m'Machitidwe, pakuti ndinu mutu womaliza womwe ukulembedwa pamene mukukhala chifukwa cha ine, ndipo posachedwa mudzawona pamene muli kumwamba ndikadzakutengerani kwa ine ndekha, pakuti ndinu anga! Ndinu mkwatibwi wanga, Mpingo wanga waulemerero, womwe ndidzadzipangira ndekha, wopanda banga kapena khwinya, kapena chinthu choterocho: koma kuti mukhale oyera ndi opanda chifukwa, pakuti ndakuyeretsani m'magazi anga owombola!

Ndikukuyitanani, Mkwatibwi wanga, ndipo ndikukuyitanirani mu mautumiki omwe simunalotepo kuti mungawakwaniritse, pakuti atumiki a Laodikaya anayesa kukupangitsani kukhala mu mdima. Musathawe mphamvu yomwe ndikuyatsa mwa inu, pakuti dzanja la Ambuye, ngakhale dzanja langa la madalitso, lili pa inu. Musathawe mautumiki anga omwe ndikuyatsa mu mzimu wanu. Pakuti ndidzayatsa lawi lalikulu mu mzimu wanu, Mkwatibwi wanga, moto womwe udzasungunula zinthu zonse zomwe zikukubwezerani m'mbuyo. Usaope! Yendani mu mphamvu yanga, pakuti atumiki opanda chikhulupirika a Laodikaya ayesa kukukhutiritsani kuti mulibe mphamvu, ndipo pali zotsatirapo zazikulu kwa iwo ngati salapa.

Taona! Ndikukutumizani m'ntchito zomwe ena alephera, ndipo mudzapita m'malo mwawo, pakuti ndinu okhulupirika: Kokha wonongani kunyada kwanu, kuti mungagwe m'manja mwa choyipa monga momwe anachitira. Musagwire malilime anu, pakuti ndiwamasula. Lankhulani mawu omwe ndikukupatsani, ndipo pitani patsogolo panga mu mphamvu yayikulu. Kodi mukuganiza kuti ndimachita tsankho? Pakuti tsopano ndi nthawi ya zozizwitsa zapadera, monga ndinalembera m'Machitidwe: Ndipo Mulungu anachita zozizwitsa zapadera ndi manja a Paulo: Kuti kuchokera m'thupi mwake anatengera kwa odwala nsalu kapena epuloni, ndipo matenda anawachokera, ndipo mizimu yoyipa inatuluka mwa iwo. Mkwatibwi wanga wa chikhulupirika chachikulu, malizitsani ntchito zomwe ndikukuyitanirani, pakuti tikukwaniritsa zolinga zanga mwa inu, ndipo dzuwa likulowa pa nthawi yanga ya chisomo, ndipo posachedwa usiku udzagwa kwathunthu pa dziko lapansi, pakuti mukuchoka. Taona! Ine, Yesu Khristu, ndidzachita zozizwitsa zambiri zamphamvu, ngakhale zozizwitsa zapadera ndi dzanja lanu, pakuti uku ndiko kutumiza kwakukulu komaliza kwa Mpingo wanga musanatengedwe mu ulemerero.

Pitani, mu mtendere, ndipo ndidzatsogolera. Dziwani kuti mudzatsutsidwa kwambiri ndi bwenzi, banja, ndi mdani yemwe. Izi ndi nthawi za kulekanitsa pamene ndikudulira osakhulupirika ndi kuyeretsa mtengo wanga wa azitona ku njira zawo zopanda chikhulupirika, pakuti sanadzikonzere mkwati, ndipo adzatenga malo awo ndi anamwali opusa. Ndipo ndikuyeretsa okhulupirika anga ku tchimo kuti athe kutulutsa nthambi zatsopano zomwe zimabala zipatso zambiri. Usaope, kuganiza kuti suyenera zozizwitsa. Pakuti ndikuvomereza, mwa inu nokha palibe amene angachite zozizwitsa; koma ndidzapereka zizindikiro zambiri, zodabwitsa, ndi zozizwitsa m'manja mwa okhulupirika anga. Pitani ndipo nenani kwa mapiri, chokani ndipo ponyedwani m'nyanja, ndipo musakayikire koma khalani ndi chikhulupirika mwa Yahweh, ndi Ine, Yeshua HaMashiach.

Palibe nyundo yauzimu yoti mugwedeze, choncho lekani kuyesetsa kwanu. Pakuti mutha kuyenda pamadzi ngati musunga maso anu pa ine ndipo osakayikira ndipo osayang'ana kumanzere kapena kumanja koma sungani maso anu pa ine, ndipo simudzamira. Lamulani mizimu, pakuti ili pansi panga ndipo chifukwa chake ili pansi panu. Lamulani mizimu kuti ichoke; musapemphe, koma lamulani! Musawalole kulankhula, koma aletseni; musalankhule nawo, pakuti mawu awo ndi poizoni; koma atulutseni m'chipululu komwe alibe nyumba, ndipo posachedwa adzaponyedwa pamoto. Chiritsani odwala! Lankhulani ku matenda m'dzina langa, ndipo auzeni kuti achoke; usaope, pakuti ndidzakupatsani mawu. Usaope! Simuyenera kulankhula mawu okoma; ingolankhulani mawu a chikhulupirika omwe ndidzauzira mitima yanu ndipo musakayikire.

Taona! Pitani patsogolo chifukwa cha ine, Mkwatibwi wanga, osakayikira chilichonse. Ndikukuyitanirani m'ntchito zomwe ndikumaliza, zomwe ambiri a inu muli kale antchito anzanga. Pali miyoyo yoti ipulumutsidwe, ngakhale yomwe Satana akuyesera kubisa. Ndidzawamasula ku maunyolo ake kudzera m'ntchito zanu zambiri zamphamvu ndi zochita zomwe tidzachita limodzi. Pitani patsogolo, Mkwatibwi wanga, osakayikira chilichonse, pakuti ndakukonzekeretsani moyo wanu wonse pa mphindi zochepa izi za mphamvu ndi kumvera, ndipo mwaphunzitsidwa bwino ndi ine, ndi omwe ndawatumiza kuti asamalire miyoyo yanu.

Mkwatibwi wanga wokhulupirika, ndimakukondani, ndipo ndinu chimwemwe changa ndi kukondwera kwanga. Ndimakumwetulirani tsiku ndi tsiku, komabe simundiwona ndi maso anu, ndipo muli ndi chikhulupirika. Ndimalira chifukwa cha zowawa zanu, ngakhale ndimalira mwakachetechete nthawi zambiri. Ndimamva kulira kwanu kopempha thandizo, ndipo ndine thandizo lopezeka nthawi zonse pa nthawi ya kusowa. Ndimakukondani ndi kukusamalirani, ndipo ndinu mwana wa diso langa, ndipo posachedwa tidzakhala limodzi. Ndikudziwa kuti mwatopa, pakuti mwakhala okhulupirika ku Mawu anga ndi dzina langa, choncho ndidzakulimbitsani mu mphamvu ya Mulungu wopanda malire ndi wamuyaya, ngakhale Yahweh, pakuti palibe wina wofanana naye! Musafooke, pakuti ngakhale mnofu wanu ungakhale wotopa, ndidzakulimbitsani mu mphamvu yanga, pakuti Ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndalengeza izi!

Ndikubwera kwa inu mu mphamvu yayikulu.

Ndikukutumizani mu mphamvu yayikulu.

Ndikukuyitanani, Mkwatibwi wanga, mu mautumiki omwe simunadziwe kuti munali nawo mpaka tsopano.

Ndikutumiza ena a inu kuti mumalize ntchito za osakhulupirika, ndipo mudzakhala ndi mphoto yawo.

Ndikudulira osakhulupirika.

Ndikuyeretsa okhulupirika anga ku machimo anu kuti muthe kubala zipatso zambiri.

Ndine thandizo lopezeka nthawi zonse pa nthawi ya kusowa.

Pitani njira yanu, ndipo yimani mu Kachisi, ndipo lankhulani kwa anthu mawu onse a moyo uwu. Pakuti ndinu kachisi wa Mulungu wamoyo!
