Meyi 8, 2024

Imvani Mawu Anga Otonthoza Ndipo Imvani Mawu Anga Amphamvu!

Imvani mawu a Ambuye, ngakhale mawu a Yeshua HaMashiach. Bwerani kwa ine, Mkwatibwi wanga; bwerani kwa ine okonzeka, pakuti ndikubwera kudzakutengani. Pumulani mwa ine, tiana twanga, ndipo khalani pansi pamapazi anga. Imvani mawu anga otonthoza, ndipo imvani mawu anga amphamvu, pakuti ndakupatsani wotonthoza ndipo mwa wotonthozayo muli mphamvu yayikulu. Ndimveni ndipo khulupirirani mawu anga, chepetsani kunyada kwanu kuti mumve mawu a amithenga anga, pakuti ndi odzichepetsa mumtima. Sindinatumize amphamvu a dziko lino kwa inu; sindinatumize anthu a chuma chambiri ndi kutchuka kwa inu (ngakhale amanamizira). Ayi, ndakutumizirani omwe dziko lapansi lawataya monyoza chifukwa amandilankhulira ndipo mzimu wanga uli mwa iwo ndipo sungachotsedwe.

Musadabwe kuti dziko lapansi limakudani, pakuti dziko lapansi linayamba kudana ndi ine, ndipo kapolo saposa mbuye wake. Ngati adadana ndi ine, adadananso ndi inu. Koma inu ndinu ndani kuti muwope zomwe munthu angakuchitireni? Ndinu ndani kuti mukhale mu mdima ndi mantha pamene ndakutsogolerani kwa Atate wanga ndi Atate wanu, ngakhale Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya, pakuti palibe wina! Ine, ngakhale ine, Khristu Yesu, ndakukhazikani m'malo akumwamba, ndipo onse omwe ali kumwamba akuyembekezera mwachidwi kufika kwanu posachedwa, Mkwatibwi wanga.

Pakuti ngakhale Mulungu wamkulu ndi wamuyaya, ngakhale Atate wathu, ngakhale Yahweh mwiniwake (yemwe ali kuyambira muyaya mpaka muyaya) akukuyembekezerani mwachidwi! Pakuti posachedwa mudzaonekera pamaso pa mpando wake wachifumu mu ulemerero waukulu, ndipo m'chipinda chake chachifumu mudzakhala pansi, pakuti mudzalandira nawo mpando wanga wachifumu. Angelo akukuyembekezeraninso mwachisangalalo, ana anga, pakuti pali chimwemwe pamaso pawo pamene wochimwa mmodzi alapa. Pakuti zili choncho: Koma mwafika ku phiri la Ziyoni, ndi ku mzinda wa Mulungu wamoyo, Yerusalemu wakumwamba, ndi ku gulu la Angelo osawerengeka. Ngakhale ena mwa angelo omwewa ndi atumiki a omwe adzalandira chipulumutso, ndipo izi zikuphatikizapo inu, Mkwatibwi wanga. Ndipo ine, ngakhale Khristu Yesu, ngakhale ine ndikulakalaka kupezeka kwanu pamaso panga. Pakuti ndimakukondani ndipo ndimakufunani, ndipo posachedwa tidzakhala ogwirizana kwamuyaya kudzera mu ukwati wa Mwanawankhosa. Ndine Mkwati wanu yemwe amalakalaka Mkwatibwi wanga, ndipo ndikubwera kudzakutengani! Ndakukonzerani zinthu zambiri, ndipo chuma chambiri ndi mphotho za kukhulupirika zikukuyembekezerani. Posachedwa mudzafika pa cholowa chanu chonse mwa ine, ndipo mudzakhala ndi muyaya wonse kuti muzindikire ukulu ndi ulemerero wa zomwe ndikukusonkhanitsirani kwa ine kuti mukwaniritse, ndi zomwe ndidzakupangani, pakuti ndinu cholengedwa chatsopano, munthu watsopano, wolengedwa mwa ine! Ndipo mudzandichitira zinthu zambiri, osati mu Ufumu wanga wa Chilungamo wokha komwe ndidzalamulira dziko lapansi m'chilungamo kwa tsiku limodzi! Koma makamaka kumwamba kwatsopano ndi dziko lapansi latsopano ukulu wanu udzawala, ndipo mudzakhala nane kwamuyaya. Pakuti tsiku limodzi kwa Ambuye lili ngati zaka chikwi, ndi zaka chikwi ngati tsiku limodzi. Ndili ndi funso kwa inu, okondedwa anga, ngakhale ine, Yesu Khristu, Ambuye wa Ambuye ndi Mfumu ya Mafumu! Ndimveni ndipo lingalirani mawu anga, pakuti mawu a Ambuye ndi mawu oyera: ngati siliva woyesedwa m'ng'anjo yadothi, woyeretsedwa kasanu ndi kawiri. Chifukwa chiyani mumadzichepetsa nokha? Ndikumvetsa kuti ndinu ofatsa ndi odzichepetsa mtima, ndipo izi zimachokera ku chikhalidwe chatsopano chomwe ndinalenga mwa inu, pakuti munthu wachibadwa nthawi zonse amakhala wonyada. Koma chifukwa chiyani mumadziona ngati opanda pake, kudzifunsa kuti chifukwa chiyani Mulungu angafune kuchita nanu, ndipo chifukwa chiyani ndingakupatseni chipulumutso pamene pali ofuna bwino? Chifukwa chiyani mumakhala pansi mu kupsinjika maganizo, kudzidzudzula nokha pamene mwachimwa? Bwanji osadzikung'unta fumbi? (Fumbi lidzakhala chakudya cha njoka.) Dzambatukani! Nyamukani! Galamukani! Inde, nonse mudzachimwa kuyambira m'mawa mpaka kulowa kwa dzuwa. Kodi ichi ndi chinthu chomwe Yahweh anachinyalanyaza m'kudziwiratu kwake? Ndiye ndi chiyani? Kodi Mulungu anayiwala kuti mudzachimwa pambuyo pa chipulumutso? Mulungu aletse: inde, Mulungu akhale woona, koma munthu aliyense wabodza! Pakuti mu dongosolo lake la chiwombolo, ngakhale mapulani ake okhudza ine, ngakhale chinsinsi chachikulu cha Mulungu, ngakhale zinthu zonsezi zinapangidwa pamodzi mu chisomo ndi chifundo; ingolandirani chisomo changa ndi chifundo changa, pakuti ambiri a inu mumadzikwapula nokha m'malo mwake.

Ana anga, ndinalipira mtengo wa machimo anu. Pamene mukuyenda mowongoka ndi moona mtima ndipo mukuchimwa chifukwa thupi lanu laipitsidwa, ingovomerezani tchimolo kwa ine ndikudziyeretsa m'magazi anga owombola, pakuti ndine chiwombolo chanu ndipo ndakuwombolani. Oomboledwa a Ambuye anene motero: omwe wawaombola m'manja mwa mdani. Palibe chomwe mungachite kuti muwonjezere pa ntchito zanga zomalizidwa, pakuti ntchito zomalizidwa bwino zikufuna chiyani chinanso? Pamene ndinati pamtanda pa mpweya wanga womaliza m'nyumba yadothi lofiira ija, ndinali wovala panthawiyo: Zatha, sindinasiye chilichonse chosachitidwa. Ndipo pamene Mulungu alandira nsembe yomalizidwa, yangwiro, palibe amene ayesere kuwonjezera chilichonse; pakuti izo ndi ntchito zachabechabe, ngakhale kunyada kwanu kochenjera ndi chabechabe chanu, pakuti pa dziko lapansi pali chabechabe chachikulu. Chabechabe cha zinthu zachabechabe, atero Mlaliki: chabechabe cha zinthu zachabechabe, zonse ndi zachabechabe. Pakuti chabechabe chimalankhula, ndipo chimakunamizani, kunena kuti simuli abwino mokwanira ndipo nsembe yanga ndi yosakwanira.

Choncho, muyenera kuchita zambiri kuti mutsimikizire kuyenera kwanu mukalapa pamaso panga. Zoonadi ndikunena, zipatso zoyenera kulapa ndizovomerezeka, koma osati kudzidzudzula, osati kudzidana, osati maganizo odzigonjetsa a kupsinjika maganizo. Pakuti pamene mukuchita machitidwe opanda pakewa a thupi, simukudziwa kuti mukutcha choonadi bodza ndikuyesera kuyanjanitsa thupi lanu ndi mzimu. Ana anga, chobadwa m'thupi ndi thupi ndipo chobadwa m'mzimu ndi mzimu. Pakuti thupi limalakalaka kutsutsana ndi mzimu, ndipo mzimu kutsutsana ndi thupi: ndipo izi zimasemphana wina ndi mzake: kotero kuti simungathe kuchita zinthu zomwe mungafune. Pakuti kudzera mu nsembe yanga mwayanjanitsidwa kwa ine, Ambuye wanu Yesu Khristu, ndipo ndakuyanjanitsani kwa Mulungu, pakuti chotero ndi chinsinsi chachikulu cha Mulungu chomwe inu muli mbali yake. Chifukwa chiyani mukukayikira mawu anga? Uzeni thupi lanu kuti likhale chete, ndipo tsekerezani mawu omwe akutsutsana ndi chiphunzitso changa cholondola! Pakuti ndakukonzerani ukulu. Ndakupatsani mphamvu mu mzimu wanga wa umwana womwe mufuula, ABBA, Atate. Ndakupatsani mphamvu ndi kukutheketsani kuchita zinthu zazikulu chifukwa cha ine; ndipo koposa zonse, ndakutumizani mwa ine, ngakhale m'makalata anga achikondi kwa Mkwatibwi wanga. Ngakhale makalata omwe ndinauza Paulo kuti alembere Mpingo wanga akufotokoza izi m'mawu omveka bwino kuti mumvetsetse ndi kukhulupirira. Pakuti ndinauza chiyani Mpingo wanga wa ku Korinto kudzera mwa Paulo? Chifukwa chake, ngati munthu aliyense ali mwa Khristu, ali cholengedwa chatsopano: zinthu zakale zapita, taonani, zinthu zonse zakhala zatsopano. Ndipo zinthu zonse ndi za Mulungu, amene anatiyanjanitsa kwa iye yekha kudzera mwa Yesu Khristu, ndipo anatipatsa ife utumiki wa chiyanjanitso. Ana anga, pamene mukuyenda m'mzimu wanga ndikukhala okhulupirika ku malangizo anga, ndikukulengani mwatsopano mwa ine.

Pakuti munthu wakale wadothi lofiira akuwonongeka, ndipo Myuda ndi Wamitundu yemwe adzapangidwa kukhala munthu mmodzi watsopano mwa ine, ngakhale ndi mzimu wanga wa umwana, pakuti ndidzapulumutsa ena a onse. Tsopano, ngati pali munthu aliyense amene alibe mzimu wa Khristu, yemweyo si wake. Mwa iyenso mutakhulupirira, munasindikizidwa ndi mzimu woyera wa lonjezo, Womwe ndi chikole cha cholowa chathu, mpaka chiwombolo cha chuma chogulidwa, ku matamando a ulemerero wake. Choncho yendani mu unyowani wa moyo ndikumvetsetsa kuti posachedwa chuma changa chogulidwa chidzalandira mokwanira (muli ndi malipiro oyamba a mzimu wanga), ndipo mudzavekedwa matupi atsopano, matupi osawonongeka, matupi amphamvu mu ukulu ndi luso, ngakhale nyumba zoyenera mfumu! Pakuti mudzakhala mafumu ndi ansembe pamaso pa Mulungu wathu. Choncho ndakuyanjanitsani kwa Mulungu, ndipo palibe udani winanso, ndipo ndakupatsani utumiki wa chiyanjanitso. O, koma chifukwa chiyani ndingachite chinthu chotere, mungafunse? Mverani ndipo ndikuuzani.

Ndipo motero Akorinto akupitiriza umboni wawo: Kuti, Mulungu anali mwa Khristu, kuyanjanitsa dziko lapansi kwa iye yekha, osawawerengera zolakwa zawo, ndipo waika mwa ife mawu a chiyanjanitso. Tsopano tili Akazembe a Khristu, monga ngati Mulungu akukupemphani kudzera mwa ife; tikukupemphani m'malo mwa Khristu, kuti muyanjanitsidwe kwa Mulungu. Ana anga, ndinu akazembe anga, ndipo muli ndi udindo wolemekezeka kwambiri, pakuti mumandiimira. Pakuti kukhala kazembe wanga ndiko kuimira Mfumu ya Mafumu ndi Ambuye wa Ambuye ku dziko lomwe likufa, ndipo ndine mkulu wa ansembe kwamuyaya monga mwa dongosolo la Melikizedeki. Choncho, chonde ndimveni: Ndakupangani kukhala akazembe anga. Ndakuyanjanitsani kwa Mulungu Wamphamvuyonse, ngakhale El Shaddai, pakuti zolinga zake zidzachitika! Ndakupatsani mawu a chiyanjanitso, ngakhale mawu olankhula bwino oyenera akazembe anga, pakuti Mawu a Mulungu ndi kuyambira muyaya mpaka muyaya. Ndipo ndapatsa aliyense wa inu utumiki wa chiyanjanitso. Izi zikutanthauza kuti ndavomereza mzimu uliwonse womwe wabadwa mwatsopano ndi mzimu wanga wamphamvu monga atumiki anga amphamvu, ndipo mdani amakuwopani kwambiri ndipo adzakuthawani pamene mukuyenda mu mphamvu yanga, ngakhale adzayesera kukunong'onezani mawu abodza ndikubangula ngati mkango. Koma pakamwa pake ponunkha palibe mano, pakuti anathyoka ndi mphamvu ya kuuka kwanga, ndipo sadzachira. Mnyalanyazeni mkango wopanda manowo, ndipo yang'anani pa mawu anga, ngakhale Mawu a Mawu anga.

Mu udindo waukulu uwu wa ulamuliro ndi mphamvu (pakuti mumayenda m'dzina langa ndipo dzina langa ndi lalikulu kuposa mayina onse, ndipo Yahweh yekha ndi wamkulu!) Ndakuikani monga atumiki anga ndi akazembe, ndipo ndakukonzekeretsani ndi kukukonzekeretsani ndikukuvekani mphamvu yayikulu! Ana anga, simukumvetsa malangizo a Mawu anga, pakuti abusa ampatuko analemba ntchito atumiki a ku Laodikaya kuti akunamizeni za mphamvu ndi mphatso yolengedwa mwa inu pa kubadwa kwatsopano. Pakuti Paulo anafotokoza ukulu wa mphamvu yoperekedwa mu mzimu wa umwana wolengedwa mwa inu. Pakuti anauza Akorinto: Mu zinthu zauzimu, abale, sindikufuna kuti mukhale osadziwa. Ndiponso: Pali kusiyana kwa mphatso, zoonadi, koma mzimu umodzi. Ndiponso: Koma kuwonetseredwa kwa Mzimu kumaperekedwa kwa munthu aliyense, kuti apindule nako. Musakhale osadziwa za zinthu zauzimu; mvetsetsani kusiyana pakati pa mphatso ndi kuwonetseredwa, pakuti si mawu ofanana m'malemba.

Ndapereka mphatso ya mzimu woyera, ndipo mu mphatso imeneyo muli kuwonetseredwa. Pakuti Mawu anga amati chiyani: Koma kuwonetseredwa kwa mzimu, kumaperekedwa kwa munthu aliyense kuti apindule nako.

Atumiki anu ofunda a ku Laodikaya abwereza mabodza omwe Satana wanena m'mibadwo yonse, mabodza okuchititsani kukhulupirira kuti mulibe mphamvu, ndi mabodza oyesera kubisa mphamvu yanu yeniyeni mwa ine, pakuti mzimu wanu ndi Khristu mwa inu, chiyembekezo cha ulemerero! Choncho kuwonetseredwa kwanga kwa mzimu kwalembedwa ngati izi: mawu anzeru, mawu a chidziwitso, chikhulupiriro, mphatso za machiritso, kugwira ntchito za zozizwitsa, uneneri, kuzindikira mizimu, malilime osiyanasiyana, kumasulira malilime. Tisanapitirize, ikani pansi ndemanga zanu zadziko lapansi ndi mtanthauzira mawu wadziko lapansi ndi ma concordance adziko lapansi, ndipo siyani kuwalola kuti akunamizeni kuti mufotokozezere malemba ku zolinga za Satana.

Mu mzimu uliwonse mwa munthu aliyense muli kuthekera kumeneku kowonetsa mphamvu yochokera kumwamba! Koma zonsezi zimagwira ntchito mzimu umodzi womwewo, kugawa kwa munthu aliyense payekha monga momwe akufunira. Ana anga, chifukwa chiyani mungaganize kuti izi ndi mphatso pamene malemba amati ndi kuwonetseredwa? Akunamizani; werengani mawu anga ndipo khalani ndi chikhulupiriro m'mawu omwe omasulira anga anasankha, pakuti ndinali nawo. Muli ndi maluso onsewa, ndipo ngati mufuna kuwawonetsa, ndidzawagwiritsa ntchito mwa inu kuti mupulumutse akapolo m'manja mwa Satana kuwala kwaulemerero kwamuyaya. Nyalanyazani aliyense amene amalankhula zosemphana ndi mawu awa, ndipo dziphunzitseni m'Mawu anga. Siyani kudandaula za momwe zinthu izi zimagwirira ntchito, pakuti sizigwira ntchito kudzera m'mawu olankhula bwino ndi maganizo osangalatsa. Zimagwira ntchito malinga ndi chosowa, ndi chikhumbo cha munthu yemwe ayenera kukhala ndi chifuniro chochita chifuniro changa, ndipo makamaka ndi chikhulupiriro mwa ine. Pakuti ndidzachita ntchito zamphamvu ndi manja anu, ndipo ndakutumizani, mkwatibwi wanga, pakuti uwu ndi utumiki womaliza.

Ndapereka mphatso ya mzimu woyera, ndipo mu mphatso imeneyo muli mphatso zina zambiri, pakuti Aefeso amati chiyani: Koma kwa aliyense wa ife kwaperekedwa chisomo, monga mwa muyeso wa mphatso ya Khristu. Chifukwa chake amati: Pamene anakwera kumwamba, anatsogolera ukapolo kukhala kapolo, ndipo anapereka mphatso kwa anthu. Ndiponso kuchokera kwa Aroma: Pakuti ndinena, kudzera mu chisomo choperekedwa kwa ine, kwa munthu aliyense amene ali pakati panu, kuti asadziyese wamkulu kuposa momwe ayenera kuganizira, koma kuganiza modziletsa, monga Mulungu wagawira kwa munthu aliyense muyeso wa chikhulupiriro. Choncho mvetsetsani kuti muyenera kudziona ngati ofunika pakuti mwalengedwanso m'chilungamo ndi chiyero chenicheni ngakhale kuwonetsa ulemerero wa Mulungu mwiniwake, ngakhale Atate wa kuwala, pakuti ndinu kuwala kwa dziko lapansi. Koma ganizani modziletsa, pakuti simuli aakulu kuposa abale ndi alongo anu omwe abadwa ndi mzimu womwewo ngati inu: Pakuti nonse muli ndi maluso awa, ndipo nonse muli ndi mphatso izi.

Pakuti ndapereka mphatso kwa anthu: Ndipo anapereka ena kukhala atumwi: ndi ena aneneri: ndi ena alaliki: ndi ena abusa, ndi aphunzitsi: Kuti akonze oyera mtima, ku ntchito ya utumiki, ku kumanga thupi la Khristu. Pali mautumiki ambiri omwe ndapatsa Mkwatibwi wanga, ndipo ndi anu ngati mukufuna. Pemphani kwa ine ndipo ndidzagwira ntchito mautumiki awa mwa inu, koma muyenera kukhala ndi chikhumbo. Choncho funitsitsani mphatso zabwino kwambiri: Ndipo komabe ndikuwonetsani njira yabwino kwambiri. Njira yabwino kwambiri imeneyo, ana anga, ndi chikondi chosagonjetseka cha Mulungu. Muyenera kufunitsitsa mphatso zabwino kwambiri, pakuti ndakupatsani mphamvu. Koma kutumikira chifukwa cha chikondi cha Mulungu, kupereka nsembe za milomo yanu ndi matamando a mtima wanu wokhulupirika m'chikondi, ndikopambana. Perekani mwaulere monga mwalandira mwaulere. Yendani mu mphamvu iyi chifukwa mumakonda Mulungu ndipo mumandikonda ine, Yesu Khristu, ndipo perekani mwaulere zonse zomwe mwapatsidwa, ndipo pitani, osakayikira chilichonse.

Mkwatibwi wanga, choncho ndakutumizani kuti mumalize ntchito izi, ndipo posachedwa maphunziro anu adzatha, ndipo pamene ndikusonkhanitsani kwa ine m'mitambo, mudzadziwa, monga momwe ndinadziwira pamtanda, ndipo mudzatha kunena (monga momwe ndinanenera kale): Zatha. Posachedwa mudzalowa m'chisangalalo cha Ambuye wanu!

Ndikubwera kudzakutengani posachedwa.

Ndikulakalaka kupezeka kwanu pamaso panga.

Ndine Khristu Yesu ndipo ndakulengani mwatsopano mwa ine.

Ndine mkulu wa ansembe kwamuyaya monga mwa dongosolo la Melikizedeki. Ndine amene wapereka mphatso kwa anthu.

Ndine amene wakupatsani utumiki ndi mawu a chiyanjanitso.

Ndine amene wakutumizani monga akazembe anga okhulupirika.

Ndine amene ndili, choncho auzeni kuti Ndine wakutumizani.

NDINE wandituma kwa inu.
