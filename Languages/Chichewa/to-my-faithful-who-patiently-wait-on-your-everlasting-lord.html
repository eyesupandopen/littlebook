Kwa Okhulupirika Anga Amene Akuyembekezera Moleza Mtima Ambuye Wanu Wamuyaya!

Imvani mawu a Ambuye wanu wamuyaya, ngakhale Yeshua HaMashiach ndipo bwerani kwa ine ndi mitima yanu yokonzeka kulandira ndi kuyembekezera mphoto yanu kuchokera m'dzanja langa, pakuti ndithudi nthawi ndi ino. Ngakhale mphindi yomwe yakhala ikuyembekezeredwa kwanthawi yayitali pamene ndidzatsika kuchokera kumwamba ndi mfuu waukulu, ndi mawu a mngelo wamkulu ndi lipenga la Mulungu (pakuti lipengalo lidzalira) ndipo akufa mwa Khristu adzaukitsidwa osavunda ndipo inu amene muli amoyo ndi otsala mudzatengedwa m'mitambo kumene mudzavala kusafa ndipo mudzakhala ndi, ndi kufanana ndi, Ambuye wanu wamuyaya mpaka kalekale.

Ndine Mbuye wa Ambuye ndi Mfumu ya Mafumu, ndipo ndine amene ndagula dziko lapansi ndi magazi anga otetezera. Ndithudi, ndithudi, ndinalipira mtengo wa machimo a anthu kuti anthu ayanjanitsidwe ndi Yahweh, chifukwa Ine, Yesu wa ku Nazarete ndine pangano lanu lamtendere limene anthu amayanjanitsidwa nalo kwa Mulungu, chifukwa chake ndinauza Paulo kuti alembere Akorinto: Chotero ife ndife akazembe a Khristu: monga ngati Mulungu akukupemphani kudzera mwa ife, tikukupemphani m'malo mwa Khristu, kuti muyanjanitsidwe ndi Mulungu. Pakuti iye anampanga iye kukhala tchimo chifukwa cha ife, amene sanadziwe tchimo, kuti ife tipangidwe kukhala chilungamo cha Mulungu mwa iye.

Choncho, ana anga, ndakupangani kukhala chilungamo cha Mulungu kudzera mwa Khristu mwa inu chiyembekezo cha ulemerero, ngakhale mzimu wa umwana wamuyaya umene mumalira ABBA, Atate ndipo choncho mwabadwanso monga ana amuyaya a Mulungu ndipo mwasindikizidwa ndi mzimu woyera wa lonjezo. Mvetsetsani kuti chidindo cha Yahweh sichingasweke, kapena kusinthidwa, ndipo onse amene asindikizidwa nacho ali otetezeka kosatha, ndipo choncho mvetsetsani kuti chimene Yahweh walemba ndi kulengeza palibe amene angachisinthe. Ndiye ndani amene angakuchotseni m'manja mwanga? Ndani amene angagonjetse Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya, ngakhale Yahweh, amene ali Atate wa Kuwala? Ndiye chifukwa chiyani mukudandaula ndi kuda nkhawa kuti ndidzakutayani? Komabe, ndani wa inu amene angataye ana ake, ngati kuti chinthu choterocho ndi chopepuka pamaso pa Ambuye ndi Mpulumutsi wanu, ngakhale Yesu wa ku Nazarete.

Ndithudi, ndithudi, mphatso ndi kuitana kwa Mulungu zilibe kulapa ndipo amene apatsidwa mphatso ya moyo wosatha povomereza Yesu monga Ambuye kwinaku akukhulupirira kuti Mulungu anandiukitsa kwa akufa amapatsidwa moyo wosatha ndipo amapangidwa kukhala chilungamo cha Mulungu mwa Khristu Yesu Ambuye wawo. Tsopano ngati munthu aliyense apanga chivomerezo chimenecho kuchokera mumtima wopanda chikhulupiriro, ndimazuwa chidindo cha Mulungu wamoyo kuti ndiwakokere kwathunthu kwa ine kuti afike pakulapa machimo mokwanira, kuopa kuti mwina mbewu yanga yagwera pamalo amiyala. Tsopano ngati munthu aliyense alibe mzimu wa Khristu, iye si wake. Ndine amene ndimayesa ndi kuyesa mitima ya anthu kuti ayeretsedwe m'moto wanga woyeretsa chifukwa ndine amene ndimakhala ndi mvukuto m'manja mwanga kusonkhezera malawi a moto woyeretsa pakuti ndine wosula amene amavukuta makala kuti mufike ku cholowa chanu chokwanira mwa ine, Khristu Yesu, choncho khalani ofatsa ku zoyesayesa zanga ndipo musakane kukoka kwa mzimu wa umwana wobadwa mwa inu womwe umabweretsa chilungamo ndi chiyero chenicheni. Ndipo choncho ndikukupemphani ana anga yendani mwa mzimu kuti musakwaniritse zilakolako za thupi.

Komabe, ambiri a inu mumalola mitima yanu kukuvutitsani mosafunikira kuti chidindo cha Mulungu chikhoza kusweka ndi kupusa kwa munthu. Ndikukufunsani, zikondi zanga? Kodi El Shaddai m'kudziwiratu kwake ananyalanyaza mfundo yakuti anali kunditumiza Ine, Yesu wa ku Nazarete, kudzawombola ochimwa amene adzachimwanso pambuyo pa chipulumutso? Kodi Yahweh, Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya amene safuna phungu ngakhale zikwi khumi zochulukitsa zikwi khumi zikuzungulira mpando wake wachifumu, ndipo amene amagwira ntchito zinthu zonse monga mwa uphungu wa chifuniro chake sanamvetsetse kupanda ungwiro kwa Adamu woipitsidwa, ngakhale anthu onse obadwa ku dothi lofiira amene amadya magazi a Adamu?

Ayi ana anga, n'zosatheka kuti Yahweh asamvetsetse zinthu izi ndipo wakupatsani m'njira iliyonse ndikupereka njira iliyonse kuti chiwombolo chanu chokwanira chibwere mokwanira pa inu zikondi zanga. Choncho yendani m'chilamulo cha chikhulupiriro ndipo kudzera mwa mzimu wanga wobadwa mwa inu ndipo yendani monga mwa mzimu wa moyo mwa Khristu Yesu. Pakuti mwa Khristu Yesu mdulidwe supindula kanthu, kapena kusadulidwa, koma cholengedwa chatsopano. Ndipo onse amene amayenda monga mwa lamulo ili, mtendere udzakhala pa iwo, ndi chifundo, ndi pa Isiraeli wa Mulungu. Kodi simukumvetsetsa kuti wolungama adzakhala ndi moyo ndi chikhulupiriro? Choncho mvetsetsani kuti palibe okhulupirika anga amene ali angwiro panthawi ino, ayi, mpaka chiwombolo chawo chokwanira. Ndipo owomboledwa onse amene asindikizidwa ndi mzimu woyera wa lonjezo onse adzachimwa, mwadala kapena mosadziwa, tsiku lonse. Choncho, dziyeretseni m'magazi anga otetezera tsiku ndi tsiku. Bwerani kwa ine ndipo yatsani ubale wanu ndi ine ndikuusunga wamphamvu, zikondi zanga. Vomerani machimo anu kwa ine tsiku ndi tsiku, ndipo khalani ofatsa ku mawu anga ang'onoang'ono pamene ndibwera kwa inu kudzakuwonetsani chilungamo. Ndasuntha kale kulowera kwa inu ndipo ndili pomwepo pamaso panu kukusangalatsani ndikusunga njira zanu zoyera kuti muthe kuyenda njira yowongoka ndi yopapatiza yomwe mwaitanidwa kuyenda. Ndipo pitirizani kuyembekezera tsiku limenelo la chiwombolo chanu chokwanira pamene mudzaima angwiro pamaso pa Yahweh mwiniwake ovala chilungamo chomwe ndidzakupatsani mokwanira pakuti ndine, Khristu Yesu, amene ndidzakvekani nsalu yabafuta yoyera yopanda banga, chilema, kapena khwinya kapena chinthu choterocho.

Choncho, mvetsetsani ana anga, kuti chiweruzo chafika padziko lapansi, ngakhale chiweruzo chachikulu chimenecho chinaloseredwa kalekale ndi aneneri akale ngakhale nthawi yake yokwaniritsidwa yafika tsopano. Zowawa zobereka sizikuyima padziko lapansi tsopano popeza chiweruzo chili pano ndipo owomboledwa anga adzafika ku chiwombolo chawo chokwanira. Ndipo zili choncho kuti zochitika zidzabwera, funde ndi funde. Funde lililonse lalikulu ndi lamphamvu kwambiri pakuwononga kwake kuposa lomaliza mpaka mwadzidzidzi mwatengedwa kupita kumalo otetezeka ndipo mwadzidzidzi Babulo Wachinsinsi palibe. Funde lililonse limabweretsa chiweruzo ndipo funde lililonse limagunda mwamphamvu ndi mokweza kuposa lomaliza. Ndipo zili choncho ziweruzo zanga ndi machenjezo ku dziko lapansi, pakuti ndimabwera monga mkango wa fuko la Yuda ndipo m'chilungamo ndimabwera kudzaweruza ndi kupanga nkhondo.

Ndipo zili choncho kuti tsoka lomaliza lisanafike zidzachitika pa lamulo langa kuti okhulupirika anga amene agona mwa Khristu adzaukitsidwa m'manda awo ndipo okhulupirika anga amene ali amoyo ndi otsala adzasonkhanitsidwa pamodzi nawo ndipo kumwamba kwa kumwamba mudzapita, kumene nonse mudzaima pamaso pa mpando wachiweruzo waukulu wa Khristu kuti nonse mulandire pa zinthu zochitidwa m'thupi. Onse amene atengedwa kuyandikira Bema yanga sabwera kumeneko kudzaweruzidwa chifukwa cha machimo awo chifukwa munalandira chikhululukiro cha machimo mu nthawi ya munthu wochimwa ngakhale m'tsiku lino pamene munthu wochimwa akulamulira dziko lapansi lomwe ulamuliro wake uli kumapeto kwake. Chifukwa dziko lapansi ndi langa, Khristu Yesu, ndi kudzaza kwake, ndipo ndikuyandikira ndipo ndidzatenga mwamphamvu cholowa changa kwa olanda ndipo chiweruzo chawo chokwanira sichichedwa. Zikondi zanga, mvetsetsani kuti mwadutsa kuchokera ku imfa molunjika ku moyo wosatha mwa ine, Khristu Yesu Ambuye wanu. Ndipo ndine amene ndakuyeretsani mokwanira ku tchimo m'magazi anga otetezera chifukwa ndine Paskha wanu wangwiro amene ndadzipereka ndekha kwa Yahweh Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya, kamodzi, onse, ndi kosatha chifukwa cha machimo a anthu: choncho, palibenso nsembe ina ya machimo.

Choncho, ana a Mulungu adzayandikira mpando wanga wachiweruzo kuti ndithe kuweruza ntchito zawo kuti onse alandire pa zomwe akwaniritsa, kaya zikhale zabwino kapena zoipa. Ndipo choncho nonse mudzabweretsa nsembe za miyoyo yanu yowomboledwa padziko lapansi yomwe mwapereka potumikira Ambuye wanu wamuyaya. Monga kwalembedwa, momwemonso zidzachitika, monga kwalembedwa: Pakuti palibe munthu angathe kuika maziko ena, kuposa amene aikidwa, omwe ndi Yesu Khristu. Ndipo ngati munthu aliyense amanga pa maziko awa, golide, siliva, miyala yamtengo wapatali, matabwa, udzu, kapena ziputu, Ntchito ya munthu aliyense idzaonekera: pakuti tsikulo lidzaulula, chifukwa lidzavumbulutsidwa ndi moto: ndipo moto udzayesa ntchito ya munthu aliyense kuti ndi yotani. Ngati ntchito ya munthu aliyense, yomwe wamanga pamenepo, ikhalapo, adzalandira malipiro. Ngati ntchito ya munthu aliyense itenthedwa, adzataya, koma iye mwini adzapulumutsidwa: komabe ngati kudzera pamoto.

Choncho zidzakhala zikondi zanga kuti ndidzakupatsani mphoto mokwanira chifukwa cha utumiki wanu kwa ine ndipo zinthu zomwe zatenthedwa ngati mankhusu ndi moto wanga wachiweruzo mudzataya ndipo pa zinthu zomwe zipirira moto wanga wachiweruzo, ngakhale ntchito zomwe ndakupatsani kuti mukwaniritse, mphoto yaikulu idzakhala yanu. Pakuti kwalembedwa: Diso silinawone, kapena khutu kumva, kapena kulowa mumtima mwa munthu, zinthu zomwe Mulungu wakonzera iwo amene amamukonda. Ndipo momwemonso ndi chikhalidwe cha cholowa chanu ndi mphoto, kuti palibe munthu mmodzi padziko lapansi amene amamvetsetsa mokwanira ukulu ndi ulemerero wa cholowa cha ana amuyaya a Mulungu amene abadwanso monga mwa mzimu wa Khristu, momwe mwabadwanso ana a Mulungu kwa muyaya wonse.

Choncho mvetsetsani zikondi zanga kuti tsiku la chiwombolo chanu chokwanira lili pafupi kuposa momwe mukuganizira, ndipo likuthamangira kwa inu pa sekondi yomweyi ndipo tsiku limenelo latsala pang'ono kukwaniritsidwa, posachedwa kuposa momwe mukuyembekezera. Mvetsetsani kuti palibenso nthawi yotsala yochenjeza, palibenso nthawi yotsala yopempha, palibenso nthawi yotsala yokambirana ndi Babulo wachinsinsi chifukwa chiweruzo chake ndi chomaliza ndipo sichingasinthidwe. Ayi, nthawi zake zolapa zinawonongedwa, ndipo ananyalanyaza mokwanira mawu a aneneri anga ndi atumwi akunena mumtima mwake pamene ankanyoza mawu anga “Ndimakhala ngati mfumukazi ndipo sindine wamasiye, mawa adzakhala ngati lero ndipo lero ndi lofanana ndi dzulo.”

Komabe mu ola limodzi tsoka lake lidzamugwera mokwanira, ndipo sadzaukanso. Koma m'malo mwa mtundu womwe unali waukulu womwe unkalemekeza njira za Yahweh ndipo kumene anthu ankatsatira Yesu wa ku Nazarete ndipo sankachita manyazi kukonda anansi awo kudzatsala chipululu kumene Babulo Wachinsinsi adzawonongedwa mu ola limodzi chifukwa wamphamvu ndi Ambuye Mulungu amene amamuweruza. Ndipo pamene tsoka limenelo likuyandikira kwambiri zikondi zanga, momwemonso chiwombolo chanu chokwanira, chipulumutso, mphoto, ndi chimwemwe chamuyaya, choncho musadandaule pa chiweruzo chomwe chikubwera cha ochimwa chomwe sichanu ngati mutuluka mokwanira m'machimo anu.

Ndipo choncho kuthwanima kwa diso kumeneko kukuyandikira kumene mu kachigawo ka sekondi mudzipeza mutaima m'chipinda chachifumu cha Yahweh mwiniwake kumene mipando yanu yachifumu yakongoletsedwa ndipo kumene mwakhala ngati mafumu amuyaya ndi ansembe ndi cholowa cha Ambuye wanu wamuyaya, momwe mudzalamulira mitundu ndi ndodo yachitsulo pakuti zapatsidwa kwa ine, ndipo momwemonso ndidzapereka kwa iwo amene agonjetsa. Kodi ogonjetsa anga ndani? Ngakhale iwo amene anatuluka mokwanira mu tchimo mwa kulapa mokwanira ndi kukana machimo awo. Ndipo ndine Khristu Yesu amene ndagonjetsa machenjera a mdierekezi mokwanira ndi kwa muyaya wonse, kumusiya mdani wosakazidwa ndi wogonjetsedwa mokwanira yemwe malawi amoto amuyaya a chilango asonkhezeredwa ndipo ali okonzeka kulandira, pakuti chotero ndi cholowa chawo ndipo chotero iwo ndi oyenera.

Choncho, ndakonza njira yamphamvu kwa onse amene amabwera kwa ine mokwanira kuti agonjetse mokwanira machenjera a mdierekezi mofanana ndi momwe ndinachitira. Choncho bwerani kwa ine mokwanira kuti ndikutsogolereni panjira yowongoka ndi yopapatiza yopita ku chipulumutso chanu chokwanira. Imvani mawu anga inu amene mwateleza ndi kugwa m'njira pakuti munthu wolungama amagwa kasanu ndi kawiri, ndikuukanso, choncho ukani Oh' anthu a Mulungu ndipo bwerani kwa ine mokwanira kuti ndikugwirizanitseninso ku mphoto yanu pokuyeretsani m'magazi anga otetezera kuti muime ovala mokwanira m'chilungamo changa. Mvetsetsani kuti ogonjetsa anga adzalandira zinthu zonse ndipo palibe chinthu chabwino chomwe chidzamanidwa kwa iwo. Ndipo choncho ogonjetsa anga, adzawala kosatha, ngakhale monga kuwala kwa thambo: ndipo inu amene mwatembenuza ambiri ku chilungamo, mudzawala ngati nyenyezi, kosatha. Choncho tsopano podziwa izi ana anga kuti tsopano ndi nthawi yosiya nkhawa za dziko lino ndikupuma mwa ine ndikukhala oyera ngati namwali m'magazi anga otetezera.

Choncho, zikondi zanga khalani chilili pamaso pa tsoka lomwe likubwera, chifukwa mukuwona tsiku loopsa limenelo likuyandikira, ndipo ndine amene ndatsegula maso a odzichepetsa mumtima kuti muthe kuwona choonadi cha dziko lanu ndikusagonjera ku chinyengo champhamvu chomwe sichanu. Mvetsetsani kuti Ine, Yeshua HaMashiach ndine amene ndimapangitsa akhungu kuwona, choncho, bwerani kwa ine kuti ndidzoze maso anu ndi mankhwala a maso ndipo kuti muthe kuwona mwa mbali dziko loipali monga momwe liliri momwe mukukhala. Choncho musawope chiweruzo cha ochimwa pakuti chiweruzo chimenecho sichanu zikondi zanga, ayi chiweruzo sicha owomboledwa chifukwa mwapulumutsidwa ku mkwiyo wa Mwanawankhosa ndi ku mkwiyo wa Mulungu, chifukwa chake khalani chilili ndi otetezeka mu chipulumutso chanu zikondi zanga chifukwa tsiku lili pafupi ndipo likuthamangira kwa inu pafupi kwambiri ndi sekondi iliyonse.

Inde mphindi imeneyo ya nthawi ya chiwombolo chanu chokwanira yafika pa inu zikondi zanga ndipo mphindi yamuyaya imeneyo yatsala pang'ono kufika ndipo ikubwera pa inu pafupi ndi pafupi ndi sekondi iliyonse. Ndatsimikizira m'mizimu yanu, ndawonetsa zizindikiro kumwamba ndi zizindikiro padziko lapansi, ndapangitsa zodabwitsa kumwamba ndipo ngakhale kutulutsa masoka mu chiweruzo pa ochimwa ndi kudzutsa iwo amene akugona kuti amvetsetse kuti tsopano ndi nthawi ya chiwombolo chokwanira cha ana a Mulungu Wamphamvuyonse, ngakhale mkwatibwi wanga ndi thupi langa.

Musagwirizane nawo m'njira zawo zopanda chikhulupiriro, chifukwa akufuna kutenga ufulu ndikufotokoza zizindikiro zambiri ndi zodabwitsa zomwe zikuwonetsedwa kumwamba zomwe zambiri zajambulidwa ndi chala cha Yahweh mwiniwake. Kodi mwayiwala Genesis zikondi zanga? Pakuti kwalembedwa: Ndipo Mulungu anati, Pakhale zounikira m'thambo la kumwamba, kulekanitsa usana ndi usiku, ndipo zikhale zizindikiro, ndi nyengo, ndi masiku ndi zaka. Ndipo zikhale zounikira m'thambo la kumwamba kuunikira padziko lapansi: ndipo zinali choncho. Choncho, mvetsetsani kuti Yahweh amalamulira pamwambamwamba ndipo palibe chomwe chimaloledwa pa chilengedwe chake chomwe salola, ndipo chomwe sakuchidziwa. Chifukwa chake, musagwirizane nawo m'malingaliro awo opanda chikhulupiriro akuti anthu tsopano mwanjira ina amalamulira ndi kusintha kumwamba, ngakhale anthu amayesetsa kugonjetsa Mulungu ndipo zoyesayesa zake nthawi zonse zimathera pachabe.

Komabe, angelo akugwa amayesetsa kubisa zizindikiro zanga ndi zilengezo zambiri zomwe Yahweh waika kumwamba za ukwati wa mkwati ndi mkwatibwi wake. Choncho tondozedwani mu ulemerero wa Mulungu, podziwa kuti chilichonse chomwe angelo akugwa aloledwa kuchita chimangotumikira zolinga zamuyaya za Mulungu wamuyaya pakuti njira zake zili choncho moti ngakhale adani ake amamvera mawu ake. Koma musalakwitse Yahweh amalamulira pamwambamwamba ndipo palibe chomwe chimachitika chomwe salola mu chisomo chake, chifundo, ndi chiweruzo chamuyaya chotsiriza pa osalapa ndi angelo akugwa. Ngakhale chiweruzo chachikulu chimenecho choloseredwa kalekale chomwe tsopano chatsala pang'ono kugwa pa dziko lapansi mwamphamvu.

Ndipo tsikulo lafika pa inu okondedwa anga ndipo NDIKUBWERA KUDZAKUTENGANI! Ngakhale olanda malo angafune kubisa ulemerero wanga, sindigawana ulemerero wanga ndi wina ndipo choncho kuyesetsa kwa zinthu zofooka ndi zopanda pake n'kopanda phindu kubisa kuonekera kwa Khristu Yesu kwa mkwatibwi wanga ndi thupi langa. Ayi, sindingalephereke kapena kubisidwa ngakhale pang'ono ndipo dziko lonse lapansi lidzadziwa za kuonekera kwanga, ngakhale angelo akugwa adzafuna kubisa kuonekera kwanga kudzera mu nkhondo ndi kuwukira kwawo kwachinyengo kwa alendo a kumlengalenga. Mvetsetsani kuti palibe alendo a kumlengalenga ochokera ku maiko ena onama ozungulira koma angelo akugwa oletsedwa kumwamba kwamuyaya omwe amanamizira chifukwa onse ndi onamizira omwe amabwera kudzanyenga ndi kusocheretsa.

Mvetsetsani kuti kuonekera kwanga sikofanana ndi kubwera kwanga kwachiwiri ndipo ndi chinsinsi chobisika mkati mwa chinsinsi chachikulu cha Mulungu chomwe Ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndinavumbulutsa kwa mtumwi wanga Paulo. Kubwera kwanga kwachiwiri kwadziwika ndipo kwayembekezeredwa kwa mibadwo yambiri tsopano ndipo pamodzi nako pakubwera kuuka kwa olungama. Pa kuonekera kwanga ndimabwera kudzasonkhanitsa oyera mtima anga kwa ine ndekha kuti ndiwalipire ndi kukwatira mkwatibwi wanga ndi kudya ndi alendo anga olemekezeka. Ndi pa kuonekera kwanga pamene ndidzakwatula kupita nako kwa ine ndekha okhulupirika omwe abwera kwa ine mu kulapa kwathunthu ndi kuwatengera ku chitetezo cha kumwamba kufikira chiweruzo pa dziko lapansi chitadutsa, pakuti nthawi za chiweruzo zafika pa dziko lonse lapansi ndipo makamaka pa angelo akugwa.

Mvetsetsani kuti kubwera kwanga kwachiwiri ndi pamene ndimabwera ndi owomboledwa anga ndi 144,000 anga, ndi gulu lonse la kumwamba kudzawononga ufumu wa Satana kotheratu womwe chiwonongeko chake ufumu wake sudzachiranso. Ndipo pa kubwera kwanga kwachiwiri onse adzaona ndipo onse adzadziwa ndipo palibe chinyengo chomwe chingathe kunyenga aliyense pa chochitika ichi. Satana si mfumu ndipo angelo akugwa si milungu koma onamizira omwe amanamizira. Ndipo Satana adzamangidwa ndi kuponyedwa kuphompho kwa zaka chikwi kuti asanyengenso mitundu ya anthu ndipo kufikira atamasulidwa kwa nyengo yake yochepa ndipo kumeneko adzakhala womangidwa m'unyolo waukulu mu mazunzo ndi mantha oopsa a cholowa chake m'nyanja yamoto yoyaka ndi sulufule yomwe sadzathawamo. Ndipo pambuyo pa nyengo yake yochepa adzaponyedwa kotheratu m'nyanja yamoto yoyaka nthawi zonse ya sulufule yoyaka yomwe imayaka malawi amoto otentha kwambiri a buluu. Ndipo pamenepo chiweruzo cha mpando wachifumu waukulu woyera chidzachitika, ngakhale ndithudi Yahweh asanalenge kumwamba kwatsopano ndi dziko lapansi latsopano, ndi kupanga zinthu zonse zatsopano mu kukwaniritsidwa kwamuyaya ndi kwangwiro.

Ndipo momwemo kwalembedwa ndipo momwemo kudzachitika posachedwa kuti ndidzatsika kuchokera kumwamba ndi mfuu wamphamvu ndi mawu a mngelo wamkulu ndi lipenga la Mulungu ndipo kulira kwa lipenga lamphamvulo sikuzengereza ndipo ndi lipenga lomaliza la m'badwo wanga wa chisomo pamene dziko likutembenuka kuyang'anizana ndi chiweruzo chake chomwe chikubwera ndi chowopsa. Ndipo ndidzasonkhanitsa osankhidwa anga kuchokera ku ngodya zinayi za dziko lapansi, ngakhale onse omwe agona mwa Khristu, inde onse omwe agona mwa Khristu osindikizidwa ndi mzimu woyera wa lonjezo. Ndipo zowawa zobereka zomwe sizitha zidzapereka mpata kwa dziko lapansi kubereka omwe agona, koma omwe n'kosatheka kuti imfa iwgwiritse ngakhale sekondi imodzi yochulukirapo. Ndipo kenako inu omwe muli amoyo ndi otsala mudzatengedwa mlengalenga pamodzi nawo kukakumana nane m'mitambo ndipo m'kuthwanima kwa diso mudzaona.

Inde, mudzaona ndi maso anu ulemerero wamuyaya wa Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya, ngakhale Atate wa Kuwala yemwe amalakalaka kukhalapo kwanu ndipo yemwe wakonzekera mphindi iyi kwa muyaya wonse chifukwa mwakhala muli mu mtima wa Yahweh nthawi zonse. Ndipo mudzandiona, Mpulumutsi wanu wamuyaya ndi wosatha yemwe ndinadzipereka ndekha kotheratu mu zowawa pamene ndinadzipereka nsembe kuti mukhale ndi moyo pamaso pa Atate wa Kuwala yemwe wabala kuwala kwa dziko lapansi. Inde, mudzandiona ndi maso anu, ngakhale inu nonse omwe muli ndi chikhulupiriro mwa Ambuye wanu wolemekezedwa yemwe simunamuwonebe ndi maso anu.

Mudzaona ndi maso anu ulemerero wanga wathunthu womwe Yahweh wapereka mu cholowa changa chomwe ndidzagawana kotheratu ndi opambana anga ndipo chomwe ndidzalipira onse omwe amabwera kwa ine ndi ntchito zomwe zimapirira kuyesedwa kwa moto. Zoonadi, zoonadi, mudzaona ndi maso anu gulu la kumwamba, omwe ambiri atumizidwa kudzatumikira omwe ndi olowa m'malo a chipulumutso chamuyaya. Ndipo mudzaima mu ungwiro wathunthu, ngakhale olengedwa mwatsopano mu ungwiro wa kumwamba ndipo mudzaima ndithu ndi kudabwa ndi zomwe mapeto anu amuyaya adzabweretsa ndi zomwe chuma cha ulemerero wa cholowa changa mwa oyera mtima chikutanthauza kwenikweni kwa inu.

Mvetsetsani okondedwa anga, (ngakhale onse omwe simukonda miyoyo yanu mpaka mapeto) ngakhale inu omwe mwatuluka mu machimo anu mu kulapa kwathunthu: Mapeto anu amuyaya ndi chiyambi chanu chamuyaya pomwe mudzaima mu sekondi yanu yoyamba ya moyo wamuyaya ndipo palibe chomwe chidzakuvunditseninso ngakhale pang'ono. Pakuti mudzalengedwa mwatsopano angwiro mu ungwiro woyera ndipo simudzavunditsidwanso chifukwa mudzalengedwa mwatsopano osavunda.

Ndipo kwamuyaya mudzatumikira Yahweh, ndipo kwamuyaya mudzakhala ndi ine kulikonse komwe ndipita. Ndipo ndidzakubwezerani zonse zomwe dzombe ladya ndi zonse zomwe Satana wakuberani. Mudzalipidwa kotheratu chifukwa cha ntchito zanu ndi ntchito yachikondi yomwe mwasonyeza ku dzina langa pakuti mwatumikira oyera mtima ndipo mukutumikira. Tidzadya zonenepa za m'dziko mu ulamuliro wanga wa zaka chikwi pomwe mudzalamulira ndi ine monga mafumu anga ndi ansembe ndipo ulamuliro wolungama udzakhala kwamuyaya.

Ndine amene ndakuyeretsani kotheratu ku tchimo m'magazi anga otetezera.

Ndine Paskha wanu wangwiro yemwe ndadzipereka ndekha kwa Yahweh Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya.

Ndine amene ndatsegula maso a odzichepetsa mu mtima.

Ndine wosula yemwe amavutira makala kuti mufike ku cholowa chanu chathunthu mwa ine, Khristu Yesu.

Ndine amene ndatumiza mawu anga kudzera mwa mtumiki wanga yemwe ndamulera kuyambira unyamata wake ndi kumpatula chifukwa cha zolinga zanga ndi kumubisa mu phodo langa kwa nthawi ngati ino ndipo kudzera mwa mtumiki wanga mawu anga amuyaya atumizidwa. Ndipo mawu a uthenga wabwino wamuyaya adzasungidwa kudutsa mu masoka omwe akubwera ndipo adzapeza maso, makutu, ndi mitima ya omwe atumizidwa chifukwa Ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, ngakhale Khristu Yesu ndikulengeza zinthu izi ndi ulamuliro wathunthu ndi mphamvu ya Yahweh mwiniwake yemwe wagawana mpando wake wachifumu ndi mwana wake wobadwa yekha kuti ndi dzanja langa lamphamvu chilengedwe chibwezeretsedwe kwamuyaya kwa mlengi wake yemwe ndi wodala kwamuyaya, inde ndi Amen.
