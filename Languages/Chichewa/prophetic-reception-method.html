Njira Yolandirira Ulosi: Momwe Ndimalandirira Mauthenga Aulosi

Pa 2 Marichi, 2024, Ambuye Yesu Khristu anayamba kundipatsa mauthenga aulosi oti ndigawane ndi anthu okhudza kuonekera kwake komwe kwayandikira kwa Mpingo wake ndi mitu ina. Maulosi amenewa anabwera kwa ine m'njira yomwe Baibulo limafotokoza ngati pamene "dzanja la Ambuye" likhala pa munthu. Kwa ine, ndi kumva kulemera kwambiri mumzimu mwanga, komwe sikuchoka mpaka nditamaliza uthengawo. Khristu amaika mawu ochepa kapena ziganizo m'maganizo mwanga zomwe sindingathe kuzichotsa ndipo sindingaiwale, ndipo nthawi zambiri ndimadzitumizira ziganizozo ngati meseji kuti ndizikumbukire. Ndikatenga nthawi kukhala pansi ndi kuyamba kulemba, Ambuye amandionetsa Mawuwo mumzimu mwanga pamene ndikulemba: Inde, nthawi zina ndimaona mawuwo m'maso mwanga, ndipo nthawi zambiri ndimamva mawuwo pamene ndikulemba, osati m'makutu koma mumzimu mwanga, ndipo zimamveka m'njira yosatsutsika.

Ndikalakwitsa ndikulemba maganizo anga, mawuwo amasiya kubwera mpaka nditafufuta zomwe ndawonjezera. Ndikaikayikira ndikufufuta chiganizo chomwe Khristu ankafuna kuti chikhale, mawuwo amasiya kubwera mpaka nditabwezeretsa chiganizocho m'mene chinalili, ndipo chiganizocho sichichoka m'maganizo mwanga mpaka nditatero. Posachedwapa, Ambuye wandipatsa mauthenga ozama kwambiri a chiphunzitso, ndipo wanditengera ku malemba enieni. Amaika chiganizo mumzimu mwanga ndipo ndimachilemba; kenako ndimafufuza chiganizocho m'Baibulo langa, ndipo ndimapeza kuti chiganizocho chili m'gawo la lemba. Pamenepo amandisonyeza malemba omwe ndikuyenera kugwira mawu, ndipo ndimawaika m'nkhaniyo, kenako mawuwo amapitirira nthawi yomweyo. Ndimadabwabe kuti Mawu omwe Ambuye amandipatsa amagwirizana bwino lomwe ndi mavesi a malemba omwe amandipatsa kuti ndigwire mawu, ndipo lemba lomwe ndimagwira mawu limayenda bwino ndi nkhaniyo nthawi zambiri m'njira yomwe sindingathe kuikwaniritsa pandekha. Sindinachitepo chinthu chotere m'moyo mwanga, mpaka pano. Nthawi zambiri sindidziwa uthenga wonse mpaka nditabwerera ndikuyang'ana masipelo ndi zizindikiro zopumira. Pamenepo ndimawerenganso, ndipo Ambuye amandipangitsa kusintha zinthu zazing'ono, mwina kuwonjezera chiganizo kuti chimveke bwino, mwina kufufuta liwu ndi kuika lina, ndi zina zotero. Kenako kulemerako kumachoka ndipo ndimadziwa kuti ndamaliza.

Chonde dziwani kuti m'mauthenga onse aulosiwa, sindinasinthepo mutu uliwonse, ngakhale pang'ono, ngakhale kamodzi; ndipo sipanakhalepo kusintha kwakukulu kwa zomwe zili mkati, ngakhale pang'ono, ngakhale kamodzi. Maulosi aatali nthawi zambiri amatenga pafupifupi ola limodzi kapena kucheperapo kulemba, ndipo ndondomeko yowongolera ikhoza kuwonjezera ola lina kapena awiri. Ndimayang'ana chilichonse ndi malemba kuti ndionetsetse kuti sindinawononge chilichonse pakumvetsetsa kwanga kwaumunthu. Pakadali pano sindinatero, koma kuti ndimveke bwino: Ambiri mwa Mawuwa amaposa kumvetsetsa kwanga pamene ndikuwalemba, ndipo ndikufunika kupita kumalemba kuti ndiphunzire zambiri potengera zomwe ndalandira. Mauthengawa sanatsutsanepo ndi malemba ngakhale kamodzi, ngakhale amatsutsa kumvetsetsa kwanga kwa malemba, ndipo inenso ndine wophunzira. Tsopano, ndine mtumiki ndipo ndili ndi zaka pafupifupi 30 m'malemba ndipo ndapanga maulaliki ambiri, ziphunzitso, ndi zina zotero. Ndiloleni ndinene izi kuchokera ku zaka zanga zambiri za chidziwitso: N'zosatheka kwa ine kulemba chilichonse cha mlingo uwu ndi tsatanetsatane mu nthawi yochepa chonchi mwa khama langa ndi kumvetsetsa kwanga. Zimanditengera masiku angapo, nthawi zina sabata kapena kuposerapo, kukonzekera ulaliki wautali, watsatanetsatane, ndipo izi sizofanana, ngakhale pang'ono. Izi sizochokera kwa ine ndipo zimaposa kumvetsetsa kwanga. Mzimu wa ulosi wagwera pa ine, pakuti Umboni wa Yesu ndi mzimu wa ulosi.
