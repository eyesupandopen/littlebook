Kukwaniritsidwa kwa Zinthu Zonse!

Imvani Mawu a Ambuye ndi Mpulumutsi wanu wamoyo, ngakhale Mesiya wanu weniweni, ngakhale Yeshua HaMashiach. Mvetsetsani ndikumvera Mawu anga ana anga pakuti tsopano ndilankhula za kukwaniritsidwa kwa zinthu zonse, pakuti kukwaniritsidwa kwawo kuli pafupi. Choncho mvetsetsani, chifukwa anthu tsopano akana pangano lamtendere la Yahweh ndikukana Yesu wa ku Nazarete (pakuti Ine ndine pangano lanu lamtendere) nyengo zapadziko lapansi tsopano zisintha kuchoka ku chisomo kupita ku chiweruzo, tsopano zisintha kuchoka ku chifundo kupita ku tsoka, tsopano zisintha kuchoka ku mtendere kupita ku nkhondo, tsopano zisintha kuchoka ku kufatsa kupita ku mkwiyo, tsopano zisintha kukhala kubwezera kwathunthu ndi chiwonongeko chotheratu kwa iwo omwe akukhala padziko lapansi. Ngakhale onse omwe akana kundipanga Ine kukhala Ambuye ndikulandira chikondi changa ndi chikhululukiro changa kwa iwo, m'mene ndikadawabwezeretsa kwa mlengi wawo ndikuwapatsa moyo wosatha, kuti asafenso.

Koma m'malo moti munthu wachibadwa abwere kwa ine kwathunthu, ambiri akana modzikuza ndi mwa kupanduka posankha kukhala ndi asatana ndi kupanduka kwawo ndipo mwachibadwa agwirizana nawo kaya mosadziwa, kapena podziwa bwino chisankho chawo. Ndipo chotero iwo omwe asankha mopusa chonchi tsopano adzakhala pansi pa mkwiyo wa Mulungu ndi mkwiyo wa mwanawankhosa womwe watsala pang'ono kuthiridwa kwathunthu. Ndipo ngakhale ambiri panthawiyi akana, khamu la makhamu lidzabwera kwa ine ndikuvomereza Yesu monga Ambuye ndipo adzaitana pa dzina la Mesiya wawo mpaka mapeto. Ndipo chotero mwa mphamvu ya Yeshua HaMashiach adzapulumutsidwa. Koma ambiri adzawonongeka, ngakhale onse omwe mayina awo sanalembedwe m'Buku la Moyo wophedwa kuyambira pachiyambi cha dziko lapansi ndipo chotero chidzakhala chisankho chawo.

Ndipo chotero mofanana ndi momwe kusintha kumeneku kukuchitikira padziko lapansi m'chiweruzo, mtendere wakhazikika kumwamba ndipo nkhondo tsopano idzawononga dziko lapansi kotheratu. Kunali nkhondo kumwamba ndipo woneneza wa abale aponyedwa pansi, aponyedwa pansi, ndipo chinjoka chachikulu chofiira tsopano chamangiriridwa padziko lapansi ndipo chikuyembekezera chiweruzo chake ndipo chiweruzo chake sichichedwa. M'kugonjetsedwa kotheratu adzatumikira zolinga zake m'makonzedwe amuyaya a Yahweh, pakuti ngakhale adani ake amamvera mawu ake, ndipo chotero wapatsidwa m'njira yochepa kwambiri ndipo ufumu wake udzawonongedwa kotheratu. Taonani! Njoka yakale ija, ngakhale chinjoka chachikulu chofiira chija, tsopano chaponyedwa pansi padziko lapansi, ndipo chili ndi mkwiyo waukulu chifukwa chikudziwa kuti chili ndi nthawi yochepa! Ndipo chotero Ine, Yeshua HaMashiach ndikulangiza aliyense ndi onse kuti athamangire kumveka kwa mawu anga ndikuitana pa dzina langa lamphamvu mpaka mapeto pakuti iwo omwe sakonda miyoyo yawo mpaka mapeto adzadzuka ku ulemerero wamuyaya, chimwemwe, ndi mphoto ndipo mphoto yawo ndi yaikulu! Pakuti onse omwe aitanira pa dzina langa mpaka mapeto adzapulumutsidwa, m'chilankhulo chilichonse chomwe angaitane ndipo Ine Yesu Khristu, yemwe ndinenso Yeshua HaMashiach, ndikulengeza mawu awa!

Ndipo chotero mapangano ndi malonjezo akale adzakwaniritsidwa, ngakhale mapangano onse a nthawi zonse pakuti ndiwo uthenga wabwino wosatha. Ndipo pamene nkhondo ikukhazikika padziko lapansi, mtendere tsopano wakhazikitsidwa kumwamba komwe kumwamba tsopano kwakonzedwa kulandira okhulupirika mwa Khristu, ngakhale omwe amwalira ali okhulupirika mwa Khristu, ndi omwe ali amoyo ndikutsala ndikukhala miyoyo yawo mwa Khristu. Pakuti onse omwe amayenda mwa lamulo la mzimu wa moyo mwa Khristu Yesu m'nthawi yanga ya chisomo adzasonkhanitsidwa ndikulandira mphoto yawo yamuyaya ndipo adzamvetsetsa ndipo adzaikidwa m'madongosolo awo kuti akwaniritse cholinga chamuyaya cha Yahweh chomwe analengedwera mwatsopano mwa Khristu. Ndipo chotero ukwati wa Mkwati ndi Mkwatibwi tsopano udzachitika, ndipo kudzakhala chikondwerero chachikulu kumwamba mu ulemerero ndi ukulu wamuyaya!

Pakuti kuyambira pachiyambi, Mulungu analenga Adamu, ndipo kuchokera kwa Adamu analenga Hava kuti akhale mnzake wamuyaya ndi kukhala mayi wa amoyo onse ndi kuti abalane ndi kuchuluka. Analamulidwa kuti pa mitengo yonse ya m'munda wa Edeni, akhoza kudya momasuka ku mtengo uliwonse kupatula mtengo wodziwa chabwino ndi choipa. Ndipo chotero ndi chiyeso chochenjera cha njoka Hava ananyengedwa, ndipo anatenga chipatso choletsedwa ndikudya, ndipo anapatsa mwamuna wake Adamu, ndipo anadya modziwa ndipo motero tchimo linaipitsa anthu ndipo m'kufa akhala akumwalira kuyambira pamenepo. Ndipo chotero Adamu ndi Hava sanakwaniritse maitanidwe awo apamwamba olamulira ndi kugonjetsa dziko lapansi m'chilungamo ndi kubalana ndi kuchuluka ndi kuphimba dziko lapansi m'chilungamo koma m'malo mwake anachimwa ndikukhala malipiro a tchimo mpaka malipirowo anafika pachimake m'kufa kwawo ndipo m'kufa anthu akhala akumwalira kuyambira pamenepo. Ndipo m'kuuka kwa olungama adzalandira mphoto yawo ndi malipiro awo pakuti anali okhulupirika mpaka mapeto.

Ndipo chotero zili choncho kuti Yahweh, m'kudziwiratu kwake kwamuyaya, anadziwa zisanachitike kuti anthu adzisankhira okha tchimo ndi imfa kuyambira pachiyambi. Ndipo El Shaddai, ngakhale m'chikondi chake chachikulu chomwe amakondera anthu, (ndi chilengedwe chake chonse), anapereka chisomo ndi chifundo chosamvetsetseka pamene analengeza kuti adzaika chidani pakati pa mbewu ya asatana ndi mbewu ya mkazi. Mbewu ya asatana inaluma chidendene changa pa Kalvare pokwaniritsa Mawu a Yahweh ndipo tsopano ndisuntha kuti ndiphwanye kotheratu mutu wa njoka pakuti ndiwo tsogolo lake ndi onse omwe amatsatira, ndipo monga kwalembedwa, chotero kupitiriza kuchitidwa mpaka kumaliza.

Komabe, Mulungu wasankha m'nzeru zake zopanda malire kuvumbulutsa mapulani ake a chisomo, chifundo, ndi mtendere kaamba ka chiwombolo chathunthu cha anthu pang'onopang'ono, ndipo komabe nthawi zonse wakhala akupereka chiyembekezo chachikulu kwa olungama kuti tsiku lina posachedwa adzaukitsidwa ku moyo watsopano kuchokera kwa akufa kuti asafeanso, ndipo adzakhala m'kuchuluka ndi mtendere masiku onse a miyoyo yawo ndipo masiku amenewo sadzakhala ndi mapeto. M'mibadwo yonse okhulupirika amwalira ndi chikhulupiriro chathunthu mwa Yahweh kuti akwaniritse malonjezo ake ndipo amwalira akukhulupirira mapangano a Yahweh monga chiyembekezo chawo cha chipulumutso ndipo mapangano onse a Yahweh adzakwaniritsidwa kwathunthu pamaso pa Khristu Yesu, ngakhale Ine, Ambuye ndi Mpulumutsi wanu wouka. Ndipo chotero anakhulupirira Mulungu ndipo kwa iwo kunawerengedwa kukhala chilungamo ngakhale kwa onse omwe anakhulupirira ndikukhala ndi chiyembekezo mu mbewu yolonjezedwa ya mkazi.

Ndipo chotero dongosolo la Yahweh la mibadwo kaamba ka chiwombolo chathunthu cha anthu nthawi zonse lakhala lozikidwa pa ine, Khristu Yesu ngakhale Yesu wa ku Nazarete yemwe alinso Yeshua HaMashiach pakuti Ine ndine Ambuye wa Ambuye wokwera kumwamba yemwe ndine mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu ndipo yemwe wabweretsa chipulumutso chamuyaya kwa Myuda ndi Wamitundu mofanana, ndipo ndidzapulumutsabe Yakobo kwa omwe alowa m'dziko lolonjezedwa ndipo chiwonongeko chawo sichichedwa. Ine ndine yemwe mkango udzagona pansi ndi mwanawankhosa pakuti mwa ine mkango wa fuko la Yuda ndi mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu adzafika pakukwaniritsidwa kwathunthu. Pakuti Ine ndine mkango wa fuko la Yuda ndipo Ine ndine Mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu pakuti Ine ndine Mfumu ya Mafumu ndi Ambuye wa Ambuye ndipo Ine ndine Mkulu wa Ansembe kwamuyaya monga mwa dongosolo la Melikizedeki.

Ndipo chotero Adamu ndi Hava anakhala mokhulupirika kupita mtsogolo pamaso pa Ambuye Mulungu wawo, ndipo anabalana ndikuchuluka, komabe Satana akadapanga chiwembu china ndi kupanduka kwa alonda ogwa. Anagwirizana ndi kupanduka kwa asatana ndipo kuti akwaniritse zilakolako zawo anasiya ulemerero wamuyaya wa malo awo oyamba, omwe analengedwera, kuti adziipitse ndi akazi, ngakhale ndi thupi ndi magazi, pakuti akadakhala mu ulemerero wamuyaya koma m'malo mwake anasankha chilango chawo chamuyaya kuti akwaniritse zilakolako zawo pa mtengo ndi imfa ya chilengedwe chomwe chinalipo panthawiyo.

Ndipo chotero angelo ogwawa anaphunzitsa munthu wogwa momwe angadziwonongere yekha kudzera m'mzera wa Kaini ndi kudzera m'chidziwitso choletsedwa ndi machenjerero omwe anaphunzitsa anthu. Anadziyerekezera ngati milungu ndikufuna kupembedzedwa ndipo ANATENGA akazi mwa mphamvu kwa onse omwe anawasankha, ndipo akazi ambiri anadzipereka okha motsogozedwa ndi asatana kuti ayese ndi kukopa alonda m'machimo awo a chilakolako omwe anadzimanga okha kwamuyaya ku temberero lawo pakuti anapanga matemberero pakati pawo. Izi zinachitika m'masiku a Yaredi ndi zonyansa zawo, ngakhale Anefilimu awo anafalikira padziko lapansi mu ufumu wawo wapadziko lonse lapansi pa nthawi yomwe amati ndi nthawi yawo yagolide.

Ndipo alonda ndi mbewu yawo anaipitsa dziko lapansi ndi zonse zomwe zinalimo ndipo kusintha kwachilendo kwa majini kwamitundu yonse kunapangidwa ndikuchuluka padziko lapansi pakuti anachimwira thupi lonse ndipo zilakolako zawo zinalibe malire, ndipo anadziyerekezera kukhala milungu, ngakhale milungu yomwe imalenga koma kwenikweni sali kanthu koma onyenga omwe anatenga mbali m'kusakaniza mitundu kwakukulu pakati pa zamoyo. Ndipo chotero dziko lapansi linaipitsidwa kotheratu ndipo linalamulidwa ndi Anefilimu, omwe anadya anthu ndi zopeza za anthu. Ndipo onse omwe anatsala osadetsedwa pa nthawi ya Nowa (pakuti Nowa anali wolungama ndi wowongoka ndipo anayenda ndi Yahweh ndipo anapeza chisomo pamaso pa Yahweh) anali miyoyo isanu ndi itatu yopulumutsidwa yamoyo ndi nyama zomwe Yahweh anabweretsa ku Chingalawa kaamba ka chipulumutso cha mitundu yawo. Ndipo chotero Mulungu anasefukira dziko lapansi ndipo kuipitsidwa konse kwa dziko lomwe linalipo panthawiyo kunawonongeka, ndipo alonda alibe mtendere, kapena chikhululukiro ndipo kwamuyaya ndi mpaka kalekale kudzakhala chiweruzo chawo chaukali pakuti ndi oyenera. Ndipo osati alonda okha, koma onse omwe amadzigwirizanitsa okha ku kupanduka kwa asatana adzavutika m'njira yotere ndikubwezeredwa kwamuyaya m'nyanja yaikulu yamoto wa sulufule wabuluu yomwe imayaka malawi otentha kwambiri ndi oyera kwambiri a chilango.

Pambuyo pa chigumula, Mulungu anakhazikitsa dziko lomwe lilipo tsopano, ngakhale kukhazikitsidwa poyamba m'chilungamo. Ndipo Yahweh analowa m'pangano ndi Nowa, pomwe Yahweh anaitana Nowa kuti adzutse chilungamo padziko lapansi ndikubalana ndi kuchuluka kuti mbewu yolonjezedwa ya mkazi ibwere. Yahweh analonjeza kuti sadzasefukiranso dziko lapansi ndipo ngakhale pali chikhalidwe choipa cha anthu, Mulungu anagwira ntchito ndi anthu kuteteza ndi kudalitsa mibadwo yolungama padziko lapansi yomwe Ine Yeshua HaMashiach ndinatulukamo.

Mulungu anawadalitsa kuti abalane ndi kuchuluka ndi kudzaza dziko lapansi, ndipo Mulungu analonjeza munthu ndi nyama kuti sadzawononganso thupi lonse padziko lapansi ndi madzi ndipo El Shaddai sadzasefukiranso dziko lonse lapansi koma adzasunga dziko lapansi, ndikusunga anthu ndi mbewu yake, ndipo dziko lomweli lasungidwa kuyambira tsiku limenelo la pangano pomwe Atate wa Kuwala anaika utawaleza wake kumwamba monga chizindikiro ndi chikumbutso cha pangano lake ndi Nowa. Pakuti izi iwo mofunitsitsa sakhala nazo chidwi, kuti ndi mawu a Mulungu kumwamba kunaliko kale, ndi dziko lapansi loima m'madzi ndi m'madzi: M'mene dziko lomwe linalipo panthawiyo, litasefukira ndi madzi, linawonongeka: Koma kumwamba ndi dziko lapansi, zomwe zilipo tsopano, ndi mawu omwewo zasungidwa, zosungidwira moto kufikira tsiku la chiweruzo ndi chiwonongeko cha anthu osapembedza.

Ndipo chotero Yahweh anadzutsa Abulamu kukhala Abrahamu, ndipo analonjeza Abulamu kuti dzina lake silidzakhala Abulamu koma Abrahamu ndi kuti adzamupanga kukhala Atate wa mitundu yambiri ndi kuti adzapatsa Abrahamu ndi mbewu yake dziko la Kanani kukhala cholowa chamuyaya ndi kuti Yahweh adzakhala Mulungu wawo! Ndipo chotero Abrahamu anakhala Atate wa mitundu yambiri, zomwe Yahweh analonjeza m'pangano. Tsopano, kwa Abrahamu ndi mbewu yake malonjezo anapangidwa. Sananene kuti, Ndi kwa mbewu, monga za zambiri; koma monga za imodzi, Ndi kwa mbewu yako, yomwe ndi Khristu. Ndipo chotero wolungama adzakhala ndi moyo ndi chikhulupiriro!

Ndipo chotero ana anagwa mu ukapolo ku Igupto pambuyo poti ana a lonjezo anapita kumeneko pansi pa chitetezo ndi chisamaliro cha Yosefe pakuti Yosefe analamulira mwachilungamo pansi pa Farao yemwe anali wamkulu kuposa Yosefe pampando wachifumu, koma Yosefe anatumikira NDINE wamkulu! Kunauka farao yemwe sanadziwe Yosefe, ndipo namsongole amenewa anapondereza ndikuyesetsa kupha mzera wa Khristu kachiwiri, ngakhale ana a lonjezo. Ndipo Yahweh anadzutsa mpulumutsi wa Chihebri kuchokera m'nyumba ya Farao, ngakhale iye yemwe anatulutsidwa m'madzi ndikupulumutsidwa wamoyo ndi chisomo cha Yahweh. Anali Mose mwiniwake yemwe adzatsogolera ana a lonjezo kuchoka mu ukapolo kupita ku ufulu waulemerero wa dziko lolonjezedwa, pakuti iwo ndi ana a lonjezo ndipo ndidzawaukitsa ku tulo tawo ndikupereka mphoto kwa okhulupirika ndi olungama mochuluka, ngakhale omwe anakhala okhulupirika mpaka mapeto. Ndipo chotero Mose anapulumutsa ana a Israyeli m'manja mwa owapondereza ndipo onse omwe anawathamangitsa anamira m'nyanja, pakuti ndani angathe kuima motsutsana ndi El Shaddai?

Ndipo chotero Yahweh anatsogolera Mose ndi ana a lonjezo ku Phiri la Sinai komwe Yahweh anapereka malangizo, malamulo ndi malemba omwe ali m'malamulo kwa Mose kuti ana onse amvere. Ndipo madalitso ndi matemberero anakhazikitsidwa kaamba ka kumvera ndi kusamvera. Ndipo pamene Yahweh anapereka m'pangano kwa Mose malamulo omwe ali m'malemba kaamba ka kumvera, Mose ndiye anavomereza pangano kukhala Chipangano mwa kukhetsa magazi. Ndipo Mose anatenga theka la magazi, ndikuika m'mabeseni, ndipo theka la magazi anawaza pa Guwa Lansembe. Ndipo anatenga buku la pangano, ndikuwerenga pamaso pa anthu: ndipo anati, Zonse zomwe Ambuye wanena, tidzachita, ndipo tidzamvera. Ndipo Mose anatenga magazi ndikuwaza pa anthu, ndipo anati, Taonani magazi a Pangano lomwe Ambuye wapanga ndi inu, ponena za mawu onsewa. Kuonjezera apo, anawaza ndi magazi Chihema, ndi ziwiya zonse za Utumiki. Ndipo pafupifupi zinthu zonse mwa Lamulo ziyeretsedwa ndi magazi: ndipo popanda kukhetsa magazi palibe chikhululukiro cha machimo.

Ndipo chotero mbiri inachitika monga kwalembedwa, ndipo m'kupita kwa nthawi mtundu wa Israyeli unakhazikitsidwa kwathunthu, komabe panalibe malo okhala a Yahweh pakati pa anthu. Ndipo Yahweh anadzutsa Mfumu yolungama, ngakhale munthu wapamtima pa Mulungu! M'kupita kwa nthawi Yahweh anatumiza Natani Mneneri kwa Davide ndikumuuza kuti pamene masiku ako akwaniritsidwa, ndipo udzagona ndi makolo ako, ndidzadzutsa mbewu yako pambuyo pako, yomwe idzatuluka m'matumbo ako, ndipo ndidzakhazikitsa ufumu wake. Iye adzamanga nyumba ya dzina langa, ndipo ndidzakhazikitsa mpando wachifumu wa ufumu wake kwamuyaya. Ine ndidzakhala atate wake, ndipo iye adzakhala mwana wanga. Ndipo nyumba yako ndi ufumu wako zidzakhazikitsidwa kwamuyaya pamaso pako: mpando wako wachifumu udzakhazikitsidwa kwamuyaya. Malinga ndi mawu onsewa, ndi malinga ndi masomphenya onsewa, chotero Natani analankhula kwa Davide.

Ndipo chotero anthu atsatira Yahweh kuchokera ku pangano kupita ku pangano kuwonjezera pa pangano lomwe linavomerezedwa ndi nsembe za nyama ndipo pangano linawonjezedwa ku chipangano. Komabe, pa khama lalikulu la anthu iye mwa luso lake sakanatha kufika pa chilungamo cha Mulungu, ngakhale lamulo lolungama ndi loyera la Mose lomwe ndimalikonda ndikulikwaniritsa. Ngati wina aliyense atcha lamulo la Mose ndi mapangano ambiri a Yahweh kukhala otembereredwa, atembereredwe. Pakuti lamulo ndi labwino ndi lolungama ndi loyera ndipo malonjezo onse a Yahweh ndi inde ndi ameni. Komabe, lamulo linaperekedwa monga mphunzitsi kuwabweretsa kwa Khristu. Pakuti Ine, Yesu wa ku Nazarete ndine yemwe Yahweh wapereka kuti akwaniritse zomwe anthu sangathe chifukwa cha tchimo loperekedwa kuchokera ku mbadwo kupita ku mbadwo kuchokera ku magazi a Adamu, omwe aipitsidwa kwamuyaya. Pakuti chomwe lamulo silikanatha kuchita popeza linali lofooka chifukwa cha thupi: chimenecho anachita Mulungu, ndikutumiza mwana wake m'chifanizo cha thupi lauchimo.

Choncho m'kukwaniritsidwa kwa nthawi Ine, Yesu wa ku Nazarete, ndinabadwa mwa mkazi malinga ndi zofunikira za lamulo la Mose ndi m'kukwaniritsidwa kwa mapangano, ndipo Ine ndine mbewu yolonjezedwa ya mkazi yonenedweratu ndi Yahweh mwiniwake mibadwo yambiri yapitayo. Pakuti Mulungu anakonda Dziko lapansi kotero kuti anapereka mwana wake wobadwa yekha, kuti aliyense wokhulupirira mwa iye asawonongeke, koma akhale ndi moyo wosatha. Ndipo chotero Ine ndine mwana wobadwa yekha wa Mulungu, ngakhale mbewu ya lonjezo yomwe aneneri ananenera kuyambira kale mpaka tsopano. Ndipo Ine ndine yemwe waikidwa kaamba ka kukwaniritsidwa kwa zinthu zonse ndipo mwa ine, Khristu Yesu, zinthu zonse ndi malonjezo onse adzafika pakukwaniritsidwa ndipo palibe lonjezo limodzi lomwe lidzasiyidwe losachitidwa, koma onse adzakwaniritsidwa nthawi zonse.

Pakuti kwalembedwa mu Ahebri pomwe ndinapangitsa Paulo kulemba: Pakuti ngati magazi a Ng'ombe, ndi a mbuzi, ndi phulusa la ng'ombe yaikazi yowaza odetsedwa, ziyeretsa ku kuyeretsedwa kwa thupi: Koposa kotani nanga magazi a Khristu, yemwe kudzera mwa Mzimu wamuyaya, anadzipereka yekha wopanda chilema kwa Mulungu, adzayeretsa chikumbumtima chanu ku ntchito zakufa, kutumikira Mulungu wamoyo? Ndipo chifukwa cha ichi iye ndi Mkhalapakati wa Chipangano Chatsopano, kuti mwa imfa, kaamba ka chiwombolo cha zolakwa zomwe zinali pansi pa Chipangano choyamba, iwo omwe aitanidwa, alandire lonjezo la cholowa chamuyaya.

Choncho mvetsetsani kuti mwa ine, Khristu Yesu, malonjezo akale amasungidwa ndipo mapangano akale amasungidwa ndipo chipangano chakale chimasungidwa ndi kukwaniritsidwa pakuti ndine kukwaniritsidwa kwawo kwangwiro. Ndipo komabe pali malonjezo oti akwaniritsidwe ndipo tsopano ndi nthawi yakukwaniritsidwa. Pakuti zili choncho nthawi, pamene ine mwadzidzidzi ndidzadzutsa ku tulo awo amene agona mwa Khristu, ndipo ine mwadzidzidzi ndidzasonkhanitsa awo amene ali amoyo ndi otsala kuti akomane ndi ine m'mitambo ndipo mwadzidzidzi tidzakhala ogwirizana m'chipinda cha mpando wachifumu cha Yahweh mwiniwake. Pakuti inu Mpingo wanga ngakhale mkwatibwi wanga ndinunso kukwaniritsidwa kwa lonjezo la pangano ndipo mwabadwanso ndi mbewu yosawonongeka ndikuwomboledwa ndi malonjezo ambiri mu Chipangano changa Chatsopano, chomwe ndi chipangano chatsopano chomwe chimaphatikizapo lonjezo lililonse la mapangano akale ndipo ngakhale kuwonjezera malonjezo aakulu ndi amtengo wapatali omwe inu mukuwomboledwa ku moyo wosatha ndi mphoto yosatha ndipo mpaka kalekale tidzakhala limodzi.

Mvetsetsani kuti miyoyo yomwe mwakhala m'thupi, ngakhale inu okhulupirika anga ndi osankhidwa, miyoyo imeneyo yatha pakuwomboledwa kwanu kwathunthu. Mvetsetsani kuti mukudziwika bwino ndi ine, Khristu Yesu, pamlingo uliwonse. Pamene ndinapachikidwa chifukwa cha machimo anu, inunso munapachikidwa pamodzi ndi ine. Pamene ndinafa pamtengo waukaliwo, inunso munafa pamodzi ndi ine. Pamene ndinaukitsidwa ku moyo pambuyo pa masiku atatu ndi usiku utatu mumtima mwa dziko lapansi inunso munaukitsidwa ku moyo watsopano, ndipo pamene ndinakwera kumwamba kwa kumwamba inunso munakwera pamodzi ndi ine ndipo choncho mwakhala m'malo akumwamba. Pakuti ndine Paskha wanu wangwiro, ndipo ndine wolowa m'malo mwanu amene anatenga malo anu m'chiweruzo ndi chilango choyenera. Ndipo Ine, Yesu wa ku Nazarete, ndinakuwombolani mofunitsitsa ku temberero la chilamulo chifukwa cha thupi lanu lochimwa simungathe kukwaniritsa chilamulo ndi khama lanu.

Mvetsetsani kuti sindinabwere kudzawononga chilamulo, kapena mapangano, koma kukwaniritsa zonse mu nthawi yakukwaniritsidwa. Ngati wina aliyense atcha chilamulo cha Mose ndi mapangano ambiri a Yahweh kukhala otembereredwa, atembereredwe. Pakuti chilamulo ndi chabwino ndi cholungama ndi choyera, ndipo malonjezo onse a Yahweh ndi inde ndi ameni. Komabe, chilamulo chinaperekedwa ngati mphunzitsi kuti awabweretse kwa Khristu. Pakuti Ine, Yesu wa ku Nazarete, ndine amene Yahweh wapereka kuti akwaniritse zomwe anthu sangathe chifukwa cha tchimo lomwe limaperekedwa kuchokera ku mbadwo ndi mbadwo kuchokera ku magazi a Adamu, omwe awonongeka mpaka kalekale. Pakuti chimene chilamulo sichikanatha kuchita chifukwa chinali chofooka chifukwa cha thupi: Mulungu anachita zimenezo, ndipo anatumiza mwana wake m'chifanizo cha thupi lochimwa.

Lonjezo lililonse latsala pang'ono kukwaniritsidwa kwathunthu mwa ine Khristu Yesu ndipo ndidzalowa m'pangano la ukwati ndi Mkwatibwi wanga kuti pamodzi adzalamulira ndi ine mu cholowa changa ndipo adzatero, motsogozedwa ndi ine ndi ine, kuchiritsa dziko lapansi ndikupita ndikulamulira ngati ansembe ndi mafumu padziko lapansi ndipo pangano lililonse lidzakwaniritsidwa kuchokera kwa Adamu mpaka kwa Mose Pamaso pa Yesu Khristu. Awa ndi malonjezo a moyo wosatha omwe adzakwaniritsidwe mwa olungama, ngakhale onse omwe amapangidwanso ngati munthu mmodzi watsopano mwa Khristu Yesu. Pakuti onse adzapangidwanso kwathunthu ndikupatsidwa mphoto yamuyaya chifukwa cha gawo lawo ndipo zonse zidzakhala inde ndi ameni. Pakuti yod iliyonse, ndi munga uliwonse wa yod udzakwaniritsidwa kwathunthu ndipo nthawi zonse.

Mvetsetsani kuti ndinu kachisi wa Mulungu wamoyo, Mpingo wanga wokhulupirika ndipo ndakupangani ngati nyumba yopangidwa yopanda manja kuti mukhale malo okhala amuyaya a Mulungu wosatha, ngakhale a Yahweh Atate wanu. Ndipo ndidzakwatira mkwatibwi wanga ndipo ndidzakhala nawo mpaka kalekale m'pangano lamuyaya, kuti kumene ine ndili, iwonso adzakhala komweko, ndipo mpaka kalekale adzakhala ngati ine, Khristu Yesu Ambuye wawo wamuyaya. Ndipo mvetsetsani okhulupirika anga kuti nthawi zakulekana zatsala pang'ono kutha ndipo sipadzakhalanso kulekana pakati pa Yahweh ndi ana ake okondedwa ndipo sipadzakhalanso kulekana pakati pa Khristu Yesu Ambuye wanu wamuyaya ndi omwe ndimawakonda kwambiri. Mvetsetsani kuti tidzayenda ngati munthu mmodzi ndipo sipadzakhala tchimo kapena chopinga. Mudzachita ntchito zambiri zamphamvu zomwe sizinalowe m'mitima ya anthu. Mudzakhala mafumu anga amphamvu ndi ansembe omwe ine ndine Mfumu ya Mafumu ndi Ambuye wa Ambuye ndipo tidzalamulira dziko lapansi m'chilungamo.

Ndipo ambiri adzaukitsidwa ku moyo kumapeto kwa sabata la makumi asanu ndi awiri la Danieli ndipo onse adzapatsidwa mphoto malinga ndi kukhulupirika kwawo. Ndipo onse amene anamwalira okhulupirika poyembekezera kuti Ambuye ndi Mesiya wawo sadzasiya miyoyo yawo m'manda adzadzuka ku moyo watsopano ndi kubwezeretsedwa kwamuyaya. Pakuti ndidzawalumikiza ndi okondedwa wawo ndipo ndidzawabwezera zomwe dzombe ladya ndipo ndidzawabwezera zomwe Satana anawaba panthawi yomwe anali alendo padziko lapansi akuyembekezera kuwomboledwa kwawo kwathunthu kuchokera ku dziko lapansi logwa ndi loipali.

Ndipo momwemonso uthenga wabwino wosatha ndipo nthawi zonse zinthu zonse zidzakwaniritsidwa m'chilungamo, chiyero, ndi choonadi. Pakuti palibe chomwe chidzalowe m'zipata za ufumu wanga chomwe chili chodetsedwa kapena choipa kapena chomwe chimakonda ndikupanga mabodza koma m'choonadi ndi chilungamo chosatha ulamuliro wanga udzakhala. Choncho imvani mawu a Yeshua HaMashiach ngakhale onse omwe mawuwa atumizidwa kwa iwo. Ambuye wanu wosatha sanaiwale malonjezo Akale kapena mapangano alionse sanakhale opanda pake. Koma zonse zidzakwaniritsidwa ndipo ndithudi, zambiri zakwaniritsidwa kale choncho yang'anani ku zinthu zimenezo kuti mukhale ndi chiyembekezo. Ndipo m'chilungamo ndi mtendere tidzakhala mogwirizana ndi chilengedwe cha Mulungu ndipo kudzakhala mtendere wokhalitsa ndi wosatha kwa ana a lonjezo, ndipo adzatumikirana wina ndi mzake, ndipo adzanditumikira ine, ndipo adzatumikira Yahweh ndipo mumtendere ndi mgwirizano chilengedwe cha Yahweh chidzabwezeretsedwa.

Ndipo pamene m'badwo wanga wa chisomo ukutha m'badwo womaliza watsala pang'ono kufika ana anga, ngakhale tsiku lomalizalo pamene tonse tidzapuma ku ntchito zathu ndi mavuto athu, ndipo tidzasangalala ndi mafuta a dziko lapansi pamene tikupuma mu tsiku la mpumulo ngakhale tsiku la Sabata la mgwirizano lidzakhalapo. Kwakhala masiku asanu ndi limodzi a ntchito, pakuti tsiku limodzi kwa Ambuye lili ngati zaka chikwi ndipo zaka chikwi ngati tsiku limodzi.
