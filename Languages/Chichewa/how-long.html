Mpaka Liti?

Mawu a Ambuye operekedwa ndi Yesu Khristu.

Mpaka liti, mpaka liti, anthu anga adzanyalanyaza kuitana kwanga? Adzamamatira ku machimo awo mpaka liti? Adzakhala m'zosangalatsa za tchimo mpaka liti? Lapani. Lapani. Lapani! Bwererani kwa ine ndi mtima wanu wonse. Bwererani kwa Yehova Mulungu wanu ndi m'manja mwanga otambasulidwa!

Kwa Mkwatibwi wanga: Ndakupatsani moyo, koma moyo wanu wabisika mwa ine; ndipo pamene ndidzawonekera, inunso mudzawonekera m'moyo umene ndakupatsani. Mwalandira kwaulere, koma zinanditengera mtengo waukulu kukuwombolani. Kodi mudzagwiritsa ntchito chisomo changa ngati nthawi yogona? Kapena mudzagwiritsa ntchito chisomo changa ngati nthawi yodziyeretsa mwa mphamvu ya magazi anga amene ndinakukhetselani pa Kalvare? Kodi mudzalandira kuitana kwanga kuti mulalikire ku dziko lomwe likufa ndi kupulumutsa abale anu ambiri? Kapena mudzanyalanyaza kuitana kwanga ndikusankha zosangalatsa za tchimo mu nyengo yomalizayi?

Kwa onse, ndikuti: Bwererani kwa ine ndi mtima wanu wonse; lapani ndi moyo wanu wonse! Funafunani nkhope yanga ndikulandira chikhululuko changa, pakuti tsiku la Ambuye layandikira, monga momwe ndawachenjezera anthu anga. Sindidzakhala chete, ndipo ndikukulimbikitsani kuti mutsegule pakamwa panu kuti mupulumutse omwe muwakonda ndi omwe ndidzawabweretsa kwa inu. Kumbukirani chikondi chanu choyamba, ndipo bwerani kwa ine lipenga lisanaliye.
