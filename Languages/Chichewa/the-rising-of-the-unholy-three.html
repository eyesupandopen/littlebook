Kutuluka kwa Atatu Osayera!

Ndipo nthawi yatha ndipo kuonekera kwanga kukuyandikira! Tsopano ndikuuzani ndondomeko ya zochitika zomwe zatsala pang'ono kukwaniritsidwa, choncho tcherani khutu kuti mumve mawu a Mesiya wanu, ngakhale Yeshua HaMashiach, ngakhale mawu a Mbuye wa Ambuye, Mfumu ya Mafumu, Mesiya, ndi mkulu wa ansembe wamuyaya monga mwa dongosolo la Melikizedeki. Mwina kunyada kwanu kwa dziko kudzatseka mitima yanu kwa ine, inu Babulo Wachinsinsi: ngakhale maiko a United States awa. Pakuti mukuganiza kuti mwakhala ngati mfumukazi ndipo palibe tsoka lomwe lidzakugwereni, ndipo mukuti mwa inu nokha, sindine wamasiye! Koma chiwonongeko chanu chidzafika mu ola limodzi ndipo simudzauka nkomwe kapena utsogoleri wanu sudzasautsanso dziko lapansi la Yehova, pakuti gawo lowirikiza la chiweruzo lalengezedwa ndi Yehova, ndipo chimene Yehova walengeza palibe amene angachisinthe.

Tangoganizani, ngati mungathe, dziko lopanda Chikhristu ndi mayiko achikhristu. Sipadzakhalanso Mipingo ndipo Chikhristu chidzakhala choletsedwa padziko lonse lapansi ndipo nthawi ya Mpingo yafika kumapeto ngakhale nthawi yanga yachisomo yomwe inalengezedwa ndi atumwi ndi aneneri anga m'zaka za zana loyamba, ndipo anabzala mbewu pomwe mutha kupeza pothawirapo mu nthawi zino zamavuto, ngakhale Mawu anga oyera ndi zinsinsi za Mulungu! Ndikofunikira kumasulira zochitikazi ndi chilankhulo chenicheni chomwe chinagwiritsidwa ntchito ndi Yohane Wovumbulutsa, pakuti anati: Ndipo ndinaima pamchenga wa nyanja: ndipo ndinaona chilombo chikutuluka m'nyanja, chokhala ndi mitu isanu ndi iwiri, ndi nyanga khumi, ndipo panyanga zake panali akorona khumi, ndipo pamitu yake, dzina lamwano. Ndiponso: Ndipo ndinaona chilombo china chikutuluka pansi, ndipo chinali ndi nyanga ziwiri ngati mwanawankhosa, ndipo chinalankhula ngati chinjoka. Ndiponso: chinjoka chinachipatsa mphamvu yake, ndi mpando wake, ndi ulamuliro waukulu. Ndipo momwemo mudzakhala kutuluka kwa atatu osayera, chilombo chochokera kunyanja, chilombo chochokera pansi chomwe chilinso mneneri wonyenga ndi Mesiya wonyenga, ndi chinjoka chomwe chimawapatsa ulamuliro waukulu.

Palibe wotsutsakhristu mmodzi wamkulu, ngakhale mizimu ya wotsutsakhristu yapita m'dziko lapansi ndikusocheretsa ambiri, koma zilombo ziwiri ndi chinjoka. Chimodzi cholamulira nyanja, kapena mitundu ya anthu akunja, ndi chimodzi cholamulira mayiko oyera, yemwe akuchokera kufuko la Dani ndipo ndi womanga kachisi wonga chilombo. Chilombo chochokera kunyanja chidzalamulira mitundu, kapena goyim kudzera m'malamulo a Nowa, ndipo ali kale m'malo mwake, ndipo onse awiri ndi otsutsakhristu mwachilengedwe. Wotsutsakhristu amatsutsana nane, Khristu Yesu ndipo ali m'malo mwa Khristu Yesu komanso. Pakuti taonani! Chinsinsi cha kusayeruzika chapita patsogolo kwambiri ndipo Satana akudikira kunyamuka kwanu, Mpingo wanga, ngakhale Mkwatibwi wanga. Adzalamulira kuchokera ku chipembedzo chogwirizana, ndipo adzati ndi Mahdi wawo. Kugwirizana kwa zipembedzo kudzawagwirizanitsa, pansi pa bodza lakuti misewu yonse imapita kwa mulungu mmodzi. Kupatulapo, ine Yesu Khristu Mesiya woona sindidzaloledwa, ndipo ndilibe gawo mwa iwo ndipo chiwonongeko chawo sichichedwa. Choncho kugwirizana kwawo kudzawatsogolera ku gulu lawo la chilombo kumene chizindikiro chidzakakamizidwa pa mitundu kuti atenge nawo mbali m'gulu lawo. Musatenge chizindikirocho, pakuti aliyense amene atenga adzagawana nyanja yamoto ndi chilombo ndi mneneri wonyenga. Ndipo onse amene atenga chizindikirocho adzasinthidwa chibadwa chawo kwamuyaya. Kodi simunawerenge? Ndipo pamene munaona chitsulo chosakanizidwa ndi dongo lonyowa, adzadziyika okha ndi mbewu ya anthu: koma sadzamatirirana wina ndi mzake, monga chitsulo sichisakanizidwa ndi dongo. Omwe atenga chizindikirocho adzakhala akapolo a dongosolo lomwe likubwera ndipo adzakhala akapolo a malingaliro a gulu omwe adzachitika chifukwa chosakaniza thupi ndi luso lamakono.

Nyanja ikuyimira mitundu ya anthu akunja. Malamulo onyenga a Nowa amanena kale kuti Chikhristu ndi kupembedza mafano ndipo amapereka chilango chodula mutu. Malamulowa ali m'malo mwake ndipo adzakhazikitsidwa mwankhanza. Ndipo omwe adzaitana pa dzina la Ambuye mpaka mapeto adzapulumutsidwa, pakuti kwalembedwa: Ndipo Mulungu adzapukuta misozi yonse m'maso mwawo: ndipo sipadzakhalanso imfa, kapena chisoni, kapena kulira, kapena sipadzakhalanso kupweteka: pakuti zinthu zoyamba zapita. Ndipo iye amene anakhala pampando wachifumu, anati, Taonani, ndipanga zinthu zonse zatsopano. Ndipo anati kwa ine, lemba: pakuti mawu awa ndi oona ndi okhulupirika. Ndipo momwemo mudzakhala tsogolo lanu, oyera mtima anga a chisautso, pakuti ndidzapulumutsa Yakobo ndi onse amene aitanira pa dzina langa lamphamvu m'chilankhulo chilichonse chomwe angaitane.

Chilombo chochokera pansi, chomwe chilinso mneneri wonyenga chidzalamulira Isiraeli, ndipo chidzalamulira Dziko Lapansi kuchokera kudziko lolonjezedwa, koma osati anthu a Mulungu, osati Isiraeli wa Mulungu pakuti inu ndinu Isiraeli wa Mulungu, ngakhale ana a wam'mwambamwamba omwe asindikizidwa ndi chidindo cha Mulungu wam'mwambamwamba, ndipo chidindo cha Yehova sichingasweke. Ndipo khamu lalikulu lidzatuluka mu Chisautso cha Yakobo ndipo lidzatenga malo awo oyenera pakati pa Isiraeli wa Mulungu, pakuti onse Ayuda ndi Akunja adzapulumutsidwa ndipo adzakhala munthu mmodzi watsopano pakuti ndidzawalenganso mwa ine pa kuuka kwa olungama.

Ana anga, Sunagoge wa Satana wakonza ulamuliro wawo ndipo alibe chisoni, inu ndinu nyama zamiyendo iwiri kwa iwo, pakuti amatsatira Talmud ya ku Babulo yamwano, bukhu lomwe limachokera kwa Satana osati Yehova, pakuti Isiraeli anatembenuka kuchoka kwa Yehova kalekale pamene anandikana ine, Yeshua wa ku Nazarete, ndipo ndidzapulumutsabe anthu anga. Awa ndi mtundu womwewo wa namsongole pamene ndinauza Abambo awo pamene ndinawauza momveka bwino: Inu ndinu a atate wanu mdierekezi, ndipo zilakolako za atate wanu mudzachita: iye wakhala wakupha kuyambira pachiyambi, ndipo sanakhale m'choonadi, chifukwa mulibe choonadi mwa iye. Pamene alankhula bodza, ndiye kuti alankhula zake: pakuti iye ndi wabodza, ndi atate wake. Mvetsetsani Ana anga kuti Dani ndi mwana wa mkango ndipo Dani alibe woimira mu 144,000. Namsongole amenewa akuyembekezera mwachidwi womanga kachisi wawo, ndipo amamuzindikira kale ngati mfumu yawo. Chilombo chochokera pansi ndi chomwe chimabweretsa chizindikiro cha chilombo. Ngakhale ntchito ya mbewu yankhondo, yomwe tsopano yayesedwa ndikukonzedwa bwino kuti igawidwe ndikukakamizidwa ndi imfa kwa omwe akukana. Dziko lapansi likuyimira dziko lolonjezedwa.

Ana anga, siyani kukhulupirira mabodza a omwe sandilankhulira, pakuti sindinawatumize. Aneneri anga oona amalankhula mogwirizana, ndipo ndi ambiri, ndipo satsutsana wina ndi mzake, kapena malemba, pakuti Ine ndine Mawu wopangidwa thupi. Zilombo ziwiri zoipazi sizilandira mphamvu zawo kuchokera kwa bambo wina wa mpingo woipa, koma Chinjoka chenichenicho, pakuti Chinjoka ndi mdierekezi. Mvetsetsani kuti Chikhristu sichidzakhala ndi gawo lililonse chifukwa mipingo yonse yonyenga imaweruzidwa ndikuwonongedwa pakuti chiweruzo chimayambira panyumba ya Mulungu, ndipo Mpingo wanga woona, ngakhale Okhulupirika anga oitanidwa ndi osankhidwa adzatengedwa kupita kumwamba kukalandira mphotho zawo ndikuyamba umuyaya wawo. Ndipo kwaperekedwa kwa chinjoka kuti chilamulire, ndipo alendo adzaonekera, koma si alendo koma angelo ogwa omwe Chinjoka ndi mutu wawo. Ambiri mwa awa asungidwa m'maunyolo kuyambira masiku a Nowa koma tsopano amasulidwa kuti alandire chiweruzo chawo chomaliza chomwe sichigona.

Chilombo chochokera kunyanja, ngakhale iye amene dzina lake limamasuliridwa mu Chingerezi kuchokera ku Chihebri: Mphezi igwa kuchokera kumwamba: phunzirani kuti mudziwe. Pakuti sakhumba akazi, kapena kulemekeza mulungu aliyense: pakuti adzidzikweza yekha pamwamba pa onse. Chilombo cha chinjoka chochokera kunyanja, ndi chilombo chochokera pansi chidzauka kuchokera ku phulusa ngati ngwazi chifukwa chowononga Babulo Wachinsinsi, hule lalikulu, ndipo onse amagwira ntchito mogwirizana. Amadana ndi America ndi chilichonse chokhudzana ndi dzina la Yesu Khristu, koma adzagwada chimodzimodzi mu chiweruzo chawo, chomwe sadzachira. Choncho, malemba sanganthweke mu kasinthidwe katsopano ka mayiko ndi dziko lapansi lenilenilo. Pakuti Yehova akukonzekera kubweretsa kugwedezeka kwakukulu padziko lapansi mu chiweruzo ndipo malire adzajambulidwanso. Iyi ndi nkhondo yawo yomaliza yowononga dongosolo lakale, ndikubweretsa dongosolo la Satana yemwe adzalamulira anthu kudzera mwa chilombo chochokera kunyanja ndi chilombo chochokera pansi chomwe chilinso mneneri wonyenga. Nyanga khumi, omwe ndi mafumu khumi omwe adzalandira mphamvu zolamulira kwa ola limodzi, pakuti zaperekedwa choncho, adzapereka mphamvu zawo ndi chilolezo kwa gulu la zilombo atatuwa.

Taonani! Ndikupatsani ndondomeko yoyambirira ya zochitika kuti mudziwe nthawi yomwe mukukhala. Zina mwa zochitikazi zimachitika motsatizana, ndipo zina zimachitika nthawi imodzi, ndipo zili motere!

  • Babulo Wachinsinsi akuwonongedwa pamene nkhondo yapadziko lonse ikufalikira padziko lapansi, ndipo ngakhale tsopano akukonzekera magulu awo ankhondo ndipo akukonzekera kuukira kogwirizana bwino kwambiri pa Babulo Wachinsinsi. Musakhulupirire atsogoleri anu, kapena ofalitsa nkhani, pakuti ndi guwa la abodza omwe ali mbali ya kuukirako.
  • Chilombo chochokera kunyanja, ndi chilombo chochokera pansi, ndi chinjoka zidzauka kuchokera ku phulusa la nkhondo.
  • Zilombo ziwiri zoipazi zidzayima motsutsana ndi mboni 2 zomwe zidzanenera kwa miyezi 42, panthawi yomwe adzaphedwa ndipo adzakwera kumwamba m'masiku atatu ndi theka ndipo momwemo kuuka kwawo kudzakhala.
  • Pamene kachisi akumangidwa, nkhondo pa oyera mtima ikupitirira miyezi 42 ndipo chizindikiro cha chilombo chikukhazikitsidwa, kuperekedwa ndi kukakamizidwa pansi pa ululu wa imfa. Kenako nthawi yosinthira, pamene chonyansa cha chiwonongeko chikuchitidwa ndipo chilombo chochokera pansi chidzafuna kupha onse omwe angabwere kwa ine, ngakhale Yeshua HaMashiach panthawi ino.
  • Chilombo chochokera pansi chikudzilengeza chokha kukhala Mulungu ndikutsutsana ndi anthu kuti chiwaphe, pakuti chikupereka anthu mofanana ndi Antiochus Epiphanes.
  • Ndipo ndinayang'ana, ndipo taonani, Mwanawankhosa anaima pa phiri la Ziyoni, ndipo pamodzi naye zikwi zana limodzi ndi makumi anayi mphambu zinayi, ali ndi Dzina la Atate wake lolembedwa pamphumi pawo. (Odala ndi akufa omwe amafa mwa Ambuye, kuyambira tsopano, inde, atero Mzimu, kuti apumule ku ntchito zawo, ndipo ntchito zawo zimawatsatira.)
  • 144,000 anga adzateteza anthu anga ndikuwatengera kumalo otetezeka kuti apulumuke namondwe pamene dziko likuyeretsedwa ku chosalungama chonse, ngakhale namsongole woipa. Pakuti ndikadapanda kufupikitsa masikuwo palibe thupi lomwe likadapulumuka, ndipo choncho ndidzateteza ambiri m'chipululu. Izi zidzachitika mwa zina kuti otsala a anthu apulumutsidwe.
  • 144,000 akubwera padziko lapansi atavala zovala zomwe ndidzawapangira mwapadera, ndipo sizingasweke ndipo adzakhala amphamvu ndi aakulu ndipo palibe chinthu cha mdani chomwe chidzapambana, ndipo choncho zolinga za Yehova zidzakwaniritsidwa.

Ndine Yeshua HaMashiach ndipo ndatumiza mawu awa kudzera mwa mtumiki wanga, ndipo kudzera mwa mtumiki wanga mawu anga atumizidwa.

Yang'anani kumwamba Mpingo wanga wokhulupirika pakuti ndikuyandikira ndipo ndidzakutengerani m'nyumba ya Atate wanga komwe ndakukonzerani malo ndipo mudzalamulira nane ngati Mafumu ndi Ansembe, omwe Ufumu wanga ulibe mapeto, choncho dzilimbikitseni ndi Mawu awa.
