Mabingu 1 & 2

Kwa Ogwawo: Tsopano Chiweruzo Chanu Chafika!

Kuchokera kwa Yeshua HaMashiach:

Oitanidwa anga, osankhidwa, ndi okhulupirika, imvani Mawu a Ambuye ndi mpulumutsi wanu wamoyo, ngakhale Yeshua Mesiya! Mitundu yomwe mukukhalamo ndi mbiri yomwe mwaphunzitsidwa ndi aphunzitsi anu a Jesuit ndi maziko a chinyengo chachikulu, chomwe kuvumbulutsidwa kwake kwathunthu kuli pafupi ndipo kwayamba kale, momwe ogwawo adzawonekera ngati alendo abwino koma m'choonadi ndi angelo ogwa oyipa. Kodi nchiyani chomwe chakhalapo? Ndi chomwe chidzakhale: ndipo nchiyani chomwe chachitidwa? Chomwe chidzachitidwe: ndipo palibe chinthu chatsopano pansi pa dzuwa.

Satana wapereka chidziwitso chake chomwe chimatchedwa chapamwamba kuyambira pachiyambi, ngakhale chidziwitso chake chabwino ndi choyipa. Iye ndi katswiri wonyenga ndi wabodza yemwe waphunzitsa zinthu zambiri kwa anthu zomwe Yahweh sanazifune, pakuti izi ndi njira za imfa. Chilengedwe cha Yahweh sichinapangidwe kuti chigwiritsidwe ntchito mwachinyengo kuti munthu apeze mphamvu ndi phindu kapena ngati njira yothawira chiweruzo cha Mulungu, kapena kukhazikitsa njira ndi dongosolo la munthu mwini. Pakuti Satana waipitsa anthu kuyambira pachiyambi, ndipo waipitsa alonda ogwa omwe adalumikizana ndi kupanduka kwawo.

O, iwe mdani wamkulu wa chilungamo chonse, ngakhale inu a Satana: machimo anu oyambirira adalembedwa ndi Enoke mlembi nthawi yonseyi yapitayo, ndipo aima mpaka lero, pakuti machimo anu adalembedwa choncho. Ndipo chotero ndidzafotokoza mwachidule, pakuti mwachita zinthu izi, ndipo zambiri mwachita: Mwasocheretsa ana onse a Mulungu, ndikuwatsitsira pansi padziko lapansi, ndikuwasocheretsa kudzera mwa ana aakazi a anthu. Mwapereka uphungu woyipa kwa ana oyera a Mulungu, ndikuwasocheretsa kuti aipitse matupi awo ndi ana aakazi a anthu. Mwawonetsa ana a anthu mabala onse a imfa, ndikusocheretsa Hava, ndikuwonetsa zida za imfa kwa ana a anthu: chishango ndi malaya achitsulo, ndi lupanga lankhondo, ndi zida zonse za imfa kwa ana a anthu. Ndipo kuchokera m'dzanja lanu apita kukamenyana ndi omwe akukhala padziko lapansi kuyambira tsiku limenelo mpaka kalekale.

Inu a Satana mwaphunzitsa ana a anthu zowawa ndi zotsekemera, ndipo mwawaphunzitsa zinsinsi zonse za nzeru zawo. Mwaphunzitsa anthu kulemba ndi inki ndi pepala, ndipo potero ambiri achimwa kuyambira muyaya mpaka muyaya ndipo mpaka lero. Pakuti anthu sanalengedwe chifukwa cha cholinga choterocho, kuti atsimikizire chikhulupiriko chawo chabwino ndi cholembera ndi inki. Pakuti anthu adalengedwa chimodzimodzi ngati angelo, ndi cholinga choti apitirize kukhala oyera ndi olungama; ndipo imfa, yomwe imawononga chilichonse, sikanatha kuwagwira, koma kudzera mu chidziwitso ichi akuwonongeka, ndipo kudzera mu mphamvu iyi ikuwononga anthu.

Inu a Satana mwawonetsa ana a anthu kumenya konse koyipa kwa mizimu ndi ziwanda, ndi kumenya kwa mwana wosabadwa m'mimba, kuti amwalire, ndi kumenya kwa moyo, kulumidwa kwa njoka, ndi kumenya komwe kumachitika kudzera mu kutentha kwa masana, mwana wa njoka wotchedwa Tabââ'ĕt. Pakuti mwapemphanso Mikayeli kuti akuwonetseni dzina lobisika, kuti mulitchule m'lumbiro, kuti awo anjenjemere pamaso pa dzina ndi lumbiro lomwe lidavumbulutsa zonse zomwe zinali mseri kwa ana a anthu.

Pakuti mwapangitsa alonda kuti alumikizane ndi kupanduka kwanu pamene mwaipitsa anthu kwambiri. Ndipo chotero machimo a alonda adalembedwa choncho ndi Enoke mlembi, ndipo ndidzafotokoza mwachidule motere: Adaphunzitsa anthu kupanga malupanga, ndi mipeni, ndi zishango, ndi zoteteza pachifuwa, ndikuwadziwitsa zitsulo za dziko lapansi ndi luso lozigwiritsa ntchito, ndi zibangili, ndi zokongoletsera, ndi kugwiritsa ntchito antimoni, ndi kukongoletsa zikope, ndi mitundu yonse ya miyala yamtengo wapatali, ndi mitundu yonse ya utoto. Adaphunzitsa matsenga, ndi kudula mizu, kuthetsa matsenga, kupenda nyenyezi, magulu a nyenyezi, chidziwitso cha mitambo, zizindikiro za dziko lapansi, zizindikiro za dzuwa, ndi mayendedwe a mwezi.

Ndipo chotero kudzera mu ziphuphu ndi mabodza anu, mwaipitsa chilengedwe cha Yahweh ndi njira zanu zoyipa ndi zonyansa zamwano. Mwasocheretsa anthu ndikuchimwira Yahweh, ndipo mwalakwira anthu, ndipo ngakhale kuchimwira mbalame, ndi zilombo, ndi zokwawa, ndi nsomba, ndipo machimo anu abweretsa chiweruzo chachikulu chomwe simudzachira. Mwaipitsa chilengedwe cha Yahweh ndi matekinoloje anu ndi chinyengo chanu, ndipo mwapangitsa anthu kudabwa ndi zidule ndi mabodza anu. Pakuti Yahweh adangokuwonetsani zinsinsi zopanda phindu za chilengedwe chake ndikusunga zinsinsi zake zamtengo wapatali zobisika mumtima mwake kutali ndi kusakhulupirika kwanu.

Inu oyipa mwaphunzitsa njira zanu zopanda phindu kwa anthu pamene adachuluka padziko lapansi, ndipo mwabweretsa masautso aakulu ndi chiwonongeko kudzera m'ziwembu ndi matekinoloje anu oyipa. Pakuti simubwera koma kudzaba, kupha, ndi kuwononga. Mwaphunzitsa kusintha kwa mpweya, pakuti ndinu kalonga wa mphamvu ya mpweya, pakuti mumasintha aithēr. Pakuti pamene Kaini adachimwa, adamanga mzinda; ndipo mwaphunzitsa anthu zinsinsi zanu zopanda phindu ndi chinyengo chanu. Mizinda iyi idagwiritsidwa ntchito poyang'anira ndi chinyengo komanso kubweretsa chiwonongeko cha anthu, monga momwe mudayigwiritsira ntchito kupanga zonyansa zanu zachiwonongeko.

Mwapangitsa mtundu kuukira mtundu wina kunkhondo ndi mikangano yosatha, ndipo mwapangitsa anthu anga kuchimwa, ndipo Yahweh wamwaza Mafuko a Israyeli kumphepo zinayi. Palibe kanthu, cholinga chanu choyipa chathandizira zolinga za Yahweh, pakuti kudzera m'kumwazika kwawo kusonkhanitsidwa kwa nkhosa zotsala kudzachitika. Ndipo ndidzatumiza mboni zanga ziwiri kudzulosera masiku 1260 atavala chiguduli. M'kuyesetsa kwanu konse kuwononga ana a Yahweh mwalephera kotheratu, ndipo simukanatha kuwona kuti kuyesetsa kwanu kwathandizira zolinga za Yahweh, pakuti ngakhale adani ake amamvera mawu ake.

Pakuti mwazunza ndi kuvutitsa mafuko 12 padziko lonse lapansi pamene mumafunafuna chiwonongeko chawo. Mwaba ana kwa amayi, ndikutenga ana m'mabanja ndikuwasamutsira padziko lonse lapansi, ndiyeno mwawanamiza ndikuwapangitsa kukhulupirira nthano za a Jesuit zokhudza mbiri yawo yeniyeni, zolinga, ndi komwe adachokera. Palibe kanthu, mwalephera, chifukwa Ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndidzawalumikizanso kwathunthu ndi zakale zawo, mtundu ndi cholowa chawo chenicheni, ndipo ndidzapanga onse omwe ali olowa m'malo a chipulumutso kukhala munthu mmodzi watsopano, ngakhale mwa ine, Khristu Yesu.

Mwa chinyengo ndi chiwonongeko chachikulu, mwabisa mochenjera matekinoloje anu, ngakhale kunena mabodza kuti anthu akale anali mbuli ndipo analibe tekinoloje. Pakuti mwawonetsa anthu akale zinsinsi zanu zopanda phindu, ndipo palibe chinthu chatsopano pansi pa dzuwa. Ndipo chotero mumabisa choonadi chokhudza kusintha kwanu kwa chilengedwe, ngakhale kuwabisa kwa omwe mukufuna kuwanyenga ndi chinyengo chanu chachikulu. Mwayambitsanso zidule zanu zamatsenga kachiwiri, monga mwakhazikitsa bwalo kuti muthe kudzibisa ngati alendo. Ndipo kudzera m'kuwukira kwanu kwabodza kwa alendo mudzapangitsa onse kudabwa ndi zizindikiro ndi zozizwitsa zanu zabodza, ngakhale omwe mayina awo sanalembedwe m'Buku la Moyo kuyambira kukhazikitsidwa kwa dziko lapansi. Koma Ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndidzakuwululani kotheratu, ndipo chinsinsi chilichonse chobisika chidzabwera poyera.

Taonani! Ndidzatenga okhulupirika anga kupita kumwamba, pakuti ndikubwera kudzachitenga Mkwatibwi wanga ndipo sindidzaletsedwa. Ndipo Ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndikubwera kudzaweruza ndi kupanga nkhondo ndikubweretsa chiweruzo chachikulu padziko lapansi, ndipo simudzathawa.

Ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, Mbuye wa Ambuye ndi Mfumu ya Mafumu, ndatumiza mawu awa kudzera mwa mtumiki wanga, ndipo kudzera mwa mtumiki wanga mawu anga atumizidwa, ndipo zonse zidzakwaniritsidwa.

Kuchokera kwa Yahweh, Mulungu Wamkulu Wamuyaya:

O, inu a Satana, mwanyenga chilengedwe changa kuti chitsatire zizindikiro ndi zozizwitsa zanu zabodza, ndipo mkwiyo wanga uli pa inu. Anthu anga adzalekanitsidwa kotheratu ndi namsongole wanu yemwe mwafesa pakati pa tirigu wanga m'munda mwanga wa chisomo; ndipo Mwana wanga, Khristu Yesu, adzasonkhanitsa tirigu wanga m'nkhokwe yanga, ndipo chotero Mbuye wa zokolola adzasonkhanitsa, kumanga ndi kutentha namsongole wanu. Chiweruzo chachikulu chili pa inu, ndipo simudzathawa dzanja langa. Kuyambira pachiyambi, mudakwawa ndikudzibisa pakati pa ana anga, ngakhale kuipitsa ana anga kuyambira pachiyambi penipeni ndi mabodza ndi chinyengo chanu.

Inu sindinu milungu, ndipo omwe adatsatira chinyengo chanu ndi malingaliro anu achipambano nawonso si milungu. Simungathe kulenga, pakuti mulibe mphamvu, ndipo sindinakulengeni ndi maluso otere chifukwa ndimadziwa kuti mudzapanduka musanalengedwe; ndipo m'chilungamo changa ndi chikondi changa ndidakulengani m'chilungamo, ndipo munali angwiro tsiku lomwe mudalengedwa. M'kunyada kwanu kopanda phindu mumadzitama ndi ukulu wanu, koma mulibe ukulu, ndipo ndidzakuwululani kotheratu chifukwa cha chinyengo chomwe muli. Ndidzakuikani pamaso pa Mafumu, ndipo adzakuwonani ndi momwe mulibe mphamvu. Mwana wanga, Yeshua HaMashiach, adakugonjetsani kotheratu pa Kalvare, ndipo mulibe chiyembekezo.

Muloledwa kuchita zokhazo zomwe ndimalola, popeza ndinu mdani wogonjetsedwa. Mudaba chikalata cha dziko lapansi m'chinyengo chanu ndi mabodza kwa Adamu, ndipo tsopano mwana wanga wobadwa yekha, ngakhale Yeshua HaMashiach, wakugonjetsani m'chikondi chake pa ine ndi chilengedwe changa; ndipo dziko lapansi ndi lake, ngakhale Mbuye wa Ambuye ndi Mfumu ya Mafumu, pakuti ndalengeza. Pakuti adagula dziko lapansi ndi anthu ndi magazi ake opanda tchimo, ndipo omwe asankha moyo adzawomboledwa. Ntchito zanu zonse zopanda phindu zidzathetsedwa, ndipo onse omwe asankha moyo adzatuluka m'mabodza ndi chinyengo chanu kulowa m'moyo watsopano, ndipo ndidzapanga okhulupirika kukhala aulemerero kuposa momwe munalili kale. Pakuti mwalephera, o akalonga ofooka ndi opemphapempha a dziko lapansi, ngakhale kalonga aliyense m'dongosolo lanu walephera pamodzi ndi inu, o njoka yokwawa, yonama.

Tsopano yafika nthawi ya Chiweruzo chanu ndi chiwonongeko chotheratu cha ufumu wanu, ngakhale ufumu womwe mwamanga m'kuyesetsa kwanu kuyesa kundibisa, ngakhale Yahweh, ku chilengedwe changa. Simudzathawa. Simudzalowanso kumwamba, pakuti mwaponyedwa pansi padziko lapansi kuti musabwererenso, ndipo malo anu sali kumwamba kachiwiri. Mwamangiriridwa ku dziko lapansi lomwe mudalilakalaka, lomwe silinali lanu kuti mulilande. Ntchito yanu iliyonse idzathetsedwa m'miyoyo ya omwe mudayesa kuwawononga (ndi padziko lapansi pomwe); adzakhala mumtendere ndi chitetezo kwamuyaya, ndipo adzakhala ndi chimwemwe changwiro, ndipo ndidzakhala pakati pa anthu ndipo ndidzakhala Mulungu wawo.

Koma iwe, o wokwawa, ulibe mphamvu pamaso panga, ndipo sungachite chilichonse kundiletsa. M'masiku asanu ndi limodzi kuyambira kugwa kwa munthu, pakuti tsiku limodzi ndi zaka chikwi pamaso panga, simunakwaniritse chilichonse koma chiweruzo chanu, chilango, chiwonongeko, ndi mazunzo kwa inu ndi onse omwe akutsatira. Pakuti mudzamangidwa ndikuponyedwa m'nyanja yayikulu yamoto momwe ndakupangirani malawi otentha kwambiri, ngakhale malawi oyera a buluu, ndipo mudzayaka kwamuyaya, ndipo ndidzakubwezerani ntchito iliyonse yoyipa yomwe mudachitapo, mochulukirapo. Mwapha makamu pa makamu, ndipo mudzalipira moyo uliwonse womwe watayika, ndipo simudzathawa. Mudzadziwa machimo onse omwe mudachita, ndipo simudzathawa chiweruzo, ndipo mudzazunzidwa kwamuyaya chifukwa cha machimo omwe mwachita. Mudzadziwa bwino machimo anu ndi zolakwa zanu m'mazunzo anu, ndipo omwe mumawada adzakhala ndi chigonjetso chomaliza pa inu. Ndipo osalungama onse omwe mudawakopa kuti achimwe ndikudzipereka kuti alumikizane ndi kupanduka kwanu, ngakhale omwe mayina awo sanalembedwe m'Buku la Moyo, adzagawana nawo mazunzo anu. Nonse mudzalipira tchimo lililonse lomwe mwachita, pakuti mwachimwira Yahweh ndi chilengedwe changa, ndipo ndidzabwezera. Pakuti m'chilungamo changa ndidzabwezera machimo anu, zochita zanu, ndi njira zanu zoyipa kwathunthu m'zifuwa zanu mochulukirapo ndipo mpaka muyaya. Ndipo Ine, Yahweh, ngakhale Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya, ndikulumbira zinthu izi ndi dzina langa, pakuti sindingathe kulumbira ndi wamkulu wina.

Mudzagwira ntchito zolinga zanga, ndipo m'chidani chanu ndi chiwonongeko mudzathamangitsira osankhidwa anga kwa ine; pakuti ndidzawatsogolera ndi dzanja la Yeshua HaMashiach ndi gulu lake lankhondo la amuna amphamvu, pakuti adzasonkhanitsa nkhosa zanga zotsala. (Taonani! A 144,000 otumizidwa ndi Yeshua HaMashiach adzachita ntchito zamphamvu pamaso pa zoyipa zanu zonyansa.) Ndipo palibe mmodzi yemwe ndamuika ku moyo wamuyaya adzagwa m'ziwembu zanu, pakuti sangachotsedwe m'dzanja langa, ndipo palibe amene angawachotse m'manja mwa Mwana wanga: Pakuti ndamupatsa dzina loposa dzina lililonse, ndipo akubwera mu mphamvu ya Yahweh mwiniwake, pakuti ndagawana naye mpando wanga wachifumu. Ndipo adzaphwanya mutu wanu kotheratu. Mwalephera, kalonga wamng'ono, ndipo nthawi yanu yafika.

Ine, Yahweh, Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya, ndatumiza mawu awa kudzera mwa mtumiki wanga, ndipo kudzera mwa mtumiki wanga mawu anga atumizidwa, ndipo adzakwaniritsidwa. Pakuti Yahweh, Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya, walengeza choncho, ndipo palibe chomwe chingaswe zolengeza zanga. NDINE El Shaddai!
