Nthawi Yakwana!

Kuchokera kwa Yeshua HaMashiach:

Taonani! Nthawi yakulekanitsa ikupita kumapeto, ndipo kuonekera kwanga kwatsala pang'ono kufika tsopano. Kuli pafupi kwambiri ndipo ngakhale angelo ndi gulu la kumwamba ali okonzeka pakulira kwa lipenga. Ndatambasula manja anga ku dziko lomwe likufa kwa zaka zambiri za yubile tsopano, ndipo ambiri alandira pangano lamtendere lomwe Atate wanga, Yahweh, anatumiza kwa anthu, ngakhale ine, Khristu Yesu, pakuti ndine mtendere wanu. Koma ambiri akana pempho langa ndi chisomo ndi chifundo cha Atate wanga chomwe chatsanulidwa pa dziko lapansi.

Awo omwe asindikizidwa ndi kuwomboledwa ndi omwe anasankhidwa chikhazikitsireni dziko lapansi. Pakuti Atate wanga anakudziwani kuyambira muyaya mpaka muyaya, ndipo mwakhala muli pamtima pake nthawi zonse, monga inenso ndakhala pamtima pake, pakuti Ine, Yesu wa ku Nazarete, ndine mwana wobadwa yekha wa Yahweh. Ndipo komabe kudzera mu ntchito zanga zokwaniritsidwa monga Paskha wangwiro ndi kudzera mu kumvera kwa wobadwa yekha, ana a Mulungu ambiri, ambiri abadwanso mu umuyaya wonse, ngakhale kutengedwa kuchokera kwa ana a anthu, pakuti ndine Mwana wa Munthu!

Nthawi izi zachisomo zinali chinsinsi chobisika mu mtima wa Yahweh chomwe sanauze aliyense mpaka atachivumbulutsa kwa ine, ndipo ndachivumbulutsa kwa atumwi anga oyera ndi aneneri ndipo ndasunga Mawu anga oyera m'makutu ndi m'mitima ya osankhidwa ndi okhulupirika. Pakuti inu, Mkwatibwi wanga, ndinu gawo la chinsinsi chachikulu cha Mulungu, ndipo miyoyo yanu (yomwe yabisika mwa ine) yatsala pang'ono kuvumbulutsidwa. Ndipo ndidzabwezera okhulupirika pa ntchito zawo zonse zokhulupirika zomwe achita mokhulupirika mu chikhulupiriro chachikulu kwa ine, Yesu Khristu.

Ndipo chotero ndapulumutsa makamu akulu ndi magazi anga otetezera, pakuti magazi anga analibe tchimo ndipo mpaka lero ndilibe tchimo, pakuti sindidzachita tchimo. Awo omwe akhala miyoyo yawo mokhulupirika mwa Khristu adzalandira mphotho yaikulu, ndipo adzawala ngati nyenyezi zakumwamba, ndipo ulemerero waukulu ndi wawo, pakuti sanadzilemekeze okha ngati kuti anali akuluakulu. Koma andilemekeza ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndipo ndidzawapereka kwa Atate wanga opanda banga kapena khwinya, ndipo zovala zawo zidzakhala zoyera ngati matalala ndipo sizidzadetsedwa konse. Ndipo onse adzakhala ndi moyo ndipo sadzachitanso tchimo, kaya kudzera mu kusapembedza kapena kudzera mu kunyada. Ndipo adzalemekezedwa mwa ine.

Pakuti ndine mbusa wamkulu, ndipo ndatsogolera gulu langa ku msipu wobiriwira mpaka nthawi ya chimaliziro, yomwe ikubwera mwachangu pa dziko lapansi, pakuti palibenso mchenga wotsala woti udutse mu galasi. Ndipo ndidzabwezera onse, kuyambira wamng'ono mpaka wamkulu, m'njira yomwe simungathe kuimvetsa panthawiyi; koma kumvetsa kumeneko kuli pakhomo panu, pakuti mudzatengedwa kupita kumwamba posachedwapa. Ndipo chotero Mkwatibwi wanga adzatengedwa mu ulemerero, komwe ndidzaweruza ntchito zake ndi kuwombola kwathunthu mwamuna, mkazi, ndi mwana aliyense womaliza. Ndidzawabwezera mochuluka ngakhale pa zinthu zing'onozing'ono zomwe zakwaniritsidwa potumikira ine. Pakuti Mulungu sali wosalungama, kuti aiwale ntchito yanu ndi kudzivutitsa kwa chikondi, komwe mwasonyeza kudzina lake, pakuti mwatumikira Oyera mtima, ndipo mukuwatumikira.

Usaope, Mkwatibwi wanga, pakuti Mulungu saweruza monga momwe munthu aweruzira, pakuti munthu ndi wosalungama ndipo amakondera anthu. Munthu amalemekedza omwe munthu amawaona ngati ofunika, ndipo munthu wachibadwa nthawi zambiri amanyalanyaza kudzichepetsa ndi ukoma m'malo mwa odzikuza ndi odzichitira okha. Pakuti munthu wachibadwa amalira kuti ayesedwe wolungama pamaso pa Mulungu, koma munthu wachibadwa sangathe kugonjetsa chikhalidwe chake chauchimo ndipo amakana kukhulupirira kuti ndi woipa, ngakhale kukana Mbuye wa Ambuye, yemwe angamupulumutse.

Ndipo chotero kunyada kumatsogolera chiwonongeko, ndi mzimu wodzikuza usanagwe. M'malo mopinda mitima yawo ku mawu anga ndi nzeru ndi chikondi chomwe ndachisefukiritsa pa dziko lapansi, munthu wachibadwa m'malo mwake amasankha kutsatira zilakolako zake ndi zikhumbo zake, pakuti mayendedwe a dziko lapansi adapangidwa ndi Satana kuti ayese ndi kuyambitsa zikhumbozo, ngakhale mphotho za chosalungama. Ndipo chotero nthawi za Akunja zikupita kumapeto, ndipo ndi nthawi ya chisautso cha Yakobo; pakuti ndidzawombola Isiraeli, ngakhale osankhidwa anga okhulupirika, omwe adzabwera kwa ine mu kulapa machimo awo ndi machimo a atate awo omwe anandikana. Ndipo monga kwalembedwa, chotero chichitidwe.

O, munthu wachibadwa, bwanji ukusankhabe machimo ako ndikunyalanyaza chisomo changa ndi chipulumutso ngati kuti ndi chinthu chopepuka? Kaya Yahweh kapena Ine, Khristu Yesu, tilibe chikondwerero m'maweruzo omwe akubwera ndi mantha aakulu omwe posachedwapa adzamasulidwa. Koma Mulungu sanasankhe gawo ili la dongosolo lake kuti akhale wankhanza ku chilengedwe chake: pakuti Mulungu ndi chikondi, ndipo Mulungu ndi kuwala, ndipo mwa Iye mulibe mdima ngakhale pang'ono. Chisautso chachikulu chomwe chikubwera chapangidwa kuti chipatse anthu mwayi umodzi womaliza nthawi isanathe kwa iwo. Khamu lalikulu lidzapulumutsidwa mu chisautso, ngakhale omwe agwira dzina langa mpaka mpweya wawo womaliza ndipo sakonda miyoyo yawo mpaka mapeto.

Munthu wachibadwa, ndiwe wouma khosi kwambiri, ndipo sumvera, ndipo ukukana mopusa chisomo changa, ndipo ndikupempherabe chimodzimodzi: Atate, awakhululukire, pakuti sadziwa chomwe akuchita. Kwa omwe asiyidwa ku chisautso chachikulu, ngakhale omwe akana pempho langa lamtendere, ambiri adzatayika ku chiwonongeko, pakuti akana Ambuye Yesu Khristu. Komabe, ambiri adzapulumutsidwa, ngakhale onse omwe aitanira pa dzina la Ambuye adzapulumutsidwa. Ndipo chotero mayesero amoto atsala pang'ono kuyamba, pakuti chiweruzo chikuyandikira.

Chiweruzo chachikulu chikubwera pa ufumu wa Satana! Ndipo iwe, munthu wachibadwa, ngakhale mu kusakhulupirira kwako kouma khosi, ukukana ngakhale pempho losavuta kwambiri. Pakuti mu nthawi zanga zachisomo, chipulumutso chakhala chaulere ndi chosavuta, ndipo chomwe munthu amayenera kuchita chinali kuvomereza Yesu ngati Ambuye ndikukhulupirira kuti Mulungu anandiukitsa kwa akufa. Ndipo chotero tsopano kudzakhala njala ya zinthu zonse zabwino m'dziko, ngakhale Mawu anga Oyera. Nthawi yaikulu, yaikulu ya chisautso ikuyandikira, ndipo Satana akukonzekera mopusa kuyimirira kwake, ngakhale kuganiza modzikuza kuti akhoza kupambana kuti iye ndi angelo ake akhale ndi dziko lawo, dziko lopanda Mulungu, chifukwa njira zawo ndi zopotoka. Zoonadi, chomwe chadzipinda chokha sichingawongoledwe, ndipo palibe chikhululuko kapena mtendere kwa Satana ndi angelo ake, kapena mbewu yawo.

Ndine mwana wa Mulungu yemwe wabwera m'thupi.

Ndine Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda.

Ndine amene ndidzalamulira mitundu ndi ndodo yachitsulo.

Ndine amene ndidzamasula zisindikizo posachedwapa.

Ndine amene ndikubwera kudzatenga Mkwatibwi wanga.

Ndine amene ndidzakumana nanu mumlengalenga.

Ndine amene Ndine, ndipo lipenga latsala pang'ono kulira.

Ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndatumiza Mawu awa.

Kuchokera kwa Yahweh, Mulungu Wamkulu Wamuyaya:

Tsopano pakubwera chiweruzo cha Mulungu wamuyaya, ngakhale Yahweh, pakuti ndikutumiza Mwana wanga, ndipo palibe nthawi yotsala. O, inu opanda pake omwe munaganiza kuti mungathe kugonjetsa Yahweh, ngakhale Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya! O, inu opanda pake omwe munaganiza mopusa kuti mungathe kugonjetsa Mwana wanga wobadwa yekha, Yeshua HaMashiach. Mwadziumbikira mkwiyo wamuyaya, ndipo mkwiyo wanga ndi woopsa pa inu, pakuti ndine Mulungu wansanje. Pakuti kuyambira pachiyambi mwatsutsana ndi mapulani anga, ndipo mwakhumudwitsa, kuvutitsa, kuzunza, kuzunza, ndi kuwononga chilengedwe changa. Mulibe mtendere.

Ndimvereni ndipo ndimvereni bwino, pakuti ndikulengeza zomwe zidzachitike kwa inu, ndipo zomwe Yahweh alengeza palibe amene angasinthe, ngakhale kamodzi, ngakhale pang'ono. Ndipo chotero chiweruzo chanu chikubwera. Ndidzatumiza Mwana wanga kusonkhanitsa osankhidwa anga ndi okhulupirika, ngakhale ana a Adamu omwe tsopano akhala ana a Mulungu. Mwalephera, o, iwe njoka yopanda pake, yomwe imakwawa mu chinyengo ndi bodza. Pakuti inu sindinu milungu, ndipo simungathe kudzipulumutsa nokha m'manja mwanga. Pakuti ndidzabwezera!

Ndidzabwezera kwa inu ntchito iliyonse yoipa ndi ululu wonse ndi kuvutika komwe mwachititsa osalakwa, ndipo mudzayaka kwamuyaya m'malawi amoto otentha kwambiri a buluu, ndipo utsi wapoizoni wowawa udzakhala mpweya wanu, ndipo mu chizunzo chachikulu mudzakhala kwamuyaya. Pakuti munandichititsa kuwononga chilengedwe changa, ngakhale dziko lapansi monga momwe linalili chigumula cha Nowa chisanachitike, ngakhale chifukwa cha mbewu yanu yopanda pake yomwe munapanga kuti iwononge anthu; ndipo chotero chifukwa cha zinthu izi, ndidzakuwonongani mu cholowa chanu chamoto.

Ndipo munayesa kuwononga dziko lapansi pambuyo pa chigumula cha Nowa, ngakhale kupanga zonyansa zanu kachiwiri, koma sadzakhala ndi muzu kapena nthambi; pakuti namsongole yense adzazulidwa, kumangidwa, ndi kuponyedwa pamoto. Ndipo chotero ndidzawononga dziko lapansi mu chiweruzo chamoto, pakuti ndinalonjeza kuti sindidzasefukiritsanso dziko lonse lapansi. Koma pamoto chiweruzo changa chikubwera, pakuti ndine moto wonyeketsa, ndipo mkwiyo wanga ukuwira pa oipa. Ndipo chotero chifukwa cha zinthu izi, ndidzakuwonongani pamoto wotentha kwambiri ndi sulufule woyaka.

Ndimvereni ndipo ndimvereni bwino, pakuti ndikulengeza zomwe zidzachitike kwa inu, ngakhale inu alonda opanda pake omwe munasiya malo anu oyamba. Mulibe mtendere, ndipo ngakhale ndakumasulani m'maunyolo anu kwa nyengo yochepa, ndachita izi kokha kuti mutumikire zolinga zanga. Ndiye chiweruzo chachikulu chili pa inu, ndipo mulibe mtendere. Pakuti munandichititsa kusefukiritsa dziko lapansi chifukwa cha zonyansa zanu. Ndinu ndani kuti musewere mlengi ndi chilengedwe cha Yahweh? Ndinu ndani kuti muipitse munda wa Mulungu ndi njira zanu zodetsedwa? Munapanga ana anu apathengo ndi okanidwa podziyipitsa nokha ndi akazi, ndipo adzagawana cholowa chanu chamoto. Pakuti ndinamuuza chiyani Malaki: Pakuti taonani, tsiku likubwera lomwe lidzayaka ngati ng'anjo, ndipo onse odzikuza, inde, ndi onse omwe amachita zoipa, adzakhala ngati chiputu, ndipo tsiku lomwe likubwera, lidzawatentha, atero Ambuye wa Makamu, ndipo silidzawasiyira muzu kapena nthambi. Taonani tsikulo likuyandikira ndithu, ndipo nonse mudzalipira molingana ndi njira zanu zoipa.

Pakuti ndapatsa amuna akazi kuti asakhale akusowa kanthu, akazi a chiyanjano ndi kuwaberekera ana. Koma munatsikira pa Phiri la Herimoni, ngakhale kusiya malo anu oyamba a ulemerero, ndipo mwadziyipitsa nokha ndi akazi, ndipo mulibe mtendere kapena mpumulo. Monga ndinauza Enoke mlembi kuti akuuzeni mu m'badwo wa cholakwa chanu: Mulibe mtendere: chigamulo chaukali chatuluka motsutsana ndi iwe kukuika m'maunyolo: Ndipo sudzakhala ndi kulekerera kapena pempho loperekedwa kwa iwe, chifukwa cha chosalungama chomwe waphunzitsa, ndi chifukwa cha ntchito zonse zosapembedza ndi chosalungama ndi tchimo zomwe wasonyeza kwa anthu. Ndipo m'maunyolo mudzapita pamene mukuponyedwa m'nyanja yamoto.

Munavulaza chilengedwe changa chifukwa cha zilakolako zanu, ngakhale kutenga mokakamiza ambiri omwe munasankha ngati akazi anu, ndipo mudzalipira. Mwakhala mbali ya Asatana, ngakhale akalonga omwe adzikonzekeretsa okha motsutsana ndi ine, ndipo munasankha mabodza a aserafi. Choncho, mudzakhala nawo m'nyanja yaikulu yamoto wamuyaya, ndipo mudzayaka kwamuyaya, ndipo simudzakhala ndi mpumulo. Mwavulaza chilengedwe changa ndikupha ambiri, ndipo mulibe ulemu pa miyoyo yomwe ndalenga. Choncho, sindidzakhala ndi ulemu kwa inu, ndipo ndinakulengani, ndipo tsopano pakubwera chiweruzo chanu ndi chiwonongeko chomaliza.

Ndikunena kwa Asatana, alonda, akerubi akugwa, aserafi akugwa, ngakhale onse omwe alowa mu kupanduka kwa Asatana: mudzayaka kotheratu, mobwerezabwereza kwa umuyaya wonse; pakuti ndidzakumangani ndi kukuponyeni pamoto pomwe mbozi yanu simafa, ngakhale malawi amoto osungidwa kwa inu opanda pake omwe adzayaka malawi otentha kwambiri kwa inu nokha, pakuti ndinu oyenera. Pakuti ndidzachita kwa inu ndendende zomwe mwachita kwa ena. Ululu wonse, chisoni, kudandaula, kuvutika, ngakhale mchitidwe uliwonse wonyansa udzabwezeredwa kwa inu nthawi zonse, ndipo simudzathawa. Ndidzakuikani poyera pamaso pa mitundu ndi kukuululani chifukwa cha chinyengo chomwe muli, ndipo adzati kwa milungu ing'onoing'ono (yomwe si milungu): Kodi inunso mwakhala ofooka ngati ife? Kodi mwakhala ngati ife?

Ndidzabwezera kotheratu njira zanu zoipa m'zifuwa zanu, ndipo mudzalipira kwamuyaya. Mudzaponyedwa amoyo m'malawi amoto, ndipo mudzayaka pamaso pa Mwanawankhosa, ndipo angelo ndi olungama onse omwe mwawalakwira adzalandira kuchokera kwa ine zochuluka kuposa zomwe munatenga kwa iwo. Pakuti pamene mukubwezeredwa zoipa zanu, ndidzakhala nditabwezera ana anga osankhidwa ndi okhulupirika ndi mphotho yaikulu yomwe idzaposa zomwe mwatenga mochuluka kuposa ntchito zanu zopanda pake.

Ndipo chotero chidzakhala. Ndidzalenga kumwamba kwatsopano ndi dziko lapansi latsopano momwe mukukhala chilungamo, ndipo ana anga adzakhala ndi ine, ndipo ndidzapanga malo anga okhala pakati pa anthu, ndipo chotero chidzakhala. Koma mudzayaka m'njira zanu zoipa, ndipo chinthu chilichonse choipa chomwe mwachita chidzabwezeredwa kwa inu kwamuyaya. Ndipo chotero ndidzayika chisindikizo, ndipo mudzaiwalika, ndipo simudzakumbukiridwa. Ndipo onse adzakhala mumtendere, ndipo simudzakhala ndi mtendere.

Taonani, nthawi yatha, ndipo ndikutumiza Mwana wanga kwa Mkwatibwi wake, ngakhale Yesu Khristu Wolungama! Pakuti mwa iye ndikondwera kwambiri! Ndipo onse omwe wawapulumutsa ndi kuwapanga athunthu kuchokera m'njira zanu zopanda pake, ngakhale omwe mwawaimba mlandu mopanda chilungamo, adzapuma mwa ine ndikukhala ndi mtendere wangwiro, chimwemwe, ndi chikondi. Pakuti ndidzakuikani ngati iwo ndikuwapanga kukhala aulemerero kuposa momwe munalili kale, ndipo mulibe ufumu, pakuti sindinu mfumu.

Tsoka kwa okhala pa dziko lapansi, pakuti mkwiyo waukulu uli pa inu. Pakuti mwakana mlengi wanu ndipo mumakonda imfa. Pakuti kundikana ine ndikukonda imfa, ndipo pakufa mudzafa. Mkwiyo wanga ukutentha pa inu, ndipo ndidzachotsa zosangalatsa zonse, chimwemwe chonse, ngakhale zosowa zanu zoyambirira sizidzakwaniritsidwa. Pakuti pamene ndinakutsanulirani chisomo ndi chifundo, munasankha m'malo mwake kugwiritsa ntchito chisomo ngati mwayi wolowa nawo opanda pake, ngakhale ufumu wa Satana, pamene mukanatha kuthawa. Ndipo chotero tsopano ambiri adzawonongeka, omwe mayina awo sanalembedwe m'Buku la Moyo kuyambira pachiyambi. Mwana wanga, Yesu Khristu, akuyandikira, ndipo adzakwatula mkwatibwi wake kupita kumwamba, ndipo ndidzachotsa woletsa. Taonani! Ndikutumiza zimphona kukwaniritsa mkwiyo wanga! Munatenga chisomo changa ndi chifundo pachabe ndipo mwakhala miyoyo yachisangalalo chauchimo, ndipo ndidzabwezera.

Pakuti pamene mukanakhala ndi mtendere kwa umuyaya wonse, ngakhale moyo wamuyaya wachisangalalo chathunthu ndi chimwemwe, munasankha imfa zanu, ndipo ndidzakupatsani zomwe mukufuna. Mukufuna dziko loipa ndi mdima; ndidzakupatsani ndendende zomwe mukufuna, ndipo ndidzakubwezeraninso ululu wonse womwe munachititsa ena pamene munkakhala mu tchimo lalikulu. Ndidzakuvulani zosangalatsa zonse, ndipo mudzadya zonyansa za njira zanu. Chiweruzo chachikulu chili pa dziko lapansi, ndipo sipadzakhala kuchedwa kwinanso.

Taonani! Onse omwe aitanira pa dzina la Ambuye adzapulumutsidwa. Pakuti khamu lalikulu lidzapulumutsidwa ku chisautso chomwe chikubwera, ndipo ndidzawalandira kwa ine ndekha. Pakuti kwalembedwa: Awa ndiwo omwe adatuluka mu chisautso chachikulu, ndipo atsuka mikanjo yawo, ndikuiyera m'magazi a Mwanawankhosa. Chifukwa chake ali pamaso pa mpando wachifumu wa Mulungu, ndipo amamutumikira usana ndi usiku mu Kachisi wake: ndipo iye wokhala pa Mpando Wachifumu adzakhala pakati pawo. Sadzamvanso njala, kapena ludzu, kapena dzuwa silidzawawala, kapena kutentha kulikonse. Pakuti Mwanawankhosa yemwe ali pakati pa mpando wachifumu, adzawadyetsa, ndipo adzawatsogolera ku akasupe amadzi amoyo: ndipo Mulungu adzapukuta misozi yonse m'maso mwawo.

Ndikukulimbikitsani nonse kuti muitanire pa dzina la Yesu Khristu, Mesiya woona, ngakhale mwana wobadwa yekha wa Mulungu, pakuti akubwera. ndipo mkwiyo wa Mwanawankhosa ndi mkwiyo wa Yahweh watsala pang'ono kumasulidwa.

Ndine Yahweh, Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya.

Ndine El Shaddai, Mulungu Wamphamvuyonse.

Ndine Yehova Jireh.

Ndine Elohim wamkulu, Mulungu wa Isiraeli.

Ndine amene ndinatumiza mwana wanga wobadwa yekha kuti awombole omwe adzawomboledwa.

Ndine amene ndinaukitsa Khristu Yesu kwa akufa ndikumupatsa dzina loposa dzina lililonse, kuti bondo lililonse lipinde ndipo lilime lililonse lidzavomereza.

NDINE NDINE Wamkulu, ndipo mkwiyo wanga posachedwapa ukwaniritsidwa, ndipo palibe wamkulu, ndipo ndalankhula.

Ine, Yahweh, Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya, ndatumiza mawu omwe ndatumiza, ndipo ndatumiza mawu awa. Ndatumiza mwana wanga, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndipo watumiza mawu ake kwa okhulupirika. Nthawi yatha.
