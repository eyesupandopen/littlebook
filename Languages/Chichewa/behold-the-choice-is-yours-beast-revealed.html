Mverani mawu a mpulumutsi wanu, Ambuye ndi Mfumu ana anga ndipo tcherani khutu ku Mawu a Ambuye wanu, ngakhale Yesu Khristu wolungama, pakuti ndine mwana wa chilungamo ndipo ndimauka mu ulemerero wanga, ndipo mwakula ngati ana a ng'ombe a m'khola mu mtendere ndi chitetezo ndipo ndakulerani chifukwa cha cholinga chakumwamba chomwe chatsala pang'ono kuvumbulutsidwa kwathunthu mwa inu, okhulupirika anga ndi osankhidwa. Taonani! Ndine amene ndimayesa mitima ya anthu kuti mudziwe motsimikiza ngati muli m'chikhulupiriro, kapena ngati mwasocheretsedwa ndi kutengedwa ndi machimo anu ndi chinyengo champhamvu chomwe chagwira Dziko lapansi.

Pakuti ndine amene Yahweh wagawana naye mpando wake wachifumu, ngakhale Khristu Yesu Ambuye woukitsidwa ndi wopatsidwa mphamvu, ndipo ndine Mbuye wa ambuye ndi Mfumu ya mafumu ndipo taonani Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda ukubwera m'mitambo kudzalandira okhulupirika anga kwa ine kuti atengedwe kumwamba ku ukwati wa Mwanawankhosa, pakuti Mkwatibwi wanga wadzikonzera yekha. Ndipo nyale zake zadzaza, ndipo wadula zingwe zake, ndipo akuyembekezera kuonekera kwanga ngati namwali wanzeru, ngakhale omwe ndawayeretsa kwathunthu ndipo alibe cholakwa pamaso pa mpando wachifumu wa Mulungu. Ngakhale omwe sanatengedwe mwachinyengo ndi Baraba, pakuti Baraba amabweretsa kuukira kwachiwawa, nkhondo ndi tsoka, pakuti pakali pano ndi mutu umodzi pakati pa mitu ingapo yomwe imagwira ntchito mogwirizana, koma adzauka ngati Mesiya wonyenga, ndipo wasankhidwa ndi anthu a m'dzikolo kuti alamulire sunagoge wa Satana. Pakuti iye ndi wolowa m'malo wa amene makolo awo anamusankha, ndipo akudzikonzera okha kuti amulandire ngati Mfumu yawo.

Koma inu, O Mpingo wa Laodikaya, tsopano ikubwera nthawi yanu ya chiwonongeko ndi chiweruzo. Pakuti mwadzipezera nokha mpulumutsi wina ngati Mfumu yanu, ngakhale iye amene kubwera kwake kukulengezedwa ndi zizindikiro ndi zozizwitsa zonyenga ndipo pakali pano wakutsogolerani ndi matsenga ndi ufiti. Ndine Yeshua HaMashiach ndipo ndakugulani ndi magazi anga okhululukira machimo, opanda tchimo, ndipo ndine amene ndingakuchiritseni, ndi kukulengani mwatsopano mu mzimu wanga, pakuti ndapangidwa kukhala mzimu wopatsa moyo pakuti momwemo Yahweh wakhazikitsa ulamuliro wonse pa Mwana wake, ngakhale iye amene ali kukwaniritsidwa kwa mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu ndi Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda! Ndipo m'chilungamo ndidzaweruza ndi kuchita nkhondo pakuti mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu ndi Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda akwaniritsidwa mwa ine, Khristu Yesu, ngakhale Yeshua HaMashiach pakuti ndine amene!

Ndidzamaliza chipulumutso chanu, O Mpingo wa Laodikaya, koma simunakonzekere kulowa mu Ufumu wanga panthawiyi chifukwa mwasokera pa cholinga chanu chimodzi mwa Khristu! Ndidzakuwonetsani cholakwa chanu, ndipo muli ndi mwayi woti mulape machimo onse, koma mulibe nthawi yotsala yotsutsana ndi mawu a Ambuye wanu, pakuti tchimo lalikululi ndi limodzi mwa ambiri omwe Satana wagwiritsa ntchito kukumangani kuti musabwere kwathunthu kwa ine mukulapa kuti ndikuyeretseni kwathunthu m'magazi anga okhululukira machimo anu.

Taonani! Nthawi za anthu amitundu ina zatha ndipo mtengo wa azitona wakuthengo womwe unalumikizidwa pakati pawo, ndipo pamodzi nawo umadya muzu ndi mafuta a mtengo wa Azitona, tsopano udzadulidwa. Pakuti mwanyalanyaza Mawu a Khristu Yesu, pakuti ndinakuuzani momveka bwino kuti musadzitamandire pa nthambi zomwe zinagwa chifukwa chosakhulupirira: koma ngati mudzitamandira, simunyamula muzu, koma muzu umakunyamulani inu. Ndine Muzu wa Jese, ndipo ndine amene ndimayima ngati chizindikiro cha chiyembekezo nthawi zonse, ngakhale iye amene amabweretsa chipulumutso ndi njira za Yahweh padziko lapansi ndipo ndidzalamulira cholowa changa kuchokera pampando wachifumu wa Davide m'chilungamo ndipo ndagula dziko lapansi m'magazi anga opanda tchimo ndipo ndidzatenga mwamphamvu zomwe zili zanga kuchokera m'manja mwa olanda, ngakhale omwe adagwa.

Ndine amene ndapereka chisomo ndi chifundo padziko lapansi, pakuti nthawi yonse ya chisomo inaperekedwa kwa ine ndi Yahweh kuti anthu amitundu ina apulumutsidwe. Pakuti zolinga za Yahweh mu Israyeli nthawi zonse zinali zoti anthu amitundu ina adzadalitsidwa ndi kubwera ku chipulumutso kudzera mukumvera kwa Israyeli. Koma m'kupita kwanthawi anakhala onyada, ndi odzitamandira, ndipo anadulidwa mumtengo wa azitona wachilengedwe chifukwa chosakhulupirira. Koma inu, Mpingo wanga wa Laodikaya, ngakhale inu ndinu chitsanzo cha mtundu womwewu wa kudzikuza, kusakhulupirira, ndi khungu lomwe linawonetsedwa ndi nthambi zachilengedwe zomwe zinadulidwa chifukwa chosakhulupirira, ndipo tsopano mudzadulidwa mumtengo wa azitona wachilengedwe. Ndipo tsopano ndisuntha kuti ndimalize ntchito yanga pa mtengo wa azitona wachilengedwe, ngakhale Israyeli m'bale wanga ndi anthu anga, ngakhale onse omwe ndawasankha kuti andivomereze ngati Ambuye wawo kuti ndiwapange kukhala munthu mmodzi watsopano ndipo chinsinsi cha Mulungu chiyenera kumalizidwa, ngakhale pachimake pa ntchito zanga zopulumutsa.

Ndipo inu, Mpingo wanga wa Laodikaya, mwasiya chikondi chanu choyamba ndipo muli m'manja mwa wonyenga amene mwamulumbirira kukhulupirika. Mwadzipezera nokha Baraba watsopano ngakhale ngati Mfumu yanu ndipo mwakana Yesu wa ku Nazarete ngati Ambuye wanu ndipo muli m'manja mwa mpulumutsi wina wonyenga. Ngakhale iye amene amadziyika ngati mpulumutsi wa Babulo wachinsinsi, amene amalankhula ndi kuchita ngati mmodzi wa inu, koma amalankhula ngati chinjoka. Pakuti Babulo wachinsinsi amakhala mokoma komanso m'chuma chambiri, chisangalalo ndi chuma.

Ndipo Babulo wachinsinsi waledzeretsa dziko lonse lapansi ndi uve wa dama lake lomwe lili m'kapu yake yagolide, koma zotero ndi njira za omwe adagwa, omwe amapereka uve woyipitsitsa, ndipo mumamwa mofunitsitsa komanso mosangalala chifukwa mwakhala akhungu ndi kapu yagolide ku uve weniweni womwe mumadya mwauve. Pakuti ngakhale Mpingo wanga wa Laodikaya sumasonkhananso kuzungulira malemba anga oyera koma pamisonkhano ya Baraba, amene mumamukhulupirira. Ndipo choncho mumasankha mpulumutsi watsopano chifukwa mumakonda dziko lapansi ndi zinthu za dziko lapansi ndipo simungathe kumvetsetsa lingaliro loti mudzataya zakudya zanu zokoma, kupatula kuti ndidzakutengerani zosangalatsa zonse mu mkwiyo wanga womwe ukubwera, ndipo ngakhale tsopano kutayika kwanu kukuchulukirachulukira!

Pakuti ndinu akhungu ndi osokonezeka m'kudzikuza kwanu kuti Yahweh angapereke mpulumutsi wina kuposa ine, Yesu wa ku Nazarete, pakuti ndine mbewu yolonjezedwa ya mkazi ndipo Dani ndi mwana wa mkango amene palibe Mesiya adzauka. Mverani ine ndipo mverani bwino, O mpingo wa Laodikaya, pakuti tsopano ndi kulapa kwanu kuchokera ku zolakwa za mitima yanu kapena tsopano ndi nthawi yomwe ndidzakusiyani kumoto wa woyenga ndipo onse amene aitanira pa dzina la Ambuye mpaka mapeto adzapulumutsidwa ndipo ambiri amene anavomereza dzina langa mopanda chikhulupiriro adzagwa m'chiwonongeko, atataya mwayi wawo pa mawonekedwe a umulungu omwe anakana mphamvu yake: Alaodikaya anga, ndine mphamvu yake ndipo mwa ine Yahweh wayika ulamuliro wonse, ndipo mwakukana ine, Ambuye ndi mpulumutsi wanu, ndipo ndidzakusankhani m'kamwa mwanga pokhapokha mutalapa tsopano!

Mu nthawi yanga ya chisomo, chipulumutso chinafalikira pakati pa mitundu, ndipo mtundu ndi mtundu unagawana ndi kudya muzu wa mtengo wa azitona wachilengedwe. Ndipo tsopano, mitundu sidzakhalanso chizindikiro cha chipulumutso ndipo nthawi yawo mu Mwana inabwera chifukwa cha kukanidwa kwa m'bale wanga ndi anthu anga pakuti anafuula mosakhulupirira kuti atimasulire Baraba! Koma monga zinsinsi za Yahweh zinavumbulutsidwa ndi atumwi anga oyera ndi aneneri, ndamugwira Baraba ndipo sindinamumasule kwathunthu, koma ndangolola chinsinsi cha kusayeruzika kupita patsogolo. Ndipo tsopano nthawi yafika yomwe ndidzamasula Baraba kwathunthu pamene wolepheretsa wanga akuchotsedwa ndipo monga okhulupirika anga, oitanidwa, ndi osankhidwa akutengedwa kumwamba komwe ndawakonzera malo awo okhala kwamuyaya, ndipo adzavekedwa nsalu zabwino zopanda banga zomwe sizingadetse kapena kuipitsidwa.

Kodi ndinu akhungu kwambiri kudziko lanu, ndi ogontha kwambiri moti simumvanso mawu anga O Mpingo wa Laodikaya? Mwakhulupirira mabodza obweretsedwa ndi chinsinsi cha kusayeruzika, ndipo mwalola chotupitsa chapoizoni m'Mabaibulo anu oipitsidwa omwe abweretsa katangale weniweni m'Mipingo yanu yomwe yaipitsa mitima yanu ndi nthano m'kusakhulupirira kolakwika. Mwalola mabodza a amatsenga a nthawi kuti akusengeni ndi malemba onyenga ndi mawu abodza. Simumvetsetsanso malemba, ndipo mwadziunjikira mavuto akulu omwe adzakugwerani mwadzidzidzi. Mawu onse a Mulungu ndi oyera ndipo cholinga chake ndi kumvera kwa chikhulupiriro ndi kulimbikitsa olandira. Mvetsetsani Mawu a Mawu anga ndipo mvetsetsani nthawi yomwe mukukhala. Pakuti ndinauza Yohane Wovumbulutsa kulemba: Ndipo ndinaona chilombo chikutuluka m'nyanja, chokhala ndi mitu isanu ndi iwiri, ndi nyanga khumi, ndipo pa nyanga zake panali akorona khumi, ndipo pa mitu yake dzina la mwano.

Ndipo kachiwiri Yohane Wovumbulutsa analemba: Ndinaona mkazi atakhala pa chilombo chofiira, chodzaza ndi mayina a mwano, chokhala ndi mitu isanu ndi iwiri, ndi nyanga khumi. Ndipo mkaziyo anali atavala zofiirira ndi zofiira, ndipo anakongoletsedwa ndi golide, ndi miyala yamtengo wapatali ndi ngale, ali ndi kapu yagolide m'dzanja lake, yodzaza ndi zonyansa ndi uve wa dama lake.

Ndipo kachiwiri: Ndipo nyanga khumi zomwe munaziona pa chilombo, izi zidzadana ndi hule, ndipo zidzamupanga kukhala wabwinja, ndi wamaliseche, ndipo zidzadya mnofu wake, ndi kumutentha ndi moto. Pakuti Mulungu wayika m'mitima yawo kukwaniritsa chifuniro chake, ndi kuvomerezana, ndi kupereka ufumu wawo kwa chilombo, mpaka mawu a Mulungu adzakwaniritsidwa. Ndipo mkazi, amene munamuona, ndi Mzinda waukulu umene umalamulira mafumu a dziko lapansi.

Ana anga, mvetsetsani kuti chilombo chikhoza kutanthauza anthu komanso maufumu, phunzirani kuti mudziwe. Chilombo chomwe chimanyamula hule chidzawononga hule ndipo sichidzakhala pansi pa hule, pakuti walamulira mafumu a dziko lapansi mu dama lake. Choncho chilombo chotuluka m'nyanja chidzatulutsa Mahdi wawo woyembekezeredwa, komanso chidzawononga kwathunthu Babulo Wachinsinsi, pakuti Mulungu wapereka hule lalikulu m'manja mwa adani ake omwe amamudana naye kale.

Chilombo chochokera kunyanja ndi chilombo chochokera pansi zisanauluke kwathunthu mu Ufumu wa chinjoka, pali olamulira ambiri omwe ali ogwirizana pa cholinga chopereka mphamvu zawo ndi ulamuliro wawo kwa chinjoka, pakuti Mulungu wayika m'mitima yawo kuti akwaniritse. Ndipo adzawononga Babulo wachinsinsi. Kodi mukuganiza kuti mwadziwa bwino malemba anga oyera chifukwa mwakhulupirira akatswiri anu azaumulungu abodza? Mvetsetsani ndi kumvera Mawu a Ambuye wanu!

Kachiwiri, ndinauza Yohane Wovumbulutsa kulemba: Ndipo chilombo chomwe ndinachiona, chinali ngati Kambuku, ndi mapazi ake ngati chimbalangondo, ndi pakamwa pake ngati pakamwa pa mkango: ndipo chinjoka chinamupatsa mphamvu zake ndi mpando wake wachifumu, ndi ulamuliro waukulu. Ndipo ndinaona umodzi wa mitu yake ngati kuti wavulazidwa mpaka kufa, koma chilonda chake chakupha chinachiritsidwa, ndipo dziko lonse lapansi linadabwa ndikutsatira chilombocho.

Ana anga, mawu a Yahweh ali ndi kuya kwamitundumitundu, komwe palibe munthu angathe kudziwa bwino. Chilombo chomwe chafotokozedwa ndi ufumu womwe udzauka kuchokera ku chiwonongeko cha hule, ndipo kuchokera ku dongosolo lakale dongosolo latsopano lidzauka! Likuyimira chilombo cha nyanja, pakuti adzauka ngati Mahdi wawo, ndipo likuyimira chikhalidwe cha ufumu womwe adzalamulira nyanja. Chifukwa chiyani simulemekeza mawu a Mawu anga? Sikunena kuti chilombo chochokera kunyanja chokha chinavulazidwa, pakuti ndi munthu uti amene ali ndi mitu isanu ndi iwiri? Koma umodzi wa mitu yake ngati kuti wavulazidwa mpaka kufa. O, opusa, ndi odekha mtima kukhulupirira zonse zomwe Aneneri alankhula! NGATI KUTI wavulazidwa mpaka kufa. Mawu akuti "ngati kuti" ayenera kukuimitsani m'njira zanu, koma simukhulupirira ngakhale mawu anga ochepa, ndipo ndine Mawu opangidwa thupi. Kodi sizikuwonekerani bwino, NGATI KUTI, ndi mwambi wakale? Kodi sizikuwonekerani bwino kuti "kuti" ndi subjunctive? Kodi sizikuwonekerani bwino kuti ndi kufupikitsa kwa, ngati zinali choncho?

Koma ayi, mumakhulupirira mabodza a chinjoka, pakuti Baraba amalankhula ngati chinjoka, koma si Baraba, koma chilombo chochokera pansi. Ndipo tsopano dziko lonse lapansi lidzadabwa pambuyo pa chilombo chifukwa cha zizindikiro zake zonyenga ndi zozizwitsa, ndipo adzachita zizindikiro zambiri zonyenga ndi zozizwitsa, ndipo sanauke kwathunthu pakuti hule silinatenthedwe ndi moto, koma tsopano wavumbulutsidwa mu nthawi yake. Amalankhula ngati chinjoka, nati "Mulungu yekha ndiye anamupulumutsa ku chilonda chake chamutu" ndipo ili ndi bodza lamwano. Yahweh, Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya, wanditumiza ine Yesu wa ku Nazarete ngati mpulumutsi wa dziko lapansi. Ine ndi Atate wanga ndife amodzi mu umodzi ndi cholinga ndipo Yahweh sanapulumutse Baraba ndipo inenso, Khristu Yesu. Mwasocheretsedwa ndi kutengedwa ndi kukodwa m'chinyengo chachikulu, ndipo ndikukuuzani kuti lapani ndikubwerera ku chikondi chanu choyamba ndipo chitani izi tsopano, pakuti palibe nthawi!

Pakuti ndine amene ndimayesa mitima, ndipo ndine amene ndimayesa onse omwe ndimawaitana ku chipulumutso, pakuti ndimayeretsa onse pamoto wa woyenga! Taonani! Ndine amene ndimakhala ngati woyenga ndi woyeretsa siliva: ndipo ndimayeretsa ana a Yahweh, ndi kuwayeretsa ngati golide ndi siliva, kuti apereke kwa Ambuye nsembe m'chilungamo. Kodi simukumvetsa kuti ndidzakupangani kukhala ansembe ndi mafumu pamaso pa Atate wanga? Ngakhale mphatso yanga ya mzimu woyera, yomwe mwabadwa mwatsopano ndi mbewu yosawonongeka ndi yangwiro ndipo sifunikira kuyeretsedwa, mwabadwa kuchokera kwa Adamu ndi magazi ndipo muyenera kulola kwathunthu ntchito yanga kuti ikwaniritsidwe m'miyoyo yanu, pokhapokha ngati mbewu yanga yagwera panthaka yamiyala.

Tsopano ikubwera nthawi ya chiweruzo ndipo nthawi ya chisomo yatha. Ngakhale ambiri adzanyoza mawu anga ndi kusiya m'kusakhulupirira, zadziwika kale ndi Yahweh zotsatira zake. Pakuti Yahweh nthawi zonse amatsimikizira anthu njira zawo zoyipa ndikudzudzula anthu chifukwa cha tchimo, podziwa m'chikondi chake ndi nzeru zake zamuyaya kuti adzapulumutsa ena. Ndipo monga Yahweh anaumitsa mtima wa Farao, momwemonso adzaumitsa mitima ya ambiri omwe amamva koma samamvetsera. Yahweh adzayesa munthu mobwerezabwereza. Ndipo ena adzakhala ngati Farao ndipo adzaumitsa mitima yawo kwambiri m'kusakhulupirira koma ena adzachoka m'njira za Farao ndikuuka ku moyo wosatha mwa Khristu Yesu, komabe kudzera pamoto wa woyenga.

Mvetsetsani kuti omwe asankha Baraba adzadzipanga okha kukhala ogawana nawo milandu ya Baraba, ndipo omwe akana kuitana pa dzina langa mpaka mapeto onse adzawonongedwa chifukwa anali ndi chisangalalo m'chinyengo. Pakuti mpulumutsi wanu watsopano si mpulumutsi koma chilombo cholusa. Ndipo dongosolo lake ndi ufumu wake zimapangidwa ndi omwe amapweteka ana. Kodi simukumvetsa njira za Chinjoka? Pakuti omwe amatsatira njira za chinjoka amadya ana. Amapereka ana nsembe kwa Moloki, amafunafuna kasupe wawo wa unyamata kuchokera m'magazi a ana ozunzidwa, amagwiririra ana chifukwa cha kukhutitsidwa kwawo kodwala, ndipo amachita kumenya mluza m'mimba kuti aphe ana asanabadwe, ndipo akutsogolerani mwachinsinsi m'njira zawo, pakuti zotero ndi njira za atsogoleri a Babulo Wachinsinsi komanso ufumu wa chinjoka. Ndipo tsopano ikubwera chiweruzo cha Mulungu pa zinthu izi ndi zina zambiri. Pakuti mukanakhala kuti simunawone zinthu izi mukanakhala opanda tchimo, koma tsopano mukuwona ndipo tchimo lanu likadali.

Kodi simunawerenge? Choncho, atero Ambuye, Mawu anamveka kumwamba, kulira ndi kulira kowawa. Rakeli, akulirira ana ake, anakana kutonthozedwa chifukwa cha ana ake, chifukwa analibe. Pakuti mwalephera udindo womwe munapatsidwa, ndipo munapatsidwa zinsinsi zanga ndipo munayima ngati kuwala kwa chipulumutso ndi chizindikiro cha chiyembekezo. Koma tsopano mwagwirizana ndi omwe ndinakutumizani kuti muwatsutse. Ndipo tsopano ikubwera nthawi yomwe ndidzakusiyani ku tsogolo lanu.

Mverani ine ndipo mverani bwino onse omwe angakane mawu a Yeshua HaMashiach, ngakhale mawu a Khristu Yesu: mulibe nthawi! Ndikukulangizani kuti mundimvere m'chikondi changa kwa inu ana anga ndipo lapani tsopano! Pakuti moto wa woyenga watsala pang'ono kukugwerani kwathunthu, ndipo udzayeretsa dziko la ufumu wonse wa chinjoka ndipo dziko lapansi silidzakhala pansi pa njira za Satana ndipo ufumu wake sudzaukanso, pakuti ndine Mbuye wa ambuye ndi Mfumu ya mafumu, ndipo ndidzawononga kwathunthu omwe adagwa. Onse omwe akana kulapa adzakhala ndi gawo lawo m'nyanja yamoto ndipo adzagawana chilango cha mdierekezi ndi angelo ake m'nyanja yamoto yoyaka sulfure. Momwemonso, onse omwe alapa pochoka m'tchimo ndi kuitana pa dzina langa mpaka mapeto adzapulumutsidwa ndikupatsidwa mphotho yayikulu yamuyaya mwa ine.

Taonani! Tsopano ndi nthawi yosonkhanitsa okhulupirika, ndipo tsopano ndidzasiya omwe akana kwa Baraba, pakuti asankha.

Awo amene satembenuka kuchoka kwa mesiya wonamayu adzapeza chipulumutso mu moto wa woyenga, kapena kugwera m'chiwonongeko mpaka kalekale: Chosankha ndi chanu!

Ndipo ambiri mu Babulo Wachinsinsi, ngakhale omwe amakhulupirira mabodza ake ndikutsatira mawu ake abwino ndi zolankhula zake zokongola akugwidwa ndi kukodwa mu mtendere wonama kumene amaganiza kuti miyoyo yawo yokoma ndi yochimwa idzapitirira kumene lero lili ngati dzulo ndipo mawa adzakhala chimodzimodzi ngati lero Ndipo ambiri adzafuula, kulira, ndi kudandaula kuti chiyembekezo chawo tsopano chachotsedwa kwa iwo Ndipo adzamenyana ndi anzawo mpaka kufa ndipo nthawi yonseyi kuukira kwawo kodzidzimutsa kudzamasulidwa kwathunthu, ngakhale pamene woletsa wanga akuchotsedwa pa dziko lapansi. Ndipo Babulo Wachinsinsi adzagwa ku gawo lowirikiza la chiweruzo mu ola limodzi pamene masoka ambiri akugwa. Ndipo omwe amalambira ma kiyubu awo akuda adzadzitopetsa okha mu kuphana, ngakhale kuphana komwe kumabweretsedwa mwadala ndi chinyengo chachikulu ndipo kuchokera ku phulusa adzauka.
