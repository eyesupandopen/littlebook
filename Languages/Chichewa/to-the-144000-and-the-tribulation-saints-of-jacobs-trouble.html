Kwa a 144,000 ndi Oyera Mtima a Chisautso cha Mavuto a Yakobo

Kwa a 144,000 ndi oyera mtima a chisautso cha mavuto a Yakobo: Taonani! Ndikukuuzani zinthu zambiri zisanachitike kuti munthu wanzeru pakati panu amene amamva mawu anga akhale wanzeru kwambiri, ndipo kuti mudziwe chitsimikizo cha mawu anga. Pakuti Satana adzayesa kubisa kuchoka kwa okhulupirika mu Mpingo wanga (pakuti ndidzawatenga pakuchoka kwawo pa dziko lapansi kupita kumwamba) ndi nkhondo ndi kuonekera konyenga kwa otchedwa alendo (aliens) omwe sali kanthu koma ogwa omwe akudziwonetsera okha ku dziko lapansi mu mphulupulu pa mphulupulu zozikidwa kwathunthu m'mabodza awo. Pakuti iwo ndi chinyengo.

Ananama za chilengedwe chomwe chimatchedwa universe; pakuti palibe chilengedwe chomwe chikukulirakulira cha zinthu zopanda pake, koma pamwamba pa dziko lapansi pali kumwamba kwa kumwamba, ngakhale (kumwamba kwapamwamba) kumene Yahweh Mulungu wam'mwambamwamba amakhala pampando wake wachifumu, ndipo komabe adzapanga malo ake okhala ndi anthu. Komabe iye ali paliponse ndipo ali zonse mu zonse mkati mwa Thupi la Khristu. Pa dziko lapansi pamakhala ngale yake yamtengo wapatali, ngakhale omwe anasankhidwa mu nthawi zamuyaya zapitazo mu mtima wa Yahweh mwiniwake. Ndipo choncho dziko lapansi ndi la Ambuye ndi kudzaza kwake; pakuti Ine, Yesu wa ku Nazarete, ndagula dziko lapansi ndi magazi anga owombola.

Phunzirani za mapangidwe enieni a chilengedwe (phunzirani kuti mudziwe) ndipo khulupirirani kufotokozera ndi mawu mu Malemba Oyera okhudza chilengedwe, pakuti zili monga Yahweh wafotokozera m'Malemba, ndipo n'kosatheka kuti Yahweh aname. Ngati, pamene ndikuuzani zinthu za pa dziko lapansi, simukhulupirira, mudzakhulupirira bwanji ngati ndikuuzani zinthu za kumwamba? Ayi, palibe alendo (aliens) m'njira yomwe mabodza awo amafotokozera, koma angelo ogwa, opanduka, ngakhale Satana ndi ufumu wake omwe anama ndi kunyenga dziko lapansi kuti onse aweruzidwe omwe amakondwera ndi chosalungama.

Aluka nthano zambiri zopangidwa mochenjera kukhala chinsalu cha mabodza, ndipo alamulira mbali zonse ziwiri za nkhani zawo kuyambira pachiyambi pamene akukankhira zolinga zawo. Pakuti amalamulira lingaliro ndipo amalamulira chotsutsana nalo, ndipo amaphatikiza ntchito zawo zoipa pang'onopang'ono, ndipo chinyengo chawo ndi chachikulu! Koma kuwala kwa Malemba Oyera kumavumbulutsa chinyengocho, komabe munthu amakana thandizo la Mulungu mpaka chiweluzo chitaperekedwa ndipo tsoka litagunda. Ndiye anthu amafuna kulapa pamene kwachedwa pambuyo poti chiweluzo chaperekedwa, ndipo omwe amakhala m'njira yotere sadzapulumuka chiweluzo chawo. Pakuti chilichonse chomwe chalengezedwa ndi Yahweh palibe amene angachisinthe!

Ambiri adzagonjera ku zinyengo zomwe zikubwera zomwe zidzatuluka mu nkhondo yapadziko lonse ndi tsoka lomwe latsala pang'ono kugwera dziko lapansi. Pakuti adzadziwonetsera kwa anthu ngati zolengedwa zabwino zomwe zachokera kumayiko otukuka kudzagaŵana nzeru zawo zabwino. Koma ndi nzeru chabe za chabwino ndi choipa zopakidwa mwatsopano kuti zigwirizane ndi zolinga zawo. Adzakhala ndi matekinoloje apamwamba ndipo adzawoneka otukuka kwambiri, kuposa munthu wamba. Koma chitukuko chawo chokha chili mu zoipa, ndipo sali a dziko lapansi lino koma analengedwa ndi Yahweh pachiyambi cha chilengedwe kumwamba. Analipo pamene Yahweh analenga dziko lapansi. Atate mu kudziwiratu kwake anadziwa kuti adzapereka ndipo choncho anasunga chinsinsi cha mtima wake wamtengo wapatali chobisika kwa omwe anadziwa kuti adzabweretsa chiwonongeko choipa ku chilengedwe chake.

Ndikuwitanabe munthu wamba ku chilungamo, koma munthu wamba m'malo mwake amakonda matenda, tchimo, ndi mdima; ndipo choncho ndidzapatsa anthu zomwe akuzifuna kwambiri. Popeza munthu wamba amachita zoipa ndipo amakonda mdima, ndidzamupatsa mdima wosaletseka.

Chifukwa pakali pano, munthu wamba akuyima modzikuza mu chisomo changa akugwiritsa ntchito chifundo ngati chifukwa choikira tchimo pa tchimo, m'malo movomereza pangano lamtendere la Yahweh, ngakhale ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, amene ndinapereka magazi anga opanda tchimo pomvera Yahweh ngati malipiro a machimo onse a anthu nthawi zonse. Musalakwitse: Ndine Mesiya weniweni, ndipo ena onse ndi zotsanzira zotsika mtengo zopanda phindu.

Satana anapanduka patangopita nthawi yochepa chiyambireni chilengedwe ndipo anaipitsa chilengedwe cha Atate m'njira yopanda manyazi, yoipa, yaudani, ndi yowononga; koma zinachitika mochenjera kwambiri ndi zizindikiro ndi zozizwitsa zonyenga, ngakhale matekinoloje apamwamba omwe amadabwitsa mphamvu zakumva. Ndipo pobwezera amafuna kupembedzedwa ndi kumvera mosamalitsa njira zawo, komabe njira zawo nthawi zonse zimabweretsa imfa ndi chiwonongeko cha anthu, pakuti zapangidwa choncho.

Palibe chatsopano pansi pa dzuwa, ana anga. Otchedwa milungu ya mbiri yakale ndi ana awo onyansa otchedwa theka-milungu adzabwerera, ndipo monga m'masiku a Nowa, momwemonso kudzakhala. Pakuti amasulidwa ku maunyolo awo ndi mazunzo kuti alandire chiweluzo chawo chonse, ndipo ndidzabwezera, atero Ambuye Yesu Khristu, ndipo Yahweh adzawabwezera mpaka kotheratu.

Satana ndi ogwa ake anakana kugwada pamaso pa dongosolo la Yahweh ndipo m'malo mwake anayesa kuba dziko lapansi kwa anthu kudzera mu chinyengo chachikulu. Satana wayesa ngakhale kuba cholowa changa kwa Atate wa Kuwala! Koma palibe amene angathe kulanda ngakhale wamng'ono wa inu m'manja mwanga, ndipo palibe amene angalande cholowa changa; pakuti chaperekedwa ndi Yahweh mwiniwake, ndipo aliyense amene akana kugwada kwa ine, Yesu Khristu, akupandukira dongosolo la Yahweh, kulumikizana ndi ogwawo mpaka muyaya.

Choncho ndikukuuzani, musakhulupirire mabodza a Copernicus, yemwe anali wa Jesuit ndi Freemason yemwe anatumikira Satana. Iye anangophunzitsanso mawu a afilosofi achikunja achigiriki akale. Ndipo choncho mapangidwe a chilengedwe omwe mwawavomereza anaganiziridwa ndi opembedza dzuwa ndi akunja potengera mabodza a Satana, ngakhale a Jesuit amwano omwe aipitsa machitidwe anu a maphunziro ndi kuphunzira motsutsana ndi kuphunzira, ndi cholinga chonse choti chilengedwe chawo chonyenga chilembedwe m'maganizo mwa anthu kudzera m'mabodza opakidwa ngati maphunziro. M'malo mwake, kuloŵetsedwa maganizo kopanda nzeru kwachitika, ndipo dziko lapansi monga momwe liliri silikumvetsetsedwa ndi omwe amakhala pa dziko lapansi.

Tsopano bwalo lakonzedwa la chinyengo champhamvu kwambiri, ndipo mabodza amangidwa kale, pakuti maziko ake ndi mphulupulu ndi chinyengo chachikulu. Zinyengo izi zazikidwa m'maphunziro anu a Jesuit. Pakuti dziko lonse lapansi lanyengedwa ndi mabodza awo; ngakhale osankhidwa enieni akanyengedwa ngati kuli kotheka. Ndipo choncho msampha uli wokonzeka kutchera, ndipo chinthu chokha chomwe chikuugwira ndi choletsa, chomwe chidzachotsedwa mwadzidzidzi ndi posachedwa. Ndiye nkhondo yaikulu idzabuka pa dziko lapansi pamene okhulupirika anga akusonkhanitsidwa kwa ine kumwamba. Ndiye kuchokera ku phulusa kudzauka chilombo ndi mneneri wonyenga, omwe adzakhala ofunika kwambiri mu chinyengo chomwe chikubwera.

Choncho musakhulupirire mabodza awo, amphamvu anga, pakuti ndinu anthu apadera ndipo ndakuitanani ndi kukusindikizani ndi chidindo cha Mulungu Wamoyo, kuti mkwiyo wa Mulungu usakukhudzeni. Ndidzakutumizani ngati amphamvu anga. Mudzakhala mawu anga ambiri akulira m'chipululu kulikonse komwe mwatumizidwa, ndipo ndikukutumizani kuti mukasonkhanitse nkhosa zanga zotayika zomwe zatsala.

Pakuti khamu lalikulu lidzatuluka m'mavuto a Yakobo, ndipo mudzachita ntchito zamphamvu ndi kulamula zosatheka, pakuti ndidzapereka zizindikiro zamphamvu zambiri, zozizwitsa, ndi zodabwitsa kuti zichitidwe ndi manja anu. Mudzachita zozizwitsa zamphamvu zakale zolembedwa m'Malemba Oyera, ngakhale mu mphamvu ya Eliya, kupatula ntchito zazikulu ndi zamphamvu kwambiri mudzachita kuposa zomwe zalembedwa za iye kalekale.

Pakuti Malemba amati amene akhulupirira mwa ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, sadzangochita ntchito zomwe ndinachita pa dziko lapansi mu utumiki wanga ku Yudeya ngati mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu, koma ntchito zamphamvu kuposa zomwe ndinachita mudzachita, pakuti ndidzapereka zizindikiro zamphamvu, zozizwitsa, ndi zodabwitsa m'manja mwanu pamene mukulamula zosatheka mwa chikhulupiriro mwa ine. Choncho yang'anani kwa ine nthawi zonse. Ndipo sungani maso anu pa ine, pakuti ndidzakutsogolerani pang'onopang'ono. Kuyenda kwanu kudzakhala kwa nzeru, ndipo mudzakhala ndi lilime la anzeru, ndipo ndidzakhala mphamvu yanu ndi chipulumutso chanu, pakuti mwa ine, Khristu Yesu, kudzaza konse kumakhala.

Ndipo choncho ndikukulangizani nonse kuti posachedwapa ndalama zidzakhala zopanda phindu, ndipo ndalama zokha zovomerezeka zidzafuna chizindikiro kuti mukhale ndi mwayi wopeza katundu wa dziko lapansi. Musatenge chizindikirocho, pakuti kungakhale bwino kufa m'moyo uno (osakonda miyoyo yanu mpaka mapeto) kuposa kutenga chizindikirocho ndi kuponyedwa m'nyanja yamoto yamuyaya yokonzedwera Mdyerekezi ndi angelo ake, pakuti nthawi zawo zafika; koma musaphatikizane nawo. Pakuti adzalonjeza malonjezo omwe akuwoneka akulu ndi amtengo wapatali, koma mawu awo ndi onama, ndipo poizoni ali m'mawu awo, ndipo malonjezo awo ndi onama ndi odzaza ndi mitundu yonse ya zinthu zodetsedwa. Sadzatha kukwaniritsa malonjezo awo, pakuti chiweluzo chawo chikubwera; alibe mtendere, ndipo mtendere udzachotsedwa kotheratu pa dziko lapansi.

Zoipa za anthu sizidzakhala zoletsedwa konse; ndipo kwa ambiri, choletsacho chikachotsedwa, adzadzipereka okha mpaka muyaya ku misala ndi zoipa. Kupatula amene amakhala ndi lupanga ayenera kuphedwa ndi lupanga, ndipo adzagwa ndithu kumanzere ndi kumanja mu unyinji waukulu, koma sizidzafika kwa inu pamene mukuyenda chifukwa cha ine; koma musakonde miyoyo yanu mpaka mapeto ndipo tsatirani chitsanzo changa cha utumiki wopanda dyera ndi nsembe. Pakuti mphoto yaikulu idzakhala yanu chifukwa cha kumvera ndi kukhulupirika.

Choncho zilakolako ndi zoipa za anthu sizidzakhala zoletsedwa konse, ndipo Mdyerekezi ndi angelo ake sadzakhala oletsedwa konse, ndipo zonyansa zawo zonse zoipa sizidzakhala zoletsedwa konse, ndipo adzabwera ngati othandiza akale. Chinyengo chawo chimapita pansi ndipo chili ndi mizu yozama yomwe yatsala pang'ono kuzulidwa kotheratu. Sali milungu, ndipo sali otukuka abwino, koma onyenga, ndipo adziipitsa okha m'njira zonse. Ndipo choncho sadzakhala oletsedwa konse, ndipo kudzaza kwa ufumu wa Satana kudzatulutsidwa kuphompho, pakuti kudzatsegulidwa.

Izi zidzachitika kusonyeza munthu wamba zomwe wasankha ndi kupereka mwayi womaliza wa kulapa; pakuti zoipa sizidzabisidwa ndi kuletsedwa kachiwiri, ndipo chinsinsi chilichonse chidzabwera poyera. Ngakhale mabodza a chinyengo chawo chachikulu ndi zinyengo zamphamvu zidzaikidwa poyera kuti onse awone, pakuti Yahweh walengeza m'Malemba Oyera, ndipo zomwe Yahweh alengeza palibe amene angasinthe.

Ndipo choncho kulekanitsidwa komaliza kudzachitika, ndipo ambiri a okhutira omwe ndimawakonda adzasiyidwa ku mayesero ndi kuyesedwa komwe kukubwera, ngakhale kuti apulumutsidwe. Pakuti palibenso nthawi, ndipo chisomo changa ndi chifundo changa sizidzaperekedwanso m'njira yomweyo, ndipo palibenso nthawi ya njira zofunda. Aliyense adzayenera kupanga chisankho; ndipo kwa amantha ndi osakhulupirira adzakhala atasankha Satana ndi ufumu wake pamene akanatha kusankha ine, ngakhale Yesu Khristu, mwana wa Mulungu amene wabwera m'thupi ndipo ndadzipereka ndekha ngati Paskha wangwiro chifukwa cha machimo onse a anthu kamodzi ndi kwamuyaya. Ambiri adzakanabe mwamakani dzanja langa lothandiza mu nthawi ya kusowa kwakukulu, pakuti thandizo langa ndi thandizo lamuyaya ndi losatha la mphoto yaikulu, chifundo, chisomo, ndi chikondi.

Komabe, mbali yaikulu ya dziko lapansi idzakhulupirira mabodza ndipo idzadabwa pambuyo pa chilombocho, kuti, ndani angafanane ndi chilombocho? Adzalola mosavuta kulumidwa ndi kulumidwa kwa njoka komwe poizoni wake sangachiritsidwe, pakuti ndi wamuyaya. Kuti munthu asankhe zoipa mu nthawi zosankha kudzakhala chisankho chamuyaya, ndipo omwe apanga chisankhocho adzaima mu chiweluzo chachikulu.

Amene asankha zoipa mofunitsitsa adzayankha pa lingaliro lililonse loipa, mawu alionse oipa, ndi ntchito iliyonse yoipa yomwe achita moipa pa dziko lapansi, pakuti mabuku adzatsegulidwa ndipo sipadzakhala chifundo. Ndipo palibe munthu amene adzatha kuima mu chilungamo chake, pakuti munthu alibe chilungamo chake; pakuti njoka inawaipitsa kuyambira pachiyambi, ndipo masiku ake a kuipitsa akufika kumapeto. Choncho Yahweh ananditumiza ine, mwana wake wobadwa yekha, ngati pangano lamtendere kwa munthu wamba, ndipo munthu wamba wakana mtendere wosatha chifukwa munthu wamba amakonda zoipa.

Chiweluzo chachikulu chatsala pang'ono kugwera ufumu wa Satana. Chiweluzo ichi chidzaipiraipira, ndipo zochitika zomwe zachitidwa mu chiweluzo zidzaipiraipira. Tsoka lidzawonjezedwa pa tsoka ndipo mazunzo adzawonjezedwa pa mazunzo kwa onse omwe akana kulapa ntchito zawo zoipa. Pakuti ambiri sadzalapa ntchito za manja awo, kuti asakapembedze ziwanda, ndi mafano agolide, ndi asiliva, ndi amkuwa, ndi amwala, ndi amitengo: omwe sangawone, kapena kumva, kapena kuyenda: Ngakhale sadzalapa zakupha zawo, kapena ufiti wawo, kapena dama lawo, kapena zakuba zawo.

Pakuti ogwawo si milungu, koma onyenga onama, odziyesa, ochita sewero, ndi onyenga. Amabweretsa imfa ndi chiwonongeko chokha kwa omwe amawatsatira, ndipo onse omwe anyengedwa adzakhumudwa kotheratu, pakuti ziyembekezo zawo zidzasungunuka m'nyanja yaikulu yamoto yomwe mulibe chiyembekezo. Ndipo Ine, Khristu Yesu, ndidzapasula kotheratu ufumu wa Satana mu chiweluzo chachikulu, pakuti chiweluzo chachikulu chidzagwera pa onsewo, ndipo ndine Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda.

Ndipo choncho chidindo chilichonse chidzawonjezera chiweluzo pa chiweluzo, ndipo lipenga lililonse lidzawonjezera chiweluzo pa chiweluzo, tsoka lililonse lidzawonjezera tsoka pa tsoka, ndipo ndi mbale iliyonse kumabwera mkwiyo wonse wa Mulungu, ngakhale mkwiyo pa mkwiyo. Ufumu wa Satana udzavutika kwambiri, ndipo ufumu wake udzawonongedwa kotheratu ndi kupasulidwa kotheratu.

Pakuti adzasonkhana ndi kudzikonza okha motsutsana ndi kubwera kwa Ambuye, pakuti ndidzabwera ndithu. Kuyambira pachiyambi cha mavuto a Yakobo, kudzakhala masiku asanu ndi awiri a zaka, pakuti sabata la makumi asanu ndi awiri la Danieli lidzakwaniritsidwa chifukwa cha chiwonongeko cha chosalungama ndi chipulumutso cha Isiraeli. Pakuti ndidzayeretsa Isiraeli ku zoipa zomwe zagwira dzikolo.

Pakuti ngakhale Yahweh kapena ine ndemwe, Yeshua HaMashiach, sitinatumize kapena kulamula olamulira awo, pakuti amalamulira mu chosalungama ndipo kupha kwawo fuko ndi konyansa, ndipo anthu anga amalipira mtengo wa nkhondo zawo. Ndipo komabe ambiri a Mpingo wanga amakhulupirira mabodza awo a Zionist, ndipo choncho mvetsetsani kuti ndine si Zionist, pakuti iwo ndi opambana achiyuda, ndipo iwo ndi namsongole, mbewu yeniyeni ya Satana; ndipo ndidzawazula m'dzikolo, pakuti atenga cholowa changa, ndipo ndikubwera kudzachotsa chomwe chili changa. Chifukwa Ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, ngakhale ine, ndili ndi chikalata cha dziko lapansi; pakuti ndinaligula ndi magazi anga opanda tchimo, ndipo ndili ndi makiyi a imfa ndi helo. Ndipo ndalipira machimo a anthu, ndipo aliyense amene aitana pa dzina la Ambuye adzapulumutsidwa.

Choncho dziko lapansi ndi langa ndi kudzaza kwake, ndipo ndidzayeretsa dzikolo ku zoipa zawo, ndipo sunagoge wa Satana adzawonongedwa kotheratu, ndipo choncho ndidzabweretsa chipangano chatsopano pa dziko lapansi ndipo ndidzalamulira mu chilungamo. Pakuti pamene dziko lapansi likuda ndi kuipa kwambiri pa sekondi iliyonse, kubwera kwanga kwachiwiri kuli pafupi pa sekondi iliyonse.

Khalani ndi chiyembekezo chachikulu cha kubwera kwanga; pakuti ndidzabwera ndipo ndidzaphulika kuchokera kumwamba ndi magulu ankhondo akumwamba, ngakhale gulu lankhondo losafanana ndi lina lililonse, pakuti gulu lankhondo ngati langa silinakhalepo, gulu lankhondo losagonjetseka lopangidwa ndi omwe amakhala moyo wamuyaya ndipo sangaphedwe kapena kugonjetsedwa. Pakuti ndapatsidwa mphamvu ndi Yahweh chifukwa cha cholinga choterocho, ndipo ndidzakhala nditawapatsa mphamvu ndi kuwapatsa mphoto chifukwa cha kukhulupirika kwawo ndi chikondi chawo kwa ine ndi dzina langa, ngakhale Yesu Khristu! Pakuti aliyense amene amakonda ndi kutumikira Yeshua HaMashiach amakondanso ndi kutumikira Yahweh, pakuti palibe wamkulu kuposa Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya wa Isiraeli! Aliyense amene akana Mwana, yemweyo alibe Atate; ndipo ndine Yesu Khristu wobadwa yekha wa Atate amene anadzipereka yekha chifukwa cha machimo a onse, ndipo ndine wansembe wamkulu wamuyaya monga mwa dongosolo la Melikizedeki.

Ndipo ndidzagonjetsa kotheratu chinjoka, chilombo, ndi mneneri wonyenga. Pakuti chilombo ndi mneneri wonyenga adzaponyedwa amoyo m'nyanja yamoto yomwe imayaka malawi otentha kwambiri a buluu omwe sangazimitsidwe ndipo momwe simudzakhala ngakhale sekondi imodzi yopuma. Koma ntchito zawo zonse zoipa zidzabwezedwa kwa iwo mpaka muyaya. Kuvutika konse komwe abweretsa kudzabwezedwa mpaka muyaya, pakuti Yahweh adzabwezera ntchito iliyonse yoipa mpaka kotheratu m'zifuwa zawo zoipa, ndipo adzalipira mpaka muyaya chifukwa cha milandu yawo.

Ndipo choncho Satana adzamangidwa kuphompho kwa zaka chikwi, pakuti choncho kwalengezedwa ndi Yahweh. Ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndidzalamulira dziko lapansi mu chilungamo kwa tsiku limodzi. Pakuti kwa Yahweh tsiku lili ngati zaka chikwi ndipo zaka chikwi ngati tsiku limodzi. Ndipo ndidzalamulira kuchokera pampando wachifumu wa Davide, ndipo mu Isiraeli ulamuliro wanga udzakhala pokwaniritsa malonjezo omwe anapangidwa ndi Yahweh kwa Atate, ndipo tsiku langa latsala pang'ono kufika.

Koma choyamba chiweluzo chiyenera kubwera pa dziko lapansi ndipo dziko lapansi lidzayeretsedwa ku chinthu chilichonse choipa. Pakuti palibe chinthu choipa chomwe chidzalolezedwa mu ufumu wanga, momwe mudzakhala mtendere ndi chitetezo monga mwalonjezedwa ndi Yahweh mwiniwake. Ndipo mkango udzagona pansi ndi mwanawankhosa. Ine, ngakhale ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndikulengeza mawu asanachitike, kuti mudziwe kuti Mulungu wa Isiraeli wabwera kudzasonkhanitsa nkhosa zake zotayika za nyumba ya Isiraeli kwa ine, Mbusa Wamkulu, ndipo ndidzakantha mitundu ndi ndodo yachitsulo, ndipo ndidzagonjetsa kotheratu chilengedwe cha Atate mu chilungamo ndi chiyero chenicheni. Palibenso nthawi.

Ndine amene ndidzasonkhanitsa otsala anga ngati khamu lalikulu kuchokera ku chisautso chomwe chikubwera.
