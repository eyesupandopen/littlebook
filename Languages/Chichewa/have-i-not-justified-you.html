Epulo 1, 2024

Kodi Sindinakuyeseni Olungama?

Khalani chete ndipo khalani pamtendere, ndipo dziwani kuti Ine ndine Ambuye amene ndakuitanani kuti muime pamaso panga moongoka m'njira zanu zonse. Kodi sindinakuyeseni olungama? Kodi sindinakupangeni kukhala olungama mwa Ine, Ambuye Yesu Khristu? Kodi simukudziwika pamodzi ndi Ine m'masautso anga ndi mu ulemerero wanga? Ine ndine Mbuye wa ambuye ndi Mfumu ya mafumu, ndipo sindinama. Kodi Malemba Oyera sananene zimenezi? Imani mokhulupirika ndi moongoka, ndipo khalani pamtendere m'mitima yanu, ndipo dziwani kuti Ine, Ambuye Yesu Khristu, ndakupatsani chikhululuko changwiro ndi mtendere pamaso panga ngati mumvebe mawu anga ndi kulandira malangizo anga.

Nzeru ndiyo chinthu chachikulu; chotero, pezani nzeru — osati nzeru za dziko lapansi zomwe sizithandiza kanthu, koma nzeru za Ambuye. Ndipo pumulani pansi pa dzanja langa lamphamvu. Pakuti kuopa Ambuye ndiko kuleka zoipa ndi kuyenda moongoka pamaso panga, atero Ambuye wamphamvu, inde Ambuye Yesu Khristu. Imvani mawu anga! Imvani mawu anga ndipo musamvere wina aliyense! Mawu ambiri amatsutsana ndi Mawu anga ndipo amakunaminani, pamene akufunafuna ulamuliro pa miyoyo yanu kuti akupangeni kukhala chofunkha ngati muwatsatira. Musakonde dziko lapansi, ana anga, kapena zinthu za m'dziko lapansi. Dziko loipali likutha, ndipo Ine, Ambuye, ndanena zimenezi, ndipo sindidzatonthozedwa ndi kusakhulupirira kwanu, ndipo sindidzatonthozedwa ndi kusowa kwanu chikhulupiriro. Ayi, ndidzafuula kwambiri kuti mudziwe choonadi cha mawu amene ndikulankhula. Yerekezerani ndi Malemba Oyera. Kodi Ine sindine mawu amene anasanduka thupi amene akukhala pakati panu? Kodi sindinakupatseni kudzoza ndi mtonthozi kufikira nditabwera?

Dziwani, ana anga, kuti ndakugulani ndi magazi anga, ndipo dziko lapansi ndi langa, ndipo posachedwapa chilungamo chidzalamulira pa dziko lapansi. Ndalankhula zimenezi. Ndakupatsani moyo mwa Ine; koma musakane njira zanga. Ndakupatsani cholinga; koma musakane moyo umene ndakuitanirani. Ndakupatsani maitanidwe, inde, maitanidwe apamwamba omwe ndi amuyaya ndipo alibe mapeto a masiku ngati mutembenukira kwa Ine. Tembenukirani kwa Ine ndi nkhope ndi mtima wotseguka, ndipo ndiloleni ndikuyeretseni machimo anu ndi zoyipa zanu m'magazi anga otetezera. Onse amene ndiwakonda, ndimawadzudzula, kuti muime pamaso panga moongoka mu bafuta woyera bwino amene ndidzakupatsani posachedwa.

Zovala zanu zonse ndi nsanza zonyansa chabe zomwe mwadzisankhira kuti mubisalire Ine. Koma palibe pamene wina aliyense angabisale pamaso panga, ndipo ndimakuonani monga momwe mulili, ndiponso monga momwe ndakulengerani atsopano mwa Ine. Pali mphoto yaikulu yosungidwira ana anga okhulupirika; koma kwa iwo amene akundikana Ine, ndidzawakana. N’chifukwa chiyani mukugwiritsisabe dziko lomwe likufali ndi mavuto ake ndi zowawa zake? Muli ndi chisankho mwa Ine, chisankho chomwe mibadwo ina inalibe nsembe yanga yotetezera isanachitike. Chotero, aliyense amene andikana Ine mu nthawi zotsitsimula ali ndi tchimo lalikulu. Chikondi changa pa inu ndi chamuyaya ndipo sichingathe. Atate wanga ndi Ine ndife amodzi, ndipo zolinga zathu ndi mapulani athu sizingasiyanitsidwe. Atate wandipatsa zinthu zonse kuti ndipulumutse ena.

Musatenge malo anu pakati pa osakhulupirira, popeza ndakupatsani zifukwa zambiri zokhala ndi chikhulupiriro chachikulu. Chikhulupiriro cha kambewu ka mpiru ndicho chokhacho chomwe mukufunikira, kudalira pang'ono chabe. Dziyikeni m'zovala zomwe ndakupatsani. Kodi sindinathire mzimu wanga pa inu, inu okayikira anga? Musakhale osakhulupirira koma khulupirirani chabe, popeza nthawi ndi nyengo zili pa inu ndipo palibe kubwerera m'mbuyo. Dziko lomwe munkalidziwa silidzabwereranso, ndipo dziko lomwe mukulidziwa lero ndi lachinyengo chachikulu. Ngakhale zinthu zikuwonekabe zodziwika kwa inu, zonse zomwe munkazikonda zatengedwa ndi woyipayo; pakuti ali ndi ola loikidwa, ola lomwe ndikadapanda kulifupikitsa, palibe thupi lomwe likadatsala lamoyo. N’chifukwa chiyani mukulakalaka tsiku la Ambuye lomwe likubwera pa dziko lapansi kudzayesa mitima ya anthu? Ndi tsiku lamdima lomwe palibe munthu angathe kuima. Ndi tsiku loopsa lachiweruzo ndi zotulukapo zake, ndipo ngakhale m'masiku amdima kwambiri, aliyense amene adzaitana pa dzina la Ambuye mpaka mapeto adzapulumutsidwa.

Nthawi ndi nyengo zikusintha pamaso panu, koma simukukhulupirirabe maso anu. Ngakhale ndimachita zizindikiro ndi zozizwitsa mwa inu ndi pakati panu, mumatsekabe maso anu ndi kutseka makutu anu m'malingaliro opanda chikhulupiriro. Chisomo changa ndi chifundo changa ndi zamuyaya kuchokera ku mbadwo mpaka mbadwo, koma nthawi zikusintha, ndipo chiweruzo ndi tsoka zikuyandikira dziko lomwe mumalikonda kwambiri, ndipo zinthu zomwe mumazikonda zidzadutsa m'zala zanu ngati mchenga. Posachedwapa dziko lapansi silidzakhala ndi chiitano cha chisomo ndi chifundo, koma omwe adzapulumutsidwe adzayenera kupirira mpaka mpweya wawo womaliza kuti apulumutsidwe mwa Ine. Tembenukirani kwa Ine ndipo landirani chiitano changa, pakuti kukhala mwa Khristu ndiko kukhala ndi ubale wopulumutsa ndi Ine, Mbuye wa ambuye ndi Mfumu ya mafumu. Ndinu nokha amene mungalepheretse chipulumutso chanu potembenuza nkhope yanu kwa Ine mu kusakhulupirira ndi kukana kuitana kwanga. Taganizirani za Esau, amene anagulitsa ukulu wake chifukwa cha kachidutswa ka mkate kamene kanatayidwa kudzala, amene anapereka cholowa chake kwa m'bale wake mu kusakhulupirira. Kunyalanyaza mawu anga ndi kuyenda mumdima ndi tchimo, choncho musachimwe ndipo imani pamaso panga.

N’chifukwa chiyani mukudziyesabe osayenera chisomo changa, pamene chomwe muyenera kuchita ndi kulandira chabe? Landirani ntchito zanga zotetezera m'miyoyo yanu ndipo ndiloleni ndilowe kuti ndikuthandizeni mu nthawi yanu yosowa, ngati mitima yanu yosakhulupirira itandilola. Sankhani moyo, pakuti lero ndi nthawi yosankha; imvani mawu anga ndipo musaumitse mitima yanu m'chipululu monga momwe makolo anachitira ndikugwa m'mbali mwa njira. Musagwe kuchoka kwa Ine; musatsatire chiwonongeko ndi njira zowononga za kusakhulupirira, koma yang'anani kwa Ine ndipo khulupirirani mawu anga. Inde, nkhosa zanga zimamva mawu anga, choncho nditsatireni ku chitetezo. Musaganize kuti mtima wosakhulupirira ungathe kuima woyeretsedwa pamaso panga, pakuti kusakhulupirira ndiko kukana chikhulupiriro chomwe ndakupatsani mwa Ine.

Ndiyeretseni Ine, Ambuye Yesu Khristu, m'mitima yanu ndipo musaope, pakuti kuonekera kwanga kuli pafupi ndipo kuli pafupi kwambiri. Khulupirirani mawu anga ndipo yendani m'mapazi anga, pakuti njira yomwe ndakukonzerani imatsogolera ku moyo watsopano pamaso pa Mulungu. Pakuti Ine ndine njira yokhayo yopita kwa Mulungu; Ine ndine njira, choonadi, ndi moyo. Ngakhale woyipayo akulonjeza zinthu zambiri, sangathe kupereka chilichonse, ndipo sangathe kudzipulumutsa yekha m'dzanja langa. Ayi, mkwiyo waukulu wasungidwa pa iye, ndipo Satana ali ndi cholowa chomwe chikubwera chomwe anadzisankhira yekha m'nyanja yamoto ija. Onse amene amamusankha iye, onse amene amatenga chizindikiro chake, onse amene amalambira chilombo ndi fano lake adzamutsatira mu cholowa chake cha mkwiyo wosatha wa Mulungu.

Kodi Atate wanga sali wolungama poteteza chilengedwe chake? Kodi sali wolungama m'maweruzo ake ndi njira zomwe waika pamaso panu? Chotero, sankhani moyo ndipo sankhani cholowa chomwe ndakupatsani, pakuti ndikulonjeza kumwamba kwatsopano ndi dziko lapansi latsopano momwe mukukhala chilungamo. Ndikukulonjezani moyo wosatha, moyo wachuluka, mtendere, ndi chimwemwe. Komabe, mu kusowa kwanu chikhulupiriro, mukukanabe. Kodi ndakuchitirani chiyani kuti ndikukhumudwitseni mpaka kukana ubale ndi Ine, Mwana wa Mulungu, ndi Atate wa ulemerero? Kusankha dziko lino ndi njira zake ndiko kusankha imfa, ndipo pamapeto pake chisankho cha aliyense chidzakhala chomaliza, popeza nthawi zolekanitsa zafika; ndipo omwe sasankha, adzasankhiridwa.

Palibe chisangalalo mu chiwonongeko chomwe chikubwera chomwe posachedwapa chidzagwera dziko lanu. Masoka achilengedwe adzachuluka, ndipo dziko lapansi lidzagwedezeka, ndipo mafunde akulu adzagunda pa Babulo Wachinsinsi; pakuti dziko lapansi lidzang'ambika m'malo ambiri. Moto waukulu udzaphimba dzikolo, ndipo amalonda adzaima patali chifukwa choopa kuzunzika kwake. Chiwonongeko chachikulu chili pa hule, Babulo wamkulu, amene waledzeretsa dziko lapansi ndi zigololo zake ndi dama lake, pakuti wakhala ndi zibwenzi zambiri. Omwe sadzadzipatula kwa iye adzagawana naye chiweruzo chake, nanga n’chifukwa chiyani mungasankhe imfa m'malo mwa moyo womwe ndakupatsani, wolipidwa ndi magazi anga opanda tchimo? Nkhondo yatsala pang'ono kufika kwathunthu m'dziko lanu ndi mtundu wanu, ndipo ngakhale yayamba kale — inde, nkhondo yosalengezedwa yomwe akumenyana nanu — posachedwapa idzafalikira padziko lonse lapansi pamene zisindikizo zidzamasulidwa.

Nkhondo yaikulu ndi chizunzo chachikulu zidzabwera pa dziko lapansi pamene adzayesa kuwononga chilichonse cha Mulungu ndi Khristu wake pamene pamapeto pake adzalengeza poyera nkhondo yopatulika pa inu. Adzagwira ambiri, ndipo ambiri adzatengedwa, ndipo omwe adzapulumutsidwe adzakhala ndi chisankho chimodzi chokha: kupirira mpaka mapeto ndi kusakonda miyoyo yawo mpaka mapeto ndi kuitana pa dzina langa mpaka mapeto. Atsogoleri anu omwe akuperekeni ndipo adzapereka nsembe ambiri kwa woyipayo m'nkhondo zomwe posachedwapa zidzayaka. Mtundu wanu udzaukiridwa kuchokera mkati ndi kunja. Magulu ankhondo ofiira ali kale pano, ndipo akuyembekezera malamulo awo; pakuti akukonzekera kuukira kwadzidzidzi, ndipo ambiri adzatengedwa. Magulu ankhondo a woyipayo adzatenga chilichonse m'dzikolo kukhala chawo komanso kwa omwe akundikana Ine. Adzaba malo anu, nyumba zanu, mbadwa zanu; ndipo miyoyo yambiri idzatayika mu kusakhulupirira. Nkhondo ikubwera kuchokera kunja popeza mitundu yambiri yagwirizana kulimbana nanu; ndipo ali ndi chilolezo cha atsogoleri anu, ndipo atsogoleri anu akugwirira ntchito imfa yanu. Akufuna dziko lawo, dziko loperekedwa kwa Satana komwe angathe kutsatira kwathunthu zoyipa zomwe zikukhala mwa iwo, ndipo palibe chomwe chidzaletsa zilakolako zawo zoyipa, popeza woletsa adzakhala atachoka.

Adzatentha kwathunthu Babulo Wachinsinsi ndi moto, ndipo aliyense amene sadzafa ndi malawi amoto adzafa ndi njala; aliyense amene sadzafa ndi njala adzafa ndi mliri; aliyense amene sadzafa ndi lupanga adzaperekedwa ku mtengo wopachikira chifukwa cha dzina langa, ndipo ambiri adzadulidwa mitu monga momwe Mawu anga Oyera amanenera. Ndipo omwe aphedwa chifukwa cha dzina langa adzakhala ndi mphoto yaikulu, ndipo ndidzapukuta misozi yonse m'maso mwawo.

Dziwani, ana anga, kuti zokoma zonse ndi zosangalatsa za tchimo zomwe anthu oyipa amadzisungira zidzangosowa ngati mpweya, ndipo chisautso ndi zowawa zidzaima m'malo mwawo. Mdima udzaphimba dzikolo, ndipo kudzakhala kusowa kwa Mawu anga, ndipo mawu a mkwatibwi ndi mawu a mkwati sadzamvekanso mwa iye.

Ngakhale pamenepo, ndidzatumiza aneneri anga pakati panu, popeza ndidzasindikiza chiwerengero kuchokera ku mafuko otsala kuti atumikire kwa iwo, ndipo adzachita zozizwitsa zazikulu. Zizindikiro zambiri ndi zozizwitsa zidzaperekedwa m'masiku amdima amenewa, ndipo omwe adzaitana pa dzina la Ambuye mpaka mapeto adzapulumutsidwa. Nsanja zanu zidzagwa. Milatho yanu idzagwa. Njala idzagwa m'dzikolo, ndipo kudzakhala kusayeruzika kwathunthu, ndipo adzasaka omwe akuima m'malo mwa Ine, ndipo ambiri adzaphedwa; komabe mphoto yaikulu yakonzedwa kwa omwe adzaima mpaka mapeto. N’chifukwa chiyani mukulakalaka tsiku la Ambuye? Pakuti likadawononga thupi lonse pa dziko lapansi ndikadapanda kufupikitsa masikuwo. Kenako dziko lapansi lidzandiona ndikutuluka kumwamba ndi magulu ankhondo akumwamba, pakuti dziko lapansi ndi la Ambuye ndi kudzaza kwake.

Inde, ndikukuuzani zinthu izi zisanachitike, osati kuti muime pamenepo mu kusakhulupirira, koma kuti mukhale ndi chikhulupiriro ngati kambewu ka mpiru, pakuti chidzakula kuposa mitengo ina ndipo chidzakhala chosagwedezeka mwa Ine ngati mundilola kuyeretsa machimo anu ndipo ngati mubwera kwa Ine kuti muchiritsidwe ndi kutsitsimutsidwa. Kulira kwa lipenga kuli pafupi kwambiri ndipo kwatsala pang'ono kufika pa inu, ndipo ndidzakwatula mkwatibwi wanga pakati panu. Ndidzakhazikitsa malo patebulo langa kwa aliyense amene adzandipanga kukhala Ambuye poyankha zitseko zawo; pakuti taonani, ndaima ndikugogoda!

Ine ndine Mwana wa Davide ndipo ndidzalamulira dziko lapansi kuchokera pampando wake wachifumu.

Ine ndine muzu wa Jese; imvani mawu anga!

Ine ndine Mwana wa Munthu; musaope ndipo yang'anani kwa Ine.

Ine ndine Mwana wa Mulungu, inde, wobadwa yekha wa Atate.

Ine ndine woweruza wolungama amene amaweruza mu chiyero ndi chilungamo.

Ine ndine mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu wosankhidwa pakati pa gulu, wopanda banga ndi wopanda chilema.

Ine ndine khomo la nkhosa, ndipo palibe mimbulu yolusa yomwe ingalowe.

Ine ndine mkate wamoyo; aliyense amene adya umenewo sadzamva njala.

Ine ndine mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu amene ali woyenera kumasula zisindikizo zomwe zimamanga mpukutu.
