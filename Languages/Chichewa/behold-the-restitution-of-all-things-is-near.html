Zowawa Zotsekemera
Julayi 9, 2024

Taonani! Kubwezeretsedwa kwa Zinthu Zonse Kwayandikira!

Kuchokera kwa Yeshua HaMashiach:

Nthawi yakubwezeretsedwa ikuyandikira dziko lapansi, koma choyamba nthawi ndi kukwaniritsidwa kwa chiweruzo chachikulu ziyenera kukwaniritsidwa. Ndipo Ine, Khristu Yesu, ndidzaweruza dziko lapansi chifukwa dziko lapansi ndi langa ndi chidzalo chake, ndipo ndagula dziko lapansi ndi magazi anga owombola pa Kalvare. Mwa choonadi chosasinthika, ndinapereka dontho lililonse la magazi anga osalakwa omwe anakhetsedwa chifukwa cha machimo a anthu. Kwa iwo omwe amalandira pempho langa lamtendere ndi moyo wosatha kudzakhala mtendere, ndi moyo wosatha, ndipo njira zachilungamo zidzapambana. Kwa iwo omwe akukana pempho langa asankha njira zaimfa, chiweruzo, ndi chilango chamuyaya, pakuti dziko lapansi likupita ndipo palibe chisankho china. Ndipo chotero adzanyamula chiweruzo chawo ndipo tchimo lililonse, mawu oyipa, lingaliro loyipa, ndi mchitidwe woyipa zidzaululidwa kuti athe kupereka mlandu. Koma iwo omwe akukana chipulumutso changa sadzatha kuima, ndipo mwachisawawa asankha nyanja yamoto yomwe yakonzedwa chifukwa cha mdierekezi ndi angelo ake ndipo chinthu choterocho sichofunikira, koma aliyense ali mfulu kusankha. Ndine amene ndikubwera kudzaweruza ndi kuchita nkhondo, ndipo njira za Yahweh zidzalamulira dziko lapansi m'chilungamo mu ufumu wanga womwe ukubwera. Kwa inu Mpingo wanga, ngakhale Mkwatibwi wanga wokondedwa mudzatengedwa kupita kumwamba, ndipo mudzalandira cholowa chanu, ndipo mudzatenga malo anu pakati pa magulu omwe ndakukonzerani, ngakhale malo anu pakati pa Yakobo pakuti inu ndinu Isiraeli wa Mulungu.

Pakuti mwa ine, Khristu Yesu, mulibe Myuda kapena Wamitundu koma cholengedwa chatsopano, ndipo cholengedwa chimenecho chidzayenda ngati munthu mmodzi watsopano. Taonani! Mudzakhala munthu watsopano, ndipo mudzakhala cholengedwa chatsopano, kodi sindinakupatseni chikole cha mzimu woyera? Ngakhale chidindo cha Mulungu wamoyo, chomwe sichingasweke? Ndani wa inu angathe kusintha kubadwa kwa ana anu akuthupi? Simungathe chifukwa chibadwa chanu chimaperekedwa kuchokera ku mbadwo kupita ku mbadwo ndipo ana ndi amuyaya. Ndipo zili choncho, mbewu ya Yahweh yolengedwa mwa ana a Mulungu singawonongeke, pakuti n'zosatheka kuti mukwatulidwe m'manja mwanga. Kodi simunawerenge? Pakuti mwabadwanso, osati ndi mbewu yowonongeka, koma yosawonongeka, ndi mawu a Mulungu omwe ali amoyo, ndipo amakhala kosatha. Ana anga, kodi mawu anga ndi odabwitsa kwambiri? Kodi mwalola mawu onyenga kukusocheretsani kuchoka ku mtima umodzi mwa Khristu? Palibe chomwe chingachotse mbewu ya Yahweh ikalengedwa mkati, pakuti mphatso ndi kuitana kwa Mulungu zilibe kulapa. Nanga n'chifukwa chiyani mukukayikira chipulumutso chanu? Ngakhale thupi lanu lidzakuchimwitsani, ndipo ndidzakukhululukirani ndi kukuyeretsani pamene mukuulula machimo anu m'kulapa, palibe chomwe chingachotse ana a Mulungu m'banja la Mulungu. Kwa iwo omwe amakhala moyo wosalungama ntchito zawo zidzapsa, ndipo adzaima amaliseche ndi amanyazi, ndipo kwa iwo omwe ntchito zawo zimaoneka ngati zolungama adzalandira mphotho yaikulu, koma ana a Mulungu amalandira chipulumutso chamuyaya. Ngati munthu alibe mzimu wa Khristu, iye sali wake.

Kwa iwo omwe amakhala moyo wampatuko ndi kwa iwo omwe amanamizira kukhala ndi moyo wa Khristu m'dzina lokha adzagwa pamene Mpingo woona ukuchoka. Pakuti iye amene afesa m'thupi adzakolola chivundi m'thupi. Ndipo kwa iwo omwe anayesa kundipanga Ine kukhala Ambuye kuchokera ku mtima wopanda chikhulupiriro ndikupitiriza m'tchimo adzasiyidwa ku nsautso ya Yakobo chifukwa cha chiwonongeko cha thupi ndipo adzafunika kuitana pa dzina la Ambuye mpaka mapeto, ndipo zidzakhala choncho. Iwo omwe anadzinyenga okha mpaka kupuma kwawo komaliza ndipo sanafe mwa Khristu ndipo sanakhale ndi ubale wopulumutsa ndi ine adzakumana ndi chiweruzo chawo. Kodi mukuganiza kuti Yahweh yemwe amasanthula mitima ndi wopusa? Kodi mukuganiza kuti ine, Khristu Yesu, sindiyesa mitima ya anthu chifukwa cha chikhulupiriro ndi kudalira mwa ine? Koma zidzakhala choncho, ndipo ndidzati kwa ambiri omwe amanditcha Ambuye: Chokani kwa ine, inu ochita zoyipa, sindinakudziweni n'komwe.

Koma kwa inu Mpingo wanga, oitanidwa anga ndi okhulupirika ndi osankhidwa: Ngakhale mzimu uliwonse womwe wapeza chipulumutso wapangidwanso kukhala wolowa m'malo wa malonjezo opangidwa ndi Yahweh kwa Abambo. Pakuti kwalembedwa kuti Amitundu ayenera kukhala olowa m'malo anzathu, ndi a thupi limodzi, ndi ogawana nawo lonjezo lake mwa Khristu kudzera mu uthenga wabwino, ndipo ndawalumikiza mu Mtengo wa Azitona wachilengedwe. Ndipo onse omwe alengedwa mwatsopano mwa ine adzabwezeretsedwa kwathunthu ku ungwiro ndi chilungamo ndi chiyero choona. Kodi sindinakulonjezeni zovala zopanda banga, ana anga? Ndipo ndakuikirani cholowa chanu pakati pa mafuko, pakuti inu ndinu Isiraeli wa Mulungu yemwe ndamuwombola kuchokera pa dziko lapansi.

Siyani kuganiza za mibadwo ya Adamu, pakuti magazi a Adamu awonongeka m'tchimo! Monga kwalembedwa: munthu woyamba Adamu anapangidwa kukhala mzimu wamoyo, ndipo Adamu womaliza anapangidwa kukhala mzimu wopatsa moyo. Kodi simunawerenge? Sali onse Isiraeli, omwe ali a Isiraeli. Pakuti ana a lonjezo ndi mbewu ya Abrahamu mwa chikhulupiriro, ndipo monga kwalembedwa Wolungama adzakhala ndi moyo ndi chikhulupiriro. Ndipo mwa ine, Khristu Yesu, zinthu zonse zikukwaniritsidwa pakuti Mkango udzakhala mu umodzi wangwiro ndi Mwanawankhosa ndipo inu mukudziwika kwathunthu ndi ine, Khristu Yesu Ambuye wanu. Taonani! Koma kwa inu omwe mumaopa Dzina langa, mwana wachilungamo adzauka! Ndine mwana wa Chilungamo, ngakhale mwana wobadwa yekha wa Mulungu wam'mwambamwamba, Yahweh, pakuti palibe wina wofanana naye, ndipo ndine amene ndili pano kudzalanditsa mpingo wanga ku tsoka, ngakhale mkwatibwi wanga kupita kunyumba kwake kumwamba, ndipo adzakhala nane nthawi zonse. Pakuti zidzakhala choncho, ndidzakulumikizani ku banja lanu lenileni pakuti Isiraeli anamwazikira kudziko lina ndipo mu Isiraeli wa Mulungu adzasonkhanitsidwa, kuti asadzamwazikenso, pakuti inu ndinu Isiraeli wa Mulungu!

Chotero ndidzatenga Mpingo wanga kupita kumwamba, okhulupirika anga, ndipo mudzakhala nane kosatha. Pakuti mwakwatiwa ndi mwamuna mmodzi: Yeshua HaMashiach, ndipo ndi mzimu wanga ndakupangani kukhala namwali woyera ndikuyeretsa okhulupirika kuti athe kuima olungama mu nsembe yanga yowombola. Pamene mudzatengedwa mudzalandira mphotho chifukwa cha kukhulupirika kwanu ndipo mudzatenga akorona ambiri, ngakhale ena adzataya. Mudzalandira mphotho kuposa momwe mungaganizire, pakuti diso silinawone kapena khutu kumva kapena kulowa mu mtima wa munthu zinthu zomwe Yahweh wakonzera iwo omwe amamukonda. Ndipo okhulupirika, ngakhale Mkwatibwi wanga, adzakhala nane kosatha, ndipo adzalamulira ngati mafumu ndi ansembe kosatha, pakuti Ufumu wanga ulibe mapeto.

Ndipo chotero mngelo wanga adzakwera kumene mudzakhala nane kumwamba ndikuika chidindo kwa 12,000 kuchokera ku fuko lililonse. Kodi simunawerenge Mawu anga operekedwa kwa Yohane Wovumbulutsa? Ndipo ndinawona mngelo wina akukwera kuchokera kum'mawa, ali ndi chidindo cha Mulungu wamoyo: ndipo anafuula ndi mawu akulu kwa angelo anayi, omwe anapatsidwa mphamvu zovulaza dziko lapansi ndi nyanja, nati, musavulaze dziko lapansi, kapena nyanja kapena mitengo, mpaka titaika chidindo kwa atumiki a Mulungu wathu pamphumi pawo. Kodi simunawone mawu oti kukwera? Angelo oyera akumwamba amatsikira pa dziko lapansi, pakuti nyumba yawo ili kumwamba. Kuti mngelo woyera akwere, akukwera kupita kumwamba. Ndi angelo ogwa omwe amakwera pa dziko lapansi kuchokera ku phompho, kodi simulemekeza Mawu a mawu anga oyera?

Ndipo adzaika chidindo kwa 12,000 kuchokera ku fuko lililonse lotchulidwa: Yuda, Rubeni, Gadi, Aseri, Nafutali, Manase, Simeoni, Levi, Isakara, Zebuloni, Yosefe, Benjamini. Ndipo chotero a 144,000 anga adzakhala ndi chidindo cha Mulungu wamoyo pamphumi pawo. Ndipo adzaima nane pa Phiri la Ziyoni, ndipo adzayimba ngati nyimbo yatsopano pamaso pa mpando wachifumu, ndi pamaso pa zamoyo zinayi, ndi akulu: ndipo palibe munthu amene akanatha kuphunzira nyimboyo koma zikwi zana limodzi ndi makumi anayi mphambu zinayi, omwe anawomboledwa kuchokera pa dziko lapansi. Awa ndiwo omwe sanadziyipitse ndi akazi; pakuti ndi anamwali. Awa ndiwo omwe amatsatira Mwanawankhosa kulikonse komwe akupita. Pakuti awa anawomboledwa pakati pa anthu, kukhala zipatso zoyambirira kwa Mulungu ndi kwa Mwanawankhosa. Ndipo m'kamwa mwawo simunapezeke chinyengo: pakuti alibe cholakwa pamaso pa mpando wachifumu wa Mulungu.

A 144,000 asankhidwa ndi Yahweh lisanakhazikitsidwe dziko lapansi chifukwa cha nthawi yotere. Amapangidwa m'masautso, ndipo adzakhala ndi maluso ndi mphamvu zapadera zomwe ndidzawaveka m'zovala zawo zatsopano zomwe ndidzawapatsa, pakuti zovala zanu zapangidwa makamaka chifukwa cha inu ndipo zidzakhala ndi zomwe mwakwaniritsa ndi makhalidwe anu. Adzatengedwa pakusonkhanitsidwa kwa okhulupirika koma asankhidwa chifukwa cha zolinga zanga, ngakhale zolinga za Yahweh ndi Yeshua HaMashiach. Amabwera pa nthawi ya chisautso chachikulu kudzatsogolera Isiraeli ku chitetezo. Koma choyamba amatengedwa kukakwatiwa ndi Khristu pakuti ndi Mkwatibwi wake, oitanidwa, osankhidwa, okhulupirika, ndi oona. Ngakhale iwo omwe Khristu wawapanga kukhala oyera ngati namwali m'magazi ake owombola. Ndipo ndinu mkwatibwi wanga amene mudzatumikira zolinga za Yahweh pa lamulo langa mu nthawi za mavuto akulu kwambiri.

Pakuti inu ndinu zipatso zoyambirira zosonkhanitsidwa kuchokera m'minda yokolola, pakuti ndine Ambuye wa zokolola, ndipo tsopano ndi nthawi yoti ndikusonkhanitseni m'nkhokwe yanga kuti mulandire mphotho ya ntchito zanu. Pakuti iwo omwe asonkhanitsa tirigu adzakhala ndi zambiri ndipo iwo omwe asonkhanitsa udzu adzataya pamene mankhusu akupsa pamoto, koma adzapulumutsidwa. Ndipo chotero antchito anga ndi anthu anga adzabwera ku cholowa chawo mwa ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, pakuti ndine Mbuye wa Ambuye ndi Mfumu ya Mafumu ndipo sindidzatontholetsedwa kapena kuimitsidwa.

Kodi sikunalembedwe? Chotero, atero Ambuye MULUNGU; Taonani, ndidzatenga ana a Isiraeli pakati pa amitundu, kulikonse komwe anapita, ndipo ndidzawasonkhanitsa mbali zonse, ndi kuwabweretsa m'dziko lawo: Ndipo ndidzawapanga kukhala mtundu umodzi m'dziko pa mapiri a Isiraeli; ndipo mfumu imodzi idzakhala mfumu kwa iwo onse: ndipo sadzakhalanso mitundu iwiri, kapena kugawidwa kukhala maufumu awiri kachiwiri: Ndipo sadzadziyipitsanso ndi mafano awo, kapena ndi zinthu zawo zonyansa kapena ndi zolakwa zawo zilizonse: koma ndidzawapulumutsa m'malo awo onse okhala, momwe anachimwira, ndipo ndidzawayeretsa: chotero adzakhala anthu anga, ndipo Ine ndidzakhala Mulungu wawo.

Ine Yeshua HaMashiach ndatumiza Mawu awa kudzera mwa mtumiki wanga, ndipo kudzera mwa mtumiki wanga Mawu awa atumizidwa! Taonani! Ndine amene ndikubwera m'mitambo, chotero dzekonzekereni, anamwali anga anzeru.

Kuchokera kwa Yahweh Mulungu Wamkulu Wamuyaya:

Ndikuwona mantha anu, inu ogwa, pakuti tsopano pakubwera chiweruzo chanu ngakhale chiweruzo chachikulu cholonjezedwa kale, ndi nthawi za chiweruzo chachikulu cholengezedwa m'nthawi zakale ndi ine, Yahweh Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya yemwe analengeza chiweruzo chanu ndipo mabingu asanu ndi awiri anatulutsa mawu awo ndipo chotero mawu a Mulungu wamphamvuyonse adzamveka! Ndipo zolinga zanga zidzabweretsedwa pamodzi, ngakhale mapulani anga a mibadwo adzakwaniritsidwa mwa mwana wanga Yesu Khristu. Pakuti mu zipatso zoyambirira kumabwera mphotho za zokolola kwa antchito otumizidwa ndi Yeshua HaMashiach ndipo tirigu wanga tsopano adzasonkhanitsidwa m'nkhokwe yanga ndipo kulekanitsa komaliza pa dziko lapansi kudzayamba ndipo nsautso ya Yakobo yafika.

Taonani! Ndidzang'amba kumwamba pamwamba ndikugwedeza dziko lapansi pansi, ndipo ndidzasonkhanitsa olowa m'malo otsala a chipulumutso kuchokera pa dziko lapansi, ndipo adzabwera ku chipulumutso, ndipo adzaitana pa dzina la Ambuye mpaka mapeto, ndipo adzanditumikira m'kachisi wanga ndipo khamu lamphamvu ndi lalikulu lidzapulumutsidwa kuchokera mu sabata la makumi asanu ndi awiri la Danieli. Ine, ngakhale Yahweh, ndidzang'amba dziko lapansi ndikuchotsa mapiri m'malo awo ndipo dziko lonse lapansi lidzadziwa kuti El Shaddai wauka kuchokera kumwamba ndipo chiweruzo chachikulu tsopano chidzagwa pa dziko lapansi pamene Okhulupirika anga asonkhanitsidwa pakuti zolakwa zidzatha, ngakhale cholakwa cha Yakobo ndi machimo a nyumba ya Isiraeli.

M'malo mwawo mudzabwera ulamuliro wachilungamo, ngakhale ulamuliro wachilungamo wa mwana wanga wobadwa yekha, Yeshua HaMashiach, mwa yemwe ndimakondwera naye nthawi zonse. Ndi iye amene adzakantha mitundu ndi ndodo yachitsulo ndipo ulamuliro wachilungamo udzakhala, pamene Mkango udzagona pansi ndi mwanawankhosa. Ndipo chotero adzakhala mkulu wa ansembe kosatha monga mwa dongosolo la Melikizedeki ndipo Mbuye wa Ambuye ndi Mfumu ya Mafumu adzagwirizana kwathunthu mu kukwaniritsidwa kwamuyaya ndipo ulamuliro wake udzakhala kosatha, ngakhale Yeshua HaMashiach pakuti yemweyo ndi Yesu Khristu!

Konzekerani inu amene mumakhala pa dziko lapansi m'chosalungama pakuti ndikutumiza mwana wanga kudzachita chiweruzo changa ndi kuphwanya ulamuliro wonse wa ogwa, pakuti nthawi yawo yafika ndipo chiweruzo chidzagwa cholemera pa dziko lapansi. Pakuti mwadziunjikira mkwiyo waukulu pa tsiku la mkwiyo ndipo tsiku la mkwiyo lili pa inu, inu opanda pake, pakuti mwadziyipitsa nokha mu kuwonongeka kwakukulu, ndipo mulibe mtendere chifukwa cha njira zanu zonyansa. Koma musanapite ku cholowa chanu chamuyaya kumene mudzamezedwa m'malawi amoto otentha kwambiri a buluu, choyamba mudzatumikira zolinga zanga, pakuti ngakhale adani anga amamvera mawu anga.

Ndipo chotero chiweruzo chidzayambira m'nyumba ya Mulungu ndipo ndidzatumiza mwana wanga ngakhale Khristu Yesu Mesiya wouka wa Isiraeli ndi mpulumutsi wa dziko lapansi, ndipo adzachita zonse zomwe ndinakhazikitsa ndi kulonjeza kwa Abambo ndi kwa ana ndipo zonse zidzakhala inde ndi ameni pamene kukwaniritsidwa kwa malonjezo kukuchitika.

Pakuti kumwamba kwakonzedwa chifukwa cha Ana anga, ngakhale onse omwe ndinawakhazikitsa chifukwa cha zolinga zanga chilengedwe chisanayambe. Pakuti ndinadziwa aliyense wa inu m'nthawi zamuyaya zakale, ndipo ndinakuikani ngati chinsinsi chobisika mu mtima mwanga chomwe ndinavumbulutsa pa nthawi yoyenera mu dongosolo langa lamuyaya la chipulumutso. Palibe chomwe chingachitike chomwe sindikuchidziwa chisanachitike, ndipo Ndine, Mulungu Wamphamvuyonse, yemwe amatcha zinthu zomwe palibe ngati kuti zilipo kale. Oh, inu ogwa, mudzapsa kwathunthu mu chiweruzo chanu chomwe chikubwera, ndipo munali opusa mokwanira kuganiza kuti mungatenge Yahweh mwachinyengo, koma NDINE AMENE amatenga onyada mu chiweruzo, pakuti ndi anzeru m'malingaliro awo. Pakuti ndinangokuwonetsani zinsinsi zopanda pake, pakuti ndinadziwa kuti mudzapereka zonse zomwe zili zanga. Ndinu opusa bwanji inu ogwa ndipo simuli anzeru. Zonse zomwe mwakwaniritsa ndi kuzunzika kwanu ndi chiwonongeko chopitirira m'malawi amoto otentha kwambiri, ndipo mudzasambira mu sulufule wamoto, pakuti chotero nyanja yaikulu imayaka kosatha, ndipo ndidzayika chidindo, ndipo mudzaiwalika kwathunthu, ndipo simudzakumbukiridwa.

Koma tsoka ili silanu inu osankhidwa anga, okhulupirika anga, ngakhale onse omwe amatchedwa ndi dzina langa. Lanu ndi tsogolo la chimwemwe chamuyaya, chisangalalo chamuyaya, mtendere wamuyaya ndipo sipadzakhala mapeto a masiku ndipo sipadzakhala tsiku loyipa kwa iwo omwe amalowa m'paradaiso. Taonani! Ndidzabwezeretsa zinthu zonse ndikuzipanga kukhala zabwino kuposa ungwiro wanga woyambirira chivundi chisanafikire, pakuti ndidzalenga kumwamba kwatsopano ndi dziko lapansi latsopano momwe mumakhala chilungamo ndipo palibe chomwe chidzalowe chomwe chili choyipa kapena chodetsedwa. Pakuti kunja kuli agalu, ndi amatsenga, ndi adama, ndi opha anthu, ndi opembedza mafano, ndi aliyense amene akonda ndi kupanga bodza, ndipo onse adzakhala ndi gawo lawo m'nyanja yamoto yoyaka kumene nyongolotsi yawo sifa.

Chotero palibe choti muwope m'tsogolo mwanu oitanidwa anga, osankhidwa, ndi okhulupirika, ndipo mudzalamulira kosatha ndi Yeshua HaMashiach ndipo palibe mapeto a ufumu wake kapena ulamuliro wake. Ndipo ine Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya, ngakhale Yahweh mwiniwake ndidzapanga malo anga okhala pakati pa anthu ndipo kudzakhala mtendere ndi ungwiro wogwirizana pa chilengedwe changa chonse ndipo ndidzapukuta misozi yonse m'maso mwawo ndipo sipadzaukanso wina aliyense amene adzakuvutitseni ngakhale pang'ono, pakuti Ine, Yahweh, ngakhale Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya, ndikulumbira zinthu izi ndi dzina langa pakuti sindingathe kulumbira ndi wamkulu wina. Koma kwa inu omwe mumaopa dzina langa Mwana wa Chilungamo adzauka ndi machiritso m'mapiko ake; ndipo mudzatuluka, ndikukula ngati ana a ng'ombe a m'khola.

Ine Yahweh, Mulungu Wamkulu wamuyaya, ndatumiza Mawu anga kudzera mwa mtumiki wanga, ndipo kudzera mwa mtumiki wanga Mawu anga atumizidwa.

NDINE NDINE Wamkulu ndipo palibe wina!

Mpsompsoneni Mwana kuti angakwiye, ndipo muwonongeke m'njira, pamene mkwiyo wake uyaka pang'ono.
