Zowawa Zotsekemera
Novembala 16, 2024

Taonani! Mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu!

Imvani Mawu a Yeshua HaMashiach ndipo tcherani makutu anu ku phokoso la Mawu anga ndipo dulani mitima yanu kuti mulandire Mesiya wanu weniweni, O' Israyeli, ndipo mvetsetsani kulankhula kwanga kuti mukhale anzeru m'njira zanu, ndi kuti mundilole kuti ndilunjikitse mapazi anu ndi kusintha njira yanu kuchoka ku njira yotakata ya chiwonongeko yomwe mukutsatira, kupita ku njira yopapatiza ya chipulumutso yomwe ili njira zanga ndipo yomwe ine ndili. Chifukwa ine ndine amene Yahweh anatumiza chifukwa cha chipulumutso chanu, ndipo ine ndine mbewu yolonjezedwa ya mkazi yomwe idzaphwanya mutu wa njoka, ndipo ndikukuitanirani kwa ine ndekha abale anga, ngakhale abale anga omwe ine ndine muwomboli wawo. Choncho ndikukulamulani kuti mudule mitima yanu ndikusiya mikangano yanu yopanda phindu kuti mubale zipatso mwa Mesiya wanu weniweni choncho imvani malangizo anga omwe aperekedwa kuti mumvere kuti mukhale ndi moyo pamaso pa Yahweh Wamkulu INE NDINE, ngakhale pondilandira ine monga Mesiya wanu wamuyaya yemwe mwaonadi, mwaonadi: Ine ndine.

Ndipo monga momwe Yahweh anatumizira chilamulo cha Mose kwa Makolo anu (chilamulo chomwe ndi mphunzitsi wanu wofuna kukubweretsani kwa Khristu), momwemonso Yahweh wanditumiza ine monga momwe analonjezera kale pamene analonjeza chipulumutso kwa Makolo omwe ana awo ndikuwalankhula tsopano, ngakhale ana a Israyeli omwe ndimawakonda ndi chikondi choyaka nthawi zonse. Koma mu umbuli ndi kusakhulupirira kopanda chikhulupiriro ndinakanidwa ndi Makolo anu ndikuperekedwa m'manja mwa anthu ochimwa kuti achite ndi nkhanza zikulu ndi chidani zinthu zomwe chilamulo chinawaletsa kuchita; komanso kuti aoneke ngati olungama kwa anthu a m'dzikolo pamene anapha mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu ndi chilolezo cha Makolo anu, ngakhale mlandu womwe ambiri a inu mukuvomerezabe mopusa.

Komabe, mvetsetsani Israyeli kuti sindinatengedwe mokakamiza ndi adani anga ndi adani anu. Mvetsetsani, kuti sindinatengedwe mokakamiza, kapena mwachinyengo ndipo palibe chida chopangidwa motsutsana ndi ine chomwe chingapambane, chifukwa Yahweh walengeza choncho ndipo zomwe Yahweh walengeza palibe amene angasinthe! Zoonadi, Israyeli, ndine amene ndinati kwa Yudasi Isikarioti pamene ndinaperekedwa ndi dzanja lake pa ndalama zasiliva makumi atatu, ichi chomwe ukuchita, chita msanga! Chifukwa mtengo woyenera kwa mwini kapolo yemwe kapolo wake anagundidwa ndi Ng'ombe unali moyo wanga wogulitsidwa m'manja mwa omwe anandiboola, woperekedwa ngakhale ndi manja a ansembe aakulu ndi Sanhedrini omwe ndinawatsutsa chifukwa cha njira zawo zoipa ndi zachiphamaso. Ndipo choncho Yudasi anandipereka m'manja mwa Esau, ngakhale iye yemwe wanena kuti dziko lolonjezedwa ndi lake kachiwiri mokakamiza, ngakhale chidziwitso chomwe Esau anagulitsa pa kachidutswa ka mkate. Ndipo komabe, ndinadzilola mofunitsitsa kutengedwa ndi adani anga pamene ndikanatha kuthawa mosavuta, ndipo mwaonadi, mwaonadi, ndikanapemphera kwa Yahweh, akanandipatsa ulamuliro wa magulu a Angelo oposa khumi ndi awiri, koma kodi Malemba akanakwaniritsidwa bwanji, ndipo choncho zinatha.

Komabe, zili choncho, kuti zolinga zanga ndi njira zanga zinamveka molakwika ndi abale anga ndi a mtundu wanga omwe ndimawakonda kwamuyaya ndi chikondi chozama ndi choyaka chomwe sichingazimitsidwe kapena kugonjetsedwa, chifukwa ndine Thanthwe lanu la Maziko lomwe popanda ilo palibe womanga angamange, ndipo pomwe opanda chikhulupiriro amapunthwa. Chifukwa chinali m'chikondi changa pa inu, ndi Adamu yense yemwe wagonjetsedwa ndi ogwawo, kuti ndinadzipereka mofunitsitsa m'manja mwa adani anga kuti ndikuwomboleni ku themberero la chilamulo.

Ndipo choncho ndi lamulo langa lamuyaya, kuti mukondane wina ndi mzake, monga momwe ndakukonderani inu. Palibe munthu ali ndi chikondi choposa ichi, pamene munthu aliyense apereka moyo wake nsembe chifukwa cha abwenzi ake, zomwe ndinachita mofunitsitsa podziwa kuti popanda kumvera kwanga chifuniro cha Atate wanga anthu akanaatayidwa kwamuyaya. Ndipo ndine mpulumutsi wa Adamu ngakhale iye yemwe wawombola anthu ku mkhalidwe wawo wogwa ngati munthu aliyense abwera kwa ine atagwada ndi kundivomereza monga Ambuye wawo ndipo ndithudi ndine Mesiya wanu weniweni O' Israyeli.

Mvetsetsani abale anga, kuti sindinadzinenera zinthu zomwe sizinalembedwe ndi chala cha Yahweh pa magome amiyala ndikunenedwa m'kamwa mwa atumiki ake aneneri kuti zilembedwe chifukwa cha chikhulupiriro ndi kumvera kwa olowa m'malo a lonjezo, omwe inu muli O' Israyeli, ngati simudzaumitsa mitima yanu monga momwe Makolo anu anachitira m'chipululu pakuputa kwawo Yahweh, ndipo m'chipululu miyoyo yawo yopanda chikhulupiriro inatha pachabe, ndipo iwo eniwo sanalandire malonjezo koma ana a chikhulupiriro!

Ndipo komabe m'khungu lanu lina mumakhulupirira mabodza a Esau akuti ndine wonyenga ndipo ndithudi ndinakanidwa ndi Esau chifukwa Esau akufuna kutenga cholowa chomwe anagulitsa kwa wamng'ono ndipo palibe njira yolapa kwa Esau, ngakhale anaifunafuna ndi misozi. Ndipo Makolo anu sanamvetsetse zolinga zanga zenizeni chifukwa anafunafuna mtsogoleri wapadziko lapansi kuti agonjetse omwe anadzipereka kwa iwo mu ugonjera ngakhale kuti athe kusunga maudindo awo pakati pa anthu. Komabe, ndinatumizidwa kudzalanditsa anthu m'manja mwa ogwawo omwe Adamu anawaphatikiza mopusa pakusamvera kwake lamulo la Yahweh chifukwa cha machenjera a njoka yomwe inamunyenga iye ndi mayi wa amoyo onse pamene anachimwa ku Edene pachiyambi. Ndipo ngakhale mitundu yambiri yauka ndi kugwa, ndipo maufumu ambiri auka ndi kugwa kuyambira nthawi ya nsembe yanga, Israyeli adakali mu ukapolo kwa adani awo ngakhale mpaka lero ndipo amatumikira zolinga za Esau, yemwe akufuna chiwonongeko chawo chotheratu.

Inde, Israyeli wakhala mu ukapolo chifukwa mtundu wanu unatengedwa kwa inu monga chilango chifukwa chopha magazi osalakwa, ngakhale kupha mofunitsitsa ndi mwankhanza munthu wopanda tchimo yemwe anatumizidwa ndi Yahweh chifukwa cha chiwombolo chanu. Ngakhale ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, pamene kukwanira kwa nthawi kunafika, Mulungu anatumiza Mwana wake wobadwa mwa mkazi, ndipo wobadwa pansi pa chilamulo, kuti awombole omwe anali pansi pa chilamulo. Ndipo m'moyo wanga wopanda tchimo ndi kumvera ndinadzipereka ndekha monga mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu mu nsembe yowawa ndi yankhanza pamene ndinadzipereka ndekha monga mkulu wa ansembe kwamuyaya monga mwa dongosolo la Melikizedeki.

Ndipo utumiki womwe watha ndipo ndakhala pansi kudzanja lamanja la Atate wanga, ngakhale Yahweh, INE NDINE wamkulu. Ndipo ndalandira zinthu zonse, ndipo m'manja mwanga mwamphamvu mwaperekedwa zinthu zonse kumwamba ndi padziko lapansi mpaka adani anga atapangidwa chopondapo mapazi anga koma Ufumu wanga sudzatha! Ufumu womwe Mfumu yanu idzalamulira ukuyandikira ndithu kumapeto kwa nsautso ya Yakobo komwe ndidzawononga ufumu wa adani ndikubweretsa chiweruzo chachikulu ndi zotulukapo pa ogwawo ndipo m'chilango adzapita chifukwa cha machimo awo omwe ndi ambiri ndipo chiweruzo chawo chamuyaya chomwe sipadzakhala kupuma kwa mphindi imodzi. Ndipo ena a iwo adzapita m'nyanja yamoto yoyaka ndi sulufule pa kubwera kwanga kachiwiri kudzalamulira dziko lapansi pamene chilombo ndi mneneri wonyenga adzaponyedwa amoyo mmenemo. Ndipo a Satana adzamangidwa m'unyolo waukulu womwe adzamangidwa nawo mu chizunzo ndi mantha aakulu pamene amangidwa mu mantha oyembekezera zomwe zikuwayembekezera mu cholowa chawo chamuyaya cha chilango kwamuyaya. Ngakhale chiweruzo choopsa chomwe Satana sangathawe komwe amangidwa ndikuponyedwa kumoto wotentha kwambiri kwamuyaya kumapeto kwa nyengo yake yaing'ono.

Chifukwa, ndine amene ndinaperekedwa chifukwa cha chipulumutso cha Yakobo, ngakhale mbewu yolonjezedwa ya mkazi yomwe Adamu anakhulupirira mu chiyembekezo, ndipo adzaukitsidwa ku moyo watsopano mu umuyaya wonse. Chifukwa ndine amene ndatumizidwa chifukwa cha chiwombolo cha Adamu, ndipo ndinapereka dontho langa lomaliza la magazi lomwe linakhetsedwa ndikuloŵa m'nthaka yofiira ku Kalvare, ngakhale komwe ndinanena mawu osatha aja Zatha. Ndipo choncho zili, kuti ndine amene m'chikondi changa pa Yahweh ndi chikondi changa pa munthu mnzanga ndinadzipereka nsembe mu ululu waukulu, manyazi, ndi chizunzo ndipo ndagonjetsa tchimo ndi imfa ndipo tsiku likuyandikira pamene chinthu chilichonse choipa sichidzakhalaponso.

Ndipo ndinachita izi mofunitsitsa, ngakhale modzifunira chifukwa cha ulemerero womwe unayikidwa pamaso panga ndipo ndinachita izi ponyoza manyazi onse omwe sanali anga kunyamula. Komabe m'chikondi changa pa inu, ndinatenga manyazi anu ndi chilango chanu pamsana panga, ngakhale pamene anang'amba mnofu wanga m'mafupa anga ndi zikwapu zawo. Inde, ndinatenga manyazi anu ndi chilango chanu mu nkhanza ndi chidani cha anthu ochimwa omwe anasonkhezeredwa ndi ogwawo enieni pamene anandipha mosadziwa m'malo mwanu osamvetsetsa m'njira zawo zankhanza kuti popha mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu anali kusindikiza tsogolo lawo kumoto kwamuyaya chifukwa n'zosatheka kuti imfa indimange, chifukwa sindinachimwepo ngakhale kamodzi, ngakhale pang'ono, ndipo mwaonadi sindidzachimwa!

Inde, Israyeli, ndinatenga malo anu m'chilango ndi malo a Makolo anu omwe anandikana mosadziwa pa lamulo la Esau! Komabe mukanawomboledwa bwanji kupatula mwa kupereka kwawo kopanda chikhulupiriro chifukwa kuti awombole Adamu magazi opanda tchimo okha ndi omwe akanakwaniritsa zofunikira za chilango zofunidwa ndi malamulo amuyaya ndi olungama a Yahweh chifukwa popanda kukhetsa magazi palibe chikhululukiro cha tchimo. Ndipo popeza Adamu analengedwa wopanda tchimo ndi wangwiro pa tsiku lomwe analengedwa, momwemonso ine, Yeshua HaMashiach (yemwe ndi mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu) ndinalengedwa wangwiro ndi wopanda tchimo chifukwa cha chiwombolo chokwanira cha anthu. Pakuti ngati magazi a ng'ombe ndi a mbuzi, ndi phulusa la ng'ombe yayikazi, powaza omwe ali odetsedwa, amayeretsa kukhudza kuyeretsedwa kwa thupi, kuli bwanji magazi a Khristu yemwe kudzera mwa Mzimu wamuyaya anadzipereka wopanda chilema kwa Mulungu, adzayeretsa chikumbumtima chanu ku ntchito zakufa, kuti mutumikire Mulungu wamoyo?

Choncho ndikukupatsani kuti mumvetsetse kuti ndinatenga malo anu m'chiweruzo ndi chilango, ndipo ndinachita izi mofunitsitsa komanso mopanda kukakamizidwa; ngakhale ndinapemphera m'munda wa Getsemane ngati pali njira ina iliyonse, koma osati chifuniro changa koma cha Yahweh chichitidwe. Ndipo choncho m'manyazi anga, ndinatengedwa kunja kwa zipata za mzinda waukuluwo, (ngakhale Yerusalemu yemwe amapha aneneri) komwe ndinatumizidwa kwa anga, ndipo kumeneko zovala zanga zinagawidwa pamene anachita maere! Inde, kumeneko ku Kalvare, nditapirira kale maola a chilango chosatha chomwe ndinali wosalakwa, ndinapachikidwa kumeneko ndi manja a anthu ochimwa, omwe machimo awo sindinali nawo gawo kapena mbali ngakhale pang'ono. Komabe mofunitsitsa ndi modzifunira ndinamangidwa ndi kukhomereredwa m'manja ndi mapazi anga kuti ndipachikidwe pamtengo waukaliwo m'malo mwanu, ngakhale chilango chomwe sichinali changa kunyamula. Pakuti ndi liti pamene kwaperekedwa kwa wosalakwa kuti adzikongoletse mofunitsitsa m'manyazi pamtengo wonyozeka mu manyazi aakulu ndi ululu chifukwa cha chipulumutso cha abale ake osalungama kuti avutike m'malo mwawo, ndipo komabe m'chikondi changa pa inu ndinachita zomwezo. Mvetsetsani kuti nthawi iliyonse ndikanatha kuwaletsa popempha Atate wanga ndipo akanandipatsa magulu a angelo oposa 12 pa ulamuliro wanga, ndipo komabe, sindinapemphe koma m'malo mwake ndinasankha kupirira mokwanira ndi mofunitsitsa chilango chanu chomwe sichinali changa kunyamula. Ndipo choncho m'chikondi changa ndinadzipereka chifukwa cha inu, ndipo ndikanachitanso, ngakhale chinthu choterocho sichofunikira ndipo Yahweh walandira nsembe yanga kamodzi, konse, ndi kwamuyaya, choncho palibenso nsembe ina ya tchimo.

Choncho, Israyeli, mvetsetsani chikondi, kuleza mtima, chisomo ndi chifundo chomwe ndikukuonetsani mu mkhalidwe wanu wamakono wa kukana kopanduka kwa Mesiya wanu weniweni, ndipo ndikubwera kwa inu mu nthawi yomaliza iyi m'chikondi changa pa inu chomwe chidakali chosafa ndi chosagonjetsedwa, ngati mungovomereza Mesiya wanu weniweni. Chifukwa ndine amene mofunitsitsa ndinatenga malo anu pakati pa chiweruzo, chilango, ndi kuphedwa kwa ochimwa chifukwa mphoto ya tchimo ndi imfa, ndipo komabe ndine wopanda tchimo kwamuyaya, ndipo nthawi zonse ndimachita chifuniro cha Atate wanga, ngakhale chitsanzo chomwe mumakana pakusamvera kwanu kwadala kwa Yahweh, ndi pakukana kwanu Mesiya wanu weniweni.

Ndipo chifukwa chiyani ndinakanidwa? Ngakhale Pontiyo Pilato yemwe anali Bwanamkubwa wa Yudeya panthawiyo anali wotsimikiza kundimasula ndipo analankhula zoona za ondineneza, ngakhale a Esau omwe anasonkhanitsa Makolo anu kuti amufuulire kuti timasulireni Baraba! Ngakhale pamene anafuula pafupifupi mu mpweya womwewo "M'pachikeni!", ponena za Pilato kuwamasulira Mesiya wawo weniweni ndi magazi osalakwa omwe anawakana. Pakuti anadziwa, bwino, kuti chifukwa cha nsanje anandipereka. Komanso pamene anakhala pampando woweruza, mkazi wake anatumiza kwa iye, nati, usachite kanthu ndi munthu wolungamayo: pakuti ndavutika zinthu zambiri lero m'maloto chifukwa cha iye, ndipo zinali choncho ndipo zalembedwa m'malemba oyera ngati mungapeze chikhulupiriro chokhulupirira umboni wanga.

Ndipo zonse chifukwa Makolo anu anafuna zinthu za dziko lapansi lino ndipo anakonda maudindo awo pakati pa anthu komwe akanatha kukhala ndi moyo pa chakhumi ndi zopereka za anthu ndikuyima ngati aneneri onyenga odzilungamitsa komwe anabwereza nzeru za Esau m'makutu a anthu: ngakhale nzeru zabodza zomwe akulu awo anazikonda kwambiri kuposa mawu ndi nzeru za Yahweh, INE NDINE wamkulu, yemwe anamukana pamene anakana ndikudzipachikira okha Yesu wa ku Nazarete.

Ndipo choncho zili, kuti pakundikana kwanu, ngakhale Yesu wa ku Nazarete, ndinatembenukira kwa amitundu, ndipo anamva mawu anga kwa nyengo ndipo ambiri, ambiri omwe akanakhala opanda chiyembekezo, tsopano abwera ku chipulumutso mwa Ambuye wawo ngakhale khamu lalikulu lomwe ndidzaliukitsa lamoyo kwa ine ndekha kuti ndilipereke kwa Yahweh pa msonkhano waukulu wa oyera mtima womwe watsala pang'ono kuchitika. Ndipo ambiri ochokera ku Yudeya ndi ambiri omwe anamwazikira kunja asindikizidwa chifukwa cha chipulumutso chokwanira. Ndipo mwa abale anu, ngakhale omwe akuchokera ku ufumu wakumpoto omwe alembedwa chifukwa cha chipulumutso chamuyaya, ngakhale ambiri a iwo tsopano asindikizidwa kwamuyaya ndi kosatha ndipo adzaukitsidwa ku moyo watsopano. Mwaonadi, mwaonadi, onsewa asiya chitsanzo cha kulapa kuti mutsatire, ngati mumvere mawu a Mesiya weniweni yekha yemwe Yahweh adzatumiza, ngakhale Yesu wa ku Nazarete Mesiya wanu weniweni!

Chifukwa chifukwa cha machimo awo mafuko akumpoto anamwazikira pakati pa mitundu, komwe asungidwa ndi kusungidwa chifukwa cha chipulumutso, ngakhale ndi dzanja lamphamvu la Yahweh. Ndipo komabe zalembedwa ndi dzanja la Mtumwi wanga Paulo pamene analankhula kwa Aroma zaka zambiri za yubile zapitazo: Tikudziwa kuti zinthu zonse zimagwirira ntchito limodzi kuti zikhale zabwino kwa omwe amakonda Mulungu, ngakhale kwa omwe aitanidwa monga mwa chifuniro chake. Pakuti omwe anawadziwa kale, anawadziwiratu kuti afanane ndi chifaniziro cha Mwana wake, kuti akhale woyamba kubadwa pakati pa abale ambiri. Komanso, omwe anawadziwiratu, iwo anawaitananso, ndipo omwe anawaitana, iwo anawayesanso olungama, ndipo omwe anawayesa olungama, iwo anawapatsanso ulemerero. Ndipo choncho mu Israyeli wa Mulungu kusonkhanitsidwa kwa mafuko otayika kudzakwaniritsidwa, ndipo Aisrayeli ambiri anabwera ku chipulumutso povomereza ine monga Ambuye wawo, ngakhale Yesu wa ku Nazarete, mu nthawi yanga ya chisomo ndi chipulumutso chomwe apeza.

Ngakhale kuti panopa mumanyoza ntchito zanga, ngakhale ntchito zanga za chipulumutso zomwe ndakwaniritsa m'mitundu pa lamulo la Yahweh mu nthawi yanga ya chisomo yomwe tsopano yafika kumapeto kwake; choncho mvetsetsani zomwe Yahweh wayeretsa kudzera m'magazi a Yeshua HaMashiach, musazitchule wamba kapena zodetsedwa! Ndipo ine, Yeshua HaMashiach ndikukupemphani kuti mutsatire njira za abale anu kaya ali pafupi kapena kutali omwe abwera kwa ine mu kulapa kokwanira kuti ndikulandireni kwa ine ndekha ndikukubwezeretsani kwa Yahweh, chifukwa yemwe ali ndi mwana alinso ndi Atate koma yemwe alibe mwana alibenso Atate, ndipo choncho Yahweh walengeza!

Ndipo choncho zili kuti ndikutembenukira kwa abale anga kachiwiri ngakhale pamene ndikutembenukira kutali ndi mitundu mu nthawi yomaliza iyi ya chimaliziro ndipo kulekanitsidwa komaliza tsopano kudzachitika mu sabata la makumi asanu ndi awiri la Danieli, lomwe lafika kudzayeretsa dziko lapansi m'moto wanga woyenga ndipo Esau adzalekanitsidwa ndi Yakobo kwamuyaya ndi kosatha. Chifukwa Esau anagulitsa ukulu wake, ndipo Yahweh wavomereza kwamuyaya chisankho chake kuti chikhale chisankho chamuyaya, chifukwa ana a lonjezo ndi olowa m'malo a malonjezo osati ana a thupi, ngati muli a Khristu, ndiye kuti muli mbewu ya Abrahamu, ndi olowa m'malo mwa lonjezo. Ndipo choncho zili kuti malonjezo a cholowa opangidwa ndi Yahweh mwiniwake anapangidwa mu chikhulupiriro kwa omwe anapangidwira, chifukwa wolungama adzakhala ndi moyo ndi chikhulupiriro! Ndipo pambuyo poti malonjezo okhulupirika anaperekedwa momwemonso mapangano anapangidwa ndipo zizindikiro za lonjezo zinaperekedwa ndipo mapangano anakhala mapangano mwa kukhetsa magazi ndipo mapangano onse akale ndi malonjezo onse a Yahweh kuyambira wamng'ono mpaka wamkulu aphatikizidwa mu Chipangano changa Chatsopano cha lonjezo, momwe chilengedwe chidzapangidwa cholungama kwamuyaya mu kubwezeretsedwa kwamuyaya kwa Atate wa Kuwala, yemwe alibe kusintha kapena mthunzi wotembenuka.

Ndipo chotero ndikubwera kwa abale anga m'chikondi ndi kudzichepetsa kwakukulu, ndipo ndikubwera kwa inu mofunitsitsa m'chikondi changa pa inu osafuna kuti wina aliyense wa inu awonongeke m'kusakhulupirira kopanda chikhulupiriro. Ndipo indetu, indetu otsala adzapulumutsidwa amoyo kuchokera kwa Yakobo ngakhale pa nthawi ya nsautso ya Yakobo yomwe ndi sabata la makumi asanu ndi awiri la Danieli. Choncho, ndikukupemphani abale anga mverani mawu anga chifukwa ino ndi nthawi yotsiriza ndipo uku ndiko kulekanitsa komaliza mkwiyo usanakhuthulidwe mokwanira ndipo ndisanabwere kudzawononga ufumu wa adani kotheratu, chifukwa m'chilungamo ndikubwera ngati Mkango wa fuko la Yuda ndipo ndikubwera kudzaweruza ndi kuchita nkhondo!

Ndipo onse amene adzatembenukira kwa ine pa nthawi ino, O Israyeli, ngakhale onse amene ndiwalamula omwe anabadwa kwa Makolo akale kuti amvere mawu anga, komanso ngakhale onse amene adzaitana pa dzina langa lamphamvu mpaka chimaliziro kuchokera ku mitundu ya anthu adzapulumutsidwa ndipo adzapulumutsidwa m'madongosolo awoawo. Mitundu ya anthu idzalandira ulemu wofanana ndi wanga pamene ndinadzipereka ndekha chifukwa cha machimo a mtundu wa anthu, ndipo adzaphedwa chifukwa cha chikhulupiriro chawo pamene akuitana pa dzina langa lopulumutsa mpaka chimaliziro. Ndipo mu ulemerero waukulu adzalowa kwamuyaya ndipo mphotho zawo ndi zosatha chifukwa cha kuima kwawo, ndipo mwa kumvera kwawo adzakhala atagonjetsa njira zawo zopanda chikhulupiriro ndi zofunda, ngakhale zili ngati kupyera pamoto.

Ndipo za Israyeli, abale anga, ndidzabwerabe kudzakuulumutsani ndipo ndidzabwera kudzathandiza inu pamodzi ndi 144,000 anga ngakhale omwe adziyeretsa okha mokwanira ngati ogonjetsa anga m'magazi anga otetezera ndi owombola mu tsiku lawo la chipulumutso ndipo adzasonkhanitsidwa kwa ine pa msonkhano waukulu umene watsala pang'ono kuchitika mwadzidzidzi pa dziko lapansi. Pakuti ndidzatsogolera 144,000 anga kutsikira pa Phiri la Ziyoni, ndipo adzanditsatira pang'onopang'ono pamene akutsogolera otsala anga a Yakobo ku malo otetezeka m'chipululu kumene malo akonzedwa ndi Yahweh kuti akhale otetezeka, ndipo adzadyetsedwa ndi kusamalidwa ndipo chinjoka, chilombo, ndi mneneri wonyenga sadzawakhudza, kapena kuwapeza, omwe inu ndinu otsalawo O Israyeli. Koma Baraba adzabweretsa chonyansa cha kupasula ndipo namsongole wambiri adzayeretsedwa kuchoka m'dzikolo, ndipo ndidzabisa otsala anga kutali ndi nkhondo ya tsiku lalikululo, ngakhale tsiku la kubwera kwanga kwachiwiri lomwe Yahweh analemba m'nthawi zamuyaya zapitazo ndipo lakhazikitsidwa kwamuyaya mu ndandanda yake ya chipulumutso ndipo tsikulo likuyandikira ndithu, pakuti zinalembedwa choncho ndipo zidzachitika choncho.

Choncho, abale anga amene ndimawakonda kwamuyaya ndi mpaka kalekale, komabe ndimalemekeza ufulu wanu wopatsidwa ndi Mulungu wosankha nokha njira yomwe mudzayendamo. Choncho ndikukufunsani, kodi mudzakhala pa njira yotakata yopita ku chiwonongeko yomwe imatsogolera ku malawi amoto osazimitsidwa omwe anakonzedwera omwe adagwa? Kapena, kodi mudzamvera mawu a Mesiya wanu woona, ngakhale iye amene anapereka dontho lililonse lomaliza la magazi ake opanda uchimo mu kuzunzika kwakukulu ndi ululu kuti mukhale ndi moyo pamaso pa Mulungu wanu, ngakhale Yahweh Ine Ndine Wamkulu? Inde, Yahweh amene wagwetsa misozi yambiri m'kusweka mtima kwake chifukwa cha nkhosa zotayika za nyumba ya Israyeli, ngakhale onse amene akukana mawu ake ndi mawu anga. Kodi mudzandiphatikiza mwa kumvera kwanu kwa Mesiya wanu woona? Kapena mudzaphatikizana mofunitsitsa ndi Baraba amene Esau wamutumiza kuti akuwonongeni?

Choncho mverani Mawu anga O Israyeli ndipo mvetsetsani chisankho chomwe muyenera kupanga ndikumvetsetsa kuti ndikukukokerani kwa ine ndipo ndidzatumiza mboni zanga ziwiri zamphamvu ndipo ndikukulamulani kuti muwamvere kuti mulandire umboni wawo pakuti adzachitira umboni za zinthu zambiri zobisika zomwe zabisidwa m'mitima ya anthu koma zomwe adzaziulula ndipo maumboni awo adzaima kwamuyaya ndi mpaka kalekale. Choncho, mverani mawu a mwanawankhosa woona wa Mulungu, O Israyeli, pakuti palibe chipulumutso ndi chiwombolo mu dzina lina lililonse lomwe linaperekedwapo, ndipo indetu, indetu palibe dzina lina lomwe lidzaperekedwa pa cholinga ichi, kupatula Yeshua HaMashiach yemwenso ndi Yesu wa ku Nazarete ngakhale monga mwa thupi sindidziwikanso ndipo ndine Khristu Yesu ngakhale iye amene ali Mbuye wa ambuye ndi Mfumu ya mafumu.

Ndine Mkango wa fuko la Yuda ndipo m'chilungamo ndikubwera kudzaweruza ndi kuchita nkhondo.

Ndine amene ndatumiza Mawu awa kudzera mwa mtumiki wanga ndipo kudzera mwa mtumiki wanga Mawu awa atumizidwa, ndipo ndikuchitira umboni ndi kutsimikizira kuti Mawu a Uthenga Wabwino Wosatha omwe aululidwa ndi dzanja langa, ndipo ndithudi ndi Yahweh mwiniwake, ndi oona, ndi osatha, ndipo mtumiki wanga walankhula mokhulupirika Mawu omwe aululidwa kwa iye mu nthawi yawo ndipo adzasungidwa kudutsa m'masoka omwe akubwera ndipo adzapeza maso, makutu, ndi mitima ya omwe atumizidwa kwa iwo.

Ndine Mesiya wanu wosatha ndi wamuyaya amene ndikulakalaka chipulumutso ndi chiwombolo chanu Israyeli, ndipo indetu, indetu ndine wokonzeka kulandira kwa ine aliyense ndi onse amene amayankha kumveka kwa mawu anga ndipo mudzalowa mu Ufumu wa zaka chikwi ndipo mudzakhala anthu apadera ndipo mudzakhala ndi moyo wosatha ndipo mudzapeza msipu wosatha pakati pa Israyeli wa Mulungu, ndipo simudzasiyanitsidwanso ndi ulamuliro wolungama wa Mfumu yanu, Mbuye, Mkulu wa Ansembe, ndi Mesiya ndipo mpaka kalekale mudzatsogoleredwa mu mtendere ndi chilungamo ndipo mudzakula ngati ana a ng'ombe a m'khola.

Ndine Yeshua HaMashiach ndipo ndine amene Ine Ndine Wamkulu wandipanga ndipo ndine mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu woperekedwa ngati pasika wanu wangwiro ndi wopanda uchimo mpaka kalekale ndi nthawi zonse.

Kodi mudzasankha mwanawankhosa uti, O Israyeli?
