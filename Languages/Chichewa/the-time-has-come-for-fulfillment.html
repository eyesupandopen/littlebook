Nthawi Yakwanira Kuti Zikwaniritsidwe: Inde ndi Ameni

Kuchokera kwa Yeshua HaMashiach:

Amene Mose analamula Isiraeli kuti amumvere ndi kumugonjera.

Tcherani makutu anu kuti mumve mawu a Yeshua HaMashiach mu nthawi yotsiriza ino, ndipo weramitsani mitima yanu pomvera ndikulola mitima yanu kulandira malangizo anga. Chifukwa mawu anga ndi moyo kwa omwe awapeza ndipo amabweretsa thanzi ndi lonjezo, ngakhale mu nthawi zamavuto akulu. Choncho mvetsetsani kuti mavuto akulu afika ndipo tsoka likuyandikira, ndipo nthawi ndi ino. Zoonadi, zoonadi mwa choonadi chamuyaya ndine, Yeshua HaMashiach, ngakhale Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda amene akubwera kudzaweruza ndi kuchita nkhondo, ndipo mvetsetsani kuti palibe chobisika pamaso panga, kapena palibe chobisika pamaso pa Yahweh ndipo ndikuwona chisalungamo ndi kupha fuko komwe kukuchitidwa pa anthu a m'dzikolo ndi olanda ochokera ku Sunagoge la Satana ndipo ndidzabwezera akutero Ambuye Wolungama!

Pakuti mwa choonadi, kwa zaka zambiri za yubile akugwa apititsa patsogolo chinsinsi chawo cha kusayeruzika, ngakhale mpaka nthawi zino pamene ziwembu zawo zili zokonzeka kukwaniritsidwa mokwanira. Koma mvetsetsani kuti amangololedwa kukwaniritsa zolinga za Yahweh m'mapulani ake amuyaya ndi osatha a chipulumutso kuti anthu awomboledwe mokwanira. Chifukwa munthu wachibadwa wagonetsedwa m'chinyengo chachikulu ndi kukhutira kwakukulu.

Ndipo choncho akugwa amagwiritsa ntchito anthu kupititsa patsogolo chiwonongeko cha anthu pomwe akutchula kuwala kuti mdima ndi mdima kuti kuwala. Ndipo momwemonso Sunagoge la Satana omwe alanda mayiko a lonjezo ndipo ndidzawazula kotheratu. Pakuti Sunagoge la Satana likufuna kutenga malonjezo a Yahweh omwe analembedwa ndi kutsimikiziridwa kwa Abambo akale. Sunagoge la Satana ladzaza ndi namsongole woipa amene akugwa adabzala pakati pa tirigu wanga ndipo nthawi yowazula yafika ndipo zalembedwa choncho ndipo zidzachitika choncho: Pakuti taonani, tsiku likubwera lomwe lidzayaka ngati ng'anjo, ndipo onse onyada, inde, ndi onse ochita zoipa, adzakhala ngati chiputu, ndipo tsiku lomwe likubwera, lidzawatentha, akutero Ambuye wa makamu, ndipo silidzawasiyira muzu kapena nthambi.

Komabe, zakwaniritsidwa ndi akugwa kuti kangaude waluka ukonde wake padziko lonse lapansi tsopano, ndipo mwachinyengo amachita nkhondo. Ndipo tsopano ndi nthawi ya kuwuka kwawo ndipo ulamuliro wawo waufupi wopanda chiyero uli pafupi. Chifukwa Yahweh m'chilungamo chake adzapatsa munthu wachibadwa ndendende zomwe akuzilakalaka mopusa pakufunafuna zosangalatsa za tchimo. Ndipo choncho mdima ndi zoipa zomwe munthu wachibadwa akuzifuna, munthu wachibadwa tsopano adzakhala nazo. Komabe, ngati Uthenga Wanga Wabwino uli wobisika, uli wobisika kwa iwo omwe atayika. Mwa iwo omwe mulungu wa dziko lino waphimba mitima yawo omwe sakhulupirira, kuti kuwala kwa Uthenga Wabwino waulemerero wa Khristu, yemwe ali chifaniziro cha Mulungu, kusaunikire kwa iwo. Ndipo choncho munthu wachibadwa mu umbuli wake wajowina kangaude m'chinyengo chake, pakuti mwachinyengo amachita nkhondo.

Choncho, El Shaddai, akuwatumizira chinyengo champhamvu kuti akhulupirire mabodza opangidwa ndi akugwa, kuti onse atsutsidwe omwe sanakhulupirire choonadi, koma amasangalala ndi chosalungama. Komabe m'chiweruzo cha Yahweh ndi chifundo chake chokhalitsa, zosangalatsa tsopano zidzachotsedwa padziko lapansi ndipo zokoma zomwe munthu wachibadwa amalakalaka kwambiri kuziunjika zidzachotsedwa padziko lapansi. Ndipo ngakhale zosangalatsa za tchimo zidzachotsedwa padziko lapansi, kotero kuti anthu adzafika pochita misala pamene akufuna kukhutiritsa zilakolako za mitima yawo. Komabe, Yahweh sasankha zochita izi kuti akhale wankhanza ku chilengedwe chake koma kulanga ochita zoipa ndi kudzutsa omwe, mwinamwake, akana kukana kudzuka ku tulo tawo chifukwa kudzera m'zosangalatsa za tchimo ndi malipiro a chosalungama anenepetsedwa ndi kuchititsidwa khungu. M'malo mwa chiweruzo chokwanira ndi chadzidzidzi munthu wachibadwa amapatsidwa chisomo chifukwa munthu wachibadwa wagonjetsedwa ndi akugwa.

Inde, m'chisomo ndi chifundo cha Yahweh ndi m'dongosolo lake lamuyaya Yahweh akufuna kudzutsa munthu wachibadwa ku tchimo ndi kuyitana munthu wachibadwa ku chilungamo. Kuitana komwe ambiri ayankha mofunitsitsa ku chipulumutso changa mu nthawi zanga za chisomo kudzera mu Uthenga Wabwino wa Chipulumutso. Mu nthawi zomwe, chipulumutso chinali mphatso yaulere pomwe munthu akhoza kundipanga kukhala Ambuye ndikukhala ndi nthawi yogwirira ntchito chipulumutso chawo ndi mantha ndi kunjenjemera pamene akubwera kwa ine mokwanira ngakhale kutuluka mokwanira mu tchimo. Ndipo zili choncho kuti nthawi zanga za chisomo zafika kumapeto, ngakhale chisomo ndi chifundo zili nane nthawi zonse. Ndipo posachedwapa zidzachitika kuti nthawi zodikira sizidzakhalakonso ndipo maufumu a dziko lino adzakhala maufumu a Ambuye wanu, ndi a Khristu wake, ndipo ndidzalamulira kwamuyaya ndi okhulupirika mu cholowa chathu chamuyaya chomwe ndidzagawana mokwanira ndi opambana anga.

Ndipo khamu lalikulu lidzabwera kwa ine pa nthawi ya masautso a Yakobo kuti ndiwapulumutse kwamuyaya. Ndipo onse amene adzaitana pa dzina la Ambuye mpaka mapeto adzapulumutsidwa, mosasamala kanthu za chilankhulo kapena mtundu womwe akuitanira. Ndipo ndikuyenda kuti ndipulumutse Isiraeli, ndipo ndikuitana abale anga enieni omwe asocheretsedwa ndi zipembedzo zonyenga ndipo aponderezedwa ndi Sunagoge la Satana, ndipo adzapeza chipulumutso chamuyaya ndipo ndidzapulumutsabe Yakobo yemwe chipulumutso chake chidzakwaniritsidwa ndi moto wa woyenga. Koma mvetsetsani kuti nthawi za moyo wautali ndi chitukuko chakuthupi padziko lapansi zafika kumapeto mpaka Ufumu wanga utakhazikitsidwa posachedwa, ndipo dziko lapansi lagwidwa m'maunyolo kuyembekezera ziweruzo zake zomaliza kuti zitsanulidwe mu mkwiyo waukulu. Taonani! Mkwiyo wa Mwanawankhosa ukubwera poyamba, ndipo mavuto akulu adzasesa dziko lapansi ngati chigumula cha chiwonongeko chotheratu, ndipo ambiri adzawonongeka.

Komabe, mavuto akulu ndi masautso adzamasulidwa pamene zisindikizo zathyoka ndi mkwiyo waukulu, ndi miliri yambiri ndi matenda, ndi njala, ndi chilala, ndi nkhondo, ndi chisalungamo, ndi imfa ndi zina zambiri zidzabwera mokwanira padziko lapansi; komabe khamu la makamu lidzadzuka ku njira zawo zofunda. Ndipo adzakhala m'kuwawa kwakukulu ndi masautso a moyo pamene azindikira choonadi chosalephera cha malemba ndi momwe anaphonyera mwaumakani mwayi wawo wa chipulumutso ndi kuthawa mkwiyo ndi chiweruzo pa kusonkhanitsidwa kwa Mpingo wanga wokhulupirika kwa ine ndekha. Komabe m'masautso awo owawa ndi zowawa ndidzawauzira kutsimikiza mtima kosagonjetseka mwa iwo ndipo ndidzawakokera kwa ine mokwanira, ngakhale onse omwe amabwera kwa ine m'mazunzo ndi masautso ndi kudzera m'mayesero a kuphedwa chifukwa cha chikhulupiriro. Ndipo adzakhala ndi mphotho yaikulu, mphotho yaikulu kotero kuti mavuto awo omwe akubwera sayenera kuyerekezedwa ndi ulemerero wamuyaya womwe udzaululidwe mokwanira mwa iwo.

Choncho zalembedwa ndipo choncho zidzamalizidwa kotheratu: Awa ndiwo amene anatuluka m'chisautso chachikulu, ndipo atsuka mikanjo yawo, ndikuyiyeretsa m'magazi a Mwanawankhosa. Chifukwa chake, ali pamaso pa mpando wachifumu wa Mulungu, ndipo amamtumikira usana ndi usiku mu Kachisi wake: ndipo iye amene akhala pa Mpando Wachifumu adzakhala pakati pawo. Sadzamvanso njala, kapena ludzu, kapena dzuwa silidzawawomba, kapena kutentha kulikonse. Pakuti Mwanawankhosa, amene ali pakati pa mpando wachifumu, adzawadyetsa, ndipo adzawatsogolera ku akasupe amadzi amoyo: ndipo Mulungu adzapukuta misozi yonse m'maso mwawo.

Ndipo choncho kangaude waluka ukonde wake padziko lonse lapansi m'chinyengo chachikulu, kugwiririra, kulanda ndipo m'njira zambiri poizoni wa kangaude amagwira ntchito yake yoipa kudzera m'njira zake zachinyengo ndi zapoizoni. Pakuti waluka ukonde wachinyengo ndipo waluka ukonde wa ngongole kotero kuti dziko lapansi tsopano lalukidwa mokwanira m'kukumbatira kwa kangaude ndipo lafa ziwalo ndi poizoni wake. Kangaude wayenda ndi chinyengo chachikulu kudzera m'chinsinsi cha kusayeruzika, ndipo dziko lapansi tsopano lili mokwanira mu ukonde wake. Ndipo m'chinyengo chachikulu onyenga awa (opangidwa ndi akugwa) alanda mayiko ngati dzombe. Ngakhale mofanana ndi momwe kangaude amakulungira mazira ake mpaka khamu la ana ake litamasuka ndi kuthamanga kukaluka maukonde awo pambali pake. Ndipo choncho sunagoge la Satana latenga dziko lapansi mwa mphamvu ndi mwa luso.

Ndipo m'kupita kwa nthawi ndi kudzera m'machenjera ndi ziwembu zambiri adzipanga okha kukhala amphamvu, pakuti zaperekedwa kwa iwo ndi Yahweh m'mapulani ake amuyaya a chipulumutso kuti akwaniritse. Chifukwa munthu wachibadwa wachititsidwa khungu ndi mabodza awo ndipo amawatsatira mosazindikira ku chiwonongeko chake. Mapulani omwe ndikunena adzafika pachimake pakulekanitsa komaliza pakati pa tirigu ndi namsongole ndi pakati pa odzipereka mokwanira ndi omwe amakonda machimo awo ndipo mofunitsitsa anasankha malawi amoto m'chikondi chawo chonyansa ndi chiwonongeko chawo, ndipo adzanong'oneza bondo kwamuyaya chisankho chawo chomwe asankha kudzigwirizanitsa okha kuti agwirizane ndi zolinga za kangaude. Ndipo choncho mbale za mkwiyo wa Mulungu zidzatsanulidwa mokwanira.

Mvetsetsani kuti kangaude ndi Sunagoge la Satana yemwe tsopano ali wokonzeka kutenga dziko lapansi mu dongosolo lawo lomaliza la chilombo lomwe ulamuliro wake ndidzauthetsa kwamuyaya ndipo chiwonongeko chake ndi chiweruzo chake sichichedwa. Mvetsetsani, okondedwa anga, kuti Sunagoge la Satana onse ndi onyenga omwe amanyenga. Ndi namsongole wa Aedomu omwe makolo awo ndinakumana nawo ngati mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu pamene ndinadzipereka mofunitsitsa kulipira machimo a anthu. Ndipo choncho ndinawaulula, ndipo choncho ndinawauza pamaso pawo oipa: Inu ndinu a atate wanu mdierekezi, ndipo zilakolako za atate wanu mudzazichita: iye wakhala wakupha kuyambira pachiyambi, ndipo sanakhale m'choonadi, chifukwa mulibe choonadi mwa iye. Pamene alankhula bodza, amalankhula zake: pakuti iye ndi wabodza, ndi atate wake.

Ndipo choncho kuyambira kuukira kwawo koyamba kwa dziko lolonjezedwa lomwe silili lawo kuti alitenge, azunza, kuzunza, ndi kupha fuko lonselo kwa eni ake enieni a mayiko a lonjezo. Amanyenga kukhala olowa m'malo enieni a chipulumutso, ngakhale kugwiritsa ntchito molakwika mbali za chilamulo cha Mose m'chipembedzo chawo chonyenga cha Talmudic. Ndipo mwa njira zotere achititsa khungu ambiri omwe akuganiza kuti amatsatira chifuniro changa poganiza kuti ndidzadalitsa aliyense amene adalitsa namsongole uyu ndipo ndidzatemberera aliyense amene atemberera namsongole uyu. Ndipo choncho ambiri a anthu anga mosazindikira amadzigwirizanitsa okha ku Sunagoge la Satana mwa chilolezo ndipo ndikukulamulani ndithu kuti mulape mokwanira mabodza awo omwe mwasankha chifukwa cha chinyengo chanu.

Ana anga ang'ono mvetsetsani kuti Ine, Khristu Yesu, sindinalembe Mabaibulo aliwonse ophunzirira koma awa amagwiritsidwa ntchito ngati zida zachinyengo ndi mabodza operekedwa m'mbali mwake kutanthauzira choonadi. Ndipo akugwa asinthira mawu anga m'Mabaibulo onyenga, chifukwa chake, yang'anani ku mzere wa malemba a Byzantine, ngakhale Textus Receptus komwe ndinasunga Mawu anga. Chifukwa chake, bwerani kwa ine okondedwa anga ndipo muphunzire za ine ndipo ndidzakutengerani mokwanira m'choonadi cha chinyengo chawo chamakono ngati mudzachepetsa mtima wanu ku mawu anga operekedwa ndi omwe amandilankhulira.

Mvetsetsani kuti ndine, Yesu wa ku Nazarete, amene ndinakumana ndi mtundu womwewo wa onyenga chifukwa cha miyambo yawo yapakamwa yomwe anakweza pamwamba pa Mawu oyera a Yahweh operekedwa kwa Mose. Pakuti Chilamulo cha Mose ndi choyera, ndi cholungama, ndi chowongoka kwamuyaya ndipo ngati munthu aliyense atemberera Chilamulo cha Mose ndiye kuti munthuyo adzatembereredwa. Pakuti Ine, Yesu wa ku Nazarete, ndinakwaniritsa Chilamulo cha Mose pamene ndinapereka magazi anga opanda tchimo pa Kalvare chifukwa cha machimo a Adamu (ndi mbadwa zake zonse) ndipo Chilamulo cha Mose chimasungidwa mokwanira ndi okondedwa anga omwe amayenda mwachikhulupiriro mwa ine, Khristu Yesu, Ambuye wawo wokwera ndi wosatha.

Choncho mvetsetsani kuti n'zosatheka kuphwanya Chilamulo cha Mose pamene munthu akuyenda mwachikhulupiriro pamene ndikuwatsogolera ndi mzimu wanga womwe ndawalengera mwa iwo kwamuyaya. Ngakhale, aliyense amene amayenda m'thupi akuphwanya chifukwa Mose anabwera kudzatsutsa tchimo m'thupi popereka chilamulo chamuyaya cha chisangalalo cholembedwa ndi chala cha Yahweh mwiniwake. Ndipo palibe thupi lomwe liri lolungama pamaso panga ndipo ngakhale pamaso pa Mulungu Wamuyaya wa Chilungamo. Chifukwa chake, kuulula machimo anu ndi zofooka zanu kwa ine, mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu yemwe ali mkulu wanu wansembe wamuyaya kwamuyaya monga mwa Ndondomeko ya Melikizedeki ndipo ndidzakuyeretsani mokwanira m'magazi anga okhululukira omwe akhetsedwa kuti ayeretse onse omwe amabwera kwa ine kudzapeza chipulumutso.

Ndani wamva chinthu chotere? Ndani waona zinthu zotere? Kodi dziko lapansi lidzabadwa tsiku limodzi? Kapena mtundu udzabadwa nthawi imodzi? Komabe zili choncho kuti mtundu wa Isiraeli unabwereranso kukhala mtundu mwa chilengezo cha namsongole, ndipo anali Mulungu mwiniwake amene analola adani ake kunyenga mosazindikira m'chinyengo chawo kuti iwo ndi Yakobo osati Esau, chifukwa anali Esau amene anagulitsa ufulu wake wobadwa kwa wamng'ono. Ndipo choncho kangaude mosazindikira ndi modzikuza akuganiza kuti adzabweretsa m'badwo wagolide wamuyaya padziko lapansi pakuti zotero ndi zilakolako za Abambo awo zomwe adzazichita ndipo akugwa ndi Abambo awo ngakhale Satana omwe chikhalidwe chawo amachitenga.

Ndipo komabe alanda mayiko ndi chiwawa chachikulu pakuti zaperekedwa kwa iwo kuti akwaniritse kudzutsa omwe akugona, ngakhale onse omwe ali m'khungu pang'ono omwe adzayankha kuitana kwa Mesiya wawo, ngakhale Yeshua Mesiya Woona. Ndipo mvetsetsani kuti ndikuwona choyipa chilichonse chomwe amachita, ndipo ndikuwona chisalungamo chilichonse chomwe amachitira osalakwa, ndipo ndidzabwezera akutero Ambuye Wamuyaya yemwe Yahweh wamupanga Mesiya, Mfumu, Mkulu wansembe, ndi Ambuye! Choyipa chilichonse chalembedwa mwangwiro kumwamba, ndipo adzabwezeredwa kwamuyaya ndi kotheratu m'cholowa chawo chamuyaya m'malawi otentha kwambiri a buluu wa cobalt mu mdima wandiweyani kwamuyaya.

Ndipo ndikuwona Rakele akulirira ana ake pamene akuvutika ndi kupha fuko ndi masautso opanda chilungamo ndi Sunagoge la Satana ndipo ndidzabwezera, akutero Ambuye wa Ambuye ndi Mfumu ya Mafumu ndipo ndidzapulumutsabe otsalira a Isiraeli amoyo kudzera mu sabata la makumi asanu ndi awiri la Danieli, ngakhale masautso a Yakobo. M'dongosolo lamuyaya la Yahweh, amamvetsetsa kuti ambiri sadzachoka m'chilungamo kuchoka ku zosangalatsa ndi chinyengo cha tchimo. Choncho m'malo mobweretsa chiweruzo chofulumira, Yahweh m'malo mwake wasankha kubweretsa chiweruzo pa chiweruzo kotero kuti zochitika zikuunjikana ndi kupita patsogolo kukhala zoipa. Ndipo chiweruzo chidzaunjikana m'njira yotere onse omwe pamapeto pake adzasankha kuitana pa ine m'masautso awo ndi tsoka ndiwo omwe ndidzawapulumutsa kotheratu, ndipo adzatsogoleredwa ku chitetezo.

Ndipo zili choncho kuti zolinga zawo tsopano ziwululidwa. Pakuti ndidzatsogolera anthu anga 144,000 kutsikira pa Phiri la Ziyoni, ndipo adzanditsatira pang'onopang'ono pamene akutsogolera otsalira anga a Yakobo kupita kumalo otetezeka m'chipululu kumene malo akonzedwa ndi Mulungu kuti atetezeke, ndipo adzadyetsedwa ndi kusamalidwa ndipo chinjoka, chilombo, ndi mneneri wonyenga sadzawakhudza, kapena kuwapeza. Koma mneneri wonyenga adzabweretsa chonyansa cha kupasula ndipo namsongole wambiri adzayeretsedwa m'dziko, ndipo ndidzabisa otsalira anga ku nkhondo ya tsiku lalikululo, ngakhale tsiku la kubwera kwanga kwachiwiri lomwe Yahweh analemba mu nthawi zamuyaya zapitazo ndipo lakhazikitsidwa kwamuyaya mu ndandanda yake ya chipulumutso ndipo tsikulo likuyandikira ndithu pakuti kwalembedwa.

Ndipo ndinaona kumwamba kotseguka, ndipo taonani kavalo woyera, ndipo iye amene anakwerapo anatchedwa wokhulupirika ndi woona, ndipo m'chilungamo iye amaweruza ndi kuchita nkhondo. Maso ake anali ngati lawi lamoto, ndipo pamutu pake panali akorona ambiri, ndipo anali ndi dzina lolembedwa, lomwe palibe munthu analidziwa koma iye mwini. Ndipo anali atavala chovala choviikidwa m'magazi, ndipo dzina lake limatchedwa, Mawu a Mulungu. Ndipo magulu ankhondo amene anali kumwamba anamutsatira pa akavalo oyera, atavala bafuta wabwino, woyera ndi woyengeka. Ndipo m'kamwa mwake mutuluka lupanga lakuthwa, kuti nalo, akanthe mitundu ya anthu: ndipo adzawalamulira ndi ndodo yachitsulo: ndipo akuponda moponderamo mphesa mwa ukali ndi mkwiyo wa Mulungu Wamphamvuyonse. Ndipo ali nalo pa chovala chake, ndi pa ntchafu yake dzina lolembedwa, Mfumu Ya Mafumu, Ndi Mbuye Wa Ambuye.

Chotero zidzachitika kuti ndidzabwera kudzalandira cholowa changa mwamphamvu kuchokera m'manja mwa olanda ndi okhala mwachisawawa omwe nthawi yawo yafika pamapeto amuyaya, chifukwa ufumu wa Satana sudzauka nsonso mwanjira yofananayo. Ndipo ndidzayeretsa ndi kuchotsa chosalungama chonse padziko lapansi, ndipo Satana adzamangidwa zaka chikwi chimodzi mu chizunzo ndi mantha aakulu a cholowa chake chamuyaya chomwe chikubwera m'sulufule woyaka wonunkha womwe umayaka malawi otentha kwambiri a chilango.

Ngakhale malawi amoto omwe analengedwa kubwezera mchitidwe uliwonse woipa womwe anachita kuyambira mphindi yomwe analengedwa ndikusankha chosalungama. Ndipo tchimo lililonse la omwe adagwa lidzabwezeredwa mokwanira kwa aliyense wa iwo, ngakhale tchimo lililonse lomwe anachita mwachidani chachikulu motsutsana ndi Yahweh mwiniwake ndi chilengedwe chake chomwe iwo aipitsa. Ndipo zili choncho pamene amangidwa ndi kuponyedwa amoyo m'malawi otentha kwambiri ndi oyera kwambiri a chilango kumene moto udzayaka mofiyira kwambiri ndi mithunzi ya chiyero choyera chotentha. Ndipo m'malawi amoto a chilango amenewo adzabwezeredwa kotheratu pa zochita zawo zonse zopanda pake ndi zonyansa zomwe zidzaperekedwa mokwanira m'zifuwa zawo nthawi zambiri mpaka muyaya.

Ndipo adzabwezeredwa mokwanira m'chiweruzo changwiro ndi chamuyaya cha Yahweh chomwe sichichedwa.

Ndipo motero ulamuliro wanga wa zaka chikwi udzabwera padziko lonse lapansi mokwanira kumapeto kwa nsautso ya Yakobo, ndipo udzakhala ulamuliro wa chilungamo. Mu nthawi iyi olungama onse adzapatsidwa moyo, ndipo aliyense adzakhala ataukitsidwa mu nthawi yawo ndi m'madongosolo a kukolola kwawo. Okhulupirika mwa Khristu Yesu omwe ali amoyo ndi omwe agona mwa Khristu adzakwatulidwa kumwamba m'kuthwanima kwa diso, ndipo adzafupidwa kwambiri ndipo ena adzataya, koma onse adzapatsidwa moyo mokwanira ndi kwamuyaya.
