Meyi 23, 2024

Imvani Mawu Anga Ndipo Mvetsetsani Nthawi Zomwe Mukukhalamo!

Imvani Mawu anga, anthu anga, ngakhale inu nonse amene ndikukuitanani, ndipo mvetsetsani nthawi zomwe mukukhalamo. Pakuti dziko lomwe mukulidziwa tsopano lidzatha, ndipo m'malo mwake mudzatsala mabwinja ambiri, ngakhale phulusa ndi mabwinja a Babulo Wachinsinsi, pakuti nkhondo yafika. Pakuti pamene ndikukwatula Mkwatibwi wanga kupita kumwamba, tsoka lalikulu lidzameza Babulo Wachinsinsi, ndipo adzawonongedwa mu ola limodzi, ndipo mu ola limodzi adzawonongedwa, ndipo palibe chomwe chingasinthe tsogolo lake, pakuti Yehova wanena tero.

Koma iwe, Babulo Wachinsinsi, wakana mawu anga ndikundiika Ine, Yesu Khristu Wolungama, pambali chifukwa cha zosangalatsa za tchimo. Pakuti ngakhale machenjezo akulu omwe anaperekedwa pa mtundu wanu kumwamba anangokhala ngati zosangalatsa za mitima yanu yopanda chikhulupiriro ndi maso anu a chilakolako, ndipo kwa inu linali tsiku lina chabe pamene munkakhala mosangalala m'machimo anu omwe akukutsogolerani kukuwonongeka kwanu. Mukanayenera kulapa!

Pakuti mwasanduka mtundu wa opembedza mafano, mtundu wa okonda nkhondo, mtundu womwe umadya mabodza ndipo umakonda chinyengo chawo, mtundu womwe wadzaza dziko lapansi ndi zonyansa zanu; ndipo mitundu ikumwa m'chikho chanu chagolide chomwe chadzaza ndi zonyansa ndi uve wa dama lanu.

Chifukwa chake, ndikutsutsana nawe, ndipo chiwonongeko chako chafika ndipo chayamba kale, pakuti wanyalanyaza kuitana kwa Yehova komwe kunaperekedwa kudzera mwa ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndipo ndikanakupulumutsa. Ndipo ndalira chifukwa cha njira zanu za mitima youma, koma simukufunabe kusiya zoyipa; choncho ndakutembenukirani msana, ndipo nthawi za Amitundu zatsala pang'ono kukwaniritsidwa pamene ndikukwatula okhulupirika mu Mpingo wanga pa kulira kwa lipenga.

Koma inu ndinu mtundu womangidwa m'unyolo, ndipo mudzawonongedwa kotheratu, ndipo simudzauka nkomwe. Pamene Mkwatibwi wanga akutengedwa, dziko lapansi lidzadabwa kwambiri ndi zomwe aona, pakuti chochitikachi sichidzachitika mwachinsinsi, koma poyera, kuti dziko lonse lapansi lidziwe kuti kuli Mulungu kumwamba ndipo wandituma Ine, Khristu Yesu, kudzachotsa golide, siliva, ndi miyala yamtengo wapatali ya dziko lapansi. Pakuti chuma chachikulu cha Atate, ngakhale ngale zamtengo wapatali, zidzatengedwa kuchoka ku chiweruzo ndi chiwonongeko chomwe chikubwera.

Pakuti Mkwatibwi wanga wapulumutsidwa ku mkwiyo chifukwa cha nsembe yanga, ndipo ndidzapulumutsa okhulupirika ndi omwe apezeka okhulupirika mwa Khristu pa nthawi ya kuonekera kwanga ndi onse omwe anamwalira okhulupirika mwa Khristu, ngakhale osindikizidwa ndi ine, Khristu Yesu, pakuti ndapangidwa kukhala mzimu wopatsa moyo. Ndipo ndidzaukitsa akufa mwa Khristu, ndipo omwe ali amoyo ndi otsala adzakwatulidwa pamodzi nawo kukakumana nane m'mitambo. Choncho mvetsetsani kuti chochitikachi ndi chofuna kupereka mphotho kwa okhulupirika ndi kukwaniritsa ukwati wa Mkwati kwa Mkwatibwi wanga, pakuti tidzaphatikizidwa mu pangano kwamuyaya ndipo sadzachoka kwa ine. Ndipo ndine amene ndidzawapanga kukhala mafumu ndi ansembe pamaso pa Atate wanga.

Ndipo motero adzachoka pa dziko lapansi, ndipo dziko lapansi lidzaona, ndipo ambiri adzadabwa kwambiri. Pakuti mwa zina, chifukwa cha chochitikachi ndi kubweretsa kulapa pakati pa omwe asiyidwa, ngakhale omwe anakana kuitana kwanga mu nthawi ya chisomo, komanso ambiri omwe sanamve zokwanira kuti akhulupirire omwe adzapulumutsidwa pa nthawi ya soka la Yakobo, makamaka a m'dziko la lonjezo.

Pakuti ndikukuitana iwe, O, Isiraeli: kodi wakonzeka kumva mawu a Mesiya wako weniweni, Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda? Pakuti mawu a Mesiya wako weniweni akuitana, ndipo ndine amene ndidzalamulira kuchokera pampando wachifumu wa Davide mu mzinda woyera, ngakhale Ine, Yeshua HaMashiach; koma usanyalanyaze mawu a kuitana kwanga! Ndipo motero nthawi ya chisomo, ngakhale nthawi zotsitsimula, tsopano zikutha kotheratu. Ndipo mapeto amenewo adzafika kwathunthu pamene ndidzasonkhanitsa okhulupirika a Mpingo wanga kwa ine m'mitambo ndipo pamene kuchoka kwawo kudzagwedeza dziko lonse lapansi.

Zidzagwedeza dziko lapansi kwenikweni, ndipo kugwedezeka kwakukulu kudzameza Babulo Wachinsinsi, ndipo adzang'ambika pakati pamene oyera mtima anga akuchoka. Adzatenthedwa kotheratu ndi moto wochokera kumwamba pamene mizinga idzagwa. Mipendero yake idzaphimbidwa ndi madzi ochokera kum'mawa ndi kumadzulo! Pakuti mngelo wamphamvu adzatenga mwala ngati mphero yaikulu, ndikuuponya m'nyanja, nati, Motero ndi chiwawa mzinda waukuluwo Babulo udzagwetsedwa pansi, ndipo sudzapezekanso konse. Pakuti motero kwalembedwa ndipo motero kudzachitika!

Pakuti chiweruzo chake ndi chachikulu, ndipo chiweruzo chayamba m'nyumba ya Mulungu. Mipingo yake yampatuko idzawonongedwa, pakuti ndawaweruza onse omwe sali anga, ngakhale mipingo yonse yonyenga yomwe imalankhula mabodza akulu mu chinyengo. Babulo Wachinsinsi, O, mukanatembenukira kwa ine, Yesu Khristu, kusiya njira zanu zoyipa, pakuti ndikanakupulumutsani, ndipo Ine kapena Yehova tilibe chimwemwe m'ziweruzo zomwe zatsala pang'ono kumasulidwa.

Koma komabe, ziweruzo zidzagwa monga zinanenedwa, ndipo chiweruzocho chidzakhala chowawa ndi chamuyaya. Pakuti ndinatembenukira kwa Amitundu kwa nyengo, ndipo tsopano ndidzatembenukira kutali ndi Amitundu ndipo ndidzatembenukira ku gulu lomwe ndinatulukamo, ngakhale Isiraeli. Ngakhale kuti aliyense amene adzaitana pa dzina la Ambuye mpaka mapeto adzapulumutsidwa, kaya Myuda kapena Wamitundu, pakuti chipulumutso sichimangidwa ndi malire a mayiko.

O, Babulo Wachinsinsi, milatho yanu idzagwa; misewu yanu idzagubuduzidwa, kusweka, ndi kusapitika; maunyolo anu operekera katundu akugwa kale ndipo posachedwa adzakhala abwinja. Palibe zofunikira zomwe zidzaperekedwe kwa inu, pakuti palibe amene adzabwere kudzathandiza, ndipo mudzakhala nokha mu ola lanu la chiwonongeko, O, hule lalikulu. Simudzakhala ndi chimwemwe, ndipo omwe adzapulumuka adzapezeka akumenyana kuti angomwa madzi. Njala idzaphimba dziko. Ndipo mliri waukulu udzakupezani, ndipo ngakhale nyama zidzaukira, pakuti nthawi zafika.

Magulu ankhondo ofiira omwe boma lanu lachinyengo linalola m'malire anu adzapha mwamuna, mkazi, ndi mwana aliyense amene sakugwirizana ndi zolinga zawo. Pakuti adzapha ndi kusandutsa akapolo onse omwe adzawapeza. Ndiwo magulu ankhondo a chirombo omwe asonkhanitsidwa kuchokera ku ngodya zinayi za dziko lapansi chifukwa cha cholinga ichi, ndipo chirombo chobisika ndi mneneri wonyenga wobisika awasonkhanitsa.

Alibe chifundo, alibe chisoni, alibe chisomo, alibe kulapa, ndipo amadana kotheratu ndi okhala mu Babulo Wachinsinsi chifukwa cha machimo owopsa omwe atsogoleri anu achita m'maiko awo. Ndipo nthawi yonseyi ambiri a inu munkakondwerera nkhondo zanu zopanda chilungamo pamene munkawonera mosangalala pa ma wailesi yakanema anu, pamene munkasangalala kwambiri ndi machimo a atsogoleri anu.

Mwapereka chilolezo chanu kwa atsogoleri anu omwe machimo awo munawavomereza povota, ndipo motero mudzagawana nawo chiweruzo chawo, pakuti mwadzipanga kukhala oyenera gawo lowirikiza la chiweruzo, ndipo chidzakupezani mwadzidzidzi.

Ndipo motero Mkwatibwi wanga adzaonekera pa mpando wanga wachiweruzo, ndipo ena adzataya, ena adzatenga mphotho yaikulu, ndipo onse adzalandira mokwanira gawo loyamba lomwe alandira kale: Pakuti ndidzawapatsa matupi atsopano, opanda tchimo omwe sadzaola konse! Ndipo motero zisindikizo zidzamasulidwa, pakuti ngakhale chisautso ndi zowawa zachuluka pa dziko lapansi kwa zaka zambiri tsopano, si mkwiyo wa Mulungu kapena mkwiyo wa mwanawankhosa.

Ambiri a inu mukuganiza mopusa kuti muyenera kupirira mkwiyo wa Mulungu, mukuganiza modzikuza kuti mutha kupirira zomwe zikubwera. Mvetsetsani kuti ndapulumutsa Mpingo wanga ku mkwiyo, pakuti ndinabwera kwa anga ndi kwa Amitundu mu nthawi ya chisomo ngati pangano lamtendere, kupereka mtendere ndi chiyanjanitso kwa Atate wa Kuwala, ngakhale El Shaddai! Ndipo omwe ndawapulumutsa avomereza mwa kufuna kwawo chisomo changa chosayenera, choncho tsatirani chitsanzo chawo!

Ndipo ndidzasindikiza 144,000 anga kuchokera ku mafuko otsatirawa, ngakhale 12,000 kuchokera ku fuko lililonse lotsatirali: Yuda, Rubeni, Gadi, Aseri, Nafutali, Manase, Simeoni, Levi, Isakara, Zebuloni, Yosefe, ndi Benjamini. Choncho imvani mawu anga!

Ndipo mudzandichitira ntchito zambiri zamphamvu, ndipo mudzatsogolera ambiri ku chipulumutso kuchoka m'dziko lomwe likufa ndi lachiwawa. Pakuti Satana wakonza kale chinyengo chachikulu ndipo adzachita zonse zomwe angathe kuti aname, kuphimba, ndi kubisa kusonkhana kwakukulu m'mitambo komwe kwatsala pang'ono kuchitika. Mabodza ake azika kale mizu m'mitima ndi m'maganizo mwanu kudzera m'maphunziro anu a Chiyesuti omwe amakana Atate wanga ndipo abweretsedwa kwa inu ndi omwe adagwa. Apanga zinthu zonse kukhala zonyenga, ngakhale kutsogolera ambiri a inu kukhulupirira kuti pamwamba pa dziko lapansi pali malo opanda malire a mapulaneti ndi zamoyo zina, ndipo ambiri adzakhala opusa kwambiri kuti ameze mabodza awa. Ngakhale omwe mayina awo sanalembedwepo m'Buku la Moyo, pakuti adzalandira chizindikiro chawo.

Dziwani, anthu anga, ndipo imvani chenjezo langa. Palibe zamoyo pa mapulaneti ena! Phunzirani kuti mudziwe, pakuti mapulaneti ndi nyenyezi zoyendayenda zomwe zinakhazikitsidwa kuti zilengeze ntchito ya manja a Yehova mu mlengalenga pansi pa thambo. Awa sadzakhala alendo ochokera ku maiko ena omwe amazungulira monama; komabe, sali a dziko lapansi, pakuti ndi angelo ogwa omwe ali a kumwamba, ndipo adzipangira zonyansa zambiri. Musawakhulupirire!

Choncho kuchokera ku phulusa la wokwera pahatchi ndi kuchokera ku chiwonongeko chachikulu chirombo ndi mneneri wonyenga adzauka, pakuti chinsinsi cha kusayeruzika chikugwira kale ntchito. Pakuti Babulo Wachinsinsi waperekedwa m'manja mwawo ndipo mgwirizano womwe ukukhazikitsidwa, ngakhale nyanga khumi zidzamuwononga, pakuti ndi mafumu khumi omwe amapereka zonse zawo kwa chinjoka, chirombo, ndi mneneri wonyenga. Pakuti wolepheretsa akachotsedwa, adzauka kwathunthu malinga ndi Malemba; pakuti kuchokera mu chisokonezo kudzauka dongosolo lawo latsopano, ngakhale dongosolo la Chinjoka, pakuti ndiye mphamvu ndi ulamuliro wawo, pakuti wapatsidwa motero m'njira yochepa kwa nthawi yochepa kwambiri.

Chinjoka chili ndi mkwiyo waukulu ndipo chidzayenda mu mzimu wa Hamani kupanga zinthu zonse za Khristu kukhala zosaloledwa, ndipo aliyense amene akugwirizana ndi dzina langa, ngakhale Yesu Khristu, adzasakidwa ndi kuphedwa. Ndipo omwe sakonda miyoyo yawo mpaka mapeto adzapulumutsidwa ndipo adzakhala ndi mphotho yaikulu. Choncho sungani maganizo ndi mtima wanu pa chimwemwe chomwe chaikidwa pamaso panu, pakuti m'njira yotereyi ndinatsimikizira chipulumutso chanu mu mazunzo owawa omwe anali anu, kupatulapo kuti ndinatenga chilango chanu mofunitsitsa ndikulipira mokwanira machimo a anthu kamodzi kokha, ngakhale kwa nthawi zonse.

Chirombo chidzayenda mwachangu, pakuti chidzapitiriza kwa miyezi 42 kulankhula zinthu zazikulu zamwano. Chidzachita nkhondo ndi oyera mtima, ndipo chapatsidwa kuwagonjetsa. Chidzadula mitu ya ambiri. Chidzachita zizindikiro zambiri zonyenga ndi zozizwitsa, ndipo kuchokera ku phulusa kudzauka dongosolo lake latsopano. Ndipo mneneri wonyenga adzamuloza ndi kumutamanda ndikupangitsa onse kupembedza fano lake, pakuti zinthu izi zalembedwa momveka bwino m'Malemba. Ndipo motero chirombo chidzayeretsa dziko lapansi ku chilichonse chabwino, choyera, ndi chamtengo wapatali. Komabe, ndidzabisa okhulupirika anga, ndipo adzachita zozizwitsa zambiri, ndipo ambiri adzapulumutsidwa.

Koma mneneri wonyenga, ngakhale chirombo chochokera pansi, chidzapangitsa onse, ang'ono ndi akulu, olemera ndi osauka, aufulu ndi akapolo, kulandira chizindikiro padzanja lawo lamanja kapena pamphumi pawo: Ndipo kuti pasakhale munthu amene angagule kapena kugulitsa, kupatulapo amene ali ndi chizindikirocho, kapena dzina la chirombocho, kapena nambala ya dzina lake. Choncho mvetsetsani kuti kutenga nawo mbali mu gulu lawo latsopano lonyenga la mabodza kudzafuna chizindikirocho, ndipo onse omwe atenga chizindikirocho sadzakhululukidwa konse. Ndipo adzakhala ndi gawo lawo m'nyanja yaikulu yomwe imayaka ndi moto ndi sulufule kwamuyaya, ndipo motero utsi wa kuzunzika kwawo udzakwera.

Mboni zanga ziwiri zidzauka, ndipo zidzanenera zitavala ziguduli! Izi ndi mitengo ya azitona iwiri, ndi zoyikapo nyali ziwiri zoima pamaso pa Mulungu wa dziko lapansi. Ndipo palibe amene adzatha kuima motsutsana nawo, pakuti adzanenera kwa miyezi 42. Ndipo ngati munthu aliyense afuna kuwapweteka, moto umatuluka mkamwa mwawo, ndikuwononga adani awo: ndipo ngati munthu aliyense afuna kuwapweteka, ayenera kuphedwa m'njira imeneyi. Awa ali ndi mphamvu yotseka kumwamba, kuti isagwe mvula m'masiku a uneneri wawo: ndipo ali ndi mphamvu pa madzi kuwasandutsa magazi, ndi kukantha dziko lapansi ndi miliri yonse, nthawi iliyonse yomwe afuna.

Ndipo akadzamaliza umboni wawo, chirombo chomwe chikutuluka m'phompho chidzachita nkhondo ndi iwo, ndipo chidzawagonjetsa, ndi kuwapha. Ndipo adzasiya matupi awo m'misewu kwa masiku atatu ndi theka, ndipo dziko lonse lapansi lidzakondwera, ndipo kenako adzaukitsidwa ndi kukwatulidwa kupita kumwamba.

Mneneri wonyenga wadzipangitsa kale kukondedwa ndi omwe ali m'dzikolo kuti aganize kuti ndiye Mesiya ndi mfumu yawo. Ndi womanga kachisi wonyenga ndipo amachokera ku fuko la Dani, ndipo si Koresi. Adzapereka thandizo lonse kwa chirombo choyamba chomwe chikutuluka m'nyanja. Pakuti chirombo choyamba chidzakakamiza mwankhanza malamulo a Nowa pa mitundu mu kusayeruzika kwakukulu, ndipo mneneri wonyenga adzalamulira pampando wachifumu womwe si wake, pakuti ndi wanga, ngakhale wa Yeshua HaMashiach. Ndipo motero kachisi adzamangidwa, ndipo mneneri wonyenga adzachita zizindikiro zambiri zonyenga ndi zozizwitsa, pakuti wapatsidwa mphamvu mofanana ndi chirombo choyamba, ndipo motero awiriwo adzagwira ntchito ndi maganizo amodzi ndi cholinga chimodzi, ngakhale ndi maganizo ndi zolinga za chinjoka chachikulu chonyengacho, koma alibe phindu ndipo alibe mano, choncho musamuwope!

Pakuti akudziwa kuti ndikubwera kudzawononga ufumu wake, pakuti si mfumu koma mdani wogonjetsedwa kotheratu, yemwe alibe chiyembekezo chilichonse. Choncho musamuwope ndipo khalani ndi maganizo amodzi pa ine. Pakuti taonani, ndikukuuzani zinthu izi zisanachitike kuti mukhale ndi chikhulupiriro mwa ine ndi kukhala ndi chikhulupiriro mwa Atate wanga amene wandituma, ngakhale Yehova Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya, ndi ngakhale ine mwana wake, Yeshua HaMashiach, yemwe adzakwaniritsa malonjezo omwe anapangidwa kwa atate.

Ndipo motero kumapeto kwa miyezi 42 yoyamba chonyansa cha kupasula chidzachitika m'manja mwa mneneri wonyenga, ngakhale chirombo chomwe chidzauka kuchokera pansi. Mkati mwa miyezi 42 yachiwiri, ngakhale mu nthawi imeneyo chisautso chachikulu kwambiri chidzachitika ndi chonyansa cha kupasula, ndipo adzadzilengeza yekha kukhala Mulungu! Ambiri adzaphwanyidwa ndipo ambiri adzawonongeka, koma omwe akumvera mawu anga adzatengedwa kupita kumalo m'chipululu komwe adzabisidwa ndi kusungidwa otetezeka kwa nyengo, popeza izi zidzakhala nthawi zankhanza kwambiri, ndipo nthawi izi za mayesero ndi kuyesedwa zidzakhala zowawa kwambiri. Kenako mudzaona kumwamba kutatsegulidwa, ndipo mudzandiona Ine, Yesu Khristu, ndikutsogolera kuchokera kumwamba gulu lankhondo lalikulu ndi lamphamvu lakumwamba, ndipo lidzakhala gulu lankhondo losayerekezeka, konse! Pakuti Mkwatibwi wanga adzakhala nane. Ndipo ndidzaphwanya chirombo ndi mneneri wonyenga ndi magulu awo ankhondo onse ndi zonyansa zawo zonse zomwe apanga monyansa, ndipo motero adzawonongedwa ngakhale ndi kuwala kwa kubwera kwanga ndi lupanga lakuthwa konsekonse lomwe likutuluka mkamwa mwanga, ndipo sadzaima koma adzawonongedwa kotheratu. Ndipo chirombo ndi mneneri wonyenga adzaponyedwa amoyo m'nyanja yamoto ndi sulufule yoyakayo, ndipo sadzamasulidwa konse ku kuzunzika kwawo, pakuti adzipangitsa kukhala oyenera.

Ndipo kenako kuuka kwa olungama kudzachitika, pamene oyera mtima akale ndi oyera mtima a chisautso adzaukitsidwa kwa akufa kupita ku moyo watsopano, ndipo sadzafanso, ndipo ndidzawapatsa mphotho yaikulu, yotere yomwe isanalowe m'mitima ya anthu pa nthawi ino. Pakuti ndine Ambuye wachifundo ndi Mfumu yachifundo, ndipo ndine chidzalo cha Umulungu m'thupi.

Ndipo motero ulamuliro wanga wa zaka chikwi pa dziko lapansi udzayamba, ndipo chilungamo chidzalamulira pa dziko lapansi, ndipo ndidzalamulira mitundu ndi ndodo yachitsulo, ndipo dziko lapansi lidzasefukira ndi njira za Yehova. Pakuti ndikubwera ndi gulu lalikulu la mafumu ndi ansembe, ndipo adzalamulira pampando wanga wachifumu, ndipo adzalamulira m'chilungamo. Ndipo mkango udzagona pansi ndi mwanawankhosa.

Ndikukuitanani kuti mumvetsetse nthawi zomwe mukukhalamo.

Ndine mzimu wopatsa moyo, pakuti ndapangidwa motero.

Ndine amene ndidzawapanga kukhala mafumu ndi ansembe pamaso pa Atate wanga.
