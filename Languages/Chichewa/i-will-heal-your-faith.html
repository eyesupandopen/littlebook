Mboni Ziwiri: Mpingo
M'ntchito za Mtumwi Paulo
Meyi 2, 2024

Ndidzachiritsa Chikhulupiriro Chanu, atero Ambuye Wamkulu!

Taonani! Ine, ngakhale Ine, Khristu Yesu Ambuye woukitsidwa ndi mpulumutsi wa dziko lapansi, ndili ndi funso kwa inu, mkwatibwi wanga. Kodi mudzandiyankha moona mtima? Kodi mudzatha kundipatsa yankho loyenera pa mbiri ya choonadi? Chotero, ndikufunsani: Pamene mwana wa munthu akubwera, mukuganiza, kuti ndidzapeza chikhulupiriro pansi pano? Mwachidziwikire ili ndi funso longofuna kuganizira, koma ngati mutayenera kuima pakuita kwanga kuti muyankhe, likanakhala lotani? Kodi munganene kwa ine za chikhulupiriro chanu ndi momwe chilili chodabwitsa? Kodi mudzandiuza za maganizo anu abwino, omwe mwa kupusa mukuganiza kuti mumalamulira dziko lapansi ndi maganizo anu? Kodi simukuzindikira kuti mfundo zofunika kwambiri m'chikhulupiriro changa, ngakhale chikhulupiriro cha Yesu Khristu, ngakhale mfundo zofunika kwambiri zakhala zikupangidwa mwachinyengo ndi kutanthauzidwanso ngati mabodza? Koma inu mwa kupusa mumati: "O, Mulungu sangalole kuti zimenezo zichitike!" Zomwe ndikukufunsani, kodi mumamudziwa bwino bwanji Mulungu? Kodi ndinu phungu wake? Kodi mudzaima ndi kulamula Yahweh? Kodi mudzaima ndi kundilamula Ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, kuti ndichite zimene mukufuna? Komabe ambiri a inu mwa kupusa mumayesa kuchita zomwezo, pakuti mwanyengedwa m'njira yomvetsa chisoni kwambiri.

Mwalola aneneri onyenga, ngakhale namsongole amene Satana wabzala m'mipingo yake yonyenga (yomwe si yanga), aneneri onyenga omwewa omwe m'bale wanga Yuda analankhula pamene ndinauzira mawu anga mumtima mwake ndi pamene ndinasuntha mzimu wake mwa iye m'chiwuziro choyera: Tsoka kwa iwo, pakuti apita m'njira ya Kaini ndipo athamangira mwaumbombo pambuyo pa cholakwa cha Balamu chifukwa cha mphotho, ndipo awonongeka m'kutsutsana kwa Kora. Onyenga awa amayenda poyera pakati panu, ndipo ambiri a inu mumawatsatira, poganiza m'malo mwake kuti mukunditsatira ine, pakuti ndi achinyengo, ndipo amayesa kutengera mawu anga kuti osazindikira pakati pa nkhosa zanga awatsatire m'cholakwa chachikulu, chomwe iwo ali. Pakuti n'chiyani chinanso chomwe Yuda ananena za opanda phindu awa? Awa ndiwo madontho m'maphwando anu a chikondi, pamene achita phhwando ndi inu, kudzidyetsa okha opanda mantha: ndiwo mitambo yopanda madzi, yotengekatengeka ndi mphepo, mitengo yomwe zipatso zake zikufota, yopanda zipatso, yakufa kawiri, yozulidwa ndi mizu. Mafunde aukali a nyanja, akuchita thovu manyazi awoawo, nyenyezi zosochera, zomwe zasungiridwa mdima wandiweyani kosatha.

Ndikudziwa kuti chinyengo n'chokopa kwa ena a inu, pakuti ena a inu simundidziwa ine mwachoonadi koma mumayesetsa kupembedza Yahweh ndi kunditsatira ine, Ambuye wanu Yesu Khristu, kudzera m'mfundo za uthenga wabwino wa chitukuko. Musalakwitse: Uthenga wabwino wa chitukuko ndi bodza, ndipo sindinadzipereke ndekha ngati mwanawankhosa wanu wa Paskha kuti mudzisonkhanitsire chuma: Pakuti kukonda ndalama ndi muzu wa zoipa zonse: zomwe pamene ena anazilakalaka, asochera kuchoka m'chikhulupiriro, ndipo adzibaya okha ndi zowawa zambiri. Ambiri a inu mwatsatira umodzi mwa mauthenga abwino onyenga ambiri, koma palibe uthenga wabwino wina. Ambiri a inu mwatsatira mmodzi mwa Akhristu onyenga ambiri, koma palibe Khristu wina koma ine, Yeshua HaMashiach. Ndikukuuzani momveka bwino, mwanyengedwa, ndipo ambiri a inu mwasinthanitsa chikhulupiriro choona cha Yesu Khristu ndi chikhulupiriro chonyenga. Chikhulupiriro chonyenga ndi chikhulupiriro chabodza pomwe liwu lenileni lakuti chikhulupiriro (kodi sindinakupatseni Mawu oona a Chikhulupiriro?) silinatanthauzenso kudalira, kukhulupirira, ndi kukhazikika mwa Yahweh, Atate wanu, ndi ine, Yesu Khristu Ambuye wanu, koma latanthauzidwanso ku zinthu zopanda phindu zopitirira malire ndi nthano ndi maganizo amatsenga a zamizimu. Mwalola opanda phindu kukusocheretsani, ndipo mipingo yanu yalowedwa ndi asatana, namsongole weniweni amene woipayo anabzala pakati panu.

Zinthu zopitirira malire izi zimathera pamaganizo abwino ndi oipa, ndipo mwa kupusa mukuganiza kuti chilichonse chomwe mumasinkhasinkha kwa nthawi yayitali chidzachitika, monga ngati Mulungu anaika mphamvu yake ndi kukoma mtima kwake kwa omwe ali ndi chikhulupiriro chonyenga kwambiri, pomwe mumalamulira zinthu zachilengedwe ndi maganizo anu. Kodi simukumvetsa kuti onse amene amachita zachabechabe zoterezi agwidwa ndi kukodwa mu msampha ndi kugwidwa ukapolo ku zilakolako zawo ndi mabodza awa, ngakhale kumangidwa ku kulephera, pakuti mukutsatira njira za Satana mosazindikira? Mvetsetsani kuti anali angelo ogwa, inde, Asatana ndi alonda omwe anawaphatikiza, omwe anaphunzitsa anthu ufiti, kudula mizu, matsenga, ndi zithumwa; ndipo anthu oipa ambiri alowa mumpingo wanga wa Laodikaya ndipo atenga ambiri ngati zofunkha omwe akhulupirira mabodza awo.

Izi zachitika chifukwa ambiri mumpingo wanga wa Laodikaya akukana kuima ndi kumenya nkhondo m'chilungamo kuti mugonjetse choipa ndi chabwino! Ndiloleni ndikuthandizeni kumvetsa choonadi. Palibe aliyense wa inu amene ali ndi mphamvu ndi kuthekera mwa inu nokha, pakuti ndi m'kufooka kwanu pamene pomaliza mumazindikira kupanda phindu kwa kuyesetsa kwanu. Ndiye, nthawi zambiri, mumamvetsa kuti pamene mudalira ine, Khristu Yesu, ndi kutsatira malangizo anga m'njira yomwe ndakusankhirani, kuti mumadalira ine ndi mphamvu yanga ndipo muli ndi chikhulupiriro chonse mwa ine. Pakuti ndidzatsimikizira kwa inu nonse m'njira yanga, pamene mukhala ofatsa ku mawu anga ndi kutembenuka pamene ndikuuzani kutero, kuti m'kuyesetsa kwanu ndi mphamvu zanu simuli kanthu koma mchenga m'chipululu, wotayika ndi mikuntho, ndipo sindinafe kuti ndipulumutse mchenga koma dothi lofiira la Adamu, pakuti ndine Adamu womaliza, ndipo ndine mzimu wopatsa moyo. Komabe, mwa ine Khristu Yesu, ndinu amphamvu ndi osaletseka, pakuti muli ndi chuma ichi m'ziwiya zadothi, ndipo ndidzapereka zozizwitsa zambiri, zizindikiro, zodabwitsa ndi zina zotero m'manja mwanu pamene muli ndi chikhulupiriro cha ubale mwa ine, Yesu Khristu, m'malo mwa chikhulupiriro mwa inu nokha. Chonde ndiloleni ndikutsogolereni, ndipo siyani mabodza a ogwawo, pakuti ndi zochita zawo mkwiyo waukulu wasungidwa motsutsana nawo mu tsiku la mkwiyo, pomwe mapeto awo adzanyekedwa pamoto ndi sulufule woyaka.

Mvetsetsani momwe chinyengo ichi chinakwaniritsidwira, chifukwa m'ma Yubile aposachedwa panali kuunikiridwa konyenga komwe omwe gulu lanu limawaona ngati akulu anakana Mulungu mokomera kulingalira kwawo kopanda phindu. Anapanga ngakhale fano la kulingalira pa nthawi ya kusintha kwawo; kodi simukudziwa mbiri yakale? Zitsime zopanda madzi izi ndi nzeru zawo zowonongeka ndi zachabechabe zinakana Mulungu ndipo zinaphunzitsa molakwika kuti Mulungu anali wongoyang'ana chabe yemwe alibe chidwi kwenikweni ndi anthu koma amapereka zonse ali kutali ndipo amalola anthu kuti adzipezere okha, ndi kukoma mtima kwa Mulungu kupita kwa mfiti yamphamvu kwambiri pakati pawo. Ndiye, m'kupita kwa nthawi, miyoyo yambiri yosakhazikika, ngakhale namsongole weniweni wa Satana, analowa mumpingo wanga wa Laodikaya kuti akwaniritse mabodza awo mu nthawi ndi nyengo yoyenera. Analowa mwa inu ndi mawu abwino ndi zolankhula zokongola ndipo anaphunzitsa mphamvu ya maganizo amatsenga kwa Mkwatibwi wanga, ndipo ambiri pakati pa Thupi langa anawatsatira mwaumbombo, pakuti umbombo wawo unadzutsidwa.

Ana oipa a Satana awa anasakaniza ufiti mwamseri m'ziphunzitso zanga ndipo anakwanitsa kuipitsa Mawu anga oyera ndi chotupitsa, kupatula kuti Mawu anga sangaipitsidwe ndi nthano zawo, pakuti mabodza awo amaima pa mabodza. Kodi simukumvetsa kuti chotupitsa pang'ono chimatupitsa mtanda wonse? Kodi simukumvetsa kuti chotupitsa cha Herode ndi chotupitsa cha Afarisi si njira zokha za katangale? Pakuti mwanyengedwa ndi chotupitsa cha ufiti wa Chikhristu! Choncho, LAPANI! Pakuti ndinawauza chiyani Akorinto kudzera m'dzanja la Mtumwi Paulo? Chotsani chotupitsa chakale, kuti mukhale mtanda watsopano, monga muli opanda chotupitsa. Pakuti ngakhale Khristu Paskha wathu anaperekedwa nsembe chifukwa cha ife. Imvani Mawu anga! Chotero zilombo zopanda nzeru izi, zitsime zopanda madzi izi, ngakhale namsongole wopanda phindu ndi wapoizoni analalikira ndi kuyambitsa magulu awoawo a Chikhristu onyenga, ngakhale magulu ampatuko, kuba ambiri a omwe akawatsatira kuchoka ku Mpingo woona, komwe ndimasamalira Mkwatibwi wanga m'chikondi ndi chisomo ndi chifundo, kuwatsogolera kuchoka kwa ine kupita ku nyumba zopembedzera zonyenga zoperekedwa mwamseri kwa Baala, pakuti mtundu wanu ndi Babulo Wachinsinsi!

Monga ngati izi sizinali zoipa zokwanira, amfiti ambiri amphamvu ndi olemera analipira ndalama zambiri ndi kugwiritsa ntchito khama lalikulu kuyika mabodza awo m'Mabaibulo onyenga. Anabweretsa malemba a ku Alekizandriya, a gnostic, kupangitsa Mpingo wanga kukhala wa mitima iwiri kuchoka pa cholinga chimodzi cha malemba omwe ndinasunga kuchokera ku Antiokeya, ngakhale Mabaibulo ena ofotokozera komwe mabodza awa anawonjezedwa m'mbali. Mabuku awa anayambitsidwa m'maseminale anu owonongeka ndipo anakhala Mabaibulo ophunzirira okhazikika kwa aneneri onyenga awa omwe alalikira mabodza mwachinyengo kuyambira pamenepo, ndipo adzalipira mokwanira. Kuyambira nthawi imeneyo, mayiko anu adzaza ndi mipingo yonyenga yotsogoleredwa ndi mimbulu yolusa, ndipo nkhosa zanga zambiri zikuganizabe molakwika kuti zikhoza kuyanjana ndi mimbulu. Mipingo yanu yonyenga ndi nyumba za mwano; ndi nyumba za zosangalatsa komwe amaimba matamando kwa Satana pomwe inu mukuyang'ana, ndipo mulibe kuzindikira kwauzimu kuti muone. Pali ambuye ambiri pakati pa ufumu wa Mdyerekezi, ndipo amaimba Ambuye, Ambuye kwa iwo, kupatula pamene anena kwa ine Ambuye, Ambuye, ndidzati, Chokani kwa ine, inu ochita zoipa pakuti sindinakudziweni inu.

Kuti zinthu ziipireipire, anatenga uthenga wabwino wa chitukuko wamwano ndikuusinthanso powonjezera psychology yodzithandiza, ndipo ndanena chiyani pa nkhani zoterezi: Chenjerani kuti asapezeke munthu wina akulanda inu ngati zofunkha kudzera m'nzeru za anthu ndi chinyengo chopanda pake, potsata miyambo ya anthu, ndi malangizo a dziko lapansi, osati potsata Khristu. Mipingo yonyenga iyi ndi yowonongeka yopanda phindu lowombola, ndipo yambiri inakhazikitsidwa ndi omanga oipa omwe ali omasuka kwa ine, mwala wapangodya waukulu, ndipo nyumba zawo zonse zidzagwa m'mchenga wosuntha. Awa ndi omanga amwano ndi osema miyala onyenga ndi omanga njerwa onyenga omwe akana mwala wapangodya waukulu, ndipo alibe choonadi mwa iwo! Amaonetsa ngakhale zizindikiro zawo m'nyumba zanu zomwe mumati ndi zopembedzera, komwe amakunyozani poyera. Imvani Miyambo yomwe ndili nayo kwa inu, pakuti ndi yoona ndipo idzabweretsa kuunikiridwa koona ku miyoyo yanu: Munthu wopanda pake, munthu woipa, amayenda ndi pakamwa poyipa. Amatsinzina ndi maso ake, amalankhula ndi mapazi ake, amaphunzitsa ndi zala zake.

Kusamvera kuli mumtima mwake, amapanga chiwembu mosalekeza, amafesa kusamvana. Ndipo momwemo alili ndipo momwemo amachitira pamaso panu, koma simungathe kuona, pakuti mwakhala akhungu m'ziphunzitso zawo zonyenga ndi njira zawo zosamvera.

Ana anga, tulukani pakati pawo ndipo mudzilekanitse; pakuti Khristu ali ndi chiyanjano chanji ndi Beliyali. Musakhale ndi chiyanjano ndi ntchito zopanda zipatso za mdima, ndipo mudzilekanitse nawo kotheratu. Pakuti inu, mpingo wanga wa Laodikaya, ngakhale inu ndinu ofunda ndi opanda msana, ndipo izi si njira zanga ngakhale pang'ono, pakuti ndinathamangitsa osintha ndalama m'Kachisi m'changu changa cha Yahweh. Bwererani kwa ine, Yesu Khristu, mbusa woona ndi sing'anga wa miyoyo yanu, ndipo phunzirani kwa ine. Pakuti kuvomereza kwabwino ndi maganizo abwino opanda phindu si a ine, koma mabodza onong'onezedwa kwa inu ndi atumwi onyenga, ngakhale atumiki enieni a Satana omwe adzisandutsa okha kukhala atumiki a chilungamo. Thawirani kwa ine, tiana twanga, pakuti nthawi yatha, ndipo inu mosazindikira mukuganiza kuti muli ndi nthawi yonse m'dziko lapansi, ndipo muli ndi mphindi zochepa chabe kuti mulape ndi kubwerera kwa ine modzichepetsa kuti ndikhoze kukukonzerani malo patebulo langa ndi kuti ndikhoze kukutsegulirani khomo kumwamba kuti mulowe m'chisangalalo cha Ambuye wanu!

Pakuti ambiri a inu mukuganiza kuti mudzasonkhanitsidwa kwa ine pamene mukukhala mabodza awa ndi pamene mukudya zinyalala zomwe aneneri onyenga amakudyetsani, mwamwano m'dzina langa. Dziwani kuti sindinawatume ndipo mawu awo si a ine, ndipo ngati simuchoka kwa iwo, mudzayenera kudzitsimikizira nokha kwa ine m'mayesero ambiri ndi kuyesedwa pa nthawi ya nsautso ya Yakobo, yomwe yafika pa dziko lonse lapansi ndipo yatsala pang'ono kumasulidwa! Ndiyang'aneni ine mu mzimu wanu; tcherani khutu, pakuti mukhoza kumva zowawa zobereka mu mzimu wanu, pakuti ndikukuitanani kunyumba ndipo Mkwatibwi wanga watsala pang'ono kupulumutsidwa. Taonani, ndikusiya ndi inu kalata yomwe ndinakutumizirani, inu a Laodikaya anga, pakuti m'mawu awa mwaunikiridwa njira yobwerera kwa ine; ndipo ngati mungamvere, ndidzabwezeretsa mphotho yanu kwa inu, ndipo mudzadya nane patebulo langa kumwamba komwe ndakonzekera Mkwatibwi wanga.

Ndipo kwa mthenga wa mpingo, womwe uli ku Laodikaya, lemba: Atero (Ameni) mboni yokhulupirika ndi yoona, chiyambi cha zolengedwa za Mulungu. Ndikudziwa ntchito zanu, kuti simuli ozizira kapena otentha. Ndikanakonda mukanakhala ozizira kapena otentha. Chotero chifukwa muli pakati pa zonse ziwiri, ndipo simuli ozizira kapena otentha. Ndidzakulavulani m'kamwa mwanga: chifukwa mukuti ndine wolemera ndipo ndachuluka ndi katundu, ndipo sindikusowa kanthu, ndipo simukudziwa kuti ndinu wosauka ndi womvetsa chisoni, wosauka, wakhungu ndi wamaliseche. Ndikukulangizani kuti mugule kwa ine golide woyesedwa pamoto, kuti mukhale olemera, ndi zovala zoyera, kuti muvekedwe, kuti maliseche anu onyansa asaonekere, ndipo dzozani maso anu ndi mankhwala a maso, kuti muone. Onse amene ndimawakonda, ndimawadzudzula ndi kuwalanga. Khalani achangu, chotero, ndipo lapani. Taonani, ndaima pakhomo ndikugogoda. Ngati munthu aliyense amva mawu anga ndi kutsegula khomo, ndidzalowa kwa iye, ndipo ndidzadya naye, ndipo iye ndi ine. Kwa iye amene apambana ndidzamupatsa kukhala nane pampando wanga, monga momwe inenso ndinapambana ndipo ndakhala ndi Atate wanga, pampando wake.

Taonani!

Ndine amene ndimayenda pakati pa zoyikapo nyale zisanu ndi ziwiri, chotero bwererani kwa ine, kuti ndisachotse choyikapo nyale chanu kwa inu.

Ndine mbusa wabwino amene anadzipereka yekha chifukwa cha gulu la nkhosa.

Ndine amene ndidzalamulira dziko lapansi kuchokera pampando wachifumu wa Davide ndipo ndidzakantha mitundu ndi ndodo yachitsulo.

Ndine amene ndili woyenera kutenga bukhu ndi kuthyola zisindikizo.

Ndine amene ndimalamula mngelo wokwera kuchokera kum'mawa, ali ndi chisindikizo cha Mulungu wamoyo, pakuti adzasindikiza 144,000 anga, ngakhale atumiki a Mulungu wamoyo.

Ndine amene ndidzaima pa Phiri la Ziyoni ndi 144,000 anga, ndipo adzaimba nyimbo yatsopano yomwe palibe munthu angathe kuiphunzira.

Ndine Ambuye wa Ambuye ndi Mfumu ya Mafumu, ndipo ndikubwera kudzachita nkhondo ndi mafumu a dziko lapansi, pakuti nthawi yawo yakwana.

Ndine amene ndikubwera kudzatenga Mkwatibwi wanga, pakuti kuonekera kwanga kuli pafupi. Konzekerani, Mkwatibwi wanga!
