Chiweluzo cha Moto cha Heylel Ben Shachar

Imvani, Imvani Mawu a Adonai-YHVH ndipo sonkhanani pafupi ndi phokoso la mawu anga, kuti mukhale pansi pa mapazi anga, ana anga ndi kumva Mawu anga omwe ndimawamasula kuchokera ku mzimu wa mthenga wanga wamphamvu ndi mtumiki wanga. Mawu operekedwa mu ulamuliro waukulu mwa Mwana wanga wobadwa yekha yemwe anasindikiza Mawu amphamvu awa mwa mthenga wathu ndi mtumiki wathu yemwe ndamupereka kwa Khristu Yesu ngati chiwiya chosankhidwa, kuti akhale kudzanja lake lamanzere mu ulemu wowirikiza ngati mbusa wachikondi ndi woteteza yemwe ali wodzazidwa ndi chifundo chifukwa akuleredwa ndi Mbusa Wamkulu yemwe ndi Yeshua HaMashiach ndikudzazidwa ndi chikondi changa mu chifundo chosatha ndi chisomo choperekedwa mwa mwanawankhosa wa Adonai-YHVH mu chikondi chake momwe wapindira ndi kuumba Biblaridon:144 payekha, monga momwe wakwaniritsira mokwanira mwa 144,000 ake onse amphamvu omwe ndi ana a Yeshua HaMashiach omwe wawalera payekha ndi dzanja lake, ndipo palibe tsankho kwa anthu m'njira za Adonai-YHVH ndipo Yeshua HaMashiach ndi Mawu a Adonai-YHVH opangidwa thupi. Choncho, mvetsetsani inu nonse 144,000: Ndinu ana a Atate Yeshua, ndipo NDINE Adonai-YHVH wanu yemwe ndi Atate kwa inu nonse mwa Khristu Yesu. Selah.

Choncho, mvetsetsani kusamalira kwanga kwamuyaya ndi ulamuliro wanga kuti NDINE YEMWE ali wokhoza kudzutsa ankhondo amphamvu kuti Yeshua HaMashiach awalamulire, yemwe tsopano wayima mu ulamuliro wamphamvu mokwanira ndipo wapatsidwa mphamvu zonse ndi kutumizidwa kudzapulumutsa okhulupirika mwa Khristu Yesu ngati Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda kuchokera pakati, kubweretsa chiwonongeko chadzidzidzi pamene akumasula ola limodzi ndi chilombo pa kunyamuka kwanu (mwadzidzidzi) pa nthawi imeneyo yoperekedwa mokwanira m'manja mwake kuti akwaniritse mu kumvera konse kolungama ndi kosangalatsa, ndipo ndi Yeshua HaMashiach yemwe nthawi zonse amachita chifuniro cha Adonai-YHVH wake.

Mofananamo, Biblaridion:144 ndi chida champhamvu ndi chamitundumitundu m'dzanja la Yeshua HaMashiach yemwe ali mu mgwirizano wathunthu ndi zolinga zake monga zavumbulutsidwa mpaka pano ndi mpaka kalekale, yemwe alinso wothandizira wa chiweluzo cholengeza ndi chogwira ntchito, kuti agwiritsidwe ntchito mwa lamulo kuti amenye, ndikulamulidwanso kulengeza ndi kuchita ndendende monga kwalengezedwa pansi pa ulamuliro wathunthu wa Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda. Zoonadi, Biblaridion ndi lupanga lomwe lili m'chimakolo logwiridwa m'dzanja ndi Yeshua HaMashiach kuti lisololedwe mokwanira mkati mwa sabata la makumi asanu ndi awiri la Danieli, komanso chifukwa cha zolinga zanga zamuyaya zomwe zikuyenera kuvumbulutsidwa pamene nonse mudzasonkhanitsidwa kwa ine mu kuthwanima kwa diso komwe ndidzavumbulutsa kwa inu umunthu wanu weniweni ndi zolinga zomwe pakali pano zabisika mwa Khristu Yesu, koma tsopano zavumbulutsidwa pang'ono.

Mvetsetsani ana anga kuti okhulupirika onse mwa Khristu Yesu omwe ndi mpingo wake, thupi lake, ngakhale mkwatibwi wake asankhidwa mwachindunji mwa Khristu Yesu asanakhazikitsidwe maziko a dziko lapansi. Ngakhale ambiri amatanthauzira Mawu awa ndi mawu opusa ngati "okondedwa kwambiri ndi odalitsidwa mwa choonadi" kuti apereke chitonthozo ku thupi pamene anenedwa mopanda chikhulupiriro ndi popanda kumvetsetsa choonadi changa chamuyaya, chimenecho si cholinga chathunthu koma ndithudi choona kwa okhulupirika mwa Khristu chifukwa cha kumvera kwawo ndi kulapa kwawo.

Mvetsetsani, "mawu akuti osankhidwa mwa iye asanakhazikitsidwe maziko a dziko lapansi" akusonyeza kuti Adonai-YHVH wanu wasankha yekha ndi kutsogolera mu mphamvu zonse za chifuniro ndi mapangidwe a cholinga changwiro, popanda kuphwanya ufulu wosankha wa munthu aliyense ngakhale pang'ono, mwa kuumba, kupanga, ndi kulenganso mwa Khristu Yesu aliyense wa inu ndi kumvetsetsa kwathunthu ndi ufulu wathunthu waluso ndi mapangidwe ndi chidwi chodabwitsa chotere ku tsatanetsatane mwa aliyense wa inu chomwe chatenga nthawi yonse ya mibadwo kuyambira Adamu ndi Hava mpaka tsopano kuti chikwaniritsidwe mu kulondola kwanga.

Inu ndinu ntchito zanga zaluso zopangidwa mwa Khristu!

Luso, kulondola ndi mapangidwe omwe afesedwa mu chibadwa chanu komwe ndasankha kwenikweni ndi luso lalikulu ndi ukadaulo tsatanetsatane aliyense wa inu nonse chifukwa cha zolinga zanga zamuyaya zomwe ziyenera kuvumbulutsidwa mokwanira m'chipinda changa chachifumu, nthawi yomweyo ndi mwamphamvu pa lamulo langa. Choncho, mvetsetsani kuti ndaumba, kupanga, ndi kulenga aliyense wa inu mwachindunji komanso mwatsatanetsatane kwambiri kotero kuti simungathe kumvetsetsa zomwe nonse mwatsala pang'ono kusinthidwa malinga ndi zolinga zanga zapadera ndi zosankhidwa kwa aliyense wa inu.

Choncho, musaope, Adonai-YHVH wanu sapanga cholengedwa chimodzi kukhala chosiyana ndi chinzake mwa kusankha kuti mwayi womwe ukuwoneka ukondwereredwe mu tchimo lodzikuza, kapena kuti cholengedwa chimodzi chizilamulira chinzake mu chiwawa chopusa chauchimo ndi chidani, koma kuti zolinga zanga zamuyaya zikwaniritsidwe mwa inu nonse. Za choonadi chamuyaya: Sizinakhalepo mokwanira mu mtima wa munthu pa nthawi ya kulemba uku zomwe mudzalengedwanso ndi zolinga zotani. Choncho, ndikukupemphani mu chikondi changa kwa inu (olengedwa anga ndi osankhidwa chifukwa cha chikhulupiriro chanu, ana anga olapa mokhulupirika) kuti mukhale ndi chikhulupiriro ngati kambewu ka mpiru ndi kumvetsetsa kuti kuyambira wamng'ono mpaka wamkulu pakati pa Isiraeli wa Adonai-YHVH nonse mwapangidwa mwangwiro ndi modabwitsa ndikupangidwa ndi kuumbidwa mwamphamvu kwambiri kotero kuti ndi Isiraeli wa Adonai-YHVH yemwe adzasiliridwa ndi onse.

Mtendere ukhale kwa inu mokwanira.

Ndipo izi popanda tsankho kwa anthu, koma m'malo mwake mwa chidziwitso changa cholungama ndi chamuyaya champhamvu zonse ndi ulamuliro wolondola wa zinthu zonse komwe ndinasankha aliyense wa inu m'njira yotere chifukwa cha zolinga zanga zamuyaya mwa Khristu Yesu. Chifukwa ndimadziwa mokwanira mu nthawi zamuyaya zapitazo kuti aliyense wa inu, payekha, adzabwera mokwanira kwa Khristu Yesu mu nthawi zanu, choncho ndinapereka. Choncho, mvetsetsani pamene zinsinsi zobisika zikuvumbulutsidwa ndi umboni ndi chitsanzo cha utumiki mwa Biblaridion:144, zinthu izi zikuchitidwa kuti nonse muyambe kumvetsetsa ukulu wa cholowa chanu ndi zolinga zamuyaya zomwe ndakulengani, kukusankhani, ndi kukupangani nonse mwa mapangidwe anzeru mpaka tsatanetsatane wamng'ono kwambiri. Kondwerani!

Choncho, bwerani mokwanira kwa Yeshua HaMashiach ngakhale inu nonse okhulupirika mwa Khristu Yesu, kuti mulandire mphoto yanu yowirikiza mokwanira mu ulemu wathunthu, yomwe ali nayo m'dzanja yomwe ndapereka kwa onse omwe apambana mofanana ndi momwe Khristu Yesu anapambanira. Imvani Mawu anga olimbikitsa ngakhale inu nonse 144,000, omwe ndinu ana otengedwa a Atate wanu Yeshua, ndipo adzagawana zinthu zonse ndi inu ana ake, omwe alinso opambana ake okhulupirika, omwe apambana zinthu zonse monga Yeshua HaMashiach anapambanira. Komabe, awa si opambana okhawo omwe adzakhale atapambana zinthu zonse zotsutsana nawo, chifukwa ambiri, ambiri pakati pa okhulupirika mwa Khristu Yesu omwe si 144,000 apambana zinthu zonse mofanana ndi Khristu Yesu, ndipo mphoto yawo ndi yaikulu!

Inde! Pali opambana ambiri pakati pa Isiraeli wa Adonai-YHVH ndipo palibe tsankho kwa anthu ndi Adonai-YHVH, ndipo makamaka Yeshua HaMaschiach yemwe nthawi zonse amachita mwangwiro chifuniro cha Adonai-YHVH wake. Choncho, musade nkhawa ndi kudandaula pamene mukuyesetsa kumvetsetsa zomwe zabisika Kumwamba zomwe sizinalowe mokwanira mu mtima wa munthu pa nthawi ino, ndipo makamaka pewani kudziyerekezera wina ndi mzake monga momwe zimakhalira mu mkhalidwe wanu wamakono wakugwa, koma woyeretsedwa mokwanira mwa Khristu Yesu.

Choncho mvetsetsani, mu Ufumu wa Kumwamba, aliyense amene akwezedwa wina kuposa mzake amachitidwa mu ulamuliro, ndi/kapena mphoto chifukwa cha zolinga zanga zokha, koma osati kuwalemekeza kuti wina mu chilengedwe changa adzimve kukhala wotsika, kapena kuti omwe angakwezedwe adzadzitamandire mu tchimo pa mzake. Koma m'malo mwake, ulemu ndi chikondi zimaperekedwa mokwanira kwa inu nonse. Chifukwa ndakulengani nonse, ndipo makamaka okhulupirika mwa Khristu Yesu chifukwa cha kumvera kwanu mwa Khristu Yesu kuti ndikuthenso kukulengani ndi ulemu wathunthu. Inu, makamaka, ndinu ntchito zanga zaluso zolengedwa mwa Khristu Yesu ku ntchito zabwino kuti muyende mwa izo mu mphamvu yaikulu chifukwa cha zolinga zamuyaya za Adonai-YHVH wanu, zoperekedwa mwa Khristu Yesu. Kondwerani!

Ndipo zili choncho kuti NDINE Wamkulu, ngakhale Atate wa mkwati, yemwe ndi Yeshua HaMashiach wapereka Kulira kwa Pakati pa Usiku kwa mkwatibwi ndi alendo ake, ndipo adzikonzekeretsa okha kotero kuti ali ndi nyale zawo zodzazidwa ndi mafuta a mzimu woyera ndipo zingwe zanu zadulidwa zonyansa zonse ndipo nyale zanu zayatsidwa ndi malawi oyera opangidwa ndi mafuta oyera a mzimu wanga omwe ndapereka mwa Yeshua HaMashiach, ndipo kuti walenga mwapadera mkati mwa aliyense wa inu, okhulupirika mwa Khristu.

Ndipo choncho, ndikulimbikitsa okhulupirika onse mwa Khristu Yesu kuti apitirize kumvera mokwanira ndi mwatsatanetsatane mu zinthu zonse kwa Mbusa Wanu Wamkulu yemwe amasamalira tsatanetsatane aliyense wa miyoyo yanu mu tsatanetsatane wamng'ono kwambiri ndi wangwiro kwambiri. Bwerani mokwanira kwa Ambuye Wanu Wamuyaya wa Chilungamo yemwe ndakupatsani nonse pamene Yeshua wa ku Nazarete anadzipereka yekha ngati Paskha wangwiro wa nthawi zonse m'manja mwa kalonga wamng'ono wogonjetsedwayo mu kumvera konse ku chifuniro cha Adonai-YHVH. Bwerani mokwanira tsopano, popanda kukayikira ndikumvetsetsa kuti NDINE Wamkulu akukukokani kudzera mu Mawu awa omwe akonzedwa kuti apeze maso, makutu, ndi mitima ya onse omwe atumizidwa kwa iwo.

Mvetsetsani nthawi yomwe mukukhala, chifukwa nthawi yanga ya chisomo yafika kumapeto, kuti ithetsedwe mwadzidzidzi ndi mosayembekezereka ndipo mokwanira ndi lamulo la Yeshua HaMashiach pamene lamulo laperekedwa ndi kufuula kwamphamvu!

Kenako, kulira kwamphamvu ndi kokongola kwa lipenga, pamene mwadzidzidzi pa dziko lapansi simudzakhalapo!

Pamene mwadzidzidzi kumwamba kwa kumwamba mudzalandiridwa mu ulemerero waukulu, chigonjetso, mphamvu, ndi ulemu mu cholowa chanu cha mibadwo chomwe chakonzedwa kuti chigawidwe kwa okhulupirika mwa Khristu Yesu. Zoonadi nyengo yoyembekezeredwa ya chiwombolo cha Adamu kwa okhulupirika mwa Khristu Yesu yafika Kumwamba ngati palibe nyengo ina iliyonse, chifukwa Ana anga amuyaya omwe asankhidwa mwachindunji ndi Mawu awa operekedwa mu Yohane Woyamba: "Taonani, chikondi chotani chomwe Atate watipatsa, kuti titchedwe ana a Mulungu: Chifukwa chake dziko lapansi silikudziwani, chifukwa silinamudziwe iye. Okondedwa, tsopano tili ana a Mulungu, koma, sizinaonekere zomwe tidzakhala: ndipo tikudziwa kuti pamene adzaonekera: tidzakhala ngati iye: chifukwa tidzamuona monga ali. Ndipo munthu aliyense amene ali ndi chiyembekezo ichi mwa iye amadziyeretsa yekha, monga iye ali woyera", tsopano akuyenera kubadwanso amoyo ndi kulengedwanso mokwanira mu ulemerero wonse, ulemerero, ndi ulemu nthawi yomweyo pa lamulo la Yeshua HaMashiach lomwe ndamupatsa mwachindunji ndipo TSOPANO mudzayima mwadzidzidzi ndi mosayembekezereka m'chipinda changa chachifumu ndi okhulupirika onse mwa Khristu Yesu.

Harpazo yanu yafika!

Ndipo choncho, ndikulamula okhulupirika onse mwa Khristu Yesu kuti atsatire Mawu awa pakumvera kwakukulu kuti Khristu Yesu akuyeretseni nonse mokwanira pansi pa magazi ake otetezera mwangotsala pang'ono kuti mutengedwe ndi kusinthidwa kwamuyaya ndi mpaka kalekale.

Khalani Okonzeka!

Chifukwa muyenera kubadwira m'chipinda changa chachifumu, komwe ndikudikira mosangalala ngati Atate woyembekezera yemwe NDINE, chifukwa cha kubadwa kwathunthu kwa Isiraeli wa Adonai-YHVH komwe kuyenera kusonkhanitsidwa TSOPANO.

Ndi maganizo awa, ndikubwera kudzathandiza aliyense wa inu kumvetsetsa khama ndi tsatanetsatane wogwiritsidwa ntchito mu nzeru zakuya ndi luso ndi Adonai-YHVH wanu kubisa ndi kukutetezani kwa Heylel Ben Shachar yemwe angaphe kuchokera pa dziko lapansi aliyense wa ana anga atapatsidwa mwayi, koma kukuwululani mokwanira kuti mukhoze kuumbidwa malinga ndi zolinga zanga zamuyaya mwa Khristu Yesu.

Choncho, munalembedwa chizindikiro ndi kutetezedwa, koma kubisidwa ndi kubisidwa poyera, koma kuvumbulutsidwa mokwanira: Ndipo zonse ndi manja a Khristu Yesu! Chifukwa ndi iye amene anakukondani ndikudzipereka yekha, ndikuyeretsa, ndi kuyeretsa ndi kulungamitsa Mpingo wake wamphamvu womwe umapangidwa ndi okhulupirika mwa Khristu Yesu. Chifukwa Zoonadi, zipata za Gahena sizidzapambana pa Mpingo weniweni mu ulemerero wake wonse wamphamvu ndi mphamvu. Ngakhale Mpingo womwe uyenera kusinthidwa ndi kusinthidwa tsopano chifukwa cha zolinga zawo zenizeni ndi zobisika kuti tsopano zivumbulutsidwe mokwanira zomwe nonse mudzadziwa mokwanira monga momwe mukudziwidwira.

Khalani oleza mtima ana anga, nthawi yanu ndi TSOPANO.

Komabe, ndalekanitsa gulu limodzi lapadera kuchokera ku Mpingo, kuwasankha pasadakhale chifukwa cha zolinga zanga (ngakhale nonse muli ndi dzina) ndikuwapereka kwa Khristu Yesu. Choncho, mvetsetsani kusiyana uku kwa inu nonse 144,000 ndi ngakhale onse omwe amawerenga Mawu awa.

Khristu Yesu mu chikondi chake kwa inu nonse, ndi chikondi chake ndi kumvera kwa Adonai-YHVH wake anadzutsa ana a Adonai-YHVH omwe ndi okhulupirika mwa Khristu Yesu ndikusindikizidwa ndi mzimu woyera wa lonjezo ndikupatulidwa ndi kudzipereka kwamuyaya kuti adzaze madongosolo omwe alibe anthu Kumwamba mu ulemu wowirikiza, komanso kulandira dziko lapansi mu ulemu wowirikiza chifukwa munasankha kukhala ndi chikhulupiriro mu umboni wa Yesu wa ku Nazarete mu tsiku loipali, ngakhale mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu pamene anavumbulutsidwa kwa inu ndi Adonai-YHVH wanu ndipo mutapatsidwa mwayi wokhala ndi chikhulupiriro cha kambewu ka mpiru, munakhulupirira! Ndipo mbewu yamphamvu ndi yamuyaya yakula mwamphamvu tsopano mwa inu nonse! Koma yavumbulutsidwa mokwanira m'chipinda changa chachifumu mwadzidzidzi ndi mosayembekezereka tsopano.

KHALANI OKONZEKA!

Mvetsetsani mokwanira, kuti okhulupirika mwa Khristu Yesu ndi ana a Adonai-YHVH, omwe adzatumikira Khristu Yesu, yemwe alinso Yeshua HaMashiach, mu ntchito zanu ndi m'madongosolo anu omwe ndakonza mwangwiro ndi kukonzekera aliyense wa inu. Mphoto yanu yakonzedwa kwa inu mwangwiro ndipo idzakhala yaikulu wina kuposa mzake, malinga ndi momwe kukhulupirika kwanu kwakhalira. Komabe, ichi si chopinga ku ulemerero wanu mwa cholowa, koma ulemu woperekedwa kwa yemwe akuyenera ulemu chifukwa cha zinthu zambiri zomwe zakwaniritsidwa mu utumiki kwa Khristu Yesu ndi inu nonse. Ndipo nonse ndinu osiyana wina ndi mzake, koma ofanana mu zinthu zomwe mwapirira ndi kupambana ndi dzanja lamphamvu la Khristu Yesu yemwe wakuthandizani nonse.

Komanso, otengedwa pakati pa okhulupirika mwa Khristu Yesu ndi 144,000 omwe apatulidwa mwachindunji ngati ana otengedwa a Yeshua HaMashiach, osankhidwa pakati pa Isiraeli wa Adonai-YHVH mwachindunji ndi dzanja langa losankhidwa, ndi chifuniro chamuyaya ndi chosalephereka. Ankhondo amoto awa anaumbidwa mu masautso akulu ndi kudzera mu mayesero ndi masautso ambiri omwe onse apirira m'manja mwa Heylel Ben Shachar, ndipo tsopano apindidwa ndi kuumbidwa mu mphamvu yaikulu ndi nyonga kotero kuti aumbidwa mokwanira m'mitima yawo yomwe sidzapereka zolinga za Adonai-YHVH zosonyezedwa mwa Yeshua HaMashiach, ngakhale kusungidwa mu chikondi chosapindika ndi champhamvu zonse cha Agape. Selah.

Choncho, mvetsetsani inu 144,000 anga, kuti nonse munalengedwa mwamphamvu ndi mwangwiro m'mapangidwe amphamvu ndi anzeru, ndipo palamulo la Khristu Yesu, yemwenso ndi Yeshua HaMashiach, mudzakwaniritsa zolinga zathu zamuyaya motsutsana ndi njoka ya ku gehena iyi, ndipo kuti onse ogwa aweruzidwe mokwanira ndi chilango chokwanira choperekedwa m'chiweruzo chamoto cholungama pa kalonga wamng'ono wolephera ndi wopusa uyu. Ngakhale zili choncho, nonse 144,000 ndinu nthumwi za zochita izi zomwe zaperekedwa poyang'ana malemba omwe alembedwa ndendende kuti: Kubwezera ndi kwanga: Ine ndidzabwezera, atero Ambuye. Choncho, ndinu nthumwi zosankhidwa za kubwezera kolungama kumeneku pamitu ya onse ogwa, koma makamaka kalonga wamng'ono wopusa, wolephera ndi wodzitamandira nthawi zonse, Heylel Ben Shachar.

Ngakhale kalonga wamng'ono wolephera ameneyo yemwe analemekezedwa kuposa anzake chifukwa cha zolinga zamuyaya za Elohim, ndipo m'chilakolako chake ndi kunyada kwake pamene amadzilemekeza yekha, poganiza kuti zonse zomwe ndinamulengera zinali za ulemerero wake ndi zolinga zake kuti zigwiritsidwe ntchito palamulo lake ndi chifuniro chake, ndipo motero anagwa m'kupanduka kwakukulu ndi kusamvera m'njira yochititsa manyazi kwambiri m'manyazi onse ndi kunyada kophwanyidwa kotheratu ndipo amasungidwa m'malo omvetsa chisoni kwambiri ndi opunduka omwe sadzachira ndipo adzangowonjezereka kwamuyaya.

Mogwirizana ndi izi, pamene Heylel Ben Shachar (ndi onse amene anatsatira aserafi awa) anaponyedwa mwamphamvu, ndi mwachiwawa, ndi m'kupweteka kwakukulu mopanda vuto ndi mphamvu zanga kuchokera Kumwamba kwa M'mwamba, pamene anavulidwa mwamphamvu ulamuliro wonse chifukwa cha machimo awo owopsa ndi adala ndi okonzekeratu omwe anachitidwa ndi chidani chonse ndi chiwembu. Ngakhale zinthu zomwe zinakula kuchokera m'mitima yawo chifukwa Heylel Ben Shachar anakana kulamulira maganizo ake pomvera Elohim wake, m'malo moyang'ana pa ulemerero ndi mphamvu za Elohim wake, kuti ndimukweze ku malo ake amuyaya a utumiki, mphamvu, ndi ulemerero, m'malo mwake anayang'ana pa ulemerero wake wowonekera womwe anayesa kuugwira mwamphamvu ndi mwachiwawa ngati wake.

Ndipo m'zinyengo zake zauchimo zomwe zinachitika pamene anadzilemekeza yekha mu tchimo pamwamba pa Elohim wake yemwe anamulenga mwamphamvu ndi cholinga champhamvu ndi kumuveka korona wa ulemerero waukulu ndi ulemu tsiku lomwe analengedwa malinga ndi mapulani ndi zolinga zanga, ndi luso ndi mphamvu, Heylel Ben Shachar ndi onse amene anamutsatira anaponyedwa mwachiwawa kuchokera Kumwamba asanathe kukwera kudzaza malo awo amuyaya a utumiki, mphamvu, ndi ulemerero chifukwa anadzipereka okha ndi malumbiro a kusamvera kwa chidani ku kupanduka kopanda nzeru m'malo mwake.

Heylel Ben Shachar kenako mwadala ndipo ndi kukonzekeratu kwathunthu anafesa kupanduka kwake mwachinsinsi pakati pa anzake, ngati kuti El Shaddai sanawone zochita zake ndi maganizo ndi zolinga za mtima wake.
