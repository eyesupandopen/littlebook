Kufotokozera kwa Mabingu Asanu ndi Awiri

Chinsinsi cha Kabuku

Kuchokera kwa Yeshua HaMashiach:

Taonani, chinsinsi cha kabuku! Pakuti angelo asanu ndi awiri atumiza mabingu awo, ndipo mabinguwo atulutsa mawu awo. Iwo ndi chinsinsi cha kabuku, pakuti kabukuko kanali kotsekedwa. Pakuti mawu anachokera kumwamba, kuuza Yohane Wovumbulutsa: Tseka zinthu zimene mabingu asanu ndi awiri ananena, ndipo usazilembe. Taonani, inu munadya kabukuko, pakuti kavumbulutsidwa, ndipo kanali kokoma ngati uchi m'makutu anu pamene munali kugona, koma kanali kowawa m'mimba mwanu komwe kunakudutsani m'masomphenya; ndipo taonani, mwadzuka ku tulo twanu! Taonani, chinsinsi cha kabuku chafika kuti chivumbulutsidwe mu nthawi ino, pakuti ndi chinsinsi chomaliza kuvumbulutsidwa mu m'badwo wanga wa chisomo, ngakhale kabuku ka zokoma zowawa. Pakuti mawu a mabingu akuyenera kuvumbulutsidwa, ndipo tsopano ndi nthawi yovumbulutsa, pakuti chinsinsi cha kabuku ndi chachikulu, ngakhale kali kabuku. Pakuti kabukuko kanavumbulutsidwa kwa Yohane Wovumbulutsa, ndipo tsopano ndikuvumbulutsirani inu, pakuti kuvumbulutsidwa kwake kuli pafupi. Imvani mawu anga, mkwatibwi wanga, ndipo tcherani khutu lanu kwa ine, Khristu Yesu, pakuti nthawi yakwana. Pakuti palibenso nthawi.

Ndine mwana wa Mulungu wobwera m'thupi, ndipo Ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndatumiza mawu awa.

Kuchokera kwa Yahweh, Mulungu wamuyaya:

Mawu a mabingu asanu ndi awiri AKULANKHULA! Konzekerani, inu mitundu, pakuti chiweruzo chachikulu chili pa inu, ndipo mawu a mabingu asanu ndi awiri avumbulutsidwa. Tcherani makutu anu kuti mumve zonena za chinsinsi cha Kabuku. Pakuti mipukutu idzatsekulidwa, ndipo kumasulidwa kwa zizindikiro kukubwera pa dziko lonse lapansi, ngakhale chiweruzo chachikulu ndi tsoka. Pakuti machimo a dziko lapansi ndi fungo loipa m'mphuno mwanga, ndipo Ine, ngakhale Yahweh, ndidzayeretsa dziko lapansi, ndipo ndidzawononga chinthu chilichonse choipa; ndipo mwana wanga, amene ndikumutumiza, adzalamulira m'chilungamo pa dziko lapansi, ndipo mtendere waukulu udzakhala kwa okhulupirika amene ndawateteza ku tsiku loipa. Ndipo mtendere waukulu ndidzapereka kwa onse amene aitanira pa dzina la Yesu Khristu, ngakhale Yeshua HaMashiach, pakuti onse amene aitanira pa dzina la Ambuye adzapulumutsidwa. Ndipo mu mtendere ndi chitetezo ndidzawatsogolera, ndipo m'chilungamo ndidzawaveka, ndipo m'chiyero chenicheni adzandilemekeza; pakuti ndawalemekeza, ndipo adzakhala ndi moyo watsopano.

Choncho ndikubweretsa chilungamo changa ndi mkwiyo wanga kuti ndibwezere amene amachita zoipa ndi njira zawo zonyansa, choncho aliyense amene akundikana ndi kusankha zoipa, akhalebe woipa, ndipo mkwiyo wa Mulungu ukhale pa iye! Ndipo aliyense amene adzakhala wolungama ndi onse amene achoka ku zoipa pamene akuitanira pa dzina la Ambuye adzakhala ndi mphoto yaikulu; choncho alekeni akhale ndi zipatso zamtendere za chilungamo, ndipo adzakhala ndi cholowa chachikulu mwa mwana wanga, Khristu Yesu, amene ali ndi mphoto yawo m'dzanja lake. Choncho aliyense amene adzakhala woipa, akhalebe woipa. Ndipo aliyense amene atembenukira ku chilungamo ndi kuitanira pa dzina la Ambuye, akhalebe wolungama, pakuti tsikulo lidzachilengeza. Taonani, chinthu chimene ndidzakuuzani chisanachitike, pakuti maulosi atsala pang'ono kukwaniritsidwa, ndipo sipadzakhalanso kuchedwa. Pakuti nthawi za chisomo ndi chifundo zikufika kumapeto, ndipo nthawi ya mkwiyo waukulu yatsala pang'ono kumasulidwa pa dziko lapansi, ndipo sipanakhalepo nthawi ngati iyi pa dziko lapansi, ngakhale m'masiku a Nowa, ndipo sipadzakhalanso nthawi yotere. Pakuti Ine, ngakhale Ine, ndalumbira pa dzina langa ndipo ndalengeza zinthu izi. Chilichonse chimene Ine, ngakhale Yahweh Mulungu wamuyaya, ndilengeza sichingasinthidwe ndipo chidzachitika. Pakuti m'masiku a mngelo wa chisanu ndi chiwiri, pamene adzayamba kulira, ngakhale chinsinsi cha Mulungu chiyenera kutha.

Ndine amene NDINE, choncho auzeni kuti NDINE wamkulu watumiza mawu awa, ngakhale Yahweh, Mulungu wamkulu ndi wamuyaya!

Umboni ndi Zokumana Nazo, ndi William Brooks

Ndipo tsopano kuti timvetsetse bwino mbali ya uthenga uwu pamene Yeshua HaMashiach ananena kuti, "Taonani, inu munadya kabukuko, pakuti kavumbulutsidwa, ndipo kanali kokoma ngati uchi m'makutu anu pamene munali kugona, koma kanali kowawa m'mimba mwanu komwe kunakudutsani m'masomphenya; ndipo taonani, mwadzuka ku tulo twanu." Nthawi ina m'mwezi wathawu kapena kuposerapo, sindikudziwa tsiku lenileni, koma ndinali m'tulo pakati pa usiku, ndipo ndinadzuka m'masomphenya kuchokera ku tulo twanga (kapena linali loto lomveka bwino ndipo ndinadzuka pachiyambi). Mawu anali kuwerenga m'maganizo ndi mzimu wanga, ndipo ndinadziwa kuti anali mawu a Ambuye, ndipo anali kuwerenga m'makutu mwanga mawu omwe sindimawamvetsa chifukwa mawuwo amangoyenda mosalekeza. Koma pamodzi ndi mawuwo panabwera mtendere waukulu womwe unandigwera kotheratu. Mawuwo anali okoma kwambiri m'makutu mwanga, ndipo ndinali pa mpumulo. Kenako mwadzidzidzi, ndinatuluka mu mkhalidwe umenewo ndi vuto lalikulu la asidi m'mimba, mimba yanga inali kupotoka, ndipo ndinadumpha kuchoka pabedi, kutsamwa ndi kukhosomola. Ndinapita pansi ndikumwa madzi ambiri, zomwe zinathandiza pang'ono koma sizinakhazikitse mimba yanga. Ndinali maso kwa kanthawi pambuyo pake, koma sindinkadziwa kuti zonsezi zikutanthauza chiyani.

Ndipo pa umboni, pamene Yahweh akuti, "Mawu a mabingu asanu ndi awiri AKULANKHULA!" Pa Meyi 7, 2024, ndinali ndi tsiku lovuta kwambiri, ndipo ndinabwera kunyumba ndikugona mofulumira. Ndinagona mochedwa usiku wapitawu chifukwa sindinkatha kugona, zomwe zimandichitikira kawirikawiri. Kunali mphepo yamkuntho yoipa ndi mvula yamphamvu pamene ndinagona, koma mphezi ndi mabingu zonse zinali kutali. Ndinagona cha m'ma 7 koloko usiku umenewo, yomwe ndi nthawi yoyambirira kwa ine, ndipo ndinapita mu zomwe ndimaganiza kuti ndi tulo. Sindikudziwa ngati ndinagonadi, koma ndinalowa m'masomphenya kapena loto lomveka bwino, ndipo ndinkadziwa bwino za mphepo yamkuntho yomwe inali kuchitika. Mawu anali kubwera kwa ine kuchokera kumbali zosiyanasiyana, ndipo anali paliponse. Sindikukumbukira zomwe mawu osiyanasiyanawo ankanena, kupatula amodzi. Anali mawu omveka bwino kwambiri, ndipo mawu ena onse anakhala chete. Mawuwo anati, "Mawu a mabingu asanu ndi awiri AKULANKHULA!" Pamene mawu amenewo ananenedwa, ndinadzutsidwa nthawi yomweyo ndi mkokomo waukulu kwambiri wa bingu womwe sindinawumvepo. Ndinakhala pansi pabedi panga, wodabwa kotheratu.

Pamene ndinagawana izi ndi mkazi wanga tsiku limodzi kapena awiri pambuyo pake, tinali pa khonde lathu lakutsogolo. Anandigwira dzanja ndikunditsogolera kuzungulira nyumba kupita kuseri kwa nyumba. Anandiuza kuti iye ndi mwana wathu wamwamuna anali m'chipinda chochezera pamene bingu limenelo linagunda. Kuwala kwa mphezi kunawunikira ndikugwedeza chipinda chochezera. Mwachidziwikire, inagunda pafupi kwambiri ndi kuseri kwa nyumba yathu, komwe ndi kochepa, popeza tikukhala mumzinda. Koma sitinaone kuwonongeka kulikonse. Ndinaonapo zotsatira pamene mphezi igunda mwamphamvu dziko lapansi kapena chinachake pa dziko lapansi, ndipo imasiya chiwonongeko pamlingo wina kapena wina. Anaona kuwala kwa mphezi, ndipo tonse tinamva mkokomo waukulu kwambiri wa bingu, koma palibe kuwonongeka kulikonse komwe kunapezeka.


Juni 12, 2024

Ambuye wandivumbulutsira ndendende zomwe ntchito iyi ili, ndipo ili ndi mutu wotere. Ndalandira zomwe zili mu Kabuku komwe kali m'manja mwa Mngelo wamphamvu mu Chivumbulutso 10, amene wavala mtambo ndipo utawaleza unali pamutu pake ndipo nkhope yake inali ngati dzuwa ndipo mapazi ake ngati zipilala zamoto. Izi zikuyimira mibadwo ndi mapangano ofanana, omwe onse amafika pachimake pamaso pa Yesu Khristu, wobadwa yekha wa Yahweh, ndi chifaniziro cha Mulungu wamuyaya.

Utawaleza ndi chizindikiro choperekedwa pa pangano la Nowa lomwe Yahweh anapanga ndi Nowa pambuyo pa chigumula. Mtambo ndi zipilala zamoto zikuyimira pangano la Mulungu ndi Mose. Nkhope ya Yeshua HaMashiach ikuwala ngati dzuwa ndipo ndi pangano latsopano. Mauthenga asanu ndi awiri awa ali ndi chiweruzo pa omwe adagwa ndi ena kuyambira chigumula cha Nowa chisanachitike mpaka kumapeto, mu zomwe zidakali zamtsogolo pofika tsiku lalero.
