Kusintha kwa Okhulupirika mwa Khristu, Kenako Chiwonongeko Chidzidzidzi pa Dziko Lapansi

Taonani! Kusintha kwakukulu ndi kwamphamvu kukuchitika, ndipo kusamutsidwa kwatsala pang'ono kutha, ngakhale pamene dziko lapansi likusinthidwa kuchoka ku Ufumu wa Kumwamba kumene dziko lapansi likulamulidwa pansi pa Chipangano Chatsopano ndipo choipa chikuletsedwa kupita ku Ufumu wa Chinjoka, womwe iwo amaulakalaka. Monga momwe zakhalira m'nthawi zanga zachisomo ndi chifundo pa dziko lapansi zomwe zinayamba ndi tsiku lalikulu la Pentekoste zaka za Yubile zambiri zapitazo, tsopano sizidzakhalanso. Ngakhale kusamutsidwa kuchokera ku Chipangano changa Chatsopano, kupita ku ulamuliro wa Chinjoka, chilombo chochokera kunyanja, ndi chilombo chochokera pansi chomwe chilinso mneneri wawo wonyenga, koma Chipangano changa Chatsopano sichidzathetsedwa chifukwa indetu, indetu, Chipangano changa Chatsopano ndi Chipangano chamuyaya, ngakhale Chipangano chomwe Yahweh wavomereza nthawi zonse ndi mpaka kalekale, Sela.

Taonani! Kusamutsidwa kwa mphamvu kuchokera ku Ufumu wa Kumwamba kupita ku dongosolo lomaliza la chilombo, lomwe silidzakhalanso lina. Taonani! Ine ndine Yeshua HaMashiach ndipo ndikubwera kudzabweretsa uthenga wabwino wamtendere ndi chimwemwe kwa okhulupirika mwa Khristu, ngakhale kuti chimwemwe chanu chikhale chokwanira, ndipo musakhumudwe m'chiyembekezo chanu ndipo musataye chidwi pa chiyembekezo chanu, chomwe chidzawululidwa mwadzidzidzi pamene ndidzawonekera m'mitambo mosayembekezereka tsopano, ndipo mudzatengedwa. Pakuti, ndikubwera kudzachotsa okhulupirika mwa Khristu kupita kumwamba, ndipo ndani angaletse dzanja langa lamphamvu? Ndikuti ndani?!

Chifukwa ndikubwera ndi mphamvu zonse ndi ulamuliro wa mpando wachifumu wa Yahweh mwiniwake ndipo ndikubwera kudzatenga chuma changa chomwe ndinagula ndi magazi anga owombola, ngakhale magazi anga opanda tchimo omwe ndinapereka mofunitsitsa mu mphamvu yayikulu ndi chikondi chosweka chifukwa cha machimo a dziko lapansi. Ngakhale kuti machimo a Adamu akhale pa Yesu wa ku Nazarete, ngakhale iye amene Yohane Mbatizi anamuzindikira ndi Mawu akuti "Taonani! Mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu, amene achotsa machimo a dziko lapansi!"

Choncho zili choncho, kuti indetu, indetu, ndalipira chilango cha Mose kwa onse amene alandira Yesu wa ku Nazarete monga pangano lawo lamtendere pakati pawo ndi Yahweh, chifukwa Adamu ndi mwana woloŵerera amene sanamvere Yahweh ndipo anakhala pansi pa gulu la nkhumba za Satana monga akapolo a tchimo. Komabe, Mawu anga amakhalabe otsimikizika chifukwa iye amene ali kapolo wa tchimo sakhala pansi pa Ufumu wa Satana ngati avomereza ndi kuvomereza Yesu monga Ambuye. Indetu, indetu, kapolo sakhala m'nyumba ya tchimo mpaka kalekale ngati asankha kulandira chipulumutso chamuyaya mwa Yesu Khristu chifukwa palibe dzina lina lomwe lidzaperekedwa kaamba ka chipulumutso cha Adamu, ndipo ogwa alibe chikhululuko.

Komabe, omwe akana kuitana kwanga ndi onse omwe adzisintha okha m'njira za Kanani ndi onse omwe adzalandira chizindikiro cha chilombo adzakhala m'nyumba ya tchimo mpaka kalekale chifukwa omwe amakhala ana a tchimo adzakhala pansi pa mphamvu ya ziphuphu za majini a njoka mpaka kalekale, ndipo onse omwe amachita mwano kutsutsana ndi dzina la Yahweh, lomwe Adamu analengedwa nalo, alibe chikhululuko, ndithudi chomwe chadzipinda chokha sichingawongoleredwe. Choncho, m'chiweruzo changwiro cha Yahweh onse omwe amachita machimo a ogwa ndi kusintha majini awo achita tchimo lakufa, monga momwe ndinalembera Yohane Wovumbulutsa m'makalata ake kwa okhulupirika mwa Khristu. Ndipo onse omwe akana magazi oyera ndi opanda tchimo a Yesu wa ku Nazarete monga chinthu chosayera adzaphatikizana ndi ogwa mu cholowa chawo cha sulufule woyaka moto, chifukwa indetu, indetu chiwombolo chanu chinanditengera mtengo woopsa womwe ndikanalipiranso mofunitsitsa, ngakhale chinthu choterocho sichikufunikanso mpaka kalekale.

Chifukwa chiyani mwakhumudwa, okondedwa anga? Chifukwa chiyani chidwi chanu chikuchepa? Kodi ndingafunse kuti ndi mkwatibwi uti amene amakhala wachisoni, ngakhale pamene mkwati akuyandikira? Ngakhale mkwati wanu walengeza kuyandikira kwa kubwera kwake, koma ambiri a inu muli achisoni mumtima ngati kuti ndakukanani? Komabe, zili choncho kuti omwe ali pafupi kwambiri mu nthawi ya kusonkhana kwakukulu kwa okhulupirika mwa Khristu mwanjira ina adzilola okha kutaya chisangalalo chawo, ngakhale pomvera zifukwa zopanda chikhulupiriro za aneneri ndi atumiki onyenga, ndi kulola mivi yamoto kukhala m'maganizo mwawo yomwe ingagonjetse chikhulupiriro chawo ngati isanazimitsidwe ndi Mawu anga ndi mzimu! Pakuti Mawu anga, ndi mzimu, ndipo ndi moyo kwa omwe amayenda mwachikhulupiriro! Choncho, musadzilole kuyiwala kuti pali korona wachilungamo wosungidwa kwa onse omwe amakonda kuwonekera kwanga, koma musataye akorona anu okondedwa anga, chifukwa Satana akufuna kukulandani cholowa chanu.

Chifukwa chiyani mkwatibwi wanga ali ndi nkhope yachisoni ndi kukhumudwa, ngati kuti wasiyidwa ndi mkwati wosakhulupirika? Kodi, Ine Khristu Yesu, ndakupatsanipo Mawu osakhulupirika, kapena ndakunamizanipo, okondedwa anga? Ayi, ndithudi Ine ndine Ambuye Wachilungamo Wokhulupirika Nthawi Zonse ndi Wamuyaya yemwe nthawi zonse amakhala inde ndi ameni chifukwa ndine amene malonjezo onse a Yahweh ndi Yeshuah HaMashiach amakwaniritsidwa mpaka kalekale. Choncho, mvetsetsani chikhalidwe cha kusintha komwe kwatsala pang'ono kukwaniritsidwa mwa inu, ngakhale inu omwe ndikulankhula nanu, ngakhale okhulupirika mwa Khristu! Ngakhale mpingo wanga, thupi langa, ndi ngakhale Mkwatibwi wanga, kwa omwe ndikutumiza Mawu otonthoza awa! Choncho kondwerani!

Ambiri a inu mumawona kubadwa kwatsopano ngati mphamvu pa machimo anu, ndipo ndithudi ndikukupatsani mphamvu pa machimo anu kuti mukhale opambana ndi kugonjetsa mu zinthu zonse, komabe kubadwa kwatsopano ndi koposa kugonjetsa machimo. Ngakhale ndasindikiza okhulupirika mwa Khristu, ngakhale chidindo chosweka cha Yahweh mwiniwake, ngakhale ndi mzimu wa umwana womwe pawokha si chidzalo cha kubadwa kwatsopano, koma malipiro oyamba a chuma chogulidwa cha chiwombolo chawo chathunthu chomwe chikubwera ndi cholonjezedwa. Monga momwe kwalembedwa, Mwa iyenso inu munakhulupirira, mutamva mawu a choonadi, ngakhale Uthenga Wabwino wa chipulumutso chanu, m'menenso mutakhulupirira, munasindikizidwa ndi mzimu woyera wolonjezedwa, Womwe ndi chikole cha cholowa chathu, kaamba ka chiwombolo cha ufulu wogulidwa ku chitamando cha ulemerero wake.

Choncho, chidindo cha Mulungu wamoyo chimakhalabe chotsimikizika ndi chokhazikika pa onse omwe ndawasindikiza, ndipo mukhoza kudzitcha obadwanso, ndipo ndithudi ndakupatsani mphamvu ndi chikhalidwe changa chatsopano kuti muyende mwachilungamo ndi mowongoka mu kumvera konse kwa chikhulupiriro pamene mukuyenda ndi mzimu, chifukwa wolungama adzakhala ndi moyo ndi chikhulupiriro! Choncho, mvetsetsani kuti ngakhale muli ndi chikhulupiriro chonse, ndi mawonetseredwe onse a mphamvu, ndi ngakhale mphatso zonse ndi zopatsa mphamvu zimaperekedwa mu mzimu wa umwana womwe munalandira pamene munasindikizidwa, mphamvu yonse ndi ukulu wa kubadwa kwatsopano sizidzakwaniritsidwa mpaka mwadzidzidzi pa dziko lapansi mulibe!

Chifukwa, ndikubwera kudzachotsa inu kupita kumwamba, ngakhale komwe mudzadabwa ndi ulemerero womwe mudzalengedwanso ndi kuvekedwa. Mudzaima m'chipinda chachifumu cha Yahweh komwe ndidzawonetsa Kuwala kwa Dziko Lapansi kwa Atate wa Kuwala ndipo mudzawona ndipo mudzadziwa ndipo mudzamva ndipo mudzamvetsetsa. Ndipo pamene mukuima olemekezedwa mu ulemerero waukulu pamaso pa mpando wachifumu wa ulemerero wamuyaya, mudzawala ngati nyenyezi m'mwezi wakumwamba, ndipo mudzakhala ndi chikhalidwe chowala. Ndipo mngelo adzasindikiza 144,000 anga pamphumi pawo ndipo mudzakhala ndi zopatsa mphamvu, maluso, ndi mphamvu zambiri monga momwe kukhulupirika kwanu kwakhalira. Chifukwa mu mphindi imeneyo nonse mudzakhala mbali ya Umulungu wamuyaya mpaka kalekale, osachokanso. Sela.

Ndipo pa nthawi imeneyo pamene kusonkhana pamodzi kwa okhulupirika mwa Khristu kwa Ambuye wawo Wachilungamo Wamuyaya kumachitika mwadzidzidzi ndi mosayembekezereka tsopano, ngakhale onse omwe amamva kulira kwa lipenga lomaliza, ndi omwe amamva Mawu akuti "Bwerani kuno" adzadzipeza mwadzidzidzi ndi mosayembekezereka obadwanso ngakhale monga chilengedwe chatsopano ndi changwiro ndi chopanda tchimo ndi cholemekezedwa, chopangidwa ngakhale pambuyo pa Ambuye wanu Wachilungamo Wamuyaya; chifukwa Ine ndine Adamu Womaliza yemwe wapangidwa kukhala mzimu wopatsa moyo ndipo chipulumutso chanu chathunthu chili pakhomo panu.

Choncho, ndikubwera kudzakuuzani kachiwiri kuti mudzikonzekeretse ndikukhala ndi chiyembekezo chanu chonse cha chiwombolo chathunthu patsogolo pa maganizo ndi mitima yanu, chifukwa ndithudi nthawi yanu yafika. Choncho, dzikonzekeretseni pochoka ku machimo onse ndi mawonekedwe onse a choipa. Pitirizani kutuluka m'dziko lodzaza ndi tchimo ndi loweruzidwa kuti musatenge nawo mbali pa miliri ndi ziweruzo zawo ngakhale pang'ono.

Vomerezani machimo anu kwa Ine, Khristu Yesu, ndipo ndifunseni za miyoyo yanu kuti ndikuwonetseni machimo ndi zofooka zanu zomwe mwina simukuzidziwa, ndipo ngakhale chikondi cha Yahweh chimaphimba machimo ambiri chifukwa n'zosatheka kuti muwavomereze onse. Konzani zochitika zanu ndikumvetsetsa kuti moyo wanu pa dziko lapansi m'thupi lochimwa watha ndipo simudzakhalanso ndi mwayi wotembereredwa wotsatira chikhalidwe cha tchimo chomwe chimafuna chidwi chanu ndi kumvera mu chosalungama chonse, koma mudzabadwanso, kubadwa kuchokera kumwamba, ngakhale kubadwa wamoyo watsopano mpaka kalekale osachimwanso ndipo osafanso, ndipo ndine Khristu Yesu amene akulengeza choncho ndipo mudzavekedwa chilungamo chathunthu, omasuka ku ziphuphu za tchimo mpaka kalekale.

Ine ndine Khristu Yesu, ndipo ndatumiza Mawu awa kudzera mwa mthenga ndi mtumiki wanga ndipo kudzera mwa mthenga ndi mtumiki wanga Mawu awa atumizidwa, ngakhale kuti mpingo wanga, thupi langa, ndi ngakhale mkwatibwi wanga adzaima chilili mu chiyembekezo chachikulu ndi chosangalatsa cha chiwombolo chawo chathunthu chomwe chatsala pang'ono kusweka pa dziko lapansi ngakhale monga mphezi imatuluka kum'mawa ndikupita kumadzulo ndipo nonse mudzasinthidwa m'kuphethira kwa diso!

Kenako zidzachitika, ngakhale monga zinanenedweratu, kuti chiwonongeko chidzidzidzi chidzagwa pa Babulo Wachinsinsi ndipo magombe ake adzaphimbidwa, adzagawidwa pakati kuchokera ku gombe kupita ku nyanja, moto waukulu udzagwa ndipo mizinga yawo idzayambitsidwa, ngakhale Mngelo wanga wamphamvu adzaponya mwala waukulu m'nyanja ndipo mzinda waukulu kwambiri wa Babulo Wachinsinsi sudzapulumuka, kapena mzinda wa Angelo chifukwa indetu, indetu, mizinda yake yochimwa idzayaka ndikugonjetsedwa. Magulu ankhondo a chilombo adzamasulidwa ndipo adzasaka munthu aliyense ndipo adzapha makamu, ndipo ali ndi malamulo othetsera onse omwe sakugwirizana ndi zolinga zawo, kapena omwe angauke kuti amenyane nawo tsiku lina. Choncho, nkhondo yawo yachitatu yapadziko lonse idzachitika.

Kugwedezeka kwakukulu ndi nkhondo yonse zidzawononga mayiko ambiri ndi mizinda ndi matauni ndipo dziko lapansi lidzagawidwa m'malo ambiri, ndipo woletsa adzachotsedwa mwadzidzidzi, ndipo madzi oundana adzagwa, ndipo zonyansa zawo zidzamasulidwa ndipo phompho lidzatsegulidwa ndipo ufumu wonse wa Satana udzamasulidwa ndi kugwirizana mu choipa mu nkhondo yawo yolimbana ndi oyera mtima komwe adzawagonjetsa ngakhale monga kwalembedwa. Ndipo chiweruzo chachikulu chidzagwa mwadzidzidzi, ngakhale monga machenjezo, ndi kulengezedwa ndipo zidzakhala choncho kuti chiweruzo chachikulu chidzagwa pa dziko lonse lapansi pamene kusamutsidwa kwatha. Ndipo izi chifukwa munthu wachibadwa amalakalaka ogwa ndipo amalakalaka choipa ndi mdima wawo, choncho, choipa ndi mdima tsopano munthu wachibadwa adzakhala nazo ndipo kuthawa kwake kokha ndikuitana pa dzina la Yesu mpaka mapeto ake, chifukwa opanduka a Adamu tsopano adzaperekedwa kwathunthu kwa Satana omwe adzawononga. Komabe, ndidzabwera kudzapulumutsa otsalira amphamvu kuchokera kwa Yakobo m'manja mwa 144,000 anga amphamvu! Sela!

Ine ndine, Khristu Yesu, ndipo ndalengeza kale kusonkhana kwa okhulupirika mwa Khristu kwa Ambuye wawo Wamuyaya ndi Wosatha, ngakhale mu Uthenga Wabwino wa Chipulumutso ndi Chinsinsi cha Mulungu ndi Uthenga Wabwino Wamuyaya ndipo ndikubwera kudzakhazikitsa chitsimikizo cha zolengeza zanga komanso zolengeza za Yahweh, pakuti zolengeza zilizonse zomwe zimapangidwa mu mpando wachifumu ndi mphamvu ya Yahweh zidzakwaniritsidwa ndendende monga zalengezedwa ndipo sipadzakhalanso kuchedwa.

Ine ndine Ambuye wanu Wachilungamo Wamuyaya, ndipo ndikubwera kudzadzudzula onse omwe ndimawakonda, chifukwa sikoyenera kuti aliyense wa okhulupirika mwa Khristu, ngakhale Mkwatibwi wanga yemwe wagonjetsa zinthu zonse ngakhale monga ndinagonjetsera akhale wokhumudwa ngati kuti chiyembekezo chanu cha chiwombolo mu kubadwa kwatsopano kwathunthu kumwamba kwa kumwamba kwayimitsidwa kapena kuchedwa kapena kuti mwina Ambuye wanu adachedwa panjira mu kusakhulupirika. Mulungu aletse chinthu chosatheka chomwe sichidzachitika!

Pakuti zinthu zoterezi n'zosatheka, ngakhale Satana angafune kugwetsa chikhulupiriro chanu ndi mivi yake yamoto, choncho, zimitsani mivi yake ndi chishango cha chikhulupiriro, (ndi ngakhale zopatsa mphamvu zanga zonse zomwe ndakupatsani) ndipo imani mu chiyembekezo chachikulu ndi chosangalatsa cha kuwonekera kwanga. Musadandaule za okhazikitsa masiku ndi mawu ambiri omwe amalira ndi kung'ung'udza, koma yang'anani kwa Ambuye wanu wa Ambuye ndi Mfumu ya Mafumu yemwe akuyandikira ndipo adzachita ndendende monga kwalengezedwa mu mpando wachifumu wa Yahweh.

Ndipo choncho ndikufunsa, ndani angaletse dzanja langa lamphamvu kuchotsa omwe ndimawakonda ndi kuwasindikiza ndi mzimu woyera wolonjezedwa kuti mubweretse kufanana kumwamba ndi kubweretsa chilungamo pa dziko lapansi pamene mubwera kudzalamulira ndi Mfumu yanu ya Mafumu ndi Ambuye wa Ambuye, ngakhale ulamuliro wamphamvu pansi pa Mkulu wa Ansembe mpaka kalekale pambuyo pa Dongosolo la Melikizedeki? Sela!

Taonani, ndili pafupi. Choncho, dikirani mu chiyembekezo chonse chachikulu chifukwa okhulupirika mwa Khristu sadzasiyidwa ku moto wa woyenga mu kukhumudwa kwakukulu. Indetu, mwagonjetsa. Sela.
