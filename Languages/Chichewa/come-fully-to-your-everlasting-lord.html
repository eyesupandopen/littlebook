Bwerani Kwathunthu kwa Ambuye Wanu Wamuyaya!

Imvani, imvani! Imvani mawu a Ambuye wanu ndipo imvani mawu a Yeshua HaMashiach ndipo weramitsani mitima yanu pomvera chifuniro cha Ambuye wanu ndikulola mawu anga kutonthoza miyoyo yanu. Choncho ndikukufunsani funso? Kodi mukumvetsera? Pamene Mwana wa Munthu akubwera, kodi ndidzapeza chikhulupiriro pansi pano? Zoonadi, zoonadi, ndidzapeza chikhulupiriro pakati pa okhulupirika anga amene ndidzawatengera kuchitetezo cha kumwamba chifukwa cha kukhulupirika kwawo, koma unyinji wa anthu pansi pano alibe chikhulupiriro. Ndipo choncho ndikupitiriza kukuchenjezani okondedwa anga kuti muchoke ku machimo a ochimwa, ndikutuluka m'njira zawo ndikukhala osiyana, kuti mungatenge nawo mbali m'maweruzo awo.

Komabe, zili choncho, kuti anthu ambiri amayandikira kwa ine mwachiphamaso ndi milomo yawo pamene mitima yawo ili kutali ndi ine. Pa tsiku limenelo ambiri adzati kwa ine Ambuye Ambuye, ndipo ndidzati kwa iwo, chokani kwa ine sindinakudziweni konse! Mvetsetsani kuti sikokwanira kunditchula Ambuye kuchokera mumtima wopanda chikhulupiriro, koma amene amandipanga kukhala Ambuye weniweni ndi amene amalapa ndikusiya machimo awo ndikufunafuna kukhala mwachilungamo pamaso panga. Kodi simukudziwa kuti Ine, Yesu wa ku Nazarete, ndingakonde kukhala ndi mtima wanu wonse m'chikondi ndi kudzipereka m'malo mwa nsembe za milomo yanu? Inde, ndikufuna chifundo osati nsembe, pakuti mwa zina ndi kudzera m'chifundo changa, kudziletsa ku mkwiyo woyenera, kuti chikondi changa chimaonetsedwa, ngakhale chikondi cha Yehova Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya! Komabe mawu anga achenjezo amamvedwa ndi amene amandikonda ndipo ayankha mwa kulapa ku chikondi changa pa iwo monga momwe ndidadziwululira kwa iwo kuti apulumutsidwe. Ndipo zili choncho, chikondi changa chimalandiridwa pakati pa amene mitima yawo ili yofewa ku mawu anga ndipo, ndithudi, kwa onse amene amandikonda ndipo amene ndawalamula kuti aphunzire za ine ngakhale amene ndimawatsogolera m'njira zanga zachipulumutso.

Chifukwa chiyani mukulola mitima yanu kuvutika, tiana tanga? Kodi atumwi ndi aneneri anga sanachenjeze za tsiku lino kuyambira m'bandakucha wa nthawi? Kodi aneneri akale sananeneratu za sabata la makumi asanu ndi awiri la Danieli likubwera m'chiweruzo chachikulu pansi pano? Choncho, mvetsetsani, mukukhala m'masiku omwe sautso ya Yakobo isanagwe kwathunthu pansi pano ndipo m'masiku ano masoka ambiri, zizindikiro, zozizwitsa, zodabwitsa, ngakhale maweruzo zinanenedweratu ndipo tsopano zatulutsidwa, nanga chifukwa chiyani mukudabwa? Izi ndi machenjezo omaliza pamlingo waung'ono wa zomwe zikubwera pa dziko lonse lapansi pamene okhulupirika anga adzachoka kupita kwa ine kumwamba kuchokera pansi pano pamene ndikuwasonkhanitsa pamodzi ngati ana a ng'ombe a m'khola, ndipo adzapeza chitetezo chokwanira ndi kukwaniritsidwa pamene akuima mu mphindi zawo zoyamba atabadwanso mu umuyaya wonse, kumene sadzawonongeka, kapena kuvutika, kapena kupirira matenda, koma adzakhala ndi chimwemwe changwiro ndi mgwirizano wangwiro ndi Yehova, ndipo ndithudi ine Yeshua HaMashiach ndipo mphoto yaikulu ndi cholowa ndidzawapatsa, pakuti ndili ndi mphoto yawo m'dzanja langa lamphamvu.

Okhulupirika anga ambiri afufuma ndi ziphunzitso zonyenga zochokera ku uthenga wabwino wa chitukuko womwe umalalikidwa molakwika ndi aneneri ndi aphunzitsi onyenga omwe amanamiza anthu anga, kuwauza kuti tsoka silidzawakhudza malinga ngati akuganiza malingaliro osangalatsa. Zitsime zopanda madzi izi zimalalikira Yesu wina ndipo sandidziwa ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndipo sindinawatume. Nthawi zonse yerekezerani mawu a amene amati akulankhula m'malo mwanga ndi malemba ndipo pamene akutsutsana ndi malemba ndi pamene akulankhula kuchokera m'mitima mwawo chifukwa cha phindu lawo laumbombo.

Zoonadi, zoonadi pali atumiki ambiri onyenga omwe afesa mabodza pakati pa mipingo yanga ndipo uthenga wabwino wonyenga wa chitukuko umatsogolera ku njira yotakata ya chiwonongeko kumene zipata za gehena zidzatseguka kwambiri kwa amene amatsatira ku nyanja yamoto yoyaka nthawi zonse yoyaka malawi otentha kwambiri a buluu wa cobalt okonzedwera ogwa ndi amene amawatsatira, choncho musagwe m'mabodza awo! Sindikulankhula za aneneri anga okhulupirika omwe mwina angalakwitse mawu m'malo mwanga, kapena amene amapanga cholakwa choona mtima ndikudzikonza okha. Mvetsetsani kuti palibe mneneri wanga amene amanenera mokwanira, koma aneneri anga onse amanenera mwa mbali ndipo sadzatsutsana ndi malemba pamene akulankhula moona m'malo mwanga, Khristu Yesu, chifukwa ine ndine mawu a Mulungu wopangidwa thupi amene amakhala pakati panu, ngakhale mwa thupi sindidziwikanso.

Tiana tanga musanyengedwe chifukwa nthawi zonse m'dziko lino muli chizunzo ndi masautso, makamaka tsopano kumapeto kwa ufumu wa Satana pamene ogwa akubwera m'chiweruzo ndipo pamene akukonzekera kuchita zomwe aloledwa kukwaniritsa m'chiweruzo chawo chifukwa ngakhale adani a Yehova amamvera mawu ake. Kodi simunawerenge mu Aroma kumene ndinalangiza Paulo m'njira zanga? Ndani adzatisiyanitsa ndi chikondi cha Khristu? Kodi ndi chisautso, kapena kupsinjika, kapena chizunzo, kapena njala, kapena umaliseche, kapena zoopsa, kapena lupanga? (Monga kwalembedwa, chifukwa cha inu tikuphedwa tsiku lonse, tikuwerengedwa ngati nkhosa zokaphedwa.) Iyai, m'zinthu zonsezi ndife opambana, mwa iye amene anatikonda. Pakuti ndatsimikiza mtima, kuti ngakhale imfa, kapena moyo, kapena angelo, kapena maufumu, kapena mphamvu, kapena zinthu zilipo, kapena zinthu zikubwera, kapena utali, kapena kuya, kapena cholengedwa china chilichonse, sichidzatha kutisiyanitsa ndi chikondi cha Mulungu, chomwe chili mwa Khristu Yesu Ambuye wathu.

Ndipo choncho osankhidwa anga nthawi zonse avulala zinthu zambiri chifukwa cha dzina langa ndipo kwakhala khamu lalikulu la ofera chikhulupiriro m'nthawi yanga yachisomo omwe apereka miyoyo yawo potumikira popititsa patsogolo uthenga wabwino wachipulumutso, ngakhale uthenga wabwino womwewo womwe wapulumutsa miyoyo yanu mwa chikhulupiriro mwa Yesu wa ku Nazarete chifukwa mwa zina zinali kudzera mu ntchito ya ofera chikhulupiriro anga uthenga wabwino wachipulumutso unadzaza dziko lapansi. Komabe, ambiri a inu mwafufumitsidwa ndi chotupitsa cha ziphunzitso zonyenga zosiyanasiyana za tchulani ndikuchitenga zomwe zadzaza Mpingo wanga wa Laodikaya.

Ziphunzitso zoipazi sizochokera kwa ine ndipo malingaliro anu sakwaniritsa chilichonse kunja kwa inu nokha pokhapokha mutalankhula mawu anga pa milomo yachikhulupiriro pamene ndipereka zizindikiro, zozizwitsa, ndi zodabwitsa kuti zichitidwe ndi manja anu. Choncho simungathe kudziganizira nokha kutuluka m'masautso ndi chizunzo, kapena oitanidwa anga, osankhidwa ndi okhulupirika sakhululukidwa ku tsoka. Kodi ndi kuti m'Mawu a Mulungu pamene pamati aliyense amene amakhulupirira mwa ine sadzavutika ndi chisautso, kapena kupsinjika, kapena chizunzo, kapena njala, kapena umaliseche, kapena zoopsa, kapena lupanga? M'malo mwake zina kapena zonsezi zikutsimikiziridwa kwa onse amene amakhala mokhulupirika kwa ine, ndipo choncho ndikukulangizani kuti mukane mawu awo chifukwa mawu awo ndi nthano zopeka mwanzeru zotumizidwa ndi ogwa kuti agwetse chikhulupiriro chenicheni cha osankhidwa ngati chinthu choterocho chingatheke. Komabe, mvetsetsani kuti okhulupirika anga onse amene amanditsatira ndi mtima wawo wonse apulumutsidwa ku mkwiyo wa Mwanawankhosa ndi mkwiyo wa Mulungu ndipo adzatengedwa kupita kuchitetezo m'chipinda chachifumu cha Yehova chiweruzo chisanagwe kwathunthu pansi pano.

Ndipo zili choncho ana anga kuti ambiri a inu mudzavutika ndi masoka osiyanasiyana ndi zizunzo zobweretsedwa ndi ogwa ndipo ambiri adzataya phindu la dziko lino m'mapeto ano ndisanasonkhanitse okhulupirika anga. Ndi sekondi iliyonse yomwe ikudutsa, kuonekera kwanga kuli pafupi kwambiri. Choncho ndikukulimbikitsani, okondedwa anga, kuti mundiimire ndikugwiritsitsa chikondi changa monga mphamvu yanu yolimbikitsa kupirira zomwe zikufunika kwa inu kuti mubweretse chipulumutso kwa otayika pamene mukuwatsogolera mu Chingalawa tsoka lisanagwe.

Mvetsetsani zomwe ndinapatsa Paulo kuti alembere Akolose: Tsopano ndikukondwera m'masautso anga omwe ndikuvutika chifukwa cha inu, ndikukwaniritsa zomwe zatsala za zowawa za Khristu m'thupi langa chifukwa cha thupi lake, lomwe ndi mpingo, womwe ndapangidwa mtumiki malinga ndi lamulo la Mulungu, lamulo lomwe linapatsidwa kwa ine kwa inu, kukwaniritsa mawu a Mulungu. Zili choncho, okondedwa anga, kuti pamene mukuvutika chifukwa cha dzina langa, ndiye kuti mukupitiriza ndikutenga nawo mbali m'masautso anga omwe ndinapirira pa Kalvare. Ngakhale, sindinasiye chilichonse chosachitidwa m'chipulumutso chanu, ndipo ndine Paskha wathunthu ndi wangwiro nthawi zonse, ndaitana aliyense wa inu kuti apirire zinthu zambiri chifukwa cha dzina langa, choncho ziweni ngati chimwemwe chonse! Ndipo dziwani ana anga kuti diso silinaone, kapena khutu kumva, kapena kulowa m'mitima ya anthu zinthu zomwe Mulungu wakonzera amene amamukonda. Choncho thamangani, thamangani kuti mukapeze! Pakuti Mulungu sali wosalungama, kuti aiwale ntchito yanu, ndi ntchito yachikondi, yomwe munasonyeza kudzina lake, poti mwatumikira Oyera mtima, ndipo mukuwatumikirabe.

Ambiri a inu omwe mwasocheretsedwa m'ziyembekezo zanu moti simukuzindikiranso kuti nthawi zambiri oitanidwa anga, osankhidwa, ndi okhulupirika mofunitsitsa amapirira zovuta, manyazi, kutaya, ndi zina zambiri ndipo zotsatira zomaliza za kupirira kwawo m'masautso a Khristu ndi chipulumutso kwa otayika ndi mphoto yaikulu ndi malipiro kwa amene amadzipereka okha m'njira yotere. Kodi simunawerenge mu Machitidwe kumene Paulo ndi Sila anapirira zovuta zambiri ku Makedoniya, kumenyedwa koopsa ndi kuponyedwa m'ndende mopanda chilungamo ndikumangidwa m'matangadza ku Filipi?

Komabe ndinatumiza chivomezi kuti chiwalanditse m'maunyolo awo, ndipo choncho woyang'anira ndende wa ku Filipi ndi banja lake anapeza chipulumutso kudzera mu kupirira kwa Paulo ndi Sila. Ndipo kwa ambiri a amene amanditsatira, adzapirira zovuta ndi chizunzo ndi chisautso. Koma m'zinthu zonsezi ndinu opambana ndipo ndimakumenyerani nkhondo ana anga, ngati mungokhala omvera ku mawu anga ndikutsatira chifuniro changa ndi mtima wanu wonse ndipo ndidzakupangitsani kugonjetsa zinthu zonse ngati mungokhala okhulupirika ku mawu anga ndikundilola kutsogolera mapazi anu, pakuti chipulumutso chanu chili mu kumvera mpulumutsi wanu wamuyaya.

Ndipo choncho ndikukulangizani, okondedwa anga, kuti mundiimire ndikupereka chikondi changa ku dziko lomwe likufa ndi loweruzidwa lomwe lili m'maunyolo pamene chiweruzo chatsala pang'ono kugwa kwathunthu. Kodi mudzandilola kukuthandizani kugonjetsa kukhumudwa kuti musunge chiyembekezo chanu m'malingaliro? Kodi mwayiwala mawu anga, okondedwa anga? Ndipo tsopano kukhala chikhulupiriro, chiyembekezo ndi chikondi, koma chachikulu pa izi ndi chikondi. Ndipo choncho ndikukutumizani kuti mukonde otayika, chifukwa ndi ubwino wa Mulungu womwe umatsogolera anthu ku kulapa nthawi zambiri.

Choncho, nditumikireni m'madera anu, pemphererani amene ataya chilichonse chifukwa pamene akupumabe zikhoza kukhala kuti kutaya kwa dziko lino kudzawapangitsa kupindula umuyaya wonse. Lalikirani kwa ochimwa ndikubweretsa chipulumutso kwa otayika ndikukumbukira kuti ndinu akazembe anga okondedwa anga ndikukwaniritsa kuitana kwanu mwa ine ndikukhala okhazikika m'mapeto a nthawi ino. Choncho ndikukutumizani kuti mukonde anansi anu ndikupereka chakudya ndi thandizo kwa amene akusowa. Ngakhale chakumwa chamadzi kapena mbale yachakudya, perekani chilichonse chomwe mungathe kuthandiza amene akuvutika osakayikira chilichonse. Ndipo ngati mutavutika pamodzi ndi otayika ndiye akondeni kwathunthu ndikuwonetsa umboni wanga ndi mphamvu ndikundilankhulira pa milomo yakhulupiriro kuchokera mumtima wodzaza ndi chikhulupiriro, chiyembekezo, ndi chikondi!

Ndipo choncho ndikukufunsani, kodi mukuopa kuthandiza ochimwa? Kodi simukumvetsetsa kuti pamene ndinatumizidwa osati koma kwa nkhosa zotayika za nyumba ya Isiraeli kuti ndinathera nthawi yanga ndi amisonkho ndi ochimwa? Kodi mwayiwala kuti inunso munali kukhala moyo wa tchimo lozoloŵereka? Kodi mwayiwala kuti ndine munthu yekha wopanda tchimo ngakhale mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu? Choncho awomboledwa a Ambuye anene choncho amene ndawawombola m'dzanja la mdani. Kondani, kondani, kondani mpaka mpweya wanu womaliza pakuti ndi bwino kufa mukutumikira m'chikondi cha Khristu kusiyana ndi kubwerera m'mbuyo mwamantha pa zoyesayesa zomvetsa chisoni za zinthu zofooka ndi zaumphawi, zomwe ndazigonjetsa kwathunthu ndikupereka chigonjetso kwa onse amene amandipanga kukhala Ambuye wawo wamuyaya. Choncho musakonde miyoyo yanu mpaka mapeto ndikutsatira chitsanzo changa cha utumiki wopanda mantha pamaso pa zoipa ndi imfa zomwe zagonjetsedwa kwathunthu ndi chikondi chamuyaya cha Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya, ngakhale El Shaddai!

Musaope, pakuti ndili nanu: musachite mantha, pakuti ndine Mulungu wanu: ndidzakulimbikitsani, ndikukuthandizani, ndipo ndidzakuchirikizani ndi dzanja lamanja la chilungamo changa. Choncho bwerani kwa ine ana anga ndikupumula m'chikondi changa pa inu ndipo ndidzatonthoza miyoyo yanu ndikuchiritsa mabala anu ndipo ndidzakulimbikitsani m'kusweka kwanu kuti mukhale ndi zopereka kwa amene akukusowani pakuti mwatumizidwa choncho. Chifukwa ndi inu akazembe anga omwe simunasankhidwe ndi kuitanidwa ku ulemerero wa Ufumu wanga kokha, komanso kuvutika chifukwa cha dzina langa.

Zoonadi, zoonadi, ndinu ogawana nawo masautso anga, ingotsatirani chitsanzo changa ndipo musakomoke mu ola lanu lakusowa chiyembekezo. Pakuti ndili nanu pa sitepe iliyonse ya njira, ndipo ndine, Ambuye Yesu Khristu amene amayeretsa miyoyo yanu m'moto wanga woyeretsa, ngakhale muli ziwiya zadothi m'manja mwa woumba, moto woyeretsa udzapangitsa nkhope yanu kukhazikitsidwa ngati mwala wamoto kuti mukhale osasweka mu mphamvu yanga komabe muli m'kufooka kwa thupi lanu. Komabe, zili choncho kuti m'kufooka kwa thupi lochimwa chikondi changa chimakhala changwiro mwa inu pamene ndikukukokerani pafupi ndi ine ndikukukonzekeretsani mopitirira zolinga zanu zamuyaya. Choncho, ndi chinthu cholungama kwa owomboledwa kuvutika chifukwa cha dzina langa chifukwa ndinapereka moyo wanga wonse mu nsembe yowawa kuti mukhale ndi moyo pamaso pa Yehova, ngakhale Mulungu wamkulu wamphamvuyonse. Mvetsetsani kuti m'mayesero ndi masautso anu simukubweretsa chisomo changa chopulumutsa kwa amene akusowa kokha komanso mukugwirira ntchito chipulumutso chanu mwa mantha ndi kunjenjemera kuti ndikubweretseni ku mphoto yanu yonse ndipo choncho chiyembekezo chanu chatsala pang'ono kukwaniritsidwa!

Mwina mwamvapo za kupirira kwa Yobu? Momwe Yobu analiri munthu wokhulupirika amene anakumana ndi nthawi zovuta ndipo anaukiridwa mopanda chilungamo ndi Satana. Mwina mwamvapo za mapeto a Yobu chifukwa Yobu sanali munthu wosalungama amene anagonjetsedwa ndi zoipa, koma munthu wolungama amene anapeza chifundo amene anagonjetsa zoipa ndi zabwino potsatira mawu a Yehova ndikukhala m'chilungamo ngakhale pamaso pa chizunzo choipa. Ndipo choncho Yehova anabwezeretsa kwa Yobu kuwirikiza kawiri kwa zonse zomwe zinatengedwa kwa iye mopanda chilungamo, ndipo Yehova analipira Yobu chifukwa cha mavuto ake ndi chizunzo. Taonani, timawayesa odala amene amapirira. Mwamva za kuleza mtima kwa Yobu, ndipo mwadziwa mapeto omwe Ambuye anapanga. Pakuti Ambuye ndi wodzaza ndi chifundo ndi wachifundo.

Ndipo choncho kumatsala chikhulupiriro, chiyembekezo, ndi chikondi, ndipo chachikulu pa izi ndi chikondi. Choncho mvetsetsani okondedwa anga kuti kwa okhulupirika chiyembekezo chatsala pang'ono kukwaniritsidwa kwathunthu pamene mukutengedwa kuchitetezo cha kumwamba kuchokera ku maweruzo akubwera. Imvani mawu anga ndikumvetsetsa chikondi changa pa inu oitanidwa anga, okhulupirika, ndi osankhidwa, ngakhale thupi langa ndi ngakhale mkwatibwi wanga! Choncho, pumulani m'chikondi changa chifukwa ngakhale m'masautso aakulu chikondi changa chimakhala champhamvu ndi chosagonjetseka chifukwa ndine mmodzi mu umodzi ndi cholinga ndi Atate wanga, ndipo ndi chikondi chamuyaya cha Yehova chomwe ndimapereka kwa onse amene ali olowa m'malo a moyo wamuyaya ndi onse amene amabwera kwa Yesu wa ku Nazarete kuti apulumutsidwe.

Mvetsetsani palibe munthu angabwere kwa Yehova pokhapokha kudzera mwa ine, Khristu Yesu. Ndipo zili choncho kuti chikondi cha Yehova ndi chosagonjetseka chifukwa Yehova ndi wosagonjetseka, ndipo choncho ndine wosagonjetseka, pakuti ndagonjetsa. Ndipo ndine Ambuye wamuyaya wachipulumutso, ngakhale iye amene Yehova wamupanga Ambuye wa Ambuye ndi Mfumu ya Mafumu, ndipo wandipatsa dzina lalikulu kuposa dzina lililonse kuti m'dzina la Yesu Khristu bondo lililonse lipinde kumwamba ndi pansi pano ndi pansi pa dziko lapansi. Mvetsetsani kuti ogwa amakuopani okondedwa anga ndipo mantha aakulu agwira mitima yawo chifukwa cha chiweruzo chawo chomwe chikubwera chomwe sichichedwa.

Ndikukulamulani ndikukulimbikitsani, ana anga, kuti mutengere chikondi changa ku dziko lodwalali ndi lomwe likufa ndi loweruzidwa m'nthawi yomaliza ino tsoka lisanagwe kwathunthu. Ndikukuuzani chiritsani odwala, ukani akufa, yeretsani akhate, tulutsani ziwanda pakuti iye amene amakhulupirira mwa ine ntchito zomwe ndinachita pansi pano nayenso adzachita ndipo ntchito zazikulu ndithudi chifukwa ndapita kwa Atate wathu. Yendani m'chikondi chachikulu ndi kudzichepetsa, ndikukumana ndi amene akusowa ndi mphamvu ya Khristu yomwe ndidzapatsa mphamvu mwa inu. Pakuti mwa inu, ziwiya zanga zadothi, mphamvu ya ukulu wa Mulungu mwa Khristu mwa inu imakhala. Lankhulani ku mavuto omwe mukukumana nawo m'chikhulupiriro. Khalani ndi chikhulupiriro mwa Yehova, ndi chikhulupiriro mwa Yeshua HaMashiach ndikunena ku phiri ilo CHOKA NDI KUPONYEDWA M'NYANJA! Ndipo pamene mukuchita choncho mokhulupirika, ndidzapereka zizindikiro zambiri zozizwitsa ndi zodabwitsa kuti zichitidwe m'manja mwa okhulupirika.

Khalani olimba mtima ndi achibindi, pakuti ndine Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda amene amakumenyerani nkhondo. Ndikukupemphani ndikukukakamizani ndi chikondi cha Mulungu chomwe chili mwa Khristu Yesu Ambuye wanu kuti mofunitsitsa mupirire chifukwa cha dzina langa ndikubweretsa otayika kunyumba kwa Atate wathu ngakhale m'nthawi yomaliza ino. Ndikukupemphani osankhidwa anga perekani chitsanzo pakuti ndinu okhulupirika anga ndi osankhidwa omwe ndakuumbani m'masautso kuti m'kufooka ndi kusweka kwa thupi lanu muwonetse mphamvu ya Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya, ngakhale Yehova mwiniwake pamene mukupita mu utumiki ndi ulamuliro waukulu kwa amene ndakutumizani kuti muwatumikire.

Ndili nanu pa sitepe iliyonse ya njira.

Ndine mmodzi mu umodzi ndi cholinga ndi Atate wanga.

Ndine Ambuye wamuyaya wachipulumutso.

Ndine amene Yehova wamupanga Ambuye wa Ambuye ndi Mfumu ya Mafumu.

Ndine Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda amene amakumenyerani nkhondo.

Ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndatumiza mawu awa kudzera mwa mtumiki wanga, ndipo kudzera mwa mtumiki wanga mawu anga atumizidwa chifukwa cha kumvera ndi chilimbikitso cha onse amene apeza mawu awa. Ndipo Mawu anga adzapeza maso, makutu, ndi mitima ya amene atumizidwa. Imani mwamphamvu okondedwa anga pakuti mphoto yanu ili pakhomo panu, koma musakomoke. Pumulani m'chikondi changa ndikupumula m'ntchito zanga, mutasiya zanu ndipo tsopano ndi nthawi ya chiwombolo chanu ndipo tsopano ndi nthawi ya chipulumutso chanu chonse, ndipo ndikubwera kudzakutengani okondedwa anga. Ndimveni ndipo imvani mawu anga, sindidzakusiyani kapena kukutayani okhulupirika anga mwa Khristu, ngakhale onse amene amamvera mawu anga. Yang'anani kumwamba, pakuti kuonekera kwanga kwatsala pang'ono kuphulika kumwamba pa nthawi ndi ola lomwe simukuyembekezera. Ndikubwera kudzakutengani okhulupirika anga, kodi mwakonzeka?
