Awiri Mboni: Israyeli
Zowawa Zotsekemera
Julayi 15, 2024

Chosankha Nchanu!

Mverani mawu a mpulumutsi wanu, Ambuye ndi Mfumu ana anga ndipo tcherani makutu anu ku Mawu a Ambuye wanu, ngakhale Yesu Khristu wolungama, pakuti ndine mwana wa chilungamo ndipo ndikuwuka mu ulemerero wanga, ndipo mwakula ngati ana a ng'ombe a m'khola mu mtendere ndi chitetezo ndipo ndakulerani chifukwa cha cholinga chakumwamba chomwe chatsala pang'ono kuvumbulutsidwa kwathunthu mwa inu, okhulupirika anga ndi osankhidwa. Taonani! Ndine amene ndimayesa mitima ya anthu kuti mudziwe motsimikiza ngati muli m'chikhulupiriro choona, kapena ngati mwasocheretsedwa ndi kutengedwa ndi machimo anu ndi chinyengo champhamvu chamakono chomwe chagwira Dziko lapansi.

Pakuti ndine amene Yahweh wagawana naye mpando wake wachifumu, ngakhale Khristu Yesu Ambuye woukitsidwa ndi wopatsidwa mphamvu, ndipo ndine Mbuye wa Ambuye ndi Mfumu ya Mafumu ndipo taonani Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda ukubwera m'mitambo kudzalandira okhulupirika anga kwa ine kuti atengedwe kumwamba ku ukwati wa Mwanawankhosa, pakuti Mkwatibwi wanga wadzikonzera yekha. Ndipo nyale zake nzodzaza, ndipo wacheka zingwe zake, ndipo akuyembekezera kuonekera kwanga ngati namwali wanzeru, ngakhale omwe ndawayeretsa kwathunthu ndipo alibe cholakwa pamaso pa mpando wachifumu wa Mulungu. Ngakhale omwe sanagwidwe mwachinyengo ndi Baraba, pakuti Baraba amabweretsa chiwembu chachiwawa, nkhondo ndi tsoka, pakuti panopa ndi mutu umodzi pakati pa mitu ingapo yomwe imagwira ntchito mogwirizana, koma adzauka ngati Mesiya wonyenga, ndipo wasankhidwa ndi anthu a m'dzikolo kuti alamulire sunagoge wa Satana. Pakuti iye ndi wolowa m'malo wa amene makolo awo anamusankha, ndipo akudzikonzera okha kuti amulandire ngati Mfumu yawo.

Koma inu, inu Mpingo wa Laodikaya, tsopano yafika nthawi yanu ya chiwonongeko ndi chiweruzo. Pakuti mwadzipezera mpulumutsi wina ngati Mfumu yanu, ngakhale iye amene kubwera kwake kukulengezedwa ndi zizindikiro ndi zozizwitsa zonyenga ndipo amene panopa wakutsogolerani ndi matsenga ndi ufiti. Ndine Yeshua HaMashiach ndipo ndakugulani ndi magazi anga okhululukira machimo, opanda tchimo, ndipo ndine amene ndingakuchiritseni, ndi kukulengani mwatsopano mu mzimu wanga, pakuti ndapangidwa kukhala mzimu wopatsa moyo pakuti momwemo Yahweh wakhazikitsa ulamuliro wonse pa Mwana wake, ngakhale iye amene ali kukwaniritsidwa kwa mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu ndi Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda! Ndipo m'chilungamo ndidzaweruza ndi kuchita nkhondo pakuti mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu ndi Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda akukwaniritsidwa mwa ine, Khristu Yesu, ngakhale Yeshua HaMashiach pakuti ndine amene!

Ndidzamaliza chipulumutso chanu, inu Mpingo wa Laodikaya, koma simunakonzekere kulowa mu Ufumu wanga panthawiyi chifukwa mwasokera pa cholinga chanu chimodzi mwa Khristu! Ndidzakuwonetsani cholakwa chanu, ndipo muli ndi mwayi woti mulape machimo onse, koma mulibe nthawi yotsala yotsutsana ndi mawu a Ambuye wanu, pakuti tchimo lalikululi ndi limodzi mwa ambiri omwe Satana wagwiritsa ntchito kukumangani kuti musabwere kwathunthu kwa ine mukulapa kuti ndikuyeretseni kwathunthu m'magazi anga okhululukira machimo anu.

Taonani! Nthawi za Amitundu zatha ndipo mtengo wa azitona wakuthengo womwe unalumikizidwa pakati pawo, ndipo pamodzi nawo umadya muzu ndi mafuta a mtengo wa Azitona, tsopano udzadulidwa. Pakuti mwanyalanyaza Mawu a Khristu Yesu, pakuti ndinakuuzani momveka bwino kuti musadzitamandire pa nthambi zomwe zinagwa chifukwa chosakhulupirira: koma ngati mudzitamandira, simunyamula muzu, koma muzu umakunyamulani inu. Ndine Muzu wa Jese, ndipo ndine amene ndimayima ngati chizindikiro cha chiyembekezo nthawi zonse, ngakhale iye amene amabweretsa chipulumutso ndi njira za Yahweh padziko lapansi ndipo ndidzalamulira cholowa changa kuchokera kumpando wachifumu wa Davide m'chilungamo ndipo ndagula dziko lapansi m'magazi anga opanda tchimo ndipo ndidzatenga mwamphamvu zomwe zili zanga m'manja mwa olanda, ngakhale omwe adagwa.

Ndine amene ndapereka chisomo ndi chifundo padziko lapansi, pakuti m'badwo wonse wa chisomo unaperekedwa kwa ine ndi Yahweh kuti Amitundu apulumutsidwe. Pakuti zolinga za Yahweh mu Israyeli nthawi zonse zinali zoti Amitundu adzadalitsidwe ndikufika kuchipulumutso kudzera mukumvera kwa Israyeli. Koma m'kupita kwanthawi anakhala onyada, ndi odzitamandira, ndipo anadulidwa mumtengo wa azitona wachilengedwe chifukwa chosakhulupirira. Koma inu, Mpingo wanga wa Laodikaya, ngakhale inu ndinu chitsanzo chenicheni cha kudzikuza komweku, kusakhulupirira, ndi khungu lomwe linawonetsedwa ndi nthambi zachilengedwe zomwe zinadulidwa chifukwa chosakhulupirira, ndipo tsopano mudzadulidwa mumtengo wa azitona wachilengedwe. Ndipo tsopano ndisuntha kuti nditirize ntchito yanga pa mtengo wa azitona wachilengedwe, ngakhale Israyeli m'bale wanga ndi anthu anga, ngakhale onse omwe ndawasankha kuti andivomereze ngati Ambuye wawo kuti ndiwapange kukhala munthu mmodzi watsopano ndipo kuti chinsinsi cha Mulungu chithe, ngakhale pachimake pa ntchito zanga zopulumutsa.

Ndipo inu, Mpingo wanga wa Laodikaya, mwasiya chikondi chanu choyamba ndipo muli m'manja mwa wonyenga amene mwamulumbirira kukhulupirika. Mwadzipezera Baraba watsopano ngakhale ngati Mfumu yanu ndipo mwakana Yesu wa ku Nazarete ngati Ambuye wanu ndipo muli m'manja mwa mpulumutsi wina wonyenga. Ngakhale iye amene amadziyika ngati mpulumutsi wa Babulo wachinsinsi, amene amalankhula ndi kuchita ngati mmodzi wa inu, koma amalankhula ngati chinjoka. Pakuti Babulo wachinsinsi amakhala mokoma komanso m'chuma chambiri, zosangalatsa ndi chuma.

Ndipo Babulo wachinsinsi waledzeretsa dziko lonse lapansi ndi zonyansa za dama lake zomwe zili m'chikho chake chagolide, koma momwemonso njira za omwe adagwa, omwe amapereka zonyansa zoyipitsitsa, ndipo mumazimwa mofunitsitsa komanso mosangalala kwambiri chifukwa mwakhala akhungu ndi chikho chagolide ku zonyansa zomwe mumadya monyansa. Pakuti ngakhale Mpingo wanga wa Laodikaya sukusonkhananso mozungulira malemba anga oyera koma pamisonkhano ya Baraba, amene mumamukhulupirira. Ndipo choncho mumasankha mpulumutsi watsopano chifukwa mumakonda dziko lapansi ndi zinthu za dziko lapansi ndipo simungathe kumvetsa lingaliro loti mudzataya zokoma zanu zamtengo wapatali, kupatula kuti ndidzakuchotserani zosangalatsa zonse mu mkwiyo wanga womwe ukubwera, ndipo ngakhale tsopano kutayika kwanu kukuchulukirachulukira!

Pakuti ndinu akhungu ndi osokonezeka m'kudzikuza kwanu kuti Yahweh angapereke mpulumutsi wina kuposa ine, Yesu wa ku Nazarete, pakuti ndine mbewu yolonjezedwa ya mkazi ndipo Dani ndi mwana wa mkango amene palibe Mesiya adzauka mwa iye. Ndimvereni ndipo ndimvereni bwino, inu mpingo wa Laodikaya, pakuti tsopano ndi kulapa kwanu kuchokera ku cholakwa cha mitima yanu kapena tsopano ndi nthawi yomwe ndidzakusiyirani kumoto wa woyenga ndipo onse amene aitanira pa dzina la Ambuye mpaka mapeto adzapulumutsidwa ndipo ambiri amene anavomereza dzina langa mopanda chikhulupiriro adzagwa m'chiwonongeko, atataya mwayi wawo pa mawonekedwe a umulungu omwe anakana mphamvu yake: Alaodikaya anga, ndine mphamvu yake ndipo mwa ine Yahweh wayika ulamuliro wonse, ndipo mwakukana ine, Ambuye ndi mpulumutsi wanu, ndipo ndidzakusankhani m'kamwa mwanga pokhapokha mutalapa tsopano!

M'nthawi yanga ya chisomo, chipulumutso chinafalikira pakati pa mitundu, ndipo mtundu ndi mtundu unagawana ndi kudya muzu wa mtengo wa azitona wachilengedwe. Ndipo tsopano, mitundu sidzakhalanso chizindikiro cha chipulumutso ndipo nthawi yawo mu Mwana inabwera chifukwa cha kukanidwa kwa m'bale wanga ndi anthu anga pakuti anafuula mosakhulupirira kuti timasulireni Baraba! Koma pamene zinsinsi za Yahweh zinavumbulutsidwa ndi atumwi anga oyera ndi aneneri, ndamugwira Baraba ndipo sindinamumasule kwathunthu, koma ndangolola chinsinsi cha kusayeruzika kupita patsogolo. Ndipo tsopano nthawi yafika yomwe ndidzamasula Baraba kwathunthu pamene wolepheretsa wanga achotsedwa ndipo pamene okhulupirika anga, oitanidwa, ndi osankhidwa atengedwa kumwamba komwe ndawakonzera malo awo okhala kwamuyaya, ndipo adzavekedwa nsalu zabwino zopanda banga zomwe sizingadetse kapena kuipitsidwa.

Kodi ndinu akhungu kwambiri ku dziko lanu, ndi ogontha kwambiri moti simukumvanso mawu anga inu Mpingo wa Laodikaya? Mwakhulupirira mabodza obwera chifukwa cha chinsinsi cha kusayeruzika, ndipo mwalola chotupitsa chapoizoni m'Mabaibulo anu oipitsidwa omwe abweretsa katangale wathunthu m'Mipingo yanu yomwe yaipitsa mitima yanu ndi nthano m'kusakhulupirira kolakwika. Mwalola mabodza a afiti a nthawi kuti akulodzeni ndi malemba onyenga ndi mawu abodza. Simukumvetsanso malemba, ndipo mwadziunjikira mavuto akulu omwe adzakugwerani mwadzidzidzi. Mawu onse a Mulungu ndi oyera ndipo cholinga chake ndi kumvera kwa chikhulupiriro ndi kulimbikitsa olandira. Mvetsetsani Mawu a Mawu anga ndikumvetsetsa nthawi yomwe mukukhala.

Pakuti ndinauza Yohane Wovumbulutsa kulemba kuti: Ndipo ndinaona chirombo chikutuluka m'nyanja, chokhala ndi mitu isanu ndi iwiri, ndi nyanga khumi, ndipo panyanga zake panali akorona khumi, ndipo pamitu yake panali dzina lamwano.

Ndipo kachiwiri Yohane Wovumbulutsa analemba kuti: Ndinaona mkazi atakhala pachirombo chofiira, chodzaza ndi mayina amwano, chokhala ndi mitu isanu ndi iwiri, ndi nyanga khumi. Ndipo mkaziyo anali atavala zofiirira ndi zofiira, ndipo anali atavekedwa golide, ndi miyala yamtengo wapatali ndi ngale, atagwira chikho chagolide m'dzanja lake, chodzaza ndi zonyansa ndi zonyansa za dama lake.

Ndipo kachiwiri: Ndipo nyanga khumi zomwe unaziona pachirombo, izi zidzadana ndi hule, ndipo zidzamusiya wabwinja, ndi wamaliseche, ndipo zidzadya mnofu wake, ndi kumutentha ndi moto. Pakuti Mulungu waika m'mitima yawo kukwaniritsa chifuniro chake, ndi kuvomerezana, ndi kupereka ufumu wawo kwa chirombo, kufikira mawu a Mulungu atakwaniritsidwa. Ndipo mkazi, amene unamuona, ndi Mzinda waukulu umene umalamulira mafumu a dziko lapansi.

Ana anga, mvetsetsani kuti chirombo chikhoza kutanthauza anthu komanso maufumu, phunzirani kuti mudziwe. Chirombo chomwe chimanyamula hule chidzawononga hule ndipo sichidzagonjeranso hule, pakuti walamulira mafumu a dziko lapansi m'dama lake. Choncho chirombo chotuluka m'nyanja chidzatulutsa Mahdi wawo woyembekezeredwa, koma chidzawononganso kwathunthu Babulo Wachinsinsi, pakuti Mulungu wapereka hule lalikulu m'manja mwa adani ake omwe amamuda kale kwambiri.

Chirombo chochokera kunyanja ndi chirombo chochokera pansi zisanauluke kwathunthu mu Ufumu wa chinjoka, pali olamulira ambiri omwe ali ogwirizana pazolinga zopereka mphamvu zawo ndi ulamuliro wawo kwa chinjoka, pakuti Mulungu waika m'mitima yawo kuti akwaniritse. Ndipo adzawononga Babulo wachinsinsi. Kodi mukuganiza kuti mwadziwa bwino malemba anga oyera chifukwa mwakhulupirira akatswiri anu azaumulungu abodza? Mvetsetsani ndi kumva Mawu a Ambuye wanu!

Kachiwiri, ndinauza Yohane Wovumbulutsa kulemba kuti: Ndipo chirombo chomwe ndinachiona, chinali ngati Nyalugwe, ndi mapazi ake ngati chimbalangondo, ndi pakamwa pake ngati pakamwa pa mkango: ndipo chinjoka chinamupatsa mphamvu zake ndi mpando wake wachifumu, ndi ulamuliro waukulu. Ndipo ndinaona umodzi wa mitu yake ngati kuti wavulazidwa mpaka kufa, koma bala lake lakupha linachiritsidwa, ndipo dziko lonse lapansi linadabwa ndikutsatira chirombocho.

Ana anga, mawu a Yahweh ali ndi kuya kwamitundumitundu, komwe palibe munthu angathe kukudziwa bwino. Chirombo chomwe chafotokozedwa ndi ufumu womwe udzauka kuchokera kukuwonongeka kwa hule, ndipo kuchokera ku dongosolo lakale dongosolo latsopano lidzauka! Likuyimira chirombo cha nyanja, pakuti adzauka ngati Mahdi wawo, ndipo likuyimira chikhalidwe cha ufumu womwe adzalamulira nyanja. Chifukwa chiyani simulemekeza mawu a Mawu anga? Sikunena kuti chirombo chochokera kunyanja chokha chinavulazidwa, pakuti ndi munthu uti amene ali ndi mitu isanu ndi iwiri kwenikweni? Koma umodzi wa mitu yake ngati kuti wavulazidwa mpaka kufa. Oh, opusa, ndi ozengereza pamtima kukhulupirira zonse zomwe Aneneri ananena! NGATI KUTI wavulazidwa mpaka kufa. Mawu akuti "ngati kuti" akuyenera kukuimitsani m'njira zanu, koma simukhulupiriranso ngakhale mawu anga ochepa, ndipo ndine Mawu opangidwa thupi. Kodi sizikuwonekerani bwino, NGATI KUTI, ndi mwambi wakale? Kodi sizikuwonekerani bwino kuti "kuti" ndi yosonyeza zokayikitsa? Kodi sizikuwonekerani bwino kuti ndi kufupikitsa kwa, ngati zikanakhala choncho?

Koma ayi, mumakhulupirira mabodza a chinjoka, pakuti Baraba amalankhula ngati chinjoka, koma si Baraba, koma chirombo chochokera pansi. Ndipo tsopano dziko lonse lapansi lidzadabwa pambuyo pa chirombo chifukwa cha zizindikiro zake zonyenga ndi zozizwitsa, ndipo adzachita zizindikiro zonyenga ndi zozizwitsa zambiri, ndipo sanauke kwathunthu pakuti hule silinatenthedwe ndi moto, koma tsopano wavumbulutsidwa mu nthawi yake. Amalankhula ngati chinjoka, nati "Mulungu yekha ndiye anamupulumutsa ku bala lake lamutu" ndipo ili ndi bodza lamwano. Yahweh, Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya, wandituma ine Yesu wa ku Nazarete ngati mpulumutsi wa dziko lapansi. Ine ndi Atate wanga ndife amodzi mu umodzi ndi cholinga ndipo Yahweh sanapulumutse Baraba ndipo inenso, Khristu Yesu sindinatero. Mwasocheretsedwa ndi kutengedwa ndi kukodwa m'chinyengo chachikulu, ndipo ndikukuuzani kuti lapani ndikubwerera ku chikondi chanu choyamba ndipo chitani izi tsopano, pakuti palibe nthawi!

Pakuti ndine amene ndimayesa mitima, ndipo ndine amene ndimayesa onse omwe ndimawaitanira kuchipulumutso, pakuti ndimayeretsa onse pamoto wa woyenga! Taonani! Ndine amene ndimakhala ngati woyenga ndi woyeretsa siliva: ndipo ndimayeretsa ana a Yahweh, ndi kuwayeretsa ngati golide ndi siliva, kuti apereke kwa Ambuye nsembe m'chilungamo. Kodi simukumvetsa kuti ndidzakupangani kukhala ansembe ndi mafumu pamaso pa Atate wanga? Ngakhale mphatso yanga ya mzimu woyera, yomwe mwabadwa nayo mwatsopano ndi mbewu yosawonongeka ndi yangwiro ndipo sifunikira kuyengedwa, mwabadwa kuchokera kwa Adamu ndi magazi ndipo muyenera kulola kwathunthu ntchito yanga kuti ikwaniritsidwe m'miyoyo yanu, pokhapokha ngati mbewu yanga yagwera panthaka yamiyala.

Tsopano yafika nthawi ya chiweruzo ndipo nthawi ya chisomo yatha. Ngakhale ambiri adzanyoza mawu anga ndikuchoka m'kusakhulupirira, zotsatira zake zadziwika kale ndi Yahweh. Pakuti Yahweh nthawi zonse amatsimikizira anthu njira zawo zoyipa ndikudzudzula anthu chifukwa cha tchimo, podziwa m'chikondi chake ndi nzeru zake zamuyaya kuti adzapulumutsa ena. Ndipo monga Yahweh anaumitsa mtima wa Farao, momwemonso adzaumitsa mitima ya ambiri omwe amamva koma samvetsera. Yahweh adzayesa munthu mobwerezabwereza. Ndipo ena adzakhala ngati Farao ndipo adzaumitsa mitima yawo kwambiri m'kusakhulupirira koma ena adzachoka m'njira za Farao ndikuukira ku moyo wosatha mwa Khristu Yesu, komabe kudzera pamoto wa woyenga.

Mvetsetsani kuti omwe asankha Baraba adzadzipanga kukhala ogawana nawo milandu ya Baraba, ndipo omwe akana kuitana pa dzina langa mpaka mapeto onse adzatsutsidwa chifukwa anali ndi chisangalalo m'chinyengo. Pakuti mpulumutsi wanu watsopano si mpulumutsi koma chirombo cholusa. Ndipo dongosolo lake ndi ufumu wake zimapangidwa ndi omwe amapweteka ana. Kodi simukumvetsa njira za Chinjoka? Pakuti omwe amatsatira njira za chinjoka amadya ana. Amapereka ana nsembe kwa Moloki, amafunafuna kasupe wawo wa unyamata kuchokera m'magazi a ana ozunzidwa, amagwiririra ana chifukwa cha kukhutitsidwa kwawo kodwala, ndipo amachita kupha mwana wosabadwa m'mimba kuti aphe ana ang'onoang'ono asanabadwe, ndipo akutsogolerani mwachinsinsi m'njira zawo, pakuti momwemonso njira za atsogoleri a Babulo Wachinsinsi komanso ufumu wa chinjoka. Ndipo tsopano pakubwera chiweruzo cha Mulungu pa zinthu izi ndi zina zambiri. Pakuti mukanakhala kuti simunawone zinthu izi mukanakhala opanda tchimo, koma tsopano mukuwona ndipo tchimo lanu likadali.

Kodi simunawerenge? Choncho, atero Ambuye, Mawu anamveka kumwamba, kulira ndi kulira kowawa. Rakele, akulirira ana ake, anakana kutonthozedwa chifukwa cha ana ake, chifukwa analibeko. Pakuti mwalephera udindo womwe munapatsidwa, ndipo munapatsidwa zinsinsi zanga ndipo munayima ngati kuwala kwa chipulumutso ndi chizindikiro cha chiyembekezo. Koma tsopano mwagwirizana ndi omwe ndinakutumizani kuti muwatsutse. Ndipo tsopano yafika nthawi yomwe ndidzakusiyirani ku tsogolo lanu.

Ndimvereni ndipo ndimvereni bwino nonse omwe mungakane mawu a Yeshua HaMashiach, ngakhale mawu a Khristu Yesu: mulibe nthawi! Ndikukulangizani kuti mundimvere m'chikondi changa kwa inu ana anga ndipo lapani tsopano! Pakuti moto wa woyenga watsala pang'ono kukugwerani kwathunthu, ndipo udzayeretsa dziko la ufumu wonse wa chinjoka ndipo dziko lapansi silidzagonjeranso njira za Satana ndipo ufumu wake sudzaukanso, pakuti ndine Mbuye wa Ambuye ndi Mfumu ya Mafumu, ndipo ndidzawononga kwathunthu omwe adagwa. Onse omwe akana kulapa adzakhala ndi gawo lawo m'nyanja yamoto ndipo adzagawana chilango cha mdierekezi ndi angelo ake m'nyanja yayikulu yamoto wa sulufule. Momwemonso, onse omwe alapa potuluka m'tchimo ndikuitana pa dzina langa mpaka mapeto adzapulumutsidwa ndikupatsidwa mphoto yayikulu yamuyaya mwa ine.

Taonani! Tsopano ndi nthawi yosonkhanitsa okhulupirika, ndipo tsopano ndidzasiya omwe akana kwa Baraba, pakuti apanga chisankho chawo.
