Kwa a 144,000: Kananani Mabodza Awo

Tcherani makutu anu ku mawu anga ndikumva nzeru zanga, pakuti nzeru ikulira mokweza m'misewu: kodi mudzamva mawu anga? Ndine Yeshua HaMashiach, ngakhale mwana wa Yahweh, amene anabwera m'thupi ndipo ndine mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu amene achotsa tchimo la dziko lapansi. Mvetsetsani kuti dziko lomwe mukukhalamo silithunzi lonyenga lomwe aphunzitsi anu a Chijesuti anajambula kuti akusocheretseni. Ambiri a inu mwaphunzitsidwa mabodza a Ajesuti, ngakhale kuphunzira motsutsana ndi kuphunzira. Nkhondo iyi yolimbana ndi choonadi inakonzedwa kuti iumbe maganizo a anthu kuti agonjere Satana ndipo ikuyesetsa kuchotsa ndi kulanda ufulu wosankha wa munthu kuti munthu atsatire mopanda nzeru zimene waphunzitsidwa.

Mbiri yoona singamvetsetsedwe kuchokera ku mabodza a afilosofi a Chijesuti, pakuti ndi abodza omwe akugulitsa chinyengo mu chinyengo chachikulu. Lemba likunena momveka bwino pa nkhaniyi, ndipo Aroma akulengeza choonadi chonenedwa momveka bwino: Pakuti mkwiyo wa Mulungu uwonekera kuchokera kumwamba pa kusapembedza konse ndi chosalungama cha anthu, omwe amalepheretsa choonadi mu chosalungama.

Kodi mwalola mawu amenewo kufika pamtima panu? Anthu amalepheretsa choonadi mwadala mu kusapembedza ndi chosalungama, ndipo mkwiyo wa Yahweh uli pa iwo omwe amatero. Choncho maphunziro anu asandutsidwa abodza ndi mabodza awo.

Achita izi kuti akonze njira ya chinyengo chachikulu chomwe chikubwera, ndikuti ufumu wa Satana ukhazikitsidwe kuti ulamulire mu chinyengo chachikulu ndi mabodza. Mabodza omwe akuuzani ngati maphunziro amatumikira kuchititsa khungu unyinji wa anthu ku choonadi: Palibe alendo ochokera kumlengalenga (aliens), koma angelo ogwa okha omwe akupita kunyanja yamoto kuti akamangidwe ndi kuwotchedwa mpaka kalekale. Musakhulupirire mabodza awo ndipo thawirani kwa ine, Khristu Yesu, pakuti mwa ine muli choonadi chonse chomwe chidzakumasulani. Ndikukuuzani zinthu izi kuti musanyengedwe pamodzi ndi omwe adzagwirizane ndi Satana ndikulandira chizindikiro cha chilombo chomwe chikubwera.

Mbiri imamvetsetsedwa potsatira choonadi chokhazikitsidwa m'Malemba ndi mabuku ena omwe sali m'Baibulo 1 Enoke, Yasheri, Yubile, ndi ena, kuyambira pachiyambi. Pakuti anthu osapembedzawa ndi mabodza omwe amagwiritsa ntchito kubisa choonadi ndi mbali chabe ya nkhondo yaikulu, ngakhale nkhondo ya mbewu pakati pa Yahweh ndi Satana yomwe ikupitirirabe mpaka mphindi ino, koma yapambanidwa mpaka kalekale ndi ntchito zanga zowombola. Pakuti nkhondo inalengezedwa ndi Yahweh pa njoka yakale ija chifukwa cha kupanduka kwake, ngakhale kuyambira pachiyambi cha chilengedwe cha anthu pamene Satana anayesa kuwononga Adamu ndi Hava. Yahweh ananena kwa njoka m'chilengezo chake: Ndidzaika chidani pakati pa iwe ndi mkaziyo, ndi pakati pa mbewu yako ndi mbewu yake: idzaphwanya mutu wako, ndipo iwe udzaluma chidendene chake. Ndipo choncho nkhondo za mbewu zakhala zikuchitika kuyambira pamenepo.

M'Munda wa Edeni, Satana anakwanitsa kuwononga mbewu ya Adamu, kutsutsa Adamu pansi pa mphamvu ndi katangale ka tchimo. (Mwa ichi mwana wa Mulungu anaonekera, kuti awononge ntchito za mdierekezi.) Pakuti Satana anagwiritsa ntchito chidziwitso chake choyipa cha chabwino ndi choyipa kuti awononge kotheratu anthu, ndipo kwa iwo omwe amamvera mawu anga, kuwonongeka kumeneko kumathetsedwa mwa ine, Khristu Yesu, mwa amene inu muli cholengedwa chatsopano chomwe sichingawonongeke. Mbewu yanga singawonongeke, pakuti Yahweh waipanga kukhala yosawonongeka, ndipo kudzera m'kubadwa kwatsopano ndasindikiza osankhidwa anga ndi okhulupirika ngakhale ndi mzimu woyera wa lonjezo!

Yohane m'kalata yake yoyamba akulengeza choonadi chamuyaya: Aliyense wobadwa mwa Mulungu sachita tchimo: pakuti mbewu yake imakhala mwa iye, ndipo sangachimwe, chifukwa wabadwa mwa Mulungu.

M'kanthawi kochepa pambuyo pa kugwa kwa Edeni, pamene Adamu anachimwa ndikugwa kuchoka ku ungwiro kupita ku kuwonongeka, alonda anatsikira pa Phiri la Herimoni m'masiku a Yaredi. Ndipo onse anadzimanga okha ndi matemberero, ngakhale lumbiro lawo losayera lomwe anadzimanga nalo mpaka kalekale ku tchimo ndi chilango chamuyaya. Anachita izi kuti onse atenge akazi mwa onse omwe anawasankha, motero kusintha dongosolo la chilengedwe chawo.

Anabwera kwa anthu kupereka chidziwitso chawo choyipa cha angelo ogwa, pakuti anagwirizana ndi kupanduka kwa Satana.

Anaphunzitsa anthu kupanga malupanga, ndi mipeni, ndi zishango, ndi zovala zapachifuwa, ndipo anawadziwitsa zitsulo za dziko lapansi ndi luso lozigwiritsa ntchito, ndi zibangili, ndi zokongoletsera, ndi kugwiritsa ntchito antimoniyo, ndi kukongoletsa zikope, ndi mitundu yonse ya miyala yamtengo wapatali, ndi mitundu yonse ya utoto. Anaphunzitsa matsenga, ndi kudula mizu, kuthetsa matsenga, kupenda nyenyezi, magulu a nyenyezi, chidziwitso cha mitambo, zizindikiro za dziko lapansi, zizindikiro za dzuwa, ndi mayendedwe a mwezi.

Choncho alonda ogwa anatenga akazi awo ndikuchita nawo dama, ndipo anatenga mimba ndikuwaberekera zimphona zazikulu. Ndipo m'kupita kwa nthawi mbewu yosayerayi inameza zonse zomwe zinali pa dziko lapansi, ndi zonse zomwe zinaperekedwa kwa anthu, ndipo pamapeto pake inameza anthu ambiri momwe ikanathera. Pakuti anali omwa magazi ndi odya anthu, ndipo anachita choyipa chachikulu pamaso pa Yahweh, pakuti moyo wa thupi uli m'magazi ndipo anthu analengedwa m'chifanizo cha Yahweh. Anakula ndikuchulukana ndipo pamapeto pake anakhazikitsa maufumu awo padziko lonse lapansi. Taonani! Mabwinja a nyumba zawo zazikulu zamiyala adakali paliponse padziko lapansi ngati mboni yowatsutsa. Analamulira dziko lapansi nthawi imeneyo, ndipo anapangidwa kuti awononge anthu. Ndipo choncho mitundu yonse ya zonyansa inachitika, pakuti malemba oyera amalongosola momwe zinachitikira.

Monga ngati sikunali kokwanira kuti anthu asirire thupi lachilendo, kuchita dama ndi angelo ogwa, ndikubala ana azimphona, chifukwa cha alonda onse anachimwira mbalame, ndi zilombo, ndi zokwawa, ndi nsomba. Kuyambira pachiyambi cha chilengedwe, Yahweh anakhazikitsa mtundu uliwonse wa moyo monga mwa mtundu wake. Koma alonda oyipa anasokoneza chilengedwe, ngakhale kuphatikiza zolengedwa za mtundu wina ndi zolengedwa za mtundu wofanana kuti abweretse kusakhazikika kwa majini ndikusintha chilengedwe choyambirira cha Yahweh cha ungwiro wogwirizana. Ndondomeko iyi inabwerezedwa, kuphatikiza zolengedwa zofanana za mtundu wina ndi zolengedwa zofanana za mtundu wina mpaka zotsatira zomwe ankazifuna zitapezeka. Ndipo choncho dziko lomwe linalipo nthawi imeneyo, linawonongeka ndi mitundu yonse ya zonyansa zoyipa zomwe zinachimwira kwambiri Yahweh, anthu, ndi chilengedwe chonse. Mvetsetsani, ogwa akufuna dziko lawo, ngakhale dziko lamdima losiyana kotheratu ndi Yahweh, ndipo adzalandira chimodzimodzi m'nyanja yamoto yomwe Yahweh mwiniwake anawakonzekera.

Ndipo choncho masiku a Nowa anafika ndipo dziko lomwe linalipo nthawi imeneyo, loima m'madzi ndi m'madzi, linasefukira ndi madzi ndikuwonongeka. Pakuti akasupe a kuya kwakukulu anaphulika, kuchititsa chiwawa chachikulu pamene dziko lapansi linang'ambika ndi mphamvu ya madzi achiwawa, ndipo mazenera akumwamba anatsegulidwa, kulola madzi ochokera pamwamba pa thambo (ndi pansi pa dziko lapansi) kuphimba ndi kuwononga dziko lonse lapansi.

Ndipo choncho Enoke mlembi akufotokoza tsogolo la zimphonazi motere, pakuti Yahweh analengeza choncho: Ndipo tsopano, zimphona, zomwe zapangidwa kuchokera ku mizimu ndi thupi, zidzatchedwa mizimu yoyipa pa dziko lapansi, ndipo pa dziko lapansi padzakhala malo awo okhala. Mizimu yoyipa yatuluka m'matupi awo; chifukwa anabadwa kuchokera kwa anthu, kuchokera kwa alonda oyera ndi chiyambi chawo ndi magwero awo oyambirira; adzakhala mizimu yoyipa pa dziko lapansi, ndipo adzatchedwa mizimu yoyipa. Koma mizimu yakumwamba, kumwamba kudzakhala malo awo okhala, koma mizimu ya dziko lapansi yomwe inabadwira pa dziko lapansi, pa dziko lapansi padzakhala malo awo okhala. Ndipo mizimu ya zimphona imazunza, kupondereza, kuwononga, kuukira, kuchita nkhondo, ndi kugwira ntchito yowononga pa dziko lapansi, ndikuyambitsa mavuto: satenga chakudya, koma amamva njala ndi ludzu, ndikuyambitsa zolakwa. Ndipo mizimu iyi idzaukira ana a anthu ndi akazi, chifukwa yatuluka mwa iwo. Mvetsetsani kuti magawo asanu ndi anayi mwa magawo khumi a mizimu yoyipayi ali ku phompho chifukwa cha pempho ndi pemphero la Nowa, ndipo anthu akhala akulimbana ndi gawo limodzi mwa magawo khumi okha kuyambira pamenepo. Izi zatsala pang'ono kusintha pamene phompho lidzatsegulidwa ndipo onse adzamasulidwa.

Choncho dziko lapansi, ndi zonyansa zonse za alonda, zinawonongedwa. Alondawo anamangidwa m'maweruzo awo osiyanasiyana kwa mibadwo makumi asanu ndi awiri, kuti adzamasulidwe ku chiweruzo mu nthawi ya chiweruzo chachikulu. (Pakuti chiweruzo chachikulu chatsala pang'ono kuyamba.) Komabe, Nowa anapeza chisomo pamaso pa Yahweh chifukwa anali munthu wolungama, ndipo banja lake Yahweh analipulumutsa lamoyo ngakhale miyoyo isanu ndi itatu yonse, pamodzi ndi zolengedwa zina zambiri m'Chombo. Nowa anadzisunga yekha ndi banja lake ku kusokonezedwa kwa majini chigumula chisanachitike. Koma mwana wake Hamu ndi mbewu yake yoyipa pamapeto pake adzakana Nowa ngakhale Yahweh mwiniwake (amene anawalenga ndi kuwapulumutsa), kudziphatikiza okha ku kupanduka kwa Satana.

Mawu a Genesis 6 anakwaniritsidwa ndi machimo a mbewu ya Nowa, ngakhale mzera wa Hamu. Kunali zimphona pa dziko lapansi masiku amenewo: ndiponso pambuyo pake. "Masiku amenewo" anali masiku kuyambira Yaredi mpaka Nowa, ndipo "zimphona pa dziko lapansi" zinali mbewu ya alonda ndi ana aakazi a anthu chigumula chisanachitike. "Pambuyo pake" ndi pambuyo pa masiku a Nowa, pamene majini a anthu anawonongedwanso ndi chidziwitso cha angelo ogwa. Ndikukupemphani kuti musakhulupirirenso chinyengo cha chiphunzitso cholakwika cha Aseti ndikukhulupirira mawu a lemba loyera, pakuti mawu awa ndi choonadi.

Pamene dziko lapansi linagawidwa ndi maere pakati pa ana a Nowa (Semu, Hamu, ndi Yafeti), maere a Hamu anali oti alandire maiko a kumwera, ndipo anali ake. Komabe, Kanani anasirira gawo la cholowa cha Semu ndikusamukira ku maiko omwe sanali ake kuti akhale. Pakuti anali Kanani amene Nowa anamutemberera. Pakuti Kanani anasiya cholowa chake ndikukhazikitsa mzinda wa Turo pa zomwe zinakhala gombe la Foinike ndikutsogolera ku Sidoni, komwe Yezebeli anachokera. Anachita izi kuti akhale pafupi ndi Phiri la Herimoni, komwe alonda anatsikira ndipo komwe ine ndinasandulika.

Kanani anapeza zomwe alonda anasiya chigumula cha Nowa chisanachitike, chifukwa anafuna kusunga kupanduka kwawo ngakhale pambuyo pa chigumula, pakuti anajambula zambiri m'matanthwe ndi miyala. Kanani anagwiritsa ntchito chidziwitso chawo cha angelo ogwa mu ufiti waukulu, ndipo miyambo yambiri yoyipa inachitidwa ndi Mkanani, pakuti anapereka nsembe ngakhale ana awo. Anali oyipa kwambiri ndipo anafalikira kwambiri moti anakwanitsa kuyambitsa zonyansa zake zatsopano m'dziko la Kanani, komwe Satana anagwira ntchito chifukwa cha zolakwa zake.

Ndipo choncho mafuko a zimphona za pambuyo pa chigumula, ngakhale mafuko onyansa a Akanaani, anatuluka m'zolakwa ndi machimo ndi ufiti wa Kanani. Ku dziko lomwe Kanani anaba kwa Semu kunabwera Ahiti, ndi Ahivi, ndi Aperizi, ndi Agirigasi, ndi Aamori, ndi Ayebusi. Iwo anali oopsa, ngakhale Arefaimu. Kunali mafuko ambiri kuposa omwe atchulidwa, koma oopsawa anali kuyembekezera m'dziko la lonjezo poyesa kupha omwe ankateteza mzera wa Khristu, ngakhale makolo anu a Aisiraeli ndi atate omwe anali Ahebri.

Yoswa analowa m'dziko lolonjezedwa kuti atenge zomwe zinaperekedwa moyenera kwa Semu ndi maere ndi dziko lomwe linali la Isiraeli moyenera ndi lonjezo la Yahweh mwiniwake. Koma ana a Isiraeli sanakwaniritse mokwanira malamulo a Yahweh osasiya namsongole aliyense wamoyo, pakuti anali oyipa ndipo sanali anthu, ndipo machitidwe awo anali oyipa, ndipo anachita choyipa chachikulu pamaso pa Yahweh, pakuti umu ndi momwe chilengedwe chawo chinalili. Ndipo choncho Yahweh anauza Isiraeli monga kwalembedwa m'malemba: Koma ngati simudzathamangitsa okhala m'dzikomo pamaso panu, ndiye kuti chidzachitika kuti omwe muwasiye mwa iwo, adzakhala zaminga m'maso mwanu, ndi minga m'mbali mwanu, ndipo adzakuvutitsani m'dziko lomwe mukukhalamo. Ndipo zinali choncho.

Mbiri mwa zina ikuwonetsa kufalikira ndi kuyenda kwa zonyansazi, ngakhale namsongole wobzalidwa ndi Satana mwiniwake, popeza akhala akukhala pakati pa tirigu kuyambira masiku akale. Akhazikitsa zitukuko zambiri pogwiritsa ntchito matekinoloje awo ndi chidziwitso cha angelo ogwa; ndi omanga aluso, ndi amatsenga, ambiri a iwo ali ndi maluso osakhala achibadwa, ndipo amalumikizidwa ndi atate wawo Mdierekezi, ngakhale kugawana makhalidwe ake ndi zilakolako zake. Namsongole ameneyu walowa m'mbali iliyonse ya anthu, ndipo si onse omwe ndi zimphona. Ambiri amawoneka ngati anthu, kupatula kuti ndi zolengedwa zosakanizidwa zomwe zinasinthidwa mpaka kalekale kuchokera ku mapangidwe a Yahweh m'chilengedwe ndipo sadzawomboledwa. Musawawope, pakuti wamkulu ndi amene ali mwa inu kuposa amene ali m'dziko lapansi.

Namsongole ameneyu akufuna kutenga zomwe ndi za ana a Mulungu mwa cholowa ndi lonjezo. Sizingatheke kugwirizana nawo, pakuti ndi a atate wawo Mdierekezi, ndipo zilakolako za atate wawo adzazichita. Iye anali wakupha kuyambira pachiyambi, ndipo pamene anena bodza amalankhula zake, pakuti ndi wabodza ndi atate wake wa bodza. Adzasokoneza, kuipitsa, kuwononga, kupha, kufunkha, kulanda, ndikuchita zoyipa zawo zonse motsutsana ndi chilichonse chaumulungu chomwe ayika zilakolako zawo. Alibe mtendere, ndipo chikhalidwe chawo ndi chowonongeka kotheratu. Asokoneza dziko lapansi, ndipo adzakhazikitsa ufumu wawo kwa ola limodzi pamene chinjoka chili ndi ulamuliro wake pa dziko lapansi. Komabe akufuna kukhazikitsanso nthawi yawo yagolide, koma sadzakhala ndi nthawi yagolide koma chiweruzo chaukali, ndipo sadzapulumuka.

Namsongole woyipawa wawononga chidziwitso, kuphunzira, mbiri, chipembedzo, sayansi, ndipo apitiriza kuwononga. Afalitsa tekinoloje ya angelo ogwa ndipo palibe chatsopano pansi pa dzuwa. Pamene nthawi zakwaniritsidwa, aphimba kotheratu dziko lapansi ndi mabodza, ndipo dziko lonse lapansi ligona m'choyipa. Mwa kuunikiridwa kwawo, namsongole wa Chijesuti wawononga kotheratu anthu ndikutsogolera anthu m'nyanja ya mabodza, komwe kuthawa kokha kuli kudzera m'kulapa ndi kukana machimo onse ndi mabodza. (Pakuti Roma anapanganso Chisilamu; phunzirani kuti mudziwe.) Chidziwitso chawo cha angelo ogwa ndi mabodza zikutsutsana ndi mbiri ya m'malemba ndipo m'malo mwake amapereka nthano zopanda pake zomwe zimachititsa khungu maganizo ndi mitima ya anthu. Choncho, tulukani pakati pawo ndipo mukhale osiyana.

Dziko lapansi masiku ano ndi lomwe anthu ambiri, ambiri ali ndi kuiwala kwakukulu chifukwa cha maphunziro a Chijesuti. Ambiri a inu mwaiwala osati mapangano ndi njira za Yahweh zokha, koma mwaiwala kotheratu kuti ndinu ndani komanso makolo anu enieni ndi ndani. Izi zinachitidwa mwadala kudzera m'nkhondo, mabodza, ndi kuyesetsa kukhazikitsanso anthu m'zaka zapitazi zomwe zamwaza ambiri ku ngodya zinayi za dziko lapansi. Pa izi iwo mofunitsitsa sakhala odziwa kuti n'zosatheka kubisa chilichonse pamaso pa Yahweh; ndipo ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndimadziwa anga ndi omwe ndikuwaitanira ku chipulumutso.

Ena mwa omwe anamwazidwa anali amasiye ndipo anatengedwa m'mabanja awo kudzera m'nkhondo kapena kubedwa kotheratu. Miyoyo iyi inakulira pa mbiri yabodza, ngakhale kunamizidwa ndi malingaliro okondweretsa a zakale zabodza, ndipo choncho chinyengo chazika mizu mozama m'mitima ya anthu, ndipo ambiri achititsidwa khungu mpaka kalekale ndi mabodza omwe amavomerezedwa ngati choonadi. Koma kwa iwo omwe atembenukira kwa ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, mudzadziwa choonadi ndipo choonadi chidzakumasulani. Gulu lanu lamakono lamangidwa pa mabwinja ndi phulusa la zitukuko za ogwa zakale, ndipo khama lalikulu lapita pakubisa choonadi. Zomwe zakhalapo zidzakhalaponso, ndipo palibe chatsopano pansi pa dzuwa.

Popanda nsembe yanga yowombola ndi mwayi wanga wa chipulumutso, nonse mukanatayika. Choncho bwerani kwa ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndipo ndidzakutsogolerani m'choonadi chonse ndipo ndidzakuphunzitsani mbiri yanu yoona. Ndidzakulumikizaninso ndi makolo anu enieni, pakuti ambiri a inu ndinu a m'dzikolo, ngakhale chipulumutso ndi cha mitundu yonse. Ndidzakuphunzitsani m'njira za chiyero, ndipo mudzakhala molungama mwa chikhulupiriro mwa ine, Yesu Khristu, ndipo mudzakhala ndi moyo wosatha. Palibe chinsinsi chomwe sichidzawonekera, ndipo zonse zidzaululidwa kotheratu, kuti choonadi chidziwike ndi onse.

Choncho siyani kudya mabodza pamene mukudzikwirira m'kukhutira, pakuti nthawi za kukhutira zatha. Nthawi zowulula zili pafupi. Pakuti taonani! Monga m'masiku a Nowa, momwemonso kudzakhala. Satana wagwiritsa ntchito ukonde wake wa mabodza kukonza njira ya chinyengo chake chachikulu cha nthawi yotsiriza, ndipo dziko lonse lapansi lidzatsatira mabodza ake. Pakuti ufumu wonse wa mdierekezi udzamasulidwa kuchokera ku phompho, ndipo zimphona zidzakhalanso pakati pa anthu, pakuti zikubwera kudzachita mkwiyo wa Yahweh.

Satana amanena mabodza akulu, ndipo ogwawa adzanena kuti akuchokera ku maiko ena ndipo apita patsogolo kuposa anthu ndi zaka masauzande ambiri. Adzabwera akupereka chidziwitso chomwe chikuwoneka ngati chabwino, ndipo adzadziveka okha mu ubwino wabodza. Koma awa ndi a ufumu wa Satana, choncho musagwe m'zinyengo. Musatsatire njira za dziko lapansi, pakuti dziko lapansi lidzadabwa pambuyo pa ufumu wa Satana, ngakhale kunena kuti, ndani wofanana ndi chilombo?

Ndidzakubisani, ndidzakutetezani, ndidzakulimbitsani, ndidzakutsogolerani pang'onopang'ono, ndipo ndidzang'amba kotheratu chophimba cha mabodza chomwe chaphimba dziko lapansi mu chinyengo. Ine, ngakhale Yeshua HaMashiach, ndidzakupatsani mphamvu ndikukupangitsani kuchita zozizwitsa zambiri zamphamvu mu nthawi iyi ya masautso a Yakobo. Ndipo choncho ndikukuuzani zinthu izi, kuti choonadi chikumasuleni, ndikuti mukhale omasuka ndithu ndipo musagwe m'chinyengo chawo chachikulu. Pakuti maziko ake ndi mabodza, chinyengo, ndi bodza lofalitsidwa ndi mbewu ya njoka.

Ndine njira, choonadi, ndi moyo; palibe munthu amabwera kwa atate koma kudzera mwa ine.

Ndine amene ndakuitanani ku ulemerero ndi ukoma.

Ndine amene ndidzalamulira mpaka kalekale monga Mbuye wa Ambuye ndi Mfumu ya Mafumu.

Ndine Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda.

Ndine amene ndimalamulira magulu ankhondo akumwamba.

Ndine amene ndidzaonekera m'mitambo kusonkhanitsa Mpingo wanga.

Ndine amene ndili ndi makiyi a imfa ndi helo, ndipo ndidzaweruza m'chilungamo.

Ndine amene bondo lililonse lidzagwada pamaso panga ndipo lilime lililonse lidzavomereza.

Ndine amene NDINE Wamkulu wandituma, choncho mverani mawu anga.

Taonani! Ndikukuuzani zinthu izi osati kuti ndikupatseni phunziro lathunthu la mbiri koma kuti ndikutsogolereni sitepe imodzi patsogolo ku choonadi. M'kupita kwa nthawi, zonse zidzawonekera ndipo ufumu wa chinjoka udzaululidwa, pakuti ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndalengeza izi, ndipo ndine amene Yahweh wandituma.
