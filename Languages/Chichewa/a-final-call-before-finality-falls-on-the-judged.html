Muitano Womaliza Chimaliziro Chisanafike pa Oweruzidwa

Taonani! Ine Ndine Yehova, Ine Ndine Wamkulu, ndipo ndabwera kudzatumiza Mawu anga achikondi ndi muitano womaliza kwa iwo omwe akuyang'anizana ndi chimaliziro cha chiweruzo ngati akana muitano wanga womaliza. Ngakhale kuti, onse amene aitanira pa dzina la Yesu mpaka chimaliziro adzapeza chipulumutso chachikulu ndi chamuyaya ndi mphoto yaikulu, anthu ambiri adzagwa m'chiweruzo chifukwa cha machimo awo ndi kuuma mtima. Chifukwa, Ine Ndine amene nditsutsa osalungama kuti alape, ndipo amene akana kulapa ndikupitiriza kuwatsutsa kufikira mitima yawo itauma kotheratu ndipo atengedwa m'chiweruzo.

Chotero, ndabwera kudzapereka uthenga uwu m'chikondi changa pa inu, ndi kutumiza chisomo changa chosayenerera ku dziko loweruzidwa kachiwiri m'kuyesetsa kwanga kufewetsa mitima yanu yosalapa kuti mulandire chipulumutso; kapena kuti m'chidani chanu ndi kupanduka kwanu musiyidwe mu mkhalidwe wokana komaliza, okhwima ndi okonzekera chiweruzo chomaliza. Zindikirani, palibe nthawi yochulukirapo m'nthawi zanga zachisomo ndi chifundo ya moyo wonse wa kulapa chifukwa cha mapulani anga achipulumutso kwa anthu onse amene adzafika ku chipulumutso chokwanira m'nthawi zawo ndi madongosolo awo kwa iwo amene adzalandira chikondi changa pa iwo. Chotero, nthawi zanu ndi tsopano ndipo muitano womaliza ukutumizidwa tsopano. Sela.

Imvani, O Imvani liwu la Mulungu Wamkulu Wamuyaya wa Chilungamo, ngakhale Yehova, Ine Ndine Wamkulu, amenenso ali Mulungu Wamuyaya wa Chilungamo ndipo zindikirani tanthauzo la kulankhula kwanga, ndipo dziwani kuti liwu langa lili ngati mkokomo wa madzi ambiri othamanga ndi amoyo, ndipo zindikirani kuti liwu langa limabangula ngati mkokomo wa mabingu ambiri, (ngakhale mabingu a mabingu) m'kukwiya kwanga kolungama pa okhala pa dziko lapansi amene akukhalamo m'kusamvera kwakukulu ndi m'chosalungama chachikulu ndi tchimo.

Taonani! Ine Ndine Amene Ndine, ngakhale Ine Ndine Wamkulu, ndipo ndinalenga kumwamba ndi dziko lapansi ndi chidzalo chake chonse, ndipo m'kapangidwe kanga kolungama ndinapanga kumwamba ndi dziko lapansi kukhala zangwiro m'njira zanga za ungwiro, ngakhale kuzilenga mu ungwiro wogwirizana ndi wolungama kumene zolengedwa zanga zonse zinayenera kukhala mu ungwiro wogwirizana ndi chikondi ndi kutumikirana m'chikondi wina ndi mnzake. Ndipo m'chikondi chawo pa Atate wawo ndi mlengi, iwo akamvera liwu langa ndi malamulo a miyoyo yawo kuti ndithudi adzimvetsetse okha monga analengedwera ndiponso kumvetsetsa njira zanga. Komabe, chosalungama chinapezeka mwa Heylel, ngakhale iye amene anagwa kuchokera ku ungwiro waulemerero, ngakhale iye amene analengedwa mu ulemerero waukulu ndi woopsa, kokha kuti agwere pa dziko lapansi, wonyozedwa m'tchimo ndi mdima kuti akhale m'maonekedwe a njoka omwe ali otembereredwa chifukwa cha kusamvera kwa Aserafi. Sela.

Chifukwa cha choonadi chamuyaya, njira zanga zili zapamwamba kuposa njira za munthu, ndipo Ine Ndine amene ndazungulira mpando wanga wachifumu ndi zikwi khumi kuchulukitsa zikwi khumi, ngakhale unyinji wosawerengeka wa angelo amene amapambana mu nzeru, mphamvu, ndi nyonga, ndipo amene amakonda Yehova ndi amene amakonda kuphunzira njira za Yehova, Ine Ndine Wamkulu m'kumvera konse kotentha ndi kokhulupirika nthawi zonse: Sela.

Zoonadi, za choonadi chachikulu ndi chamuyaya, ndawalenga amphamvu ndi aulemerero ndikuwaveka mu ulemerero wowala, ndi chilungamo chamuyaya, ndi ulemu wosatha. Zoonadi, za choonadi chachikulu ndi chamuyaya, sanasowepo pa chosowa kapena kusowa ngakhale m'zinthu zing'onozing'ono nthawi iliyonse; ndipo amanditumikira Ine ndi chilengedwe changa mokwanira m'mitima yawo yachikondi pa Atate wawo ndi mlengi, ngakhale Elohim, m'kumvera kwawo kwa Khristu Yesu.

(Ngakhale Iye amene, Yehova, Ine Ndine Wamkulu ndi Wosatha waika zinthu zonse kumwamba ndi dziko lapansi m'manja mwake amphamvu ndi osatha ndi omvera.)

Chotero dziwani za choonadi chamuyaya kuti Khristu Yesu ndiye Mbuye wa Ambuye ndi Mfumu ya Mafumu ndipo ndiye amene adzabweretsa mtendere kumwamba komanso pa dziko lapansi ndipo ndiye amene adzabwezera ku dzanja langa laulemerero (m'chikondi chake pa Atate wake ndi Mlengi, Elohim, ngakhale Ine Ndine Wamkulu) Ufumu wogonjetsedwa pa chimaliziro cha zinthu zonse pamene manja ake amphamvu adzakhala atakwaniritsa zinthu zonse zimene zinalembedwa ndi kunenedweratu zokhudza Mbuye Wosatha wa Chilungamo! Ngakhale pamene Mwana wa Munthu ameneyo adzakhala atamaliza zinthu zonse mu mphamvu yaikulu, ndi m'chikondi chachikulu. Ngakhale zili choncho pa nthawi imene, ndidzakhala nditakwaniritsa mu mphamvu yanga ndi manja ake amphamvu ndi nsembe zinthu zonse zoperekedwa m'mapulani anga amuyaya ndi zinsinsi kuti iye akwaniritse.

Chotero, ndikukupatsani kuti mumvetsetse, kuti Ine Ndine amene mu mphamvu zonse ndi ulamuliro ndingathe kumvetsetsa, kukwaniritsa, kudziwa mokwanira, ndi kulamulira tsatanetsatane aliyense wokhudza mbali iliyonse ya chilengedwe changa mosasamala kanthu kuti ndi chachikulu kapena chooneka ngati champhamvu motani mpaka ku kachidutswa kakang'ono kwambiri, ndipo palibe chimene chingabisike pamaso panga! Ine Ndine amene ndingathe kukwaniritsa zinthu zonse nthawi yomweyo mu nyonga yanga ndi mphamvu yamuyaya. Komabe, Ine ndinenso amene ndimagwira ntchito zinthu zonse monga mwa uphungu wa chifuniro changa ndipo amene sasowa phungu, chotero, mapulani anga ndi zinsinsi zidzakwaniritsidwa mokwanira m'nthawi izi za kukwaniritsidwa kwawo, ngakhale nthawi za TSOPANO.

Komabe, m'chikondi changa pa chilengedwe changa ndi chikhumbo changa cha ana ndi banja, ndimalenga zolengedwa ndi anthu enieni (ndi ufulu wosankha) kaamba ka zolinga zanga zenizeni zamuyaya zoperekedwa kwa omvera mu utumiki waukulu ndi ulemu, ndipo ndimawapatsa mphamvu moyenerera. Chotero, ndikukulangizani kuti muphunzire njira zanga m'chipulumutso kapena kusankha malawi a moto a chiwonongeko kwamuyaya. Koma dziwani, O munthu wakugwa, muli ndi chisankho chomwe mwadzidzidzi tsopano chidzasintha kwamuyaya kukhala munali ndi chisankho, ndipo onse amene adzakhala atasankha choipa pomaliza pamene chimaliziro chidzafika, sadzapulumuka ku ziweruzo zawo kwamuyaya ndi nthawi zosatha. Sela.

Ndinu ndani, O munthu wakugwa, ngakhale inu amene mwadziphatika nokha ku kupanduka kopanda nzeru ndi kopusa ndi kwamisala kwa akugwa mu chosalungama chonse, umbombo, chilakolako, ndi kupotoza kuti mudzikweze nokha mu kudzikuza motsutsana ndi Mulungu Wamkulu ndi Wamuyaya amene anakulengani kaamba ka zolinga zanga ndi chikondwerero?

Ndinu ndani, O munthu wakugwa kuti mu umbuli wanu ndi chilakolako mungayerekeze kukweza chidendene chanu motsutsana ndi Mulungu wamkulu ndi wamuyaya wa Kumwamba kwa Kumwamba (ngakhale IYE amene anakupatsani moyo) m'njira za Satana? Chotero, zindikirani zolinga zanga ndi chifuno changa chifukwa ndikutumiza Mawu awa kwa INU monga muitano wanga womaliza kwa ambiri a inu amene mopusa mukuganiza kuti muli ndi nthawi yomwe mulibe, ndipo ndikukupatsaninso mawu achenjezo m'chikondi changa chosafa pa inu pokukumbutsani kachiwiri za zilengezo za chiweruzo ndi tsoka zomwe zaperekedwa kale zomwe mwazinyalanyaza mokwanira zomwe zapangidwa ndi Khristu Yesu mu mpando wachifumu ndi mphamvu ya Yehova, ndiponso zolengezedwa ndi Yehova mwiniwake, ngakhale Ine Ndine Wamkulu!

(Kodi simukumvetsa kuti zilengezo zanga sizingasinthidwe?)

Komabe, mumanyalanyaza ngakhale mawu a mabingu asanu ndi awiri omwe ndinawavumbulutsa kwa, ndipo ndinapangitsa Yohane Wovumbulutsa kuwasindikiza m'nthawi yake mpaka nthawi zoyenera zovumbulutsa zomwe nthawi zake ndi tsopano. (Ndipo zidzapitirira mpaka umboni wa mboni zanga ziwiri utamalizidwa m'chiweruzo chomaliza, chisanafike chisautso chachikulu kwambiri kugwa mokwanira pa osalungama, Sela.) Ngakhale mabingu omwe ananenedwa ndi Yeshua HaMashiach ndi Yehova, Ine Ndine Wamkulu, kuchokera ku mpando wanga wachifumu wamphamvu wa chilengezo ndi chiweruzo, ndipo omwe tsopano akuperekedwa ndi dzanja la mthenga wanga wamphamvu amene kudzera mwa iye ndikutumiza Mawu awa achikondi ndi machenjezo okhwima kwambiri ndi oopsa, ngakhale kuti onse amene angabwere abwere, chiweruzo chisanagwe mokwanira ndipo palibe nthawi yochulukirapo!

Chotero, ngati mukhumba kupulumuka chiweruzo chanu ndi chiwonongeko chamuyaya, ndikukulamulani kuti mupange Yesu kukhala Mbuye wanu mokwanira ndi mopanda kukayika. Chifukwa ziweruzo zanga zikugwa, ndipo zidzagwa pa aliyense amene zatumizidwa, ngakhale ziweruzo zomwezo za kutsutsidwa zomwe zili zomaliza kwamuyaya m'chimaliziro chawo ngakhale kwamuyaya ndi nthawi zosatha. Chotero, ndikulangiza onse amene sanavomereze Yesu Khristu monga Mbuye kuchokera mu mtima wodzaza ndi chikhulupiriko kuti atero tsopano, podziwa kuti Ine Ndine amene ndinamuukitsa kwa akufa mu mphamvu yaikulu koposa kuti asafe nso ndi kupitiriza mu cholowa chake kwamuyaya ndi nthawi zosatha. Komabe, unyinji waukulu udzagwa m'chiweruzo chachikulu ndi chamuyaya ndendende monga kwalembedwa m'malemba Oyera akale ndi malemba Oyera a tsopano.

Koma ndabwera kudzapereka machenjezo anga ngakhale zili choncho, ngakhale pambuyo poti zilengezo za chiweruzo chanu chomaliza zalengezedwa ndipo zili mkati mochitika. Chifukwa njira zanga ndi njira zachisomo ndi chifundo ndi chikondi ndi za choonadi chamuyaya, ndikhumba kuti pasakhale wina aliyense amene angawonongeke koma kuti anthu onse afike ku chipulumutso chamuyaya ndi kubwezeretsedwa, ngakhale kuti ambiri adzakana chopereka changa ndikusankha chizunzo chawo chamuyaya ndi chiweruzo m'malo mwake. Ngakhale, ndabwera kudzachenjeza anthu, palibe machenjezo operekedwa omwe adzasinthe ziweruzo zanga, kapena nthawi yawo, kapena zotulukapo zawo, kapena mapulani anga amuyaya owombola zipatso zanga zoyamba, ngakhale kuwakwatula kuti akwaniritse cholowa chawo chamuyaya tsoka lisanafike. Sela.

Ndipo ndikutumiza uthenga uwu wachikondi ndi chenjezo m'chikondi changa kwa munthu woipa wachibadwa, ngakhale m'chikondi chomwecho ndimatumiza mauthenga achikondi ndi chenjezo kwa ana anga omwe amene avomereza momvera Yesu Khristu; chifukwa ndimagwetsa mvula pa olungama ndi osalungama omwe. Chifukwa cha chikondi changa pa inu, ndabwera kudzapereka muitano wanga womaliza tsoka lisanamenye kuchokera ku mpando wanga wachifumu wa mphamvu yamuyaya, chiweruzo, ndi chilengezo kumene liwu langa lamkokomo likubangulabe mu mkwiyo waukulu ndi wolungama. Ngakhale zili choncho, chifukwa MUKUKANA kundimvera ngakhale ndachenjeza ndi kuchenjeza kwa zaka zambiri, zambiri za yubile tsopano! Ndimveni ndipo ndimveni bwino chifukwa nthawi zanu za chiwonongeko chamuyaya zidzatumizidwa kumanga m'chiweruzo onse amene akonda ndi kupanga bodza, ndi amene ali ndi chikondwerero m'chosalungama, ndipo ngati mundikana m'nthawi izi zachisomo, mudzagwa m'chiweruzo chamuyaya chifukwa palibe chisankho china.

Komabe, mu kudzikuza kwanu kwakhungu ndi kuuma mtima, mukukana munthu mmodzi amene ndinamutumiza kaamba ka chipulumutso chanu chokwanira, ngakhale Yesu wa ku Nazarete, ngakhale Mwana wodzichepetsa ameneyo ndi mtumiki wa Yehova amene ndamupanga kukhala mzimu wopatsa moyo, ngakhale kupangidwa wamng'ono pang'ono chabe kuposa Yehova mwiniwake m'kuuka kwanga kwa akufa ndi kulemekezedwa kwa Khristu Yesu! Chifukwa Ine Ndine wapamwambamwamba ndi wolamulira pa chilengedwe changa chonse ndi wa choonadi chamuyaya ndi chosasinthika, Ine Ndine amene ndimatumiza mvula pa olungama ndi osalungama omwe m'chikondi changa pa chilengedwe changa, ndipo m'njira zanga mulibe tsankho la anthu m'ziweruzo zanga zolungama pa chilengedwe changa.

Chotero, zindikirani kuti kukhala watsankho ndi imodzi mwa njira zambiri zoipitsidwa za akugwa, chotero ndabwera kudzachenjeza ngakhale wochimwa woipitsitsa pakati pa okhala pa dziko lapansi chiweruzo chawo chisanawamenye mokwanira, ngakhale kuti atha kupulumuka m'masekondi ochepa otsala ndi othamanga a chisomo tsoka lisanamenye. Chotero ndikulangiza onse amene amva kapena kuwerenga Mawu awa kuti atembenukire kwa Yesu wa ku Nazarete nthawi yomweyo, osakayika kanthu chifukwa kwa unyinji waukulu sipadzakhala mwayi wina, chifukwa m'machimo awo oipa ndi kunyoza kotheratu zinthu zonse Zoyera, adzagwa m'chiweruzo chamuyaya, ngakhale kusungidwa m'chizunzo kumene adzayembekezera chiweruzo cha mpando wachifumu waukulu woyera, kumene sadzaima koma m'malo mwake adzamangidwa ndi kuperekedwa ku malawi a moto a chilango kumene machimo awo adzaperekedwa m'malawi a moto kaamba ka kubwezera mokwanira kwa chilungamo kwamuyaya.

Komabe, inu, O munthu wakugwa, mungakhale m'njira zanu zosalungama, ngakhale kugonjetsedwa kotheratu ndi akugwa mu umbuli wanu ndi khungu loyambitsidwa ndi mabodza awo, ngakhale mabodza omwe mumawakonda chifukwa mumakonda njira za mabodza anu. Chotero, ndatumiza pa dziko lapansi chinyengo chachikulu ndi champhamvu kuti inu nonse mutsutsidwe amene muli ndi chikondwerero m'chosalungama ndi kukana Yesu wa ku Nazarete, ngakhale Yeshua HaMashiach. Ndipo pamene mukufufuza mu umbuli m'njira zanu zakhungu ndi zouma mtima, pamene mukhala mu mdima mu ulesi ndi tchimo lalikulu, pamene mukuyendayenda mu zachabe kudziunjikira nokha mkwiyo waukulu ndi zotulukapo zamuyaya m'kusamvera kwanu, ndidzakwezabe liwu langa, ngakhale kutumiza Mawu awa monga umboni motsutsana ndi iwo amene adzakana, ngakhale m'chiyembekezo chakuti mungatembenukire kwa Khristu Yesu, kuti ndikupulumutseni ku chiweruzo cha akugwa: Sela.

O munthu Wachibadwa, ngakhale inu nonse ana a Adamu osawomboledwa ndi osalapa, ngakhale onse amene ndimawalanga pa dziko lapansi m'chikondi changa pa inu! Komabe mwakhulupirira mabodza opusa kwambiri operekedwa kwa inu monga maphunziro m'kuzikidwa kwanu ndi akugwa. Ngakhale maphunziro a imfa operekedwa m'mabungwe anu achinyengo ndi oipitsa a imfa kumene njira za ogonana amuna kapena akazi okhaokha tsopano zikulamulira pamene akulemba ndi kukonzekeretsa ana anu enieni kuti athe kuchita phwando pa osalakwa m'zilakolako zawo zowola. Komabe, mwakopeka kwambiri ndi zokondweretsa za tchimo, ndipo mwazunguliridwa kwambiri ndi umbombo wanu wofuna kudziunjikira chuma chochuluka, kotero kuti mumapereka nsembe mofunitsitsa ana anu enieni m'njira za Baala kaamba ka kachidutswa ka mkate woipitsidwa. Chotero, ndalola akugwa kuika poizoni m'chakudya chanu, ndipo ndawapatsa kuchuluka kwanu konse ngakhale kuti chinjoka, chilombo chochokera kunyanja, ndi chilombo chochokera pa dziko lapansi akwaniritse zolinga zanga, chifukwa ngakhale adani anga amamvera liwu langa.

Chifukwa maphunziro anu otchedwa choncho ndi onama ndi achinyengo mwa kapangidwe ndipo izi chifukwa mukukana chidziwitso choona ndi njira za Elohim mlengi wanu, kusankha m'malo mwake kugonjetsedwa maganizo ndi magalasi anu akuda omwe mumawagwira mokhulupirika m'manja pamene mukupita kuchokera pa chophimba kupita pa chophimba mu umbuli wamdima kwambiri. Komabe mwagonjetsedwa kotheratu ndi kuchititsidwa khungu m'chisangalalo chotsika mtengo ndi chothamanga pamene maganizo anu akusinthidwa mwa mankhwala mwa kapangidwe kudzera mu zipangizo zomwe mumadzimangira nokha m'tchimo. Ndipo chotero chidziwitso chonama chimayambitsa kudzikuza ndipo kudzikuza kumatsogolera chiwonongeko chachikulu. Zindikirani, O Munthu, kuti chidziwitso chanu sichingakupulumutseni ku ziweruzo zanga, ndipo chiweruzo cha akugwa tsopano chakhazikitsidwa kuti chigwe. Komabe, inu, O munthu Wachibadwa ndinu opusa kotheratu kuti musamvetsetse kuti akugwa akuzikani ndi mabodza awo, ngakhale kuti apangitse amuna ambiri momwe angathere kuti agwirizane nawo mu cholowa chawo cha sulufule woyaka, wowola, ndi wogubuduzika. Ngakhale chilango chaukali chomwe chinapangidwira mdierekezi ndi angelo ake, osati Adamu. Sela.

Komabe, mwa chilolezo chanu chofunitsitsa mumanyadira kwambiri m'zopambana zanu zadziko zopanda phindu kumene mumasonkhanitsa mwadyera mapepala anu onama opanda phindu ndi manambala anu onama opanda phindu m'maakaunti onama opanda phindu omwe adzatengedwa kwa inu mwadzidzidzi m'chiweruzo tsopano, ngakhale zili choncho chifukwa izi ndi njira za Esau amene anagulitsa ukulu wake kaamba ka kachidutswa ka mkate. Komabe, mwa chilolezo chanu chofunitsitsa mumalola akugwa kutenga ndalama kwa inu mwa chiwopsezo cha mphamvu kuti mulipire kuzikidwa kwa ana anu enieni m'njira za imfa. Ngakhale kuti onse amene azikidwa mu umbuli wotere ndi khungu adzatumikira mofunitsitsa akugwa pamene akuganiza kuti akuchitira Mulungu mwiniwake chiyanjo chachikulu m'machimo awo, ngati kuti Yehova ayenera pambali kuchokera ku chilengedwe chake ndi ulamuliro chifukwa cha kudzikuza kwawo kopanda phindu, kwauchimo. Ngakhale chinthu chosatheka chomwe sichingachitike, chifukwa Ine Ndine.

Ndipo m'njira zanu zouma mtima za chosalungama mwataya ngakhale makutu anu ndipo mwayima ogontha, osalankhula, ndi akhungu m'njira zanu zosalungama zomwe zakuchepetsani mu ukapolo monga kapolo wa tchimo kuti mukondweretse zilakolako za akugwa, omwe mumawatumikira m'njira zanu zopanda chikhulupiriko. Komabe, mtumiki sakhala m'nyumba kwamuyaya ngati asankha momvera kulowa pa khomo lokhalo la kumwamba lomwe ndidzatsegulira mtundu wa anthu: Yesu Khristu amenenso ali, Yesu Mesiya, amene dzina lake lilinso Yeshua HaMashiach, amene ali khomo lalikulu ndi lokhalo lotseguka la chipulumutso lomwe lidzatsegulidwa ndi dzanja langa kaamba ka okhulupirika olungama mwa Khristu Yesu. Sela!

Ine Ndine, ndipo ndatumiza Mawu awa achikondi kwa otsutsidwa kudzera mwa mthenga wanga ndi mtumiki, ndipo kudzera mwa mthenga wanga ndi mtumiki ndatumiza Mawu awa kwa iwo amene adzaweruzidwa posachedwa.

Zindikirani onse amene amva Mawu awa, kuti uku sikulengeza komaliza kwa chiweruzo, komwe kumabweretsa chimaliziro kwa munthu wachibadwa. M'malo mwake, uthenga uwu ukutumikira kuchenjeza osamvera ndi osalungama a Babulo Wachinsinsi ndi ngakhale onse amene Mawu awa atumizidwa kwa iwo, (ndipo ena adzafika ku chipulumutso m'masekondi omalizawa) komanso monga umboni womaliza wa chenjezo woperekedwa chiweruzo chisanagwe mokwanira pa Babulo Wachinsinsi.

Zindikirani, kuti Mawu anga adzapeza maso, makutu, ndi mitima ya iwo amene atumizidwa kwa iwo, ndipo palibe khama lochokera kwa akugwa, zonyansa zawo, kapena matekinoloje awo opanda phindu ndi aumbuli omwe angaletse chimene Ine Ndine Wamkulu ndikufuna kukwaniritsa.

Ndipo chotero ndikusaina kalata yanga yachikondi kwa oweruzidwa m'chiyembekezo chakuti mwa kumvera Mawu anga otumizidwa m'chikondi changa pa iwo angapulumuke ziweruzo zawo dzanja lomaliza lisanamenye sekondi yomalizayo.

Ndipo chotero ndikusaina kalata yanga yachikondi m'Chikondi chachikulu ndi Ulamuliro Wamuyaya!

Ine Ndine Amene Ndine, ndipo Ine Ndine Yehova, Ine Ndine Wamkulu ndi Wamuyaya.
