Kwa Okhulupirika mwa Khristu: Musadabwe!

Musadabwe, abale anga, musadabwe, ngakhale dziko lapansi likudani nanu. Dziwani kuti dziko lapansi linadana nane lisanadane nanu, okondedwa anga, ndipo kapolo saposa mbuye wake. Choncho, mvetsetsani nthawi yomwe mukukhalamo ndipo yandikirani kwambiri m'mitima yanu kwa Yeshua HaMashiach, chifukwa ndikukuitanani kuti muyandikire kwambiri kwa ine mpingo wanga, thupi langa, ngakhale mkwatibwi wanga, ndipo ndikukuitanani kuti muyandikirebe mu nthawi zino musanatengedwe kupita kumwamba kuti mukathe kugonjetsa zinthu zonse mwa Khristu.

Ndipo mwadzidzidzi komanso mosayembekezereka tsopano mudzatengedwa kupita kumwamba, chifukwa ndikubwera pa ola lomwe simukuliyembekezera. Taonani! Ndikubwera ngati mbala usiku! Koma monga masiku a Nowa analiri, momwemonso kudzakhala kubwera kwa Mwana wa munthu. Pakuti monga m'masiku chigumula chisanafike, anali kudya ndi kumwa, kukwatira, ndi kukwatiwa, kufikira tsiku limene Nowa analowa m'Chombo, ndipo sanadziwe kanthu, kufikira chigumula chinabwera, ndi kuwatenga onse, momwemonso kudzakhala kubwera kwa Mwana wa munthu.

Choncho ndakulangizani okondedwa anga, kuti muchoke ku nkhawa za dziko lino, podziwa kuti kuchoka kwanu m'dziko loipali kuli pafupi. Komabe, zili choncho kuti muli ndi moyo woti mukhale, ndipo muyenera kupitiriza kufikira simuli, chifukwa pamene mutengedwa kupita kumwamba mosayembekezereka ndi dzanja langa lamphamvu la chipulumutso mayendedwe anu adzakhala atatha ndipo miyoyo yanu pa dziko lapansi idzakhala itamalizidwa pa nthawi ino. Choncho ndikukulangizani tiana twanga, khalani moyo wanu pomvera kotheratu mawu a Ambuye ndi Mpulumutsi wanu, ngakhale Khristu Yesu Ambuye wanu wokwezedwa ndipo ndidzakupangitsani kugonjetsa kotheratu!

Chifukwa chake, pamene mukukhala moyo wanu perekani zonse kwa ine mu kudzipereka kwa mtima wanu ndi cholinga chifukwa ine ndine wokwanira wanu mu zinthu zonse. Musaganize zonena m'mitima yanu kuti ndinu opambana ndi olemera ndi mphamvu ya manja anu, kapena ndi digiri ya mabungwe yomwe ambiri a inu mwaumbuli mumaisunga kuposa chidziwitso cha Yahweh, ngakhale mabodza ogulitsidwa kwa inu ngati choonadi m'maphunziro anu abodza omwe amamangidwa kotheratu pa mabodza, komabe inu mwaumbuli mumadzitamandira ndi luso lanu lomwe mwalipeza pamenepo. Ndipo choncho ndikukuuzaninso, kuti mutuluke pakati pawo ndikukhala osiyana mu zinthu zonse, apo ayi mudzagawana nawo m'maweruzo awo ndi miliri pamene chinthu choterocho sichofunikira ngakhale chifukwa nthawi zonse za tsopano ndikukuitanani ndi kukupemphani kuti mubwere kotheratu kwa ine m'chikondi changa kwa inu ana anga. Choncho chokani ku kunyada kopanda phindu komwe chidziwitso chimabweretsa!

Chifukwa chake, ndikukupatsani kuti mumvetsetse chilichonse chomwe mwapeza m'moyo uno chokhudza katundu wa dziko lino chaperekedwa ndi kukondwera kwabwino ndi chifuniro chabwino ndi chisomo cha Yahweh, WAMKULU NDINE, yemwe amafuna koposa zonse kuti mupambane ndi kukhala ndi thanzi labwino monga momwe moyo wanu ukupambanira. Komabe, musakhale opusa kuganiza kuti mwadzipanga nokha kapena kuti mu ulemu wanu wa dziko lapansi mwavomerezedwa pamaso pa Yahweh, WAMKULU NDINE kapena Yesu Khristu, chifukwa palibe thupi lomwe limalungamitsidwa pamaso pathu. Choncho mvetsetsani kuti omwe amadzikweza okha m'njira yotere adzataya zambiri chifukwa cha kunyada kwawo, komwe kumatsogolera chiwonongeko chachikulu.

Chifukwa cha inu nokha, ndikukupemphani kuti mutuluke m'dziko lino ndi makhalidwe ake onse osapembedza a kudzilungamitsa, kudzipanga, zifukwa zabodza zosapembedza ndi kudzitamandira kwa thupi. Chifukwa, zoonadi, zoonadi, zikadakhala kuti chisomo cha Yahweh, WAMKULU NDINE, sichinatsanulidwe m'chikondi chake chosatha kwa inu, ngakhale pamene chikondi chake chosatha ndi kuwala kwake kwawala m'mitima yanu ndi miyoyo yanu pamaso pa Yesu Khristu: nonse mukanakhala ngati Sodomu ndi Gomora ndipo mukanakhala mulibe muzu kapena nthambi, choncho kudzitamandira kwanu kukhale mwa Khristu. Komabe, tsoka limenelo silanu okondedwa anga, choncho, m'chikondi chanu kwa ine mverani mawu anga kuti mukathe kugonjetsa zinthu zonse chifukwa ngati mumvera mawu anga, ndidzakupangitsani kugonjetsa!

Zili choncho, ndipo zidzakhala choncho, kuti kwa ogonjetsa anga kuli mphoto yambiri, ngakhale kwa omwe agonjetsa mofanana ndi momwe ine ndagonjetsera, ndipo mphoto yawo idzakhala yaikulu kwambiri. Komabe, ambiri a okhulupirika anga mwa Khristu akuyesedwa ndi kugonetsedwa, monga ngati kubwerera kwa akufa! Ngakhale pamaso pa malamulo anga omwe ndinapatsa Paulo zaka zambiri zapitazo m'kalata yake yopita kwa Aefeso, ndipo analemba choncho, ndipo ndikukumbutsani okondedwa anga: Chifukwa chake akuti, dzuka iwe wogona, ndipo uka kwa akufa, ndipo Khristu adzakuunikira. Choncho samalani kuti muyende mosamala, osati ngati opusa, koma ngati anzeru, mukuwombola nyengo: chifukwa masiku ndi oipa. Chifukwa chake, musakhale opanda nzeru, koma mvetsetsani chifuniro cha Ambuye chomwe chili.

Kodi mukumvetsetsa chifuniro cha Ambuye wanu, tiana twanga? Kodi ndicho cholinga chanu chonse? Kapena mumadzitumikira nokha mu kukhulupirika kwa mtima wanu, kufunafuna chuma chambiri kuti muwonjezere m'matumba anu tsiku lililonse? Kodi mwayika mitima yanu kutumikira makampani, kapena magulu a anthu ochimwa chifukwa cha ndalama zonyansa, ngakhale pa nthawi yomwe ndalama zomwe anthu amasonkhanitsa ndi kusunga mwachimo kwa iwo eni posachedwapa zidzalengezedwa kukhala zopanda phindu pamaso pawo? Kapena mwapitiriza kumvera mawu anga ndi kulingalira mosamala Mawu aliwonse omwe ndikukupatsani chifukwa cha chipulumutso chanu? Ngati mupitiriza kumvera mawu anga ndidzakupangitsani kugonjetsa zinthu zonse, choncho, musataye mtima!

Ndikukupemphanibe, inu nonse okhulupirika mwa Khristu ndi onse omwe adzakhala okhulupirika mwa Khristu kuti mupitirize kutembenukira kwa ine chifukwa ndikukuitanani kuti muyandikire kwambiri kwa ine kuti ubwenzi wathunthu wa chikondi changa kwa inu ukulimbikitseni ndi kukusungani mu zinthu zonse, choncho, musaope, kapena kutaya nthawi kudzidzudzula nokha. Choncho, pitirizani kukhala achangu m'chikondi chanu ndi kudzipereka kwa mitima yanu kwa Ambuye wanu Yesu Khristu, tiana twanga, chifukwa palibenso nthawi yotaya! Ndipo pamene lipenga lidzalira mudzakhala pakati pa anamwali opusa omwe asiyidwa ku moto wa woyenga, kapena mudzapezeka pakati pa anamwali anzeru omwe adzatengedwa kupita kumwamba ndi mkwati wawo nthawi ina iliyonse tsopano. Kodi mudzakhala uti ndipo mudzaima kuti pamene ndidzaonekera mwadzidzidzi nthawi ina iliyonse tsopano?

Kodi mudzandilolabe kuti ndikupatseni mafuta a nyale zanu kuti mudzaime pa mpando wanga woweruza? Kodi mudzadulabe zingwe zanu moyenera pomvera malangizo anga? Kapena mudzasankha mwaumbuli kukhalabe odzikwanira nokha kuima mu chilungamo chanu chomwe mwadzipangira nokha ndikukhalabe oima pakati pa anamwali opusa? Ngakhale pamene ndakukonzerani zabwino, ngati mutamvera kotheratu mawu anga. Choncho, ndikukulangizani okhulupirika anga, pitirizani kubwera kwa ine m'chikondi chonse ndi kumvera kuti ndikukwezeni kwambiri kumwamba komwe kuli pamwamba kuti muwale ngati nyenyezi za mlengalenga chifukwa cha zinthu zomwe mwazichita momvera potumikira Ambuye wanu Wosatha wa Chilungamo!

Choncho, ndikufunsa nonse omwe mukudziwa mawu anga, ngakhale inu omwe mwaitanidwa ndi dzina, ndi omwe mumaitana pa dzina langa: Kodi mwadzuka kotheratu? Kodi maso anu atseguka kotheratu ku dziko lapansi monga momwe liliri lero? Mvetsetsani kuti dziko lomwe mukukhalamo tsopano silofanana ndi momwe linaliri miyezi isanu ndi umodzi yapitayo, ndipo dziko lapansi likupitiriza kusintha kwambiri pamaso panu, ngakhale ambiri a inu mukupitirizabe kulingalira zizindikiro ndi zozizwitsa mu kusakhulupirira kopanda chikhulupiriro kufunafuna kubwerera kukagona. Choncho, dzifufuzeni nokha ndi mapulani anu ndi Ambuye wanu wamuyaya kuti nditsogolere mapazi anu ndi kukusungani ku zoipa m'chikondi changa ndi chisomo.

Zili choncho, mabodza a chinjoka tsopano adzaza dziko lapansi ngakhale kuposa momwe analiri chinjoka chisanaponyedwe pansi; chifukwa chinjoka chaponyedwa pansi, chaponyedwa pansi pa dziko lapansi ndipo chamangidwa pansi pa mlengalenga chifukwa cha chiweruzo, ndipo chikudziwa kuti chili ndi nthawi yochepa ndipo mkwiyo wake ndi waukulu! Ndipo chikubwera kudzachita nkhondo ndipo nkhondo zake zidzakhala zazikulu, komabe njoka yakale imeneyo sidzakhala ndi ukulu uliwonse koma sulufule woyaka ndi kugubuduzika nthawi zonse yemwe amayaka malawi otentha kwambiri a chilango.

Ndipo choncho ndikubwera kwa inu okondedwa anga kukulimbikitsani kuti mupitirize kuyandikira kwambiri ku mtima wanga mwa kumvera kwanu Mawu anga, ndipo pa makoma a mtima wanga mayina anu onse athunthu alembedwa m'chikondi changa kwa inu mmenemo, chomwe chili chosatha ndi chamuyaya. Inde, m'chikondi changa kwa inu, (Ndikudziwa aliyense wa inu bwino kuposa momwe mumadzidziwira nokha) Ndikukusungani pafupi kwambiri ndi mtima wanga nthawi zonse ndipo makamaka mu nthawi zanu za masautso ndi mayesero omwe mukukumana nawo ndithu. (Ndasonkhanitsa misozi yanu yonse m'botolo langa la misozi kuti ndikubwezereni pa uliwonse) Ndipo mu nkhondo zomwe zikuchitika mu nkhondo yopitiriza ya mbewu iyi, yomwe yapambanidwa mwa Khristu ndipo yomwe ikulimbana ndi omwe adagwa; mvetsetsani, kuti nkhondo zanu zapambanidwa kotheratu mwa Ambuye wanu wosatha! Choncho, pumulani mwa Ambuye wanu wamuyaya, ngakhale Khristu Yesu ndipo lekani ntchito zanu zopanda phindu ndipo pumulani kotheratu mmenemo, ndipo malizitsani ntchito za chilungamo zomwe ndakupatsani kuti muchite kuti mupambane adani anu ndipo ndidzakupatsani Sabata! Chifukwa chake, mvetsetsani masiku omwe mukukhala chifukwa palibe aliyense wa inu yemwe adakhalapo masiku ano omwe abwera pa dziko lapansi mu nthawi yotsiriza iyi, ngakhale akuwoneka mwachinyengo ngati ofanana ndi omwe mwawadziwa. Chifukwa dziko lapansi lili mu kusintha ndipo zoonadi dziko lapansi likusinthidwa, ndipo zoonadi, zoonadi dziko lapansi lidzapitiriza kusintha mwamphamvu malinga ndi zolinga ndi mapulani a Yahweh.

Mvetsetsani kuti nthawi ya kusintha kwakukulu yabwera pa dziko lapansi, ngakhale nthawi yomwe dziko lapansi likukonzedwanso ndi kukonzedwanso ndi zochitika ndi masoka ndi maweruzo omwe akubwera mu kubwezeretsedwa kwa dziko lapansi komwe kunalipo m'masiku a Nowa! Ndipo zochitika ndi maweruzo zidzachitika monga momwe zinanenedweratu ndi aneneri akale omwe adzachitika kotheratu pa nthawi ya masautso a Yakobo. Chifukwa chake mvetsetsani, kuti maweruzo ambiri ayamba kale kugwa ngakhale mu nthawi iyi ya kusintha chifukwa zikanakhala zosatheka kuti zinthu zichitike m'njira ina iliyonse.

Ndipo choncho kwalembedwa, ndipo choncho zidzachitika, pamene ndidzasonkhanitsa okhulupirika mwa Khristu posachedwa ndi mosayembekezereka tsopano pamene woletsa adzachotsedwa, ndipo madzi oundana adzagwa, ndipo zisindikizo zidzatsegulidwa, ndipo malipenga adzalira! Kuti mboni zanga ziwiri zidzauka zomwe zidzalamula moto kugwa kuchokera kumwamba, zomwe zidzakantha dziko lapansi ndi miliri yamtundu uliwonse nthawi zambiri momwe zikufunira, ndipo zomwe zidzapangitsa kuti isagwe mvula pa dziko lapansi m'masiku a ulosi wawo pa lamulo lawo, ndipo ambiri adzakanabe kulapa! Mvetsetsani kuti adzaima motsutsana ndi chinjoka, ndi chilombo ndi mneneri wonyenga ndipo ngati chifuniro cha munthu aliyense chili kuwapweteka ndiye kuti munthuyo ayenera kupwetekedwa mofananamo. Ngati chifuniro cha munthu aliyense chili kuwapha munthuyo ayenera kuphedwa mofananamo. Izi ndi zoikapo nyale zanga ziwiri ndi mitengo yanga ya azitona iwiri ndipo zinthu zambiri, zambiri adzaziulula pa nthawi ya kuwululidwa kwawo ndipo maumboni awo adzalembedwa kwamuyaya.

Izi ndi nthawi zomwe mukukhala, tiana twanga, ndipo izi ndi nthawi zomwe muyenera kupitiriza kupirira mwa kuyandikira kwambiri mu chifuniro changa pa miyoyo yanu chifukwa mmenemo muli chitetezo chanu ndi chipulumutso. Mvetsetsani, kuti munakula ngati ana a ng'ombe a m'khola mu nthawi zanga za chisomo ndi chifundo zomwe zinaperekedwa chifukwa cha chipulumutso cha mafuko otayika omwe anabalalitsidwa pakati pa mitundu ndipo potero, mitundu inadalitsidwa ndi kuthiriridwa m'chikondi, chifundo, ndi chisomo ndipo ambiri anapeza chipulumutso potero. Koma tsopano nthawi zasintha kwambiri, ndipo ndikukulamulani kuti muyende ndi nzeru ZAZIKULU, ndipo kuti mukhale anzeru ngati njoka ndi opanda vuto ngati nkhunda chifukwa nthawi zino ndi zoopsa mwachinyengo.

Chifukwa chinjoka tsopano chamangidwa pa dziko lapansi ndipo omwe adagwa akukula mphamvu masekondi aliwonse, komabe alibe mphamvu kotheratu kuima motsutsana ndi mawu anga amphamvu ndi mphamvu zanga zonse! Choncho, ndikuwadzudzula ndi kuwaletsa chifukwa cha inu kuposa momwe mungamvetsetsere pa nthawi ino ndipo ndayika zinthu zonse za ufumu wa adani pansi pa mapazi anu kuti muthe kuwapondereza ngati kuli kofunikira. Choncho, musawaope chifukwa ndagonjetsa zinthu zonse, ngakhale ufumu wonse wa Satana; ndipo inunso mudzagonjetsa mofananamo, ngati mukhala okhulupirika ku Mawu anga a malangizo omwe ndapereka ndipo ndikupereka kuti mumvere.

Musadabwe, okondedwa anga, ngakhale pamene omwe adagwa akudziulula okha ku dziko lapansi, musadabwe ngakhale pang'ono! Chifukwa masiku a Nowa abwera! Ngakhale masiku omwe Satana ndi angelo ake aponyeredwa pa dziko lapansi, osabwereranso kumwamba komwe tsopano athamangitsidwa kwamuyaya mu kugonjetsedwa kotheratu. Ngakhale masiku omwe oyang'anira tsopano amasulidwa m'ndende zawo momwe anamangidwa ndi kusungidwa osiyana wina ndi mzake mu mazunzo akulu chifukwa cha milandu yawo yonyansa yomwe anachita motsutsana ndi Yahweh ndi chilengedwe chake m'masiku akale ndipo mu kugonjetsedwa kotheratu anamangidwa ku kupanduka kwawo ndi malumbiro omwe analumbira okha kuti akwaniritse. Komabe, malumbiro awo adzakwaniritsidwa kwamuyaya pamene adzamangidwa ndi kuponyeredwa ku moto wa chilango m'nyanja yaikulu ya sulufule woyaka komwe mphutsi yawo sifa.

Ngakhale masiku, kachiwiri, omwe akubwerera ngati m'masiku a Nowa pamene oyang'anira anatsikira pa Phiri la Herimoni m'masiku a Yaredi. Ngakhale masiku omwe abwerera komwe omwe adagwa tsopano akugwira ntchito mwachindunji limodzi ndi mafumu a dziko lapansi ndi abwanamkubwa awo a dziko lapansi omwe amabisala kumbuyo kwawo omwe akugwirizana ndi mapulani awo omwe akufuna kubweretsa chiwonongeko chachikulu ndi masoka ndi nkhondo posachedwa pamene woletsa adzachotsedwa, pamene kuipa kwawo kudzakhalako kopanda chiletso kotheratu. Musadabwe, okondedwa anga, ngakhale pamene omwe adagwa akuunikira mlengalenga pansi pa mlengalenga ndi magetsi awo, ndi mipira yawo, monga momwe tsopano akudzionetsera okha m'maonekedwe omwe amatenga ndipo makina awo tsopano ali omasuka kuwuluka ndipo chinyengo chambiri ndi chisokonezo adzuka kuti apangitse pa nkhope ya dziko lapansi ndi matekinoloje awo, ngakhale pa nthawi ya kutha kwa zowawa za kubereka, zomwe mwadzidzidzi ndi mosayembekezereka zidzatha mu tsoka posachedwapa tsopano. Ndipo choncho munthu wachibadwa amadabwa ndi kulingalira ndi kufotokoza zinthu zauzimu zomwe tsopano zakhala zachibadwa kachiwiri.

Inde, anthu mopanda chikhulupiriro amatsutsa mphamvu ya Yahweh yomwe inasonyezedwa ndi cholinga choti anthu adzuke m'chilungamo ndi kupeza chipulumutso. Musakhale ndi gawo kapena mbali m'malingaliro awo opanda chikhulupiriro ndipo pitirizani kulemekeza Yahweh chifukwa cha ntchito zake zachipulumutso zomwe zaperekedwa m'makonzedwe ake amuyaya a chipulumutso cha Adamu pamaso pa Yesu Khristu, yemwe ndi Adamu wotsiriza.

Choncho ndikukupatsani kuti mumvetsetse: mosasamala kanthu kuti ndi chinthu chiti chomwe chimayambitsa kusintha kumwamba kapena padziko lapansi, kapena kusintha kwa mlengalenga, kapena aliyense amene amasintha chilengedwe cha Yahweh mwanjira ina iliyonse: Ndikukulamulani kuti muzindikire mokwanira kuti Yahweh ndi wolamulira wamkulu pa chilengedwe chake ndipo chilichonse chomwe chimachitika chimachitika kokha ndi chilolezo cha Yahweh, ndipo ndi chidziwitso chake chonse, komanso kukwaniritsa makonzedwe ake amuyaya a chipulumutso kwa mwana wake wosokera Adamu. Mvetsetsani, kuti Yahweh amalola osalungama, a mitima yowuma, ndi osamvera kuti akhale ndi moyo wawo wauchimo kuti adzaze machimo awo mpaka pamwamba ndipo chiweruzo chawo chomwe chikubwera sadzachithawa, pokhapokha atabwera kwa Yesu Khristu mu kulapa kwenikweni pamene nthawi ilipo.

Ngakhale zili choncho, Yahweh amalola osalungama kuchimwa kuti mu chiweruzo alipidwe mokwanira polandira malipiro onse a ntchito zawo zoyipa pamitu pawo mpaka muyaya ndi kosatha m'malawi a chilango, ngakhale pamene chinthu choterocho sichofunikira ndipo chikhoza kupewedwa mu chipulumutso mwa kumvera chilungamo. Mu chisomo cha Yahweh, ndi chifundo chake m'makonzedwe ake amuyaya a chipulumutso kwa Adamu; Yahweh safuna kuti munthu aliyense atayike m'malawi amoto, komabe, anthu onse apatsidwa ufulu wosankha choyipa, kapena kusankha chilungamo pamaso pa Yesu Khristu yemwe adzalenga mwatsopano onse omwe abwera ku chipulumutso monga munthu mmodzi watsopano, ndipo adzakhala ndi moyo kosatha.

Ndipo motero omwe adagwa adzamasulidwa mokwanira mu chiweruzo pa okhala padziko lapansi omwe ndi osamvera pokana dzina la Yeshua HaMashiach yemwe ndi Yesu Khristu! Choncho, mvetsetsani kuti onse omwe akana Yesu Khristu monga Ambuye, kapena omwe amakhala mopanda chikhulupiriro mu kusamvera malamulo anga adzasiyidwa pansi pa ulamuliro wawo wankhanza ndi kuponderezana komwe akufuna kufafaniza Adamu kotheratu padziko lapansi; m'makonzedwe awo omwe adawapanga kwanthawi yayitali omwe akufuna kuba dziko lapansi m'manja mwanga amphamvu, ngakhale zolinga zomwe sizingatheke kuti azikwaniritse mu misala yawo yopanduka.

Komabe, anthu akupitirizabe kuyenda mwadala m'msampha wawo, ngakhale pamene akufotokoza zizindikiro zomwe zaperekedwa za chiweruzo chake chomwe chikubwera chomwe akukanabe kukhulupirira kuti chikubwera m'malingaliro ake opanda chikhulupiriro operekedwa ndi maphunziro ake abodza omwe malingaliro ake achititsidwa khungu kotheratu ku dziko lenileni lomwe akukhalamo, ngakhale dziko lomwe lidalengedwa ndi Yahweh WAMKULU NDINE, yemwe kukhalapo kwake anthu akufuna kukana m'maphunziro awo abodza opangidwa ndi a Jesuit a kuphunzira motsutsana ndi kuphunzira.
