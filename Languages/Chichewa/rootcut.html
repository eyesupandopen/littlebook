Kudulidwa pa Muzu

Tsegulani maso anu kuti muwerenge Mawu anga ndikukhazikitsa mitima ndi maganizo anu pa Mawu anga, Inu Ana a Adonai-YHVH ndikumvetsetsa mkhalidwe wanu ndi zopanda chilungamo zazikulu zomwe zachitika kudzera m'mapulani awo akale obisika. Mvetsetsani phokoso la kulankhula kwanga ndipo mundipatse makutu anu kuti ndiwadzaze ndi phokoso la Mawu anga Oyera a chilungamo omwe akugwirizana ndi mafunde a Yeshua HaMashiach, ngakhale iye amene nthawi zonse amachita chifuniro cha Adonai-YHVH mwangwiro!

Nditsegulireni mitima yanu yotetezedwa yomwe muyenera kuyibisa ku dziko lino lomwe likufuna kukuphatikizani mu dongosolo lomaliza la chilombochi, kuti monga momwe zilili pansi, zikhalenso choncho kumwamba. Ndipo ndi akachisi otembenuzidwa awa, ana a kusamvera, omwe amatembenuzira zinthu mozondoka ndikufuna kusokoneza dziko lomwe mukukhalamo, ngakhale kukusokeretsani nonse ndi zomwe amati ndi zosangalatsa zomwe mumazitsata mwadyera pamene mukulipira kuti musandutse machimo anu kukhala a digito.

Ndi zotsatira zonse zomwe zikuyembekezeredwa mwa chilolezo chanu ndi kutenga mbali kwanu kuti maganizo anu adzalandidwa zida mu mkhalidwe wongokhala, kumene dopamine imalimbikitsidwa ndi kutulutsidwa ndi mafunde awo kuti musangalale, nthawi yonseyi mukuyang'ana zithunzi zawo zabodza zomwe zimayambitsa zoipa m'mitima ndi m'maganizo mwanu mosalekeza. Mwa kubwereza uku, onse omwe amayang'ana mopanda nzeru magalasi awo akuda, omwe ndi zowonera, amakopeka kuti akhale ndi moyo m'machimo awo opangidwa mu chinyengo champhamvu chomwe chidzafika pachimake mu chinyengo chachikulu chomwe chidzakolola m'chiweruzo onse omwe amalumbira kuti aphatikizidwe mu chilombo chomaliza chomwe chikuwuka cha mitundu ya anthu akunja.
