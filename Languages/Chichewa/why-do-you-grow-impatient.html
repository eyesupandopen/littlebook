Chifukwa Chiyani Mukusowa Mtima?

Bwerani, weramitsani mitima yanu ndi makutu anu ndikumva Mawu a Ambuye wanu, Mpingo wanga ngakhale Mkwatibwi wanga. Chifukwa chiyani mwadzidzidzi mwachotsedwa pa chiyembekezo chanu cha kuonekera kwanga, ngati kuti sindikubwera? Ngati kuti zolengeza za Yahweh zikhoza kusinthidwa? Kodi Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya, Yahweh, waiwala malonjezo ake? Kodi ndaiwala mawu anga ndi malonjezo anga? Zoonadi, zoonadi, ndikuti ayi! Ndipo onse amene amapereka utsiru kwa Yahweh, Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya, ndi kwa ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, Ambuye Wamkulu wa Ambuye ndi Mfumu ya Mafumu, ayenera kulapa nthawi yomweyo. Pemphani chikhululukiro ana anga ndikupitiriza kudzikonzekeretsa pakuti ndidzaonekera mwadzidzidzi, ndipo mudzadabwa ndi mmene chochitika chachikulu chotsatira mu dongosolo la Yahweh la chiwombolo cha anthu chidzachitikira mofulumira, pakuti chidzachitika mwadzidzidzi ndi mosayembekezereka pakuti chatero chalengezedwa. Pakuti chidzachitika kuti ndidzaukitsa akufa mwa Khristu poyamba ndipo inu amene muli amoyo ndi otsala mudzatengedwa kukakumana nane m'mitambo, ndipo zidzachitika m'kuthwanima kwa diso.

Chifukwa chiyani mukusowa mtima? Chifukwa chiyani ena a inu mukudziyesa ngati zinthu zabwerera m'mene zinalili? N'zoonekeratu kuti palibe kubwerera ku njira zakale, pakuti dziko lomwe lilipo tsopano lidzakumana ndi tsoka lalikulu ndipo lidzasinthidwa kwamuyaya, ndipo lidzasinthanso panthawi ya nsautso ya Yakobo ndipo dziko lapansi lidzakhala ngati m'masiku a Nowa. Pamene woletsa achotsedwa sipadzakhala kanthu koletsa magulu oipa, ngakhale magulu ankhondo a omwe adagwa omwe adzaukira zonse zomwe zili zanga, pakuti Yahweh wapatsa chinjoka mphamvu yochita nkhondo ndi oyera mtima ndi kuwagonjetsa. Ambiri adzabwera kwa ine kudzera m'chiwonongeko cha thupi lawo, ngakhale thupi lomwe amalikonda ndipo likuwamangabe. Pakuti onse amene adzaitana pa dzina la Ambuye adzapulumutsidwa, ndipo kapolo saposa mbuye wake.

Ngakhale ndapereka chipulumutso ku chiwonongeko chomwe chikubwera kwa okhulupirika, ambiri sanakonzekere koma adzapulumutsidwa ndikutumikira cholinga chachikulu mu Ufumu wanga womwe ukubwera, pakuti ndidzalamulira kuchokera pampando wachifumu wa Davide ndipo ndimagawana pampando wachifumu wa Atate wanga, ndipo inu mudzagawana pampando wanga wachifumu okhulupirika anga. Kwa omwe sanakonzekere adzavutika kwambiri chifukwa cha dzina langa, ndipo adzavutika monga ine, Yesu wa ku Nazarete, ndinavutika kuti nditsimikizire chipulumutso cha anthu ndipo kudzera m'kupachikidwa kwanga ndasiya chitsanzo cha kumvera mpaka imfa kuti onse atsatire. Ndipo kudzera m'chisautso chachikulu makamu ambiri adzapulumutsidwa ndikubwera ku mphoto yaikulu mwa ine Khristu Yesu, ndikugwiranso ntchito kwa Yahweh m'kachisi wake. Mphoto yaikulu ikuyembekezera onse amene aitanira pa dzina langa, ndipo omwe aitanira pa dzina langa mpaka mapeto adzapulumutsidwa, ngakhale omwe sakonda miyoyo yawo mpaka mapeto. Pakuti dziko lino likuwonongeka ndipo lidzawonongedwa ndi kulangidwa kotheratu Ufumu wanga wa chilungamo usanakhazikitsidwe padziko lapansi.

Choncho musataye mtima Ana anga, pakuti malonjezo anga ndi inde ndi ameni ndipo ndidzatenga mkwatibwi wanga kupita kumwamba monga mwalonjezedwa, ndipo palibe munthu mmodzi amene adaikidwiratu chipulumutso chamuyaya ndi Yahweh maziko a dziko lapansi asanaikidwe, adzakwatulidwa m'manja mwanga amuyaya ndipo chipulumutso chanu ndi chotetezeka mwa ine, Khristu Yesu, kokha khalani okhulupirika ndikukhala mwa Khristu mpaka mapeto. Ichi chakhala chofunikira nthawi zonse kuchokera kwa Yahweh, kuti munthu apezeke Wokhulupirika m'njira zake. Ndipo ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndimabweretsa njira za Yahweh ndipo omwe amaphunzira za ine amaphunzira njira za Yahweh, pakuti ndine mmodzi mu umodzi ndi cholinga ndi Atate wanga. Posachedwa mudzakhala amodzi ndi ife, kodi sikunalembedwe kuti mwapangidwa kukhala gawo la Umulungu wamuyaya, pakuti chotero ndi cholowa chanu ndipo chotero ndi mphoto yanga kwa ana a Mulungu okhulupirika omwe adapezeka okhulupirika mwa ine. Pakuti mwa ine, Khristu Yesu, mukukhala chidzalo chonse cha Umulungu m'thupi, ndipo mudzagawana m'cholowa changa chifukwa cha kukhulupirika kwanu, ndipo mudzagawana pampando wanga wachifumu monga Mafumu ndi Ansembe ndipo Ufumu wanga ulibe mapeto.

Choncho khalani olimba kwa ine m'masekondi omaliza a nthawi ino, ndikumvetsetsa kuti nthawi yatha. Inde, nthawi yatha kuti Babulo Wachinsinsi alape. Nthawi zanga za chisomo ndi chifundo zatha, ngakhale chifundo ndi chisomo zili ndi ine mpaka kalekale. Tsopano kukubwera chiweruzo chachikulu padziko lapansi, ndi mitundu ngakhale dziko. Koma chiweruzo sichidzagwa chomwe chidzafafaniza amoyo onse, koma chidzagwa ndikuunjikana. Chiweruzo pa chiweruzo, chidindo pambuyo pa chidindo, lipenga pa lipenga, mbale pa mbale, tsoka pa tsoka, ndi mliri pa mliri. Ndipo zotsatira zake anthu ambiri, ambiri, ambiri adzabwera ku chipulumutso mwa ine, Yesu wa ku Nazarete, pakuti ndine Mwanawankhosa wa Paskha wangwiro wa nthawi zonse, ndipo palibenso chosowa cha nsembe. Koma kwa inu, Mkwatibwi wanga wokhulupirika, nthawi zodikira zatha.

Sipadzakhala kuchedwa kwinanso, ndipo palibenso kudikira, ndipo mudzabwera ku mphoto yanu yonse ndi cholowa mwa ine. Ndipo ngakhale ena adzataya, diso silinaone kapena khutu kumva, kapena kulowa mumtima mwa anthu, zinthu zomwe Yahweh wakonzera omwe amamukonda. Ndipo zili choncho okondedwa anga okhulupirika, mwasonyeza chikondi chanu kwa ine, Khristu Yesu, ndi chikondi chanu kwa Yahweh chifukwa mwamvera malamulo anga ndi chikhulupiriro m'tsiku loipali. Ngakhale simunandione ndi maso anu, kapena kuona ulemerero wamuyaya wa Yahweh ndi maso anu mwakhala okhulupirika mwa ine mpaka mapeto, chifukwa chake pali mphoto yaikulu yosungidwa kwa inu kumwamba, choncho musataye mtima!

Pitirizani moleza mtima, Ana anga, pakuti ndikuyandikira. Musamvere onyoza omwe amakunyozani chifukwa kunyoza kwawo posachedwa kudzasanduka chisoni chachikulu, ndipo ambiri omwe amanyoza adzakhala chete pamene miyoyo yawo ikufika kumapeto. Pakuti zotero ndi ziweruzo zomwe zikubwera. Kwa omwe akana manja anga otambasulidwa ndikuwona magazi anga otetezera ngati chinthu chosayera ndikudziyesa osayenera moyo wamuyaya mu Ufumu wanga womwe ukubwera, ngakhale omwewo adzalandira mdima ndi imfa zomwe adazikonda kwambiri ndikuzilakalaka ndipo ambiri adzagwa mofunitsitsa ku imfa yachiwiri.

Pakuti m'chikondi chachikulu cha Atate wanga, ngakhale Yahweh Mulungu wamuyaya pakuti palibe wina wofanana naye, sadzakakamiza ngakhale mmodzi wa zolengedwa zake kumutsatira ndi kumukonda. Onse apatsidwa ufulu wosankha ndipo onse adzalandira mphoto ya chisankho chawo. Pakuti chotero ndi malamulo a Yahweh. Omwe amakonda zoipa, omwe amakonda zosangalatsa za tchimo ndikukonda zoipa zomwe machimo awo amayambitsa kwa ena omwe amavutika mopanda chilungamo chifukwa cha zilakolako zawo ndi njira zawo zopotoka adzalandira njira zawo zoipa kubwerera m'zifuwa zawo ndipo chotero chilango chawo chidzakhala ndipo tchimo lililonse lidzabwezeredwa kwamuyaya. Koma kwa omwe amavomereza pangano lamtendere la Yahweh, ngakhale ine Yesu wa ku Nazarete, adzalandira moyo wosatha ndi mtendere ndi mphoto ndi chikondi ndi chimwemwe chamuyaya pamaso panga ndi pamaso pa Yahweh mwiniwake, pakuti posachedwa malo okhala Yahweh adzakhala pakati pa anthu pakuti chotero chalembedwa. Ndipo ndikuyandikira kuti ndikukwatuleni kupita kuchipinda chachifumu cha Yahweh mwiniwake, komwe mwakhala kale, ndipo akorona anu akukuyembekezerani.

Musakhulupirire mabodza awo ana anga, ndikubwera kudzakutengani, kokha khalani olimba sekondi ina kapena ziwiri, pakuti kuonekera kwanga kuli pafupi ndipo kudzachitika sekondi iliyonse tsopano, ndipo ndidzaposa ziyembekezo zanu. Dzikonzetseni ndikudziyeretsa m'magazi anga otetezera. Pitirizani miyoyo yanu yolapa ndikupitiriza kupachika njira zanu zathupi, pakuti sizochokera kwa ine ndipo palibe chinthu choipa chomwe chidzalowa mu Ufumu wanga ndipo palibe chinthu choipa chomwe chidzaima pamaso pa Yahweh. Ndine nsembe yanu yamuyaya ndipo ndakupangani opanda banga kapena khwinya ndipo palibe banga pa okhulupirika anga chifukwa magazi anga amakuyeretsani mosalekeza pamene mukuulula machimo anu m'kulapa, ngakhale thupi lanu lili loipa kotheratu.

Bwerani kwa ine ndi mtima wotseguka ndipo ndidzakuwonetsani mwachikondi zofooka zanu kuti muulule machimo anu kwa ine, ndipo potero ndidzakuyeretsani ndipo ndidzapitiriza kukupangani kukhala athunthu monga muli cholengedwa chatsopano mwa ine, Khristu Yesu. Ndipo zoonadi, zoonadi onse omwe asindikizidwa ndi mzimu wanga woyera wa lonjezo, kaya Myuda kapena Wamitundu, (kaya kapolo kapena mfulu) adzapangidwanso kwathunthu ngati munthu mmodzi watsopano mwa ine Khristu Yesu ndipo adzaima mu ufulu wolemekezeka mpaka kalekale! Pakuti ndine mzimu wopatsa moyo, pakuti chotero Atate wanga wandipanga. Ndipo ndine amene NDINE wamkulu wandipanga mpaka kalekale. Ndipo tsopano inunso mudzapangidwa ngati ine m'thupi lolemekezeka, thupi lopangidwa ngati mphoto lomwe lidzaphatikiza zomwe mwakwaniritsa pakuima kwanu kwa ine m'tsiku loipali. Palibe ngakhale imodzi mwa khama lanu yomwe idzakhala yopanda mphoto: Pakuti Mulungu sali wosalungama, kuti aiwale ntchito yanu, ndi ntchito yachikondi, yomwe munasonyeza kudzina lake, pakuti mwatumikira Oyera mtima, ndipo mukutumikirabe.

Miyoyo yanu yeniyeni yabisika mwa ine, ngakhale mpulumutsi wanu ndi Mesiya. Mpaka kalekale mudzakhala ndi ine, ndipo mudzakhala amalingaliro amodzi ndi mtima umodzi ndi ine Yeshua HaMashiach ngakhale ndi Yahweh, pakuti El Shaddai Wamkulu, ngakhale Mulungu Wamphamvuyonse adzakhala zonse mu zonse pakuti zolinga zake zidzakwaniritsidwa mwa inu, oitanidwa anga, osankhidwa ndi okhulupirika. Ndikubwera kudzakutengani, Mkwatibwi wanga, mwakonzeka? Kodi muli ndi mafuta m'nyale zanu? Kodi mwasamba mokwanira m'magazi anga otetezera? Kodi mwasiya kusambitsana mapazi?

Dikirani! Onani! Mverani! Dikirani kuonekera kwanga ndikulimbikitsana chifukwa Satana akhoza kubisa kuonekera kwanga ngati chinthu choterocho chikanakhala chotheka. Yang'anani kwa ine, woyambitsa ndi womalizitsa wa chikhulupiriro chanu, pakuti m'kuthwanima kwa diso mudzasinthidwa ndikupangidwa ngati ine mpaka kalekale. Mverani Mawu anga ndikukhala okonzeka kuonekera kwanga ndipo koposa zonse khalani omvera chifukwa cha Chikondi kwa Yahweh ndi kwa ine kusunga Mawu anga oyera m'kuyera kwa mtima, podziwa kuti chiwombolo chanu sichikuyandikiranso koma chafika.

Ndine mmodzi mu umodzi ndi cholinga ndi Atate wanga, ndipo posachedwa mudzakhala amodzi ndi ife.

Ndine Mwanawankhosa wa Paskha wangwiro wa nthawi zonse.
