Tengani Maliro a Babulo Wachinsinsi!

Imvani mawu anga ndipo imvani liwu la wofuula m'chipululu! Ndikuitana, ndikuitana, kodi mundimva? Gwirani malilime anu odzaza ndi tchimo mukhale chete ndipo gwadani pansi polapa ndikumva liwu langa lamuyaya pamene mukanatha, pakuti chikondi changa pa inu ndi chachikulu, ndipo ndine amene ndikugwetsa misozi yambiri m'kusweka mtima kwakukulu chifukwa cha zomwe zidzachitikire awo amene amanyalanyaza liwu langa. Pakuti ngakhale mu mphindi yomalizayi onse amene ayankha akuitanidwa ku moyo wosatha. Choncho ndivomerezeni monga Ambuye wanu, pakuti ndine Yesu wa ku Nazarete, ndipo ndine amene Mulungu wamuyaya wandituma kudzakuwombolani tsoka ndi chiweruzo zisanafike. Chifukwa chiyani mukudziyesa osayenera moyo wosatha? Kodi mudzasankha malawi amoto achiwonongeko m'malo mwa minda yamtendere yachonde kumene mudzapeza msipu wamuyaya?

Pakuti monga momwe wochita zoipa amene anapachikidwa pamodzi ndi ine pa Kalvare adzakhala ndi ine m'paradaiso, momwemonso ndidzakupulumutsani mpaka pomaliza ngati mundipanga kukhala Ambuye wanu tsopano! PALIBE NTHAWI YOTAYA. Pakuti ndinafera kunja kwa zipata za Yerusalemu pamodzi ndi ochimwa pamene ndinatenga malo anu m'chiweruzo, ndipo mudzapulumutsidwa ngati mutembenukira kwa ine m'kulapa pakali pano. Ndikunena kwa inu mwachoonadi, mwachoonadi: Mawu awa sanapeze makutu anu mwangozi, ndipo posachedwapa kudzakhala chiwonongeko chachikulu ndipo ambiri sadzapulumuka ndipo kwamuyaya kukana kwawo kudzamveka m'moto wamuyaya wosungiridwa onse amene asankha kumamatira ku machimo awo ndikukhalabe ogwirizana ndi akugwa.

Kulowa m'chipanduko cha Satana, ngakhale mu umbuli wakhungu, ndiko kutenga nawo mbali mofunitsitsa m'machimo awo, ndipo onse amene achita izi adzanyamula chiweruzo chawo kwamuyaya. Kodi mudzalandira chopereka changa, podziwa kuti Mulungu anandiukitsa kwa akufa kuti ndisafe nso ndikudziwa kuti ndine Ambuye wamkulu wa Ambuye ndi Mfumu ya Mafumu? Lapani pamene mukanatha, pakuti Yehova ali wokonzeka kutseka chitseko cha chingalawa, ndipo palibe nthawi ndipo pa sekondi iliyonse ndidzakwatula okhulupirika kupita kumwamba, ngakhale onse amene apulumutsidwa ku mkwiyo mu nsembe yanga yopulumutsa nthawi zonse ndi yamuyaya. Choncho ndilengezeni, Yesu wa ku Nazarete monga Ambuye wanu ndipo dziwani kuti ndili ndi moyo kwamuyaya pakuti Mulungu wandiukitsa kwa akufa kuti ndisafe nso.

Ana anga, mverani ndipo pitirizani kugwada pansi m'kulapa kosalekeza ndikumva liwu la Ambuye wanu Yesu Khristu, ngakhale iye amene Yehova wamutuma monga mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu. Imvani Mawu anga, Mpingo wanga ngakhale Mkwatibwi wanga, Pakuti monga mkulu wa ansembe wanu wamuyaya wa dongosolo la Melikizedeki ndidadzipereka ndekha monga Paskha wanu wangwiro, ndipo ngakhale kupereka magazi anga opanda tchimo mpaka dontho lomaliza kuti muwomboledwe kwathunthu ndi kotheratu ku mkhalidwe wanu wamakono wakugwa ndipo chiwombolo chanu chathunthu chili pano, choncho kondwerani!

Ndipo onse amene andilandira ndi kuvomereza dzina langa lamuyaya monga Ambuye wawo adzakhala ndi moyo wosatha. Pakuti ndine Yesu wa ku Nazarete Ambuye wanu wamuyaya, ndipo ndapanga onse amene avomereza dzina langa mu nthawi izi za chisomo ndi chifundo kukhala ana amuyaya a Mulungu. Taonani, chikondi chotani chomwe Atate wakupatsani, kuti mutchedwe ana a Mulungu: chifukwa chake dziko lapansi silikudziwani, chifukwa silikundidziwa ine, Khristu Yesu Ambuye wa Ambuye ndi Mfumu ya Mafumu. Okondedwa, tsopano ndinu ana a Mulungu, koma sikunawonekere poyera zomwe mudzakhala: ndipo mukudziwa kuti pamene ndidzawonekera poyera, mudzakhala ngati ine: pakuti mudzandiona monga momwe ndilili.

Tiana tanga, mvetsetsani kuti ndidzawonekera poyera nthawi ina iliyonse tsopano popeza kuonekera kwanga m'mitambo kuli pano. Pakuti ine, Khristu Yesu, ndidzatsika kuchokera kumwamba ndi mfuu, ndi liwu la Mngelo wamkulu, ndi lipenga la Mulungu: ndipo akufa mwa Khristu adzauka choyamba. Kenako inu amene muli amoyo, ndikutsala, mudzakwatulidwa pamodzi ndi iwo m'mitambo, kukakumana ndi Ambuye wanu wamuyaya mumlengalenga: ndipo motero mudzakhala ndi Ambuye wanu nthawi zonse. Kodi mudzalola mawu awa kukutonthozani, ana anga? Kodi mwalola Satana kukuchititsani kukana mawu omveka bwino awa olembedwa kuti onse aone? Kodi mudzasankha kukhalabe pakati pa oweluzidwa m'chipanduko chouma khosi? Kukana chochitika ichi cholembedwa momveka bwino m'malemba ndi dzanja la Mtumwi wanga Paulo ndiko kundikana ine pakuti ndikubwera kudzapulumutsa okhulupirika ku mkwiyo pakuti momwemo muli malonjezo a Yehova owululidwa m'chinsinsi chachikulu cha Mulungu cholembedwa ndi Paulo zaka zambiri za yubile zapitazo.

Ndikukulangizani: musandikane, ndipo lowani m'chingalawa nthawi isanathe. Pakuti onse amene asankha kukhala m'ziweruzo zikubwera adzabwera kwa ine poitanira pa dzina langa mpaka mapeto, ndipo ambiri adzavutika mpaka mpweya wawo womaliza. Koma komabe adzauka ku moyo wosatha ndi mphoto yaikulu ndi chimwemwe chosatha! Ndipo motero onse amene aitanidwa ku moyo wosatha adzabwera ku cholowa chawo chosatha, pakuti palibe amene angakwatule ngakhale wamng'ono wa inu m'manja mwanga mwamuyaya. Choncho imvani mawu anga ndipo khalani m'maganizo amodzi omwe ali mwa ine, Khristu Yesu, chifukwa ndine Mawu a Mulungu osanduka thupi ndipo ndimalankhula choonadi nthawi zonse. Siyani kumvera mawu ambiri opanda chikhulupiriro omwe amakana Mawu anga apo ayi mudzapezeka mutasiyidwa pakati pawo pamene chinthu choterocho sichofunikira.

Pakuti inu opulumutsidwa anga, ngakhale inu tsopano ndinu Ana a Mulungu Wamoyo, ngakhale a Yehova kudzera mu ndi m'chozizwitsa cha kubadwa kwatsopano mwapulumutsidwa kwamuyaya ndipo mwabadwanso ndi mbewu yosawonongeka. Komabe, ndinu alendo ndi ogonera m'maiko achilendo, ndipo ambiri a inu muli m'malire a hule lalikululo, ngakhale maiko omwe ali m'maunyolo akudikira chiweruzo chake chathunthu ndi chiwonongeko: Pakuti gawo lowirikiza la chiweruzo lalengezedwa pa Babulo Wachinsinsi ndipo zidzakhala choncho. Pakuti ndi nthawi iti yomwe Yehova sanapulumutse olungama ku Chiweruzo, pakuti palibe cholengedwa chomwe chingapereke kupusa kwa Yehova moyenera, ndipo mboni ya Loti ikuchitira umboni mpaka lero. Ngakhale kuchokera m'maiko onyansa a Sodomu ndi Gomora Yehova anatumiza Angelo ake kudzapulumutsa munthu mmodzi wolungamayu amene anakhala m'zipata za mzinda wa mdani.

Ndipo momwemonso ndidzapulumutsa Mpingo wanga wokhulupirika, ngakhale onse omwe ndawadinda ndi mzimu woyera wa lonjezo kuyambira tsiku la Pentekoste pa kubadwa kwa Mpingo wanga kulembedwa mu umboni wa Machitidwe! (Kodi simunawerenge?) Ndipo onse amene amwalira okhulupirika mwa Khristu kuyambira chiyambi cha m'badwo wa Mpingo wanga ndi onse amene ali amoyo ndikutsala adzasonkhanitsidwa pamodzi m'kuthwanima kwa diso ndipo kwamuyaya adzakhala ndi ine! Pakuti ndine amene ndikubwera kudzachitira okhulupirika anga, oitanidwa, ndi osankhidwa, ngakhale Thupi langa ndi Mpingo wanga ndiponso Mkwatibwi wanga. Ndidzawombola okhulupirika, ngakhale omwe ayenda molingana ndi mzimu wanga ndi onse omwe alapira machimo awo ndikundipanga ine Ambuye kuchokera mumtima wodzaza ndi chikhulupiriro pakuti iwo ndi Isiraeli wa Mulungu! Ndipo chochitika chikubwerachi chidzakhala chachikulu ndipo kugwedezeka kudzakhala kwakukulu ndipo ndi zizindikiro zambiri kuyandikira kwanga kwalengezedwa ndipo kukupitirizabe kulengezedwa kumwamba ndi zizindikiro pamwamba ndi zizindikiro pansi pano. Ndipo motero kudzakhala kusonkhanitsa kwa zipatso zoyamba kuti onse aone ndikuyang'ana modabwa!

Ndipo motero liwu langa likumvekabe m'dziko lakugwali, kupeza maso, makutu, ndi mitima ya onse omwe atumizidwa kwa iwo. Kodi mukumva liwu la Yeshua HaMashiach, kodi mukuzindikira liwu langa? Kodi mudzalandira mawu anga? Kodi mungandimve pamene ndikulankhula ku mtima wanu? Pakuti monga kwalembedwa: mitima ya anthu awa yanenepa chifukwa cha kudya kwambiri, ndipo makutu awo ndi ogontha pakumva, ndipo maso awo atseka, kuti nthawi ina iliyonse asamaone ndi maso awo, ndikumva ndi makutu awo, ndikumvetsetsa ndi mtima wawo, ndikutembenuzidwa, ndipo ndiwachiritse. Ambiri mwa awa agonetsedwa tulo ndi mawu onyansa koma otonthoza a Yesu wina, koma dziwani kuti palibe wina.

Pakuti m'chinenero chilichonse chomwe dzina langa litchulidwa, Ine Yesu wa ku Nazarete, ngakhale Yeshua HaMashiach, ndidzamva ndipo ndidzapulumutsa mpaka pomaliza onse amene aitanira pa dzina langa mpaka mapeto. Pakuti mawu anga anzeru, ngakhale uthenga wabwino wosatha awululidwa ndipo akumveka padziko lonse lapansi. Ndipo mawu anga amapeza omwe atumizidwa kwa iwo, pakuti momwemo muli Mawu a Mulungu Wam'mwambamwamba, ngakhale Yehova, pakuti palibe Mawu amodzi a Mulungu omwe amabwerera chabe, ndipo adzakwaniritsa zomwe anafunidwa ndipo odala ndi maso anu, pakuti akuona: ndi makutu anu, pakuti akumva ndipo mitima yanu yodzaza ndi chikhulupiriro, ndipo ndadzaza chikondi cha Mulungu m'mitima yanu momwe mumafuula Abba Atate! Ndinu ana a Mulungu ndipo sikuwoneka zomwe mudzakhala, koma pamene ndidzawonekera mudzaona kwathunthu ndipo miyoyo yanu yabisika mwa ine, Khristu Yesu, ndipo miyoyo yanu yatsala pang'ono kuwululidwa kwathunthu ndipo tsopano ndi nthawi yowulula.

Taonani! Babulo Wachinsinsi wakhala m'maunyolo pamene nyanga khumi zikumaliza kukonzekera kwawo kwa chiwonongeko chake chotheratu. Mwachoonadi, mwachoonadi, ku Babulo Wachinsinsi ndipo kwa yubile zambiri tsopano ndatumiza atumwi anga, ndatumiza aneneri anga, ndatumiza alaliki anga, ndatumiza abusa anga, ndatumiza aphunzitsi anga, ndatumiza ophunzira anga okhulupirika ndi abale ndipo ndatumiza ngakhale amasiye m'kusungulumwa kwawo, ndipo ndathira mzimu wanga kotheratu m'masiku omaliza awa a chisomo ndi chifundo!

Koma m'malo moti mitundu ikhale m'ziguduli ndi phulusa akusangalatsidwa ndi zizindikiro ndi machenjezo operekedwa a chiweruzo chikubwera pamene akutenga zithunzi ndi mavidiyo kuti asangalale. Akana amithenga anga omwe anabweretsa kuwala kwa uthenga wanga wabwino wa chipulumutso, anyoza ndi kuseka aneneri anga, aukira alaliki anga, aipitsa abusa anga, ndikuzunza ndi kusokoneza aphunzitsi anga.

Magulu ankhondo a chinjoka chachikulu chofiira akuzungulirani inu babulo wachinsinsi ndipo ali kwathunthu m'malire anu. Pakuti ngakhale atsogoleri anu achiwembu a Ziyoni akugulitsani chifukwa cha kachidutswa ka mkate kwa adani anu komanso kupititsa patsogolo ziwembu zawo za satana. Ndipo El Shaddai walola misala iyi kupitiriza, ngakhale kuyika m'mitima mwawo kukwaniritsa zolinga zake. Ndi wamphamvu El Shaddai amene akuweruzani, inu hule lofiira amene mwakhala modzikuza m'chisangalalo ngakhale pamene mukuvulidwa ulemerero wonse, kuvulidwa zakudya zonse zokoma, kuvulidwa ngakhale nyumba zanu pakuti ndapereka nyumba zanu ndi maiko m'manja mwa adani anu. Ngakhale m'manja mwa chilombo ndi mneneri wonyenga, pakuti amagwira ntchito limodzi kuti akuwonongeni kotheratu kuseri kwa chophimba cha chidole chakale. Pakuti chilombo chatsala pang'ono kugwetsa hule lalikulu lomwe chilombo chatopa nalo kunyamula, ndipo zolinga zake zakwaniritsidwa. Choncho, adzatenthedwa kotheratu, ndikuwonongedwa kotheratu mu ola limodzi ndipo chiwonongeko chake sichichedwa.

Pakuti mizere yawo yankhondo, ngakhale mizere yawo yofiira yoperekedwa mwachinyengo kuti isadutsidwe, yaikidwa pansi ndikudutsidwa mobwerezabwereza ndi mwadala. Musakhulupirire mabodza awo, kapena zifukwa zawo zonyenga chifukwa akufuna kuwoneka olungama munkhondo yawo ikubwera ndipo chilombo ndi mneneri wonyenga omwe sanauke kwathunthu adzauka kwathunthu kuchokera ku phulusa ndikunena kuti chiweruzo cha Mulungu chinagwa kuti chitumikire zolinga zawo, choncho musakhulupirire ngakhale limodzi la mabodza awo ang'onoang'ono ndipo chiweruzo chawo chomaliza sichichedwa.

Pakuti adzapitiriza ndikuchita machimo oyipa kwambiri kuposa Babulo Wachinsinsi, ndipo ali olakwa potsogolera Babulo Wachinsinsi kusochera kwathunthu ndi ku chiwonongeko chake chotheratu, pakuti Mulungu waika m'mitima mwawo kukwaniritsa chifuniro chake. Amagwira ntchito mogwirizana ndipo onse omwe amawoneka ngati adani amagwira ntchito limodzi mwachinsinsi pamene akusewera molingana ndi zolemba zawo ndikuchita mbali zawo pa siteji ya dziko lapansi, ndipo adzapha onse m'magulu awo omwe asankha kusayeserera ndikuchita mbali zawo mokwanira kuti akwaniritse zolinga za chinjoka. Pakuti chinjoka chimapatsa zilombo mphamvu zake ndi mpando wake wachifumu, ndi ulamuliro waukulu, Ndipo nyanga khumi zimalandira mphamvu monga mafumu ola limodzi ndi chilombo ndipo awa ali ndi mtima umodzi, ndipo adzapereka mphamvu zawo ndi nyonga kwa chilombo pakuti momwemo apatsidwa ndi Yehova kuti akwaniritse. Mverani ana anga ndipo phunzirani za ine ndikutsatira njira zanga, pakuti njira zanga ndi njira za Yehova, Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya!

Mvetsetsani ndikumvetsera, pakuti zilombo zachiwembu izi ndi mafumu oyipa ndi a magulu a Chinjoka omwe alamulira Babulo Wachinsinsi kuchokera m'magulu awo achinsinsi kuyambira chiyambi chake. Munthu amene amanyenga ndi kudziyesa, ngakhale munthu woyipa, amayenda ndi pakamwa pokhotakhota. Inde amadziyesa kukhala ena pakuti onse ndi onyenga, ndipo obisika pakati pa mitundu yonse namsongole wafesedwa. Amapanga zizindikiro ndi maso awo: amasonyeza ndi mapazi awo: amalangiza ndi zala zawo. Zinthu zonyansa zili m'mitima mwawo: amaganiza zoyipa nthawi zonse, ndikuyambitsa mikangano. Choncho, chiwonongeko chawo chidzafika mwachangu: adzawonongedwa mwadzidzidzi popanda kuchira. Zinthu zisanu ndi chimodzi izi Ambuye amadana nazo: inde, moyo wake umanyansidwa ndi zisanu ndi ziwiri: Maso odzikuza, lilime lonama, ndi manja omwe amakhetsa magazi osalakwa, mtima womwe umaganiza ziwembu zoyipa, mapazi omwe ali othamanga kuthamangira ku zoipa, Mboni yonyenga yomwe imalankhula mabodza, ndi iye amene amayambitsa mikangano pakati pa abale.

Kodi ndinu akhungu? Kodi mukukana kuzindikira zizindikiro zawo kuti mupitirize kukhala mu umbuli wadala kuti atsogoleri anu ndi a akugwa? Kodi simukumvetsetsa kuti osangalatsa anu ndi a akugwa? Kodi simukuzindikira kuti sali a chikhalidwe chofanana ndi inu, pakuti ambiri adzisintha kwamuyaya mu misala yawo ndipo ena ndi namsongole kuyambira kubadwa ndipo Mdyerekezi ndi Atate wawo? Komabe mumasankha kukhala mumdima mofunitsitsa, ndikusangalala ndikukhala m'machimo anu pamene mukugubuduzika m'matope ndi nkhumba zomwe zakutsogolerani ku zipata zenizeni za gehena kumene ambiri amadziwonetsera okha mu umbuli mu zachabechabe ndi kunyada. Chifukwa chake ndanena m'kalata yanga yopita ku Efeso yoperekedwa kwa Paulo: Galamuka iwe amene ukugona, ndipo uka kwa akufa, ndipo Ine, Khristu Yesu, ndidzakupatsa kuwala! Choncho samalani kuti muyende mosamala: osati ngati opusa, koma ngati anzeru. Ndipo motero ndikuwulula atsogoleri oyipawa a Babulo Wachinsinsi kwa oitanidwa osankhidwa ndi okhulupirika anga kuti musagwe m'chinyengo chawo ndikuti mutuluke mwa iye kwathunthu ndikukhala osiyana m'njira zanu zonse ndikuti musagawane nawo miliri yake.

Imvani mawu anga ndi kumvetsetsa zomwe zikuchitika pakali pano, pakuti kuwukira kwawo kwadzidzidzi kukuchitika. Ndikulankhula tsopano kwa Babulo Wachinsinsi ngakhale ali wogontha, wosayankhula, ndi wakhungu ndipo womangidwa m'unyolo kotheratu chifukwa cha chiweruzo chake. Monga akonzekera kuwukira kwawo kwadzidzidzi kwa zaka zambiri tsopano, ndipo atsimikizira chiwonongeko cha Babulo Wachinsinsi kudzera mu katapira, ndi njira zina zachinyengo, chifukwa ngakhale ndalama zake si kanthu koma lonjezo lopanda kanthu la ngongole zomwe zachitika m'manja mwa atsogoleri anu omwe amatumikira osintha ndalama ndipo Babulo wachinsinsi akugulitsidwa m'manja mwa adani awo ndi njira zochenjera izi. Kodi simukumvetsa za mabanki osunga ndalama pang'ono ndi ndalama zongopangidwa? Phunzirani kuti mudziwe.

Kodi sindinakupatseni chitsanzo pamene ndinathamangitsa osintha ndalama m'kachisi? Komabe, m'malo mowathamangitsa, mumawaitanira m'kati kuti adzadye ndi mabanja anu, ndipo mwayika ana anu ndi zidzukulu zanu ngati chikole kuti mukwaniritse zilakolako zanu zadyera ndipo choncho mbadwa zanu zagulitsidwa kuti zitsimikizire chiwonongeko chanu kuti muthe kukhala m'zosangalatsa za tchimo. Iwe, Babulo Wachinsinsi, umapha osabadwa ako m'chimwemwe chachikulu ndi kunyada ndipo opha osalakwa ali ndi chiweruzo chawo choikidwa ndipo chiweruzo chako sichichedwa. Ngakhale otsalira ochepa akuthamangira m'Chombo El Shaddai asanatseke chitseko, ambiri adzapezeka atasiyidwa ku chiweruzo chifukwa adzasankha kukhala panja akunyoza ndi kukhala mosangalala.

Mwadzidzidzi, mu ola limodzi chiwonongeko chake chidzagwa mwadzidzidzi ndipo sadzapulumuka. Popeza wadzipereka yekha potumikira adani ake amuyaya tsopano adzabweretsa chiwonongeko chake chamuyaya pakuti Babulo Wachinsinsi sadzauka nkomwe. Oh Babulo Wachinsinsi, popeza wagulitsa ana ako ngati chikole cha ngongole zako kuti upitirize m'zosangalatsa za tchimo (pakuti ngakhale ndalama zako ndi katapira), popeza wapha osalakwa, ndipo zambiri wachita. Tsopano ndikupereka iwe kotheratu m'manja mwawo ndipo Yahweh walengeza ngakhale gawo lowirikiza la chiweruzo pa iwe Babulo wachinsinsi. Ndipo ambiri mwa ana ako adzatumizidwa ku misasa yawo yogwirira ntchito ndi kukonzanso makhalidwe ndipo ambiri adzatengedwa kupita kumayiko achilendo pakuti chinjoka chaloledwa kutenga mayiko ndi anthu omwe andikana Ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, m'malo mwa Baraba Mesiya wawo wonyenga. Ndipo ambiri adzatenga chizindikiro chawo cha chilombo ndipo agulitsa miyoyo yawo ndi mbadwa zawo ku chilango chamuyaya kuchokera pamaso pa Mulungu wamuyaya ndi wosatha chifukwa cha zosangalatsa za tchimo kwa nyengo yochepa.

Tengani maliro chifukwa cha anthu a Babulo Wachinsinsi, ngakhale inu omwe mukhala mumdima ndi umbuli wotheratu wa chiwonongeko chanu chomwe chikubwera. Pamaso panu penipeni ndi mwa chilolezo cha voti yanu ndi kudzera mu ulesi wanu, mtundu wanu wonse wagwetsedwa pamaso panu penipeni. Mulibe nkomwe mphamvu kapena zida zomenyera nkhondo, pakuti Ine, ngakhale Khristu Yesu, ndavula mphamvu zanu zankhondo ndikuzipereka m'manja mwa adani anu. Mizinda yanu yomwe kale inali yayikulu tsopano yakhala mabwinja a umbanda ndi umphawi, zomwe osangalatsa anu ndi inu nonse mumadzitamandira nazo monyadira. Aedomu achotsa kale mafakitale awo m'maiko anu, ndipo mulibe mphamvu zopangira zinthu zomwe munali nazo kale komanso ngakhale zida zopangira zomwe mukufunikira kuti mumenye nkhondo zanu zosatha.

Pakuti mumapita kunkhondo ndi nkhondo, ndipo mwawononga kotheratu mayiko, akunja ndi apakhomo. Pakuti kuchokera kwa iwe, Babulo Wachinsinsi, kwatuluka mliri wa Magulu Ankhondo Ofiira, ngakhale kufalitsa padziko lonse lapansi chotupitsa chofiira cha Chinjoka chachikulu chofiira, pakuti mwatumikira ogwa mwachinsinsi kuyambira pachiyambi ndipo mukupitiriza mpaka lero. Babulo Wachinsinsi, umapereka mofunitsitsa chilolezo chako kwa olamulira ako a Satana, omwe akudziwa bwino mapulani awo ndipo akudziwa bwino kuti ali mkati mowononga kotheratu mtundu wanu womwe kale unali waukulu ndi inu. Pakuti muli ndi mlandu wa magazi a oyera mtima, ndipo magazi a ofera chikhulupiriro akulira kuchokera pansi chifukwa cha chiweruzo chanu ndipo ngati Yahweh sakanakuweruzani akanayenera kulapa kwa mitundu yomwe watenga m'chiweruzo m'nthawi zakale, ngakhale omwe amatumikira ngati chenjezo la chiweruzo chanu chomwe chikubwera chomwe mumachinyalanyaza mwadala.

Kuchokera kwa iwe, Babulo Wachinsinsi, kunabwera chiwonongeko chobweretsedwa kumayiko akunja ndi Magulu Ankhondo Ofiira pamene Aedomu anadzipangitsa kukhala okondedwa kwa gulu la ogwira ntchito ndi chotupitsa chawo chapoizoni, ngakhale chikalata chawo chamwano chothandizidwa ndi Chishango Chofiira. Pakuti achifwamba a nthawi zakale, ngakhale atsogoleri anu a mafakitale a mibadwo yapitayo, (ngakhale omwe mwawagulitsira mbadwa zanu) ndi omwe ali ndi udindo pa mliri wa magulu ankhondo ofiira. Babulo Wachinsinsi ndi ukonde wake wa ngongole wathandizira ndikuthandizira Magulu Ankhondo Ofiira m'zochita zawo kuyambira pachiyambi, ndipo Babulo Wachinsinsi wakhala gawo la netiweki yawo kuyambira pachiyambi pamene Chishango Chofiira chinakhazikitsidwa padziko lonse lapansi. Ngakhale mpweya wamoto wa chinjoka womwe wachokera ku Chishango Chofiira, pakuti iwo ndi ogwa omwe amayenda pakati panu.

Kuchokera ku Babulo wachinsinsi atsogoleri awo "osintha zinthu" anaphunzitsidwa mwachinsinsi pakuti amakhala pamadzi ambiri, ndipo anatumizidwa mwachinsinsi kumayiko akunja, ngakhale ku Gogi ndi Magogi ndipo kusintha kwawo kunali kwakukulu, ndi kwachiwawa, ndi kwankhanza ndipo tsopano akuphimba dziko lapansi. Anafalitsa chotupitsa chawo cha satana kudera lonse la kum'mawa ndikuwononga kotheratu zonse m'njira zawo. Anatuluka kuchokera ku Babulo Wachinsinsi ndipo akhala mliri padziko lapansi, ndipo nthawi yonseyi Babulo wachinsinsi wanama ndipo hule lalikulu ladzinenera kuti likundilankhulira ndikudzitcha Mkhristu. Kugwetsedwa kwachiwawa kumeneku kwa dongosolo lakale kwapita padziko lonse lapansi ndipo tsopano kukupita ku gawo lake lomaliza, ndipo adzaloledwa dongosolo lawo, koma kokha kutumikira zolinga zamuyaya za Yahweh, pakuti m'choonadi ogwa amanjenjemera ndipo mantha amagwira mitima yawo yoipa pakuti akudziwa kuti chiweruzo chawo chomwe chakhala chikubwera kwa nthawi yayitali chili pa iwo. Pakuti iwe Babulo Wachinsinsi m'dama lako ndi uhule wako, umagona ndi mdani.

Taonani! Hule lalikulu, ngakhale Babulo Wachinsinsi mtundu wa mayiko ambiri, ndi mkazi uja wowonetsedwa kwa Yohane Wovumbulutsa, ngakhale mkazi uja woledzera ndi magazi a Oyera mtima, ndi magazi a Ofera chikhulupiriro anga ndipo chiweruzo chanu chili pa inu. Pakuti m'njira yotere, mwakhala mosangalala pamene atsogoleri anu anawononga mayiko ena ndi chilolezo chanu ndipo ndi ndalama zanu muli olakwa monga namsongole wa Aedomu awa omwe mwawagulitsira mbadwa zanu chifukwa cha zosangalatsa za tchimo. Munathandizira ndikuthandizira ndikugwirizanitsa mwachinsinsi kugwetsedwa kwa mayiko ambiri! Ndipo mayiko onse omwe agwetsedwa ndi magulu ankhondo ofiira mothandizidwa ndi Chishango Chofiira aphedwa ndikuzunzidwa ndikuzunzidwa ndikuphedwa ndi njala ndikuphedwa m'njira yomvetsa chisoni kwambiri. Oyera mtima omwe anali m'maiko awa mwawaphera chikhulupiriro ngakhale mamiliyoni osawerengeka. Ndipo nthawi yonseyi mumavina ndikugwiritsa ntchito ufiti wa Satana pokondwerera poganiza kuti sizidzafika kwa inu, ngakhale mankhwala osokoneza bongo omwe mwapatsidwa chifukwa cha imfa zanu. Babulo Wachinsinsi, mwapereka nsembe m'badwo wonse wa ana omwe tsopano ndi amuna azaka zankhondo omwe akanatha kukutetezani koma m'malo mwake tsopano akukhala m'misewu ya mizinda yanu yonyansa ndipo ali osokonezeka kotheratu ndi ufiti uwu ndipo asanduka ma zombie a akufa oyenda pamene mankhwala awa akuwola thupi lawo ngakhale ali amoyo.

Tsopano pakubwera chiweruzo chanu chonse Babulo Wachinsinsi ndipo ndinu chochititsa chidwi kuti onse awone, komabe mukupitiriza mu umbuli ndi khungu kunena m'mitima yanu kuti tsoka silidzafika pa inu, ngakhale mwakhala mukukondwera pamene atsogoleri anu anabweretsa tsoka kugombe la mayiko osawerengeka m'nkhondo zawo zopanda chilungamo zomwe mumasangalatsidwa nazo pamene mumawonera pa wailesi yakanema. Ndipo choncho mwagawidwa pakati pa adani anu ndipo akusangalala kale ndi zofunkha zankhondo ngakhale mizinga isanagwe. Pakuti kugonjetsedwa kwa Babulo Wachinsinsi ndi kotsimikizika ndipo kwaperekedwa ndi Yahweh mwiniwake ndipo palibe chomwe chidzaletsa chiwonongeko chake chotheratu ndipo sadzauka nkomwe.
