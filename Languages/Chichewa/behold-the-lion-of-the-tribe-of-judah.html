Taonani! Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda!

Tsegulani makutu anu, ndipo ndiloleni nitsegule maso anu, O' Israyeli, ndipo dzukani ku khungu lanu lina ndikumva liwu la Mesiya wanu weniweni, ngakhale Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda, pakuti ndine amene. Choncho mvetsetsani mawu a Yeshua HaMashiach, ngakhale iye amene inunso muli naye chidwi, O' Israyeli, pakuti ndine amene.

Zoonadi, zoonadi, imvani mawu anga abale anga kuti mumvetsetse nthawi imene mukukhala ndipo kuti mumvetsetse chiyembekezo cha kuitanidwa kwanu komwe kukubwerera kudziko la lonjezo, pakuti ndine chiyembekezo cha kuitanidwa kwanu, ndipo ndikukuitanani, O' Israyeli, kuti mumvetsetse zomwe zikubwera komanso mkwiyo womwe ukuyenera kumasulidwa munthawi zino zomwe mukukhala. Ndipo ndine Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda ndipo m'chilungamo ndikubwera kudzaweruza ndi kuchita nkhondo, choncho mvetsetsani kuuma kwa Mawu anga, ngakhale Mawu a iye amene inunso muli naye chidwi pakuti ndi momwe Yahweh wandipangira kukhala.

Chifukwa ndine thanthwe lokhumudwitsa, ngakhale mwala waukulu wopunthwitsa pomwe onse opanda chikhulupiriro adzapunthwa, chifukwa olungama sakhala ndi moyo kudzera mwa Mose koma olungama adzakhala ndi moyo ndi chikhulupiriro! Chifukwa Mose amakhala mwa Mesiya wanu weniweni ngakhale iye amene adasunga bwino lamulo lomwe ndi losatheka kuti Israyeli asunge, ndipo ngakhale Adamu yense kusunga chifukwa cha tchimo m'thupi lanu loipa. Ndipo ndine amene ndinalipira chilango cha Mose pamene ndinadzipereka ndekha ngati mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu kwa Yahweh nsembe yotetezera yamuyaya chifukwa chilango cha ochimwa sichinali changa kuti ndilipire, komabe m'chikondi changa kwa Yahweh, ndi chikondi changa kwa anthu ndinalipira zilango zanu zonse. Ngakhale zilango za kuzunzika kwakukulu, zowawa, ndi imfa ndinalipira zonse ndipo Yahweh walandira nsembe yanga mpaka kalekale.

Choncho mvetsetsani, mkwiyo wa Mwanawankhosa watsala pang'ono kumasulidwa ndipo malipenga akonzeka kulira, ndipo taonani, mbale za mkwiyo wa Mulungu zikuyandikira ndipo zili pakhomo panu, chifukwa chake, imvani liwu langa kuti inu ndi ana anu mukhale ndi moyo ndikuthawa msampha womwe Baraba akugwira ntchito kuti aikire anthu anga, ngakhale mwanawankhosa wa chinjoka amene wasankhidwa ndi Esau ngati Moshiach wawo, yemwenso ndi womanga kachisi wachirombo. Ndipo adzauka ngati mwanawankhosa wokhala ndi nyanga ziwiri, ndipo akubwera kudzagawa maiko mopitilira kwa okhalamo oyenera omwe athamangitsidwa ndi kuphedwa kwawo, kugawa pakati pa olanda kuchokera kwa Esau omwe anena zonama, ndi owukira otumizidwa ndi chinjoka omwe ndi mbadwa za Asikenazi osati Yakobo koma adziphatikiza okha kwa Esau ndi njira zawo za Talmud.

Ndipo ndine amene Yahweh adatumiza ngati mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu, ngakhale ngati pangano lamtendere langwiro, ngakhale ngati Paskha wanu wangwiro komanso nsembe yomaliza ya machimo a anthu kamodzi, onse, ndi mpaka kalekale ndipo palibenso nsembe ina ya machimo. Ndipo ndinabwera kwa anga m'chikondi ndi mtendere, ngakhale omwe anakana nsembe yanga, komabe ndatumizidwa ngati Paskha wangwiro pomvera chifuniro cha Yahweh, ndipo ndine mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu, yemwe Yahweh adapereka mwangwiro chifukwa cha machimo anu, chifukwa chake imvani liwu langa O' Israyeli ndikumvetsetsa kuti sindidzabweranso padziko lapansi ngati mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu, ndipo mtendere udzachotsedwa padziko lapansi m'chiweruzo chomwe chafika. Ngakhale chiweruzo chomwe chidzamasulidwa mwadzidzidzi choyamba panyumba ya Mulungu chifukwa chiweruzo chimayambira panyumba ya Mulungu.

Chifukwa okhulupirika mwa Khristu adzatengedwa kupita kumwamba, ndipo ofunda adzasiyidwa kumoto woyenga kuti awononge thupi, ngakhale kuti mzimu upulumutsidwe koma ngati kudzera pamoto. Ndipo makamu ambiri, ambiri adzabwera kwa Yesu wa ku Nazarete kuchokera kumitundu yonse ndi mafuko ndi malilime ndipo ndidzawayenga m'chilungamo ndipo ndidzawalandira kwa ine ndipo ndidzawalenganso m'chilungamo ndi chiyero chenicheni ndipo pa mphotho yawo palibe kufananiza ndi masautso omwe adzapirire, chifukwa kulemera kwa mphotho yawo kudzaposa ziyembekezo za anthu ndipo adzakhala ndi moyo wamuyaya mumtendere wangwiro ndi chiyero ndi chilungamo chenicheni.

Pakuti kwalembedwa za iwo ndipo kudzakhala choncho: Awa ndiwo amene anatuluka m'chisautso chachikulu, ndipo atsuka mikanjo yawo yotalika, ndipo aiyeretsa mikanjo yawo yotalika m'mwazi wa Mwanawankhosa. Chifukwa chake, ali pamaso pa mpando wachifumu wa Mulungu, ndipo amamtumikira usana ndi usiku m'Kachisi wake, ndipo iye wokhala pampando wachifumu adzakhala pakati pawo. Sadzamvanso njala, kapena ludzu, kapena dzuwa silidzawagwera, kapena kutentha kulikonse. Pakuti Mwanawankhosa, amene ali pakati pa mpando wachifumu, adzawalamulira, ndipo adzawatsogolera kuzitsime zamadzi amoyo, ndipo Mulungu adzapukuta misozi yonse m'maso mwawo.

Choncho mvetsetsani kuti ndine amene ndamaliza ntchito zanga pakati pa amitundu ndipo nthawi zanga zachisomo ndi chifundo zatha, ngakhale chisomo ndi chifundo zili ndi ine mpaka kalekale, ndipo Yahweh mwiniwake walengeza kuti adzachitira chifundo amene adzamuchitira chifundo, choncho chisomo ndi chifundo zikhaladi mpaka kalekale. Zoonadi, zoonadi, ndinapita kwa amitundu ngakhale ngati wotengedwa ukapolo wokanidwa ndi anga omwe atengedwa ndi Esau mu ukapolo ku miyambo yake yamwano yomwe amafuna kuwononga Mawu amuyaya ndi osatha a Yahweh, omwe kuwonongeka kwawo nkosatheka. Komabe adapanga zikhulupiriro zawo zaku gehena m'manja mwa alembi awo omwe adalemba miyambo ya anthu oyipa m'buku lomwe limati ndi loyera, koma buku lamwano lingakhale bwanji loyera? Zoonadi, zoonadi, tsoka kwa iwo amene amatcha mdima kuwala ndi kuwala mdima, pakuti chiweruzo chawo sichichedwa!

Ndipo nthawi zanga pakati pa amitundu zatha chifukwa agwera m'machimo omwewo omwe Abambo anu adagwera pamene adakana mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu yemwe Yahweh adawatumizira mosayenera ngati pangano lamtendere lamuyaya kumitundu, omwe khamu lalikulu labwera kuchipulumutso. Ndipo tsopano omwe akadakhala ana a lonjezo pakati pa Israyeli wa Mulungu adzitamandira okha motsutsana ndi nthambi zachilengedwe ngakhale za mtengo wa Azitona wachilengedwe womwe adalumikizidwa m'malo mwake pamene nthambi zachilengedwe zidadulidwa m'kusakhulupirira komweko.

Komabe adatengedwa kumtengo wa azitona wakuthengo, ngakhale mtengo wopanda chiyembekezo kapena lonjezo ndipo mwa iwo omwe adabwera kwa ine ndikundipanga kukhala Ambuye wawo wamuyaya wachilungamo munthawi zamtendere, chisomo, ndi chifundo tsopano atengedwa kukhala ana mu Israyeli wa Mulungu ndipo amalandira mokwanira malonjezo opangidwa kwa Abambo ndipo ambiri mwa awa adzanditumikira mpaka kalekale ngati thupi langa ndipo ndidzawatenga kwa ine ngati mkwatibwi wanga ndipo ndidzasindikiza 144,000 anga omwe adziyeretsa m'mwazi wanga wotetezera oyera ngati anamwali omwe sanadetsedwe ndi hule lalikulu. Ndipo kudzera m'chipulumutso cha amitundu mitundu idadalitsidwa ndipo ndithudi omwe a mafuko otayika omwe adamvera liwu langa abwera kuchipulumutso mwa Ambuye wawo ndipo adzakhala ndi moyo wamuyaya pakati pa mafuko mu Israyeli wa Mulungu! Choncho mvetsetsani, ndidzapulumutsa ena onse kuchokera kwa Adamu kulikonse komwe adasokera padziko lapansi m'masiku ake a chilango.

Chifukwa Yahweh wandipanga mzimu wopatsa moyo, ndipo pambuyo pa thupi sindidziwikanso. Zoonadi, zoonadi, ndine amene tsopano wadula mtengo wa azitona wakuthengo chifukwa cha kusakhulupirira kwawo ndipo chifukwa aswa pangano langa, ngakhale Chipangano changa Chatsopano. Ndipo izi chifukwa akuyenera kukhala ndi Ambuye mmodzi, ngakhale ine, Khristu Yesu Ambuye wa Ambuye ndi Mfumu ya Mafumu ndipo mu umbuye wanga muli chiyembekezo chawo chokha. Komabe adzitengera okha chiyembekezo chonyenga mwa Baraba ndikuyika ziyembekezo zawo mopusa mwa ambuye wonyenga yemwe adalowa m'Mipingo yawo ndi ziyembekezo zonyenga za kulemera kwakuthupi, ngakhale Mpulumutsi wonyenga yemwe amalonjeza kupulumutsa Babulo Wachinsinsi koma yemwe amagwira ntchito mwachinsinsi kuwononga Babulo Wachinsinsi. Ngakhale wonamizira yemweyo yemwe ndi chilombo chochokera pansi chomwe chidzauka ngati Moshiach wonyenga wa Esau, ngakhale mwanawankhosa wa chinjoka yemwe adzauka ali ndi nyanga ziwiri. Ndipo motero Mpingo wanga wolowedwa wa Laodikaya walakwitsa ndipo tsopano akuvomereza imfa zawo, pakuti Baraba amangobweretsa imfa, ndipo adzawononga kotheratu hule lalikululo, ngakhale Babulo Wachinsinsi, yemwe chiweruzo chake chafika chifukwa cha kuipa kwake kotheratu ndi chivundi chomwe waipitsa nacho dziko lapansi m'dama lake.

Choncho asankha mopanduka kwa iwo okha ngakhale iye amene amabweretsa chiwonongeko chawo ngati mpulumutsi wawo wonamizira, chifukwa Sunagoge wa Satana ndi onamizira omwe onse amanamizira. Choncho phunzirani, O' Israyeli, zomwe Baraba watsala pang'ono kukwaniritsa chifukwa awagulitsa m'manja mwa adani awo, ndipo adzanamizira bala lake lamutu ndikudzibisa yekha padziko lapansi ku masoka omwe atsala pang'ono kugunda. Ndipo adzauka m'chinyengo chachikulu ngati Moshiach wonyenga kwa Israyeli chifukwa adzakhala ndi nkhondo yawo, ngakhale nkhondo yawo yachitatu yotere, ndipo yoyipitsitsa mwa zitatuzo idzakhala. Ngakhale zili choncho, pakuti kwalembedwa choncho ndipo kudzachitika choncho: Ndipo ndinaona chilombo chikutuluka m'nyanja, chokhala ndi mitu isanu ndi iwiri, ndi nyanga khumi, ndipo panyanga zake panali akorona khumi, ndipo pamutu pake dzina lamwano. Ndipo chilombo chimene ndinachiona chinali ngati nyalugwe, ndipo mapazi ake ngati a chimbalangondo, ndipo pakamwa pake ngati pakamwa pa Mkango: ndipo chinjoka chinachipatsa mphamvu yake ndi mpando wake wachifumu, ndi ulamuliro waukulu. Ndipo ndinaona umodzi wa mitu yake ngati kuti wavulazidwa mpaka imfa, koma bala lake lakupha linachiritsidwa, ndipo dziko lonse lapansi linadabwa ndikutsatira chilombocho.

Ndipo zili choncho kuti mawu m'malemba amavumbula chiwembu chake, ngakhale Mawu omwe ndinapatsa Yohane Wovumbulutsa zaka za yubile zambiri zapitazo. Ndipo zidzawoneka kuti Baraba adzalandira bala lamutu, koma adzanama ndikupusitsa dziko lapansi lomwe lidzakhulupirira kwathunthu zizindikiro zake zonama ndi zozizwitsa pakuti wapatsidwa kukokera onse omwe amasangalala ndi chosalungama kwa iye yekha m'chinyengo champhamvu, pakuti iye ndi wowaipitsa ndi wowawononga. Ndipo m'manja mwake amabweretsa kulumwa kwa njoka, ngakhale jekeseni wachiromboyo, ndipo adzasakaniza chitsulo ndi dongo ngakhale kuti sadzamatirira wina ndi mzake, komabe mwanawankhosa wa chinjoka adzauka. Ndipo adzauka pamwamba pa dongosolo lomaliza la chilombo cha wogwayo, lomwe lidzakhala ufumu wawo womaliza kulamulira dziko lapansi kuchokera kudziko la lonjezo. Komabe sadzauka yekha, koma adzauka pamodzi ndi chilombo chochokera kunyanja, ndipo adzalamulira mgwirizano wawo wa mafumu omwe apereka mphamvu zawo zonse ndi ulamuliro kwa chinjoka chifukwa onse amalambira chinjoka ndipo kwa icho adzipereka kwathunthu chifukwa ndi Atate wawo, ngakhale iye amene wabzala namsongole wake woyipa pakati pa tirigu wa Yahweh, ndipo ndidzawazula kotheratu ndikuwaponya kumoto.

Ndipo motero mwanawankhosa wa chinjoka adzauka m'chinyengo chokwanira ndipo zizindikiro zambiri zonama ndi zozizwitsa adzawonetsa kutsimikizira maulosi ake onama, ndipo adzadzipangira zonena zambiri zodzitamandira ndi zamwano ngakhale kudzinenera kuti iye mwini ndi Mulungu. Ndipo chilombo chimodzi chidzaloza chinzake ndipo adzakhala ndi ulamuliro wamgwirizano, ngakhale molingana ndi mapulani a chinjoka omwe adzalamulira ufumu wake woyipa kwakanthawi kochepa. Ndipo adzapanga zinthu zonse zachikhristu kukhala zosaloledwa, ndipo adzaletsa zolemba zoyera, ndipo zaperekedwa m'manja mwawo kuchita nkhondo ndi oyera mtima ndikuwagonjetsa, ndipo motero adzakwaniritsa. Komabe adzaphatikiza zipembedzo zadziko lapansi kuti onse alambire makyubu awo akuda chifukwa pamapazi a Satana adzigwirizanitsa okha m'kupanduka komaliza ndipo sadzathawa dzanja langa lamphamvu, akutero Mesiya wanu wamuyaya, ngakhale Yeshua HaMashiach!

Komabe m'chidani chawo pa zinthu zonse zokhudzana ndi Khristu Yesu adzachititsa chipulumutso kubwera pa khamu lalikulu lomwe adzalipha, ngakhale omwe adzaitana pa dzina la Yesu wa ku Nazarete mpaka mpweya wawo womaliza ndipo ndidzawalimbikitsa ndikuwapatsa chiyembekezo chachikulu, ndipo adzabwera kwa ine kwathunthu, ngakhale onse omwe ali olowa m'malo a chipulumutso chamuyaya, ndipo palibe amene angathe kuwakwatula m'manja mwanga ndipo onse omwe mayina awo alembedwa m'buku la moyo la mwanawankhosa adzapulumutsidwa. Choncho musadandaule za okondedwa anu omwe akuwoneka otayika, chifukwa ambiri a iwo ali m'manja mwanga amphamvu ndipo ndidzawabweretsabe kuchipulumutso mwa Khristu Yesu ndipo m'chilankhulo chilichonse chomwe aitana pa dzina langa lamphamvu ndidzawamva!

Komabe, kudzakhala choncho kuti mwanawankhosa wonyenga wokhala ndi nyanga ziwiri adzauka kudzalamulira dziko lapansi kuchokera kudziko lolonjezedwa lomwe Esau akufuna kunena kuti ndi lake, koma zonena zake ndi zabodza ndipo ufulu wobadwa wa Yakobo ndi wotsimikizika mpaka kalekale, ngakhale ufulu wobadwa womwe Esau adagulitsa chifukwa cha kachidutswa ka mkate. Chifukwa Esau wadziphatikiza yekha kwa ogwawo ndipo wadziphatikiza yekha kwa Asikenazi omwe adzitengera okha miyambo ndi machitidwe a Akanaani oyipa komabe amanama ndikunena kuti ndi a Israyeli pamene sali. Chifukwa anali Kanaani amene analakalaka njira za ogwawo pambuyo pa chigumula chachikulu, motero kusintha mbadwa zake mpaka kalekale kukhala chiwonongeko chamuyaya chifukwa onse omwe amadzisintha okha mwachibadwa kuchokera ku chifaniziro cha Mulungu chomwe adalengedwa nacho alibe chikhululukiro chifukwa izi ndi njira za Satana zomwe Esau adalemba mwa zina mu Talmud yake yamwano komwe chinyengo chake chimachokera.

Ndipo motero Asikenazi, yemwe ndi wa Yafeti, wajowina Esau m'zonena zake zonama za cholowa, zomwe akufuna kuba cholowa cha Yakobo kwa ana a lonjezo. Komabe, kuyesetsa koteroko nkopanda phindu ndipo sikudzayima chifukwa ndine amene Yahweh wapereka zinthu zonse m'manja mwanga amphamvu ndi okhoza. Choncho mvetsetsani kuti ndine amene ndalandira zinthu zonse zolonjezedwa kwa Yakobo ndipo ndine Mesiya wanu weniweni, ndipo m'manja mwanga muli mphotho zanu O' Israyeli ndipo m'manja mwa Baraba chiwonongeko chanu chamuyaya chikuyembekezera, chifukwa chake musamujowine!

Choncho mvetsetsani kuti mphotho za lonjezo zopangidwa kwa Abambo akale zili zolimba m'manja mwanga ndipo palibe amene angathe kuba zomwe zili zanga mwa ufulu pakuti ndi mphamvu yopatsidwa kwa ine ndi NDINE wamkulu ndipo ndidzapereka cholowa ichi kwa ana a lonjezo, ngakhale olungama omwe amakhala ndi chikhulupiriro mwa Mwana wa Yahweh, yemwe adawakonda ndikudzipereka yekha chifukwa cha chipulumutso chamuyaya cha ana a lonjezo, chifukwa chake O' Israyeli ndilandireni ngati Mesiya wanu kuti inu ndi ana anu mukhale ndi moyo ndikulemera m'njira. Ndipo ndidzakusungani ndikukutetezani kuti mufike ku gawo lanu lonse la cholowa mu ufumu wanga wazaka chikwi womwe uli pakhomo panu. Choncho imvani kuitana kwanga, O' Israyeli! Pakuti ndikukuitanani kuti mudzigonjetse nokha m'dzanja langa lamphamvu kuti inunso mukhale ana a lonjezo chifukwa Mose amasungidwa kwathunthu ndi chikhulupiriro mwa ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, chifukwa chake, pumulani m'ntchito zanga ndikusiya zanu.

Ndipo motero Asikenazi wajowina Esau m'zonena zake zonama, zomwe akufuna kuba cholowa cha Yakobo kwa ana a lonjezo. Komabe, kuyesetsa koteroko nkopanda phindu ndipo sikudzayima chifukwa ndine amene Yahweh wapereka zinthu zonse m'manja mwanga amphamvu ndi okhoza. Choncho mvetsetsani kuti ndine amene ndalandira zinthu zonse zolonjezedwa kwa Yakobo, ndipo ndine Mesiya wanu weniweni, ndipo m'manja mwanga muli mphotho yanu O' Israyeli ndipo m'manja mwa Baraba chiwonongeko chanu chikuyembekezera. Chifukwa chake, mvetsetsani chisankho chomwe muyenera kupanga, ndi iye amene inunso muli naye chidwi, chifukwa ndine amene ndatumizidwa ndi Yahweh chifukwa cha chipulumutso chanu, ngakhale iye amene Mose adalamula kumvera kwanu, O' abale anga osamvera.

Ndipo monga kwalembedwa za iwo ndipo kudzachitika ndi dzanja langa lamphamvu, chifukwa Yahweh adavumbula choonadi cha Esau kwa aneneri ake m'masiku a zolakwa zawo zomwe zakhala zambiri. Pakuti kwalembedwa kalekale: Chifukwa cha nkhanza zanu pa m'bale wanu Yakobo, manyazi adzakuphimbani, ndipo mudzadulidwa mpaka kalekale. Pamene munayima kutsidya lina, tsiku limene alendo anatenga chuma chake, ndipo alendo analowa m'zipata zake, ndikuchita maere pa Yerusalemu, ngakhale inu munali ngati mmodzi wa iwo.

Choncho mvetsetsani O' Israyeli kuti kulekanitsidwa komaliza kwa Yakobo ndi Esau kwakugwerani, ndipo ndikubwera kudzayeretsa dziko la lonjezo m'manja mwa ogwawo ndipo Esau adzazulidwa kotheratu, ndipo za Esau kwalembedwa: Ndipo nyumba ya Yakobo idzakhala moto, ndi nyumba ya Yosefe lawi lamoto, ndi nyumba ya Esau ngati chiputu, ndipo adzawayatsa ndi kuwawononga: ndipo sipadzakhala wotsalira wa nyumba ya Esau: pakuti Ambuye wayankhula. Choncho mvetsetsani inu a Esau omwe mukuwerenga Mawu anga, zomwe Yahweh walengeza palibe amene angasinthe ndipo mapeto anu adzakhala omaliza ndipo mudzazulidwa kotheratu ndipo simudzakhalakonso.

Chifukwa ndine Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda ndipo m'chilungamo ndikubwera kudzaweruza ndi kuchita nkhondo. Choncho imvani Mawu anga O' Isiraeli ndipo bisalani ku tsiku lomwe lili pa inu. Bisalani kwa munthu wauchimo amene akubwera m'njira yanu, chifukwa Baraba akubwera ndi imfa yamuyaya m'manja mwake yoperekedwa ngati machiritso ozizwitsa olonjezedwa monama kuti apereke moyo wosatha. Koma sadzakhala ndi moyo woti akhale nawo mu imfa yachiwiri koma chiwonongeko chosalekeza m'malawi otentha kwambiri a chilango kumene mphutsi yawo simafa.

Ndine amene ndidzathyola zisindikizo pa mpukutu wanga wa cholowa, ngakhale chikalata chenicheni cha dziko lapansi chomwe ndagula ndi magazi anga owombera kuchokera m'dzanja la Yahweh mwiniwake. Ndipo ndikubwera kudzazula okhala mwachisawawa ndi onse amene ogwa awatumiza, ngakhale ogwawo enieni ndipo nthawi zawo zatha. Choncho mvetsetsani zomwe zikubwera, Isiraeli, chifukwa adzakhazikitsa ufumu wawo ndipo adzawoneka opambana ndipo zizindikiro zambiri zonama ndi zozizwitsa adzaziwonetsa kuti asocheretse onse amene akondwera ndi chosalungama. Ndipo adzaganiza kuti akhazikitsa chiyambi cha nthawi yawo yagolide yomwe amalakalaka kuyikhazikitsa, chifukwa ndi a Atate wawo mdierekezi, ndipo mdierekezi ndiye chinjoka chachikulucho, chotero, zilakolako za chinjokacho adzazichita.

Komabe, sadzakhala ndi nthawi yagolide koma chiweruzo chaukali ndi chokhwima, chomwe chiweruzochi chidzayamba mwachangu pamene okhulupirika anga mwa Khristu asonkhanitsidwa kwa ine m'mitambo pamene ndikuwonekera komwe dziko lapansi lidzawona. Komabe, adzanama ndikuuza dziko lapansi kuti alendo adani abera ambiri kuchoka pa dziko lapansi ndipo adzabwerera ndipo dziko lapansi liyenera kukhala lokonzeka kumenyana ndi owukira onyengawa. Koma m'choonadi akudzikonzera okha kunkhondo ya tsiku lalikululo, ngakhale tsiku la kubwera kwanga kwachiwiri, ngakhale tsiku lomwe mbalame zamlengalenga zidzadya kwambiri mnofu wa mafumu awo ogwa a dziko lapansi ndipo zidzakhala choncho.
