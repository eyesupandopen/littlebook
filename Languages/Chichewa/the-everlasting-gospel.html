Zowawa ndi Zotsekemera
Ogasiti 4, 2024

Uthenga Wabwino Wosatha!

Imvani Mawu a Yeshua HaMashiach ndipo tcherani khutu lanu pomvera. Ndipo nyengo zikusintha ndipo tsiku lafika pamene tsoka lidzagwa mwadzidzidzi padziko lapansi. Mwadzidzidzi ndidzasonkhanitsa okhulupirika anga kwa ine m'mitambo ndipo mwadzidzidzi chiwonongeko chidzagwa padziko lonse lapansi ndipo mwadzidzidzi dziko lomwe mukulidziwa lidzasinthidwa kwamuyaya, osabwereranso ku mkhalidwe woyipa ndi wauchimo uwu. Ngakhale kutha kwa ulamuliro wauchimo wa Satana kukuwoneka, tsikulo silidzafika kwathunthu kufikira mphamvu zonse, ndi ulamuliro wa Chinjoka zitagonjetsedwa pakubwera kwanga kwachiwiri pamene ndidzabwera kudzalamulira dziko lapansi m'chilungamo ndi ankhondo akumwamba, ngakhale magulu ankhondo akulu akumwamba omwe mwa iwo mudzakhala zipatso zoyamba kuchokera kwa akufa ndipo ndidzawasonkhanitsa kwa ine ndekha.

Pakuti amene adzakhala ndi ine ndi oitanidwa, ndi osankhidwa, ndi okhulupirika ndi oona. Ndipo adzitsimikizira okha kukhala okhulupirika mu zinthu zochepa, kotero ndidzawapanga kukhala olamulira pa zambiri ndipo amalowa m'chimwemwe cha Ambuye wawo. Adzakhala gulu lankhondo losawonongeka ndi lamuyaya lomwe silingagonjetsedwe kapena kuyimitsidwa mwanjira iliyonse, ndipo adzapondereza pansi mbali zonse za Ufumu wa adani mu chiwonongeko chotheratu ndi kugonjetsa zinthu zonse zoyipa. Pakuti kubwezera ndi kwanga, atero Ambuye, ndipo ndidzabwezera. Ndipo Ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndine Mbuye wa Ambuye ndi Mfumu ya Mafumu, ndipo m'chilungamo ndikubwera kudzaweruza ndi kuchita nkhondo.

Choncho imvani mawu anga ndikumvetsetsa chiyembekezo cha chilengedwe chonse, pakuti ngakhale chilengedwe chikuyembekezera mwachidwi kuwululidwa kwa Ana a Mulungu! Pakuti chilengedwe chinagonjetsedwa ku zachabechabe, osati mwa kufuna kwake, koma chifukwa cha iye amene anachigonjetsa m'chiyembekezo: Chifukwa chilengedwe chenichenicho chidzamasulidwa ku ukapolo wa chivundi, kulowa mu ufulu waulemelero wa ana a Mulungu. Ndipo kotero Ine, Yesu Khristu, ndidzamasula chilengedwe m'chilungamo ndipo ndidzachiritsa dziko lapansi ndipo m'chilungamo ndidzalamulira. Ndipo ndidzagonjetsa ulamuliro wonse ndi mphamvu za mdani, ndipo ufumu wa Satana sudzaukanso.

Monga kwalembedwa, momwemonso kudzakhala, ndipo kotero ndinapangitsa Yohane Wovumbulutsa kulemba: Ndipo ndinaona kumwamba kutatseguka, ndipo taonani kavalo woyera, ndipo iye amene anakwera pa iye amatchedwa wokhulupirika ndi woona, ndipo m'chilungamo amaweruza ndi kuchita nkhondo. Maso ake anali ngati lawi lamoto, ndipo pamutu pake panali akorona ambiri, ndipo anali ndi dzina lolembedwa, lomwe palibe munthu analidziwa koma iye mwini. Ndipo anali atavala chovala choviikidwa m'magazi, ndipo dzina lake limatchedwa, Mawu a Mulungu. Ndipo magulu ankhondo omwe anali kumwamba anamutsatira pa akavalo oyera, ovala bafuta wabwino, woyera ndi woyera. Ndipo m'kamwa mwake mutuluka lupanga lakuthwa, kuti nalo, akanthe mitundu ya anthu: ndipo adzawalamulira ndi ndodo yachitsulo: ndipo akuponda chopondera mphesa cha ukali ndi mkwiyo wa Mulungu Wamphamvuyonse. Ndipo ali pa chovala chake, ndi pa ntchafu yake dzina lolembedwa, Mfumu Ya Mafumu, Ndi Mbuye Wa Ambuye. Ndipo chomwecho ndi chiyembekezo cha kubwera kwanga kwachiwiri, ndipo tsikulo likuyandikira kwambiri ndi sekondi iliyonse yomwe ikudutsa.

Taonani! Pa tsikulo ndidzabweretsa ankhondo anga pakuti chotero ndi cholowa chawo, ngakhale gulu lankhondo losafanana ndi lina lililonse, pakuti gulu lankhondo ngati langa silinakhalepo, gulu lankhondo losagonjetsedwa lopangidwa ndi owomboledwa, ngakhale omwe anabadwa akufa m'zolakwa ndi machimo koma tsopano adzakhala ndi moyo wosatha ndipo omwe sangaphedwe kapena kugonjetsedwa. Pakuti ndapatsidwa mphamvu ndi Yahweh chifukwa cha cholinga choterocho, ndipo ndidzakhala nditawapatsa mphamvu ndi kuwafupa chifukwa cha kukhulupirika kwawo ndi chikondi chawo pa ine ndi dzina langa, ngakhale Yesu Khristu! Pakuti aliyense amene amakonda ndi kutumikira Yeshuah HaMashiach amakondanso ndi kutumikira Yahweh, pakuti palibe wamkulu kuposa Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya wa Israyeli! Pa tsiku lomwe likubweralo mudzaona kumwamba kutatseguka, ndipo mudzandiona, Yesu Khristu, ndikutsogolera kuchokera kumwamba gulu lankhondo lalikulu ndi lamphamvu lakumwamba, ndipo lidzakhala gulu lankhondo losafanana ndi lina lililonse, mpaka kalekale! Pakuti Mkwatibwi wanga adzakhala ndi ine.

Ndipo ndidzaphwanya kotheratu chilombo ndi mneneri wonyenga ndi magulu awo ankhondo onse ndi zonyansa zawo zonse zomwe apanga monyansa ndipo kotero adzawonongedwa ngakhale ndi kuwala kwa kubwera kwanga ndi lupanga lakuthwa lakuthwa konsekonse lotuluka m'kamwa mwanga, ndipo sadzaima koma adzawonongedwa kotheratu. Ndipo chilombo ndi mneneri wonyenga adzaponyedwa amoyo m'nyanja yamoto ndi sulufule yoyaka ndipo sadzamasulidwa ku mazunzo awo, pakuti adzipanga okha kukhala oyenera. Koma choyamba dziko lapansi liyenera kuyeretsedwa kudzera m'masautso a Yakobo ndipo ambiri adzabwera kwa ine, ngakhale makamu ambiri ndipo mzimu uliwonse womwe uitana pa dzina la Ambuye mpaka mapeto udzapulumutsidwa kotheratu, ndipo Israyeli ayenera kusonkhanitsidwa pakuti ndidzapulumutsabe otsalira a Yakobo omwe adzabwerabe kwa Mesiya wawo weniweni ndi wamuyaya, pakuti ndine Yeshua HaMashiach ndipo ndikubwera monga malonjezo kwa Atate alengezedwa kuyambira kale ndi Yahweh mwiniwake, ndipo ndine kukwaniritsidwa kwawo ndipo nthawi yanga yafika.

Choncho kwa omwe akana dzina langa lopulumutsa, ngakhale Yesu wa ku Nazarete, ndikukulangizani kuti mumve mawu anga ndikumvetsetsa nthawi yomwe mukukhala ndikuthamangira ku phokoso la mawu anga ndikuitana pa dzina langa pakali pano ndikundivomereza ngati Ambuye wanu, chifukwa mulibe nthawi yotaya. Monga momwe kunali omwe anabadwa koyamba mu kubadwa kwatsopano pa kubadwa kwa mpingo wanga pa tsiku la Pentekoste monga kwalembedwa m'malemba, momwemonso kudzakhala omaliza kubwera ku kubadwa kwatsopano mu m'badwo uno wa chisomo, ngakhale onse omwe amabwera ku chipulumutso pamene kutsanulidwa kwanga kwa mzimu woyera mu m'badwo wanga wa chisomo ndi chifundo kukutha.

Ndipo kotero kusowa kwa zinthu zonse zaumulungu tsopano kudzagwa padziko lapansi ndipo uchimo tsopano udzakhala wosalamulirika pamene ndikuchotsa choletsa changa pamene ndikusonkhanitsa Mpingo wanga. Munthu wachibadwidwe wakana mawu anga, munthu wachibadwidwe wakana chisomo changa, munthu wachibadwidwe wakana chifundo changa, munthu wachibadwidwe wakana kuitana kwanga ku chilungamo, ndipo munthu wachibadwidwe wandikana ine Yesu wa ku Nazarete ndipo chifukwa chake munthu wachibadwidwe wakana Yahweh Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya amene anandituma, ndipo tsopano ndidzapatsa dziko lapansi mdima womwe amawufuna kwambiri.

Koma mvetsetsani kuti ambiri adzandichitira ntchito zamphamvu, ndipo mboni zanga ziwiri, ngakhale zoyikapo nyali zanga ziwiri zidzanenera, ndipo ndidzatumiza 144,000 anga kudzapulumutsa ambiri, choncho musaope! Chipulumutso chidzakwaniritsidwa, ngakhale kudzera mu chiwonongeko cha thupi ndipo makamu akulu adzabwera ku chipulumutso panthawi ino. Pakuti mwa chowonadi, Yahweh sanadzisiye ndipo sadzadzisiya wopanda mboni!

Nthawi za Amitundu zabala zipatso zambiri pakati pa omwe akanakhala opanda chiyembekezo pokhapokha ataphatikizidwa m'malonjezo opangidwa kwa Atate, ngakhale omwe amakhala ndi chikhulupiriyo cha Mwana wa Mulungu amene anawakonda ndikudzipereka yekha chifukwa cha chipulumutso chawo. Pakuti monga kwalembedwa: Wolungama adzakhala ndi moyo ndi chikhulupiriyo! Ndipo kotero mwa chikhulupiriyo mwa ine, Khristu Yesu, asanduka ana a chikhulupiriyo, ngakhale ana a chikhulupiriyo cha Abrahamu. Pakuti Abrahamu anakhulupirira Mulungu chizindikiro cha mdulidwe chisanaperekedwe, ndipo chinawerengedwa kwa iye kukhala chilungamo, ngakhale monga Mose akuchitira umboni! Ndipo kotero Abrahamu ndiye atate wa onse okhulupirira ndi omwe ayesedwa olungama ndi chikhulupiriyo mwa Yesu Khristu Wolungama, pakuti ndine wolungama ndi woyesa wolungama wa onse omwe amakhulupirira pa dzina langa lamphamvu!

Komabe ambiri agwiritsa ntchito chisomo changa ndi chifundo changa ngati chilolezo chochitira tchimo, ngakhale kusangalala ndi kudzitamandira ndi njira zawo zauchimo ndi zowononga, ngakhale kuphunzitsa ana awo kukhala m'njira zawo zauchimo ndipo kotero mibadwo ndi yoyipa m'njira zawo ndipo njira zawo ndi zonyansa ndipo kotero anthu oyipa ndi onyenga aipaipa. Musanyengedwe, ngati munthu afesa m'thupi munthuyo adzakolola chivundi m'thupi. Ndipo onse omwe akana mwayi wanga wa moyo wosatha ndikusankha chivundi cha Adamu adzakolola chipatso cha chivundi chomwecho mu chiweruzo chamuyaya, pakuti chiweruzo chili pano ndipo nthawi zowulula zafika.

Zoonadi, zoonadi, nthawi za chiweruzo chachikulu zoloseredwa kale ndi Enoke mlembi (pakuti umboni wake unaperekedwa kwa m'badwo uno), zafika padziko lapansi ngakhale monga kwalembedwa ndipo onse adzapereka mlandu. Omwe alandira mwayi wanga wa chipulumutso ndipo omwe apezeka okhulupirika mwa Khristu pakuwonekera kwanga adzasinthidwa kwamuyaya, kaya agona mwa Khristu kapena kaya ali amoyo ndikukhalabe mwa Khristu, omwe ali mwa Khristu pakuwonekera kwanga adzatengedwa kupita kumwamba kwa kumwamba. Kukhala mwa Khristu ndikuyenda ndi mzimu wanga wamphamvu, ngakhale lamulo la mzimu wa moyo! Ndipo omwe anakhala molungama mpingo wanga usanabadwe ndi kwa oyera mtima a chisautso ndi kuuka kwa olungama, kumene olungama adzauka ku moyo watsopano mu mphotho yaikulu, yosatha ndi yamuyaya. Kumapeto kwa ulamuliro wanga wa zaka 1000 kudzakhala chiweruzo cha mpando wachifumu woyera waukulu ndipo onse omwe anakhala ndi kusangalala mu chosalungama ndi uchimo adzabwezeredwa malinga ndi ntchito zawo ndipo ambiri adzagwa ku imfa yachiwiri pakuti asankha kutero.

Ndipo kotero nthawi zowulula zafika, ndipo uthenga wabwino wosatha wawululidwa mu nthawi yake ngakhale kabuku kakang'ono m'dzanja la mngelo wanga wamphamvu tsopano kawululidwa ndipo mawu a mabingu asanu ndi awiri alengezedwa! Pakuti ntchito iyi ndi ntchito yosatha, ndipo ndi pachimake pa mapangano ndi malonjezo akale mu mapangano ndi malonjezo a chatsopano, ngakhale Chipangano changa Chatsopano chomwe ndachivomereza m'magazi anga. Pakuti Ine Yeshua HaMashiach, ngakhale Yesu Khristu Wolungama amene ali Mfumu ya Mafumu ndi Mbuye wa Ambuye, ndine amene waperekedwa chifukwa cha kukwaniritsidwa kwa malonjezo onse ndi mapangano onse.

Pakuti Yohane Wovumbulutsa analemba zomwe anaziwona, ndipo kotero analemba: Ndipo ndinaona Mngelo wina wamphamvu akutsika kuchokera kumwamba, atavala mtambo, ndipo utawaleza unali pamutu pake, ndipo nkhope yake inali ngati dzuwa, ndi mapazi ake ngati zipilala zamoto. Ndipo anali ndi kabuku kotseguka m'dzanja lake: ndipo anaika phazi lake lamanja panyanja, ndi phazi lake lamanzere padziko lapansi. Ndipo kachiwiri Yohane Wovumbulutsa analengeza: Ndipo ndinaona Mngelo wina akuuluka pakati pa kumwamba, ali ndi uthenga wabwino wosatha, kuti alalikire kwa iwo omwe amakhala padziko lapansi, ndi kwa mtundu uliwonse, ndi fuko, ndi lilime, ndi anthu, akunena ndi mawu akulu, opani Mulungu, ndikumupatsa ulemerero, pakuti ola la chiweruzo chake lafika: ndipo mupembedzeni iye amene anapanga kumwamba ndi dziko lapansi, ndi nyanja, ndi akasupe amadzi.

Ndipo kotero kwalembedwa ndipo kotero kwachitika. Uthenga wabwino wosatha wafika kuti uwululidwe mu nthawi yake ndipo kabuku kakang'ono sikulinso chinsinsi. Mngelo wamphamvu amene wavala mtambo ndi utawaleza unali pamutu pake ndipo nkhope yake inali ngati dzuwa ndipo mapazi ake ngati zipilala zamoto. Utawaleza ndi chizindikiro choperekedwa pa pangano la Nowa lomwe Yahweh anapanga ndi Nowa pambuyo pa chigumula kuti sadzasefukiranso dziko lonse lapansi. Mtambo ndi zipilala zamoto zikuyimira pangano la Mulungu ndi Mose, lomangidwa mopitilira pa mapangano am'mbuyomu. Pakuti pamene Mose analankhula lamulo lililonse kwa anthu onse malinga ndi chilamulo, anatenga magazi a ana a ng'ombe ndi a mbuzi, ndi madzi, ndi ubweya wofiira, ndi hisope, ndikuwaza buku lonse, ndi anthu onse ndipo kotero Chipangano Chakale chinavomerezedwa!

Ndipo monga kwalembedwa mu Ahebri: Pakuti ngati magazi a ng'ombe ndi a mbuzi, ndi phulusa la ng'ombe yayikazi yowaza odetsedwa, zikuyeretsa mpaka kuyeretsedwa kwa thupi: Nanga magazi a Khristu, amene kudzera mwa Mzimu wamuyaya anadzipereka yekha wopanda chilema kwa Mulungu, adzayeretsa bwanji chikumbumtima chanu ku ntchito zakufa kuti mutumikire Mulungu wamoyo? Ndipo chifukwa cha ichi iye ndiye mkhalapakati wa chipangano chatsopano, kuti kudzera mu imfa, chifukwa cha chiwombolo cha zolakwa zomwe zinali pansi pa chipangano choyamba, iwo omwe aitanidwa alandire lonjezo la cholowa chamuyaya.

Ndipo kotero monga kwalembedwa kotero kwakwaniritsidwa okhulupirika anga, ndipo kotero ndi Chipangano Chatsopano. Ndipo ngakhale monga El Shaddai amene analamula kuwala kuti kuwale kuchokera mu mdima pachiyambi, mofananamo Mulungu Wamphamvuyonse wawala m'mitima yathu kuti apereke kuwala kwa chidziwitso cha ulemerero wa Mulungu, pankhope ya Yesu Khristu. Kotero zili, mu pangano langa latsopano lomwe lili ndi mphamvu zambiri, popeza ndinalivomereza ndi magazi anga opanda tchimo, makwaniritsidwa mapangano onse a nthawi zonse, pakuti ndinanena pa kalvare mu mpweya wanga womaliza: Kwatha. Ndipo kotero pangano latsopano linavomerezedwa ndi magazi anga mu Chipangano Chatsopano!

Ndipo kotero mwa ine, Khristu Yesu, mapangano akale akwaniritsidwa ndi ulemerero wa nkhope yotseguka, ngakhale pankhope ya mpulumutsi wanu wamuyaya ndi Mesiya, ngakhale ine Yeshuah HaMashiach. Ndipo mngelo uyu Yohane Wovumbulutsa akufotokoza anali ndi phazi limodzi panyanja ndi phazi limodzi padziko lapansi pakuti chipulumutso changa chili chotseguka kwa onse awiri ochokera kunyanja ya mitundu, (Amitundu) ndi omwe ochokera kudziko la lonjezo ngakhale onse omwe andipanga kukhala Ambuye wawo wamuyaya, ngakhale ambiri monga Ambuye Mulungu wathu adzawaitana! Ndipo kotero uthenga wanga wabwino wosatha udzapeza maso, makutu, ndi mitima ya onse omwe watumizidwa kwa iwo ndipo ambiri adzabwera ku chipulumutso pakuti mawu aliwonse a Mulungu ndi amoyo ndi amuyaya ndipo palibe mawu amodzi a Yahweh omwe adzalephera kapena kudutsa mpaka onse atakwaniritsidwa. Pakuti ndi Yahweh malonjezo onse ndi inde ndi ameni ndipo onse adzakwaniritsidwa ndipo Ine, Khristu Yesu, ndinadzipereka m'mazunzo opweteka kuti ndisindikize pangano langa latsopano m'magazi anga opanda tchimo, kamodzi kokha ndipo mpaka muyaya chipangano chatsopano chidzakhala ndi moyo.

Choncho imvani mawu anga ndikumvetsetsa kulankhula kwanga, ngakhale mawu a Ambuye wanu ndi Mesiya. Nthawi zafika ana anga ndipo mawu anga adzaza dziko lapansi ndipo aneneri anga ndi atumiki aphimba malekezero a dziko lapansi, ndipo m'zilankhulo zonse, ndi malilime, ndi mitundu uthenga wanga wabwino wa chipulumutso wamveka, ngakhale kumalekezero a dziko lapansi. Ndipo kotero zili, ndipo kotero ndikulengeza: Uthenga wanga wabwino umamangidwa nthawi zonse pa maziko a chilamulo ndi aneneri ndipo ndine mwala wapakona waukulu womwe kachisi yense wa Mulungu wamoyo amapumulapo! Ana anga, inu ndinu kachisi wa Mulungu wamoyo!

Ndipo kotero ndikukupatsani kuti mumvetsetse pali zigawo zambiri za uthenga wabwino wa Khristu! Kodi ndinu opusa mokwanira kukhulupirira kuti pali mauthenga abwino anayi okha? Ndipo palinso zinthu zina zambiri zomwe ndinachita, zomwe, ngati zikanalembedwa iliyonse, ndikuganiza kuti ngakhale dziko lapansi lenilenilo silingathe kukhala ndi mabuku omwe akadalembedwa. Ndipo kotero zili pali uthenga wabwino wa kubwera kwanga koyamba, pali uthenga wabwino wa chinsinsi cha Mulungu chomwe ndinawulula kwa Paulo Mtumwi, pali uthenga wabwino wosatha womwe umatitsogolera ku pachimake pa zinthu zonse. Ndi Yahweh palibe mapeto a uthenga wabwino!

Ndipo kotero ndikukufunsani funso? Kodi ndinu phungu wa Yahweh? Kodi akatswiri anu azaumulungu onama ndi atumiki ofunda amatha kupereka uphungu kwa Yahweh? Ndinu ndani, inu munthu, kuti mudziwe nthawi ndi momwe Yahweh amalankhulira ndi zomwe zili ndi chikhalidwe cha Mawu ake, kapena kwa ndani asankha kutumiza mawu ake? Ndipo kotero zili. Mawu a Mulungu adzapitiriza kuyenda ndipo maumboni onse adzamalizidwa ndipo mabuku adzalembedwa mpaka muyaya pakuti Mawu a Mulungu ali ndi moyo ndipo amakhala kwamuyaya!

Ndipo kotero ndikukufunsani, kodi Chipangano Chatsopano cha Khristu Yesu chinafafaniza Chipangano Chakale ndi mapangano Akale? Mulungu aletse! Mawu aliwonse a Mulungu ndi oyera ndipo onse adzakwaniritsidwa ngakhale kamodzi, onse, ndi kwamuyaya! Ndipo kotero ndi chikhalidwe cha Mulungu wosasinthika! Kuzungulira mpando wake wachifumu kuli zikwi khumi kuwirikiza zikwi khumi komabe safuna phungu. Yahweh, Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya amagwira ntchito zonse pambuyo pa uphungu wa chifuniro chake ndipo iye ndi Mulungu wosatha! Komabe wawulula mapulani ake, ngakhale chifuniro chake chamuyaya ndi chosatha pang'onopang'ono. Chifukwa chiyani Yahweh angachite chinthu choterocho mungafunse? Kodi simunawerenge zomwe Paulo Mtumwi analemba pamene ndinamupangitsa kulemba? Koma timalankhula nzeru za Mulungu m'chinsinsi, ngakhale nzeru zobisika, zomwe Mulungu anatsimikiza dziko lisanakhale, ku ulemerero wathu. Zomwe palibe mwa mafumu a dziko lino anazidziwa: pakuti akadanazidziwa, sakadapachika Ambuye wa ulemerero.

Ndipo kotero Mulungu wamuyaya, ngakhale Yahweh amene safuna phungu ndipo mu nzeru zake zosatha amawulula njira zake ndi Mawu m'njira yomwe asankha pakukwaniritsa mapulani ake osatha a chiwombolo kwa anthu. Mvetsetsani okondedwa anga, ngakhale Mkwatibwi wanga, kuti mwakhala mu mtima wa Yahweh kuyambira chilengedwe chisanakhale ndipo mwabisidwa mwanzeru mu mtima wa Atate ndipo kwabisidwa kwa inu zomwe mudzakhala pakuwonekera kwanga, pakuti mwafa ndipo moyo wanu wabisidwa mwa ine Khristu Yesu. Ndipo pamene ndiwonekera, inunso mudzawonekera ndi ine mu ulemerero! Ana anga ang'onoang'ono mwatsala pang'ono kulengedwanso kwathunthu mwa ine ndipo mudzadabwa ndi zomwe Yahweh wakonzera omwe amamukonda. Pakuti monga momwe ziliri palibe munthu padziko lapansi amene wamvetsetsa kwathunthu zomwe ana a Mulungu adzakhala pa chiwombolo chawo chathunthu, ndipo mwatsala pang'ono kudziwa pakuti tsiku lafika ndipo kuwonekera kwanga kuli pafupi.

Ndikulangiza mmodzi ndipo ndikulangiza onse, ngakhale Khristu Yesu Ambuye wanu wamuyaya! Landirani Mawu anga ndikupeza nzeru ndi kumvetsetsa, pakuti umunthu wanu weniweni ndi chinsinsi chamuyaya chobisika mwa Mulungu mwiniwake chomwe chatsala pang'ono kuwululidwa kwathunthu. Posachedwapa mudzipeza mutayima m'chipinda chenicheni cha mpando wachifumu wa Yahweh, ngakhale Atate wathu, ndipo akuyembekezera mwachidwi ndikulakalaka kupezeka kwanu pakali pano, ngakhale kuyambira muyaya wapitayo. Ndipo maholo akuluakulu kumwamba akonzedwa chifukwa cha kufika kwanu ndipo matebulo akhazikitsidwa ndipo ukwati wa Mkwati kwa Mkwatibwi wake uli pafupi ndipo mkwatibwi wanga wadzikonzera yekha ndipo ndidzawalandira kwa ine ndekha ndipo mpaka muyaya adzakhala ndi ine mu pangano losatha ndipo adzachita ntchito zambiri zamphamvu zomwe sizinalowe m'mitima ya anthu panthawiyi.

Ndipo kotero Yahweh Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya ndi wosatha wakonza ndi kukonzekera zinthu zambiri kwa inu, ndipo Ine, Khristu Yesu Ambuye wokwezedwa ndi wokwera kumwamba ndili ndi mphotho yanu m'dzanja langa ngakhale kwa okhulupirika anga. Ndipo gulu lakumwamba likuyembekezera mwachidwi kupezeka kwanu ndipo mu mphotho yakumwamba ndi yosatha tsopano mudzapita. Choncho musaope chifukwa miyoyo yanu tsopano iwululidwa ndipo pa sekondi iliyonse tsopano lipenga lomaliza lidzalira, kodi mwakonzekera kuwonekera kwanga ana anga ang'onoang'ono? Pakuti kuli pafupi kuposa momwe mukuganizira ndipo nthawi pakati pa pamenepo ndi tsopano ilibe phindu ndipo imatumikira zolinga zosatha za Yahweh Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya!

Kodi simukudziwa? kapena simunamve, kuti Mulungu wosatha, Ambuye walenga malekezero a dziko lapansi? sakomoka, kapena kutopa ndipo palibe kufufuza kwa kumvetsetsa kwake.

Ndine amene ndimalankhula za zinthu zomwe zikubwera ngati kuti zachitika kale.

Ndine amene ndakuitanani ku moyo wosatha ndikukusindikizani ndi chidindo cha Mulungu wosatha!

Ndine mwala wapakona waukulu womwe nyumba yonse imapumulapo.

Ndine Yeshua HaMashiach ndipo ndidzapulumutsabe Yakobo!

Ndine wolungama ndi woyesa wolungama wa onse omwe amakhulupirira pa dzina langa lamphamvu!

Dziyeretseni okhulupirika anga, ndipo musalole manja anu kuda! Pitirizani kudzisambitsa m'magazi anga otetezera kuti muyime pamaso panga olengedwa kumene ndi oyera ngati namwali pakuti ndakusambitsani m'magazi anga otetezera. Yang'anani kumwamba!
