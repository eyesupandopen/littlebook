Pamene Adzati Mtendere ndi Chitetezo!

Kuchokera kwa Yeshua HaMashiach yemwenso ndi Khristu Yesu:

Imvani Mawu a Yeshua HaMashiach, amene Yahweh wamutumiza monga Mesiya wanu, Mfumu, Ambuye, ndi Mkulu wa Ansembe kwamuyaya monga mwa Ndondomeko ya Melikizedeki ndipo weramitsani mitima yanu pomvera Mawu anga chifukwa ndikubwera kudzachenjeza okhulupirika mu Mpingo wanga, ngakhale Mkwatibwi wanga, (ndi onse amene ali ndi makutu akumva kaya ali pafupi kapena kutali) kachiwiri za chinyengo champhamvu chamakono chomwe tsopano chikugwira ntchito mokwanira padziko lonse lapansi, chomwe chidzaipiraipira pamene woletsa adzachotsedwa posachedwa, ndiponso modzidzimutsa! Kodi sindinanene kuti mlenje amatchera msampha mbalame ikuona? Inu a chikhulupiriro chochepa!

Chotero ndikupatsani inu kuti mumvetsetse kachiwiri zomwe ndinaphunzitsa abale anga pamene ndinabwera ku Israyeli monga Mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu, choncho imvani Mawu anga! Pakuti kudzauka akhristu onyenga, ndi aneneri onyenga, amene adzaonetsa zizindikiro zazikulu ndi zozizwitsa, kotero kuti, ngati kuli kotheka, adzasocheretsa ngakhale osankhidwa. Taonani, ndakuuzani kale. Chifukwa chake ngati adzati kwa inu, Taonani, ali kuchipululu, musatuluke: Taonani, ali m'zipinda zamkati, musakhulupirire. Pakuti monga mphezi imatuluka kum'mawa, nikuonekera mpaka kumadzulo, kotero kudzakhala kubwera kwa Mwana wa Munthu! Chotero ndikupatsani inu kuti mumvetsetse kuti kuonekera kwanga kuli pafupi ndipo kukubwera modzidzimutsa ndi mwamphamvu, ndipo sindidzakanidwa chuma changa chomwe ndachilipirira ndi magazi anga, ngakhale mkwatibwi wanga wokhulupirika ndi thupi langa, ngakhale mpingo wanga wokhulupirika womwe ndimaukonda ndi chikondi chosatha! Ndinu anga!

Ndipo zili choncho, okondedwa anga, kuti akhristu onyenga auka ndipo akhalapo ambiri. Koma tsopano nthawi za chinjoka zafika ndipo nsautso ya Yakobo yafika ndipo onse otsala omwe ali a Adamu padziko lapansi ayenera kupanga chisankho chamuyaya. Chifukwa kwaperekedwa kwa chinjoka kulamulira dziko lapansi pamodzi ndi abusa ake awiri a mphere, ngakhale chilombo chochokera kunyanja ndi chilombo chochokera pansi, yemwenso ndi mneneri wawo wonyenga. Kodi mudzadzisunga kuti musagwe mu chinyengo champhamvu pomvera mawu anga? Kodi mungayese kutsatira dziko lapansi mu zinyengo zake ndi mabodza ake ku chiwonongeko chotheratu mwa kusamvera mawu anga, ngakhale pamene ndikubwera kudzachenjezani za chinyengo champhamvu chamakono chomwe chagonjetsa dziko lapansi? Kodi mwalola mitima yanu ndi chikhulupiriro chanu kugwedezeka ndi chinyengo champhamvu choperekedwa kwa inu kaamba ka chiyembekezo chonyenga?

Ndipo zili choncho kuti palibe chomwe chingasinthe zigamulo zoperekedwa ndi Yahweh mwiniwake pa Babulo Wachinsinsi, ngakhale chiweruzo chomwe chaperekedwa m'manja mwanga amphamvu kuti ndichimasule pamene okhulupirika asonkhanitsidwa ndipo woletsa wachotsedwa, ndipo chidzachitika modzidzimutsa monga momwe analonjezera! Ngakhale ziweruzo zomwe zinalengezedwa monga chenjezo kwa okhala mu Babulo Wachinsinsi, chenjezo lomwe analinyalanyaza kotheratu pamene ankanyodola mopanda chikhulupiriro. Ngakhale machenjezo anga ambiri, ambiri operekedwa m'kamwa mwa aneneri anga ambiri omwe alengeza mokhulupirika Mawu awa achenjezo kwa zaka zambiri tsopano. Machenjezo omwe ndinawaletsa ndipo m'malo mwawo tsopano pali zilengezo, ngakhale zilengezo za Yahweh, ndi za Yeshua HaMashiach, ngakhale zilengezo zomwe sizingatheke kuti bungwe lililonse liziimitse kapena kuzisintha. Pakuti chomwe chalengezedwa mu mphamvu ndi mpando wachifumu wa Yahweh palibe amene angachisinthe, pakuti njira za Mulungu Wamuyaya wa Chilungamo zili choncho, amene amagwira ntchito zonse monga mwa uphungu wa chifuniro chake.

Ndipo chotero ndikuuzani okhulupirika anga, monga momwe ndinauzira Simoni Petulo pa ola la nsembe yanga: Simoni, Simoni, taona, Satana wakhumba inu, kuti akupeteni ngati tirigu. Koma ndakupempherera, kuti chikhulupiriro chako chisalephere: chifukwa chake pamene watembenuka, ulimbitse abale ako. Ndipo chotero ndikubwera kwa inu mu kuchedwa kwa ola lomalizali (pakuti ola latsala pang'ono kutha) kudzachenjezani mofananamo kuti mudzisunge m'njira ndipo ndikulamulani inu omwe mukuwala ngati kuwala kwa Dziko lapansi kuti mulimbitse abale anu ndi kulimbitsana wina ndi mzake ndi Mawu awa mu chikhulupiriro chachikondi ndi kumvera kuti nonse mudzisunge ndi kudziteteza m'njira, chotero mverani mawu anga ndipo yendani momwemo.

Taonani! Kwalembedwa: Ndipo chifukwa cha ichi Mulungu adzawatumizira chinyengo champhamvu, kuti akhulupirire bodza: kuti onse atsutsidwe amene sanakhulupirire choonadi, koma anakondwera ndi chosalungama. Kodi mudzagonjera chinyengo ichi chomwe sichanu, okondedwa anga? Indetu, indetu, ndinena kwa mpingo wanga, kodi mudzathamangira m'manja mwa onyenga awa? Ngakhale omwe atumizidwa kudzakuwonongeni ndi chinjoka chenichenicho? Chifukwa chake, okondedwa anga, pitirizani kutuluka pakati pawo ndipo mukhale osiyana! Musatenge nawo mbali mu ntchito zawo zoipa ndi ziyembekezo zawo chifukwa ambiri asangalatsidwa ndi ufiti wawo ndi matsenga awo, ndipo uyo amene amusankha tsopano akulankhula ngati chinjoka, ngakhale kupanga malonjezo akulu ndi nkhani zazikulu za iye mwini ndipo ambiri adzatsatira njira zake zowononga ndipo ambiri adzanyengedwa ndi kukodwa mu msampha ndi kugwidwa, chotero tembenukirani ku phokoso la mawu anga ndipo imvani mawu anga ndipo thamangirani kwa ine okondedwa anga, musatembenukire kumanzere, kapena kumanja ndipo ndidzakusungani bwino m'manja mwanga mamuyaya momwe palibe amene angakuchotseni m'manja mwanga amphamvu.

Nanga n'chifukwa chiyani mukulola mitima yanu kuvutika, okhulupirika anga ndi oona, ngakhale omwe ndimawakonda ndi chikondi chosatha, ngakhale onse omwe chinjoka chikufuna kuwakola mu chinyengo ndi chiweruzo chomwe sichanu. Chifukwa chake, musagwirizane ndi zinyengo zawo ndipo tulukani pakati pawo ndipo mukhale osiyana! Ndipo kwa inu omwe mwatembenuka moyenera kuchoka ku chinyengo champhamvu, ndikukulamulani kuti musabwerere ngakhale pang'ono. Taonani! Ngakhale Yahweh, Mulungu Wamkulu Wamuyaya, kapena Yeshua HaMashiach sadzatumiza kapena kutumiza njira zothetsera ndale kapena olamulira a dziko lapansi kuti athetse mavuto a dziko lapansi panthawiyi. Ngakhale mavuto omwe akubwera mu chiweruzo pa omwe ali padziko lapansi kuchokera kwa Yahweh mwiniwake, chiweruzo chomwe palibe amene angachithawe, kupatula okhulupirika mwa Khristu Yesu. Mvetsetsani ndendende zomwe ndinauza Paulo kuti alembere Aefeso mu kalata yomwe ndinamuuza kuti alembe m'malo mwanga kwa mpingo wanga wokondedwa:

Pomaliza, abale anga, khalani amphamvu mwa Ambuye, ndi mu mphamvu ya ukulu wake. Valani zida zonse za Mulungu, kuti muthe kuima polimbana ndi machenjera a mdierekezi. Pakuti sitilimbana ndi thupi ndi magazi, koma ndi maufumu, ndi maulamuliro, ndi olamulira a dziko lapansi, akalonga a mdima wa dziko lino, ndi mizimu yoipa, yomwe ili m'malo akumwamba. Chotero mvetsetsani okondedwa anga kuti mawu anga sasinthika, nanga n'chifukwa chiyani ine, Khristu Yesu Ambuye wanu wokwezedwa, ndingakutumizireni bwanamkubwa wapadziko lapansi, kapena wolamulira kapena mfumu yomwe ingaletse ziweruzo zomwe zikubwera? Kodi Yahweh, yemwe chifuniro chake ndimachikwaniritsa bwino lomwe ndipo yemwe n'kosatheka kuti anganame angadzitsutse bwanji mu mapulani ake amuyaya omwe aperekedwa kwa ine mokwanira kuti ndiwakwaniritse?

Chifukwa olamulira onse a dziko lapansi apereka chilolezo chawo ndi mphamvu zawo kwa chinjoka, pakuti zaperekedwa kwa iwo kuti akwaniritse chifukwa ngakhale adani a Yahweh amamvera mawu ake ndipo adzakwaniritsa chifuniro chake chomwe chaperekedwa kwa iwo mu nyengo yaifupi kwambiri iyi yomwe yatsala pang'ono kugwa padziko lapansi ndi mkwiyo waukulu! Pakuti nthawi za mkwiyo wa Mwanawankhosa ndi mkwiyo wa Mulungu Wamphamvuyonse zafika ndipo palibe chomwe chingalepheretse, kapena kuletsa ngakhale pang'ono zomwe zatsala pang'ono kumasulidwa ndikasonkhanitsa okhulupirika anga kumwamba. Ndipo chotero ndikufuula! Ndani angatsutsane ndi chifuniro chamuyaya cha Yahweh, NDINE wamkulu?!

Komabe, mvetsetsani mu nthawi yanga ya chisomo ndinalangiza Mpingo wanga kupempherera olamulira awo a dziko lapansi, podziwa kuti olamulira awo a dziko lapansi anali oipa mwachibadwa ndipo kuti mwa kupembedzera kwa oyera mtima zoipa zikanaletsedwa kupitirira (ndipo woletsa wanga akadaletsabe), ndipo olamulira awa akadasankha zomwe zingagwire ntchito kaamba ka ubwino ndi moyo wabwino wa omwe amakonda Mulungu, ndipo inali nthawi yanga ya chisomo, ngakhale mazunzo anali ochuluka. Ndipo ngakhale, pemphero lamphamvu la munthu wolungama limathandiza kwambiri, mvetsetsani kuti palibe atsogoleri a dziko lapansi operekedwa kaamba ka chipulumutso cha dziko lapansi mpaka nditabwera ndi gulu lalikulu la Mafumu ndi Ansembe amuyaya omwe adzalamulira dziko lapansi mu chilungamo pa lamulo langa chifukwa ndine Ambuye wa Ambuye ndi Mfumu ya Mafumu ndipo ndine Mkulu wa Ansembe kwamuyaya monga mwa Ndondomeko ya Melikizedeki.

Mvetsetsani kwathunthu ndipo mopanda kugonjera, palibe atsogoleri a dziko lapansi omwe angabweretse mtendere padziko lapansi. Palibe atsogoleri a dziko lapansi omwe angabweretse chitetezo padziko lapansi. Palibe atsogoleri a dziko lapansi omwe angabwezeretse chitukuko padziko lapansi. Ngakhale, adzalonjeza mtendere ndi chitetezo ndi chitukuko chobwezeretsedwa, mvetsetsani kuti alibe mphamvu zopereka zinthu zomwe amalonjeza, ngakhale adzapereka katundu wopanda pake wa dziko lapansi kwa omwe alandira chizindikiro cha chilombo, ngakhale malipiro a chosalungama omwe adzachotsedwa kotheratu kwa iwo mu chiweruzo! Chotero musawatsatire chifukwa aliyense ndi onse omwe amatsatira pamapeto pake adzafunidwa kulandira chizindikiro cha chilombo ndipo omwe atero amaperekedwa kwamuyaya ku malawi amoto osazimitsidwa a chilango ndipo dziko lapansi ndi katundu wake wopanda pake zikupita. Ndipo chinjoka chidzauka mokwanira ndi zilombo zake, ndipo chilombo chochokera pansi chakonza ululu wake woperekedwa ndi kulumidwa kwa njoka kuti chibaye ululu wake ndipo chitsulo chidzasakanizidwa ndi dongo, koma sizidzaphatikizana wina ndi mzake.

Ndipo chotero kwalembedwa ndi kusungidwa ndendende mu umboni wa kuvumbulutsidwa kwanga, ngakhale Chivumbulutso cha Yesu Khristu kuti onse amvetsetse omwe ali ndi maso akuona ndi makutu akumva, ndi mtima wofewa kuti amvetsetse, ndipo chotero Yohane analemba: Ndipo mngelo anati kwa ine, Wazizwira chiyani? Ndidzakuuza chinsinsi cha mkaziyo, ndi cha chilombo chomunyamula, chomwe chili ndi mitu isanu ndi iwiri ndi nyanga khumi. Chilombo chomwe unachiona chinali, ndipo palibe; ndipo chidzatuluka m'phompho, ndi kupita ku chiwonongeko: ndipo iwo okhala padziko lapansi adzazizwa omwe mayina awo sanalembedwe m'buku la moyo kuyambira kukhazikitsidwa kwa dziko lapansi, pamene aona chilombo chomwe chinali, ndipo palibe, koma chilipo.

Chifukwa chake, ana anga ang'ono landirani maphunziro ndipo ndikupatsani inu kuti mumvetsetse ngakhale ufumu wa chinjoka sungathe kuuka mokwanira (chifukwa woletsa adzaletsa mpaka woletsa saletsanso, ngakhale pamene okhulupirika mwa Khristu atengedwa kumwamba) ogwa akuloledwa kukonzekera kuukira kwawo kwadzidzidzi ndipo Mulungu amatumiza omwe amakhala padziko lapansi mu kusamvera chinyengo champhamvu. Koma zinthu izi sizinakonzedwere inu okhulupirika anga ndi osankhidwa, ndipo ine, Yeshua HaMashiach ndikulengeza ndi kuchitira umboni choonadi ichi ndi mawu awa omwe ndatumiza kudzera mwa mthenga wanga ndi mtumiki wanga kuti awerengedwe kaamba ka chitonthozo ndi kumvera kwa onse omwe mawu awa akonzedwera. Ndipo chotero ndikufunsa inu omwe mungakonde chinyengo ichi m'malo mwa choonadi changa chamuyaya, n'chifukwa chiyani mukuzizwa? Kodi mudzasankhabe mabodza a abusa a mphere m'malo mwa choonadi ndi chitonthozo chomwe mzimu wa uneneri umabweretsa?

Chifukwa chake, ndikupatsani inu kuti mumvetsetse kuti abusa awiri a mphere awa omwe alipo ndipo palibe ndipo adzakhalapo MOKWANIRA, sanauke mokwanira, ndipo adzauka mokwanira kuchokera ku phulusa ndi mabwinja a dongosolo lakale ndipo dongosolo lawo latsopano lidzauka mokwanira! Tsopano adzisankhira okha Moshiach wawo wonyenga, ngakhale Baraba, ndipo adzamanga kachisi wawo, ndipo chilombo chochokera kunyanja chikuwongolerabe chidole chake chokalamba ndipo adzapambana, ndipo adzabweretsa chiwonongeko chokwanira cha Babulo Wachinsinsi, pakuti mwa chinyengo amachita nkhondo ndipo umu ndi momwe chilengedwe chawo chilili.

Ndipo atumizidwa ndi kangaude yemwe walowa m'maiko, ndipo ndi kangaude yemwe wamusankha kuti atsogolere maiko pamene auka mokwanira. Chifukwa ndi Sunagoge wa Satana yemwe wapanga chisankho chawo ndikusankha Moshiach wawo watsopano wonyenga ndipo Babulo Wachinsinsi waperekanso chilolezo chake kwa chinjoka, ndipo chotero adzaperekedwa mokwanira m'manja mwa chinjoka chomwe chimawapatsa mphamvu kuti apereke mkwiyo ndi chiweruzo, chiweruzo chomwe chimatumizidwa ngakhale ngati gawo lowirikiza kuchokera kwa Yahweh mwiniwake, chiweruzo chomwe sichichedwa ndipo chili pafupi ndipo chotero adzisankhira okha chiwonongeko chawo.

Ndipo zili choncho kuti Babulo Wachinsinsi tsopano akulengeza tsogolo lake kukhala lopanda chiweruzo cha Yahweh pamene akufuna kuthawa pamene akunyodola mu njira zawo zopanda chikhulupiriro. Chifukwa Babulo Wachinsinsi walukidwa mokwanira mu ukonde wa kangaude, ndipo sangathe kuthawa, ndipo Babulo Wachinsinsi ali m'maunyolo akuyembekezera chiweruzo chake chomaliza. Ndipo adzapitiriza kuwononga, kuba, kupha, kufunkha ndi kutenga mwamphamvu chitukuko chake ndi mbadwa zake pakuti zaperekedwa kwa iwo kuti akwaniritse ndipo chipululu chidzatsala komwe adzafunkha dziko lapansi kupitirira kaamba ka chuma chomwe amachilakalaka kwambiri.

Ndipo chiwonongeko chadzidzidzi chidzagwa modzidzimutsa, ndipo mu ola limodzi adzawonongedwa. Kachiwiri ndinena, kuti nkhondo idzayambika modzidzimutsa padziko lapansi, ndipo moto udzagwa, ndipo mizinga yambiri adzaitumiza kaamba ka chiwonongeko chake. Mzinda wake waukulu kwambiri udzayaka ndipo mafunde akulu a chiwonongeko adzagonjetsa magombe ake akum'mawa ndi akumadzulo. Dziko lapansi lidzagwedezeka mwamphamvu, ndipo adzagawidwa pakati ndipo pomwe poyamba panali malo otukuka, madzi adzatsala omwe adzamugawa pawiri, ndipo adzagwa mokwanira mu gawo lake lowirikiza la chiweruzo. Ngakhale chiweruzo chomwe anadzisankhira okha pamene ananyalanyaza mawu anga omwe ndinatumiza kudzera mwa atumwi anga ndi aneneri ndi atumiki akulu ndi ang'ono omwe: indetu wanyalanyaza aliyense ndi onse ku chiwonongeko chake ndipo sadzaukanso. Ndipo chotero dziko lapansi laperekedwa m'manja mwa chinjoka ndipo adzapereka ulamuliro wake wa gehena kwa abusa ake a mphere omwe wawaulutsa kaamba ka zolinga zake ndipo aipitsidwa kwamuyaya.

Chifukwa chake, musagwe mu chinyengo chawo chomwe chilombo chochokera pansi chimabweretsa chifukwa chimalankhula ngati chinjoka. Musatsatire omwe a Babulo Wachinsinsi omwe akuyembekezera chiweruzo chawo, pakuti chiweruzo chake chidzabwera mu ola limodzi ndipo modzidzimutsa palibe! Chifukwa omwe akadanatsatira Yesu wa ku Nazarete monga Ambuye wawo tsopano akopeka m'manja mwa wonyenga chifukwa amakonda kukhala mu zinyengo zawo, poganiza kuti monga momwe ziliri lero, zinali dzulo, ndipo ndithudi mfumu yayikulu iyi idzandipanganso wamkulu kachiwiri! Koma, iwe Babulo Wachinsinsi, sudzakhalanso wamkulu ndipo machimo ako akadalipo ndipo sudzathawa chiweruzo chako chomwe chili pa iwe.

Ndipo ndine Yeshua HaMashiach, ngakhale Mfumu ya Mafumu ndi Ambuye wa Ambuye yemwe sanapereke mfumu padziko lapansi panthawiyi chifukwa palibe ulamuliro wina womwe Yahweh wakhazikitsa, kupatula Ufumu wa Kumwamba womwe, Ine Yeshua HaMashiach ndine Mfumu ya Mafumu ndi Ambuye wa Ambuye! Chotero mvetsetsani kuti onse omwe adzipereka okha kwa wonyenga uyu ndikusiya Ambuye ndi mpulumutsi wawo Yesu Khristu adzasiyidwa ku moto wa woyenga chifukwa muli m'manja mwa Khristu wonyenga, yemwenso ndi Moshiach wawo wonyenga. Ndipo chotero kulekanitsidwa komaliza kwafika kaamba ka nthawi yanga ya chisomo ndipo ambiri anyengedwa ndikutengedwa ngati chofunkha ndi chilombo chochokera pansi. Ndipo ngakhale, ambiri adzachoka mu chinyengo chake kupita ku moyo wosatha kudzafunika nsautso ya Yakobo kuti iwaswe kuchoka ku chinyengo champhamvu ngati atanyalanyaza kuitana kwanga kuti alape nthawi yomweyo mu masekondi omalizawa.

Ndipo chotero ndikupatsani inu kuti mumvetsetse kuti Yahweh wapereka lipenga lomaliza kuti lilire, ngakhale lipenga langa lomaliza mu nthawi yanga ya chisomo ndipo lipenga lidzalira. Chifukwa nthawi yafika ndipo ili pa inu okondedwa anga, pomwe ndidzatsika kuchokera kumwamba ndi mfuu, ndi mawu a mngelo wamkulu ndi lipenga la Mulungu, ndipo akufa mwa Khristu adzauka choyamba ndipo inu omwe muli mwa Khristu mudzakuphatikizani nawo mumlengalenga ndipo chotero mudzakhala nane nthawi zonse okondedwa anga ndipo sindidzasiya okhulupirika anga ku moto wa woyenga chifukwa kuyenga kwawo ndakumaliza mu chikondi changa pa iwo chifukwa anabwera kwa ine mofunitsitsa ngakhale pamene ndinavumbulutsidwa kwa iwo.

Choncho mvetsetsani okondedwa anga kuti nthawi yakwana yoti chinjoka chidzuke kwathunthu ndipo nkhondo ndi chiweruzo zibwere kwathunthu ndipo chilombo chochokera kunyanja ndi chilombo chochokera pansi chidzuke kwathunthu ndi chinjoka chawo. Ndikanapanda kufupikitsa masikuwo palibe thupi limene likanatsala lamoyo, choncho kuukira kwawo kodzidzimutsa kudzabwera kwathunthu padziko lapansi ndipo adzawononga kotheratu Babulo Wachinsinsi. Choncho musagwe m'zinyengo zawo ndi mabodza pamene akudziyamikira okha ndi kunena poyera pamaso pa ziweruzo za Yahweh ndi mkwiyo wa Mwanawankhosa kuti "mtendere ndi chitetezo" "mtendere ndi chitetezo"! Chifukwa Ine ndine Yeshua HaMashiach amene ndidzachotsa kwathunthu mtendere ndi chitetezo padziko lapansi mpaka mapeto a sabata la makumi asanu ndi awiri la Danieli pamene ndidzakhazikitsa ufumu wanga kwathunthu padziko lapansi. Choncho, musagwe m'chinyengo chawo.

Ndipo Ine ndine amene ndikutumiza Mawu awa kudzera mwa mthenga wanga ndi mtumiki wanga ndipo kudzera mwa mthenga wanga ndi mtumiki wanga Mawu awa atumizidwa.

Taonani! Nthawi yakwana yoti Ambuye wanu awonetse ulemerero wake ndi ukulu wake m'mwamba kuti onse aone ndipo ndidzasonkhanitsa okhulupirika anga onse mwa Khristu ngakhale kuchokera kumalekezero a dziko lapansi pamene ndikuonekera mumlengalenga kusonkhanitsa okhulupirika mwa Khristu ndipo kuonekera kwanga kudzaoneka m'mwamba mofanana ndi mphezi yotuluka kum'mawa, ndi kuonekera mpaka kumadzulo, ndipo sindidzakanidwa!

Ine ndine Yeshua HaMashiach amene ndidzabwera kudzaweruza ndi kuchita nkhondo padziko lapansi ndipo ufumu wanga sudzakhala ndi mapeto.

Ine ndine amene NDINE wamkulu wandipanga ndipo nthawi yakwana.

Ine ndine Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda ndipo nthawi zanga zakwana.

Zonse zidzakhala mpaka kalekale, Inde ndi Amen!

Kuchokera kwa Yahweh Mulungu wamkulu Wamuyaya:

Imvani, Imvani mawu a Mulungu Wosatha ndi Wamuyaya! Nthawi za masautso a Yakobo zafika padziko lapansi ndipo nthawi zokolola zafika. Kenako mwadzidzidzi ndi mosayembekezereka mwana wanga, Khristu Yesu, amene alinso Yeshua HaMashiach adzaonekera m'mitambo kuti onse akufa ndi amoyo mwa Khristu adzuke pansi mu kusafa ndi kusawonongeka kuti aime atangolengedwa kumene mu muyaya wonse ngakhale pamene adzaima m'chipinda changa chachifumu, ngakhale nthawi imene ndakhala ndikuilakalaka ndi kuikhumba mu nthawi zamuyaya zapitazo.

Choncho, mverani Yeshua HaMashiach chifukwa ndapereka lamulo ndipo nthawi imeneyo ikuyandikira ndithu ndipo tsiku ndi nthawi yeniyeni tsopano zikudziwika kumwamba, ngakhale kuti sizinavumbulutsidwe kwathunthu padziko lapansi. Ndipo choncho nthawi za kulira kwa lipenga lomaliza zafika ndipo zipatso zoyamba zidzasonkhanitsidwa ndipo mkwati adzadziphatika yekha mu pangano lamuyaya kwa mkwatibwi wake wokhulupirika ndipo adzakwaniritsa zolinga zanga zamuyaya ndipo Chipangano Chatsopano cha Yeshua HaMashiach chidzakhazikitsidwa kwathunthu mpaka kalekale, chifukwa malonjezo anga onse ndi Inde ndi Amen!

Okhulupirika mwa Khristu adzaima pa mpando woweruza wa Ambuye wawo ndikulandira mphoto ya ntchito zawo kaya zikhale zabwino kapena zoipa koma onsewo ali ndi moyo wosatha. Ndipo ambiri adzatenga mphoto yaikulu yotere yomwe siinalowe mumtima mwa anthu kwathunthu zinthu zomwe ndiwakonzekera. Chifukwa Khristu Yesu adzawapanga kukhala Mafumu ndi Ansembe pamaso pa mpando wanga wachifumu ndipo adzalandira kwathunthu mipando yawo yachifumu ndi ulamuliro kuchokera kwa Yeshua HaMashiach mu cholowa chake chomwe ndamupatsa kuti agawane ndi okhulupirika, ndi oitanidwa, ndi otsimikiziridwa, ndi oona.

Ndipo choncho ndikulengeza kwa okhala padziko lapansi kuti amvere ndi kumvera kwathunthu mawu a Yeshua HaMashiach chifukwa akubwera kudzaweruza ndi kuchita nkhondo ndipo kulekanitsa komaliza kukuyandikira. Koma choyamba nthawi za sabata la makumi asanu ndi awiri la Danieli zidzakwaniritsidwa ndipo Yesu Khristu amene alinso Yeshua HaMashiach akubwera padziko lapansi kudzapulumutsa otsala a Yakobo chifukwa NDINE amene NDINE, ngakhale YHVH Mulungu Wamkulu Wamuyaya, ndipo ndani amene ali ngati ine? Wofanana nane palibe, ndipo ndithudi sadzakhalapo.

NDINE amene ndapereka mapulani anga amuyaya omwe ndinawabisa mumtima mwanga mpaka atavumbulutsidwa kwa Mwana wanga, Khristu Yesu ndipo Chinsinsi Chachikulu cha Mulungu chavumbulutsidwa ndipo chatsala pang'ono kukwaniritsidwa. Ndipo ndapereka Uthenga Wabwino Wamuyaya kwa Mwana wanga Khristu Yesu ndipo ndauvumbulutsa pamodzi ndi iye kwa mthenga wanga wosankhidwa amene wabwerera mokhulupirika ku kuitana kwake kwamuyaya ndipo adzakwaniritsa zolinga zanga zamuyaya pansi pa Mfumu ya Mafumu ndi Ambuye wa Ambuye, kwa amene ali wake kwathunthu.

NDINE NDINE Wamkulu choncho auzeni NDINE Wamkulu watumiza Mawu awa ndipo nthawi ndi tsopano.

Taonani! Yang'anani ndi kudikira chifukwa ola lafika pa inu, mverani ndipo musatseke makutu anu kuti mumve kulira kwa lipenga, pakuti lipenga lomaliza lalamulidwa kuti lilire ndipo ola la kulira kwake lafika pa inu, kokha musagwere m'tulo kubwerera m'chinyengo champhamvu.

Kodi mwakonzeka? Choncho zikwaniritsidwe pa nthawi yoikidwa yolembedwa mumtima mwanga dziko lapansi lisanapangidwe.

NDINE amene NDINE ndipo Khristu Yesu wakonzeka kuonekera ngakhale pa nthawi imene ndinasankha mu uphungu wanga mu nthawi zamuyaya zapitazo, ngakhale nthawi yangwiro yoperekedwa kwa chiwombolo chathunthu cha zipatso zoyamba za chilungamo! Taonani! Nthawi ndi tsopano!

Ndipo choncho Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya akuti, Inde ndi Amen!
