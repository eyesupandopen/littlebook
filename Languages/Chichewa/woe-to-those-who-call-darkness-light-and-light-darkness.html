Tsoka kwa Iwo Amene Atcha Mdima Kuti Ndi Kuwala ndi Kuwala Kuti Ndi Mdima!

Imvani mawu a Ambuye ndi mpulumutsi wanu Mpingo wanga, ngakhale Mkwatibwi wanga, pakuti ndine amene ndipatsa nzeru kwa anzeru, kuti anzeru pakati panu akhale anzeru kwambiri. Ndipo ndine wosula amene amasonkhezera moto wa chiyeretso, choncho imvani mawu anga nonse, ndipo gwedamani pansi pa chiitano changa cha moyo wosatha ndikufunafuna nzeru pakati nzeru ndiye chinthu chachikulu, chotero pezani nzeru. Choncho bwerani kwa ine ndi mtima wotseguka ndi manja otseguka ndikugwada ndikupitiriza kundipanga ine, Yesu wa ku Nazarete, kukhala Ambuye wanu wamuyaya pakuti ndikuitanabe onse amene ali olowa nyumba a moyo wosatha, ngakhale ena ali ogontha kumva.

Koma kanani nzeru za dziko lino zomwe sizipindula kanthu, pakuti nzeru za dziko lapansi zimachokera kwa akalonga a dziko lapansi omwe posachedwapa adzamangidwa kwa zaka chikwi chimodzi m'phompho mu mazunzo ndi chilango chifukwa cha njira zawo. Pakuti ngakhale m'unyolo wolemera womwe adzamangidwa posachedwa adzamvetsa tsogolo lawo, ndipo adzanjenjemera ndi kugwedezeka kwa zaka chikwi chimodzi monga umuyaya wawo woikidwa m'nyanja yoyaka moto ya sulufule wonunkha ikugubuduzika ndi kuyaka m'malawi otentha kwambiri a buluu wa cobalt pakuti Yahweh wakonza choncho ndipo cholowa chawo chikuwayembekezera.

Pakuti chotero ndicho chikhumbo cha mtima wawo kwa ana a Mulungu wamoyo, choncho Yahweh adzabwezera chikhumbo cha mtima wawo m'zifuwa zawo, ndipo motero adzayaka kosatha mobwerezabwereza mu umuyaya wa muyaya. Pakuti makamaka ogwa adzalandira za njira zawo pakuti iwo ndi atate a tchimo, kulakwa, mphulupulu, ndi kusayeruzika ndipo machitidwe awo onyansa adzabwezedwa kwa iwo kosatha. Ndipo adzadziwa ndi kulipira mokwanira tchimo lililonse limene anachita nthawi zambiri mobwerezabwereza ndipo chilango chawo chidzakhala chotero, pakuti zotero zapezeka m'mitima yawo ndipo machitidwe awo ndi onyansa kosatha ndi kwamuyaya. Pakuti zotero ndi njira za Yahweh, m'mene munthu aweruzira wina, momwemonso adzaweruzidwa ndipo chotero ndi tsogolo ndi chilango cha onse amene asankha chiwonongeko chawo m'malo mwa moyo wosatha mwa ine Yesu wa ku Nazarete pakuti ndine Mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu.

Pakuti aliyense amene ali wopusa mokwanira kupandukira dongosolo la chilengedwe, ngakhale madongosolo achilengedwe a Yahweh amene anapanga moyo ngati nsalu yolukidwa yodalirana, adzagawana nawo chilango chawo. Kupandukira madongosolo a moyo ndiko kubweretsa imfa ndi chiwonongeko kwa ena, ndipo choipa chachikulu ndi vuto lalikulu zimachitidwa ndi onse amene apanduka m'njira yotere ndipo amatsatira zilakolako zawo kuti awononge ena. Kukondwera m'kupanduka kwa ogwa ndiko kulowa m'njira zawo ndipo onse amene atero adzagawana nawo kosatha cholowa chawo m'malawi amoto amuyaya, ndipo palibe chisankho china.

Pakuti kuyambira kale ndi chiyambi anthu apatsidwa ufulu wosankha ndipo Adamu anakopeka ndi Hava amene anatenga kwa njoka yomwe inamunyenga mu kuchenjera kwake. Choncho Adamu ndi Hava anasankha imfa monga zotulukapo za tchimo, pakuti anthu analengedwa chimodzimodzi ngati angelo, ndi cholinga chakuti apitirize kukhala oyera ndi olungama, ndipo imfa, yomwe iwononga chilichonse, sikanatha kuwagwira.

Ndipo chotero chidziwitso cha chabwino ndi choipa chaipitsa onse amene adya kuyambira pamenepo. Ndipo nkhondo ya mbewu yakhala ikubangula ndi kukwiya kuyambira pa Genesis ndi mbewu yolonjezedwa ya mkazi, mu chiwonongeko chachikulu, ndipo mu nkhondo iyi palibe chomwe chasungidwa ku zotulukapo zake. Mpaka lero chilengedwe chonse chikumva zowawa ndi kubuula m'zowawa ngakhale tsopano chifukwa chilengedwe chidzapulumutsidwanso ku ukapolo wa chivundi kulowa mu ufulu waulemerero wa ana a Mulungu, ndipo chipulumutso sichichedwa! Koma mvetsetsani ana anga kuti mpaka lero nkhondo ya mbewu yapambanidwa mokwanira mwa ine, Khristu Yesu, Pakuti mwabadwanso ndi mbewu yosawonongeka ndipo mwa mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu chitetezero chinapangidwa chifukwa cha machimo a anthu kamodzi ndi kosatha. Ndipo ndi mphamvu yosayeseka ya Yahweh, imfa inagonjetsedwa nthawi zonse mu kuuka kwanga kwa akufa, pakuti n'kosatheka kuti imfa indigwire pakuti ndine wopanda tchimo ndipo mphoto ya tchimo ndi imfa.

Komabe ndinalipira mtengo wosaganizirika chifukwa cha chipulumutso chanu ndi ufulu wanu, ndipo ana a Mulungu wamoyo nthawi ina iliyonse adzipeza ataimirira m'chipinda chachifumu cha Mulungu atavala moyo wosatha. Zili choncho ndipo zidzakhala choncho, kuti aliyense amene aitana pa dzina la Ambuye adzapulumutsidwa kosatha ndi kwamuyaya, ngati akhala okhulupirika poyitana pa dzina langa mpaka mapeto. Pakuti ogwa ndi adani ogonjetsedwa, ndipo tsopano ikubwera nthawi ya chiwonongeko chotheratu pa ufumu wawo ndi chilango chokwanira pamene mkwiyo wa Mwanawankhosa ndi mkwiyo wa Mulungu ukutsanuliridwa m'masiku akubwera. Pakuti tsiku la Ambuye lafika, choncho mverani mawu anga chitseko cha Chingalawa chisanatsekedwe.

Imvani mawu anga ndikumvetsa kulankhula kwanga. Pakuti ndikulankhula za zinthu zomwe zikuchitika panopa ngakhale chinyengo chachikulu chomwe chinganyenge ngakhale osankhidwa ngati asankha kutseka makutu awo ku mawu anga. Tsopano ndilankhula za nkhumba zakuthengo zomwe zikufuna kuoneka ngati sizili m'makola momwe zasungidwa ndi woletsa wanga. Ngakhale gulu ndi madongosolo a nkhumba zomwe zikupititsa patsogolo chiwonongeko tsiku ndi tsiku kubweretsa dziko lapansi pafupi ndi nkhondo yonse, ndi tsoka lonse, pakuti zapatsidwa kwa iwo kuti akwaniritse ndipo Mulungu waika m'mitima yawo kukwaniritsa chifuniro chake pakuti ngakhale adani a Yahweh amamvera mawu ake.

Nkhumba zimagubuduzika m'litsiro lawo, zikufukula m'matope, ndipo sizikhuta, koma zidzadya chilichonse chomwe zingathe pamene zikufukula ndi mphuno zawo pansi pakuti ndi za dziko lapansi, ndipo ndi zakhungu ku zina zonse koma chikhumbo cha mimba yawo ndi zilakolako za atate wawo adzachita. Pakuti otero ndi atsogoleri a dziko panopa, ndipo akulira m'zowawa pamene akumenya omwe akuwazunza. Pakuti palibe chomwe chingakhutiritse nkhumba izi mokwanira, ndipo zili zakupha kotheratu kuchokera mkati ndi kadyedwe kawo, pakuti zonse ndi zodetsedwa ndipo ndi namsongole weniweni ndipo chotero ndi chikhalidwe chawo.

Zilombo zoipazi ndi mitengo yowola ndipo yopanda zipatso, yakufa kawiri, ndi yozulidwa ndi mizu. Sizidzakhala ndi moyo wosatha, ndipo zawo ndi njira za imfa yamuyaya ndi chiwonongeko, ngakhale chiwonongeko chosatha mu cholowa cha Atate awo m'nyanja yayikulu yamoto wa buluu. Ndi zolusa mwachibadwa, ndipo zimadzipangitsa kukhala zokondedwa poyamba kufunafuna pogona pamene zikulira ngati wozunzidwa, ngakhale kuimba mlandu ena chifukwa cha chidani chomwe zimachita pa mitundu. Zimalira m'zowawa pamene zikumenya omwe zikufuna kuwawononga kuti ziwoneke zolungamitsidwa m'njira zawo zoipa.

Pakuti zilombo zankhanzazi sizingamveredwe chisoni pakuti zawo ndi njira za chiwonongeko ndipo izi zidzachita zilakolako za Atate wawo mdierekezi pakuti zilakolako zake ndi chisangalalo chawo ndipo motero zimakwaniritsidwa. Tsoka kwa iwo amene amakondwerera choipa ndipo amatcha kuwala kuti ndi mdima ndi mdima kuti ndi kuwala. Pakuti zawo ndi njira za chiwonongeko, ndipo kumoto wamuyaya sadzathawa. Mvetsetsani ana anga kuti nthawi ina iliyonse tsopano nkhumba zidzamasulidwa m'makola momwe zakhala zikusungidwa ndi woletsa. Pakuti pamene woletsa akuchotsedwa, nkhumba zomwe zikuwoneka ngati zoweta ndi zopanda vuto zomwe zimadziyesa osankhidwa a Mulungu zidzakhala zakuthengo zikangomasulidwa ku kugwira kosasweka kwa woletsa, pakuti chotero ndi chikhalidwe chawo chenicheni.

Adzalowa m'maiko ambiri ndipo adzadya zonse zomwe apeza. Adzadya imfa ndi chiwonongeko cha ena, pakuti chotero ndi chikhalidwe chawo. Ayendayenda kuchokera kumalo ndi malo, ndipo ali ndi madongosolo awo ndi magulu awo, pakuti Yahweh amapereka ngakhale mlingo wa nzeru ndi dongosolo ngakhale kwa nkhumba, pakuti njira za Yahweh ndi zolungama ndi zoyenera. Nkhumba izi zilibe chisoni, zilibe kumva, ndipo zilibe vuto kudya akufa ngakhale kuti zikwaniritse zilakolako zawo, ndipo zidzadya ngakhale amoyo ngati zikugwirizana ndi zolinga zawo. Taonani ola tsopano ndi pamene nkhumba zidzakhala zopanda choletsa ndipo pamene zili, zidzadya dziko, zidzadya mafuta a dziko, zidzawononga mtendere kotheratu pa dziko lapansi ndipo zidzadya ndi kugubuduzika m'kukhetsa magazi kwa nkhondo yonse. Pakuti ngakhale osangalatsa anu oipa amayimba za njira zawo, ndipo njira zawo si chinsinsi.

Nkhumba zakuthengo izi zinapangidwa chifukwa cha chiwonongeko ndi kudya litsiro la dziko kuyeretsa dziko lapansi chifukwa cha zolinga za Yahweh, kenako zidzamangidwa ndi kuponyedwa m'moto wamuyaya kosatha. Pakuti machimo awo ndi aakulu, ndipo machimo awo ndi ambiri. Koma mvetsetsani kuti nkhumba izi zasungidwa m'khola lawo pa nthawi yoikidwa ndipo ambiri amadziyesa oikidwa ngati ziwiya zopatulika za ufumu wawo womwe ukukwera. Ndipo tsopano ndi nthawi ya kumasulidwa kwawo, ndipo tsopano adzawonetsa chikhalidwe chawo chenicheni chomwe abisa m'zovala zomwe zikuwoneka ngati zopembedza, ndipo kyubu yakuda ya Saturn ili pakati pa nyenyezi yawo ndipo ngakhale pamwamba pa maso. Akufuna kudzipatula pakuti adzisankha okha kukhala a Mulungu. Koma palibe munthu amabwera kwa Yahweh koma kudzera mwa ine, Yesu wa ku Nazarete, yemwe Atate wawo anamukana ndipo omwe andipanga ine kukhala Ambuye ndi Isiraeli weniweni wa Mulungu ndipo adzalengedwanso mokwanira mwa ine ngati munthu mmodzi watsopano, kaya Myuda kapena Wamitundu, kaya kapolo kapena mfulu. Namsongole wa Aedomu amenewa adzawululidwa mokwanira chifukwa cha chinyengo chomwe ali ndipo adzachotsedwa kotheratu m'dziko ndi dziko lonse lapansi m'chiweruzo chachikulu ndi chilango chachikulu.

Koma asanachotsedwe, adzaloledwa chilakolako chawo cha magazi pamene chikhalidwe chawo chenicheni chikuwululidwa kuti onse awone, pakuti panopa amadzibisa m'zovala zomwe zikuwoneka ngati zachipembedzo. Amadya zinyalala, amadya akufa, amaukira ndi kudya amoyo chifukwa chilakolako chawo cha magazi ndi chachikulu ndipo sichingakhutiritsidwe! Ndipo nthawi yonseyi amadziyesa oyera, amadziyesa kuti ndi a dziko ndipo ndi olowa nyumba oyenera, ndipo amadziyesa kuti si owukira pakuti malo awo achibadwa ndi chipululu osati dziko lolonjezedwa. Ndipo chotero ndi chikhalidwe cha nkhumba pakuti zikangobwezedwa kuthengo chikhalidwe chawo chenicheni chakuthengo chimawululidwa mokwanira. Komabe, amadziyesa kukhala ndi zizindikiro za omwe akufuna kuwagonjetsa ndipo adzazulidwa kotheratu m'dziko ndipo alibe muzu kapena nthambi pakuti Yahweh walonjeza choncho. Choncho mvetsetsani nkhumba izi zikuwoneka ngati atsogoleri abwino a dziko lolonjezedwa pamene kwenikweni ndi zilombo zolusa zomwe zimakula pa kupha fuko ndipo zidzafunafuna kotheratu kuwononga chilichonse cha ine, Khristu Yesu, ndipo adzapereka ngakhale omwe ali a Dziko omwe amatsatira madongosolo awo a Talmudic mu chiwonongeko ndipo komabe otsalira a dziko adzabwera kwa ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, pakuti ndidzapulumutsabe Yakobo!

Tsoka kwa iwo amene atcha mdima kuti ndi kuwala ndi kuwala kuti ndi mdima! Ngakhale omwe amaukira Yahweh, ndi Yeshua HaMashiach poukira ana a Mulungu Wamoyo. Pakuti popeza anachitira mmodzi wa abale anga ang'onoang'ono anandichitira ine, ndipo kubwezera ndi kwanga, atero Ambuye wamuyaya, ndipo ndidzabwezera. Choncho tsegulani maso anu, ana anga, ndipo kanani omwe akuulutsa nyenyezi ya Remphan pa mbendera yawo! Chifukwa kuphatikizana nawo ndiko kukhala m'chihema cha Moloch ndi kugwada pamaso pa Baphomet wawo! Kodi sindinakuuzeni kuti muwerenge nambala ya chilombo ndipo nambala imeneyo ikuwonetsedwa monyadira m'chifaniziro chomwe anadzipangira okha kupembedza mulungu wawo wa nyenyezi, pakuti pakati pa nyenyezi yawo pali kyubu yawo! Mvetsetsani fanizo ili, pakuti mfundo zisanu ndi imodzi zimatsogolera ku makona atatu asanu ndi limodzi omwe amapanga hekzagoni ndipo mkati mwake mumalowa kyubu yawo, choncho werengani nambala ya chilombo ndipo siyani kukhulupirira mabodza a Aedomu chifukwa amapembedza ogwa ndipo mulungu wawo ndi chinjoka.

Ndipo kuyambira masiku a Nowa, ngakhale pambuyo pa chigumula, magawo asanu ndi anayi mwa magawo khumi a mizimu yopanda matupi ya Nephilim yaletsedwa m'phompho, pakuti Nowa anapempherera zidzukulu zake zomwe zinaukiridwa ndi iwo ndipo zinagonjetsedwa nawo. Ndipo choncho Yahweh analemekeza pempho la Nowa ndikumanga oyipa kwambiri ndi onyansa kwambiri, ndipo magawo asanu ndi anayi mwa magawo khumi a chiwerengero chawo asungidwa m'phompho kuyambira pamenepo. Ndimveni ndipo ndimveni bwino, Ana anga, ndipo mvetsetsani mawu a Ambuye wanu. Pa kusonkhana kwa okhulupirika m'Mpingo wanga ngakhale Mkwatibwi wanga kwa ine m'mitambo woletsa adzachotsedwa, ndipo phompho lakhazikitsidwa kuti litsegulidwe mokwanira.

Choipa sichidzaletsedwanso, chifukwa Yahweh waletsanso kudzaza kwa ufumu wa Satana kuti omwe ali olowa nyumba a chipulumutso chamuyaya apulumutsidwe, ngakhale omwe andipanga ine kukhala Ambuye wawo m'nthawi zanga za chisomo ndi chifundo. Popeza anthu tsopano andikana, choipa sichidzaletsedwanso kwa anthu. Mvetsetsani kuti onse, ngakhale khamu lalikulu la mizimu iyi onse adzamasulidwa, ngakhale chiwerengero chachikulu kwambiri chomwe chidzameza dziko lapansi ndi choipa chosaletsedwa ndi chosaneneka. Pakuti Enoke mlembi analemba za izi: Ndipo mizimu ya zimphona imasautsa, kupondereza, kuwononga, kuukira, kuchita nkhondo, ndi kugwira ntchito ya chiwonongeko pa dziko lapansi, ndi kuyambitsa mavuto: satenga chakudya, koma komabe amamva njala ndi ludzu, ndi kuyambitsa zokhumudwitsa. Ndipo mizimu iyi idzaukira ana a anthu ndi akazi, chifukwa yachokera kwa iwo.

Kodi simunawerenge m'mawu anga oyera pamene ndinayenda m'dziko la Agadara? Pakuti ndinatulutsa mizimu mwa awiri omwe anali nayo, ndipo choncho ndinalola mizimu yoipayi kulowa m'gulu la nkhumba lapafupi. Mvetsetsani kuti kudzaza kwa ufumu wa Chinjoka kwatsala pang'ono kumasulidwa kwa nthawi yochepa, kuti athamangitsire omwe aikidwa ku moyo wosatha m'manja mwanga opulumutsa! Pakuti mizimu yomangidwayi idzamasulidwa kuti ilowe mu ufumu wa Satana mokwanira pa nkhope ya dziko lapansi. Ndipo idzalowa m'nkhumba kamodzinso. Pakuti amachita nkhondo ndi anthu ndipo adzagonjetsa anthu pakuti zilakolako zawo sizingakhutiritsidwe ndipo iyi ndi mizimu yomwe imapatsa mphamvu nkhumba ndipo imagwira ntchito limodzi ndi nkhumba ndipo nkhumba zakuthengo izi ndi galimoto yawo.

Osati iwo okha koma onse omwe atenga chizindikiro chawo cha chilombo adzakhalanso magalimoto ofunitsitsa a mizimu iyi yomwe idzachita nkhondo ndi anthu kamodzinso. Pakuti adzadyanso munthu wachibadwa m'nthawi zotsiriza izi, pakuti chotero chaikidwa kuti chichitike m'nthawi zotsiriza izi. Ndipo mapeto ayamba kale ndipo akubwera mokwanira posachedwapa pamene nyengo ya masautso a Yakobo ikuphulika m'dziko. Zowawa zobereka zili pafupi kwambiri tsopano sizikuyima ndipo Yahweh wachita zizindikiro zambiri m'dziko lapansi ndi zizindikiro zambiri ndi zozizwitsa m'mwezi ndi m'kumwamba ndi m'dziko lapansi, komabe ambiri akupitirizabe kulingalira kuthetsa machenjezo awa m'kulingalira kopanda chikhulupiriro.

Taonani! M'malo mobwera ku kulapa, makamu akusangalatsidwa ngakhale ndi magulu a tizilombo omwe agonjetsa ambiri ndi m'maiko ambiri padziko lapansi. Kuseka ndi kusekerera pa machenjezo operekedwa ndi Yahweh ku dziko lapansi pakuti Mulungu wamuyaya adzauka kuchokera kumalo ake okhala ndipo adzadziwonetsera ndipo ogwa akunjenjemera pakuti nthawi zawo zafika, ndipo agwidwa ndi mantha amuyaya. Pakuti inu ogwa munaganiza kuti mungatenge Mulungu wamuyaya mwachinyengo, pamene chinthu choterocho n'chosatheka. Pakuti ndi nthawi yanji pamene ziwiya zopanda ulemu zauka kuchita nkhondo ndi kutenga woumba mwachinyengo? Koma wotero ndi misala ndi kudzikuza kwa ogwa, ndipo sadzathawa ng'anjo yawo yoyaka moto nthawi zonse.

Choncho mvetsetsani nthawi yomwe mukukhala ndipo khalani mwa ine, Mpingo wanga, ngakhale Mkwatibwi wanga! Pakuti chiwombolo chanu chokwanira ndi cholowa chokwanira sizichedwa, ndipo simudzasiyidwa pakati pa nkhumba chifukwa munakhulupirira pa dzina langa m'masiku awa a chisomo ndi chifundo, ndipo choncho chisomo ndi chifundo chamuyaya mudzakhala nacho kosatha. Ndipo abale anu adzakuphatikizani mu umuyaya ndi mu Ufumu wanga ukubwera wa chilungamo, ngakhale onse omwe sanakonzekere kuwonekera kwanga.

Pakuti ndine woyenga amene amasonkhezera moto wa chiyeretso kuti ndiyeretse chuma cha Yahweh, kuchotsa zinyalala, ndipo ndine amene ndimakhala ngati wosula ndi mvukuto m'manja kusonkhezera makala ndipo amene amapinda ndi kusula zitsulo zamtengo wapatali za Yahweh kutulutsa mphamvu yamuyaya ndi yosatha. Khamu lalikulu, ngakhale khamu la makamu, lidzabwera kwa ine kudzera mu nyengo ya masautso a Yakobo, ndipo adzakolola mphoto yaikulu, ndipo ndi olowa nyumba a chipulumutso chamuyaya ndipo mu chisangalalo chamuyaya adzalowa ndipo Isiraeli adzapulumutsidwa! Choncho musawope ana anga, ndipo musadandaule za okondedwa anu ndipo khalani ndi chikhulupiriro mwa ine Khristu Yesu, pakuti ndikutha kupulumutsa kotheratu onse omwe aitana pa dzina langa, ndipo palibe mmodzi amene waikidwa ku moyo wosatha angazulidwe m'dzanja langa, ndipo ndidzapulumutsa ambiri ku moto wamuyaya, ngakhale kuwatulutsa m'malawi amoto.

Ine Yeshua HaMashiach ndatumiza mawu awa kudzera mwa mtumiki wanga, ndipo kudzera mwa mtumiki wanga mawu awa atumizidwa!

Ndine amene ndipatsa nzeru kwa anzeru, kuti munthu wanzeru akhale wanzeru kwambiri.

Ndine Mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu amene akubwera ngati Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda.

Ndine wopanda tchimo, komabe ndinalipira mtengo wosaganizirika chifukwa cha chipulumutso chanu ndi ufulu wanu.

Ndine amene ndimakhala ngati wosula amene amapinda ndi kusula zitsulo zamtengo wapatali za chuma cha Yahweh.

Ndikutha kupulumutsa kotheratu onse omwe aitana pa dzina langa, ndipo palibe mmodzi amene waikidwa ku moyo wosatha angazulidwe m'dzanja langa.

Dikirani kuwonekera kwanga, pakuti nthawi zafika. Nthawi yotsala yaperekedwa kokha kutumikira zolinga za Yahweh ana anga, choncho musalole kuti mutonthozedwe kugona kamodzinso, ngakhale kunyalanyaza mawu anga a nzeru ndi machenjezo ambiri.

Munthu wanzeru amawona mliri ndipo adzabisala, koma opusa amapitirizabe, ndipo amakumana ndi vuto. Kodi sindinakuitanireni ku nzeru yopirira nthawi zonse ya Yahweh?

Ndipo ndine amene ndidzakubisani komwe simungathe kudzibisa nokha, pakuti ndidzakubisani m'chipinda chachifumu cha Yahweh ngakhale m'mwamba mwa kumwamba komwe mudzalowa mu chisangalalo cha Ambuye wanu ndipo maholo a phwando a kumwamba adzakukondwererani nonse, pakuti posachedwa kuposa momwe mukuganizira mudzawona ndi maso anu ulemerero waukulu, wamuyaya womwe ukukuyembekezerani mu cholowa chanu. Pitirizanibe m'chikondi changa, pakuti ndinu okondedwa kwambiri ndi Atate wanga ndipo ndinu mwana wa diso langa, omwe sindichita manyazi kuwatcha abale anga.

Ine Yeshua HaMashiach ndalankhula ndi kutumiza mawu awa ndipo choncho alembedwa ndipo Uthenga Wabwino Wamuyaya wawululidwa mu nthawi yake.
