Nthawi Yakwana Tsopano

Nthawi yakwana, ana anga, nthawi yakwana. Nthawi yakwana yobwera kunyumba, pamene lipenga lidzalira ndipo mpingo wanga udzasonkhanitsidwa, kumene Yahweh akuyembekezera ndi chimwemwe mumtima mwake chifukwa cha kufika kwanu kwadzidzidzi. Nthawi yakwana yobwera kunyumba, pamene kulira kwa lipenga kudzaitana ana a Wam'mwambamwamba kuti apite kumene angelo amakhala ndi kuyendayenda. Nthawi yakwana ana anga kuti ndikusonkhanitseni kuchokera kumakona anayi a dziko lapansi, ngakhale onse amene anagona mwa Khristu adzauka poyamba ndipo inu amene muli amoyo ndi otsala mudzatengedwa pamodzi ndi iwo m'mitambo. Pakuti Ine Khristu Yesu ndine Ambuye ndi Mpulumutsi wanu wamoyo, ndipo ndikukuitanirani kunyumba kwanu kumwamba kumene mphotho zanu zikuyembekezera, kumene ndakukonzerani malo m'nyumba ya Atate wanga, pakuti m'nyumba ya Atate wanga muli malo okhala ambiri. Ndikukuitanirani kunyumba kuti mudzaime pamaso pa Ambuye wanu, kuti onse alandire za zinthu zochitidwa m'thupi, kaya zabwino kapena zoipa, kuti onse alandire kwa Ambuye mphotho yawo, ndi kuti onse atenge malo awo m'madongosolo a kumwamba amene akonzedwera aliyense wa inu.

Taonani! Ndine Yeshua HaMashiach, ndipo ndine mkwati amene ndikuyandikira kudzasonkhanitsa mkwatibwi wanga pakuti nthawi ya ukwati wathu yafika, pamene ndidzalowa m'pangano losatha ndi osankhidwa anga, ngakhale omwe adzikonzekeretsa, ndipo ndawayeretsa m'mwazi wanga wotetezera chifukwa anabwera kwa ine kwathunthu ndikupereka mitima yawo yonse kwa ine m'chikondi ndi kudzipereka. Ngakhale sanayende mwangwiro, ndipo ambiri anagonjetsa tchimo lalikulu, iwo anadziyeretsa okha kwathunthu m'mwazi wa mwanawankhosa, ndipo tsopano akuima pamaso panga oyeretsedwa kwathunthu ku machimo awo ndipo ali oyera ngati anamwali omwe sanachite dama ndipo adzisunga okha kwa woipayo. Taonani! Ogonjetsa anga amene ndawakhululukira machimo onse ndikuthira mafuta m'nyale zawo chifukwa anabwera kwa ine m'nthawi zawo zosowa ndipo ndinawawonetsa momwe angadulire zingwe zawo ndipo ndinawachiritsa ndikuwayika padera kwa ine ndekha, ngakhale onse amene Yahweh anawasankha chisanakhazikitsidwe maziko a dziko lapansi kuti aime ngati mkwatibwi wanga ndi kukhala ndi ine mpaka kalekale, ndipo adzalamulira ndi ine ngati Mafumu ndi Ansembe osatha pakuti atero kuitanidwa, kuyeretsedwa ndi kusankhidwa mwa ine, Khristu Yesu Ambuye wawo woukitsidwa.

Ndipo 144,000 anga adzasinthidwa ndi kusindikizidwa ndi kulengedwanso kwathunthu pazolinga zomwe aitanidwira ndi kukonzekera kukwaniritsa. Zoonadi, zoonadi, mzimu uliwonse womwe wakhulupirira m'dzina langa lamphamvu ndikundipanga kukhala Ambuye wawo wosatha udzasinthidwa kwathunthu ndikulengedwanso mwatsopano ngati cholengedwa chatsopano, ndipo adzapangidwa monga momwe ndiliri. Ndipo sadzachimwanso, kaya kudzera m'kunyada kapena kusapembedza ndipo sadzatembereredwanso, ndipo matenda kapena ululu, kapena imfa kapena chisoni sizidzawakhudzanso pakuti zinthu zakale zapita ndipo kuwala koona kukuwala tsopano. Ndipo ndine, Khristu Yesu, amene tsopano ndidzasuntha kudziulula ndekha kwa Yakobo ndipo aliyense amene aitanira pa dzina langa lamphamvu mpaka chimaliziro adzapulumutsidwa, ngakhale onse okhala pa dziko lapansi omwe aikidwiratu ku moyo wosatha ndipo kudzakhala khamu lalikulu lomwe lidzandilandira ngati Mesiya wawo ndipo ndidzawapulumutsa mpaka kumapeto.

Ndikubwera kudzawatengani, okondedwa anga, ndipo nthawi ndi tsopano. Ndadikira m'badwo wonse wa chisomo ndipo ndapulumutsa ambiri, ndipo ambiri adzaukitsidwa ku moyo watsopano mwa ine, Khristu Yesu Ambuye wawo wamuyaya, ndipo akufa mwa Khristu adzauka poyamba, ndipo lipenga lomaliza lili lokonzeka tsopano. Zizindikiro zambiri, zozizwitsa ndi zodabwitsa zaphimba dziko lapansi mu ulemerero wawo ndipo zizindikiro zikuchitika mosalekeza padziko lonse lapansi tsopano zomwe zikulengeza kuonekera kwanga, zomwe zikulengeza ukwati wa Mwanawankhosa kwa mkwatibwi wake wokhulupirika, ndipo maholo aphwando kumwamba akonzedwa ndipo ali okonzeka.

Ndipo zili choncho, chikondwerero chakumwamba chikuyembekezera okhulupirika anga, oitanidwa, ndi osankhidwa, ndipo tsopano ndi nthawi yoti apumule mpaka kalekale ku ntchito zawo zomwe agwira padziko lapansi m'nyumba zawo zadongo, ndipo agwira ntchito m'thupi ngati antchito anzanga ndipo asonkhanitsa ndi kubweretsa ambiri kwa ine, Ambuye Yesu Khristu kuti alandire chipulumutso chosatha mpaka kalekale. Pakuti ngakhale thupi lawo linaipitsidwa mu uchimo ndi Satana, ndawapatsa cholinga ndikuwaitana kuti asiye mpaka kalekale zachabechabe zomwe anatsutsidwa nazo ndi uchimo.

Ndipo choncho ali ndi chuma ichi m'ziwiya zadongo kuti mphamvu ya Mulungu imakhala ndi kukhala mwa iwo, ndipo anachita ntchito zambiri zamphamvu m'dzina langa, ndipo ndine amene ndinawapatsa mphamvu kuti akhale olowa nyowa a chipulumutso ndi olowa nyowa a moyo wosatha ndipo ndawaombola ndikupereka malipiro oyamba osatha m'kubadwa kwatsopano ndipo ndawasindikiza onse ndi mzimu woyera wa lonjezo, ngakhale chidindo chosweka cha lonjezo. Ndipo tsopano ndi nthawi yowombola kwathunthu chuma chogulidwa, ngakhale onse amene andipanga kukhala Ambuye kuchokera mumtima wodzazidwa ndi chikhulupiriro podziwa kuti Yahweh anandiukitsa kwa akufa kuti ndisafe nso. Tsopano ndi nthawi ya chiombolo chawo chokwanira pamene adzalandira kwathunthu malipiro awo oyamba olandiridwa kwa ine m'kubadwa kwatsopano kwa Khristu mwa inu chiyembekezo cha ulemerero!

Taonani! Imvani mawu anga ndikumvetsetsa mawu anga ndikudziwa kuti tsogolo lanu laulemerero siliri zaka zambiri kutsogolo, kapena silinakonzedwe kuti lifike mu Zaka za Yubile zikubwerazo, koma lili pa inu okondedwa anga ndipo mwadzidzidzi mu kamphindi kosatha nonsenu mudzasinthidwa ndipo nthawi yakusintha ndi tsopano. Mudzipeza mwadzidzidzi mwasinthidwa kukhala ulemerero wosatha, ndipo ndidzakuvekani zovala zopanda banga zomwe sizingaipitsidwe. Mwadzidzidzi mudzipeza mukuima m'chipinda chachifumu cha Yahweh, ngakhale mu ulemerero wosatha wa Atate wa Kuwala, ndipo mudzatenga mbali mpaka kalekale mu chikhalidwe chake chosatha pakuti ndinu ana a Mulungu wamoyo, ngakhale onse amene anatengedwa kwa Adamu ndi kuomboledwa kuchokera ku dothi lofiira.

Ndipo unali mwazi wanga wotetezera womwe unakhetsedwa padziko lapansi pa Gologota womwe unatsimikizira chipulumutso chanu kamodzi, ndi mpaka kalekale pamene ndinapereka moyo wanga ndi mawu akuti "Zatha!" Ndipo Ine Khristu Yesu, ndine mpaka kalekale Ambuye wa Ambuye ndi Mfumu ya Mafumu woukitsidwa ndi wokwera kumwamba chifukwa Yahweh anandiukitsa kwa akufa kuti ndisafenso! Ndine Adamu womaliza, ndipo ndine mwana wa munthu amene waombola ana a anthu omwe anaitana pa dzina langa ndikuwapanga kukhala ana a Mulungu osatha omwe adzakhala mpaka kalekale mu ulemerero wosatha ndi ulemerero wamuyaya ndipo mpaka kalekale Yahweh adzalemekezedwa mwa iwo, ndi iwo mwa Yahweh pakuti mwa ine, Yeshua HaMashiach kudzaza konse kumakhala mpaka kalekale, ndipo Yahweh adzakhala zonse mu zonse ndipo mpaka kalekale tidzasuntha ngati cholengedwa chimodzi chatsopano ndi munthu mmodzi watsopano. Ndipo inu, oitanidwa anga osankhidwa ndi okhulupirika, mwaomboledwa mpaka kalekale, ndipo mwatsala pang'ono kumvetsetsa miyoyo yanu yeniyeni pakuti miyoyo yanu ndi yosatha. Choncho mvetsetsani kuti ndinu akufa, ndipo miyoyo yanu yabisika ndi ine, Khristu Yesu, mwa Yahweh. Pamene Ine, Khristu Yesu, amene ndine moyo wanu, ndidzawonekera, pamenepo inunso mudzawonekera pamodzi ndi ine mu ulemerero. Kodi simukudziwa kuti umunthu wanu weniweni wabisika mwa ine, Khristu Yesu?

Nanga n'chifukwa chiyani muli achisoni osankhidwa anga, okhulupirika anga, Mpingo wanga ngakhale Mkwatibwi wanga? Ndikubwera kudzawatengani ndipo taonani, ndikuyandikira kwa inu. Ndipo ndidzakusonkhanitsani mwadzidzidzi kwa ine ndekha. Ndipo pamene dziko lapansi likuzungulira kulowa m'chiweruzo ndi nkhondo, kumwamba kudzakondwera ndi kukhalapo kwanu ndipo kudzakhala chikondwerero chachikulu pamene ana a Mulungu adzagwirizanitsidwa mpaka kalekale ndi Ambuye wawo wa Ambuye ndi Mfumu ya Mafumu mu ulemerero wosatha ndipo moyo watsopano udzakhala ndi onse. Ndipo sipadzakhalanso kulekanitsidwa pakati pa omwe aomboledwa ndi mwazi wa Mwanawankhosa ndi Atate wawo ndi Mulungu wawo.

Pakuti tsopano ndi nthawi yomwe Yahweh wasankha kuyamba kudziulula kwathunthu ku chilengedwe chake ndipo kudziulula kwake kwathunthu kumayambira ndi nyumba ya Mulungu, ndipo watsala pang'ono kudziulula kwathunthu kwa osankhidwa ake ndi okhulupirika, ngakhale zipatso zoyambirira, pakuti omwe ndikuwasonkhanitsa kwa ine ndekha pakulira kwa lipenga lomaliza ndi zipatso zoyambirira kuchokera kwa akufa. Ndipo tsopano ndi nthawi yoti adziwe kwathunthu, monga momwe nonsenu mukudziwika kwathunthu ndi ine ndi Atate wanga. Ndipo choncho ndikulengeza kuonekera kwanga ndipo ndikukulangizani okhulupirika anga kuti mupitirize kudzikonzera kuonekera kwanga, pakuti tsopano ndi nthawi pamene chiweruzo chikuyamba panyumba ya Mulungu. Ndipo mwadzidzidzi, zidzachitika.

Taonani! Musawope mphepo zamkuntho zomwe zikubwera zomwe ambiri a inu mukuziona zikusonkhana: mphepo zamkuntho za nkhondo, mphepo zamkuntho za masoka omwe akuyandikira, mphepo zamkuntho za masoka achilengedwe zomwe zatsala pang'ono kuchitika. Mvetsetsani kuti palibe chomwe chidzakuvulazeni mwanjira iliyonse, ngakhale ambiri akukhala m'mayesero ndi nkhondo yauzimu yomwe yapambanidwa kwathunthu mwa ine, Khristu Yesu. Taonani! Nthawi ya kuonekera kwanga kwa okhulupirika anga yafika ndipo monga zowawa zobereka zili zosalekeza ndipo mwadzidzidzi adzasonkhanitsidwa ndi kulandidwa ku chiweruzo chomwe sichawo.

Ndikukuitanirani kunyumba kwanu kumwamba kumene mphotho zanu zikuyembekezera.

Ndikukuitanirani kunyumba kuti mudzaime pamaso pa Ambuye wanu, kuti mulandire za zinthu zochitidwa m'thupi.

Ndikubwera kudzawatengani, okondedwa anga, ndipo nthawi ndi tsopano.

Ndine amene ndakupatsani mphamvu kuti mukhale olowa nyowa a chipulumutso ndi olowa nyowa a moyo wosatha.

Ndikubwera kudzawatengani kuti ndikulandeni kupita ku chitetezo cha kumwamba ndipo nthawi ndi tsopano.

Ine Yeshua HaMashiach ndatumiza mawu awa kudzera mwa mtumiki wanga, ndipo kudzera mwa mtumiki wanga mawu awa atumizidwa.

Khalani okonzeka ndipo musasiye malo anu ndipo pitirizani kundiyembekezera. Musakhale opanda chikhulupiriro, koma dikirani ndikumvetsetsa kuti zizindikiro za kuonekera kwanga zili padziko lonse lapansi, ndipo mwadzidzidzi mudzaima pamaso posatha pa NDINE wamkulu!
