Kwa Okhulupirika mwa Khristu: Konzekerani ndi Kudzikonza Kuti Musonkhanitsidwe, Mkwati Wanu Akuyandikira!

Imvani, imvani Mawu a Yeshua HaMashiach ndipo weramitsani mitima yanu pomvera phokoso la Mawu anga! Ndipo zili choncho, kuti anthu anga ambiri apotokera kumanja ndipo ambiri apotokera kumanzere, ngakhale kutalikirana ndi Khristu Yesu Ambuye wawo, ngakhale pamene ndikubwera kudzasonkhanitsa inu kwa ine ndekha ngati mukhala okhulupirika ku mawu anga pomvera Mawu anga onse. Chifukwa, indetu, indetu, Ine ndine Ambuye wanu weniweni, Mfumu, Mkulu wa Ansembe, ndi Mesiya! Inde, ndikukuitanani nonse m'manja mwanga achikondi kuti musonkhanitsidwe kwa ine mwadzidzidzi ndi mosayembekezereka tsopano, ngakhale onse omwe amadikira momvera m'chikhulupiriro chonse, ndipo makamaka onse omwe agwa m'chinyengo ichi chokonzedwa ndi Heylel Ben Shachar kuti anyenge mitima ya anthu wamba, ndi kuphwanya ziyembekezo zanu zolungama za chiwombolo cholungama ndi chokwanira, ngakhale chiwombolo chanu chokwanira chomwe chidzapeza okhulupirika mwa Khristu, mwadzidzidzi ndi mosayembekezereka tsopano!

Ngakhale mawu onama operekedwa monama ndi aneneri onama omwe amakhazikitsa masiku, ngakhale kulankhula monama m'dzina la Yesu Khristu, ngakhale kunena kuti akulankhula m'malo mwanga! Komabe salankhula Mawu anga, kapena kubala zipatso zanga, ndipo sandidziwa, ndipo ndidzati kwa onse omwe amanena zinthu zotere monama pa tsiku lalikulu ilo: "Chokani kwa ine inu ochita zoyipa, sindinakudziweni konse!" Ndipo zili choncho, kuti ambiri mwa okhulupirika mwa Khristu omwe ali mpingo wanga thupi langa, ndipo ngakhale mkwatibwi wanga tsopano wakhala pansi mwachisoni mokhumudwa, ngakhale pamene Mkwati wawo akuyandikira monga momwe zinalengezedwera, kunenedwa ndi kulonjezedwa! Komabe ambiri a inu tsopano mwakhala pansi mwakachetechete ngati kuti kusonkhanitsidwa kwakukulu kwa okhulupirika mwa Khristu kungatsekedwe, kapena kuletsedwa ndi Heylel Ben Shachar, chifukwa indetu, indetu, ndi iye amene anakonza monama kunyamuka kwa okhulupirika mwa Khristu ndipo ambiri mwa okhulupirika anga anadya mawu ake onama mwadyera ngati kuti mawu onamawa akuchokera kwa Khristu Yesu mwiniwake! Imvani Mawu anga mpingo wanga thupi langa ndi ngakhale mkwatibwi wanga! Kodi simudziwa mawu anga? Kodi simuzindikira Mawu anga? Chifukwa palibe ngakhale yod imodzi, kapena munga wa yod womwe udzapitirire mpaka zinthu zonse zitakwaniritsidwa NDENDENDE monga momwe zalembedwera ndi kulonjezedwa.

Komabe zili choncho, kuti ambiri a inu tsopano mwakhala pansi mwachisoni, ndi osweka mtima m'kusamvera kwanu, ngakhale kotero kuti tsopano mwakhala pansi mwachisoni pansi pa ulamuliro wa mawu a onyoza ndi onyoza, omwe chiweruzo chawo chidzawapeza mwadzidzidzi ndi mosayembekezereka tsopano ndipo ambiri, ambiri a awa adzagwa m'chiweruzo chosatha, ndipo sadzapatsidwa ulemu woitana pa dzina la Yesu mpaka mapeto awo, chifukwa chipulumutso kudzera mu kuphedwa chifukwa cha chikhulupiriro ndi mwayi ndi ulemu woperekedwa kwa omwe ali olowa nyowa a moyo wosatha ndi wamuyaya omwe adzabwera ku mphoto yaikulu chifukwa cha kuima kwawo, ndipo ndidzawalandira kwa ine ndekha kwamuyaya ndi kosatha m'chikondi changa pa iwo, ndipo komabe ndi Mkwatibwi wanga wokhulupirika amene adzabwera kudzasiliridwa ndi onse pa tsiku ilo! Choncho, imani chilili Mkwatibwi wanga ndipo dzikoleni nokha ku Mawu anga, ngakhale Mawu a Uthenga Wabwino Wosatha omwe ndapereka m'dziko lapansi kuti ndikukonzeni okondedwa anga, choncho mangani Mawu a umboni wa mthenga wanga ndi mtumiki wanga mwamphamvu m'mitima yanu, podziwa kuti Mawu oyera awa aperekedwa mwachindunji kuchokera kwa Yahweh, pamaso pa Khristu Yesu, ndipo ndimawamasula m'kumvetsetsa pa lamulo langa pamene ndikuwavumbulutsa mwangwiro ndi molondola mu mtima wa mthenga wanga, ngakhale kuchokera komwe ndawafesa, ngakhale kusungidwa kuti amasulidwe pa lamulo langa, ndipo indetu Mawu anga aperekedwa m'choonadi chonse ndi kulondola. Sela.

Indetu, indetu, monga momwe ziliri: Heylel Ben Shachar, mu chidani chachikulu, akufunafuna mwachangu kugwetsa chikhulupiriro cha onse omwe chikhulupiriro chawo chingagwetsedwe ndi mawu ake onama ndi chinyengo chake chobisika, ngakhale onse omwe chikhulupiriro chawo sichili mokwanira pa thanthwe loona la maziko, ngakhale Khristu Yesu, koma m'malo mwake akuyang'ana m'kusamvera pa mawu onama a anthu ochimwa, omwe sadziwa Khristu Yesu kapena kunditumikira Ine ngakhale pang'ono, koma m'malo mwake Heylel Ben Shachar wonama. Ndipo ndi mawu awo onama ndi zipatso za chosalungama muyenera kuwadziwa; koma ambiri mwa okhulupirika anga adakali ndi makutu oyabwa omwe mumafuna kuti atikitidwe ndi mawu onama ndi otonthoza monama, ngakhale mawu onama omwe Khristu Yesu sanatumize, kapena kuvomereza, ndipo sindidzatumiza ngakhale mawu amodzi onama kuti atsutsane ndi zolemba zanga Zoyera, kapena sindidzalankhula mawu amodzi otsutsana ndi malemba okhazikitsidwa omwe ndapereka, chifukwa indetu, indetu, Ine ndine Mawu a YHVH, NDINE Wamkulu wopangidwa thupi, ndipo ndapangidwa mzimu wopatsa moyo, ndipo sindidzadzitsutsa ndekha ngakhale pang'ono, ngakhale akunama. Choncho, ndikukulamulani mu ulamuliro wonse kuti mugwire Mawu anga mwamphamvu m'mitima yanu ndi kudzipereka kwatsopano kuti mudzibwezeretse nokha chifukwa cha kusakhulupirika kwanu, ngakhale kuti nonse omwe munakhulupirira mawu onamawa mukhoza kulapa ndi kuwavomereza kwa ine, kuti ndikuyeretseni mokwanira m'mwazi wanga wotetezera ndi kuti mukonzekere mokwanira kuonekera kwanga komwe kuli pa inu mwadzidzidzi ndi mosayembekezereka tsopano, ngakhale pamene dziko lonse lapansi likubwerera m'mbuyo kuchokera kwa Khristu Yesu, yemwe angawapulumutse mpaka kumapeto. Ngakhale pamene akubwerera m'mbuyo mu kudzipereka konse ku chifaniziro cha wofera chikhulupiriro wonama wopangidwa ndi kuperekedwa kuti alembe omwe adzapeza chipulumutso ndi moto woyenga, chifukwa ichi ndi chiyambi cha nkhondo pa oyera mtima yomwe yatsala pang'ono kumasulidwa mokwanira kuti chipulumutso cha osamvera omwe adakali mu ukapolo wa uchimo, kuti thupi ligwe ku chiwonongeko, kuti indetu mzimu upulumutsidwe, Sela.

Indetu indetu, Ine ndine Khristu Yesu, ngakhale Yeshua HaMashiach, ndipo ndikubwera kudzatonthoza mabala a osamvera pakati pa okhulupirika anga, ngakhale onse omwe angadzilole kutembenuzidwa kuchokera ku Mawu anga a Uthenga Wabwino Wosatha, ngakhale Mawu operekedwa ndi atumwi anga ndi aneneri pa chiyambi cha m'badwo uno wa chisomo, ngakhale Mawu oona omwe anavumbulutsa zinsinsi zanga mu nthawi zawo zovumbulutsidwa, ngakhale Mawu omwe ali oona lero monga momwe analiri pamene anaperekedwa koyamba ndi ine m'makutu a omwe analipo, koma makamaka ophunzira anga omwe atumwi anga anatulukamo: Samalani: dikirani, ndi kupemphera: chifukwa simudziwa nthawi yomwe ili.

Komabe ambiri a inu mumalola mawu onama omwe amatsutsana ndi Mawu anga oona a lonjezo, ngakhale mawu opangidwa kuti agwetse chikhulupiriro chanu pomanga ziyembekezo zonama pa tsiku lomwe sindinapereke, ngakhale ziyembekezo zomwe zimaphwanyidwa mwadala kuti zikumizeni m'ziyembekezo zosakwaniritsidwa ndi omwe angakuvutitseni, ngakhale onse omwe amabwera kwa inu mu chinyengo chachikulu kupereka mabodza omwe amatsutsana ndi malemba, ngakhale mawu onama operekedwa ndi omwe amalankhula motsutsana ndi Mawu anga, ngakhale omwe amanenera monama m'dzina langa.

Komabe, ambiri mumpingo wanga thupi langa ndi ngakhale mkwatibwi wanga akupitiriza kulola abusa onamawa kugwetsa ziyembekezo zawo kuti Mkwati wawo akuyandikira pa tsiku lomwe iwo eni asankha, ngakhale ulosi wonama wotsutsana ndi zomwe zalengezedwa kale ndi YHVH, NDINE Wamkulu, komanso ngakhale ndi Khristu Yesu pa lamulo la Adonai-YHVH chifukwa nthawi zonse ndimachita chifuniro cha iye amene anandituma. Chifukwa Ine ndine Mawu opangidwa thupi omwe amakhala pakati panu ndipo ngakhale mwa inu ndi mzimu woyera wa umwana, womwe umadzaza mokwanira magetsi a Dziko lapansi omwe avala mphamvu kuchokera kumwamba! Komabe ambiri a inu mofunitsitsa mumasankha kukhala mu mdima ngakhale kunyalanyaza Mawu omwe angazimitsire mivi yamoto ya woyipayo, yomwe ambiri a inu mopusa mumakonda. Indetu, indetu, Ine ndine amene ndimakhala ndi mvukuto m'manja ngakhale kusonkhezera malawi a chiyeretso, kuti indetu indetu, nonse mukonzekere kuonekera kwa Ambuye wanu, chifukwa ndikubwera kudzakutengani Okondedwa anga, ndipo ndani alipo amene angandiletse? Ndinafunsa, ndipo choncho ndikufunsanso, NDITI NDANI!

Komabe, zili choncho. Kuti Mkwatibwi wanga tsopano wakhala pansi mwachisoni modabwa, ngakhale kukakamira pa mawu a omwe amalira ndi kung'ung'udza! Ndipo choncho ndikukulamulani kuti mukane mawu onamawa, ngakhale onse omwe angaike chikhulupiriro chawo ndi chidaliro mwa amatsenga onama omwe amalira ndi kung'ung'udza ndipo omwe amalankhula motsutsana ndi Mawu anga a moyo, chifukwa ndanena momveka bwino m'malemba, ndipo malemba sangasweke: Kuti palibe munthu padziko lapansi amene akudziwa tsiku, nthawi, kapena ola la kusonkhanitsidwa kwakukulu kwa okhulupirika kwa Khristu komwe kudzachitika mwadzidzidzi ndi mosayembekezereka tsopano. Ndipo ndakulamulani nonse kuti musunge chiyembekezo cha mitima yanu ndi maganizo anu mokwanira pa Ambuye wanu Wosatha wa Chilungamo yemwe akuyandikira ndithu kwa inu Okondedwa anga, ngakhale anamwali anga onse anzeru omwe adula zingwe zawo, ndi kudzaza nyali zawo ndi mafuta oyera a mzimu wanga, kuti indetu indetu mukonzekere kuonekera kwa Mkwati wanu ndi kuti mukhale okonzeka mokwanira ndi kudikira okonzeka pamodzi ndi alendo athu. Ngakhale onse omwe aitanidwa, osankhidwa, ndi kuitanidwa ku ukwati waukulu wa Mkwati kwa mkwatibwi wake, ngakhale tsiku lomwelo lomwe likubwera pa inu nonse mwadzidzidzi ndi mosayembekezereka tsopano.

Nanga n'chifukwa chiyani Mkwatibwi wanga wakhala pansi mwachisoni ngati kuti Mkwati wanu wakukanani ndikukusiyani mutayima m'mbali mwa njira okanidwa, okanidwa ndi osweka mtima m'ziyembekezo zanu? Ayi, okhulupirika anga zinthu izi sizanu, koma zidzaperekedwa m'chiweruzo kwa osakhulupirika, ndi onse omwe akukana kuitana kwa Ambuye wawo Wosatha wa Chilungamo kuti adzikonze okha molingana ndi malamulo anga, mu kumvera konse kwachikondi ku mawu ndi malangizo a Ambuye wawo!

Choncho, ndikubwera kudzokumbutsani inu kachiwiri za Mawu awa operekedwa nthawi zakale kwa Ana a Lonjezo, ngakhale Isiraeli pambuyo pa thupi mu nthawi za kuvumbulutsidwa kwawo, ndi ngakhale Mawu omwe ayima mpaka lero okondedwa anga, choncho, imvani Mawu a Ambuye wanu ndipo landirani malangizo anga mu chifatso chonse, kudzichepetsa, kudzichepetsa kwa mtima, ndi kukonzeka kwa maganizo. Imvani ndi Kumvera: Ndipo ngati muganiza mu mtima mwanu, Tidzadziwa bwanji mawu omwe Ambuye sanalankhule? Pamene Mneneri alankhula m'Dzina la Ambuye, ngati chinthucho sichitsatira kapena kuchitika, chimenecho ndi chinthu chomwe Ambuye sanalankhule, koma Mneneri walankhula modzikuza: musamuwope iye.

Komabe, ambiri mwa inu mwayiwala Mawu awa omwe aperekedwa monga malire ndi chitetezo kwa mimbulu yolusa, yomwe chiweruzo chawo sichichedwa. Choncho, yerekezerani mwachangu mawu a aliyense amene amanena kuti akulankhula m'malo mwa Khristu Yesu, yemwe alinso Yeshua HaMashiach mwachangu ku Mawu anga okhazikitsidwa operekedwa ndi kutetezedwa m'zolemba zoyera, ngakhale Mawu oyera operekedwa mu nthawi zakale ndi Adonai-YHVH, NDINE Wamkulu, ndi ngakhale Mawu oyera operekedwa mu nthawi za tsopano ndi Adonai-YHVH ndi Khristu Yesu, yemwe alinso Yeshua HaMashiach.

Choncho, Okondedwa anga, imvani Mawu a Ambuye wanu wosatha wa Chilungamo, ngakhale Mawu operekedwa ndi manja anga pamene ndikuwavumbulutsa mu mtima wa mthenga wanga wamphamvu ndi mtumiki ndipo ndi manja anga ndi chifuniro changa Mawu awa avumbulutsidwa m'kumvetsetsa kuchokera mu mtima wa mthenga wanga wamphamvu ndi mtumiki kumene Ine, Khristu Yesu ndimapereka Mawu awa a chitonthozo, ngakhale Mawu otonthoza kutonthoza okhulupirika mwa Khristu, ngakhale anamwali anga anzeru omwe adzikonzekeretsa okha ndi Mawu awa omwe aperekedwa kuti amasulidwe m'dziko lapansi kwa okhulupirika mwa Khristu mu kumvera konse ngakhale kotero kuti nonse mwakonzeka ndi kukonzekera ndi kudikira m'mbali mwa njira ndi nyali zanu zodzaza, ndi zingwe zanu zodulidwa, ngakhale nonse omwe mwatenga moto wanga ndi kuyatsa nyali zanu mu kumvera kolungama ku Mawu anga, chifukwa ndikuyandikira kwa inu Okondedwa anga.

Ndipo zili choncho, Mkwati akuyandikira kwa Mkwatibwi wake ndipo dziko lapansi mwadzidzidzi tsopano lidzaphimbidwa ndi mdima wandiweyani, chifukwa sindidzagawana ulemerero wanga ndi wina, ndipo pamene mdima wandiweyani ukuphimba dziko lapansi, ulemerero ndi kuwala kwa kuonekera kwanga kudzawunikira dziko lapansi ngakhale monga mphezi imatuluka kum'mawa ndi kupita kumadzulo, ndipo mwadzidzidzi ndi mosayembekezereka tsopano Mpingo wanga, Thupi langa, ndi Ngakhale Mkwatibwi wanga adzadzipeza okha atayima m'mwamba mwa miyamba, ngakhale kuyima m'chipinda cha mpando wachifumu cha YHVH, NDINE Wamkulu, ngakhale mphindi ya nthawi yomwe Adonal-YHVH wakhala akudikira kuchokera ku nthawi zosatha zakale, ngakhale nthawi yakale kwambiri yomwe simungamvetsetse panthawiyi. Ndipo nonse mwabisidwa mu mphamvu yake ndi mtima wodabwitsa wa chikondi ndi kuwala, ngakhale kumene anakusungani otetezedwa mu chinsinsi chake chachikulu kwambiri ngakhale Chinsinsi Chachikulu Cha Yahweh, NDINE Wamkulu, ndipo indetu indetu miyamba yakonzeka ndipo ikukondwera kale pa kufika kwanu kwadzidzidzi tsopano, ngakhale musanafike. Chifukwa ngakhale angelo oyera ndi zolengedwa zokongola za kumwamba akudikira mwachidwi kuonekera kwanu ndi kukhalapo kwanu Okondedwa anga.

Choncho, pumutsani maganizo anu ndi mitima yanu mokwanira m'chikondi changa kwa inu ana anga, ndipo musalole mawu onama operekedwa ndi aneneri onama omwe amalira ndi kung'ung'udza ndipo omwe amakhazikitsa masiku a ukwati wanga wa ku Galileya kugwetsa chikhulupiriro chanu, kapena kukupangitsani kukhala achisoni mu mtima ngati kuti ndidzasiya Mkwatibwi wanga wokongola yemwe wadzikonzekeretsa yekha mu kumvera kwachikondi ku mawu a Mkwati wake. Ndipo choncho ndikukulamulani okondedwa anga kuti muyike mtima wanu wonse, moyo, maganizo, ndi mphamvu pa Mawu a Ambuye wanu Wosatha wa Chilungamo, ngakhale Mawu anga oyera a malemba omwe ndavumbulutsa m'kabuku kowonekera komwe ndatsegula m'manja mwa mthenga wanga ndi mtumiki, chifukwa kabuku ka chivumbulutso chaputala 10 kali ndi mawu a mabingu asanu ndi awiri komanso uthenga wabwino wosatha mwa mbali, ndipo awa ndi Mawu omwe aperekedwa kutsogolera anthu anga kudzera m'makolola a masautso a Yakobo ngakhale sabata la makumi asanu ndi awiri la Danieli. Ngakhale Mawu omwe asindikizidwa ndi kusungidwa chinsinsi pa lamulo la YHVH, NDINE Wamkulu, chifukwa indetu, indetu, mawu ndi phokoso la mabingu asanu ndi awiri amalankhula, ndipo mabingu asanu ndi awiri awa ndi kuyambitsa kwa bukhu la chivumbulutso, ngakhale sabata la makumi asanu ndi awiri la Danieli lomwe lidzabweretsa chiwonongeko chokwanira ndi chotheratu cha ufumu wa Heylel Ben Shachar kwamuyaya, ndipo dziko lapansi lidzalamuliridwa mu Chilungamo, ngakhale chilungamo chomwe palibe chinthu choyipa chomwe chidzalowa mu ufumu wanga wa zaka chikwi, choncho pumulani mu chiyembekezo chachikulu m'Mawu awa a chiyembekezo ndi lonjezo losatha Okondedwa anga.

Ine ndine Khristu Yesu, ndipo ndikubwera kudzabweretsa Mawu awa a chilango, chidzudzulo, ndi chikondi kwa omvera, ndi osamvera kuti indetu, indetu, simugwera ku mbali yosamvera ya lupanga langa la mzimu, ngakhale kotero kuti mudzawerengedwa oyenera kusonkhanitsidwa ndi okhulupirika mwadzidzidzi ndi mosayembekezereka tsopano. Chifukwa ngakhale mu mphindi zomaliza izi mfuu wamphamvu usanayankhe, ndipo lipenga lamphamvu ilo lisanalire mwamphamvu ndi mosayembekezereka tsopano, ndikugwedezabe ndi kupangitsa kugwedezeka kwakukulu kubwera pa anthu anga, ngakhale kotero kuti ndikhoza kugwedeza osakhulupirika kuti akhale okhulupirika mu kumvera kolungama, kapena kugwera ku moto woyenga m'kusamvera kwawo, ngakhale ndendende mphindi ya kairos isanadutse ndi nthawi wamba yomwe yatsala pang'ono kukupezani nonse mwadzidzidzi ndi mosayembekezereka tsopano. Ngakhale pamene dziko lapansi likulira mpulumutsi wawo wonama, ngakhale wofera chikhulupiriro wonama yemwe sindinatumize, koma yemwe anaperekedwa ndi Baraba m'malo mwake, ndipo komabe ambiri tsopano akutsatira wofera chikhulupiriro wonamayu, ngakhale kusocheretsedwa kuchokera ku mawu a Khristu Yesu yemwe alinso Yeshua HaMashiach, Sela.

Ndipo zili choncho, kuti ndimakonzekeretsa omwe adzasiyidwa mokwanira ku moto woyenga, ngakhale onse omwe adzapatsidwa mwayi woitana pa dzina la Yesu Khristu mpaka mapeto awo, chifukwa onse omwe sakonda miyoyo yawo mpaka mapeto m'njira yotere adzapeza chipulumutso chosatha chomwe indetu indetu, sichingafanane ndi kuvutika komwe adzapirira chifukwa cha dzina langa.

Konzekerani Mpingo wanga Thupi langa ndi Ngakhale Mkwatibwi wanga, konzekerani ndi kudzikonza nokha molingana ndi Mawu anga oyera ndi zolemba za Uthenga Wabwino Wosatha ndipo ndidikireni okondedwa anga chifukwa ndikubwera kudzokutengani ndipo simudzakhumudwa m'ziyembekezo zanu. Choncho, landirani chidzudzulo changa ndi chilango ndipo mukhale omvera ku Mawu anga ndi machenjezo chifukwa ndikubwera kudzokutengani Okondedwa anga, ndipo sindidzasiya okhulupirika mwa Khristu ku moto woyenga, kapena ku chiweruzo cha ochimwa chomwe sichanu.

Konzani nokha Okondedwa anga, mu kumvera konse ndi chiyembekezo, ndipo dzimizeni nokha m'Mawu a Uthenga Wabwino Wosatha, ndipo muwasunge m'maganizo anu ndi mitima yanu ndikumvetsetsa chuma cha malemba chomwe ndapereka m'dziko lapansi kuti ndikusungeni mu nthawi izi za kuvumbulutsidwa, ngakhale kuvumbulutsidwa komwe ndinayamba ndi malipenga ambiri okonzeka mu gulu langa laling'ono omwe alira mokhulupirika, ngakhale malipenga omwe anakonza njira ya mthenga wanga ndi mtumiki yemwe ndi chiwiya chosankhidwa kwa Ambuye wake Wosatha, ngakhale Khristu Yesu, yemwe alinso Yeshua HaMashiach, ngakhale kumene ndimavumbulutsa Mawu anga mu chiwiya changa chopatulidwa chomwe chili mu mgwirizano ndi chodzipereka mokwanira ku zolinga zomwe ndamutumira, ndipo ndi lamulo langa ndi mphamvu kuti zala zake zimapeza makiyi ngakhale kuvumbulutsa Mawu oyera awa omwe ndapereka mwa iye kuti amasulidwe m'dziko lapansi kwa nthawi yoyamba, ngakhale Mawu omwe akonzedwa kuti apeze maso, makutu, ndi mitima ya onse omwe atumizidwa kwa iwo, Sela.

Choncho, imvani phokoso la mawu anga m'chilengezo choyera ichi chomwe chaperekedwa kukoka okhulupirika ku lamulo langa, ndi kukukonzerani nonse mu kumvera ku phokoso la lipenga ilo, kuti mukasonkhanitsidwe kwa ine pa lamulo langa.

Taonani! Ndikusaina chilengezo ichi ndi kalata ndi dzanja la mthenga wanga ndi mtumiki wanga, komabe yosainidwa m'dzina langa lenileni mu chikondi chonse ndi ulamuliro,
