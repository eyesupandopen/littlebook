Magazi a Mwanawankhosa wa Yahweh

Bwerani kwa Ambuye wanu wamuyaya ndipo imvani Mawu anga, abale anga, ndipo weramitsani mitima yanu pomvera Mawu a Yeshua HaMashiach. Chifukwa Ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndidzapanga munthu wanzeru pakati panu, O' Isiraeli, kukhala wanzeru kwambiri pamene mumvera malangizo anga kuti mulandire nzeru ndi chidziwitso chowonjezereka chifukwa nzeru ndi chidziwitso zidzakhala kukhazikika kwa nthawi zanu ndi mphamvu ya chipulumutso, kuopa Ambuye ndiye chuma chanu.

Choncho, mvetsetsani nthawi zomwe mukukhala kuti muyende mwachindunji ndipo kuti zolinga zanga za chipulumutso zikwaniritsidwe mwa inu omwe mwaitanidwa monga mwa dzina langa, ngakhale inu O' Isiraeli, ngakhale kuti panopa simukundidziwa kapena kukhala m'njira zanga chifukwa Isiraeli akulamulidwa ndi Esau ndi Asikenazi, pamene kwanenedwa momveka bwino kuti wamkulu adzatumikira wamng'ono ndipo Esau kapena Asikenazi alibe ufulu uliwonse pa cholowa cha ukulu wa Yakobo. Ndipo komabe, Yakobo anakana Yeshua HaMashiach ndipo Ine ndinatembenukira kwa amitundu kwa nyengo yochepa kuti ndikwaniritse zomwe Isiraeli sanamvere kuti akwaniritse mokwanira kuti kuchokera kwa Isiraeli amitundu adzadalitsidwa ndipo kuti mwa mafuko otayika otsalira adzapulumutsidwa.

Komabe, pamene mutembenuza mitima yanu pomvera mawu a malangizo a Mesiya wanu weniweni mudzabwezeretsedwa monga anthu apadera kwa Yahweh, ngakhale kuti makolo anu anatembenuka kuchoka kwa Yahweh kalekale. Inde, anatembenuka kuchoka kwa Yahweh pamene anandikana Ine, Yesu wa ku Nazarete, yemwenso ndi Yeshua HaMashiach, ngakhale m'masiku omwe Ponto Pilato ankalamulira Yudeya pamene ndinaperekedwa ndi ansembe awo aakulu ndi Sanihedirini m'manja mwa anthu ochimwa kuti achite zinthu zomwe chilamulo chinawaletsa kuchita, komabe chifukwa cha chidani chawo pa Ine ndi pa zinthu zonse za Yahweh anachita zimenezo mofunitsitsa ndi njiru ndi nkhanza yaikulu. Ndipo motero chipulumutso cha Adamu chikukwaniritsidwa ndi manja a adani ake, kuti aliyense wokhulupirira ndi kuitana pa dzina la Yesu wa ku Nazarete yemwenso ndi Yeshua HaMashiach adzapulumutsidwa mpaka kumapeto.

Ndipo zili choncho chifukwa Ine ndine mwanawankhosa wa Yahweh, kodi chitetezero cha machimo a anthu chikanakwaniritsidwa bwanji kupatula ngati magazi anga opanda tchimo akhetsedwa nsembe kuti alipire mtengo womwe ulibe malire. Mvetsetsani kuti ngakhale munthu wachibadwa amadzitama modzikuza kwambiri kuti amadzipangira yekha ndipo amadzidalira pa zinthu zonse, munthu wachibadwa wagwa mu mkhalidwe wochimwa ndi womvetsa chisoni wa chosalungama ndi chitonzo chomwe chiri choyenera.

Komabe, mu chisomo changa ndi chifundo ndi kudzipereka nsembe kwanga pa Gologota pamene ndinadzipereka ndekha kwa Yahweh monga mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu pamene ndinadzipereka mu zowawa zazikulu pamene dontho langa lomaliza la magazi linayenderera pa Gologota, ngakhale mpaka mpweya wanga wotsiriza pamene ndinanena mawu akuti kwatha. Ndipo ndinaikidwa m'manda, ndikuuka kachiwiri tsiku lachitatu monga mwa malemba; monga momwe Yahweh analonjezera kuti adzachita, chifukwa n'zosatheka kuti imfa igwire magazi opanda tchimo. Ndipo Yahweh anandiukitsa kumene, patangopita nthawi yochepa ndinalowa m'malo opatulika kwambiri m'mwamba momwemo, kumene ndinadzipereka ndekha monga mkulu wa ansembe kwamuyaya kwa Yahweh, NDINE wamkulu, nsembe yomwe anaivomereza kwamuyaya. Poona tsono kuti muli ndi mkulu wa ansembe wamkulu chotere, amene walowa kumwamba, ngakhale Yesu mwana wa Yahweh, tiyeni tigwirisishe chivomerezo chathu. Pakuti mulibe mkulu wa ansembe, O' Isiraeli, amene sangakhudzidwe ndi kumva kwa zofooka zanu, koma anayesedwa m'zinthu zonse mofananamo, koma wopanda tchimo.

Choncho ndikufunsa, O' Isiraeli, n'chiyani chatha? Choncho ndikuyankha momveka bwino: Pangano langa latsopano, lomwe ndi pangano lamuyaya ngakhale Chipangano changa Chatsopano! Pakuti chipangano chimatsimikiziridwa pamene anthu amwalira: pakuti sichikhala ndi mphamvu malinga ngati wochipanga ali moyo; ndipo pafupifupi zinthu zonse mwa chilamulo zimayeretsedwa ndi magazi, ndipo popanda kukhetsa magazi palibe chikhululukiro cha tchimo. Mofananamo ndi Chipangano Chakale pamene Mose analankhula lamulo lililonse kwa anthu, monga mwa Chilamulo, anatenga magazi a ana a ng'ombe ndi a mbuzi, ndi madzi ndi ubweya wofiirira ndi hisope, ndikuwaza buku, ndi anthu onse, nati, Ichi ndi magazi a chipangano, chomwe Mulungu wakuikirani inu.

Komabe, chipangano cha Kale, ngakhale chipangano choyamba cha Mose sichinapangidwe kuti chikhale mapeto a dongosolo la Yahweh la chiwombolo cha anthu, kapena Isiraeli sanayenera kusonkhana modzikuza, kupanga magulu ogawanika m'magawo ambiri osafunikira, ndi akupha. Koma m'malo mwake Isiraeli anayenera kuima monga chowunikira cha chipulumutso kwa amitundu kuti mitundu iwone kuti kuli Mulungu mu Isiraeli ndipo kuti mitundu ithiridwe mu Isiraeli kuti iphunzire za Yahweh monga momwe zinalembedwera nthawi zakale.

Choncho, mvetsetsani chikhalidwe chosakhalitsa cha Mose monga momwe chinkachitikira ndi Isiraeli m'nthawi zisanachitike Chipangano changa Chatsopano ndipo mvetsetsani kuti Mose amakhala mokwanira mwa Mesiya wanu weniweni, ngakhale woyera wa Mulungu yemwe anakhala mwangwiro monga mwa Mose ndipo anapereka moyo wake kamodzi, konse, ndi kwamuyaya kuti alipire chilango cha Mose m'malo mwanu. Monga kwalembedwa, choncho, mvetsetsani Chipangano changa Chatsopano, O' Isiraeli: Pakuti Chilamulo pokhala ndi mthunzi wa zinthu zabwino zikubwera, osati chifaniziro chenicheni cha zinthuzo, sichingathe ndi nsembe zomwezo, zomwe amapereka chaka ndi chaka mosalekeza, kuyeretsa obwera kumeneko. Pakuti kodi sizikanaleka kuperekedwa, chifukwa chakuti obwerawo, atayeretsedwa kamodzi, sakanakhalanso ndi chikumbumtima cha machimo? Koma mu nsembe zimenezo, muli chikumbutso cha machimo chaka chilichonse.

Pakuti n'zosatheka kuti magazi a ng'ombe ndi mbuzi achotse machimo. Ndipo zili choncho abale anga, kuti Mose ankangotha kuyeretsa machimo kwakanthawi, komabe machimo ambiri pansi pa Mose anatsalira kumene omwe anachita zinthu zotere anadulidwa kwa anthu kwamuyaya chifukwa cha machimo awo. Choncho, Mose anali mthunzi wa zinthu zikubwera, zinthu zomwe zakwaniritsidwa mokwanira mu nsembe yanga ya Ine ndekha, chifukwa magazi anga opanda tchimo amatetezera machimo onse, kamodzi konse ndi kwamuyaya, ngakhale kuti onse omwe ali a Adamu apeze chipulumutso kudzera mmenemo. Komabe kwa omwe amalandira chizindikiro cha chilombo, sadzakhala ndi chikhululukiro chilichonse.

Ndipo motero kwalembedwa za Yeshua HaMashiach, kuti mwa moyo wanga ndi kumvera Adamu akhoza kubwezeretsedwa kwa Elohim, mlengi wake wamuyaya ndi wolungama. Chifukwa chake, pamene abwera m'dziko lapansi, amati, nsembe ndi chopereka simunafune: koma thupi mwandikonzera ine. Mu nsembe zopsereza, ndi nsembe za uchimo, simunakondwere nazo. Pamenepo ndinati, Taonani, ndikubwera (Kumayambiriro kwa buku kwalembedwa za ine) kuti ndichite chifuniro chanu, O Mulungu. Pamwambapa, pamene anati, Nsembe ndi chopereka, ndi nsembe zopsereza, ndi nsembe za uchimo simunafune, kapena kukondwera nazo (zomwe zimaperekedwa ndi Chilamulo) Pamenepo anati, Taonani, ndikubwera kudzachita chifuniro chanu, O Mulungu, amachotsa choyamba, kuti akhazikitse chachiwiri. Mwa chifuniro chimenecho ife tayeretsedwa, ngakhale mwa kupereka kwa thupi la Yesu Khristu komwe kunachitika kamodzi. Ndipo Wansembe aliyense wa Levi amaima tsiku ndi tsiku akutumikira, ndipo nthawi zambiri amapereka mtundu umodzi wa nsembe, zomwe sizingachotse machimo: Koma munthu uyu atapereka nsembe imodzi chifukwa cha machimo, amakhala kwamuyaya kudzanja lamanja la Mulungu, Ndipo kuyambira tsopano akudikira, mpaka adani ake atapangidwa chopondapo mapazi ake. Pakuti ndi nsembe imodzi wapatula kwamuyaya omwe ayeretsedwa mmenemo.

Pakuti Yahweh amatichitiranso umboni: pakuti atatha kunena kale, Ichi ndi Chipangano chomwe ndidzapanga nawo pambuyo pa masiku amenewo, akutero Elohim, Ndidzaika malamulo anga m'mitima mwawo, ndipo m'maganizo mwawo ndidzawalemba. Ndipo machimo awo ndi zoyipa zawo sindidzazikumbukiranso. Tsopano pamene pakhala chikhululukiro cha zinthu izi, palibenso nsembe ya uchimo. Ndipo mukuima mu nthawi ya umbuli wa mtundu pamene Makolo anu kalekale anakana iye yemwe ali Mwanawankhosa wangwiro wa Paskha wa Yahweh mwiniwake, ngakhale pamene Yahweh anakonda dziko lapansi kotero kuti anapereka mwana wake wobadwa yekha kuti aliyense wokhulupirira pa dzina la Yeshua HaMashiach, ngakhale Yesu Khristu ngakhale munthu ameneyo adzapulumutsidwa mpaka kumapeto. Ndipo choncho ndikukuitanani kuti mutembenuke kuchoka ku umbuli wanu ndi khungu lina, O' Isiraeli, ngakhale pokana ndi kutsutsa mokwanira nzeru zamwano za Esau zomwe zili mu Talmud yake yonyansa ndikubwerera ku Mawu oyera a Yahweh, ngakhale Mawu omwe asungidwa mu mzere wa Malemba a Byzantine, ngakhale Textus Receptus monga momwe asungidwira mu lemba la Stephens.

Choncho, mvetsetsani zomwe kukana Yeshua HaMashiach kwamuyaya kumatanthauza kwa inu Isiraeli kuti mumvetsetse kuopsa kwa kukanako. Pakuti ngati muchimwa mofunitsitsa mutalandira ndi kuvomereza choonadi chimenecho, palibenso nsembe ya machimo, koma kuyembekezera koopsa kwa chiweruzo, ndi moto waukali, womwe udzawononga adani. Iye amene ananyoza Chilamulo cha Mose, anamwalira popanda chifundo pansi pa mboni ziwiri, kapena zitatu: Kodi mukuganiza kuti mudzakhala oyenera chilango chowawa chotani, inu omwe mukuponda pansi Mwana wa Mulungu, ndikuwona magazi a Chipangano Chatsopano ngati chinthu chosayera. Choncho, landirani nsembe yanga ndipo kuulula machimo anu kwa ine kuti ndikukhululukireni pakuti ndine wokhulupirika ndi wolungama kuti ndikukhululukireni machimo anu kuti muyeretsedwe m'magazi anga otetezera ndipo kuulula zolakwa zanu wina ndi mzake.

Choncho, ndikubwera kwa inu mu chifundo changa ndi chisomo kuti ndikutsogolereni kutali ndi khungu lanu lina, O' Isiraeli, ngakhale kuti mupeze chipulumutso mwa kumvera kwanu mawu anga ndipo kuti muchoke ku kukana kwa Makolo anu komwe kwasungidwa kwamuyaya mu Talmud yanu yonyansa ndi mu chipembedzo chanu chonyansa chomwe Esau wapanga kuti mukuwonongekeni, koma chomwe mukuchigwiririra ngati kuti nzeru zake zinaperekedwa ndi Yahweh mwiniwake, osati Satana. O' Isiraeli, imvani mawu anga opulumutsa ndipo landirani chikondi changa pa inu ndipo bwerani kwa ine ndi mitima yanu yonse yotseguka ndi yokonzeka kulandira kuti mudzazidwe ndi ulemerero wonse ndi ukoma.

Ndikukupatsani kuti mumvetsetse chikhululukiro chokwanira ndi kubwezeretsedwa komwe magazi anga otetezera amabweretsa kwa onse omwe amandilandira monga Ambuye wawo wopulumutsa, komanso monga Mesiya wawo weniweni, pakuti ndine amene. Ndipo monga Adamu analengedwa, kupangidwa, ndi kukhala wopanda tchimo ndi mu ungwiro wokwanira mpaka tsiku lomwe anachimwa mofunitsitsa, chimodzimodzinso malipiro a tchimo la Adamu amafuna magazi opanda tchimo, ngakhale chofunikira chosatheka kwa onse omwe amakhala pa dziko lapansi mu chivundi kuti apereke. Ndipo mu chikondi cha Yahweh anapereka ungwiro wotere ndi magazi opanda tchimo m'thupi la mwana wake wobadwa yekha yemwe waperekedwa nsembe chifukwa cha machimo a Adamu, choncho palibenso nsembe ya tchimo.

Ndine amene ndinadzipereka mokwanira chifukwa cha machimo a Adamu wogwa.

Ndine amene ndinadzipereka mokwanira mu chitetezero chamuyaya kuti onse omwe amadzigonjera ku chipulumutso chamuyaya ayeretsedwe mmenemo.

Ndine amene ndili Mkulu wanu wa Ansembe kwamuyaya monga mwa Ndondomeko ya Melikizedeki, ngakhale iye amene anadzipereka monga Paskha wangwiro kamodzi, konse, ndi kwamuyaya.

Ndine Yeshua HaMashiach, ngakhale iye amene ali Mesiya wanu weniweni ndipo ulamuliro wanga ulibe mapeto.

Ndine Khristu Yesu, ndipo ndatumiza Mawu anga kudzera mwa mthenga wanga ndi mtumiki wanga ndipo kudzera mwa mthenga wanga ndi mtumiki wanga Mawu anga atumizidwa.

Choncho, mvetsetsani kuti kukwaniritsidwa kwa zinthu zonse kwafika, O' Isiraeli, ndipo ndikukuitanani kwa ine kudzera mu Uthenga Wabwino Wosatha womwe waperekedwa mu nthawi zoyenera za kuwulula ndipo nthawi za chivumbulutso ndi tsopano. Chifukwa kuyambira pachiyambi Yahweh ananena kwa njoka m'munda mu nthawi zomwe tchimo linalowa m'dziko lapansi, Ndidzaika chidani pakati pa iwe ndi mkazi ndi pakati pa mbewu yako ndi mbewu yake, iwe udzaluma chidendene chake, ndipo iye adzaphwanya mutu wako.

Ndipo motero m'kupita kwa nthawi Davide, pamene anali mnyamata wamng'ono woweta nkhosa, yemwe anakhala Mfumu ya Isiraeli ufumu usanagawanike; yemwenso anakhala munthu wapamtima pa Yahweh, anateteza Isiraeli ndikupha chimphona cha Mfilisiti chija, ngakhale Goliati wa ku Gati yemwe anali namsongole wochokera kwa Arefaimu! Choncho Davide mnyamata wamng'ono woweta nkhosa anapita kukamenya nkhondo ndi Goliati wa ku Gati ngakhale monga mbusa, ndi ndodo, thumba ndi legeni, kupita kukamenyana ndi wankhondo wachimphona, koma Davide anapita kunkhondo m'dzina la Yahweh, yemwe Afilisiti anamunyoza, ndipo Davide anaima kuti amenyane ndi adani a Isiraeli kuti gulu lankhondo la Elohim lipambane.

Pamene mwala wochokera mu legeni ya Davide unapangitsa chimphona chosayerachi kugwa pansi chikugubuduzika, Davide anasolola lupanga la chimphonachi ndikudula mutu wake. Ndipo monga kwalembedwa kalekale: Ndipo Davide anatenga mutu wa Mfilisitiyo, ndikuubweretsa ku Yerusalemu, koma anaika zida zake m'hema mwake. Choncho Davide anaikira chigaza cha Goliati wa ku Gati kunja kwa makoma a mzinda wa Yerusalemu. Komabe, mu nsembe yanga ya Ine ndekha monga mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu, ndinatengedwa kunja kwa makoma a mzinda ndipo kumeneko pa Gologota ndinapachikidwa, ngakhale malo a chigaza. Ndipo pamene ndinapachikidwa, anandikhomera pa mtengo waukaliwo kumene analuma chidendene changa ndi misomali yawo pamene anandikhomera pa mtengo waukaliwo. Ndipo motero chipambano chinapezedwa kumeneko kwa Adamu pamene chidendene changa chinalumidwa kumeneko pa Kalvare kumene mutu wa Satana unaphwanyidwanso pamene magazi anga ankayenderera kubweretsa chipambano pa mbewu ya njoka kwamuyaya. Choncho, mofananamo ndi mnyamata woweta nkhosa yemwe anagonjetsa mbewu ya Satana, momwemonso Mbusa wamkulu anagonjetsa Satana kwamuyaya mu nsembe yanga ya chikondi yomwe ndinapanga chifukwa cha machimo a Adamu.

Komabe, pamene ndinkamwalira mu zowawa zazikulu monga chigawenga wamba (chigawenga chomwe ndilibe mlandu nacho) kunja kwa zipata za mzinda woyerawo, magazi anga opanda tchimo anakhetsedwa m'thupi mwanga kumeneko monga choyimira chokwanira cha munthu wochimwa wa dothi lofiira akubwera ku chiwombolo chokwanira. Ndipo pamene magazi anga ananyowetsa dothi lofiira pa malo a chigaza kumeneko pa kalvare mutu wa njoka unaphwanyidwa kwamuyaya ndi kosatha, ndipo pakutha pa zinthu zonse chinjoka chakalecho chidzamangidwa ndikuponyedwa kwamuyaya kumoto wa chilango kumene mphutsi yawo simafa, pakuti chimenecho ndi cholowa chake.

Ndipo ndine amene ndikubwera kudzawononga kotheratu ufumu wa adani kuti chinjoka chimangidwe ndikusindikizidwa m'phompho kwa zaka chikwi kuti ulamuliro wanga wolungama pa dziko lapansi ukhazikitsidwe. Pamapeto pake adzamasulidwa kwa nyengo yake yochepa kumene adzasonkhanitsa kupanduka kwake komaliza. Ndipo pamene akuukira msasa wa oyera mtima moto udzatsika kuchokera kwa Yahweh mwiniwake ndikuwawononga kotheratu pa nthawi yomwe Satana adzaponyedwa kumoto wamuyaya woyaka kwamuyaya ndi sulufule wonunkha.

Chifukwa chake, ndikukupatsani kuti mumvetsetse, O' Isiraeli, chilango cha Mose chalipidwa mokwanira ndi nsembe ya Yeshua HaMashiach yemwe ndi Yesu Khristu m'chinenero cha Chingerezi ngakhale kuti mumvetsetse kukwanira kwa nsembe yanga ndi ungwiro womwe wakwaniritsidwa ndi magazi anga otetezera nthawi zonse omwe anakhetsedwa ndikuperekedwa chifukwa cha machimo a Adamu. Mvetsetsani mokwanira, O' Isiraeli, kuti ngati ogwawo akadamvetsetsa chinsinsi cha Yahweh, sakanapachika Ambuye wanu wa Ulemerero! Pakuti mwa kupachikidwa kwanga kumene njoka inaluma chidendene changa kwakanthawi, mutu wa njoka tsopano waphwanyidwa kwamuyaya, ngakhale bala lomwe sadzachirapo.

Choncho, O' Isiraeli, imvani Mawu anga ndipo mvetsetsani chikondi changa pa inu ndipo kuti ndikukuitanani mu nthawi yomalizayi. Choncho mvetsetsani kulankhula kwanga chifukwa kwalembedwa: Mulungu wa mtendere amene anabweretsanso kuchokera kwa akufa Yeshua HaMashiach, mbusa wamkulu wa nkhosa, kudzera m'magazi a pangano lamuyaya, akupangeni inu kukhala okwanira mu ntchito zonse zabwino, kuti muchite chifuniro chake, akugwira ntchito mwa inu zomwe ziri zokondweretsa pamaso pake kudzera mwa Yesu Khristu, kwa iye kukhale ulemerero kwamuyaya, Ameni.
