Koma mwanyengedwa inu okhala pa dziko lapansi kuganiza kuti munthu aliyense ndi wofanana ndi Khristu Yesu Ambuye Wamuyaya wa Chilungamo, amene simumudziwa, ngakhale inu nonse amene mukhala mu mdima okonda zoyipa mu kusamvera kwakukulu kwa mawu a Khristu Yesu amenenso ndi Mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu amene wakhala kudzanja lamanja la Yahweh Wamkulu NDINE AMENE NDINE, amene mpando wake wachifumu ndi ulamuliro wake ndimagawana naye chifukwa ndapangidwa kukhala mzimu wopatsa moyo, amene tsopano wayima mu mphamvu yayikulu ngati Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda chifukwa palibe Mwana wa Munthu wina, Sela, ndipo mvetsetsani kuti mkwiyo wanga ndi ukali wanga wakhazikitsidwa kuti ugwe mu chiwonongeko chadzidzidzi chomwe simudzachiwona chikubwera, kapena kuthawa.

Tsoka, TSOKA LAKUKULU kwa inu ana a anthu osamvera omwe tsopano Mkwiyo wa Mwanawankhosa udzagwa modzidzimutsa tsopano, kubweretsa chiwonongeko chadzidzidzi ndi chosayembekezereka chifukwa cha njira zanu zopembedza mafano iwe hule lalikulu, ndipo mudzamva kubangula kwanga pamene dziko lapansi likuda, chifukwa sindidzagawana ulemerero wanga ndi wina, Sela!

Imva Mawu anga Heylel, ngakhale iwe kalonga wamng'ono wogonjetsedwa ndipo khala pansi mu kugonjetsedwa ndi kusiya kubangula kwako kopanda mano, chifukwa ndiwe mdani wogonjetsedwa amene sadzakhala ndi nthawi yagolide koma chiweruzo chowawa m'malo mwake, koma choyamba udzatumikira zolinga za YHVH Wamkulu NDINE AMENE NDINE, pansi pa ulamuliro wanga mu kugonjetsedwa kwako, (ndipo mu manyazi ako ndi kugonjetsedwa) udzachita zokhazo zomwe wapatsidwa chilolezo ndi ulamuliro wanga, chifukwa palibe paliponse mu chilengedwe cha YHVH pamene dzanja langa ndi ulamuliro wanga wathunthu sizifika ndipo ndikuwona mantha ako, iwe njoka yogonjetsedwa! Khala pansi mu chete iwe serafi wopanduka!

M'dzanja langa .. kiyi .. ndi unyolo waukulu, ndipo posachedwa udzamangidwa mu kugonjetsedwa kotheratu pansi pa mpando wachifumu wa YHVH Wamkulu NDINE AMENE NDINE, pamene ufumu wako udzagwa kotheratu mu chiweruzo chachikulu ndipo ndikuwona mantha ako kalonga wamng'ono, ndipo sudzathawa m'manja mwanga choncho imva Mawu a Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda, ndipo mvetsetsa mkwiyo wanga ndi ukali wanga ndi mphamvu zanga ndikuti sindidzakanidwa chuma changa!

Khala mu chete kalonga wamng'ono chifukwa dzanja langa lili pakhosi pako mwamphamvu, ndipo chotchedwa AI chako chili pansi pa mpando wachifumu wa YHVH, Wamkulu, NDINE AMENE NDINE pa lamulo langa iwe kalonga wamng'ono wogonjetsedwa! Ndipo ndani alipo amene angaletse dzanja lamphamvu, mphamvu, ndi ulamuliro woperekedwa mwa Khristu Yesu, Ambuye Wamuyaya wa Chilungamo, amenenso ndi Yeshua HaMashiach, Sela!

Inu nonse ogwa mudzachita zokhazo zomwe mwapatsidwa chilolezo kuchita mu manyazi anu ndi kugonjetsedwa kwanu, komabe ola lanu lafika kalonga wamng'ono, ndipo mukuwona bwino, ngakhale mukunama kwambiri, iwe wokwawa. Koma njira zanu zili kumapeto ndipo Mavuto a Yakobo omwe ndi sabata la makumi asanu ndi awiri la Danieli adzayeretsa dziko lapansi ku njira zanu mpaka kalekale: Kupatula mu nyengo yanu yomaliza yaying'ono ya kupanduka mudzasonkhanitsa mankhusu otsala omwe adzaphuka mwatsopano mu kupanduka pansi pa ulamuliro Wolungama wa Uthenga Wabwino Wamuyaya womwe tsopano wavumbulutsidwa mwa mbali m'Buku Laling'ono la Chivumbulutso Mutu 10, ngakhale buku laling'ono lotseguka lomwe ndatsegula m'dzanja la mtumiki wanga.

Komabe zili choncho! Zotsala za Chipangano Chatsopano zidzaperekedwa kwa Yakobo amene adzakhala pamapazi a mboni zanga ziwiri zomwe zidzafika modzidzimutsa m'maiko mu njira yomwe simungathe kulosera, iwe kalonga wamng'ono wogonjetsedwa. Ndipo Mitengo yanga Yiwiri ya Azitona, yomwe ndi Zoyikapo Nyale zanga Ziwiri idzakonzekera ndi kulekanitsa ngakhale otsala amphamvu a tirigu omwe adzagwadira Yeshua HaMashiach motero kuchotsa khungu laling'ono kwa anthu anga owona omwe adzalekanitsidwa ndi namsongole wanu! Komabe bwalo la anthu amitundu lidzaponderezedwa pansi pa mapazi mu nkhondo yolimbana ndi oyera mtima yochitidwa ndi chirombo chochokera kunyanja, chomwe chimadzitcha mphezi ikugwa kuchokera kumwamba m'Chihebri, ngakhale chobisidwa ngati Barack Hussein Obama amene amagwira ntchito limodzi ndi chirombo chochokera pamtunda chomwe chili ndi ululu wake ndi kulumwa kwa njoka m'dzanja chomwe chidzasakaniza chitsulo ndi dongo koma sizidzaphatikizana wina ndi mzake.

Ndipo Enoke Mlembi ndi Eliya wa ku Tishibe adzakonzekera tirigu wanga kuti achotsedwe, ngakhale tirigu wanga wosungidwa kwamuyaya ndi chitetezo cha pangano ndi lonjezo kuchokera kwa ana osalungama a kusamvera mpaka kalekale pokwaniritsa Chipangano changa Chatsopano cha Chilungamo chomwe Baibulo langa Lopatulika linali chabe malipiro oyamba a chuma chogulidwa chomwe chidzayima nthawi zonse mu chowonadi chamuyaya chifukwa ndine Mawu a Adonai-YHVH opangidwa thupi, amenenso ndi maziko anu amuyaya a chipulumutso, ngakhale mwala wanu waukulu wapangodya, amene posachedwa tsopano adzapereka lamulo: Ndipo mfuu wamphamvu! Ndipo kulira kwa lipenga kumeneko kudzaimba ndi kulengeza modzidzimutsa, mwamphamvu, ndi mosayembekezereka tsopano mu chikondwerero cha okhulupirika mwa Khristu, kuti zimvekedwe kokha ndi omwe akonzedwa ku mawayilesi ake mu kulapa kolungama, omwe zingwe zawo za nyale zadulidwa mu kumvera njira zanga!

Lolani omwe akumva: Mvetsetsani mawu akulira mu chikhulupiriyo ndi mtendere, chifukwa ndi Atate uti amene angapatse njoka kwa ana ake, kupatula pansi pa mapazi awo, yomangidwa mu chete chogonjetsedwa kuti imasulidwe kulamulira osakhulupirika, ngakhale ndipo pokhapokha pamene okhulupirika mwa Khristu akuchoka modzidzimutsa tsopano!

Ndipo pamene ulemerero wa kuwala kwanga ukuphulika kudutsa mlengalenga ndipo lipenga lamphamvu limenelo likulira mokongola kwambiri mu kuitana koyembekezera kwa Mkwatibwi wanga ndi Alendo athu komwe kudzalira kwa okhulupirika mwa Khristu, ngakhale inu nonse omwe ndakukonzerani ku mawayilesi a lipenga lamphamvu la Mulungu lomwe ndi lipenga lomaliza la m'badwo wanga wa chisomo lomwe liti lithe modzidzimutsa ndi mosayembekezereka: Imvani Mawu anthu anga chifukwa woyang'anira wanu ndi muwomboli wanu tsopano wayima wokonzeka kukuwombolani kwathunthu Mpingo wanga, Thupi langa ndi Ngakhale Mkwatibwi wanga, Kondwerani!

Konzekerani mkwatibwi wanga ndipo khalani m'mbali mwa njira ndi nyale zanu zodzazidwa kwathunthu ndi mafuta anga oyera, omwe ndi chidindo cha Chilungamo ndi mzimu wa Umwana, womwe ndi mzimu woyera wa lonjezo wobadwa mkati mwa aliyense wa ana a kuwala, omwe ali ndi malipiro oyamba athunthu a chuma chogulidwa chimenecho, chomwe chatsala pang'ono kuwomboledwa kwathunthu ndi Khristu Yesu, Ambuye wawo Wamuyaya ndi Mawu oyera a umboni wanga ndi mzimu chifukwa mkwiyo wanga suli wa inu.

Choncho, siyani ntchito zanu ndipo pumulani m'chikondi changa anthu anga, komabe yimani chilili ndikulola lupanga la mzimu wanga lomwe lidzalekanitsa mankhusu omaliza kuchokera m'miyoyo yanu okondedwa anga.

Konzekerani okondedwa anga chifukwa chiwombolo chanu chafika ndipo posachedwa mudzamva mfuu wamphamvu ndipo mudzakwezedwa modzidzimutsa tsopano ndi nthawi yomweyo m'chipinda chachifumu cha YHVH mwiniwake, choncho konzekerani anthu anga chifukwa ndikufika ngati mbala usiku osati ngati chochitika chokonzedwa, komabe lolani kulekanitsa kwanga kuyime ndikuwerenga manambala molingana ndi Mawu anga Opatulika ndi njira zanga; osati amatsenga omwe amayang'ana ndi kung'ung'udza, kapena kumangiriridwa ku madeti osagwirizana pa kalendala yanu yonyansa ya Gregory.

Imvani Mawu anga okhala pa dziko lapansi chifukwa ndine Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda ndipo ndikubwera kudzaweruza ndi kupanga nkhondo!

Imvani mawu a nzeru ndikugwada bondo ndikumva mawu amoyo a Yeshua HaMashiach, chifukwa ndine Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda, ndipo ndine Mfumu ya Mafumu ndi Mbuye wa Ambuye! Ngakhale iye amene El Shaddai, ngakhale Mulungu Wamphamvuyonse ameneyo, wamukweza kwambiri ndikumupatsa dzina loposa dzina lina lililonse. Kuti m'Dzina la Yesu bondo lililonse lipinde, la zinthu zakumwamba, ndi zinthu zapadziko lapansi, ndi zinthu zapansi pa dziko lapansi: Ndikuti lilime lililonse livomereze, kuti Yesu Khristu ndi Ambuye, ku ulemerero wa Atate wa Zowunikira, ngakhale Yahweh, Mulungu wamuyaya ndi wosatha, chifukwa palibe wina wofanana ndi Atate wathu!

Imvani mawu a Ambuye wanu ndikumvetsetsa zinthu zomwe zikuchitika kale ndi zinthu zomwe zatsala pang'ono kuchitika, chifukwa taonani ndine amene ndimalankhula za zinthu zomwe zikubwera ngati kuti zachitika kale chifukwa ndithudi Ambuye wanu wamkulu, ngakhale ine Khristu Yesu wopambana sindidzachita kanthu, kupatula ndivumbulutsa zinsinsi zanga kwa atumiki anga Aneneri. Chifukwa chiyani mukumvera mawu ambiri otsutsana, ndipo chifukwa chiyani mukuyima pakati pa malingaliro awiri? Kodi sindinanene kuti aneneri anga amalankhula mogwirizana ndipo sadzatsutsana ndi Mawu anga opatulika? Chifukwa ndine Mawu opangidwa thupi amene amakhala pakati panu ndi mzimu wanga woyera womwe umabadwa mkati mwanu mpaka kalekale, chifukwa mzimu uliwonse womwe wabadwanso umasindikizidwa ndi mzimu woyera wa lonjezo womwe ndi mbewu yanga yosawonongeka ndipo chidindo cha Mulungu Wamoyo sichingasweke. Ndipo tsiku la chiwombolo cha chuma chogulidwa lili pafupi ndipo chiwombolo chanu chathunthu chili pano.

Atero Ambuye wa Makamu, musamve mawu a aneneri omwe amakuloserani, ndikukuphunzitsani zachabechabe: amalankhula masomphenya a mitima yawo, osati otuluka m'kamwa mwa Ambuye. Bwerani kwa ine ana anga chifukwa kudzoza komwe mwalandira kwa ine, kumakhala mwa inu: ndipo simukusowa kuti munthu aliyense akuphunzitseni: Koma, monga kudzoza komweko kumakuphunzitsani zinthu zonse, ndipo ndi kowona, ndipo sikuli bodza: ndipo ngakhale monga kwakuphunzitsani, mudzakhala mwa ine. Ana anga khalani mwa ine ndikuyerekezera mawu a aliyense amene amati akulankhula m'malo mwanga ndi malemba ndikundipempha kuti nditsimikizire mu mzimu wanu, ngati mwina mudzamvera mawu anga chifukwa ambiri a inu ndinu ogontha, ndipo ambiri a inu mumakonda mawu abodza omwe amakondweretsa makutu anu kuti muwoneke anzeru koma m'malo mwake mumatsatira mawu abodza a zitsiru zodzikuza.

Taonani! Ndikulankhula za nthawi za mavuto a Yakobo zomwe zili zokonzeka kuphulika kudutsa dziko lonse lapansi. Chifukwa chiweruzo chikuchitika kale ndipo sichingaletsedwe, chifukwa chomwe Yahweh alengeza palibe amene angasinthe! Misozi yambiri, yambiri ndalira pa chiweruzo cha Babulo Wachinsinsi ndipo ndatumiza aneneri anga kwa zaka zambiri, zambiri tsopano ndipo ndatumiza ngakhale alendo ochokera kumayiko achilendo kudzachitira umboni ndi kulosera za ziweruzo zomwe zinkayandikira koma tsopano zili pano.

Chifukwa zidzakhala choncho chifukwa zalembedwa choncho: Nyanga khumi zomwe unaziwona pa chirombo, izi zidzadana ndi hule, ndipo zidzamupanga kukhala wabwinja, ndi wamaliseche, ndipo zidzadya mnofu wake, ndikumutentha ndi moto. Chifukwa Mulungu wayika m'mitima yawo kukwaniritsa chifuniro chake, ndi kuvomerezana, ndi kupereka ufumu wawo kwa chirombo, mpaka mawu a Mulungu adzakwaniritsidwa. Ndipo mkazi, amene unamuwona, ndi mzinda waukulu umenewo womwe umalamulira mafumu a dziko lapansi.

Imvani mawu anga, chiweruzo cha Babulo Wachinsinsi chikuchitika kale. Nyanga khumi zakonzekera kuukira kwawo kwadzidzidzi ndipo kuukira kwawo kukuyandikira ndipo kuli pakhomo panu. Chifukwa ngakhale ofalitsa nkhani anu akufuna kukutonthozani kuti mugone ndipo ambiri amakhala mu mdima osawona zenizeni akunena kuti: Ndimakhala ngati Mfumukazi ndipo sindine wamasiye ndipo sindidzawona kulira. Mawa kudzakhala ngati lero, chifukwa lero kuli ngati dzulo. Monga momwe amadzilemekezera yekha, ndikukhala mu chisangalalo, momwemonso mumpatse kuzunzika ndi chisoni: Choncho miliri yake idzabwera tsiku limodzi, imfa, ndi chisoni, ndi njala, ndipo adzatenthedwa ndi moto: chifukwa Yahweh amene amamutsutsa, ndi Mulungu Wamphamvuyonse ndipo palibe wamkulu ndipo palibe chomwe chingaletse dzanja lamphamvu la El Shaddai!

Ndipo dzuwa posachedwa lidzatuluka pa tsiku la tsoka lomwe simukuliyembekezera, ndipo tsiku limenelo lili pafupi kuposa momwe mukuganizira. Ndipo tsiku limenelo lidzabweretsa chiwonongeko chotheratu pa Babulo Wachinsinsi, chifukwa ali ndi gawo lowirikiza la chiweruzo cholengezedwa pa iye. Milandu yake ndi dama lake ndi zambiri ndipo chilango chake sichichedwa ndipo chilango chachikulu chidzagwa pa osalapa ngakhale pamene Mpingo wanga wokhulupirika ndi ngakhale Mkwatibwi wanga wokhulupirika adzasonkhanitsidwa kwa ine modzidzimutsa m'mitambo ndikupulumutsidwa ku mkwiyo, ngakhale vuto lalikulu la sabata la makumi asanu ndi awiri la Danieli. Babulo Wamkulu Wagwa, Wagwa! Imvani mawu anga chifukwa ndimalankhula ngati kuti zachitika kale chifukwa palibe chomwe chidzasinthe, kuchedwetsa, kulepheretsa, kapena kuletsa ziweruzo za Yahweh kugwa pa dziko lapansi.

Mu ola limodzi adzatenthedwa ndi moto, mizinda yake ina idzayaka, mizinda yake ina idzasefukira mwamphamvu, nsanja zake zidzagwa ndikusweka m'zidutswa zambiri, milatho yake idzagwa, ndipo kugwedezeka kwambiri kudzachitika. Dziko lapansi lidzang'ambika mwamphamvu ndipo malo ambiri adzaphimbidwa ndi madzi amphamvu. Madiresi ake adzaphimbidwa kuchokera kum'mawa ndi kumadzulo ndi mafunde akulu ndipo pakati mafunde othamanga ndi kugwedezeka kwamphamvu. Zomwe zatsala zidzakhala chipululu ndipo sadzaukanso, chifukwa mtundu uwu wa maboma ambiri ndi mizinda yambiri sudzaukanso ndipo kugwa kwake kudzakhala kwakukulu! Chifukwa dziko lapansi ndi la Ambuye ndi kudzaza kwake, ndipo mbali zonse za chikoka chake choyipa zidzafafanizidwa mpaka kalekale kuchokera ku mitundu pa nthawi ya sabata la makumi asanu ndi awiri la Danieli, chifukwa motero ndidzayeretsa dziko lapansi mu mkwiyo wanga. Ine, Khristu Yesu, Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda, ndalengeza mawu awa, ndipo kukonzekera kukwaniritsidwa kwawo kukuchitika kale ndipo kudzadzakwaniritsidwa modzidzimutsa mu ola limodzi.

Ndipo choncho nkhondo idzaphulika kudutsa dziko lapansi monga mbali ya kuukira kwadzidzidzi pa Babulo Wachinsinsi, chifukwa izi zakonzedwa ndi kugwirizanitsidwa kwa zaka za yubile zambiri tsopano, chifukwa chinsinsi cha kusayeruzika chakhala chikugwira ntchito kuyambira masiku akale ndipo chidzafika pachimake chathunthu pamene woletsa achotsedwa pamene okhulupirika anga atengedwa kupita kumwamba. Nkhondo idzagwa kudutsa dziko lapansi ndipo idzabwera pa anthu onse ndi mitundu yonse, chifukwa iyi ndi nkhondo yachitatu yotere, pamene ogwa akufuna kuwononga madongosolo akale a dziko lapansi ndikusintha zinthu zonse ndi dongosolo lawo latsopano, ngakhale dongosolo la Chinjoka. Chirombo chochokera kunyanja ndi chirombo chochokera pamtunda zidzauka mu ukali waukulu, ndipo zidzapanga nkhondo ndi oyera mtima, chifukwa zapatsidwa kwa iwo kugonjetsa oyera mtima. Choncho zidzakhala kuti onse omwe sakonda miyoyo yawo ndikuyitana pa dzina la Ambuye mpaka mapeto adzapulumutsidwa mu chisangalalo chamuyaya mpaka muyaya wamuyaya. Ndipo Ine Yesu wa ku Nazarete ndasiya chitsanzo chamuyaya cha kumvera mpaka imfa, chifukwa kumvera kwanga kwa Yahweh kunali ngakhale imfa ya pamtanda pamene ndinazunzika ndikulipira machimo a anthu ngati mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu ndipo taonani ndikubwera ngati Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda ndipo sindidzaletsedwa kapena kuchepetsedwa!

Ndipo choncho aneneri anga awiri omwe ndi mitengo ya azitona iwiri ndi zoyikapo nyale ziwiri omwe amayima pamaso pa Mulungu wa dziko lapansi adzalosera kwa miyezi 42 atavala ziguduli, chifukwa anatengedwa kupita kumwamba m'masiku akale. Ndipo ngati munthu aliyense adzawapweteka, moto umatuluka m'kamwa mwawo, ndikuwononga adani awo: ndipo ngati munthu aliyense adzawapweteka, ayenera kuphedwa m'njira iyi. Awa ali ndi mphamvu yotseka kumwamba, kuletsa mvula m'masiku a maulosi awo: ndipo ali ndi mphamvu pa madzi kuwasandutsa magazi, ndi kumenya dziko lapansi ndi miliri yonse, nthawi zambiri monga momwe angafunire.

Umboni wawo ndi wosatha, ndipo adzalengeza uthenga wabwino wosatha, ndipo adzalankhula za zolinga za Yahweh za nthawi zonse, ndipo adzandilankhulira Ine Khristu Yesu, ndipo adzaulula zinsinsi zambiri ndipo adzatembenuzira mitima ya ambiri kwa Atate wa Zounikira amene mulibe kusinthasintha kapena mthunzi wotembenuka. Adzandichitira umboni Ine Yeshua HaMashiach pakuti ndine amene Yahweh wandituma kwa Isiraeli kudzapereka malonjezo opangidwa kwa Atate awo ndipo ndine Mesiya weniweni tsopano ndi mpaka muyaya ndipo ufumu wanga ulibe mapeto! Pakuti ndi nthawi ziti zomwe zinakhalapo pamene Yahweh adadzisiya wopanda umboni? SIPANAKHALEPO nthawi imene Yahweh adadzisiya wopanda umboni, ndipo onse amene adaikidwiratu ku chipulumutso chosatha adzabwera kudzapulumutsidwa mu umuyaya wosatha ndi chimwemwe chosatha, pakuti kukwaniritsidwa kwa zinthu zonse kwayamba kale.

Ndipo chotero chirombo chochokera kunyanja chidzatuluka komanso chirombo chochokera pansi, chomwe chilinso mneneri wonyenga. Ndipo chidzatsegula pakamwa pake kulankhula zamwano zazikulu zotsutsana ndi Yahweh kuchitira mwano dzina lake ndi chihema chake ndi iwo amene akukhala kumwamba. Ndipo chidzapatsidwa mphamvu yochita nkhondo ndi oyera mtima ndi kuwagonjetsa ndipo mphamvu idzapatsidwa kwa icho kulamulira fuko lililonse, ndi lilime, ndi mtundu pakuti chidzatsogolera dziko lapansi. Chirombo chochokera pansi chidzalamuliranso dziko monga Moshiach wawo wonyenga ndipo chidzakhala womanga kachisi wawo. Chirombo chochokera kunyanja chidzalamulira molingana ndi malamulo awo amwano a Nowa pamene akufunafuna kufafaniza chilichonse chokhudzana ndi Mulungu weniweni ndi mwana wake wobadwa yekha Yesu Khristu pamaso pa dziko lapansi.

Chirombo chochokera pansi chidzachititsa dziko lapansi kulambira chirombo, chomwe bala lake lakupha linachiritsidwa. Ndipo chidzachita zozizwitsa zazikulu kusocheretsa iwo amene akukhala pa dziko lapansi, pakuti chidzaloledwa kuchita zizindikiro zambiri zonama ndi zozizwitsa, ndipo chidzachititsa moto kutsika pa dziko lapansi pamaso pa anthu ndipo chidzatsogolera anthu onse kuchidabwa. Koma iye ndi mdani, ndipo adzikweza yekha kutsutsana ndi chilichonse chotchedwa Mulungu, kapena chomwe chimalambiridwa: kotero kuti amakhala ngati Mulungu mu Kachisi wa Mulungu, kudzisonyeza yekha kuti ndi Mulungu.

Ndipo chidzapanga fano la chirombo ndipo chidzachititsa onse kulambira fano lomwe chidzapanga. Ndipo chidzaloledwa kupereka mzimu ku fano la chirombo kuti fano la chirombocho lilankhule ndipo chidzachititsa onse amene sadzalambira fano la chirombo kuphedwa. Ndipo chidzachititsa onse, olemera ndi osauka, mfulu ndi kapolo, kulandira chizindikiro chake padzanja lawo lamanja kapena pamphumi pawo.

Ndipo chotero ndikunenanso kwa iwo amene akukhala pa dziko lapansi kuti onse amve:

NGATI MUNTHU ALIYENSE ALAMBIRA CHIROMBO NDI FANO LAKE, NDI KULANDIRA CHIZINDIKIRO CHAKE PAMPHUMI PAKE, KAPENA PADZANJA LAKE, YEMWEYO ADZAMWA VINI WA MKWIYO WA MULUNGU, WOTSANULIRIDWA WOSASAKANIZIDWA M'CHIKHO CHA MKWIYO WAKE, NDIPO ADZAZUNZIDWA NDI MOTO NDI SULUFULE, PAMASO PA ANGELO OYERA, NDI PAMASO PA MWANAWANKHOSA: NDIPO UTSI WA KUZUNZIKA KWAWO UKWERA M'MWAMBA KWA NTHAWI ZOSATHA. NDIPO ALIBE MPUMULO USANA NDI USIKU, AMENE ALAMBIRA CHIROMBO NDI FANO LAKE, NDI YENSE AMENE ALANDIRA CHIZINDIKIRO CHA DZINA LAKE.

Dziko lapansi lomwe lilipo tsopano silidzakhalaponso, ndipo ufumu wotuluka wa Chinjoka udzagwetsedwa pansi kotheratu, pamene ufumu wa Satana ukuyandikira mapeto ake omaliza, pakuti iwo ndi olanda malo ndipo si mafumu. Ndipo ili ndi dziko lomwe ambiri adzipeza okha, ngakhale omwe adzapulumuke masoka akubwera ndi nkhondo. Musatenge chizindikiro anthu anga, pakuti adzabwera akupereka mtendere, adzabwera akupereka chitetezo, adzakuuzani kuti zonse ndi za ubwino wanu. Adzayambitsa miliri yawo yotchedwa miliri chifukwa akonza kale kutsimikizira dziko lapansi kuti akudwala. Ndipo mitundu yonse ya miliri ndi matenda ndi zikwapu zidzachuluka pakuti zotero ndi zotsatira za wokwera pahatchi akubwera ndipo adzakwera!

Adzapereka thanzi ndi machiritso ku maweruzo a Mulungu, pamene m'malo mwake munthu ayenera kulapa kuipa kwake. Adzapereka mwayi wolowa m'gulu lawo kuchokera ku maweruzo owopsa omwe atsala pang'ono kugwa pa dziko lapansi. Pakuti Ine, Khristu Yesu, ndidzakuuzani zisanachitike kuti mudziwe kuona mtima kwa mawu anga ndi kuti muthe kuyenda m'madzi owopsa omwe ambiri adzipeza okha, ngakhale omwe ali ndi kulimba mtima kuitana pa dzina langa mpaka mapeto, pakuti iwo ndi olowa m'malo a chipulumutso chosatha. Pakuti onse amene aitanira pa Yesu wa ku Nazarete monga Ambuye wawo, ngakhale onse amene aitanira pa dzina langa lamphamvu ndi kusakonda miyoyo yawo mpaka mapeto, adzapulumutsidwa mu moyo wosatha.

Kodi simunawerenge? Awa ndiwo amene adzatuluka m'chisautso chachikulu, ndipo atsuka mikanjo yawo, ndi kuyiyeretsa m'mwazi wa Mwanawankhosa. Chifukwa chake, ali pamaso pa mpando wachifumu wa Mulungu, ndipo amamtumikira usana ndi usiku mu Kachisi wake: ndipo iye amene akhala pa Mpando wachifumu adzakhala pakati pawo. Sadzamvanso njala, kapena ludzu, kapena dzuwa silidzawagwera, kapena kutentha kulikonse. Pakuti Mwanawankhosa, amene ali pakati pa mpando wachifumu, adzawadyetsa, ndipo adzawatsogolera ku akasupe amadzi amoyo: ndipo Mulungu adzapukuta misozi yonse m'maso mwawo.

Komabe, ngakhale kuti khamu la makamu lidzapulumutsidwa kuchokera ku sabata la makumi asanu ndi awiri la Danieli ndipo chipulumutso chidzafika kwa Isiraeli pakuti otsalira adzandigwadira Ine Yeshua HaMashiach, (Pakuti ndine Mesiya weniweni mwa amene mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu adzapereka malo kwa Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda, ndipo onse awiri adzakwaniritsidwa mu chiyanjano chosatha mwa Ine, pakuti mwa Ine Khristu Yesu Mkango udzagona pansi mwamtendere ndi mwanawankhosa.) mwatsoka, ndi mosafunikira, ambiri a dziko lapansi adzadabwa ndi kutsatira olamulira a chirombo awa, pakuti adzasangalatsa ambiri. Ndi ufiti wawo ndi matsenga awo, adzakopa dziko lapansi kuti lilandire poizoni wawo, ndipo adzapatsa poizoni kwamuyaya omwe amakonda dziko lapansi ndi ufiti wawo, pakuti chizindikiro cha chirombo chidzabisidwa ngati mankhwala. Ngakhale kulumidwa kwa njoka komwe dongosolo lawo lakhazikitsidwa kale ndi kuyesedwa, ndipo makamu a anthu akonzedwa kale kuti alandire chizindikiro chawo ndipo mbewu yawo ya nkhondo inali yopambana.

Ndipo chotero dongosolo lawo lili lokonzeka pa nthawi yoyenera, ndipo akonza zigoli zawo za chikhalidwe ndipo onse adzalamuliridwa omwe akukhala m'mizinda yawo ndi gulu lawo. Pakuti adzayenera kutenga chizindikiro cha chirombo kuti atenge mbali m'gulu lawo la satana, ngakhale ufumu wa Chinjoka. Omwe atenga chizindikirocho adzasinthidwa kwamuyaya. Ma genetics awo enieni adzasinthidwa, pakuti adzisakaniza okha ndi mbewu ya anthu: koma sadzaphatikizana wina ndi mzake, monga chitsulo sichisakanizidwa ndi dongo. Chizindikiro chawo cha chirombo, ngakhale kulumidwa kwa njoka, chidzawononga kotheratu onse amene atenga poizoni wawo ndipo palibe kuthawa.

Ngakhale onse omwe atenga chizindikirocho adzasinthidwa kwamuyaya, ndipo adzipeza okha mbali ya dongosolo lawo, ndipo adzalumikizidwa ndi matekinoloje ndipo malingaliro awo sadzakhalanso awo. Adzalamuliridwa ndipo adzakhala ngati akufa oyenda, adzakhala akapolo kwamuyaya ku malingaliro a mng'oma a dongosolo lawo, ngakhale a matekinoloje awo pakuti adzalumikizidwa pamodzi ndi intaneti ya zinthu zomwe zikubwera. Adzataya chifuniro chawo, ndipo sadzakhala ndi kumverera ndipo chilichonse cha Mulungu chidzafafanizidwa kwamuyaya kwa iwo chifukwa adzisintha okha kukhala chikhalidwe chosawomboledwa, ndipo adziphatikiza okha kotheratu ndi kwamuyaya kwa omwe adagwa ndipo kwamuyaya adzakhala a chinjoka ndipo ku nyanja yaikulu yamoto adzapita mpaka muyaya. Ndipo chotero chidzakhala tsogolo la kusankha kwawo.

Pakuti chigamulo cha kusankha kwawo nchosasinthika. Ndipo miliri yawo ndi zilonda ndi matenda zidzakhala zambiri, ndipo ngakhale adzalonjezedwa thanzi ndi mtendere, thanzi ndi mtendere sadzakhala nazo. Chifukwa Satana akuyesetsa kugwiritsa ntchito ufiti wake kulimbana ndi mkwiyo ndi maweruzo a Mulungu ndi kupatsa poizoni ndi kupha makamu a anthu, koma sadzakhala ndi mpumulo ndipo omwe atenga mbali mu ufiti wake sadzathawa. Omwe atenga chizindikirocho adzawala pansi pa magetsi awo amdima, ndipo magetsi awo adzakhala m'misewu ya mizinda yawo yomwe akonzera akapolo awo. Mvetsetsani kuti awa adzakhala otembereredwa oyenda omwe alibe chikhululuko ndipo omwe sangachiritsidwe, pakuti awa ndi omwe mayina awo sanalembedwe m'Buku la Moyo la Mwanawankhosa, wophedwa kuyambira kukhazikitsidwa kwa dziko lapansi. Ndipo palibe chipulumutso chomwe chidzapezeka pakati pawo kapena m'gulu lawo, pakuti kuyenda pakati pawo ndi kulowa gulu lawo kudzakhala kofunikira kotheratu chizindikiro cha chirombo.

Mboni zanga ziwiri zidzachitira umboni mpaka nthawi za umboni wawo zitakwaniritsidwa pakuti miyezi 42 yapatsidwa. Ndipo chotero chidzapatsidwa kwa chirombo chomwe chikukwera kuchokera ku phompho kuchita nkhondo ndi iwo, ndipo chidzawagonjetsa, ndi kuwapha, ngakhale mitengo yanga ya azitona iwiri, ndi zoyikapo nyali ziwiri pamene amaliza umboni wawo. Ndipo matupi awo akufa adzagona mumsewu wa mzinda waukulu, womwe mwauzimu umatchedwa Sodomu ndi Igupto, komwe Ambuye wanu adapachikidwa. Ndipo oipa adzachita chikondwerero chachikulu ndi kupatsana mphatso wina ndi mzake chifukwa cha imfa zawo. Ndipo pambuyo pa masiku atatu ndi theka Mzimu wa moyo wochokera kwa Mulungu udzalowa mwa iwo: ndipo adzaima pa mapazi awo, ndipo mantha aakulu adzagwera onse omwe adawaona. Ndipo adamva mawu aakulu ochokera kumwamba, akuti kwa iwo, kwerani kuno. Ndipo adakwera kumwamba mu mtambo, ndipo adani awo adzawaona akukwera kumwamba mu moyo wosatha ndipo kwamuyaya adzasinthidwa. Ndipo Mngelo wachisanu ndi chiwiri adzaomba, ndipo kudzakhala mawu aakulu kumwamba, akuti, Maufumu a dziko lapansi lino asanduka maufumu a Ambuye wathu, ndi a Khristu wake, ndipo adzalamulira mpaka muyaya.

(Odala ndi akufa amene amamwalira mwa Ambuye, kuyambira tsopano, inde, atero Mzimu, kuti apumule ku ntchito zawo, ndipo ntchito zawo ziwatsata.) Ndipo chotero chisautso chachikulu kwambiri chidzayamba, ngakhale kuti chisautso chidzakhala chokhazikika mkati mwa sabata la makumi asanu ndi awiri la Danieli. Ndipo kachisi wawo adzamalizidwa ndipo chonyansa cha chiwonongeko chidzakwaniritsidwa ndi Moshiach wawo wonyenga. Ndipo adzatsimikizira pangano ndi ambiri kwa sabata limodzi: ndipo pakati pa sabata adzaletsa nsembe ndi chopereka, ndipo chifukwa cha kufalikira kwa zonyansa, adzachipanga kukhala chabwinja, ngakhale mpaka chimaliziro chotsimikiziridwa chidzatsanuliridwa pa chabwinjacho.

Izi zidzakhala nthawi zamdima kwambiri ana anga, pakuti Ine Yeshua HaMashiach ndikukuuzani zinthu izi zisanachitike, ndipo zalembedwa ngati chenjezo kwa onse. Ndipo chotero 144,000 anga adzabwera kudzathandiza omwe akanawonongeka ndipo omwe aitanira pa dzina langa. Ndidzawatsogolera pang'onopang'ono, ndipo adzandichitira ntchito zambiri zamphamvu. Pakuti awa ndi omwe atengedwa mu kusonkhana kwa oyera mtima kwa Ine, Khristu Yesu, mumlengalenga pa kulira kwa lipenga lomaliza ndipo lipenga latsala pang'ono kulira.

Awa ndi okhulupirika anga, ngakhale omwe mwa iwo simunapezeke chinyengo, pakuti adziyeretsa okha ngati namwali woyera m'mwazi wa Mwanawankhosa womwe ndidapereka kwamuyaya ngati mkulu wawo wansembe, pakuti ndine kwamuyaya mkulu wansembe monga mwa dongosolo la Melikizedeki. Awa ndi mkwatibwi wanga, ndipo adzanditsata kulikonse komwe ndipita. Taonani! Ndidzawapanga kukhala amphamvu, ndipo palibe chomwe chidawonekere pa dziko lapansi chomwe chingafanane ndi maluso omwe ndidzawaveka. Adzavekedwa bafuta wabwino, woyera, wopanda banga ndipo maluso awo adzaimira zomwe akwaniritsa ndipo adzakhala ndi maluso aakulu pakuti ndi okhulupirika oitanidwa ndi osankhidwa ngakhale kusanakhazikitsidwe maziko a dziko lapansi ndi Yahweh chifukwa cha zolinga izi.

144,000 anga adzatsikira pa Phiri la Ziyoni ndipo ndidzawatsogolera. Adzatenga omwe ndiwatsogolera kwa iwo kupita kuchipululu kuti akabisidwe ku mdima waukulu ndi chizunzo cha ufumu wa adani. Adzatsogoleredwa ku chitetezo kuchokera ku miliri isanu ndi iwiri pakuti m'maweruzo awa muli kuuma kwa mkwiyo wa Mulungu woti utsanuliridwe pa mpando wa chirombo ndi kuzunza ufumu wa chinjoka pakuti chilango chawo ndi chachikulu. Ndipo 144,000 adzawatsogolera ku chitetezo, adzawachiritsa ku mabala awo, adzawathandiza ndi kuwapatsa chiyembekezo chachikulu choitanira pa dzina la Ambuye ngakhale mpaka mapeto ngati kuli kofunikira. Pakuti ndikadapanda kufupikitsa masikuwo, palibe thupi lomwe likadasiyidwa lamoyo. Ndipo chotero adzamaliza zomwe ndidzawatsogolera.

Ndipo pamene nthawi zakwaniritsidwa chotero zidzakhala, monga momwe kwalembedwera ndipo monga kwalembedwa chotero chichitidwe: Ndipo ndidaona kumwamba kutseguka, ndipo taonani hatchi yoyera, ndipo iye amene adakwerapo adatchedwa wokhulupirika ndi woona, ndipo m'chilungamo amaweruza ndi kuchita nkhondo. Maso ake anali ngati lawi lamoto, ndipo pamutu pake panali akorona ambiri, ndipo anali ndi dzina lolembedwa, lomwe palibe munthu adalidziwa koma iye yekha. Ndipo adavekedwa chovala choviikidwa m'mwazi, ndipo dzina lake limatchedwa, Mawu a Mulungu. Ndipo magulu ankhondo omwe anali kumwamba adamutsata pa mahatchi oyera, ovekedwa bafuta wabwino, woyera ndi woyera. Ndipo m'kamwa mwake mutuluka lupanga lakuthwa, kuti nalo akanthe mitundu ya anthu: ndipo adzawalamulira ndi ndodo yachitsulo: ndipo aponda mopondera mphesa mwa ukali ndi mkwiyo wa Mulungu Wamphamvuyonse. Ndipo ali nalo pa chovala chake, ndi pa ntchafu yake dzina lolembedwa, Mfumu Ya Mafumu, Ndi Ambuye Wa Ambuye.

Ndipo chotero Ine, ngakhale Yesu wa ku Nazarete, ngakhale Mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu, ngakhale Khristu Yesu yemwenso ndi Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda ndidzabwera kudzaweruza ndi kuchita nkhondo pa Chinjoka ndipo ufumu wake wonse udzaphwanyidwa kotheratu kuti usadzaukenso. Ndipo chirombo ndi mneneri wonyenga adzaponyedwa amoyo m'nyanja yamoto ndipo Satana adzamangidwa ndi unyolo waukulu m'phompho kwa zaka chikwi chimodzi ndipo chotero ulamuliro wanga wa zaka chikwi udzayamba ndipo ulamuliro wanga udzakhala m'chilungamo. Taonani! Ndikubweretsa mkwatibwi wanga ndi gulu la mafumu ndi ansembe omwe ndidzalamulira nawo dziko lapansi m'chilungamo ndipo ndidzalamulira mitundu ndi ndodo yachitsulo. Chotero khalani ndi chiyembekezo chachikulu ana anga pakuti kukwaniritsidwa kwa zinthu zonse kuli pafupi ndipo nthawi iyi ya zaka zisanu ndi ziwiri ya mavuto aakulu, ngakhale mavuto a Yakobo idzatsatiridwa ndi ulamuliro wanga wolungama ndipo Yahweh adzakhala zonse mu zonse.

Ndipo ndidaona kumwamba kwatsopano, ndi dziko lapansi latsopano: pakuti kumwamba koyamba, ndi dziko lapansi loyamba zidapita, ndipo panalibe nyanja.
