Mboni Ziwiri: Israyeli
Zowawa Zotsekemera
Novembala 25, 2024

Ndipo Panabwera Kangaude

Imvani, imvani, Mawu anga O Israyeli ndipo weramitsani mitima yanu pomvera mawu anga ndikulandira mofatsa Mawu a Yeshua HaMashiach kuti mwa Mawu a Uthenga Wabwino Wosatha mupeze chipulumutso ndikufika ku moyo wosatha mwa Mesiya wanu weniweni amene amakumenyerani nkhondo m'chikondi. Chotero ndabwera kudzachenjeza inu, ngakhale a Yakobo ndi onse amene amawerenga ndi kulandira mawu a Uthenga Wabwino Wosatha m'chikhulupiriro chopulumutsa kuti akhale ndi chikhulupiriro mwa Yeshua HaMashiach amene alinso Yesu Khristu, ndi kuti akhale ndi chikhulupiriro mwa Yahweh WAMKULU NDINE amene mu mtima mwake Uthenga Wabwino Wosatha unabisika kufikira nthawi ya kuvumbulutsidwa kwake ndipo chotero Yohane Wovumbulutsa anatseka mawu a mabingu asanu ndi awiri amene mawu awo anamva. Ndipo chotero ndabwera kudzachenjeza inu za kangaude amene waluka ukonde wake ndipo wagwira dziko lapansi m'kukumbatira kwake; ngakhale pamene akudya magazi a ena, ndi kuti muthe kupewa maukonde ake ambiri kuti musagwidwe m'kukumbatira kwake kowopsa.

Chifukwa chake, weramitsani mitima yanu pomvera ndikumva mawu a Mesiya wanu weniweni, ngakhale Yeshua HaMashiach! Pakuti zoonadi ndabwera kudzapanga munthu wanzeru pakati panu kukhala wanzeru kwambiri kuti muthe kuzindikira nthawi yomwe mukukhala ndikuti mumvetsetse adani anu ndi chikhalidwe chawo ndi njira zawo zonama; chifukwa amayenda mwachinsinsi chachikulu ndi mochenjera ndipo mwachinyengo amachita nkhondo. Chifukwa onse ndi onyenga omwe amanamizira, ndipo kangaude amayenda mwachinsinsi chachikulu ndi mochenjera kufikira nyama ya kangaude itagwidwa mu ukonde wake womwe kangaude nthawi zonse adzagwiritsa ntchito pazolinga zake zoyipa.

Chifukwa chake, ndabwera kudzachenjeza inu za kangaude ndikukuwonetsani njira zake kuti muthe kupewa poizoni wake ndikudzipulumutsa ku kukumbatira kwake kofooketsa ndi kowopsa. Koma ndi kangaude uti amene anadzipangira yekha ufumu ndi mtundu? Ndipo ndi kangaude uti amene analowa m'mgwirizano wa omwe adagwa, ngakhale kuchita gawo lake mosangalala mu zoyipa zopanduka? Ndipo ndi kangaude uti amene anatenga mayina a omwe agwidwa mu ukonde wake? Komabe ndi momwemo kangaude amene ndabwera kudzachenjeza inu ndipo zoonadi, zoonadi, kangaude ndi chimodzi mwa zigawo zambiri za ufumu wa adani ndipo ndi kangaude wapoizoni ndi wowopsa zoonadi.

Chifukwa kangaude amaluka ukonde wake mochenjera ndi mwachinyengo. Ngakhale ukonde wachinyengo ndi ukonde wa ngongole kangaude amaluka, ndipo ukonde wake tsopano ukukulunga dziko lonse lapansi m'kukumbatira kwake kochenjera. Taganizirani za kangaude m'malo ake achilengedwe, amaluka ukonde wake mochenjera m'malo momwe angagwire nyama yake ngakhale pamene nyama yake igwa mosazindikira mu ukonde wake. Ndipo pamene wogwidwayo akumenyana kuti adzimasule yekha ku ukonde womwe anakodwa mosazindikira kudzikola yekha kwambiri, kangaude amayamba kugwira ntchito ndikubaya poizoni wake pamene akukulunga nyama yake mu ukonde wake. Kenako kangaude amasunga nyama yake pazolinga zake ndikudya pang'onopang'ono wogwidwayo kufikira palibe chomwe chatsala, kusiya wogwidwayo ali wopanda kanthu kufikira zoonadi wogwidwayo palibe.

Komabe ndi momwemo njira za kangaude wachilengedwe wolengedwa pazolinga za Yahweh, kupatula ndani wa inu amene anawonapo kangaude akugwira ntchito limodzi ndi ufumu ndi mtundu wa akangaude ena; komabe omwe adagwa agwirizanitsa akangaude awo m'masiku otsiriza ano. Ndipo chotero ndabwera kudzachenjeza inu za anthu ochokera kwa Yafeti, amene anabala Gomeri, ndipo kuchokera kwa Gomeri kunabwera Asikenazi, ndipo kwa Asikenazi mtundu chifukwa Asikenazi ali m'gome la mitundu m'madongosolo akale ngakhale m'masiku omwe anthu ankakhala pansi pa pangano la Nowa lomwe pangano anthu analisiya; ndipo komabe panali Abulamu wokhulupirika amene anafunafuna njira za Yahweh pamene dziko lapansi linapandukiranso mlengi wawo. Komabe ngakhale, munthu wachilengedwe anakana pangano la Yahweh; Yahweh ndi wokhulupirika ku malonjezo ake chimodzimodzi. Ndipo palibe lonjezo limodzi la pangano lomwe Yahweh anapanga ndi Nowa (amene anali munthu wolungama pamaso pa Yahweh) lomwe linagwera pansi losakwaniritsidwa ndipo onse adzakhala inde ndi ameni mpaka kalekale chifukwa Yahweh nthawi zonse ndi wokhulupirika mwangwiro!

Pambuyo poti dziko lapansi linasefukira m'masiku a Nowa, dziko lapansi linagawidwa mwa maere a cholowa pakati pa ana a Nowa m'masiku a Pelegi, ndipo chotero Yafeti analowa m'cholowa chake m'maiko a kumpoto. Ndipo anthu anali a chilankhulo chimodzi, ndipo ana a Hamu anasonkhana m'dziko lomwe linakhala Babulo. Ndipo akalonga a Nimrodi anapangana pamodzi ndi Futi, Miziraimu, Kushi, ndi Kanani ndi mabanja awo kuti amange mzinda wawo ndipo mmenemo nsanja yolimba ngakhale nsanja yayitali kuti athe kuchita nkhondo kumwamba ndikuti adzisonkhanitse okha kuti adzipangire dzina m'kupandukira lamulo la Yahweh lakuti mubalane ndi kuchuluka kuti athe kudzazanso dziko lapansi. Koma ndi momwemo njira za munthu wachilengedwe wopanda chikhulupiriro, amene m'zilakolako zake ndi kunyada amatumikira tchimo ndipo chotero amatumikira Atate wa tchimo; ngakhale mdierekezi, kudzera m'kusamvera kwa anthu kwa Yahweh chifukwa cha tchimo m'thupi la munthu wachilengedwe.

Ndipo kuchokera ku mbadwa za Nimrodi amene anakula m'kuyipa kumanenedwa kuti kuchokera kwa woyipa kumatuluka kuyipa kwambiri. Ndipo chotero Nimrodi anaipitsa dziko lapansi m'njira za omwe adagwa ndipo kuchokera kwa amatsenga ake ndi afiti kunabwera masukulu achinsinsi akale komwe amatsenga a omwe adagwa anaphunzitsa zinsinsi zawo zopanda pake kwa iwo omwe adzaponyedwa kumoto mpaka kalekale m'machimo a Satana, ndipo adzaphatikizana nawo m'cholowa chawo chosatha. Ndipo Nimrodi anauka mu ufumu woyamba pambuyo pa chigumula cha Nowa, ngakhale pamene Nimrodi analamulira dziko lapansi m'chinyengo; ngakhale ulamuliro womwe omwe adagwa amafunafuna kukhala nawo okha kwathunthu pa dziko lapansi, komabe palibe m'badwo wagolide womwe adzakhale nawo koma chiweruzo chowopsa.

Chotero Yahweh m'chiweruzo anapangitsa dziko lapansi kutseguka ndikumeza gawo limodzi mwa magawo atatu a nsanja yawo, ndipo moto unagwa kuchokera kumwamba ndikunyeketsa gawo lina limodzi mwa magawo atatu a nsanja, ndipo gawo limodzi mwa magawo atatu linaloledwa kukhalabe ngati chikumbutso chotsutsana nawo. Chifukwa chake, dzina lake limatchedwa Babele, chifukwa Yahweh kumeneko anasokoneza chilankhulo cha dziko lonse lapansi: ndipo kuchokera kumeneko Yahweh anawamwaza ponseponse pa nkhope ya dziko lonse lapansi. Ndipo mitundu yambiri ndi anthu anakana ndikuyiwala Yahweh amene analowa m'pangano ndi Nowa, ndipo chotero anayiwala pangano lomwe Nowa anapanga ndi Yahweh molingana ndi cholowa chawo ndipo molingana ndi zilankhulo zomwe ankayankhula anayiwala pangano la Yahweh ndi Nowa, ndipo anatembenukira ku njira za omwe adagwa chifukwa palibe chisankho china. (Ndipo komabe Abulamu anakhala Abrahamu ndipo wolungama adzakhala ndi moyo mwa chikhulupiriro!)

Ku Babulo Nimrodi anachita zoyipa zambiri ngakhale pamene anapanga mafano ambiri ndikudzitcha yekha mulungu ndikupanga utatu wake wamwano, ngakhale atatu oyipa, opangidwa kuti anyoze umulungu weniweni ndi mbewu yolonjezedwa ya mkazi ndikusocheretsa ana a anthu m'chinyengo. Ndipo chotero Nimrodi anakhazikitsa masukulu ake otchedwa achinsinsi pamene chidziwitso cha omwe adagwa chinayambanso kufalikira ndikuphimba dziko lapansi ndipo anthu anasinthanso choonadi cha Yahweh kukhala bodza ndikupembedza ndi kutumikira cholengedwa kuposa mlengi amene ali wodala mpaka kalekale, Ameni!

Ndipo pamene anthu anamwazikana kuchokera ku Babulo momwemonso chidziwitso chabodza ndi njira za omwe adagwa zinamwazikana ponseponse ndi iwo, ngakhale ndi miyambo yawo yonse yonyansa ndi yamwano ya iwo omwe amadya nyama ndi magazi ndi iwo omwe amamwa magazi a Adamu komwe moyo wa thupi umakhala; ngakhale machitidwe oyipa kwambiri ndi onyansa omwe Yahweh waletsa kamodzi, konse, ndi mpaka kalekale chifukwa Adamu analengedwa m'chifanizo cha Yahweh ndipo moyo wa thupi uli m'magazi. Komabe akupitiriza m'njira zawo zonyansa ndi zamwano mpaka lero, ndipo machitidwe awo ndi miyambo yawo yasungidwa mwa iwo omwe amapembedza ziwanda ndikugwadira mafano osalankhula ndipo izi ndi njira za kangaude, ndipo komabe kangaude amanamizira kukhala Yakobo wolungama amene alibe gawo kapena maere mwa iye.

Ndipo chotero njira zoyipa zinakhazikika m'njira za anthu a mitundu inanso. Ndipo kwa Gomeri kunabwera Asikenazi ndipo kwa Asikenazi m'kupita kwanthawi kunabwera mtundu waukulu wa anthu omwe anakhala oyendayenda omwe ankakhala ndikuyendayenda m'chigwa cha Eurasia ndi madera oyandikana nawo ngakhale komwe anapanga ufumu wawo. Ngakhale za izo, ufumu wawo unakula m'chinyengo ndikugwera kwa adani awo chimodzimodzi. Ngakhale omwe adani awo anawaukira chifukwa cha chinyengo chawo ndipo m'malo molandira Yesu wa ku Nazarete ndikuchoka ku zoyipa iwo m'malo mwake amanamizira kukhala Yakobo, ndipo kwa Yakobo sikunakhale fuko la Asikenazi la Yafeti. Ngakhale zili choncho anakana Yesu wa ku Nazarete chifukwa ndi a atate wawo mdierekezi ndipo zilakolako zake amamangidwa mpaka kalekale kuti azichite.

Ndipo kwa anthu a Asikenazi omwe njira zawo zinali zoyipa ndi zachinyengo kunabwera anthu, kupatula a mtundu wa Adamu sanali kwathunthu, koma namsongole woyipa wobzalidwa pakati pa tirigu wa Yahweh omwe adziipitsa okha mpaka kalekale m'chinyengo. Ngakhale iwo omwe anabadwa kwa atate wawo mdierekezi chifukwa mbewu ya mdierekezi ikadalipo, ngakhale ndidzawazula kwathunthu ndikuwaponyera kung'anjo monga momwe Yahweh wanenera. Ndipo kwa Asikenazi kunabwera kangaude koma kangaude wachilengedwe sanali, koma mtundu wa akangaude onyansa ngakhale osinthidwa kuchokera ku chifanizo cha Yahweh mlengi wawo, omwe amamangidwa mpaka kalekale ku zilakolako za Atate wawo mdierekezi omwe zilakolako zake adzachita. Ndipo chotero akangaude anamwazikana ponseponse, ngakhale pamene anathawa m'manja mwa adani a chinyengo omwe anayima motsutsana ndi njira za kangaude. Ndipo chotero Asikenazi anakana Yeshua HaMashiach, amene alibe gawo kapena maere mwa iye.

Chotero m'choonadi anthu asocheretsedwa ndi omwe adagwa kuyambira pamene Adamu anagwa kuchokera ku mkhalidwe wake wopanda tchimo womwe analengedwa m'munda wa Edeni ndipo chivundi chinafalikira pa dziko lapansi kudzera m'mzera wamagazi wa Kaini. Chivundi chomwe alonda omwe adagwa anachiipitsa kwambiri pamene anatsikira pa Phiri la Herimoni m'masiku a Yaredi. Chifukwa chake, Yahweh anatsuka chivundi chawo kuchokera pa nkhope ya dziko lapansi m'chigumula chachikulu chimenecho m'masiku a Nowa ndipo komabe omwe adagwa anakhazikitsanso njira zawo pambuyo pa chigumula chifukwa chotero Yahweh analola anthu kudziyenera okha. Chifukwa zoonadi, zoonadi, Yahweh wapatsa onse omwe anawalenga ndi ufulu wosankha m'chilengedwe chake ufulu wosankha ngati adzakhala m'njira za Yahweh kapena kusankha kutumikira Satana m'kupanduka kwawo komwe kupanduka kuli pafupi kutha. Chifukwa Ine, ngakhale Yeshua HaMashiach, ndine amene ndidzaphwanya mutu wa njoka, ngakhale iye amene adzawononga kwathunthu ufumu wake kuti usadzaukenso.

Chotero zili choncho ndipo chotero ndi choonadi chomwe ndinaphunzitsa chokhudza ufumu wakumwamba pamene ndinabwera kwa Israyeli ngati Mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu pa nthawi yanga m'thupi ku Yudeya. Pakuti chotero, kwalembedwa: Ufumu wakumwamba uli ngati munthu amene anabzala mbewu yabwino m'munda mwake. Koma pamene anthu anali m'tulo, mdani wake anabwera, ndikubzala namsongole pakati pa tirigu, ndikupita njira yake. Ndipo pamene tsamba linamera, ndikubala chipatso, pamenepo namsongole anawonekeranso. Kenako antchito a mwini nyumba anabwera, ndikumuuza kuti, Mbuye, kodi simunabzale mbewu yabwino m'munda mwanu? Nanga namsongole wachokera kuti? Ndipo anawauza kuti, Munthu wansanje wachita izi. Kenako antchito anati kwa iye, Kodi mukufuna kuti tipite tikawasonkhanitse? Koma anati, Ayi, kuwopa kuti pamene mupita kukasonkhanitsa namsongole, muzulenso tirigu pamodzi nawo. Lolani zonse zikule pamodzi mpaka nthawi yokolola, ndipo pa nthawi yokolola ndidzauza okolola kuti, Sonkhanitsani kaye namsongole, ndikumumanga m'mitolo kuti amutenthe: koma sonkhanitsani tirigu m'nkhokwe yanga.

Ngakhale zili choncho chifukwa tirigu amawerama pomvera pamene namsongole amayima chilili m'kunyoza koyipa. Sela!

Komabe zili choncho kuti nthawi yosonkhanitsa yafika, ndipo ndidzasonkhanitsa tirigu wanga m'nkhokwe ya Yahweh chifukwa nthawi yokolola zipatso zoyambirira yafika. Ndipo chidzachitika kuti ndidzazula namsongole yense ndi kumumanga, ndipo adzaponyedwa kumoto m'chiweruzo chawo. Choncho mvetsetsani dziko lapansi lomwe mukukhalamo ngakhale nkhondo yomwe munabadwiramo. Ngakhale nkhondo ya mbewu yomwe yakhala ikuchitika kuyambira m'munda wa Edene pamene Yahweh adalengeza nkhondo pa njoka ndi omwe adagwa chifukwa cha kuipa kwawo komanso chifukwa choipitsa munda wa Yahweh. Nkhondo yomwe imathera pakugonjetsedwa kotheratu kwa onse omwe apanduka m'njira yotereyi pamodzi ndi omwe adagwa ndipo cholowa chawo cha sulufule woyaka moto chikuwayembekezera.

Ndipo Yahweh adalengeza kuti: Ndidzaika chidani pakati pa iwe ndi mkazi ndi pakati pa mbewu yako ndi mbewu yake, ndipo iwe udzaluma chidendene chake ndipo iye adzaphwanya mutu wako. Ndipo chidendene changa chidalumidwa pakupachikidwa kwanga pamene ndidadzipereka ndekha ngati Paskha wangwiro monga Mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu mofunitsitsa m'manja mwa adani anga ankhanza. Ndipo Ine ndine mbewu yolonjezedwa ya mkazi yomwe idzaphwanya kotheratu mutu wa Satana ndipo ndidzasandutsa adani anga kukhala chopondapo mapazi anga, chifukwa Ine ndine Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda ndipo m'chilungamo ndikubwera kudzaweruza ndi kuchita nkhondo!

Choncho, mvetsetsani kuti nkhondo ya mbewu yapambanidwa kwathunthu mwa Khristu Yesu monga momwe ndidapangitsa Petulo kulemba zaka za yubile zambiri zapitazo: Kubadwanso mwatsopano, osati ndi mbewu yowonongeka, koma yosawonongeka, mwa mawu a Mulungu omwe ali amoyo ndi okhala kosatha. Ndipo kachiwiri ndidapangitsa Yohane Wovumbulutsa kulemba m'kalata yake: Yense wobadwa wa Mulungu, sachita tchimo: chifukwa mbewu yake imakhala mwa iye, ndipo sangachimwe, chifukwa wabadwa wa Mulungu. Mwa ichi ana a Mulungu awonekera, ndi ana a mdierekezi: yense wosachita chilungamo, sali wa Mulungu, ngakhale iye wosakonda m'bale wake. Choncho, onse omwe asindikizidwa mwa Khristu sangawonongeke m'njira yotereyi ndipo sangachimwe pamene asinthidwa kuchoka ku chisindikizo cha Mulungu wamoyo, ngakhale Yahweh yemwe ali ake. Chifukwa chake, musalole kupusa kotereku kukuvutitseni kuti omwe abadwanso mwatsopano ndi mzimu wanga woyera wa umwana ndi kusindikizidwa ndi chisindikizo cha Yahweh angathe kulandidwa m'dzanja langa lamphamvu m'njira iliyonse. Choncho, kwa omwe apeza chipulumutso chipulumutso chanu ndi chotetezedwa.

Ndipo njira za kangaude ndidzaziwulula, ndipo ndidzawulula kangaude kwathunthu, ngakhale akangaude amakonda kuyenda mwachinsinsi chachikulu, ndidzawulula njira zawo. Chifukwa akangaude osinthika awa ndi omwe amatenga maonekedwe a omwe akuwakonzera chiwembu ndi kuwapha. Awa ndi akangaude omwe amakula mu nkhanza yayikulu ndipo amakonda kuzunza nyama zawo popanda chifukwa china koma kukwaniritsa zilakolako za Atate wawo omwe amangidwa kuti awatumikire ndipo ndi mbadwa zawo. Akangaude osinthika awa omwe ndikunena za iwo ndi achifwamba, ndi mbala, ndi opha anthu ndi onyenga (ndipo ali ndi mlandu waukulu kwambiri) omwe sakhutira ndipo amalakalaka imfa ya Adamu kuti akhale ndi nthawi yawo yagolide kosatha pamodzi ndi omwe adagwa. Ndipo akangaude afalikira padziko lonse lapansi ndikuluka maukonde awo pamodzi mwachinsinsi chachikulu.

Ngakhale ukonde waukulu wa ngongole chifukwa kangaude amakula ndipo wadzipangitsa kukhala wolemera ndi golide yemwe wamupeza kudzera mu chiwongoladzanja chosalungama. Kangaude wadzibisa pakati pa ndikudziyesa ngati Yakobo ndipo adzipangira chinyengo chokhutiritsa. Chifukwa kangaude sangathe kudziwonetsa yekha ngati kangaude woipa, wosinthika; pakuti ndi msodzi uti amatchera msampha pamaso pa mbalame? Koma kangaude amadzibisa ndikusintha maonekedwe ake ndi makhalidwe ake kuti agwirizane ndi omwe akufuna kuwadyetsa. Ndipo pamene kangaude akuyamba kuluka ukonde wake pakati pa omwe mosazindikira alola kangaude malo oti aluke pakati pa mtundu wawo; kangaude ndiye amabweretsa akangaude ambiri omwe amabwera kudzakhala naye m'kugonjetsa kwake koipa kapena kudzera mwa namsongole wambiri woswa m'thumba la mazira a kangaude omwe amathamanga kukaluka maukonde awoawo ndikupitiriza kuzungulira kwa imfa. Ndipo chomwecho Adamu amamangidwa ndi kukulungidwa ndi tizilombo toipa ti. Ndipo pang'onopang'ono ndi chisangalalo chachikulu amakola ndi kudya Adamu ndipo ndi nkhanza yayikulu ndi chidani kangaude amafunafuna dziko lonse lapansi kwa iye mwini ndi Atate awo omwe zilakolako zawo amangidwa nazo.

Chifukwa m'chinyengo chake akangaude a Ashkenaz adziphatikiza okha kwa Esau, kudzera mu Talmud chifukwa m'zaka za yubile zambiri zapitazo adatenga Talmud ngati mbuye wawo ndi wolamulira wawo, komabe wotembenukira weniweni wa Mose sali, ngakhale kutsatira njira ya Yeshua HaMashiach koma amangonyenga. Ndipo a Esau anali opha anga ndipo ndi nzeru zabodza za Esau, ngakhale miyambo yawo yonyoza ya anthu yomwe amayikweza pamwamba pa njira za Yahweh, aipitsa Yakobo ndikukola m'bale wa Esau nthawi zambiri. Ngakhale zili choncho ndidawauza nthawi yanga m'thupi ngati Mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu: Inu ndinu a atate wanu mdierekezi, ndipo zilakolako za atate wanu mudzachita: iye wakhala wopha anthu kuyambira pachiyambi, ndipo sakhala m'choonadi, chifukwa mulibe choonadi mwa iye. Akalankhula bodza, amalankhula zake: chifukwa iye ndi wabodza, ndi atate wake wa bodza.

Komabe kangaude sanachokepo m'njira za omwe adagwa ndipo kangaude amapitirizabe kutenga mbali ndi kusangalala ndi machitidwe ake oipa. Chifukwa kangaude amagwira ana omwe wawakulunga ngati nyama yake ndikumwa magazi awo kuchokera kwa ang'ono omwe amawazunza m'manja mwake. Chifukwa kangaude amakwaniritsa zilakolako zake zonyansa pa ang'ono omwe amawaba mwachinsinsi chachikulu kuchokera ku mtundu womwe akuwalandira pamene ang'ono akutengedwa mu ukonde wake. Ndipo ndi kangaude uti yemwe wakhalapo yemwe amaluka ukonde komanso amakumba ngalande pansi pa dziko lapansi. Chifukwa ukonde wa kangaude umapitanso pansi pa dziko lapansi ndipo ukonde wake umaphimba malo aakulu ndipo ufumu ndi ufumu wapadziko lonse lapansi kangaude amafunafuna. Ndipo chomwecho kangaude amayenda m'chinyengo chachikulu chifukwa mwa chinyengo amachita nkhondo.

Ndipo komabe onyenga awa omwe adziphatikiza okha kwa omwe adagwa kosatha ndipo omwe ali mbadwa zawo zenizeni; amadziyesa ngati Yakobo ndipo anena monama kuti dziko lolonjezedwa ndi lawo, lomwe alibe gawo kapena mwayi koma ndi onyenga omwe amanyenga. Ndipo m'njira zawo mumabwera msampha womwe chilombo chochokera pansi padziko lapansi chidzatchera Yakobo chifukwa kudzera mu Talmud ya Esau Ashkenaz yagwirizana mu mgwirizano ndi Esau, ndipo adzagwirizanitsidwa kwambiri ndi mwanawankhosa wa chinjoka, ngakhale mwanawankhosa wawo wabodza wa nyanga ziwiri. Ngakhale mwanawankhosa wosakhala wachibadwa komwe nyanga imodzi imalamulira nyanja malinga ndi Noahide ndipo nyanga ina imalamulira dziko lolonjezedwa m'mwano waukulu ndi chinyengo chifukwa iye ndi nyanga yaing'ono ya Danieli, ndipo chomwecho dziko lapansi lidzalamuliridwa ndi ulamuliro wawo kwa nthawi yochepa. Chifukwa adzagwirizanitsa magulu onse ndi magulu a zoipa pansi pa ulamuliro wawo, ndipo adzawasonkhanitsa pamodzi kuti amenye nkhondo pa tsiku lalikululo, komwe onse adzaponyedwa pansi mwachiwawa kosatha ndipo m'chilango chosatha ndi chiweruzo adzapita kosatha.

Ndipo komabe njira za kangaude ndi njira zowopseza, ndi kulanda, ndi kupha, ndi ziphuphu ndi njira zambiri, zambiri zoipa kangaude amaphunzitsa ndi kukhala nazo chifukwa kangaude nthawi zonse amafunafuna kupanga akangaude ambiri omwe ali ana a gehena kuwirikiza kawiri ngati iwo eni. Ndipo chomwecho akangaude amavina ndi kudzikondwerera okha pa kupha kwawo anthu a m'dzikolo, ngakhale kuvina ndi manja olukana pamene akuvina mwachisangalalo pa imfa ndi chiwonongeko cha omwe awazunza omwe ambiri mwa iwo ndi amayi ndi ana ndi makanda; ndipo chomwecho chiwonongeko chawo sichichedwa. Komabe atenga atsogoleri a dziko lapansi ndikukola mafumu a dziko lapansi mwa chilolezo ndi mwa kugonjetsa, ndipo atsala pang'ono kugonjetsa dziko lonse lapansi m'maukonde awo ambiri ndipo m'maukonde awo dziko lapansi lamangidwa ndikuperekedwa m'manja mwa chinjoka kuti chiweruzidwe ndi kuwonongedwa komwe kwatsala pang'ono kufika kwathunthu. Ndipo komabe kangaude amalira mokweza m'mowawa pamene akudya nyama yake ngakhale kuti nthawi zonse aziwoneka ngati wozunzidwa wosauka, pamene m'choonadi ndi kangaude yemwe amazunza ndi kuvutitsa Adamu.

Choncho, mverani mawu anga anthu a m'dziko langa, ngakhale onse omwe amawerenga mawu awa ndikudzipatula nokha kwa kangaude ndi maukonde ake, chifukwa pamene wina aliyense atembenuka kuchoka ku chiwonongeko chawo kuitana pa dzina la Ambuye, ngakhale Yesu wa ku Nazarete yemwe alinso Yeshua HaMashiach yemwe ndi Mesiya weniweni wa Israyeli; indetu, indetu munthu ameneyo adzapulumutsidwa.

Indetu, indetu, ndikukulamulani nonse kuti mukane kwathunthu ndikukana Talmud yawo yonyoza yomwe ndi bukhu la zoipa zazikulu, zonyansa, ndi chinyengo; ngakhale bukhu lolembedwa ndi Esau ndikutengedwa ndi Sunagoge la Satana, sunagoge lomwe muli zoipa ndi zonyansa zokha. Chifukwa kangaude ndi Sunagoge la Satana, ngakhale wobadwa m'fuko la Ashkenaz la Yafeti omwe alibe gawo kapena mwayi m'cholowa cha Semu, ndipo makamaka cha Yakobo wolungama yemwe ndidzamupulumutsa wamoyo wotsalira wamphamvu.

Chifukwa chake, tulukani pakati pawo ndipo mukhale opatulika ndikutembenukira kwa Yeshua HaMashiach kuti muphunzire za Ine ndikuti muphunzire kukhala m'njira zolungama za Yahweh zomwe njira zake ndili nazo ndipo yemwe Ine ndine Mawu wopangidwa thupi, chifukwa Mose ali wamoyo kwathunthu mwa Yeshua HaMashiach ndipo m'Chipangano Changa Chatsopano muli malonjezo onse ndi mapangano akale, malonjezo omwe adzakwaniritsidwa kwathunthu ndi mokwanira mu ulamuliro wanga wa zaka chikwi ndi Ufumu womwe ndidzakhazikitsa m'chilungamo padziko lapansi.

Ine ndine Yeshua HaMashiach yemwe ndi Mesiya weniweni wa Israyeli, ndipo indetu Ine ndine Mfumu yanu, Ambuye, Mkulu wa ansembe, ndi Mesiya kosatha ndipo Ufumu wanga ndi ulamuliro wanga sizidzatha.

Ine ndine amene ndikubwera kudzachenjeza anthu a m'dziko langa za chinyengo chachikulu ichi ndi chiwonongeko chomwe chatsimikizika kumeza dziko lapansi mu ukonde wake chifukwa akangaude aluka maukonde awo padziko lonse lapansi tsopano.

Ine ndine Khristu Yesu, ndipo ndatumiza Mawu awa kudzera mwa mthenga wanga ndi mtumiki wanga ndipo kudzera mwa mthenga wanga ndi mtumiki wanga mawu awa atumizidwa.

Ine ndine Yesu Khristu, yemwenso ndi Yeshua HaMashiach yemwe wavumbulutsa Mawu a Uthenga Wabwino Wosatha pamodzi ndi Yahweh mwiniwake m'nthawi zovumbulutsa zomwe zili tsopano. Ngakhale nthawi zovumbulutsa zisanayambe zovuta za Yakobo kwathunthu kuti Yakobo apulumutsidwebe.

Choncho, mvetsetsani kulemera kwa Mawu anga ndipo musakhale opanda chikhulupiriro koma khulupirirani umboni wanga ndikumva Mawu anga ndikuchoka ku kusakhulupirira kowuma khosi ndi kupanduka chifukwa tsopano ndi nthawi yobwerera kwa Yahweh kudzera mwa Yeshua HaMashiach ngakhale m'nthawi zamavuto zomwe nthawi zake zafika.

Ndipo onse omwe amabwera kwa Mesiya wawo Wosatha ndikundilandira Ine ndi Chipangano Changa Chatsopano adzapeza chipulumutso m'madongosolo awoawo. Omwe amayitana pa dzina la Ambuye Yesu Khristu m'maulemu a kuphedwa chifukwa cha chikhulupiriro adzaukitsidwa pa kuuka kwa olungama ndipo kulemera kwa mphotho yawo yamuyaya kudzaposa kwambiri kulimbana kwawo ndi mavuto awo omwe ndidzawalimbikitsa kuti agonjetse monga momwe Yahweh adandilimbikitsira kuti ndigonjetse.

Ndipo omwe ochokera kwa Yakobo omwe ndidzawabisira m'chipululu kuchokera ku nthawi za chisautso chachikulu kwambiri kuposa kale lonse; pambuyo pake adzatengedwa amoyo mu Ufumu wanga wa zaka chikwi ngakhale kuti akhale obala zipatso ndi kuchulukana, ndipo tidzakhala m'njira za Yahweh chifukwa m'chilungamo dziko lapansi lidzalamuliridwa, ndipo palibe chinthu choipa chomwe chidzalowamo.

Ndipo ndine, Yeshua HaMashiach yemwe akulengeza kudzaza kwa nthawi kuti dziko lapansi libale omwe agona mwa Khristu ndi kupulumutsa omwe ali amoyo ndi otsala pa kuwonekera kwanga ndipo omwe apezeka okhulupirika mwa Khristu, chifukwa indetu, indetu nthawi zili tsopano. Selah!

Ine ndine, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndipo ndikubwera kudzakutengani okondedwa anga, ndipo Ine ndine amene NDINE Wamkulu wandipanga.

Choncho mvetsetsani chikondi chomwe ndili nacho pa inu ndikumvetsetsa chikondi chomwe Yahweh ali nacho pa inu chifukwa indetu, indetu chikondi chathu ndi moto wonyeketsa, ndipo chikondi cha Yahweh sichingagonjetsedwe chifukwa Yahweh sangagonjetsedwe ndipo Yahweh ndi chikondi ndipo Yahweh ndi kuwala, ndipo ndikukuyitanirani ku ufumu wa chikondi ndi kuwala ndi chilungamo; ngakhale komwe ulemerero wa owomboledwa udzawala kosatha, ndipo chilengedwe chidzamasulidwa mu kumasulidwa kwaulemerero kwa ana olungama a Yahweh, omwe ndidzawatsogolera ngati Mfumu yawo ya Mafumu ndi Ambuye wa Ambuye.

Ndipo chomwecho onse adzadziwa kuti inu ndinu anga, ndipo Ine ndine wanu mwa chikondi chomwe mumandiwonetsa pomvera malamulo anga ndi mwa chikondi chomwe mumawonetsana wina ndi mnzake pamene mukupitiriza ndi miyoyo yanu mosasamala kanthu za mavuto. Kuti mukonde Yahweh ndi mtima wanu wonse, ndi moyo wanu wonse, ndi maganizo anu onse, ndi mphamvu zanu zonse, ndikuti indetu, indetu mukonde mnzanu ngati inu mwini ndipo chomwecho ndi malamulo a Yahweh.

Pitirizani kupirira mkwatibwi wanga, chifukwa ola la chiwombolo chanu chokwanira lafika. Ndipo chomwecho ndikusaina dzina langa mu umboni wa Uthenga Wabwino Wosatha, ngakhale kuti zichitike.

Zosainidwa m'chikondi cha Yahweh ndi m'magazi anga otetezera kosatha!
