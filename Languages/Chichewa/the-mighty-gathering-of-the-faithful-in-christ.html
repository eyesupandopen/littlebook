Kusonkhana Kwamphamvu kwa Okhulupirika mwa Khristu

Imvani Mawu a Ambuye wanu Wamuyaya, mpingo wanga, thupi langa, ngakhale mkwatibwi wanga, ndipo tcherani makutu anu pomvera Mawu a Yeshua HaMashiach. Imvani mawu anga ndipo bwerani kwa Ambuye wanu ndi mitima yokonzeka kulandira Mawu anga, kuti chitonthozo chibwere pa inu mokwanira, chifukwa nthawi zachitonthozo ndi chiwombolo chokwanira zili pa inu okondedwa anga, choncho khalani okonzeka!

Monga momwe ziliri, Ine Yesu wa ku Nazarete, ngakhale iye amene alinso Yeshua HaMashiach m'chinenero cha Chihebri, ndikubwera kudzakukanizani okondedwa anga chifukwa palibe nthawi inanso ndipo kusonkhana kwa okhulupirika mwa Khristu kwakhazikitsidwa kuti kuyambe mwadzidzidzi ndi mosayembekezereka tsopano. Choncho musachite mantha okondedwa anga, ndipo mvetsetsani kuti maweruzo a omwe akana machenjezo anga si anu, ndipo mudzapulumutsidwa mokwanira ku mkwiyo womwe ukubwera! Indetu, indetu, chifukwa mwakhulupirira ndikulandira chipulumutso ndi chikhululukiro cha machimo m'kubadwa kwatsopano molingana ndi Chipangano Changa Chatsopano munthawi yachisomo ndi chifundo, mwapulumutsidwa kotheratu ku mkwiyo womwe ukubwera!

Komabe, zili choncho, kuti miyoyo yambiri yosakhazikika ndi yosawona mtima yomwe imalankhula mabodza mwachiphamaso ndikupotoza Mawu anga ku chiweruzo chawo ndi chiwonongeko chawo, omwe mwa chinyengo ndi kudzitamandira konyada kwa thupi amati ndi mphamvu yayikulu ya Yehova, ndipo amasocheretsa onse omwe amamva ndikumvera mabodza awo onenedwa kuchokera m'mitima yachinyengo. Choncho, mvetsetsani malonjezo anga omwe ndinawulula kwa Paulo pamene ndinamphunzitsa Chinsinsi Chachikulu cha Mulungu chomwe chavumbulutsidwa mokwanira m'makalata ake, ngakhale chinsinsi chomwe ambiri a inu simukumvetsetsabe. Komabe, iye anachitira umboni ndikuchitira umboni za mkwiyo wa Yehova: Koma Mulungu atsimikizira chikondi chake kwa ife, poona kuti pamene tinali chikhalire ochimwa, Khristu adatifera. Makamaka tsono, popeza tayesedwa olungama ndi magazi ake, tidzapulumutsidwa ku mkwiyo kudzera mwa iye. Pakuti ngati pamene tinali adani, tinayanjanitsidwa ndi Mulungu kudzera mu imfa ya Mwana wake, makamaka titayanjanitsidwa, tidzapulumutsidwa ndi moyo wake!

O, inu a chikhulupiriro chaching'ono, ndidzakupirirani mpaka liti, okondedwa anga? Choncho ndikubwera kudzapereka chitonthozo kwa inu chifukwa sindidzakusiyani kapena kukutayani, ngakhale inu nonse omwe muli mwa Khristu, ndipo indetu ndidzapirira nanu mpaka muyaya chifukwa mudzakhala nane nthawi zonse ndipo mudzakhala ngati ine nthawi zonse.
