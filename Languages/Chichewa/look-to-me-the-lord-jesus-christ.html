Umboni Wawiri: Mpingo
M'ntchito za Yohane Mbatizi
Marichi 28, 2024

Yang'anani kwa ine, Ambuye Yesu Khristu!

Ana anga, tsegulani maso anu ndikuyang'ana kwa ine, Ambuye Yesu Khristu. Galamukani kutulo ndikubwerera kwa ine; bwererani kwa ine, pakuti manja anga atambasulidwa kwa inu, osankhidwa anga. Posachedwa mukhala ndi ine, popeza nthawi yatsala pang'ono kutha ndipo nthawi yachisomo yatsala pang'ono kutha, ndipo ndidzaifikitsa pakukwaniritsidwa kwake. Galamukani kutulo, ndipo ndidzathyola matsenga a dziko lapansili m'maso mwanu. Lirani ndi kubuma chifukwa cha machimo anu ndi kukana kwanu kubwera kwa ine, ndipo dziwani kuti ndikukuitanani pa miniti yomalizayi. Ndikukuitanani kuti mubwerere kwa ine, chikondi chanu choyamba, ngakhale mwalandira ena mumtima mwanu.

Thawani kwa onsewo, pakuti ndi achinyengo; inde, ndi mimbulu yovala zikopa zankhosa, ndipo mwagona m'manja mwawo oipa. Musagone, ana anga, koma galamukani ndikundiyembekezera, pakuti zizindikiro zanga zili kale m'mlengalenga ndipo ndikubwera ngati mbala usiku. Kodi ndidzapeza chiyani ndikadzabwerera kudzachitenga Mpingo wanga? Kodi ndidzapeza chikhulupiriro? O, mpingo wa Laodikaya, muli ofunda, ndipo ndikanakonda mukanakhala ozizira kapena otentha. Tembenukirani kwa ine kachiwiri, ndipo ndidzakuyeretsani ku tulo tanu, ndipo ndidzatonthoza mabala omwe mwavulazidwa ndi oipa. Ndilibe chikhumbo chokulavulani m'kamwa mwanga, choncho bwererani kwa ine kachiwiri, ndipo ndidzakulandirani. Dziwani kuti chikondi changa pa inu sichifa ndipo nthawi zonse ndi champhamvu komanso chosasinthika.

Palibe wina koma ine amene angakupulumutseni, palibe wina koma ine amene angakuchiritseni ndi kukupangani kukhala athunthu, palibe wina koma ine amene angakutetezeni ndi kukupatsani moyo wosatha, palibe wina koma ine amene angakupulumutseni ku machimo anu, chifukwa palibe dzina lina loperekedwa limene anthu angapulumutsidwe nalo. Ndine chiyembekezo chanu chosatha amene ndakulengani mwatsopano m'kubadwa kwanga kwatsopano komwe ndakutsanulirani. Dziwani kuti ndinu ana a Atate anga, ndipo muli ndi cholowa champhamvu chifukwa cha kukhulupirika kwanu. Komabe, ngakhale ndidzaweruza ntchito zanu kuti mulandire mphoto kapena kutaya, mwadutsa kuchoka ku chosalungama kulowa m'moyo wosatha mwa chikhulupiriro chanu mwa ine ndi Ufumu wanga wosatha. Taonani! Ndakupangani kukhala mafumu ndi ansembe pamaso pa Atate anga, pakuti ndine mkulu wa ansembe wamuyaya monga mwa dongosolo la Melikizedeki.

Ndani amene angadzilimbikitse kulimbana nanu? Pakuti kulimbana ndi abale anga ndikulimbana ndi ine, ndipo ndidzabwezera, akutero Ambuye wamkulu wamuyaya waulemerero wakumwamba! Zinthu zonse kumwamba ndi padziko lapansi zaperekedwa m'manja mwanga, choncho dziwani kuti ndili ndi ulamuliro waukulu, ndipo palibe wamkulu kuposa ine kupatula Atate anga, omwe andikweza pamwamba pa zonse. Nanga ndani amene muyenera kumuopa? Kodi chizunzo ndi masautso sizikupangitsani kukhala amphamvu mwa ine? Kodi si zoona kuti pamene mphamvu zanu zikulepherani, ine ndili pomwepo kuti ndikulimbikitseni m'kufooka kwanu?

Choncho imani nji ndikukhala amphamvu mwa ine! Chifukwa chiyani mukukayikira mawu anga? Koma m'malo mwake, khalani ndi chikhulupiriro mwa ine ndikuyankhula mawu omwe ndikukupatsani, pakuti mawu awa ndi amphamvu ngati mungawayankhule mwachikhulupiriro. Yankhulani motsimikiza ndi ulamuliro, ndikulamula zinthu zopanda pake m'dzina la Yesu Khristu, dzina langa lamphamvu loposa onse. Posachedwa mudzandiona ndi maso anu, ndipo kukhalapo kwanu kumwamba kukufunidwa kwambiri ndi ine ndi Atate anga, inde, ngakhale ndi Yeshua ndi Yahweh. Ndinu chuma chamtengo wapatali cha Atate anga, ndipo ndinu osatheka kugulidwa. Pakuti ndi kuti kwina komwe magazi opanda tchimo amaperekedwa chifukwa cha machimo anu kupatula mwa ine?

O, inu oipa omwe mwakhala ndikunyadira, kuganiza kuti mwapambana kwambiri. Ndidzasokoneza mapulani anu oipa, ndipo mudzatha kukwaniritsa zokhazo zomwe Atate anga atsimikiza kuti mungathe kuchita kuti mupititse patsogolo mapulani awo. Ndinu ofooka ndi opanda pake ndipo mwagonjetsedwa kotheratu. O, munthu wamthupi, chifukwa chiyani mukusankhabe njira zoipa za oipa omwe akukusocheretsani moipa? Kodi zosangalatsa za tchimo n'zofunika kuposa kulekanitsidwa kwamuyaya ndi ulemerero wa Mulungu wamuyaya? Kodi mudzapeza kuti moyo kunja kwa ine? Ndani adzakupulumutsani kupatula uyo amene mukumukanabe? Kodi chisomo changa ndi chifundo changa sizinabweze dzanja la mdani?

Chilichonse chabwino chomwe muli nacho chatsika kuchokera kwa Atate wa zounikira, mwa iye mulibe kusinthasintha kapena mthunzi wotembenuka. Koma ayi, mwakana chisomo changa ndikugwiritsa ntchito chifundo changa kuti musangalale ndikukhala m'machimo anu. Taonani! Chiwonongeko chachikulu chidzakugwerani. Mwakhala mukuperekedwa ndi atsogoleri anu, omwe akukonza chiwonongeko chanu ndipo alola magulu ankhondo ofiira kulowa. Chiwonongeko ndi choipa chachikulu chidzakugwerani mwadzidzidzi, ndipo chidzabwera pamene ndikulandula mkwatibwi wanga pakati pa njira zanu zoipa. Pamenepo chiwonongeko chadzidzidzi chidzakukolani, pakuti ndi msodzi uti wa mbalame amatchera msampha mbalame ikuona? Ayi, msampha wobisika pang'ono uli wokonzeka kutchera, ndipo zidzachitika pamene woletsa wanga achotsedwa, ndipo simudzathawa.

Pamenepo mitima yanu idzasungunuka mwa inu ndipo mphamvu zanu zidzathawa pamene mukuona ndi maso anu zolengedwa zomwe zatsala pang'ono kumasulidwa kuchokera pansi pa dziko lapansi kudza kwa inu. Ndidzatsegula phompho, pakuti ndili ndi ulamuliro pa ilo; pakuti ndili ndi makiyi a imfa ndi helo, ndipo palibe amene angawalandwe kwa ine. Mukanangomvetsa kuti mulibe chilichonse padziko lapansi, ndipo zonse ndi mphatso chabe yachisomo ndi chifundo, chifukwa chikondi cha Mulungu chimatsogolera anthu kukulapa. Koma ayi, mwaumbombo mumasunga ubwino wa Mulungu kwa inu nokha ndikupha mnzanu m'malo mowakonda ngati inu nokha, monga ndalamulira. Mulungu anakonda dziko lapansi kotero kuti anapereka mwana wake wobadwa yekha, Yesu wa ku Nazarete, kuti akuyanjanitseni kwa iye m'chikondi kudzera mwa ine.

Koma m'kudzikuza kwanu ndi njira zanu zadziko, mwaumitsa mitima yanu kutsutsana ndi ine ndi chipulumutso changa. Choncho, chiwonongeko chotheratu chidzabwera pa mtundu wanu mu ola limodzi, ndipo Babulo Wachinsinsi sadzakhalaponso. Inde, Babulo wamkulu, zinsinsi zanu zidzaululidwa kuti onse aone, ndipo omwe amakakamira kwa inu adzalandira chiweruzo chomwecho.

Choncho, o, munthu wokana Mulungu, dzichepetseni ndikubwera kwa ine tsopano! Nthawi yanu yosowa ndi tsopano. Tembenukirani kwa ine; ndikukupemphani, ndipo ndikukuitanani potchula dzina lanu! Tulukani m'dziko lofali, pakuti muli chipulumutso mwa ine ngati mungovomereza.

Ndilibe chimwemwe m'chiweruzo chanu, ndipo Atate anga akufuna kuti mukhale ndi moyo pamaso pawo, ndipo m'chikondi chawo apatsa zolengedwa zawo mwayi wosankha: Ndikaika pamaso panu lero moyo ndi imfa, choncho, sankhani moyo kuti inu ndi mbewu yanu mukhale ndi moyo! Choncho bwererani kwa ine nthawi isanathe ndipo dziwani kuti dziko loipali lomwe mukukakamira lidzawonongedwa kotheratu, ndipo zidzachitika kudzera mu mkwiyo wa Mwanawankhosa. Kodi Mulungu sadzaweruza ochita zoipa omwe apha ana ake, omwe apundula ndi kuzunza osalakwa, omwe apha mwana wobadwa yekha wa Mulungu? Ngakhale ndinapereka moyo wanga mofunitsitsa chifukwa cha inu ndipo palibe munthu anatenga moyo wanga, pali chiweruzo chachikulu ndi chilungamo choti chichitidwe ndi ine. Pakuti ndinagula dziko lapansi ndi magazi anga ndi nsembe yotetezera, ndipo ndi langa. Ndidzakhazikitsa ufumu wachilungamo ndi chiyero chenicheni, kumene mkango ndi mwanawankhosa adzagona pamodzi, kumene palibe chinthu choipa chidzalowa. Choncho, tulukani m'machimo anu, pakuti ndimakukondani, ndipo ndikuitana ndi mpweya wanga womaliza mchenga womaliza usanagwe. Ndipo zatsala pang'ono kufika.
