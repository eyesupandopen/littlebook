Novembala 20, 2024

Mwanawankhosa ndi Mkango Zikhala Chimodzi!

Bwerani kwa Yeshua HaMashiach, m'bale wanga, ndipo mvetsetsani ntchito ya muwomboli wanu, pakuti ine ndine muwomboli wanu. Chifukwa ndine amene ndidzawombola otsala a Yakobo kuti akhale ndi moyo pamaso pa NDINE wamkulu, ngakhale YHVH, Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya ndi wosatha. Ndipo ulamuliro wanga ndi ufumu wanga sizidzatha, chotero, mverani Mawu a Mbuye wanu wamuyaya wa Lonjezo, ngakhale Yeshua HaMashiach, yemwenso ndi Yesu Khristu, yemwenso ndi Khristu Yesu, pakuti chomwecho NDINE wamkulu wandipanga ine kukhala wamuyaya.

Ndipo zoonadi Yahweh wandipatsa dzina loposa maina onse kuti m'dzina la Yesu bondo lililonse lipinde, kumwamba ndi padziko lapansi ngakhale pansi pa dziko lapansi. Chifukwa ndine amene Yahweh wapereka zinthu zonse zakumwamba ndi zapadziko lapansi m'manja mwanga amphamvu kuti zinthu zonse zikwaniritsidwe. Chotero, O' Isiraeli, imvani mawu anga pomvera mwachikondi poyankha chikondi chomwe ndakuwonetsani pofera m'malo mwanu m'mazunzo aakulu omwe sanali anga kuti ndipirire. Komabe, m'chikondi changa pa inu, ndinatenga malo anu kuti ndikuwomboleni ku chilango cha Mose, chilango chomwe n'chosatheka kuti mudzilipirire nokha chifukwa cha tchimo m'thupi lanu. Ngakhale zoonadi chifukwa cha chiwombolo cha Adamu ndinatumizidwa m'dziko lapansi.

Chifukwa Mose ndi wolungama ndi woyera ndipo Mose amakhala mwa Mesiya wanu weniweni ndi Mbuye wamuyaya wa Chilungamo, pakuti ndine wotero. Ndipo ndine amene ndinasunga bwino chilamulo cha Mose mwa chikondi changa ndi kumvera Yahweh yemwe ananditsogolera m'chikondi chake chosatha pa fuko la Adamu lomwe lachitiridwa chinyengo ndi kugonjetsedwa ndi ogwa, chotero chifundo ndi chisomo zaperekedwa mochuluka kuti ziletse kuuma mtima konse kwa chiweruzo ndi mkwiyo kuti kulekanitsa kwathunthu ndi komaliza kuchitike pakati pa tirigu wa Yahweh ndi namsongole wa ogwa, ndipo onse omwe ali olowa nyumba a moyo wosatha mwa Khristu Yesu adzawomboledwa ndi chisankho chawo chodzigonjera kwa Yeshua HaMashiach, yemwe ndi Khristu Yesu, ndipo alinso Yesu wa ku Nazarete.

Ndipo kwa mitundu ya anthu Yahweh anandituma, ndipo potumikira mafuko otayika a Isiraeli ndinapita chifukwa ndinakaniwa ndi abale anga m'kusakhulupirira, ngakhale ndi ansembe aakulu ndi Sanhedrini omwe amatumikira Chinjoka mwachinsinsi chifukwa ndi a Esau yemwe wadziphatikiza yekha m'chidani chake pa m'bale wake Yakobo kwa Ashkenazi wa Yafeti, kuti adzisonkhanitsire zinthu zomwe zili zanga ndi kuti apereke kwa Yakobo bala lomaliza la kumutu lomwe Yahweh sadzalola, ngakhale chinthu chomwe adzakaniza ndi dzanja langa lamphamvu ndi lomvera nthawi zonse. Chotero, m'bale wanga, dzigonjereni pomvera mawu anga kuti mukhale ndi moyo pamaso panga, ndi kuti mukhale ndi moyo pamaso pa Yahweh yemwe palibe munthu angayandikire popanda Yesu Khristu chifukwa chomwecho Yahweh wasankha m'dongosolo lake lamuyaya la chiwombolo ndi nzeru. Choncho mvetsetsani popanda Mesiya wanu weniweni, ngakhale Yeshua HaMashiach mumakhala akufa m'zolakwa ndi machimo omwe ndawagonjetsa pamene ndinalipira mokwanira chilango cha Mose cha nthawi zonse ndi kwa onse omwe asankha kulandira nsembe yanga.

Ndipo zili choncho kuti Mose amakhala mwa Yeshua HaMashiach, ndipo ndine amene Mose analamula kumvera kwanu konse, choncho imvani mawu anga ndipo dulani mitima yanu ndi makutu anu kuti mumve kulankhula kwanga. Chifukwa mitima yanu yanenepa m'kusamvera, ndipo mwadzaza makutu anu ndi nzeru za Esau ndi kuipitsa mitima yanu ndi mabodza awo amwano a Talmud. Mvetsetsani kuti Mose ndi wangwiro ndi wolungama ndi woyera ndipo ngati munthu aliyense atcha chilamulo cha Mose chotembereredwa, ndiye kuti munthuyo atembereredwe chifukwa cha mwano wake, ngakhale ndi chilamulo cha Mose chomwe anatemberera. Chotero, ndikukupatsani kuti mumvetsetse kuti chilamulo chinaperekedwa kuti chitsimikize tchimo m'thupi, pakuti pamene palibe chilamulo tchimo silimawerengedwa malinga ndi chilamulo, ngakhale tchimo lilipo m'thupi chimodzimodzi. Chotero, ndi ntchito za chilamulo cha Mose palibe thupi lidzayesedwa lolungama pamaso pa Yahweh: pakuti mwa chilamulo cha Mose kumabwera kudziwa tchimo.

Komabe, monga ndinalembera Paulo ku Mpingo wanga m'nthawi yanga ya Chisomo kuti tsopano chilungamo cha Mulungu chaonekera popanda chilamulo cha Mose, kukhala ndi umboni wa chilamulo ndi wa Aneneri. Oh, olankhula anga akale omwe mwagwa kuchokera ku gawo lanu lakale lomwe linaperekedwa ku mtundu wanu womwe unali waukulu ndi Yahweh mwiniwake chifukwa munayenera kukhala anthu apadera olekanitsidwa ndi osiyana ndi mitundu ina! Komabe mwandikana ine chimodzimodzi monga Atate anu m'kupanduka kwanu kouma mtima motsutsana ndi zolinga zamuyaya ndi mapulani a Mulungu Wamphamvuyonse, ngakhale El Shaddai. Ndipo zili choncho chifukwa cha kukana kwanu Mawu anga operekedwa m'uthenga wabwino wa chipulumutso pamene ndinavumbulutsa chinsinsi chachikulu cha Mulungu kwa atumwi anga oyera ndi aneneri mwakhala ngati wofuna mkaka, ndipo simungathe kugaya chakudya cholimba chifukwa muli makanda opanda kumvetsetsa kwathunthu, ngakhale mumadziona modzikuza ngati anzeru ndi zounikira koma mwaunikiridwa mofalitsa ndi ogwa kuti musocheretse anthu m'njira zanu zosaphunzira.

Chotero, mvetsetsani, m'bale wanga, kuti wolungama adzakhala ndi moyo ndi chikhulupiriro ndipo kuti ndi ntchito za chilamulo palibe thupi lidzayesedwa lolungama! Zalembedwa m'malemba oyera operekedwa ndi manja a Paulo, yemwe analidi Mbenjamini womvera yemwe poyamba anali wosamvera ndi wouma khosi m'kukana kwake Yesu wa ku Nazarete, ngakhale chimodzimodzi monga inu: Chotero awo omwe ali a chikhulupiriro, adzadalitsidwa pamodzi ndi Abulahamu wokhulupirika. Pakuti onse omwe ali a ntchito za chilamulo, ali pansi pa temberero la chilamulo: pakuti kwalembedwa, wotembereredwa ndi munthu aliyense amene sapitiriza m'zinthu zonse, zomwe zalembedwa m'buku la Chilamulo, kuzichita.

Komabe, chifukwa cha chidani, chinyengo, ndi machenjera a njoka pa tsiku lomwe Adamu anagwa kuchokera kumwamba; tchimo lalamulira m'thupi kuyambira pamenepo ndipo mawonekedwe oipitsidwa a Adamu si kwawo kwanu, ndipo simunayikidwe kuti mukhale muyaya m'nyumba zadongo zomwe nyumbazo zibwerera kufumbi la dziko lapansi komwe chiyambi chanu chinatengedwa. Chotero, Yahweh wandipanga ine mzimu wopatsa moyo, ndipo ndine Adamu wotsiriza. Ndipo ndine amene ndapangidwa ngati chitsanzo cha moyo wosatha kuti inu nonse mulengedwenso m'kukwanira kwathunthu ndi umodzi, ngakhale ngati munthu mmodzi watsopano. Chifukwa sikudzakhala mitundu iwiri ya anthu muyaya koma umodzi chifukwa mawonekedwe a Adamu adzawonongeka kwamuyaya pamodzi ndi kuipitsidwa komwe kuli m'thupi loipitsidwa.

Chotero, mvetsetsani kuti ogwa angafune kukubisirani choonadi m'khungu lanu lapano ndi kunyada, ngakhale choonadi chomwe chidzakumasulani kwathunthu mu ufulu womwe ndikufuna kukumasulani. Choncho ndikukupatsani kuti mumvetsetse, O' Isiraeli, (yemwe ndimamukonda) kuti palibe munthu amayesedwa wolungama ndi ntchito za chilamulo cha Mose pamaso pa Yahweh, n'zoonekeratu: pakuti wolungama adzakhala ndi moyo ndi chikhulupiriro. Ndipo chilamulo si cha chikhulupiriro: koma munthu amene adzachita zinthu izi, adzakhala ndi moyo mwa izo. Ndipo ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndakuwombolani ku temberero la chilamulo, pokhala temberero m'malo mwanu, pakuti kwalembedwa, wotembereredwa ndi aliyense amene apachikidwa pamtengo. Chotero, O' Isiraeli, landirani nsembe yanga kuti inu ndi mbadwa zanu mukhale ndi moyo ndi kuti mukhale ndi mwayi wolowa m'ufumu wanga wa zaka chikwi womwe waperekedwa ngati mpumulo wa masiku a Sabata m'mapulani amuyaya a Yahweh a chiwombolo cha anthu, pakuti zaka chikwi zili ngati tsiku limodzi ndipo tsiku limodzi ngati zaka chikwi pamaso pa Yahweh, yemwe muli naye chita, O' Isiraeli.

Chotero, chokani kwa omwe amakunaminani ndipo dzilekanitseni ku buku lamwano la mabodza lotumizidwa ndi ogwa kuti likunyengeni ndi kukumangani m'cholakwa kuti muwonongeke kwathunthu. Chokani ku mabodza a njoka ndipo tulukani pakati pawo, ndipo mukhale olekanitsidwa, O' Isiraeli, ndipo ndidzakulandirani kwa ine ndikukusungani amoyo ku chiwonongeko cha sabata la makumi asanu ndi awiri la Danieli, ngakhale tsoka la Yakobo lomwe likubwera kudzayeretsa dziko lapansi m'manja mwa ochita zoipa m'chiweruzo chachikulu ndi cholungama.

Chifukwa nthawi za kulekanitsa zikubwera kwathunthu padziko lapansi pamene Yakobo, yemwe cholowa chake ndi cha lonjezo kwa ana a lonjezo omwe amakhala ndi chikhulupiriro, adzalekanitsidwa kwathunthu ndi kwamuyaya kwa m'bale wake wosamvera, ngakhale Esau yemwe akufuna kuba zomwe anagulitsa m'kusakhulupirira chifukwa analibe ulemu kapena chikhulupiriro m'malonjezo okhulupirika opangidwa m'chikhulupiriro ndi Yahweh kwa Abulahamu wokhulupirika, koma m'malo mwake anasamala kwambiri za zinthu za dziko lapansi pokhala wadama ndi munthu wamwano yemwe anagulitsa cholowa chake chamuyaya ndi ukulu wake chifukwa cha kachidutswa ka mkate.

Choncho ndikukupatsani kuti mumvetsetse tsogolo lomwe ndikukuitanani kuti mutengeko mbali ndipo lomwe ndidzakusungani m'chitetezo cha mphamvu yanga ngati mudzadzigonjera ku ulamuliro wanga, pakuti potero ndidzakubwezeretsani kwathunthu kwa Yahweh ngakhale Atate wa Zounikira yemwe mulibe kusintha kapena mthunzi wotembenuka. Ndipo zili choncho monga kwalembedwa m'malonjezo akale ndipo zidzakhala choncho m'bale wanga yemwe ndine muwomboli wake!

Ndipo zidzachitika kumapeto kwa tsoka la Yakobo kuti ndidzakhazikitsa ufumu wanga m'chilungamo padziko lapansi, ndipo palibe chinthu choipa chidzalowamo. Mvetsetsani kuti ndidzabweretsa mapeto amuyaya ku ufumu wa Satana ndipo chinjoka sichidzauka kudzalamuliranso ndipo chilombo chochokera kunyanja ndi chilombo chochokera padziko lapansi zidzaponyedwa zamoyo m'nyanja yamoto yoyaka, sulufule wogubuduzika yemwe amayaka malawi otentha kwambiri a buluu ndipo m'nyanja yoyipa ya chilango imeneyo adzaponyedwa kwamuyaya, komwe mphutsi yawo sifa pakuti ndiwoyenera kutero.

Satana mwiniwake adzagwidwa ndi kumangidwa m'unyolo waukulu wa mazunzo ndi chilango komwe adzadikira mwa mantha ndi kupsinjika kwa chiwonongeko chake chomwe chikubwera, cholowa chomwe adzagawana ndi onse omwe amamutsatira. Zoonadi, zoonadi, m'phompho adzakhala wosindikizidwa ndi womangidwa kwa nthawi ya zaka chikwi, pamapeto pa nthawiyo adzamasulidwa kuti asonkhanitse omwe adzakhala atapanduka ndi kulowa njira zake, ngakhale kupanduka kwake komaliza kuti choyipa chiwonongeke kwamuyaya m'chiweruzo chomaliza. Ndipo kumapeto kwa nyengo yake yaing'ono pamene adzakhala atawasonkhanitsa pamodzi ngati mchenga wa kunyanja motsutsana ndi msasa wa oyera mtima pa m'lifupi mwa dziko lapansi, moto udzagwa kuchokera kwa Yahweh kumwamba womwe udzawawononga ndi kuwameza onse, ndipo Satana adzamangidwa kwamuyaya ndi kuponyedwa ku cholowa chake chamuyaya m'malawi amoto amuyaya a chilango ndipo chiweruzo cha mpando wachifumu waukulu woyera chidzayamba.

Ndipo m'nyanja yamoto yoyaka nthawi zonse ya chilango yomwe imayaka malawi otentha kwambiri a mazunzo, ngakhale m'kukumbatira kwake kwamuyaya koyaka nthawi zonse Satana adzalipira kwathunthu tchimo lililonse lomwe anachitapo nthawi zambiri, ndipo chinthu chilichonse choipa chalembedwa bwino kumwamba ndipo chidzaperekedwa m'malawi a chilango ngati cholowa chake chamuyaya. Zoonadi, cholengedwa chilichonse chomwe chatsatira ndi kudzipereka ku cholinga chake chidzakumana ndi chilango chomwecho ndipo sadzakhala ndi chifundo chifukwa sanawonetse chifundo, chotero palibe chifundo chidzaperekedwa kwamuyaya. Ndipo monga achitira ena, ndi monga avulazira chilengedwe cha Yahweh momwemonso zidzabwezeredwa pamitu pawo, ngakhale monga analonjezera kale chifukwa palibe lonjezo limodzi la Yahweh lomwe lidzagwa pansi losakwaniritsidwa. Zoonadi, zoonadi onse omwe akana mwayi wanga wa chipulumutso adzaponyedwa kumoto ndipo palibe tsankho.

Komabe, tsogolo la ogwa lomwe asankha ndi kudzipangira okha m'kupanduka kwawo koipa motsutsana ndi Yahweh ndi chilengedwe chake si lanu m'bale wanga, ngati mungandilandire ngati Mesiya wanu. Ayi, m'malo mwake, pali mphotho zambiri ndi madalitso osungidwa kwa inu omwe mudzazindikira kwathunthu m'ufumu wanga womwe ndidzalamulira m'chilungamo chosatha. Kudzakhala Mfumu imodzi ya Mafumu ndi Mbuye wa Ambuye yemwe adzalamulira dziko lapansi m'chilungamo chamuyaya, ngakhale kuchokera ku Yerusalemu, ngakhale kuchokera ku mpando wachifumu wa Davide womwe ndine wolowa nyumba wamuyaya! Ndipo mkwatibwi wanga wokhulupirika yemwe ndidzamutenga kwa ine m'pangano lamuyaya ndidzamuveka korona ndi kupatulira gulu lalikulu la Mafumu ndi Ansembe malinga ndi kukhulupirika kwawo ndipo adzalamulira m'chilungamo m'mgwirizano wangwiro ndi Mfumu yawo ya Mafumu ndi Mbuye wa Ambuye ndipo tidzalamulira ngati mmodzi.

Ndipo anthu a m'dzikolo ndi anthu a mitundu adzasula malupanga awo kukhala zolimira ndipo mitundu sidzamenyananso nkhondo pakati pawo, ndipo anthu a padziko lapansi sadzaphunzira kumenya nkhondo ndi kumenyana m'ufumu wanga woyera. Ngakhale monga kwalembedwa ndi aneneri akale: Mmbulu udzakhala pamodzi ndi mwanawankhosa, ndipo nyalugwe adzagona pansi ndi mwana wambuzi, ndi mwana wang'ombe, ndi mkango, ndi chiweto chonenepa pamodzi, ndipo mwana wamng'ono adzawatsogolera. Ndipo ng'ombe ndi chimbalangondo zidzadya: ana awo adzagona pamodzi: ndipo mkango udzadya udzu ngati ng'ombe. Ndipo mwana woyamwa adzasewera pa dzenje la mamba, ndipo mwana woletsedwa kuyamwa adzaika dzanja lake pa dzenje la mphiri. Pamenepo palibe amene adzapweteka kapena kuwononga m'phiri langa lonse loyera: pakuti dziko lapansi lidzadzaza ndi kudziwa kwa Ambuye, monga madzi aphimba nyanja.

Ndipo m'tsiku limenelo madzi amoyo adzatuluka mu Yerusalemu, theka lawo kulowera kunyanja ya Kum'mawa, ndi theka lawo kulowera kunyanja yotsiriza, ndipo kudzakhala choncho m'chilimwe ndi m'nyengo yozizira. Ndipo Ambuye adzakhala Mfumu pa dziko lonse lapansi: m'tsiku limenelo kudzakhala Ambuye mmodzi, ndi Dzina lake lidzakhala limodzi. Dziko lonse lidzasanduka ngati chigwa kuyambira ku Geba mpaka ku Rimoni, kumwera kwa Yerusalemu, ndipo lidzakwezedwa, ndi kukhalamo anthu m'malo mwake: kuyambira pachipata cha Benjamini mpaka pamalo a chipata choyamba, mpaka pachipata chapangodya, ndi kuyambira pansanja ya Hananieli, mpaka kopondera mphesa kwa Mfumu. Ndipo anthu adzakhalamo, ndipo sikudzakhala chiwonongeko chinanso, koma Yerusalemu adzakhalamo anthu mosatekeseka.

Choncho mvetsetsani, Inu Isiraeli zomwe ndidzawachitira onse amene amandikonda pomvera malamulo anga osatha a moyo ndi mtendere omwe ndikukulamulani nawo. Choncho mvetsetsani kuti kukonda Yahweh ndiko kusunga malamulo ake ndipo malamulo a Yahweh amalamula kumvera Mwana wa Yahweh, amene ndine: chotero, mpsompsoneni Mwanayo kuti ndingakwiye ndipo mungawonongeke panjira.

Chifukwa, wanga ndi ufumu umene zolinga zanga zamuyaya zidzakwaniritsidwa pamodzi mu ukwaniri ndi chimaliziro chathunthu pamene mwanawankhosa adzakhala amodzi ndi mkango.

Chifukwa, Ine ndine Mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu, woperekedwa chifukwa cha machimo a anthu amene magazi ake ndi nsembe yotetezera yamuyaya imene osalungama amayeretsedwa ngati matalala mu chiyero chopatulika.

Chifukwa, Ine ndine Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda amene amakumenyerani nkhondo Inu Isiraeli ndipo amene adzawononga kotheratu adani anu monga momwe kwalembedwera za ine; kubwezera chilango ndi kwanga ndipo ndidzabwezera, atero Mbuye wa ambuye ndi Mfumu ya mafumu!

Chotero, mvetsetsani zolinga zanga ndi amene Yahweh wamutuma kudzakuwombolani ndipo pali mbali zambiri za zolinga zanga za chipulumutso. Ndipo Ine ndine wopambana, ngakhale iye amene wapambana chipulumutso chanu kwamuyaya ngati mungasankhe kulandira dzanja langa lachikondi ndi kulandira pangano langa latsopano.

Chifukwa ndi mwa pangano langa latsopano limene ndinalivomereza m'magazi anga, limene tsopano liri Chipangano changa Chatsopano chimene dziko lapansi lidzalamuliridwa nacho m'chilungamo! Ndipo chidziwitso cha Yahweh chidzaphimba dziko lapansi ndipo ulemerero wanga udzawala kuchokera ku Yerusalemu kuti mitundu ya anthu idzisonkhanitse yokha ndi kulowa m'zipata zopatulika za mzinda wopatulikawo kuti aphunzire za Yahweh ndi kuti akhale m'njira zake pa dziko lapansi.

Sikudzakhalanso mu ufumu wanga khanda la masiku ochepa, kapena nkhalamba, imene sinakwaniritse masiku ake: pakuti mwana adzamwalira ali ndi zaka zana limodzi: koma wochimwa wazaka zana limodzi, adzatembereredwa. Ndipo kudzakhala kuti amene adzakhala ndi moyo kufika zaka zana limodzi adzakhala ngati khanda.

Ngakhale zili choncho, dziko lapansi lidzabwezeretsedwa ku ulemerero wake wakale asanali akugwa aipitse dziko lapansi ndi zonyansa zawo pamene anatsikira pa Phiri la Herimoni kudzaika lumbiro lawo lotembereredwa kuti athe kukhala m'zilakolako zawo mu tchimo lalikulu ndi kupandukira madongosolo a moyo omwe analengedwa nawo mwangwiro. Sadzakhala mu ufumu wanga; kapena dziko lapansi silidzalamuliridwa mu chiwawa chodzaza chidani kumene amphamvu okha amapulumuka, pakuti zopanda pake zoterezi ndi kusintha kwa malamulo a Yahweh kumene Satana nthawi zonse amafuna kusintha madongosolo a Yahweh kuti chiwonongeko chilamulire.

Ndipo ndine, Yeshua HaMashiach amene ndidzalamulira mitundu ya anthu ndi ndodo yachitsulo m'manja mwa Mafumu ndi Ansembe ambiri amene ndidzawaveka korona ndi kuwaika pa utumiki wapamwamba kwambiri, ndipo iwo ndi zipatso zoyamba za akufa, ndipo ndi osafa osafanso ndipo mphotho yawo ilibe mapeto, chifukwa anabwera kwa ine mofunitsitsa m'masiku a chisomo ndipo ndinawasonkhanitsa kwa ine ndekha m'thupi langa lolengedwanso ngati munthu mmodzi watsopano kaya anali Myuda kapena Wamitundu, kaya anali kapolo kapena mfulu. Adzakhala ndi mphamvu zambiri ndi maluso ndi makhalidwe opatsidwa kwa iwo chifukwa cha kukhulupirika kwawo chifukwa iwo ndi opambana anga amene anapambana zinthu zonse ndi magazi a mwanawankhosa.

Chifukwa ndi Mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu amene anagonjetsa akugwa pa kalvare kwamuyaya ndipo ndi Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda amene udzagonjetsa kotheratu chilengedwe kwa Yahweh, ngakhale kuti Yahweh akhale zonse mu zonse pamene ndidzapereka kwa iye chilengedwe chogonjetsedwa chifukwa ndiyenera kulamulira kufikira adani anga onse atapangidwa chopondapo mapazi anga, ndipo ndi Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda umene udzapondereza mbali iliyonse ya ufumu wa Satana pansi pa mapazi anga amphamvu ndi amuyaya.

Chotero, Ine ndine amene mwanawankhosa adzagwirizana mwa mtendere wathunthu ndi umodzi ndi mkango chifukwa zolinga zanga zamuyaya zidzachitidwa kwamuyaya ndipo palibe kanthu ndipo palibe amene angathe kuima motsutsana ndi Yeshua HaMashiach ndipo nthawi zanga zafika pamene ndidzadzivumbulutsa ndekha kwa okhala pa dziko lapansi. Taonani, ndikubwera ndi mitambo, ndipo diso lililonse lidzandiona: inde, ngakhale iwo amene anandibaya: ndipo mafuko onse a dziko lapansi adzalira pamaso panga, ngakhale zili choncho, Amen.

Ine ndine Yeshua HaMashiach amene watsala pang'ono kudzivumbulutsa yekha ku dziko lapansi ndipo dziko lonse lapansi lidzadziwa kuti kuli Mulungu kumwamba ndipo njira za Yahweh zidzadzaza dziko lapansi m'chilungamo chopatulika.

Ine ndine Khristu Yesu, Ambuye wanu wokwera ndi woukitsidwa, Mfumu, Mkulu wa Ansembe, ndi Mesiya!

Ine ndine amene ndatumiza Mawu anga oyera kudzera mwa mthenga ndi mtumiki wanga ndipo kudzera mwa mthenga ndi mtumiki wanga Mawu anga oyera atumizidwa! Chifukwa mthenga wanga wamphamvu wabwerera mokhulupirika ku maitanidwe ake amuyaya pambuyo pa chilango chambiri, kulapa, ndi kuyeretsedwa m'magazi anga otetezera; ndipo ndamulandira kwa ine ndekha m'chikondi changa kwa olapa aliyense amene angakhale amene amabwera kwa ine ndi mtima wosweka. Ndipo kwa mthenga wanga ndinavumbulutsa Kabuku Kaja kamene Yohane Wovumbulutsa analamulidwa kukasindikiza kuti kadzavumbulutsidwe mu nthawi yake, bukhu limene lili ndi mawu a mabingu asanu ndi awiri ngakhale onse omwe ndi Uthenga Wabwino Wosatha ndipo walengeza Mawu anga mokhulupirika monga momwe analamulidwira, umboni umene Yohane Wovumbulutsa analemba ndi kuchitira umboni.

Ine ndine amene sindingoyang'ana nkhope za anthu, ngakhale Yeshua HaMashiach, ndipo aliyense ndi onse amene amabwera kwa ine kuchokera ku fuko la Adamu m'njira yotere ndidzawalandira, kupatulapo iwo amene mopusa amatenga chizindikiro cha chilombo, kapena amene amadzisintha okha m'njira yotere. Zoonadi, zoonadi onse amene amabwera kwa ine m'njira yotere amene amatembenuka kuti akhale ndi moyo wolungama adzalandira mphotho kwamuyaya ndipo kulemera kwa mphotho yanu ndi kwamuyaya.

Ndipo zili choncho m'bale wanga, kuti chipulumutso ndi mphatso ya moyo wosatha, imene Ine Yeshua HaMashiach ndinapeza mwa kumvera kwanga mokhulupirika chilamulo cha Mose ndi mwa nsembe yanga yamuyaya ya ine ndekha monga Mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu wopanda tchimo ndi wangwiro. Chotero, onse amene amabwera kwa ine mu kulapa kwathunthu ndi kudzichepetsa ndi kumvera ndidzawabwezeretsa kwa ine ndekha ndi kwa Yahweh ndipo ndidzawapatsa ntchito zambiri zolungama zimene Yahweh angafune kuti akwaniritse m'zolinga zake zosatha ndi zamuyaya. Chotero, mphatso ya moyo wosatha ili m'manja mwanga kwa onse amene apempha mu chifatso ndi kulapa ndipo amene amabwera kwa ine m'nthawi zoperekedwa za kulapa.

Ine ndine Yeshua HaMashiach, ndipo ndimakukondani inu anthu a dziko langa ndipo ndikubwera kudzawapulumutsani ndipo ndidzapulumutsa omvera pakati panu mpaka kotheratu, chotero mverani Mawu a INE NDINE wamkulu pomvera Mesiya wanu woona, mwa amene mwanawankhosa amayenda dzanja ndi dzanja ndi mkango, pakuti zoonadi, Ine ndine.

Taonani! Chipulumutso cha Isiraeli chili pafupi ndipo mwanawankhosa akuimba mogwirizana ndi mkango umene ukubangula mu chitetezo choopsa cha ana ake, chotero, konzekerani Inu Isiraeli chifukwa chiwombolo chanu chafika.

Ndimvereni m'chikondi changa pa inu, anthu a dziko langa, chifukwa Mesiya wanu woona ali pafupi!
