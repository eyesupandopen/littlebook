Kwa Mpingo Wanga, Thupi Langa, Ngakhale Mkwatibwi Wanga: Imvani Mawu A Ambuye Wanu!

Kwa Mpingo wanga, Thupi langa, ngakhale Mkwatibwi wanga: Ndikupemphani kuti mutchere khutu ku nzeru zanga ndi kumva mawu anga, pakuti tsopano ndilankhula nanu. Kodi mukumvera? Ikani pambali ziphunzitso zanu zosiyanasiyana zopangidwa ndi anthu ndi zomwe zimatchedwa maphunziro a zaumulungu, ndipo tcherani mtima wanu ku Malemba Oyera, ngakhale mawu anga oyera. Ikani pambali ziphunzitso zanu zofufumitsa, pakuti kusakaniza kulikonse kwa Mawu a Mulungu, ngakhale Mawu oyera a Mulungu, kumafufumitsa mtanda wonse wa ufa ndipo mulibenso malemba. Pakuti ndine Mawu a Mulungu wopangidwa thupi, ndipo ndimakhala mwa inu, ndipo ndikukuitanirani kunyumba yanu kumwamba posachedwapa.

Ikani pambali ndemanga zanu, ndipo siyani kumvera mawu ambiri onama, ngakhale mawu omwe sali anga; koma m'malo mwake, sungani Mawu anga m'mitima yanu ndikuwayerekezera ndi malemba kuti mukhale ndi chikhulupiriro m'Mawu anga ndi chikhulupiriro mwa ine, Khristu Yesu. Pakuti ngati wina abwera kwa inu yemwe salankhula chiphunzitso cha Ambuye, ngakhale Mawu oyera a Mulungu, musamufunire ngakhale mtendere. Pakuti ndidzakupangani kukhala anzeru m'Malemba anga Oyera, ngakhale Mawu a moyo, ndipo nzeru zanga sizivunda. Choncho sonkhanitsani chuma chanu pamodzi kumwamba, kumene dzimbiri kapena njenjete sizivunditsa, ndipo kumene mbala siziboola kapena kuba. Pakuti kumene kuli chuma chanu, kumeneko kudzakhala mitima yanu. Ana anga, ndine chuma chanu, choncho sungani maganizo anu ndi mitima yanu pa ine ndekha ndi mtima umodzi.

Kodi simunawerenge Mawu omwe amavumbulutsa chinsinsi chachikulu cha Mulungu ichi? Pakuti tili ndi chuma ichi m'mbiya zadothi kuti mphamvu yopambana iwoneke ngati ya Mulungu, osati yathu. Kwa iwo omwe Mulungu anafuna kuwadziwitsa chuma cha ulemerero wa chinsinsi ichi pakati pa amitundu, chomwe ndi Khristu mwa inu, chiyembekezo cha ulemerero. Choncho musaope, pakuti mwabadwanso mwatsopano ndi mzimu wanga woyera; chifukwa chake, mvetsetsani Mawu a Mawu anga. Pakuti ndidzakuuzani nzeru zomwe zidzakutonthozani ndi kukusungani ku chinyengo champhamvu chomwe chagonjetsa dziko lapansi; pakuti dziko lapansi lamangidwa unyolo kaamba ka chiweruzo ndipo lili m'mantha aakulu ndi masautso.

Choncho, ndabwera kudzayankhula nanu kuti ndichitire umboni kwa anzeru pakati panu za atsogoleri awiri a dziko omwe panopa akuwoneka ngati abusa abwino, ngakhale nkhosa zosankhidwa pakati pa gulu kuti zitumikire mu ntchito za boma, omwe akuwoneka kuti abwera kudzaweta nkhosa zotayika za Babulo Wachinsinsi kuti zikhale zotetezeka. Koma abusa awiri ampherewa si nkhosa, koma ndi zilombo zoyipa zobisala ngati nkhosa ndipo akuchokera ku khola lina, ngakhale la chinjoka choweta. Chinjoka choweta si mbusa weniweni wa nkhosa, pakuti gulu lake si gulu koma gulu la omwe adagwa kuchokera ku ulemerero ndipo asanduka zinthu zodetsedwa zamitundumitundu zoyenera chiwonongeko. Ndikulankhula za chinyengo chamakono chomwe chikugwiritsidwa ntchito kukonzekera kuwuka kwa atatu osayera. Pakuti alipo atatu omwe amachitira umboni kumwamba, Atate, Mawu, ndi Mzimu Woyera: ndipo atatu awa ndi amodzi. Ndipo pali atatu osayera omwe akuwuka (ngakhale chinyengo chachikulu cha atatu omwe amachitira umboni kumwamba): chinjoka, chirombo, ndi mneneri wonama (yemwenso ndi chirombo). Ndipo zilombo zankhanza izi zapangidwa kuti ziwonongedwe, pakuti tsogolo lawo ndi moto woyaka ndi sulufule m'nyanja yamoto yomwe imayaka malawi oyera a buluu mu mdima wandiweyani kosatha.

Ndikulankhula za mkhalidwe wawo wamakono ndi ulamuliro wawo wapano, pakuti sanavumbulutsidwe kwathunthu, pakuti woletsa adakalipo; koma ndidzawadziwitsa kwa okhulupirika anga, ngakhale omwe ali ndi maso owona ndi makutu omva. Mvetsetsani kuti onyenga awa amagwira ntchito yachinyengo chachikulu ngakhale asanavumbulutsidwe ndi kupatsidwa mphamvu yeniyeni, pakuti adzapatsidwa ulamuliro kuchokera kwa chinjoka; pakuti izi zapatsidwa kwa iye kwa nthawi yochepa kwambiri ndi m'njira yochepa kuti ayesere ndi kuyesa dziko lapansi, ndi kuchita nkhondo ndi oyera mtima ndi kuwagonjetsa. Ndipo ngakhale chinsinsi cha kusayeruzika chikugwira ntchito mwamphamvu pa zolinga izi, kuvumbulutsidwa kwawo sikunafike koma kuli pafupi.

Pakuti ngakhale tsopano akuwoneka ngati anthu akulu, anthu abwino, anthu olimba mtima ndi othandiza, omwe amakhala moyo wotumikira mopanda dyera. Koma musanyengedwe ndi abusa amphere, pakuti akuwoneka oyera ndi angwiro kwa unyinji omwe adzawadabwira kale ndipo posachedwapa adzati: Ndani angafanane ndi chirombo? (Pakuti ngakhale tsopano akusocheretsa otayika, omwe asangalatsidwa ndi ufiti wawo.) Koma kwa omwe ali ndi maso owona ndi makutu omva, akuwoneka monga momwe alili: pakuti ndi abusa amphere omwe amalandira mphamvu zawo kuchokera kwa chinjoka chachikulu, ngakhale Satana mwiniwake. Pakuti wina adzatuluka m'nyanja ndipo panopa akugwira ntchito mwachinsinsi (kumbuyo kwa chithunzi cha chidole chokalamba) kuti awononge Babulo Wachinsinsi; ndipo winayo adzatuluka m'dziko lapansi ndipo atumizidwa monama kwa omwe ali m'Dziko Lolonjezedwa ndipo adzakhala mneneri wonama wa sunagoge wa Satana, ngakhale Moshiach wawo wonama.

Pakuti mneneri wonama wachirombo ali mgwirizano ndi chirombo, chomwe chimalamulira mwachinsinsi, ndipo onse awiri akugwira ntchito limodzi kuti apititse patsogolo chinsinsi cha kusayeruzika, kuti posachedwapa avumbulutsidwe; pakuti nthawi zawo zafika, ngakhale alibe ufumu pakali pano. Musagonjere ku chinyengo, pakuti mbusa wamphere mmodzi yemwe amalamulira mwachinsinsi amayendetsa chidole chokalamba, pamene mbusa wamphere winayo yemwe akuwoneka ngati akuzunzidwa ndi chidole chokalamba akugwira ntchito limodzi ndi mbusa wamphere yemwe amalamulira mwachinsinsi. Pakuti amanamizira kuti ndi adani pa nthawi ino, koma onse akuchokera ku gulu limodzi lamphere, kukwaniritsa zolinga za chinjoka.

Mwachisoni, ambiri omwe akuganiza kuti apulumutsidwa povomereza mopanda chikhulupiriro Yesu ngati Ambuye kuchokera mu mtima wosakhulupirira pa milomo yosakhulupirira (pakuti ndi mtima munthu amakhulupirira mpaka chilungamo) asangalatsidwa ndi ziwanda izi, ndipo adzanyenga osankhidwa anga ngati n'kotheka, ngakhale omwe amawatsatira mosazindikira. Pakuti abusa awa sali oyera, ndipo mikanjo yawo yapangidwa ndi ubweya womwe ndi wodetsedwa ndi wodwala, pakuti ndi abusa amphere omwe akuweta gulu lamphere, ndipo mudzawazindikira ndi zipatso zawo.

Sali oyera ndi oyera ngati matalala, ngati nkhosa zanga; pakuti ndine Mwanawankhosa wopanda banga ndi chilema, yemwe anasankhidwa kuchokera mu gulu kuti akhale Paskha wangwiro wa nthawi zonse. Ndimayeretsa nkhosa zanga m'magazi anga owombola, ndipo mphere sizipezeka mwa izo. Ngakhale amanena kuti atumizidwa ndi Mulungu Wamphamvuyonse, Mulungu Wamphamvuyonse sanawatume. Ngakhale aneneri ambiri onama amachitira umboni wonama za iye, sindinamutume, koma chinjoka. Pakuti ndine mkulu wa ansembe kosatha monga mwa dongosolo la Melikizedeki, ndipo palibe ana ankhosa ena koma ine, Khristu Yesu.

Chipulumutso chili m'dzina langa lokha, pakuti palibe dzina lina lopatsidwa pakati pa anthu lomwe anthu angapulumutsidwe nalo kupatula langa, Ambuye Yesu. Pakuti palibe munthu mmodzi yemwe ndikufuna kuti alandire Mawu anga yemwe sadzawamva. Ngati alandira mawu anga mwachikhulupiriro kapena ngati awakanira mopanda chikhulupiriro ndi chisankho chomwe Atate wapatsa mphamvu aliyense kuti apange, pakuti Mulungu sadzikakamiza yekha ndi chikondi chake pa munthu aliyense. Ndipo ndikubwera kudzapereka chiweruzo pa onse ndi kutsutsa onse omwe ndi osapembedza pakati pawo, pa ntchito zawo zonse zosapembedza zomwe achita mosapembedza, ndi pa mawu awo onse ouma, omwe ochimwa osapembedza alankhula motsutsana naye.

Ndalankhula nanu m'njira zambiri, ndipo m'ntchito zanga zambiri ndatumiza aneneri anga ndi atumiki anga, pakuti nthawi yatha. Ndipo choncho ndidzalankhula za zinthu zomwe sizinatsimikizidwe ku mibadwo yapitayi koma tsopano zavumbulutsidwa, pakuti nthawi za kuvumbulutsidwa kwanga zafika. Pakuti ndatsala pang'ono kuvumbulutsidwa kuchokera kumwamba, ngakhale ine, pakuti ndine Mesiya weniweni, ngakhale Yeshua HaMashiach. Ndipo ndidzalankhula m'mafanizo kwa iwo omwe ali kunja, koma omwe ali ndi maso owona ndi makutu omva adzamvetsetsa, pakuti ndidzatsimikizira mu mzimu wanga womwe unalengedwa mwa iwo.

Ndabwera kudzayankhula za mawu omwe Yohane Wovumbulutsa anapatsidwa, ndipo ndidzakufotokozerani njira momveka bwino ngati mulandira Mawu anga ndi kudzichepetsa mitima yanu ku mawu anga.

Taonani! Ndikulankhula za olamulira awiri omwe akubwera omwe akugwira ntchito limodzi, ndipo adzanyenga Dziko lapansi ndi kutenga ambiri kupita nawo kumoto wa phompho. Ngakhale omwe mayina awo sanalembedwe m'Buku la Moyo adzawatsatira ndipo adzalumidwa ndi njoka, zomwe dziko lapansi lachenjezedwa, ngakhale chizindikiro chomwe palibe munthu adzachiritsidwa; pakuti machenjezo ndi omaliza, ndipo zomwe Mulungu walengeza sizingasinthidwe.

Atsogoleri awa, ngakhale abusa amphere, panopa akudziwonetsera ngati atumiki a boma a Babulo Wachinsinsi. Pakuti pansi pa mikanjo ya abusa amphere yomwe amavala (pakuti aliyense wabisala m'njira yake), ndi zilombo zolusa zomwe chilakolako chawo sichingakhutitsidwe, ndipo adzafuna kuti onse awapembedze ndi fano la chirombo ndi chinjoka ngati kuti ndi Mulungu; pakuti atsala pang'ono kumasulidwa pamene woletsa achotsedwa.

Pakuti posachedwapa ndi modzidzimutsa, pamene ndidzasonkhanitsa Mpingo wanga kwa ine m'mitambo, chiwonongeko chodzidzimutsa chidzagwera dziko lapansi; pakuti adzawononga Babulo Wachinsinsi, ndipo nkhondo yaikulu idzabuka m'dziko lonse ndipo chiwonongeko chodzidzimutsa chidzagwera dziko lapansi. Atatu osayera adzauka kuchokera ku phulusa la mabwinja ndi chisokonezo chosiyidwa ndi okwera pamahatchi, omwe posachedwapa adzamasulidwa pambuyo poti Mkwatibwi wanga watengedwa kwa ine kumwamba. (Pakuti pokhapokha m'pamene ndidzatsegula zisindikizo za chipangano changa pakuti Mpingo wanga wapulumutsidwa ku mkwiyo womwe ukubwera, ndipo ndidzawapulumutsa.) Adzauka kuchokera ku phulusa la nkhondo, mliri, njala, ndi chisalungamo chachikulu, ndi ku imfa za unyinji wambiri.

Adzapereka mtendere ndi chitetezo, ndipo adzalankhula mabodza ambiri omwe adzawapereka ngati nzeru za Mulungu, ndipo adzalankhula zinthu zambiri zamwano ndi zowononga; ndipo wina adzaloza winayo, ndipo m'manja mwawo mudzakhala yankho lawo, ngakhale poizoni wawo yemwe sangachotsedwe kapena kukhululukidwa. Pakuti aliyense amene adzipereka ku kulumidwa kwa njoka, adzakhala wolembedwa chizindikiro kosatha. Koma uwu udzakhala mtengo wolowera m'gulu lawo latsopano, ngakhale dongosolo lawo latsopano lopangidwa ndi Satana ndi angelo ake lomwe akupangira mbewu yawo yonyansa mopusa.

Adzalonjeza gulu latsopano lopanda imfa ndi chiwonongeko chomwe chikubwera posachedwa pa dziko lonse lapansi, pamene chiwonongeko chodzidzimutsa chidzafika pa kusonkhanitsidwa kwa Mpingo wanga. (Akufuna kuyambitsanso nthawi yawo yagolide yomwe Mulungu anawononga mu chigumula cha Nowa.) Adzalonjeza mtendere, moyo wautali, kumasuka ku matenda, thanzi ndi chitetezo, ndipo adzalonjeza tsiku latsopano. Koma abusa awiri ampherewa (omwe akuwoneka ngati akuchokera ku gulu) adzanena kuti Mulungu Wamphamvuyonse wawatuma, ndipo dziko lonse lapansi lidzawadabwira, koma si abusa. Ndikukufunsani, kodi chinjoka chingawete gulu la nkhosa? Kodi chinjoka chinawetapo nkhosa liti, kupatula kusindikiza chiwonongeko chawo chotheratu ndi kulekanitsidwa ndi Atate wa Kuwala, ngakhale Yahweh? Pakuti chirombo ndi mneneri wonama adzalandira mphamvu zawo kuchokera kwa chinjoka, ndipo ndi akapolo a zilakolako ndi chifuniro cha chinjoka.

Pa nthawi ya kuvumbulutsidwa kwawo, adzataya zovala zawo zachinyengo za atumiki a boma otsika; ndipo kudzikuza konse, ngakhale mikanjo yawo yamphere ya zikopa zankhosa zamphere ndi chikhalidwe chawo chenicheni, mphamvu, ndi zolinga zawo zidzavumbulutsidwa. Dziko lapansi lidzawadabwira ndi njira zawo, ngakhale kuwapembedza mpaka chiwonongeko chawo. Adzalowa pa siteji ya dziko lapansi mu mphamvu yaikulu, ndipo mgwirizano wa mafumu khumi udzalamulira. Ngakhale tsopano, mgwirizanowo wakonza kale magulu awo mwachinsinsi kuti amenye nkhondo mwachinsinsi pa Babulo Wachinsinsi. Adzikonzekeretsa mwachinsinsi, ndipo akonza ndithu chiwembu, ndipo alowa poyera mu Babulo Wachinsinsi malinga ndi chiweruzo cholengezedwa ndi Mulungu chomwe chatsala pang'ono kumugwera, chomwe sadzaukanso.

Pakuti zoyipa zake zafika pamaso pa Wam'mwambamwamba, ndi machimo ake pamaso pa mpando wachifumu waukulu wakumwamba, ngakhale pamaso pa Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya, pakuti palibe wamkulu kuposa iye; ndipo chiweruzo chaperekedwa pa iye. Pakuti El Shaddai amagwiritsa ntchito ngakhale manja a adani ake kukwaniritsa mapulani ake ndipo ngakhale adani ake amamvera mawu ake; pakuti ndani angayime motsutsana ndi Mulungu Wamphamvuyonse? Musalakwitse, abusa awiri amphere panopa ali mgwirizano kumbuyo kwa chithunzi cha chidole. Sanavumbulutsidwe ku Dziko lapansi, ndipo akadzavumbulutsidwa, dziko lapansi lidzawadabwira ndi kuwatsatira mpaka imfa yawo. Koma si nkhosa nkomwe, koma ndi zilombo zolusa mkati zomwe posachedwapa zidzapatsidwa mphamvu yaikulu ndi chinjoka, pakuti izi zapatsidwa kwa iye kwa nthawi yochepa. Pakuti adzadziyika okha motsutsana ndi, ndipo m'malo mwa, mbusa wabwino; kupatula omwe ali ndi kuzindikira kwauzimu adzawona mphere, ngakhale kuyipa ndi kuwonongeka kotheratu komwe kuli mwa iwo, pakuti ali ndi chikhalidwe chodetsedwa ndipo ndi oyipa mpaka pansi pa mitima yawo. Mukudziwa kale omwe amanamizira kukhala, koma simukudziwa omwe ali kwenikweni, ndipo adzipatsa ngakhale mayina onama, kotero nambala yawo singawerengedwe.

Pakuti chirombo chomwe chidzatuluka m'nyanja kudzalamulira mitundu chinalipo kale, ndipo chikuwoneka kuti panopa palibe koma kwenikweni chidalipo, ngakhale kutsogolera manja a chidole chokalamba chopanda nzeru. Uyu ndi amene watchulidwa m'Chihebri pogwiritsa ntchito mawu omwe ndinalankhula pamene ndinawona Satana ngati mphezi ikugwa kuchokera kumwamba. Mphezi ikugwa kuchokera kumwamba: phunzirani kuti mudziwe. Pakuti sakhumba akazi, kapena kulemekeza mulungu aliyense: pakuti adzadzikweza pamwamba pa onse. Ndikukufunsani tsopano ndipo onani ngati mwamuna amamva zowawa za kubereka mwana? Koma mkazi wake akuwoneka ngati mkazi ali ndi ana, ngakhale si ana ake.

Chirombo chomwe chidzatuluka m'dziko lapansi mukuchidziwa kale, ndipo ambiri a inu mumachitsatira mosazindikira, ngakhale kunyengedwa kale pamene mumayenera kulalikira motsutsana ndi chinyengocho. Pakuti akuchokera m'dziko, koma m'fuko lake palibe munthu mmodzi wosindikizidwa wa 144,000. Ndipo za Dani anati, Dani ndi mwana wa mkango: adzadumpha kuchokera ku Basana, ndipo akuchokera ku fuko la Dani, ngakhale kunyadira mzera wake wamagazi, pakuti magazi a buluu amayenda m'mitsempha yake. Amadziwika ngati Koresi wamakono ndipo adzakhala womanga kachisi kuti abweretse chonyansa cha chiwonongeko mu nthawi yake, ndipo panopa akumanga kale m'Dziko Lolonjezedwa kwa sunagoge wa Satana.

Ana anga ang'ono, ndikusiyirani nzeru zoti muganizire, ndipo izi sizilinso zinsinsi koma zavumbulutsidwa mu nthawi yawo. Koma nthawi zawo sizidzavumbulutsidwa ku dziko lapansi mpaka woletsa atachotsedwa, ndipo zidzachitika mwachangu kwambiri kuposa momwe mukuganizira. Pakuti zinthu izi zatsala pang'ono kuchitika. ndipo palibenso nthawi, pakuti manambala agwa pa nkhope ya wotchi. Khalani okhulupirika mwa ine, ndipo pamene ndatsimikizira mu mzimu wanu kuti ndi ndani, tembenuzani abale anu. Taonani, kuwonekera kwanga kwatsala pang'ono kukwaniritsidwa.

Ndine Mawu a Mulungu wopangidwa thupi, ndipo ndimakhala mwa inu.

Ndikukuitanirani kunyumba yanu kumwamba posachedwapa.
