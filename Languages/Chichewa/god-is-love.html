Epulo 30, 2024

Mulungu ndiye Chikondi!

Ana anga, mvetsetsani chikhalidwe chanu chenicheni, pakuti chanu ndi chikhalidwe chenicheni choperekedwa ndi Mulungu mwa ine, Khristu Yesu, pakuti Mulungu ndiye chikondi! Komabe, chikondi cha Mulungu sichofanana ndi chikondi cha munthu monga momwe njira za Mulungu sizili njira za munthu; koma mukuphunzira njira za Mulungu mwa ine, pakuti ndine mphunzitsi ndi mbusa wanu. Pakuti Mulungu anakonda dziko lapansi kotero, kuti anapatsa Mwana wake wobadwa yekha: kuti yense wokhulupirira Iye, asatayike, koma akhale nawo moyo wosatha. Ndipo m'chita ichi cha Mulungu chondipereka ine, Yesu Khristu, mwana wake wobadwa yekha chifukwa cha machimo a anthu, chikondi chake chikuwonetsedwa mu umodzi wangwiro mwa ine. Pakuti ndine wa Atate ndipo wa chikhalidwe chomwecho ndi mzimu womwewo, pakuti Mulungu ndiye mzimu, ndipo omupembedza Iye ayenera kumupembedza mu mzimu ndi m'choonadi, pakuti Ine ndi Atate wanga ndife amodzi mu mzimu, umodzi, ndi cholinga.

Mvetsetsani kuti chikondi cha Mulungu sichofanana ndi chikondi cha munthu, pakuti chikondi cha munthu chimakhala cha zikhalidwe, ndipo munthu wakugwa amakonda kuwonetsa chikondi chake popereka ndi kulandira zabwino, ndipo chikondi cha munthu chimatembenukira mkati kuti chipindule munthu amene amakonda, pakuti chotero ndi chikondi cha munthu. Chikondi cha Mulungu nthawi zambiri chimawonetsedwa popereka mwaulere kuti wina apindule, ndi kutumikira mnzanu wopanda malipiro nthawi zina, ndi kuyendera amasiye, kuchiritsa odwala, kumanga Thupi la Khristu. Chotero ndikukupemphani, ana anga, kuti muphunzire za ine Khristu Yesu, pakuti iye amene wandiona ine waona Atate, ndipo ngakhale simunandionebe ndi maso anu, posachedwapa mundiona.

Pakuti wodala ndi amene sanawone koma amakhulupirira, ndipo mwandiwaona mu mzimu ndi m'choonadi, ndipo mwandiwaona m'njira zanga ndi mwa chikondi chomwe ndimatumiza ndi kudzera mu ntchito yomwe atumiki anga amandigwirira chifukwa cha inu. Chotero mumandidziwa ndipo mwandiwaona, ndipo pamene muyenda mu mzimu wa Khristu womwe umabadwa mkati, mukuyendadi m'chikondi, pakuti chotero ndi chikhalidwe changa chomwe ndakupatsani, ndipo chotero ndi Atate wanga amene wapereka chikhalidwe chake kwa ine: Onani, chikondi chotani chomwe Atate watipatsa, kuti titchedwe ana a Mulungu: chifukwa chake dziko lapansi silotidziwa, chifukwa silinamudziwe Iye. Ndipo kachiwiri kuchokera ku kalata yoyamba ya Yohane: Mmenemo muli chikondi, sikuti ife tinakonda Mulungu, koma kuti Iye anatikonda ife, ndipo anatumiza Mwana wake kukhala nsembe yoyanjanitsa machimo athu. Okondedwa, ngati Mulungu anatikonda ife chotero, ifenso tiyenera kukondana wina ndi mzake.

Tiana twanga, inu ndinu ana a Mulungu wamoyo, ngakhale Yahweh, pakuti monga momwe Yahweh anabzala munda kum'mawa mu Edeni, wabzala mbewu za mzimu wa umwana mu umunthu wanu weniweni, pakuti ndine wamunda wake amene amasamalira miyoyo yanu. Pakuti mwalengedwanso mwa ine, ndipo mwalengedwa m'chikondi, ndipo chikondi cha Mulungu chili mu chikhalidwe chanu chenicheni. Mvetsetsani kuti chiwombolo chanu chokwanira chili pakhomo pamene mudzadziwa monga momwe mukudziwidwira. Mkwatibwi wanga, ndili ndi zambiri zoti ndikufotokozereni ndi kukuphunzitsani ndi kukuwululirani za inu nokha ndi zolinga zomwe mwayeretsedwa kuti mukwaniritse, pakuti chakhala chinsinsi mu mtima wa Atate kuyambira chilengedwe chisanakhale, ndipo posachedwa mudzamvetsetsa.

Nanga ndani adzakulekanitsani ndi chikondi cha Mulungu? Kodi ndi chisautso, kapena kupsinjika, kapena chizunzo, kapena njala, kapena umaliseche, kapena zoopsa, kapena lupanga? Iyai, ndinu opambana mwa ine, Khristu Yesu, ndipo chikondi changa chikufikitsidwa pakukwaniritsidwa kwathunthu mwa inu, Mkwatibwi wanga, pakuti nthawi zili pafupi ndipo posachedwa mudzadziwa. Chikondi cha Atate wanga nchosagonjetseka, pakuti Iye ndi wosagonjetseka, pakuti Mulungu ndiye chikondi, ndipo chikondi ndi chikhalidwe chenicheni cha Yahweh pakuti Mulungu ndiye chikondi. Chimodzi mwa zinyengo zambiri za Satana zomwe amagwiritsa ntchito kukusocheretsani ndikuwonetsa chikondi ngati chofooka, chosadzipereka, chodzikonda komanso chopanda phindu: Pakuti izi ndi njira za munthu wachibadwa.

Zoonadi, Satana akukana, pakuti ndi iye amene wapangidwa kukhala wopanda phindu, ndipo chikondi cha Atate wanga sichingagonjetsedwe, pakuti palibe wina wofanana naye! Mwa ine chikondi chake chinakwaniritsidwa m'thupi, pakuti chinali chifukwa cha chikondi changa kwa Atate wanga ndi kwa inu, Mkwatibwi wanga, kuti ndinadzipereka ndekha ngati dipo lopanda uchimo; pakuti mumakondedwa kwambiri. Ndipo dziwani kuti ndinu mwana wa diso la Yahweh, ndipo inu, Mkwatibwi wanga, ndinu mwana wa diso langa, ndipo m'chikondi ndinakuwombolani m'manja mwa mdani mwangwiro, popanda chosowa, m'mwazi wanga wotetezera. Oomboledwa a Ambuye anene chotero, amene Iye wawawombola m'manja mwa mdani!

Ana anga, popeza Mulungu amakukondani chotero, ndiye kuti muyenera kuvomereza chikondi chake ndi kuvomereza mawu ake okhudza inu ndi chipulumutso champhamvu chomwe Atate anachita mu nsembe yanga ngati Paskha wanu wangwiro. Ambiri a inu samamvetsetsa chimaliziro cha chilengedwe chomwe ndakupangani mwa ine, Khristu Yesu, ndi mu mzimu womwe ndalenga mkati mwanu, pakuti mzimuwo ndi wamphamvu, ndipo ndi chikondi; pakuti ndi chikhalidwe chenicheni cha Atate, pakuti Yahweh ndiye chikondi. Onani, palibe munthu ali ndi chikondi choposa ichi, chakuti munthu ataya moyo wake chifukwa cha abwenzi ake. Ndipo ndinataya moyo wanga chifukwa cha abwenzi anga, ndipo potero ndakupangani kukhala abale anga ndipo ndakupangani kukhala ana a Mulungu: Onani, chikondi chotani chomwe Atate watipatsa, kuti titchedwe ana a Mulungu: chifukwa chake dziko lapansi silikudziwani, chifukwa silikumudziwa Iye.

Imvani Mawu anga omwe ndinapatsa Yohane mu kalata yake yoyamba, ndipo muwamve bwino. Yahweh wakupatsani chisomo chachikulu kwambiri motsutsana ndi woneneza ndi motsutsana ndi omwe amakutsutsani, ndipo wapanga zoneneza zawo kukhala zopanda maziko motsutsana nanu, pakuti mwagulidwa m'mwazi wanga wopanda uchimo, ndipo mwamasulidwa ku milandu yonse mu ntchito yanga yotetezera. Kodi simunawerenge zomwe ndinauza Aroma ma Yubile ambiri apitawo? Ndani adzaneneza osankhidwa a Mulungu? Ndi Mulungu amene amayesa olungama: Ndani amene amatsutsa? Ndi Khristu amene anamwada, inde makamaka amene anauka, amene ali kudzanja lamanja la Mulungu, amenenso amatiyankhera. Kodi simunamve umboni wa Ahebri, kumene mawu awo amachitira umboni za unsembe wanga wosasinthika? Chifukwa chake akhoza kupulumutsa kwathunthu iwo amene abwera kwa Mulungu kudzera mwa iye, poona kuti amakhala ndi moyo nthawi zonse kuti awapempherere.

Imvani mawu awa, ndipo aloleni alowe pansi pa mtima wanu, ndipo imvani mawu omwe ndinapatsa mpingo wa ku Roma: Pakuti Iye anampanga iye kukhala uchimo chifukwa cha ife, amene sanadziwe uchimo, kuti ife tipangidwe chilungamo cha Mulungu mwa iye. Kodi mawu awa ndi odabwitsa kwambiri kwa inu? Kodi mudzilola nokha kumvetsetsa, kapena mudzatseka makutu anu mu miyambo yanu yopanda phindu ndi maumboni onama opanda phindu omwe mumagwiritsitsa, operekedwa ndi aneneri onama opanda phindu opangidwa kuti akusocheretseni? Kodi ndi chinthu chovuta kwambiri kuvomereza kuti mwapangidwa kukhala chilungamo cha Mulungu mwa ine, Khristu Yesu?

Ndikufuna kuti mukhale chete mu malingaliro ndi mtima wanu ndikulola mawu anga kukhazika mtima pansi mzimu wanu ndikumva mawu anga. Fewetsani mitima yanu ndikugwetsa kunyada kwanu; pakuti ndili ndi chinthu choti ndikuuzeni chomwe chidzakumasulani ku ukapolo ngati mungokhulupirira mawu enieni omwe ndikupatsani kachiwiri. Pakuti mawu a Aroma amalankhula kwa inu pamene mukuwerenga, choncho werengani ndikumvetsetsa; ndipo pakumvetsetsa, landirani choonadi cha kaimidwe kanu mwa ine, Khristu Yesu. Ndipo mawu anga ndi awa: Chifukwa chake tsopano palibe kutsutsidwa kwa iwo amene ali mwa Khristu Yesu, amene sayenda monga mwa thupi, koma monga mwa mzimu. Pakuti lamulo la mzimu wa moyo, mwa Khristu Yesu, landimasula ku lamulo la uchimo ndi imfa. Musalole maso anu kungoyang'ana mawuwo, koma werengani lililonse la iwo pang'onopang'ono ndi chikhulupiriro, chikhulupiriro mwa ine, pakuti ndalankhula mawu awa pa inu, ana anga; ndipo ine, Khristu Yesu, ndakumasulani ku kutsutsidwa konse mwa ine. Kodi simukumvetsetsa kuti mwapangidwa kukhala chilungamo cha Mulungu? Ndimvetsetseni ndipo imvani mawu anga ndipo mverani mawu anga: Ndani amene amakutsutsani? Ndi Khristu amene anamwada, inde makamaka amene anauka, amene ali kudzanja lamanja la Mulungu, amenenso amatiyankhera.

Ana anga, sindinakuitanireni kwa ine ndekha m'chilungamo ndi chiyero chenicheni kuti ndikutsutseni, ndipo muyenera kumvetsetsa choonadi: Mulibe ufulu wodzitsutsa nokha, ndipo ambiri a inu mumatero, ndipo miyoyo yanu imazunzika chifukwa cha kudzida kumeneku komwe mdani waika pa inu. Ndiloleni ndidule maunyolo onama awa a mdani, ndipo imvani mawu anga ndikuwakhulupirira, pakuti ndi oona, ndipo alembedwa chifukwa cha inu kuti mukhale omasuka mwa ine. Pakuti ndakumasulani ku kutsutsidwa konse, ndipo mdani ndi woneneza wa abale aponyedwa pansi: Tsopano kwadza chipulumutso, ndi mphamvu, ndi ufumu wa Mulungu wathu, ndi ulamuliro wa Khristu wake: pakuti woneneza wa abale athu aponyedwa pansi, amene anawaneneza pamaso pa Mulungu wathu usana ndi usiku.

Mvetsetsani, ana anga, kuti ndinu olungama, ndipo kudzitsutsa nokha ndikunyalanyaza mawu anga omwe Atate wakupatsani; choncho musakhale opanda chikhulupiriro, koma khalani ndi chikhulupiriro chachikulu m'mawu anga, pakuti ndi oona, ndipo palibe umboni woona woposa womwe waperekedwa. Mvetsetsani kusiyana pakati pa kudzitsutsa ndi mzimu wanga wogwira ntchito mwa inu kuti ukutsutseni za uchimo; pakuti ndinamuuza chiyani Yohane mu kalata yake yoyamba: Pakuti ngati mtima wathu utitsutsa, Mulungu ndi wamkulu kuposa mtima wathu, ndipo amadziwa zinthu zonse. Okondedwa, ngati mtima wathu susutsa ife, ndiye kuti tili ndi chidaliro kwa Mulungu. Kodi simukumvetsetsa kuti mzimu wanga womwe ndalenga mkati mwa aliyense wa inu, Mkwatibwi wanga, umakutsutsani za machimo anu ngakhale mutabadwanso mwa ine, Khristu Yesu, Ambuye wanu?

Kodi simunawerenge umboni wanga wolembedwa mu uthenga wabwino wa Yohane wokhudza wotonthoza? Ndipo pamene iye abwera, adzadzudzula dziko lapansi za uchimo, ndi za chilungamo, ndi za chiweruzo. Za uchimo, chifukwa sakhulupirira mwa ine. Za chilungamo, chifukwa ndikupita kwa Atate wanga, ndipo simundionanso. Za chiweruzo, chifukwa kalonga wa dziko lino woweruzidwa. Ana anga, wotonthoza anakutsutsani za uchimo, ndipo mwatembenukira kwa ine, ndipo mu kubadwa kwatsopano ndi mu mphamvu ya mzimu womwe ndalenga mkati mwanu, ngakhale mzimu wa umwana, ngakhale Khristu mwa inu chiyembekezo cha ulemerero, ndakupangani kukhala chilungamo cha Mulungu mwa ine. Chifukwa chake, simutsutsidwanso za uchimo, koma mumatsutsidwa za chilungamo pamene muchimwa, ngakhale pamene mutembenukira ku njira zanu zakale, pakuti njoka imakonda kusocheretsa anthu anga ndiyeno kubweretsa mlandu pamaso pa Atate wanga ndi pamaso panga pa ntchito zake zosapembedza, ndipo ndine mkhalapakati wanu.

Pakuti kachiwiri, umboni wa Yohane mu kalata yake yoyamba umalankhula pa nkhaniyi: Tiana twanga, zinthu izi ndikukulemberani, kuti musachimwe. Ndipo ngati munthu aliyense achimwa, tili ndi nkhoswe kwa Atate, Yesu Khristu wolungama: Ndipo iye ndiye nsembe yoyanjanitsa machimo athu: ndipo osati athu okha, komanso machimo a dziko lonse lapansi. Ana anga, ndine nkhoswe yanu, ndipo ndinu olungama mwa ine, ndipo ndinu olungama mpaka kalekale, pakuti chilungamo chabadwa mu chikhalidwe chanu chenicheni. Choncho khalani ndi mtima wofewa kwa ine, ndipo ndidzakuwonetsani machimo anu ndi zolakwa zanu, ngakhale zizolowezi zanu zoipa ndi njira zanu zoipa zakale zomwe nthawi zina mumayesedwa kuti mubwerere. Pakuti pamene mtima wanu ukutsutsani, tembenukirani kwa ine ndikuvomereza machimo anu, pakuti lemba limati chiyani: Koma ngati tiyenda m'kuwala, monga iye ali m'kuwala, tili ndi chiyanjano wina ndi mzake, ndipo mwazi wa Yesu Khristu Mwana wake umatiyeretsa ku uchimo wonse. Ngati tinena kuti tilibe uchimo, timadzinyenga tokha, ndipo choonadi mulibe mwa ife. Ngati tivomereza machimo athu, iye ndi wokhulupirika, ndi wolungama kuti atikhululukire machimo athu, ndi kutiyeretsa ku chosalungama chonse.

Ana anga, ndakuweruzani olungama mwa ine, ndipo Yahweh wakumasulani ku milandu yonse mwa ine; chifukwa chiyani simudzalola nokha kukhulupirira mawu anga achisomo ndi achikondi? Pakuti chikondi cha Mulungu chikuwonetsedwa mwa iwo komanso chikondi changa kwa Mkwatibwi wanga komanso dziko lonse lapansi, pakuti aliyense wofuna akhoza kubwera, ingobwerani chitseko chisanatsekedwe pakuti nthawi yatha. Bwererani kwa ine tsiku ndi tsiku ndikudziyeretsa m'mwazi wanga wotetezera, pakuti nsembe yanga ndi yangwiro ndipo ndi ya kamodzi kokha: Pakuti Ahebri amanena momveka bwino za ine: Amene pokhala kuwala kwa ulemerero, ndi chifaniziro chenicheni cha umunthu wake, ndi kunyamula zinthu zonse ndi mawu ake amphamvu, atayeretsa machimo athu mwa iye yekha, wakhala kudzanja lamanja la Ulemerero m'malo am'mwamba. Ana anga, mvetsetsani kuti wotonthoza wadzudzulanso dziko lapansi za chiweruzo, pakuti kalonga wa dziko lino woweruzidwa, ndipo chilango chake ndi choyenera. Koma mpaka mphindi ino akufuna kukukokerani pansi mu kutsutsidwa kwake koyenera, choncho amabwera kudzadzudzula inu; choncho musamvere mawu ake ndikukana mabodza ake.

Tiana twanga, yendani m'chikondi cha Mulungu mwa ine, Khristu Yesu ambuye wanu. Kondani Atate ndipo ndikondeni posunga malamulo anga, pakuti Yohane Woyamba amati chiyani? Iye amene anena, Ndimamudziwa iye, ndipo sasunga malamulo ake, ndi wabodza, ndipo choonadi mulibe mwa iye. Koma aliyense amene asunga mawu ake, mwa iye zoonadi chikondi cha Mulungu chakwaniritsidwa: mwa ichi tidziwa kuti tili mwa iye. Iye amene anena kuti amakhala mwa iye, ayenera iyenso kuyenda chotero, monga momwe iye anayendera. Ndikukulamulani, Mkwatibwi wanga, kuti mupitirize kuyenda monga ndinayendera ndikusunga malamulo anga, pakuti nthawi zonse ndimachita chifuniro cha Atate wanga, ndipo inunso muyenera kuyesetsa nthawi zonse kuchita chifuniro changa, pakuti changa ndi chifuniro cha Yahweh. Posunga malamulo anga mudzayenera kutembenukira kwa abale, pakuti Yohane Woyamba amati chiyani? Mwa ichi tidziwa kuti timakonda ana a Mulungu, pamene tikonda Mulungu ndikusunga malamulo ake. Pakuti ichi ndi chikondi cha Mulungu, kuti tisunge malamulo ake, ndipo malamulo ake sali olemetsa. Imvani mawu anga ndikutsatira mawu anga mpaka kumapeto kwa kumvera, ndikupitiriza kukonda abale anu ndikutumikira m'chikondi, osati chifukwa cha phindu loipa; pakuti mphoto yanu ili m'manja mwanga, osati m'manja mwa omwe mumawatumikira.

Musalole kuti chilichonse chichitidwe kudzera mu mkangano kapena ulemerero wopanda pake; koma mu kudzichepetsa kwa malingaliro aliyense aone wina kukhala wabwino kuposa iye mwini.

Ndine mphunzitsi ndi mbusa wanu.

Ndine wa Atate ndipo wa chikhalidwe chomwecho ndi mzimu womwewo.

Ndine chilungamo cha Mulungu, ndipo ndakupangani kukhala chilungamo cha Mulungu.

Ndine amene ndili wolungamitsidwa m'njira zanga.

Ndine wopanda uchimo, ndipo ndakulungamitsani mwa ine, Khristu Yesu.

Ndine amene ndili woyeretsedwa nthawi zonse ndi mpaka kalekale, pakuti wanga ndi Ufumu wa Mulungu, ndipo ndakuyeretsani ndi mzimu wa umwana.

Ndine amene ndinapereka dontho langa lomaliza la mwazi pamtanda kuti ndikuyeretseni ku uchimo wonse, pakuti palibe uchimo mu mzimu wa umwana womwe ndakupatsani.

Ndine mkhalapakati wanu.

Ndine nkhoswe yanu.

Khalani okonzeka, Mkwatibwi wanga, pakuti nthawi za kubwezeretsedwa zili pafupi, ndipo kuwonekera kwanga kuli pafupi kuposa momwe mukuganizira.
