Imvani Imvani Mawu a Yeshua HaMashiach pamene mukutengedwa kupita kumwamba mu Nyengo yanu ya TSOPANO!

Imvani Imvani Mawu a Yeshua HaMashiach pamene ndikubwera kudzauza mpingo wanga, thupi langa ngakhale mkwatibwi wanga, (omwe ndi 144,000 anga ngakhale inu nonse ana otengedwa a Atate wanu Yeshua HaMashiach) kuti munalengedwa ndi kupangidwa ndi luso lonse la Adonai-YHVH Mwiniwake ndipo nonse ndinu ntchito zake zaluso ngakhale inu nonse okhulupirika mwa Khristu Yesu oitanidwa chisanakhazikitsidwe maziko a dziko lapansi!

Imvani Imvani Mawu a Yeshua HaMashiach kuti mumvetsetse kuti ineyo pandekha ndakulera inu nonse 144,000 ngati ana anga, chotero imvani Mawu anga ndipo mudzimvetsetse, ana anga. Pakuti indetu indetu, palibe zolengedwa ngati inu zoti zipangidwe mwadzidzidzi ndi mosayembekezereka tsopano, palibe ngakhale pang'ono paliponse m'chilengedwe chonse cha El Shaddai, indetu ndinu apadera.

Chotero imvani Mawu anga ndipo mvetsetsani ulamuliro wawo wamphamvu, kuti ndakonza Mawu awa kuchokera m'kabuku kotseguka kamene ndikatsegula m'dzanja la mthenga ndi mtumiki wanga kuti inu nonse 144,000 tsopano muyambe kumvetsetsa umunthu wanu weniweni ndi zolinga zomwe zavumbulutsidwa pang'ono koma zambiri zobisika kwa inu kumwamba kuti zivumbulutsidwe pa nthawi yokumana yomwe yafika pa dziko lonse lapansi TSOPANO, mu nyengo ya TSOPANO yomwe dziko lapansi TSOPANO layimitsidwa!

Chifukwa ndinapangitsa Biblaridion:144 kukweza manja ake polengeza ndi kulengeza kuti nthawi sidzakhalaponso, ndipo TSOPANO zili choncho mu mphamvu yayikulu ndi ulamuliro woperekedwa ndi kumasulidwa ndi mthenga ndi mtumiki wanga wamphamvu. Sela.

Kuti kenako aneneredwe ndi Mawu awa a ulamuliro wamphamvu omwe tsopano atulutsidwa pa dziko lapansi pa lamulo langa ndi Biblaridion:144 omwe adzapeza maso, makutu, ndi mitima ya onse omwe atumizidwa kwa iwo!

Ndipo ndi kudzera mu uthenga uwu womwe tsopano ndauphatikiza ndi kuumasulira kwa ana anga mwachindunji. Koma komanso kuti okhulupirika onse mwa Khristu Yesu ayambe kumvetsetsa ukulu wa zomwe musinthidwe TSOPANO, ndi zolinga zotani komanso mapeto otani.
