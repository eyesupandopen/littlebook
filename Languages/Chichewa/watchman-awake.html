Mlonda Galamuka!

Mlonda, galamuka, ndipo manga m'chiuno mwako ndi choonadi, ndipo vala chapachifuwa cha chilungamo!

Mlonda, galamuka, ndipo tenga chisoti cha chipulumutso ndi lupanga la mzimu, lomwe ndi Mawu a Mulungu!

Mlonda, galamuka, ndipo mapazi ako akhale okonzekera Uthenga Wabwino wamtendere!

Chenjeza anthu anga ndipo usaletse lilime lako, pakuti ndalimasula! Menyera nkhondo anthu anga ndipo chenjeza osankhidwa anga, pakuti mphoto ya mneneri udzalandira ngati unena mawu amene ndidzakupatsa. GALAMUKA! Kodi sindinauze anthu anga kuti adikire kubwera kwanga? Kodi nthawi ndi nyengo sizikuoneka ndipo zili pa inu? Choncho, yang'anani kuonekera kwanga, pakuti kuli pafupi. Yang'anani zizindikiro ndi zozizwitsa mumlengalenga, pakuti Atate anga anakonza mayendedwe awo kuyambira pachiyambi, ndipo tsikulo lili lolembedwa. Yang'anani kuwala kwanga mumlengalenga pamene lipenga latsala pang'ono kulira; taonani, ndalengeza zimenezi.

Anthu anga, fufuzani Mawu anga ndipo yang'anani Malemba anga Oyera kuti mumvetsetse kuti ndidzatenga mkwatibwi wanga kumuchotsa pachiweruzo cha ochita zoipa. Ndidzateteza mkwatibwi wanga ndi thupi langa; pakuti ndani anadapo thupi lake, koma kulilera ndi kulikonda? Yang'anani, mverani, onani nyengo, ndipo dziwani kuti nthawi zafika ndipo tsikulo lidzatuluka mwadzidzidzi ngati dzuwa lalikulu lotuluka mwadzidzidzi m'mbali mwa thambo. Sindidzakanidwa, ndipo sindidzaletsedwa. Ngakhale mbala idzayesa, idzabangula ngati mkango; koma ndi mkango wopanda mano chabe. Iye sadzandiletsa, ndipo sindidzakanidwa chuma changa, pakuti ndagula dziko lapansi makamaka anthu anga ndi magazi anga opanda tchimo. Ndipo dzina langa lili pamwamba pa onse kupatula la Atate anga, amene ali wamkulu kuposa ine; ndipo sindidzakanidwa, kutsekerezedwa, kapena kuletsedwa.

Ndikubwera, ndipo sindidzakhala chete, ndipo mkwiyo wanga ukuyaka pa Babulo Wachinsinsi, inde, Babulo Wachinsinsi amene amagulitsa ana ang'onoang'ono ngati malonda m'manja mwa anthu oipa, amene adzaletsedwa. Zikanakhala bwino kuti sakanabadwa kusiyana ndi kubadwa ndikuchita machimo osayeruzika ndi oipitsitsa kwa ana. Zikanakhala bwino kuti mwala wamphero ukolowekedwe m'khosi mwawo, ndipo aponyedwe m'nyanja; koma asungiridwa moto wosatha, ndipo chilungamo chidzachitika posachedwa, popeza mkwiyo wa Mwanawankhosa watsala pang'ono kuphimba dziko lonse lapansi ndipo nsautso ya Yakobo idzayamba pamene woletsa adzachotsedwa.

Khazikikani, anthu anga, ndipo chirimikani mwa ine ndi mtima umodzi ndi cholinga chimodzi. Siyani zinthu zadziko zomwe mwazilakalaka, ndipo ikani ine, Ambuye Yesu Khristu, monga chikhumbo chanu; pakuti ndine nsanja yanu yolimba, thanthwe lachitetezo, ndi woteteza kumbuyo kwanu. Ndani adzabwera kudzalimbana nanu amene angathe kupirira dzanja langa lamphamvu? Osankhidwa anga ndi okhulupirika ali m'manja mwanga otambasulidwa, ndipo palibe amene angakuchotseni kwa ine. Ndinu anga, ndipo muli otetezeka mwa ine; choncho pumulani mwa ine ku ntchito zanu, pakuti nthawi yobwezera idzafika mwadzidzidzi. Kubwezera kwa chiweruzo pa ochita zoipa kwafika, ndipo kubwezera kwa okhulupirika anga kuli pa osankhidwa anga, pakuti ndidzawapatsa mphoto ndi cholowa chawo, pakuti ndawapanga kukhala oyenera.

O, iwe chinjoka chopanda phindu, chopanda mano; iwe njoka yakale: nthawi yako yafika ndipo chiweruzo chili pa iwe. Ngakhale ukuganiza modzikuza kuti udzapambana, ndidzatenga zonse zomwe wasonkhanitsa m'manja mwako akuba, ndipo omwe akukutsatira m'chilakolako chawo chaumbombo adzagawana nawo cholowa chako m'nyanja yamoto, kumene aliyense adzalandira mphoto molingana ndi ntchito zawo mochulukirapo. Mkwiyo wa Mulungu udzakhala pa iwe mpaka kalekale, ndipo mpumulo sudzakhala nawo.

Chilango chachikulu chidzakhala mtonthozi wako, ndipo utsi wonunkha wa sulufule woyaka udzakhala m'mphuno mwako mpaka kalekale, ndipo udzalipira mpaka kalekale. Ndipo utsi wa kuzunzika kwako udzakwera mpaka kalekale, ndipo onse amene akukutsatira kuyambira wamng'ono mpaka wamkulu adzakhala ndi cholowa chawo ndi mphoto yawo pamodzi ndi iwe m'malawi amoto oyera ndi abuluu omwe adzakuphimba mpaka kalekale pamene udzamangidwa ndi kuponyedwa ku tsoka lako. Kudzakhala kulira kwakukulu ndi kukukuta mano. Ndipo mumadziganizira kuti mutha kulimbana ndi Yehova ndi Khristu wake, komabe mudzasandutsidwa chabe koma mnyozo wosatha m'kuzunzika kwanu kosatha, ndipo mudzatsekeredwa mpaka kalekale. Ndipo simudzavulazanso chilengedwe changa, ndipo mudzaiwalika kotheratu, ndipo simudzakumbukiridwa.

Choncho anthu anga, mkwatibwi wanga wokhulupirika amene ndimamukonda kwambiri, pali chiyani choti muwope? Mkwiyo wanga ndi mkwiyo wa Atate wanu si wanu ndipo sudzabwera pa inu, ndipo simudzakhala pachiwopsezo cha imfa yachiwiri. Choncho musawope pakuti ndidzabwezera chilango changwiro pa adani anu, pakuti kubwezera ndi kwanga, atero Ambuye! Kodi simukudziwa kuti mudzaweruza angelo? Kodi simukudziwa kuti mudzaweruza dziko lapansi? Choncho dziyeretseni mwa ine, pakuti ndi magazi anga okha okhululukira machimo omwe adzakupangani kukhala oyenera kuima pamaso panga mu ulemerero. Mangani mawu anga m'mitima yanu, ndipo sungani malemba anga pamphumi panu, ndipo pumulani m'mawu anga akale; pakuti ndi moyo kwa inu, ndipo moyo wanu wabisika mwa ine, ndipo ndidzawuwulula!

O, Babulo Wachinsinsi, mukanamvetsetsa nsembe yamtendere imene Atate anga anakutumizirani, ndipo tsopano mwakukana ine, Yesu wa ku Nazarete, ndipo zachitika malinga ndi malemba oyera kuti anthu oipa ndi onyenga adzaipiraipira. Koma iwe, O, Babulo Wachinsinsi wamkulu, waipitsa utawaleza wa Atate anga wamitundu yambiri umene anapatsa Nowa ndi anthu onse monga lonjezo lakuti sadzawononganso dziko lonse lapansi ndi madzi, ndipo adzasunga mawu ake. Koma iwe, O, Babulo Wachinsinsi, udzawonongedwa mu ola limodzi.

Moto waukulu udzabwera pa iwe; kugwedezeka kwakukulu kudzakugwetsa pansi; nsanja zako zidzagwa; zomangamanga zako zidzasanduka mabwinja, ndipo mafunde akulu adzamiza magombe ako, ndipo ndidzakupatsa mphoto molingana ndi ntchito zako, ndipo chiweruzo chachikulu chili pa iwe. Pakuti nthawi ina unali chizindikiro cha chiyembekezo ndi kuwala kwa dziko lapansi kwa nyengo yochepa, koma wasankha umbombo, dyera, ndi katangale monga anzako ogona nawo, O, hule lalikulu! Ndani adzakupulumutsa kwa ine ndi mkwiyo wanga ndi mkwiyo wanga womwe ukubwera womwe ukuyaka pa iwe? Ukuima m'maunyolo, kuyembekezera chiweruzo chako chomaliza, chomwe chidzaonekera kuti dziko lapansi lione, ndipo mantha akulu adzagwera ambiri.

Taonani! Mitundu yambiri Atate anga aigonjetsa m'chiweruzo, pakuti amakhazikitsa wina ndi kugwetsa wina, pakuti Yehova ndi wolamulira ndipo palibe wamkulu kuposa Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya amene amaimira ndi kupulumutsa ake kumoto wosungiridwa oipa. Taonani! Mitundu yomwe inali maufumu akuluakulu idakalipo, ngakhale m'njira yochepa, koma yapitiriza. Koma iwe, O, hule lalikulu, wadziika monga hule ndi okonda ambiri chifukwa cha umbombo woyipa ndi zosangalatsa za tchimo. Watsogolera anthu anga kutali ndi ine padziko lonse lapansi ndi zonyansa zomwe wapereka kwa mitundu kuti idye ndi kumwa, ndipo udzaponyedwa pansi kotheratu, ndipo sudzakhalakonso. Ndipo m'phulusa lako mtundu waukulu sudzauka, komabe otsala adzapulumutsidwa. Dzanja la mdani lili pakhosi pako, ndipo ndabweretsa zinyalala m'malire ako. Adzafunkha, kulanda, ndi kutenga zomwe zatsala. Ndani adzakuthandiza?

Pakuti wakana mnzako, ndipo mulibe mantha a Ambuye m'mitima yanu. Wanena kuti, Sindine wamasiye, komabe ndiwe wamasiye ndipo ndiwe wakhungu ndi wogontha pamaso panga. Palibe chipulumutso kwa iwe chifukwa cha chigololo chako chomwe wachita ndi dziko lapansi; choncho, ndidzakubweretsera chiwonongeko chotheratu. Zochitika zomwe zikubwera zidzachitika mwachangu ndi mwachangu; kenako chigumula chachikulu chidzaphimba madiresi ako. Moto udzakutentha kotheratu ndipo udzayeretsa ochita zoipa m'dzikolo, ndipo akapolo anga adzamasulidwa, ndipo palibe chomwe chingaletse zomwe zatsala pang'ono kuchitika. Ndine Ambuye Yesu Khristu, ndipo ndikukuuzani zisanachitike, ndipo ndikulengeza zomwe zatsala pang'ono kuchitika ngati kuti zachitika kale, pakuti mawu awa ndi otsimikizika, ndipo palibe chomwe chingawasintha, popeza maulosi adzakwaniritsidwa.

Chirimikani, anthu anga, pakuti ndidzatenga mkwatibwi wanga; pakuti ndili ndi mapulani ndi zolinga pa iye mu ufumu wanga womwe ukubwera. Adzakhala nane nthawi zonse, ndipo sadzachoka kwa ine. Ndimamukonda ndi kumusamalira m'chikondi changwiro ndi chifundo. Ndidzakupangani kukhala amphamvu mu ulemerero wanga, ndipo mudzachita ntchito zamphamvu mu cholowa chanu chomwe chikubwera, choncho musawope. Dziwani kuti mtima wanga ukusweka, ndipo ndili wachisoni ndi anthu anga omwe sanadzikonzekeretse. Ena adzasiyidwa chifukwa mulungu wa dziko lino waphimba mitima yawo, ndipo ambiri sakonzeka. Musawope, ngakhale mudzapirira zambiri chifukwa cha ine, nonse mudzapulumutsidwa omwe muli ndi chidindo cha Mulungu wamoyo. Ndidzakupatsani zosowa zanu ngati simukana dzina langa, ngati, ndipo mukundigwirira ntchito mu ntchito yomwe ndakupatsani.

Mudzechita ntchito zamphamvu ngati mungondipempha kuti ndikupatseni ntchito. Ndakuitanani ndipo ndimakukondani kwambiri, ndipo omwe akuitana pa dzina langa ndi kupirira mpaka mapeto adzapulumutsidwa ndi kuukitsidwa mu ulemerero waukulu ndi kupatsidwa mphoto mpaka kalekale chifukwa cha kuima kwanu, pakuti ndimakukondani ndipo ndayankhula izi m'makutu anu. Pali zokolola zingapo ndipo chilichonse mu nthawi yake, choncho musawope; pakuti nthawi zovuta nthawi zonse zimapatsidwa amuna akulu a Mulungu kuti awete gulu lankhosa, ndipo ndakuitanani. Angelo anga adzaika chidindo pa 144,000, ndipo ena a inu mudzawathandiza mu ntchito zawo ndi ntchito yomwe ndawapatsa. Musawope, pakuti ndidzachita zizindikiro zambiri, zozizwitsa, ndi zodabwitsa ndi manja anu, choncho pumulani m'chikondi changa ndi kupumula mu mphamvu yanga; pakuti ndili nanu ndipo sindidzakusiyani kapena kukutayani.

Mudzodziwa kuti ndatenga mkwatibwi wanga, ndipo ndidzakhala nditasonkhanitsa okhulupirika kwa ine kuti abweretse kulapa kwa ambiri m'dziko lomwe likufa. Choncho lolani dziko lapansi lipite ndipo musagawane nawo tsoka lake ndi tsogolo lake pakuti si lanu ngati mudzayeretsa Ambuye m'mitima yanu ndi kukhala moyo wopatulidwa kwa ine. Pakuti choyipa chachikulu chikubwera, ndipo sichinauke mwanjira iyi, ndipo sichidzaukanso, ana anga ang'ono, pakuti ndachilengeza. Tonzeredwani m'njira yomwe ndakukonzerani, ndipo sungani mapazi anu okhazikika m'njira. Ndidzakhala nanu ndipo ndidzakupatsani mtendere womwe upambana kumvetsetsa, ndipo mudzakhala ndi mtendere pakati pa dziko la chisokonezo, mtendere womwe udzakusungani ngati simutembenukira kumanzere kapena kumanja.

Musatenge chizindikiro, ndipo musakhulupirire mabodza. Adzapereka mtendere ndi thanzi ndi chitetezo kwa omwe atenga chizindikiro, ndipo adzakuuzani kuti mukulowa m'dziko latsopano lamtendere ndi chitukuko ndi chitetezo. Musakhulupirire mabodza a njoka, pakuti mfumu yawo sidzasunga mawu ake. Munthu wochimwayo ali ndi cholinga chimodzi chokha, ndipo ndi ichi: kuwononga.

Mulibe choonadi mwa iye, ndipo Satana mwiniwake adzalamulira zingwe zake. Sipadzakhala mtendere kuchokera kwa iye, koma mawu aliwonse adzakhala bodza lotonthoza, lopangidwa kuti litsogolere dziko lapansi kukuwonongeka kotheratu. Ndipo ndikanapanda kufupikitsa masikuwo, palibe thupi lomwe likanapulumutsidwa ku mliri womwe adzabweretse m'dzikolo, pakuti adzapatsidwa mphamvu kuti akwaniritse zolinga zanga kwa nthawi yochepa. Kenako adzamangidwa m'maunyolo olemera amdima kuphompho kuti amasulidwe nyengo yochepa kuti anyenge dziko lapansi kachisanu ndi chimodzi. Akawasonkhanitsa kwa iye, adzayambitsa kuukira mzinda waukuluwo, ndipo moto wochokera kwa Mulungu udzawawononga onse. Kenako chiweruzo chomaliza, inde, chiweruzo cha mpando wachifumu waukulu woyera chidzabwera pa iwo onse, ndipo adzakhala opanda thandizo. Ndikukuuzani zinthu izi kuti ndikupatseni chiyembekezo ndi kukonzekeretsa anthu anga, pakuti Chinsinsi Chachikulu cha Mulungu chidzafika pakukwaniritsidwa.

Ndine njira, choonadi, ndi moyo.

Ndine Mfumu ya Mafumu ndi Ambuye wa Ambuye.

Ndine amene ndili woyenera kutsegula zizindikiro, ndipo nthawi zafika.

Ndine amene ndimayesa zingwe za osankhidwa anga kuti ndikuyeretseni ndi kukutsukani m'magazi anga okutetezani.

Ndine Mwana wa Mulungu wobwera m'thupi, wopachikidwa, ndi kuukitsidwa tsiku lachitatu, wooneka ndi ambiri, ndi kulandiridwa kumwamba pamaso pawo.

Ndine mwana wa Davide, ndipo ndidzasonkhanitsa otsala a Yakobo pansi pa manja anga achitonthozo ndi mphamvu.

Ndine amene ndidzakwaniritsa chifuniro chonse cha Atate anga.

Ndatsala pang'ono kupanga adani anga kukhala chopondapo mapazi anga, ndipo tsoka kwa omwe akutsutsana nane, pakuti mkwiyo wa Mwanawankhosa ndi waukulu.

Ndine amene ndidzagonjetsa chilengedwe cha Atate anga ndi kupereka zinthu zonse m'manja mwake, kuti Mulungu akhale zonse mu zonse.

Ndine amene ufumu wake uli wamuyaya mpaka muyaya, ndipo ndidzapereka kwa osankhidwa anga kuti adye ku mtengo wamoyo ndi kumwa mitsinje yamadzi amoyo, ndipo simudzamvanso njala kapena ludzu, ndipo ndidzapukuta misozi yonse m'maso mwanu.

Ine, Ambuye, ndayankhula izi, ndipo ndidzachita momwemo. Nthawi yafika, choncho yang'anani kuonekera kwanga, pakuti palibe chomwe chingaletse dzanja langa lamphamvu.
