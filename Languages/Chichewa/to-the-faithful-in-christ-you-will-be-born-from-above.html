Kwa Okhulupirika mwa Khristu: Mudzabadwa Kuchokera Kumwamba: Nthawi ya Kubadwa Kwanu Kwatsopano Kwathunthu Yafika

Imvani Imvani, Mawu a Yeshua HaMashiach ndipo weramitsani mitima yanu ku phokoso la Mawu anga Oyera ndikumvetsetsa nthawi yomwe mukukhala okondedwa anga, chifukwa palibe ndi mmodzi yemwe wa inu adakhalapo m'nthawi zino, kapena zochitika izi zomwe tsopano zandandalikidwa kaamba ka chiweruzo chadzidzidzi ndi chofulumira ndi chiwonongeko pa Babulo Wachinsinsi, ngakhale United States iyi, kuti zitulutsidwe mwadzidzidzi ndi mosayembekezereka tsopano pa kulira kwa lipenga lomwe lakhazikitsidwa kuti lilire mwadzidzidzi ndi mosayembekezereka tsopano. Ngakhale pa phokoso la mfuu wamphamvu chifukwa nthawi zafika okondedwa anga, ndipo nthawi zino zili pa inu monga momwe zidalengezedwa ndi kulonjezedwa. Ndidzakusonkhanitsani kwa ine mwadzidzidzi ndi mosayembekezereka tsopano, kuti mukumane nane mumlengalenga monga mwalonjezedwa, choncho ndikukulamulani: KHALANI OKONZEKA!

Konzekerani Mkwatibwi wanga, chifukwa ola la chiwombolo chanu chathunthu likuthamangira kwa inu ndi mphamvu zonse ndipo chiyembekezo chili pano! Choncho, bwerani kwathunthu kwa Khristu Yesu ndikudziulula nokha kwathunthu kwa Ambuye wanu Wamuyaya wa Chilungamo, kuti zoonadi zoonadi, ndikuyeretseni kwathunthu ku machimo anu onse ndipo ndikupangitseni kugonjetsa zinthu zonse m'chigonjetso chachikulu cha dziko lapansi lodzaza ndi machimoli, monga momwe ine ndinagonjetsera zinthu zonse za dziko lapansi lodzaza ndi machimoli m'chigonjetso chachikulu.

Ndipo pamene zowawa zomaliza za kubadwa izi zikukulirakulira, mvetsetsani kuti zikuyandikira kutha, ngakhale pa nthawi yeniyeni yomwe nyengo iyi ya Kairos ya Harpazo idzafika pachimake pamene mwana woyera adzabadwanso wamoyo kumwamba kwa kumwamba, ngakhale kuperekedwa ndi kubadwa wamoyo kwamuyaya ndi wosawonongeka mpaka kalekale m'chipinda chachifumu cha Adonai-YHVH, monga momwe zidalengezedwa ndi kulonjezedwa poyamba ndi Mawu a mthenga wanga wamphamvu ndi mtumiki, ngakhale m'manja mwa ndani Mawu awa akuperekedwa ndi kutumizidwa m'chilengezo ndi kaamba ka kukonzekera kowonjezereka ndi kukonzekera kwa anamwali anga anzeru, kuti inu nonse mulekanitsidwe ndi anamwali opusa omwe amanyoza ndi kunyalanyaza Mawu anga, ndipo kuti ndikugwirizanitseni kwathunthu ndi mphotho yanu yathunthu yomwe yakonzedwa kwathunthu kwa inu nonse m'manja mwanga, yokonzedwa kuti inu mupatsidwe kuchokera kwa ine kupita kwa inu pokwaniritsa lonjezo la malipiro oyamba a chuma chogulidwa.

Komabe, ola limodzi ndi chilombo likuyandikira ndipo lili pano ndipo pali mawu ambiri osayera omwe amalira ndi kung'ung'udza zopusa zokhazokha! Zoonadi, zoonadi, mawu ambiri onyenga pakali pano akulira mokweza, kupereka mawu onyenga m'chipululu kwa omwe ali ndi makutu oyabwa. Zoonadi, zoonadi a Yezebeli awa azolowera chipululu ngati bulu wamtchire pa nthawi ya chilakolako chake akuitana odutsa ake a ziwanda, ndipo m'nyengo yake ndani angamudutse popanda kudumphira m'ziwiya zotembenuzidwa izi, omwe chiweruzo chawo sichichedwa.

Komabe ndi momwemonso mizimu iyi ya Yezebeli yomwe imanenera mawu onyenga a chinyengo ndi bodza, ngakhale mawu achinyengo omwe ambiri amakonda m'malo mwa Mawu anga oyera a Chilungamo operekedwa kuchokera mumtima mwa Adonai-YHVH mwiniwake, pamaso pa Khristu Yesu, yemwenso ndi Yeshua HaMashiach! Komabe, lekani kulekanitsa uku kuyime chifukwa kumayambitsidwa ndi lupanga la mzimu wanga, koma musadzilole kugwera kumanzere kwa lupanga langa kapena mudzatenga nawo mbali m'ziweruzo zawo, mutakana kudzilekanitsa nokha kwathunthu pa lamulo langa!

Ndipo izi chifukwa ambiri a inu mumakonda mawu ambiri ndi oipitsidwa ndi oipitsa operekedwa ndi aneneri onyenga otumizidwa kuti aipitse anamwali anga anzeru omwe akufunabe kukhala pakati pa anamwali opusa, ngakhale ndikufuna kukulekanitsani kwathunthu ku mawu awo onama operekedwa kuti asokoneze ndi kugawa maganizo anu ku mawu anga omveka bwino ndi ngakhale Mawu anga oyera a Ambuye wanu Wamuyaya wa Chilungamo, ndipo ndikuyandikira kuti ndikusonkhanitseni Mkwatibwi wanga ndi alendo athu choncho musafooke, ndipo khulupirirani Mawu anga Okondedwa anga ndipo pumulani m'chikondi changa kwa inu chifukwa goli langa ndi lofewa ndipo katundu wanga ndi wopepuka.

Bwerani kwa ine kwathunthu okondedwa anga, bwerani kwathunthu kwa ine popanda mantha ndipo ndi mitima yanu yathunthu m'chiyembekezo chathunthu ndipo ndi nkhope yotseguka kwathunthu, osabisira kalikonse. Taonani! Ndikuwona kwathunthu m'kati mwa mitima yanu ndi maganizo anu, ndipo ndikumvetsetsa kachitidwe ka mkati mwa maganizo anu ndi ngakhale mantha anu ambiri opanda nzeru, ndipo choncho ndikunena kwa inu kachiwiri musaope!

Ndipo ndi ndani kwenikweni yemwe muyenera kumuopa okondedwa anga? Mwa choonadi chamuyaya ndikukugwirani nonse mwamphamvu m'manja mwanga, kuti palibe bungwe limodzi mu ufumu wonse wa omwe adagwa, (ayi, ngakhale Heylel Ben Shachar mwiniwake chinjoka chachikulu chakale), yemwe angamasule ngakhale pang'ono kugwira kwanga kwamuyaya komwe ndikukutetezani kwathunthu! Chifukwa palibe m'chilengedwe chonse cha Adonai-YHVH yemwe angakuchotseni m'manja mwanga amphamvu a mphamvu ndi nyonga zamuyaya.

Zoonadi, zoonadi, palibe omwe angayime motsutsana nanu pamene mukhala omvera kwa Ambuye wanu Wamuyaya wa Chilungamo, ndipo choncho ndikukufunsani kachiwiri, ndi ndani yemwe muyenera kumuopa? NDIKUTI NDANI!

Mvetsetsani kuti ndine ndani, okondedwa anga, chifukwa ndine mwanawankhosa wa Adonai-YHVH, ngakhale mwanawankhosa wopanda tchimo kwamuyaya ndi wopanda banga popanda kuperewera, banga, kapena chilema. Ndipo mu mphamvu yanga ya chifuniro ndi chikondi chosatheka kusweka kwa inu okondedwa anga okhulupirika, ndinapereka moyo wanga mu nsembe yowawa kaamba ka machimo anu, ngakhale zili choncho, kuti ndiwombole anthu kwathunthu m'manja mwa Heylel Ben Shachar, ngakhale kalonga wamng'ono wolephera uja. Ndipo m'njira zake zankhanza, kodi Heylel Ben Shachar adapereka nsembe mwanawankhosa wa Adonai-YHVH mu chidani chake chankhanza ndi chopanda nzeru ndi cha chilakolako cha zinthu zonse zabwino. Ndipo potero Adamu adawomboledwa kwathunthu ndi magazi anga opanda tchimo, ndipo Adonai-YHVH wandiukitsa kwa akufa, kwamuyaya kuti ndisafe nso chifukwa n'zosatheka kuti imfa indigwire.

Komabe zili choncho, kuti ndinadzipereka ndekha kwa Adonai-YHVH monga Mkulu wa Ansembe kwamuyaya pambuyo pa Dongosolo la Melikizedeki ndipo Adonai-YHVH wavomereza nsembe yanga mwamphamvu kwambiri kotero kuti wasintha Yesu wa ku Nazarete kukhala mzimu wopereka moyo womwe udzakulengani nonse mwadzidzidzi ndi mosayembekezereka tsopano pa mfuu wamphamvu uja ndipo kulira kwa lipenga lamphamvu kumeneko kwakhazikitsidwa kuti kulire mwadzidzidzi ndi mosayembekezereka tsopano!

Kumene ndidzakulengani inu monga munthu mmodzi watsopano, kumene kulibe Myuda kapena wamitundu: kulibe kapolo kapena mfulu: kulibe mwamuna kapena mkazi: chifukwa nonse ndinu amodzi mwa Khristu Yesu, yemwenso ndi Yeshua HaMashiach! Ndipo popeza ndinu a Khristu Yesu, ndiye kuti ndinu mbewu ya Abrahamu, ndi olowa m'malo mwa lonjezo ndipo omasulidwa molungama mpaka kalekale m'manja mwa adani anu mwa Khristu Yesu Ambuye wanu wamuyaya, Mfumu, Mkulu wa Ansembe, ndi Mesiya, choncho chiwombolo chanu ndi chokwanira, chokwanira, chokwanira kwathunthu!

Inde, chiwombolo chanu ndi chathunthu ndi chokwanira kotero kuti simukusowa kalikonse ndipo nonse mwagulidwa ndi mtengo wosatheka kugulidwa kwamuyaya! Ndipo choncho ndikufunsa, ndani wa inu angapereke magazi opanda tchimo kuti atetezere machimo a Adamu, yemwe anachimwa pamene anali wopanda tchimo? Choncho, mwagulidwa ndi mtengo, choncho lemekezani Adonai-YHVH okondedwa anga chifukwa wapereka kale lamulo loti akusonkhanitseni kwathunthu pa nthawi yeniyeni yangwiro, ndipo choncho ndikukulamulani: KONDWERANI!

Komabe, ambiri a inu samamvetsetsa ukulu wa chochitika ichi, poganiza m'malo mwake kuti chiwombolo chanu ndi chinthu chokha chomwe chiyenera kukwaniritsidwa chofunikira. Komabe zili choncho, kuti ambiri a inu m'malo mwake mumangoyang'ana pa pachimake pa kusonkhana kwanu kumene mudzayima owomboledwa kwathunthu ndi osinthidwa kwathunthu mpaka kalekale kumwamba kwa kumwamba pamene kulira kwa lipenga kumeneko kudzamveka, chifukwa kwatsala pang'ono kumveka!

Ndipo ngakhale pamene Mawu awa akuperekedwa monga chilimbikitso, mawu a Ahebri akuyima monga mboni motsutsana ndi ambiri a inu: Chifukwa pamene ponena za nthawi yomwe mumayenera kukhala aphunzitsi, komabe mukusowa kachiwiri kuti tikuphunzitseni zomwe zili mfundo zoyamba za mawu a Mulungu: ndipo mwakhala ngati omwe akusowa mkaka, osati nyama yolimba.


Chotero ndikunenanso, chimodzimodzi monga momwe zinanenedwera kuti Mawu anga akhazikitsidwe:

Ndipo ngakhale Mawu awa aperekedwa monga chilimbikitso, liwu la Ahebri likuyima monga mboni yotsutsana ndi ambiri a inu: Pakuti pamene pa nthawi yomwe munayenera kukhala aphunzitsi, koma mukusowanso kuti tikuphunzitseni zomwe ziri mfundo zoyamba za mawu a Mulungu: ndipo mwakhala ngati osowa mkaka, osati chakudya chotafuna.

Ambiri a inu mwayiwala mfundo zoyamba za mawu a Mulungu kotero kuti mwalamulidwa kusunga Mawu anga a lonjezo m'maganizo ndi kuyika chidwi chanu chonse pa Khristu Yesu, yemwenso ndi Yeshua HaMashiach mu mtima umodzi ndi kukonzeka kwa maganizo. Kuti mutembenuzire maganizo ndi mitima yanu ndi kugwira Mawu anga a lonjezo mwamphamvu ndi mokwanira m'maganizo ndi m'mitima yanu omwe ndapereka kudzera m'kagulu kanga kankhosa komwe kalengeza ndi kukonzekeretsa anthu anga kaamba ka Kabuku Kaling'ono aka komwe ndi Uthenga Wabwino Wosatha wovumbulutsidwa pang'ono, womwe ndatsegula m'manja mwa mthenga ndi atumiki anga, monga momwe zinalonjezedwera ndi Yohane Wovumbulutsa zaka zonse za yubile zapitazo ngakhale pamene mawu a mabingu asanu ndi awiri anasindikizidwa ndi kusungidwa mu Chivumbulutso chaputala 10. Komabe, analengezedwanso ndi kukhazikitsidwa monga Uthenga Wabwino Wosatha mu Chivumbulutso chaputala 14. Komabe, ambiri a inu omwe ndimakukondani, mopusa mukukana Mawu anga m'malo mwa akatswiri anu azaumulungu abodza, omwe mabodza awo adzayimitsidwa m'chiweruzo chadzidzidzi. Chifukwa chake, tulukani pakati pawo ndipo mukhale osiyana mu zinthu zonse kuti musagawane nawo chiweruzo chawo.

Ndipo izi chifukwa mawu a mabingu asanu ndi awiri ndiwo chiyambi chenicheni cha zochitika zolengezedwa mu Apokalupsis ya Yesu Khristu, ngakhale bukhu lamphamvu lobisika lakale: Chivumbulutso cha Yesu Khristu; chomwe chinaperekedwa mwadala pang'ono ndi mwadala chosakwanira. Ngakhale zili choncho, kuti anthu abwere kwa Yesu Khristu Woweruza Wolungama ndi Wopembedzera kuti ndiweruze miyoyo yanu m'chilungamo, ngakhale kuchotsa machimo anu ngati wina aliyense angakhale womvera m'chikhulupiriro ndi chikondi kumvera malamulo operekedwa ndi liwu langa.

Chifukwa chake, mvetsetsani kuti mabingu asanu ndi awiri avumbulutsidwa, ndipo ayambitsidwa, ndipo tsopano akugwira ntchito ndipo sangathe kuyimitsidwa kapena kulepheretsedwa ngakhale pang'ono. Sizingathekanso kubweza kugwedezeka kwamphamvu kwa mawu a mabingu asanu ndi awiri omwe atuluka pampando wachifumu wa Adonai-YHVH, operekedwa m'liwu la El Shaddai komanso ndi Yeshua HaMashiach yemwe walandira zinthu zonse kuchokera m'manja amphamvu a Adonai-YHVH wanga zomwe ndidzagawana mokwanira ndi opambana anga okhulupirika.

Choncho mvetsetsani, kuti Ine ndine Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda womwe tsopano ukuyimira anthu anga! Ndipo m'chilungamo ndikubwera kudzaweruza ndi kuchita nkhondo!

Koma choyamba ndidzasonkhanitsa Mkwatibwi wanga wankhondo ndikumulandira kwa ine ndekha, ngakhale onse omwe adzikonzekeretsa okha kaamba ka Mkwati wawo yemwe akuyandikira, ndipo ndikuwona nyale zanu zikuyaka zomwe wazikonza momvera ndi kudzaza ndi kuyatsa ndi moto wanga m'mbali mwa njira m'chiyembekezo cholungama pamene ndikuyandikira!

Choncho mvetsetsaninso zomwe izi zikutanthauza okondedwa anga, chifukwa palibenso kuchedwa, ndipo Ine, Khristu Yesu, ndikulengeza m'thupi la mthenga wanga wamphamvu ndi mtumiki, kuti nthawi sidzakhalaponso!

Chifukwa chiitano changa chalengezedwa padziko lonse lapansi kwa nthawi yonse ya chisomo tsopano, ndipo ambiri ayankha, ndipo ambiri adzawomboledwa mokwanira mwadzidzidzi ndi mosayembekezereka tsopano. Ndipo opambana anga adzadzaza mokwanira magulu omwe asiyidwa opanda anthu kumwamba ndi Heylel Ben Shachar ndipo chotero ndidzavumbulutsa mitu isanu ndi iwiri iyi yomwe ndi asatana asanu ndi awiri, motsogozedwa ndi Heylel Ben Shachar. Ngakhale, m'chiweruzo chawo avulidwa umunthu wonse ndipo amangodziwika ngati asatana, ndipo aserafi oipa awa adzamangidwa, ndi kuponyedwa kumalawi otentha kwambiri a buluu ndipo chilango chawo ndi chaukali kwambiri pa onse oweluzidwa, omwe adzaperekedwa kumalawi a chilango, omangidwa kwamuyaya kaamba ka kubwezera kokwanira ndi chiweruzo chamoto kwa muyaya wonse kuti sadzathawa, kapena kukhala ndi mphindi yopuma.

Komabe, ndidzawavumbulutsa popeza akufuna kudzibisa chifukwa onse ndi onyenga omwe amanyenga, ndipo palibe chinsinsi chimodzi chomwe sichidzabwera poyera.

Pali Heylel Ben Shachar, chinjoka chachikulu chopanduka chakale chomwe chinayambitsa nkhondo kumwamba ndikuukira cholowa cha Adamu kuchititsa iwo eni ndi gawo limodzi mwa magawo atatu a angelo awo omwe anatsatira kupanduka kwawo koyamba pa nthawi yomwe Elohim analenga kumwamba ndi dziko lapansi kugwera kwamuyaya kumalawi amuyaya a chilango chamoto.
