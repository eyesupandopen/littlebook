1Kuchokera kwa Yeshua HaMashiach: Bingu Loyamba!

Oitanidwa anga, osankhidwa, ndi okhulupirika, imvani Mawu a Ambuye ndi mpulumutsi wanu wamoyo, ngakhale Yeshua Mesiya! Mitundu yomwe mukukhalamo ndi mbiri yomwe mwaphunzitsidwa ndi aphunzitsi anu a Jesuit ndi maziko a chinyengo chachikulu, chomwe kuvumbulutsidwa kwake kwathunthu kuli pafupi ndipo kwayamba kale, pamene akugwa adzawoneka ngati alendo abwino koma m'choonadi ndi angelo akugwa oyipa. Kodi nchiyani chomwe chakhalapo? Ndi chomwe chidzakhale: ndipo nchiyani chomwe chachitidwa? Chomwe chidzachitidwe: ndipo palibe chinthu chatsopano pansi pa dzuwa.

Satana wapereka chidziwitso chake chomwe chimatchedwa chapamwamba kuyambira pachiyambi, ngakhale chidziwitso chake cha chabwino ndi choyipa. Iye ndi katswiri wonyenga ndi wabodza yemwe waphunzitsa zinthu zambiri kwa anthu zomwe Yehova sanazifune, pakuti izi ndi njira za imfa. Chilengedwe cha Yehova sichinapangidwe kuti chigwiritsidwe ntchito molakwika pofuna mphamvu ndi phindu la munthu kapena ngati njira yothawira chiweruzo cha Mulungu, kapena kukhazikitsa njira ndi dongosolo la munthu mwini. Pakuti Satana waipitsa anthu kuyambira pachiyambi, ndipo waipitsa alonda akugwa omwe adalumikizana ndi kupanduka kwawo.

O, iwe mdani wamkulu wa chilungamo chonse, ngakhale inu asatana: machimo anu oyamba adalembedwa ndi Enoke mlembi nthawi yonseyi yapitayo, ndipo aima mpaka lero, pakuti machimo anu adalembedwa choncho. Ndipo chotero ndidzafotokoza mwachidule, pakuti mwachita zinthu izi, ndipo zambiri mwachita: Mwasocheretsa ana onse a Mulungu, ndikuwatsitsira pansi pa dziko lapansi, ndikuwasocheretsa kudzera mwa ana aakazi a anthu. Mwapereka kwa ana oyera a Mulungu uphungu woyipa, ndikuwasocheretsa kuti aipitse matupi awo ndi ana aakazi a anthu. Mwawonetsa ana a anthu mikwingwirima yonse ya imfa, ndikusocheretsa Hava, ndikuwonetsa zida za imfa kwa ana a anthu: chishango ndi malaya achitsulo, ndi lupanga lankhondo, ndi zida zonse za imfa kwa ana a anthu. Ndipo kuchokera m'dzanja lanu zapita kwa omwe akukhala pa dziko lapansi kuyambira tsiku limenelo mpaka kalekale.

Inu asatana mwaphunzitsa ana a anthu zowawa ndi zotsekemera, ndipo mwawaphunzitsa zinsinsi zonse za nzeru zawo. Mwaphunzitsa anthu kulemba ndi inki ndi pepala, ndipo potero ambiri achimwa kuyambira muyaya mpaka muyaya ndipo mpaka lero. Pakuti anthu sanalengedwe chifukwa cha cholinga choterocho, kuti atsimikizire chikhulupiriro chawo chabwino ndi cholembera ndi inki. Pakuti anthu adalengedwa chimodzimodzi ngati angelo, ndi cholinga chakuti apitirize kukhala oyera ndi olungama; ndipo imfa, yomwe imawononga chilichonse, sikanawagwira, koma kudzera mu chidziwitso ichi akuwonongeka, ndipo kudzera mu mphamvu iyi ikuwononga anthu.

Inu asatana mwawonetsa ana a anthu kumenya konse koyipa kwa mizimu ndi ziwanda, ndi kumenya kwa mwana wosabadwa m'mimba, kuti apite, ndi kumenya kwa moyo, kulumidwa kwa njoka, ndi kumenya komwe kumachitika kudzera mu kutentha kwa masana, mwana wa njoka wotchedwa Tabââ'ĕt. Pakuti mudapemphanso Mikayeli kuti akuwonetseni dzina lobisika, kuti mulitchule m'lumbiro, kuti omwe adavumbulutsa zonse zomwe zinali mwachinsinsi kwa ana a anthu anjenjemere pamaso pa dzina ndi lumbiro limenelo.

Pakuti mwapangitsa alonda kuti alumikizane ndi kupanduka kwanu pamene mukuipitsa anthu kwambiri. Ndipo chotero machimo a alonda adalembedwa choncho ndi Enoke mlembi, ndipo ndidzafotokoza mwachidule motere: Adaphunzitsa anthu kupanga malupanga, ndi mipeni, ndi zishango, ndi zovala zapachifuwa, ndikuwadziwitsa zitsulo za dziko lapansi ndi luso lozigwiritsa ntchito, ndi zibangili, ndi zokongoletsera, ndi kugwiritsa ntchito antimoni, ndi kukongoletsa zikope, ndi mitundu yonse ya miyala yamtengo wapatali, ndi mitundu yonse ya utoto. Adaphunzitsa matsenga, ndi kudula mizu, kuthetsa matsenga, kupenda nyenyezi, magulu a nyenyezi, chidziwitso cha mitambo, zizindikiro za dziko lapansi, zizindikiro za dzuwa, ndi mayendedwe a mwezi.

Ndipo chotero kudzera mu ziphuphu ndi mabodza anu, mwaipitsa chilengedwe cha Yehova ndi njira zanu zoyipa ndi zonyansa zamwano. Mwasocheretsa anthu ndikuchimwira Yehova, ndipo mwalakwira anthu, ndipo ngakhale kuchimwira mbalame, ndi zilombo, ndi zokwawa, ndi nsomba, ndipo machimo anu abweretsa chiweruzo chachikulu chomwe simudzachira. Mwaipitsa chilengedwe cha Yehova ndi matekinoloje anu ndi chinyengo chanu, ndipo mwapangitsa anthu kudabwa pambuyo pa zidule zanu ndi mabodza anu. Pakuti Yehova adangokuwonetsani zinsinsi zopanda phindu za chilengedwe chake ndikusunga zinsinsi zake zamtengo wapatali zobisika mumtima mwake kutali ndi kusakhulupirika kwanu.

Inu oyipa mwaphunzitsa njira zanu zopanda phindu kwa anthu pamene adachuluka pa nkhope ya dziko lapansi, ndipo mwabweretsa masautso aakulu ndi chiwonongeko kudzera mu ziwembu zanu zoyipa ndi matekinoloje. Pakuti simubwera koma kudzaba, kupha, ndi kuwononga. Mwaphunzitsa kusintha kwa mpweya, pakuti ndinu kalonga wa mphamvu ya mpweya, pakuti mumasintha aithēr. Pakuti pamene Kaini adachimwa, adamanga mzinda; ndipo mwaphunzitsa anthu zinsinsi zanu zopanda phindu ndi chinyengo. Mizinda iyi idagwiritsidwa ntchito pofuna kulamulira ndi kunyenga komanso kubweretsa chiwonongeko cha anthu, monga momwe mudayigwiritsira ntchito kupanga zonyansa zanu za chiwonongeko.

Mwapangitsa mtundu kuukira mtundu wina kunkhondo ndi mikangano yosatha, ndipo mwapangitsa anthu anga kuchimwa, ndipo Yehova wamwaza Mafuko a Israyeli kumphepo zinayi. Zilibe kanthu, cholinga chanu choyipa chapititsa patsogolo zolinga za Yehova, pakuti kudzera mu kumwazikana kwawo kusonkhanitsidwa kwa nkhosa zotsala kudzakwaniritsidwa. Ndipo ndidzatumiza mboni zanga ziwiri kudzulosera masiku 1260 atavala chiguduli. Mu zoyesayesa zanu zonse zowononga ana a Yehova mwalephera kotheratu, ndipo simukanatha kuwona kuti zoyesayesa zanu zapititsa patsogolo zolinga za Yehova, pakuti ngakhale adani ake amamvera mawu ake.

Pakuti mwazunza ndi kuvutitsa mafuko 12 padziko lonse lapansi pamene mumafunafuna chiwonongeko chawo. Mwaba ana kwa amayi, ndikutenga ana m'mabanja ndikuwasamutsira padziko lonse lapansi, ndiyeno mwawanamiza ndikuwapangitsa kukhulupirira nthano za Jesuit zokhudza mbiri yawo yeniyeni, zolinga, ndi chiyambi chawo. Zilibe kanthu, mwalephera, chifukwa Ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndidzawalumikizanso kwathunthu ndi zakale zawo, mtundu ndi cholowa chawo chenicheni, ndipo ndidzapanga onse omwe ali olowa m'malo a chipulumutso kukhala munthu mmodzi watsopano, ngakhale mwa ine, Khristu Yesu.

Mwa chinyengo ndi chiwonongeko chachikulu, mwabisa mwanzeru matekinoloje anu, ngakhale kunena mabodza kuti anthu akale anali osazindikira ndipo analibe tekinoloje. Pakuti mudawonetsa akale zinsinsi zanu zopanda phindu, ndipo palibe chinthu chatsopano pansi pa dzuwa. Ndipo chotero mumabisa choonadi chokhudza kusintha kwanu kwa chilengedwe, ngakhale kuwabisa kutali ndi omwe mungawanyenge ndi chinyengo chanu chachikulu. Mwayambitsanso zidule zanu zamatsenga kachiwiri, pamene mwakhazikitsa bwalo kuti muthe kudzibisa ngati alendo. Ndipo kudzera mu kuwukira kwanu kwabodza kwa alendo mudzapangitsa onse kudabwa ndi zizindikiro zanu zabodza ndi zozizwitsa, ngakhale omwe mayina awo sanalembedwe m'Buku la Moyo kuyambira kukhazikitsidwa kwa dziko lapansi. Koma Ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndidzakuwululani kotheratu, ndipo chinsinsi chilichonse chobisika chidzawonekera.

Taonani! Ndidzatenga okhulupirika anga kupita kumwamba, pakuti ndikubwera kudzachitira Mkwatibwi wanga ndipo sindidzaletsedwa. Ndipo Ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndikubwera kudzaweruza ndi kupanga nkhondo ndikubweretsa chiweruzo chachikulu pa dziko lapansi, ndipo simudzathawa.

Ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, Mbuye wa Ambuye ndi Mfumu ya Mafumu, ndatumiza mawu awa kudzera mwa mtumiki wanga, ndipo kudzera mwa mtumiki wanga mawu anga atumizidwa, ndipo onse adzakwaniritsidwa.

2Kuchokera kwa Yehova, Mulungu Wamkulu Wamuyaya: Bingu Lachiwiri!

O, inu asatana, mwanyenga chilengedwe changa kuti chitsatire zizindikiro zanu zabodza ndi zozizwitsa, ndipo mkwiyo wanga uli wotentha pa inu. Anthu anga adzalekanitsidwa kotheratu ndi namsongole wanu yemwe mwafesa pakati pa tirigu wanga m'munda mwanga wa chisomo; ndipo Mwana wanga, Khristu Yesu, adzasonkhanitsa tirigu wanga m'nkhokwe yanga, ndipo chotero Mbuye wa zokolola adzasonkhanitsa, kumanga ndi kutentha namsongole wanu. Chiweruzo chachikulu chili pa inu, ndipo simudzathawa dzanja langa. Kuyambira pachiyambi, mudakwawa ndikudzibisa pakati pa ana anga, ngakhale kuipitsa ang'ono anga kuyambira pachiyambi penipeni ndi mabodza anu ndi chinyengo.

Inu sindinu milungu, ndipo omwe adatsatira chinyengo chanu ndi malingaliro anu opambana nawonso si milungu. Simungathe kulenga, pakuti mulibe mphamvu, ndipo sindinakulengeni ndi maluso oterowo chifukwa ndimadziwa kuti mudzapanduka musanalengedwe; ndipo m'chilungamo changa ndi chikondi changa ndidakulengani m'chilungamo, ndipo munali angwiro tsiku lomwe mudalengedwa. M'kunyada kwanu kopanda phindu mumadzitamandira ndi ukulu wanu, koma mulibe ukulu, ndipo ndidzakuwululani kotheratu chifukwa cha chinyengo chomwe muli. Ndidzakuikani pamaso pa Mafumu, ndipo adzakuwonani ndi momwe mulibe mphamvu. Mwana wanga, Yeshua HaMashiach, adakugonjetsani kotheratu pa Kalvare, ndipo mulibe chiyembekezo.

Mumaloledwa kuchita zokhazo zomwe ndimalola, popeza ndinu mdani wogonjetsedwa. Mudaba chikalata cha dziko lapansi mu chinyengo chanu ndi mabodza kwa Adamu, ndipo tsopano mwana wanga wobadwa yekha, ngakhale Yeshua HaMashiach, wakugonjetsani m'chikondi chake pa ine ndi chilengedwe changa; ndipo dziko lapansi ndi lake, ngakhale Mbuye wa Ambuye ndi Mfumu ya Mafumu, pakuti ndalengeza. Pakuti adagula dziko lapansi ndi anthu ndi magazi ake opanda tchimo, ndipo omwe asankha moyo adzawomboledwa. Ntchito zanu zonse zopanda phindu zidzathetsedwa, ndipo onse omwe asankha moyo adzatuluka m'mabodza anu ndi chinyengo kulowa m'moyo watsopano, ndipo ndidzapanga okhulupirika kukhala aulemerero kuposa momwe munalili kale. Pakuti mwalephera, o akalonga ofooka ndi opemphapempha a dziko lapansi, ngakhale kalonga aliyense m'dongosolo lanu walephera pamodzi ndi inu, o njoka yokwawa, yonama.

Tsopano yafika nthawi ya Chiweruzo chanu ndi chiwonongeko chotheratu cha ufumu wanu, ngakhale ufumu womwe mwamanga m'zoyesayesa zanu zoyesa kundibisa, ngakhale Yehova, ku chilengedwe changa. Simudzathawa. Simudzalowanso kumwamba, pakuti mwaponyedwa pansi pa dziko lapansi kuti musabwererenso, ndipo malo anu sali kumwamba kachiwiri. Mwamangiriridwa ku dziko lapansi lomwe mudalilakalaka, lomwe silinali lanu kuti mulilande. Ntchito yanu iliyonse idzathetsedwa m'miyoyo ya omwe mudayesa kuwawononga (ndi pa dziko lapansi lenilenilo); adzakhala mumtendere ndi chitetezo kwamuyaya, ndipo adzakhala ndi chimwemwe changwiro, ndipo ndidzakhala pakati pa anthu ndipo ndidzakhala Mulungu wawo.

Koma iwe, o wokwawa, ulibe mphamvu m'maso mwanga, ndipo sungachite chilichonse kundiletsa. M'masiku asanu ndi limodzi kuyambira kugwa kwa munthu, pakuti tsiku limodzi ndi zaka chikwi chimodzi pamaso panga, simunakwaniritse chilichonse koma chiweruzo chanu, chilango, chiwonongeko, ndi mazunzo kwa inu ndi onse omwe akutsatira. Pakuti mudzamangidwa ndikuponyedwa m'nyanja yamoto yomwe ndakupangirani malawi otentha kwambiri, ngakhale malawi oyera a buluu, ndipo mudzayaka kwamuyaya, ndipo ndidzakubwezerani ntchito iliyonse yoyipa yomwe mudachitapo, nthawi zambiri. Mwapha makamu pa makamu, ndipo mudzalipira moyo uliwonse womwe watayika, ndipo simudzathawa. Mudzadziwa machimo onse omwe mudachita, ndipo simudzathawa chiweruzo, ndipo mudzazunzidwa kwamuyaya chifukwa cha machimo omwe mwachita. Mudzadziwa bwino machimo anu ndi zolakwa zanu m'mazunzo anu, ndipo omwe mumawada adzakhala ndi chigonjetso chomaliza pa inu. Ndipo onse osalungama omwe mudawakopa kuti achimwe ndikudzipereka okha kuti alumikizane ndi kupanduka kwanu, ngakhale omwe mayina awo sanalembedwe m'Buku la Moyo, adzagawana m'mazunzo anu. Nonse mudzalipira tchimo lililonse lomwe mwachita, pakuti mwachimwira Yehova ndi chilengedwe changa, ndipo ndidzabwezera. Pakuti m'chilungamo changa ndidzabwezera machimo anu, zochita zanu, ndi njira zanu zoyipa kotheratu m'zifuwa zanu nthawi zambiri ndipo mpaka muyaya. Ndipo Ine, Yehova, ngakhale Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya, ndikulumbira zinthu izi ndi dzina langa, pakuti sindingathe kulumbira ndi wamkulu.

Mudzatumikira zolinga zanga, ndipo m'chidani chanu ndi chiwonongeko mudzathamangitsira osankhidwa anga kwa ine; pakuti ndidzawatsogolera ndi dzanja la Yeshua HaMashiach ndi gulu lake lankhondo la amuna amphamvu, pakuti adzasonkhanitsa nkhosa zanga zotsala. (Taonani! 144,000 otumizidwa ndi Yeshua HaMashiach adzachita ntchito zamphamvu pamaso pa zoyipa zanu zonyansa.) Ndipo palibe mmodzi yemwe ndamuika ku moyo wosatha adzagwa m'ziwembu zanu, pakuti sangalandidwe m'dzanja langa, ndipo palibe amene angawalandidwe m'manja mwa Mwana wanga: Pakuti ndamupatsa dzina loposa dzina lililonse, ndipo akubwera mu mphamvu ya Yehova mwiniwake, pakuti ndagawana mpando wanga wachifumu ndi iye. Ndipo adzaphwanya mutu wanu kotheratu. Mwalephera, kalonga wamng'ono, ndipo nthawi yanu yafika.

Ine, Yehova, Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya, ndatumiza mawu awa kudzera mwa mtumiki wanga, ndipo kudzera mwa mtumiki wanga mawu anga atumizidwa, ndipo adzakwaniritsidwa. Pakuti Yehova, Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya, walengeza choncho, ndipo palibe chomwe chingaphwanye malengezo anga. NDINE El Shaddai!

3Kuchokera kwa Yeshua HaMashiach: Bingu Lachitatu

Taonani! Nthawi zolekanitsa zikuyandikira kutha, ndipo kuonekera kwanga kwatsala pang'ono kufika tsopano. Kuli pafupi kwambiri ndipo ngakhale angelo ndi gulu la kumwamba ali okonzeka pa kulira kwa lipenga. Ndakhala ndikutambasulira manja anga ku dziko lomwe likufa kwa mayubile ambiri tsopano, ndipo ambiri alandira pangano lamtendere lomwe Atate wanga, Yehova, anatumiza kwa anthu, ngakhale ine, Khristu Yesu, pakuti ndine mtendere wanu. Koma ambiri akana mwayi wanga ndi chisomo ndi chifundo cha Atate wanga chomwe chatsanuliridwa pa dziko lapansi.

Awo omwe asindikizidwa ndi kuwomboledwa ndi omwe anasankhidwa chiyambire cha dziko lapansi. Pakuti Atate wanga anakudziwani kuyambira muyaya mpaka muyaya, ndipo mwakhala muli pamtima pake nthawi zonse, monga inenso ndakhala pamtima pake, pakuti Ine, Yesu wa ku Nazarete, ndine mwana wobadwa yekha wa Yehova. Ndipo kudzera mu ntchito zanga zokwaniritsidwa monga Paskha wangwiro ndi kumvera kwa wobadwa yekha, ana a Mulungu ambiri, ambiri abadwanso mwatsopano mu muyaya wonse, ngakhale kutengedwa kuchokera kwa ana a anthu, pakuti ndine Mwana wa Munthu!

Nthawi izi zachisomo zinali chinsinsi chobisika mu mtima wa Yehova chomwe sanauze aliyense mpaka anachiulula kwa ine, ndipo ndachiulula kwa atumwi anga oyera ndi aneneri ndipo ndasunga Mawu anga oyera m'makutu ndi m'mitima ya osankhidwa ndi okhulupirika. Pakuti inu, Mkwatibwi wanga, ndinu mbali ya chinsinsi chachikulu cha Mulungu, ndipo miyoyo yanu (yomwe yabisika mwa ine) yatsala pang'ono kuwululidwa. Ndipo ndidzawafupa okhulupirika chifukwa cha ntchito zawo zonse zokhulupirika zomwe achita mokhulupirika mu chikhulupiriro chachikulu kwa ine, Yesu Khristu.

Ndipo momwemo ndapulumutsa makamu ambiri ndi magazi anga otetezera, pakuti magazi anga anali opanda tchimo ndipo mpaka lero ndine wopanda tchimo, pakuti sindidzachita tchimo. Awo omwe akhala miyoyo yawo mokhulupirika mwa Khristu adzalandira mphoto yaikulu, ndipo adzawala ngati nyenyezi zakumwamba, ndipo ulemerero waukulu ndi wawo, pakuti sanadzilemekeze okha ngati kuti anali akulu. Koma andilemekeza ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndipo ndidzawapereka kwa Atate wanga opanda banga kapena khwinya, ndipo zovala zawo zidzakhala zoyera ngati matalala ndipo sizidzadetsedwa. Ndipo onse adzakhala ndi moyo ndipo sadzachitanso tchimo, kaya kudzera mu kusapembedza kapena kudzera mu kunyada. Ndipo adzalemekezedwa mwa ine.

Pakuti ndine mbusa wamkulu, ndipo ndatsogolera gulu langa ku msipu wobiriwira mpaka nthawi za chimaliziro, zomwe zikubwera mofulumira pa dziko lapansi, pakuti palibenso mchenga wotsala woti udutse mu galasi. Ndipo ndidzawafupa onse, kuyambira wamng'ono mpaka wamkulu, m'njira yomwe simungathe kuimvetsa panthawiyi; koma kumvetsa kumeneko kuli pakhomo panu, pakuti mudzatengedwa kupita kumwamba posachedwapa. Ndipo momwemo Mkwatibwi wanga adzatengedwa mu ulemerero, kumene ndidzaweruza ntchito zake ndi kuwombola kwathunthu mwamuna, mkazi, ndi mwana aliyense womaliza. Ndidzawafupa mochuluka ngakhale pa zinthu zing'onozing'ono zomwe zakwaniritsidwa potumikira ine. Pakuti Mulungu sali wosalungama, kuti aiwale ntchito yanu ndi kudzivutitsa kwanu kwachikondi, komwe mwakuwonetsa ku Dzina lake, pakuti mwatumikira Oyera mtima, ndipo mukuwatumikira.

Musaope, Mkwatibwi wanga, pakuti Mulungu saweruza monga momwe munthu aweruzira, pakuti munthu ndi wosalungama ndipo amakondera anthu. Munthu amalemekeza omwe munthu amawaona ngati ofunika, ndipo munthu wamthupi nthawi zambiri amanyalanyaza kudzichepetsa ndi ukoma m'malo mwa odzikweza ndi odzidalira. Pakuti munthu wamthupi amalirira kuti ayesedwe wolungama pamaso pa Mulungu, koma munthu wamthupi sangathe kugonjetsa chikhalidwe chake chauchimo ndipo akukana kukhulupirira kuti ndi woipa, ngakhale kukana Mbuye wa Ambuye, yemwe akamupulumutsa.

Ndipo momwemo kunyada kumatsogolera chiwonongeko, ndi mzimu wodzikweza usanagwe. M'malo mopereka mitima yawo ku mawu anga ndi nzeru ndi chikondi chomwe ndachisefukiritsa pa dziko lapansi, munthu wamthupi m'malo mwake amasankha kutsatira zilakolako zake, pakuti njira za dziko lapansi zinapangidwa ndi Satana kuti ziyese ndi kuyambitsa zilakolakozo, ngakhale mphoto za chosalungama. Ndipo momwemo nthawi za Akunja zikuyandikira kutha, ndipo ndi nthawi ya masautso a Yakobo; pakuti ndidzawombola Isiraeli, ngakhale osankhidwa anga okhulupirika, omwe adzabwera kwa ine mu kulapa machimo awo ndi machimo a makolo awo omwe anandikana. Ndipo monga kwalembedwa, momwemo zichitike.

Oh, munthu wamthupi, chifukwa chiyani ukusankhabe machimo ako ndi kunyalanyaza chisomo changa ndi chipulumutso ngati kuti ndi chinthu chopepuka? Kaya Yehova kapena ine, Khristu Yesu, tilibe chisangalalo m'maweruzo omwe akubwera ndi mantha aakulu omwe posachedwapa adzamasulidwa. Zonyansa zanu zonse zosayera za namsongole zomwe munabzala podutsa malire a Yahweh m'kusintha kwanu kwa majini pakati pa munthu ndi mngelo, pakati pa munthu ndi chilombo, pakati pa munthu ndi zolengedwa zam'nyanja, pakati pa zilombo zamitundumitundu, ngakhale chinthu chilichonse chonyansa chomwe mwapanga chidzaponyeedwa pamoto pamodzi ndi inu, pakuti mulibe mtendere kapena chikhululuko!

Mwatsatira a Satana amene anayesa kuwononga chilengedwe cha Yahweh, ngakhale mpaka kundipha ine, Yesu Khristu, pamene ndinadzipereka mofunitsitsa m'manja mwake monga Paskha wangwiro wa dziko lapansi, potero kusindikiza chiwombolo cha anthu ndi kusindikiza tsogolo lanu mpaka muyaya m'moto waukulu womwe sungazimitsidwe! Mudzalipira kotheratu machimo anu onse ndi zonyansa zanu, ndipo ntchito iliyonse yoyipa yomwe munachitapo idzatembenuzidwa pa inu kuwirikiza kakhumi, pakuti onse adzabwezeredwa monga mwa ntchito zawo. Ndipo njira zanu zonse zoyipa zidzabwezeredwa pamitu panu kachulukidwe, pakuti munaganiza kuti mungandigonjetse, ndipo modzikuza mukuganizabe kuti mungapambane pamene mwagonjetsedwa kotheratu.

Imvani mawu anga, inu njoka yakale, pakuti chiweruzo chanu chili pa inu ndipo ndikuwona mantha anu, pakuti mukuwopa tsogolo lanu kuposa chilichonse, o, inu mkango wopanda mano wopanda pake! Kubwezera ndi kwanga, ndipo ndidzabwezera, ndipo nthawi yanu yafika. Mkwiyo wa Mulungu ndi mkwiyo wa mwanawankhosa watsala pang'ono kutulutsidwa pamene ndidzathyola zisindikizo zisanu ndi ziwiri, monga angelo asanu ndi awiri atumizidwa kudzayimba maseŵera a malipenga, ndipo angelo asanu ndi awiri atumizidwa omwe ali ndi miliri isanu ndi iwiri yomaliza, ndipo idzatulutsidwa, pakuti mwa iyo mwadzazidwa mkwiyo wa Mulungu. Masoka ambiri adzakupezani nonse, pamene ndikulanga kotheratu ndi kugwetsa ufumu wanu. Mfumu yanu yayikulu ndidzaigonjetsa ndipo mneneri wanu wonyenga ndidzamugonjetsa, ndipo adzaponyedwa amoyo m'nyanja yamoto yoyaka ndi sulufule; ndipo kumeneko adzakudikirirani kuti mukhale nawo, ndipo kumeneko mudzamezedwa ndi moto ndi sulufule, ndipo chiweruzo chanu chatsala pang'ono kuyamba!

Mkwatibwi wanga, usaope! Mawu anga achiweruzo si anu koma akukutonthozani, pakuti ndakupulumutsani ku mkwiyo womwe watsala pang'ono kutulutsidwa, ndipo ndidzakubisani m'chitetezo ndi mphotho yayikulu, ndipo mudzakhala nane mpaka kalekale. Pakuti ndakusankhani, pakuti ndakuyitanani, ndipo ndinu chuma changa chachikulu, ndipo ndinu chuma chachikulu cha Yahweh; ndinu ngale yamtengo wapatali, pakuti ndapereka zonse zanga chifukwa cha inu. Ndikulangiza Mkwatibwi wanga kuti afunefune kuzindikira ndikubwera kwa ine kuti adziwe nzeru, pakuti ndidzakuphunzitsani. Pakuti ena a inu mwatsatira zina mwa njira za aneneri onyenga awa; ingobwererani m'kulapa kwa Ambuye wanu, ndipo ndidzakulandirani.

Pakuti akukopani ndi ufiti wachikhristu, ndipo mwakhulupirira mabodza ambiri ogulitsidwa kwa inu, ngakhale kukuuzani kuti ndi choonadi, koma ndi mabodza onyansa. Zitsime zopanda madzi izi zayesa kukuchotsani kwa ine, ndipo izo nzosatheka, choncho musaope, pakuti sindidzakanidwa! Ndidzapitiriza kukumasulani kuti mukhale omasuka ndithu, ndipo sindidzatonthozedwa! Ndikumenyerani nkhondo m'njira zambiri, zambiri zomwe simukuzidziwa. Pakuti nonse munalembedwa chizindikiro musanabadwe, pakuti Atate anakudziwani ngakhale m'mimba mwa amayi anu, ndipo anadikira aliyense wa inu kwa nthawi yomwe simungamvetsetse, ndipo mwakhala muli pamtima pake nthawi zonse. Munalembedwa chizindikiro pobadwa, ndipo angelo anga anakusungani pansi pa ulamuliro wanga ndipo akulonderani ndi kukutetezani, pakuti atumizidwa kutero. Kodi izi zikukudabwitsani? Kodi mudzamvera zomwe Ahebri akunena? Koma kwa mngelo uti ananena nthawi iliyonse, khala kudzanja langa lamanja, kufikira nditaika adani ako pondaponda mapazi ako? Kodi onse si mizimu yotumikira, yotumidwa kukatumikira iwo, amene adzakhala olowa nyumba a chipulumutso?

Mkwatibwi wanga, inu ndinu olowa nyumba a chipulumutso mwa ine, Yesu Khristu. Mwayitanidwa mwa ine; mwatetezedwa mwa ine ndi ine; mwapatsidwa mwayi womva kulalikidwa kwa chikhulupiriro chokhudza ine kuti mundipange kukhala Ambuye wanu, ndipo chifukwa chake ndakusindikizani ndi mzimu woyera wa lonjezo. Dziwani kuti chisindikizo sichingasweke, ndipo mwatsala pang'ono kuwomboledwa kotheratu mpaka muyaya, pakuti mulibe lingaliro la kudzaza kwa ntchito yolenga yomwe ndatsala pang'ono kumaliza mwa inu, Mkwatibwi wanga! Dziwani kuti akugwa ndi namsongole wawo akukuchitirani nsanje, ndipo amadana ndi chilichonse chomwe Yahweh amakonda, ndipo ndidzakubwezerani kotheratu pamitu pawo, pakuti ndine amene ndidzavulaza mutu wa njoka yakaleyo, pakuti kwalengezedwa, ndipo zomwe Yahweh alengeza sizingasinthidwe.

Palibe amene angakulekanitseni ndi ine; palibe kuchuluka kwa zoyipa komwe kungaswe chisindikizocho; ndinu anga ndipo ndinu a Atate, pakuti zonse zomwe Atate andipatsa ndidzazibwezera kwa Mulungu kuti tikhale onse mwa onse! Ndipo wandipatsa zinthu zonse kumwamba ndi padziko lapansi, kupatula pampando wake wachifumu iye ndi wamkulu kuposa ine!

Ndipo ine, Khristu Yesu, ndikulengeza mawu awa kwa inu, ndipo ndine, Yesu Khristu, amene ndikupereka chiweruzo pa adani anu, ndipo ndidzachikwaniritsa mpaka khobiri lomaliza. Mkwatibwi wanga, chikondi changa pa inu sichingazimitsidwe, ndipo ndakukonzerani malo, pakuti m'nyumba ya Atate wanga muli malo okhala ambiri! Choncho posachedwapa mudzakwatiwa nane mpaka kalekale, ndipo mudzakhala nane nthawi zonse, ndipo pomaliza mudzamvetsetsa ukulu wa cholinga chanu mwa ine chomwe munalengedwa kuti mukwaniritse!

Pakuti mwa ine, Yahweh wakupatsani tsogolo ndi cholinga, ngakhale cholinga cha mibadwo chatsala pang'ono kuwululidwa kwa inu, pakuti ndikubwera! Ndipo sindidzakanidwa. Yang'anani kuwonekera kwanga. Yang'anani kuwala kwa kubwera kwanga, pakuti kwatsala pang'ono kuwala pa dziko lapansi pamene ndikuwonekera m'mitambo ndi mfuu waukulu, ndi mawu a mngelo wamkulu, ndi lipenga la Mulungu, pakuti ndikukuuzani moona kuti ngakhale angelo akudikira lipenga lomwe latsala pang'ono kulira, ndipo ali okonzeka! Khalani okonzeka kwa ine ndipo yang'anani, penyani, onani monga mwalamulidwa; pakuti posachedwa mudzandiwona ine ndi ulemerero wanga womwe Atate wandiveka, pakuti momwemonso ndidzakuvekani, pakuti mudzakhala ngati ine, ndipo mudzandiwona monga ndiliri.
