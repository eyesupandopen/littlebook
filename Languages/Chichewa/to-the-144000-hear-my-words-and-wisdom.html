Kwa a 144,000: Imvani Mawu Anga ndi Nzeru Zanga!

Imvani mawu anga ndipo tcherani khutu ku nzeru zanga, pakuti Ine, Ambuye Yesu Khristu, ndilankhula nanu ngati mumva mawu anga. Pakuti ndidzaika chizindikiro pa a 144,000 anga; kodi simunawerenge? Ndipo ndinaona Mngelo wina akukwera kuchokera Kum'mawa, ali ndi chizindikiro cha Mulungu wamoyo: ndipo anafuula ndi mawu akulu kwa Angelo anayi amene anapatsidwa mphamvu zowononga dziko lapansi ndi nyanja, nati, musawononge dziko lapansi, kapena nyanja kapena mitengo, kufikira titasindikiza atumiki a Mulungu wathu pamphumi pawo. Ndipo chotero ndidzatumiza amene ndidzawasindikiza, ndipo mudzandichitira ntchito zamphamvu, ndipo mudzayenda mu mphamvu yaikulu. Koma izi sizidzakhala ngati mu nthawi ya chisomo, pamene alaliki m'mimbale ankaphunzitsa kuchokera m'malemba oyera. (Ena analankhula monama ndipo ena anandichitira ntchito zamphamvu m'mipingo yawo.) Nthawi ya mpingo ikufika kumapeto, ndipo nthawi yanga ya chisomo yatsala pang'ono kutha, ndipo chotero nthawi zoyesedwa zidzayamba, ngakhale sautsiyo ya Yakobo.

Ndipo chotero ndidzakutumizani kuti muwete gulu langa la nkhosa lomwe lidzamwazikana kwambiri ndikuyesedwa ndi tsoka pambuyo pa tsoka. Pakuti masiku adzakhala oipa, ndipo mudzawala ngati magetsi ndikuyima ngati mizati pakati pa omwazikana. Ndipo chotero ndikukutumizani kuti mukonde ndi kusamalira mnansi wanu. Pakuti inu amene muli okhulupirika ku kuitana kwanga mudzakhala ndi moyo mu lamulo limene ndapereka, osati lamulo la Mose. Pakuti Ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndakwaniritsa lamulo la Mose, ndipo Torah imasungidwa mwa chikhulupiriro mwa Ine, pakuti wolungama adzakhala ndi moyo ndi chikhulupiriro.

Ndipo chotero zidzakhala: Kondani Ambuye Mulungu wanu ndi mtima wanu wonse, moyo wanu wonse, nzeru zanu zonse, ndi mphamvu zanu zonse, ndipo kondani mnansi wanu monga mudzikondera nokha. Pakuti pochita zinthu izi mudzapulumutsa moyo wanu ku chiwonongeko komanso awo amene ndikukutumizirani. Ino si nthawi ya moyo wautali ndi chitukuko, pakuti masiku amenewo apita padziko lapansi kufikira nditabwera kudzalamulira dziko lapansi m'chilungamo. Iye amene sakonda moyo wake mpaka mapeto adzapulumutsidwa ndikukhala ndi moyo wosatha.

Muli ndi moyo mu nthawi za nkhondo, ndi mkwiyo waukulu, ndi vumbulutso lalikulu la zinthu zonse. Pakuti Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya, ngakhale Yahweh, sadzabisalanso ku dziko lapansi; pakuti adzatuluka m'malo ake okhala, ndipo akunditumiza Ine, ngakhale Yeshua HaMashiach, kukwaniritsa mkwiyo wake ndi kukwaniritsa malonjezo ake amene anapanga kwa Makolo. Koma choyamba, chiweruzo chidzagwa, ndipo mkwiyo waukulu udzagwa pa dziko lapansi, ndipo simudzathanso kutenga nawo mbali m'gulu lawo. Pakuti kutenga nawo mbali m'gulu lawo lomwe lidzauka kuchokera ku phulusa kudzapempha aliyense kulandira chizindikiro chawo. Ngati muphatikizana nawo, mudzagawana nawo chiwonongeko chawo chamuyaya m'nyanja yamoto yomweyo, ndipo nyanja imeneyo inakonzedwa ndi Mulungu kwa Mdyerekezi ndi angelo ake, choncho musaphatikizane nawo.

Simudzapeza mtendere m'dziko lawo lomwe likukwera koma zoipa zosalamulirika zokha, pakuti ndidzakhala nditachotsa woletsa. Ndipo ngati simuli mbali ya malingaliro awo amodzi, ndiye kuti simudzakhala ndi mbali nawo, pakuti adzapha onse otsutsa. Pakuti chizindikirocho chidzasintha kwamuyaya awo amene achivomereza posintha DNA yawo yeniyeni, pakuti mtundu wa anthu unapangidwa m'chifanizo cha Yahweh, ngakhale Satana akufuna kusintha chifanizocho mwamwano mosasinthika kudzera m'mankhwala ake omwe amagulitsidwa m'mabodza akulu. Adzakulonjezani moyo wautali, mtendere, ndi chitukuko, koma mabodza amenewo ndi nyambo chabe. Pakuti chizindikirocho chikatengedwa, palibe chomwe chidzakonze kuwonongeko, pakuti Yahweh walengeza choncho!

Pakuti kapolo sali wamkulu kuposa mbuye wake, ndipo ndikukuitanirani ku ntchito kumene ndidzakutsogolerani pang'onopang'ono, ndipo monga m'masiku a Nowa, momwemonso kudzakhala. Ndipo chotero Ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndinakwaniritsa cholinga changa monga mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu, pakuti ndinapereka magazi anga opanda tchimo kwa Yahweh mwa kufuna kwanga komanso chifukwa cha chikondi changwiro chomwe chikuyaka mumtima mwanga.

Ndipo chotero chifukwa cha chimwemwe chomwe chinaikidwa patsogolo panga, ndinapirira mavuto akulu, mazunzo, ndi zowawa, (ngakhale mopanda chilungamo) chifukwa ndinatenga zotulukapo zonse za machimo onse a anthu pa Ine ndekha. Ndipo ndinalipira tchimo lililonse, mokwanira, ndipo ndinachita mofunitsitsa. Choncho yang'anani kwa Ine, Yesu, woyambitsa ndi womalizitsa wa chikhulupiriro chanu, amene chifukwa cha chimwemwe chomwe chinaikidwa patsogolo panga, ndinapirira mtanda, ndikunyoza manyazi, ndipo ndakhala kudzanja lamanja la mpando wachifumu wa Mulungu. Choncho tsatirani chitsanzo changa ku chipambano chachikulu, pakuti ngakhale m'chipululu ndidzawongola njira yanu.

Choncho mvetsetsani, sipadzakhala mtendere, ndipo ndikukutumizani kukatumikira mu nthawi ya nkhondo yaikulu ndi mavuto. Pakuti chinjoka chidzachita nkhondo ndi oyera mtima ndipo chidzawagonjetsa, pakuti chapatsidwa mphamvu kuti chikwaniritse zimenezo. Ndipo awo amene aitanira pa dzina la Ambuye adzapulumutsidwa, pakuti awo amene sakonda miyoyo yawo mpaka mapeto adzapeza miyoyo yawo kwamuyaya mwa Ine, Khristu Yesu. Pakuti ndatetezera machimo a anthu m'magazi anga opanda tchimo, ndipo ndine mkulu wa ansembe kwamuyaya monga mwa dongosolo la Melikizedeki, amene wapereka magazi anga opanda tchimo kwa Yahweh kamodzi kokha. Choncho, musabwerere ku njira zakale, kapena miyambo yawo, kapena Talmud yawo, kapena Torah; pakuti Torah imasungidwa mokwanira mwa chikhulupiriro mwa Ine, Yesu Khristu.

Ndipo chotero kudzera m'mavuto akulu, zowawa zazikulu, kulimbana kwakukulu, oyera anga adzawala, ndipo mphoto yawo idzakhala yaikulu, pakuti mphoto yanga ndi yaikulu. Pakuti lonjezo likuti chiyani: Awa ndiwo amene anatuluka m'chisautso chachikulu, ndipo atsuka mikanjo yawo, ndi kuyiyeretsa m'magazi a Mwanawankhosa. Chifukwa chake, ali pamaso pa mpando wachifumu wa Mulungu, ndipo amamtumikira usana ndi usiku m'Kachisi wake: ndipo iye amene akhala pa Mpando Wachifumu adzakhala pakati pawo. Sadzamvanso njala, kapena ludzu, kapena dzuwa silidzawawomba, kapena kutentha kulikonse. Pakuti Mwanawankhosa, amene ali pakati pa mpando wachifumu, adzawadyetsa, ndipo adzawatsogolera ku akasupe amadzi amoyo: ndipo Mulungu adzapukuta misozi yonse m'maso mwawo.

Ndipo chotero ndidzatumiza awo amene ndawaitana. Ndikukuitanani kuti mukonde Mulungu ndi mtima wanu wonse, moyo wanu wonse, nzeru zanu zonse, ndi mphamvu zanu zonse, ndipo ndikukutumizani kuti mukonde mnansi wanu monga mudzikondera nokha. Pakuti kupulumuka kudzabwera kuchokera ku mawu opatsa moyo omwe Atate wanga apereka kuti onse amvere. Ndipo chotero kondani mnansi wanu, samaliranani, thandizanani ndi kuchirikizana; pakuti anthu anga adzamwazikana. Adzasonkhana m'madera m'chipululu, ndipo ngakhale chipululu sichidzakhala chotetezeka, ndidzapereka pothawira kwa anthu anga ndipo ndidzapereka chakudya pa zosowa zawo.

Ndipo chotero ndidzatumiza awo amene ndawaitana. Ndikukutumizani kuti mulamulire zosatheka pamene mukuyenda m'chikondi ndi mphamvu yaikulu, ngakhale mphamvu yomwe ndidzakuvekani. Pakuti mudzandiyendera pang'onopang'ono nthawi zambiri pomvera, pamene mukudalira Ine, Khristu Yesu, amene ndidzakutsogolerani ndi mzimu wanga; ndipo kudzoza komwe muli nako kudzakupangitsani kuyenda mopambana. Koma zidzachitika monga lemba limanenera: Odala ndi akufa amene amamwalira mwa Ambuye, kuyambira tsopano, inde, atero Mzimu, kuti apume ku ntchito zawo, ndipo ntchito zawo zikuwatsata.

Pang'onopang'ono mudzayenda, ndipo ndidzakutsogolerani m'njira yotere. Pakuti ndine pothawira panu ndi linga lanu, ngakhale Mesiya wanu weniweni amene mudzamudalira. Ndipo ngakhale chikwi chidzagwa pambali panu, ndi zikwi khumi kudzanja lanu lamanja: koma sizidzakuyandikirani. Pakuti mwasindikizidwa kuti mukwaniritse zolinga zanga, ndipo mphoto yaikulu ndi lonjezo ndakupatsani kuti zikuchirikizeni ndi kukulimbitsani, pakuti ndikukutumizani m'ntchito zomwe Atate wanga wandiikira, ndipo chotero ndikukutumizani monga atumiki ndi aneneri anga.

Choncho pamene mukupita kukalamulira zosatheka, dziwani kuti mudzalamulira zambiri ndi mzimu wobadwa mwa inu. Choncho lamulirani mapiri kuti achotsedwe ndi kuponyedwa m'nyanja, monga muli ndi chikhulupiriro mwa Yahweh. Choncho lamulirani mizimu kuti ichoke pamene mukulankhula mawu amphamvu ndi chikhulupiriro chachikulu mwa Yahweh. Choncho lamulirani matenda onse ndi miliri kuti zichoke pamene mukulankhula mawu omwe ayenera kumveredwa (ngakhale ndi zinthu zonse) pamene mukulankhula mawu amphamvu kwambiri ndi chikhulupiriro chachikulu mwa Yahweh. Pakuti mwa mapulani ndi zolinga za Yahweh ndatumizidwa, ndipo m'mapulani ndi zolinga zimenezo ndikukutumizani, ndipo mudzandichitira ntchito zamphamvu. Pakuti Yahweh ndi zonse mu zonse ndi mzimu wanga, womwe ndidzasindikiza nawo osankhidwa.

Pakuti mwa Ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, chidzalo chonse chimakhala, pakuti Atate wandipatsa zinthu zonse kumwamba ndi padziko lapansi, kuti ndikwaniritse zonse. Ndipo chotero Yahweh ndi zonse mu zonse, ndipo muli ndi mwayi wofika kwa Atate ndi kwa Mwana wake, pakuti chotero Mose anaphunzitsa: Ambuye Mulungu wako adzakuutsira Mneneri pakati pako, wa abale ako, ngati ine, kwa iye mudzamvera. Ndine mneneri ameneyo amene Mose analankhula za iye (ngakhale kuutsidwa kuchokera ku fuko la Yuda), ndipo ndine woposa pamenepo; pakuti Atate watsimikiza kuti Ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndikhale ndi ulemerero wonse, ndipo mwa Ine chidzalo chonse chikhale. Ndine mneneri ameneyo amene Mose analankhula za iye, ngakhale kuutsidwa kwa akufa chifukwa cha cholinga choterocho.

Ndipo chotero ndidzakupatsani zosowa zanu ndi za awo amene ndidzakutumizirani. Pakuti mudzakhala chowunikira, ngakhale chobisika poonekera m'dziko lamdima ndi losakhazikika. Mudzalalikira zinthu zonse zokhudza Ine, Ambuye ndi mpulumutsi wanu, pakuti ndine mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu, ngakhale Paskha wangwiro. Palibe chipulumutso m'dzina lina kupatula langa, ngakhale Yesu Khristu Wolungama.

Ena a inu ndidzakutengani kuchoka kumalo ndi malo, ndipo mudzakhala olankhulira anga, aneneri, ndi atumiki. Mudzafunika kuyesa mizimu ya awo amene mukukumana nawo ndikuyenda mu nzeru yaikulu; pakuti dziko lomwe mwakhala mukulidziwa lidzasinthidwa ndi dziko lopanda lamulo la zipululu zambiri, ndipo ena a inu mudzatumizidwa kukatumikira awo omwe ali m'zipululu, ndipo mudzamvetsetsa malangizo anga, ndipo mudzachita ntchito zazikulu.

Ndipo chotero mvetsetsani kuti pambuyo pa nkhondo ndi chiwonongeko chomwe chidzachitike nditasonkhanitsa okhulupirika anga (ndi kuchotsa woletsa), kudzakhala chisokonezo ndi chiwonongeko chambiri. Anthu adzathamangathamanga, ndipo ambiri adzawonongeka m'kusakhulupirira kopanda chikhulupiriro, ndipo adzasaka ndi kugwira ndi kupha ambiri. Ndipo kwa ambiri, mawu oyamba omwe adzamve sadzakhala anga, ngakhale mawu amenewo adzapereka chipulumutso chonyenga. Ayi, ngakhale chilombo chomwe chidzauka mu mphamvu yaikulu kuchokera ku chinjoka chidzaitana monama ambiri ku mapeto awo amuyaya omwe alibe chiyembekezo.

Onse adzayerekezera, pakuti ndi oyerekezera akulu, ndipo adzayerekezera kuti ndi Mesiya weniweni, ndipo otsatira awo adzati akuimira Mesiya weniweni ndi Mulungu weniweni; koma sindinawatumize, choncho musatsatire zizindikiro zawo zonyenga za chiyembekezo. Pakuti adzapereka mwayi wolowa m'gulu lawo latsopano kuti amange dziko lawo latsopano; koma kuti ulowe, munthu ayenera kutenga chizindikiro. Ndipo kwa awo amene atenga chizindikiro, mwayi wolowa udzaperekedwa m'malawi amoto a buluu m'nyanja yamoto yaikulu yoyaka ndi sulufule. Pakuti onse amene atenga chizindikiro adzakhala ndi mbali yawo m'moto womwe suzimitsidwa ndipo adzakhala atasinthanitsa moyo wosatha ndi kachidutswa ka mkate m'njira za Esau.

Mudzafunika kunyalanyaza mawu onse kupatula anga ndi kuchoka kwa onse amene akundikana. Zidzafuna nzeru yaikulu, luso, ndi kuchenjera kuti mupulumuke, ndipo ndidzakupatsani lilime la anzeru kuti mudziwe momwe mungayankhire munthu aliyense. Pakuti ndidzamveketsa bwino mawu anga kwa inu, ngakhale ali mawu abata, aang'ono. Mudzadziwa mawu anga, pakuti ndidzatsimikizira mawu anga kwa inu nthawi zambiri, choncho musakhale opanda chikhulupiriro koma khalani ndi chikhulupiriro chachikulu m'mawu anga a lonjezo.

Ndidzakuphunzitsani zinthu zonse, ndipo mudzadziwa momwe mungachitire ntchito zomwe ndikukutumizani kuti mukwaniritse. Mawu anga ndi mawu angwiro, ngakhale mawu a wofuula m'chipululu, ndipo ndikulankhula kudzera m'mawu a wofuula m'chipululu; pakuti chotero atumizidwa, ngakhale aneneri anga ndi atumiki omwe apita patsogolo kukawongola njira zanu ndi kumveketsa bwino njira zanu. Ndakuikirani ntchito zambiri zamphamvu, ndipo mudzachita zamphamvu.

Chifukwa chake, dziwani mafuko anga, abale, ndi ana a lonjezo: zokolola ndi zazikulu ndithu, koma antchito ndi ochepa; chifukwa chake pempherani kwa Ambuye wa zokolola, kuti atumize antchito m'zokolola zake. Pitani njira zanu: Taonani, ndikukutumizani ngati ana ankhosa pakati pa mimbulu. Pakuti ndine Ambuye wa Zokolola, ndipo ngakhale antchito akhala ochepa nthawi zonse, pali mphoto yaikulu ndi phindu lamuyaya ndi ulemerero pa ntchito yomwe mudzachita. Pakuti mudzakolola mphoto yambiri ndi zipatso mwa Ine; kokha musafooke pa masautso ndi mavuto anu. Uku ndiko kulekanitsa komaliza, ndipo onse amene anaikidwiratu ku moyo wosatha kuyambira asanakhazikitse maziko a Dziko lapansi adzapezeka, ndipo sadzatayika konse, pakuti palibe amene angathe kuwalanda m'dzanja langa.

Ndipo chotero ntchito zanu zidzatsata mpaka muyaya, ndipo m'ntchito zanu zambiri mudzalemekeza Yahweh, amene wanditumiza Ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, monga Mesiya wanu weniweni. Ndipo mwa zochita zanu zambiri zamphamvu ndi mawu ambiri amchere a choonadi omwe mudzalankhula, mudzalengeza kubwera kwanga posachedwa ndikupita m'ntchito za Yohane Mbatizi kukonzekera kubwera kwa Ambuye: choncho lengezani kubwera kwanga, pakuti ndidzakwaniritsa ndithu mawu anga a lonjezo. Pakuti ndithu kumapeto kwa sautsiyo ya Yakobo, ngakhale kumapeto kwa sabata lomaliza la zaka, ngakhale sabata la makumi asanu ndi awiri la Danieli, ndidzabwera ndi magulu ankhondo akumwamba kudzakhazikitsa Ufumu wanga pa Dziko Lapansi, ngakhale Ufumu wa Mulungu; ndipo ulamuliro wanga wa zaka chikwi udzayamba, ngakhale ulamuliro wa chilungamo, chiyero, ndi mtendere pamene Mkango udzagona pansi ndi Mwanawankhosa!

Ndikukuitanirani ku ntchito kumene ndidzakutsogolerani pang'onopang'ono.

Ndikukuitanani kuti mukonde Mulungu ndi mtima wanu wonse, moyo wanu wonse, nzeru zanu zonse, ndi mphamvu zanu zonse, ndikukonda mnansi wanu monga mudzikondera nokha.

Ndikukutumizani kuti mulamulire zosatheka pamene mukuyenda m'chikondi chachikulu ndi mphamvu yaikulu.

Ndine pothawira panu ndi linga lanu, ngakhale Mesiya wanu weniweni amene mumamudalira.

Ndikukutumizani m'ntchito zomwe Atate wanga wandiikira, ndipo chotero ndikukuikirani monga atumiki ndi aneneri anga.

Ndikukutumizani, ndipo mudzandichitira ntchito zamphamvu.

Ndine mneneri ameneyo amene Mose analankhula za iye, ngakhale kuutsidwa kuchokera ku Yuda chifukwa cha cholinga choterocho.

Ndine woposa pamenepo, pakuti mwa Ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, chidzalo chonse chimakhala.

Ndine mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu, ngakhale Paskha wangwiro.
