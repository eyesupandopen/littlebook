Chifukwa Chiyani Mwasiya Zinsinsi Zanga?

Chifukwa chiyani mwasiya zinsinsi zanga, inu mpingo wofunda wa Laodikaya? Chifukwa chiyani mwasiya chinsinsi chachikulu cha Mulungu? Mwasinthanitsa zinsinsi zopatulika za Atate, ngakhale Khristu mwa inu chiyembekezo cha ulemerero, ndi chuma chonyansa, ndipo Mulungu wanu wasanduka mimba yanu. Komabe, mudzati kwa ine, "Mulibe cholakwa mwa ine. Sindikuthamangira chuma chonyansa, pakuti ndine woongoka pamaso panu." Ndipo ndikuyankha: Kodi simuthamangira uthenga wabwino wabodza wa chitukuko womwe umaphunzitsidwa m'mipingo yanu ya Laodikaya? Mwogulitsa choonadi cha zinsinsi zopatulika za Mulungu kuti mupeze phindu lamba, ndipo mulibenso chiyembekezo cha ulemerero chomwe ndikufuna ndi mtima wonse kuti muchimvetsetse, koma mwasintha chiyembekezo cha ulemerero kukhala chiyembekezo cha phindu lakuthupi lomwe mukufuna kudziunjikira nokha ndi maganizo anu odetsedwa. Sindinakuitanireni ku ndalama ndi kukonda zinthu zakuthupi, koma ku chilungamo ndi chiyero chenicheni chomwe chimapezeka mwa ine.

Tembenukani ku zolakwa zanu ndikugula mankhwala a maso kwa ine, kuti mudzoze maso anu kuti muone choonadi m'Malemba chomwe ndakupatsani kuti mupulumutsidwe. Mwapusitsidwa kuti muthandizire maboma a dziko lapansi, poganiza kuti powathandiza mukuthandiza osankhidwa a Mulungu. Muyenera kuchita manyazi pamaso panga chifukwa mu umbuli wanu simukumvetsa kuti Israyeli weniweni wa Mulungu ndani, ndipo simukumvetsanso chinsinsi chachikulu cha Mulungu chakuti Amitundu ayenera kukhala olowa nyumba anzanu, ndi a thupi limodzi, ndi ogawana nawo lonjezo lake mwa Khristu, kudzera mu Uthenga Wabwino. Pakuti ine ndine mtendere wanu, amene anapanga onse awiri kukhala amodzi, ndipo anagwetsa khoma lapakati logawanitsa pakati panu.

Mwalola kuti chizindikiritso chanu chibedwe ndi sunagoge wa Satana, ndipo mwatayika m'mawu a aneneri onyenga. Kodi simukumvetsa kuti inu ndinu Israyeli wa Mulungu? Onse amene ayenda monga mwa lamulo ili, mtendere ukhale pa iwo, ndi chifundo, ndi pa Israyeli wa Mulungu. Oh, mpingo wofunda, ndani adzakupulumutsani ku dama lanu lachigololo lomwe lakuipitsani kuchoka ku mtima umodzi womwe uli mwa ine, Khristu Yesu Ambuye wanu? Simungathe kupitiriza kugwiritsitsa dziko lapansi lomwe likufali, poganiza kuti potero mukunditumikira; ndipo muyenera kulapa chifukwa chothandizira maboma oipa a dziko lapansi, pakuti Khristu ali ndi chiyanjano chanji ndi Beliyali, ndipo iwo sanditumikira ine koma Satana. Pitani ku Malemba anga ndikufunafuna umunthu wanu weniweni womwe ndaubisa m'makalata anga; pakuti mwafa, ndipo moyo wanu wabisika pamodzi ndi Khristu mwa Mulungu. Ndipo amene wafa wamasulidwa ku tchimo, ndipo lamulo la Mzimu wopatsa moyo lakumasulani ku lamulo la tchimo ndi imfa.

Chifukwa chake, ngati munthu aliyense ali mwa Khristu, iye ndi cholengedwa chatsopano: zinthu zakale zapita, taonani, zinthu zonse zakhala zatsopano. Ndakulengani mwa ine ngati wakhanda, mukupuma mpweya wanu woyamba mu umuyaya; pakuti umuyaya unayamba kwa inu mphindi yomwe munabadwanso mwatsopano ndi mzimu wanga, pakuti ndapanga munthu mmodzi watsopano kuchokera kwa awiri, motero kupanga mtendere. Inde, munthu mmodzi watsopano mwa ine wopangidwa ndi Myuda ndi Wamutundu mofanana mu mpingo wanga wa Mulungu. Amene ndawaitanira ku moyo watsopano mwa ine ndi anthu osankhidwa a Mulungu, ndipo obadwa ndi mzimu wanga ndi ana enieni a Mulungu, ndipo ngati munthu aliyense alibe mzimu wa Khristu, iye sali wake.

Chifukwa chiyani mukukana mbali zina za Malemba, kuziyesa zosayera chifukwa cha zomwe mumati kuganiza bwino? Chifukwa chiyani mukugawa Malemba, poganiza kuti Mawu onse a Mulungu sali a chiphunzitso chanu, machitidwe, ndi kuphunzira? Kuganiza bwino kwabodza pofuna kuwononga choonadi kumakuchititsani khungu ku njira zanga ndi zolinga zanga zomwe ndakukonzerani pasadakhale. Inde, muyenera kuika maganizo anu pa zinthu zakumwamba, koma musatchule mbali za Malemba anga kuti ndi chiwonongeko ndi mdima chifukwa simukuziona ngati kuganiza bwino. Mwakhudzidwa mu ufiti wachikhristu, mukuchita chidziwitso chenicheni cha angelo ogwa omwe anaphunzitsa anthu ula ndi ufiti ndi kudula mizu poyamba.

Chidziwitso chanu cha angelo ogwa ndi bodza lopangidwa mochenjera lomwe lakonzedwa kuti likole moyo wanu ndi mawu abwino ndi zolankhula zokoma. Dziwani kuti anthu ambiri oipa m'zaka zapitazi alowetsa zamatsenga mu Chikhristu, kukuuzani kuti ngati muganizira chinthu kwa nthawi yaitali, chiyenera kuchitika monga momwe mukuganizira. Wopusa iwe! Mulungu sanakulenge kuti umange chinyengo chako chokhudza Chikhristu chako chodzithandiza ndi maganizo achimwemwe. Mukutsatira Yesu wina ndipo mwasiya chikhulupiriro chomwe chili mwa ine, ndipo mwasiya mawu anga ndi malangizo anga m'malo mwa mawu abwino ndi zolankhula zokoma, ndipo mwakolezedwa ndi mimbulu yaumbombo yomwe imakunaminani.

Amakunaminani ndikugwiritsa ntchito molakwika lamulo la Mose, kuuza Akhristu kuti ayenera kupereka chakhumi kuti athandizire mabungwe awo omwe sanamangidwe pa ine, mwala waukulu wapangodya. Fufuzani Malemba, ndikumvetsa kuti monga munthu watsimikiza mumtima mwake, apereke ndipo apereke mokondwera, pakuti Mulungu amakonda wopereka mokondwera. Ndipo ili ndi lamulo lalikulu mu Chipangano Chatsopano lovomerezedwa m'mwazi wanga. Chakhumi chinali chothandizira ansembe a Chi Levi, ndipo iwo anathandiza mkulu wa ansembe ndi nsembe yokwezedwa. Izi zathetsedwa mwa ine, pakuti ndine mkulu wa ansembe kosatha monga mwa dongosolo la Melikizedeki ndipo ndakupangani kukhala ansembe ndi mafumu pamaso panga. Koma inu, oh, mpingo wofunda, mwakhala akhungu ndi masomphenya a mphamvu zabodza ndi maluso, maluso omwe sapezeka mwa Khristu.

Simulamulira chilichonse ndi maganizo anu okha, ndipo ndinu akhungu kwa ine ndi zolinga zanga zomwe ndakukonzerani. Mwatseka makutu anu kuti musamve chiphunzitso cholondola m'malo mwa ziphunzitso zabodza zomwe zikuyabwa makutu anu. Osati zokhazo, koma mukuukira abale omwe asanduka adani anu chifukwa amakuuzani choonadi pa nkhani izi, ndipo mwakana maganizo a anthu a ku Bereya m'malo mwa kutengeka maganizo komwe kumatchedwa Chikhristu molakwika. Mvetsetsani izi, abusa osokera: mudzabwezeredwa m'chiweruzo chifukwa chosocheretsa nkhosa zanga, ndipo kubwezera ndi kwanga ndipo ndidzabwezera.

Mvetsetsani izi, ana anga: simulamulira chilichonse ndi kuganiza kwanu kwabwino, ndipo simuli maginito a chitukuko chifukwa cha maganizo anu achimwemwe. Mwapusitsidwa ndipo mwasocheretsedwa ndi onyenga ambiri. Kuonjezera apo, simungathe kuchita chilichonse popanda ine monga momwe sindingathe kuchita chilichonse popanda Atate wanga; pakuti ndi Mulungu amene amapatsa mphamvu olungama ndipo Atate amene analenga zonse, ndipo aliyense alemekeze dzina lolungama la Yahweh kuposa onse!

Tulukani mu umbombo wanu ndikusiya kukonda ndalama, pakuti kukonda ndalama ndi muzu wa zoipa zonse.

Mukatseka maso anu ku uthenga wanga wonse ndikusankha zomwe mukuziona ngati "zabwino," muli m'dzenje ndi atsogoleri akhungu omwe anakutsogolerani nonse ku mapeto amenewo. Sindinakuitanireni kuti mugwere m'dzenje ana anga, koma ndinakuitanirani kuti mukhale pamaso panga m'chilungamo ndi chiyero chenicheni, choncho dzukani m'matope. Ndinakuitanirani kuti musiye machimo anu ndikulapa njira zanu zosalungama. Ndi mwazi wanga wokha wotetezera mungathe kuyeretsedwa ku machimo anu; maganizo anu abwino sagwira ntchito yachilungamo, ngakhale mwawapanga kukhala mafano a mtima wanu.

Chifukwa chiyani mumalola abale onyenga ndi Ayuda pakati panu kuti azonde ufulu womwe muli nawo mwa ine, Ambuye wolungama? Mukanamvetsa chinsinsi chachikulu cha Mulungu, mukanamvetsa kuti Khristu ndiye mapeto a Lamulo kuti akhale chilungamo kwa aliyense amene akhulupirira, Pakuti Mose akufotokoza chilungamo chomwe chili cha Lamulo, kuti munthu amene achita zinthu izo adzakhala ndi moyo mwa izo. Pakuti ndikuchitiranso umboni kwa munthu aliyense amene wadulidwa, kuti ali ndi ngongole yosunga lamulo lonse. Nanga bwanji za lamulo la Mose, pamenepo?

Ndikwaniritsile zofunikira za lamulo ndikuyeretsa munthu ndi chikhulupiriro mwa ine, pakuti ndapambana, ndipo ndakwaniritsa lamulo, kudzipereka ndekha ngati Paskha wangwiro kamodzi kokha; chifukwa chake, wolungama adzakhala ndi moyo ndi chikhulupiriro. Kodi simunawerenge kuti sindinabwere kudzawononga lamulo kapena aneneri koma kukwaniritsa lamulo ndikupereka chilungamo kwa anthu podzidziwikitsa ndi ine, Ambuye wawo Yesu Khristu.

Ndipo ndikuti, yendani mwa mzimu, ndipo simudzakwaniritsa chilakolako cha thupi, ndipo palibe lamulo lotsutsana ndi chipatso cha mzimu. Chifukwa chake, mwaikidwa m'manda pamodzi ndi ine kudzera mu ubatizo mu imfa yanga, kuti monga Khristu anaukitsidwa kwa akufa ndi ulemerero wa Atate: momwemonso inunso muyenera kuyenda m'moyo watsopano. M'ntchito zanga zotetezera zokha, monga mwanawankhosa wopanda tchimo wa Mulungu, aliyense angathe kugonjetsa tchimo ndikumasulidwa ku temberero la lamulo; pakuti ndapangidwa temberero chifukwa cha inu, pakuti wotembereredwa ndi munthu aliyense wopachikidwa pamtengo.

Mose anafotokoza chilungamo chomwe chili cha lamulo, Kuti munthu amene achita zinthu izo adzakhala ndi moyo mwa izo. Pakuti mwa Khristu Yesu mdulidwe supindula kanthu kapena kusadulidwa, koma cholengedwa chatsopano. Khristu ndiye mapeto a lamulo kwa aliyense amene akhulupirira, komabe ambiri a inu mudzakana mawu omwewa chifukwa mumalemekeza mawu a abusa anu, alaliki, ndi arabi kuposa mawu a Ambuye ndi Mpulumutsi wanu, Khristu Yesu. Kodi simukumvetsa kuti mwamangidwa pa maziko a atumwi ndi aneneri, Yesu Khristu mwiniwakeyo ali mwala waukulu wapangodya? Pakuti palibe maziko ena amene munthu angathe kuika, kuposa omwe aikidwa, omwe ndi Yesu Khristu; chifukwa chake, simuli pansi pa lamulo koma chisomo.

Pakuti amene akhulupirira angathe kulowa mu mpumulo wanga, pakuti amene walowa mu mpumulo wanga wasiyanso ntchito zake, monga Mulungu anachita pa zake pa tsiku lachisanu ndi chiwiri. Kodi simukudziwa kuti lamulo linali mphunzitsi wanu kukuwongolerani kwa ine, Khristu Yesu? Komabe ambiri andikana ine ndi chisomo changa ndi chifundo changa m'malo mwa ntchito zawo zopanda pake zachilungamo. Khungu mwa mbali lina lafika kwa Israyeli kufikira kudzala kwa Amitundu kutakwaniritsidwa. Oh, nthambi ya azitona yakuthengo, usadzitamandire wekha motsutsana ndi mtengo wa azitona womwe unalumikizidwa, ndipo usatsatire khungu lawo; pakuti ndikhoza kupulumutsa Yakobo ndipo ndidzachita zomwezo monga otsala adzapulumutsidwa.

Chifukwa chake munthu asakuweruzeni pa chakudya, kapena chakumwa, kapena pa tsiku lopatulika, kapena mwezi watsopano, kapena masiku a Sabata. Chifukwa chiyani mukuika katundu pa ana anga, kuwauza kuti ayenera kutchula dzina langa m'chilankhulo china chopatulika? Ndikumvetsa zilankhulo zonse ndikuyang'ana pamtima, choncho ndimadziwa pamene ana anga aitana pa dzina langa m'chilankhulo chilichonse chomwe aitana, ndipo ndimawadinda ndi chidindo cha Atate wanga moyenerera, ndipo ndimawabweretsa mu Mpingo wa Mulungu. Ngakhale atate kapena inu simungathe kusunga lamulo, koma lamulo lakwaniritsidwa mwa inu m'chilungamo changa; pakuti lamulo limabwera ku malamulo awiri akulu: Kondani Ambuye Mulungu wanu ndi mtima wanu wonse, moyo, maganizo, ndi mphamvu zanu zonse, ndipo kondani mnansi wanu monga mudzikonda nokha.

Chilungamo sichibwera ndi lamulo koma ndi chikhulupiriro mwa ine, Ambuye Yesu Khristu. Pakuti ndine mkulu wa ansembe kosatha monga mwa dongosolo la Melikizedeki, ndipo ndi mwazi wanga wotetezera mwayesedwa olungama, pakuti ndine Paskha wangwiro, ndipo ndine mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu. Munthu asakusocheretseni pa nkhani izi. Lamulo silinapangidwe chifukwa cha munthu wolungama, koma chifukwa cha osamvera malamulo ndi osamvera. Taonani! Ndifafaniza zolembedwa za malemba, zomwe zinali zotsutsana nanu, zomwe zinali zotsutsana nanu, ndikuzichotsa panjira, kuzikhomera pamtanda wanga. Komabe, mukufunabe kukhala mu ukapolo wa zinthu zofooka ndi zosauka. Malemba ati chiyani: Tulutsani mdzakazi ndi mwana wake, pakuti sitili ana a mdzakazi koma a mfulu.

Kodi sindinanene momveka bwino: Taonani! Ndikuwonetsani chinsinsi, sitidzagona tonse, koma tonse tidzasinthidwa, pakuti ndidzatsika kuchokera kumwamba ndi mfuu, ndi mawu a mngelo wamkulu ndi lipenga la Mulungu ndipo akufa mwa Khristu adzauka poyamba, kenako inu amene muli amoyo ndi otsala mudzatengedwa m'mitambo kukakumana ndi ine, Khristu Yesu mumlengalenga, ndipo mudzakhala nane nthawi zonse. Oh, mpingo wa Laodikaya, mukukana ngakhale mawu anga omveka bwino kwa inu. Chifukwa chake, dziwani kuti ndidzakukanani pagome langa, pamene ndikufunitsitsa kukuikirani malo kumwamba.

Mukuganiza kuti ndi chinthu chopepuka kukana mawu anga ndikusocheretsa ena mu cholakwa chanu. Chifukwa chake, popeza mukufuna tsiku la Ambuye, ndidzakusiyani kuti mupirire tsiku la Ambuye, poona kuti mukuganiza modzikuza kuti mungathe kuima. Mukukana kuonekera kwanga ndikukulitsa mawu a aneneri onyenga kuposa chiphunzitso cholondola chomwe ndapereka mwa ine. Kodi simunawerenge fanizo la anamwali khumi? Ndikukulangizani kuti mukhale ndi chikhulupiriro m'mawu anga ndipo musandikane, kuti ndisadzakukaneni. Kodi mudzakhala ati, ana anga? Kodi mudzakhala m'gulu la anamwali anzeru omwe anadzikonzera okha ndi zingwe zokonzedwa ndi mafuta m'nyale zanu? Kapena mudzatenga malo anu ndi anamwali opusa, kuti, Ambuye, Ambuye titsegulireni; ndipo ndidzati kwa iwo, Sindikukudziwani!

Kodi simunawerenge? Chifukwa chake munthu adzasiya atate wake ndi amayi wake, ndipo adzaphatikizidwa ndi mkazi wake, ndipo awiriwo adzakhala thupi limodzi. Ichi ndi chinsinsi chachikulu: koma ndikunena za Khristu ndi Mpingo. Kodi simukumvetsa mawu a mkwati, mkwatibwi wanga? Muyenera kundigwiritsitsa ngati mutu wanu mofanana ndi momwe mkazi amagwiritsira mwamuna wake ngati mutu wake, pakuti Mkwatibwi ndi Mpingo wanga ndi thupi langa, ndipo ndi munthu uti amene anadana ndi thupi lake? Komabe, mumalola abusa osokera kukusocheretsani pa nkhani iyi, ndipo ambiri a inu mumalemekeza mawu opotoka ochokera kwa aneneri onyenga opotoka kuposa Mawu anga Oyera.

Mwaika chikhulupiriro chanu mwa anthu oipa, anthu omwe sindinawatume; komabe amakupusitsani ndi mawu abwino ndi zolankhula zokoma zomwe sindinawapatse. Mumawatsatira ngati kuti mukunditsatira ine. Lapani! Ambiri a inu mukutsatira atumiki onyenga omwe amaoneka ngati atumiki achilungamo. Koma sizodabwitsa, pakuti Satana amadzisandutsa mngelo wa kuwala, choncho siziyenera kukhala zodabwitsa kuti atumiki ake amachita chimodzimodzi. Ambiri a inu mwakhala akhungu ndi kuwala kwabodza, kuchokera kwa atumiki onyenga omwe sindinawatume, ndipo amalankhula mawu akulu otupa omwe amatsutsana ndi chiphunzitso cholondola mwa ine, Ambuye Yesu Khristu.

Dziimireni nokha, oh, Akhristu opanda msana, ndipo tulukani mwa iye ndikuchoka ku Babulo wa Chinsinsi. Ndikadakonda kuti mukhale otentha kapena ozizira osati ofunda, ndipo sindikufuna kukusanzani m'kamwa mwanga. Chifukwa chake, bwererani kwa ine, ana anga, ndipo bwererani ku mawu anga. Ndigwiritsitseni ngati mutu wanu, pakuti ndine Ambuye amene anakuferani, ndipo munauka pamene ndinauka. Chifukwa chiyani mukudalira aneneri onyenga? Simukusowa kuti munthu aliyense akuphunzitseni, koma kudzoza komwe mwalandira kwa ine kumakhala mwa inu ndikukuphunzitsani zinthu zonse, oh, inu a chikhulupiriro chochepa! Tembenukirani kwa ine ndikubwerera ku njira yopapatiza. Lirani, fuulani, ndikulapa, ana anga! Onse amene ndiwakonda, ndimawadzudzula, kuti kuitana kwanu ndi kusankhidwa kwanu kukhale kotsimikizika.

Dziwani kuti ndimakukondani kwambiri ndipo ndikufuna mtima wanu wonse kuposa ntchito zanu. Bwerani kwa ine ndi mtima wotseguka ndikundipatsa zonse zanu, monga momwe ndakupatsirani zonse zanga. Musaope ndipo musadziweruze nokha, pakuti palibe kutsutsidwa kwa iwo omwe ali mwa Khristu Yesu. Ingobwererani kwa ine ndikuvomereza machimo anu, ndipo ndidzakukhululukirani ndikukubwezeretsani ku zolinga ndi mphatso zomwe ndakupatsani. Imani mwamphamvu ndi okhazikika, pakuti posachedwa ndidzaonekera mwadzidzidzi, ndipo dziko lapansi lidzadziwa kuti ndakutengani, pakuti chinthu ichi sichidzachitika mwachinsinsi. Nthawi ndi yaifupi, ana anga, ndipo posachedwa nthawi sidzakhalaponso.

Ndikhoza kupulumutsa Yakobo ndipo ndidzachita zomwezo monga otsala adzapulumutsidwa.

Ndine mkulu wa ansembe kosatha monga mwa dongosolo la Melikizedeki.

Ndine Paskha wangwiro.

Ndine mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu.

Ndine Ambuye amene anakuferani, ngakhale Yeshua HaMashiach.
