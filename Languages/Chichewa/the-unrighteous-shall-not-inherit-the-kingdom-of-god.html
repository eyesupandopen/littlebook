M'ntchito za Petulo
Epulo 19, 2024
Mabingu 5 & 6

Osalungama Sadzalowa Ufumu wa Mulungu!

Kuchokera kwa Yeshua HaMashiach:

Mwana wanga, ngati udzalandira mawu anga, ndi kubisa malamulo anga mwa iwe; kuti utchere khutu lako ku nzeru, ndi kupereka mtima wako ku kumvetsa. Musaumitse mitima yanu, monga m'kuputa, m'tsiku la chiyeso m'chipululu: ndipo dziwani kuti Ine, Ambuye Yesu Khristu, ndili nanu nthawi zonse ndipo ndili nanu m'miyoyo yanu pamene mugwira Ine ndi chipulumutso changa, pakuti ndine mutu wa Thupi, ndipo ndine Ambuye wanu wokhulupirika, Mkwatibwi wanga wokondedwa. Pakuti ndakukonzerani malo ndi tsogolo, ndipo pali mphoto yaikulu kwa oitanidwa anga, ndi okhulupirika, ndi osankhidwa.

Sindinakuitanireni ku kutayika ndi chilango kapena sindinakuitanireni ku manyazi ndi kudzitsutsa; kodi simunawerenge kuti palibe kutsutsidwa kwa iwo amene ali mwa Khristu Yesu, amene sayenda motsata thupi koma motsata mzimu? Choncho khulupirirani mawu anga! Sindinakuitaneni kuti mukhale mumthunzi wa machimo anu akale, kapena kukhala mumthunzi wa zochita zanu za unyamata, koma ndakuitanani kuti mufe ku njira zanu zakale zauchimo ndi kutenga moyo watsopano mwa ine pamene mukuvula thupi lanu, pamene mukuvula machimo anu ndi kuyenda m'moyo watsopano mwa ine. Taonani! Ndipanga zinthu zonse kukhala zatsopano! Kodi iyi si chiyembekezo chanu?

Ana anga, ndakuchotsani mu kuipa kwa dziko lino lomwe likufa, ndipo ndakulanditsani ku mphamvu ya mdima ndipo ndakusamutsirani mu ufumu wanga, ufumu wa Mulungu, wotsogozedwa ndi ine, Mfumu ya Mafumu ndi Mbuye wa Ambuye, omwe Yahweh ndi Mulungu, pakuti palibe wina. Ndakuitanirani ku ulemerero ndi ukoma mwa ine, ndipo ndakupulumutsani ku ufumu wa Satana, ufumu wa mdima, kulowa mu Ufumu wanga, kumene kuli kuwala! Choncho, galamukani inu amene mukugona, ndipo ukani kwa akufa, ndipo Ine, Yesu Khristu, ndidzakupatsani kuwala.

Kodi simunawerenge m'makalata amene ndinatumiza kwa Mkwatibwi wanga, amene ndinatumiza kwa inu, Mpingo wanga weniweni? Kodi simukudziwa kuti osalungama sadzalowa ufumu wa Mulungu? Musanyengedwe: ngakhale adama, kapena opembedza mafano, kapena achigololo, kapena olobodoka, kapena odziipitsa okha ndi amuna, kapena mbala, kapena osirira, kapena oledzera, kapena olalatira, kapena olanda, sadzalowa ufumu wa Mulungu. Ndipo ena a inu munali otero: koma mwasambitsidwa, koma mwayeretsedwa, koma mwayesedwa olungama m'Dzina la Ambuye Yesu, ndi Mzimu wa Mulungu wathu.

Kodi simukumvetsa malangizo anga, inu, mpingo wofunda wa Laodikaya? Sindinakuitaneni kuti mubwerere ku thupi lanu, kapena sindinakuitaneni kuti mupitirize m'njira zanu zosalungama. Taganizirani, ndipo lingalirani; iye amene akuganiza kuti waima, asamale kuti asagwe! Dzisamalireni nokha, ndi ku chiphunzitso: pitirizani mwa izo: pakuti pochita izi, mudzadzipulumutsa nokha, ndi iwo amene akumva inu. Mvetsetsani, ana anga ofunda, kuti simungathe kukhala moyo wonyengerera pakati pa chabwino ndi choipa, kuganiza kuti muli owongoka pamaso panga, pamene musankha choipa chocheperako pa ziwiri m'miyambo yanu yachisankho yomwe sindivomereza kapena kuchirikiza.

Tulukani mwa iye, anthu anga, popeza SINDINApereke yankho la ndale, ndipo sindinakupatseni mtsogoleri wa dziko kuti apange chilichonse kukhala chachikulu kachiwiri. Mwanyengedwa, ndipo mukukonzedwa kuti mutsatire ndi kuvomereza mwana wa chiwonongeko amene akuyenda poyera pakati panu, ngakhale kuti sanawululidwebe. Koma inu amene mukupempha kuzindikira mudzadziwa kuti iye ndani, kuti mupewe chinyengo chake. Ayi, kunyengerera sikwanu, popeza ndinu ana anga ndi abale anga, ndipo sindinapambane chipulumutso chanu popereka moyo wanga ngati dipo la ambiri ponyengerera ndi Satana; pakuti kunyengerera ndiko kulowa m'magulu awo. Choncho musalakwitse: Mungakhale mukuima m'malo mwanga m'chilungamo ndi chiyero chenicheni, kapena mwalowa m'magulu a Satana mu ufumu wake womwe ukufa, choncho LAPANI!

Kodi mumapindula chiyani ponyengerera ndi choipa? Kodi simukumvetsa kuti muyenera kugonjetsa choipa ndi chabwino? Choncho imani chilili mwa ine ndipo lekani kugwedezeka ngati nsomba yotuluka m'madzi. Ambiri a inu mukuwulutsidwabe ndi mphepo iliyonse ya chiphunzitso ndi chinyengo, pamene akudikirira kuti anyenge. Ine, Khristu Yesu, ndalankhula, ndipo sindidzatonthozedwa. Ambiri a inu mwadzinyengerera nokha ndi mpingo wanga, ndipo ndikukufunsani: mudzayima pakati pa maganizo awiri mpaka liti? Ngati Ambuye ali Mulungu, mutsateni: koma ngati Baala, ndiye mutsateni. Mwachidule, simungathe kunyengerera ndi kusankha mtundu uliwonse wa choipa ndikuganiza kuti mukuima m'malo mwanga; pamene musankha choipa, mumasankha magwero a choipacho ngati mbuye wanu ndi bwana wanu kukhala kapolo wa tchimo. Kodi simudziwa, kuti kwa amene mudzipereka nokha kukhala akapolo kuti mumvere, ndinu akapolo a amene mumamumvera: kaya a tchimo ku imfa, kapena a kumvera ku chilungamo?

Ndimveni ndipo ndimveni bwino, Mpingo wanga; dziwani kuti ndikuima monyansidwa ndi machitidwe omwe afala m'dziko lanu ndi padziko lonse lapansi, omwe mwanyengerera ndi kulola mu Mpingo wanga kuti asautse ana anga. Monga m'masiku a Nowa, momwemonso kudzakhala. Masiku a Nowa inali nthawi yomwe dziko lapansi linkalamulidwa ndi zonyansa zoipa zopangidwa ndi angelo ogwa, mbewu yawoyeni, mbewu ya choipa yopangidwa kuti ikuwonongeni kotheratu: ndipo adzabwerera, pakuti taonani, ndikubweretsa zimphona kuti zikwaniritse mkwiyo wanga. Iwo anakwaniritsa izi pochotsa ndi kuphwanya malire achilengedwe omwe Atate wanga anakhazikitsa kuti mtendere ndi chilungamo zipambane, koma pochotsa ndi kuwoloka mwachisembwere malire omwe ali oonekera m'chilengedwe chenicheni, omwe Atate wanga anakhazikitsa, ambiri a inu mwadziipitsa nokha mwa kuvomereza kwanu choipa chomwe chafala m'nthawi yanu. Chinthu chomwe chakhalapo, ndicho chomwe chidzakhale: ndipo chomwe chachitidwa, ndicho chomwe chidzachitidwe; ndipo palibe chinthu chatsopano pansi pa dzuwa. Kodi simunawerenge, inu a chikhulupiriro chochepa? Kuchotsa malire a Mulungu kumayambitsa misala, ndi matenda, ndi chisokonezo cha zinthu zonse. Ambiri a inu mumasangalala ndi matenda a maganizo, kuganiza kuti mwamuna akhoza kukhala mkazi ndipo mkazi akhoza kukhala mwamuna, ndipo mukutsogolera ana anu m'zinyengo zanu. Ambiri a inu mumapita kumatchalitchi otsogozedwa ndi atumiki onyenga a mathanyula omwe ali omasuka ndi odzionetsera m'matenda awo, ndipo mukuganiza kuti akulankhula m'malo mwanga? Dziwani izi: ngati muli mu mkhalidwe wauchimo umenewo, ndidzakuperekani ku zilakolako zomwe mumazilakalaka kwambiri, ndipo mudzakolola chipatso cha njira zanu zodetsedwa.

Taonani! Chifukwa cha ichi, Mulungu anawapereka ku zilakolako zonyansa: pakuti ngakhale akazi awo anasintha kagwiritsidwe ntchito kachilengedwe kukhala komwe kuli kotsutsana ndi chilengedwe: Ndipo chimodzimodzinso amuna, kusiya kagwiritsidwe ntchito kachilengedwe ka mkazi, anatentha m'chilakolako chawo wina ndi mzake, amuna ndi amuna akuchita zomwe zili zosayenera, ndi kulandira mwa iwo okha malipiro a cholakwa chawo omwe anali oyenera. Koma ayi, sikokwanira kutsogozedwa ndi olobodoka ndi mabodza awo; mumalola ana anu kuti anyengedwe ndipo ngakhale kudulidwa ziwalo pamaso panga.

Pali zotsatirapo zazikulu zomwe zasungidwa chifukwa cha zochita izi, ndipo sizidzakhala zosalangidwa. Ndipo komabe mukupitiriza m'misala yanu, ndipo pokhapokha mutalapa, ndidzakusanzani m'kamwa mwanga. Bwerani kwa ine m'kulapa, ndipo khalani m'chiguduli ndi phulusa, kuti ndidziwe kuwona mtima kwa kulapa kwanu ndikulira ndi kuwuwa chifukwa cha machimo anu kuti musalipire mtengo m'chiweruzo. Ambiri a inu ndinu odzikuza chifukwa mumayesa kundipanga Ambuye pamene muli ndi mtima wopanda chikhulupiriro, pakuti ndi mtima munthu amakhulupirira ku chilungamo. Choncho khalani ndi chikhulupiriro kuti mupulumutsidwe; chikhulupiriro chanu chidzaonetsedwa kwa onse mwa kukana kwanu tchimo ndi kutuluka m'njira zanu zonyansa. Oipa amayenda mbali zonse, pamene anthu onyansa kwambiri akwezedwa. Koma inu mukuvomerezabe njira za wachiwerewere, ndipo mukuthandiza kubweretsa choipa chachikulu m'dziko, mpaka kufika poti mumavotera ogwiririra ana kuti atsogolere mayiko anu. Mumasowa ngakhale mlingo woyambirira wa kuzindikira, ndipo simutsatira njira yanga; ndipo ngati mupitiriza, ndidzakuperekani kotheratu ku zilakolako zanu zonyansa kuti thupi liwonongedwe kuti mzimu upulumutsidwe.

Ndiloleni nditsegule maso anu, inu amene muli mbuli mofunitsitsa ndi akhungu, inu amene mumakhala m'litsiro ndi mdima, kugubuduzika m'matope. Simungathe kuvomereza choipa ndikuganiza kuti mukunditumikira, pakuti ndinakaniza choipa ndi chiyeso mpaka dontho lomaliza la magazi litatuluka mwa ine pa nthaka yofiira, pakuti ndine Adamu wotsiriza ndipo ndapangidwa mzimu wopatsa moyo. Ambiri a inu mwakhungu ndi mawu abwino ndi zolankhula zokongola ndipo mumavomereza mokwanira kwa zigawenga ndi anthu owopsa omwe amagwira ana. Amagwiririra ana, amazunza ana chifukwa cha zilakolako zawo zoipa: Pakuti motero kumabwera mkwiyo wa Mulungu pa Babulo Wachinsinsi, koma ambiri a inu mukupitiriza kuvomereza zilombo zankhanza izi zomwe zayikidwa pambali pa moto wa helo wamuyaya womwe sudzazimitsidwa. Tulukani mwa iye, anthu anga, kapena mudzagawana m'miliri yake ndi chiweruzo chowopsa chomwe chikubwera pa iye; ndipo ndakuchenjezani. Pakuti Khristu ali ndi chiyanjano chanji ndi Beliyali? Ngati mugawana m'mabodza ake, mudzagawana m'miliri yake; choncho dzipatuleni kotheratu m'mawu ndi m'zochita, ndipo musagwirizane nawo ngakhale pang'ono. Musakhale moyo wosasamala, wonyengerera; pakuti ndidzalanga onse amene amatero. Mukuganiza kuti chifukwa muli ndi moyo wosatha simungathe kukumana ndi kutayika kwakukulu ndi zotsatirapo? Ndidzavula mphoto zanu ndikuzipereka, ndipo mudzakhala wamng'ono kwambiri mu ufumu wakumwamba ngati mupitiriza, pakuti akutero Yeshua HaMashiach, Mkwati, kwa Mkwatibwi wanga!

Kuchokera kwa Yahweh, Mulungu Wamkulu Wamuyaya:

Inu opanda phindu amene mumavulaza chilengedwe changa, inu opanda phindu amene mwadzigulitsa nokha kwa Satana, inu opanda phindu amene mwapanga Satana kukhala Mulungu wanu, inu opanda phindu amene mumakana mwana wanga Yesu Khristu ndi chipulumutso chomwe ndapereka m'dzina lake, inu opanda phindu amene mwalola ana anu kuti atenthedwe m'moto wa Moloki. Ndidzakupatsani dziko lomwe mumalakalaka, ndipo ndakukonzerani dziko lopanda ine, dziko lopanda mwana wanga Khristu Yesu, dziko lokhala ndi nyanja yaikulu yamoto ndi sulufule yanu yokha.

Ndidzakupatsani mdima womwe mumalakalaka kwambiri, pakuti kuwala kwanga sikudzakhala komweko, ndipo mudzakhala muyaya wanu wolekanitsidwa ndi ine, Yahweh, Mulungu wamkulu wowala, yemwe ulemerero wake ndi kuwala kwake palibe thupi lomwe lingathe kuyang'ana ndipo yemwe ali kuyambira muyaya mpaka muyaya. Inu opanduka, oipa amene mwawononga chilengedwe changa kuyambira pachiyambi m'zilakolako zanu ndi chidani ndi nsanje, dziwani kuti mwadziunjikira chilango chachikulu ndi mazunzo kwa inu nokha omwe simudzachiritsidwa ndipo kwa onse amene asocheretsedwa ndi mabodza anu ndi kukana Yesu Khristu, kukana kutembenuka kuchoka ku mabodza anu kupita ku choonadi changa.

Mukuganiza kuti kupotoza njira zanga ndi kuchotsa malire anga ndi chinthu chopepuka, koma mudzamvetsa mkwiyo wanga nthawi yomweyo lawi loyamba likakukhudzani, ndipo mudzamezedwa m'moto ndi sulufule zomwe simudzathawa. Kuonjezera apo, mwapha, mwagwiririra, mwafunkha, mwazunza, mwapweteka, mwapha, mwawononga, ndipo mwatenga mbali m'zolakwa zopweteka kwambiri, ngakhale kulakalaka thupi lachilendo; ndipo mwadziphatikiza nokha kwa angelo ogwa, choncho ndidzakulumikizani ku nyanja yamoto yomwe ndakonza kwa Mdyerekezi ndi angelo ake, ndipo simudzathawa. Mulibe mtendere.

Ndidzakupatsani chilungamo, pakuti ndine Mulungu wa chilungamo, ndipo ndine Mulungu wobwezera. Komabe, mudzaima modzikuza kutsutsana nane, kudandaula mlandu wanu, kuganiza kuti ndidzabwerera m'mbuyo ku njira zanu za satana za: chita chomwe ufuna, chidzakhala lamulo lonse. Mulibe mtendere. Ndidzabwezera, ndipo mukuti ndine Mulungu wachikondi (zomwe ndili), koma ndiye mukuyembekezera kuti ndisapereke chilungamo kwa iwo amene aipitsa chilengedwe changa, kwa iwo amene anazunza ndi kupha mwana wanga wobadwa yekha pamene anadzipereka yekha ngati dipo la onse; inde, mukuyembekezera kuti ndisapereke chilungamo kwa inu chifukwa cha njira zanu zopanda phindu pamene mwapha, kuzunza, ndi kupundula osalakwa ndipo mwapha ndi njala ana anga chifukwa cha zolinga zanu.

Zoonadi, Satana mwiniwake angawononge zonse zomwe zili zanga chifukwa cha phindu lake laumbombo, ndipo ngakhale pamenepo mumayesa kundineneza za kupusa m'ziweruzo zanga zolungama pamene ndibwera kudzeteza anga. Masiku anu osokeretsa ana anga atsala pang'ono kutha. Taonani! Chilombo chanu ndi mneneri wonyenga adzawonongedwa kotheratu, ndipo ndidzawaponya amoyo m'nyanja yaikulu yotentha ndi moto ndi sulufule; adzakudikirirani kuti mupite nawo mu cholowa chanu.

Chiweruzo chachikulu chili pa iwe, kalonga wa mdima; ufumu wako ndi onse amene amakutsatira atsala pang'ono kuwonongedwa kotheratu. Pakuti inu sindinu milungu, ndipo simungathe kudzipulumutsa nokha opanda phindu m'dzanja langa, ndipo ndidzakumangani m'mdima waukulu womwe mumalakalaka kwambiri, ngakhale phompho lakuya kwambiri ndidzakumangani ndi unyolo waukulu womwe simungathe kuudula. Inde, m'phompho mudzakhala tsiku limodzi, pakuti tsiku limodzi kwa Ambuye lili ngati zaka chikwi, ndi zaka chikwi ngati tsiku limodzi. Ndipo ngakhale pamenepo ndidzakumasulani kwa kanthawi kochepa kuti muyese ndi kusonkhanitsa omaliza a onyansa omwe angakutsateni inu ndi njira zanu. Ndipo ndidzakuwonongani nonse ndi moto, ndipo mipando yachiweruzo idzakhazikitsidwa, ndipo ndidzaweruza nonse ochita zoipa, ndipo mudzapita ku chilango chamuyaya kuchokera pamaso pa Ambuye. Mudzatentha pamaso pa angelo ndi pamaso pa mwanawankhosa, ndipo adzakuonani m'mazunzo ndi chilango, pakuti mwawalakwira onse, ndipo mudzalipira mokwanira machimo anu kwa muyaya wonse.

Ndiyeno mudzasindikizidwa m'ndende yanu, ndipo mudzaiwalika, ndipo simudzakumbukiridwanso, pakuti zinthu zakale zidzapita ndi kuiwalika. Pakuti taonani, ndidzapanga kumwamba kwatsopano ndi dziko lapansi latsopano momwe mukukhala chilungamo; ndipo Ine, Yahweh, Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya wa milungu yonse, pakuti palibe wina wofanana nane, taonani, ndidzapanga zinthu zonse kukhala zatsopano, pakuti Ine, Yahweh ndalankhula! Yahweh, Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya, adzakhazikitsa mpando wake wachifumu padziko lapansi, ndipo malo anga okhala adzakhala pakati pa anthu, ndipo simudzakhalakonso, inu opanda phindu, ndipo mudzatentha kwamuyaya m'ndende yanu yoiwalika ya mazunzo yomwe simungathe kuthawa, pakuti ndidzayika chidindo ndipo palibe amene angachithyole.

Komabe, ambiri a inu amene mumamvera chisoni Satana mumandineneza za kupusa chifukwa chotenga chilungamo ndi kubwezera m'manja mwanga kuti ndipereke iwo amene adzipanga okha kukhala oyenera chilango chamuyaya ku malawi amoto. Imvani mawu anga, inu okhala padziko lapansi: Mwana wanga akubwera, ndipo akubwera ndi kubwezera kwakukulu, mkwiyo, ndi ukali chifukwa cha zolakwa zomwe zachitidwa ndi manja anu. Nthawi za chiweruzo, ngakhale chiweruzo cha tsiku lalikulu la Khristu chatsala pang'ono kuphulika padziko lonse lapansi, ndipo sikudzakhala mankhwala kwa oipa, palibe mankhwala kwa Satana, ndipo palibe mankhwala kwa onse amene adziphatikiza naye, popeza onse adzapita ku chiwonongeko chamuyaya, ndipo ndidzawabwezera zonse zomwe abweretsa pa osalakwa.

Choncho, ndidzabweretsa zilakolako za mitima yawo yoipa ndi ntchito zawo zonse zoipa kubwerera pa iwo kwa muyaya wonse. Onani mkwiyo wanga ukuwira ndipo dziwani kuti nthawi za kubwezeretsedwa kwa zinthu zonse zili pafupi, ndipo palibe manambala omwe atsala pa mawotchi akumwamba, pakuti manambala onse agwa kuchokera pa nkhope ya wotchi, pakuti nthawi yatha! Ndikulangiza onse amene awerenga mawu awa kuti athamangire kwa mwana wanga Yesu Khristu m'kulapa ndi kumupanga kukhala Ambuye; pakuti palibe amene adzabwera kwa ine kupatula kudzera mwa Yesu Khristu, pakuti palibe dzina lina lomwe ndapereka la chipulumutso, ndipo mafano anu onse adzatenthedwa. Pali nthawi yotsala ya chipulumutso, ndipo aliyense amene aitana pa dzina la Ambuye mpaka mapeto adzapulumutsidwa. Choncho thamangirani kwa Yesu Khristu pamene mukanatha, pakuti tsiku la Ambuye lafika, ndipo ndidzamasula Mwana wanga, ndipo mkwiyo wa Mwanawankhosa udzakhutitsidwa. Mpsompsoneni Mwanayo kuti asakwiye, ndipo muwonongeke m'njira, pamene mkwiyo wake uyaka pang'ono: Odala ndi onse amene amayika chikhulupiriro chawo mwa iye.


Kuti Muloze: Yesaya 13:1 kuchokera ku Septuagint: Ndikupereka lamulo, ndipo ndikuwabweretsa: zimphona zikubwera kudzachita mkwiyo wanga, kusangalala nthawi yomweyo ndi kutukwana.
