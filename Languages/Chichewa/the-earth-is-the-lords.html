Dziko Lapansi ndi la Ambuye!

Dziko lapansi ndi la Ambuye ndi chidzalo chake: Ngakhale wolanda ndi mbala wayika chidziwitso chake, akhoza kuchita kokha zomwe Atate wanga wapereka m'manja mwake kuti akwaniritse mu dongosolo lake loyera la chiwombolo cha anthu. Musamuwope! Ayenera kupitiriza kwa nyengo, popeza ola lake lafika; koma musamuwope, inu olungama anga, okhulupirika anga; musamuwope! Kodi sindinakupatseni chigonjetso pa iye? Mavuto a Yakobo si a okhulupirika anga, ndipo omwe abwera kwa ine mu nthawi zotsitsimula adzakwezedwa kwambiri m'chipinda cha mpando wachifumu wa Mulungu. Inde, anthu anga okhulupirika, mphoto yayikulu yasungidwa kwa inu chifukwa cha kukhulupirika kwanu komwe mwasonyeza mokhulupirika kwa ine.

Ndakupangani kukhala mafumu ndi ansembe kwa ine mu Ufumu wanga womwe ukubwera ndi pamaso pa Atate wathu, choncho chilimikani, ana anga, mu mphindi zomaliza izi za ola lomaliza. Kuonekera kwanga kuli pafupi, ndipo chiwombolo chanu chili pafupi ngati mpweya wanu wotsatira, choncho imani mokhulupirika pamaso panga, podziwa kuti ndine mphamvu yanu ndi chipulumutso chanu ndipo mwasindikizidwa mwa ine, monga ndakugulani ndi magazi anga pamene ndinapereka moyo wanga chifukwa cha inu kuti muyanjanitsidwe m'chikondi kwa Atate wathu wa kumwamba. Ndikulakalaka tsikulo, ndipo Atate wathu akulakalaka tsikulo, pamene mudzaima pamaso pake m'matupi omwe ndili nawo kwa inu mu chiwombolo chanu chathunthu, ndipo sipadzakhalanso temberero pa osankhidwa anga.

O, munthu, ndiwe ndani kuti utenge gawo pa cholowa changa? Kodi sukudziwa kuti ndinagula dziko lapansi ndi zonse zili mmenemo ndi magazi anga opanda tchimo? Dziko lapansi ndi la Ambuye ndi chidzalo chake, ndipo chilungamo chikubwera pa dziko lapansi! Ngakhale mutawononga dziko lapansi ndi kulipanga kukhala malo abwinja a zonyansa zanu, ngakhale mutalumbira kukhulupirika kwa mdani wanga wamuyaya ndi kufunafuna kuzunzika kwanu chifukwa cha phindu lakanthawi, simukumvetsa kuti mukuchita nkhondo ndi miyoyo yanu pamene mukudziunjikira mkwiyo pa tsiku la mkwiyo, pamene ndidzabwezera munthu aliyense monga mwa ntchito zake.

O, munthu wopusa, ukunena wekha kuti milungu yako idzakupulumutsa, pamene ilibe mphoto kwa iwe, ndipo ukunyengedwa ndi kunyenga ena m'zinyengo zako. Milungu yako yamwala singathe kulankhula; mafano ako m'mitima yanu sangathe kumva; sangathe kukupulumutsa, o, wopembedza mafano! Zikanakhala bwino kwa iwe kuti ukanapanda kubadwa, chifukwa mkwiyo wa Mulungu ukhala pa iwe chifukwa cha njira zako zouma mtima. Ngakhale pakamwa pako pakhala manda otseguka, kulavula zinthu zazikulu zotsutsana ndi Mbuye wa Ambuye, mawu ako adzabwerera kwa iwe, ndipo zilakolako zako zoyipa zidzatembenuzidwa pa iwe mu chilango chako chamuyaya chomwe chikubwera. O, bwenzi utatembenukira kwa ine ndi kulandira chipulumutso kuchokera ku mkhalidwe wako wogwa. O, bwenzi utangovomereza dzina langa kuchokera pamilomo yako yonama. Koma ayi, wakonda mabodza ako, ndipo wakonda zinyengo zako, ndipo wagulitsa miyoyo yanu ku chiwonongeko chamuyaya chifukwa cha kachidutswa ka mkate komwe sikangakuthandizeni; pakuti Ine ndine mkate wamoyo! Tsoka lako lidzakugwera mwadzidzidzi ndipo popanda mankhwala. Ndidzang'amba mtundu wako; ndidzawononga malonda ako ndi chuma chako; mu kamphindi ndidzakulanda chuma chako; zoonadi, ndidzakuvula chisangalalo chonse mu kamphindi, ndipo umphawi wako ndi masautso ako zidzakutha. Mkwiyo wa Mwanawankhosa ukuyandikira, pamene kubwezera kwanga komwe Atate wanga wandipatsa kudzachitidwa, pakuti ndine woyenera kutsegula mabuku ndi kumasula zizindikiro.

Mwaganiza molakwika kuleza mtima kwanga ndi kupirira kwanga kukhala kufooka ndi kunyalanyaza. Mwanena m'mitima yanu: Ali kuti Ambuye? Iye ndi nthano chabe yakale. Taphunzira zambiri kuposa njira zopulumutsa za Ambuye; mu chidziwitso changa, ndine wapamwamba, pakuti matekinoloje anga ndi amphamvu kuposa buku lina lakale la zotsutsana ndi nthano. Zoonadi, ambiri a inu simukhulupirira ngakhale kuti ndinabwera poyamba ndi kukhetsa magazi anga owombola chifukwa cha machimo ndi chipulumutso cha anthu. Ndi bwino kuti mukanapanda ngakhale kubadwa, chifukwa mkwiyo womwe mwadziunjikira motsutsana ndi moyo wanu udzakugwerani popanda mankhwala, ndipo mudzadziwa bwino machimo anu, ndipo masautso anu onse ndi chiwonongeko chidzakhala mkazi wanu. Mudzadya zonyansa zanu, ndipo njira zanu zonyansa zidzakhala nanu nthawi zonse, pamene mukuikidwa m'zipatso za mkwiyo mu ntchito zanu zoyipa zomwe mwachita mu choyipa chachikulu motsutsana ndi dzina langa. Chokani kwa ine; sindikukudziwani!

Ndani mwa inu amene amalankhula monama m'dzina langa? Mukunena kuti mukundidziwa, kundiyimira; ena a inu mukuganiza ngakhale kuti mungathe kundilamula kuti ndichite zomwe mukufuna ndi kudzipezera phindu lakuthupi ndi ntchito zanu zabwino, zomwe sizili kanthu koma nsanza zonyansa. Mungalankhule bwanji m'malo mwanga pamene simukundidziwa? Mungalankhule bwanji m'malo mwanga pamene sindinakupatseni mawu? Mungalankhule bwanji m'malo mwanga kwa anthu anga pamene sindinakutumizeni? Ayi, mwadzitumiza nokha, kugulitsa katundu wanu woyipa chifukwa cha phindu loyipa laumbombo ndi chifukwa cha kutamandidwa ndi anthu komwe mukulakalaka kwambiri. Wopusa iwe! Choka kwa ine, pakuti sindinakudziwe konse!

Inu aneneri onyenga, omwe mumanditcha Ambuye m'dzina lokha; inu aneneri onyenga, omwe mumalankhula mabodza akulu motsutsana ndi miyoyo yanu; mudzawonongeka kotheratu, ndipo ndidzakulavulani mkamwa mwanga. Choncho, lapani! Bwererani kwa ine ndipo ndidzabwerera kwa inu. Tulukani pakati pa mafano anu ndipo chokani m'magulu anu otembereredwa ndipo bwerani kwa ine ndi kundipanga Ambuye wanu! O, bwenzi mutalandira chipulumutso changa; komabe mukupitiriza m'machimo anu ndi kukhululukira liwongo lanu pamene mukugwira mawu a Mawu anga opatulika kuchokera mumtima wopanda kanthu, mtima wodzaza ndi chigololo ndi dama pamene mukuthamangira kwa mafano anu. Ndani adzakupulumutsani ku mkwiyo wanga pamene mwasocheretsa anthu anga chifukwa cha phindu lanu? Mukanandipanga Ambuye monga pakamwa panu ponama palengezera, ndikanakupulumutsani ku machimo anu ndi kukupangani kukhala athunthu mwa ine. Ndikanaweta mabala omwe woyipayo amakuchitirani. Ndikanakupatsani njira ndi cholinga ndi kukutumizani pa dziko lapansi ndi pakamwa podzaza ndi madalitso ndi choonadi pamene mukanatsogolera anthu anga kwa ine.

Musaganize kuti ndikufuna thandizo lanu, pakuti ndine Ambuye woona wa kumwamba ndi dziko lapansi, ndipo ndine Mfumu ya Mafumu ndi Mbuye wa Ambuye. Omwe ndawadzoza mu utumiki apatsidwa ulemu waukulu kulankhula m'malo mwanga ndi kusamalira gulu langa, ulemu womwe ndidzaufupa ndi chuma chamuyaya; komabe, mwandisinthanitsa ndi mtundu wonyenga wa Khristu. Yesu wina mwadzipangira nokha; mwakonda mzimu wa wokana Khristu, ndipo mwachimwa kwamuyaya motsutsana ndi miyoyo yanu polengeza kuti palibe mu Mpingo wanga womwe ndinaugula ndi magazi anga, tchimo lomwe simudzachira. Zoonadi, zinthu zonsezi ndi zina zidzakugwerani mu mkwiyo wanga pokhapokha mutadzichepetsa pamaso panga ndi kundipanga Ambuye weniweni polapa m'chiguduli ndi phulusa. Musamapereke zifukwa pa mawu anga pamene mukusewera ndi matanthauzo a mawu anga! Lapani! Khalani m'chiguduli ndi phulusa ndipo lirani chifukwa cha zoyipa zanu, ndipo ndidzakulandirani kwa ine ndi kukubwezerani ku mphoto yanu yomwe ndikanapereka kwa wina.

Ndine Ambuye wolungama.

Ndine mpulumutsi wa dziko lapansi, ngakhale Mfumu ya Mafumu ndi Mbuye wa Ambuye.

Ndine amene ndimayenda pakati pa zoyikapo nyale zisanu ndi ziwiri ndipo amene amalamulira angelo asanu ndi awiri a mipingo isanu ndi iwiri.

Ndine mwanawankhosa amene anaphedwa kuyambira pachiyambi cha dziko lapansi, ndipo mwa ine muli moyo wosatha.

Ndigwira makiyi a imfa ndi gehena, ndipo Atate wanga wagawana mpando wake wachifumu ndi ine, kuti ndigawe mpando wanga wachifumu ndi okhulupirika anga, oitanidwa, ndi osankhidwa. Ndimayenda ndi anthu anga ndipo ndimalankhula ndi anthu anga, ndipo ndine mbusa woona amene amaweta gulu lomwe Atate wanga wandipatsa. Ndinu mwana wa diso langa, okhulupirika anga, ndipo ndinu mwana wa diso la Atate wanga, ndipo pa nthawi iyi ndi ola ili mwabwezeretsedwa ku chilungamo ndi chiyero choona.

Ola likuyandikira, ndipo muyenera kusiya ntchito zanu ndi kuyang'ana kuonekera kwanga, pakuti kwatsala pang'ono kugwera dziko lapansi pamene ndikukusonkhanitsani kuchokera kumakona anayi a dziko lapansi. Zoonadi, zoonadi, ndidzasonkhanitsa nkhosa zanga zoona m'nkhokwe yanga mphepo zamkuntho zisanabwere zomwe zidzawononga dziko lapansi loyipali, pakuti ufumu wa Satana udzang'ambidwa ndi kuwonongedwa kotheratu kuti upereke njira kwa Ambuye wolungama amene adzalamulira dziko lapansi kuchokera ku mpando wachifumu wa Davide mu chilungamo ndi chiyero choona, ndipo ndidzapatsa mphamvu mkwatibwi wanga mu ufumu womwe ukubwera, pakuti iwo ndi mafumu anga ndi ansembe, ndipo adzalamulira mu chilungamo choona ndi chiyero, pakuti mphoto yanga ili m'manja mwanga kwa okhulupirika.

Ndine mphamvu yanu ndi chipulumutso chanu, ndipo mwasindikizidwa mwa ine.

Ndine mwanawankhosa amene anaphedwa kuyambira pachiyambi cha dziko lapansi.

Ndine mbusa woona amene amaweta gulu lomwe Atate wanga wandipatsa.
