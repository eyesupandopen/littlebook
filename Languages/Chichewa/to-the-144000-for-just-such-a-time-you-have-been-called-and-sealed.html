Kwa 144,000: Nthawi Ngati Ino Mwayitanidwa ndi Kusindikizidwa

Ndiimireni ndi kutsatira chitsanzo changa, pakuti Ahebri amati chiyani pakundiimira kwanga kwa Yahweh? Amene chifukwa cha chimwemwe choikidwa patsogolo pake, anapirira mtanda, nanyoza manyazi, ndipo wakhala kudzanja lamanja la mpando wachifumu wa Mulungu. Chotero m'chikondi changa kwa Atate, chomwe sichingasweke, ndi m'kumvera kwanga chifukwa cha chikondi, ndi m'chikondi changa kwa anthu ndi chikhumbo changa chodzipereka nsembe kuti ndikupulumutseni, ndinapirira chilango choopsa, zowawa, ndi masautso ndipo ndinachita mofunitsitsa, ndi chimwemwe mumtima mwanga. Ndinadziwa kuti mwa nsembe yanga ndi imfa, ambiri adzayesedwa olungama ndi amoyo ndi mphamvu ya kuuka kwanga chifukwa Yahweh mwiniwake anandiukitsa kwa akufa, pakuti nkosatheka kuti imfa indigwire. Ndipo ndi mphamvu yomweyo, inunso mudzaukitsidwa ku moyo watsopano, pakuti ndalipira mtengo popereka magazi anga opanda tchimo ngati dipo la onse amene adzakhulupirira dzina langa, ndipo aliyense amene adzaitana pa dzina la Ambuye adzapulumutsidwa, amene sakonda miyoyo yawo mpaka chimaliziro!

Ndipo tsopano ndikukupemphani, okhulupirika anga ndi osankhidwa, ngakhale abale anga, kuti mundiimire ndipo musadzibweze. Pakuti ndinanena momveka bwino m'kalata yanga kwa Ahebri: Tsopano wolungama adzakhala ndi moyo ndi chikhulupiriro: koma ngati wina abwerera m'mbuyo, moyo wanga sudzakondwera naye.

Lolani zimenezo zilowe m'maganizo, ndipo muwerengenso, ndipo musabwerere ku Torah, pakuti Torah imasungidwa ndi chikhulupiriro mwa ine; chotero, wolungama adzakhala ndi moyo ndi chikhulupiriro. Inu mwadziwika ndi ine, ndipo ndine amene ndakuyesani olungama m'ntchito zanga zotetezera ndi magazi anga opanda tchimo.

Ambiri a inu mumavutika ndi lingaliro lakuti ndinatenga malo anu m'chiweruzo pamtengo chifukwa cha machimo omwe sindinachite, ndipo ndinachita zimenezo mofunitsitsa ndi mwachimwemwe kuti ndibweretse chipulumutso kwa munthu wachibadwa woipa, kuti ndiwaombole kwa Yahweh, ngakhale Mulungu wanu ndi Atate wanu, ngakhale Atate wa Kuunika amene mulibe kusintha kapena mthunzi wotembenuka. Pakuti Yahweh sasintha; iye ndi yemweyo dzulo, lero, ndi kunthawizonse!

Iye ndi Mulungu yemweyo amene makolo anu anapangana naye pangano, ngakhale pa Phiri la Sinai pamene Mose analandira chilamulo kuchokera kwa Yahweh mwiniwake. Ndipo ngakhale chilamulo chili choyera, ndi lamulo lili loyera, ndi lolungama, ndi labwino, anthu sakanatha kufika pa miyezo yake chifukwa chilamulo chinaperekedwa kuti chitsimikize tchimo m'thupi, ndipo inu nonse munabadwa akufa m'zolakwa ndi machimo chifukwa tchimo lili m'thupi lanu. Torah sinaperekedwe kwa olungama koma kwa osalungama, kuti tchimo lionekere kukhala tchimo m'thupi. Torah inaperekedwa ngati mphunzitsi wanu kuti akubweretseni kwa ine, ngakhale Khristu Yesu, Ambuye wa Ambuye ndi Mfumu ya Mafumu wokwera kumwamba!

Ndipo chotero ndinatenga mofunitsitsa zotulukapo za tchimo pa ine ndekha, pakuti chotero ndinabadwa kuti ndiombole anthu ku temberero la chilamulo. Pakuti chilamulo sichinatembereredwe; inde, chilamulo chiime ndipo munthu aliyense atembereredwe amene atemberera chilamulo cha Mose, pakuti sindinabwere kudzachotsa chilamulo, koma kukhazikitsa chilamulo ndi kupitirira chilamulo cha Mose. Mwano mukuti?

Umboni wa Ahebri uli pakati pathu: Pakuti ngati magazi a ng'ombe ndi a mbuzi, ndi phulusa la ng'ombe yaikazi yowaza odetsedwa, zimalekanitsa kuyeretsa thupi: Nanga magazi a Khristu, amene mwa Mzimu wosatha anadzipereka yekha wopanda chilema kwa Mulungu, adzayeretsa bwanji chikumbumtima chanu ku ntchito zakufa kuti mutumikire Mulungu wamoyo?

Popeza mumakonda Mose (monga inenso), ndikukulangizani kuti mumvere mwachikondi malangizo a Mose; pakuti anati: Yahweh Elohim wanu adzakuukitsirani Mneneri pakati panu, wa abale anu, ngati ine; mudzamumvera iye. Choncho imvani mawu a Yeshua HaMashiach! Mwayeretsedwa ku tchimo ndi machimo onse amene mumachita ndi magazi anga opanda tchimo otetezera amene ndinapereka kwa Yahweh ngati dipo la miyoyo yanu, ndipo ndakuombolani m'manja mwa mdani. Musachimwe! Ndipo ngati munthu achimwa, avomereze machimo ake kwa ine, pakuti ndine amene ndikukuyeretsani m'magazi anga otetezera.

Sindinatetezere machimo anu ndi zotulukapo zake ndi mbuzi yotenga machimo, kapena sindinapereke mwana wankhosa wa chaka choyamba; pakuti ndine Mwana wankhosa wa Mulungu wa chaka choyamba, ndipo ndine amene ndinadzipereka ndekha wopanda banga ndi wopanda chilema, ngakhale woyera, wangwiro, ndi wopatulidwa kwa Yahweh mwiniwake (ngakhale Atate wanga) chifukwa cha machimo anu kamodzi kokha, ngakhale nthawi zonse. Ndinatetezera machimo anu m'magazi anga opanda tchimo; chotero, palibenso nsembe ina ya tchimo. Ndipo kundikana ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, ngakhale pangano lamtendere langwiro la Yahweh kwa anthu, ndikukana Yahweh ndi kulowa m'chipanduko cha Satana, chifukwa Satana amapandukira dongosolo la Mulungu kuyambira pachiyambi.

Pakuti ndinauza Ahebri: Pakuti ngati tichimwa dala titatha kulandira chidziwitso cha choonadi, palibenso nsembe ina ya machimo. Chotero kuchimwa dala pokana magazi anga otetezera, ndi kukana pangano lamtendere lotumizidwa ndi Yahweh, n'komvetsa chisoni kwambiri, ndipo palibenso nsembe. Pakuti kwalembedwa m'Ahebri: Iye amene ananyoza chilamulo cha Mose anamfa wopanda chifundo pamaso pa mboni ziwiri kapena zitatu: Kodi mukuganiza kuti adzayenera chilango chowawa chotani, iye amene wapondereza Mwana wa Mulungu, ndi kuyesa magazi a pangano, amene anayeretsedwa nawo, chinthu chodetsedwa, ndi kuchitira chipongwe Mzimu wa chisomo?

Chotero ndikukulimbikitsani nonse kuti mulandire nsembe yanga ndi kumvetsa kuti palibenso nsembe zina zofunika ndipo pali Mesiya mmodzi yekha woona, ngakhale ine, Yeshua HaMashiach. Ndipo kwalembedwa za ine: Koma munthu uyu, atapereka nsembe imodzi ya machimo kunthawizonse, anakhala kudzanja lamanja la Mulungu; Kuyambira pamenepo akuyembekezera kufikira adani ake atayesedwa chopondapo mapazi ake. Pakuti ndi nsembe imodzi wayeretsa kunthawizonse iwo amene akuyeretsedwa.

Chotero mvetsetsani kuti ndakuyeretsani m'mbali zonse zokhudza tchimo ndi zotulukapo zake. Mwayesedwa oyera mwa ine; mwayesedwa olungama mwa ine; mwapangidwa kukhala chilungamo cha Mulungu mwa ine; ndipo ndakuombolani kwathunthu kwa ine ndekha, ngakhale ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, amene Yahweh wamutumiza ngati Mesiya wanu. Ndipo chotero mvetsetsani kuti palibenso Akulu a Ansembe a Chi Levi, ndipo Unsembe wa Aroni wabwera ndi kupita, pakuti unali mthunzi wa zinthu zikubwera. Pakuti ndine mkulu wa ansembe wanu kunthawizonse monga mwa dongosolo la Melikizedeki.

Chifukwa chilamulo sichinapange kanthu kangwiro: koma chinali chiyambi cha chiyembekezo chabwino, mwa chiyembekezo chimenecho timayandikira kwa Yahweh. Ndiponso: Mkulu wa Ansembe wotere anatiyenera kukhala naye amene ali woyera, wopanda chinyengo, wosadetsedwa, wolekanitsidwa ndi ochimwa, ndi wopangidwa wamkulu kuposa kumwamba. Ndiponso: Pakuti chilamulo chimapanga anthu kukhala ansembe, amene ali ndi zofooka: koma mawu a lumbiro amene anabwera pambuyo pa chilamulo, amapanga mwana kukhala wansembe, amene ali wangwiro kunthawizonse. Kodi simunawerenge zomwe lemba limanena za ine? Yahweh analumbira ndipo sadzalapa: Ndiwe Wansembe kunthawizonse monga mwa dongosolo la Melikizedeki.

Mvetsetsani kuti palibe chosowa mu nsembe yanga, ndipo muli otetezeka mwa ine, Khristu Yesu. Torah imasungidwa ndi chikhulupiriro mwa ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndipo nkosatheka kuti inu muphwanye malamulo aliwonse a Mulungu poyenda mwa ine, Khristu Yesu. Pakuti mumayenda m'chilamulo cha Mzimu wa moyo, ndipo ndakumasulani ku chilamulo cha tchimo ndi imfa. Chotero khalani ndi chikhulupiriro mwa ine, pakuti wolungama adzakhala ndi moyo ndi chikhulupiriro. Ndili ndi ntchito zambiri zamphamvu zomwe ndakuitanirani, ndipo mudzathandiza kubweretsa chipulumutso kwa ambiri. Pali chiyembekezo chachikulu ndi mphoto yosungidwa kwa inu kumwamba chifukwa cha kuima kwanu ndi kukhulupirika kwanu.

Pakuti ndikukutumizani, osindikizidwa pamphumi panu ndi chizindikiro cha Yahweh mwiniwake, m'dziko lomwe lidzakudanani kotheratu, ndipo musadabwe, pakuti dziko linandida ine kotheratu poyamba, ndipo kapolo saposa mbuye wake. Ndinu mboni zanga ndi aneneri anga ndi ochiritsa anga ndi opereka anga. Ndikukutumizani mu mdima wandiweyani womwe sunachitikepo kuphimba chilengedwe cha Yahweh. Pa nthawi ngati ino mwayitanidwa, kusankhidwa, ndi kusindikizidwa, ndipo nthawi zino zidzakhala zopotoka ndi zoipa kwambiri zomwe dziko silinawonepo, ngakhale kuipa kuposa masiku a Nowa. Pakati pawo ndikukutumizani, ndipo ndikukuuzani kuti musaope! Musaope ziwopsezo zawo; musaope zomwe munthu angakuchitireni; musaope nkhope zawo; musaope mizimu yomwe imawapatsa mphamvu; musaope chilombo ndi mneneri wonyenga; ndipo musaope Satana mwiniwake: Ngakhale chinjoka chachikulu chija. Pakuti mudzapondereza mbali iliyonse ya ufumu wa Satana, ndipo palibe chomwe chidzakulepheretseni ngati mukhala okhulupirika ku mawu anga ndi kundilola kuti ndikutsogolereni pang'onopang'ono. Pakuti zoonadi, ndinu antchito anzanga mu nthawi ino ya masautso a Yakobo, ndipo ndakupatsani mphamvu.

Ndinu amphamvu ndi aakulu, ndipo ndikukutumizani mofatsa ndi modzichepetsa. Ndikukutumizani kuti musunge chilamulo ndi chikhulupiriro chanu mwa ine, ndipo chotero kondani Yahweh ndi mtima wanu wonse, moyo wanu wonse, maganizo anu onse, ndi mphamvu zanu zonse, ndipo kondani mnansi wanu ngati inu nomwe. Mnansi wanu ndani? Onse omwe akusowa thandizo lanu omwe ndikukutumizirani, ndipo sindikukuletsani ndi malire a dziko. Pitani mukawete gulu langa, pakuti ndine Mbusa Wamkulu amene ali ndi mphoto yanu m'dzanja langa chifukwa cha kukhulupirika. Pitani mukasamalire gulu langa, samalirani mabala awo, adyetseni, achiritseni, atsogolereni, ndipo koposa zonse abweretseni kwa ine; pakuti mwa inu nokha sindinu kanthu, koma mwa ine, ndinu amuna anga amphamvu, aakulu a Mulungu. Aphunzitseni kuitana pa dzina langa mpaka chimaliziro, pakuti aliyense amene adzatero adzapulumutsidwa.

Yang'anani pa mphoto ndi chimwemwe chomwe chaikidwa patsogolo panu, ndipo pitirizani kupita patsogolo ku chizindikiro cha mphoto ya kuitana kwapamwamba kwa Mulungu mwa Khristu Yesu. Pakuti mphoto yanu idzakhala yaikulu, ndipo mudzadabwa ndi zomwe zasungidwa kwa inu kumwamba. Ndipo mphoto yanga si yakanthawi koma yamuyaya, ndipo mphoto zanu sizidzawonongeka koma zidzakhala cholowa chamuyaya pokwaniritsa malonjezo omwe Yahweh anapanga kwa makolo; yod iliyonse kapena munga wa yod udzakwaniritsidwa. Simudzazindikira ukulu wa ntchito zanu mpaka tsiku la mphoto, koma Lemba likunena momveka bwino za chipatso cha ntchito zanu.

Pakuti Yohane Wovumbulutsa analemba mokhulupirika zomwe ndinamuwonetsa: Pambuyo pa zinthu izi ndinawona, ndipo taonani khamu lalikulu, lomwe palibe munthu akanatha kuliwerenga, la mitundu yonse ndi mafuko, ndi anthu, ndi manenedwe, linaima pamaso pa mpando wachifumu, ndi pamaso pa Mwana wankhosa, ovala mikanjo yoyera italiitali, ndi mgwalangwa m'manja mwawo. Ndipo anafuula ndi mawu akulu, nati, Chipulumutso chichokera kwa Mulungu wathu, amene akhala pa mpando wachifumu, ndi kwa Mwana wankhosa. Pakuti khamu lalikulu mudzathandiza kupulumutsa ndi kutumikira, ndipo mphoto zanu za kukhulupirika zidzakhala zazikulu.

Ndipo mudzanditsatira kulikonse komwe ndipita, ndipo ndinu zipatso zoyambirira zogulidwa mwa anthu, ndipo mudzayimba nyimbo yatsopano pamaso pa mpando wachifumu yomwe inu nokha mungathe kuiphunzira. Mudzalamuliranso ndi ine mu Ufumu wanga zaka chikwi chimodzi ndipo mudzakhala ndi ine nthawi zonse mu umuyaya. Pakuti wanu ndi chimwemwe chamuyaya, pakuti mudzakhala ndi ine mu Ufumu wanga pa dziko lapansi pamene ndikulamulira dziko lapansi m'chilungamo kuchokera pa mpando wachifumu wa Davide, ndipo Ufumu wanga ulibe mapeto. Ndipo palibe chinthu choipa chomwe chidzalowe, ndipo sipadzakhalanso masautso, ndipo wanga ndi ufumu wa chilungamo kumene mwana wankhosa ndi mkango zidzagona pamodzi mumtendere. Ndipo kumapeto kwa ulamuliro wanga wa zaka chikwi chimodzi, Satana adzamasulidwa kanthawi kochepa kuti asonkhanitse kuukira kwake komaliza, ndipo moto udzatsika kuchokera kwa Yahweh ndi kuwawononga kotheratu. Satana adzaponyedwa m'nyanja yamoto, kuti asadzaukenso, ndipo Yahweh adzalenga kumwamba kwatsopano ndi dziko lapansi latsopano, ndipo adzapanga malo ake okhala pakati pa anthu, ndipo chotero mudzakhala mu Yerusalemu Watsopano, ngakhale mzinda woyera umenewo wolengedwa ndi Yahweh mwiniwake. Ndipo Yahweh mwiniwake wanena: Iye amene apambana adzalandira zinthu zonse; ndipo Ine ndidzakhala Mulungu wake, ndipo iye adzakhala mwana wanga.
