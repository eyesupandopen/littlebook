Tsoka Lalikulu Likubwera

Mawu a Ambuye anadza kwa ine; chonde imvani.

Imvani mawu anga, atero Yehova wa Makamu! Anthu anga, bwererani kwa ine, pakuti tsoka lalikulu latsala pang'ono kugwera mtundu wanu. Tsoka lalikulu lili pakhomo panu, ndipo tsoka lalikulu lidzafika m'mbali mwa nyanja zanu. Bwererani kwa ine ndipo mundiope, atero Yehova wa Makamu. Mulungu wapereka chiweruzo pa America m'mbali ziwiri. Kudzakhala chiweruzo chochokera mkati ndipo kudzakhala chiweruzo chochokera kunja. Kuchokera mkati kudzakhala kugwedezeka kwakukulu, kugwedezeka kwakukulu, ndipo America idzagwa mwamphamvu ndi kugwedezeka kwakukulu. Kuchokera kunja, magulu ake ankhondo abwera kudzaba ndi kupha ndi kuwononga zonse zomwe zili m'njira zawo. Mulibe chifundo m'mitima yawo. Ali ndi mtima umodzi. Ndipo adzakulandani zochuluka zanu; adzakulandani katundu wanu; adzagwira mbadwa zanu; ndipo njala yaikulu idzafika m'dziko lanu. Amene sanaphedwe ndi nkhondo adzaphedwa ndi tsoka; amene sanaphedwe ndi tsoka adzaphedwa ndi zilombo zakutchire; amene sanaphedwe ndi zilombo adzafa ndi njala. Koma ngakhale m'tsoka lanu, aliyense amene adzaitana pa dzina la Ambuye adzapulumutsidwa.

Ndipo ndidzapulumutsa anthu anga, ngakhale kudzera m'chisautso chachikulu ndi m'moto wa woyenga. Bwerani kwa ine, anthu anga, nthawi ilipo. Bwerani kwa ine, Ambuye Yesu Khristu, pakuti ndine Ambuye wolungama. Ndine Mfumu ya Mafumu. Ndine Mbusa wabwino, ndipo palibe amene angathe kulanda nkhosa zanga m'dzanja langa. Bwerani kwa ine! Bwerani kwa ine nthawi ikalipo, ndipo vulani machimo anu; vomerezani dzina langa lamphamvu monga Ambuye, pakuti palibe chipulumutso mu dzina lina lililonse. Pakuti ine, Yesu Khristu, ndakhazikitsidwa chifukwa cha chipulumutso chanu chomwe ndachigula ndi magazi anga. Bwerani kwa ine, inde, bwerani kwa ine m'chikondi changa chomwe ndili nacho pa inu. Musandikanize, koma bwerani kwa ine, kuti ndikupulumutseni ndi kukupatsani moyo wosatha kuti ndidalitse mbadwa zanu. Bwerani kwa ine, anthu anga, ndipo musaumitse mitima yanu, monga momwe makolo anachitira m'chipululu. Pakuti Yehova Mulungu wanu wanena chiweruzo chachikulu pa mtundu wanu, pakuti machimo ake ndi ambiri ndi owopsa.

Inde, ndalipira machimo amenewo ndi machimo onse a anthu, koma chopereka changa ndi manja anga sizidzakhala zotambasulidwa nthawi zonse. M'tsiku la tsoka, chipulumutso chidzapezeka kudzera m'masautso akulu ndi chisautso chachikulu chomwe ndidzabweretsa pa dziko lapansi kuyesa mitima ya anthu, ndipo iye amene apirira mpaka chimaliziro adzapulumutsidwa. Musatsatire munthu wauchimo. Musatenge chizindikiro chake ndipo musatenge mankhwala ake, pakuti kulumidwa kwa njoka kuli mmenemo, ndipo simudzachiritsidwa ku poizoni wake. Aliyense amene adzatenga chizindikiro cha chilombo adzakhala ndi gawo lawo m'nyanja yamoto pamodzi ndi chilombo ndi mneneri wonyenga. Kodi sizinalembedwe m'Mawu anga Oyera? Kodi simunawerenge? Kodi simunamve? Bwererani ku chikondi changa ndipo bwerani kwa ine, ana anga, pakuti ndikukuitanani mu ola lomalizali, pakuti tsoka lalikulu lili pakhomo panu.

Mverani ine, atero Yehova wa Makamu:

Pakuti ndine amene, Mwana wa Munthu amene anapachikidwa pansi pa Ponzio Pilato, amene anafera machimo anu ndipo anaukitsidwa tsiku lachitatu. Ndinaukitsidwa ndipo ndinalemekezedwa.

Ndine Ambuye wa Ambuye ndi Mfumu ya Mafumu.

Ndine Mwana wa Munthu, Ambuye wolungama, Ambuye woona amene ali wokhulupirika ndi woona, ndipo palibe ndi mmodzi yemwe wa inu angalandidwe m'manja mwanga.

Ndine mbusa wabwino, ndipo palibe amene angathe kulanda nkhosa zanga m'dzanja langa.

Zonse zomwe Atate wandipatsa, ndidzazipereka kwa iye, ndipo ndikhumba kukuperekani kwa Atate opanda cholakwa, banga, kapena chitonzo, ovala bafuta woyera woviikidwa m'magazi anga. Njira yokhayo imene munthu aliyense angaimire pamaso panga ndiyo kulandira nsembe yanga chifukwa cha machimo anu. Musachedwe ndi ochimwa! Musachedwe; musachedwe! Tulukani pakati pawo ndipo mudzilekanitse ndi machimo awo. Vomerani machimo anu kwa ine ndipo vomerani machimo a makolo anu kwa ine, pakuti amapita kwa inu. Khalani ndi moyo womwe ndakupatsani, ndipo yendani m'njira yomwe ndakukonzerani, popeza ndakuitanani m'dzina. Musatembenukire kumanzere ndipo musatembenukire kumanja, koma mverani ndi kukhala ndi mtima umodzi pa mawu anga. Komabe, ambiri adzakana kuitana kwanga, ndipo ambiri adzawonongeka. Khalani ndi chikhulupiriro mwa ine, pakuti chikondi cha Mulungu chidzapulumutsa onse amene aitanira pa dzina la Ambuye. Khalani ndi chikhulupiriro mwa ine, ndipo mitsinje ya madzi amoyo idzayenda kuchokera m'mimba mwanu pamene machimo anu asambitsidwa ndi mzimu wanu kupangidwa wathunthu. Bwerani kwa ine nthawi ikalipo; vomerani dzina langa; ndipangeni kukhala Ambuye, pakuti palibe chipulumutso mu dzina lina lililonse loperekedwa pansi pa thambo limene anthu angapulumutsidwe nalo.
