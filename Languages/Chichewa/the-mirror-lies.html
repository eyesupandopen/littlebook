Galasi Limanama!

Galasi, galasi pakhoma, ndiuze za dziko lomwe zowonera zachuluka zokopa onse! Pobwereza mabodza omwe amabwerezedwa mobwerezabwereza, ndi zithunzi zabodza zosayima kotero mu chinyengo chachikulu amapanga maganizo awo ndi zosangalatsa zamwambo kuti nkhosa zisocheretsedwe ndi malingaliro omwe maganizo awo amawona! Ngakhale zili choncho, iwo akopeka ndi zilakolako za thupi, ndi zilakolako za maso ndi kunyada kwa moyo komwe kumasonkhezeredwa ndi kuyatsidwa mu chidziwitso chabodza choperekedwa chifukwa cha chomwe chimatchedwa maphunziro paliponse pomwe mabodza awo amagulitsidwa mochenjera. Mwa izi, makutu amayabwa ndi kukhazikitsidwa pansi ndi mafunde obwerezabwereza kotero kuti onyada ndi opusa amagwiritsidwa ntchito ndi kulamuliridwa. Ndipo m'kupita kwanthawi apeza kuti kudzera mu nyimbo za ana zolamulira maganizo khalidwe la anthu limasinthidwa molingana ndi izo ndipo mu matsenga awo amamangidwa!

Komabe ndi momwe liliri dziko lomwe mukukhalamo, O' Isiraeli, ngakhale dziko lomwe lagona kotheratu mu kuipa, ngakhale dziko lomwe latengedwa ukapolo ndi kukopeka kotheratu ndi ukonde wa mabodza omwe amaperekedwa kuti akonze makamu a anthu ku nkhani zawo zakupha zomwe zikuperekedwa mosayima tsopano chifukwa cha chiwonongeko cha Adamu. Magalasi, awonetsedwa paliponse, koma m'kuyang'ana kwa galasi choonadi palibe, chifukwa choonadi sichiwonedwa ndi maso omwe amayang'ana kumeneko chifukwa magalasi awo ndi achinyengo kuti aluke zenizeni zabodza zopangidwa ndi matekinoloje a omwe adagwa opangidwa kuti akapolo anthu ku mabodza awo kuti Adamu amwalire kwamuyaya.

Magalasi akuda, awonetsedwa paliponse ndikuyikidwa kuti awonedwe paliponse pomwe maso angayang'ane, kotero kuti magalasi amanena mabodza omwewo ogwirizanitsidwa ndi mapangidwe oyipa, kotero munthu wamthupi amasangalatsidwa ndi tchimo ndi imfa ndipo amakopeka ndi matsenga ambiri ndi akulu omwe amapakidwa ngati nzeru ndi chitukuko koma m'malo mwake amayambitsa imfa ndi chiwonongeko chifukwa mulibe choonadi m'zithunzi zabodza zomwe amapereka. Magalasi akuda, awonetsedwa ndi kugwiridwa m'manja momwe chithunzi pambuyo pa chithunzi chimamanga misala ndi chinyengo champhamvu padziko lapansi, pomwe choonadi sichilekanitsidwa ndi mabodza, omwe onse omwe amasangalala ndi chosalungama sadzapulumuka koma adzafa kwamuyaya m'malawi a chiwonongeko chosatha ngakhale chilango chomwe amamangidwa kwamuyaya ndipo palibe komwe angathawe kapena kubisala komwe kupewa chiweruzo kungapezeke.

O', amveni akuimba! Mverani ndikumva gulu la mbalame zotengera mawu zikuimba mogwirizana, mogwirizana ndi zithunzi zabodza zomwe magalasi awo akuda amapereka nthawi zonse. Amaimba pafupi kwambiri ndi nyimbo za mbalame zoimba zenizeni koma m'choonadi yawo ndi nyimbo yachinyengo yonena mabodza kotero kuti mawu awo amagwirizana ndi kuwona mtima kwabodza m'zinyengo zawo zomwe amafuna kubisa. Ndipo komabe mbalame zotengera mawu zabodzazi zilibe nyimbo zawozawo koma zimatengera ndi kubwereza nyimbo za mbalame zoimba zenizeni popanda kulemba kapena kumvetsetsa mawu m'nyimbo zomwe amaimba, ndipo kotero zithunzi zomwe zimawulutsidwa pa magalasi akuda omwe ndi zowonera zimagwirizanitsidwa kotero kuti mabodza amabwereza ndipo makamu a anthu amafa ziwalo mwa izo. Ngakhale malingaliro abodza ndi zenizeni zabodza pomwe dziko lonse lapansi latengedwa m'mabodza a omwe adagwa, ngakhale zenizeni zabodza zomwe zabwera padziko lapansi kuti ziwonjezeke pamene ndikuchotsa woletsa wanga pamene ndikusonkhanitsa okhulupirika mwa Khristu ku chitetezo cha kumwamba mwadzidzidzi ndi mosayembekezereka tsopano.

Chifukwa chake, mverani mawu a Yeshua HaMashiach ndikumvetsetsa dziko lomwe mukukhalamo ndi chinyengo chomwe chalolezedwa kugwira dziko lapansi m'kukumbatira kwake kwabodza. Ngakhale chinyengo choperekedwa m'chiweruzo pomwe omwe adagwa amaloledwa kukhala ndi ulamuliro wawo, ndipo izi chifukwa munthu wamthupi amafuna kulowa nawo a Satana m'kupanduka kwawo chifukwa cha malipiro a kuipa, kuti asangalale ndi chosalungama mpaka imfa yake, ndipo kotero munthu wamthupi tsopano adzaperekedwa m'manja mwa adani ake, komabe ngakhale zili choncho gulu lalikulu lidzapulumutsidwa ngakhale zili choncho ndi moto wa woyenga. Ndipo kupatukana kwina ndi kwathunthu kudzachitika pa nthawi ya masautso a Yakobo, ngakhale kupatukana komwe otsalira a Isiraeli amamasulidwa ku ukapolo wa Esau ndikumasulidwa mu ufumu wanga wa zaka chikwi womwe watsala pang'ono kukhazikitsidwa padziko lapansi!

Komabe, munthu wamthupi amakonda zenizeni zabodza za omwe adagwa komwe amafuna kubisa Yahweh (zomwe kubisa nkosatheka), komabe ndi momwe liliri chinyengo champhamvu chomwe awulutsa padziko lapansi pomwe onse omwe akana Yesu wa ku Nazarete, ngakhale Yeshua HaMashiach, amakhala akapolo mwa izo. Chifukwa chake, mverani Mawu a Yeshua HaMashiach, kuti mumvetsetse dziko lomwe mukukhalamo ndi chinyengo champhamvu chomwe chakumbatira dziko lapansi m'kugwira kwake. Mvetsetsani, O' Isiraeli, kuti Ine, Yeshua HaMashiach yemwenso ndi Khristu Yesu ndine amene ndidzachotsa khungu lanu lina pamene mutembenuza mitima yanu mukumvera kwathunthu kumveka kwa mawu anga, olankhulidwa pa milomo yokhulupirika ya okhulupirika omwe amakhala ndi chikhulupiriro mwa Mwana wa Yahweh, chifukwa olungama adzakhala ndi moyo ndi chikhulupiriro!

Kodi nchiyani chomwe chakhalapo? Icho, chomwe chidzakhala: ndipo nchiyani chomwe chachitidwa? Icho chomwe chidzachitidwanso: ndipo palibe chinthu chatsopano pansi padzuwa. Kodi pali chilichonse, chomwe wina anganene, Taonani ichi, ndi chatsopano? Chakhalapo kale m'nthawi zakale zomwe zinali ife tisanakhale. Palibe chikumbukiro cha nthawi yoyamba iyi, kapena sipadzakhala chikumbukiro cha nthawi yotsiriza yomwe idzakhala, ndi iwo omwe adzabwera pambuyo pake. Zoonadi, zoonadi, kudzabwera nthawi yomwe omwe adagwa sadzakumbukiridwa, kapena kubwera m'maganizo ndipo zinsinsi zawo zabodza zidzatha kwamuyaya pamene aponyedwa m'malawi a moto.

Chifukwa chake, mvetsetsani mabodza akale operekedwa ndi omwe adagwa kwa anthu m'dziko lomwe linalipo chigumula chachikulu cha Nowa chisanachitike, ngakhale mabodza omwewa amapakidwanso ndikugwiritsidwa ntchito kuwononga anthu kachiwiri. Chifukwa chake mvetsetsani, omwe adagwa amabweretsa matekinoloje awo ndi zinyengo zawo ndi zizindikiro zabodza ndi zozizwitsa zomwe adagwiritsa ntchito kukopa ndi kuwononga anthu m'masiku a Yaredi. Ndipo dziko lapansi lidzabwerera monga momwe linaliri m'masiku a Nowa ndipo momwemonso masautso a Yakobo adzakhala, ngakhale nthawi padziko lapansi yolamulidwa ndi adani a Yahweh. Ngakhale chinjoka chachikulu chakale chomwe tsopano chamangidwa padziko lapansi kuti chiweruzidwe, ndipo zonyansa zambiri zidzamasulidwa m'dziko la anthu; monga momwe Yahweh wanenera kuti adzabweretsa zimphona kuti akwaniritse mkwiyo wake.

Pakuti izi iwo mofunitsitsa sakhala nazo chidziwitso, kuti ndi mawu a Mulungu kumwamba kunaliko kale, ndi dziko lapansi loima m'madzi, ndi m'madzi, momwe dziko lomwe linalipo, litasefukira mwamphamvu ndi madzi, linawonongeka. Koma kumwamba ndi dziko lapansi zomwe zilipo tsopano, ndi Mawu omwewo zasungidwa, zosungidwira moto kufikira tsiku la chiweruzo, ndi chiwonongeko cha anthu osapembedza.

Zomvetsa chisoni, makamu ambiri, ambiri padziko lapansi mofunitsitsa sakhala ndi chidziwitso kuti dziko lomwe linalipo Yahweh analigumula mwamphamvu ndipo linawonongeka chifukwa cha Anefili ndi zonyansa zawo zambiri; ndipo chifukwa cha omwe adagwa akudziyesa kuti anali milungu pamene adawononga njira za anthu padziko lapansi ndi zinsinsi zawo zopanda phindu zomwe adaphunzitsa anthu chifukwa cha chiwonongeko chawo. Choncho mverani choonadi, ndikumvetsetsa Mawu anga, ndikumva mawu a Yeshua HaMashiach kuti mumvetsetse choonadi ndikukhala kuti simukodwa ndi matekinoloje awo ndipo kuti simusinthidwa ndi chizindikiro cha chilombo kukhala chonyansa chosungidwira malawi a chilango kwamuyaya.

Chifukwa matekinoloje awo ndi omwe adachitidwapo kale, ndipo tsopano alipo, ndipo adzabweranso kwathunthu nthawi yomaliza ya chimaliziro. Zoonadi, matekinoloje awo adzawululidwa kwathunthu pamene akupitiriza kutulutsa dongosolo lawo lomaliza la chilombo, ndi zizindikiro zonse zabodza ndi zozizwitsa zoperekedwa kwa iwo omwe akuwonongeka. Ndipo kotero chinjoka chidzauka kuti chilamulire dziko lapansi mu chinyengo chachikulu kudzera mwa mwanawankhosa wa nyanga ziwiri.

Ndipo omwe adagwa adzadziwonetsera okha ngati zolengedwa zabwino zochokera kumayiko ozungulira monama mu sayansi yawo yabodza ya zakuthambo ya mayiko ozungulira monama nthawi zonse omwe amagwiritsidwa ntchito m'masiku akale. Mvetsetsani kuti pamwamba pa dziko lapansi pali kumwamba ndi thambo ndipo ngakhale kumwamba kwa kumwamba komwe Yahweh amakhala pampando wake wachifumu, ndipo zoonadi, zoonadi Yahweh amalamulira pamwamba pa chilengedwe chake chonse chomwe iye yekha pachiyambi adalenga pambuyo pa uphungu wa chifuniro chake ndi zolinga zake zamuyaya.

Chifukwa chake, phunzirani kuti mumvetsetse dziko lanu monga momwe liliri ndikumvetsetsa kuti mapulaneti ndi nyenyezi zoyendayenda zoperekedwa ngati zizindikiro ndi nyengo ndipo si mayiko ena okhala ndi zamoyo zina, chifukwa chake, kanani mabodza a omwe adagwa. Alendo otchedwa aliens awa ndi omwe adapandukira Yahweh kuyambira pachiyambi ndipo ndi ochokera kumlengalenga m'lingaliro lakuti chiyambi chawo chili kumwamba pa tsiku lomwe adalengedwa mu ungwiro wolungama ndi Yahweh mwiniwake. Komabe, m'kupanduka kwawo adagwa kuchokera ku ungwiro wawo woyambirira, ndipo adzisintha okha kwamuyaya ndi kosatha, ndipo sadzapulumuka. Komabe munthu wamthupi amakonda mabodza awa kuti akwaniritse zilakolako za thupi lake lowola mu kuipa konse ndi chosalungama, ndipo onse omwe amayenda njira yotakata iyi ya chiwonongeko adzagwera m'malawi amuyaya mwa izo.

Ndipo motero adzawonekera, akudziyesa kuti amasamala za zochitika za anthu ndipo adzadziwonetsera okha kwathunthu pamene woletsa achotsedwa pamene Mpingo wanga ukusonkhanitsidwa kwa ine m'mitambo. Adzawonekera akunena kuti abwera kudzapulumutsa anthu ku kuwukira ndikupulumutsa anthu ku nkhondo ndipo adzabwera ndi mabodza ambiri opakidwa bwino ndi omveka bwino. Komabe, nyanga khumi zili ndi maganizo amodzi ndipo zidzapereka mphamvu zawo ndi ulamuliro wawo kwa chilombo! Ndipo nyanga khumi ndi zomwe zidzadana ndi hule, ndipo zidzamupanga kukhala wabwinja ndi wamaliseche, ndipo zidzadya mnofu wake, ndikumutentha ndi moto mu nkhondo yawo yachitatu yapadziko lonse yomwe yatsala pang'ono kumeza dziko lapansi. Ndipo komabe adzawonekera ndi matekinoloje awo m'manja, koma amangobweretsa mayankho abodza ndi njira zabodza za mabodza ndi chinyengo zomwe zimatulutsa chiweruzo chamuyaya ndi chiwonongeko kwa onse omwe alowa nawo m'kupanduka kwawo.

Komabe, motero adzauka, ngakhale chilombo chochokera kunyanja ndi chilombo chochokera pansi, ndipo adzauka ngati mwanawankhosa wa nyanga ziwiri amene amalankhula ngati chinjoka, ngakhale Mwanawankhosa wa Chinjoka yemwe Esau wadzisankhira yekha kuti alamulire dziko lapansi mu chosalungama. Ngakhale ulamuliro womwe mitundu imalamuliridwa mwankhanza ndi Ashkenaz kudzera mwa Noahide ndi chilombo chochokera kunyanja ndipo chilombo chochokera pansi chimalamulira dziko ndi dziko lapansi ndi Talmud ya Esau mu chinyengo chachikulu pamene akutchera msampha wake kwa Yakobo.

Mvetsetsani kuti chilombo chochokera kunyanja chadzipatula chokha ku Babulo Wachinsinsi kuti chidzipatule ku milandu yake yomwe ndi yambiri, ndipo adzadetsa dzina lake ndikuwoneka ngati akumuimba mlandu. Koma mvetsetsani kuti chilombo chochokera kunyanja ndi chilombo chochokera pansi ndi ochokera ku mibadwo yonyansa yomweyo ndipo amagwira ntchito limodzi, ngakhale awiri a chilombo, kutumikira ndi kukhutiritsa zolinga za chilakolako cha chinjoka. Chilombo chochokera kunyanja chidzauka mu nthawi yake ndipo chidzauka kukhala wankhondo wawo wolonjezedwa, ngakhale Mahdi wawo yemwe akumuyembekezera panopa. Ndipo adzabisa chikhalidwe chake chenicheni ndi zakale zake ndi milandu yake, ngakhale kuyesetsa koteroko nkopanda phindu chifukwa tchimo lililonse lomwe adachitapo lalembedwa bwino kumwamba. Ngakhale, adzapusitsa omwe amamutsatira ndi malonjezo ake opanda pake.

Ndipo pamene akuuka kuchokera ku phulusa la nkhondo, adzadzigwirizanitsa poyera ndi chinjoka ndi omwe adagwa omwe ali awo kale, ndipo adzasuntha mwachangu kudzikhazikitsa okha mu ufumu wawo womaliza, womwe akhazikitsa kale dongosolo lake, ngakhale Babulo Wachinsinsi asanawonongedwe kudzera mu chinsinsi cha kuipa. Komabe, adzafuna kumanga zomwe zakhalapo kale pamene omwe adagwa akufuna kukhazikitsa m'badwo wawo watsopano wagolide, ngakhale m'badwo wagolide womwe sadzakhala nawo koma chiweruzo chankhanza m'malo mwake. Komabe, adzauka mu mphamvu yayikulu ndipo chilombo chimodzi chidzaloza ku chinzake ndipo adzachitirana umboni wina ndi mzake ndipo chinzake cha chimodzi ndipo zinthu zambiri zazikulu ndi zamwano adzalankhula ndipo zizindikiro zambiri zabodza ndi zozizwitsa adzabweretsa.

Komabe, motero adzauka monga momwe kudalembedwera. Ndipo Mwanawankhosa wa nyanga ziwiri adzagwira ntchito limodzi ndi chinjoka ndipo adzaphatikiza kukhala chimodzi zinthu zonse m'gulu lawo latsopano. Adzaphatikiza machitidwe awo azachuma, adzaphatikiza maboma awo, adzamanga zinthu zonse za gulu lawo latsopano pamodzi ndikudziphatikiza okha ngati amodzi mu dongosolo lawo latsopano. Adzaphatikiza zipembedzo zawo zamwano zachinsinsi zakale kukhala chipembedzo chatsopano chomwe chidzawoneka ngati chipembedzo cha Abambo awo omwe amalambira ma cubes awo akuda, komabe adzaphatikiza zipembedzo zawo kukhala chipembedzo china chabodza chomwe Abambo awo sanachidziwe koma ma cubes awo adzakhalapo. (Komabe, sadzasamala za Mulungu wa makolo ake, kapena zilakolako za akazi, kapena kusamala za Mulungu aliyense: pakuti adzadzikweza yekha pamwamba pa onse).

Mvetsetsani zomwe adzachita, O' Isiraeli, chifukwa adzachita nkhondo ndi oyera mtima ndipo adzawagonjetsa, ndipo onse omwe amayitanira pa dzina la Yesu wa ku Nazarete yemwenso ndi Yeshua HaMashiach mpaka mapeto adzapulumutsidwa, pakuti kotero kwalonjezedwa ndi kulembedwa. Adzaimba mlandu omwe amayitanira pa dzina la Yesu chifukwa cha milandu yawo ndipo Baraba adzapereka oyera mtima a Babulo Wachinsinsi chifukwa ambiri a iwo mosadziwa apereka Mipingo yawo kwa chilombo chochokera pansi, chomwe chikubwera pa nthawi ya kulembedwa uku. Ndipo wadzipanga yekha kukhala Ambuye wawo ndikugulitsa Mabaibulo m'dzina lake, komabe Mkhristu amavomereza mosavuta kuti sali, ngakhale, ambiri mopusa amakhulupirira kuti ali.

Ndipo pamene dziko lapansi likutaya ulamuliro ndikugwera mu nkhondo yayikulu, ndi mliri, ndi njala, ndi chisalungamo ndi imfa adzaimba mlandu zinthu zonse za Khristu chifukwa cha mavuto awo omwe iwo eni, anawayambitsa ndipo adzanena kuti alendo (aliens) anatenga okhulupirika osonkhanitsidwa mwa Khristu, pamene m'choonadi ndidzakhala nditawasonkhanitsa ku chipinda chachifumu cha Yahweh chifukwa cha ukwati wa mkwati kwa mkwatibwi wake ndipo kuti alandire cholowa chawo ndikulemekezedwa kwamuyaya. Komabe padziko lapansi mabodza ambiri adzanena ndipo adzapanga zinthu zambiri, zambiri pa magalasi awo akuda pogwiritsa ntchito zinyengo ndi matekinoloje a omwe adagwa ndipo zizindikiro zambiri zabodza ndi zozizwitsa adzawulutsa pa zowonera zawo zomwe makamu a anthu adzakhulupirira mosavuta.

Mvetsetsani kuti omwe amakhala m'mizinda yawo ndi omwe amalandira chizindikiro cha chilombo adzasinthidwa kwamuyaya ndipo adzasakaniza chitsulo ndi dongo koma sadzamatirirana wina ndi mzake. Omwe amalandira chizindikiro chawo adzasinthidwa ndipo adzakhala akapolo a maganizo awo a mng'oma ndi intaneti ya zinthu zomwe zikubwera ndipo adzakhala mu ukapolo kuchita chifuniro cha chinjoka, chomwe adzakhala atadzimanga okha kwamuyaya chifukwa adzakhala atadziyika okha kuchokera ku chifaniziro cha Yahweh chomwe adalengedwa. Ndipo onse omwe amachita zinthu zotere alibe chikhululukiro, chifukwa chomwe chili chokhotakhota sichingawongoleredwe.

Choncho mvetsetsani kuti gulu lawo latsopano lidzakhazikika kotheratu pa chinyengo ndi mabodza ndipo okhala pa dziko lapansi adzasiyanitsidwa kotheratu m'chisokonezo ndi dziko lenileni ndi zenizeni zomwe adzakhalamo. Dziko lonse lapansi lidzamangidwa m'chinyengo chachikulu ndipo makamu a anthu adzadabwa pambuyo pa chilombocho n'kumati, ndani angafanane ndi chilombochi ndipo ndani angathe kuchita nacho nkhondo? Ndipo anthu ambiri, ambiri adzagwera m'malawi a moto pogwera m'mabodza awo ndipo ngakhale anthu omwewa omwe mayina awo sanalembedwe m'buku la moyo chikhazikitsireni dziko lapansi adzagwa kwamuyaya chifukwa adzakhala atamukana kotheratu Yahweh pokana Yesu Khristu yemwenso ndi Yeshua HaMashiach.

Kukana kumeneku kudzakhala komaliza ndipo sadzakhala ndi chikhululuko chifukwa anayesa Mawu a chipulumutso omwe akadawapulumutsa kotheratu ngati chinthu chosayera ndipo anatsimikiza kuti choonadi cha Mulungu ndi bodza, ngakhale tchimo lomwe sadzachira. Koma kudzakhala kulekanitsidwa komaliza kwa nthawi kuti palibe chinthu choipa chomwe chidzalowe mu Ufumu wanga wa zaka chikwi koma okhawo omwe amabwera kwa Yeshua HaMashiach yemwe ndi Yesu Khristu m'kulapa mu nthawi za chipulumutso chifukwa palibe munthu angabwere kwa Yahweh m'dzina lina lililonse kapena palibe njira ina iliyonse, chifukwa Ine ndine njira yoona ndi yamoyo.

Izi ndi nthawi zomwe mukukhala, O' Isiraeli, ndipo simungathe kukhala m'mizinda yawo ndipo simungathe kulowa m'gulu lawo chifukwa kulowa nawo kumafuna chizindikiro ndipo onse omwe amalandira chizindikiro cha chilombocho sadzakhululukidwa. Komabe, anthu aphunzitsidwa kudzera mu maphunziro awo abodza omwe amawakonda nthawi zonse, ngakhale chiphunzitso chomwe chimagulitsidwa ngati maphunziro ndipo makamu a anthu azolowera kutero, ndipo adzalandira mabodza awo ndipo unyinji wa anthu achibadwa udzadziphatika kwa omwe adagwa kwamuyaya.

Komabe kudzakhala tero, kuti onse omwe mofunitsitsa amakhulupirira bodza ndipo amakondwera mu chosalungama akhoza kumangidwa ku chilango chamuyaya mpaka kalekale chifukwa cholowa nawo kwa omwe adagwa, ndipo onse omwe alowa nawo kwa omwe adagwa adzagawana mokwanira mu cholowa chawo chomwe chimaperekedwa popanda chifundo chifukwa sanasonyeze chifundo koma chidani chankhanza chokha ku zinthu zonse za Yahweh.

Ine ndine Khristu Yesu yemwenso ndi Yesu Khristu yemwenso ndi Yeshua HaMashiach ndipo ndimabwera ngati mkango wa fuko la Yuda ndipo m'chilungamo ndimabwera kudzaweruza ndi kuchita nkhondo.

Ine ndine amene ndidzawononga chisokonezo chachikulu ndi zenizeni zabodza za omwe adagwa, ndipo mu ufulu waulemerero wa choonadi ndi chilungamo chilengedwe chidzapulumutsidwa mu ufulu waulemerero wa ana a Yahweh.

Ine ndine amene ndikuitana a Isiraeli, ndipo moona onse a Adamu ku chipulumutso kuti ayanjanitsidwe kotheratu kwa Yahweh kudzera m'magazi a mwanawankhosa, chifukwa ndine tero.

Ine ndine Khristu Yesu Ambuye wanu wokwera ndi wouka ndipo ndapangidwa kukhala mzimu wopatsa moyo kuti onse omwe amabwera kwa ine ndi mtima wotseguka ndi nkhope yotseguka abwere kotheratu ku chipulumutso mwa Ambuye wawo wamuyaya!

Ine ndine Yeshua HaMashiach yemwe pamodzi ndi Yahweh Wamkulu INE NDINE ndatumiza Mawu a Uthenga Wabwino Wamuyaya kudzera mwa mthenga wanga wamphamvu ndi mtumiki yemwe wagonjetsa zinthu zonse pobwera kotheratu kwa Ambuye ndi Mpulumutsi wake wamuyaya mu kudzichepetsa ndi kulapa kwachisoni ndipo kudzera mwa mthenga wanga wamphamvu ndi mtumiki Mawu a Uthenga Wabwino Wamuyaya atumizidwa kuti amvere onse omwe Mawuwa atumizidwa kwa iwo.

Choncho, mvetsetsani nthawi zachinyengo zomwe mukukhala pomwe dziko lonse lapansi lidzatengedwa mu misala ndi chisokonezo ndipo choipa chidzakhala chosalamulirika kotheratu komabe khamu lalikulu, lalikulu lidzapeza chipulumutso chamuyaya poyitana pa dzina la Ambuye wawo Yesu mpaka mapeto. Ndipo mofananamo Ine, Yeshua HaMashiach yemwe ndi Mesiya woona wa Yakobo, ndidzapulumutsa amoyo otsalira amphamvu a Isiraeli omwe adzatengedwa ndikupatsidwa mwayi wolowa mu Ufumu wanga wa zaka chikwi pomwe lonjezo lililonse lidzakhala inde ndi ameni!

Chifukwa chake, O' Isiraeli, kanani matekinoloje awo ndipo musapitirize kukopeka ndi magalasi awo akuda, ngakhale zowonera zawo pomwe anthu pakali pano ali akapolo pamene amathera miyoyo yawo pachabe kuchoka pa chowonera china kupita ku china pamene akugwidwa ndi kusangalatsidwa ndi tchimo. Ngakhale zenizeni zabodza zomwe zikuwoneka ngati zenizeni koma m'choonadi, ndi nkhani yabodza ndipo palibe china koma nthano zolukidwa mwanzeru zolinga kuwononga anthu.

Choncho, tulukani pakati pawo ndipo mukhale olekanitsidwa mu zinthu zonse kwa iwo. Dziyeretseni m'magazi anga otetezera povomereza machimo anu onse kwa ine ndipo dziyeretseni m'mawu anga oyera, ngakhale Mawu a Yahweh, chifukwa Ine ndine Mawu wopangidwa thupi yemwe amakhala ndi kukhala pakati panu ndipo moona, mkati mwanu kudzera mwa mzimu wanga wa lonjezo ngati mukhala omvera ku Mawu anga.

Ndimakukondani anthu a dziko langa, ndipo moona ndimakumenyerani nkhondo ndipo ndidzakupulumutsani kotheratu ngati simukomoka m'mavuto anu koma kupuma m'mphamvu zanga ndi ukulu wanga, chifukwa ndimabwera mu ulamuliro wonse wa Yahweh Wamkulu INE NDINE yemwe wagawana mpando wake wachifumu mpaka adani anga atapangidwa chopondapo mapazi anga ndipo ulamuliro wanga sulibe mapeto ndipo ndidzapulumutsa omvera kotheratu m'chikondi chamuyaya ndi chipulumutso ndipo mudzakhalanso anthu apadera, O' Isiraeli, Sela ndi Ameni!
