Chinthu Chodabwitsa

Mawu a Ambuye ali pa ine, taonani mawu a Ambuye!

Taonani! Ndikuchita chinthu chodabwitsa pamaso panu, ndipo ambiri a inu mukukana kuona!

Taonani! Ndikugwira ntchito yamphamvu pakati panu yomwe posachedwapa idzakwaniritsidwa!

Taonani! Ndikugwira ntchito yamphamvu mwa inu, yomwe mupulumutsidwa nayo ndi kupangidwa kukhala athunthu!

Ndachita zambiri kuposa kungokonza zidutswa zochepa zosweka. Ndachita zambiri kuposa kukuphatikizani pamodzi kachiwiri, pamene misoko ndi zipsera zikuonekabe. Ndapanga osankhidwa anga ndi okhulupirika kukhala athunthu mwa ine, Khristu wamoyo, ndipo ndakulengani inu mwatsopano. Dziwani kuti miyoyo yanu yabisika mwa ine, ndipo pamene ndidzawonekera, inunso mudzawonekera pamodzi ndi ine mu ulemerero ndipo kuyambira pamenepo mudzakhala ndi ine mpaka kalekale. Ndinu mkwatibwi wanga ndipo ndinu thupi langa; kodi sindinakuitaneni m'maina anu? Kodi sindinakukonzereni njira yoti mubwerere kwa Atate wanu, Atate wa zounikira?

Ndine amene ndidadziwika ndi Atate wanga dziko lisanayambe, amene ndidatumizidwa ndi kubadwa chifukwa cha chipulumutso chanu, amene ndidapachikidwa, kupereka dontho langa lomaliza la magazi chifukwa cha machimo anu. Palibe chomwe chasiyidwa ndipo palibe chomwe chosachitidwa, ndipo zomwe zanenedwa zidzachitidwa ndi kukwaniritsidwa mpaka pomaliza. Ndakulengani ndi mzimu wanga woyera womwe Atate anapereka kuti utsanulidwe pa atumwi anga pa tsiku lija lakale la Pentekoste, pamene analankhula ntchito zodabwitsa za Mulungu m'malilime omwe iwo eni sakanatha kuwamvetsa. Ndaitana, ndipo ngakhale ambiri andikana, otsala a chisomo ndi chisankho ayankha, ndipo ndagwira ntchito yamphamvu mwa iwo yomwe yatsala pang'ono kuwululidwa. Zoonadi, mukamadziona pagalasi, umunthu wanu weniweni simuuona. Ndakupangani kukhala amphamvu m'kufooka kwanu, ndipo pakuwonekera kwanga inunso mudzawonekera chomwecho. Mudzakhala ngati ine, ndipo mudzakhala ndi ine nthawi zonse. Ndine zipatso zoyambirira za akufa, ndipo kukolola kuli pafupi ndi inu. Pamene ndidzawonekera, mudzauka kudzakumana nane, ndipo mudzakhala m'nyumba ya Atate wanga pamene ndikukutengani kupita kwanu kumwamba.

Zoonadi, zoonadi, m'Mawu anga oyera (popeza ndine mawu osanduka thupi amene ali woyera ndipo akuyenda pakati panu), inde, m'mawu anga oyera, kodi simunawerenge kuti chiweruzo chimayambira m'nyumba ya Mulungu? Ndatsala pang'ono kumaliza ntchito mwa osankhidwa anga, olungama, ndi osankhidwa, m'mene mudzalengedwanso mwatsopano mwa ine ndipo mudzakhala monga momwe ndilili, pakuti mwafanizidwa ndi chifaniziro cha mwana wobadwa yekha wa Mulungu. Mphoto yanga ili ndi ine, ndipo sindidzakuweruzani chifukwa cha machimo anu; kodi sindinawalipire kale? Mwa kuvomereza kwanu kwa Ufumu wanga ndi kudziwika kwanu ndi ine, mwakhululukidwa ndipo ndinu olungama mwa ine, pakuti ndinalipira mtengo woopsa womwe sunali wanga kuti ndiulipire popeza ndilibe tchimo. Ayi, ndinatenga machimo anu, zoyipa zanu, ufiti wanu, ndi mkwiyo ndi chilango kuchokera kwa inu kuti mukhale amodzi ndi Atate ndi Mwana. Ayi, ndidzaweruza ntchito zanu zomwe munazichita padziko lapansi.

Ndipo pamodzi ndi chiweruzo changa pali mphoto yayikulu, ndipo ntchito zambiri zidzapulumuka zomwe ndi golide, siliva, ndi miyala yamtengo wapatali. Zoonadi, mtengo, udzu, ndi ziputu zidzangopsa ngati mankhusu, ndipo mudzataya zinthu, ngakhale mudzapulumutsidwa ngati kudzera pamoto. Pakuti moto wanga kwa inu ndi moto wa woyenga, m'mene ndayeretsa miyoyo yanu monga mmene wosula golide amayeretsera golide wake, kuchotsa zonyansa. Imani chilili ndipo khalani chete mwa ine. Ndani angakuvutitseni, ngakhale zoonadi mukuzunzidwa ndi kupirira masautso? Musaope, anandida ine ndipo amadanso inu. Komabe, m'chiwombolo chanu mudzasiriridwa kwambiri mu ulemerero womwe ndidzakuvekeni pa tsiku lija lomwe lili pafupi ndipo likubwera mwachangu. Atate wanga akulakalaka kukuonani pamaso pake m'chipinda chake chachifumu, kumene mipando yanu ikukudikirirani, pakuti Atate wanga wakukhazikani m'malo akumwamba. Kodi sindinu osankhidwa ake, akazembe ake, ulemerero wake, korona, ndi mphoto yamtengo wapatali wosayerekezeka? Zoonadi, ndinu amtengo wapatali.

Chiweruzo chidzagwa pa Babulo Wamkulu posachedwapa, ndipo chidzakhala chiweruzo choopsa, koma nchoyenera. O, munthu, ndidzakupirira mpaka liti? Mwachoka pa kukhala mtundu wa oyera mtima omwe anali ngati chowunikira ku dziko lapansi kuti libwere kwa ine, kupita ku mtundu wa mahule achigololo. Ndalekanitsa anthu anga ndi inu, ndipo kulekanitsidwa kwatsala pang'ono kukwaniritsidwa pamene m'badwo uno wa chisomo ukutha. O, munthu, chifukwa chiyani ukutsatira woyipayo, kulandira ziphuphu zake zopanda pake m'malo mwa ine, Khristu wamuyaya, mu ulemerero ndi ukulu wanga wonse.

Atate wanga anapereka nthawi kuti mitundu yonse ilape, ndipo anthu ambiri, ngakhale khamu lalikulu, alandira pempho langa mu chisomo changa chopulumutsa chomwe chinatsanulidwa pa iwo.

Koma iwe, o, munthu wathupi amene amakana zinthu za mzimu ndi kunyoza mnzake, udzapirira chiweruzo chachikulu pa dziko lako, ndipo Babulo Wamkulu sadzakhalakonso. Babulo, wasanduka mtundu wa mahule omwe amasewera mbali yawo ndi masiketi awo pamwamba pa mitu yawo ngati bulu wamtchire wofuna yamphongo akudikirira wodutsa wotsatira. Mwadzidetsa nokha ndi thupi lachilendo, mwasangalala ndi kukonda machimo anu, ndipo chiweruzo chamuyaya chidzagwa pa onse a mitima yowuma omwe amaona nsembe yanga ngati chinthu chopepuka ndi chopanda pake kuchiona. Mwasanduka mtundu wa ochita zoyipa ndi oswa mapangano omwe amasintha akazi ngati zovala zakale ndi zatsopano mpaka zatsopanozo zitada. Mawu anu akulu omwe mwalankhula motsutsana ndi Atate wanga adzaweruzidwa. Maganizo anu opanda pake adzayikidwa pamaso panu, ndipo mudzafotokoza. Koma kufotokoza kwanu sikudzakwanira kuti muime olungama pamaso panga. Kukhalapo kwanu kwenikweni kwaipitsidwa, ndipo mu misala yanu mwasankha thupi lanu lowola, pakuti monga Atate wanga ananenera, mudzafa ndithu. Komabe, zikuonekabe zachilendo kwa inu kuti pakufa mudzafa. Ngakhale kutha kwanu kwathunthu sikunali kwanthawi yomweyo, mwakhala mukufa kuyambira tsiku la mpweya wanu woyamba. Chiweruzo chalengezedwa pa mtundu wanu, ndipo kodi kumwamba sikukufuula chimodzimodzi? Ndidzawononga kotheratu dziko lanu, ndipo nsanja zanu zazikulu zidzagwa, ndipo mdima wandiweyani udzaphimba dziko lapansi. Makoma anu agwetsedwa kale, ndipo mamiliyoni abwera kwa inu kudzalandira kwa inu zomwe ndawapatsa, chifukwa ndidzanenepetsa dzanja la adani anu m'chiweruzo chanu. Nthawi yanu yatsala pang'ono kutha, ndipo ndinu mtundu womangidwa ndi maunyolo pamaso panga, kuyembekezera angelo anga kuti abwere m'chiweruzo. Mudzagwa kwambiri pamene madzi akubwera pa inu, pamene kugwedezeka kukubwera pa inu, pamene magulu ankhondo akumasulidwa, ndipo dziko lanu lidzang'ambika pakati. Njala idzakhazikika pa dziko lapansi, ndipo nkhondo idzabwera ku dziko lonse lapansi pamene chilombo chikuuka kuchokera ku phulusa m'ziwembu zawo zomwe anakonza kwanthawi yayitali.

Nthawi yaperekedwa ya kulapa, ndipo ambiri ayankha m'chikhulupiriro, chifundo chachikulu chomwe ndapereka chiweruzo chisanachitidwe. Sindisangalala ndi chiwonongeko chomwe chikubwera, koma ngakhale tsopano, ambiri akukhala m'tchimo lalikulu, ngakhale kugwiririra ana anu omwe, ndipo simusamala bola ngati ndalama zanu zili pafupi. Ndidzatenga chuma chonse, zakudya zokoma, chisangalalo, ndi chimwemwe kuchokera kwa inu. Mudzafota pamaso pa kumwamba, kufunafuna kachidutswa ka chakudya komwe ambiri sadzakapeza. Komabe, iwo amene adzaitana pa dzina la Ambuye adzapulumutsidwa.

Tiana tanga, ndi ola lomaliza la nthawi yomaliza m'm'badwo uno wa chisomo. Nditumiza aneneri anga mu ntchito ya bwenzi langa lokondedwa Yohane Mbatizi. Kodi sanakhale mawu ofuula m'chipululu? Kodi si abusa anga, oitana ndi kufunafuna nkhosa zanga zomaliza zotayika? Zoonadi, ndili ndi khola linanso, ndipo nthawi ya chipulumutso chawo ili pafupi, monga Yakobo adzapulumutsidwa ndipo otsala a Yakobo adzandivomereza ngati Ambuye wawo, pamene osankhidwa onse akukhala munthu mmodzi watsopano. Nditumiza ambiri padziko lonse lapansi, kulengeza kubwera kwanga.

Ngakhale anthu anga ambiri anyengedwa ndipo ali ofunda, mzimu wanga udzadzutsa ambiri, ndipo ambiri adzabwerera kwa ine. Kuwonekera kwanga kuli pafupi. Imani mwamphamvu ndikufuula kubwera kwanga ndikufuula mawu anga a chipulumutso kwa onse. Nthawi yatsala pang'ono, ndipo kuli chete pansi panyanja pamene magulu a kumwamba akudikirira kulira kwa lipenga komwe kuli pafupi kuposa momwe mungaganizire. Komabe, kwatsala nthawi yochepa kwambiri, ndipo ambiri adzapulumutsidwa, choncho musataye mtima.

Ndine amene ndimaweta mpingo wanga ndipo ndapereka mphatso kwa anthu.

Ndine Ambuye wamkulu ndi wamphamvu, wophedwa kuyambira pa maziko a dziko lapansi.

Ndine amene ndinapereka moyo wanga chifukwa cha gulu la nkhosa, Khristu wamoyo amene Mulungu wamuukitsa kwa akufa.

Ndine Mawu osanduka thupi amene amakhala pakati panu.

Ndine mwana wobadwa yekha wa Mulungu wobwera m'thupi, ngakhale Mesiya wamphamvu wa Israyeli wa Mulungu.

Ndili pafupi, ndipo kuwonekera kwanga kuli pafupi.

Ndine woweruza wolungama amene ndili woyenera kutsegula zisindikizo.

Ndine mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu, wophedwa chifukwa cha machimo a anthu anga.

Ndine mpulumutsi wopanda tchimo amene amapulumutsa mpaka pomaliza.

Ndine munthu wodziwa chisoni chambiri ndi zowawa, komabe chimwemwe changa chimakhala chathunthu.

Ndine Ambuye wamoyo, mbusa wolungama.

Ndine amene ndinanenedweratu ndi aneneri onse ndipo ndili ndi makiyi a imfa ndi helo, ndipo Atate waika zinthu zonse kumwamba ndi padziko lapansi m'manja mwanga amphamvu.
