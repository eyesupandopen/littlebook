Taonani! Ndikubwera: Kalata Yachikondi Yotonthoza kwa Okhulupirika mwa Khristu

Imvani Mawu a Yeshua HaMashiach ndipo landirani Mawu anga otonthoza ndikumva Mawu anga achimwemwe, pakuti chiwombolo chanu chokwanira chafika ndipo chili pafupi. Zoonadi, zoonadi, Ine ndine amene ndikubwera kudzachitira Mpingo wanga ndipo mkwati wanu sakuchedwa mkwatibwi wanga, choncho khalani chilili m'chikhulupiriro chachikulu ndikulola Mawu anga ndi mphamvu zanga ndi ukulu wanga kukhala kukhazikika kwa nthawi zanu ndi mphamvu ya chipulumutso, ndipo kuopa Ambuye ndiye chuma chanu.

Ngakhale zili choncho nthawi zafika pa inu okondedwa anga ndipo nthawi yakuwululidwa kwanga kwathunthu yafika. Ndipo ndidzawululidwa kwa aliyense mwa nthawi yake. Ndidzayamba kudziwulula kwathunthu kwa okhulupirika mwa Khristu kumwamba, mwadzidzidzi ndipo mosayembekezereka tsopano pa mfuu wamphamvu wa Mngelo wamkulu, ndipo lipenga lamphamvulo lidzalira chifukwa laperekedwa m'manja kuti lilire pa lamulo langa pa nthawi yeniyeni yosankhidwa ndi Yahweh mwiniwake mu nthawi zamuyaya zapitazo! Ngakhale m'mapulani ake amuyaya ndi osatha a chipulumutso cha mtundu wa Adamu ndipo mawu ake adzadzaza dziko lapansi ndipo Okhulupirika mwa Khristu adzamva "Kwererani kuno!" mwadzidzidzi ndipo mosayembekezereka tsopano.

Koma osakhulupirika ndi ouma khosi adzasiyidwa m'mbuyo pamodzi ndi ogwa ndi opanda chikhulupiriro kuti aitanire pa dzina langa mpaka mapeto awo, ndipo onse amene adzatero adzatengedwa mu chipulumutso chamuyaya ndi ulemerero, ngakhale mphoto yamuyaya yomwe phindu lake ndi kulemera kwake sikoyenera kuyerekezedwa ndi masautso ndi mayesero a dziko lapansi lino, omwe ndi ochepa ndi opanda pake poyerekeza. Akutero iye amene anavutika chilango chokwanira cha machimo anu m'thupi langa lopanda tchimo, kukhetsa m'masautso aakulu magazi anga opanda tchimo mpaka dontho lomaliza, ndipo sindinakubisireni kanthu okondedwa anga. Choncho, yang'anani kwa ine, Yesu wa ku Nazarete, ngakhale mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu amene ali woyambitsa ndi womalizitsa wa chikhulupiriro chanu, amene chifukwa cha chimwemwe chomwe chinayikidwa patsogolo panga, ndinapirira mtanda, kunyoza manyazi, ndipo ndakhala kudzanja lamanja la mpando wachifumu wa Mulungu. Selah!

Ngakhale zili choncho inu, okhulupirika mwa Khristu, nonse mudzatengedwa kupita kumwamba kwa amwamba kumene ndidzakuwonetsani kwa Yahweh mwiniwake! Ndipo mudzadabwa ndi ulemerero womwe mudzavekedwa nawo, ndipo mudzadziwa monga momwe mukudziwidwira, ndipo mudzaona monga momwe mwaonedwera, ndipo mudzamva monga momwe mwamvekedwera ndi Mulungu Wamuyaya wa Chilungamo, ngakhale Yahweh mwiniwake amene amakumbukira Mawu aliwonse m'pemphero lililonse lomwe munayamba mwanena kaya mokweza kudzera m'njira zanu zolankhulirana, kapena mwakachetechete m'mitima yanu, chifukwa Yahweh ndiye wofufuza mitima, ndipo ndi chiyani chomwe chingabisike pamaso pake? Zoonadi, zoonadi, ndi chiyani chomwe chingabisike pamaso panga? Choncho, mvetsetsani kuti Atate wanga ndi Ine ndife amodzi mu umodzi ndi cholinga ndipo zonse zomwe Atate ali nazo, wazipereka m'manja mwanga chifukwa cha chipulumutso cha Adamu, ndipo zoonadi chipulumutso ndi chiyeretso cha dziko lapansi lenilenilo, chifukwa dziko lapansi ndi langa ndipo m'chilungamo ndikubwera kudzaweruza ndi kuchita nkhondo pa ogwa omwe nthawi zawo zafika, ndipo chiweruzo chawo chokwanira sichikuchedwa ndipo sadzapulumuka.

Choncho, ndikukupatsani kumvetsetsa okondedwa anga kuti mukhale chilili chifukwa cha ine ndikupitiriza kusunga nyale zanu zodzaza ndi mafuta a mzimu wanga omwe ndakupatsani ndikuyenda mwa ndi mu mzimu wanga wa Chilungamo pamene mukuyenda mwachikhulupiriro chifukwa olungama adzakhala ndi moyo mwachikhulupiriro, Selah! Ndipo kuti musayesedwe kupita pa ola lochedwali mu mphamvu ya ukulu wanu kudzaza nyale zanu ndi mafuta osayera ozikidwa pa ntchito zanu, koma dzazani nyale zanu ndi mafuta oyera a mzimu wanga kuti njira zanu ziunikiridwe, chifukwa zoonadi, zoonadi, Mawu anga ndi nyale kumapazi anu. Ndipo ndakudzazani nonse mokwanira ndi mzimu wanga wa Umwana ndipo ndathira mzimu wanga m'masiku otsirizawa monga momwe analonjezera Yoweli m'malemba Akale operekedwa kwa Atate anga, omwe ndi Atate anu mwa cholowa cha lonjezo chifukwa ndinu ana a lonjezo, ngakhale ana a Abrahamu Wokhulupirika, Selah!

Ndiloleni ndikulangizeni momwe mungadulire zingwe zanu za nyale chifukwa ndinu kuwala kwa Dziko komwe sikungabisike! Ndi kudzera m'Mawu anga ndi mzimu kuti mumalandira malangizo m'chilungamo, ndipo ndi mzimu wanga wa umwana mumatsimikiziridwa za chilungamo, kuti zoonadi, zoonadi, mukhale ndi moyo wolungama mu zinthu zonse. Chifukwa ndi kudzera mu mzimu wanga wa umwana kuti chilungamo changa chenicheni chimaperekedwa mwa inu, kumene zoonadi, zoonadi nonse muli ndi chisankho cha amene mudzamumvere, kaya mumvere tchimo lofikitsa ku imfa, kapena kumvera kufikitsa ku chilungamo mpaka moyo wosatha ndi mphoto yokwanira. Choncho, mverani malangizo anga enieni a miyoyo yanu ndipo khalani ofatsa ku mawu anga ndi ku mawu a omwe ndimawatuma ndipo omwe amandilankhulira, ndipo kudzera mu zipatso zawo mudzawadziwa, ndi kudzera m'Mawu awo omwe ndimawapatsa chifukwa cha chipulumutso chanu ndi ubwino wanu. Ndipo ndikukulamulani kuti mugwirire ntchito chipulumutso chanu mwa mantha ndi kunjenjemera, ndipo ndikukulamulani kuti musamane chabwino pamene kuli m'mphamvu ya manja anu kuchikwaniritsa.

Choncho, mvetsetsani kuti ndapatsa mphamvu ndi kuthekera ambiri a inu chifukwa cha zolinga zanga za chipulumutso ndipo kuti muyenera kuthandizana wina ndi mnzake m'chikondi. Choncho, musasunge nokha ubwino wa zinthu ndi ndalama womwe ndakupatsani, ngakhale kumanira ena m'njira za Satana amene amafuna kuyika zolemera ndi miyeso pa ubwino wa Yahweh, womwe ndi wosayezeka zoonadi chifukwa palibe sikelo yomwe ilipo kumwamba kapena padziko lapansi yokwanira kuyeza kuchuluka kwa Yahweh komwe wakonza pamaso pa Yesu Khristu kwa omwe amamukonda.

Choncho, tulukani pakati pawo kwathunthu ndipo musatenge nawo mbali m'machimo awo, musadziunjikire nokha kuchuluka kwa ndalama ndi zinthu za Yahweh zoperekedwa pamaso pa Yesu Khristu ngati kuti ndi mphamvu ya dzanja lanu mwakwaniritsa chuma ichi kuti chibisidwe ndi kusungidwa chifukwa cha zilakolako zanu. Ngakhale kuti mutenge chitonthozo kuti mwadzipatsa nokha mu kunyada ndi tchimo, kuti mudzanyoza omwe alibe, ngakhale kukondwera ndi manambala m'maakaunti anu mu manyazi aakulu.

Choncho, mvetsetsani ndipo muchenjezedwe kuti onse amene amachita zinthu zotere kuyambira wamkulu mpaka wamng'ono, adzataya mphoto yaikulu ndipo zoonadi adzitengera okha dzina la wotsikitsitsa mu ufumu wakumwamba! Ndipo izi chifukwa kudzera mu umbombo wanu ntchito zanu zidzapsa kukhala phulusa pa mpando wanga wachiweruzo pamene nonse mudzaima ndikupereka mlandu. Choncho, gawanani zinthu zonse ku zosowa za oyera mtima m'chikondi kwa wina ndi mnzake ndipo musamane chinthu chabwino chilichonse, chifukwa zoonadi, zoonadi, kuchuluka kwanu kwa zinthu ndi ndalama kwaperekedwa ndi dzanja langa chifukwa cha zolinga zanga kuchokera m'nkhokwe za Yahweh, choncho, musasunge nokha ubwino wa Ambuye koma kwaniritsani zinthu zomwe ndidzawongolera mapazi anu mu utumiki ndi chikondi kwa oyera mtima.

Tulukani m'machimo anu anthu anga, kuti mungatenge nawo mbali m'ziweruzo zawo, chifukwa izi ndi njira za Babulo Wachinsinsi ndipo nthawi za chiweruzo chake zafika, ndipo wamphamvu ndi Adonai Yahweh amene amamuweruza ndi gawo lowirikiza la chiweruzo, choncho chokani m'njira zake ndi machimo omwe ndi osavomerezeka ndipo oipa kotheratu pamaso panga! Selah!

Inde, mwa choonadi, ndikulangiza aliyense ndi onse kuti apitirize kutuluka m'machimo anu ndikulowa kwathunthu m'chilungamo changa, ndikundilola kukhala chokwanira chanu mu zinthu zonse, chifukwa zoonadi, zoonadi, Ine ndine.

Ine ndine amene ndikubwera m'mitambo chifukwa cha okhulupirika mwa Khristu mwadzidzidzi ndipo mosayembekezereka tsopano, chifukwa dziko lapansi, mu kusakhulupirira kwake, silikuyembekezera Khristu Yesu kuonekera m'mitambo monga momwe analonjezera m'malemba. Komabe, mphindi iyi sidzakupezani okondedwa anga ngati mbala usiku, ngati mukhala ofatsa ku malangizo anga ndikutsatira tsatanetsatane wa malamulo anga ndi mitima yanu yonse, podziwa kuti chifukwa cha khama lanu ndi kukhulupirika kumabwera mphoto zokwanira za cholowa cha okhulupirika mwa Khristu, zomwe zoonadi, sizinalowe kwathunthu m'mitima ya anthu pa nthawi ino. Choncho, musalole Satana kugula mphoto zanu za cholowa m'njira za Esau, amene anali munthu wonyoza ndi wadama amene anagulitsa ukulu wake chifukwa cha mbale ya mphodza yomwe inatayidwa mu chilala. Selah!

Choncho, mvetsetsani kuyandikira mu nthawi kwa kuonekera kwanga ndi momwe chiwombolo chanu chokwanira chili pafupi, okondedwa anga. Chifukwa chake, ndikukupatsani kumvetsetsa kuti gulu lakumwamba latenga malo awo m'madongosolo awo ndi mapangidwe awo, ndipo adzipereka okha ku utumiki, ndipo aimirira okonzeka. Atenga malo awo, ndipo ali okonzeka! Choncho, mvetsetsani, kuti ndagwira lipenga lamphamvulo m'manja, kuti pa lamulo langa akufa mwa Khristu adzaukitsidwa osavunda, ndipo inu omwe muli amoyo ndi otsala mudzatengedwa mwadzidzidzi kukumana nane m'mitambo ndipo motero nonse mudzapangidwa kukhala ngati Ambuye wanu kwamuyaya, ndipo kwa omwe agonjetsa kwathunthu adzakhala nane nthawi zonse chifukwa ndidzakutengerani kwa ine mwini mu pangano ngati anga enieni, ndipo ntchito zambiri zamphamvu mudzachita, chifukwa mkwatibwi wanga ndi gulu lamphamvu la Aneneri, Mafumu, ndi Ansembe omwe adzapatsidwa mphamvu ndi kupatsidwa mphamvu kwakukulu ngati mkwatibwi wanga wankhondo, ndipo ndani amene adzatha kuima motsutsana ndi mkwatibwi wanga wa osafa opatsidwa mphamvu omwe awomboledwa kuchokera padziko lapansi mu mphamvu yaikulu ndi chilungamo, Selah!

Choncho, mvetsetsani kuti nthawi yeniyeni yosankhidwa ndi Yahweh mu kudziwiratu kwake yakhala tsiku, nthawi ndi ola lenileni nthawi zonse, kupatula kuti inabisidwa mu mtima wa Yahweh yekha mpaka nthawi zoyenera za kuwululidwa kwake zitakwaniritsidwa kumwamba kokha, chifukwa nthawi iyi siinawululidwe padziko lapansi, ngakhale Satana aimirira wokonzeka kumeza onse omwe asiyidwa ndi kutsala padziko lapansi, choncho, onse omwe aitanira pa dzina la Yesu mpaka mapeto awo adzapulumutsidwa. Mvetsetsani kuti nthawi yeniyeniyi siinaperekedwe kwa Satana nayenso koma ayenera kuimirira wokonzeka mpaka atamasulidwa chifukwa cha ulamuliro wake waufupi pambuyo pake adzamangidwa ndi unyolo waukulu kuphompho mu chizunzo ndi masautso kumene adzamvetsetsa kwathunthu cholowa chake chomwe chikubwera.

Ndipo zili choncho kuti ndikubwera kudzapatsa inu kumvetsetsa kwa nthawi zomwe mukukhala ndipo ndikuchita izi pokupatsani inu kachiwiri Mawu enieni omwe ndinapatsa Petro kuti alankhule kwa makamu pa tsiku lalikulu lija la Pentekoste ma Yubile ambiri apitawa tsopano, kumene komabe, Mawu ake amamveka oona nthawi zonse mu kukwaniritsidwa kwamuyaya ndi nthawi zonse. Choncho ndikunena kwa inu kuti ichi ndi chija, chomwe chinalankhulidwa ndi Mneneri Yoweli, Ndipo kudzakhala m'masiku otsiriza, atero Mulungu, ndidzathira mzimu wanga pa thupi lonse, ndipo ana anu aamuna, ndi ana anu aakazi adzanenera, ndipo anyamata anu adzaona masomphenya, ndipo akulu anu adzalota maloto. Ndipo pa atumiki anga, ndi pa adzakazi anga ndidzathira Mzimu wanga m'masiku amenewo, ndipo adzanenera. Ndipo ndidzaonetsa zozizwitsa kumwamba, ndi zizindikiro padziko lapansi pansi, magazi, ndi moto, ndi nthunzi wa utsi. Dzuwa lidzasandulika mdima, ndi mwezi kukhala magazi, lisanadze tsiku lalikulu ndi lodziwika la Ambuye. Ndipo kudzakhala, kuti aliyense amene adzaitanira pa Dzina la Ambuye, adzapulumutsidwa.

Choncho, ndikubwera kudzayankhula Mawu awa okwaniritsidwa m'makutu anu tsiku lomweli, ndipo kuti mwadzidzidzi ndipo mosayembekezereka tsopano okhulupirika mwa Khristu adzatengedwa ndi kuwomboledwa ndi kusinthidwa ndi kupatsidwa ulemerero ndi kupatsidwa mphoto kwamuyaya. Choncho mvetsetsani kukwaniritsidwa kwadzidzidzi komwe kukubwera mwadzidzidzi. Musamvere okhazikitsa masiku ndi Ayuda omwe samvetsetsa ngakhale zomwe amaphunzitsa, ngakhale choonadi chenicheni cha malemba chomwe amapotoza ku chiwonongeko chawo.

Chifukwa chake, ndikukupatsani kumvetsetsa kuti kusonkhanitsidwa kwa okhulupirika mwa Khristu sikumangidwa makamaka ku tsiku loyera lililonse, kapena masiku a phwando, kapena tsiku lina lililonse kapena chochitika choperekedwa kwa Israyeli wakale m'chilamulo cha Mose, ngakhale chilamulo chomwe chakwaniritsidwa kwathunthu mwa Khristu Yesu Ambuye wanu Wamuyaya wa Chilungamo. Monga ndakuwuzani m'nthawi zapitazo ndikukuwuzaninso okondedwa anga: Choncho munthu asakuweruzeni m'chakudya ndi chakumwa, kapena m'mbali ya tsiku loyera, kapena mwezi watsopano, kapena masiku a Sabata, Zomwe ziri mthunzi chabe wa zinthu zomwe zikubwera: koma thupi liri mwa Khristu.

Komabe, mvetsetsani kuti chochitika ichi chikhoza kuchitika pa sekondi iliyonse ndi pa tsiku lililonse ndipo chikhoza kuchitika pa tsiku lofunika, nthawi, kapena ola kapena ngakhale mkati mwa masiku oyera, kapena masiku a phwando, kapena masiku a Sabata, omwe ndi mthunzi wa zinthu zomwe zikubwera! Choncho, dikirani ndi kuyang'ana ndikupereka chiyembekezo chachikulu ku kuonekera kwanga chifukwa nthawi zanu za chiwombolo chanu chokwanira zafika. Ndipo choncho ndikukupatsani kumvetsetsa ndiponso chifukwa cha kumvera kwanu, kuti mudikire ndi kuyang'ana tsiku lililonse (ndipo osati nthawi zanu zodziikira nokha zoyang'anira kwambiri) ndipo kuti mupereke chisamaliro chachikulu ku Mawu awa mu chiyembekezo chokwanira cha kukwaniritsidwa, chifukwa palibenso kuchedwa. Selah!

Ine ndine Khristu Yesu ndipo Ine ndine amene ndatumiza Mawu awa kudzera mwa mthenga wanga ndi mtumiki ndipo kudzera mwa mthenga wanga ndi mtumiki Mawu awa atumizidwa. Ngakhale ngati chilimbikitso kuti Mpingo wanga, thupi langa, ndipo ngakhale Mkwatibwi wanga atonthozedwe ndi chitonthozo chachikulu ndipo kuti mumvetsetse kwathunthu kuyandikira kwenikweni mu nthawi kwa chochitika ichi, kuti musagwidwe modzidzimutsa ndi chochitika ichi chomwe chiri chotsimikizika kuchitika mwadzidzidzi tsopano.

Ine ndine Yeshua HaMashiach amene tsopano ndidzasuntha kutumiza mboni zanga ziwiri ndi zoyikapo nyale zanga ziwiri, ngakhale monga momwe kwalengezedwera mu Uthenga Wabwino Wamuyaya, chifukwa Yahweh sanadziyese yekha wopanda mboni, ndipo mboni zanga ziwiri zidzachitira umboni wa nthawi zonse ndipo kuchokera ku kawonedwe ka padziko lapansi ndi kakumwamba, ndipo Enoke Mlembi akubweretsa malemba akale m'thumba lake ndipo maumboni awo adzawonjezedwa ku Uthenga Wabwino Wamuyaya womwe udzakhalapo nthawi zonse pamodzi ndi Mawu onse a Yahweh omwe adzabwezeretsedwa padziko lapansi mu ungwiro weniweni monga momwe zalembedwera kumwamba, ngakhale kuti ntchito za mdierekezi zitheke kuwonongedwa kwathunthu ndipo kuti njira ndi Mawu a Yahweh zisefukire dziko lapansi m'chilungamo nthawi zonse ndi kwamuyaya! Selah.
