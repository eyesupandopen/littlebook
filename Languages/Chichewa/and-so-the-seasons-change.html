Ndipo Choncho Nyengo Zikusintha!

Dziyikeni pamodzi, Mpingo wanga, ndipo tcherani makutu anu ku nzeru. Tcherani makutu anu ku liwu la Mfumu yanu yankhondo, ngakhale iye amene waima kuti akumenyereni nkhondo, ngakhale ine Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda amene ndine Mbuye wa ambuye ndi Mfumu ya mafumu. Ndine amene Yehova wamupatsa zinthu zonse kumwamba ndi padziko lapansi, m'manja mwanga amphamvu pakuti ndili ndi moyo kwamuyaya ndipo ndigwira makiyi a imfa ndi a gehena m'manja mwanga, ndipo dziko lapansi ndi langa pakuti ndaligula ndi magazi anga owombola. Ndipo ndili ndi dzina loposa maina onse kuti m'Dzina la Yesu bondo lililonse lidzapinda, la zinthu za kumwamba, ndi zinthu za padziko lapansi, ndi zinthu za pansi pa dziko lapansi. Ndipo kuti lilime lililonse lidzavomereza kuti Yesu Khristu ndiye Mbuye, ku ulemerero wa Atate wa Kuwala, amene mulibe kusinthasintha kapena mthunzi wotembenuka. Ndipo ndani angathe kuima pamaso pa lupanga lakuthwa konsekonse limene lituluka m'kamwa mwanga? Pakuti ndi ilo, ndidzakantha mitundu ya anthu: ndipo ndine amene ndidzawalamulira ndi ndodo yachitsulo: ndipo ndine amene ndiponda mwa mkwiyo choponderamo mphesa cha ukali ndi mkwiyo wa El Shaddai ngakhale Mulungu Wamphamvuyonse. Ndipo ndine amene ndili ndi dzina lolembedwa pa chovala changa, ndi pa ntchafu yanga, Mfumu Ya Mafumu, Ndi Mbuye Wa Ambuye, ndipo nthawi yanga YAKWANA!

Kodi pali chiyani chomwe chingapezeke pakati pa chilengedwe chonse cha Yehova, ana anga, chomwe muyenera kuchiopa? Dziwani choonadi, tiana twanga, kuti ogwawo amakuopani. Pakuti akudziwa kuti tsopano ali ngati inu, ndipo inu mudzapangidwa kukhala aulemerero waukulu kuposa momwe iwo analiri poyamba, ngakhale onse amene anasiya malo awo oyamba. Kuchoka pa malamulo amene Yehova anaika pa chilengedwe chake ndi tchimo lalikulu chifukwa chilengedwe cha Yehova chinapangidwa kuti chigwire ntchito mu ungwiro wogwirizana. Ndipo Yehova amapereka ufulu wosankha, choncho kusankha kupanduka m'zilakolako zadyera kumabweretsa imfa ndi kuvutika pakati pa moyo. Ndipo choncho tchimo la ogwawo likumvekabe mpaka lero, ndipo nthawi ya chiweruzo chawo ndi tsopano! Kodi simukudziwa, ana anga, kuti mudzaweruza angelo?

Ndani adzakulekanitsani ndi chikondi changa? Kodi ndi chisautso kapena kuwawa mtima, kapena chizunzo, kapena njala, kapena umaliseche, kapena zoopsa, kapena lupanga? Ayi! Mu zinthu zonsezi ndinu oposa opambana mwa ine Khristu Yesu Mbuye wanu wokwezedwa ndi wopatsidwa mphamvu, pakuti Yehova wagawana mpando wake wachifumu ndi ine ndipo ndidzagawana mpando wanga wachifumu ndi okhulupirika, ngakhale omwe amandikonda ndi omwe ndimawakonda. Mvetsetsani kuti Yehova mwiniwake amakumenyerani nkhondo ndipo Yehova wandituma, ngakhale Mbuye wanu, Mfumu ndi mtetezi wanu. Pakuti ndine nsanja yanu yolimba ndi thanthwe lachitetezo, pakuti ndine Thanthwe la Mibadwo, ngakhale Mbuye ndi mpulumutsi wanu. Ndipo ndine woyenga amene amakhala ndi mvukuto ndi nyundo m'manja kusonkhezera moto wa chiyeretso kuchotsa zinyalala! Ndipo ndine amene ndimasula ndi kupinda zitsulo zamtengo wapatali za nyumba yosungiramo chuma ya Yehova, yomwe chuma chake chambiri ndi chuma chamtengo wapatali chomwe ndi ana a Mulungu kukhala mu ungwiro waulemerero, ndipo ntchito zanga zatsala pang'ono kukwaniritsidwa mwa inu Ana anga, ngakhale zipatso zanga zoyamba ndi oitanidwa ndi osankhidwa ndi okhulupirika.

Taonani! Nthawi yakwana ya kumalizidwa ndi chiwombolo chokwanira cha inu, okondedwa anga, ndipo ndidzamaliza chiwombolo chanu ndipo mu umuyaya wa muyaya tsopano mudzapita! Ndipo m'kuthwanima kwa diso mudzadzipeza mutaima m'chipinda chachifumu cha Yehova modabwitsa kwakumwamba ndi chimwemwe chosatha, pakuti tsopano mudzalowa m'chimwemwe cha Mbuye wanu. Ndipo mudzadzipeza mutamezedwa m'kuwala kwaulemerero kosatha kwa Yehova, Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya ndi kuwala kosatha kwa Mwana wa Chilungamo ndipo mudzalengedwanso mwatsopano, ndipo mudzadabwa ndi ulemerero umene mudzavekedwa nawo. Ana anga, ntchito iliyonse imene munayamba mwaigwira m'dzina langa potumikira Khristu, pakuti mwatumikira oyera mtima ndipo mukuwatumikira, yalembedwa kumwamba! Diso silinaone, ngakhale khutu silinamve, ngakhale sizinalowe mumtima mwa munthu zinthu zimene Yehova wakonzera iwo amene amamukonda ndipo mphoto yanu ili m'dzanja langa, pakuti ndinu opambana anga, ndipo ndakulengani mwatsopano mwa ine kwamuyaya, ndipo simudzawola ndipo chanu ndi cholowa chosatha chamuyaya chomwe sichifota ndipo ulemerero wanu ndi wamuyaya. Pakuti popeza mwakhala okhulupirika m'chaching'ono kwambiri ndidzakupangani kukhala olamulira pa zambiri.

M'kamphindi, m'kamphindi kochepa, m'kagawo ka sekondi chiwombolo chanu chokwanira chidzachitika ndipo mavuto anu onse adzatha nthawi yomweyo. Mwadzidzidzi sipadzakhala matenda, mwadzidzidzi sipadzakhala ululu, mwadzidzidzi sipadzakhala mantha, mwadzidzidzi mudzakhala mu umodzi wangwiro ndi Yehova, mwadzidzidzi mudzakhala mu umodzi wangwiro ndi Yeshua HaMashiach, mwadzidzidzi mudzadzipeza odzaza ndi chimwemwe, mtendere, ndipo kuwala kosatha kudzawala kuchokera kwa inu kwamuyaya. Pakuti popeza mwalemekeza Mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu, Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda tsopano udzakulemekezani, pakuti ndine amene mwamukhulupirira, ndipo ndine wokhulupirika ndi woona, amene ndasunga chimene munachiika kwa ine mpaka tsiku lino. Ndipo inu amene ndakupangani kukhala anzeru, mudzawala kwamuyaya, ngakhale ngati kuwala kwa thambo: ndipo inu amene mwatembenuza ambiri ku chilungamo, mudzawala ngati nyenyezi, kwamuyaya ndi muyaya. Choncho tsopano podziwa izi ana anga kuti tsopano ndi nthawi yosiya nkhawa za dziko lino ndi kupuma mwa ine ndi kukhala oyera ngati namwali m'magazi anga owombola.

Taonani! Wotsutsa abale aponyedwa pansi aponyedwa pansi, ngakhale njoka yakale ija, ngakhale chinjoka chachikulu chofiira chomwe mchira wake unakoka gawo limodzi mwa magawo atatu a nyenyezi ndikuziponya padziko lapansi. Ndipo kunali nkhondo kumwamba, Mikayeli ndi Angelo ake anamenyana ndi chinjoka, ndipo chinjoka chinamenya nkhondo ndi angelo ake. Koma sanapambane, ngakhale malo awo sanapezekenso kumwamba. Ndipo chinjoka chachikulu, njoka yakale ija, yotchedwa mdierekezi ndi Satana, chinaponyedwa kunja, chomwe chinyenga dziko lonse lapansi: aponyedwa padziko lapansi, ndipo angelo ake aponyedwa kunja pamodzi naye. Pakuti panali kung'anima ndi mkokomo ndipo panali kugwedezeka ndipo chizindikiro cha kukhalapo kwake chinamveka, ndipo mabodza a wonyenga wa lilime logawanika afalikira kale chifukwa akhala akuyang'anira kubwera kwa chizindikiro chake. Chizindikiro chake chinali pa fano lake lamwano.

Tsoka kwa okhala padziko lapansi pakuti mdierekezi ndi angelo ake aponyedwa pansi pakati panu ndikumangiriridwa padziko lapansi kuti aweruzidwe ndipo palibe malo kumwamba kwa iwo kwamuyaya ndipo sadzalowanso. Kondwerani inu kumwamba, pakuti wotsutsa abale aponyedwa pansi amene ankawatsutsa usana ndi usiku pamaso pa Mulungu wathu, ndipo malo ake sali kumwamba kachiwiri ndipo kwamuyaya. Tsopano chipulumutso chikubwera, tsopano ulemerero wa oyera mtima ukubwera, ndipo adzalemekezedwa pakuti adzalemekeza Yehova mwa ine Khristu Yesu ndipo ndidzawaveka chilungamo chosatha, ngakhale bafuta woyera wopanda banga amene sangadetseke, ndipo ndidzawapatsa mphoto yosatha ndipo ndidzawapanga kukhala Mafumu ndi Ansembe pamaso pa Yehova ndipo adzalamulira kuchokera pampando wanga wachifumu monga momwe chikondi chawo ndi kukhulupirika kwawo kwakhalira. Kwa okhulupirika kuli mphoto yaikulu ndipo kwa osakhulupirika kuli kutayika, koma adzapulumutsidwa, koma ngati kudzera pamoto.

Ndimveni, inu nonse amene mukhala padziko lapansi, ngakhale muli ogontha ndikukana zizindikiro zanga! Mdierekezi tsopano waponyedwa pansi pakati panu, ndipo akudziwa kuti ali ndi nthawi yochepa. Ali ndi mkwiyo waukulu ndi kubwezera, pakuti ndi mdani wogonjetsedwa womangidwa chifukwa cha zolinga za Yehova zomwe zidzakwaniritsidwa m'nyanja yamoto yamtundu wa buluu ya chiwonongeko chosalekeza. Ndipo chifukwa inu, inu munthu wachibadwa mukonda mdima m'malo mwa kuwala, chifukwa inu, inu munthu wachibadwa mukonda ntchito zanu zoipa ndikunyoza dzanja langa lopulumutsa lachisomo, (Pakuti aliyense amene achita zoipa, amadana ndi kuwala, ngakhale kubwera kukuwala, kuti ntchito zake zisadzudzulidwe) choncho tsopano ndidzakupatsani zoipa zomwe mukuzilakalaka. Chifukwa tsopano mukukhala m'chihema cha Moloki ndikugwedeza nyenyezi ya mulungu wanu Remfani pa mbendera yanu, mafano omwe munapanga kuti muwapembedze.

Popeza mumapereka ana nsembe kwa Moloki, popeza mumakonda Baphomet wanu wonyansa, taonani! Chinjoka chanu tsopano chili pakati panu. Ndipo mudzaphunzira nokha kuti mbala sibwera koma kudzaba, kupha ndi kuwononga. Kupatula kuti tsopano sipadzakhala chofunda, sipadzakhala woletsa, sipadzakhalanso chifundo ndi chisomo chochokera kumwamba pakuti mwasankha chinjoka kukhala mulungu wanu, ndipo mwasankha Baraba kukhala mpulumutsi ndi mfumu yanu. Choncho tsopano ndikuwapereka kuti akulamulireni monga momwe mitima yanu yoipa ikufunira. Taonani! Pano pali nzeru, iye amene ali ndi chidziwitso awerenge nambala ya chilombo pakuti ili mu nyenyezi ya Remfani yomwe amanyamula pa mbendera yawo, pakuti iwo ndi sunagoge wa Satana omwe akufuna kuba dziko ndi kupha eni ake oyenera ndipo ndidzapulumutsa Yakobo!

Taonani! Satana akulowa mwa Baraba, ndipo akutenga ulamuliro, ndipo zizindikiro zake zabodza ndi zozizwitsa zidzatsogolera ku chiwonongeko chanu chotheratu. Pakuti adzagwiritsa ntchito zonena zake kuti Mulungu yekha anamupulumutsa kuti anene kuti watumidwa ndi Mulungu ndipo kuti iye ndi Mulungu, pakuti amakhulupirira kuti iwo ndi Mulungu m'thupi ndipo ena onse ndi nyama. Adzagwiritsa ntchito zizindikiro zake zabodza ndi zozizwitsa ndipo pamodzi ndi chilombo chochokera kunyanja adzabweretsa chiwonongeko chachikulu padziko lapansi, pakuti mwasankha chinjoka, ndipo tsopano mudzadya mokwanira zonyansa zotheratu zomwe chinjoka chimabweretsa.

Inu, inu okhala padziko lapansi, mwamalavulira chisomo ndi chifundo cha Yehova chomwe anachitumiza mwa ine, ngakhale pankhope pa Yesu Khristu, ndipo popeza mwakana Mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu, tsopano mudzakumana ndi Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda. Pakuti ndinabwera mumtendere ndi chikondi ndi chifuniro chabwino ndi chisomo ndi chifundo, ndipo mwakana mopusa chipulumutso m'malo mwa imfa zanu ndi chilango chamuyaya, pakuti palibe chisankho china. Kulavulira kwa Yehova pamene atambasula dzanja lake mumtendere ndi chikondi, ngakhale kupereka moyo wa mwana wake wobadwa yekha, ndi tchimo lamuyaya ndipo palibe kuthawa mkwiyo kupatula kuitana pa dzina lamphamvu la Yesu mpaka mapeto ndipo iye amene sakonda moyo wake mpaka mapeto adzapulumutsidwa, ndipo khamu la makamu lidzapulumutsidwa.

Koma iwe, iwe munthu wachibadwa, wasankha zosangalatsa za tchimo pamene chisomo chinaperekedwa kwa iwe ndipo unapatsidwa mpata woti ulape. Pakuti ndakufikirani, ndafikira atate anu, ndafikira atate a atate anu, ngakhale kubwerera m'mbuyo zaka za yubile zambiri tsopano kuyambira pamene ndinatumiza atumwi ndi aneneri anga koyamba ndipo chinsinsi chachikulu cha Mulungu chalengezedwa ngakhale kumalekezero a dziko lapansi! Ndipo tsopano kukana kwanu kwakwanira, ndipo ndikulandira chigamulo chanu chomaliza, ndipo ndine Khristu Yesu, ndipo ndine amene tsopano ndikulengeza kuti nthawi za Akunja zatha ndipo tsopano chiweruzo chikubwera kuyambira panyumba ya Mulungu. Popeza milomo yanu yakana Yehova, ngakhale Mbuye wa Mizimu, cholowa chanu choikidwa tsopano chili m'nyanja yaikulu yamoto, ya sulufule wogubuduzika, ndipo kukanakhala kwabwino kwa inu mukanapanda kubadwa. Mukuganiza mopusa kuti muli ndi nthawi yonse padziko lapansi ndipo kuti Yehova adzapitiriza kuyembekezera mpaka mwina tsiku lina mudzatembenukira ku chilungamo kuchoka ku machimo anu onyansa. Ndipo ndikukuuzani momveka bwino tsopano, nthawi yatha ndipo kwa ambiri aphonya kwamuyaya mwayi wawo wa moyo wosatha ndipo ngakhale mwayi wawo wokhala pakati pa Mkwatibwi wanga amene adzabwera kudzasiliridwa ndi onse pa tsiku limenelo.

Pakuti chotero ndi tsogolo la onse amene alandira chizindikiro. Ndipo choncho ndikulengeza kwa mitundu ya anthu kachiwiri:

NGATI MUNTHU ALIYENSE APEMPHEDZA CHILOMBO NDI FANO LAKE, NDI KULANDIRA CHIZINDIKIRO CHAKE PA MPHUMI YAKE, KAPENA PADZANJA LAKE, YEMWEYO ADZAMWA Vinyo WA MKWIYO WA MULUNGU, AMENE WATHIRIDWA WOSASAKANIZIDWA M'CHIKHO CHA MKWIYO WAKE, NDIPO ADZAZUNZIDWA NDI MOTO NDI SULUFULE, PAMASO PA ANGELO OYERA, NDI PAMASO PA MWANAWANKHOSA: NDIPO UTSI WA KUZUNZIKA KWAWO UKWERA KWAMUYAYA NDI MUYAYA. NDIPO ALIBE MPUMULO USANA NDI USIKU, AMENE APEMPHEDZA CHILOMBO NDI FANO LAKE, NDI ALIYENSE AMENE ALANDIRA CHIZINDIKIRO CHA DZINA LAKE.

Pakuti chizindikiro chikuyandikira ndithu. Chidzakhala khomo lolowera m'gulu lawo, ndipo adzakuuzani kuti ndi mankhwala. Adzakuuzani kuti chidzabweretsa moyo wosatha, adzakuuzani kuti simudzadwalanso, adzakuuzani mabodza ambiri kuti mulandire jekeseni wawo wa chilombo, ndipo adzakopa ambiri kuti ndi kwa ubwino wawo. Taonani! Ogwa akubwera akudziyesa alendo ochokera kumlengalenga ndi matekinoloje awo m'manja mwawo. Mvetsetsani kuti chizindikirocho chidzakupangani kukhala kapolo wa matekinoloje awo, pakuti adzisakaniza okha ndi mbewu ya anthu: koma sadzamatirirana wina ndi mzake, ngakhale monga chitsulo sichisakanizika ndi dongo. Chizindikiro chawo cha chilombo, ngakhale kulumwa kwa njoka, chidzawononga kotheratu onse amene alandira poizoni wawo ndipo palibe kuthawa.

Taonani! Ndipo ndinamva liwu lalikulu lochokera m'Kachisi, likunena kwa Angelo asanu ndi awiri, Pitani njira zanu, ndipo thirani mbale zisanu ndi ziwiri za mkwiyo wa Mulungu padziko lapansi. Ndipo woyamba anapita nathira mbale yake padziko lapansi: ndipo panagwa chilonda choipa, ndi chowawa pa anthu, amene anali ndi chizindikiro cha chilombo, ndi pa iwo amene anapembedza fano lake. Ndipo choncho tsogolo ili likuyembekezera onse amene amavomereza mofunitsitsa kuzunzika kwawo kwamuyaya ndi iwo amene anasankha imfa yachiwiri. Zonse chifukwa mwakana Yesu wa ku Nazarete, amene akanakupulumutsani mpaka kotheratu. Ndipo onse amene alandira chizindikiro adzakhala ndi zilonda zawo zoipa ndi zowawa ndipo onse amene alandira chizindikiro adzakhala kwamuyaya m'cholowa chawo m'malawi amoto a buluu mu mdima wandiweyani kwamuyaya, ndipo komabe tsogolo ili lasankhidwa mofunitsitsa ndi iwo omwe lakonzedwera ndipo choncho zidzakhala!

Ndipo choncho kulekanitsa komaliza tsopano kukuyamba ndipo monga kunali nkhondo kumwamba tsopano kudzakhala nkhondo padziko lapansi pakuti ndidzachotsa mtendere nthawi yomweyo ndipo nthawi yomweyo woletsa adzachotsedwa ndipo misala yadzidzidzi idzaphulika padziko lapansi pakuti zoipa sizidzaleletsedwanso, ndipo ndidzapatsa munthu aliyense monga mwa ntchito zawo. Ndipo choncho nthawi za Akunja zatsekedwa ndipo nkhondo yatsala pang'ono kuphulika padziko lapansi, ndipo adzakonzanso malire awo kukhala ufumu womaliza wa chilombo womwe chinjoka, chilombo chochokera kunyanja ndi chilombo chochokera pansi chomwe chilinso Moshiach wawo wabodza chidzauka ndipo vuto la Yakobo lidzaphulika padziko lapansi m'kamphindi, ndipo sadzathawa!

Taonani! Ndikutumiza uthenga wanga wabwino wosatha, pakuti kutumizidwa kwake kunalengezedwa kalekale ndi Yohane Wovumbulutsa pamene ndinamulembera kuti: Ndinaona Mngelo wina akuuluka pakati pa thambo, ali ndi Uthenga Wabwino wosatha, kuti alalikire kwa iwo amene akukhala padziko lapansi, ndi kwa mtundu uliwonse, ndi fuko, ndi lilime, ndi anthu. Taonani! Umboni wa Yesu ndi mzimu wa uneneri ndipo dziwani kuti mawu anga adzapeza maso, makutu, ndi mitima kwa onse amene atumizidwa ndipo Mawu a uthenga wabwino wosatha avumbulutsidwa mu nthawi yawo ndipo adzasungidwa kwa iwo amene atumizidwa, choncho tcherani khutu ku liwu la Moshiach wanu Woona! Pakuti ndine Yeshua HaMashiach!

Ndipo choncho Mawu a Yohane Wovumbulutsa aima ngati chitonthozo ndi chowunikira cha chiyembekezo kwa iwo amene adzapezabe chipulumutso:

Chivumbulutso 21:1-7

Ndipo ndinaona kumwamba kwatsopano, ndi dziko lapansi latsopano: pakuti kumwamba koyamba, ndi dziko lapansi loyamba zinapita, ndipo kunalibe nyanja. Ndipo ine Yohane ndinaona mzinda woyera Yerusalemu Watsopano ukutsika kuchokera kwa Mulungu kumwamba, wokonzedwa ngati mkwatibwi wokongoletsedwa kwa mwamuna wake. Ndipo ndinamva liwu lalikulu lochokera kumwamba, likuti, Taonani, Chihema cha Mulungu chili ndi anthu, ndipo adzakhala nawo: ndipo adzakhala anthu ake, ndipo Mulungu mwiniwake adzakhala Mulungu wawo pamodzi nawo. Ndipo Mulungu adzapukuta misozi yonse m'maso mwawo: ndipo sipadzakhalanso imfa, ngakhale chisoni, ngakhale kulira, ngakhale sipadzakhalanso ululu uliwonse: pakuti zinthu zoyamba zapita. Ndipo iye amene anakhala pampando wachifumu, anati, Taonani, ndipanga zinthu zonse zatsopano: ndipo anati kwa ine, Lemba: pakuti mawu awa ndi okhulupirika ndi oona. Ndipo anati kwa ine, Kwatha, Ndine Alefa ndi Omega, chiyambi ndi mapeto. Ndidzamupatsa iye amene ali ndi ludzu, kasupe wa madzi a moyo kwaulere. Iye amene apambana, adzalandira zinthu zonse, ndipo ndidzakhala Mulungu wake, ndipo iye adzakhala mwana wanga.

Ndine amene Yehova wamupatsa zinthu zonse kumwamba ndi padziko lapansi.

Ndine amene ndidzawalamulira ndi ndodo yachitsulo.

Ndine amene ndiponda mwa mkwiyo choponderamo mphesa cha ukali ndi mkwiyo wa El Shaddai.

Ndine amene ndili ndi dzina lolembedwa pa chovala changa, ndi pa ntchafu yanga, Mfumu Ya Mafumu, Ndi Mbuye Wa Ambuye, ndipo nthawi yanga YAKWANA.

Ndine nsanja yanu yolimba ndi thanthwe lachitetezo, pakuti ndine Thanthwe la Mibadwo, ngakhale Mbuye ndi mpulumutsi wanu.

Ndine woyenga amene amakhala ndi mvukuto ndi nyundo m'manja kusonkhezera moto wa chiyeretso kuchotsa zinyalala.

Ndine amene ndimasula ndi kupinda zitsulo zamtengo wapatali za nyumba yosungiramo chuma ya Yehova, yomwe chuma chake chambiri ndi chuma chamtengo wapatali chomwe ndi ana a Mulungu kukhala mu ungwiro waulemerero.

Ndine amene mwamukhulupirira.

Ndine wokhulupirika ndi woona, amene ndasunga chimene munachiika kwa ine mpaka tsiku lino.

Ine, Yeshua HaMashiach ndatumiza Mawu awa kudzera mwa mtumiki wanga, ndipo kudzera mwa mtumiki wanga Mawu awa atumizidwa. Tcherani khutu ku Mawu anga pakuti mwa iwo muli moyo wosatha.
