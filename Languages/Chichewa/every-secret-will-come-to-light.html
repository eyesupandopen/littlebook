Chinsinsi Chilichonse Chidzawululidwa!

Kodi simunawerenge? Chinsinsi chilichonse chidzawululidwa. Kodi simunawerenge? Chinthu chilichonse chobisika chidzaikidwa poyera. Kodi simunawerenge? Cholinga chilichonse cha mtima chidzabwera pamaso panga. Lilime lililonse lidzavomereza kwa ine, akutero Ambuye Wolungama ndi Mfumu Yolungama, amene amalamulira m'chilungamo. O, anthu anga, chifukwa chiyani mukusankha cholakwa cha Adamu ndi Hava, omwe kuyambira pachiyambi anayesa kubisa manyazi awo m'ntchito zawo, pakuti anasoka masamba a mkuyu pamodzi ndikudzipangira zovala. Komabe, Mulungu anaona zomwe zinachitika ndipo anadziwa kuti adzapunthwa asanawalenge. Ndipo Atate anawachitira chiyani? ANAWAPANGIRA malaya a zikopa ndikuwaveka padera pa ntchito zawo.

Chifukwa chiyani mukuganiza kuti mungathe kundibisira machimo anu, ana anga? Ndimakuonani nonse ndipo ndimakudziwani bwino kuposa momwe mumadzidziwira nokha. Lekani kumvera abusa osokera omwe amalalikira Yesu wina; ndithudi m'zitsime zosweka amapereka madzi ofunda kwa mpingo wofunda wokutidwa m'chinyengo ndi zomwe zimatchedwa kuganiza bwino. Amakunaminani ndi Chikhristu chodzithandiza, kukuuzani choonadi chopanda chifukwa monga Mulungu amakukondani momwe muliri. Ngakhale chikondi cha Atate nchosatha ndipo nchotentha kwambiri kwa ana ake, Chikhristu choona chimalalikira kulapa ndi kukhululukidwa kwa machimo, machimo omwe amavomerezedwa moona mtima ndi mtima woyera womwe umatsogolera ku chipulumutso ndi kukhala wathunthu. Komabe, ambiri mu Mpingo wanga amapereka zifukwa ndikudziyeretsa m'machimo awo. Amapereka zifukwa m'mafilosofi awo ndikufunafuna malo abodza, kuyang'ana pansi abale awo modzikuza pamene akukulunga zoyipa zawo m'kudziyeretsa kwawo kwabodza. Kodi uku sikudziwa kwa Satana pamene anapereka chipatso choletsedwa kwa mayi wa amoyo onse? Kodi izi sizintchito za thupi?

Ndikukufunsani funso; nonse mukudziwa yankho. Kodi malipiro a uchimo ndi chiyani? Kodi Malemba Oyera amati malipirowo ndi chiyani? Kodi si imfa? Komabe, ambiri a inu mukuona kuti machimo anu ndi oyenera ndipo ndi mbali yanu ndi zomwe zimakupangani kukhala apadera, pamene mukugwiritsa ntchito molakwika chisomo changa. Pakufa, mudzafa. Ngakhale muli ndi moyo wosatha mwa ine, ndipo ndidzakukhululukirani nonse ndipo sindidzakutayani ngati mukhala mwa ine, pakufa mudzafa.

Kuyambira mpweya wanu woyamba, mukuwonongeka chifukwa cha chikhalidwe cha uchimo mwa inu.

Mukapitiriza kuchimwa ndikufesa m'thupi, mudzakolola chivundi nthawi zonse, chifukwa pakufa mudzafa. Uchimo udzapha mbali iliyonse ya moyo wanu ngakhale thupi lanu mpaka mpweya wanu womaliza ngati mupitiriza. Osati zokhazo, koma mudzataya mphoto yanu ndikuyima mwamanyazi kumwamba pa mpando wanga woweruza pamene mtengo, udzu, ndi ziputu zomwe munadzikundikira zikutenthedwa kukhala palibe pamaso panu, ndipo mudzataya pamene ndidzakulondani mphoto yanu ndikupatsa wina, pakuti ndine woweruza wolungama. Chifukwa chiyani mukubwera kwa ine kudzilapa ndikubwereranso ku masanzi anu? Kodi mukukwaniritsa chiyani? Kodi simukumvetsa kuti simungathe kutumikira ine ndi Satana nthawi imodzi? Ngati muchimwa, ndinu kapolo wa uchimo mpaka mutayeretsedwa ndi ine, ndipo lilime lililonse lidzavomereza! Chifukwa chiyani mukulakalaka kubwerera ku zinthu zofooka ndi zaumphawi? Zomwe adzachite ndikufuna kukuphani pamene akukutengani ngati zofunkha.

Dziwani kuti mizimu yoyipa imadzitama pa ana anga omwe amatha kuwagwira. Chifukwa chiyani mukusankha kubweretsa manyazi pa dzina lanu ndikudzipanga kukhala choseketsa? Mwayiwala chikondi chanu choyamba, ndipo ambiri a inu mukuchita ufiti wa Chikhristu ndi Chikhristu chodzithandiza, mtundu wa chikhulupiriro wopanda ine, pakuti sindidzalowa mu mpingo wa Laodikaya koma ndimayima pakhomo ndikugogoda. Ndikukuuzani momveka bwino, kuganiza kwanu kwabwino sikulamulira chilichonse, ndipo mwasocheretsedwa.

Kodi zosangalatsa za uchimo za kanthawi kochepa ndi zofunika kwambiri kwa inu? Kodi simukumvetsa kuti ndidzakupatsani mphoto yochuluka ndi zinthu zakumwamba chifukwa cha mavuto ndi mayesero anu? Ndidzabwezera zomwe mwapereka nsembe chifukwa cha ine m'dzina langa. Bwererani ku chikondi chanu choyamba, ana anga, ndipo bwerani kwa ine ndikutuluka mu Babulo. Pakuti masiku a hule lalikulu awerengedwa, ndipo sipadzakhala mankhwala kwa iye; chotero, tulukani mwa iye, anthu anga, ndipo khalani opatulika kuti musagawane m'miliri yake. Chokani ku chilakolako ndi ntchito za thupi. Pakuti zonse zomwe zili m'dziko lapansi, chilakolako cha thupi, ndi chilakolako cha maso, ndi kunyadira kwa moyo, sizochokera kwa Atate, koma zochokera m'dziko lapansi ndipo dziko lapansi likupita.

Thawani dama ndipo musagawane m'manyazi ndi zotsatira zawo. Ana anga, zili ngati mukuchita mchitidwewo nokha pamene mukupeza chisangalalo m'kuyipa ndi kuwonongeka. Siyani njira zanu zaumbombo, pakuti ndidzalanga omwe amadzikundikira chuma, ndipo posachedwa ndalama zanu zidzakhala zopanda phindu. Kankhirani mu mzimu wanga, pakuti mzimu wanga ndi moyo ndi mtendere. Chokani ku zoyipa ndipo musachimwe! Kololani chipatso cha mzimu mwa ine, pakuti ndakupatsani mphamvu pa machimo anu ngati mudzayeretsedwa ndi ine. Kodi mukuganiza kuti mungathe kubisa chinachake pamaso panga? Ndapatsidwa mphamvu zowona, ndipo ine ndi Atate wanga ndife amodzi mu umodzi ndi cholinga. Ndi zosatheka kubisa, ndipo ndi zosatheka kundibisira machimo anu. Ambiri a inu simukudziwa momwe mumaonekera opusa pamene mukuchita modzilungamitsa ngati kuti mulibe uchimo, komabe maso anu akuyaka ndi chilakolako chomwe chimayatsa m'chiuno mwanu.

Mvetsetsani kuti mbala imagwiritsa ntchito zoyipa ndi machenjera ambiri motsutsana nanu. Ngati mutsatira chifuniro changa kukana ndikutembenukira kwa ine, ndine thandizo lopezeka nthawi zonse pa nthawi ya kusowa ndipo ndidzakuthandizani, ndidzakutetezani, ndidzakuyeretsani ndikupanga njira yothawira mayesero ambiri omwe mukukumana nawo, koma muyenera kukana ndi zonse zomwe zili mwa inu. Sizikutanthauza chilichonse pamene munena kuti mukupepesa ndikubwereranso ku masanzi anu, koma mungachite mantha kuona galu akuchita chimodzimodzi, komabe m'kudzikuza kwanu mukuganiza kuti ndinu osiyana, choncho musachite ngati galu wamtchire.

Uchimo ndi uchimo. Njira yokhayo yogonjetsera uchimo ndi kudzera m'magazi anga owombola ndikupumula mwa ine. Mzimu womwe ndakuthirirani udzakupatsani mphamvu. Ndalenga chikhalidwe chatsopano mwa inu, ndipo ngati mungopempha, ndidzalenga mtima woyera mwa inu.

Choncho tsekani pakamwa panu ponyansa, tsekani maso anu a chilakolako m'pemphero, ndipo chokani ku zoyipa, ndipo ndidzakuyeretsani. Inde, ndidzakuyeretsani kwathunthu kukhala oyera ngati matalala atsopano pamene muvomereza komanso kusintha ndikutembenuka kuchoka m'njira zanu zonyansa. Onse omwe ndimawakonda, ndimawadzudzula; ndipo onse omwe ndimawakonda, ndimawalanga. Ndi atate uti amene salanga mwana wake? Ngakhale ambiri amachita zimenezo, izi si njira zanga, ndipo izi si njira za Atate wanga. Popeza Mulungu amachita nanu ngati ana, adzakulangani ngati ana; pakuti ngati sakulangani, ndiye kuti sindinu ana ake koma ana a padale.

Koma ndinu ana ake, choncho tcherani khutu ku mawu anga ndikupirira chilango, ndipo mverani machenjezo, pakuti moyo ndi chimwemwe zikudikira. Palibe chilango chomwe chikuwoneka chosangalatsa pa nthawiyo, koma chidzatsogolera ku zipatso zamtendere za chilungamo. Bwerani kwa ine, ndipo ndidzakuyeretsani ku njira zanu zakale, ndipo ndidzakuphunzitsani njira zanga kuti muyende m'chilungamo ndi mtendere. Thandizani abale anu ndikukhala chitsanzo cha kulapa, pakuti m'kuyima kwanu mudzadula maunyolo ambiri ndi zomangira zambiri, ngakhale zomangira zomwe mwagwira m'manja mwanu ndikuzigwiritsitsa kwa inu nokha. Ndikukulangizani kuti muzisiye zonse. Uchimo umatsogolera ku ukapolo, ndipo umalipira imfa; chotero, tulukani mwa iye, kuti musagawane m'maweruzo ake.

Ndine woyera, choncho khalani oyera.

Ndine amene ndimalungamitsa ana anga.

Ndine amene ndimayeretsa ana anga.

Ndine amene ndimapanga ana anga kukhala olungama mwa ine.

Ndine amene ndimawombola ana anga m'chisomo ndi chifundo changa.

Ndine amene ndimakutsukani kukhala oyera m'magazi anga opanda uchimo.

Ndine woweruza wolungama.

Nthawi ndi yochepa, ana anga, ndipo maphunziro anu posachedwa adzatha. Musaganize kuti chosalungama chidzalowa mu ufumu wanga, kapena kuti mudzayima pamaso panga m'machimo anu ndikutenga mphoto. Okhawo omwe apambana adzayima ndi ine. Mudzapulumutsidwa, koma chifukwa chiyani mukufuna kuvutika ndi kutaya ukulu womwe ndakukonzerani, pakuti simungathe ngakhale kumvetsa zomwe mukupereka nsembe chifukwa cha zilakolako zanu.

Bwerani kwa ine, pakuti ndine wolemera m'chifundo.

Bwerani kwa ine, pakuti chisomo changa chidzakuchirikizani.

Bwerani kwa ine, pakuti ndine amene ndimayenda pakati pa zoyikapo nyale zisanu ndi ziwiri ndipo amene adzasunga mphoto yanu ngati mungobwera kwa ine modzichepetsa, kusiya kunyadira kwanu kopanda phindu. Ndimakukondani, ana anga, ndipo posachedwa mudzakhala ndi ine. Taonani! Ndimayima pakhomo ndikugogoda kwa nthawi yochepa chabe. Ndikukuuzani zinthu izi osati kuti ndikutsutseni, koma kuti mukhale ndi mafuta m'nyale zanu ndikukhala okonzeka kwa ine pamene ndikubwera. Ndikubwera pa nthawi yomwe simukuyembekezera, ndipo ndikubwera posachedwa kuposa momwe mungaganizire. Nthawi yatsala pang'ono kutha.
