Meyi 3, 2024

Ndakupatsani Chiyembekezo Chachikulu!

Bwerani kwa ine ndipo mumve mawu anga, Mkwatibwi wanga, pakuti ndili pano kudzatonthoza miyoyo yanu, kulimbikitsa mitima yanu, ndi kukupangani kukhala athunthu mwa ine, Khristu Yesu. Musafooke ndi mawu anga a chidzudzulo, pakuti amene ndimkonda, ndimamulanga, kuti kuitanidwa kwanu ndi kusankhidwa kwanu kukhale kotsimikizika mwa ine, Mkwati, ngakhale Khristu Yesu. Ana anga, tcherani makutu anu ku mawu anga ndipo mumve zolankhula zanga, pakuti sindinakuitanireni ku chiweruzo ndi kutsutsidwa, koma ndakuyesani olungama mwa ine, ngakhale Ambuye wanu Yesu Khristu. Kodi simunamve zomwe Paulo anauza Akorinto m'kalata yake yoyamba? Ndipo tsopano zitsalira chikhulupiriro, chiyembekezo ndi chikondi, ngakhale izi zitatu: koma chachikulu pa izi ndi chikondi. Ndipo choncho chikondi ndicho chachikulu pa izi zitatu, pakuti chikondi cha Yahweh sichingagonjetsedwe. Chikondi cha Yahweh ndi guluu yomwe imagwirizanitsa. Ndi cholimbikitsa chachikulu, ndipo kupereka mwaulere monga mwalandira mwaulere ndiko kukhala wotsanzira Mulungu!

Ambiri a inu mwataya chiyembekezo, ngakhale chiyembekezo chanu mwa ine, Yesu Khristu, pakuti ndakupatsani chiyembekezo chachikulu, ana anga. Mvetsetsani kuti mbala ingakusokonezeni ndi kukusocheretsani, koma Mulungu si woyambitsa chisokonezo, koma mtendere monga m'mipingo yonse ya oyera mtima. Satana wayesetsa kubisa malangizo anga omwe ndinapereka m'chivumbulutso changa cha Chinsinsi Chachikulu cha Mulungu kwa Mtumwi Paulo zaka za Yubile zambiri zapitazo tsopano. Ana anga, chiyembekezo chanu ndi lingaliro lenileni lomwe limakupangitsani kukhala olimbikitsidwa kupirira mayesero ndi masautso ambiri omwe muli nawo m'moyo uno. Pakuti popanda chiyembekezo chanu, mukanakhala monga Paulo analengeza kwa Akorinto: Ngati m'moyo uno wokha tili ndi chiyembekezo mwa Khristu, ndife omvetsa chisoni kwambiri pa anthu onse. Koma tsopano Khristu wauka kwa akufa, ndipo wakhala chipatso choyamba cha iwo omwe anagona. Ana anga, ndakupatsani chiyembekezo chachikulu pa chiyembekezo chanu, pakuti ndanena: inu omwe muli amoyo ndi otsala mudzatengedwa m'mitambo, monga akufa mwa Khristu adzauka poyamba, ndipo nonse mudzasinthidwa, pakuti mudzakhala ngati ine mpaka kalekale ndipo Mkwatibwi wanga adzakhala ndi ine mpaka kalekale.

Pakuti chiyembekezo chanu mwa Khristu ndi chiyani, ana anga? Kodi si lonjezo la chiwombolo chathunthu ndi kubwezeretsedwa, komwe mudzaona kumwamba ndi ukulu ndi ulemerero wa Yahweh ndi maso anu? Kodi chiyembekezo chanu si tsogolo lomwe mudzanditumikira mu ufumu wanga wa chilungamo, pakuti monga mwakhala okhulupirika pa zinthu zochepa, choncho ndidzakupangani kukhala olamulira pa zinthu zambiri. Kodi chiyembekezo chanu si lonjezo la moyo wosatha komwe mudzadya za mtengo wa moyo ndi kuyenda m'misewu ya Yerusalemu Watsopano, komwe kuwala kwa Yahweh ndi kuwala kwa Yeshua HaMashiach kudzawala mpaka kalekale mu kuwala kwaulemerero? Kodi chiyembekezo chanu si ulemerero wa akorona ambiri ndi mphotho chifukwa cha kukhulupirika kwanu ndi chikondi chanu kwa ine, Khristu Yesu? Kodi chiyembekezo chanu si chiyembekezo cha thanzi langwiro, palibenso matenda kapena nthenda? Kodi chiyembekezo chanu si chakuti ndidzagonjetsa adani anu pansi pa mapazi anu ndi kupangitsa ngakhale sunagoge wa Satana kugwada pa mapazi anu? (Pakuti adzadziwa kuti ndakukondani!) Kodi chiyembekezo chanu si cha chimwemwe chosatha pamaso pa Yahweh mwiniwake? Pakuti adzakhazikitsa malo ake okhala pakati pa anthu, ndipo sipadzakhalanso ufumu wogawanika, koma Ufumu wa Mulungu wokha, pakuti palibe wamkulu kuposa Yahweh; ayi, palibe amene angafanane ndi ulemerero wa Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya, ngakhale Atate wathu!

Kondwerani m'tsogolo mwanu, ana anga, pakuti ndakupatsani tsogolo ndi cholinga choyera mwa ine, Ambuye wanu wowala! Kodi chiyembekezo chanu si chiyembekezo chachikulu komwe mwapulumutsidwa ku mkwiyo mwa ine? Pakuti chanu ndi chiyembekezo chachikulu kwambiri, chiyembekezo chomwe chinalankhulidwa ndi chiwiya changa chosankhidwa Mtumwi Paulo, woyang'anira wamkulu wa Chinsinsi cha Mulungu? Pakuti ndinavumbulutsa kwa Paulo zomwe analembera Akolose: Ngakhale chinsinsi chomwe chinabisika kuyambira nthawi zakale, ndi mibadwo, koma tsopano chaonetsedwa kwa oyera mtima ake, Kwa iwo omwe Mulungu anafuna kuwadziwitsa chuma cha ulemerero wa chinsinsi ichi pakati pa Amitundu, chomwe ndi Khristu mwa inu, chiyembekezo cha ulemerero. Chanu ndi chiyembekezo chaulemerero choyenera mfumu, pakuti ndidzakupangani mafumu ndi ansembe pamaso pa Mulungu wathu; pakuti ndine wansembe wamkulu mpaka kalekale monga mwa dongosolo la Melikizedeki, ndipo sipadzakhala wina.

Ana anga ang'ono, musawope mabodza a mdani, pakuti akufuna kukutopetsani pachabe ngati akanatha; koma wakumana ndi wofanana naye mwa ine ndipo ndamugonjetsa kotheratu, ndipo ndikubwera kudzawononga ufumu wake, pakuti ndinatumizidwa kudzapasula ntchito za Mdyerekezi, ndipo choncho ndakutumizani! Njoka yakale ndi ufumu wake wonse alibe chiyembekezo. Akudziwa kuti aweruzidwa ku chiwonongeko chosatha ndi chilango, komwe adzayaka m'malawi otentha kwambiri a buluu pamene akusambira m'sulufule woyaka mpaka kalekale. Amakuchitirani nsanje ndipo angapereke chilichonse kuti akhale inu, choncho musamvere mawu awo. Ndimvereni, Mkwatibwi wanga; Satana adzachita chilichonse kuti akuchotseni ku mphotho yanu mwa ine, Khristu Yesu. Adzachita chilichonse kubisa chiyembekezo chanu chomwe ndakupatsani, ndipo njira zake m'gulu ili nthawi zambiri ndikuchepetsa lingaliro kudzera m'kuyerekezera choonadi ndi kudzera m'lilime lake labodza.

Musamuwope, koma chokani ku mabodza ake ndipo sungani chiyembekezo chanu chenicheni m'maganizo, pakuti kodi simunawerenge? Mzimu weniweniwo umachitira umboni, pamodzi ndi mzimu wathu, kuti ndife ana a Mulungu. Ndipo ngati ana, ndiye olowa m'malo, olowa m'malo a Mulungu, ndi olowa m'malo anzake a Khristu: ngati tivutika naye, kuti ifenso tikalemekezedwe pamodzi. Pakuti ndimawerengera, kuti masautso a nthawi yamakono, sayenera kufananizidwa ndi ulemerero womwe udzavumbulutsidwa mwa ife. Ana anga, ngati mukanamvetsetsa ukulu wa zomwe zikutanthauza kukhala wolowa m'malo mnzake wa Khristu, koma posachedwa mudzadziwa monga momwe mukudziwidwira. Kodi simukumvetsetsa kuti Mulungu wandipatsa zinthu zonse kumwamba ndi dziko lapansi, ndipo cholowa changa ndi cholowa chachikulu kwambiri? Ana anga, simukudziwa umunthu wanu weniweni, pakuti umunthu wanu weniweni wabisika mwa ine, ndipo pamene ndidzaonekera, inunso mudzaonekera ndi ine mu ulemerero!

Musawope, ndipo khalani okhazikika m'chikondi chanu ndi chikhulupiriro, pakuti chiyembekezo chanu chidzakupangitsani kuyenda m'kupirira kwakukulu; pakuti kodi chiyembekezo changa ndi chimwemwe chomwe Atate anachiika pamaso panga chinandipangitsa kukwaniritsa chiyani? Pakuti Ahebri amati: Kuyang'ana kwa Yesu Woyambitsa ndi womalizitsa wa chikhulupiriro chathu, amene chifukwa cha chimwemwe chomwe chinaikidwa pamaso pake, anapirira mtanda, nanyoza manyazi, ndipo wakhala pansi kudzanja lamanja la mpando wachifumu wa Mulungu. Kodi sindinakusiyireni chitsanzo, ndipo kodi sindinakhale mtundu womwe muyenera kutsatira? Kodi simukumvetsetsa? Kuti iye amene akhulupirira mwa ine sadzachita ntchito zomwe ndinakwaniritsa pa kubwera kwanga koyamba zokha, koma ntchito zazikulu kuposa zomwe ndinachita? Chifukwa ndapita kwa Atate ndipo ndakupatsani mphamvu ndipo ndikuyimirirani ndi mphamvu zanga zonse, pakuti ndine wamphamvu, pakuti Mulungu wandipanga choncho m'kumvera kwanga.

Anthu anga, mawu awa akuyenera kukulimbikitsani ndi kukupatsani mphamvu zoyimirira, pakuti ndaika chimwemwe chachikulu pamaso panu! Musakhulupirire mabodza a mdani okhudza chiyembekezo chanu, pakuti angayese kukusiyani opanda chiyembekezo ndi oponderezedwa pansi pa mapazi, ndipo ngakhale m'zofooka zanu mwapangidwa kukhala amphamvu mwa ine, Ambuye wanu Yesu Khristu! Tsopano ndidzafotokoza mwachidule tsogolo lanu, Mkwatibwi wanga, choncho tcherani makutu anu ku mawu a pakamwa panga, pakuti ndi mawu a chikhulupiriro, ndi mawu a chikondi, ndipo mawu anga adzayatsanso chiyembekezo chanu m'choonadi, pakuti chiyembekezo chachikulu chili m'choonadi chomwe ndakupatsani!

Choyamba, mvetsetsani kuti ndakuitanani; ndakupulumutsani m'mzimu wanga wa mphamvu ndi umwana; ndakupangani kukhala cholengedwa chatsopano mwa ine, Khristu Yesu! Chifukwa cha nsembe yanga monga Paskha wangwiro, onse omwe amakhulupirira mwa ine m'chikhulupiriro chopulumutsa chochokera m'mitima yawo adzapulumutsidwanso ku mkwiyo mwa ine. Ana anga, mvetsetsani kuti mawu awa ndi odabwitsa kwambiri kwa anthu ambiri, pakuti machimo awo amawamenya pansi kukhala m'kudziimba mlandu, kutsutsidwa, ndi manyazi, ndipo izi zimachitika chifukwa nthawi zambiri amakana kudziyeretsa m'magazi anga otetezera, ndipo amanyalanyaza kuvomereza machimo awo, ndipo amanyalanyaza ubale wawo ndi ine, kalonga wamtendere, pakuti chipulumutso chimabwera kuchokera ku chikhulupiriro cha ubale mwa ine, Ambuye wamkulu!

Ambiri a inu samamvetsetsa kuti ndakuyesani olungama, ndipo chifukwa cha kulungamitsidwa kumeneko mwa ine, mwapulumutsidwa ku mkwiyo, pakuti ndinauza Aroma: Makamaka tsopano, popeza tayesedwa olungama ndi magazi ake, tidzapulumutsidwa ku mkwiyo kudzera mwa iye. Ndiyankheni izi, Mkwatibwi wanga yemwe ndikubwera kudzamtenga, chifukwa chiyani mukulakalaka tsiku la Ambuye? Chifukwa chiyani mwamvera atumiki a Satana omwe amadziveka ngati atumiki anga pamene akunena kuti muyenera kupirira masautso a Yakobo ndi ngakhale chisautso chachikulu chomwe chimachitika mu theka lomaliza la sabata la makumi asanu ndi awiri la Danieli? Amakunamiirani ndipo inu mumawakhulupirira. Amakopa chikhalidwe chanu cha uchimo, kukuuzani kuti ndinu opanda phindu pamene ndinu ngale yamtengo wapatali; amakuuzani kuti ndinu oyenera mkwiyo waukulu mwa ine, ndipo amanama! Amakuuzani kuti simuyenera kuyembekezera kupulumutsidwa ku chisautso, ndipo amagwira mawu molakwika Mawu anga oyera. Pakuti mwa choonadi ndinauza Paulo zotsatirazi kuti alangize Timoteo: Mazunzo, masautso omwe anandigwera ku Antiokeya, ku Ikoniyo, ku Lisitra, mazunzo omwe ndinapirira: koma mwa onsewo Ambuye anandilanditsa. Inde, ndipo onse omwe adzakhala moyo wopembedza mwa Khristu Yesu, adzazunzidwa. Ndipo ndithudi, Satana adzazunza onse omwe amakhala moyo wopembedza, ndipo mwa mazunzo ake onse ndidzakulanditsani pamene mutembenukira kwa ine m'mavuto anu ndi kukhala okhulupirika m'chiyembekezo chanu. Mvetsetsani kusiyana kwake, pakuti Satana wasokoneza nkhaniyi: Satana adzazunza onse omwe amakhala moyo wopembedza, ndipo kumeneko ndi kuzunzidwa; ndipo nthawi zambiri muyenera kupirira mkwiyo wake, pakuti m'kugonjetsedwa kwake ali ndi mkwiyo waukulu. Koma musawope kubangula kwake, pakuti ndinalangiza Petro, kuti: Khalani odziletsa, ndipo dikirani: pakuti mdani wanu mdyerekezi ngati mkango wobangula amayendayenda, kufunafuna wina kuti amumeze: Yemwe mumkanize okhazikika m'chikhulupiriro, podziwa kuti masautso omwewo akukwaniritsidwa mwa abale anu omwe ali m'dziko lapansi.

Mvetsetsani, ana anga, kuti mkwiyo womwe ukubwera si mkwiyo wa Satana koma mkwiyo wa Mulungu ndi mkwiyo wa Mwanawankhosa womwe wapangidwira Satana. Pakuti Satana ndi wamantha yemwe anakwawa m'chilengedwe chonse kuyesa kudzibisa ku mkwiyo womwe wausunga, poganiza kuti angagonjetse Yahweh m'zinthu zotere; koma zolinga za Atate zanenedwa motere: Mbuye, kodi simunafese mbewu zabwino m'munda mwanu? Nanga namsongole wachokera kuti? Iye anati kwa iwo, Mdani wachita izi. Atumiki anati kwa iye, Kodi mukufuna kuti tipite tikawasonkhanitse? Koma iye anati, Ayi: kuopa kuti pamene mukusonkhanitsa namsongole, mungazule ndi tirigu yemwe. Siyani zonse zikule pamodzi mpaka nthawi yokolola: ndipo mu nthawi yokolola, ndidzauza okolola, Sonkhanitsani poyamba namsongole, ndikumumanga m'mitolo kuti amutenthe: koma sonkhanitsani tirigu m'nkhokwe yanga. Choncho tirigu adzasonkhanitsidwa m'nkhokwe, koma namsongole ndi onse omwe anawabzala ndi kuwathirira adzaponyedwa amoyo m'nyanja yamoto ndi sulufule yomwe Atate wanga wakonzera Mdyerekezi ndi angelo ake, koma palibe gawo la mkwiyo wa Mulungu lomwe lili lanu, ngakhale Satana adzayesa kudzitchinjiriza kuseri kwanu ndipo adzafuna kukutengani ukapolo ngati zofunkha. Musawope, pakuti mkwiyo si wanu. Ndimamvetsetsa kuti miyoyo yambiri yosakhazikika imapotoza fanizo ili, kukuuzani kuti mudzapirira mkwiyo pamodzi nawo, koma amachita izi ponyalanyaza dongosolo lomveka bwino la malemba pa nkhaniyi, ndipo amayesetsa kukuchititsani khungu pogwira mawu molakwika fanizo langa.

Mvetsetsani kuti ndidzakutengani kupita kumwamba, pakuti ndakonzera malo achitetezo ndi mphotho kwa Mkwatibwi wanga; pakuti m'nyumba ya Atate wanga ndidzakubisani ndipo muli malo okhala ambiri. Choncho inde, ndidzakusonkhanitsani pamodzi kwa ine pa kuonekera kwanga, komwe kwatsala pang'ono kuchitika. Pakuti ndinanena kwa Atesalonika: Pakuti Ambuye mwiniwake adzatsika kuchokera kumwamba ndi mfuu, ndi mawu a Mngelo Wamkulu, ndi lipenga la Mulungu: ndipo akufa mwa Khristu adzauka poyamba. Kenako ife omwe tili amoyo, ndi otsala, tidzatengedwa pamodzi nawo m'mitambo, kukakumana ndi Ambuye mumlengalenga: ndipo choncho tidzakhala ndi Ambuye nthawi zonse. Chifukwa chake, tonthozanani wina ndi mnzake ndi mawu awa. Ndikukufunsani, Mkwatibwi wanga wonamizidwa, kodi tsopano mudzakana mabodza a Satana ndikugwiritsitsa mawu anga ndikutonthozedwa nawo ndi kutonthoza abale anu? Pakuti posachedwa zidzakhala choncho, ndipo ndikulakalaka kupezeka kwanu, pakuti ndimakukondani kwambiri, pakuti ndine Mkwati wanu.

Mukadzachotsedwa pa dziko lapansi, masautso a Yakobo adzagwa pa dziko lapansi ndipo chiwonongeko chachikulu chidzafika mwadzidzidzi, koma si chanu, popeza mudzatengedwa kupita kumwamba. Ndipo kenako mudzaima pamaso pa mpando wanga woweruzira kuti ndikupatseni mphotho monga mwa ntchito zanu. Ndipo kwa okhulupirika kuli mphotho yaikulu ndi akorona ambiri, ndipo mudzavekedwa m'nyumba yomwe ndakupangirani, ngakhale nyumba yomwe idzakonzedwa makamaka kwa inu ndipo idzakhala ndi zopambana zanu zambiri, zomwe ndidzazipatsa maluso ambiri, pakuti thupi latsopano lomwe ndikukudikirirani lili choncho. Palibe matenda, palibe ululu, palibe chisoni, ndipo palibenso temberero, ana anga, pakuti temberero lidzatembenukira pa mitu ya otembereredwa. Ndipo choncho mudzakhala kumwamba kwa nthawi ya zaka zisanu ndi ziwiri imeneyo, ngakhale sabata la makumi asanu ndi awiri la Danieli. Ndipo kenako, mudzakwera nane monga momwe gulu lonse la kumwamba lidzachitira, pakuti kwanenedweratu ndipo zidzakhala choncho: Ndipo ndinaona kumwamba kutatseguka, ndipo taonani kavalo woyera, ndipo iye amene anakwera pa iye anatchedwa wokhulupirika ndi woona, ndipo m'chilungamo amaweruza ndi kuchita nkhondo. Maso ake anali ngati lawi lamoto, ndipo pamutu pake panali akorona ambiri, ndipo anali ndi dzina lolembedwa, lomwe palibe munthu analidziwa koma iye yekha. Ndipo anavala chovala choviikidwa m'magazi, ndipo dzina lake limatchedwa, Mawu a Mulungu. Ndipo magulu ankhondo omwe anali kumwamba anamutsatira pa akavalo oyera, ovala bafuta wabwino, woyera ndi woyera.

Ana anga, tsogolo lanu ndi la ulemerero wosayerekezeka, ndipo simudzasowanso, pakuti mwadzitsimikizira kwa ine, ndipo simukufuna kuyesedwa kwina; nanga chifukwa chiyani mungafunike kupirira masautso a Yakobo? Pakuti cholinga chake ndikupulumutsa Yakobo, pakuti otsala adzapulumutsidwa. Koma dziwani zotsimikizika, iwo omwe amakana kuonekera kwanga m'kuyesetsa kwawo kopanda phindu kuwononga chiyembekezo chanu chenicheni, ngakhale abusa osokera omwewo, adzapirira masautso a Yakobo, ndipo adzapulumutsidwa ngati aitanira pa dzina langa ndipo ali okhulupirika mpaka mapeto. Koma sadzakhala ndi malo pa tebulo langa, pakuti kukana kuonekera kwanga ndikukana ine, Mkwati, pamene ndikubwera kudzatennga Mkwatibwi wanga, ndipo ndatsala pang'ono kuchita zimenezo.

Yang'anani kuwala kwa kuonekera kwanga! Mverani, pakuti mudzamva mawu anga ndi kulira kwamphamvu kwa lipenga lomaliza; pakuti ngakhale ndine Mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu, ndikubwera ngati Mkango wa Yuda, ndipo ufumu wonse wa Satana wagwidwa ndi mantha a ine, Khristu Yesu, pakuti nthawi yawo yakwana, ndipo malawi amoto akolezedwa otentha kwa iwo. Koma sizidzafika pafupi ndi inu, pakuti ndakukonzerani mphotho yaikulu ndi cholowa chachikulu, Mkwatibwi wanga. Werengani mawu anga m'buku lalikulu la Chivumbulutso, pakuti ndidzakusonyezani pang'ono za tsogolo lanu: Ndipo mozungulira Mpando Wachifumu munali mipando makumi awiri mphambu inayi, ndipo pa mipandoyo ndinaona Akulu makumi awiri mphambu anayi atakhala, ovala zovala zoyera, ndipo anali ndi akorona agolide m'mitu mwawo. (ndiponso) Ndipo pamene zamoyozo zipereka ulemerero, ndi ulemu, ndi chiyamiko kwa iye amene anakhala pa Mpando Wachifumu, amene ali ndi moyo mpaka kalekale, Akulu makumi awiri mphambu anayi amagwa pansi pamaso pa iye amene anakhala pa Mpando Wachifumu, ndi kumulambira iye amene ali ndi moyo mpaka kalekale, ndi kuponya akorona awo pamaso pa Mpando Wachifumu, nati, Ndinu woyenera, O Ambuye, kulandira ulemerero, ndi ulemu, ndi mphamvu: pakuti munalenga zinthu zonse, ndipo chifukwa cha kukondwera kwanu zilipo, ndipo zinalengedwa.

Ana anga, musawope tsogolo lanu, koma yang'anani kwa ine m'chiyembekezo chachikulu, pakuti posachedwa mudzakhala kumwamba mukuponya akorona anu pamaso pa mpando wachifumu wa Mulungu wamkulu!

Ndine woweruza wolungama ndipo posachedwa mudzalandira mphotho yaikulu pa Bema yanga.

Ndine Mkwati kwa mkwatibwi wanga.

Ndikubwera kudzatenga Mkwatibwi wanga!

Ndine Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda ndipo dziko lapansi latsala pang'ono kumvetsetsa.

Ndine amene wapatsidwa ulamuliro kumwamba ndi dziko lapansi, pakuti dzina langa ndi lalikulu kuposa mayina onse kupatula Yahweh.

Ndine Mwana wa Mulungu wobwera m'thupi chifukwa cha machimo a anthu, ndipo ndinaukitsidwa tsiku lachitatu ndi kulandiridwa kumwamba.

Ndine amene ndidzalamulira m'chilungamo pa dziko lapansi, ndipo Mkwatibwi wanga adzalamulira nane.

Ndine amene ndakuitanani ku ulemerero ndi ukoma, choncho musafooke!

Ndine.
