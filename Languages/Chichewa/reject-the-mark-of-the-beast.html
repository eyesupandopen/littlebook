Julayi 27, 2024

Kanani Chizindikiro cha Chilombo!

Imvani mawu anzeru ndipo gwedamani ndikumva mawu amoyo a Yeshua HaMashiach, pakuti Ine ndine Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda, ndipo ndine Mfumu ya Mafumu ndi Mbuye wa Ambuye! El Shaddai, ngakhale Mulungu Wamphamvuyonse, wandikweza kwambiri ndikundipatsa dzina loposa maina onse. Kuti m'Dzina la Yesu bondo lililonse lipinde, la zinthu zakumwamba, ndi zinthu zapadziko lapansi, ndi zinthu zapansi padziko: Ndipo kuti lilime lililonse livomereze, kuti Yesu Khristu ndiye Mbuye, ku ulemerero wa Atate wa Kuwala, ngakhale Yahweh, Mulungu wosatha ndi wamuyaya, pakuti palibe wina wofanana ndi Atate wathu!

Imvani mawu a Mbuye wanu ndikumvetsetsa zinthu zomwe zikuchitika kale ndi zinthu zomwe zatsala pang'ono kuchitika, pakuti taonani ndine amene ndimalankhula za zinthu zomwe zikubwera ngati kuti zachitika kale pakuti ndithudi Mbuye wanu wamkulu, ngakhale ine Khristu Yesu wopambana sindidzachita kanthu, pokhapokha ndivumbulutse zinsinsi zanga kwa atumiki anga Aneneri. Chifukwa chiyani mukumvera mawu ambiri otsutsana, ndipo chifukwa chiyani mukukayika pakati pa malingaliro awiri? Kodi sindinanene kuti aneneri anga amalankhula mogwirizana ndipo sadzatsutsana ndi Mawu anga oyera? Pakuti Ine ndine Mawu amene anasanduka thupi amene amakhala pakati panu mwa mzimu wanga woyera amene wabadwa mwa inu mpaka muyaya, pakuti mzimu uliwonse umene wabadwanso umasindikizidwa ndi mzimu woyera wa lonjezo umene uli mbewu yanga yosawonongeka ndipo chidindo cha Mulungu Wamoyo sichingasweke. Ndipo tsiku la chiwombolo cha chuma chogulidwa lili pafupi ndipo chiwombolo chanu chokwanira chafika.

Atero Mbuye wa Makamu, musamvere mawu a aneneri amene amanenera kwa inu, ndikukuphunzitsani zachabechabe: amalankhula masomphenya a mitima yawo, osati otuluka mkamwa mwa Mbuye. Bwerani kwa ine ana anga chifukwa kudzoza komwe mwalandira kwa ine, kumakhala mwa inu: ndipo simukusowa kuti munthu aliyense akuphunzitseni: Koma, monga kudzoza komweko kukuphunzitsani zinthu zonse, ndipo kuli koona, ndipo sikuli bodza: ndipo monga momwe kwakuphunzitsirani, mudzakhala mwa ine. Ana anga khalani mwa ine ndikuyerekezera mawu a aliyense amene amati akulankhula m'malo mwanga ndi malemba ndikundipempha kuti nditsimikizire mumzimu mwanu, ngati mwina mudzamvera mawu anga pakuti ambiri a inu ndinu ogontha, ndipo ambiri a inu mumakonda mawu abodza omwe amakondweretsa makutu anu kuti muwoneke anzeru koma m'malo mwake mumatsatira mawu abodza a zitsiru zodzikuza.

Taonani! Ndikulankhula za nthawi ya masautso a Yakobo yomwe yatsala pang'ono kugwa padziko lonse lapansi. Pakuti chiweruzo chayamba kale ndipo sichingayimitsidwe, pakuti chimene Yahweh walengeza palibe amene angachisinthe! Misozi yambiri, yambiri ndalira chifukwa cha chiweruzo cha Babulo Wachinsinsi ndipo ndatumiza aneneri anga kwa zaka zambiri, zambiri tsopano ndipo ndatumizanso alendo ochokera kumayiko achilendo kudzachitira umboni ndi kunenera za ziweruzo zomwe zinkayandikira koma tsopano zafika.

Pakuti zidzakhala choncho pakuti kwalembedwa kuti: Nyanga khumi zomwe unaziona pa chilombo, izi zidzadana ndi hule, ndipo zidzamulanda zonse, ndi kumusiya wamaliseche, ndipo zidzadya mnofu wake, ndi kumutentha ndi moto. Pakuti Mulungu waika m'mitima yawo kuti akwaniritse chifuniro chake, ndi kuvomerezana, ndi kupereka ufumu wawo kwa chilombo, kufikira mawu a Mulungu adzakwaniritsidwa. Ndipo mkazi, amene unamuona, ndiye mzinda waukulu umene umalamulira mafumu a dziko lapansi.

Imvani mawu anga, chiweruzo cha Babulo Wachinsinsi chayamba kale. Nyanga khumi zakonza kuukira kwawo kwadzidzidzi ndipo kuukira kwawo kukuyandikira ndipo kuli pakhomo panu. Pakuti ngakhale ofalitsa nkhani anu akufuna kukugonekani ndipo ambiri amakhala mumdima osawona zenizeni nati: Ndikhala ngati Mfumukazi ndipo sindine wamasiye ndipo sindidzawona kulira. Mawa kudzakhala ngati lero, pakuti lero kuli ngati dzulo. Monga momwe amadzilemekezera yekha, ndikukhala m'chisangalalo, momwemonso mumpatse mazunzo ndi chisoni: Chifukwa chake miliri yake idzabwera tsiku limodzi, imfa, ndi chisoni, ndi njala, ndipo adzatenthedwa ndi moto: pakuti Yahweh amene amamuweruza, ndiye Mulungu Wamphamvuyonse ndipo palibe wamkulu ndipo palibe chomwe chingaletse dzanja lamphamvu la El Shaddai!

Ndipo dzuwa lidzatuluka posachedwa pa tsiku la tsoka lomwe simukuliyembekezera, ndipo tsikulo lili pafupi kuposa momwe mukuganizira. Ndipo tsikulo lidzabweretsa chiwonongeko chotheratu pa Babulo Wachinsinsi, pakuti ali ndi gawo lowirikiza la chiweruzo cholengezedwa pa iye. Milandu yake ndi dama lake ndi zambiri ndipo chilango chake sichichedwa ndipo chilango chachikulu chidzagwa pa osalapa ngakhale Mpingo wanga wokhulupirika ndipo ngakhale Mkwatibwi wanga wokhulupirika adzasonkhanitsidwa kwa ine mwadzidzidzi m'mitambo ndikupulumutsidwa ku mkwiyo, ngakhale masautso aakulu a sabata la makumi asanu ndi awiri la Danieli. Babulo Wamkulu Wagwa, Wagwa! Imvani mawu anga pakuti ndikulankhula ngati kuti zachitika kale pakuti palibe chomwe chidzasinthe, kuchedwetsa, kulepheretsa, kapena kuyimitsa ziweruzo za Yahweh kugwa padziko lapansi.

Mu ola limodzi adzatenthedwa ndi moto, mizinda yake ina idzatenthedwa, mizinda yake ina idzasefukira mwamphamvu, nsanja zake zidzagwa ndikusweka mzidutswa zambiri, milatho yake idzagwa, ndipo kugwedezeka kwakukulu kudzachitika. Dziko lapansi lidzang'ambika mwamphamvu ndipo malo ambiri adzaphimbidwa ndi madzi amphamvu. Mipendero yake idzaphimbidwa kuchokera kum'mawa ndi kumadzulo ndi mafunde akuluakulu ndipo pakati padzakhala mafunde othamanga ndi kugwedezeka kwamphamvu. Zomwe zatsala zidzakhala chipululu ndipo sadzaukanso, pakuti mtundu uwu wa mayiko ambiri ndi mizinda yambiri sudzaukanso ndipo kugwa kwake kudzakhala kwakukulu! Pakuti dziko lapansi ndi la Mbuye ndi kudzaza kwake, ndipo mbali zonse za chikoka chake choyipa zidzafafanizidwa kwamuyaya m'mitundu yonse pa sabata la makumi asanu ndi awiri la Danieli, pakuti momwemo ndidzayeretsa dziko lapansi mu mkwiyo wanga. Ine, Khristu Yesu, Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda, ndalengeza mawu awa, ndipo kukonzekera kukwaniritsidwa kwawo kwayamba kale ndipo kudzachitika mwadzidzidzi mu ola limodzi.

Ndipo nkhondo idzabuka padziko lonse lapansi monga gawo la kuukira kwadzidzidzi pa Babulo Wachinsinsi, pakuti izi zakonzedwa ndikugwirizanitsidwa kwa zaka zambiri za yubile tsopano, pakuti chinsinsi cha kusayeruzika chakhala chikugwira ntchito kuyambira masiku akale ndipo chidzafika pakukwaniritsidwa kwathunthu pamene woletsa achotsedwa pamene okhulupirika anga atengedwa kupita kumwamba. Nkhondo idzagwa padziko lonse lapansi ndipo idzabwera pa anthu onse ndi mitundu yonse, pakuti iyi ndi nkhondo yachitatu yotereyi, pamene ogwa akufuna kuwononga madongosolo akale a dziko lapansi ndikusintha zinthu zonse ndi dongosolo lawo latsopano, ngakhale dongosolo la Chinjoka. Chilombo chochokera kunyanja ndi chilombo chochokera pansi padzauka mu mkwiyo waukulu, ndipo adzachita nkhondo ndi oyera mtima, pakuti kwaperekedwa kwa iwo kugonjetsa oyera mtima. Choncho zidzakhala kuti onse amene sakonda miyoyo yawo ndikuitana pa dzina la Mbuye mpaka mapeto adzapulumutsidwa kukhala m'chisangalalo chosatha mu muyaya wa muyaya. Ndipo Ine Yesu wa ku Nazarete ndasiya chitsanzo chosatha cha kumvera mpaka imfa, pakuti kumvera kwanga kwa Yahweh kunali ngakhale imfa ya pamtanda pamene ndinavutika ndikulipira machimo a anthu monga mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu ndipo taonani ndikubwera monga Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda ndipo sindidzayimitsidwa kapena kulepheretsedwa!

Ndipo aneneri anga awiri omwe ndi mitengo iwiri ya maolivi ndi zoyikapo nyali ziwiri omwe amaima pamaso pa Mulungu wa dziko lapansi adzanenera kwa miyezi 42 atavala chiguduli, pakuti anatengedwa kupita kumwamba mu nthawi zakale. Ndipo ngati munthu aliyense afuna kuwapweteka, moto umatuluka mkamwa mwawo, ndikuwononga adani awo: ndipo ngati munthu aliyense afuna kuwapweteka, ayenera kuphedwa mwanjira imeneyi. Iwo ali ndi mphamvu yotseka kumwamba, kuletsa mvula m'masiku a maulosi awo: ndipo ali ndi mphamvu pa madzi kuwasandutsa magazi, ndi kukantha dziko lapansi ndi miliri yonse, nthawi iliyonse yomwe afuna.

Umboni wawo ndi wosatha, ndipo adzalengeza uthenga wabwino wosatha, ndipo adzalankhula za mapulani a Yahweh a nthawi zonse, ndipo adzandilankhulira ine Khristu Yesu, ndipo adzavumbulutsa zinsinsi zambiri ndipo adzatembenuzira mitima ya ambiri kwa Atate wa Kuwala amene alibe kusinthasintha kapena mthunzi wotembenuka. Iwo adzandichitira umboni ine Yeshua HaMashiach pakuti ndine amene Yahweh wanditumiza ku Isiraeli kudzachita malonjezo omwe anapangidwa kwa Atate awo ndipo ndine Mesiya weniweni tsopano ndi mpaka muyaya ndipo ufumu wanga ulibe mapeto! Pakuti ndi nthawi ziti zomwe zakhalapo pomwe Yahweh adzisiya wopanda umboni? SIKUNAKHALEPO nthawi yomwe Yahweh adzisiya wopanda umboni, ndipo onse omwe adakonzedweratu ku chipulumutso chosatha adzabwera kudzapulumutsidwa mu muyaya wosatha ndi chisangalalo chosatha, pakuti kukwaniritsidwa kwa zinthu zonse kwayamba kale.

Ndipo chilombo chochokera kunyanja chidzauka komanso chilombo chochokera pansi, chomwe chilinso mneneri wonyenga. Ndipo adzatsegula pakamwa pake kulankhula zamwano zazikulu motsutsana ndi Yahweh kuchitira mwano dzina lake ndi chihema chake ndi iwo omwe amakhala kumwamba. Ndipo kudzaperekedwa kwa iye kuchita nkhondo ndi oyera mtima ndi kuwagonjetsa ndipo mphamvu idzaperekedwa kwa iye kulamulira fuko lililonse, ndi lilime, ndi mtundu pakuti adzatsogolera dziko lapansi. Chilombo chochokera pansi chidzalamuliranso dziko monga Moshiach wawo wonyenga ndipo chidzakhala womanga kachisi wawo. Chilombo chochokera kunyanja chidzalamulira malinga ndi malamulo awo amwano a Nowa pamene akufuna kufafaniza chilichonse chokhudzana ndi Mulungu weniweni ndi mwana wake wobadwa yekha Yesu Khristu pamaso pa dziko lapansi.

Chilombo chochokera pansi chidzachititsa dziko lapansi kupembedza chilombo, chomwe bala lake lakupha linachiritsidwa. Ndipo adzachita zozizwitsa zazikulu kusocheretsa iwo omwe amakhala padziko lapansi, pakuti kudzaloledwa kwa iye kuchita zizindikiro zambiri zabodza ndi zozizwitsa, ndipo adzachititsa moto kutsika padziko lapansi pamaso pa anthu ndipo adzatsogolera anthu onse kudabwa nawo. Koma iye ndi mdani, ndipo amadzikweza yekha motsutsana ndi zonse zomwe zimatchedwa Mulungu, kapena zomwe zimapembedzedwa: kotero kuti amakhala ngati Mulungu mu Kachisi wa Mulungu, kudzisonyeza yekha kuti ndi Mulungu.

Ndipo adzapanga fano la chilombo ndipo adzachititsa onse kupembedza fano lomwe adzapanga. Ndipo kudzaloledwa kwa iye kupereka mzimu ku fano la chilombo kuti fano la chilombo lilankhule ndipo adzachititsa onse omwe sadzapembedza fano la chilombo kuphedwa. Ndipo adzachititsa onse, olemera ndi osauka, aufulu ndi akapolo, kulandira chizindikiro chake padzanja lawo lamanja kapena pamphumi pawo.

Ndipo ndikunenanso kwa iwo omwe amakhala padziko lapansi kuti onse amve:

NGATI MUNTHU ALIYENSE APEMBEDZA CHILOMBO NDI FANO LAKE, NDI KULANDIRA CHIZINDIKIRO CHAKE PAMPHUMI PAKE, KAPENA PADZANJA LAKE, YEMWEYO ADZAMWA VINYO WA MKWIYO WA MULUNGU, WOTSANULIRIDWA WOSASAKANIZIDWA M'CHIKHO CHA MKWIYO WAKE, NDIPO ADZAZUNZIDWA NDI MOTO NDI SULUFULE, PAMASO PA ANGELO OYERA, NDI PAMASO PA MWANAWANKHOSA: NDIPO UTSI WA KUZUNZIKA KWAWO UKWERA KWA MUYAYA NDI MUYAYA. NDIPO ALIBE MPUMULO USANA NDI USIKU, IWO OMWE AMAPEMBEDZA CHILOMBO NDI FANO LAKE, NDI ALIYENSE AMENE ALANDIRA CHIZINDIKIRO CHA DZINA LAKE.

Dziko lapansi lomwe lilipo tsopano silidzakhalaponso, ndipo ufumu womwe ukukwera wa Chinjoka udzagwetsedwa pansi kotheratu, pamene ufumu wa Satana ukuyandikira mapeto ake, pakuti iwo ndi olanda malo ndipo si mafumu. Ndipo ili ndi dziko lomwe ambiri adzipeza alimo, ngakhale omwe adzapulumuke masoka ndi nkhondo zomwe zikubwera. Musatenge chizindikiro anthu anga, pakuti adzabwera kudzakupatsani mtendere, adzabwera kudzakupatsani chitetezo, adzakuuzani kuti zonsezi ndi zokuthandizani. Adzayambitsa miliri yawo yotchedwa miliri chifukwa akonza kale zokopa dziko lapansi kuti akudwala. Ndipo mitundu yonse ya miliri ndi matenda idzachuluka pakuti zoterezi ndi zotsatira za wokwera pahatchi yemwe akubwera ndipo adzakwera!

Adzapereka thanzi ndi machiritso ku ziweruzo za Mulungu, pamene m'malo mwake munthu ayenera kulapa kuipa kwake. Adzapereka mwayi wolowa m'gulu lawo kuchokera ku ziweruzo zoopsa zomwe zatsala pang'ono kugwa pa dziko lapansi. Pakuti Ine Khristu Yesu ndidzakuuzani zisanachitike kuti mudziwe kuona mtima kwa mawu anga ndi kuti muthe kuyenda m'madzi owopsa omwe ambiri adzipeza alimo, ngakhale omwe ali ndi kulimba mtima kuitana pa dzina langa mpaka mapeto, pakuti iwo ndi olowa nyumba a chipulumutso chamuyaya. Pakuti onse amene aitanira pa Yesu wa ku Nazarete monga Ambuye wawo, ngakhale onse amene aitanira pa dzina langa lamphamvu ndipo sakonda miyoyo yawo mpaka mapeto, adzapulumutsidwa ku moyo wosatha.

Kodi simunawerenge? Awa ndiwo amene adzatuluka m'chisautso chachikulu, ndipo achapa mikanjo yawo, ndi kuyiyeretsa m'mwazi wa Mwanawankhosa. Chifukwa chake, ali pamaso pa mpando wachifumu wa Mulungu, ndipo amamtumikira usana ndi usiku m'Kachisi wake: ndipo iye wokhala pa Mpando wachifumu adzakhala pakati pawo. Sadzamvanso njala, kapena ludzu, kapena dzuwa silidzawawomba, kapena kutentha kulikonse. Pakuti Mwanawankhosa, amene ali pakati pa mpando wachifumu, adzawadyetsa, ndipo adzawatsogolera ku akasupe a madzi amoyo: ndipo Mulungu adzapukuta misozi yonse m'maso mwawo.

Komabe, ngakhale kuti khamu la makamu lidzapulumutsidwa mu sabata la makumi asanu ndi awiri la Danieli ndipo chipulumutso chidzafika kwa Isiraeli pakuti otsala adzandigwadira Ine Yeshua HaMashiach, (Pakuti Ine ndine Mesiya woona mwa amene mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu adzapereka malo kwa Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda, ndipo onse awiri adzakwaniritsidwa mu mgwirizano wamuyaya mwa Ine, pakuti mwa Ine Khristu Yesu Mkango udzagona pansi mumtendere ndi mwanawankhosa.) n'zomvetsa chisoni, ndipo mosafunikira, dziko lonse lapansi lidzadabwa ndi kutsatira olamulira a chilombo awa, pakuti adzasangalatsa ambiri. Ndi ufiti wawo ndi matsenga awo, adzakopa dziko lapansi kuti lilandire poizoni wawo, ndipo adzapatsa poizoni kwamuyaya omwe amakonda dziko lapansi ndi ufiti wawo, pakuti chizindikiro cha chilombo chidzabisidwa ngati mankhwala. Ngakhale kulumidwa kwa njoka komwe dongosolo lawo lakhazikitsidwa kale ndi kuyesedwa, ndipo makamu a anthu akonzedwa kale kuti alandire chizindikiro chawo ndipo mbewu yawo ya nkhondo inali yopambana.

Ndipo motero dongosolo lawo liri lokonzeka pa nthawi yoyenera, ndipo akonza ziwerengero zawo za chikhalidwe ndipo onse adzalamuliridwa omwe amakhala m'mizinda yawo ndi gulu lawo. Pakuti adzayenera kutenga chizindikiro cha chilombo kuti atenge nawo mbali m'gulu lawo la satana, ngakhale ufumu wa Chinjoka. Omwe atenga chizindikirocho adzasinthidwa kwamuyaya. Ma genetics awo enieni adzasinthidwa, pakuti adzisakaniza okha ndi mbewu ya anthu: koma sadzamatirira wina ndi mzake, monga chitsulo sichisakanizidwa ndi dongo. Chizindikiro chawo cha chilombo, ngakhale kulumidwa kwa njoka, chidzawononga kotheratu onse omwe atenga poizoni wawo ndipo palibe kuthawa.

Ngakhale onse omwe atenga chizindikirocho adzasinthidwa kwamuyaya, ndipo adzipeza ali mbali ya dongosolo lawo, ndipo adzalumikizidwa ndi matekinoloje ndipo malingaliro awo sadzakhalanso awo. Adzalamuliridwa ndipo adzakhala ngati akufa oyenda, adzakhala akapolo kwamuyaya ku malingaliro a gulu la dongosolo lawo, ngakhale a matekinoloje awo pakuti adzalumikizidwa pamodzi ndi intaneti ya zinthu zomwe zikubwera. Adzataya chifuniro chawo, ndipo sadzakhala ndi kumverera ndipo chilichonse cha Mulungu chidzafafanizidwa kwamuyaya kwa iwo chifukwa adzisandutsa okha kukhala chikhalidwe chosawomboledwa, ndipo adzadziphatika okha kwathunthu ndi kwamuyaya kwa omwe adagwa ndipo kwamuyaya adzakhala a chinjoka ndipo ku nyanja yaikulu yamoto adzapita kwamuyaya. Ndipo motero chidzakhala tsogolo la chisankho chawo.

Pakuti chigamulo cha chisankho chawo nchosasinthika. Ndipo miliri yawo ndi zilonda ndi matenda zidzakhala zambiri, ndipo ngakhale adzalonjezedwa thanzi ndi mtendere, thanzi ndi mtendere sadzakhala nazo. Chifukwa Satana akuyesetsa kugwiritsa ntchito ufiti wake kulimbana ndi mkwiyo ndi ziweruzo za Mulungu ndi kupatsa poizoni ndi kupha makamu a anthu, koma sadzakhala ndi mpumulo ndipo omwe atenga nawo mbali mu ufiti wake sadzathawa. Omwe atenga chizindikirocho adzawala pansi pa magetsi awo amdima, ndipo magetsi awo adzakhala m'misewu ya mizinda yawo yomwe akonzera akapolo awo. Mvetsetsani kuti awa adzakhala oyenda otembereredwa omwe alibe chikhululuko ndipo omwe sangathe kuchira, pakuti awa ndi omwe mayina awo sanalembedwe m'Buku la Moyo la Mwanawankhosa, wophedwa kuyambira kukhazikitsidwa kwa dziko lapansi. Ndipo palibe chipulumutso chomwe chidzapezeka pakati pawo kapena m'gulu lawo, pakuti kuyenda pakati pawo ndi kulowa gulu lawo kudzafunika kwenikweni chizindikiro cha chilombo.

Mboni zanga ziwiri zidzachitira umboni mpaka nthawi za umboni wawo zitakwaniritsidwa pakuti miyezi 42 yaperekedwa. Ndipo motero chidzaperekedwa kwa chilombo chomwe chikutuluka m'phompho kuti chipange nkhondo kulimbana nawo, ndipo chidzawagonjetsa, ndi kuwapha, ngakhale mitengo yanga ya azitona iwiri, ndi zoyikapo nyali ziwiri pamene amaliza umboni wawo. Ndipo mitembo yawo idzagona mumsewu wa mzinda waukulu, womwe mwauzimu umatchedwa Sodomu ndi Igupto, komwe Ambuye wanu adapachikidwa. Ndipo oipa adzachita chikondwerero chachikulu ndi kupatsana mphatso wina ndi mzake chifukwa cha imfa zawo. Ndipo pambuyo pa masiku atatu ndi theka Mzimu wa moyo wochokera kwa Mulungu udzalowa mwa iwo: ndipo adzaima pa mapazi awo, ndipo mantha aakulu adzagwa pa onse omwe adawaona. Ndipo adamva mawu aakulu ochokera kumwamba, akuwauza kuti, Bwerani kuno. Ndipo adakwera kumwamba mu mtambo, ndipo adani awo adzawaona akukwera kumwamba ku moyo wosatha ndipo kwamuyaya adzasinthidwa. Ndipo Mngelo wachisanu ndi chiwiri adzaomba, ndipo kudzakhala mawu aakulu kumwamba, akuti, Maufumu a dziko lapansi lino asanduka maufumu a Ambuye wathu, ndi a Khristu wake, ndipo adzalamulira kwamuyaya.

(Odala ndi akufa amene amamwalira mwa Ambuye, kuyambira tsopano, inde, atero Mzimu, kuti apume ku ntchito zawo, ndipo ntchito zawo ziwatsata.)

Ndipo motero chisautso chachikulu kuposa zonse chidzayamba, ngakhale kuti chisautso chiri chokhazikika mu sabata la makumi asanu ndi awiri la Danieli. Ndipo kachisi wawo adzamalizidwa ndipo chonyansa cha chiwonongeko chidzakwaniritsidwa ndi Moshiach wawo wonyenga. Ndipo adzatsimikizira pangano ndi ambiri kwa sabata limodzi: ndipo pakati pa sabata adzaletsa nsembe ndi chopereka, ndipo chifukwa cha kufalikira kwa zonyansa, adzachipanga kukhala chabwinja, ngakhale mpaka chimaliziro chotsimikiziridwa chidzathiridwa pa chabwinjacho.

Ino idzakhala nthawi yamdima kwambiri ana anga, pakuti Ine Yeshua HaMashiach ndikukuuzani zinthu izi zisanachitike, ndipo zalembedwa ngati chenjezo kwa onse. Ndipo motero a 144,000 anga adzabwera kudzathandiza omwe akanawonongeka ndipo omwe aitanira pa dzina langa. Ndidzawatsogolera pang'onopang'ono, ndipo adzandichitira ntchito zambiri zamphamvu. Pakuti awa ndi omwe atengedwa mu kusonkhana kwa oyera mtima kwa Ine, Khristu Yesu, mumlengalenga pa kulira kwa lipenga lomaliza ndipo lipenga latsala pang'ono kulira.

Awa ndi okhulupirika anga, ngakhale omwe mwa iwo munalibe chinyengo, pakuti adziyeretsa okha ngati namwali woyera m'mwazi wa Mwanawankhosa womwe ndinapereka kwamuyaya ngati mkulu wawo wa ansembe, pakuti Ine ndine mkulu wa ansembe kwamuyaya monga mwa dongosolo la Melikizedeki. Awa ndi mkwatibwi wanga, ndipo adzanditsata kulikonse komwe ndipita. Taonani! Ndidzawapanga kukhala amphamvu, ndipo palibe chomwe chidawonekerepo pa dziko lapansi chomwe chingafanane ndi maluso omwe ndidzawaveka. Adzavekedwa bafuta wabwino, woyera, wopanda banga ndipo maluso awo adzaimira zomwe akwaniritsa ndipo adzakhala ndi maluso aakulu pakuti ndi okhulupirika oitanidwa ndi osankhidwa ngakhale kusanakhazikitsidwe maziko a dziko lapansi ndi Yahweh chifukwa cha zolinga izi.

A 144,000 anga adzatsikira pa Phiri la Ziyoni ndipo ndidzawatsogolera. Adzatenga omwe ndiwatsogolera kwa iwo kupita kuchipululu kuti akabisidwe ku mdima waukulu ndi chizunzo cha ufumu wa adani. Adzatsogoleredwa ku chitetezo ku miliri isanu ndi iwiri pakuti mu ziweruzo izi muli kuuma kwa mkwiyo wa Mulungu kuti uthiridwe pa mpando wa chilombo ndi kuzunza ufumu wa chinjoka pakuti chilango chawo ndi chachikulu. Ndipo a 144,000 adzatsogolera iwo ku chitetezo, adzawachiritsa ku mabala awo, adzawathandiza ndi kuwapatsa chiyembekezo chachikulu choitanira pa dzina la Ambuye ngakhale mpaka mapeto ngati kuli kofunikira. Pakuti ndikadapanda kufupikitsa masikuwo, palibe thupi lomwe likadasiyidwa lamoyo. Ndipo motero adzamaliza zomwe ndidzawatsogolera.

Ndipo pamene nthawi zakwaniritsidwa zidzakhala choncho, ngakhale monga kwalembedwa ndipo monga kwalembedwa zikhale choncho: Ndipo ndinaona kumwamba kutatseguka, ndipo taonani hatchi yoyera, ndipo iye wokhala pa iyo anatchedwa wokhulupirika ndi woona, ndipo m'chilungamo aweruza ndi kupanga nkhondo. Maso ake anali ngati lawi lamoto, ndipo pamutu pake panali akorona ambiri, ndipo anali ndi dzina lolembedwa, lomwe palibe munthu analidziwa koma iye mwini. Ndipo anali atavala chovala choviikidwa m'mwazi, ndipo dzina lake limatchedwa, Mawu a Mulungu. Ndipo magulu ankhondo omwe anali kumwamba anamutsata pa mahatchi oyera, ovala bafuta wabwino, woyera ndi woyera. Ndipo m'kamwa mwake mutuluka lupanga lakuthwa, kuti nalo akanthe mitundu ya anthu: ndipo adzawalamulira ndi ndodo yachitsulo: ndipo aponda chopondera mphesa cha ukali ndi mkwiyo wa Mulungu Wamphamvuyonse. Ndipo ali nalo pa chovala chake, ndi pa ntchafu yake dzina lolembedwa, Mfumu Ya Mafumu, Ndi Mbuye Wa Ambuye.

Ndipo motero Ine, ngakhale Yesu wa ku Nazarete, ngakhale Mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu, ngakhale Khristu Yesu yemwe alinso Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda ndidzabwera kudzaweruza ndi kupanga nkhondo pa Chinjoka ndipo ufumu wake wonse udzaphwanyidwa kotheratu kuti usadzaukenso. Ndipo chilombo ndi mneneri wonyenga adzaponyedwa amoyo m'nyanja yamoto ndipo Satana adzamangidwa ndi unyolo waukulu m'phompho kwa zaka chikwi ndipo motero ulamuliro wanga wa zaka chikwi udzayamba ndipo ulamuliro wanga udzakhala m'chilungamo. Taonani! Ndikubweretsa mkwatibwi wanga ndi gulu la mafumu ndi ansembe omwe ndidzalamulira nawo dziko lapansi m'chilungamo ndipo ndidzalamulira mitundu ya anthu ndi ndodo yachitsulo. Choncho khalani ndi chiyembekezo chachikulu ana anga pakuti chimaliziro cha zinthu zonse chili pafupi ndipo nthawi iyi ya zaka zisanu ndi ziwiri ya mavuto aakulu, ngakhale mavuto a Yakobo idzatsatiridwa ndi ulamuliro wanga wolungama ndipo Yahweh adzakhala zonse mu zonse.

Ndipo ndinaona kumwamba kwatsopano, ndi dziko lapansi latsopano: pakuti kumwamba koyamba, ndi dziko lapansi loyamba zidapita, ndipo kunalibe nyanja. Ndipo Ine Yohane ndinaona Mzinda woyera, Yerusalemu watsopano ukutsika kuchokera kwa Mulungu kumwamba, wokonzedwa ngati mkwatibwi wokongoletsedwa kwa mwamuna wake. Ndipo ndinamva mawu aakulu ochokera kumwamba, akuti, Taonani, Chihema cha Mulungu chili ndi anthu, ndipo adzakhala nawo, ndipo adzakhala anthu ake, ndipo Mulungu mwini adzakhala nawo, ndi kukhala Mulungu wawo.
