Meyi 10, 2024

Ochimwa! Bwerani Kwa Ine mu Nthawi Yotsiriza Ino!

Imvani Mawu anga, ngakhale Mawu a Yesu wa ku Nazarete, pakuti ndikubwera kwa inu mu mzimu wa chikondi, chifundo, kudzichepetsa, chisomo, ndipo makamaka chifundo. Pakuti posachedwapa chisomo changa ndi chifundo zidzasinthidwa ndi sautso yaikulu, ngakhale sautso ya Yakobo, ndipo nthawi yonseyi ya zaka zisanu ndi ziwiri idzakhala yosiyana ndi ina iliyonse imene dziko lapansi linaionapo, ngakhale kuyambira pachiyambi cha chilengedwe. Idzayamba ndi chiwonongeko chadzidzidzi ndi chizunzo chomwe chidzangowonjezereka. Choncho, ndikubwera nthawi yotsiriza ino kwa ochimwa omwe sanalape mu chisomo ndi chifundo changa. Pakuti kwatsala kamphindi kochepa kuti mulape ngati mubwera kwa ine, Yesu Khristu, ndikundivomereza ngati Ambuye wanu ndikukhulupirira mumtima mwanu kuti Mulungu wandiukitsa kwa akufa. Ndakhala ndikutambasula manja anga mu chisomo ndi chifundo ku dziko lomwe likufa kwa nthawi yonse ya chisomo tsopano, ndipo ambiri andipanga kukhala Ambuye wawo, ngakhale ambiri omwe Ambuye Mulungu wathu anawaitana ndi kuwasankha asanakhazikitsidwe maziko a dziko lapansi kuti avumbulutsidwe mwa ine, Yesu Khristu Ambuye wamphamvu: Pakuti ndine Mbuye wa ambuye ndi Mfumu ya mafumu.

Ndipo ndaitana osankhidwa, ndi onse omwe akhala okhulupirika mu chipulumutso changa. Ambiri apulumutsidwa! Mwapulumutsidwa ku chiyani, mukufunsa? Mwapulumutsidwa ku chiweruzo ndi chiwonongeko chomwe chatsala pang'ono kumasulidwa pa dziko lino, milungu yake, olamulira ake, maufumu ake, ndi onse omwe amatsatira Satana, pakuti iye ndi mulungu wa dziko lino ndipo chiweruzo chake chafika. Ngakhale kupulumutsidwa ku chiweruzo cha mpando wachifumu waukulu woyera ndi nyanja yamoto yomwe yakonzedwera Mdyerekezi ndi angelo ake. Chiweruzo chaperekedwa pa olamulira a mdima wamakono uwu, ndipo posachedwapa chikufika pakukwaniritsidwa ndi kumalizidwa, pakuti dziko lapansi lidzalamuliridwa ndi dzanja langa m'chilungamo, pakuti nthawi yanga yafika yoti ndivumbulutsidwe, ndipo dziko lapansi ndi langa ndi kudzaza kwake, ngakhale Ine, Yeshua HaMashiach!

Chiweruzo chikubwera ndipo chilipo kale koma osati chokwanira. Ndipo ambiri a inu mukukhulupirirabe kuti muli ndi nthawi yochita machimo anu ndikufunafuna kukhutitsidwa kwa zilakolako zanu zochimwa zomwe zimachokera m'thupi lanu loipa. Mvetsetsani kuti ndilibe chikhumbo choweruza ndi kutsutsa aliyense wa inu, pakuti ndapereka moyo wanga m'chikondi changa pa inu ndi kulipira kwathunthu machimo anu mu zowawa zazikulu ndi kuzunzika pamtanda! Pakuti palibe tchimo limodzi lomwe silinalipidwe mokwanira ndi nsembe yanga yotetezera monga Paskha wangwiro. Ndine Mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu amene achotsa machimo a dziko lapansi. Ndipo sindinabwere kudzaweruza inu, koma ndabwera kudzapulumutsa inu. Dziko monga mukulidziwira latsala pang'ono kusintha kwambiri moti simudzazindikira zomwe zidzatuluka m'phulusa, pakuti mbali yaikulu ya dziko latsala pang'ono kutenthedwa ndi kuwonongedwa m'njira zambiri mu chiweruzo cholungama cha Yahweh. Ndipo zoopsa zazikulu zikutuluka m'phulusa limeneli.

Pakuti mkulu wa dziko lino weweruzidwa, ndipo chiweruzo chake chidzakwaniritsidwa ndi kumalizidwa mokwanira. Pakuti Satana wachita zoipa zambiri ndipo njira zake ndi zonyansa, ndipo amangobwera kudzaba, kupha, ndi kuwononga. Waipitsa anthu mokwanira komanso m'njira zosiyanasiyana, ndipo akufuna kuwononga zonse zomwe Mulungu amakonda. Pakuti Mulungu anakonda dziko lapansi kotero kuti anapatsa mwana wake wobadwa yekha kuti aliyense wokhulupirira mwa ine asawonongeke, koma akhale ndi moyo wosatha. Choncho mvetsetsani mokwanira kuti ndine mwanawankhosa womaliza wa Paskha nthawi zonse (sipadzakhalanso wina), ndipo ndalipira machimo onse. Satana waipitsa chibadwa cha anthu kuyambira m'munda wa Edeni, pamene anayesa Adamu ndi Hava kuti alandire chipatso chake chowola ndikukhulupirira chidziwitso chake chopanda phindu, pakuti chidziwitso chake chimabweretsa imfa kwa onse omwe amadya. Ndipo pachifukwa ichi Mulungu Wamphamvuyonse wapereka pangano la mtendere ku chilengedwe chake, ngakhale ine, Yesu Khristu.

Pakuti liwu la Aefeso litani, pamene Paulo anawalembera za ntchito zanga zomalizidwa (pakuti ndinati mu mpweya wanga womaliza pamtanda, "Zatha")! Koma tsopano, mwa Khristu Yesu, inu amene kale munali kutali mwayandikitsidwa ndi magazi a Khristu.

Pakuti iye ndiye mtendere wathu, amene anapanga onse awiri kukhala amodzi, ndipo wagwetsa khoma lomwe linali chotchinga pakati pathu. Imvani liwu langa. Ndine mtendere wanu, pakuti chilamulo chinatsutsa tchimo m'thupi; ngakhale thupi lanu ndi magazi anu zaipitsidwa mu tchimo, ngakhale kuipitsidwa kuyambira Edeni. Ine, Yesu Khristu, mu nsembe yanga yotetezera pa Kalvare ndagwetsa khoma lapakati logawanitsa pakati pa Myuda ndi Wamitundu, kuwapanga onse awiri kukhala munthu mmodzi watsopano, ngakhale kuwalenganso m'chilungamo ndi chiyero chenicheni kwa onse omwe amalandira mtendere pondilengeza ngati Ambuye wawo woukitsidwa!

Koma pali zambiri kuchokera kwa Aefeso (ngati kuti izi sizodabwitsa zokwanira), choncho Aefeso akupitiriza: ndipo wachotsanso kudzera m'thupi lake chifukwa cha chidani (ndiko kuti, chilamulo cha malamulo olembedwa) kuti apange a awiri kukhala munthu mmodzi watsopano mwa iye yekha, motero kupanga mtendere: ndi kuyanjanitsa onse awiri kwa Mulungu m'thupi limodzi kudzera pamtanda wake, ndipo anapha chidani kudzera mmenemo. Ndimveni, inu anthu omwe mwakana kuitana kwanga: Ndikukuitanani tsopano mu masekondi omalizawa, ndipo nthawi yatsala pang'ono kutha! Ndilandireni ngati nsembe yanu yamtendere, pakuti ndikukupatsani mwaufulu chipulumutso ndi chiwombolo kuchokera m'matupi anu oipa, ngakhale moyo wosatha mwa ine, Khristu Yesu. Musalakwitse: Mphatso iliyonse yabwino, ndi mphatso iliyonse yangwiro imachokera kumwamba, ndipo imatsika kuchokera kwa Atate wa zounikira, amene mulibe kusinthasintha, kapena mthunzi wotembenuka.

Imvani liwu langa ndikumvetsetsa kuti chilichonse chabwino m'miyoyo yanu, chilichonse chamtengo wapatali ndi ukoma, ndi cha Atate ndipo chaperekedwa ndi Atate wa Zounikira kuti mukhale ndi moyo wochuluka ndikubwera kwa ine, Ambuye Yesu, ngati mpulumutsi wanu. Ndipo kuti mudziwe kuti pali moyo wosatha kwa inu ngati mungovomereza. Kodi mumadzida nokha kwambiri moti mwakonzeka kupereka nsembe chilichonse chomwe mumakonda m'moyo chomwe chaperekedwa kuchokera kwa Atate kuti mukhutitse zilakolako zanu zadyera? Kodi machimo anu ndi ofunika kwambiri kwa inu moti mudzasankha chiweruzo chosatha ndi Satana m'malo mwa ubwino wa Mulungu ndi moyo watsopano mwa ine, Khristu Yesu? Kodi machimo anu ndi ofunika choncho kwa inu?

Ndikukuitanani, inu anthu adziko, ndipo ndikukuitanirani ku mtendere ndi moyo wosatha, moyo wopanda matenda ndi tchimo, ngakhale moyo wopanda matenda ndi kusweka mtima, moyo wopanda kuvutika komwe simudzawonanso omwe mumawakonda akuvutika; pakuti zinthu zakale zidzatha posachedwapa. Palibenso nthawi yokhutiritsa machimo anu. Pakuti ambiri a inu mwatenga chisomo cha Mulungu pachabe ndikukhulupirira mabodza a mbala, yomwe ikufuna kuba miyoyo yanu kuti mukhale naye mu chilango chake chosatha. Musalakwitse. Kukana ine, Yesu wa ku Nazarete (ngakhale iye amene anadzipereka yekha chifukwa cha machimo anu ndi chipulumutso), ndikukana moyo ndikusankha kwamuyaya imfa yachiwiri yomwe Satana ndi angelo ake aweruzidwa: Pakuti alibe chikhululuko, ndipo sadzakhala ndi mpumulo, ndipo alibe mtendere. Ndipo nyanja yamoto yakonzedwera Mdyerekezi ndi angelo ake.

Kukana nsembe yanga ndikukana moyo wosatha ndikukana Mulungu mokwanira. Choncho, sipadzakhala chifundo, chisomo, chikondi, kuwala, kapena mtendere koma chisoni, mdima, ndi kuzunzika komwe mudzakolola chipatso chowola cha thupi lanu mpaka kalekale. Kodi simukuwona momwe izi ziliri zopusa? Ndalipira machimo anu m'magazi anga otetezera! Koma mukukana njira zanga, ngakhale ndikukuitanani pakali pano ndipo kwa ambiri nthawi yotsiriza. Mukukana chikondi changa, ngakhale mtima wanga ukuswekabe chifukwa cha inu ndi mabanja anu, ndipo ndikadakupulumutsani mpaka kumapeto ngati mungolapa ndikusiya machimo anu. Kodi simukumvetsa kuti ndinavutika ngati wochimwa wotsika kwambiri mu nsembe yanga yotetezera kuti ndikukwezeni kwa Mulungu mu chipulumutso? Pakuti ndinakwezedwa pamtanda kuti ndipulumutse miyoyo yanu ndikukuyanjanitsani ndi Atate, ndipo ndakhala wopanda tchimo nthawi zonse, choncho landirani magazi anga opanda tchimo!

Pakuti Lemba litani? Pakuti ndithudi mwamva kuti Mulungu anakonda dziko lapansi kotero kuti anapatsa Mwana wake wobadwa yekha: kuti aliyense wokhulupirira mwa iye, asawonongeke, koma akhale ndi moyo wosatha. Ndikudziwa kuti mawu awa ndi odziwika kwa inu, pakuti ngakhale osangalatsa anu omwe amakusungani mu ukapolo wa Satana agwiritsa ntchito molakwika mawu anga mwamwano. Koma mungachite bwino kupitiriza kuwerenga, choncho chonde imvani liwu langa! Pakuti Mulungu sanatumize Mwana wake m'dziko lapansi kudzaweruza dziko lapansi: koma kuti dziko lapansi kudzera mwa iye lipulumutsidwe. Iye amene amakhulupirira mwa iye, saweruzidwa: koma iye amene sakhulupirira, waweruzidwa kale, chifukwa sanakhulupirire m'Dzina la Mwana wobadwa yekha wa Mulungu.

Kodi kulankhula kwanga sikumveka bwino? Kodi mawu anga samveka mosavuta? Ngati mukana ine, Ambuye Yesu, Mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu, mudzalumikizana kwamuyaya ndi mdani wanga, ngakhale omwe ali ndi udindo wokhetsa magazi osalakwa: Pakuti kundikana ndikulumikizana ndi kupanduka kwa Satana, pakuti Mdyerekezi wachimwa kuyambira pachiyambi. Ndipo uku ndiko kutsutsidwa, kuti kuwala kwabwera m'dziko lapansi, ndipo anthu anakonda mdima m'malo mwa kuwala, chifukwa ntchito zawo zinali zoipa. Ndipo chomwechonso ndi choonadi chokhudza inu, inu anthu adziko, ngakhale inu omwe mungakane mzimu wanga wa moyo, ndipo pa zonsezi ndikukuitananibe, ngakhale uku ndiko kuitana komaliza ndi kotsiriza chiweruzo chisanayambe. Pakuti palibenso nthawi. Kodi machimo anu ndi ofunika kwambiri kwa inu moti mungakhale nthawi yosatha mu mdima wakunja ndi chilango mu nyanja yamoto yaikulu yomwe yakonzedwera Mdyerekezi ndi angelo ake? Pakuti Mulungu safuna chilango ichi kwa inu ndipo wanditumiza ngati nsembe yake yamtendere kuti akuyanjanitseni naye kudzera m'magazi anga otetezera.

Imvani mawu anga ndikulandira mtendere wanga. Kwa onse omwe amamva mawu awa, dziwani kuti ine, Yesu wa ku Nazarete, ndikukuitanani; ndipo sindisamala kuti ndi machimo anji omwe akukumangani chifukwa ndawalipira onse. Mvetsetsani kuti kutenga chizindikiro cha chilombo kudzakhala kosakhululukidwa, ndipo ndikuyesera kukupulumutsani ku mayesero awa (ngakhale chizindikirocho sichinafikebe); pakuti ambiri adzataya miyoyo yawo mu kuzunzika chifukwa chokana. Koma mvetsetsani zotsatirazi: Simungatsike kwambiri moti sindingathe kukufikirani; koma musatenge chizindikirocho. Ngakhale odzikuza sangadzikweze okha mu tchimo kwambiri moti sindingathe kuwatsitsa mofatsa ku chipulumutso ndikuwasindikiza ndi mzimu wanga kuti ndiwakweze! Pakuti ndalipira machimo onse a anthu nthawi zonse, ndipo sindinasiye chilichonse chosachitika. Pali machimo ambiri, koma ndidzaitana ena a inu mwachindunji ndi zoipa zanu. Dziwani kuti ndidzakhululukira machimo anu onse osati awa okha, ndipo ndidzakuyeretsani mokwanira m'magazi anga ku machimo anu onse.

Kwa woledzera ndi wogwiritsa ntchito mankhwala osokoneza bongo, ndikuti: Tembenukirani kwa ine tsopano mu mphindi yosatha ya kumvetsetsa ndikumva mawu anga, pakuti ngati mumvetsera mwachikhulupiriro, mawu anga adzakubweretserani kuwala kwaulemerero kwa chipulumutso. Mawu anga adzamanga ziwanda zomwe zimakupangitsani kumwa ndi kugwiritsa ntchito mankhwala osokoneza bongo, ngakhale mungawapange kukhala abwenzi anu. Ndikukupatsani kumvetsetsa kozizwitsa kuti muthe kusankha chomwe mudzakhala nacho mpaka kalekale: Moyo wosatha ndi ine, Yesu Khristu, mu umodzi wangwiro ndi Mulungu mwiniwake? Kapena chiwonongeko chosatha m'moto womwe suzimitsidwa? Kodi tchimo lomwe mwagwira mwadyera m'manja mwanu, ngakhale kuledzera kwanu ndi kugwiritsa ntchito mankhwala osokoneza bongo, ndi lofunika kwambiri moti mungasankhe kuwonongeka, ngakhale kupereka tsoka lanu kwa omwe mumawakonda omwe amakutsatirani m'malo mwa ine?

Simuli pansi kwambiri moti sindingathe kukukwezani, ndipo ndikufunitsitsa kukukhululukirani, koma muyenera kuyankha liwu langa ndikutuluka m'njira zanu mu kulapa. Tayani mowa wanu ndikusiya kudzipha nokha, ndipo bwerani kwa ine mu kulapa, ndipo ndidzakulanditsani. Palibe nthawi yotaya; musandinyalanyaze ndikupita ku kuledzera ndikuyiwala mawu anga, pakuti mawu anu okana adzalembedwa kumwamba ngati mboni yotsutsana nanu pa tsiku lachiweruzo, ndipo simudzapulumuka!

Kwa wakupha, ndikuti: Siyani nthawi yomweyo chiwawa chanu chakupha cha omwe adapangidwa m'chifanizo cha Mulungu, ngakhale mnzanu, pakuti ili ndi tchimo lalikulu. Zoonadi, ndi choonadi chosasinthika, mwadzipha nokha ndi moyo uliwonse womwe mwatenga, ndipo popanda ine palibe mankhwala a chilango chanu chomwe chikubwera. Koma ngakhale kupha kwanu kunalipidwa mokwanira mu nsembe yanga, pakuti sindinasiye chilichonse chosachitika. Ndawonetsa chisomo changa m'moyo wa Mtumwi wamkulu Paulo ngati mboni yolimbikitsa, inu akupha.

Pakuti ngakhale Saulo ananyengedwa ndipo anali kulowera ku chiweruzo chachikulu panjira yopita ku Damasiko, komwe ndinamupulumutsa kwa iye mwini. Paulo anazunza, kuvulaza, ndikuvomereza ndikuchita kupha motsutsana ndi Mpingo wanga woyera! Pakuti amavomereza zimenezi mu Machitidwe: Ndipo ndinazunza njira iyi mpaka imfa, kumanga ndi kupereka m'ndende amuna ndi akazi. Komabe analandira pempho langa la moyo wosatha ndikundipanga ine, Yesu Khristu, Ambuye wake ndikunditumikira mokhulupirika ngati chitsanzo cha kulapa kwa onse, ndipo ndinamukweza kwambiri chifukwa cha kumvera kwake. Ndalipira njira zanu zakupha ngati mulapa kuchokera kwa izo ndikubwera kwa ine mu kulapa kwenikweni, pakuti ngati muchita izi tsopano, ndidzakulandirani mu chisomo ndi chifundo. Musanyengedwe; palibe nthawi yotaya.

Kwa otsika kwambiri, ndikuti: Ndakupangirani njira yoti mutuluke mu misala yanu ndi chisokonezo, pakuti ndakuperekani ku zilakolako zanu zonyansa monga momwe Aroma amalengezera: Chifukwa chake Mulungu anawapereka ku zilakolako zonyansa: pakuti ngakhale akazi awo anasintha kagwiritsidwe ntchito kachibadwa kukhala komwe kuli kotsutsana ndi chilengedwe: Ndipo chimodzimodzinso amuna, kusiya kagwiritsidwe ntchito kachibadwa ka mkazi, anatentha m'chilakolako chawo wina ndi mzake, amuna ndi amuna akuchita zomwe zili zosayenera, ndikulandira mwa iwo okha malipiro a cholakwa chawo omwe anali oyenera.

Tulukani mu chidetso chanu, ndipo tulukani mu kunyada kwanu kopanda phindu, ndikusiya kuchitira mwamwano chizindikiro chomwe chinaperekedwa kwa Nowa ndi kwa cholengedwa chilichonse chamoyo, ngakhale utawaleza womwe umawonekera m'mitambo yamvula. Utawaleza ndi woti dziko lapansi lidziwe kuti Mulungu sadzawononganso dziko lapansi ndi madzi, ngakhale chiweruzo cha moto chili pafupi. Bwerani kwa ine ndikulapa, sinthani njira zanu ndikuitana pa dzina langa, ndivomerezeni ngati Ambuye kuchokera mumtima wodzaza ndi chikhulupiriro mu kuuka kwanga, ndipo ndidzakulandirani. Fulumirani! Pakuti mulibe nthawi yoganizira, pakuti chiweruzo chatsala pang'ono kumasulidwa. Palibe nthawi yotaya.

Imvani mawu anga ndipo bwerani kwa ine, pakuti sindikondwera ndi chiweruzo ndi chiwonongeko chomwe chikubwera, ndipo ngakhale Yahweh, ayi, ngakhale Mulungu Wamphamvuyonse! Pakuti Adamu analengedwa m'chikondi cha Atate ndi ungwiro m'chilungamo ndi chiyero choona. Mulungu anamupatsa moyo wathunthu wopanda chosowa chilichonse. Ndipo choncho Satana anaukira chilengedwe ndikuwononga thupi lanu lenileni ndi chikhalidwe chanu kuti musochere kuyambira m'mimba, pakuti tchimo lili m'magazi anu: Moyo wa thupi uli m'magazi. Koma Mulungu, m'chifundo chake chachikulu, pakuti chifundo chake nchosatha, ngakhale Mulungu Wamphamvuyonse anandipereka ine, mwana wake wobadwa yekha, monga nsembe yangwiro yomwe idzakupulumutsani mpaka kumapeto ngati mungobwera kwa ine mukulapa machimo anu. Ndidzawononga ntchito za Satana m'miyoyo yanu ngati mubwera kwa ine. Chonde bwerani, pakuti manja anga sakhala otambasulidwa kwa nthawi yayitali!

Kwatsala nthawi yochepa kwambiri, ndipo tsoka la Yakobo latsala pang'ono kubuka padziko lonse lapansi, ndipo mitima ya anthu idzakhumudwa chifukwa cha kuopsa kwa zomwe zikubwera. Satana wakonza zonyansa zambiri zomwe adzazitulutsa, pakuti kukana kuitana kwanga ndikusankha ufumu wa Satana wa imfa ndi chiwonongeko; ndipo ndidzakusiyirani chisankho chanu, ndipo ndidzakupatsani mdima ndi masautso omwe mumawakonda kwambiri, ndipo mudzadya zipatso za njira zanu pokhapokha mutalapa. Pakuti ndi izi, uku ndiko kuitana komaliza chiweruzo chisanabwere mwachangu. Ngakhale atumiki anga ndi ansembe akulira mpaka kumalekezero a dziko lapansi pa sekondi yomweyi, kupatula kuti adzalirira chipulumutso chanu mpaka adzatengedwa kupita kwa ine m'mitambo pamene ndidzabwera kudzachitenga mkwatibwi wanga mumlengalenga posachedwa, ndipo izi zidzachitika mosayembekezereka, ngakhale ndikukuuzani momveka bwino zisanachitike. Ndipo pamenepo kuitana kwanga komaliza kwa chisomo ndi chifundo kudzatha. Ndipo pamene ndidzatenga mkwatibwi wanga kupita kumalo otetezeka, chiwonongeko chadzidzidzi chidzabuka padziko lapansi, ndipo ambiri a inu omwe mukumva mawu awa simudzapulumuka. Ena a inu mukuganiza mopusa kuti mutha kupitiriza kugwiritsa ntchito molakwika chisomo changa ndipo chidzapitiriza mpaka kalekale, koma choonadi chomvetsa chisoni nchakuti chisomo chidzasanduka chiweruzo, ndipo zidzachitika mwadzidzidzi, ndipo ambiri sadzapulumuka.

Pakuti omwe sadzaphedwa ndi chiwonongeko chadzidzidzi ichi chomwe chatsala pang'ono kutulutsidwa pa Babulo Wachinsinsi adzipeza ali m'dziko lomwe sanalotepo kuti lingatheke, dziko lomwe muli imfa ndi chiwonongeko chosalekeza, pakuti Satana adzachotsa mwadongosolo chilichonse chokhudzana ndi ine, Yesu Khristu, ndipo adzachita izi mopanda chifundo. Kudzakhala mazunzo m'dziko lonse ndi nkhanza zopanda chifukwa, ndipo kubwera kwa ine mu nthawi iyi ya chiweruzo ndi kuyesedwa kudzakhala kovuta kwambiri; koma aliyense amene adzaitana pa dzina la Ambuye adzapulumutsidwa, ndipo adzayenera kupirira mpaka kumapeto, ndipo ambiri adzadulidwa mitu, ndipo ambiri adzamwalira m'mazunzo aakulu.

Koma ngakhale pamenepo, Mulungu adzapukuta misozi yonse m'maso mwawo, pakuti Yohane Wovumbulutsa analengeza za iwo: Awa ndiwo amene anatuluka m'chisautso chachikulu, ndipo atsuka mikanjo yawo, ndi kuyiyeretsa m'magazi a Mwanawankhosa. Chifukwa chake, ali pamaso pa mpando wachifumu wa Mulungu, ndipo amamtumikira usana ndi usiku m'Kachisi wake: ndipo iye wokhala pa Mpando Wachifumu adzakhala pakati pawo. Sadzamvanso njala, kapena ludzu, ndipo Dzuwa silidzawawomba, kapena kutentha kulikonse. Pakuti Mwanawankhosa, amene ali pakati pa mpando wachifumu, adzawadyetsa, ndipo adzawatsogolera ku akasupe a madzi amoyo: ndipo Mulungu adzapukuta misozi yonse m'maso mwawo.

Ine, ngakhale ine, Khristu Yesu, Ambuye woukitsidwa ndi wokwera kumwamba, ngakhale Mesiya woona: Ndikukuitanani, ndipo chiyembekezo changa ndi chikhumbo changa nchakuti mulandire nsembe yanga yamtendere ndikubwera ku chipulumutso changa kuti mukhale ndi moyo wosatha ndi ine pachifuwa cha Atate! Koma musakane kuitana kwanga, pakuti sindidzaitana kwa nthawi yayitali, ndipo sindidzaitana m'chisomo ndi chifundo mwanjira iyi kachiwiri, pakuti nthawi yatha (ngakhale chisomo ndi chifundo zili ndi ine mpaka kalekale). Chonde bwerani kwa ine kuti ndikupulumutseni m'masekondi omaliza awa a chisomo. Pakuti simuli osiyana ndi omwe anakhulupirira pachiyambi cha nthawi yanga ya chisomo, ndipo chipulumutso changa ndi mphoto yanga zidzakhala zanu ngati mumvera mawu anga pakali pano. Pakali pano ndi nthawi ya chipulumutso! Lapani ndipo bwerani kwa ine modzichepetsa. Ndipangeni kukhala Ambuye wanu ndipo dziwani kuti ndili ndi moyo mpaka kalekale, pakuti Mulungu anandiukitsa kwa akufa kuti sindidzafanso. Ngati mulandira pempho langa, mudzakhala mutagonjetsa, ndipo mudzalandira mphoto za wogonjetsa:

Taonani, ndiyimira pakhomo, ndikugogoda: ngati munthu aliyense amva mawu anga, ndikutsegula chitseko, ndidzalowa kwa iye, ndipo ndidzadya naye, ndipo iye ndi ine. Kwa iye amene agonjetsa, ndidzamupatsa kukhala ndi ine pampando wanga wachifumu, monga inenso ndinagonjetsa, ndipo ndakhala pansi ndi Atate wanga pampando wake wachifumu. Werengani izi mobwerezabwereza, pakuti ndikukupatsani mwayi womaliza wa chipulumutso kudzera m'chisomo. Koma musakane kuitana kwanga, pakuti palibenso nthawi ya zinthu zotere. Kwa ambiri a inu, kundikana kudzakhala komaliza ndi kosatha, ndipo simudzachira kapena kupulumuka chiweruzo chomwe chikubwera, pakuti chiwonongeko chadzidzidzi chili pa inu. Ndipo Mzimu ndi Mkwatibwi akuti, bwerani. Ndipo iye amene akumva anene kuti, Bwera. Ndipo iye amene ali ndi ludzu, abwere. Ndipo aliyense amene afuna, atenge madzi a moyo kwaulere.

Ndipo pomaliza: Iye amene ali wosalungama, apitirize kukhala wosalungama: ndipo iye amene ali wodetsedwa, apitirize kukhala wodetsedwa: ndipo iye amene ali wolungama, apitirize kukhala wolungama: ndipo iye amene ali woyera, apitirize kukhala woyera. Ndipo taonani, ndikubwera msanga, ndipo mphoto yanga ili ndi ine, kupatsa munthu aliyense monga mwa ntchito yake. Choncho zikhale tero.
