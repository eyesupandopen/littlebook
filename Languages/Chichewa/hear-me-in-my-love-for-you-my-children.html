Epulo 21, 2024

Ndimvereni M'chikondi Changa pa Inu Ana Anga!

(M'nkhani ya uthenga wa uneneri uwu, "M'edomi" akutanthauza omwe anatengera njira za Akanani zosintha chibadwa, matsenga akuda, kupereka nsembe ana, kudya anzawo, ndi zina zotero. Esau kwenikweni anakwatira akazi awiri a Chihiti, omwe ndi Akanani, ndipo anatenga mkazi wachitatu kuchokera kunyumba ya Isimaeli. Kukwatira kwa Esau akazi a Chikanani kumasonyeza kusamvera ndi kugonjera komanso kuwonongeka kwa chibadwa, popeza Kanani anali atasokoneza chibadwa mwanjira ina. Liwu loti M'edomi likugwiritsidwa ntchito m'nkhaniyi kwa omwe amachitabe miyambo ya satana imeneyi, ndipo dzinali likhoza kugwiritsidwa ntchito pa fuko lililonse kapena mtundu womwe ukugwirizana ndi izi. Mfumu Herode anali M'edomi, chimodzimodzi ndi Afarisi ambiri, ndipo Yesu anawatcha ana a Mdyerekezi, zomwe zikusonyeza mbewu. Koma dziwani kuti, liwu loti M'edomi monga momwe lagwiritsidwira ntchito mu uthengawu silikunena za munthu aliyense potengera mtundu kapena dziko lake. Ndi chizindikiro chomwe chikugwiritsidwa ntchito kutchula omwe amachita mwachinsinsi zamatsenga za Chikanani kapena omwe adachokera m'mibadwo imeneyo ndipo amwazikana m'mitundu yonse.)

— William Brooks


Imvani mawu anga, Mpingo wanga, ndipo tcherani makutu anu ku nzeru ndi choonadi, pakuti taonani: Mawu anga ndi nzeru ndi choonadi. Nzeru ikufuula m'misewu, ndipo kumvetsa kukufuna kukambirana nanu; kodi tsopano munditchera khutu m'kumvera kwakufuna kwanu? Pakuti Ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndikufuula kuti ndimveke kwa Mkwatibwi wanga, ngakhale onse omwe ali m'thupi la Khristu. Imvani mawu anga, akutero Ambuye wamkulu ndi wokhulupirika, ngakhale iye amene ufumu wake suli wa dziko lapansi, pakuti ufumu wanga ndi ufumu wakumwamba. Chotero mu ufumu wanga, monga momwe kuliri kumwamba, momwemonso kudzakhala padziko lapansi; pakuti ndidzabweretsa chilungamo padziko lapansi, ndipo palibe chinthu choipa chomwe chidzalowe m'zipata zanga, pakuti ufumu wanga ndi ufumu wachilungamo kumene mkango udzagona pansi ndi mwana wankhosa mumtendere ndi chitetezo. Ambiri a inu, Mkwatibwi wanga, mwakhala odzikuza m'masiku ano a kuwonjezeka kwa chidziwitso, ndipo izi sizosayembekezereka, pakuti Mawu anga amati chiyani: Pakuti chidziwitso chitukumula! Koma chikondi chimamanga. Tsopano ndimvereni m'chikondi changa pa inu, ana anga!

Imvani mawu anga, pakuti ndilankhula za Aedomi omwe ali pakati panu, pakuti ambiri a inu simukudziwa omwe ndikuwanena. Ndilankhula za omwe amavala zikopa zankhosa koma ndi mimbulu yolusa, pakuti amadzibisa ndikuyenda pakati panu. Ndilankhula za sunagoge la Satana ndi onse omwe alowa m'chilengedwe cha Atate anga, otumizidwa ndi Satana mwiniwake, pakuti iwo ndi mbewu yeniyeni ya njoka. O, dikirani, musatseke makutu anu ndi kuphimba maso anu chifukwa simukugwirizana ndi mawu anga, o, inu ana anga ang'onoang'ono akhungu ndi ogontha mwa kufuna kwanu, koma pindani maondo anu modzichepetsa ndikutchera khutu lanu ku mawu anga, pakuti kunyada kutsogolera kuwonongeka ndipo mzimu wodzikuza utsogolera kugwa.

Dziwani, ana anga ang'onoang'ono, kuti mawu omwe ali m'Mawu anga a Mulungu ndi oona, olondola, ndi odalirika: ngakhale simukumvetsa mawu awa ndikuyesera kufotokoza choonadi chenicheni, kudzilankhulira nokha kuti: O, izo si zenizeni koma zophiphiritsa. O, izo sizinalembedwe kwa ine koma kwa wina. O, izo ndi nthano chabe zakale ndi njira zolemba. O, izo ndi nkhani chabe za nthawi zakale ndipo siziyenera kutengedwa monga zenizeni. Wopusa iwe! Kodi ukulola abusa osokera kunena kuti Mawu enieni a Mulungu, mawu omwewo omwe chipulumutso chako chimabwera, mawu a Yahweh mwiniwake si oona koma akhoza kufotokozedwa mopanda chikhulupiriko? O, chokani m'njira zanu zopanda chikhulupiriko, anthu anga, ndipo bwerani kwa ine ndi mtima wotseguka ndi malingaliro omwe avala kudzichepetsa.

Kodi simunawerenge? Kodi Genesis amanena chiyani pa nkhaniyi pamene Yahweh anakumana ndi njoka chifukwa cha kuipa kwake kochenjera komwe inachita m'Munda zaka za Yubile zambiri zapitazo? Ndipo ndidzaika udani pakati pa iwe ndi mkaziyo, ndi pakati pa mbewu yako ndi mbewu yake: idzaphwanya mutu wako, ndipo iwe udzaluma chidendene chake. Kodi mukuganiza kuti mawu awa ndi ongopeka ndi ophiphiritsa, ana anga? Kodi mukukhulupirira mawu opanda chikhulupiriko a kuunikiridwa komwe kwachititsa khungu malingaliro anu ku mawu anga nthawi yonseyi tsopano? Mabodza omwe amavomerezedwa mu sayansi yanu, pakuti apangidwa kukhala abodza ndi mabodza awo. Mabodza omwe amavomerezedwa mu maphunziro anu a zakuthambo, pakuti apangidwa kukhala abodza ndi mabodza awo. Mabodza omwe amavomerezedwa m'mbiri yanu, pakuti mwayiwala kuti ndinu ndani, ana anga ang'onoang'ono, ndipo mwadulidwa ku mbiri yanu yeniyeni ndipo mukukhala m'mabodza a mdani mu umbuli wotheratu wa dziko lenileni lomwe mukukhalamo.

Monga momwe ndiliridi mbewu yolonjezedwa ya mkaziyo, inde, mkazi wodabwitsa uja m'malemba (yemwe ena amamupembedza mochimwa) yemwe anati, Zikhale kwa ine monga mwa chifuniro chanu, kudzera mwa iye ndinabwera m'dziko lino. Ndinabadwa ngakhale kwa Mariya mu Betelehemu wa Yudeya ndipo ndinakulira monga mwana wa mmisiri wamatabwa, woleredwa ndi Yosefe yemwe anali wa mzera wa Davide, pamene sanafune kumuika poyera (pakuti ankamukonda kwambiri), pakuti anapezeka ali ndi mwana wa Mzimu Woyera. Monga momwe ndiliri mbewu yolonjezedwa yotumizidwa ndi Mulungu, momwemonso Satana ali ndi mbewu. Osati kuti akhoza kulenga chilichonse pa iye yekha, pakuti iye ndi msundu ndi mbala, koma wapanga zonyansa kuti zikuwonongeni.

Chifukwa chiyani izi zili zovuta kwa inu kukhulupirira? Kumvetsera mabodza omwe amaperekedwa ngati choonadi, mwanyalanyaza zinthu zoyambirira kwambiri, pakuti ndakuuzani zinthu zapadziko lapansi, ndipo simukhulupirira: mudzakhulupirira bwanji ngati ndikuuzani zinthu zakumwamba? Kodi simunawerenge mawu a Yahweh kwa mtumiki wake wokhulupirika Yobu? Unali kuti pamene ndinayika maziko a dziko lapansi? Fotokoza, ngati uli ndi kumvetsa. Ndani anayika miyeso yake, ngati ukudziwa? Kapena ndani anatambasula chingwe pa ilo? Maziko ake anamangiriridwa pa chiyani? Kapena ndani anayika mwala wake wapangodya? Pamene nyenyezi zam'mawa zinayimba pamodzi, ndipo ana onse a Mulungu anafuula mwachimwemwe. Werenganinso gawo lomalizalo, ana anga,

"ana a Mulungu anafuula mwachimwemwe" — inde, angelo analipo pa chilengedwe, ndipo amadziwa zinsinsi zambiri zakumwamba zomwe si za anthu kuti adziwe, pakuti anthu afufuza kwa zaka zambiri m'zilakolako zawo za mphamvu ndi ulemerero.

Ichi ndi chidziwitso cha angelo ogwa, ana anga, kodi simunawerenge? Kapena mukukhulupirirabe mabodza a Aedomi omwe afesa pakati panu? Koma kodi Genesis amanena chiyani pa nkhaniyi? Kunali zimphona padziko lapansi masiku amenewo: ndiponso pambuyo pake, pamene ana a Mulungu analowa kwa ana aakazi a anthu, ndipo anawaberekera ana; omwewo anakhala anthu amphamvu, omwe anali akale, anthu otchuka. Ambiri a inu mumatopetsa kuleza mtima kwanga pokhulupirira nthano yolakwika yakuti ana a Mulungu anali ochokera kwa Seti, ndipo anakwatira ana aakazi oipa a anthu ndikubala zimphona mu kuipa. Anthu akhala oipa kuyambira kugwa, ndipo ngakhale anthu oipa ataberekana mochuluka bwanji, ana awo sadzakhala zimphona zazikulu kwambiri. Ayi. Imeneyo ndi nthano yofuna kunyenga, pakuti gawo lina la kupambana kwa Satana ndi chifukwa cha chinsinsi chake chachinyengo, ndipo iye wagonjetsedwa, choncho kupambana kwake kwakanthawi kwatha mu kulephera kotheratu ndipo sadzathawa nyanja yake. Musamuwope, koma mvetsetsani mawu a Mawu anga m'nkhani yawo ya m'Baibulo, ndipo musakhale opanda nzeru ndi osadziwa choonadi.

Ana a Mulungu ndi alonda m'nkhani iyi, angelo omwe anapatsidwa udindo wothandiza anthu koma m'malo mwake anasiya malo awo oyamba, pakuti analakalaka ana aakazi a anthu. Pakuti ndinamuuza Yuda kuti alengeze chiyani m'malo mwanga? Ndipo Angelo omwe sanasunge malo awo oyamba, koma anasiya malo awo okhala, wawasunga m'maunyolo osatha pansi pa mdima, mpaka chiweruzo cha tsiku lalikulu. Werenganinso ndipo musakhale opanda chikhulupiriko, koma khulupirirani chabe. Ana a Mulungu ndi angelo ogwa omwe anadzidetsa okha ndi akazi; ambiri mwa akazi awa anatengedwa ndikuzunzidwa, ngakhale ena a iwo anachita izi mofunitsitsa ndipo adzalandira chiweruzo chawo.

Ana omwe anabala mu kudetsedwa kwawo, inde, mgwirizano woyipawu unabala zimphona, zolengedwa zoipa zopangidwa kuti zikuwonongeni. Mukudzichitira nokha choyipa chachikulu podalira kwambiri mabuku anu 66 okha. Pali mabuku ambiri ndipo mabuku anu 66 a Chipulotesitanti alibe maulosi onse omwe anaperekedwapo koma ali ndi zinthu zonse zokhudzana ndi moyo ndi umulungu mwa ine Khristu Yesu. Werengani mabuku a 1 Enoke ndi Yasha ndi Yubile. Sali malemba kwa inu koma ndithudi ndi mabuku ovomelezeka kuwerenga omwe akuperekedwa ndikugwidwa mawu m'malemba omwewo omwe mumawakonda. Musalamuliridwe ndi kuganiza kuti mabuku okhawo omwe amaloledwa m'gulu linalake ndiwo maulosi onse omwe anaperekedwapo. Phunzirani kwa ine.

Komabe, Mulungu anawononga dziko lomwe linalipo panthawiyo, pakuti bwenzi langa Petulo akuti chiyani: Pakuti iwo mofunitsitsa sakhala odziwa izi, kuti ndi mawu a Mulungu kumwamba kunaliko kale, ndi dziko lapansi loima kutuluka m'madzi, ndi m'madzi, Mwa izo dziko lomwe linalipo panthawiyo, litamizidwa ndi madzi, linawonongeka. Ndipo m'masiku a Nowa Atate anga anatumiza chigumula chachikulu chomwe chinafafaniza zonyansa zawo zonse zoipa, pakuti anapanga zonyansa zambiri zomwe zinabweretsa chiwonongeko chachikulu padziko lapansi. Anaphwanya dongosolo lachilengedwe la moyo ndikubweretsa kusakhazikika mu DNA yeniyeni ya anthu, ndipo munthu anaphatikizidwa ndi chilombo ndipo chilombo chinaphatikizidwa ndi nsomba ndipo mtundu wina wa chilombo unawetedwa ndi mtundu wina wa chilombo mpaka zonyansa zomwe ankazifuna zinapezeka. Kenako zonyansazo zinawononga anthu. ndipo chotero Atate anga anawawononga, kupulumutsa miyoyo isanu ndi itatu yamoyo. Nowa anapeza chisomo pamaso pa Mulungu, ndipo Nowa ndi banja lake ndiwo okha omwe sanawonongeke padziko lapansi m'kusokoneza kwawo chibadwa, pakuti Nowa anali munthu wolungama.

Werengani mawu anga ndikumvetsa dziko lanu, pakuti ambiri a inu mumakhulupirira mabodza a kuunikiridwa kwabodza komwe kunakana chilichonse chauzimu ndipo kunathandiza kupusitsa anthu ambiri ku mabodza awo opanda chikhulupiriko akuti zinthu zakuthambo ndizo zokha zofunika. Kodi simukumvetsa, anakana Mulungu m'kulankhula kwawo kopanda chikhulupiriko? Akunyengeni mwadala ndi maphunziro otsutsana ndi maphunziro: phunzirani kuti mumvetsetse chiphunzitso ichi cha a Jesuit.

Koma kodi Genesis amanena chiyani pa nkhaniyi? Kunali Zimphona padziko lapansi masiku amenewo: ndiponso pambuyo pake.

Pambuyo pa masiku ati, mukufunsa? Pambuyo pa masiku a Nowa. Pakuti pambuyo pa chigumula dziko lapansi linagawidwa mwa maere a cholowa, ndipo m'masiku a Pelegi dziko lapansi linagawidwa mwa cholowa pakati pa ana a Nowa, Semu, Hamu ndi Yafeti. Kuti tikwaniritse zolinga zathu tidzatsata mzera wa Hamu, pakuti kuchokera kwa Hamu kunachokera mibadwo ya otembereredwa, ngakhale Nimrode wochokera kwa Kushi ndi Igupto kapena Mizaraimu, ndipo Akanani oipa anachokera kwa Kanani woipa. Pakuti anali Kanani yemwe Nowa anamutemberera. Pakuti Kanani anasiya cholowa chake ndikukhazikitsa mzinda wa Turo pa zomwe zinakhala gombe la Foinike ndikutsogolera ku Sidoni komwe Yezebeli anachokera. Anachita izi kuti akhale pafupi ndi Phiri la Herimoni, komwe alonda anatsikira ndipo komwe ndinasandulika.

Kanani anapeza zomwe alonda anasiya, ndipo anagwiritsa ntchito chidziwitso chawo cha angelo ogwa m'matsenga akulu, ndipo miyambo yambiri yoipa inachitidwa ndi Mkanani, pakuti anapereka nsembe ngakhale ana awo. Anali oipa kwambiri ndipo anafalikira kwambiri moti anakwanitsa kutenga dziko la Kanani ndi zonyansa zake zatsopano, komwe Satana ankagwira ntchito chifukwa cha machimo ake. Chotero monga momwe Atate anga anatulutsira Ahebri mu ukapolo kuchokera ku Igupto, momwemonso anawatumiza kuti akalande cholowa chawo kuchokera ku mafuko onyansa a zimphona awa. (Kodi simukukhulupirirabe Mawu anga?) Kodi simunawerenge lipoti loipa lomwe linaperekedwa ndi azondi khumi mwa khumi ndi awiri omwe anatumizidwa m'dziko la Kanani ndi Mose kuti akalizonde? Pakuti Yoswa ndi Kalebe okha ndi omwe analankhula mawu achikhulupiriro, koma lipoti loipa lomwe linaperekedwa linali loona kwenikweni, kupatula kuti linali lipoti lochimwa, lopanda chikhulupiriko lomwe linakana mawu a Atate anga mokomera zomwe anaona ndi maso awo, pakuti Mulungu ndi wamkulu kuposa maso a aliyense, ana anga ang'onoang'ono. Werengani lipoti mu Deuteronomo: Koma amuna omwe anakwera naye, anati, Sitingathe kukwera kukamenyana ndi anthuwo, pakuti ndi amphamvu kuposa ife. Ndipo anabweretsa lipoti loipa la dziko lomwe analifufuza, kwa ana a Isiraeli, nati, Dziko lomwe tadutsamo, kulifufuza, ndi dziko lomwe limadya okhalamo ake, ndipo anthu onse omwe tinawaona mmenemo, ndi anthu aatali kwambiri. Ndipo kumeneko tinaona zimphona, ana a Anaki, omwe amachokera ku zimphona: ndipo tinali m'maso mwathu ngati ziwala, ndipo momwemonso tinali m'maso mwawo.

Chotero Yoswa anayeretsa dziko la zonyansa zoipa izi, ndipo sanali omwe mungawatchule kuti anthu; anali zonyansa zosakanizidwa zopangidwa kuti ziwononge mzera wa Khristu, monga momwe Satana ankafunira kuletsa kubwera kwanga koyamba. Komabe, ana a Isiraeli sanagwire mokhulupirika ntchito yomwe Atate anga anawapatsa polanda cholowa chawo kuchokera kwa olanda achiwawa. Zambiri mwa zolengedwa izi zinali zodya anzawo; kodi simunangowerenga kumene dziko linadya okhalamo ake? Pakuti Oweruza amatiuza chiyani pa nkhaniyi: Ndipo Ambuye anali ndi Yuda, ndipo anathamangitsa okhala m'phiri, koma sanathe kuthamangitsa okhala m'chigwa, chifukwa anali ndi magaleta achitsulo.

Ndipo Oweruza amanenanso kuti: Manase sanathamangitse okhala mu Beteseani, ndi midzi yake, kapena Taanaki ndi midzi yake, kapena okhala mu Dori, ndi midzi yake, kapena okhala mu Ibleamu, ndi midzi yake, kapena okhala mu Megido, ndi midzi yake: koma Akanani anafuna kukhala m'dziko limenelo. Ndipo zinachitika pamene Isiraeli anali wamphamvu, kuti anaika Akanani kukhala amisonkho, ndipo sanawathamangitse kotheratu. Pakuti Numeri akuti chiyani pa nkhani iyi: Koma ngati simudzathamangitsa okhala m'dziko pamaso panu, ndiye kuti zidzachitika kuti omwe muwalole kuti atsare mwa iwo, adzakhala zaminga m'maso mwanu, ndi minga m'mbali mwanu, ndipo adzakuvutitsani m'dziko lomwe mukukhalamo.

Ndipo chotero lemba linakwaniritsidwa ndipo mafuko a Chikanani amavutitsabe ana a Mulungu mpaka lero. Adzisakaniza okha pakati pa chilengedwe cha Mulungu ndipo adzibisa poyera, ngakhale kutenga chikhalidwe ndi makhalidwe a mitundu yomwe akuikhalamo. Koma mapeto ake ndi ofanana nthawi zonse: Amawononga mwadala mitundu yomwe akuikhalamo (Mulungu wawapanga kukhala othawa ndi oyendayenda padziko lapansi). Alankhula mabodza akulu ndikugulitsa mabodza omwewo ngati choonadi. Alanda mayiko anu, ndipo simukuwaona nkomwe, pakuti mwapangidwa khungu ku njira zawo chifukwa ambiri a inu mwagonjera kwa iwo, ngakhale sunagoge la Satana.

Bwererani ku Mawu anga oyera ndikunyalanyaza mawu ndi njira za Aedomi. Siyani kukweza mawu ochimwa kuposa Mawu Oyera kwambiri a Atate anga. Pakuti ambiri mwa Akanani awa anakhala Aidumea pakapita nthawi ndipo ankakhala pafupi ndi Yuda. Pamene Yohane Hirikano anagonjetsa Idumea, anatembenuzira Aidumea ku Chiyuda, ndipo Yudeya anabadwa kuchokera ku kuphatikizana kwa Yuda ndi Idumea. Ndipo chotero ndinaima kuwatsutsa mu Isiraeli zaka pafupifupi 2000 zapitazo, ndipo kuima kwanga kwalembedwa m'mauthenga abwino kuti muyende monga momwe ndinayendera. Mawu awo anasokoneza Torah, ndipo miyambo yawo yapakamwa ndi miyambo yamatsenga yochokera ku Babulo inadzibisa m'dziko langa, pakuti dziko langa latengedwa ndi achinyengo, Sunagoge lenileni la Satana.

Kuyambira pa Genesis 3:15 pakhala nkhondo ya mbewu pakati pa Mulungu ndi Satana ndipo mbewu ya Satana ikumenyera ulamuliro, womwe sadzakhala nawo. Ndagona chigonjetso, ana anga, ndipo ndakupatsani mphamvu, ana anga, kulimbana ndi zoipa zamitundu yonse, ndipo palibe chimene chidzakulepheretseni mwanjira iliyonse; pakuti Ine, Yesu Khristu, ndagonjetsa ufumu wa mdani. Limbani nawo ndipo siyani kulimbikitsa njira zawo zoipa. Tulukani pakati pawo chifukwa adzipanga kuti azioneka ngati inu. Kodi simukhulupirira fanizo langa la tirigu ndi namsongole? Fanizo lina anawapatsa, nati; Ufumu wakumwamba ufanana ndi munthu amene anafesa mbewu yabwino m'munda mwake: Koma pamene anthu anali m'tulo, mdani wake anabwera nafesa namsongole pakati pa tirigu, napita njira yake. Tulukani pakati pawo ndipo mudzilekanitse ndi sunagoge wa Satana omwe amati ndi Ayuda koma akunama ndipo sali. Pakuti Isiraeli wamwazikana pakati pa Amitundu, ndipo sindinatumizidwe koma kwa nkhosa zotayika za nyumba ya Isiraeli; motero nthawi za Amitundu zatsala pang'ono kutha, ndipo ndapanga aŵiriwo kukhala munthu mmodzi watsopano mwa Ine, motero kupanga mtendere.

Ana anga, ndikukupatsani chidule cha mbiri yanu, pakuti mbiri ya anthu imachokera kwa Nowa mpaka kwa Adamu, ndipo Mulungu anapanga mitundu yonse kuchokera ku mwazi umodzi. Ana anga, nthawi yatsala pang'ono kutha, koma sizikutanthauza kuti ntchito yanu yatha. Ndikuwaitanabe ana anga kuti andiimire ndi kunditumikira. Pakuti ndakupangani kukhala atumiki oyenerera a Chipangano Chatsopano m'mwazi wanga. Kodi simunawerenge pamene ndinauza otsatira anga kuti ngati anganene kwa phiri ili kuti choka ndipo ponyedwa m'nyanja, ndipo ngati sakayikira, adzakhala ndi zinthu zomwe alamula? Ndikukulangizani kuti mubwerere vesi limodzi m'mbuyo, pakuti nkhaniyo imayamba ndi kukhala ndi chikhulupiriro mwa Mulungu (pakuti kwa Mulungu palibe chosatheka) ndipo simungathe kuchita kanthu kopanda Ine, tiana tanga.

Ambiri a inu mwaphunzitsidwa ndi Aedomu mabodza omwe amakutsogolerani m'njira zawo. Koma ngati muli ndi chikhulupiriro mwa Mulungu, mudzalamula zosatheka, ndipo mudzafunika kuchita zomwezo mu ntchito yomwe ndikukutumizani. Ndikukutumizani, ana anga, kuti musonkhanitse nkhosa zomwe zatsala, pakuti ambiri adzabwera kwa Ine mu mphindi zomalizazi ndipo sindidzawakana, koma ndikuwafunafunabe ndi mtima wonse. Tsegulani pakamwa panu mwachikhulupiriro ndipo ndiimireni ndi kutumikira ana anga.

Ndakuuzani momwe mungagwiritsire ntchito mphamvu zanga, koma mukukana malangizo anga, choncho ndidzawauzanso omwe amaiwala pakati panu. Chifukwa chiyani mukupempha mzimu mwamantha kuti usiye munthu wake ndi milomo yochita chibwibwi ndikugwiritsa ntchito dzina langa, Yesu Khristu, mopanda chikhulupiriro? Musagwiritse ntchito dzina langa pachabe! Lamulani mizimu m'dzina langa ndi chikhulupiriro chachikulu ndipo khalani ndi chikhulupiriro m'mawu omwe ndidzakupatsani, ndipo mizimu yoipa idzachoka ndi mphamvu zanga kudzera mwa inu!

Chifukwa chiyani mukupempha matenda mwamantha kuti achoke pamene muyenera kulankhula ndi matendawo molimba mtima m'dzina langa ndikulamula zosatheka m'dzina langa ndi kuchiritsa odwala. Nenani kwa phiri ilo, choka ndipo ponyedwa m'nyanja, chiritsani odwala, tulutsani mizimu, ukani akufa ndikulamula zosatheka, pakuti ndatero kukutheketsani.

Kodi simunawerenge kuti oyamba adzakhala omaliza, ndipo omaliza adzakhala oyamba? Dziwani, ana anga, kuti ndikuyenda mu mphamvu yayikulu kuposa momwe ndinachitira m'zaka za zana loyamba, ndipo ndathira mzimu wanga mokwanira padziko lonse lapansi. Ndani mwa inu ali ndi kulimba mtima kondiimira? Kundilankhulira mawu omwe ndikukupatsani? Ndani mwa inu ali ndi chikhulupiriro chosabwerera m'mbuyo chifukwa cha lipoti loipa koma kulimbana ndi choipacho pamaso ndi pamaso? Pakuti wamkulu ndi amene ali mwa inu kuposa amene ali m'dziko lapansi. Musakhale ogonjera kwa Akanaani, pakuti atembereredwa kuti akhale ogonjera kwa inu. Kuonjezera apo, musatengeke ndi mibadwo ya mitundu, pakuti ndatumizidwa ku mitundu yonse kuti ndipulumutse ena, ndipo potero, ndikupulumutsa Isiraeli weniweni, pakuti waponedwa pakati pa mitundu, ndipo inu nonse ndinu Isiraeli weniweni wa Mulungu!

Ndiimireni ndipo ndilankhulireni, pakuti nthawi yatsala pang'ono kutha. Kodi mudzakhala mmodzi wa omwe akuyandikira mpando wanga wachiweruzo atasonkhanitsa matabwa, udzu, ndi ziputu podikira tsiku lalikulu lomwe likubweralo? Kapena mudzandipatsa golide, siliva, ndi miyala yamtengo wapatali, pakuti anthu anga ndi chuma changa. Masulani amangwa ndipo gwirani ntchito ndi Ine mu mphindi zomalizazi. Ndipo mosakayikira, chinsinsi cha umulungu ndi chachikulu: Mulungu anaonekera m'thupi, anayesedwa wolungama mu Mzimu, anaonedwa ndi Angelo, analalikidwa kwa Amitundu, anakhulupiriridwa m'dziko lapansi, analandiridwa mu ulemerero.

Ana anga, Ine ndine Mawu amene anasanduka thupi, ndipo Ine ndi Atate wanga ndife amodzi. Mofananamo, khalani amodzi ndi ife mwa kumvera kwanu kwa Ine, pakuti posachedwapa mudzakhala ngati Ine ndipo mudzalandiridwa mu ulemerero, pakuti Ine ndine zipatso zoyamba za mwa akufa, ndipo ndidzakuperekani mpingo waulemerero, wopanda banga, kapena khwinya, kapena chinthu choterocho; koma kuti ukhale woyera ndi wopanda chilema. Nthawi yatsala pang'ono kutha, choncho musazengereze. Gwirani ntchito ndi Ine mu mphindi zomalizazi, ndipo musazengereze kutsegula pakamwa panu. Ndidzawabweretsa kwa inu, choncho chitani ntchito zanga ndipo siyani zanu. Ndidzachita ntchito zambiri zamphamvu ndi manja anu, kokha musakayikire!
