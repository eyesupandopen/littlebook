Mukhale ndi Chikhulupiriro mwa Ine

Imvani mawu anga, ana anga, ndipo mukhale ndi chikhulupiriro mwa ine, Ambuye Yesu Khristu. Ndakuitanani ku ulemerero ndi ukoma ndikuti muime pamaso panga mutayeretsedwa m'njira zanu m'magazi anga otetezera, pakuti magazi a mwanawankhosa amatsuka zonyansa zonse. Mwayeretsedwa mwa ine. Dziwani, ana anga, kuti palibe kutsutsidwa kwa iwo omwe ali mwa ine, ndiko kuti, mwa Khristu. Kukhala amodzi ndi ine ndi zolinga zanga ndiko kulowa m'chiyanjano ndi Atate, Yahweh, ndi ine, mwana wake Yeshua. Ndine Ambuye amene ndakuitanani maina anu ndikukupatsani chiitano cha moyo wosatha kumene sikudzakhalanso mavuto, kapena zowawa, kapena chisoni; pakuti ndidzapukuta misozi yonse m'maso mwanu m'chipulumutso changa kwa inu. Kodi ndingayambe ntchito osaimaliza? Kodi Mulungu wakumwamba ndi dziko lapansi angasiye chinthu chosachitika kapena kulephera pa malonjezo ndi mawu ake? Kodi simunawerenge, n'kosatheka kuti Mulungu aname? Zoonadi, lonjezo lililonse lidzachitika ndikukwaniritsidwa, choncho dziwani kuti chiwombolo chanu chayandikira — osati chiwombolo chochepa, koma chiwombolo chathunthu chopanda chosiyidwa ndipo chopanda chosakwanira.

Zinthu zonsezi zakwaniritsidwa, ndipo zosatheka zachitika mwa ine chifukwa ndinagonjetsa imfa kuti ndisafe n'komwe. Dziwani kuti ntchito iliyonse yoipa imene Satana wayesera kumanga m'miyoyo yanu ndidzaipasula. Inde, ndidzawononga ntchito zonse za Mdyerekezi; chifukwa cha ichi ndinawonetsedwa. Ndakupatsani umphumphu, ndipo ndidzakuchiritsani mpaka pomaliza kuchokera m'matupi anu owonongeka, ndipo mudzakhala ngati ine. Fufuzani Mawu anga, imvani Mawu anga, yerekezerani ndi Malemba, ndipo dziwani kuti chiwombolo chanu chathunthu chili pafupi ngati mpweya wanu wotsatira ndipo chidzachitika nthawi yomweyo. Imanipamphamvu chifukwa cha ine; lankhulani m'malo mwanga. Musachite chilichonse ndi opembedza mafano ndi achigololo omwe alowa m'Mpingo wanga ndipo omwe angayesere kusocheretsa ana anga ang'ono. Mkwiyo wanga ukhala pa iwo ndi pa aliyense amene akuukira anthu anga kuti awasocheretse.

Pakuti n'kosatheka kuti mulandidwe m'dzanja langa, ndipo omwe ndawachitira chifundo ndi omwe andipanga kukhala Ambuye wawo povomereza dzina langa lamphamvu, Yesu Khristu, monga Ambuye. Sindidzakhumudwitsa, ndipo sizinalowe mumtima mwa munthu zinthu zomwe zikukudikirirani, popeza ndidzakutengani posachedwa. Musalole mawu oipa kuvutitsa mitima yanu, ana anga, popeza mwapulumutsidwa ku mkwiyo wa Mulungu, ndipo okhulupirika anga sadzapirira nthawi ya zaka zisanu ndi ziwiri ya mkwiyo yomwe yatsala pang'ono kumasulidwa. Mitima yanu ikhale pamtendere ndikupuma mwa ine, ngakhale m'masautso anu ndi chizunzo pumani mwa ine, ndipo ndidzakupatsani mtendere wosafotokozeka. Chifundo changa ndi chisomo changa zikhalabe mpaka kalekale, ndipo ndalipira mtengo wa machimo anu, choncho pitirizani kutuluka mwa iwo. Ndikuwona chipwirikiti chomwe mukupirira. Ndikumva mawu onse aukali omwe akulankhulidwa kwa inu ndi za inu. Kuonjezera apo, ndikukulimbitsani m'kufooka kwanu, ndipo ndakupatsani mzimu wanga, ndipo ndinu amphamvu ndi aakulu mwa ine. M'mphamvu zanu nokha mudzalephera, choncho pumani m'mphamvu zanga, pakuti Atate wanga wandikweza pamwamba pa zonse.

Ndidzabwezera chilango kwa oipa posachedwa, ana anga, choncho musaope. Kubwezera kumeneku sikwanu, ndipo sikunapangidwe kwa ana a Mulungu koma kwa adani ake omwe alakkwira anthu ndi kwa anthu oipa omwe amapha ndi kufunkha mnzawo ndi m'bale wawo.

Inde, anthu oipa ambiri agwirizana ndi Satana chifukwa cha phindu laumbombo, ndipo ali ndi gawo lawo ndi mphoto yawo m'nyanja yamoto yokonzedwera Mdyerekezi ndi angelo ake. Kodi nonsenu sindinu mtundu umodzi, mtundu wa anthu? Komabe, dziwani kuti mkwiyo waukulu watsala pang'ono kumasulidwa ndipo kumwamba kwatsala pang'ono kuwululidwa padziko lapansi. Ntchito iliyonse yalembedwa. Mawu alionse opanda pake olankhulidwa alembedwa. Palibe chinsinsi chomwe chingabisidwe kwa ine, ndipo palibe pobisala pamaso panga. Ngakhale oipa akumba ngalande m'dziko lapansi kuthawa mkwiyo wanga womwe ukubwera, adzakumana ndi mapeto awo poyesera kundibisala.

Ndidzabwezera, ndipo ndidzabwezera chilango ndikukhazikitsa chilungamo kwa olakwiridwa. Ndili ndi lupanga lakuthwa konsekonse lotuluka mkamwa mwanga, ndipo palibe chinthu kapena munthu amene angandiletse, popeza ndine Mfumu ya Mafumu ndi Ambuye wa Ambuye. Ndani angakulekanitseni ndi chikondi cha Mulungu chomwe chili mwa ine? Sindinalipire machimo anu ndikukupatsani moyo wosatha kuti ndimasule mkwiyo ndi kutsutsidwa pa inu, okhulupirika anga. Palibe chinthu kapena munthu amene angatilekanitse, ndipo posachedwa mudzayang'ana Atate wanga ndi ine ndi maso anu. Ngakhale mwamanga chikhulupiriro chachikulu ndi chidaliro mwa ine, musanandiwonepo ndi maso anu, posachedwa mudzawona, ndipo mudzadziwa monga momwe mukudziwidwira. Ndani amene akukuvutitsani? Kodi sadzakhala wopanda pake ndipo ntchito zake zoipa zidzabwezedwa kwa iye mowirikiza? Anthu oipa adziunjikira mkwiyo waukulu, inde, mkwiyo waukulu chifukwa chozunza chilengedwe cha Atate wanga, ndipo ndidzabwezera, atero Ambuye.

Imani pamaso panga ndikupitiriza kudzilekanitsa ndi dziko loipali, lomwe likufa ndikulola ntchito yanga yotetezera kukutsukani, kukuyeretsani mpaka pomaliza. Taonani! Ndikupanga zinthu zonse kukhala zatsopano, ndipo machimo anu ndi zoyipa zanu sindidzazikumbukiranso. Choncho tulukani m'ntchito zanu ndikutenga ntchito zanga, pakuti potero mudzabweretsa umphumphu m'miyoyo yanu. Musaope oipa! Pakuti posachedwa ntchito zawo zidzamwazika ngati fumbi pamaso pa mphepo yamkuntho, ndipo ndidzapanga zinthu zonse kukhala zoyera ndi zatsopano. Pali mphoto yaikulu kwa okhulupirika anga ndi osankhidwa; ndipo posachedwa, inde, posachedwa kwambiri mudzakolola mphoto za ntchito zanu ngati simukomoka.

Imani m'malo mwanga mpaka mapeto, popeza iye amene ataya moyo wake mwa ine adzapeza moyo ndipo adzakolola chimwemwe chachikulu ndi chipulumutso ndi kusangalala mpaka kalekale pamaso pa Atate wanga. Ndakupangani kukhala mafumu ndi ansembe pamaso pa Atate wanga, popeza ndine mkulu wa ansembe wamuyaya mpaka kalekale monga mwa dongosolo la Melikizedeki. Imanipamphamvu m'chisomo changa ndikudziwa kuti posachedwa chitseko chidzatsekedwa, monga momwe Mulungu anatsekera Nowa ndi chuma chake mkati mwa Chingalawa. Momwemonso ndidzakusonkhanitsani kwa ine ndekha kumwamba. Pali chitseko chotseguka chomwe palibe munthu angatsegule kapena kutseka, ndipo ndidzakulandirani kwa ine ndekha, Mkwatibwi wanga. Choncho ndiyang'anireni popeza nthawi yatsala pang'ono kutha.

Ndine Ambuye wokhulupirika.

Ndine njira, choonadi, ndi moyo. Ndine mutu wa thupi.

Ndine mkwati kwa mkwatibwi, ndipo ndikuti bwerani!

Ndine amene sindingafenso, pakuti n'kosatheka kuti imfa indigwire.

Ndine amene ndakuitanani ku ulemerero, ukoma, ndi choonadi.

Ndine Ambuye Yesu Khristu, mwana wa Mulungu wowonetsedwa m'thupi amene anafera machimo anu.

Ndine amene ndauka tsiku lachitatu kuti ndisafe n'komwe.

Ndine amene Mulungu wandilandira kumwamba pamene atumwi anga ndi ophunzira anga ankandiwona ndikupita m'mitambo.

Ndine mkulu wa ansembe wamuyaya mpaka kalekale monga mwa dongosolo la Melikizedeki.
