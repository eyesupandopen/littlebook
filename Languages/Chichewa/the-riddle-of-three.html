Mwambi wa Atatu!

Imvani liwu la Yeshua HaMashiach, okondedwa anga, ndipo bwerani kwa ine kuti mutonthozedwe ndikupeza mpumulo m'chikondi changa pa inu, tiana twanga. Pakuti mwakhala okhulupirika pa zinthu zochepa, ndipo tsopano ndidzakupangani kukhala olamulira pa zambiri pamene mukulowa m'chimwemwe cha Ambuye wanu. Chiweruzo sichanu, okhulupirika anga; ngakhale onse amene ali mwa Khristu ndi onse amene agona mwa Khristu adzaukitsidwa ku moyo watsopano mwadzidzidzi ndi m'kuthwanima kwa diso. Pakuti zoonadi, ndidzatsika kuchokera kumwamba ndi mfuu, ndi liwu la Mngelo wamkulu, ndi lipenga la Mulungu: ndipo akufa mwa Khristu adzaukitsidwa ku moyo wosawonongeka ndipo inu amene muli amoyo ndi otsala mudzatengedwa pamodzi ndi iwo m'mitambo ndipo mudzakhala ndi Ambuye wanu wamuyaya nthawi zonse ndikukhala ngati Iye. Mudzasinthidwa nthawi yomweyo kuchoka ku mkhalidwe wanu wogwa wamakono kupita ku chilengedwe chowomboledwa kwathunthu chosawonongeka, ngakhale kulengedwa mwatsopano ngati munthu mmodzi watsopano, ndipo monga momwe ndiliri, inunso mudzakhala momwemo; ndipo monga kwalembedwa, kotero kudzachitika mwadzidzidzi.

Chifukwa chiyani mukulola mitima yanu kuvutika, ana anga? Chifukwa chiyani mukulola mawu a woneneza kukuvutitsani mpaka kuganiza kuti palibe kukwatulidwa, kapena kuti simuli oyenera chisomo changa chopulumutsa? Ndikukulangizani, ana anga, kuti mupitirize kuyenda ndi chikhulupiriro osati ndi kuona; ndipo ndine amene ndakupangani kukhala oyenera, ngakhale onse amene ali mwa Khristu ndipo ali ndi ubale wopulumutsa ndi Ambuye wawo wolungama, choncho musakayikire! Ngakhale, simunandione ndi maso anu akuthupi, mukudziwa bwino lomwe kuti ndine Ambuye ndi mpulumutsi wanu wamoyo, ndipo mwa ine muli chidzalo cha moyo ndi chipulumutso. Zoonadi, zoonadi, amene akhulupirira mwa ine sadzamvanso njala kapena ludzu mpang'ono pomwe.

Pakuti ndine mkate wamoyo, ndipo onse amene adya sadzamvanso njala; ndipo ndine amene mitsinje yamadzi amoyo imayenda kuchokera kwa ine mochuluka ndi moyo watsopano, ndipo amene amwa sadzamvanso ludzu. Koma inu nonse mudzapangidwa kukhala amoyo mu thanzi langwiro, mgwirizano wangwiro, ndi umodzi wangwiro ndi Yahweh, ndi Ambuye ndi mpulumutsi wanu wamoyo, pakuti ndine Khristu Yesu, amene ndinakugulani ndi magazi anga otetezera, ndipo inu ndinu amtengo wapatali ndithu. Ndipo kotero malipiro amtengo wapatali anaperekedwa omwe ndinapereka kwamuyaya mwa ine ndekha ngati mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu, ndipo kotero ndinapereka nsembe yanga m'malo mwanu ngati mkulu wa ansembe kwamuyaya monga mwa dongosolo la Melikizedeki ndikulipira dipo lanu ndi magazi anga opanda tchimo.

Ndipo kotero kuli kwamuyaya kuti palibenso chifukwa cha nsembe ya tchimo, chifukwa ngati magazi a ng'ombe ndi mbuzi, ndi phulusa la ng'ombe yayikazi, kuwaza iwo amene ali odetsedwa, kuyeretsa kukhudza kuyeretsedwa kwa thupi, Nanga magazi a Khristu amene kudzera mwa Mzimu wamuyaya anadzipereka yekha wopanda chilema kwa Mulungu, adzayeretsa bwanji chikumbumtima chanu ku ntchito zakufa, kuti mutumikire Mulungu wamoyo? Ndipo chimene nsembe ya nyama isanakwaniritse, magazi anga otetezera anatha kukwaniritsa kamodzi ndi kwamuyaya, monga ndapanga chitetezero cha machimo a anthu kamodzi ndi kwamuyaya, ngakhale kwa onse amene asankha kulandira chipulumutso m'dzina langa, pakuti palibe dzina lina loperekedwa pansi pa thambo limene anthu angapulumutsidwe nalo. Choncho musakhulupirire mabodza awo, pakuti chinjoka, chilombo, ndi mneneri wonyenga adzauka, ndipo adzafuna kugwirizanitsa zipembedzo zonse zonyenga zapadziko lapansi pansi pa mbendera yatsopano, kupatulapo Yesu Khristu sadzaloledwa pakati pawo, ngakhale m'dzina kapena m'zochita, ndipo kwaperekedwa kwa iwo kupanga nkhondo ndi oyera mtima ndi kuwagonjetsa. Zoonadi, zoonadi, ndilibe gawo kapena mbali ndi iwo, ndipo ndine amene ndidzawawononga kotheratu, chifukwa m'chilungamo ndikubwera kudzaweruza ndi kupanga nkhondo.

Ndipo tsopano ndikulankhula kwa iwo amene adzabwera kwa ine pa nthawi ya masautso a Yakobo, ngakhale oyera mtima a chisautso amene adzabwera ku mphoto yaikulu ndi cholowa chamuyaya mwa Ambuye ndi Mesiya wawo wamuyaya, ngakhale Yeshua HaMashiach. Ndipo kotero kwalembedwa ngati choonadi chamuyaya, ndipo kotero ndikunena kwamuyaya ndi nthawi zonse kuti muyenera kulowa pa chipata chopapatiza: pakuti chipata ndi chachikulu, ndi njira yotakata imene imatsogolera ku chiwonongeko: ndipo alipo ambiri amene amalowa pamenepo, Chifukwa chipata ndi chopapatiza, ndi njira yopapatiza imene imatsogolera ku moyo, ndipo alipo ochepa amene amachipeza. Choncho ndikukulamulani, tiana twanga, kuti mukhale pa njira yowongoka ndi yopapatiza ndikupewa chiwonongeko cha opusa, ngakhale iwo amene mayina awo sanalembedwe m'Buku la Moyo kuyambira pachiyambi cha dziko lapansi, chifukwa amakonda njira za chiwonongeko, ndipo amakonda chosalungama, ndipo chinyengo champhamvu amachikonda kuposa chilungamo ndi choonadi.

Pakuti adzathamangira uku ndi uku, ndipo nkhondo yotheratu ikubwera pa dziko lapansi, ndipo chiwonongeko chotheratu chikuwonekera ndipo chili pafupi; ngozi, chenjezo ladzidzidzi la 911, ndipo tsikulo likuyandikira kwambiri ndipo lili pa inu. Ndipo kuchokera m'phulusa la nkhondo adzauka, ndipo kuchokera m'phulusa la nkhondo adzadziwonetsera kwa anthu kachiwiri, ndipo anthu adzadabwa pambuyo pa ogwawo modabwa kwambiri ndi zidule zawo zomwe asocheretsa nazo otayika kuyambira pachiyambi. Adzagwirizanitsa zipembedzo zawo zonyenga, ndipo adzakuuzani mabodza ambiri olankhulidwa kuchokera m'kamwa mwa mizimu yonama ya wokana Khristu, pakuti okana Khristu ambiri alowa m'dziko.

Wokana Khristu ndi chiyani, ana anga? Wokana Khristu amatsutsana ndi Khristu, mpulumutsi woona ndi Mesiya, komanso m'malo mwa Khristu; pakuti ndine Yeshua HaMashiach, ndipo palibe wina. Ndipo kotero mabodza awo adzaza kale dziko lapansi pamene akonzekeretsa unyinji wa anthu kuti amvere atatu osayera, ngakhale chinjoka, chilombo chochokera kunyanja, ndi chilombo chochokera pansi, chomwe chilinso mneneri wawo wonyenga. Koma kodi Yohane analemba chiyani pa nkhaniyi m'kalata yake yoyamba? Sindinakulemberani, chifukwa simukudziwa choonadi: koma chifukwa mukuchidziwa, ndipo kuti palibe bodza limene lili la choonadi. Ndani ali wabodza, koma uyo amene akana kuti Yesu ndiye Khristu? Iye ndiye Wokana Khristu, amene akana Atate, ndi Mwana. Yense amene akana Mwana, yemweyo alibe Atate: koma uyo amene avomereza Mwana, ali ndi Atate yemwe.

Ndipo zili choncho, kuti Ine, Yesu wa ku Nazarete, ndine njira, choonadi, ndi moyo; ndipo palibe munthu aliyense amabwera kwa Yahweh koma kudzera mwa ine. Ndine khomo lokhalo, ndipo ndine njira yokhayo yopita kwa Yahweh, Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya; pakuti kotero Yahweh walengeza ndipo kotero Yahweh wakonza ndi kusankha kuyambira lisanakhazikitsidwe dziko lapansi m'kudziwiratu kwake kwamuyaya ndi nzeru zake. Ndani wa inu angathe kupereka uphungu kwa Yahweh, Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya? Ngakhale zikwi khumi kachulukiridwe ndi zikwi khumi azungulira mpando wake wachifumu, safuna mlangizi; ndipo ndi Yahweh, Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya, amene amagwira ntchito zinthu zonse monga mwa uphungu wa chifuniro chake kuti inu mukhale ku matamando a ulemerero wake amene munayamba kukhulupirira mwa Khristu.

Choncho mvetsetsani chinyengo chomwe chikubwera chomwe chafika kale pa dziko lapansi. Pakuti atumiki a Satana amalankhula motsutsana ndi choonadi, ndipo akufuula ponseponse ndikufuula mokweza kuti zipembedzo zonse zapadziko lapansi zimatsogolera kwa mulungu mmodzi mosasamala kanthu kuti ndi chikhulupiriro chanji, mosasamala kanthu za zikhulupiriro zawo, kuti mayina ena onse operekedwa ndi ogwawo ndi oona ndipo amatsogolera kwa mulungu woona, koma kwa chinjoka chokha ndi komwe amatsogolera. Zoonadi, zoonadi ndikukuuzani, musakhulupirire mabodza awo, ndipo musawafunire ngakhale zabwino omwe amakhulupirira zopusa zotere. Pakuti chipata chopita ku chiwonongeko ndi chachikulu, chomwe chimatsogolera ku nyanja yaikulu yamoto yoyaka, ndipo Satana akufuna kukankhira ambiri momwe angathere panjira yake yopita ku chiwonongeko chamuyaya, pakuti kotero ndi cholowa chake, ndipo kotero ndi chidani chake pa inu nonse. Musakhulupirire mabodza awo a mgwirizano wa zipembedzo, ndipo thawani ku mgwirizano wawo wosayera, pakuti mapeto ake ndi malawi amoto otentha kwambiri a buluu pamene sulufule woyaka akugubuduzika ndi kuyaka kwamuyaya, ndipo palibe amene adzathawa amene amangidwa ndi kuponyedwa m'moto.

Mvetsetsani, ana anga, kuti mayina awo onse amatsogolera kwa mulungu mmodzi, ngakhale Satana, pakuti iwo si milungu koma onyenga amene amanamizira. Ndipo kotero adzagwedeza mbendera zawo za kusiyana, ndipo adzanena kuti mayina ambiri amatsogolera kwa mulungu mmodzi. Ndipo mbali ina akunena zoona chifukwa njira zambiri zimatsogolera kwa mulungu wa dziko lino, amene chiweruzo chake chafika ndipo chilango chake ndi chiwonongeko chake sizikuchedwa. Ndipo mbali inayi, amanamiza dziko lapansi ndikufuna kukopa onse amene adzatsatira njira zawo zowononga m'gulu lawo latsopano lomwe lidzauka kuchokera m'phulusa la nkhondo lomwe lidzafuna chizindikiro kuti alowe.

Musatenge chizindikiro chawo, pakuti onse amene atenga atembereredwa kwamuyaya ndikusinthidwa kwamuyaya. Ndipo amanama momvetsa chisoni kuti mayina onse amatsogolera kwa Mulungu woona, ngakhale Yahweh, amene ali Mulungu wamuyaya wa Isiraeli, chifukwa Yahweh anasankha Yakobo kwa iye yekha kuti mukhale anthu apadera kwa iye yekha. Ndipo kotero Satana nthawi zonse amafuna kutsanzira njira za Mulungu mmodzi woona, ngakhale El Shaddai, amene ali Mulungu Wamphamvuyonse! Ndipo kotero wapanga zipembedzo zambiri zotsanzira zomwe zapangidwa kuti zitsanzire njira za Yahweh, Mulungu wamuyaya wa muyaya wonse, ndipo zikhulupiriro zonse zotsanzirazi zimathera mu chiwonongeko chamuyaya kwa iwo amene akodwa. Pakuti ndi mlenje uti amene amatchera msampha pamaso pa mbalame?

Ndipo kotero iwo amapereka kale madalitso kwa onse, ngakhale zipembedzo zonse, ndipo amafuna kale kugwirizanitsa zipembedzo zonse pansi pa mbendera zawo za mgwirizano wa zipembedzo, pakuti kotero njira zawo zakusokoneza maganizo zimakwaniritsidwa. Mbali ina amapereka lingaliro; mbali inayi amapereka chotsutsana nalo; ndipo kuchokera mu mkangano wolamuliridwa amabweretsa mgwirizano wawo, pakuti amafuna kugwirizanitsa zikhalidwe zonse, mitundu, ndi mafuko ndi ziyankhulo m'gulu lawo la satana kuti awononge onse amene amatsatira njira zawo zowononga. Ndipo kotero mvetsetsani, chomwe chili chokhotakhota sichingawongoledwe, ndipo mawu awo abwino ndi nkhani zawo zokongola zapangidwa kuti zikope malingaliro a anthu ndi zilakolako ndi chifundo chonyenga chomwe adzachitcha chiyero. Mvetsetsani kuti munthu mwa iye yekha ndi woipa kotheratu ndipo sangathe kuwongola njira yake, ndipo mulibe chabwino mwa munthu, koma munthu anaipitsidwa ndi kusamvera kwa Adamu ndipo makamaka ndi mabodza ndi chinyengo cha njoka, yomwe mutu wake ndidzauphwanya ndithu, pakuti Yahweh walengeza kuyambira pachiyambi mapeto a njoka yonamayo yomwe imadya fumbi la dziko lapansi.

Zakhala choncho, zili choncho, ndipo zidzakhala choncho, kuti palibe chatsopano pansi pa dzuwa. Ndipo pamene dziko likutembenuka kuchoka ku chisomo kupita ku chiweruzo, tsoka ladzidzidzi latsala pang'ono kugwa, ndipo lidzakhala mwadzidzidzi. Choncho ndikulangiza onse amene awerenga mawu awa kuti amvetsetse kuti mawu awa sanakupezeni mwangozi, koma atumizidwa ndi Yahweh mwiniwake kudzera mwa mwana wake wobadwa yekha, ngakhale Yeshuah HaMashiach, olankhulidwa mogwirizana ndi atumiki anga aneneri. Ndipo ndine, Yesu wa ku Nazarete, ngakhale Yeshua HaMashiach, amene ndinalipira mtengo wamuyaya wa chipulumutso chanu ndi chipulumutso cha onse amene adzabwera ku moyo wamuyaya m'chikondi changa chosatha ndi chamuyaya chomwe Yahweh anandilenga kuti ndikwaniritse. Ndikunena kwa inu chimodzimodzi monga ndanenera kuyambira pachiyambi: kuti Ine, Yesu Khristu, ngakhale Yeshua HaMashiach, ndine njira, choonadi, ndi moyo, ndipo palibe munthu amabwera kwa Atate koma kudzera mwa ine; ndipo palibe dzina lina loperekedwa pa dziko lapansi, nthawi zonse, limene anthu ayenera kupulumutsidwa nalo, ndipo mayina ena onse mosasamala kanthu kuti chotsanzira chikuwoneka chotsimikizika bwanji samatsogolera kwa Mulungu woona, ngakhale Atate wa Kuwala mwa amene mulibe kusinthasintha kapena mthunzi wotembenuka. Choncho mvetsetsani, tiana twanga, kuti inu ndinu kuwala kwa dziko lapansi komwe Yahweh wabala, ngakhale kulengedwa mwatsopano mwa ine, Khristu Yesu Ambuye wolungama wamuyaya, ndipo ndapangidwa kukhala mzimu wopatsa moyo.

Taonani, ndikuwonetsani mwambi womwe udzapereka nzeru kwa anzeru ndipo udzaletsa opusa ndi osaphunzira kugwirizana ndi omwe akupita ku chiwonongeko m'njira zawo. Atatu, awiri, mmodzi: ndipo pa zipembedzo zambiri zapadziko lapansi pali zitatu zomwe zimalamulira zomwe zidzatsogolera kwa mmodzi wonyenga wosayera, ndipo atatuwo amawoneka ngati adani ndipo amenya nkhondo zambiri. Komabe, pa atatuwo, awiri ndi ogwirizana mwachinsinsi, ndipo mmodzi adzagwiritsa ntchito winayo, ndipo onse awiri amatsogolera ku chiwonongeko. Pakuti pa atatuwo, awiri adzatembenukira mwankhanza kwa mmodzi, ngakhale woyera amene amamuda koma amene awagonjetsa kotheratu. Ndipo kotero woyerayo adzabweretsa chiweruzo ku nyumba ya Mulungu choyamba, chifukwa chiweruzo chimayambira pa nyumba ya Mulungu ndipo tsiku la chiweruzo lafika.

Ngakhale woyera wapambana, awiri a atatuwo adzatembenuka kuti awononge mmodziyo, koma chiwonongekocho n'chosatheka, ndipo mmodziyo wapeza kale chigonjetso chotheratu. Koma chinyengo chidzakhala chotero kuti awiri a atatuwo adzatembenukira mmodziyo ndikupanga milandu yonama m'kamwa mwa mboni zambiri zonyenga, ndipo adzapanga nkhondo ndi oyera mtima ndi kuwagonjetsa. Ndipo ndi mabodza ambiri amwano amafalikira pa dziko lapansi, koma mmodziyo, ngakhale woyera wa Mulungu, wawagonjetsa kale ndipo adzaphwanya mutu wawo kotheratu. Koma awiriwo adzawoneka opambana pa mmodzi amene akufuna kumuwononga, ndipo pa awiriwo adzapanga nkhondo pa mmodziyo, ngakhale chiwonongeko chawo chomaliza sichikuchedwa. Ndipo kotero msampha udzatcheredwa kwa dziko lapansi, kuti kuchokera mu chiwawa chachikulu ndi nkhondo adzadzigwirizanitsa okha kotero kuti atatu, awiri, mmodzi akhale awiri, mmodzi, atatu chifukwa adzadzigwirizanitsa okha kukhala chipembedzo chimodzi chonyenga chomwe chikutsogoleredwa ndi atatu osayera, ngakhale chinjoka, chilombo chochokera kunyanja, ndi chilombo chochokera pansi, chomwe chilinso mneneri wawo wonyenga.

Ndipo kotero ndikuwonetsani yankho la mwambi wanga wa atatu. Atatuwo ndi zipembedzo zitatu zazikulu zomwe zimalamulira dziko lapansi lomwe lilipo tsopano; ndipo pa atatuwo, mmodzi yekha ndi woona, ngakhale woyera wa Mulungu; pakuti Ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndine woyera wa Mulungu amene ndagonjetsa woipayo ndipo ndine Mbuye wa Ambuye ndi Mfumu ya Mafumu. Sindingagonjetsedwe, ndipo ndapatsidwa dzina loposa dzina lina lililonse, kuti pa dzina la Yesu Khristu, ngakhale Yeshua HaMashiach (pakuti ndine mpulumutsi wa Amitundu ndi Ayuda omwe) bondo lililonse lipinde, kumwamba ndi padziko lapansi ndiponso pansi pa dziko lapansi. Ndipo kotero zipembedzo ziwiri zonyenga zimawoneka zotsutsana koma zayesetsa kudziyanja kwa mmodziyo, ndipo zoonadi Satana watsanzira mpingo wanga woona ndi hule lonyenga lomwe limalemekeza ndi kupembedza mfumukazi yakumwamba. Ndipo kotero amafuna mgwirizano wonyenga ndipo asakaniza mabodza ambiri mu choonadi, kupatulapo mabodza ndi choonadi sizisakanizika: mwina muli ndi bodza kapena muli ndi choonadi: palibe chisankho china.

Ndipo pamene mpingo wanga woona utengedwa kumwamba ndipo woletsa achotsedwa, momwemonso mpingo wotsanzira udzawonongedwa, ndipo kotero mmodziyo amachotsedwa kotheratu. Zipembedzo ziwiri zotsalazo zidzauka mu chigonjetso chonyenga, ndipo adzakhala ndi mtendere wonyenga, ndipo umodzi wonyenga adzafuna kugwirizanitsa otayika, ngakhale kupereka moyo wamuyaya, thanzi langwiro, ndi tsiku latsopano chinjoka chidzapereka. Ndipo adzagwirizanitsa awiriwo kukhala chipembedzo chomwe makolo awo sanachidziwe koma chidzawoneka chimodzimodzi chifukwa anthu sadziwa zakale zawo zenizeni. Ndipo mwa mgwirizano uwu wa chiwonongeko adzapita kumoto wamuyaya, chifukwa onse amene agwirizana nawo ndi omwe amakondwera ndi chosalungama, ndipo osati izo zokha, koma omwe amakonda ndi kukhulupirira ndi kupanga ndi kupembedza mabodza chifukwa njira zawo ndi zonyenga ndi zopotoka ndipo njira zawo zimatsogolera ku imfa yachiwiri.

Ndipo kotero adzasocheretsa dziko, lomwe lidzadabwa pambuyo pawo, kuti, ndani angafanane ndi chilombo? Ndipo chilombo chimodzi chidzapatsidwa mphamvu ya chilombo chinacho, ndipo chilombo chochokera kunyanja chidzawalamulira monga mwa ulamuliro wake, ndipo chilombo chochokera pansi chidzalamulira dziko monga mwa ulamuliro wake, ndipo adzalamulira dziko lapansi m'chosalungama. Ndipo pa awiri otsalawo, adzagwirizana ngati mmodzi. Kupatulapo, njira zawo ndi imfa, ndipo pamenepo msampha watcheredwa, pakuti ndi mlenje uti amene amatchera msampha pamaso pa mbalame? Onse amene amatsatira njira zawo za mgwirizano wa zipembedzo adzagwirizanitsidwa, kokha mu cholowa cha ogwawo; ndipo onse amene adzigwirizanitsa okha kudzera mu kulumwa kwa njoka yawo adzagawana cholowa cha ogwawo m'nyanja yaikulu yamoto wa sulufule woyaka komwe amayaka kwamuyaya ndipo mphutsi yawo sifa. Ndipo kotero mgwirizano wawo wa zipembedzo wadzaza ndi misampha yomwe yabisidwa mochenjera, pakuti ndi mlenje uti amene amatchera msampha pamaso pa mbalame?

Choncho ndikulangiza onse amene awerenga mawu awa ndi onse amene ali ndi maso owona ndi makutu omva ndi mtima womvetsetsa, kuti chipulumutso ndi moyo wamuyaya ndi Mulungu woona, ngakhale Yahweh mwiniwake, zimapezeka mu dzina lina lililonse kupatulapo dzina la mwana wobadwa yekha wa Mulungu, ngakhale Yesu Khristu, amene alinso Yeshua HaMashiach; ndipo ndine mpulumutsi wa anthu onse, ngakhale mtundu wa Adamu omwe magazi awo mtundu wonse wa anthu umagawana kwathunthu. Ndipo mu lilime lililonse, ndi kuchokera ku mtundu uliwonse, onse akhoza kubwera mwaulere ndikulandira mphatso yanga ya chipulumutso chamuyaya poyitana pa dzina langa loyera mpaka mapeto. Pakuti ndine Ambuye ndi mpulumutsi wanu, kokha musalole dzina langa kuchoka pamilomo yanu, ndipo ndidzakupulumutsani kotheratu.

Musalowe mu mgwirizano wawo wa zipembedzo, pakuti sadziwa chomwe amapembedza, ndipo asocheretsedwa ndi mabodza omwe adzawatsogolera ku chiwonongeko chawo chamuyaya. Ndipo zili choncho kuti chipulumutso chimapezeka mu dzina lina lililonse, ndipo palibe dzina lina lomwe laperekedwa komwe anthu angapulumutsidwe ndipo kuti anthu angayandikire Yahweh, pakuti amene alibe mwana alibe Atate. Ndipo kotero ndidzathandiza ndi kulimbikitsa onse amene ayitana pa dzina langa loyera mpaka mapeto. Ndipo mudzandichitira ntchito zazikulu, ndipo ndikukutumizani kuti mukonde Ambuye Mulungu wanu ndi mtima wanu wonse, moyo, maganizo, ndi mphamvu zanu zonse ndi kukonda mnansi wanu ngati inu mwini ndi kuthandizana ndi kukondana wina ndi mzake m'nthawi zoyipitsitsa zomwe zatsala pang'ono kugwa pa dziko lapansi.

Chotero atatu, awiri, mmodzi, pamene awiri adzaukira mmodzi, ndipo awiri otsalawo adzaphatikizana kukhala mmodzi ndipo adzalamuliridwa ndi atatu oyipawo, omwe adzalamulira omwe mayina awo sanalembedwe m'Buku la Moyo chisanakhazikitsidwe maziko a dziko lapansi. Koma musagwirizane nawo, oyera mtima anga; pakuti n'kwabwino kutaya moyo wanu kuti muupeze kusiyana ndi kukonda moyo wanu koma ndikuutaya kosatha m'chiwonongeko chosatha. Taonani! Kupatukana komaliza kwatsala pang'ono kuyamba ndipo nthawi yakwana. Musawaope, ana anga ang'ono, koma dalirani Ambuye ndi Mpulumutsi wanu wamuyaya. Ndine thandizo lamuyaya pa nthawi ya masautso, ndipo ndidzapulumutsabe khamu la makamu pa nthawi ya masautso a Yakobo, ndipo ndidzapulumutsabe otsala a Isiraeli, ndipo palibe amene angathe kukukwatulani m'manja mwanga.

Zindikirani kuti chiyembekezo chanu ndi chiyembekezo chamuyaya, ndipo sindinakusiyeni opanda chiyembekezo, ndipo ndakusiyirani chitsanzo chamuyaya. Pakuti kwalembedwa za ine mu Ahebri: Tiyang'ane kwa Yesu woyambitsa ndi womaliza wa chikhulupiriro chathu, amene chifukwa cha chimwemwe choikidwa patsogolo pake, anapirira mtanda, nanyoza manyazi, ndipo wakhala kudzanja lamanja la mpando wachifumu wa Mulungu. Chotero zindikirani kuti chitsanzo changa cha kumvera chikumveka ngati chitsanzo cholimbikitsa chikhulupiriro chanu kuti mupirire mpaka mapeto, monga momwe inenso ndinapirira mpaka mapeto pamene ndinanena mawu omaliza pamtanda akuti: Kwatha. Ndipo chotero Mulungu anandiukitsa kwa akufa chifukwa chinali chosatheka kuti imfa indigwire, pakuti magazi anga ndi osalakwa.

Ndipo chotero ndakupatsani chiyembekezo chachikulu, ana anga ang'ono, ngakhale kuti mukhazikitse maganizo anu pa chimwemwe choikidwa patsogolo panu chifukwa masautso a nthawi ya masautso a Yakobo sangafanane kapena kukhala oyenera kutchulidwa poyerekeza ndi chimwemwe chamuyaya choikidwa patsogolo panu. Taonani! Kwalembedwa ndi Yohane Wovumbulutsa: Awa ndiwo amene anatuluka m'chisautso chachikulu, ndipo anatsuka mikanjo yawo, naiyeretsa m'magazi a Mwanawankhosa. Chifukwa chake ali pamaso pa mpando wachifumu wa Mulungu, ndipo amamtumikira usana ndi usiku m'Kachisi wake: ndipo iye wokhala pa Mpando wachifumu adzakhala pakati pawo. Sadzamvanso njala, kapena ludzu, ndipo Dzuwa silidzawawotcha, kapena kutentha kulikonse. Pakuti Mwanawankhosa, amene ali pakati pa mpando wachifumu, adzawadyetsa, ndipo adzawatsogolera ku akasupe a madzi amoyo: ndipo Mulungu adzapukuta misozi yonse m'maso mwawo. Adzakhala Ansembe a Mulungu, ndi a Khristu, ndipo adzalamulira naye zaka chikwi.

Ndipo chotero ndi chiyembekezo chanu, okondedwa anga, kuti mupirire mpaka mapeto. Ndipo chanu ndi chiyembekezo chamuyaya chifukwa El Shaddai, Mulungu Wamphamvuyonse, adzalenga kumwamba kwatsopano ndi dziko lapansi latsopano momwe mukukhala chilungamo, ndipo mudzapatsidwa mwayi wolowa mumzinda woyerawo wokonzedwa ndi kukongoletsedwa ndi Yahweh mwiniwake ngati mkwatibwi wokonzekera mkwati, ndipo mudzadya za mtengo wa moyo. Ndipo monga kwalembedwa: Iye amene apambana, adzalandira zinthu zonse, ndipo Ine ndidzakhala Mulungu wake, ndipo iye adzakhala mwana wanga.

Ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndine amene ndatumiza mawu anga kudzera mwa mtumiki wanga, ndipo kudzera mwa mtumiki wanga mawu anga atumizidwa kuti onse amene awerenga amvere. Ndipo ndavumbulutsa uthenga wabwino wamuyaya, pakuti nthawi yakuuvumbulutsa yakwana.

Ndine amene ndikubwera kudzachitira omwe ali mwa Khristu ndipo ndidzapulumutsa okhulupirika mu mpingo wanga ku moto wa woyenga ndi mkwiyo womwe ukubwera.

Ndine amene ndimalekanitsa tirigu ndi namsongole, ndipo palibe ndi mmodzi yemwe wa inu angakwatulidwe m'dzanja langa.

Ndiimireni, oyera mtima anga a m'chisautso, ndipo bwerani kwa ine, inu Isiraeli, pakuti ndine Yeshua HaMashiach, Mesiya wanu weniweni, ndipo ndakupatsani chiyembekezo chachikulu ndi mphamvu yayikulu. Ndipo ambiri a inu mudzandichitira ntchito zamphamvu, ndipo palibe ndi mmodzi yemwe wa inu amene sadzalandira mphotho, pakuti ngakhale zinthu zing'onozing'ono zomwe mumachita pa nthawi ya masautso aakulu zimabweretsa mphotho yamuyaya, ulemerero womwe masautso anu sali oyenera kutchulidwa; chotero itanani pa dzina langa lamphamvu, anthu anga! Chipulumutso ndi mphotho yayikulu zili m'dzanja langa kwa omwe apambana, pakuti chotero Yeshua HaMashiach akulonjeza kwa okhulupirika.

Taonani, nthawi yakwana!
