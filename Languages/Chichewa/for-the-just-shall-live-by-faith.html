Epulo 27, 2024

Pakuti Wolungama adzakhala ndi moyo ndi Chikhulupiriro!

Pakuti wolungama adzakhala ndi moyo ndi chikhulupiriro! Galamukani, ndipo galamutsani abale anu, mpingo wanga wofunda wa Laodikaya, womwe wayiwala zinsinsi zanga; pakuti nthawi yapita ndipo nthawi yatsala pang'ono kutha. Bwerani kwa ine, ana anga, ndi manja otseguka ndi mtima wotseguka (pakuti manja anga ndi mtima wanga zili zotseguka kwa inu); bwerani kwa ine ndi mtima wofewa ndi wokonzeka kulandira mawu anga. Pakuti m'bale wanga Yakobo anati chiyani? Chifukwa chake tayani chidetso chonse, ndi kuchuluka kwa zoipa, ndipo landirani ndi chifatso mawu obzalidwa, omwe amatha kupulumutsa miyoyo yanu. Ana anga, khalani otsatira anga, pakuti ndine wofatsa ndi wodzichepetsa mtima, ndipo landirani ndi chifatso Mawu obzalidwa, omwe ndawayika pakati panu kuti mukhale ndi maso openya ndi makutu akumva. Tembenuzirani mitima yanu kwa ine, pakuti ndine njira, choonadi, ndi moyo, ndipo palibe amene amabwera kwa Atate koma kudzera mwa ine, pakuti chifuniro cha Atate wanga chafotokozedwa momveka bwino, ndipo iye wachita zimenezo.

Chifukwa chiyani mukulola mitima yanu kuvutika, ana anga? Chifukwa chiyani mukupitirizabe kupatuka pa mawu anga ndi kusocheretsa ena m'cholakwa chanu? Pakuti inu, mpingo wanga wa Laodikaya, mwayiwala kuti ndinu ndani mwa ine, Khristu Yesu, ndipo mwataya zinsinsi zanga, ngakhale ndinakupatsani. Pakuti ndine Ambuye amene ndinakugulani ndi magazi anga; ndine Mbuye wa ambuye ndi Mfumu ya mafumu amene ndalandira dziko lapansi kuchokera kwa Atate wanga, ndipo ndidzagawana cholowa changa ndi okhulupirika ndi osankhidwa; ndidzagawana cholowa changa ndi inu, Mkwatibwi wanga! Choncho musaope, pakuti ngakhale ndinu osauka, ndinu olemera mwa ine, pakuti Atate wapereka m'manja mwanga zinthu zonse. O, mpingo wa Laodikaya, mwakhala mkazi wopanda chikhulupiriro wogonjera zolakwika.

Ayi, simusamala za nyama ya mawu anga, ndipo simunditsatira ndi mtima wanu wonse; kodi simunawerenge Miyambo? Kumvetsa kwabwino kumapeza ulemu, koma njira ya osakhulupirika ndi yovuta. Ndipo njira zogonjera zomwe mwasankha ndi zovuta; njira zanu zopanda chikhulupiriro sizochokera kwa ine, ndipo mukuyesetsa kuyenda m'njira yomwe ndakupatsani ndi phazi limodzi m'njira ndi lina m'ngalande, komabe muli ndi kulimba mtima kundimba mlandu ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, pamene mugwera m'ngalande monga momwe mwatsogoleredwera. Pakuti ulendo wanu uli ndi kugwa kambiri, ndipo chiyembekezo changa kwa inu n'chakuti kugwa kwanu kuli ngati kugwa kwa wolungama: Pakuti munthu wolungama amagwa kasanu ndi kawiri ndikudzukanso: koma oipa adzagwera m'tsoka.

Sindinakuitaneni kuti mugwe m'tsoka, ana anga, ngakhale inu, mpingo wanga wa Laodikaya, pakuti sindinakusiyeni ku nthawi za mayesero pakali pano. Pakuti taonani! Ndikuyimabe pakhomo ndikugogoda chifukwa sindidzatenga nawo mbali m'njira zanu zofunda, zogonjera, koma ndikukuitanirani ku chilungamo. Ndikukuitanirani kuti muime m'chilungamo, pakuti abusa osokera akupusitsani, kukutsogolerani m'njira za Balamu; ndipo ambiri a inu mumanenera monama ndi mizimu ya Yezebeli, omwe angafooketse mawu a aneneri anga oona ndikufuna kupha aneneri omwe ndawapatula mwa ine, pakuti ndine amene ndapereka mphatso kwa anthu. Pakuti ndinalembera Agalatiya pamene anapunthwa monga momwe inu mukupunthwira: Ndikudabwa, kuti mwamsanga chotere mukuchotsedwa kwa iye amene anakuitanani m'chisomo cha Khristu, kupita ku Uthenga Wabwino wina: Womwe suli wina. Inde, werenganinso zimenezo, pakuti palibe Uthenga Wabwino wina ndipo palibe dzina lina loperekedwa limene anthu adzapulumutsidwa nalo kupatula langa, Yesu Khristu. Chifukwa chiyani mukusocheretsedwa kuchoka kwa ine, ana anga? Kodi sindikukupatsani ndi kukusamalirani, pakuti ndine mbusa wabwino amene anapereka moyo wake chifukwa cha nkhosa. Koma mawu omwe sindinalankhule, omwe mukuwalakalaka ndi kuwatsatira, akukuyabwani m'makutu; pakuti kumverera kumeneko kwakhala chikondi chanu choyamba m'njira zanu zodzitukumula; pakuti mumasunga mabodza kwa inu nokha ngati kuti ndi choonadi, ndipo mwataya kuzindikira konse.

Kodi mwayiwala mbiri yoyambirira ya mpingo? Kodi mwakhala aulesi kwambiri kuti muphunzire? Kodi nkhani iyi sikukuyabwani m'makutu mokwanira kuti mumve mawu anga ndi kukhala ndi chikhulupiriro mwa iwo? Pakuti munthu sadzakhala ndi moyo ndi mkate wokha koma ndi MAWU onse amene amatuluka mkamwa mwa Mulungu! Mpingo wanga unabadwa pa tsiku la Pentekoste ndipo walembedwa m'Machitidwe kuti muwunikenso. Pakuti mpingo wanga unayambira mu Isiraeli, ku Yudeya, ngakhale sanamvetsetse mawu omwe ndinalankhula kudzera mwa Petulo pa tsiku laulemerero limenelo pamene ndinatsanulira mzimu woyera pa osankhidwa anga, pamene analankhula ndi malilime ena monga mzimu unawapatsa kulankhula! Pakuti Petulo anauza chiyani khamu la anthu ku Yerusalemu tsiku limenelo pamene ananenera m'malo mwanga? Pakuti lonjezo lili kwa inu, ndi kwa ana anu, ndi kwa onse amene ali kutali, ngakhale onse amene Ambuye Mulungu wathu adzawaitana. Ana anga, inu ndinu amene muli kutali, pakuti ambiri a inu sindinu a Isiraeli monga mwa thupi, koma ndakutengani kuchokera ku mitundu m'chisomo changa kuti muime m'chifundo changa, pakuti mwaitanidwa.

Kodi simunawerenge? Koma tsopano mwa Khristu Yesu, inu amene nthawi ina munali kutali, mwayandikitsidwa ndi magazi a Khristu. Koma m'nyumba zanu zopembedzera zogonjera zomwe mumazipereka mwamwano m'dzina langa (ngakhale sindidzalowa koma kuima pakhomo ndikugogoda), mukuganiza kuti mwalandira chilamulo cha Mose kuchokera kwa ine, ndipo m'kudzikuza kwanu simukumvetsa kuti Mose anali wa Isiraeli, ndipo inali Isiraeli yomwe inapatsidwa Torah kuti isunge, udindo womwe analephera; pakuti palibe munthu amene angathe kusunga chilamulo cha Mose chifukwa cha chivundi chomwe njoka inayambitsa m'thupi lanu, pakuti chidziwitso chake nthawi zonse chimaipitsa, ndipo mphoto ya uchimo ndi imfa. Pakuti Akorinto akuchitira umboni mawu anga: Amene anatipanganso kukhala atumiki okhoza a Chipangano Chatsopano, osati a chilembo, koma a mzimu: pakuti chilembo chimapha, koma mzimu umapereka moyo. Mwaitanidwa kuyenda mumzimu, ngakhale ambiri a inu mwalakwitsa kulakalaka chilembo cha chilamulo, chomwe sichanu, koma chakwaniritsidwa mwa ine.

Ndiyankheni izi: Kodi mzimu unabwera kwa inu mwa kukwaniritsa kwanu kwa Torah? Kapena unabwera mwa kukwaniritsa kwanga kolungama kwa Torah monga Paskha wanu wangwiro? Pakuti umboni wa Agalatiya ukulira chiyani? Ichi chokha ndikufuna kuphunzira kwa inu, kodi munalandira mzimu, ndi ntchito za Chilamulo, kapena ndi kumva kwa chikhulupiriro? Ndipo kachiwiri Paulo akuti kwa Agalatiya: Sindikugwiritsa mwala chisomo cha Mulungu: pakuti ngati chilungamo chibwera kudzera m'Chilamulo, ndiye kuti Khristu anafera pachabe. Kodi simukumvetsa kuti pachiyambi cha nthawi panali mpingo wa Ayuda ndi mpingo wa Akunja? Ngakhale mpingo wanga unabadwira ku Yudeya, Paulo anauza Agalatiya ndipo ndinachisunga pamenepo: Pakuti iye amene anagwira ntchito mwamphamvu mwa Petulo ku Utumwi wa mdulidwe, yemweyo anali wamphamvu mwa ine kwa Akunja. Pakuti poyamba ndinatumiza Petulo kwa Akunja, ndipo zalembedwa m'Machitidwe pamene ndinauza Petulo: Chimene Mulungu wayeretsa, usachitche chonyansa. Pakuti pamene ndinatumiza Petulo kwa Koneliyo, umboni wotsatirawu unalembedwa: Ndipo iwo a mdulidwe amene anakhulupirira, anadabwa, onse amene anabwera ndi Petulo, chifukwa chakuti pa Akunja naponso panatsanuliridwa mphatso ya Mzimu Woyera. Ndikukufunsani izi, inu a Laodikaya, kodi Koneliyo analandira chipulumutso potsatira Torah (pakuti anali munthu wodzipereka)? Kapena analandira lonjezo kudzera m'chikhulupiriro? Akorinto ali ndi mawu pa nkhaniyi pamene Paulo anawalembera: Mwa ichinso mwapulumutsidwa, ngati musunga m'chikumbukiro chimene ndinalalikira kwa inu, pokhapokha ngati munakhulupirira pachabe. Chipulumutso chinabwera mwa kulalikira kwa chikhulupiriro osati mwa chilembo cha chilamulo, pakuti kudzibweretsa wekha pansi pa chilamulo cha Mose, ngakhale chikhulupiriro chanu chili chopanda pake.

Ndipo motero Aroma akulengeza: Koma tsopano chilungamo cha Mulungu chopanda Chilamulo chaonetsedwa, chochitiridwa umboni ndi Chilamulo ndi Aneneri. Ngakhale chilungamo cha Mulungu, chomwe chili mwa chikhulupiriro cha Yesu Khristu kwa onse, ndi pa onse amene akhulupirira: pakuti palibe kusiyana: Pakuti onse achimwa, ndipo aperewera pa ulemerero wa Mulungu. Pakuti ndinauza mpingo wanga m'Agalatiya momwe Paulo analemba momvera: Pakuti mwa Yesu Khristu, mdulidwe supindula kanthu, kapena kusadulidwa, koma chikhulupiriro chomwe chimagwira ntchito mwa chikondi. Ndipo kachiwiri kwa Agalatiya Paulo analemba: Pakuti mwa Khristu Yesu mdulidwe supindula kanthu kapena kusadulidwa, koma cholengedwa chatsopano. Choncho chipulumutso sichiperekedwa ndi ntchito za chilamulo, kapena chipulumutso chanu sichikhalidwa ndi kukwaniritsidwa ndi ntchito za chilamulo. Pakuti kwa Aroma kunalembedwa: Taonani, ndiyika mu Ziyoni mwala wopunthwitsa, ndi thanthwe lochimwitsa: ndipo aliyense amene akhulupirira mwa iye, sadzachita manyazi. Ana anga, ndine mwala wopunthwitsa ndi thanthwe lochimwitsa, pakuti ndine Yesu wa ku Nazarete, mwanawankhosa weniweni wa Mulungu, ndipo ndakwaniritsa chilamulo.

O, inu a Laodikaya, kodi mukuganiza kuti ndinu nokha amene mukupunthwa pa nkhani iyi? Ndikuti ayi. Pakuti mukhoza kugawa makalata anga m'magulu awiri akuluakulu, kupatula kuti onse alembedwa kwa munthu mmodzi watsopano mwa Khristu Yesu. Pakuti makalata anga alembedwa kwa mipingo ya Akunja, ndipo ena alembedwa kwa mipingo ya Ayuda, ndipo motero Petulo analemba za vumbulutso la Chinsinsi chomwe ndinavumbulutsa kwa Paulo: Monganso m'Makalata ake onse, polankhula mwa iwo za zinthu izi, m'mene muli zinthu zina zovuta kumvetsa, zomwe osaphunzira ndi osakhazikika amazipotoza, monga momwe amachitiranso Malemba ena, ku chiwonongeko chawochawo.

Choncho vuto lanu silachilendo, pakuti palibe chiyeso chomwe chakugwerani koma chotero chomwe chili chofala kwa munthu: koma Mulungu ndi wokhulupirika, amene sadzalola kuti muyesedwe kuposa momwe mungathere; koma pamodzi ndi chiyeso adzapanganso njira yopulumukira, kuti muthe kupirira. Kodi mudzatenga njira yopulumukira yomwe Mulungu wakupatsani m'chisomo changa ndi chifundo changa kuti mupulumuke kwa omwe angakubereni mphoto yanu pokupangitsani kugwa kuchoka m'chisomo? Kodi mudzakana mawu anga m'malo mwa Ayuda omwe mosazindikira amati muyenera kusunga Torah kuti mupulumuke? Kodi ndinu opusa mokwanira kuganiza kuti, mutayamba chilamulo cha moyo mumzimu wa Yesu Khristu, tsopano mwaperekedwa ku chilamulo cha uchimo ndi imfa? Nanga bwanji za chilamulo? Ana anga, ndikuuzani. Musaganize mopusa kuti ndine liwu lonyenga lomwe likukutsogolerani m'cholakwa, pakuti ambiri a inu simukudziwa liwu langa ndipo simukundidziwa, ngakhale ndikukuitanani! Musaganize kuti ndabwera kudzawononga chilamulo kapena aneneri. Sindinabwere kudzawononga, koma kudzakwaniritsa. Pakuti indetu ndinena kwa inu, Kufikira kumwamba ndi dziko lapansi zipita, yod imodzi kapena munga wa yod, sizidzachoka m'chilamulo, kufikira zonse zitakwaniritsidwa.

Ndine amene ndakwaniritsa chilamulo cha Mose, ndipo malangizo ake amasungidwa pomvera mawu anga a chisomo ndi chifundo m'makalata anga ndi kudzera m'chikhulupiriro cha Yesu Khristu. Mukuganiza mosazindikira chifukwa chakuti ndine kukwaniritsidwa kwa chilamulo kuti ndikulimbikitsa aliyense kuphwanya malamulo a Mulungu, kaya chilamulo cha Mose kapena malamulo a makhalidwe a Mulungu. Pakuti ndinauza chiyani omwe amakhala ku Korinto? Mpingo wanga wa Laodikaya, womwe ndimaukonda, sindinu oyamba kuthana ndi chiyeso ichi, ndipo motero ndinapangitsa Paulo kulemba: Amene anatipanganso kukhala atumiki okhoza a Chipangano Chatsopano, osati a chilembo, koma a mzimu: pakuti chilembo chimapha, koma mzimu umapereka moyo. Koma ngati utumiki wa imfa wolembedwa, ndi wojambulidwa m'miyala, unali waulemerero, kotero kuti ana a Isiraeli sakanatha kuyang'anitsitsa nkhope ya Mose, chifukwa cha ulemerero wa nkhope yake, ulemerero womwe unayenera kuthetsedwa: Kodi utumiki wa mzimu, sudzakhala waulemerero koposa? Musalakwitse, ana anga, chilembo chimapha chifukwa cha uchimo m'thupi lanu womwe umatsutsidwa ndi chilembo chomwecho, ngakhale chilamulo cha Mose: Choncho chilamulo ndi choyera, ndipo lamulo ndi loyera ndi lolungama ndi labwino. Kodi pamenepo chimene chili chabwino, chinakhala imfa kwa ine? Mulungu aletse. Koma uchimo, kuti uwonekere kukhala uchimo, ukugwira ntchito imfa mwa ine ndi chimene chili chabwino: kuti uchimo kudzera m'Lamulo ukhale wochimwa kwambiri. Ndipo motero ndinapangitsa Paulo kuuza Agalatiya: Sindikugwiritsa mwala chisomo cha Mulungu: pakuti ngati chilungamo chibwera kudzera m'Chilamulo, ndiye kuti Khristu anafera pachabe. Ana anga, mwasocheretsedwa, ndipo ndinakuuzani kuti vinyo watsopano ayenera kuikidwa m'matumba a vinyo atsopano, choncho imani chilili m'chisomo chomwe ine, Khristu Yesu, ndakumasulani nacho.

Ana anga, simungaphwanye malamulo a Mulungu pamene mukuyenda mumzimu, pakuti n'zosatheka. Mwamangidwa pa maziko a atumwi ndi aneneri, Yesu Khristu mwiniwake pokhala mwala wapakona waukulu. Ndipo kwa Ahebri kunanenedwa: Pakuti ndidzakhala wachifundo ku chosalungama chawo, ndipo machimo awo ndi zoyipa zawo sindidzazikumbukiranso. Pamenepo akuti, chipangano chatsopano, wapanga choyamba kukhala chakale. Tsopano chimene chikuwola ndi kukalamba chakonzeka kutha. Ana anga, chilungamo sichibwera posunga chilamulo, pakuti ndi mphatso yolandiridwa ndi chikhulupiriro mwa ine, Khristu Yesu. Ndipo chifukwa mwapangidwa olungama mwa ine, mwamasulidwa ku chilamulo cha uchimo ndi imfa.

Pakuti ndi mwano kuganiza kuti kuyenda mumzimu wa moyo ndi kuchimwa, pakuti aliyense amene amakhulupirira zoterezi amaona nsembe yanga chifukwa cha machimo a anthu kukhala chinthu chosayera. Pakuti ndine wamkulu kuposa Mose, pakuti Mose mwiniwake akuti: Ambuye Mulungu wanu adzakuutsirani Mneneri pakati panu, wa abale anu, ngati ine, kwa iye mudzamvera. Mverani liwu langa, ana, ndipo mverani kulankhula kwanga, pakuti sindinakupangeni kukhala opanda chilamulo, pakuti ana a Satana ndi opanda chilamulo, osati ana a Mulungu! Pakuti m'Aroma Paulo analemba momveka bwino: Choncho tsopano palibe kutsutsidwa kwa iwo amene ali mwa Khristu Yesu, amene sayenda monga mwa thupi, koma monga mwa mzimu. Pakuti chilamulo cha mzimu wa moyo, mwa Khristu Yesu, chandimasula ku chilamulo cha uchimo ndi imfa.

Pakuti chimene chilamulo sichikanatha kuchita, chifukwa chinali chofooka kudzera m'thupi, Mulungu potumiza Mwana wake, m'chifanizo cha thupi lochimwa, ndi chifukwa cha uchimo anatsutsa uchimo m'thupi: Kuti chilungamo cha chilamulo chikwaniritsidwe mwa ife, amene sitiyenda monga mwa thupi, koma monga mwa mzimu. Ndipo kachiwiri Aroma akulankhula: Kulengeza, ndikuti, pa nthawi ino chilungamo chake: kuti iye akhale wolungama, ndi wolungamitsa wa iye amene akhulupirira mwa Yesu. Kuli kuti kudzitamandira, pamenepo? Kwachotsedwa. Ndi Chilamulo chiti? Cha ntchito? Ayi: koma ndi Chilamulo cha chikhulupiriro. Choncho, tikumaliza, kuti munthu alungamitsidwa ndi chikhulupiriro, popanda ntchito za Chilamulo. Pakuti ndanena chiyani: Pakuti mmenemo chilungamo cha Mulungu chavumbulutsidwa kuchokera ku chikhulupiriro kupita ku chikhulupiriro; monga kwalembedwa, Wolungama adzakhala ndi moyo ndi chikhulupiriro.

Komabe ambiri a inu mukuika mitolo yosayenera pa wina ndi mnzake chifukwa mwakhulupirira aneneri a Satana omwe atumizidwa kudzakupherani msampha kuti musunge Torah monama monga chofunikira cha chipulumutso komanso kukhala ndi moyo wa chipulumutso; ndipo ena a inu mumanena mwamwano kuti ndinakwaniritsa chilamulo ndi nsembe yanga ya ine ndekha kuti tsopano muthe kusunga Torah. Kumbali imodzi, mukunena zoona, chifukwa kuyenda m'chilamulo cha mzimu wa moyo ndiko kusunga Torah mwangwiro mwa kudziwika kwanu ndi ine; pakuti mpingo wanga wamangidwa molimba m'chilungamo ndi chiyero choona pa ine, Khristu Yesu, mwala wapakona waukulu. Mumasunga Torah pomvera chikhulupiriro chomwe chili mwa ine. Chifukwa chiyani mumasankha liwu la Ayuda m'malo mwa liwu langa loyera? Chifukwa chiyani mukuika mitolo yosayenera pa abale anu, pakuti kodi simunawerenge? Pakuti ndinauza kale Akolose: Choncho munthu asakuweruzeni m'chakudya, kapena m'chakumwa, kapena pa nkhani ya tsiku Loyera, kapena la Mwezi watsopano, kapena la masiku a Sabata Zomwe zili mthunzi wa zinthu zikubwera, koma thupi ndi la Khristu.

Ana anga, sinditsutsa chilamulo; ndikungoloza zoonekeratu kuti chifukwa cha uchimo m'thupi lanu simungathe kusunga Torah kupatula ndi chikhulupiriro chanu mwa ine, pakuti wolungama adzakhala ndi moyo ndi chikhulupiriro! Chikhulupiriro mwa ine ndi chiyani, mungafunse? Chikhulupiriro mwa ine chamangidwa pa kukhulupirira mawu anga ndi kumvetsa kuti mukudziwika ndi ine pa mlingo uliwonse, pakuti ndinu mpingo wanga womwe ndawutsuka m'magazi anga opanda uchimo; pakuti chikhululukiro cha machimo n'chosatheka kuchipeza ndi nsembe ya nyama. Khalani ndi chikhulupiriro mwa ine, pakuti kudzera m'chikhulupiriro mwa ine Torah imasungidwa, kudzera m'chikhulupiriro mwa ine chilamulo chimakwaniritsidwa mwa inu, ndipo ndinu olungama, olungamitsidwa, ndi oyeretsedwa! Pamene ndinaukitsidwa ku moyo watsopano, inunso munaukitsidwa; ndipo pamene ndinakwera ku dzanja lamanja la Atate, inunso munakhazikitsidwa m'zakumwamba. Pakuti Aefeso akuchitira umboni: Koma Mulungu amene ali wolemera m'chifundo, chifukwa cha chikondi chake chachikulu chomwe anatikonda nacho, Ngakhale pamene tinali akufa m'machimo, watipatsa moyo pamodzi ndi Khristu (mwa chisomo mwapulumutsidwa), Ndipo watiukitsa pamodzi, ndipo watikhazika pamodzi m'malo akumwamba mwa Khristu Yesu. Muyenera kumvetsa kuti chilamulo cha Mose si chipangano chanu, pakuti chanu ndi chipangano chatsopano chovomerezedwa m'magazi anga ndi kulipidwa ndi kuvutika kwanga.

Choncho mverani Chikhulupiriro, anthu anga, ndipo musakhalenso ofunda. Sankhani moyo mwa ine, Yesu Khristu, pakuti motero munaitanidwa. Chitani ntchito zambiri zamphamvu zomwe ndakupatsani, ngakhale mu mphindi yomaliza iyi. Ndikufuna mumvetsetse kuti popanda ine, simungachite kanthu, ndipo mumasunga mbali zonse za Torah ndi chikhulupiriro mwa ine. Kuonjezera apo, Ayuda a dzina lopatulika adzaza mpingo wanga, kuika mitolo yosayenera pa omwe akufunafuna chipulumutso ndi omwe ali anga. Atate wanga anandipatsa dzina loposa dzina lina lililonse, kuti pa dzina la Yesu Khristu bondo lililonse lipinde. Kodi mukuganiza kuti ndingaike mtolo wosayenera pa mitundu yomwe imalankhula zinenero zambiri kuti igwiritse ntchito dzina langa, Yeshua HaMashiach, m'Chihebri chokha? Mverani ine ndipo mverani bwino, pakuti ndikubwera, ndipo ndidzalimbana ndi osamvera ndi omwe angakuvutitseni.
