Epulo 10, 2024

Limbikani Mtima!

Ana anga ang'ono, limbikani mtima; kodi sindinagonjetse woyipayo ndikukuwombolani ndi mwazi wanga? Chifukwa chiyani mukutaya mtima? Chifukwa chiyani muli ndi chisoni mumtima; mukuganiza kuti ndakuyiwani? Ayi, m'malo modalira nzeru zanu, funani nzeru zanga, zomwe zimachokera kumwamba; pakuti ndi nzeru ya Mulungu mudzatsogoleredwa, choncho musayisiye! Kodi nzeru siikufuulabe m'misewu? Choncho musaope ndipo limbikani mtima, pakuti ndagonjetsa ndipo inunso mudzagonjetsa, kudzera mwa mwazi wanga. Lolani mtendere wanga utonthoze mitima yanu ndipo lolani mawu anga atsitsimutse miyoyo yanu, pakuti ndili nanu.

Ndani adzakulekanitsani ndi chikondi changa? Kodi ndi ngozi, kapena lupanga, kapena njala yomwe idzakulekanitsani? Ndikuti ayi, ndipo onani ndi kudziwa kuti muli otetezeka mwa ine. Dziwani kuti ndakudindani ndi chidindo cha Atate wanga, ndipo chidindocho sichingasweke kapena kuchotsedwa kwa inu, choncho khalani mwa ine, Ambuye wanu, mphamvu yanu, ndi momboli wanu. Taonani! Ndakugulani ndi mwazi wanga, ndakuitanani pamaina anu, ndakudindani molingana ndi chikhulupiriro chanu m'dzina langa, ndipo aliyense wokhulupirira mmenemo adzapulumutsidwa ndi chikhulupiriro, pakuti wolungama adzakhala ndi moyo ndi chikhulupiriro! Sungani chiyembekezo chanu chamoyo ndipo chiloleni chikupatseni mphamvu, ndipo onjezerani kupirira pa chikondi chanu ndi chikhulupiriro, pakuti mwapulumutsidwa ku chiyembekezo. Chiyembekezo chachikulu ndi kuyembekezera kwakukulu ndi zanu, pakuti ndalengeza zimenezi.

Chifukwa chiyani mwagwetsedwa pansi ndi kukhala achisoni? Taonani! Ndagonjetsa, ndipo posachedwa mudzakhala ndi ine, ndipo mudzandiona ndi maso anu, ndipo mudzadziwa monga momwe mukudziwidwira.

Onani! Atero Ambuye wamkulu, Ambuye wamphamvu amene ali Mbuye wa ambuye. Ndiyang'aneni ine m'chisoni chanu ndi m'mavuto anu. Ndikudziwa mitima yanu, ndipo ndikudziwa bwino lomwe kuti mumandikonda ndipo mumalakalaka kukhala ndi ine monga momwe ine ndimalakalakira kukhala ndi inu; koma musataye mtima. Ndiyankheni ichi, nthawi yochepa yomwe yatsala pakudikira kwanu — kodi ingafanane ndi umuyaya pamodzi ndi ine, mkwatibwi wanga? Ayi, masautso anu ndi zovuta zanu padziko lapansi sizoyenera kufananizidwa ndi ulemerero womwe ndatsala pang'ono kuwuwulula mwa inu, pakuti mudzakhala monga momwe ndiliri, ndipo mudzandiona monga momwe ndiliri, choncho musalole mitima yanu kuvutika. Mvetsetsani nthawi zanu ndipo nthawi zonse khalani okonzeka, pakuti kuonekera kwanga kudzachitika modzidzimutsa, ndipo kupitirira ziyembekezo zanu.

Taonani, ana anga! Dziko lapansi lidzadziwa kuti ndimakukondani ndipo ndinu anga, ndipo Satana adzabweretsa chinyengo chachikulu kuti anyenge omwe akuwonongeka, koma sichidzafika pafupi ndi inu. Ayi, chiweruzo sichanu, ndipo mkwiyo wanga siwanunso. Mvetsetsani, ana anga, kuti mwa ine, ndinu olungama, osati olungama m'ntchito zanu, koma olungama m'chilungamo chomwe ndakuvekani. Musataye zovala zomwe ndakuvekani, ndipo musazimitse mzimu wanga. Ngakhale ndi nthawi zotsiriza, ndidzathira mzimu wanga, ndipo ana anu aamuna ndi aakazi adzanenera, ndipo akulu anu adzalota maloto. Pali ntchito yoti ichitidwebe, ndipo kusuntha kwanga komaliza mu nthawi zino zachisomo kupitirira tsiku la Pentekoste pamene ndinathira koyamba pa osankhidwa anga mzimu womwe ndinalandira kwa Atate wanga. Ntchito zazikulu zili m'manja mwanu ndipo pali ambiri omwe akuyenera kupulumutsidwa, choncho gwirani ntchito yanga ndikutumikira ana anga ndikufunafuna nkhosa zanga zotayika. Pemphani kwa ine ndipo ndidzakutumizani, monga ndakupatsani mphamvu zazikulu ndipo ndapereka mphatso kwa anthu. Ndani angalimbaneni nanu, ngakhale muli ndi chuma ichi m'ziwiya zadothi? Chifukwa chiyani mukukayikirabe ine? M'kufooka kwanu mphamvu yanga imangidwa mwa inu. Choncho, chulukani m'ntchito ya Ambuye, ana anga.

Fufuzani Mawu anga Oyera, pakuti monga ndinaukitsa akufa, inunso mungachite chimodzimodzi. Dzozani maso anu ndi mankhwala a maso kuti muone machiritso ambiri amphamvu omwe ndinachita kumasula amangwa pamene ndinabwera kwa anga; inunso mungachite chimodzimodzi. Kodi sindinanene kuti wokhulupirira mwa ine, adzachita ntchito zomwezo, ndipo adzachita ntchito zazikulu kuposa izi chifukwa ndapita kwa Atate wanga ndi Atate wanu, ndi kwa Mulungu wanga ndi Mulungu wanu: Ndinu mafumu ndi ansembe pamaso panga ndipo mwakhala m'malo akumwamba, nanga chifukwa chiyani mukukayikirabe? Khalani ndi chikhulupiriro ngati kambewu kampiru, pakuti ndi kambewu kakang'ono kwambiri komwe kamatulutsa mtengo waukulu, momwemonso mudzakhala ngati mupitiriza kukhazikika mwa ine.

Chifukwa chiyani mukusokoneza mawu anga ndikunyalanyaza tsatanetsatane m'mawu anga? Sindinama: mungathe kuchita zomwe ndakutumizani kuti mukwaniritse ngati mukhala okhulupirika. Lankhulani Mawu anga ndi ulamuliro waukulu ndi kulimba mtima. Nenani kumapiri amenewo, Pponyeni m'nyanja! Mawu anga ndi choonadi ndi moyo, choncho musakhulupirire mabodza a abusa osokera omwe amati simungathe kuchita zomwe ndikukuuzani. Tengani phunziro. Mukufuna kuchiritsa odwala? Lamulani matenda kuti achoke m'dzina langa, ndipo nenani mawuwo ndi chikhulupiriro mwa ine ndi chikhulupiriro m'mawu omwe ndidzakupatsani. Lankhulani kuti zikhale choncho, pakuti moyo ndi imfa zili m'mphamvu ya lilime; malilime anu ndi amphamvu bwanji pamene mukulankhula mphamvu ya Mulungu ndikulamula zosatheka.

Lankhulani molimba mtima. Lankhulani motsimikiza. Lankhulani mwachikhulupiriro, osakayikira kanthu; pakuti ndakutumizani ndikukupatsani mphamvu. Chifukwa chiyani mukupempha mzimu kuti uchoke m'dzina langa ndi mantha pamilomo yanu? Ulamuleni kuti uchoke m'dzina langa ndi kulimba mtima konse ndi chitsimikizo, chifukwa wamkulu ndi amene ali mwa inu kuposa amene ali m'dziko lapansi. Taonani! Mizimu yoyipa imakuopani ndipo imathawa mawu anu, nanga chifukwa chiyani mukuopa zinthu zofooka ndi zaumphawi zomwe zagonjetsedwa ndipo zikuyembekezera moto? Musaope, koma khulupirirani kokha!

Pitani m'dzina langa ndikuchita ntchito zanga zamphamvu. Kumbukirani chikondi chanu choyamba ndikupuma m'mpumulo womwe ndakupatsani. Lekani ntchito zanu ndi katundu wanu, ndipo tengani goli langa pa inu; pakuti goli langa ndi lofewa, ndipo katundu wanga ndi wopepuka. Chitani ntchito zomwe ndakukonzerani ndikuyenda mwa izo, pakuti potero mudzapulumutsa ambiri kumoto, ndipo zipatso zidzachuluka ku akaunti yanu. Nthawi yafika, mkwatibwi wanga, ndipo mapeto a njira yanu ali pafupi, pafupi kuposa momwe mukuganizira. Koma ndimveni, ana anga; ndimveni, mkwatibwi wanga! Pemphererani ena pamene nthawi ilipobe. Chiritsani odwala pamene Mwana akuwala m'moyo mwanu, kukupatsani mphamvu ndi mphamvu yanga.

Funafunani nkhosa zanga zotayika; ziitaneni, pakuti pali ambiri oti apulumutsidwe m'masekondi omaliza otsitsimutsa awa. Pakuti posachedwa mudzafika ku cholowa chanu mwa ine, ndipo mudzazindikira moyo wanu wathunthu mwa ine; pakuti miyoyo yanu yabisika mwa ine, ndipo pamene ndidzaonekera, momwemonso kudzakhala kudzaza kwa moyo komwe Atate wakupatsani mwa ine. Ndimakukondani kwambiri, ana anga ang'ono, choncho kondani otayika mofananamo. Musawaweruze kapena kunyoza mavuto awo, pakuti agonjetsedwa ndi Satana monga momwe inu munaliri kale; choncho kumbukirani chipulumutso chomwe ndinakuchitirani ndikuchipereka kwa omwe akusowa. Ngakhale ambiri adzakutsutsani, musasiye mpaka nkhosa zanga zitasonkhanitsidwa, pakuti posachedwa tirigu wanga adzasonkhanitsidwa bwinobwino m'nkhokwe yanga. Omwe amakana kuonekera kwanga amandikana ine ndikukana mawu anga; choncho dzilimbikitseni m'mawu anga ndikusiya kukhulupirira mbala, pakuti mbala ndi abodza.

Ndine amene ndinapirira mpaka mpweya wanga womaliza, kulimbana ndi tchimo ndi mwazi wanga wopanda tchimo.

Ndine amene ndinaukitsidwa tsiku lachitatu ndikukwera kwa Atate wanga.

Ndine amene ndinayenda pakati pa ophunzira anga ndi atumwi masiku makumi anayi, ngakhale kuonedwa ndi abale opitirira mazana asanu panthawi imodzi, ndikuonedwa ndi kugwidwa ndi ambiri, pakuti mzimu ulibe thupi ndi mwazi.

Ndine amene ndinatengedwa kupita m'mwamba, pamene ankandiyang'ana, m'mitambo.

Ndine amene kuwala kwanga n'kowala kuposa dzuwa, pakuti ndimaonetsa ulemerero wa Atate wanga.

Ndatsala pang'ono kuwulula ulemerero waukulu mwa inu.

Ndili nanu nthawi zonse, mpaka chimaliziro cha nthawi pansi pano.
