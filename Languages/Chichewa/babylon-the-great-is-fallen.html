Mboni Ziwiri: Israyeli
Zowawa Zotsekemera
Ogasiti 18, 2024

Babulo Wamkulu Wagwa, Wagwa!

Penyetsani mitima yanu ku mawu anga ndipo imvani kulankhula kwanga, ndipo lolani mawu anga alowe m'mitima yanu: pakuti ngakhale okhulupirika pakati pa Mpingo wanga ndi ngakhale Mkwatibwi wanga akukumana ndi mayesero odzilungamitsa ndi kusakhulupirira. Ndimveni ndipo ndimveni bwino ndipo tcherani makutu anu kuti mumvetsetse nthawi yomwe mukukhalamo, pakuti palibe ndi mmodzi yemwe wa inu adakhalapo m'nthawi ya zochitika zamakono zomwe zikutsutsana ndi dziko lapansi m'chiweruzo. Pakuti chiweruzo ndi tsoka zili pano ndipo zayamba kale, ngakhale izi ndi zowawa za kubadwa chabe, zomwe sizikuyima tsopano ndipo cholinga chake ndi kuchenjeza omwe akukhala mosazindikira. Komabe, ngakhale pakati pa osankhidwa anga ndi okhulupirika pali omwe angafune kusankha khungu m'malo mwa zenizeni ndikusankha mofunitsitsa kumva phokoso m'malo mwa malangizo omveka bwino ndi chitonthozo chomwe Mawu a Ambuye amabweretsa.

Pakuti Ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, ngakhale Yesu Khristu m'chinenero cha Chingerezi, ndine amene ndikutumiza mawu awa kudzera mwa mtumiki wanga yemwe ndamutulutsa ndi kumutumiza ndikumubisa m'phodo langa pa nthawi ngati ino. Ndipo musadabwe pakuti njira zanga zili choncho, pakuti Ine Khristu Yesu, ndine amene ndingathe kudzutsa atumwi, aneneri, alaliki, abusa, ndi aphunzitsi kuchokera mwa unyinji wopanda ziyeneretso za dziko lapansi logwali kapena sali a iwo omwe adzigwirizanitsa okha mu mgwirizano wa zipembedzo ndi atumiki a Satana omwe amawoneka ngati atumiki a chilungamo. Ndipo musadabwe pakuti Satana mwiniwake amasandulika mngelo wa kuwala, chifukwa chake si chinthu chachikulu ngati atumiki ake adzisandutsa ngati atumiki a chilungamo, pakuti ndithudi akufuna kugwirizanitsa onse pansi pa mbendera yawo ya dziko limodzi la zipembedzo.

Ndipo monga bwenzi langa Yohane Mbatizi adanena zaka za yubile zambiri zapitazo pamene adawongola njira zomwe ndikadayendamo: Musaganize zolankhula m'mitima yanu, Tili ndi Abrahamu monga atate wathu: pakuti ndinena kwa inu, kuti Mulungu akhoza kuukitsira Abrahamu ana mwa miyala iyi. Ndipo tsopano nkhwangwa yaikidwa pa muzu wa mitengo: chifukwa chake mtengo uliwonse wosabala chipatso chabwino udulidwa, ndi kuponyedwa pamoto.

Werengani ndi kumvetsetsa ndikupempha nzeru, tiana twanga, ndipo gwiritsani ntchito mawu awa m'nthawi yanu. Pakuti ndinena momveka bwino kwa inu kuti sindinatumize amphamvu a dziko lino ngati amithenga anga ndi aneneri, kapena sindifuna ziyeneretso za dziko, Dr uyu ndi Reverend uyo, ngakhale maudindo omwe anthu amadzipatsa okha kuti awoneke ngati aumulungu. Pakuti munthu wogwa nthawi zonse amafuna kulemekeza thupi lake m'zochita za dziko, ngakhale kufuna kuyima wolungama pamaso pa Mulungu mwiniwake m'thupi loipa lomwe palibe munthu angaliyeretse, kupatula kudzera mwa Ine Yesu wa ku Nazarete. Ndipo chotero ndikufunsa kuti ndi ziyeneretso zotani za dziko zomwe ndinali nazo pa nthawi ya utumiki wanga ku Yudeya, pamene sindinatumizidwe koma kwa nkhosa zotayika za nyumba ya Israyeli? Kodi sichinali chokhumudwitsa kwa osakhulupirira pakati pa anthu a mtundu wanga pamene adafunsa kuti nzeru iyi ndi ntchito zazikulu za munthu uyu zikuchokera kuti? Kodi uyu si mwana wa mmisiri wamatabwa?

Ndipo monga kwalembedwa momwemonso kwakwaniritsidwa: Koma Mulungu wasankha zinthu zopusa za dziko lapansi kuti asokoneze anzeru; ndipo Mulungu wasankha zinthu zofooka za dziko lapansi kuti asokoneze zinthu zamphamvu; Ndi zinthu zonyozeka za dziko lapansi, ndi zinthu zomwe zimanyozedwa, Mulungu wasankha, inde, ndi zinthu zomwe palibe, kuti athetse zinthu zomwe zilipo: Kuti pasakhale thupi lonyadira pamaso pake. Choncho imvani Mawu anga a chikhulupiriro omwe amalankhulidwa pamilomo ya okhulupirika anga ndipo ndanena kale, ndipo tsopano ndikunenanso kuti aneneri anga, omwe ndi ambiri, satsutsana ndi malemba opatulika, kapena satsutsana ndi Ine, Khristu Yesu Ambuye Wokwezedwa!

Mvetsetsani, inu a chikhulupiriro chochepa, kuti aneneri anga amalankhula mogwirizana, ndipo amalankhula Mawu omwe ndimawapatsa. Kodi simukudziwa mawu a Ambuye wanu? Kodi Mawu anga ndi achilendo kwa inu, tiana twanga? Kodi mukuwopa choonadi? Pakuti Ine ndine Mawu a Mulungu wopangidwa thupi yemwe amakhala pakati panu ndipo ndine amene ndapereka mzimu wanga wamphamvu womwe ndathira pa thupi lonse m'masiku otsiriza ano monga mwa lonjezo. Pakuti kwalembedwa: ana anu aamuna ndi aakazi adzanenera, ndipo anyamata anu adzaona masomphenya, ndipo akulu anu adzalota maloto: Ndipo pa atumiki anga ndi pa adzakazi anga ndidzathira masiku amenewo Mzimu wanga; ndipo adzanenera: Ndipo ndidzaonetsa zozizwitsa kumwamba, ndi zizindikiro pansi pano; magazi, ndi moto, ndi utsi: Dzuwa lidzasanduka mdima, ndi mwezi udzasanduka magazi, lisanadze tsiku lalikulu ndi lolemekezeka la Ambuye.

Choncho imvani mawu anga ndipo landirani Mawu anga pakuti ndi choonadi ndipo onse adzakwaniritsidwa, ngakhale yod iliyonse ndi munga uliwonse wa yod udzachitika pakuti Mawu onse a Mulungu aperekedwa kuti amveredwe ndipo Mawu onse a Mulungu adzakwaniritsidwa mwangwiro popanda chosowa, pakuti njira za Yahweh ndi zokwanira ndipo chilichonse chomwe akayika dzanja lake kuti akwaniritse chimatulutsa ungwiro wodabwitsa. Kodi simukudziwa njira za Atate wanu, tiana twanga?

Choncho imvani mawu anga, okhulupirika anga oitanidwa ndi osankhidwa, ngakhale Mpingo wanga ndi ngakhale Mkwatibwi wanga! Kodi mupitiriza kunyalanyaza mawu anga ndikuchoka ku mawu anga mokomera mabodza a dziko lapansi? Kodi mudzatsatira mawu a ogwa pamene mukuthera miyoyo yanu mukuyang'ana pazenera mopanda nzeru? Ndikukupemphani, imvani mawu anga ndipo ndinena kwa inu, chifukwa chiyani mitima yanu ikukuvutitsani? Kodi simukumvetsa kuti ndine amene ndimakhala ndi chofukutira m'manja kusonkhezera malawi a moto oyeretsa kuti ndichotse zinyalala kuzitsulo zamtengo wapatali pakati pa chuma chambiri chamtengo wapatali cha Yahweh?

Ndipo momwemonso ntchito za manja anga zimatulutsa m'mitima yanu ndi m'miyoyo yanu okondedwa anga: pakuti ndine amene ndimachotsa kusakhulupirira, ndine amene ndimachotsa njira zanu zopanda chikhulupiriro, ndine amene ndimakuyeretsani kwathunthu ku mitundu yonse ya katangale wa thupi ndi zoipa pamene mubwera kwa Ine ndikuvomereza machimo anu ndi zolakwa zanu kuti ndikuyeretseni kwathunthu m'magazi anga otetezera kuti muyime opanda chifukwa pamaso pa mpando wachifumu wa Yahweh pamene ndidzakuperekani kwathunthu kwa Atate wanga pamasom'pamaso m'tsogolo mwanu mwapafupi kwambiri.

Komabe, ambiri a inu mukufuna kubwerera ku miyoyo yanu yakale pamene sindinakwaniritse chifuniro cha Yahweh malinga ndi nthawi yanu ndi zomwe mukuyembekezera. Pakuti zili choncho, ngakhale ophunzira anga apamtima ndi atumwi adabwerera kukawedza nsomba pambuyo pa kupachikidwa kwanga ndi kuukitsidwa kwanga, komabe ndidawasandutsa asodzi a anthu. Ndipo chotero ndikubwera kudzasonkhanitsanso inu ku phokoso la mawu anga, ndipo inu simukumvetsadi zoopsa zomwe zakuzungulirani m'dziko lino loipa. Ndipo chotero ndikukuitanani kwa Ine ndekha m'masiku otsiriza ano kuti mubwere kwathunthu kwa Ine ndikutuluka kwathunthu mu tchimo ndi kusakhulupirira, pakuti ndine amene ndakuitanani ku ukoma ndi choonadi, ngakhale ambiri a inu mukukondabe kubwerera ku Igupto.

Ndipo chotero ndikukulangizani kuti mutenge maphunziro pamtima olembedwa akale omwe amapereka chenjezo ndi nzeru kwa anzeru. Pakuti adanena kwa Mose: Chifukwa chakuti ku Igupto kunalibe manda, kodi mwatitengera ife kuti tidzafere m'chipululu? Chifukwa chiyani mwatichitira ife motere, kutitulutsa ife mu Igupto? Chifukwa chiyani mukulakalakabe masiku akale? Kodi mwakopekabe kukhala m'zosangalatsa za tchimo? Chifukwa chiyani mukufuna kukhala pakati pa anamwali opusa pamene ndikuyima pamwamba panu ndikukupatsani dzanja kuti mudzuke, pakuti mwagwa mosazindikira ndipo muli m'fumbi la dziko lapansi, pakuti fumbi lidzakhala chakudya cha njoka. Choncho dzukani inu amene mukugona ndikugwedeza fumbi la dziko lapansi ndikudzuka mu ulemerero wakumwamba ndikukhala ndi chikhulupiriro m'mawu anga a chikhulupiriro omwe okhulupirika alankhula pamilomo ya chikhulupiriro!

Ndipo chotero mvetsetsani zomwe zikuchitika panopa ndikumvetsetsa nthawi yomwe mukukhalamo, pakuti ambiri adzilola okha kugonekedwa ndipo akhulupirira mawu opanda chikhulupiriro a Satana pamene akunong'oneza mabodza omveka bwino omwe angapusitse ngakhale osankhidwa ngati atasankha kukhulupirira mabodza ake. Mvetsetsani kuti Satana, ngakhale mdierekezi yemwe amapusitsa dziko lonse lapansi, tsopano waponyedwa pansi kuchokera kumwamba ndipo wamangidwa padziko lapansi chifukwa cha chiweruzo chomwe chikubwera chomwe sichichedwa.

Ndipo chotero pali kupuma komwe ogwa akuloledwa kusuntha zidutswa zawo m'malo mwake pa bolodi la chess la dziko lapansi, ndi kupuma komwe miyoyo yotsala yomaliza tsopano ikukwera m'Chombo cha moyo wosatha mwa Ine Khristu Yesu Ambuye wawo woukitsidwa ndi Mpulumutsi. Komabe, ambiri a inu m'kulingalira kwanu kopanda chikhulupiriro mukumaliza Mawu anga ngati abodza ndikuganiza mopusa kuti muli ndi nthawi yobwerera ku miyoyo yanu yakale ya tchimo ndi zosangalatsa. Kupatula apo, mwadzidzidzi, okhulupirika adzatengedwa kothawirako ku chipinda chachifumu cha Yahweh mwiniwake mu mphotho yayikulu ndi ulemerero wamuyaya ndi cholowa chamuyaya, ndipo okhulupirika onse adzasinthidwa kwamuyaya.

Kodi mudzakhala pakati pa okhulupirika? Kodi muli ndi kudzichepetsa kuti muzindikire kusakhulupirika kwanu ndikulapa kuti mulowe m'magulu a okhulupirika? Ngati mutasankha kuwona, Ine Khristu Yesu, ndidzakuwonetsani. Nanga n'chifukwa chiyani mukutopa pakuchita zabwino? Kodi mwaganiza kuti omwe ndikubweretsani kwa iwo ndi omwe ndikubweretsa kwa inu si oyenera moyo wosatha? Ndipo chotero ndikukulangizani kuti muwonetse chifundo ndi chisomo chomwecho kwa mnzanu chomwe ndakuwonetsani inu mu kupuma komaliza uku chiwonongeko chisanatsike. Ndipo pochita izi mudzapulumutsa ambiri, pakuti mudakali akazembe anga. Choncho tsegulani pakamwa panu m'chikhulupiriro, osakayikira chilichonse, ndipo ndidzakuuzirani mawu oti mulankhule.

Ndipo chotero tiana twanga ndi ngakhale okhulupirika anga akusowa mtendere ndi kuleza mtima, ngakhale mu kupuma komaliza uku Mpingo wanga ndi Mkwatibwi wanga asanatengedwe kumwamba. Komabe, monga momwe zinalili m'chipululu pamene Mose adakumana ndi Yahweh pa Sinai kuti ana adadzuka kudya ndi kumwa ndi kusewera, ndipo adadzipangira okha mwana wang'ombe wagolide kuti amupembedze m'malo mwa Mulungu wamoyo, yemwe pamaso pake sanaloledwe koma adayima pafupi ndikuwona ulemerero wa kukhalapo kwa Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya pa Sinai! Ndipo mofananamo monga Mose adapempherera ana, momwemonso Ine ndimapempherera onse omwe ali olowa nyumba a chipulumutso chamuyaya, kuti onse oitanidwa abwere kwathunthu ku chipulumutso ndi kulapa kuchokera ku njira zawo zoipa ndi zopanda chikhulupiriro. Bwererani ku mawu anga ndikukhala m'malo othawirako omwe ndakupatsani, ndipo ndidzakutetezani ku chiweruzo chomwe sichanu. Kodi sindinakuuzeni kuti mutuluke pakati pawo ndikukhala osiyana? Mverani mawu anga!

Ndipo chotero chiweruzo tsopano chikugwa padziko lapansi ndipo chiweruzo chakonzedwa kale kwa osakhulupirira ndi ouma khosi, ngakhale omwe mofunitsitsa kapena mosazindikira amamatira ku kupanduka kwa Satana. Pakuti anthu ambiri, ambiri, ambiri adzasankha mofunitsitsa malawi a moto omwe akonzedwera mdierekezi ndi angelo ake m'malo mochoka ku tchimo ndikubwera ku moyo wosatha poyitana Yesu wa ku Nazarete kuti awapulumutse. Ndipo zidzakhala choncho, pakuti El Shaddai, ngakhale Mulungu Wamphamvuyonse, wapereka ufulu wosankha kwa anthu kuti anthu asankhe kukhala molungama ndi mwamtendere pamaso pa Yahweh, kapena kusankha chiwonongeko chamuyaya m'nyanja yayikulu ya moto wabuluu yopangidwira ogwa komwe nyongolotsi yawo sifa. Ndipo nthawi za chiweruzo zili pano.

Choncho ndikuuza onse omwe adzamva za zochitika zomwe zidzachitika zisanachitike. Zochitika izi si zachinsinsi koma zalengezedwa ndi aneneri anga ndi atumiki anga kwa zaka zambiri tsopano, ngakhale onse omwe ndawatumiza ku Babulo Wachinsinsi. Pakuti nthawi za Chiweruzo cha hule lalikulu ilo zili pano, ndipo chilombo chomwe chimamunyamula tsopano chidzamuwononga kotheratu. Pakuti Mulungu wayika m'mitima ya nyanga khumi kuti akwaniritse chifuniro chake, ndipo amadana ndi hulelo, ndipo adzapitiriza kumusandutsa wabwinja ndi wamaliseche, ndipo adzadya mnofu wake, ndikupitiriza kumutentha ndi moto. Ndipo momwemonso Babulo wachinsinsi adzasandutsidwa wabwinja kotheratu ndikuwululidwa poyera mu manyazi kuti onse awone.

Mizinda yake yomwe kale inali yayikulu yomwe inali zitsanzo za kugwira ntchito molimbika ndi kupanga ndi kuchuluka kwa El Shaddai tsopano yakhala mabwinja ndi mbadwa zake zomwe zili ndi chizolowezi cha mankhwala osokoneza bongo ndikukhala m'zinyalala m'misewu yake yonyansa. Ndipo wasandutsidwa wamaliseche pamene chitetezo chake chikutumizidwa kutali ndipo chuma chake ndi mphamvu zake zikuperekedwa m'manja mwa adani ake a Edomu omwe amanamizira kuti ndi ogwirizana nawo. Hule lalikulu lomwe lidachita nkhondo ndi mitundu yonse yomwe lidatumizidwa kukagonjetsa tsopano lamangidwa, ndipo lilibe chitetezo ndipo lakonzeka kugonjetsedwa kwathunthu. Ndipo thupi lonse lidzadyedwa m'masautso ndipo onse omwe agwidwa adzaphedwa ndipo omwe angathe kutumikira zolinga za chilombocho adzatengedwa ukapolo ndipo otsala adzaphedwa, ngakhale otsala adzapulumuka. Ndipo akuyaka moto ndipo pali moto wambiri kale m'malire ake ndi m'maiko a anansi ake kumpoto omwe amagawana naye miliri yake. Komabe izi ndi zowawa za kubadwa chabe ndipo si ziweruzo zomaliza koma chenjezo kwa onse la chiwonongeko chawo chomwe chikubwera. Pakuti mwadzidzidzi zidzachitika, ndipo mwadzidzidzi zidzachitika. Zidzachitika pokhapokha ndipo ngakhale pamene ndikwatula okhulupirika anga padziko lapansi kupita kuchitetezo ndipo ndidzasonkhanitsa mwadzidzidzi tirigu wa Yahweh m'nkhokwe yake yamuyaya, pakuti nthawi za kukolola zili pano. Komabe si kukolola komaliza, koma koyamba mwa angapo, ndipo chotero chiweruzo chimayambira panyumba ya Mulungu.

Babulo Wachinsinsi, nthawi zako zafika, ndipo masiku ako ali kumapeto. Ine, Yesu wa ku Nazarete, ndakhetsa misozi yosawerengeka pa chiwonongeko chako chomwe chikubwera ndipo ndatumiza zizindikiro kumwamba, ndi zozizwitsa m'mlengalenga, ndi zizindikiro padziko lapansi ndipo ngakhale masoka ambiri adzidzidzi akuchitika mu nthawi yeniyeni ndipo pakati pake ndipo komabe Babulo Wachinsinsi amatseka makutu ake mu kupanduka kouma khosi, ndipo tsopano adzadya kwathunthu njira zake zopanduka. Choncho mvetsetsani, kuti monga momwe ndimasonkhanitsira mwadzidzidzi okhulupirika kuchitetezo ndi mphotho, momwemonso chiwonongeko chadzidzidzi chidzagwa pa Babulo wachinsinsi pamene woletsa achotsedwa kwathunthu. Imvani mawu anga ndikumvetsetsa mawu anga chifukwa woletsa ndiye chinthu chokha chomwe chikuletsa zilakolako zowononga za ogwa mpaka uyo amene aletsa saletsanso ndipo atengedwa pakati.

Pamenepo chiwonongeko chadzidzidzi chidzadza pa dziko lonse lapansi. Mizere ya nkhondo yajambulidwa, ndipo akonzekera nkhondo yawo yachitatu ya padziko lonse, ngakhale nkhondo yawo yotembenuza kwathunthu njira zakale ndi madongosolo akale. Ndipo chotero moto udzagwa mwadzidzidzi pa Babulo Wachinsinsi ndipo mizinda yake idzawonongedwa. Moto udzagwa mwadzidzidzi pamene mizinga ikugwa m'maiko ake, kumusiya wotenthedwa ndi wamaliseche. Komabe mizinda yake yambiri m'mbali mwa nyanja zake zochimwa idzagwa m'madzi omwe adzabwera mwadzidzidzi ndipo adzagunda m'mbali mwa nyanja zake kusefukira kumtunda kwa mailosi ambiri, ambiri ndipo izi zidzachitika kuchokera kum'mawa ndi kumadzulo. Dziko lapansi lidzagwedezeka mwadzidzidzi mosalamulirika, ndipo chiwonongeko chachikulu chidzadza pa maiko a Babulo wachinsinsi, ndipo maiko adzaipitsidwa ndi kusinthidwa. Milatho yake idzagwa, ndipo misewu yake idzakhala yosapitika, ndipo chipululu ndi bwinja zidzatsala. Fungo la imfa lidzaphimba maiko ake ndipo mliri ndi matenda zidzadzaza maiko ake ndipo adzakhala wopanda thandizo kotheratu.

Ndipo pamene kuukira komaliza uku kukuchitika, magulu a Gulu Lankhondo Lofiira adzamasulidwa kwathunthu pa okhala m'dzikolo otsala. Adzapha onse omwe akukana ndipo adzapha ambiri omwe sakukana. Pakuti anthu a Babulo wachinsinsi agulitsidwa mu ukapolo ndi ngongole zamapiri zomwe wapanga, pakuti kudzera mu katapira ogwa amagwira ntchito kuwononga unyinji. Ndipo monga momwe anthu ake alonjezera ndikusaina miyoyo yawo chifukwa cha phindu lakuthupi ndi zosangalatsa za tchimo, momwemonso omwe asankha kuwasandutsa akapolo adzatumikira omwe ali nawo ngongole mu ntchito yokakamiza ndipo chizindikiro chidzakakamizidwa kwa onse omwe apulumuka, koma musatenge chizindikirocho.

Ndipo dziko lapansi lomwe lilipo tsopano, lidzasinthidwa mwadzidzidzi ndi phulusa la mabwinja ndi zotsalira zofuka utsi za gulu la anthu lomwe kale linali lamphamvu. Ndipo njala idzagwa m'maiko, pakuti Babulo Wachinsinsi wabweretsa njala m'maiko ambiri ndipo tsopano njala idzakhala mlendo wake wa chakudya chamadzulo, pakuti monga momwe waperekera momwemonso tsopano adzalandira mowirikiza kawiri. Madzi adzakhala chinthu chofunidwa kwambiri, ndipo madzi ambiri adzasandutsidwa owawa chifukwa cha chiwonongeko ndi moto wa nkhondo komanso miliri ya anthu osaikidwa m'manda yomwe idzaipitsa dziko. Ndipo ambiri adzavutika mosayenera ndi kuwonongeka m'chiweruzo chifukwa cha machimo awo, pamene chinthu choterocho sichinali chofunikira kwa iwo koma omwe atsala pakati pa oweruzidwa nawonso adzaweruzidwa, komabe aliyense ndi onse amene aitanira pa dzina la Ambuye wawo mpaka chimaliziro adzapulumutsidwa, koma adzayenera kupirira mpaka mpweya wawo womaliza. Pakuti kukhulupirika mpaka chimaliziro nthawi zonse kwakhala chofunikira kwa okhulupirika, ngakhale ana enieni a Yahweh a Abrahamu omwe ndi ana a lonjezo, pakuti wolungama adzakhala ndi moyo ndi chikhulupiriro.

Ndipo momwemonso Babulo wachinsinsi adzang'ambika kotheratu pakati ndipo madzi adzathamangira pamene poyamba panali mtunda wouma ndi wachonde, ngakhale madera apakati adzasefukira mpaka pakati. Ndipo chifundo ndi chisomo cha Mulungu zidzachotsedwa kotheratu padziko lapansi pamene woletsa adzachotsedwa mwadzidzidzi ndipo chiwonongeko chidzakwatirana ndi chiwonongeko, pakuti ino ndi nthawi ya chiwonongeko pamene ufumu wa Satana udzaweruzidwa kotheratu ndi kuwonongedwa kotheratu, koma poyamba adzatumikira zolinga za Yahweh pakuwononga kotheratu Babulo wachinsinsi ndipo motero adzatenthedwa kotheratu ndi kuwonongedwa m'moto wambiri waukali womwe udzameza ndi kuyamba kuyeretsa dziko. Ndipo chiwonongeko cha Babulo Wachinsinsi chidzachitika mu ola limodzi, pakuti monga kwalembedwa momwemonso kudzachitika, ndipo adzalandira gawo lowirikiza kawiri la chiweruzo. Ndipo monga kwalembedwa, momwemonso kudzachitika.

Pakuti kwalembedwa kalekale ndi Yohane Wovumbulutsa: Ndinaona Mngelo wina akutsika kuchokera kumwamba, ali ndi mphamvu yaikulu, kotero kuti dziko lapansi linaunikiridwa ndi ulemerero wake, Ndipo anafuula mwamphamvu ndi mawu akulu, nati, Wagwa, wagwa, Babulo mzinda waukuluwo, ndipo wasanduka malo okhala ziwanda, ndi malo ogwirira mizimu yonse yoipa, ndi khola la mbalame iliyonse yodetsedwa ndi yodedwa. Pakuti mitundu yonse yamwa vinyo wa mkwiyo wa dama lake, ndipo Mafumu a dziko lapansi achita naye dama, ndipo amalonda a dziko lapansi alemela ndi kuchuluka kwa zokondweretsa zake.

Ndipo ndinamva mawu ena ochokera kumwamba akuti, tulukani mwa iye, anthu anga, kuti musakhale ogawana nawo machimo ake, ndi kuti musalandire miliri yake. Pakuti machimo ake afika mpaka kumwamba, ndipo Mulungu wakumbukira zosalungama zake. M'bwezereni, monga momwe iye wakubwezerani inu, ndipo mumpatse mowirikiza kawiri monga mwa ntchito zake: ndipo m'chikho chimene wakudzazerani, m'dzereni iye mowirikiza kawiri. Monga momwe anadzilemekeza yekha, nakhala m'zokondweretsa, momwemonso mumpatse mazunzo ndi chisoni: pakuti anena mumtima mwake, Ndikhala ngati mfumukazi, ndipo sindine wamasiye, ndipo sindidzaona kulira. Chifukwa chake, miliri yake idzafika tsiku limodzi, imfa, ndi chisoni, ndi njala, ndipo adzatenthedwa ndi moto: pakuti Mulungu amene amuweruza, ndi Ambuye wamphamvu!

Ndipo motero mtundu uwu womwe kale unali waukulu wa zigawo ndi mizinda yambiri udzagwa mwadzidzidzi, ndipo mngelo wa chiwonongeko panopa akuuluka pamwamba pa mzinda wake waukulu kwambiri ndi waukulu kwambiri pamadzi akum'mawa ndipo kunyadira kwake ndi chimwemwe chake ndi nsanje ya dziko lapansi zidzawonongedwa kotheratu kuti zisadzukenso. Ndipo momwemonso chiwonongeko chake chidzagawidwa ndi onse amene adziphatika naye, pakuti iye ndiye hule lalikulu lomwe likhala pamadzi ambiri omwe ndi anthu ndi makamu ndi mitundu ndi malilime.

Pakuti chiweruzo chawo sichichedwa, ndipo mafumu onse a dziko lapansi aumiriza nyanga khumi pakuukira kwawo kwadzidzidzi. Pakuti atsogoleri awo amatumikira mofunitsitsa omwe adagwa, pakuti momwemonso alili olamulira onse a dziko lapansi. Palibe abwino, ayi, ngakhale mmodzi. Atsogoleri awa agwira ntchito kwa zaka zambiri kuti abweretse kuukira uku ndipo apereka mitundu yawoyomwe yomwe anatumizidwa kuti ainyenge. Ndipo anthu omwe anawatsatira mofunitsitsa m'zokondweretsa adzagwa m'moto wa nkhondo, mliri, imfa ndi chiwonongeko. Ndipo zinthu izi zili zokonzeka kumasulidwa mwadzidzidzi kotheratu padziko lapansi. Ndipo motero mitundu yonse yotchedwa yachikhristu idzagwa ndi lupanga pamene chiweruzo chikuyamba panyumba ya Mulungu. Ndipo monga momwe zinalili m'masiku akale pamene Isiraeli anasiya Atate wa Kuwala natumikira milungu ina, yomwe mwachibadwa si milungu, momwemonso awa adzaperekedwa m'manja mwa adani awo, monga momwe mafuko khumi ndi awiri anagawidwira ndi kugonjetsedwa kalekale chifukwa cha kupandukira kwawo mapangano ndipo zinabweretsedwa ndi temberero la chilamulo kwa osamvera ndi a mitima youma.

Ndipo kuchokera ku phulusa la nkhondo yonse ndi chiwonongeko kudzauka dongosolo lomaliza la chilombo, pakuti chinjoka chikukonzekera kale dongosolo la chilombo kuti liuke, ngakhale omwe adziphatika ndi mafumu khumi omwe adzapereka mphamvu zawo zonse kwa chilombo chochokera kunyanja ndi chilombo chochokera pamtunda chomwe ndi mneneri wawo wonyenga ndi Moshiach wonyenga. Chilombo chochokera kunyanja chidzauka ngati Mahdi wawo, ndipo kwa icho, kupatsidwa mphamvu yochita nkhondo ndi oyera mtima ndi kuwagonjetsa. Ndipo motero chilombo chochokera pamtunda chigawana mphamvu yofanana ndi chilombo chochokera kunyanja, ndipo ndi icho chomwe chili Nyanga Yaing'ono yoloseredwa kalekale ndi Danieli.

Ndipo motero zilombo ziwiri izi zidzapatsidwa mphamvu zonse ndi chinjoka chachikulu chofiyiracho ndipo zidzauka kotheratu ndipo zidzagonjetsa ndi kupita kukagonjetsa, ndipo adzati: Ndani afanana ndi chilombo? Ndipo ndani angathe kuchita nkhondo ndi chilombo? Ndipo motero onse adzafunikira kulandira chizindikiro chawo omwe adziphatika nawo m'gulu lawo la satana. Ndipo misala idzaphimba kotheratu dziko lapansi, popeza woletsa sadzakhalaponso kuti atsimikizire mtundu wa anthu za tchimo ndi kuletsa zilakolako zake ndi zilakolako zoipa. Ndipo motero choipa chokwanira, chosaletsedwa chakonzedwa kuti chimasulidwe padziko lapansi ndipo phompho lidzatsegulidwa kotheratu, pakuti okhala padziko lapansi akana Yahweh, chifukwa akana ine, Yesu wa ku Nazarete amene anatumizidwa kuti awapulumutse. Koma adziyesa okha osayenera moyo wosatha ndipo akana Yahweh pamene Yahweh anabwera kwa iwo m'chikondi, kufatsa, chisomo, ndi chifundo ndikupereka pangano la mtendere wosatha kwa mtundu wa anthu. Pangano la mtendere lomwe tsopano akana. Komabe, aliyense amene aitanira pa dzina la Ambuye mpaka chimaliziro adzapulumutsidwa.

Ndipo ngakhale kuti nthawi za chiweruzo, chiwonongeko chachikulu, nkhondo, njala, mliri ndi zina zotero zidzamasulidwa kotheratu padziko lapansi, sabata lomalizali ngakhale sabata la makumi asanu ndi awiri la Danieli laperekedwa kuti Yakobo apulumutsidwe ndipo Ine, Yeshua HaMashiach ndidzapulumutsabe otsala a Isiraeli omwe adzandivomereza ngati Mesiya wawo weniweni ndi woyenera pakuti, motero ndinalengedwa, kupangidwa, ndi kutumizidwa ndi Mulungu Wamphamvuyonse polemekeza malonjezo opangidwa m'pangano ndi chipangano kwa Atate awo.

Pakuti Yahweh ndiye Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya amene akhala pamwamba ndipo tsopano adzayenda kudziulula yekha ku chilengedwe chake ndipo Ine, Khristu Yesu, ndine Ambuye wamuyaya wa Ambuye ndi Mfumu ya Mafumu omwe ufumu wawo ulibe mapeto. Ndipo ndidzatumiza aneneri anga awiri, ngakhale zoyikapo nyali zanga ziwiri zomwe zidzalosera, ndipo ndidzatumiza 144,000 anga kuti apulumutse amoyo omwe atumizidwa kwa iwo m'chipululu pamalo osankhidwa ndi Yahweh ndipo ndi Yahweh adzadyetsedwa ndi kusamaliridwa. Ndipo Mulungu wamuyaya walengeza motero kalekale. Pakuti ndi Yahweh amene amalankhula za zinthu zomwe zikubwera ngati kuti zachitika kale pakuti njira za Mulungu Wamuyaya zili motero amene wandipatsa Uthenga Wabwino Wamuyaya kuti ulengezedwe padziko lapansi kuti chipulumutso chamuyaya chibwere kwa onse amene adzaitanira pa dzina langa lamphamvu, lamuyaya.

Ine Yeshua HaMashiach, ngakhale Khristu Yesu, ndatumiza mawu awa kudzera mwa mtumiki wanga ndipo kudzera mwa mtumiki wanga mawu awa atumizidwa.

Ndipo dziwani kuti ndine amene Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya ndi wosatha wapereka zinthu zonse m'manja mwanga mwamphamvu ndipo wandipatsa dzina loposa dzina lina lililonse, kuti pa dzina la Yesu maondo onse apinde kumwamba, padziko lapansi ndi pansi pa dziko lapansi. Ndipo ndine amene ndili Mkango wa fuko la Yuda ndipo m'chilungamo ndimabwera kudzaweruza ndi kuchita nkhondo ndi kupereka chiweruzo kwa omwe adagwa, ndipo ola la chiweruzo tsopano lafika. Ndipo motero ndikulangiza omwe akukhala padziko lapansi kuti atsatire mawu a Yahweh ndi Kupsompsona Mwana, kuti ndingakwiye, ndipo mungawonongeke panjira, pamene mkwiyo wanga uyaka pang'ono chabe.

Odala ndi onse amene akayika chikhulupiriro chawo mwa ine, ngakhale Yeshua HaMashiach ndipo onse amene aitanira pa dzina langa mpaka chimaliziro adzapulumutsidwa, ngakhale onse omwe ndi olowa nyumba a chipulumutso kuyambira asanaike maziko a dziko lapansi.
