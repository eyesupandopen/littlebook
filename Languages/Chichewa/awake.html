M'ntchito za Yohane Mbatizi
Marichi 2, 2024

Galamukani!

Galamukani! Galamukani! Galamukani! Anthu anga, galamukani, pakuti Ine Ambuye Yesu Khristu ndikuitana anthu anga kuti abwerere kwa ine. Musakane mawu a Ambuye pamene ndikukuitanani ndi kukupatsani chifundo, chikhululuko, ndi mtendere. Mulibe mtendere m'dziko lino; ngakhale adzafuula kuti mtendere ndi chitetezo, musawakhulupirire. Palibe mtendere kunja kwa ine, Yesu Khristu. Galamukani! Galamutsani abale anu! Ambiri adzagwa kumanzere ndi kumanja, koma sizidzafika pafupi ndi inu ngati mukhala okhulupirika ku mawu anga, mawu a Ambuye.

Mkwatibwi wanga adzatengedwa kupita kuzipinda zamkati zakumwamba mumtendere ndi chitetezo, popeza achapa zovala zawo m'magazi anga, magazi a Mwanawankhosa! Amene amandikana ndi kuona nsembe ya Yesu Khristu, Paskha wangwiro, chifukwa cha machimo a anthu ngati chinthu chopanda pake sadzakhala ndi mtendere. Lapani ndi kutembenuka kusiya machimo anu, popeza ino ndi nthawi yotsiriza. Musadziperekenso ku zifukwa ndi kusakhulupirira, popeza palibe munthu angathe kuima pamaso panga, Yesu Khristu, pokhapokha atachapa zovala zawo m'magazi a mwanawankhosa ndi kudziyeretsa ku tchimo.

Tulukani pakati pake, anthu anga, popeza hule lalikulu lidzaweruzidwa modzidzimutsa ndipo mopanda mankhwala. Nthawi ya masautso a Yakobo yatsala pang'ono kufika, ndipo chiweruzo chachikulu ndi tsoka zidzabwera padziko lapansi. Muli ndi chipulumutso pamilomo panu ngati muvomereza ine, Yesu wa ku Nazarete, monga Ambuye wanu ndi kukhulupirira kuti Mulungu anandiukitsa kwa akufa. Kodi machimo anu ndi amtengo wapatali kwambiri kwa inu? Kodi zosangalatsa za tchimo ndi zofunika kwambiri kuti muike pangozi moyo wosatha mumdima wakunja? Kodi mukulakalaka nyanja yamoto? Itanani pa Ambuye pamene ndikupezeka, ndipo ndiitaneni pamene ndidzakumvani, chifukwa nthawi ikuyandikira pamene palibe munthu angagwire ntchito.

Ngakhale Ambuye adzatumiza aneneri ake mu nthawi ya masautso a Yakobo, mudzaima kuti pamene Ambuye adzatenga anthu ake? Ndani adzakutumikirani pamene Mpingo udzachotsedwa pakati panu? Kudzakhala kusowa kwa malemba m'dziko, ndipo mudzafunika kupeza Khristu popirira mpaka mpweya wanu wotsiriza, ngati mwadalitsidwa mokwanira kufika pamenepo. Lapani ndi kubwerera kwa Ambuye! Tulukani mwa iye, anthu anga, ndipo mukhale opatulika. Ndiima pakhomo ndikugogoda.

Mzimu wa uneneri watsikira pa anthu anga padziko lonse lapansi, monga ndinalonjeza m'malemba oyera.

Machitidwe 2:16-21

Koma ichi ndi chimene chinanenedwa ndi Mneneri Yoweli;

Ndipo kudzachitika m'masiku otsiriza (atero Mulungu) ndidzathira Mzimu wanga pa thupi lonse: ndipo ana anu aamuna ndi ana anu aakazi adzanenera, ndipo anyamata anu adzaona masomphenya, ndipo akulu anu adzalota maloto:

Ndipo pa atumiki anga ndi pa adzakazi anga ndidzathira m'masiku amenewo Mzimu wanga, ndipo adzanenera: Ndipo ndidzaonetsa zozizwitsa kumwamba, ndi zizindikiro padziko lapansi pansi: magazi, ndi moto, ndi utsi wochuluka: Dzuwa lidzasanduka mdima, ndi mwezi kukhala magazi, lisanadze tsiku lalikulu ndi loonekera la Ambuye. Ndipo kudzachitika, kuti yense amene adzaitana pa dzina la Ambuye adzapulumutsidwa.
