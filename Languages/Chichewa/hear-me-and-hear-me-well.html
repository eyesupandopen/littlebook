M'ntchito za Mtumwi Paulo

Bingu 7

Ndimvereni Ndipo Ndimvereni Bwino!

Ndimvereni ndipo ndimvereni bwino, inu aneneri onama amene mumasokoneza Mawu anga oyera, pakuti simuli anga, ndipo mulibe mzimu wanga woyera, ndipo mwaukira Mkwatibwi wanga, ngakhale Mpingo wanga umene ndinawuitana! Imvani ndi kumvera chenjezo langa, inu mtundu wopanda chikhulupiriro ndi wokhota ndi wopotoka, pakuti mudzayankha pa chinthu chilichonse choipa! Imvani mawu anga achenjezo, inu amene mumasocheretsa Mpingo wanga, pakuti mkwiyo wanga wayaka pa inu, pakuti ndine Mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu! Pakuti ambiri a inu mudzabisala m'mapanga, ndi m'matanthwe a mapiri. Ndipo mudzati kumapiri ndi kumatanga, tigwereni, ndi kutibisa pankhope pa iye amene akhala pampando wachifumu, ndi kumkwiyo wa Mwanawankhosa: Pakuti tsiku lalikulu la mkwiyo wake lafika, ndipo ndani adzatha kuima?

Pakuti dziko lapansi lidzaona kuonekera kwanga, ndipo kugwedezeka kwakukulu kudzafika pa onse, ndipo chiwonongeko chadzidzidzi chidzagwera dziko lapansi pamene Mkwatibwi wanga adzatengedwa kupita kuchitetezo. Pakuti ndidzachotsa choletsa, ndipo choipa chonse chidzakhala chopanda choletsa, ndipo chiweruzo chachikulu chidzaponyedwa pansi kuchokera kumwamba, monga momwe kwalembedwera m'buku lalikulu la vumbulutso langa, ngakhale buku la Chivumbulutso! Chotero ndikulengeza chiweruzo pa inu, nonse amene mukuipitsa Mpingo wanga: masiku anu awerengedwa, ndipo posachedwa mitima yanu idzakulepherani pamene mkwiyo wanga udzavumbulutsidwa. Inu aneneri onama opanda pake amene mwalowa m'Mpingo wanga, mwaphunzitsa ufiti wosakanikirana ndi zamatsenga pamodzi ndi Mawu anga oyera ndi njira zanga zoyera, ndipo mudzalipira.

Mwachititsa ang'ono anga kuchimwa ndi kugwidwa m'mavuto aakulu, kotero kuti ndidzabwezera kwa inu mowirikiza kakhumi m'chiweruzo changa pa inu. Mwatanthauziranso mawu m'malemba anga oyera, mwapanga malemba anga oyera achinyengo powalembanso ndi kusintha mawu a Mulungu Wamuyaya, ngakhale mawu a Yehova, ndipo mudzalipira mpaka muyaya. Mwalowetsa malemba oipitsidwa m'Mpingo wanga mofanana ndi momwe makolo anu opanda pake anaipitsira zitsime ndi kuipitsa zakudya zomwe zinaperekedwa kuti anthu akhale ndi moyo, ndipo mudzalipira mpaka muyaya. Mawu onse opanda pake amene mwayankhula, ndalama iliyonse yomaliza yachinyengo imene mumasunga kuti mudziwononge nokha, lingaliro lililonse loipa limene munakhalapo nalo, cholinga chilichonse choipa chimene munachitsata chidzaperekedwa kwa inu, ndipo mudzalipira, ngakhale mpaka kakhobiri komaliza; ndipo simudzatha kuima pamaso panga mwanjira iliyonse, ndipo mudzatenga malo anu m'moto umene Atate wanga anakonzera Mdyerekezi ndi angelo ake.

Koma mvetsetsani kuti simuli odabwitsa ndipo mulibe zodabwitsa mwa inu, pakuti ndinu atumwi onama, antchito achinyengo, odzisandutsa atumwi a Khristu. Ndipo nzosadabwitsa, pakuti Satana mwiniwake adzisandutsa mngelo wakuwala. Chifukwa chake, si chinthu chachikulu ngati atumiki akenso adzisandutsa ngati atumiki achilungamo, omwe mapeto awo adzakhala monga mwa ntchito zawo. Pakuti momwemo ndinalembera ku Korinto!

Mudzapitiriza ntchito zanu kudzaza machimo anu mpaka pamwamba ndi kudzaza muyaya mpaka pamwamba ndi chilango chanu ndi mazunzo anu, pakuti mkwiyo wanga ndi woopsa pa inu, ndipo mudzaphatikizana ndi atate anu pamoto. Pakuti ndinu namsongole pakati pa tirigu, ndipo ndidzakuzulani mpaka kumapeto; pakuti Atate wanga, ngakhale Yehova, walengeza kuti simudzakhala ndi muzu kapena cholowa, pakuti mbewu yanu ndi ya woipayo, ndipo nonse ndinu akupha ndi abodza. Pakuti ndinawauza chiyani atate anu ku Yudeya zaka za Yubile zambiri zapitazo? Inu muli a atate wanu mdyerekezi, ndipo zolakalaka za atate wanu mufuna kuchita: iye anali wakupha kuyambira pachiyambi, ndipo sanakhale m'choonadi, chifukwa mulibe choonadi mwa iye. Pamene ayankhula bodza, ayankhula za iye mwini: pakuti iye ndi wabodza, ndi atate wake wa bodza.

Dziwani izi, nonse amene mwabadwa m'mwano, ngakhale mwano wotsutsana ndi Yehova pamene atate anu opanda pake anayesa kusintha chifaniziro cha Mulungu, chomwe Adamu analengedwa nacho, kukhala zonyansa zopangidwira chiwonongeko cha anthu, masiku anu atsala pang'ono kutha. Pakuti ndani mwa aneneri amene atate anu sanawazunze? Ndipo anawapha amene anaonetsa pasadakhale za kubwera kwa Wolungamayo; amene inu tsopano mwakhala opereka ndi akupha ake. Simudzapulumuka, mosasamala kanthu kuti mupita kutali bwanji m'dziko lapansi kukabisala mkwiyo wanga; mkwiyo wanga udzakupezani, pakuti palibe chomwe chingabisike pamaso panga, ndipo dziko lapansi ndi langa ndi kudzaza kwake, pakuti ndalagula ndi magazi anga omwe makolo anu anakhetsa mwamwano, ngakhale kusindikiza tsogolo lanu m'moto. Ngakhale Ine, Khristu Yesu, wokwera ndi wolemekezedwa, ndikubwera kudzakutengani, ndipo simudzapulumuka.

Ndimvereni, inu ogwa, ngakhale nonse amene munandibaya, pakuti munati mwa inu nokha, tamugonjetsa Yehova ndi kupha mwana wake amene anamutuma kutsutsana nafe kuti agonjetse dziko lapansi, ndipo ndife opambana mpaka muyaya, pakuti Yeshua wamwalira, ndipo munakondwera mopusa! Inu opusa odzikuza opanda chidziwitso! Simunamvetsetse kuti Mulungu adzandiukitsa, atamasula zowawa za imfa: chifukwa sikunali kotheka kuti ndigwidwe nayo. Munangondivulaza chidendene changa, ndipo mulibe mphamvu pa ine, ndipo simunamvetsetse kuti wam'mwambamwamba, ngakhale mlengi wanu, Yehova, anangokuonetsani zinsinsi zopanda pake!

Pakuti anadziwa kuti mudzamutembenukira iye ndi chilengedwe chake ndipo mudzayesa kutenga zomwe anandikonzera ine, Ambuye Yesu Khristu. Ofatsa anga adzalandira dziko lapansi, pakuti dziko lapansi ndi langa ndi kudzaza kwake. Pakuti mwaphunzitsa anthu chidziwitso choletsedwa; mwaphunzitsa nkhondo; mwasocheretsa akazi m'kudzikongoletsa zikope ndi nkhope zopaka utoto kuti mukwaniritse zolakalaka zanu pa iwo; mwaipitsa Munda wa Mulungu pobzala namsongole pakati pa tirigu, ndipo namsongole aliyense amene munabzala nthawi zonse adzazulidwa, kumangidwa, ndi kuponyedwa pamoto pamodzi ndi inu! Pakuti munagonjetsedwa pamene ndinadzipereka mwaufulu chifukwa cha chipulumutso cha anthu, ndipo Mulungu anandiukitsa kwa akufa kuti ndisafe nso!

Koma munali opusa kwambiri kuti musamvetsetse kuti ndidzapereka moyo wanga chifukwa cha chikondi cha Mulungu chomwe analenga mwa ine, pakuti ndine mpulumutsi wa dziko lapansi! Chikondi cha Yehova sichingagonjetsedwe, pakuti Yehova ndi wosagonjetsedwa, ndipo palibe paliponse m'chilengedwe cholengedwa chofanana naye, pakuti iye ndi wam'mwambamwamba, ndipo kuwala kwake ndi ukulu wake ndi wamphamvu zonse kuyambira muyaya mpaka muyaya! Pakuti ndinakuuzani, "ichi chimene uchita chita msanga!" Inu mosadziwa ndi modzikuza simunamvetsetse kuti ndinadzipereka kuti ndigwidwe ndi aganyu anu opanda pake omwe anachita ntchito yoipa yopha magazi osalakwa! M'kudzikuza kwanu kosadziwa, minalephera kuzindikira kuti ndinali ndi magulu khumi ndi awiri a angelo pansi pa ulamuliro wanga, pakuti nthawi iliyonse ndikanatha kukuletsani. Koma kodi chifuniro cha Yehova chikanakwaniritsidwa bwanji, kupatula mwa chikondi changa ndi kumvera Atate wanga? Pakuti chikondi cha Yehova palibe amene angachigonjetse! Ayi, osati kumwamba ndipo osati padziko lapansi, osati mwanjira iliyonse ndipo osati mpaka kalekale!

Inu ogwa, ngakhale inu akalonga a dziko lapansi logwali, inu asatana, nonse mudzapsa m'moto wosazimitsika chifukwa cha chidani chanu chosayera ndi njira zanu zoipa kwambiri zakusamvera ndi chiwonongeko. Monga munachitira mmodzi wa ang'ono awa, abale anga, munachitira ine. O, inu alonda ogwa, ngakhale inu angelo osamvera omwe munasiya malo anu oyamba, mulibe mtendere! Mudzaponyedwa m'moto womwe simudzatulukamo, ndipo mudzapsa kotheratu. Zonyansa zanu zonse zosayera za namsongole zomwe munabzala podutsa malire a Yehova m'kusintha kwanu kwa majini pakati pa munthu ndi mngelo, pakati pa munthu ndi chilombo, pakati pa munthu ndi zolengedwa zam'nyanja, pakati pa zilombo zamitundumitundu, ngakhale chinthu chilichonse chonyansa chomwe mwapanga chidzaponyedwa m'moto pamodzi ndi inu, pakuti mulibe mtendere kapena chikhululukiro!

Mwatsatira asatana omwe anayesa kuwononga chilengedwe cha Yehova, ngakhale mpaka kundipha ine, Yesu Khristu, pamene ndinadzipereka mofunitsitsa m'manja mwake monga Paskha wangwiro wa dziko lapansi, potero kusindikiza chiwombolo cha anthu ndi kusindikiza tsogolo lanu mpaka muyaya m'moto waukulu womwe sungazimitsidwe! Mudzalipira kotheratu machimo anu onse ndi zonyansa zanu, ndipo ntchito iliyonse yoipa yomwe munachitapo idzatembenuzidwa pa inu mowirikiza kakhumi, pakuti onse adzabwezeredwa monga mwa ntchito zawo. Ndipo njira zanu zonse zoipa zidzabwezeredwa pamitu yanu kachulukirachulukira, pakuti munaganiza kuti mungandigonjetse, ndipo modzikuza mukuganizabe kuti mungapambane pamene mwagonjetsedwa kotheratu.

Imvani mawu anga, o, njoka yakale, pakuti chiweruzo chanu chili pa inu ndipo ndikuona mantha anu, pakuti mukuopa tsogolo lanu kuposa china chilichonse, o, mkango wopanda mano wopanda pake! Kubwezera ndi kwanga, ndipo ndidzabwezera, ndipo nthawi yanu yafika. Mkwiyo wa Mulungu ndi mkwiyo wa mwanawankhosa watsala pang'ono kumasulidwa pamene ndidzathyola zisindikizo zisanu ndi ziwiri, pamene angelo asanu ndi awiri atumizidwa kuti alize maseŵenga a chiweruzo, ndipo angelo asanu ndi awiri atumizidwa omwe ali ndi miliri isanu ndi iwiri yomaliza, ndipo idzamasulidwa, pakuti mwa iyo mwadzazidwa mkwiyo wa Mulungu. Tsoka lambiri lidzakupezani nonse, pamene ndikulanga kotheratu ndi kugwetsa ufumu wanu. Mfumu yanu yayikulu ndidzaigonjetsa ndipo mneneri wanu wonama ndidzamugonjetsa, ndipo adzaponyedwa amoyo m'nyanja yamoto yoyaka ndi sulufule; ndipo kumeneko adzakudikirirani kuti muphatikizane nawo, ndipo kumeneko mudzakhala omizidwa m'moto ndi sulufule, ndipo chiweruzo chanu chatsala pang'ono kuyamba!

Mkwatibwi wanga, usaope! Mawu anga achiweruzo sali a inu koma a chitonthozo chanu, pakuti ndakupulumutsani ku mkwiyo womwe watsala pang'ono kumasulidwa, ndipo ndidzakubisani m'chitetezo ndi mphoto yayikulu, ndipo mudzakhala nane mpaka kalekale. Pakuti ndakusankhani, pakuti ndakuitanani, ndipo ndinu chuma changa chachikulu, ndipo ndinu chuma chachikulu cha Yehova; ndinu ngale yamtengo wapatali, pakuti ndapereka zonse zanga chifukwa cha inu. Ndikulangiza Mkwatibwi wanga kuti afunefune kuzindikira ndi kubwera kwa ine kuti apeze nzeru, pakuti ndidzakuphunzitsani. Pakuti ena a inu mwatsatira zina mwa njira za aneneri onamawa; ingobwererani m'kulapa kwa Ambuye wanu, ndipo ndidzakulandirani.

Pakuti akukopani ndi ufiti wachikhristu, ndipo mwakhulupirira mabodza ambiri ogulitsidwa kwa inu, ngakhale kukuuzani ngati choonadi, koma ndi mabodza onyansa. Zitsime zopanda madzi izi zayesa kukukokani kuchoka kwa ine, ndipo zimenezo nzosatheka, choncho musaope, pakuti sindidzakanidwa! Ndidzapitiriza kukumasulani kuti mukhale omasuka ndithu, ndipo sindidzatontholetsedwa! Ndikumenyerani nkhondo m'njira zambiri, zambiri zomwe simukuzidziwa. Pakuti nonse munalembedwa chizindikiro musanabadwe, pakuti Atate anakudziwani ngakhale m'mimba mwa amayi anu, ndipo anadikira aliyense wa inu kwa nthawi yomwe simungaimvetsetse, ndipo mwakhala muli pamtima pake nthawi zonse. Munalembedwa chizindikiro pobadwa, ndipo angelo anga anakusungani palamulo langa ndipo akulonderani ndi kukutetezani, pakuti atumizidwa kutero. Kodi izi zikukudabwitsani? Kodi mudzamvera zomwe Ahebri akunena? Koma kwa mngelo uti anati nthawi iliyonse, khala kudzanja langa lamanja, kufikira nditaika adani ako pansi pa mapazi ako? Kodi onse sali mizimu yotumikira, yotumidwa kukatumikira iwo, amene adzakhala olowa nyumba a chipulumutso?

Mkwatibwi wanga, ndinu olowa nyumba a chipulumutso mwa ine, Yesu Khristu. Mwaitanidwa mwa ine; mwatetezedwa mwa ine ndi ine; mwapatsidwa mwayi womva kulalikidwa kwa chikhulupiriro chokhudza ine kuti mundipange kukhala Ambuye wanu, ndipo chifukwa chake ndakusindikizani ndi mzimu woyera wa lonjezo. Dziwani kuti chisindikizo sichingathyokeke, ndipo mwatsala pang'ono kuwomboledwa kotheratu mpaka muyaya, pakuti mulibe lingaliro la kudzaza kwa ntchito yolenga yomwe ndatsala pang'ono kumaliza mwa inu, Mkwatibwi wanga! Dziwani kuti ogwa ndi namsongole wawo akukuchitirani nsanje, ndipo amadana ndi chilichonse chomwe Yehova amakonda, ndipo ndidzakubwezerani kotheratu pamitu yawo, pakuti ndine amene ndidzavulaza mutu wa njoka yakaleyo, pakuti kwalengezedwa, ndipo zomwe Yehova alengeza sizingasinthidwe.

Palibe amene angakulekanitseni ndi ine; palibe kuchuluka kwa choipa komwe kungathyole chisindikizocho; ndinu anga ndipo ndinu a Atate, pakuti zonse zomwe Atate andipatsa ndidzazibwezera kwa Mulungu kuti tikhale onse mwa onse! Ndipo wandipatsa zinthu zonse kumwamba ndi padziko lapansi, kupatula pampando wake wachifumu iye ndi wamkulu kuposa ine!

Ndipo Ine, Khristu Yesu, ndikulengeza mawu awa kwa inu, ndipo ndine, Yesu Khristu, amene ndikupereka chiweruzo pa adani anu, ndipo ndidzachikwaniritsa mpaka kakhobiri komaliza. Mkwatibwi wanga, chikondi changa pa inu nchosazimitsika, ndipo ndakukonzerani malo, pakuti m'nyumba ya Atate wanga muli malo okhala ambiri! Chotero posachedwa mudzakwatiwa ndi ine mpaka kalekale, ndipo mudzakhala nane nthawi zonse, ndipo mudzamvetsetsa ukulu wa cholinga chanu mwa ine chomwe munalengedwera kuti mukwaniritse!

Pakuti mwa ine, Yehova wakupatsani tsogolo ndi cholinga, ngakhale cholinga cha mibadwo chatsala pang'ono kuvumbulutsidwa kwa inu, pakuti ndikubwera! Ndipo sindidzakanidwa. Yembekezerani kuonekera kwanga. Yembekezerani kuwala kwa kubwera kwanga, pakuti kwatsala pang'ono kuwala padziko lapansi pamene ndikuonekera m'mitambo ndi mfuu wamphamvu, ndi mawu a mngelo wamkulu, ndi lipenga la Mulungu, pakuti ndikukuuzani choonadi kuti ngakhale angelo akudikira lipenga lomwe latsala pang'ono kulira, ndipo ali okonzeka! Khalani okonzeka kwa ine ndipo yang'anani, dikirani, penyani monga mwalamulidwira; pakuti posachedwa mudzandiona ine ndi ulemerero wanga womwe Atate wandiveka, pakuti momwemo ndidzakuvekani, pakuti mudzakhala ngati ine, ndipo mudzandiona monga ndiliri.

Ndine Mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu!

Ndikubwera kudzapereka mkwiyo pa ufumu wonse wa Satana, ndipo sadzapulumuka.

Ndine mpulumutsi wa Dziko Lapansi!

Ndine Khristu Yesu ndipo kubwezera ndi kwanga ndipo ndidzabwezera!

Ndine amene ndidzavulaza mutu wa njoka, pakuti Mulungu Wamphamvuzonse walengeza izi! Ndatsala pang'ono kuvumbulutsidwa m'mitambo chifukwa cha Mkwatibwi wanga, amene ndidzamutenga.

Ndine amene ndikubwera pa kavalo woyera.

Ndine amene ndimatchedwa wokhulupirika ndi woona, ndipo m'chilungamo ndidzaweruza ndi kupanga nkhondo.

Ndikubwera!
