Ambiri a inu mumagwiritsitsa vesi la m'Chipangano Chakale lotengedwa kunja kwa nkhani yake: kuganiza mwaumbuli kuti muyenera kuthandiza anthu amisala opha fuko kuti mudalitsidwe ndi ine. Ndimadalitsa amene aitanidwa ndi dzina langa, ndipo ngakhale ndimagwetsa mvula pa olungama ndi osalungama omwe, madalitso onse ali mwa ine, Khristu Yesu Ambuye wanu, Ambuye wa ambuye woona ndi Mfumu ya mafumu yoona. Chotero, bwererani kwa ine mu kulapa, pakuti mwatenga mabodza a Yezebeli ngati choonadi cha uthenga wabwino, ndipo mwadziunjikira mkwiyo pochita zimenezi; pakuti ndidzapatsa ngakhale ana anga malinga ndi ntchito zawo, ngakhale sindidzakuweruzani ndi machimo anu.

Ndine Mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu wosankhidwa lisanakhazikitsidwe Dziko lapansi.

Ndine Mfumu yankhondo yotumizidwa ndi Yahweh.

Ndine amene kumwamba kuyenera kumulandira kufikira nthawi yakubwezeretsedwa kwa zinthu zonse.

Ndine amene anabatizidwa ndi Yohane Mbatizi pamene mzimu unatsika ngati nkhunda ku phokoso la mawu a Atate wanga.

Ndine amene ndinabwera kwa anga ndipo ndinakanidwa, munthu wachisoni amene analipira mtengo wapatali kwambiri chifukwa cha inu nonse.

Ndine wamkulu kuposa onse kupatula Atate wanga, Yahweh.
