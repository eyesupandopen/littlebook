O Isiraeli! Ndi Mwanawankhosa Uti Amene Mudzasankhe?

Imvani Mawu a Yahweh, Ine Ndine Wamkulu, ndipo tathamangirani kumveka kwa Mawu anga kuti mulandire nzeru ndipo kuti munthu wanzeru pakati panu akhale wanzeru kwambiri.

Taonani! Ndabwera kudzakuuzani za zinthu zimene zikubwera kuti mudziwe chitsimikizo cha Mawu anga ndi kuti mufike kuchipulumutso chanu chokwanira mwa Yeshua HaMashiach, yemwe alidi Mesiya wanu weniweni, Mfumu, Ambuye, ndi Mkulu wa Ansembe kwamuyaya monga mwa ndondomeko ya Melikizedeki.

Choncho, ndikulamula kuti mumumvere ndi moyo wanu wonse ndipo mudule mitima yanu pomvera! Chifukwa, ndi Yeshua HaMashiach, ngakhale Yesu Khristu m'chinenero cha Chingerezi yemwe waperekedwa osati kokha chifukwa cha chipulumutso cha Isiraeli, komanso chifukwa cha chipulumutso cha mtundu wonse wa Adamu chifukwa onse amene adzaitana pa dzina la Ambuye mpaka mapeto adzapulumutsidwa kaya akhale Mwisiraeli kapena wa mitundu ina, ndipo Yeshua HaMashiach adzapulumutsa amoyo otsalira a Isiraeli ngakhale kuchokera ku mtundu wa Adamu omwe adzapitirize mu ufumu wa zaka chikwi.

Ndipo zili choncho, kuti ndinapereka zomwe zinali zosatheka kuti mtundu wa Adamu udziperekere wekha pa chipulumutso chake chifukwa mtundu wa Adamu ukanangopeza chiwombolo chokwanira m'mwazi wa mwanawankhosa. Chifukwa ndine, Yahweh, Ine Ndine Wamkulu amene ndinapereka Paskha wanu wangwiro ngakhale kamodzi, tsopano, ndi kwamuyaya ndipo nthawi zonse nsembe yake imalandiridwa, choncho, palibenso chifukwa cha nsembe, O Isiraeli.

Monga kwalembedwa mu Ahebri, momwemonso ndikunenanso kwa inu tsopano: Pakuti ngati mwazi wa ng'ombe, ndi wa mbuzi, ndi phulusa la ng'ombe yaikazi yowazidwa pa odetsedwa, zimalekanitsa kuyeretsa thupi: Nanga kuli bwanji mwazi wa Khristu, amene kudzera mwa Mzimu wosatha, anadzipereka yekha wopanda chilema kwa Mulungu, kuyeretsa chikumbumtima chanu ku ntchito zakufa, kuti mutumikire Mulungu wamoyo? Ndipo choncho ndikukulangizani, O Isiraeli, landirani nsembe yanga chifukwa palibenso nsembe ina ya tchimo!

Choncho mvetsetsani Isiraeli, kuti n'zosatheka kwa wina aliyense kuyandikira kwa Mulungu Wamuyaya wa Chilungamo kudzera mwa Mose kachiwiri, chifukwa, ndapanga Yeshua HaMashiach kukhala njira, choonadi, ndi moyo womwe palibe munthu angabwere kwa Yahweh kupatula kudzera mwa Yeshua HaMashiach!

Ndipo ndalamula kumvera kwanu ku mawu ake, ngakhale kudzera mu Torah kumene mawu enieni a Mose akulira mpaka lero monga mboni yotsutsana nanu ndipo m'njira zanu zosaphunzira ndi zopanda chikhulupiriro m'malo momvera inu m'malo mwake mukufunafuna kukhazikitsanso kachisi wopangidwa ndi munthu, pamene mukusocheretsedwa ndi Esau! Chifukwa mwakana kachisi womangidwa ndi mlengi wanu, ngakhale kachisi wopangidwa popanda manja, ngakhale kachisi weniweni wopangidwa ndi Yahweh mwiniwake kwa Isiraeli wa Mulungu yemwe Yesu Khristu ndiye mwala waukulu wapangodya. Choncho, lekani ntchito zanu ndipo pumulani mu ntchito za Mesiya wanu weniweni ndipo ndidzakupatsani Sabata.

Choncho ndikukulamulani O Isiraeli ndipo ndine amene ndinapereka Mawu enieni olankhulidwa ndi Mose kwa Atate anu pa Sinai, Mawu omwe ndikulamula kuti muwamvere nthawi zonse ndi kwamuyaya ngakhale mukusamvera kwanu kwadala ndi kwa mtima wouma. Ndipo choncho Mose anati kuti onse amvere: Ambuye Mulungu wanu adzakuukitsirani Mneneri wofanana ndi ine, pakati panu, ngakhale wa abale anu: kwa iye mudzamvera!

Pamene Mose anali mkhalapakati wa Chipangano choyamba (cholembedwa ndi chala changa pa Sinai) choperekedwa kuti chitsutse tchimo m'thupi, Chipangano chomwe tsopano chikuwala pankhope pa Yesu Khristu, ngakhale Yeshua HaMashiach yemwe ali Ambuye ndi Mesiya wanu weniweni! Ndipo komabe Chilamulo cha Mose ndi mthunzi wa zinthu zikubwera zomwe zakwaniritsidwa mwa Khristu Yesu ndipo ndamupanga iye kukhala mzimu wopereka moyo ndipo pambuyo pa thupi Yesu wa ku Nazarete sadziwikanso!

Ndipo monga momwe zinakwaniritsidwira ndi dzanja langa lamphamvu, ngakhale chala chenicheni cha YHVH Ine Ndine Wamkulu, ndinalemba malemba anga m'magome amiyala pa Sinai pamene ndinapatsa Mose malemba anga kuti Isiraeli akhalebe ndi moyo pamaso panga. Komabe, chomwe chinaperekedwa kwa Isiraeli pa Sinai chinali chitsutso cha tchimo m'thupi kuti Isiraeli akhale ndi mphunzitsi wowabweretsa kwa Mesiya wawo weniweni, ngakhale Yeshua HaMashiach yemwe ndamutumiza chifukwa cha chipulumutso chanu chokwanira.

Ndipo zili choncho, chilamulo cha Mose ndi chabwino, ndi cholungama, ndi changwiro, ndi choyera ndipo ngati munthu aliyense atemberera chilamulo cha Mose aloledwe kuweruzidwa nazo mu kuuma mtima konse komwe kwalembedwa pansi pa temberero ndi chilango cha chilamulo cha Mose. Ndipo komabe chomwe chili chabwino ndi cholungama ndi changwiro ndi choyera chinagwira ntchito imfa kwa onse otsutsidwa mmenemo ndi tchimo m'thupi, chifukwa pamene palibe chilamulo tchimo silimawerengedwa, chifukwa chake chilamulo cha Mose chaperekedwa kuti chiulule ndi kutsutsa tchimo m'thupi.

Ndipo komabe ndi mwa mzimu wa chilamulo osati chilembo kuti anthu amapeza chifundo m'chikondi changa pa iwo, chifukwa chilembo cha chilamulo chimabweretsa kuuma mtima (Kodi simunawerenge kuti ndikanafuna chifundo kuposa nsembe?) ndipo choncho m'chikondi changa kwa ana a Isiraeli omwe ndikufuna kuwapulumutsa ngati mutamvera Yeshua HaMashiach, ndinalamula nsembe chifukwa popanda kukhetsa mwazi palibe chikhululukiro cha tchimo. Ndipo choncho ndi dzanja loyang'anira la Mose anthu anga asungidwa mpaka Khristu, ngakhale ambiri a inu mwatembenukira kwa milungu yonyenga ndipo mumakonda makiyubu anu akuda, zinthu zomwe zili zonyansa kotheratu pamaso panga, O Isiraeli, choncho chokani nthawi yomweyo!

Choncho mvetsetsani mkwiyo wanga pa inu O Isiraeli, chifukwa INE NDINE YHVH, Ine Ndine wamkulu ngakhale iye amene ali Mulungu Wamphamvuyonse Wamuyaya wa Chilungamo amene sakomoka ndipo palibe chinthu chovuta kwambiri kuti dzanja langa lamphamvu lichikwaniritse. Ndipo ndine, El Shaddai, Mulungu Wamphamvuyonse amene anapanga chilengedwe mu nzeru zanga zamuyaya ndipo ndi mphamvu yaikulu ya mzimu wanga zinthu zonse zimagwiridwa pamodzi pomvera mawu anga.

INE NDINE Elohim wanu, amene 10,000 kuwirikiza 10,000 azungulira mpando wanga wachifumu, ngakhale gulu lalikulu la Angelo la mizimu yotumikira omwe atumiki awo ndi lawi lamoto omwe amapambana mu nzeru ndipo analengedwa mwanzeru ndi mwangwiro monga momwe ndawalengera m'chikondi changa pa iwo! Ndipo m'chikondi chawo pa ine, amamvera mwangwiro chifuniro changa chamuyaya ndipo ndapatsa Yeshua HaMashiach ulamuliro pa onsewo.

Komabe, Ine YHVH Ine Ndine Wamkulu, ngakhale iye amene wazunguliridwa ndi gulu la Angelo losawerengeka SAKUFUNA WOPEREKA UPANGIRI mu nzeru zanga zamuyaya ndipo pa chilengedwe changa ndine wolamulira ndipo ndimagwira ntchito ZONSE pambuyo pa uphungu wa chifuniro changa, ndipo pa anzeru akuluakulu a muyaya onse palibe anzeru kuposa Ine Ndine Wamkulu! Choncho imvani mawu anga ndipo tseketsani malilime anu ngati musankha kulandira moyo wosatha m'malo mwa malawi amoto osazimitsidwa a chilango O Isiraeli chifukwa chimenecho ndi chisankho chomwe munthu aliyense ayenera kupanga.

Ndipo mu nzeru zanga zamuyaya ndinatumiza Yesu wa ku Nazarete kwa Isiraeli, ngakhale pamene Yudeya ankalamulidwa ndi Pontiyo Pilato, ngakhale pamene ansembe aakulu ndi Sanhedrini anamuneneza mopanda chilungamo ndipo ndi manja oipa mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu anaphedwa ndi kupachikidwa ngakhale kuti thupi la tchimo lomwe limamanga Adamu liwonongedwe mwa onse omwe amakhala ndi chikhulupiriro momvera, chifukwa wolungama adzakhala ndi moyo ndi chikhulupiriro! Ndipo INE NDINE iye, ngakhale El Shaddai (Mulungu Wamphamvuyonse) amene waika zinthu zonse kumwamba ndi padziko lapansi m'manja amphamvu a Khristu Yesu, ngakhale Yeshua HaMashiach ndipo iye ndi Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda amene INE NDINE ndikumutumiza kudzaweruza ndi kupanga nkhondo ndi chifukwa cha chipulumutso cha okhulupirika ndi omvera. Choncho, imvani Mawu anga pomvera Mesiya wanu weniweni, pakuti palibe wina!

Choncho mvetsetsani mkwiyo wanga pa inu O Isiraeli kuti mu khungu lanu lina lomwe munatengera kwa Atate anu a mitima youma, omwe mawu awo a Talmudic mumawalemekeza kwambiri kuposa Elohim wanu. Inde, mumasunga miyambo ya omwe amatchedwa anzeru anu ndi anthu anzeru mwaulemu waukulu kuposa Mawu a Ine Ndine Wamkulu, yemwe ndi mlengi wamuyaya ndi wosatha wa zonse zomwe zalengedwa! Pakuti ndi YHVH zinthu zonse zinalengedwa, zomwe zili kumwamba, ndi zomwe zili padziko lapansi, zinthu zooneka ndi zosaoneka: kaya zikhale mipando yachifumu kapena maufumu, kapena maulamuliro, kapena mphamvu, zinthu zonse zinalengedwa ndi ine, ndi chifukwa cha ine, Ndipo INE NDINE ndisanakhale zinthu zonse, ndipo mwa YHVH zinthu zonse zikhalapo.

Komabe, mukuganiza kuti ndinu wopereka uphungu wanga ndipo mopusa mumadzitcha anthu anga osankhidwa pamene mukukana Mawu anga mu kusamvera kopanda chikhulupiriro? Oh Isiraeli mwapatukira ku mafano osalankhula a mtima ndi mawu onyenga a Esau omwe uphungu wawo mumaukonda kuposa Mulungu Wamuyaya wa Chilungamo, ngakhale Elohim wanu! Ndipo mukupunthwa mu khungu lina chifukwa mwa Mose mitima yanu yaphimbidwa. Komabe, pamene mtima wanu udzatembenukira kwa Mesiya wanu weniweni, chophimbacho chidzachotsedwa ndipo m'malo mwa khungu lina mudzaona!

Ndinu ndani, O Isiraeli, kuti mudziike nokha kukhala wopereka uphungu wa Yahweh? Komabe m'malo mwa Mawu a Yahweh operekedwa pankhope pa Yeshua HaMashiach, mwakhulupirira mabodza operekedwa mu miyambo ya akulu anu omwe anali mu ukapolo kwa Esau, omwe zolemba zawo mwadzimanga nokha kwa Esau m'njira yofananayo ndipo kwa Esau mwamangidwa maunyolo! Ndipo ndinalemba momveka bwino kuti Mose apereke kwa Atate anu pa Sinai: Chinthu chilichonse chomwe ndikukulamulani, samalani kuchichita: musawonjezerepo, kapena kuchotserapo. Ndipo kachiwiri ndinachilemba mu nzeru za Solomoni, nzeru zomwe zimaikidwa kwamuyaya: Mawu onse a Elohim ndi oyera: iye ndi chishango kwa iwo, omwe amamukhulupirira iye. Musawonjezere kalikonse pa mawu ake, kuti angakudzudzule, ndipo mupezeka wabodza.

Oh Isiraeli, kuti muweramitse mitima yanu pomvera Mawu operekedwa chifukwa cha chipulumutso chanu ndi chimwemwe chamuyaya, ngati mutamveranso Mawu a mlengi wanu! Ndipo INE NDINE iye amene adzakubwezeretsani ndi kukubweretsani pakati pa gulu lamuyaya lomwe lili pansi pa dzanja lamphamvu la Mbusa wanga Wamkulu yemwe ndamupatsa zinthu zonse! Ngakhale Yeshua HaMashiach, yemwe amaweta Isiraeli weniweni wa Mulungu mwangwiro m'chikondi chake chomvera mawu anga, ngakhale chitsanzo chomwe mumachinyoza. Chifukwa Yeshua HaMashiach ndi munthu yekhayo amene amamvera mwangwiro chifuniro cha YHVH, Ine Ndine Wamkulu yemwe ndimakondwera naye nthawi zonse, choncho ndamupanga iye kukhala mzimu wopereka moyo choncho mumvereni iye amene ndamutumiza chifukwa cha inu chifukwa palibe wina.

Ndipo mwaumitsa mitima yanu molimba ngati Mwala wa Maziko wogwidwa ukapolo ndi Sunagoge wa Satana chifukwa mwakana mawu a uphungu wanga operekedwa pankhope pa Khristu Yesu, yemwe mukumukana mosazindikira, potero mukukana chiyembekezo chanu chokha pa kubwezeretsedwa ndi chipulumutso kwamuyaya ndi nthawi zonse. Ndipo choncho mukuima akhungu ndi amaliseche ndi amanyazi mu tchimo lanu, mukuganiza mosazindikira kuti mwakhala wopereka uphungu kwa YHVH, Ine Ndine Wamkulu!

Choncho ndikukupatsani kuti mumvetsetse mu khungu lanu lina kuti mwapunthwa pa mwala wanga waukulu wapangodya womwe omanga anu anaukana, ngakhale mwala wanga wopunthwitsa. Chifukwa Yeshua HaMashiach ndi mwala waukulu wapangodya ngakhale mwala weniweni wa maziko, ngakhale Yesu wa ku Nazarete yemwe mukumukana mu kusamvera kwadala ngakhale ndinakusankhani kwa ine ndekha ngati anthu apadera ndipo mtima wanga ukusweka.

Imvani, imvani, O Isiraeli ndipo mvetsetsani kuti INE NDINE iye amene amalemba chilamulo changa chamuyaya, ngakhale chiwonetsero cha chifuniro changa chamuyaya mu dongosolo langa lamuyaya la chiwombolo lomwe ndinapanga pambuyo pa uphungu wa chifuniro changa chamuyaya ndi changwiro m'magome amiyala chifukwa cha kumvera kwa Isiraeli pa Sinai! Choncho, INE NDINE iye amene adzalemba chilamulo changa pa magome a mnofu a mitima yanu chifukwa m'chikondi changa pa inu, ngakhale mphamvu yanga yaing'ono kwambiri imatha kulemba chilamulo changa pa magome anu amiyala ndipo ndi chisomo changa ndi chifundo changa chowonetsedwa pankhope pa Yeshua HaMashiach ndidzaphwanya magome anu amiyala kukhala dongo la woumba kuti mulengedwenso kwamuyaya mu chilungamo ndi chiyero chenicheni monga momwe ndinalumbirira m'mapangano anga kwa Atate anu kuti ndidzachita.

Choncho, O Isiraeli, ndikukulamulani kuti mumvere mawu a Yeshua HaMashiach chifukwa pali chipulumutso mu dzina lina loperekedwa kuposa Yesu Khristu Wolungama, yemwe mosazindikira mumamutcha fano la amitundu chifukwa muli mu ukapolo kwa Esau!

Komabe zili zolembedwa choncho ndipo zakhala choncho: Kuchokera kwa Abrahamu mpaka kwa Isake, pamene Yakobo ndi Esau anabadwa kwa iye, dzanja la Yakobo linagwira chidendene cha Esau poyamba. Pakuti Esau ndi mapeto a dziko lapansi, ndipo Yakobo ndi chiyambi chake chomwe chikutsatira. Dzanja la munthu lili pakati pa chidendene ndi dzanja. Komabe zili choncho kuti tsopano ndidzasuntha kulekanitsa Esau ndi Yakobo chifukwa Esau ndi Sunagoge wa Satana omwe ndi namsongole woipa omwe alowa m'dziko la lonjezo kachiwiri, ndipo Isiraeli wadzazidwanso ndi Akanaani.

Ndipo monga kwalembedwa momwemonso kudzachitika akutero Ine Ndine Wamkulu: Pakuti taonani tsiku likubwera, lomwe lidzayaka ngati ng'anjo, ndipo onse onyada, inde ndi onse omwe amachita zoipa adzakhala chiputu: ndipo tsiku lomwe likubwera, lidzawatentha, akutero Ambuye wa makamu, kuti silidzawasiyira muzu kapena nthambi. Koma kwa inu omwe mumaopa Dzina langa, Mwana wa Chilungamo adzauka ndi machiritso m'mapiko ake, ndipo mudzatuluka ndi kukula ngati ana a ng'ombe a m'khola.

Ndipo pamene Yeshua HaMashiach akusonkhanitsa okhulupirika mu mpingo, ngakhale thupi lake, ndi mkwatibwi wake tsopano ndidzasuntha Yeshua HaMashiach chifukwa cha kulekanitsa komaliza pakati pa Esau ndi Yakobo, ndipo Yakobo adzapulumutsidwa ngakhale kudzera m'mayesero a sabata la makumi asanu ndi awiri la Danieli. Choncho, pasakhale wadama, kapena munthu wonyoza ngati Esau, yemwe chifukwa cha gawo limodzi la nyama anagulitsa ukulu wake. Pakuti mukudziwa momwe pambuyo pake pamene anafuna kulandira dalitso, anakaniwa: pakuti sanapeze malo a kulapa, ngakhale anafunafuna dalitsolo ndi misozi.

Ndipo choncho anthu ali pakati pa dzanja la Yakobo ndi chidendene cha Esau, kupatula kuti Esau akufuna kupatsa Yakobo bala la kumutu lomaliza, lomwe sindidzalilola. Ndipo zili choncho, kuti onse okhala padziko lapansi omwe asiyidwa ku nsautso ya Yakobo kuti apirire moto wa woyenga adzakhala a Yakobo kudzera mwa Yesu wa ku Nazarete, omwe ndi ana a cholowa, kapena adzakaniwa pakati pa Esau yemwe anagulitsa ukulu wake chifukwa cha kachidutswa ka mkate. Choncho, munthu wachibadwa adzaitana pa dzina la Yesu wa ku Nazarete mpaka mapeto ake kuti aukitsidwe mu Isiraeli wa Mulungu kapena adzagulitsanso kuthekera kwake ndi cholowa chamuyaya chifukwa cha kachidutswa ka mkate koperekedwa pa dzanja la Baraba, pakuti Baraba adzatsogolera Sunagoge wa Satana ndipo iye ndi mwanawankhosa wa chinjoka.

Ndipo komabe msampha udzakhazikitsidwa m'dzikomo kuti ukole Yakobo kwamuyaya ndipo Baraba akubweretsa chinyengo chachikulu ndi ulamuliro wake ndipo adzachita zoipa zambiri pamtunda ndi panyanja. Komabe, zidzakhala choncho kuti pamene moto wa nkhondo ukuyaka ndi kutentha kuti Baraba adzauka ngakhale kunena kuti waukitsidwa kuti akwaniritse zolinga za Mulungu ndipo adzanena zinthu zambiri zamwano ndi zoipa. Ndipo adzabweretsa nthambi yake ya Azitona ndipo adzadzitcha yekha ngati Nowa, ngakhale wamoyo kuti apulumutse Dziko lapansi ku chiweruzo chokonzedwera anthu oipa.

Chifukwa chilombo chochokera pansi chomwe chilinso mneneri wawo wonyenga chidzabweretsa yankho lake chifukwa ndi mwanawankhosa wa nyanga ziwiri yemwe amalankhula ngati chinjoka. Ndipo ulamuliro wake udzakhala wa chikhalidwe chapawiri pamene adzafuna kugwirizanitsa anthu onse pansi pa ulamuliro wa chinjoka, yemwe ndi mdierekezi wakale uja mwiniwake. Pakuti Baraba sabwera kudzalamulira yekha ngakhale adzanena kuti iye mwiniwake ndi Mulungu koma akubweretsa pangano la kugawikana kumene nyanga ziwiri zidzalamulira nyanja ndi dziko lapansi, ndipo pa ziwirizo imodzi ndi nyanga yaing'ono ya Danieli. Komabe ndalonjeza kuti Isiraeli sadzagawikananso ndipo pa mayiko ake awiri sipadzakhala yankho koma Yeshua HaMashiach adzagwirizanitsa Isiraeli mokwanira ndi kwamuyaya nthawi zonse.

(Koma zidzakhala choncho okondedwa anga, choncho mvetsetsani zomwe ndidzakwaniritse kudzera mwa Yeshua HaMashiach: Ndidzatenga ana a Israyeli pakati pa amitundu, komwe anapita, ndipo ndidzawasonkhanitsa mbali zonse, ndi kuwabweretsa m'dziko lawo. Ndipo ndidzawapanga kukhala mtundu umodzi m'dzikomo, pamapiri a Israyeli, ndipo mfumu imodzi idzakhala mfumu yawo onse: ndipo sadzakhalanso mitundu iwiri, kapena kugawidwanso kukhala maufumu awiri kuyambira tsopano. Ndipo sadzadziipitsanso ndi mafano awo, kapena ndi zonyansa zawo, kapena ndi zolakwa zawo zilizonse: koma ndidzawapulumutsa m'malo awo onse okhala, momwe anachimwira, ndipo ndidzawayeletsa: motero adzakhala anthu anga, ndipo Ine ndidzakhala Mulungu wawo.)

Chilombo china ndi chochokera kunyanja chomwe chalembedwa za icho kuti: Pakuti sakhumba akazi, kapena kulemekeza mulungu aliyense: pakuti adzidzikweza yekha pamwamba pa onse. Ndikukufunsani tsopano ndipo onani ngati mwamuna amamva zowawa zobereka mwana? Koma mkazi wake akuwoneka ngati mkazi ali ndi ana, ngakhale kuti si ana ake. Ndipo chilombo chochokera kunyanja chidzauka chogwirizana kwathunthu mu cholinga ndi chilombo chochokera pansi ndipo amanamizira kukhala adani omwe akuwoneka ogawikana pa malingaliro ndi chipembedzo.

Koma amanama ndipo amanamizira ndipo atate wawo ndi chinjoka chomwecho chomwe chapanga zilombo ziwiri zoipazi chifukwa cha zolinga zake zoyipa, ngakhale kuwononga Adamu kuti abe dziko lapansi kwamuyaya kukhala lake ndipo n'zosatheka kuti akwaniritse mapulani ake oyipa chifukwa dziko lapansi ndi la Ambuye ndi kudzaza kwake, akutero YHVH, WAMKULU NDINE AMENE NDINE!

Chifukwa Baraba akubweretsa mayiko awiri omwe adzagwirizane pansi pa chinjoka ndipo adzakhala ndi mtendere wabodza pamene akulonjeza moyo wosatha ndi thanzi ndi mtendere ngati mungotenga chizindikiro chawo ndi kuphatikizana nawo pamene akusanganiza chitsulo ndi dongo koma sadzamatirira wina ndi mzake. Mvetsetsani, inu nonse omwe mungaganize zodziphatikiza nokha kwamuyaya kwa omwe adagwa kudzera mu chizindikiro chawo kuti adzanena mabodza ambiri ponena kuti ndi mankhwala ozizwitsa omwe adzapereka moyo ndi thanzi ndipo ndi angwiro modabwitsa m'mapangidwe ake!

Koma onse omwe atenga poizoni wawo adzadzisintha okha kwamuyaya ngakhale kusintha komwe kulibe kuchira. Chifukwa kuchitira mwano chifanizo cha YHVH chomwe Adamu analengedwa nacho ndikudziphatikiza wekha ku machimo a omwe adagwa omwe amanamizira kuti ndi milungu koma sali, ndipo onse omwe amatero alibe chikhululuko ndipo alibe mtendere ndipo m'nyanja yamoto yosazimitsidwa adzapita kwamuyaya chifukwa ndi zomwe adzakhala atasankha.
