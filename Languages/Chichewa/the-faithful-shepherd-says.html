Mbusa Wokhulupirika Akuti

Mbusa wokhulupirika akuti kwa okhulupirika: Indetu, indetu, ndinena kwa inu, chokani ku chosalungama ndipo musachimwe. Pakuti mphotho yanga ili ndi ine kupatsa munthu yense monga mwa ntchito zake. Ndakuitanani m'chikondi cha Atate wanga kuti mukhale ndi moyo, ndikukhala nawo wosatha. Ndakuitanani kuti muyende mu Ufumu wanga, kunditumikira ndi kukhala pa dziko lapansi m'chilungamo. Ndakupatsani chisomo changa kuti mukule mwa ine, Ambuye wanu Yesu Khristu. Musafooke ndi zilangizo zanga, pakuti onse amene ndiwakonda, ndiwadzudzula, kuti chisomo changa chopulumutsa chizike mizu m'kati mwa moyo wanu kuti muchite ntchito zamphamvu, ntchito zamadalitso, ntchito zamachiritso, ndi kuti mubale zipatso zambiri mwa ine, anthu anga.

Pakuti Atate wanga ndi Mulungu wachikondi, ndipo chikondi cha Atate wanga sichingagonjetsedwe. Mwa ine, Ambuye wolungama, muli chidzalo chonse chimene Atate wandipatsa. Wapereka zinthu zonse m'manja mwanga, ndipo monga Farao anakweza Yosefe ndikupereka m'manja mwake Igupto yense, momwemonso Atate wanga wandikweza ine ndikupereka zinthu zonse zakumwamba ndi zapadziko lapansi m'manja mwanga. Wandipatsa dzina loposa maina onse, kuti m'dzina la Yesu bondo lililonse lipinde, lakumwamba ndi lapadziko lapansi, ndipo lilime lililonse livomereze kuti ndine Ambuye, kumwamba ndi padziko lapansi.

Ndakupatsani moyo wosatha, ndipo ndakukonzerani njira yothawira chipululu ndi kuthawa tsiku lalikulu la mkwiyo lomwe likubwera, choncho musafooke! Musatope pakuchita zabwino, koma imani pamaso panga, ndipo ndidzapitiriza kukukokerani pamaso panga. Mwakhazikitsidwa m'malo akumwamba ndipo mudzatenga mbali pampando wanga wachifumu monga momwe inenso ndimatengera mbali pampando wachifumu wa Atate wanga. Ndili ndi akorona ambiri ndi madalitso ambiri omwe asungidwa kwa inu, anthu anga okhulupirika. Imani pamaso panga ndipo mukhale angwiro mwa ine. Chokani ku machimo anu ndipo lolani nthawi zotsitsimula ziwulutse mungu m'moyo mwanu. Mwa ine muli moyo wosatha, ndipo mwa ine muli chilengedwe chatsopano, munthu watsopano, ngakhale Khristu mwa inu, chiyembekezo cha Ulemerero! Chokani ku choipa ndipo chokani ku tchimo. Pitirizani kundilengeza pakati pa amitundu, popeza pali anthu oti apulumutsidwe mu ola lomalizali. Pitirizani kulengeza dzina langa, pakuti palibe dzina lina pansi pa thambo limene anthu angapulumutsidwe nalo. Tonzeredwani mu mpumulo umene ndakupatsani, ndipo ikani katundu wanu pamapazi anga; pakuti goli langa lili lofewa, ndipo katundu wanga ali wopepuka.

Kwa okhulupirika (awo amene agonjetsa tchimo ndi masautso kudzera m'mwazi wanga wotetezera) kwa okhulupirika, ndikuti: Ndili ndi mphotho yaikulu yosungidwa kwa inu m'nyumba ya Atate wanga. Diso silinapenye, khutu silinamve, ndipo sizinalowe m'mitima ya anthu zinthu zomwe zakonzedwa kwa iwo. Ndidzakufupirani mochuluka ndi akorona ambiri, choncho imani m'malo mwa ine ndipo mukhale okhulupirika ku mawu anga ndi malangizo anga; pakuti m'mawu amenewo mudzapeza moyo. Kodi sindine Mawu amene anasanduka thupi nakhala pakati panu?

Ndine woweruza wolungama.

Ndine mbusa wabwino amene aweta gulu lankhosa.

Ndine machiritso anu, ndipo ndidzakuchiritsani mpaka pomaliza.

Ndinabadwa mwa mkazi ndikusanduka thupi, ngakhale mulibe tchimo mwa ine.

Ndinapachikidwa chifukwa cha machimo anu, ndipo zoonadi, palibe munthu anatenga moyo wanga, koma ndinaupereka mofunitsitsa chifukwa cha chikondi chomwe ndili nacho pa ana aamuna ndi aakazi a Atate wanga.

Ndinafukulidwa ku moyo tsiku lachitatu, pakuti zinali zosatheka kuti imfa indigwire. Atate wanga anadula maunyolo a imfa ndi mphamvu ya kuuka kwanga.

Ndine zipatso zoyamba za akufa.

Posachedwapa mudzakumana nane mumlengalenga, ndipo zowawa zenizeni za imfa zidzamasula awo amene agona tulo, ndipo pamenepo inu amene muli amoyo ndi otsala mudzakwatulidwa pamodzi, ndipo mudzakhala ndi ine mpaka kalekale. Ndine Mwana wa Mulungu, ndipo m'dzanja langa muli mphotho yanga kwa okhulupirika anga. Imani chilili, ana anga, ndipo musafooke. Khalani amphamvu mwa Ambuye ndi mu mphamvu ya ukulu wanga, pakuti Atate wanga yekha ndi wamkulu kuposa ine. Ndamva mapembedzero anu, ndipo ndakulandirani monga anga.

Khalani oleza mtima ndipo pitirizani kundigwirira ntchito. Pakuti posachedwapa mudzakhala kunyumba ndi ine, ndipo mpaka kalekale tidzakhala pa ntchito ya Atate wathu. Khalani amphamvu ndipo kondwerani. Ndikudziwa kuti ambiri a inu mukuona chisautso ndi tsoka zikuyandikira, ndipo ambiri a inu mwawona magulu ankhondo a woyipayo akuwoloka malire anu. Musawaope! Musawaope, ngakhale atafuna kukuwonongani ngati angathe, koma sali pano chifukwa cha okhulupirika anga, popeza kuthawa kwakukulu ndi kusonkhana kwakukulu kudzapezabe kupulumutsa okhulupirika anga ku ola la chiyeso ndi mayesero lomwe latsala pang'ono kugwera dziko lonse lapansi. Pumulani m'chikondi changa, podziwa kuti sindidzakusiyani kapena kukutayani, monga momwe Atate wanga sanandisiye kapena kunditaya mu ola langa la nsembe. Pakuti chipulumutso chanu ndi chotetezedwa mwa ine; chotero, kankhirani mwa ine, popeza ndidzakulimbitsani ngakhale kuposa momwe ndachitira. Chinsinsi cha umulungu nchachikulu!
