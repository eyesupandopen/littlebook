Kwa a 144,000: Imvani Kuitana Kwanga!

Imvani, imvani, mafuko anga khumi ndi awiri, liwu la Mesiya wanu, Mbuye, Mfumu ndi Mkulu wa ansembe, ngakhale ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, Mwana wa Munthu amene Yahweh anamuukitsa kwa akufa. Pakuti:

Ndine Mesiya wanu weniweni, wochokera kumpando wachifumu wa Davide, ndipo kudzera kumpando wake wachifumu ndidzalamulira Dziko la Lonjezo la Yahweh, Mulungu wamuyaya, pakuti anatero kulonjeza kwa Makolo anu. Ndipo mvetsetsani kuti ndine Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda, ndipo palibe mfumu kapena mneneri wapatsidwa kuchokera kwa Dani, kumene mneneri wonyenga amachokera.

Ndine Mbuye wanu, pakuti Yahweh wandipanga kukhala Mbuye wa Ambuye, ndipo ndakugulani ndi magazi anga monga Paskha wanu wangwiro, woperekedwa chifukwa cha machimo a anthu a mitundu ina ndi Ayuda omwe, kuti nonse mukhale munthu mmodzi watsopano mwa ine, ngakhale Khristu Yesu. Pakuti Yahweh wandipatsa dzina loposa maina onse ndipo wandipatsa gawo pampando wake wachifumu chifukwa cha kumvera kwanga. Ndipo ufumu wanga waperekedwa monga chowunikira cha chipulumutso, pakuti ndapangidwa kukhala mzimu wopatsa moyo, ndipo Yahweh yekha ndiye wamkulu kuposa ine, pakuti Iye ndiye Mulungu wamuyaya.

Ndine Mfumu yanu, pakuti Yahweh wandipanga kukhala Mfumu ya Mafumu, ndipo ndidzalamulira dziko lapansi m'chilungamo. Ndipo taonani! Ndikubweretsa Mkwatibwi wanga, amene adzalamulira nane, pakuti ndawapanga kukhala mafumu ndi ansembe pamaso pa Yahweh wamuyaya, amene palibe wina wofanana naye. Pakuti mu ulamuliro wanga wa zaka chikwi womwe ukubwera, ndidzakhala ulamuliro wapamwamba kwambiri, ndipo ndidzatumiza mafumu anga ndi ansembe anga kuti alamulire mu cholowa chawo mwa ine; pakuti ndawalenga mwatsopano mwa ine, ngakhale kutengedwa kwa Ayuda ndi a mitundu ina omwe ndikuwapanga kukhala munthu mmodzi watsopano, ngakhale kulengedwanso mu ungwiro. Ndine mmodzi mu umodzi ndi cholinga ndi Atate wanga, Yahweh, ndipo pampando wake wachifumu Iye ndi wamkulu kuposa ine.

Ndine mkulu wa ansembe wanu kwamuyaya monga mwa dongosolo la Melikizedeki, ndipo ndinapereka magazi anga okhululukira machimo monga nsembe pamene ndinadzipereka ndekha monga Paskha wanu wangwiro ndi wathunthu. Pakuti Ahebri amapereka umboni wawo pamene ndinatumiza Mtumwi Paulo kuti alankhule nawo m'malo mwanga (iye anali wa fuko la Benjamini): Nanga kuli bwanji magazi a Khristu, amene kudzera mwa Mzimu wamuyaya anadzipereka yekha wopanda chilema kwa Mulungu, adzayeretsa chikumbumtima chanu ku ntchito zakufa kuti mutumikire Mulungu wamoyo? Ndipo nsembe yanga ya ine ndekha ndi yanthawi imodzi ndi kwamuyaya, ndipo palibe Mesiya wina.

Ngakhale sunagoge wa Satana adzalandira mesiya wawo wonyenga ndi mneneri wonyenga amene alinso womanga kachisi wa chilombo (pakuti iye ndi chilombo chochokera pansi). Pakuti inu ndinu kachisi wa Mulungu wamoyo, ndipo palibenso chifukwa cha nsembe ya nyama, pakuti ndine nsembe yomaliza, ngakhale Paskha wangwiro, wopanda uchimo, ndipo ntchito zanga ndi zathunthu ndi zangwiro. Choncho, palibenso chifukwa cha chinthu choterocho, koma kuti mubwere kwa ine ndi machimo anu mu kulapa, ndipo ndidzakuyeretsani ku ntchito zanu zakufa za thupi. Pakuti mu Aroma kwalembedwa: Pakuti lamulo la Mzimu wa moyo mwa Khristu Yesu landimasula ine ku lamulo la uchimo ndi imfa. Pakuti momwemo ndakumasulani mwa ine.

Mvetsetsani, mafuko anga ndi anthu anga, kuti nthawi za a mitundu ina zikuthera. Nthawi za a mitundu ina zinabwera chifukwa cha kuuma kwa mitima ya makolo anu. Pakuti Paulo ananena chiyani mu Machitidwe pa nkhaniyi: Kunali koyenera kuti mawu a Mulungu alankhulidwe kwa inu poyamba: koma poona kuti mukuwakana, ndi kudziona ngati osayenera moyo wosatha, taonani, tikutembenukira kwa a mitundu ina.

Ndikukulangizani, anthu anga, musapange zolakwa zomwezo ndipo musakhale m'machimo a makolo anu, koma lapani machimo anu ndipo lapani machimo a makolo anu, ndipo bwerani kwa ine ndipo ndidzakulandirani, pakuti ndinu ana a lonjezo. Ndipo yod iliyonse kapena munga wa yod udzakwaniritsidwa; pakuti chimene chalonjezedwa ndi Yahweh n'chosatheka kusintha, pakuti n'chosatheka kuti Yahweh aname. Choncho, ndine kukwaniritsidwa kwa malonjezo ambiri, pakuti ndine Mesiya wanu weniweni, ngakhale chifaniziro cha Mulungu wamuyaya. Pakuti ndine mwana wake wobadwa yekha, ngakhale Mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu amene Yohane Mbatizi anamuzindikiritsa nthawi zonse. Pakuti anati: Taonani Mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu amene achotsa uchimo wa Dziko lapansi!

Ndipo choncho ndinatembenukira kwa mitundu chifukwa dziko langa linandikana, ngakhale nditamwalira chifukwa cha machimo awo ndi kuukitsidwa. Pakuti ndinatumiza atumwi anga ndi aneneri kwa iwo, ndipo ambiri anapulumutsidwa. Pakuti pachiyambi cha nthawi ya chisomo, ndinawatumiza ku Kachisi ndi m'masunagoge, ndipo ambiri anatembenukira kwa ine ndi kundilandira monga Mbuye wawo. Ndipo choncho ndikukuitanani kuti mukhale athunthu mu ulamuliro wanga, pakuti ndakuwombolani kwa Atate wanga kuti inunso mukhale ana Ake kudzera mwa mzimu wanga womwe ndidzakusindikizani nawo. Ndipo choncho nthawi za a mitundu ina zatha, pakuti tsopano iwonso andikana m'malo mwa Yesu wina amene amamupembedza ngati mulungu, potero kudzipanga okha opembedza mafano; pakuti Satana anawayesa motero. Pakuti pali Mulungu mmodzi yekha, Yahweh wamuyaya, ndipo pali Mesiya mmodzi yekha weniweni, ngakhale ine, Yeshua HaMashiach.

Pakuti anataya zinsinsi zanga, ananyalanyaza masomphenya anga ndi zolinga za malemba anga oyera. Anaiwala dongosolo la Atate la chiwombolo, ndipo amakana modzikuza mawu anga omwe ndinawalemba kwa Aroma. Pakuti Paulo anati: Ndikunena tsono, Kodi Mulungu wataya anthu ake? Adalitsike Mulungu. Pakuti inenso ndine Mwisiraeli, wa mbewu ya Abrahamu, wa fuko la Benjamini. Mulungu sanataye anthu ake, amene anawadziwiratu. Ndipo kachiwiri: Nanga bwanji? Isiraeli sanapeze chimene akuchifunafuna; koma osankhidwa anachipeza, ndipo ena onse anachititsidwa khungu. Ndipo chisankho chinali chotsimikizika kwa iwo amene ndawasankha pakati pa mafuko; pakuti atumwi anga onse anali a dzikolo ndi a lonjezo, ndipo choncho ndinawatumiza kwa mitundu, omwe atsogoleri anu achipembedzo amanama mwamwano, kuwatcha onyenga. Atsogoleri onyenga omwewo ndi abusa opanda pake amakananso kuukitsidwa kwanga, pakuti ndaukitsidwa kwa akufa, ndipo sindifanso kwamuyaya.

Pakuti Aroma ananena chiyani chinanso pa nkhaniyi: Davide anati, Gome lawo likhale msampha, ndi khoka, ndi chokhumudwitsa, ndi kubwezera kwa iwo: Maso awo achitidwe mdima kuti asawone, ndipo muweramitse msana wawo nthawi zonse. Ndikunena tsono, Kodi anakhumudwa kuti agwe? Adalitsike Mulungu: koma kudzera m'kugwa kwawo chipulumutso chafika kwa a mitundu ina, kuti awapangitse nsanje.

Pakuti sindikufuna, abale, kuti mukhale osadziwa chinsinsi ichi, kuti mungakhale anzeru m'malingaliro anu; kuti khungu mwa mbali lachitikira Isiraeli, kufikira kudzaza kwa a mitundu ina kutalowa. Ndipo choncho ndikulengeza kuti kudzaza kwa a mitundu ina kwafika, ndipo zinsinsi za Mulungu zidzabwezedwa kwa Isiraeli chifukwa cha kumvera kwa chikhulupiriro. Pakuti ndidzapulumutsabe Yakobo, ndipo otsalira okhulupirika adzatembenukira kwa ine mokhulupirika, m'chikhulupiriro chachikulu!

Ndipo choncho ndabwera kudzaputa nsanje m'mitima yanu kwa ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndi Yahweh Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya amene anandituma, amene simumupembedzanso molingana ndi chidziwitso, pakuti chophimba cha khungu chidzaphimba aliyense amene amatsatira lamulo la Mose. Pakuti ndinati chiyani kwa Akorinto pa nkhaniyi: Ndipo osati ngati Mose, amene anaika chophimba pankhope pake, kuti ana a Isiraeli asayang'ane motsimikiza mpaka kumapeto kwa chimene chinathetsedwa: Koma mitima yawo inachititsidwa khungu: pakuti mpaka lero chophimba chomwecho chikadali chosachotsedwa powerenga Chipangano Chakale; chophimba chomwe chachotsedwa mwa Khristu. Koma ngakhale mpaka lero, pamene Mose awerengedwa, chophimba chili pamtima pawo.

Komabe, pamene chidzatembenukira kwa Mbuye, chophimbacho chidzachotsedwa.

Pakuti ndakwaniritsa lamulo la Mose, pakuti inu kapena makolo anu simungathe kukwaniritsa mwangwiro chifukwa cha uchimo m'magazi anu. Pakuti ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndakwaniritsa lamulo mokwanira, ndipo lamulo limasungidwa mwangwiro kokha ndi chikhulupiriro mwa ine, Yesu Khristu. Mumaonedwa ngati olungama chifukwa cha kudzizindikiritsa kwanu kwathunthu ndi ine, ndipo olungama adzakhala ndi moyo ndi chikhulupiriro. Ndipo choncho ndidzachotsa chophimba m'mitima yanu kuti mundiwone, Yeshua, Mesiya wanu weniweni, pakuti sindinakusiyeni! Ndipo ndikubwera kwa inu mu chipangano chatsopano, osati ukalamba wa chilembo. Pakuti ndinatsimikizira chipangano changa chatsopano m'magazi anga, molingana ndi chifuniro ndi malonjezo a Yahweh, chifukwa cha chiwombolo chanu chathunthu. Ndipo ndidzakutsogolerani ku cholowa chanu, pakuti malonjezo a Mulungu ndi inde ndi ameni. Ngakhale yod kapena munga wa yod sudzalephera, pakuti zonse zatsala pang'ono kukwaniritsidwa; ndipo m'kupita kwa nthawi, ndithudi, ndidzakwaniritsa zonse, pakuti ndatumizidwa ndi Yahweh kuti ndikwaniritse.

Ndipo choncho a mitundu ina atembenuka kuchoka kwa ine ngati galu wobwerera ku masanzi ake, ndipo nthawi yakwana yowasiya. Pakuti mu nthawi yanga ya chisomo, palibe munthu mmodzi amene Atate wanga wamusankha kuti apulumutsidwe amene waphonyedwa, ndipo palibe amene angawalandwe m'dzanja langa. Pakuti iwo amene anandivomereza ine, Yesu, monga Mbuye wawo, ndi chikhulupiriro m'mitima yawo kuti Mulungu anandiukitsa kwa akufa, anasindikizidwa ndi mzimu woyera wa lonjezo, ndipo zolinga za Atate wanga zakwaniritsidwa mwa ine kwa iwo. Ndipo adzakhala nane nthawi zonse.

Pakuti mtengo wa azitona umavumbulutsa choonadi. Ndipo ndinati chiyani za mtengo wa azitona m'kalata ya Paulo kwa Aroma: Ndipo ngati nthambi zina zinathyoledwa, ndipo iwe, pokhala mtengo wa azitona wakuthengo, unalumikizidwa pakati pawo, ndipo pamodzi nawo umagawana muzu ndi mafuta a mtengo wa azitona; Usadzitamandire motsutsana ndi nthambi. Koma ngati udzitamandira, sunyamula muzu, koma muzu umakunyamula iwe.

Ndipo choncho apanga zolakwa ndipo atenga machimo a makolo anu ndipo atembenukira kutali ndi ine kupita ku mafano awo, ndipo sadzamvanso liwu langa. Ngakhale ngati aitanira pa dzina la Mbuye, adzapulumutsidwa amene sakonda miyoyo yawo mpaka mapeto.

Pakuti umboni wa Aroma m'malemba umanena chiyani chinanso monga chenjezo kwa mitundu: Udzanena tsono, Nthambi zinathyoledwa, kuti ine ndilumikizidwe. Chabwino; chifukwa cha kusakhulupirira zinathyoledwa, ndipo iwe uima ndi chikhulupiriro. Usakhale wodzikuza, koma uope: Pakuti ngati Mulungu sanalekerere nthambi zachilengedwe, samala kuti asakulekererenso iwe. Taona tsono ubwino ndi kuuma kwa Mulungu: pa iwo amene anagwa, kuuma; koma kwa iwe, ubwino, ngati upitiriza mu ubwino wake: apo ayi iwenso udzadulidwa. Ndipo iwonso, ngati sakhala m'kusakhulupirira, adzalumikizidwa: pakuti Mulungu akhoza kuwalumikizanso. Pakuti ngati unadulidwa mu mtengo wa azitona womwe ndi wakuthengo mwachilengedwe, ndipo unalumikizidwa mosiyana ndi chilengedwe mu mtengo wa azitona wabwino: nanga bwanji izi, zomwe ndi nthambi zachilengedwe, zidzalumikizidwa mu mtengo wawo wa azitona?

Pakuti sindikufuna, abale, kuti mukhale osadziwa chinsinsi ichi, kuti mungakhale anzeru m'malingaliro anu; kuti khungu mwa mbali lachitikira Isiraeli, kufikira kudzaza kwa a mitundu ina kutalowa. Ndipo choncho Isiraeli yense adzapulumutsidwa: monga kwalembedwa, Ku Ziyoni kudzachokera Mpulumutsi, ndipo adzachotsa kusapembedza kwa Yakobo.

Ndipo choncho, abale anga, nthawi za a mitundu ina zikuthera, ndipo kusintha kwakukulu kukuchitika, kumene chisomo changa chidzatembenuka nthawi yomweyo kukhala chiweruzo. Ndipo ngakhale tsopano zidutswa zikuloledwa m'malo mwake ndi dzanja la Satana, pamene adzaima, ngakhale adzaphwanyidwa kotheratu, ndipo ndidzapasula ufumu wake kotheratu; pakuti iye si mfumu.

Ndipo tsopano yafika nthawi yolumikiza nthambi zachilengedwe kubwerera mu mtengo wa azitona, pakuti zolinga za Yahweh zakwaniritsidwa mu chisomo ndi chifundo chosayenera chomwe chinasonyezedwa kwa a mitundu ina (pakuti chisomo ndi chifundo cha Mulungu ndi zosayenera kwa munthu aliyense), pakuti ngakhale chisomo ndi chifundocho chinagwiritsidwa ntchito kupeza nkhosa zotayika za nyumba ya Isiraeli. Pakuti simuyenera kukhala osadziwa mbiri yanu, malinga ngati muika pansi ma Talmud anu a ku Babulo amwano ndikuphunzira za ine; pakuti sindinatumize bukhulo, ndipo muyenera kulapa ku ziphunzitsozo ndikukana mabodza a Satana. Bwererani ku mawu anga oyera a Mulungu ndipo chokani m'machimo a makolo anu, pakuti ndidzakulumbolani ngati mungamve liwu langa.

Pakuti sindinatumizidwe koma kwa nkhosa zotayika za nyumba ya Isiraeli. Ndipo choncho mafuko khumi anamwazikana pakati pa mitundu kuchokera ku ukapolo wawo ndi Asiriya nthawi yonseyi yapitayo. Ayendayenda ndi kusochera padziko lapansi monga alendo ndi apaulendo. Kuchokera ku Asiriya anapita kumayiko a kumpoto, ku Scythia, ndi ku Ulaya, ndipo pamapeto pake ku America, kumene ndinawapangira malo okhala mwamtendere kwa kanthawi. Ndipo ndinaitana onse amene angabwere kuchokera ku mitundu ku chipulumutso changa. Ngakhale Aisiraeli ambiri anapulumutsidwa ndipo awomboledwa komanso ambiri ochokera ku mitundu, omwe akanakhala opanda chiyembekezo. Ndipo khamu lalikulu layankha, ndipo choncho nthawi za a mitundu ina zatha, pakuti palibenso nthawi. Koma mvetsetsani kuti onse amene aitanira pa dzina la Mbuye adzapulumutsidwa ngati ali okhulupirika mpaka mapeto, pakuti ndidzawalandira mosasamala kanthu za mtundu womwe akuchokera.

Ndipo zili choncho, kuti vuto la Yakobo likubwera pa dziko ndi mitundu, pakuti ndi nthawi yoti chiweruzo chachikulu chigwe padziko lapansi. Ndipo choncho zilombo mwa mphamvu ya chinjoka zidzauka; koma musatenge chizindikiro chake ndipo musagwe m'zinyengo zake. Pakuti wadziyanja kale kwa olamulira a mtundu wanu, ngakhale sunagoge wa Satana ndi njira zawo za Edomu. Ndipo amalankhula kale zamwano zazikulu, pakuti akukonzekera cholinga chake chenicheni.

Pakuti chonyansa cha chiwonongeko chikubwera. Pakuti ndinapangitsa Luka wanga wokhulupirika kulemba chiyani nthawi zonse: Ndipo pamene mudzaona Yerusalemu atazingidwa ndi magulu ankhondo, pamenepo zindikirani kuti chiwonongeko chake chayandikira. Pamenepo amene ali m'Yudeya athawire kumapiri; ndipo amene ali pakati pake atuluke; ndipo amene ali m'madera asalowemo. Pakuti awa ndi masiku a kubwezera, kuti zonse zolembedwa zikwaniritsidwe.

Ndipo zidzakhala choncho pa nthawi yoyenera, ndipo ndidzateteza anga, pakuti ndikukutumizani monga aneneri anga, abusa, ochiritsa, ndi atumiki. Pakuti ndikukuitanani ku utumiki, ngakhale kukwaniritsa kuitana kwanu kwenikweni koperekedwa ndi lonjezo kwa makolo ndi Yahweh Yemwe, ndipo ndi dzanja langa zidzakwaniritsidwa. Pakuti Yahweh waika mpando wanga pampando wake wachifumu, ndipo ndagawana mpando wanga wachifumu ndi Mkwatibwi wanga, amene ali nane nthawi zonse. Ndipo choncho palibe padziko lapansi wamkulu kuposa ine; palibe aneneri anu onyenga omwe angafanane, pakuti onse ndi ofooka ndi opemphapempha ndipo atumizidwa kudzanyenga ndi kusocheretsa, pakuti ndi abusa opanda pake.

Dziwani, anthu anga, kuti mdani wakuikireni misampha yambiri, choncho tsatirani chitsogozo changa ndipo mudzaipewa yonse. Pakuti posachedwapa zosangalatsa zonse zidzachotsedwa padziko lapansi. Ambiri sadzapeza ngakhale kachidutswa, ndipo sadzakhala ngakhale ndi gome loti adyerepo koma adzakhala othawa padziko lapansi chifukwa cha chikhulupiriro chawo mwa ine.

Musadzilole kuyesedwa ndi mana awo onyenga omwe adzafuna chizindikiro cha chilombo kuti mulowe m'gulu lawo lopotoka. Pakuti chizindikirocho sichidzakhululukidwa, ndipo onse amene agwa mu msampha uwu sadzamasulidwa; pakuti ndi chokhazikika kwa onse amene amapanga chisankho chomalizacho, pakuti adzakhala atasindikiza tsogolo lawo ndipo tsogolo limenelo ndi lomaliza. Choncho musayesedwe ndi nyambo, apo ayi mudzathera mu ukonde ndikukodwa kwamuyaya kudzera m'nsagwada zanu.

Ndikukutumizani kuti musonkhanitse gulu langa, ndipo ndidzakutsogolerani kwa iwo ndikuwatsogolera kwa inu, ndipo mudzawaweta m'malo mwanga, pakuti ndine mbusa wamkulu. Simudzakana mtundu wina kapena dziko kapena mtundu wina uliwonse, pakuti lonjezo la Yahweh likuima, ndipo onse amene aitanira pa dzina la Mbuye adzapulumutsidwa. Musakhale ouma khosi ngati makolo anu, ndipo mukhale ofatsa ku liwu langa. Pakuti makolo anu anandikana, choncho vomerani machimo a makolo anu ndi anu mu kulapa kwa ine, ndipo ndidzakukhululukirani ndikukutsanulirani chikondi changa ndi chisomo, pakuti ndidzapangitsa mitsinje ya madzi amoyo kuyenda kuchokera m'mimba mwanu. Ndakupatsani mphamvu zochita zozizwitsa zazikulu. Ndipo ndikukutumizani kwa abale anu kuti muwatembenuzire kwa ine, pakuti otsalira adzapulumutsidwabe; ndipo ndidzapulumutsa Yakobo m'dzanja la adani awo, pakuti ndalengeza ndipo zidzakwaniritsidwa.

Ine, ngakhale Yeshua HaMashiach amene Yahweh wamutuma, ndine Mesiya weniweni wa Isiraeli ndi dziko lapansi. Ngakhale ine ndidzatumiza aneneri anga awiri, ngakhale omwe Yahweh anawatenga padziko lapansi m'nthawi zawo: pakuti chifukwa cha cholinga choterocho anatengedwa, ndipo choncho adzatumizidwa. Ndili ndi zinthu zambiri zoti ndikuuzeni ndipo zinthuzo zidzanenedwa, ndipo ndidzakukonzekeretsani mautumiki anu, ndipo mudzandichitira ntchito zazikulu ndi zamphamvu, ngakhale ntchito zomwe simunaganizirepo kuti n'zotheka. Pakuti nthawi zoyipitsitsa nthawi zonse zimatulutsa amuna akulu a Yahweh, ngakhale amuna omwe sadzathyoka, pakuti mudzapuma mu mphamvu yanga, ndipo ine sindithyoka. Pakuti momwemo Yahweh wandipangira, ndipo momwemo ndidzakupangirani. Choncho imvani liwu langa, pakuti ndikuitana ndipo ndidzatumiza angelo anga kuti asindikize a 144,000 ndi chidindo cha Yahweh.

Ndidzakusungani motetezeka ku chilombo ndi chonyansa chake; ndidzakubisani, ndipo mudzadyetsedwa kuchokera kumwamba ngakhale mutafunikira, choncho musaope. Musaope chifukwa cha miyoyo yanu, pakuti mphoto yaikulu ndi yanu chifukwa cha utumiki ndi kukhulupirika, ndipo ndidzakupatsani mphoto chifukwa cha kuima kwanu.

Ndine Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda, ndipo mudzamva kubangula kwanga, pakuti ndikukuitanani.

Ndine amene ndidzasindikiza 12,000 kuchokera ku fuko lililonse kupatula Dani ndi Efuraimu.

Ndine amene ndidzaphwanya mwana wa mkango amene adzatumphuka kuchokera kwa Dani.

Ndine amene ndidzagonjetsa chilombo ndi mneneri wonyenga, ndipo ndidzawaponya amoyo m'nyanja yaikulu yamoto.

Ndine amene ndidzatsogolera anthu anga kumabusa abwino kudzera mwa abusa anga, pakuti ndine mbusa wamkulu.

Ndine amene ndidzakwaniritsa malonjezo opangidwa kwa makolo, pakuti ndatumizidwa kutero.

Ndine amene ndidzaphwanya mutu wa Satana, pakuti akalonga a dziko lino adzalipira.

Ndine amene ndidzalamulira kuchokera kumpando wachifumu wa Davide, pakuti ufumu wanga ulibe mapeto.

Ndine amene ndikubwera m'mitambo, ndipo ndidzabwera ku mtundu wanga ndipo ndidzatenga ulamuliro wanga, ndipo sindidzaletsedwa.

Ine, ngakhale ine, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndatumiza mawu awa kwa iwo amene ndikuwaitana. Choncho imvani mawu anga mu kumvera, ndipo dziwani kuti ndidzamasula dziko ndi dziko lonse lapansi ndipo ndidzalamulira m'chilungamo. Choncho imvani kuitana kwanga, pakuti ndikubwera kwa inu ndipo ndidzakuphunzitsani njira zanga ndipo ndidzakutsogolerani m'choonadi chonse. Choncho musakane zolemba zanga, pakuti mawu a Mulungu adzabwezedwa kwa Isiraeli, ndipo dziko lapansi lidzadziwa kuti kuli Mulungu mu Isiraeli, ngakhale Yahweh, Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya, pakuti palibe wina, ndipo palibe wina wofanana Naye. Shalom! Shalom! Choncho khalani mumtendere ndipo khalani bwino, pakuti ndidzakuchiritsani nonse kotheratu amene amaika chikhulupiriro chawo mwa Yeshua HaMashiach.
