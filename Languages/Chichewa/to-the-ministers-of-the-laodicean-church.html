Kwa Atumiki a Mpingo wa Laodikaya: Imvani Mawu anga!

Mkwatibwi wanga ndi Mpingo wanga, ndimveni m'chikondi changa pa inu, ana anga, ndipo tcherani makutu anu ku kulankhula kwanga. Imvani Mawu anga, inu atumiki anga a ku Laodikaya, ngakhale inu amene ndakupatsani mphatso. Pakuti ndapereka mphatso kwa anthu, ndipo ndapereka ena kukhala atumwi, ndi ena aneneri, ndi ena alaliki, ndi ena abusa, ndi aphunzitsi. Ndakupatsani mphatso izi kuti akonze oyera mtima, pa ntchito ya utumiki, kuti amange thupi la Khristu. Pakuti ndinawauza chiyani atumwi anga ndi ophunzira anga m'zolemba za uthenga wabwino wa Yohane: Ndipo taonani, ndidzatumiza lonjezo la atate wanga pa inu. Koma khalani inu mu mzinda wa Yerusalemu, kufikira mutavekedwa mphamvu yochokera kumwamba.

Ndipo m'Machitidwe a Atumwi anga mwalembedwa kukwaniritsidwa kwa lonjezo ili: Ndipo pamene tsiku la Pentekoste linafika kwenikweni, onse anali ndi mtima umodzi pamalo amodzi. Ndipo modzidzimutsa kunamveka phokoso lochokera kumwamba ngati la mphepo yamkuntho, ndipo linadzaza nyumba yonse imene anali kukhalamo. Ndipo kunaonekera kwa iwo malilime ogawanika, ngati a moto, ndipo anakhala pa aliyense wa iwo. Ndipo onse anadzazidwa ndi mzimu woyera, ndipo anayamba kulankhula ndi malilime ena, monga mzimu unawapatsa kulankhula. Ndipo kukhazikitsidwa kwa Mpingo wanga kwalembedwa choncho m'malemba kuti mudziwe kukwaniritsidwa kwa lonjezo lalikulu ili la uthenga wabwino, kuti ndakudzazani ndi mphamvu yochokera kumwamba, ngakhale m'kubadwa kwatsopano, ngakhale mwa ine, Khristu Yesu Ambuye wanu. Ndipo Ine ndine mutu wa thupi, Mpingo: Ine ndine chiyambi, woyamba kubadwa mwa akufa, kuti m'zinthu zonse ndikhale woyamba: Pakuti kunamukomera Atate kuti MWA INA mukhale chidzalo chonse.

Atumiki anga a ku Laodikaya, chidzalo changa sichikhala m'mipingo yanu yopangidwa m'zifanizo zanu, pakuti ndaima pakhomo ndikugogoda! Atumiki anga a ku Laodikaya, sindinasunge mwa dyera chidzalo chonse cha Atate chomwe chili mwa ine, koma ndinatsanulira mzimu wanga ndikukupangani kukhala amphamvu, ndipo ndakusindikizani ndi mzimu woyera wa lonjezo. Palibe chomwe chingathe kuswa chidindo chimenecho, ngakhale mutha kuchoka kupita ku mpatuko ngati musankha kutero. Koma chidindo cha Atate, ngakhale Yahweh Mulungu wamuyaya, sichingasweke; pakuti n'zosatheka kuswa chidindo cha Mulungu, ndipo muli otetezeka mwa ine ngati muli okhulupirika mwa ine, pakuti malonjezo anga ndi inde ndi ameni!

Atumiki anga a ku Laodikaya, mphatso zanga kwa inu zikunyalanyazidwa ndi njira zanu zofunda, ndipo mwazimitsa mzimu womwe ndakupatsani, ndipo mukukana kutsegula mphatso zomwe ndakupatsani, ndipo mukusiya mphatso zanga zosatsegulidwa mu ulesi ndi kunyalanyaza. Iyi si nkhani yaing'ono yoti muione mopepuka, pakuti ndi tchimo lalikulu pa mlandu wanu, ndipo ndinu osamvera mawu anga. Simulemekeza mawu anga, ndipo simulemekeza kapena kuphunzitsa njira zanga. Pakuti ndinamulembera chiyani Paulo kwa mtsogoleri wachinyamata wa Mpingo wanga womwe unali utangobzalidwa kumene, ngakhale Timoteo: Usapeputse mphatso yomwe ili mwa iwe, yomwe inapatsidwa kwa iwe kudzera mu Ulosi, ndi kuikidwa manja kwa mkulu. Zinthu izi uzichite, ndipo udzipereke wekha kwa izo, kuti kuwonekere momwe ukupindulira m'zinthu zonse.

O, mukanawerenga mawu anga ndi kumvetsetsa! O, mukanakumbukira mawu anga m'chikhulupiriro, koma simukumvetsa ndipo mulibe chikhulupiriro! O, mukanamvera mawu anga, pakuti kumvera ine, Ambuye Yesu Khristu, ndiko kumvera kasupe wa moyo, ndipo m'mimba mwanu mudzayenda mitsinje ya madzi amoyo, pakuti ndakupatsani mzimu wanga. Koma m'malo mowerenga mawu anga, mumayang'ana pazowonera zanu mukusamvera kopanda nzeru; kuchoka pa chowonera china kupita ku china mumapita, kusangalatsidwa ndi tchimo! Ndipo ndinu opusa kwambiri kuganiza kuti kusamvera kopanda nzeru ndi chikhulupiriro chogwira ntchito mwa ine, ndipo mwanyengedwa. Mwanyalanyaza mphatso zomwe ndakupatsani, ngakhale munthu watsopano, ndipo m'malo mwake mwathera nthawi mukugwiritsa ntchito chikhalidwe chanu cha munthu wakale pachabe pamene mukufunafuna kukhutiritsa thupi lanu.

M'malo modzichita masewera olimbitsa thupi m'zinthu zauzimu, inu mwachabechabe mumachita masewera olimbitsa matupi anu omwe ali owonongeka ndi akuwonongeka, pakuti masewera olimbitsa thupi amapindula kwa nyengo yochepa chabe, ndipo matupi anu akuwonongeka. Pakuti mawu anga akumveka kudutsa nthawi kuyambira kalata ya Paulo yopita ku Efeso: Atathetsa m'thupi lake chidani, ngakhale Chilamulo cha Malamulo okhala m'Malangizo, kuti apange mwa iye yekha, mwa awiriwo, munthu mmodzi watsopano, potero kupanga mtendere. Ndimveni, ana anga, pakuti ndimakukondani ndipo ndimasamalira miyoyo yanu, ngakhale inu a ku Laodikaya ofunda. Pakuti mumakonda kuyenda mwa munthu wanu wakale ndi njira zake, ngakhale thupi lanu lowonongeka, pakuti mumasonyeza thupi lanu zokonda zonse. M'malo mwake, muyenera kuyika maganizo anu ndi mitima yanu pa ine ndi mawu anga onse: Pakuti munthu sadzakhala ndi moyo ndi mkate wokha, koma ndi mawu onse amene atuluka mkamwa mwa Mulungu. Pakuti mwa kumvera koonekera poyera kwa mawu anga (ngakhale Mawu a Mulungu, pakuti Ine ndine Mawu wopangidwa thupi ndipo ndimakhala pakati panu ndi mwa inu), mumayenda mu mzimu osati m'thupi.

Pakuti ndauza Mpingo wa ku Korinto zotsatirazi: Koma ife tonse, ndi nkhope yosaphimbidwa poyang'ana ngati m'galasi ulemerero wa Ambuye, tikusinthidwa kukhala chifanizo chomwecho, kuchokera ku ulemerero kupita ku ulemerero, ngakhale monga mwa mzimu wa Ambuye. Ana anga, makalata anga opita ku Mpingo wanga (ngakhale makalata achikondi omwe ndinatumiza kwa Mkwatibwi wanga) ndi galasi la inu enieni, pakuti inu enieni mwabisika m'chinsinsi chachikulu cha Mulungu. Mwa kumvera mawu anga ndi malangizo anga mudzayenda mu mzimu, ndipo simudzapereka chikhutiro ku thupi lanu lowonongeka. Lemba lonse liyenera kulemekezedwa ndi kuphunziridwa, koma inu ndinu chinsinsi chomwe chinabisika mwa Mulungu ndipo tsopano chavumbulutsidwa, ndipo muyenera kuphunzira zomwe ndinavumbulutsa kwa Paulo kuti mumvetsetse chilengedwe chatsopano chomwe ndikukupangani mwa ine, Khristu Yesu.

Atumiki anga a ku Laodikaya, pali cholakwa chachikulu pakati panu. Mwataya ulemu wonse wa mawu anga, ndipo mumanyalanyaza mphatso zomwe ndakupatsani mpaka kufika poti simuchiritsanso koma mumapereka mpingo wanga ku mankhwala ndi mpeni wa dokotala; pakuti mukanakhala omvera kwa ine, ndikanapereka zozizwitsa kuti zichitidwe ndi manja anu. Koma mumalemekeza mawu a abusa ampatuko kuposa mawu a Yeshua HaMashiach, ndipo mpingo wanga wa ku Laodikaya umalipira mtengo wa kusamvera kwanu; pakuti simuwawonanso, koma m'malo mwake mumawasiya akuvutika mu tchimo lopanda mphamvu. Abusa ampatuko amadziwika ndi mawu omwe ndinapatsa Yohane m'kalata yake yoyamba: Tiana, ndi nthawi yotsiriza: ndipo monga mwamva kuti Wokana Khristu adzabwera, ngakhale tsopano alipo Okana Khristu ambiri, mwa ichi tikudziwa kuti ndi nthawi yotsiriza. Iwo anatuluka mwa ife, koma sanali a ife: pakuti akadakhala a ife, mosakayikira akadapitiriza kukhala nafe: koma anatuluka kuti awonekere poyera, kuti sanali onse a ife.

Kodi simukumvetsa kuti omwe amalamulidwa ndi mizimu yokana Khristu amapereka Yesu wina m'malo mwa mbusa weniweni, ngakhale ine, Khristu Yesu? Pakuti awa ndi adani a mtanda wa Khristu, ndipo amatsutsana nane, ndipo amadziyika okha m'malo mwanga, kufunafuna kulanda ulamuliro wanga, zomwe ndi zosatheka. Awa alowa m'mipingo yanu, m'maseminale anu, m'mabungwe anu osiyanasiyana, ndipo amalamulira mipingo yanu mwa chilolezo chanu chogwiritsa ntchito 501(c)(3), pakuti onse omwe ali pansi pa mgwirizano wotere amamangidwa pakamwa ndi maboma a dziko lapansi. Ma 501(c)(3) anu amasonyeza umbombo wanu wa phindu lonyansa, pakuti mwadziika nokha mu uhule pansi pa njira za Balamu, ndipo mumapereka mawu onama chifukwa cha ndalama kuti mudzisungire nokha ndalama zonse zomwe ana anga ang'onoang'ono anakupatsani mu chigololo chanu chosakhoma msonkho! Mwakhala bwenzi la dziko lapansi, ndipo dziko lapansi likubwera kudzatenga chilichonse cholumikizidwa ndi dzina langa pamene woletsa achotsedwa. Mumasowa kuzindikira kwauzimu ndipo ndinu akhungu! Mumasowa chisamaliro choteteza gulu la nkhosa kwa mimbulu yolusa iyi, pakuti mumayamikira maudindo olumikizidwa ndi mayina anu, maudindo omwe sindinakupatseni; komabe mumawasirira. Pakuti ndakuuzani kuti musirire ndi mtima wonse mphatso zabwino kwambiri: Ndipo komabe ndikukuwonetsani njira yopambana kwambiri: njira yopambana kwambiri yomwe mumakana m'njira zanu zaulesi, zofunda.

Simulalikiranso za tchimo ndi kulapa kuchoka ku tchimo, koma mumalalika nthano zomveka zosangalatsa zomwe sizochokera kwa ine, ndipo nthano zanu zimapangidwa ndi mawu onama ndipo zimatsogolera ku njira zonyenga; pakuti simuli kanthu koma wofotokoza nthano yemwe amatekenya makutu a aulesi. Ndikulankhula ndi inu, atumwi anga: pakuti maso anu ndi amdima, ndipo simuwonanso kuwala kwanga, ndipo mumalengeza bodza! Ndikulankhula ndi inu, aneneri anga: pakuti mumalankhulira Yesu wina, ndipo mumanyalanyaza mawu anga, ndipo simundidziwa, ndipo mumanenera za tsogolo lonyenga! Ndikulankhula ndi inu, alaliki anga: Pakuti mumalalika mitundu ya anthu ku ziphunzitso zonyenga za ziwanda, ndipo mumawamwetsa madzi akuda, pakuti mumawadzaza ndi mizimu yonyansa! Ndikulankhula ndi inu, abusa anga: Pakuti mumasamalira zilakolako zanu, ndipo mwapanga mimba yanu kukhala mulungu wanu ndi zinyalala zanu za nzeru zodzithandiza nokha; wopusa iwe, sungathe kuchiritsa nkhosa zanga ndi lilime lonama! Ndikulankhula ndi inu, aphunzitsi anga: Pakuti simuphunzitsanso mawu oyera a uthenga wanga wabwino, koma mumaphunzitsa mawu a kugonjera kwa zipembedzo zosiyanasiyana, ndipo pali cholakwa chachikulu pakati panu nonse, atumiki anga a ku Laodikaya. Nonse muli ndi matenda ofanana: Simulemekezanso malangizo a m'Mawu anga, ndipo simulemekezanso mawu aliyense payekha m'malemba anga.

Mumaganiza modzikuza kuti ndinu omasulira chifukwa mutha kusankha matanthauzo kuchokera m'mabuku a matanthauzo a mawu a dziko lapansi ndi ma konikodansi a dziko lapansi kuti agwirizane ndi zolinga zanu: Mabuku omwe sindinalembe, pakuti ndimalemba malemba kudzera mwa aneneri anga, pakuti mzimu wa ulosi ndi umboni wa Yesu! Omasulira omwe amalankhula chilankhulo chimodzi chokha, ndipo mwakhala chinthu choseketsa! M'malo mwake, mumalemekeza mawu a abusa ampatuko, ngakhale onyenga opanda pake a namsongole. Iwo analowa m'mipingo yanu kuti athe kudziyesa inu (pakuti inu ndinu tirigu yemwe ndikadasonkhanitsa m'nkhokwe yanga ngati mutatembenukira kwa ine). Koma m'malo mwake iwo ndi namsongole, abale onyenga omwe anakhala atumiki onyenga omwe amakusocheretsani. Chomera chonyenga ndi chapoizoni iwo ali!

Oipa awa satulukanso pakati pa Mpingo wanga kuti awonekere poyera, koma mumawaitanira mkati ndikuwakakamiza kuti adye nanu ndi kulemekeza mawu awo onyenga ndi malilime onama kuposa mawu omwe ndikadakupatsani, ngakhale mawu a Mulungu Wamoyo, ngakhale mawu a Yahweh! Mumachita malonda ndi mawu anga kuti mukhale ndi ulemerero wonse ndi matamando omwe ndi anga, Ambuye wanu Yesu Khristu. Zoonadi mwalandira mphoto yanu, ndipo ndidzakulondani mphoto yanu ndikuipereka kwa wina yemwe anali wokhulupirika m'malo mwanu. Pakuti simundilankhuliranso, ndipo ndiyenera kubwera ku Mpingo wanga monga mawu a wofuula m'chipululu, pakuti mwanditsekera chitseko pankhope panga, ngakhale ndikuyimabe ndi kugogoda! Manyazi aakulu ndi mnyozo zili pa inu pokhapokha mutalapa!

Ngati mutanyalanyaza mawu anga ndi kusamva mawu anga, ngati mupitiriza m'njira zanu zofunda, ndidzakusiyirani ku nsautso ya Yakobo; pakuti ndinanena kale kwa inu, a ku Laodikaya anga, ndidzakulavulani mkamwa mwanga pokhapokha mutalapa! Ndipo kachiwiri ndinanena za mneneri wamkazi wonyenga uja Yezebeli pamene ndinalembera Tiyatira, pakuti ndinamuuza chiyani Yohane Wovumbulutsa?

Komabe, ndili ndi zinthu zochepa zokutsutsa, chifukwa umalola mkazi uja Yezebeli, amene adzitcha yekha Mneneri wamkazi, kuphunzitsa ndi kusocheretsa atumiki anga kuchita dama, ndi kudya zinthu zoperekedwa nsembe kwa mafano. Ndipo ndinamupatsa mpata woti alape dama lake, ndipo sanalape. Taonani, ndidzam'ponya pabedi, ndi iwo amene achita chigololo naye, m'chisautso chachikulu, pokhapokha atalapa ntchito zawo.

Kodi simunawerenge mawu awa? Kodi ndinu odzikuza mokwanira kuganiza kuti kaya Yahweh kapena Yeshua HaMashiach amakondera anthu? Kodi mukuganiza kuti ndimalemekeza maudindo omwe mumadzipatsa nokha kuti muwapachike pamitu ya anthu anga, pamene mukudzipanga nokha ambuye pa cholowa cha Mulungu?

Mbusa uyu, Episkopi uyo; mukanapanda kunyalanyaza mphatso zanga, mphatso yanu ikanakupatsani malo pamaso pa anthu akulu ndipo simukanafunikira maudindo anu opanda pake, pakuti ndikanakulemekezani ndipo inu mukanandilemekeza, koma inu mukundinyozetsa! Pali cholakwa chachikulu pakati panu, ndipo mkwiyo wanga wayaka pa inu, chifukwa mwalephera ntchito zomwe ndinakupatsani ndipo m'malo mwake mwakhala ampatuko, ngakhale kutsogolera ang'ono anga m'dzenje momwe mwamanga mahema anu. Mukukonda madzi onyansa m'dzenje lanu kuposa madzi anga oyera a mzimu woyera omwe onse akumwa sadzamvanso ludzu!

Mukupereka madzi onyansa, auve kwa ang'ono anga m'zitsime zosweka, ndipo mkwiyo wanga uli pa inu! Ndidzakulondani mphoto yanu ndikuipereka kwa wina, pakuti monga ndanenera za inu: Indetu, mwalandira mphoto yanu. Muli ndi mphoto yanu ya mipando yanu yomwe mumakonda yodzipatsa ulemu, ndipo muli ndi mphoto yanu ya maudindo opanda ntchito, ndipo muli ndi mphoto yanu ya chuma chonyansa, ndipo mudzakhala ndi mphoto ya mkwiyo wanga pokhapokha mutalapa ndikubwerera kwa ine modzichepetsa. Pakuti mphoto ya mkwiyo wanga ndi ukali, ndipo pamene mudzandibweretsera mitengo yanu, udzu, ndi ziputu, zidzapsa kukhala phulusa pamaso pa onse, ndipo mudzapezeka kuti ndinu atumiki onyenga!

Pali cholakwa chachikulu pakati panu nonse, ngakhale inu, atumwi anga, aneneri, alaliki, abusa, ndi aphunzitsi! Simudzigwiranso mu ubale wopulumutsa ndi ine, Yesu Khristu, koma mwandisiya; pakuti ine ndine mutu wa thupi, ndipo muyenera kundigwira ine pamwamba pa zonse! SIMUNGATHE kunditumikira ngati simukudziwa ngakhale kumveka kwa mawu anga. Ndinu ogontha, ndipo muli ndi kuwonongeka kwa makutu kosatha pokhapokha mutalapa, pakuti ndidzachiritsa makutu anu ku mizimu ya tulo kuti mumve mawu anga, pakuti ndikuyankhula nanu! Pokhapokha mutandigwira monga mutu wanu ndikulemekeza Mawu anga, ndidzakusiyani akhungu ndipo m'dzenje pamodzi ndi omwe anakutsogolerani. Ndinu akhungu, ndipo muli ndi kuwonongeka kwa maso kosatha pokhapokha mutalapa, pakuti ndikadachiritsa maso anu ku mizimu ya tulo kuti muthe kuwerenga mawu anga ndikuwona nkhope yanga: koma tulo pang'ono, kupinda manja pang'ono.

Mwakhala choseketsa ndi chamanyazi, pakuti njira zanu zofunda zikunyozetsa dzina langa loyera. Osati kokha kuti simundigwira ine, Khristu Yesu, monga mutu wanu (pakuti mukanatero, mukanatha kuyankhula m'malo mwanga), koma mukupitirira mu mpatuko ponyozetsa mawu anga oyera, pakuti Ine ndine Mawu wosanduka thupi! Ndikukufunsani, kodi simunawerenge machenjezo anga? Pakuti ndikuchitira umboni kwa munthu aliyense amene amva mawu a ulosi wa bukhu ili, Ngati munthu aliyense adzawonjezera pa zinthu izi, Mulungu adzamuwonjezera miliri yomwe yalembedwa m'bukhu ili. Ndipo ngati munthu aliyense adzachotsa pa mawu a bukhu la ulosi uwu, Mulungu adzachotsa gawo lake m'Bukhu la Moyo, ndi mu mzinda woyera, ndi pa zinthu zomwe zalembedwa m'bukhu ili. Kodi ndinu opusa, ogontha, ndi akhungu kotero kuti simukumvetsa kuti ndinapereka chenjezo limenelo osati kokha kwa bukhu lalikulu la vumbulutso langa, Chivumbulutso! Koma chenjezo limenelo likugwira ntchito pa malemba onse oyera! Pakuti Miyambo yanga ikulira mokweza m'misewu kwa onse omwe ali ndi makutu akumva: Mawu onse a Mulungu ndi oyera ndi oyengedwa; pakuti iye ndiye chishango kwa onse omwe amamukhulupirira. Choncho usawonjezere kanthu pa mawu ake, kuti angakudzudzule ndipo ukapezeka wabodza.

Atumiki anga a ku Laodikaya, omwe mwapanga ubwenzi ndi dziko lapansi. Atumiki anga a ku Laodikaya, omwe mukudzipanga ambuye pa cholowa cha Mulungu, kudzionetsera kuti ndinu akulu. Atumiki anga a ku Laodikaya, omwe mwanyalanyaza mzimu wanga womwe ndinalenga mwa inu. Atumiki anga a ku Laodikaya, omwe mukudzipatsa maudindo opanda pake ndi mayina owonjezera pa mayina anu. Atumiki anga a ku Laodikaya: Imvani mawu anga ndipo ndiyankheni, pakuti lilime lililonse lidzavomereza!

Ndani anakulolezani kusintha mawu anga, ngakhale Mawu a Ambuye omwe ndapereka kudzera mwa amithenga anga? Njira zanu, inu atumiki aulesi a ku Laodikaya, si zachilendo, pakuti atangoyamba kufalitsa makalata anga ku Mipingo yanga yobadwa kumene yolembedwa m'Machitidwe ndi kwina, magulu a agnostiki otsogoleredwa ndi aneneri onyenga a agnostiki anasintha mawu anga ndikulembanso malemba, pakuti anatuluka pakati pa Mpingo wanga kuti onse adziwe kuti anali abusa ampatuko!

Mudzakolola mphoto za njira zawo, pakuti mwadziphatikiza nokha kwa iwo, ndipo mukukhala m'njira zawo, ndipo mukuwonetsa mizimu yonyansa ya Yezebeli, mneneri wamkazi wonyenga yemwe mumamutcha woyera! Ndikuwona ntchito zanu zoyipa, ndipo zalembedwa kumwamba mwatsatanetsatane motsutsana nanu! Muyenera kuyankhula mawu omwe ndikukupatsani, ndipo muyenera kuwayankhula moona mtima, ndi umphumphu, popanda kusintha ngakhale liwu limodzi la ulosi uliwonse! Sindikudziyankhulira ndekha, pakuti ndakuuzani: Chita khama kudzionetsera wovomerezeka kwa Mulungu, wantchito wopanda chifukwa chochitira manyazi, wolunjika bwino pofotokoza mawu a choonadi. Mawu ambiri asinthidwa m'malemba anga m'zaka zapitazi, ndipo ngati mudzaphunzira ndikulemekeza mawu anga omwe ndapereka, ndidzakutsogolerani m'choonadi chonse, pakuti chotero ndapereka mtonthozi.

Ndidzakuwonetsani, ngati mudzamvera mawu ambiri omwe ndakusiyirani. Tsatirani malemba omwe ndinapereka ku Antiokeya, pakuti ndi mawu a mboni zambiri, ndipo mboni zanga sizimanama ndipo sizitsutsana. Yang'anani ku Textus Receptus ndipo khalani kutali ndi Mabaibulo ampatuko: phunzirani kuti mumvetsetse! Koma inu, atumiki anga a ku Laodikaya, simulemekeza mawu anga, ndipo mumasintha mawu anga kuti agwirizane ndi zolinga zanu zonyansa, kuphunzitsa anthu anga kuti apitirize mu tchimo, ngakhale kuwatsogolera mozama m'njira zanu zauve! Pali cholakwa chachikulu pakati panu, ndipo simutsatira njira zanga, pakuti ndinu odzikuza ndi a mitima yowuma, ndipo ndidzakusiyani ku chiwonongeko cha thupi pokhapokha mutalapa ndikusintha njira zanu. Pakuti Ine, Yesu Khristu, ndatumiza mawu awa! Koma musawanyalanyaze, ndipo lapani m'ziguduli ndi phulusa. Lapani poyera kwa mipingo yanu ndikuvomereza machimo anu kwa omwe munawasocheretsa, pakuti ndidzakuchotserani choyikapo nyale chanu. Lapani ndikuyenda mowongoka, ndipo ndidzabweza nyale yanu ndi lawi loyatsidwa kumene, pakuti ndine amene ndidzayatsa moto wa mzimu wanga mu moyo wanu.

Bwererani ku chikondi chanu choyamba, pakuti mumandikonda, koma osati monga mwa chidziwitso. Musakane mawu anga, pakuti ndikubwera posachedwa kuposa momwe mukuganizira, pakuti ndikubwera ngati mbala usiku. Ngakhale m'kulakwa kwanu, mukuganiza kuti mbala usiku ndi munthu yemwe amalowa mwachinsinsi pa zenera ngati chigawenga. Wopusa iwe! O, mukanamvetsetsa njira za Ahebri. Mbala usiku anali mkulu wa ansembe yemwe ankabwera mosadziwitsa kuti awone ngati ansembe ake anali okhulupirika pa ntchito zawo za m'kachisi, pakuti ntchito zina zinkapitirira usiku wonse mpaka ntchitozo zitatha monga mwa chilamulo. Ngati mkulu wa ansembe atapeza wansembe yemwe akumuyang'anira akugona pa ntchito, ankamuponyera khala lamoto m'zovala zake, kuti wansembeyo agugude zovala zake zoyaka moto ndikutuluka m'kachisi ali maliseche ndi wamanyazi. Musakhale m'tulo pamene ndikubwera, apo ayi mudzagwa pamaso panga muli maliseche ndi amanyazi m'manyazi oyipitsitsa omwe mungawaganizire, pakuti ndidzachita nanu manyazi ndikukulondani mphoto yonse. Chilango changa chili pa inu ndipo dzanja langa lili lolemera pa inu, ndipo pokhapokha mutalapa, mudzakhala ndi mavuto akulu, akulu omwe mukanatha kuwathawa. Lapani, kuti ndikupatseni malo pa tebulo langa, ndipo mudzaphatikizana ndi Mkwatibwi wanga mu ukwati kwa ine, Khristu Yesu, Mkwati.

Ndine Mawu wosanduka thupi amene amakhala pakati panu.

Ndine mbusa wabwino ndipo nkhosa zanga zimamva mawu anga.

Ndine Mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu wotumizidwa chifukwa cha machimo a dziko lapansi.

Ndine mkulu wa ansembe kosatha monga mwa dongosolo la Melikizedeki.

Ndikubwera ndipo mphoto yanga ili ndi ine.

Ndine amene ndimayenda pakati pa zoyikapo nyale zisanu ndi ziwiri ndi angelo asanu ndi awiri.

Ndine mneneri woukitsidwa pakati pa abale amene Mose ananena za iye: Imvani mawu anga pomvera.

Ndine amene ndapereka mphatso kwa anthu, choncho musanyalanyaze mphatso zomwe ndapereka.

Ndikubwera posachedwa kuposa momwe mukuganizira; dzekonzekereni, Mkwatibwi wanga!

Ine, Ambuye Yesu Khristu, ndatumiza mawu awa ndipo ndakhala mawu a wofuula m'chipululu; pakuti Mpingo wanga wa ku Laodikaya ndi wofunda ndipo posachedwa udzakhala maliseche ndi wamanyazi pokhapokha atalapa. Ndimayima pakhomo ndikugogoda kanthawi kochepa chabe! Palibe manambala enanso pa wotchi, pakuti manambala agwa; ndipo palibe mchenga winanso womwe udzapezeke pamene mchenga womaliza ukudutsa mu galasi la mchenga, ndipo watsala pang'ono kugwa.
