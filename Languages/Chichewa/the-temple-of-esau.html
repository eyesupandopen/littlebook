Mboni Ziwiri: Isiraeli
Chowawa Chotsekemera
Novembala 23, 2024

Kachisi wa Esau

Imvani, imvani! O Isiraeli mawu a Yeshua HaMashiach, ngakhale mwana wobadwa yekha wa Yahweh, NDINE wamkulu! Kuti ndikuchenjezeni za zinthu zomwe zikubwera, ndiponso kuti mudziwe zambiri za msampha womwe Baraba adzatchera kuti musakodwe pamodzi ndi opanda chikhulupiriro; zinthu zomwe zinalembedwa kale pang'ono mu Uthenga Wabwino Wosatha. Koma ndabwera kudzakuuzani za msampha womwe kangaude amatchera m'dziko lolonjezedwa ndikuwunikira poyera kuti musawonongedwe ndi ziwembu zawo. Ndipo ndikufunitsitsa kuwongola njira zanu ndikukusungani m'njira zachilungamo, ngakhale njira za Yahweh, zomwe njira zake zimakhala zangwiro mwa Yeshua HaMashiach Mesiya wanu weniweni yemwe nthawi zonse amamvera chifuniro cha Yahweh mwangwiro. Ndiponso kuti mupitirize kuponda pa njira yowongoka ndi yopapatiza, yomwe ndidzakhazikitsa mapazi anu molimba ngati mupitiriza kunditsata m'chikhulupiriro chomvera. Chifukwa olungama adzakhala ndi moyo ndi chikhulupiriro!

Choncho mvetsetsani kuti nthawi yakwana, monga momwe zinalengezedwa ndi kudziwitsidwa pamene ndidzasonkhanitsa mwadzidzidzi okhulupirika mwa Khristu kumwamba kaya akhale amoyo kapena akufa; ndipo pamene oyera mtima okhulupirika atengedwa mwadzidzidzi chiwonongeko chadzidzidzi chidzagwera Babulo Wachinsinsi yemwe amakhala pamadzi ambiri, omwe ndi anthu, ndi makamu, ndi mitundu, ndi ziyankhulo. Ndipo pamene phulusa la nkhondo likufuka ndipo dongosolo lamakono la mitundu likuwonongedwa ndi kukonzedwanso kuti ligwirizane ndi zolinga zawo, dongosolo lomaliza la chirombo la ogwa lidzauka kwathunthu.

Komabe, chirombo chochokera kunyanja ndi chirombo chochokera pansi zakhala zikuchita ngati abusa abwino omwe anabwera kudzaweta nkhosa zotayika za Babulo Wachinsinsi ndi malonjezo ambiri opanda pake; ngakhale malonjezo abodza omwe pamapeto pake amabweretsa imfa ndi chiwonongeko. Ndipo amanamizira kukhala adani kuseri kwa chigoba cha chidole chokalamba chomwe amabisala kuseri kwake ndipo chidolecho chilipo kuti chitenge mlandu wa milandu yawo; ndipo amalamulira mayendedwe ake onse ndikulemba mawu ake onse. Chifukwa chinyengo chachikulu ichi chaperekedwa monga chiweruzo pa Babulo Wachinsinsi; kuti chinyengo champhamvu chimugwire kwathunthu chifukwa waphimba dziko lapansi ndi mabodza ake ali ndi chikho chagolide m'dzanja lake, chodzaza ndi zonyansa ndi uve wa dama lake.

Ndipo komabe zilombo ziwiri zoipazi zafunkha Babulo Wachinsinsi, ngakhale chiwonongeko chake chisanabwere ndipo zatenga mphamvu zake zankhondo ndi chuma chake chifukwa cha zolinga za chinjoka mu dongosolo lawo lomaliza la chirombo lomwe likubwera. Chifukwa nkhondo yomwe ikubwera idzanyeketsa Babulo Wachinsinsi, ndipo ndithudi adzawonongedwa mu ola limodzi, chifukwa zatero zalengezedwa ndi Yahweh, ngakhale NDINE Wamkulu. Komabe, izi ndi zokonzedwa ndipo zili mu dongosolo la Yahweh la chiwombolo (kuti adani ake amvere mawu ake) ndipo choncho chiweruzo cha Hule lalikulu chalengezedwa, ndikulembedwa, ndipo zidzakhala choncho mpaka kalekale, ndipo sadzaukanso. Ngakhale zili choncho, adzang'amba dziko lapansi ndipo chivomezi chachikulu chidzachitika, ndipo akonza chiwonongeko chake kuti asonkhanitse chuma chomwe amachilakalaka chomwe chakwiriridwa pansi. Ngakhale chuma chomwe akufunikira kuti apereke mphamvu ndi kukulitsa matekinoloje a ogwa; ngakhale zomwe dziko lapansi pakali pano lasangalatsidwa nazo ndikukopeka mu chinyengo chake champhamvu, kusungidwa mu ukapolo wa mafunde awo ndi machenjerero awo omwe amalimbikitsidwa ndi kulamuliridwa nawo.

Ndipo mwa anthu omwe adzatsale pambuyo pa chiwonongeko chomwe chikubwera cha Babulo Wachinsinsi; chirombo chochokera pansi ndi chirombo chochokera kunyanja zabweretsa gulu lankhondo la owukira ndi opha m'malire ake omwe chiwerengero chawo chili ngati mliri waukulu wa dzombe, ndipo adzamasulidwa pa chizindikiro chawo! Chifukwa awa ndi magulu ankhondo a chirombo omwe asonkhanitsidwa kuti awononge Babulo Wachinsinsi. Ambiri mwa awa akuchokera kumayiko a chinjoka ndi mayiko a chimbalangondo, ndipo adzachita monga momwe Babulo Wachinsinsi anawachitira mwachinsinsi m'zaka za yubile zapitazo: Adzapha mwamuna, mkazi, ndi mwana aliyense mopanda chifundo yemwe sakugwirizana ndi zolinga zawo ndipo ambiri omwe adzaloledwa kukhala ndi moyo adzakakamizidwa kulowa m'misasa yawo yaukapolo kukagwira ntchito m'migodi yawo mpaka imfa yawo. Ndipo adzagwira ntchito molimbika mpaka mpweya wawo womaliza; chifukwa zonse zomwe Babulo Wachinsinsi anachitira ena mwachinsinsi tsopano zidzachitidwa poyera kwa iye mu chiweruzo ndi chilango. Chifukwa Babulo Wachinsinsi waledzera ndi magazi a oyera mtima, ndi magazi a ofera chikhulupiriro a Yesu omwe anawapha mochuluka m'mayiko akunja; nthawi yonseyi akunena kuti iye mwini amatumikira Yesu wa ku Nazarete. Koma m'choonadi amatumikira ogwa ndipo zolinga zake tsopano zatha, ndipo amalakalaka chiwonongeko chake kuti athe kubisa milandu yawo ndiponso kuti abusa awo a mphere awoneke ngati abwino pamene akuuka kuti abise msampha wawo.

Komabe, sangabisale kwa Yahweh, kapena Yeshua HaMashiach ndipo milandu yawo yalembedwa mwangwiro kumwamba ndipo tchimo lililonse lalembedwa mwatsatanetsatane pamodzi ndi maganizo ndi zolinga za mitima yawo yoipitsidwa. Ndipo chirombo chochokera kunyanja ndi chirombo chochokera pansi ndi zonyansa zoipa zomwe zinalengedwa kuti ziwononge mtundu wa anthu. Zilombo zoipazi ndi ogwiririra ana, ndipo amatenga ndi kugwiritsa ntchito matupi a ana omwe awapha kuti apange zonyansa zawo ndipo choncho zonse zomwe amawononga zimaperekedwa nsembe kwa mulungu wawo. Chifukwa anatenga Chihema cha Moloki, ndi nyenyezi ya mulungu wawo Remfani, mafano omwe anapanga, kuti awapembedze, ndipo achita zambiri zomwe zidzaululidwa kwathunthu pa nthawi yoyenera youlula chifukwa chinsinsi chilichonse chidzabwera poyera.

Choncho zidzakhala kuti chirombo chochokera pansi chidzawoneka ngati chikuuka kuchokera kumanda; ngakhale kunena kuti wauka kwa akufa, ngakhale pamtima penipeni pa dziko lapansi chifukwa imfa sinathe kumugwira! Ndipo adzakhala ndi kuuka kwake kwabodza ndikubweretsa mtendere wabodza padziko lapansi kuti athetse nkhondo yawo, ndipo adzawoneka ngati wabwera kudzapulumutsa mtundu wa anthu. Ndipo ogwa adzadziulula kwa mtundu wa anthu ngati othandiza abwino omwe ali apamwamba kwambiri mu nzeru ndi opita patsogolo m'njira za tekinoloje, mankhwala, ndi mtendere.

Adzatulutsa zizindikiro ndi zozizwitsa zambiri zabodza, ndipo adzanena kuti omwe asonkhanitsidwa mwa Khristu pa msonkhano waukulu ndikutengedwa kupita kuchitetezo chakumwamba tsoka lalikulu lisanagwe; kuti ngakhale awa anatengedwa mokakamiza ndi mdani wonamizira wochokera kumlengalenga. Ngakhale gulu lankhondo la alendo omwe adzabwerenso kudzawononga anthu, ndipo adzasonkhanitsa munthu wachibadwa kuti agwirizane nawo kuti adzipulumutse ku choipa ichi chomwe amachiganizira m'chilengedwe chawo chonamizira cha maiko onamizira omwe amazungulira monama m'malo opanda kanthu omwe akukulirakulira.

Ngakhale sayansi yabodza ya zakuthambo yomwe ogwa anaganizira ndikugwiritsa ntchito m'mbuyomu mu nthawi yawo yagolide yakale, yomwe nthawi yagolideyo inatha m'manja mwa Yahweh mu chigumula chachikulu m'nthawi ya Nowa, zomwe zolembedwa zake zojambulidwa m'malemba oyera akale. Ndipo mabodza omwe adzaponyedwa pansi chifukwa chinyengo chawo chachikulu chidzaululidwa kwathunthu kuti onse awone onyenga awa omwe amanamizira kuti ndi ndani kwenikweni chifukwa ndi zolengedwa zogwa zochokera kumwamba zomwe zakhala zikudziipitsa mu kupandukira kopanda pake kuchokera ku chilungamo cha Yahweh ngakhale kuti alandire chiwonongeko chawo chomwe adzipangira okha mu cholowa chawo cha sulufule woyaka moto womwe sadzathawa.

Ndipo choncho chirombo chochokera pansi chidzauka ngati chochokera kwa akufa ndipo ndi zizindikiro ndi zozizwitsa zambiri zabodza chidzawonekera, ngakhale ndi nthambi yake ya azitona m'dzanja. Ndipo adzauka ngati mwanawankhosa wokhala ndi nyanga ziwiri pamene akuuka mu mphamvu zonse ndi ulamuliro wa chinjoka ngakhale ndi chirombo chochokera kunyanja, ngakhale iye amene wasankhidwa ndi chinjoka ndi chirombo chochokera pansi kuti alamulire mitundu kuti alamulire nyanja ya mitundu molingana ndi malamulo awo a Noahide amwano ndi opha anthu. Komabe mayiko ake awiri sadzakhala yankho chifukwa ndine amene ndidzagwirizanitsa Isiraeli mpaka kalekale kuti asagawidwenso maufumu awiri.

Ndipo choncho adzagwiritsa ntchito malamulo awo oipa ndi zotulukapo zake zomwe zili mu Noahide kuti achite nkhondo ndi oyera mtima ndi kuwagonjetsa, kupatulapo aliyense amene aitanira pa dzina la Ambuye mpaka mapeto adzapulumutsidwa, ngakhale omwe aitanira pa Yesu wa ku Nazarete monga Ambuye wawo wopulumutsa yemwe adzawapulumutsa mpaka kumapeto ngati sakomoka m'masautso awo. Ndipo choncho chirombo chochokera kunyanja chidzamasulidwa pa zomwe zatsala za mitundu yomwe adzakhala ataiwononga, ndipo phulusa lake linali mitundu yomwe ankailamulira mu chinyengo chachikulu. Ndipo adzawoneka ngati akuuka ngati mbalame ya phoenix yachikulu kuchokera ku phulusa ndi malawi a nkhondo omwe akufukabe kuchokera ku chinyengo chawo. Ndipo idzakhala nkhondo yaikulu, ngakhale yaikulu kwambiri pa nkhondo zitatu zoterezi zomwe ayambitsa ndi kulamulira; kuti chinjoka chiuke kwathunthu mu nthawi yake ndi zilombo zake zoipa, zonyansa zomwe chidzakhala ndi ulamuliro wake waufupi, kuti chisalamulirenso.

Chifukwa chinsinsi cha kusayeruzika chakhala chikugwira ntchito m'mibadwo yonse; pamene hule lalikulu lomwe limakhala pamapiri asanu ndi awiri, ngakhale hule lalikulu lofiira la mwano lomwe limapembedza mfumukazi yakumwamba ndipo limaphunzitsa Yesu wina monga gawo la utatu wawo wachikunja. Ngakhale omwe mizu yawo yakhazikika mu Babulo wakale komwe Nimrodi anayamba nthano zake ndi mfumukazi yakumwamba. Omwe cholowa chawo ndi ansembe ndi mibadwo yawo amatsata mzera wawo kwa ogwa omwe analamulira Babulo wakale ndi atatu awo osayera omwe amagwiritsa ntchito kunyoza umulungu weniweni, ndi mbewu yolonjezedwa ya mkazi. Zochita ndi miyambo yachikunja iyi yalamulira dziko lapansi mwachinsinsi chachikulu komwe ansembe oyambitsidwa a ogwa amapembedza dzuwa mwachinsinsi koma amati ndi Gulu la Yesu; ndipo choncho tchalitchi chawo chabodza ndi chamwano chonamizira chachita chitsenderezo ndi chizunzo padziko lapansi masiku omwe chinsinsi cha kusayeruzika chinafalikira mwachinsinsi. Choncho, chiwonongeko chawo sichichedwa ndipo nthawi yawo yakwana!

Komabe zilombo ziwiri zoipazi, ngakhale zomwe pakali pano zikugwira ntchito limodzi kuti ziwononge kwathunthu Babulo wachinsinsi, ngakhale chirombo chochokera kunyanja ndi chirombo chochokera pansi; ngakhale awiriwa omwe onse akhala mitu yolamulira ya Babulo Wachinsinsi ndipo omwe alamulira ndi malonjezo ambiri akulu ndi opanda pake omwe abusa awiri a mpherewa aweta nkhosa zotayika za Babulo Wachinsinsi kulonjeza kulemera kwabodza kwakuthupi ndi kuchuluka kwa omwe amawaweta. Ndipo m'manja mwawo ndi machenjerero awo akonza ndithu ndipo adzachita zomwe zaperekedwa m'manja mwa chinjoka kuti chikwaniritse.

Chifukwa Babulo Wachinsinsi, ngakhale hule lalikulu lomwe limakhala pamadzi ambiri ndi mtundu womwe unapangidwa kuti zilombo ziwiri zoipazi zithe kuuka kuchokera ku phulusa lake mu nthawi yawo youlula. Ngakhale mtundu wachigololo, ngakhale mtundu wa dama, mtundu womwe ulidi hule lalikulu lodzinenera kuti ndi wotsatira wolungama wa Yesu wa ku Nazarete. Koma m'choonadi ndi mtundu womwe omanga ake ali omasuka ku Thanthwe lenileni la Maziko, ngakhale mwala wapakona waukulu womwe opanda chikhulupiriro amapunthwa, ngakhale Yesu wa ku Nazarete yemwe alinso Yeshua HaMashiach. Ndipo choncho omanga oipawa adzimasula okha ku chipulumutso ndikudzipereka okha ku nyanja yamoto komwe nyongolotsi yawo sifa pokana mwala wapakona waukulu ndi thanthwe lokhumudwitsa ndipo sadzathawa!

Komabe ogwa nthawi zonse akhala ndi zolinga zomwezo m'maganizo ndipo mapulani awo ndi zolinga zawo zimakhalabe zomwezo. Chifukwa akufuna kuwononga kwathunthu Adamu padziko lapansi, ndipo akufuna kwathunthu kuwononga Yakobo pansi padzuwa ngakhale kuti apitirize kukhala ngati okhala mopanda chilolezo ndi olanda dziko lapansi osalungama kuti athe kukhala ndi nthawi yawo yagolide yomwe amalakalaka. Koma mvetsetsani kuti nthawi yawo yagolide inali nthawi yomwe Adamu anagonjetsedwa ndi kulamuliridwa ndi ufumu wapadziko lonse wa zonyansa zoipa zomwe zinapangidwa kuti ziwononge kwathunthu mtundu wa anthu, ndipo zonyansa zawo zidzabwerera! Koma sadzakhala ndi nthawi yagolide koma ziyembekezo zosweka ndi chiweruzo chaukali pamene ufumu wa ogwa ukuwonongedwa kotheratu pamaso pawo ndi dzanja langa lamphamvu!

Ndipo yomwe amati nthawi yawo yagolide inali nthawi yomwe mzera wolungama wa Seti unabisala m'chipululu kuchokera ku gulu lawo loipa m'dziko lomwe linalipo panthawiyo. Ngakhale kuti mzera wa magazi wa Seti ukhalebe woyera ku kuipitsidwa kwa majini ndipo mbewu yolonjezedwa ya mkazi (yomwe ndine), ikhoza kubwera monga momwe zinalonjezedwa kale ndi Yahweh mwiniwake. Taonani! Yahweh walengeza kuti Adzabweretsa Zimphona kuti akwaniritse mkwiyo wake, ndipo zidzakhala choncho. Komabe, choncho ufumu wawo woipa udzauka ndikulamulira dziko lonse lapansi ndipo adzabweretsa zoipa zambiri, ndipo zonyansa zambiri zidzamasulidwa kuchokera kuseri kwa madzi oundana ndi kuchokera pamtima pa dziko lapansi ndipo phompho lidzatsegulidwa.

Kudzakhala choipa chachikulu padziko lapansi ndipo ngakhale adzauka kuti abweretse mtendere wabodza; nkhondo idzalamulira ulamuliro wawo nthawi zonse chifukwa mtendere udzachotsedwa padziko lapansi mu chiweruzo pakuchotsedwa kwa woletsa wanga. Ndipo mtendere sadzakhala nawo chifukwa Yahweh walengeza kale kuti ogwa alibe mtendere chifukwa alakwira mtundu wa anthu m'zilakolako zawo zoipa ndi chidani cha zinthu zonse zopangidwa ndi Yahweh. Ndipo adzakhazikitsa mtendere wabodza m'dziko ndipo chirombo chochokera pansi chidzabwera kwathunthu mu mphamvu yake, ndipo chidzalamulira ngati mwanawankhosa wokhala ndi nyanga ziwiri yemwe amalankhula ngati chinjoka.

Pakuti mwanawankhosa wa Esau, ngakhale chirombo chochokera pansi yemwenso ndi Baraba, wasankhidwa ngati womanga kachisi wawo wachirombo ndipo waperekedwa ngati mwanawankhosa wa chinjoka kwa Moshiach wawo wabodza ndi mfumu yonamizira. Zoonadi, zoonadi, adzagwirizanitsa Esau ndi Ashkenazi ndi onse omwe agwirizana nawo pansi pa mbendera ya chipembedzo chomwe Atate awo sanachidziwe ndipo njira zawo za Talmudic zidzalamulira dziko lapansi. Adzagonjetsa mwankhanza mitundu pansi pa Noahide ndipo adzalamulira mitundu ndi nkhanza yaikulu; ndipo chirombo chochokera pansi chidzachititsa onse, ang'ono ndi akulu, olemera ndi osauka, aufulu ndi akapolo kuti alandire chizindikiro chake m'manja mwawo amanja kapena pamphumi pawo kuti pasakhale munthu amene angagule kapena kugulitsa, kupatulapo iye amene ali ndi chizindikiro, kapena dzina la chirombo, kapena nambala ya dzina lake. Chifukwa amabweretsa kulumwa kwa njoka komwe palibe munthu angachire ndipo onse omwe atenga nawo mbali adzadzisintha okha mpaka kalekale komwe kusintha kumeneko sadzakhala ndi chikhululukiro, ndipo adzasakaniza chitsulo ndi dongo, koma sadzagwirizana wina ndi mzake. Chomwe chili chokhotakhota sichingawongoledwe ndipo onse omwe adzipanga okha okhotakhota m'njira yotere adzakhala ndi gawo lawo mu nyanja yaikulu yoyaka sulufule ya malawi amoto osazimitsidwa komwe nyongolotsi yawo sifa.

Choncho chirombo chochokera pansi chomwenso ndi mneneri wawo wabodza ndi Moshiach wabodza chidzapanga zolengeza zake ndipo chidzamanga kachisi wake chifukwa chatumizidwa ngati womanga kachisi wachirombo yemwe wadzipangira nsanja zambiri mu ufumu wake zoperekedwa ku dzina lake, ndipo adzadzilankhula yekha modabwitsa kwambiri ngakhale kulengeza kuti iye mwini ndi Mulungu. Pakuti amabwera kudzapanga malonjezo ambiri akulu omwe alibe mphamvu zowakwaniritsa, ndipo amabwera kudzayankhula zinthu zambiri zazikulu ndi zamwano, ndipo choncho adzachita chifukwa zaperekedwa kwa iye kuti akwaniritse. Chifukwa ndi womanga ndi wotukula yemwe wadzipangira nsanja zambiri ndi malo ochitira tchuthi, ndipo zoonadi amakhala pamwamba pa ufumu wake womwe wadzipereka mu ulemu wake; chifukwa nthawi zonse amadzitamanda yekha chifukwa zotero ndi njira za chinjoka yemwe ndi Atate wake weniweni.

Pamene kachisi wake akumangidwa ndi kumalizidwa pakati pa chisangalalo chachikulu ndi malonjezo ambiri amwano ndi abodza, choncho adzapanga zolengeza zambiri zabodza mu ofesi yake ngati mneneri wabodza ndipo ulamuliro wake udzakhala woopsa ndi wankhanza. Komabe, adzatchera msampha wake koma ndi mlenje uti amene amatchera msampha pamaso pa mbalame? Mwachinsinsi chachikulu amatchera msampha wake ndipo ndi lonjezo lalikulu adzalonjeza kupereka kachisi wake kwa anthu a m'dzikolo ndipo ndi chisangalalo chachikulu Esau ndi Ashkenazi adzadzikondwerera okha kuzungulira mwanawankhosa wa Esau, ndipo adzanamizira kuti akukhazikitsanso njira za Torah, koma akubweretsa njira za imfa zomwe zalembedwa m'buku lawo la imfa yamwano, ngakhale Talmud yawo. Taonani! Chirombo chochokera pansi ndi wakupha ndi mtsogoleri wa kupanduka kotsutsana ndi Yahweh m'njira za Baraba. Adzapereka onse omwe amawapangira malonjezo ake akulu ndipo mopanda chifundo kudzakhala kupereka kwake ndipo ndi chidani chachikulu chomwe walandira kuchokera kwa Atate wake, mdierekezi yemwe zilakolako zake wamangidwa mu kusayeruzika kuti akwaniritse.

Komanso ndi momwe ilili chikhalidwe cha kangaude amene waluka dziko lapansi lonse mu ukonde wake. Kangaude amakhala ndikudikirira nyama yake kuti igwe mu ukonde wake ndipo kenako amalumpha kuti agwire, kupuwaza ndi kukulunga nyama yake kuti adye pang'onopang'ono moyo wa omwe wamagwira; pamene akumezedwa pang'onopang'ono ndi choipa chomwe akodwa nacho. Ndipo komabe makamu a anthu adzabwera kuchokera pafupi ndi patali pakumva kuitana kwake; ndipo kuitanira kwa kudzipereka kwake ndi kuyeretsedwa kwa kachisi wake kudzamveka padziko lonse lapansi ndipo ambiri amene akana Mesiya wawo woona adzagwidwa mu msampha wake ndi kuwonongeka nawo. Koma sudzaoneka ngati msampha kwa omwe wawalodza, koma chitsitsimutso cha Mose chotsogozedwa ndi Sanihedirini yawo ndi kulamulidwa ndi miyambo ndi nzeru za Esau zomwe zili m'buku lawo lamwano ndi losayera. Ndipo gulu la ansembe onyenga omwe sali a Levi chifukwa Levi akupezeka kwathunthu mwa Mkulu wa ansembe wa Melikizedeki, chifukwa Ine, Yeshua HaMashiach ndine Mkulu wa ansembe wanu kosatha monga mwa dongosolo la Melikizedeki, pakuti Yahweh walengeza kutero.

Koma monga momwe ndinaphunzitsira choonadi mu nthawi yanga pamene ndinabwera kwa anga monga Mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu; pamene ndinakanidwa ndi abale anga, komabe ndinaphunzitsa choonadi chimodzimodzi, chifukwa chake, O Isiraeli mulibe chowiringula. Ngakhale zili choncho, m'chikondi changa pa inu ndi malinga ndi malonjezo ndi mapangano akale, ndikubwera kudzakuuzani inu kachiwiri kuti Yakobo asagwere kwa Esau.

Pakuti kwalembedwa m'malemba oyera: Chifukwa chake pamene mudzaona chonyansa cha kupasula, chonenedwa ndi mneneri Daniele, chitaimirira m'malo oyera, (amene awerenga, azindikire). Pamenepo amene ali m'Yudeya, athawire kumapiri. Amene ali pamwamba pa nyumba, asatsike, kukatenga kanthu m'nyumba mwake: Kapena amene ali kumunda, asabwerere m'mbuyo kukatenga zovala zake. Ndipo tsoka kwa iwo amene ali ndi pakati, ndi kwa iwo amene akuyamwitsa masiku amenewo. Koma pempherani kuti kuthawa kwanu kusakhale m'nyengo yozizira, kapena tsiku la Sabata: Pakuti pamenepo padzakhala chisautso chachikulu, chotere sichinakhale kuyambira chiyambi cha dziko lapansi kufikira tsopano, iyayi, ndipo sichidzakhala. Ndipo pamenepo chisautso chachikulu koposa chidzagwera dziko lapansi ndipo magazi adzayenda padziko lapansi mochuluka. Chotero, chisautso chachikulu koposa chidzachitika, ngakhale chisautso chofanana ndi chomwe sichinachitikepo padziko lapansi kale, ndipo ndithudi, sichidzachitikanso. Taonani!
