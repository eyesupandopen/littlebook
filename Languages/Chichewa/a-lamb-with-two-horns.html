Mwanawankhosa Wokhala Ndi Nyanga Ziwiri

Kuchokera kwa YHVH: Wamkulu NDINE!

Taonani! Chinyengo chachikulu chachokera ku Babulo Wachinsinsi ndipo chagwira dziko lapansi m'manja mwake mopha! Bwerani pafupi kuti mumvetsetse chinyengo chachikulu, inu amene mumakhala pa dziko lapansi, ndikumvetsetsa nthawi yomwe mukukhala, chifukwa chinyengo chachikulu chafika chomwe dziko lapansi likusangalatsidwa nacho chifukwa dziko lawo ndi bwalo la zisudzo ndipo atsogoleri awo ndi ochita zisudzo, ngakhale onyenga omwe amanyenga. Ndipo chotero tsopano ndidzavumbulutsa choonadi chokhudza bwalo lawo la zisudzo lomwe amagwiritsa ntchito kusangalatsa makamu a anthu.

Chenjerani! Chinyengo chachikulu chakugwerani ndi zizindikiro ndi zozizwitsa zambiri zonama ndipo chinyengo chachikuluchi cholinga chake ndi chiwonongeko cha Yakobo, ngakhale zolinga zomwe Esau wakhala akuzisunga mwachinsinsi m'mibadwo yonse, koma tsopano zidzavumbulutsidwa ndi kuwululidwa kuti onse aone. Ndipo monga mwana wanga, Yesu wa ku Nazarete, anauza Atate awo ku Yudeya m'Zikondwerero zapitazi: Inu ndinu a Atate wanu mdierekezi ndipo zilakolako za Atate wanu mudzazichita. Ndipo chotero ndikubwera kwa iwe O' Isiraeli kuti usatengedwe ndi chinyengo chachikuluchi, chifukwa ndiwe amene ukufunidwa (ngakhale dziko lapansi lidzagwedezeka) chifukwa ndi mlenje uti amene amatchera msampha pamaso pa mbalame?

Ndipo ndine, Yahweh ngakhale Wamkulu NDINE, amene ndikubwera kudzachenjeza anthu anga kudzera m'Mawu awa omwe ndawavumbulutsa ndi kuwatumiza kudzera mwa mthenga ndi mtumiki wanga amene watumidwa pa zolinga izi. Chotero ndikubwera kwa iwo omwe ali a mafuko kaya ali pafupi kapena kutali, kaya ali m'dzikolo kapena obalalika kunja, ndipo ndidzasonkhanitsa otsalira anga onse kwa Mesiya wawo ndi Mbusa Wamkulu, Yeshua HaMashiach.

Pakuti ndiye amene adzatsogolera Isiraeli ku chitetezo m'manja mwa 144,000 anga amphamvu chifukwa Yesu wa ku Nazarete ndamupanga kukhala Mfumu ya Mafumu ndi Mbuye wa Ambuye! Ndipo ndiye amene adzalamulira dziko lapansi m'chilungamo mu ulamuliro wake wa zaka chikwi chimodzi kuchokera pampando wachifumu wa Davide mu Isiraeli womwe walandira ngati cholowa. Pakuti choncho ndinalonjeza Atate anu ndipo NDINE amene amakwaniritsa lonjezo lililonse lomwe ndimapanga nthawi zambiri m'chilungamo changa chosatha kuti zonse zikhale inde ndi ameni ndipo NDINE Mulungu Wamuyaya wa Chilungamo amene safoka!

Komabe, zili choncho kuti chinjoka chidzadzuka kwathunthu ndipo chilombo chochokera kunyanja chadzipisa chokha kuti chidzuke kwathunthu pakati pa omwe chidzawatsogolera, ndipo chidzadzuka kuchokera ku phulusa mu nthawi yake yoikidwa. Chafunkha ndi kukonzekera chiwonongeko chathunthu cha Babulo Wachinsinsi, ngakhale kugwira ntchito limodzi ndi chilombo chochokera pansi kukonza ndi kuchita chiwonongeko chawo, ndipo chotero ndawapatsa kuti akwaniritse. Ndipo dziko lapansi lasangalatsidwa ndi hule lalikulu ndipo silingathenso kuganizira dziko lopanda kuponderezedwa kwa hule lalikulu lomwe lapondereza nalo anthu a pa dziko lapansi ndikuwononga chilengedwe changa ndi uve wa dama lake ngakhale kuperekedwa ku dziko lapansi kuchokera ku chikho chagolide chonyansacho m'manja mwake.

Ndipo zili choncho, kuti ndikubwera kudzakukanjeza, O' Isiraeli, za chinyengo chachikulu chomwe chatsala pang'ono kugwetsa dziko lapansi mu nkhondo yeniyeni ndi chisokonezo, ngakhale chinyengo chomwe ayeserera pogwiritsa ntchito matekinoloje awo chifukwa onse ndi onyenga omwe amanyenga, ndipo mu chinyengo chawo mneneri wawo wonyenga adzalowa m'dzikolo ngakhale monga Moshiach wonyenga wa Esau.

Ndipo Esau amadzibisa ndi kudzibisa pakati pa Isiraeli, ngakhale kunamizira kuti ndi Isiraeli pamene akufuna kudziphatikiza pakati pa Isiraeli, ngakhale kubisa chikhalidwe chawo chenicheni pakati pa omwe amanamizira. (Iwo ndi namsongole pakati pa tirigu ndipo Mbuye wa Zokolola adzawazula kwathunthu.) Ndipo chotero chinjoka chikudzuka chifukwa ndi ola lake, ndipo Khristu Yesu adzachotsa mwadzidzidzi okhulupirika mwa Khristu pa dziko lapansi, (amoyo ndi akufa omwe) ndipo kenako chiweruzo chidzabwera kwathunthu pa Babulo Wachinsinsi ngakhale hule lalikulu lofiira lija loloseredwa ndi Yohane Wovumbulutsa zikondwerero zambiri zapitazi.

Ndipo NDINE Nkhalamba ya Masiku, ngakhale Mbuye wa Mizimu ndipo ndikuchitira umboni, kuti onse amvetsetse kulankhula kwanga: Chinthu chomwe chakhala chidzachitikanso: ndipo chinthu chomwe chachitika, chikuchitikanso, ndipo palibe chinthu chatsopano pansi pa dzuwa. Ndipo zili choncho kuti ogwa agwira ntchito kuwononga Adamu pa dziko lapansi kuyambira pachiyambi kuti alitenge kukhala lawo, ngakhale munda wanga wa moyo kumene ndimasamalira ndi kulera miyoyo ya anthu kuti ndiwawombole ku chivundi mwa Khristu Yesu amene ndamulenga monga Adamu womaliza. Ndipo ndi mwa Mesiya wanu, O' Isiraeli, kuti Isiraeli wa Mulungu adzasonkhanitsidwa kwathunthu ndi kuwomboledwa monga munthu mmodzi watsopano wopangidwa mofanana ndi Mbuye wanu ndi muwomboli, amene anagula chiwombolo chanu m'mwazi wake womwe wotetezera.

Choncho mvetsetsa O' Isiraeli kuti Yesu wa ku Nazarete ndi Yeshua HaMashiach amene alinso Khristu Yesu (mvetsetsani kutsindika kwa mayina ake chifukwa chipulumutso chanu ndi chokwanira) choncho imvani Mawu ake pomvera chifukwa cha miyoyo yanu, chifukwa mulibe nthawi yotsutsana ndi mlengi wanu komanso mulibe nthawi yokana Mesiya wanu weniweni.

Ndipo kwalembedwa chimodzimodzi monga ndinalengeza, ndipo chotero ndinapangitsa Yeshua HaMashiach kuvumbulutsa kwa mtumwi wake Paulo za iye: Chifukwa chake, Elohim wamukwezanso kwambiri, ndikumupatsa dzina loposa dzina lililonse, Kuti m'dzina la Yesu bondo lililonse lipinde, la zinthu za kumwamba, ndi zinthu za pa dziko lapansi, ndi zinthu za pansi pa dziko lapansi, Ndipo kuti lilime lililonse livomereze kuti Yesu Khristu ndi Mbuye, ku ulemerero wa Mulungu Atate. Ndipo zili choncho, kuti Ine YHVH, Wamkulu NDINE, ndikutsimikiziranso kudalirika kwa Mawu awa kuti chikhulupiriro chanu chisagwedezeke.

Chinyengo chachikulu chafika ndipo ambiri adzakodwa mmenemo ndipo ambiri adzagwera m'malawi a moto, ngakhale onse omwe mayina awo sanalembedwe m'Buku la Moyo la Mwanawankhosa kuyambira pachiyambi. Chifukwa, Ine El Shaddai, NDINE amene amadziwa chiyambi kuchokera kumapeto ndipo ngakhale, ndimapatsa mzimu uliwonse mwayi wokhala ndi moyo wolungama pamaso panga, ndimadziwa omwe adzasankhe kudzaza machimo awo kwathunthu kuchokera kwa omwe adzabwere kwa Khristu kuti alengedwenso m'chilungamo ndipo ziweruzo zanga ndi zangwiro m'chilungamo changa.

Koma iwe O' Isiraeli, ulibe chilungamo chifukwa wakana Mulungu Wamuyaya wa Chilungamo chifukwa ukukana Mwana wanga wobadwa yekha amene ndinamutumiza kuti akhululukire machimo monga mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu, chifukwa chake, machimo ako akadalipo. Ndipo chotero ndikukulangiza, Isiraeli, imva Mawu a Wamkulu NDINE mu nthawi yomalizayi ndipo landira nsembe yanga!

Inde, Isiraeli, ndinakupatsa nsembe yangwiro chifukwa Adamu anaipitsidwa mu ungwiro ndipo moyo wa thupi uli m'mwazi. Ndipo chotero tchimo limaperekedwa ndi mwazi womwe ndi moyo wa thupi kuchokera kwa Atate kupita kwa ana ake kuti kaya ndi aamuna kapena aakazi amalandira chivundi chifukwa Adamu ndi woipitsidwa nthawi zonse. Mu chifundo changa chamuyaya ndi chikondi ndinapereka chomwe nchosatheka kwa cholengedwa china chilichonse kukwaniritsa: Ndinalenga ndikupereka Mwana wanga wobadwa yekha, amene Mose akulamula kumvera kwanu, chifukwa cha machimo a anthu onse, kaya ndi Ayuda kapena amitundu, kaya ndi akapolo kapena aufulu.

Komabe zili choncho Isiraeli kuti adani ako amalakalaka chiwonongeko chako chifukwa amalakalaka kukhala ndi nthawi yawo yagolide kachiwiri, ngakhale nthawi yagolide yomwe sadzaipezanso chifukwa m'malo mwa nthawi yawo yagolide yodzaza ndi machimo asanachitike chigumula kumene zonyansa zamitundu yonse zinkayendayenda, m'malo mwake adzakhala ndi chiweruzo chaukali ndi chilango pamene ufumu wawo ukuwonongedwa kotheratu. Ndipo posachedwapa zidzachitika kuti chilombo ndi mneneri wonyenga adzaponyedwa amoyo m'nyanja yamoto yoyaka sulufule ndipo Satana adzamangidwa zaka chikwi chimodzi mu mazunzo ndi mantha aakulu a cholowa chake chomwe chikubwera m'malawi amoto amuyaya a chilango kumene mphutsi yawo sifa.

Ndipo chinjoka chamangiriridwa pa dziko lapansi kuti asaloŵenso kumwamba kwamuyaya koma asungidwa pansi pa thambo omangiriridwa pa dziko lapansi chifukwa cha chiweruzo chomwe anachilakalaka, ndipo chinjoka chili ndi mkwiyo waukulu pakugonjetsedwa kwake, chifukwa chagonjetsedwa kotheratu m'manja mwa Yeshua HaMashiach amene ndi mbewu yolonjezedwa ya mayi wa amoyo onse, pakuti choncho ndinalonjeza Adamu ndi Hava m'chikondi changa pa iwo. Chotero zili kuti amaloledwa kokha kukwaniritsa zolinga zanga zamuyaya asanatumizidwe ku malawi amoto kwamuyaya, ndipo ndidzaika chidindo, ndipo sadzakumbukiridwa kapena kutchulidwa pakuti chotero ndi tsogolo lanu la lonjezo kumene mwamasulidwa kwa opondereza anu kwamuyaya ndipo mu mtendere wamuyaya ndi wangwiro mudzakhala ngati mungopinda bondo kwa Mesiya wanu weniweni, ngakhale Yeshua HaMashiach, pakuti palibe njira ina!

Choncho zili choncho kuti tsopano ndidzakupatsa kuti umvetsetse zinthu zomwe zatsala pang'ono kuchitika, ndipo ndithudi msampha wotcheredwa ndi mlenje uyenera kuvumbulutsidwa, ngakhale msampha wotcheredwa ndi chinjoka chomwe chikufuna kukola nkhunda zanga zamtengo wapatali, ngakhale anthu anga apadera omwe ndinadziikira ndekha. Ndipo NDINE amene ndinapangitsa nkhunda ya Nowa kubwerera ndi tsamba la azitona mkamwa mwake, ndipo mofananamo, NDINE amene ndatumiza pangano langa lamtendere lamuyaya pamaso pa Yesu Khristu, Mesiya wanu woukitsidwa O' Isiraeli!

Ndipo choncho, mvetsetsani nthawi yomwe mukukhala. Sunagoge la Satana likuyenda ndi zizindikiro ndi zozizwitsa zambiri zonama, ndipo mwa chinyengo amachita nkhondo. Amachita miyambo yawo poyera, yobisika pang'ono ngati zosangalatsa ndi nkhani ndi misonkhano yandale. Amaliza ndondomeko yawo yosankha, kumene Esau tsopano wasankha mwanawankhosa wake pakati pa Sunagoge la Satana chifukwa cha chinyengo chawo chamwano chomwe akufuna kukola nacho. Koma chilombo chochokera pansi si mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu amene amamunyoza mopusa ndi mwamwano, koma mneneri wawo wonyenga amene tsopano adzisankhira okha amene ali ndi nyanga ziwiri ngati mwanawankhosa, koma amalankhula ngati chinjoka.

Ndipo choncho ndinali ndi Mwana wanga, Yesu Khristu Wolungama, kuvumbulutsa mu The Choice is Yours (phunzirani kuti mudziwe) pamene Baraba analandira bala kumutu ngati kuti linali la imfa ndipo chochitikacho chinali ndi matanthauzo angapo omwe sanawavumbulutse panthawiyo, koma zinali pa msonkhano uwu, ngakhale mwambo wake wosankha kumene amati, kuti Yahweh mwiniwake anaboola dzenje m'khutu lake kumupanga kukhala mtumiki wanga waumulungu. Ndipo zili choncho, ndikulumbira pa dzina langa, ngakhale YHVH Wamkulu NDINE, kuti sindinamupange kukhala kapolo wanga, komanso sindinapereke Mesiya wina aliyense kupatula Mwana wanga wobadwa yekha, ngakhale Yeshua HaMashiach, amene ali mwanawankhosa yekhayo wa Mulungu amene ndidzamutumiza. Ndipo chotero kuyesa kumupha kwake kunagwiritsidwa ntchito ngati chimodzi mwa zizindikiro ndi zozizwitsa zambiri zonama kumene wonyengayu amanyenga. Ndipo za wonyengayu sali wa chikhalidwe changa kapena gulu langa ndipo ndi mdani wanga.

Komabe, ambiri mu Babulo Wachinsinsi omwe ananamizira kuti Yesu Khristu ndi Mbuye wawo tsopano asiya Mbuye wawo yemwe angakhale m'malo mwa Baraba, amene amalankhula ngati chinjoka komabe ndi mwanawankhosa wokhala ndi nyanga ziwiri. Ndipo komabe ambiri mwa omwe apanga Baraba kukhala Mbuye wawo adzasiyidwa ku moto wa woyenga chifukwa chipulumutso chawo sichinamalizidwe kwathunthu chifukwa sanabwere kwa Khristu kwathunthu ndi mitima yodzaza ndi chikhulupiriro ndi yolapa koma m'malo mwake ndi zilakolako zomwe uthenga wabwino wa chitukuko umatulutsa. Chifukwa Sunagoge la Satana linaipitsa malemba awo oyera ndi chilolezo chawo ndipo tsopano asanduka a Ziyoni. Ndipo komabe ambiri mwa miyoyo yomweyi, adzapatsidwa ulemu wa kuphedwa chifukwa cha chikhulupiriro chawo pa chipulumutso chawo ngati asankha kulandira chipulumutso poitanira pa dzina la Mbuye wawo Yesu Khristu mpaka mapeto, ndipo ndidzawalandira kwamuyaya ndi nthawi zonse ndipo masautso awo sadzafanana ndi ulemerero womwe udzavumbulutsidwa mwa iwo.

Choncho, moshiach wonyengayu amayenda ndi kulalikira pamene akulankhula ngati chinjoka ndipo amanamizira ndi kunena zinthu zambiri zazikulu ndi zamwano ngakhale asanadzuke kwathunthu, ndipo adzanena kuti wadzuka. Mvetsetsani anthu anga kuti mneneri wonyengayu amene alinso chilombo chochokera pansi amene amatumikira Sunagoge la Satana tsopano wasankhidwa ngati mwanawankhosa wawo. Ndipo zidzakhalabe kuti mutu wake udzaoneka ngati wavulazidwa mpaka imfa, koma bala lake lakupha linachiritsidwa, ndipo dziko lonse lapansi lidzadabwa ndi kutsatira chilombocho. Ndipo chotero wonyengayu adzadzuka.

Pakuuka kwake adzanena kuti wadzipereka nsembe ngati mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu kubweretsa mtendere ku Isiraeli ngati nkhunda yawo yomwe imawabweretsera tsamba la azitona! Ndipo osati Isiraeli yekha, koma dziko lapansi lenilenilo ndipo zidzaoneka kuti tsopano wabwerera kudzabweretsa mtendere, koma njira zamtendere sadziziwa ndipo pa chiweruzo cha Babulo Wachinsinsi nkhondo yeniyeni idzayamba. Chifukwa mzimu wonyenga wa chitonthozo wochokera ku chinjoka wakhazikika pa dziko lapansi, ndipo ndatumiza ngakhale chinyengo champhamvu kuti onse omwe amasangalala ndi chosalungama akhulupirire bodza kuti onse atsutsidwe omwe amasangalala ndi chosalungama. Pakuti pamene adzati mtendere ndi chitetezo, pamenepo chiwonongeko chadzidzidzi ndipo sadzapulumuka.

Ndipo ambiri mu Babulo Wachinsinsi, ngakhale omwe amakhulupirira mabodza ake ndi kutsatira mawu ake abwino ndi nkhani zake zokongola akugwidwa ndi kukodwa ndi mtendere wonyenga kumene amaganiza kuti miyoyo yawo yokoma ndi yochimwa idzapitirira kumene lero lili ngati dzulo ndipo mawa lidzakhala lofanana ndi lero ndipo ambiri adzafuula, kulira, ndi kudandaula kuti chiyembekezo chawo tsopano chachotsedwa kwa iwo, ndipo adzamenyana ndi anzawo mpaka imfa ndipo nthawi yonseyi kuukira kwawo kwadzidzidzi kwathunthu kudzamasulidwa kwathunthu, ngakhale pamene woletsa wanga akuchotsedwa pa dziko lapansi ndipo Babulo Wachinsinsi adzagwa ku gawo lowirikiza la chiweruzo mu ola limodzi pamene masoka ambiri akugwa.

Ndipo Sunagoge la Satana lidzakhala ndi nkhondo yawo yomwe apha nayo osalakwa mu chinyengo chawo kuti ayambitse ndipo omwe amalambira ma kiyubu awo akuda adzitopetsa okha m'kupha, ngakhale kupha komwe kumabweretsedwa mwadala ndi chinyengo chachikulu ndipo kuchokera ku phulusa adzadzuka. Ndipo ogwa adzadzivumbulutsa kwathunthu kupereka kwa anthu thandizo lomwe likuwoneka ngati labwino koma njira zawo ndi matekinoloje awo cholinga chake ndi kukola onse omwe angathe mu cholowa chawo m'malawi amoto a chilango, omwe sadzawathawa ndipo kumene adzalandira nthawi zambiri pa tchimo LILILONSE lomwe achita ndipo chilungamo chidzachitika. Koma ndi mlenje uti amene amatchera msampha pamaso pa mbalame?

Choncho ndikukupatsani kuti mumvetsetse, kuti chiweruzo cha Babulo Wachinsinsi chakonzedwa ndipo ali okonzeka. Koma choyamba mwanawankhosa wonyenga uyu wa nyanga ziwiri adzadziyesa wolungama pamene kwenikweni ali mbusa wamphere, mmodzi wa nyanga ziwiri zolamulira, ngakhale nyanga yaing'ono ya Danieli. Ndipo adzaoneka ngati ayesetsa kupulumutsa Babulo Wachinsinsi poyeretsa ziphuphu zake koma m'choonadi iye ndi woipitsa wamkulu amene anakola ambiri omwe tsopano adzawayeretsa ndi kuwaimba mlandu chifukwa cha ziphuphu zake zomwe anagwiritsa ntchito kukakamiza ambiri kuti agonje. Ndipo tsopano onse amene sakugwirizana ndi zolinga zawo, kapena amene angachitire umboni maso ndi maso za kuipa kwawo adzachotsedwa ndi manja a mbusa wamphere uyu ndipo adzaimbidwa mlandu chifukwa cha machimo omwe akupitirizabe kuchita ndipo omwe akutsogolera ena, ndipo sadzapulumuka.

Ndipo, mvetsetsaninso kuti ambiri omwe ali mbali ya milandu yawo ndipo omwe ali namsongole ngati abusa amphere awa adzalowa m'malo awo obisalamo ndi kudzibisa pansi pa dziko lapansi ku mkwiyo umene ukubwera ndipo adzawononga umboni wonse wa miyoyo yomwe anatenga mu Babulo Wachinsinsi m'chinyengo chachikulu, ndipo sadzapulumuka mkwiyo umene ukubwera! Komabe, zili choncho kuti machimo awo adzaululidwa mwachiphamaso ndi mneneri wonyenga uyu ndipo adzaoneka ngati wolungama ndi wowongoka, ngakhale munthu wotumidwa ndi Mulungu kuti apulumutse Israyeli ndipo motero msampha uli wokonzeka kutchera. Ndipo komabe, chilichonse cha zochita zawo ndachilemba m'mabuku akumwamba, ndipo ineyo pandekha ndimakumbukira chilichonse ndipo ndikudziwa milandu yawo yonse ndipo ndidzawaulula ndipo adzalipira kwamuyaya chifukwa cha machimo awo onyansa.

Ndipo choncho chilombo chochokera kunyanja chikukonzekera kudzuka ngati Mahdi wawo ndipo chilombo chochokera pansi chikukonzekera kudzuka ngati mwanawankhosa wonyenga pakuti ndi mwanawankhosa uti amene ali ndi nyanga ziwiri ndipo amalankhula ngati chinjoka. Komabe Esau wakonzekera kumulandira kwa iwo okha m'dziko lomwe akufuna kuba ndipo adzazulidwa kotheratu ndi dzanja lamphamvu la Yeshua HaMashiach amene ndamupatsa mphamvu, ngakhale kugawana mpando wanga wachifumu ndi Mwana wanga wokondedwa ndipo adzaphwanya kotheratu mutu wa njoka ndipo adani ake onse adzayesedwa chopondapo mapazi ake chifukwa ndi omwe adagwa omwe anakana kugwada pa lamulo langa m'kupanduka kwawo ndipo ndi iwo omwe anaukira chilengedwe changa mwachinyengo ndi chidani ndipo adzalipira kwamuyaya.

Ndipo choncho ndikulumbira pa dzina langa kachiwiri kuti, Ine YHVH Wamkulu NDINE, ndikulumbira kuti Mesiya yekhayo amene ndinamutumiza kwa Israyeli ndipo ndidzamutumiza ndithu ndi mwana wanga wobadwa yekha, Yeshua HaMashiach amene Atate anu anamukana ndipo amene inu mukumukana chifukwa cha mabodza awo omwe munatengera kuchokera m'mitima yawo yowuma yomwe inaperekedwa mu Talmud yanu yamwano. Ndipo choncho ndikubwera kwa inu m'chikondi changa pa inu O' Israyeli kuti ndikuululireni mokwanira msampha womwe wakutchererani ndi Esau amene akufunabe chiwonongeko cha Yakobo.

Chilombo chochokera pansi chakulodzani ngati mneneri wanu wonyenga ndipo adzadzuka ndi kubwera kwa inu ngati mwanawankhosa wanu wansembe amene anadzipereka yekha chifukwa cha Israyeli ndipo adzadziyesa kukhala Moshiach wanu weniweni. Pamene kwenikweni waipitsidwa ndi Esau ndipo palibe Moshiach amene adzadzuka kuchokera kwa Dani. Zoonadi, akubwera kudzawononga Yakobo koma msampha womwe akutchera udzakola omwe akumutsatira mofunitsitsa ndipo choncho adzatengedwa kwamuyaya ndi mabodza ake ndipo adzalandira chizindikiro chake chomwe adzafuna kukakamiza dziko lapansi kuti alipange kapolo ndi kuliwononga.

Ndipo choncho ndikuti kwa inu, O' Israyeli, gwadani pomvera Mesiya wanu weniweni amene anabwera kwa inu ngati mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu, amene Atate anu anamukana chifukwa cha Esau. Gwadani kwa Mbuye wanu wa Ambuye ndi Mfumu ya Mafumu ndipo landirani chipulumutso changa kuti ndikuwomboleni ku temberero la chilamulo chifukwa palibe munthu wolungama amene amabwera kwa ine kudzera mwa Mose, kupatula munthu Khristu Yesu amene ndamusintha kwamuyaya, ndipo mwa thupi sadziwikanso. Chifukwa, ndamulemekeza mofanana ndi mmene Farao analemekezera Yosefe ndikugawana naye mpando wake wachifumu, kupatula kuti pampando wachifumu anali wamkulu kuposa Yosefe ndipo anasunga ulamuliro wake.

Chifukwa:

NDINE amene ali kuyambira muyaya mpaka muyaya ndipo palibe wamkulu kuposa NDINE Wamkulu!

NDINE amene ndatumiza Mawu awa kwa anthu anga okondedwa kuti ndibwere kwa inu ndi kuchotsa khungu lanu lina pamene mitima yanu itembenukira kwathunthu kwa Mesiya wanu! Ngakhale Mesiya wanu weniweni, Khristu Yesu Mbuye woukitsidwa ndi wolemekezedwa!

NDINE Mulungu Wamuyaya wa Chilungamo amene safoka, ndipo ndikufunsa, kodi pali chilichonse chovuta kuti dzanja langa lamphamvu lichikwaniritse?

Choncho ndikukupemphani, O' Israyeli, kuti mumvere mawu a YHVH ngakhale Elohim wanu amene anali mbusa wa Atate anu kudzera m'dzanja la mtumiki wanga Mose amene ndinamupulumutsa pamene anatulutsidwa m'madzi a nile kupita kuchitetezo ndi kukonzekera kwachifumu m'nyumba ya Farao yomwe anaigonjetsa pomvera YHVH!

NDINE, ngakhale YHVH Wamkulu NDINE, amene ndikulumbira pa dzina langa ndipo ndikutsimikizira mokwanira m'choonadi chamuyaya kuti Yeshua HaMashiach anakwaniritsa chilamulo cha Mose ngati Paskha wangwiro wa nthawi zonse amene ndinapereka mwangwiro ndi dzanja langa, choncho palibenso chifukwa cha nsembe.

NDINE YHVH, Wamkulu NDINE, ndipo ndikulumbira pa dzina langa kuti ndine amene ndinaukitsa Yesu wa ku Nazarete kwa akufa atadzipereka nsembe ngati mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu, ngakhale nsembe yomwe ndinaivomereza nthawi zonse ndi kwamuyaya chifukwa cha chiwombolo cha Adamu ngati munthu watsopano mwa Khristu Yesu amene ali Yeshua HaMashiach Mesiya wanu weniweni!

Ndipo NDINE amene ndikutsimikizira kudalirika kwa Mawu awa omwe ndawatumiza mu Uthenga Wabwino Wamuyaya womwe waululidwa ndi ine ndemwe, ndi Yeshua HaMashiach kwa mthenga wathu wamphamvu amene wakonzedwa ndi Yeshua HaMashiach m'chilango chambiri ndi chiyeretso chifukwa cha zolinga zanga zamuyaya ndipo mokhulupirika wabwerera kwa Yeshua HaMashiach ndipo choncho wabwezeretsedwa!

Ndipo choncho ndikukuitanani anthu anga, ngakhale Israyeli amene ndimamukonda ndi chikondi chamuyaya, kuti mulowe mu Israyeli wa Mulungu ndi kubwera kwa Yahweh, ngakhale Mulungu wa Abrahamu, ndi wa Isake, ndi wa Yakobo kudzera mwa Yeshua HaMashiach kuti musonkhanitsidwe kwamuyaya mu Israyeli wa Mulungu ndi kubwerera kwa ine kudzera mwa Mesiya wanu weniweni, ngakhale Yeshua HaMashiach. Ndipo ndidzakulandirani kwa ine ndekha ndi kukubwezeretsani ku cholowa chanu ndi mphoto yomwe ili m'dzanja lamphamvu la Khristu Yesu.

Palibe dzina lina loperekedwa limene anthu angayandikire nalo kwa YHVH, Wamkulu NDINE pakuti ndatero kulengeza, choncho mpsompsoneni mwanayo kuti angakwiye ndipo mungawonongeke panjira.

Taonani! Nthawi ndi tsopano ndipo kukolola kwa zipatso zoyamba kwafika ndipo adzasonkhanitsidwa kwa Mbuye wawo ndipo adzalemekezedwa chifukwa cha zolinga zanga zamuyaya.

NDINE amene NDINE ndipo NDINE YHVH choncho mverani mawu anga amuyaya ndipo bwerani kwa Atate wa Kuwala amene wakonza kale njira yanu.
