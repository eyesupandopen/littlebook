Mwadzidzidzi Chiyembekezo Chanu Chavumbulutsidwa!

Dziyikeni pamodzi ndikumva mawu a Ambuye wanu, ngakhale mawu a Yesu wa ku Nazarete pakuti ndine amene ndinadzipereka kamodzi, kotheratu, ndiponso kwamuyaya monga Mwanawankhosa wa Paskha wangwiro kwa Yehova chifukwa cha machimo anu, ngakhale Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya, monga mkulu wa ansembe wanu kwamuyaya monga mwa dongosolo la Melikizedeki kuti mukhale ndi chikhululukiro cha machimo. Bwerani kwa ine, ndipo bweretsani makutu anu ndi mitima yanu kuti mumvetsere ndi chikhulupiriro chonse ku mawu anga a chikhulupiriro! Inde ndikukupemphani kuti mumvetsere, osati kungomva phokoso la mawu anga koma kumvetsera ndi mitima yanu kuti muzindikire cholinga changa ndikupeza chitonthozo m'chikondi changa pa inu ndikuti mulandire ndi chifatso mawu omwe abzalidwa mwa inu, omwe amatha kupulumutsa miyoyo yanu.

Pakuti ndikulankhula kwa okhulupirika mu Mpingo wanga, ngakhale thupi langa, ngakhale mkwatibwi wanga ngakhale onse amene ndatsala pang'ono kuwasonkhanitsa kwa ine ndekha m'chitetezo cha kumwamba m'chipinda chachifumu cha Yehova. Pakuti ndine amene ndimasamalira miyoyo yanu ndipo ndine amene ndimasamalira kukula kwanu kosalekeza kuti mukule ngati chomera cholungama pamaso panga, pakuti ndine amene Yehova wandipereka monga mlimi ndi wosamalira munda wake wopatulika kwambiri wa miyoyo ya owomboledwa. Pakuti inu ndinu ngale yamtengo wapatali ndipo pakati pa chuma chambiri cha Yehova inu muli m'gulu la zamtengo wapatali zomwe palibe mwala womwe wasiyidwa wosatembenuzidwa mu chipulumutso chanu ndipo palibe khama lomwe lasiyidwa kuti likuduleni ndi kukuthandizani ndikukuperekani kwa Yehova, ngakhale Atate wanu wamphamvuyonse monga namwali woyera wopanda banga, kapena khwinya, kapena chinthu china chotero. Choncho musaope, kapena kulola kukayikira kulikonse ponena za Mawu anga ang'onoang'ono!

Ndipo chotero ndikukufunsani, okondedwa anga, n'chifukwa chiyani mitima yanu ikukuvutitsani? N'chifukwa chiyani mukupukusa mitu yanu ngati kuti mukunyamula chitonzo? Kodi mwalola asatana kulowa m'mitima yanu ndikunong'oneza mawu abodza achitonthozo omwe amangotulutsa kusakhulupirira pamene akufuna kung'amba ndi kung'amba zonse zoyera? Kodi mudzitengera nokha njoka yakale ija mu kuchenjera kwake konama nthawi zonse ndi chinyengo chake chomwe chikupita patsogolo nthawi zonse yomwe ingalowe m'makutu anu mawu onama omwe angagonjetse chikhulupiriro chanu, monga momwe zachitikira kwa ambiri omwe alola maganizo awo kuipitsidwa kuchoka pa kuyang'ana kwa mtima umodzi pa ine, Khristu Yesu Ambuye wanu?

Pakuti palibe wina amene adzakusamalirani, ndipo ena onse ndi onyenga omwe amanyenga ndipo palibe Yesu wina koma zotsanzira zotsika mtengo zokha ndi zachinyengo zopanda pake zopangidwa kuti zinyenge. Choncho ndikukupemphani kuti musanyalanyaze kuzindikira kumeneku komwe ndingakupatseni kuti mupitirize kuyima ngati chowunikira cha kuwala ngakhale m'mphindi zomaliza izi dziko lomwe mwakhala mukulidziwa lisanathe ndikusinthidwa kwamuyaya kuchoka ku kusapembedza kwake kwamakono kupita ku chiweruzo chamuyaya chomwe tsopano chidzagwa ngati chilango chamuyaya pa osapembedza omwe akufuna kutenga cholowa changa. Pakuti dziko lapansi ndi langa ndi kudzaza kwake, ndipo ndine Ambuye wamkulu wamuyaya wa Ambuye ndi Mfumu ya Mafumu, ngakhale Ambuye wanu wolungama ndipo ndine amene ndidzatenga cholowa changa m'manja mwa okhala mwachisawawa omwe alibe ufulu ndipo si mafumu ndipo si milungu ndipo si alendo ochokera kumayiko ena koma ogwa omwe amanyenga pakuti onse ndi onyenga omwe amanyenga. Ndipo masiku awo adzakhala mapeto amuyaya mu cholowa chawo chamuyaya m'malawi otentha kwambiri a buluu.

Pakuti zowawa zobereka sizikuyima tsopano, ndipo pali zizindikiro zambiri kumwamba ndi zizindikiro zambiri padziko lapansi zomwe zikuwonekera nthawi zonse ndipo sizikuyima. Ndipo izi zaperekedwa ngati chilengezo ndikuti mukhale ndi chiyembekezo, choncho musazinyalanyaze ndi kuziganizira m'njira ngati ya onyoza! Ntchito yamanja ya Yehova yoperekedwa ndi manja a angelo ake amphamvu, ndipo ndithudi yojambulidwa ndi chala cha Mulungu mwiniwake nthawi zina, ngakhale pa lamulo lake lamuyaya imalengeza zochitika zakumwamba zomwe zikuyandikira izi, pakuti tsiku lafika ndipo lili pa inu okondedwa anga, choncho musaope! Nthawi yakwana yoti okhulupirika anga asonkhane kuti akumane nane m'mitambo pamene ndikutsika kuchokera kumwamba ndi mawu a mngelo wamkulu ndi lipenga lamphamvu la Mulungu, choncho khulupirirani Mawu anga pakuti ndi inde ndi ameni!

Ndani ndi chiyani chomwe chilipo chomwe chiyenera kukuvutitsani ana anga? Kodi mukuwopa kuti mkwati sadzafika ndikugwira dzanja la mkwatibwi wake mu pangano lamuyaya monga mwa lonjezo lakale? Kodi Mawu a Mulungu asanduka opanda phindu? Mulungu Aletse! Monga kwalembedwa ndi kulengezedwa momwemonso kudzakhala kuti mwadzidzidzi ndidzakulandani ku mkwiyo kupita ku chitetezo cha kumwamba kumene ndapita kukakukonzerani malo, pakuti m'nyumba ya Atate wanga muli malo okhala ambiri. Ndipo chotero mudzakhala nane nthawi zonse ndipo chotero mudzakhala mafumu ndi ansembe pamaso pa Yehova Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya, pakuti chotero wakhazikitsa ndi kukonzekera m'miyoyo yanu, ndipo ndani angathe kuletsa zolinga ndi kukonzekera kwa Mulungu wamuyaya?

Ndikukuuzani choonadi, palibe chinthu ndipo palibe munthu amene angathe kulepheretsa ngakhale pang'ono zolinga za El Shaddai ndipo palibe amene angayime (ngakhale mu chitsutso chaching'ono) popanda kutonthozedwa mu mantha pa mphamvu yeniyeni, ulemerero ndi kunjenjemera komwe kumabwera pamene Yehova asuntha ngakhale pang'ono kuyima kuchokera ku mpando wake wachifumu wamuyaya ndipo kumwamba konse ndi dziko lapansi zimagwedezeka pa kukhalapo kwake kwamphamvu. Pakuti ngakhale Yehova wadzibisa yekha mu chifatso, chikondi, ndi chifundo kwa masiku ambiri tsopano (pakuti tsiku limodzi lili ngati zaka chikwi pamaso pa Mulungu wamuyaya) tsopano ndi nthawi yomwe Yehova wasankha pamene adzadziwulula yekha ku chilengedwe chake, pamene onse adzadziwa kuti kuli Mulungu kumwamba, ndipo adzamvetsa ndithu kuti monga momwe zilili kumwamba, momwemonso zidzakhala pansi ngakhale padziko lapansi pakuti Yehova ndi Mulungu wa kumwamba ndi dziko lapansi ndipo amalamulira kwambiri pa chilengedwe chake ndipo palibe wina woposa iye mu mphamvu, ulemerero ndi mphamvu. (Ndipo ogwawo agwidwa ndi mantha amuyaya) Choncho musaope Ana a Wam'mwambamwamba pakuti Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya, ngakhale Yehova mwiniwake ndiye amene amakumenyerani nkhondo ndipo ndiye amene adzapulumutsa, kuwombola, ndi kusintha miyoyo yanu kukhala ulemerero wamuyaya m'kuthwanima kwa diso.

Nanga n'chifukwa chiyani mukukhala osaleza mtima? Kodi mukumva ngati Ambuye wanu wamuyaya ndi mkwati wakukanani, mkwatibwi wanga? Kodi mpulumutsi wanu wamuyaya wasiya thupi lake, kulisiya lopanda mutu? Mulungu aletse kuti chinthu choterocho chiganiziridwe. Ndi chinthu chiti chomwe Yehova walengeza ku chilengedwe chake monga choyandikira chomwe sichinachitike pambuyo pake? Kodi Mawu a Nowa adagwa pa nthaka yowuma ngati malonjezo opanda pake pamene anali mlaliki wa chilungamo kwa zaka zana limodzi ndi makumi awiri m'dziko lomwe linalipo panthawiyo? Ayi! Koma mwadzidzidzi ndi chiwawa chachikulu madzi anatulutsidwa mu kamphindi ndipo anakwera pamwamba pa mapiri ataliatali padziko lapansi m'masiku omwe anatsatira.

Ndipo chotero ndikukulangizani kuti musaphenso m'gulu la omwe mofunitsitsa ali osadziwa. Iwo mofunitsitsa amasankha umbuli kunyalanyaza kuti ndi mawu a Mulungu kumwamba kunaliko kale, ndipo dziko lapansi loyima m'madzi ndi m'madzi: Kumene dziko lomwe linalipo panthawiyo, litasefukira ndi madzi, linawonongeka: Koma kumwamba ndi dziko lapansi, zomwe zilipo tsopano, ndi mawu omwewo zasungidwa, zosungidwira moto mpaka tsiku la chiweruzo ndi chiwonongeko cha anthu osapembedza. Ndipo chotero ndikulengezanso kuti tsiku la moto ndi chiweruzo ndi chiwonongeko cha anthu osapembedza lafika, monga momwe zinalonjezedwera kale, ndipo chotero zidzakhala mwadzidzidzi.

Ndipo chotero ndikukufunsaniso, ana anga ang'ono, Kodi Mulungu ndi munthu kuti aname? Kodi ine, Yesu Khristu wolungama, ndine wosadalirika kuti ndikuyiwale inu ndikukupatsani chiyembekezo chabodza? Kodi ndakuyiwalani ndipo m'malo mwake ndapita kukawedza? Ndikukufunsani okondedwa anga kodi ndinapereka dontho langa lomaliza la magazi mu zowawa pamtanda kuti ndikusiye inu pamene ndakulonjezani malonjezo aakulu ndi amtengo wapatali, ngakhale malonjezo operekedwa ndi Yehova mwiniwake mu chinsinsi chake chamuyaya, ngakhale chinsinsi chachikulu cha Mulungu?

Mulungu aletse kuti zinthu zoterezi ziganiziridwe ndipo chotero zindikirani maganizo oipa a Satana omwe amakuponyera inu, ndikuwakana ndi kuwadzudzula ndikusintha kuganiza kopanda pake ndi koipa koteroko ndi malemba oyera, ngakhale malonjezo a Yehova omwe ndi inde ndi ameni. Koma nthawi zonse ndipo makamaka nthawi ngati izi kwalembedwa ndipo chotero Paulo analembera Akorinto m'malo mwanga: Koma ndikuwopa kuti monga njoka inanyenga Hava kudzera mu kuchenjera kwake, momwemonso maganizo anu angaipitsidwe kuchoka ku kuwona mtima komwe kuli mwa Khristu. Ndipo chotero ndikukulangizani ana anga, ngakhale okondedwa anga kwambiri ndi olakalakidwa kuti mupitirize kuyang'ana kwanu kwa mtima umodzi pa ine, ndi mawu anga, pakuti ndine Mawu a Mulungu opangidwa thupi ndipo Mawu onse a Mulungu adzakwaniritsidwa ndithu, ndipo palibe ngakhale limodzi la Mawu anga ang'onoang'ono lomwe lidzalephera lomwe lalankhulidwa ndi kuuziridwa ndi Mzimu Woyera wamkulu wamuyaya, yemwe ndi Yehova, Mulungu wamkulu wamuyaya!

Ndipo chotero ndikukulangizani ana anga, ngakhale okondedwa anga kwambiri ndi olakalakidwa, ngakhale inu chimwemwe changa ndi korona wanga: pumulani mwa ine ndipo pumulani mu ntchito zanga ndikusiya zanu. Pakuti kwalembedwa: Chotero kwatsala mpumulo kwa anthu a Mulungu. Pakuti iye amene walowa mu mpumulo wake, iyenso wasiya ntchito zake, monga Mulungu anachita pa zake. Gwirani ntchito chotero kuti mulowe mu mpumulo wanga, kuti wina asagwe pambuyo pa chitsanzo chomwecho cha kusakhulupirira. Ana anga gwirani ntchito kuti musiye ntchito zanu ndikuchita ntchito zomwe ndikukupatsani kuti mukwaniritse ndipo musadalire kumvetsa kwanu koma m'njira zanu zonse ndivomerezeni ine, Khristu Yesu Ambuye wanu wokwera kumwamba, ndipo mudzapeza mpumulo pakati pa mapiri obiriwira a msipu ndi chitetezo, makamaka pansi pa chisamaliro ndi chitetezo cha mkono wanga wamphamvu, pakuti ndine Yeshua HaMashiach yemwe alinso mbusa wanu wamkulu yemwe watsala pang'ono kuwonekera!

Ndipo chotero ndikukufunsani, okondedwa anga, kodi mudzabwerera kukakhala pakati pa onyoza opusa omwe amanyoza ndikufuna kukuyesani m'njira zawo zopanda chikhulupiriro? Kodi mudzanyalanyaza mawu anga ndi ngakhale zizindikiro zambiri, zozizwitsa ndi zodabwitsa zomwe zakuzungulirani? Kodi mukuganiza mopusa kuti mutha kupitiriza kukhala ngati omwe sadziwa za tsoka ndi chiweruzo chomwe chikubwera? Kodi mudzabwerera kukakhala pakati pa oweruzidwa oyenda, omwe amakana kulapa ndikusankha m'malo mwake chiweruzo chaukali ndi chilango? Ndikukuuzani momveka bwino ngati mutayesa mudzadzipeza nokha ngati opusa omwe amapitiriza kupitirira machenjezo anga ndikulangidwa mosayenera ndipo chotero mwachenjezedwa.

Mvetsetsani kuti dziko lomwe mukukhala tsopano silofanana ndi momwe linaliri ngakhale milungu ingapo yapitayo, ndipo likupitiriza kusintha pa liwiro lomwe likuwonjezeka nthawi zonse. Mvetsetsani kuti palibe ngakhale mmodzi wa inu amene adakhalapo m'dziko lomwe lilipo tsopano, pakuti dziko lomwe mukukhala tsopano lamangidwa mu chiweruzo ndipo komabe likuloledwa kupereka zilengezo zaulemerero za ukwati womwe ukubwera kumwamba kumene ine Khristu Yesu ndidzakwatira kwamuyaya mkwatibwi wanga wokhulupirika ndipo chotero adzakhala nane nthawi zonse. Ndipo pamene mkwatibwi wanga ndi Mpingo wanga akulandidwa mwadzidzidzi, ndiye chiwonongeko chadzidzidzi chidzagwa pa onyoza onse ndi anthu oipa omwe anakusekani monyoza nati: Mawa kudzakhala kofanana ndi lero, ndipo lero ndi kofanana ndi dzulo, ndipo sindidzawona tsoka ndipo sindine wamasiye koma ndine mfumukazi yolamulira pa zinthu zonse! Kupatulapo mwadzidzidzi chiwonongeko chawo chidzabwera pa iwo ngati mkazi wam'zowawa, ndipo sadzapulumuka.

Choncho tulukani pakati pawo ndipo mukhale olekanitsidwa mu zinthu ZONSE ndi m'njira zanu zonse. Simungakhale ndi gawo kapena mwayi ndi iwo kapena mudzagawana nawo miliri yawo. Mvetsetsani kuti zochitika zayamba kale, ngakhale zochitika zomwe zidzakhudza ochimwa, koma ndidzapereka chitetezo kwa okhulupirika anga kuti ziweruzo izi sizidzakuvulazani ana anga. Pakuti zowawa zobereka zosalekeza izi cholinga chake ndi kudzutsa omaliza a omwe akugona ndikulekanitsa omaliza a omwe adzakwera m'Chombo tsoka lisanagwe mwadzidzidzi ndipo nthawi yatha. Simungathenso kukhala moyo wosasamala ndipo muyenera kuyenda mu nzeru yayikulu choncho mverani machenjezo anga ndikumvetsetsa kuti ndakupatsani nzeru yayikulu ndi kumvetsetsa kuti muthe kuzindikira nthawi yomwe mukukhala, ngakhale pakati pa omwe ali m'tulo tofa nato ku tsoka lomwe likuyandikira ndipo omwe adzavutika chifukwa chake.

Choncho mvetsetsani kuti nthawi yomwe yatsala ilibe phindu ndipo imangotumikira zolinga za Yehova chiweruzo chisanagwe mwadzidzidzi. Inde, mdani wapatsidwa mwayi wokonzekera kuukira kwake pa Babulo Wachinsinsi pakuti chotero wapatsidwa kwa iye ndi Yehova kuti akwaniritse. Koma mvetsetsani bwino kuti Satana ndi mdani wogonjetsedwa yemwe wamangidwa padziko lapansi kuti aweruzidwe ndipo amangololedwa kupititsa patsogolo zolinga za Yehova ndipo adzakhala chete ndikudikirira kuti woletsa achotsedwe ndipo sangathe kuchita chilichonse kuposa momwe waloledwa, choncho musaope kubangula kwa mkango wopanda mano umenewo pakuti kalonga wamng'ono wamangidwa mu kugonjetsedwa! Pakuti mwa choonadi, ogwawo amakuwopani ndipo amamvetsetsa ndikudziwa kuti omwe adawazunza kale adzakhala omwe adzabweretse chiweruzo ndi chilango cholungama m'zifuwa zawo kwamuyaya ndi kosatha. Chiyani? Kodi simukudziwa kuti mudzaweruza angelo?

Ndipo chotero ndikukufunsani okondedwa anga, kodi tsopano mudzakana chisomo changa ndi chifundo chomwe chaperekedwa kwa otayika, ngakhale pamene chiweruzo ndi tsoka zakonzedwa kuti zigwe mwadzidzidzi? Kodi mwayiwala zaka zambiri za chisomo ndi chifundo zomwe ndinakupatsani mosayenera kuti muthe kukwaniritsa machimo anu musanatembenukire ku chilungamo? Kodi mudzakana omwe akufuna kukwera m'Chombo kuti tikathe kugwirizana mphindi imodzi m'mbuyomo? Ndikukhulupirira zabwino za inu, ana anga, ndipo chotero ndikupempha kuleza mtima kwanu ndi kupirira kwanu, ngakhale tsoka lisanagwe mwadzidzidzi.

Ngati wina aliyense mwa inu akuganiza kuti ali ndi chifukwa chobweretsa chilungamo pa Satana ndi ufumu wake, ine ndina chifukwa chochuluka kuposa chomwe chinalowapo m'mitima yanu. Pakuti ndani mwa inu amene wamenyana ndi Satana, ngakhale mpaka dontho lanu lomaliza la magazi pamene munapachikidwa mofunitsitsa pamtanda waukaliwo mu zowawa zenizeni. Pakuti ndinanyamula mtanda wanga mofunitsitsa ndikudzipereka ndekha mu chigonjetso chamuyaya pamene ndinalakwiridwa m'manja mwa ochita zoipa, pamene ndinatenga malo anu mu chiweruzo mu zowawa zazikulu kunja kwa zipata pakati pa ochimwa oyipitsitsa. Magazi anga anali osalakwa ndipo palibe choyipa chomwe chikanatha kuikidwa pa akaunti yanga moyenera. Komabe m'chikondi changa pa Atate wanga ndi chikondi changa pa inu, ndinapereka moyo wanga mofunitsitsa ndipo ndikadachitanso mu kamphindi, ngakhale chinthu choterocho sichikufunika. Choncho ngakhale m'masautso anu apano tsatirani chitsanzo changa cha kupirira podziwa kuti mwadzidzidzi mudzalowa mu chipulumutso chamuyaya!

Ndipo chotero ndikukupemphani kuti mupitirize kukhala okhazikika mpaka mapeto, ngakhale mapeto pamene mwadzidzidzi mumatengedwa m'kuthwanima kwa diso kupita kumwamba mu mphoto yamuyaya ndi cholinga chamuyaya, ndipo komabe akufa mwa Khristu adzauka poyamba ndipo mudzawalowa mwadzidzidzi mu chikondwerero chamuyaya. Pakuti zolinga zamuyaya zomwe mwayitanidwa, kusankhidwa, kusindikizidwa, ndi kusungidwa zidzakwaniritsa zolinga zamuyaya za Mulungu wam'mwambamwamba ndipo zonse zatsala pang'ono kuvumbulutsidwa mwa inu ana anga, choncho walani kwa ine ngati magetsi anga pakali pano mu kupuma komaliza uku tsoka ladzidzidzi lisanagwe padziko lapansi. Ndipo ndidzawabweretsa kwa inu kuti muwabweretse kwa ine, pakuti mudakali akazembe anga mpaka mwadzidzidzi mumatengedwa kupita kumwamba. Ndipo mwadzidzidzi tsoka lalikulu lidzagwa.

Mvetsetsani kuti nthawi yatha ndipo palibe chomwe chingabwezere m'mbuyo zochitika zomwe zikubwera ndipo zikudziyika zokha motsutsana ndi okhala padziko lapansi, pakuti chiweruzo chomwe chikubwera cha ochimwa ndi chachikulu. Ndipo chotero ndikukupemphani ndi kukukakamizani ndi chifundo chomwecho, chisomo ndi chifundo chomwe Yehova wakusonyezani pamaso pa Yesu Khristu chifukwa cha chipulumutso chanu. Kodi mungathe kukhala okhulupirika mpaka mapeto? Kodi mudzilola nokha kulowa mu mpumulo wanga wamuyaya popanda kusiya pa mphindi yomaliza? Kodi mudzayang'ana mtima wanu wonse ndi chidwi chanu pa mawu anga ndikumvetsetsa kuti mwadzidzidzi zidzasintha kwamuyaya ndipo mwadzidzidzi ndi tsopano.

Palibe nthawi yotsala yoti Babulo Wachinsinsi alape ndipo palibe nthawi yochulukirapo mu m'badwo wanga wa chisomo ndi chifundo pakuti nthawi yatha. Ndipo ku nyengo iliyonse kuli nthawi ndi cholinga ndipo mu kukwaniritsidwa kwa nthawi chiwombolo chadzidzidzi chidzagwa pa owomboledwa choncho aloleni owomboledwa a dziko lapansi anene chotero omwe wawaombola m'manja mwa mdani. Ndipo musalole mbala kuba chimwemwe chanu pakuti ndi mkwatibwi uti amene amalira tsiku la ukwati wake, ngakhale tsiku la ukwati waulemerero, woyera lomwe palibe amene angawononge kapena kuvutitsa.

Choncho tengerani chitonthozo m'Mawu anga achitonthozo ndipo siyani kukhala ngati omwe aponyedwa pansi mu manyazi ndi liwongo. Pakuti inu mkwatibwi wanga, ndinu omwe ndakupangani kukhala opanda banga ndi opanda cholakwa pamaso pa mpando wachifumu weniweni wa Yehova, ndipo ndinu zipatso zoyamba kuchokera kwa akufa. Ngakhale kuti omwe mwa inu omwe ali amoyo ndi otsala sadzalawa imfa, munabadwa akufa mu zolakwa ndi machimo. Ndipo mudzakhala mutadutsa mwachindunji kuchokera ku imfa (yomwe mu kufa mukadafa), molunjika ku moyo wamuyaya womwe mukukhala kale malipiro oyamba, ngakhale chisindikizo chachikulu cha Yehova chomwe mwasindikizidwa nacho pa tsiku ladzidzidzi la chiwombolo. Pakuti mwabadwanso ndi mbewu yosawonongeka ndipo ndinu ana a Mulungu wam'mwambamwamba, ngakhale ana a Mulungu Wamphamvuyonse!

Chifukwa chake, tengerani chitonthozo ndipo mukhale pa mpumulo ndikugwira ntchito ndi ine mu kuzungulira kwa chigonjetso kwa mibadwo. Pakuti sipanakhalepo nthawi padziko lapansi ngati iyi ndipo sipadzakhalanso nthawi ngati iyi. Ngakhale nthawi yomwe ana amuyaya a Mulungu akudikirira mozindikira mpweya wawo woyamba wa moyo wamuyaya ngati makanda a muyaya mu kukhalapo kwamuyaya kwa Mulungu wamuyaya kumene adzakwatira Ambuye wawo wamuyaya mu pangano lamuyaya, ndipo...mwadzidzidzi, zidzakhala chotero!

Ndine amene ndinadzipereka kamodzi, kotheratu, ndiponso kwamuyaya monga Mwanawankhosa wa Paskha wangwiro.

Ndatsala pang'ono kusonkhanitsa okhulupirika kwa ine ndekha m'chitetezo cha kumwamba m'chipinda chachifumu cha Yehova.

Ndine amene ndimasamalira miyoyo yanu ndipo ndine amene ndimasamalira kukula kwanu kosalekeza kuti mukule ngati chomera cholungama.

Ndine amene Yehova wandipereka monga mlimi ndi wosamalira munda wake wopatulika kwambiri wa miyoyo ya owomboledwa.

Ndine Ambuye wamkulu wamuyaya wa Ambuye ndi Mfumu ya Mafumu, ngakhale Ambuye wanu wolungama.

Ndine amene ndidzatenga cholowa changa m'manja mwa okhala mwachisawawa omwe alibe ufulu.

Ndine Mawu a Mulungu opangidwa thupi ndipo Mawu onse a Mulungu adzakwaniritsidwa ndithu.

Ndine Yeshua HaMashiach yemwe alinso mbusa wanu wamkulu yemwe watsala pang'ono kuwonekera!

Ine, Yeshua HaMashiach ndatumiza Mawu awa kudzera mwa mtumiki wanga, ndipo kudzera mwa mtumiki wanga Mawu awa atumizidwa!

Ndipo chotero ndikukulimbikitsani okondedwa anga kuti musafooke ndikukhala okhazikika mpaka mapeto adzidzidzi omwe adzathetsa kwamuyaya nthawi yanu padziko lapansi mu mkhalidwe wanu wamakono. Mwadzidzidzi, ndipo m'kuthwanima kwa diso mudzadzipeza nokha mutayima m'chipinda chachifumu cha Yehova mu chimwemwe chamuyaya ndipo mudzamvetsetsa ndi kudziwa bwino monga momwe mukumvetsetsedwera ndi kudziwika bwino. Ndipo chotero lonjezo lidzakumana ndi kukwaniritsidwa mu chimwemwe chamuyaya ndi ulemerero wamuyaya womwe ukukudikirirani ndipo zonse zidzakwaniritsidwa mwa inu m'kuthwanima kwa diso, ndipo mwadzidzidzi zidzakhala chotero. Khalani mwa ine ana anga pakuti ndimakukondani kwambiri ndipo sindichita manyazi kukutchani abale anga.
