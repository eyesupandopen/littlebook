O Isiraeli, NDINE Ndikukuitanani!

Kuchokera kwa Yahweh, Mulungu Wamkulu Wamuyaya:

Imvani Mawu a Yahweh, NDINE wamkulu ndipo taganizirani funso langa. Chifukwa chiyani makoswe amathawa m'nyumba yomwe ikugwa, ana anga? Yankho lodziwikiratu ndiloti apulumuke, kupatula makoswe omwe amathawa m'Babulo Wachinsinsi amatero kuti abise milandu yawo, ndipo kuti athe kumulanda katundu wake kwathunthu kuti akhale ndi ulamuliro waufupi womwe ndimawapatsa. Popeza munthu wamba amakonda mdima, tsopano ndimupatsa mdima womwe amawukonda kwambiri. Komabe, ndalemba (ndipo ndidzapitiriza kulemba) chinthu chilichonse chochitidwa ndi cholengedwa chilichonse m'chilengedwe changa chomwe chili ndi ufulu wosankha chifukwa kwa amene wapatsidwa zambiri, zambiri zimafunidwa.

Ndipo palibe chobisika pamaso panga, ndipo palibe chobisika m'maso mwanga, ndipo NDINE amene ndimaona, kulemba, ndi kukumbukira chinthu CHILICHONSE choipa chomwe chinachitikapo m'chilengedwe changa. Ndipo palibe chomwe chingachitike chomwe sindikuchidziwa ndi kuchilola! Pamaso panga, maganizo onse ali poyera. Pamaso panga cholinga chilichonse cha mtima chili poyera ndipo palibe chinthu kapena munthu amene analengedwa kapena kupangidwa yemwe sali pansi pa dzanja langa lamphamvu, chifukwa ndine Mulungu Wamuyaya wachilungamo amene mukuchita naye, O Isiraeli ndipo ndikukuonani kwathunthu, ngakhale mumasankha kundimvera m'khungu lina chifukwa cha kuuma kwa mitima yanu.

Chifukwa, pamene ndinabwera kwa inu mwa Mesiya yekhayo amene ndinatumizapo ndipo Mesiya yekhayo amene ndidzatumizapo, ndinakanidwa kwathunthu ndi Makolo anu. Chifukwa, NDINE amene ndinalenga Yeshua HaMashiach m'thupi kuti apulumutse onse amene aitanira pa dzina lake lamphamvu, yemwe inu mukumukana mosazindikira ngakhale modzikuza ndi mwamwano kumutcha fano la amitundu. O Isiraeli, ambiri a inu mwadziphatikiza nokha ku Sunagoge la Satana ndipo tsopano mukuyembekezera mwachidwi Moshiach wanu wonyenga yemwe Esau wakonzera kuphedwa kwa Yakobo.

Choncho imvani mawu a NDINE WAMKULU chifukwa mawu anga amphamvu ngati bingu, amuyaya akukuitanani mu nthawi yotsiriza ino ndipo ndikutumiza Mwana wanga kwa inu kachiwiri kuti awombole Yakobo ndipo otsala omwe amalandira Mwana wanga Wamuyaya, Yeshua HaMashiach, adzapulumutsidwa ngakhale ndi dzanja lake lamphamvu ndipo palibe chisankho china cha chipulumutso. Kapena sipadzakhala chisankho china kapena dzina loperekedwa la chipulumutso chifukwa ndamupatsa dzina loposa dzina lililonse, kuti pa dzina la Yesu wa ku Nazarete bondo lililonse lipinde. Ndipo kwa omwe akukana, adzapangidwa kugwadira Mkango wa Fuko la Yuda yemwe ndikumutumiza padziko lapansi kuti aweruze ndi kuchita nkhondo ngakhale m'chilungamo chamuyaya, ndipo ndiye amene adzabweretsa chilengedwe changa chonse pansi pa ugonjero wolungama ndipo adzalamulira ndi ndodo yachitsulo ndipo chiweruzo, chisomo, ndi chifundo zili naye kwamuyaya. Zindikirani kuti njira ya chiwonongeko ndi yotakata ndipo mayina ambiri ndi milungu yonyenga ikufuna kukutengerani ku cholowa chawo cha nyanja yamoto yoyaka nthawi zonse, yomwe imayaka ndi sulufule malawi otentha kwambiri a chilango.

O Isiraeli, mwaswa bwanji mtima wa Yahweh mukusamvera kwanu ndipo mtima wanga ukuswekabe chifukwa cha inu kuyambira muyaya wapitayo komwe ndimadziwa m'kudziwiratu kwanga kuti mu dongosolo langa la chiwombolo Makolo anu adzakana Yesu wa ku Nazarete m'malo mwa Baraba, yemwe anali wakupha ndi mtsogoleri wa zigawenga m'maiko panthawiyo. Ndipo choncho ndinamupangitsa kutembenukira kwa amitundu, ndipo anamumvera kwa nyengo, komabe khungu lina linachitikira Isiraeli kuti otsala a Isiraeli athe kubwera ku chipulumutso pa nthawi yoyenera. Zindikirani kuti ambiri ochokera ku Yudeya anamupanga kukhala Ambuye wawo ndipo anapeza chipulumutso chamuyaya mu Isiraeli wa Mulungu ndipo adzakhala ndi mwana wanga kwamuyaya. Ndipo NDINE amene ndatumiza Yesu wa ku Nazarete pakati pa mitundu kuti awombole abale anu omwe anabalalitsidwa kunja chifukwa cha kupanduka kwawo, ndipo ngakhale omwe analibe chiyembekezo ndi ambiri ochokera ku mitundu apeza chipulumutso chamuyaya ndipo tsopano alumikizidwa kwamuyaya m'mafuko. Choncho zindikirani O Isiraeli kuti zomwe NDINE Wamuyaya wayeretsa musazitche wamba kapena zodetsedwa!

Koma inu O Isiraeli, munakana ndi kupha mwana wanga Yesu Khristu ndikumupereka m'manja mwa anthu ochimwa omwe anali otsimikiza kumumasula mu kusalakwa kwake. Koma kodi dongosolo langa la chipulumutso chamuyaya likanakwaniritsidwa bwanji kupatula ngati zofunikira za malamulo amuyaya ndi osasinthika a Yahweh zakwaniritsidwa, malamulo omwe ndinakhazikitsa kuti dongosolo lolungama la chilengedwe changa chonse lilamuliridwe m'chilungamo changwiro. Ndipo choncho Yesu wa ku Nazarete anadzipereka yekha ngati Mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu ngati Mkulu wa Ansembe wanu wamuyaya kwamuyaya motsatira ndondomeko ya Melikizedeki kuti alipire chilango chomwe chinaperekedwa ndi Mose chifukwa cha machimo ndi zolakwa za anthu ndi kuwombola anthu kwathunthu ku themberero la chilamulo. Chifukwa Mose anabwera ngati mphunzitsi wa Isiraeli kuti atenge ndi kusunga Isiraeli kwa Yeshua HaMashiach ndipo walipira kwathunthu chiwombolo chanu, ndipo ngakhale muli ophunzira opanduka otsala adzamvera chilango changa ngakhale vuto lomwe likubwera kwa inu mu sabata la makumi asanu ndi awiri la Danieli lomwe lili pakhomo panu ndipo posachedwa lidzatsegula zitseko zanu ku chisankho chamuyaya.

Choncho zindikirani yemwe mwamukana, O Isiraeli, yemwe ndimamukonda ndi chikondi chamuyaya, chifukwa ndinu omwe ndinalonjeza Makolo anu m'mapangano ambiri m'nthawi zakale kuti m'nthawi zino za chiweruzo ndidzakusungani chifukwa cha iwo, ndipo ndine Mulungu Wamuyaya Wachilungamo yemweyo amene sasinthika! Ndipo NDINE YEMWE ndimakumbukira mapembedzero ambiri opangidwa ndi Makolo anu m'malo mwanu ndipo pembedzero lililonse ndimalikumbukira liwu ndi liwu ndipo ndidzalemekeza pempho lawo kwa inu omwe muli ofunitsitsa ndi omvera. Ndipo ngakhale tsopano Mwana wanga, yemwe mukumuda mosazindikira, akukupembedzerani m'chikondi chake chamuyaya kwa m'bale wake chifukwa Yeshua HaMashiach ndi m'womboli wa Isiraeli, ngakhale iye amene wagula dziko lapansi ndi magazi ake owombola ndipo wakuwombolani m'manja mwa mdani ngati mutasankha kuvomereza. Ngakhale, ambiri mwa inu mwalandira chikondi chanu cha Igupto kuchokera kwa Makolo anu, musadzilole kugwa kwamuyaya m'chipululu cha opanda chikhulupiriro.

Chifukwa Yeshua HaMashiach ndi amene adzapulumutse otsala a Yakobo omwe adzabwera kwa iye kwathunthu, ngakhale m'mayesero a sabata la makumi asanu ndi awiri la Danieli. Choncho zindikirani nthawi yomwe mukukhala ndikudzuka ku khungu lanu lina pa lamulo langa pamene mutembenukira kwa Yeshua HaMashiach, yemwe ndamutumiza kuti akupulumutseni ngati Mesiya wanu (pamene simudzachititsidwanso khungu ndi kuuma kwa mitima yanu). Chifukwa mudzatembenukira kwa mwana wachilungamo ndipo adzakulandirani kwa iye mwini, ndipo adzakutengerani ku chitetezo ndi kukubisani ku chiwonongeko chanu m'manja mwa Moshiach wonyenga wa Esau.

Choncho zindikirani amene mukuchita naye chifukwa NDINE Mulungu wa mapangano ambiri, ndipo NDINE Mulungu wa malonjezo ambiri, ndipo NDINE Mulungu Wamuyaya Wachilungamo amene akubweranso kudzaitana anthu anga kuti abwerere kwa mbusa wawo wamkulu yemwe ndamutumiza kuti apulumutse Adamu. Ndipo pali mbali zambiri za yemwe ndine, ndipo dzina lililonse lili ndi tanthauzo lake lokhudza Mulungu Wamuyaya. Choncho ndimvereni ndipo ndimvereni bwino ndikumvetsa Yahweh, yemwe akukuitanani kwa ine mwini kuti muthe kupeza chipulumutso. Ndipo choncho ndikukukumbutsani ndi kukuphunzitsani ngati makanda, pamene kwenikweni ndinu omwe muyenera kukhala aphunzitsi, O Isiraeli! Choncho phunzirani za Mulungu wanu Wamuyaya Wachilungamo!

Ndipo ndili ndi mayina ndi makhalidwe ena ambiri, choncho imvani mawu anga omwe amabangula ngati mabingu kumwamba, O Isiraeli, chifukwa NDINE Mulungu wa Makolo anu yemwe mumamupembedza monyenga m'kuuma mtima.

NDINE Mulungu wa Abulahamu yemwe mwamusiya!

NDINE Mulungu wa Yakobo ngakhale yemwe mwamusiya kuti mupite m'manja mwa wina, ndipo mwathamangira ku milungu yanu yonyenga ngakhale kunyadira kupanduka kwanu. Zindikirani Isiraeli kuti muli m'manja mwa Sunagoge la Satana ngakhale omwe amati ndi a Isiraeli omwe amanama ndipo sali.

Imvani mawu anga O' Israyeli, ndipo bwerani kwa Yeshua HaMashiach mu nthawi yotsiriza iyi chifukwa ndikukuitanani m'chikondi changa pa inu ndipo ndikupereka chisomo ndi chifundo changa kwa inu mu nthawi ino. Koma ndinu mtundu wouma khosi ndipo kuuma khosi ndi kuuma mtima kwa Makolo anu mwakupanga kukhala kwanu, ndipo mwamukana Mulungu wa Makolo anu amene mumamupembedza m'njira yosaphunzira ndi yonama chifukwa anthu anga akuwonongeka chifukwa chosowa chidziwitso, choncho bwerani kwa Yeshua HaMashiach ndipo muphunzire za iye kuti muthe kupeza chipulumutso m'dzanja lake lamphamvu.

O' Israyeli, mtima wanga ukusweka chifukwa cha inu chifukwa ndinakutengani ngati chomera chofewa m'chipululu chouma chokonzeka kuwonongeka ndi kufota mpaka kalekale ndikukupatsani moyo ndikukupangani kukhala anthu apadera kwa ine ndekha. Ndinalowa m'mapangano ambiri ndi Makolo anu, ngakhale mapangano omwe sanathe kuwasunga chifukwa cha tchimo ndi katangale zomwe zili m'magazi a Adamu. Koma NDINE Yehova Mulungu Wanu amene amakumbukira ndipo wasunga malonjezo ndi mapangano anga ndipo ndidzakwaniritsa lonjezo lililonse ndi pangano lililonse lomwe linapangidwa kwa anthu ngakhale pamaso pa Yeshua HaMashiach, amene ndi mwana wanga wokondedwa Yesu Khristu.

O' Israyeli, kodi mukhala opanda chikhulupiriro ndi osamvera? Kodi mudzaphatikizana ndi adani anu mpaka kalekale m'chilango chawo chamuyaya? Ndipo zili choncho kuti mwakhala osamvera ndipo mwayenda m'khungu lina, ndipo ndakuchotserani mtundu wanu ndipo simukutsogoleredwanso ndi Yahweh Mulungu Wamkulu Wamuyaya. Koma m'kupanduka kwanu mukutsogoleredwa ndi dzanja la adani anu omwe amanamizira ndi kudzibisa kuti ndi Yakobo pamene m'choonadi ndi Esau. Mvetsetsani kuti onse amene akana Yeshua HaMashiach atembenuka kuchoka kwa Yahweh kupita kwa milungu yonyenga ndi kusakhulupirira ndipo ambiri asokonezedwa ndi kugwidwa ukapolo ku makyubu awo akuda omwe amawapembedza mosazindikira m'kusakhulupirira. Choncho mvetsetsani, onse amene asocheretsedwa ndi Moshiach wawo wonyenga kapena Mahdi wawo wonyenga m'zipembedzo zawo zonyenga zopanda chikhulupiriro zomwe sizitsogolera kwa Yahweh kapena moyo wamuyaya, adzapeza chiwonongeko chamuyaya kwa onse amene asankha kukhala mmenemo.

O' Israyeli, bwerani kwa ine kudzera mwa mwana wanga Yeshua HaMashiach amene alinso Yesu wa ku Nazarete, amene mumamukana monga momwe anachitira Atate anu m'mbuyomu. Koma musawonongeke m'machimo a Atate anu ndipo landirani chikondi changa ndi nsembe yomwe ndinapereka m'malo mwanu, ngakhale nsembe yomwe Adamu yense wadalitsidwa nayo ndipo ambiri ochokera kumayiko ndi mitundu abwera kwa Ambuye wawo m'kulapa ndipo adalitsidwa ndi kuwomboledwa mpaka kalekale. Ndipo mvetsetsani kuti NDINE amene waika zinthu zonse mwa Khristu Yesu woukitsidwa wamphamvu, ndi manja osinthidwa chifukwa NDINE amene wamupanga iye kukhala mzimu wopatsa moyo ndipo ulamuliro wake ndi ufumu wake sizidzatha!

Choncho, mapangano anga onse kuyambira Adamu mpaka Mose, ndipo ngakhale Chipangano cha Mose tsopano chakhazikitsidwa kuti chikwaniritsidwe pamaso pa Yesu Khristu ndi Pangano langa Latsopano lomwe ndi Chipangano changa Chatsopano chomwe chaperekedwa kuti chikwaniritse zinthu zonse ngakhale kuyambira Adamu mpaka Khristu. Chifukwa awo amene apeza moyo wamuyaya ayenera kudzigonjera ndi kugwada kwa mwana wanga wobadwa yekha, amene ndimakondwera naye nthawi zonse. Choncho bwerani kwa ine pamaso pa Yeshua HaMashiach m'magazi ake opanda tchimo omwe anavomereza Pangano lake Latsopano, lomwe ndi Chipangano Chatsopano, chomwe ndachivomereza kwathunthu ndikuchikonzekera maziko a dziko lapansi asanaikidwe. Ndipo ndidzatumiza 144,000 anga, motsogoleredwa ndi Yeshua HaMashiach, chifukwa cha chipulumutso chanu ndipo adzakubisani pamalo omwe ndakukonzerani ndipo kumeneko mudzasamaliridwa ndi kupulumutsidwa amoyo kwa Esau amene akukonzekera chiwonongeko chanu.

Koma Esau, amene akupondereza anthu anga, akuthawa Babulo Wachinsinsi chifukwa akufuna kubisa machimo awo. Tchimo lomwe makoswe othawa nyumba yogwa alibe mlandu. Choncho, adzayesa kubisa milandu yawo ngakhale mafosholo awo ochimwa omwe anagwiritsa ntchito kubisa milandu yawo ndipo ndidzawawulula, atero Yehova wa Makamu. Pakuti afunkha kwathunthu Babulo Wachinsinsi monga momwe ndalolera kuti zichitike m'gawo langa lowirikiza la chiweruzo pa hule lalikululo! Ndipo choncho adzisonkhanitsira okha mphamvu zonse zankhondo za Babulo Wachinsinsi ndipo adzapitiriza kuwononga mpaka Babulo Wachinsinsi adzawonongedwa posachedwa mu ola limodzi ndipo sadzaukanso. Mvetsetsani kuti palibe chida chomwe chapangidwa kapena kuumbidwa motsutsana ndi inu chomwe chidzapambana ngati mupitiriza kuitana pa Mesiya wanu weniweni, ngakhale Yeshua HaMashiach!

Choncho mvetsetsani kuti Sunagoge wa Satana ali ndi mawonekedwe a umulungu m'mawonekedwe awo akunja, koma ndi a Atate wawo mdierekezi ndipo zilakolako za Atate wawo adzazichita chifukwa Esau anaipitsa mbewu yake mpaka kalekale. Namsongole woyipawa, ngakhale atsogoleri anu omwe amawoneka ngati olungama ndi namsongole woyipa ndipo ambiri ndi ogwiririra ana omwe amagwirira achinyamata ndipo asochera m'machimo a Sodomu ndi Gomora, ndipo amagwirira ana, ndipo si abusa abwino koma a mphere. Ndipo chinjoka chomwe ndi Atate wawo ndi amene akuwatsogolera, ngakhale yemweyo amene wapereka abusa awiri a mphere kuti alamulire ndi kuweta dziko lapansi m'chinyengo. Ngakhale chilombo chochokera kunyanja chomwe chidzauka ngati Mahdi wawo ndi chilombo chochokera pansi, chomwe chilinso Moshiach wawo wonyenga ndipo chidzabwera kudzalamulira dziko ndipo ndithudi adzalamulira dziko lapansi m'chinyengo.

Ndipo banga la mphere zawo limadetsa umunthu wawo wonse, ndipo chipulumutso sangachipeze. Chifukwa onse amene amadziyika okha m'njira yotereyi kapena omwe anabadwa kwa ogwa alibe chipulumutso chifukwa anapangidwa m'chifanizo cha chinjoka ndipo chifanizo chimenecho ali nacho monsemo. Chomwe chili chokhotakhota sichingawongoledwe, ndipo chonyansa chilichonse cha ogwa chingangochita zoyipa chifukwa anapangidwa kuti abweretse chiwonongeko cha anthu, ndipo akanapambana ndikadapanda kuwaletsa m'masiku a Nowa.

Ndipo komabe adzauka kachiwiri, chifukwa ndikubweretsa zimphona kukwaniritsa mkwiyo wanga! Ndipo Sunagoge wa Satana akugwira ntchito kubisa zizindikiro zonse za omwe ali kwenikweni tsoka lisanagwe ndipo chinyengo chawo chimafika pachimake mu tsoka chifukwa ogwa akuloledwa kuwononga Babulo Wachinsinsi. Koma tchimo lililonse lidzaperekedwa kwathunthu m'zifuwa zawo ndipo choncho, n'zosatheka kubisala kwa Yahweh ndi mwana wanga Yeshua HaMashiach ndipo mkwiyo wathu udzawapeza ngakhale ayesa kudzibisa mkatikati mwa dziko lapansi. Palibe omwe ali pansi pa chiweruzo chachikulu omwe angathawe nyanja yamoto, kupatula omwe alembedwa m'buku la moyo la mwanawankhosa ndipo palibe omwe angathawe machimo awo kupatula omwe chipulumutso chikufunidwa ngakhale kwa omwe amavomereza pangano langa lamtendere. Ngakhale atawononga mitundu ingati ndipo ngakhale atadzibisa patali bwanji m'dziko lapansi, sadzathawa kubwezera ndi mkwiyo womwe adziunjikira okha ndipo ndidzawabwezera mokwanira atero Mulungu Wamuyaya.

Chifukwa ndidzasintha dziko lapansi kuchoka pamakonzedwe ake apano kupita ku ulemerero wake woyambirira, ndipo owomboledwa a dziko lapansi adzayenda kumeneko mwa chitetezo. Ndipo chomwe chili, chidzasinthidwa kukhala chomwe chidzakhala, ndipo sadzathawa, ngakhale onse omwe asiyidwa ku chiweruzo komwe masoka ndi matsoka ambiri adzachitika, ndipo madzi oundana adzasungunuka, ndipo mitundu idzasintha, ndipo dziko lapansi lidzabwezeretsedwa ku ulemerero wake wakale. Ulemerero womwe ogwa anausintha kukhala chivundi kuti ugwirizane ndi zilakolako zawo.

Ndipo Ine ndine Yahweh, Mulungu Wamkulu Wamuyaya, ngakhale iye amene analenga kumwamba ndi dziko lapansi, ngakhale iye amene ali kuyambira muyaya mpaka muyaya, ndipo mwa chifuniro changa ndi uphungu wanga ndinalankhula kuti zinthu zomwe zili ngakhale kuchokera ku zomwe zinalibe. Ndipo choncho imvani mawu anga, O' Israyeli, ndi ngakhale inu nonse omwe mukufuna kunyalanyaza Mawu anga m'kuuma mtima. Chiweruzo chatsala pang'ono kubwera kwathunthu pa dziko lapansi ndipo ndidzawulula Esau yense pa omwe ali kwenikweni, ndipo ndidzawulula kwathunthu Atate wawo omwe zilakolako zawo amamangidwa nazo mpaka kalekale.

Taonani! Awo a Esau amadzinenera chilungamo chawo, poganiza mosazindikira kuti ndi anthu anga osankhidwa, ndipo Moshiach wawo wonyenga, ngakhale nyanga yaing'ono iyo, adzanena kuti iye, mwiniwake, ndi Mulungu! (Palibe wina ngati Yahweh, Mulungu Wamkulu Wamuyaya ndi Wamphamvuyonse!) Ndipo ogwa sangathe kudzipulumutsa okha m'manja mwanga amphamvu zonse, ndipo Yeshua HaMashiach adzaphwanya kwathunthu mutu wa Satana monga momwe ndinalonjezera Adamu. Palibe kuya m'dziko lapansi komwe angadzibise, ngakhale ayesa, ndipo tsopano amangidwa ku dziko lapansi ngakhale pansi pa thambo lomwe sangathe kuthawa. Palibe mayina ena omwe angatenge komwe sindidzawaona pa omwe ali kwenikweni. Palibe mawonekedwe omwe angatenge komwe sindiwazindikira ndi kuwaona kwathunthu, mpaka m'mitima yawo yoipitsidwa. Ndipo aliyense womaliza wa ogwa kuyambira wamng'ono mpaka wamkulu ndi onse omwe amatsatira njira zawo zoyipa adzakhala ndi cholowa chawo chamuyaya m'malawi a chilango m'nyanja yamoto yoyaka nthawi zonse komwe nyongolotsi yawo sifa.

Choncho tengani maliro, O' Israyeli, kwa inu omwe mukukana mwamakani kuitana kwanga ku moyo wamuyaya, ngakhale onse omwe adzasankha kutsatira ogwa ndi onse omwe adzadziphatikiza okha ku chinjoka ndi onse omwe ali a Adamu omwe anali ndi mwayi wosankha chipulumutso koma m'malo mwake anasankha machimo awo omwe adzawatsogolera ku imfa yachiwiri ngati sayitana pa dzina la Yeshua HaMashiach kuti apulumutsidwe.

Choncho ndifotokoza zosankha zomwe mwatsala nazo. Mungavomereze Mesiya wanu weniweni, Yeshua HaMashiach amene ndamutumiza, ndipo mudzapulumutsidwa ku mphoto yamuyaya ndi ulemerero, zomwe mayesero anu ndi chizunzo chanu sizoyenera kuyerekezera. Kapena ngati wina aliyense apitiriza kapena kuphatikizana ndi chinjoka mu dongosolo lawo latsopano, mudzakhala mutakana moyo wamuyaya ndipo m'malo mwake mwalandira kwathunthu chiwonongeko chanu chifukwa cha machimo a thupi, ndipo mudzanyamula moyenera chiweruzo chanu pamene munapemphedwa kuthawa mwa kumvera, komwe mudzakhala mutakana.

Aliyense ndi onse amene atenga chizindikiro cha chilombo, adzakhala atasintha mpaka kalekale chifanizo cha Yahweh chomwe analengedwa nacho, kuwasiya mu mkhalidwe wosawomboleka. Choncho, ndikukupemphani kuti musankhe moyo kuti mukhale ndi moyo muyaya m'chikondi ndi madalitso a mlengi wanu omwe awonetsedwa kwathunthu pamaso pa Yesu Khristu, ngakhale Yeshua HaMashiach! Kupanda kutero, mudzaphatikizana ndi ogwa m'cholowa chawo cha sulufule woyaka m'nyanja yamoto yamuyaya, komwe chilango ndi kubwezera machimo ndiwo muyaya wokha womwe mudzaudziwa.

Mvetsetsani, kuti cholinga changa si kukhala wankhanza kapena wopanda chilungamo ku chilengedwe changa, koma malamulo anga a chilungamo sasintha ndipo sangasinthidwe. Ndipo choncho chilengedwe chikuusa moyo ndi kulira kwa ine mwamphamvu pansi pa kulemera kwa machimo a ogwa ndi machimo a anthu, ndipo nthawi yoyeretsa ndi kutsuka dziko lapansi m'chiweruzo kuchokera ku zoyipa yafika; ngakhale mbewu za kupanduka zidzatsala koma ufumu wa adani sudzaukanso. Ngakhale Satana adzayesa kuukira kwake komaliza mu nyengo yake yaing'ono asanaponyedwe mpaka kalekale kumoto chifukwa iye si mfumu, komabe mpando wake wachifumu udzakhala malawi osazimitsidwa a chiyero chotentha kwambiri, ndipo sadzakhala ndi chifundo.

Pakuti malamulo anga amuyaya amanena chiyani kwa osamvera, choncho kumbukirani kuti malipiro a tchimo ndi imfa, ndipo choncho Adamu anachimwa ndikuthetsa moyo wake mu malipiro a tchimo, omwe ndi imfa. Koma nsembe zopsereza ndi nsembe zakale sizinali zokwanira kuwombola kwathunthu mwana wanga wosakaza, ndipo choncho munthu wangwiro wopanda tchimo ndiye nsembe yokhayo yovomerezeka chifukwa Adamu anali wangwiro ndi wopanda tchimo pamene anachimwa.
