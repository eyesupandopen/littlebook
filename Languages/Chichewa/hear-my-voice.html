Mu Ntchito za Yohane Mbatizi
Marichi 14, 2024

Imvani Mawu Anga!

Mawu a Ambuye anadza pa ine. Imvani mawu anga, tiana tanga, ndipo bwerani m'manja mwanga otambasulidwa. Dziwani kuti chikondi changa pa inu nchosasweka ndipo sichingagonjetsedwe, pakuti ndi chikondi cha Atate wanga popeza wapereka chikhalidwe chake kwa ana ake, ndipo mwa ine chidzalo chonse chimakhala. Ndakuitanani maina ndipo ndakuitanani kuti muime pamaso panga. Nkhosa zanga zimamva mawu anga, monga zimadziwa mawu a mbusa wawo. Kodi simukumva mawu anga? Kodi mwatseka makutu anu ku mawu anga? Ndimalira mokweza m'misewu ndi m'njira zazing'ono; ndayendera ngakhale ndende zanu ndikubweretsa chipulumutso kwa ozunzidwa ndi oiwalidwa.

O, munthu, bwanji ukusiya Ambuye wako? Bwanji wapatuka m'njira zanga? Koma, udzati, Sindinapatuke m'njira zanu; ndine amene Ambuye wamuitana. Zoonadi, ndinaitana, ndipo unayankha kwa nyengo, nyengo ya unyamata ndi chisangalalo chachikulu. Ena a inu munatengedwa kupita kumene mimbulu imakhala, ndipo ambiri a inu mwasocheretsedwa kudzera m'mawu abwino, zolankhula zokoma, ndi malilime onama omwe posachedwa adzayimitsidwa.

Kumbukirani chikondi chanu choyamba, ndipo lapani; kuulula machimo anu kwa ine, popeza ndine wokonzeka nthawi zonse kuyeretsa machimo anu m'magazi anga otetezera.

Imvani mawu anga, ana anga, popeza mawu ambiri amamveka mofanana ndipo akhristu onyenga ambiri amayesa kutengera mawu anga. Zoonadi, ndimasamalira ndi kuweta mpingo wanga ndi dzanja langa, kuti ndikuperekeni kwa Mulungu oyera ngati namwali ndi ovala bafuta woyera bwino, ndi madontho a machimo anu okhululukidwa, ochotsedwa, ndi oiwalidwa, monga ndawaponya m'kuya kwakukulu, kuti asadzukenso. Koma, ambiri a inu mwasokonezedwa ndipo ngakhale kukopeka ndi dziko loipali. Aukira ndi ufiti wawo kudzera m'kulumidwa kwa njoka, koma mvetsetsani kuti ngakhale njoka ikulume, palibe chovulaza chidzafika kwa iwe, pakuti chidindo cha Atate wanga sichingasweke. Ikani chidwi chanu pa ine, mwana wake wobadwa yekha, amene anamupereka mofunitsitsa kuti atetezere machimo anu kuti tigwirizanenso, osasiyananso. Ndiloleni ndikuchiritseni; ndiloleni ndikuchiritseni mpaka pomaliza ndikuchotsa zipsera zanu kuti mukhale athanzi ndi amoyo pamaso panga. Tsatirani njira zanga ndikutsatira Mawu anga, pakuti:

Ndine Mawu osanduka thupi amene amakhala pakati panu.

Ndine mkate wamoyo, ndipo aliyense amene adya sadzamvanso njala.

Ndine amene ufumu wake suli wa dziko lino, ndipo mphoto yanga ili ndi ine kwa okhulupirika ndi osankhidwa anga.

Ndine amene ndimayenda pakati pa zoyikapo nyale zisanu ndi ziwiri ndikulamulira angelo asanu ndi awiri a Mpingo.

Ndine woyera wa Mulungu.

Ndine mkhalapakati wanu.

Ndine amene ndimayesa mitima ya anthu.

Ndine wokonzeka nthawi zonse kuyeretsa machimo anu m'magazi anga otetezera.

Ndine woweruza wolungama.

Ndine Khristu weniweni woloseredwa kuyambira pachiyambi.

Ndine mwana wa Mulungu wobwera m'thupi.

Ndine amene ndinapereka magazi anga opanda tchimo chifukwa cha ochimwa, kuti alandire moyo wosatha.

Ndine mwana wa Mulungu wobwera m'thupi.

Ndine mbusa wabwino.

Ndine amene ndinabwera kwa anga ndipo ndinakanidwa ndi anga.

Ndine Khristu weniweni, wosankhidwa ndi Atate wanga kuyambira pamaziko a dziko lapansi.

Ndine amene Ndine.

Ana anga, ndikukuuzani zoonadi kuti ndatambasula dzanja langa ku dziko lomwe likufa kwa ma Yubile ambiri tsopano, ndipo dzanja langa silidzakhala lotambasulidwa nthawi zonse mu chifundo ndi chisomo, ngakhale chifundo ndi chisomo zili ndi ine nthawi zonse. Ndaitana ambiri, ndipo ambiri akana. Komabe, omwe ndawaitana ndi kuwasankha ndi kuwayeretsa ndi kuwasambitsa ndi kuwachiritsa, omwewo adzaima pamaso pa mpando wanga woweruza ndikutenga mphoto yayikulu chifukwa cha kukhulupirika kwawo.

Ngakhale ndili ndi ntchito zambiri zoti mukwaniritse, ndipo ngakhale mwapanga zambiri chifukwa cha ine ndi dzina langa, ndikufuna mtima wanu wonse kuposa ntchito zanu. Ndikufuna kuti mutembenukire kwa ine ndikusiya machimo anu. Bwerani oyera kwa ine, monga Ambuye wanu ali wokonzeka ndi dzanja langa lochiritsa lotambasulidwa kuti ndikuyeretseni ngati chipale chofewa chatsopano. Ngakhale manja anga anabooledwa, magazi anga otetezera anakhetsedwa kuchokera kwa iwo ndi thupi langa mpaka dontho lomaliza. Ndinapereka zonse zanga kuti mukhale ndi moyo pamaso pa Atate wathu mpaka kalekale.

Zoonadi, sindinatetezere tchimo limodzi lokha; zoonadi, sindinatetezere machimo ochepa okha; zoonadi, sindinatetezere machimo ambiri, koma ndinatetezera machimo onse nthawi zonse ngati mungandiyankhe pamene ndikutsutsa mitima yanu kudzera mwa mzimu wanga wamphamvu. Kodi simukumvetsa kuti sindikufuna kukutsutsani? Pali wina amene ali ndi chikhumbo chimenecho ndipo amalakalaka chiwonongeko chanu ndikukuimbani mlandu usana ndi usiku pamaso pa Atate wanga, ngakhale ndine mkhalapakati wanu. Iye waponyedwa pansi ndikugonjetsedwa; koma, bwanji mukukweza mbala pamalo apamwamba m'mitima yanu pamene ndili ndi chigonjetso chokwanira pa iye ndi ufumu wake womwe posachedwa udzatha? Khalani chete ndipo khalani pamtendere ndipo bwerani kwa ine ndi mtima wodzichepetsa. Kodi simukudziwa kuti ngati mungadziweruze nokha, simungaweruzidwe? Ndipempheni kuti ndikuwonetseni moyo wanu, popeza ndidzakuwululirani zinthu zambiri kuti muulule zolakwa zanu ndikuchiritsidwa.

Musaope, tiana tanga, popeza sindine amene ndikukutsutsani. Mverani chikumbumtima chanu ndipo musanyalanyaze mawu anga. Dziko lino latsala pang'ono kuponyedwa pansi, ndipo pamene dziko lino likusinthidwa kwamuyaya, mudzakwezedwa kumwamba, kuti ndikuperekeni kwa Atate, kuti muzindikire cholowa chanu ndikulandira mphoto zanu za kukhulupirika. Ndakhala ndikulakalaka tsiku lomwe tidzaonana kumwamba kuti pomaliza muwululiridwe moyo wanu weniweni pamene mwapulumuka moyo wakanthawi uwu wa zoipa, machimo, ndi kuyipa. Ndine amene ndimayesa mitima ya anthu, osati kuti ndikutsutseni, koma kuti kudzera m'moto wa woyenga muyeretsedwe ku njira zanu zakale ndikuzindikira njira zanga za chikondi, kuwala, chisangalalo, mtendere, kuleza mtima, ndi kufatsa.

Imvani kudzudzula kwanga ndikulandira chilango changa chifukwa Atate wanu amachita nanu ngati ana, ndipo ndi mwana uti amene Atate amamukonda samadzudzulidwa ndi kulangidwa. Chilango changa chimabala zipatso zamtendere za chilungamo, popeza chilango changa ndikukuyeretsani kwathunthu ku machimo anu ngati mungamve mawu anga ndikutsatira mawu anga. Pitani, imani, ndikulankhula mawu onse a moyo uwu. Ena adzabwerabe ku chipulumutso mu ola lomalizali, ndipo mu dzina la Yesu Khristu lokha, dzina langa lamphamvu momwe muli chipulumutso, pakuti palibe dzina lina lomwe chipulumutso chimaperekedwa. Dzina langa lamphamvu limaima lolimba, likukuyembekezerani kuti muwabweretse kwa ine, kuti ndipereke olapa kwa Atate ngati khanda lobadwa kumene loperekedwa kumene ku moyo wosatha. Ndili ndi chikondi chachikulu pa inu, ndipo monga Atate wandipatsa, ndidzapereka kwa okhulupirika ndi osankhidwa anga.

Ndine mwana wa Mulungu wobwera m'thupi, wopanda tchimo ndi woyera mpaka kalekale. Momwemonso, ndine amene ndinapereka magazi anga opanda tchimo chifukwa cha ochimwa kuti alandire moyo wosatha. Kodi inu, okhulupirika ndi osankhidwa anga, simuli m'gulu la olandira ulemerero wosatha? Bwanji mukuimabe chete ngati mukuyembekezera mawu oyenera. Lankhulani! Tsegulani pakamwa panu ndikulankhula, popeza ndidzatsogolera zolankhula zanu kuti mutumikire ku zowawa zomwe oitanidwa anga akuvutikabe nazo, ndipo ambiri adzayankhabe. Lowani nane mu ntchito yanga mu ola lomalizali ndisanagwedezeke maziko a dziko lapansi ndikuling'amba m'malo ambiri ndipo chiweruzo chachikulu chikuphimba dziko lapansi. Ndidzabwezeretsa dziko lino kwa inu mu Ufumu wanga womwe ukubwera, koma choyamba ufumu wa oipa udzawonongedwa ndikutengedwa mu chiweruzo kuti umangidwe m'phompho, ndipo akudziwa kuti nyanja yotentha yoyera komwe adzakhala mpaka kalekale ikuwayembekezera.

Ndidzawononga kotheratu mipando yawo ya mphamvu; ndidzawononga kotheratu maulamuliro awo onse; ndidzawatentha kotheratu pansi ndi moto womwe sungazimitsidwe, monga ngakhale ine, Ambuye wokhulupirika, ndakuuzani zisanachitike. M'nyumba ya Atate wanga muli malo okhala ambiri, ndipo ukulu ndi ulemerero waukulu ukukuyembekezerani. Choncho, musalole wina aliyense kukunyengeni mphoto yanu ndipo khalani okhulupirika kwa ine. Ikani chidwi chanu pa ine, Ambuye wamuyaya, ngakhale Ambuye wa ulemerero. Ndine mbusa wabwino; ndine Khristu weniweni wosankhidwa ndi Atate wanga kuyambira pamaziko a dziko lapansi. Ndine amene ndinabwera kwa anga ndipo ndinakanidwa ndi anga, ngakhale kudzera m'mayesero akulu Yakobo adzapulumutsidwa ndi dzanja langa lamphamvu. Kukwaniritsidwa kwa zinthu zonse kuli pafupi, ndipo mapeto a kalonga wa dziko lino ali pafupi. Ngakhale adzamasulidwa kwa nyengo yochepa, chiweruzo chake chosatha chikutsatira, chomwe sangapulumutsidwe ndipo sipadzakhala mankhwala. Bwererani kwa ine, lankhulani nane, nditumikireni, popeza Atate wanga wandipatsa zinthu zonse, ndipo mphoto yanga kwa inu yasungidwa kumwamba kwa okhulupirika ndi osankhidwa anga ndipo ndi yotsimikizika ngati chidindo cha Atate wanga chomwe ndakudindani nacho. Kwatsala mphindi zochepa chabe za nthawi ya chisomo, ndipo chiweruzo changa chidzagwa mwadzidzidzi pamene mkwiyo wa Mwanawankhosa ukubwera pa dziko lapansi. Konzani mitima yanu ndikudzikonzera nokha, pakuti nthawi ili pafupi.
