Mboni Ziwiri ndi Kutsekedwa kwa Nthawi Yochepa kwa Umboni

Kuchokera kwa Yahweh, WAMKULU NDINE:

Taonani! Ndabwera kudzakuuzani za mboni zanga ziwiri, zoyikapo nyali zanga ziwiri ndi mitengo yanga ya azitona iwiri ndikupatsa maso akhungu povumbulutsa zinsinsi zomwe zabisidwa kudziko lapansi mu nzeru zanga, ngakhale kuti mudziwe chitsimikizo cha zinthu zomwe zikubwera ndikuti njira ya chipulumutso iwonekere kwa onse omwe amawerenga ndikukhala ndi chikhulupiriro chomvera Mawu awa a Uthenga Wabwino Wosatha.

Taonani! NDINE Yahweh, ngakhale iye amene analenga kumwamba ndi dziko lapansi chifukwa cha zolinga zanga zamuyaya ndi ulemerero, ndipo NDINE amene adzabwezeretse chilengedwe kuchokera ku chivundi, ngakhale kuti njira zolungama za Yahweh zatsala pang'ono kudzaza dziko lapansi m'chilungamo pamaso pa Yesu Khristu. Ndipo motero njira zanga zidzadzaza dziko lapansi mu Ufumu wa Zaka Chikwi wa Khristu Yesu, ngakhale Ufumu womwe palibe chinthu choipa chomwe chidzalowemo, ngakhale Ufumu womwe dziko lapansi limalamulidwa m'chilungamo, ndipo mtendere weniweni ndi kubwezeretsedwa kudzadzaza dziko lapansi, ndipo okhulupirika a mibadwo yonse adzayenda mmenemo mu mtendere wangwiro ndi bata chifukwa njira za Yahweh zidzaphimba dziko lapansi m'chilungamo. Ngakhale nthawi yomwe Satana amangidwa mu mazunzo ndi mantha aakulu ndi zoopsa za cholowa chake chomwe chikubwera cha chilango chamuyaya, komwe adzawona chilombo ndi mneneri wonyenga akuponyedwa m'malawi a chilango chamuyaya ndikuwotchedwa, chifukwa onse ndi oyenera kutero, komabe adzakwaniritsa zolinga zanga zamuyaya za chilungamo asanamangidwe ndikuponyedwa kumoto kwamuyaya kuti asachokemo, ndipo ndidzayika chidindo ndipo sadzakumbukiridwanso.

Ndipo ndidzapereka (ndikusangalala) ndi mpumulo wa tsiku la Sabata ndi opambana anga olungama ndi onse omwe aloledwa kulowa mu Ufumu wa Zaka Chikwi wa Khristu Yesu! (Chifukwa zaka chikwi zili ngati tsiku limodzi pamaso panga ndipo tsiku limodzi ngati zaka chikwi ndipo zaka chikwi ndi mileniyamu.) Ngakhale onse omwe adzasonkhanitsidwe ndikuukitsidwa kuchokera ku mibadwo yonse, yapano ndi yakale, mwa Khristu Yesu; ngakhale Satana asanaponyedwe m'malawi amoto, nyengo yake yochepa yomaliza isanachitike. Pamapeto pake njoka yakaleyo idzamangidwa kwamuyaya ndikuponyedwa kumoto, kuti isachokepo pa chilango chake chosatha m'malawi otentha kwambiri a sulufule woyaka. Chifukwa Satana ndi onse omwe amatsatira mabodza ake alibe mphamvu pamaso panga amuyaya! Ndani angagonjetse Yahweh, WAMKULU NDINE, ngakhale amanama ndikudzitamandira? Kapena ndani angasinthe chifuniro changa chamuyaya ndi zilengezo za ziweruzo zomwe zakhazikitsidwa kuti zigwe kwathunthu pamene choletsa changa chichotsedwa mwadzidzidzi tsopano pakulira kwa lipenga lomwe laperekedwa kuti lilire pa nthawi yeniyeni ndi yangwiro yomwe ndasankha mu dongosolo langa lamuyaya kuchokera ku nthawi zamuyaya zakale. Ngakhale mphindi mu nthawi yomwe yatsala pang'ono kuchitika komwe lonjezo lolungama limakumana ndi kukwaniritsidwa kwamuyaya mu nthawi za tsopano.

Chifukwa NDINE, WAMKULU NDINE, ngakhale iye amene amadziwa mapeto kuchokera pachiyambi ndi chiyambi kuchokera kumapeto. Chifukwa chake, ndikubwera kudzavumbulutsa mbali za dongosolo langa lamuyaya la chipulumutso lomwe lakhala chinsinsi, ngakhale zinthu zomwe zikadali zobisika mu Chinsinsi cha Mulungu chomwe chatsala pang'ono kutha mu chilungamo chamuyaya. Monga momwe zinalembedwera ndi Yohane Wovumbulutsa pamene analemba mokhulupirika Mawu omwe anapatsidwa kwa iye ndi Khristu Yesu! Ndipo motero kwalembedwa kuti m'masiku a liwu la Mngelo wachisanu ndi chiwiri, pamene adzayamba kuomba lipenga, ngakhale chinsinsi cha Mulungu chidzatha, monga momwe adalengezera kwa atumiki ake Aneneri.

Komabe, ambiri achititsidwa khungu ku choonadi cha Mawu awa ndi afiti abodza a nthawi omwe analipidwa ndi chishango chofiira kuti asokoneze zolemba zanga zoyera ndi mabodza a nthawi ndi a Ziyoni ndi mabodza a maphunziro a zaumulungu olowa m'malo, ngakhale mabodza operekedwa mwachinyengo ndi ogwa kudzera mwa anthu oipa kuti anyenge mitima ya ofatsa ndi odzichepetsa. Ndipo adzanyenga osankhidwa anga enieni ngati osankhidwa anga asankha kunyalanyaza Mawu anga a chilungamo modzikuza poganiza kuti sanganyengedwe chifukwa cha kusankhidwa kwawo. Komabe onse omwe amalakwitsa mwanjira imeneyi pokana choonadi mokomera mabodza awo adzanyengedwa. Chifukwa chake, mvetsetsani anthu anga kuti lipenga lachisanu ndi chiwiri si lipenga lomwelo lomwe laperekedwa kuti lilire kuti lithetse nthawi zanga za chisomo ndikusonkhanitsa okhulupirika mwa Khristu, choncho musanyengedwe!

Komabe, choonadi cha Yahweh chimaonekera motsimikizika kwamuyaya ndipo sichingasinthidwe, ndipo Mawu anga sangasweke, ndipo Mawu anga adzakwaniritsa zomwe ndikufuna kwa aliyense amene Mawu anga atumizidwa. Palibenso aliyense amene angaletse mapulani anga amuyaya! Palibenso aliyense amene angaletse Khristu Yesu, mwa amene dongosolo langa lamuyaya la chipulumutso limakhazikika kwathunthu. Ngakhale iye amene ndamupatsa mphamvu kuposa cholengedwa chilichonse m'chilengedwe changa ndikumupatsa ulamuliro wathunthu pa chilengedwe changa mpaka adani ake atapangidwa chopondapo mapazi ake, panthawi yomwe adzapereka Ufumu wogonjetsedwa kwa Adonai Yahweh.

Komabe mwa choonadi chamuyaya, palibe mapeto a ulamuliro wolungama wa Yeshua HaMashiach, amene adzalamulira m'chilungamo mpaka muyaya, ngakhale ndi mkwatibwi wake amene adzapitiriza naye kwamuyaya ndi mpaka muyaya m'maudindo awo a ulamuliro ndi madongosolo a utumiki. Ndipo izi chifukwa adalandira Yesu wa ku Nazarete monga Ambuye wawo wamuyaya mu nthawi za chisomo ndi chifundo, ndipo adamukonda kwathunthu pomvera Mawu ake, ngakhale ndi Mawu omwe adzakhala atagonjetsa kwathunthu zinthu zonse za ufumu wa adani kwamuyaya.

Chifukwa cha choonadi chamuyaya, chofunda cha Melikizedeki chimakhala cholimba pamapewa amuyaya a Yeshua HaMashiach, kuti chisachokepo! Chifukwa kwamuyaya ndi mpaka muyaya Mfumu ya Chilungamo idzalamulira kwamuyaya ndi gulu lake la Ansembe, Mafumu, ndi Aneneri omwe adzadzaza dziko lapansi ndi njira zolungama ndi chidziwitso cha Yahweh, ngakhale mu nthawi zomwe maulosi adzatha kwamuyaya chifukwa ndidzapanga malo anga okhala ndi anthu kumwamba kwatsopano ndi dziko lapansi latsopano lomwe latsala pang'ono kulengedwa m'chilungamo ndi chiyero chenicheni! Ngakhale chilengedwe chomwe chidziwitso cha chabwino ndi choipa chidzatha kwamuyaya ndipo chilungamo chokha ndi chikondi ndi mtendere zidzapezeka kwamuyaya kuti zisachokepo kapena kuchepetsedwa kapena kuipitsidwa ngakhale pang'ono. Ngakhale zili choncho chifukwa chivundi chidzaponya m'malawi amoto kwamuyaya.

Komabe, zili choncho kuti ogwa aphimba ndikufalitsa mabodza awo pa chilengedwe changa ngakhale kuwawulutsa mosalekeza pa matekinoloje awo a gehena, ngakhale kuti munthu wachibadwidwe athe kulamuliridwa ndi kusinthidwa ndi mafunde awo ndi matekinoloje a gehena. Ngakhale zithunzi zolamulira malingaliro ndi kusintha makhalidwe, nkhani zoipa, ndi matsenga omwe amawulutsidwa pa magalasi awo akuda komwe ambiri amakhala mu mdima atatengeka ndi zithunzi ndi mabodza awo. Ndipo mwa choonadi, ogwa asangalatsa makamu a anthu ndi ziphe ndi mankhwala (pharmakeia) omwe amaperekedwa ngati mankhwala, ngakhale kuti apha ndi kuchepetsedwa nawo. Komabe, ziphe zawo ndi matekinoloje ndi magalasi adzawonongedwa kwamuyaya, ndipo mawayilesi awo adzatha kwamuyaya, omwe amaponyera matsenga awo ndikudzutsa zilakolako ndi zikhumbo za munthu wachibadwidwe mu tchimo lalikulu ndipo matekinoloje awo opanda phindu adzakhala opanda pake, kuti asakhaleponso.

Komabe, zili choncho, kuti ndinalola ogwa kuti aphimbe chilengedwe changa m'mabodza awo, ngakhale kuti onse omwe angatsatire njira zawo zonyansa asankhe kukhalabe mu cholowa chawo chamuyaya cha sulufule woyaka chifukwa adasangalala ndi chosalungama pochita tchimo motsutsana ndi Yahweh, WAMKULU NDINE, ndi motsutsana ndi osalakwa m'chilengedwe changa, omwe awalakwira. Ndipo mu nzeru zanga zamuyaya ndi chikondi pa chilengedwe changa, ndalola aliyense kuyambira Adamu kusankha amene adzamutumikire: kaya tchimo lofikitsa ku imfa, kapena kumvera chilungamo kufikitsa ku moyo wosatha.

Zotero ndi njira za Yahweh, chifukwa njira zanga ndi njira za chilungamo, mtendere, ndi chikondi! Ngakhale chikondi chamuyaya ndi chosatha, komwe chilengedwe changa chonse, chopangidwa ndi gulu la zolengedwa zosiyanasiyana ndi zopangidwa modabwitsa zamitundu yonse ndi zolinga, zidzakhala mu mgwirizano wolungama ndi chikondi. Chifukwa chilengedwe changa chakonzedwa ngati nsalu yolumikizana ya moyo yolamulidwa m'chilungamo ndi chikondi kuti chipindule kwathunthu ndi ulemerero wa chilengedwe changa chilichonse, komwe palibe cholengedwa chimodzi chomwe chidzapindule pakuwononga ndi kutaya china, koma onse adzagawana momasuka za zochuluka zosatha zomwe zili m'nkhokwe zanga zomwe zafunidwa kuti zipindule ndi ulemerero wa omwe amamvera malamulo anga olungama kuchokera m'mitima yawo m'chikondi kwa Atate wawo wamuyaya, omwe mwa choonadi, NDINE!

Komabe, zili choncho, kuti NDINE WAMKULU NDINE ndipo sindivomereza kapena kuvomerezana ndi mabodza awo, ndipo sindidzalola mabodza awo kupitiriza. Zili choncho, kuti ndikulengeza, kuti Ine, Yahweh, WAMKULU NDINE amene ali Mulungu Wosatha wa Chilungamo ndidzavumbulutsa kwathunthu mbali iliyonse ya ufumu wa adani yomwe yakhazikitsidwa kuti idzuke kwa nyengo yochepa kwambiri yomwe chinjoka chapatsidwa ulamuliro, ndipo chidzakhala ndi ulamuliro wochepa kwambiri. Ndipo chimodzimodzi, ndidzavumbulutsa ogwa omwe adzibisa okha pakati pa chilengedwe changa, omwe akuyenera kuzulidwa ndi kuvumbulutsidwa mu manyazi awo amuyaya kwathunthu asanaponyedwe m'malawi a chilango kwamuyaya, komwe mphutsi yawo sifa. Ndipo motero chilengezo changa chidzapita, chifukwa chomwe chalengezedwa mu ulemerero ndi mphamvu ya mpando wachifumu wa Yahweh, ngakhale WAMKULU NDINE, sichingasinthidwe kapena kusinthidwa, ndipo chidzachitika ndendende monga momwe chalengezedwera. Selah!

Komabe, zili choncho, kuti sindinabise ogwa kwathunthu, ndipo ndayamba kale kuwavumbulutsa kwathunthu ndipo chophimba chikuchepa. Ngakhale chophimba chobisika chomwe ndawabisa nacho kuti asawonedwe ndi anthu, ndipo choletsa chidzachotsedwa chomwe chimapangidwa ndi mfundo zosiyanasiyana zoletsa zomwe zidzachotsedwa kwathunthu pamene Mikayeli apatsidwa lamulo pamene lipenga lomaliza lilira mwadzidzidzi tsopano. Ndipo motero madzi oundana adzagwa, ndipo dziko lapansi lidzasinthidwa, ndipo adzakhala ndi nkhondo yawo yayikulu ndipo ambiri adzawonongeka mmenemo, ngakhale monga momwe zinalembedwera ndi kunenedweratu ndi Yohane Wovumbulutsa mu umboni wake wamuyaya. Ndipo motero ndikukupatsani kuti mumvetsetse kuti panthawi yosonkhanitsa pamodzi okhulupirika mwa Khristu kuti ndidzachotsa chophimba chobisika pang'ono mwadzidzidzi ndi mwamphamvu monga momwe ndinang'ambira chophimba cha kachisi pakati kuchokera pamwamba mpaka pansi chomwe chinawonetsa kutha kwa utumiki wa Levi ndi kubwezeretsedwa kwathunthu kwa Melikizedeki. Ngakhale kuti ndichotse khungu pang'ono kwa ana a Israyeli kwamuyaya, kuti asabwererenso kwa nkhunda zanga.

Ndipo izi zidzachitika mwadzidzidzi ndi mosayembekezereka chifukwa dziko lapansi silikuyembekezera Khristu Yesu kuunikira m'mbali ndi mlengalenga kwathunthu ndi ulemerero wake wamuyaya mu nthawi izi za zowawa zobereka zomwe zikufika pachimake, ngakhale zili choncho, chifukwa dziko lapansi liridi lopanda chikhulupiriro. Ndipo mwa omwe akanidwa kulowa kumwamba pamene chitseko chachikulu chimenecho chitsekedwa, ambiri omwe ndi ochimwa oyipa adzawonongeka ndikutengedwa mu chiweruzo mpaka muyaya, ngakhale omwe akana Yesu wa ku Nazarete kwathunthu mu mwano waukulu ndi kunyada motsutsana ndi uta wanga woyera, ndi ena ambiri omwe ali ochimwa chimodzimodzi mu kukana kwawo kopanduka omwe adzagwa kwamuyaya ku chiweruzo chaukali chifukwa cha zoyipa zomwe achita. Ngakhale zoyipa zomwezo zomwe akupitiriza kudzipereka kwambiri kuchita, ndipo sadzapulumuka.

Mvetsetsani kuti onse omwe akufuna kuphwanya malire achilengedwe ndi madongosolo a chilengedwe changa powoloka malire ndi madongosolo amenewo, ngakhale kufunafuna kudziyika okha kukhala zonyansa (m'njira za ogwa) nawonso adzagwa ku chiweruzo chamuyaya chimodzimodzi: Chifukwa ili ndi tchimo losakhululukidwa, ngakhale mwano motsutsana ndi Yahweh amene ali Mzimu Woyera, kufunafuna kudzasintha okha kuchokera ku chifaniziro cha Yahweh chomwe analengedwa poyamba. Chifukwa chake, kanani nthano zawo za a Jesuit zomwe zasintha umulungu weniweni kukhala nthano zobweretsedwa ndi Nimrodi ndi atatu ake oyambirira osayera! Chifukwa atatu oyera a Atate, Mwana, ndi mzimu woyera aipitsidwa kuchokera ku tanthauzo lake loyambirira ndi cholinga chake, komabe choonadi cha Yahweh sichingabisidwe ndi zinthu zofooka ndi zopemphapempha ngakhale amanama! Komabe, Yesu wa ku Nazarete ndi Yahweh wowonetsedwa m'thupi, ndipo mzimu wanga woyera wa umwana umadzaza kwathunthu okhulupirika mwa Khristu omwe alidi mbali ya umulungu mu ulamuliro wawo ndi Khristu Yesu pansi pa Dongosolo la Melikizedeki monga Ansembe ndi Mafumu kwamuyaya! Chifukwa chake, mvetsetsani kuti ndinu ndani mwa Khristu ana anga chifukwa ogwa akufuna kubisa choonadi chokhudza inu ndi nthano zawo zabodza.

Komabe, okhulupirika mwa Khristu akhala ndi moyo ndikumwalira ndipo akuyendabe pamaso pa dziko lapansi mu chikhulupiriro chachikulu, ndipo mphindi yodabwitsa ndi yaulemerero iyi sidzawapeza mu umbuli chifukwa adzamvetsetsa kulira kwa mfuu waukuluwo, ndipo adzamvetsetsa liwu la lipenga lomaliza lomwe laperekedwa kale m'manja ndi malamulo athunthu oti lilire pamene lamulo laperekedwa ndi Khristu Yesu, amene nthawi zonse amachita chifuniro cha Adonai Yahweh mwangwiro. Okhulupirika mwa Khristu adzaloledwa kulowa pakhomo lalikulu limenelo kumwamba lomwe palibe munthu padziko lapansi amene angatseke kapena kutsegula, kaya agona kapena ali amoyo pamaso pa dziko lapansi panthawi ya kuitanidwa kwawo!

Ndipo masautso aakulu adzapeza osakhulupirika omwe adavomereza Yesu wa ku Nazarete kuchokera mu mtima wopanda chikhulupiriro, ngakhale kukana kukhala ndi moyo wolapa chifukwa adakonda machimo awo. Zidzachitika kuti omwe adasankha kupitiriza mu tchimo, omwe ndi mitima yosamvera ndi youma khosi adavomereza Yesu ngati Ambuye koma napitiriza mu ntchito za thupi adzasiyidwa ku moto wa oyeretsa kuti awononge thupi, ngakhale kuti atha kuthawa zosangalatsa za tchimo ndikuti atha kuthawa thupi lawo lomwe lawagwira mu zilakolako zake. Ndipo ambiri adzabwera ku chipulumutso poyitana pa Yesu wa ku Nazarete mpaka mpweya wawo womaliza, chifukwa nthawi zonse chakhala chofunikira kwa okhulupirika, kuti munthu apezeke wokhulupirika mpaka mpweya wake womaliza, ngati sakhala m'modzi mwa ochepa omwe amatengedwa amoyo kupita ku chitetezo cha kumwamba mu nthawi zawo za kukhulupirika. Selah!

Ndipo nkhondo yawo idzayaka ndikupitiriza pa nthawi yake yoikidwa ndipo kuchokera ku phulusa la chiwonongeko chawo adzauka. Chinjoka, chilombo chochokera kunyanja ndi chilombo chochokera kudziko lapansi chomwe chilinso mneneri wawo wonyenga adzauka mu mwano waukulu kuti akhale ndi ulamuliro wawo wankhanza koma wochepa mwachifundo. Komabe, Yahweh, WAMKULU NDINE, sanadzisiye yekha wopanda umboni, monga momwe zikuchitiridwa umboni ndi gulu la mboni m'zolemba zanga zamuyaya ndi zoyera zomwe zasungidwa mwangwiro kumwamba. Ndipo monga momwe ziliri, maumboni anga ambiri atayika ku nthawi ndi kunyalanyazidwa ndi omwe anapatsidwa udindo wowasamalira. Mvetsetsani mibadwo ana anga ndikukana mabodza awo achinyengo a nthawi. Mvetsetsani kuti chilengedwe sichili ndi zaka mamiliyoni ambiri malinga ndi mabodza a ogwa. Chilengedwe chili ndi zaka zosakwana zikwi zisanu ndi chimodzi ndipo ndagawa nthawi izi m'mibadwo. Panali mbadwo wa chisokonezo chochepa chifukwa cha ogwa omwe anaipitsa dziko lapansi lomwe linalipo panthawiyo ndi zonyansa zawo, ngakhale zonyansa zomwe zinawonongedwa kwathunthu mu chiweruzo chomwe chinafika pachimake mu chigumula chachikulu m'masiku a Nowa.

Komabe, Nowa anapitiriza kukhala ndi moyo kudzera mu chigumula, ngakhale kupulumutsidwa wamoyo pa Chingalawa ndi mbadwa zake (ndi nyama ndi zolengedwa) zomwe zinapatsidwa malo oti zipitirize chifukwa Nowa anali munthu wolungama ndi wolamulira wolungama pansi pa Dongosolo la Melikizedeki, pa mapewa ake omwe chofunda chinakhala mpaka mtumiki wanga anapita ku mpumulo wake wamtendere mpaka adzutsidwa posachedwa tsopano m'chilungamo. M'kupita kwa nthawi Mose anatulutsidwa wamoyo m'madzi a Nailo, ndipo anauka mu mphamvu yayikulu ndipo anabweretsa mbadwo wanga wa Torah, ngakhale pamene Israyeli anapangidwa mtundu waukulu ndi wamphamvu womwe ndi mthunzi wa zinthu zomwe zikubwera.

Ndipo pa nthawi yangwiro ndi yeniyeni yoikidwa Yesu wa ku Nazarete anabadwa kwa namwali ndipo anauka kukhala mwanawankhosa wa Mulungu, ngakhale kudzipereka yekha mu chitetezero chamuyaya cha chilango cha Mose, ngakhale nsembe yomwe ndinaivomereza kwamuyaya chifukwa cha chikhululukiro cha machimo kwamuyaya, kwa onse omwe ayeretsedwa ndi izo, ngakhale mu Chipangano chake Chatsopano cha chilungamo chomwe Khristu Yesu anavomereza ndikusindikiza kwamuyaya m'magazi ake opanda tchimo, chifukwa chake, palibenso chifukwa cha nsembe ya nyama. Zoonadi, Yesu wa ku Nazarete ndi maziko a chilungamo nthawi zonse, ngakhale nthawi yomwe inafika pachimake ndi kugonjetsedwa kwa ogwa kwamuyaya, ngakhale nthawi yomwe inagwidwa ndi Mawu amuyaya "ZATHA!"

Ndipo motero mbadwo wanga wa chisomo unabweretsedwa pa tsiku limenelo la Pentekoste pamene ndinapatsa Khristu Yesu mzimu wanga woyera wa Umwana womwe anauthira pa lamulo langa chifukwa cha chiwombolo cha onse omwe anasankha kukhala ana a Mulungu operekedwa ndi mzimu wa Umwana mu nthawi izi. Ndipo motero kunali kubadwa kwa Mpingo wa Khristu Yesu womwe unaperekedwa chifukwa cha chisomo ndi chifundo pa amitundu, ngakhale chiweruzo chaukali ndi chomaliza chisanafike. Ngakhale nthawi za chifundo ndi chisomo komwe mafuko otayika a nyumba ya Israyeli akhoza kusonkhanitsidwa mu Israyeli wa Mulungu, pamodzi ndi aliyense wochokera ku mitundu omwe anaitanidwa ndikuyankha popanga Yesu wa ku Nazarete kukhala Ambuye wawo wamuyaya. Ndipo anthu anga omwe anabalalitsidwa mu chilango ndipo omwe anayendayenda padziko lapansi ngati alendo m'maiko achilendo, ndi onse ochokera kwa Adamu omwe anavomereza chipulumutso mu nthawi izi adzakhala mbali ya Israyeli wa Mulungu kwamuyaya, kuti asachokepo pa madongosolo awo amuyaya a utumiki kwa Khristu Yesu Ambuye wawo wokwera kumwamba!

Choncho mvetsetsani kuti dziko lomwe lilipo tsopano lafika kumapeto ake amuyaya ndipo dziko loipali silidzakhalaponso kuti lisautse omwe ndimawakonda mpaka kalekale.
