Inzwai Mashoko Angu uye Muungane kuInzwi raShe Wenyu!

Inzwai mashoko angu uye muungane kuinzwi raShe wenyu, Mambo, Mesia, uye Mupristi Mukuru, iye Yeshua HaMashiach. Munopokana nei nemashoko angu pamwe nekurongeka kwezviitiko zvawo, kunyange neruzivo rwakadzama rwemashoko angu? Nokuti ndakataura pachena pamusoro pechakavanzika cheBhabhironi nemagumo aro ari kusvika:

Zvakazarurwa 17

[17:13] Ava vane murangariro mumwe, uye vachapa simba ravo nesimba ravo kuchikara.

[17:16] Uye nyanga gumi dzawaona pamusoro pechikara, idzi dzichavenga mhombwe, uye dzichaiita dongo, isina kupfeka, uye dzichadya nyama yayo, uye dzichaipisa nemoto.

[17:17] Nokuti Mwari akaisa mumwoyo yavo kuti vazadzise kuda kwake, uye kuti vabvumirane, uye vape umambo hwavo kuchikara, kusvikira mashoko aMwari azadziswa.

Asi vazhinji venyu havana ruzivo rwakakodzera rwemagwaro uye vazhinji venyu muchine kutenda mumunhu wenyama. Mune kutenda mukugona kwemunhu wenyama, simba remunhu wenyama, mhinduro dzemunhu wenyama, uye nhema dzemunhu wenyama, hamuna kuverenga here kuti vanhu vose varevi venhema, vana vangu? Ndinzwirei munzwisise: Hakuna mhinduro inopiwa nemunhu wenyama isingatungamiriri kushato, uye shato ichatonga nyika kwenguva pfupi kana mudzivisi abviswa, uye kunyange iye zvino ndiye mwari wenyika ino, izvi hazvina kunyorwa pachena here mumagwaro?

Sei muchiri kupokana nemashoko angu, vadiwa vangu neavo vandinoshuvira? Tendai mashoko angu uye munzwisise kuti munhu wenyama haana simba, munhu wenyama haana kugona kwake pachake kurwisa Satani, mhinduro dzemunhu wenyama dzinoguma pasina uye dzinoshandira zvinangwa zvaSatani, uye hapana chokwadi mumunhu wenyama nevatungamiriri venyu venyika. Nokuti munhu wenyama akaoreswa kubva pakutanga paMunda weEdeni nenyoka uye anotsauka kubva mudumbu achitoberekwa akafa mukudarika nezvivi, uye nokuda kwechinangwa chakadai ini, Jesu weNazareta, ndakazviratidza kuti ndiparadze mabasa adhiabhorosi mune avo vanogamuchira chipo changu cherunyararo neruponeso.

Tarirai! Vatungamiriri vose venyika vanoshanda pamwe chete vari kumashure, uye vose vanoshumira Satani. Hamuna vatungamiriri vakasarudzwa, asi munopiwa sarudzo yenhema nokuti hamuna kumbosarudza avo vamunofunga kuti munosarudza nekuvhota, asi vanounzwa kwamuri kuti muvavhotere kuitira kuti vawane mvumo yenyu uye mvumo yenyu chivi nokuti hamugoni kusarudza nemaune chakaipa chiduku uye muchindimiririra panguva imwe chete, iko kunyengedzwa. Tendevukai! Budai pakati pavo uye muzvitsaure! Ini, kunyange Ishe Jesu Kristu mururimi rweChirungu naYeshua HaMashiach muchiHebheru, handina kupa mhinduro yezvematongerwo enyika uye hapana mutungamiriri wenyika achamuka nokuti handina kupa munhu, uye avo vanotenda neimwe nzira vakanyengedzwa zvinosiririsa. Vatungamiriri venyika vose vari mukudyidzana, uye havazoputsi chibvumirano nokuti vatungamiriri venyu vose vanoshumira Satani kana muchivande, kana pachena. Muchiri kuda mashoko akanaka nekutaura kwakanaka here? Hamuna kunyeperwa zvakakwana here, mararama upenyu hwekunyengedzwa naSatani kubva pakuzvarwa? Sei muchiri kutsvaka vatungamiriri venyika panzvimbo pangu, Kristu Jesu? Nokuti ndini Ishe wenyu, Mambo, Mesia, uye Mupristi Mukuru uyo Yahweh akapa, uye ndakatenga nyika kunyange neupenyu hwevakadzikinurwa neropa rangu risina chivi rinoyananisa. Nokuti saMupristi wenyu Mukuru, ndakazvipa segwayana renyu rePasika risina chivi uye rakakwana. Uye ndiri Ishe wemadzishe naMambo wemadzimambo, uye ndiri kuuya seShumba yeRudzi rwaJudha uye ndichatonga kubva pachigaro chaDhavhidhi panyika uye zvinangwa zvaYahweh zvisingaperi zveruponeso nerudzikinuro zvichazadziswa mandiri apo Shumba ichavata pasi negwayana muUmambo hwangu huri kuuya nekutonga kusingagumi.

Tarirai! Ndinokuudzai chinhu chisati chaitika. Bhabhironi yakavanzika, kunyange idzi United States dzakamboshanda sechiedza kunyika, dziri mumaketani kunyange dzakachengeterwa kutongwa nemoto. Kutongwa kwayo nekuparadzwa kwayo kuchauya kamwe kamwe uye muawa imwe ichapiswa nemoto, muawa imwe ichabvarurwa, muawa imwe nguo dzayo dzichafukidzwa nemvura kunyange kubva kumabvazuva nekumadokero. Mauto matsvuku echikara akatopinda mumiganhu yayo uye atogadzirira nekumirira mirairo, uye achauraya murume wese, mukadzi, nemwana asingashandiri zvinangwa zvawo. Nokuti chikara nemuporofita wenhema vari kuda kuzarurwa vatoshanda pamwe chete mukuparadza Bhabhironi yakavanzika, uye vatove mumiganhu yayo.

Vose vari vaviri vaimbova vatungamiriri veBhabhironi Yakavanzika. Mumwe anoita kunge asiri iye uye anofambisa chidhori chekare chine uremu chiri iye, uye mumwe anoita kunge ari kutambudzwa nechidhori chekare, asi vanhu venyika vakamuzivisa saKoreshi wavo uye vanomuda samambo wavo, anoti ndiye akasarudzwa nokuti shato yakamusarudza kunyengedza vanhu venyika yechipikirwa, uye achavaka temberi yavo, kunyange pasina chikonzero chechinhu chakadaro nokuti ini Yeshua HaMashiach ndiri Pasika yakakwana uye imi muri temberi yaMwari mupenyu, kunyange temberi yakaitwa pasina maoko evanhu asi nesimba raYahweh kuti akudzwe uye achakukudzai mandiri Kristu Jesu Ishe wenyu!

Tarirai! Zvikara izvi zvakaipa nemubatanidzwa wazvo uri kuumbwa wenyanga gumi zvakatogadzirira kuparadzwa kweBhabhironi Yakavanzika. Nokuti vakagadzirira pasi rose, uye vakagadzirira nekuronga pasi rose kuparadza kwekupedzisira kwenyika sezvamunoiziva uye kubva mumadota kuchamuka hurongwa hwavo hutsva, kubva munyonganyonga kuchamuka hurongwa hwavo hwaSatani uye chiratidzo chichamanikidzwa kupinda munzanga yavo uye vanhu vaMwari vachagara murenje semumazuva aNoa uye vashoma vachapona. Nokuti chikara nemuporofita wenhema vacharwa nevatsvene uye vovakunda. Ava ndivo vachabuda mukutambudzika kukuru, uye vakasuka nguo dzavo, uye vakadziita chena muropa reGwayana. Naizvozvo, vari pamberi pechigaro chaMwari, uye vanomushumira masikati nousiku muTemberi yake: uye iye anogara paChigaro achagara pakati pavo. Havachazovi nenzara, kana nyota zvakare, uye zuva harizovapisi, kana kupisa kupi nokupi. Nokuti Gwayana, riri pakati pechigaro, richavafudza, uye richavatungamirira kuzvitubu zvipenyu zvemvura: uye Mwari achapukuta misodzi yose pameso avo.

Tarirai! Muvengi anouya uye kurongeka kwezviitiko kunotevera: Mudzivisi achadzivisa kusvikira ndaunganidza vakatendeka muKereke yangu kuti vasangane neni mumakore pakuzviratidza kwangu kuKereke yangu, kunyange Mwenga wangu. Kunyange vazhinji venyu vachiramba magwaro akajeka pamusoro pechiitiko ichi, chichaitika, uye chichazivikanwa pasi rose. Hakuna kubviswa kwakavanzika, nokuti iwayo mashoko evaporofita venhema vechiZionist, uye kuzviratidza kwangu kuchave kusingarambiki pasi rose. Vachatsvaka kuvhara kubva kwenyu nehondo nekupinda kwenhema kwevatorwa, asi hapana vatorwa asi vakawa vanonyepedzera. Kana Mwenga wangu atorwa kuenda kumatenga uye mudzivisi abviswa, kuparadzwa kamwe kamwe kuchawira kamwe kamwe pasi rose uye marudzi ose aimbonyepedzera kuva echiKristu acharega kuvapo nenzira yaaripo iye zvino, asi pachave nevanhu vakasara vanokundwa nekuderedzwa. Bhabhironi yechakavanzika ichawa kutanga uye kamwe kamwe panguva imwe chete nekurwisa kwavo kwechimbichimbi, asi haizombomuki zvakare, nokuti Yahweh akazivisa chikamu chepiri chekutongwa pamusoro payo.

Nokuti Bhabhironi yechakavanzika yakatungamirira marudzi muchivi chikuru nekunyengedza kuburikidza nehuroyi hwayo nemishonga yayo kunyange varaidzo yayo inonzi yetsika uye nemishonga yayo inonyangadza yakapfuudzwa semishonga iyo muchokwadi iri chepfu inouraya. Uye saizvozvo magumo ayo achauya kamwe kamwe uye pasina mushonga. Uye dongo richasara, kunyange zvazvo vakasara vachaponeswa, nokuti ani naani anodana pazita raShe uye asingadi upenyu hwake kusvika kumagumo achaponeswa.

Saka nzwisisai nguva dzamunorarama uye nzwisisai kurongeka kwezviitiko zviri kuuya uye nzwisisai kuti hapana chinogona kushandura zviitiko izvi, nokuti izvo Yahweh akazivisa hapana anogona kuzvishandura!

Tarirai! Ndiri Yeshua HaMashiach uye ndatumira Mashoko aya nomuranda wangu, uye nomuranda wangu Mashoko aya atumwa.

Ndichaonekwa kamwe kamwe, wagadzirira here Mwenga wangu?
