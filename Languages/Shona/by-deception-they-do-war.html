Vanorwa Hondo Nokunyengera!

Tarirai! Muteyi upi anoteera musungo shiri ichizviona? Asi zvirokwazvo, zvirokwazvo, vanhu venyika yechipikirwa vari kuteerwa musungo, uye hondo iri panyika sezvo hasha norutongo zvoda kudonha. Zviunganidzei kunzwi raYeshua HaMashiach munzwe mashoko angu. Tarirai! Ndinokuudzai zvinhu zvichauya zvisati zvaitika, kuti mashoko angu amire seyambiro kune vose avanotumirwa. Nokuti ndizvo zvakaita mashoko oupenyu andinotaura, nokuti ini, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndiri Shoko raMwari rakaitwa nyama, uye ndinogara mukati menyu, nokuti imi muri tembere yaMwari mupenyu, uye ini ndiri mupristi mukuru nokusingaperi maererano nourongwa hwaMerkizedeki.

Zvirokwazvo, zvirokwazvo, mashoko angu achawana meso, nzeve, nemwoyo yeavo avanotumirwa, uye acharamba akachengetedzwa muhondo nematambudziko anouya, kunze kwokuti kuchava nokushomeka kwemagwaro matsvene pasi rose nokuti panguva yakadirwa nyika zhinji mashoko aYahweh, iye Mwari mukuru wekusingaperi, nyika yakatambira mashoko iwayo kwechinguva. Asi zvino nyika yaramba mashoko aYahweh ichida nhema dzeShumba, saka zvino Shoko raMwari richashomeka, uye kuchava nenzara yeShoko raMwari, nokuti ndizvo zvakaita matongero anouya. Kunyange zvakadaro, mweya mupenyu wose unodana kuzita raShe kusvika kumagumo uchaponeswa, kunyange naivo vasingadi upenyu hwavo kusvika kumagumo.

Asi inzwai munzwisise mashoko angu, muzive uye munzwisise kuti mashoko angu acharamba akachengetedzwa, uye achaziviswa kunyika, uye nguva dzokuzivisa dzasvika. Saka ini, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndinotuma vhangeri risingaperi munguva dzenhamo huru, iyo nhamo yaJakobho, kuti vazhinji-zhinji vaponeswe sokunge nomoto. Saka vhangeri risingaperi rasvika kuzoziviswa panguva yaro, uye zvipupuriro zvichatonyorwa, uye mashoko aYahweh achayerera senzizi dzemvura mhenyu, uye akanyorerwa kuteerera kwevose vane nzeve dzokunzwa nemeso okuona nemwoyo wokudzidza. Uye mashoko aYahweh mashoko asingaperi ayo pasina munhu anogona kurasa, kuodza, kana kunyaradza, nokuti munhu anoita izvozvo achazvirasa, asi mashoko aMwari wekusingaperi anogara nokusingaperi.

Tarirai! Nguva dzenhamo yaJakobho dzasvika, uye nguva dzasvika dzokuzivisa zvinhu zvose, nokuti Yahweh akasarudza nguva ino kuti azvizivise kuchisikwa chake, kunyange pachiso chomwanakomana wake mumwe oga, Jesu Kristu. Nokuti ini ndiri Jesu Kristu, iye Ishe wemadzishe naMambo wemadzimambo akakwidzwa kudenga, uye mumaoko angu makapiwa gwaro renyika nokuti ndakaitenga neropa rangu risina chivi, uye mubairo wangu uneni. Uye ndichagovera nhaka yangu pakati pevakavimbika, uye uyo anokunda achagara nhaka yezvinhu zvose kubva paruoko rwangu somubairo wokuvimbika kwavo, kuti vagoratidza nokusingaperi kubwinya kusingaperi kwaYahweh. Uye Yahweh achavakudza, uye avo vakazvininipisa mukuzvininipisa, kuteerera, nokutendeuka vachakwidziridzwa mukubwinya kusingaperi uye vachapenya senyeredzi mudenga, uye kubwinya kwavo kuchapenya nokusingaperi-peri. Saka ndinokukurudzirai, vadikani vangu, kuti munzwe inzwi rangu uye mukotamise mwoyo yenyu mukuteerera.

Nditeererei, uye mundinzwisise zvakanaka; hondo yasvika, uye Bhabhironi Rakavanzika rakasungirwa kuparadzwa; uye kutongwa nokurangwa kwaro hakunonoki asi kwava pedyo. Uye pandinobvuta Kereke yangu, Muviri wangu, uye kunyange Mwenga wangu kubva munjodzi ndichimuendesa mukuchengeteka kwokudenga, mudzivisi achabviswa, uye vachaita kurwisa kwavo kwechimbichimbi, uye nguva yokurwisa kwavo yava ikozvino. Madzimambo gumi vakarangana pakati pavo, uye zvirokwazvo vakagadzirira maoko avo; vakaronga, vakagadzirira, uye vakafukidza nyika nenhema dzavo, kuti vaite sevakarurama mukuuraya mamiriyoni nemamiriyoni achawira munjodzi nemarwadzo ehondo yavo.

Uye isinagoge raSatani chairo rinotonga nyika zvino, uye vanozvivanza munyika yechipikirwa sedandemutande rinozvihwandisa kubva pane charinoda kubata kusvikira chabatwa mudandemutande raro. Zvirokwazvo, zvirokwazvo, vakaruka dandemutande ravo pasi rose, uye vakabata madzimambo enyika mumadandemutande avo okunyepera, nokuti vanorwa hondo nokunyengera, pamwe nokuponda, chiokomuhomwe, kutyisidzira, kugoronyera, nezvimwe zvakaipa zvizhinji. Nokuti nzira dzavo dzakaipa kwazvo, nokuti Satani anoisa vakadereresa vevadereresa uye avo vakanyanya kuora sevatungamiriri venyika kuti vagone kudzorwa nyore nyore uye kushandiswa zvizere nemasora ayo Satani akadyara pakati peminda yegorosi yaYahweh. Uye nguva dzekukohwa kwekupedzisira dzava kusvika apo gorosi richaunganidzwa mudura raYahweh risingaperi kuburikidza nekukohwa kunouya, uye masora aSatani achadzurwa zvachose, osungwa, okandwa ari mapenyu mudziva remoto unopfuta nesarufa unopfuta nemirazvo yebhuruu inopisa kwazvo munhaka yemadzibaba avo; uye ndizvo zvakaita ramangwana ravo, saka musavatya.

Asi ndizvo zvazviri, dandemutande rinotonga dandemutande raro sezvo richitsvaga kutora kutonga kwenyika; nokuti nyika yakapiwa mumaoko eshumba nenzira shoma kwechinguva chidiki, iyo vhiki rechimakumi manomwe raDhanieri, idzo nguva dzava ikozvino. Saka vakavimbika muKereke yangu vachatorwa kuenda kudenga kumubairo mukuru, uye vachagamuchira nhaka yavo, uye chikomba chichabatana nomwenga wake musungano isingaperi, uye nokusingaperi vachava neni kwose kwandinoenda. Saka gwaro renyika randakabata mumaoko angu iro rakavharwa parizvino rava kuda kuzarurwa, uye zvisimbiso zvichasunungurwa, uye vacharidza hwamanda dzavo, uye ndiro dzichadururwa nehasha. Uye kutonga kukuru nokuparadza kuchafukidza nyika, uye njodzi ichawira pasi rose apo hondo yokupedzisira yokubatanidza marudzi pasi pokutonga kwaSatani ichienderera mberi, kuti chikara nomuprofita wenhema vasimuke vatonga nyika noutsinye hukuru, uye ndizvo zvichaitika. Vachazivisa hondo pazvinhu zvose zvaMwari wechokwadi, uye chiKristu chichava chisingabvumirwi nomutemo, uye zvakapiwa kuzvikara kuti zvirwe navatsvene uye zvivakunde, uye vazhinji vacharasikirwa nemisoro yavo nokuda kwezita rangu, uye vose vanodaro vachawana upenyu hwavo nokusingaperi mumubairo mukuru nokubwinya kusingaperi, uye vachararama upenyu husingaperi hworugare, uye vachafarikanya zvakakora zvenyika.

Asi, sezvo nyika inounzwa kumabvi ayo asina kutenda mukuzviisa pasi pezvakaipa zvakachena kuburikidza nehondo huru, utsinye, nokuparadza, chikara chinobva mugungwa nechikara chinobva panyika zvichasimuka zvizere, kunyange zvazvo parizvino vachinyepedzera kuva vavengi mukunyengedza kwavo kwazvino nokunyepera kuparadza Bhabhironi Rakavanzika. Nzwisisai kuti baba vavo vakafanana, nokuti ndevaSatani pachake, kunyange vaviri ivavo vanonyangadza avo shumba yakasarudza kuzadzisa zvinangwa zvayo. Saka chikara chinobva mugungwa nechikara chinobva panyika zvichasimuka, uye vachayananisa rugare pakati pemarudzi, kunze kwokuti ruchava rugare rwenhema. Nzwisisai kuti hakuna rugare kunze kwechibvumirano chorugare chakapiwa naYahweh nokuda kwezvivi zvenyika, nokuti ini, Jesu weNazareta, ndiri muchinda worugare uye ndinounza rugare rusingaperi, uye mukururama ndichatonga nyika kubva kunyika yechipikirwa kubva pachigaro choushe chaDhavhidhi, nokuti ndiri mugari wenhaka yake akarurama.

Saka vachasimuka. Hondo yenyika yechitatu ichamutswa pakati peavo vanopfugamira kuutanda hwavo hutema uye vacharwa hondo panyika sezvo vachitsvaga kuparadza chero chinhu chine chekuita nezita raJesu Kristu. Kunze kweavo vanopfugamira kuutanda hwavo hutema vachaiswa zvizere pasi pokutonga kwechikara chinobva mugungwa, icho chichavatungamirira saMahdi wavo. Uye avo vari munyika dzechipikirwa vachava namambo wavo wenhema, muprofita wenhema, Moshiach wenhema, uye achava muvaki wetembere yechikara, uye chikara chinobva panyika chichavatungamirira mukunyengedza kukuru. Uye avo vanopfeka utanda hutema pahuma dzavo vachayananisa rugare neavo vanopfugamira kuutanda hwavo hutema, uye nyika ichabatana sezvo vachitevera zvikara izvi vachiti pakati pavo, ndiani akafanana nechikara? Uye ndiani anogona kurwa nechikara? Asi ini, Kristu Jesu, ndiri Mambo wemadzimambo naIshe wemadzishe, ndichauya kuzotonga nokurwa hondo, uye ndichaparadza zvachose chikara nomuprofita wenhema, uye vachakandwa vari vapenyu mudziva guru rinopfuta nomoto nesarufa mavachamirira Shumba kuti ibatane navo panguva yakatarwa.

Saka vachaita zvipikirwa zvikuru zvorugare nokuchengeteka, uye vachaunganidza avo vakarasika nokusingaperi kukuparadzwa kwavo. Vachapikira nzanga itsva nourongwa hutsva huchada chiratidzo chavo chechikara kuti vatore chikamu, saka musatora chiratidzo chacho. Nechiratidzo chavo vachapikira mushonga wedenda uye vachapikira utano noupenyu hurefu, uye vachazvifungidzira ramangwana guru, uye vachaita zvipikirwa zvizhinji zvenhema kune avo vachaparara nokutora chiratidzo chavo chechikara.

Saka ndinotaura zvakare kune avo vanogara panyika, kuti vose vanzwe:

KANA MUNHU UPI ZVAKE AKANAMATA CHIKARA NOMUFANANIDZO WACHO, AKAGAMUCHIRA CHIRATIDZO CHACHO PAHUMA YAKE, KANA PARUOKO RWAKE, IYEYO ACHAnwa WO WEWaini YEHASHA DZAMWARI, INODURURWA ISINA KUSANGANISWA MUKOMBE YOKUTSAMWA KWAKE, UYE ACHARWADZISWA NOMOTO NESARUFA, PAKATARIRA VATUMWA VATSVENE, UYE PAKATARIRA GWAI: UYE UTSI HWOKURWADZISWA KWAVO HUNOKWIRA NOKUSINGAPERI-PERI. UYE HAVANA ZORORO MASIKATI KANA USIKU, AVANONAMATA CHIKARA NOMUFANANIDZO WACHO, NOUPI ZVAKE ANOGAMUCHIRA CHIRATIDZO CHEZITA RACHO.

Asi nzwisisai, vana vangu, kuti nzanga yavo haizovi norugare, uye zvipikirwa zvavo ihwambo hwokukwezva vasingafungiri mumusungo wavo; nokuti muteyi upi anoteera musungo shiri ichizviona? Asi shiri zhinji dzichabatwa mumusungo wavo, kunyange vose avo mazita avo asina kumbonyorwa muBhuku roUpenyu. Nzwisisai kuti vose vanotora chiratidzo vachashandurwa nokusingaperi, uye vachange vamhura Yahweh, nokushandura DNA yavo uye nokudaro vachiodza mufananidzo waMwari wakasikwa munhu mauri, uye vose vanodaro havana kuregererwa, saka musatora chiratidzo chavo. Zviri nani kurasikirwa noupenyu hwenyu hwepanyika kuti ndikuponesei nokusingaperi uye kuti muwane upenyu husingaperi mandiri, Yeshua HaMashiach, nokudana kuzita rangu kusvika kumagumo pane kuedza zvisina kuchenjera kuponesa upenyu hwenyu hwepanyika uye kurasikirwa nomukana upi zvawo woupenyu husingaperi kurufu rwechipiri rwemirazvo isingaperi apo honye yavo isingafi. Saka sarudzai nokuchenjera!

Nyika ichasimuka kubva mumadota ehondo ichava nyika yedenda, rufu, nzara, kusvibiswa, kusaruramisira, utsinye, kupenga, nenhamo huru. Mufaro wose uchabviswa panyika, asi chikara chinobva panyika chichamanikidza chiratidzo chacho panyika, iko kurumwa kwenyoka, uye ani naani anoramba acharasikirwa noupenyu hwake. Uye ndiyo sarudzo iyo vazhinji vachafanira kuita kuti vawane upenyu husingaperi muruponeso, uye vose vanodana kuzita raShe kusvikira kumagumo vachaponeswa. Asi, nzanga yavo itsva haizounzi rugare, uye vose vanotora chiratidzo vachava chikamu chepfungwa dzakabatana, uye vachasanganiswa nematekinoroji, uye vachava varanda vekuda kweshumba, uye ndiyo yavachashumira, uye mudziva guru remoto iroro vachaenda, uye ndirwo rufu rwechipiri.

Dhiragoni, chikara chinobva mugungwa, nechikara chinobva panyika vachatungamirira mubatanidzwa wamadzimambo, uye vacharwa navose vanotsvaka kurarama nenzira dzaYahweh, uye vachapomera ini, Yeshua HaMashiach, mhosva yematambudziko anowira pasi pano. Mukutonga nomukupanduka kukuru vachamhura zita raMwari wokudenga nomwanakomana wake mumwe oga wakaberekwa, uye vachataura mashoko mazhinji okumhura. Chikara chinobva mugungwa nechikara chinobva panyika vachabatana vachirwisana nezvapupu zvangu zviviri nezvigadziko zvangu zviviri zvemwenje uye vachazvipikisa nezviratidzo zvizhinji zvenhema nezvishamiso, uye vachataura mashoko mazhinji okumhura. Uye kana zvapupu zvangu zviviri nezvigadziko zvangu zviviri zvemwenje zvapedza uchapupu hwazvo, chikara chinokwira chichibva mugomba risina magumo chicharwa nazvo, chikazvikunda, nokuzviuraya. Asi mumazuva matatu nehafu vachamutswa, uye inzwi guru rinobva kudenga richati kwavari, Kwirai pano. Uye vachakwira kudenga vari mugore, uye vavengi vavo vachavaona.

Saka chikara chinobva panyika, iye anouya nechiratidzo chake chechiara muruoko rwake, iye achava muprofita wavo wenhema naMoshiach wenhema, achauya kuzotonga nyika yechipikirwa. Uye akatozvifadza kuvatongi venyika, kunyange vatungamiriri vesinagogi raSatani, nokuti anogoverana navo ropa ravo rakashata, uye anodada nedzinza rake, uye Dhani mwana weshumba uyo pasina Mesia achazombomuka maari. Saka achavaka temberi yavo sezvavanotsvaka zvisina musoro kumisazve mutemo waMosesi, zvinhu zvakazadziswa mukukwana kwakakwana muchibayiro changu chandakazviita seGwayana raMwari, nokuti ndini ndakazvipa sePaseka yakakwana yezvivi zvevanhu. Asi muteyi weiko anoteera musungo shiri ichiona?

Zvirokwazvo, zvirokwazvo, vachateya musungo muZioni, uye vachatsvaka kutora avo venyika, nokuti Satani anotsvaka kuparadza mweya wose worudzi rwaAdhamu, kunyange rudzi rwevanhu; uye dai mazuva okutambudzika kwaJakobho asina kupfupiswa, hapana nyama yaizosara iri mhenyu. Asi saka danho richagadzirwa netemberi yavo yechitatu, umo munyengeri unoparadza uchaitikira. Uye saka ndakayambira: Naizvozvo, kana muchiona nyengeri inoparadza, yakataurwa nomuprofita Dhanieri, imire panzvimbo tsvene (unorava, ngaanzwisise). Zvino avo vari muJudhea, ngavatizire kumakomo. Uyo ari pamusoro pedenga remba, ngaarege kuburuka, kuzotora chinhu mumba make: Uye uyo ari mumunda, ngaarege kudzokera kuzotora nguo dzake. Uye vane nhamo avo vane mimba, naavo vanomwisa mumazuva iwayo. Asi nyengeterai kuti kutiza kwenyu kurege kuva munguva yechando, kana pazuva reSabata: Nokuti panguva iyoyo kuchava nokutambudzika kukuru, kusina kumbovapo kubva pakutanga kwenyika kusvikira panguva ino, kwete, uye hakuchazovipo.

Nzwisisai kuti nguva ino yava pedyo uye ichaitika mushure memwedzi 42 yokutanga yokutambudzika kwaJakobho. Mukati memwedzi 42 yokutanga, zvapupu zviviri zvichaprofita, uye vachavaka temberi yavo apo chinhu chakadaro chisingachadiwi; nokuti ini, Jesu weNazareta, ndiri Paseka yakakwana yezvivi zvevanhu nokusingaperi-peri. Asi, zvikara izvi zvisina njere zvinondipikisa, uye chikara chinobva panyika chichaedza kuzviisa saMoshiach wavo wenhema, uye uyu ndiwo musungo wavo.

Vachakwezva avo venyika vachiri kundiramba, ini Yeshua HaMashiach, Mesia wavo wechokwadi, uye vazhinji vachamhanya vachiungana kuna Moshiach wavo wenhema, asi vanomutevera mukusaziva vachienda kukuparadzwa kwavo. Nokuti chikara chinobva panyika ndiye anoteera musungo munyika, uye achakwezva vazhinji mudandemutande rake renyengedzo; ipapo dandemutande richasvetukira pamhuka yaro, uye richatengesa vanhu venyika. Hamuna kuverenga here? Nokuti, zvakanyorwa zvichinzi: Munhu ngaarege kukunyengerai nenzira ipi neipi: nokuti zuva iroro harisviki, kunze kwokunge kwatanga kwava nokutiza, uye munhu iyeye wechivi abudiswa pachena, kunyange mwanakomana wokuparadzwa, Uyo anopikisa, uye achazvikwidziridza pamusoro pezvose zvinonzi Mwari, kana zvinonamatwa: zvokuti anogara saMwari muTemberi yaMwari, achizviratidza kuti ndiye Mwari. Saka nzwisisai kuti kutiza kutanga ndipo pandichaunganidza vakatendeka muKereke yangu kwandiri kuti tisangane neni mumhepo uye panguva iyoyo mudzivisi achabviswa uye munhu iyeye wechivi achabudiswa pachena panguva yake.

Asi Yahweh akambosiya riini vagari venhaka yoruponeso kukuparadzwa kusingaperi, uye ndinozvizivisa pachena zvisati zvaitika: Kuti ini, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndichaponesa vakasara vaJakobho, uye vazhinji vanobva munyika vachandigamuchira saMesia wavo. Uye ndichavanunura. Tarirai, zvakanyorwa zvichinzi: Ndakatarira, uye tarira, Gwayana rakanga rimire pagomo reZioni, uye pamwe naro vane zviuru zana nemakumi mana nezvina, vane Zita raBaba vake rakanyorwa pahuma dzavo. Uye zvichava saizvozvo kuti ndichauya kuzonunura vakasara pakuparadzwa, uye ndinouya neuto risingamisiki richafamba nhanho nenhanho sezvandinovatungamirira kuti nditungamirire vadiki vangu kunzvimbo yakachengeteka. Zvichida mungashamisika kuti vane zviuru zana nemakumi mana nezvina ndivanaani uye vanonditeverera kuGomo reZioni nechinangwa chei?

Vane zviuru zana nemakumi mana nezvina vakasarudzwa naYahweh nyika isati yavambwa nokuda kwenguva yakadai. Vakapfurwa mukutambudzika, uye vachava neunyanzvi hwakasiyana nesimba randichavafukidza nenguo dzavo itsva dzandichavapa, nokuti nguo dzavo dzakasonerwa ivo chaivo uye dzichava nezvavakaita nounhu hwavo. Vachatorwa pakuunganidzwa kwevakatendeka asi vakatsaurirwa zvinangwa zvangu, kunyange zvinangwa zvaYahweh naYeshua HaMashiach. Vanouya panguva yokutambudzika kukuru kuzotungamirira Israeri kunzvimbo yakachengeteka asi vanotanga vatorwa kunoroorwa naKristu, nokuti ini ndiri Chikomba, uye ivo Mwenga wangu, vakadanwa, vakasarudzwa, vakatendeka, uye vechokwadi, kunyange avo vandakaita vakachena semhandara muropa rangu rinoyananisa. Uye imi, Mwenga wangu, ndimi muchashandira zvinangwa zvaYahweh nomurairo wangu munguva dzekutambudzika kukuru.

Tarirai, zvakanyorwa! Uye mukadzi wakatizira kurenje, kwaakanga akagadzirirwa nzvimbo naMwari, kuti vamudyise ikoko mazuva ane chiuru chimwe, namazana maviri, namakumi matanhatu. Uye zvichava saizvozvo kuti ndichagovera nzvimbo yokupotera murenje kuvanhu vangu, nokuti ndichaponesa Jakobho pamatambudziko avo, uye vazhinji vanobva munyika yechipikirwa vachatendeukira kwandiri, Yeshua HaMashiach, anova Mesia wavo wechokwadi. Uye ndichavaponesa pamusungo wavachateerwa, uye saka ndinoyambira vose zvisati zvaitika kuti vagare kure nomusungo uye varege kuvatevera. Musatenda zviratidzo zvavo zvenhema nezvishamiso, uye musatenda mashoko avo okumhura, asi danai kuzita raShe, kunyange Yeshua HaMashiach, anova Jesu Kristu mururimi rweChirungu, nokuti mururimi rupi norupi munhu anodaidza zita rangu, kunyange ivo vachaponeswa kana vakadaidza zita rangu kusvikira kumagumo.

Uye saka ndinouya kuzozivisa vanhu vangu nezvemusungo wavachateerwa nomuprofita wenhema anouya nechiratidzo chake chechiara muruoko rwake. Uye saka ndinoudza vanhu vangu pachena zvisati zvaitika zvichauya pamusoro penyika zvisati zvaitika. Zvirokwazvo, Ishe Mwari haangaiti chinhu, asi anozivisa chakavanzika chake kuvaranda vake vaprofita. Uye ndichauya kuzonunura vazhinji muIsraeri avo vangadai vakaparara uye ndichavatungamirira kurenje kunzvimbo yavakagadzirirwa naYahweh, uye imomo vachadyiswa, nokufudzwa, nokuchengetwa kusvikira dambudziko rapfuura. Nokuti zvichaitika pakuguma kwokutambudzika kwaJakobho, uye saka zvakanyorwa uye saka zvichaitwa:

Ndakaona denga razaruka, uye tarira bhiza jena, uye wakanga akaritasva wainzi Wakatendeka nowechokwadi, uye mukururama anotonga nokurwa hondo. Meso ake akanga akaita somurazvo womoto, uye pamusoro wake paiva nekorona zhinji, uye akanga ane zita rakanyorwa, risingazivikanwi nomunhu asi iye oga. Uye akanga akafuka nguo yakanyikwa muropa, uye zita rake rinonzi, Shoko raMwari. Uye mauto akanga ari kudenga akamutevera akatasva mabhiza machena, vakapfeka mucheka wakaisvonaka, muchena uye wakachena. Uye mumuromo make munobuda munondo unopinza, kuti nawo arove marudzi: uye achaavatonga netsvimbo youtare: uye anotsika chisviniro chewaini chokutsamwa nokushatirwa kwaMwari Wemasimbaose. Uye ane zita rakanyorwa panguo yake, napachidya chake, Mambo Wamadzimambo, NaShe Wadzishe.

Ndini Yeshua HaMashiach, uye ndatumira Mashoko aya nomuranda wangu, uye nomuranda wangu mashoko aya atumwa. Uye ini, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndini ndakazivisa vhangeri risingaperi panguva yaro, uye nguva dzokuzivisa dzava ikozvino.

Tarirai! Nguva dzokuzivisa dzasvika panyika, uye ndinozivisa kuti kutambudzika kwaJakobho, kunyange vhiki rechimakumi manomwe raDhanieri, kwasvika. Pakarepo ndichabvuta vakatendeka panyika ndichienda navo kukuchengeteka kwokudenga, uye pakarepo mudzivisi achabviswa, uye pakarepo njodzi huru, kutonga, nenjodzi zvichawira pamusoro pavagari venyika. Uye vose vakagadzirirwa upenyu husingaperi vachauya kuruoko rworuponeso mandiri, Kristu Jesu, Ishe akamutswa asingaperi. Uye umambo hwaSatani hucharangwa zvachose nokuparadzwa, husingazomukizve.

Ndini iye anouya kuzotonga nokurwa hondo, uye ndinouya seShumba yorudzi rwaJudha, uye vakasara vaJakobho ndichavaponesa, uye ndichatonga nyika mukururama muumambo hwangu hwemireniyamu, uye ndinouya neuto guru ramadzimambo navapristi kuzotonga nyika mukururama nomurairo wangu.

Ndini Yeshua HaMashiach, Mambo mukuru waMadzimambo naShe waMadzishe, saka inzwai Mashoko angu, nokuti nguva dzokuzadziswa kwawo dzasvika.
