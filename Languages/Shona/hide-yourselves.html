Vachapa utano nekuporeswa kubva mukutonga kwaMwari, asi panzvimbo pezvo munhu anofanira kutendeuka kubva pakuipa kwake. Vachapa mukana wekupinda munharaunda yavo kubva mukutonga kunotyisa kuri kuda kuitika pasi rose. Nekuti ini, Kristu Jesu, ndichakuudzai zvisati zvaitika kuti muzive kutendeseka kwemashoko angu uye kuti mugone kufamba mumvura ine njodzi iyo vazhinji vachazviwana vari mairi, kunyange avo vane ushingi hwekudana zita rangu kusvika kumagumo, nekuti ivo ndivo vadyi venhaka yeruponeso rusingaperi. Nekuti vose vanodana Jesu weNazareta saIshe wavo, kunyange vose vanodana zita rangu rine simba uye vasingadi upenyu hwavo kusvika kumagumo, vachaponeswa muupenyu husingaperi.

Hamuna kuverenga here? Ava ndivo vachabuda mukutambudzika kukuru, uye vakasuka nguo dzavo, vakadziita chena muropa reGwayana. Naizvozvo, vari pamberi pechigaro cheushe chaMwari, uye vanomushumira masikati neusiku muTemberi yake: uye iye anogara paChigaro cheUshe achagara pakati pavo. Havachazovi nenzara, kana nyota zvakare, uye zuva harichavapisi, kana kupisa kupi zvako. Nekuti Gwayana, riri pakati pechigaro cheushe, richavafudza, uye richavatungamirira kumatsime emvura mhenyu: uye Mwari achapukuta misodzi yose pameso avo.

Zvisinei, kunyange zvazvo boka guru revanhu richaponeswa kubva muvhiki rechimakumi manomwe raDhanieri uye ruponeso ruchauya kuna Israeri nekuti vachasara vachandikotamira ini Yeshua HaMashiach, (Nekuti ndini Mesiya wechokwadi uyo gwayana raMwari richapa nzira kuShumba yeRudzi rwaJudha, uye vose vachasanganiswa mukuwirirana kusingaperi mandiri, nekuti mandiri Kristu Jesu Shumba ichavata murugare negwayana.) zvinosuwisa, uye zvisina basa, vazhinji venyika vachashamiswa uye vachitevera vatongi ava vechihedheni, nekuti vachanakidza vazhinji. Neuroyi hwavo nemashiripiti avo, vachanyengedza nyika kuti igamuchire muchetura wavo, uye vachaisa chepfu zvachose avo vanoda nyika neuroyi hwavo, nekuti mucherechedzo wechikara uchavanzwa semushonga. Kunyange kurumwa kwenyoka uko hurongwa hwavo hwatotangwa uye hwaedzwa, uye ruzhinji rwevanhu rwakatojairira kugamuchira mucherechedzo wavo uye chirongwa chavo chehondo chakabudirira.

Uye saka hurongwa hwavo hwagadzirira nguva chaiyo, uye vakagadzirira zvibodzwa zvavo zvemagariro uye vose vachadzorwa vanogara mumaguta avo nemunharaunda yavo. Nekuti vachafanira kutora mucherechedzo wechikara kuti vatore chikamu munharaunda yavo yadhiabhorosi, kunyange umambo hweShumba. Avo vanotora mucherechedzo vachashandurwa zvachose. Majini avo chaiwo achashandurwa, nekuti vachazvisanganisa nembeu yevanhu: asi havachanamatirani, sezvo simbi isingasanganiswi nevhu. Mucherechedzo wavo wechihedheni, kunyange kurumwa kwenyoka, uchaparadza zvachose vose vanotora muchetura wavo uye hapana kutiza.

Kunyange vose avo vanotora mucherechedzo vachashandurwa zvachose, uye vachazviwana vari chikamu chehurongwa hwavo, uye vachabatanidzwa nematekinoroji uye pfungwa dzavo hadzichazovi dzavo. Vachadzorwa uye vachange vakaita sevakafa vanofamba, vachava varanda zvachose kupfungwa dzakabatana dzehurongwa hwavo, kunyange zvematekinoroji avo nekuti vachabatanidzwa neinternet yezvinhu zvichauya. Vacharasikirwa nekuda kwavo, uye havachazovi nekunzwa uye chero chinhu chaMwari chichabviswa zvachose mavari nekuti vachazvishandura kuva mumamiriro asingagoni kudzikinurwa, uye vachazvibatanidza zvizere uye zvachose neavo vakawa uye vachava ve shato nekusingaperi uye kugungwa guru remoto vachaenda nekusingaperi. Uye ndizvo zvichava mugumo wekusarudza kwavo.

Nekuti kuguma kwesarudzo yavo hakuchinjiki. Uye matenda avo nemaronda nezvirwere zvichawanda, uye kunyange vachavimbiswa utano nerugare, utano nerugare havazombovi nazvo. Nekuti Satani anoedza kushandisa uroyi hwake kurwisa kutsamwa nekutonga kwaMwari uye kuisa chepfu nekuuraya ruzhinji, asi havazombowana zororo uye avo vanotora chikamu muhuroyi hwake havazombopukunyuki. Avo vanotora mucherechedzo vachapenya pasi pemwenje yavo yerima, uye mwenje yavo ichamisa migwagwa yemaguta avo avakagadzirira varanda vavo. Nzwisisai kuti ava vachange vari vakafa vanofamba vasina ruregerero uye vasingagoni kupora, nekuti ava ndivo vane mazita asina kunyorwa muBhuku reUpenyu reGwayana, rakabayiwa kubva pakuvambwa kwenyika. Uye hapana ruponeso ruchazombowanikwa pakati pavo kana munharaunda yavo, nekuti kufamba pakati pavo nekubatana nenharaunda yavo kuchada zvachose mucherechedzo wechikara.

Zvapupu zvangu zviviri zvichapupura kusvikira nguva dzeuchapupu hwavo dzapera kwemwedzi 42. Uye saka zvichapiwa kuchikara chinokwira kubva mugomba risina chigumo kuti chirwe navo, uye chichavakunda, nekuvauraya, kunyange miti yangu miviri yemiorivhi, nezvigadziko zvemwenje zviviri kana vapedza uchapupu hwavo. Uye zvitunha zvavo zvichavata mumugwagwa weguta guru, iro pamweya rinonzi Sodhoma neEgipita, uko Ishe wenyu akarovererwawo. Uye vakaipa vachapembera zvikuru uye vachapana zvipo nekuda kwekufa kwavo. Uye mushure memazuva matatu nehafu Mweya weupenyu unobva kuna Mwari uchapinda mavari: uye vachamira netsoka dzavo, uye kutya kukuru kuchawira pamusoro pevose vakavaona. Uye vakanzwa inzwi guru richibva kudenga, richiti kwavari, kwirai pano. Uye vakakwira kudenga mugore, uye vavengi vavo vachavaona vachikwira kumatenga kuupenyu husingaperi uye vachashandurwa nekusingaperi. Uye Mutumwa wechinomwe acharira, uye kuchava nemanzwi makuru kudenga, achiti, Umambo hwenyika ino hwava umambo hwaIshe wedu, nehwaKristu wake, uye achatonga nekusingaperi-peri.

(Vakaropafadzwa vakafa, vanofira munaShe kubva zvino; zvirokwazvo, anodaro Mweya, kuti vazorore pamabasa avo; nekuti mabasa avo anovatevera.) Uye nokudaro dambudziko guru risati ramboitika richatanga, kunyange zvazvo dambudziko riripo nguva dzose mukati mevhiki ramakumi manomwe raDhanieri. Uye temberi yavo ichapedzwa uye chinonyangadza chinoparadza chichaitwa naMoshiach wavo wenhema. Uye achasimbisa sungano nevazhinji kwevhiki rimwe; uye pakati pevhiki achagumisa chibayiro nechinopiwa, uye nokuda kwokupararira kwezvinonyangadza, achaita kuti pave nedongo, kusvikira pakuparadzwa kwakatarwa kuchadururirwa pamusoro pedongo racho.

Idzi dzichava nguva dzerima guru vana vangu, nekuti ini Yeshua HaMashiach ndinokuudzai zvinhu izvi zvisati zvaitika, uye zvakanyorwa seyambiro kune vose. Uye saizvozvowo vane zviuru zana nemakumi mana nezvina vangu vachauya kuzobatsira avo vangadai vakaparara uye vanodana kuzita rangu. Ndichavatungamirira nhanho nenhanho, uye vachandiitira mabasa mazhinji ane simba. Nekuti ava ndivo vanotorwa pakuunganidzwa kwevatsvene kwandiri, Kristu Jesu, mudenga pakurira kwehwamanda yekupedzisira uye hwamanda yava kuda kurira.

Ava ndivo vakatendeka vangu, kunyange avo vasina kuwanikwa hunyengeri mavari, nekuti vakazvinatsa semhandara yakachena muropa reGwayana randakapa nokusingaperi semupristi wavo mukuru, nekuti ndiri mupristi mukuru nokusingaperi netsika yaMerkizedeki. Ava ndivo mwenga wangu, uye vachanditevera kwose kwandinoenda. Tarirai! Ndichavaita vane simba, uye hapana chakamboonekwa panyika chingafananidzwa neunyanzvi hwandichavafukidza nahwo. Vachapfekedzwa mucheka wakaisvonaka, muchena, usina ruvara uye unyanzvi hwavo hucharatidza kubudirira kwavo uye vachava nounyanzvi hukuru nekuti vakadanwa vakatendeka uye vakasarudzwa kunyange nyika isati yavambwa naYahweh nokuda kwezvinangwa izvi.

Vane zviuru zana nemakumi mana nezvina vangu vachaburukira paGomo reZioni uye ini ndichavatungamirira. Vachatora avo vandichavatungamirira kuenda navo kurenje kuti vanovanzwa kure nerima guru nekutambudzwa kweumambo hwevavengi. Vachatungamirirwa kunzvimbo yakachengeteka kubva kumatambudziko manomwe nekuti mukutonga uku ndimo mune kuomarara kwehasha dzaMwari kuchadururirwa pachigaro chechikara uye kutambudza umambo hweshato nekuti kurangwa kwavo kukuru. Uye vane zviuru zana nemakumi mana nezvina vachavatungamirira kunzvimbo yakachengeteka, vachavarapa maronda avo, vachavabatsira uye vachavapa tariro huru yekudana kuzita raShe kusvika kumagumo kana zvichidikanwa. Nekuti dai ndisina kupfupisa mazuva, hapana nyama yaizosara iri mhenyu. Uye nokudaro vachapedzisa izvo zvandichavatungamirira mazviri.

Uye kana nguva dzazadziswa zvichadaro, sezvazvakanyorwa uye sezvazvakanyorwa saizvozvo ngazviitwe: Zvino ndakaona denga razaruka, uye tarira, bhiza jena; wakanga akaritasva ainzi Wakatendeka Wazvokwadi, unorwa nokutonga nokururama. Meso ake akanga akaita somurazvo womoto, uye pamusoro wake paiva nekorona zhinji; uye akanga ane zita rakanyorwa, risingazivikanwi nomunhu asi iye amene. Uye akanga akafuka nguo yakanyikwa muropa, uye zita rake rinonzi, Shoko raMwari. Uye hondo dzaiva kudenga dzakamutevera dzakatasva mabhiza machena, dzakafuka mucheka wakaisvonaka, muchena wakachena. Uye mumuromo make munobuda munondo unopinza, kuti nawo arove ndudzi; uye achavadzora netsvimbo youtare; uye anotsika chisviniro chewaini chehasha nokutsamwa kwaMwari Wemasimbaose. Uye panhuo yake, napachidya chake pane zita rakanyorwa, Mambo wamadzimambo, naShe wamadzishe.

Uye nokudaro ini, kunyange Jesu weNazareta, kunyange Gwayana raMwari, kunyange Kristu Jesu uyo ariwo Shumba yorudzi rwaJudha ndichauya kuzotonga nokurwa neShato uye umambo hwayo hwose huchaputswa zvachose kuti husazomukazve. Uye chikara nomuprofita wenhema vachakandwa vari vapenyu mugungwa romoto uye Satani achasungwa nengetani huru mugomba risina chigumo kwemakore ane chiuru uye nokudaro kutonga kwangu kwemireniyamu kuchatanga uye kutonga kwangu kuchava mukururama. Tarirai! Ndinouya nemwenga wangu neuto ramadzimambo navapristi vandichatonga navo nyika mukururama uye ndichatonga ndudzi netsvimbo youtare. Saka ivai netariro huru vana vangu nekuti kuguma kwezvinhu zvose kwava pedyo uye iyi nguva yemakore manomwe yedambudziko guru, kunyange dambudziko raJakobho richateverwa nokutonga kwangu kwakarurama uye Yahweh achava zvose muna zvose.

Zvino ndakaona denga idzva nenyika itsva; nekuti denga rokutanga nenyika yokutanga zvakanga zvapfuura; uye gungwa rakanga risisipo.
