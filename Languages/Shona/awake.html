Chapupu Chiviri: Kereke
Mumabasa aJohane Mubhabhatidzi
Kurume 02, 2024

Mukai!

Mukai! Mukai! Mukai! Vanhu vangu, mukai, nekuti ini Ishe Jesu Kristu ndiri kudana vanhu vangu kuti vadzoke kwandiri. Musaramba inzwi raIshe pandinokudanai ndichikupai tsitsi, kuregererwa, nerugare. Munyika muno hamuna rugare; kunyange vachazhamba vachiti rugare nekuchengeteka, musavatenda. Hakuna rugare kunze kwangu, Jesu Kristu. Mukai! Mutsai hama dzenyu! Vazhinji vachawira kuruboshwe nekurudyi, asi hazvizosvika pedyo nemi kana mukaramba makatendeka kuinzwi rangu, inzwi raIshe.

Mwenga wangu achatorwa kuenda kumakamuri emukati medenga murugare nekuchengeteka, sezvo vakasuka nguvo dzavo muropa rangu, ropa reGwayana! Avo vanondiramba uye vachiona chibayiro chaJesu Kristu, Pasika yakakwana, chezvivi zvevanhu sechinhu chisina maturo havazovi nerugare. Tendevukai mufuratire zvivi zvenyu, sezvo ino inguva yekupedzisira. Musazvipa zvakare mukuzvipa zvikonzero nekusatenda, nekuti hakuna munhu anogona kumira pamberi pangu, Jesu Kristu, kunze kwekunge asuka nguvo dzake muropa regwayana uye azvinatsa pazvivi.

Budai pakati paro, vanhu vangu, sezvo hure guru richatongwa pakarepo uye pasina mushonga. Nguva yekutambudzika kwaJakobho yave pedyo, uye kutonga kukuru nenjodzi zvichauya panyika. Mune nzira yekupukunyuka pamiromo yenyu chaiyo kana mukandipupura, Jesu weNazareta, saIshe wenyu uye mukatenda kuti Mwari akandimutsa kubva kuvakafa. Zvivi zvenyu zvakakosha zvikuru kwamuri here? Mufaro wechivi wakakosha zvekuti mungaisa upenyu husingaperi murima rekunze panjodzi here? Munoshuvira dziva remoto here? Danai kuna Ishe ndichiri kuwanikwa, uye mudane kwandiri pandichakunzwai, nekuti nguva yoswedera apo pasina munhu anogona kushanda.

Kunyange Ishe achazotuma vaporofita vake munguva yekutambudzika kwaJakobho, muchamira kupi kana Ishe atora vanhu vake? Ndiani achakushumirai kana Kereke yabviswa pakati? Kuchave nekushomeka kwemagwaro munyika, uye muchafanira kuwana Kristu nekutsungirira kusvika pakufema kwenyu kwekupedzisira, kana makaropafadzwa zvekukwanisa kusvika ipapo. Tendevukai mudzokere kuna Ishe! Budai mariri, vanhu vangu, muve vakaparadzana. Ndiri pamusuwo ndichigogodza.

Mweya wehuporofita waburukira pavanhu vangu pasi rese, sezvandakavimbisa mumagwaro matsvene.

Mabasa 2:16-21

Asi ichi ndicho chakataurwa nomuprofita Joere;

Zvino pamazuva okupedzisira, ndizvo zvinotaura Mwari, ndichadurura zvoMweya wangu pamusoro penyama yose; vanakomana venyu navanasikana venyu vachaporofita, majaya enyu achaona zviratidzo, navatana venyu vacharota hope.

Zvirokwazvo, pamusoro pavaranda vangu napamusoro pavarandakadzi vangu, pamazuva iwayo ndichadurura zvoMweya wangu, uye vachaporofita. Ndicharatidza zvishamiso kudenga kumusoro, nezviratidzo panyika pasi, ropa, nomoto, noutsi hunopfungaira. Zuva richashandurwa rikava rima, nomwedzi ukava ropa, zuva guru raIshe rine mbiri risati rasvika. Zvino zvichaitika kuti ani naani anodana kuzita raIshe achaponeswa.
