Zvinovava Zvinotapira
3 Mbudzi, 2024

Haiwa Israeri, NDIRI Kukushevedza!

Kubva kuna Yahweh, Mwari Mukuru Asingaperi:

Inzwai Mashoko aYahweh, iye mukuru NDIRI uye fungisisai mubvunzo wangu. Sei makonzo achitiza imba iri kuwa, vana vangu? Mhinduro iri pachena ndeyekuponesa upenyu hwawo, kunze kwemakonzo ari kutiza Bhabhironi Rakavanzika anoita izvozvo kuti avanze mhosva dzawo, uye kuti agogona kuipamba zvizere kuti awane kutonga kupfupi kwandinovapa. Sezvo munhu wenyama achida rima, zvino ndichamupa rima raanoda zvikuru. Asi, ndakanyora (uye ndichiri kunyora) chiito chese chechisikwa chese muchisiko changu chine rusununguko rwekusarudza nekuti kune uyo anopiwa zvakawanda, zvakawanda zvinodiwa kwaari.

Uye hapana chakavanzika pameso angu, uye hapana chakavanzika pakuona kwangu, uye NDIRI iye anoona, anonyora, uye anorangarira chiito CHESE chakaipa chakamboitwa muchisiko changu. Uye hapana chinogona kuitika chandisingazivi uye chandisingabvumiri! Pamberi pangu, pfungwa dzose dzakafukurwa. Pamberi pangu, vavariro dzose dzemwoyo dzakafukurwa uye hapana kana chinhu kana munhu akasikwa kana kuitwa asiri pasi peruoko rwangu rune simba, nekuti ndiri Mwari Asingaperi wekururama wamunofanira kuzvidavirira kwaari, haiwa Israeri, uye ndinokuonai zvizere, kunyange muchisarudza kundisateerera muhupofu hwechikamu nekuda kwekuoma kwemwoyo yenyu.

Nekuti, pandakauya kwamuri mumunhu waMesiya mumwe chete wandakambotuma uye Mesiya mumwe chete wandichazotuma, ndakarambwa zvizere naMadzibaba enyu. Nekuti, NDIRI iye akasika Yeshua HaMashiach munyama kuti aponese vose vanodana pazita rake rine simba, iye wamunoramba mukusaziva, kunyange nekuzvikudza nekumhura muchimuti chidhori chevahedheni. Haiwa Israeri, vazhinji venyu makazvibatanidza neSinagogi raSatani uye zvino mave kumirira nemwoyo wose Moshiach wenyu wenhema uyo Esau akagadzirira kuurayiwa kwaJakobho.

Saka inzwai izwi raiye MUKURU NDIRI nekuti izwi rangu guru, risingaperi riri kukushevedzai munguva ino yekupedzisira uye ndinotuma Mwanakomana wangu kwamuri zvakare kamwe chete kuti adzikinure Jakobho nevakasara vanogamuchira Mwanakomana wangu Asingaperi, Yeshua HaMashiach, vachaponeswa kunyange neruoko rwake rune simba uye hapana imwe sarudzo yeruponeso. Uye hakuzombovi neimwe sarudzo kana zita rakapihwa rekuponeswa nekuti ndakamupa zita riri pamusoro pemazita ose, kuti pazita raJesu weNazareta mabvi ose apfugame. Uye avo vanoramba, vachamanikidzwa kupfugamira Shumba yeRudzi rwaJudha yandiri kutuma panyika kuti itonge nekuita hondo kunyange mukururama kusingaperi, uye ndiye achaisa chisiko changu chose pasi pekutonga kwakarurama uye achatonga netsvimbo yesimbi uye kutonga, nyasha, netsitsi zvinaye nekusingaperi. Nzwisisai kuti nzira inoenda kukuparadzwa yakafara uye mazita mazhinji navamwari venhema vanotsvaka kukuendesai kunhaka yavo yegungwa remoto risingaperi, iro rinopfuta nesarufa, mirazvo inopisa zvikuru yekurangwa.

Haiwa Israeri, makapwanya sei mwoyo waYahweh mukusateerera kwenyu uye mwoyo wangu uchiri kupwanyika pamusoro penyu kubva kusingaperi kwakapfuura kwandaiziva mukuziva kwangu kwekare kuti muchirongwa changu cherudzikinuro Madzibaba enyu aizoramba Jesu weNazareta vachisarudza Bharabhasi, aiva mhondi nemutungamiri wevapanduki munyika dzenguva iyoyo. Saka ndakamuita kuti atendeukire kuvahedheni, uye vakamunzwa kwechinguva, asi upofu hwechikamu hwakaitika kuna Israeri kuti vakasara veIsraeri vagogona kuuya kuruponeso panguva yakakodzera. Nzwisisai kuti vazhinji vekuJudhiya vakamuita Ishe wavo uye vakawana ruponeso rusingaperi muIsraeri yaMwari uye vachava nemwanakomana wangu nekusingaperi. Uye NDIRI iye akatuma Jesu weNazareta pakati pemarudzi kuti adzikinure hama dzenyu dzakapararira nekuda kwekupanduka kwavo, uye kunyange avo vakanga vasina tariro uye vazhinji vekumarudzi vakawana ruponeso rusingaperi uye zvino vakabatanidzwa mumarudzi nekusingaperi. Saka nzwisisai haiwa Israeri kuti izvo zvakachenurwa naiye Asingaperi NDIRI, musazviti zvakajairika kana zvisina kuchena!

Asi imi haiwa Israeri, makaramba nekuuraya mwanakomana wangu Jesu Kristu uye mukamuisa mumaoko evanhu vane zvivi vakanga vakatsunga kumuregedza mukusava nemhosva kwake. Asi chirongwa changu cheruponeso rusingaperi chaigona sei kuitika kunze kwekunge zvinodiwa nemitemo isingaperi uye isingashanduki yaYahweh zvazadzikiswa, iyo mitemo yandakagadza kuti hurongwa hwakarurama hwechisiko changu chose hutongwe mukururamisira kwakakwana. Saka Jesu weNazareta akazvipa seGwayana raMwari seMupristi wenyu Mukuru asingaperi nekusingaperi zvichienderana neurongwa hwaMerkizedeki kuti abhadhare murango wakatemwa naMosesi wezvivi nekudarika kwevanhu uye kuti adzikinure vanhu zvizere kubva kutuko yemutemo. Nekuti Mosesi akauya semudzidzisi wechikoro waIsraeri kuti atore nekuchengetedza Israeri kuna Yeshua HaMashiach uye akabhadhara zvizere rudzikinuro rwenyu, uye kunyange muri vadzidzi vanopanduka, vakasara vachadaira kuranga kwangu, kunyange dambudziko riri kuuya kwamuri mukati mevhiki rechimakumi manomwe raDhanieri riri pamikova yenyu uye richakurumidza kukwikwidza kuvhura mikova yenyu kusarudzo isingaperi.

Saka nzwisisai wamakaramba, haiwa Israeri, wandinoda nerudo rusingaperi, nekuti ndimi avo vandakapikira Madzibaba enyu muzvibvumirano zvizhinji mumazera akapfuura kuti munguva idzi dzekutonga ndaizokuchengetedzai nekuda kwavo, uye ndiri Mwari Asingaperi weKururama mumwe chete asingashanduki! Uye NDIRI IYE anorangarira kureverera kwakawanda kwakaitwa naMadzibaba enyu akamiririra imi uye kureverera kwese ndinorangarira shoko neshoko uye ndichakudza chikumbiro chavo kune avo venyu vanoda uye vanoteerera. Uye kunyange zvino Mwanakomana wangu, wamunovenga mukusaziva, anokureverererai murudo rwake rusingaperi kuhama dzake nekuti Yeshua HaMashiach ndiye mudzikinuri wehama waIsraeri, iye chaiye akatenga nyika neropa rake rekuyananisa uye akakudzikinurai kubva mumaoko emuvengi kana mukasarudza kugamuchira. Kunyange zvakadaro, vazhinji pakati penyu vakagara nhaka rudo rwenyu rweIjipita kubva kuna Madzibaba enyu, musazvibvumira kuwa nekusingaperi murenje revasingatendi.

Nekuti Yeshua HaMashiach ndiye achazoponesa vakasara kubva kuna Jakobho vachauya kwaari zvizere, kunyange panguva yemiedzo yevhiki rechimakumi manomwe raDhanieri. Saka nzwisisai nguva dzamunorarama uye mukai kubva muhupofu hwenyu hwechikamu pamurairo wangu pamunotendeukira kuna Yeshua HaMashiach, wandakatumira ruponeso rwenyu saMesiya wenyu (pamusingazove mapofu nekuoma kwemwoyo yenyu). Nekuti muchatendeukira kumwanakomana wekururama uye achakugamuchirai kwaari, uye achakuendesai kunzvimbo yakachengeteka uye achakuvanzai kubva mukuparadzwa kwenyu kubva mumaoko aMoshiach wenhema waEsau.

Saka nzwisisai uyo wamunofanira kuzvidavirira kwaari nekuti NDIRI Mwari wezvibvumirano zvizhinji, uye NDIRI Mwari wezvipikirwa zvizhinji, uye NDIRI Mwari Asingaperi weKururama anouya zvakare kamwe chete kuzoshevedza vanhu vangu kuti vadzokere kumufudzi wavo mukuru wandakatumira ruponeso rwaAdhamu. Uye kune mativi mazhinji ekuti ndiri ani, uye zita rega rega rine revo yaro maererano naMwari Asingaperi. Saka ndinzwirei uye ndinzwirei zvakanaka uye nzwisisai Yahweh, ari kukushevedzai kwandiri kuti mugogona kuwana ruponeso. Uye saka ndinokuyeuchidzai nekukudzidzisai se vacheche, asi ndimi chaizvo munofanira kunge muri vadzidzisi, haiwa Israeri! Saka dzidzai nezvaMwari wenyu Asingaperi weKururama!

Uye mamwe mazita mazhinji neunhu ndinazvo, saka inzwai izwi rangu rinodzvova semabhanan'ana kumatenga, haiwa Israeri, nekuti NDIRI Mwari waMadzibaba enyu wamunonamata zvenhema mukusindimara.

NDIRI Mwari waAbrahama wamakasiya!

NDIRI Mwari waJakobho kunyange wamakasiya kuti muende mumaoko emumwe, uye makamhanyira kuna vamwari venyu venhema kunyange muchidada nekupanduka kwenyu. Nzwisisai Israeri kuti muri mumaoko eSinagogi raSatani kunyange avo vanoti ndevaIsraeri vanoreva nhema uye vasiri.

Inzwai izwi rangu haiwa Israeri, uye uyai kuna Yeshua HaMashiach munguva ino yekupedzisira nekuti ndiri kukushevedzai murudo rwangu kwamuri uye ndinotambanudza nyasha dzangu netsitsi dzangu kwamuri panguva ino. Asi imi muri rudzi rwakashinga uye kusindimara nekuoma mwoyo kwaMadzibaba enyu makazviita zvenyu, uye makaramba Mwari waMadzibaba enyu wamunonamata nenzira isina kudzidzwa uye yenhema nekuti vanhu vangu vanoparadzwa nekushaya ruzivo, saka uyai kuna Yeshua HaMashiach mudzidze nezvake kuti mugogona kuwana ruponeso paruoko rwake rune simba.

Haiwa Israeri, mwoyo wangu unopwanyika sei pamusoro penyu nekuti ndakakutorai semuti munyoro murenje risina chinhu wagadzirira kuparara nekuoma nekusingaperi ndikakupai upenyu ndikakuitai vanhu vakakosha kwandiri. Ndakapinda muzvibvumirano zvizhinji naMadzibaba enyu, kunyange zvibvumirano zvavakakundikana kuchengeta nekuda kwechivi nehuori zviri muropa raAdhamu. Asi NDIRI Ishe Mwari wenyu anorangarira uye akachengeta zvipikirwa zvangu nezvibvumirano uye ndichazozadzisa chipikirwa chese nechibvumirano chakamboitwa kuvanhu kunyange pamberi paYeshua HaMashiach, anova mwanakomana wangu anodiwa Jesu Kristu.

Haiwa iwe Israeri, ucharamba usina kutendeka uye usingateereri here? Ucharamba wakabatana nevavengi vako mukurangwa kwavo kusingaperi here? Uye ndizvo zvazviri kuti wanga usingateereri uye wakafamba muupofu hwechikamu, uye ndakatora rudzi rwako kubva kwauri uye hauchatungamirirwi naYahweh Mwari Mukuru Asingaperi. Asi mukupanduka kwako unotungamirirwa neruoko rwevavengi vako vanonyepedzera nekuzvishandura kuti ndiJakobho asi muchokwadi vari Esau. Nzwisisai kuti vose vanoramba Yeshua HaMashiach vakafuratira Yahweh vakananga kuna vanamwari venhema nokusatenda uye vazhinji vakabatwa nousingaoneki uye vakasungwa kumakuva avo matema avanonamata mukusaziva mukusatenda. Saka nzwisisai, vose vanotsauswa naMoshiach wavo wenhema kana Mahdi wavo wenhema muzvitendero zvavo zvenhema zvisina kutendeka zvisingatungamiriri kuna Yahweh kana kuupenyu husingaperi, vachawana kuparadzwa kusingaperi kune vose vakasarudza kuramba varimo.

Haiwa iwe Israeri, huya kwandiri kuburikidza nemwanakomana wangu Yeshua HaMashiach uyo ariwo Jesu weNazareta, waunoramba sezvakaita Madzibaba ako pamberi pako. Asi usaparara muzvivi zvaMadzibaba ako uye gamuchira rudo rwangu nechibayiro chandakapa nokuda kwako, kunyange nechibayiro ichocho vose vekwaAdhamu vakaropafadzwa uye vazhinji vanobva kunyika dzenyika uye kumarudzi vakauya kuna Ishe wavo mukutendeuka uye vakaropafadzwa uye vakadzikinurwa nokusingaperi-peri. Uye nzwisisa kuti NDINI iye akaisa zvinhu zvose muna Kristu Jesu ane simba akamuka, uye maoko akashandurwa nokuti NDINI iye akamuita mweya unopa upenyu uye kutonga kwake noumambo hwake hakungavi nomugumo!

Naizvozvo, sungano dzangu dzose kubvira kuna Adhamu kusvika kuna Mosesi, uye kunyange Testamende yaMosesi zvino yagadzirirwa kuzadzikiswa pachiso chaJesu Kristu neSungano yangu Itsva inova Testamende yangu Itsva yakapiwa kuzadzikiswa kwezvinhu zvose kunyange kubvira kuna Adhamu kusvika kuna Kristu. Nokuti avo vanowana upenyu husingaperi vanofanira kuzviisa pasi uye kupfugamira mwanakomana wangu mumwe oga akaberekwa, wandinogara ndichifadzwa naye. Saka huyai kwandiri pachiso chaYeshua HaMashiach uyo muropa rake risina chivi akasimbisa Sungano yake Itsva, inova Testamende Itsva, yandakagamuchira zvizere uye ndakaronga nheyo dzenyika dzisati dzavambwa. Uye ndichatuma 144,000 vangu, vachitungamirirwa naYeshua HaMashiach, kuti mupone uye vachakuvigai panzvimbo yandakakugadzirirai uye imomo muchachengetwa uye muchaponeswa muri vapenyu kubva kuna Esau uyo ari kugadzirira kuparadzwa kwenyu.

Asi Esau, anodzvinyirira vanhu vangu, ari kusiya Bhabhironi Rakavanzika nokuti vanoda kufukidza zvivi zvavo. Chivi icho makonzo anosiya imba iri kuwa asina mhosva nacho. Naizvozvo, vachaedza kuviga mhosva dzavo uye kunyange mafoshoro avo ezvivi avakashandisa kufukidza mhosva dzavo uye ndichavaratidza, anodaro Ishe weHondo. Nokuti vakapamba zvizere Bhabhironi Rakavanzika kunyange sezvandakabvumira kuti zviitike muchikamu changu chepiri chekutonga pamusoro pehure guru iroro! Uye saka vakazviunganidzira simba rose rehondo reBhabhironi Rakavanzika uye vacharamba vachipamba kusvikira Bhabhironi Rakavanzika raparadzwa munguva pfupi muawa imwe uye harichazomukizve. Nzwisisai kuti hapana chombo chinoumbwa kana kugadzirwa pamusoro penyu chichabudirira kana mukadana Mesia wenyu wechokwadi, kunyange Yeshua HaMashiach!

Saka nzwisisai kuti Sinagogi raSatani rine chimiro cheumwari pakuonekwa kwavo kwekunze, asi ivo ndevababa vavo dhiabhorosi uye kuchiva kwababa vavo vachaita nokuti Esau akaodza vana vake nokusingaperi-peri. Masora aya akaipa, kunyange vatungamiriri venyu vanoita sevakarurama masora akaipa uye vazhinji vanoshinha nevana vanobata vadiki uye vakatsauka muzvivi zveSodhoma neGomora, uye vanobata vana, uye havasi vafudzi vane hunhu asi vane gwembe. Uye shato inova Baba vavo ndiyo inovutungamirira, kunyange iye akapa vafudzi vaviri vane gwembe kuti vatonga nokufudza nyika mukusarurama. Kunyange chikara chinobva mugungwa chichamuka saMahdi wavo uye chikara chinobva panyika, uyo ariwo Moshiach wavo wenhema uye achauya kuzotonga nyika uye zvirokwazvo vachatonga nyika mukusarurama.

Uye tsvina yegwembe ravo inosvibisa munhu wavo wose, uye ruponeso havangambowani. Nokuti vose vanozvishandura pachavo nenzira yakadaro kana kuti vakaberekwa nevekudenga vakawa havana ruponeso nokuti vakaitwa nomufananidzo weshato uye nomufananidzo iwoyo ndizvo zvavari chose. Izvo zvakakombama hazvigoni kururamiswa, uye zvinonyangadza zvose zvevakawa zvinogona kuita zvakaipa chete nokuti zvakaitirwa kuti zviunze kuparadzwa kwevanhu, uye zvingadai zvakabudirira dai ndisina kuzvimisa mumazuva aNoa.

Asi vachamukazve, nokuti ndinounza hofori kuti dzizadzise hasha dzangu! Uye Sinagogi raSatani riri kushanda kufukidza zviratidzo zvose zvekuti ndivanaani chaizvo njodzi isati yarova uye kunyengera kwavo kunoguma nenjodzi nokuti vakawa vanobvumirwa kuparadza Bhabhironi Rakavanzika. Asi chivi chose chichapiwa zvizere muzvipfuva zvavo uye naizvozvo, hazvibviri kuvanzika kuna Yahweh nomwanakomana wangu Yeshua HaMashiach uye hasha dzedu dzichavawana kunyange vachiedza kuzvivanza mumwoyo wenyika. Hakuna ani naani ari pasi pekutonga kukuru anogona kupukunyuka dziva remoto, kunze kweavo vakanyorwa mubhuku reupenyu regwayana uye hakuna anogona kupukunyuka zvivi zvake kunze kweavo ruponeso rwakaitirwa kunyange kune avo vanogamuchira chibvumirano changu cherugare. Hazvinei kuti nyika ngani dzavanoparadza uye hazvinei kuti vanozvivanza kure zvakadini panyika, havangapukunyuki kutsiva nehasha dzavakazviunganidzira uye ndichavadzorera zvizere anodaro Mwari Asingaperi.

Nokuti ndichashandura nyika kubva pakurongeka kwayo kwazvino kuenda kukubwinya kwayo kwekutanga, uye vakadzikinurwa venyika vachafamba imomo vakachengeteka. Uye saka izvo zviripo, zvichashandurwa kuva izvo zvichavepo, uye havangapukunyuki, kunyange vose vakasiiwa kuti vatongwe uko kuchaitika njodzi zhinji nenjodzi, uye aizi ichanyunguduka, uye nyika dzichashanduka, uye nyika ichadzorerwa kukubwinya kwayo kwekare. Kubwinya uko vakawa vakashandura kuva uori kuti zvienderane nokuchiva kwavo.

Uye ini ndiri Yahweh, Mwari Mukuru Asingaperi, kunyange iye akasika matenga nenyika, kunyange iye aripo kubvira narini kusvikira narini, uye nokuda kwangu pachangu nezano rangu ndakataura kuti zvinhu zviripo zviitike kunyange kubva kune izvo zvisipo. Saka inzwa inzwi rangu, haiwa iwe Israeri, uye kunyange imi mose munotsvaka kufuratira Mashoko angu mukusindimara kwemwoyo wakaoma. Kutonga kwava kuda kuuya zvizere panyika uye ndichafumura Esau wose kuti ndivanaani chaizvo, uye ndichafumura zvizere Madzibaba avo avakasungirirwa nokusingaperi kukuchiva kwavo.

Tarirai! Vana vaEsau vanozviti vakarurama, vachifunga mukusaziva kuti ndivo vanhu vangu vakasarudzwa, uye Moshiach wavo wenhema, kunyange runyanga rudiki irworwo, achati iye, pachake, ndiMwari! (Hakuna akafanana naYahweh, Mwari Mukuru Asingaperi uye Wemasimbaose!) Uye vakawa havagoni kuzvinunura kubva mumaoko angu ane simba rose, uye Yeshua HaMashiach achapwanya musoro waSatani zvachose sezvandakavimbisa Adhamu. Hakuna nzvimbo dzakadzika panyika kwavanogona kuzvivanza, kunyange vachiedza, uye zvino vakasungirirwa panyika kunyange pasi pedenga ravasingagoni kupukunyuka. Hakuna humwe hunhu hwavangatora kwandisingazovaoni kuti ndivanaani chaizvo. Hakuna zvimiro zvavanogona kutora kwandisingavazivi uye ndisingavaoni zvizere, kusvika kumwoyo yavo yakasvibiswa. Uye mumwe nomumwe wevakawa kubvira kumuduku kusvika kumukuru uye vose vanotevera nzira dzavo dzakaipa vachava nenhaka yavo isingaperi mumirazvo iyoyo yokurangwa mudziva remoto rinogara richipfuta umo honye yavo isingafi.

Saka chemai, haiwa iwe Israeri, nokuda kweavo venyu vanoramba zvakaoma kukoka kwangu kuupenyu husingaperi, kunyange vose vachasarudza kutevera vakawa uye vose vachazvibatanidza neshato uye vose vari vekwaAdhamu vaiva nesarudzo yoruponeso asi vakasarudza zvivi zvavo zvichavatungamirira kurufu rwechipiri kana vakasadana pazita raYeshua HaMashiach kuti vaponeswe.

Saka ndichatsanangura sarudzo dzamava nadzo. Munogona kugamuchira Mesia wenyu wechokwadi, Yeshua HaMashiach wandakatumira, uye muchaponeswa mumubairo usingaperi nokubwinya, uko miedzo yenyu nokutambudzwa kwenyu hazvina kukodzera kuenzaniswa. Kana kuti kana munhu upi noupi akaramba achienderera mberi kana kubatana neshato muhurongwa hwavo hutsva, muchange maramba upenyu husingaperi uye panzvimbo pazvo muchange magamuchira zvizere kutongwa kwenyu nokuda kwezvivi zvenyama, uye muchatakura zvakarurama kutongwa kwenyu pamakakumbirwa kuti mupukunyuke nokuteerera, uko muchange maramba.

Ani naani anotora mucherechedzo wechikara, achange achinja nokusingaperi mufananidzo waYahweh waakasikwa nawo, zvichivasiya mumamiriro asingagoni kudzikinurwa. Naizvozvo, ndinokukumbirai kuti musarudze upenyu kuti mugare nokusingaperi murudo nemakomborero emusiki wenyu anoratidzwa zvizere pachiso chaJesu Kristu, kunyange Yeshua HaMashiach! Zvikasadaro, muchabatana nevakawa munhaka yavo yesarufa inopfuta mudziva remoto risingaperi, umo kurangwa nokudzorera zvivi ndiko kusingaperi kwoga kwamuchaziva.

Nzwisisai, kuti chinangwa changu hachisi chekuva nehutsinye kana kusarurama kuchisikwa changu, asi mitemo yangu yokururamisira haichinji uye haigoni kuchinjwa. Uye saka chisikwa chinogomera uye chinochema kwandiri nesimba pasi pehuremu hwezvivi zvevakawa nezvivi zvevanhu, uye nguva yokuchenesa nokunatsa nyika mukutonga kubva kune zvakaipa yasvika; kunyange mbeu dzekupanduka dzichasara asi umambo hwevavengi hahuchazomukizve. Kunyange Satani achaedza kurwisa kwake kwekupedzisira mumwaka wake mudiki asati akandwa nokusingaperi mumoto nokuti haasi mambo, asi chigaro chake chichava mirazvo isingadzimiki youtsvene hunopisa zvikuru, uye havangavi netsitsi.

Nokuti mitemo yangu isingaperi inotii kune vasingateereri, saka rangarirai kuti mubairo wechivi rufu, uye saka Adhamu akatadza uye akagumisa upenyu hwake mumubairo wechivi, unova rufu. Asi zvipiriso zvinopiswa nezvibayiro zvekare zvakanga zvisina kukwana kuti zvidzikinure zvizere mwanakomana wangu akarasika, uye saka murume akakwana asina chivi ndicho chibayiro chega chinogamuchirwa nokuti Adhamu aiva akakwana uye asina chivi paakatadza.

Saka ini, kunyange Yahweh Mukuru NDINI, ndakatumira Yeshua HaMashiach sezvakavimbiswa segwayana raMwari rakadeura ropa raro risina chivi nokuzvidira kuti ribhadhare murango waMosesi nokudeura ropa raro risina chivi mukurwadziwa kukuru kuti vanhu vadzikinurwe kubva pakutukwa kwomurayiro, kunyange vose vanosarudza kuva chisikwa chitsva muna Kristu Jesu nokusingaperi-peri nokuti uyu Kristu Jesu ndiye Yeshua HaMashiach!

NDINI Yahweh, Mwari Mukuru Asingaperi, uye NDINI ZVANDIRI.

NDINI iye akatuma Jesu Kristu, kunyange Yeshua HaMashiach, kuti aponese chisikwa changu saka munzwei uye muteerere Mashoko ake sezvakarayirwa naMosesi.

NDINI iye akatuma Mashoko aya nenhume yangu nomuranda wangu uyo mwanakomana wangu Kristu Jesu akatuma uye akamutsa nokuda kwechinangwa chakadaro nomurayiro wangu nokuti Yeshua HaMashiach anogara achiita kuda kwaBaba vake uye isu tiri vamwe mukubatana nechinangwa.

NDINI Mwari Mupenyu anokudanai, haiwa iwe Israeri, kunyange kuitira kuti vose vakagadzirirwa upenyu husingaperi vauye pakuchengeteka kwomufudzi wavo mukuru.

Saka inzwai mashoko angu haiwa iwe Israeri uye huyai kumwanakomana wangu mukutendeuka kuzere uye mugamuchire chibayiro chake, nokuti zvirokwazvo kana mukaramba kudanwa kwake hakuchina chimwe chibayiro chechivi asi kutongwa kusingaperi nokuti muchange makasarudza nokuzvidira uye nokusingaperi zvakaipa panzvimbo pokururama, uye ndichapa vose vanoita sarudzo yakadaro migumisiro yakazara yesarudzo yavo, asi nzwisisai kuti vose vakasununguka kusarudza.

NDINI iye achaita zvinhu zvose zvitsva!
