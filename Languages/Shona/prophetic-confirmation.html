Chisimbiso cheChiporofita naJ. Leland Earls

Inzwai mashoko angu, vanhu vangu; teererai izvo zvandinozivisa kwamuri panguva ino. Ko handisi kuuya kwamuri iye zvino senhumwa ine simba kuti ndikuzarurirei izvo zvakanga zvakavanzwa kwemazera nemazera? Nokuti kunyange iye zvino ndinoda kuzarurira avo vane nzeve dzokunzwa izvo zvandakagadzirira muviri wangu munguva dzino dzokupedzisira. Munofunga kuti ndichakusiyai musina nhungamiro neruzivo rwezvinangwa zvangu kwamuri munguva dzino dzokupedzisira here? Munofunga kuti handikwanisi kusarudza nhumwa dzakakosha kuti kuburikidza nadzo ndizarure shoko rangu nechinangwa changu kwamuri pakuguma kwezvinhu zvose here? Inzwai mashoko angu: Zvekare, ndinoti, teererai izvo zvandinotaura kwamuri panguva ino. Nokuti zvino ndine kabuku kadiki mumaoko angu, uye ndiri kuzivisa zviri mukati mebhuku iri kuvaranda vangu vandakasarudza.

Uye rinotii? Rinoti izvi. Vanhu vangu, ndiri kuuya kwamuri neshoko rangu renguva yekupedzisira nezvinangwa zvaro, zvichava kuguma kwezvose zvandakakugadzirirai kunyange kwemazera mazhinji nemakore. Uye ndichaita kuti mupinde mukuzara kweizvo zvandakagadzirira, kunyange kuzara kweizvo zvandakapedzisa pamuchinjikwa pandakadurura ropa rangu rehupenyu nokuda kwenyu, kuti ndisimbise Sungano Isingaperi kwamuri, kunyange sungano yekukwana kuvanhu vangu; sungano inosanganisira kuzara kwese kwekubwinya kwangu nesimba rangu kune avo vakagadzirirwa zvakanyanya kuti vagamuchire.

Nokuti ndinoti kwamuri vashoma, hongu, vakasara, vachagadzirirwa kugamuchira zaruriro iyi yenguva yekupedzisira nechinangwa chayo, chandiri kuzivisa zvino. Nokuti ine simba rakanyanya zvekuti haigoni kugamuchirwa kunze kweboka rakagadzirirwa, kunyange "kaboka kadiki," kachagamuchira kuzara kwesungano. Nokuti muchafamba mumazuva ano okupedzisira mukuzara kweizvo zvandakakugadzirirai. Nokuti kunyange iye zvino ndiri kukupai zaruriro yeizvo zvakaiswa chisimbiso, zvakasimbiswa, uye zvakaitwa kuti zviwanikwe uye zvigadzirire pandakadurura ropa rangu paKarivhari. Ko hazvina kunyorwa here kuti ndakataura mashoko okuti, "Zvapera"? Chii chakanga chapera? Kuzara kwesungano yangu kwakanga kwapera; kunyange panguva iyoyo kwakanga kwapedzwa zvizere kuti pave nerusununguko ruzere kubva musimba rose remuvengi, uye kupinda kuzere mune zvose zvandakagadzirira uye zvandinokwanisa kupa, kusanganisira kubviswa kwerufu. Hongu, simba rizere rekukunda mabasa ose emuvengi, uye kubwinya kuzere kwakagadzirirwa kubva pakutanga kwenyika kune avo vakagadzirira kugamuchira nekupinda.

Asi hazvina kunyorwawo here kuti vakaganhurira Mutsvene waIsraeri? Vana vangu, INI ndiri mutsvene waIsraeri! Asi vanhu vakandiganhurira. Vakaomesa mwoyo yavo kuti vatende nhema uye nokudaro vakatadza kutora zvizere zvose zvandakagadzirira uye ndikaisa chisimbiso, ndikasimbisa, pamuchinjikwa weKarivhari. Hongu, ndinoti kwamuri zvinhu zvose zvakanga zvagadzirira panguva iyoyo. Asi ndaigona kuwanepi vanhu vakanga vakagadzirira—vakanga vakagadzirirwa kutenda zvizere? Hongu, hapana akawanikwa, kunze kwevakaita saJoshua naKarebhi. Ane nzeve, ngaanzwe! Nokuti kunyange pano ndinoona kuti nzeve dzenyu dzinorema, uye handigoni kukuzarurirai zvizere panguva ino kuti izvi zvinorevei, asi muchazvinzwisisa mazuva asati anyanya kuwanda.

Asi zvino ndichazarura zvakawanda sezvamunokwanisa kugamuchira. Nokuti kabuku kadiki kane zvakawanda zvezuva rino nenguva ino. Asi ndinofanira kuzvizivisa zvishoma nezvishoma, nokuti kunyange iye zvino vazhinji venyu makaita sevacheche vachangobva kurumurwa mukaka uye hamusati magadzirira nyama. Vamwe vachanzwisisa zvakawanda zvezvandinotaura. Saka regai ndikugadzirirei uye ndidzingise mwoyo yenyu zvizere kuti mugamuchire shoko iri renguva yekupedzisira. Nokuti kune uyo anopiwa zvakawanda, zvakawanda zvinodikanwa. Munoda kuziva zviri mukati mekabuku aka here? Zvino gadzirirai mwoyo yenyu kuti mugamuchire. Nokuti zvishoma nezvishoma zvichazarurwa kwamuri sezvamunenge magadzirira kugamuchira.

Nokuti kunyange iye zvino ndiri kugadzirira vanhu vachatora nyika yakagadzirirwa zvizere—vachayambuka mafungu eJorodhani—vachaseka masimba eshato—kunyange iye ane kuuya kune zviratidzo nenhema nezvishamiso uye ane kushanda kwakaita semvura inoyerera yeJorodhani. Funga nezveizvi, vanhu vangu! Ko hazvina kunyorwa here kuti shato ichauya serwizi rukuru rwerufu nekuparadza? Uye shato iyoyo haingauyi kuzorwisa vanhu vangu nerwizi rwenhema nekunyengera muzuva rino here?

Asi hamungakundwi, nokuti ndichaita kuti mvura idzivirirwe pamunenge muchiyambuka neruoko rwangu rune simba kuti mutore nyika yamakagadzirirwa. Nokuti zvirokwazvo, iyi inyika yakanaka. Uye vanhu vangu vakadzungaira murenje rekuparadzwa pamweya kwenguva yakareba zvakakwana. Ndinoti kwamuri 'makumi mana ekuedzwa' apera, uye kechipiri vanhu vangu vari kuuya kuNyika Yakapikirwa. Kunyange iye zvino, ndiri kukugadzirirai kuti muyambuke musimba remweya. Teererai uye fungai zvandinotaura kwamuri. Nokuti kabuku kadiki kandinako mumaoko angu kane zvakavanzika zvose nezvakavanzika zvandinoda kuti muzive mumazuva ano.

Haiwa vanhu vangu, handina kumirira neshungu here nguva ino kuti ndikuzarurirei izvo zvakanga zvakavanzwa? Haisi nguva ino here yakamirirwa nenhoroondo yose? Hakusi iko here kwakataurwa nezvako nevaporofita, kunyange ivo pachavo vasina kuzvinzwisisa? Haiwa vanhu vangu, makafunga here nezverukudzo nekubwinya kwamakapiwa kuti murarame munguva ino uye kuti mutore chikamu muushumiri uhu hwenguva yekupedzisira? Haiwa, dai maizomutswa kubva mukuneta kwenyu kuti muone kubwinya kwakagadzirirwa nguva ino. Sei muchiti mumwoyo menyu kuti nguva haisati yasvika? Sei muchimirira uye musingapindi nemwoyo wenyu wose nemweya wenyu wose? Ndinoti kwamuri, ZVINO NDIYO NGUVA YOKUNDITSVAKA NEMWOYO WENYU WOSE, kuti nditaure nemi mukamuri remukati memwoyo wenyu uye ndikuzivisei zano rangu renguva ino. Musateerera avo vanotsvaka kukuodzai mwoyo nekukupofumadzai kune izvo zvandinotaura.

Varipi avo vane ushingi hweKUBVIRA pamusoro pemvura yezvinetso nekuedzwa uye VOFAMBA VAKANANGA KWANDIRI NERUTENDO, vachiziva kuti ndinokwanisa kukutsigirai nekukusimudzai panguva ino nekusingaperi, kuitira kuti mufambe mukukunda pamusoro pemvura yekutambudzika nekukanganiswa, zvichayedza vanhu vose muzuva rino? Fambai, ndinodaro, makatarisa zvakasimba kwandiri, musingatarisi kurudyi kana kuruboshwe; musingatarisi kune avo vakakupoteredzai kana kumvura ine mhirizhonga ingatsvaka kukumedzai.

Nokuti ndinoti kwamuri hamungambosviki pakudanwa nekumutswa kwenguva yekupedzisira kusvikira mada nerutendo kubuda muchikepe chekurerukirwa kwenyu nekuchengeteka kwazvino uye mopinda MURUKOVA RWECHINANGWA CHANGU CHITSVENE, musingaregi mafungu ekusava nechokwadi nekutya achikukundai, kana kurega avo vanosarudza kugara muchikepe vachikudzivisai. Asi UYAI VANHU VANGU, nokuti ndichava neavo vanoshinga kufamba pamusoro pemvura yezvisati zvamboitwa, uye vosangana neni pandinouya kwamuri musimba nekubwinya muzuva rino.

Havana kuona here kuti waive muviri waifamba pamusoro pemvura uye kwete mweya sezvavaifungidzira? Uye Petro, mumuviri wake, haana kufamba pamusoro pemvura here? Saizvozvowo, vanhu vangu, hausi mweya chete asi MUVIRI WENYAMA uchauyiswa kwandiri muzuva rino kuti uite mabasa makuru pamusoro pemvura yekuvhiringidzika nekutambudzika kwevanhu. Hamusi imi here MUVIRI waKristu; hamusi imi here nyama yangu nemapfupa angu? Saka muchava chinhu chimwe neni muzuva rino; saka tichabatanidzwa pamwe chete PAMUSORO pemvura kuti tiende mberi muzuva rino, anodaro Ishe. Uye saizvozvo muchava mumazuva anouya, anodaro Ishe, MUVIRI WEVAKUNDI—avo vakakunda kusvibiswa kwese kwemanzwiro kwenzvimbo dzemvura dzemuvengi uye vari kufamba mukukunda musimba rekukunda.

Vanhu vangu, inzwai mashoko angu! Ko maasidhi ari mudumbu haasi mufananidzo wemabasa angu pakati penyu here? Mungawana chikafu kubva pane chisiri chingwa uye kubva pane chisingagutsi here? Ndinoti kwamuri, mabasa eMweya Wangu anodiwa kuti mugamuchire izvo zvinopa upenyu. Nokuti handikwanisi here kutora izvo zvakakosha kubva mushoko rangu uye ndichiita kuti zvipinde mumuviri wenyu kuitira kuti mugamuchire chikafu chamunoda? Asi sei muchidya izvo zvisiri shoko rangu? Sei muchitsvaka izvo zvamunoziva kuti hazvingagutsi mukati menyu?

Ndinoti kwamuri, kana mukasanditsvaka zvizere uye mukarega Mweya wangu uchipa simba kune izvo zvamunopinza mumiviri yenyu, muchazviwana muri mumamiriro ezvinhu pamweya akafanana neanotsanangurwa pano. Mabasa emweya wangu anodiwa. Zvikasadaro, munenge muchingotora izvo zvakafa uye zvisingaiti chinhu kuunza simba remweya mukati menyu. Naizvozvo, teererai izvo zvamuri kuverenga, nokuti ichokwadi kumufananidzo weizvo zviri kuitika kuvanhu vangu nhasi. Nokuti hazvigoneki, mumamiriro azvino enyika yechechi, kuti ini ndishande neMweya wangu nenzira yekuti Shoko rangu ripe upenyu neutano nerusununguko kune vose vanoshaiwa. Naizvozvo, zvakare, ndinoti, nditsvakei uye regai ndikupei izvo zvinodiwa kuti murarame hupenyu hwemweya hune simba uye huzere, anodaro Ishe Jesu Kristu.
