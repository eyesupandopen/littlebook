Ndichaporesa Kutenda Kwenyu, anodaro Ishe Mukuru!

Tarisai! Ini, kunyange ini, Kristu Jesu Ishe akamuka uye muponesi wenyika, ndine mubvunzo kwamuri, mwenga wangu. Muchandipindura nokutendeka here? Muchakwanisa kundipa mhinduro yakakodzera kunyorwa muchinyorwa chechokwadi here? Saka, ndichakubvunzai: Kana mwanakomana womunhu achiuya, munofunga here, kuti ndichawana kutenda panyika? Zviri pachena kuti uyu mubvunzo usingadi mhinduro, asi dai maifanira kumira pakushevedza kwangu kuti mupindure, mhinduro yenyu yaizova yei? Maizotaura neni nezvekutenda kwenyu uye kuti kwakanaka sei? Maizondiudza nezvepfungwa dzenyu dzakanaka, idzo dzamunofunga noupenzi kuti munodzora nyika nadzo here? Hamuzivi here kuti dzidziso dzinokosha zvikuru mukutenda kwangu, kunyange kutenda kwaJesu Kristu, kunyange dzidziso idzodzo dzinokosha dzakatevedzerwa zvenhema uye dzikatsanangurwa patsva senhema? Asi imi noupenzi munoti: "Ah, Mwari haangatenderi izvozvo zviitike!" Panyaya iyi ndinokubvunzai, munoziva Mwari zvakadii? Muri chipangamazano chake here? Muchamira pamberi morayira Yahweh here? Muchamira pamberi morayira ini, Yeshua HaMashiach, kuti ndiite zvamunoda here? Asi vazhinji venyu vanoedza kuita izvozvo noupenzi, nokuti makanyengerwa nenzira inorwadza zvikuru.

Makatendera vaporofita venhema, kunyange masora chaiwo akadyarwa naSatani mukereke dzake dzenhema (dzisiri dzangu), ivava vaporofita venhema avo hama yangu Judha akataura nezvavo pandakafemera mashoko angu mumwoyo make uye pandakafambisa mweya wake maari mukufemerwa kutsvene: Vane nhamo, nokuti vakafamba munzira yaKaini uye vakamhanyira nokuchiva shure kwokukanganisa kwaBharami kuti vawane mubairo, uye vakaparara mukupikisa kwaKora. Ava venhema vanofamba pachena pakati penyu, uye vazhinji venyu munovatevera, muchifunga kuti muri kunditevera ini, nokuti ivo ndevenhema, uye vanoedza kutevedzera inzwi rangu kuitira kuti vasina ruzivo pakati pemakwai angu vavatevere mukukanganisa kukuru, kwavari. Nokuti chii chimwe chakataurwa naJudha pamusoro peava vasina maturo? Ava makwapa pamitambo yenyu yorudo, pavanodya nemi, vachizvidyisa vasina kutya: makore asina mvura, anotakurwa nemhepo, miti ine michero inosvava, isina michero, yakafa kaviri, yakadzurwa nemidzi. Mafungu anopenga egungwa, anopupuma kunyara kwawo, nyeredzi dzinodzungaira, dzakachengeterwa rima guru nokusingaperi.

Ndinoziva kuti kunyengera kunogutsa vamwe venyu, nokuti vamwe venyu hamundizivi zvechokwadi asi munoedza kunamata Yahweh nokunditevera, Ishe wenyu Jesu Kristu, kuburikidza nedzidziso dzevhangeri rebudiriro. Musakanganise: Vhangeri rebudiriro inhema, uye handina kumbobvira ndazvipa segwayana renyu rePasika kuti muzviunganidzire pfuma: Nokuti kuda mari mudzi wezvakaipa zvose: izvo vamwe pavakazvichiva, vakatsauka pakutenda, uye vakazvibaya nemarwadzo mazhinji. Vazhinji venyu makatevera rimwe remavhangeri mazhinji enhema, asi hakuna rimwe vhangeri. Vazhinji venyu makatevera mumwe wevaKristu vazhinji venhema, asi hakuna mumwe Kristu kunze kwangu, Yeshua HaMashiach. Ndinotaura kwamuri pachena, makanyengerwa, uye vazhinji venyu makatsinhanisa kutenda kwechokwadi kwaJesu Kristu nokutenda kwenhema. Kutenda kwenhema nokutenda kunoreva nhema uko shoko rokuti kutenda (handina kukupai Shoko rechokwadi roKutenda here?) haricharevi kuvimba, kutenda, nokusimba muna Yahweh, Baba venyu, uye neni, Jesu Kristu Ishe wenyu, asi rakatsanangurwa patsva kusvika pakunyanyisa kusina maturo nengano uye kufunga kwemashiripiti. Makarega vasina maturo vachikurasai, uye kereke dzenyu dzakapindirwa nevanamati vaSatani, masora chaiwo akadyarwa nomuipi pakati penyu.

Kunyanyisa uku kunosvika pakufunga kwakanaka nokusina kunaka, uye imi noupenzi munofunga kuti chero chamunofungisisa kwenguva yakareba chichaitika, sokunge kuti Mwari akaisa simba rake nenyasha kune avo vane kutenda kwenhema kukuru, kwamunorayira zvinhu nokufunga kwenyu. Hamunzwisisi here kuti vose vanoita zvisina maturo zvakadaro vanobatwa nokuteyewa uye vanobatwa nhapwa nokuchiva kwavo nenhema idzi, kunyange kusungwa kukukundikana, nokuti muri kutevera nzira dzaSatani nokusaziva? Nzwisisai kuti dzaive ngirozi dzakawa, hongu, vanaSatani nevarindi vakabatana navo, vakadzidzisa vanhu uroyi, kutema midzi, zviperengo, nen'anga; uye varume vazhinji vakaipa vakapinda mukereke yangu yeRaodhikiya uye vakatora vazhinji sechakapambwa avo vakatenda nhema dzavo.

Izvi zvakaitika nokuti vazhinji mukereke yangu yeRaodhikiya vanoramba kumira nokurwa mukururama kuitira kuti mukunde zvakaipa nezvakanaka! Regai ndikubatsirei kunzwisisa chokwadi. Hapana nomumwe wenyu ane simba kana kugona kwake, nokuti muunhu hwenyu ndipo pamunozopedzisira maziva kusabatsira kwekuedza kwenyu. Zvino, kazhinji, munonzwisisa kuti kana muchivimba neni, Kristu Jesu, uye muchitevera mirayiridzo yangu munzira yandakakusarudzirai, munovimba neni nesimba rangu uye mune kutenda kuzere mandiri. Nokuti ndicharatidza kwamuri mose nenzira yangu, kana mukaramba munyoro kunzwi rangu uye mukatendeuka pandinokuudzai, kuti mukuedza kwenyu nesimba renyu hamusi chinhu kunze kwetsanga yejecha mugwenga, yakarasika nemadutu, uye handina kufira kununura jecha asi ivhu dzvuku raAdhamu, nokuti ndiri Adhamu wokupedzisira, uye ndiri mweya unopa upenyu. Zvisinei, mandiri Kristu Jesu, mune simba uye hamukundiki, nokuti mune pfuma iyi mumidziyo yevhu, uye ndichapa zvishamiso zvizhinji, zviratidzo, nezvinoshamisa nezvimwe zvakadaro pamaoko enyu kana mune kutenda kwehukama mandiri, Jesu Kristu, panzvimbo pokutenda mamuri. Ndapota nditenderei kuti ndikutungamirirei, uye regai nhema dzevakawa, nokuti nemabasa avo hasha huru dzinounganidzwa pavari pazuva rehasha, apo kuguma kwavo kunopedzwa mumoto nesarufa inopfuta.

Nzwisisai kuti kunyengera uku kwakaitwa sei, nokuti mumaJubhiri achangopfuura makanga mune kujekerwa kwenhema uko avo vanonzi vakuru munharaunda yenyu vakaramba Mwari vachifarira kufunga kwavo kusina maturo. Vakatogadzira chidhori chekufunga panguva yekumukira kwavo; hamuzivi nhoroondo here? Aya matsime asina mvura ane uzivi hwawo hwakaora uye husina maturo akarambidza Mwari uye akadzidzisa zvisizvo kuti Mwari aingova muoni asingaiti chinhu asina basa nevanhu asi anopa zvese ari kure uye anotendera vanhu kuti vazvigadzirise, nenyasha dzaMwari dzichienda kumuroyi ane simba guru pakati pavo. Zvino, nekufamba kwenguva, mweya mizhinji isina kugadzikana, kunyange masora chaiwo aSatani, yakapinda mukereke yangu yeRaodhikiya kuti ipedzise nhema dzayo munguva nemwaka yakakodzera. Vakakupindirai nemashoko akanaka nekutaura kwakanaka uye vakadzidzisa simba rekufunga kwemashiripiti kuMwenga wangu, uye vazhinji pakati peMuviri wangu vakavatevera nokuchiva, nokuti makaro avo akamutswa.

Vanakomana vakaipa vaSatani ava vakasanganisa uroyi mudzidziso dzangu chinyararire uye vakakwanisa kusvibisa Shoko rangu dzvene nembiriso, kunze kwekuti Shoko rangu harigoni kusvibiswa nengano dzavo, nokuti nhema dzavo dzinomira panhema. Hamunzwisisi here kuti mbiriso shoma inovirisa mukanyiwa wose? Hamunzwisisi here kuti mbiriso yaHerodhe nembiriso yevaFarisi hadzisi nzira dzoga dzekuora? Nokuti makaroyiwa nembiriso yeuroyi hwechiKristu! Naizvozvo, TENDEUKAI! Nokuti chii chandakaudza vaKorinde noruoko rwoMuapostora Pauro? Bvisai naizvozvo mbiriso yekare, kuti muve mukanyiwa mutsva, sezvo musina kuviriswa. Nokuti kunyange Kristu Pasika yedu akabayirwa isu. Inzwai Mashoko angu! Saka mhuka dzesango idzi, matsime asina mvura aya, kunyange masora asina maturo uye ane chepfu akaparidza uye akatanga zvikwata zvavo zvenhema zvechiKristu, zvitendero kunyange, vachiba vazhinji veavo vaizovatevera kubva kuKereke yechokwadi, kwandinochengeta Mwenga wangu murudo nenyasha netsitsi, vachivatungamirira kubva kwandiri kuenda kudzimba dzenhema dzekunamatira dzakatsaurirwa pachivande kuna Bhaari, nokuti rudzi rwenyu iBhabhironi Rakavanzika!

Sokunge izvi zvisina kuipa zvakakwana, varoyi vazhinji vane simba uye vakapfuma vakabhadhara mari zhinji uye vakapedza simba rakawanda vachiisa nhema dzavo mumaBhaibheri enhema. Vakaburitsa magwaro eAlexandria, eGnostic, zvichiita kuti Kereke yangu ive nepfungwa mbiri kubva pakutarisa kumwe chete kwemagwaro andakachengetedza kubva kuAndiyoki, kunyange mamwe maBhaibheri ekutarisa uko nhema idzi dzakawedzerwa kumiganhu. Mabhuku aya akazopinzwa muzvikoro zvenyu zvebhaibheri zvakaora uye akava maBhaibheri ekudzidza evarofita venhema ava avo vakaparidza nhema zvenhema kubvira ipapo, uye vachabhadhara zvizere. Kubvira panguva iyoyo, nyika dzenyu dzizere nekereke dzenhema dzinotungamirirwa nemapere anoparadza, uye zvakadaro makwai angu mazhinji anofunga zvisizvo kuti anogona kushamwaridzana nemapere. Kereke dzenyu dzenhema dzimba dzekumhura; dzimba dzekuvaraidza kwavanorumbidza Satani pamberi penyu chaipo, uye imi munoshaya njere dzekuona. Kune madzishe mazhinji pakati peushe hwaDhiyabhorosi, uye vanoimba Ishe, Ishe kwavari, kunze kwekuti kana vachiti kwandiri Ishe, Ishe, ndichati, Ibvai kwandiri, imi vaiti vezvakaipa nokuti handina kumbokuzivai.

Kuita kuti zvinhu zviwedzere kuipa, vakatora vhangeri rebudiriro rinomhura uye vakarishandura zvakare nekupinza psychology yekuzvibatsira, uye chii chandakataura pamusoro penyaya dzakadaro: Chenjerai kuti kurege kuva nomunhu anouya akakuparadzai nouzivi nokunyengera kusina maturo, netsika dzevanhu, nemirairo yenyika, uye kwete naKristu. Kereke dzenhema idzi dzakaora uye hadzina kukosha, uye zhinji dzakavambwa nevavaki vakaipa vakasununguka kubva kwandiri, dombo guru rekona, uye zvivakwa zvavo zvose zvichawira mujecha rinofamba. Ava vavaki vanomhura uye vavezi venhema vematombo nevavaki venhema vezvidhinha vakaramba dombo guru rekona, uye havana chokwadi mavari! Vanototaridza zviratidzo zvavo mudzimba dzenyu dzinonzi dzekunamatira, kwavanokusekai pachena. Inzwai Chirevo chandinacho kwamuri, nokuti ichokwadi uye chichaunza kujekerwa kwechokwadi kumweya yenyu: Munhu asina maturo, munhu akaipa, anofamba nomuromo wakaipa. Anobwaira nemaziso ake, anotaura netsoka dzake, anodzidzisa neminwe yake.

Kusarurama kuri mumwoyo make, anoronga zvakaipa nguva dzose, anodyara kupesana. Uye ndizvo zvavari uye ndizvo zvavanoita pamberi pemaziso enyu, asi hamugoni kuona, nokuti makapofumadzwa mudzidziso dzavo dzenhema nenzira dzakaipa.

Vana vangu, budai pakati pavo muzvitsaure; nokuti Kristu anowirirana papi naBheriari. Musawirirana nemabasa erima asingabereki, uye zvitsaurei zvachose kubva kwavari. Nokuti imi, kereke yangu yeRaodhikiya, kunyange imi munodziya uye hamuna musana, uye idzi hadzisi nzira dzangu zvachose, nokuti ndakadzinga vachinji vemari muTemberi mukushingairira kwangu Yahweh. Dzokerai kwandiri, Jesu Kristu, mufudzi wechokwadi nachiremba wemweya yenyu, uye mudzidze kwandiri. Nokuti kureurura kwakanaka nokufunga kwakanaka kusina maturo hakusi kwangu, asi nhema dzinonyeyerwa kwamuri nevaapostora venhema, kunyange vashumiri vaSatani vakazvishandura kuva vashumiri vekururama. Mhanyirai kwandiri, vana vangu vadiki, nokuti nguva yapera, uye imi nokusaziva munofunga kuti mune nguva yose yenyika, uye mune maminitsi mashoma ekutendeuka nokudzokera kwandiri mukuzvininipisa kuti ndigokugadzirirai nzvimbo patafura yangu uye kuti ndigokuzarurirai mukova kumatenga kuti mupinde mumufaro waIshe wenyu!

Nokuti vazhinji venyu munofunga kuti muchaunganidzwa kwandiri kana muchirarama nhema idzi uye kana muchidya tsvina inodyiswa nemaporofita enhema, vachimhura muzita rangu. Zivai kuti handina kuvatuma uye mashoko avo haasi angu, uye kana musingabvi kwavari, muchafanira kuzviratidza kwandiri mukuedzwa kukuru nekuyedzwa panguva yekutambudzika kwaJakobho, kwauya pamusoro penyika yose uye kwava kuda kusunungurwa! Tarisai kwandiri mumweya wenyu; tarisai, nokuti munogona kunzwa marwadzo ekuzvara mumweya wenyu, nokuti ndiri kukushevedzai kumba uye Mwenga wangu ava kuda kusununguka. Tarisai, ndinosiya nemi tsamba yandakakutumirai, imi vaRaodhikiya vangu, nokuti mumashoko aya munovhenekerwa nzira yekudzokera kwandiri; uye kana mukangoteerera, ndichadzosera mubairo wenyu kwamuri, uye muchadya neni patafura yangu kumatenga kwandakagadzirira Mwenga wangu.

Uye kunotumwa kweungano, iri muRaodhikiya, nyora kuti: Zvanzi na(Ameni) chapupu chakatendeka nechechokwadi, kutanga kwezvisikwa zvaMwari. Ndinoziva mabasa ako, kuti hausi kutonhora kana kupisa. Dai waiva kutonhora kana kupisa. Saka zvino nokuti uri pakati pezvose, uye hausi kutonhora kana kupisa. Ndichakusvipira kubva mumuromo mangu: nokuti wakapfuma uye wakawedzerwa nenhumbi, uye haushayiwi chinhu, uye hauzivi kuti uri murombo uye unonzwisa tsitsi, murombo, bofu uye usina kupfeka. Ndinokuraira kuti utenge kwandiri goridhe rakaedzwa mumoto, kuti uve mupfumi, nenguo chena, kuti upfeke, kuti kushama kwako kunonyadzisa kusaonekwe, uye uzodze maziso ako nemafuta emaziso, kuti uone. Vose vandinoda, ndinovatuka nokuvaranga. Naizvozvo, shingaira, utendeuke. Tarira, ndimire pamukova ndichigogodza. Kana munhu akanzwa inzwi rangu akazarura mukova, ndichapinda kwaari, uye ndichadya naye, naiyewo neni. Uyo anokunda ndichamupa kugara neni pachigaro changu, sezvandakakunda uye ndakagara naBaba vangu, pachigaro chavo.
