Chipupuriro Chiviri: Kereke
Mumabasa aJohane MuZaruri
Chikumi 04, 2024

Tarirai! Kutonga Kunosvika uye Nguva Dzava Zvino!

Nguva dzasvika dzekuzadziswa, imi vadiki vangu, kunyange kuzadziswa kwezvinhu zvese zvine chekuita nevhiki rechimakumi manomwe raDhanieri. Nekuti vhiki rechimakumi manomwe raDhanieri ivhiki remakore apo zuva rega rega riri gore. Vhiki rechimakumi manomwe raDhanieri rava kuda kuzadziswa uye richatanga apo vakatendeka mukereke yangu vanotorwa kuti vasangane neni mumakore. Hwangwa yekupedzisira icharira uye nezwi guru rengirozi huru vakafa muna Kristu vachatanga kumuka, uye imi muri vapenyu uye makasara muchatorwa mumakore pamwe chete navo, uye saizvozvo muchagara neni nekusingaperi, kunyange Kristu Jesu, nekuti ndini MuNazareta akamutswa akafira chivi kamwe chete uye haachafi zvakare. Nekuti umambo hwangu hunobva kusingaperi kusvika kusingaperi, uye umambo hwangu hahuzovi nemagumo, nekuti Yahweh vakazvidudzira saizvozvo.

Apo vakatendeka vangu vanotorwa kuenda kumatenga, saizvozvo anodzivisa achabviswawo, uye saizvozvo kutanga kwevhiki rechimakumi manomwe raDhanieri kuchatanga. Vazhinji venyu munonetsekana nepfungwa yekuti ini, Yeshua HaMashiach, nditore vakatendeka vangu kubva mukutonga kusina kuitirwa ivo, asi ndichakuitai kuti munzwisise, kana mukauya kwandiri nekuzvininipisa kwemwana mudiki, kunyange kusava nemhosva nekuda kuziva kwemudiki. Nekuti imi muri vadiki vangu, kunyange vadiki vangu vanoona negirazi zvisina kujeka uye vanoporofita muchidimbu. Uye saka ndinoti kwamuri, zvininipisei uye mutendeuke kubva mukuzvikudza kwenyu kusina maturo, nekuti icho chivi, uye uyai kwandiri kuti mudzidze nekugamuchira rugwaro rutsvene. Kuramba chero chikamu cherugwaro chivi, nekuti Shoko rega rega raMwari rakachena uye rinofanira kugamuchirwa neruremekedzo rwekuteerera, uye ini, Jesu MuNazareta, ndiri shoko rakaitwa nyama.

Kuunganidzwa kwevatsvene vangu kwandiri mumakore chaive chakavanzika chakavigwa mukati mechakavanzika chikuru chaMwari. Saka chaive chakavanzika mukati mechakavanzika uye saka hachina kuzarurwa kusvikira chazarurwa kuna Muapositori Pauro mutsamba dzake. Kwaari kwakazarurwa chakavanzika chikuru chaMwari, nekuti Pauro aivewo mutariri wezvakavanzika zvaMwari, nekuti ndakamutumira saizvozvo. Uye saka nzwisisai kuti kuramba rugwaro pamusoro pechiitiko ichi kuramba ini uye kuva pakati pemhandara mapenzi.

Uye saka ndichakutsanangurirai zvinhu zvizhinji zvava kuda kuitika, kunyange kuenderera mberi nemabasa andakapa kuna Johane MuZaruri, nekuti uchapupu hwekuzarura kwangu hwakanyorwa kuti huvanze sezvahunozarura. Uye saka zvino ndichakuudzai zvinhu zvisati zvaitika kuitira kuti mugadzirire mazuva ari mberi. Nekuti ini, Kristu Jesu, ndichaita kuti vanhu vangu vamire mumabasa kwandiri kuvatuma kuitira kuti mupedzise zvizere mabasa mazhinji makuru andinayo kuti muite.

Sezvo kuunganidzwa pamwe chete kunoitika, anodzivisa anobviswa, uye kuparadzwa kukuru kuchawira pasi rose, kamwe kamwe uye munguva pfupi. Nekuti sezvo anodzivisa anobviswa, hapana chichasara kuti chidzivise huipi huri munyika uye kuchiva nezvishuwo zvakaipa zvevanhu. Hondo huru ichapararira pasi rose, uye hama ichamukira kuuraya hama yayo zvakare, uye kuparadzwa kwavo kuchava kukuru. Marudzi mazhinji achawa seBhabhironi Rakavanzika, kunyange hure guru iroro rinogara pamusoro pemvura zhinji, richaitwa dongo muawa imwe chete.

Guta guru iroro, kunyange rudzi iroro rweNyika Dzakabatana zhinji, richaparadzwa zvachose; nekuti rakawana chikamu chakapetwa kaviri chekutongwa, uye rakakodzera. Nekuti rakararama zvinonaka muchivi chikuru; rinogara mukuzvikudza kukuru richifunga kuti harimbofi rakawa uye zvinhu zvichaenderera mberi, zuro sezvanhasi, uye mangwana zvakanyanya kufanana uye zvisingachinji. Uye hure guru rakaodza nyika nekapu yegoridhe muruoko rwaro, izere nezvinonyangadza netsvina yehupombwe hwaro. Uye saka kutongwa kwaro kuchawira kamwe kamwe. Moto mukuru uchawira maguta aro sezvo zvombo zvinoputika zvichiwira; kuzununguka kukuru kuchaitika, uye rudzi iroro ruchakamurwa nepakati. Mhenderekedzo dzaro dzichafashamira zvikuru, uye harichazomukizve, nekuti zvitadzo zvaro zvikuru uye kutongwa kwaro kukuru.

Sezvo hondo inopararira munyika uye vatasvi vemabhiza vachiita kutonga kwavo panyika, nhamo huru nekuparadzwa zvichauya panyika. Nyika iripo zvino haichazovipo zvakare; kana anodzivisa abviswa, zvese zvichange zvakasiyana. Pachava nerufu rukuru, denda, nzara huru, nekusaruramisira kukuru; asi 144,000 vangu vachadzivirirwa kubva mukutsamwa nekutongwa, nekuti vachatakura chisimbiso chaMwari Mupenyu, kunyange Yahweh, pahuma dzavo; nekuti saizvozvo vachaiswa chisimbiso, uye ndinotuma ngirozi dzangu kuti dzivaise chisimbiso. Uye saka nyonganyonga huru nekusateerera mutemo zvichatonga zuva. Mwedzi 42 yekutanga ichava nguva dzekutambudzwa kukuru nedambudziko guru, asi kutambudzika kukuru kuchaitika mumwedzi 42 yechipiri: nekuti kune mwedzi 84 mumazuva manomwe emakore edambudziko raJakobho. Uye saka hafu yechipiri ndiyo kutambudzika kukuru, asi kutambudzika nekushungurudzwa panyika zvichange zvichityisa kune vagari vepanyika nguva yese.

Nekuti ini, Kristu Jesu Ishe Akakwidzwa, kana Yahweh, Mwari mukuru wekusingaperi (nekuti hapana akafanana Naye), hatina mufaro mukutonga nematambudziko ari kuuya. Baba vangu havana kusika vanhu murudo kuti vagovatonga nekuvaparadza. Asi, Satani akaodza bindu raMwari renyasha, uye zvino huori huchacheneswa kuitira kuti kururama kutonge. Uye saka hapana imwe sarudzo yeruponeso kune avo vakaramba kushevedzera kwangu kweruponeso munguva yenyasha. Vanhu vanofanira kumuka kubva mukurara kwavo nemafaro echivi, nekuti hapasisina nguva uye zvino ndiyo nguva yekusarudza nekusingaperi, kana kuti sarudzo ichaitwa mukutongwa kune avo vanondiramba nekuramba kusarudza. Matambudziko mazhinji nekutonga kuri kuuya zvichamutsa vazhinji kuruponeso rusingaperi, kunyange kuvabvuta kubva mumoto usingaperi; nekuti vazhinji vakadhakwa nemafaro echivi, uye saka mafaro achabviswa. Havachazovi nemufaro mumazuva ano, uye kurarama kuchava nyaya yeminiti neminiti dzimwe nguva, uye njodzi zhinji dzichawanda.

Saka zvichava kuti vakatendeka vangu nevakasarudzwa pakati peKereke yangu vachavanzwa kubva kunguva dzedambudziko raJakobho kumatenga. Uye mabasa avo achatongwa nemoto, uye mabasa emunhu wese anosara achagamuchira mubairo uye mabasa emunhu wese anopiswa acharasikirwa, asi ivo pachavo vachaponeswa, asi sekunge nemoto. Vachatora nzvimbo dzavo muhurongwa hwakavagadzirirwa kumatenga. Nekuti ini, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndakakodzera kutora bhuku nekuvhura zvisimbiso, uye nekuvhurwa kwezvisimbiso vatasvi vemabhiza vanosunungurwa. Uye vachaenda panyika kunokunda, kunorwa hondo, kukonzera denda nerufu rukuru. Hwangwa nomwe dzicharira, nhamo dziri kuuya, uye ndiro nomwe dzekutsamwa kwehasha dzaMwari dzichadururwa panyika zvizere.

Hondo yenyika iri kuuya haizogari makore, nekuti Satani anoziva kuti ane nguva pfupi, asi hondo ichagara iripo panguva idzi dzedambudziko. Kubva mumatongo nekutyisa kwehondo kuchamuka chikara chinobva mugungwa, nechikara chinobva panyika, anovawo muporofita wenhema. Vaviri ava vachashanda pamwe chete, sezvavanotoshanda pamwe chete muchivande kuparadza Bhabhironi Rakavanzika. Uye nyanga gumi, idzi dzichavenga hure, uye dzichariita dongo, uye risina kupfeka, uye dzichadya nyama yaro, uye dzicharipisa nemoto. Nekuti Mwari akaisa mumwoyo yavo kuti vazadze kuda kwake, uye kuti vabvumirane, uye vape umambo hwavo kuchikara, kusvikira mashoko aMwari azadziswa. (Uye saka vatasvi vemabhiza vachatasva.)

Vachamuka vachipa rugare, kuchengeteka, nehumwari hwenhema. Vacharambidza chero chinhu chine chekuita neni, Jesu Kristu, pasi rose, uye vacharwa hondo nevatsvene uye vovakunda, nekuti zvakapihwa mumaoko avo kuti vazviite. Vanozvidaidza kuti vatorwa vachazviratidza uye vachapa rubatsiro rwavo kuvanhu, asi idzi ingirozi dzakawa dzinounza kunyengera kukuru. Tarirai, ndinounza hofori kuti dzizadze hasha dzangu, uye saizvozvo Yahweh vakazvidudzira. Sezvakanga zvakaita mumazuva aNoa, saizvozvowo zvichaitika kungirozi dzakawa dzekare; kunyange vanonzi vamwari vekare (havasi vamwari) vachatsvaga kuvakazve zera ravo regoridhe panyika, uye vakasunungurirwa kutongwa, asi hapana zera regoridhe ravachava naro. Zvinonyangadza zvavo zvichasunungurwa, nekuti anodzivisa achange aenda uye abviswa zvachose, uye hapana chichavadzivisa. Avo vanoda hupenyu hwavo vachahurasikirwa nekusingaperi, nekuti vachada chiratidzo kuti vatore chikamu munharaunda yavo itsva, kunyange hurongwa hwavo hutsva. Uye vese vanotora chiratidzo chechikara vachava nechikamu chavo mugungwa remoto.

144,000 vangu havana kutumirwa kunogara mumaguta avo, kwavaizofanira kutora chiratidzo kuti vararame. Asi vachatumwa kuno nekoko uye vachaunganidza vazhinji kwavari uye vochengeta vazhinji uye vachabatsira kuunza mapoka makuru muruponeso kwandiri, Yeshua HaMashiach (mune chero mutauro): vese vachadana zita rangu kusvika kumagumo vachaponeswa. Mwari achapukuta misodzi yese pamaziso avo; uye hakuchazovi nerufu, kana kushungurudzika, kana kuchema, uye hakuchazovi nekurwadziwa: nekuti zvinhu zvekare zvapfuura. Sezvakanga zvakaita mumazuva aNoa, avo vanotya Mwari vachararama kunze kwenharaunda yavo yakaipa yengirozi dzakawa, nekuti yavo ichava nharaunda yechivi inotungamirira kukutongwa kusingaperi mumoto usingadzimwi; saka musabatana navo nekuti sarudzo ndeyekusingaperi. Uye saka 144,000 vangu vachaunza ruponeso nerubatsiro kune vazhinji.

Vachaita zviratidzo zvikuru, zvishamiso, nezvishamiso, uye ndichavatungamirira nhanho nenhanho, uye ndichatungamirira nzira dzavo kune avo kwandiri kuvatuma. Uye ndichaunganidza makwai angu kwavari kuti vatungamirirwe kunzvimbo yakachengeteka. Uye ani nani asingadi hupenyu hwake kusvika kumagumo achaponeswa. Uye avo vakachenjera vachapenya sekupenya kwedenga; uye avo vanotendeutsira vazhinji kukururama senyeredzi nekusingaperi-peri.

Chikara chinobva mugungwa chichamuka uye chichashandira zvinangwa zvakaipa zveavo vanotonga nyika yechipikirwa muchivi chikuru, kunyange sinagogi raSatani. Chichatonga marudzi uye chichataura kumhura kuzhinji, uye chichaita zviratidzo zvikuru zvenhema nezvishamiso, uye vakanamata dhiragoni rakapa simba kuchikara, uye vakanamata chikara, vachiti, Ndiani akafanana nechikara? Ndiani anokwanisa kurwa nacho? Sinagogi raSatani richaita zvese zvarinogona kuparadza chero munhu anodana zita rangu, uye chikara nemuporofita wenhema vachamanikidza zvine hutsinye Noahide pamarudzi uye vachaonekwa sekunge vari kuchenesa huipi kubva munyika. Asi pachinzvimbo chazvo vacharwa hondo nevatsvene uye vovakunda, uye zvakapihwa kuchikara chinobva mugungwa kuti chienderere mberi mwedzi 42.

Chikara chinobva panyika chichamukawo kubva mumadota. Chichava nesimba rese rechikara chekutanga, uye chichaita zviratidzo zvizhinji zvenhema nezvishamiso pamberi pechikara chekutanga, uye chichaita mufananidzo wechikara, uye mweya uchabvumirwa kuita kuti mufananidzo wechikara utaure, uchiti ani nani asingapfugami nekunamata mufananidzo wechikara chekutanga anofanira kuurayiwa.

Nekuti chikara chinobva panyika vachachigadza mambo wenyika dzechipikirwa, uye achava Moshiach wavo wakamirirwa kwenguva refu, uye achavaka temberi yavo (kunyange Yahweh vasina kumutumira), uye saizvozvo zvichava. Vachamugamuchira semutongi mukuru, ane mutsa, muporofita, uye mambo. Uye chikara chinobva panyika (kunyange muporofita wavo wenhema) chichaita kuti vese, vadiki nevakuru, vapfumi nevarombo, vakasununguka nevaranda, vagamuchire chiratidzo muruoko rwavo rwerudyi kana pahuma dzavo, Uye kuti pasave nemunhu angatenga kana kutengesa, kunze kweuyo aive nechiratidzo, kana zita rechikara, kana nhamba yezita racho. Vakatogadzirira nyika kugamuchira chiratidzo, uye hurongwa hwacho hwakanatswa panguva yavanoti denda ravo.

(Zvakanyorwa muna Zvakazarurwa: Uye ngirozi yechitatu yakavatevera, ichiti nezwi guru, Kana munhu akanamata chikara nemufananidzo wacho, uye akagamuchira chiratidzo chacho pahuma yake, kana muruoko rwake, Iyeyu achanwa waini yehasha dzaMwari, inodururwa isina kusanganiswa mukapu yekutsamwa kwake; uye achatambudzwa nemoto nesarufa pamberi pengirozi tsvene, uye pamberi peGwayana: Uye utsi hwekurwadziswa kwavo hunokwira nekusingaperi-peri: uye havana zororo masikati kana usiku, avo vanonamata chikara nemufananidzo wacho, uye ani nani anogamuchira chiratidzo chezita racho.)

Mumwedzi 42 yekutanga, ndichapa simba kuzvapupu zvangu zviviri, uye zvichaporofita mazuva chiuru nemazana maviri nemakumi matanhatu, zvakapfeka masaga. Ava ndivo miti miviri yemiorivhi, nezvigadziko zvemwenje zviviri zvimire pamberi paMwari wenyika. Uye kana munhu akada kuvakuvadza, moto unobuda mumuromo mavo, uye unoparadza vavengi vavo: uye kana munhu akada kuvakuvadza, anofanira kuurayiwa nenzira iyi. Ava vane simba rekuvhara denga, kuti mvura isanaye mumazuva ekuporofita kwavo: uye vane simba pamusoro pemvura kuti vaiite ropa, uye kurova nyika nematambudziko ese, kakawanda sezvavanoda. Uye kana vapedza uchapupu hwavo, chikara chinokwira kubva mugomba risina magumo chicharwa navo, uye chichavakunda, uye chichavauraya.

Chikara chinobva panyika, kunyange muporofita wavo wenhema, chichaunza chokwadi chinonyangadza chekuparadza. Chichaita kuti chibayiro chezuva nezuva chigume uye choisa chinonyangadza chekuparadza panzvimbo yacho. Uye saka ndakataura uye saizvozvo Mateo akanyora: Kana muchiona chinonyangadza chekuparadza, chakataurwa nemuporofita Dhanieri, chimire panzvimbo tsvene (ani nani anoverenga, ngaanzwisise), Ipapo avo vari muJudhiya ngavatizire kumakomo: Uyo ari pamusoro pedenga reimba ngaarege kuburuka kunotora chero chinhu mumba make: Uye uyo ari mumunda ngaarege kudzokera kunotora nguo dzake. Uye vane nhamo avo vane pamuviri, neavo vanoyamwisa mumazuva iwayo! Asi nyengeterai kuti kutiza kwenyu kusave munguva yechando, kana pazuva resabata: Nekuti ipapo pachava nekutambudzika kukuru, kusina kumbovapo kubva pakutanga kwenyika kusvika panguva ino, kwete, uye hakuchazombovipo.

(Vakaropafadzwa vakafa vanofira muna Ishe kubva zvino: Hongu, anodaro Mweya, kuti vazorore pamabasa avo; uye mabasa avo anovatevera.)

Uye saka ndichadzivirira vazhinji uye ndichavanza vazhinji murenje kubva mukutambudzika kukuru, nekuti dai mazuva asina kupfupiswa, hapana nyama yaizopona. Ipapo chikara nemuporofita wenhema vachaunganidza madzimambo enyika nevose vanomira navo kubva kuumambo hwedhiragoni kwezuva guru iroro rehondo. Uye saka ndakaita kuti Johane MuZaruri anyore: Uye ndakaona denga rakazaruka, uye tarira bhiza jena; uye uyo akanga akagara pamusoro paro ainzi Akatendeka uye Wechokwadi, uye mukururama anotonga uye anorwa hondo. Maziso ake aive semurazvo wemoto, uye pamusoro wake paive nekorona zhinji; uye aive nezita rakanyorwa, risingazivikanwi nemunhu, kunze kwake. Uye akanga akapfeka nguo yakanyikwa muropa: uye zita rake rinonzi Shoko raMwari. Uye mauto aive kudenga akamutevera pamabhiza machena, akapfeka mucheka wakanaka, muchena uye wakachena. Uye mumuromo make munobuda munondo unopinza, kuti nawo, arove marudzi: uye achavatonga netsvimbo yesimbi: uye anotsika chisviniro chewaini chehasha nekutsamwa kwaMwari Wemasimbaose.

Uye chikara nemuporofita wenhema vose vachabatwa vari vapenyu uye vokandwa mugungwa remoto rinopfuta nesarufa. Uye vakasara vakaurayiwa nemunondo weuyo akanga akagara pabhiza, munondo wakabuda mumuromo make: uye shiri dzose dzakaguta nenyama yavo. Uye ndakaona zvigaro zveushe, uye vakagara pamusoro pazvo, uye kutonga kwakapihwa kwavari: uye ndakaona mweya yeavo vakagurwa misoro nekuda kweuchapupu hwaJesu, uye nekuda kweshoko raMwari, uye avo vakanga vasina kunamata chikara, kana mufananidzo wacho, uye vakanga vasina kugamuchira chiratidzo chacho pahuma dzavo, kana mumaoko avo; uye vakararama uye vakatonga naKristu makore chiuru.

Uye saka ngirozi yangu ichaburuka kubva kumatenga, iine kiyi yegomba risina magumo necheni huru yaichasunga nayo Satani mugomba risina magumo kwemakore chiuru uye yoisa chisimbiso kuitira kuti arege kunyengera marudzi zvakare kwemakore chiuru. Pakupera kwekutonga kwangu kwemakore chiuru, Satani achasunungurwa kubva mugomba kwenguva diki.

Achaenda kunonyengera marudzi kumativi mana enyika, Gogi naMagogi kuti avaunganidze pamwe chete sejecha regungwa, uye acharwisa musasa wevatsvene uye okomba guta dzvene, uye moto uchaburuka kubva kuna Yahweh uye uchavaparadza zvachose. Uye dhiyabhorosi akavanyengera achasungwa uye okandwa ari mupenyu mugungwa guru iroro remoto, rinopfuta nesarufa mumirazvo inopisa zvikuru yebhuruu. Uye pachava nemubairo mukuru wehuipi uye kururamisira kukuru kuchaitwa pamusoro waSatani, ngirozi dzake dzakawa, nevose vakabatana nekupanduka kwavo; uye havachazomukizve, uye vachakanganwika zvachose; uye havachazombouyi mupfungwa.

Ipapo Yahweh vachaita denga idzva nenyika itsva, uye vachaita ugaro hwavo pakati pevanhu, uye murugare nekuwirirana zvichava nekusingaperi, uye hakuchazombovi nenguva yedambudziko, uye havachazombotadzizve, kana nekuzvikudza kana nekusatya Mwari, uye mazuva avo ese acharopafadzwa.

Ndini MuNazareta akamutswa akafira chivi kamwe chete uye haachafi zvakare.

Ndini Yeshua, kunyange Mesia wenyu wechokwadi, uyo Yahweh vakatumira kuzadzisa zvipikirwa zvakaitwa kuMadzibaba.

Ndini iye ane maziso ari murazvo wemoto, uye pamusoro pangu korona zhinji.

Ndini Ishe weMadzishe naMambo weMadzimambo, uye mukururama ndinouya kuzotonga nekunorwa hondo.

Ndini iye achutungamirira nekutungamira 144,000 vangu, uye vachaimba rwiyo rutsva rusingagoni kudzidzwa nemunhu.

Ndini iye achakunda umambo hwaSatani, uye ndichapwanya musoro wake.

Ndini iye uyo Yahweh vakatumira ruponeso rwevose vanotenda, uye nguva yangu yasvika. Tarirai! Kutonga kunosvika uye nguva dzava zvino.
