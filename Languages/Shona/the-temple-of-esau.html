Chapupu Chiviri: Israeri
Zvinovava Zvinotapira
Mbudzi 23, 2024

Temberi yaEsau

Inzwai, inzwai! Imi Israeri, inzwi raYeshua HaMashiach, iye Mwanakomana mumwechete akaberekwa waYahweh, iye Mukuru NDIRI! Kuti ndikuyambirei pamusoro pezvinhu zvichauya, uye kuti muwedzere kuziva musungo uchateyiwa naBharabhasi kuitira kuti murege kubatwa pamwechete nevasina kutenda; zvinhu izvo zvakanyorwa kare muchikamu muEvhangeri Risingaperi. Asi ndauya kuzokuudzai nezvemusungo unoteyiwa nedandemutande munyika yechipikirwa uye kuti ndiujekese kuitira kuti murege kuparadzwa nemazano avo. Uye ndinoshuvira kujekesa nzira dzenyu nekukuchengetai munzira dzekururama, idzo nzira dzaYahweh, idzo nzira dzinorarama zvakakwana muna Yeshua HaMashiach, Mesia wenyu wechokwadi anogara achiteerera kuda kwaYahweh zvakakwana. Uye zvakare kuti mugone kuchengeta tsoka dzenyu munzira iyo yakamanikana, iyo yandichachengeta tsoka dzenyu dzakasimba kana mukaramba muchinditevera mukutenda kunoteerera. Nekuti vakarurama vachararama nekutenda!

Saka nzwisisai kuti nguva yasvika, sekuziviswa kwazvakaitwa pandichaunganidza pakarepo vakatendeka muna Kristu kumatenga vangave vari vapenyu kana vakafa; uye apo vatsvene vakatendeka vachatorwa pakarepo, kuparadzwa kunongoerekana kwaitika kuchawira Bhabhironi Rakavanzika rigere pamusoro pemvura zhinji, vanova vanhu, nemapoka, nemarudzi, nendimi. Uye apo madota ehondo achipfungaira uye hurongwa hwazvino hwemarudzi hwoparadzwa nekugadziriswazve kuti zvienderane nezvinangwa zvavo, hurongwa hwekupedzisira hwechikara cheuyo akawa huchasimuka zvizere.

Asi, chikara chinobva mugungwa nechikara chinobva panyika zvakaita basa revafudzi vakarurama vakauya kuzofudza makwai akarasika eBhabhironi Rakavanzika nezvipikirwa zvizhinji zvisina maturo; zvipikirwa zvenhema zvinoguma zvaunza rufu nekuparadzwa. Uye vanonyepedzera kuva vavengi vari shure kwechiso cheharahwa isisina njere yavanovanda shure kwayo uye iyo harahwa iripo kuti ipiwe mhosva yemhosva dzavo; uye vanodzora kufamba kwake kwese nekunyora shoko rake rese. Nekuti kunyengera kukuru uku kunopiwa mukutonga Bhabhironi Rakavanzika; kuti kunyengedzwa kukuru kuiputire zvizere nekuti yakafukidza nyika nenhema dzayo iine mukombe wendarama muruoko rwayo, uzere nezvinhu zvinonyangadza netsvina yehupombwe hwayo.

Asi zvikara zviviri izvi zvakaipa zvakapamba Bhabhironi Rakavanzika, kunyange kuparadzwa kwaro kusati kwasvika uye zvakatora simba raro rechiuto nekuwanda kwaro nokuda kwezvinangwa zveshato muhurongwa hwavo hwekupedzisira hwechikara huchauya. Nekuti hondo ichauya ichaparadza Bhabhironi Rakavanzika, uye zvirokwazvo richaparadzwa muawa imwe, nekuti ndizvo zvakataurwa naYahweh, iye Mukuru NDIRI. Asi, izvi zvakarongwa uye zvakaiswawo muhurongwa hwaYahweh hwerudzikinuro (kuti vavengi vake vateerere inzwi rake) uye saka kutongwa kweHure guru kwaziviswa, nekunyorwa, uye ndizvo zvichaitwa nekusingaperi-peri, uye harichazomukizve. Kunyange zvakadaro, vachapatsanura nyika uye kuzununguka kukuru kuchaitika, uye vakaronga kuparadzwa kwaro kuti vagone kuunganidza zviwanikwa zvavanoshuvira zvakavigwa muvhu. Zviwanikwa zvavanoda kuti vape simba nekuwedzera matekinoroji euyo akawa; ayo nyika parizvino yakakwezvwa nekuroyiwa nawo mukunyengedzwa kwayo kukuru, ichichengetwa yakabatwa nemafrequency avo nemanomano avo avanokurudzirwa nekudzorwa nawo.

Uye pavanhu vachasara mushure mekuparadzwa kuri kuuya kweBhabhironi Rakavanzika; chikara chinobva panyika nechikara chinobva mugungwa zvakaunza uto revapambi nevaurayi mukati memiganhu yaro vane huwandu hwakaita sedenda guru remhashu, uye vachasunungurwa pachiratidzo chavo! Nekuti aya ndiwo mauto echikara akaunganidzirwa kuparadzwa kweBhabhironi Rakavanzika. Vazhinji veava vanobva kunyika dzeshato nenyika dzebheya, uye vachaita sezvakaitwa neBhabhironi Rakavanzika pachivande kwavari mumajubhiri apfuura: Vachauraya murume wese, mukadzi, nemwana pasina kuzvidemba vasingaenderane nezvinangwa zvavo uye vazhinji vachabvumidzwa kuramba vari vapenyu vachamanikidzwa mumisasa yavo yevaranda kuti vashande mumigodhi yavo kusvika pakufa kwavo. Uye vachabata basa nekushanda kusvika pakufema kwavo kwekupedzisira; nekuti zvese zvakaitwa neBhabhironi Rakavanzika pachivande kune vamwe zvino zvichaitwa pachena kwariri mukutongwa nekurohwa. Nekuti Bhabhironi Rakavanzika rakadhakwa neropa revatsvene, uye neropa revakafira Jesu raakafira muhuwandu hukuru munyika dzekunze; nguva yese richizviti iro rinoshumira Jesu weNazareta. Asi muchokwadi rinoshandira vakawa uye zvinangwa zvaro zvapera, uye vanoshuvira kuparadzwa kwaro kuti vavhare mhosva dzavo uye kuti vafudzi vavo vane gwembe vaonekwe sevakarurama pavanosimuka kuitira kuti vavige musungo wavo.

Asi havangambozvivanza pamberi paYahweh, kana Yeshua HaMashiach uye mhosva dzavo dzakanyorwa zvakakwana kumatenga uye chitadzo chese chakanyorwa zvakadzama pamwechete nepfungwa nezvinangwa zvemwoyo yavo yakasvibiswa. Uye chikara chinobva mugungwa nechikara chinobva panyika zvinonyangadza zvakaipa zvakasikirwa kuparadzwa kwevanhu. Zvikara izvi zvakaipa zvinoshinha nevana, uye zvinounganidza nekushandisa miviri yevadiki zvazvakauraya kugadzira zvinonyangadza zvazvo uye saka zvese zvazvinoparadza zvinopiwa sechibayiro kuna mwari wazvo. Nekuti vakatora Tabhenakeri yaMoroki, nenyeredzi yamwari wavo Remfani, zvifananidzo zvavakagadzira, kuti vazvinamate, uye zvimwe zvakawanda-wanda zvavakaita zvichaziviswa zvizere panguva dzakakodzera dzekuzivisa nekuti chakavanzika chese chichaonekwa pachena.

Saka zvichaitika kuti chikara chinobva panyika chichaonekwa sekunge chiri kumuka kubva kuvakafa; chichizviti chakamuka kubva kuvakafa, kubva pamwoyo penyika nekuti rufu haruna kukwanisa kuchibata! Uye chichava nerumuko rwenhema uye chichaunza rugare rwenhema panyika kuti chipedze hondo yavo, uye chichaonekwa sekunge chauya kuzoponesa vanhu. Uye vakawa vachazviratidza kuvanhu sevabatsiri vane mutsa vakuru muhuchenjeri uye vakafambira mberi munzira dzetekinoroji, mushonga, nerugare.

Vachaburitsa zviratidzo nezvishamiso zvizhinji zvenhema, uye vachati avo vakaunganidzwa muna Kristu pakuungana kukuru uye vakatorwa kuenda kunzvimbo yakachengeteka kumatenga njodzi huru isati yawira; kuti kunyange ava vakatorwa nechisimba nemuvengi wekunze kwenyika anonyepedzera. Simba remutorwa rine ruvengo richauyazve kuzoparadza vanhu, uye vachaunganidza munhu wenyama kuti abatane navo kuitira kuti azviponese kubva kune ichi chakaipa chavanofungidzira muchadenga chavo chenhema chenyika dzinogara dzichitenderera dzenhema munzvimbo isina chinhu inogara ichiwedzera.

Kunyange kosimoroji yenhema iyo vakawa vakafungidzira nekushandisa kare munguva yavo yendarama yekare, iyo nguva yendarama yakasvika kumagumo ayo mumaoko aYahweh mumafashamo makuru munguva dzaNoa, izvo zvinyorwa zvakanyorwa muzvinyorwa zvitsvene zvekare. Uye nhema idzo dzichawisirwa pasi nekuti kunyengera kwavo kukuru kuchafukurwa zvizere kuti vese vaone vanyepedzeri ava vanonyepedzera kuti ndivanaani chaizvo nekuti zvisikwa zvakawa zvinobva kunzvimbo yekudenga zvakazvishatisa kare mukupanduka kusina maturo kubva mukururama kwaYahweh kuitira kuti vagare nhaka yekuparadzwa kwavo kwavakazvigadzirira munhaka yavo yesarufa inopfuta iyo yavasingazombopukunyuki.

Uye saka chikara chinobva panyika chichasimuka sekunge chabva kuvakafa uye nezviratidzo nezvishamiso zvizhinji zvenhema chichaonekwa, kunyange nebazi remuorivhi muruoko rwacho. Uye chichasimuka segwayana rine nyanga mbiri pachinosimuka musimba rizere nechiremera cheshato kunyange nechikara chinobva mugungwa, iye akasarudzwa neshato nechikara chinobva panyika kuti atonge marudzi kuti agone kutonga gungwa remarudzi maererano nemitemo yavo inomhura neyekuuraya rudzi yeNoaide. Asi nyika dzacho mbiri hadzizombovi mhinduro nekuti ndini iye achabatanidza Israeri nekusingaperi kuti risazopatsanurwazve kuita humambo huviri.

Uye saka vachashandisa mitemo yavo yakaipa nemigumisiro iri muNoaide kurwisa vatsvene nekuvakunda, kunze kwekunge ani nani anodana zita raShe kusvika kumagumo achaponeswa, kunyange avo vanodana Jesu weNazareta saShe wavo anoponesa uyo achavaponesa zvakakwana kana vasakaenda simba mumatambudziko avo. Uye saka chikara chinobva mugungwa chichasunungurwa pane zvasara zvemarudzi avachange vaparadza, uye ane madota aive marudzi avaimbotonga mukunyengera kukuru. Uye vachaonekwa vachisimuka sefenikisi huru kubva mumadota nemirazvo yehondo ichiri kupfungaira kubva mukunyengera kwavo. Uye ichava hondo huru, kunyange huru pane hondo nhatu dzakadaro dzavakakurudzira nekushandisa; kuitira kuti shato igone kusimuka zvizere munguva yayo nezvikara zvayo zvakaipa, zvinonyangadza zvayichava nekutonga kupfupi nayo, isingazotongizve.

Nekuti chakavanzika chekusarurama chave chichishanda kuburikidza nemazera; apo hure guru rinogara pamakomo manomwe, iro hure guru dzvuku rekunyomba rinonamata mambokadzi wekudenga uye rinodzidzisa mumwe Jesu sechikamu cheutatu hwavo hwechihedheni. Iro rine midzi yakadzika muBhabhironi rekare apo Nimrodhi akatanga ngano dzake namambokadzi wekudenga. Rine nhaka nevapristi nemadzinza eropa anoteedzera dzinza rawo kuvakawa vaitonga Bhabhironi rekare neutatu hwavo husina kuchena hwavakashandisa kuseka uMwari hwechokwadi, nembeu yakapikirwa yemukadzi. Tsika nemitambo iyi yechihedheni zvakatonga nyika muchivande chikuru apo vapristi vakadzidziswa vevakawa vainamata zuva pachivande asi vachizviti iwo Sosaiti yaJesu; uye saka kereke yavo yenhema neyekunyomba yakaisa hudzvinyiriri nekutambudza pamusoro penyika mukati memazuva chakavanzika chekusarurama chakapararira muchivande. Naizvozvo, kuparadzwa kwavo hakunonoki uye nguva dzavo dzasvika!

Asi zvikara zviviri izvi zvakaipa, izvo parizvino zvinoshanda pamwechete mukuparadzwa kuzere kwebhabhironi rakavanzika, icho chikara chinobva mugungwa nechikara chinobva panyika; izvi zviviri zvave zviri vatungamiriri veBhabhironi Rakavanzika uye zvakatonga nezvipikirwa zvizhinji zvikuru zvisina maturo izvo vafudzi vaviri ava vane gwembe vakafudza nazvo makwai akarasika eBhabhironi Rakavanzika vachipikira kubudirira kwenhema kwezvinhu nekuwanda kune avo vavanofudza. Uye mumaoko avo uye nemazano avo zvirokwazvo vakaronga uye vachaita izvo zvakapihwa mumaoko eshato kuti zviitwe.

Nekuti Bhabhironi Rakavanzika, iro hure guru rinogara pamusoro pemvura zhinji, rudzi rwakagadzirwa kuti zvikara zviviri izvi zvakaipa zvigone kusimuka kubva mumadota aro munguva dzazvo dzekuziviswa. Rudzi rwemhombwe, rudzi rwehupombwe, rudzi ruri hure guru zvirokwazvo rinozviti muteveri akarurama waJesu weNazareta. Asi muchokwadi rudzi rwavaki varo vakasununguka kubva paDombo rechokwadi reNheyo, iro dombo guru rekona iro vasina kutenda vanogumburwa naro, iye Jesu weNazareta anovawo Yeshua HaMashiach. Uye saka vavaki vakaipa ava vakazvisunungura kubva paruoko uye vakazvipira kudhamu remoto iro honye yavo isingafi nekuramba dombo guru rekona nedombo rinogumbura uye havangapukunyuki!

Asi vakawa vagara vaine zvinangwa zvakafanana mupfungwa uye zvirongwa nezvinangwa zvavo zvinoramba zvakafanana. Nekuti vanoda kuparadza zvachose Adhamu kubva panyika, uye vanoda zvachose kuparadza Jakobho kubva pasi pezuva kuitira kuti varambe vari vagari vechibharo nevatoreri vasina kururama venyika kuti vave nenguva yavo yendarama yavanoshuvira. Asi nzwisisai kuti nguva yavo yendarama yaive nguva iyo Adhamu akakurirwa nekutongwa nehumambo hwepasi rose hwezvinonyangadza zvakaipa zvakagadzirirwa kuparadzwa kwakazara kwevanhu, uye zvinonyangadza zvavo zvichadzoka! Asi hapana nguva yendarama yavachava nayo asi tariro dzakaputsika nekutongwa kwakaomarara sezvo humambo hwevakawa huchiparadzwa zvachose pamberi pemaziso avo neruoko rwangu rune simba!

Uye iyo inonzi nguva yavo yendarama yaive nguva iyo dzinza rakarurama raSeti rakazvivanza murenje kubva kunzanga yavo yakaipa munyika yaivapo. Kuti dzinza raSeti rirambe rakachena kubva mukusvibiswa kwemajini uye mbeu yakapikirwa yemukadzi (ndini), igouya sezvakapikirwa kare naYahweh pachake. Tarirai! Yahweh akazivisa kuti Achauyisa Hofori kuti dzizadzise hasha dzake, uye ndizvo zvichaitika. Asi, ndizvo zvichaita humambo hwavo hwakaipa huchisimuka nekutonga nyika yese uye zvakaipa zvizhinji zvavachauyisa, uye zvinonyangadza zvizhinji zvichasunungurwa kubva mhiri kweaizi uye kubva pamwoyo penyika uye gomba rakadzika richavhurwa.

Pachava nehuipi hukuru panyika uye kunyange vachizomuka kuti vaunze rugare rwenhema; hondo ichagara ichitonga kutonga kwavo nekuti rugare ruchabviswa panyika mukutonga pakubviswa kwemudzivisi wangu. Uye rugare havazombovi narwo nekuti Yahweh akazivisa kare kuti vakawa havana rugare nekuti vakatadzira vanhu mukuchiva kwavo kwakaipa neruvengo rwezvinhu zvese zvakagadzirwa naYahweh. Uye vachamisa rugare rwenhema panyika uye chikara chinobva panyika chichauya zvizere musimba racho, uye chichatonga segwayana rine nyanga mbiri rinotaura seshato.

Nekuti gwayana raEsau, icho chikara chinobva panyika chinovawo Bharabhasi, rakasarudzwa semuvaki wavo wetemberi yechikara uye rinopiwa segwayana reshato nokuda kweMoshiach wavo wenhema namambo wavo wekunyepedzera. Zvirokwazvo, zvirokwazvo, richabatanidza Esau neAshkenazi nevose vanobatana navo pasi pemureza wechitendero chisingazivikanwi naMadzibaba avo uye nzira dzavo dzeTalmudic dzichatonga nyika. Vachakunda marudzi nehutsinye pasi peNoaide uye vachatonga marudzi nehutsinye hukuru; uye chikara chinobva panyika chichaita kuti vose, vadiki nevakuru, vapfumi nevarombo, vakasununguka nevaranda vagamuchire mucherechedzo wacho mumaoko avo erudyi kana pahuma dzavo kuti pasave nemunhu angatenga kana kutengesa, kunze kweuyo aive nemucherechedzo, kana zita rechikara, kana nhamba yezita racho. Nekuti chinounza kurumwa kwenyoka uko kusina munhu anogona kupora uye vose vanotora vachange vazvishandura nekusingaperi kubva mukushandurwa ikoko kwavasingazove nekuregererwa, uye vachasanganisa simbi nevhu, asi hazvizobatani. Chakakombama hachigoni kururamiswa uye vose vanozvikombamisa nenzira yakadaro vachava nechikamu chavo mudhamu guru iro resarufa inopfuta remirazvo isingadzimiki iro honye yavo isingafi.

Saka chikara chinobva panyika chinovawo muporofita wavo wenhema neMoshiach wenhema chichaita zviziviso zvacho uye chichavaka temberi yacho nekuti chinotumwa semuvaki wetemberi yechikara akazvivakira shongwe zhinji muhumambo hwake dzakatsaurirwa kuzita rake, uye chichataura pamusoro pacho nekuyemura kukuru kunyange kuzivisa kuti icho chiri Mwari. Nekuti chinouya kuzoita zvipikirwa zvizhinji zvikuru zvachisina simba rekuzadzisa, uye chinouya kuzotaura zvinhu zvizhinji zvikuru zvinomhura, uye ndizvo zvachichaita nekuti ndizvo zvakapihwa kuti chiite. Nekuti muvaki nemugadziri akazviitira shongwe nenzvimbo dzekuzororera zhinji, uye zvirokwazvo anogara pamusoro pehumambo hwake hwaakatsaurira mukukudzwa kwake; nekuti anogara achizvirumbidza nekuti ndidzo nzira dzeshato inova Baba vake vechokwadi.

Sezvo tembere yake inovakwa nekupedzwa pakati pemheremhere huru nezvipikirwa zvizhinji zvekumhura Mwari nezvenhema, saizvozvowo achataura mashoko mazhinji enhema muhofisi yake semuporofita wenhema uye kutonga kwake kuchava nehasha neutsinye. Asi, achateya musungo wake asi muteyi upi anoteera shiri ichiona? Neunyanzvi hukuru anoteza musungo wake uye nechivimbiso chikuru achavimbisa kutsaurira tembere yake kuvanhu venyika uye nemufaro mukuru Esau naAshkenazi vachazvipemberera vakatenderedza gwayana raEsau, uye vachanyepedzera kuti vari kudzoreredza nzira dzeTorah, asi vari kuunza nzira dzerufu dzakanyorwa mubhuku ravo rekumhura Mwari nerufu, iyo Talmud yavo. Tarirai! Chikara chinobva panyika muurayi uye mutungamiriri wekumukira Jehovha nenzira dzaBharabhasi. Achatengesa vose vaakavimbisa zvikuru uye kutengesa kwake kuchava kusina kuzvidemba uye neruvengo rukuru rwaakagara nhaka kubva kuna Baba vake, dhiabhorosi uyo waakasungirirwa mukuchiva kwake kuti aite zvakaipa.

Asi ndizvo zvakaita dandemutande rakaruka nyika yose mudandemutande raro. Dandemutande rinogara rakamirira mhuka yaro kuti igare padandemutande raro richibva ratanga kuita basa rekubata, kuomesa mitezo nekupombera mhuka yaro kuti rigodya zvishoma nezvishoma upenyu hwevasungwa varo; sezvavanomedzwa zvishoma nezvishoma zvizere nehuipi hwavakabatwa nahwo. Asi vazhinji vachauya vachibva kure nekure nenzwi rekushevedzera kwake; uye kukokwa kwekutsaurira nekucheneswa kwetembere yake kuchanzwika pasi rose uye vazhinji vanoramba Mesiasi wavo wechokwadi vachabatwa mumusungo wake ndokuparara nawo. Asi haungaite semusungo kune vaakanga aroya, asi kumutsidzirwa kwaMosesi kunotungamirirwa neSanhedrin yavo uye kunotongwa netsika neuchenjeri hwaEsau huri mubhuku ravo dzvene risina kuchena. Uye boka revapristi venhema vasiri vaRevhi nekuti Revhi anowanikwa zvizere muMupristi Mukuru waMerkizedeki, nekuti ini, Yeshua HaMashiach ndiri Mupristi wenyu Mukuru nekusingaperi zvichienderana neurongwa hwaMerkizedeki, nekuti ndizvo zvakataurwa naJehovha.

Asi sezvandakadzidzisa chokwadi panguva yangu pandakauya kune vekwangu seGwayana raMwari; pandakarambwa nevanhu vekwangu, asi ndakadzidzisa chokwadi zvimwe chetezvo, naizvozvo, haiwa iwe Israeri hauna chikonzero. Kunyange zvakadaro, murudo rwangu kwamuri uye maererano nezvipikirwa nezvisungo zvekare, ndinouya kuzokuudzai zvakare kunyange kuti Jakobho arege kuwira kuna Esau.

Nekuti zvakanyorwa mumagwaro matsvene: Naizvozvo, kana muchinge maona nyangadzi yokuparadza, yakataurwa nomuporofita Dhanieri, imire panzvimbo tsvene, (unorava, ngaanzwisise). Zvino vari muJudhea, ngavatizire kumakomo. Uri pamusoro pedenga remba, ngaarege kuburuka, kuzotora chinhu mumba make: Uyo uri mumunda, ngaarege kudzokera kunotora nguo dzake. Uye vane nhamo vakadzi vane pamuviri, navanomwisa pamazuva iwayo. Asi nyengeterai kuti kutiza kwenyu kurege kuva munguva yechando, kana pazuva reSabata: Nokuti panguva iyoyo kuchava nokutambudzika kukuru, kusina kumboitika kubva pakutanga kwenyika kusvikira panguva ino, kwete, uye hakuchazovipo. Uye kunze kwekunge mazuva iwayo apfupiswa, hapana nyama yaizoponeswa; asi nekuda kwevasanangurwa, mazuva iwayo achapfupiswa.

Sezvazvakanyorwa, ndizvo zvichaitwa uye Bharabhasi achateya musungo wake waachange aviga neuchenjeri. Nekuti vakawa vanotsvaka kuparadza rudzi rwaAdhamu zvachose kubva panyika. Naizvozvo, vacharwa nevatsvene vovakunda, uye Esau pamwe naAshkenazi vanotsvaka kuteya Jakobho, kunyange kuti Jakobho agowira paronda remusoro ravanotsvaka kuuraya Jakobho naro. Nzwisisai kuti dai zvisiri nekuda kwenyasha netsitsi dzaJehovha uyo akazvipira murudo rwake rusingaperi kuna Israeri muzvipikirwa zvizhinji nezvisungo zvekare kuti anunure nekudzikinura Jakobho kubva mumaoko evavengi vake; imi mose maizoparara mumaoko evatatu vasina kuchena!
