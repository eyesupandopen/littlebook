Tarirai! Mitongo Yekuzivisa Yakazara uye Isina Kupfupikiswa paBhabhironi Rakavanzika: Yaziviswa Zvizere Mutongo Usati Wawira Zvizere

Mutongo Wakazara paBhabhironi Rakavanzika: Waziviswa neSvondo Chikumi 22, 2025. Zvikamu zvese zvakatorwa kubva muBhuku Diki raZvakazarurwa Chitsauko 10 iro razarurwa kubva muna Kurume 2, 2024, kusvika Kurume 20, 2025. Mashoko ese andakapihwa naYeshua HaMashiach naYHVH, Mukuru IYE NDINI kuburikidza neMweya weChiporofita unova uchapupu hwaJesu Kristu. Sera!

Mitongo inotevera inobva muBhuku Diki raZvakazarurwa Chitsauko 10, rinovawo Evhangeri Isinaperi yaZvakazarurwa Chitsauko 14. Mutongo paBhabhironi Rakavanzika wakaziviswa kwemajubhiri nemakore mazhinji muBhuku raZvakazarurwa. Zvisinei, Mabhanan'ana Manomwe neBhuku Diki zvino zvazarurwa uye zvaziviswa uye bhuku rine simba iri rakazaruka muruoko rwangu nemumaoko evose vanoverenga chiziviso ichi kubva kuBoka Diki, rakatodanwa naYeshua HaMashiach nechinangwa nenguva yakadai! Sera!

Inotevera ndiyo mitongo yekuzivisa yakatorwa kubva muBhuku Diki iyo ichaitika zvechokwadi nekukasika uye zvisingatarisirwi zvino. Pachinzvimbo ichi, ndinoshanda senhumwa yehutongi yakatumwa kuzivisa mitongo inobva kuchigaro chehushe chaYHVH, yakapiwa pamberi paJesu Kristu, wandinoshandira. Ndapota cherechedzai, mimwe yemitongo iyi iri kutoitika uye mimwe ichaitika munguva pfupi, asi yose ichazadziswa sekuziviswa kwayakaitwa uye mutongo paBhabhironi Rakavanzika uchashanda seyambiro kumarudzi ose uye utsi hwekurwadziswa kwaro huchakwira sechiziviso chakarurama chemitongo yakarurama yaKristu Jesu, uye naYHVH Mukuru IYE NDINI!

Ngazvizivikanwe, kuti nhasi uno Chikumi 22, 2025 paKarenda yaGregory (karenda imwecheteyo inoshandiswa neBhabhironi Rakavanzika) kuti Ishe wangu Akakwidzwa Kristu Jesu, iye Yeshua HaMashiach, anondiraira kuti ndiverenge mitongo iyi paBhabhironi Rakavanzika, idzo nyika dzeUnited States of America, kuitira kuti ani nani atendeuke kuchine nguva pfupi inopfuura, uye kuti mutongo uziviswe moto usati wawira. Sera!

Chiziviso ichi chakakwana uye iyi haisi mitongo mitsva asi yakaburitswa zvizere kubva muna Kurume 2, 2024, kusvika Kurume 20, 2025. Naizvozvo, ndinotumira ani nani kuBhuku Diki raZvakazarurwa chitsauko 10 pamalink akapihwa. Ndapota cherechedzai: Mitongo iyi inobva kuBhanan'ana rechi5 chaizvo uye kuverenga uku kuchatanga neVakarurama Havangagari Nhaka yeHumambo hwaMwari, Mabhanan'ana 5 ne6, zvakapihwa naYeshua HaMashiach uye naYHVH Mukuru IYE NDINI!

Zviitiko izvi zvichaitika sezvazvakapiwa naKristu Jesu uye neruoko rwaYHVH, Mukuru IYE NDINI, uyo zvinhu zvese zvinobva kwaari zvakapihwa muzita rine simba nechiremera chaKristu Jesu, anovawo Yeshua HaMashiach.

Izvi zvakatora nguva nesimba kuti zviunganidzwe, uye zvinoshanda sekuverenga kwemutongo wakapfupikiswa paBhabhironi Rakavanzika, uye zvakare segwara rezviitiko kuitira kuti Mwenga wechokwadi waKristu, Vakatendeka muna Kristu, ne144,000 vasatsauswe nezvinhu zvenhema. Iri iBhuku Diki richatonga nguva dzekupedzisira kuburikidza neMabhanan'ana Manomwe ayo zvino asunungurwa, kwete vaporofita venhema vanozvitumira vachitaura zvisina musoro zvinobva mumwoyo yavo. Ndakakudanirai kukubwinya nekunaka mandiri, uye ndakakununurai kubva kuhumambo hwaSatani, humambo hwerima, ndichikupinzai muHumambo hwangu, mune chiedza! Hamuna kuverenga here mutsamba dzandakatumira kuMwenga wangu, dzandakakutumirai, Kereke yangu chaiyo? Hamuzivi here kuti vasakarurama havangagari nhaka yeHumambo hwaMwari? Uye vamwe venyu makanga makadaro: asi makashambidzwa, asi makanatswa, asi makaruramiswa muZita raShe Jesu, uye neMweya waMwari wedu.

Nzwisisai, vadiki vangu vanodziya, kuti hamugoni kurarama hupenyu hwekubvumirana pakati pezvakanaka nezvakaipa, muchifunga kuti makarurama pamberi pangu, pamunosarudza chakaipa chidiki pane zviviri mumitambo yenyu yesarudzo yandisingabvumiri kana kutsigira.

Makanyengerwa, uye muri kugadzirirwa kutevera nekupa mvumo kumwanakomana wekurashwa anofamba pachena pakati penyu, kunyange asati aratidzwa zvizere.
