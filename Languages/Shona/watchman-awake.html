Chapupu Chiviri: Kereke
Mumabasa aJohane Mubhabhatidzi
Kubvumbi 05, 2024

Murindi, Muka!

Murindi, muka, uzvisunge chiuno nechokwadi, uye usimire chipfeko chekururama chepadada!

Murindi, muka, utore ngowani yoruponeso nomunondo womweya, rinova Shoko raMwari!

Murindi, muka, uye tsoka dzako dzive dzakashongedzerwa negadziriro yeEvhangeri yorugare!

Yambira vanhu vangu uye usanyarara, sezvo ndasunungura rurimi rwako! Rwire vanhu vangu uye uyambire vasanangurwa vangu, nokuti uchawana mubairo womuprofita kana ukangotaura mashoko andichakupa. MUKA! Handina kuudza vanhu vangu here kuti varindire kuuya kwangu? Nguva nemwaka hazvioneki uye hazvisati zvasvika pamuri here? Naizvozvo, tarisirai kuonekwa kwangu, nokuti kwava pedyo. Tarisai zviratidzo nezvishamiso mudenga, nokuti Baba vangu vakaronga nzira dzazvo kubva pakutanga, uye zuva racho rakatemwa. Tarisai kupenya kwangu mudenga sezvo hwamanda yava kuda kurira; tarirai, ndazvizivisa.

Vanhu vangu, ongororai Shoko rangu uye mutarise kuMagwaro angu Matsvene kuti munzwisise kuti ndichabvuta mwenga wangu kubva pakutongwa kwevaiti vezvakaipa. Ndichadzivirira mwenga wangu nomuviri wangu; nokuti ndiani murume akambovenga nyama yake, asi anoipa zvokudya uye anoichengeta? Tarirai, teererai, cherechedzai mwaka, uye muzive kuti nguva dzasvika uye zuva richabuda pakarepo sezuva guru rinobuda pakarepo pamupendero wedenga. Handicharambidzwi, uye handichamiswi. Kunyange mbavha ichaedza, ichadzvova seshumba; asi haisi chinhu kunze kweshumba isina mazino. Haizondimisi, uye handicharambidzwi pfuma yangu, nokuti ndakatenga nyika uye zvikurukuru vanhu vangu neropa rangu risina chivi. Uye zita rangu rinomira pamusoro pezvose kunze kwaBaba vangu, vakuru kwandiri; uye handicharambidzwi, handichadziviswa, kana kumiswa.

Ndiri kuuya, uye handicharamba ndakanyarara, uye hasha dzangu dzinopisa pamusoro peBhabhironi Rakavanzika, hongu, Bhabhironi Rakavanzika rinotengesa vana vadiki kuti vave nhumbi mumaoko evanhu vakaipa, avo vachamiswa. Zvaiva nani kuti dai vasina kumboberekwa pane kuberekwa voita zvivi zvisina murau uye zvinorwadza pamusoro pevana. Zvaiva nani kuti guyo riremberedzwe pamitsipa yavo, vakandwa mugungwa; asi vakachengeterwa moto usingaperi, uye kururamisira kuchaitwa nokukurumidza, sezvo hasha dzeGwayana dzava kuda kufukidza nyika yose uye kutambudzika kwaJakobho kwotanga kana mudzivisi abviswa.

Zvisimbisei, vanhu vangu, uye mumire makasimba mandiri nomwoyo mumwe chete nechinangwa chimwe chete. Siyai zvinhu zvenyika zvamaishuva, uye muiise ini, Ishe Jesu Kristu, sechuya chamunoshuva; nokuti ndini shongwe yenyu yakasimba, dombo renyu rokudzivirira, nomurindi wenyu weshure. Ndiani achauya kuzokurwisai angamirisana noruoko rwangu rune simba? Vasanangurwa vangu nevatendeki vari mumaoko angu akatambanudzwa, uye hapana angakubvisai mandiri. Muri vangu, uye makachengeteka mandiri; saka zororai mandiri kubva pamabasa enyu, nokuti nguva dzemibairo dzichatanga pakarepo. Mubairo wokutonga vaiti vezvakaipa wasvika, uye mubairo wevatendeki vangu uri pamusoro pevasanangurwa vangu, sezvo ndichavapa mubairo wenhaka yavo, nokuti ndakavaita vakakodzera.

Haiwa iwe, dhiragoni isina maturo, isina mazino; iwe nyoka yekare: nguva yako yasvika uye kutongwa kwava pamusoro pako. Kunyange uchifunga nokuzvikudza kuti uchakunda, ndichatora zvose zvawakaunganidza mumaoko ako embavha, uye avo vanokutevera mukuchiva kwavo kworuchiva vachagoverana newe nhaka yako mugungwa romoto, umo munhu wose achapiwa mubairo maererano namabasa ake kakawanda. Hasha dzaMwari dzichagara pamusoro pako nokusingaperi-peri, uye zororo hauzombovi naro.

Kurangwa kukuru kuchava munyaradzi wako, uye utsi hunovava hwesarufa inopfuta huchagara huri mumhino dzako nokusingaperi, uye ucharipira nokusingaperi-peri. Uye utsi hwokurwadziswa kwako huchakwira nokusingaperi, uye vose vanokutevera kubva kumuduku kusvika kumukuru vachava nenhaka yavo nomubairo wavo pamwe chete newe mumirazvo yomoto mitema inopisa zvikuru iyo ichakufukidza nokusingaperi sezvo uchasungwa wokandwa kune ramangwana rako. Kuchava nokuchema kukuru nokugeda-geda kwemazino. Uye makazviti munokwanisa kumirisana naYahweh naKristu wake, asi muchashaiswa maturo kusvika pakuzvidzwa kusingaperi mukurwadziswa kwenyu kusingagumi, uye nokusingaperi muchavharirwa kure. Uye hauzombokuvadzi chisikwa changu zvakare, uye uchakanganikwa zvachose, uye hauzombofungwi.

Saka vanhu vangu, mwenga wangu akatendeka wandinoda zvikuru, chii chamunofanira kutya? Hasha dzangu nehasha dzaBaba venyu hadzisi dzenyu uye hadzichauyi pamusoro penyu, uye hamuzombovi mungozi yorufu rwechipiri. Saka musatya sezvo ndichatsiva zvakakwana pavavengi venyu, nokuti kutsiva ndekwangu, anodaro Ishe! Hamuzivi here kuti muchatonga ngirozi? Hamuzivi here kuti muchatonga nyika? Saka zvinatsei mandiri, nokuti ropa rangu rokuyananisa chete ndiro richakuitai vakakodzera kumira pamberi pangu mukubwinya. Sungai shoko rangu mumwoyo yenyu, uye chengetai zvinyorwa zvangu pahuma dzenyu, uye muzorore mumashoko angu ekare; nokuti upenyu kwauri, uye upenyu hwenyu hwakavigwa mandiri, uye ndichahuzivisa!

Haiwa, Bhabhironi Rakavanzika, dai wakanzwisisa chipiriso chorugare chakatumirwa naBaba vangu kwauri, uye zvino waramba ini, Jesu weNazareta, uye zvaitika maererano nezvinyorwa zvitsvene kuti vanhu vakaipa navanyengeri vachawedzera kuipa. Asi iwe, haiwa, Bhabhironi Rakavanzika guru, wamhura uta hwaBaba vangu hwemavara mazhinji hwavakapa kuna Noa navanhu vose sechivimbiso chokuti havazomboparadzi nyika yose nemvura zvakare, uye vachachengeta shoko ravo. Asi iwe, haiwa, Bhabhironi Rakavanzika, uchaparadzwa muawa imwe chete.

Moto mukuru uchauya pamusoro pako; kuzununguka kukuru kuchakupwanya; shongwe dzako dzichawa; zvivakwa zvako zvichanyangarika mumatongo, uye mafungu makuru achafukidza mahombekombe ako, uye ndichakupa mubairo maererano namabasa ako, uye kutonga kukuru kuri pamusoro pako. Nokuti waimbova chiedza chetariro nechiedza kunyika kwenguva pfupi, asi wasarudza makaro, udyire, nouori sevadyidzani vako, haiwa, iwe chifeve chikuru! Ndiani achakuponesa kubva kwandiri nehasha dzangu nehasha dzangu dzava kusvika dzinopisa pamusoro pako? Wamira uri mumaketani, wakamirira kutongwa kwako kwekupedzisira, uko kuchave pachena kuti nyika ione, uye kutya kukuru kuchauya pamusoro pevakawanda.

Tarirai! Marudzi mazhinji Baba vangu vakaparadza mukutonga, nokuti vanosimudza mumwe voputsa mumwe, nokuti Yahweh ndiye ane hutongi hwose uye hapana mukuru kuna Mwari mukuru asingaperi anomiririra uye anoponesa vake kubva mumoto wakachengeterwa vakaipa. Tarirai! Marudzi aiumba umambo hukuru achiripo, asi nenzira yakaderedzwa, asi akaenderera mberi. Asi iwe, haiwa, chifeve chikuru, wakazviita chifeve navadikani vazhinji nokuda kwokuchiva kwakashata nemafaro echivi. Wakatungamirira vanhu vangu kure neni pasi rose netsvina yawapa marudzi kuti adye nokunwa, uye uchawisirwa pasi zvachose, uye hauchazovipo zvakare. Uye kubva mumadota ako rudzi rukuru haruchazomuki, asi vakasara vachaponeswa. Ruoko rwomuvengi ruri pamitsipa yenyu, uye ndakaunza masvisvinwa mumiganhu yenyu. Vachapamba, vachatora, uye vachatora zvasara. Ndiani achakubatsira?

Nokuti wakaramba muvakidzani wako, uye hapana kutya Ishe mumwoyo yenyu. Wakati, handisi chirikadzi, asi uri chirikadzi nebofu nematsi pamberi pangu. Hapana ruponeso kwauri nokuda kwoupombwe hwako hwawaita nenyika; naizvozvo, ndichakuunza kuparadzwa kwakakwana. Zviitiko zviri kuuya zvichaitika nokukurumidza uye nokukurumidza; ipapo mafashamo makuru achafukidza nguo dzako. Moto uchakupisa zvachose uye uchachenesa vaiti vezvakaipa kubva munyika, uye vasungwa vangu vachasunungurwa, uye hapana chinogona kumisa zvava kuda kuitika. Ndini Ishe Jesu Kristu, uye ndinokuudzai zvisati zvaitika, uye ndinozivisa zvava kuda kuitika sekunge zvatoitika, nokuti mashoko aya ndeazvokwadi, uye hapana chinogona kuashandura, sezvo uprofita huchazadziswa.

Mirai makasimba, vanhu vangu, sezvo ndichabvuta mwenga wangu; nokuti ndine zvirongwa nezvinangwa naye muumambo hwangu huri kuuya. Achagara aneni, uye haazombobvi kwandiri. Ndinomuda uye ndinomuchengeta murudo rwakakwana netsitsi. Ndichakuitai vane simba mukubwinya kwangu, uye muchaita mabasa makuru munhaka yenyu iri kuuya, saka musatya. Zivai kuti mwoyo wangu unoputsika, uye ndinosuwa nevanhu vangu vasina kuzvigadzirira. Vamwe vachasiiwa shure nokuti mwari wenyika ino akapofumadza pfungwa dzavo, uye vazhinji havana kugadzirira. Musatya, kunyange muchanditamburira zvakawanda, imi mose muchaponeswa munotakura chisimbiso chaMwari mupenyu. Ndichakugoverai kana mukasaramba zita rangu, kana, uye mukandishandira mubasa randakakupai.

Muchaita mabasa makuru kana mukangondikumbira kuti ndikupei basa. Ndakakudanai uye ndinokudai zvikuru, uye avo vanodana pazita rangu votsungirira kusvika kumagumo vachaponeswa vomutswa mukubwinya kukuru vopiwa mubairo nokusingaperi-peri nokuda kwokumira kwenyu, sezvo ndinokudai uye ndakazvitaura kuti nzeve dzenyu dzinzwe. Kune kukohwa kunoverengeka uye kumwe nokumwe panguva yako, saka musatya; nokuti nguva dzenhamo dzinogara dzichipiwa varume vakuru vaMwari kuti vafudze boka, uye ndakakudanai. Ngirozi dzangu dzichaisa chisimbiso pazviuru zana nemakumi mana nezvina, uye vamwe venyu muchavabatsira mumabasa avo nebasa randakavapa. Musatya, nokuti ndichaita zviratidzo zvizhinji, zvishamiso, nezvishamiso namaoko enyu, saka zororai murudo rwangu uye muzorore musimba rangu; nokuti ndinemi uye handizombokusiyi kana kukurasai.

Muchaziva kuti ndabvuta mwenga wangu, uye ndichave ndaunganidza vakatendeka kwandiri kuti ndikonzere kutendeuka kune vakawanda munyika iri kufa. Saka regai nyika ipfuure uye musagoverana munjodzi yayo neramangwana rayo nokuti hazvisi zvenyu kana muchizotsaura Ishe mumwoyo yenyu morarama makazvitsaurira kwandiri. Nokuti uipi hukuru huri kusvika, uye hahuna kumbomuka nenzira iyi, uye hahuchazomukizve, vana vangu vadiki, nokuti ndakazvizivisa. Nyaradzwa nenzira yandakakugadzirirai, uye chengetai tsoka dzenyu dzakasimba munzira. Ndichava nemi uye ndichakupai rugare runopfuura kunzwisisa, uye muchava norugare pakati penyika yenyonganyonga, rugare ruchakuchengetai kana mukasatsaukira kuruboshwe kana kurudyi.

Musatora chiratidzo, uye musatenda nhema. Vachapa rugare noutano nokuchengeteka kune avo vanotora chiratidzo, uye vachakuudzai kuti muri kupinda munyika itsva yorugare nokubudirira nokuchengeteka. Musatenda nhema dzenyoka, nokuti mambo wavo zvirokwazvo haazochengeti shoko rake. Munhu uyo wechivi ane basa rimwe chete, uye ndiro: kuparadza.

Hapana chokwadi maari, uye Satani pachake achadzora matomu ake. Hakuzovi norugare kubva kwaari, asi shoko rimwe nerimwe richava nhema inonyaradza, yakagadzirirwa kutungamirira nyika mukuparadzwa kwakakwana. Uye dai ndisina kupfupisa mazuva, hapana nyama yaizoponeswa kubva padambudziko raachauyisa panyika, nokuti achapiwa simba rokuzadzisa zvinangwa zvangu kwenguva pfupi. Ipapo achasungwa mumaketani anorema erima mugomba rakadzika-dzika kuti asunungurwe kwenguva duku kuti anyengere nyika kekupedzisira. Kana aunganidza kwari, vacharwisa guta iroro guru, uye moto unobva kuna Mwari uchavaparadza vose. Ipapo kutongwa kwekupedzisira, hongu, kutongwa kwechigaro chikuru chichena kuchaitika pavari vose, uye vachashaya mushonga. Ndinokuudzai zvinhu izvi kuti ndikupei tariro uye ndigadzirire vanhu vangu, nokuti Chakavanzika Chikuru chaMwari chichatozadziswa.

Ndini nzira, chokwadi, noupenyu.

Ndini Mambo wamadzimambo naShe wamadzishe.

Ndini iye akakodzera kuzarura zvisimbiso, uye nguva dzasvika.

Ndini iye anoedza matomu evasanangurwa vangu kuti ndikunatsai nokukuchenesai muropa rangu rokuyananisa.

Ndini Mwanakomana waMwari akauya panyama, akarovererwa, akamutswa pazuva rechitatu, akaonekwa navakawanda, uye akagamuchirwa kumatenga pamberi pameso avo.

Ndini mwanakomana waDhavhidhi, uye ndichaunganidza vakasara vaJakobho pasi pemaoko angu enyaradzo nesimba.

Ndini iye achazadzisa kuda kwose kwaBaba vangu.

Ndava kuda kuita vavengi vangu chitsiko chetsoka dzangu, uye vane nhamo avo vanondipikisa, nokuti hasha dzeGwayana dzikuru.

Ndini iye achaisa chisikwa chaBaba vangu pasi pake uye ndopa zvinhu zvose muruoko rwake, kuti Mwari ave zvose mune zvose.

Ndini iye ane umambo husingaperi-peri, uye ndichapa kune vasanangurwa vangu kuti vadye kubva pamuti woupenyu uye vanwe kubva munzizi dzemvura mhenyu, uye hamuchazovi nenzara kana nyota zvakare, uye ndichapukuta misodzi yose pameso enyu.

Ini, Ishe, ndazvitaura, uye ndichazviita. Nguva yasvika, saka tarisirai kuonekwa kwangu, sezvo pasina chinogona kumisa ruoko rwangu rune simba.
