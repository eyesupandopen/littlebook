Chipupuriro Chiviri: Kereke
Mumabasa aJohane Mubhabhatidzi
Kurume 15, 2024

Chinhu Chinoshamisa

Shoko raIshe riri pamusoro pangu, tarirai shoko raIshe!

Tarisai! Ndiri kuita chinhu chinoshamisa pamberi penyu, uye vazhinji venyu munoramba kuona!

Tarisai! Ndiri kuita basa guru pakati penyu richakurumidza kuzadziswa!

Tarisai! Ndiri kuita basa guru mamuri, ramunoponeswa nekuitwa vatsva naro!

Ndakaita zvinopfuura kugadzirisa zvidimbu zvishoma zvakaputsika. Ndakaita zvinopfuura kukubatanidzai zvakare, apo pane misono nemavanga. Ndakaita vasanangurwa vangu nevanotendeka kuti vave vakakwana mandiri, Kristu mupenyu, uye ndakakusikai patsva. Zivai kuti upenyu hwenyu hwakavigwa mandiri, uye pandichazviratidza, nemiwo muchazviratidza pamwe neni mukubwinya uye kubva ipapo muchava neni nekusingaperi. Muri mwenga wangu uye muri muviri wangu; handina kukudanai nemazita here? Handina kukugadzirirai nzira yekudzokera kuna Baba venyu, Baba vezviedza here?

Ndini iye aizivikanwa naBaba vangu nyika isati yavapo, akatumwa akazvarirwa ruponeso rwenyu, akarovererwa, achipa donhwe rangu rekupedzisira reropa nokuda kwezvivi zvenyu. Hapana chakasiiwa uye hapana chisina kuitwa, uye zvakataurwa zvichaitwa nekuzadziswa kusvika pakupedzisira. Ndakakusikai nemweya wangu mutsvene wakapiwa naBaba kuti udururirwe pamusoro pevaapositori vangu pazuva iro rekare rePendekosti, pavakataura mabasa anoshamisa aMwari nendimi dzavaisanzwisisa ivo pachavo. Ndakadana, uye kunyange vazhinji vakandiramba, vakasara venyasha nesarudzo vakadavira, uye ndakaita basa guru mavari richakurumidza kuratidzwa. Zvirokwazvo, pamunotarisa muchionioni, hamuoni unhu hwenyu chaihwo. Ndakakuitai vane simba muutera hwenyu, uye pakuzviratidza kwangu ndizvo zvamuchaitawo. Muchafanana neni, uye muchava neni nekusingaperi. Ndini chibereko chekutanga chevakafa, uye kukohwa kwava pedyo nemi. Pandichazviratidza, muchasimuka kuzosangana neni, uye muchava muimba yaBaba vangu pandinokutorai kuenda kumusha wenyu kumatenga.

Zvirokwazvo, zvirokwazvo, muShoko rangu dzvene (sezvo ndiri shoko rakaitwa nyama riri dzvene uye rinofamba pakati penyu), hongu, mushoko rangu dzvene, hamuna kuverenga here kuti kutonga kunotanga paimba yaMwari? Ndava kuda kupedzisa basa muvasanangurwa vangu, vakarurama, uye vakasarudzwa, ramuchasikwa patsva zvizere mandiri uye muchava sezvandiri, nekuti makafananidzwa nemufananidzo weMwanakomana mumwechete akaberekwa waMwari. Mubairo wangu uneni, uye handizokutongii nekuda kwezvivi zvenyu; handina kutozvibhadharira here? Nekugamuchira kwenyu Ushe hwangu nekuzvizivisa kwenyu neni, makaregererwa uye makarurama mandiri, nekuti ndakabhadhara mutengo unotyisa wakanga usiri wangu kubhadhara sezvo ndisina chivi. Kwete, ndakatora zvivi zvenyu, kusarurama kwenyu, huroyi hwenyu, nehasha nekutongwa kubva kwamuri kuitira kuti mubatanidzwe naBaba neMwanakomana. Kwete, ndichatonga mabasa enyu akaitwa panyika.

Uye nekutonga kwangu kune mubairo mukuru, uye mabasa mazhinji achapona ari endarama, sirivha, nematombo anokosha. Zvirokwazvo, huni, uswa, nemashanga zvichangopiswa sehundi, uye mucharasikirwa, kunyange muchizoponeswa sekunge nemoto. Nekuti moto wangu kwamuri imoto unonatsa, mandakanatsa mweya yenyu semupfuri wendarama anochenesa ndarama yake, achibvisa tsvina. Mirai makasimba uye munyarare mandiri. Ndiani angakunetsai, kunyange zvirokwazvo muchitambudzwa nekutsungirira dambudziko? Musatya, vakandivenga uye vanokuvengaiwo. Asi, muruponeso rwenyu muchazosvika pakuyemurwa zvikuru mukubwinya kwandichakupfekedzai pazuva iro riri pedyo uye riri kusvika nekukurumidza. Baba vangu vanoshuva kukuonai pamberi pavo muimba yavo yechigaro cheushe, apo zvigaro zvenyu zvakamirira imi, nekuti Baba vangu vakakugadzikai muzvigaro zvekudenga. Hamusi vasanangurwa vavo here, vamiririri vavo, kubwinya kwavo, korona, nemubairo wekukosha kusingaverengeki? Zvirokwazvo, hamuna mutengo.

Kutonga kuchawira pamusoro peBhabhironi Guru munguva pfupi, uye kuchava kutonga kunotyisa, asi kwakakodzera. Haiwa iwe munhu, ndichakutsungirira kusvika rinhi? Wakabva parudzi rwevatsvene rwaishanda sechiedza kunyika kuti iuye kwandiri, ukava rudzi rwemhombwe dzinoupombwe. Ndakaparadzanisa vanhu vangu kubva kwamuri, uye kuparadzaniswa kwava kuda kuzadziswa sezvo nguva ino yenyasha yava kusvika kumagumo. Haiwa iwe munhu, unotevererei wakaipa, uchigamuchira fufuro dzake dzisina maturo pamusoro pangu, Kristu wekusingaperi, mukubwinya kwangu kwese nekukudzwa.

Baba vangu vakapa nguva kuti marudzi ese atendeke, uye vanhu vazhinji, kunyange boka guru, vakagamuchira chipo changu munyasha dzangu dzinoponesa dzakadururirwa pavari.

Asi iwe, haiwa munhu wenyama anoramba zvinhu zvemweya uye anozvidza muvakidzani wake, uchatsungirira kutonga kukuru panyika yako, uye Bhabhironi Guru harichazovipozve. Bhabhironi, wava rudzi rwemhombwe dzinotamba basa racho nezvikabudura zviri pamusoro pemisoro yadzo sembizi yemusango iri pakupisa ichimirira mupfuuri anotevera. Wazvisvibisa nenyama isingazivikanwe, wakafarira uye wakada zvivi zvako, uye kutonga kusingaperi kuchawira pamusoro pevose vane moyo yakaoma vanoona chibayiro changu sechinhu chakareruka uye chisina maturo chekuona. Wava rudzi rwevaiti vezvakaipa nevanoputsa zvivimbiso vanochinja vakadzi sembatya dzekare vachitsvaga itsva kusvikira itsva yasvibiswa. Mashoko ako makuru awakataura uchipikisa Baba vangu achazvidavirwa. Zvifungidziro zvako zvisina maturo zvichaiswa pamberi pako, uye uchazvidavirira. Asi kuzvidavirira kwako hakuzombokwani kuti umire wakarurama pamberi pangu. Kuvapo kwako kwese kwakaora, uye mukupenga kwako wakasarudza nyama yako inoora, nekuti sekutaura kwakaita Baba vangu, muchafa zvirokwazvo. Asi, zvichiri kuita sezvisinganzwisisike kwauri kuti mukufa muchafa. Kunyange kufa kwako kuzere kusina kuitika pakarepo, wanga uchifa kubva pazuva rekutanga kufema kwako. Kutonga kwaziviswa pamusoro perudzi rwako, uye matenga haadanidziri zvimwechetezvo here? Ndichaparadza zvachose nyika yako, uye shongwe dzako huru dzichawa, uye rima guru richafukidza nyika. Masvingo ako akatoputswa, uye mamiriyoni auya kuzokutorera zvandakavapa, nekuti ndichakodza ruoko rwevavengi vako mukutonga kwako. Nguva yako yava kuda kupera, uye uri rudzi rwakasungwa pamberi pangu, uchimirira ngirozi dzangu kuti dziuye mukutonga. Uchawa zvikuru sezvo mvura ichiuya pamusoro pako, sekuzununguka kunouya pamusoro pako, sezvo mauto achisunungurwa, uye nyika yako ichatsemurwa nepakati. Nzara ichagara pamusoro penyika, uye hondo ichauya kunyika yese sezvo chikara chichisimuka kubva mumadota muzvirongwa zvavo zvakarongwa kwenguva refu.

Nguva yakapiwa yekutendeuka, uye vazhinji vakadavira mukutenda, tsitsi huru dzandakapa kutonga kusati kwaitwa. Handifadzwi nekuparadzwa kuri kuuya, asi kunyange ikozvino, vazhinji vanogara muchivi chikuru, vachitodya vana venyu chaivo, uye hamuna basa nazvo chero mari yenyu iri pedyo. Ndichatora hupfumi hwese, zvinonaka, mufaro, uye rufaro kubva kwamuri. Muchaoma pamberi pematenga, muchitsvaga chidimbu chekudya icho vazhinji vasingazowani. Asi, avo vanodana pazita raIshe vachaponeswa.

Vana vangu vadiki, inguva yekupedzisira yenguva yekupedzisira munguva ino yenyasha. Ndakatuma vaporofita vangu mubasa reshamwari yangu inodiwa Johane Mubhabhatidzi. Havana kuva inzwi rinodanidzira murenje here? Havana kuva vafudzi vangu, vachidana nekutsvaga vekupedzisira veboka rangu rakarasika here? Zvirokwazvo, ndine rimwe dangawo, uye nguva yeruponeso rwavo yava pedyo, sezvo Jakobho achaponeswa uye vakasara vaJakobho vachandigamuchira saIshe wavo, sezvo vose vakasanangurwa vachiva munhu mumwe mutsva. Ndakatuma vazhinji pasi rese, vachizivisa kusvika kwangu.

Kunyange vazhinji vevanhu vangu vakanyengedzwa uye vachidziya, mweya wangu uchamuka muvazhinji, uye vazhinji vachadzokera kwandiri. Kuzviratidza kwangu kwava pedyo. Mirai makasimba uye danidzirai kusvika kwangu uye danidzirai mashoko angu eruponeso kune vose. Nguva yava kuda kusvika, uye kunyarara kuri pakadzika sezvo hondo dzekudenga dzakamirira kurira kwehwamanda kuri pedyo kupfuura zvamunofungidzira. Asi, yasara nguva pfupi pfupi, uye vazhinji vachiri kuzoponeswa, saka musaneta.

Ndini iye anofudza kereke yangu uye akapa zvipo kuvanhu.

Ndini Ishe mukuru uye ane simba, akabayiwa kubva pakuvambwa kwenyika.

Ndini iye akapa upenyu hwake nokuda kweboka, Kristu mupenyu uyo Mwari akamutsa kubva kuvakafa.

Ndini Shoko rakaitwa nyama rinogara pakati penyu.

Ndini mwanakomana mumwechete akaberekwa waMwari akauya munyama, kunyange Mesia ane simba weIsraeri yaMwari.

Ndiri pedyo, uye kuzviratidza kwangu kwava pedyo.

Ndini mutongi akarurama akakodzera kuzarura zvisimbiso.

Ndini gwayana raMwari, rakabayiwa nokuda kwezvivi zvevanhu vangu.

Ndini muponesi asina chivi anoponesa kusvika pakupedzisira.

Ndini murume akajairana neshungu huru nemarwadzo, asi mufaro wangu unoramba wakazara.

Ndini Ishe mupenyu, mufudzi akarurama.

Ndini iye akaporofitwa nezvake nevaporofita vose uye ndine makiyi erufu negehena, uye Baba vakaisa zvinhu zvose kudenga nepanyika mumaoko angu ane simba.
