Chapupu Chiviri: Israeri
Zvinovava Zvinotapira
Chikunguru 15, 2024

Sarudzo Ndeyenyu!

Teererai kumashoko omuponesi wenyu, Ishe naMambo, Vana vangu, uye rerekai nzeve dzenyu kuMashoko aShe wenyu, iye Jesu Kristu akarurama, nokuti ndiri mwanakomana wokururama uye ndinosimuka mukubwinya kwangu, uye makura semhuru dzomuchidyiro murugare nokuchengeteka, uye ndakakurererai chinangwa chokudenga chava kuda kuzarurirwa zvizere mamuri, imi vakatendeka vangu nevakasarudzwa. Tarirai! Ndini iye unoedza mwoyo yavanhu kuti muzive nechokwadi kuti muri mukutenda zvechokwadi here, kana kuti makanyengerwa uye makatorwa nezvivi zvenyu uye nokunyengera kukuru kwazvino kwakabata Nyika.

Nokuti ndini iye Yahweh waakagoverana naye chigaro chake chovushe, iye Kristu Jesu Ishe akamuka uye akapiwa simba, uye ndiri Ishe wamadzishe naMambo wamadzimambo uye tarirai Shumba yoRudzi rwaJudha iri kuuya mumakore kuzogamuchira vakatendeka vangu kwandiri kuti vatorerwe kudenga kumuchato weGwayana, nokuti Mwenga wangu wazvigadzirira. Uye marambi ake azere, uye wachekerera tambo dzawo, uye wakamirira kuonekwa kwangu semhandara yakachenjera, ivo vandakanatsa zvizere uye vasina chavanopomerwa pamberi pechigaro chovushe chaMwari. Ivo vasina kutorwa nounyanzvi naBharabhasi, nokuti Bharabhasi unounza kupandukira kwechisimba, hondo nenjodzi, nokuti iye zvino musoro mumwe pakati pemisoro yakawanda inoshanda pamwe chete, asi achamuka saMoshiach wenhema, uye akasarudzwa navanhu venyika kuti atonge sinagogi raSatani. Nokuti ndiye mugari wenhaka waivavo vakasarudzwa namadzibaba avo, uye vari kuzvigadzirira kumugamuchira saMambo wavo.

Asi imi, haiwa Kereke yeRaodhikiya, zvino nguva dzenyu dzokuparadzwa nokutongwa dzasvika. Nokuti makazvisarudzira mumwe muponesi saMambo wenyu, iye kuuya kwake kunoziviswa nezviratidzo nenhamo dzenhema uye iye zvino akakutungamirirai nouroyi nezvitsinga. Ndini Yeshua HaMashiach uye ndakakutengai neropa rangu rinoregerera, risina chivi, uye ndini iye aizokuitai vatano, uye kukusikai patsva mumweya wangu, nokuti ndakaitwa mweya unopa upenyu nokuti saizvozvo Yahweh akaronga hukuru hwose paMwanakomana wake, iye kupera kwegwayana raMwari neShumba yoRudzi rwaJudha! Uye mukururama ndichatonga uye ndichaita hondo nokuti gwayana raMwari neShumba yoRudzi rwaJudha zvakaperera mandiri, Kristu Jesu, iye Yeshua HaMashiach nokuti ndizvo zvandiri!

Ndichapedzisa ruponeso rwenyu, haiwa Kereke yeRaodhikiya, asi hamuna kugadzirira kupinda muHumambo hwangu panguva ino nokuti makatsauka pachinangwa chenyu chimwe chete muna Kristu! Ndichakuratidzai kukanganisa kwenyu, uye mune mukana wokutendeuka pazvivi zvose, asi hamuchina nguva yasara yokupikisana nezwi raShe wenyu, nokuti chivi ichi chinorwadza ndechimwe chezvizhinji zvakashandiswa naSatani kukusungai kuti musauye kwandiri zvizere mukutendeuka kuti ndikunatsai zvizere muropa rangu rinoregerera pazvivi zvenyu.

Tarirai! Nguva dzeveMarudzi dzapera uye muti womuorivhi wemusango wakabatanidzwa pakati pavo, uye pamwe chete navo unodya pamudzi nomukobvu woMuti woMuorivhi, zvino uchagurwa. Nokuti makafuratira Mashoko aKristu Jesu, nokuti ndakakuudzai pachena kuti musazvirumbidza pamusoro pemapazi akadonha mukusatenda: asi kana muchizvirumbidza, hamutakuri mudzi, asi mudzi unokutakurai. Ndini Mudzi waJese, uye ndini iye unomira sechiedza chetariro chenguva dzose, iye unounza ruponeso nenzira dzaYahweh panyika uye ndichatonga nhaka yangu kubva pachigaro chovushe chaDhavhidhi mukururama uye ndakatenga nyika neropa rangu risina chivi uye ndichatora izvo zviri zvangu nechisimba kubva mumaoko evagari zvisiri pamutemo, ivo vakawa.

Ndini iye akapa nyasha netsitsi kunyika, nokuti nguva yose yenyasha yakapiwa kwandiri naYahweh nokuda kworuponeso rweveMarudzi. Nokuti zvinangwa zvaYahweh muna Israeri zvaigara zviri zvokuti veMarudzi varopafadzwe uye vauye kuruponeso kuburikidza nokuteerera kwaIsraeri. Asi nokufamba kwenguva vakava vanodada, uye vanozvirumbidza, uye vakagurwa pamuti womuorivhi wemusikirwo mukusatenda. Asi imi, Kereke yangu yeRaodhikiya, imi ndimi muenzaniso chaiwo worudzi urwu rwokuzvikudza, kusatenda, noupofu hunoratidzwa namapazi omusikirwo akagurwa mukusatenda, uye zvino muchagurwa pamuti womuorivhi wemusikirwo. Uye zvino ndichafamba kuti ndipedzise basa rangu pamuti womuorivhi wemusikirwo, iye Israeri hama yangu navanhu vangu, vose vandakasarudza kuti vandigamuchire saShe wavo kuti ndivasike patsva somunhu mumwe mutsva uye kuti chakavanzika chaMwari chipedziswe, iko kupera kwamabasa angu oruponeso anoregerera.

Uye imi, Kereke yangu yeRaodhikiya, makasiya rudo rwenyu rwokutanga uye muri mumaoko omunyengeri wamakapikira kuvimbika. Makazvisarudzira Bharabhasi mutsva saMambo wenyu uye makaramba Jesu weNazareta saShe wenyu uye muri mumaoko omumwe muponesi wenhema. Iye anozviita somuponesi weBhabhironi rakavanzika, anotaura uye anoita somumwe wenyu, asi anotaura seshato. Nokuti Bhabhironi rakavanzika rinorarama zvakapfuma uye mukuwanda kukuru kwezvinhu, mufaro noupfumi.

Uye Bhabhironi rakavanzika rakadhakisa nyika yose netsvina youpombwe huri mumukombe waro wendarama, asi ndidzo nzira dzevakawa, vanopa tsvina yakaipisisa, uye imi munoimwa nokuzvidira uye nomufaro mukuru nokuti makapofumadzwa nomukombe wendarama kutsvina huru yamunodya zvine tsvina. Nokuti kunyange Kereke yangu yeRaodhikiya haichakungani pamagwaro angu matsvene asi pamisangano yaBharabhasi, wamunovimba naye. Uye saka munosarudza muponesi mutsva nokuti munoda nyika nezvinhu zvenyika uye hamugoni kufungidzira pfungwa yokuti mungarasikirwa nezvinonaka zvenyu zvinokosha, kunze kwokuti ndichabvisa mufaro wose kwamuri mukutsamwa kwangu kuri kusvika, uye kunyange zvino kurasikirwa kwenyu kuri kuwanda!

Nokuti muri mapofu uye makanyengerwa mukuzvikudza kwenyu kuti Yahweh aizopa mumwe muponesi kunze kwangu, Jesu weNazareta, nokuti ndini mbeu yakapikirwa yomukadzi uye Dhani mwana weshumba uyo pasina Mesia achamuka maari. Ndinzwei uye ndinzwei zvakanaka, haiwa kereke yeRaodhikiya, nokuti zvino inguva yokutendeuka kwenyu pakudarika kwemwoyo yenyu kana kuti zvino inguva yandichakusiyai kumoto wokunatsa uye vose vanodana zita raShe kusvikira kumagumo vachaponeswa uye vazhinji vakareurura zita rangu zvisina kutendeka vachawira mukukanganwa, vapambadza mukana wavo pachitarisiko choumwari chakaramba simba racho: Imi vaRaodhikiya vangu, ndini simba racho uye mandiri Yahweh akaisa hukuru hwose, uye makandiramba, Ishe wenyu nomuponesi wenyu, uye ndichakusvipai mumuromo mangu kunze kwokunge matendeuka zvino!

Munguva yangu yenyasha, ruponeso rwakapararira pakati pemarudzi, uye rudzi pashure porudzi rwakagoverana uye rwakadya pamudzi womuti womuorivhi wemusikirwo. Uye zvino, marudzi haachazovi chiedza choruponeso uye nguva yawo muMwanakomana yakauya nokurambwa kwehama yangu navanhu vangu nokuti vakadanidzira mukusatenda kuti sunungurai kwatiri Bharabhasi! Asi sezvo zvakavanzika zvaYahweh zvakazarurwa navapostori vangu vatsvene navaprofita, ndakadzora Bharabhasi uye handina kumusunungura zvizere, asi ndakangobvumira chakavanzika chokusarurama kuti chienderere mberi. Uye zvino nguva dzasvika dzandichasunungura Bharabhasi zvizere sezvo mudzori wangu abviswa uye sezvo vakatendeka vangu, vakadanwa, uye vakasarudzwa vatorwa kudenga kwandakavagadzirira dzimba dzavo dzechigarire, uye vachapfekedzwa mucheka wakaisvonaka usina ruvara usingambosvibiswi kana kushatiswa.

Makapofumara kudaro kunyika yenyu, uye makafa nzeve zvokuti hamuchanzwi inzwi rangu here haiwa Kereke yeRaodhikiya? Makatenda nhema dzakaunzwa nechakavanzika chokusarurama, uye makabvumira mbiriso ine chepfu muMaBhaibheri enyu akaora izvo zvakaunza kuora kwakakwana muMakereke enyu izvo zvakasvibisa mwoyo yenyu nengano mukusatenda kusina kururama. Makabvumira nhema dzen'anga dzedhisipensesheni kuti dzikuroyei nemagwaro enhema namashoko enhema. Hamuchanzwisisi magwaro, uye makazviunganidzira matambudziko makuru achauya pamusoro penyu pakarepo. Shoko rose raMwari rakachena uye rakaitirwa kuteerera kwokutenda nokuvakwa kwevanogamuchira. Nzwisisai Mashoko eMashoko angu uye nzwisisai nguva dzamunorarama.

Nokuti ndakaita kuti Johane Muzaruri anyore kuti: Uye ndakaona chikara chichibuda mugungwa, chine misoro minomwe, nenyanga gumi, uye panyanga dzacho paiva nekorona gumi, uye pamisoro yacho zita rokumhura Mwari.

Uye zvakare Johane Muzaruri akanyora kuti: Ndakaona mukadzi agere pamusoro pechikara chitsvuku, chakazara namazita okumhura Mwari, chine misoro minomwe, nenyanga gumi. Uye mukadzi wacho akanga akapfeka nguo tsvuku neruvara rwepepuru, uye akashongedzwa nendarama, namabwe anokosha nemaparera, aine mukombe wendarama muruoko rwake, wakazara nezvinonyangadza netsvina youpombwe hwake.

Uye zvakare: Uye nyanga gumi dzawakaona pamusoro pechikara, idzi dzichavenga chifeve, uye dzichachimuparadza, nokumusiya asina kupfeka, uye dzichadya nyama yacho, nokumupisa nomoto. Nokuti Mwari akaisa mumwoyo yavo kuti vazadzise kuda kwake, uye kuti vabvumirane, uye vape humambo hwavo kuchikara, kusvikira mashoko aMwari azadziswa. Uye mukadzi, wawaona, ndiro Guta guru iro rinotonga pamusoro pamadzimambo enyika.

Vana vangu, nzwisisai kuti chikara chinogona kureva vanhu uyewo humambo, dzidzai kuti mudzidze. Chikara chinotakura hure chichaparadza hure uye hachichazovi pasi pehure, nokuti chakatonga madzimambo enyika muupombwe hwacho. Saka chikara chichabuda mugungwa chichabudisa Mahdi wavo anotarisirwa, asi chichaparadzawo zvachose Bhabhironi Rakavanzika, nokuti Mwari akaisa hure guru mumaoko evavengi varo vanotorivenga zvachose.

Chikara chinobva mugungwa nechikara chinobva panyika zvisati zvamuka zvizere muHumambo hweshato, kune vatongi vazhinji vakabatana pachinangwa chokupa simba ravo nokutonga kwavo kushhato, nokuti Mwari akaisa mumwoyo yavo kuti vazviite. Uye vachaparadza Bhabhironi rakavanzika. Munofunga kuti makagona magwaro angu matsvene nokuti makatenda vadzidzisi venyu venhema? Nzwisisai uye munzwe Mashoko aShe wenyu!

Zvakare, ndakaita kuti Johane Muzaruri anyore kuti: Uye chikara chandakaona, chakanga chakafanana nembada, uye tsoka dzacho dzakaita sedzebheya, uye muromo wacho somuromo weshumba: uye shato yakachipa simba rayo nechigaro chayo chovushe, nesimba guru. Uye ndakaona mumwe wemisoro yacho sokunge wakuvadzwa kusvika pakufa, asi ronda rawo rinouraya rakaporeswa, uye nyika yose yakashamisika ikatevera chikara.

Vana vangu, mashoko aYahweh ane udzamu hwakawanda, husingagoni kugonwa nomunhu. Chikara chinorondedzerwa humambo huchamuka kubva mukuparadzwa kwehure, uye kubva muhurongwa hwekare huchamuka hurongwa hutsva! Chinomiririra chikara chegungwa, nokuti chichamuka saMahdi wavo, uye chinomiririra chimiro chehumambo hwaachatonga gungwa. Sei musingaremekedzi mashoko eShoko rangu? Haritauri kuti chikara chinobva mugungwa pachacho chakakuvadzwa, nokuti munhu upi ane misoro minomwe chaiyo? Asi mumwe wemisoro yacho sokunge wakuvadzwa kusvika pakufa. Haiwa, mapenzi, uye mune mwoyo inononoka kutenda zvose zvakataurwa naVaprofita! SOKUNGE wakuvadzwa kusvika pakufa. Mashoko okuti "sokunge" anofanira kukumisai panzira dzenyu, asi hamuchatendi kunyange duku remashoko angu, uye ini ndiri Shoko rakaitwa nyama. Hazvina kujeka kwamuri here, SOKUNGE, idimikira rekare? Hazvina kujeka kwamuri here kuti "kunge" iri muchimiro chechishuwo? Hazvina kujeka kwamuri here kuti kupfupikisa kwe, sokunge zvaive zvakadaro?

Asi kwete, munotenda nhema dzeshato, nokuti Bharabhasi anotaura seshato, asi haasi Bharabhasi, asi chikara chinobva panyika. Uye saka zvino nyika yose ichashamisika nechikara nokuda kwezviratidzo zvacho zvenhema nenhamo, uye chichaita zvimwe zviratidzo zvakawanda zvenhema nenhamo, uye hachina kumuka zvizere nokuti hure haisati yapiswa nomoto, asi zvino razarurwa munguva yaro. Rinotaura seshato, richiti "Mwari oga akamuponesa kubva paronda rake romusoro" uye idzi inhema dzinomhura Mwari. Yahweh, Mwari mukuru wekusingaperi, akatuma ini Jesu weNazareta somuponesi wenyika. Ini naBaba vangu tiri vamwe mukubatana nechinangwa uye Yahweh haana kuponesa Bharabhasi uye neniwo handina, Kristu Jesu. Makanyengerwa uye makatorwa uye makabatwa mukunyengera kukuru, uye ndinoti kwamuri tendeukai uye mudzokere kurudo rwenyu rwokutanga uye muitire izvozvi, nokuti hapana nguva!

Nokuti ndini iye unoedza mwoyo, uye ndini iye unoedza vose vandinodana kuruponeso, nokuti ndinonatsa vose mumoto wokunatsa! Tarirai! Ndini iye unogara somunatsi nomuchenesi wesirivha: uye ndinonatsa vanakomana vaYahweh, uye ndinovachenesa sendarama nesirivha, kuti vape kuna Ishe chipiriso mukururama. Hamunzwisisi here kuti ndichakuitai vapristi namadzimambo pamberi paBaba vangu? Kunyange zvazvo chipo changu chomweya mutsvene, chamunoberekwa nacho patsva nembeu isingaori chakakwana uye chisingadi kunatswa, imi makabva kuna Adhamu neropa uye munofanira kubvumira zvizere basa rangu kuti riitwe muupenyu hwenyu, kunze kwokunge mbeu yangu yawira panzvimbo ine matombo.

Zvino nguva dzokutonga dzasvika uye nguva dzenyasha dzapera. Kunyange zvazvo vazhinji vachaseka mashoko angu uye vachifuratira mukusatenda, zvakatozivikanwa naYahweh mugumo wacho. Nokuti Yahweh anogara achiratidza kuvanhu nzira dzake dzakaora uye anoranga vanhu pachivi, achiziva murudo rwake nouchenjeri husingaperi kuti achaponesa vamwe. Uye sezvo Yahweh akaomesa mwoyo waFarao, saizvozvowo achaomesa mwoyo yevazhinji vanonzwa asi vasingateereri. Yahweh achaedza munhu kakawanda. Uye vamwe vachaita saFarao uye vachaomesa mwoyo yavo zvakare mukusatenda asi vamwe vachabva panzira dzaFarao uye vachamukira kuupenyu husingaperi muna Kristu Jesu, asi kuburikidza nomoto wokunatsa.

Nzwisisai kuti avo vanosarudza Bharabhasi vachazviita vatori vechikamu mumhosva dzaBharabhasi, uye avo vanoramba kudana zita rangu kusvikira kumagumo vose vachatongwa nokuti vakafadzwa nokusarurama. Nokuti muponesi wenyu mutsva haasi muponesi asi chikara chinoparadza. Uye hurongwa hwake noumambo hwake hunoumbwa naavo vanokuvadza vana. Hamunzwisisi nzira dzeShato here? Nokuti avo vanotevera nzira dzeshato vanodya vana. Vanobayira vana kuna Moreki, vanotsvaka tsime ravo roudiki kubva muropa revana vanotambudzwa, vanoshungurudza vana nokuda kwekugutsikana kwavo kunorwara, uye vanoita tsika yokurova mwana ari mudumbu kuti vauraye vaduku vasati vaberekwa, uye vakakutungamirirai muchivande munzira dzavo, nokuti ndidzo nzira dzevatungamiriri veBhabhironi Rakavanzika uyewo humambo hweshato. Uye zvino kutonga kwaMwari kunouya nokuda kwezvinhu izvi nezvimwe zvakawanda. Nokuti dai musina kuona zvinhu izvi mungadai musina chivi, asi zvino mava kuona uye chivi chenyu chinoramba chiripo.

Hamuna kuverenga here? Zvanzi naIshe, Inzwi rakanzwika kumusoro, kuchema nokuungudza kunovava. Rakeri, achichema vana vake, akaramba kunyaradzwa nokuda kwevana vake, nokuti vakanga vasipo. Nokuti makakundikana pabasa ramakapiwa, uye makavimbiswa zvakavanzika zvangu uye makamira sechiedza choruponeso uye chiedza chetariro. Asi zvino majoinha avo vandakakutumai kuti murwise. Uye saka zvino nguva dzasvika dzandichakusiyai kune ramangwana renyu.

Ndinzwei uye ndinzwei zvakanaka mose mungaramba inzwi raYeshua HaMashiach, iro inzwi raKristu Jesu: hamuna nguva! Ndinokurairai kuti mundinzwe murudo rwangu kwamuri vana vangu uye mutendeuke zvino! Nokuti moto wokunatsa wava kuda kuwira pamusoro penyu zvizere, uye uchanatsa nyika yose youmambo hweshato uye nyika haichazovi pasi penzira dzaSatani uye humambo hwake hahuchazomukizve, nokuti ndiri Ishe wamadzishe naMambo wamadzimambo, uye ndichaparadza zvachose vakawa. Vose vanoramba kutendeuka vachava nechikamu chavo mugungwa romoto uye vachagoverana mukurangwa kwadhiabhorosi nengirozi dzake mugungwa guru iroro remoto wesurferi. Saizvozvowo, vose vanotendeuka nokubuda muchivi uye nokudana zita rangu kusvikira kumagumo vachaponeswa uye vachapiwa mubayiro mukuru usingaperi mandiri.

Tarirai! Zvino inguva yokuunganidzwa kwevakatendeka, uye zvino ndichasiya avo vanoramba kuna Bharabhasi, nokuti vakaita sarudzo yavo.

Avo vasingatendeuki kubva kumessia uyu wenhema vachawana ruponeso mumoto wokunatsa, kana kuti vachawira mukukanganwa nokusingaperi: Sarudzo ndeyenyu!
