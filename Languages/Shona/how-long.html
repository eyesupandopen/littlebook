Dzokerai kwandiri nemwoyo yenyu yose. Dzokerai kuna Ishe Mwari wenyu uye kumaoko angu akatambanudzwa!

KuMwenga wangu: Ndakakupai upenyu, asi upenyu hwenyu hwakavigwa mandiri; uye pandichazoratidzwa, nemiwo muchazoratidzwa muupenyu hwandakakupai. Makagamuchira pachena, asi zvakandibhadharisa mutengo mukuru serudzikinuro rwenyu. Muchashandisa nyasha dzangu senguva yekukotsira here? Kana kuti muchashandisa nyasha dzangu senguva yekuzvinatsa kubudikidza neropa rangu randakakudeurirai paKarivhari? Muchagamuchira kukoka kwangu kuti muparidzire nyika iri kufa uye muponese vazhinji vehama dzenyu here? Kana kuti muchafuratira kukoka kwangu uye mokoshesa mafaro ezvivi kwemwaka uri kuvharwa?

Kune vose, ndinoti: Dzokerai kwandiri nemwoyo yenyu yose; tendevukai nemunhu wenyu wose! Tsvakai chiso changu uye mugamuchire kuregerera kwangu, nekuti zuva raIshe rava pedyo, sezvandakayambira vanhu vangu. Handisi kuzonyarara, uye ndinokukurudzirai kuti mushamise miromo yenyu kuti muponese avo vamunoda uye avo vandichauyisa kwamuri. Rangarirai rudo rwenyu rwekutanga, uye muye kwandiri hwamanda isati yarira.
