Chipupuriro Chiviri: Kereke
Mumabasa aJohane MuZaruri
Chikumi 01, 2024

Kune vane 144,000: Nokuda kweNguva Yakadai Makadanwa uye Mukaiswa Chisimbiso

Ndimirirei uye mutevere muenzaniso wangu, nokuti VaHebheru vanotii pamusoro pokumira kwangu nokuda kwaYahweh? Uyo nokuda kwomufaro wakanga wakaiswa pamberi pake, wakatsungirira muchinjikwa, akazvidza kunyadziswa, uye akagadzwa kuruoko rworudyi rwechigaro choumambo chaMwari. Saka murudo rwangu kuna Baba, urwo rusingatyoki, uye mukuteerera kwangu kunobva murudo, uye murudo rwangu kuvanhu uye chido changu chokuzvipira kuti ndikuponesei, ndakatsungirira kurangwa kunotyisa, kurwadziwa, uye kutambura uye ndakazviita nokuzvidira, nomufaro mumwoyo mangu. Ndaiziva kuti kubudikidza nechibayiro changu norufu rwangu, vazhinji vaizoitwa vakarurama uye vapenyu nesimba rokumuka kwangu kuvakafa nokuti Yahweh pachake akandimutsa kuvakafa, nokuti hazvibviri kuti rufu rundibate. Uye nesimba iroro rimwe chete, nemiwo muchamutswa kuupenyu hutsva, nokuti ndakabhadhara mutengo nokupa ropa rangu risina chivi serudzikinuro kuna vose vachatenda muzita rangu, uye ani naani anodana zita raShe achaponeswa, avo vasingadi upenyu hwavo kusvikira kumagumo!

Uye zvino ndinokukumbirai, imi vakatendeka vangu uye vakasarudzwa, kunyange hama dzangu, kuti mundimirire uye musazvidzora. Nokuti ndakataura zvakajeka mutsamba yangu kuVaHebheru: Zvino wakarurama uchararama nokutenda; asi kana munhu akazvidzora, mweya wangu haungafadzwi naye.

Regai izvozvo zvinyure, uye muzviverenge zvakare, uye musadzokera kuTorah, nokuti Torah inochengetwa nokutenda mandiri; naizvozvo, wakarurama uchararama nokutenda. Munozivikanwa neni, uye ndini iye akakururamisai mumabasa angu oruyananiso uye neropa rangu risina chivi.

Vazhinji venyu kwazvo munotamburira kunzwisisa pfungwa yokuti ndakatora nzvimbo yenyu mukutongwa pamuti nokuda kwezvivi zvandisina kupara, uye ndakazviita nokuzvidira uye nomufaro kuti ndiunze ruponeso kumunhu wenyama akaora, kuti ndivadzorere kuna Yahweh, kunyange Mwari wenyu naBaba venyu, kunyange Baba veZveneko uyo asina kuchinjika kana mumvuri wokutendeuka. Nokuti Yahweh haachinji; iye ndiye mumwe chete zuro, nhasi, uye nokusingaperi!

Ndiye Mwari mumwe chete uyo madzibaba enyu akabvumirana naye musungano, kunyange paGomo reSinai apo Mosesi akagamuchira murayiro kubva kuna Yahweh pachake. Uye kunyange zvazvo murayiro uri mutsvene, uye murayiro wacho uri mutsvene, uye wakarurama, uye wakanaka, vanhu havana kumbobvira vakwanisa kurarama maererano nemitemo yawo nokuti murayiro wakapiwa kuti uparire chivi munyama, uye imi mose makaberekwa makafa mukudarika nezvivi nokuti chivi chiri munyama yenyu. Torah haina kupiwa nokuda kwavakarurama asi nokuda kwavasakarurama, kuti chivi chionekwe sechivi munyama. Torah yakapiwa somudzidzisi wenyu kuti ikuuyisei kwandiri, kunyange Kristu Jesu, Ishe wemadzishe naMambo wemadzimambo akakwidzwa!

Uye saka ndakazvidira kutora migumisiro yechivi pamusoro pangu, nokuti ndakaberekerwa kudzikinura vanhu kubva pakutukwa kwomurayiro. Nokuti murayiro wakanga usina kutukwa; hongu, murayiro ngaumire uye munhu wose ngaatukwe anotuka murayiro waMosesi, nokuti handina kuuya kuzoparadza murayiro, asi kusimbisa murayiro uye kunyange kudarika murayiro waMosesi. Munoti kumhura Mwari here?

Chipupuriro cheVaHebheru chinomira mukuyananisa pakati pedu: Nokuti kana ropa renzombe nerembudzi, namadota etsiru richisasa vasina kuchena, richivatsvenesa pakucheneswa kwenyama: Ko ropa raKristu, uyo kubudikidza noMweya usingaperi akazvipa asina gwapa kuna Mwari, haringatonyanyi kuchenesa hana dzenyu pamabasa akafa kuti mushumire Mwari mupenyu here?

Sezvo muchida Mosesi (seniwo), ndinokurayiridzai kuti muteerere norudo mirayiridzo yaMosesi; nokuti wakati: Yahweh Elohim wenyu achakumutsirai Muporofita pakati penyu, wehama dzenyu, akafanana neni; iye, muchamuteerera. Saka inzwai izwi raYeshua HaMashiach! Makanatswa pachivi nezvivi zvose zvamunopara neropa rangu risina chivi roruyananiso randakapa kuna Yahweh serudzikinuro rweupenyu hwenyu, uye ndakatokudzikinurai kubva muruoko rwomuvengi. Musatadza! Uye kana munhu upi noupi akatadza, reururai zvivi zvenyu kwandiri, nokuti ndini iye anokuchenesai muropa rangu roruyananiso.

Handina kuyananisira zvivi zvenyu nemigumisiro yazvo nembudzi inotakura zvivi, uyewo handina kupa gwayana regore rokutanga; nokuti ndini Gwayana raMwari regore rokutanga, uye ndini iye akazvipa asina gwapa uye asina chaanopomerwa, kunyange mutsvene, akachena, uye akatsaurirwa kuna Yahweh pachake (kunyange Baba vangu) nokuda kwezvivi zvenyu kamwe chete zvachose, kunyange nokuda kwenguva dzose. Ndakayananisira zvivi zvenyu muropa rangu risina chivi; naizvozvo, hapachina chimwe chibayiro chechivi. Uye kundiramba, Yeshua HaMashiach, kunyange chibvumirano chorugare chakakwana chaYahweh kuvanhu, ndiko kuramba Yahweh uye kubatana nokupandukira kwaSatani, nokuti Satani anopandukira hurongwa hwaMwari kubvira pakutanga.

Nokuti ndakaudza VaHebheru: Nokuti kana tikatadza nokuzvidira pashure pokunge tagamuchira ruzivo rwechokwadi, hapachina chimwe chibayiro chezvivi. Saka kutadza nokuzvidira nokuramba ropa rangu roruyananiso, uye nokudaro kuramba chibvumirano chorugare chakatumwa naYahweh, kunonyanya kuipa, uye hapachina chimwe chibayiro. Nokuti zvakanyorwa muna VaHebheru: Uyo akazvidza murayiro waMosesi akafa asina tsitsi pasi pezvapupu zviviri kana zvitatu: Ko munofunga kuti achafanirwa nokurangwa kwakakura sei, iye akatsika Mwanakomana waMwari, uye akaverenga ropa resungano, raakaitwa mutsvene naro, sechinhu chisina utsvene, uye akazvidza Mweya wenyasha?

Saka ndinokukurudzirai mose kuti mugamuchire chibayiro changu uye munzwisise kuti hapachina zvimwe zvibayiro zvinodiwa uye kune Mesiya mumwe chete wechokwadi, kunyange ini, Yeshua HaMashiach. Uye zvakanyorwa pamusoro pangu: Asi murume uyu, pashure pokunge apa chibayiro chimwe chete chezvivi nokusingaperi, akagara kuruoko rworudyi rwaMwari; Kubvira ipapo achitarisira kusvikira vavengi vake vaitwa chitsiko chetsoka dzake. Nokuti nechibayiro chimwe chete wakakwanisa nokusingaperi avo vanoitwa vatsvene.

Saka nzwisisai kuti ndakakunatsai muzvinhu zvose nemativi ose ane chokuita nechivi nemigumisiro yacho. Munoitwa vatsvene mandiri; munoruramiswa mandiri; munoitwa kururama kwaMwari mandiri; uye ndakakudzikinurai zvizere kwandiri, kunyange ini, Yeshua HaMashiach, uyo Yahweh akatuma saMesiya wenyu. Uye saka nzwisisai kuti hapachina Vapristi Vakuru vechiRevhi, uye Upirisita hwaAroni hwakauya ndokuenda, nokuti wakanga uri mumvuri wezvinhu zvaizouya. Nokuti ndini mupristi wenyu mukuru nokusingaperi maererano nourongwa hwaMerkizedeki.

Nokuti murayiro hauna kuita kuti chinhu chive chakakwana: asi wakanga uri sumo yetariro iri nani, iyo tariro yatinoswedera nayo pedyo naYahweh. Uye zvakare: Mupristi Mukuru wakadai wakanga wakakodzera isu kuva naye uyo ari mutsvene, asina mhosva, asina kusvibiswa, akaparadzaniswa navatadzi, uye akaitwa mukuru kudarika denga. Uye zvakare: Nokuti murayiro unoita vanhu vapristi, vane utera: asi shoko remhiko rakauya kubvira pamurayiro, rinoita mwanakomana mupristi, uyo akakwana nokusingaperi. Hamuna kuverenga here zvinotaurwa nerugwaro pamusoro pangu? Yahweh akapika uye haazozvidembi: Iwe uri Mupristi nokusingaperi maererano nourongwa hwaMerkizedeki.

Nzwisisai kuti hapana chinoshayikwa muchibayiro changu, uye makachengeteka mandiri, Kristu Jesu. Torah inochengetwa nokutenda mandiri, Yeshua HaMashiach, uye hazvibviri kuti mutyore upi noupi wemitemo yaMwari nokufamba mandiri, Kristu Jesu. Nokuti munofamba mumurayiro woMweya woupenyu, uye ndakakusunungurai pamurayiro wechivi norufu. Saka ivai nokutenda mandiri, nokuti wakarurama uchararama nokutenda. Ndine mabasa mazhinji ane simba andakakudanirai, uye muchabatsira kuunza ruponeso kune vazhinji. Kune tariro huru nomubayiro zvamakachengeterwa kumatenga nokuda kwokumira kwenyu nokutendeka kwenyu.

Nokuti ndinokutumai, makaiswa chisimbiso pahuma dzenyu nechisimbo chaYahweh pachake, munyika ichakuvengai zvachose, uye hazvishamisi, nokuti nyika yakandivenga zvachose pakutanga, uye muranda haasi pamusoro patenzi wake. Imi muri zvapupu zvangu navaporofita vangu navarapisi vangu navagoveri vangu. Ndinokutumai murima guru kwazvo rakambofukidza chisiko chaYahweh. Nokuda kwenguva yakadai makadanwa, makasarudzwa, uye makaiswa chisimbiso, uye nguva idzi dzichava dzakanyanya kuipa uye dzakaipa zvikuru nyika isati yamboona, kunyange kudarika mazuva aNoa. Pakati pavo ndinokutumai, uye ndinokuudzai kuti musatya! Musatya kutyisidzira kwavo; musatya zvingaitwa nemunhu kwamuri; musatya zviso zvavo; musatya mweya inovapa simba; musatya chikara nomuporofita wenhema; uye musatya Satani pachake: Kunyange shato huru iyo. Nokuti muchatsika pamusoro pezvinhu zvose zvoumambo hwaSatani, uye hapana chinhu chingakudzivisai kana mukaramba makatendeka kuizwi rangu uye mukandirega ndichikutungamirirai nhanho nenhanho. Nokuti zvirokwazvo, muri vashandi pamwe chete neni munguva ino yenhamo yaJakobho, uye ndakakupai simba.

Mune simba uye mune masimba, uye ndinokutumai nounyoro nokuzvininipisa. Ndinokutumai kuti muchengete murayiro nokutenda kwenyu mandiri, uye saka idai Yahweh nomwoyo wenyu wose, mweya, pfungwa, nesimba, uye idai muvakidzani wenyu sezvamunozviita. Muavakidzani wenyu ndiani? Vose vanoda rubatsiro rwenyu kwandiri ndinokutumai, uye handisi kukudzivisai nemiganhu yenyika. Endai mufudze boka rangu, nokuti ndini Mufudzi Mukuru ane mubayiro wenyu muruoko rwangu nokuda kwokutendeka. Endai munochengeta boka rangu, chengetai maronda avo, vadyisei, varapisei, vatungamirirei, uye pamusoro pezvose muvauyise kwandiri; nokuti imi pachenyu hamusi chinhu, asi mandiri, muri varume vangu vane simba, vane masimba aMwari. Vadzidzisei kudana zita rangu kusvikira kumagumo, nokuti ani naani anoita izvozvo achaponeswa.

Rambai makatarisa pamubayiro nomufaro zvakaiswa pamberi penyu, uye rumbidzirai mberi muchienda kuchinangwa chomubayiro wokudanwa kukuru kwaMwari muna Kristu Jesu. Nokuti mubayiro wenyu uchava mukuru, uye muchashamiswa nezvamakachengeterwa kumatenga. Uye mubayiro wangu hausi wenguva pfupi asi usingaperi, uye mibayiro yenyu haingaparari asi ichashanda senhaka isingaperi mukuzadziswa kwezvipikirwa zvakaitwa naYahweh kumadzibaba; yodhi yose kana munzwa weyodhi zvichazadziswa. Hamuzozivi ukuru hwemabasa enyu kusvikira pazuva iro romubayiro, asi Rugwaro rwakajeka pamusoro pezvibereko zvemabasa enyu.

Nokuti Johane MuZaruri akanyora nokutendeka zvandakamuratidza: Shure kwezvinhu izvi ndakatarira, uye tarira vanhu vazhinji, vasingagoni kuverengwa nomunhu, vemarudzi ose namarudzi, navanhu, nendimi, vakamira pamberi pechigaro choumambo, napamberi peGwayana, vakapfeka nguvo chena refu, nemichindwe mumaoko avo. Uye vakadanidzira nenzwi guru, vachiti, Ruponeso runobva kuna Mwari wedu, anogara pachigaro choumambo, uye kubva kuGwayana. Nokuti vanhu vazhinji muchabatsira kuponesa nokushumira, uye mibayiro yenyu yokutendeka ichava mikuru.

Uye muchanditevera kwose kwandinoenda, uye imi muri zvibereko zvokutanga zvakatengwa kubva kuvanhu, uye muchaimba rwiyo rutsva pamberi pechigaro choumambo urwo imi moga munogona kudzidza. Muchatongawo neni muUmambo hwangu makore ane chiuru uye muchava neni nokusingaperi mukusingaperi. Nokuti mufaro wenyu usingaperi, nokuti muchava neni muUmambo hwangu panyika pandinotonga nyika mukururama kubva pachigaro choumambo chaDhavhidhi, uye Umambo hwangu hahuna magumo. Uye hapana chinhu chakaipa chichapinda, uye hapachazovi nedambudziko, uye umambo hwangu ndohwokururama apo gwayana neshumba zvichavata pasi pamwe chete murugare. Uye pamagumo okutonga kwangu kwemakore ane chiuru, Satani achasunungurwa kwenguva duku kuti aunganidze kurwisa kwake kwekupedzisira, uye moto uchaburuka kubva kuna Yahweh uye uchavaparadza zvachose. Satani achakandwa mugungwa romoto, kuti asazomukazve, uye Yahweh achasika denga idzva nenyika itsva, uye achaita ugaro hwake pakati pavanhu, uye saka muchagara muJerusarema Idzva, kunyange guta dzvene iro rakasikwa naYahweh pachake. Uye Yahweh pachake akataura kuti: Anokunda achagara nhaka yezvinhu zvose; uye ini ndichava Mwari wake, uye iye achava mwanakomana wangu.
