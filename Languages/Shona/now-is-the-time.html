Zvino Ndiro Nguva

Yasvika nguva, vana vangu, yasvika nguva. Yasvika nguva yekudzokera kumusha, kana hwamanda yorira uye kereke yangu younganidzwa, uko Yahweh akamirira nemufaro mumwoyo make nokuda kwekusvika kwenyu kamwe kamwe. Yasvika nguva yekudzokera kumusha, kana kurira kwehwamanda kuchidana vana veWokumusoro-soro kuti vaende kunogara nekufamba ngirozi. Yasvika nguva vana vangu kuti ndikuunganidzei kubva kumativi mana enyika, kunyange vose vakafa vari muna Kristu vachatanga kumuka uye imi muri vapenyu makasara muchatorwa pamwe chete navo mumakore. Nokuti ini Kristu Jesu ndiri Ishe wenyu mupenyu noMuponesi, uye ndiri kukudanirai kumusha wenyu kumatenga uko mibayiro yenyu yakamirira, kwandakakugadzirirai nzvimbo mumba maBaba vangu, nokuti mumba maBaba vangu mune dzimba zhinji. Ndiri kukudanirai kumusha kuti muzomira pamberi paIshe wenyu, kuti vose vagamuchire zvinhu zvakaitwa mumuviri, zvingava zvakanaka kana zvakaipa, kuti vose vagamuchire mubayiro wavo kubva kuna Ishe, uye kuti vose vatore nzvimbo dzavo muhurongwa hwematenga hwakagadzirirwa mumwe nomumwe wenyu.

Tarirai! Ndiri Yeshua HaMashiach, uye ndiri chikomba chiri kusvika kuzotora mwenga wangu nokuti nguva dzomuchato wedu dzasvika, kwandichapinda musungano isingaperi navakasarudzwa vangu, kunyange avo vakazvigadzirira, uye vandakachenesa muropa rangu rerudzikinuro nokuti vakauya kwandiri zvizere vakapa mwoyo yavo yose kwandiri murudo nokuzvipira. Kunyange vasina kufamba zvakakwana, uye vazhinji vakakunda chivi chakakomba, vakazvichenesa zvizere muropa reGwayana, uye zvino vamire pamberi pangu vakacheneswa zvizere pazvivi zvavo uye vakachena semhandara dzisina kuita unzenza uye dzakazvidzivirira kune akaipa. Tarirai! Vakundi vangu vandakaregerera pazvivi zvose uye ndikadira mafuta mumarambi avo nokuti vakauya kwandiri munguva dzokushaiwa kwavo uye ndikavaratidza nzira yokugera tambo dzemarambi avo uye ndikavaporesa ndikavatsaura kuti vave vangu, kunyange vose vakasarudzwa naYahweh nyika isati yavambwa kuti vamire semwenga wangu uye vave neni nokusingaperi-peri, uye vachatonga neni saMadzimambo naVapristi nokusingaperi nokuti ndizvo zvavanodanwa, vakaitwa vatsvene uye vakasarudzwa mandiri, Kristu Jesu Ishe wavo wakamutswa.

Uye 144,000 vangu vachashandurwa, voiswa chisimbiso uye vosikwa patsva zvizere nokuda kwezvinangwa zvavakadanirwa uye vakagadzirirwa kuzadzisa. Zvirokwazvo, zvirokwazvo, mweya wose wakatenda muzita rangu rine simba ukandiita Ishe wavo usingaperi uchashandurwa zvizere wosikwa patsva sechisikwa chitsva, uye vachagadzirwa sezvandiri. Uye havazombotadzizve, kungava nokuzvikudza kana kusada Mwari uye havazombotukwizve, uye havazomborwarizve kana kurwadziwa, kana kufa kana kusuwa zvichizovabata zvakare nokuti zvinhu zvekare zvapfuura uye chiedza chechokwadi chava kupenya. Uye ndini, Kristu Jesu, ndichafamba zvino kuzviratidza kuna Jakobho uye ani naani anodana zita rangu rine simba kusvika kumagumo achaponeswa, kunyange vagari vose vepanyika vakatemirwa upenyu husingaperi uye kuchava neboka guru richandigamuchira saMesiasi wavo uye ndichave ndavaponesa zvakakwana.

Ndiri kuuya kuzokutorai, vadikani vangu, uye nguva dzasvika. Ndakamirira zera rose renyasha uye ndakaponesa vazhinji, uye vazhinji vachamutsirwa kuutsva hwoupenyu mandiri, Kristu Jesu Ishe wavo wekusingaperi, uye vakafa vari muna Kristu vachatanga kumuka, uye hwamanda yokupedzisira yagadzirira zvino. Zviratidzo zvizhinji, zvishamiso nezvishamiso zvakafukidza nyika mukubwinya kwazvo uye zviratidzo hazvimiri pasi rose zvino zvinozivisa kuuya kwangu, zvinozivisa muchato weGwayana kumwenga wake akatendeka, uye dzimba dzemabiko kumatenga dzagadzirirwa uye dzakamira zvakanaka.

Uye saka ndizvo, mhemberero yokudenga yakamirira vakatendeka vangu, vakadanwa, uye vakasarudzwa, uye zvino yasvika nguva yokuti vazorore nokusingaperi pamabasa avo avakabata panyika mudzimba dzavo dzevhu, uye vakabata munyama sevashandi pamwe chete neni uye vakaunganidza vakauyisa vazhinji kwandiri, Ishe Jesu Kristu kuti vagamuchire ruponeso rusingaperi nokusingaperi-peri. Nokuti kunyange nyama yavo yakanga yakaodzwa muchivi naSatani, ndakavapa chinangwa ndikavadana kuti vasiye nokusingaperi kusabatsira kwavakanga vapomerwa nechivi.

Uye saka vane pfuma iyi mumidziyo yevhu kuti simba raMwari rigare mavari, uye vakaita mabasa mazhinji ane simba muzita rangu, uye ndini iye akavapa simba rokuva vadyi venhaka yoruponeso navadyi venhaka youpenyu husingaperi uye ndakavadzikinura ndikavapa rubatso irworwo rusingaperi mukuzvarwa kutsva uye ndakavaisa chisimbiso vose nomweya iwoyo mutsvene wechivimbiso, kunyange chisimbiso ichocho chisingatyoki chechivimbiso. Uye zvino yasvika nguva yokudzikinura zvizere pfuma yakatengwa, kunyange vose vakandiita Ishe kubva pamwoyo uzere nokutenda vachiziva kuti Yahweh akandimutsa kubva kuvakafa kuti ndirege kuzofazve. Zvino ndidzo nguva dzekudzikinurwa kwavo kuzere kwavachagamuchira zvizere rubatso rwavo rwavakagamuchira kubva kwandiri mukuzvarwa kutsva kwaKristu mamuri tariro yokubwinya!

Inzwai izwi rangu uye nzwisisai mashoko angu uye muzive kuti ramangwana renyu rinobwinya harisi kure nemakore, uye harisati richizosvika muMaJubhiri anotevera, asi riri pamusoro penyu vadikani vangu uye kamwe kamwe munguva isingaperi muchashandurwa mose uye nguva dzeshanduko dzasvika. Muchazviwana makangoerekana mashandurwa mukuva mukubwinya kusingaperi, uye ndichakupfekedzai nguvo dzisina ruvara dzisingambosvibiswi. Kamwe kamwe muchazviwana mamire muimba yechigaro choumambo chaYahweh, kunyange mukubwinya nokukudzwa kusingaperi kwaBaba veChiedza, uye muchagoverana nokusingaperi-peri chimiro chake chisingaperi nokuti muri vanakomana vaMwari mupenyu, kunyange vose vakatorwa kubva kuna Adhamu vakadzikinurwa kubva muvhu dzvuku.

Uye rakanga riri ropa rangu rerudzikinuro rakadururirwa panyika paGorogota rakachengetedza ruponeso rwenyu kamwe, uye nokusingaperi-peri pandakarega upenyu hwangu nemashoko okuti "Zvazopera!" Uye ini Kristu Jesu, ndiri nokusingaperi-peri Ishe wakamutswa akakwidzwa waMadzishe naMambo waMadzimambo nokuti Yahweh akandimutsa kubva kuvakafa kuti ndirege kuzofazve! Ndiri Adhamu wekupedzisira, uye ndiri mwanakomana womunhu akadzikinura vanakomana vavanhu vakadana zita rangu akavaita vanakomana vaMwari vasingaperi vachararama nokusingaperi mukubwinya kusingaperi nokukudzwa kusingaperi uye nokusingaperi-peri Yahweh achakudzwa mavari, uye ivo muna Yahweh nokuti mandiri, Yeshua HaMashiach munogara kuzara kwose nokusingaperi-peri, uye Yahweh achava zvose muzvose uye nokusingaperi-peri tichafamba sechisikwa chitsva chimwe nomunhu mutsva mumwe. Uye imi, vakadanwa vangu vakasarudzwa uye vakatendeka, makadzikinurwa nokusingaperi-peri, uye mava kuda kunzwisisa upenyu hwenyu chaihwo nokuti upenyu hwenyu hahuperi. Saka nzwisisai kuti makafa, uye upenyu hwenyu hwakavigwa neni, Kristu Jesu, muna Yahweh. Kana ini, Kristu Jesu, ndiri upenyu hwenyu, ndikaonekwa, ipapo nemiwo muchaonekwa neni mukubwinya. Hamuzivi here kuti zvenyu chaizvo zvakavigwa mandiri, Kristu Jesu?

Uye ndichakuunganidzai kamwe kamwe kwandiri. Uye nyika painopinda mukutongwa nehondo, matenga achafara nokuvapo kwenyu uye kuchava nemhemberero huru kana vanakomana vaMwari vabatana nokusingaperi-peri naIshe wavo waMadzishe naMambo waMadzimambo mukubwinya kusingaperi uye utsva hwoupenyu huchava hwevose. Uye hakuchazovizve nokuparadzaniswa pakati pevakadzikunurwa neropa reGwayana naBaba vavo naMwari wavo.

Nokuti zvino yasvika nguva yakasarudzwa naYahweh yokutanga kuzviratidza zvizere kuchisikwa chake uye kuzviratidza kwake zvizere kunotangira paimba yaMwari, uye ava kuda kuzviratidza zvizere kuvakasarudzwa vake navakatendeka, kunyange zvibereko zvokutanga, nokuti avo vandinounganidza kwandiri pakurira kwehwamanda yokupedzisira ndivo zvibereko zvokutanga kubva kuvakafa.
