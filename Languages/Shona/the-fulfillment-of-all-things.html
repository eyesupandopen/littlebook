Nyamavhuvhu 11, 2024

Kuzadzikiswa kweZvinhu Zvose!

Inzwai Mashoko aIshe wenyu mupenyu noMuponesi, iye Mesiasi wenyu wechokwadi, iye Yeshua HaMashiach. Nzwisisai uye teererai kuMashoko angu vana vangu nokuti zvino ndichataura pamusoro pekuzadzikiswa kwezvinhu zvose, nokuti kuzadzikiswa kwazvo kwava pedyo. Saka nzwisisai, nokuti vanhu zvino varamba chibvumirano cherunyararo chaYahweh uye vakaramba Jesu weNazareta (nokuti ini ndiri chibvumirano chenyu cherunyararo) mwaka yepanyika zvino ichashanduka kubva panyasha kuenda pakutonga, ichashanduka kubva kutsitsi kuenda kunjodzi, ichashanduka kubva parunyararo kuenda kuhondo, ichashanduka kubva paunyoro kuenda pakutsamwa, zvino zvichashanduka kuva kutsiva kwakazara nokuparadzwa kukuru kune avo vanogara panyika. Kunyange vose vakaramba kundiita Ishe uye kugamuchira rudo rwangu nokuregerera kwangu kwavari, uko kwandaizovadzosera kumusiki wavo uye ndovapa upenyu husingaperi, kuti vasazofazve.

Asi panzvimbo pokuti munhu wenyama auye kwandiri zvizere, vazhinji vakaramba mukuzvikudza kwokupanduka vachisarudza kugara naSatani nokupanduka kwavo uye vakabatana navo vasingazivi, kana kuti vachiziva zvizere sarudzo yavo. Uye saka avo vakasarudza noupenzi hwakadai zvino vachaiswa pasi pekutsamwa kwaMwari nokutsamwa kweGwayana uko kuchadururwa zvizere nokukurumidza. Uye kunyange vazhinji vakaramba panguva ino, vazhinji-zhinji vachauya kwandiri voreurura kuti Jesu ndiye Ishe uye vachadana zita raMesiasi wavo kusvikira kumagumo. Uye saka kuburikidza naYeshua HaMashiach vachaponeswa. Asi vazhinji vachaparara, kunyange vose vane mazita asina kumbonyorwa muBhuku roUpenyu kubva pakuvambwa kwenyika uye ndizvo zvichava sarudzo yavo.

Uye saka nenzira imwecheteyo iyo shanduko idzi dziri kuitika panyika mukutonga, rugare rwagadzika kumatenga uye hondo zvino ichaparadza nyika zvachose. Kwakanga kune hondo kudenga uye mupomeri wehama akakandirwa pasi, akakandirwa pasi, uye shato huru tsvuku iyo zvino yakasungirirwa panyika uye yakamirira kutongwa kwayo uye kutongwa kwayo hakunonoki. Mukukundwa kwakakwana achaita zvinangwa zvake muzvirongwa zvaYahweh zvisingaperi, nokuti kunyange vavengi vake vanoteerera inzwi rake, uye saka anopiwa nenzira shoma kwazvo uye ushe hwake huchaparadzwa zvachose. Tarirai! Nyoka iyo yekare, iyo shato huru tsvuku, zvino yakakandirwa pasi panyika, uye ine hasha huru nokuti inoziva kuti ine nguva pfupi! Uye saka ini, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndinorayira vose kuti vamhanyire kunzwi rangu uye vadane zita rangu rine simba kusvikira kumagumo nokuti avo vasingadi upenyu hwavo kusvikira kumagumo vachamuka vachiwana mbiri isingaperi, mufaro, nomubairo uye mubairo wavo mukuru! Nokuti vose vanodana zita rangu kusvikira kumagumo vachaponeswa, mumutauro upi zvawo wavanodana uye ini Jesu Kristu, ndiriwo Yeshua HaMashiach, ndinozivisa mashoko aya!

Uye saka zvibvumirano nezvipikirwa zvekare zvichazadzikiswa, kunyange zvibvumirano zvose zvenguva dzose nokuti ndiro evhangeri risingaperi. Uye hondo painogara panyika, rugare zvino rwagadzwa kumatenga uko matenga zvino agadzirirwa kugamuchira vakatendeka muna Kristu, kunyange avo vakafa vakatendeka muna Kristu, uye avo vari vapenyu uye vanosara uye vanorarama upenyu hwavo muna Kristu. Nokuti vose vanofamba nomurayiro womweya woupenyu muna Kristu Jesu munguva yangu yenyasha vachaunganidzwa vagogamuchira mubairo wavo usingaperi uye vachanzwisisa uye vachaiswa muzvikwata zvavo kuti vazadzise chinangwa chaYahweh chisingaperi chavakasiirwa patsva muna Kristu. Uye saka muchato weChikomba noMwenga zvino uchaitika, uye kuchava nomufaro mukuru kumatenga mukubwinya nokukudzwa kusingaperi!

Nokuti kubva pakutanga, Mwari akasika Adhamu, uye kubva kuna Adhamu akasika Evha kuti ave shamwari yake isingaperi uye ave mai vevapenyu vose uye kuti vabereke uye vawande. Vakarayirwa kuti pamiti yose yomumunda weEdeni, vangadya vakasununguka pamuti upi zvawo kunze kwomuti wokuziva zvakanaka nezvakaipa. Uye saka nomuedzo unonyengera wenyoka Evha akanyengerwa, uye akatora muchero wakarambidzwa akadya, uye akapa kumurume wake Adhamu, uye iye akadya achiziva uye nokudaro chivi chakaodza vanhu uye mukufa vakafa kubvira ipapo. Uye saka Adhamu naEvha havana kuzadzisa kudana kwavo kukuru kwokutonga nokukunda nyika mukururama uye kubereka nokuwanda uye kufukidza nyika mukururama asi panzvimbo pezvo vakatadza uye vakararama mubairo wechivi kusvikira mubairo iwoyo wapera murufu rwavo uye mukufa vanhu vakafa kubvira ipapo. Uye mukumuka kwevakarurama vachagamuchira mubairo wavo nokuripirwa nokuti vakanga vakatendeka kusvikira kumagumo.

Uye saka ndizvo zvazvakaita kuti Yahweh, mukuziva kwake kusingaperi, akaziva zvisati zvaitika kuti vanhu vaizosarudza chivi norufu kubva pakutanga. Uye El Shaddai, kunyange murudo rwake rukuru rwaanoda vanhu narwo, (nezvisikwa zvake zvose), akapa nyasha huru netsitsi paakazivisa kuti aizoisa ruvengo pakati pembeu yaSatani nembeu yomukadzi. Mbeu yaSatani yakapwanya chitsitsinho changu paKarivhari mukuzadzikiswa kweMashoko aYahweh uye zvino ndichafamba kunopwanya zvachose musoro wenyoka nokuti ndiyo ramangwana rake nevose vanotevera, uye sezvazvakanyorwa, ndizvo zvicharamba zvichiitwa kusvikira zvapera.

Asi, Mwari akasarudza muhuchenjeri hwake husingaperi kuratidza zvirongwa zvake zvenyasha, tsitsi, norugare zvorudzikinuro ruzere rwevanhu zvishoma nezvishoma, asi akagara achipa tariro huru kune vakarurama kuti rimwe zuva munguva pfupi vachamutswa kuupenyu hutsva kubva kuvakafa kuti vasazofazve, uye vaizogara mukuzara norugare mazuva ose oupenyu hwavo uye mazuva iwayo haazovi nomugumo. Kwemakore akawanda vakatendeka vakafa nokutenda kuzere muna Yahweh kuti azadzise zvipikirwa zvake uye vakafa vachivimba nezvibvumirano zvaYahweh setariro yavo yoruponeso uye zvibvumirano zvose zvaYahweh zvichazadzikiswa zvizere pachiso chaKristu Jesu, ini, Ishe wenyu akamuka noMuponesi. Uye saka vakatenda Mwari uye kwavari kwakaverengwa sokururama kunyange kune vose vakatenda uye vakanga vaine tariro mumbeu yakapikirwa yomukadzi.

Uye saka chirongwa chaYahweh chenguva dzose chekudzikinura vanhu zvizere chakagara chakatarisana neni, Kristu Jesu, iye Jesu weNazareta anovawo Yeshua HaMashiach nokuti ndini Ishe akakwidzwa waMadzishe anova gwayana raMwari uye akaunza ruponeso rusingaperi kuvaJudha neveDzimwe Ndudzi zvakafanana, uye ndichaponesa Jakobho kubva kune avo vakapinda munyika yechipikirwa uye kuparadzwa kwavo hakunonoki. Ndini iye uyo shumba ichavata pasi negwayana nokuti mandiri shumba yorudzi rwaJudha negwayana raMwari zvichasvika pakuzadziswa kuzere. Nokuti ndini shumba yorudzi rwaJudha uye ndini Gwayana raMwari nokuti ndini Mambo waMadzimambo naIshe waMadzishe uye ndini Mupristi Mukuru nokusingaperi maererano nourongwa hwaMerkizedeki.

Uye saka Adhamu naEvha vakararama vakatendeka vachienda mberi pamberi paIshe Mwari wavo, uye vakabereka uye vakawanda, asi Satani aizoronga kumwe kurwisa nokupanduka kwevarindi vakawa. Vakabatana nokupanduka kwaSatani uye kuti vagutse kuchiva kwavo vakasiya mbiri isingaperi yenzvimbo yavo yokutanga, yavakanga vasikirwa, kuti vazvisvibise nevakadzi, kunyange nenyama neropa, nokuti vangadai vakaramba vari mukubwinya kusingaperi asi panzvimbo pezvo vakasarudza kurangwa kwavo kusingaperi kuitira kuti vazadzise kuchiva kwavo vachikuvadza nokuparadza chisikwa chakanga chiripo panguva iyoyo.

Uye saka ngirozi idzi dzakawa dzakadzidzisa munhu akawa nzira yokuzviparadza nayo kuburikidza nedzinza raKaini uye kuburikidza noruzivo rwakarambidzwa nemanomano avakadzidzisa vanhu. Vakazviita savanamwari uye vakada kunamatwa uye VAKAZVITORERA vakadzi nechisimba kubva kune vose vavakasarudza, uye vakadzi vazhinji vakazvipira vachitungamirirwa naSatani kuitira kuedza nokunyengera varindi kuti vapinde muzvivi zvavo zvokuchiva izvo zvavakazvisunga nazvo nokusingaperi kukutukwa kwavo nokuti vakaita pakati pavo kutukana. Izvi zvakaitika mumazuva aJaredi uye zvinonyangadza zvavo, kunyange maNefirimu avo akapararira panyika muumambo hwavo hwenyika yose panguva yainzi yendarama.

Uye varindi nembeu yavo vakaodza nyika nezvose zvaiva mairi uye shanduko dzakasiyana-siyana dzemajini dzakaitwa uye dzakawanda panyika nokuti vakatadzira nyama yose uye kuchiva kwavo kwakanga kusina muganhu, uye vakanyepedzera kuva vanamwari, kunyange vanamwari vanosika asi chaizvoizvo havasi chinhu kunze kwevanyepedzeri vakapinda mukuyambuka-yambuka kwemarudzi. Uye saka nyika yakava yakaora zvachose uye yakatongwa nemaNefirimu, aidya vanhu nezvakawanikwa nevanhu. Uye vose vakanga vasara vasina kusvibiswa panguva yaNoa (nokuti Noa akanga akarurama uye akatendeka uye akafamba naYahweh uye akawana nyasha pamberi paYahweh) mweya misere yakaponeswa iri mipenyu nemhuka dzakaunzwa naYahweh kuAreka kuti marudzi adzo aponeswe. Uye saka Mwari akafashamisa nyika uye uori hwose hwenyika yakanga iripo panguva iyoyo hwakaparara, uye varindi havana rugare, kana ruregerero uye nokusingaperi-peri kutonga kwavo kwakaoma kuchava nokuti vakakodzera. Uye kwete varindi chete, asi vose vanozvibatanidza nokupanduka kwaSatani vachatambura nenzira yakadaro uye vacharipirwa nokusingaperi mugungwa guru remoto wesurferi yebhuruu unopfuta mirazvo inopisa uye yakachena kwazvo yokuranga.

Mushure memafashamo, Mwari akagadza nyika iripo zvino, kunyange yakagadzwa pakutanga mukururama. Uye Yahweh akaita sungano naNoa, apo Yahweh akakoka Noa kuti amutse kururama panyika uye abereke uye awande kuti mbeu yakapikirwa yomukadzi iuye. Yahweh akapikira kuti haazombofashamisazve nyika uye pasinei nouipi hwevanhu, Mwari akashanda nevanhu kuchengetedza nokukomborera madzinza akarurama panyika andakabva maari ini Yeshua HaMashiach.

Mwari akavakomborera kuti vabereke uye vawande uye vazadze nyika, uye Mwari akapikira vose vanhu nemhuka kuti haazomboparadzizve nyama yose panyika nemvura uye haazombofashamisazve nyika yose asi aizochengetedza nyika, uye ochengetedza vanhu nembeu yake, uye nyika imwecheteyo iyi inochengetedzwa kubva pazuva iroro resungano apo Baba veZvivheneko vakaisa uta hwavo kumatenga sechiratidzo nechiyeuchidzo chesungano yake naNoa. Nokuti izvi vanozviramba vachiziva, kuti neshoko raMwari matenga akanga aripo kare, nenyika yakabuda mumvura uye iri mumvura: Nokudaro nyika yakanga iripo panguva iyoyo, yakafukidzwa nemvura, yakaparara: Asi matenga nenyika, zviripo zvino, neshoko rimwechetero zvakachengeterwa, zvakachengeterwa moto kusvikira pazuva rokutongwa nokuparadzwa kwevanhu vasingadi Mwari.

Uye saka Yahweh akamutsa Abramu kuti ave Abrahamu, uye akapikira Abramu kuti zita rake haringazovi Abramu asi Abrahamu uye kuti aizomuita Baba vendudzi zhinji uye kuti aizopa Abrahamu nembeu yake nyika yeKanani kuti ive nhaka isingaperi uye kuti Yahweh aizova Mwari wavo! Uye saka Abrahamu akava Baba vendudzi zhinji, idzo Yahweh akapikira musungano. Zvino, kuna Abrahamu nembeu yake zvipikirwa zvakaitwa. Haatauri kuti, Uye kumbeu, sokunge dzezhinji; asi sokunge yeimwe, Uye kumbeu yako, anova Kristu. Uye saka vakarurama vachararama nokutenda!

Uye saka vana vakawira muuranda muIjipiti mushure mokunge vana vechipikirwa vaenda ikoko vari pasi pekuchengetedzwa nekuchengetwa kwaJosefa nokuti Josefa akatonga zvakarurama pasi paFarao uyo akanga ari mukuru kuna Josefa pachigaro choushe, asi Josefa akashumira IYE MUKURU! Kwakamuka farao akanga asingazivi Josefa, uye masora aya akadzvinyirira uye akaedza kuuraya dzinza raKristu zvakare, kunyange vana vechipikirwa. Uye Yahweh akamutsa mununuri wechiHebheru kubva mumba maFarao, iye akabudiswa mumvura uye akaponeswa ari mupenyu nenyasha dzaYahweh. Aiva Mosesi pachake aizotungamirira vana vechipikirwa kubva muuranda kuenda kurusununguko runobwinya rwenyika yechipikirwa, nokuti ndivo vana vechipikirwa uye ndichavamutsa kubva kuhope dzavo uye ndichapa mubairo mukuru kune vakatendeka nevakarurama, kunyange avo vakaramba vakatendeka kusvikira kumagumo. Uye saka Mosesi akanunura vana vaIsraeri kubva muruoko rwevadzvinyiriri vavo uye vose vakavadzingirira vakanyura mugungwa, nokuti ndiani anogona kumira pamberi paEl Shaddai?

Uye saka Yahweh akatungamirira Mosesi nevana vechipikirwa kuGomo reSinai uko Yahweh akapa mirayiro, mitemo nezvisungo zviri mumirayiro kuna Mosesi kuti vana vose vateerere. Uye zvikomborero nezvituko zvakagadzwa nokuda kwokuteerera nokusateerera. Uye Yahweh paakanga apa musungano kuna Mosesi mirayiro iri muzvisungo zvokuteerera, Mosesi akabva asimbisa sungano yacho kuva Testamende nokudeurwa kweropa. Uye Mosesi akatora hafu yeropa, akariisa mumidziyo, uye hafu yeropa akarisasa paAritari. Uye akatora bhuku resungano, akaverengera vanhu vachinzwa: uye vakati, Zvose zvakataurwa naIshe, tichazviita, uye tichateerera. Uye Mosesi akatora ropa akarisasa pavanhu, akati, Tarirai ropa reSungano iyo Ishe yaakaita nemi, pamusoro pemashoko ose aya. Uyezve, akasasa neropa zvose Teberinakeri, nemidziyo yose yoUshumiri. Uye zvinenge zvinhu zvose zvinonatswa neropa noMurayiro: uye pasina kudeurwa kweropa hakuna kuregererwa kwezvivi.

Zvino nhoroondo yakazarurwa sezvayakanyorwa, uye nokufamba kwenguva rudzi rwaIsraeri rwakanyatsosimbiswa, asi kwakanga kusati kwava nenzvimbo yokugara yaYahweh pakati pavanhu. Uye Yahweh akamutsa Mambo akarurama, zvirokwazvo murume aiva nomwoyo wakafanana nowaMwari! Nokufamba kwenguva Yahweh akatuma Natan Muprofita kuna Dhavhidhi akamuudza kuti kana mazuva ako apera, uye wovata nemadzibaba ako, ndichamutsa mbeu yako inokutevera, ichabuda muura hwako, uye ndichasimbisa ushe hwake. Iye achandivakira imba yezita rangu, uye ndichasimbisa chigaro choushe chake nokusingaperi. Ndichava baba vake, uye iye achava mwanakomana wangu. Uye imba yako noushe hwako zvichasimbiswa nokusingaperi pamberi pako: chigaro chako choushe chichasimbiswa nokusingaperi. Maererano namashoko ose aya, uye maererano nechiratidzo chose ichi, ndizvo zvakataurwa naNatan kuna Dhavhidhi.

Uye saka vanhu vakatevera Yahweh kubva pachibvumirano kuenda kuchibvumirano vachiwedzera pachibvumirano chakatsigirwa nechibayiro chemhuka uye chibvumirano chakawedzerwa patestamente. Asi, pasinei nokuedza kwose kwavanhu, ivo pachavo havana kumbokwanisa kurarama maererano nokururama kwaMwari, kunyange mutemo iwoyo wakarurama waMosesi wandinoda uye wandakazadzisa. Kana paine anoti mutemo waMosesi nezvibvumirano zvizhinji zvaYahweh zvakatukwa, ngaatukwe iye. Nokuti mutemo wakanaka, wakarurama, uye mutsvene, uye zvipikirwa zvose zvaYahweh ndizvo hongu naamen. Asi, mutemo wakapiwa somudzidzisi kuti uvaunze kuna Kristu. Nokuti ini, Jesu weNazareta, ndini wandakapiwa naYahweh kuti ndizadzise izvo vanhu vasingakwanisi nokuda kwechivi chakapfuudzwa kubva kuchizvarwa kuenda kuchizvarwa kubva muropa raAdhamu, iro rakaora nokusingaperi. Nokuti izvo mutemo waisagona kuita, zvikurukuru nokuti wakanga usina simba nokuda kwenyama: izvozvo Mwari akazviita, akatuma mwanakomana wake nomufananidzo wenyama ine chivi.

Saka mukuzara kwenguva ini, Jesu weNazareta, ndakaberekwa nomukadzi maererano nezvinodiwa nomutemo waMosesi uye mukuzadziswa kwezvibvumirano, uye ndiri mbeu yakapikirwa yomukadzi yakafanotaurwa naYahweh pachake makore mazhinji apfuura. Nokuti Mwari akada nyika nokudaro, kuti akapa mwanakomana wake mumwe woga, kuti ani naani anotenda kwaari arege kufa, asi ave noupenyu husingaperi. Uye saka ndiri mwanakomana mumwe woga waMwari, zvirokwazvo mbeu iyoyo yechipikirwa iyo vaprofita vakaprofita nezvayo kubvira kare kusvikira zvino. Uye ndini iye akagadzirirwa kuzadziswa kwezvinhu zvose uye mandiri, Kristu Jesu, zvinhu zvose nezvipikirwa zvose zvichazadziswa uye hapana chipikirwa chimwe chete chichasara chisina kuitwa, asi zvose zvichazadziswa nokusingaperi.

Nokuti zvakanyorwa muna VaHebheru pandakaita kuti Pauro anyore kuti: Nokuti kana ropa rehandira, nerembudzi, namadota etsiru achisasa vasina kuchena, zvichivatsvenesa pakucheneswa kwenyama: Ko ropa raKristu, iye wakazvipa kuna Mwari asina gwapa noMweya usingaperi, haringatonyanyi kuchenesa hana dzenyu pamabasa akafa, kuti mushumire Mwari mupenyu here? Uye nokuda kwechikonzero ichi iye ndiye Murevereri weTestamente Itsva, kuti norufu, rwekuripira kudarika kwakanga kuri pasi peTestamente yokutanga, avo vakadanwa, vagamuchire chipikirwa chenhaka isingaperi.

Saka nzwisisai kuti mandiri, Kristu Jesu, ndimo munochengetwa zvipikirwa zvekare uye zvibvumirano zvekare zvinochengetwa uye testamente yekare inochengetwa uye inozadziswa nokuti ndini kuzadziswa kwazvo kwakakwana. Asi kuchine zvipikirwa zvinofanira kuzadziswa uye zvino ndidzo nguva dzokuzadziswa. Nokuti dzakadaro nguva, pandichangoerekana ndamutsa kubva kuhope avo vakarara muna Kristu, uye ndichangoerekana ndaunganidza avo vari vapenyu uye vakasara kuti vasangane neni mumakore uye tichangoerekana tabatana muimba yechigaro choushe chaYahweh pachake. Nokuti imi Kereke yangu uye kunyange mwenga wangu muriwo kuzadziswa kwechipikirwa chechibvumirano uye makaberekwa patsva nembeu isingaori uye makadzikinurwa nezvipikirwa zvizhinji zviri muTestamente yangu Itsva, icho chiri chibvumirano chitsva chinosanganisira zvipikirwa zvose zvezvibvumirano zvekare uye chinotowedzera zvipikirwa zvikuru uye zvinokosha zvikuru zvamunodzikinurwa nazvo kuupenyu husingaperi nomubayiro usingaperi uye tichava pamwe chete nokusingaperi.

Nzwisisai kuti upenyu hwamakararama munyama, kunyange imi vakatendeka vangu uye vakasarudzwa, upenyu ihwohwo hwaguma pakudzikinurwa kwenyu kuzere. Nzwisisai kuti makanyatsozivikanwa neni, Kristu Jesu, pamatanho ose. Pandakarovererwa pamuchinjikwa nokuda kwezvivi zvenyu, imi makarovererwawo pamwe chete neni. Pandakafira padanda iroro rakaoma, imi makafawo neni. Pandakamutswa kuupenyu pashure pemazuva matatu nousiku hutatu mumwoyo wenyika, saizvozvowo imi makamutsirwa kuutsva hwoupenyu, uye pandakakwidzwa kudenga rematenga, saizvozvowo imi makakwidzwa neni uye saka makagariswa kudenga. Nokuti ndini Pasika yenyu yakakwana, uye ndini mutsivi wenyu akatora nzvimbo yenyu mukutongwa nomurangiro wakakodzera. Uye ini, Jesu weNazareta, ndakakudzikinurai nokuzvidira kubva pakutukwa kwomutemo nokuti nokuda kwenyama yenyu ine chivi hamumbokwanisi kuzadzisa mutemo nokuedza kwenyu pachenyu.

Nzwisisai kuti handina kuuya kuzoparadza mutemo, kana zvibvumirano, asi kuzozadzisa zvose munguva dzokuzadziswa. Kana paine anoti mutemo waMosesi nezvibvumirano zvizhinji zvaYahweh zvakatukwa, ngaatukwe iye. Nokuti mutemo wakanaka, wakarurama, uye mutsvene, uye zvipikirwa zvose zvaYahweh ndizvo hongu naamen. Asi, mutemo wakapiwa somudzidzisi kuti uvaunze kuna Kristu. Nokuti ini, Jesu weNazareta, ndini wandakapiwa naYahweh kuti ndizadzise izvo vanhu vasingakwanisi nokuda kwechivi chakapfuudzwa kubva kuchizvarwa kuenda kuchizvarwa kubva muropa raAdhamu, iro rakaora nokusingaperi. Nokuti izvo mutemo waisagona kuita, zvikurukuru nokuti wakanga usina simba nokuda kwenyama: izvozvo Mwari akazviita, akatuma mwanakomana wake nomufananidzo wenyama ine chivi.

Chipikirwa chose chava pedyo nokuzadziswa zvizere mandiri Kristu Jesu uye ndichapinda muchibvumirano chewero neMwenga wangu kuti pamwe chete vatongi neni munhaka yangu uye ivo, nokutungamirira kwangu uye neni, vachaporesa nyika uye voenda mberi votonga sevapristi namadzimambo panyika uye chibvumirano chose chichazadziswa kubvira kuna Adhamu kusvikira kuna Mosesi muChiso chaJesu Kristu. Izvi ndizvo zvipikirwa zvoupenyu husingaperi zvichazadziswa mune vakarurama, kunyange vose avo vanosikwa patsva somunhu mumwe mutsva muna Kristu Jesu. Nokuti vose vachasikwa patsva zvizere uye vachapiwa mubayiro usingaperi nokuda kwechikamu chavo uye zvose zvichava hongu naamen. Nokuti yodhi yose, nomunzwa wose weyodhi zvichazadziswa zvachose uye nokusingaperi.

Nzwisisai kuti imi muri temberi yaMwari mupenyu, Kereke yangu yakatendeka uye ndakakusikai seimba yakaitwa pasina maoko kuitira kuti muve nzvimbo yokugara isingaperi yaMwari asingaperi, kunyange yaYahweh Baba venyu. Uye ndicharoora mwenga wangu uye ndichava navo nokusingaperi muchibvumirano chisingaperi, kuti pandiri, ndipo pavachavapowo, uye nokusingaperi vachafanana neni, Kristu Jesu Ishe wavo usingaperi. Uye nzwisisai vakatendeka vangu kuti nguva dzokuparadzana dzava pedyo uye hakuchazovi nokuparadzana pakati paYahweh navanakomana vake vaanoda uye hakuchazovi nokuparadzana pakati paKristu Jesu Ishe wenyu usingaperi naavo vandinoda zvikuru. Nzwisisai kuti tichafamba somunhu mumwe uye hakuchazovi nechivi kana chipingamupinyi. Muchaita mabasa mazhinji ane simba asina kumbopinda mumwoyo yavanhu. Muchava madzimambo angu ane simba navapristi vandinova Mambo waMadzimambo naIshe waMadzishe uye tichatonga nyika mukururama.

Uye vazhinji vachamutswa kuupenyu pakuguma kwevhiki raDhanieri rechimakumi manomwe uye vose vachapiwa mubayiro maererano nokutendeka kwavo kwave kuri. Uye vose vakafa vakatendeka vachitarisira kuti Ishe wavo naMesiasi wavo havasiyi mweya yavo muguva vachamukira kuutsva hwoupenyu nokudzoreredzwa kusingaperi. Nokuti ndichavabatanidza navadiwa vavo uye ndichavadzorera zvakadyiwa nemhashu uye ndichavadzorera zvakabiwa naSatani kwavari panguva dzavakanga vari vatorwa panyika vakamirira kudzikinurwa kwavo kuzere kubva munyika ino yakaipa uye yakaora.

Uye ndizvo zvakaita evhangeri isingaperi uye nokusingaperi zvinhu zvose zvichazadziswa mukururama, muutsvene, uye muchokwadi. Nokuti hapana chinhu chichapinda pamasuo oushe hwangu chine tsvina kana chakaipa kana chinoda uye chinoita nhema asi muchokwadi nomukururama kusingaperi ndimo muchava nokutonga kwangu. Saka inzwa inzwi raYeshua HaMashiach kunyange vose avo mashoko aya anotumirwa kwavari. Ishe wenyu usingaperi haana kukanganwa zvipikirwa zvekare uye hapana testamente yakashaya maturo. Asi zvose zvichazadziswa uye zvirokwazvo, zvizhinji zvakatozadziswa saka tarirai kuzvinhu izvozvo kuti muve netariro. Nokuti sezvazvakanyorwa ndizvo zvichaitwa uye ndizvo zvakaita evhangeri isingaperi munguva idzi dzokuzadziswa.

Ini Yeshua HaMashiach ndatumira Mashoko aya nomuranda wangu uye nomuranda wangu mashoko aya atumwa.

Ndini mbeu yakapikirwa yomukadzi.

Ndini chibvumirano chenyu chorugare.

Ndini Ishe waMadzishe akakwidzwa uyo ari gwayana raMwari.

Ndini iye uyo shumba ichavata pasi negwayana.

Ndini shumba yorudzi rwaJudha.

Ndini Gwayana raMwari.

Ndini Mambo waMadzimambo naIshe waMadzishe.

Ndini Mupristi Mukuru nokusingaperi maererano nourongwa hwaMerkizedeki.

Saka ndinokupai kuti munzwisise kuti kuzadziswa kwezvinhu zvose kuri pano uye kwatotanga. Vaya vemwenga wangu nomuviri wangu vachashumira uye vorarama utsva hwoupenyu pamwe chete naavo vachamutswa mukururama pakumuka kwevakarurama pashure pevhiki raDhanieri rechimakumi manomwe. Uye mukururama norugare tichararama mukuwirirana nezvisikwa zvaMwari uye kuchava norugare runogara uye rusingaperi kuvana vechipikirwa, uye vachashumirana, uye vachandishumira, uye vachashumira Yahweh uye murugare nokuwirirana zvisikwa zvaYahweh zvichadzoreredzwa.

Nokuti kuchine zororo rakasara kuvanhu vaMwari. Uye saka sezvo nguva yangu yenyasha yava kusvika kumagumo, nguva yokupedzisira yava pedyo vana vangu, kunyange zuva iroro rokupedzisira patichazorora tose pamabasa edu nematambudziko edu, uye tichanakidzwa nemafuta enyika sezvatinenge tichizorora pazuva rezororo, zvirokwazvo kuchava nezuva reSabata rokuwirirana. Pave nemazuva matanhatu okushanda, nokuti zuva rimwe kuna Ishe rakaita samakore ane chiuru uye makore ane chiuru sezuva rimwe. Nokudaro, mukutonga kwangu kwemakore ane chiuru vakatendeka vachapedza zuva rezororo nomusiki wavo, Baba, naMwari Wokusingaperi Yahweh asati asika denga idzva nenyika itsva umo muchangogara kururama kusingaperi. Tarirai, nguva dzava zvino, saka garai makagadzirira!
