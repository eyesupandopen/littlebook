Ndini uyo akabhabhatidzwa naJohane Mubhabhatidzi apo mweya wakaburuka senjiva kunzwi reizwi raBaba vangu.

Ndini uyo akauya kune vake uye akarambwa, murume wenhamo akakubhadharirai mutengo wepamusoro-soro imi mose.

Ndini mukuru kune vese kunze kwaBaba vangu, Yahweh.

Ndini Yeshua HaMashiach, anozivikanwawo saJesu Kristu, uye ιησου χριστου muchiGiriki uye mune mimwe mitauro yakawanda inondizivisa sese Jesu Kristu kana Kristu Jesu, nekuti ndiri Ishe!

Nenguva isipi muchandiona chiso nechiso, Mwenga wangu, uye muchaziva sezvamunozivikanwa. Naizvozvo, ndiri kukudanirai kwandiri; saka musaramba mashoko angu, uye teererai izwi rangu, nekuti hupenyu neruponeso zvinowanikwa nevanoteerera.
