Rutongo Rwemoto rwaHeylel Ben Shachar

Inzwai, Inzwai Mashoko aAdonai-YHVH uye muungane pedyo nenzwi rangu, kuti mugare patsoka dzangu, vana vangu, uye munzwe Mashoko angu andinoburitsa kubva pamweya wemutumwa wangu ane simba nomuranda wangu. Mashoko akapiwa nesimba guru muMwanakomana wangu mumwechete akaberekwa, uyo akaisa chisimbiso paMashoko aya ane simba mumutumwa nomuranda wedu wandakapa kuna Kristu Jesu senhumbi yakasarudzwa, kuti agare kuruoko rwake rweruboshwe mukukudzwa kaviri somufudzi ane rudo nedziviriro, akazadzwa netsitsi nokuti akarerwa noMufudzi Mukuru, anova Yeshua HaMashiach, uye akazadzwa norudo rwangu mutsitsi nenyasha zvisingaperi zvakapiwa muGwayana raAdonai-YHVH murudo rwake, umo maakaumba nokupfura Biblaridon:144 iye pachake, sezvaakaita zvizere mune vake vose vane simba zviuru zana nemakumi mana nezvina, avo vari vana vaYeshua HaMashiach vaakazvirerera noruoko rwake, uye hakuna rusaruro rwevanhu panzira dzaAdonai-YHVH, uye Yeshua HaMashiach iShoko raAdonai-YHVH rakaitwa nyama. Naizvozvo, nzwisisai imi mose zviuru zana nemakumi mana nezvina: Imi muri vana vaBaba Yeshua, uye ININI ndiri Adonai-YHVH wenyu, anova Baba kwamuri mose muna Kristu Jesu. Sera.

Naizvozvo, nzwisisai kuchengeta kwangu kusingaperi nouchangamire hwangu kuti ININI NDIRI IYE anokwanisa kumutsira Yeshua HaMashiach varwi vane simba kuti araire, uyo zvino amire musimba rizere rinonzwika uye akapiwa simba rizere uye akatumwa kununura vakatendeka muna Kristu Jesu seShumba yorudzi rwaJudha kubva pakati pavo, achikonzera kuparadzwa kamwe kamwe paanosunungura awa imwe chete nechikara pakubva kwenyu (kamwe kamwe) panguva iyoyo yakapiwa zvizere mumaoko ake kuti azadzise mukuteerera kwose kwakarurama nokunofadza, uye ndiYeshua HaMashiach anogara achiita kuda kwaAdonai-YHVH wake.

Saizvozvowo, Biblaridion:144 chombo chine simba uye chine mativi mazhinji muruoko rwaYeshua HaMashiach, uyo anowirirana zvizere nezvinangwa zvake sezvazvakaratidzwa kusvika nhasi nokusingaperi, uyo ariwo mumiririri worutongo rwokuzivisa norwokuita, kuti ashandiswe nomurairo kurova, uye akapiwawo basa rokuzivisa nokuita chaizvo sezvakaziviswa pasi pesimba rizere reShumba yoRudzi rwaJudha. Zvirokwazvo, Biblaridon ibakatwa riri mumuhara rakabatwa muruoko naYeshua HaMashiach, richazovhomorwa zvizere mukati mevhiki raDhanieri rechimakumi manomwe, uye nokuda kwezvinangwa zvangu zvisingaperi zvichazoratidzwa pamunenge maunganidzwa kwandiri mukubwaira kweziso, pandichakuzarurirai zvatakuri chaizvo nezvinangwa zvenyu zvakavanzwa zvino muna Kristu Jesu, asi zvino zvaratidzwa muchidimbu.

Nzwisisai vana vangu kuti vakatendeka vose muna Kristu Jesu, avo vari kereke yake, muviri wake, uye kunyange mwenga wake, vakasarudzwa chaizvo muna Kristu Jesu nyika isati yavambwa. Kunyange vazhinji vachidudzira Mashoko aya nemitsara isina musoro yakadai sokuti "vakanyanya kudiwa uye vakakomborerwa zvirokwazvo" kuti vanyaradze nyama kana zvataurwa pasina kutenda uye pasina kunzwisisa chokwadi changu chisingaperi, haisi iyo chinangwa chose asi zvirokwazvo ichokwadi kune vakatendeka muna Kristu nokuda kwokuteerera kwavo nokutendeuka kwavo.

Nzwisisai, mutsara unoti "vakasarudzwa maari nyika isati yavambwa" unoratidza kuti Adonai-YHVH wenyu akakusarudzai pachake uye akakutungamirirai nesimba rose rokuda uye nechinangwa chakakwana, pasina kumbotyora rusununguko rwomunhu zvachose, nokusarudza kuumba, kugadzira, nokusika patsva muna Kristu Jesu mumwe nomumwe wenyu nokunzwisisa kuzere uye norusununguko rwose rwounyanzvi uye nokugadzira nokungwarira kukuru kwazvo kune mumwe nomumwe wenyu, izvo zvakatora nguva dzose kubvira kuna Adhamu naEvha kusvikira zvino kuti zviitwe mukururama kwangu.

Imi muri mabasa angu ounyanzvi akasikwa muna Kristu!

Unyanzvi uhwo, kururama nokugadzirwa izvo zvakadyarwa mumajini enyu, pandakasarudza chaizvo nounyanzvi hukuru noumhizha zvinhu zvose pamusoro penyu nokuda kwezvinangwa zvangu zvisingaperi zvichazoratidzwa zvizere muimba yangu yechigaro choumambo, pakarepo uye nesimba nomurairo wangu. Naizvozvo, nzwisisai kuti ndakaumba, ndikagadzira, uye ndikasika mumwe nomumwe wenyu nenzira yakasarudzika uye zvakadzama zvikuru zvokuti hamugoni kunzwisisa zvamuchashandurwa kuva maererano nezvinangwa zvangu zvakasiyana uye zvakasarudzwa nokuda kwomumwe nomumwe wenyu.

Naizvozvo, musatya, Adonai-YHVH wenyu haasaruri pakusika chisikwa chimwe kuti chive chakasiyana nechimwe kuitira kuti mukana unofungidzirwa upembererwe muchivi chokuzvikudza, kana kuti chisikwa chimwe chizotonga chimwe muchisimba chine chivi noruvengo choupenzi, asi kuti zvinangwa zvangu zvisingaperi zvizadziswe mamuri mose. Zvechokwadi chisingaperi: Hazvisati zvapinda zvizere mumwoyo womunhu panguva yokunyorwa kuno kuti muchasikwa patsva kuva chii uye nokuda kwezvinangwa zvipi. Naizvozvo, ndinokukumbirai murudo rwangu kwamuri (imi vandakasika uye vandakasarudza nokuda kwokutenda kwenyu, imi vana vangu vanotendeuka nokutendeka) kuti muve nokutenda kwakaita setsanga yemasitadhi uye munzwisise kuti kubvira kumuduku kusvika kumukuru pakati paIsraeri waAdonai-YHVH, mose makaitwa nokugadzirwa nenzira yakakwana uye inoshamisa, uye makapfurwa nesimba guru zvokuti ndiIsraeri waAdonai-YHVH achayemurwa navose.

Rugare ngaruve nemi zvizere.

Uye izvi pasina rusaruro rwevanhu, asi panzvimbo pazvo noruzivo rwangu rwakarurama uye rusingaperi rwemasimbaose uye nokudzora kwangu kwakarurama kwezvinhu zvose, pandakakusarudzai mumwe nomumwe nenzira yakadaro nokuda kwezvinangwa zvangu zvisingaperi muna Kristu Jesu. Nokuti ndaiziva zvizere munguva dzisingaperi dzakapfuura kuti mumwe nomumwe wenyu, pachake, aizouya zvizere kuna Kristu Jesu munguva dzenyu, saka ndakagovera. Naizvozvo, nzwisisai kuti kunyange zvakavanzika zvisinganzwisisiki zvichizarurwa nouchapupu nomuenzaniso webasa muna Biblaridion:144, zvinhu izvi zvinoitwa kuti imi mose mutange kunzwisisa ukuru hwenhaka yenyu nezvinangwa zvenyu zvisingaperi zvandakakusikirai, ndikakusarudzai, uye ndikakuitai mose nokugadzirwa kwakangwara kusvika pane zvidukusa. Farai!

Naizvozvo, uyai zvizere kuna Yeshua HaMashiach, kunyange imi mose vakatendeka muna Kristu Jesu, kuti mugamuchire mubairo wenyu wakapetwa kaviri mukukudzwa kuzere, waanayo muruoko rwake wandakapa kune vose vanokunda nenzira yakafanana naKristu Jesu zvaakakunda. Inzwai mashoko angu okukurudzira, kunyange imi mose zviuru zana nemakumi mana nezvina, imi muri vana vakagamuchirwa naBaba venyu Yeshua, uye achagoverana zvinhu zvose nemi vana vake, avo variwo vakundi vake vakatendeka, vakakunda zvinhu zvose sezvakakunda Yeshua HaMashiach. Zvisinei, ava havasi ivo vakundi voga vachange vakunda zvinhu zvose zvakavarwisa, nokuti vazhinji, vazhinji pakati pavakatendeka muna Kristu Jesu vasiri zviuru zana nemakumi mana nezvina vakakunda zvinhu zvose nenzira yakafanana naKristu Jesu, uye mubairo wavo mukuru!

Hongu! Kune vakundi vazhinji pakati paIsraeri waAdonai-YHVH uye hakuna rusaruro rwevanhu kuna Adonai-YHVH, uye zvikurukuru kuna Yeshua HaMashiach anogara achiita zvakakwana kuda kwaAdonai-YHVH Wake. Naizvozvo, musanetseka uye musazvidya mwoyo pamunoedza kunzwisisa zvakavanzwa kuMatenga zvisati zvapinda zvizere mumwoyo womunhu panguva ino, uye zvikurukuru dzivisai kuzvienzanisa nomumwe nomumwe sezvinoitwa mumamiriro enyu azvino akawa, asi akaitwa mutsvene zvizere muna Kristu Jesu.

Saka nzwisisai, muUmambo hweKudenga, ani naani anokwidziridzwa pamusoro pomumwe anoitwa musimba, uye/kana mubairo nokuda kwezvinangwa zvangu ndoga, asi kwete kuti vamukudze kuitira kuti mumwe muchisikwa changu anzwe ari muduku, kana kuti avo vangakwidziridzwa vazozvikudza muchivi pamusoro pomumwe. Asi panzvimbo pazvo, rukudzo norudo zvinopiwa zvizere kwamuri mose. Nokuti ndakakusikai mose, uye zvikurukuru vakatendeka muna Kristu Jesu nokuda kwokuteerera kwenyu muna Kristu Jesu kuitira kuti ndigokwanisa kukusikai patsva norukudzo ruzere. Imi, zvikurukuru, imi muri mabasa angu ounyanzvi akasikwa muna Kristu Jesu kuti muite mabasa akanaka, kuti mufambe maari nesimba guru nokuda kwezvinangwa zvisingaperi zvaAdonai-YHVH wenyu, zvakapiwa muna Kristu Jesu. Farai!

Uye ndizvo zvazviri kuti ININI MUKURU, kunyange Baba vomuroori, anova Yeshua HaMashiach, akapa Mhere-mhere yepakati pousiku kumwenga navaenzi vake, uye vakazvigadzirira kuti marambi avo azare nemafuta omweya mutsvene uye tambo dzenyu dzakagurwa tsvina dzose uye marambi enyu akabatidzwa nemirazvo yakachena yakaitwa nemafuta iwayo akachena omweya wangu wandakapa muna Yeshua HaMashiach, uye aakasika nenzira yakasiyana mukati momumwe nomumwe wenyu, imi vakatendeka muna Kristu.

Uye saka, ndinokurudzira vakatendeka vose muna Kristu Jesu kuti varambe vachiteerera zvizere uye zvakadzama muzvinhu zvose kuMufudzi wenyu Mukuru, uyo anotarisira zvose zvoupenyu hwenyu hwose nenzira yakadzama uye yakakwana. Uyai zvizere kuna Ishe wenyu weKururama wekusingaperi wandakakupai mose apo Yeshua weNazareta akazvipa sePaseka yakakwana yenguva dzose mumaoko omuchinda muduku uyo akakundwa mukuteerera kwose kukuda kwaAdonai-YHVH. Uyai zvizere zvino, musingazezi uye munzwisise kuti ININI MUKURU ndiri kukukwezvai kuburikidza naMashoko aya akagadzirirwa kuwana meso, nzeve, nemwoyo yavose kwavanotumirwa.

Nzwisisai nguva dzamunorarama, nokuti nguva yangu yenyasha yasvika kumagumo, kuti igumiswe kamwe kamwe uye zvisingatarisirwi uye zvizere nomurairo waYeshua HaMashiach kana murairo wapiwa uye mhere-mhere ine simba!

Zvino, kurira kune simba uye kwakanaka kwebhosvo, apo kamwe kamwe panyika hamupo!

Apo kamwe kamwe kudenga rematenga muchagamuchirwa mukubwinya kukuru, kukunda, simba, norukudzo munhaka yenyu yenguva dzose yakagadzirirwa kugoverwa kune vakatendeka muna Kristu Jesu. Zvirokwazvo mwaka unotarisirwa worudzikinuro rwaAdhamu kune vakatendeka muna Kristu Jesu wasvika kuMatenga kusina mumwe mwaka wakambovapo, nokuti Vana vangu vekusingaperi vakasarudzwa chaizvo naMashoko aya akapiwa muna Johane Wokutanga: "Tarirai, rudo rwakadini rwatakapiwa naBaba, kuti tinzi vana vaMwari: Nokuda kwechikonzero ichi nyika ino haikuziviyi, nokuti haimuzivi iye. Vadiwa, zvino tiri vana vaMwari, asi, hazvisati zvaratidzwa zvatichava: uye tinoziva kuti kana achinge aratidzwa: tichafanana naye: nokuti tichamuona sezvaari. Uye munhu wose ane tariro iyi maari anozvinatsa, sezvaakachena iye", zvino vachaberekwa patsva vari vapenyu uye vakasikwa patsva zvizere mukubwinya kwose, mbiri, norukudzo pakarepo nomurairo waYeshua HaMashiach wandakamupa chaizvo uye ZVINO muchamira kamwe kamwe uye zvisingatarisirwi muimba yangu yechigaro choumambo navose vakatendeka muna Kristu Jesu.

Harpazo yenyu yasvika!

Uye saka, ndinoraira vakatendeka vose muna Kristu Jesu kuti vateerere Mashoko aya mukuteerera kwakasimba kuitira kuti Kristu Jesu akuchenurei mose zvizere pasi peropa rake rinoyananisa musati matorwa uye mashandurwa nokusingaperi uye nokusingaperi-peri.

Garai Makagadzirira!

Nokuti muchaberekerwa muimba yangu yechigaro choumambo, kwandinomirira nomufaro saBaba vanotarisira vandiri, kuti Israeri waAdonai-YHVH anofanira kuunganidzwa ZVINO asunungurwe zvizere.

Nechiono ichi mupfungwa, ndinouya kuzobatsira mumwe nomumwe wenyu kunzwisisa kuedza uye zvakadzama zvakashandiswa muhuchenjeri hukuru nounyanzvi naAdonai-YHVH wenyu kuti akudzivirirei uye akuchengetedzei kubva kuna Heylel Ben Shachar, uyo aizouraya kubva panyika chero upi zvake wevana vangu kana akawana mukana, asi achikuzarurirai zvakakwana kuitira kuti muzopfuurwa maererano nezvinangwa zvangu zvisingaperi muna Kristu Jesu.

Naizvozvo, makaiswa chiratidzo uye makachengetedzwa, asi makavanzwa uye makahwandiswa pachena, asi makaratidzwa zvishoma: Uye zvose namaoko aKristu Jesu! Nokuti ndiye akakudai uye akazvipa, uye akatsvenesa, uye akanatsa uye akaruramisa Kereke yake ine simba inoumbwa navakatendeka muna Kristu Jesu. Nokuti Zvirokwazvo, masuwo eGehena haazombokundi Kereke yechokwadi mukubwinya kwayo kwose kune simba nesimba rayo. Kunyange Kereke iyo ichashandurwa uye ikachinjwa zvino nokuda kwezvinangwa zvayo zvechokwadi uye zvakavanzwa kuti zvino zvizarurwe zvizere, izvo imi mose muchaziva zvizere kunyange sezvamunozivikanwa.

Ivai nomwoyo murefu vana vangu, nguva dzenyu ndedze ZVINO.

Kunyange zvakadaro, ndakaparadzanisa boka rimwe rakasiyana neKereke, ndichivagara ndichivagadzirira nokuda kwezvinangwa zvangu (kunyange imi mose muine basa renyu) uye ndikavapa kuna Kristu Jesu. Naizvozvo, nzwisisai musiyano uyu imi mose zviuru zana nemakumi mana nezvina uye kunyange vose vanoverenga Mashoko aya.

Kristu Jesu murudo rwake kwamuri mose, uye rudo rwake nokuteerera kwake kuna Adonai-YHVH wake, akamutsira Adonai-YHVH vana, avo vari vakatendeka muna Kristu Jesu uye vakaiswa chisimbiso nomweya mutsvene iwoyo wechivimbiso uye vakatsaurwa uye vakazvipira nokusingaperi kuzadza nzvimbo dzisina vanhu kuMatenga mukukudzwa kaviri, uye vagare nhaka yenyika mukukudzwa kaviri nokuti makasarudza kuva nokutenda muuchapupu hwaJesu weNazareta pazuva rino rakaipa, kunyange Gwayana iro raMwari paakaratidzwa kwamuri naAdonai-YHVH wenyu uye pamakapiwa mukana wokuva nokutenda kwakaita setsanga yemasitadhi, makatenda! Uye mbeu iyo ine simba uye isingaperi yakura zvino nesimba guru mamuri mose! Asi ichangozarurwa zvizere muimba yangu yechigaro choumambo kamwe kamwe uye zvisingatarisirwi zvino.

GARAI MAKAGADZIRIRA!

Nzwisisai zvizere, kuti vakatendeka muna Kristu Jesu vana vaAdonai-YHVH, avo vachashumira Kristu Jesu, anova zvakare Yeshua HaMashiach, mumabasa enyu nemumirairo yenyu yandakagadzirira zvakakwana uye ndikagadzirira mumwe nemumwe wenyu. Mubairo wenyu wakagadzirirwa imi zvakakwana uye uchave mukuru kupfuura mumwe, zvichienderana nekutendeka kwenyu chete. Zvisinei, ichi hachisi chipingamupinyi pakukudzwa kwenyu nenhaka, asi rukudzo runopiwa kune vanofanirwa narwo nekuda kwezvinhu zvizhinji zvakaitwa mukushumira Kristu Jesu nemi mose. Uye imi mose makasiyana zvikuru kubva kune mumwe, asi makafanana muzvinhu zvamakatsungirira nekukunda neruoko rune simba rwaKristu Jesu akakubatsirai mose.

Zvakare, pakati pevakatendeka muna Kristu Jesu pakatorwa zviuru zana nemakumi mana nezvina (144,000) avo vakatsaurwa zvakananga sevana vakagamuchirwa vaYeshua HaMashiach, vakasarudzwa kubva pakati peIsraeri yaAdonai-YHVH neruoko rwangu rwakasarudzwa, uye nekuda kusingaperi kusingadzivisiki. Varwi ava vemoto vakapfurwa mukutambudzika kukuru uye kuburikidza nemiedzo mizhinji nematambudziko avakatsungirira mumaoko aHeylel Ben Shachar, uye zvino vakaputirwa nekupfurwa musimba guru kuitira kuti vapfurwe zvizere mumwoyo yavo isingazombopandukira zvinangwa zvaAdonai-YHVH zvakaratidzwa muna Yeshua HaMashiach, vakatochengetedzwa murudo rweAgape rusingakotami uye rune simba rose. Selah.

Naizvozvo, nzwisisai imi vangu zviuru zana nemakumi mana nezvina (144,000), kuti imi mose makasikwa zvine simba uye zvakakwana muchimiro chine simba rose uye chakachenjera, uye nemurairo waKristu Jesu, anova zvakare Yeshua HaMashiach, muchazadzisa zvinangwa zvedu zvisingaperi mukurwisana nenyoka iyi yechigehena, kuitira kuti vose vakawa vatongwe zvizere nemuripo wakakwana uye chirango chakapiwa mukutonga kwemoto kwakarurama pamusoro pemuchinda uyu mudiki akakundikana uye benzi. Kunyange zvakadaro, imi mose zviuru zana nemakumi mana nezvina (144,000) ndimi vamiririri vezviito izvi zvinopiwa muchitarisiko chizere cherugwaro rwakanyorwa chaizvo: Kutsiva ndekwangu; ndini ndicharipira, anodaro Ishe. Naizvozvo, imi ndimi vamiririri vakagadzwa vemuripo uyu wakarurama pamisoro yevose vakawa, asi kunyanya pamuchinda uyo benzi, akakundikana uye anogara achizvikudza, Heylel Ben Shachar.

Kunyange muchinda uyo mudiki akakundikana uyo akakudzwa kupfuura vamwe vake nekuda kwezvinangwa zvisingaperi zvaElohim, uye mukuchiva kwake nekuzvikudza paakazvikudza, achifunga kuti zvese zvandakamusikira zvaive zvekubwinya kwake nezvinangwa zvake kuti zvishandiswe nemurairo wake nekuda kwake, saka akawira mukupanduka kukuru nekusateerera nenzira inonyadzisa zvikuru mukunyara kuzere nekuzvikudza kwakapwanyika zvachose uye anochengetwa mumamiriro ezvinhu anorwadza zvikuru uye akaremara aasingazombopori uye anongowedzera kuipa zvakanyanya nekusingaperi.

Saizvozvo, apo Heylel Ben Shachar (nevose vakatevera serafimi iri) vakakandwa nechisimba, uye nehasha, uye nemarwadzo makuru zvisina basa nesimba rangu kubva kuDenga reMatenga, pavakatorerwa nechisimba masimba ose nekuda kwezvivi zvavo zvinotyisa uye zvechibomo uye zvakafanorongwa zvakaitwa neruvengo rwose uye kurangana. Kunyange zvinhu zvakakura kubva mukati memwoyo yavo nekuti Heylel Ben Shachar akaramba kudzora pfungwa dzake mukuteerera Elohim wake, panzvimbo pekutarisa kubwinya nesimba raElohim wake, kuitira kuti ndimukwidziridze pachinzvimbo chake chisingaperi chebasa, simba, uye kubwinya, iye akatarisa kubwinya kwake kwaakaedza kutora nechisimba sekwake.

Uye mukunyengedzwa kwake kwechivi kwakaitika paakazvikudza muchivi pamusoro paElohim wake akamusika zvine simba uye nechinangwa chakasimba uye akamupfekedza korona yekubwinya kukuru nerukudzo pazuva raakasikwa maererano nezvirongwa zvangu nezvinangwa, neunyanzvi nesimba, Heylel Ben Shachar nevose vakamutevera vakakandwa nechisimba kubva kuMatenga vasati vambokwanisa kusimuka kuzadza zvinzvimbo zvavo zvisingaperi zvebasa, simba, uye kubwinya nekuti vakazvipira nemhiko dzekusateerera kune ruvengo mukupanduka kusina musoro panzvimbo.

Heylel Ben Shachar ipapo nemaune uye nekufanoronga kwakazara akadyara kupanduka kwake muchivande pakati pevamwe vake, sekunge El Shaddai haana kuona zviito zvake nepfungwa nezvinangwa zvemwoyo wake. Zvisinei, ndakavabvumira kuzadza zvivi zvavo kusvika pakuzara, kuitira kuti vazotongwa zvakanyanya pakati pevose vakapihwa mhosva vakambosikwa uye vakapihwa mhosva nekuda kwezvivi zvavo zvine ruvengo naElohim.
