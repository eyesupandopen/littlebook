Chapupu Chiviri: Israeri
Zvinovava Zvinotapira
Mbudzi 10, 2024

Kuunganidzwa kweVakatendeka muna Kristu!

Tarirai, Tarirai! Nzwisisai zvava kuda kusunungurwa panyika.

Tarirai, Tarirai! Zvirokwazvo hasha dzegwayana dziri pamusoro pako iwe Bhabhironi risinganzwisisike. Uye sezvandakayambira, ini, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndizvo zvazvichaitwa!

Tarirai! Tarirai! Zuva rasvika pamusoro pako iwe Bhabhironi Rakavanzika uye zuva rako rekutongwa rasvika, kunyange zuva richasunungura zuva iro guru rakapikirwa kare kare, iro zuva raIshe! Mune nhamo imi vagari vepanyika nekuti hasha huru dzava kuda kusunungurwa pamusoro penyu zvizere, uye hamungapunyuke uye zvirokwazvo, zvirokwazvo vhiki raDhanieri rechimakumi manomwe rasvika.

Saka ndinopa zano kereke yangu, muviri wangu uye kunyange mwenga wangu kuti muzvisunge mukururama kwangu uye muzvigadzirire kumira makarurama pamberi paIshe wenyu, kunyange mukamuri rechigaro cheushe chaYahweh, kwandichapa zvibereko zvekutanga, kunyange vose vandichaunganidza vakafa vari muna Kristu uye vari pakuzorora vakamirira kudaidzwa kwangu, uye kunyange vose vari vapenyu panyika vari vakatendeka muna Kristu panguva yekuonekwa kwangu. Uye nenzira imwecheteyo yandakazvipa kuna Yahweh sezvibereko zvekutanga kubva kuvakafa, nenzira yakadaro munozivikanwa naIshe wenyu weKururama wekusingaperi. Nekuti nenzira imwecheteyo yandakatorwa kuenda kudenga, saizvozvowo muchatorwa kuenda kudenga, kwandichapa zvibereko zvangu zvekutanga zvekukohwa zvisina gwapa kana kuunyana kana chinhu chakadaro.

Uye imi mose muchamira pamberi pechigaro changu chekutonga, kunyange chigaro icho chikuru chekutonga kwachichatongwa mabasa enyu uye vamwe vachapiwa mubairo, uye vamwe vacharasikirwa uye vamwe vachawana kurasikirwa nemubairo sezvo huni, uswa, nemashanga zvichipiswa nekuti hapana pakati penyu akafamba akakwana pamberi pangu asi murudo rwaYahweh muchashamiswa zvikuru neukuru hwenhaka yenyu. Uye kune vakundi vangu mubairo wenyu mukuru uye muchagara nhaka yezvinhu zvose sezvandakaita pandakakunda vakawa. Zvisinei, vose vanounganidzirwa kuna Ishe wavo wekusingaperi, kunyange Yeshua HaMashiach vachasvika paupenyu hutsva sezvo vachisikwa patsva kusingaperi uye hamuzomboori kana kushanduka kubva pakukwana kwakakumirirai, nekuti Yahweh akazivisa saizvozvo!

Uye muchatorwa pakarepo, uye nyika ichaziva kuti ndaonekwa, uye mudzivisi achabviswa! Uye mukushaikwa kwemudzivisi wangu simba rose nerukurudziro yezvakaipa zvichasunungurwa zvizere panyika. Uye ndini, Yeshua HaMashiach, kunyange Kristu Jesu, anozivisa Mashoko aya uye saka Mashoko aya achazivikanwa panyika, uye achawana maziso, nzeve, nemoyo yeavo kwaakatumirwa.

Uye ndizvo zvazviri, uye ndizvo zvazvichava, asi kwenguva pfupi zvikuru ushe hweShumba huchasimuka zvizere, nekuti ndizvo zvavakapiwa kuti vaite. Nekuti pandakarambwa nehama dzangu, kunyange Israeri wandiri mudzikinuri wehama, ndakatendeukira kumarudzi kuitira kuti marudzi akomborerwe, kunyange sezvakapikirwa naYahweh kare kare. Uye Mashoko angu akagamuchirwa nesimba, kunyange kuburikidza nekutambudzwa kukuru nemiedzo mizhinji, uye masuwo egehena haana kukunda Kereke yangu. Asi, kunyange Kereke yangu ichibviswa, uye mudzivisi wangu achibviswa, zvakaipa zvichakunda nyika zvizere, uye havangapunyuke.

Kunyange vose vanofarira kusarurama uye vanoda kuramba Jesu weNazareta vachida Bhabharabhasi uye pavachati rugare nerunyararo, ipapo kuparadzwa kunongoerekana kwasvika, uye havangapunyuke. Asi Shumba ichazviratidza, uye yakasungirirwa panyika uye kubva kudenga yakadzingwa zvachose kuti isazodzoka, uye ine hasha huru nekuti inoziva kuti nguva yayo ipfupi, asi inofunga zvisina kuchenjera kuti ichiri kundikunda ini Yeshua HaMashiach, kunyange zvazvo ndakai kunda zvachose paKarivhari mumabasa angu ekuyananisa eruponeso akaguma naZvazopera!

Uye vakawa vanofunga neimwe nzira kuti vanogona kupukunyuka kutongwa kwavo kwavakapikirwa kare nekuti vanotya ramangwana ravo kupfuura chero chinhu chipi zvacho; nekuti vanonzwisisa kuti muhutsinye hwavo havana kuratidza uye havaratidze tsitsi. Nekuti vanoziva kuti havana kuratidza tsitsi, saka, hapana tsitsi dzicharatidzwa kwavari. Vanoziva zvakanaka kuti mabasa avo ose asina kururama uye anonyangadza achapiwa kwavari zvachose kakawanda mugungwa guru remoto unopfuta nesarufa uko mirazvo yekurangwa isingagone kudzimwa. Kunyange mirazvo yakasikwa iyo icharipira basa rose rakaipa ravakaita muzvipfuva zvavo, uye vacharipa zvizere uye mukurwadziwa kukuru vachava zvachose.

Nekuti vakarwisa Yahweh uye vakarwisa bindu raMwari, kunyange sezvavainyepedzera kuti vaiva vamwari uye kutongwa kwavo kwasvika. Asi ndizvo zvazviri, kuti vakawa vakakotsirisa vanhu vazhinji nematekinoroji avo, nemafrequency, uye nemafaro ezvivi, uye nemubairo wekusarurama. Asi, ndizvo zvazviri kuti vanhu vakada rima panzvimbo yechiedza, saka zvino, ndichavapa rima ravanoshuvira zvikuru.

Nekuti mafaro ose achabviswa panyika uye upenyu hwezvivi uye hweumbozha hwakanyanyisa hahuchazomboonekwa panyika zvakare nenzira imwecheteyo apo vanhu vanozviunganidzira pfuma uye vanosiya hama dzavo mukuchiva kwavo nekusarurama, kunyange vachizvidza vanoshaya sezvavanovatsika vachienda kunounganidza pfuma yakawanda. Munhu wenyama akanyura mumafaro ezvivi zvekuti haatombosimudzi munwe kubatsira vanoshaya, uye saka handisi kuzosimudza munwe kumubatsira kupukunyuka kutongwa kwake nekuti munhu wenyama zvino apiwa mumaoko evavengi vake. Uye ndizvo zvazvichava kuti zvinhu izvi hazvizopona vhiki raDhanieri rechimakumi manomwe uye hazvizomboitika zvakare nenzira yakadaro muushe hwangu hwekururama!

Asi ndizvo zvazvichava kuti kana mudzivisi abviswa, zvakaipa zviri mukati mevanhu nekuda kwemano enyoka zvino zvicharegedzwa uye kupenga kuchakunda nyika sezvo hama ichauraya hama uye vanhu venyika imwe vacharwa nevanhu venyika yavo uye nyonganyonga yakanyanyisa nehondo zvichakunda nyika. Asi chiitiko ichi ndiko kuparadzwa nekutongwa kweBhabhironi risinganzwisisike, uye zvisimbiso zvagadzirirwa kusunungurwa, uye vatasvi vemabhiza vachatasva!

Uye saka kuramba kukuru kuri kuitika sezvakaprofitwa. Nekuti panguva yekuonekwa kwangu, apo vazhinji vaifanira kunge vakamirira uye vachizvinatsa muShoko rangu nemweya, uye panzvimbo yekupedzisa ruponeso rwavo mumaoko angu erudo, vakasiya Ishe wavo, kunyange Kristu Jesu, vachida Bhabharabhasi wavakapa mwoyo yavo. Uye sezvakaita Pontiyo Pirato, uyo aive akatsunga kundisunungura kubva kunhema dzavo dzechiFarisi dzisina hwaro nekupomerwa, akapa vanhu venyika sarudzo. Uye saka Pirato akati kwavari pavakanga vaunganidzwa pamwechete, munoda kuti ndikusunungurirei ani, Bhabharabhasi, kana Jesu anonzi Kristu? Uye vakati, Bhabharabhasi. Pirato akati kwavari, Zvino ndichaita sei naJesu, anonzi Kristu? Vose vakati kwaari, ngaarovererwe.

Uye izvi zvakaitika nekuti Esau akashandisa simba rake rakaipa pamusoro pevanhu kuitira kuti vazvitore mhondi panzvimbo yeuyo akatumirwa ruponeso rwavo, kunyange Jesu weNazareta! Asi chirango chemutemo waMozisi chaizozadziswa sei kunze kwekuzvipira kwangu mukurwadziwa kukuru segwayana raMwari, kunyange kwandakazvipa nekuda kwezvivi zvenyika semupristi mukuru wekusingaperi maererano neUrongwa hwaMerkizedeki. Kunyange zvakadaro, mukuziva kwaYahweh kumberi aiziva zvizere kuti Israeri acharamba Mesiya wavo wechokwadi uye saka akashandisa ruvengo negodo revakawa pamusoro pavo, nekuti dai vaiziva chakavanzika chikuru chaMwari havaimboroverera Ishe wenyu wekusingaperi weKubwinya!

Asi ndizvo zvazviri, kuti Merkizedeki Mambo weSaremu akaburitsa chingwa newaini: uye aive Mupristi waMwari Wekumusoro-soro. Uye saka Abhurama akapa zvipo zvake sezvaidiwa kukudza Yahweh uye saka Merkizedeki urongwa hwekutenda hwaive mumvuri wezvinhu zvaizouya. Nekuti sezvakanyorwa nezvaAbhurama akatendeka akararama nekutenda muna Mwari Wekumusoro-soro uye nemuenzaniso wake vakarurama vachararama nekutenda zvachose! Uye saka vemarudzi vakakomborerwa uye vakabatanidzwa mumadzinza pakati peIsraeri yaMwari, uye vanotora chikamu muzvipikirwa zvakaitirwa Madzibaba zvizere nekuti vakagamuchirwa naYahweh pachake kunyange neruoko rwangu rune simba, saka izvo Mwari akanatsa usazviti zvakajairika kana zvisina kuchena, iwe Israeri!

Asi ndizvo zvazviri kuti zuva rangu rasvika. Uye handisi kuzogovana kubwinya kwangu nemumwe, uye Mwari akatuma kunyengera kune simba kuti vatende nhema. Nekuti avo vanotevera Bhabharabhasi vanodaro nekuda kwekuchiva kwavo nekuti vanofungidzira kuti vari chikamu chenguva yake yegoridhe yakapikirwa iyo isiri yavo, kunyange achinyepa. Nekuti nguva yegoridhe yaanopikira yakavakirwa panguva yegoridhe yevakawa mumazuva aNoa apo vakawa vaitonga nyika mukusarurama! Naizvozvo, kana Bhabharabhasi achitaura nezvenguva yegoridhe anotaura zvechokwadi nezvechinangwa chemwoyo yavo, kunze kwekuti vanotsvaga zvizere kuparadzwa kwaAdhamu kuitira kuti zvinonyangadza zvavo nemasora zvikunde bindu raMwari zvachose. Uye ndizvo zvazviri, zvino munonzwisisa kuti sei pasina nyama yaizoponeswa mhenyu dai Yahweh asina kupfupisa mazuva.

Asi hapana nguva yegoridhe yavachava nayo nekuti ndini mbeu yakapikirwa yemukadzi ichapwanya zvachose musoro wenyoka uye ndichapwanya zvizere ushe hwake hwose uye havangamiri. Saka panzvimbo yenguva yavo yegoridhe yavanoshuvira, vachava nekutongwa kwakaomarara uye ushe hwavo huchaparadzwa zvachose pamberi pavo kuti husazombosimuka zvakare, uye zvisimbiso zvichasunungurwa, uye hwamanda dzicharira, uye vakasara vangu vachavanzwa uye ipapo ndiro dzine hasha dzaMwari dzizere dzichadururwa, uye vachava mukurwadziwa kukuru kunyange vachiona kukundwa kwavo kuchisvika zvizere.

Naizvozvo, avo vanotevera Bhabharabhasi zvisina kuchenjera kunyange achinyepedzera zvisina kuchenjera kuti akatumwa naYahweh, achiziva zvakanaka kuti anobva kushumba panzvimbo, uye waakasarudzwa kutungamira nyika uye kunyange nyika nechikara chinobva mugungwa. Asi ndizvo zvazviri, vazhinji vakawa mumaoko emunyengeri uyu uye vakasiya Kristu Jesu zvino vachapiwa mumaoko ake sezvo vakatendeka vachitorwa. Uye kune avo vaizondipomera kuti ndakasiya Kereke yangu asi ndivo vasiri muna Kristu, vasina kumbosvika paruponeso ruzere vakandisiya nekubatana naBhabharabhasi, uye ndakavayambira kuti vanofanira kugara mumuzambiringa nekuti pasina ini, hamugone kuita chinhu.

Kunyange vazhinji vachaparara vane mazita asina kumbonyorwa muBhuku reUpenyu reGwayana uye vachaparara kubva panyika kana kutongwa kwavo kwasvika, uye pakarepo tariro dzavo dzichaparadzwa.

Nekuti vakarasikirwa netariro yose yekudzoka kwaIshe wavo kwavari kunyange sezvandakapikira muTestamente Yangu Itsva uye chipikirwa chose chaYahweh naYeshua HaMashiach chichazadziswa nguva dzose uye zvachose!

Nekuti nguva yangu yenyasha netsitsi yasvika kumagumo, kunyange nyasha netsitsi zviri neni zvachose, uye sezvakanyorwa ndizvo zvazvichaitwa kuti vose Yahweh naYeshua HaMashiach vachanzwira tsitsi uyo watichanzwira tsitsi nekuti ndiani angamira pamberi pechigaro cheushe chaYahweh nekuti ndiye Mwari Wekumusoro-soro uye hakuna akafanana naye! Saka nzwisisai kuti hasha dzagadzirirwa uye dzava kuda kusunungurwa panyika kunyange sezvo marwadzo angu ekuzvara akazivisa uye akafanotaura, uye zuva iroro rasvika!

Uye saka ndinozivisa kuonekwa kwangu, uye zvichaitika sezvakanyorwa mumagwaro uye mwenga wangu achabatanidzwa zvachose muchibvumirano nemwenga wake uye vose vanotorwa kuenda kudenga panguva yekuonekwa kwangu, vangave vapenyu kana vakafa, vachasikwa patsva zvachose muutswa husingaperi hweupenyu. Uye vose vakazvipira zvechokwadi kumashoko akanaka nekutaura kwakanaka kwaBhabharabhasi vachawa kubva pakuunganidzwa kwangu pamwechete kunyange semashizha akaoma muchivi. Asi, vazhinji vanowa vachauya kwandiri kuburikidza nemiedzo mikuru nekutambudzika uye vazhinji vavo vachadana zita rangu kusvika kumagumo uye ruzhinji rweruzhinji ruchabuda muvhiki raDhanieri rechimakumi manomwe vachipinda muupenyu husingaperi, kunyange vazhinji vachatora mucherechedzo wechikara uye vachawira mumirazvo zvachose.

Naizvozvo, ini, Kristu Jesu kunyange Ishe wenyu akamuka uye akakwidzwa, ndinorayira vakatendeka vangu kuti vabude zvizere pakati pavo uye vaparadzane navo zvizere. Zvigadzirirei vadikani vangu! Musatora chikamu mukuratidzira kwavo, dzivirisai mapoka avo, uye nemutengo wose dzivirisai zvakaipa zviri kuzviunganidza pasi pemweya wenhema werugare nekuti kurwisa kwavo kwakagadzirira zvirokwazvo. Nzwisisai vadikani vangu kuti mungadai muchipinda mumusungo wavo dai maizobatana navo nenzira yakadaro. Vadikani vangu, inguva yekusiya zvinonetsa zvenyika ino, nekuti upenyu hwenyu hwakavanzwa mandiri, Kristu Jesu, uye hwava kuda kuzarurwa zvizere kuitira kuti muzive kunyange sezvamunozivikanwawo.

Saka nzwisisai kunyengera kuri pamusoro penyu vadikani vangu uye muzvivanze uye mumirire kuonekwa kwangu, nekuti kwava pedyo! Nzwisisai kunyengera kwavo, kuti gwayana rine nyanga mbiri richanyepedzera ronda raro remusoro, uye richaonekwa richimuka kubva kuvakafa uye rakapora kubva paronda raro remusoro asi zvese zvarinopa sekusimbisa kwekutaura kwaro kwenhema kuti ndiye Moshiach wavo zviratidzo zvenhema nezvishamiso uye hapana Yahweh mukuru NDIRI, kana ini, Yeshua HaMashiach takatuma munyengeri uyu, kunyange achibvumirwa kuuya panguva yake yakatarwa kuitira kuti atonge nechikara chinobva mugungwa uye neshumba semusoro wavo nekuti ndizvo zvavakapiwa kuti vaite muchirongwa chaYahweh chekusingaperi chekudzikinura Adhamu!

Uye saka ndinokuudzai vadikani vangu kuti mundimirire nemoyo murefu uye musatenda nhema dzavo nekuti hapana munhu kana chinhu chinogona kumisa kuonekwa kwangu kune vakatendeka muKereke yangu, kunyange vachatsvaga kuvhara chiitiko ichi nekurwisa kwenhema kwevatorwa uye hondo yenyika ichapfuura hondo dzose dzakambouya panyika. Asi, hondo yavo ichaenzaniswa zvisina maturo nehondo yezuva iro guru kwavachaparadzwa zvachose pandichauya panyika nemauto ekudenga, nekuti mukururama ndinouya kuzotonga nekuita hondo!

Ini, Yeshua HaMashiach ndakatumira Mashoko aya nemuranda wangu anova mutumwa wangu akatendeka, uye ndini, Yeshua HaMashiach ndinosimbisa uchapupu uhu hwakapihwa muEvhangeri isingaperi yakazarurwa panguva yayo.

Ndini uyo zvino achadzokera kuna Israeri kuti ndivadzikinure kubva panyika semudzikinuri wavo wehama uye ndiri kukudaidzai imi Israeri nekuti inguva yekugamuchira Mesiya wenyu wechokwadi, nekuti nenzira imwecheteyo iyo munyengeri uyu achapandukira nekuparadza Bhabhironi risinganzwisisike, nenzira imwecheteyo achapandukira vanhu venyika munyangadzo yake yekuparadza iyo inosvika zvirokwazvo.

Saka nzwisisai kuti vachakambaira vachienda kumabunker avo kuti vazvivanze mumatombo enyika uye vazhinji vachaparara imomo. Uye saka ndinobvunza, ndekupi kunogona kuvanzwa munhu kuti apukunyuke kutarisa kwangu kusingaperi, kana ndiani angapunyuka kuona kwaYahweh nekuti zvese zvinoonekwa uye zvinozivikanwa uye chero chinoitika panyika chinobvumirwa kuitika kungave mukutonga kana kuruponeso rweavo vachadana zita rangu kusvika kumagumo kana kune avo vanondigamuchira saMesiya wavo, uye vose vanoita zvakaipa vacharipirwa zvizere.

Uye ndini uyo achaonekwa pakarepo mudenga mukubwinya kukuru uye vachaziva uye vazhinji vachanzwisisa kuti vakapotsa chiitiko ichi chimwe chete chavaive nechokwadi nacho kuti vaive chikamu, asi vanorambwa nekuda kwehupenyu hwavo hwezvivi nekuti chivi hachizombopinda kudenga kana kumira pamberi pangu.

Tarirai! Nguva dzasvika uye saka zvino ndichafamba kunounganidza vakatendeka.

Tarisai! Rindai! Teererai! Makagadzirira here vadikani vangu? Zuva rasvika pamusoro penyu.
