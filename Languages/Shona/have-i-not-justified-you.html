Mumabasa aJohane Mubhabhatidzi
Kubvumbi 01, 2024

Handina Kukureverera Here?

Nyarara uve nerugare, uzive kuti ndini Ishe akakudana kuti umire pamberi pangu wakarurama panzira dzako dzose. Handina kukureverera here? Handina kukuita wakarurama mandiri here, Ishe Jesu Kristu? Hausi kuzivikanwa neni here mumatambudziko angu nemukubwinya kwangu? Ndini Ishe wemadzishe naMambo wemadzimambo, uye handirevi nhema. Magwaro Matsvene haana kuzvizivisa here? Mira wakavimbika uye wakarurama, uve nerugare mumwoyo mako, uzive kuti ini, Ishe Jesu Kristu, ndakupa ruregerero rwakakwana nerugare pamberi pangu kana uchizoda kunzwa mashoko angu nekugamuchira kuraira kwangu.

Uchenjeri ndicho chinhu chikuru; naizvozvo, tsvaga uchenjeri — kwete uchenjeri hwenyika hunosvika pasina, asi uchenjeri hwaIshe. Uye zorora pasi peruoko rwangu rune simba. Nekuti kutya Ishe ndiko kubva pane zvakaipa nekufamba wakarurama pamberi pangu, anodaro Ishe ane simba, hongu Ishe Jesu Kristu. Inzwa izwi rangu! Inzwa izwi rangu uye usateerere rimwe! Mazwi mazhinji anopikisa Shoko rangu uye anokurevera nhema, sezvo achitsvaga kutonga pamusoro pemweya yenyu chaiyo kuti akuitirei chibharo kana mukavatevera. Musada nyika, vana vangu, kana zvinhu zviri munyika. Nyika ino yakaipa yazvino yava kusvika kumagumo, uye ini, Ishe, ndazvitaura, uye handizonyaradzwi nekusatenda kwenyu, uye handizonyaradzwi nekushaya kwenyu kutenda. Kwete, ndichashevedzera zvakanyanya kuitira kuti muzive chokwadi chemashoko andinotaura. Aenzanise neMagwaro Matsvene. Handizi ini here shoko rakaitwa nyama rinogara pakati penyu? Handina kukupai kuzodzwa nemunyaradzi here kusvikira ndauya?

Zivai, vana vangu, kuti ndakakutengai neropa rangu, uye nyika ndeyangu, uye munguva pfupi kururama kuchatonga panyika. Ndazvitaura. Ndakakupai upenyu mandiri; chete musaramba nzira dzangu. Ndakakupai chinangwa; chete musaramba upenyu hwandakakudanirai. Ndakakupai kudana, hongu, kudana kwepamusoro kusingaperi uye kusina magumo emazuva kana mukazoda kutendeukira kwandiri. Tendeukirai kwandiri nechiso nemwoyo zvakazaruka, uye mundibvumire kuti ndikuchenesei kubva muzvivi zvenyu nekusarurama kwenyu muropa rangu rinoyananisa. Vose vandinoda, ndinovaranga, kuti mugone kumira pamberi pangu makarurama mumachira machena akaisvonaka andichakupai munguva pfupi.

Nguo dzenyu dzose dzingori mamvemve ane tsvina amakazvisarudzira kuti muzvivande kwandiri. Asi hapana panogona kuvanda munhu kubva pakuona kwangu, uye ndinokuonai sezvamuri chaizvo, uye sezvandakakusikai patsva mandiri. Kune mubairo mukuru wakachengeterwa vana vangu vakatendeka; asi kune avo vanondiramba, ndichakurambai. Sei muchiri kubatirira kunyika ino iri kufa nematambudziko ayo nemarwadzo? Mune sarudzo mandiri, sarudzo isina kuwanikwa nemamwe marudzi chibayiro changu chekuyananisa chisati chaitika. Naizvozvo, ani naani anondiramba munguva dzekuzorodzwa ane chivi chikuru. Rudo rwangu kwamuri haruperi uye harugone kuputswa. Baba vangu neni tiri vamwe, uye zvinangwa zvedu nemagadzirirwo edu hazviparadzanisike. Baba vakandipa zvinhu zvose kuti ndiponese vamwe.

Musatora nzvimbo yenyu pakati pevasina kutenda, sezvo ndakakupai zvikonzero zvizhinji zvekutenda kukuru. Kutenda kwakaita setsanga yemastadhi ndizvo zvose zvamunoda, kuvimba kudiki diki. Zvipfekedzei nguo dzandakakupai. Handina kudurura mweya wangu pamusoro penyu here, imi vanhu vangu vanokahadzika? Musava vasina kutenda asi tendai chete, sezvo nguva nemwaka zvave pamusoro penyu uye hapana kudzokera shure. Nyika yamaimboziva haichazodzoki, uye nyika yamunoziva nhasi ndeyekunyengera kukuru. Kunyange zvinhu zvichiri kuita sezvamunoziva, zvose zvamaimbokoshesa zvakatorwa neakaipa; nekuti ane awa yakatarwa, awa yandisina kupfupisa, hapana nyama yaizosara iri mhenyu. Sei muchishuvira zuva raIshe riri kuuya panyika kuzoedza mwoyo yevanhu? Izuva rerima pasina munhu anogona kumira. Izuva rinotyisa rekutonga nemigumisiro, uye kunyange mumazuva erima guru, ani naani anodana zita raIshe kusvika kumagumo achaponeswa.

Nguva nemwaka zviri kuchinja pamberi pemaziso enyu, uye muchiri kusatenda maziso enyu. Kunyange ndichiita zviratidzo nezvishamiso mamuri nepakati penyu, muchiri kuvhara maziso enyu nekudzivira nzeve dzenyu mukufunga kusina kutenda. Nyasha dzangu netsitsi dzangu ndezvekusingaperi kubva kuchizvarwa nechizvarwa, asi nguva dziri kuchinja, uye kutonga nenjodzi zviri kusvika kunyika yamunoda zvikuru, uye zvinhu zvamunokoshesa zvichayerera nemumunwe yenyu sejecha. Munguva pfupi nyika haichazovi nekukokwa kwenyasha netsitsi, asi avo vachaponeswa vachafanira kutsungirira kusvika pakufema kwavo kwekupedzisira kuti vaponeswe mandiri. Tendeukirai kwandiri mugamuchire kukoka kwangu, nekuti kuva muna Kristu ndiko kuva nehukama hunoponesa neni, Ishe wemadzishe naMambo wemadzimambo. Ndiwe wega unogona kumisa ruponeso rwako nekundifuratira mukusatenda nekukana kudana kwangu. Funga nezvaEsau, uyo akatengesa udangwe hwake nekuda kwechimedu chechingwa chakarasirwa mukusanaya kwemvura, uyo akapa nhaka yake kumukoma wake mukusatenda kusina kutenda. Kuregeredza mashoko angu nekufamba murima chivi, saka musatadza mumire pamberi pangu.

Sei muchiri kuzviona sevasina kukodzera nyasha dzangu, asi zvose zvamunofanira kuita ndezvekugamuchira? Gamuchirai mabasa angu ekuyananisa muupenyu hwenyu uye mundibvumire kupinda kuti ndikubatsirei munguva yenyu yekushaiwa, dai chete mwoyo yenyu isina kutenda yaizondibvumira. Sarudzai upenyu, nekuti nhasi inguva yekusarudza; inzwai izwi rangu uye musaomesa mwoyo yenyu murenje sezvakaita madzibaba akawira parutivi. Musawira kure neni; musatevera kuparadzwa nenzira dzinokuvadza dzekusatenda kusina kutenda, asi tarisai kwandiri muvimbe neizwi rangu. Hongu, makwai angu anonzwa izwi rangu, saka nditeverei kunzvimbo yakachengeteka. Musafunga kuti mwoyo wekusatenda unogona kumira wakatsveneswa pamberi pangu, nekuti kusatenda ndiko kuramba kutenda kwandakakupai mandiri.

Nditsvenesei, Ishe Jesu Kristu, mumwoyo menyu uye musatya, nekuti kuonekwa kwangu kwava pedyo uye kwava pedyo zvikuru. Vimbai neizwi rangu mufambe mutsoka dzangu, nekuti nzira yandakakugadzirirai inotungamirira kuutsva hweupenyu pamberi paMwari chaipo. Nekuti ndini nzira chete inoenda kuna Mwari; ndini nzira, chokwadi, neupenyu. Kunyange akaipa achivimbisa zvinhu zvizhinji, haagoni kupa chinhu, uye haagoni kuzvinunura kubva paruoko rwangu. Kwete, hasha huru dzakachengeterwa iye, uye Satani ane nhaka iri kuuya yaakazvisarudzira mugungwa remoto iroro. Vose vanomusarudza, vose vanotora mucherechedzo wake, vose vanonamata chikara nemufananidzo wacho vachamutevera munhaka yake yehasha dzaMwari dzisingaperi.

Baba vangu havana kururama here mukudzivirira chisikwa chavo? Havana kururama here mukutonga kwavo nenzira dzavakaisa pamberi penyu? Naizvozvo, sarudzai upenyu musarudze nhaka yandakakupai, nekuti ndinovimbisa denga idzva nenyika itsva munogara kururama. Ndinokuvimbisai upenyu husingaperi, upenyu hwekuwanda kukuru, rugare, nemufaro. Asi, mukushaya kwenyu kutenda, muchiri kuramba. Ndakakuitirei kuti ndikugumburei zvekuti munoramba hukama neni, mwanakomana waMwari, uye naBaba vekubwinya? Kusarudza nyika ino nenzira dzayo ndiko kusarudza rufu, uye pakupedzisira sarudzo yemunhu wese ichava yekupedzisira, sezvo nguva dzekuparadzanisa dzasvika; uye avo vasingasarudze, sarudzo ichavaitirwa.

Hapana mufaro mukuparadzwa kuri kuuya kuchakurumidza kubata nyika yenyu. Njodzi dzinongoitika dzega dzichawanda, uye nyika ichadengenyeka, uye mafungu makuru acharova paBhabhironi Chakavanzika; nekuti nyika ichabvarurwa munzvimbo zhinji. Moto mukuru uchabata nyika, uye vatengesi vachamira kure nekutya kurwadziswa kwayo. Kuparadzwa kukuru kuri pamusoro pehure, Bhabhironi guru, iro rakadhakisa nyika neupombwe hwake nemhombwe dzake, nekuti rakanga riine vadikani vazhinji. Avo vasingazviparadzanise naro vachagoverana mukutongwa kwaro, saka sei muchiri kusarudza rufu pane upenyu hwandakakupai, hwakabhadharwa neropa rangu risina chivi? Hondo yava kuda kuuya zvizere panyika yenyu nerudzi rwenyu, uye kunyange zvazvo yatotanga — hongu, hondo isina kuziviswa yavanorwa nemi — munguva pfupi ichapararira nenyika yose sezvo zvisimbiso zvichisunungurwa.

Hondo huru nekutambudzwa kukuru zvichauya panyika sezvo vachaedza kuparadza chero chinhu chaMwari naKristu wake sezvo vachazopedzisira vazivisa pachena hondo tsvene pamusoro penyu. Vachabata vazhinji, uye vazhinji vachatorwa, uye avo vachaponeswa vachava nesarudzo imwe chete: kutsungirira kusvika kumagumo uye kusada upenyu hwavo kusvika kumagumo nekudana zita rangu kusvika kumagumo. Vatungamiriri venyu vakakutengesai uye vachabayira vazhinji kune akaipa muhondo dzichakurumidza kuitika. Rudzi rwenyu rucharakashwa kubva mukati nekubva kunze. Mauto matsvuku atove pano, uye akamirira mirairo yawo; nekuti vanoronga kurwisa kwechimbichimbi, uye vazhinji vachatorwa. Mauto eakaipa achatora zvose kubva munyika kuti zvive zvavo uye kubva kune avo vanondiramba. Vachaba nyika yenyu, dzimba dzenyu, vana venyu; uye upenyu huzhinji hucharasika mukusatenda. Hondo iri kuuya kubva kunze sezvo marudzi mazhinji akabatana achikurwisai; uye vane mvumo yevatungamiriri venyu, uye vatungamiriri venyu vanoshandira kufa kwenyu. Vanoda nyika yavo, nyika yakatsaurirwa kuna Satani kwavanogona kutevera zvizere zvakaipa zvinogara mavari, uye hapana chichadzivisa zvishuwo zvavo zvakaipa, sezvo mudzivisi achange aenda.

Vachapisa zvachose Bhabhironi Chakavanzika nemoto, uye ani naani asingakundwe nemirazvo achakundwa nenzara; ani naani asingakundwe nenzara achakundwa nedenda; ani naani asingakundwe nemuromo webakatwa achaendeswa kunosungirirwa nekuda kwezita rangu, uye vazhinji vachagurwa misoro sezvinotaura Shoko rangu Dzvene. Uye avo vanofira zita rangu vachava nemubairo mukuru, uye ndichapukuta misodzi yose pamaziso avo.

Zivai, vana vangu, kuti zvose zvinonaka nemifaro yechivi izvo vanhu vakaipa vanozvichengetera zvichangonyangarika mudenga, uye kutambudzika nekusuwa zvichamira panzvimbo pazvo. Rima richafukidza nyika, uye kuchava nekushaikwa kweShoko rangu, uye izwi remwenga neizwi rechikomba hazvichazonzwiki mariri.

Kunyange zvakadaro, ndichatuma vaporofita vangu pakati penyu, sezvo ndichaisa chisimbiso nhamba kubva kumarudzi asara kuti vashumire avo, uye vachaita zvishamiso zvikuru. Zviratidzo zvizhinji nezvishamiso zvichapiwa panguva idzi dzerima, uye avo vanodana zita raIshe kusvika kumagumo vachaponeswa. Shongwe dzenyu dzichawa. Mabhiriji enyu achakoromoka. Nzara ichauya panyika, uye kuchava nekusateerera mutemo kwakakwana, uye vachavhima avo vanondimiririra, uye vazhinji vachafira kutenda; asi mubairo mukuru wakagadzirirwa avo vanomira kusvika kumagumo. Sei muchishuvira zuva raIshe? Nekuti raizoparadza nyama yose kubva panyika dai ndisina kupfupisa mazuva acho. Ipapo nyika ichandiona ndichibuda kubva kumatenga nemauto ekudenga, nekuti nyika ndeyaIshe nekuzara kwayo.

Hongu, ndinokuudzai zvinhu izvi zvisati zvaitika, kwete kuti mumirepo mukusatenda, asi kuti muve nekutenda kwakaita setsanga yemastadhi, nekuti kuchakura kupfuura mimwe miti uye kuchava kusingazununguswi mandiri kana mukandibvumira kuchenesa zvivi zvenyu uye kana mukangouya kwandiri kuzoporeswa nekuzorodzwa. Kurira kwehwamanda kwava pedyo zvikuru uye kwatova pamusoro penyu, uye ndichabvuta mwenga wangu kubva pakati. Ndichiri kugadzira nzvimbo patafura yangu kune ani naani achandiita Ishe nekupindura pamikova yavo; nekuti tarirai, ndimire ndichigogodza!

Ndini Mwanakomana waDhavhidhi uye ndichatonga nyika kubva pachigaro chake cheushe.

Ndini mudzi waJese; inzwai izwi rangu!

Ndini Mwanakomana woMunhu; musatya mutarise kwandiri.

Ndini Mwanakomana waMwari, hongu, akaberekwa mumwe oga naBaba.

Ndini mutongi akarurama anotonga muutsvo nekutonga kwakarurama.

Ndini gwayana raMwari rakasarudzwa kubva muboka, risina gwapa uye risina chaanopomerwa.

Ndini musuwo wemakwai, uye hapana mapere anoparadza anogona kupinda.

Ndini chingwa cheupenyu; ani naani anodya icho haachazovi nenzara.

Ndini gwayana raMwari rakakodzera kuzarura zvisimbiso zvinosunga bhuku rakakungurwa.
