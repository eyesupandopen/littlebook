Nzira Yekugamuchira Chiporofita: Mandigamuchira Mashoko Echiporofita

Musi wa 2 Kurume, 2024, Ishe Jesu Kristu vakatanga kundipa mashoko echiporofita ekuti ndigovane nevanhu maererano nekuuya kwavo kuri pedyo kuzotora Kereke yavo pamwe nedzimwe nyaya. Zviporofita izvi zvaiuya kwandiri mune izvo zvinorondedzerwa neBhaibheri sekuti "ruoko rwaShe" rwugere pamunhu. Kwandiri, kunzwa kuremerwa kukuru mumweya mangu, kuremerwa kusingabvi kusvikira ndapedza shoko racho. Kristu vanoisa mashoko mashoma kana mitsara mupfungwa dzangu isingabvi uye yandisingakanganwi, uye kazhinji ndinozvitumira mashoko acho parunhare rwangu kuti ndirangarire. Pandinogara pasi ndotanga kunyora, Ishe vanondiratidza Mashoko acho mumweya mangu pandinenge ndichinyora: Hongu, dzimwe nguva ndinoona mashoko acho nemaziso chaiwo, uye kazhinji ndinonzwa mashoko acho pandinenge ndichinyora, kwete nenzeve asi mumweya mangu, uye anonzwikwa nenzira isingarambiki.

Pandinokanganisa ndonyora pfungwa dzangu, mashoko anomira kusvikira ndadzima zvandawedzera. Pandinozvidavirira ndodzima mutsara waida kuchengetwa naKristu, mashoko anomira kusvikira ndadzosera mutsara wacho sezvawaiva, uye mutsara iwoyo haubvi mupfungwa dzangu kusvikira ndazviita. Munguva pfupi yapfuura, Ishe vave vachindipa mashoko akadzama edzidziso, uye vave vachindiendesa kumagwaro chaiwo. Vanoisa mutsara mumweya mangu ndoukunyora; ndobva ndatsvaga mutsara iwoyo muBhaibheri rangu, ndowana kuti mutsara wacho uri muchikamu chemagwaro. Panguva iyoyo vanondiratidza magwaro andinofanira kutora, ndoanamatidza munhoroondo yacho, uye mashoko anobva angoenderera mberi pakarepo. Ndichiri kushamiswa kuti Mashoko andinopiwa naShe anowirirana zvakakwana nezvikamu zvemagwaro zvavanondipa kuti nditore, uye gwaro randinotora rinoyerera nenhoroondo yacho kazhinji nenzira yandisingakwanisi kuita ndega. Handina kumbobvira ndaita chinhu chakadai muupenyu hwangu, kusvika zvino. Kazhinji handizivi kuzara kweshoko racho kusvikira ndadzokera shure ndoongorora zviperengo nezviratidzo. Panguva iyoyo ndinoriverenga zvakare, uye Ishe vanoita kuti ndiite shanduko diki, pamwe kuwedzera mutsara wekujekesa, pamwe kudzima shoko ndoritsiva nerimwe, zvichingodaro. Kuremerwa kwacho kunobva kwaenda ndoziva kuti ndapedza.

Ndapota cherechedzai kuti mumashoko ose aya echiporofita, handina kumbobvira ndachinja chero chinhu chine chekuita nemusoro wenyaya, kana kamwe chete; uye hapana kumbobvira pave neshanduko huru pane zvirimo, kana kamwe chete. Zviporofita zvirefu zvinowanzotora nguva inosvika awa imwe kana pasi payo kunyora, uye basa rekunatsiridza rinogona kuwedzera imwe awa kana maviri. Ndinoongorora zvese nemagwaro kuti ndive nechokwadi chekuti handina kukanganisa chero chinhu mukunzwisisa kwangu kwevanhu. Kusvika parizvino handisati, asi kuti zvijeke: Mazhinji eMashoko aya anopfuura kunzwisisa kwangu pandinoanyora, uye ndinofanira kuenda kumagwaro kuti ndidzidze zvakawanda zvichienderana nezvandagamuchira. Mashoko acho haana kumbobvira apikisa magwaro, kunyange zvazvo achipokana nekunzwisisa kwangu magwaro, uye neniwo ndiri mudzidzi. Zvino, ndiri mushumiri uye ndava nemakore anoda kusvika makumi matatu ndiri mumagwaro uye ndakaita mharidzo dzakawanda, dzidziso, nezvimwe zvakadaro. Regai nditaure izvi kubva mumakore angu eruzivo: Hazvigoneki kwandiri kuti ndinyore chero chinhu chedanho rino uye zvakadzama munguva pfupi yakadaro nekuedza kwangu uye nekunzwisisa kwangu. Zvinonditorera mazuva akati wandei, dzimwe nguva vhiki kana kupfuura, kuti ndigadzirire mharidzo refu, yakadzama, uye izvi hazvina kufanana, kana napadiki pose. Izvi hazvibvi kwandiri uye zvinopfuura kunzwisisa kwangu. Mweya wechiporofita wakawira pamusoro pangu, nekuti Kupupura kwaJesu ndihwo mweya wechiporofita.
