Inzwai Mashoko aShe: Chakavanzika Chikuru Chaziviswa!

Yeshua HaMashiach anoti kuMwenga wake: Teererai munzwe mashoko angu, imi vakasarudzwa vangu, uye munzwisise kutaura kwangu, nekuti chakavanzika cheHumwari chikuru! Vakawanda venyu havana ruzivo rwechakavanzika chikuru chaMwari, asi ndicho chiri pamwoyo chaipo uye panheyo dzevateveri veNzira, zvese padzidziso nepamaitiro: Ndicho chinhu chikuru chechiKristu chandakakusikai nacho mandiri, uye muri chisikwa chitsva muna Kristu Jesu, nekuti ini, Jesu Kristu, ndakakuitai saizvozvo.

Kune vakawanda venyu, chakavanzika chikuru chaMwari chakarasika nekufamba kwenguva netsika uye neunyanzvi hwekunyengera kwavanorindira kunyengedza. Musatya, vana vangu, uye inzwai mashoko angu nekutaura kwangu, nekuti ndichakutsanangurirai pano mumaminetsi mashoma ekupedzisira enguva ino. Nzwisisai, vana vangu, kuti kuvhiringidzika kwakawanda nenhema zhinji zvakapinzwa mukereke yangu nemapere anoparadza nedzidziso dzawo dzenhema. Vakakamura Shoko rangu (neMuviri wangu) zvisirizvo kuburikidza nedzidziso yenguva dzakasiyana (dispensationalism), uye uhwu inhema huru dzakaunzwa neavo vakavaka dzimba dzavo pajecha, kwete pandiri, ibwe guru repakona, nekuti kunze kwangu hakuna munhu anogona kuvaka.

Jecha ravanosarudza kuita ibwe repakona rezvivakwa zvavo maBhaibheri akaora anotungamirira kubatana kwezvitendero nezvinangwa zviri pamwoyo wemunhu wechivi, zvinangwa zvekudzora, rufu, nekuparadza. Jecha ravakashandisa kuvaka rine zvinyorwa zvakaora, zvinyorwa zvechiGiriki zvakabva kuAlexandria. Teverai zvinyorwa zvinobva kumutsara weAntioki, weByzantine, uye izvi zvinotungamirira kuTextus Receptus, muvengi mukuru weRoma. Hakuna nguva dzakasiyana (dispensations), uye Mwari haana kukamurwa, kunyange ndichizotsanangura nezvenguva idzi sezvadzinowanikwa mutsamba dzangu. Mwari akabata nevanhu achishandisa nguva; dzidzai kuti muzive. Saka nzwisisai, kupatsanurana uku hakubvi kwandiri asi kwakarongwa kuti kuodze Kereke yangu kubva pakutanga.

Chakavanzika chikuru chakavanzirwa imi, vana vangu, asi hachina kufukidzwa, nekuti ndakachizivisa kuna Muapostora Pauro makore mazhinji apfuura. Chakavanzwa kuburikidza nenhema, kunyengera, dzidziso dzisiridzo dzisiri dzangu; asi chakavanzwa pachena, uye mbavha inoshuva kuti dai musingazvioni, asi ndicho chinhu chikuru chiri pachena. Nekuti chinonyanya kutyisa Satani pamusoro peKereke yangu ndechekuti anoziva kuti haana simba rekukudzorerai kana muchifamba musimba rangu rizere uye muhukama hwakajeka neni. Naizvozvo, sezvo asingakwanisi kutyora chisimbiso chaMwari chandakaisa pamuri, uye pasina anogona kukubvutai mumaoko angu kana mumaoko aBaba vangu, anoda kukukundai nehurombo. Kwete hurombo hwezvinhu; Kereke yangu yaisafanira kukurudzira vhangeri renhema rebudiriro nekuti haribvi kwandiri, kunyange vakawanda venyu vachitenda nhema idzodzo. Satani anokushuvirai hurombo hwemweya kuitira kuti avanze kubva kwamuri kuzivikanwa kwenyu kwechokwadi uye akunyengererei kuti mutende kuti muri vanhu vasina simba, vanogara vachitendeutsa rimwe dama nekutiza sembwende, uko kuri kushandisa zvisirizvo mirairo yangu kudzora vanakomana vangu kuti vasafambe musimba ravo rechokwadi. Kwete, handina kukutumai kuti mutendeutse rimwe dama kuitira kuti muve vakabatwa zvisina kunaka uye mukundwe nezvakaipa; ndakakutumai kuti mukunde zvakaipa nezvakanaka uye kuti muparadze mabasa aDhiabhorosi, nekuti ndizvo zvandakatumirwa, uye ndizvo zvandakakutumai. Asi nzwisisai: kutsiva ndekwangu, saka musazvitsivira, asi tendeutsai rimwe dama pazvinenge zvakakodzera kuitira ruponeso rwemumwe uye kupa nzvimbo yekutsamwa, nekuti mweya mizhinji yakaponeswa nenzira iyi.

Nzwisisai: vakakunyengerai nenhema dzavo, uye vakaisa nhema idzi dzinopesana nechokwadi changu pedyo chaipo nezvinyorwa zvitsvene. Vakazviita mumashoko okuwedzera emaBhaibheri ekudzidza; vakazviita vachishandisa tsananguro dzisiridzo; vakaita izvi nehunyengeri vachishandura zvisizvo tsananguro dzechiGiriki dzinobva mutsika dzekare dzechihedheni kwete muchokwadi cheShoko Dzvene raMwari. Mungashandisa zvinyorwa izvi dzimwe nguva, asi ivai vakangwarira zvikuru uye regai kurega munhu wenyama achiedza kukutsanangurirai zvinhu zvemweya; nekuti munhu wenyama haagoni kunzwisisa zvinhu zvemweya, nekuti kwaari uhu upenzi. Naizvozvo, zivai kuti vazhinji vevanyengeri ava vanouraya magwaro havana kutenda muShoko raMwari uye havana kukodzera basa ravakazvipa nekuti havana mweya waKristu uye havasi vangu; nekuti dai vaiva vangu, ndingadai ndakavatungamirira muchokwadi. Ndini iye akakuisai chisimbiso cherudzikinuro ruzere musimba remweya wangu mutsvene.

Ishe Jesu Kristu anoti kuMwenga wake anodikamwa: Ndinoda kuti munzwisise chinangwa chenyu chechokwadi, uye kana musinganzwisisi chakavanzika chikuru maererano nemi, hamuzofambi mukuzara kwesimba randakakudanirai kuti mufambe mariri, asi muchaderedzwa mukuedza kwenyu sezvakaita vazhinji venyu. Zvinondirwadza uye zvinosuwisa mwoyo wangu kuona kuti vana vangu havanzwisisi kuzivikanwa kwavo kwechokwadi uye havana chido chekutsvaga. Munofanira kugara muchitsvaga mashoko angu nekushingaira kwese; asi dzidziso dzenhema dzinoita kuti vazhinji vagutsikane, nekuti hamuna zvinovaka muviri mazviri. Nekuti inhema dzaSatani, asi vazhinji vanorarama nezvekudya zvinokurumidza kugadzirwa nezvinwiwa zvine shuga, uye vazhinji vanoshaya simba rekudya chikafu chandakapa nekunwa chinwiwa chandakapa, nekuti mukuita izvozvo hamuzombovi nenzara kana nyota zvakare.

Saka inzwai mashoko angu, Mwenga wangu akagutsikana; yave nguva yekumukira kudanwa kwenyu kwechokwadi uye kuita mabasa andakakugadzirirai, uye zivai kuti ndichaita zvishamiso zvizhinji nemaoko enyu pamunenge muchindifambira mukutenda neruzivo rwechokwadi. Nekuti ndakati chii muhushumiri hwangu kuna Israeri panguva yandakafamba pachena muJudhiya? Nekuti chii chinotaurwa naRuka panyaya iyi: Akati kwavari, Imi makapiwa kuziva chakavanzika cheushe hwaMwari: asi kune avo vari kunze, zvinhu zvese izvi zvinoitwa nemifananidzo. Saka nzwisisai, vaapostora vangu vakasarudzwa kuti vagamuchire chakavanzika chikuru chaMwari chandakavazivisa. Vakachinyora pasi, uye ndakachengetedza zvinyorwa kuti muzive chokwadi chenyaya yacho.

Vakawandisa venyu vanoteerera vechiJudha vanoda kukudzosai pasi peTorah. Zvinonakidza kuziva kuti dai pasina chakavanzika chikuru chaMwari, vazhinji venyu vaisaziva nezveTorah; nekuti haina kupiwa kumarudzi evaHedheni asi kuna Israeri wekare kuti vachengete, kuti ive mudzidzisi wavo anovaunza kwandiri. Nekuti ndini kuzadzikiswa kwemurairo, uye avo vanonamatira nemwoyo wose kumurairo waMozisi vanotsvaga kukudza nyama yavo nekufamba mukururama kwavo, uye vanoshaya kunzwisisa kuti Torah inochengetwa zvizere uye nemazvo nekuzvizivisa kwenyu neni. Nekuti ndini ndoga ndakakwanisa kuchengeta murairo, uye ini, Jesu weNazareta ndakazadzisa zvinodiwa zvekururama kwemurairo. Ndakasimbisa zvandakaita neropa rangu, uye ndakakusukai nemvura inoyerera yemweya wangu sezvo makabhabhatidzwa mandiri.

Nekuti ndakaita kuti Pauro audze vaKorinde chii? Nekuti neMweya mumwe chete isu tose takabhabhatidzwa mumuviri mumwe, tingava vaJudha kana vaHedheni, tingava varanda kana vakasununguka; uye tose takanzi tinwe Mweya mumwe chete. Kana mukaedza kuchengeta murairo, muchakundikana uye muchapotsa kudanwa kwenyu, sezvo ndakapfupisa murairo mumirairo miviri mikuru: Uye unofanira kuda Ishe Mwari wako nemwoyo wako wose, nemweya wako wose, nepfungwa dzako dzose, nesimba rako rose: uyu ndiwo murairo wekutanga. Uye wechipiri wakafanana nawo, unoti, Unofanira kuda muvakidzani wako sezvaunozvida iwe. Zivai, vana vangu, kuti hakuna mirairo mikuru kupfuura iyi, nekuti mune iyi mirairo inochengetwa.

Nzwisisai: ndinotsanangura zvese izvi mutsamba dzandakatumira kumakereke angu munguva dzakapfuura. Saka nzwisisai kuti ndakazivisa chakavanzika chikuru chaMwari, uye vaprofita vekare vakatsvaga nekubvunza nekushingaira pamusoro pechakavanzika ichi chavaiziva, asi hachina kupiwa kwavari kuti vazive. Ndinoshuva kuti dai mainzwisisa kuti kunyange kavha yemaBhaibheri enyu ichiti "Dzvene," haisi tsvene pachayo, nekuti ingori kavha yebhuku. Chinoita kuti Bhaibheri rive dzvene ndechekuti mabhuku avanosunga ane Magwaro Matsvene akatumwa nemweya kuvana veWekumusoro-soro, akatumwa naYahweh, Mwari mukuru wekusingaperi, nekuti hakuna akafanana naye. Saka inzwai mashoko angu matsvene nemweya yenyu yose! Nekuti Petro akati chii kwamuri, vana vangu? Ruponeso urwo Vaprofita vakabvunza, uye vakatsvaga nekushingaira, avo vakaprofita nezvenyasha dzaifanira kuuya kwamuri, vachitsvaga kuti, kana kuti nguva yakaita sei iyo Mweya waKristu waiva mavari wairatidza, pawakapupura pamberi pematambudziko aKristu, nekubwinya kwaizotevera.

Vana vangu, muri kurarama musingazivi rubatso rwekubwinya kunofanira kutevera, uye nhaka yenyu izere yava kuda kuziviswa kwamuri mandiri, Kristu Jesu, nekuti munguva pfupi muchabvutwa uye mukudzwe zvizere. Saka chakavanzika ichi chii? Uye munochiwana kupi? Ndichakuudzai, nekuti ndini Ishe Jesu Kristu, anofudza boka, uye ndiri Chikomba kuMwenga wangu, uye muri kurarama chakavanzika chikuru chaMwari, kunyange vazhinji venyu vachifamba murima panyaya iyi. Musatya, ndichakupai kunzwisisa, uye kune vakachenjera nevanoninipiswa ndichapa uchenjeri. Asi kune avo pakati penyu vachazvidza mashoko angu, munoita izvozvo muchindipikisa; uye munogona kunge muri kudivi rangu kana kundipikisa, hapana pakati nepakati. Nekuti ndinokurairai mose kuti mudzidze kwandiri, inzwai mashoko angu uye musaramba izwi rangu, nekuti ndakakupai ramangwana nechinangwa mandiri. Ndini Mufudzi Akanaka: Mufudzi akanaka anopa upenyu hwake nokuda kwemakwai, uye makwai angu anonzwa izwi rangu, saka regai kutevera vashandi vanonyepedzera kuti ndini!

Tarirai! Mweya unoti: Kune zvakavanzika zvakati wandei mukati meChakavanzika Chikuru chaMwari! Ndichatanga neChakavanzika Chikuru, ndozotaura nezvezvimwe zvakavanzika zvirimo. Tisati taenderera mberi, bvisai mhute yedzidziso yenguva dzakasiyana pamagirovhosi enyu uye zarurai maziso enyu muone: Mavhangeri sezvaanozivikanwa, mabasa aMateo, Mako, Ruka, naJohani, akakosha kune vose. Mabhuku aya anoratidza kuzadzikiswa kwangu kwemurairo nemazvo, pasina chasara chisina kuitwa. Anondizivisa mukuuya kwangu kuna Israeri. Nekuteerera mavhangeri mana sezvaanozivikanwa, munodzidza kufamba sezvandakafamba, nekuti ndizvo zvamakarairwa. Zvisinei, haasi iwo oga mavhangeri; kwete, haasi iwo oga mashoko akanaka ane chekuita neni.

Nekuti ndakapa mashoko akanaka eChakavanzika Chikuru chaMwari kuna Muapostora Pauro mutsamba dzake kukereke, dzakatumwa kubva kwandiri kwaari kuti azivise kwamuri. Muchaseka pfungwa iyi here? Munofunga kuti ndinoparidza rimwe vhangeri uye kuti ndiri izwi renhema here? Makajaira kutevera manzwi evashandi zvekuti hamuchazivi izwi rangu here? Kune avo vangaseka, munofanira kuteerera izwi revaGaratia apo Pauro akataura achiti: Asi ndinokupupurirai, hama, kuti Vhangeri randakaparidza, harisi remunhu. Nekuti handina kurigamuchira kubva kumunhu, uye handina kuridzidziswa, asi nekuzarurirwa kwaJesu Kristu. Pauro akataura chokwadi chandakamupa nekuvimbika, asi vazhinji venyu vanoramba mashoko andakamupa, vachitoderedza mashoko acho nemashoko akaita sekuti: Ah, izvo zvakangonyorwa naPauro, kana kuti, idzo ingori tsamba dzaPauro; haasi mashoko aKristu nekuti haasi mutsvuku! Iwe benzi! Wakarasika; zarura maziso ako kuchiedza changu nechokwadi! Ndakapa Pauro vhangeri rechakavanzika. Makapusa zvekutenda kuti kune mabasa mana mapfupi chete ane Vhangeri rangu here?

Ndapota inzwai mashoko aKristu Jesu, vana vangu, nekuti ndichadzidzisa vanoda. Nekuti Chakavanzika cheHumwari chikuru uye vhangeri riri mariri, nekuti ndakazivisa mashoko akanaka echakavanzika. Asi handikusiyi neizwi remupupuri mumwe chete; shoko rega rega ngarisimbiswe pamuromo wezvapupu zviviri kana zvitatu. Nekuti tsamba yandakatumira kuvaRoma inoda kutaura nemi: Zvino kwaari iye ane simba rekukusimbisai maererano nevhangeri rangu, nekuparidzwa kwaJesu Kristu, maererano nekuzarurirwa kwechakavanzika, chakachengetwa chakavanzika kubva pakatanga nyika. Mashoko akanaka aPauro kuzarurirwa kwechakavanzika chakachengetwa chakavanzika kubva pakatanga nyika, sezvakangoita Mateo, Mako, Ruka, naJohani vari mashoko akanaka ekuuya kwangu kwekutanga kuna Israeri.

Ndini Ishe Akatendeka uye mashoko angu ndeechokwadi, saka nditeererei nenzeve dzenyu uye kunyanya nemwoyo yenyu, nekuti nemwoyo munhu anotenda kusvika pakururama. Ndinokukumbirai kuti muve nekutenda kwakaita setsanga yemastadhi, nekuti mbeu iyoyo ichakura zvikuru mamuri nekuchengetwa kwakakodzera. Nekuti Pauro akati chii kuvaKorinde: Asi tinotaura uchenjeri hwaMwari muchakavanzika, kunyange uchenjeri hwakavanzwa hwakarongwa naMwari nyika isati yavapo, kuti tive nekubwinya. Uhwo husina kuzivikanwa nemachinda enyika ino: nekuti dai vakaziva, vaisazombofa vakaroverera Ishe wekubwinya. Zvakanyorwa pachena, vana vangu, uye hazvidi tsananguro, asi ndichatsanangura zvishoma.

Munonzwisisa mashoko aya here, vana vangu? Munonzwisisa here kukosha kwenyu zvekuti Mwari akakutarirai kubva nyika isati yasikwa, uye sezvo ari wekusingaperi, nemiwo makadaro, nekuti manga muri pamberi pepfungwa dzake nguva isati yavapo, uye akakupai upenyu husingaperi mandiri, Kristu Jesu? Nguva dzenyu nehupenyu hwenyu zvakarongwa naYahweh, Mwari mukuru uye anoshamisa, uyo kupenya kwake kusina nyama ingaone. Dai Satani aiziva zvirongwa zvaBaba, angadai asina kumbondiroverera, nekuti mukuteerera kwangu kusvika parufu uye mukumuka kwangu makasimbiswa simba rakamukunda uye akaparadzwa zvachose. Angadai asina kumboda kereke ine simba inopiwa simba nemweya wangu uye yandinoita mabasa makuru nemaoko avo.

Izvozvo zvinoreva chimwe chinhu kwamuri here? Regai kuvhiringidzika kwepfungwa dzenyu nekuti nhume dzangu dzinokuudzai chimwe chinhu chamusingadi kunzwa, uye kundei kuzvikudza kwenyu, nekuti ndichatungamirira vanoninipiswa chete uye ndicharamba vanozvikudza. Inzwai mashoko angu uye ngaanyure mukatikati memweya yenyu; nekuti ndakakusikai mandiri, uye mune simba, uye munoshamisa; nekuti makanatswa muropa rangu, kunyange ropa regwayana. Nekuti ndini musoro weMuviri wangu unoita mabasa mazhinji makuru kuvanhu vangu vandichiri kudana, ingonamatirai kuna Ishe wenyu, Yeshua HaMashiach, nemwoyo mumwe uye kubatana kwechinangwa, nekuti ndini Ishe wemadzishe.

Chapupu chakatendeka, uye dangwe rakaberekwa kubva kuvakafa, rinoti kune vakatendeka navakasarudzwa vake! Kwaiva nekereke yekare muEfeso, uye kwavari kwakaziviswa kuzara kweChakavanzika Chikuru chaMwari mutsamba yakatumirwa naPauro kubva kwandiri, nekuti haana kutaura pamusoro pake. Ndichatsanangura zvishoma, asi zivai kuti ndinotarisira kuteerera kwenyu mukutenda kwandiri, Jesu weNazareta, uye kune uyo anopiwa zvizhinji, zvizhinji zvinotarisirwa. Ndisati ndaenderera mberi, ndinoda kuti mufunge zvinotevera kubva kuKorose, nekuti ndakatumirawo tsamba kuvaKorose: Nekuti makafa, uye upenyu hwenyu hwakavigwa naKristu muna Mwari. Kana Kristu, anova upenyu hwedu, achizoonekwa, imiwo muchazoonekwa pamwe naye mukubwinya.

Vana vangu, makafa pandakafa, uye munozvifira zuva nezuva nekutora hunhu hwemunhu wekare sehwakafa; nekuti makarovererwa pamwe neni, uye hapana imwe nzvimbo mumagwaro maungawana chionioni chehunhu hwenyu chaihwo, nekuti hapana chimwe chikamu chemavhangeri chinochizivisa kunze kwevhangeri reChakavanzika Chikuru chandakazivisa kuKereke yangu. Kunyangwe muchingoona zvisina kujeka segirazi rine rima, izvozvo zvichakurumidza kuchinja, uye muchaziva sezvamunozivikanwawo. Zvinyudzei muVhangeri reChakavanzika Chikuru chaMwari, nekuti kereke yangu yeRaodhikea yakarasa zvakavanzika zvangu, nekuti vazhinji venyu hamuwanzo verenga tsamba dzangu kuKereke nekuti vafudzi vakatsauka vakakutungamirirai kune imwe nzvimbo muchitsvaga mashoko matsvuku. Vana vangu, ndinoziva kuti munondida, saka ndidei maererano neruzivo, uye musafamba mukunyengedzwa.

Saka ndinokubvunzai, tsamba kuvaEfeso inotii? Nekuti tarirai, chakavanzika chikuru chirimo! Zvaakatizivisa chakavanzika chekuda kwake, maererano nekufadzwa kwake kwakanaka, kwaakanga aronga maari, Kuti mukurongeka kwekuzara kwenguva, aunganidze pamwechete zvinhu zvose muna Kristu, zvose zviri kudenga, nezviri panyika, maari. Ndakakuzivisai chakavanzika chekuda kwaYahweh, chaakaviga maari, nekuti haadi mupi wezano. Musafunga nezvetsanangudzo yenhema yamakadzidza kubva kuvaprofita venhema mumanotsi avo asina maturo emumativi maererano nenguva dzekurongeka, nekuti Satani anonyengedza achichinja pfungwa dzemagwaro kuti zvienderane nekuda kwake nezvirongwa zvake zvinoshaya basa.

Chakavanzika ichi chakapiwa Pauro, uye aiva mutariri akapiwa basa rekuchigovera kuKereke yangu, kunyange kwamuri kana mune nzeve dzekunzwa nemeso ekuona. Nekuti vhangeri reChakavanzika Chikuru rakapiwa Pauro, uye ndakamupa basa iri munzira inoenda kuDhamasiko. Mukuzara kwenguva, Baba vachaunganidza pamwechete zvinhu zvose zviri kudenga nezviri panyika mandiri, Kristu Jesu. Hakuchazovi nekupatsanurana, hakuchazovi nekuparadzaniswa pakati paMwari nevana vake, hakuchazovi neruvengo pakati pemuJudha nemuHedheni, nekuti mose makaberekwa patsva uye muri chisikwa chitsva mandiri, Kristu Jesu. Mashoko aya anoshamisa zvikuru kwamuri here? Mashoko angu anokugumburai here nekuti munotenda dudziro yevafudzi vakatsauka pamusoro pawo?

Dzidzai kwandiri nekuzvininipisa kuti mugone kunzwisisa. Zvakare, muna VaEfeso Pauro akanyora mashoko andakamupa: Nechikonzero ichi ini Pauro, musungwa waJesu Kristu nokuda kwenyu imi veMarudzi, Kana makanzwa nezvekurongeka kwenyasha dzaMwari, dzandakapiwa kwamuri: Kuti nekuzarurirwa akandizivisa chakavanzika. Zvakare, musakanganiswe nemashoko evaprofita venhema, sezvo kurongeka kwenyasha dzaMwari kwaiva kushumira kwake kuKereke, nekuti iye (nevamwe) vakasarudzwa sevatariri vezvakavanzika zvaMwari.

Uye zvakare: Izvo mune mamwe mazera zvisina kuziviswa kuvanakomana vevanhu, sezvazvinoziviswa kuvaApostora vake vatsvene neVaprofita neMweya, Kuti veMarudzi vave vadyi venhaka pamwechete, uye vemuviri mumwe, uye vatori vechikamu chechivimbiso chake muna Kristu, neVhangeri. Hongu, verengai nemazvo uye verengai zvakare: veMarudzi vanotenda muzita rangu dzvene, iro Jesu Kristu, vadyi venhaka pamwechete uye vemuviri mumwechete seavo veIsraeri vakandiita Ishe, nekuti hapana musiyano mandiri.

Uye zvakare: Kunyange chakavanzika chakanga chakavigwa kubva kumazera, uye kubva kumarudzi, asi zvino chaziviswa kuvatsvene vake, Kune avo Mwari aida kuzivisa kuti chii hupfumi hwekubwinya kwechakavanzika ichi pakati peveMarudzi, anova Kristu mamuri, tariro yekubwinya. Hongu, makaberekwa patsva nemweya wangu, uye mweya wangu unogara zvachose muvana vangu, nekuti ndiwo mweya wekuva vanakomana, rubatso rwenhaka yenyu, uye mweya iwoyo unosanganisira kugona kwose kwandakakupai, nekuti ndakapa zvipo kuvanhu. Makaitwa nomufananidzo wangu, uye makasikwa patsva mandiri; nekuti Baba vakakuberekai mandiri, nekuti ndiri mweya unopa upenyu.

Kristu Jesu akakwidzwa anoti: Vana vangu vadiki, nyaradzwanai zvikuru mandiri, nekuti ruponeso rwangu, kana rwangowanikwa nekuita kuti ndive Ishe wenyu mukutenda nekutendeuka, harugone kurasika kana kudzimwa; nekuti ndiani pakati penyu anogona kudzimura kusikwa kwehunhu hwenyu muvana venyu, kunyangwe vachigona kurasikirwa nenhaka kana mukavaramba. Ruponeso chipo chinopiwa naBaba vangu kuburikidza neni, Yeshua HaMashiach, uye zvipo nekudanwa kwaMwari hazvina kutendeuka. Zvino, ndichakupai zvimedu zvekutevera parwendo rwenyu muzvakavanzika zviri mukati meChakavanzika Chikuru chaMwari, saka dzidzisai kuti muzviratidze makatenderwa naMwari, mushandi weShoko, muchigovanisa shoko rechokwadi nemazvo, kuti murege kunyadziswa pamberi pangu pakuuya kwangu.

Kune chakavanzika muna VaRoma, tichifunga nezvebofu hweIsraeri muchikamu. Kune chakavanzika chekuunganidzwa kwemwenga wangu, icho vamwe vanoti kubvutwa, uye izvo zviri muna VaKorinde (nekumwewo); nekuti munguva pfupi, mukubwaira kweziso, hwamanda yekupedzisira icharira, uye kurira kwayo kuri pamusoro penyu. Zvino kune chakavanzika chemurume nemukadzi vachiva nyama imwe, uye ichi chakavanzika chikuru: asi ndinotaura pamusoro paKristu nekereke, uye munogona kuwana izvozvo muna VaEfeso. Nekuti ndichabvisa bofu kubva kuna Israeri kana vatendeutsa mwoyo yavo kwandiri. Nekuti ndichaunganidza Mwenga wangu kwandiri, nekuti ndiri Chikomba. Ndini uyo wamucharoorwa naye, kuti tigare tiri vamwe mandiri naBaba vangu, nekuti Mwari ndiye zvose mune zvose. Mwoyo wangu unozorora pamusoro penyu, vana vangu, uye munguva pfupi muchandiona chiso nechiso; uye musakanganise, ndiri kukudanirai kwandiri, mhandara dzangu dzakangwara, uye ndiri kuuya kuzokutorai munguva pfupi iri kutevera, uye ndinoshuvira kukuonai mose patichange tiri chiso nechiso, uye muchaziva, sezvamunozivikanwawo.

Ndini iye akakuisai chisimbiso cherudzikinuro ruzere musimba remweya wangu mutsvene. Ndini kuzadzikiswa kwemurairo.
