Zvinovava Zvinotapira
Gumiguru 29, 2024

Nguva Yasvika Yekuzadziswa: Hongu naAmeni

Kubva kuna Yeshua HaMashiach:

Uyo Mosesi akaraira Israeri kuti vateerere uye vatevedzere.

Rerekai nzeve dzenyu munzwe mashoko aYeshua HaMashiach munguva ino yekupedzisira, uye kotamisai mwoyo yenyu mukuteerera uye bvumidzai mwoyo yenyu kugamuchira kuraira kwangu. Nekuti mashoko angu upenyu kune avo vanoawana uye anounza utano nechipikirwa, kunyangwe munguva dzekutambudzika kukuru. Saka nzwisisai kuti kutambudzika kukuru kwasvika uye njodzi yava pedyo, uye nguva dzacho ndidzo dzino. Zvirokwazvo, zvirokwazvo zvechokwadi chisingaperi ndini, Yeshua HaMashiach, iyo Shumba yeRudzi rwaJudah inouya kuzotonga nekuita hondo, uye nzwisisai kuti hapana chakavanzika pamberi pangu, uye hapana chakavanzika pamberi paYahweh uye ndinoona kusaruramisira nekuuraya rudzi kunoitwa kuvanhu venyika nevapambi vanobva kuSinagogi raSatani uye ndichatsiva ndizvo zvinotaura Ishe Akarurama!

Nekuti zvirokwazvo, kwemakore mazhinji ejubhiri vakawira vakafambisira mberi chakavanzika chavo chekusarurama, kusvika munguva dzazvino apo zvirongwa zvavo zvagadzirira kuzadziswa zvizere. Asi nzwisisai kuti vanongobvumidzwa kuzadzisa zvinangwa zvaYahweh muzvirongwa zvake zvisingaperi uye zvechigarire zveruponeso rwerudzikinuro ruzere rwevanhu. Nekuti munhu wenyama akakotsiriswa mukunyengedzwa kukuru nekugutsikana kukuru.

Saka vakawira vanoshandisa vanhu kufambisira mberi kuparadzwa kwevanhu pachavo vachiti chiedza rima uye rima chiedza. Uye ndizvo zvakaitawo Sinagogi raSatani vakapamba nyika dzechipikirwa uye ndichavadzura zvizere. Nekuti Sinagogi raSatani rinotsvaga kutora zvipikirwa zvaYahweh zvakanyorwa nekusimbiswa kuMadzibaba ekare. Sinagogi raSatani rakazara nemasora akaipa ayo vakawira vakadyara pakati pegorosi rangu uye nguva dzekuadzura dzasvika uye ndizvo zvakanyorwa uye ndizvo zvichaitwa: Nekuti tarirai, zuva rinouya, richapisa sechoto; uye vose vanozvikudza, hongu, navose vanoita zvakaipa, vachava mashanga; uye zuva rinouya richavapisa, ndizvo zvinotaura Ishe wehondo, uye harizovasiyiri mudzi kana davi.

Asi, zvakaitwa nevakawira kuti dandemutande rakadzikira dandadzo raro pasi rose zvino, uye nekunyengedza vanoita hondo. Uye zvino yava nguva yekusimuka kwavo uye kutonga kwavo kupfupi kusina utsvene kwava pedyo. Nekuti Yahweh mukururamisira kwake achapa munhu wenyama chaizvo zvaanoshuvira neupenzi mukutsvaga kwake mafaro echivi. Saka rima nehuipi hunoshuviwa nemunhu wenyama, munhu wenyama achava nazvo zvino. Asi, kana Evhangeri yangu yakavanzika, yakavanzika kune avo vakarasika. Muna avo mwari wenyika ino akapofumadza pfungwa dzeavo vasingatendi, kuti chiedza cheEvhangeri inokudzwa yaKristu, unova mufananidzo waMwari, chirege kuvhenekera kwavari. Saka munhu wenyama mukusaziva kwake abatana nedandemutande mukunyengedza kwaro, nekuti nekunyengedza vanoita hondo.

Naizvozvo, El Shaddai, anovatumira kunyengedzwa kune simba kuti vatende nhema dzakagadzirwa nevakawira, kuti vose vagopiwa mhosva avo vasina kutenda chokwadi, asi vakafarira kusarurama. Asi mukutonga kwaYahweh netsitsi dzake dzisingaperi, mafaro achabviswa panyika uye zvinonaka izvo munhu wenyama anochiva zvikuru kuunganidza zvichabviswa panyika. Uye kunyange mafaro echivi achabviswa panyika, zvekuti vanhu vachapenga pavachatsvaga kugutsa kuchiva kwemwoyo yavo. Zvisinei, Yahweh haasarudze zviito izvi kuti ave nehutsinye kuzvisikwa zvake asi kuti arange vaiti vezvakaipa uye kumutsa avo, dai zvisina kudaro, vaizoramba kumuka kubva kuhope dzavo nekuti kuburikidza nemafaro echivi nemubairo wekusarurama vakakodzwa uye vakapofumadzwa. Panzvimbo pekutongwa kuzere uye kamwe kamwe, munhu wenyama anopiwa nyasha nekuti munhu wenyama anokundwa nevakawira.

Hongu, munyasha netsitsi dzaYahweh uye muchirongwa chake chisingaperi Yahweh anotsvaga kumutsa munhu wenyama kubva muchivi uye kudaidza munhu wenyama kukururama. Kune kudaidzwa uku vazhinji vakadavira nekuzvidira kuruponeso rwangu munguva dzangu dzenyasha kuburikidza neEvhangeri yeRuponeso. Munguva idzodzo, ruponeso rwaive chipo chemahara apo munhu aigona kundiita Ishe uye kuva nenguva yekushandira ruponeso rwake nekutya nekudedera sezvaanouya kwandiri zvizere kunyange kubuda zvizere muchivi. Uye ndizvo zvazviri kuti nguva dzangu dzenyasha dzasvika kumagumo, kunyangwe nyasha netsitsi zvinogara neni. Uye munguva pfupi zvichaitika kuti nguva dzekumirira hadzichazovipo uye kuti humambo hwenyika ino huchava humambo hwaIshe wenyu, naKristu wenyu, uye ndichatonga nekusingaperi-peri nevatendi munhaka yedu isingaperi yandichagovana zvizere nevakundi vangu.

Uye boka guru richauya kwandiri panguva yekutambudzika kwaJakobho kuti ndivaponese nekusingaperi. Uye vose vanodana zita raIshe kusvika kumagumo vachaponeswa, zvisinei nemutauro kana rudzi kwavanodana. Uye ndiri kufamba kunoponesa Israeri, uye ndiri kudaidza hama dzangu dzechokwadi dzakatsauswa nezvitendero zvenhema uye dzakadzvinyirirwa neSinagogi raSatani, uye vachawana ruponeso rusingaperi uye ndichaponesa Jakobho uyo ruponeso rwake ruchazadziswa nemoto wekunatsa. Asi nzwisisai kuti nguva dzehupenyu hurefu nebudiriro yezvinhu panyika dzasvika kumagumo kusvikira Humambo hwangu hwamiswa munguva pfupi, uye nyika yakasungwa nemaketani ichimirira kutongwa kwayo kwekupedzisira kuchadururwa mukutsamwa kukuru. Tarirai! Hasha dzeGwayana dzinotanga kuuya, uye matambudziko makuru achafukidza nyika semafashamo ekuparadza kwakakwana, uye vazhinji vachaparara.

Asi, matambudziko makuru nenhamo zvichasunungurwa kana zvisimbiso zvavhurwa uye hasha huru, nematenda mazhinji nezvirwere, nenzara, nekusanaya kwemvura, nehondo, nekusaruramisira, nerufu nezvimwe zvizhinji zvichauya zvizere panyika; asi boka guru guru richamuka kubva munzira dzavo dzekudziya. Uye vachava mukuvava kukuru nekushungurudzika kwemweya pavachaziva chokwadi chisingakanganisi chemagwaro uye kuti vakapotsa sei nemawoko mukana wavo weruponeso uye wekutiza hasha nekutongwa pakuunganidzwa kweKereke yangu yakatendeka kwandiri. Asi mukutambudzika kwavo kunovava nekushungurudzika ndichafemera kutsunga kusingakundiki mukati mavo uye ndichavakwevera kwandiri zvizere, kunyange vose vanouya kwandiri mukurwadziwa nekutambudzika uye kuburikidza nemiedzo yekufira kutenda. Uye mubairo mukuru vachava nawo, mubairo mukuru zvekuti matambudziko avo anouya haakodzeri kuenzaniswa nekubwinya kusingaperi kuchazoratidzwa zvizere mavari.

Saka zvakanyorwa uye zvichazadziswa kusvika pakupedzisira: Ava ndivo vakabuda mukutambudzika kukuru, uye vakasuka nguo dzavo, vakadziita chena muropa reGwayana. Naizvozvo, vari pamberi pechigaro cheushe chaMwari, uye vanomushumira masikati neusiku muTemberi yake: uye uyo anogara paChigaro cheUshe achagara pakati pavo. Havachazovi nenzara, kana nyota futi, uye zuva harizovapisi, kana kupisa kupi zvako. Nekuti Gwayana, riri pakati pechigaro cheushe, richavafudza, uye richavatungamirira kuzvitubu zvipenyu zvemvura: uye Mwari achapukuta misodzi yose pameso avo.

Saka dandemutande rakadzikira dandadzo raro pasi rose mukunyengedza kukuru, kubiridzira, nekumanikidza uye nenzira dzakawanda uturu hwedandemutande hunoshanda huipi hwaro kuburikidza nenzira dzaro dzekunyengedza nedzeuturu. Nekuti rakadzikira dandadzo rekunyengedza uye rakadzikira dandadzo rechikwereti zvekuti nyika yose zvino yakanyatsoiswa mukumbundirwa kwedandemutande uye yakaoma mitezo neuturu hwaro. Dandemutande rakafamba nekunyatsotsotsonya nekunyengedza kuburikidza nechakavanzika chekusarurama, uye nyika yose zvino yava mudandadzo raro. Uye mukunyengedza kukuru vanyepedzeri ava (vakagadzirwa nevakawira) vakapamba nyika semhashu. Nenzira imwecheteyo iyo dandemutande rinoputira mazai aro kusvikira boka revana varo rabuda uye richimhanya kunodzikira matandadzo aro parutivi parwo. Saka sinagogi raSatani rakatora nyika zvese nechisimba nehunyanzvi.

Uye nekufamba kwenguva uye kuburikidza nemanomano mazhinji nezvirongwa vakazviita vane simba, nekuti ndizvo zvavakapiwa naYahweh muzvirongwa zvake zvisingaperi zveruponeso kuti vazadzise. Nekuti munhu wenyama anopofumadzwa nenhema dzavo uye anovatevera asingazivi mukuparara kwake. Zvezvirongwa izvi zvandinotaura zvichaguma mukuparadzaniswa kwekupedzisira pakati pegorosi nemasora uye pakati pevakazvipira zvizere neavo vanoda zvivi zvavo uye vakasarudza nemoyo wose mirazvo murudo rwavo rwakashata nekuparadzwa kwavo, uye vachazvidemba nekusingaperi sarudzo yavo yavakasarudza kuti vazvibatanidze nezvinangwa zvedandemutande. Uye ndipo pachadururwa ndiro dzekutsamwa kwehasha dzaMwari zvizere.

Nzwisisai kuti dandemutande iSinagogi raSatani iro rava kugadzirira kutora nyika muhurongwa hwaro hwekupedzisira hwechikara uhwo kutonga kwacho ndichakuita magumo asingaperi uye kuparadzwa nekutongwa kwacho hakunonoki. Nzwisisai, vadikani vangu, kuti Sinagogi raSatani vose vanyepedzeri vanonyepedzera. Ivo masora eEdomu ayo madzitateguru avo ndakasangana nawo ndiri gwayana raMwari pandakazvipa nemoyo wose kubhadharira zvivi zverudzi rwevanhu. Saka ndakavafumura, uye ndakavaudza zvakananga kuzviso zvavo zvakaipa: Imi muri vababa venyu dhiabhorosi, uye kuchiva kwababa venyu muchaita: anga ari mhondi kubva pakutanga, uye haana kugara muchokwadi, nekuti hapana chokwadi maari. Kana achitaura nhema, anotaura zvake: nekuti murevi wenhema, uye baba vadzo.

Saka kubva pakupamba kwavo kwekutanga kwenyika yechipikirwa isiri yavo kuti vatore, vakatambudza, vakarwadzisa, uye vakaita kuuraya rudzi kwakajeka kuvagari vepamutemo venyika dzechipikirwa. Vanonyepedzera kuva vagari venhaka vepamutemo veruponeso, kunyange vachishandisa zvisizvo zvikamu zvemutemo waMosesi muchitendero chavo chenhema cheTalmud. Uye nenzira dzakadaro vakapofumadza vazhinji vanofunga kuti vanonamatira kuchido changu vachifunga kuti ndicharopafadza ani naani anoropafadza masora aya uye ndichatuka ani naani anovatuka. Saka vazhinji vevanhu vangu vanozvibatanidza vasingazivi neSinagogi raSatani nekubvumirana uye zvirokwazvo ndinokurairai kuti mutendeuke zvizere kubva kunhema dzavo dzamakasarudzira kunyengedzwa kwenyu.

Vana vangu vadiki nzwisisai kuti ini, Kristu Jesu, handina kumbonyora maBhaibheri ekudzidza asi aya anoshandiswa sezvishandiso zvenyengedzo nenhema dzinopiwa mumajini kududzira chokwadi. Uye vakawira vakashandura mashoko angu mumaBhaibheri enhema, naizvozvo, tarisai kumutsara wemagwaro eByzantine, kunyange Textus Receptus kwandakachengetedza Mashoko angu. Naizvozvo, uyai kwandiri vadikani vangu mudzidze kwandiri uye ndichakupinzai zvizere muchokwadi chekunyengedza kwavo kwazvino kana mukazvininipisa mwoyo wenyu kuinzwi rangu rinopiwa neavo vanotaura vachindimiririra.

Nzwisisai kuti ndini, Jesu weNazareta, akatarisana nerudzi rumwe cheterwo rwevanyepedzeri nekuda kwetsika dzavo dzemuromo dzavakakwidziridza pamusoro peMashoko akachena aYahweh akapiwa kuna Mosesi. Nekuti Mutemo waMosesi mutsvene, uye wakarurama, uye wakatwasuka nekusingaperi uye kana munhu upi zvake akatuka Mutemo waMosesi ipapo munhu iyeye achava akatukwa. Nekuti ini, Jesu weNazareta, ndakazadzisa Mutemo waMosesi pandakapa ropa rangu risina chivi paCalvary nokuda kwezvivi zvaAdhamu (nevana vake vose) uye Mutemo waMosesi unochengetwa zvizere nevadikani vangu vanofamba nekutenda mandiri, Kristu Jesu, Ishe wavo akakwidzwa uye asingaperi.

Saka nzwisisai kuti hazvigoneki kudarika Mutemo waMozisi kana munhu achifamba nekutenda sezvo ndichivatungamirira nemweya wangu wandakasika mukati mavo nokusingaperi-peri. Zvisinei, ani naani anofamba munyama anenge achiudarika nekuti Mozisi akauya kuzopa mhosva chivi chiri munyama kuburikidza nekupa mutemo iwoyo usingaperi wemufaro wakanyorwa nemunwe waYahweh pachake. Uye hakuna nyama yakarurama pamberi pangu, uye kunyange pamberi paMwari Asingaperi weKururama. Naizvozvo, reururai zvivi zvenyu nezvikanganiso zvenyu kwandiri, gwayana raMwari ndiri mupristi wenyu mukuru nokusingaperi-peri maererano neUrongwa hwaMerkizedeki, uye ndichakunatsai zvizere muropa rangu rerudzikinuro rakadeurirwa kunatswa kwevose vanouya kwandiri kuzoponeswa.

Ndiani akambonzwa chinhu chakadai? Ndiani akamboona zvinhu zvakadai? Nyika ingaberekwa nezuva rimwe here? Kana kuti rudzi rungazvarwa kamwe kamwe here? Asi ndizvo zvazvakaita kuti rudzi rwaIsraeri rwakavapo zvakare serudzi nekuzivisa kwemasora, uye aiva Mwari pachake akabvumira vavengi vake kunyepedzera mukusaziva kwavo mukunyengera kwavo kuti ndiJakobho kwete Esau, nekuti aiva Esau akatengesa kodzero yake yeudangwe kumunin'ina. Uye saka dandemutande mukusaziva nekuzvikudza kwaro rinofunga kuti richaunza nguva yendarama isingaperi panyika nekuti ndizvo zvido zvaMadzibaba avo zvavanoita uye vakawa ndivo Madzibaba avo, kunyange naSatani vane hunhu hwavanogovana.

Asi vakapinda munyika nechisimba chikuru nekuti ndizvo zvavakapiwa kuti vaite kuti vamutse avo vakarara, kunyange vose vari muupofu hwechikamu vachadaira kushevedzera kwaMesia wavo, iye Yeshua Mesia wechokwadi. Uye nzwisisai kuti ndinoona chiito chese chakaipa chavanoita, uye ndinoona kusaruramisira kwese kwavanoitira vasina mhosva, uye ndicharipira, anodaro Ishe Asingaperi uyo Yahweh aita Mesia, Mambo, Mupristi Mukuru, naIshe! Chiito chese chakaipa chakanyorwa zvakakwana kumatenga, uye vacharipirwa nokusingaperi uye kusvika pakupedzisira munhaka yavo isingaperi mumirazvo inopisa zvikuru yebhuruu rekobharuti murima guru nokusingaperi.

Uye ndinoona Rakeri achichemera vana vake sezvavanotambura nekuurayiwa kwerudzi nekutambudzwa kusina kururama neSinagogi raSatani uye ndicharipira, anodaro Ishe weMadzishe naMambo weMadzimambo uye ndichaponesa vakasara vaIsraeri vari vapenyu kuburikidza nevhiki rechimakumi manomwe raDhanieri, kunyange dambudziko raJakobho. Muhurongwa hwaYahweh husingaperi, anonzwisisa kuti vazhinji havazombobvi pazvivi vachipinda mukururama kubva pamufaro nekunyengera kwechivi. Saka panzvimbo pekuunza kutonga nekukurumidza, Yahweh akasarudza kuunza kutonga pamusoro pekutonga kuitira kuti zviitiko zviwande uye zvirambe zvichiipa. Uye kutonga kuchawedzera nenzira yakadaro vose avo vachazosarudza kudana kwandiri mukutambudzika kwavo nenjodzi ndivo vandichaponesa kusvika pakupedzisira, uye vachatungamirirwa kunzvimbo yakachengeteka.

Uye saka ndizvo zvazviri kuti zvinangwa zvavo zvichazoratidzwa. Nekuti ndichatungamirira 144,000 vangu kuti vaburukire paGomo reZioni, uye vachanditevera nhanho nenhanho sezvavanotungamirira vakasara vangu vaJakobho kunzvimbo yakachengeteka murenje apo nzvimbo yakagadzirirwa naMwari kuti vachengetedzwe, uye vachadyiswa nekuchengetwa uye shato, chikara, nemuporofita wenhema havazovabati, kana kuvawana. Asi muporofita wenhema achauyisa nyangadzi yokuparadza uye masora mazhinji achanatswa kubva munyika, uye ndichavanza vakasara vangu kubva kuhondo yezuva iro guru, kunyange zuva rekuuya kwangu kwechipiri iro zuva Yahweh akataridza kubva kusingaperi uye rakaiswa nokusingaperi muhurongwa hwake hweruponeso uye zuva iroro riri kuswedera zvechokwadi nekuti zvakanyorwa.

Uye ndakaona denga rakazaruka, uye tarira bhiza jena, uye uyo akanga akatasva pairi ainzi Akatendeka uye Wechokwadi, uye mukururama anotonga nekurwa hondo. Maziso ake akanga akaita somurazvo womoto, uye pamusoro wake paiva nekorona zhinji, uye akanga aine zita rakanyorwa, risingazivikanwi nomunhu asi iye oga. Uye akanga akafuka nguo yakanyikwa muropa, uye zita rake rinonzi, Shoko raMwari. Uye hondo dzaiva kudenga dzakamutevera dzakatasva mabhiza machena, dzakafuka mucheka wakanaka, muchena uye wakachena. Uye mumuromo make munobuda munondo unopinza, kuti nawo, arove marudzi: uye achavatonga netsvimbo youtare: uye anotsika chisviniro chewaini chehasha nokutsamwa kwaMwari Wemasimbaose. Uye ane zita rakanyorwa panguo yake, nepachidya chake, Mambo WeMadzimambo, NaIshe WeMadzishe.

Saka zvichaitika kuti ndichauya kuzotora nhaka yangu nechisimba kubva mumaoko evatoreri nevagari zvisiri pamutemo vane nguva yavo yasvika kumagumo asingaperi, nekuti humambo hwaSatani hahuzombomukizve nenzira yakafanana. Uye ndichanatsa nekuchenesa nyika kubva mukusarurama kwese, uye Satani achasungwa kwemakore churu mukurwadziswa nekutya kukuru kwenhaka yake isingaperi iri kusvika mugomba iroro rinopfuta resarufa rinonhuwa rinopfuta nemirazvo inopisa zvikuru yekurangwa.

Kunyange mirazvo iyo yakasikirwa kuripira chiito chese chakaipa chavakamboita kubvira panguva yavakasiwa vakasarudza kusarurama. Uye chivi chese chimwe nechimwe chevakawa chichapiwa zvizere mukuripirwa kumumwe nemumwe wavo, kunyange chivi chese chavakaita neutsinye hukuru vachipesana naYahweh pachake nezvisikwa zvake zvavakaodza. Uye saka ndizvo zvazviri sezvavanosungwa nekukandwa vari vapenyu mumirazvo inopisa zvikuru uye yakachena yekurangwa apo moto uchapfuta nebhuruu rinopisa zvikuru nemimvuri yeuchena hunopisa hwekunatswa. Uye mumirazvo iyoyo yekurangwa vacharipirwa zvizere zviito zvavo zvese zvisina maturo uye zvinosemesa izvo zvichapiwa zvizere muzvipfuva zvavo kakawanda-wanda nokusingaperi-peri.

Uye vacharipirwa zvizere mukutonga kwaYahweh kwakakwana uye kusingaperi kusinganonoki.

Uye saka kutonga kwangu kwemireniyamu kuchauya panyika zvizere pamagumo edambudziko raJakobho, uye kuchava kutonga kwekururama. Munguva iyi vose vakarurama vachaitwa vapenyu, uye mumwe nemumwe achange amutswa munguva yake uye muhurongwa hwekukohwa kwavo. Vakatendeka muna Kristu Jesu vari vapenyu neavo vakavata muna Kristu vachatorwa kuenda kumatenga mukubwaira kweziso, uye vachapiwa mubayiro mukuru uye vamwe vacharasikirwa, asi vose vachaitwa vapenyu zvizere uye nokusingaperi-peri.

Zvino pamagumo edambudziko raJakobho kuchaitika rumuko rwevakarurama apo ndichamutsira kuupenyu vatsvene vose venguva yekutambudzika vakafira zita rangu uye ndichamutsira kuupenyu husingaperi vose vakafa mukutenda pasi pezvibvumirano zvizhinji zvaYahweh kunyange vose vakasvika pakufema kwavo kwekupedzisira panyika kubvira kuna Adhamu kusvika pakuzvarwa kweKereke yangu pazuva rePendekosti.

Asi zvichaitika vadikani vangu saka nzwisisai zvandichaita: Ndichatora vana vaIsraeri kubva pakati pevahedheni, kwavakaenda, uye ndichavaunganidza kumativi ose, ndovaunza munyika yavo. Uye ndichavaita vanhu vamwe munyika, pamusoro pemakomo aIsraeri, uye mambo mumwe achava mambo kwavari vose: uye havazovi vanhu vaviri zvakare, kana kupatsanurwa zvakare kubvira zvino kuva humambo huviri. Uye tichanakidzwa nemafuta enyika uye mhumhi ichavata negwayana sezvo rugare runouya panyika yose sezvo nyika inodzoreredzwa mukururama kukubwinya kwayo kwakarongerwa. Uye mandiri, Yeshua HaMashiach, shumba ichasvika pakuzadziswa kuzere negwayana kunyange sezvo zvinangwa zvangu zvisingaperi zvinounzwa pamwechete apo Gwayana raMwari riri mukubatana kuzere neShumba yorudzi rwaJudha uye saka kururama kuchatonga nyika uye nyika ichapa kuzara kwayo uye hapana chinhu chakaipa chichapinda muumambo hwangu.

Uye saka zvichava kuti nyika ichagarwa vanhu patsva mushure mevhiki rechimakumi manomwe raDhanieri, nekuti dai nguva dzisina kupfupiswa hapana nyama yaizopona. Uye vakasara vaJakobho vachauya kwandiri vondigamuchira saMesia wavo uye ndichavagamuchira kwandiri nokusingaperi sezvakapikirwa nekufanotaurwa naYahweh. Uye saka nyika ichagarwa vanhu patsva mukururama uye kuzara kwehumambo hwaYahweh kuchadzoswa apo usingaperi achagara pakati pevanofa uye apo ngirozi dzichafamba zvakasununguka uye apo chidzitiro nekutuka kwemutemo zvinobviswa nokusingaperi-peri. Kuchava nemitambo mizhinji, uye maSabata mazhinji atichapemberera, uye marudzi achadururira muHumambo hwangu kuzokudza musiki wavo naIshe asingaperi naMwari Mukuru Asingaperi, nekuti nyika ichatongwa mukururama uye ichamira mukukudzwa nerudo kuna Yahweh akasika zvose nenzira dzekusika dzaakasarudzira chimwe nechimwe chezvisikwa zvake. Uye murudo nekukurama nyika ichatongwa.

Asi huori hwemunhu wechisikigo hucharamba huripo uye vamwe vachabva panzira dzaYahweh uye mafungu ekupanduka achatanga kuzviratidza zvakare. Uye Satani achaenda kunovaunganidza pamwechete, vane huwandu huchaita sejecha regungwa. Uye vachakomba upamhi hwenyika, uye moto uchaburuka kubva kuna Yahweh pachake kuzoparadza vose avo vaizosarudza zvakaipa zvakare uye vaizorwisa musasa wevatsvene.

Asi nzwisisai kuti zvakaipa hazvizokundi uye humambo hwaSatani hahuzovipo zvakare uye vose vanotevera nzira dzake dzakaipa zvakare vachazviwana vari mukutonga ikoko kukuru kwechigaro chichena. Kwavachatongwa zvizere uye vopihwa mutongo nokusingaperi-peri kugungwa iroro risingaperi rekurwadziswa uko Satani pachake achasungwa nokusingaperi okandwa mumirazvo muumambo hwake husingaperi hwesarufa rinopfuta rinonhuwa rinoungana nekuti ndizvo zvavakafanirwa. Havana mambo uye havasi vamwari nekuti havagoni kuzvinunura kubva muruoko rwangu rune simba uye ndichapwanya musoro waSatani zvizere nokusingaperi-peri uye vakundi vangu vachatsika-tsika chikamu chese chehumambo hwemuvengi sezvo hunoparadzwa zvachose.

Ndini Mambo weMadzimambo naIshe weMadzishe.

Ndini iye akatuma Vhangeri Risingaperi, ndarizivisa kumutumwa wangu nemuranda, uye kuburikidza nemutumwa wangu nemuranda mashoko eVhangeri Risingaperi atumwa kuti ateererwe nevose vachateerera sungano isingaperi yandakasimbisa muropa rangu, kunyange Testamende Itsva inosanganisira zvizere zvipikirwa zvese nezvibvumirano zvekare zvichazadziswa zvizere.

Ndini iye akatumwa naYahweh pachake kuti zvinhu zvese zvizadziswe uye nguva dzangu dzasvika, uye ndichaponesa Israeri zvizere ndovachenesa zvizere kubva kuSinagogi raSatani rane nguva dzekutongwa, njodzi, nekutambudzika dzasvika.

Ndini Yeshua HaMashiach uyo Baba vakandituma, vakandipa basa, vakandipa mvumo, uye wavakagovana naye chigaro chavo chehushe nesimba ravo!

Ndini iye anotuma Mashoko eVhangeri rangu Risingaperi kuti ateererwe nevose vanotumirwa Mashoko aya nekuti muMashoko aya mune upenyu nerugare kune vakadzikinurwa, uye upenyu husingaperi hunowanikwa imomo.

Uye Kutonga kwakarurama kwaYahweh naYeshua HaMashiach kwaziviswa uye izvo zvaziviswa naYahweh naYeshua HaMashiach hapana anogona kuzvishandura nekuti zviziviso izvi zvinotsigirwa nechigaro chehumambo chaYahweh pachake.

Kubva kuna Yahweh Mwari Mukuru Asingaperi!

Inzwai, inzwai imi vaIsraeri, inzwa izwi raYahweh, Mwari Mukuru Asingaperi weKururama uyo Madzibaba enyu aiziva uye wavakasiya pavakaramba mwanakomana wangu anodikanwa uyo akatumwa kuruoko rwavo uye kunyange ruponeso rwose rwaAdama, mwanakomana wangu akarasika, avo vakawanda sejecha regungwa. Inzwai izwi rangu uye munzwisise kuti NDINI iye asingaperi uye asingashanduki, uye NDINI iye uyo zvese zvipikirwa zvangu zvinogara zviri Hongu naAmeni nekuti hakuna mukuru kupfuura Mwari wenyu Asingaperi weKururama uyo wamunofanira kubata naye.

Saka nzwisisai, kuti kunyange Madzibaba enyu akaramba Mesia wavo wechokwadi wandakatumira kwavari apo Judea yaitongwa naPontio Pirato vachisarudza kuti avasunungurire Barabhasi. Asi, ndichiri kukudanai kuti mugamuchire mwanakomana wangu anodikanwa nekuti NDIRI kumutumira kwamuri zvakare kuti muponeswe zvizere uye zvino inguva dzekutambudzika kwaJakobho dzichazoguma naJakobho achisunungurwa kubva kuSinagogi raSatani iro kutongwa kwaro kusinganonoki.

Uye nzwisisai kuti ndinoona hutsinye hwese hunoitwa nemasora aya akaipa, uye vachabhadhara, uye ndichadzosera zvakakwana zvese zvavakatora nezvese zvavachazotora kubva kuvana vangu vasingaperi kakawanda. Asi ndakapa rusununguko rwekusarudza kuti vese vasarudze, uye nekuramba kwenyu Yeshua HaMashiach, kunyange Jesu Kristu, makazvitorera vamwari venhema uye vazhinji venyu makazvibatanidza neSinagogi raSatani. Naizvozvo, ndiri kuuya kwamuri panguva ino uye ndichikudanai neMashoko aya nekuti ndakatora Madzibaba enyu semuti mudiki uye ndikavarera nemudzidzisi wangu aida kuvaunza kuna Kristu, kunyange Mutemo waMosesi.

Uye ndakapinda muzvibvumirano zvakawanda nevanhu vangu vakasarudzwa; zvibvumirano zvavakakundikana kuchengeta nekuda kwechivi nehuori zviri muropa ravo. Asi, NDINI ndakatendeka kuMashoko angu uye NDINI iye ari kutumira mashoko aya kwamuri, kunyange nemabasa enhume yangu ine simba yandakasika uye ndikapa kuMwanakomana wangu, Jesu Kristu kuti amumutse nekuda kwezvinangwa zvangu zvisingaperi. Uye ndizvo zvakaita nyika zvekuti vazhinji vakasarudza huori nekubvumirana nevakawira pasi chivi chamunacho imi vaIsraeri vandakatsaura uye ndikasarudza kuti muve vanhu vakakosha kwandiri kana mukangopfugamira Yeshua HaMashiach nekuti hakuna mumwe Mesia wandichazombotuma.

Uye NDINI iye anoona, anonyora, uye anorangarira chiito chose chimwe nechimwe chakaitwa neavo vandakasika. Kunyanya vese vakandipandukira nenzira dzangu; avo vandichatsiva zvizere nekuda kwenzira dzavo dzakaipa nekuda kwefungidziro yese yakaipa, uye nekuda kwechinangwa chose chakaipa chemwoyo yavo inonyangadza, uye nekuda kwechiito chose chakaipa uye chinonyangadza zvisinei nekuti chidiki sei nekuti kururamisira kwangu nekutonga kwangu kwakakwana mukuzadzikiswa nekuitwa kwazvo. Nekuti avo vanosarudza kurarama zvisina kururama nekuda kwesarudzo yavo vachagara nhaka yekuparadzwa kusingaperi, nepo vese vanosarudza kurarama zvakarurama vachararama mumufaro usingaperi, kuzorora, nerugare uye vachakura semhuru dzedanga.

Naizvozvo, NDIRI kukurayirai imi vaIsraeri, vandinoda nerudo rusingaperi, kuti mubve padandemutande redandemutande uye ndichakusunungurai zvizere muupenyu husingaperi nemufaro. Ibvai paTalmud yenyu inomhura nekuti ini, Yahweh, Mwari Mukuru Asingaperi, handina kutumira bhuku iroro nekufemerwa kana kuzarurirwa uye handina kubvumidza shoko rimwe chete mubhuku iroro, uye chinhu chinonyangadza chakagadzirirwa kutsausa vanhu vangu vanodikanwa, kunyange chakagadzirwa neavo vakawira pasi vanonyangadza.

Uye saka ndinouya kwamuri neMashoko mamwe chete andakaita kuti Mosesi ape Madzibaba enyu pandakavasunungura kubva kuEgypt ndichivapinza munyika yechipikirwa yakabiwa nevaKanani vakaipa. Uye saka Mosesi akadanidzira zvikuru kuMadzibaba enyu: Ndinodana denga nenyika kuti zvipupurire nhasi pamusoro penyu, kuti ndaisa pamberi penyu upenyu nerufu, kuropafadza nekutuka. Naizvozvo, sarudzai upenyu, kuti imi nembeu yenyu murarame, nekuda Ishe Mwari wenyu, nekuteerera izwi rake, uye nekunamatira kwaari: nekuti ndiye upenyu hwenyu, nehurefu hwemazuva enyu: kuti mugare munyika iyo Ishe akapikira madzibaba enyu, Abrahamu, Isaka, naJakobho, kuti achavapa.

Zvakare, ndakaita kuti Mosesi azivise kuMadzibaba enyu zvokwadi zvisingaperi zvinotevera zvaakapiwa neni pamusoro peMadzibaba enyu, chiziviso chandiri kutumira nenhume yangu zvakare kuti vaIsraeri vateerere, uye Mashoko aya anotumirwa neni Mwari Asingaperi weIsraeri uyo pasina mumwe. Uye Mosesi akazivisa kuti ndichavamutsira Muporofita kubva pakati pehama dzavo akafanana newe, uye ndichaisa mashoko angu mumuromo make, uye achataura kwavari zvese zvandichamurayira. Uye ani nani asingateereri mashoko angu, aachataura muZita rangu, ndichazvibvunza kwaari. Nzwisisai imi vaIsraeri kuti Yeshua HaMashiach, wamunoramba ndiye wandakatumira kuruoko rwenyu uye kumuramba ndiko kuramba Mwari Asingaperi weKururama, kunyange Yahweh pachake.

Naizvozvo, nzwisisai kukomba kwenguva yamunorarama imi vaIsraeri. Kune rumwe rutivi vavengi venyu vakatora nyika dzenyu, kunyange vazukuru vaEsau uyo akatengesa udangwe hwake kuna Jakobho, uye vanotsvaga kuparadzwa kwenyu kwakazara, kunyange vazhinji venyu makabatana navo mukusaziva mukupanduka kwenyu kwamakadzidza kubva kuMadzibaba enyu. Asi, vachakupikirai zvinhu zvakawanda uye zvipikirwa zvakawanda zvichaitwa nemuporofita wavo wenhema naMoshiach wenhema nekuti achataura seshato uye zvipikirwa zvake zvinongounza kuparadzwa, saka musatora chiratidzo chavo kana kuti muchabatana navo munhaka yavo isingaperi mugungwa remoto risingadzimi umo honye yavo isingafi.

Kune rumwe rutivi mune upenyu husingaperi nerugare zvinopiwa neruoko rwangu, kunyange ruoko rwerudo rusingaperi rwaYahweh pachake pachiso chemwanakomana wangu asingaperi uye anodikanwa Yeshua HaMashiach, kunyange Jesu weNazareta chigumbuso changu icho vese vasina kutenda vachagumburwa nacho. Naizvozvo, imi vaIsraeri musaomesa mwoyo yenyu pakunzwa nekuona mashoko angu uye ngaapinde zvakadzama mumwoyo yenyu nekudzika kwehunhu hwenyu uye musaita chikanganiso chakafanana chakaitwa neMadzibaba enyu icho vachazvidavirira. Musaramba chipo changu cheupenyu husingaperi chandiri kukupai iye zvino ini pachangu zvakare. Uyai zvizere kuna Yeshua HaMashiach mukuteerera kwakazara uye mirirai Ishe wenyu asingaperi. Naizvozvo, vadikani vangu, ndinokukumbirisai kuti mutendeukire kuMesia wenyu wechokwadi nekuti hapachina chikonzero chechibayiro nekuti Jesu weNazareta ndiyewo Gwayana raMwari riri kuuya seShumba yeRudzi rwaJuda uye kutonga kwakawanda kukuru uye kwakarurama ndakaisa mumaoko ake kuti aite.

Uye saka nzwisisai, kuti ini, Yahweh, Mwari Mukuru Asingaperi ndini ndiri kudana vaIsraeri kuti vatendeuke kuti vagamuchire Mesia wavo wechokwadi uye kuti ndigokununurai neruoko rwake rune simba. Inzwai Mashoko angu imi vaIsraeri uye sarudzai upenyu kuti imi nembeu yenyu shure kwenyu mubudirire. Uye saka ndinokurayirai zvakare murudo rwangu kwamuri vana vangu: Tsvodai Mwanakomana kuti arege kutsamwa, uye muparare munzira, kana hasha dzake dzangobatidzwa zvishoma.
