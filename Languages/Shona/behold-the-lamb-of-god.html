Chapupu Chipiri: Israeri
Zvinovava Zvinotapira
Mbudzi 16, 2024

Tarirai! Gwayana raMwari!

Inzwai Mashoko aYeshua HaMashiach uye rerekai nzeve dzenyu kunzwi reMashoko angu, mudzingise mwoyo yenyu kuti mugamuchire Messiasi wenyu wechokwadi, imi Israeri, uye munzwisise kutaura kwangu kuti muve vakachenjera panzira dzenyu, uye kuti mundibvumire kutungamirira tsoka dzenyu patsva nekushandura nzira yenyu kubva munzira hombe yekuparadzwa yamunotevera, kuenda kunzira nhete yeruponeso inova nzira dzangu uye ndini iye. Nokuti ndini iye akatumwa naYahweh kuti ndikuponesei, uye ndini mbeu yakapikirwa yemukadzi ichapwanya musoro wenyoka, uye ndiri kukudanirai kwandiri, imi hama dzangu, zvirokwazvo, rudzi rwangu rwandiri mudzikinuri warwo. Saka ndinokurairai kuti mudzingise mwoyo yenyu uye murege nharo dzenyu dzisina zvibereko kuti mubereke zvibereko muna Messiasi wenyu wechokwadi, saka inzwai mirairo yangu yakapiwa kuti muteerere kuitira kuti murarame pamberi paYahweh Mukuru Ndiri, zvirokwazvo, nokundigamuchira saMessiasi wenyu wekusingaperi uyo zvirokwazvo, zvirokwazvo: ndini iye.

Uye sezvo Yahweh akatuma mutemo waMozisi kuMadzibaba enyu (mutemo iwoyo uri mudzidzisi wenyu wekukusvitsai kuna Kristu), saizvozvowo Yahweh akandituma sezvakapikirwa kare-kare paakapikira ruponeso kuMadzibaba avo vane vana vandiri kutaura navo zvino, zvirokwazvo, vana vaIsraeri vandinoda nerudo rusingadzime. Asi mukusaziva nekusatenda kusina kutendeka ndakarambwa neMadzibaba enyu ndikaiswa mumaoko evanhu vane zvivi kuti vandiitire nehutsinye hukuru nehuipi zvinhu izvo mutemo waivarambidza kuita; uye kuti varatidzike sevakarurama kuvanhu venyika pavakauraya gwayana raMwari nemvumo yeMadzibaba enyu, zvirokwazvo, mhosva iyo vazhinji venyu muchiri kubvumirana nayo neupenzi.

Asi, nzwisisai Israeri kuti handina kutorwa nechisimba nevavengi vangu uye nevavengi venyu. Nzwisisai, kuti handina kutorwa nechisimba, kana neunyanzvi uye hapana chombo chinogadzirirwa kundirwisa chingabudirira, nokuti ndizvo zvakataurwa naYahweh uye zvinotaurwa naYahweh hapana angazvishandura! Zvirokwazvo, Israeri, ndini iye akati kuna Judhasi Iskarioti pandakatengeswa neruoko rwake nemari makumi matatu esirivha, izvi zvauri kuita, zviite nekukurumidza! Nokuti mutengo waifanira kupiwa muridzi wemuranda aive nemuranda akatungwa neNzombe ndihwo upenyu hwangu hwakatengeswa mumaoko evaya vakandibaya, hwakapiwa nemaoko evapristi vakuru neSanhedrini vandaipikisana navo nokuda kwenzira dzavo dzakaipa nedzehunyengeri. Uye saka Judhasi akanditengesa mumaoko aEsau, zvirokwazvo, iye akatora nyika yechipikirwa zvakare nechisimba, zvirokwazvo, kodzero yakatengeswa naEsau nechidimbu chechingwa. Asi, ndakazvibvumira ndichida kutorwa nevavengi vangu nepo ndaigona kutiza nyore, uye zvirokwazvo, zvirokwazvo, dai ndakanyengetera kuna Yahweh, angadai akandipa simba pamusoro pemauto engirozi anodarika gumi nemaviri, asi Magwaro aizozadziswa sei neimwe nzira, uye saka zvakanga zvapera.

Asi, ndizvo zvazviri, kuti zvinangwa zvangu nenzira dzangu hazvina kunzwisiswa nehama dzangu uye neavo verudzi rwangu vandinoda rudo rusingaperi, rwakadzama uye runopfuta rusingagoni kudzimwa kana kukundwa, nokuti ndini Dombo reNheyo renyu risina iro pasina muvaki angavaka, uye panogumburwa vasina kutenda. Nokuti murudo rwangu kwamuri, uye kuna Adhamu wese akakurirwa nevakawira, ndakazvipa ndichida mumaoko evavengi vangu kuti ndikudzikinurei kubva pakutukwa kwemutemo.

Uye saka uyu ndiwo murairo wangu wekusingaperi, kuti mudanane, sezvandakakudai. Hakuna rudo rukuru kupfuura urwu, kana munhu upi zvake achipa upenyu hwake sechibayiro cheshamwari dzake, izvo ndakaita ndichida ndichiziva kuti pasina kuteerera kwangu kuchido chaBaba vangu vanhu vaigona kuraswa nekusingaperi. Uye ndini muponesi waAdhamu, zvirokwazvo, iye akadzikinura vanhu kubva mumamiriro avo ekunge vawa kana munhu upi zvake akauya kwandiri akapfugama ndokundipupura saIshe wavo uye zvirokwazvo ndini Messiasi wenyu wechokwadi imi Israeri.

Nzwisisai hama dzangu, kuti handina kutaura zvinhu pamusoro pangu zvisina kunyorwa nemunwe waYahweh pamahwendefa emabwe uye zvakataurwa nemuromo wevaranda vake vaporofita kuti zvinyorwe kuitira kutenda nekuteerera kwevagari venhaka yechipikirwa, vamuri imi Israeri, kana mukasaomesa mwoyo yenyu sezvakaita Madzibaba enyu murenje mukudenha kwavo Yahweh, uye murenje upenyu hwavo husina kutenda hwakaguma pasina, uye ivo pachavo havana kugara nhaka yezvipikirwa asi vana vekutenda!

Asi muupofu hwenyu hwechikamu munotenda nhema dzaEsau kuti ndiri munyengeri uye chokwadi ndakarambwa naEsau nokuti Esau anotsvaga kutora nhaka yaakatengesera mudiki uye hapana nzira yekutendeuka kuna Esau, kunyange akaitsvaga nemisodzi. Uye Madzibaba enyu havana kunzwisisa zvinangwa zvangu zvechokwadi nokuti vakatsvaga mutungamiri wenyika kuti akunde avo vavakanga vazvipira kuzviisa pasi pavo kuitira kuti vachengetedze zvinzvimbo zvavo pakati pevanhu. Asi, ndakatumwa kununura vanhu kubva muruoko rwevakawira avo Adhamu akabatana navo neupenzi mukusateerera kwake murairo waYahweh nokuda kwemano enyoka yakamunyengera pamwe naamai vevapenyu vese pavakatadza paEdeni pakutanga. Uye kunyange marudzi mazhinji akasimuka akawa, uye umambo huzhinji hwakasimuka hukawa kubvira panguva dzechibayiro changu, Israeri achiri muuranda kuvavengi vavo kusvikira nhasi uye anoshandira zvinangwa zvaEsau, uyo anotsvaga kuparadzwa kwavo kwakakwana.

Hongu, Israeri akaramba ari muuranda nokuti rudzi rwenyu rwakatorwa kubva kwamuri mukurangwa nokuda kwekuuraya ropa risina mhosva, zvirokwazvo, kuuraya nemaune uye nehuipi murume asina chivi akatumwa naYahweh kuti akudzikinurei. Zvirokwazvo, ini, Yeshua HaMashiach, pakuzara kwenguva, Mwari akatuma Mwanakomana wake akazvarwa nemukadzi, akaitwa pasi pemutemo, kuti adzikinure avo vaiva pasi pemutemo. Uye muupenyu hwangu husina chivi nekuteerera ndakazvipa segwayana raMwari muchibayiro chinorwadza uye chine hutsinye pandakazvipa semupristi mukuru nekusingaperi maererano neurongwa hwaMerkizedeki.

Uye basa iri rakapera uye ndakagara kuruoko rwerudyi rwaBaba vangu, zvirokwazvo, Yahweh, mukuru Ndiri. Uye ndakagara nhaka yezvinhu zvose, uye mumaoko angu ane simba zvinhu zvose zvakapiwa kudenga nepanyika kusvikira vavengi vangu vaitwa chitsiko chetsoka dzangu asi Umambo hwangu hahuzomboperi! Umambo uhwo huchatongwa naMambo wenyu zvirokwazvo huri kusvika pakupera kwedambudziko raJakobho pandichaparadza umambo hwevavengi uye ndounza kutonga kukuru nemigumisiro pamusoro pevakawira uye vachaenda mukurangwa nokuda kwezvivi zvavo zvizhinji uye kutongwa kwavo kusingaperi uko kusati kuchizombova nenguva yekuzorora. Uye vamwe vavo vachaenda mugungwa guru remoto nesarufa inopfuta pakudzoka kwangu kwechipiri kuzotonga nyika apo chikara nemuporofita wenhema vachakandwa imomo vari vapenyu. Uye maSatani achasungwa necheni huru iyo vachasungwa nayo mukurwadziswa nekutyiswa kukuru sezvavanosungwa mukutya kunotyisa kwezvakavamirira munhaka yavo yekusingaperi yekurangwa nekusingaperi. Zvirokwazvo, mutongo unotyisa uyo Satani haangatongopukunyuki paanosungwa okandwa mumoto unopisa zvikuru nekusingaperi pakupera kwemwaka wake mudiki.

Nokuti, ndini iye akapiwa kuti aponese Jakobho, zvirokwazvo, mbeu yakapikirwa yemukadzi iyo Adhamu akatenda mairi netariro, uye achamutsirwa kuupenyu hutsva nekusingaperi. Nokuti ndini iye akatumwa kuzodzikinura Adhamu, uye ndakapa donhwe rangu rekupedzisira reropa rakadeuka rikanyikwa nevhu dzvuku paKarivhari, zvirokwazvo, pandakataura mashoko iwayo asingaperi Zvapera. Uye ndizvo zvazviri, kuti ndini iye murudo rwangu kuna Yahweh nerudo rwangu kumunhu biyangu akazvipa sechibayiro mukurwadziwa kukuru, kunyadziswa, nekutambudzwa uye ndakakunda chivi nerufu uye zuva riri kusvika apo chinhu chose chakaipa chichange chisisipo.

Uye ndakazviita ndichida, zvirokwazvo, nokuzvidira nokuda kwembiri yakanga yakaiswa pamberi pangu uye ndakazviita ndichizvidza kunyadziswa kwose kwakanga kusiri kwangu kutakura. Asi murudo rwangu kwamuri, ndakatora kunyadziswa kwenyu nekurangwa kwenyu pamusoro pangu, zvirokwazvo, pavakabvarura nyama yangu kubva pamapfupa angu nezvikwapuro zvavo. Hongu, ndakatora kunyadziswa kwenyu nekurangwa kwenyu mukushungurudzwa neruvengo rwevanhu vane zvivi vakafurirwa nevakawira pachavo sezvavakandiuraya vasingazivi munzira dzavo dzechi mhuka kuti nekuuraya gwayana raMwari vakanga vachisimbisa magumo avo kumoto nekusingaperi nokuti hazvigoneki kuti rufu rungandibata, nokuti handina kumbotadza kana kamwe chete, kana riini, uye zvirokwazvo handife ndakazviita!

Hongu, Israeri, ndakatora nzvimbo yenyu mukurangwa nenzvimbo yeMadzibaba enyu akandiramba asingazivi nokuda kwekurudziro yaEsau! Asi maizodzikinurwa sei kunze kwekunge nekutengesa kwavo kusina kutenda nokuti kudzikinura Adhamu ropa risina chivi chete ndiro raigona kuzadzisa zvinodiwa zvechirango chinodiwa nemitemo isingaperi uye yakarurama yaYahweh nokuti pasina kudeurwa kweropa hapana kuregererwa kwezvivi. Uye sezvo Adhamu akasikwa asina chivi uye akakwana pazuva raakasikwa, saizvozvowo ini, Yeshua HaMashiach (uyo ari gwayana raMwari) ndakasikwa ndakakwana uye ndisina chivi kuitira kudzikinurwa kuzere kwevanhu. Nokuti kana ropa renzombe nerembudzi, nemadota etsiru, zvichisasa avo vasina kuchena, zvichitsvenesa pakucheneswa kwenyama, ropa raKristu iro kuburikidza neMweya usingaperi rakazvipa risina mhosva kuna Mwari, haringatonyanyi kuchenesa hana yenyu pamabasa akafa, kuti mushumire Mwari mupenyu here?

Saka ndinokupai kuti munzwisise kuti ndakatora nzvimbo yenyu mukushorwa nekurohwa, uye ndakazviita ndichida uye pasina kumanikidzwa; kunyange ndakanyengetera mubindu reGetsemane kana paine imwe nzira, asi kwete kuda kwangu asi kwaYahweh kuitwe. Uye saka mukunyadziswa kwangu, ndakatorwa kunze kwemasuwo eguta guru iroro, (zvirokwazvo, Jerusarema rinouraya vaporofita) kwandakatumwa kune vangu, uye ipapo nguo dzangu dzakagovaniswa sezvavakakanda mijenya! Hongu, ipapo paKarivhari, mushure mekunge ndatotsungirira maawa ekurangwa kusingaperi kwandakanga ndisina mhosva, ndakarovererwa ipapo nemaoko evanhu vane zvivi, avo zvivi zvavo ndakanga ndisina mugove kana fungu mazviri kunyange zvishoma. Asi ndichida uye nokuzvidira ndakasungwa ndikarovererwa nemaoko netsoka dzangu kuti ndiremberere padanda iroro rakakasharara panzvimbo yenyu, zvirokwazvo, chirango chakanga chisiri changu kutakura. Nokuti riiniko zvakambopiwa kune asina mhosva kuti azvishongedze nokuzvidira mukunyadziswa pamuti wekuzvidzwa mukunyadziswa kukuru nekutambudzika kuitira ruponeso rwehama dzake dzisina kururama kuti atambure panzvimbo yadzo, asi murudo rwangu kwamuri ndakaita izvozvo chaizvo. Nzwisisai kuti chero nguva ndaigona kuvamisa nokukumbira Baba vangu uye vangadai vakandipa mauto engirozi anodarika gumi nemaviri kuti anditungamirire, asi, handina kumbokumbira asi ndakasarudza kutsungirira zvizere uye ndichida kurangwa kwenyu kwakanga kusiri kwangu kutakura. Uye saka murudo rwangu ndakazvipa nokuda kwenyu, uye ndaizozviita zvakare, kunyange chinhu chakadaro chisingadiwi uye Yahweh akagamuchira chibayiro changu kamwe chete, zvose, uye nekusingaperi, naizvozvo hapachina chimwe chibayiro chechivi.

Naizvozvo, Israeri, nzwisisai rudo, mwoyo murefu, nyasha netsitsi dzandiri kukuratidzai mumamiriro enyu azvino ekuramba Messiasi wenyu wechokwadi neupanduki, uye ndiri kuuya kwamuri panguva ino yekupedzisira murudo rwangu kwamuri ruchiri rusingafi uye rusingakundiki, kana mukangogamuchira Messiasi wenyu wechokwadi. Nokuti ndini iye akazvidira kutora nzvimbo yenyu pakati pekushorwa, kurangwa, nekuurayiwa kwevatadzi nokuti mubairo wechivi rufu, asi ini handina chivi nekusingaperi, uye ndinogara ndichiita kuda kwaBaba vangu, zvirokwazvo, muenzaniso wamunoramba mukusateerera kwenyu kwemaune kuna Yahweh, uye mukuramba kwenyu Messiasi wenyu wechokwadi.

Uye ndakarambirwei? Kunyange Pondiyo Pirato aiva Mutongi weJudhiya panguva iyoyo aida kundiregedza uye akataura chokwadi pamusoro pevaipomera, zvirokwazvo, avo vaEsau vakaunganidza Madzibaba enyu kuti vamudanidzire kuti tisunungurirei Bharabhasi! Zvirokwazvo, sezvavakadanidzira vangangove nemweya mumwe chete "Murovererei!", maererano nekusunungurirwa naPirato Messiasi wavo wechokwadi neropa risina mhosva ravakaramba. Nokuti aiziva, zvakanaka, kuti vakanga vandipa nokuda kwegodo. Zvakare paakanga agara pachigaro chekutonga, mudzimai wake akatuma shoko kwaari, achiti, usava nechokuita nemurume uyo akarurama: nokuti ndatambura zvinhu zvizhinji nhasi muchiroto nokuda kwake, uye ndizvo zvakanga zvakaita uye ndizvo zvakanyorwa mumagwaro matsvene kana mukawana kutenda kuti mutende uchapupu hwangu.

Uye zvose nokuda kwekuti Madzibaba enyu akashuva zvinhu zvenyika ino uye akada zvinzvimbo zvavo pakati pevanhu kwavaigona kurarama nezvegumi nezvipiriso zvevanhu uye vomira sevaprofita venhema vanozviruramisa vachidzokorora uchenjeri hwaEsau munzeve dzevanhu: kunyange huchenjeri hwenhema uhwo vakuru vavo vaikoshesa zvikuru kupfuura izwi nouchenjeri hwaYahweh, iye mukuru NDIRI, uyo wavakaramba pavakaramba uye vakazvirovhera Jesu weNazareta.

Saka zvakaitika ndezvekuti, mukuramba kwenyu ini, kunyange Jesu weNazareta, ndakatendeukira kuvaHedheni, uye vakanyatsonzwa izwi rangu kwechinguva uye vazhinji, vazhinji zvikuru avo neimwe nzira vakanga vasina tariro, zvino vasvika paruoko muchinunuro munaShe wavo, kunyange boka guru revanhu randichamutsa riri kurarama kwandiri kuti ndiriratidze kuna Yahweh pakuungana kukuru kwevatsvene kwave kuda kuitika. Uye vazhinji vanobva kuJudhiya nevazhinji vakanga vakapararira kune dzimwe nyika vakaiswa chisimbiso cheruponeso ruzere. Uye vehama dzenyu, kunyange avo vanobva kuumambo hwekuchamhembe vakatarirwa ruponeso rusingaperi, kunyange vazhinji vavo zvino vakaiswa chisimbiso nekusingaperi uye vachamutsirwa kuupenyu hutsva. Zvirokwazvo, zvirokwazvo, vose ava vakasiya muenzaniso wekutendeuka kuti mutevere, kana mukateerera izwi raMesiya wechokwadi chete uyo Yahweh achazombotuma, iye Jesu weNazareta Mesiya wenyu wechokwadi!

Nokuti nokuda kwezvivi zvavo madzinza ekuchamhembe akaparadzirwa pakati pemarudzi, kwavakachengetedzwa nokuda kworuponeso, kunyange noruoko rune simba rwaYahweh. Uye zvakadaro zvakanyorwa noruoko rwoMuapostora wangu Pauro paainyorera vaRoma makore mazhinji apfuura: Tinoziva kuti zvinhu zvose zvinobata pamwe chete kuti zviitire zvakanaka avo vanoda Mwari, kunyange avo vakadanwa maererano nechinangwa chake. Nokuti avo vaakaziva kare, akavatemawo kuti vafananidzwe nomufananidzo woMwanakomana wake, kuti ave dangwe pakati pehama zhinji. Uyezve, avo vaakatemera, ivavo akavadanawo, uye avo vaakadana, ivavo akaruramisazve, uye avo vaakaruramisa, ivavo akavakudzawo. Uye saka muIsraeri yaMwari kuunganidzwa kwemarudzi akarasika kuchaitwa, uye vaIsraeri vazhinji vakasvika paruponeso nokundipupura saShe wavo, kunyange Jesu weNazareta, munguva yangu yenyasha noruponeso rwavakawana.

Kunyange zvazvo parizvino muchizvidza mabasa angu, kunyange mabasa angu oruponeso andakaita mumarudzi nomurayiro waYahweh munguva yangu yenyasha iyo zvino yasvika kumagumo ayo; saka nzwisisai izvo Yahweh akanatsa neropa raYeshua HaMashiach, musazviti zvakashata kana kuti zvisina kuchena! Uye ini, Yeshua HaMashiach ndinokukumbirisai kuti mutevere nzira dzehama dzenyu dzingava pedyo kana kure dzakauya kwandiri mukutendeuka kuzere kuti ndigokugamuchirai kwandiri uye ndikudzorerei kuna Yahweh, nokuti uyo ane mwanakomana anewo Baba asi uyo asina mwanakomana haanawo Baba, uye ndizvo zvakataurwa naYahweh!

Saka ndizvo zvazviri kuti ndinotendeukira kuhama dzangu zvakare kunyange pandinotendeuka kubva kumarudzi munguva ino yekupedzisira uye kupatsanurwa kwekupedzisira kuchaitika muvhiki rechimakumi manomwe raDhanieri, iro rasvika kuzonatsa nyika mumoto wangu wokunatsa uye Esau achaparadzaniswa naJakobho zvachose nekusingaperi. Nokuti Esau akatengesa udangwe hwake, uye Yahweh akagamuchira sarudzo yake nekusingaperi kuti ive sarudzo isingaperi, nokuti vana vechivimbiso ndivo vadyi venhaka yezvivimbiso uye kwete vana venyama, kana muri vaKristu, ipapo muri mbeu yaAbrahama, uye vadyi venhaka nechivimbiso. Uye ndizvo zvazviri kuti zvivimbiso zvenhaka zvakaitwa naYahweh pachake zvakaitwa mukutenda kune avo zvakaitirwa, nokuti akarurama achararama nokutenda! Uye shure kokunge zvivimbiso zvokutendeka zvapiwa, saizvozvowo sungano dzakaitwa uye zviratidzo zvechivimbiso zvakapiwa uye sungano dzikava testamente nokudeurwa kweropa uye sungano dzose dzekare nezvivimbiso zvose zvaYahweh kubva paduku kusvika pakukura zvakabatanidzwa muTestamente Yangu Itsva yechivimbiso, umo chisikwa chichaitwa chakarurama nekusingaperi mukudzoreredzwa kusingaperi kuna Baba veZveneko, uyo asina kusanduka kana mumvuri wokushanduka.

Saka ndinouya kuhama dzangu murudo nokuzvininipisa, uye ndinouya kwamuri nokuzvidira murudo rwangu kwamuri ndisingadi kuti chero ani wenyu aparare mukusatenda. Uye zvirokwazvo, zvirokwazvo vakasara vachaponeswa vari vapenyu kubva kuna Jakobho kunyange panguva yekutambudzika kwaJakobho inova vhiki rechimakumi manomwe raDhanieri. Naizvozvo, ndinokukumbirisai hama dzangu teererai kunzwi rangu nokuti ino inguva yekupedzisira uye uku ndiko kupatsanurwa kwekupedzisira kutsamwa kusati kwadururwa kuzere uye ndisati ndauya kuzoparadza umambo hwemuvengi zvachose, nokuti mukururama ndinouya seShumba yedzinza raJudha uye ndinouya kuzotonga nokuita hondo!

Uye vose vachatendeukira kwandiri panguva ino, Iwe Israeri, kunyange vose vandinorayira vakaberekwa naMadzibaba ekare kuti vateerere izwi rangu, uye kunyange vose vachadana zita rangu rine simba kusvika kumagumo kubva kumarudzi vachaponeswa uye vachaponeswa mumirairo yavo. Marudzi achagamuchira rukudzo rwakafanana nerwangu pandakazvipa nokuda kwezvivi zvevanhu, uye vachaurairwa kutenda kwavo sezvavanodana zita rangu rinoponesa kusvika kumagumo. Uye vachaenda mukubwinya kukuru nekusingaperi uye mibayiro yavo ndeyekusingaperi nokuda kwekumira kwavo, uye nokuteerera kwavo vachange vakunda nzira dzavo dzokusatenda nedzokusafarira, kunyange zvakadaro nomoto.

Uye veIsraeri, hama dzangu, ndichauya kuzokuponesai uye ndichauya kuzokubatsirai ne144,000 vangu kunyange avo vakazvinatsa zvizere sevakundi vangu muropa rangu rinoyananisa nerinodzikinura pazuva ravo rekuponeswa uye vachaunganidzirwa kwandiri pakuungana kukuru ikoko kwave kuda kuitika pakarepo panyika. Nokuti ndichatungamirira 144,000 vangu kuti vaburukire paGomo reZioni, uye vachanditevera nhanho nenhanho sezvavanotungamirira vakasara vangu vaJakobho kunzvimbo yakachengeteka murenje kwakagadzirirwa nzvimbo naYahweh kuti vachengetedzwe, uye vachadyiswa nokuchengetwa uye shato, chikara, nomuprofita wenhema hazvizovabati, kana kuvawana, avo vakasara muri imi, Iwe Israeri. Asi Bharabhasi achaunza nyangadzi yokuparadza uye masora mazhinji achanatswa kubva munyika, uye ndichavanza vakasara vangu kubva kuhondo yezuva iro guru, kunyange zuva rokuuya kwangu kwechipiri iro zuva Yahweh akatara kubva kare kusingaperi uye rakaiswa nekusingaperi muchirongwa chake choruponeso uye zuva iroro zvirokwazvo rinoswedera, nokuti ndizvo zvakanyorwa uye ndizvo zvichaitwa.

Naizvozvo, hama dzangu dzandinoda nekusingaperi, uye zvakadaro ndichiri kuremekedza kodzero yenyu yakapiwa naMwari yokuzvisarudzira nzira yamuchafamba nayo. Naizvozvo ndinokubvunzai, mucharamba muri panzira iyo yakafara inoenda kukuparadzwa inotungamirira kumirazvo isingadzimiki yakagadzirirwa vakawa here? Kana kuti, muchateerera izwi raMesiya wenyu wechokwadi, kunyange iye akapa donhwe rega rega reropa rake risina chivi mukurwadziwa kukuru nokutambura kuti murarame pamberi paMwari wenyu, kunyange Yahweh Mukuru NDIRI? Hongu, Yahweh akadeura misodzi yakawanda mukusuwa kwake nokuda kwemakwai akarasika eimba yaIsraeri, kunyange vose vanoramba izwi rake neizwi rangu. Muchabatana neni here nokuteerera kwenyu Mesiya wenyu wechokwadi? Kana kuti muchabatana naBharabhasi nokuzvidira uyo Esau akatuma kuti akuparadzei?

Saka inzwai Mashoko angu Iwe Israeri uye nzwisisai sarudzo yamunofanira kuita uye nzwisisai kuti ndiri kukukweverai kwandiri uye ndichatuma zvapupu zvangu zviviri zvine simba uye ndinokurayirai kuti muzvinzwe kuitira kuti mugamuchire uchapupu hwazvo nokuti zvichapupura zvinhu zvakawanda zvakavanzika zvakavanzirwa mwoyo yevanhu asi zvazvichazivisa uye uchapupu hwazvo huchamira nekusingaperi. Naizvozvo, teererai izwi reGwayana rechokwadi raMwari, Iwe Israeri, nokuti hapana ruponeso kana rudzikinuro mune rimwe zita rakambopiwa, uye zvirokwazvo, zvirokwazvo hapana rimwe zita richazombopiwa nokuda kwechinangwa ichi, kunze kwaYeshua HaMashiach uyo anovawo Jesu weNazareta kunyange zvazvo panyama ndisingazivikanwi uye ndiri Kristu Jesu kunyange iye anova Ishe wemadzishe naMambo wemadzimambo.

Ndini Shumba yedzinza raJudha uye mukururama ndinouya kuzotonga nokuita hondo.

Ndini iye akatuma Mashoko aya nomuranda wangu uye nomuranda wangu Mashoko aya akatumwa, uye ndinopupura nokusimbisa kuti Mashoko eEvhangeri Isigaperi akaziviswa noruoko rwangu, uye zvirokwazvo naYahweh pachake, ndeechokwadi, uye haaperi, uye muranda wangu akataura nokutendeka Mashoko aakaziviswa panguva yawo uye achachengetedzwa kuburikidza nenjodzi dziri kuuya uye achawana maziso, nzeve, nemwoyo yeavo vaanotumirwa.

Ndini Mesiya wenyu asingagumi uye anogara nekusingaperi anoshuvira ruponeso rwenyu norudzikinuro Israeri, uye zvirokwazvo, zvirokwazvo ndakagadzirira kugamuchira kwandiri ani naani anopindura kunzwi rangu uye muchapinda muUmambo hwemakore ane chiuru uye muchava vanhu vakasiyana uye muchararama upenyu husingaperi uye muchawana mafuro asingaperi pakati peIsraeri yaMwari, uye hamuzomboparadzaniswi zvakare nokutonga kwakarurama kwaMambo wenyu, Ishe, Mupristi Mukuru, naMesiya uye nekusingaperi muchatungamirirwa murugare nokururama uye muchakura semhuru dziri muchidyiro.

Ndini Yeshua HaMashiach uye ndiri izvo mukuru NDIRI akandiita uye ndiri gwayana raMwari rakapihwa sepasika yenyu yakakwana uye isina chivi nekusingaperi uye nokusingagumi.
