Kurume 13, 2024

Simudzai Kuchema

Mashoko andakapiwa naIshe, ndinozivisa pachena: Inzwai Shoko raIshe!

Simudzai kuchema; zvirokwazvo, simudzai kuchema pamusoro peavo vachakabatirira kunyika ino yakawa. Nokuti vakati mumwoyo yavo, "Chii chingawira pamusoro pangu? Sezvazvakanga zvakaita kare, zvicharamba zvakadaro nokusingaperi; uye ini nevemhuri yangu ticharamba tichirarama zvakapfuma, uye tichazviunganidzira zvinhu zvakanaka zvenyika ino nekuunganidza mari, izvo zvichatinyatsoponesa panguva yekushaiwa.” Makati mumwoyo yenyu, "Takachenjera, uye takabudirira; njodzi haingauyi pamusoro pedu, sezvo isingambouyi kwatiri, nokuti Mwari wedu haangatenderi rudzi rwedu kuti ruwe; nokuti takachenjera uye tine simba.” Asi hamuna kuchenjera, asi makachenjera mukuzvifunga kwenyu; uye ndakapa vavengi venyu kukunda pamusoro penyu.

Haiwa iwe munhu, ndiani achakuponesa kubva paawa yako yekutongwa yava kusvika kunyika yose? Vatungamiri vako vachakuponesa here? Zvirokwazvo, ndinoti kwete, sezvo mwanakomana wekurashwa ava kuuya kwauri uye atotofamba pachena pakati penyu, achinyengera vazhinji nemashoko akanaka nekutaura kwakanaka. Mari yako ichakuponesa here? Kwete, pfuma yako yose ichanzi haina maturo pakarepo, uye hapazovi nemuripo wemabasa ehupenyu hwako awakachengeta nemakaro. Haiwa, dai usina kurasa nzira dzaIshe uye wakada muvakidzani wako ukabatsira varombo nezvakawanda zvako.

Asi kwete, munochengeta zvakawanda zvamakapiwa naMwari kuti mugutse mweya yenyu. Zvirokwazvo, ndakapa vazhinji venyu zvinopfuura zvakakwana kwamuri nekukomborera muvakidzani wenyu; asi kwete, makati, ndichazvichengetera zvakawanda izvi nokuti Mwari zvirokwazvo akandikomborera nekuda kwekunaka kwangu nesimba rangu. Tarirai, ndinokuudzai chinhu chisati chaitika, kuitira kuti muawa yenyu yenjodzi muchazoyeuka izwi rangu nemashoko emuromo wangu andakafuridzira muvaporofita vangu. Nokuti handivatumi kuti vataure zvavo, asi ndinovatuma senyevero kwamuri, kuti muponese mweya yenyu nekutendeukira kwandiri, Ishe Jesu Kristu, anokwanisa kukuponesai zvakakwana. Asi makati mumwoyo menyu, Ruponeso? Handidi kuponeswa, sezvo ndakakosha sezvandakazvarwa. Asi zvivi zvenyu munhuhwi wakaipa mumhino dzaBaba vangu, uye nyika yaora nekuda kwezvakaipa zvenyu. Hamuzivi here kuti makazvarwa makafa mukudarika nezvivi, zvichishatisa chimiro chenyu chaicho?

Kunyange ndaizokupai chimiro chitsva, chakachena, munotora chibayiro changu sechisina maturo uye munonamatira kunyama yenyu yakaora.

Simudzai kuchema pamusoro pevanhu verudzi rwenyu rwaimbova rukuru vanoramba izwi rangu. Nokuti kusuwa kukuru nemarwadzo zvichauya pakarepo pamusoro penyu. Kuchava nekuzununguswa kukuru, uye hondo yangu ine simba yekudenga ichanayisa kuparadza kukuru pamusoro pehure guru, uye harichazomukizve, kunyange zvazvo vakasara vachaponeswa. Kunyange munjodzi yenyu nekuparadzwa kuri kuuya zvirokwazvo ndichatumira vaporofita vangu kwamuri. Muchavaziva nezvibereko zvavo, mashoko, nekuporesa; kuporesa kwakawanda nezvishamiso zvakawanda ndichaita nemaoko avo. Asi, kunyange zvakadaro vazhinji vachaparara nekufa muzvivi zvavo, asi vanyoro vachagara nhaka yenyika; nokuti nhaka yangu ineni, yakagadzirira kugoverwa kune vakatendeka. Ndingatambanudza ruoko rwangu kusvika rinhi kuvanhu vanopikisa vanomira vachindipikisa nenzira dzangu uye mukunyengera kwavo vachizviti vakarurama pamberi paMwari nenguvo dzavo dzakasviba?

Tendeukai! Chemai muungudze pamusoro pezvivi zvenyu, uye danidzirai kwandiri kuti ndikuponesei. Makereke enyu mazhinji akapindirwa nemhumhi dzinoparadza dzinokuparidzirai mashoko anozokutungamirirai kumagumo enyu, uye kunyange zvakadaro hamuzotendeuki kubva kwavari uye kutendeukira kwandiri nechiso nemwoyo zvakazaruka, sezvo ndaizokuponesai kubva mumatambudziko enyu nekuchengetedza vana venyu kubva kumauto emuipi, ayo anofamba pachena mumigwagwa yenyu uye atokubirai zvakanyanya. Kuchiva kwavo hakuzogutswi kusvikira vatora zvose kubva kwamuri nekuremadza vazhinji. Asi avo vanodana zita rangu vachaponeswa kana vakatsungirira kusvika kumagumo.

Sei muchiomesa mwoyo yenyu kuna Ishe weHondo? Sei muchiramba muchinyepa kumwoyo yenyu, muchiti, "Hakuna njodzi; sezvazvakanga zvakaita nezuro, zvichava nhasi, uye mangwana kunouya zvimwe chetezvo?" Imi vaseki muchakohwa mubairo wenzira dzenyu dzekusatenda, uye avo vanondiramba vachaparadzwa zvachose uye vachawira mumatambudziko makuru. Inzwai mashoko angu, imi chizvarwa choupombwe nechakaipa. Inzwai mashoko angu uye teererai nenzeve dzenyu kuti munzwe vara rega rega randichataura. Kuzununguswa kukuru kuchabvarura rudzi rwenyu rwamunokoshesa zvikuru, uye kubva mukuzununguswa kukuru kuchauya kuparadzwa kukuru. Ndichaparadza maguta enyu aimbova makuru uye ndichatounza mvura kuti ifukidze mahombekombe enyu ane zvivi; nokuti hamudi Ishe asi munoda mafaro kupfuura kuda Mwari.

Handifadzwi nekutongwa kwenyu, sezvo nyasha dzangu netsitsi zvakawanda kwamuri kwenguva refu zvino. Vazhinji veavo vakasvibisa sungano yaNoa vachafukidzwa nemvura nekuda kwekusvibisa uta hutsvene hwemavara mazhinji hwakapiwa naBaba vangu sechitsidzo. Moto uchanaya pamusoro penyu neruoko rwevavengi venyu, uye vazhinji vachaparara mumirazvo nechepfu inotevera. Nyika yenyu ichabvarurwa, uye njodzi huru ichava chirariro chenyu, nokuti makaramba Ishe weMadzishe naMambo weMadzimambo.

Muipi akatopinza Hondo yake Tsvuku mumaguta enyu, mataundi, nemisha. Vanofamba pakati penyu, vachironga kuparadzwa kwenyu; uye vakamirira mirairo yavo nekubva kwemudzivisi wangu, wandichasunungura munguva pfupi. Vachachenesa nekuparadza nekukunda zvasara. Kwete izvozvo chete, asi vachazvitora sezvavo, uye vachatapa vazhinji venyu kumisasa yavo yavatogadzirira. Vazhinji vacharasikirwa nemisoro yavo nekuda kwezita rangu, uye ndichavapa mubairo wakawanda kana vakaramba vakatendeka kusvika kumagumo. Hakuzovi nechiedza; kuchava nemabhiriji akawa nemigwagwa yakapidigurwa. Ndiani achakupai chikafu nemvura kana rudzi rwenyu rwarara rwakaparadzwa rusingazomukizve? Asi ndichaponesa vakasara, uye vakasara ndichavaponesa, nokuti ndakazvizivisa!

Musatya, vanhu vangu nevatendeki vangu. Ndichakubvisai pakati mukubwaira kweziso kuenda kuimba yaBaba vangu, kwandakakugadzirirai nzvimbo netafura kuti tidye pamwe chete, vana vangu. Ndinoshuva kuvepo kwenyu, uye munguva pfupi muchandiona nechiso chenyu nemaziso enyu, uye muchava nemubairo wekutenda kwenyu mandiri nokuti mune kutenda mumwoyo menyu kunyange musati maona nemaziso enyu kubwinya kwangu, kana kubwinya kwaBaba vangu, vakuru kwandiri. Uye ndakakudanai nekukusarudzai sevekwangu, uye hamuzombobvi kwandiri, sezvo muchagara naIshe wenyu nemudzikinuri wenyu nokusingaperi. Zivai kuti ndichakupai mubairo wakawanda nekukudururirai makomborero amusingagoni kunzwisisa panguva ino. Rambai makatendeka kusvika kumagumo, nokuti munguva pfupi zvinhu izvi zvichaitika; zvirokwazvo, awa yanonoka, uye usiku hwatotowira panyika.

Ndini Mambo weMadzimambo naIshe weMadzishe, uye ndakapiwa zita rinopfuura mazita ose, kuti pazita raJesu Kristu mabvi ose kudenga nepanyika apfugame.

Ndini Muchinda weRugare, uye kunze kwangu hakuna rugare.

Ndini iye akakupai zororo, kuti muzorore mandiri uye muwane pokupotera munyasha dzangu.

Ndini iye anofamba pakati pezvigadziko zvemwenje zvinomwe, anorayira ngirozi dzaBaba vangu, uye zvinhu zvose kudenga nepanyika zviri mumaoko angu, sezvo Baba vangu vakazvipa kwandiri mukukunda kwangu pamusoro pemuipi kusvikira vavengi vangu vaitwa chitsiko chetsoka dzangu.

Ndini Ishe wenyu nemudzikinuri wenyu, uye ndini mufudzi akanaka akapfekedzwa korona yekubwinya kwose, uye ndakabata upenyu husingaperi mumaoko angu kune avo vachazvininipisa uye vokumbira.

Ndizivisei saIshe! Musatya, vana vangu vadiki, sezvo dziri nguva dzekupedzisira uye boka guru revanhu richaponeswa kubva mukutambudzika kuri kuuya. Ani naani anodana zita raJesu Kristu achaponeswa, uye ndichavaporesa nekuvapa mubairo wekutendeka kwavo. Zvirokwazvo, ndinofamba pakati penyu, uye chinhu chipi chikuru chakarongwa naIshe chaasina kuudza vaporofita vake chisati chaitika? Ndinokuudzai pachena, teererai izwi rangu, sezvo nguva diki yasara hwamanda yekupedzisira isati yarira. Kunyange ngirozi dzakamira dzakagadzirira kumatenga kumusoro, dzakamirira kurira kwehwamanda! Rudzikinuro rwenyu ruzere ruri pamusuwo penyu, saka musatya uye munamatire kwandiri, nokuti upenyu hwenyu hwakavanzwa mandiri uye hwava kuda kuzarurwa.
