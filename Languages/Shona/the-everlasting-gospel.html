Vhangeri Risingaperi!

Inzwai Mashoko aYeshua HaMashiach uye rerekai nzeve dzenyu mukuteerera. Uye saka mwaka inoshanduka uye zuva rasvika apo njodzi ichawira pasi pano kamwe kamwe. Kamwe kamwe ndichaunganidza vakatendeka vangu kwandiri mumakore uye kamwe kamwe kuparadzwa kuchawira pasi rose uye kamwe kamwe nyika yamunoziva ichashandurwa zvachose, isingazombodzokeri kumamiriro aya akaora uye ane zvivi. Kunyange magumo ekutonga kwaSatani kuzere nezvivi ava pedyo, zuva iroro harichasviki zvizere kusvikira simba rose, nehutongi hweShumba zvaparadzwa pakudzoka kwangu kwechipiri pandichauya kuzotonga nyika mukururama pamwechete nevarwi vekudenga, kunyange hondo huru dzekudenga pakati padzo pachava nezvibereko zvekutanga kubva kuvakafa uye ndichavaunganidza kwandiri.

Nokuti avo vachava neni vakadanwa, uye vakasarudzwa, uye vakatendeka uye ndivo vezvokwadi. Uye vakazviratidza sevakatendeka muzvinhu zvishoma, saka ndichavaita vabati pane zvizhinji uye vachapinda mumufaro waShe wavo. Vachava hondo isingaparadzwi uye isingaperi isingagoni kukundwa kana kumiswa nenzira ipi zvayo, uye vachatsika-tsika chaizvo pasi petsoka dzavo zvinhu zvose zveUmambo hwevavengi mukuparadza kwakakwana nekuzviisa pasi kwezvinhu zvose zvakaipa. Nokuti kutsiva ndekwangu, ndizvo zvinotaura Ishe, uye ndicharipira. Uye ini, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndiri Ishe wemadzishe naMambo wemadzimambo, uye mukururama ndinouya kuzotonga nekurwa hondo.

Saka inzwai mashoko angu uye nzwisisai tariro yezvisikwa zvose, nokuti kunyange zvisikwa zvinomirira zvakanyanya kuratidzwa kweVanakomana vaMwari! Nokuti zvisikwa zvakaitwa kuti zviiswe pasi pezvisina maturo, kwete nokuda kwazvo, asi nokuda kwaiye akazviisa pasi pazvo netariro: Nokuti zvisikwa pachazvo zvichasunungurwawo kubva muuranda hwekuora, kupinda murusununguko runobwinya rwevana vaMwari. Uye saka ini, Jesu Kristu, ndichasunungura zvisikwa mukururama uye ndichaporesa nyika uye mukururama ndichatonga. Uye ndichaparadza hutongi hwose nehutongi hwemuvengi, uye umambo hwaSatani hahuchazomukizve.

Sezvazvakanyorwa, ndizvo zvazvichava, uye saka ndakaita kuti Johane Muratidzwi anyore kuti: Zvino ndakaona denga razaruka, uye tarira, bhiza jena, naiye wakanga akaritasva wainzi Wakatendeka nowezvokwadi, unotonga nokurwa hondo nokururama. Meso ake akanga akaita somurazvo womoto, uye pamusoro wake paiva nekorona zhinji; wakanga ane zita rakanyorwa, risina munhu unoziva, asi iye amene. Wakanga akafuka nguvo yakanyikwa muropa, uye zita rake rinonzi, Shoko raMwari. Uye hondo dzaiva kudenga dzakamutevera dzakatasva mabhiza machena, dzakafuka mucheka wakaisvonaka, muchena wakachena. Uye mumuromo make munobuda munondo unopinza, kuti nawo arove marudzi; uchavatonga netsvimbo youtare; unotsika chisviniro chewaini yekutsamwa kukuru nokuhasha kwaMwari Wemasimbaose. Uye pane nguvo yake, napachidya chake pane zita rakanyorwa, richinzi, Mambo wamadzimambo, naShe wamadzishe. Uye ndizvo zvakaita tariro yekuuya kwangu kwechipiri, uye zuva iroro riri kuswedera pedyo nesekondi imwe neimwe inopfuura.

Tarirai! Pazuva iroro ndichauya nevarwi vangu nokuti ndiyo nhaka yavo, kunyange hondo isina imwe yakafanana nayo, nokuti hondo yakaita seyangu haina kumbovapo, hondo isingakundiki inoumbwa nevakadzikinurwa, kunyange avo vakaberekwa vakafa mukudarika nezvivi asi zvino vachararama upenyu husingaperi uye vasingagoni kuurayiwa kana kukundwa. Nokuti ndakapiwa simba naYahweh nokuda kwechinangwa chakadaro, uye ndichave ndavapa simba nokuvaripira nokuda kwekutendeka kwavo norudo rwavo kwandiri nezita rangu, kunyange Jesu Kristu! Nokuti ani naani anoda nokushumira Yeshua HaMashiach anodawo nokushumira Yahweh, nokuti hakuna mukuru kupfuura Mwari mukuru asingaperi weIsraeri! Pazuva iroro riri kusvika muchaona matenga akazaruka, uye muchaona ini, Jesu Kristu, ndichitungamirira kubva kumatenga hondo huru ine simba yekudenga, uye ichava hondo isina imwe yakafanana nayo, zvachose! Nokuti Mwenga wangu achava neni.

Uye ndichapwanya chose chikara nemuporofita wenhema nehondo dzavo dzose nezvinyangadzo zvavo zvose zvavakaita zvinonyangadza uye saka vachaparadzwa kunyange nokupenya kwekuuya kwangu uye nomunondo iwoyo unopinza unocheka kumativi ose unobuda mumuromo mangu, uye havangamiri asi vachaparadzwa zvachose. Uye chikara nomuporofita wenhema vachakandwa vari vapenyu mugungwa remoto rinopfuta nesarufa uye havazombosunungurwi pakurwadziswa kwavo, nokuti vakazviita vakakodzera. Asi kutanga nyika inofanira kucheneswa kuburikidza nedambudziko raJakobho uye vazhinji vachauya kwandiri, kunyange vazhinji-zhinji uye mweya wose unodana kuzita raShe kusvika kumagumo uchaponeswa zvakakwana, uye Israeri inofanira kuunganidzwa nokuti ndichaponesa vakasara kubva kuna Jakobho vachazosvika kuna Mesiasi wavo wechokwadi uye asingaperi, nokuti ndiri Yeshua HaMashiach uye ndinouya maererano nezvipikirwa kuna Madzibaba zvakaziviswa kare naYahweh pachake, uye ndiri kuzadzikiswa kwazvo uye nguva yangu yasvika.

Saka kune avo vakaramba zita rangu rinoponesa, kunyange Jesu weNazareta, ndinokurairai kuti munzwe mashoko angu uye munzwisise nguva dzamunorarama uye mumhanyire kunzwi reizwi rangu uye mudane kuzita rangu iye zvino uye mundireurure saShe wenyu, nokuti hamuna nguva yekutambisa. Sezvakanga zvakaita vaya vakaberekwa patsva pakutanga pakuzvarwa kwekereke yangu pazuva rePendekosti sezvakanyorwa mumagwaro, saizvozvowo kuchava nevekupedzisira vachauya pakuberekwa patsva munguva ino yenyasha, kunyange vose vanouya kuruoko apo kudururwa kwangu kwemweya mutsvene munguva yangu yenyasha netsitsi kunosvika kumagumo.

Uye saka kushomeka kwezvinhu zvose zvaMwari zvino kuchawira panyika uye chivi zvino hachichadzorwi pandinobvisa mudzivisi wangu pandinounganidza Kereke yangu. Munhu wenyama akaramba izwi rangu, munhu wenyama akaramba nyasha dzangu, munhu wenyama akaramba tsitsi dzangu, munhu wenyama akaramba kukoka kwangu kukururama, uye munhu wenyama akandiramba ini Jesu weNazareta uye naizvozvo munhu wenyama akaramba Yahweh Mwari mukuru asingaperi akandituma, uye zvino ndichapa nyika rima ravainyanya kuda.

Asi nzwisisai kuti vazhinji vachandiitira mabasa makuru, uye zvapupu zvangu zviviri, kunyange zvigadziko zvangu zviviri zvemwenje zvichaporofita, uye ndichatuma zviuru zvangu zana nemakumi mana nezvina (144,000) kununura vazhinji, saka musatya! Ruponeso ruchaitwa, kunyange kuburikidza nekuparadzwa kwenyama uye vazhinji-zhinji vachauya kuruoko panguva ino. Nokuti zvirokwazvo, Yahweh haana uye haazombozvisiyi asina chapupu!

Nguva dzevaHedheni dzakabereka zvibereko zvizhinji pakati peavo vangadai vakaramba vasina tariro kunze kwekunge vaiswa muzvipikirwa zvakaitwa kuna Madzibaba, kunyange avo vanorarama nekutenda kweMwanakomana waMwari akavada uye akazvipa iye nokuda kweruponeso rwavo. Nokuti sezvakanyorwa: Akarurama uchararama nokutenda! Uye saka nokutenda mandiri, Kristu Jesu, vava vana vekutenda, kunyange vana vekutenda kwaAbrahama. Nokuti Abrahama akatenda Mwari chisimbiso chekudzingiswa chisati chapihwa, uye zvakaverengerwa kwaari sekururama, kunyange sezvinopupura Mosesi! Uye saka Abrahama ndibaba vevose vanotenda uye avo vakaitwa vakarurama nokutenda muna Jesu Kristu Akarurama, nokuti ndiri akarurama uye mururamisi wevose vanotenda muzita rangu rine simba!

Asi vazhinji vakashandisa nyasha dzangu netsitsi semvumo yekutadza, kunyange vachifarira nekuzvirumbidza nenzira dzavo dzine zvivi nedzinoparadza, kunyange vachidzidzisa vana vavo kurarama nenzira dzavo dzine zvivi uye saka zvizvarwa zvakaipa munzira dzazvo uye nzira dzazvo dzinonyangadza uye saka vanhu vakaipa nevanyengeri vawedzera kuipa. Musanyengerwa, kana munhu achidyara kunyama munhu iyeye achakohwa kuora kubva kunyama. Uye vose vanoramba chipo changu cheupenyu husingaperi uye panzvimbo pazvo vosarudza kuora kwaAdhamu vachakohwa chibereko chekuora ikoko mukutongwa kusingaperi, nokuti kutongwa kwasvika uye nguva dzekuratidzwa dzasvika.

Zvirokwazvo, zvirokwazvo, nguva dzekutonga kukuru dzakaporofitwa kare naEnoki munyori (nokuti uchapupu hwake hwakapiwa kuchizvarwa chino), dzasvika panyika kunyange sezvakanyorwa uye vose vachazvidavirira. Avo vakagamuchira chipo changu cheruponeso uye vanowanikwa vakatendeka muna Kristu pakuonekwa kwangu vachashandurwa zvachose, vangave vakavata muna Kristu kana kuti vari vapenyu uye vachigara muna Kristu, avo vari muna Kristu pakuonekwa kwangu vachatorwa kudenga rematenga. Kuva muna Kristu ndiko kufamba nemweya wangu une simba, kunyange mutemo wemweya weupenyu! Uye avo vakararama vakarurama kusati kwazvarwa Kereke yangu uye kune vatsvene vekutambudzika ndiko kumuka kwevakarurama, uko vakarurama vachamukira kuutsva hweupenyu mumubairo mukuru, usingaperi uye wekusingaperi. Pakupera kwekutonga kwangu kwemakore 1000 kuchava nekutonga kukuru kwechigaro chichena uye vose vakararama uye vakafara mukusarurama nezvivi vacharipirwa maererano nemabasa avo uye vazhinji vachawira murufu rwechipiri nokuti ndizvo zvavakasarudza.

Uye saka nguva dzekuratidzwa dzasvika, uye vhangeri risingaperi razarurirwa panguva yaro kunyange kabhuku kari muruoko rwemutumwa wangu ane simba zvino razarurwa uye kutaura kwemabhanan'ana manomwe kwaziviswa! Nokuti basa iri ibasa risingaperi, uye ndiko kuunganidzwa kwezvibvumirano nezvipikirwa zvekare muzvibvumirano nezvipikirwa zvezvitsva, kunyange Testamente Yangu Itsva yandakasimbisa neropa rangu. Nokuti ini Yeshua HaMashiach, kunyange Jesu Kristu Akarurama uyo ari Mambo weMadzimambo naShe weMadzishe, ndini iye akapiwa kuzadzikiswa kwezvipikirwa zvose nezvibvumirano zvose.

Nokuti Johane Muratidzwi akanyora zvaakaona, uye saka akanyora kuti: Zvino ndakaona mumwe Mutumwa une simba, achiburuka kudenga, akafukidzwa negore; muraraungu wakanga uri pamusoro wake, chiso chake chakanga chakaita sezuva, uye tsoka dzake seshongwe dzomoto. Wakanga ane kabhuku kakazarurwa muruoko rwake; akaisa rutsoka rwake rworudyi pagungwa, norworuboshwe panyika. Uye zvakare Johane Muratidzwi akazivisa kuti: Zvino ndakaona mumwe Mutumwa achibhururuka pakati pedenga, ane vhangeri risingaperi, kuti aparidzire vanogara panyika, nokumarudzi ose, nendimi dzose, navanhu vose, achiti nenzwi guru, Ityai Mwari, mumukudze, nokuti nguva yokutonga kwake yasvika; munamate iye wakasika denga nenyika, negungwa, nezvinyuka zvemvura.

Uye saka zvakanyorwa uye saka zvaitwa. Vhangeri risingaperi rasvika pakuzarurirwa panguva yaro uye kabhuku hakuchisiri chakavanzika. Mutumwa ane simba uyo akafukidzwa negore uye muraraungu wakanga uri pamusoro wake uye chiso chake chakanga chakaita sezuva uye tsoka dzake seshongwe dzomoto. Muraraungu chiratidzo chakapiwa pachibvumirano chaNoa icho Yahweh akaita naNoa mushure memafashamo kuti haaizombofashamidzizve nyika yose. Gore neshongwe dzomoto zvinomiririra chibvumirano chaMwari naMosesi, chakavakirwa pamusoro pezvibvumirano zvekare. Nokuti Mosesi wakati ataura murayiro umwe neumwe kuvanhu vose sezvinoreva murayiro, akatora ropa remhuru nerembudzi, nemvura, nemvere dzemakwai tsvuku, nehisopi, akasasa bhuku, navanhu vose uye saka Testamente Yekare yakasimbiswa!

Uye sezvakanyorwa muna VaHebheru: Nokuti kana ropa renzombe nerembudzi, namadota etsiru anosaswa pane vasina kuchena, zvichitsvenesa pakucheneswa kwenyama: Ko ropa raKristu, iye wakazvipa amene kuna Mwari asina gwapa noMweya usingaperi, haringatonyanyi kuchenesa hana dzenyu pamabasa akafa, kuti mushumire Mwari mupenyu here? Uye nokuda kwechikonzero ichi ndiye murevereri wetestamente itsva, kuti nenzira yerufu, nokuda kwerudzikinuro rwekudarika kwakanga kuri pasi petestamente yekutanga, avo vakadanwa vagamuchire chipikirwa chenhaka isingaperi.

Uye saka sezvakanyorwa ndizvo zvazvakaitwa vakatendeka vangu, uye ndizvo zvakaita Testamente Itsva. Uye kunyange sezvo ari El Shaddai akaraira kuti chiedza chivheneke murima pakutanga, nenzira imwecheteyo Mwari Wemasimbaose akavhenekera mumwoyo yedu kuti ape chiedza cheruzivo rwekubwinya kwaMwari, pachiso chaJesu Kristu. Saka ndizvo, muchibvumirano changu chitsva chine simba rakasimba, sezvo ndakachisimbisa neropa rangu risina chivi, zvinozadzikiswa zvibvumirano zvose zvenguva dzose, nokuti ndakataura paKarivhari mukufema kwangu kwekufa: Zvapera. Uye saka chibvumirano chitsva chakasimbiswa neropa rangu kuva Testamente Itsva!

Uye saka mandiri, Kristu Jesu, sungano dzekare dzinozadzikiswa mukubwinya kunoonekwa pachena, kunyange pamberi pechiso chemuponesi wenyu wekusingaperi naMesia, iyeni Yeshuah HaMashiach. Uye mutumwa uyu Johane Muratidziri anorondedzera aiva netsoka imwe pagungwa uye imwe tsoka panyika nekuti ruponeso rwangu rwakazarukira vose avo vanobva kugungwa remarudzi, (veDzimwe Ndudzi) neavo vanobva kunyika yechipikirwa, kunyange vose vakandiita Ishe wavo wekusingaperi, kunyange vazhinji sekudanwa kuchaitwa naIshe Mwari wedu! Uye saka vhangeri rangu risingaperi richawana meso, nzeve, nemoyo yevose kwarinotumirwa uye vazhinji vachauya kuruponeso nekuti shoko rega rega raMwari irarama uye risingaperi uye hapana shoko rimwe chete raYahweh richatongokundikana kana kupfuura kusvikira zvese zvazadzikiswa. Nekuti kuna Yahweh zvipikirwa zvese ndizvo hongu naamen uye zvese zvichazadzikiswa uye ini, Kristu Jesu, ndakazvipa mukurwadziwa kukuru kuti ndisimbise sungano yangu itsva muropa rangu risina chivi, kamwe chete zvachose uye kusvika mukusingaperi kusingagumi testamente itsva ichararama.

Saka inzwai mashoko angu uye munzwisise kutaura kwangu, kunyange izwi raIshe wenyu naMesia. Nguva dzasvika vana vangu uye mashoko angu azadza nyika uye vaporofita vangu nevashumiri vangu vafukidza magumo enyika, uye mumitauro yose, nendimi, nemarudzi vhangeri rangu reruponeso ranzwikwa, kusvika kumagumo enyika. Uye saka zviri saizvozvo, uye ndinozivisa kudaro: Nhau dzangu dzakanaka dzinogara dzakavakirwa panheyo yemurairo nevaporofita uye ini ndiri dombo guru rekona iro tembere yose yaMwari mupenyu inozorora pariri! Vana vangu, imi muri tembere yaMwari mupenyu!

Uye saka ndinokupai kuti munzwisise kuti kune zvikamu zvakawanda zvevhangeri raKristu! Makapusa zvakakwana here zvekutenda kuti kune mavhangeri mana chete? Uye kune zvimwe zvinhu zvakawanda zvandakaita, izvo, kana zvaizonyorwa chimwe nechimwe, ndinofunga kuti kunyange nyika pachayo yaisazokwanisa kuchengeta mabhuku aizonyorwa. Uye saka zviripo kuti kune nhau dzakanaka dzekuuya kwangu kwekutanga, kune nhau dzakanaka dzechakavanzika chaMwari chandakaratidza kuna Pauro Muapositori, kune vhangeri risingaperi rinotitungamirira kumagumo ezvinhu zvese. Kuna Yahweh hakuna magumo enhau dzakanaka!

Uye saka ndinokubvunzai mubvunzo? Imi muri chipangamazano chaYahweh here? Vafundisi venyu venhema nevashumiri venyu vanodziya vanokwanisa here kurayira Yahweh? Ndiwe ani, iwe munhu, kuti usarudze kuti Yahweh anotaura rini uye sei uye zviri mukati nemaitiro eMashoko ake, kana kuti kunaani waanosarudza kutumira mashoko ake? Uye saka zviri saizvozvo. Mashoko aMwari acharamba achiyerera uye zvipupuriro zvese zvichapedziswa uye mabhuku achanyorwa kusvika mukusingaperi nekuti Shoko raMwari rinorarama uye rinogara nekusingaperi!

Uye saka ndinokubvunzai, Testamente Itsva yaKristu Jesu yakadzima here Testamente Yekare nesungano dzekare? Mwari ngaasadaro! Shoko rega rega raMwari rakachena uye zvese zvichazadzikiswa kunyange kamwe chete, zvese, uye nekusingaperi! Uye ndizvo zvakaita Mwari asingachinji! Pedyo nechigaro chake cheushe pane zviuru gumi zvakapetwa nezviuru gumi asi haadi chipangamazano. Yahweh, Mwari mukuru wekusingaperi anoita zvinhu zvese maererano nezano rekuda kwake uye ndiye Mwari wekusingaperi! Asi akaratidza zvirongwa zvake, kunyange kuda kwake kwekusingaperi uye kusingagumi muzvikamu. Mungabvunza kuti sei Yahweh aizoita chinhu chakadaro? Hamuna kuverenga here zvakanyorwa naPauro Muapositori pandakamuita kuti anyore? Asi tinotaura uchenjeri hwaMwari muchakavanzika, kunyange uchenjeri hwakavanzwa, uhwo Mwari hwaakasarudza nyika isati yavapo, kuti tive nekubwinya kwedu. Uhwo husina kuzivikanwa nemachinda enyika ino: nekuti dai vakazviziva, vangadai vasina kuroverera Ishe wekubwinya.

Uye saka Mwari wekusingaperi, kunyange Yahweh asingadi chipangamazano uye muhuchenjeri hwake husingaperi anoratidza nzira dzake neMashoko ake nenzira yaanosarudza mukuzadzikiswa kwezvirongwa zvake zvisingaperi zverudzikinuro rwevanhu. Nzwisisai vadikani vangu, kunyange Mwenga wangu, kuti manga muri mumoyo maYahweh kubva kusati kwasikwa uye manga makavanzwa neuchenjeri mumoyo maBaba uye zvakavanzirwa imi zvamuchazova pakuonekwa kwangu, nekuti makafa uye upenyu hwenyu hwakavanzwa mandiri Kristu Jesu. Uye pandichaonekwa, nemiwo muchaonekwa neni mukubwinya! Vana vangu vadiki mava kuda kusikwa patsva zvizere mandiri uye muchashamiswa nezvamakagadzirirwa naYahweh avo vanomuda. Nekuti sezvazviri hakuna munhu panyika akambonyatsonzwisisa kuti vanakomana vaMwari vachazovei pakudzikinurwa kwavo kuzere, uye mava kuda kuzviziva nekuti zuva rasvika uye kuonekwa kwangu kwava pedyo.

Ndinorayira mumwe chete uye ndinorayira vose, kunyange Kristu Jesu Ishe wenyu wekusingaperi! Gamuchirai Mashoko angu uye muwane uchenjeri nekunzwisisa, nekuti hunhu hwenyu hwechokwadi chakavanzika chisingaperi chakavigwa muna Mwari pachake chava kuda kuratidzwa zvizere. Munguva pfupi iri kutevera muchazviwana makamira muimba chaiyo yechigaro cheushe chaYahweh, kunyange Baba vedu, uye vanomirira nechido uye vanoshuvira kuvepo kwenyu izvozvi, kunyange kubva mukusingaperi kwakapfuura. Uye dzimba huru dzekudenga dzakagadzirirwa kusvika kwenyu uye matafura akagadzirwa uye muchato weChikomba kuMwenga wake wava pedyo uye mwenga wangu azvigadzirira uye ndichavagamuchira kwandiri uye nekusingaperi vachagara neni musungano isingaperi uye vachaita mabasa mazhinji ane simba asina kumbopinda mumoyo yevanhu panguva ino.

Uye saka Yahweh Mwari mukuru wekusingaperi uye asingagumi akugadzirirai uye akakugadzirirai zvinhu zvakawanda, uye ini, Kristu Jesu Ishe akakwidziridzwa uye akakwira kudenga ndine mubairo wenyu muruoko rwangu kunyange kune vakatendeka vangu. Uye hondo yekudenga inomirira nechido kuvepo kwenyu uye mumubairo wekudenga uye wekusingaperi muchapinda zvino. Saka musatya nekuti upenyu hwenyu hwava kuda kuratidzwa uye chero sekondi ipi zvayo kubva zvino hwamanda yekupedzisira icharira, makagadzirira here kuonekwa kwangu vana vangu vadiki? Nekuti kwava pedyo kupfuura zvamunofungidzira uye nguva iri pakati pezvino naipapo haina basa uye inoshandira zvinangwa zvisingaperi zvaYahweh Mwari mukuru wekusingaperi!

Hamuzivi here? kana kuti hamuna kunzwa, kuti Mwari wekusingaperi, Ishe akasika magumo enyika? haaneti, kana kuneta uye kunzwisisa kwake hakugoni kutsvakwa.
