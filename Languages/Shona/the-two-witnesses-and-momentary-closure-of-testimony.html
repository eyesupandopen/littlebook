Zvapupu Zviviri neKuvharwa Kwechinguvana kweChipupuriro

Kubva kuna Yahweh, Iye Mukuru NDIRI:

Tarirai! Ndinouya kuzokuudzai nezvezvapupu zvangu zviviri, zvigadziko zvangu zviviri zvemwenje nemiti yangu miviri yemiorivhi uye kuti ndipe mapofu maziso kuburikidza nekuzivisa zvakavanzika zvakachengetwa zvakavanzika kubva kunyika muhuchenjeri hwangu, kuitira kuti muzive chokwadi chezvinhu zvichauya uye kuti nzira inoenda kuruponeso ijekeswe kune vose vanoverenga uye vane kutenda kuteerera Mashoko aya eEvhangeri isingaperi.

Tarirai! NDIRI Yahweh, iye akasika matenga nenyika nokuda kwezvinangwa zvangu zvisingaperi nembiri, uye NDIRI iye achadzorera chisikwa kubva pakuora, kuitira kuti nzira dzakarurama dzaYahweh dzichakurumidza kuzadza nyika mukururama pachiso chaJesu Kristu. Uye saka nzira dzangu dzichazadza nyika muHumambo hwaKristu Jesu hweMireniyamu, kunyange Humambo husingazopindi chinhu chakaipa, kunyange Humambo apo nyika inotongwa mukururama, uye rugare rwechokwadi nekudzoreredzwa zvichazadza nyika, uye vakatendeka vezvizvarwa zvose vachafamba imomo murugare rwakakwana nekunyarara nekuti nzira dzaYahweh dzichafukidza nyika mukururama. Kunyange nguva apo Satani akasungwa mukutambudzika nekutya kukuru nekutyisa kwenhaka yake iri kuuya yekurangwa kusingaperi, kwaachaona chikara nemuporofita wenhema vachikandwa mumirazvo yemoto yekurangwa kusingaperi vachipiswa, nekuti vose vakakodzera saizvozvo, asi achazadzisa zvinangwa zvangu zvisingaperi zvekururama asati asungwa nekukandwa mumoto nokusingaperi-peri kuti asazobvako, uye ndichaisa chisimbiso uye havazombofungwi nezvavo zvakare.

Uye ndichapa (nekunakidzwa) nezuva reSabata rekuzorora nevakundi vangu vakarurama nevose vanobvumirwa kupinda muHumambo hwaKristu Jesu hweMireniyamu! (Nekuti makore churu akaita sezuva rimwe pamberi pangu uye zuva rimwe semakore churu uye makore churu imireniyamu.) Kunyange vose avo vachange vakaunganidzwa nekumutswa kubva kuzvizvarwa zvose, zvazvino nezvakapfuura, muna Kristu Jesu; kunyange Satani asati akandwa mumirazvo yemoto, mwaka wake mudiki wekupedzisira usati wasvika. Pakupera kwazvo nyoka iyo yekare ichasungwa nokusingaperi nekukandwa mumoto, kuti isazombobva pakurangwa kwayo kusingaperi mumirazvo inopisa zvikuru yesarufa inopfuta. Nekuti Satani nevose vanotevera nhema dzake havana simba zvachose pamberi pangu kusingaperi! Ndiani angakunda Yahweh, Iye Mukuru NDIRI, kunyange vachireva nhema nekuzvirumbidza? Kana kuti ndiani angashandura kuda kwangu kusingaperi nezviziviso zvekutonga zvakagadzirirwa kuwira zvizere kana mudzivisi wangu abviswa kamwe kamwe zvino pakuridzwa kwehwamanda yakapiwa kuti irire panguva chaiyo yakakwana yandakasarudza muchirongwa changu chisingaperi kubvira kare kare. Kunyange nguva iri pedyo zvikuru yekuti isvike apo chipikirwa chakarurama chinosangana nekuzadzikiswa kusingaperi munguva dzazvino.

Nekuti NDIRI, Iye Mukuru NDIRI, kunyange iye anoziva magumo kubva pakutanga uye kutanga kubva kumagumo. Naizvozvo, ndinouya kuzoratidza zvimwe zvezvirongwa zvangu zvisingaperi zveruponeso zvakaramba zviri chakavanzika, kunyange zvinhu zvichiri zvakavanzwa muChakavanzika chaMwari chava pedyo kupedziswa mukururama kusingaperi. Kunyange sezvazvakanyorwa naJohane Muratidziri paakanyora akatendeka Mashoko aakapiwa naKristu Jesu! Uye saka zvakanyorwa kuti mumazuva ezwi reMutumwa wechinomwe, paachatanga kuridza hwamanda, kunyange chakavanzika chaMwari chichapedziswa, sezvaakazivisa kuvaranda vake Vaporofita.

Asi, vazhinji vakapofumadzwa kuchokwadi cheMashoko aya nen'anga dzenhema dzedhisipensesheni dzakabhadharwa nenhoo tsvuku kuti dzikanganise zvinyorwa zvangu zvitsvene nenhema dzedhisipensesheni neZionist nedzidziso dzenhema dzekutsiva, kunyange nhema dzakapihwa nehunyanzvi nevakawira kuburikidza nevanhu vakaipa kunyengedza mwoyo yevanyoro nevakaderera. Uye vachanyengedza vasanangurwa vangu chaivo kana vasanangurwa vangu vakasarudza kufuratira Mashoko angu ekururama mukuzvikudza vachifunga kuti havangatongonyengedzwi nekuda kwekusarudzwa kwavo. Asi vose vanokanganisa nenzira yakadaro nekuramba chokwadi vachifarira nhema dzavo vachanyengedzwa. Naizvozvo, nzwisisai vanhu vangu kuti hwamanda yechinomwe haisi hwamanda imwe chete yakapiwa kuti irire kupedza nguva dzangu dzenyasha nekuunganidza vakatendeka muna Kristu, saka musanyengedzwa!

Zvisinei, chokwadi chaYahweh chinomira chakasimba nokusingaperi uye hachichinjiki, uye Shoko rangu harigoni kuputswa, uye Shoko rangu richaita zvandinoda kune wese anenge atumirwa Shoko rangu. Uye hakuna munhu anogona kumisa zvirongwa zvangu zvisingaperi! Uye hakuna munhu anogona kumisa Kristu Jesu, maari makazara chirongwa changu chisingaperi cheruponeso. Kunyange iye wandakapa simba pamusoro pechisikwa chose muchisikwa changu uye ndikamupa kutonga kuzere pamusoro pechisikwa changu kusvikira vavengi vake vaitwa chitsiko chetsoka dzake, panguva iyoyo achapa Humambo hwakazviisa pasi kuna Adonai Yahweh.

Asi pachokwadi chisingaperi, hakuna magumo ekutonga kwakarurama kwaYeshua HaMashiach, uyo achatonga mukururama nokusingaperi-peri, kunyange nemwenga wake uyo achaenderera mberi naye nokusingaperi-peri uye nokusingaperi-peri mumahofisi avo ehutongi nemirairo yebasa. Uye izvozvo nekuti vakagamuchira Jesu weNazareta saIshe wavo wekusingaperi munguva dzenyasha netsitsi, uye vakamuda zvizere mukuteerera Mashoko ake, kunyange neMashoko iwayo avachange vakunda zvizere zvinhu zvose zvehumambo hwevavengi nokusingaperi-peri.

Nekuti pachokwadi chisingaperi, jasi raMerkizedeki rakagara zvakasimba pamapfudzi aYeshua HaMashiach asingaperi, kuti risazombobva! Nekuti nokusingaperi-peri uye nokusingaperi-peri Mambo weKururama achatonga nokusingaperi-peri nemurairo wake weVapristi, Madzimambo, neVaporofita avo vachazadza nyika nenzira dzakarurama neruzivo rwaYahweh, kunyange kusvika kunguva apo vaporofita vachaguma nokusingaperi-peri nekuti ndichaita ugaro hwangu nevanhu mudenga idzva nenyika itsva zvava pedyo kusikwa mukururama neutsvene hwechokwadi! Kunyange chisikwa apo ruzivo rwezvakanaka nezvakaipa ruchaguma nokusingaperi-peri uye kururama chete nerudo nerugare zvichawanikwa nokusingaperi-peri zvisingazombobvi kana kuderedzwa kana kushomeka kana kuoreswa zvishoma. Kunyange zvakadaro nekuti uori huchakandwa mumirazvo yemoto nokusingaperi-peri.

Asi, ndizvo zvazviri, kuti vakawira vakafukidza nekuparadzira nhema dzavo pamusoro pechisikwa changu kunyange vachidzitepfenyura zvisingamiri pamusoro pematekinoroji avo egehena, kuitira kuti munhu wenyama adzorwe nekushandiswa nemafrequency avo nematekinoroji egehena. Kunyange mifananidzo inodzora pfungwa nekuchinja maitiro, nhoroondo dzakaipa, nezvitsinga zvinotepfenyurwa pazviratidziro zvavo zvitema apo vazhinji vanogara murima vakabatwa nemifananidzo yavo nenhema. Uye pachokwadi, vakawira vakafadza mapoka evanhu nechepfu nepharmakeia inopfuudzwa semushonga, kuitira kuti vaiswe chepfu nekuteurwa neropa nazvo. Asi, chepfu dzavo nematekinoroji nezviratidziro zvavo zvichaparadzwa nokusingaperi-peri, uye kutepfenyura kwavo kuchaguma nokusingaperi, kwavanokanda zvidhoma zvavo nekumutsa zvishuwo neruchiva rwemunhu wenyama muchivi chikuru nematekinoroji avo asina maturo zvichashaya basa, zvisingazovipo zvakare.

Asi, ndizvo zvazviri, kuti ndakabvumira vakawira kuti vafukidze chisikwa changu nenhema dzavo, kuitira kuti vose vaizotevera nzira dzavo dzinonyangadza vasarudze kugara munhaka yavo isingaperi yesarufa inopfuta nekuti vakafadzwa nekusarurama nekutadzira Yahweh, Iye Mukuru NDIRI, uye nevasina mhosva muchisikwa changu, avo vavakatadzira. Uye muhuchenjeri hwangu husingaperi nerudo rwechisikwa changu, ndakabvumira mumwe nemumwe kubva kuna Adhamu kuti asarudze waachashandira: kana chivi kusvika parufu, kana kuteerera kururama kusvika kuupenyu husingaperi.

Ndokudaro nzira dzaYahweh, nekuti nzira dzangu inzira dzekururama, rugare, nerudo! Kunyange rudo rusingaperi uye rusingagumi, apo chisikwa changu chose, chakaumbwa neuto rezvisikwa zvakasiyana-siyana uye zvakaitwa zvinoshamisa zvemarudzi ose nezvinangwa, chichararama mukuwirirana kwakarurama nerudo. Nekuti chisikwa changu chakaitirwa semucheka wakabatanidzwa wehupenyu unotongwa mukururama nerudo kuitira kubatsirwa kuzere nembiri yechisikwa changu chimwe nechimwe, apo pasina chisikwa chichazombobatsirwa nekurasikirwa kwechimwe, asi vose vachagovana vakasununguka kubva pakuwanda kusingaperi kuri mumatura angu kwakaitirwa kubatsira nembiri yeavo vanoteerera mirairo yangu yakarurama kubva pamwoyo yavo murudo kuna Baba vavo vekusingaperi, avo zvechokwadi, NDIRI!

Asi, ndizvo zvazviri, kuti NDIRI Iye Mukuru NDIRI uye handitsigiri kana kubvumirana nenhema dzavo, uye handizobvumiri nhema dzavo kuti dzienderere mberi. Saka ndizvo zvazviri, kuti ndinozivisa, kuti ini, Yahweh, Iye Mukuru NDIRI uyo ari Mwari weKururama wekusingaperi ndichafumura zvizere zvese zvehumambo hwevavengi hwakagadzirirwa kusimuka kwemwaka mupfupi zvikuru uyo dhiragoni yakapiwa kutonga, uye kutonga kushoma zvikuru kwaachava nako. Uye saizvozvowo, ndichafumura vakawira vakazvivanza pakati pechisikwa changu, avo vanofanira kudzupurwa nekufumurwa mukunyara kwavo kusingaperi zvizere vasati vakandwa mumirazvo yemoto yekurangwa nokusingaperi-peri, apo honye yavo isingafi. Uye saka chiziviso changu chichaenda mberi, nekuti zvakaziviswa mumbiri nesimba rechigaro cheushe chaYahweh, kunyange Iye Mukuru NDIRI, hazvigoni kuchinjwa kana kushandurwa, uye zvichaitika chaizvo sezvakaziviswa. Sera!

Asi, ndizvo zvazviri, kuti handina kumbovanza vakawira zvizere, uye ndakatotanga kufumura kwavo kuzere uye chifukidzo chiri kutetepa. Kunyange chifukidzo ichocho chekuvanza chandakavavanzira nacho kubva pakuona kwevanhu, uye mudzivisi achabviswa uyo akaumbwa nenzvimbo dzakasiyana-siyana dzekudzivisa dzichabviswa zvizere kana Mikaeri apihwa murairo kana hwamanda yekupedzisira yarira kamwe kamwe zvino. Uye saka aizi ichawira, uye nyika ichashandurwa, uye vachava nehondo yavo huru uye vazhinji vachaparara nazvo, kunyange sezvakanyorwa nekufanotaurwa naJohane Muratidziri muchipupuriro chake chisingaperi. Uye saka ndinokupai kuti munzwisise kuti panguva yekuunganidzwa kwevakatendeka muna Kristu kuti ndichabvisa chifukidzo ichocho chekuvanzika kwechikamu kamwe kamwe uye nesimba sekubvarura kwandakaita chifukidzo chetembere nepakati kubva kumusoro kusvika pasi izvo zvakaratidza kupera kwebasa raRevhi nekudzoreredzwa kuzere kwaMerkizedeki. Kunyange kuitira kuti ndibvise upofu hwechikamu kubva kuvana vaIsraeri nokusingaperi-peri, kuti husazombodzoka kunjiva dzangu.

Uye izvi zvichaitika kamwe kamwe uye zvisingatarisirwi nekuti nyika haitarisiri kuti Kristu Jesu ajekese zvizere madokero nematenga nembiri yake isingaperi munguva idzi dzekupedzisira dzemarwadzo ekuzvara, kunyange zvakadaro, nekuti nyika zvechokwadi haina kutenda. Uye veavo vanorambirwa kupinda mumatenga kana gonhi iroro guru ravharwa, vazhinji vari vatadzi vakaipa vachaparara nekutorwa mukutongwa nokusingaperi-peri, kunyange avo vakaramba Jesu weNazareta zvizere mukumhura kukuru nekuzvikudza vachipesana neuta hwangu hutsvene, nevamwe vazhinji vakaita saivo vakaipa mukuramba kwavo kwekupanduka avo vachawira nokusingaperi mukutongwa kwakaomarara nekuda kwezvakaipa zvavakaita. Kunyange zvakaipa zvimwe chetezvo zvavanoenderera mberi nekuzvipira kukuru kuita, uye havazopukunyuki.

Nzwisisai kuti vose vanotsvaka kutyora miganhu yechisikigo nemirairo yechisikwa changu nekuyambuka miganhu iyoyo nemirairo, kunyange kutsvaka kuzvishandura kuva zvinonyangadza (munzira dzevakawira) vachawirawo saizvozvo mukutongwa kusingaperi zvakafanana: Nekuti ichi ndicho chivi chisingaregererwi, kunyange kumhura Yahweh uyo ari Mweya Mutsvene, kutsvaka kuzvishandura kubva pamufananidzo waYahweh wavakambosikwa nawo. Naizvozvo, rambai ngano dzavo dzechiJesuit dzakashandura humwari hwechokwadi kuva ngano dzakaunzwa naNimrodhi nevatatu vake vepakutanga vasina utsvene! Nekuti vatatu vatsvene veBaba, Mwanakomana, nemweya mutsvene vakashatiswa kubva pazvinoreva nezvavaida pakutanga, asi chokwadi chaYahweh hachigoni kuvanzwa nezvinhu zvisina simba uye zvechipemhi kunyange vachireva nhema! Zvisinei, Jesu weNazareta ndiYahweh akazviratidza munyama, uye mweya wangu mutsvene wehumwana unozadza zvizere vakatendeka muna Kristu avo zvechokwadi vari chikamu chehumwari mukutonga kwavo naKristu Jesu pasi peMurairo waMerkizedeki seVapristi neMadzimambo nokusingaperi-peri! Naizvozvo, nzwisisai kuti muri vanani muna Kristu vana vangu nekuti vakawira vanotsvaka kuvanza chokwadi pamusoro penyu nengano dzavo dzenhema.

Asi, vakatendeka muna Kristu vakararama vakafa uye vachiri kufamba pamusoro penyika mukutenda kukuru, uye nguva ino inoshamisa uye ine mbiri haizovabati mukusaziva nekuti vachanzwisisa kutaura kwekudanidzira ikoko kukuru, uye vachanzwisisa izwi rehwamanda yekupedzisira iyo yakatopiwa muruoko nemirairo yakazara yekuti irire kana murairo wapiwa naKristu Jesu, uyo anogara achiita zvakakwana kuda kwaAdonai Yahweh. Vakatendeka muna Kristu vachabvumirwa kupinda mugonhi iroro guru rinoenda kumatenga risingagoni kuvharwa kana kuvhurwa nemunhu upi zvake panyika, vangave vakarara kana vari vapenyu pamusoro penyika panguva yekudanwa kwavo!

Uye kutambudzika kukuru kuchabata vasina kutendeka avo vakapupura Jesu weNazareta kubva pamwoyo usina kutenda, kunyange kuramba kurarama hupenyu hwekutendeuka nekuti vaida zvivi zvavo. Zvichaitika kuti avo vakasarudza kuenderera mberi muchivi, avo nemwoyo isingateereri uye yakaoma vakapupura Jesu saIshe asi vakaenderera mberi mumabasa enyama vachasiirwa kumoto wekunatsa wekuparadzwa kwenyama, kuitira kuti vapukunyuke mafaro echivi uye kuti vapukunyuke nyama yavo yakavabata muruchiva rwayo. Uye vazhinji vachauya kuruponeso nekudana kuna Jesu weNazareta kusvika pakufema kwavo kwekupedzisira, nekuti zvagara zvichidiwa kune vakatendeka, kuti munhu awanikwe akatendeka kusvika pakufema kwake kwekupedzisira, kana asiri mumwe wevashoma vanotorwa vari vapenyu vachienda mukuchengeteka kwematenga munguva dzavo dzekutendeka. Sera!

Uye hondo yavo ichapisa uye ichaenderera mberi kwenguva yayo yakatarwa uye kubva mumadota ekuparadzwa kwavo vachamuka. Shato, chikara chinobva mugungwa nechikara chinobva panyika uyo ariwo muporofita wavo wenhema vachamuka mukunyumwira kukuru kuti vave nekutonga kwavo kwehutsinye asi kupfupi nenyasha. Zvisinei, Yahweh, INI NDINI MUKURU, haana kumbosiya asina chapupu, sezvinopupurirwa neboka guru rezvapupu muzvinyorwa zvangu zvisingaperi uye zvitsvene zvakachengetedzwa zvakakwana kumatenga. Uye sezvazviri, zvizhinji zvezvapupu zvangu zvakarasika nekufamba kwenguva nekuregeredzwa neavo vakapiwa basa rekuzvichengeta. Nzwisisai makore enhoroondo vana vangu uye rambai nhema dzavo dzekunyepa dzemadhisipensesheni. Nzwisisai kuti chisikwa hachina mamiriyoni emakore sekureva kwenhema dzevakawa. Chisikwa chine makore asingasviki zviuru zvitanhatu uye ndakakamura nguva idzi kuita makore enhoroondo. Paive nenguva yekusagadzikana kukuru nekuda kwevakawa vakasvibisa nyika yaivepo panguva iyoyo nezvinyangadzo zvavo, kunyange zvinyangadzo izvo zvakaparadzwa zvizere mukutongwa kwakazoguma nemafashamo makuru mumazuva aNoa.

Asi, Noa akaramba ari mupenyu panguva yemafashamo, akatoponeswa ari mupenyu muAreka nevana vake (nemhuka nezvisikwa) zvakapiwa nzvimbo yekuenderera mberi nekuti Noa aive murume akarurama uye mutongi akarurama maererano neHurongwa hwaMerkizedeki, uyo jasi rakazorora pamapfudzi ake kusvikira muranda wangu aenda kuzororo rake rerunyararo kusvikira amutswa munguva pfupi iri kutevera mukururama. Nekufamba kwenguva Mosesi akadhonzwa ari mupenyu kubva mumvura dzeNire, uye akasimuka nesimba guru uye akapinza nguva yangu yeTorah, kunyange apo Israeri yakaitwa rudzi rukuru uye rune simba runova mumvuri wezvinhu zvichauya.

Uye panguva yakakwana uye yakatarwa chaizvo Jesu weNazareta akaberekwa nemhandara uye akasimuka kuti ave gwayana raMwari, achizvipira murudzikinuro rusingaperi rwechirango chaMosesi, kunyange chibayiro chandakagamuchira nekusingaperi kwekuregererwa kwezvivi nekusingaperi, kune vose vanoitwa vatsvene nacho, kunyange muTestamente yake Itsva yekururama iyo Kristu Jesu akasimbisa uye akaisa chisimbiso nekusingaperi muropa rake risina chivi, naizvozvo, hapachina kudikanwa kwechibayiro chemhuka. Zvirokwazvo, Jesu weNazareta ndiye musimboti wekururama kwenguva dzose, kunyange panguva yakazoguma nekukundwa kwevakawa nekusingaperi, kunyange panguva yakatorwa neMashoko asingaperi "ZVAPERA!"

Uye saka nguva yangu yenyasha yakatanga pazuva rePendekosti pandakapa Kristu Jesu mweya wangu mutsvene weUwana waakadurura pamurairo wangu werudzikinuro rwevose vakasarudza kuva vanakomana vaMwari vakapiwa nemweya weUwana panguva idzi. Uye ndiko kwaive kuberekwa kweChechi yaKristu Jesu yakapiwa nyasha netsitsi kune vemamwe marudzi, kunyange kutonga kwakaomarara uye kwekupedzisira kusati kwasvika. Kunyange nguva dzetsitsi nenyasha apo marudzi akarasika eimba yaIsraeri aigona kuunganidzwa muIsraeri yaMwari, pamwe chete nemunhu wese anobva kumarudzi akadanwa uye akadavira nekuita Jesu weNazareta Ishe wavo wekusingaperi. Uye vanhu vangu vakaparadzirwa mukurangwa uye vakadzungaira panyika sevafambi munyika dzisingazivikanwi, uye vose vanobva kuna Adama vakagamuchira ruponeso panguva idzi vachava chikamu cheIsraeri yaMwari nekusingaperi, vasingazombobvi pamirairo yavo yekusingaperi yekushumira Kristu Jesu Ishe wavo akakwidzwa!

Saka nzwisisai kuti nyika iripo zvino yasvika kumagumo ayo ekusingaperi uye nyika ino yakaipa iripo haizombovipozve kuti inetse avo vandinoda nekusingaperi. Uye saka nguva yangu yenyasha inoenderera mberi kusvika pasekondi yekupedzisira apo hwamanda yekupedzisira yenguva yenyasha inorira iyo yakatopiwa kare kuti iridzwe. Pakupera kwedambudziko raJakobho, rinovawo vhiki raDhanieri rechimakumi manomwe, kunyange vhiki remakore apo zuva rega rega riri gore muvhiki remazuva manomwe, Umambo hwaYeshua HaMashiach hweMireniyamu huchamiswa kwezuva rekuzorora iro pakupera kwaro pachange pave nemakore zviuru zvinomwe. Naizvozvo, nzwisisai kuti pachange pave nenguva nhatu dzemakore zviuru zviviri imwe neimwe, nezuva resabata remakore churu richaitika mukupedzisa kutsvene uye kwakarurama apo chisikwa changu chinodzikinurwa zvizere kubva kuchivi, chisingazotadzizve.
