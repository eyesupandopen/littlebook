Chapupu Chiviri: Israeri
Zvinovava Zvinotapira
Mbudzi 17, 2024

Tarirai! Shumba yeRudzi rwaJudha!

Dzururai nzeve dzenyu, uye ndibvumirei kuti ndivhure maziso enyu, imi Israeri, uye mukai kubva muupofu hwenyu hwechikamu uye munzwe izwi raMesiasi wenyu wechokwadi, iyo Shumba yeRudzi rwaJudha, nekuti ndini iye. Saka nzwisisai mashoko aYeshua HaMashiach, iye wamunofanirawo kuzvidavirira kwaari, imi Israeri, nekuti ndini iye.

Zvirokwazvo, zvirokwazvo, inzwai mashoko angu hama dzangu kuti munzwisise nguva dzamunorarama madziri uye kuti munzwisise tariro yekudanwa kwenyu kuri kudzokera kunyika yechipikirwa, nekuti ndini tariro yekudanwa kwenyu, uye ndiri kukudanai, imi Israeri, kuti munzwisise zvichauya uye nehasha dzichaburitswa munguva idzi dzamunorarama. Uye ndini Shumba yeRudzi rwaJudha uye mukururama ndinouya kuzotonga nekuita hondo, saka nzwisisai kuomarara kweMashoko angu, iwo Mashoko aIye wamunofanirawo kuzvidavirira kwaari nekuti ndizvo zvakaitwa naYahweh kuti ndive.

Nekuti ndini dombo iroro rinogumbusa, iro dombo guru rinogumbura apo vose vasina kutenda vachagumburwa, nekuti vakarurama havachirarami naMosesi asi vakarurama vachararama nekutenda! Nekuti Mosesi anogara muna Mesiasi wenyu wechokwadi, iye akachengeta zvakakwana murairo chaiwo usingagoneki kuti Israeri achengete, uye kunyange naAdhamu wose kuti achengete nekuda kwechivi munyama yenyu yakaora. Uye ndini iye akabhadhara chirango chaMosesi pandakazvipa segwayana raMwari kuna Yahweh muchibayiro chekuyananisa chisingaperi nekuti chirango chevatadzi chakanga chisiri changu kubhadhara, asi murudo rwangu kuna Yahweh, uye nerudo rwangu kuvanhu ndakabhadhara zvirango zvenyu zvizere. Kunyange zvirango zvekutambudzika kukuru, kurwadziwa, nerufu ndakabhadhara zvizere uye Yahweh akagamuchira chibayiro changu nokusingaperi-peri.

Saka nzwisisai, hasha dzeGwayana dzave kuda kusunungurwa uye hwamanda dzave kuda kuridzwa, uye tarirai, ndiro dzekutsamwa kwehasha dzaMwari dzinoswedera zvirokwazvo uye dziri pamukova penyu, naizvozvo, inzwai izwi rangu kuti imi nevana venyu murarame uye mupukunyuke musungo uyo Barabhasi anoshanda kuisa kuvanhu vangu, iro gwayana reshato rakasarudzwa naEsau seMoshiach wavo, uyo ariwo muvaki wetemberi yechikara. Uye achasimuka segwayana rine nyanga mbiri, uye anouya kuzokamura nyika zvakare kubva kuvagari vemo vakadzingwa nekuparadzwa kwemarudzi avo, kuti agovane pakati pevagari vechibharo vanobva kuna Esau vakatora zvisiri pamutemo, nevapambi vakatumwa neshato vari vazukuru vaAshkenazi kwete vaJakobho asi vakazvibatanidza naEsau netsika dzavo dzeTalmud.

Uye ndini iye Yahweh waakatuma segwayana raMwari, sechisungo cherunyararo chakakwana, sePaseka yenyu yakakwana uye kunyange chibayiro chekupedzisira chezvivi zvevanhu kamwe chete, zvose, uye nokusingaperi-peri uye hapachina chimwe chibayiro chezvivi. Uye ndakauya kune vangu ndine rudo nerunyararo, avo vakaramba chibayiro changu, asi ndakatumwa sePaseka yakakwana mukuteerera kuda kwaYahweh, uye ndini gwayana raMwari, iro Yahweh raakagovera zvakakwana nokuda kwezvivi zvenyu, naizvozvo inzwai izwi rangu imi Israeri uye munzwisise kuti handichauyi panyika segwayana raMwari, uye runyararo ruchabviswa panyika mukutonga kwauya. Kunyange kutonga ikoko kuchaburitswa pakarepo kutanga paimba yaMwari nekuti kutonga kunotanga paimba yaMwari.

Nekuti vakatendeka muna Kristu vachatorwa kuenda kumatenga, uye vanodziya vachasiirwa kumoto wekunatsa wekuparadzwa kwenyama, kuti mweya uponeswe asi sokunge kuburikidza nemoto. Uye vazhinji-zhinji, mapoka makuru evanhu vachauya kuna Jesu weNazareta kubva kumarudzi ose nendimi dzose uye ndichavanatsa mukururama uye ndichavagamuchira kwandiri uye ndichavasika patsva mukururama neutsvene hwechokwadi uye mubairo wavo hauna kuenzaniswa nekutambura kwavachatsungirira, nekuti huremu hwemubairo wavo huchapfuura zvikuru zvinotarisirwa nevanhu uye vachararama nokusingaperi murunyararo rwakakwana neutsvene uye kururama kwechokwadi.

Nekuti zvakanyorwa pamusoro pavo uye zvichadaro: Ava ndivo vakabuda mukutambudzika kukuru, vakasuka nguo dzavo refu, vakachenesa nguo dzavo refu muropa reGwayana. Naizvozvo, vari pamberi pechigaro cheushe chaMwari, uye vanomushumira masikati nousiku muTemberi yake, uye iye anogara pachigaro cheushe achagara pakati pavo. Havachazovi nenzara, kana nyota zvakare, uye zuva harichavapisi, kana kupisa kupi zvako. Nekuti Gwayana, riri pakati pechigaro cheushe, richavatonga, uye richavatungamirira kuzvitubu zvipenyu zvemvura, uye Mwari achapukuta misodzi yose pamaziso avo.

Saka nzwisisai kuti ndini iye apedza mabasa angu pakati pevahedheni uye nguva dzangu dzenyasha netsitsi dzasvika kumagumo, kunyange nyasha netsitsi zviri neni nokusingaperi, uye Yahweh pachake akazivisa kuti achanzwira tsitsi waanoda kunzwira tsitsi, saka nyasha netsitsi zvinogara nokusingaperi. Zvirokwazvo, zvirokwazvo, ndakaenda kuvahedheni somunhu akadzingwa akarambwa nevangu vakatorwa naEsau muhusungwa hwetsika dzake dzinomhura dzaanotsvaga kuodza nadzo Mashoko aYahweh asingagumi uye anogara nokusingaperi, uko kuora kusingagoneki. Asi vakagadzira zvitendero zvavo zvegehena kuburikidza nevanyori vavo vakanyora tsika dzevanhu vakaipa mubhuku rinonzi idzvene, asi bhuku rekumhura ringava sei dzvene? Zvirokwazvo, zvirokwazvo, vane nhamo avo vanoti rima chiedza uye chiedza rima, nekuti kutongwa kwavo hakunonoki!

Uye nguva dzangu pakati pevahedheni dzasvika kumagumo nekuti vakawira muzvivi zvimwe chetezvo zvakawira Madzibaba enyu pavakaramba gwayana raMwari rakatumirwa kwavari naYahweh zvisina kufanira sechisungo cherunyararo chisingaperi kumarudzi, avo boka guru rakauya kuruoko. Uye zvino avo vangadai vakava vana vechipikirwa pakati peIsraeri yaMwari vakazvikudza pamusoro pematavi echisikigo emuorivhi wechisikigo kwavakaiswa panzvimbo apo matavi echisikigo akagurwa mukusatenda kumwe cheteko.

Asi vakatorwa kubva pamuorivhi wemusango, muti usina tariro kana chipikirwa uye veavo vakauya kwandiri vakandiita Ishe wavo wekururama asingagumi munguva dzerunyararo, nyasha, netsitsi zvino vatorwa muIsraeri yaMwari uye vanogoverana zvizere muzvipikirwa zvakaitwa kuMadzibaba uye vazhinji veava vachandishumira nokusingaperi semuviri wangu uye ndichavatora kwandiri semwenga wangu uye ndichaisa chisimbiso pavangu 144,000 vakazvinatsa muropa rangu rekuyananisa vakachena semhandara dzisina kusvibiswa nehure guru. Uye neruponeso rwevahedheni marudzi akaropafadzwa uye zvirokwazvo avo vemarudzi akarasika vakateerera izwi rangu vakauya kuruoko muna Ishe wavo uye vachararama nokusingaperi pakati pemarudzi muIsraeri yaMwari! Saka nzwisisai, ndichaponesa vamwe vevose vanobva kuna Adhamu kubva kwose kwaakadzungaira panyika mumazuva ake ekurangwa.

Nekuti Yahweh akandiita mweya unopa upenyu, uye handichazivikanwi maererano nenyama. Zvirokwazvo, zvirokwazvo, ndini iye zvino agura muorivhi wemusango nekuda kwekusatenda kwavo uye nekuti vakaputsa sungano yangu, iyo Testamente Yangu Itsva. Uye izvozvo nekuti vanofanira kuva naIshe mumwe chete, ini, Kristu Jesu Ishe wemadzishe naMambo wemadzimambo uye muhushe hwangu ndimo mune tariro yavo chete. Asi vakazvitorera tariro yenhema muna Barabhasi uye vakaisa tariro dzavo zvisina kuchenjera muna ishe wenhema akapinda muChechi dzavo netariro dzenhema dzekubudirira kwezvinhu, iye Muponesi wenhema anovimbisa kuponesa Bhabhironi Rakavanzika asi anovanda achishandira kuparadzwa kweBhabhironi Rakavanzika. Iye munyengeri mumwe cheteyo anova chikara chinobva panyika chichasimuka seMoshiach wenhema waEsau, iro gwayana reshato richasimuka riine nyanga mbiri. Uye saka Chechi yangu yakapindwa yeRaodikia yakatadza uye zvino yave kubvumirana nerufu rwayo, nekuti Barabhasi anongounza rufu, uye achaparadza chose uye kuparadza hure iroro guru, iro Bhabhironi Rakavanzika, rine kutongwa kwauya nekuda kwehuipi hwaro hukuru uye kuora kwarakaita nyika nahwo muhupombwe hwaro.

Saka vakazvisarudzira mukupanduka iye anounza kuparadzwa kwavo semuponesi wavo wekunyepedzera, nekuti Sinagogi raSatani vanyengeri vanonyengedzera vose. Saka dzidzai, imi Israeri, izvo Barabhasi ave kuda kuita nekuti akavatengesa mumaoko evavengi vavo, uye achanyepedzera ronda rake remusoro uye achazvivanza panyika kubva kunjodzi dzave kuda kurova. Uye achasimuka mukunyengera kukuru seMoshiach wenhema kuIsraeri nekuti vachava nehondo yavo, iyo hondo yavo yechitatu, uye ichava yakaipisisa pane nhatu. Zvakadaro, nekuti zvakanyorwa uye zvichaitwa saizvozvo: Uye ndakaona chikara chichibuda mugungwa, chine misoro minomwe, nenyanga gumi, uye panyanga dzacho paiva nekorona gumi, uye pamusoro pacho zita rekumhura. Uye chikara chandakaona chakanga chakafanana nembada, uye tsoka dzacho dzakaita sedzebheya, uye muromo wacho semuromo weShumba: uye shato yakachipa simba rayo nechigaro chayo cheushe, nesimba guru. Uye ndakaona mumwe wemisoro yacho wakanga wakuvadzwa kusvika pakufa, asi ronda racho rinouraya rakapora, uye nyika yose yakashamisika ikatevera chikara.

Uye saka ndizvo kuti mashoko ari mumagwaro anoburitsa unyengeri hwake, iwo Mashoko andakapa kuna Johane Muratidziri makore mazhinji apfuura. Uye zvichaonekwa sekuti Barabhasi achawana ronda remusoro, asi achareva nhema uye kunyengedza nyika iyo ichatenda zvizere zviratidzo zvake zvenhema nezvishamiso nekuti akapiwa izvozvo kuti akwezve vose vanofarira kusarurama kwaari mukunyengerwa kune simba, nekuti ndiye musvibisi wavo nemuparadzi wavo. Uye mumaoko ake anounza kurumwa nenyoka, iro jekiseni rechikara, uye vachasanganisa simbi nevhu kunyange vasingazonamatirani, asi gwayana reshato richasimuka saizvozvo. Uye achasimuka pamusoro pehurongwa hwekupedzisira hwechikara cheuyo akawa, huchava umambo hwavo hwekupedzisira kutonga nyika kubva kunyika yechipikirwa. Asi haazosimuki ari oga, asi achasimuka pamwe chete nechikara chinobva mugungwa, uye vachatonga mubatanidzwa wavo wemadzimambo akapa simba ravo rose nesimba kushhato nekuti vose vanonamata shato uye vakazvipira zvizere kwairi nekuti ndiyo Baba vavo, iye akadyara masora ake akaipa pakati pegorosi raYahweh, uye ndichavadzura chose ndovakanda mumoto.

Uye saka gwayana reshato richasimuka mukunyengera kuzere uye zviratidzo zvizhinji zvenhema nezvishamiso richaratidza kuratidza huporofita hwaro hwenhema, uye richaita zvirevo zvizhinji zvekuzvikudza nekumhura pamusoro paro kunyange richiti iro pacharo ndiMwari. Uye chimwe chikara chichanongedzera kune chimwe uye vachava nekutonga pamwe chete, kunyange maererano nezvirongwa zveshato iyo ichatonga umambo hwayo hwakaipa kwenguva pfupi. Uye vachaita zvinhu zvose zvechiKristu zvisiri pamutemo, uye vacharambidza zvinyorwa zvitsvene, uye zvakapiwa mumaoko avo kuti vaite hondo nevatsvene uye vavakurire, uye vachazviita saizvozvo. Asi vachabatanidza zvitendero zvenyika kuitira kuti vose vanamate mabhokisi avo matema nekuti patsoka dzaSatani vakazvibatanidza mukupanduka kwekupedzisira uye havangapunyuki ruoko rwangu rune simba, anodaro Mesiasi wenyu wekusingaperi, iye Yeshua HaMashiach!

Asi muruvengo rwavo rwezvinhu zvose zvine chekuita naKristu Jesu vachaita kuti ruponeso ruuye pamusoro peboka guru revanhu ravachauraya, kunyange avo vachadana zita raJesu weNazareta kusvika pakufema kwavo kwekupedzisira uye ndichavasimbisa uye ndovapa tariro huru, uye vachauya kwandiri zvizere, kunyange vose vari vagari venhaka yeruponeso rusingaperi, uye hapana anokwanisa kuvabvuta mumaoko angu uye vose vane mazita akanyorwa mubhuku reupenyu regwayana vachaponeswa. Saka usanetseka pamusoro pevadikani vako vanoita sevakarasika, nekuti vazhinji vavo vari mumaoko angu ane simba uye ndichavaunza kuruoko muna Kristu Jesu uye mumutauro upi zvawo wavanodana zita rangu rine simba ndichavanzwa!

Asi, zvichava saizvozvo kuti gwayana renhema rine nyanga mbiri richasimuka kutonga nyika kubva kunyika yechipikirwa iyo Esau anotsvaga kutora seyakewo, asi zvirevo zvake ndezvenhema uye kodzero yehudangwe yaJakobho yakasimba nokusingaperi-peri, iyo kodzero yehudangwe yakatengeswa naEsau nechidimbu chechingwa. Nekuti Esau akazvibatanidza neavo vakawa uye akazvibatanidza naAshkenazi avo vakazvitorera tsika nemaitiro emuKanani akaipa asi vanoreva nhema vachiti ndeveIsraeri asi vasiri. Nekuti aiva Kanani akachiva nzira dzeavo vakawa mushure memafashamo makuru, nokudaro achishandura vana vake nokusingaperi kuti vave mukutongwa kusingaperi nekuti vose vanozvishandura pachavo kubva pamufananidzo waMwari wavakagadzirwa nawo havana kuregererwa nekuti idzi ndidzo nzira dzaSatani dzakanyorwa naEsau muchikamu muTalmud yake inomhura kunobva kunyengera kwake.

Uye saka Ashkenazi, anobva kuna Jafeti, akabatana naEsau muzvirevo zvake zvenhema zvenhaka, umo vanotsvaga kuba nhaka yaJakobho kubva kuvana vechipikirwa. Zvisinei, kuedza kwakadaro hakuna maturo uye hakungamiri nekuti ndini iye Yahweh waakapa zvinhu zvose mumaoko angu ane simba uye anokwanisa. Saka nzwisisai kuti ndini iye akagara nhaka yezvinhu zvose zvakapikirwa kuna Jakobho uye ndini Mesiasi wenyu wechokwadi, uye mumaoko angu mune mibairo yenyu imi Israeri uye mumaoko aBarabhasi kuparadzwa kwenyu kusingaperi kunomirira, naizvozvo musabatana naye!

Saka nzwisisai kuti mibairo yechipikirwa yakaitwa kuMadzibaba ekare yakasimba mumaoko angu uye hapana anokwanisa kuba izvo zviri zvangu pamutemo nekuti ndiro simba randakapiwa naIYE MUKURU uye ndichapa nhaka iyi kuvana vechipikirwa, vakarurama vanorarama nekutenda muMwanakomana waYahweh, akavada uye akazvipa nokuda kweruponeso rusingaperi rwevana vechipikirwa, naizvozvo imi Israeri ndigamuchirei saMesiasi wenyu kuti imi nevana venyu murarame uye mubudirire munzira. Uye ndichakuchengetai uye ndichakuchengetedzai kuti musvike pamugove wenyu wakazara wenhaka muumambo hwangu hwemakore ane chiuru huri pamukova penyu. Saka inzwai kudanwa kwangu, imi Israeri! Nekuti ndiri kukudanai kuti muzviise pasi peruoko rwangu rune simba kuti nemiwo muve vana vechipikirwa nekuti Mosesi anochengetwa zvizere nekutenda mandiri, Yeshua HaMashiach, naizvozvo, zororai mumabasa angu uye regai enyu.

Uye saka Ashkenazi akabatana naEsau muzvirevo zvake zvenhema, umo vanotsvaga kuba nhaka yaJakobho kubva kuvana vechipikirwa. Zvisinei, kuedza kwakadaro hakuna maturo uye hakungamiri nekuti ndini iye Yahweh waakapa zvinhu zvose mumaoko angu ane simba uye anokwanisa. Saka nzwisisai kuti ndini iye akagara nhaka yezvinhu zvose zvakapikirwa kuna Jakobho, uye ndini Mesiasi wenyu wechokwadi, uye mumaoko angu mune mubairo wenyu imi Israeri uye mumaoko aBarabhasi kuparadzwa kwenyu kunomirira. Naizvozvo, nzwisisai sarudzo yamunofanira kuita, uye iye wamunofanirawo kuzvidavirira kwaari, nekuti ndini iye akatumwa naYahweh nokuda kweruponeso rwenyu, iye Mosesi waakaraira kuteerera kwenyu, imi hama dzangu dzisingateereri.

Uye saka sezvazvakanyorwa pamusoro pavo uye zvichazadzikiswa neruoko rwangu rune simba, nekuti Yahweh akaratidza chokwadi chaEsau kuvaporofita vake mumazuva ekudarika kwavo kwakanga kuri kuzhinji. Nekuti zvakanyorwa kare: Nekuda kwehutsinye hwako pamusoro pehama yako Jakobho, kunyara kuchakufukidza, uye uchagurwa nokusingaperi. Pawakamira kune rumwe rutivi, pazuva iro vatorwa vakatakura pfuma yake, uye vatorwa vakapinda pamasuwo ake, vakakanda mijenya pamusoro peJerusarema, kunyange iwe wakanga wakafanana nomumwe wavo.

Saka nzwisisai, imi vaIsraeri, kuti kupatsanurwa kwekupedzisira kwaJakobho naEsau kwasvika pamuri, uye ndinouya kuzochenesa nyika yechipikirwa kubva mumaoko evakawa, uye Esau achadzurwa zvachose, uye pamusoro paEsau zvakanyorwa kuti: Uye imba yaJakobho ichava moto, neimba yaJosefa murazvo, neimba yaEsau semashanga, uye vachavabatidza nokuvaparadza; uye hapazovi nomusaririra weimba yaEsau, nekuti Ishe vakataura izvozvo. Saka nzwisisai imi vekwaEsau munoverenga Mashoko angu, izvo zvakataurwa naYahweh hapana anogona kuzvishandura uye magumo enyu achava ekupedzisira uye muchadzurwa zvachose uye hamuzovipo zvakare.

Nekuti ndini Shumba yeRudzi rwaJudha uye mukururama ndinouya kuzotonga nekuita hondo. Saka inzwai Mashoko angu, imi vaIsraeri, muzvivande kubva pazuva rasvika pamuri. Muzvivande kubva kumunhu wechivi anouya munzira yenyu, nekuti Bharabhasi anouya nerufu rusingaperi mumaoko ake rwunopiwa semushonga unoshamisa unovimbiswa zvenhema kupa upenyu husingaperi. Asi havazovi noupenyu hwekurarama murufu rwechipiri asi kuparadzwa kunopfuurira mumirazvo inopisa zvikuru yekurangwa uko honye yavo isingafi.

Ndini iye achavhuna zvisimbiso pamupumburu wangu wenhaka, iro gwaro renyika chaiyo randakatenga neropa rangu rekuyananisa kubva muruoko rwaYahweh pachake. Uye ndinouya kuzodzura vagari zvisiri pamutemo nevose vakatumwa nevakawa, uye kunyange vakawa vacho pachavo nenguva dzavo dzapera. Saka nzwisisai zvichauya, imi vaIsraeri, nekuti vachamisa umambo hwavo uye vachaonekwa sevakundi uye vacharatidza zviratidzo zvizhinji zvenhema nezvishamiso kuitira kuti vanyengere vose vanofarira kusarurama. Uye vachafunga kuti vamisa kutanga kwenguva yavo yendarama yavanoshuvira kumisa, nekuti ndeveBaba vavo dhiabhorosi, uye dhiabhorosi ndiye shato huru iyo, naizvozvo, vachaita kuchiva kweshato.

Asi, havazovi nenguva yendarama asi kutongwa kwakaomarara uye kwakasimba chete, uko kutongwa kuchatanga nekukurumidza apo vakatendeka vangu muna Kristu vanounganidzwa kwandiri mumakore pandinozviratidza izvo nyika ichaona. Asi, vachanyepa vouudza nyika kuti vavengi vekune dzimwe nyika vakaba vazhinji kubva panyika uye vachadzoka uye nyika inofanira kunge yakagadzirira kurwisa vapambi vekunyepedzera ava. Asi muchokwadi vari kuzvigadzirira kurwa kwezuva iro guru, iro zuva rekuuya kwangu kwechipiri, zuva iro shiri dzedenga dzichadya zvikuru nyama yemadzimambo avo akawa epanyika uye zvichadaro.

Uye sezvo kutongwa kunowedzera, sezvo zvisimbiso zvichivhunwa (vatasvi vemabhiza vachatasva) uye hwamanda dzicharira uye zvichaitika sekunyorwa kwaJohane Muratidziri uye zvakare maererano neMashoko eEvhangeri Isingaperi yakaratidzwa panguva yayo kuitira kuti Israeri igadzirirwe zuva redambudziko ravo uye igadzirirwe zuva reruponeso rwavo, risinganonoki. Uye vazhinji vachamhura Mwari wekudenga nekuda kwekutongwa kuchauya pamusoro pavo uye vazhinji havazotendeuki, uye vazhinji vachatora jekiseni ravo rechikara vozvisanganisa nematekinoroji avasinganzwisisi, nekuti matekinoroji avo ndeekuparadza Adhamu saka muzvivande, imi vaIsraeri.

Uye vaprofita vangu vaviri, izvo zvigadziko zvangu zviviri zvemwenje, vachamuka voprofita zvinhu zvizhinji zvakavanzika kubva pamwoyo yevanhu uye vacharatidza zvakawanda zvakarasika muzvinyorwa zvekare uye chokwadi chichaziviswa, uye vakawa vachafumurwa zvizere, uye Edhomu ichafukurwa kubva pakabva Esau uye vose vakazvibatanidza mubatanidzwa vachafumurwa uye chokwadi chichaziviswa. Uye vachaparidza Evhangeri Isingaperi uye zvipupuriro zvavo zvichawedzerwa kuzvinyorwa zvitsvene nekusingaperi uye nekusingagumi.
