Ndinounza Zvikara Kuti Zvizadzise Hasha Dzangu!

Tarirai, zvouya! Ndinounza zvikara kuti zvizadzise hasha dzangu! Inzwai mashoko angu, imi vagari vepanyika, iwo mashoko aYahweh, Mwari mukuru wekusingaperi. Nokuti sezvakanga zvakaita mumazuva aNoa, ndizvo zvazvichaitawo: Uye panyika pakanga paine zvikara mumazuva iwayo, kubva pamazuva aJaredi kusvika pamazuva aNoa zvakasikwa uye zvakafamba-famba panyika mukuipa kukuru. Uye sezvazvakanga zvakaita, ndizvo zvazvichaitawo, uye zvikara zvichadzoka. Mumazuva aJaredi varindi vakaburukira pagomo reHemoni, vari mazana maviri vose mumazuva aJaredi vakaburuka kubva kumatenga kumusoro. Uye vakazvisunga pachavo nekutukana, iyo mhiko yavo yavakazvisunga nayo yakava chituko chisingaperi kune vose vakasungwa nayo uye chavasingazombopukunyuki, uye nokudaro vakanyengera uye vakatsausa vanhu kuti vapinde muchivi chikuru.

Varindi vakanyengerwa naSatani, maererano nokuchiva kwemwoyo yavo, uye vakawira mumuedzo kubva panzvimbo yavo yekutanga inokudzwa, uye vakanga vaine upenyu husingaperi kumatenga, nokuti ndizvo zvavakanga vasikwa. Asi vakawa, sezvinongoita nyeredzi dzinowa ndizvo zvakaitika pakuwa kwavo, uye vakabatana naSatani nokusingaperi. Saka Satani akanyengera vakadzi vaida uye akavadzidzisa kunyengera varindi maererano nokuchiva kwemwoyo yavo, uye vamwe vakadzi vakazvipa nokuzvidira kuvarindi, uye vachatongwa nokuda kwaizvozvo. Vamwe vakatorwa nechisimba nokuti varindi vakakanganisa vatungamiriri vavo venyika vekare, uye vakaenda vakatora vakadzi nechisimba kubva kuvarume vavo nemhuri dzavo, uye nokudaro varindi vakatora vose vavakasarudza sevakadzi vavo, uye vakashungurudza vazhinji, uye kubva pamubatanidzwa uyu usiri mutsvene pakazvarwa zvikara, zvikara zvikuru zvekare. Nokuti varindi vakawedzera kusvibisa majini evanhu. Uye nokudaro vakatadzira mhuka, neshiri, nehove uye nemarudzi ose enyama vakasanganisa imwe neimwe, mukusateerera kwokuzvidira uye kwakazara ruvengo kumiganhu yakatemerwa neni, ini Yahweh, Mwari Mukuru wekusingaperi! Kubva pakutanga ndakasika nyika nembeu imwe neimwe maererano norudzi rwayo, uye nokudaro miganhu yakapfuurwa isingafaniri kumbodarika, uye kudarika miganhu yechisikigo yakatemerwa naYahweh kunounza kupenga, kutambura, uye rufu pachisikwa chaYahweh, uye vose vanoita izvi vachatongwa. Upenyu hwakagadzirwa seruchira rwakabatana, apo upenyu hwose hwakabatanidzwa murugare newirirano, apo rudo runotonga zuva uye vose vanotsvaka kunaka kwevamwe kwete kwavo chete. Chisikwa chisina kuchiva nechishuwo chakaipa chokuzviratidza murufu nekuparadza, uye ndizvo zvazvichaita nokusingaperi-peri, uye nguva dzekuzadziswa dzavepo.

Uye nokudaro vatumwa vakuru vana vekudenga, ivo Mikaeri, Urieri, Rafaeri, naGabrieri, vakamhan'ara pamusoro pezvakanga zvaitwa nevaimbova shamwari dzavo panyika, uye nokudaro kutonga kwakaziviswa uye kwakaitwa nokukurumidza kune vose vaibatanidzwa uye kunyika pachayo, nokuti nyika yaivapo panguva iyoyo yakanga yosvibiswa zvachose. VaNefirimi, ivo vana vevarindi, vakaitwa kuti varwisane kusvika parufu muhondo zhinji huru pakati pemadzinza anopikisana, uye izvozvo zvakaitika nokuti ndakabvisa rugare pakati pavo, uye vakarwa kusvika parufu uye vakaurayana apo madzibaba avo (varindi) vakamanikidzwa kuona kuparadzwa kwezvisikwa zvavo zvinonyangadza zvavakanga vagadzirira kuparadza vanhu. Kubva ipapo, varindi vakasungwa mukurangwa nekutongwa uye vakachengetwa vakaparadzaniswa kwezvizvarwa makumi manomwe. Uye nenzira imwecheteyo yandaifanira kuona chisikwa changu chandakanga ndichida zvikuru chichiparadzwa nezvinyangadzo zvakaipa, ndizvo zvandakaita kuti zvisikwa zvakaipa zviparadzane mukurangwa kwemadzibaba azvo, uye ndizvo zvandakaripira, nokuti ndakarurama mukutonga kwangu nguva dzose nokusingaperi, uye ndiri Mwari wekutonga kwakarurama uko kutonga kwake kusingakundikani, kana kunonoka kuzadziswa.

Uye nokudaro ndakaunza mafashamo makuru panyika, uye mafashamo acho aiva chiitiko chine chisimba mumazuva aNoa. Zvitubu zvenzvimbo dzakadzika zvakabvinza uye mvura yakabuda nechisimba kubva munyika pachayo, uye mahwindo ekudenga akazarurwa, zvichibvumira mvura yaiva pamusoro pedenga kuti ifukidze nyika, kunyange pamusoro pemakomo akareba-reba, uye yakanyudza upenyu hwose panyika, kunze kwemweya misere yakaponeswa iri muareka, nokuti Noa akawanikwa akarurama pamberi pameso angu. Uye nokuda kwechikonzero ichi ndakatora Noa ndikamurayira kuti aite areka maererano nemirayiridzo yakapiwa noruoko rwangu. Uye ndakaunza mhuka dzaizoponeswa dziri mhenyu kwaariwo, uye nokudaro Noa akanga akatendeka kuita zvose zvaakarayirwa, uye mafashamo akauya sezvakanga zvafanotaurwa uye zvaziviswa, nokuti zvandinotaura, ini Yahweh Mwari mukuru wekusingaperi, hapana anogona kuzvishandura kana kuzvitadzisa napaduku pose!

Uye nokudaro Enoki, munyori wekare, anotsanangura magumo ezvikara izvi saizvozvi, nokuti ini, Yahweh, ndakazivisa kudaro: Uye zvino, zvikara, zvinobva pamweya nenyama, zvichanzi mweya yakaipa panyika, uye panyika pachava nougaro hwazvo. Mweya yakaipa yakabuda mumiviri yazvo; nokuti zvakaberekwa kubva kuvanhu, kubva kuVarindi vatsvene ndiko kutanga kwazvo nokuvamba kwazvo; zvichava mweya yakaipa panyika, uye zvichanzi mweya yakaipa. Kana zviri zvemweya yekudenga, kudenga kuchava nougaro hwayo, asi kana zviri zvemweya yenyika yakaberekerwa panyika, panyika pachava nougaro hwayo. Uye mweya yezvikara inotambudza, inodzvinyirira, inoparadza, inorwisa, inorwa, uye inoita kuparadza panyika, uye inokonzera dambudziko: haidyi chikafu, asi zvakadaro ine nzara nenyota, uye inokonzera kutadza. Uye mweya iyi ichamukira vana vevanhu nevakadzi, nokuti yakabva kwavari.

Uye nokudaro panguva yakafanira, Noa akamhara paGomo reArarati, apo areka yakazorora mvura ichiderera. Uye ndakapinda musungano naNoa, uye nokufamba kwenguva vazukuru vaNoa vakatanga kubva pasungano yandakaita naNoa, uye semugumisiro mweya yakaipa yakatanga kuvarwisa. Uye nokudaro mweya iyi, yaiva mizhinji, yakakurira vana vevanakomana vaNoa, uye nokudaro Noa akavareverera, uye ndakasunga zvikamu zvipfumbamwe kubva pagumi zvemweya iyi yakaipa mugomba rakadzikadzika, kwazvakachengetwa kubva panguva iyoyo kusvika nhasi. Uye nokudaro ndakarayira, uye Noa akadzidziswa mishonga yavo yose, uye kunyengera kwakadzidziswa kwaari, kuti Noa avaporese nemishonga yenyika. Uye nokudaro chikamu chimwe kubva pagumi chemweya iyi chakabvumirwa kuramba chakusununguka, kuti chitsause muuranda vanakomana vevanhu vaizochiva nhema dzayo. Uye nokudaro mweya yasara yakasungirwa kutongwa mugomba rakadzikadzika ichasunungurwa munguva dzekutambudzika kwaJakobho, apo gomba rakadzikadzika richazarurwa zvizere uye zvikamu zvipfumbamwe kubva pagumi zvavo zvakasungwa zvichasunungurwa kuti zviite nzira dzazvo dzakaipa sedivi rekutongwa kwemunhu wechisikigo kuri pano, uye zvichabuda panyika kamwe kamwe nekuparadza kukuru!

Uye nokudaro Kanani, muzukuru waNoa uyo Noa akatuka nokuda kweuipi hwake, akaita chivi chikuru (zvekare) achishandisa ruzivo rwevatumwa vakawa, uye munzira dzaSatani akafamba achida, maari akatungamirirwa achida, uye akaita zvimwe zvinyangadzo mushure memafashamo aNoa. Uye zvinyangadzo izvi zvakazadza nyika dzaShemi dzakapiwa nemijenya yenhaka, idzo Kanani akatukwa akatora nokuzvibira, uye vakatumidza nyika yake yaakaba kuti nyika yeKanani. Vazhinji vevana vake vezvinyangadzo vaiva zvisikwa zvakashandurwa majini zvakazadza nyika dzechipikirwa, kwandakatumira Mosesi kuti aunganidze vana vaIsraeri kubva kunyika dzeEgipita kuti vatore nhaka yavo kubva kune vezvinyangadzo. Uye nokudaro Joshua akarwa hondo huru nezvinyangadzo izvi sezvakanyorwa mumagwaro matsvene, uye vakatora kubva kuzvinyangadzo izvi nhaka yavo yakarurama, uye nokudaro zvakanyorwa semumvuri wezvinhu zvichauya. Nokuti nenzira imwecheteyo mwanakomana wangu, Yeshua HaMashiach, wandakaita Ishe weMadzishe naMambo weMadzimambo, achauya kuzotora nhaka yake kubva kune vanogara zvisiri pamutemo nevatoreri, nokuti kugara kwavo hakusi pamutemo. Havana umambo uye havana ushe, asi dziva guru rinopfuta nemoto nesarufa ndiyo nhaka yavo isingaperi.

Asi, Israeri haana kuva akatendeka zvizere mukudanwa kwake, uye vakasara vezvikara vakabvumirwa kusara. Asi kunyange mukusateerera kwavo ndakadzora zvinyangadzo izvi uye ndakaparadza zvizhinji zvazvo kubva panyika kubvira panguva idzodzo. Uye ndakadzingira zvizhinji zvazvo pasi pevhu, uye zvizhinji zvazvo zvakaenda kunyika dzisingazivikanwi mhiri kweaizi uko vanhu vasingafambi vakasununguka. Nzwisisai kuti vanhu havanzwisisi nyika yavakaitirwa uye yavanogara, nokuti vanhu vanodya nhema dzenhema dzinotengeswa sechokwadi, dzakagadziridzwa sedzidzo, uye nokudaro vanhu vanoramba vasingazivi nokuzvidira kuti neshoko raMwari matenga aivapo kare, uye nyika yakamira kunze kwemvura uye mumvura: Nokudaro nyika yaivapo panguva iyoyo, yakafukidzwa nemvura, yakaparara: Asi matenga nenyika, zviripo zvino, neshoko rimwechetewo zvakachengeterwa, zvakachengeterwa moto pazuva rekutongwa nekuparadzwa kwevanhu vasingadi Mwari.

Uye nokudaro nenguva yakafanira ndakabvisa zvikara panyika mutsa nenyasha dzangu uye nechido changu chokuponesa vanhu kubva mukuparadzwa, uye zvisaririra zvazvo zvakapararira panyika uye zvinosvibisa chisikwa changu sechapupu chekuvapo kwazvo. Kunyange zvisaririra zvebudiriro dzazvo dzekare zvichiri kumira sechapupu chokuti zvakambotonga umambo hwemadzinza panyika panguva yainzi yegoridhe, kunyange mumazuva aNoa mafashamo asati auya. Matongo azvo makuru akapararira sechapupu chinopupurira pamusoro pazvo, uye kuvapo kwematongo aya kunomira sechapupu chinopupurira nhema dzaSatani, uyo anoshingaira kuvanza chokwadi kuti awane kurwisa kwake kwechimbichimbi. Uye nokudaro ndinoudza avo vane nzeve dzokunzwa nemeso okuona nemwoyo wokudzidza zvichaitika zvisati zvaitika, kuti mwoyo yavo irege kupera simba nokushamisika nokusatenda, asi kuti vazive chokwadi uye vagadzirire zvichauya kuti vaponeswe, vose vanodana zita raJesu Kristu kusvikira kumagumo avo.

Saka zvakaziviswa uye zvakaitika mukuzara kwenguva, kuti ndakasunungura varindi kubva mumajeri avo kwavakanga vakasungwa mukutambudzwa nokurangwa, vakanga vakasungwa vakaparadzaniswa. Uye nokudaro vakasunungurwa mushure mekunge nguva dzekusungwa kwavo dzapera: kunyange zvizvarwa makumi manomwe. Nhoroondo haigoni kuvanza zvizere kusunungurwa kwavo, uye hondo zhinji nenjodzi nezvimwe zviitiko zvakaitika pasi rose nekusunungurwa kwavo, uye vakazviwisira kumabvazuva, uye vakazvibatanidza nekupandukira kwaSatani, zvizere, zvakare, nokuti ndizvo zvavakazvisunga nokusingaperi mumhiko yavo yakatukwa, uye mudziva remoto mhiko dzavo dzichazadziswa nokusingaperi, nokuti vakasungirwa kurangwa kwaSatani nenhaka yake nokusingaperi.

Uye nokudaro vakazvigadzirira munguva yakapiwa uye nenzira yakadzorwa kwemakore akati wandei ezvinyararire zvino. Zvechokwadi vakabereka zvinyangadzo zvavo zvakare, uye muchivande vanozvigadzirira uye vanomirira kubviswa kwemudzivisi apo vachaedza zvakare kutyora miganhu yandakavatemerera, nokuti vanogara vachipanduka. Uye kunyange zvazvo vakasungirwa kuzadzisa zvinangwa zvaEl Shaddai, nokuti ndiri Mwari Wemasimbaose, vachatsvaka kumutsiridza nguva yavo yegoridhe uye kutora nyika pachavo, izvo zvisingabviri; nokuti nyika ndeyaIshe nokuzara kwayo, uye gwaro renyika rakatengwa nemwanakomana wangu, Yeshua HaMashiach, uye mumaoko ake ane simba rakapihwa senhaka isingaperi, uye achaigovana zvizere nevakatendeka, vakadanwa, vakasarudzwa, uye vakadzikinurwa.

Uye nokudaro Satani nevarindi vakazvigadzirira, uye vanomirira nechido kubviswa kwemudzivisi, nokuti mukubviswa ikoko umambo hwavo hunosunungurwa zvizere, uye nyika ichava zvakare sezvayakanga yakaita mumazuva aNoa. Tarirai! Ndinopa murayiro, uye ndinovaunza: zvikara zviri kuuya kuzozadzisa hasha dzangu, zvichifara panguva imwecheteyo uye zvichituka, uye ndichazvibvumira kusunungurwa panyika zvakare kuitira kuzadzisa zvinangwa zvangu. Zvichabuda kamwe kamwe kubva kwazvakavanda, uye zvakavanda mumaguta azvo pasi penyika, uye zvakavanda kunze kweaizi iyo ichakurumidza kuchinjwa, uye munyika yevanhu zvichabvumirwa kupinda, kupamba, nekukunda, uye Dhiragoni ichazvitungamirira nokuti zviri muchikamu cheumambo hwayo. Asi kwenguva pfupi chete, kunyange vhiki rechimakumi manomwe raDhanieri, vhiki remakore manomwe richasunungurwa apo zuva rega rega riri gore, uye nokudaro makore manomwe achava. Uye zvichaparadza zvizhinji, uye mwoyo yevanhu ichapera simba nokuda kwezviso zvazvo zvinotyisa uye ukuru hwazvo hwakanyanya zvichienzaniswa nemunhu wechisikigo.

Tarirai, ndini MUKURU NDINI, ndakatuma Mashoko angu nomuranda wangu, uye nomuranda wangu mashoko angu akatumwa. Uye nokudaro ndinokuudzai zvinhu zvisati zvaitika kuti vanhu vangu vagadzirire uye vanzwisise zvichauya, uye vave nekunzwisisa sezvo zvinhu izvi zvichakurumidza kuitika. Uye vose vanodana zita raYeshua HaMashiach, kunyange Jesu Kristu mururimi rweChirungu, vachaponeswa.

Uye nokudaro hasha huru nekutonga kukuru sezvakapikirwa kare zvino zvagadzirira kuuya zvizere panyika, uye nguva dzavepo, anodaro Mwari Mukuru wekusingaperi! Kwenguva yose yenyasha vhangeri reruponeso nechakavanzika chikuru chaMwari (chakaratidzwa kuna Muapostora Pauro) chakazadza nyika uye chakaenda kunzvimbo dziri kure dzenyika. Uye kunyange zvazvo vazhinji, vazhinji, vazhinji vakaponeswa, kunyange vose vakauya kuna Jesu weNazareta mukutendeuka kuzere, vachimuita Ishe wavo wekusingaperi, nguva dzasvika dzekuzadziswa nokuti vanhu vakaramba zvakare musiki wavo. Uye ini, El Shaddai, kunyange Mwari Wemasimbaose, ndini ndakadzora uipi huzhinji kuti vadyi venhaka yeruponeso vauye kuupenyu husingaperi muna Kristu Jesu, Ishe wavo akakwidzwa. Uye zvino ndinozivisa zvinhu izvi zvisati zvaitika, nokuti ndiri Yahweh, MUKURU NDINI! Saka vaudze kuti MUKURU NDINI anozivisa zvakasimba kuti pakuunganidzwa kwevana vangu vakatendeka kumwanakomana wangu, pavachamutswa kuti vasangane naye mumhepo uye sezvo mudzivisi achibviswa, kuti umambo hwose hwaSatani huchasunungurwa panyika uye nyika ichachinjwa.

Ndinozivisa mukutonga pamusoro pevagari vepanyika kuti ndichasunungura umambo hwaSatani hwose, uye zvinyangadzo zvavo zvose zvichabuda panyika, uye mweya yose yandakasunga mugomba rakadzikadzika mumazuva aNoa ichasunungurwa panyika, nokuti nguva dzekuparadzaniswa kwekupedzisira nekutonga kwekupedzisira dzasvika panyika, uye zuva rekutonga kukuru ragadzwa. Uye nokudaro zvichava kuti umambo hwaSatani huchapiwa kutonga pamusoro penyika, nokuti mukururama kwangu, vanhu vakashuva zvikuru kutongwa naSatani, kunyange kutenda nhema dzake kuti iye mwari ane mutsa uye muvheneki anounza chiedza, kuti zvino ndichapa vanhu zvavanoshuva zvikuru.

Uye vakawa vakasunungurwa zvizere kuti vatongwe, uye vakaita zvinyangadzo zvavo zvakare. Uye vakasungirwa kunyika nedhiragoni, nokuti ndiyo nguva yavo, uye vachazviita sevatorwa, kunyange zvisikwa zvine mutsa zvinobva kunyika dzekunze. Asi ini, Yahweh Mwari Mukuru wekusingaperi, ndinopika nezita rangu, nokuti handigoni kupika neakakwirira, kuti hakuna nyika dzinotenderera zvenhema munzvimbo isina chinhu inogara ichiwedzera, asi pamusoro penyika pane matenga, kunyange denga rematenga rinova denga repamusoro-soro, apo Mwari Wekumusoro-soro anogara muushe husingaperi pachigaro chake cheushe uye hapana chinogona kuvanzika pameso angu.

Tarirai! Vatozvizivisa, uye zvinhu zvavo nemabhora zviri kwose kwose, uye nyika iri kuchinja nekukurumidza zvikuru zvino. Uye sezvo munhu wechisikigo zvino aramba chibvumirano changu cherugare, kunyange mwanakomana wangu mumwechete, Jesu weNazareta, saka zvino ndinoramba munhu wechisikigo uye ndichamusiya kuti atongwe zvizere nevavengi vake, uye vanochiva kuparadzwa kwake. Asi, kuramba kwangu hakusi kuzere kana kwekupedzisira, nokuti boka guru richaponeswa panguva yekutambudzika kwaJakobho, kunyange vose vanodana zita raIshe, kunyange Jesu Kristu, kusvikira kumagumo. Nokuti ruponeso panguva yekutambudzika kwaJakobho harugoni kuwanikwa neimwe nzira. Saka kuparadzaniswa kwekupedzisira kuchatanga, uye vose vandakagadza kuupenyu husingaperi kubva pamberi pekuvakwa kwenyika vachauya kuupenyu husingaperi, uye ndichavamutsa kuutsva hweupenyu, uye mubairo mukuru vachava nawo.

Saka inzwai mashoko angu uye isa mwoyo yenyu pamashoko angu eyambiro nekuzivisa, nokuti nguva dzevaHedheni dzapera, uye hasha nekutonga nekuzarurwa kukuru zvave panyika, nokuti ini, kunyange El Shaddai, kunyange Mwari Wemasimbaose, ndini ndinogara kumusoro, uye hapana chakavanzika pameso angu, uye zvino ndichafamba kuti ndiponese zvizere vose vari vadyi venhaka yeruponeso sezvo ndichivaparadzanisa kubva kune avo vakatarirwa kuparadzwa kusingaperi. Uye nokudaro gorosi richaparadzaniswa kubva kumasora nokusingaperi, uye vose vari muchikamu chekupandukira kwaSatani vachapiwa kutongwa, uye nguva dzekutongwa kwavo dzagadzirira kubuda panyika.

Inzwai Mashoko angu uye nzwisisai kuoma kwezvichauya, nokuti zuva raIshe izuva rerima, uye ndiani anogona kumira?

Ini, Yahweh, Mwari Mukuru wekusingaperi, ndinozivisa zvinhu izvi zvisati zvaitika, uye hapasisina nguva. Ani naani anoda kuuya, ngaauye anwe pachena mvura yeupenyu husingaperi, uye kukoka kwangu kuupenyu husingaperi kuripo, uye vose vanodana zita raIshe kusvikira kumagumo vachaponeswa.

Ini, Yahweh, Mwari Mukuru wekusingaperi, ndakatuma Mashoko aya nomuranda wangu, uye nomuranda wangu Mashoko aya akatumwa!

Nguva dzavepo!
