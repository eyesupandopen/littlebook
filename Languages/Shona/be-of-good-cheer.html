Chapupu Chiviri: Kereke
Mumabasa aPetro
Kubvumbi 10, 2024

Farisai!

Vana vangu vadiki, farisai; handina here kukunda wakaipa ndikakudzikinurai neropa rangu pachangu? Munofanira kuorei mwoyo? Mune mwoyo inorema nei; munofunga kuti ndakukanganwai here? Kwete, panzvimbo pokutarira kunzwisiso yenyu, tsvakai uchenjeri hwangu, hunobva kumusoro; nokuti neuchenjeri hwaMwari muchatungamirirwa, saka musamusiya! Ko uchenjeri hahuchadanidziri here mumigwagwa? Saka musatya uye farisai, nokuti ndakakunda uye nemiwo muchakunda, saizvozvo neropa rangu pachangu. Bvumirai rugare rwangu runyaradze mwoyo yenyu uye bvumirai mashoko angu anyaradzikise mweya yenyu, nokuti ndinemi.

Ndiani achakuparadzanisai norudo rwangu? Ko njodzi, kana munondo, kana nzara zvichakuparadzanisai here? Ndinoti kwete, uye tarirai muzive kuti makachengetedzeka mandiri. Zivai kuti ndakakuisai chisimbiso chaBaba vangu, uye chisimbiso ichocho hachigoni kuputswa kana kubviswa kwamuri, saka garai mandiri, Ishe wenyu, simba, nomudzikinuri wenyu. Tarirai! Ndakakutengai neropa rangu pachangu, ndakakudanai nezita, ndakakuisai chisimbiso maererano nokutenda kwenyu muzita rangu, uye ani naani anotenda mariri achaponeswa nokutenda, nokuti vakarurama vachararama nokutenda! Chengetai tariro yenyu iri mhenyu uye ngaikusimbisei, uye wedzerai kutsungirira parudo rwenyu nokutenda, nokuti makaponeserwa kutariro. Tariro huru nokutarisira kukuru ndezvenyu, nokuti ndakazvizivisa.

Makaondomokerei uye makasuwa? Tarirai! Ndakakunda, uye nokukurumidza muchava neni, uye muchandiona nemaziso enyu, uye muchaziva sezvamunozivikanwa.

Tarirai! Anodaro Ishe mukuru, Ishe ane simba ari Ishe wemadzishe. Tarirai kwandiri mukusuwa kwenyu nematambudziko enyu. Ndinoziva mwoyo yenyu, uye ndinoziva zvakakwana kuti munondida uye munoshuva kuva neni sezvandinoshuva kuva nemi; chete musaneta. Ndipindurei izvi, nguva pfupi yasara mukumirira kwenyu — ichaenzaniswa nokusingaperi neni here, mwenga wangu? Kwete, kutambura kwenyu nedambudziko renyu panyika hazvina kufanira kuenzaniswa nokubwinya kwandava kuda kuratidza mamuri, nokuti muchava sezvandiri, uye muchandiona sezvandiri, saka musabvumira mwoyo yenyu kutambudzika. Nzwisisai nguva dzenyu uye garai makagadzirira nguva dzose, nokuti kuonekwa kwangu kuchaitika pakarepo, uye kuchapfuura zvamunotarisira.

Tarirai, vana vangu! Nyika ichaziva kuti ndinokudai uye kuti muri vangu, uye Satani achakonzera kunyengera kukuru kunyengera avo vanoparara, asi hazvizoswedera pedyo nemi. Kwete, kutongwa hakusi kwenyu, uye hasha dzangu hadzisi dzenyuwo. Nzwisisai, vana vangu, kuti mandiri, makarurama, musina kururama mumabasa enyu, asi makarurama mukururama kwandakakupfekedzai. Musarasa nguvo dzandakakupfekedzai, uye musadzima mweya wangu. Kunyange zvazvo dziri nguva dzokupedzisira, ndichadurura mweya wangu, uye vanakomana venyu navanasikana vachaporofita, uye vakwegura venyu vacharota zviroto. Kuchine basa rinofanira kuitwa, uye kufamba kwangu kwekupedzisira munguva idzi dzenyasha kuchapfuura zuva rePendekosti pandakatanga kudururira pamusoro pevakasarudzwa vangu mweya wandakagamuchira kuna Baba vangu. Mabasa makuru ari pamunwe penyu uye kuchine vazhinji vachazoponeswa, saka itai basa rangu mushumire vana vangu uye mutsvake makwai angu akarasika. Kumbirai kwandiri uye ndichakupai basa, sezvo ndakugonesai nokugonesa kukuru uye ndakapa zvipo kuvanhu. Ndiani angamirisana nemi, kunyange mune pfuma iyi mumidziyo yevhu? Muchiri kundikahadzikeraniko? Muutera hwenyu ndimo munosimbiswa simba rangu mamuri. Naizvozvo, wedzerai mubasa raIshe, vana vangu.

Tarirai Shoko rangu Dzvene, nokuti sezvandakamutsa vakafa, nemiwo munogona kuita zvimwe chetezvo. Zodzai maziso enyu nemushonga wemaziso kuti muone kuporesa kuzhinji kune simba kwandakaita kuti ndisunungure vasungwa pandakauya kune vangu; saizvozvowo nemi munogona kuita zvimwe chetezvo. Handina here kutaura kuti uyo anotenda mandiri, iye achaita mabasa mamwe chetewo, uye mabasa makuru achaita nokuti ndaenda kuna Baba vangu naBaba venyu, uye kuna Mwari wangu naMwari wenyu: Muri madzimambo navapristi pamberi pangu uye makagariswa kudenga, saka muchiri kukahadzikanirei? Ivai nokutenda kwakaita setsanga yemasitadhi, nokuti imbeu duku kwazvo inobereka muti une simba, saizvozvowo muchava kana mukaramba makasimba mandiri.

Munovhiringidzirei mashoko angu uye mukaregeredza zviri mukutaura kwangu? Handirevi nhema: munogona kuita zvandakakupai basa kuti muzviite kana mukaramba makatendeka. Taurai Shoko rangu nesimba guru nokushinga. Itiyi kumakomo iwayo, Kandirwa mugungwa! Mashoko angu ichokwadi noupenyu, saka musatenda nhema dzevafudzi vakatsauka vanoti hamugoni kuita zvandiri kukuudzai. Tora chidzidzo. Munoda kuporesa varwere here? Rairai chirwere kuti chibve muzita rangu, uye taurai mashoko nokutenda mandiri uye nokutenda mumashoko andichakupai. Taurai kuti zviitike, nokuti upenyu norufu zviri musimba rorurimi; rurimi rwenyu rune simba rakadii pamunotaura simba raMwari uye muchiraira zvisingagoneki.

Taurai nokushinga. Taurai nechivimbo. Taurai mukutenda, musingakahadziki chinhu; nokuti ndakutumai uye ndakakupai simba. Munokumbirirei mweya kuti ubve muzita rangu nokutya pamiromo yenyu? Urairei kuti ubve muzita rangu nokushinga kwose nechivimbo, nokuti mukuru ndiye ari mamuri pane uyo ari munyika. Tarirai! Mweya yakaipa inokutyai uye inodududza pamashoko enyu, saka munotyirei zvinhu zvisina simba nezvinoshoreka zvakakundwa uye zvakamirira moto? Musatya, asi tendai chete!

Endai mberi muzita rangu muite mabasa angu ane simba. Rangarirai rudo rwenyu rwokutanga uye zororai muzororo randakakupai. Regai mabasa enyu nematambudziko enyu, uye mutore joko rangu pamusoro penyu; nokuti joko rangu rakareruka, uye mutoro wangu hauremi. Itai mabasa andakakugadzirirai uye mufambe maari, nokuti mukuita izvozvo muchaponesa vazhinji pamirazvo, uye muchero uchawanda kuakaundi yenyu. Nguva yava pedyo, mwenga wangu, uye kuguma kwenzira yenyu kwava pedyo, pedyo kupfuura zvamunofunga. Asi ndinzwei, vana vangu; ndinzwei, mwenga wangu! Revererai kuchine nguva. Poresai varwere Mwanakomana achiri kupenya muupenyu hwenyu, achikupai simba nesimba rangu.

Tsvakai makwai angu akarasika; adanei, nokuti kuchine vazhinji vanofanira kuponeswa mumasekondi aya okupedzisira ekuzorodzwa. Nokuti nokukurumidza muchauya kunhaka yenyu mandiri, uye muchaziva upenyu hwenyu hwakazara mandiri; nokuti upenyu hwenyu hwakavigwa mandiri, uye pandichaonekwa, saizvozvowo kuzara kwoupenyu kwakapiwa nemi naBaba mandiri kucharatidzwa. Ndinokudai zvikuru, vana vangu vadiki, saka idai vakarasika nenzira imwe cheteyo. Musavatonga kana kuzvidza dambudziko ravo, nokuti vakundwa naSatani sezvamaimbova; saka rangarirai ruponeso rwandakakuitirai uye murupe kune vanoshaiwa. Kunyange vazhinji vachikurambai, musamira kusvikira makwai angu aunganidzwa, nokuti nokukurumidza kwazvo gorosi rangu richaunganidzwa zvakachengeteka mudura rangu. Avo vanoramba kuonekwa kwangu vanondiramba uye vanoramba shoko rangu; saka zvisimbisei mushoko rangu uye regai kutenda mbavha, nokuti mbavhawo ndivo varevi venhema.

Ndini iye akatsungirira kusvika pakufema kwake kwekupedzisira, achirwisa chivi neropa rangu risina chivi.

Ndini iye akamutswa pazuva rechitatu ndikakwira kuna Baba vangu.

Ndini iye akafamba pakati pevadzidzi vangu navaapositori kwemazuva makumi mana, ndichitoonekwa nehama dzinopfuura mazana mashanu panguva imwe, uye ndikaonekwa ndikabatwa nevazhinji, nokuti mweya hauna nyama neropa.

Ndini iye akatorwa, ivo vachinditarisa, ndichipinda mumakore.

Ndini iye ane kupenya kunopfuura kwezuva, nokuti ndinoratidza kubwinya kwaBaba vangu.

Ndava kuda kuratidza kubwinya kukuru mamuri.

Ndinemi nguva dzose, kusvikira pakuguma kwenyika.
