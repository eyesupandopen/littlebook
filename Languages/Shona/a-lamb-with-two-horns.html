Chapupu Chiviri: Israeri
Zvinovava Zvinotapira
7 Mbudzi, 2024

Gwayana rine Nyanga Mbiri

Kubva kuna YHVH: IYE MUKURU!

Tarisai! Kunyengera kukuru kwabva kuBhabhironi Rakavanzika uye kwatapa nyika mukumbundiro yarwo inouraya! Swederai pedyo kuti munzwisise kunyengera kukuru, imi vagari vepanyika, uye munzwisise nguva dzamunorarama, nokuti kunyengera kukuru kwasvika uko nyika inofadzwa nako nokuti nyika yavo inhambo uye vatungamiri vavo vatambi, kunyange vanyepedzeri vanonyepedzera. Saka zvino ndichaburitsa pachena chokwadi pamusoro penhandare yavo yavanoshandisa kuroya vanhu vazhinji.

Chenjerai! Kunyengera kukuru kwauya pamusoro penyu nezviratidzo zvizhinji zvenhema nezvishamiso uye kunyengera kukuru uku kwakarongerwa zvakasimba kuparadza Jakobho, kunyange zvinangwa zvakachengetwa naEsau muchivande mumazera ose, asi zvino zvichaburitswa pachena uye zvichafukurwa kuti vose vaone. Uye sezvo mwanakomana wangu, Jesu weNazareta, akaudza Madzibaba avo muJudhiya maJubhiri apfuura aya: Imi muri vababa venyu dhiabhorosi uye kuchiva kwababa venyu ndiko kwamuchaita. Saka ndinouya kwamuri imi Israeri kuti murege kutorwa nekunyengera kukuru uku, nokuti imi ndimi makanangwa (kunyange nyika ichazununguka) nokuti muteyi upi anoteera musungo shiri ichiona?

Uye ndini, Yahweh kunyange IYE MUKURU, ndinouya kuzonyevera vanhu vangu kuburikidza neMashoko aya andakaburitsa pachena uye andakatumira kuburikidza nenhume yangu nomuranda wangu akagadzwa nokuda kwezvinangwa izvi. Saka ndinouya kune avo vari vemarudzi angava ari pedyo kana kure, angava ari munyika kana akapararira kunze, uye ndichaunganidza vose vakasara vangu kuna Mesia wavo noMufudzi Mukuru, Yeshua HaMashiach.

Nokuti ndiye achazotungamirira Israeri kunzvimbo yakachengeteka mumaoko evane simba vangu 144,000 nokuti Jesu weNazareta ndakamuita Mambo wemadzimambo naIshe wemadzishe! Uye ndiye achazotonga nyika mukururama mukati mekutonga kwake kwemakore churu kubva pachigaro chaushe chaDhavhidhi muIsraeri chaakagara nhaka. Nokuti ndakavimbisa Madzibaba enyu uye ndini IYE anozadzisa vimbiso imwe neimwe yandinoita kakawanda-wanda mukururama kwangu kusingaperi kuti zvose zvive hongu naamen uye ndini Mwari Asingaperi weKururama asingaziyi!

Asi, zvakadaro kuti shato ichasimuka zvizere uye chikara chinobva mugungwa chakazvivanza kuti chigozosimuka zvizere pakati peavo vachachatungamirira, uye chichasimuka kubva mumadota panguva yacho yakatarwa. Chakapamba uye chakarongera kuparadzwa kuzere kweBhabhironi Rakavanzika, kunyange kushanda pamwe chete nechikara chinobva panyika kuronga nekuuraya kuparadzwa kwavo, uye saka ndakavapa kuti vazviite. Uye nyika yakabatwa nemhombwe huru uye haichakwanisi kufungidzira nyika isina hudzvinyiriri hwechifeve chikuru chayakadzvinyirira nacho vanhu venyika uye ichishatisa chisikwa changu netsvina yeupombwe hwayo kunyange yakapiwa kunyika kubva mumukombe iwoyo unonyangadza wegoridhe uri mumaoko ayo.

Uye saka, ndinouya kuzokunyeverai, imi Israeri, pamusoro pekunyengera kukuru kwave kuda kunyudza nyika muhondo huru nenyonganyonga, kunyange kunyengera kwavakadzidzira vachishandisa matekinoroji avo nokuti vose vanyepedzeri vanonyepedzera, uye kubva mukunyengera kwavo muporofita wavo wenhema achapinda munyika kunyange saMoshiach wenhema waEsau.

Uye Esau anozvifukidza uye anozvivanza pakati paIsraeri, kunyange achinyepedzera kuti ndiIsraeri sezvavanotsvaga kuzvisanganisa pakati paIsraeri, kunyange achivanza chimiro chavo chechokwadi kubva pakati peavo vavanonyepedzera kuva. (Iwo masora pakati pegorosi uye Ishe weKukohwa achavabvisa zvizere.) Uye saka shato inosimuka nokuti iawa yayo, uye Kristu Jesu achabvisa pakarepo vakatendeka muna Kristu panyika, (vose vapenyu nevakafa) uye ipapo kutonga kuchauya zvizere pamusoro peBhabhironi Rakavanzika kunyange chifeve chikuru chitsvuku ichocho chakafanotaurwa naJohane Muratidziri maJubhiri mazhinji apfuura aya.

Uye ndini Mutana weMazuva, kunyange Ishe weMweya uye ndinopupura, kuti vose vanzwisise kutaura kwangu: Chinhu chakaitika chichaitikazve: uye chinhu chakaitwa, chinoitwazve, uye hapana chinhu chitsva pasi pezuva. Uye saka zvakaitika kuti vakawa vakashanda kuparadza Adhamu panyika kubva pakutanga kuti vazvitore, kunyange bindu rangu reupenyu mandinorera nekuchengeta mweya yevanhu kuitira kuti ndivaregerere kubva pakuora muna Kristu Jesu wandakasika saAdhamu wekupedzisira. Uye zviri muna Mesia wenyu, imi Israeri, kuti Israeri yaMwari ichaunganidzwa zvizere uye icharegererwa semunhu mutsva mumwe akaumbwa nenzira yakafanana naIshe wenyu nomuregereri wenyu, uyo akatenga kuregererwa kwenyu muropa rake rekuyananisa.

Saka nzwisisai imi Israeri kuti Jesu weNazareta ndiYeshua HaMashiach uyo anovawo Kristu Jesu (nzwisisai kusimbiswa kwemazita ake nokuti ruponeso rwenyu rwakakwana) saka teererai Mashoko ake mukuteerera nokuda kwemweya yenyu, nokuti hamuchina nguva yekupokana nemusiki wenyu uye hamuchina nguva yekuramba Mesia wenyu wechokwadi.

Uye zvakanyorwa sezvandakazivisa, uye saka ndakaita kuti Yeshua HaMashiach aburitse pachena kumuapostora wake Pauro pamusoro pake: Naizvozvo, Elohim akamukwidziridzawo zvikuru, akamupa zita riri pamusoro pemazita ose, Kuti muzita raJesu mabvi ose apfugame, ezvinhu zviri kudenga, nezvinhu zviri panyika, nezvinhu zviri pasi penyika, Uye kuti rurimi rwose rupupure kuti Jesu Kristu ndiye Ishe, kuti Mwari Baba vakudzwe. Uye saka, ndini YHVH, IYE MUKURU, ndinosimbisazve chokwadi cheMashoko aya kuti kutenda kwenyu kusazununguswe.

Kunyengera kukuru kwasvika uye vazhinji vachabatwa imomo uye vazhinji vachawira mumirazvo, kunyange vose avo mazita avo asina kumbonyorwa muBhuku reUpenyu reGwayana kubva pakutanga. Nokuti, ini El Shaddai, ndini IYE anoziva kutanga kubva kumagumo uye kunyange, ndichipa mweya wose mukana wekurarama upenyu hwakarurama pamberi pangu, ndinoziva kuti ndiani achasarudza kuzadza zvivi zvake kusvika pakuzara kubva kune avo vachauya kuna Kristu kuti vagosikwa patsva mukururama uye kutonga kwangu kwakakwana mukururama kwangu.

Asi imi Israeri, hamuna kururama nokuti makaramba Mwari Asingaperi weKururama nokuti munoramba Mwanakomana wangu mumwe chete wandakatumira kuti ndiregerere zvivi segwayana raMwari, naizvozvo, zvivi zvenyu zvinoramba zviripo. Uye saka ndinokurairai, Israeri, teererai Mashoko aIYE MUKURU munguva ino yekupedzisira uye mugamuchire chibayiro changu!

Hongu, Israeri, ndakakupai chibayiro chakakwana nokuti Adhamu akashatiswa mukukwana uye upenyu hwenyama huri muropa. Uye saka chivi chinopfuudzwa neropa rinova upenyu hwenyama kubva kuna Baba kuenda kuvana vake kuti vangava varume kana vakadzi vanogara nhaka yekuora nokuti Adhamu anoramba akashata. Mungoni dzangu dzisingaperi nerudo ndakapa izvo zvisingagoneki kuitwa nechero chisikwa chipi zvacho: ndakasika uye ndakapa Mwanakomana wangu mumwe chete, uyo Mosesi anorayira kuteerera kwenyu, nokuda kwezvivi zvevanhu vose, vangava vaJudha kana vemamwe marudzi, vangava varanda kana vakasununguka.

Asi zvakadaro Israeri kuti vavengi venyu vanochiva kuparadzwa kwenyu nokuti vanoshuvira kuva nenguva yavo yegoridhe zvakarezve, kunyange nguva yegoridhe yavasati vachazombowanazve nokuti panzvimbo penguva yavo yegoridhe isati yaitika mafashamo, yakazadzwa nechivi nemaune umo zvinonyangadza zvemarudzi ose zvaifamba, vachava nekutongwa kwakaoma nekurangwa sezvo umambo hwavo hunoparadzwa zvachose. Uye kuchakurumidza kuitika kuti chikara nemuporofita wenhema vachakandwa vari vapenyu mugungwa remoto unopfuta nesarufa uye Satani achasungwa kwemakore churu mukurwadziswa nekutya kukuru kwenhaka yake iri kuuya mumirazvo isingaperi yekurangwa umo honye yavo isingafi.

Uye shato yakasungirirwa panyika kuti isapinde kudenga zvakare nekusingaperi asi inochengetwa pasi pedenga rakasimba yakasungirirwa panyika nokuda kwekutongwa kwavaka chiva, uye shato ine hasha huru mukukundwa kwayo, nokuti yakundwa zvachose mumaoko aYeshua HaMashiach uyo ari mbeu yakavimbiswa yaamai vevapenyu vose, nokuti ndakavimbisa Adhamu naEvha murudo rwangu kwavari. Saka zvinongobvumidzwa kuti azadzise zvinangwa zvangu zvisingaperi asati atumirwa kumirazvo nekusingaperi, uye ndichaisa chisimbiso, uye havazouyi mupfungwa kana kutaurwa nezvavo nokuti ndiyo ramangwana renyu revimbiso umo munosunungurwa kubva kuvadzvinyiriri venyu nekusingaperi uye murugare rusingaperi uye rwakakwana muchararama kana mukangopfugamira Mesia wenyu wechokwadi, kunyange Yeshua HaMashiach, nokuti hapana imwe nzira!

Saka ndichakupai kuti munzwisise zvinhu zvave kuda kuitika, uye zvirokwazvo musungo wakateyiwa nemuteyi uchaburitswa pachena, kunyange musungo wakateyiwa neshato inotsvaga kubata njiva dzangu dzinokosha, kunyange vanhu vangu vakasarudzika vandakazvichengetera. Uye ndini IYE akaita kuti njiva yaNoa idzoke ine shizha remuorivhi mumuromo mayo, uye nenzira imwe cheteyo, ndini IYE akatumira chibvumirano changu cherugare rusingaperi pachiso chaJesu Kristu, Mesia wenyu akamuka imi Israeri!

Uye saka, nzwisisai nguva dzamunorarama. Sinagogi raSatani rinofamba nezviratidzo zvizhinji zvenhema nezvishamiso, uye nekunyengera vanorwa hondo. Vanoita tsika dzavo pachena, dzakafukidzwa zvishoma sevaraidzo nenhau nemisangano yezvematongerwo enyika. Vapedza nzira yavo yekusarudza, apo Esau zvino asarudza gwayana rake kubva pakati peSinagogi raSatani nokuda kwekunyengera kwavo kwekumhura kwavanotsvaga kubata nako. Asi chikara chinobva panyika hachisi gwayana raMwari ravanoseka zvisina musoro uye vachimhura, asi muporofita wavo wenhema wavazvisarudzira iye zvino ane nyanga mbiri segwayana, asi anotaura seshato.

Uye saka ndakaita kuti Mwanakomana wangu, Jesu Kristu Akarurama, aburitse pachena muSarudzo Ndeyako (dzidzai kuti mudzidze) apo Bharabhasi akagamuchira ronda mumusoro sekunge raive rekufa uye chiitiko ichocho chaive nezvirevo zvakawanda zvaasina kuburitsa pachena panguva iyoyo, asi zvaive pamusangano uyu, kunyange tsika yake yekusarudza kwavanoti, Yahweh pachake akaboora gomba munzeve yake achimuita muranda wangu waMwari. Uye saka, ndinopika nezita rangu, kunyange YHVH IYE MUKURU, kuti handina kumuita muranda wangu, uye handina kupa mumwe Mesia kunze kweMwanakomana wangu mumwe chete, kunyange Yeshua HaMashiach, anova ndiye gwayana raMwari chete randichazombotumira. Uye saka kuedza kwake kuurayiwa kwakashandiswa sechimwe chezviratidzo zvizhinji zvenhema nezvishamiso apo munyepedzeri uyu anonyepedzera. Uye pamusoro pemunyepedzeri uyu haasi wechimiro changu kana boka rangu uye muvengi wangu.

Asi, vazhinji muBhabhironi Rakavanzika vakanyepedzera kuti Jesu Kristu ndiye Ishe wavo zvino vasiya Ishe wavo angadai aripo vachifarira Bharabhasi, anotaura seshato asi ari gwayana rine nyanga mbiri. Uye vazhinji veavo vakaita Bharabhasi Ishe wavo vachasiirwa kumoto wekunatsa nokuti ruponeso rwavo rwakanga rusina kukwana zvizere nokuti havana kuuya kuna Kristu zvizere nemoyo yakazadzwa nekutenda nekutendeuka asi nekuchiva kunoburitswa nevhangeri rebudiriro. Nokuti Sinagogi raSatani rakashatisa magwaro avo matsvene nemvumo yavo uye maZionist zvino vava. Uye vazhinji vemweya iyi, vachapiwa rukudzo rwekufira kutenda kwavo kana vakasarudza kugamuchira ruponeso nekudana zita raIshe wavo Jesu Kristu kusvika kumagumo, uye ndichavagamuchira nekusingaperi uye nekusingaperi uye kutambura kwavo hakungaenzaniswi nekubwinya kuchazoburitswa mavari.

Saka, moshiach uyu wenhema anofamba uye anoparidza achitaura seshato uye anonyepedzera uye anotaura zvinhu zvizhinji zvikuru zvekumhura kunyange asati asimuka zvizere, uye achati achasimuka. Nzwisisai vanhu vangu kuti muporofita uyu wenhema anovawo chikara chinobva panyika chinoshandira Sinagogi raSatani zvino asarudzwa segwayana ravo. Uye zvichaitika kuti musoro wake uchaonekwa sekunge wakuvadzwa kusvika pakufa, asi ronda rake rinouraya rakaporeswa, uye nyika yose ichashamisika uye ichatevera chikara. Uye saka munyengeri uyu achasimuka.

Pakumuka kwake achazviti akazvipira segwayana raMwari achiunza rugare kuna Israeri senjiva yavo inovaunzira shizha remuorivhi! Uye kwete Israeri chete, asi nyika pachayo uye zvichaonekwa kuti zvino adzoka kuzounza rugare, asi nzira dzerugare haazidziwi uye pakutongwa kweBhabhironi Rakavanzika hondo huru ichatevera. Nekuti mweya wenhema wekunyaradza unobva kushato wagadzikana panyika, uye ndakatotumira kunyengera kune simba kuitira kuti vose vanofarira kusarurama vatende nhema kuti vose vagopihwa mhosva vanofarira kusarurama. Nekuti kana vachiti rugare nekuchengeteka, ipapo kuparadzwa kunongoerekana kwasvika uye havangapunyuki.

Uye vazhinji muBhabhironi Rakavanzika, kunyange avo vanotenda nhema dzake uye vachitevera mashoko ake akanaka nekutaura kwake kwakanaka, vanokwezvwa nekuteyiwa nerugare rwenhema kwavanofungidzira kuti hupenyu hwavo hwemafaro nehutadzi huchaenderera mberi apo nhasi kwakafanana nezuro uye mangwana achange akafanana nanhasi uye vazhinji vachadanidzira, kuchema, nekuungudza kuti tariro yavo zvino yatorwa kubva kwavari, uye vacharwa nevanhu venyika yavo kusvika parufu uye panguva yose iyoyo kurwisa kwavo kwechivande kuchabudiswa pachena zvizere, kunyange apo mudzivisi wangu anobviswa panyika uye Bhabhironi Rakavanzika richawira muchikamu chekutongwa chakapetwa kaviri muawa imwe chete apo njodzi zhinji dzinowira.

Uye Sinagogi raSatani richava nehondo yavo yavakashandisa kuuraya vasina mhosva mukunyengera kwavo kuti vakurudzire uye avo vanonamata mabhokisi avo matema vachazvinetsa mukuuraya, kunyange kuuraya kunounzwa nemaune nekunyengera kukuru uye kubva mumadota vachamuka. Uye vakawa vachazviratidza zvizere vachipa vanhu rubatsiro runoita serwakanaka asi nzira dzavo nematekinoroji avo zvakagadzirirwa kuteya vose vavanogona munhaka yavo mumirazvo yekurangwa, kwavasingazombopukunyuki uye kwavachagamuchira kakawanda kune CHESE chitadzo chavakaita uye kururamisira kuchaitwa. Asi muteyi upi anoteera musungo shiri ichiona?

Saka ndinokupai kuti munzwisise, kuti kutongwa kweBhabhironi Rakavanzika kwakagadzirirwa uye vakamira vakagadzirira. Asi kutanga gwayana renhema rine nyanga mbiri iri richanyepedzera kuva rakanaka asi pachokwadi mufudzi ane gwembe, imwe yenyanga mbiri kutonga, kunyange nyanga diki yaDhanieri. Uye achaonekwa sekunge aedza kuponesa Bhabhironi Rakavanzika nekuchenesa huori hwaro asi pachokwadi muori mukuru akateya vazhinji vaakazochenesa nekupomera huori hwake hwaakashandisa kumanikidza vazhinji kuti vazviise pasi pake. Uye zvino zvose zvisingakodzeri zvinangwa zvavo, kana vanogona kupupura pachena nezvehuipi hwavo zvichabviswa nemaoko emufudzi ane gwembe uyu uye vachapomerwa zvitadzo zvavachiri kuita uye zvavanotungamirira vamwe mazviri, uye havangapunyuki.

Uye, nzwisisaiwo kuti vazhinji vari vatori vechikamu mumhosva dzavo uye vari masora akafanana nevafudzi ava vane gwembe vachapinda mumabhanga avo uye vachazvivanza mumoyo menyika kubva mukutsamwa kuri kuuya uye vachaparadza humbowo hwose hwehupenyu hwavakararama muBhabhironi Rakavanzika mukunyengera kukuru, uye havangapunyuki kutsamwa kuri kuuya! Asi, ndizvo zvazviri kuti zvitadzo zvavo zvichafumurwa nehunyengeri nemuporofita wenhema uyu uye achaonekwa seakarurama uye akatwasanuka, kunyange murume akatumwa naMwari kuti aponese Israeri uye saka musungo wagadzirira kusvetuka. Asi, chiito chavo chose ndakachinyora mumabhuku ari kumatenga, uye ini pachangu ndinorangarira zvese uye ndinoziva mhosva dzavo dzose uye ndichazvifumura uye vacharipira nekusingaperi zvitadzo zvavo zvakaipa.

Saka chikara chinobva mugungwa chiri kuzvigadzirira kumuka seMahdi wavo uye chikara chinobva panyika chiri kuzvigadzirira kumuka segwayana renhema nekuti gwayana ripi rine nyanga mbiri uye rinotaura seshato. Asi Esau akagadzirira kumugamuchira kwavari munyika yaanotsvaga kuba uye vachadzurwa zvachose neruoko rune simba rwaYeshua HaMashiach wandakapa simba, kunyange kugoverana chigaro changu chehushe neMwanakomana wangu anodikanwa uye achapwanya zvachose musoro wenyoka uye vavengi vake vose vachaitwa chitsiko chetsoka dzake nekuti ndivo vakawa vakaramba kupfugama nemurairo wangu mukupanduka kwavo uye ndivo vakarwisa chisikwa changu neunyanzvi nehuipi uye vacharipira nekusingaperi.

Saka ndinopika nezita rangu zvakare kuti, ini YHVH Mukuru NDIRI, ndinopika kuti Mesia chete wandakambotuma kuna Israeri uye wandichazombotuma ndiye mwanakomana wangu mumwe chete akaberekwa, Yeshua HaMashiach uyo madzibaba enyu akaramba uye wamunoramba nekuda kwenhema dzavo dzamakagara nhaka kubva pamwoyo yavo yakaomeswa yakapfuudzwa muTalmud yenyu inomhura. Saka ndinouya kwamuri murudo rwangu kwamuri imi Israeri kuitira kuti ndikufumurirei zvizere musungo wamakateerwa naEsau uyo achiri kutsvaga kuparadzwa kwaJakobho.

Chikara chinobva panyika chakakuroyai semuporofita wenyu wenhema uye achamuka ouya kwamuri segwayana renyu rekupira rakazvipira kuna Israeri uye achanyepedzera kuva Moshiach wenyu wechokwadi. Asi pachokwadi akasvibiswa naEsau uye hapana Moshiach achazombomuka kubva kuna Dhani. Zvirokwazvo, anouya kuzoparadza Jakobho asi musungo waanoteera uchateya avo vanomutevera nekuzvidira uye saka vachatorwa nekusingaperi nenhema dzake uye vachagamuchira chiratidzo chake chaachatsvaga kumanikidza kunyika kuti iite uranda nekuparadzwa.

Saka ndinoti kwamuri, imi Israeri, pfugamai mukuteerera Mesia wenyu wechokwadi akauya kwamuri segwayana raMwari, uyo madzibaba enyu akaramba nekuda kwaEsau. Pfugamai kuna Ishe wemadzishe naMambo wemadzimambo uye mugamuchire ruponeso rwangu kuti ndigokudzikinurai kubva mukutukwa kwemurairo nekuti hapana munhu akarurama anouya kwandiri kuburikidza naMozisi, kunze kwemunhu Kristu Jesu wandakashandura nekusingaperi, uye maererano nenyama haachazivikanwi. Nekuti, ndakamukudza nenzira yakafanana naFarao paakakudza Josefa uye akagoverana chigaro chake chehushe, kunze kwekuti pachigaro chehushe aive mukuru kuna Josefa uye akaramba aine masimba ake.

Nekuti:

NDINI iye anobva kusingaperi kusvika kusingaperi uye hapana mukuru kupfuura Mukuru NDIRI!

NDINI iye akatuma Mashoko aya kuvanhu vangu vanodikanwa kuti ndigouya kwamuri ndibvise upofu hwenyu hwechikamu apo mwoyo yenyu inotendeukira zvizere kuna Mesia wenyu! Kunyange Mesia wenyu wechokwadi, Kristu Jesu Ishe akamutswa uye akakudzwa!

NDINI Mwari Asingaperi weKururama asingaziyi, uye ndinobvunza, pane chakaoma here kuti ruoko rwangu rune simba ruitakure?
