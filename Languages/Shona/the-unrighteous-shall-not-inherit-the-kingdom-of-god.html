Kutinhira 5 & 6

Vasakarurama Havangagari Nhaka yeHumambo hwaMwari!

Kubva kuna Yeshua HaMashiach:

Mwanakomana wangu, kana ukagamuchira mashoko angu, nokuviga mirairo yangu mauri; kuti urerekere nzeve yako kungwara, uye uise mwoyo wako pakunzwisisa. Musaomesa mwoyo yenyu, sepamukakatyamadzo, pazuva rokuedzwa murenje: uye muzive kuti ini, Ishe Jesu Kristu, ndinemi nguva dzose uye ndiripo nguva dzose muupenyu hwenyu pamunonamatira kwandiri nokuponeswa kwangu, nokuti ndini musoro woMuviri, uye ndini Ishe wenyu akatendeka, Mwenga wangu anodiwa. Nokuti ndakakugadzirirai nzvimbo neramangwana, uye kune mubairo mukuru kune vakadanwa vangu, vakatendeka, nevasanangurwa.

Handina kukudanirai kurasikirwa nokurangwa uye handina kukudanirai kunyadziswa nokuzvipa mhosva; hamuna kuverenga here kuti hakuna kupiwa mhosva kune avo vari muna Kristu Jesu, vasingafambi nenyama asi nomweya? Saka tendai mashoko angu! Handina kukudanirai kuti murarame mumumvuri wezvivi zvenyu zvekare, kana kurarama mumumvuri wezviito zvenyu zveuhwana, asi ndakakudanirai kuti mufe kunzira dzenyu dzekare dzechivi uye mutore upenyu hutsva mandiri pamunobvisa nyama yenyu, pamunobvisa zvivi zvenyu uye mofamba muupenyu hutsva mandiri. Tarirai! Ndinoita zvinhu zvose zvitsva! Iyi haisi iyo tariro yenyu here?

Vana vangu, ndakakutorai kubva pakuora kwenyika ino irikufa, uye ndakakununurai kubva musimba rerima uye ndakakushandurirai muumambo hwangu, umambo hwaMwari, hunotungamirirwa neni, Mambo wemadzimambo naIshe wemadzishe, uyo Yahweh ari Mwari, nokuti hakuna mumwe. Ndakakudanirai kukubwinya nokunaka mandiri, uye ndakakununurai kubva muumambo hwaSatani, umambo hwerima, ndichikupinzai muHumambo hwangu, umo mune chiedza! Saka, mukai imi makavata, uye mukai kubva kuvakafa, uye ini, Jesu Kristu, ndichakupai chiedza.

Hamuna kuverenga here mutsamba dzandakatumira kuMwenga wangu, dzandakakutumirai, Kereke yangu chaiyo? Hamuzivi here kuti vasakarurama havangagari nhaka yeumambo hwaMwari? Musanyengerwa: kana mhombwe, kana vanonamata zvifananidzo, kana mhombwe, kana vakapfava, kana vanozvishandisa zvisina kufanira nevanhu, Kana mbavha, kana vanokara, kana zvidhakwa, kana vanomhura, kana makororo, havangagari nhaka yeumambo hwaMwari. Uye vamwe venyu makanga makadaro: asi makashambidzwa, asi makaitwa vatsvene, asi makaruramiswa muZita raIshe Jesu, uye noMweya waMwari wedu.

Hamunzwisisi here mirairo yangu, haiwa, kereke inodziya yeRaodhikiya? Handina kukudanirai kuti mudzokere kunyama yenyu, uye handina kukudanirai kuti muenderere mberi munzira dzenyu dzisina kururama. Funga, uye rangarira; uyo anofunga kuti amire, ngaachenjere kuti arege kuwa! Zvichenjererei, nedzidziso: rambai mazviri: nokuti mukuita izvi, muchazviponesa imi, nevanokunzwai. Nzwisisai, vadiki vangu vanodziya, kuti hamugoni kurarama upenyu hwekubvumirana pakati pezvakanaka nezvakaipa, muchifunga kuti makarurama pamberi pangu, pamunosarudza chidiki pane zvakaipa zviviri mumitambo yenyu yesarudzo yandisingabvumiri kana kutsigira.

Budai maari, vanhu vangu, sezvo ndisina kupa mhinduro yezvematongerwo enyika, uye handina kana kumbokupai mutungamiri wenyika kuti aite chero chinhu chikuru zvakare. Makanyengerwa, uye muri kugadzirirwa kutevera nekupa mvumo kumwanakomana wokuparadzwa anofamba pachena pakati penyu, kunyange asati aratidzwa. Asi imi munokumbira kungwara muchaziva kuti ndiani, kuti mudzivise kunyengera kwake. Kwete, kubvumirana hakusi kwenyu, sezvo muri vana vangu nehama, uye handina kuwana ruponeso rwenyu nekupa upenyu hwangu serudzikinuro rwevazhinji nekubvumirana naSatani; nokuti kubvumirana ndiko kujoina mitsara yavo. Saka musakanganisa: Munomira neni mukururama neuchenjeri hwechokwadi, kana kuti majoina mitsara yaSatani muumambo hwake huri kufa, saka TENDEUKAI!

Munowaneyi nekubvumirana nezvakaipa? Hamunzwisisi here kuti munofanira kukunda zvakaipa nezvakanaka? Saka mirai makasimba mandiri uye regai kuchinja-chinja sehove yabuda mumvura. Vazhinji venyu muchiri kupupurutswa nemhepo yose yedzidziso nounyanzvi hwemaoko, zvavanorindira kunyengera nazvo. Ini, Kristu Jesu, ndataura, uye handinganyaradzwi. Vazhinji venyu makazvibvumiranisa nekereke yangu, uye ndinokubvunzai: mucharamba muchikamhina pakati pemafungiro maviri kusvika rini? Kana Ishe ari Mwari, muteverei: asi kana ari Bhaari, muteverei. Zviri nyore, hamugoni kubvumirana uye kusarudza chero rudzi rwezvakaipa uye muchifunga kuti muri kundimiririra; pamunosarudza zvakaipa, munosarudza manyuko ezvakaipa izvozvo saishe wenyu natenzi wenyu muchiva muranda wechivi. Hamuzivi here kuti kune uyo wamunozvipa sevaranda kuti muteerere, muri varanda vake wamunoteerera: kana vechivi kusvika parufu, kana vekuteerera kusvika pakururama?

Ndinzwei uye mundinzwe zvakanaka, Kereke yangu; zivai kuti ndinomira ndichisema miitiro yakatekeshera murudzi rwenyu nepasirese, yamakabvumirana nayo uye mukaitendera muKereke yangu kuti inetse vadiki vangu. Sezvazvakanga zvakaita mumazuva aNoa, ndizvo zvazvichaitawo. Mazuva aNoa yaive nguva iyo nyika yaitongwa nezvinyangadzo zvakaipa zvakaitwa nengirozi dzakawa, mbeu yadzo chaiyo, mbeu yezvakaipa yakaitirwa kuparadzwa kwenyu kwakakwana: uye dzichadzoka, nokuti tarirai, ndinouya nemahofori kuti azadzise hasha dzangu. Vakazviita nekubvisa nekutyora miganhu yechisikigo iyo Baba vangu vakaisa panzvimbo kuitira kuti rugare nekururama zvikunde, asi nekubvisa nekuyambuka zvisina hunhu miganhu chaiyo inooneka muzvisikwa zvacho, iyo Baba vangu vakagadza, vazhinji venyu makazvisvibisa nekubvumirana kwenyu nezvakaipa zvakatekeshera munguva dzenyu. Chinhu chakanga chiripo, ndicho chichavapo: uye chakaitwa, ndicho chichaitwa; uye hakuna chinhu chitsva pasi pezuva. Hamuna kuverenga here, haiwa, imi vekutenda kudiki? Kubvisa miganhu yaMwari pachake kunokonzera kupenga, nehurwere, nekuvhiringidzika kwezvinhu zvose. Vazhinji venyu munofara mukurwara kwepfungwa, muchifunga kuti murume anogona kuva mukadzi uye mukadzi anogona kuva murume, uye munotungamirira vana venyu mumanyepo enyu. Vazhinji venyu munopinda machechi anotungamirirwa nevashumiri venhema veungochani vari pachena uye vanozviratidza muhurwere hwavo, uye munofunga kuti vanotaura vachindimiririra? Zivai izvi: kana muri mumamiriro ezvivi iwayo, ndichakupai kukuchiva kwamunoda zvisina tsarukano, uye muchakohwa zvibereko zvenzira dzenyu dzisina kuchena.

Tarirai! Nokuda kwechikonzero ichi, Mwari akavaisa kukuchiva kunonyadzisa: nokuti kunyange vakadzi vavo vakashandura kushandiswa kwechisikigo kuva kwezvisiri zvechisikigo: Uye saizvozvowo varume, vachisiya kushandiswa kwechisikigo kwemukadzi, vakatsva mukuchiva kwavo mumwe kune mumwe, varume nevarume vachiita zvisina kufanira, uye vachigamuchira mavari mubairo wekukanganisa kwavo wakanga wakakodzera. Asi kwete, hazvina kukwana kutungamirirwa nevakapfava nemanyepo avo; munotendera vana venyu kuti vabvumiraniswe uye kunyange kuremadzwa pamberi pangu.

Kune mhedzisiro huru yakachengeterwa zviito izvi, uye hazvingaendi zvisina kurangwa. Uye zvakadaro munoramba muchipenga, uye kunze kwekunge matendeuka, ndichakusvipirai kubva mumuromo mangu. Uyai kwandiri mukutendeuka, uye mugare mumasaga nemadota, kuti ndizive kutendeseka kwekutendeuka kwenyu uye mucheme uye muungudze pamusoro pezvivi zvenyu kuitira kuti musabhadhara mutengo mukutongwa. Vazhinji venyu munozvikudza zvikuru nokuti munoedza kundiita Ishe muchine mwoyo usina kutenda, nokuti nomwoyo munhu anotenda kusvika pakururama. Saka ivai nekutenda kuti muponeswe; kutenda kwenyu kucharatidzwa kune vose nokuramba kwenyu chivi nokubuda munzira dzenyu dzine tsvina. Vakaipa vanofamba kumativi ose, kana vanhu vakaipisisa vachikudzwa. Asi imi muchiri kubvumirana nenzira dzevasodhomi, uye munobatsira kuunza zvakaipa zvikuru panyika, kusvika pakuti munovhotera vanobata vana chibharo kuti vatungamirire nyika dzenyu. Munoshaya kunyange danho rekutanga rekungwara, uye hamuteveri nzira yangu; uye kana mukaramba, ndichakupai zvakakwana kukuchiva kwenyu kunonyadzisa kwekuparadzwa kwenyama kuti mweya uponeswe.

Regai ndivhure maziso enyu, imi musingazivi uye mapofu nokuzvidira, imi munogara mutsvina nerima, muchiumburuka mumatope. Hamugoni kupa mvumo yenyu kuzvakaipa uye muchifunga kuti muri kundishumira, nokuti ndakaramba zvakaipa nemuedzo kusvika donhwe rekupedzisira reropa rakayerera kubva mandiri richienda kunyika tsvuku, nokuti ndini Adhamu wekupedzisira uye ndakaitwa mweya unopa upenyu. Vazhinji venyu munopofumadzwa nemashoko akanaka nekutaura kwakanaka uye munopa mvumo yenyu yakazara kumatsotsi nevanhu vanotyisa vanobata vana. Vanobata vana chibharo, vanotambudza vana nokuda kwekuchiva kwavo kwakaipa: Nokuti saizvozvo kunouya hasha dzaMwari paBhabhironi Rakavanzika, asi vazhinji venyu munoramba muchipa mvumo yenyu kumhuka dzesango idzi dzakachengeterwa moto wegehena usingaperi usingazombodzimwi. Budai maari, vanhu vangu, kana kuti muchagoverana naye matambudziko ake nekutongwa kunotyisa kuri kuuya paari; uye ndakakuyambirai. Nokuti Kristu anowirirana papi naBheriari? Kana mukagoverana naye manyepo ake, muchagoverana naye matambudziko ake; saka zviparadzanisei zvakakwana mushoko nechiito, uye musabvumirana navo kana napadiki pose. Musararama upenyu husina hanya, hwekubvumirana; nokuti ndicharanga vose vanoita saizvozvo. Munofunga nokuti mune upenyu husingaperi hamugoni kusangana nekurasikirwa kukuru nemhedzisiro? Ndichabvisa mibairo yenyu ndoipa kune vamwe, uye muchava vadiki muumambo hwekudenga kana mukaramba, nokuti ndizvo zvinotaura Yeshua HaMashiach, Chikomba, kuMwenga wangu!

Kubva kuna Yahweh, Mwari Mukuru Asingaperi:

Haiwa, imi vasina maturo munokuvadza chisikwa changu, imi vasina maturo makazvitengesa kuna Satani, imi vasina maturo makaita Satani Mwari wenyu, imi vasina maturo munoramba mwanakomana wangu Jesu Kristu neruponeso rwandakapa muzita rake, imi vasina maturo makatendera vana venyu kuti vatsve mumoto waMoreki. Ndichakupai nyika yamunoda, uye ndakakugadzirirai nyika isina ini, nyika isina mwanakomana wangu Kristu Jesu, nyika ine dziva guru remoto nesarufa rakagadzirirwa imi chete.

Ndichakupai rima ramunoda zvikuru, nokuti chiedza changu hachizovipo imomo, uye muchapedza nguva isingaperi makaparadzaniswa neni, Yahweh, Mwari mukuru anopenya, ane kubwinya nekupenya kusingaonekwi nenyama uye aripo kubvira narini kusvika narini. Imi vapanduki, vakaipa makaparadza chisikwa changu kubvira pakutanga mukuchiva kwenyu neruvengo negodo, zivai kuti makazviunganidzira kurangwa kukuru nekutambudzwa kusingazomboporeswi uye kune vose vanotsauswa nemanyepo enyu uye vachiramba Jesu Kristu, vachiramba kutendeuka kubva kumanyepo enyu kuenda kuchokwadi changu.

Munofunga kuti kukanganisa nzira dzangu nekubvisa miganhu yangu chinhu chakareruka, asi muchanzwisisa hasha dzangu pakarepo moto wekutanga paunokubatai, uye muchamedzwa nemoto nesarufa zvisingazombopukunyuki. Uyezve, makauraya, makabata chibharo, makapamba, makabata chibharo, makakuvadza, makauraya, makaparadza, uye makapinda mumhosva dzinonyangadza zvikuru, kunyange kuchiva nyama isiri yenyu; uye makazvibatanidza nengirozi dzakawa, saka ndichakubatanidzai nedziva remoto randakagadzirira Dhiyabhorosi nengirozi dzake, uye hamungapukunyuki. Hamuna rugare.

Ndichakutongai, nokuti ndiri Mwari wekururamisira, uye ndiri Mwari wekutsiva. Asi, muchamira muchindipikisa nekuzvikudza, muchikumbira nyaya yenyu, muchifunga kuti neimwe nzira ndichatya nzira dzenyu dzesatani dzekuti: ita zvaunoda, ndiyo ichava mutemo wose. Hamuna rugare. Ndicharipira, uye munoti ndiri Mwari ane rudo (zvinova ndizvo zvandiri), asi ipapo munotarisira kuti ndisatonga avo vakasvibisa chisikwa changu, avo vakatambudza vakauraya mwanakomana wangu mumwechete akazvarwa paakazvipa serudzikinuro rwevose; hongu, munotarisira kuti ndisatonga imi nokuda kwenzira dzenyu dzisina maturo pamakauraya, makatambudza, uye makaremara vasina mhosva uye makaziyisa nenzara vadiki vangu nokuda kwezvirongwa zvenyu.

Zvirokwazvo, Satani pachake aizoparadza zvose zviri zvangu nokuda kweudyire hwake, uye kunyange ipapo munoedza kundipomera upenzi mukutonga kwangu kwakarurama pandinouya kuzodzivirira vangu. Mazuva enyu ekutungamirira vadiki vangu mukurasika ava kuda kupera. Tarirai! Chikara chenyu nemuporofita wenhema zvichaparadzwa zvachose, uye ndichazvikanda zviri zvipenyu mudziva guru remoto nesarufa; zvichakumirirai kuti muzobatana nazvo munhaka yenyu.

Kutongwa kukuru kuri pamusoro pako, haiwa, muchinda werima; umambo hwako nevose vanokutevera zvava kuda kuparadzwa zvachose. Nokuti hamusi vamwari, uye hamugoni kuzvinunura imi vasina maturo kubva muruoko rwangu, uye ndichakusungai murima rakadzika-dzika ramunoda zvikuru, kunyange mugomba rakadzika-dzika ndichakusungai necheni huru yamusingagoni kudambura. Hongu, mugomba muchange muri kwezuva rimwe, nokuti zuva rimwe kuna Ishe rakaita semakore ane chiuru, uye makore ane chiuru sezuva rimwe. Uye kunyange ipapo ndichakusunungurai kwechinguva chidiki kuti muedze uye muunganidze vekupedzisira veavo vanyangadzi vaizokuteverai nenzira dzenyu. Uye ipapo ndichakuparadzai mose nemoto, uye zvigaro zveutongi zvichagadzwa, uye ndichatonga imi mose vaiti vezvakaipa, uye muchaenda kukurangwa kusingaperi kubva pachiso chaIshe. Muchatsva pamberi pengirozi napamberi pegwayana, uye dzichakuonai mukutambudzwa nokurangwa, nokuti makavatadzira vose, uye muchabhadhara zvakakwana zvivi zvenyu nokusingaperi.

Ipapo muchaiswa chisimbiso mujeri renyu, uye muchakanganikwa, uye hamuzomboyeukwi zvakare, nokuti zvinhu zvekare zvichapfuura zvokanganikwa. Nokuti tarirai, ndichaita denga idzva nenyika itsva munogara kururama; uye ini, Yahweh, Mwari mukuru asingaperi wevamwari vose, nokuti hakuna akafanana neni, tarirai, ndichaita zvinhu zvose zvitsva, nokuti ini, Yahweh ndazvitaura! Yahweh, Mwari mukuru asingaperi, achaita chigaro chake cheushe panyika, uye nzvimbo yangu yekugara ichava pakati pevanhu, uye hamuzovipozve, haiwa, imi vasina maturo, uye muchatsva nokusingaperi mujeri renyu rakakanganikwa rekutambudzwa ramusingagoni kupukunyuka, nokuti ndichaisa chisimbiso uye hakuna anogona kuchidambura.

Asi, vazhinji venyu munonzwira tsitsi Satani munondipomera upenzi nokuda kwekutora kururamisira nekutsiva mumaoko angu kuti ndinunure avo vakazviita vakakodzera kurangwa kusingaperi kumoto. Inzwai mashoko angu, haiwa, vagari vepanyika: Mwanakomana wangu ari kuuya, uye anouya nekutsiva kukuru, nehasha, neukasha nokuda kwezvakaipa zvakaitwa nemaoko enyu. Nguva dzekutongwa, kunyange kutongwa kwezuva guru raKristu dzava kuda kuputika panyika yose, uye hakuzovi nemushonga kune vakaipa, hakuna mushonga kuna Satani, uye hakuna mushonga kune vose vakamubatana naye, sezvo vose vachaenda mukuparadzwa kusingaperi, uye ndichavadzosera zvose zvavakaitira vasina mhosva.

Naizvozvo, ndichaisa pavari zvido zvemwoyo yavo yakaipa nemabasa avo ose akaipa kwavari nokusingaperi. Onai hasha dzangu dzichivira uye zivai kuti nguva dzekudzoreredzwa kwezvinhu zvose dzava pedyo, uye hapana nhamba dzasara pawachi dzekudenga, nokuti nhamba dzose dzadonha kubva pachiso chewachi, nokuti nguva yapera! Ndinopa zano kune vose vanoverenga mashoko aya kuti vamhanyire kumwanakomana wangu Jesu Kristu mukutendeuka uye vamuite Ishe; nokuti hakuna munhu anouya kwandiri kunze kwekuna Jesu Kristu, nokuti hakuna rimwe zita randakapa rekuponeswa, uye zvifananidzo zvenyu zvose zvichatsva. Kuchine nguva yekuponeswa, uye ani naani anodana zita raIshe kusvika kumagumo achaponeswa. Saka mhanyirai kuna Jesu Kristu muchiri kugona, nokuti zuva raIshe rasvika, uye ndichasunungura Mwanakomana wangu, uye hasha dzeGwayana dzichagutswa. Tsvodai Mwanakomana kuti arege kutsamwa, uye muparare munzira, kana hasha dzake dzangomutswa zvishoma: Vakaropafadzwa vose vanovimba naye.


Kuti Munzwisise: Isaya 13:1 kubva muSeptuagint: Ndinopa murairo, uye ndinouya navo: mahofori ari kuuya kuzozadzisa hasha dzangu, vachifara panguva imwe chete uye vachituka.
