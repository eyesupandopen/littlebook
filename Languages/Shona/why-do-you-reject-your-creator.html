Mumabasa aJohane Mubhabhatidzi
Kurume 09, 2024

Sei Muchiramba Musiki Wenyu?

Ishe vakandipa mashoko aya. Haasi angu, uye ndiri kuapa ndisina chandawedzera kana kubvisa nepandinogona napo.

Sei muchiri kuramba musiki wenyu nenzira dzake? Haana kukupai upenyu husingaperi here? Haana kukupai chingwa choupenyu pachena kune vose vanokumbira here? Haana kukupai rusununguko kubva muchisikwa chino chakawa mandiri, Ishe mukuru uye akatendeka Jesu Kristu here? Ndini nzira, nezvokwadi, noupenyu. Ndini Mambo wamadzimambo naShe wadzishe, uye ndakapa upenyu hwangu pamuchinjikwa nokuda kwenyu, kuti mugofamba muumambo hwangu kana mukangogamuchira chipo choupenyu husingaperi. Handina kukudanai nemazita here? Sei muchizviti hamuna kukodzera upenyu husingaperi, iro ropa rangu rorudzikinuro richikuitai vakakodzera kana mukangodana pazita rangu mukandiita Ishe? Ndini Mwanakomana waMwari, Mwanakomana woMunhu, Kristu: Jesu weNazareta, Ishe akatendeka, zvirokwazvo Ishe mukuru! Hakuna mukuru kunze kwaMwari, akandipa zvinhu zvose.

Tarirai! Zuva renjodzi riri pamusoro penyu, zuva rokutongwa nenhamo. Zvivi zveAmerica zvakafanana nezvivi zveSodhoma neGomora, uye dai aisazotonga hure guru, angadai asina kururama kumarudzi aakaparadza mukutonga. Hure iroro guru, rinoshuva nyama isiri yaro, iro rakabatsira zvikuru mukuvaka hurongwa hwechikara nokuda kwemwanakomana wokuparadzwa, zvirokwazvo, uyo anofamba pakati penyu achinyengera vasanangurwa vangu nguva yake isati yasvika. Kutongwa kukuru nenjodzi zvichawira pakarepo. America, unotongwa kubva kunze uye unotongwa kubva mukati.

Ruoko rwevavengi vako rwava kuda kuva pamutsipa wako, uye uchapidigurwa nechisimba, uye marudzi haachazonwi zvakare upombwe hwako hwehupombwe hwawakafadza nahwo vose. Vavengi vako vakapiwa kukunda pamusoro pako, kunyange iwe uchivimba zvisina maturo nesimba rako rechiuto randakakutorera. Uri rudzi ruri mumaketani, rwakamirira kurangarirwa kwako kwekupedzisira pamberi paIshe! Hapana chinhu chikuru pamusoro pako, asi zvivi zvako nezvakaipa zvako munhuhwi unonyangadza mumhino dzaBaba vangu.

Uchatongwa kubva mukati, sezvo kuzununguswa kune simba, kwechisimba kuchakuparadza pakarepo uye pasina mushonga. Hure guru richawisirwa pasi seyambiro kune vose uye richava kutanga kwenguva yerima guru kupfuura dzose. Harichazopidiguri marudzi zvisina kururama.

Harichazotambudzi nyika nezvakaipa zvaro. Harichazonyengeri vanhu vangu nezvinonyangadza zvaro. Hure guru rakasvibisa sungano yaBaba vangu, murarabungu iwoyo unokudzwa waakapa kuna Noa sevimbiso yokuti haachazofukidzi nyika nemvura zvakare. Kunyange nyika isingazombofukidzwi nemvura zvakare, mafungu okuparadza achawira pamusoro pevakawanda, uye moto unopedza zvose wava kusvika.

Vanhu vangu, musava vadyidzani vezvivi zvaro, matambudziko, kutongwa, nenjodzi. Ndichiri kukugadzirirai nzvimbo patafura yangu kana mukatendeukira kwandiri nemoyo wenyu wose.

Tendevukai! Reururai zvivi zvenyu kwandiri, Kristu Jesu! Hamuna kuverenga here kuti vasakarurama havangagari nhaka yoUmambo hwaMwari? Munoda here kuti mubairo wenyu utorwe upiwe mumwe? Musafunga kuti muchapinda pamberi pangu muri mukusateerera kwokuzvidira; Baba vangu havana kuzivisa here kuti, "Ndini mutsvene, naizvozvo ivai vatsvene?" Mhanyirai kwandiri muzviise pasi poruponeso rwangu, nokuti ndini Ishe wadzishe naMambo wamadzimambo, uye mazuva okukumbirisa rudzi rune mutsipa wakaoma ava kusvika kumagumo.

Ndakatambanudza ruoko rwangu rwokuponesa kwauri kwenguva yakareba sei, asi iwe uchiramba Mwari wemadzibaba ako nenyasha dzangu dzinoponesa dzinogona kukuponesa zvakakwana? Uyai kwandiri muvanze muchipfuva changu; zvirokwazvo, ndichakuvanzai kubva pazuva guru renjodzi riri pamusoro penyu. Musabatana neavo vakaramba zita rangu; Kristu haana kuwirirana naBheriari, asi vanhu vangu vachiri kuroyiwa nechiedza chake chenhema. Chemai muungudze mutendevuke kubva panzira dzenyu dzokudziya. Dzokerai kurudo rwenyu rwokutanga mutsvake chiso changu kuchine nguva.

Ropa rangu rorudzikinuro richakusukai kana mukangobuda muzvivi zvenyu. Ndini Mwanakomana woMunhu, mupristi mukuru nokusingaperi maererano nourongwa hwaMerkizedeki. Ndini iye akazvichengeta akachena pamberi pemiedzo yose, ndakatomboyedzwa naSatani pachake asi ndichiri kukunda. Ndini Mwanakomana mumwe oga waMwari akadira mweya mutsvene pamusoro pevose vakatendeukira kwandiri nevose vachazotendeukira kwandiri. Ini, Jesu Kristu, ndiri zvinhu zvose izvi nezvimwe, uye ndinoranga avo vandinoda kuti kudanwa kwavo kuve nechokwadi uye kwakakwana.

Haiwa iwe munhu, zvivi zvako zvizhinji, asi iwe unozivisa utsvene hwako pachako! Zvivi zvoupombwe nohuroyi. Zvivi zvepharmakeia, uroyi, nouroyi huzhinji. Vanokara, vasina tsitsi, vanozvida, vanoda mafaro, uye vanoda mari. Chii chichaitika nokutenda kwako mupfuma apo pfuma yako inoshaya maturo pakarepo uye urombo nokuparadzwa kukuru zvinouya pamusoro pako pakarepo? Uchanyomba here pamberi petsitsi? Ucharamba uchisarudza zvivi zvako here? Avo vanosarudza zvivi zvavo nokushinga vachaparara mazviri. Musafunga kuti Mwari anosekwa, sezvo ava kuda kuseka vaseki; musava vadyidzani navo.

Nguva yakwana, uye maminitsi okupedzisira ava kusvika uye nguva yava kuda kupera munguva yangu yenyasha, uye hasha dzichabata nyika apo Kereke yechokwadi inotorwa pamberi pemeso enyu chaiwo. Handifari kuona chisikwa changu chichiparadzwa nokuda kwekudarika nokusarurama; asi handina kukupai sarudzo yokugara muumambo hwangu here, asi vazhinji vachiramba nokushinga? Budai maari! Tsiurai murase nzira dzenyu; ndireururei, Jesu Kristu, saIshe, uye regai kuita zvivi.

Handina kupa rugare rukuru kuvanakomana vangu nevanasikana, vakakunda zvivi zvavo neropa rangu chaiyo here? Asi vazhinji vachiri kuramba uchapupu hwavo uye vachiri kuramba kutenda, vachisarudza kuzvikudza kwavo panzvimbo penzira dzangu dzokuzvininipisa. Nzira dzenyu pachadzo musungo uye dzichakutungamirirai kukuparadzwa kwakakwana, kwamusingazomboporeswi. Tendeukirai kwandiri kuchine maminitsi mashoma asara kuti mudaro. Nguva yakwana, uye vose vanodana pazita raIshe vachaponeswa!

Ndinokukokai kuti mugovane mukubwinya kwechigaro changu choumambo uye mugamuchire upenyu neni. Tendeukai kubva panzira dzenyu mudzokere kwandiri, sezvo ndiri Ishe wekusingaperi uye hakuna ruponeso mune mumwe kunze kwangu! Ini, Jesu Kristu, ndiri Mwanakomana waMwari uye ndakauya munyama, ndakarovererwa pamuchinjikwa, ndakamutswa pazuva rechitatu, ndakaitwa mweya unopa upenyu uye ndakagamuchirwa kumatenga kusvikira nguva dzazadziswa. Zvirokwazvo, usiku hwakanyanya, uye nguva yakwana, uye nguva dzokudzorera dzava kusvika.

Vanhu vangu, mirai makasimba musimba rangu muve norugare. Zororai mandiri uye kanguva kadiki kubwinyiswa kwenyu kuchauya pakarepo, uye mubairo mukuru wakachengeterwa imi mumba maBaba vangu. Zvinhu zvandakakugadzirirai hazvina kumbopinda mumoyo menyu, zvirokwazvo, kwete mumoyo womunhu upi noupi. Rugare nokuchengeteka muchawana, uye imi makanga makatendeka pazvinhu zvishoma ndichakupai mubairo wakawanda. Zororai mandiri uye chengetai pfungwa dzenyu pandiri. Zivisanai kutsanya pakati penyu uye muvandudze simba renyu munguva ino yokupedzisira uye muzivise nzira dzangu dzakarurama uye muzivise kutonga kwangu kuri kusvika, sezvo kunyange zvakadaro vazhinji vachaponeswa.

Ndakakuratidzai tsitsi huru, mwoyo murefu mukuru, nyasha zhinji, asi hamuchapi zvinhu izvi kune vamwe. Muchabayira mubairo wenyu here mondimanikidza kuupa mumwe? Kana kuti muchada Mwari nomwoyo wenyu wose, nomweya wenyu wose, nepfungwa dzenyu dzose, nesimba renyu rose, nomuvakidzani wenyu sezvamunozvida? Sei muchishora muvakidzani wenyu uye muchimutonga zvisizvo kuti haana kukodzera upenyu husingaperi imi makamira ipapo makanyarara? Handina kukupai zvakavanzika zvangu kuti muzivise kune vose here? Mirai makasimba mandiri, uye ndichakupai simba rokusvika kune avo wakaipa angada kuvanza kubva paruoko rwangu runoponesa. Shamisai miromo yenyu muzivise mashoko ose oupenyu huno uye mashoko chaiwo okutaura ndichakupai. Goveranai rudo rwenyu kusvikira pasekondi yokupedzisira, nokuti sekondi iyoyo yokupedzisira iri kuswedera pedyo sezvo hwamanda yava kuda kudana vana vangu kumba.

Ndini Ishe akatendeka asingakundikani.

Ndini chiedza chenyika, uye ani naani anodana pazita rangu rine simba haangaparari mumirazvo isingadzimiki; parudzi rwakadaro, rufu rwechipiri haruna simba.

Ndini ane simba guru pakati peane simba, uye zvinhu zvose zviri kudenga nepanyika zvakaiswa mumaoko angu kusvikira kudzorera kwapera, apo vavengi vangu vava chitsiko chetsoka dzangu. Ndini nzira, nezvokwadi, noupenyu.

Ndini Mambo wamadzimambo naShe wadzishe.

Ndini Mwanakomana waMwari, Mwanakomana woMunhu, Kristu: Jesu weNazareta. Ndini mutsvene, naizvozvo ivai vatsvene.

Ndini mupristi mukuru nokusingaperi maererano nourongwa hwaMerkizedeki. Ndini iye akazvichengeta akachena pamberi pemiedzo yose.

Ndini Ishe wekusingaperi, uye hakuna ruponeso mune mumwe kunze kwangu!
