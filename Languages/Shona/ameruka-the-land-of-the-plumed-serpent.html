AmeruKa: Nyika yeNyoka ine Munhenga / Gadzirirai, Ndiri Kuuya!

Inzwai Mashoko aYeshua HaMashiach, anovawo Kristu Jesu, uye nzwisisai kutaura kwangu kuitira kuti mugone kunzwisisa mavambo echokwadi eBhabhironi Rakavanzika, chimiro charo, nechinangwa charo, nekuti zvirokwazvo ndiyo Nyika yenyoka ine munhenga, iyo AmeruKa, nyika inokambaira muchivande ichidya vagari vayo, ichiuraya vasina mhosva, uye ichibayira vose baba naamai muhuwandu hukuru kubvira kare, kusvikira panguva dzino. Nyika yakatukwa inosvibisa vagari vayo muchivande uye nehuwandu hwemhemberero dzinonyangadza nedzinosvibisa dzezvibayiro zveropa neutsinye zvakavigwa munyika ino yakasvibiswa mumirwi mizhinji yezvifananidzo, tsika, kupiswa kweTofeti, neropa rinoyerera pachena, ropa iro rakanyorovesa ivhu rayo apo pasi rinochemera kururamisira kweropa rakadeurwa, uye richiri kudeurwa muhuwandu hukuru. Idzo nyika dzakasvibiswa idzi dzinochema zvikuru uye nenzwi guru kumatenga dzichikumbira kururamisira panguva ino chaiyo, kunyange apo pasi riri kuzununguka zvikuru semunhu akadhakwa ava kuda kuwa, richitokumbira kutongwa kwakarurama uko zvirokwazvo kusiri kunonoka.

Uye iwe Bhabhironi Rakavanzika, hongu iwe: Uri kondinendi isiri tsvene uye yakasvibiswa, nyika yakapedzisira kugarwa kubvira pamazuva aNoa, nyika yakatsaurirwa kuna Heylel Ben Shachar nenzira dzenyoka, nyika yakanyikwa muropa revasina mhosva netsika nemagariro erudzi urwu rwakashata rwenyoka nevasvibisi vakaenda kunosvibisa munguva dzekare. Nyika yakachengetedzwa muchivande nemaoko akavanzika evakawira, yakachengeterwa uye yakarongerwa seAtlantis yavo Itsva, rudzi rwakagadzirwa uye rwakatsaurirwa kumuka kwechikara chinobva mugungwa nechikara chinobva panyika. Rudzi runonyepedzera kuva rwechiKristu, asi muchokwadi irudzi rwevanamati vasatani vakavanzika vakazvisimbisa uye vakagara zvakanaka mumisangano yaro yakasvibiswa uye inosvibisa kubvira kumuduku kusvika kumukuru uye ndiwo masangano aya akavanzika akatsaurirwa kuna Heylel Ben Shachar (uyo wavanonyepa uye nekusaziva vachiti Rusifa), vachizviti nekusaziva kwavo kuti ndivo vanopenya uye vakavhenekerwa, kunyange mukunyengedzwa kwavo nemanyepo avo avakazvisunga nawo nekusingaperi kumhiko dzavo dzisiri tsvene zvachose, vachinyengedza, asi nguva yose vachinyengedzwa neavo vavakasungirirwa kwavari nekusingaperi sevanakomana vechivi.

Ngaizivikanwe, kuti Bhabhironi Rakavanzika ndiyo United States Of America, iyo yekare yainzi Ameru-Ca, nyika yenyoka ine munhenga yakazvonyongoka ikazvishandura kuita hure guru iroro rinogara pamusoro pemvura zhinji uye irudzi rwakaitwa, kuitira kuti chikara chinobva mugungwa nechikara chinobva panyika zvigone kumuka kubva mumadota aro munguva dzazvo dzekuzarurwa. Rudzi rwemhombwe, rudzi rwehupombwe, rudzi rwuri hure guru zvirokwazvo rinozviti muteveri akarurama waJesu weNazareta. Asi muchokwadi irudzi rine vavaki varo vakasununguka kubva paDombo rechokwadi reNheyo, iro dombo guru rekona iro vasina kutenda vanogumburwa naro, iye Jesu weNazareta anovawo Yeshua HaMashiach. Zvirokwazvo, AmeruKa irudzi rwakagadzwa neruoko irworwo rusiri rutsvene uye rwakavanzika, nyika yemasangano akavanzika akatsaurirwa kunyoka muzvishongo zvemukati uye apo simba reRosy Cross rakapinda muAmeruKa, ndipo pakagadzwawo chifukidzo chekupedzisira cheNew Atlantis, chirongwa chakavanzwa kwenguva refu chaizobatanidza zvitendero zvenyoka pasi rose, chichiparadza nekubayira vose kubvira kuboka revapristi vemarudzi aya akawira, uye marudzi aya pachawo aizozvikudza nekupusa achitsvaga kumisa nyoka imwe cheteyo yavakazvitsaurira mumhiko zhinji dzeropa kuti vashumire zvizere, vasinganzwisisi chimiro chechisikwa chaicho chavakazvisunga nacho.

Saka ndiwe AmeruKa, nekuti ndinotaura nekondinendi iyo kubva kuchamhembe kusvika kuchamhembe uye kubva kumabvazuva kusvika kumadokero iri nyika yakatsaurirwa kunyoka ine munhenga yekare iyo yakasvibisa nekunyadzisa nyika idzi, kunyange sezvo hushe hwakamuka ndokuwira mukati memiganhu yayo, hushe huzhinji husina kunyorwa hwenyoka, rudzi rwenyoka ine minhenga uye inobhururuka rwakatsaurirwa kunzanga dzinotungamirirwa nevakadzi nedzemubatanidzwa wevanhu apo mhomho dzakaipa dzaigona kutonga pasi pesimba raHeylel Ben Shachar, nerudzi rwake rwenyoka rwakanyadzisa nyika dzeBhabhironi rakavanzika zvachose, inova nyika yakagarwa apo munhu aidzungaira panyika kubva pakamhara Areka paGomo reArarati nguva yose iyi yapfuura. Uye sezvo vanhu vakarairwa naYahweh, Mukuru NDIRI, vakasungwa pasi pechibvumirano chaNoa kuti vazviparadzire nekupararira nekuzadzazve nyika mukururama kwechibvumirano; vana vaHamu panzvimbo pezvo vakazviunganidza mukusateerera kukuru kuti vazviitire zita munyika dzeShinari, vachizviti: uyai, ngatizvivakirei shongwe yakasimba kuti tizvichengetedze, uye kuti zvirokwazvo tirwe nematenga nekuti Nimurodhi akazviita muvhimi ane simba pamberi paIshe, muvhimi ane simba wevanhu vaakavhima achitamba.

Saka vanhu vakauya kunyika yenyoka ine munhenga kubva kumabvazuva vachiyambuka bhiriji renyika reBering sezvarinozivikanwa mazuva ano, uye vakauya kubva kugungwa kubva kumadokero nenzira dzakasiyana-siyana dzekutengeserana nedzekufamba vakauya kunyika idzi dzakachengeterwa naHeylel Ben Shachar zvinangwa zvake zvakaipa, nyika iyo zvirokwazvo vakawira vakatora zvizere munyika yaivapo panguva iyoyo, nyika yavakaviga zvakavanzika zvizhinji zvavachazobvumidzwa kutora nekukasira uye zvisingatarisirwi, kunyange sezvo mudzivisi wangu achibviswa uye sezvo kutonga kwavo kupfupi kuchibvumidzwa kuitira ruponeso rwaJakobho, uye kuti naivowo vagowira mukutongwa kwemoto sezvo nyaya iyi ichibvumidzwa kuenderera mberi kuti vaparadzwe zvachose, nekuti nguva dzevakawira dzasvika zvirokwazvo uye ndini Kristu Jesu, iyo Shumba yeRudzi rwaJudha ndichapwanya musoro wenyoka iyi yekare mukupedzisa kwekutonga.

Saka zvakaitika kuti Kanani akawana zvakavigwa nevachengeti, uye akatungamirirwa maererano nekuchiva kwemoyo wake paakapinda ndokuba nyika yakanga isiri yake kuti agare mairi achityora chibvumirano chaNoa. Asi akachiva mazuva ekare akanga aparadzwa mumafashamo makuru iwayo mumazuva ekare apo nyika yaivapo panguva iyoyo yakaparara nekuda kwekukwezva kwaSatani kuvachengeti vakawira kuti vazvisanganise nevakadzi ndokubereka vana vavo vakatukwa, vana avo vasiri chinhu kunze kwevana vekunze uye vakarashwa, kutevedzera upenyu uko kunyangadza kwavo kunosungirwa nekusingaperi kuita kuchiva kwakashata uye kwakaora kwemadzibaba avo, uye kuchiva kwemadzibaba avo vachakuita. Naizvozvo, nzwisisai kuti masora aya akaipa, ayo ari zvinonyangadza zvinonyangadza chose, haana mudzi kana bazi kudenga, panyika, uye kunyange pasi penyika uye achadzurwa zvachose, nekusingaperi uye zvachose, kubva pasi pezuva maererano nechivimbiso chechibvumirano chaAdonai-YHVH Mukuru NDIRI, uye vachasungwa nekupiswa mumoto mikuru iyo yekuripira uko honye yavo isingafi uye vose vanokandwa mumirazvo vachagamuchira zvivi zvavo kakawanda muzvipfuva zvavo mukutongwa kwemoto kusingaperi uye kwakarurama kwavasingazombopukunyuki.

Saka zvakaitika kuti Heylel Ben Shachar, muchinda mudiki akakundwa uyo, muzvikudzi mukuru uyo uye musvibisi wezvose zvakachena, akaenda kunodyara masora ake mumunda waYahweh, Mukuru NDIRI, munda iwoyo wakadyarwa apo Elohim akati ngakuve nechiedza kukava nechiedza. Nekuti pakutanga, Elohim akasika matenga nenyika, uye nyika ikava isina chimiro uye isina chinhu sezvo rima rakauya pamusoro peudzamu. Saka zvakaitika kuti Heylel Ben Shachar nechikamu chimwe muzvitatu chengorozi dzake dzakatevera kupanduka kwake vakakandirwa pasi mukunyara, nekukundwa, uye ndiHeylel Ben Shachar, muchinda mudiki akakundwa uyo akachema mukunyara nekukundwa paakakandwa kubva kudenga rematenga mukutsamwa kukuru, akachengeterwa kutongwa kwekupedzisira kwemoto mumoto wezuva guru iroro, moto uyo wakabatidzwa nemirazvo inopisa zvikuru uye yakachena yebhuruu, uye wakagadzirirwa kugamuchira vose vakabatana nekupanduka kwaSatani kubvira kumuduku kusvika kumukuru pakati pehushe hwevakawira.

Saka Elohim akati ngakuve nechiedza kukava nechiedza, uye saka Elohim akagadza nyika yakagadzwa patsva nemurairo wake oga uye neMashoko ake oga uye nezano rake oga chisikwa chakaumbwa, chikaitwa, uye chikasikwa kunyange neMashoko ake ane simba uye asingaperi nesimba nezano rake oga nekuti YHVH haadi chipangamazano. Hakuna chisikwa chakafanana naMwari Asingaperi weKururama, kunyange vachireva nhema uye vachinyepedzera zvinosiririsa. Saka nyika yakaitwa nezvose zvaiva mairi uye nyika iyoyo yakapiwa kuna Adhamu, nekuti munhu akaitirwa nyika, uye nyika yakaitirwa munhu, uyo zvirokwazvo akasikwa nomufananidzo womusiki wake, iye YHVH - Mukuru NDIRI!

Asi nyoka ine mano iyo yakarwisa nhaka yaAdhamu nemanomano ayo, kurwisa nekutyora uko kwakagadziriswa zvizere paKarivhari, pandakanga ini, Jesu weNazareta, ndikataura Mashoko asingaperi ekuti zvapera uye saka ndakatenga vanhu, nyika, nezvose zviri mairi muropa rangu rinoyananisa uye nyika ndeyaIshe nekuzara kwayo, uye ndinouya seShumba yeRudzi rwaJudha kuzotonga nekuita hondo, saka inzwai kuomba kwangu, imi vagari vepanyika nekuti nguva dzenyu dzasvika!

Saka vakawira vakaenda kunosvibisa nekushandura chisikwa kubva pakukwana kwacho kwepakutanga kwachakasikwa nacho. Ivo pachavo havagoni kusika nekuti YHVH haana kumbovasika nesimba rakadaro kana kugona. Naizvozvo, nzwisisai kuti vakawira ava vanozvikudza kuti ndivamwari asi ivo vose vanyepedzeri vanonyepedzera.

Naizvozvo, inzwai Mashoko aYeshua HaMashiach, iye Kristu Jesu sezvo ndichiuya kuzoudza Kereke yangu, Muviri wangu, uye kunyange mwenga wangu nezvenyika dzakaora uye dzakasvibiswa uko vazhinji venyu makaberekerwa nekugara, sezvo Madzibaba enyu aidzungaira panyika pasi pekurangwa kubva kuna Adonai-YHVH, Mukuru NDIRI, iye akadzinga marudzi ekuchamhembe pakati pemarudzi aine gwaro rekurambana muruoko, uye imomo, vakapararira pakati pemarudzi vakachengetwa nekuchengetedzwa kubvira ipapo kunyange nemudzidzisi wavo, nekuti vakabatirira kumurairo waMosesi pavaifamba vachienda kunyika dzekuchamhembe pavakakundwa mukusateerera nemaoko evaAsiriya, asi vakaparadzwa nevaBhabhironi, avo vakazokunda nekurova vasingateereri pakati pemadzibaba angu kubva kumarudzi aJudha naBhenjamini, iro Humambo hwekuChamhembe hwaJudha mumazuva ekare hwakava Judhea apo Idumea naJudha zvakabatanidzwa nenyanga yebakatwa.

Saka makwai akarasika eimba yaIsraeri akaparadzirwa pakati pemarudzi mukusateerera kwawo. Humambo hwekuchamhembe hwakaparadzirwa kumarudzi ekuchamhembe kunyika dzaGogi naMagogi, mhiri kweEurope uye kuAmericas ndiko kwavakaenda. Saka Hama dzavo nevamwe vedzinza ravo vehumambo hwekumaodzanyemba vakaparadzirwa kunze kwenyika mumazuva aTito naVespasian uye zvakare panguva yekupanduka kwakatungamirirwa naSimon Bar Kokhba uye vazhinji vevanhu vangu vakaparadzirwa zvakare kunyika dzekumaodzanyemba nekumabvazuva kweJerusarema kuNorth Africa nekuAlexandria yeEgypt nedzimwe nzvimbo zhinji dzavakadzungaira mukurangwa kwavo, asi vakachengetedzwa naMosesi, kunyange vazhinji vari muuranda kuna Esau, naAshkenazi, uye vazhinji vavo vakanyengedzwa uye vakamirira Mahdi wavo, panzvimbo paYeshua HaMashiach anova Mesia wechokwadi, uye mununuri waIsraeri akavimbiswa kare naAdonai-YHVH, Sera.

Asi, sezvazvakanyorwa ndizvo zvazviri kuti vanhu vangu vazhinji havazivi uye vakapofumadzwa zvishoma pamusoro pezivo yavo yechokwadi nehunhu hwavo. Vakatodimburwa midzi kubva kuna Yahweh, Mukuru NDIRI, uyo muorivhi wechisikigo unodya mafuta emudzi, mudzi iwoyo uri Yehovah-YHVH, Mukuru NDIRI.

Asi vanhu vangu vakashandurwa vasingazivi kuti vavengane mune nhema neruvengo nekusaziva kwezivo yavo yechokwadi, kunyange sezvo vakadzidziswa kutenda nhema dzerudzi urwu rwevapristi venyoka mune chero rudzi rwavakazvisimbisa, rudzi irworwo rwevapristi venyoka vanoshumira Heylel Ben Shachar, serafimi iroro rinopfuta uye rakawira rakachengeterwa moto wekurangwa kusingaperi nekutongwa uko moto wacho wakachengeterwa kutongwa kwakaomesesa kwenyoka iyi yakaipa yakazvisvibisa mukupanduka kusina musoro, yakabatwa mumamiriro ekukundwa nekuwira ayo isingazombopori.

Saka vanhu venyika yangu nehama dzangu vakadzidziswa kuvengana nenzira dzenhema zhinji dzakapaketwa kudzidzisa vanhu vazhinji, asi zvakanyorwa kuti: Zvinhu zvitanhatu izvi zvinovengwa naIshe: hongu, mweya wake unonyangadzwa nezvinomwe: meso anozvikudza, rurimi runoreva nhema, nemaoko anoteura ropa risina mhosva, moyo unofunga zvirongwa zvakaipa, tsoka dzinokurumidza kumhanyira kune zvakaipa, chapupu chenhema chinotaura nhema, naiye anomutsa kukakavara pakati pehama. Saka zvakaitika kuti vana vehumambo hweKudenga vakadzidziswa muchivande, nekunyengedza, neruvengo ruzhinji kuti ivo pachavo ndivo vanoita hama dzavo varanda muTrans-Atlantic, chiito chakaitwa naEsau naAshkenazi mukushumira Heylel Ben Shachar, kwavanopomera vana veHumambo hweKudenga zvivi zvemasora akaba vana kubva kunyika uye kubva kuna vanaamai navanababa vakatengeswa muruvengo rukuru nevavakidzani vavo nemadzinza avo mumaoko evanhu vakaipa munguva dzekare. Saka nzwisisai kuti zviito izvi zvakashata zvakanyorwa zvakakwana kumatenga uye kururamisira nekuripira zvichanaya nekukasira pavagari vepanyika vachiri kutsvaga kuparadzwa kwaJakobho, kunyange zvazvo kuri kuparadzwa kwavo kuchavawira nekukasira uye zvisingatarisirwi zvino, Sera.

Asi, vazhinji venyu munoramba musingazivi nekuti ruvara rweganda renyu rwakasiyana nerwehama dzenyu nehunyanzvi hwaYahweh nekugadzira kwake kwakanaka, asi hapana chimwe chisikwa panyika chinozvivenge nenzira yakadaro nekuda kwenhema dzenyoka muzvisikwa zvose zvaElohim, asi vanhu vangu vanozvibvumira kusungwa neupenzi hwakadaro nenhema dzisina hwaro dzeupenzi dzakapihwa nevakawira pachavo kuti vaparadze vanhu vangu. Tendevukai kubva paupenzi hwakadaro zvino, nekuti hamuna nguva!

Asi, vazhinji venyu munoramba musingazivi kuti muna Kristu Jesu hamuna muJudha kana muHedheni, kana muranda, kana akasununguka asi chisikwa chitsva muna Kristu, zvirokwazvo chisikwa chitsva apo madzinza ekare aAdhamu anonyungudutswa nokusingaperi nenzira yomweya iwoyo woUwana, nokuti zvipikirwa zvakaitirwa Abhurahama akatendeka zvichagarwa nhaka navakarurama vanorarama nokutenda kumwechete kwaAbhurahama akatendeka, zvino zvapiwa zvizere pachiso chaJesu Kristu mukuzvarwa kutsva, asi vazhinji venyu munosarudza kusaziva munzira dzenyu dzisina kudzama dzisiri dzangu, uye dzinofanira kutendeukirwa, nokuti ndakakurayirai kuti mude Mwari nomwoyo wenyu wose, mweya, pfungwa, nesimba; uye kuti mude muvakidzani wenyu sezvamunozviita. Murayiro uyo vazhinji venyu munoramba muruvengo rwenyu rwedzinza rwerudzi rumwechete rwaAdhamu rune ruvara rwakasiyana mukunaka kwokugadzira kwaYahweh. Tendeukai!

Uye zvakanga zvakadaro mumazuva ekare, dzinza raKanani rakawedzera kuzvishatisa nokuzvibatanidza navakawira, kunyange mumazuva ekare, uye sezvavakazvisvibisa muchivi vakazvipira zvizere kune vakawira imomo munyika dzeShinari kunyange mumumvuri weshongwe huru iyoyo yeBhabheri, iyo shongwe yakakoromotswa naYHVH mukukundwa. Nokuti zvakanyorwa zvichinzi: Mushure mokunge nyika yafashamwa nemvura mumazuva aNoa, nyika yakakamurwa nomujenya wenhaka pakati pavanakomana vaNoa mumazuva aPeregi, saka Jafeti akaenda kunhaka yake kunyika dzokuchamhembe. Uye vanhu vakanga vaine mutauro mumwe, uye vanakomana vaHamu vakaungana munyika yakazova Bhabhironi. Uye machinda aNimurodhi akarangana pamwe chete naPuti, Miziraimi, Kushi, naKanani nemhuri dzavo kuti vavake guta ravo uye mariri shongwe yakasimba, zvirokwazvo shongwe refu kuti vagorwa hondo nedenga uye kuti vazviunganidze kuti vazviitire zita mukupandukira murayiro waYahweh wokubereka nokuwanda kuti vagare zvakare panyika. Asi ndidzo nzira dzomunhu wenyama asina kutenda, uyo mukuchiva kwake nokuzvikudza anoshumira chivi uye nokudaro anoshumira Baba vechivi; zvirokwazvo dhiabhorosi, nenzira yokusateerera kwavanhu kuna Yahweh nokuda kwechivi chiri munyama yomunhu wenyama.

Uye kubva kuvazukuru vaNimurodhi vakakura muuipi zvinonzi kubva kune wakaipa kunobuda uipi huzhinji. Uye saka Nimurodhi akasvibisa nyika nenzira dzevakawira uye kubva kuvarozi vake nen'anga dzake kwakauya zvikoro zvezvakavanzika zvekare apo varozi vevakawira vakadzidzisa zvakavanzika zvavo zvisina maturo kune avo vachakandwa mumirazvo nokusingaperi muzvivi zvaSatani, uye vachabatana navo munhaka yavo isingaperi. Uye Nimurodhi akasimuka muumambo hwokutanga pashure pemafashamo aNoa, kunyange apo Nimurodhi akatonga nyika mukusarurama; zvirokwazvo kutonga uko vakawira vanotsvaka kuzviwanira zvizere panyika, asi hapana nguva yendarama yavachava nayo asi kutongwa kwakaomarara panzvimbo pazvo.

Saka Yahweh mukutonga kwake akaita kuti nyika ishamuke ikamedza chikamu chimwe chete pazvitatu cheshongwe yavo, uye moto wakaburuka kubva kudenga ukapedza chimwe chikamu chetatu cheshongwe, uye chikamu chetatu chakasiiwa chichibvumirwa kuramba chiri chiyeuchidzo pamusoro pavo. Naizvozvo, zita rayo rakanzi Bhabheri, nokuti Yahweh akavhiringidza ipapo mutauro wenyika yose: uye kubva ipapo Yahweh akavaparadzira pamusoro penyika yose. Uye marudzi mazhinji navanhu vakaramba uye vakakanganwa Yahweh uyo akapinda musungano naNoa, uye saka vakakanganwa sungano yakaitwa naNoa naYahweh maererano nenhaka yavo uye maererano nemitauro yavaitaura vakakanganwa sungano yaYahweh naNoa, uye vakatendeukira kunzira dzevakawira nokuti hapana imwe sarudzo. (Asi Abhuramu akava Abhurahama uye vakarurama vachararama nokutenda!)

PaBhabhironi Nimurodhi akaita zvakaipa zvizhinji kunyange paakagadzira zvidhori zvizhinji akazviti mwari uye akagadzira utatu hwake hunomhura, zvirokwazvo vatatu vasina utsvene, zvakaitirwa kuseka humwari hwechokwadi nembeu yakapikirwa yomukadzi uye kutsausa vanakomana vavanhu mukusarurama.
