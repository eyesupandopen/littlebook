Chapupu Chiviri: Israeri
Zvinovava Zvinotapira
Zvita 3, 2024

Ropa reGwayana raYahweh

Uyai kuna Ishe wenyu wekusingaperi munzwe Mashoko angu, imi hama dzangu, uye kotamisai mwoyo yenyu mukuteerera Mashoko aYeshua HaMashiach. Nokuti ini, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndichaita kuti munhu akachenjera pakati penyu, imi Israeri, awedzere kuchenjera paunoteerera mirayiridzo yangu kuti mugozogamuchira uchenjeri neruzivo rwakawedzerwa nokuti uchenjeri neruzivo zvichava kugadzikana kwenguva dzenyu nesimba reruponeso, kutya Ishe ndiyo pfuma yenyu.

Naizvozvo, nzwisisai nguva dzamunorarama kuti mufambe zvakarurama uye kuti zvinangwa zvangu zveruponeso zvisvike pakuzadziswa kwakakwana mamuri imi makadanwa maererano nezita rangu, kunyange imi Israeri, kunyange hazvo parizvino musingandizivi uye musingararami nenzira dzangu nokuti Israeri inotongwa naEsau naAshkenaz, asi zvichitaurwa pachena kuti mukuru achashandira mudiki uye Esau kana Ashkenaz havana kodzero kunhaka yaJakobho. Asi Jakobho akaramba Yeshua HaMashiach uye ini ndakatendeukira kumarudzi kwechinguva kuti ndizadzise izvo Israeri isina kumboteerera kuti izadzise zvizere kuti kubva kuna Israeri marudzi akomborerwe uye kuti vemarudzi akarasika, vakasara vaponeswe.

Kunyange zvakadaro, pamunotendeutsa mwoyo yenyu mukuteerera inzwi reMashoko emurayiridzo weMessia wenyu wechokwadi, muchadzorerwa sevanhu vakasarudzika vaYahweh, kunyange madzibaba enyu akatsauka kubva kuna Yahweh kare kare. Hongu, vakatsauka kubva kuna Yahweh pavakandiramba, ini Jesu weNazareta, anovawo Yeshua HaMashiach, kunyange mumazuva aPondiyo Pirato paaibata ushe muJudhiya pandakaunzwa nevapristi vavo vakuru neSanihedrini mumaoko evanhu vane zvivi kuti vaite zvinhu izvo mutemo waivarambidza zvachose kuita, asi muruvengo rwavo kwandiri uye kuzvinhu zvose zvaYahweh vakazviita nekukurumidza neutsinye hukuru. Uye saizvozvo ruponeso rwaAdhamu rwakaitwa nemaoko evavengi vake, kuti ani nani anotenda uye anodana zita raJesu weNazareta anovawo Yeshua HaMashiach achaponeswa zvakakwana.

Uye ndizvo zvazviri nokuti ndiri gwayana raYahweh, rudzikinuro rwezvivi zvevanhu rwaigona sei kuitwa kunze kwekuti ropa rangu risina chivi radeurwa sechibayiro kuti ribhadhare mutengo usingatengeki. Nzwisisai kuti kunyange hazvo munhu wenyama achizvirumbidza nekuzvikudza kukuru kuti akazvisika uye anozvigutsa muzvinhu zvose, munhu wenyama akawira mumamiriro ezvivi neekushorwa ekusarurama nekuzvidzwa kwaakakodzera.

Asi, munyasha dzangu netsitsi nechibayiro changu paGogota pandakazvipa kuna Yahweh segwayana raMwari pandakazvipa mukurwadziwa kukuru donhwe rangu rekupedzisira reropa parakayerera paGogota, kusvika pakufema kwangu kwekupedzisira pandakataura mashoko okuti zvapera. Uye ndakavigwa, ndikamukazve pazuva rechitatu maererano nemagwaro; sekupika kwakaita Yahweh kuti achaita, nokuti hazvigoneki kuti rufu rubate ropa risina chivi. Uye Yahweh akandimutsa uko, nguva pfupi pashure pacho ndakapinda munzvimbo tsvenetsvene kudenga chaiko, uko ndakazvipa semupristi mukuru nekusingaperi kuna Yahweh, iye mukuru NDINI, chibayiro chaakagamuchira nekusingaperi. Zvino sezvo muine mupristi mukuru akadaro, akapinda kudenga, iye Jesu mwanakomana waYahweh, ngatibatisisei chitendero chedu. Nokuti hamuna mupristi mukuru, imi Israeri, asingagoni kunzwira tsitsi kushaya simba kwenyu, asi wakaedzwa pazvinhu zvose saizvozvo, asi asina chivi.

Saka ndinobvunza, imi Israeri, chii chapera? Saka ndinopindura pachena: Sungano yangu itsva, inova sungano isingaperi kunyange Testamente yangu Itsva! Nokuti testamente inosimbiswa kana vanhu vafa: nokuti haisati yava nesimba kana uyo akaiita achiri mupenyu; uye zvinenge zvinhu zvose zvinonatswa neropa maererano nemutemo, uye pasina kudeurwa kweropa hapana kuregererwa kwezvivi. Nenzira imwecheteyo seTestamente Yekare apo Mosesi akanga ataura murayiro wose kuvanhu, maererano neMutemo, akatora ropa remhuru nerembudzi, nemvura neshinda yepepuru nehisopi, akasasa bhuku rose, nevanhu vose, achiti, Iri iropa retestamente, yamakarayirwa naMwari.

Asi, testamente Yekare, kunyange testamente yekare yaMosesi haina kumborongerwa kuva mhedziso yeurongwa hwaYahweh hwerudzikinuro rwevanhu, uye Israeri haina kumbofanira kuungana mukuzvikudza, ichiumba mapoka anokakavadzana pamusoro pezvikamu zvakawanda zvisingakoshi, uye zvekuuraya. Asi panzvimbo pezvo Israeri yaifanira kumira sechiratidzo cheruponeso kune vemamwe marudzi kuti marudzi aone kuti kuna Mwari muIsraeri uye kuti marudzi adire muIsraeri kuzodzidza nezvaYahweh sezvazvakanyorwa munguva dzekare.

Naizvozvo, nzwisisai chimiro chenguva pfupi chaMosesi sezvaiitwa naIsraeri munguva dzisati dzavepo Testamente yangu Itsva uye nzwisisai kuti Mosesi anorarama zvizere muMessia wenyu wechokwadi, iye mutsvene waMwari akararama zvakakwana maererano naMosesi uye akapa upenyu hwake kamwe chete, zvachose, uye nekusingaperi kuti abhadhare murango waMosesi nokuda kwenyu. Sezvazvakanyorwa, naizvozvo, nzwisisai Testamente yangu Itsva, imi Israeri: Nokuti Mutemo une mumvuri wezvinhu zvakanaka zvinouya, uye kwete mufananidzo chaiwo wezvinhu, haugoni nezvibayiro izvozvo, zvavanopa gore negore nguva dzose, kutsvenesa vanouya ikoko. Nokuti hazvaizorega here kupiwa, nokuti vanouya, kana vachinge vanatswa, vaisazova nehana yezvivi zvakare? Asi muzvibayiro izvozvo, mune kurangarirwa kwezvivi zvakare gore rega rega.

Nokuti hazvigoneki kuti ropa renzombe nerembudzi ribvise zvivi. Uye ndizvo zvazviri hama dzangu, kuti Mosesi aigona kungonatsa zvivi kwenguva pfupi, asi zvivi zvakawanda pasi paMosesi zvakaramba zviripo apo avo vakaita zvinhu zvakadaro vakagurwa kubva kuvanhu nekusingaperi nokuda kwezvivi zvavo. Naizvozvo, Mosesi aiva mumvuri wezvinhu zvaizouya, zvinhu izvo zvakazadziswa zvizere muchibayiro changu, nokuti ropa rangu risina chivi rinodzikinura zvivi zvose, kamwe chete uye nekusingaperi, kunyange kuitira kuti vose vari vaAdhamu vawane ruponeso nazvo. Asi kune avo vanogamuchira chiratidzo chechikara, havazomboregererwi.

Uye ndizvo zvazvakanyorwa pamusoro paYeshua HaMashiach, kuti noupenyu hwangu nokuteerera kwangu Adhamu aigona kudzorerwa kuna Elohim, musiki wake wekusingaperi uye akarurama. Naizvozvo, paanouya munyika, anoti, chibayiro nechipo hamuna kuda: asi muviri makandigadzirira. Muzvibayiro zvinopiswa, nezvibayiro zvezvivi, hamuna kufadzwa nazvo. Ipapo ndakati, Tarirai, ndinouya (Pakutanga kwebhuku zvakanyorwa pamusoro pangu) kuti ndiite kuda kwenyu, imi Mwari. Pamusoro, paakati, Chibayiro nechipo, nezvibayiro zvinopiswa, nezvibayiro zvezvivi hamuna kuda, uye hamuna kufadzwa nazvo (zvinopiwa noMutemo) Ipapo akati, Tarirai, ndinouya kuzoita kuda kwenyu, imi Mwari, anobvisa chekutanga, kuti asimbise chechipiri. Nokuda ikoko tinotsveneswa, kunyange nokupiwa kwomuviri waJesu Kristu kamwe chete. Uye Mupristi wose waRevhi anomira zuva nezuva achishumira, uye kazhinji anopa rudzi rumwe rwechipo, chisingagoni kubvisa zvivi: Asi murume uyu mushure mekunge apa chibayiro chimwe chete chezvivi, anogara nekusingaperi kuruoko rwerudyi rwaMwari, Uye kubva zvino anomirira, kusvikira vavengi vake vaitwa chitsiko chetsoka dzake. Nokuti nechipo chimwe chete akatsvenesa nekusingaperi avo vanotsveneswa nacho.

Nokuti Yahweh anotipupurirawo: nokuti mushure mokunge ataura kare, Iyi ndiyo Testamente yandichaita navo mushure memazuva iwayo, anodaro Elohim, ndichaisa mitemo yangu mumwoyo yavo, uye mupfungwa dzavo ndichainyora. Uye zvivi zvavo nokuipa kwavo handichazozvirangariri. Zvino pane kuregererwa kwezvinhu izvi, hapachina chipo chechivi. Uye imi munomira munguva yekusaziva kwenyika apo Madzibaba enyu kare akarega uyo ari Gwayana rePasika rakakwana raYahweh pachake, kunyange apo Yahweh akada nyika zvikuru zvokuti akapa mwanakomana wake mumwe oga kuti ani naani anotenda muzita raYeshua HaMashiach, kunyange Jesu Kristu kunyange munhu iyeye achaponeswa zvakakwana. Uye saka ndinokudanai kuti mutendeuke kubva mukusaziva kwenyu noupofu hwenyu hwechikamu, imi Israeri, kunyange nokuramba zvizere uchenjeri hunomhura hwaEsau huri muTalmud yake yakasviba uye mudzokere kuMashoko akachena aYahweh, kunyange Mashoko iwayo akachengetedzwa mukati memutsara weMagwaro eByzantine, kunyange Textus Receptus sezvakachengetedzwa mugwaro raStephens.

Naizvozvo, nzwisisai zvinoreva kuramba Yeshua HaMashiach nekusingaperi kwamuri Israeri kuti munzwisise kukomba kwekuramba kwakataurwa. Nokuti kana mukatadza nokuzvidira mushure mokunge magamuchira uye mabvuma chokwadi ichocho, hapachina chibayiro chezvivi, asi kutarisira kunotyisa kwekutongwa, nomoto unotyisa, uchaparadza vadzivisi. Uyo akazvidza Mutemo waMosesi, akafa asina tsitsi pasi pezvapupu zviviri, kana zvitatu: Munofunga kuti kurangwa kwakanyanya sei kwaachakodzera, uyo anotsika Mwanakomana waMwari, uye anoverenga ropa reTestamente Itsva sechinhu chisina kuchena. Naizvozvo, gamuchirai chibayiro changu uye mureurure zvivi zvenyu kwandiri kuti ndikuregererei nokuti ndakatendeka uye ndakarurama kuti ndikuregererei zvivi zvenyu kuti munatswe muropa rangu rinodzikinura uye mureururirane zvikanganiso zvenyu.

Naizvozvo, ndinouya kwamuri mutsitsi dzangu nenyasha dzangu kuti ndikubvisei paupofu hwenyu hwechikamu, imi Israeri, kunyange kuti muwane ruponeso nokuteerera kwenyu inzwi rangu uye kuti mubve pakuramba kweMadzibaba enyu kwakachengetedzwa nekusingaperi muTalmud yenyu yakasviba uye muchitendero chenyu chakasviba chakaitwa naEsau kuti muparadzwe, asi chamunonamatira sekunge uchenjeri hwake hwakapiwa naYahweh pachake, uye kwete naSatani. Imi Israeri, inzwai inzwi rangu rinoponesa uye mugamuchire rudo rwangu kwamuri uye muuye kwandiri nemwoyo yenyu yose yakazaruka uye yakagadzirira kugamuchira kuti muzadzwe nekubwinya kwose nounhu hwakanaka.

Ndinokupai kuti munzwisise kuregererwa kwakakwana nekudzoreredzwa kunounzwa neropa rangu rinodzikinura kune vose vanondigamuchira saIshe wavo anoponesa, uye saMessia wavo wechokwadi, nokuti ndizvo zvandiri. Uye sezvo Adhamu akasikwa, akaumbwa, uye akaitwa asina chivi uye mukukwana kuzere kusvikira pazuva raakatadza nokuzvidira, saizvozvowo kubhadhara chivi chaAdhamu kunoda ropa risina chivi, kunyange chinodikanwa chisingagoneki kune vose vanogara panyika muuori kuti vape. Uye murudo rwaYahweh akapa kukwana kwakadaro neropa risina chivi mumuviri wemwanakomana wake mumwe oga anopiwa sechibayiro chezvivi zvaAdhamu, naizvozvo hapachina chibayiro chechivi.

Ndini iye akazvipa zvizere nokuda kwezvivi zvaAdhamu akawa.

Ndini iye akazvipa zvizere murudzikinuro rusingaperi kuti vose vanozviisa pasi poruponeso rusingaperi vanatswe nazvo.

Ndini iye ari Mupristi wenyu Mukuru nekusingaperi maererano neUrongwa hwaMerkizedeki, kunyange iye akazvipa sePasika yakakwana kamwe chete, zvachose, uye nekusingaperi.

Ndini Yeshua HaMashiach, kunyange iye ari Messia wenyu wechokwadi uye kutonga kwangu hakuna magumo.

Ndini Kristu Jesu, uye ndatumira Mashoko angu nomutumwa wangu nomuranda wangu uye nomutumwa wangu nomuranda wangu Mashoko angu atumwa.

Naizvozvo, nzwisisai kuti kuzadziswa kwezvinhu zvose kwasvika, imi Israeri, uye ndiri kukudanai kwandiri nenzira yeEvhangeri Isigaperi inopiwa munguva dzakakodzera dzekuzarurirwa uye nguva dzekuzarurirwa ndidzo zvino. Nokuti kubva pakutanga Yahweh akataura kunyoka mumunda panguva apo chivi chakapinda munyika, ndichaisa ruvengo pakati pako nomukadzi uye pakati pembeu yako nembeu yake, iwe uchamukuvadza chitsitsinho, uye iye achakukuvadza musoro.

Uye saka mukufamba kwenguva Dhavhidhi, paaiva mukomana mudiki wemufudzi, akava Mambo weIsraeri ushe husati hwakamurwa; uyo akavawo murume maererano nemwoyo waYahweh, akadzivirira Israeri uye akauraya hofori iyoyo yechiFirisitia, kunyange Goriati weGati aiva masora akabva kuRephaim! Saka Dhavhidhi mukomana mudiki wemufudzi akaenda kunorwa naGoriati weGati kunyange semufudzi, netsvimbo, homwe nechireyi, achienda kunorwa nehofori yehondo, asi Dhavhidhi akaenda kunorwa muzita raYahweh, uyo vaFirisitia vakashora, uye Dhavhidhi akamira kunorwa nevavengi veIsraeri kuti hondo yaElohim ikunde.

Sezvo dombo kubva pachireyi chaDhavhidhi rakaita kuti hofori iyi isina kuchena iwire pasi ichikambaira, Dhavhidhi akatora munondo wehofori iyi akagura musoro wayo. Uye sezvazvakanyorwa kare: Uye Dhavhidhi akatora musoro womuFirisitia, akauisa kuJerusarema, asi nhumbi dzake dzokurwa nadzo akadzisa mutende rake. Saka Dhavhidhi akaviga dehenya raGoriati weGati kunze kwemasvingo eguta reJerusarema. Asi, muchibayiro changu segwayana raMwari, ndakatorwa kunze kwemasvingo eguta uye ipapo paGogota ndakarovererwa, kunyange nzvimbo yedehenya. Uye pandakarovererwa, vakandiroverera pamuti iwoyo wakaoma apo vakakuvadza chitsitsinho changu nezvipikiri zvavo pavakandiroverera padanda iroro rakaoma. Uye saka kukunda kwakawanikwa ipapo nokuda kwaAdhamu apo chitsitsinho changu chakakuvadzwa ipapo paKarivhari apo musoro waSatani wakapwanywawo sezvo ropa rangu rakayerera richiunza kukunda pamusoro pembeu yenyoka nekusingaperi. Naizvozvo, nenzira imwecheteyo iyo mukomana wemufudzi akakunda mbeu yaSatani, saizvozvowo Mufudzi mukuru akakunda Satani nekusingaperi muchibayiro changu cherudo chandakaita nokuda kwezvivi zvaAdhamu.

Asi, pandakanga ndichifa mukurwadziwa kukuru semupari wemhosva (mhosva yandisina mhosva nayo) kunze kwemasuo eguta iro dzvene, ropa rangu risina chivi rakabuda mumuviri wangu mukumiririra kwakakwana kwemunhu ane zvivi wevhu dzvuku achisvika parudzikinuro ruzere. Uye sezvo ropa rangu rakanyorovesa ivhu dzvuku panzvimbo yedehenya ipapo paKarivhari musoro wenyoka wakakuvadzwa nekusingaperi, uye mukupedziswa kwezvinhu zvose shato iyoyo yekare ichasungwa uye ikandwe nekusingaperi mumoto wekurangwa uko honye yavo isingafi, nokuti ndiyo nhaka yake.

Uye ndini iye anouya kuzoparadza zvachose ushe hwevavengi kuti shato isungwe uye isimbiswe mugomba rakadzika kwemakore ane chiuru kuti kutonga kwangu kwakarurama panyika kusimbiswe. Pakupera kwazvo achasunungurwa kwechinguva chake chidiki kwaachaunganidza kupanduka kwake kwekupedzisira. Uye pavanorwisa musasa wevatsvene moto uchaburuka kubva kuna Yahweh pachake uye uvaparadze zvachose panguva iyo Satani achakandwa mumoto usingaperi unopfuta nekusingaperi nesarufa inonhuwa.

Naizvozvo, ndinokupai kuti munzwisise, imi Israeri, murango waMosesi unobhadharwa zvizere nechibayiro chaYeshua HaMashiach anova Jesu Kristu mururimi rweChirungu kunyange kuitira kuti munzwisise kukwana kwechibayiro changu nekukwana kwakaitwa neropa rangu rinodzikinura nguva dzose rakadeurwa uye rikapirwa nokuda kwezvivi zvaAdhamu. Nzwisisai zvizere, imi Israeri, kuti dai vakawa vaiziva chakavanzika chaYahweh, vaisazombofa vakaroverera Ishe wenyu weKubwinya! Nokuti nokurovererwa kwangu uko nyoka yakakuvadza chitsitsinho changu kwechinguva, musoro wenyoka zvino wakakuvadzwa nekusingaperi, kunyange kuvadziko yaasingazombopori.

Naizvozvo, imi Israeri, inzwai Mashoko angu uye munzwisise rudo rwangu kwamuri uye kuti ndiri kukudanai munguva ino yekupedzisira. Saka nzwisisai kutaura kwangu nokuti zvakanyorwa: Mwari worugare akamutsa kubva kuvakafa Yeshua HaMashiach, mufudzi mukuru wemakwai, kuburikidza neropa resungano isingaperi, akukwanisei pamabasa ose akanaka, kuti muite kuda kwake, achishanda mamuri izvo zvinofadza pamberi pake kuburikidza naJesu Kristu, kwaari ngakuve nokurumbidzwa nekusingaperi-peri, Ameni.
