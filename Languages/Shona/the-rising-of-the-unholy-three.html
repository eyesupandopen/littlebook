Chapupu Chiviri: Israeri
Zvinovava Zvinotapira
Chikunguru 13, 2024

Kumuka kweVatatu Vasiri Vatsvene!

Saka nguva yasvika kumagumo uye kuuya kwangu kwava pedyo! Zvino ndichakuudzai kurongeka kwezviitiko zvava pedyo kuitika, saka teererai kuti munzwe izwi raMesia wenyu, iye Yeshua HaMashiach, iwo mashoko aIshe wemadzishe, Mambo wemadzimambo, Mesia, uye mupristi mukuru nokusingaperi maererano nourongwa hwaMerkizedeki. Zvichida kuzvikudza kwenyu serudzi kuchapofumadza mwoyo yenyu kwandiri, imi Bhabhironi risinganzwisisike: imi United States. Nokuti munofunga kuti makagara samambokadzi uye hapana njodzi ichakuwirai, uye munozviti, handisi chirikadzi! Asi kuparadzwa kwenyu kuchauya muawa imwe chete uye hamuchazomukizve uye hutungamiri hwenyu hahuchazotambudzizve nyika yaYahweh, nokuti rutongeso rwakapetwa kaviri rwakatemwa naYahweh, uye izvo zvinotemwa naYahweh hapana angazvishandure.

Fungidzirai, kana muchida, nyika isina ChiKristu nenyika dzechiKristu. Hakuchazovi neChechi uye ChiKristu chichange chisiri pamutemo pasi rose uye nguva yeChechi ichange yapera, kunyange nguva yangu yenyasha yakaziviswa navaapositori vangu nevaprofita muzana ramakore rokutanga, uye vakadyara mbeu kwamunokwanisa kuwana pokuhwanda munguva dzino dzenhamo, iro Shoko rangu dzvene nezvakavanzika zvaMwari! Zvakakosha kududzira zviitiko izvi nemutauro chaiwo wakashandiswa naJohane Muoni, nokuti akati: Uye ndakamira pajecha regungwa: ndikaona chikara chichibuda mugungwa, chine misoro minomwe, nenyanga gumi, uye panyanga dzacho korona gumi, uye pamisoro yacho, zita rokumhura. Uye zvakare: Uye ndakaona chimwe chikara chichibuda munyika, uye chakanga chine nyanga mbiri segwayana, uye chakataura seshato. Uye zvakare: shato yakachipa simba rayo, nechigaro chayo, nesimba guru. Uye ndiko kuchaita kumuka kwevatatu vasiri vatsvene, chikara chinobva mugungwa, chikara chinobva panyika uyo ariwo muporofita wenhema naMoshiach wenhema, neshato inovapa simba guru.

Hakuna muvengi waKristu mumwe chete ari pakati, kunyange hazvo mweya yevavengi vaKristu yakabuda munyika ikanyengera vazhinji, asi zvikara zviviri neshato. Chimwe chokutonga gungwa, kana kuti marudzi evaHedheni, uye chimwe chokutonga nyika tsvene, uyo anobva kurudzi rwaDhani uye ari muvaki wetemberi yechikara. Chikara chinobva mugungwa chichatonga marudzi, kana kuti vahedheni kuburikidza nemitemo yaNoa, uye yatovepo, uye yose iri miviri inopesana naKristu. Muvengi waKristu anopesana neni, Kristu Jesu uye anotsiva Kristu Jesu zvakare. Nokuti tarirai! Chakavanzika chekusarurama chakura kwazvo uye Satani akamirira kubva kwenyu, Chechi yangu, iwe Mwenga wangu. Achatonga kubva muchitendero chakabatanidzwa, chakavhenganiswa uye achazviti ndiye Mahdi wavo. Kubatana kwezvitendero kuchavabatanidza, pasi penhema dzokuti nzira dzose dzinoenda kuna mwari mumwe chete. Kunze kwekuti, ini Jesu Kristu Mesia wechokwadi handichabvumidzwi, uye handina mugove mavari uye kuparadzwa kwavo hakunonoki. Saka kubatana kwezvitendero kwavo kuchatungamirira kunzanga yavo yechikara apo chiratidzo chichamanikidzwa pamarudzi kuti atore rutivi munzanga yavo. Musatora chiratidzo, nokuti ani naani anochitora achagoverana mugungwa remoto nechikara nomuprofita wenhema. Uye vose vanotora chiratidzo vachashandurwa majini avo nokusingaperi. Hamuna kuverenga here? Uye pawakaona simbi yakavhenganiswa nevhu rakanyorova, vachazvisanganisa nembeu yevanhu: asi havachanamatirani, sezvo simbi isingasanganiswi nevhu. Avo vanotora chiratidzo vachava varanda vehurongwa huri kusimuka uye vachava varanda vepfungwa dzakabatana dzichaitika kubva mukusanganiswa kwenyama nehunyanzvi.

Gungwa rinomiririra marudzi evaHedheni. Mitemo yenhema yaNoa yatotaura kuti ChiKristu kunamata zvidhori uye inorayira kugurwa musoro sechiroango. Mitemo iyi iripo uye ichatevedzwa nehutsinye. Uye avo vanodana zita raIshe kusvika kumagumo vachaponeswa, nokuti zvakanyorwa: Uye Mwari achapukuta misodzi yose pameso avo: uye hakuchazovi norufu, kana kuchema, kana kurira, uye hakuchazovi norumwe ruvava: nokuti zvinhu zvekare zvapfuura. Uye uyo akanga agere pachigaro choushe, akati, Tarira, ndinoita zvinhu zvose zvitsva. Uye akati kwandiri, nyora: nokuti mashoko aya ndeechokwadi uye akatendeka. Uye ndizvo zvichaita ramangwana renyu, imi vatsvene vangu venguva yekutambudzika, nokuti ndichaponesa Jakobho navose vanodana zita rangu rine simba mururimi rupi zvarwo rwavanodana naro.

Chikara chinobva panyika, uyo ariwo muporofita wenhema, achatonga Israeri, uye achatonga Nyika kubva kunyika yechipikirwa, asi kwete vanhu vaMwari, kwete Israeri yaMwari nokuti imi muri Israeri yaMwari, kunyange vana veWokumusoro-soro vakaiswa chisimbiso cheWokumusoro-soro Mwari, uye chisimbiso chaYahweh hachigoni kuputswa. Uye boka guru richabuda muNhamo yaJakobho uye richatora nzvimbo dzaro dzakakodzera pakati peIsraeri yaMwari, nokuti vose vaJudha nevaHedheni vachaponeswa uye vachava munhu mutsva mumwe chete nokuti ndichavasika patsva mandiri pakumuka kwevakarurama.

Vana vangu, Sinagogi raSatani rakagadzirira kutonga kwavo uye havana kuzvidemba, imi muri mhuka dzine makumbo maviri kwavari, nokuti vanotevera Talmud yeBhabhironi inonyomba, bhuku rinobva kuna Satani kwete kuna Yahweh, nokuti Israeri yakafuratira Yahweh kare kare pavakandiramba, ini Yeshua weNazareta, uye ndichaponesa vanhu vangu. Aya ndiwo marudzi akafanana emakungu apo ndakaudza Madzibaba avo pandakavaudza pachena: Imi muri vababa venyu dhiabhorosi, uye kuchiva kwababa venyu muchaita: anga ari mhondi kubva pakutanga, uye haana kugara muchokwadi, nokuti hamuna chokwadi maari. Kana achitaura nhema, anotaura zvake: nokuti murevi wenhema, uye baba vadzo. Nzwisisai Vana vangu kuti Dhani mwana weshumba uye Dhani haana mumiririri mu144,000. Makungu aya anomirira nechido muvaki wetemberi yavo, uye vatomuziva samambo wavo. Chikara chinobva panyika ndicho chinounza chiratidzo chechikara. Kunyange chirongwa chehondo chembeu, icho chave chakaedzwa uye chakagadziridzwa kuti chigoverwe uye chitevedzwe nerufu kune avo vanoramba. Nyika inomiririra nyika yechipikirwa.

Vana vangu, regai kutenda nhema dzevasingatauri neni, nokuti handina kuvatuma. Vaporofita vangu vechokwadi vanotaura pamwe chete, uye vazhinji, uye havapesani, kana magwaro, nokuti ini ndiri Shoko rakaitwa nyama. Zvikara zviviri izvi zvakaipa hazviwani simba razvo kubva kuna baba vechechi yakaora, asi kuShato pachayo, nokuti Shato ndiye dhiabhorosi. Nzwisisai kuti ChiKristu hachizovi nechikamu zvachose nokuti machechi ose enhema anotongwa uye anoparadzwa nokuti kutonga kunotanga paimba yaMwari, uye Chechi yangu yechokwadi, kunyange vakadanwa vangu Vakatendeka nevakasarudzwa vachatorwa kuenda kumatenga kunogamuchira mibairo yavo uye kutanga kusingaperi kwavo. Uye zvakapiwa kushumba kuti itonge, uye vatorwa vachaonekwa, asi havasi vatorwa asi ngirozi dzakawa idzo Shato iri musoro wadzo. Vazhinji veava vakachengetwa mumaketani kubva pamazuva aNoa asi zvino vasunungurwa nokuda kwekutongwa kwavo kwekupedzisira kusingavati.

Chikara chinobva mugungwa, kunyange iye ane zita rinoshandurwa muchiRungu kubva muchiHebheru: Mheni inowa kubva kudenga: dzidzai kuti mudzidze. Nokuti haadi vakadzi, uye haaremekedzi mwari upi noupi: nokuti achazvikudza pamusoro pavose. Chikara cheshato chinobva mugungwa, nechikara chinobva panyika zvichamuka kubva mumadota semagamba nokuda kwekuparadza Bhabhironi Rakavanzika, hure guru, uye vose vanoshanda pamwe chete. Vanovenga America nechinhu chipi nechipi chine chekuita nezita raJesu Kristu, asi vachapfugama ibvi zvakafanana mukutongwa kwavo, kwavasingazombopori. Saka, magwaro haagoni kunzwisiswa mumamiriro azvino emarudzi nenyika pachayo. Nokuti Yahweh ari kugadzirira kuunza kuzununguka kukuru panyika mukutonga uye miganhu ichatarwa patsva. Iyi ihondo yavo yekupedzisira yekuparadza hurongwa hwekare, nekuunza hurongwa hwaSatani huchatonga vanhu kuburikidza nechikara chinobva mugungwa nechikara chinobva panyika uyo ariwo muporofita wenhema. Nyanga gumi, avo vari madzimambo gumi vachagamuchira simba rekutonga kweawa imwe, nokuti zvakapiwa saizvozvo, vachapa simba ravo nemvumo yavo kuvatatu ava vechikara.

Tarirai! Ndichakupai kurongeka kwekutanga kwezviitiko kuitira kuti muzive nguva dzamunorarama. Zvimwe zvezviitiko izvi zvinoitika zvichiteverana, uye zvimwe zvinoitika panguva imwe chete, uye zviri sezvizvi!

  • Bhabhironi Risinganzwisisike rinoparadzwa apo hondo yenyika inopararira panyika, uye kunyange zvino vari kuronga mauto avo uye vari kuronga kurwisa kwakarongedzwa zvakanaka paBhabhironi Risinganzwisisike. Musatenda vatungamiriri venyu, kana venhau, nokuti ipurupiti yevarevi venhema vari chikamu chekurwisa.
  • Chikara chinobva mugungwa, nechikara chinobva panyika, neshato zvichamuka kubva mumadota ehondo.
  • Zvikara zviviri izvi zvakaipa zvichamira zvichipikisa zvapupu zviviri zvichaprofita kwemwedzi 42, panguva iyo zvichaurayiwa uye zvichakwira kudenga mumazuva matatu nehafu uye ndizvo zvichaita kumuka kwazvo.
  • Sezvo temberi inovakwa, hondo pavatsvene inoenderera mberi kwemwedzi 42 uye chiratidzo chechikara chinomisikidzwa, chinopiwa uye chinotevedzwa pasi pekurwadziwa kwerufu. Zvino nguva yekuchinja, apo nyangadzi yokuparadza inoitwa uye chikara chinobva panyika chichatsvaka kuuraya vose vaizouya kwandiri, kunyange Yeshua HaMashiach panguva iyi.
  • Chikara chinobva panyika chinozviti ndimwari uye chinotendeukira kuvanhu kuti chivauraye, nokuti chinotengesa vanhu nenzira yakafanana naAntioko Epifanesi.
  • Uye ndakatarira, uye tarira, Gwayana rakamira pagomo reZioni, uye pamwe naro zviuru zana nemakumi mana nezvina, zvine Zita raBaba vake rakanyorwa pahuma dzazvo. (Vakaropafadzwa vakafa vari muna Ishe, kubva zvino, hongu, anodaro Mweya, kuti vazorore pamabasa avo, uye mabasa avo anovatevera.)
  • Vangu 144,000 vachadzivirira vanhu vangu uye vachaenda navo kunzvimbo dzekuchengeteka kuti vapone mudutu apo nyika inocheneswa kubva mukusarurama kwose, kunyange makungu akaipa. Nokuti dai ndisina kupfupisa mazuva hapana nyama yaizopona, uye saka ndichadzivirira vazhinji murenje. Izvi zvichaitika muchikamu kuitira kuti vasara vevanhu vaponeswe.
  • Vane 144,000 vanouya panyika vakapfeka nguo dzandichavagadzirira chaidzo, uye hadzigoni kuputswa uye vachava nesimba uye vane simba uye hapana chinhu chemuvengi chichakunda, uye saka zvinangwa zvaYahweh zvichazadzikiswa.

Ndini Yeshua HaMashiach uye ndatumira mashoko aya nomuranda wangu, uye nomuranda wangu mashoko angu atumwa.

Tarisai kumatenga Chechi yangu yakatendeka nokuti ndava kusvika uye ndichakuendesai kuimba yaBaba vangu kwandakakugadzirirai nzvimbo uye munotonga neni saMadzimambo naVapristi, uko Umambo hwangu husingaperi, saka nyararisanai neMashoko aya.
