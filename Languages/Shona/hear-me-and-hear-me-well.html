Chipupuriro Chiviri: Kereke
Mumabasa aPauro Muapostora
Chivabvu 01, 2024
Kutinhira 7

Ndinzwei uye Ndinzwei Zvakanaka!

Ndinzwei uye ndinzwei zvakanaka, imi vaporofita venhema munokanganisa Shoko rangu dzvene, nokuti hamusi vangu, uye hamuna mweya wangu mutsvene, uye makarwisa Mwenga wangu, iyo Kereke yangu yandakadaidza! Inzwai uye teererai yambiro yangu, imi rudzi rusina kutenda, rwakakombama uye rwakatsauka, nokuti muchazvidavirira pamusoro pechakaipa chose! Inzwai mashoko angu eyambiro, imi munotungamirira Kereke yangu munzira isiriyo, nokuti hasha dzangu dzinopfuta pamusoro penyu, nokuti ndini Gwayana raMwari! Nokuti vazhinji venyu muchazvivanza mumapako, nomumatombo emakomo. Uye muchati kumakomo nokumatombo, Wirai pamusoro pedu, mutivanze pachiso chaiye ugere pachigaro choushe, napakutsamwa kweGwayana: Nokuti zuva guru rokutsamwa kwake rasvika, ndianiko ungamira?

Nokuti nyika ichaona kuonekwa kwangu, uye kuzununguka kukuru kuchauya pamusoro pavose, uye kuparadzwa kamwe kamwe kuchabata nyika apo Mwenga wangu anobvutwa achiiswa pakachengeteka. Nokuti ndichabvisa mudzivisi, uye zvakaipa zvose zvichasunungurwa zvachose, uye kutonga kukuru kuchakandwa pasi kubva kudenga, sezvazvakanyorwa mubhuku iro guru rekuzarurirwa kwangu, iro bhuku raZvakazarurwa! Saka ndinozarura kutonga pamusoro penyu, imi mose munosvibisa Kereke yangu: mazuva enyu akaverengwa, uye nokukurumidza mwoyo yenyu ichakundikana kana hasha dzangu dzaratidzwa. Imi vaporofita venhema vasina maturo vakapinda muKereke yangu, makadzidzisa huroyi hwakavhenganiswa nechihedheni neShoko rangu dzvene nenzira dzangu tsvene, uye mucharipira.

Makaita kuti vaduku vangu vatadze uye vabatwe mumatambudziko makuru, zvandichadzorera kwamuri kakapetwa kazana mukutonga kwangu pamusoro penyu. Makatsanangurazve mashoko mumanyoro angu matsvene, makatevedzera manyoro angu matsvene nokuanyorazve nokushandura mashoko aMwari Wechigarire, iwo mashoko aYahweh, uye mucharipira nokusingaperi-peri. Makapinza magwaro akaora muKereke yangu nenzira imwecheteyo madzitateguru enyu asina maturo akaisa chepfu mutsime uye akaisa chepfu muzvokudya chaizvo zvakapiwa kuti vanhu vararame, uye mucharipira nokusingaperi-peri. Shoko rimwe nerimwe risina maturo ramakataura, mari imwe neimwe yetsvina yamakachengetera kuparadzwa kwenyu, pfungwa imwe neimwe yakaipa yamakambova nayo, vavariro imwe neimwe yakaipa yamakatevera icharatidzwa kwamuri, uye mucharipira, kusvika pakobiri rekupedzisira; uye hamungatongogoni kumira pamberi pangu, uye muchatora nzvimbo yenyu mumirazvo yakagadzirirwa naBaba vangu Dhiyabhorosi nengirozi dzake.

Asi nzwisisai kuti hamusi chinhu chinoshamisa uye hamuna zvinoshamisa mamuri, nokuti muri vaapostora venhema, vashandi vanonyengera, munozvishandura kuva vaapostora vaKristu. Uye hazvishamisi, nokuti Satani amene anozvishandura kuva ngirozi yechiedza. Naizvozvo, hachisi chinhu chikuru kana vashumiri vake vakashandurwawo sevashumiri vokururama, vane mugumo uchava maererano nebasa ravo. Nokuti ndizvo zvandakanyorera vaKorinde!

Mucharamba muchiita mabasa enyu muchizadza zvivi zvenyu kusvika pakuzara uye muchizadza kusingaperi kusvika pakuzara nekurangwa kwenyu nokutambudzika kwenyu, nokuti hasha dzangu dzinotyisa pamusoro penyu, uye muchabatana namadzibaba enyu mumoto. Nokuti muri masora pakati pegorosi, uye ndichakudzururai kusvika pakupedzisira; nokuti Baba vangu, iye Yahweh, vakazivisa kuti hamungatongovi nemidzi kana nhaka, nokuti mbeu yenyu ndeyowakaipa, uye mose muri mhondi navanoreva nhema. Nokuti chii chandakaudza madzibaba enyu muJudhiya maJubhiri mazhinji apfuura aya? Imi muri vababa venyu dhiyabhorosi, uye kuchiva kwababa venyu muchaita: aiva mhondi kubva pakutanga, uye haana kugara muchokwadi, nokuti hamuna chokwadi maari. Kana achireva nhema, anotaura zvake: nokuti murevi wenhema, uye baba vadzo.

Zivai izvi, imi mose makaberekerwa mukumhura, iko kumhura Yahweh sezvo madzibaba enyu asina maturo akaedza kushandura mufananidzo waMwari, uyo Adamu akasikwa mauri, kuva zvinonyangadza zvakagadzirirwa kuparadzwa kwevanhu, mazuva enyu ava kuda kupera. Nokuti ndoupi wevaporofita asina kutambudzwa namadzibaba enyu? Uye vakauraya avo vakaratidza kare nezvokuuya kwoWakarurama; wamakava vatengesi navamuurayi zvino. Hamungapunyuki, zvisinei nokuti munoenda kure zvakadini munyika kuti muvanze hasha dzangu; hasha dzangu dzichakuwanai, nokuti hapana chinogona kuvanzwa pameso angu, uye nyika ndeyangu nokuzara kwayo, nokuti ndakaiitenga neropa rangu iro madzitateguru enyu akateura achimhura, nokusimbisa mugumo wenyu mumoto. Kunyange ini, Kristu Jesu, iye akakwidzwa uye akakudzwa, ndiri kuuya kuzokubatai, uye hamungapunyuki.

Ndinzwei, imi vakawa, kunyange imi mose makandibaya, nokuti makazviti, takunda Yahweh uye takauraya mwanakomana wake waakatuma kuzorwisa isu kuti akunde nyika, uye takunda nokusingaperi, nokuti Yeshua afa, uye makafara noupenzi! Imi mapenzi anozvikudza asina kunzwisisa! Hamuna kunzwisisa kuti Mwari aizondimutsa, asunungura marwadzo orufu: nokuti zvakanga zvisingabviri kuti ndibatwe narwo. Makangopwanya chitsitsinho changu, uye hamuna simba pamusoro pangu, uye hamuna kunzwisisa kuti Wokumusoro-soro, iye musiki wenyu, Yahweh, akangokuratidzai zvakavanzika zvisina maturo!

Nokuti aiziva kuti maizomupandukira iye nechisikwa chake uye maizoedza kutora zvaakanga andigadzirira ini, Ishe Jesu Kristu. Vanyoro vangu vachagara nhaka yenyika, nokuti nyika ndeyangu nokuzara kwayo. Nokuti makadzidzisa vanhu ruzivo rwakarambidzwa; makadzidzisa hondo; makatungamirira vakadzi munzira isiriyo mukushongedza maziso nezviso zvakapendwa kuti muzadzise ruchiva rwenyu pavari; makasvibisa Bindu raMwari nokudyara masora pakati pegorosi, uye sora rimwe nerimwe ramakadyara nguva dzose richadzurwa, risungwe, uye rikandwe mumoto pamwechete nemi! Nokuti makakundwa pandakazvipa pachena kuti ndiponese vanhu, uye Mwari akandimutsa kubva kuvakafa kuti ndirege kuzofazve!

Asi makanga musina kuchenjera zvokuti hamuna kunzwisisa kuti ndaizopa upenyu hwangu nokuda kworudo rwaMwari rwaakasika mandiri, nokuti ndini muponesi wenyika! Rudo rwaYahweh harugoni kukundwa, nokuti Yahweh haakundiki, uye hapana muchisikwa chose chine chisikwa chakafanana naye, nokuti ndiye Wokumusoro-soro, uye kupenya kwake noukuru hwake zvine simba rose kubvira narini kusvikira narini! Nokuti ndakakuudzai, "izvi zvaunoita ita nokukurumidza!" Mukusaziva kwenyu nokuzvikudza hamuna kunzwisisa kuti ndakazvibvumira kutorwa navashandi venyu vasina maturo vakaita basa racho rakasviba rokuuraya ropa risina mhosva! Mukusaziva kwenyu kwokuzvikudza, makatadza kuziva kuti ndaiva nemauto engirozi gumi nemaviri andairaira, nokuti panguva ipi neipi ndaigona kunge ndakakumisa. Asi kuda kwaYahweh kwaizoitika sei neimwe nzira, kunze kworudo rwangu nokuteerera kwangu kuna Baba vangu? Nokuti rudo rwaYahwenyi hapana anogona kurukunda! Kwete, kwete kudenga uye kwete panyika, kwete neimwe nzira uye kwete nokusingaperi!

Imi vakawa, kunyange imi machinda enyika ino yakawa, imi maSatani, mose muchatsva mumoto usingadzimiki nokuda kworuvengo rwenyu rusina utsvene nenzira dzenyu dzakaipa kwazvo dzokusateerera nokuparadza. Sezvamakazviita kune mumwe wevaduku ava, hama dzangu, makazviita kwandiri. Imi varindi vakawa, kunyange imi ngirozi dzisina kuteerera dzakasiya nzvimbo yadzo yokutanga, hamuna rugare! Muchakandwa mumoto wamusingazombobvi, uye muchatsva zvachose. Zvinyangadzo zvenyu zvose zvisina utsvene zvamakadyara nokuyambuka miganhu yaYahweh mukushandura kwenyu majini pakati pomunhu nengirozi, pakati pomunhu nechikara, pakati pomunhu nezvisikwa zvegungwa, pakati pezvikara zvemarudzi akasiyana-siyana, kunyange chinhu chose chinonyangadza chamakaita chichakandwa mumoto pamwechete nemi, nokuti hamuna rugare kana kuregererwa!

Makatevera maSatani akaedza kuparadza chisikwa chaYahweh, kusvika pakundiuraya ini, Jesu Kristu, pandakazvipa nemaoko ake sePasika yakakwana yenyika, nokudaro ndichisimbisa rudzikinuro rwevanhu uye ndichisimbisa mugumo wenyu kusvika narini mumoto mukuru usingatongogoni kudzimwa! Mucharipira zvachose zvivi zvenyu zvose nezvinyangadzo, uye basa rimwe nerimwe rakaipa ramakamboita richadzoserwa kwamuri kakapetwa kazana, nokuti vose vachapiwa mubayiro maererano namabasa avo. Uye nzira dzenyu dzose dzakaipa dzichadzoswa pamisoro yenyu kakawanda, nokuti makafunga kuti munogona kundikunda, uye muchiri kufunga nokuzvikudza kuti munogona kukunda asi makakundwa zvachose.

Inzwai mashoko angu, imi nyoka yekare, nokuti kutongwa kwenyu kuri pamusoro penyu uye ndinoona kutya kwenyu, nokuti munotya ramangwana renyu kupfuura chinhu chipi nechipi, imi shumba isina mazino isina maturo! Kutsiva ndokwangu, uye ndicharipira, uye nguva dzenyu dzasvika. Hasha dzaMwari nehasha dzeGwayana dzava kuda kusunungurwa sezvo ndichavhuna zvisimbiso zvinomwe, sezvo ngirozi nomwe dzinotumwa kuridza matarenda okutonga, uye ngirozi nomwe dzinotumwa dzine matambudziko manomwe okupedzisira, uye dzichasunungurwa, nokuti madziri makazadzwa hasha dzaMwari. Nhamburiko zhinji dzichakubatai mose, sezvo ndichiranga zvachose nokuparadza umambo hwenyu. Mambo wenyu mukuru ndichamukunda uye muporofita wenyu wenhema ndichamukunda, uye vachakandwa vari vapenyu mugungwa romoto rinopfuta nesarufa; uye imomo vachakumirirai kuti mubatane navo, uye imomo muchagara makakomberedzwa nomoto nesarufa, uye kutongwa kwenyu kwava kuda kutanga!

Mwenga wangu, usatya! Mashoko angu okutonga haasi ako asi okukunyaradza, nokuti ndakakuponesa pahasha dzava kuda kusunungurwa, uye ndichakuviga pakachengeteka nomubayiro mukuru, uye nokusingaperi uchava neni. Nokuti ndakakusarudza, nokuti ndakakudaidza, uye ndiwe pfuma yangu huru, uye ndiwe pfuma huru yaYahweh; ndiwe kunyange parera romutengo mukuru, nokuti ndakupa zvose zvangu nokuda kwako. Ndinorayira Mwenga wangu kuti atsvake kunzwisisa uye auye kwandiri kuti awane uchenjeri, nokuti ndichakudzidzisa. Nokuti vamwe venyu makatevera dzimwe dzenzira dzevaporofita venhema ava; ingodzokai mukutendeuka kuna Ishe wenyu, uye ndichakugamuchirai.

Nokuti vakakunyengerai nouroyi hwechiKristu, uye makatenda nhema zhinji dzamakatengeserwa, kunyange kudzitaurirwa sechokwadi, asi idzo inhema dzinonyangadza. Matsime aya asina mvura akaedza kukubvisai kwandiri, uye izvozvo hazvigoneki, saka musatya, nokuti handingarambwi! Ndicharamba ndichikusunungurai kuti muve vakasununguka zvechokwadi, uye handinganyaradzwi! Ndinokurwirai nenzira dzakawanda kwazvo dzamusingazivi. Nokuti mose makanga makatarwa musati mazvarwa, nokuti Baba vaikuzivai kunyange mudumbu ramai venyu, uye vakamirira mumwe nomumwe wenyu kwenguva refu yamusingagoni kunzwisisa, uye makagara muri pakati pomwoyo wavo. Makatarwa pakuzvarwa, uye ngirozi dzangu dzakakuchengetai nomurayiro wangu uye dzakakurindai nokukudzivirirai, nokuti ndizvo zvadzinotumirwa. Izvi zvinokushamisai here? Muchateerera here kune zvine vaHebheru zvekutaura? Asi ndoupi weNgirozi waakati panguva ipi neipi, Gara kuruoko rwangu rworudyi, kusvikira ndaita vavengi vako chitsiko chetsoka dzako? Hadzisi dzose here mweya inoshumira, inotumwa kushumira avo, vachava vagari venhaka yoruponeso?

Mwenga wangu, muri vagari venhaka yoruponeso mandiri, Jesu Kristu. Makadaidzwa mandiri; makadzivirirwa mandiri uye neni; makapiwa mukana wokunzwa kuparidzirwa kwokutenda pamusoro pangu kuitira kuti mugone kundiita Ishe wenyu, uye naizvozvo ndakakusimbisai nomweya iwoyo mutsvene wechivimbiso. Zivai kuti chisimbiso hachigoni kuputswa, uye mava kuda kudzikinurwa zvizere kusvika narini, nokuti hamuna ruzivo rwekuzara kwebasa rokusika randava kuda kupedzisa mamuri, Mwenga wangu! Zivai kuti vakawa namasora avo vanokugodorai, uye vanovenga chose chinodiwa naYahweh, uye ndichakutsivirai zvizere pamisoro yavo, nokuti ndini ndichakuvadza musoro wenyoka iyo yekare, nokuti zvakaziviswa, uye izvo zvinotaurwa naYahweh hazvigoni kushandurwa.

Hapana anogona kukuparadzanisai neni; hapana huwandu hwezvakaipa hunogona kuputsa chisimbiso ichocho; muri vangu uye muri vaBaba, nokuti zvose zvinopiwa naBaba kwandiri ndichazvidzosera kuna Mwari kuti tive tose muna vose! Uye akandipa zvinhu zvose kudenga napanyika, kunze kwechigaro chake choushe iye mukuru kwandiri!

Uye ini, Kristu Jesu, ndinozarura mashoko aya kwamuri, uye ndini, Jesu Kristu, ndinotonga vavengi venyu, uye ndichazviita kusvika pakobiri rekupedzisira. Mwenga wangu, rudo rwangu kwamuri harudzimiki, uye ndakakugadzirirai nzvimbo, nokuti mumba maBaba vangu mune dzimba zhinji! Saka nokukurumidza zvikuru mucharoorwa neni nokusingaperi-peri, uye saizvozvo muchagara neni, uye muchazopedzisira manzwisisa ukuru hwechinangwa chenyu mandiri chamakasikirwa kuzadzisa!

Nokuti mandiri, Yahweh akakupai ramangwana nechinangwa, kunyange chinangwa chenguva dzose chava kuda kuzarurirwa kwamuri, nokuti ndiri kuuya! Uye handingarambwi. Tarisirai kuonekwa kwangu. Tarisirai kupenya kwokuuya kwangu, nokuti kwava kuda kuedza pamusoro penyika sezvo ndichionekwa mumakore nokudanidzira kunesimba, nenzwi rengirozi huru, uye nehwamanda yaMwari, nokuti ndinokuudzai zvechokwadi kuti kunyange ngirozi dzinomirira hwamanda iyo yava kuda kurira, uye dzakagadzirira! Ndakagadzirirei uye tarisai, rindai, cherechedzai sezvakarairwa; nokuti nokukurumidza muchandiona nokubwinya kwangu kwakandipfekedza naBaba, nokuti saizvozvo ndichakupfekedzai, nokuti muchava seni, uye muchandiona sezvandiri.

Ndini Gwayana raMwari!

Ndiri kuuya kuzotonga pamusoro poushe hwose hwaSatani, uye havangapunyuki.

Ndini muponesi weNyika!

Ndini Kristu Jesu uye kutsiva ndokwangu uye ndicharipira!

Ndini ndichakuvadza musoro wenyoka, nokuti Mwari Wemasimbaose akazvizivisa! Ndava kuda kuzarurirwa mumakore nokuda kweMwenga wangu, wandichabvuta.

Ndini iye anouya akatasva bhiza jena.

Ndini anonzi Wakatendeka neWechokwadi, uye mukururama ndichatonga nokuita hondo.

Ndiri kuuya!
