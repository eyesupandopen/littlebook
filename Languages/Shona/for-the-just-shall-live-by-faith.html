Kubvumbi 27, 2024

Nokuti Vakarurama Vachararama Nokutenda!

Nokuti vakarurama vachararama nokutenda! Mukai, uye mutse vamwe venyu, kereke yangu yeRaodhikiya isingapisi kana kutonhora, yakanganwa zvakavanzika zvangu; nokuti nguva yapera uye yaswedera pedyo. Uyai kwandiri, vana vangu, nemaoko akazaruka nomwoyo wakazaruka (nokuti maoko angu nomwoyo wangu zvakakuzarukirai); uyai kwandiri nomwoyo munyoro wakagadzirira kugamuchira mashoko angu. Nokuti chii chakarehwa nomukoma wangu Jakobho? Naizvozvo rasai tsvina yose, nokuwanda kwakaipa, mugamuchire nomwoyo munyoro shoko rakasimwa, rinogona kuponesa mweya yenyu. Vana vangu, ivai vateveri vangu, nokuti ndiri munyoro nomwoyo unozvininipisa, uye gamuchirai nomwoyo munyoro Shoko rakasimwa, randakaisa pakati penyu kuti muve nameso okuona nenzeve dzokunzwa. Dzosai mwoyo yenyu kwandiri, nokuti ndini nzira, nechokwadi, noupenyu, uye hakuna munhu anouya kuna Baba kunze kwokubudikidza neni, nokuti kuda kwaBaba vangu kwakaratidzwa pachena, uye vakazviita saizvozvo.

Sei muchitendera mwoyo yenyu kuti ikutambudzei, vana vangu? Sei muchiri kuramba mashoko angu uye muchitsausa vamwe mukukanganisa kwenyu? Nokuti imi, kereke yangu yeRaodhikiya, makanganwa kuti muri ani mandiri, Kristu Jesu, uye makarasikirwa nezvakavanzika zvangu, kunyange ndakazvipa kwamuri. Nokuti ndini Ishe akakutengai neropa rangu; ndini Ishe wemadzishe naMambo wemadzimambo akagara nhaka yenyika kubva kuna Baba vangu, uye ndichagovana nhaka yangu nevakatendeka nevakasarudzwa; ndichagovana nhaka yangu nemi, Mwenga wangu! Saka musatya, nokuti kunyange muri varombo, makapfuma mandiri, nokuti Baba vakaisa zvinhu zvose mumaoko angu. Haiwa, kereke yeRaodhikiya, mava mukadzi asina kutendeka anobvumirana nezvakaipa.

Kwete, hamuna hanya nenyama yeshoko rangu, uye hamunditeveri nomwoyo wenyu wose; hamuna kuverenga here Chirevo? Kunzwisisa kwakanaka kunounza nyasha, asi nzira yevasina kutendeka yakaoma. Uye nzira dzokubvumirana nezvakaipa dzamakasarudza dzakaoma; nzira dzenyu dzisina kutendeka hadzisi dzangu, uye munoedza kufamba munzira yandakakupai netsoka imwe munzira imwe mugoronga, asi mune ushingi hwokundipa mhosva, Yeshua HaMashiach, pamunowira mugoronga sezvamakatsauswa. Nokuti rwendo rwenyu rune kuwa kwakawanda, uye tariro yangu kwamuri ndeyokuti kuwa kwenyu kwakafanana nokuwa kwevakarurama: Nokuti munhu akarurama anowa kanomwe uye anomukazve: asi vakaipa vachawira munjodzi.

Handina kukudanirai kuti muwire munjodzi, vana vangu, kunyange imi, kereke yangu yeRaodhikiya, nokuti handina kukusiyai munguva dzekuedzwa parizvino. Nokuti tarirai! Ndichiri kumira pamukova ndichigogodza nokuti handimbofa ndakava nerutivi munzira dzenyu dzisingapisi kana kutonhora, dzinobvumirana nezvakaipa, asi ndiri kukudanirai kukururama. Ndiri kukudanirai kuti mumire mukururama, nokuti vafudzi vakatsauka vakakunyengerai, vachikutungamirirai munzira dzaBharami; uye vazhinji venyu munoprofita zvenhema nemweya yaJezebheri, yaizoderedza mashoko evaprofita vangu vechokwadi uye inoshuva kuuraya vaprofita vandakatsaura mandiri, nokuti ndini ndakapa zvipo kuvanhu. Nokuti ndakanyorera vaGaratiya pavakagumburwa semi: Ndinoshamiswa, kuti makurumidza kubviswa kwaari, akakudanirai munyasha dzaKristu, kune rimwe Evhangeri: Risiri rimwe. Hongu, verengai izvozvo zvakare, nokuti hakuna rimwe Evhangeri uye hakuna rimwe zita rakapihwa iro vanhu vachaponeswa naro kunze kwerangu, Jesu Kristu. Sei muchitsauswa kubva kwandiri, vadiki vangu? Handikupihizve nekukuchengetai here, nokuti ndini mufudzi akanaka akapa upenyu hwake nokuda kwemakwai. Asi mashoko andisina kutaura, amunoshuva zvikuru uye muchitevera, anonyengedza nzeve dzenyu; nokuti kunzwa ikoko kwava rudo rwenyu rwokutanga munzira dzenyu dzokuzvikudza; nokuti munochengeta nhema kwamuri sekunge ichokwadi, uye makarasikirwa nokunzwisisa kwose.

Makanganwa here nhoroondo yekereke yekutanga? Makanga mune usimbe hwokudzidza here? Musoro wenyaya uyu haunyengedzi nzeve dzenyu zvakakwana kuti munzwe mashoko angu uye muve nokutenda maari here? Nokuti munhu haangararami nechingwa chete asi neshoko RINOSE rinobuda mumuromo maMwari! Kereke yangu yakazvarwa pazuva rePendekosti uye yakanyorwa muna Mabasa kuti muongorore. Nokuti kereke yangu yakatanga muIsraeri, muJudhiya, kunyange vasina kunzwisisa mashoko andakataura naPetro pazuva iroro rinobwinya pandakadurura mweya mutsvene pane vakasarudzwa vangu, pavakataura nedzimwe ndimi sezvavakapiwa kutaura nomweya! Nokuti Petro akaudzei boka revanhu paJerusarema pazuva iroro paakandiprofitira? Nokuti chipikirwa ndechenyu, nechevana venyu, nechevose vari kure, vose vachadanwa naIshe Mwari wedu. Vana vangu, imi ndimi avo vari kure, nokuti vazhinji venyu hamusi veIsraeri panyama, asi ndakakutorai kubva kumarudzi munyasha dzangu kuti mumire mutsitsi dzangu, nokuti makadanwa.

Hamuna kuverenga here? Asi zvino muna Kristu Jesu, imi maimbova kure, mava pedyo neropa raKristu. Asi mudzimba dzenyu dzokunamatira dzakashata dzamunotsaura zvinomhura muzita rangu (kunyange ndisingapindi asi ndinomira pamukova ndichigogodza), munofunga kuti makagamuchira murayiro waMozisi kubva kwandiri, uye mukuzvikudza kwenyu hamunzwisisi kuti Mozisi aiva weIsraeri, uye yaiva Israeri yakapiwa Torah kuti ichengete, basa ravakatadza; nokuti hakuna munhu anokwanisa kuchengeta murayiro waMozisi nokuda kwehuori hwakakonzerwa nenyoka munyama yenyu, nokuti ruzivo rwayo runogara ruchiunza huori, uye mubayiro wechivi rufu. Nokuti vaKorinde vanopupurira mashoko angu: Uyo akatiitawo vashumiri vakakwana veTestamente Itsva, kwete yebhii, asi yomweya: nokuti bhii rinouraya, asi mweya unopa upenyu. Makadanirwa kufamba mumweya, kunyange vazhinji venyu muchishuva zvisizvo bhii romurayiro, risiri renyu, asi rakazadziswa mandiri.

Ndipindurei izvi: Mweya wakauya kwamuri nokuzadzisa kwenyu Torah here? Kana kuti wakauya nokuzadzisa kwangu kwakarurama Torah sePasika yenyu yakakwana? Nokuti chapupu chevaGaratiya chinodanidzira chii? Izvi chete ndingadzidza kwamuri, makagamuchira mweya, nemabasa oMurayiro, kana nokunzwa kwokutenda? Uye zvakare Pauro anoti kuvaGaratiya: Handishori nyasha dzaMwari: nokuti kana kururama kuchiuya noMurayiro, ipapo Kristu akafa pasina. Hamunzwisisi here kuti pakutanga kwenguva kwakanga kune kereke yeJudhiya nekereke yeVemamwe Marudzi? Kunyange kereke yangu yakazvarirwa muJudhiya, Pauro akati kuvaGaratiya uye ndakazvichengeta ipapo: Nokuti iye wakashanda nesimba muna Petro kuUapostora hwevakadzingiswa, iye wakanga ane simba mandiri kuVemamwe Marudzi. Nokuti pakutanga ndakatumira Petro kuVemamwe Marudzi, uye zvakanyorwa muna Mabasa pandakaudza Petro: Izvo Mwari waanatsa, usazviti zvisina kuchena. Nokuti pandakatumira Petro kuna Koneriyasi, chapupu chinotevera chakanyorwa: Uye vekudzingiswa vakatenda, vakashamiswa, vose vakauya naPetro, nokuti paVemamwe Marudziwo pakadururwa chipo chomweya mutsvene. Ndinokubvunzai izvi, vaRaodhikiya vangu, Koneriyasi akawana ruponeso nokutevera Torah here (nokuti aiva munhu anozvipira)? Kana kuti akagamuchira chipikirwa kubudikidza nokutenda? VaKorinde vane izwi panyaya iyi Pauro paakavanyorera: Iro ramunoponeswa naro, kana muchirangarira zvandakakuparidzirai, kunze kwekunge makatenda pasina. Ruponeso rwakauya nokuparidzwa kwokutenda kwete nebhii romurayiro, nokuti kuzviisa pasi pomurayiro waMozisi, kunyange kutenda kwenyu kunova pasina.

Uye saka vaRoma vanozivisa: Asi zvino kururama kwaMwari pasina Murayiro kwakaratidzwa, kuchipupurirwa noMurayiro neVaprofita. Kunyange kururama kwaMwari, kuri nokutenda kwaJesu Kristu kuna vose, napamusoro pavose vanotenda: nokuti hakuna musiyano: Nokuti vose vakatadza, vakasasvika pakubwinya kwaMwari. Nokuti ndakaudza kereke yangu muGaratiya apo Pauro akanyora achiteerera: Nokuti muna Jesu Kristu, kudzingiswa hakubatsiri chinhu, kana kusadzingiswa, asi kutenda kunoshanda norudo. Uye zvakare kuGaratiya Pauro akanyora: Nokuti muna Kristu Jesu kudzingiswa hakubatsiri chinhu kana kusadzingiswa, asi chisikwa chitsva. Saka ruponeso harupiwi nemabasa omurayiro, uye ruponeso rwenyu haruraramiwi uye haruzadzikiswi nemabasa omurayiro. Nokuti kuvaRoma kwakanyorwa: Tarirai, ndinoisa muZioni ibwe rinogumbusa, nedombo rinogumbusa: uye ani naani anotenda maari, haanganyadziswi. Vana vangu, ndini ibwe iroro rinogumbusa nedombo rinogumbusa, nokuti ndini Jesu weNazareta, gwayana raMwari, uye ndakazadzisa murayiro.

Haiwa, imi veRaodhikiya, munofunga kuti ndimi moga makagumburwa panyaya iyi here? Ndinoti kwete. Nokuti munogona kuisa tsamba dzangu muzvikamu zviviri zvikuru, kunze kwekuti zvose zvakanyorerwa munhu mumwe mutsva muna Kristu Jesu. Nokuti tsamba dzangu dzakanyorerwa makereke eVemamwe Marudzi, uye dzimwe dzakanangidzirwa kumakereke eJudhiya, uye nokudaro Petro akanyora nezvekuratidzwa kweChakavanzika chandakaratidza kuna Pauro: Sezvazvakaitawo mutsamba dzake dzose, achitaura madziri pamusoro pezvinhu izvi, mazviri mune zvimwe zvinhu zvakaoma kunzwisiswa, izvo vasingadzidzi nevasina kugadzikana vanokanganisa, sezvavanoitawo mamwe Magwaro, kusvika pakuparadzwa kwavo.

Saka dambudziko renyu harisi idzva, nokuti hakuna muedzo wakakubatai asi wakadai seunowanzoitika kuvanhu: asi Mwari akatendeka, haangakutenderi kuti muedzwe kupfuura zvamunogona; asi pamwe chete nomuedzo achaitawo nzira yokupukunyuka, kuti mugone kuutakura. Muchatora nzira yokupukunyuka iyo Mwari akakupai munyasha dzangu netsitsi dzangu kuti mupukunyuke avo vangakubirai mubayiro wenyu nokukukonzera kuti muwe kubva panyasha here? Mucharamba mashoko angu muchifarira vaJudha vanokudzidzisai zvisizvo kuti munofanira kuchengeta Torah nokuda kworuponeso rwenyu here? Muri mapenzi zvokufunga kuti, matanga murayiro woupenyu mumweya waJesu Kristu, zvino makapiwa kumurayiro wechivi norufu here? Nokuti zvakadini nomurayiro? Vana vangu, ndichakuudzai. Musafunga zvisizvo kuti ndiri izwi renhema rinokutungamirirai mukukanganisa, nokuti vazhinji venyu hamuzivi izwi rangu uye hamundizivi, kunyange ndiri kukudanai! Musafunga kuti ndakauya kuzoparadza murayiro kana vaprofita. Handina kuuya kuzoparadza, asi kuzozadzisa. Nokuti zvirokwazvo ndinoti kwamuri, Kusvikira denga nenyika zvapfuura, yodhi imwe kana munzwa weyodhi, hazvingatongopfuuri pamurayiro, kusvikira zvose zvazadziswa.

Ndini iye akazadzisa murayiro waMozisi, uye mirayiro yawo inochengetwa mukuteerera mashoko angu enyasha netsitsi mutsamba dzangu uye kubudikidza nokutenda kwaJesu Kristu. Munofunga zvisizvo nokuti ndini kuzadziswa kwomurayiro kuti ndinokurudzira ani naani kutyora mirayiro yaMwari, ingava murayiro waMozisi kana mirayiro yaMwari yetsika. Nokuti ndakaudzei avo vaigara paKorinde? Kereke yangu yeRaodhikiya, yandinoda, hamusi imi vokutanga kubata nomuedzo uyu, uye nokudaro ndakaita kuti Pauro anyore: Uyo akatiitawo vashumiri vakakwana veTestamente Itsva, kwete yebhii, asi yomweya: nokuti bhii rinouraya, asi mweya unopa upenyu. Asi kana ushumiri hworufu rwakanyorwa, uye rwakavezwa pamabwe, rwaiva noubwinyi, zvokuti vana veIsraeri vakanga vasingagoni kunyatsotarisa chiso chaMozisi, nokuda kwoubwinyi hwechiso chake, ubwinyi hwaizobviswa: Ushumiri hwomweya hahungavi noubwinyi zvikuru sei? Musakanganisa, vana vangu, bhii rinouraya nokuda kwechivi chiri munyama yenyu chinopomerwa nebhii iroro, kunyange murayiro waMozisi: Saka murayiro mutsvene, uye murayiro mutsvene uye wakarurama uye wakanaka. Izvo zvakanga zvakanaka zvakaitwa rufu kwandiri here? Mwari ngaadaro. Asi chivi, kuti chionekwe sechivi, chichishanda rufu mandiri nechinhu chakanaka: kuti chivi noMurayiro chive chivi chakanyanya. Uye saka ndakaita kuti Pauro audze vaGaratiya: Handishori nyasha dzaMwari: nokuti kana kururama kuchiuya noMurayiro, ipapo Kristu akafa pasina. Vana vangu, makatsauswa, uye ndakakuudzai kuti waini itsva inofanira kuiswa muhombodo itsva, saka mirai makasimba munyasha dzandakakusunungurai nadzo, ini, Kristu Jesu.

Vana vangu, hamugoni kutyora mirayiro yaMwari pamunofamba mumweya, nokuti hazvigoneki. Makavakirwa panheyo dzevaapostora nevaprofita, Jesu Kristu pachake ari ibwe guru rekona. Uye kuvaHebheru kwakataurwa: Nokuti ndichanzwira tsitsi kusarurama kwavo, uye zvivi zvavo nokuipa kwavo handichazozvirangariri. Pakuti anoti, sungano itsva, waita yekutanga kuva yekare. Zvino izvo zvinowora uye zvinosakara zvagadzirira kunyangarika. Vana vangu, kururama hakubvi pakuchengeta murayiro, nokuti chipo chinogamuchirwa nokutenda mandiri, Kristu Jesu. Uye nokuti makaitwa vakarurama mandiri, makasunungurwa pamurayiro wechivi norufu.

Nokuti kumhura kufunga kuti kufamba mumweya woupenyu kune chivi, nokuti ani naani anotenda zvakadaro anoona chibayiro changu chezvivi zvevanhu sechinhu chisina kuchena. Nokuti ndiri mukuru kuna Mozisi, nokuti Mozisi pachake anoti: Ishe Mwari wako achakumutsira Mprofita kubva pakati pako, wehama dzako, akafanana neni, iye muchamuteerera. Inzwai izwi rangu, vana, uye teererai kutaura kwangu, nokuti handina kukuitai vasina murayiro, nokuti vanakomana vaSatani ndivo vasina murayiro, kwete vanakomana vaMwari! Nokuti muna vaRoma Pauro akanyora zvakajeka: Naizvozvo zvino hakuna kupiwa mhosva kune avo vari muna Kristu Jesu, vasingafambi nenyama, asi nomweya. Nokuti murayiro womweya woupenyu, muna Kristu Jesu, wakandisunungura pamurayiro wechivi norufu.

Nokuti izvo murayiro waisagona kuita, pakuti wakanga usina simba nenyama, Mwari achituma Mwanakomana wake, mumufananidzo wenyama yechivi, uye nokuda kwechivi akapa mhosva chivi munyama: Kuti kururama kwomurayiro kuzadziswe matiri, isu tisingafambi nenyama, asi nomweya. Uye zvakare vaRoma vanotaura: Kuzivisa, ndinoti, panguva ino kururama kwake: kuti ave akarurama, uye mururamisi weuyo anotenda muna Jesu. Kuzvirumbidza kuripi, zvino? Kwakabviswa. NoMurayiro upi? Wemabasa? Kwete: asi noMurayiro wokutenda. Naizvozvo, tinogumisa, kuti munhu anoruramiswa nokutenda, pasina mabasa oMurayiro. Nokuti ndakati chii: Nokuti mariri kururama kwaMwari kunoratidzwa kubva pakutenda kusvika pakutenda; sezvazvakanyorwa, Vakarurama vachararama nokutenda.

Asi vazhinji venyu munoisirana mitoro isina kufanira pakuti makatenda vaprofita vaSatani vanotumwa kukuteyai kuti muchengete Torah zvisizvo sechinhu chinodiwa pakuchengetedzwa uye kurarama ruponeso; uye vamwe venyu munomhura muchiti ndakazadzisa murayiro nechibayiro changu kuti imi zvino mugone kuchengeta Torah. Kune rumwe rutivi, muri pachokwadi, nokuti kufamba mumurayiro womweya woupenyu ndiko kuchengeta Torah zvakakwana nokuzvizivisa kwenyu neni; nokuti kereke yangu yakavakirwa zvakasimba mukururama nouchenjeri hwechokwadi pandiri, Kristu Jesu, ibwe guru rekona. Munochengeta Torah nokuteerera kutenda kuri mandiri. Sei muchisarudza izwi revaJudha panzvimbo peizwi rangu dzvene? Sei muchiisira hama dzenyu mitoro isina kufanira, nokuti hamuna kuverenga here? Nokuti ndakatoudza vaKorose: Naizvozvo ngakurege kuva nomunhu anokutongai pakudya, kana pakunwa, kana pamusoro pezuva ripi neripi Dzvene, kana reMwedzi Mutsva, kana remazuva eSabata Izvo zviri mumvuri wezvinhu zvinouya, asi muviri ndewaKristu.

Vana vangu, handipi murayiro mhosva; ndinongoratidza pachena kuti nokuda kwechivi chiri munyama yenyu hamungambochengeti Torah kunze kwokutenda kwenyu mandiri, nokuti vakarurama vachararama nokutenda! Kutenda mandiri chii, mungabvunza? Kutenda mandiri kwakavakirwa pakuvimba nemashoko angu uye kunzwisisa kuti makaziviswa neni pamatanho ose, nokuti muri kereke yangu yandakageza muropa rangu risina chivi; nokuti kuregererwa kwezvivi hakugoneki kuwanikwa nechibayiro chemhuka. Ivai nokutenda mandiri, nokuti kubudikidza nokutenda mandiri Torah inochengetwa, kubudikidza nokutenda mandiri murayiro unozadziswa mamuri, uye muri vakarurama, makaruramiswa, uye makatsaurwa! Pandakamutswa kuupenyu hutsva, nemiwo makamutswa; uye pandakakwidzwa kuruoko rworudyi rwaBaba, nemiwo makagariswa kudenga. Nokuti vaEfeso vanopupura: Asi Mwari akapfuma mutsitsi, nokuda kworudo rwake rukuru rwaakatida narwo, Kunyange patakanga takafa muzvivi, wakatiita vapenyu pamwe naKristu (nenyasha makaponeswa), Uye wakatiita kuti timuke pamwe chete, uye akatiita kuti tigare pamwe chete munzvimbo dzokudenga muna Kristu Jesu. Munofanira kunzwisisa kuti murayiro waMozisi hausi sungano yenyu, nokuti yenyu isungano itsva yakasimbiswa muropa rangu uye yakabhadharwa nokutambura kwangu.

Saka teererai Kutenda, vanhu vangu, uye musave musingapisi kana kutonhora zvakare. Sarudzai upenyu mandiri, Jesu Kristu, nokuti ndizvo zvamakadanirwa. Itai mabasa mazhinji makuru andakakupai, kunyange muminiti ino yekupedzisira. Ndinoda kuti munzwisise kuti pasina ini, hamugoni kuita chinhu, uye munochengeta zvikamu zvose zveTorah nokutenda mandiri. Uyezve, vaJudha vanoshandisa mazita matsvene vakazadza kereke yangu, vachiisa mitoro isina kufanira pane avo vanotsvaka ruponeso uye avo vari vangu. Baba vangu vakandipa zita riri pamusoro pemazita ose, kuti pazita raJesu Kristu mabvi ose apfugame. Munofunga kuti ndingaisa mutoro usina kufanira pamarudzi anotaura mitauro yakawanda kuti ashandise zita rangu, Yeshua HaMashiach, muchiHebheru chete here? Ndinzwisisei uye ndinzwisisei zvakanaka, nokuti ndiri kuuya, uye ndichabata nevasina kuteerera uye avo vangakutambudzai.
