Chapupu Chiviri: Kereke
Mumabasa aJohane Mubhabhatidzi
Kurume 17, 2024

Nyika NdayaIshe!

Nyika ndeyaIshe, nokuzara kwayo: Kunyange mubvuti nembavha vakaisa chitsidzo chavo, vanongogona kuita izvo Baba vangu zvavakapa kuti ruoko rwavo ruzadzise muhurongwa hwavo hutsvene hwerudzikinuro rwevanhu. Musavatya! Vanofanira kuramba vari po kwechinguva, nokuti awa yavo yasvika; asi musavatya, imi vakarurama vangu, vakatendeka; musavatya! Handina kukupai here kukunda pamusoro pavo? Kutambudzika kwaJakobho hakusi kwevakatendeka vangu, uye avo vakauya kwandiri munguva dzekuzorodzwa vachakudzwa zvikuru muimba chaiyo yechigaro choushe chaMwari. Hongu, vanhu vangu vakatendeka, makachengeterwa mubairo mukuru nokuda kwekutendeka kwenyu kwamakaratidza kwandiri nokutendeka.

Ndakakuitai madzimambo navaprista kwandiri muUmambo hwangu huri kuuya uye pamberi paBaba vedu, saka mirai makasimba, vana vangu, mumaminetsi ano okupedzisira eawa yokupedzisira. Kuonekwa kwangu kwava pedyo, uye rudzikinuro rwenyu rwuri pedyo sekufema kwenyu kunotevera, saka mirai makatendeka pamberi pangu, muchiziva kuti ndini simba renyu noruponeso rwenyu uye makaiswa chisimbiso mandiri, sezvo ndakakutengai neropa rangu pandakapa upenyu hwangu nokuda kwenyu kuti muyananiswe murudo naBaba vedu vari kudenga. Ndinoshuva zuva iroro, uye Baba vedu vanoshuva zuva iroro, ramuchamira pamberi pavo mumiviri yandakakuchengeterai murudzikinuro rwenyu ruzere, uye hakuchazovi nokutukwa pamusoro pevasanangurwa vangu.

Haiwa iwe munhu, ndiwe ani kuti uise chitsidzo panhaka yangu? Hauzivi here kuti ndakatenga nyika nezvose zviri mairi neropa rangu risina chivi? Nyika ndeyaIshe nokuzara kwayo, uye kururama kwouya panyika! Kunyange iwe uchida kuparadza nyika nekuita kuti ive nzvimbo isina chinhu yezvinyangadzo zvako zvinonyangadza, kunyange iwe uchipika kuvimbika kumuvengi wangu wekusingaperi uye uchitsvaka kurwadziswa kwako nokuda kwekuwana zvinhu zvenguva pfupi zvisina maturo, haunzwisisi kuti unorwa nemweya yako pachako sezvaunozvichengetera kutsamwa kusvikira pazuva rekutsamwa, pandichapa mubairo kumunhu wese maererano nemabasa ake.

Haiwa iwe munhu benzi, unozviti vamwari vako vachakuponesa, asi ivo vasina mubairo wako, uye wakanyengedzwa uye unonyengedza vamwe mukunyengedzwa kwako. Vamwari vako vematombo havagoni kutaura; zvidhori zvako mumwoyo mako hazvigoni kunzwa; hazvigoni kukuponesa, haiwa iwe munamati wezvidhori! Zvaiva nani kwauri dai usina kumboberekwa, nokuti kutsamwa kwaMwari kunogara pauri nokuda kwenzira dzako dzakaoma mwoyo. Kunyange muromo wako wave guva rakashama, richiburitsa zvinhu zvikuru zvinopikisa Ishe weMadzishe, mashoko ako achadzokera kwauri, uye zvishuwo zvako zvakaipa zvichatendeutsirwa pauri mukurangwa kwako kuri kuuya kusingaperi. Haiwa, dai wakanga watendeukira kwandiri ukagamuchira ruponeso kubva pauri parizvino pakuwa kwako. Haiwa, dai wakanga wangoreurura zita rangu nemiromo yako inoreva nhema. Asi kwete, wakada nhema dzako, uye wakada kunyengedzwa kwako, uye wakatengesa mweya yako kukutongwa kusingaperi nokuda kwechimedu chechingwa chisingagoni kukusimbisa; nokuti ndini chingwa choupenyu! Dambudziko rako richauya pauri pakarepo uye pasina mushonga. Ndichabvarura rudzi rwako; ndichaparadza kutengeserana kwako nehupfumi hwako; munguva pfupi ndichakutorera pfuma yako; zvirokwazvo, ndichakubvisira mufaro wose munguva pfupi, uye urombo hwako nenhamo yako zvichakupedza. Kutsamwa kweGwayana kwoswedera, apo kutsiva kwangu kwandakapiwa naBaba vangu kuchaitwa, nokuti ndakafanira kuzarura mabhuku nokusunungura zvisimbiso.

Makafunga zvisizvo kuti kushivirira kwangu nomwoyo murefu kushaya simba nokuregeredza. Makati mumwoyo menyu: Ishe aripiko? Iye haasi chinhu kunze kwengano yekare. Takadzidza zvakawanda kupfuura nzira dzinoponesa dzaIshe; muruzivo rwangu, ndiri mukuru, nokuti matekinoroji angu ane simba kupfuura bhuku rekare rekupesana nengano. Zvirokwazvo, vazhinji venyu hamutombotendi kuti ndakambouya pakutanga ndikadurura ropa rangu rinodzikinura nokuda kwezvivi noruponeso rwevanhu. Zviri nani kuti dai musina kutomboberekwa, nokuti kutsamwa kwamakazvichengetera pamusoro pemweya yenyu kuchauya pamusoro penyu pasina mushonga, uye muchaziva zvizere zvivi zvenyu, uye nhamo yenyu huru nedongo zvichava shamwari yenyu. Muchadya tsvina yenyu, uye nzira dzenyu dzine tsvina dzichava nemi nokusingaperi, sezvamunovigwa mumichero yekutsamwa mumabasa enyu akaipa amakaita mukuipa kukuru kunopesana nezita rangu. Ibvai kwandiri; handikuziviyi!

Ndianiko pakati penyu anotaura zvenhema muzita rangu? Munoti munondiziva, munondimiririra; vamwe venyu munotofunga kuti munogona kundirayira kuti ndiite zvamunoda uye muzviwanire pfuma nemabasa enyu akanaka, ayo asiri chinhu kunze kwemamvemve ane tsvina. Mungataura sei muchindimiririra imi musingandizivi? Mungataura sei muchindimiririra ini ndisina kukupai mashoko? Mungataura sei muchindimiririra kuvanhu vangu ini ndisina kukutumai? Kwete, makazvituma pachenyu, muchitengesa zvinhu zvenyu zvakaora nokuda kwekuwana pfuma nokuchiva uye nokuda kwekuyemurwa nevanhu kwamunoshuvira zvikuru. Iwe benzi! Ibva kwandiri, sezvo ndisina kumbokuziva!

Imi vaporofita venhema, vanoti ndini Ishe muzita chete; imi vaporofita venhema, vanotaura nhema huru dzinopikisa mweya yenyu; muchaparara chose, uye ndichakusvipirai kunze kwemuromo wangu. Naizvozvo, tendeukai! Dzokerai kwandiri uye ndichadzokera kwamuri. Budai pakati pezvidhori zvenyu mubve pamapoka enyu akatukwa muuye kwandiri mundiite Ishe wenyu! Haiwa, dai maizogamuchira ruponeso rwangu; asi munoramba muri muzvivi zvenyu uye munozviregerera mhosva dzenyu sezvamunotora mashoko kubva muShoko rangu dzvene nemwoyo usina chinhu, mwoyo uzere upombwe noufeve sezvamunomhanyira kuzvidhori zvenyu. Ndiani achakuponesai pakutsamwa kwangu imi makatsausa vanhu vangu nokuda kwekuwana pfuma yenyu? Dai makanga mandiita Ishe sezvakataurwa nemiromo yenyu inoreva nhema, ndingadai ndakakuponesai pazvivi zvenyu ndikakuitai vakakwana mandiri. Ndingadai ndakafudza marwadzo anokonzerwa nemuipi pamuri. Ndingadai ndakakupai nzira nechinangwa ndikakutumai panyika nemiromo izere maropafadzo nechokwadi sezvamungadai makatungamirira vanhu vangu kwandiri.

Musafunga kuti ndinoda rubatsiro rwenyu, sezvo ndiri Ishe wechokwadi wedenga nenyika, uye ndiri Mambo weMadzimambo naIshe weMadzishe. Avo vandakagadza muushumiri vanopiwa rukudzo rukuru kuti vandimiririre uye vachengete boka rangu, rukudzo rwandichapa mubairo nepfuma isingaperi; asi, makanditsinhanisa neshanduro yenhema yaKristu. Mumwe Jesu wamakazviitira; makada mweya wemupikisi waKristu, uye makatadza nokusingaperi pamusoro pemweya yenyu nokuzivisa kuti handipo muKereke yangu yandakatenga neropa rangu, chivi chamusingazopori pachiri. Zvirokwazvo, zvinhu zvose izvi nezvimwe zvichauya pamusoro penyu mukutsamwa kwangu kunze kwekunge mazvininipisa pamberi pangu uye mukandiita Ishe zvechokwadi nokutendeuka muri mumasaga nemadota. Musaruramisa mashoko angu sezvamunotamba nemashoko ekutaura kwangu! Tendeukai! Garai mumasaga nemadota mucheme pamusoro pezvakaipa zvenyu, uye ndichakugamuchirai kwandiri ndikudzoserei kumubairo wenyu wandingadai ndakapa kune mumwe.

Ndini Ishe akarurama.

Ndini muponesi wenyika, kunyange Mambo weMadzimambo naIshe weMadzishe.

Ndini iye anofamba pakati pezvigadziko zvemwenje zvinomwe uye anorayira ngirozi nomwe dzekereke nomwe.

Ndini gwayana rakabayiwa kubva pakuvambwa kwenyika, uye mandiri mune upenyu husingaperi.

Ndakabata kiyi dzerufu negehena, uye Baba vangu vakagovana neni chigaro chavo choushe, kuti ndigovane chigaro changu choushe nevakatendeka vangu, vakadanwa, nevasanangurwa. Ndinofamba nevanhu vangu uye ndinotaura nevanhu vangu, uye ndini mufudzi wechokwadi anofudza boka iroro randakapiwa naBaba vangu. Imi muri mboni yeziso rangu, vakatendeka vangu, uye muri mboni yeziso raBaba vangu, uye nokuda kwenguva ino neawa ino makadzorerwa mukururama noutsvene hwechokwadi.

Awa yoswedera, uye munofanira kurega mabasa enyu mugotarira kuonekwa kwangu, nokuti kwava kuda kuuya panyika sezvandinokuunganidzai kubva kumakona mana enyika. Zvirokwazvo, zvirokwazvo, ndichaunganidza makwai angu echokwadi mudura rangu dutu risati rasvika richaparadza nyika ino yakaipa iripo, nokuti umambo hwaSatani huchabvarurwa huparadzwe zvachose kuti pave nenzira yaIshe akarurama achatonga nyika kubva pachigaro choushe chaDhavhidhi mukururama noutsvene hwechokwadi, uye ndichapa simba guru kumwenga wangu muumambo huri kuuya, nokuti ndivo madzimambo angu navaprista, uye vachatonga mukururama noutsvene hwechokwadi, nokuti mubairo wangu uri muruoko rwangu nokuda kwevakatendeka.

Ndini simba renyu noruponeso rwenyu, uye makaiswa chisimbiso mandiri.
