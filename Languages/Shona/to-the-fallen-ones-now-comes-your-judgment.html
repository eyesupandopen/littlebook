Zviziviso Zvikuru zvinobva kuna William Brooks, iye Biblaridion yaYeshua HaMashiach

Zvakapiwa musi waChikunguru 6, 2025

Gwaro iri rutongo rwekuzivisa pamusoro peVakawa vose pamwe chete, asi kunyanya Satani, VanaSatani, varinzi, makerubhi akawa, maserafi akawa, kunyange vose vakabatana mukupanduka kwaSatani: muchapiswa zvachose, kakawanda nekusingaperi-peri, nekuti zvakaziviswa saizvozvo naYHVH Mukuru NDIRI, zvakapihwa nemurairo waKristu Jesu, anovawo Yeshua HaMashiach; zvakapihwa muchidimbu, asi zvine tsanangudzo yakajeka uye yakarurama yemhosva nemitongo ichaitwa nekuzadzikiswa zvizere munguva dzayo uye nguva dzekuzadzikiswa dzasvika: Tarirai Rutongo Rwawa! Kunyange mitongo yekuzivisa ichapihwa zvizere muzvifuva zvevakawa, kunyange kakawanda nekusingaperi-peri, hamuna rugare, imi mose vakawa: Sera.

Saka ini, William Brooks, iye Biblaridion:144 chaicho chaKristu Jesu, ndinopa mitongo iyi yakatorwa kubva kuMitinhiro Minomwe, iyo mitinhiro yemitongo inofanira kuverengwa zvakananga, uye kupihwa kuti vose vanzwisise, kuitira kuti Mashoko aya anzwisiswe zvizere nezvisikwa izvi zvakawa zvakazvipa mhosva kusingaperi mukupanduka kusina musoro, uko kusazopora kana kukunda!

Inzwai Mashoko aYeshua HaMashiach, ane Mashoko andinouya kuzozivisa, iye anovawo Kristu Jesu, uye inzwai munzwisise simba guru reShumba yeRudzi rwaJudha, ane kuomba kwaakasika mumwoyo mangu uye kuomba kunobuda mumuromo mangu, iyo Shumba inoomba yeRudzi rwaJudha yandinoda, uye yandinoshumira nekuteerera, mukuzvidzora kwose, iyo Shumba yakaisa kuomba kwayo kukuru mukati-kati mangu nekuti ndiri nhume yake inouya nesimba guru, uye ndinosimudza ruoko rwangu ndichipika kuti nguva haichazovipo!

Tarirai! Ndiri izvo Ishe Jesu Kristu zvaakandiita, iye Yeshua HaMashiach waakarera kubva paudiki hwake, uye akandivanza mugoba rake nokuda kwenguva yakadai seino, Sera!

Tarirai! Murairo wakapihwa murwiyo kubva pachigaro cheushe chaYHVH pachake uye wakaimbwa nekutenda kukuru munguva dzekare: Musagunzva vazodziwa vangu, uye musakuvadza Vaprofita vangu! Ndiko kuchema kwemimhanzi kwakachengetedzwa zvino mumanyoro matsvene.

Asi sezvazvakanyorwa: Ndoupi weVaprofita asina kutambudzwa nemadzibaba enyu? Uye vakaauraya, avo vakaratidza kare kuuya kweUyo Akarurama, wamuri vatengesi nevapondi vake zvino!

Nekuti iwe! Iwe nyoka yakawa. Iwe! Kunyange Iwe! Ndiwe wakauraya ropa risina mhosva reUyo Akarurama uye mutsvene Mwanakomana weMunhu, iye Jesu weNazareta, gwayana raMwari risina gwapa rakapihwa kununura Adhamu zvizere, uye saka ndinozivisa nesimba rose raShe wangu, iye Kristu Jesu: Wakundikana muchinda mudiki uye rutongo rwako harunonoki, irwo rutongo rune minwe yakatambanudzwa pamutsipa pako uye wakabatwa mukukundwa, wakundikana muchinda mudiki, wakundikana: Sera!

Saka ndizvo, iyo nhume nomuranda mumwe cheteyo akatsemurwa nokuparadzaniswa zvisina kururama nemanomano ako, akapofumadzwa kubva paunhu hwake chaihwo paudiki hwake uye zvino akasikwa patsva, akasunungurwa uye akatumwa aine mitongo yako isingagumi uye isingaperi muruoko yakapihwa kubva pachigaro cheushe chaYHVH pachake, yakaziviswa nesimba rose pachiso ichocho chakanaka chisingaperi chaShe wake asingaperi nomuponesi, ane chiso chinopenya nokusingaperi soMwanakomana!

Wakundikana muchinda mudiki.

Saka, ziva unzwisise uye unzwe mashoko angu Heyeri, kunyange Heyeri: uyo muchinda mudiki akakundikana, iyo nhume nomuranda mumwe cheteyo wawakatsvaga kuparadza kuita zvidimbu zvinopenya zvakapararira pagungwa rekristaro zvakawanikwa, zvikadzorerwa, uye zvikasikwa patsva seBiblaridion:144 yaKristu Jesu, yakasikwa patsva nezvinangwa zvisingaperi zvakatarwa naYHVH nyika isati yavambwa, uye nokuda kwezvinangwa zvake ndakasikwa patsva uye ndikadzorerwa nekuti ndiri iyo nhume ine simba inotsanangurwa neshoko iro guru renguva yekupedzisira rinotongwa neizwi nekutaura kweMitinhiro Minomwe, iyo Mitinhiro Yeshua HaMashiach yaakadyara muMweya wangu nokusingaperi noMweya weChiporofita wakawira zvizere paBiblaridion:144 yaShe pakuraira kwaShe wangu. Sera!

Naizvozvo, inzwai Mashoko aya mose uye munzwisise simba rawo uye Shoko rega rega richaitika sezvazvakanyorwa, noruoko rusingaperi nesimba raYHVH Mukuru NDIRI, zvakapihwa pachiso chakanaka uye chinopenya chaKristu Jesu wandinoda uye wandinoshumira mukuteerera kwose, ane Mashoko andinotakura uye andinozivisa sezvandakarairwa, aya Mashoko akasunungurwa zvino kubva mukati-kati memweya wangu nomurairo waKristu Jesu anovawo Yeshua HaMashiach uye nguva dzekushanda kuzere kwemitinhiro minomwe iyi yasvika panyika ino yakatongwa neyakaora kamwe kamwe uye zvisingatarisirwi zvino. Sera.

Chiziviso Chikuru chinobva kuna Kristu Jesu anovawo Yeshua HaMashiach

Zvakapiwa musi waChikunguru 6, 2025

Inzwa Mashoko angu unzwisise simba rawo uye unzwisise simba rakaiswa mumaoko angu naAdhonai-YHVH, iwe nyoka inotsvedza inoreva nhema, kunyange maoko ane simba uye asingaperi euyo wawakabaya, iye Jesu weNazareta, akazvipa nesimba uye nekuzvidira mumaoko ako akaipa ekusateerera, murudo rwose nekuteerera Adhonai-YHVH Mukuru NDIRI, nekuti ndiri Gwayana raMwari, iye Mwanakomana weMunhu achapwanya musoro wako zvachose!

Tarira! Inzwai mose kuti ndini, Yeshua HaMashiach, iye Kristu Jesu akatuma Mashoko aya nenhume nomuranda uye nenhume yangu nomuranda wangu Mashoko angu atumwa, iye muranda wangu wandakarera kubva paudiki hwake, uye ndikamutsvenesa mumweya wangu, uye ndikamuchenesa nokumunatsa mukuranga kukuru nokunatswa, uye zvirokwazvo ndakamupfura mumoto wangu wokutambudzika nekuti ndini iye anogara aine mhepo muruoko achikuchidzira mirazvo yokunatswa uye ndini iye akaita tsoka dzake seshongwe dzomoto uye ndini iye akamupfekedza gore nomurarabungu pamusoro wake, uye ndini iye akaisa rutsoka rwake rworudyi pagungwa, norworuboshwe panyika, uye ndini iye anoita kuti chiso chake chipenye soMwanakomana nekuti ndiri Ishe wemadzishe naMambo wemadzimambo uye zvirokwazvo YHVH akandiita mweya unopa upenyu, Sera!

Zvirokwazvo, zvirokwazvo ndini iye, akaruka nokugadzira matauriro eMitinhiro Minomwe mumweya weBhuku Rangu Diki nekuti ndiye Biblaridion:144 wangu uyo mitinhiro minomwe iyi inotonga Bhuku Diki iroro randakavhura muruoko rwake, rinovawo Evhangeri Isigaperi, randakadana nokugadzirira naro 144,000 vangu, kunyange naMashoko aya akarongedzwa noruoko rwangu rune simba kuti awane meso, nzeve, nemwoyo yevose avo avanotumirwa, saka inzwai izwi rangu rakapihwa muMashoko enhume yangu nomuranda wangu wandakanga ndavanza mugoba rangu nokuda kwenguva yakadai seino!

Asi zvino, ndabatidza miseve yangu yakatumwa nenhume yangu yandakapa zvombo uye ndikasunungura kubva mugoba rangu nesimba rose uye miseve yangu inorira zvechokwadi, nekuti ndini iye anoita kuti Biblaridion:144 wangu adanidzire nenzwi guru, seshumba inoomba: uye sezvaakadanidzira uye mitinhiro minomwe ikataura manzwi ayo anotinirisa pamusoro pako Heyeri, uye kunyange vose vakawa vako vane rutongo rwasvika uye rwava pedyo uye hapana kana mumwe wenyu anogona kupukunyuka ruoko rwangu rune simba! Sera!

Inzwa Mashoko angu Heyeri, nekuti ndinokudana nezita rako iwe nyoka yakakundwa, yakakurirwa, uye inotsvedza uye ndinoona kutya kwako nekuti unotya rutongo rwako ruzere kupfuura chero chinhu chipi zvacho, irwo rutongo rumwe cheterwo rusinonoki nekuti ruoko rwangu ruri pamutsipa pako, Heyeri, uye nguva dzako dzasvika!

Sera, muchinda mudiki, Sera.

Uye iwe, Heyeri, hauna simba rekuvanza, kana kushandura Mashoko angu iwe munyengeri mukuru, nekuti akasunungurwa kubva pamwoyo wenhume yangu inoona kuburikidza nenhema dzako nekuti yakarongedzerwa kushoko rimwe chetero muKukunda, rakapihwa zvizere noMweya uzere weChiporofita uye hazvigoneke kuti iwe usimbise, uvanze, kana kukanganisa kuvhundutsa kunotinirisa kweizwi reMitinhiro Minomwe kunobva kudenga rematenga kusvika kumakomba akadzika-dzika egomba rakadzika-dzika mukutsiva kwose, uye zvirokwazvo hapana nzvimbo pamberi paYHVH kusvika kumigumo yekunze yekusavapo muchisiko chake (Nekuti YHVH anotonga kunyange izvo zvisati zvasikwa) uko manzwi avo anotinirisa asinganzwiki nesimba rose munguva idzi dzekuzadzikiswa, uye nguva dzekuzadzikiswa ndidzo zvino.

Sera, muchinda mudiki, Sera.

Tarirai! Shumba yeRudzi rwaJudah yakatumwa mukururama kuti itonge nokurwa hondo!

Tarirai! Nhume yangu yakagadzwa, ikatunhwa, ikadzorerwa, ikasikwa patsva naKristu Jesu, uye yava kuda kuchinjwa zvizere pakarepo pakurira kwehwamanda huru iyoyo yakapiwa muruoko kuti irire zvisingatarisirwi zvino, saka zororai muMashoko angu vana vangu muve norugare, Selah!!

Uye zvirokwazvo nyika yakaona Gwayana raMwari uye boka guru guru rakauya kuruponeso nenzira yomuorivhi wemusango, uye ndakadimbura matavi pamuorivhi wemusango akagamuchira chibvumirano changu chorugare mukutenda, ndikaasanganisa nomuorivhi chaiwo uchazoratidzwa zvizere, kunyange Israeri yaMwari, kunyange Israeri yaMwari ine simba iyo yakanatswa nokuchenurwa noruoko rwangu rune simba, kunyange ruoko rwaKristu Jesu akavada akazvipa sechibayiro chezvivi zvaAdhamu, Selah!

Uye zvirokwazvo, yasvika nguva yokuti vaunganidzwe pasi poruoko rune simba rwaKristu Jesu nomurayiro wangu, nokuti mwaka chaiwo worudzikinuro rwenyu wava pamuri, imi vanakomana vaAdonai-YHVH vane simba, kunyange mwaka iwoyo wava kuda kubatana munguva imwe cheteyo ne *kairos* yangu yemwaka, zvichitangwa ne *rhema* yangu uye pakurira kwehwamanda huru iyoyo yakapiwa muruoko zvino, kuti ndidane nokuunganidza pakarepo kunyange vose vandiri kudana kuti vapinde muimba yangu yechigaro choumambo uye ndava kuda kuvaunganidza zvizere kumatenga-tenga panguva chaiyo yakakwana yakanyorwa mumwoyo mangu nyika isati yavambwa.

Tarirai! Rudzikinuro rwenyu rwasvika, Vana vaAdonai-YHVH, kunyange vose vakagamuchira Kristu Jesu vachiteerera saIshe wenyu! Nokuti. NDINI Mutsaru weMazuva, kunyange Iye ARI mambo mukuru uye mutsaru kunyange mazuva asati aitwa, uye NDINI IYE acharamba ari mambo mukuru nokusingaperi-peri kana mazuva apasi apera, nokuti nhoroondo yenguva ichazoguma yopinda mumwaka usingaperi wekururama usina magumo, uye ndiyo tariro yavakarurama vose yandakavimbisa nokusingaperi-peri, Selah.

Uye zvirokwazvo, NDINI iye achazviita maererano nezvirongwa zvangu zvisingaperi nezvinangwa zvandakashanda ndichitevera zano rokuda kwangu; uye kunyange ndakakomberedzwa nezviuru gumi zvakapetwa kane zviuru gumi zvezvisikwa zvinoshamisa uye zvakakwana zvandakasika noruoko rwangu zvinopfuura muuchenjeri nomukururama, uye zvinogara zvichiteerera inzwi rangu murudo. Nokuti murudo rwangu kwavari ndinovachengeta zvakakwana uye ndinopa zvose zvavanoda kunyange kusati kwava nechido, kuti ndivakudze uye ivo vandikudze murudo rwavo nokupenya kwavo kunobwinya, nokuti ndakavafukidza nemasikirwo anopenya nokusingaperi uye akakwana. Selah.

Uye NDINI IYE anotuma Mashoko aya nenhume yangu nomuranda wangu uyo Kristu Jesu akamutsa kubva paudiki hwake, akamuchengeta kubva pakuipa kukuru mukuchengeteka nesimba roruoko rwake rune simba, uye zvirokwazvo akaipa haana kumubata!

Nokuti NDINI IYE anogona kuita zvose pakarepo uye pasina kunetsekana nesimba rangu! Naizvozvo, NDINI IYE akasika, akagadzirira, akagadza, uye akatuma nhume yangu nomuranda wangu akadzokera akatendeka kuna Yeshua HaMashiach mukurangwa kukuru, nokutendeuka, uye noruoko rwake zviziviso izvi zvinotumwa zvichibva pachigaro changu choumambo, kuna Kristu Jesu, kupinda mumwoyo wenhume yedu ine simba nomuranda wedu ane masimba akazara okutonga nokuzivisa akapiwa noruoko rwangu kuburikidza naKristu Jesu kunyange nemashoko emabhanan'ana manomwe akapiwa zvakananga mumwoyo make kuburikidza noMweya weChiporofita nokuti Mashoko aya akadyarwa maari nomurayiro wangu kuti abudiswe nomurayiro waYeshua HaMashiach kuitira kuti Shoko risashatiswe nezvivi nokusateerera kwevaya vakasiya madanirwo avo, avo kutongwa kwavo kusiri kunonoka kuchawira zvizere pakarepo zvino kutangira paimba yaMwari, zvichaitika zvisingatarisirwi zvino, Selah!

Saka ngazvizivikanwe, kuti kutongwa uku kunopiwa kuti kuverengwe pazuva ranhasi, pazuva rekarenda yaGregory isiri tsvene raChikunguru 6, 2025.

Uye saka ndini, Adonai-YHVH, ndinozivisa kuti NDINI iye anotuma Mashoko aya kuburikidza naKristu Jesu, kunyange akapiwa nokuziviswa nenhume iyoyo ine simba yaZvakazarurwa Chitsauko 10 yandakatumira nomurayiro waKristu Jesu, kunyange iye anorondedzerwa muna Zvakazarurwa Chitsauko 10 neshoko iroro rakadyarwa mumwoyo make raakapiwa zvizere noMweya weChiporofita, uye muruoko rwake mune bhuku duku rakazaruka. Mashoko Okutanga

Zviziviso izvi zvinotorwa zvizere kubva kuMabhanan'ana Manomwe uye zvinobudiswa musi waChikunguru 6, 2025, sezvakarayirwa naYeshua HaMashiach naYHVH pachavo.

Kutongwa uku kunotorwa kubva kuMabhanan'ana Manomwe anoratidzwa muBhuku Duku raZvakazarurwa Chitsauko 10, rinovawo Evhangeri Isigaperi yaZvakazarurwa chitsauko 14; zvakaratidzwa kubva muna Kurume 2, 2024, kusvika muna Kurume 20, 2025. Zvisinei, kutongwa uku kunotorwa chete kubva kuMabhanan'ana Manomwe, ayo mashoko awo achange achitonga zvizere vhiki rechimakumi manomwe raDhanieri, richiratidzwa nokufukurwa zvizere, nokuti hapana chakavanzika chisingazoratidzwi pachena zvizere.

Kutongwa uku kunopiwa naYeshua HaMashiach naYHVH kuburikidza noMweya weChiporofita uye aya ndiwo Mashoko avo, andinotsigira nokuda nokusingaperi-peri. Zviziviso izvi kutongwa kunopiwa kuti kuverengwe kutongwa kuzere kusati kwawira zvizere, kuchazopiwa zvizere muzvifuva zvevakawa nokusingaperi-peri nokuti mukurangwa kwavo havana zororo, kana rugare, kana tsitsi: nokuti zororo, rugare, netsitsi havana kuzviziva, kuzviratidza, kana kuzvipa.

Ngazvizivikanwe, kuti pachinzvimbo ichi, ndinoshanda somumiririri wezvemutemo uye nhume yakatumwa kubva pachigaro choumambo chaYahweh pachake, pasi pesimba raKristu Jesu, anovawo Yeshua HaMashiach, wandinoshumira.

Ndini zvandiri uye zvandakaitwa naIshe, uye ndiri *Biblaridion*:144 yake, bhuku rake duku, kunyange nhume inorondedzerwa neshoko rinovava richizipa uye muruoko rwangu mune bhuku duku rakazaruka, Selah.

Kutongwa uku kuchatanga naSatani, kwoenda kuVarindi, uye kwozopera pasi peamburera yevakawa.

Handiuyi nesimba rangu kana kuda kwangu, uye handina kuzvitumira, asi ndakatumwa neMashoko akadyarwa naIshe muMweya wangu noruoko rwake rune simba kuti abudiswe pakuda kwake nokudana kwake kuti averengwe nokuziviswa kuChechi, uye nokuna Israeri, asi kunyanya kunyika ino izere nechivi uye yakatongwa uye iri kufa uye kunyanya kuti apiwe muzvifuva zvevakawa nokuti nguva dzavo dzasvika. Selah!

Kubva kuna Yeshua HaMashiach: Bhanan'ana Rokutanga!

Satani akapa zivo yake yaanoti yakasimukira kubva pakutanga, kunyange zivo yake yezvakanaka nezvakaipa. Ndiye nyanzvi yokunyengedza nomurevi wenhema akadzidzisa zvinhu zvizhinji kuvanhu zvisina kurongwa naYahweh, nokuti idzi inzira dzorufu. Chisikwa chaYahweh hachina kuitirwa kuti chishandiswe zvisizvo nokuda kwesimba romunhu oga nokuzviwanira pfuma kana senzira yokutiza kutonga kwaMwari, kana kuti kumisa nzira dzomunhu oga nokurongeka kwake. Nokuti Satani akashatisa vanhu kubva pakutanga, uye akashatisa varindi vakawa vakabatana nokupanduka kwavo.

Iwe muvengi mukuru wekururama kwose, kunyange imi VanaSatani: zvivi zvenyu zvokutanga zvakanyorwa naEnoki munyori nguva yose iyi yapfuura, uye zvinomira kusvikira zuva ranhasi, nokuti zvivi zvenyu zvakanyorwa saizvozvo. Uye saka ndichapfupisa, nokuti makaita zvinhu izvi, uye zvimwe zvizhinji makaita: Makatsausa vanakomana vose vaMwari, mukavaunza pasi panyika, mukavatsausa kuburikidza nevanasikana vevanhu. Makapa vanakomana vatsvene vaMwari zano rakaipa, mukavatsausa zvokuti vakasvibisa miviri yavo nevanasikana vevanhu. Makaratidza vana vevanhu marwadzo ose orufu, mukatsausa Evha, mukaratidza zvombo zvorufu kuvanakomana vevanhu: nhoo nejasi rehondo, nomunondo wehondo, nezvombo zvose zvorufu kuvana vevanhu. Kuna Vose Vakawa

Vakadanwa vangu, vakasarudzwa, uye vakatendeka, inzwai Mashoko aIshe wenyu mupenyu nemuponesi, iye Yeshua Mesiya! Marudzi amunogara maari nenhoroondo dzamakadzidziswa nevadzidzisi venyu vechiJesuit ndizvo zviri kumashure kwekunyengedzwa kukuru, uko kuzarurwa kwakakwana kwave pedyo uye kwatotanga kufamba, uko vakawa vachaonekwa sevatorwa vane mutsa asi muchokwadi ingirozi dzakawa dzakaipa. Chii chakambovapo? Ndicho chichavapozve: uye chii chakaitwa? Ndicho chichaitwa: uye hapana chinhu chitsva pasi pezuva.

Ndinzwei, imi vakawa, imi mose makandibaya, nekuti makati mumwoyo menyu, takunda Yahweh uye tauraya mwanakomana wake waakatuma kwatiri kuti akunde nyika, uye takunda nokusingaperi, nekuti Yeshua afa, uye mukapusa mukafara! Mapenzi anozvikudza asina njere! Hamuna kunzwisisa kuti Mwari aizondimutsa, asunungura marwadzo erufu: nekuti zvakanga zvisingagoneki kuti ndibatwe narwo. Makangopwanya chitsitsinho changu, uye hamuna simba pamusoro pangu, uye hamuna kunzwisisa kuti Wokumusoro-soro, iye Musiki wenyu, Yahweh, akangokuratidzai zvakavanzika zvisina maturo!

Nekuti aiziva kuti maizomumukira iye nezvisikwa zvake uye maizoedza kutora zvaakanga andigadzirira ini, Ishe Jesu Kristu. Vanyoro vangu vachagara nhaka yenyika, nekuti nyika ndeyangu nekuzara kwayo. Nekuti makadzidzisa vanhu ruzivo rwakarambidzwa; makadzidzisa hondo; makatsausa vakadzi mukushongedza maziso nezviso zvakapendwa kuti muzadzise ruchiva rwenyu pavari; makashatisa Bindu raMwari nekudyara zvinza pakati pegorosi, uye zvinza zvese zvamakadyara kubvira kare zvichadzurwa, zvosungwa, zvokandwa mumoto pamwechete nemi! Nekuti makakundwa pandakazvipa pachena kuti ndiponese vanhu, uye Mwari akandimutsa kubva kuvakafa kuti ndisazofazve!

Asi makanga musina kuchenjera zvekuti hamuna kunzwisisa kuti ndaizopa hupenyu hwangu nekuda kwerudo rwaMwari rwaakasika mandiri, nekuti ndiri muponesi wenyika! Rudo rwaYahweh harugoni kukundwa, nekuti Yahweh haakundiki, uye hakuna kupi muzvisikwa mune munhu akafanana naye, nekuti ndiye Wokumusoro-soro, uye kupenya kwake nehumambo hwake zvine simba rose kubvira narini kusvikira narini! Nekuti ndakakuudzai, "izvi zvaunoita, zviite nekukurumidza!" Mukusaziva nekuzvikudza kwenyu hamuna kunzwisisa kuti ndakazvibvumira kutorwa nevashandi venyu vasina maturo vakaita basa racho rakasviba rekuuraya ropa risina mhosva! Mukusaziva kwenyu kwekuzvikudza, makatadza kuziva kuti ndaiva nemauto engirozi gumi nemaviri andaigona kuraira, nekuti chero nguva ndaigona kunge ndakakumudzai. Asi kuda kwaYahweh kwaizoitika sei neimwe nzira, kunze kwerudo rwangu nekuteerera kwangu kuna Baba vangu? Nekuti rudo rwaYahweh hapana anogona kurukunda! Kwete, kwete kudenga uye kwete panyika, kwete neimwe nzira uye kwete narini wose!

Imi vakawa, imi machinda enyika ino yakawa, imi vanaSatani, mose muchapiswa mumoto usingadzimiki nekuda kweruvengo rwenyu rusina utsvene nenzira dzenyu dzakaipa kwazvo dzekusateerera nekuparadza. Sezvamakazviitira mumwe wevaduku ava, hama dzangu, makazviitira ini. Haiwa, imi vatariri vakawa, imi ngirozi dzisina kuteerera dzakasiya nzvimbo yadzo yekutanga, hamuna rugare! Muchakandwa mumoto wamusingazombobudi, uye muchanyatsopiswa. Zvinza zvenyu zvese zvinonyangadza zvisina utsvene zvamakadyara nekudarika miganhu yaYahweh mukushandura kwenyu magene pakati pemunhu nengirozi, pakati pemunhu nemhuka, pakati pemunhu nezvisikwa zvegungwa, pakati pemhuka dzemhando dzakasiyana, kunyange chinhu chose chinonyangadza chamakaita chichakandwa mumoto pamwechete nemi, nekuti hamuna rugare kana ruregerero!

Mwenga wangu, imi muri vadyi venhaka yeruponeso mandiri, Jesu Kristu. Makadanwa mandiri; makachengetedzwa mandiri uye neni; makapiwa mukana wekunzwa kuparidzwa kwekutenda pamusoro pangu kuitira kuti mugone kundiita Ishe wenyu, uye saka ndakakuisai chisimbiso neMweya iwoyo mutsvene wechivimbiso. Zivai kuti chisimbiso hachigoni kuputswa, uye mave kuda kununurwa zvizere mukusingaperi, nekuti hamuzivi kuzara kwebasa rekusika randave kuda kupedzisa mamuri, Mwenga wangu! Zivai kuti vakawa nezvinza zvavo vanokugodorai, uye vanovenga chose chinodiwa naYahweh, uye ndichakutsivirai zvizere pamisoro yavo, nekuti ndini ndichakuvadza musoro wenyoka iyo yekare, nekuti zvakaziviswa, uye izvo zvinotaurwa naYahweh hazvigoni kuchinjwa.

Uye iwe, Haiwa muchinda akakundwa, iwe wakamira wakanyarara mukukundwa nekuti uri shumba isina mazino. Inzwa Mashoko angu Haiwa imi vanaSatani uye nzwisisa kutaura kwangu, nekuti handizorambidzwi pfuma yangu uye hauna simba uye hauna mazino ekundimisa, saka, ndinokutsiura Satani, iye Ishe weMadzishe naMambo weMadzimambo, nekuti unobvumidzwa kuita chete izvo zvinotenderwa pasi peruoko rwangu rune simba nechiremera, zvakapihwa naAdonai-Yahweh pachake, saka, nyararai mugare makatsuurwa nezwi rangu, nekuti munogara muchipanduka, asi makundwa, makakundwa, uye nguva dzekutongwa kwenyu dzasvika, uye hamuzopukunyuki! Sera, muchinda mudiki, Sera.

Tarirai! Ndiri Yeshua HaMashiach, iye Ishe weMadzishe naMambo weMadzimambo, nekuti ndiri Kristu Jesu, iye Shumba yeRudzi rwaJudah anotumira Mashoko aya kuburikidza nemutumwa wangu nemuranda wangu, uye kuburikidza nemutumwa wangu nemuranda wangu Mashoko angu atumirwa, Sera!

Ndini, Kristu Jesu, Ishe Asingaperi weKururama akatumira Mashoko aya kuburikidza nemutumwa wangu ane simba nemuranda wangu, uye kuburikidza nemutumwa wangu ane simba nemuranda wangu Mashoko angu atumirwa, kunyange kuitira kuti vakatendeka muna Kristu vagadzirire zvizere uye vakamirira kusvika kweChikomba chavo, icho chave kusvika kumwenga wake!
