Nokuti Mwari Haasi Munyori Wenyonganyonga!

Nokuti Mwari haasi munyori wenyonganyonga, asi worugare, sezvazviri mukereke dzose dzavatsvene! Verengai mashoko aya uye teererai inzwi rangu. Rerekai nzeve yenyu, munzwe mashoko avakachenjera, uye isai mwoyo wenyu kuzivo yangu. Zvichida munofanira kutevera muenzaniso wangu kubva mubindu pandakanyengetera mukurwadziwa kukuru pandakawira pasi pamberi paBaba vangu mukunyengetera nekuzvininipisa, uye vakandisimbisa kuti ndigamuchire mukombe wehasha dzezvivi zvenyu chaizvo zvandakazvitorera imi, nokuti ndinokwanisa kuzvitakura. Ndinotaura kune vanozvikudza muKereke yangu, uye ndinotaura kukuparadzana kwakawanda pakati penyu. Asi iwe unotongerei hama yako? Kana kuti iwe unozvidzirei hama yako? Nokuti isu tose tichamira pamberi pechigaro chokutonga chaKristu. Hazvina kunyorwa here mutsamba yandakatumira kuvateveri veNzira paRoma? Asi Zekaria anoti kudini pamusoro penyaya yacho: Asi vakaramba kuteerera, vakafuratira, vakadzivira nzeve dzavo, kuti varege kunzwa. Hamuna kuverenga Isaya here, vaduku vangu? Nokuti vaporofita vekare vanoti kudini? Nokuti Isaya anoti zvakare, Nokunzwa muchanzwa, asi hamunganzwisisi; uye nokuona muchaona, asi hamungazivi.

Vana vangu, ndakakuratidzai nzira yakanakisisa, asi vazhinji venyu makadonha kubva panyasha uye kuzvikudza kwenyu kwakaita kuti muzvikwidziridze kupfuura mwero. Asi, mashoko angu andakapa kuMuapostora Pauro kuGaratiya anotaura zvakajeka kuti: Nokuti kana munhu achifunga kuti iye chinhu, asi asiri chinhu, anozvinyengera. Vana vangu vaduku, ndinoziva kuti mune shungu mumwoyo yekundishumira, uye ndinoziva kuti munondida, asi dzimwe nguva zvisingawirirani nezivo. Hamunzwisisi here kuti Mwari anoramba vanozvikudza asi anopa nyasha kune vanozvininipisa? Naizvozvo, zvininipisei pasi peruoko rune simba rwaMwari, kuti akukwidziridzei panguva yakafanira. Saizvozvowo, imi vechidiki, zviisei pasi pevakuru venyu. Hongu, imi mose zviisei pasi pomumwe nomumwe, uye mupfeke kuzvininipisa. Mwari anoramba vanozvikudza asi anopa nyasha kune vanozvininipisa. Musafunge kuti mashoko angu anodzokororwa, nokuti mumuromo wezvapupu zviviri kana zvitatu shoko rimwe nerimwe ngarisimbiswe. Nzwisisai, vana vangu, hamugoni kundishumira nenyama yenyu: Nokuti zvose zviri munyika, kuchiva kwenyama, nokuchiva kwameso, nokuzvikudza kwoupenyu, hazvibvi kuna Baba, asi zvinobva kunyika. Kereke yangu, zviisei pasi pomumwe nomumwe mupfungwa dzakapfeka kuzvininipisa, nokuti ndingave ndini ndiri kupa mashoko okutaura nokufemerwa kwekukudzidzisai, kunyange vazhinji venyu vachaisa parutivi inzwi revanozvininipisa vachifarira mukurumbira nomunhu womumwe munhu. Ivai vateveri vaMwari, vana vangu, nokuti zvirokwazvo Mwari haatarisi chiso chomunhu.

Tarirai! Kubwinya kwaMwari ndiko kuvanza chinhu: asi kukudzwa kwaMadzimambo ndiko kunzvera nyaya. Nzwisisai kuti ndichakuitai madzimambo navapristi pamberi paMwari wedu naBaba vedu, saka nzwisisai zvirevo zvevakachenjera pakati penyu: Mwari haana kukupai nzeve mbiri nomuromo mumwe here? Hamuna kuverenga here? Mumwe nomumwe wenyu ane Pisarema, ane dzidziso, ane rurimi, ane zvakazarurwa, ane dudziro: Zvinhu zvose ngazviitirwe kuvaka! Asi kwete, munoruma nokuparadzana mukuzvikudza kwenyu; mukuzvikudza kwenyu munorwisa avo vanoedza kukudzidzisai, uye mava vasingadzidzisiki. Munokakavara, munopopotedzana, munopikisana, uye munorwisana panzvimbo pokufunga nokuzvininipisa pamusoro pezvinotaurwa nomumwe. Kune rumwe rutivi, mune manyawi okufunga kuti nokuti mashoko anobuda mumiromo yenyu ndeechokwadi nokuti IMI makataura? Zviisei pasi pomumwe nomumwe. Nyararai uye endai nemashoko ehama dzenyu kumagwaro kuti muedze zvinhu zvose uye mubatisise zvakanaka. Dzururai nzeve dzenyu uye teererai zvizere uye fungisisai, nokuti mukuita izvozvo ndichakuitai vakachenjera mukuzvininipisa kwenyu, saka musava vakachenjera mukuzvifunga kwenyu. Ndinopa zano kumunhu wose pakati penyu, inzwai mashoko angu. Uye mashoko iwayo anoti kudini? Nokuti ndinoti, nenyasha dzandakapiwa, kumunhu wose ari pakati penyu, kuti arege kuzvifunga kupfuura zvaanofanira kufunga, asi afunge zvine musoro, sezvo Mwari akapa munhu wose chipimo chokutenda.

Kwete, mirai, fungisisai mashoko andichangokupai, vhurai maBhaibheri enyu muverenge mashoko aya zvakare. Anzwirei uye atenderei, nokuti munofanira kuzvifunga zvakanaka pamusoro penyu, nokuti ndakakumutsai pamwe chete uye ndakakugarisai pamwe chete munzvimbo dzokudenga mandiri, Kristu Jesu. Asi panguva imwe chete munofanira kufunga zvine musoro, nokuti Mwari akapa kumunhu wose pakati penyu chipimo chimwe chete chokutenda, saka mweya wakapiwa hausi mukuru kana muduku muhama dzenyu, asi kumunhu wose wenyu kwakapiwa nyasha, maererano nechipimo chechipo chaKristu. Naizvozvo anoti: Paakakwira kumusoro, akatapa nhapwa, akapa zvipo kuvanhu. Ndakapa vamwe vaapostora; navamwe vaporofita; navamwe vaevhangeri; navamwe vafudzi, navadzidzisi: Kuti vatsvene vakwaniswe, pabasa roushumiri, nokuvakwa komuviri waKristu. Zviisei pasi pomumwe nomumwe uye rasanai nokuzvikudza kwenyu kusina maturo sezvo kuchikonzera mapoka pakati penyu. Sei musinganzwisisi kuti hapafaniri kuva nokuparadzana pakati penyu, vana vangu. Muviri waKristu wakakamurwa here? Ndakati kudini kuvaKorinde?

Zvino ndinokukumbirisai, hama, nezita raIshe wedu Jesu Kristu, kuti imi mose mutaure chinhu chimwe, uye kuti pakati penyu parege kuva nokupesana; asi kuti mubatanidzwe zvakakwana mupfungwa imwe, nomukutonga kumwe. Hamusi mose chinhu chimwe mandiri here, Ishe Jesu Kristu? Asi mune mapoka akawanda pakati penyu, uye munoruma nokuparadzana nokuda kwekusawirirana kuduku-duku. Zivai kuti mumapoka enyu nomukureverana kwenyu munofadza Satani, uye anozvirumbidza pamusoro pokushaya maturo kwenyu kwokuedza kurwisana nokuti vazhinji venyu vanoshaya kuzvininipisa. Uye nzeve dzenyu dzakadzivirwa saka hamugoni kunzwa manzwi omumwe nomumwe, uye makavhara meso enyu, uye akanamirwa mukuzvikudza nokusatenda, nokuti vazhinji venyu vanofunga kuti vane chokwadi chose. Uye munoramba mashoko ehama dzenyu, uye munoramba mashoko chaiwo andakavapa nokuda kwenyu, uye mukuzvikudza kwenyu kunoshamisa munoshaya kuzvininipisa kwokutanga kunzwa nokufunga. Nokuti kana munhu akasanzwa chinhu, upenzi nenyadzi kwaari. Uye zvakare, sei muchizvidza hama dzenyu, nokuti isu tose tinofanira kumira pamberi pechigaro chokutonga chaKristu? Ndinokupai zano, Kereke yangu, kuti murambe kuzvikudza kwenyu nenzira dzenyu dzokuzvikudza uye muuye kwandiri somudzidzi weShoko roupenyu, nokuti ndiri Shoko raMwari rakaitwa nyama.

Saka teererai nomweya wenyu wose uye teererai manzwi enzeve dzenyu uye nzwisisai mashoko angu. Saka nzwisisai kurongeka kwezviitiko zvakapiwa muShoko rangu, nokuti vazhinji venyu munonyengerwa namapere anoparadza amunotevera mukusaziva, nokuti makateerera vafudzi vakatsauka. Ndimi aniko kuti muparidze kuti hakuna kuunganidzwa kweKereke yangu kwandiri, kuti handisangani neKereke yangu mumhepo pandinouya nokudanidzira kukuru nenzwi romutumwa mukuru uye nehwamanda yaMwari? Nokuti hwamanda icharira uye vakafa muna Kristu vachatanga kumuka uye imi muri vapenyu uye makasara hamungavatangiri, uye imi mose muchatorwa mumakore kuti musangane neni uye nokusingaperi muchava neni. Mashoko aya akanaka zvakanyanya kuti muatende here? Kana kuti muchatenda mashoko evafudzi vechiJudha vakatsauka kupfuura inzwi raIshe wenyu nomuponesi, Kristu Jesu? Sei musina kutenda muShoko rangu kana mumashoko chaiwo anoumba Shoko rangu? Hamunzwisisi here kuti chakavanzika cheBhaibheri chii kana chinangwa chacho?

Ndichakuudzai: Chakavanzika chinhu chakachengetwa chakavanzika asi chakaporofitwa pamusoro pacho chinozoburitswa pachena panguva yakakodzera. Uye sei Baba vaizoita chinhu chakadaro, munobvunza? Hamuna kuverenga mushumo wangu here? Asi tinotaura uchenjeri hwaMwari muchakavanzika, kunyange uchenjeri hwakavanzwa hwakarongwa naMwari nyika isati yavapo, kuti tive nokubwinya. Izvo zvisina kuzivikanwa nomumwe wemachinda enyika ino: nokuti dai vakazviziva, vangadai vasina kuroverera Ishe wokubwinya. Ndimi aniko kuti murambe zvirongwa nenzira dzaBaba vangu? Nokuti haadi mupi wezano, kunyange zviuru gumi zvakapetwa nezviuru gumi zvichikomberedza chigaro chake choumambo.

Saka nyaradzanai nemashoko angu, nokuti makaponeswa kubva pakutsamwa mandiri. Zvino, munzira dzenyu dzokusatenda, munokakavara pamusoro penguva yechiitiko ichi. Kunyange zvazvo vamwe venyu vachitaura zvisizvo kuti hakuna kuungana, vazhinji venyu munopikisana pamusoro penguva yacho maererano nedambudziko raJakobho. Ndichazvijekesa kwamuri kana muchizoteerera, kunyange vazhinji venyu vacharamba chiitiko ichi nokushinga, kunyange kundiramba uye kunyatsosimbisa ramangwana renyu kuti mutore nzvimbo yenyu pakati pemhandara dzisina kuchenjera. Nokuti Shoko rangu rinoti kudini?

Zvino tinokukumbirisai, hama, nokuuya kwaIshe wedu Jesu Kristu, nokuungana kwedu kwaari, Kuti murege kukurumidza kuzununguswa papfungwa dzenyu, kana kutambudzika nomweya kana neshoko kana netsamba, sezvinonzi zvakabva kwatiri, sokunge zuva raKristu rava pedyo. Kurege kuva nomunhu unokunyengerai nenzira ipi neipi: nokuti zuva iro harisviki, kusvikira kwatanga kuuya kubva, uye munhu wechivi uyo abudiswe pachena, iye mwanakomana wokuparadzwa. (Tarirai kuByzantine, vana vangu, nokuti maBhaibheri mazhinji akaora akabva mumagwaro eGnostic, magwaro enhema akapiwa navanhu vane zvivi. Hamunzwisisi here musiyano pakati pegwaro reBhaibheri neshanduro yeBhaibheri?) Kuchava nokutsauka kubva pakutenda mukuramba kutenda, uye avo vanoramba kubva pakutenda vachabva panyika kuti vasangane neni mumhepo.

Nokuti pano pane mufananidzo wemhandara gumi. Mhandara shanu dzakachenjera dzakatenga mafuta dzikagadzirisa tambo dzadzo uye dzakanga dzakagadzirira pakusvika kwomuroori, uye shanu dzakanga dzisina kugadzirira uye dzakaregeredza kutenga mafuta, dzichiedza kutora kubva pane zvinodiwa navamwe. Mhandara shanu dzakagadzirira dzakaenderera mberi nomuroori, uye shanu dzisina kuchenjera dzakakumbira kupinzwa mukati shure kwaizvozvo dzikaudzwa kuti, ibvai kwandiri, handina kumbokuzivai. Musadzima mweya wangu mamuri ungasimbisa mashoko aya. Nzwisisai kuti mwanakomana wokuparadzwa haagoni kuuya musimba uye umambo hwose hwaSatani hahugoni kusimuka kubva mugomba rakadzika-dzika kusvikira mudzivisi abviswa, uye sezvo makaponeswa kubva pakutsamwa, mudzivisi achabviswa pandinounganidza Mwenga wangu kwandiri mumakore paanosangana neni mumhepo.

Zvino pamagumo emakore manomwe edambudziko raJakobho kunouya kumuka kwevakarurama. Tarirai! Uye ndakaona zvigaro zvoumambo, uye vakagara pazviri, uye kutonga kwakapiwa kwavari: uye ndakaona mweya yeavo vakagurwa misoro nokuda kweuchapupu hwaJesu, uye nokuda kweshoko raMwari, uye vakanga vasina kunamata chikara, kana mufananidzo wacho, kana kugamuchira chiratidzo chacho pahuma dzavo, kana mumaoko avo; uye vakararama vakatonga naKristu makore ane chiuru. Asi vamwe vakafa havana kurarama zvakare kusvikira makore ane chiuru apera. Uku ndiko kumuka kwekutanga. Akaropafadzwa uye mutsvene ndiye ane mugove mukumuka kwekutanga: parudzi urwu rufu rwechipiri haruna simba, asi vachava Vapristi vaMwari, naKristu, uye vachatonga naye makore ane chiuru.

Muchiri kusatenda mashoko angu here? Zvirokwazvo, vazhinji venyu vachanamatira zvisina tsarukano kukanganisa kwenyu nemashoko evafudzi vakatsauka, uye vazhinji venyu vangadai vakapukunyuka kutsamwa kwaMwari kunouya kuzoedza nyika panguva yedambudziko raJakobho, vachisarudza kutsungirira kutsamwa kukuru pandakatoedza uye ndakakuyedzai, kunze kwekuti mucharamba nokushinga kuonekwa kwangu, uye muchanamatira kutsamwa kusina kuitirwa imi. Handina kukuudzai here kuti mubude maari kuti murege kugoverana naye matambudziko ake?

Asi, kuchava nerumwe rumuko. Uye ndakaona chigaro chichena chikuru, naiye wakanga agere pachiri, kubva pachiso chake nyika nedenga zvakatiza, uye pakanga pasina nzvimbo yakawanikwa kwavari. Uye ndakaona vakafa, vaduku navakuru, vamire pamberi paMwari: uye mabhuku akazarurwa: uye rimwe bhuku rakazarurwa, rinova Bhuku roUpenyu: uye vakafa vakatongwa kubva muzvinhu izvo zvakanga zvakanyorwa mumabhuku, maererano namabasa avo. Uye gungwa rakabudisa vakafa vakanga vari mariri: uye rufu nehero zvakabudisa vakafa vakanga vari mazviri: uye vakatongwa mumwe nomumwe maererano namabasa ake, nokuti uku ndiko kumuka kwevasakarurama vanhu vangu.

Sei musinganzwisisi kuti kuungana kweKereke yangu kuti isangane neni mumakore pakuonekwa kwangu kwakasiyana nokudzoka kwangu neMwenga wangu nemauto okudenga? Sei musinganzwisisi kuti kuungana kweKereke yangu chakavanzika chakazarurwa noMuapostora Pauro muchirevo cheChakavanzika Chikuru chaMwari. Saka chakavanzika mukati mechakavanzika, uye hachina kuzarurirwa zvizere kuvanakomana vavanhu kusvikira chazarurirwa Pauro akachinyora pasi, uye ndakakuchengeterai.

Asi, kune mienzaniso yakawanda yaMwari achitora vanhu kudenga nokuda kwezvinangwa zvakati. Enoki akabva. Eria akabva. Ini ndakabva ndichienda kudenga apo vaapostora vangu vaitarisa vachishamisika. Firipi akabviswa pane imwe nzvimbo achienda kune imwe sezvo Baba vakamutora nokuda kwezvinangwa zvangu. Uye saizvozvowo mwenga wangu achaunganidzwa kwandiri mumakore kuti atorwe kudenga kuti aroorwe neni uye kuti ashumire zvinangwa zvangu zvokudzikinura.

Hamunzwisisi here kuti Mwenga wangu achava madzimambo navapristi pamberi paMwari wangu naBaba uye achatonga neni muumambo hwangu huri kuuya uye achava neni nokusingaperi? Sei muchinonoka kutenda mashoko angu? Makatswinyiwa uye makatsauswa nevafudzi vakatsauka here? Kunyange zvakadaro, vazhinji venyu vachava uporofita hunozvizadzisa uye vachazvisimbisa pakutsauka nokuramba mirayiro yangu, kunyanya dzidziso yeKereke yangu ichiunganidzwa kwandiri. Nyarai imi munobatanidza dzidziso iyi kuvaporofita venhema munguva pfupi yapfuura, muchizivisa kubiwa kwenhema, kwakavanzika kwakasungirirwa kune dzimwe dzidziso dzenhema dzenguva.

Nzwisisai kuti kune nguva munguva yaMwari uye kwete nguva dzekutonga, uye hadzisi pfungwa imwe chete. Dzidzai kuti muzviratidze makatenderwa, uye regai kuva neusimbe hwepfungwa. Regai kuvimba nokuverenga dudziro yevamwe vose yezvakanyorwa, uye rarama nazvo zvakanyorwa: Nokuti hamufaniri kuti munhu upi noupi akudzidzisei. Asi, vazhinji venyu vachaseka nokuramba zano rangu. Kunze kwekuti chinhu ichi hachiitwi muchivande uye Satani achatanga kunyengera kukuru kuti avhare chiitiko ichi nokuti chichazivikanwa pasi rose. Kuzununguka kukuru kuchaitika, uye nyika yose ichachinja kubva kunyika yamakaziva, uye munhu uyo wechivi achazarurwa uye achasimuka kubva mumadota akasiiwa shure navatasvi vemabhiza mana. Inguva yokuzvigadzirira, Mwenga wangu, uye itai saizvozvo nokukurumidza, nokuti tarirai kuonekwa kwangu kwava pedyo!

Ndini mwanakomana woMunhu wakaporofitwa naEnoki, uyo Mwari waakatora kudenga.

Ndini iye wakashandurwa chitarisiko akataura naMosesi naEria pamusoro pokubva kwangu, uye Mwari akatora Eria kudenga panguva yake.

Ndini iye wakatorwa mugore pandakagamuchirwa kudenga kusvikira kunguva dzokudzorera.

Ndini iye wakatuma Firipi kumuranda weItiopia Mwari asati amutora kune imwe nzvimbo panyika.

Ndini iye wakabata Pauro akamuendesa kudenga rechitatu uye akamuratidza zvinhu zvisingatauriki zvaakanga asina rusununguko rwokugoverana.

Ndini iye uchaburuka kubva kudenga nokudanidzira kukuru kuti ndiunganidze Mwenga wangu kuti asangane neni mumhepo.

Ndini iye uchamutsa zvapupu zvangu zviviri kuupenyu mushure mokunge vafa kwemazuva matatu nehafu, uye ndichavatora kudenga.

Ndini iye anova Arifa naOmega, nokuti ndini kutanga nokuguma, uye tarirai! Ndinouya nokukurumidza kupfuura zvamunofunga, saka ivai vakagadzirira kusangana neni kana kutsauka.

Ani naani anodana zita raIshe kusvikira kumagumo achaponeswa.
