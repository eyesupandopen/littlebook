Hutongi hwaMerikizedeki: Jasi Rakapfuudzwa, Kumbomiswa, neKudzoreredzwa

Kubva kuna Yahweh, IYE MUKURU ARIKO:

Inzwai Mashoko aYahweh Mwari mukuru wekusingaperi uye regai ndigovere huchenjeri kumunhu akachenjera pakati penyu. Saka ndinokupai kuti munzwisise Hutongi hwaMerikizedeki, kunyange hutongi uhwo Kristu Jesu akagadzwa Mupristi Mukuru, kunyange zvichienderana nehutongi hwaMerikizedeki nekusingaperi! Asi zvakadaro, nekuda kwezvivi zvevanhu, nzira dzangu dzakakanganikwa, uye sungano zhinji dzakaraswa, uye rima guru nenzira dzekusadzidza zvakafukidza nyika nekuda kwenhema dzevakawa. Uye izvi nekuda kwenhema dzinotaurwa nemaune uye neavo vanotsvaga kudzvinyirira chokwadi munzira dzavo dzisina kururama uye munhema dzavo dzenhema idzo nyika yakabatwa nadzo mukunyengera kwavo kukuru kwakauya pamusoro penyika yose. Kunyange kunyengera muchikamu kunofungidzira kuti hupenyu hwose hunobva kune kumwe kuputika kukuru kusinganzwisisike kwakapiwa kuti vanhu vadzidziswe nhema dzevakawa vanoedza kuvanza Yahweh, IYE MUKURU ARIKO, kubva kunyika yandakasika neruoko rwangu.

Uye ndini Ishe weMweya, uye ndini Mutana weMazuva uyo zvechokwadi, zvechokwadi ndiye aivako mazuva asati aitwa uye ndini ndakasika ngirozi tsvene dzakanyorwa pamusoro padzo kuti: Anoita mweya nhume dzake, uye vashumiri vake murazvo wemoto, kunyange apo chisikwa chakasikwa uye chikabudiswa kubva muhupfumi hwemoyo wangu wekusingaperi unoshanda zvinhu zvose maererano nezano rekuda kwangu. Nekuti zvechokwadi ndaiziva kuti Adhamu aizotadza achida kunyange Adhamu asati atadza uye kuti huori hwaizowa kubva kudenga muvanhu vakawa. Naizvozvo, ndakaronga zano rangu rekusingaperi rekudzikinurwa kwakazara kwaAdhamu rakaiswa zvizere pachiso chaJesu Kristu uyo ariwo Yeshua HaMashiach muTestamente yake Itsva yekururama nechokwadi!

Uye mukufanana nemufananidzo waAdhamu wekupedzisira, vanhu vachadzikinurwa mumufaro usingaperi nerunyararo, kwavachazadzisa murudo zvinangwa zvekusingaperi zvaYahweh, IYE MUKURU ARIKO. Zvechokwadi, chisikwa changu chakasikwa uye chakaitwa murudo kuti zvisikwa zvangu zviuye kwandiri murudo nekuda kwezvose zvandakavapa murudo rwangu kwavari. Uye kuti chisikwa changu chifambe neni takabatana maoko muzvinangwa zvangu zvekusingaperi mukuzadzisa kuda kwangu kwekusingaperi, uye ivo vachava nemufaro wakakwana uye uzere, runyararo, nehupenyu husingaperi kunyange vachitungamirirwa murunyararo neruoko rweMufudzi wangu Mukuru, Jesu weNazareta, uyo pasina iye hapana munhu anogona kusvika kuna Yahweh, IYE MUKURU ARIKO, zvakare, nekuti ndizvo zvandakazivisa.

Uye vakawa vakaparadzira nhema dzavo pamusoro pechisikwa changu zvichiguma nekuti nyika yava kuzara nekunyengera kwavo, kwavanovandira kuti vanyengere. Kunyange kunyengera kwakauya zvizere semugumisiro wechakavanzika chekusarurama chakapona pamafashamo aNoa nenzira yeruzivo rwenhema rwevakawa rwavakapa kumunhu wenyama kuti aparadzwe zvachose. Hazvina basa, nekuti ndinotumira vose vanofarira kusarurama kunyengera kune simba kuti vose vapiwe mhosva vanoramba ruzivo rwechokwadi vachifarira nhema dzenhema dzevakawa. Asi zvakadaro, munhu wenyama nekusaziva anotsvaga kukumbira nyaya yake yekuzviruramisa pamberi pechitarisiko changu chekusingaperi uye chakarurama, naizvozvo, nzwisisai kuti hapana nyama inoruramiswa pamberi pangu nekuda kwehuori huri muropa renyu nekuda kwechivi chiri munyama. Nekuti hupenyu hwenyama huri muropa!

Nechikonzero ichi mwanakomana waYahweh akaratidzwa kuvagari vepasi, kuti nechibayiro chinodzikinura chegwayana raMwari, Adhamu adzikinurwe muruponeso ruzere kubva mukutongwa kwake kuri kuuya kune vose vanoteerera chibvumirano changu cherunyararo pakati pangu nevanhu anova Jesu weNazareta uyo akaitwa mweya unopa hupenyu nesimba reruoko rwangu. Uye zvakare, neShumba yeRudzi rwaJuda, wandiri kutumira kunyika mukururama kuti nditonge uye ndiite hondo uye saka chisikwa chichatorwa nechisimba kubva mumaoko evatorwa vanogara vachipanduka uye vanotsvaga kumisa kuda kwangu kwekusingaperi, kumiswa uko kusingagoneki nekusingaperi, nekuti ndiani aripo anogona kumira achipikisa ruoko rwangu rune simba? Uye muhuwandu hwavo, uye mukuipa kwavo, uye mumazano avo, uye mumano avo, uye kunyange mumatekinoroji avo asina maturo (avanodada nawo) vakawa vakundwa zvachose nechibayiro chegwayana raMwari, uyo akapa ropa rake risina chivi seMupristi Mukuru nekusingaperi maererano neHutongi hwaMerikizedeki mukuteerera kwose kuna iye akamutuma, kunyange Yahweh, IYE MUKURU ARIKO wekusingaperi!

Uye humambo hwake hahuzomboperi, nekuti mukururama kwose nehumwari hwechokwadi akatenga nyika neropa rake rinodzikinura kuti hutongi hwakarurama huuye pamusoro penyika munguva yayo uye kuti tifare nezuva rekuzorora (nekuti makore ane chiuru kuna Yahweh akaita sezuva rimwe uye zuva rimwe semakore ane chiuru pamberi pangu), kunyange zororo remazuva esabata nyoka iya yekare isati yakandwa mumirazvo yemoto nekusingaperi mukurwadziwa kwakazara uye kwekusingaperi isingazombofungwi zvakare uye kutongwa kwechigaro chikuru chichena kusati kwaitika. Uye nebasa remupristi mukuru weHutongi hwaMerikizedeki, Adhamu akadzikinurwa kubva muhuori nenzira yaJesu weNazareta uyo akapa chibayiro chake chekusingaperi mukurwadziwa kunorwadza kuti abhadhare mutongo waMosesi kuti mitemo yangu yekusingaperi igutsikane iyo chisikwa changu chekusingaperi chinotongwa zvakakwana mukudzikinurwa kwakarurama.

Uye zvechokwadi zvichaitika kuti ndichasika denga idzva nenyika itsva nekuti denga rekutanga, nenyika yekutanga zvichapfuura, uye hakuchazovi negungwa; uyewo hakuchazovi nemunhu angakunetsai nekusingaperi. Uye zvichaitika nekukurumidza zvekuti vakundi vangu vachagara nhaka yezvinhu zvose, uye ndichava Mwari wavo, uye vachava vanakomana vangu. Asi vanotya nevasina kutenda, nevanonyangadza nemhondi, nemhombwe, nevaroyi, nevanonamata zvidhori, nevose vanoreva nhema vachava nechikamu chavo mugungwa rinopfuta nemoto nesarufa, runova rufu rwechipiri.

Naizvozvo, ndiani wamunofanira kutya vana vangu, nekuti makaitwa vana vaMwari mupenyu nemabasa aYeshua HaMashiach uyo ariwo Jesu Kristu, kunyange iye wandakapa simba pamusoro pezvose muchisikwa changu, kunze kwekuti pachigaro changu chehumambo ndinochengeta simba rangu, naizvozvo ndiani anogona kumira achipikisa ruoko rune simba rwaKristu Jesu, Ishe akamutswa uye akakwidzwa? Zvechokwadi, zvechokwadi, sezvazvakanga zvakaita, uye sezvazviri, uye ndizvo zvazvichava nekusingaperi uye nekusingaperi-peri kuti Yahweh naYeshua HaMashiach vamwe mukubatana nechinangwa uye ndakamupa simba rakazara mukururama kwose uye ndiye wandakapa kutonga pamusoro pehondo yose yekudenga, uye anotonga mukuteerera kwakakwana kukuda kwangu kwekusingaperi. Uye saka ndinokupai kuti munzwisise hunyengeri hwakauya pamusoro penyika, kunyange nhema dzevakawa vanotsvaga kuvanza chokwadi cheruponeso rwenyu nechokwadi chaIshe wenyu wekusingaperi, Kristu Jesu!

Naizvozvo, nzwisisai kuti vadzidzisi venyu vechiJesuit vakonzera bofu pamwoyo yenyu nekuvanza chokwadi chechisikwa changu uye nekuvanza nhoroondo yenyu yechokwadi kubva kwamuri, kuti mudzungaire murima muchizadzisa kuchiva kwavo kwakaipa, kunyange kuitira kuti njiva dzangu dzibatane navo munhaka yavo yekusingaperi yekupisa nekukunguruka kwesarufa nekuti ndizvo zvavari kuda. Uye saka zviri pachena kuti Yahweh, IYE MUKURU ARIKO, haana kumbosiya ini ndisina chapupu. Uye nesungano imwe neimwe yandakapa, uye netestamente imwe neimwe yandakapa uye ndikagamuchira, handina kumbosiya ini ndisina chapupu chakajeka chemagwaro uye nhungamiro yakapiwa nemanzwi evaprofita vangu vekare, vandakagadza. Uye zvakare yakapiwa nemanzwi evaprofita veTestamente Itsva ayo Yeshua HaMashiach akagadza avo vachamushumira sevapristi vake vane simba nemadzimambo!

Nekuti ndizvo zvakaita Hutongi hwaMerikizedeki, kunyange hutongi hwekusingaperi hwakapiwa kuvanhu, apo hutongi hwaifanira kuitwa naAdhamu panyika hwaifanira kutongwa naiye akagadzwa mupristi mukuru, mambo, nemuprofita! Nekuti ndizvo zvakanga zvakaita (uye zvichiri) Hutongi hwaMerikizedeki hwakapiwa kuvanhu, kunyange kuti vanhu vave nehutongi hwakarurama kunyangwe paine nyoka iya yekare yakanyengera Evha nehunyengeri hwayo. Uye saka Hutongi hwaMerikizedeki hwakaenderera mberi, kunyange hwakazorora pana Adhamu kubva pakutanga kwaakapiwa, asi mukufa vakazofa uye hutongi hwaMerikizedeki hwakapfuudzwa kumberi kwechinguva, mumazuva kubva kuna Adhamu kusvika kuna Jakobho.

Uye saka muNyika yaivapo mafashamo makuru asati auya, Hutongi hwaMerikizedeki hwakapfuura kubva kuna Adhamu, kuenda kuna Kainani, kuenda kuna Enosi, kuenda kuna Enoki, kuenda kuna Metusera, kuenda kuna Noa uye ndizvo zvakaita Hutongi hwaMerikizedeki mumazuva mafashamo makuru asati auya. Asi, vadzidzisi venyu vechiJesuit vakanyorazve magwaro, uye vakaita hunyengeri hwakawanda kuitira kuvanza chokwadi chenhoroondo yechokwadi yaAdhamu kunyange vachiedza kuita kuti vanhu vave seguruva risina basa muchadenga chavo chenhema icho chinonzi chakabva kukuputika kukuru kumwe kunhu munguva yavo yekare yavanofungidzira.

Nekuti maJesuit anoparidza mumwe Jesu, kunyange Jesu akaitwa mumufananidzo waNimrodhi weBhabhironi, uyo aiva mutongi wekutanga werudzi rwake mushure memafashamo. Uye kunyange vatongi vangu vakarurama vakagadzwa kare pasi peHutongi hwaMerikizedeki vakapa vanhu kururama panzvimbo pechivi, kutambura, rufu, nekurangwa kwekusingaperi. Zvisinei, munhu wenyama anowanzo sarudza chivi, kutambura, rufu, nekurangwa kwekusingaperi. Zvisinei, vose vari muchisikwa changu vakapiwa rusununguko rwekusarudza vanosununguka kusarudza vega kuti vachararamira Yahweh nenzira yaKristu Jesu, kana kuti vosarudza kurangwa kwavo kwekusingaperi nekuda kwemafaro echivi kwenguva pfupi. Uye saka zvakanyorwa kuti vose vemunyika yaivapo kunze kwemweya misere vakaponeswa vari vapenyu nekuda kwaNoa, uyo aiva murume akarurama akatonga nyika maererano neHutongi hwaMerikizedeki kwechinguva nekuti mukufa akafa.

Naizvozvo, nzwisisai nzira dzevakawa, kuti vanotsvaga nemwoyo yavo yose kukanganisa, kureva nhema, nekuvanza chokwadi kubva kunjiva dzangu, kunyange avo vandakagadza kuhupenyu husingaperi. Uye saka zviri pachena kuti sezvo zano rangu rekuponesa Adhamu rakaburitswa zvishoma nezvishoma, ndakatumira zvinyorwa zvitsvene nemweya wechiporofita wakazorora pavari, uye kunyange mweya wechiporofita wakazozvarwa muvanakomana vaMwari nekuzvarwa kutsva kwakapiwa muTestamente Itsva yaYeshua HaMashiach, kunyange muna iye wandinogara ndichifadzwa naye. Uye sezvo ndakaburitsa zano rangu zvishoma nezvishoma, ndakapawo zvinyorwa zvitsvene zvishoma nezvishoma, uye Kristu Jesu akapa zvinyorwa zvitsvene zvakagamuchirwa kubva kuruoko rwangu, kunyange iye anoshanda zvinhu zvose maererano nezano rekuda kwangu.

Uye saka vakawa vanoshanda kuti vakanganise chokwadi, kunyange nenzira yakafanana neyavakazvikanganisa nekusingaperi uye nekusingaperi-peri. Nekuti vakanyorazve magwaro, vakapa maBhaibheri ekudzidza enhema, vakapa maduramazwi enhema nemakonkodhenzi ayo vazhinji vanonyengerwa netsanangudzo dzisina kuchena, sekunge Yahweh haakwanisi kupa vaturikiri vanokwanisa kuturikira Shoko rangu nekuti Shoko rangu harigoni kuputswa kana kuvanzwa, kunyange vachiedza. Zvechokwadi, kubwinya kwaYahweh kuvanza nyaya uye rukudzo rweMadzimambo kuitsvaga! Naizvozvo, ndinokurayirai kuti mutsvage zvokwadi zvaYahweh pachiso chaYeshua HaMashiach!

Saka nzwisisai kuti avo vanoshumira hure guru iroro, kunyange vadzidzisi venyu vechiJesuit, vakasarudza muunganidzwa wakanyatsosarudzwa wenhema dzisina kunyatsopindirana dzavanovandira nadzo kuti vanyengere. Vakavanza zvipupuriro zvitsvene zvakawanda uye vakanyorazve maBhaibheri mazhinji akavakirwa pamagwaro enhema uye vakaita nhema zhinji dzenhema maererano nechokwadi chechisikwa changu. Uye muruzivo rwangu rwemberi nekutonga kwangu ndakabvumira wakawa kuti aite saizvozvo, nekuti dai zvinyorwa zvangu zvitsvene zvakachengetedzwa zvakanaka uye zvikachengetwa kunyange sezvakarayirwa uye zvakapiwa, upenzi hwakadaro hungadai husina kumbobvira hwaitwa. Asi ndizvo zvakaita nzira dzemunhu ane chivi uye ndizvo zvakaita nzira dzevakawa vanotungamirira vanhu munzira isiriyo mukuchiva kwavo kwakaipa kunogara kuchipisa. Kunyange zvakadaro, Shoko rangu harigoni kuputswa uye richazadzisa chinhu icho charakatumirwa kuti chizadzise.

Uye saka zvakanyorwa muna Jasher kuti mushure memafashamo makuru jasi raMerikizedeki rakaramba riri pana Noa uye rakazopfuudzwa kuna Shem uyo aiva wechitanhatu mutsara wekupfeka jasi uye nekufamba kwenguva hutongi hwaMerikizedeki hwakapfuudzwa kuna Abram uyo nekufamba kwenguva akava Baba vemarudzi mazhinji, uye kuvagari venhaka yake yechipikirwa ndiko kunoenda zvipikirwa zvenhaka kuti zvizadziswe nekuti mwanakomana wemurandakadzi haazovi mugari wenhaka yezvipikirwa nekuti nyama neropa hazvigoni kugara nhaka yezvipikirwa zveHumambo hwaMwari asi vakarurama vanorarama nekutenda, kunyange sezvakaita Abraham akatendeka mumazuva ekare.

Naizvozvo, ndinokupai kuti munzwisise zvinoreva Merikizedeki zvinoreva kuti Mambo weKururama. Uye saka etymology ishoko rechiHebheru rakashandurwa kuChirungu apo Melek zvinoreva Mambo uye Zedek zvinoreva kururama nezvinoreva zvakapiwa uye zvakatarisirwa zviri Mambo weKururama. Uye saka Madzimambo aya akarurama akatonga chisikwa changu, kunyange varume vakarurama vakamirira Mesia wavo, kunyange iye aizozvarwa kubva kurudzi rwaJuda.

Uye sezvazvakanyorwa muna VaHebheru neruoko rwaPauro (uyo aiva mutariri wezvakavanzika zvaKristu Jesu) uye saka akanyora kuti: Nekuti Merikizedeki uyu aiva Mambo weSaremu, Mupristi waMwari wepamusoro-soro, akasangana naAbraham, paakadzoka kubva pakuuraya Madzimambo, uye akamuropafadza: Kwaariwo Abraham akapa chegumi chezvinhu zvose: uyo kutanga nekududzirwa ari Mambo weKururama: shure kwaizvozvo, ariwo Mambo weSaremu, ndiko kuti, Mambo werunyararo, asina baba, asina amai, asina hama, uye haana kutanga kwemazuva ake, kana kupera kwehupenyu: asi akafanana neMwanakomana waMwari, uye anoramba ari Mupristi nekusingaperi. Uye saka jasi raMerikizedeki rakapfuudzwa kuna Abraham akatendeka mukuchengetedzwa kwaro kunyange hutongi hwakachengetedzwa uye hwakatarisirwa Jesu Kristu uyo ariwo Yeshua HaMashiach.

Asi, vazhinji venyu makatenda nhema dzevadzidzisi venyu vechiJesuit avo vakapofumadza vazhinji venyu nedzidzo inorwisa dzidzo, kuti ndima idzi dzinoreva Jesu Kristu pachake kwete Hutungamiri hwaMerkizedeki pachahwo. Nokuti zviri pachena, Jesu weNazareta ane Baba, iye Yahweh Mukuru NDIRI, uye zviri pachena kuti Jesu weNazareta ane mai, iye Maria aibva kuNazareta yeGarirea akati ngazviitwe kwandiri maererano nokuda kwenyu, iwo muenzaniso usingaperi wekutenda wakapiwa kuti vakatendeka vazive nzira dzekutenda dzavanofamba nadzo. Nokuti zviri pachena kuti Jesu weNazareta ane hama dzaari mudzikinuri wadzo, sei musingaremekedzi Mashoko angu akajeka akapiwa mumagwaro achida kukutungamirirai zvakanaka kure nenhema dzavo dzenhema? Asi vazhinji venyu munotenda nhema idzi kupfuura kujeka nokururama kweMashoko angu.

Asi, ndizvo zvakanyorwa kuti Shemu akaramba akatendeka kuna Noa, nokuti Hamu akarasa zvachose sungano yaNoa akakanganwa nzira dzaYahweh uye Hamu nevazukuru vazhinji vaNoa vakaedza kuvaka guta ravo guru neshongwe sezvo umambo hwaNimurodhi husina kururama hwakamuka kutonga nyika mumazuva iwayo mukusarurama, maererano nenzira dzevakawa avo vaakashandira, kunyange zvazvo ndakashanda kakawanda kuti ndipe Nimurodhi sarudzo yekudzokera kwandiri mukutendeuka, sarudzo yaakaramba nokusingaperi-peri.

Asi ndizvo zvakanyorwa zvakajeka muna Jasher pamusoro paAbhurama naShemu uyo akapa jasi kuna Abhurama pakuuraya kwake Kedoriraomeri nemauto ake: Uye paakadzoka kubva pakurova madzimambo aya, iye nevarume vake vakapfuura nomumupata weSidhimu apo madzimambo akanga arwa hondo pamwe chete. Uye Bhera mambo weSodhoma, nevamwe varume vake vakanga vainaye, vakabuda mumakomba etara mavakanga vawira, kuti vasangane naAbhurama nevarume vake. Uye Adhonizedeki (kana kuti Merkizedeki) mambo weJerusarema, iye aiva Shemu, akabuda nevarume vake kunosangana naAbhurama nevanhu vake, nechingwa newaini, uye vakagara pamwe chete mumupata waMereki. Uye Adhonizedeki (kana kuti Merkizedeki) akaropafadza Abhurama, uye Abhurama akamupa chegumi chezvose zvaakanga aunza kubva pane zvaakapamba kuvavengi vake, nokuti Adhonizedeki (kana kuti Merkizedeki) aiva mupristi pamberi paMwari.

Asi ndizvo zvakanyorwa muna Jasher: Uye mazuva aSara aiva zana nemakumi maviri nemanomwe emakore uye akafa, uye Abhurahama akaita kuchema kukuru nokurema, uye akaita tsika dzekuchema kwemazuva manomwe. Uye vagari vose venyika vakanyaradza Abhurahama naIsaka mwanakomana wake pamusoro paSara. Uye mazuva ekuchema kwavo paakapfuura Abhurahama akatuma mwanakomana wake Isaka, uye akaenda kumba kwaShemu naEbheri, kunodzidza nzira dzaIshe nemirayiridzo yake, uye Abhurahama akagara ikoko makore matatu. Saka jasi raMerkizedeki rakapfuudzwa kuna Isaka uyo akapa kodzero yeudangwe kumudiki, nokuti Esau akazvidza zvinhu zvaMwari akatengesa udangwe hwake nendiro yemuto: Iyo sarudzo yakagamuchirwa naYahweh nokusingaperi.

Asi ndizvo zvakanyorwa muna Jasher pamusoro peHutungamiri hwaMerkizedeki: Uye shure kwaizvozvi Shera mwanakomana waApakishadhi akafa mugore iroro, rinova gore regumi nesere rehupenyu hwaJakobho naEsau; uye mazuva ose akararama Shera aiva mazana mana nemakumi matatu nematatu emakore uye akafa. Panguva iyoyo Isaka akatuma mwanakomana wake mudiki Jakobho kumba kwaShemu naEbheri, uye akadzidza mirayiridzo yaIshe, uye Jakobho akagara mumba maShemu naEbheri kwemakore makumi matatu nemaviri, uye Esau mukoma wake haana kuenda, nokuti akanga asingadi kuenda, uye akagara mumba mababa vake munyika yeKanani.

Asi ndizvo zvakanyorwa muna Jasher pamusoro peHutungamiri hwaMerkizedeki, kuti nenguva, ini, Yahweh Mukuru NDIRI, ndakazivisa kuna Jakobho kuti hutungamiri hwaMerkizedeki hwaifanira kupatsanurwa kuita mahofisi matatu kunyange maererano neMashoko akanyorwa muna Jasher kuti muzive chokwadi cheMashoko angu: Uye Rubheni akanga aine godo naamai vake Rea pamusoro paizvozvi, uye akazadzwa nehasha, uye akasimuka nehasha dzake akapinda mutende raBhira uye akabvisa mubhedha wababa vake ipapo. Panguva iyoyo mugove weudangwe, pamwe chete nehumambo nehupristi, wakabviswa kuvanakomana vaRubheni, nokuti akanga asvibisa mubhedha wababa vake, uye udangwe hwakapiwa kuna Josefa, humambo kuna Judha, uye hupristi kuna Revhi, nokuti Rubheni akanga asvibisa mubhedha wababa vake.

Uye ndizvo zvakanga zvakaita uye ndizvo zvakanyorwa kuti mabasa aiva muHutungamiri hwaMerkizedeki akaiswa parutivi achitsigira kutungamirirwa kweTestamende yaMosesi, pasi peTestamende yake yeKare yakapa mahofisi matatu akasiyana uye akajeka emabasa matsvene aya, saka Israeri yaiva nedzinza reMadzimambo, uye yaiva neHofisi yehupristi hwechiRevhi, uye yaiva nechikoro chevaporofita, kunyange chikoro chakatevedzera chikoro chevaporofita vekare, kubva pamazuva asati aitika mafashamo.
