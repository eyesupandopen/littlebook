Chapupu Chiviri: Israeri
Zvinovava Zvinotapira
Chikunguru 27, 2024

Rambai Mucherechedzo weChikara!

Inzwai izwi reuchenjeri, mugopfugama, muteerere izwi mhenyu raYeshua HaMashiach, nekuti ndini Shumba yedzinza raJudha, uye ndiri Mambo wemadzimambo naIshe wemadzishe! El Shaddai, iye Mwari Wemasimbaose, akandikudza zvikuru uye akandipa zita rinopfuura mazita ose. Kuti muzita raJesu mabvi ose apfugame, ezvinhu zviri kudenga, nezviri panyika, nezviri pasi penyika: Uye kuti ndimi dzose dzipupure, kuti Jesu Kristu ndiye Ishe, kuti Baba veZvivheneko, iye Yahweh, Mwari asingaperi uye wekusingaperi, vakudzwe, nekuti hakuna akafanana naBaba vedu!

Inzwai izwi raShe wenyu uye munzwisise zvinhu zvatotanga kuitika nezvinhu zvichakurumidza kuitika, nekuti tarirai ndini iye anotaura pamusoro pezvinhu zvichauya sekunge zvatotoitika, nekuti zvirokwazvo Ishe wenyu mukuru, kunyange ini Kristu Jesu mukuru pane vose, handiiti chinhu, kunze kwekunge ndazivisa zvakavanzika zvangu kuvaranda vangu Vaprofita. Munoterekererei manzwi mazhinji anopesana, uye munogumireiko pakati pemafungiro maviri? Handina here kutaura kuti vaprofita vangu vanotaura pamwe chete uye havambopikisi mashoko angu matsvene? Nekuti ndini Shoko rakaitwa nyama rinogara pakati penyu kubudikidza nemweya wangu mutsvene unoberekwazve mukati menyu nekusingaperi, nekuti mweya wese unoberekwazve unovharwa chisimbiso nemweya iwoyo mutsvene wechivimbiso, unova mbeu yangu isingaori uye chisimbiso chaMwari Mupenyu hachigoni kuputswa. Uye zuva rekununurwa kwezvakatengwa rava pedyo uye rudzikinuro rwenyu ruzere rwasvika.

Zvanzi naIshe weHondo, musanzwa mashoko evaprofita vanokuprofitirai, uye vanokudzidzisai zvisina maturo: vanotaura zviono zvemwoyo yavo, kwete zvinobva pamuromo waIshe. Uyai kwandiri vana vangu nekuti chizoro chamakagamuchira kubva kwandiri, chinogara mamuri: uye hamufaniri kuti munhu upi zvake akudzidzisei: Asi, sezvo chizoro ichocho chinokudzidzisai zvinhu zvose, uye chiri chechokwadi, uye chisiri chenhema: uye kunyange sezvachakakudzidzisai, muchagara mandiri. Vana vangu garai mandiri muenzanise mashoko emunhu upi zvake anoti anondimiririra nemagwaro uye mundibvunze kuti ndisimbise mumweya menyu, kana zvimwe muchizoteerera izwi rangu nekuti vazhinji venyu vane nzeve dzinorema kunzwa, uye vazhinji venyu vanoda mashoko enhema anonyengedza nzeve dzenyu kuti muite sevakachenjera asi panzvimbo pazvo munotevera mashoko enhema emapenzi anozvikudza.

Tarirai! Ndinotaura nezvenguva dzekutambudzika kwaJakobho dzagadzirira kupararira pasi rose. Nekuti kutonga kwatotanga uye hakugoni kumiswa, nekuti izvo zvinotaurwa naYahweh hapana anogona kuzvishandura! Misodzi mizhinji, mizhinji ndakachema pamusoro pekutongwa kweBhabhironi Rakavanzika uye ndakatumira vaprofita vangu kwemakore mazhinji, mazhinji zvino uye ndakatumira kunyange vatorwa vanobva kunyika dzisingazivikanwi kuti vapupure nekuporofita pamusoro pekutonga kwakanga kuchisvika asi kwava pano zvino.

Nekuti zvichadaro nekuti zvakanyorwa: Nyanga gumi dzawaona pachikara, idzi dzichavenga mhombwe, uye dzichaiita dongo, isina kupfeka, uye dzichadya nyama yayo, uye dzichaipisa nemoto. Nekuti Mwari akaisa mumwoyo yavo kuti vaite kuda kwake, uye kuti vabvumirane, uye vape umambo hwavo kuchikara, kusvikira mashoko aMwari azadziswa. Uye mukadzi, wawaona, ndiro guta guru rinotonga pamusoro pemadzimambo enyika.

Inzwai mashoko angu, kutongwa kweBhabhironi Rakavanzika kwatotanga. Nyanga gumi dzakagadzirira kurwisa kwadzo kwechimbichimbi uye kurwisa kwadzo kwava kusvika uye kwava pamusuwo wenyu. Nekuti kunyange venhau venyu vanotsvaga kukunyaradzai kuti murare uye vazhinji vanogara murima vasingaoni chokwadi vachiti: Ndinogara saMambokadzi uye handisi chirikadzi uye handingaoni kuchema. Mangwana kuchaita sanhasi, nekuti nhasi kwakaita semaoko. Zvikuru sekuzvikudza kwaanoita, uye achirarama mukufara, mupei zvakawanda kudaro kutambudzika neshungu: Naizvozvo matambudziko ake achauya nezuva rimwe, rufu, neshungu, nenzara, uye achapiswa nemoto: nekuti Yahweh anomupa mhosva, ndiye Mwari Wemasimbaose uye hakuna mukuru kupfuura iye uye hapana chinogona kumisa ruoko rune simba rwaEl Shaddai!

Uye saka zuva richakurumidza kubuda pazuva renjodzi yamusiri kutarisira, uye zuva iroro riri pedyo kupfuura zvamunofungidzira. Uye zuva iroro richaunza kuparadzwa kwakakwana paBhabhironi Rakavanzika, nekuti rine chikamu chepiri chekutongwa chakaziviswa pamusoro paro. Mhosva dzaro nehupombwe zvakawanda uye chirango charo hachinonoki uye kurangwa kukuru kuchawira pamusoro pevasina kutendeuka kunyange Kereke yangu yakatendeka uye kunyange Mwenga wangu akatendeka vachaunganidzwa kwandiri kamwe kamwe mumakore uye voponeswa kubva mukutsamwa, kunyange kutambudzika kukuru kwevhiki rechimakumi manomwe raDhanieri. Bhabhironi Guru Rawa, Rawa! Inzwai mashoko angu nekuti ndinotaura sekunge zvatotoitika nekuti hapana chichachinja, kunonotsa, kukanganisa, kana kumisa kutonga kwaYahweh kubva pakuwira pasi.

Muawa imwe chete richapiswa nemoto, mamwe maguta aro achapisa, mamwe maguta aro achafashamirwa zvine simba, shongwe dzaro dzichawa dzigoputsika kuita zvidimbu zvizhinji, mabhiriji aro achawa, uye kuzununguka kukuru kuchaitika. Nyika ichabvarurwa zvine simba uye nzvimbo zhinji dzichafukidzwa nemvura ine simba. Nguo dzaro dzichafukidzwa kubva kumabvazuva nekumadokero nemafungu makuru uye pakati pacho mafungu anomhanya nekuzununguka kune simba. Zvichasara zvichava dongo uye harichazomukizve, nekuti rudzi urwu rwenyika zhinji nemaguta mazhinji haruchazomukizve uye kuwa kwaro kuchava kukuru! Nekuti nyika ndeyaIshe nekuzara kwayo, uye zvinhu zvose zvesimba raro rakaipa zvichabviswa zvachose kubva kumarudzi panguva yevhiki rechimakumi manomwe raDhanieri, nekuti ndichachenesa nyika mukutsamwa kwangu. Ini, Kristu Jesu, Shumba yedzinza raJudha, ndataura mashoko aya, uye kugadzirira kuzadziswa kwawo kwatotanga uye kuchazadziswa kamwe kamwe muawa imwe.

Uye saka hondo ichapararira pasi rose sechikamu chekurwisa kwechimbichimbi paBhabhironi Rakavanzika, nekuti izvi zvakarongwa uye zvakabatanidzwa kwemajubhiri mazhinji zvino, nekuti chakavanzika chekusarurama chave chichishanda kubva pamazuva ekare uye chichasvika pakuzadziswa kwakakwana kana mudzivisi abviswa apo vakatendeka vangu vanotorwa kuenda kudenga. Hondo ichawira pasi rose uye ichauya pamusoro pevanhu vose nemarudzi ose, nekuti iyi ndiyo hondo yechitatu yakadaro, sezvo vakawa vanotsvaga kuparadza hurongwa hwekare hwenyika uye kutsiva zvinhu zvose nehurongwa hwavo hutsva, kunyange hurongwa hweShato. Chikara chinobva mugungwa nechikara chinobva panyika zvichamuka nehasha huru, uye zvicharwa nevatsvene, nekuti zvakapiwa kwazviri kuti zvikunde vatsvene. Zvichadaro kuti vose vasingadi upenyu hwavo uye vanodana zita raIshe kusvika kumagumo vachaponeswa mumufaro usingaperi kusvika kusingaperi kwekusingaperi. Uye ini Jesu weNazareta ndakasiya muenzaniso wekusingaperi wekuteerera kusvika parufu, nekuti kuteerera kwangu kuna Yahweh kwakanga kuri kunyange rufu rwemuchinjikwa pandakatambura ndikabhadhara zvivi zvevanhu segwayana raMwari uye tarirai ndiri kuuya seShumba yedzinza raJudha uye handizomiswi kana kukanganiswa!

Uye saka vaprofita vangu vaviri vari miti miviri yemiorivhi nezvigadziko zvemwenje zviviri zvinomira pamberi paMwari wenyika vachaprofita kwemwedzi makumi mana nemiviri vakapfeka masaga, nekuti vakatorwa kuenda kudenga munguva dzekare. Uye kana munhu upi zvake akavada kuvarwadzisa, moto unobuda mumuromo mavo, ugoparadza vavengi vavo: uye kana munhu upi zvake akavada kuvarwadzisa, anofanira kuurayiwa nenzira iyi. Ava vane simba rekuvhara denga, vachimisa mvura mumazuva ekuporofita kwavo: uye vane simba pamusoro pemvura kuti vaiite ropa, uye kurova nyika nematambudziko ose, kakawanda sezvavanoda.

Hwavo huchapupu husingaperi, uye vachazivisa vhangeri risingaperi, uye vachataura nezve zvirongwa zvaYahweh zvenguva dzose, uye vachandimiririra ini Kristu Jesu, uye zvakavanzika zvizhinji vachazivisa uye vachatendeutsa mwoyo yevazhinji kuna Baba veZvivheneko avo vasina kusanduka kana mumvuri wekushanduka. Vachapupura pamusoro pangu Yeshua HaMashiach nekuti ndini uyo Yahweh akatuma kuna Israeri kuti azadzise zvivimbiso zvakaitwa kumadzibaba avo uye ndini Mesia wechokwadi zvino nekusingaperi uye umambo hwangu hahuna magumo! Nekuti inguva dzipi dzakambovapo apo Yahweh akazvisiya asina chapupu? HAKUNA kumbova nenguva iyo Yahweh akazvisiya asina chapupu, uye vose vakagadzirirwa ruponeso rusingaperi vachauya kuzoponeswa mukusingaperi kusingaperi nemufaro usingaperi, nekuti kuzadziswa kwezvinhu zvose kwatotanga.

Uye saka chikara chinobva mugungwa chichamuka pamwe chete nechikara chinobva panyika, anovawo muporofita wenhema. Uye achashamisa muromo wake achitaura kunyomba kukuru kuna Yahweh kuti anyombe zita rake netabhenakeri yake nevanogara kudenga. Uye zvichapiwa kwaari kuti arwe nevatsvene uye avakunde uye simba richapiwa kwaari kuti atonge rudzi rwose, nerurimi, nenyika nekuti achatungamira nyika. Chikara chinobva panyika chichatongawo nyika seMoshiach wavo wenhema uye chichava muvaki wavo wetemberi. Chikara chinobva mugungwa chichatonga maererano nemitemo yavo inonyomba yeNoahide sezvavanotsvaga kubvisa chero chinhu chine chekuita naMwari wechokwadi nemwanakomana wake mumwe chete Jesu Kristu kubva pachiso chenyika.

Chikara chinobva panyika chichaita kuti nyika inamate chikara, chine ronda rinouraya rakapora. Uye chichaita zvishamiso zvikuru kunyengedza vanogara panyika, nekuti zvichabvumirwa kuti chiite zviratidzo zvenhema nezvishamiso zvizhinji, uye chichaita kuti moto ubve kudenga panyika pamberi pevanhu uye chichatungamirira vanhu vose kuti vashamiswe nazvo. Asi iye mhandu, uye anozvikudza pamusoro pezvose zvinonzi Mwari, kana zvinonamatwa: zvekuti anogara saMwari muTemberi yaMwari, achizviratidza kuti ndiye Mwari.

Uye achaita mufananidzo wechikara uye achaita kuti vose vanamate mufananidzo waachaita. Uye zvichabvumirwa kwaari kuti ape mweya kumufananidzo wechikara kuitira kuti mufananidzo wechikara utaure uye uchaita kuti vose vasingadi kunamata mufananidzo wechikara vaurayiwe. Uye achaita kuti vose, vapfumi nevarombo, vakasununguka nevaranda, vagamuchire mucherechedzo wake muruoko rwavo rwerudyi kana pahuma dzavo.

Uye saka ndinotaura zvakare kune vanogara panyika kuti vose vanzwe:

KANA MUNHU UPI ZVAKE AKANAMATA CHIKARA NOMUFANANIDZO WACHO, AKAGAMUCHIRA MUCHERECHEDZO WACHO PAHUMA YAKE, KANA MURUOKO RWAKE, IYEYO ACHAMWA WEWAINI YEKUTSAMWA KWAMWARI, INODURURWA ISINA KUSANGANISWA MUKOMBE YEKUTSAMWA KWAKE, UYE ACHATAMBUDZWA NEMOTO NESURUFURU, PAMBERI PENGELOSI TSVENE, UYE PAMBERI PEGWAYANA: UYE UTSI HWEKUTAMBUDZIKA KWAVO HUNOKWIRA NEKUSINGAPERI-PERI. UYE HAVANA ZORORO MASIKATI KANA USIKU, AVANO NAMATA CHIKARA NOMUFANANIDZO WACHO, UYE ANI NAANI ANOGAMUCHIRA MUCHERECHEDZO WEZITA RACHO.

Nyika iripo zvino haichazovipozve, uye umambo huri kusimuka hweDhiragoni huchaparadzwa zvachose, sezvo umambo hwaSatani hwosvika kumagumo ahwo, nekuti ivo vapambi uye havasi madzimambo. Uye iyi ndiyo nyika ichawanikwa nevakawanda, kunyange avo vachapona munjodzi dzinouya nehondo. Musatora chiratidzo, vanhu vangu, nekuti vachauya vachivimbisa rugare, vachauya vachivimbisa chengeteko, vachakuudzai kuti zvese ndezvekunaka kwenyu. Vachatanga zvirwere zvavo zvavanodaidza kuti zvirwere zvinopararira nekuti vakatotoronga kare kunyengetedza nyika kuti iri kurwara. Uye marudzi ese ezvirwere nematenda zvichawanda nekuti ndizvo zvinoitwa nevatasvi vemabhiza vari kuuya uye vachatasva!

Vachavimbisa hutano nekuporeswa kubva mukutonga kwaMwari, asi pachinzvimbo ichocho munhu anofanira kutendeuka kubva pakuipa kwake. Vachavimbisa kupinda munharaunda yavo kubva mukutonga kunotyisa kuri kuda kuitika panyika. Nekuti ini Kristu Jesu ndichakuudzai zvisati zvaitika kuti muzive kutendeseka kwemashoko angu uye kuti mugone kufamba mumvura dzine njodzi idzo vakawanda vachazviwana vari madziri, kunyange avo vane hushingi hwekudana zita rangu kusvika kumagumo, nekuti ivo ndivo vadyi venhaka yeruponeso rusingaperi. Nekuti vese vanodana pana Jesu weNazareta saIshe wavo, kunyange vese vanodana pazita rangu rine simba uye vasingadi hupenyu hwavo kusvika kumagumo, vachaponeswa muhupenyu husingaperi.

Hamuna kuverenga here? Ava ndivo vakabuda mukutambudzika kukuru, vakasuka nguo dzavo, vakadziita chena muropa reGwayana. Naizvozvo, vari pamberi pechigaro cheushe chaMwari, uye vanomushumira masikati neusiku muTemberi yake: uye iye agere paChigaro cheUshe achagara pakati pavo. Havachazovi nenzara zvekare, kana nyota zvekare, uye zuva harichavapisi, kana kupisa kupi zvako. Nekuti Gwayana, riri pakati pechigaro cheushe, richavafudza, uye richavatungamirira kuzvitubu zvipenyu zvemvura: uye Mwari achapukuta misodzi yese pameso avo.

Zvisinei, kunyange hazvo boka guru revanhu richaponeswa kubva muvhiki rechimakumi manomwe raDhanieri uye ruponeso ruchasvika kuna Israeri nekuti kuchine vakasara vachandikotamira ini Yeshua HaMashiach, (Nekuti ndini Mesia wechokwadi uyo Gwayana raMwari richapa nzira kuShumba yeRudzi rwaJudha, uye zvose zvichazadziswa muhumwe husingaperi mandiri, nekuti mandiri Kristu Jesu Shumba ichavata murugare negwayana.) zvinosuwisa, uye zvisina basa, nyika zhinji ichashamisika yotevera vatongi ava vakaita sechikara, nekuti vachanyengedza vakawanda. Neuroyi hwavo nemashiripiti avo, vachanyengedza nyika kuti igamuchire chepfu yavo, uye vachaisa chepfu zvachose kune avo vanoda nyika neuroyi hwavo, nekuti chiratidzo chechikara chichavanzwa semushonga. Kunyange kurumwa nenyoka uko hurongwa hwavo hwakatotangwa hukaedzwa, uye ruzhinji rwevanhu rwakatodzidziswa kugamuchira chiratidzo chavo uye chirongwa chavo chehondo chakabudirira.

Saka hurongwa hwavo hwagadzirira kushandiswa, uye vakagadzirira zvikoro zvavo zvemagariro uye vese vachadzorwa vanogara mumaguta avo nemunharaunda yavo. Nekuti vachafanira kutora chiratidzo chechikara kuti vatore chikamu munharaunda yavo yadhiabhorosi, kunyange umambo hweDhiragoni. Avo vanotora chiratidzo vachashandurwa zvachose. Majini avo chaiwo achashandurwa, nekuti vachazvisanganisa nembeu yevanhu: asi havachanamatirani, sezvo simbi isingasanganiswi nevhu. Chiratidzo chavo chechikara, kunyange kurumwa nenyoka, chichaparadza zvachose vese vanotora chepfu yavo uye hapana kutiza.

Kunyange vese avo vanotora chiratidzo vachashandurwa zvachose, uye vachazviwana vari chikamu chehurongwa hwavo, uye vachabatanidzwa nematekinoroji uye pfungwa dzavo hadzichazovi dzavo. Vachadzorwa uye vachaita sevakafa vanofamba, vachava varanda zvachose kupfungwa dzakabatana dzehurongwa hwavo, kunyange matekinoroji avo nekuti vachabatanidzwa neinternet yezvinhu zvichauya. Vacharasikirwa nekuda kwavo, uye havachazovi nekunzwa uye chero chinhu chaMwari chichabviswa zvachose mavari nekuti vachazvishandura kuva mumamiriro asingadzikinurwi, uye vachazvibatanidza zvizere uye zvachose neavo vakawa uye vachava veDhiragoni zvachose uye vachaenda kugungwa guru remoto nekusingaperi. Uye ndizvo zvichava mugumo wesarudzo yavo.

Nekuti kuguma kwesarudzo yavo hakuchinji. Uye matambudziko avo nemaronda nezvirwere zvichawanda, uye kunyange vachavimbiswa hutano nerugare, hutano nerugare havazombovi nazvo. Nekuti Satani anoedza kushandisa uroyi hwake kurwisa hasha nekutonga kwaMwari uye kuisa chepfu nekuuraya ruzhinji, asi havazombowana zororo uye avo vanotora chikamu muuroyi hwake havazombopukunyuki. Avo vanotora chiratidzo vachavheneka pasi pemwenje yavo yerima, uye mwenje yavo ichamira mumigwagwa yemaguta avo avakagadzirira varanda vavo. Nzwisisai kuti ava vachange vari vakafa vakapihwa mhosva vasina kuregererwa uye vasingazombopori, nekuti ava ndivo vane mazita asina kunyorwa muBhuku reHupenyu reGwayana, rakabayiwa kubva pakuvambwa kwenyika. Uye hapana ruponeso ruchazombowanikwa pakati pavo kana munharaunda yavo, nekuti kufamba pakati pavo nekubatana nenharaunda yavo kuchada zvachose chiratidzo chechikara.

Zvapupu zvangu zviviri zvichapupura kusvikira nguva dzekupupura kwavo dzapera kwemwedzi makumi mana nemiviri. Uye zvichapihwa kuchikara chinokwira kubva mugomba risina magumo kuti chirwe navo, uye chichavakunda, nekuvauraya, kunyange miti yangu miviri yemiorivhi, nezvigadziko zvemwenje zviviri kana vapedza kupupura kwavo. Uye zvitunha zvavo zvichavata mumugwagwa weguta guru, iro pamweya rinonzi Sodhoma neIjipita, uko Ishe wenyu akarovererwawo. Uye vakaipa vachapembera zvikuru uye vachapana zvipo nekuda kwekufa kwavo. Uye mushure memazuva matatu nehafu Mweya wehupenyu unobva kuna Mwari uchapinda mavari: uye vachamira netsoka dzavo, uye kutya kukuru kuchawira vese vakavaona. Uye vakanzwa izwi guru richibva kudenga, richiti kwavari, kwirai pano. Uye vakakwira kudenga mugore, uye vavengi vavo vachavaona vachikwira kudenga muhupenyu husingaperi uye vachashandurwa zvachose. Uye Mutumwa wechinomwe acharira, uye kuchava nemanzwi makuru kudenga, achiti, Umambo hwenyika ino hwava umambo hwaIshe wedu, nehwaKristu wake, uye achatonga nekusingaperi-peri.

(Vakaropafadzwa vakafa vari muna Ishe, kubva zvino, hongu, anodaro Mweya, kuti vazorore pamabasa avo, uye mabasa avo anovatevera.)

Uye saka kutambudzika kukuru kwazvo kuchatanga, kunyange hazvo kutambudzika kuripo nguva dzose mukati mevhiki rechimakumi manomwe raDhanieri. Uye temberi yavo ichapera uye nyangadzi yekuparadza ichaitwa neMoshiach wavo wenhema. Uye achasimbisa sungano nevakawanda kwevhiki rimwe: uye pakati pevhiki achagumisa chibayiro nechipiriso, uye nekuda kwekupararira kwenyangadzi, achaiparadza, kusvikira kuparadzwa kwakatarwa kwadururwa pamusoro peyakaparadzwa.

Idzi dzichava nguva dzerima guru vana vangu, nekuti ini Yeshua HaMashiach ndinokuudzai zvinhu izvi zvisati zvaitika, uye zvakanyorwa seyambiro kune vese. Uye saka 144,000 vangu vachauya kuzobatsira avo vangadai vakaparara uye vanodana pazita rangu. Ndichavatungamirira nhanho nenhanho, uye vachandiitira mabasa mazhinji ane simba. Nekuti ava ndivo vanotorwa mukuunganidzwa kwevatsvene kwandiri, Kristu Jesu, mumhepo pakurira kwehwamanda yekupedzisira uye hwamanda yave kuda kurira.

Ava ndivo vakatendeka vangu, kunyange avo mavari musina kuwanikwa kunyengera, nekuti vakazvichenesa semhandara yakachena muropa reGwayana randakapa nekusingaperi semupristi wavo mukuru, nekuti ndini mupristi mukuru nekusingaperi maererano neurongwa hwaMerkizedeki. Ava ndivo mwenga wangu, uye vachanditevera kwese kwandinoenda. Tarirai! Ndichavaita vane simba, uye hapana chakamboonekwa panyika chinogona kuenzaniswa nehunyanzvi hwandichavafukidza nahwo. Vachapfekedzwa mucheka wakaisvonaka, muchena, usina ruvara uye hunyanzvi hwavo hucharatidza kubudirira kwavo uye vachava nehunyanzvi hukuru nekuti vakatendeka vakadanwa uye vakasarudzwa kunyange kubva pakuvambwa kwenyika naYahweh nekuda kwezvinangwa izvi.

144,000 vangu vachaburukira paGomo reZioni uye ndichavatungamirira. Vachatora avo vandichavatungamirira kurenje kuti vavanze kubva murima guru nekutambudzwa kweumambo hwevavengi. Vachatungamirirwa kunzvimbo yakachengeteka kubva kumatambudziko manomwe nekuti mukutonga uku ndimo mune kuomarara kwehasha dzaMwari dzichadururwa pachigaro chechikara uye kutambudza umambo hweDhiragoni nekuti kurangwa kwavo kukuru. Uye 144,000 vachavatungamirira kunzvimbo yakachengeteka, vachavaporesa kubva pamaronda avo, vachavabatsira uye vachavapa tariro huru yekudana pazita raIshe kunyange kusvika kumagumo kana zvichidikanwa. Nekuti dai ndisina kupfupisa mazuva, hapana nyama yaizosara iri mhenyu. Uye saka vachapedzisa izvo zvandichavatungamirira mazviri.

Uye kana nguva dzapera zvichadaro, sezvazvakanyorwa uye sezvazvakanyorwa ngazviitwe: Uye ndakaona denga rakazaruka, uye tarira bhiza jena, uye iye akanga akarigara ainzi Akatendeka neWechokwadi, uye mukururama anotonga nekuita hondo. Meso ake akanga akaita semurazvo wemoto, uye pamusoro wake paiva nekorona zhinji, uye aiva nezita rakanyorwa, risingazivikanwi nemunhu kunze kwake. Uye akanga akafuka nguo yakanyikwa muropa, uye zita rake rinonzi, Shoko raMwari. Uye mauto aiva kudenga akamutevera pamabhiza machena, akafuka mucheka wakaisvonaka, muchena uye wakachena. Uye mumuromo make munobuda munondo unopinza, kuti nawo arove marudzi: uye achavatonga netsvimbo yesimbi: uye anotsika chisviniro chewaini chehasha nekuranga kwaMwari Wemasimbaose. Uye ane zita rakanyorwa panguo yake, nepachidya chake, Mambo WeMadzimambo, NaIshe WeMadzishe.

Uye saka ini, kunyange Jesu weNazareta, kunyange Gwayana raMwari, kunyange Kristu Jesu uyo ariwo Shumba yeRudzi rwaJudha ndichauya kuzotonga nekuita hondo paDhiragoni uye umambo hwake hwese huchapwanywa zvachose kuti husazombosimukazve. Uye chikara nemuprofita wenhema vachakandwa vari vapenyu mugungwa remoto uye Satani achasungwa nengetani huru mugomba risina magumo kwemakore churu uye saka kutonga kwangu kwemireniyamu kuchatanga uye mukururama ndimo muchava nekutonga kwangu. Tarirai! Ndinouya nemwenga wangu neboka ramadzimambo nevapristi vandichatonga navo nyika mukururama uye ndichatonga marudzi netsvimbo yesimbi. Saka ivai netariro huru vana vangu nekuti kuzadziswa kwezvinhu zvese kwava pedyo uye nguva ino yemakore manomwe yekutambudzika kukuru, kunyange kutambudzika kwaJakobho kuchateverwa nekutonga kwangu kwakarurama uye Yahweh achava zvese mune zvese.

Uye ndakaona denga idzva, nenyika itsva: nekuti denga rekutanga, nenyika yekutanga zvakanga zvapfuura, uye gungwa rakanga risisipo. Uye ini Johani ndakaona Guta dzvene, Jerusarema idzva richiburuka kubva kuna Mwari richibva kudenga, rakagadzirirwa semwenga akashongedzerwa murume wake. Uye ndakanzwa izwi guru richibva kudenga, richiti, Tarirai, Tabhenakeri yaMwari iri nevanhu, uye achagara navo, uye vachava vanhu vake, uye Mwari pachake achava navo, uye ave Mwari wavo.

Ini Yeshua HaMashiach ndatumira Mashoko aya nemuranda wangu, uye nemuranda wangu Mashoko aya atumwa!

Ndini mupristi mukuru nekusingaperi maererano neurongwa hwaMerkizedeki.
