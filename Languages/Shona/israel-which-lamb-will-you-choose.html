Chapupu Chiviri: Israeri
Zvinovava Zvinotapira
13 Mbudzi, 2024

Haiwa Israeri! Nderipi Gwayana ramuchasarudza?

Inzwai Mashoko aYahwe Mukuru NDIRI, uye mhanyirai kunzwi reMashoko angu kuti mugamuchire uchenjeri, uye kuti munhu akachenjera pakati penyu awedzere kuchenjera.

Tarirai! Ndinouya kuzokuudzai zvinhu zvichauya kuti muzive chokwadi cheMashoko angu, uye kuti musvike paruoko rwenyu ruzere muna Yeshua HaMashiach, uyo zvirokwazvo ari Mesia wenyu wechokwadi, Mambo, Ishe, uye Mupristi Mukuru nokusingaperi maererano nourongwa hwaMerkizedeki.

Naizvozvo, ndinokurairai kuti mumuteerere nemunhu wenyu wose, uye kuti mudzingise mwoyo yenyu mukuteerera! Nokuti, ndiYeshua HaMashiach, iye Jesu Kristu mururimi rweChirungu, akapiwa kwete chete kuruoko rwaIsraeri, asi kunyange kuruoko rwedzinza rose raAdhamu, nokuti vose vanodana zita raShe kusvikira kumagumo vachaponeswa, vangava muIsraeri kana vemamwe marudzi, uye Yeshua HaMashiach achaponesa mupenyu musaririra waIsraeri kunyange kubva kurudzi rwaAdhamu uyo uchaenderera mberi muumambo hwemakore ane chiuru.

Uye ndizvo zvazviri, kuti ndakagovera izvo zvakanga zvisingabviri kuti dzinza raAdhamu rigovere ruponeso rwaro, nokuti dzinza raAdhamu raigona chete kuwana rudzikinuro ruzere muropa regwayana. Nokuti ndini, Yahwe, Mukuru NDIRI, ndakakupai Pasika yenyu yakakwana kamwe chete, zvino, uye nokusingaperi, uye chibayiro chake chinogamuchirwa nguva dzose, naizvozvo, hapachina kudikanwa kwechibayiro, haiwa Israeri.

Sezvazvakanyorwa muna VaHebheru, saizvozvowo ndinotaura kwamuri zvakare zvino: Nokuti kana ropa renzombe, nerembudzi, nemadota etsiru zvikasaswa pane vasina kuchena, zvichivatsvenesa pakucheneswa kwenyama: Ko ropa raKristu, uyo kubudikidza noMweya usingaperi, akazvipa asina gwapa kuna Mwari, haringatonyanyi kuchenesa hana dzenyu pamabasa akafa, kuti mushumire Mwari mupenyu here? Uye saka ndinokupai zano, haiwa Israeri, gamuchirai chibayiro changu nokuti hapachina chimwe chibayiro chezvivi!

Saka nzwisisai Israeri, kuti hazvichagoneki kuti ani naani aswedere kuna Mwari Asingaperi weKururama kubudikidza naMozisi, nokuti, ndakaita Yeshua HaMashiach nzira, chokwadi, noupenyu, zvekuti hakuna munhu anouya kuna Yahwe kunze kwaYeshua HaMashiach!

Uye ndakaraira kuteerera kwenyu kunzwi rake, kunyange kubudikidza neTorah apo izwi chairo raMozisi rinochema kusvikira nhasi sechapupu pamusoro penyu uye munzira dzenyu dzokusadzidza nedzokusatenda, panzvimbo pokuteerera imi munotsvaka nokusaziva kuvakazve temberi yakaitwa nomunhu, sezvamunotsauswa naEsau! Nokuti makaramba temberi yakavakwa nomusiki wenyu, iyo temberi isina kuitwa namaoko, iyo temberi chaiyo yakaitwa naYahwe pachake nokuda kwaIsraeri waMwari, iyo Jesu Kristu ari dombo guru repakona. Naizvozvo, regai mabasa enyu mugozorora mumabasa aMesia wenyu wechokwadi uye ndichakupai Sabata.

Saka ndinokurairai haiwa Israeri, uye ndini ndakapa Mashoko chaiwo akataurwa naMozisi kumadzibaba enyu paSinai, Mashoko andinokurairai kuti muteerere nguva dzose uye nokusingaperi kunyange mukusateerera kwenyu kwokushinga nokwemwoyo mikukutu. Uye saka Mozisi akati kuti vose vateerere: Ishe Mwari wenyu achakumutsirai Muporofita akafanana neni, kubva pakati penyu, kunyange wehama dzenyu: iye muchamuteerera!

Nepo Mozisi aiva murevereri weTestamente yokutanga (yakanyorwa nomunwe wangu paSinai) yakapiwa kuti iparire mhosva yechivi munyama, Testamente iyo zvino inopenya pachiso chaJesu Kristu, iye Yeshua HaMashiach uyo ari Ishe wenyu wechokwadi naMesia! Asi Mutemo waMozisi mumvuri wezvinhu zvichauya, zvinhu zvakazadziswa muna Kristu Jesu, uye ndakamuita mweya unopa upenyu uye maererano nenyama Jesu weNazareta haachazivikanwi!

Uye sezvazvakaitwa noruoko rwangu rune simba, iwo munwe chaiwo waYHVH mukuru NDIRI, ndakanyora mitemo yangu pamahwendefa emabwe paSinai pandakapa Mozisi mitemo yangu kuti Israeri agorarama pamberi pangu. Asi, izvo zvakapiwa kuna Israeri paSinai kwaiva kupiwa mhosva kwechivi munyama kuti Israeri ave nomudzidzisi anovapinza kuna Mesia wavo wechokwadi, iye Yeshua HaMashiach wandakatumira kuruoko rwenyu ruzere.

Uye ndizvo zvazviri, mutemo waMozisi wakanaka, uye wakarurama, uye wakakwana, uye mutsvene, uye kana munhu upi noupi akatuka mutemo waMozisi ngaatongwe nawo mukuomarara kuzere kwakarongwa pasi pekutukwa nechirango chomutemo waMozisi. Asi izvo zvakanaka uye zvakarurama uye zvakakwana uye zvitsvene zvakaunza rufu kune vose vakapiwa mhosva mazviri nechivi munyama, nokuti pasina mutemo chivi hachiverengwi, naizvozvo mutemo waMozisi wakapiwa kuti uratidze uye uparire mhosva yechivi munyama.

Asi nomweya womutemo kwete norugwaro ndipo panowanikwa ngoni norudo rwangu kuvanhu, nokuti rugwaro rwomutemo runounza kuomarara (Hamuna kuverenga here kuti ndinoda ngoni kupfuura chibayiro?) uye saka murudo rwangu kuvana vaIsraeri vandinoda kuponesa kana mukateerera Yeshua HaMashiach, ndakaronga chibayiro nokuti pasina kuteurwa kweropa hakuna kuregererwa kwezvivi. Uye saka noruoko rwakachenjerera rwaMozisi vanhu vangu vakachengetwa kusvikira kuna Kristu, kunyange zvazvo vazhinji venyu makatendeukira kuna vamwari venhema uye muchida mabhokisi enyu matema, zvinhu zvinonyangadza kwazvo pamberi pangu, haiwa Israeri, naizvozvo ibvai pakarepo!

Saka nzwisisai kutsamwa kwangu pamusoro penyu haiwa Israeri, nokuti NDIRI YHVH, mukuru NDIRI, iye Mwari Wemasimbaose Asingaperi weKururama asingaziyi uye hapana chinhu chakaoma kwazvo kuti ruoko rwangu rune simba ruchiite. Uye ndini, El Shaddai, Mwari Wemasimbaose, ndakaronga chisikwa muuchenjeri hwangu husingaperi uye nesimba guru romweya wangu zvinhu zvose zvakabatanidzwa mukuteerera inzwi rangu.

NDIRI Elohim wenyu, uyo ane zviuru gumi zvakapetwa nezviuru gumi zvinopoteredza chigaro changu choumambo, iyo hondo huru yengirozi yevashumiri, vashumiri vacho vari murazvo womoto vanokunda muuchenjeri uye vakasikwa nounyanzvi uye zvakakwana sezvandakavasika murudo rwangu kwavari! Uye murudo rwavo kwandiri, vanoteerera zvakakwana kuda kwangu kusingaperi uye ndakapa Yeshua HaMashiach kutonga pamusoro pavo vose.

Asi, ini YHVH Mukuru NDIRI, iye akakomberedzwa nehondo isingaverengeki yengirozi, HANDIDI MUPI WEZANO muuchenjeri hwangu husingaperi uye ndini ndinotonga chisikwa changu uye ndinoita ZVINHU ZVOSE maererano nezano rekuda kwangu, uye pavachenjeri vose venguva dzose hakuna akachenjera kupfuura mukuru NDIRI! Saka inzwai izwi rangu munyaradze ndimi dzenyu kana mukasarudza upenyu husingaperi panzvimbo pemirazvo isingadzimiki yechirango haiwa Israeri nokuti ndiyo sarudzo inofanira kuitwa nomunhu wose.

Uye muuchenjeri hwangu husingaperi ndakatumira Jesu weNazareta kuna Israeri, kunyange Judhea payaitongwa naPondiyo Pirato, kunyange vapristi vakuru neSanihedrini pavakamupomera zvisina kururama uye namaoko akaipa gwayana raMwari rakaurayiwa ndokurovererwa pamuchinjikwa kuitira kuti muviri wechivi unosunga Adhamu uparadzwe mune vose vanorarama nokutenda vachiteerera, nokuti vakarurama vachararama nokutenda! Uye NDIRI iye, kunyange El Shaddai (Mwari Wemasimbaose) akaisa zvinhu zvose kudenga nepanyika mumaoko ane simba aKristu Jesu, iye Yeshua HaMashiach uye ndiye Shumba yeRudzi rwaJudha wandiri kutumira kuti atonge uye arwe uye nokuda kworuoko rwevakatendeka uye vanoteerera. Naizvozvo, inzwai Mashoko angu mukuteerera Mesia wenyu wechokwadi, nokuti hakuna mumwe!

Saka nzwisisai kutsamwa kwangu pamusoro penyu haiwa Israeri, kuti muupofu hwenyu hune divi hwamakagara nhaka kubva kumadzibaba enyu ane mwoyo mikukutu, ane izwi reTalmud ramunoremekedza kupfuura Elohim wenyu. Hongu, munokoshesa tsika dzevaya vamunoti vachenjeri nevanhu vakachenjera kupfuura Mashoko aMukuru NDIRI, uyo ari musiki asingaperi uye anogara nokusingaperi wezvose zvakasikwa! Nokuti naYHVH zvinhu zvose zvakasikwa, zviri kudenga, nezviri panyika, zvinhu zvinoonekwa nezvisingaonekwi: zvingava zvigaro zvoumambo kana ushe, kana ukuru, kana masimba, zvinhu zvose zvakasikwa neni, uye nokuda kwangu, Uye NDIRI pamberi pezvinhu zvose, uye muna YHVH zvinhu zvose zvinogara.

Asi, munofunga kuti muri mupi wangu wezano uye nokupusa munozviti vanhu vangu vakasarudzwa muchiramba Mashoko angu mukusateerera kusina kutenda? Haiwa Israeri makatsaukira kuzvidhori zvisingatauri zvemwoyo nemashoko enhema aEsau ane zano ramunofarira kupfuura Mwari Asingaperi weKururama, iye Elohim wenyu! Uye munogumburwa muupofu hune divi nokuti muna Mozisi mwoyo yenyu yakafukidzwa. Zvisinei, kana mwoyo yenyu ichatendeukira kuna Mesia wenyu wechokwadi, chifukidzo chichabviswa uye panzvimbo poupofu hune divi muchaona!

Ndimi aniko, haiwa Israeri, zvamungazvigadza mupi wezano waYahwe? Zvisinei panzvimbo peMashoko aYahwe akapiwa pachiso chaYeshua HaMashiach, makatenda nhema dzakapfuudzwa mutsika dzevakuru venyu vaiva muuranda kuna Esau, uyo nemanyoro ake makazvisunga kuna Esau nenzira imwe cheteyo uye kuna Esau makasungwa nengetani! Uye ndakanyora zvakajeka kuti Mozisi ape madzibaba enyu paSinai: Chinhu chipi nechipi chandinokurairai, chenjerai kuchita: hamufaniri kuwedzera pachiri, kana kutapudza pachiri. Uye zvakare ndakazvinyorwa muuchenjeri hwaSoromoni, uchenjeri hunogara nokusingaperi: Shoko rose raElohim rakachena: iye inhovo kune avo, vanovimba naye. Usaise chinhu pamashoko ake, arege kukutsiura, ukawanikwa uri murevi wenhema.

Haiwa Israeri, dai maizokotamisa mwoyo yenyu mukuteerera Mashoko akapiwa kuruoko rwenyu nomufaro usingaperi, dai maizoteerera zvakare Mashoko omusiki wenyu! Uye NDIRI iye aizokudzorera uye akupinze pakati peboka risingaperi riri pasi poruoko rune simba rwoMufudzi wangu Mukuru wandakapa zvinhu zvose! Iye Yeshua HaMashiach, anofudza Israeri wechokwadi waMwari zvakakwana murudo rwake rwekuteerera izwi rangu, iwo muenzaniso wamunozvidza. Nokuti Yeshua HaMashiach ndiye murume chete akamboteerera zvakakwana kuda kwaYHVH, mukuru NDIRI, wandinogara ndichifadzwa naye, naizvozvo ndakamuita mweya unopa upenyu saka munzwirei iye wandakatumira nokuda kwenyu nokuti hakuna mumwe.

Uye makaomesa mwoyo yenyu seDombo reNheyo rakabatwa neSinagogi raSatani nokuti makaramba mashoko ezano rangu akapiwa pachiso chaKristu Jesu, uyo wamunoramba nokusaziva, nokudaro muchiramba tariro yenyu yoga yokudzoreredzwa noruoko nokusingaperi uye nokusingagumi. Uye saka munomira muri mapofu uye musina kupfeka uye muchinyara muchivi chenyu, muchifunga nokusaziva kuti mava mupi wezano kuna YHVH, Mukuru NDIRI!

Saka ndinokupai kuti munzwisise muupofu hwenyu hune divi kuti makagumburwa nedombo rangu guru repakona rakarambwa navavaki venyu, iro dombo rangu rinogumbusa. Nokuti Yeshua HaMashiach ndiro dombo guru repakona, iro dombo rechokwadi renheyo, iye Jesu weNazareta wamunoramba mukusateerera kwokushinga kunyange ndakakusarudzirai kuti muve vanhu vangu chaivo uye mwoyo wangu unorwadza.

Inzwai, inzwai, haiwa Israeri, uye munzwisise kuti NDIRI iye anonyora mutemo wangu usingaperi, iwo muratidziro wekuda kwangu kusingaperi muchirongwa changu chisingaperi chorudzikinuro chandakaita maererano nezano rekuda kwangu kusingaperi uye kwakakwana pamahwendefa emabwe nokuda kwekuteerera kwaIsraeri paSinai! Naizvozvo, NDIRI iye achanyora mutemo wangu pamahwendefa enyama emwoyo yenyu nokuti murudo rwangu kwamuri, simba rangu duku duku rinokwanisa kunyora mutemo wangu pamahwendefa enyu emabwe uye nenyasha dzangu nengoni zvinoratidzwa pachiso chaYeshua HaMashiach ndichapwanya mahwendefa enyu emabwe kuita ivhu romuumbi kuti mugone kusikwa patsva nokusingaperi mukururama noutsvene hwechokwadi sezvandakapika muzvibvumirano zvangu kumadzibaba enyu kuti ndichaita.

Naizvozvo, haiwa Israeri, ndinokurairai kuti muteerere izwi raYeshua HaMashiach nokuti hakuna ruoko mune rimwe zita rakapihwa kunze kwaJesu Kristu Akarurama, uyo wamunoti nokusaziva chidhori chevahedheni nokuti muri muuranda kuna Esau!

Asi ndizvo zvazvakanyorwa uye ndizvo zvazvaitika: Kubva kuna Abrahama kusvikira kuna Isaka, pakazvarwa Jakobho naEsau naye, ruoko rwaJakobho rwakatanga kubata chitsitsinho chaEsau. Nokuti Esau ndiko kuguma kwenyika, uye Jakobho ndiko kutanga kwayo kunotevera. Ruoko rwomunhu ruri pakati pechitsitsinho noruoko. Asi ndizvo zvazviri kuti zvino ndichafamba kunoparadzanisa Esau naJakobho nokuti Esau iSinagogi raSatani avo vari masora akaipa akazadza nyika yechipikirwa zvakare, uye Israeri yazara zvakare navaKanani.

Uye sezvazvakanyorwa, saizvozvo zvichaitwa, anodaro Ini Mukuru Ndiri: Nokuti tarirai, zuva rinouya, richapisa sechoto, uye vose vanozvikudza, hongu, navose vanoita zvakaipa vachava samashanga; uye zuva rinouya richavapisa, anodaro Ishe wehondo, kuti haringavasiyiri mudzi kana davi. Asi kwamuri imi munotya Zita rangu, Mwanakomana weKururama achabuda nokuporesa mumapapiro ake, uye muchaenda mberi muchikura semhuru dzechidyiro.

Uye sezvo Jesu Mesiya achiunganidza vakatendeka mukereke, iro muviri wake, nomwenga wake, zvino ndichafambisa Jesu Mesiya kuti aite kupatsanurwa kwekupedzisira pakati paEsau naJakobho, uye Jakobho achaponeswa kunyange kuburikidza nemiedzo yevhiki rechimakumi manomwe raDhanieri. Naizvozvo, ngakurege kuva nemhombwe, kana munhu asina kuchena saEsau, uyo akatengesa udangwe hwake nokuda kwechidimbu chimwe chenyama. Nokuti munoziva kuti shure kwaizvozvo, paakada kugamuchira nhaka yechikomborero, akarambwa; nokuti haana kuwana nzvimbo yokutendeuka, kunyange akatsvaka chikomborero ichocho nemisodzi.

Uye saka vanhu vari pakati peruoko rwaJakobho nechitsitsinho chaEsau, kunze kwekuti Esau anotsvaka kupa Jakobho ronda rekupedzisira mumusoro, iro randisingazobvumiri. Uye saka zviri, kuti vagari vose venyika vakasiiwa mudambudziko raJakobho kuti vatsungirire moto womunatsi vachava kana vaJakobho kuburikidza naJesu weNazareta, avo vari vana venhaka, kana kuti vacharambwa pakati paEsau uyo akatengesa udangwe hwake nokuda kwechimedu chechingwa. Naizvozvo, munhu wenyama achadana kana kuzita raJesu weNazareta kusvika kumagumo ake kuti agomutswa muIsiraeri yaMwari kana kuti saizvozvowo achatengesa ramangwana rake nenhaka yake isingaperi nokuda kwechimedu chechingwa chinopiwa noruoko rwaBharabhasi, nokuti Bharabhasi achatungamirira Sinagogi raSatani uye ndiye gwayana reshato.

Asi saizvozvowo musungo uchateyiwa munyika kuti ubate Jakobho zvachose uye Bharabhasi anounza kunyengera kukuru nokutonga kwake uye zvakaipa zvizhinji zvaachaita panyika napagungwa. Asi, zvichava saizvozvo kuti apo moto wehondo unopfuta nokupisa, Bharabhasi achasimuka achitozvisimbisa kuti akamutswa kuti azadzise zvinangwa zvaMwari uye zvinhu zvizhinji zvinomhura nezvakaipa zvaachataura. Uye achauya nedavi rake reOrivhi uye achazviti iye ndiNoa, ari mupenyu kuti aponese Nyika kubva pakutongwa kwakaitirwa vanhu vakaipa.

Nokuti chikara chinobva panyika uyo ariwo muporofita wavo wenhema achauya nemhinduro yake nokuti iye igwayana rine nyanga mbiri rinotaura seshato. Uye kutonga kwake kuchava kwechimiro chiviri sezvo achatsvaka kubatanidza vanhu vose pasi pokutonga kweshato, anova dhiabhorosi iye wekare. Nokuti Bharabhasi haauyi kuzotonga ari oga kunyange achizviti iye ndiMwari asi anounza sungano yekupatsanura apo nyanga mbiri dzichatonga gungwa nenyika, uye pane mbiri idzi imwe inyanga diki yaDhanieri. Asi ndakapikira kuti Isiraeri haichazopatsanurwi uye panyika dzake mbiri hapazombovi nemhinduro asi Jesu Mesiya achabatanidza Isiraeri zvizere uye nokusingaperi-peri.

(Asi saizvozvo zvichaitika vadikani vangu saka nzwisisai zvandichazopedzisa naJesu Mesiya: Ndichatora vana vaIsiraeri kubva pakati pavahedheni, kwavakaenda, uye ndichavaunganidza kumativi ose, ndigovaunza munyika yavo. Uye ndichavaita vanhu vamwe munyika, pamusoro pemakomo aIsiraeri, uye mambo mumwe achava mambo kwavari vose; uye havachazovi vanhu vaviri, kana kupatsanurwa zvakare kubvira zvino kuva umambo huviri. Uye havachazosvibiswi zvakare nezvifananidzo zvavo, kana nezvinyangadzo zvavo, kana nokudarika kwavo kupi nokupi; asi ndichavaponesa kubva panzvimbo dzavo dzose dzokugara, mavakatadza, uye ndichavanatsa; saizvozvo vachava vanhu vangu, uye ini ndichava Mwari wavo.)

Imwe ndiyo chikara chinobva mugungwa icho chakanyorwa pamusoro pacho kuti: Nokuti haadi vakadzi, kana kuremekedza mwari upi noupi; nokuti achazvikudza kupfuura vose. Ndinokubvunzai zvino muone kana murume anorwadziwa nokusununguka mwana? Asi mukadzi wake anoita somukadzi ane vana, kunyange vasiri vana vake. Uye chikara chinobva mugungwa chichasimuka chakabatana zvizere muchinangwa nechikara chinobva panyika uye vanonyepedzera kuva vavengi vanoita sevakapatsanurana pamafungiro nechitendero.

Asi vanoreva nhema uye vanonyepedzera uye baba vavo ishato imwe cheteyo yakagadzira zvikara zviviri izvi zvakaipa nokuda kwezvinangwa zvayo zvakaipa, kunyange kuparadza Adhamu kuti agobira nyika nokusingaperi ive yake uye hazvibviri kuti apedzise zvirongwa zvake zvakaipa nokuti nyika ndeyaIshe nokuzara kwayo, anodaro YAHWE, Ini Mukuru Ndiri!

Nokuti Bharabhasi anounza nyika mbiri dzichabatanidzwa pasi peshato uye rugare rwenhema ruchavapo sezvavanovimbisa upenyu husingaperi noutano norugare kana mukangotora chiratidzo chavo mogobatana navo sezvavanosanganisa simbi nevhu asi hazvinganamatirani. Nzwisisai, imi mose mungafunga kuzvibatanidza nokusingaperi neavo vakawa kuburikidza nechiratidzo chavo kuti vachataura nhema dzakawanda vachiti mushonga unoshamisa uchapa upenyu noutano uye wakakwana zvinoshamisa mukugadzirwa kwawo!

Asi vose vanotora muchetura wavo vachazvishandura nokusingaperi-peri kunyange shanduko isina kupora. Nokuti kumhura mufananidzo waYAHWE uyo Adhamu akasikwa nawo ndiko kuzvibatanidza nezvivi zvevakawa vanonyepedzera kuti vamwari asi vasiri, uye vose vanoita saizvozvo havana kuregererwa uye havana rugare uye mudziva remoto risingadzimwi vachaenda nokusingaperi nokuti ndizvo zvavachange vasarudza.

Asi, sezvo vatatu vasina kuchena vachisimuka saizvozvowo zvapupu zvangu zviviri zvichasimuka, uye vachaporofita mazuva 1260 vakapfeka masaga uye zvapupu zvizhinji zvavachapa. Vachauya kuIsiraeri vopupurira Mwari Asingaperi weKururama uye vachapupura nesimba pamusoro paJesu Mesiya kunyange Jesu weNazareta uyo pasina iye hapana munhu angauya kuna El Shadhai! Asi, vatatu vasina kuchena vachati Jesu Kristu mupari wemhosva uye vacharambidza zvinyorwa zvitsvene izvo Jesu ari Shoko rakaitwa nyama. Asi iye wavari kuda kurambidza achapwanya misoro yavo mukukundwa kusingaperi.

Naizvozvo, mumazuva okupupura kwavo kana kuda kwomunhu upi nokupi kuri kwekuvakuvadza, moto uchabuda mumiromo yavo kuti uparadze vavengi vavo; nokuti kana kuda kwomunhu upi nokupi kuri kwekuvakuvadza, vanofanira kukuvadzwa nenzira imwe cheteyo uye kana kuda kwomunhu upi nokupi kuri kwekuvauraya, ivo pachavo vanofanira kuurayiwa nenzira imwe cheteyo. Ava vane simba rokuvhara denga, kuti mvura isanaya mumazuva okuporofita kwavo, uye vane simba pamusoro pemvura kuti vaiite ropa, uye kurova nyika nemarudzi ose ematambudziko, kakawanda sezvavanoda. Uye saizvozvo achava mazuva okupupura kwavo.

Uye vachataura pamusoro pezvinhu zvizhinji uye vachaparidza Vhangeri Risingaperi, iro rakaziviswa panguva yaro kuti zvinhu zvose zvizadziswe pachiso chaJesu Kristu. Uye zvapupu zvavo pachavo vachapa uye zvinhu zvizhinji zvavachazivisa mumazuva okupupura kwavo uye zvose zvichanyorwa nokusingaperi-peri. Uye pamagumo emazuva avo vanofanira kuurayiwa uye mitumbi yavo icharara mumugwagwa weguta guru, iro pamweya rinonzi Sodhoma neIjipiti, kwakanga kwakarovererwawo Ishe wenyu uye vachapemberera zvikuru nokuchinjana zvipo pamusoro porufu rwavo. Uye pamagumo emazuva matatu nehafu vachanzwa inzwi guru richiti huyai kuno uye vachamutswa vari vapenyu nokusingaperi-peri vachitorerwa zvakare kumatenga uye saizvozvo zvapupu zvavo zvichapera.

Saka nzwisisai kunyengera kwauya pamukova pako Isiraeri uye nzwisisai sarudzo yamunofanira kuita. Muchagamuchira kana Bharabhasi saMesiya wenyu wenhema motora chiratidzo chake nokudaro muchibatana naEsau sezvamunotengesa udangwe hwenyu nokuda kwechimedu chechingwa nokusingaperi kwose, kana kuti muchazvininipisa mwoyo yenyu mogoteerera inzwi raYahweh Mwari mukuru asingaperi kunyange Ini Mukuru Ndiri pachiso chaJesu Mesiya uyo akafanana naKristu Jesu wandakamutsa kubva kuvakafa ndikamukwidza kumatenga, uye ndikamukudza mukubwinya kusingaperi uye pakutonga kwake noUmambo hwake hapangavi nomugumo, uye hapana munhu angasvika kuna YAHWE nezita ripi neripi rakapihwa kunyange vari kudenga kana panyika kana pasi penyika nokusingaperi-peri. Nokuti muna Kristu vose vachaitwa vapenyu uye vagadzirwe nokusingaperi somunhu mutsva mumwe nokusingaperi-peri nokuti Kristu Jesu ndiye Adhamu wekupedzisira.

Uye vacharwa navatsvene uye vachavakunda nokuti simba rakapihwa kwavari kuti vaite saizvozvo. Asi havangakundi Isiraeri, kwete, vakasara veIsiraeri vachaponeswa pavanotendeukira kuna Jesu Mesiya nemwoyo yavo yose.

Naizvozvo inzwai Mashoko angu imi Isiraeri kuti murambe muchirarama pamberi pangu uye ndichakuunganidzai kwandiri pasi pesimba guru raMesiya wenyu wechokwadi, kunyange Jesu Mesiya. Uye kwakanyorwa kuti: Kunyange zvakadaro hazvingaiti kuti shoko raMwari rishaye maturo; nokuti havasi vose vari vaIsiraeri, avo vari veIsiraeri: Uye havasi vose vari vana, nokuti ndivo mbeu yaAbrahama; asi, Muna Isaka mbeu yako ichanzi: Ndiko kuti, avo vari vana venyama, havasi vana vaMwari; asi vana vechipikirwa, vanoverengwa sembeu.

NDIRI YAHWE, Ini Mukuru Ndiri uye ndiri kukudanai imi Isiraeri kuti vakasara vakatendeka vauye kuna Jesu Mesiya kuti akuponesei kubva mumusungo uyo Bharabhasi ari kushanda kuti ateye munyika nokuda kwekuparadzwa kwekupedzisira kwaJakobho, kunyange kuparadzwa kwandisingazobvumiri.

NDIRI iye anotumira Mashoko aya nenzwi reimwe inodana murenje, yakadzikinurwa kubva kuvahedheni naKristu Jesu ikaiswa parutivi nokuda kwezvinangwa zvedu zvisingaperi, uye kuna Jesu Mesiya anoshumira YAHWE, Ini Mukuru Ndiri, nokuti hapana munhu angauya kwandiri nezita ripi neripi, kunyange naMosesi wangu anodiwa oga, nokuti Mosesi anoda kuteererwa kwaJesu weNazareta!

NDIRI iye achabvisa upofu hwechikamu kubva kuna Isiraeri uyo akapofumadzwa kuTestamente Itsva nechifukidzo chaMosesi icho chichiri chakafukidza mwoyo yavo muupofu hwechikamu. Uye vose veIsiraeri vanodana kuna Jesu Mesiya vachawanazve kuona kwavo uye vachaona zvizere, kunyange pachiso chaJesu Kristu, Mesiya wenyu asingaperi!

NDIRI Mwari Wemasimbaose waAbrahama, naIsaka, naJakobho uye ndinouya kuzozadzisa zvipikirwa zvangu zvose kakawanda uye ndichazviita zvizere nemabasa asingaperi eMwanakomana wangu anodiwa wandinorayira kuvimbika kwenyu kuzere, kutenda, rudo, nokuteerera, imi Isiraeri kunze kwekunge muchida kutengesa udangwe hwenyu, muchibatana naEsau nokusarudza mumirazvo isingaperi yokurangwa nokuripirwa kwekururamisira.

Nzwisisai, imi Isiraeri kuti nyika ichaziva kuti kuna Mwari muna Jakobho uye kuti Mwari waJakobho ndiye Yahweh nokusingaperi-peri, kunyange Ini Mukuru Ndiri, uye muna Kristu Jesu ndimo muchazounganidzwa Isiraeri yaMwari uye ndichavaunganidza kwandiri sezvakapikirwa. Kuti kunyange vari pedyo kana kure, kana kuti vari vaJudha kana vahedheni kuti vose vaunganidzwe muna Kristu avo vari vadyi venhaka youpenyu husingaperi uye avo vari vana vechokwadi vechipikirwa nokuti vana vechipikirwa ndivo vakarurama vanorarama nokutenda.

Uye saizvozvo zvichava uye YAHWE akazivisa saizvozvo uye zvose zvichava hongu naAmeni naAmeni naAMENI!
