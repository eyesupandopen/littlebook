Muchatendeukira Kuna Ani?

Shoko raIshe rakasvika kwandiri.

Ishe Jesu Kristu vanoti: Muchatendeukira kuna ani kuti mutize hasha dzangu? Muchaisa kutenda kwenyu muvanhu vakaipa vakatumwa kunyengedza kunyange vakasarudzwa kana zvichibvira? Muchaisa kutenda kwenyu muhurongwa hwechikara cheBhabhironi, hure guru iro? Makanyengedzwa mukafunga kuti munogona kusarudza mambo wenyu nesarudzo here? Hamunzwisisi here kuti Satani ndimwari wenyika ino uye anonyengedza nyika yose? Muchaisa kutenda kwenyu mumasangano akagadzirwa nevanhu nedzimba dzevaporofita venhema vakatumwa kunyengedza vakasarudzwa vangu chaivo? Muchatendeukira kuna ani? Muchaenda kupi kana pasina chinogona kuvanzwa pamberi pangu?

Muri kuona vakaipa vachigadzirira kuzvivanza mumapako avo munyika avakazvicherera kuti vanditize, asi vakacherera pasina. Hasha dzangu dzava kusvika uye hapana munhu anogona kumira. Danai zita rangu kuchine nguva. Baba vakaisa zvinhu zvose mumaoko angu, kudenga nepanyika, uye ndichazadzisa zvirongwa zvavo. Hapana chinhu uye hapana munhu anogona kundimisa nekuti Baba vangu vakandipa simba, uye handigoni kumiswa. Hasha dzeGwayana dziri pamusuwo penyu, kunyange zvazvo ndichiri kumira pamusuwo ndichigogodza. Ndinoshuva kupinda ndigodya nemi kana mukangovhura musuwo wenyu.

Vanhu vangu mukai!! Mukai!! MUKAI!! Mukai mukuitira kururama uye musatadza. Ndakanzwa minyengetero yenyu, uye ndakaona kushungurudzika kwemweya yenyu nekutambudzika. Uyai kwandiri nemoyo wenyu wose uye mubve pazvivi. Ibvai pazvivi zveBhabhironi nevatungamiri varo; ndinokuudzai pachena kuti ibvai mariri zvizere, kunyange mumakereke aro akatsauka. Nekuti kutongwa kweBhabhironi kwava pedyo, uye kuchauya kamwe kamwe, kuchisiya rava dongo muawa imwe. Nekuti zvivi zvaro zvikuru; naizvozvo, kutongwa kwaro kukuru.

Kutiza kwenyu chete kuri mukuchengeteka kwemaoko angu uye hapana anokwanisa kukubvutai mumaoko angu. Musave vanodziya-dziya, sezvo nguva dzekuparadzanisa dzatovepo. Kuvanhu: muchatendeukira kupi pandinounganidza mwenga wangu kubva pakati penyu? Hamuna kuverenga here? Hamuna kunzwa here?

Ndinoshuva kupinda ndigodya nemi kana mukangovhura musuwo wenyu.

Zvakazarurwa 18

Uye chiedza chekanduru hachichazovheneki mauri zvachose; uye inzwi rechikomba neremwenga hazvichazonzwiki mauri zvachose; nekuti vatengesi vako vaive varume vakuru venyika; nekuti nekuroyera kwako ndudzi dzose dzakanyengedzwa.

Inzwi rechikomba neremwenga harichazonzwiki nekuti ndichaunganidza mwenga wangu ndigomuviga mudzimba dzandakagadzirira nguva inouya, nekuti mumba maBaba vangu mune dzimba zhinji.
