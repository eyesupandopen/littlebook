AmeruKa: Yazarurwa Yakatongwa! Chikomba Chinorayira Mwenga Wake, Gadzirira: Ndinouya Kuzokutora!

Inzwai Mashoko aYeshua HaMashiach, anovawo Kristu Jesu, uye munzwisise kutaura kwangu kuitira kuti mugone kunzwisisa mavambo echokwadi eBhabhironi Rakavanzika, chimiro charo, nechinangwa charo. Nokuti, zvirokwazvo, inyika yeNyoka ine Munhenga, iyo AmeruKa, nyika inonyahwaira yakavanda, inodya vagari vayo, inouraya vasina mhosva, uye inobayira vose baba naamai muhuwandu hukuru kubvira kare, kusvikira panguva dzino. Nyika yakatukwa inosvibisa vagari vayo nokunyengera, uye nehuwandu hwezvisaririra zvinosvibisa nezvinoshatisa zvezvibayiro zvemhemberero zveropa neutsinye zvakabva kare zvakavigwa munyika yose iyi yakasvibiswa mumirwi mizhinji yezvifananidzo, tsika, kupiswa kweTofeti, neropa rezvibayiro rinoyerera rakasununguka. Ropa iroro rakanyorovesa ivhu rayo apo pasi rinochemera kururamisira kweropa rakadeurwa, uye richiri kudeurwa muhuwandu hukuru. Nyika idzodzi dzakasvibiswa dzinochema zvikuru uye nenzwi guru kumatenga dzichikumbira kururamisira panguva ino chaiyo, kunyange apo pasi rinodzedzereka zvikuru somunhu akadhakwa ava kuda kuwa, richitokumbira kutongwa kwakarurama uko zvirokwazvo kusiri kunonoka.

Uye iwe Bhabhironi Rakavanzika, iwe chaiwe: Uri kondinendi isiri tsvene uye yakasvibiswa, nyika yakapedzisira kugarwa kubva pamazuva aNoa, nyika yakatsaurirwa kuna Heylel Ben Shachar nenzira dzenyoka, nyika yakanyikwa muropa revasina mhosva netsika nemiitiro yerudzi rwakashata rwenyoka nevashatisi vakaenda kunoshatisa munguva dzekare. Nyika yakachengetedzwa pakavanda nemaoko akavanzika evakawira, yakachengeterwa uye yakarongerwa kuva Atlantis yavo Itsva, rudzi rwakagadzirwa uye rwakatsaurirwa kumuka kwechikara chinobva mugungwa nechikara chinobva panyika. Rudzi runonyepedzera kuva rwechiKristu, asi muchokwadi irudzi rwevanamati vasatani pakavanda vakateya uye vanogara vakasununguka muzvikoro zvaro zvakasvibiswa nezvinosvibisa (kubvira kune muduku kusvika kune mukuru) uye zvikoro izvi zvinotongwa nemasangano epachivande ayo akazvitsaurira kuna Heylel Ben Shachar (uyo wavanonyepa uye mukusaziva kwavo vachiti Rusifa), vachizviti mukusaziva kwavo vanopenya uye vakavhenekerwa, kunyange mukunyengedzwa kwavo nekunyengera kwavo kwavakazvisunga nekusingaperi kumhiko dzavo dzisiri tsvene nokusingagumi, vachinyengedza, asi panguva imwe chete vachinyengedzwa naavo vavakasungirwa nekusingaperi sevakomana vechivi kune vakawira nokusingagumi.

Ngaizivikanwe, kuti Bhabhironi Rakavanzika iUnited States Of America, iyo yekare yainzi Ameru-Ca, Nyika yeNyoka ine Munhenga, yakanyahwaira uye yakazvishandura kuva hure guru iroro rinogara pamusoro pemvura zhinji uye irudzi rwakaitwa, kuitira kuti chikara chinobva mugungwa nechikara chinobva panyika zvigone kumuka kubva mumadota aro munguva dzazvo dzekuzarurwa. Rudzi rwemhombwe, rudzi rweufeve, rudzi rwuri hure guru rinozviti muteveri akarurama waJesu weNazareta; asi muchokwadi irudzi rine vavaki varo vakasununguka kubva paDombo rechokwadi reNheyo, dombo guru iroro rinogumbusa vasina kutenda, iye Jesu weNazareta anovawo Yeshua HaMashiach.

Zvirokwazvo, AmeruKa yava United States yemazuva ano, uye irudzi rwakateyiwa nemaoko avo asiri matsvene uye akavanzika, nyika yemasangano epachivande akazvipira kunyoka muzvishongo zvisinganzwisisike; uye sezvo simba reRosy Cross rakapinda muAmeruKa, ndizvowo zvakaitwa pakuiswa kwekupedzisira kweAtlantis yavo Itsva. Urongwa hwakavanzwa kwenguva refu hwaizobatanidza nekusanganisa zvitendero izvi zvenyoka pasi rose nekuparadza nekubayira vazhinji veboka revapristi vemarudzi aya akawira, nevazhinji vemarudzi aya pachavo vaizozvikudza uye nekupusa vachitsvaga kumisa nyoka imwe cheteyo yavakazvipira nemhiko zhinji dzeropa kuti vashumire zvizere, vasinganzwisisi chimiro chechisikwa chaicho chavakazvisunga nacho, kunyange zvazvo, zvisaririra zvevanhu ava zviri munyika idzi nanhasi.

Saka ndiwe AmeruKa: Nokuti ndinotaura nekondinendi iyo kubva kuchamhembe kusvika kuchamhembe uye kubva kumabvazuva kusvika kumadokero inyika yakatsaurirwa kunyoka ine munhenga yekare iyo yakasvibisa nekushatisa nyika idzi nokuda kwezvinangwa zvayo, kunyange sezvo umambo hwakasimuka ndokuwira mukati memiganhu yayo, umambo huzhinji husina kunyorwa hwenyoka, rudzi rwenyoka ine mapapiro uye inobhururuka rwakatsaurirwa kunzanga dzinotungamirirwa nevakadzi nedzemutemo wevanhu apo mhomho yakaipa yaigona kutonga pasi pesimba raHeylel Ben Shachar. Uyezve, rudzi rwake rwenyoka rwakanyatsosvibisa nyika dzeBhabhironi rakavanzika, inova nyika yakagarwa apo munhu aidzungaira panyika kubva pakamhara Areka paGomo reArarati nguva yose iyi yapfuura. Saka vanhu vakauya kunyika yenyoka ine munhenga vachibva kumadokero, vachifamba vakananga kumabvazuva vachiyambuka Bering land bridge sezvainozivikanwa mazuva ano, uye vakauya negungwa kubva kumabvazuva vakananga kumadokero vachiyambuka Atlantic, nenzira dzakasiyana-siyana dzekutengeserana nekufamba vakauya kunyika idzi dzakachengeterwa naHeylel Ben Shachar nokuda kwezvinangwa zvake zvakaipa. Nyika iyo vakawira vakatora zvizere munyika yaimbovapo, nyika mavakaviga zvakavanzika zvizhinji zvavachazobvumidzwa kutora kamwe kamwe uye zvisingatarisirwi, kunyange sezvo mudzivisi wangu achibviswa uye sezvo kutonga kwavo kupfupi kuchibvumidzwa kuitira ruponeso rwaJakobho, uye kuti naivowo vagowira mukutongwa kwemoto sezvo nyaya iyi ichienderera mberi kubva pamazuva aNoa, kuti vaparadzwe zvachose, nokuti nguva dzevakawira dzasvika kumagumo, uye ndini Kristu Jesu, Shumba yeRudzi rwaJudha ndichapwanya musoro wenyoka iyi yekare mukupedzisa kwekutonga.

Saka zvinonzi, Heylel Ben Shachar, muchinda muduku iyeye akakundwa, muzvikudzi mukuru iyeye uye musvibisi wezvose zvakachena, akaenda kunodyara masora ake mumunda waYahweh, Mukuru IYE WANDIRI, munda iwoyo wakadyarwa apo Elohim akati ngakuve nechiedza kukavapo chiedza. Naizvozvo, inzwai Mashoko aYeshua HaMashiach, iye Kristu Jesu pandinouya kuzozarurira kereke yangu, muviri wangu, uye kunyange mwenga wangu chokwadi pamusoro penyika idzodzo dzakashata uye dzakasvibiswa kwamakaberekerwa vazhinji venyu, uye kwamunogara. Ikoko, vakapararira pakati pemarudzi vakadzungaira uye vakachengetwa, uye vakachengetedzwa kubvira ipapo, uye vazhinji vavo zvino chikamu cheIsraeri yaMwari, nekusingaperi! Vachichengetwa nemudzidzisi wavo, nokuti vakabata pamutemo waMosesi pavakafamba vachienda kunyika dzekuchamhembe mushure mekunge vakundwa mukusateerera nemaoko evaAsiriya. Saka marudzi ekuchamhembe akarambwa ndokukandirwa kumarudzi, asi vakatopararira muhutapwa apo vaBhabhironi vakakunda ndokuranga Umambo hwekuChamhembe, ivo vasina kuteerera pakati pemadzibaba angu emarudzi aJudha naBhenjamini nevamwe, vakaita Umambo hwaJudha mumazuva ekare. Saka makwai akarasika eImba yaIsraeri akaparadzirwa pakati pemarudzi nokuda kwekusateerera kwavo, kunyange zvazvo vakasara vagara vari munyika yechipikirwa kusvikira nhasi. Saka Hama dzavo nevamwe vedzinza ravo vekuUmambo hwekuChamhembe vakakundwawo nevaBhabhironi. Asi, vakadzoka kuzovakazve temberi yechipiri uye nekufamba kwenguva vakaparadzirwa kunze kwenyika mumazuva aVespasian naTitus, uye zvakare panguva yekupanduka kwakatungamirirwa naSimon Bar Kokhba, apo vazhinji vevanhu vangu vakaparadzirwazve kunyika dzekumaodzanyemba nekumabvazuva kweJerusarema kuenda kuchamhembe kweAfrica nekuAlexandria yeEgypt nedzimwe nzvimbo zhinji kwavakadzungaira mukurangwa kwavo. Asi naivowo vakachengetedzwa naMosesi, kunyange vazhinji vari muuranda kuna Esau, naAshkenazi, uye vazhinji vavo vakanyengedzwa uye vakamirira Mahdi wavo, panzvimbo paYeshua HaMashiach anova Mesia wavo wechokwadi, uye mununuri waIsraeri akapikirwa kare naAdonai-YHVH, Selah.

Asi, sezvazvakanyorwa, ndizvo zvazviri, kuti vanhu vangu havazivi uye vakapofumadzwa zvishoma kuzvokwadi yavo nekuzivikanwa kwavo. Asi, vazhinji vevanhu vangu vakazvibvumira kuti vashandurwe vachipesana mukusaziva nenhema neruvengo uye zvichikonzera kusaziva kukuru kwemavambo avo echokwadi, kunyange sezvo vakadzidziswa kutenda nhema dzerudzi urwu rwevapristi venyoka mune chero rudzi rwavakazvisimbisa, rudzi irworwo rwevapristi venyoka vanoshumira Heylel Ben Shachar, serafimi iyoyo inopfuta uye yakawira yakachengeterwa moto wekurangwa kusingaperi nekutongwa uko moto unotakura chirango chakaomesesa chenyoka iyi yakaipa yakazvisvibisa mukupanduka kusina musoro, yakabatwa mumamiriro ekukundwa nekuwira ayo isingazombopori.

Saka vanhu venyika yangu nehama dzangu vakadzidziswa kuvengana nenzira dzenhema zhinji dzakagadzirirwa kudzidzisa vanhu vazhinji. Saka zvakanyorwa kuti: Zvinhu zvitanhatu izvi zvinovengwa naIshe: hongu, mweya wake unonyangadzwa nezvinomwe: meso anozvikudza, rurimi runoreva nhema, nemaoko anoteura ropa risina mhosva, moyo unofunga zvirongwa zvakaipa, tsoka dzinokurumidza kumhanyira kune zvakaipa, chapupu chenhema chinotaura nhema, uye uyo anomutsa kukakavara pakati pehama.

Saka nzwisisai kuti zviito izvi zvakashata zvakanyorwa zvakakwana kumatenga uye kururamisira nekuripira zvichanaya kamwe kamwe pavagari vepanyika vachiri kutsvaga kuparadzwa kwaJakobho; panzvimbo pazvo kuparadzwa kwavo kuchavawira kamwe kamwe uye zvisingatarisirwi zvino, Selah.
