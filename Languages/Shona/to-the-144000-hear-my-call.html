Mumabasa aJohane Muratidzwi
Chivabvu 20, 2024

Kune vane 144,000: Inzwai Kudaidzwa Kwangu!

Inzwai, inzwai, imi marudzi angu gumi nemaviri, izwi raMesia wenyu, Ishe, Mambo, uye Mupristi Mukuru, ini, Yeshua HaMashiach, Mwanakomana woMunhu uyo Yahweh akamutsa kubva kuvakafa. Nokuti:

Ndini Mesia wenyu wechokwadi, anobva pachigaro choumambo chaDhavhidhi, uye ndichatonga Nyika yeChipikirwa ndiri pachigaro chake, nokuda kwaYahweh, Mwari wekusingaperi, nokuti ndizvo zvaakapikira Madzibaba enyu. Saka nzwisisai kuti ndini Shumba yeRudzi rwaJudha, uye hakuna mambo kana muporofita akabva kwaDhani, kunobva muporofita wenhema.

Ndini Ishe wenyu, nokuti Yahweh akandiita Ishe wemadzishe, uye ndakakutengai neropa rangu pachangu sePasika yenyu yakakwana, yakatopiwa nokuda kwezvivi zveveMarudzi pamwe neveJudha, kuti imi mose muve munhu mutsva mumwechete mandiri, iye Kristu Jesu. Nokuti Yahweh akandipa zita riri pamusoro pemazita ose uye akaita kuti ndigovane naye chigaro chake choumambo nokuda kwekuteerera kwangu. Saka Ushe hwangu hwakapiwa sechiratidzo cheruponeso, nokuti ndakaitwa mweya unopa upenyu, uye Yahweh chete ndiye mukuru kwandiri, nokuti ndiye Mwari wekusingaperi.

Ndini Mambo wenyu, nokuti Yahweh akandiita Mambo wemadzimambo, uye ndichatonga nyika mukururama. Uye tarirai! Ndinouya neMwenga wangu, uyo achatonga neni, nokuti ndakavaita madzimambo nevapristi pamberi paYahweh wekusingaperi, uyo pasina akafanana naye. Nokuti mukutonga kwangu kwechuru chemakore kwakapikirwa, ndini ndichava nesimba guru, uye ndichatuma madzimambo angu nevapristi kuti vatongere munhaka yavo mandiri; nokuti ndakavasika patsva mandiri, vakatorwa kubva kuvaJudha neveMarudzi vakaitwa munhu mutsva mumwechete, vakasikwazve mukukwana. Ndini mumwechete mukubatana nechinangwa naBaba vangu, Yahweh, uye pachigaro chavo choumambo Ivo vakuru kwandiri.

Ndini mupristi wenyu mukuru nokusingaperi zvichienderana nourongwa hwaMerkizedeki, uye ndakapa ropa rangu pachangu rekuyananisa sechibayiro pandakazvipa sePasika yenyu yakakwana uye izere. Nokuti vaHebheru vanopa uchapupu hwavo pandakaita kuti Muapostora Pauro ataure navo achindimiririra (aiva wekwaBhenjamini): Ropa raKristu, uyo kubudikidza noMweya usingaperi akazvipa asina gwapa kuna Mwari, haringatonyanyi kunatsa hana dzenyu pamabasa akafa, kuti mushumire Mwari mupenyu here? Saka chibayiro changu changu pachangu ndechenguva imwechete nokusingaperi, uye hakuna mumwe Mesia.

Kunyange zvazvo sinagogi raSatani richagamuchira mesia wavo wenhema nemuporofita wenhema uyo ariwo muvaki wetemberi yechikara (nokuti ndiye chikara chinobva panyika). Nokuti imi muri temberi yaMwari mupenyu, uye hapachina kudikanwa kwechibayiro chemhuka zvakare, nokuti ndini chibayiro chekupedzisira, Pasika yakakwana, isina chivi, uye mabasa angu akakwana uye azere. Naizvozvo, hapachazombovi nekudikanwa kwechinhu chakadaro, asi kuti muuye kwandiri nezvivi zvenyu mukutendeuka, uye ndichakunatsai pamabasa enyu akafa enyama. Nokuti muna VaRoma zvakanyorwa kuti: Nokuti murayiro woMweya woupenyu muna Kristu Jesu wakandisunungura pamurayiro wechivi norufu. Nokuti ndizvo zvandakakusunungurai mandiri.

Nzwisisai, marudzi angu nevanhu vangu, kuti nguva dzeveMarudzi dzava kusvika kumagumo. Nguva dzeveMarudzi dzakauya nokuda kwekuoma kwemwoyo yemadzibaba enyu. Nokuti Pauro akatiyi muna Mabasa pamusoro penyaya iyi: Zvakanga zvakafanira kuti shoko raMwari ritange kutaurwa kwamuri; asi zvauri kuriramba, muchizvitonga pachenyu kuti hamuna kufanira upenyu husingaperi, tarirai, tava kutendeukira kuveMarudzi.

Ndinokurairai, vanhu vangu, musaita zvikanganiso zvimwechetezvo uye musararama muzvivi zvemadzibaba enyu, asi tendeukai pazvivi zvenyu uye tendeukaiwo pazvivi zvemadzibaba enyu, muuye kwandiri uye ndichakugamuchirai, nokuti muri vana vechipikirwa. Uye yodhi yese kana munzwa weyodhi uchazadziswa; nokuti izvo zvakapikirwa naYahweh hazvigone kuchinjwa, nokuti hazvigoneke kuti Yahweh areve nhema. Naizvozvo, ndini kuzadziswa kwezvipikirwa zvizhinji-zhinji, nokuti ndini Mesia wenyu wechokwadi, mufananidzo waMwari wekusingaperi. Nokuti ndini mwanakomana wake mumwechete akaberekwa, Gwayana raMwari iro Johane Mubhabhatidzi akaratidza nokusingaperi. Nokuti akati: Tarirai Gwayana raMwari, rinobvisa chivi chenyika!

Saka ndakatendeukira kumarudzi nokuti nyika yangu yakandiramba, kunyange ndafa nokuda kwezvivi zvavo uye ndamutswa. Nokuti ndakatumira vaapostora vangu nevaporofita kwavari, uye vazhinji vakaponeswa. Nokuti pakutanga kwenguva yenyasha, ndakavatumira muTemberi nemumasinagogi, uye vazhinji vakatendeukira kwandiri vakandigamuchira saShe wavo. Saka ndiri kukudaidzai kuti muitwe vakakwana muUshe hwangu, nokuti ndakakudzikinurai kuna Baba vangu kuti nemiwo muve vana Vavo kuburikidza nemweya wangu wandichakuisai chisimbiso nawo. Saka nguva dzeveMarudzi dzapera, nokuti zvino navowo vakandiramba vachida mumwe Jesu wavanonamata samwari, nokudaro vachizviita vanamati vezvifananidzo; nokuti Satani akavaedza saizvozvo. Nokuti kuna Mwari mumwechete, Yahweh wekusingaperi, uye kuna Mesia mumwechete wechokwadi, ini, Yeshua HaMashiach.

Nokuti vakarasikirwa nezvakavanzika zvangu, vakaregeredza zviratidzo zvangu nezvinangwa zverugwaro rwangu rutsvene. Vakarasikirwa nekuona hurongwa hwaBaba hwerudzikinuro, uye vanoramba nemanyawi mashoko angu andakaita kuti anyorwe kuvaRoma. Nokuti Pauro akati: Ndinoti, zvino, Mwari wakarasha vanhu vake here? Hazviitiki. Nokuti neniwo ndiri muIsraeri, worudzi rwaAbhurahama, worudzi rwaBhenjamini. Mwari haana kurasha vanhu vake, vaakagara aziva. Uye zvakare: Zvino zvakadiniko? Israeri haana kuwana izvo zvaanotsvaka; asi vakasanangurwa vakazviwana, uye vamwe vose vakapofumadzwa. Saka sarudzo yakanga yakasimba kune avo vandakasarudza kubva pakati pemarudzi; nokuti vaapostora vangu vose vakanga vari venyika neechipikirwa, saka ndakavatumira kumarudzi, izvo vatungamiriri venyu vezvitendero vanonyepa zvinomhura, vachivati ndevanyeperi. Vatungamiriri ivavo venhema nevafudzi vasina maturo vanoramba kunyange kumuka kwangu, nokuti ndakamutswa kubva kuvakafa, uye handichazofi zvakare nokusingaperi.

Nokuti chii chimwe chinotaurwa nevaRoma pamusoro penyaya iyi: Dhavhidhi anoti, Tafura yavo ngaive musungo, nomuteyo, nechigumbuso, nomutsividzo kwavari: Meso avo ngaasvibiswe kuti varege kuona, uye musana wavo ngaurembere nguva dzose. Ndinoti, zvino, Vakagumburwa kuti vawire pasi here? Hazviitiki; asi nokuda kokuwira kwavo ruponeso rwakauya kuveMarudzi, kuti ruvamutsire godo.

Nokuti handidi, hama, kuti musaziva chakavanzika ichi, kuti murege kuzviti makachenjera; kuti upofu muchikamu hwakaitika kuna Israeri, kusvikira kuzara kweveMarudzi kwapinda. Saka ndinozivisa kuti kuzara kweveMarudzi kwasvika, uye zvakavanzika zvaMwari zvichadzoserwa kuna Israeri nokuda kwekuteerera kwekutenda. Nokuti ndichaponesa Jakobho, uye vakasara vakatendeka vachatendeukira kwandiri vakatendeka, mukutenda kukuru!

Saka ndauya kuzomutsa godo mumwoyo yenyu nokuda kwangu, Yeshua HaMashiach, naYahweh Mwari mukuru wekusingaperi akandituma, uyo wamusisiri kunamata maererano neruzivo, nokuti chifukidzo cheupofu chichafukidza ani naani anotevera murayiro waMosesi. Nokuti ndakatiyi kuvaKorinde pamusoro penyaya iyi: Uye kwete saMosesi, uyo akaisa chifukidzo pachiso chake, kuti vana vaIsraeri varege kunyatsotarisa kumagumo kweizvo zvakabviswa: Asi pfungwa dzavo dzakapofumadzwa: nokuti kusvikira nhasi chifukidzo chimwechetecho chinoramba chisina kubviswa pakuverengwa kweTestamente Yekare; chifukidzo icho chinobviswa muna Kristu. Asi kunyange kusvikira nhasi, kana Mosesi achiverengwa, chifukidzo chiri pamwoyo pavo.

Kunyange zvakadaro, kana ukatendeukira kuna Ishe, chifukidzo chichabviswa.

Nokuti ndakazadzisa murayiro waMosesi, nokuti imi kana madzibaba enyu hamugoni kuuteerera zvakakwana nokuda kwechivi chiri muropa renyu. Nokuti ini, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndakazadzisa murayiro zvizere, uye murayiro unongochengetwa zvakakwana nokutenda mandiri, Jesu Kristu. Munonzi makarurama nokuzvizivisa kwenyu kwakakwana neni, saka vakarurama vachararama nokutenda. Saka ndichabvisa chifukidzo pamwoyo yenyu kuti muone ini, Yeshua, Mesia wenyu wechokwadi, nokuti handina kukusiyai! Uye ndinouya kwamuri mutestamente itsva, kwete mukukara kwerugwaro. Nokuti ndakasimbisa sungano yangu itsva muropa rangu pachangu, maererano nekuda nezvipikirwa zvaYahweh, nokuda kwerudzikinuro rwenyu ruzere. Uye ndichakutungamirirai kunhaka yenyu, nokuti zvipikirwa zvaMwari ndihongu naamen. Kunyange yodhi kana munzwa weyodhi hazvizokundikani, nokuti zvose zvava kuda kuzadziswa; uye nenguva, zvirokwazvo, ndichazadzisa zvose, nokuti ndizvo zvandakatumwa naYahweh kuti ndiite.

Saka veMarudzi vakandifuratira sembwa inodzokera kumarutsi ayo, uye nguva yasvika yekuvasiya pazviri. Nokuti munguva yangu yenyasha, hapana munhu mumwechete akasarudzwa naBaba vangu kuti aponeswe akapotswa, uye hapana anogona kuvabvuta paruoko rwangu. Nokuti avo vakandipupura, Jesu, saShe wavo, nekutenda mumwoyo yavo kuti Mwari akandimutsa kubva kuvakafa, vakaiswa chisimbiso nemweya mutsvene wechipikirwa, uye zvinangwa zvaBaba vangu zvakazadziswa mandiri nokuda kwavo. Saka vachagara neni nokusingaperi.

Nokuti muorivhi unoratidza chokwadi. Uye ndakatiyi pamusoro pemuorivhi mutsamba yaPauro kuvaRoma: Uye kana mamwe matavi akavhunwa, iwe, uri muorivhi wemusango, ukaiswa pakati pawo, ukagovana nawo mudzi nemafuta emuorivhi; Usazvirumbidza pamusoro pematavi. Asi kana uchizvirumbidza, hausi iwe unotakura mudzi, asi mudzi ndiwo unokutakura.

Saka vakaita zvikanganiso vakatora zvivi zvemadzibaba enyu uye vakandifuratira vachienda kuzvifananidzo zvavo, uye havachazonzwi izwi rangu. Kunyange kana vakadana pazita raIshe, vachaponeswa avo vasingadi upenyu hwavo kusvika kumagumo.

Nokuti chii chimwe chinotaurwa neuchapupu hwevaRoma murugwaro seyambiro kumarudzi: Uchati ipapo, Matavi akavhunwa, kuti ini ndiiswe. Zvakanaka; nokuda kwekusatenda akavhunwa, uye iwe unomira nokutenda. Usazvikudza, asi itya: Nokuti kana Mwari asina kurega matavi echisikigo, chenjera kuti arege kusakuregawo iwe. Tarira naizvozvo kunaka nekuoma kwaMwari: kune avo vakawira, kuoma; asi kwauri, kunaka, kana ukaramba mukunaka kwake: kana zvisina kudaro newewo uchagurwa. Uye naivowo, kana vakaramba vasiri mukusatenda, vachaiswa: nokuti Mwari anogona kuvaisa zvakare. Nokuti kana iwe wakagurwa pamuorivhi uri wemusango pachisikigo, ukaiswa zvisiri pachisikigo pamuorivhi wakanaka: ko zvakadini neava, vari matavi echisikigo, vachaiswa pamuorivhi wavo?

Nokuti handidi, hama, kuti musaziva chakavanzika ichi, kuti murege kuzviti makachenjera; kuti upofu muchikamu hwakaitika kuna Israeri, kusvikira kuzara kweveMarudzi kwapinda. Uye saizvozvo Israeri wose achaponeswa: sezvazvakanyorwa, Muchabuda muZioni Muponesi, uye achabvisa kusanamata kuna Jakobho.

Saka, hama dzangu, nguva dzeveMarudzi dzava kusvika kumagumo, uye shanduko huru iri kuitika, apo nyasha dzangu dzichabva dzashanduka kuva kutonga. Uye kunyange zvino zvidimbu zviri kubvumirwa kuiswa panzvimbo yacho neruoko rwaSatani, sezvo achaita kurwa kwake, kunyange zvazvo achaparadzwa zvachose, uye ndichaparadza umambo hwake zvizere; nokuti haasi mambo.

Saka zvino yasvika nguva yekudzosera matavi echisikigo pamuorivhi, nokuti zvinangwa zvaYahweh zvakazadziswa munyasha dzisina kufanirwa netsitsi dzakaratidzwa kuveMarudzi (nokuti nyasha netsitsi dzaMwari hadzina kufanirwa nemunhu wese), nokuti kunyange nyasha idzodzo netsitsi dzakashandiswa kuwana makwai akarasika eimba yaIsraeri. Nokuti hamufaniri kusaziva nhoroondo yenyu, kana mukaisa pasi maTalmud enyu eBhabhironi anomhura uye mukadzidza nezvangu; nokuti handina kutumira bhuku iroro, uye munofanira kutendeuka kubva kudzidziso idzodzo uye kuramba nhema dzaSatani. Dzokerai kushoko rangu rakachena raMwari uye mubve pazvivi zvemadzibaba enyu, nokuti ndichakudzikinurai kana mukangonzwa izwi rangu.

Nokuti handina kutumwa asi kumakwai akarasika eimba yaIsraeri. Saka marudzi gumi akapararira pakati pemarudzi kubva muutapwa hwavo nevaAsiria nguva yose iyi yapfuura. Vakadzungaira uye vakafamba panyika sevafambi nevatorwa. Kubva kuAsiria vakaenda kunyika dzekuchamhembe, kuScythia, neEurope, uye pakupedzisira kuAmerica, kwandakavaitira nzvimbo ine rugare kwechinguva. Uye ndakadana ani naani angauya kubva kumarudzi kuruponeso rwangu. Kunyange vaIsraeri vazhinji vakaponeswa uye vakadzikinurwa pamwe nevazhinji vanobva kumarudzi, avo dai vasina kudaro vaisazombova netariro. Uye boka guru rakadavira, saka nguva dzeveMarudzi dzapera, nokuti hapachina nguva. Asi nzwisisai kuti vose vanodana pazita raIshe vachaponeswa kana vakatendeka kusvika kumagumo, nokuti ndichavagamuchira zvisinei nerudzi rwavanobva.

Saka ndizvo zvazviri, kuti dambudziko raJakobho rinouya panyika nemarudzi, nokuti yave nguva yekuti kutonga kukuru kuwire panyika. Saka zvikara nesimba reshato zvichasimuka; chete musatora chiratidzo chake uye musanyengerwa naye. Nokuti akatozvifadza kuvatongi venyika yenyu, kunyange sinagogi raSatani nemaitiro avo eEdomu. Uye atotaura kumhura kukuru, nokuti ari kugadzirirwa chinangwa chake chechokwadi.

Nokuti nyangadzi yokuparadza iri kuuya. Nokuti ndakaita kuti Ruka wangu akatendeka anyorei nokusingaperi: Uye kana mukaona Jerusarema rakombwa nemauto, ipapo zivai kuti kuparadzwa kwaro kwava pedyo. Ipapo avo vari muJudhea ngavatizire kumakomo; uye avo vari pakati paro ngavabude; uye avo vari munyika ngavarege kupinda mariri. Nokuti aya ndiwo mazuva ekutsiva, kuti zvinhu zvose zvakanyorwa zvizadziswe.

Saka zvichaitika panguva yakafanira, uye ndichachengetedza vangu, nokuti ndiri kukutumai sevaporofita vangu, vafudzi, varapi, nevashumiri. Nokuti ndiri kukudaidzai kubasa, kunyange kuzadzisa kudaidzwa kwenyu kwechokwadi kwakapiwa sechitsidzo kumadzibaba naYahweh Pachake, uye neruoko rwangu zvichazadziswa. Nokuti Yahweh akaisa chigaro changu pachigaro Chake choumambo, uye ndakagovana chigaro changu choumambo neMwenga wangu, avo vanogara neni. Saka hapana panyika mukuru kwandiri; hapana vaporofita venyu venhema vanofananidzwa, nokuti vose havana simba uye vanoshaya uye vanotumwa kunyengera nekutsausa, nokuti vari vafudzi vasina maturo.

Zivai, vanhu vangu, kuti muvengi akakuteyera misungo mizhinji, saka teverai kutungamirira kwangu uye muchainzvenga yose. Nokuti munguva pfupi iri kutevera mafaro ose achabviswa panyika. Vazhinji havazotombowani chidimbu chechingwa, uye havazotombovi netafura yekudyira asi vachava vapoteri panyika nokuda kwekutenda kwavo mandiri.

Musazvirega muchiedzwa nemana yavo yenhema iyo ichada chiratidzo chechikara kuti mupinde munzanga yavo yakatsauka. Nokuti chiratidzo hachizoregererwi, uye vose vanowira mumusungo uyu havazoregedzwi; nokuti ndechenguva dzose kune vose vanoita sarudzo iyoyo yekupedzisira, nokuti vachange vaisa chisimbiso pamagumo avo uye magumo iwayo ndeekupedzisira. Saka musaedzwa nechiredzo, kana kuti muchapedzisira mava mumumbure uye makabatwa zvachose neshaya yenyu.

Ndiri kukutumai kuti muunganidze boka rangu, uye ndichakutungamirirai kwavari uye ndichavatungamirira kwamuri, uye muchavafudza nokuda kwangu, nokuti ndini mufudzi mukuru. Hamuzorambi rumwe rudzi kana nyika kana chero rumwe rudzi, nokuti chipikirwa chaYahweh chinomira, uye vose vanodana pazita raIshe vachaponeswa. Musave nemitsipa mikukutu semadzibaba enyu, uye ivai vanyoro kuizwi rangu. Nokuti madzibaba enyu akandiramba, saka pupurai zvivi zvemadzibaba enyu nezvenyu mukutendeuka kwandiri, uye ndichakuregererai uye ndichakudururirai rudo rwangu nenyasha, nokuti ndichaita kuti nzizi dzemvura mhenyu dziyerere kubva mudumbu renyu. Ndakakupai simba rezvishamiso zvikuru. Uye ndiri kukutumai kuhama dzenyu kuti dzitendeukire kwandiri, nokuti vakasara vachaponeswa; uye ndichaponesa Jakobho kubva paruoko rwevavengi vavo, nokuti ndakazvitaura uye zvichazadziswa.

Ini, Yeshua HaMashiach uyo Yahweh akatuma, ndini Mesia wechokwadi weIsraeri nenyika. Kunyange ini ndichatuma vaporofita vangu vaviri, kunyange avo Yahweh akabvuta kubva panyika munguva dzavo: nokuda kwechinangwa chakadaro vakatorwa, uye saizvozvo vachatumwa. Ndine zvinhu zvizhinji zvekutaura kwamuri uye zvinhu izvozvo zvichataurwa, uye ndichakugadzirirai mabasa enyu, uye muchandiitira mabasa makuru uye ane simba, kunyange mabasa amusina kumbofungidzira kuti anogoneka. Nokuti nguva dzakaipisisa dzinogara dzichiburitsa varume vakuru vaYahweh, kunyange varume vasingazoputsiki, nokuti muchazorora musimba rangu, uye ini handiputsiki. Nokuti ndizvo zvakandiita Yahweh, uye ndizvo zvandichakuitai. Saka inzwai izwi rangu, nokuti ndiri kudaidza uye ndichatuma ngirozi dzangu kunoisa chisimbiso kune vane 144,000 nechisimbo chaYahweh.

Ndichakuchengetedzai makachengeteka kubva kuchikara nenyangadzi yacho; ndichakuvigai, uye muchadyiswa kubva kumatenga kunyange mukazvida, saka musatya. Musatya nezveupenyu hwenyu, nokuti mubairo mukuru ndewenyu nokuda kwebasa nekutendeka, uye ndichakukomborerai nokuda kwekumira kwenyu.

Ndini Shumba yeRudzi rwaJudha, uye muchanzwa kuomba kwangu, nokuti ndiri kukudaidzai.

Ndini iye achaisa chisimbiso kune vane 12,000 kubva kurudzi rumwe nerumwe kunze kwaDhani naEfraimi.

Ndini iye achapwanya mwana weshumba achabuda kwaDhani.

Ndini iye achakunda chikara nemuporofita wenhema, uye ndichavakanda vari vapenyu mugungwa guru remoto.

Ndini iye achatungamirira vanhu vangu kumafuro manyoro kuburikidza nevafudzi vangu, nokuti ndini mufudzi mukuru.

Ndini iye achazadzisa zvipikirwa zvakaitwa kumadzibaba, nokuti ndizvo zvandakatumirwa.

Ndini iye achapwanya musoro wemaSatani, nokuti machinda enyika ino acharipira.

Ndini iye achatonga kubva pachigaro choumambo chaDhavhidhi, nokuti umambo hwangu hahuna magumo.

Ndini iye ari kuuya mumakore, uye ndichauya kunyika yangu uye ndichatora kutonga kwangu, uye handizomiswi.

Ini, kunyange ini, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndakatumira mashoko aya kune avo vandiri kudaidza. Saka inzwai mashoko angu mukuteerera, uye zivai kuti ndichasunungura nyika nenyika yose uye ndichatonga mukururama. Saka inzwai kudaidzwa kwangu, nokuti ndiri kuuya kwamuri uye ndichakudzidzisai nzira dzangu uye ndichakutungamirirai muchokwadi chose. Saka musaramba zvinyorwa zvangu, nokuti mashoko aMwari achadzoserwa kuna Israeri, uye nyika ichaziva kuti kuna Mwari muIsraeri, kunyange Yahweh, Mwari mukuru wekusingaperi, nokuti hakuna mumwe, uye hakuna akafanana Naye. Shalom! Shalom! Saka ivai murugare uye muve zvakanaka, nokuti ndichakuporesai mose zvizere avo vanoisa kutenda kwavo muna Yeshua HaMashiach.
