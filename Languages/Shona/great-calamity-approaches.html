Chapupu Chiviri: Kereke
Mumabasa aJohane Mubhabhatidzi
Kurume 11, 2024

Dambudziko Guru Raswedera

Shoko raIshe rakasvika kwandiri; ndapota teererai.

Inzwai izwi rangu, ndizvo zvinotaura Ishe weHondo! Vanhu vangu, dzokerai kwandiri, nekuti dambudziko guru rava kuda kuwira pamusoro porudzi rwenyu. Dambudziko guru riri pamukova penyu, uye dambudziko guru richasvika pamahombekombe enyu. Dzokerai kwandiri uye munditye, ndizvo zvinotaura Ishe weHondo. Mwari vatonga America pamativi maviri. Kuchava nokutongwa kunobva mukati uye kuchava nokutongwa kunobva kunze. Mukati muchava nokuzununguswa kukuru, kuzununguswa kukuru, uye America ichawa nesimba nokuzununguswa kukuru. Kunze, mauto ake auya kuzoba nokuraya nokuparadza zvose zviri munzira dzawo. Hapana tsitsi mumwoyo yavo. Vane pfungwa imwe. Uye vachakutorerai kuwanda kwenyu; vachakutorerai nhumbi dzenyu; vachatora vana venyu; uye nzara huru ichauya munyika yenyu. Uyo asingaurayiwi nehondo achaurayiwa nenjodzi; uyo asingaurayiwi nenjodzi achaurayiwa nezvikara zvesango; uyo asingaurayiwi nezvikara achaneta nenzara. Asi kunyange mudambudziko renyu, ani naani anodana zita raIshe achaponeswa.

Uye ndichaponesa vanhu vangu, kunyange zvakadaro nokutambudzika kukuru uye nomoto womunatsi. Uyai kwandiri, vanhu vangu, nguva ichiripo. Uyai kwandiri, Ishe Jesu Kristu, sezvo ndiri Ishe akarurama. Ndiri Mambo waMadzimambo. Ndiri Mufudzi akanaka, uye hakuna anogona kubvuta makwai angu muruoko rwangu. Uyai kwandiri! Uyai kwandiri nguva ichiripo, mureurure zvivi zvenyu; reururai zita rangu rine simba saIshe, nekuti hapana ruponeso mune rimwe zita. Nekuti ini, Jesu Kristu, ndakagadzirirwa ruponeso rwenyu rwandakatenga neropa rangu. Uyai kwandiri, hongu, uyai kwandiri murudo rwangu rwandinaro kwamuri. Musandiramba, asi uyai kwandiri, kuti ndigokwanisa kukuponesai nokukupai upenyu husingaperi kuitira kuti ndikomborere vana venyu. Uyai kwandiri, vanhu vangu, uye musaomesa mwoyo yenyu, sezvakaitwa namadzibaba murenje. Nekuti Ishe Mwari wenyu vazivisa kutonga kukuru pamusoro porudzi rwenyu, nekuti zvivi zvaro zvizhinji uye zvinotyisa.

Hongu, ndakabhadharira zvivi izvozvo nezvivi zvose zvevanhu, asi chipo changu nemaoko angu hazvingarambi zvakatambanudzwa nokusingaperi. Pazuva redambudziko, ruponeso ruchawanikwa nokutambudzika kukuru uye nedambudziko guru randichauyisa pamusoro penyika kuti ndiedze mwoyo yavanhu, uye uyo anotsungirira kusvika kumagumo achaponeswa. Musatevera munhu wechivi. Musatora chiratidzo chake uye musatora mushonga wake, nekuti mariri mune kurumwa nenyoka, uye hamungaporeswi pahuturu hwacho. Ani naani achatora chiratidzo chechikara achava nomugove wake mugungwa romoto pamwe chete nechikara nomuporofita wenhema. Hazvina kunyorwa here muShoko rangu Dzvene? Hamuna kuverenga here? Hamuna kunzwa here? Dzokerai kurudo rwangu uye muuye kwandiri, vana vangu, nekuti ndinokudanai muawa ino yokupedzisira, nekuti dambudziko guru riri pamukova penyu.

Ndinzwei, ndizvo zvinotaura Ishe weHondo:

Nekuti ndini iye, Mwanakomana woMunhu akarovererwa pasi paPondiyo Pirato, akafira zvivi zvenyu uye akamutswa pazuva rechitatu. Ndakamutswa uye ndakakudzwa.

Ndiri Ishe waMadzishe naMambo waMadzimambo.

Ndiri Mwanakomana woMunhu, Ishe akarurama, Ishe wechokwadi akatendeka uye wechokwadi, uye hapana mumwe wenyu anogona kubvutwa mumaoko angu.

Ndiri mufudzi akanaka, uye hakuna anogona kubvuta makwai angu muruoko rwangu.

Zvose zvandakapiwa naBaba, ndichazvipa kwaari, uye ndinoshuva kukupai kuna Baba musina mhosva, gwapa, kana mhaka, makapfeka mucheka muchena wakanyikwa muropa rangu. Nzira chete ipi neipi iyo munhu angamira pamberi pangu ndeyokugamuchira chibayiro changu chezvivi zvenyu. Musanonoka navatadzi! Musanonoka; musanonoka! Budai pakati pavo muzvitsaure pazvivi zvavo. Reururai zvivi zvenyu kwandiri uye mureurure zvivi zvamadzibaba enyu kwandiri, nekuti zvinopfuudzwa kwamuri. Rarirai upenyu hwandakakupai, uye fambai munzira yandakakugadzirirai, sezvo ndakakudanai nezita. Musatsaukira kuruboshwe kana kurudyi, asi teererai uye muve nepfungwa imwe kumashoko angu. Asi, vazhinji vacharamba kukoka kwangu, uye vazhinji vachaparara. Ivai nokutenda mandiri, sezvo rudo rwaMwari ruchaponesa vose vanodana zita raIshe. Ivai nokutenda mandiri, uye nzizi dzemvura mhenyu dzichayerera kubva mudumbu menyu sezvo zvivi zvenyu zvinoshambidzwa uye mweya wenyu unoporeswa. Uyai kwandiri nguva ichiripo; reururai zita rangu; ndiitei Ishe, nekuti hapana ruponeso mune rimwe zita rakapiwa pasi pedenga iro vanhu vangaponeswa naro.
