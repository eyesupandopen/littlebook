Chipupuriro Chiviri: Kereke
Mumabasa aJohane Mubhabhatidzi
Kurume 10, 2024

Vafudzi Vakatsauka

Ishe Mukuru, Jesu Kristu, vanoti kuvafudzi vakatsauka:

Munoti munotaura muzita raShe, ini, Jesu Kristu; asi munongoziva zita rangu chete uye hamurarami nenzira dzangu kana kunditaurira. Munovaka matangi akaputsika asingachengeti mvura yevanhu vangu, uye vanonzwa nyota. Uyezve, makashandira dumbu renyu nemakaro sezvamunofara mukuchiva kwemwoyo yenyu, uye munopa kugutsikana kwese nekuremekedza kunyama yenyu. Mumwoyo menyu makanzvenzverwa sezvamunofunga kuti munonditevera zvizere uye munondimiririra kuvanhu vangu, asi munotaura nhema dzisina musoro, nhema dzinofanira kukandwa mugungwa guru, mashoko asina kutenda asina kupiwa neni, mashoko erima neekuzvisimbisa asi imi musina simba zvachose, musina simba rekutungamira uye musina simba rekufudza. Tengai mushonga wemaziso kwandiri muzvizodze. Vhurai nzeve dzenyu muteerere shoko rangu.

Njodzi huru nekuparadzwa zviri pamusoro pehure guru, uye nguva dzaro dzakatarwa nemazuva aro akaverengwa. Kutongwa kwaro kuchauya kamwe kamwe uye kusina mushonga. Asi iwe wakati mumwoyo mako, ndiri mumiririri waMwari. Ndiri mufudzi akarurama. Ndiri mbiri yaMwari, uye nemabasa emaoko angu ndiri muenzaniso mukuru werudo rwaMwari. Iwe benzi! Ungafudza sei vanhu vangu iwe usingatombozivi ini, Jesu Kristu, Mufudzi wechokwadi uye akarurama? Ibvai kwandiri, sezvo ndisingakuzivei! Mava mushandi wemubhadharo, anoshuvira kuyemurwa nevanhu. Makanganwa rudo rwenyu rwekutanga. Tendevukai mudzokere kwandiri. Ndimirirei. Fudzai vanhu vangu mumasaga nemadota ekutendevuka. Munoshandira dumbu renyu uye mungatengesa Shoko rangu kuti muwane pfuma yenyu yehumbimbindoga sezvamunodzidzisa vanhu vangu muchivi.

Zvirokwazvo, kutongwa kuri pamusoro porudzi rwenyu, uye kunyange ndaizokutsaurai kuti muve vanhu vangu, munotiza inzwi rangu uye munoramba nzira dzangu, asi mukunyengedzwa kwenyu munozviti muri vatsvene uye muenzaniso kuboka. Isai pfungwa dzenyu nenzeve dzenyu painzwi rangu. Tendevukai muuye kwandiri, kuti ndigokununurai pamwe nevanhu vanokuteverai; ndinoda kukununurai panjodzi nenhamo! Avo vanotora ruponeso sechitupa chekutadza vachatakura kutongwa kwavo, uye mabasa enyu achapiswa. Vanorarama saizvozvo vachakohwa rima rekunze. Munoti makapfuma, asi muri muhurombo. Munoti mune simba, munorayira simba raMwari nemashoko enyu, asi mashoko enyu akanaka nekutaura kwenyu kwakapfava hazvina simba zvachose. Handina kukudanai here? Handina kukupai chinangwa here? Asi munonditi handipo muKereke yangu, asi munozviita kuti musave pasi peUshe hwangu. Izvo zvinodyarwa nemunhu, ndizvo zvaachakohwa.

Kutongwa kuchauya kamwe kamwe parudzi rwenyu, uye imi munogona kumira neni kuti ndikununurei, kana kuti muchamira nevanotongwa mogoverana navo zvivi zvavo nematambudziko ekutongwa anouya. Chivi chine mhedzisiro kunyange kune avo vane chisimbiso chaMwari mupenyu. Zvirokwazvo, nguva yave pedyo apo kutongwa kuchatanga paimba yaMwari. Zvisukei pazvivi zvenyu muropa rangu rinoyananisa! Tendevukai pachena panzira dzenyu. Tendeukirai kwandiri nekuti nguva yave kuda kupera. Munhu wese anozviti chinhu iye asiri chinhu anonyengedzwa uye akabatwa mumumbure wezvakaipa zvakapofumadza maziso avo nekuvhara nzeve dzavo. Tendevukai mudzokere kwandiri nemwoyo wenyu wese nguva isati yapera.

Ndini Ishe ane tsitsi. Ndakapiwa zita riri pamusoro pemazita ose, uye ndini mukuru pane vose, ndakaurayiwa nyika isati yavambwa. Ndini mutongi akarurama, Ishe wenyu nemudzikinuri, Kristu Jesu! Ndini mwanakomana waMwari akaitwa panyama asi akatora chikamu chete sezvo ndakaramba ndisina chivi. Ndakarovererwa pasi paPondiyo Pirato ndikafa sePaseka yenyu isina chivi. Hakuna munhu akatora upenyu hwangu, asi ndakahupa pachena. Ndakamutswa pazuva rechitatu ndikakwira kudenga. Ndini Mwanakomana waMwari akauya panyama. Ndini Mufudzi akarurama asina kurasikirwa neuyo akapiwa kwandiri. Inzwai mashoko angu uye musaapokane. Musaisa mashoko angu parutivi sekunge chinhu chakareruka.

Vose vandinoda, ndinovaranga. Vose vandinoda, ndinovatuka. Kana Baba vachikubatai sevanakomana, saka makaropafadzwa, nekuti baba vanoranga vana vavo kuti vamire vakasimba uye vakarurama uye vagamuchire mubairo mukuru. Mibairo yekutendeka nekorona zhinji zvichaiswa pamisoro yavo nekuda kwekuteerera. Tendeukirai kwandiri nemwoyo wenyu wese munzwe mashoko angu. Ndinoona mabasa enyu, asi ndinoda mwoyo wenyu wese kupfuura mabasa enyu. Yambirai vanhu vangu nezvedutu riri kuuya, kuti vazvigadzirire uye vazvichenure pakupukunyuka kwangu kukuru kuri kuuya, pandichaunganidza Muviri wangu kwandiri ndovaviga mumba maBaba vangu kubva pazuva renjodzi. Ibvai pachivi mudzokere kwandiri, Jesu Kristu, vanhu vangu, sezvo ndakakudanai uye ndinoshuva kuita kuti kusarudzwa kwenyu kuve nechokwadi.

Ndini Ishe ane tsitsi, uye ndakapiwa zita riri pamusoro pemazita ose. Ndini mukuru pane vose, ndakaurayiwa nyika isati yavambwa. Ndini mutongi akarurama, Ishe wenyu nemudzikinuri, Kristu Jesu!

Ndini Mwanakomana waMwari akaitwa panyama asi akatora chikamu chete sezvo ndakaramba ndisina chivi.

Ndini Mwanakomana waMwari akauya panyama.

Ndini Mufudzi akarurama asina kurasikirwa neuyo akapiwa. Ndini zvose izvi nezvimwe zvakawanda.
