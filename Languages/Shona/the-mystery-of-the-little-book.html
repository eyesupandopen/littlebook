Tsanangudzo yeMabhanan'ana Manomwe

Chakavanzika cheBhuku Diki

Kubva kuna Yeshua HaMashiach:

Tarirai, chakavanzika chebhuku diki! Nekuti ngirozi nomwe dzinotumira mabhanan'ana adzo, uye mabhanan'ana ataura manzwi awo. Ndiwo chakavanzika chebhuku diki, nekuti bhuku diki rakanga rakanamwa. Nekuti izwi rakabva kudenga, richiudza Johane Muratidzwi kuti: Namira zvinhu izvo zvakataurwa nemabhanan'ana manomwe, uye usazvinyora. Tarira, wakadya bhuku diki, nekuti rakaziviswa, uye rakanga richitapira sehuchi munzeve dzako pawakanga wakarara, asi rakavava mudumbu mako izvo zvakakumutsa kubva pachiratidzo; uye tarira, wamuka kubva muhope dzako! Tarira, chakavanzika chebhuku diki chauya kuzoziviswa panguva ino, nekuti ndicho chakavanzika chekupedzisira chichaziviswa munguva yangu yenyasha, iro bhuku diki rinotapira rinovava. Nekuti manzwi emabhanan'ana achaziviswa, uye zvino yava nguva yekuzivisa, nekuti chakavanzika chebhuku diki chikuru, kunyange riri bhuku diki. Nekuti bhuku diki iroro rakaziviswa kuna Johane Muratidzwi, uye zvino ndicharizivisa kwauri, nekuti kuziviswa kwaro kwava pedyo. Inzwa izwi rangu, mwenga wangu, uye rerekera nzeve yako kwandiri, Kristu Jesu, nekuti nguva yasvika. Nekuti hapachina nguva.

Ndini mwanakomana waMwari akauya panyama, uye ini, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndatumira mashoko aya.

Kubva kuna Yahweh, Mwari wekusingaperi:

Izwi remabhanan'ana manomwe RINOTAURA! Zvigadzirirei, imi marudzi, nekuti kutonga kukuru kuri pamusoro penyu, uye mashoko emabhanan'ana manomwe aziviswa. Rerekerai nzeve dzenyu kunzwa mashoko echakavanzika cheBhuku Diki. Nekuti mipumburu ichasunungurwa, uye kusunungurwa kwezvisimbiso kuri kuuya pamusoro penyika yose, iko kutonga kukuru nedambudziko. Nekuti zvivi zvenyika munhuhwi unonyangadza mumhino dzangu, uye ini, kunyange Yahweh, ndichachenesa nyika, uye ndichaparadza chinhu chose chakaipa; uye mwanakomana wangu, wandiri kutuma, achatonga mukururama panyika, uye rugare rukuru ruchava rwevakatendeka vandakadzivirira kubva pazuva rakaipa. Uye rugare rukuru ndicharupa kune vose vanodana zita raJesu Kristu, kunyange Yeshua HaMashiach, nekuti vose vanodana zita raIshe vachaponeswa. Uye murugare nekuchengeteka ndichavatungamirira, uye mukururama ndichavafukidza, uye muutsvēne hwechokwadi vachandikudza; nekuti ndakavakudza, uye vachava neutsva hweupenyu.

Saka ndinouya nekururamisira kwangu nehasha dzangu kuti ndiripire avo vanoita zvakaipa nenzira dzavo dzine tsvina, saka ani naani anondiramba uye achisarudza zvakaipa, ngaarambe akaipa, uye hasha dzaMwari dzinogara! Uye ani naani achava akarurama nevose vanobva pane zvakaipa pavanodana zita raIshe vachava nemubairo mukuru; saka ngavave nezvibereko zverugare zvekururama, uye vachava nenhaka huru mumwanakomana wangu, Kristu Jesu, ane mubairo wavo muruoko rwake. Saka ani naani achava akaipa, ngaarambe akaipa. Uye ani naani anotendeukira kukururama uye achidana zita raIshe, ngaarambe akarurama, nekuti zuva richazvizivisa. Tarirai, chinhu chandichakuudzai chisati chaitika, nekuti zviporofita zvava kuzadziswa, uye hakuchazovi nekunonoka. Nekuti nguva dzenyasha nengoni dzava kusvika kumagumo, uye nguva yehasha huru yava kuda kusunungurwa panyika, uye hakuna kumbova nenguva yakadai panyika, kunyange mumazuva aNoa, uye hakuchazombovi nenguva yakadai zvakare. Nekuti ini, kunyange ini, ndakapika nezita rangu uye ndakazivisa zvinhu izvi. Chero chandinotaura ini, kunyange Yahweh Mwari wekusingaperi, hachigoni kuchinjwa uye chichaitika. Nekuti mumazuva engirozi yechinomwe, paachatanga kuridza, kunyange chakavanzika chaMwari chinofanira kupedziswa.

Ndini Iye Wandiri, saka vaudze kuti Mukuru Iye Wandiri atumira mashoko aya, kunyange Yahweh, Mwari mukuru wekusingaperi!

Chipupuriro neZvakaitika, naWilliam Brooks

Uye zvino kuti tiwane kunzwisisa kwakadzama kwechikamu cheshoko rino apo Yeshua HaMashiach akati, "Tarira, wakadya bhuku diki, nekuti rakaziviswa, uye rakanga richitapira sehuchi munzeve dzako pawakanga wakarara, asi rakavava mudumbu mako izvo zvakakumutsa kubva pachiratidzo; uye tarira, wamuka kubva muhope dzako." Imwe nguva yapfuura mumwedzi wapfuura kana zvakadaro, handizivi zuva chairo, asi ndakanga ndakarara pakati pehusiku, uye ndakamuka ndiri muchiratidzo kubva muhope dzangu (kana kuti chaive chiroto chakajeka uye ndakamuka pakutanga). Izwi raiverenga mashoko mupfungwa dzangu nemweya wangu, uye ndaiziva kuti raive izwi raIshe, uye aiverenga munzeve dzangu mashoko andaisakwanisa kunzwisisa nekuti mashoko aingoyerera asingamiri. Asi nemashoko iwayo kwakauya rugare rukuru rwakagara mandiri zvachose. Mashoko acho ainyanyotapira munzeve dzangu, uye ndakanga ndazorora. Zvino pakarepo, ndakabuda mumamiriro ezvinhu iwayo nechirungurira chakanyanya, dumbu rangu rakanga rakamonyoroka, uye ndakasvetuka kubva pamubhedha, ndichikachidzwa uye ndichikosora. Ndakaenda pasi ndikanwa mvura yakawanda, iyo yakabatsira zvishoma asi haina kugadzikisa dumbu rangu. Ndakaramba ndakamuka kwechinguva mushure meizvozvo, asi ndaisaziva kuti zvese izvi zvairevei.

Uye kuchipupuriro, apo Yahweh anoti, "Izwi remabhanan'ana manomwe RINOTAURA!" Musi waChivabvu 7, 2024, ndakava nezuva rakaoma zvikuru, uye ndakauya kumba ndikaenda kunorara mangwanani-ngwanani. Ndakanga ndarara ndanonoka husiku hwapfuura nekuti ndakanga ndatadza kurara, izvo zvinowanzoitika kwandiri. Pakanga paine dutu rine mvura zhinji pandakarara, asi mheni nemabhanan'ana zvese zvaive kure kure. Ndakarara kuti ndiende kunorara kuma7 o'clock husiku ihwohwo, zvinova mangwanani kwandiri, uye ndakabatwa nehope dzandakafunga kuti dzaive hope. Handizivi kana ndakamborara chaizvo, asi ndakapinda muchiratidzo kana mamiriro echiroto akajeka, uye ndakanga ndichinyatsoziva nezvedutu raive richiitika. Mashoko nemanzwi zvaibva kwandiri kubva kwaiita sekunge kwakasiyana-siyana, uye zvaive kwese kwese. Handirangariri zvirimo zvemanzwi akasiyana-siyana, kunze kwerimwe chete. Raive izwi rakasiyana zvikuru, uye mamwe manzwi ese akanyarara. Izwi rakati, "Izwi remabhanan'ana manomwe RINOTAURA!" Apo mashoko iwayo akataurwa, ndakabva ndamutswa nekutinhira kwebhanan'ana kwainzwika zvakanyanya kupfuura zvandati ndambonzwa. Ndakagara pamubhedha wangu, ndakashama muromo chose.

Pandakaudza mudzimai wangu izvi zuva rimwe kana maviri gare gare, taive pavharanda redu repamberi. Akandibata ruoko ndokunditungamirira tichipoterera imba tichienda kuchivanze chekuseri. Akandiudza kuti iye nemwanakomana wedu vaive muimba yekutandarira apo kuvhundutsa kwebhanan'ana ikoko kwakarova. Kupenya kwemheni kwakanga kwavhenekera nekuzunza imba yekutandarira. Zviri pachena, yakanga yarova munzvimbo yepedyo yechivanze chedu chekuseri, icho chidiki, sezvo tichigara muguta. Asi hatina kuona chero kukuvara kupi zvako. Ndakaona zvinoitika kana mheni ikarova nyika nechisimba kana chimwe chinhu chiri panyika, uye inosiya kuparadza pane imwe nhanho kana imwe. Akaona kupenya kwemheni, uye isu tose takanzwa kuvhundutsa kwebhanan'ana kwainzwika zvakanyanya, asi hapana kana kukuvara kwakawanikwa kupi zvako.


Chikumi 12, 2024

Ishe vakandizivisira chaizvo kuti basa iri chii, uye rine zita rakadai. Ndakagamuchira zvirimo zveBhuku Diki riri mumaoko eNgirozi ine simba muna Zvakazarurwa 10, iyo yakapfeka gore uye muraraungu waive pamusoro payo uye chiso chayo chaive sezuva uye tsoka dzayo seshongwe dzemoto. Izvi zvinomiririra nguva nesungano dzinoenderana, izvo zvese zvinoguma pachiso chaJesu Kristu, akaberekwa ega waYahweh, uye mufananidzo waMwari wekusingaperi.

Muraraungu chiratidzo chakapiwa pasungano yaNoa iyo Yahweh yaakaita naNoa mushure memafashamo. Gore neshongwe dzemoto zvinomiririra sungano yaMwari naMosesi. Chiso chaYeshua HaMashiach chinopenya sezuva uye ndiyo sungano itsva. Mashoko manomwe aya ane kutonga pamusoro pevakawa nevamwe kubva pamberi pemafashamo aNoa kusvika kumagumo, mune izvo zvichiri mune ramangwana kubva pazuva ranhasi.
