Chirabwe cheVatatu!

Inzwai izwi raYeshua HaMashiach, vadikani vangu, uye huyai kwandiri kuti munyaradzwe uye muwane zororo murudo rwangu kwamuri, vana vangu vaduku. Nokuti makanga makatendeka pazvinhu zvishoma, uye zvino ndichakuitai mutongi wezvakawanda pamunopinda mumufaro waShe wenyu. Kutongwa hakusi kwenyu, vakatendeka vangu; kunyange vose vari muna Kristu navose vakavata muna Kristu vachamutswa kuupenyu hutsva pakarepo uye nokubwaira kweziso. Nokuti zvirokwazvo, ndichaburuka kubva kudenga nokudanidzira, nenzwi romutumwa mukuru, uye nehwamanda yaMwari: uye vakafa vari muna Kristu vachamutswa kuupenyu husingaori uye imi muri vapenyu uye makasara muchatorwa pamwe chete navo mumakore uye saizvozvo muchava naShe wenyu asingaperi uye makafanana naye nokusingaperi. Muchashandurwa pakarepo kubva pamuri parizvino pakuwa kwenyu kuva chisikwa chakadzikinurwa zvizere chisingaori, kunyange kusikwa patsva somunhu mumwe mutsva, uye sezvandiri, saizvozvowo ndizvo zvamuchava; uye sezvazvakanyorwa, saizvozvo zvichaitwa pakarepo.

Sei muchitendera mwoyo yenyu kuti ikunetserei, vana vangu? Sei muchitendera mashoko omupomeri achikunetsai muchifunga kuti hakuna kubviswa, kana kuti hamuna kukwana kunyasha dzangu dzinoponesa? Ndinokurairai, vana vangu, kuti murambe muchifamba nokutenda kwete nokuona; uye ndini iye akakuitai vakakodzera, kunyange vose vari muna Kristu uye vane ukama hunoponesa naShe wavo akarurama, saka musakahadzika! Kunyange zvazvo musina kundiona nemaziso enyu enyama, munoziva zvakakwana kuti ndini Ishe wenyu mupenyu nomuponesi, uye mandiri mune kuzara kwoupenyu noruponeso. Zvirokwazvo, zvirokwazvo, uyo anotenda kwandiri haazovi nenzara kana nyota zvakare.

Nokuti ndini chingwa choupenyu, uye vose vanodya havazovi nenzara zvakare; uye ndini iye anobva kwaari nzizi dzemvura mhenyu dzinoyerera mukuwanda kwose noutsva hwoupenyu, uye uyo anomwa pairi haazovi nenyota zvakare. Asi imi mose muchaitwa vapenyu muutano hwakakwana, muwirirano yakakwana, nomubatanidzwa wakakwana naYahweh, naShe wenyu mupenyu nomuponesi, nokuti ndini Kristu Jesu, akakutengai muropa rangu rinoyananisa, uye zvirokwazvo hamuna mutengo. Uye saizvozvowo ndizvo zvakanga zvakaita muripo usingaverengeki wandakapa nokusingaperi ini pachangu segwayana raMwari, uye saizvozvo ndakapa chibayiro changu nokuda kwenyu somupristi mukuru nokusingaperi maererano nourongwa hwaMerkizedeki uye ndakabhadhara rudzikinuro rwenyu muropa rangu risina chivi.

Uye saizvozvo nokusingaperi-peri hakuchina kudikanwa kwechibayiro chechivi, nokuti kana ropa renzombe nerembudzi, namadota etsiru, zvichisasa avo vasina kuchena, zvichitsvenesa pakuchenesa nyama, Ko ropa raKristu, iro rakazvipa risina mhosva kuna Mwari kuburikidza noMweya usingaperi, haringatonyanyi kuchenesa hana dzenyu pamabasa akafa, kuti mushumire Mwari mupenyu here? Uye izvo zvisingagoni kuitwa nechibayiro chemhuka, ropa rangu rinoyananisa rakakwanisa kuzviita kamwe chete nokusingaperi-peri, sezvo ndakayananisira zvivi zvevanhu kamwe chete nokusingaperi-peri, kunyange nokuda kwavose vanosarudza kugamuchira ruponeso muzita rangu, nokuti hakuna rimwe zita rakapihwa pasi pedenga iro vanhu vangaponeswa naro. Saka musatenda nhema dzavo, nokuti shato, chikara, nomuprofita wenhema vachasimuka, uye vachatsvaka kubatanidza zvitendero zvose zvenhema zvenyika pasi pomureza mutsva, kunze kwokuti Jesu Kristu haazobvumirwi pakati pavo, kungava muzita kana muchiito, uye vakapiwa simba rokurwa navatsvene nokuvakunda. Zvirokwazvo, zvirokwazvo, handina mugove kana fungu navo, uye ndini iye achavaparadza zvachose, nokuti ndinouya kuzotonga nokurwa mukururama.

Uye zvino ndinotaura kune avo vachauya kwandiri panguva yokutambudzika kwaJakobho, kunyange vatsvene venguva yokutambudzika avo vachawana mubayiro mukuru nenhaka isingaperi muna She wavo asingaperi uye anogara nokusingaperi naMesia, kunyange Yeshua HaMashiach. Uye saizvozvo zvakanyorwa sechokwadi chisingaperi, uye saizvozvo ndinotaura nokusingaperi uye nokusingaperi-peri kuti munofanira kupinda napasuo rakamanikana: nokuti suo rakakura, nenzira yakafara inoenda kukuparadzwa: uye vazhinji varipo vanopinda napo, Nokuti suo rakamanikana, nenzira yakamanikana inoenda kuupenyu, uye vashoma varipo vanoiwana. Saka ndinokurayirai, vana vangu vaduku, kuti murambe muri munzira yakatwasuka neyakamanikana uye mudzivise kuparadzwa kwemapenzi, kunyange avo mazita avo asina kumbonyorwa muBhuku roUpenyu kubva pakuvambwa kwenyika, nokuti vanoda nzira dzokuparadza, uye vanoda kusarurama, uye vanosarudza kunyengedzwa kukuru pane kururama nechokwadi.

Nokuti vachamhanya-mhanya, uye hondo huru iri kuuya panyika, uye kuparadzwa kukuru kuri pedyo uye kwava pamusoro penyu; njodzi, yambiro yechimbichimbi 911, uye zuva riri kuswedera pedyo uye rava pamusoro penyu. Uye kubva mumadota ehondo vachasimuka, uye kubva mumadota ehondo vachazviratidza kuvanhu zvakare, uye vanhu vachashamiswa navakawira vachiyemura zvikuru manomano avo avakashandisa kuroya vakarasika kubvira pakutanga. Vachabatanidza zvitendero zvavo zvenhema, uye vachakuudzai nhema zhinji dzinotaurwa nemiromo yemweya yenhema yaantikristu, nokuti vanaantikristu vazhinji vakapinda munyika.

Chii chinonzi antikristu, vana vangu? Antikristu anopikisa Kristu, muponesi wechokwadi naMesia, uye zvakare munzvimbo yaKristu; nokuti ndini Yeshua HaMashiach, uye hakuna mumwe. Uye saizvozvo nhema dzavo dzakatozadza nyika sezvavakagadzirira mapoka evanhu kuti vateerere vatatu vasiri vatsvene, kunyange shato, chikara chinobva mugungwa, nechikara chinobva panyika, uyo ariwo muprofita wavo wenhema. Asi Johane akanyorei pamusoro penyaya iyi mutsamba yake yokutanga? Handina kukunyorerai, nokuti hamuzivi chokwadi: asi nokuti munochiziva, uye kuti hakuna nhema dzechokwadi. Ndianiko murevi wenhema, kunze kwaiye anoramba kuti Jesu ndiye Kristu? Ndiye Antikristu, anoramba Baba, noMwanakomana. Ani naani anoramba Mwanakomana, iyeyo haana Baba: asi uyo anobvuma Mwanakomana, anavo Babawo.

Uye saizvozvo ndizvo, kuti ini, Jesu weNazareta, ndini nzira, chokwadi, noupenyu; uye hapana zvachose anouya kuna Yahweh kunze kwokuburikidza neni. Ndini suo roga, uye ndini nzira yoga inoenda kuna Yahweh, Mwari mukuru asingaperi; nokuti saizvozvo Yahweh akazivisa uye saizvozvo Yahweh akaronga nokusarudza kubva pamberi pokuvambwa kwenyika mukuziva kwake kusingaperi nouchenjeri. Ndianiko pakati penyu anogona kurayira Yahweh, Mwari mukuru asingaperi? Kunyange zviuru gumi zvakapetwa nezviuru gumi zvichikomberedza chigaro chake choumambo, haadi murayiri; uye ndiYahweh, Mwari mukuru asingaperi, anoita zvinhu zvose maererano nokurayira kwokuda kwake kuti imi muve kurumbidzo yokubwinya kwake avo vakatanga kuvimba naKristu.

Saka nzwisisai kunyengera kuri kuuya kwakatouya pamusoro penyika. Nokuti vashumiri vaSatani vanotaura vachipikisa chokwadi, uye vari kudanidzira kunze nokudanidzira zvikuru kuti zvitendero zvose zvenyika zvinotungamirira kuna mwari mumwe chete zvisinei nokuti vanobva kuchitendero chipi, zvisinei nezvavanotenda, kuti mamwe mazita ose akapiwa navakawira ndeechokwadi uye anotungamirira kuna mwari wechokwadi, asi kushhato yoga ndiko kwavanotungamirira. Zvirokwazvo, zvirokwazvo ndinoti kwamuri, musatenda nhema dzavo, uye musatombovashuvira rombo rakanaka avo vanotenda upenzi hwakadaro. Nokuti suo rakakura rinoenda kukuparadzwa, iro rinotungamirira kugungwa guru remoto rinopfuta, uye Satani anotsvaka kusundidzira vakawanda sezvaanogona munzira yake inoenda kukuparadzwa kusingaperi, nokuti ndizvo zvakaita nhaka yake, uye ndizvo zvakaita ruvengo rwake kwamuri mose. Musatenda nhema dzavo dzokubatanidza zvitendero, uye itizai mubatanidzwa wavo usiri mutsvene, nokuti kuguma kwawo imirazvo inopisa zvikuru yebhuruu sezvo sarufa inopfuta ichikunguruka nokupfuta nokusingaperi-peri, uye hakuna achapukunyuka avo vakasungwa nokukandwa mumoto.

Nzwisisai, vana vangu, kuti mazita avo ose anotungamirira kuna mwari mumwe chete, kunyange Satani, nokuti havasi vamwari asi vanyengeri vanonyengera. Uye saizvozvo vachasimudza mireza yavo yokusiyana-siyana, uye vachataura kuti mazita mazhinji anotungamirira kuna mwari mumwe chete. Uye kune rumwe rutivi vakarurama nokuti nzira zhinji dzinotungamirira kuna mwari wenyika ino, uyo kutongwa kwake kwasvika uye uyo kurangwa nokuparadzwa kwake hakunonoki. Uye kune rumwe rutivi, vanorevera nyika nhema uye vanotsvaka kunyengera vose vachatevera nzira dzavo dzinoparadza munzanga yavo itsva iyo ichasimuka kubva mumadota ehondo iyo ichada chiratidzo kuti upinde.

Musatora chiratidzo chavo, nokuti vose vanochiita vakatukwa nokusingaperi uye vakashandurwa nokusingaperi-peri. Uye vanoreva nhema zvinorwadza kwazvo kuti mazita ose anotungamirira kuna Mwari wechokwadi, kunyange Yahweh, uyo ari Mwari asingaperi waIsraeri, nokuti Yahweh akazvisarudzira Jakobho kuti imi muve vanhu vake chaivo. Uye saizvozvo Satani anogara achitsvaka kunyengedzera nzira dzaMwari mumwe chete wechokwadi, kunyange El Shaddai, uyo ari Mwari Wemasimbaose! Uye saizvozvo akaita zvitendero zvizhinji zvenhema zvakagadzirirwa kunyengedzera nzira dzaYahweh, Mwari asingaperi wekusingaperi kwose, uye zvitendero zvose izvi zvenhema zvinoguma nokuparadzwa kusingaperi kune avo vakabatwa saizvozvo. Nokuti muteyi upi anoteera musungo pamberi peshiri?

Uye saizvozvo vakatopa zvikomborero nokuda kwavose, kunyange zvitendero zvose, uye vakatotsvaka kubatanidza zvitendero zvose pasi pemireza yavo yokubvumirana kwezvitendero, nokuti saizvozvo nzira dzavo dzokushandisa pfungwa dzevanhu dzinoitwa. Kune rumwe rutivi vanopa pfungwa huru; kune rumwe rutivi vanopa pfungwa yavo inopikisa; uye kubva mukukakavadzana kunodzorwa vanounza kubatana kwavo, nokuti vanotsvaka kubatanidza tsika dzose, marudzi, nehama nendimi munzanga yavo yadhiabhorosi kuti vaparadze vose vanotevera nzira dzavo dzinoparadza. Uye saizvozvo nzwisisai, izvo zvakakombama hazvigoni kuitwa zvakatwasuka, uye mashoko avo akanaka nenhaurwa dzakanaka zvakagadzirirwa kukwezva manzwiro evanhu nokuchiva norudo rwenhema rwavanoti utsvene. Nzwisisai kuti munhu pachake akaora zvachose uye haagoni kutwasanudza nzira yake, uye hapana chakanaka mumunhu, asi munhu akaodzwa nokusateerera kwaAdhamu uye zvikurukuru nenhema nounyengeri hwenyoka, iyo yandichapwanya musoro wayo zvirokwazvo, nokuti Yahweh akazivisa kubvira pakutanga kuguma kwenyoka iyoyo inoreva nhema inodya guruva renyika.

Saka zvakanga zvakadaro, ndizvo zvazviri, uye ndizvo zvazvichava, kuti hapana chinhu chitsva pasi pezuva. Uye sezvo nyika inobva panyasha ichienda kukutongwa, njodzi inongoerekana yava kuda kuwira, uye pakarepo ndizvo zvazvichava. Saka ndinopa zano vose vanoverenga mashoko aya kuti vanzwisise kuti mashoko aya haana kukuwanai netsaona, asi akatumwa naYahweh pachake nenzira yomwanakomana wake mumwe chete akaberekwa, kunyange Yeshuah HaMashiach, akataurwa pamwe chete navaranda vangu vaporofita. Uye ndini, Jesu weNazareta, kunyange Yeshua HaMashiach, akabhadhara mutengo usingaperi woruponeso rwenyu noruponeso rwavose vachazouya kuupenyu husingaperi murudo rwangu rusingaperi uye rusingagumi urwo Yahweh akandisika kuti ndizadzise. Ndinoti kwamuri zvimwe chetezvo sezvandakataura kubvira pakutanga: kuti ini, Jesu Kristu, kunyange Yeshua HaMashiach, ndini nzira, chokwadi, noupenyu, uye hakuna munhu anouya kuna Baba asi nokwandiri; uye hakuna rimwe zita rakapihwa panyika, narini, iro vanhu vanofanira kuponeswa naro, uye mamwe mazita ose zvisinei nokuti kunyengedzera kwacho kunogutsa sei haatungamiriri kuna Mwari wechokwadi, kunyange Baba veZveneko uyo asina kusanduka kana mumvuri wokutendeuka. Saka nzwisisai, vana vangu vaduku, kuti imi muri zveneko zvenyika izvo Yahweh akabereka, kunyange kusikwa patsva mandiri, Kristu Jesu Ishe akarurama asingaperi, uye ndakaitwa mweya unopa upenyu.

Tarirai, ndinokuratidzai chirabwe chichapa uchenjeri kune vakachenjera uye chichadzivirira mapenzi navasina kudzidza kuti vasabatana navaya vakatarisana nokuparadzwa munzira dzavo. Vatatu, vaviri, mumwe: uye pazvitendero zvizhinji zvenyika pane zvitatu zvinotonga zvichatungamirira kumumwe wenhema asiri mutsvene, uye zvitatu zvinoita sevavengi uye hondo zhinji dzavakarwa. Asi, pazvitatu, zviviri zvakabatana pachivande, uye chimwe chichashandisa chimwe, uye zvose zvinotungamirira kukuparadzwa. Nokuti pazvitatu, zviviri zvichapandukira chimwe zvine utsinye, kunyange mutsvene wavanovenga asi wavanokundwa naye zvachose. Uye saizvozvo mutsvene achaunza kutongwa kuimba yaMwari kutanga, nokuti kutongwa kunotanga paimba yaMwari uye zuva rokutongwa rasvika.

Kunyange zvazvo mutsvene akunda, zviviri pazvitatu zvichatendeuka kuti zviparadze chimwe, asi kuparadzwa ikoko hakugoneki, uye chimwe chakatowana kukunda kwakakwana. Asi saizvozvo ndizvo zvichaita kunyengera kwokuti zviviri pazvitatu zvichapandukira chimwe uye zvichapomera nhema mumiromo yezvapupu zvizhinji zvenhema, uye vacharwa navatsvene nokuvakunda. Uye nenhema zhinji dzokumhura vanopararira panyika, asi chimwe, kunyange mutsvene waMwari, akatovakunda uye achapwanya musoro wavo zvachose. Asi zviviri zvichaonekwa sezvakakunda pamusoro pechimwe chavanotsvaka kuparadza, uye pazviviri vacharwa nechimwe, kunyange kuparadzwa kwavo kwekupedzisira kusinonoki. Uye saizvozvo musungo uchateererwa nyika, kuti kubva mumhirizhonga huru nehondo vachazvibatanidza kuitira kuti vatatu, vaviri, mumwe ave vaviri, mumwe, vatatu nokuti vachazvibatanidza muchitendero chimwe chenhema chinotungamirirwa navatatu vasiri vatsvene, kunyange shato, chikara chinobva mugungwa, nechikara chinobva panyika, uyo ariwo muprofita wavo wenhema.

Saka ndinokuratidzai mhinduro yechirabwe changu chezvitatu. Zvitatu izvi zvitendero zvitatu zvikuru zvinotonga nyika iripo zvino; uye pazvitatu izvi, chimwe chete ndicho chechokwadi, icho chitsvene chaMwari; nekuti ini, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndiri mutsvene waMwari akakunda wakaipa uye ndiri Ishe wemadzishe naMambo wemadzimambo. Handikundiki, uye ndakapiwa zita riri pamusoro pemazita ose, kuti muzita raJesu Kristu, iye Yeshua HaMashiach (nekuti ndiri muponesi weveDzimwe Ndudzi neveJudha pamwe chete) mabvi ose apfugame, vari kudenga nepanyika uye kunyange pasi penyika. Saka zvitendero zviviri zvenhema zvinooneka zvichipikisana asi zvakaedza kuzviita shamwari nechechokwadi, uye zvirokwazvo Satani akatevedzera kereke yangu yechokwadi nemhombwe yenhema inokudza nekunamata mambokadzi wekudenga. Saka vanotsvaga mubatanidzwa wenhema uye vakasanganisa nhema zhinji muchokwadi, asi nhema nechokwadi hazvisangani: unogona kuva nenhema kana kuti une chokwadi: hapana imwe sarudzo.

Uye sezvo kereke yangu yechokwadi inotorwa kuenda kumatenga uye mudzivisi obviswa, saizvozvowo kereke yenhema ichaparadzwa, uye saka imwe yacho inobviswa zvachose. Zvitendero zviviri zvasara zvichasimuka mukukunda kwenhema, uye vachava nerunyararo rwenhema, uye vachatsvaga kubatana kwenhema kuti vabatane vakarasika, vachitovimbisa upenyu husingaperi, utano hwakakwana, uye shato ichavimbisa zuva idzva. Uye ichabatanidza zviviri izvi kuita chitendero chisina kuzivikanwa nemadzibaba avo asi chichaonekwa chakafanana nekuti vanhu havazivi nhoroondo yavo yechokwadi. Uye nekubatanidzwa uku kwekuparadza vachaenda kumoto usingaperi, nekuti vose vanobatana navo ndevaya vanofarira kusarurama, uye kwete izvozvo chete, asi vaya vanoda nekutenda nekugadzira nekunamata nhema nekuti nzira dzavo ndedzenhema uye dzakatsauka uye nzira dzavo dzinotungamirira kurufu rwechipiri.

Uye saizvozvo vachatsausa nyika, iyo ichavashamisira, ichiti, ndiani akafanana nechikara? Uye chimwe chikara chichapiwa simba rechimwe chikara, uye chikara chinobva mugungwa chichavatonga maererano nekutonga kwacho, uye chikara chinobva panyika chichatonga nyika maererano nekutonga kwacho, uye vachatonga nyika mukusarurama. Uye pazviviri zvasara, zvichabatanidzwa kuita chimwe. Asi, nzira dzavo rufu, uye imomo ndimo makateyiwa musungo, nekuti muteyi weshiri angateya sei musungo shiri ichiona? Vose vanotevera nzira dzavo dzekubatana vachabatanidzwa, asi munhaka yevakawira chete; uye vose vanozvibatanidza kuburikidza nekutsva kwenyoka yavo vachagovana nhaka yewakawira mugungwa guru remoto unopfuta nesarufa mavachatsva nekusingaperi uye honye yavo isingafi. Saka kubatana kwavo kuzere nemisungo yakavanzwa neuchenjeri, nekuti muteyi weshiri angateya sei musungo shiri ichiona?

Saka ndinopa zano kune vose vanoverenga mashoko aya uye vose vane maziso ekuona nenzeve dzekunzwa nemoyo wekunzwisisa, kuti ruponeso neupenyu husingaperi naMwari wechokwadi, iye Yahweh pachake, hazviwanikwi mune rimwe zita kunze kwezita remwanakomana mumwe oga waMwari, iye Jesu Kristu, anovawo Yeshua HaMashiach; uye ndiri muponesi wevanhu vose, irwo rudzi rwaAdhamu rune ropa rinogovanwa nerudzi rwose rwevanhu. Uye mururimi rupi zvarwo, uye kubva kurudzi rupi zvarwo, vose vanogona kuuya vakasununguka uye vagamuchire chipo changu cheruponeso rusingaperi nekudana kuzita rangu dzvene kusvika kumagumo. Nekuti ndiri Ishe nemuponesi wenyu, musamborega zita rangu richibva pamiromo yenyu, uye ndichakuponesayi zvakakwana.

Musabatana nekubatana kwavo, nekuti havazivi zvavanonamata, uye vakabatwa nenhema dzichavatungamirira kukuparadzwa kwavo kusingaperi. Uye saka zvirizvo kuti ruponeso haruwanikwi mune rimwe zita, uye hapana rimwe zita rakapihwa apo vanhu vangaponeswa uye kuti vanhu vaswedere kuna Yahweh, nekuti uyo asina Mwanakomana haana Baba. Uye saka ndichatsigira nekusimbisa vose vanodana kuzita rangu dzvene kusvika kumagumo. Uye muchandiitira mabasa makuru, uye ndinokutumai kuti mude Ishe Mwari wenyu nemoyo wenyu wose, nemweya wenyu wose, nepfungwa dzenyu dzose, nesimba renyu rose uye kuti mude muvakidzani wenyu sezvamunozviita uye kuti mubatsirane nekudanana munguva dzakaipisisa dzave kuda kuitika panyika.

Saka zvitatu, zviviri, chimwe, apo zviviri zvichamukira chimwe, uye zviviri zvasara zvichabatanidzwa kuita chimwe uye zvichatongwa nezvitatu zvisiri zvitsvene, izvo zvichatonga vaya vane mazita asina kumbonyorwa muBhuku reUpenyu kubva pakutanga kwenyika. Asi musabatana navo, imi vatsvene vangu; nekuti zviri nani kurasikirwa neupenyu hwenyu kuti mugozohuwana pane kuda upenyu hwenyu asi mogozorasikirwa nahwo nekusingaperi mukuparadzwa kusingagumi. Tarirai! Kupatsanurwa kwekupedzisira kwave kuda kutanga uye nguva dzasvika. Musavatya, imi vadiki vangu, asi vimbarai naIshe nemuponesi wenyu wekusingaperi. Ndiri mubatsiri wekusingaperi munguva dzekutambudzika, uye ndichaponesa zviuru nezviuru panguva yekutambudzika kwaJakobho, uye ndichaponesa vakasara veIsraeri, uye hapana anogona kukubvutai mumaoko angu.

Nzwisisai kuti tariro yenyu itariro isingaperi, uye handina kukusiyai musina tariro, uye ndakakusiyirai muenzaniso wekusingaperi. Nekuti zvakanyorwa pamusoro pangu muna VaHebheru: Tichitarira kuna Jesu muvambi nemupedzisi wekutenda kwedu, uyo nekuda kwemufaro wakanga wakaiswa pamberi pake, akatsungirira muchinjikwa, akazvidza kunyadziswa, uye akagara kuruoko rwerudyi rwechigaro cheushe chaMwari. Uye saka nzwisisai kuti muenzaniso wangu wekuteerera unodzokororwa semuenzaniso wekusimbisa kutenda kwenyu kuti mugone kutsungirira kusvika kumagumo, sezvandakatsungirira kusvika kumagumo pandakataura mashoko iwayo ekupedzisira pamuchinjikwa: Zvapera. Uye saka Mwari akandimutsa kubva kuvakafa nekuti zvakanga zvisingagoneki kuti rufu rundibate, nekuti rangu iropa risina mhosva.

Uye saka ndakakupai tariro huru, imi vadiki vangu, kuitira kuti mugone kuisa pfungwa dzenyu pamufaro wakaiswa pamberi penyu nekuti matambudziko enguva yekutambudzika kwaJakobho haaenzaniswi kana kuti haakodzeri kutaurwa maererano nemufaro usingaperi wakaiswa pamberi penyu. Naizvozvo vari pamberi pechigaro cheushe chaMwari, uye vanomushumira masikati neusiku muTemberi yake: uye iye anogara paChigaro cheUshe achagara pakati pavo. Havachazovi nenzara, kana nyota zvakare, uye Zuva harichavapisi, kana kupisa kupi zvako. Nekuti Gwayana, riri pakati pechigaro cheushe, richavafudza, uye richavatungamirira kuzvitubu zvipenyu zvemvura: uye Mwari achapukuta misodzi yose pamaziso avo. Vachava Vapristi vaMwari, naKristu, uye vachatonga naye makore ane chiuru.

Uye ndiyo tariro yenyu, vadikani vangu, kuti mugone kutsungirira kusvika kumagumo. Uye yenyu itariro isingaperi nekuti El Shaddai, Mwari Wemasimbaose, achasika denga idzva nenyika itsva munogara kururama, uye muchapiwa mukana wekupinda muguta dzvene iroro rakagadzirirwa nekushongedzwa naYahweh pachake semwenga akagadzirirwa chikomba, uye muchadya kubva pamuti weupenyu. Uye sezvazvakanyorwa: Uyo anokunda, achagara nhaka yezvinhu zvose, uye ini ndichava Mwari wake, uye iye achava mwanakomana wangu.

Ini, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndini ndakatuma mashoko angu kuburikidza nemuranda wangu, uye kuburikidza nemuranda wangu mashoko angu atumwa kuti ateererwe nevose vanoverenga. Uye ndakazarura vhangeri risingaperi, nekuti nguva dzekuzarurwa kwaro dzasvika.

Ndini iye anouya kuzotora vari muna Kristu uye ndichanunura vakatendeka mukereke yangu kubva mumoto wemuchenesi nehasha dzinouya.

Ndini iye anoparadzanisa gorosi nemasora, uye hapana kana mumwe wenyu anogona kubvutwa muruoko rwangu.

Ndimirirei, imi vatsvene vangu venguva yekutambudzika, uye uyai kwandiri, imi Israeri, nekuti ndiri Yeshua HaMashiach, Mesia wenyu wechokwadi, uye ndakakupai tariro huru nesimba guru. Uye vazhinji venyu muchandiitira mabasa makuru, uye hapana kana mumwe wenyu achashayiwa mubairo, nekuti kunyange zvinhu zvidiki zvikuru zvamunoita munguva dzekutambudzika kukuru zvinounza mubairo usingaperi, uyo mbiri yawo matambudziko enyu asina kukodzera kutaurwa; saka danai kuzita rangu rine simba, vanhu vangu! Ruponeso nemubairo mukuru zviri muruoko rwangu kune avo vanokunda, nekuti ndizvo zvinovimbiswa naYeshua HaMashiach kune vakatendeka.

Tarirai, nguva dzasvika!
