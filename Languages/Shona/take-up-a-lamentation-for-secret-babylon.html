Kubva kwamuri, Bhabhironi Rakavanzika, kwakauya kuparadzwa kwakaunzwa kunyika dzekunze neMauto Matsvuku apo vaEdhomu vakazviita vadiwa vevashandi nembiriso yavo ine chepfu, kunyange gwaro ravo rekumhura Mwari rakatsigirwa neNhoo Tsvuku. Bhabhironi Rakavanzika neutande hwaro hwezvikwereti rakatsigira nekutsigira Mauto Matsvuku muzviito zvawo kubva pakutanga, uye Bhabhironi Rakavanzika rave chikamu cheboka ravo kubva pakutanga apo Nhoo Tsvuku yakazvisimbisa pasi rose. Kunyange mweya wemoto wedhiragoni wakabva kuNhoo Tsvuku, nekuti ndivo vakawa vanofamba pakati penyu.

Kubva kuBhabhironi rakavanzika ndiko kwakadzidziswa vatungamiriri vavo ve“chimurenga” muchivande nekuti rinogara pamusoro pemvura zhinji, uye vakatumwa muchivande kunyika dzekunze, kunyange kuGogi neMagogi uye chimurenga chavo chaive chikuru, chine chisimba, uye chine hutsinye uye zvino vafukidza nyika. Vakaparadzira mbiriso yavo yaSatani munyika dzekumabvazuva uye vakaparadza zvachose zvese munzira dzavo. Vakabuda muBhabhironi Rakavanzika uye vave dambudziko panyika, uye nguva yose iyi Bhabhironi rakavanzika rakareva nhema uye hure guru rakazviti rinotaura nezvangu uye rakazvidaidza kuti muKristu. Kupidigura uku kwechisimba kwehurongwa hwekare kwakapararira pasi rose uye zvino kwave kusvika padanho rekupedzisira, uye vachabvumirwa hurongwa hwavo, asi kungoshandira zvinangwa zvekusingaperi zvaYahweh, nekuti muchokwadi vakawa vanodedera uye kutya kunobata mwoyo yavo yakaipa nekuti vanoziva kuti kutongwa kwavo kwagara kuripo kwave pavari. Nekuti iwe Bhabhironi Rakavanzika muhupombwe hwako neuhure hwako, unovata nemuvengi.
