Uye kunyange, munyengetero unoshanda womunhu akarurama uchibatsira zvikuru, nzwisisai kuti hapana vatungamiriri venyika vakapiwa ruponeso rwenyika kusvikira ndauya neuto guru raMadzimambo naVapristi vasingaperi vachatonga nyika mukururama nomurayiro wangu nokuti ndiri Ishe wemadzishe naMambo wamadzimambo uye ndiri Mupristi Mukuru nokusingaperi maererano neUrongwa hwaMerkizedeki.
