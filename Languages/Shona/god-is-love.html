Chapupu Chiviri: Kereke
Mumabasa aPauro Muapostora
Kubvumbi 30, 2024

Mwari Rudo!

Vana vangu, nzwisisai chimiro chenyu chechokwadi, nokuti chenyu ndicho chimiro chaicho chakapiwa naMwari mandiri, Kristu Jesu, nokuti Mwari rudo! Asi, rudo rwaMwari haruna kufanana nerudo rwemunhu nenzira imwecheteyo iyo nzira dzaMwari dzisiri nzira dzemunhu, uyewo; asi munodzidza nzira dzaMwari mandiri, nokuti ndini mudzidzisi wenyu nomufudzi wenyu. Nokuti Mwari wakada nyika nokudaro, kuti wakapa Mwanakomana wake mumwe woga: kuti ani naani unotenda kwaari, arege kufa, asi ave noupenyu husingaperi. Uye muchiito ichi chaMwari achindipa ini, Jesu Kristu, mwanakomana wake mumwe woga nokuda kwezvivi zvevanhu, rudo rwake runoratidzwa mukubatana kwakakwana mandiri. Nokuti ndiri waBaba uye ndine chimiro chimwechete nomweya mumwechete, nokuti Mwari mweya, uye avo vanomunamata vanofanira kumunamata mumweya nomuzvokwadi, nokuti ini naBaba tiri vamwe mumweya, mukubatana, uye muchinangwa.

Nzwisisai kuti rudo rwaMwari haruna kufanana nerudo rwemunhu, nokuti rudo rwemunhu runowanzova nemitemo, uye munhu akawa anowanzoratidza rudo rwake mukupa nokugamuchira zvakanaka, uye rudo rwemunhu runopinda mukati kuti rubatsire munhu anoda, nokuti rwakadaro rudo rwemunhu. Rudo rwaMwari kazhinji runoratidzwa mukupa pachena kuti rubatsire mumwe, uye kushandira wokwako pasina muripo dzimwe nguva, uye kushanyira chirikadzi, kuporesa vanorwara, kusimbisa Muviri waKristu. Saka ndinokukumbirai, vana vangu, kuti mudzidze kwandiri Kristu Jesu, nokuti uyo wandiona waona Baba, uye kunyange musati mandiona nemaziso enyu, munguva pfupi iri kutevera muchadaro.

Nokuti wakaropafadzwa uyo asina kuona asi achitenda, uye makandiona mumweya nomuzvokwadi, uye makandiona munzira dzangu uye norudo rwandinotumira uye nebasa rinoitwa navashumiri vangu nokuda kwenyu. Saka munondiziva uye makandiona, uye pamunofamba mumweya waKristu wakazvarwa mukati, munenge muchifamba murudo zvechokwadi, nokuti ndizvo zvakaita chimiro changu chandakakupai, uye ndizvo zvakaita Baba vangu vakapa chimiro chavo kwandiri: Tarirai, rudo rwakadini rwatakapiwa naBaba, kuti tinzi vana vaMwari: naizvozvo nyika haitizivi, nokuti haina kumuziva. Uye zvakare kubva mutsamba yokutanga yaJohani: Rudo ruri muna ikoku, kuti handiti isu takada Mwari, asi kuti iye wakatida, akatuma Mwanakomana wake kuti ave rudzikinuro rwezvivi zvedu. Vadiwa, kana Mwari akatida saizvozvo, nesuwo tinofanira kudanana.

Vana vangu vaduku, muri vana vaMwari mupenyu, iye Yahweh, nokuti nenzira imwecheteyo Yahweh akadyara bindu kumabvazuva muEdeni, akadyara mbeu dzomweya woukomana mukati menyu, nokuti ndini murimi wake anotarisira mweya yenyu. Nokuti makasikwa patsva mandiri, uye makasikwa murudo, uye rudo rwaMwari ruri muchimiro chenyu chaicho. Nzwisisai kuti rudzikinuro rwenyu ruzere ruri pamusiwo pamuchaziva sezvamunozivikanwa. Mwenga wangu, ndine zvakawanda zvokukutsanangurirai nokukudzidzisai nokukuzarurirai pamusoro penyu uye nezvinangwa zvamakaitirwa utsvene kuti muzviite, nokuti chave chiri chakavanzika mumwoyo maBaba kusati kwava nokusikwa, uye munguva pfupi muchanzwisisa.

Saka ndiani ungakuparadzanisai norudo rwaMwari? Dambudziko here, kana kutambudzika, kana kutambudzwa, kana nzara, kana kushama, kana njodzi, kana munondo? Kwete, imi munopfuura vakundi mandiri, Kristu Jesu, uye rudo rwangu rwuri kuunzwa kukuzara kwakakwana mamuri, Mwenga wangu, nokuti nguva dzaswedera uye munguva pfupi muchaziva. Rudo rwaBaba vangu harukundiki, nokuti iye haakundiki, nokuti Mwari rudo, uye rudo ndicho chimiro chaicho chaYahweh nokuti Mwari rudo. Humwe hwenhema dzakawanda dzaSatani dzaanoshandisa kukutsausai ndehwekuratidza rudo seusina simba, husina kuzvipira, hunozvitsvakira, uye husina maturo: Nokuti idzi ndidzo nzira dzomunhu wenyama.

Zvirokwazvo, Satani ari mukuramba, nokuti ndiye akaitwa asina maturo, uye rudo rwaBaba vangu harugoni kukundwa, nokuti hakuna akafanana naye! Mandiri rudo rwake rwakakwaniswa munyama, nokuti rwakanga ruri rudo rwangu kuna Baba vangu nokwenyu, Mwenga wangu, kuti ndakazvipa ndiri rudzikinuro rusina chivi; nokuti munodiwa zvikuru. Uye zivai kuti muri mboni yeziso raYahweh, uye imi, Mwenga wangu, muri mboni yeziso rangu, uye murudo ndakakudzikinurai kubva muruoko rwomuvengi zvakakwana, pasina chinoshayikwa, muropa rangu rorudzikinuro. Vakadzikinurwa naJehovha ngavataure kudaro, avo vaakadzikinura paruoko rwomuvengi!

Vana vangu, sezvo Mwari achikudai saizvozvo, saka mune chisungo kwamuri chokugamuchira rudo rwake nokugamuchira mashoko ake pamusoro penyu norusununguko rukuru rwakaitwa naBaba muchibayiro changu sePasika yenyu yakakwana. Vazhinji venyu hamunzwisisi kupera kwechisikwa chandakakuitai mandiri, Kristu Jesu, uye mumweya wandakasika mukati menyu, nokuti mweya iwoyo une simba, uye rudo; nokuti ndicho chimiro chaicho chaBaba, nokuti Yahweh rudo. Tarirai, hakuna rudo rukuru runopfuura urwu, kuti munhu ape upenyu hwake nokuda kweshamwari dzake. Uye ndakapa upenyu hwangu nokuda kweshamwari dzangu, uye nokuita izvozvo ndakakuitai hama dzangu uye ndakakuitai vanakomana vaMwari: Tarirai, rudo rwakadini rwatakapiwa naBaba, kuti tinzi vana vaMwari: nokuda kwechikonzero ichi nyika ino haikuziviyi, nokuti haimuzivi.

Inzwai Mashoko angu andakapa Johani mutsamba yake yokutanga, uye muainzwisise zvakanaka. Yahweh akakupai nyasha dzepamusoro-soro pamusoro pomupomeri napamusoro paavo vanokupai mhosva, uye akaita kuti kupomera kwavo kuve kusina hwaro pamusoro penyu, nokuti makatengwa muropa rangu risina chivi, uye makasunungurwa pamhosva dzose mubasa rangu rorudzikinuro. Hamuna kuverenga here zvandakaudza vaRoma maJubheri mazhinji apfuura aya? Ndianiko uchapomera vakasanangurwa vaMwari mhosva? NdiMwari unoruramisa: Ndianiko unopa mhosva? NdiKristu wakafa, zvirokwazvo wakamutswa, uri kuruoko rworudyi rwaMwari, unotirevererawo. Hamuna kunzwa here uchapupu hwevaHebheru, apo izwi ravo rinopupura nezvoupristi hwangu husingachinji? Naizvozvo unogonawo kuponesa zvakakwana avo vanouya kuna Mwari naye, achiona kuti anogara achirarama kuti avareverere.

Inzwai mashoko aya, uye ngaapinde mumwoyo menyu, uye inzwai izwi randakapa kereke yeRoma: Nokuti wakamuita kuti ave chivi nokuda kwedu, iye asina kuziva chivi, kuti isu tive kururama kwaMwari maari. Mashoko aya anoshamisa zvikuru kwamuri here? Muchazvibvumira kunzwisisa here, kana kuti muchadzivisa nzeve dzenyu mutsika dzenyu dzisina maturo nouchapupu hwenhema husina maturo hwamunobatisisa, hwakapiwa navaprofita venhema vasina maturo vakagadzirirwa kukutsausai? Chinhu chakaoma zvikuru here kugamuchira kuti makaitwa kururama kwaMwari mandiri, Kristu Jesu?

Ndinoda kuti munyarare kwazvo mupfungwa nomumwoyo menyu uye murege izwi rangu richinyaradza mweya wenyu uye munzwe mashoko angu. Pfavai mwoyo yenyu uye putsai kuzvikudza kwenyu; nokuti ndine chimwe chinhu chokukuudzai chichakusunungurai pauranda kana mukangotenda mashoko chaiwo andava kuda kukupai zvakare. Nokuti izwi revaRoma rinotaura kwamuri pamunoverenga, saka verengai munzwisise; uye mukunzwisisa, gamuchirai chokwadi chekumira kwenyu mandiri, Kristu Jesu. Uye mashoko angu ndeaya: Naizvozvo zvino hakuchina kupiwa mhosva kuna avo vari muna Kristu Jesu, vasingafambi nenyama, asi nomweya. Nokuti murayiro womweya woupenyu, muna Kristu Jesu, wakandisunungura pamurayiro wechivi norufu. Musarega maziso enyu achitarisa mashoko, asi verengai rimwe nerimwe zvishoma nezvishoma uye nokutenda, kutenda mandiri, nokuti ndakataura mashoko aya pamusoro penyu, vana vangu; uye ini, Kristu Jesu, ndakakusunungurai pakupiwa mhosva kwose mandiri. Hamunzwisisi here kuti makaitwa kururama kwaMwari? Ndinzwisisei uye munzwe izwi rangu uye muteerere mashoko angu: Ndianiko unokupai mhosva? NdiKristu wakafa, zvirokwazvo wakamutswa, uri kuruoko rworudyi rwaMwari, unotirevererawo.

Vana vangu, handina kukudanirai kwandiri mukururama noutsvene hwechokwadi kuti ndikupei mhosva, uye munofanira kunzwisisa chokwadi: Hamuna kodzero yokuzvipa mhosva, uye vazhinji venyu munodaro, uye upenyu hwenyu hunotambudzwa nokuda kwekuzvivenga uku kwakaiswa pamusoro penyu nomuvengi. Ndibvumirei kuti ndiputse ngetani idzi dzenhema dzomuvengi, uye munzwe mashoko angu uye muatende, nokuti ndeechokwadi, uye akanyorerwa imi kuti muve vakasununguka zvechokwadi mandiri. Nokuti ndakakusunungurai pakupiwa mhosva kwose, uye muvengi nomupomeri wehama wakakandirwa pasi: Zvino kwauya ruponeso, nesimba, noushe hwaMwari wedu, nesimba raKristu wake: nokuti mupomeri wehama dzedu wakakandirwa pasi, uyo waivapomera pamberi paMwari wedu masikati nousiku.

Nzwisisai, vana vangu, kuti makarurama, uye kuzvipa mhosva ndiko kuregeredza mashoko angu akakupai Baba; saka musava vasingatendi, asi ivai nokutenda kukuru mumashoko angu, nokuti ndeechokwadi, uye hakuna uchapupu hwechokwadi hwakapiwa. Nzwisisai musiyano pakati pekuzvipa mhosva nomweya wangu unoshanda mamuri kuti ukupupurirei chivi; nokuti ndakaudzei Johani mutsamba yake yokutanga: Nokuti kana mwoyo wedu uchitipa mhosva, Mwari mukuru kumwoyo wedu, uye anoziva zvinhu zvose. Vadiwa, kana mwoyo wedu usingatipi mhosva, ipapo tine chivimbo kuna Mwari. Hamunzwisisi here kuti mweya wangu wandakasika mukati memumwe nomumwe wenyu, Mwenga wangu, unokupupurirai zvivi zvenyu kunyange mushure mokunge mazvarwa patsva mandiri, Kristu Jesu, Ishe wenyu?

Hamuna kuverenga here uchapupu hwangu hwakanyorwa mumashoko akanaka aJohani pamusoro pomunyaradzi? Uye kana asvika, acharanga nyika pamusoro pechivi, napamusoro pokururama, napamusoro porutongo. Pamusoro pechivi, nokuti havatendi kwandiri. Pamusoro pokururama, nokuti ndinoenda kuna Baba vangu, uye hamuchandioni. Pamusoro porutongo, nokuti muchinda wenyika ino watongwa. Vana vangu, munyaradzi akakupupurirai chivi, uye makatendeukira kwandiri, uye mukuzvarwa kutsva uye musimba romweya wandakasika mukati menyu, kunyange mweya iwoyo woukomana, kunyange Kristu mamuri tariro yokubwinya, ndakakuitai kururama kwaMwari mandiri. Naizvozvo, hamuchapupurirwi chivi, asi munopupurirwa kururama pamunotadza, kunyange pamunodzokera kunzira dzenyu dzekare, nokuti nyoka inoda kutsausa vanhu vangu yobva yaunza kupomera pamberi paBaba vangu napamberi pangu pamusoro pemabasa ake asina umwari, uye ndini murevereri wenyu.

Nokuti zvakare, uchapupu hwaJohani mutsamba yake yokutanga hunotaura pamusoro penyaya yacho: Vana vangu vaduku, zvinhu izvi ndinokunyorerai, kuti murege kutadza. Uye kana munhu upi noupi akatadza, tine gweta kuna Baba, Jesu Kristu wakarurama: Uye iye ndiye rudzikinuro rwezvivi zvedu: uye kwete zvedu chete, asiwo rwezvivi zvenyika yose. Vana vangu, ndini gweta renyu, uye makarurama mandiri, uye makarurama nokusingaperi-peri, nokuti kururama kwakazvarwa muchimiro chenyu chaicho. Saka ivai nomwoyo munyoro kwandiri, uye ndichakuratidzai zvivi zvenyu nezvikanganiso zvenyu, kunyange tsika dzenyu dzakaipa nenzira dzenyu dzekare dzakaipa dzamunoyedzwa kudzokera kwadziri dzimwe nguva. Nokuti kana mwoyo wenyu uchikupupurirai, tendeukirai kwandiri mureurure zvivi zvenyu, nokuti rugwaro runotii: Asi kana tichifamba muchiedza, sezvaari iye muchiedza, tinowadzana, uye ropa raJesu Kristu Mwanakomana wake rinotinatsa pazvivi zvose. Kana tichiti hatina chivi, tinozvinyengera, uye chokwadi hachisi matiri. Kana tichireurura zvivi zvedu, iye wakatendeka, uye wakarurama kuti atikanganwire zvivi zvedu, uye atinatse pakusarurama kwose.

Vana vangu, ndakakutongai makarurama mandiri, uye Yahweh akakusunungurai pamhosva dzose mandiri; sei musingazvibvumiri kutenda mashoko angu ane nyasha norudo? Nokuti rudo rwaMwari runoratidzwa maari pamwe chete norudo rwangu kuMwenga wangu uye kunyange kunyika yose, nokuti ani naani anoda anogona kuuya, ingouyai musuo usati wavharwa nokuti nguva yapera. Dzokerai kwandiri zuva nezuva uye muzvinatse muropa rangu rorudzikinuro, nokuti chibayiro changu chakakwana uye ndechenguva imwe chete nokusingaperi: Nokuti vaHebheru vanotaura zvakajeka pamusoro pangu: Uyo ari kupenya kwokubwinya, uye chimiro chakanyorwa chomunhu wake, uye achitakura zvinhu zvose neshoko rake rine simba, wakazvinatsa zvivi zvedu, uye anogara kuruoko rworudyi rwoUkuru panzvimbo dzapamusoro-soro. Vana vangu, nzwisisai kuti munyaradzi akarangawo nyika pamusoro porutongo, nokuti muchinda wenyika ino watongwa, uye mutongo wake wakakodzera. Asi kusvikira paminiti ino anotsvaka kukukweverai pasi mukupiwa mhosva kwake kwakakodzera, saka anouya kuzokupai mhosva; saka musateerera mashoko ake uye rambai nhema dzake.

Vana vangu vaduku, fambai murudo rwaMwari mandiri, Kristu Jesu ishe wenyu. Idai Baba uye mundide nokuchengeta mirayiro yangu, nokuti Chokutanga Johani chinotii? Uyo unoti, ndinomuziva, uye asingachengeti mirayiro yake, murevi wenhema, uye chokwadi hachisi maari. Asi ani naani unochengeta shoko rake, maari zvirokwazvo rudo rwaMwari rwakakwaniswa: naizvozvi tinoziva kuti tiri maari. Uyo unoti unogara maari, anofanirawo kufamba saizvozvo, sezvaakafamba iye. Ndinokurayirai, Mwenga wangu, kuti murambe muchifamba sezvandakafamba uye muchengete mirayiro yangu, nokuti ndaigara ndichiita kuda kwaBaba vangu, uye saizvozvowo munofanira kuedza kugara muchiita kuda kwangu, nokuti kwangu ndiko kuda kwaYahweh. Mukuchengeta mirayiro yangu muchafanira kutendeukira kuhama, nokuti Chokutanga Johani chinotii? Naizvozvi tinoziva kuti tinoda vana vaMwari, kana tichida Mwari tichichengeta mirayiro yake. Nokuti urwu ndirwo rudo rwaMwari, kuti tichengete mirayiro yake, uye mirayiro yake hairemi. Inzwai izwi rangu uye mutevere mashoko angu kusvikira pakuteerera, uye rambai muchida hama dzenyu uye mushumire murudo, kwete nokuda kwekuwana kwakashata; nokuti mubayiro wenyu uri mumaoko angu, kwete mumaoko aavo vamunoshandira.

Ngakurege kuva nechinhu chinoitwa nokukakavara kana kuzvikudza; asi mukuzvininipisa kwepfungwa mumwe nomumwe ngaarangarire mumwe achimupfuura.

Ndini mudzidzisi wenyu nomufudzi wenyu.

Ndini waBaba uye ndine chimiro chimwechete nomweya mumwechete.

Ndini kururama kwaMwari, uye ndakakuitai kururama kwaMwari.

Ndini iye wakaruramiswa munzira dzake.

Handina chivi, uye ndakakururamisai mandiri, Kristu Jesu.

Ndini iye anogara uye nokusingaperi-peri akaitwa mutsvene, nokuti ndohwangu Ushe hwaMwari, uye ndakakuitai vatsvene nomweya woukomana.

Ndini iye akapa donhwe rake rokupedzisira reropa pamuchinjikwa kuti ndikunatsai pazvivi zvose, nokuti hakuna chivi mumweya woukomana wandakakupai.

Ndini murevereri wenyu.

Ndini gweta renyu.

Mirai makagadzirira, Mwenga wangu, nokuti nguva dzokudzorera dzaswedera, uye kuonekwa kwangu kwava pedyo kupfuura zvamunofunga.
