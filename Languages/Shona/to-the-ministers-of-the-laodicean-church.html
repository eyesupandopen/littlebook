Chivabvu 06, 2024

Kuvashumiri veKereke yeRaodhikiya: Inzwai Mashoko Angu!

Mwenga wangu neKereke yangu, ndinzwirei murudo rwangu kwamuri, vana vangu, uye rerekai nzeve dzenyu kukutaura kwangu. Inzwai Mashoko angu, imi vashumiri vangu veRaodhikiya, kunyange imi vandakapa zvipo. Nokuti ndakapa zvipo kuvanhu, uye ndakapa vamwe kuva vaapostora, nevamwe vaporofita, nevamwe vaevhangeri, nevamwe vafudzi, nevadzidzisi. Ndakakupai zvipo izvi kuti vatsvene vakwaniswe, nokuda kwebasa roushumiri, nokuda kokuvakwa komuviri waKristu. Nokuti chii chandakaudza vaapostora vangu nevadzidzi muzvinyorwa zvemashoko akanaka aJohani: Uye tarirai, ndichatumira chipikirwa chaBaba vangu pamusoro penyu. Asi garai muguta reJerusarema, kusvikira mapfekedzwa simba rinobva kumusoro.

Uye muMabasa evaApostora vangu makanyorwa kuzadziswa kwechipikirwa ichi: Zvino zuva rePendekosti rakati rasvika, vakanga vose pamwe chete panzvimbo imwe. Pakarepo kwakabva kudenga kutinhira, kwakaita semhepo inovhuvhuta nesimba, ikazadza imba yose mavakanga vagere. Ipapo kwakaonekwa kwavari ndimi dzakaparadzana, dzakaita somoto, ikamhara pamusoro pomumwe nomumwe wavo. Vose vakazadzwa noMweya Mutsvene, vakatanga kutaura nedzimwe ndimi, sezvavakapiwa kureva noMweya. Uye kuvambwa kweKereke yangu kwakanyorwa zvakadaro murugwaro kuti muzive kuzadziswa kwechipikirwa chikuru ichi chemashoko akanaka, kuti ndakakuzadzai nesimba rinobva kumusoro, kunyange mukuzvarwa kutsva, kunyange mandiri, Kristu Jesu Ishe wenyu. Uye ini ndiri musoro womuviri, iwo Kereke: ndiri kutanga, dangwe kubva kuvakafa, kuti pazvinhu zvose ndive wokutanga: Nokuti zvakafadza Baba kuti kuzara kwose kugare MANDIRI.

Vashumiri vangu veRaodhikiya, kuzara kwangu hakugari mumakereke enyu akaitwa nemifananidzo yenyu, nokuti ndimire pamukova ndichigogodza! Vashumiri vangu veRaodhikiya, handina kuchengeta ndoga kuzara kwose kwaBaba kuri mandiri, asi ndakadurura mweya wangu ndikakuitai vane simba, uye ndakakuisai chisimbiso neMweya Mutsvene wechipikirwa. Hapana chinogona kuputsa chisimbiso ichocho, kunyange muchigona kufamba muchipinda mukuramba kutenda kana mukasarudza kudaro. Asi chisimbiso chaBaba, kunyange Yahweh Mwari wekusingaperi, hachigoni kuputswa; nokuti hazvibviri kuputsa chisimbiso chaMwari, uye mune chokwadi mandiri kana makatendeka mandiri, nokuti zvipikirwa zvangu ndizvo hongu naamen!

Vashumiri vangu veRaodhikiya, zvipo zvangu kwamuri zvakaregeredzwa nemaitiro enyu ekudziya, uye makadzima mweya wandakakupai, uye munoramba kuvhura zvipo zvandakakupai, uye munorega zvipo zvangu zvisina kuvhurwa mukusava nehanya nekuregeredza. Iyi haisi nyaya diki yekurerutsa, nokuti chivi chakakomba kuverengero yenyu, uye hamuteereri izwi rangu. Hamuremekedzi mashoko angu, uye hamuremekedzi kana kudzidzisa nzira dzangu. Nokuti chii chandakaita kuti Pauro anyorere mutungamiri wechidiki weKereke yangu yakanga ichangosimwa panguva iyoyo, kunyange Timotio: Usazvidza chipo chiri mauri, chawakapiwa nohuprofita, nokuisa maoko omukuru. Zvirovedze pazvinhu izvi, uzvipe kwazviri, kuti zvionekwe kuti unobudirira sei pazvinhu zvose.

Haiwa, dai makanga maverenga mashoko angu nekunzwisisa! Haiwa, dai maizorangarira mashoko angu mukutenda, asi hamunzwisisi uye hamuna kutenda! Haiwa, dai maizoteerera mashoko angu, nokuti kuteerera kwandiri, Ishe Jesu Kristu, kuteerera kutsime reupenyu, uye mudumbu menyu muchayerera nzizi dzemvura mhenyu, nokuti ndakakupai mweya wangu. Asi panzvimbo yokuverenga mashoko angu, munotarisa zviratidziro zvenyu mukusateerera kusina njere; kubva pachiratidziro kuenda kune chimwe munoenda, muchivaraidzwa nechivi! Uye muri mapenzi zvikuru kufunga kuti kusateerera kusina njere kutenda kunoshanda mandiri, uye makanyengerwa. Makaregeredza zvipo zvandakakupai, kunyange munhu mutsva, uye panzvimbo pazvo makapedza nguva muchirovedza hunhu hwenyu hwemunhu wekare mukushaya maturo pamunenge muchitsvaka kugutsa nyama yenyu.

Panzvimbo pokuzvirovedza pazvinhu zvomweya, imi mukushaya maturo munorovedza miviri yenyu yakaora uye inoparara, nokuti kurovedza muviri kunobatsira kwenguva pfupi, uye miviri yenyu iri kuparara. Nokuti izwi rangu rinodzvova kubvira panguva yetsamba yaPauro kuEfeso: Abvisa munyama yake ruvengo, iwo Murayiro weMirairo iri muzvisungo, kuti aite maari, kubva pavaviri, munhu mumwe mutsva, achiita saizvozvo rugare. Ndinzwirei, vana vangu, nokuti ndinokudai uye ndine hanya nemweya yenyu, kunyange imi vangu veRaodhikiya vanodziya. Nokuti munofarira kufamba mumunhu wenyu wekare nenzira dzake, kunyange nyama yenyu yakaora, nokuti munoratidza nyama yenyu rudo rwose. Panzvimbo pazvo, munofanira kuisa pfungwa dzenyu nemoyo yenyu pandiri neshoko rangu rose: Nokuti munhu haararami nechingwa chete, asi neshoko rose rinobuda mumuromo maMwari. Nokuti nokuteerera pachena kumashoko angu (kunyange Shoko raMwari, nokuti ini ndiri Shoko rakaitwa nyama uye ndinogara pakati penyu nemukati menyu), munofamba mumweya kwete munyama.

Nokuti ndakaudza Kereke yeKorinde zvinotevera: Asi isu tose, nechiso chakafukurwa tichiona segirazi kubwinya kwaShe, tinoshandurwa kuva mufananidzo iwoyo, kubva pakubwinya kuenda pakubwinya, sezvinoitwa noMweya waShe. Vana vangu, tsamba dzangu kuKereke yangu (kunyange tsamba dzerudo dzandakatumira kuMwenga wangu) ndidzo girazi rezvokwadi zvenyu, nokuti zvokwadi zvenyu zvakavanzwa muchakavanzika chikuru chaMwari. Nokuteerera kumashoko angu nemirairo yangu muchafamba mumweya, uye hamuzopi kugutsikana kunyama yenyu yakaora. Rugwaro rwose runofanira kuremekedzwa nekudzidzwa, asi imi muri chakavanzika chakanga chakavanzwa muna Mwari uye zvino chafukurwa, uye munofanira kudzidza zvandakaratidza Pauro kuti munzwisise chisikwa chitsva chandiri kukuitai mandiri, Kristu Jesu.

Vashumiri vangu veRaodhikiya, pane mhosva huru pakati penyu. Marasikirwa neruremekedzo rwose rwemashoko angu, uye munoregeredza zvipo zvandakakupai kusvika pakuti hamuchatoporesi asi munoisa ungano yangu kupharmakeia nebanga; nokuti dai mainditeerera, ndaizobvumira kuti zvishamiso zviitwe nemaoko enyu. Asi munoremekedza mashoko evafudzi vakaramba kutenda kupfuura mashoko aYeshua HaMashiach, uye ungano yangu yeRaodhikiya inoripira kusateerera kwenyu; nokuti hamuchina hanya navo, asi panzvimbo pazvo munovarega vachitambura muchivi chisina simba. Vafudzi vakaramba kutenda vanozivikanwa nemashoko andakapa Johani mutsamba yake yokutanga: Vana vaduku, inguva yokupedzisira: uye sezvamakazvinzwa kuti Antikristu achauya, kunyange zvino kune vazhinji vaAntikristu, naizvozvo tinoziva kuti inguva yokupedzisira. Vakabuda kwatiri, asi vakanga vasiri vedu: nokuti dai vakanga vari vedu, vangadai vakaramba vainesu: asi vakabuda kuti varatidzwe, kuti vakanga vasiri vose vedu.

Hamunzwisisi here kuti avo vanodzorwa nemweya yaantikristu vanopa mumwe Jesu panzvimbo yomufudzi wechokwadi, kunyange ini, Kristu Jesu? Nokuti ava ndivo vavengi vemuchinjikwa waKristu, uye vanopesana neni, uye vanozviisa panzvimbo yangu, vachitsvaka kutora chiremera changu, izvo zvisingabviri. Ava vakapinda mumakereke enyu, muzvikoro zvenyu zvebhaibheri, muzvikoro zvenyu zvakasiyana-siyana, uye vanodzora makereke enyu nokubvumirana kwenyu kushanda pasi pe501(c)(3), nokuti vose vari pasi pechibvumirano chakadaro vakavharwa miromo nehurumende dzenyika. Ma501(c)(3) enyu anoratidza makaro enyu emari yetsvina, nokuti makazviita zvifeve pasi penzira dzaBharami, uye munopa mashoko enhema nokuda kwemari kuitira kuti muzvichengetere mari yose yavakakupai vana vangu vaduku muhupombwe hwenyu husingabhadhari mutero! Mava shamwari yenyika, uye nyika iri kuuya kuzotora chero chinhu chakanamirwa pazita rangu kana mudzivisi abviswa. Munoshaya njere dzomweya uye muri mapofu! Munoshaya hanya yekudzivirira boka kubva kumapere anoparadza aya, nokuti munokoshesa mazita emabasa akanamirwa pamazita enyu, mazita andisina kukupai; asi munoachiva. Nokuti ndakakuudzai kuti muchive zvikuru zvipo zvakanakisisa: Uye zvakadaro ndinokuratidzai nzira yakanakisisa: nzira yakanakisisa yamunoramba munzira dzenyu dzeusimbe, dzekudziya.

Hamuchaparidzi chivi nekutendeuka kubva pachivi, asi munoparidza ngano dzinonzwika dzichifadza dzisiri dzangu, uye ngano dzenyu dzakagadzirwa nemashoko enhema uye dzinotungamirira kunzira dzenhema; nokuti hamusi chinhu chinopfuura munyori wengano anonyengedza nzeve dzevasimbe. Ndiri kutaura nemi, vaapostora vangu: nokuti meso enyu akasviba, uye hamuchaooni chiedza changu, uye munozivisa nhema! Ndiri kutaura nemi, vaporofita vangu: nokuti munotaura muchimiririra mumwe Jesu, uye munoregeredza mashoko angu, uye hamundizivi, uye munoprofita nezveramangwana renhema! Ndiri kutaura nemi, vaevhangeri vangu: Nokuti munoevhangerisa marudzi kudzidziso dzenhema dzemadhimoni, uye munoita kuti vanwe mvura yakasviba, nokuti munovazadza nemweya isina kuchena! Ndiri kutaura nemi, vafudzi vangu: Nokuti mune hanya nokuchiva kwenyu, uye makaita dumbu renyu mwari wenyu nemarara enyu ehuzivi hwekuzvibatsira; iwe benzi, haugoni kuporesa makwai angu nerurimi rwenhema! Ndiri kutaura nemi, vadzidzisi vangu: Nokuti hamuchadzidzisi mashoko akachena eevhangeri yangu, asi munodzidzisa mashoko ekubvumirana kwezvitendero, uye pane mhosva huru pakati penyu mose, vashumiri vangu veRaodhikiya. Mese mune chirwere chimwe chete chakafanana: Hamucharemekedzi mirairo iri muShoko rangu, uye hamucharemekedzi mashoko ega ega ari murugwaro rwangu.

Munofunga nokuzvikudza kuti muri vaturikiri nokuti munogona kusarudza zvinoreva kubva mumaduramazwi enyika nemakonkodhenzi enyika kuti zvienderane nezvinangwa zvenyu: Mabhuku andisina kunyora, nokuti ndinonyora rugwaro kuburikidza nevaporofita vangu, nokuti mweya wouprofita uchapupu hwaJesu! Vaturikiri vanotaura mutauro mumwe chete, uye mava chiseko! Panzvimbo pazvo, munoremekedza mashoko evafudzi vakaramba kutenda, kunyange vanyepedzeri vemasora vasina maturo. Vakapinda muungano dzenyu kuti vanyepedzere kuva imi (nokuti imi muri gorosi randaizounganidza mudura rangu kana mukatendeukira kwandiri). Asi panzvimbo pazvo iwo masora, hama dzenhema dzakava vashumiri venhema vanokutsausai. Muti wekunyepedzera une chepfu ndiwo!

Ava vakaipa havachabudi pakati peKereke yangu kuti varatidzwe, asi munovakoka mukati uye munovamanikidza kuti vadye nemi uye munokudza mashoko avo enhema nendimi dzenhema kupfuura mashoko andaizokupai, kunyange mashoko aMwari Mupenyu, kunyange mashoko aYahweh! Munotengesa mashoko angu kuti muve nekubwinya kwose nerumbidzo ndezvangu, Ishe wenyu Jesu Kristu. Zvirokwazvo mune mubairo wenyu, uye ndichabvisa mubairo wenyu ndigoupa mumwe munhu akanga akatendeka panzvimbo yenyu. Nokuti hamuchatombonditauriri, uye ndinofanira kuuya kuKereke yangu seinzwi rounodana murenje, nokuti makavhara mukova pachiso changu, kunyange ndichiri kumira ndichigogodza! Kunyara kukuru nekuzvidzwa zviri pamusoro penyu kunze kwekunge mapfidza!

Kana mukaregeredza mashoko angu uye musinganzwi izwi rangu, kana mukaramba muri munzira dzenyu dzekudziya, ndichakusiyai mukutambudzika kwaJakobho; nokuti ndakambokuudzai, imi vangu veRaodhikiya, ndichakusvipai mumuromo mangu kunze kwekunge mapfidza! Uye zvakare ndakataura pamusoro pomuprofitakadzi wenhema uyo Jezebheri pandakanyorera Tiatira, nokuti chii chandakaudza Johani Muratidziri?

Kunyange zvakadaro, ndine zvinhu zvishoma zvinopesana nemi, nokuti munotendera mukadzi uyo Jezebheri, anozviti muprofitakadzi, kuti adzidzise uye anyengere varanda vangu kuti vaite upombwe, uye kuti vadye zvinhu zvakabayirwa kuzvidhori. Uye ndakamupa nguva yokuti apfidze upombwe hwake, uye haana kupfidza. Tarirai, ndichamukanda pamubhedha, naavo vanoita upombwe naye, mukutambudzika kukuru, kunze kwekunge vapfidza mabasa avo.

Hamuna kuverenga mashoko aya here? Mune manyawi okufunga kuti Yahweh kana Yeshua HaMashiach vanosarura vanhu here? Munofunga kuti ndinoremekedza mazita amunozvipa kuti muise pamisoro yevanhu vangu, pamunenge muchizviita madzishe pamusoro penhaka yaMwari here?

Reverend uyu, Bhishopu uyo; dai musina kuregeredza zvipo zvangu, chipo chenyu chingadai chakakugadzirirai nzvimbo pamberi pevanhu vakuru uye mungadai musingadi mazita enyu asina maturo, nokuti ndingadai ndakukudzai uye imi mungadai makandikudza, asi munondizvidza! Pane mhosva huru pakati penyu, uye hasha dzangu dzamutsirwa pamusoro penyu, nokuti makundikana pamabasa andakakupai uye panzvimbo pazvo mava vakaramba kutenda, kunyange kutungamirira vana vangu vaduku mugomba ramakadzika matende enyu. Munofarira mvura isina kuchena iri mugomba renyu kupfuura mvura yangu yakachena yomweya mutsvene iyo vose vanonwa havazombovi nenyota zvakare!

Munopa mvura isina kuchena, yakasviba kuvana vangu vaduku mumigodhi yakaputsika, uye hasha dzangu dziri pamusoro penyu! Ndichabvisa mubairo wenyu ndigoupa mumwe, nokuti sezvandakataura pamusoro penyu: Zvirokwazvo, mune mubairo wenyu. Mune mubairo wenyu wezvigaro zvenyu zvamunofarira zvekuzvikudza, uye mune mubairo wenyu wemazita asina maturo, uye mune mubairo wenyu wemari yetsvina, uye muchava nemubairo wehasha dzangu kunze kwekunge mapfidza uye madzokera kwandiri mukuzvininipisa. Nokuti mubairo wehasha dzangu kutsamwa, uye pamunondipa matanda enyu, uswa, nemashanga, zvichatsva kuva madota pamberi pevose, uye muchawanikwa muri vashumiri venhema!

Pane mhosva huru pakati penyu mose, kunyange imi, vaapostora vangu, vaporofita, vaevhangeri, vafudzi, nevadzidzisi! Hamuchazvibati muukama hunoponesa neni, Jesu Kristu, asi makandiregedza; nokuti ini ndiri musoro womuviri, uye munofanira kundibata pamusoro pezvose! HAMUGONI kundishumira kana musingatombozivi ruzha rwezwi rangu. Muri matsi, uye mune kukuvara kwenzeve kusingaperi kunze kwekunge mapfidza, nokuti ndichaporesa nzeve dzenyu kubva kumweya yekubatwa nehope kuti munzwe izwi rangu, nokuti ndiri kutaura nemi! Kunze kwekunge mandibata semusoro wenyu uye muchiremekedza Mashoko angu, ndichakusiyai muri mapofu uye muri mugomba pamwe neavo vakakutungamirirai. Muri mapofu, uye mune kukuvara kwemaziso kusingaperi kunze kwekunge mapfidza, nokuti ndaizoporesa maziso enyu kubva kumweya yekubatwa nehope kuti muverenge mashoko angu uye muone chiso changu: asi zvakadaro hope shoma, zvakadaro kupeta maoko zvishoma.

Mava chiseko nenyadziso, nekuda kwenzira dzenyu dzekudziya-dziya dzinomhura zita rangu dzvene. Hamungondibati ini, Kristu Jesu, semusoro wenyu (nekuti dai maidaro, maizogona kunditaurira), asi munoenda mberi mukuramba kutenda nekumhura mashoko angu matsvene, nekuti ini ndiri Shoko rakaitwa nyama! Ndinokubvunzai, hamuna kuverenga here yambiro dzangu? Nekuti ndinopupurira kumunhu wese anonzwa mashoko echiporofita chebhuku rino, Kana munhu upi zvake akawedzera pazvinhu izvi, Mwari achawedzera kwaari matambudziko akanyorwa mubhuku rino. Uye kana munhu upi zvake akabvisa pamashoko ebhuku rechiporofita ichi, Mwari achabvisa mugove wake muBhuku reUpenyu, nomuguta dzvene, nomuzvinhu zvakanyorwa mubhuku rino. Makapusa, matsi, nemapofu zvekuti hamunzwisise here kuti yambiro iyo handina kuipa chete nokuda kwebhuku guru rekuratidzwa kwangu, Zvakazarurwa! Asi yambiro iyo inoshanda kumagwaro matsvene ose! Nekuti Zvirevo zvangu zvinodanidzira mumigwagwa kune vose vane nzeve dzekunzwa: Mashoko ose aMwari akanatswa uye akachena; nekuti iye inhovo kune vose vanovimba naye. Naizvozvo usawedzere chinhu pamashoko ake, kuti arege kukutsiura iwe ukawanikwa uri murevi wenhema.

Vashumiri vangu veRaodhikea, vakashamwaridzana nenyika. Vashumiri vangu veRaodhikea, vanozviita madzishe panhaka yaMwari, vachizviratidza kuti muri vanhu vakuru. Vashumiri vangu veRaodhikea, vakaregeredza mweya wangu wandakasika mamuri. Vashumiri vangu veRaodhikea, vanozvipa mazita asina maturo nezvidaniso zvekuwedzera pamazita enyu. Vashumiri vangu veRaodhikea: Inzwai mashoko angu mundipindure, nekuti rurimi rwose ruchapupura!

Ndiani akakupai mvumo yekushandura mashoko angu, kunyange Shoko raShe randakapa kuburikidza nenhume dzangu? Nzira dzenyu, imi vashumiri veRaodhikea vane usimbe, hadzisi itsva, nekuti tsamba dzangu padzakangotenderedzwa kuMakereke angu achangobva kuberekwa akanyorwa muna Mabasa nedzimwewo nzvimbo, mapoka evaGnostic aitungamirirwa nevaporofita venhema vechiGnostic akashandura mashoko angu ndokunyorazve magwaro, nekuti vakabuda pakati peKereke yangu kuti vose vazive kuti vakanga vari vafudzi vakaramba kutenda!

Muchakohwa mibairo yenzira dzavo, nekuti makazvibatanidza navo, uye munorarama nenzira dzavo, uye munoratidza mweya isina kuchena yaJezebheri, muporofitakadzi wenhema wamunoti mutsvene! Ndinoona mabasa enyu akaipa, uye akanyorwa kumatenga zvakakwana kuti akupomerei! Munofanira kutaura mashoko andinokupai, uye munofanira kuataura nokutendeseka, nokururama, pasina kushandura kunyange shoko rimwe chete rechiporofita chipi zvacho! Handisi kuzvipikisa, nekuti ndakakuudzai: Shingairira kuti uzviratidze uri munhu anogamuchirwa naMwari, mushandi asingafaniri kunyadziswa, anogovera shoko rechokwadi zvakarurama. Mashoko mazhinji akashandurwa mumagwaro angu kwemakore, uye kana mukadzidza nekuremekedza mashoko angu andakapa, ndichakutungamirirai muchokwadi chose, nekuti ndizvo zvandakapa munyaradzi.

Ndichakuratidzai, kana mukateerera kumazwi mazhinji andakakusiyirai. Teverai magwaro andakapa kuAndiyoki, nekuti ndiwo mazwi ezvapupu zvizhinji, uye zvapupu zvangu hazvirevi nhema uye hazvipikisane. Tarisai kuTextus Receptus uye murambe muri kure neMabhaibheri evakaramba kutenda: dzidzai kuti munzwisise! Asi imi, vashumiri vangu veRaodhikea, hamuremekedzi mashoko angu, uye munoshandura mashoko angu kuti aenderane nezvinangwa zvenyu zvakasviba, muchidzidzisa vanhu vangu kuti varambe vari muchivi, kunyange kuvatungamirira zvakadzama munzira dzenyu dzine tsvina! Pane mhosva huru pakati penyu, uye hamuteveri nzira dzangu, nekuti munozvikudza uye mune mwoyo mikukutu, uye ndichakusiyai mukuparadzwa kwenyama kunze kwekunge matendeuka nekushandura nzira dzenyu. Nekuti ini, Jesu Kristu, ndatumira mashoko aya! Musangoaregeredza, uye tendeukai muri mumasaga nemadota. Tendeukai pachena kuungano dzenyu uye mureurure zvivi zvenyu kune avo vamakatsausa, nekuti ndichabvisa chigadziko chenyu chemwenje kubva kwamuri. Tendeukai mufambe zvakarurama, uye ndichadzorera mwenje wenyu nemurazvo uchangobatidzwa, nekuti ndini ndichabatidza moto wemweya wangu mumweya yenyu.

Dzokerai kurudo rwenyu rwekutanga, nekuti munondida, asi kwete maererano neruzivo. Musaramba mashoko angu, nekuti ndiri kuuya nekukurumidza kupfuura zvamunofunga, nekuti ndinouya sembavha usiku. Kunyange mukukanganisa kwenyu, munofunga kuti mbavha usiku munhu anopinda nepahwindo semupari wemhosva. Iwe benzi! Haiwa, dai wainzwisisa tsika dzevaHebheru. Mbavha usiku aive mupristi mukuru aiuya asina kuzivisa kuti aone kana vapristi vake vakanga vakatendeka pamabasa avo etemberi, nekuti mamwe mabasa aiitwa husiku hwose kusvikira mabasa iwayo apera maererano nemurairo. Kana mupristi mukuru akawana mupristi waaichengeta akarara pabasa, aikanda zhimbe rinopisa munguo dzake, kuti mupristi azunze nguo dzake dzaibvira moto omhanya achibuda mutemberi asina kupfeka uye achinyara. Usawanikwe wakarara pandinouya, kana kuti uchawira pamberi pangu usina kupfeka uye uchinyara munyadzi huru inofungidzirika, nekuti ndichanyara newe uye ndichakutorera mubairo wose. Kurova kwangu kuri pamusoro pako uye ruoko rwangu runorema pamusoro pako, uye kunze kwekunge watendeuka, uchava nedambudziko guru, guru rawawaigona kunge wakanzvenga. Tendeuka, kuti ndichiri kukugadzirira nzvimbo patafura yangu, uye uchabatana neMwenga wangu muwanano neni, Kristu Jesu, Chikomba.

Ndini Shoko rakaitwa nyama rinogara pakati penyu.

Ndini mufudzi akanaka uye makwai angu anonzwa izwi rangu.

Ndini Gwayana raMwari rakatumirwa zvivi zvenyika.

Ndini mupristi mukuru nekusingaperi zvichienderana neurongwa hwaMerkizedeki.

Ndiri kuuya uye mubairo wangu uneni.

Ndini iye anofamba pakati pezvigadziko zvemwenje zvinomwe nengirozi nomwe.

Ndini muporofita akamutswa pakati pehama uyo Mosesi akataura nezvake: Inzwai izwi rangu mukuteerera.

Ndini iye akapa zvipo kuvanhu, saka musaregeredza zvipo zvandakapa.

Ndiri kuuya nekukurumidza kupfuura zvamunofunga; zvigadzirirei, Mwenga wangu!

Ini, Ishe Jesu Kristu, ndatumira mashoko aya uye ndava izwi reunodanidzira murenje; nekuti Kereke yangu yeRaodhikea inodziya-dziya uye ichakurumidza kuva isina kupfeka uye ichinyara kunze kwekunge yatendeuka. Ndamira pamusuwo ndichigogodza kwekanguva kapfupi kasara! Hapachina nhamba pawachi, nekuti nhamba dzadonha; uye hapachina tsanga dzejecha dzinowanikwa kana tsanga yekupedzisira yayangarara nepakati pegirazi reawa, uye yava kuda kudonha.
