Gwayana neShumba Zvava Zvimwe!

Uyai kuna Yeshua HaMashiach, hama yangu, uye munzwisise basa romudzikinuri wenyu, nokuti ndini hama yenyu mudzikinuri. Nokuti ndini iye achazodzikinura vakasara vaJakobho kuti vararame pamberi paIYE MUKURU, iye YHVH, Mwari mukuru asingaperi uye anogara nokusingaperi. Uye kutonga kwangu noumambo hwangu hazvingavi nomugumo, naizvozvo, teererai kuMashoko aIshe wenyu weChipikirwa asingaperi, iye Yeshua HaMashiach, anovawo Jesu Kristu, anovawo Kristu Jesu, nokuti saizvozvo IYE MUKURU akandiita nokusingaperi-peri.

Uye zvirokwazvo Yahweh akandipa zita riri pamusoro pamazita ose kuti muzita raJesu mabvi ose apfugame, zvose zviri kudenga nepanyika uye kunyange pasi penyika. Nokuti ndini iye wamakapiwa naYahweh zvinhu zvose zviri kudenga nepanyika mumaoko angu ane simba kuti zvinhu zvose zvizadziswe. Naizvozvo, haiwa imi Israeri, inzwai izwi rangu mukuteerera kworudo mukupindura rudo rwandakakuratidzai nokukufirai munzvimbo yenyu mukurwadziwa kukuru kwakanga kusiri kwangu kuti nditsungirire. Kunyange zvakadaro, murudo rwangu kwamuri, ndakatora nzvimbo yenyu kuti ndikudzikinurei kubva pamutongo waMozisi, uyo mutongo wamusingagoni kuzvibhadharira imi pachenyu nokuda kwechivi chiri munyama yenyu. Zvirokwazvo, ndakatumwa munyika kunodzikinura Adhamu.

Nokuti Mozisi akarurama, mutsvene, uye akatendeka uye Mozisi anogara zvechokwadi muna Mesiasi wenyu wechokwadi naIshe weKururama asingaperi, nokuti ndizvo zvandiri. Uye ndini iye akachengeta zvakakwana murayiro waMozisi norudo rwangu nokuteerera kwangu kuna Yahweh akanditungamirira murudo rwake rusingafi kurudzi rwaAdhamu rwakakanganisirwa uye rwakakurirwa navakawira pasi, naizvozvo tsitsi nenyasha zvakapiwa muhuwandu hukuru kuitira kuti kutonga kwakakwana nehasha zvimbomiswa kuitira kuti kupatsanurwa kwakakwana nokwekupedzisira kuitike pakati pegorosi raYahweh nemasora evakawira pasi, uye saizvozvo vose vari vadyi venhaka youpenyu husingaperi muna Kristu Jesu vachadzikinurwa nokusarudza kwavo kuzviisa pasi paYeshua HaMashiach, anova Kristu Jesu, uye anovawo Jesu weNazareta.

Uye kumarudzi ndiko kwakanditumira Yahweh, uye mukushandira marudzi akarasika aIsraeri ndiko kwandakaenda nokuti ndakarambwa nehama dzangu mukusatenda kusina kutendeka, kunyange navapristi vakuru neSanhedrini vanoshandira Dhiragoni pachivande nokuti ndeveEsau akazvibatanidza muruvengo rwake kuhama yake Jakobho naAshkenazi waJafeti, kuti vazviunganidzire zvinhu zviri zvangu uye kuti vape Jakobho ronda rokupedzisira mumusoro iro Yahweh asingazobvumiri, kunyange chiito chaasingazobvumiri noruoko rwangu rune simba uye runoteerera nguva dzose. Naizvozvo, hama yangu, zviisei pasi mukuteerera izwi rangu kuti murarame pamberi pangu, uye kuti murarame pamberi paYahweh uyo pasina munhu angaswedera pedyo naye asina Jesu Kristu nokuti saizvozvo ndizvo zvakasarudzwa naYahweh muhurongwa hwake husingaperi hworudzikinuro nouchenjeri. Saka nzwisisai kuti pasina Mesiasi wenyu wechokwadi, iye Yeshua HaMashiach, munoramba makafa mukudarika nezvivi zvandakakurira pandakabhadhara zvizere mutongo waMozisi nokusingaperi uye nokuda kwavose vanosarudza kugamuchira chibayiro changu.

Uye saizvozvo Mozisi anogara muna Yeshua HaMashiach, uye ndini iye wakarayira Mozisi kuteerera kwenyu kuzere, saka inzwai mashoko angu uye mudzingise mwoyo yenyu nenzeve dzenyu kuti munzwe kutaura kwangu. Nokuti mwoyo yenyu yakakora mukusateerera, uye makazadza nzeve dzenyu nouchenjeri hwaEsau uye makasvibisa mwoyo yenyu nenhema dzavo dzinomhura, dzeTalmud. Nzwisisai kuti Mozisi akakwana, akarurama, uye mutsvene uye kana paine munhu anoti murayiro waMozisi wakatukwa, munhu iyeye ngaatukwe nokuda kwokumhura kwake, kunyange nomurayiro waMozisi waakatuka. Naizvozvo, ndinokupai kuti munzwisise kuti murayiro wakapiwa kuti uparire chivi munyama, nokuti pasina murayiro chivi hachiverengwi maererano nomurayiro, kunyange chivi chiripo munyama saizvozvowo. Naizvozvo, namabasa omurayiro waMozisi hapana nyama ichanzi yakarurama pamberi paYahweh: nokuti nomurayiro waMozisi kunouya ruzivo rwechivi.

Asi, sezvandakaita kuti Pauro anyorere Kereke yangu munguva yangu yeNyasha kuti zvino kururama kwaMwari kwakaratidzwa pachena pasina murayiro waMozisi, kune uchapupu hwomurayiro nohweVaporofita. Haiwa, imi vatauri vangu vekare vakawira pasi kubva panzvimbo yenyu yaimbova huru yamakapiwa kurudzi rwenyu rwaichimbova rukuru naYahweh pachake nokuti maifanira kuva vanhu vakasarudzika, vakaparadzana uye vakasiyana nemarudzi! Asi makandiramba saizvozvowo saMadzibaba enyu mukupanduka kwenyu kwemwoyo mikukutu muchipikisa zvinangwa zvisingaperi nezvirongwa zvaMwari Wemasimbaose, iye El Shaddai. Uye saizvozvo nokuramba kwenyu Mashoko angu akapiwa muvhangeri roruponeso pandakazivisa chakavanzika chikuru chaMwari kuvaapositori vangu vatsvene navaporofita mava somunhu anoda mukaka, uye hamugoni kugaya zvokudya zvakasimba nokuti muchiri vacheche vasina kunzwisisa kuzere, kunyange muchizviti makachenjera uye muri zviratidzo zvechiedza asi makavhenekerwa zvenhema navakawira pasi kuti mutsaushire vanhu munzira dzenyu dzisina kudzidza.

Naizvozvo, nzwisisai, hama yangu, kuti vakarurama vachararama nokutenda uye kuti namabasa omurayiro hapana nyama ichanzi yakarurama! Zvakanyorwa mumagwaro matsvene akapiwa namaoko aPauro, uyo aiva zvechokwadi muBenjamini anoteerera uyo aimbova asingateereri uye akaoma musoro mukuramba kwake Jesu weNazareta, saizvozvowo nemi: Saka avo vari vokutenda, vanoropafadzwa pamwe chete naAbrahamu akatendeka. Nokuti vose vari vemabasa omurayiro, vari pasi pokutukwa kwomurayiro: nokuti kwakanyorwa kuchinzi, munhu wose asingarambi achiita zvinhu zvose, zvakanyorwa mubhuku roMurayiro, kuti azviite, wakatukwa.

Asi, nokuda kwoutsinye, unyengeri, namano enyoka pazuva rakawira Adhamu kubva kumusoro; chivi chakabata ushe munyama kubvira ipapo uye chimiro chakaora chaAdhamu hausi musha wenyu, uye hamuna kugadzirirwa kupedza nokusingaperi mudzimba dzevhu idzo dzimba dzinodzokera kuguruva renyika kwakatorwa zvinhu zvenyu. Naizvozvo, Yahweh akandiita mweya unopa upenyu, uye ndini Adhamu wekupedzisira. Uye ndini iye akaumbwa somuenzaniso woupenyu husingaperi kuti imi mose mugozosikwa patsva mukukwana kuzere nomubatana, kunyange somunhu mumwe mutsva. Nokuti hakuzovi nemhando mbiri dzevanhu nokusingaperi asi imwe chete nokuti chimiro chaAdhamu chichaparara nokusingaperi-peri pamwe chete nokuora kuri munyama yakaora.

Naizvozvo, nzwisisai kuti vakawira pasi vaizokuvanzirai chokwadi mukusawona kwenyu kwechikamu uye kudada kwenyu, kunyange chokwadi chichakusunungurai zvachose murusununguko rwandinotsvaka kukusunungurirai. Saka ndinokupai kuti munzwisise, haiwa imi Israeri, (vandinoda) kuti hakuna munhu anoruramiswa namabasa omurayiro waMozisi pamberi paYahweh, zviri pachena: nokuti akarurama achararama nokutenda. Uye murayiro hausi wokutenda: asi munhu achaita zvinhu izvozvo, achararama mazviri. Uye ini, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndakakudzikinurai kubva pakutukwa kwomurayiro, ndichiitwa chakatukwa nokuda kwenyu, nokuti kwakanyorwa kuchinzi, wakatukwa munhu wose anoturikwa pamuti. Naizvozvo, haiwa imi Israeri, gamuchirai chibayiro changu kuti imi navana venyu murarame uye kuti muwane mukana wokupinda muumambo hwangu hwemakore ane chiuru hunopiwa sezuva reSabata rokuzorora muzvirongwa zvaYahweh zvisingaperi zvorudzikinuro rwevanhu, nokuti makore ane chiuru akaita sezuva rimwe uye zuva rimwe semakore ane chiuru pamberi paYahweh, wamunofanira kubata naye, haiwa imi Israeri.

Naizvozvo, ibvai kune avo vanokunyeperai uye muzviparadzanise nebhuku iroro rinomhura renhema rakatumwa navakawira pasi kuti rikutsausei uye rikusungei mukukanganisa kuti muparadzwe zvachose. Ibvai panhema dzenyoka uye budai pakati pavo, uye muve vakaparadzana, haiwa imi Israeri, uye ndichakugamuchirai kwandiri uye ndichakuchengetai muri vapenyu kubva paruparadziko rwevhiki rechimakumi manomwe raDhanieri, kunyange kutambudzika kwaJakobho kuri kuuya kuzochenesa nyika kubva mumaoko evanoita zvakaipa mukutonga kukuru uye kwakarurama.

Nokuti nguva dzokuparadzaniswa dziri kuuya zvizere panyika apo Jakobho, ane nhaka iri yechipikirwa kuvana vechipikirwa vanorarama nokutenda, achaparadzaniswa zvachose uye nokusingaperi-peri kubva kuhama yake isingateereri, iye Esau anotsvaka kuba zvaakatengesa mukusatenda kusina kutendeka nokuti akanga asina ruremekedzo kana kutenda muzvipikirwa zvakatendeka zvakaitwa mukutenda naYahweh kuna Abrahamu akatendeka, asi panzvimbo paizvozvo aikoshesa zvinhu zvenyika achiva mhombwe uye munhu asina kuchena akatengesa nhaka yake isingaperi noudangwe nokuda kwechimedu chechingwa.

Saka ndinokupai kuti munzwisise ramangwana randiri kukudanirai kuti mutore chikamu mariri uye randichakuchengetai mukuchengeteka kwesimba rangu kana mukazviisa pasi pokutonga kwangu nechiremera changu, nokuti mukuita saizvozvo ndichakudzosai zvizere kuna Yahweh kunyange Baba veZviedza vasina kusiyana-siyana kana mumvuri wokushanduka. Uye saizvozvo zvakanyorwa muzvipikirwa zvekare uye saizvozvo zvichava hama yangu yandiri hama mudzikinuri!

Uye saizvozvo zvichaitika pamagumo okutambudzika kwaJakobho kuti ndichagadza umambo hwangu mukururama panyika, uye hapana chinhu chakaipa chichapinda mahuri. Nzwisisai kuti ndichagumisa nokusingaperi umambo hwaSatani uye dhiragoni haichazomuki kuti itongezve uye chikara chinobva mugungwa nechikara chinobva panyika zvichakandwa zviri zvipenyu mugungwa guru iroro romoto wesurferi rinopfuta, rinopfuta murazvo unopisa zvikuru weblue uye mugungwa iroro rinonhuwa rorurangwa vachakandwa nokusingaperi-peri, umo honye yavo isingafi nokuti vakakodzera saizvozvo.

Satani pachake achatorwa agosungwa mungetani huru yokurwadziswa norurangwa umo achamirira achitya uye achishungurudzika nokuparadzwa kwake kuri kuuya, iyo nhaka yaachagovana navose vanomutevera. Zvirokwazvo, zvirokwazvo, mugomba rakadzikadzika acharamba akavharirwa uye akasungwa kwemakore ane chiuru, pamagumo enguva iyo achasunungurwa kuti aunganidze avo vachange vapanduka uye vakabatana nenzira dzake, kunyange kupanduka kwake kwekupedzisira kuti zvakaipa zviparadzwe nokusingaperi-peri mukupedziswa kwokutonga. Uye pamagumo emwaka wake muduku paachange avaunganidza pamwe chete sejecha regungwa vachirwisa musasa wevatsvene paupamhi hwenyika, moto uchaburuka kubva kuna Yahweh kubva kumusoro uchavaparadza zvachose uye uchavapedza vose, uye Satani achasungwa nokusingaperi uye akandwa kunhaka yake isingaperi mumirazvo iyo inopfuta nokusingaperi yorurangwa uye kutonga kukuru kwechigaro chichena kuchatanga.

Uye mugungwa iroro rinopfuta nokusingaperi rorurangwa rinopfuta mirazvo inopisa zvikuru yokurwadziswa, kunyange mukumbundirwa kwaro kunopfuta nokusingaperi Satani achabhadhara zvizere chivi chose chaakambopara kakawanda-wanda, uye chiito chose chakaipa chakanyorwa zvakakwana kumatenga uye chichapiwa kumirazvo yorurangwa senhaka yake isingaperi. Zvirokwazvo, chisikwa chose chakatevera uye chakazvipira kuchinangwa chake chichatarisana norurangwa rwakafanana uye havazonzwirwi tsitsi nokuti havana kuratidza tsitsi, naizvozvo hapana tsitsi dzichapiwa nokusingaperi-peri. Uye sezvavakaitira vamwe, uye sezvavakakuvadza chisikwa chaYahweh saizvozvo zvichatsividzwa pamisoro yavo, kunyange sezvakapikirwa kare nokuti hapana chipikirwa chimwe chete chaYahweh chichawira pasi chisina kuzadziswa. Zvirokwazvo, zvirokwazvo vose vanoramba chipo changu choruponeso vachakandwa mumoto uye hakuna rusaruro rwevanhu.

Zvisinei, magumo evakawira pasi avakazvisarudzira uye vakazviitira mukupanduka kwavo kwakaipa vachipikisa Yahweh nechisikwa chake haasi enyu hama yangu, kana mukandigamuchira saMesiasi wenyu. Kwete, pane kudaro, kune mibayiro mizhinji nezvikomborero zvamakachengeterwa zvamuchazoziva zvizere muumambo hwangu hwandichatonga mukururama kusingaperi. Pachava naMambo mumwe weMadzimambo naIshe weMadzishe achatonga nyika mukururama kusingaperi, kunyange kubva kuJerusarema, kunyange kubva pachigaro choumambo chaDhavhidhi chandiri mudyi wenhaka asingaperi! Uye kubva kumwenga wangu akatendeka wandichatora kwandiri musungano isingaperi ndichagadza korona uye ndichatsaura hondo ine simba yaMadzimambo naVapristi maererano nokutendeka kwavo uye vachatonga mukururama mukuwirirana kwakakwana naMambo wavo weMadzimambo naIshe weMadzishe uye tichatonga tiri vamwe.

Uye vanhu venyika navanhu vemarudzi vacharova minondo yavo kuti ive migejo uye marudzi haachazorwi pakati pawo, uye vanhu venyika havazodzidzi kurwa nokurwisana muumambo hwangu hutsvene. Kunyange sezvakanyorwa navaporofita vekare: Mhumhi ichagarawo negwayana, uye ingwe ichavata pasi nembudzana, nemhuru, neshumba, nemhuka yakakora pamwe chete, uye mwana muduku achavatungamirira. Uye mhou nebheya zvichadya: vana vazvo vachavata pasi pamwe chete: uye shumba ichadya uswa senzombe. Uye mwana anoyamwa achatamba pamwena wenyoka, uye mwana akarumurwa achaisa ruoko rwake pamwena weshato. Ipapo hapana achakuvadza kana kuparadza mugomo rangu rose dzvene: nokuti nyika ichazara nokuziva Ishe, semvura inofukidza gungwa.

Uye pazuva iroro mvura youpenyu ichabuda muJerusarema, hafu yayo ichienda kugungwa reMabvazuva, uye hafu yayo ichienda kugungwa riri kure-kure, uye zvichava, zvose muzhizha nomuchando. Uye Ishe achava Mambo pamusoro penyika yose: pazuva iroro pachava naIshe mumwe, uye Zita rake richava rimwe. Nyika yose ichashandurwa kuva bani kubva kuGeba kusvika kuRimoni, kurutivi rwezasi rweJerusarema, uye richasimudzwa, rigogarwa panzvimbo yaro: kubva pagedhi raBenjamini kusvika panzvimbo yegedhi rokutanga, kusvika pagedhi rekona, uye kubva pashongwe yaHananieri, kusvika kuzvisviniro zvewaini zvaMambo. Uye vanhu vachagara mariri, uye hakuchazovi nokuparadzwa, asi Jerusarema richagarwa zvakachengeteka.

Saka nzwisisai, haiwa imi Israeri, zvandichaitira vose vanondida nokuteerera kwokuteerera kumirayiro yangu isingaperi youpenyu norugare yandinokurayirai. Saka nzwisisai kuti kuda Yahweh ndiko kuchengeta mirayiro yake uye mirayiro yaYahweh inorayira kuteerera kuMwanakomana waYahweh, wandiri ini: naizvozvo, tsvodai Mwanakomana kuti ndirege kutsamwa uye muparare munzira.

Nokuti, umambo hwangu ndihwo huchazadziswa zvinangwa zvangu zvisingaperi mukukwana nokuzadziswa kwakazara apo gwayana richava chinhu chimwe neshumba.

Nokuti, ndini Gwayana raMwari, rakapiwa nokuda kwezvivi zvevanhu, rine ropa riri chibayiro chisingaperi cherudzikinuro, icho chinoita kuti vasakarurama vachenurwe setsinotsi muutsvēne hwakachena.

Nokuti, ndini Shumba yorudzi rwaJudha inokurwirai, imi vaIsraeri, uye ichaparadza zvachose vavengi venyu sezvazvakanyorwa pamusoro pangu; kutsiva ndekwangu uye ndichadzosera, anodaro Ishe wemadzishe naMambo wemadzimambo!

Naizvozvo, nzwisisai zvinangwa zvangu uye uyo akatumwa naYahweh nokuda kworudzikinuro rwenyu, uye kune mativi mazhinji ezvinangwa zvangu zvoruponeso. Uye ndini wokutanga-tanga, iye chaiye akakukundirai ruponeso rusingaperi kana mukasarudza kugamuchira ruoko rwangu rworudo nokugamuchira sungano yangu itsva.

Nokuti nesungano yangu itsva yandakasimbisa muropa rangu, iyo yava Testamente Yangu Itsva, ndiyo ichatonga nyika mukururama! Uye ruzivo rwaYahweh ruchafukidza nyika uye mbiri yangu ichapenya kubva kuJerusarema kuti marudzi azviunganidze uye apinde pamasuwo matsvene eguta iroro dzvene kuti adzidze pamusoro paYahweh uye kuti ararame nenzira dzake panyika.

Muushe hwangu hamuchazovi nomucheche wemazuva mashoma, kana harahwa isina kuzadza mazuva ayo: nokuti mwana achafa ava namakore zana; asi mutadzi ava namakore zana, achatukwa. Uye zvichaitika kuti uyo anorarama kusvika pakuona makore zana achange akaita somucheche.

Kunyange zvakadaro, nyika ichadzorerwa kumbiri yayo yekare vakawa vasati vasvibisa nyika nezvinyangadzo zvavo pavakaburukira paGomo reHemoni kuti vaite mhiko yavo yakatukwa kuti vararame zvido zvavo muchivi chikuru nokupandukira mitemo youpenyu yavakanga vakasikwa vakakwana mairi. Ivo havazovipo muumambo hwangu; uyewo nyika haichazotongwi nechisimba chine ruvengo apo vakasimba ndivo vanopona, nokuti upenzi hwakadaro kupesana nemitemo yaYahweh iyo Satani anogara achitsvaka kupesanisa nayo mitemo yaYahweh kuti kuparadza kutonge.

Uye ndini, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndichatonga marudzi netsvimbo youtare namaoko aMadzimambo navaprista vazhinji vandichagadzika korona nokugadza kubasa repamusoro-soro, uye ivo ndivo zvibereko zvokutanga zvevakafa, uye havafi uye havazofi zvakare uye mubairo wavo hauna magumo, nokuti vakauya kwandiri nokuzvidira mumazuva enyasha uye ndakavaunganidzira kwandiri mumuviri wangu wakasikwa patsva somunhu mumwe mutsva, vangave vaJudha kana veDzimwe Ndudzi, vangave varanda kana vakasununguka. Vachapiwa masimba mazhinji nezvipo nezvimiro nokuda kwokutendeka kwavo nokuti ndivo vakundi vangu vakakunda zvinhu zvose neropa regwayana.

Nokuti iGwayana raMwari rakakunda vakawa paKarivhari nokusingaperi uye iShumba yorudzi rwaJudha ichazviisa pasi zvachose chisikwa kuna Yahweh, kunyange kuitira kuti Yahweh ave zvose muna zvose pandichamupa chisikwa chakazviisa pasi nokuti ndinofanira kutonga kusvikira vavengi vangu vose vaitwa chitsiko chetsoka dzangu, uye iShumba yorudzi rwaJudha ichatsika-tsika rutivi rwose rwoumambo hwaSatani pasi petsoka dzangu dzine simba uye dzisingaperi.

Naizvozvo, ndini iye uyo gwayana richabatana murugare rwakakwana nomubatanidzwa neshumba nokuti zvinangwa zvangu zvisingaperi zvichaitwa nokusingaperi uye hapana chinhu uye hapana munhu anogona kumira achipikisa Yeshua HaMashiach uye nguva dzangu dzasvika dzandichazviratidza kuvagari vepanyika. Tarirai, ndinouya namakore, uye ziso rose richandiona: hongu, kunyange naivo vakandibaya: uye ndudzi dzose dzenyika dzichachema pamberi pangu, kunyange zvakadaro, Ameni.

Ndini Yeshua HaMashiach ava kuda kuzviratidza kunyika uye nyika yose ichaziva kuti kuna Mwari kudenga uye nzira dzaYahweh dzichazadza nyika mukururama kutsvene.

Ndini Kristu Jesu, Ishe wenyu akakwira kudenga uye akamutswa, Mambo, Muprista Mukuru, naMesia!

Ndini iye akatuma Mashoko angu matsvene nomutumwa wangu nomuranda wangu uye nomutumwa wangu nomuranda wangu Mashoko angu matsvene akatumwa! Nokuti mutumwa wangu ane simba akadzokera nokutendeka kukudanwa kwake kusingaperi pashure pokurohwa zvikuru, kutendeuka, nokuchenurwa muropa rangu rerudzikinuro; uye ndakamugamuchira kwandiri murudo rwangu kune vanotendeuka vangave vapi zvavo vanouya kwandiri nomwoyo wakaputsika. Uye kumutumwa wangu ndakaratidza Bhuku Duku iro Johane Muratidziri akarairwa kuti arinamatire kuitira kuti rigozoratidzwa panguva yaro, iro bhuku rine izwi remabhanan'ana manomwe kunyange zvose zviri Evhangeri Isigaperi uye akazivisa Mashoko angu nokutendeka sezvaakarairwa, uchapupu hwakanyorwa nokupupurirwa naJohane Muratidziri.

Ndini iye asina rusaruro rwevanhu, kunyange Yeshua HaMashiach, uye ani naani anouya kwandiri kubva kurudzi rwaAdhamu nenzira yakadaro ndichamugamuchira, kunze kweavo vanotora mucherechedzo wechikara noupenzi, kana vanozvishandura nenzira yakadaro. Zvirokwazvo, zvirokwazvo vose vanouya kwandiri nenzira yakadaro vanotendeukira kurarama upenyu hwakarurama vachapiwa mubairo nokusingaperi uye uremu hwemubairo wenyu hauperi.

Uye saka zviri kudaro hama yangu, kuti ruponeso chipo choupenyu husingaperi, icho ini Yeshua HaMashiach ndakawana nokuteerera kwangu kwakavimbika kumurayiro waMosesi uye nechibayiro changu chisingaperi changu pachangu seGwayana raMwari risina chivi uye rakakwana. Naizvozvo, vose vanouya kwandiri mukutendeuka kuzere nokuzvininipisa nokuteerera ndichavadzorera kwandiri nokuna Yahweh uye ndichavapa mabasa mazhinji akarurama ayo Yahweh angada kuti vaite muzvinangwa zvake zvisingaperi uye zvisingagumi. Naizvozvo, chipo choupenyu husingaperi chiri mumaoko angu kune vose vanokumbira nounyoro nokutendeuka uye vanouya kwandiri munguva dzakapiwa dzekutendeuka.

Ndini Yeshua HaMashiach, uye ndinokudai vanhu vekwangu uye ndiri kuuya kuzokununurai uye ndichaponesa vanoteerera pakati penyu kusvika pakupedzisira, naizvozvo teererai Mashoko aINI NDINI mukuru mukuteerera Mesia wenyu wechokwadi, maari gwayana rinofamba pamwe chete neshumba, nokuti zvirokwazvo, ndini.
