Dandemutande Rakasvika

Inzwai, inzwai, Mashoko angu imi Israeri, uye kotamisai mwoyo yenyu mukuteerera inzwi rangu, mugamuchire nounyoro Mashoko aYeshua HaMashiach, kuti neMashoko eEvhangeri isingaperi muwane ruponeso uye musvike kuupenyu husingaperi muna Mesia wenyu wechokwadi, uyo anokurwirai zvirokwazvo murudo. Saka ndinouya kuzokuyambirai, kunyange imi vekwaJakobho nevose vanoverenga nekugamuchira mashoko eEvhangeri isingaperi mukutenda kunoponesa, kuti vave nekutenda muna Yeshua HaMashiach, anovawo Jesu Kristu, uye kuti vave nekutenda muna Yahweh Mukuru NDIRI IYE, uyo Evhangeri isingaperi yakaramba yakavanzwa mumwoyo make kusvikira panguva yekuzarurwa kwayo, uye saka Johane Muratidziri akasimbisa manzwi emabhanan'ana manomwe aakanzwa. Uye saka ndinouya kuzokuyambirai pamusoro pedandemutande raruka dandemutande raro uye rabata nyika mukumbundira kwaro; kunyange parinodya ropa rehupenyu hwevamwe, uye kuti mugone kunzvenga matandemutande aro mazhinji kuitira kuti murege kutorwa mukumbundira kwaro kunouraya.

Naizvozvo, kotamisai mwoyo yenyu mukuteerera uye munzwe mashoko aMesia wenyu wechokwadi, iye Yeshua HaMashiach! Nokuti zvirokwazvo ndinouya kuzoita kuti munhu akachenjera pakati penyu awedzere kuchenjera, kuti mugone kunzwisisa nguva dzamunorarama madziri, uye kuti mugone kunzwisisisa vavengi venyu nemaitiro avo nenzira dzavo dzenhema; nokuti vanofamba muchivande chikuru nekunyengera, uye vanorwa nekubiridzira. Nokuti vose vanonyepedzera vanonyepedzera, uye dandemutande rinofamba muchivande chikuru nekunyengera kusvikira mhuka yedandemutande yabatiwa mudandemutande raro, iro dandemutande richagara richishandisa pazvinangwa zvaro zvakaipa.

Naizvozvo, ndinouya kuzokuyambirai pamusoro pedandemutande uye ndikuratidzei nzira dzaro kuitira kuti mugone kunzvenga uturu hwaro uye muzvichengetedze kubva mukumbundira kwaro kunoremadza nekunouraya. Asi idandemutande ripi rakambozviitira umambo nenyika? Uye idandemutande ripi rakambobatana nemubatanidzwa wevakawa, richitoita chikamu charo nemufaro mukuipa kwekupanduka? Uye idandemutande ripi rakambotora zvitupa zveavo vakabatwa mudandemutande raro? Asi ndizvo zvakaita dandemutande randiri kuuya kuzokuyambirai pamusoro paro, uye zvirokwazvo, zvirokwazvo, dandemutande chimwe chezvikamu zvizhinji zveumambo hwevavengi, uye idandemutande rine uturu uye rinouraya zvirokwazvo.

Nokuti dandemutande rinoruka dandemutande raro muchivande nekubiridzira. Kunyange dandemutande rekunyengera nedandemutande rechikwereti ndizvo zvinorukwa nedandemutande, uye dandemutande raro rava kupetera nyika yose mukumbundira kwaro kwechivande. Funga nezvedandemutande munzvimbo yaro yechisikigo, rinoruka dandemutande raro muchivande munzvimbo dzarinogona kubata mhuka dzaro, kunyange mhuka dzaro dzichimhara zvisingazivi mudandemutande raro. Uye apo nyajambwa waro anorwira kuzvisunungura kubva mudandemutande raakabatwa mariri asingazivi, achizvisunga zvakare, dandemutande rinobva ratanga kushanda richibaya uturu hwaro apo rinopetera mhuka yaro mudandemutande raro. Zvino dandemutande rinochengeta mhuka yaro pazvinangwa zvaro uye rinodya zvishoma nezvishoma nyajambwa waro kusvikira pasisina chinosara, richisiya nyajambwa waro ari dongo zvachose kusvikira zvirokwazvo nyajambwa waro asisipo.

Asi ndidzo nzira dzedandemutande rechisikigo rakasikirwa zvinangwa zvaYahweh, kunze kwekuti ndiani pakati penyu akamboona dandemutande richishanda pamwechete neumambo nenyika yemamwe madandemutande; asi vakawa vakabatanidza madandemutande avo munguva dzino dzekupedzisira. Uye saka ndinouya kuzokuyambirai pamusoro pevanhu vakabva kuna Jafeti, akabereka Gomeri, uye kubva kuna Gomeri kwakauya Ashikenazi, uye kubva kuna Ashikenazi rudzi nokuti Ashikenazi ari mutafura yenyika mumirairo yekare kunyange mumazuva apo vanhu vairarama pasi pechibvumirano chaNoa, chibvumirano icho vanhu vakasiya; asi kwakanga kuna Abramu akatendeka aitsvaga nzira dzaYahweh apo nyika yakapandukira zvakare musiki wavo. Asi kunyange zvakadaro, munhu wechisikigo akaramba chibvumirano chaYahweh; Yahweh akatendeka kuzvipikirwa zvake saizvozvo. Uye hapana chipikirwa chimwe chete chechibvumirano chakaitwa naYahweh naNoa (uyo aiva munhu akarurama pamberi paYahweh) chakambowira pasi chisina kuzadziswa, uye zvose zvichava hongu naamen nokusingaperi-peri nokuti Yahweh anogara akatendeka zvakakwana!

Mushure mekunge nyika yafashamwa mumazuva aNoa, nyika yakakamurwa nemijenya yenhaka pakati pevanakomana vaNoa mumazuva aPeregi, uye saka Jafeti akaenda kunhaka yake munyika dzekuchamhembe. Uye vanhu vakanga vari vemutauro mumwe, uye vanakomana vaHamu vakaungana munyika yakazova Bhabhironi. Uye machinda aNimirodhi akarangana pamwechete naPuti, Miziraimi, Kushi, naKenani nemhuri dzavo kuti vavake guta ravo uye mariri shongwe yakasimba, kunyange shongwe yakakwirira, kuitira kuti vagone kurwisa matenga uye kuti vazviunganidze kuitira kuti vazviitire zita mukupandukira murairo waYahweh wekubereka nekuwanda kuitira kuti vagone kuzadza nyika zvakare. Asi ndidzo nzira dzemunhu wechisikigo asina kutenda, uyo mukuchiva kwake nekuzvikudza anoshumira chivi uye naizvozvo anoshumira Baba vechivi; iye dhiabhorosi, nenzira yekusateerera kwevanhu kuna Yahweh nekuda kwechivi chiri munyama yemunhu wechisikigo.

Uye kubva kuvazukuru vaNimirodhi vakakura mukuipa zvinonzi kubva kune vakaipa kunobuda uipi huzhinji. Uye saka Nimirodhi akasvibisa nyika nenzira dzevakawa, uye kubva kuvaroyi vake nen'anga dzake kwakauya zvikoro zvezvakavanzika zvekare uko varoyi vevakawa vaidzidzisa zvakavanzika zvavo zvisina maturo kune avo vachakandwa mumirazvo nokusingaperi-peri muzvivi zvaSatani, uye vachabatana navo munhaka yavo isingaperi. Uye Nimirodhi akasimuka muumambo hwekutanga mushure memafashamo aNoa, kunyange apo Nimirodhi akatonga nyika mukusarurama; kunyange kutonga uko vakawa vanotsvaga kuzviwanira zvizere pamusoro penyika, asi hapana zera regoridhe ravachava naro asi kutongwa kwakaomarara.

Saka Yahweh mukutonga kwake akaita kuti nyika ishamise uye imedze chikamu chimwe chete pazvitatu cheshongwe yavo, uye moto wakaburuka kubva kudenga uchiparadza chimwe chikamu chetatu cheshongwe, uye chikamu chetatu chakabvumirwa kusara sechiratidzo pamusoro pavo. Naizvozvo, zita racho rinonzi Bhabheri, nokuti Yahweh akavhiringidza ipapo mutauro wenyika yose: uye kubva ipapo Yahweh akavaparadzira pamusoro penyika yose. Uye nyika dzakawanda nevanhu vakaramba uye vakakanganwa Yahweh akapinda muchibvumirano naNoa, uye saka vakakanganwa chibvumirano chakaitwa naNoa naYahweh maererano nenhaka yavo uye maererano nemitauro yavaidaura vakakanganwa chibvumirano chaYahweh naNoa, uye vakatendeukira kunzira dzevakawa nokuti hapana imwe sarudzo. (Asi Abramu akava Abrahama uye vakarurama vachararama nekutenda!)

PaBhabhironi Nimirodhi akaita zvakaipa zvizhinji kunyange paakagadzira zvidhori zvizhinji uye akazviti mwari uye akagadzira utatu hwake hwekumhura, kunyange vatatu vasina utsvene, zvakaitirwa kuseka umwari hwechokwadi nembeu yakapikirwa yemukadzi uye kutsausa vanakomana vevanhu mukusarurama. Uye saka Nimirodhi akatanga zvaainzi zvikoro zvezvakavanzika sezvo ruzivo rwevakawa rwakare rwakare rwakatanga kupararira nekufukidza nyika uye vanhu vakare vakare vakashandura chokwadi chaYahweh kuva nhema uye vakanamata nekushumira chisikwa kupfuura musiki anorumbidzwa nokusingaperi-peri, Ameni!

Uye sezvo vanhu vakaparadzirwa kubva kuBhabhironi, saizvozvowo ruzivo rwenhema nenzira dzevakawa zvakaparadzirwa navo, kunyange nemitambo yavo yose inosemesa nekumhura yeavo vanodya nyama neropa uye avo vanonwa ropa raAdhamu uko hupenyu hwenyama hunogara; kunyange tsika dzakaora zvachose uye dzinonyangadza dzakarambidzwa naYahweh kamwe, zvose, uye nokusingaperi-peri nokuti Adhamu akaitwa mumufananidzo waYahweh uye hupenyu hwenyama huri muropa. Asi vanoenderera mberi nenzira dzavo dzinosemesa nedzekumhura kusvikira nhasi, uye tsika dzavo nemitambo yavo zvakachengetedzwa mune avo vanonamata madhimoni uye vanopfugamira zvidhori zvisingatauri uye idzi ndidzo nzira dzedandemutande, asi dandemutande rinonyepedzera kuva Jakobho akarurama uyo risina mugove kana nhaka maari.

Uye saka nzira dzezvakaipa dzakadzika midzi munzira dzevanhu venyika zvakare. Uye kubva kuna Gomeri kwakauya Ashikenazi uye kubva kuna Ashikenazi nekufamba kwenguva kwakauya rudzi rukuru rwevanhu vakava vafambi vairarama nekutenderera munzvimbo yeEurasia nenzvimbo dzayo dzakapoteredza kunyange kwavakaita umambo hwavo. Kunyange izvo, umambo hwavo hwakakura mukusarurama uye hwakawira kuvavengi vavo saizvozvo. Kunyange avo vavengi vavo vakavarwisa nekuda kwekusarurama kwavo uye panzvimbo pekugamuchira Jesu weNazareta nekubva pane zvakaipa vanonyepedzera kuva Jakobho, uye kubva kuna Jakobho hakuna kumbova nedzinza raAshikenazi raJafeti. Kunyange zvakadaro vakaramba Jesu weNazareta nokuti ndevebaba vavo dhiabhorosi uye kuchiva kwake vakasungirwa kuita nokusingaperi-peri.

Uye kubva kuvanhu veAshikenazi vane nzira dzaiva dzakaipa nedzekunyengera kwakauya vanhu, kunze kwekuti verudzi rwaAdhamu vakanga vasiri zvizere, asi masora akaipa akadyarwa pakati pegorosi raYahweh vakazvisvibisa nokusingaperi mukusarurama. Kunyange avo vakaberekwa nababa vavo dhiabhorosi nokuti mbeu yadhiabhorosi ichiripo, kunyange ndichaidzura zvachose uye ndichaikanda muchoto sezvakataurwa naYahweh. Uye kubva kuna Ashikenazi kwakauya dandemutande asi rakanga risiri dandemutande rechisikigo, asi rudzi rwemadandemutande anonyangadza kunyange akashandurwa kubva pamufananidzo waYahweh musiki wavo, avo vakasungirwa nokusingaperi-peri kukuchiva kwaBaba vavo dhiabhorosi uko kuchiva kwavo vachaita. Uye saka madandemutande akaparadzirwa, kunyange paakatiza kubva mumaoko evavengi vekusarurama vakamira vachipikisa nzira dzedandemutande. Uye saka Ashikenazi akaramba Yeshua HaMashiach, uyo vasina mugove kana nhaka maari.

Saka muchokwadi vanhu vakatsauswa nevakawa kubvira pakawa kwaAdhamu kubva pamamiriro ake asina chivi aakasikirwa mumunda weEdeni uye huori hwakapararira panyika kuburikidza nerudzi rwaKaini. Uko huori hwacho varindi vakawa vakawedzera kusvibisa pavakaburukira paGomo reHerimoni mumazuva aJaredhi. Naizvozvo, Yahweh akasuka huori hwavo kubva pachiso chenyika mumafashamo makuru iwayo mumazuva aNoa asi vakawa vakadzorera nzira dzavo mushure memafashamo nokuti saizvozvo Yahweh akabvumira vanhu kuzvisarudzira. Nokuti zvirokwazvo, zvirokwazvo, Yahweh akapa zvose zvaakasika zvine rusununguko rwekusarudza mukusika kwake rusununguko rwekusarudza kana vachararama nenzira dzaYahweh kana kusarudza kushumira Satani mukupanduka kwavo uko kupanduka kwava pedyo nekuguma. Nokuti ini, kunyange Yeshua HaMashiach, ndini ndichapwanya musoro wenyoka, kunyange iye achaparadza zvachose umambo hwake kuti husazomukazve.

Saka ndizvo zvazviri uye ndicho chokwadi chandakadzidzisa pamusoro peumambo hwekudenga pandakauya kuna Israeri seGwayana raMwari panguva yangu munyama muJudhiya. Nokuti, zvakanyorwa kuti: Umambo hwekudenga hwakafanana nemunhu akadyara mbeu yakanaka mumunda make. Asi vanhu vakarara, kwakauya muvengi wake, akadyara masora pakati pegorosi, akaenda. Uye shizha parakamera, rikabereka zvibereko, ipapo masora akaonekwawo. Zvino varanda vemuridzi wemba vakauya, vakati kwaari, Tenzi, hamuna kudya mbeu yakanaka mumunda menyu here? Saka masora abvepi? Akati kwavari, Mumwe munhu ane godo ndiye aita izvi. Zvino varanda vakati kwaari, Munoda here kuti tiende kunoaunganidza? Asi iye akati, Kwete, zvimwe muchienda kunounganidza masora, modzurawo gorosi pamwechete nawo. Regai zvose zvikure pamwechete kusvikira pakukohwa, uye panguva yekukohwa ndichati kuvakohwi, Tanga kuunganidza masora, muasunge muzvisote kuti mupiswe: asi unganidzai gorosi mudura rangu.

Kunyange zvakadaro nokuti gorosi rinokotama mukuteerera asi masora anomira akati twasu mukushora kwakaipa. Sera!

Asi ndizvo zvazviri kuti nguva dzekuunganidza dzasvika, uye ndichaunganidza gorosi rangu mudura raYahweh nokuti nguva yekukohwa zvibereko zvekutanga yasvika. Uye zvichaitika kuti ndichadzura zvachose masora uye ndichiasunga, uye achakandwa mumoto mukutongwa kwawo. Saka nzwisisai nyika yamunogara uye kunyange hondo yamakaberekerwa mairi. Kunyange hondo yembeu yakarwiwa kubvira mumunda weEdeni uko Yahweh akazivisa hondo kunyoka nevakawa nekuda kweuipi hwavo uye nekusvibisa munda waYahweh. Uko hondo yacho inoguma mukukundwa kwakakwana kune vose vakapanduka nenzira yakadaro nevakawa uye nhaka yavo yesarufa inopfuta yakavamirira.

Uye saka Yahweh akazivisa kuti: Ndichaisa ruvengo pakati pako nemukadzi uye pakati pembeu yako nembeu yake, uye iwe uchapwanya chitsitsinho chake uye iye achapwanya musoro wako. Uye saka chitsitsinho changu chakapwanywa pakurovererwa kwangu pandakazvipa sePasika yakakwana seGwayana raMwari nekuzvidira mumaoko evavengi vangu vane utsinye. Uye ini ndiri mbeu yakapikirwa yemukadzi ichapwanya zvachose musoro waSatani uye ndichaita vavengi vangu chitsiko chetsoka dzangu, nokuti ndiri Shumba yeDzinza raJudha uye mukururama ndinouya kuzotonga nekurwa!

Naizvozvo, nzwisisai kuti hondo yemhodzi inokundwa zvizere muna Kristu Jesu kunyange sezvandakaita kuti Petro anyore makore mazhinji apfuura: Kuzvarwa patsva, kwete nemhodzi inoora, asi neisingaori, neshoko raMwari rinorarama nekusingaperi. Uye zvakare ndakaita kuti Johane Muratidziri anyore mutsamba yake: Ani naani akaberekwa naMwari, haatadzi; nekuti mhodzi yake inogara maari, uye haagoni kutadza, nekuti akaberekwa naMwari. Mune izvi vana vaMwari vanoonekwa, nevana vadhiabhorosi: ani naani asingaiti zvakarurama, haasi waMwari, kana uyo asingadi hama yake. Naizvozvo, vose vakaiswa chisimbiso muna Kristu havangambosvibiswi nenzira yakadaro uye havangagoni kutadza kwavanoshandurwa kubva pachisimbiso chaMwari mupenyu, iye Yahweh wavanova vake. Naizvozvo, musatendera upenzi hwakadaro kuti hukunetserei kuti avo vakazvarwa patsva nemweya wangu mutsvene wekuva vana uye vakaiswa chisimbiso chaYahweh vangambotorwa kubva muruoko rwangu rune simba nenzira ipi zvayo. Naizvozvo, kune avo vakawana ruponeso, ruponeso rwenyu rwakachengeteka.

Uye saka nzira dzemudandemutande ndichadzizarura, uye ndichazarura mudandemutande zvizere, kunyange zvazvo madandemutande achida kufamba muchivande chikuru, ndichazofumura nzira dzawo. Nekuti madandemutande akashandurwa aya ndivo vanotora zvitupa zveavo vavanoronga kuuraya. Aya madandemutande anofarira hutsinye hukuru uye anoda kutambudza mhuka dzawo pasina chimwe chikonzero kunze kwekugutsa kuchiva kwaBaba vavo vavanosungirwa kushandira uye vavanova vana vavo. Madandemutande akashandurwa aya andinotaura pamusoro pawo ndivo makororo, nembavha, nemhondi nevanyengeri (uye zvimwe zvakawanda zvavanopomerwa) vasingamboguti uye vanochiva rufu rwaAdhamu kuti vave nenguva yavo yegoridhe nekusingaperi pamwe chete neavo vakawa. Uye saka madandemutande afamba pasi rose uye akaruka matande awo pamwe chete muchivande chikuru.

Kunyange dandemutande guru rechikwereti nekuti mudandemutande unobudirira uye wakazviita mupfumi negoridhe rawakawana kuburikidza nechimbadzo chisina kururama. Mudandemutande wakazvivanza pakati paJakobho uye wakazvishandura kuva saJakobho uye vakazviitira chifukidzo chinogutsa. Nekuti mudandemutande haugoni kuzviratidza sezvauri chaizvo, mudandemutande wakaipa, wakashandurwa; nekuti muteyi upi anoteera musungo pamberi peshiri? Asi mudandemutande unozvishandura uye unoshandura chitarisiko chawo nemaitiro awo kuti uenderane neavo waunoda kudya. Uye mudandemutande paunotanga kuruka dandemutande rawo pakati peavo vakabvumira mudandemutande nzvimbo yekuruka pakati perudzi rwavo vasingazivi; mudandemutande unobva waunza mamwe madandemutande anouya kuzobatana nawo mukukunda kwawo kwakaipa kana nenzira yemamwe masora anobuda muzai remudandemutande anokurumidza kuenda kunoruka matande awo uye kuenderera mberi nedenderedzwa rerufu. Uye saka Adhamu akasungwa uye akaputirwa nemadandemutande aya akaipa. Uye zvishoma nezvishoma uye nemufaro mukuru vanoteya nekudya Adhamu uye nehutsinye hukuru neruvengo mudandemutande unotsvaga nyika yose kuti ive yavo naBaba vavo kune kuchiva kwavo kwavakasungirwa.

Nekuti mukunyengera kwawo madandemutande eAshkenazi akazvibatanidza naEsau, nenzira yeTalmud nekuti mumakore mazhinji apfuura vakatora Talmud semutungamiriri wavo nemutongi, asi havasi vateveri vechokwadi vaMosesi, uye havateveri nzira yaYeshua HaMashiach asi vanonyepedzera. Uye vaEsau ndivo vaiva mhondi dzangu uye neuchenjeri hwenhema hwaEsau, kunyange tsika dzavo dzinomhura dzevanhu dzavanokwidziridza pamusoro penzira dzaYahweh, vakasvibisa Jakobho uye vakateya hama yaEsau kakawanda. Kunyange zvakadaro ndakati kwavari panguva yangu munyama seGwayana raMwari: Imi muri vababa venyu dhiabhorosi, uye kuchiva kwababa venyu muchakuita: anga ari mhondi kubva pakutanga, uye haana kugara muchokwadi, nekuti hamuna chokwadi maari. Paanotaura nhema, anotaura zvake: nekuti murevi wenhema, nababa vadzo.

Asi mudandemutande hauna kumbobva panzira dzevakawa uye mudandemutande uchiri kutora chikamu uye unofarira maitiro awo akaipa. Nekuti mudandemutande unodya vana vawakaputira semhuka yawo uye unonwa ropa ravo kubva kuvadiki vaunotambudza mukumbundira kwawo. Nekuti mudandemutande unozadzisa kuchiva kwawo kunosemesa pavadiki kubva kurudzi rwavanobata muchivande chikuru sezvo vadiki vachitorwa mudandemutande rawo. Uye mudandemutande upi wakambovapo unomboteya dandemutande uye uchichera pasi. Nekuti dandemutande remudandemutande rinoenda pasi penyika uye dandemutande raro rinofukidza nzvimbo huru uye umambo hwenyika yose uye ushe ndizvo zvinotsvagwa nemudandemutande. Uye saka mudandemutande unofamba mukunyengera kukuru nekuti nekunyengera vanoita hondo.

Asi vanyengeri ava vakazvibatanidza neavo vakawa nekusingaperi uye vavanova vana vavo chaivo; vanonyepedzera kuva Jakobho uye vakataura zvenhema kuti vane kodzero kunyika yechipikirwa, iyo yavasina mugove kana nhaka mairi asi vari vanyengeri vanonyepedzera. Uye munzira dzavo munouya musungo uyo chikara chinobva panyika chichateyera Jakobho nekuti nenzira yeTalmud yaEsau, Ashkenazi yakabatana naEsau, uye vachawedzera kubatana negwayana reshato, kunyange gwayana ravo renhema rine nyanga mbiri. Kunyange gwayana risiri rechisikigo apo rumwe runyanga runotonga gungwa maererano neNoahide uye rumwe runyanga runotonga nyika yechipikirwa mukumhura kukuru nekunyengera nekuti ndiye runyanga rudiki rwaDhanieri, uye saka nyika ichatongwa nekutonga kwavo kwenguva pfupi. Nekuti vachabatanidza mauto ose nezvikwata zvehuipi pasi pekutonga kwavo, uye vachavaunganidza pamwe chete kuhondo pazuva iro guru, kwavachakandirwa pasi nechisimba nekusingaperi uye mukurangwa kusingaperi nekutongwa vachaenda nekusingaperi.

Asi nzira dzemudandemutande inzira dzekubiridzira, nekutorera, nekuuraya, nehuori uye nzira dzakawanda dzakaipa dzinodzidziswa nekugara nemudandemutande nekuti mudandemutande unogara uchitsvaga kuita mamwe madandemutande anova vana vegehena zvakapetwa kaviri seivo.
