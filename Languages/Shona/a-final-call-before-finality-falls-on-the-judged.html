Dano Rekupedzisira Kusati Kupera Kwawira Pamusoro Pevakatongwa

Tarirai! NDINI Yahweh, Mukuru NDINI, uye ndinouya kuzotumira Mashoko angu erudo nedano rekupedzisira kune avo vakatarisana nekuguma kwekutongwa kana vakaramba dano rangu rekupedzisira. Kunyange zvazvo, vose vanodana pazita raJesu kusvikira kumagumo vachawana ruponeso rukuru rusingaperi nemubairo mukuru, varume vazhinji vazhinji vachawira mukutongwa nekuda kwezvivi zvavo nekuoma kwemwoyo yavo. Nokuti, NDINI iye anopa vasakarurama mhosva kuti vatendeuke, uye avo vanoramba kutendeuka ndinoramba ndichivapa mhosva kusvikira mwoyo yavo yaoma zvachose uye vatorwa mukutongwa.

Naizvozvo, ndinouya kuzopa shoko iri murudo rwangu kwamuri, uye ndotumira nyasha dzangu dzisina kufanirwa kunyika yakatongwa zvakare mukuedza kwangu kunyorovesa mwoyo yenyu isingatendeuki kuti mugamuchire ruponeso; kana kuti muruvengo rwenyu nekupanduka musiiwe mumamiriro ekuramba kwekupedzisira, maibva uye makagadzirira kutongwa kwekupedzisira. Nzwisisai, hapachina nguva munguva dzangu dzenyasha nengoni dzehupenyu hwose hwekutendeuka nekuda kwezvirongwa zvangu zveruponeso rwevanhu vose vachasvika paruponeso ruzere munguva dzavo nemirairo kune avo vachagamuchira rudo rwangu kwavari. Naizvozvo, nguva dzenyu ndedzino uye dano rekupedzisira rava kutumirwa. Selah.

Inzwai, O' Inzwai izwi raMwari Mukuru Asingaperi weKururama, iye Yahweh, Mukuru NDINI, uyo ariwo Mwari Asingaperi weKururama uye munzwisise zvinoreva kutaura kwangu, uye muzive kuti izwi rangu rakaita semvura zhinji inomhanya nekurarama, uye munzwisise kuti izwi rangu rinotinhira sekutinhira kwemabhanan'ana mazhinji, (kunyange mabhanan'ana emabhanan'ana) mukutsamwa kwangu kwakarurama kune vagari vepanyika vanogara mairi mukusateerera kukuru nekusarurama kukuru nechivi.

NDINI zvandiri, iye Mukuru NDINI, uye ndakasika matenga nenyika nekuzara kwose kwazvo, uye muchirongwa changu chakarurama ndakaita matenga nenyika zvakakwana munzira dzangu dzekukwana, kunyange kuzvisika mukukwana kunowirirana nekunorurama uko zvisikwa zvangu zvose zvaifanira kurarama mukukwana kunowirirana nerudo nebasa murudo kune mumwe nemumwe. Uye murudo rwavo kuna Baba vavo nemusiki wavo, vaizoteerera izwi rangu nemirairo yehupenyu hwavo kuti zvirokwazvo vanzwisise ivo pachavo sezvavakanga vakasikwa uye vanzwisisewo nzira dzangu. Zvisinei, kutadza kwakawanikwa muna Heylel, iye akawa kubva mukukwana kunobwinya, iye akasikwa mukubwinya kukuru nekunoshamisa, kungozowira pasi, akaderedzwa muchivi nerima kuti agare muchimiro chenyoka dzinotukwa nekuda kwekusateerera kwevaSerafimi. Selah.

Nekuda kwechokwadi chisingaperi, nzira dzangu dzakakwirira kupfuura nzira dzemunhu, uye NDINI iye akakomberedza chigaro changu cheushe nezviuru gumi zvakapetwa nezviuru gumi, kunyange nhamba isingaverengeki yengirozi dzinopfuura muhuchenjeri, simba, nesimba guru, uye dzinoda Yahweh uye dzinoda kudzidza nzira dzaYahweh, Mukuru NDINI mukuteerera kwose kunopisa nekusingatadzi: Selah.

Zvirokwazvo, zvechokwadi chikuru chisingaperi, ndakavasika vane simba uye vanobwinya uye ndikavafukidza nekubwinya kunopenya, nekururama kusingaperi, nerukudzo rusingaperi. Zvirokwazvo, zvechokwadi chikuru chisingaperi, havana kumboshaiwa kana kushaya kunyange muzvinhu zvidiki diki zvachose; uye vanondishumira uye vanoshumira chisikwa changu zvizere mumwoyo yavo yerudo kuna Baba vavo nemusiki wavo, iye Elohim, mukuteerera kwavo kuna Kristu Jesu.

(Kunyange Iye uyo, Yahweh, Mukuru uye Asingaperi NDINI akaisa zvinhu zvose kudenga nepanyika mumaoko ake ane simba uye asingaperi uye anoteerera.)

Naizvozvo zivai chokwadi chisingaperi kuti Kristu Jesu ndiye Ishe wemadzishe naMambo wemadzimambo uye ndiye achauyisa rugare kumatenga pamwe chete nepanyika uye ndiye achadzokera kuruoko rwangu runobwinya (murudo rwake kuna Baba vake naMusiki, Elohim, iye Mukuru NDINI) Umambo hwakaiswa pasi pamagumo ezvinhu zvose apo maoko ake ane simba achange apedza zvinhu zvose zvakanyorwa nekufanotaurwa pamusoro paIshe Asingaperi weKururama! Kunyange apo Mwanakomana wemunhu iyeye achange apedza zvinhu zvose nesimba guru, uye nerudo rukuru. Kunyange panguva iyoyo, ndichange ndapedza nesimba rangu kuburikidza nemaoko ake ane simba nechibayiro zvinhu zvose zvakapihwa muzvirongwa zvangu zvisingaperi nezvakavanzika kuti iye azviite.

Naizvozvo, ndinokupai kuti munzwisise, kuti NDINI iye uyo musimba rose nehutongi anogona kunzwisisa, kuita, kuziva zvizere, nekudzora zvese zvine chekuita nechikamu chese chechisikwa changu zvisinei nekuti chakakura sei kana kuti chine simba sei kusvika kune chidiki diki, uye hapana chinogona kuvanzwa pamberi pangu! NDINI iye anogona kuita zvinhu zvese pakarepo musimba rangu nesimba risingaperi. Zvisinei, ndiniwo iye anoshanda zvinhu zvese maererano nezano rekuda kwangu uye asingade chipangamazano, naizvozvo, zvirongwa zvangu nezvakavanzika zvichazadziswa zvizere munguva idzi dzekuzadziswa kwazvo, kunyange nguva dzeZvino.

Zvisinei, murudo rwangu kuchisikwa changu nechishuwo changu chevana nemhuri, ndinosika zvisikwa nevanhu chaivo (vane rusununguko rwekusarudza) nekuda kwezvinangwa zvangu zvisingaperi zvakapihwa kune vanoteerera mubasa guru nerukudzo, uye ndinovapa simba maererano nazvo. Naizvozvo, ndinokurairai kuti mudzidze nzira dzangu muruponeso kana kusarudza mirazvo yekuparadzwa nekusingaperi. Asi zivai, O' munhu akawa, mune sarudzo iyo kamwe kamwe zvino ichachinja kuva maiva nesarudzo, uye vose vachange vasarudza zvakaipa mukupedzisira kana kupera kwasvika, havazombopukunyuki kutongwa kwavo nekusingaperi uye nokusingagumi. Selah.

Ndiwe ani, O' munhu akawa, kunyange iwe wakazvibatanidza nekupanduka kusina musoro, kweupenzi, nekupenga kwevakawa mukusarurama kwose, makaro, ruchiva, nekutsauka kuti muzvikudze mukuzvitutumadza muchipikisa Mwari Mukuru Asingaperi akakusika nekuda kwezvinangwa zvangu nemufaro wangu?

Ndiwe ani, O' munhu akawa, kuti mukusaziva kwako neruchiva ungashinga kusimudza chitsitsinho chako uchipikisa Mwari mukuru asingaperi weDenga reMatenga (kunyange IYE akakupa upenyu) munzira dzevaSatani? Naizvozvo, nzwisisa zvinangwa zvangu nechinangwa changu nekuti ndiri kutumira Mashoko aya KURI iwe sedanho rangu rekupedzisira kune vazhinji venyu vanofunga zvisina musoro kuti mune nguva yamusina, uye ndiri kukupai mashoko eyambiro murudo rwangu rusingafi kwamuri nekukuyeuchidzai zvakare pamusoro pezviziviso zvekutongwa nenjodzi zvakatopihwa zvamakaregeredza zvizere zvakaitwa naKristu Jesu pachigaro cheushe nesimba raYahweh, uye zvakaziviswawo naYahweh pachake, iye Mukuru NDINI!

(Hamunzwisisi here kuti zviziviso zvangu hazvigoni kuchinjwa?)

Asi, munotoregeredza kutaura kwemabhanan'ana manomwe andakaratidza, uye ndikaita kuti Johane MuZaruri aise chisimbiso panguva yake kusvikira kunguva dzakakodzera dzekuzarurwa idzo nguva dzacho dziri zvino. (Uye zvichaenderera mberi kusvikira uchapupu hwezvapupu zvangu zviviri hwagumiswa mukutongwa kwekupedzisira, nguva pfupi dambudziko guru risati rawira zvizere pamusoro pevasakarurama, Selah.) Kunyange mabhanan'ana iwayo akataurwa naYeshua HaMashiach naYahweh, Mukuru NDINI, kubva pachigaro changu cheushe chine simba chekuzivisa nekutonga, uye zvino zvapihwa neruoko rwenhume yangu ine simba yandinotumira nayo Mashoko aya erudo neyambiro yakasimba uye yakaoma zvikuru, kuitira kuti vose vangauya vauye, kutonga kusati kwawira zvizere uye pasisina nguva!

Naizvozvo, kana muchida kupukunyuka kutongwa kwenyu nekuparadzwa kusingaperi, ndinokurayirai kuti muite Jesu Ishe wenyu zvizere uye pasina kuzeza. Nokuti kutonga kwangu kuri kuwira, uye kuchawira pamusoro peumwe neumwe waanotumirwa, kunyange kutonga ikoko kwekurashwa kusingaperi mukupedzisira kwako nekusingaperi uye nokusingagumi. Naizvozvo, ndinopa zano kune vose vasati vareurura Jesu Kristu saIshe kubva pamwoyo uzere nekutenda kuti vazviite zvino, vachiziva kuti NDINI iye akamumutsa kubva kuvakafa nesimba guru kwazvo kuti arege kuzofa zvakare uye aenderere mberi munhaka yake nekusingaperi uye nokusingagumi. Zvisinei, ruzhinji rukuru ruchawira mukutongwa kukuru kusingaperi sezvakanyorwa chaizvo muzvinyorwa zviTsvene zvekare nezvinyorwa zviTsvene zvezvino.

Asi ndinouya kuzopa yambiro dzangu zvakadaro, kunyange mushure mekunge zviziviso zvekutongwa kwenyu kwekupedzisira zvaziviswa uye zvatotanga. Nokuti nzira dzangu inzira dzenyasha nengoni nerudo uye dzechokwadi chisingaperi, ndinoshuva kuti parege kuva neanoparara asi kuti vanhu vose vasvike paruponeso rusingaperi nekudzoreredzwa, kunyange zvazvo vazhinji vacharamba chipo changu uye vosarudza kutambudzwa kwavo kusingaperi nekutongwa panzvimbo pazvo. Kunyange zvazvo, ndinouya kuzonyevera vanhu, hapana yambiro dzakapihwa dzichachinja kutonga kwangu, kana nguva yacho, kana mhedzisiro yacho, kana zvirongwa zvangu zvisingaperi zvekudzikinura zvibereko zvangu zvekutanga, kunyange kuvabvuta kuti vazadzise nhaka yavo isingaperi njodzi isati yawira. Selah.

Uye ndinotumira shoko iri rerudo neyambiro murudo rwangu kumunhu wechisikigo akaipa, kunyange murudo rumwe cheterwo rwandinotumira mashoko erudo neyambiro kuvana vangu pachangu vakagamuchira Jesu Kristu nekuteerera; nokuti ndinoita kuti kunaye pamusoro pevakarurama nevasakarurama zvakafanana. Nekuda kwerudo rwangu kwamuri, ndinouya kuzopa dano rangu rekupedzisira njodzi isati yarova kubva pachigaro changu cheushe chesimba risingaperi, kutonga, nekuzivisa uko izwi rangu rinotinhira richiri kutinhira mukutsamwa kukuru kwakarurama. Kunyange zvakadaro, nokuti IMI MUNORAMBA kundinzwa kunyange zvazvo ndakayambira nekayambira kwemakore akawanda, akawanda emajubhiri zvino! Ndinzwei uye ndinzwei zvakanaka nokuti nguva dzenyu dzekuparadzwa kusingaperi dzichatumirwa kuzosunga mukutonga vose vanoda uye vanoita nhema, uye vanofarira kusarurama, uye kana mukandiramba munguva idzi dzenyasha, muchawira mukutongwa kusingaperi nokuti hapana imwe sarudzo.

Asi, mukuzvitutumadza kwenyu kweupofu hwemwoyo wakaoma, munoramba munhu mumwe chete wandakatumira kuti muponeswe zvizere, iye Jesu weNazareta, kunyange Mwanakomana nemuranda waYahweh akaderera uyo wandakaita mweya unopa upenyu, kunyange akaitwa mudiki zvishoma kuna Yahweh pachake mukumuka kwangu kubva kuvakafa nekukudzwa kwaKristu Jesu! Nokuti NDINI mukuru uye ndine hutongi pamusoro pechisikwa changu chose uye ndiri wechokwadi chisingaperi uye chisingachinji, NDINI iye anotumira mvura pamusoro pevakarurama nevasakarurama zvakafanana murudo rwangu kuchisikwa changu, uye munzira dzangu hamuna kumbova nekusarura vanhu mukutonga kwangu kwakarurama pamusoro pechisikwa changu.

Naizvozvo, nzwisisai kuti kusarura vanhu ndeimwe yenzira dzakawanda dzakasvibiswa dzevakawa, saka ndinouya kuzonyevera kunyange mutadzi akaipisisa pakati pevagari vepanyika kutonga kwavo kusati kwarova zvizere, kuitira kuti vapukunyuke mumasekonzi mashoma asara uye anokurumidza kupera enyasha njodzi isati yarova. Saka ndinopa zano kune vose vanonzwa kana kuverenga Mashoko aya kuti vatendeukire kuna Jesu weNazareta pakarepo, vasingakahadziki chinhu nokuti kune boka guru revanhu hakuzovi nemumwe mukana, nokuti muzvivi zvavo zvakaipa nekuzvidza kwakanyanya zvinhu zvose zviTsvene, vachawira mukutongwa kusingaperi, kunyange vakachengetedzwa mukurwadziswa kwavanomirira kutongwa kukuru kwechigaro chichena, kwavasingazomiri asi panzvimbo pazvo vosungwa voendeswa kumirazvo yekurangwa uko zvivi zvavo zvichapihwa kumirazvo kuti zvibhadharwe zvizere mukururamisira nekusingaperi.

Asi, iwe, O' munhu akawa, waizogara munzira dzako dzisina kururama, kunyange wakurirwa zvachose nevakawira mukusaziva kwako neupofu hunokonzerwa nenhema dzavo, kunyange nhema dzaunoda nokuti unoda nzira dzenhema dzako. Naizvozvo, ndakatumira kunyika kunyengera kukuru uye kwakasimba kuti imi mose mupiwe mhosva imi munofarira kusarurama uye muchiramba Jesu weNazareta, iye Yeshua HaMashiach. Uye pamunenge muchitsvanzvadzira mukusaziva munzira dzenyu dzeupofu nedzemwoyo wakaoma, pamunenge mugere murima mukusava nehanya nechivi chikuru, pamunenge muchifamba zvisina maturo muchizviunganidzira kutsamwa kukuru nemigumisiro isingaperi mukusateerera kwenyu, ndichiri kusimudza izwi rangu, kunyange kutumira Mashoko aya seuchapupu kune avo vacharamba, kunyange netariro yekuti mungatendeukira kuna Kristu Jesu, kuti ndikuponesei kubva mukutongwa kwevakawa: Selah.

O' Munhu wechisikigo, kunyange imi mose vana vaAdhamu vasina kudzikinurwa uye vasingatendeuki, kunyange imi mose vandinoranga panyika murudo rwangu kwamuri! Asi makatenda nhema dzeupenzi dzamakapihwa sedzidzo mukudzidziswa kwenyu nevakawira. Kunyange dzidzo yerufu yakapihwa muzvikoro zvenyu zvakaora uye zvinooza zverufu uko nzira dzevasodhomi dzava kutonga sezvavanomaka nekugadzirira vana venyu chaivo kuti vafare nevasina mhosva muruchiva rwavo rwakaputika. Asi, makanyanya kunyengedzwa nemafaro echivi, uye makaremerwa nemakaro enyu ekuunganidza pfuma yakawanda, zvekuti munobayira vana venyu chaivo munzira dzaBhaari kuitira chidimbu chechingwa chakasvibiswa. Naizvozvo, ndakabvumira vakawira kuti vakanganise chikafu chenyu, uye ndakavapa kuwanda kwenyu kwose kuitira kuti dhiragoni, chikara chinobva mugungwa, nechikara chinobva panyika zvizadzise zvinangwa zvangu, nokuti kunyange vavengi vangu vanoteerera izwi rangu.

Nokuti dzidzo dzenyu dzinonzi dzidzo ndedzenhema uye dzakaora nechikonzero uye izvi nokuti munoramba ruzivo rwechokwadi nenzira dzaElohim musiki wenyu, muchisarudza panzvimbo pazvo kunyengedzwa nezvionioni zvenyu zvitema zvamunobata nekutendeka kwose pamunenge muchibva pachiratidziro kuenda kune chimwe murima guru. Asi makurirwa zvachose uye makapofumadzwa nemafaro akachipa uye anokurumidza kupera sezvo pfungwa dzenyu dzichishandurwa nemakemikari nechikonzero kuburikidza nemidziyo yamunozvisunga nayo muchivi. Uye saka ruzivo rwenhema runokonzera kudada uye kudada kunotangira kuparadzwa kukuru. Nzwisisa, O' Munhu, kuti ruzivo rwako harugoni kukuponesa kubva mukutonga kwangu, uye kutongwa kwevakawira kwava pedyo kuwira. Asi, iwe, O' Munhu wechisikigo wakapusa zvekutadza kunzwisisa kuti vakawira vakakudzidzisa nhema dzavo, kuitira kuti vakonzere varume vakawanda sezvinobvira kuti vabatane navo munhaka yavo yekutsva, kuputika, nekukunguruka kwesarufa. Kunyange chirango chakaoma chakagadzirirwa dhiabhorosi nengirozi dzake, kwete Adhamu. Selah.

Zvisinei, nekubvuma kwenyu kwemwoyo wose munodada zvikuru nezvamunoita zvisina maturo zvenyika uko munounganidza nemakaro mapepa enyu ekunyepedzera asina maturo nemadhijiti enyu ekunyepedzera asina maturo mumaakaunzi ekunyepedzera asina maturo ayo achatorwa kubva kwamuri kamwe kamwe mukutongwa zvino, kunyange nokuti idzi ndidzo nzira dzaEsau akatengesa udangwe hwake nechidimbu chechingwa. Asi, nekubvuma kwenyu kwemwoyo wose munobvumira vakawira kuti vatore mari kubva kwamuri nekutyisidzirwa kuti mubhadhare kudzidziswa kwevana venyu chaivo munzira dzerufu. Kuitira kuti vose vanodzidziswa mukusaziva kwakadaro neupofu vashumire vakawira vachifunga kuti vari kuitira Mwari pachake nyasha huru muzvivi zvavo, sekunge Yahweh anofanira kubva pachisikwa chake nehutongi hwake nekuda kwekudada kwavo kusina maturo, kwechivi. Kunyange chinhu chisingabviri chisingazomboitiki, nokuti NDINI.

Uye munzira dzenyu dzemwoyo wakaoma dzekusarurama makatorasikirwa nenzeve dzenyu uye makamira muri matsi, mbeveve, nemapofu munzira dzenyu dzisina kururama dzakakuderedzai muuranda hwechivi kuti mugutse ruchiva rwevakawira, avo vamunoshandira munzira dzenyu dzisina kutenda. Zvisinei, muranda haagari mumba nekusingaperi kana akasarudza nekuteerera kupinda nemusuwo mumwe chete unoenda kumatenga wandichazombovhurira vanhu: Jesu Kristu uyo ariwo, Jesu Mesia, ane zita rinonziwo Yeshua HaMashiach, uyo ari musuwo mukuru uye wakazaruka chete weruponeso uchazombovhurwa neruoko rwangu kune vakarurama vanotenda muna Kristu Jesu. Selah!

NDINI, uye ndatumira Mashoko aya erudo kune vakapiwa mhosva nenhume yangu nemuranda wangu, uye nenhume yangu nemuranda wangu ndatumira Mashoko aya kune avo vachakurumidza kutongwa.

Nzwisisai mose munonzwa Mashoko aya, kuti uku hakusi kuzivisa kwekupedzisira kwekutongwa, kunounza kupera kumunhu wechisikigo. Zvakasiyana, shoko iri rinoshanda kunyevera vasingateereri nevasakarurama veBhabhironi Rakavanzika uye kunyange vose kune avo Mashoko aya anotumirwa, (uye vamwe vachauya kuruoko rweruponeso mumasekonzi ekupedzisira aya) asiwo seuchapupu hwekupedzisira hweyambiro yakapihwa kutonga kusati kwawira zvizere paBhabhironi Rakavanzika.

Nzwisisai, kuti Mashoko angu achawana maziso, nzeve, nemwoyo yeavo kwaanotumirwa, uye hapana kuedza kubva kune vakawira, zvinonyangadza zvavo, kana matekinoroji avo asina maturo uye ekusaziva anogona kumisa izvo Mukuru NDINI anoda kuita.

Uye saka ndinosaina tsamba yangu yerudo kune vakatongwa netariro yekuti nekuteerera Mashoko angu akatumirwa murudo rwangu kwavari vapukunyuke kutongwa kwavo ruoko rwekupedzisira rusati rwarova sekondi yekupedzisira iyoyo.
