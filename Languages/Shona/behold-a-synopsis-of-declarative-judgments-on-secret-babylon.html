Mitongo Yekuzivisa
Chikumi 16, 2025

Tarirai! Pfupiso yeMitongo Yekuzivisa pamusoro peBhabhironi Rakavanzika Mutongo Usati Wawira Zvizere

Mitongo inotevera inobva muBhuku Diki raZvakazarurwa Chitsauko 10, rinovawo Evhangeri Isigaperi. Mutongo pamusoro peBhabhironi Rakavanzika wakaziviswa kwemajubhiri mazhinji nemakore. Zvinotevera Mitongo yekuzivisa yakatorwa muBhuku Diki iyo zvirokwazvo ichaitika kamwe kamwe uye zvisingatarisirwi iye zvino. Pachinzvimbo ichi, ndinoshanda semutumwa wekutonga akatumwa kuzivisa mitongo kubva kuchigaro chehushe chaYahweh, yakapiwa pamberi paJesu Kristu, wandinoshumira.

Ngazvizivikanwe, kuti nhasi uno Chikumi 16, 2025 paKarenda yaGregory (iyo karenda imwechete inoshandiswa neBhabhironi Rakavanzika) kuti Ishe wangu Akakwidzwa Kristu Jesu, iye Yeshua HaMashiach, anondiraira kuti ndiverenge mitongo iyi pamusoro peBhabhironi Rakavanzika, idzo nyika dzakabatana dzeAmerica, kuitira kuti ani nani atendeuke kuchine nguva pfupi inopfuura, uye kuti mutongo uziviswe moto usati wawira. Sera!

Chiziviso ichi hachina kukwana sezvo iyi isiri mitongo mitsva asi yakaburitswa zvizere kubva munaKurume 2, 2024, kusvika munaKurume 20, 2025. Mizhinji yeiyi inobva kuna Yeshua HaMashiach, nechikamu chimwe chete kubva kuna Yahweh, Mukuru NDIRI. Zvino ndichaenderera mberi nekuzivisa.

1Tarirai! Mutongo Unouya uye Nguva Dzasvika!Chikumi 4, 2024 (Izwi: Yeshua HaMashiach)

Sezvinoitika kuungana, mudzivisi anobviswa, uye kuparadzwa kukuru kuchawira pasi rose, kamwe kamwe uye nechinguva chidiki. Nekuti kana mudzivisi abviswa, hapana chichasara chinodzivisa huipi huri munyika neruchiva nezvido zvakaipa zvevanhu. Hondo huru ichapararira pasi rose, uye hama ichamukira kuuraya hama yayo zvakare, uye kuparadzwa kwavo kuchava kukuru. Marudzi mazhinji achawa seBhabhironi risinganzwisisike, iyo hure guru rinogara pamusoro pemvura zhinji, richaitwa dongo muawa imwe chete. Guta guru iroro, irwo rudzi rwenyika dzakabatana dzakawanda, richaparadzwa zvachose; nekuti rakawana mugove wakapetwa kaviri wemutongo, uye rakakodzera.

Nekuti rakararama mukunakirwa muchivi chikuru; rinogara mukuzvikudza kukuru richifunga kuti harimbofi rakawa uye zvinhu zvicharamba zvakadaro, zuro sezvanhasi, uye mangwana zvichingoramba zvakafanana zvisingashanduki. Uye hure guru rakaodza nyika nekapu yegoridhe muruoko rwaro, izere nezvinonyangadza netsvina yehupombwe hwaro. Saka mutongo waro uchawira kamwe kamwe. Moto mukuru uchapisa maguta aro sezvo zvombo zvinopisa zvichiwira; kuzununguka kukuru kuchaitika, uye rudzi irworwo ruchatsemuka nepakati. Mhenderekedzo dzaro dzichafashamirwa zvikuru, uye harichazomukizve, nekuti zvitadzo zvaro zvikuru uye mutongo waro mukuru.

2Inzwai Mashoko Angu uye Nzwisisai Nguva Dzamunorarama Madziri!Chivabvu 23, 2024 (Izwi: Yeshua HaMashiach)

Inzwai Mashoko angu, vanhu vangu, imi mose vandiri kudaidza, uye nzwisisai nguva dzamunorarama madziri. Nekuti nyika yamunoziva ikozvino ichaguma kuvapo, uye panzvimbo payo pachasara matongo mazhinji, iwo madota nematongo eBhabhironi risinganzwisisike, nekuti hondo yasvika. Nekuti pandinobvuta Mwenga wangu ndichienda naye kumatenga, njodzi huru ichamedza Bhabhironi risinganzwisisike, uye richaparadzwa muawa imwe chete, uye muawa imwe chete richaparadzwa, uye hapana chinogona kushandura magumo aro, nekuti Yahweh akazivisa saizvozvo. Nekuti iwe, Bhabhironi Rakavanzika, wakaramba izwi rangu ukandibvisa, Jesu Kristu Akarurama, parutivi nekuda kwemafaro echivi. Nekuti kunyange yambiro huru dzakapiwa pamusoro porudzi rwako kumatenga dzakangoshanda kupa varaidzo kumwoyo yenyu isina kutenda nemeso enyu anochiva, uye kwamuri rakanga riri rimwe zuvawo zvaro sezvo makararama mukunakirwa muzvivi zvenyu zviri kukutungamirirai kukuparadzwa kwenyu. Dai makatendeuka! Nekuti mava rudzi rwevanonamata zvidhori, rudzi rwevanokurudzira hondo, rudzi runodya nhema uye runoda kunyengedzwa kwarwo, rudzi rwakazadza nyika nezvinonyangadza zvenyu; uye marudzi anonwa kapu yenyu yegoridhe izere nezvinonyangadza netsvina yehupombwe hwenyu.

Saka kuguma ikoko kuchasvika zvizere pandichaunganidza vakatendeka veKereke yangu kwandiri mumakore uye apo kubva kwavo kuchazunungusa nyika yose. Zvichazunungusa nyika chaiyo, uye kuzununguka kukuru kuchapedza Bhabhironi Rakavanzika, uye richatsemurwa nepakati apo vatsvene vangu vachibva. Richapiswa zvachose nemoto unobva kudenga sezvo zvombo zvinopisa zvichiwira. Mipendero yaro ichafukidzwa nemvura kubva kumabvazuva nekumadokero! Nekuti mutumwa ane simba achatora ibwe rakaita seguva guru, orikanda mugungwa, achiti, Saizvozvo nechisimba guta guru Bhabhironi richawisirwa pasi, uye harichazowanikwizve. Nekuti zvakanyorwa saizvozvo uye zvichaitwa saizvozvo! Nekuti mutongo waro mukuru, uye mutongo watanga paimba yaMwari. Makereke aro akatsauka achaparadzwa, nekuti ndakaatonga ose asiri angu, kunyange makereke ose enhema anotaura nhema huru mukunyengera. Bhabhironi risinganzwisisike, oh, dai wakanga watendeukira kwandiri, Jesu Kristu, kubva panzira dzako dzakaipa, nekuti ndingadai ndakakuponesa, uye ini naYahweh hatina mufaro mumitongo yave kuda kusunungurwa.

Oh, Bhabhironi Rakavanzika, mabhiriji ako achawa; migwagwa yako ichapidiguka, yoputsika, uye yosashandisika; ngetani dzako dzekugovera zvinhu dzatotanga kuparara uye dzichakurumidza kuva dongo. Hapana zvokudya zvichaunzwa kwauri, nekuti hapana achauya kuzokubatsira, uye uchava wega muawa yako yekuparadzwa, oh, hure guru. Hauchazovi nemufaro, uye avo vachapona vachazviwana vachirwira kuti vawane mvura yekunwa. Nzara ichawira nyika. Uye denda guru richakuwira, uye kunyange mhuka dzicharwisa, nekuti nguva dzasvika.

Mauto matsvuku ayo hurumende yako inotengesa nyika yakatendera kupinda mumiganhu yako achauraya murume wese, mukadzi, nemwana asingakodzeri zvinangwa zvawo. Nekuti vachauraya nekuita nhapwa vose vavanowana. Ndiwo mauto echikara akaunganidzwa kubva kumativi mana enyika nekuda kwechinangwa ichi, uye chikara chakavanzika nemuporofita wenhema akavanzika vakavaunganidza.

Havana tsitsi, havana ngoni, havana nyasha, havana kuzvidemba, uye vanovenga zvachose vagari veBhabhironi risinganzwisisike nekuda kwezvivi zvinotyisa zvakaitwa nevatungamiriri venyu munyika dzavo. Uye panguva yose iyi vazhinji venyu maipururudzira hondo dzenyu dzisina kururama sezvo mairitarisa muchinakidzwa paterevhizheni dzenyu, sezvo maifara zvikuru muzvivi zvevatungamiriri venyu. Makapa mvumo yenyu kuvatungamiriri venyu vane zvivi zvamakatsigira nekuvhota, saka muchagoverana navo mutongo wavo, nekuti makazviita vakakodzera mugove wakapetwa kaviri wemutongo, uye uchakuwirai kamwe kamwe. Saka Mwenga wangu achaonekwa pachigaro changu chekutonga, uye vamwe vacharasikirwa, vamwe vachatakura mubairo mukuru, uye vose vachagamuchira zvizere muripo wekutanga wavakatogamuchira: Nekuti ndichavapa miviri mitsva, isina chivi isingamboori!

3KuKereke Yangu, Muviri Wangu, Kunyange Mwenga Wangu: Inzwai Mashoko aShe Wenyu!Chivabvu 13, 2024 (Izwi: Yeshua HaMashiach)

Vachapinda munhandare yenyika nesimba guru, uye mubatanidzwa wemadzimambo gumi uchatonga. Kunyange iye zvino, mubatanidzwa wacho wakatogadzirira mauto awo muchivande kuti arwise Bhabhironi Rakavanzika muchivande. Vakazvigadzirira muchivande, uye zvirokwazvo vakaronga kurwisa, uye vakapinda muBhabhironi Rakavanzika pachena maererano nemutongo wakaziviswa naMwari wave kuda kuriwira, waro risingazombomukizve. Nekuti zvakaipa zvaro zvasvika pamberi peWokumusoro-soro, uye zvivi zvaro pamberi pechigaro chikuru chehushe chekudenga, kunyange pamberi paMwari mukuru wekusingaperi, nekuti hakuna mukuru; uye mutongo waziviswa pamusoro paro.

Ndinounza hasha huru paBhabhironi Rakavanzika. Nguva chinhu cheumbozha chamusina. Hamuchakwanisi kuva neusimbe, kana kuti muchazviwana muri mudambudziko raJakobho, pandingadai ndakakugadzirirai nzvimbo patafura yangu ndikakuroorai semwenga wangu, asi hamuna kutendeka muri mumaoko emumwe, iye Satani! Saka uyai kwandiri kuti ndikudzorerei pamubairo wenyu. Hazvina kukwana kungobuda nekusemeswa; munofanira kutendeuka kubva pahuipi uhu mogadzirisa nzira dzenyu moreurura zvivi izvi kwandiri, uye ndichakunatsai muropa rangu rinoyananisa ndobvisa makwapa kubva kwamuri mukuchena kwangu kwakakwana, nekuti ndakachena uye nguo dzangu chena sechando chitsva. Dzokerai kwandiri uye muchava neni nekusingaperi; budai muye kumaoko angu akatambanudzwa, nekuti ndiri pano kuzokuudzai kuti hamuna nguva yekutambisa.

5Vasakarurama Havangagari Nhaka yeHumambo hwaMwari! (Kutinhira 5 ne6)Kubvumbi 19, 2024 (Izwi: Yeshua HaMashiach, Musoro Wakapetwa muShoko)

Regai ndivhure meso enyu, imi musingadi kuziva nemapofu nokuda kwenyu, imi munogara mutsvina nerima, muchiumburuka mumatope. Hamungapi mvumo yenyu kune zvakaipa muchifunga kuti muri kundishumira, nekuti ndakaramba zvakaipa nemuedzo kusvikira donhwe rekupedzisira reropa rangu rayerera kubva mandiri richienda pasi pevhu dzvuku, nekuti ndini Adhamu wekupedzisira uye ndakaitwa mweya unopa upenyu. Vazhinji venyu munopofumadzwa nemashoko akanaka nekutaura kwakanaka uye munopa mvumo yenyu yose kumatsotsi nevanhu vanotyisa vanobata vana zvisina kunaka. Vanoshungurudza vana, vanotambudza vana nekuda kweruchiva rwavo rwakaipa: Nekuti saizvozvo hasha dzaMwari dzinouya paBhabhironi risinganzwisisike, asi vazhinji venyu munoramba muchipa mvumo yenyu kuzvikara izvi zvakaisirwa parutivi moto wegehena usingaperi usingazombodzimwi. Budai mariri, vanhu vangu, kana kuti muchagoverana naro matambudziko aro nemutongo unotyisa uri kuuya pamusoro paro; uye ndakakuyambirai. Nekuti Kristu anowirirana papi naBheriari? Kana mukagoverana nhema dzaro, muchagoverana matambudziko aro; saka zviparadzanisei zvachose mushoko nemuchiito, uye musatenderana naro kana napadiki pose. Musararama upenyu husina hanya, hwekukanganisa; nekuti ndicharanga vose vanodaro. Munofunga nekuti mune upenyu husingaperi hamungarasikirwi zvikuru nemigumisiro here? Ndichabvisa mibairo yenyu ndoipa vamwe, uye muchava vaduku-duku muumambo hwekudenga kana mukaramba, nekuti anodaro Yeshua HaMashiach, Chikomba, kuMwenga wangu!

6Murindi, Muka!5 Kubvumbi, 2024 (Izwi: Yeshua HaMashiach)

Ndiri kuuya, uye handingarambi ndakanyarara, uye hasha dzangu dzinopisa pamusoro peBhabhironi Rakavanzika, hongu, Bhabhironi Rakavanzika rinotengesa vana vadiki kuti vave nhumbi mumaoko evanhu vakaipa, avo vachamiswa. Zvingave nani kuti dai vasina kumboberekwa pane kuberekwa voita zvivi zvisina murau uye zvinorwadza pamusoro pevana. Zvingave nani kuti guyo rirembedzwe pamitsipa yavo, uye vakandwe mumakungwa; asi vakachengeterwa moto usingaperi, uye kururamisira kuchaitwa nokukurumidza, sezvo hasha dzeGwayana dzave kuda kufukidza nyika yose uye kutambudzika kwaJakobho kwotanga kana mudzivisi abviswa. Asi iwe, haiwa Bhabhironi Rakavanzika guru, wakasvibisa uta hwaBaba vangu hwemavara mazhinji hwavakapa kuna Noa nevanhu vose sechivimbiso chokuti havazomboparadzizve nyika yose nemvura, uye vachachengeta shoko ravo. Asi iwe, haiwa Bhabhironi Risinganzwisisike, uchaparadzwa muawa imwe.

Moto mukuru uchauya pamusoro pako; kuzununguka kukuru kuchakupwanya; shongwe dzako dzichawa; zvivakwa zvako zvichanyangarika mumatope, uye mafungu makuru achafukidza mahombekombe ako, uye ndichakupa mubairo wakaenderana nemabasa ako, uye kutonga kukuru kuri pamusoro pako. Nokuti waimbova chiedza chetariro nechiedza kunyika kwenguva pfupi, asi wakasarudza makaro, udyire, nehuori sevadyidzani vako, haiwa, iwe chifeve chikuru! Ndiani achakuponesa kubva kwandiri nehasha dzangu nehasha dzangu dziri kusvika dzinopisa pamusoro pako? Wakamira uri mumaketani, wakamirira kutongwa kwako kwekupedzisira, uko kuchave pachena kuti nyika yose ione, uye kutya kukuru kuchauya pamusoro pevakawanda.

Tarira! Marudzi mazhinji Baba vangu vakaparadza mukutonga, nokuti vanosimudza mumwe uye vanowisira pasi mumwe, nokuti Yahweh ndiye mutongi mukuru uye hakuna mukuru kupfuura Mwari mukuru wekusingaperi anomiririra uye anoponesa vake kubva mumoto wakachengeterwa vakaipa. Tarira! Marudzi aiumba humambo hukuru achiripo, asi nenzira yakaderedzwa, asi akaramba aripo. Asi iwe, haiwa, chifeve chikuru, wakazviita chifeve nevadiwa vazhinji nokuda kwemakaro akashata nemafaro ezvivi. Wakatungamirira vanhu vangu kure neni pasi rose netsvina yawapa marudzi kuti adye nokunwa, uye uchawisirwa pasi zvachose, uye hauchazovipozve. Uye kubva mumadota ako rudzi rukuru haruchazomuki, asi vakasara vachaponeswa. Ruoko rwemuvengi ruri pamitsipa yako, uye ndakaunza marara mumiganhu yako. Vachapamba, vachatora zvinhu, uye vachatora zvasara.

Ndiani achakubatsira? Nokuti wakaramba muvakidzani wako, uye hakuna kutya Ishe mumwoyo menyu. Wakati, handisi chirikadzi, asi uri chirikadzi uye bofu nematsi pamberi pangu. Hakuna ruponeso kwauri nokuda kweupombwe hwako hwawakaita nenyika; naizvozvo, ndichakuunza kuparadzwa kwakakwana. Zviitiko zviri kuuya zvichaitika nokukurumidza uye nokukurumidza; ipapo mafashamo makuru achafukidza nguo dzako. Moto uchakupisa zvachose uye uchachenesa vaiti vezvakaipa kubva munyika, uye vasungwa vangu vachasunungurwa, uye hapana chinogona kumisa zvave kuda kuitika. Ndini Ishe Jesu Kristu, uye ndinokuudzai zvisati zvaitika, uye ndinozivisa zvave kuda kuitika sekunge zvatoitika, nokuti mashoko aya ndeechokwadi, uye hapana chinogona kuashandura, sezvo uporofita huchizadziswa. Mirai makasimba, vanhu vangu, sezvo ndichatora mwenga wangu; nokuti ndine zvirongwa nezvinangwa naye muumambo hwangu huri kuuya.

7Handina Kukureverera Here?1 Kubvumbi, 2024 (Izwi: Yeshua HaMashiach)

Avo vasingazvibvisi paari vachagoverana mukutongwa kwake, saka sei muchiri kusarudza rufu pane upenyu hwandakakupai, hwakabhadharwa neropa rangu risina chivi? Hondo yave kuda kuuya zvizere panyika yenyu nerudzi rwenyu, uye kunyange zvazvo yatotanga — hongu, hondo isina kuziviswa yavanorwa nemi — nokukurumidza ichapararira pasi rose sezvo zvisimbiso zvichisunungurwa. Hondo huru nekutambudzwa kukuru zvichauya pamusoro penyika sezvavanoedza kuparadza chero chinhu chaMwari naKristu wake sezvavachazozivisa pachena hondo tsvene pamusoro penyu. Vachatapa vazhinji, uye vazhinji vachatorwa, uye avo vachaponeswa vachange vaine sarudzo imwe chete: kutsungirira kusvika kumagumo uye kusada upenyu hwavo kusvika kumagumo uye kudana zita rangu kusvika kumagumo.

Vatungamiri venyu vakakupandukirai uye vachabayira vazhinji kune akaipa muhondo dzichakurumidza kuitika. Rudzi rwenyu rucharakashwa kubva mukati nekunze. Mauto matsvuku atove pano, uye akamirira mirairo yawo; nokuti vanoronga kurwisa kwechimbichimbi, uye vazhinji vachatorwa. Mauto eakaipa achatora zvose kubva munyika kuti zvive zvavo uye kubva kune avo vanondiramba. Vachaba nyika yenyu, misha yenyu, vana venyu; uye upenyu huzhinji hucharasika mukusatenda. Hondo iri kuuya kubva kunze sezvo marudzi mazhinji akabatana achirwa nemi; uye vane mvumo yevatungamiri venyu, uye vatungamiri venyu vanoshandira kufa kwenyu. Vanoda nyika yavo, nyika yakatsaurirwa kuna Satani kwavanogona kutevera zvizere zvakaipa zvinogara mavari, uye hapana chichadzivisa zvishuwo zvavo zvakaipa, sezvo mudzivisi achange aenda.

Vachapisa Bhabhironi Rakavanzika zvachose nemoto, uye ani naani asingakundwi nemirazvo achakundwa nenzara; ani naani asingakundwi nenzara achakundwa nedenda; ani naani asingakundwi nechinopinza chemunondo achaendeswa kunzvimbo dzekuurayirwa nekuda kwezita rangu, uye vazhinji vachagurwa misoro sezvinotaura Shoko rangu Dzvene. Uye avo vanofira zita rangu vachava nemubairo mukuru, uye ndichapukuta misodzi yose pamaziso avo. Zivai, vana vangu, kuti zvose zvinonaka nemafaro ezvivi izvo vanhu vakaipa vanozvichengetera zvichanyangarika mudenga, uye kutambudzika nekusuwa zvichamira panzvimbo pazvo. Rima richafukidza nyika, uye kuchava nekushaikwa kweShoko rangu, uye izwi remwenga neizwi rechikomba hazvichazonzwiki maari zvakare.

8Rambai Chiratidzo cheChikara!27 Chikunguru, 2024 (Izwi: Yeshua HaMashiach)

Inzwai izwi reuchenjeri uye pfugamai mabvi uye inzwai izwi mhenyu raYeshua HaMashiach, nokuti ndini Shumba yeRudzi rwaJudha, uye ndini Mambo wemadzimambo naIshe wemadzishe! El Shaddai, kunyange Mwari Wemasimbaose, akandikwidziridza zvikuru uye akandipa zita riri pamusoro pemazita ose. Kuti paZita raJesu mabvi ose apfugame, ezvinhu zviri kudenga, nezvinhu zviri panyika, nezvinhu zviri pasi penyika: Uye kuti rurimi rwose rupupure, kuti Jesu Kristu ndiye Ishe, kuti Baba veZviedza vakudzwe, kunyange Yahweh, Mwari wekusingaperi uye wekare-kare, nokuti hakuna akafanana naBaba vedu!

Inzwai izwi raIshe wenyu uye nzwisisai zvinhu zvatotanga kuitika nezvinhu zvave kuda kuitika, nokuti tarirai ndini iye anotaura nezvezvinhu zvichauya sekunge zvatoitika nokuti zvirokwazvo Ishe wenyu mukuru, kunyange ini Kristu Jesu mukuru pane vose handiiti chinhu, kunze kwekunge ndazivisa zvakavanzika zvangu kuvaranda vangu Vaprofita. Sei muchiteerera manzwi mazhinji anopesana, uye sei muchimira pakati pemaonero maviri? Handina kutaura here kuti vaprofita vangu vanotaura pamwe chete uye havazombopikisi Mashoko angu matsvene? Nokuti ndini Shoko rakaitwa nyama rinogara pakati penyu nomweya wangu mutsvene unoberekwa mukati menyu nokusingaperi-peri, nokuti mweya wose unoberekwa patsva wakasimbiswa nomweya iwoyo mutsvene wechivimbiso unova mbeu yangu isingaori uye chisimbiso chaMwari Mupenyu hachigoni kuputswa. Uye zuva rekununurwa kwenhaka yakatengwa rava pedyo uye kununurwa kwenyu kuzere kwatosvika.
