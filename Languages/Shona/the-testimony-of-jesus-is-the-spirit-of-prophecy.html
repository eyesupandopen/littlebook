Tsanangudzo ye“Mumabasa a”

Uchapupu hwaJesu Ndiwo Mweya weChiporofita

Tarirai, ndinokuratidzai chakavanzika chebhuku diki. Nokuti bhuku rakapihwa, uye mweya wechiporofita ndihwo uchapupu hwaJesu. Uchapupu hwangu hwaJesu hwakatanga muupenyu hwangu apo Ishe Jesu Kristu vakandidanira muushumiri nebasa muna 1996 pandakanga ndichigara muNew York City. Ndakanga ndadaira kudana kwaIshe kwekutendeuka nekupinda boka rechidzidzo cheBhaibheri chemumba uye ndaiva mudzidzi mune imwe yemakirasi avo. Mamwe manheru ndakanga ndakagara muresitorendi muLong Island City neshamwari yangu yepamwoyo, uye akandikumbira kuti ndivhure Bhaibheri rangu pana Johani 4, akatanga kuverenga zvinotevera ini ndichiverengawo naye:

Johani 4:35-38

Hamuti here, Kwasara mwedzi mina, kukohwa kuchasvika? Tarirai, ndinoti kwamuri, Simudzai meso enyu, mutarire minda; nokuti yachena kare kuti ikohwewe.

Uyo anokohwa anogamuchira mubairo, uye anounganidza zvibereko kuupenyu husingaperi: kuti vose uyo anodyara nouyo anokohwa vafare pamwe chete.

Uye apa ndipo pane chokwadi chekutaura uku, Mumwe anodyara, uye mumwe anokohwa.

Ndakakutumai kunokohwa pamusina kushanda nesimba: vamwe varume vakashanda, uye imi mapinda mumabasa avo.

Shamwari yangu payaiverenga ndima idzi, mashoko aiita seanosvetuka kubva papeji achipinda zvakananga mukatikati memweya wangu. Moto wakabatidzwa mumwoyo mangu, uye saizvozvo Ishe vakandipa basa ravo muna 1996. Ndakasiya chaizvo upenyu hwangu hwose nezvose zvandaiita kuti ndipinde muushumiri. Ndakava mutungamiri mune imwe kereke huru diki, uye gare gare, pandakaziva kuti vakanga vakaora sei chaizvo, ndakabuda ndisingazombodzoki muna 2008. Ndakatambura kwemakore mashoma pandakaedza kufamba naKristu, asi zvivi zvangu zvaindichengeta kumashure. Muna 2013 upenyu hwangu hwakaparara uye ndakazviparadza, uye yaiva tsaona yechitima yaienda zvishoma nezvishoma yakanga yave nenguva ichiitika. Uye saizvozvo Ishe vakandipinza mukuranga kwakakomba uye vakandirova kakawanda kudzamara ndadzokera kupfungwa dzakanaka musi waChikunguru 7, 2020, pandakatendeuka zvizere pazvivi zvangu.

Ishe vakandigadzirira rwendo urwu nekundipa zviratidzo zvizhinji, zviratidzo, minana, kuporeswa kwakati wandei, uye ndakadzinga mweya mizhinji nekutungamirirwa naIshe, uye Ishe vakandipa simbiso huru yehuchokwadi hweushumiri hwangu nekudanwa kwangu. Uye saizvozvo ndakadzorerwa kuna Ishe wangu, ndakadzorerwa kuna Yahweh, ndakadzorerwa kumudzimai wangu nemwanakomana wangu nemubairo wangu usingaperi.

Imwe nguva kunopera kwa2023, ndakaitirwa chimwe chinhu chinoshamisa. Nguva zhinji ndinorara rugwaro ruchirira paTV yangu neparuzha. Manheru iwayo, ndakarara Testamente Yekare ichirira zvinyoro nyoro. Ndakamutswa nekuvhunduka pakati peusiku apo ruzha rwangu, urwo rwakanga rwakanyarara kwazvo, rwakava neruzha rukuru pasina chikonzero. Pakanga pasina munhu akanga akwidza ruzha, uye remote yaiva yakangogara isina kubatwa patafura yangu yepadivi pemubhedha. Ndima dzinotevera dzaEzekieri dzakarira ini ndakagara ipapo ndisingatendi:

Ezekieri 3:17-22

Mwanakomana womunhu, ndakakuita murindi weimba yaIsraeri: naizvozvo inzwa shoko pamuromo wangu, uvayambire kubva kwandiri.

Kana ndikati kune wakaipa, Uchafa zvirokwazvo; uye iwe ukasamuyambira, kana kutaura kuti uyambire wakaipa kubva panzira yake yakaipa, kuti uponese upenyu hwake; murume iyeye wakaipa achafira mukuipa kwake; asi ropa rake ndicharida paruoko rwako.

Asi kana iwe ukayambira wakaipa, uye iye akasatendeuka kubva pakuipa kwake, kana kubva panzira yake yakaipa, iye achafira mukuipa kwake; asi iwe wakanunura mweya wako.

Zvakare, Kana munhu akarurama akatsauka kubva pakururama kwake, akaita zvakaipa, uye ini ndikaisa chigumbuso pamberi pake, iye achafa: nokuti hauna kumuyambira, iye achafira muchivi chake, uye kururama kwake kwaakaita hakuzorangarirwi; asi ropa rake ndicharida paruoko rwako.

Zvisinei kana iwe ukayambira munhu akarurama, kuti akarurama asatadza, uye iye akasatadza, iye achararama zvirokwazvo, nokuti ayambirwa; uyewo iwe wakanunura mweya wako.

Uye ruoko rwaJEHOVHA rwakanga ruri pamusoro pangu ipapo

Uye ndizvo zvazvakaita. Kunyange panguva iyoyo ndakanga ndisinganzwisisi kuti Ishe vaindituma semuporofita wenguva dzekupedzisira, ndaizoziva munguva pfupi.

Musi waKurume 2, 2024, ndakatanga kunyora mharidzo dzechiporofita mune izvo Ishe vakatsanangura kwandiri semabasa aJohani Mubhabhatidzi. Izvi zvakaenderera mberi kubva musi waKurume 2 kusvika Kubvumbi 5, 2024, uye panguva iyoyo ndakagamuchira mharidzo dzechiporofita makumi maviri. Mharidzo dzose dzainge dzakatarisana nekutongwa kweBhabhironi Rakavanzika, kudana vatsvene kuti vatendeuke, uye kuzivisa kuonekwa kuri pedyo kwaJesu Kristu pachake, sezvo achaonekwa mumhepo chero nguva zvino kune vakatendeka muKereke yake. Chaizvoizvo ndakafunga kuti chiporofita chemusi waKubvumbi 5 ndicho chaiva chekupedzisira. Ndakanga ndakanganisa. Mharidzo yechiporofita yakatevera yakaitika musi waKubvumbi 10, 2024, uye Ishe vakandiudza kuti vaindituma mubasa raPetro kuti ndidyise nekuchengeta boka.

Chimiro nezviri mukati memharidzo zvakachinja kubva panguva iyoyo, kunyange zvazvo zvichiri nezvinhu zvinobva pamakumi maviri ekutanga, uye mharidzo zhinji idzi dzinodyidzana imwe neimwe. Musi waKubvumbi 21, 2024, chimiro nezviri mukati memharidzo zvakachinja zvakare, uye Ishe vakandiudza kuti vaindituma mubasa reMuapostora Pauro semutariri wezvakavanzika zvaMwari. Uye saizvozvo zviri mukati memharidzo zvakachinja zvakare uye zvakavakirwa pamharidzo dzekare uye zvinodyidzana. Mharidzo idzi dzakaenderera mberi nenzira imwecheteyo kusvika Chivabvu 16, 2024, apo zviri mukati zvakachinja zvakare. Ndakaudzwa kuti ndaitumwa mumabasa aJohani Muratidzwi (izwi randisina kumbonzwa kana kushandisa), uye musi waChivabvu 16 zviporofita izvozvo zvakatanga.

Uye saizvozvo basa remaoko aIshe rinoratidzwa mukudanwa kwaakandipa, uye akandituma kwandisina kushanda nesimba; vamwe varume vakashanda, uye ini ndapinda mumabasa avo. Hazvirevi kuti varume ava vane simba vaMwari havana kupedza nzira dzavo, asi kuti mabasa aya akaenderera mberi kubvira panguva iyoyo yose apo Jesu Kristu akavapa basa mumabasa aakavatuma. Zvikamu izvi zvakanga zvisingazivikanwi kwandiri pandakatanga kuporofita, uye sezvaakandiudza, ndaipinda mumabasa emumwe nemumwe. Handina kumboona kubatana uku kudzamara ndaziviswa naYeshua.

Kudanwa neushumiri paupenyu hwangu zvazadzikiswa. Vamwe varume vakashanda, uye akandituma mumabasa avo, uye imomo ndakashanda. Ndakatumwa mubasa raJohani Mubhabhatidzi kuzivisa kuuya kwaIshe. Ndakatumwa mubasa raPetro kufudza nekudyisa boka. Ndakatumwa mubasa reMuapostora Pauro semutariri weZvakavanzika zvaMwari. Ndakatumwa mubasa raJohani Muratidzwi kudana zviuru zana nemakumi mana nezvina uye kuratidza zviitiko zvichauya.

Pachezvangu, handina kukodzera kuzvienzanisa nechero wevarume ava vakuru vaMwari uye handina kukodzera pachangu kunyange kutaura kwaziso iri nyore kune chero wenyu. Asi Ishe vakandiita kuti ndikodzerwe, uye ndakatumwa mumabasa aya saizvozvo. Upenyu hwangu hwose hwakatungamirira kumharidzo idzi, uye shoko rega rega richawana meso, nzeve, nemwoyo yeavo kwavari kutumirwa. Kana Ishe vachida kuti nditaure zvimwe, ndichadaro. Uye saizvozvo ndichapedza nzira yangu nemabasa andakapihwa kuti ndipedzise. Uye netsanangudzo iyi, basa remaoko nechiratidzo chaIshe Jesu Kristu zvinoratidzwa mumharidzo idzi dzechiporofita dzandakapihwa. Ndapedza mabasa aakandipa kuti ndiite. Uye saizvozvo ndinomirira Ishe.
