Mashoko ekutanga

Sera!

Chirongwa cheWar-seed / Kugadzirwa kweChiratidzo cheChikara

  1. Iwe Bhabhironi Yakavanzika, nehudoyi hwako nemashiripiti ako wakanyengera nyika kuti igamuchire chepfu, iyo nheyo uye chombo chehupenyu chakasarudzwa chavakaisa chepfu nacho kune avo vanoda nyika nemishonga yavo uye vanoramba kupfidza nokuti ndingadai ndakavaporesa!
  2. Nzwisisai kuti Chirongwa cheWar-seed nekukurumwa nenyoka yacho kwakanga kusiri chiratidzo chechikara, asi chiratidzo chechikara chichavanzwa semushonga uye chiri mhiko yakazara yekuvimbika yekugamuchira matekinoroji avo ayo achadzima zvachose zita raYahweh kubva kune anobvuma.
  3. Iko kurumwa nenyoka uko hurongwa hwavo hwatovepo uye hwaedzwa, uye ruzhinji rwevanhu rwatove rwajairira kugamuchira chiratidzo chavo uye Chirongwa chavo cheWar-seed chakabudirira.
  4. Kubva kwauri Bhabhironi Yakavanzika, kwakabva matekinoroji egehena aya uye nenhema dzako nemanomano ako ruzhinji rwevanhu rwakaisirwa chepfu uye kutongwa kwako hakunonoki.

Kubvumirana nezvakaipa

  1. Vazhinji venyu munozvikudza zvikuru nokuti munoedza kundiita Ishe muchine moyo usina kutenda, nokuti nomoyo munhu anotenda kusvika pakururama. Saka ivai nekutenda kuti muponeswe; kutenda kwenyu kucharatidzwa kune vose nokuramba kwenyu chivi nekubuda munzira dzenyu dzine tsvina.
  2. Vakaipa vanofamba kumativi ose, kana vanhu vakaipisisa vachikudzwa. Asi imi muchiri kubvumirana nenzira dzevasodhomi, uye munobatsira kuunza huipi hukuru panyika, kusvika pakuti munovhotera vanobata vana chibharo kuti vatungamirire nyika dzenyu. Munoshaya kunyange ruzivo rwekutanga-tanga rwekunzwisisa, uye hamuteveri nzira yangu; uye kana mukaramba, ndichakuregererai zvizere mukuchiva kwenyu kwakashata kuitira kuparadzwa kwenyama kuti mweya uponeswe.
  3. Regai ndivhure maziso enyu, imi musingadi kuziva uye muri mapofu, imi munogara mutsvina nerima, muchiumburuka mumatope. Hamugoni kubvumirana nezvakaipa muchifunga kuti muri kundishumira, nokuti ini ndakaramba zvakaipa nemuedzo kusvika donhwe rekupedzisira reropa rakayerera kubva mandiri richienda pasi panyika tsvuku, nokuti ndiri Adhamu wekupedzisira uye ndakaitwa mweya unopa upenyu.
  4. Vazhinji venyu munopofumadzwa nemashoko akanaka nekutaura kwakanaka uye munopa mvumo yenyu yakazara kune matsotsi nevanhu vanotyisa vanobata vana. Vanobata vana chibharo, vanotambudza vana nokuda kwekuchiva kwavo kwakaipa: Nokuti saizvozvo kutsamwa kwaMwari kunouya paBhabhironi Yakavanzika, asi vazhinji venyu munoramba muchipa mvumo yenyu kumhuka dzesango idzi dzakachengeterwa moto wegehena usingaperi usingazodzimwi.
  5. Budai maari, vanhu vangu, kana kuti muchagoverana naye matambudziko ake nekutonga kunotyisa kuri kuuya paari; uye ndakakuyambirai. Nokuti Kristu anowirirana papi naBheriari? Kana mukagoverana naye nhema dzake, muchagoverana naye matambudziko ake; saka zviparadzanisei zvizere mushoko nemuchiito, uye musabvumirana navo kunyange zvishoma. Musararama upenyu husina hanya, hwekubvumirana nezvakaipa; nokuti ndicharanga vose vanodaro. Munofunga nokuti mune upenyu husingaperi hamugoni kurasikirwa zvikuru nemigumisiro yakaipa here? Ndichabvisa mibairo yenyu ndoipa vamwe, uye muchava vaduku muumambo hwekudenga kana mukaramba, nokuti saizvozvo anodaro Yeshua HaMashiach, Chikomba, kuMwenga wangu!
  6. Ngazvizivikanwe! Vavengi veBhabhironi Yakavanzika havana tsitsi, havana ngoni, havana nyasha, havana kuzvidemba, uye vanovenga zvikuru vagari veBhabhironi Yakavanzika nokuda kwezvivi zvinotyisa zvaakaita panyika dzavo. Uye panguva yose iyoyo vazhinji venyu maipururudzira hondo dzenyu dzisina kururama muchiona nekunakidzwa pamaterevhizheni enyu, sezvo maifara zvikuru nezvivi zvevatungamiriri venyu. Makapa mvumo yenyu kuvatungamiriri venyu vane zvivi zvamakatsigira nevhoti, uye saka muchagoverana navo mukutongwa kwavo, nokuti makazviita vakakodzera mugove wakapetwa kaviri wekutongwa, uye kuchakubatai kamwe kamwe.
  7. Tarirai! Vatungamiriri vose venyika vanoshanda pamwe chete vari kumashure, uye vose vanoshumira Satani. Hamuna vatungamiriri vakasarudzwa, asi munopiwa sarudzo yenhema nokuti hamuna kumbosarudza avo vamunofunga kuti munosarudza nevhoti, asi vanounzwa kwamuri kuti muvavhotere kuitira kuti vawane mvumo yenyu uye mvumo yenyu chivi nokuti hamugoni kusarudza nemaune chakaipa chiduku uye mondimiririra panguva imwe chete, ikoko kuzvinyengera.
  8. Bhabhironi Yakavanzika, iwe unopa mvumo yako nemaune kuvatongi vako vechiSatani, vanoziva zvizere zvirongwa zvavo uye vanoziva zvizere kuti vari mukati mekuparadza zvachose rudzi rwako rwaimbova rukuru newe.
  9. Nokuti iwe Bhabhironi Yakavanzika muufeve hwako nehupombwe hwako, unorara nemuvengi.
  10. Nokuti nenzira yakadaro, wakararama zvakapfuma apo vatungamiriri vako vaiparadza dzimwe nyika nemvumo yako.
  11. Nokuti nenzira yakadaro, Bhabhironi Yakavanzika, wakapururudza apo vatungamiriri vako vakauyisa njodzi kumahombekombe enyika dzisingaverengeki muhondo dzavo dzisina kururama dzawakavaraidzwa nadzo uchiona paterevhizheni.
  12. Nokuti iSinagogi raSatani rakaita sarudzo yaro uye rakasarudza Moshiach waro mutsva wenhema uye Bhabhironi Yakavanzika yakapa zvakare mvumo yayo kushato.
  13. Pamberi chaipo pemaziso enyu uye nemvumo yevhoti yenyu uye nenzira yekusava nehanya kwenyu kweusimbe, rudzi rwenyu rwose rwakaputswa pamberi pemaziso enyu.
