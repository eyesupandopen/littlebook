Ndini Ishe Wechigarire Wekururama!

Tarirai! Inzwai Mashoko aJesu Kristu mugokudza pfungwa dzenyu uye muchecheudze mwoyo nenzeve dzenyu mukuteerera Mashoko aYeshua HaMashiach, uye ndichapa uchenjeri kune vakachenjera kuitira kuti munhu akachenjera pakati penyu adzidze kutya Ishe uye kuti mudzidze kudedera pamberi paIshe weHondo, nekuti ndizvo zvakandiita Yahweh, Iye Mukuru WANDIRI.

Ndiwe ani, iwe munhu wenyama, kuti usimudzire chitsitsinho chako kuna Yahweh, Iye Mukuru WANDIRI, iye ari Mutana weMazuva, iye ari Ishe weMweya? Ndiwe ani iwe munhu wenyama kuti uzvikwidziridze mukuzvirumbidza kunozvitutumadza uchipikisa Jesu weNazareta uyo ari Kristu Jesu, iye ane zita riri pamusoro pemazita ose, iye uyo Yahweh, Iye Mukuru WANDIRI, akagovana naye chigaro chake cheushe, iye akakwidziridzwa pamusoro pezvose kunze kwaYahweh pachake kudzamara vavengi vangu vaitwa chitsiko chetsoka dzangu? Uye ushe hwangu nekutonga kwangu hazvina magumo uye ndichapa Umambo hwakazviisa pasi kuna Baba veZvivheneko kuitira kuti Adonai Yahweh ave zvose mune zvose!

Chinhu chakaumbwa chingati here kune uyo akachiumba, mandiumbirei saizvozvi? Bundu revhu rakaforomwa rikava midziyo yekukudzwa ringati here kumuumbi, mandiumbirei saizvozvi? Mandipirei rukudzo rwakadai? Ndinoda kuva chimwe chinhu uye pachinzvimbo ndichava nerukudzo rwavo! Pane muumbi here angasaparadza chisikwa chakaipa chakadai chingapandukira ruoko rwake, kunyange kutsvaga kufa kwemuumbi, pamwechete nezvimwe zvisikwa zvemuumbi; sekunge chinhu chakadai chaigoneka?

Asi ndizvo zvakaita nzira dzinosekesa nedzinopenga dzevawa vakapandukira musiki wavo nekusiya mirairo yavo nemabasa matsvene uye anokudzwa avakapihwa kuti vazadzise mukururama, kunyange zvinangwa zvakapihwa naYahweh pachake pazuva raakasika vanakomana vaMwari. Kunyange ngirozi tsvene dzakaitwa naYahweh asati aita nyika, idzo dzakanyorwa nezvadzo muna Jobho paakaita nyika: Nyeredzi dzemangwanani dzakaimba pamwechete, uye vanakomana vose vaMwari vakadanidzira nemufaro! Uye zvirokwazvo vakadanidzira nemufaro pamusoro pezvinoshamisa nezvishamiso zvavakaona pamaoko aYahweh paakasika nyika. Uye ndizvo zvakanga zvakaita ngirozi dzakawa, kunyange vanakomana vaMwari pakutanga vakasikwa vakaitwa vakakudzwa uye vane simba nokuda kwezvinangwa zvavo uye Yahweh akavafukidza nerukudzo uye akavaita kuti vakunde muhuchenjeri kuti vade Yahweh mukuteerera kwakakwana inzwi rake uye zvirokwazvo vakapihwa nzvimbo yekutanga yakakudzwa yavakanga vasikirwa pakutanga, kudzamara vapanduka mukuchiva kwemwoyo yavo uye kusarurama kwakawanikwa mavari.

Uye saizvozvo maSatani akapanduka pakutanga achipikisa musiki wavo, kunyange avo vaimbopoteredza chigaro cheushe chaYahweh, kunyange uyo ane chigaro cheushe chakapoteredzwa nezviuru gumi zvakapetwa nezviuru gumi uye Yahweh achiri mutongi uye haadi chipangamazano. Asi zvakanyorwa pamusoro peshato iyi huru tsvuku, ine misoro minomwe, nenyanga gumi, nekorona nomwe pamisoro yayo, kuti muswe wayo wakakweva chikamu chechitatu chenyeredzi dzekudenga, ukadzikandira panyika: uye saizvozvo maSatani uye kunyange chikamu chechitatu chengirozi dzakatevera kupanduka kwavo dzakakandirwa pasi panyika, kunyange mumazuva ehuori hwengirozi hwakawa kubva kudenga muchivi nekupanduka, uye vakava rima rakakwana mushure mezvivi zvavo, uye ndizvo zvavari kusvika zuva ranhasi.

Uye saizvozvo nyoka yakasvika kuna amai vevapenyu vose muMunda weEdeni makore mazhinji apfuura, uye yakanyengera Evha kuti adye chinhu chaicho icho Yahweh akavaraira kuti vasadya, nekuti pazuva ravakadya kubva pamuti wekuziva zvakanaka nezvakaipa, kunyange pazuva rimwe chetero vaizofa zvirokwazvo. Kunyange zvakadaro, Evha akatora kubva kunyoka sezvo akanyengerwa nekunyengera kwayo uye akadya, uye akapa kumurume wake muchero uyu wakarambidzwa uye Adhamu akadya achida uye mukufa vakafa sezvakavimbiswa, uye vanhu vakaiswa pasi porufu kubvira ipapo nekuda kwechivi chakawanikwa muropa rake. Nekuti Adhamu akaora zvachose muchivi uye nekuda kwechinangwa ichi Mwanakomana woMunhu akaratidzwa kuti ndiparadze mabasa adhiabhorosi uye ndounza rudzikinuro kuna Adhamu akawa nekuti ndini Adhamu wekupedzisira uyo Yahweh akaita mweya unopa upenyu. Zvirokwazvo, zvirokwazvo, ndichasikazve vose vanogamuchira chipo changu cheruponeso vave chisikwa chitsva uye chakarurama nekuti ndizvo zvinounzwa neTestamente Yangu Itsva.

Saka Adhamu naEvha vakadzingwa muMunda weEdeni, uye vakashandurwa kubva pachisikigo chakajeka chinoshamisa chavakanga vasikwa nacho mukubwinya kuzere naYahweh pachake, uye pasi pehutongi hwechivi nezvakaipa zvachinounza vakadzungaira panyika mukurangwa kwavo. Uye mumazuva aJaredi Varindi vakaburukira paGomo reHemoni, kunyange avo vakanga vakwezvwa nekuchiva kwemwoyo yavo nekuti vakachiva vanasikana vevanhu, kunyange vachida vana kubva kwavari uye mukuchiva kwemwoyo yavo kwavakanga vaedzwa namaSatani vakazvisunga kumhiko dzekutukana, kunyange mumhiko yavo isina kuchena iyo yakazova chituko chisingaperi chichazadzikiswa mugungwa guru iroro remoto unopfuta usingadzimwi, umo honye yavo isingafi.

Uye saizvozvo vanakomana vaMwari vakapinda kuvanasikana vevanhu, uye vakazvitorera vakadzi vose vavakasarudza. Vamwe vakadzi vakaenda vachida, kunyange vachizvipa zvizere kuzvivi zvezvakawa (avo vachatongwa), uye vamwe vakatorwa nechisimba uye vakashungurudzwa nehutsinye neVarindi mukuchiva kwavo kwakaipa. Uye ndizvo zvakanga zvakaita nyika yaivapo panguva iyoyo, kunyange nyika yakaparadzwa nemafashamo makuru akaitika mumazuva aNoa iyo yakaparadzwa nekuda kwezvinonyangadza zvakaipa zvakaitwa nevarindi, avo vakanga vaitirwa kuparadzwa kwevanhu kunyange kuitira kuti vanhu vasambova nenyika iyo vakawa vakachiva uye zvino vakasungwa mukutongwa.

Nekuti vakasarudza kuparadzwa kwavo nekutambudzwa kusingaperi vachitsinhanisa nezvinhu zvekuchiva kwavo izvo zvisiri zvavo kuti vatore, uye izvo zvinhu zvavakaita, uye vachiri kutsvaga kutora nechisimba. Zvisinei, zviito zvavo nezvirongwa zvavo zvose zvichashaya basa, uye havazombowanizve iyo inonzi nguva yavo yegoridhe, asi pachinzvimbo vachawana kutongwa kwakaoma uye kwakakomba uye kusingaperi. Nekuti chokwadi chisingashanduki ndechekuti: Nyika ndeyaIshe nekuzara kwayo, uye ndini Ishe Wechigarire Wekururama akatenga nyika neropa rangu rinodzikinura, uye ndinouya kuzotora nechisimba izvo zviri zvangu zviri pamutemo, uye ndiani achakwanisa kumira achipikisa simba rangu guru?

Sezvakanyorwa pamusoro pangu ndizvo zvichaitwa: Tarirai, Shumba yedzinza raJudha, mudzi waDhavhidhi, yakakunda kuti izarure bhuku, uye isunungure zvisimbiso zvaro zvinomwe. Naizvozvo, nzwisisai zvizere kuti ndini iye akakodzera kusunungura zvisimbiso nekuti gwaro renyika ndorangu! Uye sezvakanyorwa pamusoro pangu ndizvo zvichaitwa nekuti ndakakodzera kutora bhuku, nekuzarura zvisimbiso zvaro: nekuti ndakaurayiwa, uye ndakadzikinura vose vachauya kuruponeso rusingaperi kuna Yahweh neropa rangu risina chivi, uye ndakakudzikinurai kubva kumarudzi ose, nendimi, nevanhu, nenyika: uye hakuna chinhu chimwe chete chingakubvutai kubva mumaoko angu echigarire! Naizvozvo, ndiani wamunofanira kutya?

Saka vakadzi vakatorwa nevanakomana vaMwari, (kunyange varindi vakawa) vakava nemimba uye vakavaberekera hofori dzinonyangadza dzaiva dzakaipa kusvika pakadzika pemwoyo yavo. Uye muhukuru hwavo nesimba vakavimba uye vakazvirumbidza, asi mukutonga kwaYahweh pamusoro pavo nekuda kwezvakaipa zvavakatadzira vanhu akasunga mweya yavo panyika mukurangwa nekuti vakaitwa panyika vakafanana nenyama ine chivi uye mweya yavo yaiva yechigarire yakapiwa kubva kuna Madzibaba avo vane kuchiva kwavakanga vakasungirwa kuzadzisa. Uye saizvozvo sezvo maNephilim aya akafa mukutongwa kwawo kubva kuna Yahweh pachake, mweya yavo yakabviswa miviri mukufa kwavo, uye vakaramba varipo, vakasungirwa panyika mukurangwa. Naizvozvo, vane nzara nenyota asi havagoni kuwana chikafu kana mvura. Nepo vaimbova nemiviri mikuru uye ine simba yavaishandisa kutonga vagari vepanyika, vakaitwa vasina simba kunze kwekunge vakwanisa kutora muviri wavanogara mauri semaparasite emadhimoni, nekuti ndizvo zvavakava. Nzwisisai, kuti kubvira mumazuva aNoa nekuda kwekupindira kwaNoa, zvikamu zvipfumbamwe kubva pagumi zvavo zvakachengetwa mugomba rakadzika asi zvava pedyo nekusunungurwa panyika mukutongwa.

Asi, ndizvo zvakanga zvakaita kupanduka kwevakawa kubvira pazuva chairo iro kusarurama kwakawanikwa mumwoyo yavo. Kupenga ikoko nekupenga kwakazadza mwoyo yevakawa mukupanduka kwavo; pamwechete nemunhu wenyama, uyo akabatana navo nekuramba kubva pamamiriro ake akawa uye ane chivi: kuti vose vanotsvaga kuva chimwe chinhu kunze kweicho Yahweh anoda mukukwana kwose kwakarurama. Panzvimbo pekuteerera musiki wavo uyo aizova nechokwadi chekuzadzikiswa kwavo kwakakwana, mufaro, rufaro, nerugare; uyo aizovapa zvakakwana nguva dzose murudo rwake rwechisikwa chake, pachinzvimbo vakatsvaga uye vanotevera kuchiva kwemwoyo yavo uye vanoda nyika yavo vasina Yahweh, kwavanogona kurarama kuchiva kwemwoyo yavo uye vachinakidzwa nekupanduka kwavo mumafaro echivi vachikanganisa chisikwa chaYahweh. Kunyange zvishuwo zvine chivi nezvakaipa zvavasingazombowani kana kuzadzisa.

Saizvozvowo, munhu wenyama akazvibatanidza nekupanduka kwavo kunze kwekuti munhu wenyama anoberekwa mukusaziva nerima rezvinangwa zvake zvechokwadi nekubwinya kunogona kuitika uko Yahweh akavimbisa vanoteerera uye munhu wenyama akaenda achida shure kwenzira dzevakawa, kunyange zvazvo, vakawa vakazvipira kuparadzwa kwaAdhamu, uko Adhamu anokusaziva achida mukuchiva kwake, kunyange maakazvisunga mukutarisira kweupenzi kunhema dzavo dzakawanda uye dzakaipa. Kunyange nenzira yenhema idzodzo, vazhinji vachatungamirirwa kugungwa remoto apo kurangwa ikoko kwakanga kusina kuitirwa ivo.

Asi, kupanduka uku kwakaenderera mberi nemakore zvino, kunyange kupanduka kunoitwa muhondo yembeu pakati pezvisikwa zvakarurama zvaYahweh nezvinonyangadza zvisina kururama zvakaitwa nekuchinja kwemajini evakaipa, uye havasi vamwari kunyange vachinyepedzera. Uye ndidzo nguva dzaunorarama, iwe munhu, anopanduka mukusateerera kune ruvengo kubva munzira dzemusiki wake, kunyange mukusaziva achibatana neavo vakazvipira zvizere kurufu rwako nekuparadzwa kusingaperi kwavanoshuvira.

Uye saizvozvo ndinozivisa zuva, ini Yeshua HaMashiach, ndinozivisa kuti zuva raIshe rasvika uye rava kuda kutsemuka panyika mukutonga kukuru kwechisimba uye njodzi zhinji dzava kuda kusunungurwa panyika, kunyange njodzi nekutonga zvakafanotaurwa kare kare zvino nguva dzazvo dzasvika. Naizvozvo, nzwisisai kuti ndini ani anoita chiziviso ichi musimba nechigaro cheushe chaYahweh pachake, nekuti ndini iye akakodzera kutora bhuku nekuzarura, nekusunungura zvisimbiso zvirimo, uye ndichaita chaizvo izvozvo, uye ndiani angamisa ruoko rwangu rune simba?!

Nzwisisa, iwe munhu wenyama, kunyange iwe ungaramba musiki wako neruvengo, kuti nguva dzenyu dzasvika. Uye rudo rukuru nenyasha netsitsi zvakapiwa munguva idzi dzekutonga, nekuti vose vanodana pazita raJesu weNazareta kusvika kumagumo avo vachaponeswa uye huremu hwemubairo wavo hahuna kukodzera kuenzaniswa nekutambura kwavachatsungirira nekuda kwezita rangu. Nzwisisa, iwe munhu wenyama, kuti ndichatungamirira vane simba vangu zviuru zana nemakumi mana nezvina kuti ndinunure Jakobho uye ndichavanzira vakasara vane simba veIsraeri avo vacharamba vari vapenyu muUmambo hwangu hwemakore churu munzvimbo yakagadzirirwa naYahweh murenje, kunyange kuitira kuti vabereke uye vawande uye vanakidzwe nezvakakora zvenyika muumambo hwangu hwemakore churu hwava pedyo nekuuya panyika mukuzadzikiswa kwechivimbiso, sungano, netestamente.

Saka zvichava kumunhu wenyama, kuti vachagamuchira gwayana raMwari semuponesi wavo kana kuti vachange vasarudza kutongwa kwavo kusingaperi mugungwa remoto risingadzimwi nekuti hapana imwe sarudzo, uye hapana kumwe kwavanogona kuenda nekuti Yahweh haazombobvumiri zvakaipa kuti zvikunde nekuparadza chisikwa chake uye mazuva avo ekupanduka vachida ava kusvika kumagumo.

Ndini gwayana raMwari, rakapirwa zvivi zvenyika.

Ndini Shumba yeDzinza raJudha, uye mukururama ndinouya kuzotonga nekuita hondo.

Ndini iye umo gwayana raMwari neShumba yeDzinza raJudha zvakabatanidzwa zvizere muzvinangwa zvangu zvechigarire nekutonga saMambo waMadzimambo naIshe waMadzishe, saka ndiani angamira achipikisa Yeshua HaMashiach uyo ari mutsvene waAdonai, kunyange iye uyo Yahweh akatuma kuzotonga kusingaperi pamusoro pevakawa nevose vanosarudza kubatana nenzira dzavo.

Uye saka ndinorayira vakatendeka muna Kristu kuti varambe vakatendeka nokuti zuva renyu rekununurwa rasvika uye rava pedyo nemi. Tarirai chete kuna Ishe wenyu wekusingaperi wekururama nokuti vakawa vakazadza nyika nemafashamo makuru enhema nekunyepa zvakagadzirirwa kunyengedza kunyange vakasarudzwa kana zvichibvira. Naizvozvo, nzwisisai kuti vaporofita vazhinji venhema vakatunhwa vanoporofita nenzira dzaBharami uye vanotorwa nemweya yaJezebheri uye vanotaura nemweya yaantikristu vanodzidzisa mumwe Jesu anomira zvenhema panzvimbo uye achipikisa Jesu Kristu anova Yeshua HaMashiach mumwoyo yevakanyengedzwa. Vose vanotorwa mukunyengedzwa kwakadaro vachatongwa kana vakaramba kutendeuka panzira dzavo pavanosangana naIshe wavo mumiromo yevakatendeka muna Kristu. Zvirokwazvo, zvirokwazvo, vamwe vaporofita venhema vanorevesa uye vakanyengedzwa uye vanogona kusvikirwa nechokwadi chinotaurwa nemiromo yerudo, asi chokwadi chete ndicho chichavasunungura uye chokwadi chakajeka chezvivi zvavo neuporofita hwenhema kuti vanyatsozvidzora mukutendeuka.

Zvino kune avo vanoporofita kuti vawane mari, nefuma yakaipa, uye kuti vawane mbiri nekucherechedzwa uye kuti vafarire zvizere zvinhu zvenyika ino yakaipa. Hazvinei kuti ndiani anoporofita zvenhema, mutsiurei uye murambe Mashoko ake, kunyange Mashoko akagadzirirwa nevakawira kuti abate kunyange vakasarudzwa mukunyengedza kana zvichibvira. Munomhanyirei kuno nekoko, Kereke yangu yakatendeka? Handina kukurayirai here nekukurairai nekukupai simba kuti muve vakundi vangu? Kunyange avo vanokunda mativi ose ehumambo hwevavengi nenzira imwecheteyo yandakakunda nayo? Naizvozvo, uyai kuna Kristu Jesu uye ndichasimbisa mumwoyo yenyu vaporofita vangu vechokwadi nokuti vaporofita vangu vechokwadi vanotaura pamwechete nemwoyo waIshe wavo anova Shoko rakaitwa nyama. Naizvozvo, Kristu Jesu haangambopikisi rugwaro, nokuti ndizvo zvandiri!

Kune avo vanofunga nekuzvikudza kuti vanondimiririra asi vakatorwa nenhema: Munomhanyirei kuno nekoko, muchitsvaga munhu anokukwenyenyedzai nzeve dzenyu kunyange kutsvaga n'anga dzinochekera nekugunun'una dzinokuudzai chaizvo zvavanoda kuti munzwe kuti dzimutse kuchiva mumwoyo yenyu yakaipa pamunenge muchitiza kutendeuka muchienda mumaoko evanyengeri vanotumwa kunyengedza. Munomhanya kuno nekoko nokuti munoregeredza izwi raYeshua HaMashiach, nokuti munoramba chokwadi chaizokusunungurai, nokuti munoramba kudzidza kwandiri nekubata Shoko raYahweh sechiyero chenyu chepamusoro-soro uye chega. Naizvozvo, ndinorayira vose vanofunga kuti vanoporofitira Kristu Jesu, kunyange vose vanofunga kuti vanotaurira Yahweh, Mukuru Iye Wandiri kuti muzvichenjerere kuti murege kuwira murima risingaperi nekutongwa kusingagumi nokuti vose vanobatana nevakawira nenzira imwecheteyo uye vachiramba kutendeuka mazita avo achadzimwa mubhuku reupenyu kana vakaramba moto wemuchenesi wavachasiyirwa kana vakaramba vari muzvivi zvavo, nokuti hapasisina nguva.

Saka ndinorayira nekuyambira avo vandinoda kuti vadzivise kunyengedzwa kune simba kusina kuitirwa ivo kana vakafamba mukuteerera Ishe wavo, uye saka ndinorayira nekuyambira avo vandinoda kuti varege kubatwa nekunyengedza kukuru kuri kutora nyika kunyange iye zvino. Ndinorayira vose vachanzwa Mashoko aya kuti vabve pane zvakaipa uye vabve kun'anga dzinochekera nekugunun'una uye bvisai nzvimbo dzenyu patafura yaJezebheri uye rambai mibairo yaBharami. Nzwisisai kuti ani nani ane uporofita hunopikisa rugwaro ari kutaura nhema mukunyengera. Nzwisisai kuti Yahweh, Mukuru Iye Wandiri, kana Yeshua HaMashiach (uyo anovawo Kristu Jesu) haazombopikisi rugwaro. Naizvozvo, avo vanotaura vachipikisa rugwaro vanotaura vachipikisa Yahweh Mukuru Iye Wandiri uye vanotaurawo vachipikisa Yeshua HaMashiach uyo anovawo Kristu Jesu.

Nzwisisai chaizvo kuti ndini ani uye kuti Yahweh akandisika kuti ndive ani. Ndini Shoko raYahweh rakaitwa nyama rinogara mamuri vadikani vangu, uye handigoni kuzvipikisa kana kureva nhema, nokuti handina chivi uye ndicharamba ndisina chivi. Nzwisisai Baba vechiedza mavari hamuna kusanduka kana mumvuri wekushanduka, iye wamunofanira kuita naye! Nzwisisai kuti hazvibviri kuti Adhonai Yahweh azvipikise nokuti hazvibviri kuti Adhonai Yahweh areve nhema. Asi, munokotamisirei nzeve dzenyu nekupusa kwakadaro kune avo vanopfuura vachinyahwaira usiku? Muchaita chipfambi here vadikani vangu, asi imi munofanira kuva vakatendeka uye vanoteerera kuna Ishe mumwe, anova muponesi wenyu wekusingaperi, iye ari kuuya kuzobvuta vakatendeka muna Kristu kuenda kudenga chero nguva ipi zvayo kubva zvino?

Naizvozvo nzwisisai, kuti masuwo egehena haazokundi vakatendeka muna Kristu vanova Kereke yangu yechokwadi nokuti zvakanyorwa uye zvakapikirwa kare, uye zvichiri kunyorwa nekupikirwa munguva dzazvino, nokuti Mashoko aYahweh naYeshua HaMashiach mashoko akachena sesirivha, yakaedzwa muchoto chenyika, yakanatswa kanomwe. Hongu, vakawira havana simba pamusoro pevakatendeka muna Kristu uye ndichaita kuti vakatendeka vangu vatsike pasi chero chikamu chehumambo hwevavengi, kunyange kutsika avo vangatsvaga kumisa zvisingamisiki.

Ndini Kristu Jesu uye ndiani anokwanisa kumira achipikisa simba rangu neukuru hwandakagamuchira kubva kuna Yahweh pachake!

Nzwisisai kuti ini naBaba vangu tiri vamwe mukubatana nechinangwa, uye ndinogara ndichiita kuda kwaBaba vangu! Naizvozvo, hapana anogona kundipikisa, uye hapana chinhu pakati pevakawira chinogona kundikunda, kungava neunyanzvi, kana nesimba, kana nehuwandu, kana nematekinoroji avo asina maturo! Uye ndini iye akasimbisa kukundwa kwavo kusingaperi paKarivhari pandakataura Mashoko okuti, zvapera. Nzwisisai kuti munozivikanwa zvizere naKristu Jesu uye ndinokuitai kuti mumire zvine simba musimba rangu neukuru hwangu! Naizvozvo, ndiani angauya kuzokurwisai vadikani vangu? Ndiani wamunofanira kutya? Inzwai Mashoko angu vana vangu uye nzwisisai kuti handizombokusiyai kana kukurasai mumaoko evavengi venyu nokuti makavakunda uye makateerera kumiedzo mizhinji nekurohwa kwakapiwa neruoko rwangu runoponesa!

Munodederei nekutya pamberi pevavengi venyu vakakundwa? Tarirai kwandiri mukutenda uye ndichakupai Mashoko ekutaura navo kuti muvasunge, uye muvadzinge, uye munyaradze vangave vadzvinyiriri venyu vanotsvaga nekupusa kukusungai mukutongwa kwavo nenhema dzavo dzenhema dzavanotsvaga kubata nadzo njiva dzangu.

Naizvozvo, ndinokurayirai kuti muve nekutenda muna Ishe wenyu wekusingaperi uye bvumirai Mwari wekusingaperi wekururama kuti akuzadzei nesimba remukati pamberi paIshe wenyu, Kristu Jesu! Fambai nekutenda kukuru muna Yeshua HaMashiach, anova Jesu weNazareta nokuti ndakakupai simba kuti mutsike pasi zvikamu zvose zvehushe hwevavengi. Naizvozvo, nzwisisai, kuti kunyange ngirozi dzakawira dziri pasi petsoka dzangu mukukundwa kwakakwana, uye kana mukasangana naSatani pachake mungangoda kupindura sezvakapindura Mikaeri achikakavadzana pamusoro pemutumbi waMozisi, achiti mukutenda kune simba, Ishe ngaakutsiure Satani! Uye ndichatsiura, nekusunga chero upi zvake wevakawira angada kukukuvadzai kana kukumisa kuita kuda kwaiye akakutumai, kana mukaramba muchifamba makatendeka mukutenda pamunenge muchitaura Mashoko andichakupai mukutenda. Naizvozvo, ivai nekutenda mumwoyo yenyu uye muti kugomo iri bviswa! Uye richakandwa mugungwa nesimba rangu guru. Varipi avo vangafamba pamusoro pemvura saPetro meso avo akatarisa kwandiri? Varipi avo vane ushingi hwekumira nekuparidza chokwadi pamberi pezvinoratidzika sezvipingamupinyi zvisingakundiki? Hongu! Ndiani pakati penyu achava Joshua naKarebhi vangu? Mirai pamberi uye muzviratidze kuna Ishe wenyu anorayira kuteerera kwenyu mubasa!

Naizvozvo, nzwisisai simba ramunofanira kufamba mariri, vakatendeka vangu muna Kristu, uye mudzivisi wangu anodzivisa kusvikira vakatendeka muna Kristu vatorwa panguva iyoyo hapazovi nemudzivisi wekudzivisa vakawira zvakare. Panguva yamunotorwa kuenda kudenga kamwe kamwe zvino Kereke yangu, muviri wangu, uye kunyange mwenga wangu; vakawira vachasunungurwa zvizere pasina mudzivisi mukutonga kweBhabhironi Rakavanzika uye nguva pfupi mushure mezvo, zvisimbiso zvichasunungurwa, uye hwamanda dzicharira, uye nenguva ndiro dzekutsamwa kwaYahweh dzichadururwa. Nokuti zuva raIshe rasvika panyika uye nguva dzedambudziko raJakobho dzasvika, uye ndiani achakwanisa kumira?

Naizvozvo, zvichaitika kamwe kamwe kuti kupatsanurwa kwekupedzisira kuchatanga. Uye avo vakagadzwa kuti vawane ruponeso rusingaperi vachadana zita raJesu weNazareta kusvika kumagumo, uye avo veIsraeri vanogamuchira Yeshua HaMashiach saMesiya wavo wechokwadi vachatorwa kuenda kurenje vovanzwa kubva pakutsamwa kwaYahweh nehondo yezuva iro guru uye kubva kushato, chikara, nemuporofita wenhema. Uye murunyararo nekuchengeteka kwakazara vachachengetedzwa kubva mukukuvadzwa uye vachadyiswa nekuchengetwa kwavanenge vakavanzwa uye havazowanikwi.

Ndini Jesu Kristu anogara achidana vakatendeka muna Kristu kuti vaswedere pedyo muhukama hunoponesa naIshe wavo nemudzikinuri anonyatsochengeta mweya yavo.

Ndini Yeshua HaMashiach uye ndiri kudana Jakobho kuti andigamuchire saMesiya wavo wechokwadi, nokuti hapana mumwe Mesiya achazombotumwa naYahweh.

Ndini iye muna Gwayana raMwari neShumba yeRudzi rwaJudha zvakabatanidzwa nokusingaperi-peri nokuti ndini Ishe wenyu nemudzikinuri uye mukururama ndinouya kuzotonga nekuita hondo, uye ndiani anogona kumira achipikisa simba reukuru hwangu!

Ndini iye anotsigira nekusimbisa vakatendeka muna Kristu, kunyange kuitira kuti zvinangwa zvaYahweh naYeshua HaMashiach zvirarame muupenyu hwavo neushumiri hwavo uye kuti vatsike pasi chikamu chose chehumambo hwevavengi kana zvichidikanwa!

Ndini iye achazotonga munhu wese akambozviti anonditaurira sevaporofita vangu vatsvene, uye vevaporofita vangu vechokwadi mweya weuporofita unogara mavari uye vanotaura pamwechete, uye kunyanya naIshe wavo anovapa Mashoko ekutaura nokuti mweya weuporofita uchapupu hwaJesu. Uye vaporofita vangu vechokwadi vachagamuchira mibairo isingaperi yakachengeterwa vaporofita vakatendeka uye vechokwadi kuti vape Mashoko aIshe wavo uye vachapiwa rukudzo rukuru nemabasa pandichavagadzika korona nekuvagadza madzimambo nevapristi kuti vashande pasi paMambo wavo weMadzimambo naIshe weMadzishe!

Ndini iye achaita kuti vaporofita vose venhema vanyarare zvachose mukutongwa kwavo uye chirango chakaoma chavanofanira kubhadhara nekutungamirira vakarurama munzira isiriyo uye nekubatana mukupandukira kwevakawira, uye nekufunga kuti chinhu chakareruka kutaura nhema mukunyengera vachiti mashoko avo enhema anopiwa neShoko rakaitwa nyama, nokuti zvirokwazvo ndizvo zvandiri! Uye vose vanotaura zvenhema nenzira yakadaro vachatambura kutongwa kusingaperi, kurasikirwa, nekurangwa kana vakaramba kutendeuka panzira dzavo dzakaipa. Uye kune avo vanoramba kutendeuka uye vachiramba varimo vachava nemugove wavo mudziva remoto rinogara richipisa uye rinopisa zvikuru umo honye yavo isingafi.

Naizvozvo, mirai musingazununguki vakatendeka vangu vandinoda uye vandichadzikinura kamwe kamwe! Nzwisisai kuti ndichakuitai kuti mukunde zvizere kana mukaramba muchiteerera izwi rangu, uye kune avo vakakunda ndinokurayirai kuti murambe muri mumabasa andakakupai kuti muite uye muzvichengete mukuchena kwose nekugara muchizvichengeta muri mumamiriro ekutendeuka kuti ndigone kukunatsai zvizere muropa rangu rinogara richiyananisa.
