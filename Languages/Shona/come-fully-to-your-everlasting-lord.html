Uyai Zvizere kunaShe Wenyu Asingaperi!

Inzwai, inzwai! Inzwai izwi raShe wenyu uye munzwe mashoko aYeshua HaMashiach, mukotamise mwoyo yenyu mukuteerera kuda kwaShe wenyu uye bvumidzai mashoko angu kunyaradza mweya yenyu. Saka ndinokubvunzai mubvunzo? Muri kuteerera here? Kana Mwanakomana woMunhu auya, ndichawana kutenda panyika here? Zvirokwazvo, zvirokwazvo, ndichawana kutenda pakati pevakavimbika vangu vandichatora kuenda kunzvimbo yakachengeteka kumatenga nokuda kwekuvimbika kwavo, asi ruzhinji rwevari panyika havana kutenda. Saka ndinoramba ndichikuyambirai vadikanwi vangu kuti mubve pazvivi zvevatadzi, uye mubude pakati penzira dzavo muve vakaparadzana, kuti murege kugoverana navo mitongo yavo.

Asi, ndizvo zvazviri, kuti vanhu vazhinji vanoswedera pedyo neni nomuromo wavo zvisiri zvechokwadi asi mwoyo yavo iri kure neni. Pazuva iro vazhinji vachati kwandiri Ishe Ishe, uye ndichati kwavari, ibvai kwandiri handina kumbokuzivai! Nzwisisai kuti hazvina kukwana kuzivisa kuti ndiri Ishe nomwoyo usina kutenda, asi avo vanondiita Ishe zvechokwadi ndivo vanotendeuka kubva pazvivi zvavo vorega kuzviita uye vanotsvaka kurarama vakarurama pamberi pangu. Hamuzivi here kuti ini, Jesu weNazareta, ndingada mwoyo wenyu wose murudo nekuzvipira kupfuura zvibayiro zvemiromo yenyu? Hongu, ndingada tsitsi kwete chibayiro, nokuti muchidimbu imhaka yetsitsi dzangu, kudzivisa kutsamwa kwakafanira, ndimo munoratidzwa rudo rwangu, kunyange rudo rwaYahweh Mwari mukuru asingaperi! Asi mashoko angu eyambiro anoteererwa naavo vanondida uye vakapindura mukutendeuka kurudo rwangu kwavari sezvandakazviratidza kwavari kuti vaponeswe. Uye ndizvo zvazviri, rudo rwangu runogamuchirwa pakati paavo vane mwoyo yakapfava kuizwi rangu uye, zvirokwazvo, kuna vose vanondida uye vandakarayira kuti vadzidze pamusoro pangu kunyange avo vandiri kutungamirira munzira dzangu dzeruponeso.

Sei muchitendera mwoyo yenyu ichikunetsai, vaduku vangu? Ko vaapositori vangu nevaprofita havana kuyambira pamusoro pezuva rino kubvira pakutanga kwenguva here? Ko vaprofita vekare havana kufanotaura nezvevhiki raDhanieri rechimakumi manomwe richiuya mukutonga kukuru panyika here? Naizvozvo, nzwisisai, munorarama mumazuva ari pamberi chaipo pokuti dambudziko raJakobho ritange zvizere panyika yose uye mumazuva ano njodzi zhinji, zviratidzo, zvishamiso, mabasa anoshamisa, uye kunyange mitongo zvakafanotaurwa uye zvino zvasunungurwa, saka sei muchishamisika? Idzi ndidzo yambiro dzekupedzisira padiki pane zvichauya panyika yose kana vakavimbika vangu vabviswa kwandiri kumatenga kubva panyika sezvandichavaunganidza semhuru dzedanga, uye vachawana kuchengeteka kwakakwana nekugutsikana pavanomira munguva dzavo dzekutanga vakaberekwa patsva mukusingaperi kwose, kwavasingazomboparari, kana kutambura, kana kutsungirira hurwere, asi vachava nomufaro wakakwana nokuwirirana kwakakwana naYahweh, uye zvirokwazvo neni Yeshua HaMashiach uye mubairo mukuru nenhaka ndichavapa, nokuti ndine mubairo wavo muruoko rwangu rune simba.

Vazhinji vevakavimbika vangu vakaviriswa nedzidziso dzenhema dzevhangeri rebudiriro rinoparidzwa zvisizvo nevaprofita venhema nevadzidzisi vanorevera vanhu vangu nhema, vachivaudza kuti njodzi haizombovabati chero bedzi vachifunga pfungwa dzinofadza. Aya matsime asina mvura anoparidza mumwe Jesu uye havandizivi, Yeshua HaMashiach, uye handina kuvatuma. Gara uchienzanisa mashoko eavo vanoti vanotaura vachindimiririra nemagwaro uye panopesana nemagwaro ndipo pavanotaura kubva pamwoyo yavo kuti vawane makaro avo.

Zvirokwazvo, zvirokwazvo kune vashumiri venhema vazhinji vakadyara nhema pakati peungano dzangu uye vhangeri rebudiriro renhema rinotungamirira kunzira yakafara yekuparadzwa uko magedhi egehena achazaruka kune avo vanotevera kugungwa remoto unopfuta usingaperi unopfuta nemirazvo inopisa zvikuru yebhuruu yecobalt yakagadzirirwa vakawa nevanovatevera, saka musanyengerwa nenhema dzavo! Handisi kutaura nezvevaprofita vangu vakavimbika avo pamwe vangataura shoko zvisizvo vachindimiririra, kana avo vanokanganisa vakatendeka vozvigadzirisa. Nzwisisai kuti hakuna mumwe wevaprofita vangu anoprofita zvizere, asi vaprofita vangu vose vanoprofita muchidimbu uye havazombopesani nemagwaro kana vachitaura zvechokwadi vachindimiririra, Kristu Jesu, nokuti ndiri shoko raMwari rakaitwa nyama rinogara pakati penyu, kunyange ndisingazivikanwi zvakare panyama.

Vaduku vangu musanyengerwa nokuti munyika muno munogara mune kutambudzwa nenhamo, kunyanya zvino pakupera kweushe hwaSatani sezvo vakawa vachiuya mukutongwa uye sezvavanogadzirira kuita zvavanobvumirwa kuita mukutongwa kwavo nokuti kunyange vavengi vaYahweh vanoteerera izwi rake. Hamuna kuverenga here muna VaRoma kwandakarayira Pauro munzira dzangu? Ndiani achatiparadzanisa norudo rwaKristu? Dambudziko here, kana kutambudzika, kana kutambudzwa, kana nzara, kana kushama, kana njodzi, kana munondo? (Sezvazvakanyorwa, nokuda kwenyu tinourawa zuva rose, tinoverengwa semakwai okubayiwa.) Kwete, muzvinhu zvose izvi tinopfuura vakundi, kubudikidza naiye akatida. Nokuti ndinogutsikana, kuti kunyange rufu, kana upenyu, kana vatumwa, kana vabati, kana masimba, kana zvinhu zviripo, kana zvichauya, kana urefu, kana udzamu, kana chimwe chisikwa chipi zvacho, hachingagoni kutiparadzanisa norudo rwaMwari, rwuri muna Kristu Jesu Ishe wedu.

Uye saka vasanangurwa vangu vagara vachitambura zvinhu zvizhinji nokuda kwezita rangu uye kwave kune boka guru revakafira kutenda munguva yangu yenyasha vakapa upenyu hwavo mubasa rekusimudzira vhangeri reruponeso, kunyange vhangeri chairo rakachengetedza mweya yenyu nokutenda muna Jesu weNazareta nokuti muchidimbu zvakaitika kuburikidza nebasa revakafira kutenda vangu kuti vhangeri reruponeso rakazadza nyika. Asi, vazhinji venyu makaviriswa nembiriso yedzidziso dzenhema dzakasiyana-siyana dzekuti "taura uwane" dzakazadza Kereke yangu yeRaodhikiya.

Dzidziso idzi dzakaipa hadzisi dzangu uye pfungwa dzenyu hadziiti chinhu kunze kwenyu kunze kwekunge mataura mashoko angu pamiromo yekutenda pandinopa zviratidzo, zvishamiso, nemabasa anoshamisa kuti zviitwe nemaoko enyu. Saka hamugoni kuzvifungidzira muchibuda mumatambudziko nekutambudzwa, uye vakadanwa vangu, vakasarudzwa nevakavimbika havasi kunze kwenjodzi. Kupiko muShoko raMwari kunoti ani naani anotenda mandiri haazombotamburi dambudziko, kana kutambudzika, kana kutambudzwa, kana nzara, kana kushama, kana njodzi, kana munondo? Zvakasiyana, zvimwe kana kunyange zvose izvi zvakavimbiswa kune vose vanondiraramira vakatendeka, uye saka ndinokurayirai kuti murambe mashoko avo nokuti mashoko avo ingano dzakanyatsogadzirwa dzakatumwa nevakawa kuti vaparadze kutenda kwevasanangurwa kana chinhu chakadaro chingagona kuitika. Zvisinei, nzwisisai kuti vose vakavimbika vangu vanonditevera nomwoyo wavo wose vanoponeswa pakutsamwa kweGwayana nekutsamwa kwaMwari uye vachatorwa kuenda kunzvimbo yakachengeteka muchigaro choushe chaYahweh kutonga kusati kwawira zvizere panyika.

Uye saka ndizvo zvazviri vana vangu kuti vazhinji venyu muchatambura njodzi dzakasiyana-siyana nekutambudzwa kunounzwa nevakawa uye vazhinji vacharasikirwa nezvinhu zvenyika ino mukuguma uku ndisati ndaunganidza vakavimbika vangu. Nguva imwe neimwe inopfuura, kuonekwa kwangu kunenge kwava pedyo zvikuru. Saka ndinokukurudzirai, vadikanwi vangu, kuti mundimiririre uye mubatisise rudo rwangu sesimba renyu rinokurudzirai kuti mutsungirire zvinodiwa kuti muunze ruponeso kune vakarasika sezvamunovatungamirira muAreka njodzi isati yarova.

Nzwisisai zvandakapa Pauro kunyorera VaKorose: Zvino ndinofara mumatambudziko angu andinotambura nokuda kwenyu, uye ndinozadzikisa izvo zvasara zvekutambudzika kwaKristu munyama yangu nokuda kwemiviri yake, inova ungano, yandakaitwa mushumiri maererano nomurayiro waMwari, murayiro iwoyo wakapiwa kwandiri kwamuri, kuti ndizadzise shoko raMwari. Saka ndizvo zvazviri, vadikanwi vangu, kuti kana muchitambura nokuda kwezita rangu, ipapo munoenderera mberi uye munogoverana mumatambudziko angu andakatsungirira paKarivhari. Kunyange, ndisina chandakasiya chisina kuitwa muruponeso rwenyu, uye ndiri Pasika yakakwana uye yakakwana yenguva dzose, ndakakudanai mumwe nomumwe wenyu kuti mutsungirire zvinhu zvizhinji nokuda kwezita rangu, saka verengai zvose semufaro! Uye zivai vana vangu kuti ziso harina kuona, kana nzeve kunzwa, uye hazvina kupinda mumwoyo yevanhu zvinhu izvo Mwari akagadzirira avo vanomuda. Saka mhanyai, mhanyai kuti muwane! Nokuti Mwari haasi asakarurama, kuti akanganwe basa renyu, nebasa rorudo, ramakaratidza kuzita rake, pakuti makashumira Vatsvene, uye muchiri kushumira.

Vazhinji venyu makatungamirirwa zvisizvo muzvitarisiro zvenyu zvekuti hamuchazivi kuti kazhinji vakadanwa vangu, vakasarudzwa, nevakavimbika vanotsungirira nokuzvidira matambudziko, kunyadziswa, kurasikirwa, nezvimwe zvakawanda, uye mugumo wekutsungirira kwavo mumatambudziko aKristu ruponeso kune vakarasika uye mubairo mukuru nemubairo kune avo vanozvipira nenzira yakadaro. Hamuna kuverenga here muna Mabasa apo Pauro naSirasi vakatsungirira matambudziko mazhinji muMakedhonia, vachirohwa zvakaipisisa uye vachikandwa mujeri zvisina kururama uye vachisungwa zvakasimba muzvisungo paFiripi?

Asi ndakatumira kudengenyeka kwenyika kuti ndivanunure pazvisungo zvavo, uye saka mutariri wejeri reFiripi neimba yake vakawana ruponeso kuburikidza nekutsungirira kwaPauro naSirasi. Uye kune vazhinji veavo vanonditevera, vachatsungirira matambudziko nekutambudzwa nedambudziko. Asi muzvinhu zvose izvi munopfuura vakundi uye ndinokurwirai vana vangu, kana mukaramba muchiteerera izwi rangu uye muchitevera kuda kwangu nomwoyo wenyu wose uye ndichaita kuti mukunde zvinhu zvose kana mukaramba makavimbika kuizwi rangu uye muchindibvumira kutungamirira nhanho dzenyu, nokuti ruponeso rwenyu ruri mukuteerera muponesi wenyu asingaperi.

Uye saka ndinokurayirai, vadikanwi vangu, kuti mundimiririre uye mupe rudo rwangu kunyika iri kufa uye yakatongwa inomira mumaketani sezvo kutonga kwava kuda kuwira zvizere. Muchandibvumira here kukubatsirai kukunda kuora mwoyo kuitira kuti mubate tariro yenyu mupfungwa? Makanganwa here mashoko angu, vadikanwi vangu? Uye zvino zvinogara kutenda, tariro norudo, asi chikuru pane izvi rudo. Uye saka ndinokutumai kuti mude vakarasika, nokuti kazhinji kunaka kwaMwari ndiko kunotungamirira vanhu kukutendeuka.

Naizvozvo, ndishumirei munharaunda dzenyu, namatirei avo vakarasikirwa nezvose nokuti vachiri kufema zvingave kuti kurasikirwa nenyika ino kuchaita kuti vawane kusingaperi kwose. Paridzirai vatadzi uye muunze ruponeso kune vakarasika uye murangarire kuti muri vamiririri vangu vadikanwi vangu uye muzadzise kudanwa kwenyu mandiri uye murambe makasimba munguva ino yekupedzisira. Saka ndinokutumai kuti mude vavakidzani venyu uye mupe zvokudya nerubatsiro kune vanoshaya. Kunyange chinwiwa chemvura kana ndiro yezvokudya, ipai zvose zvamunogona kubatsira avo vanotambura musingakahadziki chinhu. Uye kana mukaitika kuti mutambure pamwe chete nevakarasika ipapo vadanei zvizere uye muratidze uchapupu hwangu nesimba uye mutaure muchindimiririra pamiromo yekutenda kubva pamwoyo wakazadzwa nekutenda, tariro, norudo!

Uye saka ndinokubvunzai, munotya here kubatsira vatadzi? Hamunzwisisi here kuti pandakatumwa kwete asi kumakwai akarasika eimba yaIsraeri kuti ndakapedza nguva yangu nevateresi nevatadzi? Makanganwa here kuti maimborarama upenyu hwechivi chagara chichiitwa? Makanganwa here kuti ndini murume chete asina chivi kunyange gwayana raMwari? Saka regai vadzikinurwi vaShe vataure avo vandakadzikinura kubva muruoko rwemuvengi. Idai, idai, idai kusvika pakufema kwenyu kwekupedzisira nokuti zviri nani kufa uchishumira murudo rwaKristu pane kudzokera shure mukutya pakuedza kunosiririsa kwezvinhu zvisina simba nezvinoshoreka, zvandakakunda zvizere uye ndikapa kukunda kune vose vanondiita Ishe wavo asingaperi. Saka musada upenyu hwenyu kusvika kumagumo uye mutevere muenzaniso wangu webasa risingatyi pamberi pezvakaipa nerufu zvakakundwa zvizere norudo rusingaperi rwaMwari mukuru asingaperi, kunyange El Shaddai!

Usatya, nokuti ndinewe: usatya, nokuti ndiri Mwari wako: ndichakusimbisa, uye ndichakubatsira, uye ndichakutsigira noruoko rworudyi rwekururama kwangu. Saka uyai kwandiri vana vangu muzorore murudo rwangu kwamuri uye ndichanyaradza mweya yenyu uye ndichaporesa maronda enyu uye ndichakusimbisai mukuparara kwenyu kuti muve nezvokupa kune vanokushayai nokuti ndizvo zvamakatumwa. Nokuti imi vamiririri vangu hamusi chete vakasarudzwa uye vakadanirwa kukubwinya kweHumambo hwangu, asiwo kutambura nokuda kwezita rangu.

Zvirokwazvo, zvirokwazvo, muri vagoverani vematambudziko angu, teverai chete muenzaniso wangu uye musaora mwoyo munguva yenyu yekupererwa nezano. Nokuti ndinemi nhanho imwe neimwe yenzira, uye ndini, Ishe Jesu Kristu anochenesa mweya yenyu mumoto wangu wekunatsa, kunyange muri midziyo yevhu chete mumaoko emuumbi, moto wekunatsa uchaita kuti chiso chenyu chiiswe segwenga kuitira kuti musaputsike musimba rangu asi muchiri muutera hwenyama yenyu. Asi, ndizvo zvazviri kuti muutera hwenyama ine chivi ndimo munokwaniswa rudo rwangu mamuri sezvandinokuswededzai pedyo neni uye ndichikugadzirirai zvakare zvinangwa zvenyu zvekusingaperi. Naizvozvo, chinhu chakarurama kuti vadzikinurwi vatambure nokuda kwezita rangu nokuti ndakapa chinhu changu chose muchibayiro chinorwadza kuti murarame pamberi paYahweh, kunyange Mwari mukuru wemasimbaose. Nzwisisai kuti mumiedzo yenyu nematambudziko hamusi kungounza nyasha dzangu dzinoponesa kune vanoshaya asiwo kushanda ruponeso rwenyu mukutya nekudedera kuti ndikuunzei kumubairo wenyu wakakwana uye saka tariro yenyu yava kuda kuzadzikiswa!

Zvichida makanzwa nezvekutsungirira kwaJobho? Kuti Jobho aiva murume akatendeka akasangana nenguva dzakaoma uye akarwiswa zvisina kururama naSatani. Zvichida makanzwa nezvekuguma kwaJobho nokuti Jobho akanga asiri murume asakarurama akakundwa nezvakaipa, asi murume akarurama akawana tsitsi akakunda zvakaipa nezvakanaka nokutevera mashoko aYahweh uye akaramba ari mukururama kunyange pamberi pekutambudzwa kwakaipa. Uye saka Yahweh akadzorera kuna Jobho zvakapetwa kaviri zvezvose zvakatorwa zvisina kururama kubva kwaari, uye Yahweh akaripa Jobho nokuda kwematambudziko ake nekutambudzwa. Tarirai, tinovaverenga sevakaropafadzwa vanotsungirira. Makanzwa nezvekushivirira kwaJobho, uye makaziva kuguma kwakaita Ishe. Nokuti Ishe ane tsitsi zhinji uye ane ngoni.

Uye saka zvinosara kutenda, tariro, norudo, uye chikuru pane izvi rudo. Saka nzwisisai vadikanwi vangu kuti kune vakavimbika tariro yava kuda kuzadzikiswa zvizere sezvamunotorwa kuenda kunzvimbo yakachengeteka kumatenga kubva pamitongo iri kuuya. Inzwai izwi rangu uye munzwisise rudo rwangu kwamuri vakadanwa vangu, vakavimbika, nevakasarudzwa, kunyange muviri wangu uye kunyange mwenga wangu! Naizvozvo, zororai murudo rwangu nokuti kunyange mumatambudziko makuru rudo rwangu runoramba rwakasimba uye rusingakundiki nokuti ndiri mumwe mukubatana nechinangwa naBaba vangu, uye rudo rusingaperi rwaYahweh ndirwo rwandinopa kune vose vari vadyi venhaka yeupenyu husingaperi uye vose vanouya kuna Jesu weNazareta kuti vaponeswe.

Nzwisisai hakuna munhu anogona kuuya kuna Yahweh kunze kwekunge kuburikidza neni, Kristu Jesu. Uye ndizvo zvazviri kuti rudo rwaYahweh harukundiki nokuti Yahweh haakundiki, uye naizvozvo ini handikundiki, nokuti ndakakunda. Uye ndiri Ishe asingaperi weruponeso, kunyange iye uyo Yahweh akaita Ishe weMadzishe naMambo weMadzimambo, uye akandipa zita guru kupfuura zita ripi zvaro kuti pazita raJesu Kristu ibvi rose rigopfugama kudenga nepanyika nepasi penyika. Nzwisisai kuti vakawa vanokutyai vadikanwi vangu uye kutya kukuru kunobata mwoyo yavo nokuda kwekutongwa kwavo kuri kuuya kusinganonoki.

Ndinokurayirai uye ndinokukurudzirai, vana vangu, kuti mutore rudo rwangu kunyika ino inorwara uye iri kufa uye yakatongwa munguva ino yekupedzisira njodzi isati yawira zvizere. Ndinoti kwamuri poresai vanorwara, mutsai vakafa, chenesai vane maperembudzi, dzingai madhimoni nokuti uyo anotenda mandiri mabasa andakaita panyika saizvozvowo achaita uye mabasa makuru zvirokwazvo nokuti ndaenda kuna Baba vedu. Fambai murudo rukuru nekuzvininipisa, uye musangane nevanoshaya nesimba raKristu randichashandisa nesimba guru mamuri. Nokuti mamuri, midziyo yangu yevhu, ndimo munogara simba rekunaka kwaMwari muna Kristu mamuri. Taurai kumatambudziko amunosangana nawo mukutenda. Ivai nekutenda muna Yahweh, nekutenda muna Yeshua HaMashiach uye muti kugomo iro BVA UYE UKANDWE MUGUNGWA! Uye sezvamunoita saizvozvo makatendeka, ndichapa zviratidzo zvizhinji zvishamiso nemabasa anoshamisa kuti zviitwe pamaoko evakavimbika.

Ivai vakashinga uye mune ushingi, nokuti ndiri Shumba yeRudzi rwaJudha inokurwirai. Ndinokukumbirisai uye ndinokumanikidzai norudo rwaMwari rwuri muna Kristu Jesu Ishe wenyu kuti mutsungirire nokuzvidira nokuda kwezita rangu uye muunze vakarasika kumba kuna Baba vedu kunyange munguva ino yekupedzisira. Ndinokukumbirisai vasanangurwa vangu isai muenzaniso nokuti muri vakavimbika vangu nevakasarudzwa vandakagadzira mukutambudzika kuti muutera nekuparara kwenyama yenyu muratidze simba raMwari mukuru asingaperi, kunyange Yahweh pachake sezvamunoenda mubasa nechiremera chikuru kune avo ndinokutumai kunoshumira.

Ndinemi nhanho imwe neimwe yenzira.

Ndiri mumwe mukubatana nechinangwa naBaba vangu.

Ndiri Ishe asingaperi weruponeso.

Ndini iye uyo Yahweh akaita Ishe weMadzishe naMambo weMadzimambo.

Ndiri Shumba yeRudzi rwaJudha inokurwirai.

Ini, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndatumira mashoko aya nomuranda wangu, uye nomuranda wangu mashoko angu atumirwa kuti ateererwe uye akurudzirwe vose vanowanikwa nemashoko aya. Uye Mashoko angu achawana maziso, nzeve, nemwoyo yeavo avakatumirwa. Mirai makasimba vadikanwi vangu nokuti mubairo wenyu uri pamukova penyu, musaora mwoyo chete. Zororai murudo rwangu uye muzorore mumabasa angu, marega enyu uye zvino yava nguva yekudzikinurwa kwenyu uye zvino yava nguva yeruponeso rwenyu rwakakwana, uye ndiri kuuya nokuda kwenyu vadikanwi vangu. Ndinzwei uye munzwe mashoko angu, handizokusiyei kana kukurasai vakavimbika vangu muna Kristu, kunyange vose vanoteerera izwi rangu. Tarirai kumatenga, nokuti kuonekwa kwangu kwava kuda kuonekwa kumatenga panguva neawa yamusingatarisiri. Ndiri kuuya nokuda kwenyu vakavimbika vangu, makagadzirira here?
