Kune vane zviuru zana nemakumi mana nezvina neVatsvene veDambudziko raJakobho

Kune vane zviuru zana nemakumi mana nezvina nevatsvene vedambudziko raJakobho: Tarirai! Ndinokuudzai zvinhu zvizhinji zvisati zvaitika kuitira kuti munhu akachenjera pakati penyu anonzwa izwi rangu awedzere kuchenjera, uye kuti muzive chokwadi chemashoko angu. Nokuti Satani achaedza kuvhara kubva kwevakatendeka muKereke yangu (nokuti ndichavabvuta pakubva kwavo panyika vachienda kumatenga) nehondo nekuratidzika kwenhema kwevanonzi vatorwa vasiri chinhu kunze kwevakawa vachizviratidza kunyika mukuipa pamusoro pekuipa kwakadzika midzi zvizere munhema dzavo. Nokuti ivo inyengedzo.

Vakanyepa pamusoro pechinonzi chisiko chose; nokuti hakuna chisiko chose chinongoramba chichikura chisina maturo, asi pamusoro penyika pane denga rematenga, kunyange (denga repamusoro-soro) panogara Yahweh Mwari wepamusoro-soro pachigaro chake cheushe, asi achaita ugaro hwake nevanhu. Asi iye ari kwose kwose uye ari zvose muna zvose mukati meMuviri waKristu. Panyika panogara parera rake remutengo mukuru, kunyange avo vakasarudzwa munguva dzisingaperi dzakapfuura mumwoyo maYahweh pachake. Uye saka nyika ndeyaIshe nekuzara kwayo; nokuti ini, Jesu weNazareta, ndakatenga nyika neropa rangu rinodzikinura.

Dzidzai nezvechisiko chenyu chechokwadi (dzidzisai kuti mudzidze) uye tendai tsananguro nemashoko ari muRugwaro Rutsvene maererano nekusikwa, nokuti zvakaita sezvakatsanangurwa naYahweh muRugwaro, uye hazvigoneki kuti Yahweh areve nhema. Kana ndikakuudzai zvinhu zvepanyika, mukasatenda, mungatenda sei kana ndikakuudzai zvinhu zvekudenga? Kwete, hakuna vatorwa nenzira inotsanangurwa nenhema dzavo, asi ngirozi dzakawa, vapanduki, kunyange Satani neushe hwake vakanyepa nekunyengedza nyika kuti vose vaparadzwe vanofarira kusarurama.

Vakaruka ngano zhinji dzakagadzirwa neuchenjeri kuita jira renhema, uye vakadzora mativi ose enyaya dzavo kubva pakutanga pavakafambisa zvirongwa zvavo. Nokuti vanodzora pfungwa huru uye vanodzora pfungwa inopikisa, uye vanobatanidza mabasa avo akaipa nhanho nenhanho, uye kunyengedza kwavo kukuru! Asi chiedza cheRugwaro Rutsvene chinofumura unyengeri, asi munhu anoramba rubatsiro rwaMwari kusvikira kutongwa kwaziviswa uye njodzi yarova. Ipapo vanhu vanotsvaka kutendeuka kana nguva yapera mushure mekutongwa, uye avo vanorarama nenzira yakadaro havangapunyuki pakutongwa kwavo. Nokuti chose chinotaurwa naYahweh hapana anogona kuchichinja!

Vazhinji vachakundwa nenyengedzo dziri kuuya dzichamuka kubva muhondo yenyika nenjodzi yave kuda kuwira nyika. Nokuti vachazviratidza kuvanhu sezvisikwa zvine mutsa zvakabva kunyika dzakabudirira kuzogovera ruzivo rwavo rwemutsa. Asi irwo ruzivo rwezvakanaka nezvakaipa rwakagadziridzwa kuti ruenderane nezvinangwa zvavo. Vachange vaine matekinoroji epamusoro uye vachaonekwa sevakabudirira zvikuru, kupfuura munhu wenyama. Asi kubudirira kwavo kuri mukuipa chete, uye havasi vepanyika ino asi vakasikwa naYahweh pakutanga kwekusikwa kumatenga. Vaivapo Yahweh paakasika nyika. Baba mukuziva kwavo kumberi vaiziva kuti vaizopanduka uye saka vakachengeta chakavanzika chemwoyo wavo chakakosha chakavigirwa avo vaiziva kuti vaizounza kuparadzwa kwakaipa kuchisikwa chavo.

Ndichiri kudaidza munhu wenyama kukururama, asi munhu wenyama anotosarudza hurwere, chivi, nerima; uye saka ndichapa vanhu izvo zvavanoshuvira zvikuru. Sezvo munhu wenyama achiita zvakaipa uye achida rima, ndichamupa rima risingadzorwi.

Nokuti parizvino, munhu wenyama anozvikudza akamira munyasha dzangu achishandisa tsitsi sechikonzero chekuwedzera chivi pamusoro pechivi, panzvimbo pekugamuchira chibvumirano cherunyararo chaYahweh, kunyange ini, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndakapa ropa rangu risina chivi mukuteerera Yahweh semuripo wezvivi zvose zvevanhu kwenguva dzose. Musakanganise: ndini Mesia wechokwadi, uye zvimwe zvose zvitevedzeri zvisina maturo zvisina kukosha.

Satani akapanduka nguva pfupi mushure mekutanga kwekusikwa uye akasvibisa chisikwa chaBaba nenzira isina nyadzi, yakaipa, ine ruvengo, uye inoparadza; asi zvakaitwa neunyanzvi hukuru uye nezviratidzo zvenhema nezvishamiso, kunyange matekinoroji epamusoro anokatyamadza pfungwa. Uye semubairo vanoda kunamatwa nekuteerera kwakasimba kunzira dzavo, asi nzira dzavo dzinogara dzichiunza rufu nekuparadzwa kwevanhu, nokuti dzakagadzirirwa izvozvo.

Hakuna chinhu chitsva pasi pezuva, vana vangu. Vanozonzi vanamwari venhoroondo yekare nevazukuru vavo vanonyangadza vanonzi magamba vachadzoka, uye sezvakanga zvakaita mumazuva aNoa, ndizvo zvazvichaita. Nokuti vanosunungurwa pazvisungo zvavo nekutambudzika kwavo kuti vagamuchire kutongwa kwavo kuzere, uye ndicharipira, anodaro Ishe Jesu Kristu, uye Yahweh achavaripira kusvika pakupedzisira.

Satani nevakawa vake vakaramba kupfugamira urongwa hwaYahweh uye panzvimbo pezvo vakaedza kuba nyika kubva kuvanhu kuburikidza nenyengedzo huru. Satani akatoedza kuba nhaka yangu kubva kuna Baba veZviedza! Asi hapana anokwanisa kubvuta kunyange mudiki wenyu pamaoko angu, uye hapana anogona kubvisa nhaka yangu; nokuti yakapiwa naYahweh pachake, uye ani naani anoramba kupfugamira mabvi kwandiri, Jesu Kristu, anopandukira urongwa hwaYahweh, achibatana nevakawa nokusingaperi.

Saka ndinokuudzai, musatenda nhema dzaCopernicus, aive muJesuit neFreemason aishandira Satani. Akangodzidzisazve mashoko evazivi vechihedheni vechiGiriki vekare. Uye saka maonero enyu echisiko akagadzirwa nevanonamata zuva nevahedheni zvichibva panhema dzaSatani, kunyange maJesuit anomhura akanganisa masisitimu enyu edzidzo nedzidzo inopesana nedzidzo, nechinangwa chakazara chekuti chisiko chavo chenhema chinyorwe mupfungwa dzevanhu kuburikidza nenhema dzakaputirwa sedzidzo. Panzvimbo pezvo, kudzidziswa kusingafungi kwakaitika, uye nyika sezvairi chaizvo haizivikanwi nevanogara panyika.

Zvino nhandare yagadzirirwa kunyengedzwa kukuru kwazvo, uye nhema dzakatovakwa, nokuti nheyo dzadzo ndidzo zvivi nenyengedzo huru. Nyengedzo idzi dzakadzika midzi mudzidzo dzenyu dzechiJesuit. Nokuti nyika yose yakanyengedzwa nenhema dzavo; kunyange vakasarudzwa chaivo vaizonyengedzwa kana zvichibvira. Uye saka musungo wagadzirira kubata, uye chinhu chega chiri kuudzivirira ndiye mudzivisi, uyo achabviswa nekukurumidza uye munguva pfupi. Ipapo hondo huru ichapararira panyika yose apo vakatendeka vangu vakaunganidzwa kwandiri kumatenga. Zvino kubva mumadota kuchamuka chikara nemuporofita wenhema, avo vachange vari pakati penyengedzo iri kuuya.

Saka musatenda nhema dzavo, imi vane simba rangu, nokuti muri vanhu vakasiyana uye ndakakudanai nekukuisai chisimbiso cheMwari Mupenyu, kuti hasha dzaMwari dzirege kukubatai. Ndichakutumai semasimba angu. Muchava manzwi angu mazhinji anodanidzira murenje kwose kwamunotumwa, uye ndiri kukutumai kunounganidza makwai angu asara akarasika.

Nokuti boka guru richabuda mudambudziko raJakobho, uye muchaita mabasa makuru uye mucharayira zvisingagoneki, nokuti ndichapa zviratidzo zvizhinji zvine simba, zvishamiso, nezvishamiso kuti zviitwe nemaoko enyu. Muchaita zvishamiso zvikuru zvekare zvakanyorwa muRugwaro Rutsvene, kunyange musimba raEria, kunze kwekuti mabasa makuru uye ane simba kupfuura akanyorwa pamusoro pake kare achaitwa nemi.

Nokuti Rugwaro runoti uyo anotenda mandiri, Yeshua HaMashiach, haangaiti chete mabasa andakaita panyika muushumiri hwangu kuJudhea segwayana raMwari, asi mabasa makuru kupfuura andakaita imi muchaita, nokuti ndichapa zviratidzo zvine simba, zvishamiso, nezvishamiso pamaoko enyu pamunorayira zvisingagoneki nekutenda mandiri. Saka tarirai kwandiri nguva dzose. Uye chengetai maziso enyu ari pandiri, nokuti ndichakutungamirirai nhanho nenhanho. Kufamba kwenyu kuchava kwekuchenjera, uye muchava nerurimi rwevakachenjera, uye ndichava simba renyu neruponeso, nokuti mandiri, Kristu Jesu, munogara kuzara kwose.

Uye saka ndinokurairai mose kuti munguva pfupi iri kutevera mari ichashaya basa, uye mari chete inogamuchirwa ichada chiratidzo kuti muwane zvinhu zvenyika. Musatora chiratidzo, nokuti zvingava nani kufa muupenyu huno (musina kuda upenyu hwenyu kusvika kumagumo) pane kutora chiratidzo mokandwa mugungwa remoto risingaperi rakagadzirirwa Dhiyabhorosi nengirozi dzake, nokuti nguva dzavo dzasvika; chete musabatana navo. Nokuti vachavimbisa zvipikirwa zvinoita sezvikuru uye zvakakosha, asi mashoko avo ndeenhema, uye uturu huri mumashoko avo, uye zvipikirwa zvavo ndezvenhema uye zvizere nezvinhu zvose zvisina kuchena. Havazombokwanisi kuzadzisa zvipikirwa zvavo, nokuti kutongwa kwavo kunouya; havana rugare, uye rugare ruchabviswa zvachose panyika.

Kuipa kwevanhu kuchange kusina kudzivirirwa zvachose; uye kune vazhinji, kana mudzivisi abviswa, vachazvipa zvachose kupenga nekuipa. Kunze kwekuti uyo anorarama nebakatwa anofanira kuurayiwa nebakatwa, uye vachawira kuruboshwe nekurudyi muhuwandu hukuru, asi hazvizouyi kwamuri pamunofamba neni; chete musada upenyu hwenyu kusvika kumagumo uye teverai muenzaniso wangu webasa rekuzvipira nekuzvibayira. Nokuti mubairo mukuru uchava wenyu nekuda kwekuteerera nekutendeka.

Saka zvishuwo zvevanhu nekuipa zvichange zvisina kudzivirirwa zvachose, uye Dhiyabhorosi nengirozi dzake vachange vasina kudzivirirwa zvachose, uye zvinonyangadza zvavo zvose zvakaipa zvichange zvisina kudzivirirwa zvachose, uye vachauya sevabatsiri vekare. Kunyengedza kwavo kwakadzama uye kune midzi yakadzika iyo yave kuda kudzupurwa zvachose. Havasi vanamwari, uye havasi vakabudirira vane mutsa, asi vanyengeri, uye vakazvisvibisa nenzira dzose. Uye saka vachange vasina kudzivirirwa zvachose, uye kuzara kweushe hwaSatani kuchasunungurwa kubva mugomba rakadzika, nokuti richavhurwa.

Izvi zvichaitwa kuratidza munhu wenyama zvaakasarudza uye kupa mukana wekupedzisira wekutendeuka; nokuti kuipa hakuchazovanzwa nekudzivirirwa, uye chakavanzika chose chichabuda pachena. Kunyange nhema dzenyengedzo yavo huru nenyengedzo dzakasimba dzichaiswa pachena kuti vose vaone, nokuti Yahweh akazvitaura muRugwaro Rutsvene, uye izvo zvinotaurwa naYahweh hapana anogona kuzvishandura.

Uye saka kupatsanurwa kwekupedzisira kuchaitika, uye vazhinji vevanogutsikana vandinoda vachasiyiwa kumiedzo nekuedzwa kuri kuuya, kunyange kuitira kuti vaponeswe. Nokuti hapasisina nguva, uye nyasha dzangu netsitsi hazvichazopiwa nenzira imwechete, uye hapasisina nguva yenzira dzekudziya. Munhu wose achafanira kuita sarudzo; uye kune vanotya nevasina kutenda vachange vasarudza Satani neushe hwake pavaigona kunge vakandisarudza, kunyange Jesu Kristu, mwanakomana waMwari akauya munyama uye akazvipa sePaseka yakakwana yezvivi zvose zvevanhu kamwechete zvachose. Vazhinji vacharamba vakaomesa musoro vachiramba ruoko rwangu rwerubatsiro munguva yekushaiwa kukuru, nokuti rubatsiro rwangu rubatsiro rusingaperi uye rwekusingaperi rwemubairo mukuru, tsitsi, nyasha, nerudo.

Asi, nyika zhinji ichatenda nhema uye ichashamisika nechikara, ichiti, ndiani akafanana nechikara? Vachazvibvumira nyore nyore kurumwa nekurumwa kwenyoka uko uturu hwacho husingamborapiki, nokuti ndehwekusingaperi. Kuti munhu asarudze zvakaipa munguva dzekusarudza ichava sarudzo yekusingaperi, uye avo vanoita sarudzo iyoyo vachamira mukutongwa kukuru.

Avo vanosarudza zvakaipa nekuzvidira vachazvidavirira pamusoro pekufunga kwose kwakaipa, shoko rose rakaipa, uye chiito chose chakaipa chavakaita panyika, nokuti mabhuku achavhurwa uye hakuchazovi netsitsi. Uye hapana munhu achakwanisa kumira mukururama kwake, nokuti munhu haana kururama kwake; nokuti nyoka yakavakanganisa kubva pakutanga, uye mazuva ayo ekuora ari kusvika kumagumo. Saka Yahweh akandituma, mwanakomana wake mumwechete akaberekwa, sechibvumirano cherunyararo kumunhu wenyama, uye munhu wenyama akaramba rugare rusingaperi nokuti munhu wenyama anoda zvakaipa.

Kutongwa kukuru kwave kuda kusunungurwa paushe hwaSatani. Kutongwa uku kuchawedzera kuipa, uye zviitiko zvinoitwa mukutonga zvichawedzera kuipa. Nhamburiko ichawedzerwa panhamburiko uye kutambudzika kuchawedzerwa pakutambudzika kune vose vanoramba kutendeuka pamabasa avo akaipa. Nokuti vazhinji havazotendeuki pamabasa emaoko avo, kuti varege kunamata madhimoni, nezvidhori zvendarama, nesirivha, nendarira, nematombo, nematanda: izvo zvisingagoni kuona, kana kunzwa, kana kufamba: Uye havazotendeuki pamhondi dzavo, kana pauroyi hwavo, kana paupombwe hwavo, kana pambavha dzavo.

Nokuti vakawa havasi vanamwari, asi vanyengeri venhema, vanyepedzeri, vatambi, uye vanyengeri. Vanounza rufu nekuparadzwa chete kune vanotevera, uye vose vakanyengedzwa vachanyadziswa zvikuru, nokuti zvavanotarisira zvichanyangarika mugungwa remoto iro risina tariro. Uye ini, Kristu Jesu, ndichaparadza zvachose ushe hwaSatani mukutongwa kukuru, nokuti kutongwa kukuru kuchasunungurwa pavari vose, uye ndini Shumba yeRudzi rwaJudha.

Uye saka chisimbiso chimwe nechimwe chichawedzera kutonga pamusoro pekutonga, uye hwamanda imwe neimwe ichawedzera kutonga pamusoro pekutonga, nhamo imwe neimwe ichawedzera nhamo pamusoro penhamo, uye nendiro imwe neimwe kunouya hasha dzaMwari dzizere, kunyange hasha pamusoro pehasha. Ushe hwaSatani huchatambura zvikuru, uye ushe hwake huchaparadzwa zvachose uye kuparadzwa zvachose.

Nokuti vachazviunganidza uye vachazvironga vachipikisana nekuuya kwaIshe, nokuti ndichauya zvirokwazvo. Kubva pakutanga kwedambudziko raJakobho, pachava nemazuva manomwe emakore, nokuti vhiki rechimakumi manomwe raDhanieri richazadziswa rekuparadzwa kwekusarurama neruponeso rwaIsraeri. Nokuti ndichachenesa Israeri kubva pakuipa kwakabata nyika.

Nokuti kunyange Yahweh kana ini pachangu, Yeshua HaMashiach, hatina kutuma kana kupa masimba vatongi vavo, nokuti vanotonga mukusarurama uye kuuraya kwavo kwemarudzi kunonyangadza, uye vanhu vangu vanobhadhara mutengo wehondo dzavo. Asi vazhinji veKereke yangu vanotenda nhema dzavo dzechiZionist, saka nzwisisai kuti handisi muZionist, nokuti ivo vanokoshesa maJuda, uye vari masora, mbeu chaiyo yaSatani; uye ndichavadzura kubva munyika, nokuti vakatora nhaka yangu, uye ndiri kuuya kuzotora zvinhu zvangu. Nokuti ini, Yeshua HaMashiach, kunyange ini, ndine gwaro renyika; nokuti ndakaitenga neropa rangu risina chivi, uye ndine makiyi erufu negehena. Uye ndakabhadhara zvivi zvevanhu, uye ani naani anodaidza zita raIshe achaponeswa.

Saka nyika ndeyangu nekuzara kwayo, uye ndichachenesa nyika kubva pakuipa kwavo, uye sinagogi raSatani richaparadzwa zvachose, uye saka ndichaunza sungano itsva panyika uye ndichatonga mukururama. Nokuti sezvo nyika inowedzera kusviba nekuipa sekondi imwe neimwe, kuuya kwangu kwechipiri kunowedzera kuswedera sekondi imwe neimwe.

Ivai netariro huru yekuuya kwangu; nokuti ndichauya uye ndichabuda kubva kumatenga nemauto ekudenga, kunyange uto risina kufanana nerimwe, nokuti uto rakaita serangu harisati rambovapo, uto risingakundiki rinoumbwa neavo vanorarama upenyu husingaperi uye vasingagoni kuurayiwa kana kukundwa. Nokuti ndakapiwa simba naYahweh nechinangwa chakadaro, uye ndichave ndakavapa simba nemubairo wekutendeka kwavo nerudo rwavo kwandiri nezita rangu, kunyange Jesu Kristu! Nokuti ani naani anoda uye anoshumira Yeshua HaMashiach anodawo uye anoshumira Yahweh, nokuti hakuna mukuru kupfuura Mwari mukuru asingaperi waIsraeri! Ani naani anoramba Mwanakomana, iyeye haana Baba; uye ndini Jesu Kristu mumwechete akaberekwa naBaba akazvipa nekuda kwezvivi zvavose, uye ndiri mupristi mukuru nokusingaperi maererano neurongwa hwaMerkizedeki.

Uye ndichakunda zvachose dhiragoni, chikara, nemuporofita wenhema. Nokuti chikara nemuporofita wenhema vachakandwa vari vapenyu mugungwa rinopfuta iro rinopfuta nemirazvo yebhuruu inopisa zvikuru isingambodzimiki uye umo musingazovi kunyange sekondi imwe chete yekuzorora. Asi mabasa avo ose akaipa achadzorerwa kwavari nokusingaperi. Kutambura kwose kwavakakonzera kuchatsiviwa nokusingaperi, nokuti Yahweh acharipira chiito chose chakaipa kusvika pafarthing yekupedzisira muzvipfuva zvavo zvakaipa, uye vacharipira mhosva dzavo nokusingaperi.

Uye saka Satani achasungwa mugomba rakadzika kwemakore ane chiuru, nokuti zvakaziviswa naYahweh. Ini, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndichatonga nyika mukururama kwezuva rimwe. Nokuti kuna Yahweh zuva rimwe rakaita semakore ane chiuru uye makore ane chiuru sezuva rimwe. Uye ndichatonga kubva pachigaro cheushe chaDhavhidhi, uye muIsraeri kutonga kwangu kuchagara mukuzadziswa kwezvipikirwa zvakaitwa naYahweh kumadzibaba, uye zuva rangu rava pedyo.

Asi kutanga kutongwa kunofanira kuuya panyika uye nyika ichacheneswa kubva pachinhu chose chakaipa. Nokuti hapana chinhu chakaipa chichabvumirwa muushe hwangu, umo muchava nerugare nekuchengeteka sezvakavimbiswa naYahweh pachake. Uye shumba ichavata negwayana. Ini, kunyange ini, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndinozivisa mashoko asati aitika, kuti muzive kuti Mwari waIsraeri auya kuzounganidza makwai ake akarasika eimba yaIsraeri kwandiri, Mufudzi Mukuru, uye ndicharova marudzi netsvimbo yesimbi, uye ndichakunda zvachose chisikwa chaBaba mukururama neutsvene hwechokwadi. Hapasisina nguva.

Ndini Yeshua HaMashiach, Mesia wechokwadi kuvanhu vangu Israeri.

Ndini iye achazotonga nyika mukururama kubva paChigaro cheUshe chaDhavhidhi.

Ndini iye achazoonekwa mumakore nemauto ekudenga, nokuti ndichavarayira.

Ndini iye achazounganidza vakasara vangu seboka guru kubva mudambudziko riri kuuya.

Ndini iye anoedza mwoyo yevanhu, uye ndichaparadzanisa makwai nembudzi.

Ndini Ishe wekukohwa, uye nguva dzekukohwa dzasvika.

Ndini Ishe weMadzishe naMambo weMadzimambo, uye ndiani angamira?

Tarirai zuva raIshe rava kuda kutsemuka panyika nenjodzi nekutongwa. Nokuti zuva guru rehasha dzake rasvika, uye ndiani achakwanisa kumira?
