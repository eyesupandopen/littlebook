Inzwai Izwi Rangu!

Shoko raShe rakauya pamusoro pangu. Inzwai izwi rangu, vadiki vangu, uye uyai kumaoko angu akatambanuka. Zivai kuti rudo rwangu kwamuri harugoni kuputswa uye harigoni kukurwa, nokuti rudo rwaBaba vangu mukuti vakapa hunhu hwavo kuvana vavo, uye mandiri kuzara kwose kunogara. Ndakakudanai nemazita enyu uye ndakakukokani kuti mumire pamberi pangu. Makwai angu anonzwa izwi rangu, sezvo achiziva izwi romufudzi wavo. Hamuchati munzwa izwi rangu here? Makapfumbira nzeve dzenyu kuzwi rangu here? Ndinodanidzira nomuzira mumigwagwa uye munzira dzemumisha; ndakatoona matirongo enyu uye ndakaunza ruponeso kune vakatambudzwa nevakakanganwikwa.

Haiwa, munhu, sei muchisiya Ishe wenyu? Sei makatsauka panzira dzangu? Asi, muchati, handina kutsauka panzira dzenyu; ndini wandakadanwa naShe. Zvirokwazvo, ndakadana, uye makadavira kwechinguva, nguva youjaya nokufara kukuru. Vamwe venyu vakatakurwa kunogarika mapere, uye vazhinji venyu vakatsauswa nemashoko akanaka, kutaura kwakanaka, uye ndimi dzenhema dzichakurumidza kumiriswa.

Rangarirai rudo rwenyu rwokutanga, uye tendeukei; reurirai zvivi zvenyu kwandiri, nokuti ndinogara ndakagadzirira kuchenesa zvivi zvenyu muropa rangu rinoyananisa.

Inzwai izwi rangu, vana vangu, nokuti mazwi mazhinji anonzwika akafanana uye vaKristu vazhinji venhema vanoedza kunyepedzera izwi rangu. Zvirokwazvo, ndinochengeta uye ndinofudza kereke yangu noruoko rwangu pacharo, kuti ndikupe kuna Mwari wakachena semhandara uye akapfeka mucheka wakachena wakanaka, nemavara ezvivi zvenyu akakanganwirwa, akabviswa, uye akakanganwikwa, nokuti ndakaakanda mukadzika kwakadzika, asingambosimudzizve. Asi, vazhinji venyu makavhiringidzwa uye makabatwa nenyika ino yakaipa. Vakakurwisa neuroyi hwavo kuburikidza nokurumwa kwenyoka, asi nzwisisai kuti kunyange nyoka ikakurumei, hapana kukuvara kuchauya kwamuri, nokuti chisimbiso chaBaba vangu hachigoni kuputswa. Nanganirai pfungwa dzenyu kwandiri, mwanakomana wake mumwe oga, waakapa nokuda kuyananisa zvivi zvenyu kuti tibatane, tisingambotanhanizve. Regai ndikurapei; regai ndikurapei zvachose uye ndibvise mavanga enyu kuti muve vakakwana uye vakazara pamberi pangu. Teverai nzira dzangu uye teverai Shoko rangu, nokuti:

Ndini Shoko rakaitwa nyama rinogara pakati penyu.

Ndini chingwa choupenyu, uye ani naani anodya pacho haachatonzwazve nzara.

Ndini iye umambo hwake husiri hwenyika ino, uye mubairo wangu uri neni kune vakatendeka vangu nevakasarudzwa.

Ndini iye anofamba pakati pezvigadziko zvinomwe zvemwenje uye anoraira vatumwa vanomwe veKereke.

Ndini mutsvene waMwari.

Ndini munyengetedzeri wenyu.

Ndini iye anoedza mwoyo yomunhu.

Ndinogara ndakagadzirira kuchenesa zvivi zvenyu muropa rangu rinoyananisa.

Ndini mutongi wakarurama.

Ndini Kristu chaiye wakafanotaurwa kubva pakutanga.

Ndini mwanakomana waMwari akauya munyama.

Ndini iye ndakapa ropa rangu risina chivi chevatadzi, kuti vagare nhaka youpenyu husingaperi.

Ndini mwanakomana waMwari akauya munyama.

Ndini mufudzi akanaka.

Ndini iye ndakauya kune vangu uye ndakarambwa nevangu.

Ndini Kristu chaiye, wakasarudzwa naBaba vangu kubva panheyo dzenyika.

Ndini wandiri.

Vana vangu, ndinokuudzai chokwadi kuti ndakatambanudza ruoko rwangu kunyika iri kufa kweMajubhiri mazhinji zvino, uye ruoko rwangu harungagari rwakatembanuka munyasha netsitsi, kunyange nyasha netsitsi zviri neni nguva dzose. Ndakadana vazhinji, uye vazhinji vakaramba. Zvisinei, avo vandakadana nekusarudza nekuita vatsvene nekushambidza nekuchenesa nekurapa, avo chaivo vachamira pamberi pechigaro changu chokutonga uye vachatakura mubairo mukuru wokutendeka kwavo.

Kunyange ndine mabasa mazhinji amuchiri kuita, uye kunyange makaita zvizhinji kwandiri nezita rangu, ndinoda moyo wenyu wose kupfuura mabasa enyu. Ndinoda kuti mudzokere kwandiri uye mubve pazvivi zvenyu. Uyai makachena kwandiri, nokuti Ishe wenyu akagadzirira noruoko rwangu rwokurapa rwakatembanuka kuti ndikuitei makachena sechando chitsva. Kunyange maoko angu akaboorwa, ropa rangu rinoyananisa rakateerera kubva maari uye kubva mumuviri wangu kusvikira padonhwe rokupedzisira. Ndakapa zvose zvangu kuti murarame pamberi paBaba vedu nokusingaperi.

Zvirokwazvo, handina kuyananisira chivi chimwe choga; zvirokwazvo, handina kuyananisira zvivi zvishoma; zvirokwazvo, handina kuyananisira zvivi zvizhinji, asi ndakayananisira zvivi zvose zvose kana muchingodavira kwandiri pandinokupomerai mwoyo yenyu nomweya wangu une simba. Hamunzwisisi here kuti handina chishuwo chokukupai mhosva? Kune mumwe ane chishuwo icho uye anochiva kuparadzwa kwenyu uye anokupomerai masikati nousiku pamberi paBaba vangu, kunyange ndiri munyengetedzeri wenyu. Akakandwa pasi uye akakurwa; asi, sei muchisimudzira mbavha kunzvimbo yakakwirira mumwoyo yenyu pandine kukunda kwakazara pamusoro pake noumambo hwake huchakurumidza kuva pasina? Nyararai uye rugare uye uyai kwandiri nomwoyo wakazvininipisa. Hamuzivi here kuti dai maizvitongerera, hamaizongotongerwa? Ndikumbire kuti ndikuratidzei upenyu hwenyu, nokuti ndichakufukurirai zvinhu zvizhinji kuti mureurire mhosva dzenyu uye muitwe vakakwana.

Musatya, vadiki vangu, nokuti handisi ini ndinokupai mhosva. Teererai hana yenyu uye musaite kuti izwi rangu riite zvisingaiti. Nyika ino ichakurumidza kukandwa pasi, uye senyika ino ichishandurwa nokusingaperi, muchasimudzirwa kudenga kumusoro, kuti ndikupe kuna Baba, kuti muzive nhaka yenyu uye mugamuchire mibairo yenyu yokutendeka. Ndakashuva zuva ratichazoonana kudenga kuti pakupedzisira muve noupenyu hwenyu hwechokwadi hwakaratidzwa kwamuri sezvo mapunyuka upenyu huno hwenguva hwakaipa, hwechivi, uye hwakaipa. Ndini iye anoedza mwoyo yomunhu, kwete kuti ndikupei mhosva, asi kuti kuburikidza nomoto womunatsi mungachenurwa panzira dzenyu dzekare uye muzive nzira dzangu dzerudo, chiedza, mufaro, rugare, kutsunga, uye unyoro.

Inzwai kutsiura kwangu uye gamuchirai kuranga kwangu nokuti Baba venyu vanokubatai sevana, uye mwana upi waanoda Baba asingatsurwi uye asingakangwi. Kuranga kwangu kunopa zvibereko zvorugare zvokururama, nokuti kuranga kwangu ndokukuchenesa zvachose pazvivi zvenyu kana muchingonzwa mashoko angu uye mutevere izwi rangu. Endai, mirai, uye taurai mashoko ose oupenyu uhu. Vamwe vachauya kuruponeso muawa ino yokupedzisira, uye chete muzita raJesu Kristu, zita rangu rine simba rine ruponeso, nokuti muzita rimwe chete hapana ruponeso rwakapihwa. Zita rangu rine simba rinomira rakasimba, richimirirai kuti muuye navo kwandiri, kuti ndipe vanotendeuka kuna Baba semwana mutsva achangoberekwa mukusingaperi. Ndine rudo rukuru kwamuri, uye sezvakapiwa naBaba, ndichapa kune vakatendeka vangu nevakasarudzwa.

Ndini mwanakomana waMwari akauya munyama, asina chivi uye akachena nokusingaperi. Saizvozvowo, ndini iye ndakapa ropa rangu risina chivi chevatadzi kuti vagare nhaka youpenyu husingaperi. Hamusi imi, vakatendeka vangu nevakasarudzwa, pakati pevagari venhaka yokubwinya kusingaperi? Sei muchiri kumira makanyarara sokunge mukamirira mashoko akakodzera. Taurai! Shurirai muromo wenyu mutaure, nokuti ndichafambisa kutaura kwenyu kuti mushumire pakuvara kwavakakokerwa vangu vachiri kutambura, uye vazhinji vachiri kudavira. Batanai neni mubasa rangu muawa ino yokupedzisira ndisati ndazungunusa nheyo dzenyika uye ndikaibvarura munzvimbo zhinji uye kutonga kukuru kunomedza nyika. Ndichadzosera nyika ino kwamuri muUmambo hwangu huchauya, asi kutanga umambo hwavakaipa huchaparadzwa uye huchatakurwa mukutongwa kuti husungwe mugomba, uye vanoziva kuti dziva jena-moto kwavachapedza nguva yose rakamirira.

Ndichaparadza zvachose zvigaro zvavo zvesimba; ndichaparadza zvachose masimba avo ose; ndichavapisa zvachose pasi nomoto usingagoni kudzimwa, nokuti kunyange ini, Ishe akatendeka, ndakakuudzai zvisati zvaitika. Mumba maBaba vangu mune dzimba zhinji, uye ukuru hukuru nokubwinya kwakakumirai. Naizvozvo, ngapasava nomunhu anokubvutai mubairo wenyu uye garai makatendeka kwandiri. Chengetai kutarisa kwenyu kwakanangana neni, Ishe usingaperi, kunyange Ishe wokubwinya. Ndini mufudzi akanaka; ndini Kristu chaiye wakasarudzwa naBaba vangu kubva panheyo dzenyika. Ndini iye ndakauya kune vangu uye ndakarambwa nevangu, kunyange kuburikidza nokuyedzwa kukuru Jakobho achaponeswa noruoko rwangu rune simba. Kupedzisira kwezvinhu zvose kwava pedyo, uye kuguma kwomuchinda wenyika ino kwava pedyo. Kunyange achasunungurwa kwechinguva chiduku, kutongwa kwake kusingaperi kunotevera, kwaasinga atongozombosunungurwa uye hakuna mushonga. Dzokererai kwandiri, taurai neni, shumirai kwandiri, nokuti Baba vangu vakandipa zvinhu zvose, uye mubairo wangu kwamuri wakachengetwa kudenga kune vakatendeka vangu nevakasarudzwa uye wakakasimba sechisimbiso chaBaba vangu chandakakusimbisai nacho. Kune, asi maminetsi mashoma asara ezera renyasha, uye kutonga kwangu kuchawira kamwe kamwe sehasha dweGwayana dzichauya panyika. Gadzirirai mwoyo yenyu uye muzvigadzirire, nokuti nguva yasvika.
