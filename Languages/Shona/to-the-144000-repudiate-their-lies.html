Chikumi 02, 2024

Kune vane 144,000: Rambai Manyepo Avo

Rerekai nzeve dzenyu kuinzwi rangu munzwe huchenjeri hwangu, nekuti huchenjeri hunodana zvikuru mumigwagwa: muchanzwa here inzwi rangu? Ndini Yeshua HaMashiach, iye Mwanakomana waYahweh, wakauya panyama uye ndiri Gwayana raMwari rinobvisa zvivi zvenyika. Nzwisisai kuti nyika yamunogara haisi mufananidzo wenhema wakapendwa navadzidzisi venyu vechiJesuit kuti vakutsausisei. Vazhinji venyu makadzidziswa manyepo echiJesuit, kunyange kudzidza zvinopesana nokudzidza. Hondo iyi pachokwadi yakagadzirirwa kuumba pfungwa dzevanhu kuti dzizviise pasi paSatani uye inoedza kubvisa nekutora kuda kwemunhu kwakasununguka kuitira kuti munhu atevere zvaakadzidziswa asingafungi.

Nhoroondo yechokwadi haigoni kunzwisiswa kubva kumanyepo evazivi vechiJesuit, nekuti ivo varevi venhema vanoparadzira zvisiri zvechokwadi mukunyengera kukuru. Rugwaro rwakajeka panyaya iyi, uye VaRoma inozivisa chokwadi chakanyatsotaurwa: Nekuti kutsamwa kwaMwari kunoonekwa kuchibva kudenga kuchipikisa kusada Mwari kwose nokusarurama kwose kwevanhu, vanodzivisa chokwadi mukusarurama.

Makarega here mashoko iwayo achisvika pamwoyo yenyu? Vanhu vanodzivisa chokwadi nemaune mukusada Mwari nomukusarurama, uye kutsamwa kwaYahweh kunopesana navanoita izvozvo. Saka dzidzo dzenyu dzinoitwa dzenhema nemanyepo avo.

Vakaita izvi kuti vagadzirire nzira yekunyengera kukuru kuri kuuya, uye kuti umambo hwaSatani hugone kusunungurwa kuti hutonge mukunyengera kukuru nemanyepo. Manyepo avakakuudzai pakudzidzisa anoita kuti mapoka evanhu ave mapofu pachokwadi: Hakuna vatorwa, asi ngirozi dzakadonha dzakananga kugungwa remoto kuti dzisungwe nekupiswa nokusingaperi. Musatenda manyepo avo uye mhanyirai kwandiri, Kristu Jesu, nekuti mandiri munogara chokwadi chose chichakusununurai. Ndinokuudzai zvinhu izvi kuti murege kunyengerwa pamwechete navachabatana naSatani uye vachagamuchira chiratidzo chechikara chiri kuuya.

Nhoroondo inonzwisiswa nekutevera zvokwadi zvakasimbiswa muRugwaro nemumabhuku asiri eBhaibheri 1 Enoki, Jasher, Jubilees, nezvimwewo, kubvira pakutanga. Nekuti vanhu ava vasingadi Mwari nemanyepo avanoshandisa kuvhara chokwadi chingori chikamu chehondo huru, kunyange hondo yemhodzi pakati paYahweh naSatani inoramba ichienderera mberi kusvika paminiti ino, asi yakakundwa nokusingaperi-peri nemabasa angu eruyananiso. Nekuti hondo yakaziviswa naYahweh kunyoka iyo yekare nekuda kwekupanduka kwayo, kunyange kubvira pakutanga kwekusikwa kwevanhu apo Satani akaedza kuparadza Adhamu naEvha. Yahweh akataura kunyoka mukuzivisa kwake: Ndichaisa ruvengo pakati pako nomukadzi, napakati pembeu yako nembeu yake: iyo ichakupwanya musoro, newe uchapwanya chitsitsinho chayo. Uye saka hondo dzembeu dzakaramba dzichienderera mberi kubvira ipapo.

MuMunda weEdeni, Satani akakwanisa kusvibisa mbeu yaAdhamu, achipa Adhamu mhosva pasi pesimba nehuori hwechivi. (Pachinhu ichi Mwanakomana waMwari wakaonekwa, kuti aparadze mabasa adhiabhorosi.) Nekuti Satani akashandisa ruzivo rwake rwehuori rwezvakanaka nezvakaipa kuti asvibise vanhu zvachose, uye kune avo vanoteerera inzwi rangu, huori ihwohwo hunogadziriswa mandiri, Kristu Jesu, mamuri chisikwa chitsva chisingazombosvibiswi. Mbeu yangu haigoni kusvibiswa, nekuti Yahweh akaiita isingaori, uye nenzira yekuzvarwa kutsva ndakaisa chisimbiso kune vangu vakasarudzwa uye vakatendeka kunyange nemweya iwoyo mutsvene wechivimbiso!

Johane mutsamba yake yekutanga anozivisa chokwadi chisingaperi: Ani naani wakaberekwa naMwari haaiti chivi: nekuti mbeu yake inogara maari, uye haagoni kutadza, nekuti wakaberekwa naMwari.

Munguva pfupi mushure mekudonha kweEdeni, apo Adhamu akatadza ndokudonha kubva pakukwana kuenda kuhuori, varindi vakaburukira paGomo reHemoni mumazuva aJaredi. Uye vose vakazvisunga nemhiko dzokutukana, kunyange mhiko yavo isiri tsvene yavakazvisunga nayo nokusingaperi kuchivi nokurangwa kusingaperi. Vakaita izvozvo kuitira kuti vose vazvitorere vakadzi vavanosarudza, nokudaro vachichinja kurongeka kwechimiro chavo.

Vakauya kuvanhu vachipa ruzivo rwavo rwehuori rwengirozi dzakadonha, nekuti vakabatana nekupanduka kwaSatani.

Vakadzidzisa vanhu kugadzira minondo, nemapanga, nenhoo, nezvidzitiro zvechipfuva, uye vakavazivisa simbi dzepasi nounyanzvi hwokudzishanda, nezvishongo zvemaoko, nezvishongo, nokushandiswa kweantimony, nokunatsa maziso, nemhando dzose dzematombo anokosha, nemhando dzose dzemavara. Vakadzidzisa zvemashiripiti, nokutema midzi, kugadzirisa zvemashiripiti, nyeredzi, mapoka enyeredzi, ruzivo rwemakore, zviratidzo zvepasi, zviratidzo zvezuva, uye kufamba kwemwedzi.

Saka varindi vakadonha vakatora vakadzi vavo ndokuita upombwe navo, uye vakava nemimba ndokuvaberekera hofori huru. Uye nekufamba kwenguva mbeu iyi isiri tsvene yakaparadza zvose zvaiva panyika, nezvose zvakapiwa kuvanhu, uye pakupedzisira yakaparadza vanhu vakawanda sezvayaigona. Nekuti vainwa ropa uye vaidya vanhu, uye vakaita zvakaipa zvikuru pamberi paYahweh, nekuti upenyu hwenyama huri muropa uye vanhu vakasikwa nomufananidzo waYahweh. Vakakura uye vakawanda uye pakupedzisira vakamisa umambo hwavo pachiso chenyika yose. Tarirai! Matongo ezvivakwa zvavo zvikuru achiri akazara panyika sechapupu chinovapomera. Vaitonga nyika panguva idzodzo, uye vakagadzirirwa kuparadzwa kwevanhu. Uye saka marudzi ose ezvinyangadzo akaitwa, nekuti zvinyorwa zvitsvene zvinotozivisa kuti zvakaitwa sei.

Sekunge zvainge zvisina kukwana kuti vanhu vachive nenyama isiri yavo, vachiita upombwe nengirozi dzakadonha, uye vachibereka vana vehofori, nekuda kwevarindi vose vakatadzira shiri, nemhuka, nezvinokambaira, nehove. Kubvira pakutanga kwekusika, Yahweh akaronga chimiro cheupenyu chimwe nechimwe maererano nerudzi rwacho. Asi varindi vakaipa vakakanganisa kusika, kunyange kuyambuka zvisikwa zverumwe rudzi nezvisikwa zverudzi rwakafanana kuti vaunze kusagadzikana kwemajini uye vachinje kusika kwaYahweh kwekutanga kwekukwana kunowirirana. Maitiro aya akadzokororwa, kuyambuka zvisikwa zvakafanana zverumwe rudzi nezvisikwa zvakafanana zverumwe rudzi kusvikira mhedzisiro yaidiwa yawanikwa. Uye saka nyika yaivapo panguva iyoyo, yakasvibiswa nemhando dzose dzezvinyangadzo zvakaipa zvakatadza zvikuru kuna Yahweh, vanhu, nezvisikwa zvose. Nzwisisai, vakadonha vanoda nyika yavo, kunyange nyika yerima yakaparadzana zvachose naYahweh, uye vachagara nhaka chaiyo iyo mugungwa remoto rakavagadzirirwa naYahweh pachake.

Uye saka mazuva aNoa akasvika uye nyika yaivapo panguva iyoyo, yakamira kunze kwemvura uye mumvura, yakafukidzwa nemvura ikaparara. Nekuti matsime enzvimbo dzakadzika akaputswa, zvichikonzera mhirizhonga huru apo nyika yakaputswa nesimba guru remvura ine chisimba, uye mahwindo edenga akazarurwa, zvichibvumira mvura kubva pamusoro pedenga (uye kubva pasi penyika) kufukidza nekuparadza nyika yose.

Uye saka Enoki munyori anotsanangura magumo ehofori idzi saizvozvo, nekuti Yahweh akazivisa saizvozvo: Uye zvino, hofori, dzinobva kumweya nenyama, dzichanzi mweya yakaipa panyika, uye panyika pachava neugaro hwadzo. Mweya yakaipa yakabuda mumiviri yadzo; nekuti dzakaberekwa kubva kuvanhu, kubva kuvarindi vatsvene ndiko kutanga kwadzo uye mavambo adzo; dzichava mweya yakaipa panyika, uye mweya yakaipa dzichanzi. Kana zviri zvemweya yekudenga, kudenga kuchava neugaro hwadzo, asi kana zviri zvemweya yepanyika yakaberekerwa panyika, panyika pachava neugaro hwadzo. Uye mweya yehofori inotambudza, inodzvinyirira, inoparadza, inorwisa, inorwa, uye inoita kuparadza panyika, uye inokonzera dambudziko: hadzidyi, asi zvakadaro dzine nzara nenyota, uye dzinokonzera kutadza. Uye mweya iyi ichamukira vana vevanhu nevakadzi, nekuti yakabva kwavari. Nzwisisai kuti zvikamu zvipfumbamwe kubva mugumi zvemweya iyi yakaipa zviri mugomba rakadzika-dzika nekuda kwechikumbiro nemunyengetero waNoa, uye vanhu vanga vachingobata nechikamu chimwe chete kubva mugumi kubvira ipapo. Izvozvo zvava kuda kuchinja apo gomba rakadzika-dzika richazarurwa uye zvose zvichasunungurwa.

Saka nyika, nezvinyangadzo zvose zvevarindi, zvakaparadzwa. Varindi pachavo vakasungwa mukutongwa kwavo kwakasiyana-siyana kwezvizvarwa makumi manomwe, kuti vasunungurwe kuzotongwa panguva yekutongwa kukuru. (Nekuti kutongwa kukuru kwava kuda kutanga.) Zvisinei, Noa akawana nyasha pamberi paYahweh nekuti aiva murume akarurama, uye mhuri yake Yahweh akaponesa mweya misere yose, pamwechete nezvimwe zvisikwa zvakawanda muAreka. Noa akazvichengeta iye nemhuri yake kubva mukukanganiswa kwemajini mafashamo asati auya. Asi mwanakomana wake Hamu nevana vake vakaipa vaizopedzisira varamba Noa uye kunyange Yahweh pachake (uyo ainge avasika nekuvaponesa), vachizvibatanidza nekupanduka kwaSatani.

Mashoko aGenesi 6 akazadziswa nezvivi zvevana vaNoa, kunyange dzinza raHamu. Paiva nehofori panyika mumazuva iwayo: uye shure kwaizvozvo. "Mazuva iwayo" aiva mazuva kubva kuna Jaredi kusvika kuna Noa, uye "hofori panyika" vaiva vana vevarindi nevanasikana vevanhu mafashamo asati auya. "Shure kwaizvozvo" ndiko shure kwemazuva aNoa, apo majini evanhu akasvibiswa zvakare neruzivo rwengirozi dzakadonha. Ndinokukumbirai kuti murege kutenda kunyengera kwedzidziso isiriyo yechiSethite uye mutende mashoko ezvinyorwa zvitsvene, nekuti mashoko aya ndeechokwadi.

Apo nyika yakakamurwa nemijenya pakati pevanakomana vaNoa (Shemu, Hamu, naJafeti), mujenya waHamu waiva wekuti agare nhaka yenyika dzekumaodzanyemba, uye saka dzaiva dzake. Zvisinei, Kanani akachiva chikamu chenhaka yaShemu ndokuenda kunyika dzakanga dzisiri dzake kuti agare. Nekuti aiva Kanani uyo Noa akatuka. Nekuti Kanani akasiya nhaka yake ndokuvamba guta reTire pane yakazova mhenderekedzo yeFenikiya ndokuenda kuSidhoni, kwakabva Jezebheri. Akaita izvi kuitira kuti ave pedyo neGomo reHemoni, kwakaburukira varindi uye kwandakashandurwa chimiro.

Kanani akawana zvakasiyiwa nevarindi mafashamo aNoa asati auya, nekuti vakatsvaga kuchengetedza kupanduka kwavo kunyange mafashamo apera, nekuti vakaveza zvakawanda pamatombo nemabwe. Kanani akashandisa ruzivo rwavo rwengirozi dzakadonha muhuroyi hukuru, uye tsika dzakawanda dzakaipa dzakaitwa nevaKanani, nekuti vakatozvibayira vana vavo. Vakanga vakaipa zvikuru uye vakapararira zvekuti akakwanisa kutapurira nyika yeKanani nezvinyangadzo zvake zvitsva, uko Satani akashanda nekuda kwekudarika kwake.

Uye saka madzinza ehofori dzemashure memafashamo, kunyange madzinza anonyangadza evaKanani, akamuka kubva mukudarika kwaKanani nezvakaipa zvake nehuroyi. Kunyika iyo Kanani akaba kuna Shemu kwakauya vaHiti, nevaHivhi, nevaPerizi, nevaGigashi, nevaAmori, nevaJebhusi. Ndivo vaiva vanotyisa, kunyange vaRefaimu. Paiva nemamwe madzinza akawanda kupfuura akanyorwa, asi ava vanotyisa vakanga vakamirira munyika yechipikirwa mukuedza kuuraya avo vaidzivirira dzinza raKristu, kunyange madzitateguru enyu vaIsraeri nemadzibaba enyu vaiva vaHebheru.

Joshua akapinda munyika yechipikirwa kuti atore zvakapiwa Shemu nemujenya uye nyika yaiva yaIsraeri nechivimbiso chaYahweh pachake. Asi vana vaIsraeri havana kunyatsoteerera mirairo yaYahweh yekusasiya masora ari mapenyu, nekuti aiva akaipa uye asiri vanhu, uye maitiro avo aiva akaipa, uye vakaita zvakaipa zvikuru pamberi paYahweh, nekuti ndizvo zvaiva chimiro chavo. Uye saka Yahweh akaudza Israeri sezvakanyorwa murugwaro: Asi kana musingadzingi vagari venyika pamberi penyu, zvino zvichaitika kuti avo vamunorega vasara vavo, vachava minzwa mumaziso enyu, neminzwa mumativi enyu, uye vachakutambudzai munyika mamunogara. Uye zvakanga zvakadaro.

Nhoroondo muchikamu inoratidza kupararira nekufamba kwezvinyangadzo izvi, kunyange masora akadyarwa naSatani pachake, sezvo vave vachigara pakati pegorosi kubvira pamazuva ekare. Vakamisa budiriro dzakawanda vachishandisa matekinoroji avo neruzivo rwengirozi dzakadonha; inyanzvi dzekuvaka, in'anga, vazhinji vavo vane masimba asiri echisikigo, uye vakabatana nababa vavo Dhiabhorosi, kunyange vachigovana hunhu hwake nekuchiva kwake. Masora aya akapinda muzvikamu zvose zvenzanga, uye haasi ose ari hofori. Vazhinji vanoita sevanhu, kunze kwekuti zvisikwa zvakasanganiswa zvakachinjwa zvachose kubva pazvirongwa zvaYahweh mukusika uye hazvizodzikinurwi. Musavatya, nekuti mukuru ndiye ari mamuri pane ari munyika.

Masora aya anotsvaga kutora izvo zviri zvenhaka nechivimbiso kuvana vaMwari. Hapagoni kuva nekuyanana navo, nekuti ndeababa vavo Dhiabhorosi, uye kuchiva kwababa vavo vachaita. Aiva mhondi kubvira pakutanga, uye paanotaura nhema anotaura zvake, nekuti murevi wenhema uye baba vadzo. Vachanyatsopidigura, vachasvibisa, vachaparadza, vachauraya, vachapamba, vachatora, uye vachaita zvakaipisisa zvavo kune chero chinhu chaMwari chavanoruchiva. Havana rugare, uye chimiro chavo chakashata zvachose. Vakapidigura nyika, uye vachamisa umambo hwavo kweawa imwe chete apo shato ichatonga panyika. Asi vanotsvaga kudzorera zera ravo regoridhe, asi hapana zera regoridhe ravachava naro kunze kwekutongwa kwakaomarara, uye havazopukunyuki.

Masora aya akaipa akasvibisa ruzivo, kudzidza, nhoroondo, chitendero, sainzi, uye vakaramba vachisvibisa. Vakaparadzira tekinoroji yengirozi dzakadonha uye hapana chinhu chitsva pasi pezuva. Sezvo nguva dzazadziswa, vakafukidza nyika yose nemanyepo, uye nyika yose iri mukutadza. Nekujekerwa kwavo, masora echiJesuit akasvibisa nzanga zvachose uye akatungamirira vanhu mugungwa remanyepo, uko kunzira yekubuda ndeyekutendeuka nekuzviramba kubva kuzvivi zvose nemanyepo. (Nekuti Roma yakagadzirawo Islam; dzidzai kuti muzive.) Ruzivo rwavo rwengirozi dzakadonha nemanyepo zvinopesana nenhoroondo yemagwaro uye panzvimbo pazvo zvinopa ngano dzisina simba dzinopofumadza pfungwa nemwoyo yevanhu. Naizvozvo, budai pakati pavo muzvitsaure.

Nyika yanhasi ndeyekuti vanhu vazhinji, vazhinji vane amnesia yeboka nekuda kwekudzidziswa kwechiJesuit. Vazhinji venyu makanganwa kwete chete zvisungo nenzira dzaYahweh, asi makanganwa zvachose kuti muri vanaani uye kuti madzitateguru enyu ndivanaani chaizvo. Izvi zvakaitwa nemaune kuburikidza nehondo, manyepo, uye kuedza kwekugarisa vanhu patsva kwemakore kwakaparadzira vakawanda kumativi mana enyika. Pamusoro peizvi vanozvidza nemaune kuti hazvigoneke kuvanza chero chinhu pamberi paYahweh; uye ini, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndinoziva vangu uye vandinodaidza kuruponeso.

Vamwe veavo vakaparadzirwa vaiva nherera uye vakatorwa kubva kumhuri dzavo kungave kuburikidza nehondo kana kupambwa chaiko. Mweya iyi yakakurira munhoroondo yenhema, kunyange kunyeperwa nepfungwa dzerudo dzenhoroondo yenhema, uye saka kunyengera kwakadzika midzi mumwoyo yevanhu, uye vazhinji vanopofumadzwa zvachose nemanyepo anogamuchirwa sechokwadi. Asi kune avo vanotendeukira kwandiri, Yeshua HaMashiach, muchaziva chokwadi uye chokwadi chichakusununurai. Nzanga yenyu yazvino yakavakirwa pamatongo nemadota ebudiriro dzekare dzevakadonha, uye kuedza kukuru kwakaitwa mukuviga chokwadi. Izvo zvakanga zviripo zvichavapozve, uye hapana chinhu chitsva pasi pezuva.

Pasina chibayiro changu cheruyananiso uye chipo changu cheruponeso, imi mose mungadai makarasika. Saka uyai kwandiri, Yeshua HaMashiach, uye ndichakutungamirirai muchokwadi chose uye ndichakudzidzisai nhoroondo yenyu yechokwadi. Ndichakubatanidzai nemadzitateguru enyu echokwadi, nekuti vazhinji venyu muri vemunyika, kunyange ruponeso rwuri rwemarudzi ose. Ndichakudzidzisai munzira dzeutsvene, uye muchararama zvakarurama nekutenda mandiri, Jesu Kristu, uye muchararama nokusingaperi. Hapana chakavanzika chisingazobudi pachena, uye zvose zvichafumurwa zvizere, kuti chokwadi chizivikanwe navose.

Saka regai kudya manyepo sezvamunozviviga mukugutsikana, nekuti nguva dzekugutsikana dzapera. Nguva dzekuratidzwa dzava pedyo. Nekuti tarirai! Sezvazvakanga zvakaita mumazuva aNoa, ndizvo zvazvichaita. Satani akashandisa jira rake remanyepo kugadzirira kunyengera kwake kukuru kwenguva yekupedzisira, uye nyika zhinji ichatevera manyepo ake. Nekuti umambo hwose hwadhiabhorosi huchasunungurwa kubva mugomba rakadzika-dzika, uye hofori dzichagarazve pakati pevanhu, nekuti dziri kuuya kuzozadzisa kutsamwa kwaYahweh.

Satani anotaura manyepo makuru, uye ava vakadonha vachati vanobva kune dzimwe nyika uye kuti vakapfuura vanhu nezviuru zvakawanda zvemakore. Vachauya vachipa ruzivo runoita serunobatsira, uye vachazvifukidza nerunako rwenhema. Asi ava ndeveumambo hwaSatani, saka musanyengerwa. Musatevera nzira dzenyika, nekuti nyika ichashamisika neumambo hwaSatani, kunyange ichiti, ndiani akafanana nechikara?

Ndichakuvigai, ndichakudzivirirai, ndichakusimbisai, ndichakutungamirirai nhanho nenhanho, uye ndichaparadza zvachose chifukidzo chemanyepo chakafukidza nyika mukunyengera. Ini, kunyange Yeshua HaMashiach, ndichakupa simba uye ndichaita kuti muite zvishamiso zvakawanda munguva ino yekutambudzika kwaJakobho. Uye saka ndinokuudzai zvinhu izvi, kuti chokwadi chikuitirei vakasununguka, uye kuti muve vakasununguka zvechokwadi uye musanyengerwa nekunyengera kwavo kukuru. Nekuti nheyo dzayo manyepo, kunyengera, uye nhema dzinoparadzirwa nembeu yenyoka.

Ndini nzira, nechokwadi, noupenyu; hapana munhu anouya kuna baba asi nokwandiri.

Ndini iye wakakudanirai kukubwinya nokunaka.

Ndini iye achatonga nokusingaperi saIshe wemadzishe naMambo wemadzimambo.

Ndini Shumba yeRudzi rwaJudha.

Ndini iye anorayira mauto ekudenga.

Ndini iye achaonekwa mumakore kuzounganidza Kereke yangu.

Ndini iye ane kiyi dzerufu negehena, uye ndichatonga mukururama.

Ndini iye pamberi pake mabvi ose achapfugama uye rurimi rwose ruchapupura.

Ndini iye uyo Mukuru NDINI akatuma, saka inzwai mashoko angu.

Tarirai! Ndinokuudzai zvinhu izvi kwete kuti ndikupei chidzidzo chenhoroondo chakakwana asi kuti ndikutungamirirei nhanho imwe mberi kuchokwadi. Nenguva, zvose zvichabuda pachena uye umambo hweshato huchafumurwa, nekuti ini, Yeshua HaMashiach, ndakazvizivisa, uye ndini iye uyo Yahweh akatuma.
