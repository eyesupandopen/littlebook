Inzwai Mashoko Angu, Ndizvo Zvinotaura Ishe!

Inzwai mashoko angu, ndizvo zvinotaura Ishe! Zuva raIshe rava kuda kukuburukirai somoto unoparadza chose, uye nomoto zvivi zvevazhinji zvichanatsva, avo vachadana zita rangu uye vasingadi upenyu hwavo kusvikira kumagumo. Makwai angu anonzwa inzwi rangu, uye ndichaagadzirira nzira, kunyange achasangana nematambudziko mazhinji. Avo vanobatisisa zita rangu rine simba vachaponeswa, kunyange zvakadaro asi nomoto. Muvengi ari pamusoro penyu, imi vanozvikudza vakaramba rugare rwandakakupai. Zvivi zvenyu zvasvika pamberi pangu uye kutonga kukuru kuri pamusoro penyu. Kunyange moto wangu usingazoparadzi vaduku vangu, uye nguva dzichange dzakaoma zvikuru kupfuura dzose, vatsvene vazhinji vachapukunyuka rufu rwechipiri nokutendeukira kuzita rangu munguva yavo yokutambudzika uye nokumira vakasimba kusvikira kumagumo chaiwo. Ini, Ishe Jesu Kristu, ndazvitaura uye ndatumira yambiro kune vane nzeve dzokunzwa nameso okuona.

Imi marudzi, imi marudzi anonyangadza anotengesa zvivi zvikuru. Hongu, mava vatengesi vezvakaipa vanokudza wakaipa nemashoko enyu ose akaipa nenzira dzenyu dzakaipa dzamakadzidza kusvikira pakuparadzwa kwenyu. Mava vatengesi venyama nevanhu, nokuti kunyange vana vava nhumbi dzenyu. Hasha dzeGwayana, hongu, hasha dzangu dzichaparadza nyika yenyu, uye moto wangu uchanatsa nyika pamabasa evanoita zvakaipa. Ushe hwangu husati hwasvika, kucheneswa kukuru kuchabata nyika, uye nyika ichasvibiswa neropa revanoita zvakaipa. Asi, ndichavaka ushe hwangu panyika, ushe hwokururama norugare musingazopindi chinhu chakaipa, uye ndichatonga marudzi netsvimbo youtare.

Zviripo iye zvino zvichaparadzwa uye hazvizovakwizve, uye ndichawisira pasi zvifananidzo zvenyu chose, nenzvimbo dzakakwirira dzepfungwa dzenyu dzichadzikiswa. Nokuti makati mumwoyo menyu, "Handisi chirikadzi!" Asi kuparadzwa kwenyu kuchauya pamusoro penyu muawa imwe, uye kuchaparadza chose nokushandura nyika sezvamaiziva. Munonamatirei rufu rwenyu, imi vanonamata zvifananidzo vepanyika? Munotsaurei nokutsvenesa kurangwa kwenyu kwamakaunganidza pamusoro pemweya yenyu? Hamuna kuona here? Hamuna kunzwa here? Hamuna kuverenga here? Kwete, muri mapofu, uye muri matsi, uye hamugoni kunzwisisa mashoko angu nokuti makaramba mweya wangu mutsvene wandaizotumira pamusoro penyu ndikakunatsai chose pamberi pangu dai maingogamuchira chipo changu.

Ndicharipira, ndizvo zvinotaura Ishe. Ndicharipira munhu wose maererano namabasa ake, uye ndichapa mugove wakapetwa kaviri wokutonga pandichadzosera mabasa enyu kuti aparadze mweya yenyu, uye hamuzopori. Kutonga kwava kuda kubuda panyika, uye vazhinji venyu muchiri kufuratira kushevedza kwangu uye hamuteereri mashoko angu aizokupukunyutsai zvirokwazvo padambudziko renyu. Awa yokutongwa kwenyu yava kusvika, uye vazhinji vachatora chiratidzo, vachifunga kuti vanogona kuponesa mitsipa yavo uye vagoramba vachirarama muzvivi zvavanoda kwekanguva kadiki. Mwanakomana wokuparadzwa achavimbisa zvinhu zvizhinji uye achanyengera nyika yose pavanenge vachishamisika nechikara, vachiti, "Ndianiko akafanana nechikara?"

Asi, wakaipa anouya, nezviratidzo zvake zvenhema nezvishamiso, achakutengesai, uye muchaparara chose. Muchafira muzvivi zvenyu uye muchamukira kukutongwa kwechigaro chikuru chichena kwamuchazvidavirira chivi chose, pfungwa dzose dzakaipa, hongu, shoko rose risina maturo mucharipa mhosva yaro. Uye mumamvemve enyu etsvina muchawanikwa musingakwani, uye hamuzomiri makarurama, nokuti hakuna nyama yakarurama pamberi pake, asi vose vakatsauka vakanamata kuora kwavo. Iwe munhu, ani naani anondiramba uye anoramba Musiki wake achaparara chose pasina mushonga. Ino ndiyo nguva yoruponeso. Muchatizira kunaani kana matorwa muutapwa mukakandwa muri vapenyu mugungwa romoto? Mutsago wenyu uchava sarufa inopfuta, uye magumbeze enyu achava mirazvo isingaperi yakagadzirirwa naBaba vangu Dhiyabhorosi nengirozi dzake. Asi, nokundiramba, makazvisarudzira mugumo wenyu mukabatana newakaipa, uye muchagoverana mukutongwa kwake.

Ini, Ishe Jesu Kristu, ndakaitwa nyama ndikagara pakati penyu, zvirokwazvo, ndichaparadza chose ushe hwomuvengi, uye nyika ichava nzvimbo yokupedzisira yehondo. Tizai muchikwanisa nokuti nguva yava kuda kupera! Kwasara tumwe tushoma twetsanga kuti tuwire mugirazi reawa, uye hwamanda icharira. Kereke yangu painenge yabviswa uye mwenga wangu aendeswa kumakamuri ake, Satani nehondo yake vachakandirwa panyika, uye acharwisa chose vagari vepanyika. Gomba risina magumo richazarurwa, uye zvisikwa zvizhinji zvetsvina zvichakwira zvichitsvaga chero chipi zvacho chavanogona kuparadza. Iwe munhu benzi, haunzwisisi here kuti munyasha dzangu netsitsi dzangu ndakadzora kuzara kwoushe hwomuvengi kuti husauye pamusoro pako?

Asi, ndichakupa izvo zvaunoshuvira mukuchiva kwako kwezvivi, uye ndichasunungura ushe hwake hwose pamusoro pako, uye uchaiswa chose mumaoko evavengi vako. Chinangwa chake ndechokuparadza vanhu vose kusvikira kumweya wokupedzisira, nokuti anoda nyika isina Mwari yake oga, yaasingazombovi nayo. Dai ndisina kupfupisa mazuva, aizobudirira, uye hapana nyama yaizosara iri mhenyu! Asi, iwe uchiri kuda zvivi zvako kupfuura ruponeso, rudo, nechiedza zvandinazvo nokuda kwako dai waingosarudza upenyu ukareurura zita rangu saIshe! Hongu, ndiite ini, Jesu Kristu, Ishe wako, sezvo ndiri mupenyu nokusingaperi-peri sechiedza kune avo vava kuda kuparara. Avo vanodana zita rangu vachaponeswa.

Vana vangu vaduku, nguva yava kuda kupera. Ndinoziva kuti vazhinji venyu maneta uye munotambudzwa kusvika pamwoyo yemweya yenyu nezvakaipa zvakamuka zvakakupoteredzai. Aya ndiwo maminitsi mashoma okupedzisira emarwadzo okusununguka, uye vasanangurwa vangu vachaendeswa kumatenga, kwandakavagadzirira nzvimbo yokuchengeteka kwavanogona kutizira. Mubairo wangu uri muruoko rwangu kune vakatendeka uye kunyange vasina kutendeka, avo vacharasikirwa asi vachaponeswa vari vapenyu nokusingaperi nokuti vakandiita Ishe wavo. Naizvozvo, ibvai pazvivi zvenyu mutarire kwandiri, kuti ndipenyese chiedza changu mumwoyo menyu uye mugozvinatsa neropa rangu rinoyananisa. Musatya nguva uye musatya basa rakakusarirai, nokuti nguva yava pedyo.

Zviitiko pazvinenge zvichiitika pamberi pemeso enyu, zivai kuti zviitiko izvi zvimwe chetezvo zvichakurumidza uye zvinhu zvichaitika nokukurumidza zvikuru, nokuti kutonga hakukotsiri. Nguva dzenyasha nenguva dzokuzorodzwa kweKereke yangu dzava kusvika kumagumo. Zvirokwazvo, handina kunonoka pamusoro pezvipikirwa asi ndakaratidza mwoyo murefu nokushivirira kukuru kuti mweya wose wokupedzisira wandakasarudza uuye kwandiri. Kutonga kuri pamusoro pako, iwe nyika, uye vakatendeka chete muKereke yangu ndivo vachapukunyuka. Munoregereiko vanyengeri nevaprofita venhema vachikunetsai nemashoko avo anokanganisa avanotaura vachipikisa kureruka kunowanikwa mandiri? Mungavaregereiko vachikukwezvai kubva pamuchato wangu nomwenga wangu ini ndaizokugadzirirai chigaro patafura dai maingobuda muzvivi zvenyu mukatenda mashoko chaiwo ari muShoko rangu Dzvene?
