Chipupuriro Chiviri: Kereke
Mumabasa aPauro Muapostora
Chivabvu 04, 2024

Mune nhamo imi vafudzi vakarasha kutenda!

Mune nhamo imi vafudzi vakarasha kutenda, nokuti makadyara kereke dzenyu dzenhema munyika dzose, uye hamutauri muchindimiririra, uyewo handina kumupa basa mumabasa amakazvitorera. Uye makaita mari Mwari wenyu, nokuti munovimba nepfuma yetsvina, uye makadzidzisa vanhu vangu kuti kuwana pfuma youpenyu huno ndihwo unamati. Mune nhamo imi vafudzi vakarasha kutenda, nokuti mashoko aJeremia anokurondedzerai chaizvo: Mbizi yemusango yajaira renje, inofembedza mhepo pamadiro ayo; panguva yayo ndiani angairambidza? Nokuti upombwe hwenyu noufeve huri pamwe nezvakaipa, nokuti makasarudza vanaSatani kuti vave mwari wenyu, uye machinda iwayo asina maturo haasi vamwari. Muri vashumiri venhema vanozviita sevashumiri vokururama, uye hazvishamisi, nokuti Satani anozvishandura kuva mutumwa wechiedza.

Mune nhamo imi vafudzi vakarasha kutenda, nokuti makadyara kereke dzakaita kuti mvura yangu inotapira youpenyu ive segavakava rinovava, ramunopa vaya vandakadana, mvura inodziya ine tsvina inovava mudumbu. Kunyange mvura yenyu inovava inopiwa mumidziyo yakaputsika, uye hamuna simba, kunyange muchizvirumbidza nesimba guru. Makasvibisa nyika nedzidziso dzenhema uye makavaka kereke dzenyu dzenhema semajeri omudzimu akasvibiswa nehurumende yenyu, nokuti makatengesa boka rangu muruoko rwomuvengi; naizvozvo, budai maari zvino, vanhu vangu! Kereke dzenyu dzisina maturo dzakarasha kutenda dzinoshandiswa senzvimbo dzokudzora kwamuchaunza rufu nokuparadzwa kune avo vakasara, uye muchazviripira.

Makaita Remfani Mwari wenyu. Munoshumira Bhaari. Munobayira vana kuna Moreki. Makasvibiswa zvachose. Muri matsime asina mvura, midziyo isina chinhu yakagadzirirwa kuparadzwa, nokuti nhaka yenyu isarufa rinopfuta, uye muchava mugungwa guru iroro nababa venyu, Dhiabhorosi. Muri vaapostora venhema, vaporofita venhema, vaevhangeri venhema, vafudzi venhema, uye vadzidzisi venhema; uye avo vamunotendeutsira kunzira dzenyu dzinoparadza vanoitwa vana vegehena zvakapetwa kaviri kupfuura imi, nokuti muri vekusvibiswa kunosvibisa zvachose. Nokuti Stefani akafanotaura nezvenzira dzenyu paakataura nezvemadzitateguru enyu, kunyange masora akatambudza Israeri, nokuti akati: Hongu, makatakura tebhenakeri yaMoreki, nenyeredzi yamwari wenyu Remfani, zvifananidzo zvamakaita, kuti muzvinamate. Imi vafudzi vakarasha kutenda munotofuridzira zvisina nyadzi nyeredzi yaRemfani yamunobatanidza nokusaziva naDhavhidhi, tateguru wangu; nokuti ini, Jesu Kristu, ndakabva kwaari, uye ndichatonga kubva pachigaro choushe chaDhavhidhi mukururama. Nokuti ndini iye akapiwa kutonga kwakadaro, nokuti ndichatonga nyika mukururama!

Mune nhamo imi vafudzi vakarasha kutenda, ndichakuripirai kusvika pakupedzisira nokuda kwokutaura kwenyu kwose kwakaipa, uye nzira dzenyu dzose dzakaipa dzichadzoserwa pamisoro yenyu nokusingaperi-peri. Nokuti muchabatana naDhiabhorosi navatumwa vake munhaka yavo inobva kuna Mwari Wokumusoro-soro; kunyange Yahweh akakugadzirirai gungwa guru romoto usingadzimwi. Makaramba ruponeso kune avo vaitsvaka, asi zivai kuti ndichavaponesa uye imi mucharipira. Makaramba mudzimu wangu kune avo vaizoreurura zita rangu nokuvapa mashoko enhema okutaura neminyengetero isina musoro, isina maturo uye inodzokororwa isingaponesi. Ndinzwei, vanhu vangu: ruponeso runouya nokuzivisa Jesu saIshe wenyu uye nokutenda kuti Mwari akandimutsa kubva kuvakafa, uye muukama hunoponesa neni chete ndimo mamunogona kuponeswa; nokuti ndinonyima chisimbiso chaMwari kune avo vanoreurura zita rangu nomwoyo usina kutenda, nokuti ndinonzvera mwoyo uye ndinoedza itsvo.

Munofunga nokuzvikudza kuti nokuti munondidana kuti Ishe pamberi peungano yenyu yamakapamba kuti ndinokuzivai here? Ndichati kwamuri pazuva iroro, ibvai kwandiri, imi vaiti vezvakaipa, nokuti handina kumbokuzivai! Munodzidzisa nhema muchiti ichokwadi, uye munotsausa avo vanonditsvaka, Ishe Jesu Kristu, muchivapinza muvhangeri rakasvibiswa, roupenzi, nokuti hakuna rimwe vhangeri: Nokuti mashoko akanaka ari mandiri, Kristu Jesu! Kutaura kwenyu kusina maturo kwava kuda kupera, uye hasha dzangu dzinopisa pamusoro penyu, uye hamungapunyuki. Ndinoona chiito chose chokumhura chamunoita; ndinonzwa shoko rose rokumhura ramunotaura muzita rangu, nokuti muri munyengeri. Munonyepedzera kuti munotaura mashoko angu, sezvamunonyepedzera kuti ndakakutumai nenyaya dzenyu dzokunyepedzera nezvishamiso zvenyu zvokunyepedzera nokuporesa kwenyu kwakarongwa, nokuti muri munyengeri!

Mune nhamo imi vafudzi vakarasha kutenda, nokuti munobira avo vaizouya kwandiri, muchivaudza nhema kuti vanosungirwa naMwari kukupai mari. Munokanganisa zvisina ruzivo Rugwaro rwangu Rutsvene, muchiudza avo mamakatsausa kuti vanofanira kukupai chegumi. Nokuti chii chandakaudza Pauro paakanyorera avo vaiva kuKorinde: Asi ndinoreva izvi, Uyo anodyara zvishoma, achakohwawo zvishoma; uye uyo anodyara zvakawanda, achakohwawo zvakawanda. Munhu mumwe nomumwe sezvaanofunga mumwoyo make, ngaape saizvozvo; kwete nokunyunyuta, kana nokumanikidzwa: nokuti Mwari anoda mupi anofara. Asi kwete, munodzidzisa nhema muchiti ichokwadi uye munobira vanhu vangu, kunyange kutumira mari yamunounganidza nokuda kwezvinangwa zvaSatani, uye mucharipira, nokuti hasha dzangu dzinopisa zvikuru pamusoro penyu nokuti munotungamirira vanhu vanonditsvaka, Jesu Kristu, kubva paruponeso kuenda kukuparadzwa. Asi zivai izvi, vafudzi vakarasha kutenda, hamungatongobvuti kunyange mumwe weava mumaoko angu, nokuti pavose vaakandipa Baba, ndakangorasa mumwe chete, mwanakomana wokuparadzwa, kunyange Judhasi, nokuti akasarudza kuparadzwa kwake pachake panzvimbo youpenyu mandiri! Mabasa enyu nokushanda kwenyu hazvina maturo, imi vafudzi vakarasha kutenda; makazvichengetera kutongwa kukuru nehasha. Nokuti ndakataura nezvenzira dzemadzitateguru enyu, uye imi ndimi vaya vanodya dzimba dzechirikadzi uye nokunyepedzera munoita minyengetero mirefu: MUCHAGAMUCHIRA kutongwa kukuru!

Mune nhamo imi vafudzi vakarasha kutenda, nokuti makatungamirira vanhu vangu kutsigira hurumende dzenyika dzisiri dzangu, kunyange nyika iri yangu. Haiwa, imi vafudzi vakarasha kutenda, munonongedzera vanhu vangu kuvatungamiriri venyika vari vaDhiabhorosi, muchivaudza kuti vakatumwa naMwari. Munotsausa vanhu mukufunga kuti handizovakomboreri kunze kwekunge vakomborera avo vanouraya rudzi rwevanhu vasina mhosva. Asi chii chandakataura murugwaro pamusoro penyu, nokuti izvi hazvisi zvitsva, uye vazhinji vakakutangirai mukunyengera, kunyange imi muri mapenzi zvokufunga kuti muri vekutanga. Ndinzwei, vana vangu, nokuti ndiri kufumura uipi uhwu nokuda kwenyu. Nokuti rugwaro rwangu runoti: Imi muri vaMwari, vana vaduku, uye makavakunda: nokuti mukuru ndiye ari mamuri, kupfuura iye ari munyika. Ivo ndevenyika: naizvozvo vanotaura zvenyika, uye nyika inovanzwa. Mharidzo dzenyu dzenyika, miitiro yenyu yenyika, kurangana kwenyu kwenyika naSatani, zvose zvichasvika kumagumo kamwe kamwe, uye hamuchazomukizve. Asi imi, Mwenga wangu, muri vangu, uye mashoko aya haasi enyu, nokuti muchapinda mumufaro waIshe wenyu, kunyange ini, Kristu Jesu.

Ndiri kutaura zvino kuungano dzekereke dzakarasha kutenda dzinotungamirirwa nevafudzi vakarasha kutenda, kunyange imi, ungano yangu yeRaodhikiya. Ndinoziva mabasa ako, kuti hausi unotonhora kana kupisa; dai waiva unotonhora kana kupisa. Nokuti Pauro akanyorera Timotio pamusoro pako nevamwe vakafanana newe: Nokuti nguva ichasvika yavasingazotsungiriri dzidziso yakarurama, asi vachatevera kuchiva kwavo vachazviunganidzira vadzidzisi, vane nzeve dzinovava: Uye vachafuratidza nzeve dzavo pachokwadi, uye vachatsaukira kungano. Vazhinji venyu munopinda kereke idzi dzakarasha kutenda muchimanikidzwa nevanhu uye nokunyepedzera kuedza kuti muri kunditsvaka, Kristu Jesu, izvo zvamuri kusaita zvirokwazvo.

Munoshuva kuvaraidzwa, nokuti kuvaraidzwa ndiko kushuva kwenyu, uye kuvaraidzwa kwava chidhori chenyu! Uye hamuzondinzwi, Jesu Kristu, uyewo hamuzoteereri kune chero vatumwa vangu kunze kwekunge nzeve dzenyu dzakwenyererwa, asi mashoko angu haakwenyereri nzeve! Imi ungano dzakarasha kutenda: Ndinzwei uye ndinzwei zvakanaka, nokuti ndakati mubhuku guru raZvakazarurwa: Uye ndakanzwa rimwe izwi richibva kudenga, richiti, Budai maari, vanhu vangu, kuti murege kuva vagovani vezvivi zvake, uye kuti murege kugamuchira matambudziko ake: Nokuti zvivi zvake zvasvika kudenga, uye Mwari warangarira kusarurama kwake. Budai maari, kana kuti muchagovana naye matambudziko ake nokutongwa, nokuti nguva yasvika. Kune vaRaodhikiya vangu: Siyai kereke dzenyu dzakarasha kutenda zvino, nokuti nguva yava pedyo, uye ndiri kukuyambirai, kunyange vazhinji venyu vaine usimbe zvokuti havagoni kuita maererano nemashoko angu.

Musazeza payambiro yangu, nokuti chii chandakapa Johani kuti anyore mutsamba yake yechipiri? Ani naani anodarika uye asingagari mudzidziso yaKristu, haana Mwari: uyo anogara mudzidziso yaKristu, ane zvose Baba noMwanakomana. Kana paine anouya kwamuri, uye asingauyi nedzidziso iyi, musamugamuchira mumba menyu, uyewo musamukwazisa. Nokuti uyo anomukwazisa, mugovani wemabasa ake akaipa. Asi imi vaRaodhikiya hamuteereri zvokuti munofuratira mashoko aya uye panzvimbo pokuvashuvira rombo rakanaka, izvo zvakatoipa zvakakwana, munoenda mberi muuipi uye munovapinza mudzimba dzenyu uye munovapa mukana kuvana venyu, uye mune mhosva zvachose pamberi pangu, saka TENDEUKAI!

Nokuti zvirokwazvo, makazarurira nyika nenzira dzayo masuo enyu; makapinza mapere, kunyange vepasi-pasi, kuti vadyise vana vangu, uye kunze kwekunge matendeuka, mucharipira. Tendeukai kana kuti ndichakurwisai, nokuti muri vagovani vemabasa avo akaipa, uye hamusi kuteerera kwandiri, Kristu Jesu, kunyange iye achakutorerai mubairo wenyu uye achaupa mumwe kana mukaramba muchindifuratira. Nokuti nyasha dzangu netsitsi zvava kuda kupera, uye hasha dzichagara panzvimbo pazvo, kunyange nyasha netsitsi zviri neni nguva dzose, uye ani naani anodana zita raIshe kusvika kumagumo achaponeswa. Nokuti hamugoni kuva noukama nenzira dzisina zvibereko dzerima, asi munofanira kudzitsiura.

Asi hamuna ruzivo zvokufunga kuti nokuti mumwe munhu anonyepa uye anomhura achishandisa zita rangu dzvene pamabasa ake asiri matsvene kuti akatumwa neni uye anotaura mashoko angu. Inzwai mashoko angu, nokuti nezvibereko zvavo muchavaziva! Dzokerai kuShoko rangu rakachena raMwari uye mudzidze nezvangu, nokuti hamundizivi uye izwi rangu rigen'a kwamuri, uye hamuna Baba nokuti hamuna Mwanakomana. Tendeukirai kwandiri mukutendeuka, uye ndichakugamuchirai. Garai mumasaga nemadota mumwoyo yenyu uye muzvininipise kuti ndigokukudzai! Ndakakudanai kuti mukunde, uye zvino ndiwo mukana wenyu wokupedzisira wokukunda vafudzi ava vakarasha kutenda nenzira dzavo dzakaipa; nokuti ndiri kuuya, uye ndinounza hasha huru paBhabhironi Rakavanzika.

Nguva chinhu cheumbozha chamusina. Hamugoni kuva neusimbe zvakare, kana kuti muchazviwana muri mudambudziko raJakobho, pandingadai ndakakugadzirirai nzvimbo patafura yangu uye ndikakuroorai semwenga wangu, asi hamuna kutendeka muri mumaoko omumwe, kunyange Satani! Saka uyai kwandiri kuti ndigokudzoseraimubairo wenyu. Hazvina kukwana kungobuda nokusemeswa; munofanira kutendeuka kubva pauipi uhwu uye muchinje nzira dzenyu uye mureurure zvivi izvi kwandiri, uye ndichakunatsai muropa rangu rinoyananisa uye ndichapukuta mavara kubva kwamuri mukuchena kwangu kwakakwana, nokuti ndakachena uye nguo dzangu chena sechando chitsva. Dzokerai kwandiri uye nokusingaperi muchava neni; budai uye muye kumaoko angu akatambanudzwa, nokuti ndiri pano kuti ndikuudzei kuti hamuna nguva yokutambisa.

Kumwenga wangu akatendeka, akateerera izwi rangu uye akatevera nzira dzangu: Kwamuri ndinoti, ramba muri mukutendeka kwenyu kwandiri, uye kana paine mumwe wenyu ane rutsoka mumukova wekereke yeRaodhikiya, ndinokurayirai kuti murubvise pamukova uye regai mukova uvhare! Nokuti hamugoni kuita izvo Ishe wenyu asingaiti! Hamuna kuverenga tsamba yangu kukereke yeRaodhikiya muna Zvakazarurwa here? Tarira, ndimire pamukova, uye ndinogogodza: kana munhu akanzwa izwi rangu, uye akazarura mukova, ndichapinda kwaari, uye ndichadya naye, naiyewo neni. Cherechedzai, handina kuti ndichapinda muKereke yeRaodhikiya, asi kana paine munhu mukereke yeRaodhikiya akanzwa izwi rangu uye akazarura mukova, ndichapinda ndodya naye, kwete nekereke yake yekare yakarasha kutenda, kunyange ndiri kuvadanawo kana chete vachizoteerera. Mwenga wangu, muranda haasi mukuru kuna tenzi wake, saka teverai nzira dzangu.

Mwenga wangu, ndava pedyo kusvika kuti ndikutorerei kudenga, saka rambai muchizvigadzirira uye rambai muchizivisa kuuya kwangu. Nokuti ndakatuma vazhinji venyu mubasa raJohani Mubhabhatidzi kuzivisa kuuya kwangu uye kudaidza kutendeuka. Ndakatuma vazhinji venyu mubasa raPetro kufudza nokudyisa boka. Ndakatuma vamwe venyu sevatariri vechakavanzika chikuru chaMwari, mubasa raPauro Muapostora (nevamwe vatariri vechakavanzika). Ndakatuma vazhinji venyu mubasa raFiripi MuEvhangeri, saka paridzai shoko; shingairai munguva yakafanira neisina kufanira; tsiurai, rayirai, kurudzirai nokushivirira kwose nedzidziso. Ndakatuma vamwe venyu mubasa roMuapostora Johani, uyo aivawo mufudzi uye aichengeta boka norudo, unyoro, netsitsi, kunyange kuporesa maronda akakuvadzwa pamakwai angu nomuipi. Musazvikahadzika, nokuti ndakakutumai mumabasa avo; nokuti nguva yava pedyo, nokuti ndava kuda kuroora Mwenga wangu, nokuti ndiri kuuya nokuda kwenyu, vadikani vangu! Pedzisai nzendo dzenyu, nokuti ndava pedyo. Nguva yapera.

Ndini Jesu weNazareta.

Ndini iye achatonga kubva pachigaro choushe chaDhavhidhi, nokuti ndakabva kwaari saizvozvo.

Ndiri kufumura uipi uhwu, kunyange Satani achida kuti hurambe hwakavanzika.

Ndiri kukuyambirai, kunyange vazhinji venyu vaine usimbe zvokuti havagoni kuita maererano nemashoko angu.

Ndiri kuuya, uye ndinounza hasha huru paBhabhironi Rakavanzika.

Ndakachena uye nguo dzangu chena sechando chitsva.

Ndiri pano kuti ndikuudzei kuti hamuna nguva yokutambisa.

Ndiri kudana, kunyanya imi, Kereke yangu yeRaodhikiya.

Ndava pedyo kusvika kuti ndikutorerei kudenga.

Ndava kuda kuroora Mwenga wangu.

Ndiri kuuya nokuda kwenyu, vadikani vangu!

Ndava pedyo uye ndinokudai zvikuru; muri pakati pomwoyo wangu.

Ndini tariro yenyu noruponeso, kunyange Ishe wemadzishe naMambo wemadzimambo.

Ndini Yeshua HaMashiach.
