Teererai kumashoko emuponesi wenyu, Ishe naMambo, Vana vangu, uye rerekai nzeve dzenyu kuMashoko aShe wenyu, iye Jesu Kristu akarurama, nekuti ndiri mwanakomana wekururama uye ndinosimuka mukubwinya kwangu, uye makura semhuru dzemudanga murunyararo neruchengeteko, uye ndakakurererai chinangwa chekudenga chava kuda kuzarurirwa zvizere mamuri, imi vakatendeka nevakasarudzwa vangu. Tarirai! Ndini iye unoedza mwoyo yevanhu kuti muzive nechokwadi kuti muri mukutenda zvechokwadi here, kana kuti makanyengerwa uye makatorwa nezvivi zvenyu uye nekunyengera kukuru kwazvino kwakabata Nyika.

Nekuti ndini iye Yahweh waakagovana naye chigaro chake cheushe, iye Kristu Jesu Ishe akamuka uye akapiwa simba, uye ndiri Ishe wemadzishe naMambo wemadzimambo uye tarirai Shumba yeRudzi rwaJudha iri kuuya mumakore kuzogamuchira vakatendeka vangu kwandiri kuti vatorerwe kudenga kumuchato weGwayana, nekuti Mwenga wangu azvigadzirira. Uye marambi ake azere, uye agadzirisa tambo dzawo, uye akamirira kuonekwa kwangu semhandara yakachenjera, ivo vandakanatsa zvizere uye vasina mhosva pamberi pechigaro cheushe chaMwari. Ivo vasina kutorwa neunyanzvi naBharabhasi, nekuti Bharabhasi anounza kupandukira kwechisimba, hondo nenjodzi, nekuti parizvino ari musoro mumwe pakati pemisoro yakawanda inoshanda pamwechete, asi achasimuka saMoshiach wenhema, uye akasarudzwa nevanhu venyika kuti atonge sinagogi raSatani. Nekuti ndiye mugari wenhaka weuyo madzibaba avo vaakasarudza, uye vari kuzvigadzirira kumugamuchira saMambo wavo.

Asi imi, haiwa Kereke yeRaodhikiya, zvino yasvika nguva yenyu yekuparadzwa nekutongwa. Nekuti makazvisarudzira mumwe muponesi saMambo wenyu, iye kuuya kwake kunoziviswa nezviratidzo nenhamo dzenhema uye uyo parizvino akakutungamirirai nemashiripiti neunganga. Ndini Yeshua HaMashiach uye ndakakutengai neropa rangu rinoregerera, risina chivi, uye ndini iye aizokuitai vakakwana, uye kukusikai patsva mumweya wangu, nekuti ndakaitwa mweya unopa upenyu nekuti saizvozvo Yahweh akaronga hukuru hwese paMwanakomana wake, iye ari kupedziswa kwegwayana raMwari neShumba yeRudzi rwaJudha! Uye mukururama ndichatonga uye ndichaita hondo nekuti gwayana raMwari neShumba yeRudzi rwaJudha zvakapedziswa mandiri, Kristu Jesu, iye Yeshua HaMashiach nekuti ndizvo zvandiri!

Ndichapedzisa ruponeso rwenyu, haiwa Kereke yeRaodhikiya, asi hamuna kugadzirira kupinda muHumambo hwangu panguva ino nekuti makatsauka kubva pachinangwa chenyu chimwe chete muna Kristu! Ndichakuratidzai kukanganisa kwenyu, uye mune mukana wekutendeuka kubva kuzvivi zvese, asi hamuna nguva yasara yekupokana nezwi raShe wenyu, nekuti chivi ichi chakakomba ndechimwe chezvizhinji zvakashandiswa naSatani kukusungai kuti musauye kwandiri zvizere mukutendeuka kuti ndikunatsai zvizere muropa rangu rinoregerera kubva kuzvivi zvenyu.

Tarirai! Nguva dzeveMarudzi dzapera uye muti wemuorivhi wemusango wakabatanidzwa pakati pavo, uye navo unodya pamudzi nemafuta eMuti weMuorivhi, zvino uchagurwa. Nekuti makafuratira Mashoko aKristu Jesu, nekuti ndakakuudzai pachena kuti musazvirumbidza pamusoro pematavi akadonha mukusatenda: asi kana muchizvirumbidza, hamutakuri mudzi, asi mudzi unokutakurai. Ndini Mudzi waJese, uye ndini iye anomira sechiratidzo chetariro yenguva dzese, iye anounza ruponeso nenzira dzaYahweh panyika uye ndichatonga nhaka yangu kubva pachigaro cheushe chaDhavhidhi mukururama uye ndakatenga nyika neropa rangu risina chivi uye ndichatora izvo zviri zvangu nechisimba kubva mumaoko evagari zvisiri pamutemo, ivo vakawa.

Ndini iye akapa nyasha netsitsi kunyika, nekuti nguva yese yenyasha yakapiwa kwandiri naYahweh yeruponeso rweveMarudzi. Nekuti zvinangwa zvaYahweh muna Israeri zvaigara zviri zvekuti veMarudzi vakomborerwe uye vauye kuruponeso kuburikidza nekuteerera kwaIsraeri. Asi nekufamba kwenguva vakava vanozvikudza, uye vanozvirumbidza, uye vakagurwa kubva pamuti wemuorivhi wechisikigo mukusatenda. Asi imi, Kereke yangu yeRaodhikiya, imi ndimi muenzaniso wekuzvikudza, kusatenda, neupofu hwakafanana nehwakaratidzwa nematavi echisikigo akagurwa mukusatenda, uye zvino muchagurwa kubva pamuti wemuorivhi wechisikigo. Uye zvino ndichafamba kunopedzisa basa rangu pamuti wemuorivhi wechisikigo, iye Israeri hama yangu nevanhu vangu, vese vandakasarudza kuti vandigamuchire saShe wavo kuti ndivasike patsva semunhu mumwe mutsva uye kuti chakavanzika chaMwari chipedziswe, iko kupedziswa kwemabasa angu ekuregerera eruponeso.

Uye imi, Kereke yangu yeRaodhikiya, makasiya rudo rwenyu rwekutanga uye muri mumaoko emunyengeri wamakapika kuvimbika kwaari. Makazvisarudzira Bharabhasi mutsva saMambo wenyu uye makaramba Jesu weNazareta saShe wenyu uye muri mumaoko emumwe muponesi wenhema. Iye anozviita muponesi weBhabhironi rakavanzika, anotaura nekuita semumwe wenyu, asi anotaura seshato. Nekuti Bhabhironi rakavanzika rinorarama mukunakirwa uye muhupfumi hukuru hwezvinhu, mufaro nehupfumi.

Uye Bhabhironi rakavanzika rakadhakisa nyika yese netsvina yehupombwe huri mumukombe waro wendarama, asi ndidzo nzira dzevakawa, vanopa tsvina yakaipisisa, uye imi munoinwa nekuzvidira uye nemufaro mukuru nekuti makapofumadzwa nemukombe wendarama kuti muone tsvina yakanyanya yamunodya zvisina kuchena. Nekuti kunyange Kereke yangu yeRaodhikiya haichakungani pamagwaro angu matsvene asi pamisangano yaBharabhasi, wamunovimba naye. Uye saka munosarudza muponesi mutsva nekuti munoda nyika nezvinhu zvenyika uye hamugoni kufungidzira pfungwa yekuti mungarasikirwa nezvinonaka zvenyu zvinokosha, kunze kwekuti ndichabvisa mufaro wese kubva kwamuri mukutsamwa kwangu kuri kuuya, uye kunyange zvino kurasikirwa kwenyu kuri kuwanda!

Nekuti muri mapofu uye makanyengerwa mukuzvikudza kwenyu kuti Yahweh aizopa mumwe muponesi kunze kwangu, Jesu weNazareta, nekuti ndini mbeu yakapikirwa yemukadzi uye Dhani imhuru yeshumba isingazomuki Mesia. Ndinzwei uye ndinzwei zvakanaka, haiwa kereke yeRaodhikiya, nekuti zvino inguva yekutendeuka kwenyu kubva pakugumbura kwemwoyo yenyu kana kuti zvino inguva yandichakusiyai kumoto wekunatsa uye vese vanodana zita raShe kusvika kumagumo vachaponeswa uye vazhinji vakapupura zita rangu zvisina kutendeka vachawira mukukanganwa, vatambisa mukana wavo pachitarisiko chekunamata chakaramba simba racho: Imi vaRaodhikiya vangu, ndini simba racho uye mandiri Yahweh akaisa hukuru hwese, uye makandiramba, Ishe nemuponesi wenyu, uye ndichakusvipai kubva mumuromo mangu kunze kwekunge matendeuka zvino!

Munguva yangu yenyasha, ruponeso rwakapararira pakati pemarudzi, uye rudzi nerudzi rwakagovana uye rwakatora chikamu pamudzi wemuti wemuorivhi wechisikigo. Uye zvino, marudzi haachazovi chiratidzo cheruponeso uye nguva yavo muMwanakomana yakauya nekurambwa kwehama yangu nevanhu vangu nekuti vakadanidzira mukusatenda kuti sunungurirai kwatiri Bharabhasi! Asi sezvo zvakavanzika zvaYahweh zvakazarurwa nevaapositori vangu vatsvene nevaporofita, ndakadzora Bharabhasi uye handina kumusunungura zvizere, asi ndakangobvumira chakavanzika chekusarurama kuti chienderere mberi. Uye zvino nguva dzasvika pandichasunungura Bharabhasi zvizere sezvo mudzori wangu abviswa uye sezvo vakatendeka vangu, vakadanwa, nevakasarudzwa vatorwa kuenda kudenga kwandakavagadzirira nzvimbo dzekugara dzechigarire, uye vachapfekedzwa mucheka wakachena usina gwapa usingazombosvibiswi kana kushatiswa.

Makapofumara kudaro kunyika yenyu, uye matsi pakunzwa zvekuti hamuchanzwi izwi rangu here haiwa Kereke yeRaodhikiya? Makatenda nhema dzakaunzwa nechakavanzika chekusarurama, uye makabvumira mbiriso ine chepfu mumaBhaibheri enyu akaora izvo zvakaunza huori hwakakwana mumakereke enyu izvo zvakasvibisa mwoyo yenyu nengano mukusatenda kusina kururama. Makabvumira nhema dzen'anga dzedisipensesheni kuti dzikuroyei nemagwaro enhema nemashoko enhema. Hamuchanzwisisi magwaro, uye makazviunganidzira matambudziko makuru achauya pamusoro penyu pakarepo. Shoko rega rega raMwari rakachena uye rakaitirwa kuteerera kwekutenda nekuvaka kwevanogamuchira. Nzwisisai Mashoko eMashoko angu uye nzwisisai nguva dzamunorarama. Nekuti ndakaita kuti Johane Muzaruri anyore: Uye ndakaona chikara chichibuda mugungwa, chine misoro minomwe, nenyanga gumi, uye panyanga dzacho paiva nendoro gumi, uye pamisoro yacho zita rekumhura.

Uye zvakare Johane Muzaruri akanyora: Ndakaona mukadzi agere pamusoro pechikara chitsvuku, chakazara nemazita ekumhura, chine misoro minomwe, nenyanga gumi. Uye mukadzi wacho akanga akapfeka zvitsvuku nezvitema, uye akashongedzwa nendarama, nematombo anokosha nemaparera, aine mukombe wendarama muruoko rwake, wakazara nezvinonyangadza netsvina yehupombwe hwake.

Uye zvakare: Uye nyanga gumi dzawakaona pamusoro pechikara, idzi dzichavenga hure, uye dzichamusiya ari dongo, uye asina kupfeka, uye dzichadya nyama yake, uye dzichamupisa nemoto. Nekuti Mwari akaisa mumwoyo yavo kuti vazadzise kuda kwake, uye kuti vabvumirane, uye vape humambo hwavo kuchikara, kusvikira mashoko aMwari azadziswa. Uye mukadzi, wawaona, ndiro Guta guru iro rinotonga pamusoro pemadzimambo enyika.

Vana vangu, nzwisisai kuti chikara chinogona kureva vanhu uyewo humambo, dzidzai kuti mudzidze. Chikara chinotakura hure chichaparadza hure uye hachichazovi pasi pehure, nekuti chakatonga madzimambo enyika muhupombwe hwacho. Saka chikara chichabuda mugungwa chichaburitsa Mahdi wavo anotarisirwa, asi chichaparadzawo zvachose Bhabhironi Rakavanzika, nekuti Mwari akapa hure guru mumaoko evavengi varo vanotorivenga zvachose.

Chikara chinobva mugungwa nechikara chinobva panyika zvisati zvasimuka zvizere muHumambo hweshato, kune vatongi vazhinji vakabatana muchinangwa chekupa simba ravo nekutonga kwavo kushhato, nekuti Mwari akaisa mumwoyo yavo kuti vazviite. Uye vachaparadza Bhabhironi rakavanzika. Munofunga kuti makagona magwaro angu matsvene nekuti makatenda vadzidzisi venyu venhema? Nzwisisai uye inzwayi Mashoko aShe wenyu!

Zvakare, ndakaita kuti Johane Muzaruri anyore: Uye chikara chandakaona, chakanga chakafanana nembada, uye tsoka dzacho setsoka dzebheya, uye muromo wacho semuromo weshumba: uye shato yakachipa simba rayo nechigaro chayo cheushe, nesimba guru. Uye ndakaona mumwe wemisoro yacho sekunge wakuvadzwa kusvika pakufa, asi ronda raro rinouraya rakaporeswa, uye nyika yese yakashamisika ikatevera chikara.

Vana vangu, mashoko aYahweh ane hudzamu hwakawanda, husingagoni kugonwa nemunhu. Chikara chinorondedzerwa humambo huchasimuka kubva mukuparadzwa kwehure, uye kubva muhurongwa hwekare huchasimuka hurongwa hutsva! Chinomiririra chikara chegungwa, nekuti chichasimuka saMahdi wavo, uye chinomiririra chimiro chehumambo hwaachatonga nahwo gungwa. Sei musingaremekedzi mashoko eShoko rangu? Haritauri kuti chikara chinobva mugungwa pachacho chakakuvadzwa, nekuti munhu upi ane misoro minomwe chaiyo? Asi mumwe wemisoro yacho sekunge wakuvadzwa kusvika pakufa. Haiwa, mapenzi, nemwoyo inononoka kutenda zvese zvakataurwa neVaporofita! SEKUNGE wakuvadzwa kusvika pakufa. Mashoko ekuti "sekunge" anofanira kukumisai, asi hamuchatendi kunyange mashoko angu madiki, uye ndini Shoko rakaitwa nyama. Hazvisi pachena kwamuri here, SEKUNGE, idimikira rekare? Hazvisi pachena kwamuri here kuti "kunge" iri subjunctive? Hazvisi pachena kwamuri here kuti kupfupikiswa kwe, sekunge zvaive zvakadaro?

Asi kwete, munotenda nhema dzeshato, nekuti Bharabhasi anotaura seshato, asi haasi Bharabhasi, asi chikara chinobva panyika. Uye saka zvino nyika yese ichashamisika nechikara nekuda kwezviratidzo zvaro zvenhema nenhamo, uye chichaita zvimwe zviratidzo zvenhema nenhamo, uye hachisati chasimuka zvizere nekuti hure haisati yapiswa nemoto, asi zvino razarurirwa munguva yaro. Rinotaura seshato, richiti "Mwari oga ndiye akamuponesa kubva paronda rake remusoro" uye iyi inhema yekumhura. Yahweh, Mwari mukuru wekusingaperi, akandituma ini Jesu weNazareta semuponesi wenyika. Ini naBaba vangu tiri vamwe mukubatana nechinangwa uye Yahweh haana kuponesa Bharabhasi uye neniwo handina, Kristu Jesu. Makanyengerwa uye makatorwa uye makabatwa mukunyengera kukuru, uye ndinoti kwamuri tendeukai uye mudzokere kurudo rwenyu rwekutanga uye muitewo izvozvi, nekuti hapana nguva!

Nekuti ndini iye unoedza mwoyo, uye ndini iye unoedza vese vandinodaidza kuruponeso, nekuti ndinonatsa vese mumoto wekunatsa! Tarirai! Ndini iye anogara semunatsi nemuchenesi wesirivha: uye ndinonatsa vanakomana vaYahweh, uye ndinovachenesa sendarama nesirivha, kuti vape kuna Ishe chipiriso mukururama. Hamunzwisisi here kuti ndichakuitai vapristi nemadzimambo pamberi paBaba vangu? Kunyangwe chipo changu chemweya mutsvene, chamunoberekwa nacho patsva nembeu isingaori chakakwana uye chisingade kunatswa, munobva kuna Adhamu neropa uye munofanira kubvumira zvizere basa rangu kuti riitwe muhupenyu hwenyu, kunze kwekunge mbeu yangu yawira pavhu rine matombo.

Zvino nguva dzekutonga dzasvika uye nguva dzenyasha dzapera. Kunyange vazhinji vachaseka mashoko angu uye vachifuratira mukusatenda, Yahweh anotoziva kare magumo acho. Nokuti Yahweh anogara achiratidza vanhu nzira dzavo dzakaora uye achituka vanhu pamusoro pechivi, achiziva murudo rwake nouchenjeri husingaperi kuti achaponesa vamwe. Uye sezvo Yahweh akaomesa mwoyo waFarao, saizvozvowo achaomesa mwoyo yevazhinji vanonzwa asi vasingateereri. Yahweh achaedza munhu kakawanda-kawanda. Uye vamwe vachaita saFarao uye vachaomesa mwoyo yavo zvakare mukusatenda, asi vamwe vachabva panzira dzaFarao uye vachamukira kuupenyu husingaperi muna Kristu Jesu, asi kuburikidza nomoto womunatsi.

Nzwisisai kuti avo vanosarudza Bharabhasi vachazviita vadyidzani vemhosva dzaBharabhasi, uye avo vanoramba kudana zita rangu kusvika kumagumo vose vachatongerwa rufu nokuti vakafadzwa nokusarurama. Nokuti muponesi wenyu mutsva haasi muponesi asi chikara chinoparadza. Uye hurongwa hwake noumambo hwake hunoumbwa neavo vanokuvadza vana. Hamunzwisisi here nzira dzeShumba huru? Nokuti avo vanotevera nzira dzeshumba huru vanovhima vana. Vanobayira vana kuna Moreki, vanotsvaka tsime ravo roudiki muropa revana vanotambudzwa, vanoshinha nevana nokuda kwekuzvigutsa kwavo kunorwarisa, uye vanoita tsika yekurova mwana mudumbu kuti vauraye vadiki vasati vaberekwa, uye vakakutungamirirai muchivande munzira dzavo, nokuti ndidzo nzira dzevatungamiriri veBhabhironi Rakavanzika pamwe chete noumambo hweshumba huru. Uye zvino kutonga kwaMwari kwave kusvika pamusoro pezvinhu izvi nezvimwe zvizhinji. Nokuti dai musina kuona zvinhu izvi, mungadai musina chivi, asi zvino mava kuona uye chivi chenyu chinoramba chiripo.

Hamuna kuverenga here? Zvanzi naIshe, Inzwi rakanzwika kumusoro, kuchema nokuungudza kunovava. Rakeri, achichema vana vake, akaramba kunyaradzwa pamusoro pevana vake, nokuti vakanga vasipo. Nokuti makakundikana pabasa ramakapiwa, uye makavimbwa nezvakavanzika zvangu uye makamira sechiedza cheruponeso uye chiratidzo chetariro. Asi zvino mabatana neavo vandakakutumai kuti murwise. Uye saka zvino nguva dzave kusvika dzandichakusiyirai kune ramangwana renyu.

Ndinzwei uye mundinzwe zvakanaka imi mose mungaramba inzwi raYeshua HaMashiach, kunyange inzwi raKristu Jesu: hamuna nguva! Ndinokurairai kuti mundinzwe murudo rwangu kwamuri vana vangu uye mutendeuke zvino! Nokuti moto womunatsi wava kuda kuwira pamusoro penyu zvizere, uye uchachenesa nyika kubva kuumambo hwose hweshumba huru uye pasi pano hapachazovi pasi penzira dzaSatani uye umambo hwake hahuchazomukizve, nokuti ndiri Ishe wemadzishe naMambo wemadzimambo, uye ndichaparadza zvachose vakawa. Vose vanoramba kutendeuka vachawana mugove wavo mugungwa remoto uye vachagovana chirango chadhiyabhorosi nengirozi dzake mugungwa guru iroro rinopfuta nesarufa. Saizvozvowo, vose vanotendeuka nokubuda muchivi uye nokudana zita rangu kusvika kumagumo vachaponeswa uye vachapiwa mubairo mukuru usingaperi mandiri.

Tarirai! Ino ndiyo nguva yekuunganidza vakatendeka, uye zvino ndichasiya avo vanoramba kuna Bharabhasi, nokuti vakaita sarudzo yavo.

Uye vazhinji muBhabhironi Rakavanzika, kunyange avo vanotenda nhema dzake uye vanotevera mashoko ake akanaka nekutaura kwake kwakanaka vanokwezvwa uye vanobatwa norugare rwenhema kwavanofungidzira kuti upenyu hwavo hunonaka uye hune zvivi huchaenderera mberi apo nhasi kwakafanana nezuro uye mangwana achafanana nanhasi. Uye vazhinji vachadanidzira, vachichema-chema, uye vachirira kuti tariro yavo yatorwa kubva kwavari. Uye vacharwa navanhu venyika yavo kusvika parufu uye panguva yose iyoyo kurwisa kwavo kuzere kwechimbichimbi kuchasunungurwa zvizere, kunyange apo mudzivisi wangu anobviswa panyika. Uye Bhabhironi Rakavanzika richawira muchikamu chepiri chekutongwa muawa imwe chete apo njodzi zhinji dzinowira.
