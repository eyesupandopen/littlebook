Chapupu Chiviri: Israeri
Zvinovava Zvinotapira
Gunyana 7, 2024

Sezvazvakanyorwa, Saizvozvo Zvichaitwa!

Zviunganidzei munzwe mashoko aIshe wenyu, iye Yeshua HaMashiach, vana vangu, nekuti ndiri Ishe wemadzishe naMambo wemadzimambo, uye mukururama ndichauya kuzotonga nekuita hondo. Saka nzwisisai nguva dzamunorarama madziri nezviitiko zvichauya, uye teererai nzeve dzenyu kuruzha rwezwi rangu, munzwe mashoko aIshe wenyu asingaperi uye wekusingaperi, iye Kristu Jesu akamutswa uye akakudzwa! Zvino ndichataura pamusoro pezvinhu zvichauya mukati mevhiki rechimakumi manomwe raDhanieri uye ndichaita kuti nzira dzenyu dzijeke, uye ndichatambanudza nyasha dzangu dzinoponesa kuburikidza nemaoko evangu zviuru zana nemakumi mana nezvina. Nekuti vanouya kuzoponesa vanhu vangu kubva mukuparadzwa, uye vanouya kuzovanza vanhu vangu kubva mukutsamwa, iko kutsamwa kwaMwari kwakagadzirirwa vakawa neavo vanozvisungirira zvachose kwavari nekuushe hwavo nekutora mucherechedzo wavo, iko kurumwa kwenyoka yechikara kusina mushonga.

Asi zvakadaro ndichabvuta Mwenga wangu ndichimuendesa kumatenga, uye ndichaisa chisimbiso pane vangu zviuru zana nemakumi mana nezvina vanobva kumadzinza anotevera, zviuru gumi nezviviri kubva kune rimwe nerimwe reinotevera: Juda, Rubheni, Gadhi, Asheri, Naftari, Manase, Simioni, Revhi, Isakari, Zebhuroni, Josefa, naBenjamini. Saka inzwai izwi rangu!

Uye vachava simba risingamisiki, zvirokwazvo vachava simba risingaputsiki. Nekuti hupenyu hwavo ndehwekusingaperi, husina huori hunogona kuodza. Miviri yavo haiparadziki, nekuti hakuna kuparadzwa kunogona kubata vakadzikinurwa; nekuti kukunda pamusoro perufu nekuparadzwa ndizvo zvavanazvo nekusingaperi-peri. Vachasikwa patsva sevana vechiedza, uye vachapfekedzwa kubwinya kusingaperi, uye vachava neunyanzvi hwakasiyana nematalendi akanyorwa muhunhu hwavo chaihwo izvo zvicharatidza zvavakaita zvinosanganisira nekushandisa unyanzvi hwandichasika mukati mavo. Nekuti ini, Kristu Jesu, ndiri munatsi anogara aine chipepero muruoko, achikuchidzira mirazvo yekunatsa, kuti ndikwanise kukusikai mose patsva mandiri, Kristu Jesu Ishe wenyu akakwidzwa neMuponesi; uye ani nani anokunda, iyeye achagara nhaka yezvinhu zvose.

Saka ndinokupai kuti munzwisise zviitiko zvichakurumidza kuitika, uye zvimwe zviitiko zvinoitika zvichiteverana asi zvimwe zvichiitika panguva imwe chete, asi ndichazvinyora kuitira kuti mukwanise kunzwisisa nguva dzamunorarama, uye kuti imi vatsvene vangu venguva yekutambudzika musabatwa nehunyengeri hukuru hwakafukidza nyika uye huchanyengedza vazhinji vachizotongwa. Nekuti vakawa vachazviratidza uye vachanyepa kakawanda, uye vachavimbisa hupenyu asi havana simba rekupa hupenyu, uye havagoni kupa hupenyu; asi shoko rega rega ravanotaura rakadzika midzi munhema dzinotungamirira kurufu rwechipiri, uye kuparadzwa kusingaperi chete ndiko kuchawanikwa neavo vanotevera nzira dzevakawa nevese vanogamuchira nhema dzavo dzakaipa sechokwadi. Asi uku ndiko kupiwa mhosva kwakauya munyika, kuti vanhu vanoda rima panzvimbo yechiedza uye vanhu vanoda nhema panzvimbo yechokwadi, nekuti vanhu vakaramba ini, Yeshua HaMashiach, iye Jesu Kristu, vachida muponesi wenhema; asi chokwadi chiri muna Kristu chete, uye vamwe vose ndevanyepi nevabiridzi.

Saka zvinhu zvichauya ndezvinotevera, uye zvimwe zviitiko zvinoitika panguva imwe chete uye zvimwe zvinoitika zvichiteverana uye zvinodiwa kuti zvimwe zviitiko zviitike. Uye zviitiko zvizhinji hazvina kunyorwa asi zvichauya nenguva yazvo. Uye izvo zvinonzi nevamwe kubvutwa, kana kuunganidzwa kwevose vari muna Kristu kuna Ishe wavo, ndicho chiitiko chekutanga chakagadzirirwa kutanga zvimwe zviitiko zvese panguva yekutambudzika kwaJakobho, kunonziwo vhiki rechimakumi manomwe raDhanieri, nekuti richava vhiki remakore manomwe, apo zuva rega rega riri gore. Saka vakatendeka muKereke yangu, unova Muviri wangu neMwenga wangu, vachatorwa pandinodzika ini, Kristu Jesu, kubva kumatenga nekudanidzira, uye nezwi rengirozi huru nehwamanda yaMwari. Nekuti hwamanda yekupedzisira icharira, panguva iyo vakafa muna Kristu vachamutsirwa kuhupenyu hutsva; ipapo imi muri vapenyu uye makasara muchabvutirwa mumakore pamwe chete navo, uye zvichaitika sezvazvakanyorwa, uye nenzira yakapiwa zvichaitika mukubwaira kweziso.

Saka nzwisisai, vakatendeka vangu, imi vandinoda nerudo rusingaperi uye rwekusingaperi, kuti muchangoerekana mazviwana muri muchigaro cheushe chaYahweh pachake, kwandichakuratidzai musina gwapa kana kuunyana kana zvimwe zvakadaro kuna Baba vezviedza, uye imi muri zviedza zvenyika. Uye pakarepo muchaziva sezvamunozivikanwa, uye muchaona ini, Yeshua HaMashiach, uye muchaona Yahweh, Mwari Mukuru Wekusingaperi, nemaziso enyu kekutanga, uye muchashamiswa zvikuru nezvamuchaona nekubwinya kwamuchapfekedzwa. Uye nekusingaperi muchava mukubatana kusingaperi naBaba, uye neMwanakomana, uye nekusingaperi tichava.

Panguva dzekuunganidzwa kwevatsvene, mudzivisi achabviswa, uye kutonga kuchawira zvizere paimba yaMwari. Uye avo vakandibvumidza kuti ndivanatse mumoto wemunatsi vachamira nekuti vakanatswa muropa regwayana uye vachamira mukururama kwandakavapa, nekuti ndiri Ishe Akarurama anova mweya unopa hupenyu. Uye avo vakaramba izwi rangu uye vakaramba vakaomesa moyo kuchido changu uye vakaramba kubuda muchivi vachipinda mukururama vachasiyiwa kuti vanatswe zvakare panguva dzekutambudzika kwaJakobho, uye mapoka makuru achawana ruponeso rusingaperi, mubairo, nekubwinya, uye vachararama nekusingaperi uye kubwinya kukuru kuchava kwavo nekusingaperi-peri. Uye saizvozvowo machechi enhema akasara achaparadzwa sezvo Dhiragoni, chikara chinobva mugungwa, nechikara chinobva panyika vachakurumidza kuita hondo nevatsvene vakasara uye vachavakunda. Uye saizvozvo zvichava.

Kana mudzivisi abviswa, Bhabhironi Rakavanzika richaparadzwa sezvakanyorwa. Kunyangwe muawa imwe chete magumo aro achasvika, uye nhamo huru nekuparadzwa zvichapedza nyika, uye kuzununguka kukuru kuchaitika, uye zvichave pachena kunyika kuti zuva raIshe rasvika, kunyange vakawa vachanyepa uye vachazviratidza sevatorwa vane mutsa, uye vachaedza kuvhara kuonekwa kwangu kune vakatendeka muhondo nekurwiswa kwenhema kwevatorwa. Saka musatenda nhema dzavo, vana vangu, nekuti nhema dzavo dzakagadzirirwa kukutungamirirai kukuparadzwa kusingaperi kwakachengeterwa vakawa, saka musabatana navo.

Saka zvisimbiso zvichasunungurwa uye vatasvi vemabhiza vana vachasunungurwa panyika, uye vanhu vazhinji, vazhinji vachaparara nekuda kwaizvozvo. Uye umambo hweBhabhironi Rakavanzika hunogara pamusoro pemvura zhinji, kunyange izvi zvichaparadzwa nekuderedzwa, uye zvakapambwa zvichashandira hurongwa hwechikara huri kusimuka uye hwekupedzisira; uye ndehwekupedzisira nekuti ini, Kristu Jesu, ndichaparadza zvachose nekuparadza ushe hwaSatani, kuti husazombosimuka zvakare. Saka nyika ichashamisika nechikara, uye vazhinji vachabatana neushe hwavo hwasatani. Uye hunhu hwakaipa muvanhu, hwakakonzerwa nechivi chaAdhamu apo Evha akatora kubva kunyoka akadya, huchave husina kudzivirirwa zvachose, uye vanhu vachava nekupenga kuzere mumoyo yavo, uye vazhinji vachazvisungirira zvachose kuchikara nekugamuchira nekutora mucherechedzo wechikara. Usatora mucherechedzo wacho, nekuti vose vanoudya vachakandwa vari vapenyu mugungwa remoto wesurferi, rinopisa nemirazvo yebhuruu inopisa zvikuru.

Kubva mumadota ehondo umambo hwavo hwekupedzisira huchasimuka, kunyange kubva mumadota ehurongwa hwekare, nekuti kubva munyonganyonga yavanokonzera kuchasimuka hurongwa hwavo hutsva, kunyange hurongwa hweDhiragoni. Uye saizvozvo vachatsvaga kubatanidza nyika yose pasi pemureza wavo, uye vachabatanidza zvitendero zvese zvenyika uye vachandiramba zvachose, ini, Yeshua HaMashiach, iye Jesu Kristu, uyo asingazobvumirwi pakati pavo. Uye zvirokwazvo, zvirokwazvo, handina kumbova nechikamu kana mugove navo. Uye saizvozvo vachafamba nekumhanya kukuru nehutsinye hukuru muhondo yavo yekurwisa vatsvene, uye nyika ichatendeukira zvachose kuvatsvene venguva yekutambudzika, uye vose vanokunda nekudana zita rangu kusvika kumagumo vachakudzwa nekusingaperi mumubairo usingaperi nekubwinya kusingaperi, uye mubairo wavo mukuru, uye avo vanokunda vachagara nhaka yezvinhu zvose.

Sezvo nzanga yavo itsva ichibuda, saizvozvowo miti yangu miviri yemiorivhi ichasimuka, kunyange zvigadziko zvangu zviviri zvemwenje zvimire pamberi paMwari wenyika. Simba guru richapiwa kwavari, uye vachaporofita kwemwedzi makumi mana nemiviri vakapfeka masaga; uye kana munhu upi noupi akavada kuvarwadzisa, moto uchabuda mumuromo mavo, ukaparadza vavengi vavo; uye kana munhu upi noupi akavada kuvarwadzisa, anofanira kuurayiwa saizvozvo. Ava vachava nesimba rekuvhara denga, kuti mvura isanaye pamazuva echiporofita chavo; uye vachava nesimba pamusoro pemvura kuti vaiite ropa, uye kurova nyika nematambudziko ose, kakawanda sezvavanoda. Uye ruvengo rwenyika kwavari ruchava rukuru. Uye vakamira vachipikisa miti yangu miviri yemiorivhi pachava nechikara nemuporofita wenhema, uye mumwe achanongedzera kune mumwe, uye vachazvikudza, uye vachataura zvinhu zvizhinji zvekumhura, uye vachaita zviratidzo zvenhema nezvishamiso zvizhinji pamberi pevanhu.

Zvikara izvi zvichaita zvishamiso zvikuru kunyengedza avo vanogara panyika, nekuti zvichabvumirwa kuita zviratidzo zvenhema nezvishamiso zvizhinji, uye nesimba rakafanana vachatonga. Chikara chinobva panyika chichaita kuti moto udzike panyika pamberi pevanhu, uye chichatungamirira vanhu vose kuti vashamiswe nazvo nezviratidzo zvenhema nezvishamiso zvizhinji. Uye chichaita mufananidzo wechikara uye chichaita kuti vose vanamate mufananidzo wacho wachichaita. Uye zvichabvumirwa kwachiri kupa mweya kumufananidzo wechikara kuitira kuti mufananidzo wechikara utaure uye uchaita kuti vose vasinganamati mufananidzo wechikara vaurayiwe. Uye chichaita kuti vose, vapfumi nevarombo, vakasununguka nevaranda, vagamuchire mucherechedzo wacho muruoko rwavo rwerudyi kana pahuma dzavo.

KANA MUNHU UPI NOUPI AKANAMATA CHIKARA NOMUFANANIDZO WACHO, AKAGAMUCHIRA MUCHERECHEDZO PAHUMA YAKE, KANA PARUOKO RWAKE, IYEYE ACHAnwa WO WEWaini YEKUTSAMWA KWAMWARI, INODURURWA ISINA KUSANGANISWA MUKOMBE YEKUTSAMWA KWAKE, UYE ACHARWADZISWA NEMOTO NESURFERI, PAMBERI PENGelosi TSVENE, UYE PAMBERI PEGWAYANA: UYE UTSI HWEKURWADZISWA KWAVO HUNOKWIRA NEKUSINGAPERI-PERI. UYE HAVANA ZORORO MASIKATI KANA USIKU, AVo VANONAMATA CHIKARA NOMUFANANIDZO WACHO, UYE ANI NAANI ANOGAMUCHIRA MUCHERECHEDZO WEZITA RACHO.

Uye panguva iyi temberi yavo ichavakwa, kunyange kana chibayiro chemhuka chisisadiwi nekuti ndiri Gwayana raMwari uye ndakazvibayira nekuda kwezvivi zvevanhu semupristi mukuru nekusingaperi maererano nehurongwa hwaMerkizedeki. Ruregerero rwakaitwa nekuda kwezvivi zvevanhu kamwe chete, zvose, uye nekusingaperi muropa rangu rinoregerera, risina chivi. Uye saizvozvo, hakuchina chimwe chibayiro chechivi.

Saka kana miti yangu miviri yemiorivhi yapedza uchapupu hwayo pamagumo emwedzi makumi mana nemiviri, chikara chinokwira kubva mugomba risina chigadziko chichaita hondo navo uye chichauraya zvigadziko zvangu zviviri zvemwenje, nekuti saizvozvo chakapiwa kuti chiite. Uye mitumbi yavo icharara yakafa kwemazuva matatu nehafu mumigwagwa, kunyange nyika ichipemberera nekuchinjana zvipo pamusoro pekufa kwavo. Uye saizvozvo izwi guru richabva kumatenga richiti, Kwirai pano! Uye vachamutsirwa kuhupenyu hutsva, uye vachagamuchirwa kumatenga pamberi pevavengi vavo.

Uye vachapedzisa temberi yavo uye musungo uchateyiwa, nekuti muteyi weshiri upi anoteera mambure shiri ichiona? Nekuti chikara chinobva panyika ndiye anoteera musungo munyika, achakwezva vazhinji mugumbwa rake rekunyengera, uye achatengesa vanhu venyika. Uye munhu iyeye wechivi achaziviswa, iye mwanakomana wekuparadzwa, uyo anopikisa, uye achazvikwidziridza pamusoro pezvose zvinonzi Mwari, kana zvinonamatwa; zvekuti anogara saMwari muTemberi yaMwari, achizviratidza kuti ndiye Mwari. Uye temberi yavo ichapedzwa, uye nyangadzi yekuparadza ichazadziswa naMoshiach wavo wenhema. Uye achasimbisa sungano nevazhinji kwevhiki imwe; uye pakati pevhiki achagumisa chibayiro nechipiriso, uye nekuda kwekupararira kwenyangadzi; achaiparadza, kusvikira kuparadzwa kwakatarwa kwadzikiswa pane zvakaparadzwa.

Uye saka kutambudzika kukuru kuchaitika, kutambudzika kusina kumboonekwa panyika, uye zvirokwazvo, hakuzomboitiki zvakare. Tarirai! Ini, kunyange Yeshuah HaMashiach, handingasiyi vanhu vangu mukuparadzwa kamwe kamwe, uye ndichaponesa Israeri, kunyange vakasara vane simba avo vachandigamuchira saMesia wechokwadi, nekuti ndizvo zvandakasikirwa naYahweh. Uye saka zviuru zvangu zana nemakumi mana nezvina zvichauya kuzobatsira avo vangadai vakaparara uye vanodana zita rangu. Ndichavatungamirira nhanho nenhanho, uye zviuru zvangu zana nemakumi mana nezvina zvichandiitira mabasa mazhinji ane simba.

Nekuti ava ndivo vanotorwa mukuunganidzwa kwevatsvene kwandiri, Kristu Jesu, mumhepo pakurira kwehwamanda yekupedzisira, uye hwamanda yava kuda kurira. Ava ndivo vakatendeka vangu, kunyange avo vasina kuwanikwa neunyengeri, nekuti vakazvinatsa semhandara yakachena muropa reGwayana, randakapa nekusingaperi semupristi wavo mukuru, nekuti ndiri mupristi mukuru nekusingaperi maererano neurongwa hwaMerkizedeki. Ava ndivo Mwenga wangu, uye vachanditevera kwese kwandinoenda. Tarirai! Ndichavaita vane simba, uye hapana chakamboonekwa panyika chinogona kuenzaniswa nehunyanzvi hwandichavafukidza nahwo. Vachapfekedzwa micheka yakaisvonaka, michena, isina ruvara, uye hunyanzvi hwavo hucharatidza zvavakaita, uye vachava nehunyanzvi hukuru; nekuti vakatendeka, vakadanwa, uye vakasarudzwa, kunyange nyika isati yavambwa naYahweh nekuda kwezvinangwa izvi.

Zviuru zvangu zana nemakumi mana nezvina zvichaburukira paGomo reZioni, uye ndichavatungamirira. Vachatora avo vandichavatungamirira kurenje kuti vanovandira kure nerima guru nekutambudzwa kweumambo hwevavengi. Vachatungamirirwa kunzvimbo yakachengeteka kubva kumatambudziko manomwe, nekuti mukutonga uku ndimo mune hukasha hwehasha dzaMwari hunofanira kudururwa pachigaro chechikara nekutambudza umambo hweShumba, nekuti kurangwa kwavo kukuru. Uye zviuru zana nemakumi mana nezvina zvichavatungamirira kunzvimbo yakachengeteka; vachavaporesa maronda avo; vachavabatsira nekuvapa tariro huru yekudana zita raIshe, kunyange kusvika kumagumo kana zvichidikanwa. Nekuti dai ndisina kupfupisa mazuva, hapana nyama yaizosara iri mhenyu. Uye saka vachapedzisa izvo zvandichavatungamirira mazviri.

Uye sezvo vatatu vasina kuchena vachifamba kunoparadza avo vanondigamuchira saMesia wavo, saizvozvowo hasha dzaMwari dzizere dzichazadziswa pamisoro yavo nepamusoro peumambo hwavo hwese; zvirokwazvo, zvirokwazvo, ndiro nomwe dzakazadzwa nehasha dzaMwari; uye kutonga kukuru nenhamo zviri mundiro dzavo, uye zvichaitika sezvazvakanyorwa:

Mutumwa wekutanga achadurura ndiro yake panyika, uye avo vanogamuchira chiratidzo chavo chisina kuchena vacharohwa nemaronda anorwadza uye anotyisa nendiro iyo mutumwa wekutanga achadurura.

Uye mutumwa wechipiri achadurura ndiro yake, uye makungwa achaita seropa remunhu akafa, uye zvisikwa zvese zvemugungwa zvipenyu zvichaparara.

Uye mutumwa wechitatu achadurura ndiro yake, zvichiita kuti nzizi nehova zvishanduke kuva ropa, nekuti vakateura ropa revatsvene nevaprofita, uye saka zvakakodzera kuti vapiwe ropa rekunwa.

Zvirokwazvo zvirokwazvo, mutumwa wechina achadurura ndiro yake pazuva, uye richapiwa kwaari kuti ritambudze vanhu nekupisa kwemoto, uye vanhu vachapiswa nekupisa uye vachataura zvinhu zvikuru zvekumhura Mwari wekudenga.

Uye mutumwa wechishanu achadurura ndiro yake pachigaro cheushe chechikara, uye umambo hwacho huchawedzera kusviba, zvakanyanya, uye vacharuma ndimi dzavo nekurwadziwa.

Uye mutumwa wechitanhatu achadurura ndiro yake parwizi rukuru rweYufratesi, uye mvura yaro ichaoma, kuti nzira yeMadzimambo ekuMabvazuva igadzirirwe.

Uye mutumwa wechinomwe achadurura ndiro yake mumhepo; uye kuchauya inzwi guru richibva muTemberi yekudenga kubva pachigaro cheushe, richiti, Zvaitwa. Uye kuchava nemanzwi, nekutinhira, nemheni, uye kuchaitika kudengenyeka kwenyika kukuru, kusina kumboitika kubva vanhu pavakanga vari panyika, kudengenyeka kwenyika kune simba rakadaro. Uye guta guru richakamurwa kuita zvikamu zvitatu, uye maguta emarudzi achawa. Uye saka zvinhu izvi zvichave.

Saka nzwisisai, vana vangu, kuti Yahweh achavanza vake kunyange panguva yekutambudzika kwaJakobho, kunyange panguva yekutambudzika kukuru vakasara vachaponeswa vari vapenyu uye vacharamba vari munzvimbo yakachengeteka murenje, kunyange nzvimbo yakagadzirirwa naMwari pachake, kuti vavadyise ipapo mazuva churu nemazana maviri nemakumi matanhatu. Uye hapana nyama yaizosara iri mhenyu dai mazuva asina kupfupiswa. Uye vakawa vachazviunganidza kuti varwe naMambo weMadzimambo naIshe weMadzishe, uye saka zvakanyorwa uye saka zvichaitwa.

Uye ndakaona denga rakazaruka, uye tarira bhiza jena, uye uyo akanga akatasva pamusoro paro ainzi Wakatendeka neWechokwadi, uye mukururama anotonga nekuita hondo. Maziso ake akanga akaita semurazvo wemoto, uye pamusoro wake paiva nekorona zhinji, uye akanga aine zita rakanyorwa, risingazivikanwi nemunhu kunze kwake. Uye akanga akapfeka nguo yakanyikwa muropa, uye zita rake rinonzi, Shoko raMwari. Uye mauto akanga ari kudenga akamutevera akatasva mabhiza machena, vakapfeka micheka yakaisvonaka, michena uye yakachena. Uye mumuromo make munobuda munondo unopinza, kuti nawo arove marudzi: uye achavatonga netsvimbo yesimbi: uye anotsika chisviniro chewaini chehasha nekuranga kwaMwari Wemasimbaose. Uye ane zita rakanyorwa panguo yake, nepachidya chake, Mambo WeMadzimambo, NaIshe WeMadzishe.

Uye saka mutumwa wangu achaburuka kubva kudenga, aine kiyi yegomba risina chigumo necheni huru kwaachasunga Satani mugomba risina chigumo kwemakore churu uye oisa chisimbiso kuti arege kunyengedza marudzi zvakare kwemakore churu. Pakupera kwekutonga kwangu kwemakore churu, Satani achasunungurwa kubva mugomba rakadzika kwenguva diki.

Tarirai! Sezvakanyorwa naJohane Muratidzwi, saizvozvowo zvichave: Uye ndakaona zvigaro zveushe, uye vakagara pamusoro pazvo, uye kutonga kwakapiwa kwavari: uye ndakaona mweya yeavo vakagurwa misoro nekuda kwekupupurira Jesu, uye nekuda kweshoko raMwari, uye vakanga vasina kunamata chikara, kana mufananidzo wacho, kana kugamuchira chiratidzo chacho pahuma dzavo, kana mumaoko avo; uye vakararama vakatonga naKristu makore churu. Asi vakafa vakasara havana kurarama zvakare kusvikira makore churu apera. Uku ndiko kumuka kwekutanga. Akaropafadzwa uye mutsvene ndiye ane mugove mukumuka kwekutanga: parudzi urwu rufu rwechipiri haruna simba, asi vachava Vapristi vaMwari, naKristu, uye vachatonga naye makore churu.

Uye pakuguma kwekutonga kwangu kwemakore churu Satani achasunungurwa kubva mujeri rake kwenguva diki kuti avaunganidze pamwechete sejecha regungwa. Uye achaenda kunonyengedza marudzi ari kumativi mana enyika, Gogi naMagogi, kuti avaunganidze pamwechete kuhondo: huwandu hwavo hwakaita sejecha regungwa. Uye vachakwira paupamhi hwenyika, uye vachakomba musasa wevatsvene, neguta rinodiwa: uye moto uchaburuka kubva kuna Mwari uchibva kudenga, uye uchavaparadza. Uye dhiyabhorosi akavanyengedza achakandwa mugungwa remoto nesarufa, kwauchave chikara nemuprofita wenhema, uye vachatambudzwa masikati neusiku nekusingaperi-peri.

Uye saka kuchauya kumuka kwevasakarurama, nekuti zvakanyorwa naJohane Muratidzwi: Uye ndakaona chigaro cheushe chikuru chichena, naiye akanga akagara pachiri, kubva pachiso chake nyika nedenga zvakatiza; uye hapana nzvimbo yakawanikwa kwavari. Uye ndakaona vakafa, vadiki nevakuru, vamire pamberi paMwari; uye mabhuku akazarurwa: uye rimwe bhuku rakazarurwa, rinova Bhuku reUpenyu: uye vakafa vakatongwa kubva muzvinhu zvakanga zvakanyorwa mumabhuku, maererano nemabasa avo. Uye gungwa rakabudisa vakafa vakanga vari mariri; uye rufu nehero zvakabudisa vakafa vakanga vari mazviri: uye vakatongwa mumwe nemumwe maererano nemabasa ake. Uye rufu nehero zvakakandwa mugungwa remoto. Uku ndiko rufu rwechipiri. Uye ani naani asina kuwanikwa akanyorwa muBhuku reUpenyu akakandwa mugungwa remoto.

Uye saka zvakanyorwa uye saka zvichaitwa, kuti kune kuungana pamwechete uko vakafa muna Kristu vachamutswa uye avo vari vapenyu uye vakasara vachatorwa pamwechete navo mumakore, uye izvi zvichaitika kutambudzika kwaJakobho kusati kwatanga. Zvino pakuguma kwekutambudzika kwaJakobho kuchaitika kumuka kwevakarurama, kunoumbwa nevatsvene venguva yekutambudzika uye avo veTestamente Yekare vakafa mukutenda, vakararama maererano nezvipikirwa. Uye sezvazvakanyorwa kuna Dhanieri, saizvozvowo zvichave: Asi enda nenzira yako kusvika kumagumo: nekuti uchazorora, uye uchamira pamugove wako pakuguma kwemazuva. Uye saka kuguma kwemazuva ndiko kupera kwevhiki rechimakumi manomwe raDhanieri uye pakutanga kwekutonga kwangu kwemakore churu.

Uye pakuguma kwekutonga kwangu kwemakore churu uye mushure mekunge Satani akandwa kunhaka yake yekupedzisira mugungwa remoto risingaperi, saizvozvowo kumuka kwevasakarurama kuchaitika, kunyange kutonga kukuru kwechigaro cheushe chichena kuchaitika, uye zita raani naani risina kuwanikwa rakanyorwa muBhuku reUpenyu richakandwa mugungwa remoto.

Uye saka ini, kunyange Jesu weNazareta, kunyange Gwayana raMwari, kunyange Kristu Jesu, uyo ariwo Shumba yeRudzi rwaJudha, ndichauya kuzotonga nekuita hondo neShumba, uye umambo hwayo hwese huchapwanywa zvachose, husingazomukizve. Uye chikara nemuprofita wenhema vachakandwa vari vapenyu mugungwa remoto, uye Satani achasungwa necheni huru mugomba risina chigumo kwemakore churu, uye saka kutonga kwangu kwemireniyamu kuchatanga, uye mukururama ndimo muchave kutonga kwangu. Tarirai! Ndinouya neMwenga wangu neuto reMadzimambo neVapristi vandichatonga navo nyika mukururama, uye ndichatonga marudzi netsvimbo yesimbi. Saka ivai netariro huru, vana vangu, nekuti kupera kwezvinhu zvese kwava pedyo, uye iyi nguva yemakore manomwe yekutambudzika kukuru, kunyange kutambudzika kwaJakobho, ichateverwa nekutonga kwangu kwakarurama, uye Yahweh achava zvese mune zvese.

Uye saka ndakakupai tariro huru, vadiki vangu, kunyange kuti mugone kuisa pfungwa dzenyu pamufaro wakaiswa pamberi penyu nekuti matambudziko ekutambudzika kwaJakobho haaenzaniswi kana kuti haakodzeri kutaurwa maererano nemufaro usingaperi wakaiswa pamberi penyu. Tarirai! Zvakanyorwa naJohane Muratidzwi: Ava ndivo vakabuda mukutambudzika kukuru, uye vakasuka nguo dzavo, vakadziita chena muropa reGwayana. Naizvozvo, vari pamberi pechigaro cheushe chaMwari, uye vanomushumira masikati neusiku muTemberi yake: uye iye anogara paChigaro cheUshe achagara pakati pavo. Havachazovi nenzara, kana nyota zvakare, uye zuva harichavapisi, kana kupisa kupi zvako. Nekuti Gwayana, riri pakati pechigaro cheushe, richavadyisa, uye richavatungamirira kuzvitubu zvipenyu zvemvura: uye Mwari achapukuta misodzi yese pamaziso avo. Vachava Vapristi vaMwari, naKristu, uye vachatonga naye makore churu.

Uye saka ndiyo tariro yenyu, vadikani vangu, kuti mugone kutsungirira kusvika kumagumo, uye yenyu itariro isingaperi, nekuti El Shaddai, Mwari Wemasimbaose, achasika denga idzva nenyika itsva munogara kururama, uye muchapiwa mukana wekupinda muguta dzvene iroro rakagadzirirwa nekushongedzwa naYahweh pachake semwenga akagadzirirwa chikomba, uye kubva pamuti weupenyu muchadya. Uye sezvazvakanyorwa: Uyo anokunda, achagara nhaka yezvinhu zvese, uye ini ndichava Mwari wake, uye iye achava mwanakomana wangu.

Tarirai! Ndiri Yeshua HaMashiach, uye ndatumira mashoko angu nemuranda wangu, uye nemuranda wangu Mashoko angu atumirwa.

Tarirai! Vhangeri risingaperi razarurwa uye ratumirwa panguva yaro yakakodzera, uye mashoko aya achachengetedzwa, uye achawana maziso, nzeve, nemoyo yeavo vese kwaari kutumirwa.

Tarirai! Rindai uye musasiye nzvimbo dzenyu, vanhu vangu, nekuti nguva dzasvika.
