Nuwo Katã Ƒe Vava!

Mise miaƒe Aƒetɔ kple Ðela gbagbe, miaƒe Mesia vavã, Yeshua HaMashiach ŋutɔ ƒe Nyawo. Mese gɔme eye miaɖo to nye Nyawo vinyewo elabena maƒo nu fifia tso nuwo katã ƒe vava ŋu, elabena woƒe vava gogo. Eya ta mese gɔme, elabena amegbetɔwo gbe Yehowa ƒe ŋutifafa nubabla la eye wogbe Yesu Nazaretitɔ la (elabena nyee nye miaƒe ŋutifafa nubabla) ɣeyiɣiwo le anyigba dzi atrɔ fifia tso amenuveve me ayi ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ me, atrɔ tso nublanuikpɔkpɔ me ayi fukpekpe me, atrɔ tso ŋutifafa me ayi aʋawɔwɔ me, atrɔ tso fafafa me ayi dɔmedzoe me, atrɔ ayi hlɔ̃biabia blibo kple tsɔtsrɔ̃ gã me na ame siwo le anyigba dzi. Kple ame siwo katã gbe be yewomaɖom Aƒetɔ o eye wogbe nye lɔlɔ̃ kple tsɔtske na wo, si me manya gbugbɔ wo aɖo wo Wɔla gbɔ eye mana agbe mavɔ wo, be womagaku akpɔ o.

Gake le esi amegbetɔ dzɔdzɔe nava gbɔnye bliboe teƒe la, ame geɖewo gbe le dada me eye wotia boŋ be yewoanɔ Satanawo kple woƒe aglãdzedze la me eye wova kpe ɖe wo ŋu le manyamanya me, alo nya woƒe tiatia la nyuie. Eye ame siwo wɔ tiatia bometsitɔe sia la akpe Mawu ƒe dɔmedzoe kple alẽvi la ƒe dɔmedzoe si woakɔ ɖe anyi kpuie la. Eye togbɔ be ame geɖewo gbe le ɣeyiɣi sia me hã la, ameha gã aɖewo ava gbɔnye eye woaʋu Yesu me be eyae nye Aƒetɔ eye woayɔ woƒe Mesia ƒe ŋkɔ va se ɖe nuwuwu. Eye to Yeshua HaMashiach dzi la, woakpɔ ɖeɖe. Gake ame geɖewo agatsrɔ̃, ame siwo katã ƒe ŋkɔwo womedze le Agbegbalẽ si wowu tso xexea me gɔmedzedze me o la, eye nenemae woƒe tiatia anɔ.

Eye nenema ke abe alesi tɔtrɔ siawo le dzɔdzɔm le anyigba dzi le ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ me ene la, ŋutifafa va xɔ aƒe ɖe dziƒowo eye aʋawɔwɔ atsrɔ̃ anyigba la bliboe fifia. Aʋa dzɔ le dziƒo eye wotsɔ nɔviwo nutsoala la ƒu gbe ɖe anyi, wotsɔe ƒu gbe ɖe anyi, eye wode ka dzoka dzĩ gã la ɖe anyigba dzi fifia eye wòle mɔ kpɔm na eƒe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ eye eƒe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ mehe ɖe megbe o. Le aʋadziɖuɖu blibo me la, awɔ eƒe ɖoɖowo le Yehowa ƒe ɖoɖo mavɔwo me, elabena eƒe futɔwo gɔ̃ hã ɖoa to eƒe gbe, eye wotsɔe nɛ le mɔ aɖe si seɖoƒe li na ko me eye woatsrɔ̃ eƒe fiaɖuƒe la bliboe. Kpɔ ɖa! Da xoxo ma, dzoka dzĩ gã ma ŋutɔ, wotsɔe ƒu gbe ɖe anyigba dzi fifia, eye dɔmedzoe gã aɖe le eme elabena enya be ɣeyiɣi kpui aɖe ko ye kpɔ! Eya ta nye, Yeshua HaMashiach, meɖo aɖaŋu na ame sia ame be wòaƒu du ayi nye gbe ƒe ɖiɖi gbɔ eye wòayɔ nye ŋkɔ gã la va se ɖe nuwuwu na ame siwo melɔ̃ woƒe agbe va se ɖe nuwuwu o la anyɔ ɖe atsyã, dzidzɔ, kple fetu mavɔ me eye woƒe fetu lolo! Elabena ame siwo katã yɔa nye ŋkɔ va se ɖe nuwuwu la akpɔ ɖeɖe, le gbe sia gbe si woyɔna me eye nye Yesu Kristo, si hã nye Yeshua HaMashiach, gblɔ nya siawo!

Eye nenema woava nu xoxoawo ƒe nubablawo kple ŋugbedodowo dzi, nubablawo katã na ɣeyiɣiwo katã elabena nenemae nyanyui mavɔ la le. Eye esi aʋawɔwɔ xɔ aƒe ɖe anyigba dzi la, woɖo ŋutifafa anyi le dziƒowo fifia afi si wodzra dziƒowo ɖo fifia be woaxɔ nuteƒewɔlawo le Kristo me, ame siwo ku le nuteƒewɔwɔ me le Kristo me, kple ame siwo le agbe eye wotsi agbe eye wonɔa agbe le Kristo me. Elabena ame siwo katã zɔna ɖe agbe ƒe gbɔgbɔ ƒe se nu le Kristo Yesu me le nye amenuveve ɣeyiɣia me la, woaƒo wo nu ƒu eye woaxɔ woƒe fetu mavɔ eye woase gɔme eye woaɖo wo ɖe woƒe ɖoɖowo nu be woawɔ Yehowa ƒe tameɖoɖo mavɔ si ta wowɔ wo yeyee le Kristo me la dzi. Eye nenema Ŋugbetɔsrɔ̃ kple Ŋugbetɔ ƒe srɔ̃ɖeɖe ava yi fifia, eye azã gã aɖe aɖu le dziƒowo le atsyã kple ŋutikɔkɔe mavɔ me!

Elabena tso gɔmedzedzea me la, Mawu wɔ Adam, eye tso Adam me ewɔ Xawa be wòanye eƒe zɔhɛ mavɔ eye wòanye agbagbeawo katã nɔ, eye be woádzi asɔ gbɔ. Wode se na wo be le ati siwo katã le Eden bɔa me la, woate ŋu aɖu ati sia ati ƒe tsetse faa negbe nyui kple vɔ̃ nyanya ati la ko. Eye to da la ƒe tetekpɔ aɖaŋutɔ me la, woble Xawa, eye wòtsɔ ati si wode se ɖe enu la ƒe tsetse ɖu, eye wòtsɔ na srɔ̃a Adam, eye wòɖui le nyanya me eye ale nuvɔ̃ gblẽ amegbetɔwo dome eye le kuku me la, woku tso ɣemaɣi. Eye nenema Adam kple Xawa mewɔ woƒe yɔyɔ kɔkɔ la dzi be woaɖu anyigba la dzi eye woate eɖe anyi le dzɔdzɔenyenye me eye woádzi asɔ gbɔ eye woatsɔ dzɔdzɔenyenye axyɔ anyigba la dzi o gake wowɔ nuvɔ̃ boŋ eye wonɔ agbe le nuvɔ̃ ƒe fetu me va se ɖe esime fetu mawo wu enu ɖe woƒe ku me eye le kuku me la amegbetɔ ku tso ɣemaɣi. Eye le ame dzɔdzɔewo ƒe tsitretsitsi me la woaxɔ woƒe fetu kple teƒeɖoɖo elabena wowɔ nuteƒe va se ɖe nuwuwu.

Eye nenemae wòle be Yehowa, le eƒe ŋgɔgbenunya mavɔ me, nya hafi wòdzɔ be amegbetɔ atia nuvɔ̃ kple ku na eɖokui tso gɔmedzedzea me. Eye El Shaddai, le eƒe lɔlɔ̃ gã si wòtsɔ lɔ̃ amegbetɔwo (kple eƒe nuwɔwɔwo katã) me gɔ̃ hã la, na amenuveve kple nublanuikpɔkpɔ si gɔme womate ŋu ase o esime wògblɔ be yeade ketekete Satana ƒe dzidzimevi kple nyɔnu la ƒe dzidzimevi dome. Satana ƒe dzidzimevi te nye afɔkpodzi le Kalvari le Yehowa ƒe Nyawo ƒe vava me eye maʋu fifia be matsɔ da la ƒe ta aƒo anyi gbidigbidi elabena nenemae eƒe etsɔme le kple ame siwo katã kplɔe ɖo, eye abe alesi woŋlɔe ɖi ene la, woayi edzi awɔe va se ɖe nuwuwu.

Gake, Mawu tia le eƒe nunya mavɔ me be yeaɖe yeƒe amenuveve, nublanuikpɔkpɔ, kple ŋutifafa ƒe ɖoɖowo fia na amegbetɔ ƒe ɖeɖekpɔkpɔ blibo le ɣeyiɣi vovovowo me, gake ena mɔkpɔkpɔ gã ame dzɔdzɔewo ɣesiaɣi be gbe ɖeka kpuie la woafɔ wo ɖe agbe yeye me tso ame kukuwo dome be womagaku akpɔ o, eye woanɔ agbe le agbɔsɔsɔ kple ŋutifafa me le woƒe agbemeŋkekewo katã me eye ŋkeke mawo nu mawu o. Le ɣeyiɣiwo me la, nuteƒewɔlawo ku kple xɔse blibo le Yehowa me be wòawɔ eƒe ŋugbedodowo dzi eye woku le ŋuɖoɖo ɖe Yehowa ƒe nubablawo ŋu me abe woƒe ɖeɖekpɔkpɔ ƒe mɔkpɔkpɔ ene eye Yehowa ƒe nubablawo katã ava eme bliboe le Kristo Yesu, nye ŋutɔ, miaƒe Aƒetɔ kple Ðela si fɔ tso ku me la ŋkume. Eye nenema woxɔ Mawu dzi se eye wobui dzɔdzɔenyenye na wo na ame siwo katã xɔ se eye mɔkpɔkpɔ nɔ wo si le nyɔnu la ƒe dzidzimevi si ŋugbe wodo la me.

Eye nenema Yehowa ƒe ɖoɖo na amegbetɔ ƒe ɖeɖekpɔkpɔ blibo nɔ te ɖe dzinye ɣesiaɣi, Kristo Yesu, Yesu Nazaretitɔ si hã nye Yeshua HaMashiach elabena nyee nye Aƒetɔwo ƒe Aƒetɔ si dze dzi si nye Mawu ƒe alẽvi eye wòhe ɖeɖekpɔkpɔ mavɔ vɛ na Yudeatɔwo kple Trɔ̃subɔlawo siaa, eye maɖe Yakob tso ame siwo dze ŋugbedodo nyigba la dzi la si me eye woƒe tsɔtsrɔ̃ mehe ɖe megbe o. Nyee nye ame si me dzata amlɔ anyi kple alẽvi la le elabena le dzinye la Yuda ƒe to la ƒe dzata kple Mawu ƒe alẽvi la ava wu enu bliboe. Elabena nyee nye Yuda ƒe to la ƒe dzata eye nyee nye Mawu ƒe Alẽvi elabena nyee nye Fiawo ƒe Fia kple Aƒetɔwo ƒe Aƒetɔ eye nyee nye Nunɔlagã tegbee le Melkizedek ƒe ɖoɖo nu.

Eye nenema Adam kple Xawa nɔ agbe nuteƒewɔwɔ me yi ŋgɔ le Yehowa woƒe Mawu ŋkume, eye wodzi sɔ gbɔ, gake Satana agawɔ amedzidzedze bubu to ŋudzɔla dzedzeawo ƒe aglãdzedze me. Wokpe ɖe Satanawo ƒe aglãdzedze ŋu eye be woakpɔ ŋudzedze ɖe wo ŋutɔwo ƒe nudzodzrowo ŋu la, wogblẽ woƒe nɔnɔme gbãtɔ si wowɔ wo le la ƒe ŋutikɔkɔe mavɔ ɖi, be woatsɔ nyɔnuwo, ŋutilã kple ʋu gɔ̃ hã, aƒo ɖi wo ɖokuiwo, elabena woanɔ atsyã mavɔ me hafi gake wotia wo ŋutɔwo ƒe tohehe mavɔ boŋ be woawɔ woƒe nudzodzrowo dzi le nuwɔwɔ si nɔ anyi ɣemaɣi la ƒe tsɔtsrɔ̃ kple ku me.

Eye dɔla dzedze siawo fia amegbetɔ dzedze alesi wòatsrɔ̃ eɖokui to Kain ƒe dzidzimeviwo me kple to sidzedze kple aɖaŋu vɔ̃ siwo wofia amegbetɔwo la me. wowɔ wo ɖokuiwo abe mawuwo ene eye wobia be woade ta agu na yewo eye woXƆ srɔ̃wo sesẽe tso ame siwo katã wotia la me, eye nyɔnu geɖewo tsɔ wo ɖokuiwo na le Satanawo ƒe mɔfiafia te be woate ŋudzɔlawo kpɔ eye woable wo ade woƒe nudzodzo nuvɔ̃wo me si me wotsɔ wo ɖokuiwo bla ɖe wo ŋutɔwo ƒe fiƒode ŋu tegbee elabena woƒo fi na wo nɔewo le wo dome. Esia dzɔ le Yared ƒe ŋkekewo me eye woƒe ŋunyɔnuwo, woƒe Nefilimwo gɔ̃ hã kaka ɖe anyigba dzi le woƒe xexeame katã ƒe fiaɖuƒe me le woƒe sikaɣi si woyɔna la me.

Eye ŋudzɔlawo kple woƒe dzidzimeviwo gblẽ anyigba la kple nu siwo katã le eme eye wowɔ lãwo ƒe nɔnɔme tɔxɛ vovovowo eye wosɔ gbɔ ɖe anyigba dzi elabena wowɔ nuvɔ̃ ɖe ŋutilãwo katã ŋu eye woƒe nudzodzrowo menya seɖoƒe o, eye wowɔ wo ɖokuiwo abe mawuwo ene, mawu siwo wɔa nu gɔ̃ hã gake le nyateƒe me la womanye naneke o dzro ame siwo wɔa alakpa siwo kpɔ gome le lã vovovowo ƒe dzidzime tsakatsaka me. Eye nenema anyigba la gblẽ bliboe eye Nefilimwo, ame siwo ɖu amewo kple amewo ƒe nuwo la ɖu edzi. Eye ame siwo katã susɔ siwo ŋu kɔ le Noa ƒe ɣeyiɣia me (elabena Noa nye ame dzɔdzɔe kple anukwaretɔ eye wòzɔ kple Yehowa eye wòkpɔ amenuveve le Yehowa ŋkume) la nye luʋɔ enyi siwo woxɔ ɖe agbe kple lã siwo Yehowa kplɔ va Aɖaka la me na woƒe ƒomeviwo ƒe ɖeɖekpɔkpɔ. Eye nenema Mawu ɖo tsi anyigba la dzi eye gbegblẽ katã tso xexe si nɔ anyi ɣemaɣi la me tsrɔ̃, eye ŋudzɔlawo mekpɔ ŋutifafa, alo tsɔtske o eye woƒe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ sesẽ la anɔ anyi tegbee kple yi ɖe mavɔ me elabena wodze. Eye menye ŋudzɔlawo ko o, ke ame siwo katã kpe ɖe Satanawo ƒe aglãdzedze ŋu la akpe fu le mɔ sia nu eye woaɖo teƒe na wo tegbee le dzoka dzĩ kple dzokpe ta gã ma si me tohe dzodzoetɔ kple kɔkɔetɔ tɔna le la me.

Le tsiɖɔɖɔa megbe la, Mawu ɖo xexe si li fifia anyi, eɖoe anyi gbã le dzɔdzɔenyenye me. Eye Yehowa bla nu kple Noa, afi si Yehowa kpe Noa be wòafɔ dzɔdzɔenyenye ɖe tsitre le anyigba dzi eye wòadzi asɔ gbɔ eye wòayɔ anyigba la dzi be nyɔnu la ƒe dzidzimevi si ŋugbe wodo la nava. Yehowa do ŋugbe be yemagaɖo tsi anyigba la dzi akpɔ o eye togbɔ be amegbetɔ ƒe nɔnɔme vɔ̃ɖi le anyi hã la, Mawu wɔ dɔ kple amegbetɔwo be wòakpɔ dzɔdzɔenyenye ƒe dzidzimeviwo ta eye wòayra wo le anyigba dzi si me nye Yeshua HaMashiach medo tso.

Mawu yra wo be woádzi asɔ gbɔ eye woáyɔ anyigba la dzi, eye Mawu do ŋugbe na amegbetɔ kple lã siaa be yemagatsrɔ̃ ŋutilãwo katã ɖa le anyigba dzi kple tsi akpɔ o eye El Shaddai magaɖo tsi anyigba bliboa dzi akpɔ o gake adzra anyigba la ɖo, eye wòadzra amegbetɔ kple eƒe dzidzimeviwo ɖo, eye wodzra xexe ma ke ɖo tso nubabla ŋkeke ma dzi esime Kekeliwo ƒe Fofo tsɔ eƒe anyiŋɔ ɖo dziƒowo abe dzesi kple ŋkuɖodzinu na eƒe nubabla kple Noa ene. Elabena wotsɔa lɔlɔ̃nu faa zua manyalawo le esia ŋu, be to Mawu ƒe nya la me la dziƒowo nɔ anyi tso gbe aɖe gbe xoxoxo, eye anyigba do tso tsi me eye wòle tsi me: Eya ta xexe si nɔ anyi ɣemaɣi la, esi tsi ɖɔ ɖe edzi la, etsrɔ̃: Gake dziƒowo kple anyigba, siwo li fifia la, to nya ma ke me wodzra wo ɖo, wodzra wo ɖo na dzo hena ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ kple ame mawumavɔ̃lawo ƒe tsɔtsrɔ̃ gbe.

Eye nenema Yehowa fɔ Abram ɖe tsitre be wòazu Abraham, eye wòdo ŋugbe na Abram be eƒe ŋkɔ maganye Abram o ke boŋ Abraham eye be yeawɔe dukɔ geɖewo Fofo eye be yeatsɔ Kanaan nyigba la ana Abraham kple eƒe dzidzimeviwo wòanye anyigba mavɔ eye be Yehowa anye woƒe Mawu! Eye nenema Abraham zu dukɔ geɖewo Fofo, si ŋugbe Yehowa do le nubabla me. Azɔ, wodo ŋugbe na Abraham kple eƒe dzidzimevi. Megblɔ be, Kple dzidzimeviwo, abe ame geɖewo tɔ ene o; ke abe ame ɖeka tɔ ene, Kple wò dzidzimevi, si nye Kristo. Eye nenema ame dzɔdzɔe la anɔ agbe to xɔse me!

Eye nenema viwo ge ɖe kluvinyenye me le Egipte esi ŋugbedodoviwo yi afi ma le Yosef ƒe ametakpɔkpɔ kple nunana te elabena Yosef ɖu fia le dzɔdzɔenyenye me le Farao si lolo wu Yosef le fiazikpui la dzi te, gake Yosef subɔ NYE SI NYE gã la! Farao aɖe fɔ si menya Yosef o, eye gbe vɔ̃ siawo te Kristo ƒe dzidzimeviwo ɖe anyi eye wodze agbagba be yewoawu wo ake, ŋugbedodoviwo gɔ̃ hã. Eye Yehowa fɔ Hebri ɖela aɖe tso Farao ƒe aƒe me, ame si wohe tso tsi me eye Yehowa ƒe amenuveve na wòtsi agbe. Mose ŋutɔe akplɔ ŋugbedodoviwo tso kluvinyenye me ayi ŋugbedodo nyigba la ƒe ablɔɖe gã la me, elabena wonye ŋugbedodoviwo eye mafɔ wo tso woƒe alɔ̃ me eye maɖo teƒe na nuteƒewɔlawo kple ame dzɔdzɔewo geɖe, ame siwo wɔ nuteƒe va se ɖe nuwuwu gɔ̃ hã. Eye nenema Mose ɖe Israel viwo tso wo tetedziɖulawo si me eye ame siwo katã ti wo yome la yrɔ ɖe atsiaƒu la me, elabena ame kae ate ŋu anɔ tsitre ɖe El Shaddai ŋu?

Ale Yehowa kplɔ Mose kple ŋugbedodoviwo yi Sinai To la dzi afisi Yehowa tsɔ mɔfiame, se kple ɖoɖo siwo le sewo me la na Mose be viwo katã naɖo to. Eye woɖo yayra kple fiƒode ɖi na toɖoɖo kple tomaɖomaɖo. Eye esi Yehowa tsɔ se siwo le ɖoɖowo me na Mose le nubabla me be woaɖo to vɔ la, Mose lɔ̃ ɖe nubabla la dzi wòzu Nubabla yeye to ʋukɔkɔɖi me. Eye Mose tsɔ ʋua ƒe afã, kɔe ɖe agbawo me, eye wòhlẽ ʋua ƒe afã ɖe Vɔsamkpɔ la dzi. Eye wòtsɔ nubablagbalẽ la, xlẽe na dukɔ la wose: eye wogblɔ be, Nusiwo katã Aƒetɔ la gblɔ la, míanɔ ewɔm, eye míaɖo to. Eye Mose tsɔ ʋua hlẽ ɖe dukɔ la dzi, eye wògblɔ be, Kpɔ ɖa, Nubabla si Aƒetɔ la bla kpli mi, le nya siawo katã ŋuti la ƒe ʋue nye esi. Hekpe ɖe eŋu la, ehlẽ ʋu ɖe Avɔgbadɔ la kple Subɔsubɔdɔa ƒe dɔwɔnuwo katã dzi. Eye kloe nusiwo katã le Sea me la, ʋue wotsɔ kɔa wo ŋui: eye ne womekɔ ʋu ɖi o la, nuvɔ̃tsɔtsɔke aɖeke meli o.

Ale ŋutinya va yi abe alesi woŋlɔe ɖi ene, eye le ɣeyiɣi aɖe megbe la, woɖo Israel dukɔ la anyi bliboe, gake nɔƒe aɖeke menɔ anyi na Yehowa le dukɔ la dome haɖe o. Eye Yehowa fɔ Fia dzɔdzɔe aɖe ɖe tsitre, ŋutsu si nyo Mawu ŋutɔ ƒe dzi ŋu gɔ̃ hã! Le ɣeyiɣiawo ƒe yiyi me la, Yehowa dɔ Nyagblɔɖila Natan ɖe David gbɔ eye wògblɔ nɛ be ne wode wò ŋkekewo dzi, eye nàdɔ alɔ̃ kple fofowòwo la, mafɔ wò dzidzimevi ɖe tsitre ɖe megbewò, amesi ado tso wò dɔ me, eye maɖo eƒe fiaɖuƒe anyi. Atu xɔ na nye ŋkɔ, eye maɖo eƒe fiaɖuƒe ƒe fiazikpui la anyi tegbee. Manye fofoa, eye wòanye vinye. Eye woaɖo wò aƒe kple wò fiaɖuƒe anyi tegbee le ŋkuwòme: woaɖo wò fiazikpui anyi tegbee. Le nya siawo katã nu, eye le ŋutega sia katã nu la, nenemae Natan gblɔ na David.

Ale amegbetɔwo dze Yehowa yome tso nubabla me yi nubabla me, tsɔ kpe ɖe nubabla si wolɔ̃ ɖe edzi to lãwo tsɔtsɔ sa vɔe dzi, eye wotsɔ nubabla kpe ɖe nubabla ŋu. Gake, le amegbetɔ ƒe agbagbadzedzewo katã me la, mate ŋu anɔ agbe ɖe Mawu ƒe dzɔdzɔenyenye nu le eya ŋutɔ ƒe ŋutete me gbeɖe o, Mose ƒe se dzɔdzɔe kple nyateƒe ma gɔ̃ hã si melɔ̃ eye mewu enu. Ne ame aɖe yɔ Mose ƒe se la kple Yehowa ƒe nubabla geɖeawo be woƒo fi de wo la, nena woaƒo fi de eya hã. Eya ta le ɣeyiɣiwo ƒe yiyi blibo me la, wodzi nye, Yesu Nazaret tɔ la, na nyɔnu aɖe le Mose ƒe se la ƒe nudidiwo nu eye wònye nubablawo me vava, eye nyee nye nyɔnu la ƒe dzidzimevi si ŋugbe Yehowa ŋutɔ do le ƒe geɖe siwo va yi me. Elabena Mawu lɔ̃ xexeame ale gbegbe be wòtsɔ eƒe Tenuvi la na, be amesiame si axɔe ase la natsrɔ̃ o, ke boŋ wòakpɔ agbe mavɔ. Ale nyee nye Mawu ƒe Tenuvi la, ŋugbedodovi ma gɔ̃ hã si ŋu nyagblɔɖilawo gblɔ nya ɖi tso gbe aɖe gbe va ɖo fifia. Eye nyee nye amesi woɖo ɖi be wòawu nuwo katã nu eye le dzinye, Kristo Yesu me la, nuwo katã kple ŋugbedodowo katã ava eme eye womagblẽ ŋugbedodo aɖeke ɖi awɔmasee o, ke boŋ woawu wo katã nu tegbee.

Elabena woŋlɔe ɖe Hebritɔwo me afisi mena Paulo ŋlɔe ɖo be: Elabena ne nyitsuwo, kple gbɔ̃wo ƒe ʋu, kple nyivi ƒe afi si wotsɔ hlẽ ɖe ame makɔmakɔwo dzi, kɔa ame ŋu hena ŋutilã ƒe kɔkɔ la: Aleke gbegbe Kristo, amesi to Gbɔgbɔ mavɔ la dzi, tsɔ eɖokui sa vɔe na Mawu mokpe aɖeke manɔmee la ƒe ʋu, makɔ miaƒe dzitsinya ŋu tso dɔ kukuwo me, be miasubɔ Mawu gbagbe la o? Eye le esia ta eyae nye Nubabla Yeyea ƒe Avuléla, be to ku dzi, hena dzidada siwo nɔ Nubabla gbãtɔa te la ƒe xɔxɔ, amesiwo woyɔ la, nakpɔ domenyinu mavɔ ƒe ŋugbedodo la.

Gake ŋugbedodo siwo wòle be woava eme la gali eye fifiae nye emevava ƒe ɣeyiɣiwo. Elabena nenemae ɣeyiɣiawo le, afisi mafɔ amesiwo dɔ alɔ̃ le Kristo me la tso alɔ̃ me kpoyi, eye maƒo amesiwo le agbe eye wotsi anyi la nu ƒu kpoyi be woakpe kplim le alilikpowo me eye míawɔ ɖeka kpoyi le Yehowa ŋutɔ ƒe fiazikpuixɔ me. Elabena mi nye Hame kple nye ŋugbetɔsrɔ̃ gɔ̃ hã mienye nubabla ƒe ŋugbedodo la me vava eye wogbugbɔ mi dzi tso nuku magblẽmagblẽ me eye woxɔ mi to ŋugbedodo geɖe siwo le nye Nubabla Yeyea me, si nye nubabla yeye si ƒo nubabla xoxoawo ƒe ŋugbedodo ɖesiaɖe nu ƒu eye wògatsɔ ŋugbedodo gã kple xɔasi ŋutɔwo kpe ɖe eŋu siwo dzi woto xɔ mi na agbe mavɔ kple fetu mavɔ eye míanɔ anyi ɖekae tegbee.

Mise egɔme be agbe siwo mienɔ le ŋutilã me, mi nye nuteƒewɔlawo kple tiatiawo gɔ̃ hã, agbe mawo wu enu le miaƒe xɔxɔ bliboa me. Mise egɔme be miewɔ ɖeka kplim, Kristo Yesu, bliboe le go ɖesiaɖe me. Esi woheam ɖe atitsoga ŋu ɖe miaƒe nuvɔ̃wo ta la, wohe mi hã ɖe atitsoga ŋu kplim. Esi meku ɖe ati kpo ma dzi la, mi hã mieku kplim. Esi wofɔm ɖe agbe le ŋkeke etɔ̃ kple zã etɔ̃ megbe le anyigba ƒe dzi me la, nenema kee wofɔ mi hã ɖe agbe yeye me, eye esi medze dzi yi dziƒowo ƒe dziƒo la, nenema kee miedze dzi kplim eye ale miebɔbɔ nɔ anyi le dziƒonutowo me. Elabena nyee nye miaƒe Ŋutitoto bliboa, eye nyee nye miaƒe teƒenɔla si xɔ miaƒe teƒe le ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ kple tohehe si dze mi la me. Eye nye, Yesu Nazaret tɔ la, melɔ̃ faa xɔ mi tso se la ƒe fiƒode me elabena le miaƒe nuvɔ̃ŋutilã ta la, miate ŋu awu se la nu le mia ŋutɔ miaƒe agbagbadzedzewo me gbeɖe o.

Ŋugbedodo ɖesiaɖe le vava ge bliboe le dzinye Kristo Yesu me eye made srɔ̃ɖeɖe ƒe nubabla me kple nye Ŋugbetɔsrɔ̃ be woaɖu fia kplim le nye domenyinu la me eye woato nye mɔfiafia dzi kplim, ada gbe le anyigba la ŋu eye woado go aɖu fia abe nunɔlawo kple fiawo ene le anyigba dzi eye nubabla ɖesiaɖe ava eme tso Adam dzi yi Mose dzi le Yesu Kristo ƒe Mo. Esiawoe nye agbe mavɔ ƒe ŋugbedodo siwo ava eme na ame dzɔdzɔewo, amesiwo katã wogbugbɔ wɔ abe ame yeye ɖeka ene le Kristo Yesu me gɔ̃ hã. Elabena woagbugbɔ wo katã awɔ bliboe eye woaɖo eteƒe na wo tegbee ɖe woƒe gome ta eye wo katã anye ẽ kple amen. Elabena yod ɖesiaɖe, kple yod ƒe ŋù ɖesiaɖe ava eme bliboe eye wòanɔ anyi tegbee.

Mise egɔme be mienye Mawu gbagbe la ƒe gbedoxɔ, nye Hame nuteƒewɔla eye mewɔ mi abe aƒe si wometsɔ asi wɔe o ene be mianye Mawu mavɔ la, Yehowa mia Fofo gɔ̃ hã ƒe nɔƒe mavɔ. Eye maɖe nye ŋugbetɔsrɔ̃ eye manɔ wo gbɔ tegbee le nubabla mavɔ me, be afisi mele la, woawo hã woanɔ afima, eye woanɔ abe nye, Kristo Yesu woƒe Aƒetɔ mavɔ la ene tegbee. Eye mise egɔme nye nuteƒewɔlawo be mama ƒe ɣeyiɣiwo le nu wum eye mama aɖeke maganɔ Yehowa kple via lɔlɔ̃awo dome o eye mama aɖeke maganɔ Kristo Yesu miaƒe Aƒetɔ mavɔ la kple amesiwo melɔ̃ vevie la dome o. Mise egɔme be míazɔ abe ame ɖeka ene eye nuvɔ̃ alo mɔxenu aɖeke manɔ anyi o. Miawɔ dɔ gã geɖe siwo meva amegbetɔwo ƒe dzi me kpɔ gɔ̃ hã o. Mianye nye fia kple nunɔla gãwo siwo me nyee menye Fiawo ƒe Fia kple Aƒetɔwo ƒe Aƒetɔ eye míaɖu anyigba la dzi le dzɔdzɔenyenye me.

Eye woafɔ ame geɖe wu ɖe agbe le Daniel ƒe kwasiɖa blaadre lia ƒe nuwuwu eye woaɖo eteƒe na wo katã le alesi woƒe nuteƒewɔwɔ nɔ nu. Eye amesiwo katã ku le nuteƒewɔwɔ me le mɔkpɔkpɔ me be woƒe Aƒetɔ kple Mesia magblẽ woƒe luʋɔwo ɖi le yɔdo me o la anyɔ ɖe agbe yeye kple gbugbɔgaɖoanyi mavɔ me. Elabena mado ƒome kple wo lɔlɔ̃tɔwo eye magaɖo nusiwo ʋetsuviwo ɖu la teƒe na wo eye magaɖo nusiwo Satana fi le wo si le ɣeyiɣi siwo me wonye amedzrowo le anyigba dzi lala woƒe xɔxɔ blibo tso xexe gbegblẽ kple vɔ̃ɖi sia me la teƒe na wo.

Eye nenemae nyanyui mavɔ la le eye nuwo katã ava eme le dzɔdzɔenyenye, kɔkɔenyenye, kple nyateƒe me tegbee. Elabena naneke si ŋu kɔkɔe mele o alo vɔ̃ɖi alo lɔ̃a aʋatso hewɔnɛ la mage ɖe nye fiaɖuƒea ƒe agbowo me o ke boŋ nye fiaɖuɖu anɔ nyateƒe kple dzɔdzɔenyenye mavɔ me. Eya ta mise Yeshua HaMashiach ƒe gbe, amesiwo katã woɖo nya siawo ɖo ɖe wo gɔ̃ hã. Miaƒe Aƒetɔ mavɔ la meŋlɔ Blema ŋugbedodowo be o eye nubabla aɖeke mezu dzodzro o. Ke boŋ wo katã ava eme eye le nyateƒe me la, geɖe va eme xoxo eya ta mikpɔ nu mawo mɔ na mɔkpɔkpɔ.
