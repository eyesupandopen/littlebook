Ðasefo Eve: Hame La
Le Yohanes Nyaɖela la ƒe Dɔwɔwɔwo me
Damɛ 20, 2024

Na 144,000 la: Mise nye Yɔyɔ!

Oo mise, Oo mise, nye to wuieveawo, miaƒe Mesia, Aƒetɔ, Fia kple Nunɔlagã la ƒe gbe, nyee nye Yeshua HaMashiach, Amegbetɔvi si Yahweh fɔ ɖe tsitre tso ame kukuwo dome. Elabena:

Nyee nye miaƒe Mesia vavã la, ame si dzɔ tso David ƒe fiazikpui la me, eye tso eƒe fiazikpui la dzi ye maɖu Ŋugbedodonyigba la dzi na Yahweh, Mawu mavɔ la, elabena nenemae wòdo ŋugbe na mia fofowo. Eya ta mise egɔme be nyee nye Yuda-to la ƒe Dzata la, eye wometsɔ fia alo nyagblɔɖila aɖeke tso Dan, afi si aʋatsonyagblɔɖila la atso la o.

Nyee nye miaƒe Aƒetɔ, elabena Yahweh wɔm Aƒetɔwo ƒe Aƒetɔ, eye meƒle mi kple nye ŋutɔ nye ʋu abe miaƒe Ŋutitoto vɔ blibo ene, si wotsɔ na ɖe Trɔ̃subɔlawo kple Yudeatɔwo siaa ƒe nu vɔ̃wo ta, be mi ame evea mianye ame yeye ɖeka le menye, si nye Kristo Yesu. Elabena Yahweh tsɔ ŋkɔ si ƒo ŋkɔwo katã ta nam eye wòna mekpɔ gome le eƒe fiazikpui la me le nye toɖoɖo ta. Eya ta wona nye Aƒetɔnyenye abe xɔxɔ ƒe dzesi ene, elabena wowɔm gbɔngbɔn si naa agbe, eye Yahweh ɖeka koe lolo wum, elabena eyae nye Mawu mavɔ la.

Nyee nye miaƒe Fia, elabena Yahweh wɔm Fiawo ƒe Fia, eye maɖu anyigba la dzi le dzɔdzɔenyenye me. Eye kpɔ ɖa! Mekplɔ nye Ŋugbetɔ, ame si aɖu fia kplim la vɛ, elabena mewɔ wo fiawo kple nunɔlawo le Yahweh mavɔ la ŋkume, ame si tɔgbi aɖeke meli o. Elabena le nye ƒe akpe ɖeka ƒe fiaɖuɖu si ŋugbe medo la me la, manye ŋusẽtɔ kãkãtɔ, eye madɔ nye fiawo kple nunɔlawo be woaɖu fia le woƒe domenyinu me le menye; elabena megawɔ wo yeyee le menye, wotso Yudeatɔwo kple Trɔ̃subɔlawo siaa me eye wowɔ wo ame yeye ɖeka, wogawɔ wo le blibonyenye me. Menye ɖeka le ɖekawɔwɔ kple taɖodzinu me kple Fofonye, Yahweh, eye le eƒe fiazikpui la me la, elolo wum.

Nyee nye miaƒe nunɔlagã tegbee ɖe Melkizedek ƒe ɖoɖo nu, eye metsɔ nye ŋutɔ nye avuléʋu sa vɔe esi metsɔ ɖokuinye na abe miaƒe Ŋutitoto vɔ blibo kple deblibo ene. Elabena Hebritɔwo ɖi ɖase esime mena Apostolo Paulo ƒo nu na wo le tanye (etso Benyamin): Aleke gbegbe Kristo, ame si to Gbɔngbɔn mavɔ la dzi tsɔ eɖokui na Mawu mokakape maɖimaɖii la ƒe ʋu, aklɔ miaƒe dzitsinya ŋu tso dɔ kukuwo me be miasubɔ Mawu gbagbe lae wu? Eya ta nye ɖokuinye tsɔtsɔ sa vɔe la nye zi ɖeka ɖe ɣeyiɣi katã kple tegbee ta, eye Mesia bubu aɖeke meli o.

Togbɔ be Satana ƒe ƒuƒoƒo la axɔ woƒe aʋatsomesia kple aʋatsonyagblɔɖila si hã nye lã wɔadã gbedoxɔtula (elabena eyae nye lã wɔadã si tso anyigba la me). Elabena miene Mawu gbagbe la ƒe gbedoxɔ, eye hiã megali be woasa lãwo vɔ gbeɖe o, elabena nyee nye vɔsa mamlɛtɔ, Ŋutitoto vɔ blibo, nuvɔ̃manɔmee la, eye nye dɔwɔwɔwo de blibo eye wole blibo. Eya ta, hiã megali na nu sia tɔgbi gbeɖe o, negbe ɖeko miatsɔ miaƒe nu vɔ̃wo ava gbɔnye le dzimetɔtrɔ me, eye maklɔ mi tso miaƒe ŋutilã ƒe dɔ kukuwo me. Elabena woŋlɔe ɖe Romatɔwo me be: Elabena agbe Gbɔngbɔn la ƒe se le Kristo Yesu me la ɖem tso nu vɔ̃ kple ku ƒe se la me. Elabena nenemae meɖe mi le menye.

Mise egɔme, nye towo kple nye dukɔ, be Trɔ̃subɔlawo ƒe ɣeyiɣiwo le vɔvɔm. Trɔ̃subɔlawo ƒe ɣeyiɣiwo va to mia fofowo ƒe dzi sesẽ me. Elabena nya kae Paulo gblɔ le Dɔwɔwɔwo me le nya la ŋu: Ehiã be woagblɔ Mawu ƒe nya la na mi gbã: gake esi mitsɔe ƒu gbe, eye mibu mia ɖokuiwo be miemedze na agbe mavɔ o ta la, kpɔ ɖa, míetrɔ ɖe Trɔ̃subɔlawo ŋu.

Meɖo aɖaŋu na mi, nye dukɔ, migawɔ vodada mawo ke o eye miganɔ agbe le mia fofowo ƒe nu vɔ̃wo me o, ke boŋ mitrɔ dzi me tso mia ŋutɔ miaƒe nu vɔ̃wo me eye mitrɔ dzi me tso mia fofowo ƒe nu vɔ̃wo hã me, eye miva gbɔnye eye maxɔ mi, elabena mienye ŋugbedodo viwo. Eye woava me na yod alo yod ƒe ŋù ɖe sia ɖe; elabena nu si ŋugbe Yahweh do la, manya trɔ o, elabena manya wɔ be Yahweh naka aʋatso o. Eya ta, nyee nye ŋugbedodo geɖewo, geɖewo me vava, elabena nyee nye miaƒe Mesia vavã la, Mawu mavɔ la ƒe nɔnɔme ŋutɔ. Elabena nyee nye via mɔdzi ɖeka la, Mawu ƒe Alẽvi si Yohanes Mawutsidetsila fia asi ɣeyiɣiwo katã. Elabena egblɔ be: Kpɔ Mawu ƒe Alẽvi si tsɔa xexeame ƒe nu vɔ̃ dzonɛ la ɖa!

Eya ta metrɔ ɖe dukɔwo ŋu elabena nye ŋutɔ nye anyigba gbe nyem, le esi meku ɖe woƒe nu vɔ̃wo ta eye wofɔm ɖe tsitre megbe gɔ̃ hã. Elabena medɔ nye apostolowo kple nyagblɔɖilawo ɖe wo gbɔ, eye woɖe ame geɖewo. Elabena le amenuveve ƒe ɣeyiɣia ƒe gɔmedzedze la, medɔ wo ɖe Gbedoxɔa kple ƒuƒoƒowo me, eye ame geɖewo trɔ ɖe ŋunye eye woxɔm be menyen yewoƒe Aƒetɔ. Eya ta mele mi yɔm be woawɔ mi blibo le nye Aƒetɔnyenye me, elabena meƒle mi na Fofonye be miawo hã mianye viawo to nye gbɔngbɔn si matsɔ ade dzesi mi la dzi. Eya ta Trɔ̃subɔlawo ƒe ɣeyiɣiwo vɔ, elabena azɔ la, woawo hã gbe nyem hena Yesu bubu si wosubɔna abe mawu ene la, ale wowɔ wo ɖokuiwo trɔ̃subɔlawoe; elabena Satana te wo kpɔ alea. Elabena Mawu ɖeka koe li, Yahweh mavɔ la, eye Mesia vavã ɖeka koe li, nyee, Yeshua HaMashiach.

Elabena wobu nye ŋaɣinyawo, woŋe aɖaba ƒu nye ŋutegawo kple nye ŋɔŋlɔ kɔkɔea ƒe taɖodzinuwo dzi. Wobu Fofo la ƒe xɔxɔ ƒe ɖoɖoa, eye wotsɔ dada gbe nye nya siwo mena woŋlɔ ɖe Romatɔwo me la. Elabena Paulo gblɔ be: Eya ta megblɔ be, Ðe Mawu nyã eƒe dukɔ laa? Mawu nedzɔe. Elabena nye hã Israel-vi menye, tso Abraham ƒe dzidzime me, tso Benyamin ƒe to la me. Mawu menyã eƒe dukɔ si wònya do ŋgɔ la o. Eye ake: Nu kae azɔ? Israel mekpɔ nu si dim wòle la o; ke tiatia la kpɔe, eye wodo ŋku na ame susɔeawo. Eya ta tiatia la nɔ kãkãkã na ame siwo metia tso towo me; elabena nye apostolowo katã tso anyigba la kple ŋugbedodo la me, eya ta medɔ wo ɖe dukɔwo me, si ŋu miaƒe subɔsubɔhakplɔlawo kaa aʋatso le busunya me, yɔa wo be aʋatsotɔwo. Kplɔla aʋatsotɔ mawo ke kple kplɔla dzodzrowo gbea nye tsitretsitsi gɔ̃ hã, elabena wofɔm ɖe tsitre tso ame kukuwo dome, eye nyemegakuna tegbee o.

Elabena nya bubu kae Romatɔwo gblɔ le nya la ŋu: David gblɔ be, Woƒe kplɔ̃ nezu mɔ̃, kple mɔ̃tetre, kple nukikli, kple teƒeɖoɖo na wo: Wona woƒe ŋkuwo nado viviti be woagakpɔ nu o, eye nabɔbɔ woƒe dzime ɖa ɣesiaɣi. Eya ta megblɔ be, Ðe wokli nu be woagea? Mawu nedzɔe: ke boŋ to woƒe anyigedzedze me la, xɔxɔ va Trɔ̃subɔlawo gbɔ, be wòaʋã ŋu wo.

Elabena nyemedi o, nɔviwo, be mianye manyamanyawo le ŋaɣinya sia ŋu o, be miaganye nunyalawo le mia ŋutɔ miaƒe susuwo me o; be ŋkudodo va Israel dzi le akpa aɖe me, va se ɖe esime Trɔ̃subɔlawo ƒe blibonyenye nava ge ɖe eme. Eya ta megblɔ be Trɔ̃subɔlawo ƒe blibonyenye va, eye woagbugbɔ Mawu ƒe ŋaɣinyawo ana Israel hena xɔse ƒe toɖoɖo. Elabena magaɖe Yakob, eye ame susɔe nuteƒewɔlawo atrɔ ɖe ŋunye nuteƒewɔwɔtɔe, le xɔse gã me!

Eya ta meva be maʋã ŋu le miaƒe dziwo me ɖe ŋunye, Yeshua HaMashiach, kple Yahweh Mawu gã mavɔ si dɔm ɖa, ame si miemegasubɔna le sidzedze nu o, elabena ŋkudodo ƒe nutsyɔnu atsyɔ ame sia ame si dzea Mose ƒe se la yome la dzi. Elabena nya kae megblɔ na Korintotɔwo le nya la ŋu: Eye menye abe Mose, ame si tsɔ nutsyɔnu tsyɔ eƒe mo, be Israel-viwo nagakpɔ nu si woɖe ɖa la ƒe nuwuwu tẽe o ene o: Ke woƒe susuwo do ŋku: elabena va se ɖe egbe la, nutsyɔnu ma ke gakpɔtɔ li wometsɔe ɖa le Nubabla Xoxoa xexlẽ me o; nutsyɔnu si woɖe ɖa le Kristo me. Gake va se ɖe egbe, ne woxlẽ Mose la, nutsyɔnu la le woƒe dzi dzi.

Gake, ne etrɔ ɖe Aƒetɔ la ŋu la, woaɖe nutsyɔnu la ɖa.

Elabena mewu Mose ƒe se la nu, elabena mia kple mia fofowo dometɔ aɖeke mate ŋu awɔ edzi pɛpɛpɛ o le nu vɔ̃ si le miaƒe ʋu me ta. Elabena nye, Yeshua HaMashiach, mewu se la nu bliboe, eye xɔse le menye, Yesu Kristo, me koe woate ŋu adzra se la ɖo pɛpɛpɛ. Wobu mi dzɔdzɔetɔwoe to miaƒe ɖokuitsɔtsɔ na kplim bliboe me, eya ta ame dzɔdzɔe la anɔ agbe to xɔse me. Eya ta maɖe nutsyɔnu la ɖa le miaƒe dziwo dzi be miate ŋu akpɔm, Yeshua, miaƒe Mesia vavã la, elabena nyemegblẽ mi ɖi o! Eye meva mia gbɔ le nubabla yeyea me, menye ŋɔŋlɔa ƒe xoxonyenye me o. Elabena medo kpe nye nubabla yeyea dzi le nye ŋutɔ nye ʋu me, le Yahweh ƒe lɔlɔ̃nu kple ŋugbedodowo nu, hena miaƒe xɔxɔ blibo. Eye makplɔ mi ayi miaƒe domenyinu me, elabena Mawu ƒe ŋugbedodowo nye ẽ kple amen. Yod alo yod ƒe ŋù aɖeke mado kpo nu o, elabena nuwo katã le vava ge kpuie; eye le ɣeyiɣi nyuitɔ me la, vavã, mawu nuwo katã nu, elabena nenemae Yahweh dɔm be mawu enu.

Eya ta Trɔ̃subɔlawo trɔ le ŋunye abe avu si trɔ ɖe eƒe xe ŋu ene, eye ɣeyiɣia de be woagblẽ wo ɖi na wo. Elabena le nye amenuveve ƒe ɣeyiɣia me la, ame ɖeka aɖeke si Fofonye tia na xɔxɔ la meto vɔ o, eye ame aɖeke mate ŋu ahe wo atso nye asi me o. Elabena ame siwo ʋu nye, Yesu, me be menyen yewoƒe Aƒetɔ, kple xɔse le woƒe dziwo me be Mawu fɔm ɖe tsitre tso ame kukuwo dome la, wotsɔ ŋugbedodo ƒe gbɔngbɔn kɔkɔe la de dzesi wo, eye Fofonye ƒe taɖodzinuwo va me le menye na wo. Eye nenemae woanɔ kplim tegbee.

Elabena amititi la ɖea nyateƒe fiana. Eye nya kae megblɔ tso amititi la ŋu le Paulo ƒe lɛta si wòŋlɔ na Romatɔwo me: Eye ne woŋe alɔawo dometɔ aɖewo ɖa, eye wò, esi nènye gbe me amititi la, wotsɔ wò do ɖe wo dome, eye nèkpɔ gome le amititi la ƒe ke kple dami me kpli wo; megaƒo adegbe ɖe alɔawo ŋu o. Gake ne èƒo adegbe la, menye wòe tsɔ ke la o, ke boŋ ke lae tsɔ wò.

Eya ta wowɔ vodadawo eye wotsɔ mia fofowo ƒe nu vɔ̃wo eye wotrɔ le ŋunye yi woƒe trɔ̃wo gbɔ, eye womagase nye gbe o. Togbɔ be ne woyɔ Aƒetɔ la ƒe ŋkɔ la, woaxɔ ame siwo melɔ̃ woƒe agbe va se ɖe nuwuwu o la.

Elabena nya bubu kae Romatɔwo ƒe ɖaseɖiɖi gblɔ le ŋɔŋlɔa me abe nuxlɔ̃ame na dukɔwo ene: Ekema àgblɔ be, Woŋe alɔawo ɖa, be woatsɔm ado ɖe eme. Enyo; dzimaxɔse ta woŋe wo ɖa, eye wò la, èle tsitre to xɔse me. Megado wò ɖokui ɖe dzi o, ke boŋ vɔ̃: Elabena ne Mawu mekpɔ nublanui na dzɔdzɔme alɔawo o la, kpɔ nyuie be magakpɔ nublanui na wò hã o. Eya ta kpɔ Mawu ƒe dɔmenyonyo kple sesẽ ɖa: ɖe ame siwo dze anyi la dzi, sesẽ; ke ɖe ŋuwò la, dɔmenyonyo, ne èyi edzi le eƒe dɔmenyonyo me: ne menye nenema o la, woatso wò hã ɖa. Eye woawo hã, ne wometsi dzimaxɔse me o la, woatsɔ wo ado ɖe eme: elabena Mawu ate ŋu agatsɔ wo ado ɖe eme ake. Elabena ne wotso wò le amititi si nye gbe metɔ le dzɔdzɔme nu me, eye wotsɔ wò do ɖe amititi nyui aɖe me si tsi tre ɖe dzɔdzɔme ŋu: aleke gbegbe woatsɔ ame siawo, siwo nye dzɔdzɔme alɔwo la, ado ɖe wo ŋutɔ woƒe amititi me wue?

Elabena nyemedi o, nɔviwo, be mianye manyamanyawo le ŋaɣinya sia ŋu o, be miaganye nunyalawo le mia ŋutɔ miaƒe susuwo me o; be ŋkudodo va Israel dzi le akpa aɖe me, va se ɖe esime Trɔ̃subɔlawo ƒe blibonyenye nava ge ɖe eme. Eya ta woaɖe Israel katã: abe ale si woŋlɔe ɖi ene be, Ðela la atso Zion ava, eye wòaɖe mawumavɔ̃mavɔ̃ ɖa le Yakob gbɔ.

Eya ta, nye ƒometɔwo, Trɔ̃subɔlawo ƒe ɣeyiɣiwo le vɔvɔm, eye tɔtrɔ gã aɖe le edzi yim, afi si nye amenuveve atrɔ azu ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ enumake. Eye fifia gɔ̃ hã woɖe mɔ nuwo le nɔƒe ɖom to Satana ƒe asi me, elabena awɔ eƒe tsitretsitsi, togbɔ be woagbãe gudugudu hã, eye magbã eƒe fiaɖuƒe la gbidigbidi; elabena menye fia o.

Eya ta azɔ ɣeyiɣia de be woagbugbɔ dzɔdzɔme alɔawo ado ɖe amititi la me, elabena Yahweh ƒe taɖodzinuwo va me le amenuveve kple nublanuikpɔkpɔ si medze o si woɖe fia Trɔ̃subɔlawo me (elabena Mawu ƒe amenuveve kple nublanuikpɔkpɔ medze na amegbetɔ aɖeke o), elabena wozã amenuveve kple nublanuikpɔkpɔ ma gɔ̃ hã tsɔ di Israel ƒe aƒe la ƒe alẽ siwo bu la. Elabena mele be nànye manyamanya le wò ŋutɔ wò ŋutinya ŋu o, ne ètsɔ wò busu Babilontɔwo ƒe Talmudwo ƒu anyi eye nèsrɔ̃ nu tso ŋunye; elabena nyemedɔ agbalẽ ma ɖa o, eye ehiã be nàtrɔ dzi me tso nufiafia mawo me eye nàgbe Satana ƒe aʋatsowo. Trɔ ɖe nye Mawu ƒe nya dzadzɛ la ŋu eye nàdzo le mia fofowo ƒe nu vɔ̃wo me, elabena maɖe wò ne àse nye gbe ko.

Elabena womedɔm ɖa o negbe ɖe Israel ƒe aƒe la ƒe alẽ siwo bu la gbɔ ko. Eya ta woka to ewoawo hlẽ ɖe dukɔwo dome tso woƒe aboyome me to Asiriatɔwo dzi ɣeyiɣi siawo katã me. Wotsa le anyigba dzi abe mɔzɔlawo kple amedzrowo ene. Tso Asiria woyi dziehe nyigbawo dzi, woyi Skitia, kple Europa, eye mlɔeba woyi Amerika, afi si mewɔ wo nɔƒe fafae na wo hena ɣeyiɣi aɖe. Eye meyɔ ame sia ame si ate ŋu atso dukɔwo me ava nye xɔxɔ gbɔ. Woɖe Israel-vi geɖewo gɔ̃ hã eye woɖe wo kpakple ame geɖewo tso dukɔwo me, ame siwo ne menye nenema o la, mɔkpɔkpɔ aɖeke manɔ wo si o. Eye ameha gã aɖe ɖo eŋu, eya ta Trɔ̃subɔlawo ƒe ɣeyiɣiwo vɔ, elabena ɣeyiɣi aɖeke megali o. Gake mise egɔme be ame sia ame si ayɔ Aƒetɔ la ƒe ŋkɔ la, woaɖee ne wowɔ nuteƒe va se ɖe nuwuwu, elabena maxɔ wo, dukɔ si me wotso o, meɖale eme o.

Eye nenemae wòle, be Yakob ƒe xaxa va anyigba la kple dukɔwo dzi, elabena ɣeyiɣia de be ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ gã aɖe nadze anyigba la dzi. Eya ta lã wɔadãwo ato dzoka la ƒe ŋusẽ me afɔ; ɖeko megaxɔ eƒe dzesi o eye megadze eƒe aʋatsokakawo me o. Elabena etsɔ eɖokui de miaƒe dukɔa ƒe dziɖulawo, kple Satana ƒe ƒuƒoƒo la kple woƒe Edomtɔwo ƒe mɔwo gɔ̃ hã me xoxo. Eye egblɔa busunya gãwo xoxo, elabena wole edzram ɖo ɖe eƒe taɖodzinu vavãtɔ ŋu.

Elabena aƒedzodzo ƒe ŋunyɔnu la gbɔna. Elabena nya kae mena nye nuteƒewɔla Luka ŋlɔ ɖi na ɣeyiɣiwo katã: Eye ne miekpɔ aʋakɔwo ƒo xlã Yerusalem la, ekema minya be eƒe aƒedzodzo gogo. Ekema ame siwo le Yudea la nesi du ayi towo dzi; eye ame siwo le titinina la nedzo le eme; eye migana ame siwo le kɔƒenutowo me la nage ɖe eme o. Elabena hlãbiabia ƒe ŋkeke woe esiawo nye, be nu siwo katã woŋlɔ ɖi la nava me.

Eye nenemae wòanɔ le ɣeyiɣi nyuitɔ me, eye makpɔ tɔnyewo ta, elabena mele mia dɔm abe nye nyagblɔɖilawo, kplɔlawo, dãlawo, kple subɔlawo ene. Elabena mele mi yɔm na subɔsubɔdɔ, be miawu miaƒe yɔyɔ vavã si Yahweh ŋutɔ tsɔ do ŋugbe na fofoawo la nu, eye to nye asi me woava me. Elabena Yahweh ɖo nye zikpui ɖe eƒe fiazikpui la me, eye mema nye fiazikpui la kple nye Ŋugbetɔ, ame siwo le gbɔnye ɣesiaɣi. Eya ta ame aɖeke mele anyigba dzi si lolo wum o; miaƒe aʋatsonyagblɔɖilawo dometɔ aɖeke mesɔ kplim o, elabena wo katã wogbɔdzɔ eye wonye hiãtɔwo eye wodɔ wo be woaka aʋatso ahahe ame atra, elabena wonye kplɔla dzodzrowo.

Eya ta migana nyadɔdɔ la nate mi kpɔ o, ne menye nenema o la, miage ɖe ɖɔ la me eye woaku mi ɖe miaƒe glãkui me tegbee.

Mena ŋusẽ mi na nukunu gãwo. Eye mele mia dɔm ɖe mia nɔviwo gbɔ be miatrɔ wo ɖe ŋunye, elabena woagaɖe ame susɔeawo; eye maɖe Yakob tso woƒe futɔwo si me, elabena megblɔe eye nenemae woawɔe.

Nya geɖewo le asinye magblɔ na mi eye woagblɔ nya mawo, eye madzra mi ɖo ɖe miaƒe dɔdɔwo ŋu, eye miawɔ dɔ gãwo kple ŋusẽdɔwo nam, dɔ siwo mimesusu kpɔ be woanya wɔ o gɔ̃ hã.
