O Israel, Mele Yɔwòm!

Tso Yehowa, Mawu Gã Tegbeetɔ La Gbɔ:

Mise Yehowa, NYE SI MENYE la ƒe Nyawo, eye mibu nye biabia la ŋu. Nu ka ta afiwo sina le aƒe si le mu-mum la me, vinyewo? Ŋuɖoɖo si dze ƒãa ye nye be woatsi agbe, negbe afi siwo le sisi m tso Babilon Ɣaɣla la me be woaɣla woƒe nuvlowɔwɔwo, eye be woaha nu le eŋu keŋkeŋ ale be woakpɔ dziɖuɖu kpui si metsɔ na wo. Esi amegbetɔ dzɔdzɔetɔ lɔ̃a viviti ta la, matsɔ viviti si wòlɔ̃a vevie la anɛ azɔ. Ke hã, meŋlɔ (eye magaŋlɔ) nye nuwɔwɔwo katã ƒe dɔwɔwɔ ɖe sia ɖe si si tiatia ƒe ablɔɖe le la ɖi, elabena ame si wona nu geɖe la, woabia nu geɖe tso esi.

Eye naneke meɣla ɖe nye ŋkuwo o, eye naneke meɣla ɖe nye nukpɔkpɔ o, eye NYE ye nye ame si kpɔa, ŋlɔa, eye wòɖoa ŋku nu vɔ̃ɖi ɖe sia ɖe si wowɔ kpɔ le nye nuwɔwɔwo me la dzi. Eye naneke mate ŋu adzɔ si nyemenya o eye meɖe mɔ ɖe eŋu o! Le nye ŋkume la, wogbãa tamebubuwo katã ɖa. Le nye ŋkume la, woɖea dzi ƒe tameɖoɖo ɖe sia ɖe fiana eye naneke alo ame aɖeke meli si wowɔ alo wɔ si mele nye asi kpekpe la te o, elabena nyee nye dzɔdzɔenyenye ƒe Mawu Tegbeetɔ si mia kpli mi miewɔna, O Israel, eye mekpɔa mi keŋkeŋ, togbɔ be mietiae be miagbe toɖoɖom le ŋkuagbãnvĩ me le miaƒe dziwo ƒe kuto ta hã.

Elabena, esi meva mia gbɔ le Mesia ɖeka si medɔ ɖa kpɔ kple Mesia ɖeka si madɔ ɖa gbeɖe ƒe nɔnɔme me la, mia fofowo gbe m keŋkeŋ. Elabena, NYE ye nye ame si wɔ Yeshua HaMashiach (Yesu Kristo) le ŋutilã me hena ame siwo katã yɔa eƒe ŋkɔ kpekpe la ƒe xɔxɔ, ame si miegbea manyamanya me, eye mieyɔnɛ kple dada kple busunyawo be enye trɔ̃ na trɔ̃subɔlawo. O Israel, mia dometɔ geɖe tsɔ mia ɖokuiwo kpe ɖe Satana ƒe Ƒuƒoƒo la ŋu eye azɔ miele mɔ kpɔm na miaƒe aʋatso Moshiach si Esau dzra ɖo ɖe Yakob ƒe tsɔtsrɔ̃ ŋu.

Eya ta mise NYE SI MENYE GÃ la ƒe gbe, elabena nye gbe tegbee si le gbe ɖem abe dziŋgoli ene la le yɔwòm le ɣeyiɣi mamlɛ sia me eye medɔ Vinye ɖe mi ake be wòaɖe Yakob, eye ame susɔe siwo xɔ Vinye Tegbeetɔ, Yeshua HaMashiach la, woaxɔ wo ɖe agbe to eƒe asi kpekpe la me, eye tiatia bubu aɖeke mele xɔxɔ ŋu o. Tiatia bubu alo ŋkɔ bubu aɖeke hã manɔ anyi na xɔxɔ gbeɖe o elabena metsɔ ŋkɔ si ƒo ŋkɔwo katã ta la nɛ, be le Yesu Nazaret-tɔ la ƒe ŋkɔ me la, klo ɖe sia ɖe nadze anyi. Eye ame siwo gbe la, woana woadze anyi na Yuda-to la me Dzata si medɔ ɖe anyigba dzi be wòadrɔ̃ ʋɔnu eye wòawɔ aʋa le dzɔdzɔenyenye tegbee me, eye eya ye atsɔ nye nuwɔwɔwo katã aƒo ɖe dzɔdzɔenyenye te eye wòaɖu dzi kple gayati, eye ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃, amenuveve, kple nublanuikpɔkpɔ anɔ eŋu tegbee. Miese egɔme be mɔ si kplɔa ame yia tsɔtsrɔ̃ me la keke eye ŋkɔ geɖewo kple aʋatsomawuwo le didim be yewoakplɔ mi ayi yewoƒe domenyinu si nye dzo lɔ dze si nɔa bibim tegbee la me, si fiaa dzo kple dzokpe tohe dzo xɔdzo tɔxɛwo.

O Israel, aleke miegbã Yehowa ƒe dzi le miaƒe tomahãmahã me, eye nye dzi gale gbãgbãm ɖe mia ta tso tegbee siwo va yi afi si menya le nye ŋgɔgbenyanya me be le nye xɔxɔ ƒe ɖoɖo me la, mia Fofowo agbe Yesu Nazaret-tɔ la ɖe Baraba, ame si nye amewula kple aglãdzelawo ƒe kplɔla le anyigbawo dzi ɣemaɣi la ta. Eya ta mena wòtrɔ ɖe trɔ̃subɔlawo ŋu, eye wose eƒe nya hena ɣeyiɣi aɖe, ke hã ŋkuagbãnvĩ dzɔ ɖe Israel dzi be Israel ƒe ame susɔewo nava xɔxɔ me le ɣeyiɣi nyuitɔ dzi. Miese egɔme be ame geɖewo tso Yudea tsɔe wɔ woƒe Aƒetɔ eye wokpɔ xɔxɔ tegbee le Mawu ƒe Israel me eye woanɔ vinye gbɔ tegbee. Eye NYE ye nye ame si dɔ Yesu Nazaret-tɔ la ɖe dukɔwo dome be wòaɖe mia nɔvi siwo kaka ɖe duta le woƒe aglãdzedze ta la, kple ame siwo mɔkpɔkpɔ aɖeke menɔ na o gɔ̃ hã, eye ame geɖewo tso dukɔwo me kpɔ xɔxɔ tegbee eye azɔ wotsɔ wo do ɖe towo me tegbee. Eya ta miese egɔme O Israel be nu si NYE SI MENYE Tegbeetɔ la ŋtɔ kɔ ŋu la, migayɔe be nu dzro alo nu makɔmakɔ o!

Gake wò O Israel, ègbe vinye Yesu Kristo hewui eye nètsɔe de asi na nuvɔ̃mewo siwo ɖoe kplikpaa be yewoalée ɖe asi le eƒe fɔmaɖimaɖi me. Gake aleke nye xɔxɔ tegbee ƒe ɖoɖo la ate ŋu ava emee ne menye Yehowa ƒe se tegbee kple se siwo mate ŋu atrɔ o la ƒe nudidiwo ye wowɔ dzi o, se siwo meɖo ɖe nye nuwɔwɔwo katã ƒe ɖoɖo dzɔdzɔe si woaɖu dzi le dzɔdzɔenyenye deblibo me la ŋu. Eya ta Yesu Nazaret-tɔ la tsɔ eɖokui na abe Mawu ƒe Alẽvi ene abe miaƒe Nunɔlagã tegbee le Melkizedek ƒe ɖoɖo nu be wòaxe fe si Mose ɖo ɖi ɖe amegbetɔwo ƒe nuvɔ̃wo kple dzidadawo ta eye wòaɖe amegbetɔwo keŋkeŋ tso se la ƒe fiƒode me. Elabena Mose va abe Israel ƒe nufiala ene be wòaxɔ Israel ahadzrae ɖo ɖe Yeshua HaMashiach ŋu eye wòxe fe ɖe miaƒe xɔxɔ ta keŋkeŋ, eye togbɔ be mienye nusrɔ̃la aglãdzelawo hã la, ame susɔewo aɖo nye tohehe ŋu, kple xaxa si gbɔna mia dzi le Daniel ƒe kwasiɖa blaadre lia si le miaƒe ʋɔtru nu eye wòaʋu miaƒe ʋɔtruwo ɖe tiatia tegbee aɖe nu kpuie.

Eya ta miese ame si miegbe la gɔme, O Israel, ame si melɔ̃ kple lɔlɔ̃ tegbee, elabena mi ye nye ame siwo medo ŋugbe na mia Fofowo le nubabla geɖewo me le ɣeyiɣi siwo va yi me be le ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ ɣeyiɣi siawo me la, madzra mi ɖo ɖe wo ta, eye nyee nye Dzɔdzɔenyenye ƒe Mawu Tegbeetɔ ma ke si metrɔna o! Eye NYE ye nye AME SI ɖoa ŋku koko siwo mia Fofowo fa ɖe mia ta la dzi eye meɖoa ŋku kokoɖafa ɖe sia ɖe dzi nya ɖe nya nu eye made bubu woƒe biabia ŋu na mia dometɔ siwo lɔ̃ faa eye woɖoa to. Eye fifia gɔ̃ hã Vinye, ame si mielé fu manyamanya me la, le koko fam ɖe mia ta le eƒe lɔlɔ̃ tegbee na eƒe ƒometɔwo me elabena Yeshua HaMashiach ye nye Israel ƒe ƒometɔ xɔla, eya ame si ƒle anyigba la le eya ŋtɔ ƒe avuʋɔkɔkɔ ʋu me eye wòxe tafe na mi tso futɔ la ƒe asi me ne mietiae be miaxɔe. Togbɔ be mia dometɔ geɖewo nyi miaƒe lɔlɔ̃ na Egipte dome tso mia Fofowo gbɔ hã la, migaɖe mɔ mia ɖokuiwo nadze anyi tegbee le xɔsemanɔsitɔwo ƒe gbedzi o.

Elabena Yeshua HaMashiach ye nye ame si aɖe ame susɔewo tso Yakob me ame siwo ava egbɔ keŋkeŋ, le Daniel ƒe kwasiɖa blaadre lia ƒe dodokpɔwo me gɔ̃ hã. Eya ta miese ɣeyiɣi siwo me miele la gɔme eye mianyɔ tso miaƒe ŋkuagbãnvĩ me le nye sedede nu ne mietrɔ ɖe Yeshua HaMashiach, ame si medɔ ɖa hena miaƒe xɔxɔ abe miaƒe Mesia ene (ne miaƒe dziwo ƒe kuto magagbã ŋku na mi o). Elabena miatrɔ ɖe dzɔdzɔenyenye ƒe vi la ŋu eye wòaxɔ mi ɖe eɖokui gbɔ, eye wòakplɔ mi ayi dedienɔƒe ahaɣla mi tso miaƒe tsɔtsrɔ̃ me tso Esau ƒe aʋatso Moshiach ƒe asi me.

Eya ta miese ame si mia kplii miewɔna la gɔme elabena NYE ye nye nubabla geɖewo ƒe Mawu, eye NYE ye nye ŋugbedodo geɖewo ƒe Mawu, eye NYE ye nye Dzɔdzɔenyenye ƒe Mawu Tegbeetɔ si gava ake be yeayɔ nye dukɔa be woatrɔ ava woƒe kplɔlagã si medɔ ɖa hena Adam ƒe xɔxɔ la gbɔ. Eye akpa geɖewo le ame si menye la ŋu, eye ŋkɔ ɖe sia ɖe kple eƒe gɔmesese le Mawu Tegbeetɔ la ŋu. Eya ta mise nye nya eye misee nyuie eye miese Yehowa, ame si le yɔyɔ mi ɖe eɖokui gbɔ be miate ŋu akpɔ xɔxɔ la gɔme. Eye ale meɖoa ŋku edzi na mi eye mefiaa nu mi abe vidzĩwo ene, evɔ wòle be mianye nufialawo boŋ, O Israel! Eya ta misrɔ̃ nu tso miaƒe Dzɔdzɔenyenye ƒe Mawu Tegbeetɔ la ŋu!

Mise nye gbe O Israel, eye miva Yeshua HaMashiach gbɔ le ɣeyiɣi mamlɛ sia me elabena mele yɔyɔ mi le nye lɔlɔ̃ na mi me eye meke nye amenuveve kple nublanuikpɔkpɔ ɖe mi le ɣeyiɣi sia me. Gake mienye dukɔ tomahãla eye mia Fofowo ƒe tomahãmahã kple dzikuto la miekpe ɖe mia tɔ ŋu, eye miegbe mia Fofowo ƒe Mawu si miesubɔna le manyamanya kple aʋatso mɔ nu elabena wotsrɔ̃ nye dukɔ la le sidzedze manɔamesi ta, eya ta miva Yeshua HaMashiach gbɔ eye misrɔ̃ nu tso egbɔ ale be miate ŋu akpɔ xɔxɔ le eƒe asi kpekpe la me.

O Israel, aleke nye dzi gbãna ɖe ta wòe nye esi, elabena metsɔ wò abe numiemie fɛ̃ aɖe ene le gbedzi ƒuƒui aɖe si le klalo be yeatsrɔ̃ ahayrɔ ɖaa la me, eye mena agbe wò, eye mewɔ wò dukɔ tɔxɛ na ɖokuinye. Mebla nu geɖewo kple fofowòwo, nubabla siwo womegate ŋu dzi ɖo o gɔ̃ hã, le nuvɔ̃ kple gbegblẽ si le Adam ƒe ʋu me la ta. Gake Nyee nye Aƒetɔ wò Mawu, ame si ɖoa ŋku edzi, eye wòlé eƒe ŋugbedodowo kple nubablawo me ɖe asi, eye mava wu ŋugbedodo kple nubabla ɖe sia ɖe si mewɔ na amegbetɔkpakpa la nu le Yeshua HaMashiach, ame si nye vinye lɔlɔ̃a Yesu Kristo la ŋkume.

O Israel, ɖe nàkpɔtɔ anɔ xɔsemanɔmee kple toɖomaɖomaɖoa? Ðe nàwɔ ɖeka kple futɔwòwo le woƒe tohehe mavɔ la me ɖaaa? Eye aleae wòdzɔe be mèse to o, eye nèzɔ le ŋkuagbãnviviti me, eye mexɔ wò dukɔa le asiwò, eye Yahweh, Mawu Gã Mavɔmavɔtɔ la megale ŋgɔ xɔm na wò o. Gake le wò aglãdzedzea me la, futɔwòwo, ame siwo wɔa wo ɖokuiwo abe Yakob ene evɔ le nyateƒe me la, Esau wonye la ƒe asie le ŋgɔ xɔm na wò. Se egɔme be ame siwo katã gbe Yeshua HaMashiach la trɔ le Yahweh ŋu dze trɔ̃wo kple dzixɔsemanɔmee yome, eye woble ame geɖewo hede kluvi me na woƒe katsa yibɔ siwo wosubɔna le manyamanya me le dzixɔsemanɔmee me. Eya ta se egɔme be, ame siwo katã woƒe Moshiach aʋatso tɔ alo woƒe Mahdi aʋatso tɔ la ble le woƒe aʋatsosubɔsubɔha siwo me xɔse mele o, siwo mekplɔa ame yia Yahweh alo agbe mavɔ me o la, akpɔ tsɔtsrɔ̃ mavɔ na ame siwo katã atia be yewoanɔ eme la.

O Israel, va gbɔnye to vinye Yeshua HaMashiach, ame si hã nye Yesu Nazaretɔ, ame si nègbe abe ale si fofowòwo wɔe do ŋgɔ na wò ene la dzi. Gake mègatsrɔ̃ le fofowòwo ƒe nuvɔ̃wo me o, eye nàxɔ nye lɔlɔ̃ kple vɔsa si metsɔ na ɖe tawò la, vɔsa si dzi woto yra Adam ƒe dzidzimeviwo katã, eye ame geɖewo tso anyigbawo kple dukɔwo me va woƒe Aƒetɔ gbɔ le dzimetɔtrɔ me, eye woyra wo hexe fe ɖe wo ta tegbee. Eye se egɔme be Nyee nye ame si tsɔ nuwo katã de Kristo Yesu ƒe asi siwo fɔ tso ku me kple ŋusẽ, eye wotrɔ la me, elabena Nyee nye ame si wɔe wòzu gbɔgbɔ si naa agbe, eye eƒe dziɖuɖu kple fiaɖuƒe la nu mava yi o!

Eya ta, nye nubablawo katã tso Adam dzi va ɖo Mose dzi, kple Mose ƒe Nubabla la gɔ̃ hã le klalo azɔ be woawu enu le Yesu Kristo ŋkume to nye Nubabla Yeye si nye nye Nubabla Yeye si wona hena nuwo katã nuwuwu tso Adam dzi va ɖo Kristo dzi la me. Elabena ele be ame siwo akpɔ agbe mavɔ la nabɔbɔ wo ɖokuiwo ahadze klo na vinye dzidzɔvi hekpe ɖeka, ame si ŋu nye dzi dzea ŋu le ɣesiaɣi la. Eya ta va gbɔnye le Yeshua HaMashiach, ame si ƒe ʋu si me nuvɔ̃ mele o me wòdo kpe eƒe Nubabla Yeye, si nye Nubabla Yeye la, si mexɔ bliboe eye meɖo eŋu aɖaŋu hafi woɖo xexeame gɔme la ŋkume. Eye madɔ nye ame 144,000, siwo ŋgɔ Yeshua HaMashiach xɔna la ɖa hena wò xɔxɔ, eye woaɣla wò ɖe teƒe si medzra ɖo ɖi na wò, eye afi ma woalé be na wò ahaxɔ wò agbee tso Esau, ame si le dzadzram ɖo ɖi na wò tsɔtsrɔ̃ la si me.

Gake Esau, ame siwo tea nye dukɔa ɖe anyi la, le Babilon Nya Ɣaɣla gblẽm ɖi elabena woɖoe be yewoatsyɔ nu yewoƒe nuvɔ̃wo dzi. Nuvɔ̃ si fia siwo le xɔ si le mumum gblẽm ɖi la meɖi fɔ le eŋu o. Eya ta, woate kpɔ be yewoadi yewoƒe nuvɔ̃wɔwɔwo kple yewoƒe sofi nuvɔ̃tɔ siwo wotsɔ tsyɔ nu woƒe nuvɔ̃wɔwɔwo dzi gɔ̃ hã, eye magaɖe wo afia, Aʋakɔwo ƒe Aƒetɔ lae gblɔe. Elabena woha Babilon Ɣaɣla la bliboe abe ale si meɖe mɔ wòdzɔ le nye ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ teƒe eve ɖe gbolo gã ma dzi ene! Eye ale wofo Babilon Ɣaɣla ƒe asrafowo ƒe ŋusẽ katã nu ƒu na wo ɖokuiwo, eye woayi edzi aha nu va se ɖe esime woatsrɔ̃ Babilon Ɣaɣla kpuie le gaƒoƒo ɖeka me, eye magafɔ gbeɖe o. Se egɔme be aʋawɔnu aɖeke si wotu alo wɔ ɖe ŋuwò la madze edzi o ne èyɔ wò Mesia vavã la, Yeshua HaMashiach!

Eya ta se egɔme be Satana ƒe Sinagog la ƒe gotagome dzedzeme le abe mawusubɔsubɔ ene, gake wotso wo fofo abosam gbɔ, eye wo fofo ƒe nudzodzrowoe woawɔ elabena Esau gblẽ eƒe dzidzimeviwo dome tegbee. Gbe vɔ̃ɖi siawo, wò kplɔla siwo dze abe ame dzɔdzɔewo ene gɔ̃ hã nye gbe vɔ̃ɖiwo, eye wo dometɔ geɖewo nye ɖeviwo dɔzãlawo siwo dzea sɔhɛwo dzi, eye wotra mɔ le Sodom kple Gomora ƒe nuvɔ̃wo me, eye wodzea ɖeviwo dzi, eye womenye kplɔla nyuiwo o ke boŋ kplɔla dɔlélawo wonye. Eye dzoka gã si nye wo fofo lae nye ame si kplɔa wo, ame ma ke si na kplɔla dɔléla eve be woaɖu anyigba la dzi ahakplɔe le madzɔmadzɔnyenye me. Lã wɔadã si tso atsiaƒu me si afɔ abe woƒe Mahdi ene kple lã wɔadã si tso anyigba me, ame si hã nye woƒe Moshiach aʋatso tɔ, eye wòava ɖu anyigba la dzi, eye le nyateƒe me la, woaɖu xexeame dzi le madzɔmadzɔnyenye me.
