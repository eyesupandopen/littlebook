Dzove 1, 2025

Nyee Nye Dzɔdzɔenyenye Ƒe Aƒetɔ Tegbeetɔ La!

Kpɔ ɖa! Se Yesu Kristo ƒe Nyawo eye nàtsɔ wò tamesusuwo aɖe asi, nàtso aʋa na wò dzi kple towo le toɖoɖo Yeshua HaMashiach ƒe Nyawo me, eye mana nunya nunyalawo be nunyala si le mia dome nasrɔ̃ Aƒetɔ la vɔvɔ̃ eye be miasrɔ̃ dzodzodzo le Aʋakɔwo ƒe Aƒetɔ la ŋkume, elabena nenemae Yahweh, NYE SI MENYE Gã la wɔm.

Ame kae nènye, O amegbetɔ dzro, be nàkɔ wò afɔkpodzi ɖe Yahweh, NYE SI MENYE Gã la ŋu, eya ame si nye Ŋkekewo me Xoxotɔ la, eya ame si nye Gbɔgbɔwo ƒe Aƒetɔ la gɔ̃ hã? Ame kae nènye O amegbetɔ dzro be nàdo ɖokuiwò ɖe dzi le dada me ɖe Yesu Nazaret-tɔ si nye Kristo Yesu hã ŋu, eya ame si ƒe ŋkɔ ƒo ŋkɔwo katã ta, eya ame si Yahweh, NYE SI MENYE Gã la, ma eƒe fiazikpui kple, eya ame si wodo ɖe dzi wu nu katã negbe Yahweh ŋutɔ ko va se ɖe esime wotsɔ nye futɔwo wɔ nye afɔtiɖoƒee? Eye nye fiaɖuƒe kple dziɖuɖu nu metsina gbeɖe o eye matsɔ Fiaɖuƒe si woɖu edzi la afia Kekeliwo Fofo la be Adonai Yahweh nanye nu katã le nu katã me!

Ðe nu si womè la ate ŋu agblɔ na ame si mèi be, nu ka ta nèwɔm alea? Ðe anyi kpo si womè wòzu bubuzã ate ŋu agblɔ na zemela be, nu ka ta nèwɔm alea? Nu ka ta nètsɔ bubu sia nam? Medi be manye nu bubu eye matsɔ woƒe bubu la boŋ! Ðe zemela aɖeke matsrɔ̃ nuwɔwɔ vɔ̃ɖi sia si adze aglã ɖe eƒe asi ŋu, ahanɔ zemela la ƒe tsɔtsrɔ̃ gɔ̃ hã dim, kpe ɖe zemela la ƒe nuwɔwɔ bubuwo ŋu oa; abe ɖe nu ma tɔgbi anya wɔ ene?

Ke hã nenemae ame muamu siwo dze aglã ɖe wo Wɔla ŋu to woƒe ɖoɖowo kple dɔ kɔkɔe kple bubutɔ siwo wotsɔ de asi na wo be woawɔ le dzɔdzɔenyenye me la gbegbe me ƒe mɔ hloloetsi kple tsukukuwo le, taɖodzinu siwo Yahweh ŋutɔ na le ŋkeke si dzi wòwɔ Mawu viwo dzi gɔ̃ hã. Mawudɔla kɔkɔe siwo Yahweh wɔ hafi wɔ anyigba, ame siwo ŋu woŋlɔe ɖo le Hiob me esime wòwɔ anyigba la gɔ̃ hã: Ŋdiɣletiviwo dzi ha kple wo nɔewo, eye Mawu viwo katã do ɣli le dzidzɔ me! Eye wodo ɣli le dzidzɔ me nyateƒe ɖe nukunu kple dzesi siwo wokpɔ le Yahweh ƒe asi me esime wòwɔ anyigba la ŋu. Eye nenemae mawudɔla muamuwo, Mawu vi siwo wowɔ gbã eye wowɔ wo wònya kpɔ hekpɔ ŋusẽ ɖe woƒe taɖodzinuwo ŋu la nɔ, eye Yahweh do bubu awu na wo eye wòna wonyo ŋutɔ le nunya me be woalɔ̃ Yahweh le toɖoɖo blibo me na eƒe gbe eye nyateƒe, wona nɔnɔme gbãtɔ ŋutikɔkɔetɔ si me wowɔ wo ɖo gbã la wo, va se ɖe esime wodze aglã le woƒe dzi ƒe nudzodzrowo me eye wokpɔ vodada le wo me.

Eye nenemae Satanawo dze aglã le gɔmedzedzea me ɖe wo Wɔla ŋu, ame siwo ƒo xlã Yahweh ƒe fiazikpui kpɔ gɔ̃ hã, ame si ƒe fiazikpui ŋu ame 10,000 teƒe 10,000 ƒo xlãe fifia eye Yahweh gakpɔtɔ nye dziɖula gã eye mehiã aɖaŋuɖola aɖeke o. Ke hã nenema woŋlɔ ɖe dzoka gã sia, si ta adre, kple dzo ewo, kple fiakuku adre le etawo dzi ŋu be eƒe asike he dziƒoɣletiviwo ƒe akpa etɔ̃lia, eye wòtsɔ wo ƒu gbe ɖe anyigba dzi: eye nenemae wotsɔ Satanawo kple mawudɔla siwo dze woƒe aglãdzedzea yome ƒe akpa etɔ̃lia gɔ̃ hã ƒu gbe ɖe anyigba dzi, le ŋkeke siwo me mawudɔlawo ƒe gbegblẽ ge dze anyi tso dziƒo le nuvɔ̃ kple aglãdzedze me, eye wozu viviti tsiɖitsiɖi le woƒe nuvɔ̃wo megbe, eye nenema wole va se ɖe egbe ŋkeke sia dzi.

Eye nenemae da la te ɖe agbagbeawo katã dada ŋu le Eden-bɔ la me le ɣeyiɣi didi siawo me, eye wòble Xawa be wòaɖu nu si tututu Yahweh de se na wo be woagaɖu o la, elabena gbe si gbe woaɖu nyui kple vɔ̃ nyanya tatia la, gbe ma gbe ke woaku godoo. Nenema ke, Xawa xɔe le da la si esi eƒe aʋatso blee eye wòɖui, eye wòtsɔ atikutsetse si wode se ɖo la na srɔ̃a eye Adam ɖui kple lɔlɔ̃nu eye le kuku me la woku abe ale si wodo ŋugbee ene, eye amegbetɔ zu ku ƒe tevi tso ɣemaɣi le nuvɔ̃ si wokpɔ le eƒe ʋu me ta. Elabena Adam gblẽ le nuvɔ̃ me tegbee eye taɖodzinu sia tae woɖe Amegbetɔvi la fia be matsrɔ̃ abosam ƒe dɔwɔwɔwo eye mahe xɔxɔ vɛ na Adam si dze anyi elabena nyee nye Adam mamlɛtɔ si Yahweh wɔ wòzu gbɔgbɔ agbenala. Nyateƒe, nyateƒe, magawɔ ame siwo katã xɔ nye xɔxɔ ƒe nunana la wòazu nuwɔwɔ yeye kple dzɔdzɔe elabena nenemae nye Nubabla Yeyea hena vɛ.

Eya ta wonya Adam kple Xawa le Eden-bɔ la me, eye wotrɔ wo tso nɔnɔme dzeani wɔnuku si me Yahweh ŋutɔ wɔ wo le ŋutikɔkɔe blibo me la me, eye le nuvɔ̃ kple vɔ̃ si wòhena vɛ te la, wotsa le anyigba dzi le woƒe tohehe me. Eye le Yared ƒe ŋkekewo me la, Dzɔlawo ɖi va Hermon To dzi, ame siwo woƒe dzi ƒe nudzodzrowo de aboyo wo gɔ̃ hã elabena wodzro amegbetɔwo ƒe vinyɔnuwo, eye wodi dzidzimeviwo tso wo gbɔ gɔ̃ hã eye le woƒe dzi ƒe nudzodzro siwo me Satanawo te wo kpɔ le me la, wotsɔ wo nɔewo bla kple fiƒode, le woƒe atamkaka makɔmakɔ si trɔ zu fiƒode mavɔ si woava wɔ le dzokpe kple dzokpe dzo gã ma si me woƒe nɔku mekuna le o la me.

Eye nenemae Mawu viwo va amegbetɔwo ƒe vinyɔnuwo gbɔ, eye woɖe srɔ̃wo le ame siwo katã wotia la dome. Nyɔnu aɖewo tsɔ wo ɖokuiwo na kple lɔlɔ̃nu, hetsɔ wo ɖokuiwo na bliboe ɖe ame muamuwo ƒe nuvɔ̃wo me (ame siwo atsɔ woƒe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ la), eye woɖe ame aɖewo kple ŋusẽ eye Dzɔlawo wɔ ŋutasesẽ le wo ŋu vevie le woƒe nudzodzro vɔ̃ɖiwo me. Eye nenemae xexe si nɔ anyi ɣemaɣi la nɔ, xexe si wotsrɔ̃ le tsiɖɔɖɔ gã si dzɔ le Noa ƒe ŋkekewo me si wogblẽ ɖi le ŋunyɔnu vɔ̃ɖi siwo dzɔlawo wɔ ta, ame siwo wowɔ na amegbetɔwo ƒe tsɔtsrɔ̃ ŋutɔŋutɔ gɔ̃ hã be amegbetɔwo nagaxɔ anyigba si ame muamuwo dzro eye wotsi ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ me fifia la gbeɖe o.

Elabena wotia wo ŋutɔ woƒe tsɔtsrɔ̃ kple fuwɔwɔ mavɔ ɖe woƒe nudzodzrowo ƒe nu siwo menye wo tɔ be woaxɔ o la teƒe, eye nu siwo woxɔ, eye wogale didim kokoko be yewoaxɔ kple ŋusẽ. Ke hã, woƒe nuwɔnawo kple ɖoɖowo katã azu dzodzro, eye womagakpɔ woƒe sikaɣeyiɣi si woyɔna la gbeɖe o, ke boŋ ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ sesẽ kple mavɔ anɔ wo si. Elabena le nyateƒe si metrɔna o me: Anyigba kple eƒe blibonyenye nye Aƒetɔ la tɔ, eye Nyee nye Dzɔdzɔenyenye ƒe Aƒetɔ Tegbeetɔ si tsɔ nye ŋutɔ nye ʋu si xɔa ame la ƒle anyigbae, eye meva be matsɔ ŋusẽ axɔ nu si nye tɔnye le mɔ dzɔdzɔe nu, eye ame kae ate ŋu anɔ te ɖe nye ŋusẽ gã la ŋu?

Abe ale si woŋlɔe ɖe ŋunye ene la, nenema ke woawɔe: Kpɔ ɖa, Yuda-to me Dzata, David ƒe ke la, ɖu dzi be wòaʋu agbalẽ la, eye wòaho eƒe nutrenu adreawo. Eya ta, miese egɔme bliboe be nyee nye ame si dze be wòaho nutrenuawo elabena anyigba la ƒe agbalẽ nye tɔnye! Eye abe ale si woŋlɔe ɖe ŋunye ene la, nenema ke woawɔe elabena medze be maxɔ agbalẽ la, eye maʋu eƒe nutrenuawo: elabena wowum, eye metsɔ nye ʋu si me nuvɔ̃ mele o la xɔ ame siwo katã ava xɔxɔ mavɔ me na Yahweh la, eye mexɔ mi tso ƒome, kple gbe, kple dukɔ, kple dukɔ kuku ɖe sia ɖe me: eye nuwɔwɔ aɖeke mele anyi si ate ŋu ahe mia dometɔ aɖeke le nye asi mavɔwo me o! Eya ta, ame kae li si wòle be miavɔ̃ na?

Eya ta nyɔnu siwo Mawu viwo (dzɔla muamuwo gɔ̃ hã) xɔ la fɔ fu eye wodzi dzɔtsu ŋunyɔtɔ siwo vɔ̃ɖi ɖe woƒe dziwo ƒe gogloƒe la na wo. Eye woka ɖe woƒe lolome kple ŋusẽ dzi heƒo adegbe, ke hã le Yahweh ƒe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ ɖe wo dzi le vɔ̃ si wowɔ ɖe amegbetɔwo ŋu ta la, ebla woƒe gbɔgbɔwo ɖe anyigba dzi le tohehe me elabena wowɔ wo le anyigba dzi le ŋutilã nuvɔ̃tɔ ƒe nɔnɔme me eye woƒe gbɔgbɔwo nye mavɔtɔ siwo wotsɔ na tso wo Fofowo siwo ƒe nudzodzrowo wòle be woawɔ la gbɔ. Eye esi Nephilim siawo ku le woƒe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃wo me tso Yahweh ŋutɔ gbɔ la, woƒe gbɔgbɔwo do le ŋutilã me le woƒe kuwo me, eye woyi edzi, wobla wo ɖe anyigba dzi le tohehe me. Eya ta, dɔ wua wo eye tsikɔ wua wo gake womate ŋu aɖu nu alo ano tsi o. Esi wònye be tsã la, ŋutilã gã kple ŋusẽtɔwo nɔ wo si siwo wotsɔ ɖu anyigbadzinɔlawo dzi la, wowɔ wo ŋusẽmanɔsitɔwoe negbe ɖe woate ŋu axɔ ame si me wonɔna abe gbɔgbɔ vɔ̃wo ƒe nugbagbeviwo ene la, elabena nenema wozu. Miese egɔme be, tso Noa ƒe ŋkekewo me le Noa ƒe kukuɖeameta ta la, wodzra wo dometɔ akpa asieke le ewo me ɖo ɖe aʋe goglo la me gake woaɖe asi le wo ŋu kpuie ɖe anyigba dzi le ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ me.

Ke hã, nenemae ame muamuwo ƒe aglãdzedze nɔ tso ŋkeke si dzi wokpɔ vodada le woƒe dziwo me tututu. Tsukuku kple tagbɔdɔ ma yɔ ame muamuwo ƒe dziwo me le woƒe aglãdzedzea me; kpe ɖe amegbetɔ dzro, si kpe ɖe wo ŋu to gbe si wogbe be yewomadzo le yewoƒe anyidzedze kple nuvɔ̃ nɔnɔme me o la ŋu: be wo katã wodi be yewoazu nu bubu si to vovo na nu si Yahweh ɖo le dzɔdzɔenyenye blibo katã me. Le toɖoɖo wo Wɔla si akpɔ egbɔ be woƒe dzidzeme, dzidzɔ, kple ŋutifafa blibo nava eme; ame si anya na nu wo pɛpɛpɛ ɣesiaɣi le eƒe lɔlɔ̃ na eƒe nuwɔwɔ me teƒe la, wodi woƒe dzi ƒe nudzodzrowo boŋ eye wodze wo yome eye wobia xexe si nye wo ŋutɔ tɔ si me Yahweh mele o, afi si woate ŋu anɔ agbe ɖe woƒe dzi ƒe nudzodzrowo nu eye woaƒo wo ɖokui ɖe woƒe aglãdzedze me le nuvɔ̃ ƒe vivisesewo me le Yahweh ƒe nuwɔwɔ ƒe fukpekpe me. Nudzodzro vɔ̃ɖi kple nuvɔ̃tɔ siwo womakpɔ alo aɖo gbeɖe o gɔ̃ hã.

Nenema kee amegbetɔ dzro tsɔ eɖokui kpe ɖe woƒe aglãdzedzea ŋu negbe ɖe wodzi amegbetɔ dzro le manyamanya kple viviti me le eƒe taɖodzinu ŋutɔŋutɔwo kple ŋutikɔkɔe si ate ŋu anɔ anyi si Yahweh do ŋugbee na toɖolawo ŋu eye amegbetɔ dzro dze ame muamuwo ƒe mɔwo yome kple lɔlɔ̃nu, togbɔ be, ame muamuwo ɖoe kplikpaa be yewoatsrɔ̃ Adam, si ŋu Adam menya nu le o kple lɔlɔ̃nu le eƒe nudzodzrowo me, si me wòbla eɖokui ɖo le mɔkpɔkpɔ tsukuku me ɖe woƒe aʋatso geɖe kple vɔ̃ɖiwo ŋu gɔ̃ hã. To aʋatso siawo dzi gɔ̃ hã la, woakplɔ ame geɖe ayi dzo ƒu la me esime tohehe ma menye woawoe woɖoe na o.

Ke hã, nenemae aglãdzedze sia yi edzi to ɣeyiɣiwo me fifia, aglãdzedze si wowɔna le nukuwo ƒe aʋa si le Yahweh ƒe nuwɔwɔ dzɔdzɔewo kple ŋunyɔnu madzɔmadzɔ siwo vɔ̃ɖitɔwo ƒe domenyiŋusẽfianuwo ƒe tɔtrɔ wɔ la dome gɔ̃ hã, eye womenye mawuwo o togbɔ be wowɔa wo ɖokuiwo abe mawuwo ene hã. Eye nenemae ɣeyiɣi siwo me nèle la le, O amegbetɔ, ame si dze aglã le toomaɖomaɖo vɔ̃ɖi me tso eƒe Wɔla ƒe mɔwo gbɔ, eye wòtsɔ eɖokui kpe ɖe ame siwo ɖoe kplikpaa ɖe wò ŋutɔ wò ku kple tsɔtsrɔ̃ mavɔ si wole didim la ŋu le manyamanya me.

Eya ta meɖe gbeƒã ŋkekea, Nye Yeshua HaMashiach, meɖe gbeƒã be Aƒetɔ la ƒe ŋkeke la ɖo eye wòle klalo be wòadze anyigba dzi le ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ sesẽ gã me eye woɖo afɔku geɖewo azã be woaɖe asi le wo ŋu ɖe anyigba dzi, afɔku kple ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ siwo ŋu wogblɔ nya ɖi le blema siwo ƒe ɣeyiɣiwo ɖo azɔ gɔ̃ hã. Eya ta, miese ame si tututu menye si le gbeƒãɖeɖe sia wɔm le Yahweh ŋutɔ ƒe ŋusẽ kple fiazikpui me la gɔ̃me, elabena nyee nye ame si dze be wòaxɔ agbalẽ la aʋui, eye wòaho nutrenu siwo le eme la, eye mawɔ nenema tututu, eye ame kae ate ŋu axe mɔ na nye asi kãle la?!

Se egɔme, O amegbetɔ dzro, wò ame si agbe wò Wɔla kple fuléle gɔ̃ hã, be wò ɣeyiɣiwo ɖo. Eye wona lɔlɔ̃ kple amenuveve kple nublanuikpɔkpɔ geɖe le ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ ɣeyiɣi siawo me, elabena ame siwo katã yɔa Yesu Nazaret-tɔ ƒe ŋkɔ va se ɖe woƒe nuwuwu la, woaxɔ wo, eye woƒe fetu ƒe kpekpeme medze be woatsɔ asɔ kple fukpekpe siwo woado dzi le nye ŋkɔa ta o. Se egɔme, O amegbetɔ dzro, be makplɔ nye kalẽtɔ 144,000 la ayi hena Yakob ɖeɖe eye maɣla Israel ƒe ame susɔe kãle aɖe siwo ayi edzi anɔ agbe ayi nye Ƒe Akpe Ðeka Fiaɖuƒe la me le teƒe si Yahweh dzra ɖo le gbedzi, be woate ŋu adzi asɔ gbɔ eye woaɖu anyigba la ƒe dami le nye ƒe akpe ɖeka fiaɖuƒe si le vavam ɖe anyigba dzi kpuie alea le ŋugbedodo, nubabla, kple nubabla yeye me la me.

Nenemae wòanɔ na amegbetɔ dzro, be woaxɔ Mawu ƒe alẽvi la be wòanye wo xɔla alo woatia wo ŋutɔ woƒe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ mavɔ le dzo ƒu ma si le bibim ɣesiaɣi la me elabena tiatia bubu aɖeke meli o, eye teƒe bubu aɖeke mele wo si woayi o elabena Yahweh maɖe mɔ gbeɖe be vɔ̃ naɖu eƒe nuwɔwɔ dzi ahatsrɔ̃e o eye woƒe lɔlɔ̃nu aglãdzedze ƒe ŋkekewo le nu wum.

Nyee nye Mawu ƒe alẽvi, si wotsɔ na ɖe xexeame ƒe nuvɔ̃wo ta.

Nyee nye Yuda-to me Dzata la, eye le dzɔdzɔenyenye me meva be madrɔ̃ ʋɔnu ahawɔ aʋa.

Nyee nye ame si me Mawu ƒe alẽvi la kple Yuda-to me Dzata la wɔ ɖeka bliboe le nye taɖodzinu mavɔwo kple dziɖuɖu abe Fiawo ƒe Fia kple Aƒetɔwo ƒe Aƒetɔ ene me, eya ta ame kae li si ate ŋu anɔ te ɖe Yeshua HaMashiach si nye Adonai ƒe kɔkɔetɔ la ŋu, eya ame si Yahweh dɔ be wòawɔ ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ mavɔ ɖe ame muamuwo kple ame siwo katã tia be yewoawɔ ɖeka kple woƒe mɔwo dzi.

Eya ta meɖo aɖaŋu na nuteƒewɔlawo le Kristo me be woayi edzi anɔ nuteƒe wɔm elabena miaƒe ɖeɖekpɔgbe la ɖo vɔ eye wòtu aƒe ɖe mi. Mikpɔ miaƒe Dzɔdzɔenyenye ƒe Aƒetɔ Mavɔ la ɖeka ko elabena ame dzedzeawo tsɔ aʋatso kple alakpa gã aɖe ɖi tsi xexeame be yewoatsɔ able tiatia la ŋutɔ hã ne anya wɔ. Eya ta, mise egɔme be wodɔ aʋatsonyagblɔɖila geɖewo ɖa ame siwo gblɔa nya ɖi le Balaam ƒe mɔwo nu eye Izebel ƒe gbɔngbɔnwo lé wo eye wofoa nu to Kristo-tsitretsiɖeŋula ƒe gbɔngbɔnwo me ame siwo fiaa Yesu bubu ame si tsi tsitre ɖe Yesu Kristo ame si nye Yeshua HaMashiach teƒe kple eŋu le ame blemawo ƒe dziwo me. Ame siwo katã wolé le aʋatso sia me la atsɔ woƒe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ ne wogbe be yewomadzudzɔ yewoƒe mɔwo o ne woƒe Aƒetɔ la kpe kple wo le nuteƒewɔlawo ƒe nu me le Kristo me. Nyateƒe, nyateƒe, aʋatsonyagblɔɖila aɖewo le anukwareɖi me eye woble wo eye woate ŋu aɖo wo gbɔ kple nyateƒe si wogblɔ le lɔlɔ̃ ƒe nuyiwo dzi, gake nyateƒe la ko ye ana woavo eye woƒe nuvɔ̃wo kple aʋatsonyagblɔɖiwo ƒe nyateƒe gbadzaa la ko ye ana woagbugbɔ aɖo te le dzimetɔtrɔ me.

Azɔ ame siwo gblɔa nya ɖi ɖe ga ta, kple viɖe makɔmakɔwo ta, eye be yewoakpɔ kafukafu kple ŋkuɖodzodzi eye yewoase vivi le xexe vɔ̃ɖi sia ƒe nuwo me bliboe la hã li. Aleke kee ame si gblɔa aʋatsonya ɖi la le o, mihe nya ɖe wo ŋu eye migbe woƒe Nyawo, Nya siwo ame dzedzeawo ɖo be yewoatsɔ aɖo mɔ̃ na tiatia la ŋutɔ le aʋatso me ne anya wɔ gɔ̃ hã.
