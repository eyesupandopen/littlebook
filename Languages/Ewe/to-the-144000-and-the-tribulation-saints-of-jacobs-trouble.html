Le Yohanes Nyaɖela Ƒe Dɔwɔwɔwo Me

Na Ame 144,000 Kple Yakob Ƒe Xaxa Ɣeyiɣi Me Kɔkɔetɔwo

Na ame 144,000 kple Yakob ƒe xaxa ɣeyiɣi me kɔkɔetɔwo: Mikpɔ ɖa! Megblɔ nu geɖewo na mi hafi woava dzɔ ale be nunyala si le mia dome si se nye gbe nazu nunyala geɖe wu, eye be miadze si nye nyawo ƒe kakaɖedzi. Elabena Satana adze agbagba be yeatsyɔ nu nuteƒewɔlawo ƒe dzo yiyi le nye Hamea me dzi (elabena malɔ wo adzoe le woƒe dzodzo le anyigba dzi yi dziƒowo me) kple aʋa kple amedzro siwo woyɔna be amedzrowo ƒe dzedze alakpatɔe siwo menye naneke o negbe ame dzedzeari siwo le wo ɖokui ɖem fia anyigba la le nuvlowɔwɔ kple nuvlowɔwɔ siwo ƒe ke do ɖe woƒe aʋatsonyawo me bliboe me. Elabena woye nye aʋatsokaka.

Woka aʋatso le xexeme si woyɔna la ŋu; elabena xexeme aɖeke meli si le kekem ɖe edzi le yame dzro o, ke boŋ le anyigba dzi la, dziƒowo ƒe dziƒo li, (dziƒo kɔkɔtɔ) afi si Yawe, Mawu Dziƒoʋĩtɔ la, nɔa eƒe fiazikpui dzi, gake awɔ eƒe nɔƒe kple amegbetɔwo. Gake ele afi sia afi eye wònye nu katã le nu katã me le Kristo ƒe Ŋutilã la me. Eƒe dzonu xɔasi la le anyigba dzi, ame siwo wotia le mavɔmavɔ siwo va yi me le Yawe ŋutɔ ƒe dzi me. Eya ta anyigba la nye Aƒetɔ la tɔ kple eƒe blibonyenye; elabena nye, Yesu Nazaretɔ la, metsɔ nye ŋutɔ nye ʋu si xɔa ame ɖe agbe la ƒle anyigbae.

Misrɔ̃ miaƒe xexemeŋutinunya vavãtɔ (misrɔ̃ nu be miadze sii) eye mixɔ Ŋɔŋlɔ Kɔkɔeawo me nyawo kple nuwɔwɔ ŋuti nyawo dzi se, elabena ele abe ale si Yawe gblɔe le Ŋɔŋlɔawo me ene, eye mate ŋu adzɔ be Yawe naka aʋatso o. Ne megblɔ anyigba dzi nuwo na mi eye miexɔe se o ɖe, aleke miawɔ axɔe ase ne megblɔ dziƒonuwo na mi? Ao, amedzro aɖeke meli le gɔmesese si woƒe aʋatsonyawo gblɔna me o, ke boŋ dɔla dzedzeariwo, agãdzelawo, Satana kple eƒe fiaɖuƒe siwo ka aʋatso eye woble anyigba la be woabu fɔ ame siwo katã kpɔa dzidzɔ ɖe madzɔmadzɔnyenye ŋu.

Wolɔ̃ aʋatsonya siwo woɖo aɖaŋu vɔ̃e la geɖe ɖe aʋatsokaka ƒe avɔ me, eye woɖu woƒe ŋutinyawo ƒe akpa eveawo katã dzi tso gɔmedzedzea me esime wole woƒe ɖoɖowo tutum ɖe ŋgɔ. Elabena woɖua gɔmeɖoanya la dzi eye woɖua tsitretsiɖeŋunya la dzi, eye woƒoa woƒe dɔ vɔ̃ɖiwo nu ƒu aɖaŋutɔe, eye woƒe aʋatsokaka la lolo! Gake Ŋɔŋlɔ Kɔkɔeawo ƒe kekeli ɖea aʋatso la fiana, gake amegbetɔ gbea Mawu ƒe kpekpeɖeŋu va se ɖe esime wodrɔ̃ ʋɔnu eye dzɔgbevɔ̃e dzɔ. Ekema amegbetɔwo dzea agbagba be yewotrɔ dzi me ne eku megbe akpa le ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ megbe, eye ame siwo nɔa agbe le mɔ sia nu la masi le woƒe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ nu o. Elabena nu sia nu si Yawe gblɔ la, ame aɖeke mate ŋu atrɔe o!

Ame geɖe aɖe mɔ na aʋatsokaka siwo gbɔna siwo ado tso xexemeʋa kple dzɔgbevɔ̃e si le anyigba dzi dze ge kpuie la me. Elabena woaɖe wo ɖokuiwo afia amegbetɔwo abe nuwɔwɔ nyui siwo tso xexeme siwo de ŋgɔ me be yewoama yewoƒe sidzedze nyui la ene. Gake enye nyui kple vɔ̃ ƒe sidzedze si wogbugbɔ dzra ɖo be wòasɔ ɖe woƒe taɖodzinuwo nu ko. Mɔ̃ɖaŋununya deŋgɔwo anɔ wo si eye woadze abe wode ŋgɔ ŋutɔ ene, si ƒo amegbetɔ dzro ta sãsãsã. Gake woƒe ŋgɔyiyiwo le vɔ̃ɖinyenye me ko, eye wometso anyigba sia dzi o ke boŋ Yawe ye wɔ wo le nuwɔwɔ ƒe gɔmedzedze le dziƒowo me. Wonɔ anyi esime Yawe wɔ anyigba la. Fofo la le eƒe ŋgɔgbedzesi me nya be woade yewo yome eye eya ta eɣla eƒe dzi me nya ɣaɣla xɔasi la ɖe ame siwo wònya be woahe tsɔtsrɔ̃ vɔ̃ɖi ava eƒe nuwɔwɔ dzi la.

Megale amegbetɔ dzro yɔm ɖe dzɔdzɔenyenye me, gake amegbetɔ dzro lɔ̃a dɔléle, nuvɔ̃, kple viviti boŋ; eye eya ta mana nu si amegbetɔ di vevie lae. Esi amegbetɔ dzro wɔa vɔ̃ eye wòlɔ̃a viviti ta la, mana viviti si seɖoƒe meli na o lae.

Elabena fifia, amegbetɔ dzro tsi tsitre le nye amenuveve me kple dada, eye wòzãa nublanuikpɔkpɔ abe susu si ta wòatsɔ nuvɔ̃ akpe ɖe nuvɔ̃ dzi ene, le esi teƒe be wòaxɔ Yawe ƒe ŋutifafanubabla, nye ŋutɔ, Yeshua HaMashiach, ame si tsɔ nye ʋu si me nuvɔ̃ aɖeke mele o la na le toɖoɖo Yawe me be wòanye fefe ɖe amegbetɔwo ƒe nuvɔ̃wo katã ta ɖe ɣeyiɣiwo katã me. Migada fli o: Nyee nye Mesia vavã la, eye ame bubuwo katã nye srɔ̃srɔ̃ dzro siwo me viɖe aɖeke mele o.

Satana dze agã kpuie le nuwɔwɔ ƒe gɔmedzedze megbe eye wòƒo ɖi Fofo la ƒe nuwɔwɔ le mɔ si me ŋukpe mele o, vɔ̃ɖi, fuléle, kple tsɔtsrɔ̃ me; gake wowɔe le aɖaŋu gã me kple dzesi kple nukunu aʋatsotɔwo, kple mɔ̃ɖaŋununya deŋgɔ siwo wɔa nuku na seselelãmewo gɔ̃ hã. Eye le eteƒe la, wobia be woade ta agu na yewo eye woaɖo to yewoƒe mɔwo pɛpɛpɛ, gake woƒe mɔwo hea amegbetɔwo ƒe ku kple tsɔtsrɔ̃ vɛ ɣesiaɣi, elabena aleae woɖo wo.

Naneke meyeye le ɣea te o, vinyewo. Mawu siwo woyɔna le blemaŋutinya me kple woƒe dzidzimevi ŋunyɔtɔ siwo woyɔna be mawu kpakpɛwo la agbɔ, eye abe ale si wònɔ le Noa ƒe ŋkekeawo me ene la, nenema kee wòanɔ. Elabena woɖe wo le woƒe kawo kple fuwɔamewo me be woaxɔ woƒe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ blibo la, eye maɖo eteƒe, Aƒetɔ Yesu Kristo ye gblɔe, eye Yawe aɖo eteƒe na wo keŋkeŋ.

Satana kple eƒe ame dzedzeariwo gbe be yewomadze klo na Yawe ƒe ɖoɖo o eye wote kpɔ boŋ be yewaofi anyigba la le amegbetɔwo si to aʋatsokaka gã me. Satana te kpɔ gɔ̃ hã be yeafi nye domenyinu le Kekeliwo Ƒe Fofo la si! Gake ame aɖeke mate ŋu ahe mia dometɔ suetɔ kekeake gɔ̃ hã le asinyewo me o, eye ame aɖeke mate ŋu axɔ nye domenyinu o; elabena Yawe ŋutɔ ye tsɔe na, eye ame sia ame si gbe be yemadze klo nam, Yesu Kristo, o la, dzea agã ɖe Yawe ƒe ɖoɖo ŋu, eye wòkpea ɖe ame dzedzeariwo ŋu tegbee.

Eya ta megblɔ na mi be, migaxɔ Copernicus, ame si nye Yesu-ha me tɔ kple Freemason si subɔ Satana la ƒe aʋatsonyawo dzi se o. Egbugbɔ fia blema Hela trɔ̃subɔlawo ƒe xexemenunyalawo ƒe nyawo ko. Eye eya ta miaƒe xexemeŋutinunya si miexɔ la, ɣesubɔlawo kple trɔ̃subɔlawo ye kpae ɖe Satana ƒe aʋatsonyawo dzi, Yesu-ha me tɔ busunyagblɔla siwo gblẽ miaƒe sukudede ƒe ɖoɖowo kple nusɔsrɔ̃ si tsi tsitre ɖe nusɔsrɔ̃ ŋu gɔ̃ hã, kple tameɖoɖo blibo be woaŋlɔ woƒe xexeme aʋatsotɔ la ɖe amewo ƒe tamesusuwo me to aʋatsonya siwo wotsɔ bla abe hehena ene la me. Ke boŋ, susu manɔmee ƒe nufiafia dzɔ, eye ame siwo le anyigba dzi la mese xexea me ale si wòle ŋutɔŋutɔ la gɔme o.

Fifia woɖo mɔ na aʋatsokaka sesẽ aɖe, eye wotu aʋatsonyawo xoxo, elabena eƒe gɔmeɖoanyiwo nye nuvlowɔwɔwo kple aʋatsokaka gã. Aʋatsokaka siawo ƒe ke do ɖe miaƒe Yesu-ha me tɔwo ƒe hehenawo me. Elabena woƒe aʋatsonyawo ble xexea me katã; woable ame tiatiawo ŋutɔ gɔ̃ hã ne anya wɔ. Eye eya ta mɔ̃ la le klalo be wòaɖi, eye nu ɖeka si le edzi lém ye nye nu xeɖotɔ la, si woaɖe ɖa kpuie ŋutɔ eye wòawɔ nuku. Ekema aʋa gã aɖe adze anyigba dzi esime woaƒo nye nuteƒewɔlawo nu aƒu gbɔnye le dziƒowo me. Ekema lã wɔadã la kple aʋatsonyagblɔɖila la, ame siwo anɔ vevie le aʋatsokaka si gbɔna me la, ado tso afi la me.

Eya ta migaxɔ woƒe aʋatsonyawo dzi se o, nye kalẽtɔwo, elabena mienye dukɔ tɔxɛ aɖe eye meyɔ mi eye metsɔ Mawu Gbagbe la ƒe nutrenu tre mi, be Mawu ƒe dziku nagaka mi o. Madɔ mi ɖa abe nye kalẽtɔwo ene. Mianye nye gbe geɖe siwo le ɣli dom le gbedzi afi sia afi si wodɔ mi ɖo, eye mele mi dɔm be miaƒo nye alẽ bubu siwo susɔ la nu aƒu.

Elabena ameha gã aɖe ado tso Yakob ƒe xaxa me, eye miawɔ kalẽdɔ gãwo eye miade se na nu si mate ŋu adzɔ o, elabena mana dzesi, nukunu, kple nukunu gã geɖewo be miaƒe asiwo nawɔ wo. Miawɔ blema nukunu gã siwo woŋlɔ ɖe Ŋɔŋlɔ Kɔkɔeawo me, le Eliya ƒe ŋusẽ me gɔ̃ hã, negbe ɖe miawɔ dɔ gãwo kple ŋusẽdɔwo wu ale si woŋlɔ ɖe eŋu le blema.

Elabena Ŋɔŋlɔa gblɔ be ame si xɔa dzinye sena, Yeshua HaMashiach, mawɔ dɔ siwo mewɔ le anyigba dzi le nye subɔsubɔdɔ me na Yudea abe Mawu ƒe alẽvi ene ko o, ke boŋ miawɔ dɔ gãwo wu nye ŋutɔ gɔ̃ hã, elabena mana dzesi, nukunu, kple nukunu gãwo le miaƒe asiwo me esime miede se na nu si mate ŋu adzɔ o to xɔse si le mia si ɖe dzinye me. Eya ta mikpɔm ɣesiaɣi. Eye minɔ ŋku lém ɖe ŋunye, elabena makplɔ mi afɔɖeɖe ɖe afɔɖeɖe dzi. Miaƒe zɔzɔ anye nunya tɔ, eye nunyalawo ƒe aɖe anɔ mia si, eye manye miaƒe ŋusẽ kple xɔxɔ, elabena le nye, Kristo Yesu me la, blibonyenye katã le.

Eya ta meɖo aɖaŋu na mi katã be kpuie ŋutɔ ga mazu naneke o, eye ga ɖeka si woaxɔ ye abia be dzesi la nanɔ ame si be wòakpɔ mɔ azã xexea me nuwo. Migaxɔ dzesi la o, elabena anyo wu be miaku le agbe sia me (mialɔ̃ miaƒe agbewo va se ɖe nuwuwu o) tsɔ wu be miaxɔ dzesi la eye woatsɔ mi aƒu gbe ɖe dzo ta mavɔ si wodzra ɖo ɖi na Abosam kple eƒe dɔlawo me, elabena woƒe ɣeyiɣiwo de; migakpe ɖe wo ŋu ko o. Elabena woado ŋugbe gãwo kple xɔasiwo siwo adze abe nyateƒe ene, gake woƒe nyawo nye aʋatso, eye aɖi le woƒe nyawo me, eye woƒe ŋugbedodowo nye aʋatso eye nu makɔmakɔ ƒomevi katã yɔ wo me. Womate ŋu awɔ woƒe ŋugbedodowo dzi gbeɖe o, elabena woƒe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ gbɔna; ŋutifafa aɖeke mele wo si o, eye woaɖe ŋutifafa ɖa le anyigba dzi keŋkeŋ.

Amegbetɔ ƒe vɔ̃ɖinyenye manɔ seɖoƒe o; eye na ame geɖewo la, ne woɖe nu xeɖotɔ la ɖa ko la, woatsɔ wo ɖokuiwo ana tsukuku kple vɔ̃ɖinyenye tegbee. Negbe ame si nɔa agbe ɖe yi nu la, ele be woawui kple yi, eye woage adze miame kple ɖusime le xexlẽme gã me godoo, gake mava mia gbɔ o esime miele zɔzɔm nam; migalɔ̃ miaƒe agbewo va se ɖe nuwuwu o ko eye miedze nye kpɔɖeŋu si nye ɖokuitsɔtsɔna kple vɔsasa la yome. Elabena fetu gã aɖe anye mia tɔ ɖe toɖoɖo kple nuteƒewɔwɔ ta.

Eya ta amegbetɔ ƒe dzodzrowo kple vɔ̃ɖinyenye manɔ seɖoƒe kura o, eye Abosam kple eƒe dɔlawo manɔ seɖoƒe kura o, eye woƒe ŋunyɔnu vɔ̃ɖiwo katã manɔ seɖoƒe kura o, eye woava abe blema kpeɖeŋutɔwo ene. Woƒe aʋatsokaka goglo eye ke goglowo le eya si woaho ɖa keŋkeŋ kpuie. Womenye mawuwo o, eye womenye ame nyui deŋgɔwo o, ke boŋ aʋatsokalawo, eye woƒo ɖi wo ɖokuiwo le mɔ vovovowo nu. Eye eya ta womakpɔ mɔ axe mɔ na wo kura o, eye woaɖe Satana ƒe fiaɖuƒe blibo la le aʋli la me, elabena woaʋui.

Woawɔ esia be woatsɔ afia amegbetɔ dzro nu si wòtia eye woana mɔnukpɔkpɔ mlɔeba be wòatrɔ dzi me; elabena womagaɣla vɔ̃ɖinyenye alo axe mɔ nɛ o, eye ɣaɣla ɖe sia ɖe ava kekeli me. Woaklo nu le woƒe aʋatsokaka gã kple aʋatsokaka sesẽwo ƒe aʋatsonyawo gɔ̃ hã dzi be ame katã nakpɔ, elabena Yawe gblɔe le Ŋɔŋlɔ Kɔkɔeawo me, eye nu si Yawe gblɔ la, ame aɖeke mate ŋu atrɔe o.

Eya ta mama mlɔeba aɖe ava dzɔ, eye woagblẽ ame siwo le ŋutifafa me siwo melɔ̃na la dometɔ geɖe ɖi na dodokpɔ kple dodokpɔ siwo gbɔna, ale be woate ŋu axɔ wo ɖe agbe. Elabena ɣeyiɣi aɖeke megali o, eye womagana nye amenuveve kple nublanuikpɔkpɔ le mɔ ma ke nu o, eye ɣeyiɣi aɖeke megali na xuxɔxuxɔ mɔwo o. Ame sia ame atia nu; eye na vɔvɔ̃tɔwo kple dzimaxɔsetɔwo la, woatia Satana kple eƒe fiaɖuƒe esime woate ŋu atiam, nye ŋutɔ, Yesu Kristo, Mawu ƒe vi si va le ŋutilã me eye metsɔ nye ɖokui na abe Ŋutitoto blibo la ene ɖe amegbetɔwo ƒe nuvɔ̃wo katã ta zi ɖeka ɖe ɣeyiɣiwo katã me. Ame geɖe agbe nye kpekpeɖeŋu ƒe asi le hiã gã ɣeyiɣi me kple dzimesesẽ, elabena nye kpekpeɖeŋu nye kpekpeɖeŋu mavɔ kple mavɔ si me fetu gã, nublanuikpɔkpɔ, amenuveve, kple lɔlɔ̃ le.

Gake, xexea me tɔ akpa gãtɔ axɔ aʋatsonyawo dzi ase eye woawɔ nuku ɖe lã wɔadã la ŋu, agblɔ be, ame kae le abe lã wɔadã la ene? Woalɔ̃ faa aɖe mɔ da naɖu wo si ƒe aɖi womate ŋu ada gbeɖe o, elabena enye nu si nɔa anyi tegbee. Be amegbetɔ natia vɔ̃ le tiatia ɣeyiɣiwo me la anye tiatia mavɔ, eye ame siwo wɔa tiatia ma la anɔ ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ gã me.

Ame siwo lɔ̃ faa tia vɔ̃ la abu akɔnta le tamesusu vɔ̃ ɖe sia ɖe, nya vɔ̃ ɖe sia ɖe, kple dɔ vɔ̃ ɖe sia ɖe si wowɔ vɔ̃ɖitɔe le anyigba dzi la ŋu, elabena woaʋu agbalẽawo eye nublanuikpɔkpɔ aɖeke manɔ anyi o. Eye ame aɖeke mate ŋu anɔ te le eya ŋutɔ ƒe dzɔdzɔenyenye me o, elabena dzɔdzɔenyenye aɖeke mele amegbetɔ si o; elabena da la gblẽ wo tso gɔmedzedzea me, eye eƒe gbegblẽ ŋkekewo le nu wum. Aleae Yawe dɔm, eƒe Tenuvi, abe ŋutifafanubabla ene ɖe amegbetɔ dzro gbɔ, eye amegbetɔ dzro gbe ŋutifafa mavɔ elabena amegbetɔ dzro lɔ̃a vɔ̃.

Ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ gã aɖe le dzɔdzɔ ge ɖe Satana ƒe fiaɖuƒe dzi. Ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ sia agblẽ ɖe edzi, eye nudzɔdzɔ siwo wowɔ le ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ me la agblẽ ɖe edzi. Woatsɔ baba akpe ɖe baba dzi eye woatsɔ fuwɔame akpe ɖe fuwɔame dzi na ame siwo katã gbe be yewomatrɔ dzi me le yewoƒe dɔ vɔ̃ɖiwo me o. Elabena ame geɖe matrɔ dzi me le woƒe asiwo ƒe dɔwɔwɔwo me o, be womasubɔ gbɔgbɔ vɔ̃wo, kple sika, kple klosalo, kple akɔbli, kple kpe, kple ati legbawo o: siwo mate ŋu akpɔ nu, alo ase nu, alo azɔ mɔ o: Womatrɔ dzi me le woƒe amewuwuwo, alo woƒe adzewɔwɔwo, alo woƒe ahasiwɔwɔ, alo woƒe fififiwo hã me o.

Elabena ame dzedzeariwo menye mawuwo o, ke boŋ aʋatsokalawo, alakpatɔwo, fefewɔlawo, kple ameblelawo. Wohea ku kple tsɔtsrɔ̃ ko vɛ na ame siwo dzea wo yome, eye ame siwo katã woble la akpɔ dzidzɔ kura o, elabena woƒe mɔkpɔkpɔwo nu ayi le dzo ta gã ma si me mɔkpɔkpɔ aɖeke mele o la me. Eye nye, Kristo Yesu, magbã Satana ƒe fiaɖuƒe keŋkeŋ le ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ gã me, elabena ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ gã aɖe adzɔ ɖe wo katã dzi, eye nyee nye Yuda Ƒomea ƒe Dzata la.

Eye eya ta nutrenu ɖe sia ɖe akpe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ ɖe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ dzi, eye kpẽ ɖe sia ɖe akpe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ ɖe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ dzi, baba ɖe sia ɖe akpe baba ɖe baba dzi, eye kple ze ɖe sia ɖe la, Mawu ƒe dziku blibo la gbɔna, dziku ɖe dziku dzi gɔ̃ hã. Satana ƒe fiaɖuƒe akpɔ fu ŋutɔ, eye woatsrɔ̃ eƒe fiaɖuƒe keŋkeŋ eye woagbãe gudugudu.

Elabena woaƒo wo ɖokuiwo nu ahatso ɖe Aƒetɔ la ƒe vava ŋu, elabena mava godoo. Tso Yakob ƒe xaxa ƒe gɔmedzedze la, ƒewo ƒe ŋkeke adre anɔ anyi, elabena woawu Daniel ƒe kwasiɖa blaadre lia nu hena madzɔmadzɔnyenye tsɔtsrɔ̃ kple Israel ƒe xɔxɔ. Elabena makɔ Israel ŋuti tso vɔ̃ si xɔ anyigba la me.

Elabena Yawe alo nye ŋutɔ, Yeshua HaMashiach, medɔ woƒe dziɖulawo o, eye womede dɔ asi na wo o, elabena woɖua dzi le madzɔmadzɔnyenye me eye woƒe ametsɔtsrɔ̃wo nye ŋunyɔnu, eye nye dukɔa xea woƒe aʋawo ƒe fe. Gake nye Hamea me tɔ geɖewo xɔa woƒe Zion-ha ƒe aʋatsonyawo dzi sena, eye eya ta mise egɔme be nyemenye Zion-ha me tɔ o, elabena wonye Yudatɔwo ƒe gãnyenye dzilawo, eye wonye gbe vɔ̃ɖiwo, Satana ŋutɔ ƒe fuku; eye maho wo le anyigba la dzi, elabena wobia nye domenyinu, eye megbe be maxɔ nu si nye tɔnye. Elabena nye, Yeshua HaMashiach, nye ŋutɔ, melé anyigba la ƒe agbalẽ ɖe asi; elabena metsɔ nye ŋutɔ nye ʋu si me nuvɔ̃ aɖeke mele o ƒlee, eye ku kple tsiewe ƒe safuiwo le asinye. Eye mexe fe ɖe amegbetɔwo ƒe nuvɔ̃wo ta, eye ame sia ame si ayɔ Aƒetɔ la ƒe ŋkɔ la akpɔ xɔxɔ.

Eya ta anyigba la nye tɔnye kple eƒe blibonyenye, eye makɔ anyigba la ŋuti tso woƒe vɔ̃ɖinyenye me, eye woatsrɔ̃ Satana ƒe ƒuƒoƒe la keŋkeŋ, eye eya ta matsɔ nubabla yeye la ava anyigba dzi eye maɖu dzi le dzɔdzɔenyenye me. Elabena esi anyigba la le viviti dom ɖe edzi eye wòle vɔ̃ɖi dom ɖe edzi le sekɛnd ɖe sia ɖe me la, nye vava evelia le gogom le sekɛnd ɖe sia ɖe me.

Mina mɔkpɔkpɔ gã nanɔ mia si ɖe nye vava ŋu; elabena mava eye mado tso dziƒowo me kple dziƒoaʋakɔwo, aʋakɔ si tɔgbi meli o gɔ̃ hã, elabena aʋakɔ si le abe tɔnye ene menɔ anyi kpɔ o, aʋakɔ si womate ŋu aɖu edzi o si me ame siwo nɔa agbe mavɔ eye womate ŋu awu wo alo aɖu wo dzi o le. Elabena Yawe na ŋusẽm ɖe taɖodzinu sia ta, eye mana ŋusẽ wo ahaɖo eteƒe na wo ɖe woƒe nuteƒewɔwɔ kple lɔlɔ̃ nam kple nye ŋkɔ, Yesu Kristo gɔ̃ hã ta! Elabena ame sia ame si lɔ̃a Yeshua HaMashiach eye wòsubɔnɛ la lɔ̃a Yawe hã eye wòsubɔnɛ, elabena ame aɖeke mekɔ wu Israel ƒe Mawu mavɔ gã la o! Ame sia ame si gbea Vi la, Fofo la mele eya hã si o; eye nyee nye Yesu Kristo, Fofo la ƒe Tenuvi si tsɔ eɖokui na ɖe ame katã ƒe nuvɔ̃wo ta, eye menye nunɔlagã tegbee le Melkizedek ƒe ɖoɖo nu.

Eye maɖu dzoka, lã wɔadã la, kple aʋatsonyagblɔɖila la dzi keŋkeŋ. Elabena woatsɔ lã wɔadã la kple aʋatsonyagblɔɖila la aƒu gbe agbagbee ɖe dzo ta ma si le biana kple dzo blɔtɔ xɔdzo si womate ŋu atsi gbeɖe o eye dzudzɔkeke aɖeke manɔ eme o gɔ̃ hã sekɛnd ɖeka me. Gake woagbugbɔ woƒe dɔ vɔ̃ɖiwo katã ana wo tegbee. Fuwɔame siwo katã wohe vɛ la, woaɖo eteƒe nɛ tegbee, elabena Yawe aɖo dɔ vɔ̃ɖi ɖe sia ɖe teƒe va se ɖe gaku mlɔetɔ dzi ɖe wo ŋutɔwo ƒe akɔta vɔ̃ɖiwo me, eye woaxe fe ɖe woƒe nuvlowɔwɔwo ta tegbee.

Eye eya ta woabla Satana ɖe aʋli la me ƒe akpe ɖeka, elabena aleae Yawe gblɔe. Nye, Yeshua HaMashiach, maɖu anyigba dzi le dzɔdzɔenyenye me ŋkeke ɖeka. Elabena le Yawe gbɔ la, ŋkeke ɖeka le abe ƒe akpe ɖeka ene eye ƒe akpe ɖeka le abe ŋkeke ɖeka ene. Eye maɖu dzi tso David ƒe fiazikpui dzi, eye le Israel la, nye dziɖuɖu anɔ anyi atsɔ awɔ ŋugbedodo siwo Yawe do na Fofoawo dzi, eye nye ŋkeke la ɖo vɔ kloe.

Gake gbã la, ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ ava anyigba dzi eye woakɔ anyigba la ŋuti tso nu vɔ̃ɖi ɖe sia ɖe me. Elabena womaɖe mɔ nu vɔ̃ɖi aɖeke nage ɖe nye fiaɖuƒe me o, afi si ŋutifafa kple dedienɔnɔ anɔ abe ale si Yawe ŋutɔ do ŋugbee ene. Eye dzata amlɔ anyi kple alẽvi la. Nye, nye ŋutɔ, Yeshua HaMashiach, megblɔa nyawo hafi wodzɔna, be miadze sii be Israel ƒe Mawu la va be yeaƒo yeƒe Israel ƒomea ƒe alẽ siwo bu la nu aƒu gbɔnye, Alẽkplɔlagã la, eye matsɔ ga atikplɔ aƒo dukɔwo, eye mazi Fofo la ƒe nuwɔwɔ dzi keŋkeŋ le dzɔdzɔenyenye kple kɔkɔenyenye vavã me. Ɣeyiɣi aɖeke megali o.

Nyee nye Yeshua HaMashiach, Mesia vavã la na nye dukɔ Israel.

Nyee nye ame si aɖu anyigba dzi le dzɔdzɔenyenye me tso David ƒe Fiazikpui dzi.

Nyee nye ame si adze le alilikpowo me kple dziƒoaʋakɔwo, elabena makpɔ wo dzi.

Nyee nye ame si aƒo nye ame susɔeawo nu abe ameha gã aɖe ene tso xaxa si gbɔna me.

Nyee nye ame si dodaa amewo ƒe dzi kpɔna, eye mama alẽwo ɖa le gbɔ̃wo gbɔ.

Nyee nye nuŋeŋe ƒe Aƒetɔ, eye nuŋeɣiwo de.

Nyee nye Aƒetɔwo ƒe Aƒetɔ kple Fiawo ƒe Fia, eye ame kae ate ŋu anɔ te?

Mikpɔ ɖa, Aƒetɔ la ƒe ŋkeke le dzedze ge ɖe anyigba dzi le dzɔgbevɔ̃e kple ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ me. Elabena eƒe dziku ŋkeke gã la ɖo, eye ame kae ate ŋu anɔ te?
