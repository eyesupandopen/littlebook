November 6, 2024

Ne Wogblɔ Be Ŋutifafa Kple Dedienɔnɔ!

Tso Yeshua HaMashiach si hã nye Kristo Yesu gbɔ:

Mise Yeshua HaMashiach, ame si Yahweh dɔ ɖa be wòanye miaƒe Mesia, Fia, Aƒetɔ, kple Nunɔlagã tegbee le Melkizedek ƒe ɖoɖo nu la ƒe Nyawo, eye mibɔbɔ miaƒe dziwo le toɖoɖo me na nye Nyawo elabena meva be maxlɔ̃ nu nuteƒewɔla siwo le nye Hamea me, kple nye Ŋugbetɔ hã, (kple ame siwo katã to le wo si be woasee, eɖanye wo te ɖe afi sia alo le didiƒe o) ake tso aʋatsonya gã si le dɔ wɔm le xexeame katã fifia ŋu, si agblẽ ɖe edzi ne woaɖe mexeɖenua ɖa kpuie, kple kpoyi! Nyemegblɔe be xedala kae trɔ̃a mɔ̃ ɖe xe ŋkume oa? Oo, xɔse ʋɛ tɔwo!

Eya ta mena miese egɔme ake nu si mefia nye ƒometɔwo esime meva Israel abe Mawu ƒe Alẽvi ene, eya ta mise nye Nyawo! Elabena aʋatsokristowo, kple aʋatsonyagblɔɖilawo ado, ame siwo awɔ dzesi kple nukunu gãwo, ale be ne anya wɔ la, woable tiatia la ŋutɔ hã. Kpɔ ɖa, megblɔe na mi xoxo. Eya ta ne wogblɔ na mi be, Kpɔ ɖa, ele gbedzi la, migado go o: Kpɔ ɖa, ele xɔgãwo me la, migaxɔe se o. Elabena abe ale si dzikedzo dzona tso ɣedzeƒe, eye wokpɔnɛ va se ɖe ɣetoɖoƒe ene la, nenemae Amegbetɔvi la ƒe vava hã anɔ! Eya ta mena miese egɔme be nye dzedze gogo eye wòava kple ŋusẽ kpoyi, eye womagbe nye nunɔamesi si meƒle kple nye ŋutɔ nye ʋu la nam o, nye ŋugbetɔ kple ŋutilã nuteƒewɔla, nye hame nuteƒewɔla si melɔ̃ kple lɔlɔ̃ mavɔ la gɔ̃ hã! Mianye tɔnye!

Eye nenemae wòle, nye lɔlɔ̃tɔwo, be aʋatsokristowo do eye wo dometɔ geɖe li. Gake fifia ʋɔ dã la ƒe ɣeyiɣiwo de eye Yakob ƒe xaxa la va ɖo eye ame siwo katã susɔ siwo nye Adam tɔwo le anyigba dzi la awɔ tiatia mavɔ aɖe. Elabena wotsɔe na ʋɔ dã la be wòaɖu anyigba la dzi kple eƒe kplɔla vɔ̃ɖi eveawo, lã wɔadã si tso atsiaƒu me kple lã wɔadã si tso anyigba dzi, si hã nye woƒe aʋatsonyagblɔɖila. Ðe miadzra mia ɖokuiwo ɖo be miagadze aʋatsonya gã la me to toɖoɖo nye gbe mea? Ðe miadze agbagba adze xexeame yome le eƒe aʋatsonyawo kple aʋatsokakawo me ayi tsɔtsrɔ̃ gã me to to gbegbe na nye gbe, ne meva be maxlɔ̃ nu mi tso aʋatsonya gã si xɔ xexeame fifia ŋu hã? Ðe mieɖe mɔ aʋatsonya gã si wona mi hena mɔkpɔkpɔ dzodzro la ʋuʋu miaƒe dziwo kple miaƒe xɔsea?

Eye nenemae wòle be naneke mate ŋu atrɔ ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ ƒe gbeƒãɖeɖe siwo Yahweh ŋutɔ na ɖe Babilon Ɣaɣla la dzi o, ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ si wotsɔ de nye asi sesẽwo me be maɖe asi le eŋu ne woƒo nuteƒewɔlawo nu ƒu eye woɖe mexeɖenua ɖa, eye adzɔ kpoyi abe ale si wodo ŋugbee ene! Ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ siwo wogblɔ ɖi tsɔ xlɔ̃ nu Babilon Ɣaɣla me nɔlawo, nuxlɔ̃ame si wogbe kura esime woko alɔme le xɔsemakpɔmakpɔ me. Nye nuxlɔ̃ame geɖe, geɖe siwo wotsɔ de nye nyagblɔɖila geɖewo siwo gblɔ nuxlɔ̃amenya siawo nuteƒewɔwɔtɔe ƒe geɖe fifia la ƒe nu me. Nuxlɔ̃ame siwo medzudzɔ eye le wo teƒe la, gbeƒãɖeɖewo le tsitre fifia, Yahweh ƒe gbeƒãɖeɖewo, kple Yeshua HaMashiach tɔwo, gbeƒãɖeɖe siwo mate ŋu anya wɔ na nuwɔwɔ aɖeke be wòaxe mɔ na wo alo atrɔ wo o. Elabena nu si wogblɔ ɖi le Yahweh ƒe ŋusẽ kple fiazikpui me la, ame aɖeke mate ŋu atrɔe o, elabena nenemae Dzɔdzɔenyenye ƒe Mawu Mavɔ, ame si wɔa nuwo katã le eya ŋutɔ ƒe lɔlɔ̃nu ƒe aɖaŋuɖoɖo nu la ƒe mɔwo le.

Eye eya ta megblɔ na mi nye nuteƒewɔlawo, abe ale si megblɔ na Simon Petro le nye vɔsasa ƒe gaƒoƒoa me ene: Simon, Simon, kpɔ ɖa, Satana di mi, be yeagbã mi abe lu ene. Gake medo gbe ɖa ɖe ta wò, be wò xɔse nagado kpo nu o: eya ta ne etrɔ la, do ŋusẽ nɔviwòwo. Eye eya ta meva mia gbɔ le gaƒoƒo mamlɛ sia ƒe yomedzedze me (elabena gaƒoƒoa vɔ kloe) be maxlɔ̃ nu mi le mɔ ma ke nu ale be miadzra mia ɖokuiwo ɖo le mɔ la dzi eye mede se na mi ame siwo klẽna abe Xexeame ƒe kekeliwo ene be miado ŋusẽ mia nɔviwo eye miado ŋusẽ mia nɔewo kple Nya siawo le lɔlɔ̃ ƒe xɔse kple toɖoɖo me ale be mia dometɔ ɖe sia ɖe nadzra eɖokui ɖo eye wòakpɔ eɖokui ta le mɔ la dzi, eya ta miɖo to nye gbe eye miazɔ eme.

Kpɔ ɖa! Woŋlɔe be: Eye le esia ta Mawu aɖo aʋatsonya gã ɖe wo, be woaxɔ aʋatsonya dzi ase: be woabu fɔ wo katã ame siwo mexɔ nyateƒe la dzi se o, ke boŋ wokpɔ dzidzɔ le madzɔmadzɔnyenye me. Ðe miadze aʋatsonya sia si menye mia tɔ o la mea, nye lɔlɔ̃tɔwo? Nyateƒe, nyateƒe, megblɔ na nye hamea be, ɖe miaƒu du ayi ameblela siawo ƒe akɔkpakpa mea? Ame siwo ʋɔ dã la ŋutɔ dɔ ɖa hena mia tsɔtsrɔ̃ gɔ̃ hã? Eya ta, nye lɔlɔ̃tɔwo, miyi edzi mido go tso wo dome eye miaɖe mia ɖokuiwo ɖa! Migakpɔ gome le woƒe nu vɔ̃ɖi wɔwɔwo kple mɔkpɔkpɔwo me o elabena woƒe dzo kple adzetotowo ble ame geɖe, eye ame si wotia fifia la ƒoa nu abe ʋɔ dã ene, ewɔa ŋugbedodo gãwo kple nuƒo gãwo tso eɖokui ŋu eye ame geɖe adze eƒe mɔ gbegblẽwo yome eye woable ame geɖe eye woaɖe mɔ̃ na wo ahalé wo, eya ta mitrɔ ɖe nye gbe ƒe ɖiɖi ŋu eye miese nye nyawo eye miaƒu du ava gbɔnye nye lɔlɔ̃tɔwo, migatrɔ ɖe miame o, alo ɖe ɖusime o eye madzra mi ɖo dedie le nye asi mavɔwo ƒe asitɛ me si me ame aɖeke mate ŋu ahe mi tso nye asi sesẽ la me o.

Eya ta nu ka tae mieɖe mɔ miaƒe dziwo le fu ɖem na mi, nye nuteƒewɔlawo kple nyateƒetɔwo, ame siwo melɔ̃ kple lɔlɔ̃ mavɔ gɔ̃ hã, ame siwo katã ʋɔ dã la di be yeaɖe mɔ̃ na le aʋatsonya kple ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ si menye mia tɔ o me. Eya ta, migawɔ ɖeka kple woƒe aʋatsonyawo o eye mido go tso wo dome eye miaɖe mia ɖokuiwo ɖa! Eye na mia dometɔ siwo trɔ tso aʋatsonya gã la me nyateƒetɔe la, mede se na mi be migatrɔ yi kura o. Kpɔ ɖa! Yahweh, Mawu Mavɔ Gã la, alo Yeshua HaMashiach mado alo medo dunyahehe ƒe kuxi gbɔ kpɔnu aɖeke alo xexeame ƒe kplɔla aɖeke be wòada xexeame ƒe kuxiwo le ɣeyiɣi sia me o. Kuxi siwo le vavam le ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ me ɖe ame siwo le anyigba dzi tso Yahweh ŋutɔ gbɔ gɔ̃ hã, ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ si me ame aɖeke mate ŋu asi le o, negbe nuteƒewɔla siwo le Kristo Yesu me ko. Miese nu si tututu mena Paulo ŋlɔ na Efesotɔwo le lɛta si mena wòŋlɔ ɖe tanye na nye hame lɔlɔ̃a me la gɔ̃me:

Mlɔeba, nɔviŋutsuwo, misẽ ŋu le Aƒetɔ la me, kple eƒe ŋusẽ ƒe ŋusẽ me. Mido Mawu ƒe aʋawɔnu blibo la, be miate ŋu anɔ tsitre ɖe abosam ƒe aʋadzedzewo ŋu. Elabena menye ŋutilã kple ʋu ŋue míele aʋa wɔm kpli o, ke boŋ kple dziɖuɖuwo, kple ŋusẽwo, kple xexeame ƒe nutodziɖulawo, xexe sia me ƒe viviti ƒe fiaviwo, kple gbɔgbɔ me vɔ̃ɖinyenye, siwo le teƒe kɔkɔwo. Eya ta miese egɔme nye lɔlɔ̃tɔwo be nye nyawo matrɔmatrɔe, eya ta nu ka tae nye, Kristo Yesu miaƒe Aƒetɔ si lia la, mado anyigba dzi nutodziɖula, alo kplɔla alo fia si axe mɔ na ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ siwo gbɔna la ɖa na mi gbeɖe? Aleke Yahweh, ame si ƒe lɔlɔ̃nu mewu enu bliboe eye ame si mate ŋu aka aʋatso gbeɖe o la, ate ŋu atsi tre ɖe eɖokui ŋu le eƒe ɖoɖo mavɔ siwo wotsɔ nam bliboe hena wuwnu me?

Elabena xexeame ƒe kplɔlawo katã na woƒe mɔɖeɖe kple ŋusẽ ʋɔ dã la, elabena nenemae wotsɔ na wo be woawɔe elabena Yahweh ƒe futɔwo gɔ̃ hã ɖoa to eƒe gbe eye woawɔ eƒe lɔlɔ̃nu si wotsɔ na wo le ɣeyiɣi kpui sia si le vavam kple dziku gã ɖe anyigba dzi la me! Elabena Alẽvi la ƒe dziku kple Mawu Ŋusẽkatãtɔ la ƒe dziku ƒe ɣeyiɣiwo de eye naneke meli si ate ŋu axe mɔ, alo axe mɔ kura na nu si woaɖe asi le eŋu kpuie alea ne meƒo nye nuteƒewɔlawo nu ƒu ɖe dziƒowo megbe o. Eye eya ta medo ɣli! Ame kae ate ŋu anɔ tsitre ɖe Yahweh, NYE SI MENYE gã la ƒe lɔlɔ̃nu mavɔ ŋu?!

Gake, miese egɔme be le nye amenuveve ƒe ɣeyiɣia me la, mefia nu nye Hamea be woado gbe ɖa ɖe woƒe xexeame ƒe kplɔlawo ta, kple nyanya be woƒe xexeame ƒe kplɔlawo nye vɔ̃ɖitɔwo le dzɔdzɔme nu eye ale be to ame kɔkɔewo ƒe gbedodoɖa me la, woaxe mɔ na vɔ̃ɖinyenye ɖe edzi (eye nye mexeɖenua gaxe mɔ kokoko), eye kplɔla siawo ate ŋu atia nu si awɔ dɔ na ame siwo lɔ̃a Mawu ƒe nyui kple nyonyo, eye nenemae nye amenuveve ƒe ɣeyiɣia nɔ, togbɔ be yomedzedzewo sɔ gbɔ hã. Eye togbɔ be, ame dzɔdzɔe ƒe gbedodoɖa vevie wɔa dɔ geɖe hã, miese egɔme be xexeame ƒe kplɔla aɖeke meli si wotsɔ na hena anyigba la ƒe xɔxɔ o va se ɖe esime mava kple Fiawo kple Nunɔla mavɔwo ƒe aʋakɔ gã aɖe siwo aɖu anyigba dzi le dzɔdzɔenyenye me le nye se nu elabena menye Aƒetɔwo ƒe Aƒetɔ kple Fiawo ƒe Fia eye menye Nunɔlagã tegbee le Melkizedek ƒe ɖoɖo nu.

Miese egɔme bliboe kple nubabla aɖeke manɔmee, xexeame ƒe kplɔla aɖeke meli si ate ŋu ahe ŋutifafa ava anyigba dzi o. Xexeame ƒe kplɔla aɖeke meli si ate ŋu ahe dedienɔnɔ ava anyigba dzi o. Xexeame ƒe kplɔla aɖeke meli si ate ŋu agbugbɔ kpekpeɖeŋu ava anyigba dzi o. Togbɔ be, woado ŋugbe ŋutifafa kple dedienɔnɔ kple kpekpeɖeŋu si wogbugbɔ ɖo anyi hã, miese egɔme be ŋusẽ aɖeke mele wo si be woana nu siwo ƒe ŋugbe wodo o, togbɔ be woana xexeame ƒe nu dzodzrowo ame siwo xɔ lã wɔadã la ƒe dzesi, madzɔmadzɔnyenye ƒe fetu si woaxɔ le wo si keŋkeŋ le ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ me gɔ̃ hã! Eya ta migadze wo yome o elabena ame siwo katã dzea wo yome la, mlɔeba la, woabia tso wo si be woaxɔ lã wɔadã la ƒe dzesi eye ame siwo wɔe la, wotsɔ wo na tohehe ƒe dzo mavɔ si matsi gbeɖe o tegbee eye xexeame kple eƒe nu dzodzrowo le nu yim. Eye ʋɔ dã la afɔ bliboe kple eƒe lã wɔadãwo, eye lã wɔadã si tso anyigba dzi la dzra eƒe aɖi si da ƒe aɖuɖu na la ɖo be wòado eƒe aɖi eye woatsɔ ga aƒo tsaka kple anyi, gake womaku ɖe wo nɔewo ŋu o.

Eye nenemae woŋlɔe eye woŋlɔe ɖi tututu le nye ɖeɖefia ƒe ɖaseɖiɖi me, Yesu Kristo ƒe Ðeɖefia gɔ̃ hã ale be ame siwo katã si ŋku le be woakpɔ eye to le wo si be woase, kple dzi bɔbɔe be woase egɔme la, nase egɔme, eye nenemae Yohanes ŋlɔe: Eye dɔla la gblɔ nam be, Nu ka tae nèle nukpe wɔm? Magblɔ nyɔnu la, kple lã wɔadã si tsɔe, si si ta adre kple dzo ewo le la ƒe nya ɣaɣla la na wò. Lã wɔadã si nèkpɔ la nɔ anyi, eye megali o; eye wòado tso aʋe goglo la me, eye wòayi tsɔtsrɔ̃ me: eye ame siwo le anyigba dzi la awɔ nuku ame siwo ƒe ŋkɔwo womeŋlɔ ɖe agbegbalẽ la me tso xexeame ƒe gɔmeɖoanyi ɣi o, ne wokpɔ lã wɔadã si nɔ anyi, eye megali o, gake eli la.

Eya ta, nye vi suewo mixɔ nusrɔ̃lele eye mena miese egɔme togbɔ be ʋɔ dã la ƒe fiaɖuƒe mate ŋu afɔ bliboe haɖe o (elabena mexeɖenua axe mɔ va se ɖe esime mexeɖenua megaxe mɔ o, ne wokplɔ nuteƒewɔla siwo le Kristo me yi dziƒowo gɔ̃ hã) woɖe mɔ na ame siwo dze anyi la be woadzra ɖo ɖe woƒe aʋadzedze kpoyi ŋu eye Mawu dɔa aʋatsonya gã ɖe ame siwo le anyigba dzi le tomatu me. Gake nu siawo menye mia tɔ o nye nuteƒewɔlawo kple tiatiatɔwo, eye nye, Yeshua HaMashiach meɖe gbeƒã eye meɖi ɖase le nyateƒe sia ŋu kple nya siawo siwo medɔ to nye dɔla kple subɔla dzi be woaxlẽ hena akɔfafa kple toɖoɖo na ame siwo katã nya siawo ku ɖo. Eye eya ta mebia mia dometɔ siwo alɔ̃ aʋatsonya sia wu nye nyateƒe mavɔ la be, nu ka tae miele nukpe wɔm? Ðe miagatia kplɔla vɔ̃ɖiwo ƒe aʋatsowo wu nyateƒe kple akɔfafa si nyagblɔɖi ƒe gbɔgbɔ hena?

Eya ta, mena miese egɔme be kplɔla vɔ̃ɖi eve siawo siwo li eye womeli o eye gake woanɔ anyi BLIBOE la, mefɔ bliboe haɖe o, eye woafɔ bliboe tso ɖoɖo xoxoa ƒe afi kple aƒedo me eye woƒe ɖoɖo yeyea afɔ bliboe! Wotia woƒe aʋatso Moshiach na wo ɖokuiwo fifia, Barabbas gɔ̃ hã, eye wòatu woƒe gbedoxɔ, eye lã wɔadã si tso atsiaƒu me la gakpɔtɔ le eƒe amegãxɔxɔ tsukɔ la kplɔm eye woakpɔ dzidzedze, eye woahe Babilon Ɣaɣla ƒe tsɔtsrɔ̃ blibo vɛ, elabena to aʋatso me wowɔa aʋa eye nenemae woƒe dzɔdzɔme le.

Eye yiyi si dze anyigbawo dzi lae dɔ wo ɖa, eye yiyi lae tiae be wòakplɔ anyigbawo ne wofɔ bliboe. Elabena Satana ƒe Ƒuƒoƒo lae wɔ woƒe tiatia eye wotia woƒe aʋatso Moshiach yeyea eye Babilon Ɣaɣla gana eƒe mɔɖeɖe ʋɔ dã la ake, eye nenemae woatsɔ wo ade ʋɔ dã si na ŋusẽ wo be woawɔ dziku kple ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ la si me bliboe, ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ si wodɔ abe akpa eve ene tso Yahweh ŋutɔ gbɔ gɔ̃ hã, ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ si mehe ɖe megbe o eye wòle vavam kpuie eye eya ta wotia wo ŋutɔ woƒe tsɔtsrɔ̃ na wo ɖokuiwo.

Eye nenemae wòle be Babilon Ɣaɣla ɖe gbeƒã eƒe etsɔme fifia be yevo tso Yahweh ƒe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ me esime wole didim be yewoasi esime wole alɔme kom le woƒe xɔsemakpɔmakpɔ mɔwo nu. Elabena Babilon Ɣaɣla lɔ ɖe yiyi ƒe dɔ me bliboe, eye mate ŋu asi o, eye Babilon Ɣaɣla le kɔkɔtiwo me le mɔ kpɔm na eƒe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ mamlɛtɔ. Eye woayi edzi agblẽ nu, afi fi, awu ame, aha nu eye woaxɔ eƒe kpekpeɖeŋu kple eƒe dzidzimeviwo kple ŋusẽ elabena nenemae wotsɔ na wo be woawɔ eye gbegbe asusɔ afi si woaha anyigba la ɖe edzi hena nunɔamesi siwo ƒe nudzodzro le wo me vevie.

Eye tsɔtsrɔ̃ aɖi ɖe wo dzi kpoyi, eye le gaƒoƒo ɖeka me la, woatsrɔ̃e. Megblɔe ake be, aʋa adze anyi kpoyi le anyigba blibo la dzi, eye dzo adza, eye woada aʋawɔnu geɖewo ɖe edzi hena eƒe tsɔtsrɔ̃. Eƒe du gãtɔ afia dzo eye tsɔtsrɔ̃ ƒe ƒutsotso gãwo aƒo ɖe eƒe ɣedzeƒe kple ɣetoɖoƒe ƒutawo. Anyigba aʋuʋu vevie, eye woama eme ɖe eve, eye afisi anyigba nyo le tsã la, tsiwo akpɔtɔ ɖe afima si amae ɖe eve, eye adze anyi keŋkeŋ le eƒe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ teƒe eve la me. Ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ si woawo ŋutɔwo tia esime woŋe aɖaba ƒu nye gbe si meto nye apostolowo, nyagblɔɖilawo kple subɔla gãwo kple suewo dzi dɔ ɖa la dzi: le nyateƒe me la, eŋe aɖaba ƒu wo katã dzi hena eya ŋutɔ ƒe tsɔtsrɔ̃ eye magafo di gbeɖe o. Eya ta wotsɔ anyigba la de asi na dzoka la eye wòatsɔ eƒe dzodzeƒe dziɖuɖu la ana eƒe kplɔla vɔ̃ɖi siwo wòfɔ ɖe te ɖe eya ŋutɔ ƒe taɖodzinuwo ta eye wogblẽ tegbee.

Eya ta, migadze lã wɔadã si tso anyigba me la ƒe aʋatso me o elabena eƒoa nu abe dzoka ene. Migadze Babilon Ɣaɣla la me tɔ siwo le lalam na woƒe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ la yome o, elabena eƒe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ ava godoo le gaƒoƒo ɖeka me eye kpoyi ko la, megali o!
