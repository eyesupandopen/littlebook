Eye Yiyi Aɖe Va

Mise, mise, nye Nyawo O Israel, eye mibɔbɔ miaƒe dziwo le toɖoɖo me na nye gbe ƒe ɖiɖi eye mixɔ Yeshua HaMashiach ƒe Nyawo le fafa me be to Nya Nyui Mavɔ la ƒe Nyawo dzi la, miakpɔ xɔxɔ eye miava agbe mavɔ me le miaƒe Mesia vavã la, ame si ʋlina ta na mi le lɔlɔ̃ me vavã la me. Eya ta meva be maxlɔ̃ nu mi, Yakob ƒomea kple ame siwo katã xlẽa Nya Nyui Mavɔ la ƒe nyawo he lɔ̃a ɖe wo dzi le xɔxɔ ƒe xɔse me be xɔse nanɔ wo si le Yeshua HaMashiach, ame si hã nye Yesu Kristo la me, eye be xɔse nanɔ wo si le Yahweh, I AM (Nyenye) Gã la, ame si ƒe dzi me Nya Nyui Mavɔ la ɣla ɖo va se ɖe eƒe ɖeɖefia ɣiyiɣiwo me, eye ale Yohanes Nyaɖeɖefiala la do nutre dzadza adreawo, ame siwo ƒe gbewo wòse la ƒe gbewo. Eye ale meva be maxlɔ̃ nu mi tso yiyi si lɔ eƒe dɔ eye wòlé xexeame ɖe eƒe asikpakpa me la ŋu; esi wòle ame bubuwo ƒe agbeʋu nom gɔ̃ hã, eye be miate ŋu aƒo asa na eƒe dɔ geɖeawo be womagalé mi ɖe eƒe asikpakpa wuwua me o.

Eya ta, mibɔbɔ miaƒe dziwo le toɖoɖo me eye mise miaƒe Mesia vavã la, Yeshua HaMashiach ŋutɔ ƒe nyawo! Elabena vavã meva be mawɔ nunyala siwo le mia dome la nunyalawo wu be miate ŋu adze si ɣiyiɣi siwo me miele eye miase miaƒe futɔwo kple woƒe nɔnɔme kple aʋatsokaka ƒe mɔwo gɔme; elabena wozɔna le ɣaɣla kple adzame gã me eye wotsɔa aʋatso wɔa aʋae. Elabena wo katã wonye alakpatɔ siwo wɔa alakpa, eye yiyi la zɔna le ɣaɣla kple adzame gã me va se ɖe esime yiyi la ƒe aboyonu la ɖo eƒe dɔ, si yiyi la azã ɖe eƒe tame vɔ̃wo ŋu ɣesiaɣi la me.

Eya ta, meva be maxlɔ̃ nu mi tso yiyi la ŋu eye mafia eƒe mɔwo mi be miate ŋu aƒo asa na eƒe aɖi eye miadzra mia ɖokuiwo ɖo tso eƒe asikpakpa si wɔa ame lãkuku eye wòwua ame la me. Gake yiyi kae wɔ eɖokui fiaɖuƒe kple dukɔ kpɔ? Eye yiyi kae de ha kple ame dzedzwoawo ƒe habɔbɔ kpɔ, eye wòwɔ eƒe akpa le aglãdzedze vɔ̃ɖi me kple dzidzɔ gɔ̃ hã? Eye yiyi kae tsɔ ame siwo wolé ɖe eƒe dɔ me la ƒe amenyenye wɔ eɖokui tɔe kpɔ? Gake nenemae yiyi si ŋu meva be maxlɔ̃ nu mi tso la le eye vavã, vavã, yiyi la nye futɔwo ƒe fiaɖuƒea ƒe akpa geɖewo dometɔ ɖeka eye enye yiyi aɖitɔ kple vɔ̃ɖi vavã.

Elabena yiyi la lɔa eƒe dɔ le adzame kple aʋatso me. Aʋatso ƒe dɔ kple fe ƒe dɔ gɔ̃ hãe yiyi la lɔna, eye eƒe dɔ la xatsa anyigba blibo la katã azɔ le eƒe adzame asikpakpa me. Mibu yiyi aɖe ŋu le eƒe dzɔdzɔme nɔnɔme me, elɔa eƒe dɔ le adzame le teƒe siwo wòate ŋu alé eƒe aboyonuwo le esime eƒe aboyonuawo dze eƒe dɔa me le manyamanya me gɔ̃ hã. Eye esi eƒe aboyonua ʋlina be yeaɖe ye ɖokui tso dɔ si me wòɖo le manyamanya me si wɔe be wògatsɔ eɖokui de mɔ̃ me geɖe wu la, yiyi la dzea dɔ gɔme eye wòdoa eƒe aɖi ɖe eme esime wòxatsa eƒe aboyonua ɖe eƒe dɔa me. Emegbe yiyi la dzraa eƒe aboyonua ɖo ɖe eya ŋutɔ ƒe taɖodzinuwo ŋu eye wòɖua eƒe aboyonua vivivi va se ɖe esime naneke megasusɔ o, gblẽa eƒe aboyonua ɖi aƒedoe kura va se ɖe esime vavã eƒe aboyonua megali o.

Gake nenemae dzɔdzɔme yiyi si wowɔ na Yahweh ƒe taɖodzinuwo ƒe mɔwo le, negbe mia dometɔ kae kpɔ yiyi aɖe wòwɔ dɔ aduadu kple yiyi bubuwo ƒe fiaɖuƒe kple dukɔ kpɔ; gake ame dzedzwoawo ƒo woƒe yiyiwo nu ƒu le ŋkeke mamlɛ siawo me. Eye ale meva be maxlɔ̃ nu mi tso dukɔ si dzɔ tso Yafet, ame si dzi Gomer, eye tso Gomer me Askenaz va, eye tso Askenaz me dukɔ aɖe va elabena Askenaz le dukɔwo ƒe xexlẽme me le blema ɖoɖowo me le ŋkeke siwo me amegbetɔwo nɔ Noa ƒe nubabla si amegbetɔwo gblẽ ɖi la te gɔ̃ hã; eye hã Abram nuteƒewɔla nɔ anyi si di Yahweh ƒe mɔwo esime anyigba la gadze aglã ɖe wo Wɔla ŋu ake. Gake togbɔ be, dzɔdzɔme amegbetɔ gbe Yahweh ƒe nubabla hã; Yahweh wɔa nuteƒe le eƒe ŋugbedodowo me nenema ke. Eye nubabla si Yahweh wɔ kple Noa (ame si nye ame dzɔdzɔe le Yahweh ŋkume) ƒe ŋugbedodo ɖeka pɛ hã medze anyigba womevada edzi o eye wo katã anye ẽ kple amen tegbee elabena Yahweh wɔa nuteƒe blibo ɣesiaɣi!

Esi tsi ɖɔ anyigba dzi le Noa ƒe ŋkekewo me vɔ la, woma anyigba la to dzadzadada me na Noa viwo le Peleg ƒe ŋkekewo me, eye ale Yafet yi eƒe domenyinu me le dziehe nutowo me. Eye dukɔ la nye gbe ɖeka, eye Ham viwo ƒo ƒu ɖe anyigba si va zu Babilon la dzi. Eye Nimrod ƒe fiaviwo kple Fut, Mizraim, Kus, kple Kanaan kpakple woƒe ƒomewo ɖo aɖaŋu aduadu be yewoatso yewoƒe du eye le eme la, mɔ́ sesẽ aɖe si nye mɔ́ kɔkɔ aɖe gɔ̃ hã ale be yewoate ŋu awɔ aʋa kple dziƒowo eye be yewoaƒo yewo ɖokuiwo nu ƒu ale be yewoawɔ ŋkɔ na yewo ɖokuiwo le aglãdzedze ɖe Yahweh ƒe se si nye be woawɔ vi adzi asɔ gbɔ ale be woagayɔ anyigba la dzi ake la ŋu. Gake nenemae dzɔdzɔme amegbetɔ si mexɔa nu sena o, ame si subɔa nu vɔ̃ le eƒe nudzodzrowo kple dada me eye to esia me wòsubɔa nu vɔ̃ ƒe Fofo la ƒe mɔwo le; Abosam ŋutɔ gɔ̃ hã, to amegbetɔ ƒe toomaɖomaɖo Yahweh me le nu vɔ̃ si le dzɔdzɔme amegbetɔ ƒe ŋutilã me ta.

Eye tso Nimrod, ame si tsi le vɔ̃ɖinyenye me ƒe dzidzimeviwo me la, wogblɔna be tso ame vɔ̃ɖi gbɔe vɔ̃ɖinyenye geɖe tsona. Eye ale Nimrod ƒo ɖi anyigba la le ame dzedzwoawo ƒe mɔwo nu eye tso eƒe ŋunyɔwɔlawo kple afakakalawo gbɔe blema ɣaɣlanuwo ƒe sukuwo tso afi si ame dzedzwoawo ƒe ŋunyɔwɔlawo fia woƒe ɣaɣlanu dzodzrowo ame siwo woatsɔ aƒu gbe ɖe dzo la me tegbee le Satanawo ƒe nu vɔ̃wo me, eye woawɔ ɖeka kpli wo le woƒe domenyinu mavɔ la me. Eye Nimrod do le fiaɖuƒe gbãtɔ me le Noa ƒe tsiɖɔɖɔa megbe, afi si Nimrod ɖu anyigba la dzi le madzɔmadzɔnyenye me gɔ̃ hã; dziɖuɖu si ame dzedzwoawo di be wòanye yewo tɔ bliboe le anyigba dzi gɔ̃ hã, gake sikaɣi aɖeke manɔ wo si o negbe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ sesẽ ko.

Eya ta Yahweh le ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ me na anyigba ʋu eye wòmi woƒe mɔ́ la ƒe akpa etɔ̃lia, eye dzo dze tso dziƒo fiã mɔ́ la ƒe akpa etɔ̃lia bubu, eye woɖe mɔ ɖe akpa etɔ̃lia ŋu wòsusɔ abe ŋkuɖodzinu ɖe wo ŋu ene. Eya ta, woyɔa eƒe ŋkɔ be Babel, elabena afi mae Yahweh tɔtɔ anyigba blibo la katã ƒe gbe le: eye tso afi mae Yahweh ka wo hlẽ ɖe anyigba blibo la katã dzi. Eye dukɔ kple ame geɖewo gbe Yahweh ame si bla nu kple Noa la eye woŋlɔe be, eye ale woŋlɔ nubabla si Noa wɔ kple Yahweh la be le woƒe domenyinu nu eye le gbe siwo wodo nu la, woŋlɔ Yahweh ƒe nubabla kple Noa be, eye wotrɔ ɖe ame dzedzwoawo ƒe mɔwo ŋu elabena tiatia bubu aɖeke meli o. (Eye hã Abram va zu Abraham eye ame dzɔdzɔe la anɔ agbe to xɔse me!)

Le Babilon la, Nimrod wɔ vɔ̃ geɖe esime wòwɔ trɔ̃ geɖewo eye wòyɔ eɖokui be mawu eye wòwɔ eƒe busu etɔ̃ɖeka, etɔ̃ kɔkɔemaɖomaɖo aɖe gɔ̃ hã, si ƒe taɖodzinue nye be wòaɖu mawunyenye vavã la kple nyɔnu la ƒe dzidzimevi si ŋugbe wodo la dzi eye wòakplɔ amegbetɔviwo tra le madzɔmadzɔnyenye me. Eye ale Nimrod ɖo eƒe ɣaɣlanu suku siwo woyɔna ale la anyi esime ame dzedzwoawo ƒe sidzedze gade gɔme kaka eye wòxatsa anyigba la dzi ake eye amegbetɔwo gatrɔ Yahweh ƒe nyateƒe la wòzu aʋatso eye wosubɔ nuwɔwɔawo wu Wɔla la, ame si woyra tegbee, Amen!

Eye esi woka amewo hlẽ tso Babilon la, nenema kee woka ame dzedzwoawo ƒe sidzedze kple mɔ aʋatsotɔwo hlẽ kpli wo, kple woƒe kɔnu nyɔŋu kple busutɔwo katã siwo nye ame siwo ɖua lã kple ʋu kple ame siwo noa Adam ƒe ʋu si me ŋutilã la ƒe agbe le; nuwɔna gbegblẽ kple ŋunyɔtɔ siwo Yahweh xe mɔ ɖo zi ɖeka, na ame katã, eye tegbee elabena wowɔ Adam ɖe Yahweh ƒe nɔnɔme nu eye ŋutilã la ƒe agbe le ʋu la me. Gake woyi edzi le woƒe mɔ nyɔŋu kple busutɔwo me va se ɖe egbe, eye wodzra woƒe nuwɔnawo kple kɔnuwo ɖo le ame siwo subɔa gbɔgbɔ vɔ̃wo hedea ta agu na trɔ̃ mumuwo me eye esiawoe nye yiyi la ƒe mɔwo, eye hã yiyi la wɔa eɖokui abe Yakob dzɔdzɔe la ene, ame si me gome alo akpa aɖeke mele esi o.

Eye ale vɔ̃ɖinyenye ƒe mɔwo gado ke ɖe dukɔwo me tɔwo ƒe mɔwo me ake. Eye tso Gomer me Askenaz va eye tso Askenaz me le ɣeyiɣi aɖe megbe la, dukɔ gã aɖe va siwo va zu lãnyi-tsa-nɔla siwo nɔ Eurasia gbedzi kple eƒe nutowo me hetsa le afi ma afi si wowɔ woƒe fiaɖuƒe le gɔ̃ hã. Esi me gɔ̃ hã, woƒe fiaɖuƒe la dze agbagba le madzɔmadzɔnyenye me eye wòdze woƒe futɔwo si me nenema ke. Ame siwo ƒe futɔwo gɔ̃ hã dze wo dzi le woƒe madzɔmadzɔnyenye ta eye le esi teƒe be woaxɔ Yesu Nazaretitɔ la ahatrɔ tso vɔ̃ me la, wowɔa wo ɖokuiwo abe Yakob ene boŋ, eye le Yakob me la, Yafet vi Askenaz ƒe to aɖeke menɔ anyi kpɔ o. Nenema gɔ̃ hã wogbe Yesu Nazaretitɔ la elabena wotso wo fofo Abosam gbɔ eye eƒe nudzodzrowo wɔ ge wole tegbee.

Eye tso Askenaz dukɔ si ƒe mɔwo nye vɔ̃ɖinyenye kple aʋatso me la, dukɔ aɖe va, negbe ɖeko womanye Adam ƒe dzidzimeviwo bliboe o, ke boŋ gbe vɔ̃ siwo woƒã ɖe Yahweh ƒe luwo dome siwo gblẽ wo ɖokuiwo tegbee le madzɔmadzɔnyenye me. Ame siwo wodzi tso wo fofo Abosam gbɔ gɔ̃ hã elabena Abosam ƒe fuku la gali, togbɔ be maho wo keŋkeŋ ahatsɔ wo aƒu gbe ɖe kpo la me abe ale si tututu Yahweh gblɔe ene hã. Eye tso Askenaz me yiyi la va gake menye dzɔdzɔme yiyi o, ke boŋ yiyi ŋunyɔtɔwo ƒe dukɔ aɖe si wotrɔ tso Yahweh wo Wɔla ƒe nɔnɔme me gɔ̃ hã, ame siwo wobla ɖe wo Fofo Abosam ƒe nudzodzrowo ŋu tegbee, ame si ƒe nudzodzrowo woawɔ. Eye ale woka yiyiwo hlẽ, esime wosi tso madzɔmadzɔnyenye ƒe futɔ siwo tsi tsitre ɖe yiyi la ƒe mɔwo ŋu la ƒe asi me gɔ̃ hã. Eye ale Askenaz gbe Yeshua HaMashiach, ame si me gome alo akpa aɖeke mele wo si o.

Eya ta le nyateƒe me la, ame dzedzwoawo kplɔ amegbetɔviwo tra tso esime Adam dze anyi tso eƒe nɔnɔme si me nu vɔ̃ mele o si me wowɔe le Eden bɔ la me eye gbegblẽ kaka ɖe anyigba la dzi to Kain ƒe dzidzimeviwo me. Gbegblẽ si ame dzedzwo siwo nɔ dzɔdzɔm la gagblẽ ɖe edzi esime woɖi va Hermon To dzi le Yared ƒe ŋkekewo me. Eya ta, Yahweh klɔ woƒe gbegblẽa ɖa le anyigba dzi le tsiɖɔɖɔ gã ma me le Noa ƒe ŋkekewo me eye hã ame dzedzwoawo gaɖo woƒe mɔwo anyi le tsiɖɔɖɔa megbe elabena ale Yahweh ɖe mɔ na amegbetɔviwo be woatia na wo ɖokuiwo. Elabena vavã, vavã, Yahweh na tiatia ƒe ablɔɖe nu siwo katã wòwɔ kple tiatia ƒe ablɔɖe le eƒe nuwɔwɔ me be woatia ne woanɔ agbe ɖe Yahweh ƒe mɔwo nu alo atia be yewoasubɔ Satanawo le woƒe aglãdzedze si ƒe nuwuwu tu aƒe la me. Elabena nye, Yeshua HaMashiach ŋutɔ, nyee nye ame si agbã ta na da la, ame si agbã eƒe fiaɖuƒe gudugudu be magafɔ kpɔ o gɔ̃ hã.

Ale wòle eye ale nyateƒe si mefia ku ɖe dziƒofiaɖuƒe la ŋu esime meva Israel abe Mawu ƒe Alẽvi ene le nye ŋutilã me nɔnɔ ɣeyiɣi me le Yudea la le. Elabena ale, woŋlɔe be: Dziƒofiaɖuƒe la le abe ŋutsu aɖe si ƒã nuku nyui ɖe eƒe agble me ene. Gake esime amewo dɔ alɔ̃ la, eƒe futɔ va, eye wòƒã gbe vɔ̃wo ɖe lu la dome, eye wòdzo yi. Eye esi nuku la mii, eye wòtse ku la, gbe vɔ̃awo hã dze. Eye aƒetɔ la ƒe subɔviwo va, eye wogblɔ nɛ be, Aƒetɔ, menye nuku nyuie nèƒã ɖe wò agble me oa? Ekema afi kae gbe vɔ̃awo tso? Eye wògblɔ na wo be, Ame ŋuʋãla aɖee wɔ esia. Eye subɔviawo gblɔ nɛ be, Ekema èdi be míayi ahalɔ wo aƒu ƒua? Gake egblɔ be, Ao, ne menye nenema o la, esime miele gbe vɔ̃awo lɔm la, miaho lu la hã kpli wo. Mina wo kple eve la natsi aduadu va se ɖe nuŋeɣi, eye le nuŋeɣi la magblɔ na nuŋelawo be, Milɔ gbe vɔ̃awo gbã, eye mibla wo bablawo be woatɔ dzo wo: gake milɔ lu la ɖe nye ava me.

Nenema gɔ̃ hã elabena lu bɔbɔa eɖokui le toɖoɖo me esime gbe vɔ̃wo tsia tsitre dzɔdzɔe le aglãdzedze vɔ̃ɖi me. Sela!

Gake ale wòle be nuƒoƒoƒu ƒe ɣeyiɣiwo de, eye maƒo nye luwo nu ƒu ɖe Yahweh ƒe ava me elabena ɣeyiɣi si woaŋe ŋgɔgbetsetseawo de. Eye ale wòava eme be maho gbe vɔ̃awo keŋkeŋ ahabla wo, eye woatsɔ wo aƒu gbe ɖe dzo la me le woƒe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ me. Eya ta miese anyigba si dzi miele kple aʋa si me wodzi mi ɖo gɔ̃ hã gɔme. Nukuwo ƒe aʋa si yi edzi tso Eden bɔ la me afi si Yahweh ɖe gbeƒã aʋa ɖe da la kple ame dzedzwoawo ŋu le woƒe vɔ̃ɖinyenye kple Yahweh ƒe bɔ la gbegblẽ ta gɔ̃ hã. Aʋa si ƒe nuwuwu anye aʋadziɖuɖu blibo le ame siwo katã dze aglã le mɔ sia nu kple ame dzedzwoawo dzi eye woƒe dzokpe dzo si le biba ƒe domenyinu la le mɔ kpɔm.

Eye ale Yahweh ɖe gbeƒãe be: Mado ketekakã ɖe wò kple nyɔnu la dome eye ɖe wò dzidzimevi kple eƒe dzidzimevi dome, eye nàɖu afɔkpodzi nɛ eye wòagbã ta na wò. Eye ale woɖu afɔkpodzi nam le nye atitsoga ŋu hekuku me esime metsɔ nye ɖokui na abe Ŋutitoto vɔsa deblibo abe Mawu ƒe Alẽvi ene le lɔlɔ̃nu faa me ɖe nye futɔ sẽŋutawo si me. Eye nyee nye nyɔnu la ƒe dzidzimevi si ŋugbe wodo si agbã Satanawo ƒe ta gudugudu eye matsɔ nye futɔwo awɔ nye afɔtiɖoƒee, elabena nyee nye Yuda To la ƒe Dzata eye le dzɔdzɔenyenye me meva be madrɔ̃ ʋɔnu ahawɔ aʋa!

Eya ta, miese gɔme be woɖu nukuʋa la dzi keŋkeŋ le Kristo Yesu me abe ale si megblɔ na Petro wòŋlɔe le ƒe alafa geɖewo siwo va yi me ene: Wogagbugbɔ mi dzi, menye tso nuku gbegblẽ me o, ke boŋ tso nuku magblẽmagblẽ me, to Mawu ƒe nya si le agbe, eye wònɔa anyi tegbee la me. Eye megagblɔ na Yohanes Nyaɖeɖefiala la wòŋlɔ ɖe eƒe lɛta me be: Ame siame si wodzi tso Mawu me la, mewɔa nu vɔ̃ o: elabena eƒe nuku la le eme, eye mate ŋu awɔ nu vɔ̃ o, elabena wodzi i tso Mawu me. Le esia me la, Mawu ƒe viwo dze ƒãa, kple abosam ƒe viwo: ame siame si mewɔa dzɔdzɔenyenye o la, metso Mawu me o, eye ame si melɔ̃a nɔvia o hã metso eme o. Eya ta, ame siwo katã wode dzesii le Kristo me la, womate ŋu agblẽ wo le mɔ sia nu gbeɖe o, eye womate ŋu awɔ nu vɔ̃ le afi si wotrɔ wo le tso Mawu gbagbe la ƒe nutata me o, eya koe nye Yahweh ame si tɔ wonye. Eya ta, migana bometsitsi siawo natso dzimaɖi na mi be ame siwo wogbugbɔ dzi tso nye gbɔgbɔ kɔkɔe si nye viɖe me eye wode dzesi wo kple Yahweh ƒe dzesi la, woate ŋu ahe wo atso nye asi sesẽ la me to mɔ aɖeke nu gbeɖe o. Eya ta, na ame siwo kpɔ xɔxɔ la, miaƒe xɔxɔ la li ke.

Eye ale maɖe yiyi ƒe mɔwo afia, eye maɖe yiyi la afia keŋkeŋ, togbɔ be yiyiwo lɔ̃a zɔzɔ le ɣaɣla gã me hã, maɖe woƒe mɔwo afia kokoko. Elabena yiyi siawo siwo trɔ nɔnɔme la ye nye ame siwo tsɔa ame siwo woɖo aɖaŋu ɖo eye wowua ame la ƒe nɔnɔmewo ɖea wo ɖokuiwo.
