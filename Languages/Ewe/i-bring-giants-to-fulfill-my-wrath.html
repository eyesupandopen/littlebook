Mekplɔ Dzɔtsuwo Vɛ Be Woawu Nye Dɔmedzoe Nu!

Kpɔ ɖa, wogbɔna! Mekplɔ dzɔtsuwo vɛ be woawu nye dɔmedzoe nu! Mise nye nyawo, mi amesiwo nɔa anyigba dzi, Yehowa, Mawu gã mavɔ la, ƒe nyawoe. Elabena abe alesi wònɔ le Noa ƒe ŋkekeawo me la, nenema ke wòanɔ: Dzɔtsuwo nɔ anyigba dzi le ŋkeke mawo me, tso Yared ƒe ŋkekeawo me yi ɖe Noa ƒe ŋkekeawo me wotsɔ wo, eye wotsa le anyigba dzi le vɔ̃ɖinyenye gã me. Eye alesi wònɔ la, nenema ke wòanɔ, eye dzɔtsuwo agatrɔ gbɔ. Le Yared ƒe ŋkekeawo me la, dzɔlawo ɖi va Hermon to dzi, wo katã wonye alafa eve le Yared ƒe ŋkekeawo me woɖi tso dziƒowo. Eye woka atam na wo nɔewo, atam si woka la zu fiƒode mavɔ na amesiwo katã woka atam na, si me womate ŋu asi le gbeɖe o, eye ale woble amegbetɔwo hekplɔ wo de nuvɔ̃ gã me.

Satanawo ble dzɔlawo, le woƒe dzi ƒe nudzodzrowo nu, eye wodze tetekpɔ me tso woƒe nɔnɔme gbãtɔ ŋutikɔkɔetɔ la me, eye agbe mavɔ nɔ wo si le dziƒowo, elabena nenema wowɔ wo. Gake wodze anyi, abe alesi ɣletiviwo dzena anyi ene, eye wokpe ɖe Satanawo ŋu tegbee. Ale Satanawo ble nyɔnu lɔ̃lɔ̃nuwɔlawo eye wofia wo be woable dzɔlawo ɖe woƒe dzi ƒe nudzodzrowo nu, eye nyɔnu aɖewo tsɔ wo ɖokuiwo na dzɔlawo faa, eye woatsɔ woƒe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃. Wodze ame bubuwo dzi le sesẽ me elabena dzɔlawo gblẽ woƒe xexeame kplɔla xoxoawo dome, eye woyi ɖaxɔ nyɔnuwo le sesẽ me tso wo srɔ̃wo kple woƒe ƒomewo si, eye ale dzɔlawo tsɔ amesiwo katã wotia la abe wo srɔ̃wo ene, eye wowɔ ŋutasẽnu le ame geɖewo ŋu, eye tso ƒomedodo kɔkɔe manɔmee sia me la, wodzi dzɔtsuwo, dzɔtsu gã xoxoawo gɔ̃ hã. Elabena dzɔlawo gblẽ amegbetɔwo ƒe dzɔdzɔmeŋutete dome ɖe edzi. Eye ale wowɔ nuvɔ̃ ɖe lãwo, kple xeviwo, kple tɔmelãwo ŋu eye wowɔ nuvɔ̃ ɖe ŋutilã ƒomevi katã ŋu hetsaka wo nɔewo, le toɖoɖo kple toomaɖomaɖo si me fuléle le me ɖe liƒo siwo meɖo la ŋu, nyee nye Yehowa, Mawu Gã Mavɔ la! Tso gɔmedzedzea me la, mewɔ anyigba la kple nuku ɖesiaɖe ɖe eƒomevi nu, eye ale wotso liƒo siwo megbe wòle be woagblẽ gbeɖe o, eye be woatso dzɔdzɔme liƒo siwo Yehowa ɖo la fia be woahe tsukuku, fukpekpe, kple ku ava Yehowa ƒe nuwɔwɔ dzi, eye amesiwo katã wɔe la atsɔ woƒe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃. Wowɔ agbe be wòanye avɔ si me nuwo do ƒome kple wo nɔewo, afisi agbe katã do ƒome le ŋutifafa kple ɖekawɔwɔ me, afisi lɔlɔ̃ ɖua fia le ŋkekea me eye amesiame dia ame bubuwo ƒe nyonyo ke menye woawo ŋutɔwo tɔ ko o. Nuwɔwɔ si me nudzodzro kple vɔ̃ɖinyenye ƒe didi aɖeke mele be wòaɖe eɖokui afia le ku kple tsɔtsrɔ̃ me o, eye ale wòanɔ tegbee kple tegbee, eye vava me ƒe ɣeyiɣiwo ɖo.

Eye ale dziƒodɔla gã eneawo, siwo nye Mikael, Uriel, Rafael, kple Gabriel, tsɔ nya ɖe nusi wo hati tsãtɔwo wɔ le anyigba dzi la ŋu, eye ale wodrɔ̃ ʋɔnu hewɔ edzi kaba ɖe amesiwo katã wòku ɖo kple anyigba la ŋutɔ dzi, elabena xexe si nɔ anyi ɣemaɣi la gblẽ keŋkeŋ. Wona Nefilimwo, siwo nye dzɔlawo ƒe viwo, wɔ aʋa kple wo nɔewo vaseɖe ku me le aʋa gã geɖewo me le to siwo tsi tre ɖe wo nɔewo ŋu dome, eye eya ta elabena meɖe ŋutifafa ɖa le wo dome, eye wowɔ aʋa vaseɖe ku me hewu wo nɔewo esime wo fofowo (dzɔlawo) kpɔ woƒe nuwɔwɔ ŋunyɔtɔ siwo wowɔ na amegbetɔwo ƒe tsɔtsrɔ̃ la ƒe tsɔtsrɔ̃ le sesẽ me. Tso afima la, wode ka dzɔlawo le tohehe kple ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ me eye wode wo vovo tso wo nɔewo gbɔ hena dzidzime blaadre. Eye ale le mɔ ma ke nu si wòle be makpɔ nye nuwɔwɔ si melɔ̃ vevie la tsrɔ̃na to ŋunyɔnu vɔ̃ɖiwo me la, ale mewɔe be nu vɔ̃ɖiwo tsrɔ̃ wo nɔewo tsɔ he to na wo fofowo, eye ale meɖo eteƒe, elabena mele dzɔdzɔe le nye ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃wo me ɣesiaɣi kple tegbee, eye menye ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ dzɔdzɔewo ƒe Mawu si ƒe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃wo medoa kpo nu o, alo womehe ɖe megbe tso vava me o.

Eye ale mehe tsiɖɔɖɔ gã la va anyigba dzi, eye tsiɖɔɖɔ la nye nu sesẽ aɖe le Noa ƒe ŋkekeawo me. Wogbã goglo gã la ƒe dzɔtsoƒewo eye tsi do tso anyigba la ŋutɔ me sesĩe, eye dziƒo ƒe fesrewo ʋu, si na tsi siwo le yamegboa dzi la ɖɔ ɖe anyigba dzi, to to kɔkɔtɔwo gɔ̃ hã dzi, eye wònyrɔ agbe katã le anyigba dzi, wo katã negbe luʋɔ enyi siwo woxɔ ɖe agbe le aɖakaʋu la me, elabena wokpɔ Noa be ele dzɔdzɔe le nye ŋku me. Eye le esia ta mekplɔ Noa eye mede se nɛ be wòakpa aɖakaʋu ɖe mɔfiame siwo nye asi na la nu. Eye mekplɔ lã siwo woaxɔ ɖe agbe la hã va egbɔ, eye ale Noa wɔ nuteƒe wɔ nusiwo katã wode se nɛ la dzi, eye tsiɖɔɖɔ la va abe alesi wogblɔe ɖi heɖee fia ene, elabena nusi megblɔ, Yehowa Mawu gã mavɔ la, ame aɖeke mate ŋu atrɔe alo axe mɔ nɛ kura o!

Eye ale Henox, agbalẽŋlɔla xoxoa, gblɔ dzɔtsu siawo ƒe dzɔdzɔme tsitotsito ale, elabena nye, Yehowa, meɖee fia ale: Eye azɔ, dzɔtsuwo, siwo dzɔ tso gbɔgbɔwo kple ŋutilã me la, woayɔ wo be gbɔgbɔ vɔ̃wo le anyigba dzi, eye anyigba dzi woanɔ. Gbɔgbɔ vɔ̃wo do tso woƒe ŋutilãwo me; elabena wodzi wo tso amewo me, tso Dzɔla kɔkɔewo gbɔe woƒe gɔmedzedze kple dzɔtsoƒe gbãtɔ le; woanye gbɔgbɔ vɔ̃wo le anyigba dzi, eye woayɔ wo be gbɔgbɔ vɔ̃wo. Ke dziƒo gbɔgbɔwo ya, dziƒoe woanɔ, gake anyigba gbɔgbɔ siwo wodzi ɖe anyigba dzi ya, anyigba dzie woanɔ. Eye dzɔtsuwo ƒe gbɔgbɔwo wɔa fu ame, tea ame ɖe anyi, tsrɔ̃a ame, dzea ame dzi, wɔa aʋa, eye wowɔa tsɔtsrɔ̃ le anyigba dzi, eye wohea kuxiwo vɛ: womeɖua nu o, gake dɔ wua wo eye tsikɔ wua wo, eye wohea dziku vɛ. Eye gbɔgbɔ siawo atsi tre ɖe amegbetɔviwo kple nyɔnuwo ŋu, elabena wodo tso wo me.

Eye ale wòdzɔe be le ɣeyiɣi nyuitɔ dzi la, Noa ɖi ɖe Ararat To dzi, afisi aɖakaʋu la dzudzɔ ɖo esime tsiwo mie. Eye mebla nu kple Noa, eye le ɣeyiɣi aɖe megbe la, Noa tɔgbuiyɔviwo dze dzedze tso nubabla si mewɔ kple Noa la gbɔ, eye le esia ta gbɔgbɔ vɔ̃wo dze wo dzi dzedze gɔme. Eye ale gbɔgbɔ siawo, siwo sɔ gbɔ la, xɔ aƒe ɖe Noa viwo ƒe viwo dzi, eye ale Noa do gbe ɖa ɖe wo ta, eye mebla gbɔgbɔ vɔ̃ siawo ƒe akpa asieke le ewo me le aʋali la me, afisi wodzra wo ɖo tso ɣemaɣi vaseɖe egbe. Eye ale mede se, eye ale wofia atike siwo katã wozãna la Noa, eye wofiae amebeble, be Noa nate ŋu ada gbe le wo ŋu kple anyigba dzi gbewo. Eye ale woɖe mɔ na gbɔgbɔ siawo ƒe akpa ɖeka le ewo me be woanɔ ablɔɖe me, be woable amegbetɔvi siwo adzro woƒe aʋatsonyawo la akplɔ wo ade vɔ̃ɖinyenye me. Eye ale gbɔgbɔ susɔeawo siwo wobla hena ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ le aʋali la me la, woaɖe asi le wo ŋu le Yakob ƒe xaxaɣiwo, ne woʋu aʋali la keŋkeŋ eye woaɖe asi le wo dometɔ akpa asieke le ewo me siwo wobla alea la ŋu be woawɔ woƒe mɔ vɔ̃ɖi kple ŋunyɔnuwo abe dzɔdzɔme amegbetɔ ƒe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ si le afisia ƒe akpa aɖe ene, eye woado kpo adze anyigba la dzi kpoyi kple tsɔtsrɔ̃ gã!

Eye ale Kanaan, Noa tɔgbuiyɔvi si Noa ƒo fi de le eƒe vɔ̃ɖinyenye ta la, wɔ nuvɔ̃ gã (gake) to dɔla-dzeanyiwo ƒe sidzedze zazã me, eye le Satana ƒe mɔwo nu la, eyi faa, si me wokplɔe faa, eye wowɔ ŋunyɔnu geɖewo wu le Noa ƒe tsiɖɔɖɔa megbe. Eye ŋunyɔnu siawo xɔ Sem ƒe anyigba siwo wona to domenyinu ƒe dzodzodada me la dzi, si Kanaan fiƒodela la xɔ le sesẽ me hefi na eɖokui, eye woyɔ eƒe anyigba si wofi la be Kanaan nyigba. Eƒe dzidzimevi ŋunyɔtɔ geɖewo nye nuwɔwɔ siwo wotrɔ woƒe dzɔdzɔmeŋutete siwo xɔ ŋugbedodonyigbawo dzi keŋkeŋ, siwo me medɔ Mose be wòaƒo Israel viwo nu ƒu tso Egipte nyigbawo dzi be woaxɔ woƒe domenyinu tso ŋunyɔtɔawo si. Eye ale Yosua wɔ aʋa gã kple ŋunyɔnu siawo abe alesi woŋlɔe ɖe ŋɔŋlɔ kɔkɔeawo me ene, eye woxɔ woƒe domenyinu si dze wo la tso ŋunyɔnu siawo si, eye ale woŋlɔe ɖi be enye nusiwo gbɔna la ƒe vɔvɔli. Elabena nenema ke nye vinutsu, Yeshua HaMashiach, amesi mewɔ Aƒetɔwo ƒe Aƒetɔ kple Fiawo ƒe Fia la, ava xɔ eƒe domenyinu tso amesiwo xɔ anyigba la kple amesiwo xɔe le sesẽ me la si, elabena woƒe anyigbaxɔxɔ la mesɔ kple se o. Womenye fiawo o eye fiaɖuƒe aɖeke mele wo si o, ke boŋ ta gã si me dzo kple dzokpe le biana lae nye woƒe domenyinu mavɔ.

Gake, Israel mewɔ nuteƒe keŋkeŋ le woƒe yɔyɔ la me o, eye woɖe mɔ dzɔtsu susɔewo nɔ anyi. Gake le woƒe toomaɖomaɖo me gɔ̃ hã la, mexe mɔ na ŋunyɔnu siawo eye metsrɔ̃ wo dometɔ geɖe tso anyigba dzi tso ɣemaɣi. Eye menya wo dometɔ geɖe yi anyigba te, eye wo dometɔ geɖe yi anyigba manyamanyawo dzi le tsikpe la godo afisi amegbetɔwo metsaa le faa o. Se egɔme be amegbetɔwo mese anyigba si dzi wowɔ wo ɖo eye wole edzi la gɔme o, elabena amegbetɔwo ɖua aʋatsonya siwo wodzrana abe nyateƒe ene, siwo wogbugbɔ bla abe hehenana ene, eye ale amegbetɔwo lɔ̃na faa nɔa manyamanya me be to Mawu ƒe nya me la, dziƒowo nɔ anyi tso gbe aɖe gbe ʋĩ, eye anyigba la tso tsi me eye wòle tsi me: Eya ta xexe si nɔ anyi ɣemaɣi la, esi tsi ɖɔ ɖe edzi la, etsrɔ̃: Gake dziƒo kple anyigba siwo li fifia la, to nya ma ke me wodzra wo ɖo, wodzra wo ɖo na dzo hena ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ kple ame mawumavɔ̃lawo ƒe tsɔtsrɔ̃ gbe.

Eye ale le ɣeyiɣi aɖe megbe la, meɖe dzɔtsuwo ɖa le anyigba dzi le nye nublanuikpɔkpɔ kple amenuveve kple nye didi be maɖe amegbetɔwo tso tsɔtsrɔ̃ me la me, eye woƒe susɔewo kaka ɖe anyigba dzi godoo eye wokaka ɖe nye nuwɔwɔ dzi abe woƒe anyinɔnɔ ƒe ɖasefo ene. Woƒe blema ŋutinyawo ƒe susɔewo gɔ̃ hã gakpɔtɔ le tsitre abe ɖasefo ene be woɖu towo ƒe fiaɖuƒe aɖe dzi kpɔ le anyigba dzi le woƒe ɣeyiɣi si woyɔna be sika ɣeyiɣi la me, le Noa ƒe ŋkekeawo me hafi tsiɖɔɖɔ la nava. Woƒe kpe gãwo ƒe aƒedo siwo kaka la le abe ɖasefo ɖe wo ŋu ene, eye aƒedo siawo ƒe anyinɔnɔ ŋutɔ le tsitre abe ɖasefo ɖe Satana ŋutɔ ƒe aʋatsonyawo ŋu ene, amesi dzea agbagba vevie be yeaɣla nyateƒea ale be wòate ŋu adze ame dzi le mɔ si womele mɔ kpɔm na o nu keŋkeŋ. Eye ale megblɔ na amesiwo si to le be woase nya eye ŋku le wo si be woakpɔ eye dzi le wo si be woasrɔ̃ nusi adzɔ hafi wòadzɔ la, ale be woƒe dzi nagado kpo nu le nukuku kple dzimaxɔse me o, ke boŋ be woanya nyateƒea eye woanɔ dzadzraɖo ɖi na nusi gbɔna be woate ŋu axɔ wo ɖe agbe, amesiwo katã yɔa Yesu Kristo ƒe ŋkɔ vaseɖe woƒe nuwuwu la gɔ̃ hã.

Ale wogblɔe ɖi eye ale wòva eme le ɣeyiɣiwo ƒe vava me, be meɖe asi le dzɔlawo ŋu tso woƒe gaxɔ siwo me wobla wo ɖo le fuwɔamewo kple tohehe me la me, wobla wo vovo tso wo nɔewo gbɔ. Eye ale woɖe asi le wo ŋu esi woƒe blabla ƒe ɣeyiɣiwo wu enu megbe: dzidzime blaadre gɔ̃ hã. Ŋutinya mate ŋu aɣla woƒe asiɖeɖelewoŋu keŋkeŋ o, eye aʋa kple dzɔdzɔmefɔku geɖewo kple nudzɔdzɔ bubuwo dzɔ le xexeame katã kple woƒe asiɖeɖelewoŋu, eye wotsɔ wo ɖokuiwo ƒu gbe ɖe ɣedzeƒe, eye wokpe ɖe Satana ƒe aglãdzedzea ŋu, keŋkeŋ, ake, elabena ale wobla wo ɖokuiwo tegbee le woƒe atamka fiƒodea me, eye le dzo ta la me woawu woƒe atamkawo nu hena mavɔmavɔ katã, elabena wobla wo ɖe Satana ƒe tohehe kple domenyinu ŋu tegbee.

Eye ale wodzra wo ɖokuiwo ɖo le ɣeyiɣi si wona la me eye le mɔ si woɖe dzi kpɔtɔ nu hena yubili geɖewo azɔ. Wogawɔ woƒe ŋunyɔnuwo ake godoo, eye le ɣaɣla me wodzraa wo ɖokuiwo ɖo eye wolalɔa mɔxela la ɖeɖeɖa esime woagadze agbagba ake be woagbã liƒo siwo meɖo na wo la, elabena wodzea aglã ɣesiaɣi. Eye togbɔ be wobla wo be woawu El Shaddai ƒe tameɖoɖowo nu hã, elabena nyee nye Mawu Ŋusẽkatãtɔ la, woadi be yewoagbugbɔ yewoƒe sika ɣeyiɣi la aɖo anyi eye woaxɔ anyigba la na yewo ɖokuiwo, nusi manya wɔ o; elabena anyigba la kple eƒe blibonyenye la Aƒetɔ la tɔe, eye nye vinutsu, Yeshua HaMashiach, ƒle anyigba la ƒe agbalẽ, eye wotsɔe de eƒe asi sesẽawo me abe domenyinu mavɔ ene, eye wòamae keŋkeŋ kple nuteƒewɔlawo, amesiwo woyɔ, amesiwo wotia, kple amesiwo woɖe.

Eye ale wòle be Satanawo kple dzɔlawo dzra wo ɖokuiwo ɖo, eye wole mɔ kpɔm vevie na mɔxela la ɖeɖeɖa, elabena le ɖeɖeɖa ma me la, woɖe asi le woƒe fiaɖuƒea ŋu keŋkeŋ, eye anyigba la agatrɔ azu abe alesi wònɔ le Noa ƒe ŋkekeawo me ene. Kpɔ ɖa! Mede se, eye mekplɔ wo vɛ: dzɔtsuwo gbɔna be woawu nye dɔmedzoe nu, wole dzidzɔ kpɔm le ɣeyiɣi ma ke me eye wole dzugbe dom, eye maɖe mɔ woaɖe asi le wo ŋu ɖe anyigba dzi ake be woawu nye tameɖoɖowo nu. Woado kpo tso afisi wobe ɖo la kaba, eye wobe ɖe woƒe du siwo le anyigba te la me, eye wobe ɖe tsikpe si woatrɔ kpuie la godo, eye woaɖe mɔ na wo be woage ɖe amegbetɔwo ƒe xexeame, ahafi nu, eye woaɖu dzi, eye Dzalẽ la akplɔ wo elabena wonye eƒe fiaɖuƒea ƒe akpa aɖe. Gake hena ɣeyiɣi kpui aɖe ko, Daniel ƒe kwasiɖa blaadrelia gɔ̃ hã, kwasiɖa si nye ƒe adre la woaɖe asi le wo ŋu afisi ŋkeke ɖesiaɖe nye ƒe ɖeka, eye ale wòanye ƒe adre. Eye woami nu geɖe, eye amewo ƒe dzi ado kpo nu le woƒe mo sesẽwo kple woƒe lolome gã ŋutɔ si wotsɔ sɔ kple dzɔdzɔme amegbetɔ ta.

Kpɔ ɖa, NYEE NYE Nyee Nye gã la, amesi dɔ nye Nyawo to nye dɔla dzi, eye to nye dɔla dzi wodɔ nye nyawo. Eye ale megblɔa nuwo na mi hafi woadzɔ ale be nye dukɔ la nanɔ dzadzraɖo ɖi eye woase nusi gbɔna la gɔme, eye nugɔmesese nanɔ wo si elabena nu siawo adzɔ kpuie. Eye amesiwo katã yɔa Yeshua HaMashiach ƒe ŋkɔ, Yesu Kristo gɔ̃ hã le Eŋlisigbe me la, woaxɔ wo ɖe agbe.

Eye ale wòle be dɔmedzoe gã kple ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ gã abe alesi wodo ŋugbee le blema ene la ɖo azɔ be wòava anyigba dzi keŋkeŋ, eye ɣeyiɣiwo ɖo azɔ, Mawu Gã Mavɔ la gblɔe! Hena amenuveve ƒe ɣeyiɣi blibo aɖe la, xɔxɔ ƒe nyanyui la kple Mawu ƒe ŋaŋlẽ gã la (si woɖe fia Apostolo Paulo) yɔ anyigba la dzi eye wòyi anyigba ƒe seƒewo ke. Eye togbɔ be woxɔ ame geɖe, geɖe, geɖe ɖe agbe hã, amesiwo katã va Yesu Nazaretɔ la gbɔ le dzimetɔtrɔ blibo me, hewɔe woƒe Aƒetɔ mavɔ la gɔ̃ hã, vava me ƒe ɣeyiɣiwo ɖo elabena amegbetɔwo gagbe wo Wɔla la ake. Eye nye, El Shaddai, Mawu Ŋusẽkatãtɔ la gɔ̃ hã, nyee nye amesi xe mɔ na vɔ̃ geɖe be xɔxɔ ƒe domenyilawo nava agbe mavɔ me le Kristo Yesu, woƒe Aƒetɔ si yi dzi la me. Eye ale azɔ megblɔ nu siawo ɖi hafi woadzɔ, elabena nyee nye Yehowa, NYEE NYE Gã la! Eya ta gblɔ na wo be NYEE NYE GÃ la gblɔe kple kakaɖedzi be le nye vi nuteƒewɔlawo ƒoƒo ƒu ɖe vinyea gbɔ me, ne wofɔ wo ɖe tsitre be woakpe kplii le yame eye esi woɖe mɔxela la ɖa la, woaɖe asi le Satana ƒe fiaɖuƒe bliboa ŋu ɖe anyigba dzi eye anyigba la atrɔ.

Megblɔe le ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ me ɖe anyigba dzi nɔlawo dzi be maɖe asi le Satana ƒe fiaɖuƒea ŋu keŋkeŋ, eye woƒe ŋunyɔnuwo katã ado kpo adze anyigba la dzi, eye gbɔgbɔ mawo katã siwo mebla ɖe aʋali la me le Noa ƒe ŋkekeawo me la woaɖe asi le wo ŋu ɖe anyigba dzi, elabena mama mlɔeba kple ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ mlɔeba ƒe ɣeyiɣiwo va anyigba dzi, eye woɖo ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ gã la ƒe ŋkeke la. Eye ale wòanye be woatsɔ Satana ƒe fiaɖuƒe ana wòaɖu anyigba la dzi, elabena le nye dzɔdzɔenyenye me la, amegbetɔwo di vevie be Satanawo naɖu yewo dzi, hexɔ eƒe aʋatsonyawo dzi se gɔ̃ hã be enye mawu dɔmenyotɔ kple kekelitsɔla si naa kekeli, be azɔ matsɔ nusi wodi vevie la ana amegbetɔwo.

Eye woɖe asi le amesiwo dze anyi la ŋu keŋkeŋ hena woƒe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃, eye wowɔ woƒe ŋunyɔnuwo ake. Eye wobla wo ɖe anyigba ŋu kple dzalẽ la, elabena nenemae woƒe ɣeyiɣi le, eye woawɔ wo ɖokuiwo abe amedzrowo ene, nuwɔwɔ dɔmenyotɔ siwo tso xexe bubuwo me gɔ̃ hã. Gake nye, Yehowa Mawu Gã Mavɔ la, meka atam to nye ŋutɔ nye ŋkɔ me, elabena nyemate ŋu aka atam to ame aɖeke si kɔ wu la me o, be xexe siwo le xlã ƒom le aʋatso me le yamegboa si le kekem ɖe edzi si me naneke mele o la meli o, ke boŋ dziƒowo le anyigba la dzi, dziƒowo ƒe dziƒo si nye dziƒo kɔkɔtɔ la gɔ̃ hã, afisi Mawu Dziƒoʋĩtɔ la bɔbɔ nɔ eƒe fiazikpui dzi le fiaɖuɖo mavɔ me eye naneke mate ŋu aɣla tso nye ŋku me o.

Kpɔ ɖa! Wole wo ɖokuiwo ɖem fia xoxo, eye woƒe nuwo kple nu nogooawo le afisiafi, eye anyigba la le tɔtrɔm kaba ŋutɔ azɔ. Eye esi dzɔdzɔme amegbetɔ gbe nye ŋutifafa nubabla la azɔ, nye Tenuvi, Yesu Nazaretɔ la gɔ̃ hã, ale azɔ megbe dzɔdzɔme amegbetɔ eye magblẽe ɖi be eƒe futɔwo naɖu edzi keŋkeŋ, eye wodi vevie be yeatsrɔ̃e. Gake, nye gbegbe la menye blibo alo mlɔebatɔ o, elabena woaxɔ ameha gã aɖe ɖe agbe le Yakob ƒe xaxaɣi, amesiwo katã yɔa Aƒetɔ la ƒe ŋkɔ, Yesu Kristo gɔ̃ hã, vaseɖe nuwuwu. Elabena womate ŋu akpɔ xɔxɔ le Yakob ƒe xaxaɣi to mɔ bubu aɖeke nu o. Eya ta mama mlɔeba la adze gɔme, eye amesiwo katã meɖo ɖi na agbe mavɔ tso hafi woaɖo anyigba ƒe gɔmeɖoanyiwo anyi la ava agbe mavɔ me, eye mafɔ wo ɖe agbe yeye me, eye fetu gã aɖe anɔ wo si.

Eya ta mise nye nyawo eye mitsɔ miaƒe dziwo ɖo nye nuxlɔ̃ame kple gbeƒãɖeɖe ƒe nyawo dzi, elabena Trɔ̃subɔlawo ƒe ɣeyiɣiwo wu enu azɔ, eye dɔmedzoe kple ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ kple ɖeɖefia gã le anyigba dzi azɔ, elabena nye, El Shaddai, Mawu Ŋusẽkatãtɔ la gɔ̃ hã, nyee nye amesi bɔbɔ nɔ dzi, eye naneke meɣla tso nye ŋku me o, eye azɔ maʋã be maxɔ amesiwo katã nye xɔxɔ ƒe domenyilawo la ɖe agbe keŋkeŋ esime meklã wo tso amesiwo woɖo ɖi na tsɔtsrɔ̃ mavɔ la gbɔ. Eye ale woama lu la tso gbe vɔ̃ɖiwo gbɔ tegbee, eye amesiwo katã nye Satana ƒe aglãdzedzea ƒe akpa aɖe la woatsɔ wo ade ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ me, eye woɖo woƒe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ ƒe ɣeyiɣiwo be woado kpo adze anyigba la dzi.

Mise nye Nyawo eye miese nusi gbɔna la ƒe sesẽme gɔme, elabena Aƒetɔ la ƒe ŋkeke la nye viviti ƒe ŋkeke, eye amekae ate ŋu anɔ tsitre?

Nye, Yehowa, Mawu Gã Mavɔ la, megblɔ nu siawo ɖi hafi woadzɔ, eye ɣeyiɣi aɖeke megali o. Amesiame si ava la, neva no agbe mavɔ ƒe tsiwo faa, eye nye ameyɔyɔ na agbe mavɔ la li, eye amesiwo katã yɔa Aƒetɔ la ƒe ŋkɔ vaseɖe nuwuwu la woaxɔ wo ɖe agbe.
