Ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ Dze Egɔme Tso Mawu Ƒe Aƒe La Me!

Mise Yeshua HaMashiach ƒe nyawo, nye detɔwo, be miase nye mɔwo gɔme eye miatrɔ ava miaƒe Mesia vavã la gbɔ elabena mele yɔyɔmi, O Israel, ɖe miase nye gbe oa? Eya ta, mibɔbɔ miaƒe towo le toɖoɖo me eye miatso aʋa na miaƒe dziwo, be nunyala siwo le mia dome la nagadze nunya geɖe wu ne woɖo to nye nufiafiawo eye wobɔbɔ wo ɖokui na nye mɔfiamewo. Elabena meva be magblɔ ame siwo axɔ lã wɔadã la ƒe dzesi la ŋu nya na mi, be miase woƒe gɔmedzedze kple woƒe nuwuwu gɔme, eye be woagaƒo mɔ̃ na mi ahatso mi adzoe o.

Eya ta, miese ɣeyiɣi siwo me miele la gɔme eye miese mama mamlɛtɔ si le egɔme dze ge kpuie la gɔme ne woaƒo nye nuteƒewɔla siwo le Kristo me la nu ƒu ɖe gbɔnye le alilikpowo me kpoyi kple le mɔ si womele mɔ kpɔm na o nu azɔ. Nenemae wòle eye nenemae wòanɔ be maɖi tso dziƒowo ava alilikpowo me kpoyi ahatso aɖaŋu manɔmee aƒo nuteƒewɔla siwo le Kristo me la nu ƒu ne Mawu ƒe kpẽ ɖi kple ɣli gã aɖe kple dɔlawo ƒe gã ƒe gbe. Ekema ame kuku siwo le Kristo me la afɔ gbã eye ame siwo le agbe eye wosusɔ la, woakɔ wo ayi alilikpowo me be woakpe kple woƒe Aƒetɔ mavɔ la le yame eye alea woakɔ wo ayi dziƒowo ƒe dedienɔnɔ me eye woanɔ kplii ɖaa ahatrɔ wo tegbee be woanɔ abe woƒe dzɔdzɔenyenye ƒe Aƒetɔ mavɔ la ene!

Gake, nye dzedze madzɔ le ɣaɣla me abe ale si aʋatso Biblia sɔsrɔ̃wo fiaa nu wòtɔa mɔe ene o, eye aʋatso ɖoɖo aɖeke mava anyigba dzi ama Israel ɖa tso nye Hame vavã la gbɔ o, elabena nuteƒewɔla siwo le Kristo Yesu me lae nye Mawu ƒe Israel eye woaxɔ woƒe nɔƒewo le ɖoɖowo kple towo dome le dziƒowo eye wonye ame yeye ɖeka. Nenemae woŋlɔe ɖe Paulo ƒe agbalẽ si wòŋlɔ na Galatiatɔwo me: Elabena le Kristo Yesu me la, aʋatso kple aʋamatsomatso menye naneke o, ke boŋ nuwɔwɔ yeye. Eye ame siwo katã zɔna ɖe ɖoɖo sia nu la, ŋutifafa nanɔ wo dzi, kple nublanuikpɔkpɔ, kple Mawu ƒe Israel la dzi. Eya ta, miese egɔme be nyee nye ame si ana Israel maganye fiaɖuƒe si ma ɖe akpa eve o tegbee kple tegbee. Eya ta, migbe dispensationalist, Zion-tɔwo ƒe azevetɔwo ƒe aʋatsonyawo eye miaxɔ Yeshua HaMashiach ƒe nyateƒe la!

Eya ta mena miese egɔme be ɖoɖowo kple kɔkɔyi ɣaɣlawo nye susu siwo Satanawo wɔ be yewoaƒo mɔ̃ na ame tsɛwo eye yewoatsɔ aɖaŋu vɔ̃wo kple aʋatsonyawo aɖe ame fafawo kple ame siwo bɔbɔ wo ɖokui le dzi me la aboyoe. Gake, nye dzedze adzɔ pɛpɛpɛ abe ale si woŋlɔe ɖi ene eye ame katã akpɔe eye xexeame anya be Kristo Yesu dze abe ale si wòdo ŋugbee na eƒe kɔkɔe nuteƒewɔlawo ene, eye wòagblẽ ame siwo xɔ dzo fafɛ̃ la ɖi na woƒe ŋutilã vɔ̃ɖi si lé wo ɖe nuvɔ̃ me la ƒe tsɔtsrɔ̃ eye nenemae woawɔe.

Gake, woaƒo nuteƒewɔla siwo le Kristo me la nu ƒu abe ale si woŋlɔe ɖe Ŋɔŋlɔawo me na woƒe Aƒetɔ mavɔ la ene! Eye nyemagbe nye nunyaxɔxɔ la le mɔ aɖeke nu o, eye malée sesĩe aɖe ame siwo katã nye tɔnye le nuteƒewɔwɔ me la dzoe, ne wodɔ alɔ̃ le anyigba ƒe dzi me alo ne wole agbetɔwo dome le anyigba dzi o: Woaƒo wo nu ƒu ɖe gbɔnye le yame nenema ke. Gake, ame siwo dze anyi la aka aʋatso kple woƒe amedzro dzedze aʋatso si me woagblɔ be amedzrowo tsɔ ame siwo woƒo nu ƒu le Kristo me la dzoe eye aʋa gã aɖe adzɔ le anyigba dzi, eye ame siwo dze anyi la aka aʋatso zi geɖe, gake migaxɔ nya ɖeka aɖeke si wogblɔ la dzi se o. Elabena nuteƒewɔla siwo le Kristo me la nu ƒoƒo ƒu la nye mɔkpɔkpɔ kple kpeɖodzi be nye vava evelia gogo le Daniel ƒe kwasiɖa blaadrelia ƒe nuwuwu, si nye ƒe adre ƒe kwasiɖa si me kwasiɖaa ƒe ŋkeke ɖe sia ɖe le abe ƒe ɖeka ene.

Gake, ame siwo xɔ dzo fafɛ̃ la magaxɔ dzo fafɛ̃ o elabena mama mamlɛtɔ ƒe ɣeyiɣiwo aɖe mɔ bliboe ne woɖe nye moxela ɖa eye tsikpe adza. Eye nenema kee ame siwo xɔ dzo fafɛ̃ la ava woƒe nyametsotsowo gbɔ, afi si ame aɖewo axɔ dzo sesĩe le woƒe ɖokuitsɔtsɔna kple xɔse me na woƒe Aƒetɔ Yesu Kristo, eye ame aɖewo afa miamiamia abe tsikpe si le gege dze atsiaƒu me ene, eye woatia woa ŋutɔwo ƒe tsɔtsrɔ̃ mavɔ boŋ.

Togbɔ be ele alea hã, nye, Kristo Yesu, mado dzo ɖe tameɖoɖo si womate ŋu aɖu edzi o me na ame siwo katã tia agbe mavɔ le Yesu Nazaret-tɔ la me eye made tameɖoɖo si womate ŋu agbã o la wo me be woayɔ woƒe Aƒetɔ Yesu va se ɖe nuwuwu, eye alea woava woƒe fetu mavɔ si me fukpekpewo mesɔ kple fetu mavɔ geɖe siwo woana ame siwo ɖu dzi la ƒe kpekpeme kple ŋutikɔkɔe mavɔ la o gbɔ; eye mana ame siwo nye nye dziɖulawo la naɖu dzi abe ale si woƒe Aƒetɔ wɔe ene, gake esime meɖu ame siwo dze anyi la dzi le mɔ ma ke nu le nye vɔsa abe Mawu ƒe Alẽvi la ene me.

Eye nenemae wòanɔ, be ame geɖe siwo ate ŋu atsɔ woƒe dziwo ana woƒe Aƒetɔ Yesu Kristo va se ɖe nuwuwu la atrɔ afa miamiamia na woƒe Aƒetɔ mavɔ la boŋ eye nublanuitɔe la woatrɔ ɖa tso agbe mavɔ gbɔ tegbee ayi tohehe ƒe dzo mavɔwo me elabena woagbe Yesu Nazaret-tɔ la bliboe ɖe nuvɔ̃ ƒe vivisesewo ta eye woatia woa ŋutɔwo ƒe fuwɔamewo mavɔ esime woate ŋu atia dzidzɔ mavɔ le Yahweh ŋkume tegbee. Eye ame geɖe siwo kpɔ tiatia mavɔ be woazu Mawu ƒe Viwo tegbee la atrɔ ɖa boŋ akpe ɖe fɔbuame zɔzɔla siwo adze anyi tegbee ayi ku evelia me la ŋu.

Togbɔ be ele alea hã, ablɔɖe le ame katã si be woatia, eya ta ame aɖewo atia nunyatɔe eye ame geɖe atia bometsitɔe. Eya ta mige ɖe agbo xaxɛ la nu: elabena agbo la keke, eye mɔ si kplɔa ame yia tsɔtsrɔ̃ me la le gbadzaa: eye ame geɖewo le afi ma siwo gena ɖe eme. Elabena agbo la xaxa, eye mɔ si kplɔa ame yia agbe me la xaxa, eye ame ʋɛ aɖewo koe kpɔa mɔ si kplɔa ame yia dzɔdzɔenyenye mavɔ ƒe agbewo me tegbee la eye tiatia bubu aɖeke meli woawɔ o. Eya ta, tia agbe be wò kple wò dzidzimeviwo mianɔ agbe!

Gake, meva be maxlɔ̃ nu wò, O Israel, be ame katã nase nye mɔfiamewo eye woalé to ɖe nye nuxlɔ̃amewo ŋu, ame siwo katã le teƒe kple ame siwo katã le didiƒe eye woka hlẽ elabena mama mamlɛtɔ ƒe tiatia ma ke le anyi na Adam vi siwo katã le anyigba dzi la, eɖanye dukɔ, alo ƒome, alo gbe si me wodzi wo tsoe o. Eye nenemae wòanɔ be ne wokɔ nye nuteƒewɔla siwo le Kristo me la tso anyigba dzi yi dziƒowo be woaɣla wo ɖa tso dziku si gbɔna la me eye woaxe fe na wo bliboe ɖe woƒe mɔzɔzɔwo nuwuwu le toɖoɖo nuteƒewɔwɔ me na Kristo Yesu woƒe Aƒetɔ si fɔ tso ku me eye wòyi dziƒo la ta; be xexeame adze klo le xɔsemanɔmee me le nye dzedze kple tsɔtsrɔ̃ kpoyi si akplɔe ɖo la me.

Nenema ke, elabena maɖe nye ŋutikɔkɔe afia kpoyi le nye dzedze kpoyi le alilikpowo me eye nye keklẽ aklẽ yame eye ame katã akpɔ Aƒetɔ la ƒe ŋutikɔkɔe wòayɔ dziƒowo me le ŋutikɔkɔe keklẽ me eye woakɔ ame geɖe dzoe kpoyi, eye anyigba aʋuʋu ahatrɔ ahatso dzodzoe le Kristo Yesu ŋkume, ame si ƒe keklẽ me ame geɖe adi be yewoaɣla yewoƒe mo ɖo. Elabena abe ale si dzikedzo dzona tso ɣedzeƒe, eye wokpɔnɛ yia ɣetoɖoƒe ene la, nenema kee Amegbetɔvi la ƒe vava hã anɔ.

Eye ameha gã aɖe, nuteƒewɔla siwo le Kristo me la, abu kpoyi aɖaŋu manɔmee tso anyigba dzi, elabena woƒe agbenɔnɔ le anyigba dzi le ŋutilã gbegblẽ me la se ɖe nuwuwu eye woawu woƒe mɔzɔzɔwo nu. Eya ta, woakɔ wo ayi dziƒowo ƒe ŋutikɔkɔe me, afi si woakafu wo tegbee eye woaxe fe na wo tegbee. Kpɔ ɖa! Woawɔ wo abe woƒe Dzɔdzɔenyenye ƒe Aƒetɔ mavɔ la ene, eye woado aklala ɣi nyui si me kɔ si womate ŋu aƒo ɖi alo agblẽ o la na wo. Woana fetu geɖe wo ɖe woƒe nuteƒewɔwɔ kple dɔ dzɔdzɔe siwo wowɔ le nye ŋkɔ me la ta, dɔ siwo metsɔ na wo be woawɔ na woa ŋutɔwo ƒe xɔxɔ, viɖe, kple bubu gɔ̃ hã.

Eye ame geɖe atsɔ fetu siwo meva amewo ƒe dzi me bliboe le ɣeyiɣi sia me o la adzoe. Togbɔ be ele alea hã, ame aɖewo abu nu ne klẽla ƒe dzo tɔ dzo woƒe dɔwɔwɔwo be woaɖe tsro katã ɖa le woƒe agbe me le kɔkɔenyenye mavɔ me eye be dɔ vɔ̃wo ƒe ɖiƒoƒo nate ŋu adzo le wo ŋu tegbee. Miese egɔme be, mia dometɔ aɖeke mezɔ bliboe le ŋkunyeme o, eya ta, ame aɖewo abu nu, ame aɖewo abu nu eye woaxɔ fetu, eye ame aɖewo axɔ fetu siwo nye nye dziɖula siwo aɖu dzi abe ale si meɖu ame siwo dze anyi la dzi ene eye mebɔbɔ nɔ Yahweh ŋutɔ ƒe ɖusime, NYE SI MELE gã mavɔ la gɔ̃ hã. Gake, ame siwo katã woakɔ adzoe ahaƒo wo nu ƒu la akpɔ agbe mavɔ tegbee eye woanɔ agbe dzɔdzɔe mavɔwo tegbee elabena nenemae woɖoe na wo.

Nenema ke, ne wokɔ nuteƒewɔlawo dzoe la, ame siwo wogblẽ ɖi la hã ase woƒe nɔnɔmea ƒe kpekpeme gɔme eye ameha gã aɖe alɔ̃ ɖe nyateƒea kple dodokpɔ siwo wòle be woado dzi le la dzi, eye ame geɖe afa avi ahatutu wo ɖokui le dzimetɔtrɔ me eye ame geɖe anɔ agbe si me lɔlɔ̃ kple vɔsa le, eye ame geɖe abu woƒe agbe le lɔlɔ̃ kple subɔsubɔ me na wo havi siwo hã anɔ nɔnɔme sesẽwo me eye ameha gã aɖe ava gbɔnye to Yesu Kristo si nye woƒe Aƒetɔ mavɔ la ƒe ŋkɔ yɔyɔ me va se ɖe nuwuwu!

Eye anyigba aʋuʋu ahatrɔ, eye tsɔtsrɔ̃ gã aɖe adzɔ le anyigba dzi, elabena aʋa kple dɔvɔ̃ kpoyi, kple madzɔmadzɔnyenye, kple dɔwuame kple ku ahe tsɔtsrɔ̃ gã aɖe ava anyigba dzi eye anyigba ƒe nɔnɔme si le eŋu fifia la atrɔ to anyigbaʋuʋu gã geɖewo me eye ame geɖe atsrɔ̃ kpoyi aɖaŋu manɔmee. Eye ame geɖe wu aganɔ agbe eye woatsi agbe le fukpekpe siwo gbɔna la me eye woagblẽ ame geɖe ɖi na Yakob ƒe xaxa ƒe dodokpɔ si va anyigba dzi be wòado ame siwo le anyigba dzi la kpɔ, be wòaɖe ame siwo katã ava xɔxɔ me le Yeshua HaMashiach me la gɔ̃ hã.

Eya ta, miese egɔme be nuŋeŋe geɖewo li na Adam. Lu ƒe nuŋeŋe gbãtɔ la nye na nye nuteƒewɔla siwo le Kristo me, ame siwo katã zɔ nuteƒewɔwɔ me to nye gbɔngbɔn me le nye amenuveve kple nublanuikpɔkpɔ ƒe ɣeyiɣiwo me, ame siwo de gbɔngbɔn ƒe ku le woƒe agbe me to toɖoɖo nye Nyawo me esime wozɔ le nye viɖuɖu ƒe gbɔngbɔn me si me wotre wo nu ɖo na xɔxɔ ƒe ŋkeke gã la. Woawoe nye nuteƒewɔla siwo le Kristo me kple nye dziɖulawo kple nye ŋugbetɔ kple nye ame 144,000 siwo madɔ be woaɖe Yakob agbee tso Ashkenaz kple Esau si me.

Ekema lu ƒe nuŋeŋe adze egɔme afi si lu la tsi le agblewo me eye wòbɔbɔ le toɖoɖo me tso ku si wòtse la ƒe kpekpeme me, eye gbe vɔ̃ siwo metsea ku o la atsi tsitre le gbetɔtɔ me eye gbeƒoƒo gã aɖe adzɔ, eye woaƒo nye lu la nu ƒu ɖe agbe mavɔ me eye woabla gbe vɔ̃wo, ahabla wo, ahatɔ dzo wo le tohehe ƒe dzo mavɔwo me. Eya ta miese egɔme be ame siwo katã gbe be woamaŋe yewo le toɖoɖo me le nye gbeƒoƒo me o la akpe ɖe gbe vɔ̃ siawo ŋu le ame si dze anyi la ƒe dzo mavɔ siwo wowɔ koŋ na toomaɖolawo kple aglãdzelawo ƒe domenyinu me, eye womasi o. Eye nenemae dzimaxɔsetɔwo kple xɔsemanɔsitɔwo ayi ame siwo gbe Yahweh ƒe ŋutifafa nubabla la, Yesu Nazaret-tɔ si anye ne aɖe wo bliboe la gɔ̃ hã.

Nenemae Satanawo ƒe fiaɖuƒe ƒe nuwuwu anɔ, nye detɔwo, mamawo kple nuŋeŋewo ƒe ɣeyiɣi si dzra dzɔdzɔetɔwo ɖo tegbee eye wòtsɔa madzɔmadzɔtɔwo dea tohehe mavɔ me le ame siwo dze anyi la ƒe domenyinu me, dzɔgbe vɔ̃ si wodzra ɖo ɖi na ame siwo katã yi edzi le toomaɖoɖo me eye woyi edzi le Satanawo, kple Dzɔlawo, kple ame siwo katã dzea woƒe mɔ vɔ̃wo yome ƒe aglãdzedze me. Woawo awɔ aʋa kple Alẽvi la, eye Alẽvi la aɖu wo dzi: elabena eyae nye Aƒetɔwo ƒe Aƒetɔ, kple Fiawo ƒe Fia: eye ame siwo le eƒe akpa dzi, siwo woyɔ, eye wotia, eye wonye nuteƒewɔlawo.

Eya ta, miese egɔme be woafɔ tso aʋa ƒe afi me, lã wɔadã si tso atsiaƒu me, lã wɔadã si tso anyigba dzi, kple dzoka gã la gɔ̃ hã! Woafɔ tso tsɔtsrɔ̃ gã si woa ŋutɔwo he vɛ la ƒe afi me. Gake, woatsɔ woƒe tsɔtsrɔ̃ si wohe vɛ la ƒe fɔbuame aɖo futɔ amedzrowo, kple Kristotɔwo, kple nu siwo katã ku ɖe Yesu Nazaret-tɔ la ŋu dzi, eye alea woawɔ Kristo wòanye sedzimawɔla le woƒe Satana ƒe fiaɖuƒe me. Nyateƒe, nyateƒe, nye, Kristo Yesu, gome alo akpa aɖeke mele asinye kpli wo nenema ke o, gake ame siwo katã yɔa woƒe Aƒetɔ Yesu ƒe ŋkɔ va se ɖe nuwuwu la, woaxɔ wo.

Eya ta woawɔ aʋa kple kɔkɔetɔwo eye woaɖu wo dzi kple ŋutasesẽ gã aɖe si woagbugbɔ atsɔ axe fe na wo nublanuimakpɔmakpɔe tegbee. Nenema kee, woaxɔ ameha gãwo ƒe ameha gãwo tso tsɔtsrɔ̃ mavɔ me to woƒe ŋutilã nuvɔ̃me si lé wo kluviwoe na nuvɔ̃ la tsɔtsrɔ̃ me. Eye nenema ke maɖe dzidzɔkpɔkpɔwo katã ɖa le anyigba dzi eye magbã atamkakawo kple adzewɔwɔ siwo vɔ̃ɖitɔ la he va wo dzi la be woate ŋu anyɔ tso woƒe alɔ̃ me anɔ agbe dzɔdzɔe le ŋkunye me esi wole dodom le nuvɔ̃ me le dzimetɔtrɔ blibo kple Satana ƒe fiaɖuƒe, si wodo alɔe tsã to lɔlɔ̃nu me le toomaɖomaɖo me la gbegbe me.

Gake, nenema kee ame dzedzeariwo aɖe wo ɖokui fia esi wowɔa wo ɖokui abe amedzrowo tso xexe bubu siwo mele anyi o siwo trɔna alakpatɔe la me, siwo mele anyi le mɔ si nu woƒe Yesu ƒe habɔbɔ me nufialawo fia nu amehawo to nusrɔ̃srɔ̃ tsɔ tsi tre ɖe nusrɔ̃srɔ̃ ŋu la me o. Nenema kee be ame dzedzeariwo ƒe aʋatsonyawo ate ŋu axɔ dzi ase le aʋatso gã aɖe me na ame siwo kpɔa dzidzɔ le madzɔmadzɔnyenye me. Eye nenemae woɖo mɔa ɖi na aʋatsokala siawo be woaɖe wo ɖokui fia elabena wo katã wonye aʋatsokala siwo wɔa aʋatso.

Woawɔ wo ɖokui abe kpeɖeŋutɔ dɔmenyotɔ siwo va be yewoaxɔ Adam tso eya ŋutɔ ƒe tsɔtsrɔ̃ me, ame si ƒe tsɔtsrɔ̃ me le nyateƒe me la, woaɖe wo ɖokui fia be yewoado alɔe le adzame gã aɖe me kple aʋatso katã kple aʋatsodzesiwo kple nukunu geɖewo. Elabena woatsɔ woƒe mɔ̃ɖaŋununyawo kple ayemɔwo kple ŋunyɔnuwo avae eye woaʋu aʋali la me bliboe. Womagate ŋu axe mɔ na vɔ̃ɖinyenye o eye vɔ̃ɖinyenye ƒomevi katã aɖu anyigba dzi le Yakob ƒe xaxaɣi. Esi amegbetɔ dzɔdzɔe di vɔ̃ɖinyenye kple viviti ŋutɔ ta la, Yahweh atsɔ amegbetɔ dzɔdzɔe ade asi na Satana ƒe fiaɖuƒe la bliboe ale be woate ŋu axɔ ame geɖewo ɖe agbe, abe to dzo me ene. Eye esia le esi amegbetɔ nye vɔ̃ɖitɔ eye eƒe dɔwɔwɔwo nye vɔ̃ɖi siwo amegbetɔ vɔ̃ɖi lɔ̃na le eƒe aglãdzedze kple Satanawo me ta. Eya ta, woatsɔ amegbetɔwo ade asi na Satanawo bliboe ame siwo ɖo aɖaŋu xoxo ɖe Adam ƒe nuwuƒe mavɔ ŋu, togbɔ be woƒe ɖoɖowo azu dzodzro eye ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ sesẽ kple vevie ko woakpɔ elabena nenemae wodze.

Eya ta woado kple woƒe kuxi gbɔkpɔnuwo kple mɔ̃ɖaŋununyawo le asi me eye woado awɔ wo ɖokui abe amedzrowo tso aʋatso xexe bubu siwo mele anyi o me ene. Eye Barabbas afɔ, kple lã wɔadã si tso anyigba me la gɔ̃ hã eye wòfɔ kple da ƒe aɖuɖu le asi me.
