Kele 29, 2024

Ɣeyiɣi La Dze Na Vava: Ɛ̃ Kple Amen

Tso Yeshua HaMashiach Gbɔ:

Amesi Mose de se na Israel be woaɖo to ahawɔ edzi.

Miɖo to ahasé Yeshua HaMashiach ƒe nyawo le ɣeyiɣi mamlɛ sia me, eye mibɔbɔ miaƒe dziwo le toɖoɖo me ne miaƒe dziwo naxɔ nye mɔfiame. Elabena nye nyawo nye agbe na amesiwo ke ɖe wo ŋu eye wotsɔa lãmesẽ kple ŋugbedodo vɛ, le xaxa gã ɣeyiɣiwo gɔ̃ hã me. Eya ta mise egɔme be xaxa gã la ɖo eye dzɔgbevɔ̃e le aƒe tum, eye ɣeyiɣi lae nye fifia. Le nyateƒe me, le mavɔ nyateƒe me la, nyee nye Yeshua HaMashiach, Yuda-to la me Dzata la amesi gbɔna ʋɔnu drɔ̃ ge ahawɔ aʋa, eye mise egɔme be naneke meɣla ɖe nye ŋku me o, eye naneke meɣla ɖe Yahweh ƒe ŋku me o eye mekpɔa madzɔmadzɔnyenye kple ametsɔtsrɔ̃ gã si Satana ƒe Sinagog me tɔ siwo dze anyigba la dzi le wɔwɔm ɖe anyigba la dzi tɔwo ŋu eye maɖo eteƒe, Aƒetɔ Dzɔdzɔe lae gblɔe!

Elabena le nyateƒe me, le yubile geɖewo me la, dzedzlawo do woƒe nuvɔ̃ɖiwɔwɔ ƒe nya ɣaɣla la ɖe ŋgɔ, va se ɖe fifi ɣeyiɣi siwo me woƒe aɖaŋuvɔ̃wo le klalo be woava eme bliboe. Gake mise egɔme be woɖe mɔ na wo ko be woawɔ Yahweh ƒe tameɖoɖowo dzi le eƒe xɔxɔ ƒe ɖoɖo mavɔwo me hena amegbetɔwo ƒe ɖeɖekpɔkpɔ blibo la. Elabena wode alɔ̃ amegbetɔ dzro la le aʋatso gã kple ŋudzɔmanɔmanɔ gã me.

Eye nenemae dzedzlawo zãa amegbetɔwo tsɔ doa amegbetɔwo ŋutɔ ƒe tsɔtsrɔ̃ ɖe ŋgɔ esime woyɔa kekeli be viviti eye viviti be kekeli. Eye nenema kee Satana ƒe Sinagog si dze ŋugbedodonyigbawo dzi la hã le eye maho wo keŋkeŋ. Elabena Satana ƒe Sinagog la di be yewoaxɔ Yahweh ƒe ŋugbedodo siwo woŋlɔ ɖi hedo kpe edzi na blema Fofowo la. Satana ƒe Sinagog la yɔ fũ kple gbe vɔ̃ɖi siwo dzedzlawo ƒã ɖe nye luwo dome eye ɣeyiɣi si me woaho wo la ɖo eye nenema woŋlɔe ɖi eye nenema woawɔe: Elabena kpɔ ɖa, ŋkeke la gbɔna, si afi dzo abe kpodzo ene, eye dadalawo katã, ɛ̃, kple amesiwo katã wɔa nuvɔ̃ɖi la, azu gbe ƒuƒui, eye ŋkeke si gbɔna la, atɔ dzo wo, Aƒetɔ sabaot gblɔe, eye magblẽ ke alo alɔ aɖeke ɖi na wo o.

Gake, nenemae dzedzlawo wɔe be yiyi la lɔ eƒe ɖɔ ɖe xexeame katã fifia, eye aʋatso mee wowɔa aʋa le. Eye fifia ye nye woƒe tsitretsitsi ɣeyiɣi eye woƒe dziɖuɖu kpuie makɔmakɔ la ɖo vɔ. Elabena Yahweh le eƒe dzɔdzɔenyenye me ana nusi tututu amegbetɔ dzro la di le bometsitsi me le eƒe nuvɔ̃ ƒe vivisesewo yome titi me lae. Eye nenema viviti kple vɔ̃ɖinyenye si amegbetɔ dzro la di la asu amegbetɔ dzro la si fifia. Gake, ne nye Nyanyui la ɣla la, eɣla ɖe amesiwo tsrɔ̃ la. Amesiwo me xexe sia me mawu la gbã ŋku na dzimaxɔsetɔwo ƒe tamesusuwo, be Kristo, amesi nye Mawu ƒe nɔnɔme la ƒe Nyanyui ŋutikɔkɔetɔ la ƒe kekeli naga klẽ ɖe wo dzi o. Eye nenemae amegbetɔ dzro la le eƒe manyamanya me kpe ɖe yiyi la ŋu le eƒe aʋatso me, elabena aʋatso mee wowɔa aʋa le.

Eya ta, El Shaddai, dɔ aʋatso gã aɖe ɖe wo be woaxɔ aʋatsonya siwo dzedzlawo wɔ la adzi ase, be woabu fɔ wo katã amesiwo mexɔ nyateƒe la dzi se o, ke boŋ wokpɔ dzidzɔ ɖe madzɔmadzɔnyenye ŋu. Gake le Yahweh ƒe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ kple nublanuikpɔkpɔ mavɔ me la, woaɖe vivisesewo ɖa le anyigba dzi fifia eye nudzɔdzɔ siwo amegbetɔ dzro la di vevie be yeakpe ɖo la, woaɖe wo ɖa le anyigba dzi. Eye nenema ke woaɖe nuvɔ̃ ƒe vivisesewo gɔ̃ hã ɖa le anyigba dzi, ale gbegbe be amewo atsi tsukpe ne wole didim be yewoana yewoƒe dzi ƒe nudzodzrowo nade yewo dzi. Gake, Yahweh metia nuwɔna siawo be yeawɔ anya helĩhelĩ ɖe yeƒe nuwɔwɔwo ŋu o ke boŋ be yeahe to na nuvɔ̃ɖiwɔlawo ahanyɔ amesiwo, ne menye nenema o la, magbe be yewomanyɔ tso yewoƒe alɔ̃ me o elabena to nuvɔ̃ ƒe vivisesewo kple madzɔmadzɔnyenye ƒe fetuwo me la, wowɔ wo wodam hegbã ŋku na wo. Le ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ blibo kple kpoyi teƒe la, wona amenuveve amegbetɔ dzro la elabena dzedzlawo ɖu amegbetɔ dzro la dzi.

Ɛ̃, le Yahweh ƒe amenuveve kple nublanuikpɔkpɔ me kple eƒe ɖoɖo mavɔ me la, Yahweh di be yeanyɔ amegbetɔ dzro la tso nuvɔ̃ me akayɔ amegbetɔ dzro la ɖe dzɔdzɔenyenye me. Yɔyɔ sia si ame geɖewo lɔ̃ faa xɔ nye xɔxɔ le nye amenuveve ɣeyiɣiwo me to Xɔxɔ ƒe Nyanyui la dzi. Ɣeyiɣi siwo me la, xɔxɔ nye nunana femaxee si me ame ate ŋu awɔm Aƒetɔe eye ɣeyiɣi anɔ esi be wòawɔ eya ŋutɔ ƒe xɔxɔ ŋudɔ kple vɔvɔ̃ kple dzodzodzoe ne wova gbɔnye bliboe, ɛ̃, wodo le nuvɔ̃ me bliboe. Eye nenemae wòle be nye amenuveve ɣeyiɣiwo de se, togbɔ be amenuveve kple nublanuikpɔkpɔ li kplim ɖaa hã. Eye ava eme kpuie be lalam ɣeyiɣiwo maganɔ anyi o eye xexe sia me fiaɖuƒewo azu miaƒe Aƒetɔ la, kple eƒe Kristo la ƒe fiaɖuƒewo, eye maɖu fia tso mavɔ me yi mavɔ me kple nuteƒewɔlawo le miaƒe domenyinu mavɔ si mama bliboe kple nye dziɖulawo me.

Eye ameha gã aɖe ava gbɔnye le Yakob ƒe xaxaɣi be maxɔ wo ɖe agbe tegbee. Eye amesiwo katã yɔa Aƒetɔ la ƒe ŋkɔ va se ɖe nuwuwu la, woaxɔ wo ɖe agbe, gbegbɔgblɔ alo dukɔ ka ke me wotso yɔe o. Eye meʋã be maxɔ Israel ɖe agbe, eye mele nye ƒometɔ vavã siwo alakpasubɔsubɔhawo tra mɔ na eye Satana ƒe Sinagog te wo ɖe anyi la yɔm, eye woakpɔ xɔxɔ mavɔ eye magaxɔ Yakob amesi ƒe xɔxɔ woawɔ to klẽla ƒe dzo me la ɖe agbe. Gake mise egɔme be agbenɔnɔ didi kple ŋutilãme kesinɔnuwo ƒe ɣeyiɣiwo le anyigba dzi la de se va se ɖe esime woaɖo nye Fiaɖuƒe la anyi kpuie, eye wolé anyigba la ɖe kɔsɔkɔsɔwo me le mɔ kpɔm na eƒe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ mamlɛawo be woakɔ wo ɖe anyi le dziku gã me. Kpɔ ɖa! Alẽvi la ƒe Dziku la gbɔna gbã, eye xaxa gãwo aƒo ɖe anyigba la dzi abe tsɔtsrɔ̃ blibo ƒe tsiɖɔɖɔ ene, eye ame geɖewo atsrɔ̃.

Gake, xaxa kple hiã gãwo aɖo vava ne woʋu nutrenuwo eye dziku gã, kple dɔvɔ̃ kple dɔléle geɖewo, kple dɔwuame, kple kuxiaxi, kple aʋawɔwɔ, kple madzɔmadzɔnyenye, kple ku kple bubu geɖewo ava anyigba la dzi bliboe; gake ameha gãwo ƒe ameha gã aɖe anyɔ tso woƒe xuxɔe nɔnɔmewo me. Eye woanɔ vevesese kple luʋɔ me xaxa gã me ne wode dzesi ŋɔŋlɔawo ƒe nyateƒe si mate ŋu ada fɔ o kple alesi wogbe kple dzikui be yewoaƒo asa na yewoƒe mɔnukpɔkpɔ hena xɔxɔ kple be yewoasi le dziku kple ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ nu le nye Hame nuteƒewɔla la ƒoƒo ƒu ɖe ɖokuinye gbɔ me. Gake le woƒe xaxa kple vevesese veviwo me la, made dzideƒo si womate ŋu aɖu edzi o la wo me eye mahe wo ava gbɔnye bliboe, ɛ̃, amesiwo katã va gbɔnye le fuwɔame kple xaxa me kple to xɔse ta kuku ƒe dodokpɔwo me. Eye fetu gã aɖe anɔ wo si, fetu si lolo ale gbegbe be woƒe xaxa siwo gbɔna la mesɔ kple ŋutikɔkɔe mavɔ si woaɖe afia le wo me bliboe la o.

Nenema woŋlɔe ɖi eye nenema woawɔe bliboe: Ame siawoe nye amesiwo tso xaxa gã la me, eye wonya woƒe awutewuiwo, hewɔ wo ɣii le Alẽvi la ƒe ʋu me. Eya ta, wole Mawu ƒe fiazikpui la ŋgɔ, eye wosubɔnɛ zã kple keli le eƒe Gbedoxɔ me: eye amesi bɔbɔ nɔ Fiazikpui la dzi la anɔ wo dome. Dɔ magawu wo o, tsikɔ magawu wo o, ɣe magaʋã wo o, alo dzoxɔxɔ aɖeke o. Elabena Alẽvi la, si le fiazikpui la titina la, anyi wo, eye wòakplɔ wo ayi agbetsidzɔƒewo: eye Mawu atutu aɖatsiwo katã ɖa le woƒe ŋkuwo me.

Eye nenema yiyi la lɔ eƒe ɖɔ xlã xexeame le aʋatso gã, adzodada, amedzizizi me eye le mɔ geɖewo nu la yiyi la ƒe aɖi wɔa eƒe vɔ̃ɖinyenye to eƒe aʋatso kple aɖivɔ̃ mɔwo dzi. Elabena elɔ aʋatso ƒe ɖɔ eye wòlɔ fe ƒe ɖɔ ale be xexeame katã le yiyi la ƒe asikpakpa me bliboe fifia eye eƒe aɖi la wɔe be wòku atri. Yiyi la ʋã kple adzame kple aʋatso gã to nuvɔ̃ɖiwɔwɔ ƒe nya ɣaɣla la dzi, eye anyigba la le eƒe ɖɔ me bliboe fifia. Eye le aʋatso gã me la, alakpatɔ siawo (siwo dzedzlawo wɔ) dze anyigbawo dzi abe ʋetsuviwo ene. Ɛ̃, le mɔ ma ke si nu yiyi la tsɔa eƒe aziwo ɣlae va se ɖe esime eƒe vi geɖewo do go heƒu du yi ahalɔ woƒe ɖɔwo ɖe eŋu. Eye nenema Satana ƒe sinagog la xɔ xexeame to ŋusẽ kple aɖaŋu vɔ̃ɖi me.

Eye le ɣeyiɣi aɖe megbe kple ayemenu geɖewo me la, wowɔ wo ɖokuiwo ŋusẽtɔwo, elabena nenema Yahweh na wo le eƒe xɔxɔ ƒe ɖoɖo mavɔwo me be woawɔe. Elabena woƒe aʋatsonyawo gbã ŋku na amegbetɔ dzro la eye wòdze wo yome le manyamanya me yi eya ŋutɔ ƒe tsɔtsrɔ̃ me. Ðoɖo siwo ŋu meƒo nu tsoe la aƒo eta le mama mamlɛtɔ si anɔ luwo kple gbe vɔ̃ɖiwo dome kple amesiwo tsɔ wo ɖokuiwo na bliboe kple amesiwo lɔ̃a woƒe nuvɔ̃wo eye wotia dzo la faa le woƒe lɔlɔ̃ ƒe kadodo vɔ̃ɖi kple woa ŋutɔwo ƒe tsɔtsrɔ̃ me, eye woave wo ɖokuiwo tegbee le tiatia si wowɔ be yewoawɔ ɖeka kple yiyi la ƒe tameɖoɖowo la ta. Eye emegbe woakɔ Mawu ƒe dziku ƒe dɔmedzoe ƒe zeawo ɖe anyi bliboe.

Mise egɔme be yiyi lae nye Satana ƒe Sinagog si le klalo fifia be yeaɖu anyigba la dzi le woƒe lã wɔadã ƒe ɖoɖo mamlɛtɔ si ƒe dziɖuɖu matsɔ ahe nuwuwu mavɔ vɛ eye woƒe tsɔtsrɔ̃ kple ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ mehe ɖe megbe o. Mise egɔme, nye lɔlɔ̃tɔwo, be Satana ƒe Sinagog la katã nye alakpatɔ siwo wɔa alakpa. Wonye Edomtɔwo ƒe gbe vɔ̃ɖi siwo ƒe tɔgbuiwo medo goe abe Mawu ƒe alẽvi la ene esime metsɔ ɖokuinye na faa be maxe fe ɖe amegbetɔwo ƒe nuvɔ̃wo ta. Eye nenema meɖe wo goe, eye nenema megblɔe na wo tẽ ɖe woƒe ŋku vɔ̃ɖiwo me: Mitso mia fofo abosam gbɔ, eye mia fofo ƒe nudzodzrowo miedina be miawɔ: amewulae wònye tso gɔmedzedzea me, eye menɔ nyateƒe la me o, elabena nyateƒe aɖeke mele eme o. Ne eƒo aʋatso la, eƒoa eya ŋutɔ tɔ: elabena aʋatsokala wònye, eye eyae nye aʋatso fofo.

Eye tso esime wodze ŋugbedodonyigba la dzi gbã si menye wo tɔ be woaxɔ o la, woti ŋugbedodonyigbawo dzi nɔla vavãwo yome, wɔ fu wo, eye wotsrɔ̃ wo keŋkeŋ. Wowɔa alakpa be yewoe nye xɔxɔ ƒe domenyila vavãwo, eye wozãa Mose ƒe se la ƒe akpa aɖewo gɔ̃ hã le mɔ gbegblẽ nu le woƒe Talmud alakpasubɔsubɔha la me. Eye to mɔ siawo dzi la wogbã ŋku na ame geɖewo siwo susuna be yewowɔa nye lɔlɔ̃nu hesusuna be mayra amesi ke yra gbe vɔ̃ɖi siawo eye maƒo fi amesi ke ƒoa fi gbe vɔ̃ɖi siawo. Eye nenema nye dukɔ la me tɔ geɖewo tsɔa wo ɖokuiwo kpea ɖe Satana ƒe Sinagog ŋu le manyamanya me to lɔlɔ̃nu me eye mele se dom na mi godoo be miatrɔ dzi me bliboe tso woƒe aʋatsonya siwo mietia na mia ŋutɔwo ƒe aʋatso la me.

Vinye dzaawo, mise egɔme be nyee nye Kristo Yesu, nyemeŋlɔ nusɔsrɔ̃ ƒe Biblia aɖeke kpɔ o gake wozãa esiawo abe aʋatso ƒe dɔwɔnuwo ene kple aʋatsonya siwo wona le axawo dzi be woatsɔ aɖe nyateƒe la gɔmee. Eye dzedzlawo trɔ nye nyawo le alakpa Bibliawo me, eya ta, mikpɔ Bizantiatɔwo ƒe nuŋɔŋlɔwo, ɛ̃, Textus Receptus afisi medzra nye Nyawo ɖo la. Eya ta, miva gbɔnye nye lɔlɔ̃tɔwo eye misrɔ̃ nu tso gbɔnye eye makplɔ mi ayi woƒe fifi aʋatsowo ƒe nyateƒe la me bliboe ne miabɔbɔ miaƒe dzi ɖe nye gbe si amesiwo ƒoa nu ɖe nunye la na la te.

Mise egɔme be nyee nye Yesu Nazaret-tɔ la, amesi do go alakpatɔ ƒomevi siawo ke ɖe woƒe kɔnyinyi siwo wotsɔ nu gblɔe si wodo ɖe dzi wu Yahweh ƒe Nya dzadzɛ siwo wona Mose la ta. Elabena Mose ƒe Se la le kɔkɔe, eye wòle dzɔdzɔe, eye wòle nuteƒe tegbee eye ne ame aɖe aƒo fi Mose ƒe Se la, ekema ame ma ŋutɔ azu ame ƒofi. Elabena nye, Yesu Nazaret-tɔ la, mewɔ Mose ƒe Se la dzi esime metsɔ nye ʋu si me nuvɔ̃ aɖeke mele o la na le Kalvari ɖe Adam (kple eƒe dzidzimeviwo katã) ƒe nuvɔ̃wo ta eye nye lɔlɔ̃tɔ siwo zɔna le xɔse me le nye, Kristo Yesu, woƒe Aƒetɔ si dze dzi henye mavɔtɔ la me la, léa Mose ƒe Se la me ɖe asi bliboe.

Eya ta miese gɔme be mate ŋu adzɔ be ame aŋe Mose ƒe Se la ne ezɔna le xɔse me esi mekplɔa wo to nye gbɔgbɔ si mewɔ ɖe wo me tegbee la dzi o. Togbɔ be, ame sia ame si zɔna le ŋutilã me la le se ŋem elabena Mose va be yeabu fɔ nuvɔ̃ le ŋutilã me to dzidzɔ se mavɔ ma si Yahweh ŋutɔ ƒe asibidɛ ŋlɔ la tsɔtsɔ na me. Eye ŋutilã aɖeke mele dzɔdzɔe le ŋkuvye me o, eye mele dzɔdzɔe le Dzɔdzɔenyenye ƒe Mawu Mavɔ la hã ŋkume o. Eya ta, miʋu miaƒe nuvɔ̃wo kple gbɔdzɔgbɔdzɔwo me na nye, Mawu ƒe alẽvi si nye miaƒe nunɔlagã mavɔ tegbee le Melkizedek ƒe ɖoɖo la nu, eye maklɔ mia ŋu keŋkeŋ le nye avulédoblo si wokɔ ɖi hena ame siwo katã vaa gbɔnye hena xɔxɔ la ŋuklɔklɔ me.

Ame kae se nu sia tɔgbi kpɔ? Ame kae kpɔ nu siawo tɔgbi kpɔ? Ðe woadzi anyigba la le ŋkeke ɖeka mea? Alo ɖe woadzi dukɔ aɖe zi ɖeka? Ke hã nenemae wòdzɔe be Israel dukɔa gava zu dukɔ to gbe vɔ̃wo ƒe gbeƒãɖeɖe me, eye Mawu ŋutɔe ɖe mɔ na eƒe futɔwo be woawɔ alakpa le manyamanya me le woƒe aʋatsokaka me be yewonye Yakob ke menye Esau o, elabena Esau ye tsɔ eƒe ŋgɔgbezizi dzra na nɔvia ɖevi. Eye ale yi dze aʋatso le manyamanya kple dada me be yeana sikagbe mavɔ aɖe nava anyigba dzi elabena nenemae wo fofowo ƒe nudzodzrowo le si woawɔ, eye ame siwo dze anyi lae nye wo fofowo, Satanawo ŋutɔ siwo ƒe nɔnɔme woɖuna.

Ke hã wodze anyigbawo dzi kple ŋutasesẽ gã aɖe elabena nenemae wona wo be woawɔe be woanyɔ ame siwo le alɔ̃ dɔm, kple ame siwo katã le ŋkuagbãnvui me siwo aɖo to woƒe Mesia la ƒe yɔyɔ, Yeshua, Mesia Vavã la ŋutɔ. Eye miese gɔme be mekpɔa nu vɔ̃ɖi sia nu vɔ̃ɖi si wowɔna, eye mekpɔa fɔmaɖilawo dzi wɔwɔ madzɔmadzɔ sia madzɔmadzɔ, eye maɖo eteƒe, Aƒetɔ Mavɔ si Yahweh wɔ Mesia, Fia, Nunɔlagã, kple Aƒetɔ lae gblɔe! Woŋlɔ nu vɔ̃ɖi sia nu vɔ̃ɖi ɖi pɛpɛpɛ le dziƒowo, eye woaɖo eteƒe na wo tegbee kple bliboe le woƒe domenyinu mavɔ me le dzo blɔtɔ si xɔ dzo wu la me le viviti bliba me tegbee.

Eye mekpɔ Raxel wòle avi fam na viawo esi wole tsɔtsrɔ̃ kple fukpekpe madzɔmadzɔ tom to Satana ƒe Sinagog la me eye maɖo eteƒe, Aƒetɔwo ƒe Aƒetɔ kple Fiawo ƒe Fia lae gblɔe, eye makpɔ Israel ƒe ame susɔeawo ta agbagbe to Daniel ƒe kwasiɖa blaadre lia me, si nye Yakob ƒe xaxa la ŋutɔ. Eya ta, tia agbe, be wò kple viwòwo siaa mianɔ agbe, to lɔlɔ̃ na Aƒetɔ wò Mawu me, to toɖoɖo eƒe gbe me, kple to kuɖeɖe eŋu me: elabena eyae nye wò agbe, kple wò ŋkekewo ƒe didime: be nànɔ anyigba si Aƒetɔ la ka atam na mia fofowo, Abraham, Isak, kple Yakob, be yeatsɔ ana wo la dzi.

Gakpe ɖe eŋu la, mena Mose gblɔ nyateƒe mavɔ siwo gbɔna siwo metsɔ nɛ ku ɖe mia fofowo ŋu la fia mia fofowo, gbeƒãɖeɖe si medɔ to nye dɔla dzi ake hena Israel ƒe toɖoɖo, eye Nya siawo dɔlae nye nye Israel ƒe Mawu Mavɔ si bubu aɖeke meli kpe ɖe eŋu o. Eye Mose gblɔe ɖi be mafɔ Nyagblɔɖila aɖe ɖe tsitre na wo tso wo nɔviwo dome abe wò ene, eye matsɔ nye nyawo ade nu me nɛ, eye wòagblɔ nu siwo katã made se nɛ la na wo. Eye ame sia ame si maɖo to nye nyawo, si wòagblɔ le nye Ŋkɔ me o la, mabiae tso esi. Se egɔme O' Israel be Yeshua HaMashiach, ame si miegbe lae nye ame si medɔ ɖa hena miaƒe xɔxɔ eye eya gbegbe nye Dzɔdzɔenyenye ƒe Mawu Mavɔ, Yahweh ŋutɔ gbegbe.

Eya ta, se ɣeyiɣi siwo me miele la ƒe kpekpeme gɔme O' Israel. Le go ɖeka me la, miaƒe ketɔwo xɔ miaƒe anyigbawo, Esau si dzra eƒe ŋgɔgbedzinyenye na Yakob ƒe dzidzimeviwo gɔ̃ hã, eye wodi miaƒe tsɔtsrɔ̃ blibo kple keŋkeŋ, togbɔ be mia dometɔ geɖe wɔ ɖeka kpli wo le manyamanya me le miaƒe agutso si miesrɔ̃ tso mia fofowo gbɔ me hã. Ke hã, woado ŋugbe geɖewo na mi eye ŋugbedodo geɖewo ye woƒe aʋatsonyagblɔɖila kple aʋatso Moshiach awɔ elabena aƒo nu abe dzoka ene eye eƒe ŋugbedodowo ahe tsɔtsrɔ̃ ko vɛ, eya ta migaxɔ woƒe dzesi o ne menye nenema o la miawɔ ɖeka kpli wo le woƒe domenyinu mavɔ me le dzo ta ma si nɔa bibim ɣesiaɣi si me woƒe nɔvi meku o la me.

Le go bubu me la, agbe mavɔ kple ŋutifafa le mia si si nye ŋutɔ nye asi na, Yahweh ŋutɔ ƒe asi lɔlɔ̃ kple mavɔ gɔ̃ hã le vinye mavɔ kple lɔlɔ̃tɔ Yeshua HaMashiach, Yesu Nazaretɔ nye nukikli kpe si dzi ame siwo katã maxɔse o la akli nu le la ƒe ŋkume. Eye metsɔ miaƒe etsɔme de eƒe asi sẽŋuwo me eye ame aɖeke mate ŋu ate ɖe ŋunye to Mose dzi azɔ o, negbe to Kristo dzi ko elabena mewɔ Yesu Nazaretɔ la mɔ la nyateƒe la kple agbe la si manɔmee ame aɖeke mate ŋu ate ɖe Yahweh, Mawu Gã Mavɔ la ŋu o elabena aleae megblɔe ɖi!

Eya ta, Israel migasẽ miaƒe dziwo le nye nyawo sese kple kpɔkpɔ me o eye mina woage ɖe miaƒe dziwo kple miaƒe amenyenye ƒe gogloƒe ŋutɔ eye migawɔ vodada ma ke si mia fofowo wɔ si ŋu woabia akɔnta wo le la o. Miga gbe nye agbe mavɔ ƒe nunana si mele tsɔtsɔm na mi ŋutɔŋutɔ ake la o. Miva Yeshua HaMashiach gbɔ bliboe le toɖoɖo blibo me eye mialala miaƒe Aƒetɔ mavɔ la. Elabena eyae ava ɖe mi eye wòakplɔ mi ayi dedieƒe kple eƒe aʋakɔ sẽŋu si nye Israel vi 144,000 siwo wogbugbɔ dzi le towo dome eye Mawu Gbagbe la ƒe dzesi anɔ woƒe ŋgonuwo.

Eya ta, nye lɔlɔ̃tɔwo, mele kuku ɖem na mi be miatrɔ ɖe miaƒe Mesia vavã la ŋu elabena vɔsa megahiã o elabena Yesu Nazaretɔ la hã nye Mawu ƒe Alẽvi si gbɔna abe Yuda Ƒomea ƒe Dzata ene eye metsɔ ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ gã kple dzɔdzɔe geɖewo de asi nɛ be wòawɔ.

Eye ale miese egɔme be, Nyee, Yahweh, Mawu Gã Mavɔ lae le Israel yɔm ɖe dzimetɔtrɔ me be woaxɔ woƒe Mesia vavã la atuu eye be mate ŋu aɖe mi to eƒe asi sẽŋu la dzi. Se nye Nyawo Israel eye nàtia agbe be wò kple viwòwo le megbewò miakpɔ dzidzedze. Eye ale meɖo aɖaŋu na mi ake le nye lɔlɔ̃ na mi vinyewo me: Miegbugbɔ ta na Vi la ne megado dziku o, eye miatsrɔ̃ le mɔ la dzi, ne eƒe dɔmedzoe bi xaa.

NYE ye nye Dzɔdzɔenyenye ƒe Mawu Gã Mavɔ kple Tegbeetɔ, Yahweh dziƒowo kple anyigba wɔla gɔ̃ hã eye ame aɖeke mele abe nye ene o.

NYE ye nye ame si dɔ nya siawo ɖe nye dukɔ lɔlɔ̃a to nye dɔla sẽŋu si meɖe fia eye medɔ Nyanyui Mavɔ la ɖa kple tameɖoɖo be Nya siawo akpɔ ŋkuwo, towo, kple dziwo na ame siwo katã wodɔ wo ɖo la gbɔ. Eye metia nye dɔla hafi woɖo anyigba ƒe gɔmeɖoanyiwo, eye meyɔe, eye mekɔ eŋuti, eye mede dɔ asi nɛ hena Nyanyui Mavɔ si woɖe fia le eƒe ɣeyiɣi me la ɖeɖefia.

NYE ye nye Dzɔdzɔenyenye ƒe Mawu Mavɔ si aɖe nye nuwɔwɔ lɔlɔ̃a kple nye dukɔ lɔlɔ̃a tso ame muamuawo si me eye woayi mavɔme kple ŋutifafa kple dzidzɔ mavɔ me eye mawɔ dziƒo yeye kple anyigba yeye na wo. Eye mawɔ nye nɔƒe ɖe wo dome eye woakpɔ nye ŋkume, eye woazɔ kplim le nye lãnyiƒe dediewo, eye malée ɖe asi esime wole zɔzɔm kplim ayi mavɔme blibo la me.

NYE ye nye ame si anye nuwo katã le nuwo katã me. Eye mana woaɖu agbeti la eye woazɔ Yerusalem Yeye ƒe mɔdodoawo dzi eye woanɔ dzidzɔ kple blibodede blibo me tegbee. Eye matutu aɖatsiwo katã ɖa le woƒe ŋkuwo me: eye ku maganɔ anyi o, alo nuxaxa, alo avi, alo vevesese aɖeke maganɔ anyi o: elabena tsã nuwo nu ayi.

Shalom! Se gbenye Israel eye nàtrɔ dzi me eye nàva ame si medɔ ɖa hena wò xɔxɔ gbɔ, Yeshua HaMashiach gɔ̃ hã!

Shalom! Na Mawu ƒe Israel elabena Mawu ƒe Israel mee woaƒo nye dukɔa nu ƒu ɖo eye womagaka wo hlẽ gbeɖe o, ke boŋ wo katã woanɔ agbe le ɖekawɔwɔ, dzɔdzɔenyenye, kple ŋutifafa mavɔ me.

Eye ŋutifafa mavɔ anɔ Mawu ƒe Israel dzi tegbee, eye Nyee, Mawu Mavɔ lae gblɔe ale eye ale woawɔe tegbee.
