Yɔyɔ Mlɔeba Hafi Ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ Nage Ðe Ame Siwo Wodrɔ̃ Ʋɔnui Dzi

Kpɔ ɖa! Nye ye nye Yahweh, NYE LA Gã la, eye meva be maɖo nye lɔlɔ̃ ƒe Nyawo kple yɔyɔ mlɔeba ɖe ame siwo adze ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ mlɔeba mɔ nu ne wogbe nye yɔyɔ mlɔeba la. Togbɔ be, ame siwo katã yɔa Yesu ƒe ŋkɔ va se ɖe nuwuwu la akpɔ xɔxɔ gã kple mavɔ kpakple fetu gã hã la, amegbetɔ geɖewo age ɖe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ me le woƒe nu vɔ̃wo kple dzi sesẽ ta. Elabena, Nye ye nye ame si fiaa nu madzɔmadzɔwɔlawo be woatrɔ dzi me, eye ame siwo gbe dzimetɔtrɔ la, meyi edzi fiaa nu wo va se ɖe esime woƒe dziwo ku atri keŋkeŋ eye wotsɔ wo de ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ me.

Eya ta, meva be mana gbedasi sia le nye lɔlɔ̃ na mi me, eye maɖo nye amenuveve si medze na mi o la ɖe xexe si wodrɔ̃ ʋɔnui la me ake le nye agbagbadzedzewo me be mable miaƒe dzi siwo metrɔna o la be miaxɔ xɔxɔ; alo be le miaƒe fuléle kple aglãdzedze me la, woagblẽ mi ɖe gbegbe mlɔeba ƒe nɔnɔme me, si dze eye wòle klalo na ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ mlɔeba. Mise egɔme be, ɣeyiɣi aɖeke megali le nye amenuveve kple nublanuikpɔkpɔ ƒe ɣeyiɣiwo me na dzimetɔtrɔ ƒe agbemeŋkekewo katã o le nye xɔxɔ ƒe ɖoɖowo na amegbetɔƒomea katã siwo ava xɔxɔ blibo me le woƒe ɣeyiɣiwo kple ɖoɖowo nu na ame siwo axɔ nye lɔlɔ̃ na wo la ta. Eya ta, miaƒe ɣeyiɣiwo ye nye fifia eye wole yɔyɔ mlɔeba ɖom ɖa fifia. Sela.

Mise, O' Mise Dzɔdzɔenyenye ƒe Mavɔ Mawu Gã la, Yahweh, NYE LA Gã la ƒe gbe, eye mise nye nuƒoƒo gɔme, eye minya be nye gbe le abe tsi geɖe siwo le du dzi kple agbetsiwo ƒe ɖiɖi ene, eye mise egɔme be nye gbe ɖina abe dziɖegbe geɖewo ƒe ɖiɖi ene, (dziɖegbewo ƒe dziɖegbewo gɔ̃ hã) le nye dzɔdzɔenyenye ƒe dziku me ɖe anyigbadzinɔla siwo le edzi le tomazãmazã gã kple madzɔmadzɔnyenye kple nu vɔ̃ gã me la ŋuti.

Kpɔ ɖa! Nye ye nye Ame si me nye, NYE LA Gã la gɔ̃ hã, eye mewɔ dziƒowo kple anyigba kple eme blibonyenye katã, eye le nye dzɔdzɔenyenye ƒe ɖoɖo me la, mewɔ dziƒowo kple anyigba wode pɛpɛpɛ le nye blibonyenye ƒe mɔwo nu, mewɔ wo le ɖekawɔwɔ kple dzɔdzɔenyenye ƒe blibonyenye me afi si wòle be nye nuwɔwɔwo katã nanɔ ɖekawɔwɔ blibo kple lɔlɔ̃ kple subɔsubɔ me le lɔlɔ̃ me na wo nɔewo. Eye le woƒe lɔlɔ̃ na wo Fofo kple wɔla me la, woaɖo to nye gbe kple sewo na woƒe agbe be nyateƒe la woase wo ɖokuiwo gɔme abe ale si wowɔ wo ene eye woase nye mɔwo hã gɔme. Ke hã, wokpɔ vodada le Heylel me, ame si dze anyi tso ŋutikɔkɔe blibonyenye me, ame si wowɔ le atsyã gã kple wɔnuku me, gake wòdze anyigba dzi, wòdo vlo le nu vɔ̃ kple viviti me be wòanɔ da siwo woflo le Serafim ƒe tomazãmazã ta la ƒe nɔnɔme me. Sela.

Le nyateƒe mavɔ aɖe ta la, nye mɔwo kɔ wu amegbetɔ ƒe mɔwo, eye Nye ye nye ame si ƒo xlã nye fiazikpui la kple akpe ewo teƒe akpe ewo, mawudɔla gbogbo aɖewo siwo ƒo ame ta le nunya, ŋusẽ, kple ŋusẽ me, eye wolɔ̃a Yahweh eye wolɔ̃a Yahweh, NYE LA Gã la ƒe mɔwo sɔsrɔ̃ le toɖoɖo vevie kple nuteƒewɔwɔ ɣesiaɣi me: Sela.

Le nyateƒe me, le nyateƒe gã kple mavɔ me la, mewɔ wo wosẽ ŋu eye wole ŋutikɔkɔe me eye medo atsyã dzidzedze, kple dzɔdzɔenyenye mavɔ, kple bubu mavɔ na wo. Le nyateƒe me, le nyateƒe gã kple mavɔ me la, womehiã nu alo hiã nu gbeɖe o, le nu suetɔ kekeake gɔ̃ hã me o; eye wosubɔa nye kple nye nuwɔwɔwo bliboe le woƒe lɔlɔ̃ dziwo me na wo Fofo kple wɔla, Elohim gɔ̃ hã, le woƒe toɖoɖo Kristo Yesu me.

(Eya ame si, Yahweh, NYE LA Gã kple Mavɔ la tsɔ nuwo katã le dziƒo kple anyigba dzi de eƒe asi sesẽ kple mavɔ kple toɖoɖo me.)

Eya ta minya le nyateƒe mavɔ me be Kristo Yesu ye nye Aƒetɔwo ƒe Aƒetɔ kple Fiawo ƒe Fia eye eya ye ahe ŋutifafa ava dziƒowo kpakple anyigba dzi eye eya ye atrɔ Fiaɖuƒe si wotsɔ de te la ade nye asi ŋutikɔkɔetɔ me (le eƒe lɔlɔ̃ na eƒe Fofo kple Wɔla, Elohim, NYE LA Gã la gɔ̃ hã ta) le nuwo katã ƒe nuwuwu esime eƒe asi sesẽwo awɔ nu siwo katã woŋlɔ ɖi eye wogblɔ ɖi ku ɖe Dzɔdzɔenyenye ƒe Aƒetɔ Mavɔ la ŋu! Ne Amegbetɔvi ma awɔ nuwo katã vɔ le ŋusẽ gã me, kple lɔlɔ̃ gãtɔ me. Nenema ke le ɣeyiɣi ma me la, mawɔ nu siwo katã wona le nye mavɔ ɖoɖowo kple nya ɣaɣlawo me be wòawɔ la le nye ŋusẽ me to eƒe asi sesẽwo kple vɔsa me.

Eya ta, mena miese egɔme be, Nye ye nye ame si le ŋusẽ kple dziɖuɖu katã me ate ŋu ase egɔme, awɔe, anyae bliboe, eye wòaɖu nu sia nu si ku ɖe nye nuwɔwɔ ƒe akpa sia akpa ŋu dzi ale si wòlolo alo dze abe ɖe ŋusẽ le eŋu ene va se ɖe nu suetɔ kekeake dzi, eye naneke mate ŋu agbe ɣaɣla tso nye ŋku me o! Nye ye nye ame si ate ŋu awɔ nuwo katã enumake le nye ŋusẽ kple ŋusẽ mavɔ me. Ke hã, nye hã menye ame si wɔa nuwo katã le nye ŋutɔ nye lɔlɔ̃nu ƒe aɖaŋuɖoɖo nu eye mehiã aɖaŋuɖola aɖeke o, eya ta, nye ɖoɖowo kple nya ɣaɣlawo ava eme bliboe le woƒe emevava ƒe ɣeyiɣi siawo me, FIFIA ƒe ɣeyiɣiwo gɔ̃ hã.

Ke hã, le nye lɔlɔ̃ na nye nuwɔwɔ kple nye didi na viwo kple ƒome ta la, mewɔa nu gbagbewo kple ame tɔxɛwo (kple tiatia ƒe ablɔɖe) na nye mavɔ tameɖoɖo tɔxɛ siwo wona toɖolawo le subɔsubɔ kple bubu gã me, eye medoa ŋusẽ wo le mɔ ma nu. Eya ta, meɖo aɖaŋu na mi be miasrɔ̃ nye mɔwo le xɔxɔ me alo atia tsɔtsrɔ̃ ƒe dzo la tegbee. Gake nyae, O' amegbetɔ si dze anyi, tiatia aɖe le asiwò si atrɔ kpata fifia tegbee be tiatia aɖe nɔ asiwò, eye ame siwo katã atia vɔ̃ɖinyenye le nuwuwu ne nuwuwu la va la, womasi le woƒe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃wo nu tegbee kple tegbee o. Sela.

Ame kae nenye, O' amegbetɔ si dze anyi, wò ame si nede wò ɖokui nu kple ame siwo dze anyi la ƒe aglãdzedze si gɔmesese mele o, si nye bometsitsi kple tsukuku le madzɔmadzɔnyenye, ŋukeklẽ, nudzodzro, kple gbegblẽ katã me be nàkɔ wò ɖokui dzi le dada me ɖe Mawu Gã kple Mavɔ si wɔ wò na nye tameɖoɖowo kple dzidzɔkpɔkpɔ la ŋu?

Ame kae nenye, O' amegbetɔ si dze anyi be le wò manyamanya kple nudzodzro me la, nàdo dzi akɔ wò afokpodzi ɖe Dziƒowo ƒe Dziƒo ƒe Mawu gã kple mavɔ la ŋu (Eya ame si na agbe wò gɔ̃ hã) le Satanawo ƒe mɔwo nu? Eya ta, se nye tameɖoɖowo kple susu gɔme elabena mele Nya siawo ɖom ɖe WÒ abe nye yɔyɔ mlɔeba na mi dometɔ geɖe siwo susui le bometsitsi me be ɣeyiɣi le yewo si si mele yewo si o, eye mele nuxlɔ̃amenyawo gɔ̃ hã nam wò le nye lɔlɔ̃ mavɔ na wò me to ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ kple fukpekpe ƒe gbeƒãɖeɖe siwo wona xoxo siwo nèŋe aɖaba ƒu wo katã dzi siwo Kristo Yesu wɔ le Yahweh ƒe fiazikpui kple ŋusẽ me, eye Yahweh ŋutɔ hã gblɔe, NYE LA Gã la gɔ̃ hã dzi ɖoɖo na wò ake me!

(Mese egɔme be nye gbeƒãɖeɖewo mate ŋu atrɔ gbeɖe oa?)

Ke hã, nèŋea aɖaba ƒua dziɖegbe adreawo ƒe nuƒoƒo si meɖe fia, eye mena Yohanes Nyaɖeɖefiala tre enu le eƒe ɣeyiɣia me va se ɖe eɖeɖefia ƒe ɣeyiɣi nyuitɔ siwo nye fifia la dzi gɔ̃ hã. (Eye ayi edzi va se ɖe esime nye ɖasefo eveawo ƒe ɖaseɖiɖi awu enu le ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ mlɔeba me, tututu hafi xaxa gãtɔ kekeake nadze madzɔmadzɔwɔlawo dzi bliboe, Sela.) Dziɖegbe siwo Yeshua HaMashiach kple Yahweh, NYE LA Gã la, gblɔ tso nye gbeƒãɖeɖe kple ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ ƒe fiazikpui gã la dzi, eye fifia wotsɔe to nye dɔla gã si dzi meto ɖo Nya siawo siwo nye lɔlɔ̃ kple nuxlɔ̃ame sesẽ kple vɔ̃ɖi ŋutɔ la ɖa la ƒe asi me, ale be ame sia ame si ava la nava, hafi ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ nadze bliboe eye ɣeyiɣi aɖeke megali o!

Eya ta, ne èdi be yeasi le wò ŋutɔ wò ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ kple tsɔtsrɔ̃ mavɔ nu la, mede se na wò be nàwɔ Yesu wò Aƒetɔ bliboe kple hehematsɔmatsɔe. Elabena nye ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃wo le anyi dzem, eye woadze ame sia ame si woɖo wo ɖo la dzi, fɔbuʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ mawo ke siwo nye mlɔeba tegbee le woƒe nuwuwu me tegbee kple tegbee gɔ̃ hã. Eya ta, meɖo aɖaŋu na ame siwo katã meʋu Yesu Kristo la me be eyae nye Aƒetɔ tso dzi si yɔ fũu kple xɔse me o la be woawɔe fifia, le nyanya me be Nye ye nye ame si fɔe tso ame kukuwo dome le ŋusẽ gã ŋutɔ me be magaku azɔ o eye wòayi edzi le eƒe domenyinu me tegbee kple tegbee. Ke hã, ame akpa gãtɔ age ɖe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ gã kple mavɔ me pɛpɛpɛ abe ale si woŋlɔe ɖe blema Ŋɔŋlɔ Kɔkɔewo kple fifi Ŋɔŋlɔ Kɔkɔewo me ene.

Gake meva be mana nye nuxlɔ̃amewo kokoko, le esime wogblɔ miaƒe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ mlɔeba ƒe gbeƒãɖeɖewo vɔ eye wole edzi yim xoxo megbe gɔ̃ hã. Elabena nye mɔwo nye amenuveve kple nublanuikpɔkpɔ kple lɔlɔ̃ kple nyateƒe mavɔ ƒe mɔwo, medi be ame aɖeke natsrɔ̃ o ke boŋ be amewo katã nava xɔxɔ kple gbugbɔɖoanyi mavɔ me, togbɔ be ame akpa gãtɔ agbe nye nunana la eye woatia wo ŋutɔ woƒe fuwɔamewo kple ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ mavɔ boŋ hã. Togbɔ be, meva be maxlɔ̃ nu amewo hã, nuxlɔ̃ame aɖeke meli si woana si atrɔ nye ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃wo, alo woƒe ɣeyiɣiwo, alo woƒe emetsonuwo, alo nye mavɔ tameɖoɖowo be maxɔ nye ŋgɔgbetsetsewo, woaxɔ wo kpla gɔ̃ hã be woawɔ woƒe domenyinu mavɔ la dzi hafi fukpekpe nadze anyi o. Sela.

Eye meɖoa lɔlɔ̃ kple nuxlɔ̃ame ƒe gbedasi sia ɖa le nye lɔlɔ̃ na dzɔdzɔme amegbetɔ vɔ̃ɖi me, le lɔlɔ̃ ma ke si me meɖoa lɔlɔ̃ kple nuxlɔ̃ame ƒe gbedasiwo ɖe nye ŋutɔ vinye siwo xɔ Yesu Kristo la le toɖoɖo me la me; elabena menaa tsi dzana ɖe ame dzɔdzɔewo kple madzɔmadzɔwɔlawo katã dzi sɔsɔe. Le nye lɔlɔ̃ na wò ta la, meva be mana nye yɔyɔ mlɔeba hafi fukpekpe nadze tso nye ŋutɔ nye ŋusẽ, ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃, kple gbeƒãɖeɖe mavɔ ƒe fiazikpui si dzi nye dziɖegbe gbe gadi le dziku gã kple dzɔdzɔe me la dzi. Nenema gɔ̃ hã, elabena ÈGBE be yease nye gbe togbɔ be mexlɔ̃ nu wò hexlɔ̃ nu wò ƒe gbogbo aɖewo fifia hã! Se nye gbe eye nàsee nyuie elabena woaɖo wò tsɔtsrɔ̃ mavɔ ƒe ɣeyiɣiwo ɖa be woabla ame siwo katã lɔ̃a aʋatso hewɔnɛ, eye wokpɔa dzidzɔ ɖe madzɔmadzɔnyenye ŋu la le ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ me, eye ne ègbe nye gbe le amenuveve ƒe ɣeyiɣi siawo me la, àge ɖe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ mavɔ me elabena tiatia bubu aɖeke meli o.

Ke hã, le wò dzi sesẽ ƒe ŋkuagbãtɔ dada me la, ègbe ame ɖeka si medɔ ɖa na wò xɔxɔ blibo la, Yesu Nazaretitɔ la gɔ̃ hã, Yahweh ƒe Vi kple dɔla bɔbɔe ma gɔ̃ hã si mewɔ agbenagbɔgbɔe, si mewɔ wòbɔbɔ ɖe te wu Yahweh ŋutɔ vi aɖe ko le nye tsitretsitsi tso ame kukuwo dome kple Kristo Yesu ƒe ŋutikɔkɔe me! Elabena menye kɔkɔtɔ kple dziɖula ɖe nye nuwɔwɔwo katã dzi eye le nyateƒe mavɔ kple tɔtrɔmase me la, Nye ye nye ame si naa tsi dzana ɖe ame dzɔdzɔewo kple madzɔmadzɔwɔlawo katã dzi sɔsɔe le nye lɔlɔ̃ na nye nuwɔwɔ me, eye le nye mɔwo nu la, ameŋkumekpɔkpɔ aɖeke menɔa nye dzɔdzɔe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃wo me ɖe nye nuwɔwɔ dzi gbeɖe o.

Eya ta, mise egɔme be ameŋkumekpɔkpɔ nye ame siwo dze anyi la ƒe mɔ gbegblẽ geɖeawo dometɔ ɖeka, eya ta meva be maxlɔ̃ nu nu vɔ̃ wɔla vɔ̃ɖitɔ kekeake gɔ̃ hã le anyigbadzinɔlawo dome hafi woƒe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ naƒo wo bliboe, ale be woate ŋu asi le amenuveve ƒe sekɛnd ʋɛ siwo susɔ eye wole yiyim la me hafi fukpekpe nadze anyi. Eya ta meɖo aɖaŋu na ame siwo katã sea Nya siawo alo xlẽa wo la be woatrɔ ɖe Yesu Nazaretitɔ la ŋu enumake, mɔkpɔkpɔ aɖeke manɔmee elabena mɔnukpɔkpɔ bubu manɔ anyi na ameha gã aɖe o, elabena le woƒe nu vɔ̃ vɔ̃ɖiwo kple vlodoɖo gã si wotsɔ ɖe nu kɔkɔewo katã ŋu me la, woage ɖe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ mavɔ me, woakpɔ wo ta le fuwɔamewo me gɔ̃ hã afi si woalala fiazikpui ɣi gã ma ƒe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ le, afi si womatsɔ te o ke boŋ woabla wo atsɔ wo ade tohehe ƒe dzowo me afi si woatsɔ woƒe nu vɔ̃wo ade dzowo me na dzɔdzɔenyenye ƒe fetu blibo tegbee.

Ke hã, wò, O' amegbetɔ si dze anyi, ànɔ anyi le wò madzɔmadzɔ mɔwo nu, ame siwo dze anyi la gɔ̃ hã aɖu dziwò bliboe le wò manyamanya kple ŋkuagbãtɔ si woƒe aʋatsokakawo he vɛ me, aʋatsokaka siwo nèlɔ̃ gɔ̃ hã elabena èlɔ̃ wò aʋatsokakawo ƒe mɔwo. Eya ta, medɔ aʋatso gã kple sesẽ ɖe anyigba dzi be woabu fɔ mi ame siwo katã kpɔa dzidzɔ ɖe madzɔmadzɔnyenye ŋu eye wogbe Yesu Nazaretitɔ la, Yeshua HaMashiach gɔ̃ hã. Eye esi nèle lili lim le manyamanya me le wò ŋkuagbãtɔ kple dzi sesẽ mɔwo nu la, esi nèle anyi nɔm le viviti me le ɖekematsɔleme kple nu vɔ̃ gã me la, esi nèle tsatsam le dzodzro me hekpe dziku gã kple emetsonu mavɔ ɖo ɖe mia ɖokuiwo dzi le miaƒe tomazãmazã me la, makɔ nye gbe dzi kokoko, matsɔ Nya siawo aɖo ɖa gɔ̃ hã abe ɖaseɖiɖi ɖe ame siwo agbe la ŋu, le mɔkpɔkpɔ me gɔ̃ hã be miate ŋu atrɔ ɖe Kristo Yesu ŋu, ale be maɖe mi tso ame siwo dze anyi la ƒe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ me: Sela.

O' Dzɔdzɔme amegbetɔ, mi Adam vi siwo womexɔ o eye wometrɔ dzi me o la katã gɔ̃ hã, mi ame siwo katã mehea to na le anyigba dzi le nye lɔlɔ̃ na mi me gɔ̃ hã! Ke hã miexɔ aʋatso bometsitɔ kekeake siwo wona mi abe hehena ene dzi se le miaƒe aʋatsosuku si ame siwo dze anyi la na mi me. Ku ƒe hehena si wona le miaƒe ku ƒe dɔwɔƒe gbegblẽ kple gbegblẽwo me gɔ̃ hã afi si ŋutsu kple ŋutsu alo nyɔnu kple nyɔnu dɔwɔwɔ ƒe mɔwo ɖua dzi le fifia esi wodea dzesi mia ŋutɔ mia viwo hedzraa wo ɖo ale be woate ŋu aɖu ame maɖifɔwo le woƒe nudzodzro vɔ̃ɖiwo me. Ke hã, nu vɔ̃ ƒe dzidzɔkpɔkpɔwo lɔ̃ mi ŋutɔ, eye miaƒe ŋukeklẽ be miaƒo kesinɔnu geɖe nu ƒu la te mi ɖe anyi ale gbegbe be mielɔ̃na faa tsɔa mia ŋutɔ mia viwo saa vɔe le Baal ƒe mɔwo nu ɖe abolo gbegblẽ kakɛ aɖe ta. Eya ta, meɖe mɔ na ame siwo dze anyi la be woade aɖi miaƒe nuɖuɖu me, eye mena miaƒe agbɔsɔsɔ blibo wo gɔ̃ hã ale be drakɔn la, lã wɔadã si tso atsiaƒu me, kple lã wɔadã si tso anyigba dzi la nate ŋu awɔ nye tameɖoɖowo dzi, elabena nye ketɔwo gɔ̃ hã ɖoa to nye gbe.

Elabena miaƒe nu siwo mieyɔna be hehenawo la nye aʋatso kple gbegblẽ le ɖoɖo nu eye esia le esi miegbe sidzedze vavã kple Elohim mia wɔla ƒe mɔwo ta, eye mietia boŋ be miaƒe ahuhɔ̃e yibɔ siwo mieléa ɖe asi nuteƒewɔwɔtɔe ɣesiaɣi esi miele yiyim tso mɔ̃ dzi yi mɔ̃ dzi le manyamanya dovivitɔ kekeake me la nable mi. Ke hã dzidzɔkpɔkpɔ bɔbɔe kple ɣeyiɣi kpui aɖe tɔ xɔ aƒe ɖe mia dzi bliboe eye wògbã ŋku na mi esi wotrɔa miaƒe tamesusuwo le atike mɔ nu le ɖoɖo nu to mɔ̃ siwo mietsɔ blaa mia ɖokuiwo ɖe nu vɔ̃ me la dzi. Eye ale sidzedze aʋatso hea dada vɛ eye dada yia tsɔtsrɔ̃ gã ŋgɔ. Se egɔme, O' Amegbetɔ, be wò sidzedze mate ŋu aɖe wò tso nye ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃wo me o, eye ame siwo dze anyi la ƒe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ le klalo fifia be wòadze anyi. Ke hã, wò, O' Dzɔdzɔme amegbetɔ nènye bometsila bliboe ale gbegbe be mèse egɔme be ame siwo dze anyi la tsɔ woƒe aʋatsokakawo fia nu mi o, ale be woana amegbetɔ geɖe ale si woate ŋui nakpe ɖe wo ŋu le woƒe dzokpe si le dzo dzem, le ʋeʋẽm, eye wòle mlilim la ƒe domenyinu me gɔ̃ hã. Tohehe sesẽ aɖe si wowɔ na abosam kple eƒe dɔlawo, ke menye Adam o gɔ̃ hã. Sela.

Ke hã, to miaƒe lɔlɔ̃nu faa ƒe mɔɖeɖe me la, mietsɔa dada gã aɖe le miaƒe xexeme nuwɔwɔ dzodzrowo me afi si mieƒoa miaƒe aʋatso pepa dzodzrowo kple miaƒe aʋatso xexlẽdzesi dzodzrowo nu ƒu le ŋukeklẽ me le kɔmpiuta dzi akɔnta dzodzrowo me siwo woaxɔ le mia si kpata le ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ me fifia, nenema gɔ̃ hã elabena esiawoe nye Esau si dzra eƒe ŋgɔgbezinyenye ɖe abolo kakɛ aɖe ta la ƒe mɔwo. Ke hã, le miaƒe lɔlɔ̃nu faa ƒe mɔɖeɖe me la, mieɖea mɔ na ame siwo dze anyi la be woaxɔ ga le mia si to ŋusẽzãzã ƒe ŋɔdzidoname me atsɔ axe fe ɖe mia ŋutɔ mia viwo fiafia le ku ƒe mɔwo nu ta. Nenema gɔ̃ hã ale be ame siwo katã wofia nu le manyamanya kple ŋkuagbãtɔ sia me la nasubɔ ame siwo dze anyi la faa esime wosusu be yewowɔa dɔ nyui gã aɖe na Mawu ŋutɔ le woƒe nu vɔ̃wo me, abe ɖe wòle be Yahweh naɖe mɔ tso eya ŋutɔ ƒe nuwɔwɔ kple dziɖuɖu ŋu le woƒe dada dzodzro kple nu vɔ̃ ta ene. Nya si mate ŋu adzɔ gbeɖe o gɔ̃ hã, elabena Nye ye nye.

Eye le miaƒe dzi sesẽ ƒe madzɔmadzɔ mɔwo me la, miebu miaƒe towo gɔ̃ hã eye mietsi tokunɔ, aɖetututɔ, kple ŋkuagbãtɔ le miaƒe madzɔmadzɔ mɔ siwo ɖe mi kpɔtɔ le kluvinyenye me abe nu vɔ̃ ƒe kluvi ene be miafa akɔ na ame siwo dze anyi la ƒe nudzodzrowo, ame siwo miesubɔna le miaƒe xɔsemakpɔmakpɔ mɔwo me la nu. Ke hã, dɔla la menɔa aƒea me tegbee o ne etia le toɖoɖo me be yeage ɖe ʋɔtru ɖeka si le dziƒowo si maʋu na amegbetɔƒomea gbeɖe la nu: Yesu Kristo si hã nye, Yesu Mesia la, ame si ƒe ŋkɔ hã nye Yeshua HaMashiach, ame si nye xɔxɔ ƒe ʋɔtru gã kple ɖeka si ʋu si nye asi maʋu gbeɖe na nuteƒewɔla dzɔdzɔewo le Kristo Yesu me. Sela!

Nye ye nye, eye metsɔ lɔlɔ̃ ƒe Nya siawo ɖo ɖe ame siwo wobu fɔe la to nye dɔla kple subɔla dzi, eye to nye dɔla kple subɔla dzi la meɖo Nya siawo ɖe ame siwo woadrɔ̃ ʋɔnui kpuie.

Mise egɔme mi ame siwo katã sea Nya siawo, be esia menye ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ ƒe gbeƒãɖeɖe mlɔeba, si hena nuwuwu vɛ na dzɔdzɔme amegbetɔ o. Ke boŋ, gbedasi sia le abe nuxlɔ̃ame ene na tomazãlawo kple madzɔmadzɔwɔlawo le Ɣaɣla Babilon kple ame siwo katã woɖo Nya siawo ɖo gɔ̃ hã, (eye ame aɖewo ava xɔxɔ me le sekɛnd mlɔeba siawo me) gake abe nuxlɔ̃ame ƒe ɖaseɖiɖi mlɔeba si wona hafi ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ nadze Ɣaɣla Babilon dzi bliboe hã.

Mise egɔme, be nye Nyawo akpɔ ame siwo woɖo wo ɖo la ƒe ŋkuwo, towo, kple dziwo, eye agbagbadzedze aɖeke tso ame siwo dze anyi la, woƒe ŋunyɔnuwo, alo woƒe mɔ̃ɖaŋununya dzodzro kple manyamanyawo mate ŋu axe mɔ na nu si NYE LA Gã la ɖo be yeawɔ la o.

Eye ale mede dzesi nye ŋutɔ nye lɔlɔ̃ lɛta na ame siwo wodrɔ̃ ʋɔnui la le mɔkpɔkpɔ me be to toɖoɖo nye Nya siwo woɖo ɖa le nye lɔlɔ̃ na wo me la, woasi le woƒe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃wo nu hafi asi mlɔeba ma naƒo sekɛnd mlɔeba ma.

Eye ale mede dzesi nye lɔlɔ̃ lɛta le Lɔlɔ̃ gã kple Ŋusẽ Mavɔ me!

Nye ye nye Ame si me nye, eye Nye ye nye Yahweh, NYE LA Gã kple Mavɔ la.
