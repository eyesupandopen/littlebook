Ɣeyiɣi La Ðo Azɔ

Ɣeyiɣi la ɖo, vinyeawo, ɣeyiɣi la ɖo. Ɣeyiɣi la ɖo be miava aƒeme, ne kpẽ la ɖi eye woƒo nye hamea nu ƒu, afisi Yahweh le mɔ kpɔm kple dzidzɔ le eƒe dzi me ɖe miaƒe vava kpata la ŋu. Ɣeyiɣi la ɖo be miava aƒeme, ne kpẽ la ƒe ɖiɖi yɔ Dziƒoʋĩtɔ la ƒe viwo be woayi afisi mawudɔlawo le eye wotsana le. Ɣeyiɣi la ɖo vinyeawo be maƒo mi nu ƒu tso anyigba ƒe dzogoe ene awo dzi, amesiwo katã dɔ alɔ̃ le Kristo me la afɔ gbã eye mi amesiwo le agbe eye mitsia anyi la, woakɔ mi kple wo adzoe le lilikpowo me. Elabena nye Kristo Yesu ye nye miaƒe Aƒetɔ kple Ðela gbagbe la, eye mele mi yɔm be miava miaƒe aƒe me le dziƒowo afisi miaƒe fetuwo le mɔ kpɔm le, afisi madzra teƒe ɖo di na mi le Fofonye ƒe aƒe me, elabena xɔ geɖewo le Fofonye ƒe aƒe me. Mele mi yɔm be miava aƒeme ava tɔ ɖe miaƒe Aƒetɔ la ŋkume, be amesiame naxɔ nusiwo wowɔ le ŋutilã me la, eɖanye nyui alo vɔ̃ o, be amesiame naxɔ eƒe fetu tso Aƒetɔ la gbɔ, eye be amesiame naxɔ eƒe nɔƒe le dziƒowo ƒe ɖoɖo siwo wodzra ɖo ɖi na mi dometɔ ɖesiade la me.

Kpɔ ɖa! Nyee nye Yeshua HaMashiach, eye nyee nye ŋugbetɔsrɔ̃ si le aƒe tum be maƒo nye ŋugbetɔ nu ƒu elabena miaƒe srɔ̃ɖeɣi la ɖo, afisi mado nubabla mavɔ me kple nye tiatiawo, amesiwo dzra wo ɖokuiwo ɖo, eye mekɔ wo ŋu le nye avuléʋu la me elabena wova gbɔnye bliboe eye wotsɔ woƒe dzi blibo nam le lɔlɔ̃ kple ɖokuitsɔtsɔna me. Togbɔ be womazɔ bliboe o, eye ame geɖewo ɖu nuvɔ̃ gãwo dzi hã la, wokɔ wo ɖokuiwo ŋu bliboe le alẽvi la ƒe ʋu me, eye wole tsitre ɖe ŋkunye me azɔ wokɔ wo ŋu bliboe tso woƒe nuvɔ̃wo me eye wole dzadzɛ abe ɖetugbui siwo mewɔ gbolo o eye wodzra wo ɖokuiwo ɖo tso vɔ̃ɖitɔ la si ene. Kpɔ ɖa! Nye dziɖula siwo metsɔ nuvɔ̃ katã ke, eye mekɔ ami ɖe woƒe akaɖiwo me elabena wova gbɔnye le woƒe hiãɣiwo eye mefia wo alesi woado woƒe akaɖikawoe eye meda gbe le wo ŋu eye meɖe wo ɖe vovo na ɖokuinye, amesiwo katã Yahweh tia tso xexeame ƒe gɔmeɖoanyiwo me be woanɔ tsitre abe nye ŋugbetɔ ene eye woanɔ gbɔnye tegbee, eye woaɖu fia kplim abe Fia kple Nunɔla mavɔwo ene elabena nenema woyɔ wo, wokɔ wo ŋu eye wotia wo le nye, Kristo Yesu woƒe Aƒetɔ si fɔ tso ku me la me.

Eye woatrɔ nye ame 144,000 la, ade dzesi wo eye woagawɔ wo yeyee bliboe ɖe taɖodzinu siwo ta woyɔ wo eye wodzra wo ɖo be woawɔ la ŋu. Nyateƒe, nyateƒe, luʋɔ ɖesiade si xɔ nye ŋkɔ gã la dzi se eye wòwɔm woƒe Aƒetɔ mavɔ la, woatrɔe bliboe eye woagawɔe yeyee abe nuwɔwɔ yeye ene, eye woawɔ wo abe alesi mele ene. Eye womagawɔ nuvɔ̃ gbeɖe o, to dada alo mawumavɔ̃mavɔ̃ me o eye womagaƒo fi wo gbeɖe o, eye dɔléle alo vevesese, alo ku alo nuxaxa magaka wo gbeɖe o elabena nu gbãtɔwo nu va yi eye kekeli vavã la le klẽm azɔ. Eye nyee, Kristo Yesu, ye aʋã azɔ be maɖe ɖokuinye afia Yakob eye amesiame si ayɔ nye ŋkɔ gã la vaseɖe nuwuwu la akpɔ ɖeɖe, anyigbadzinɔla siwo katã woɖo ɖe agbe mavɔ me la, eye ameha gã aɖe anɔ anyi siwo axɔm abe woƒe Mesia ene eye maɖe wo bliboe.

Mele vavam na mi, nye lɔlɔ̃tɔwo, eye ɣeyiɣiawo ɖo azɔ. Melala amenuveve ƒe ɣeyiɣi blibo aɖe eye meɖe ame geɖewo, eye woafɔ ame geɖewo ɖe agbe yeye me le nye, Kristo Yesu woƒe Aƒetɔ mavɔ la me, eye amesiwo ku le Kristo me la afɔ gbã, eye kpẽ mamlɛtɔ la le dzadzraɖo me azɔ. Dzesiwo, nukunuwo kple nukunu geɖewo xɔ anyigba la dzi le woƒe atsyã me eye dzesiawo nu metsina o le xexeame katã azɔ siwo le gbeƒã ɖem nye dzadza, siwo le gbeƒã ɖem Alẽvi la ƒe srɔ̃ɖeɖe kple eƒe ŋugbetɔ nuteƒewɔla la, eye wodzra kplɔ̃ɖoƒewo ɖo ɖi le dziƒowo eye wole dzadzraɖo me.

Eye nenema wòle, dziƒo ƒe azãɖuɖu le mɔ kpɔm na nye nuteƒewɔlawo, amesiwo woyɔ, eye wotia, eye azɔ ye nye ɣeyiɣi be woadzudzɔ tegbee tso woƒe agbagbadzedzewo me siwo wodze le anyigba dzi le woƒe tsu aƒewo me, eye wodze agbagba le ŋutilã me abe nye hadɔwɔlawo ene eye woƒo ame geɖewo nu ƒu hekplɔ wo va gbɔnye, Aƒetɔ Yesu Kristo be woaxɔ xɔxɔ mavɔ tegbee. Elabena togbɔ be Satana gblẽ woƒe ŋutilã le nuvɔ̃ me hã la, mena taɖodzinu aɖe wo eye meyɔ wo be woagblẽ dzodzro si me wobu fɔ wo ɖo to nuvɔ̃ me la ɖi tegbee.

Eye nenema kesinɔnu sia le wo si le anyize gbawo me be Mawu ƒe ŋusẽ la le agbe eye wònɔa wo me, eye wowɔ dɔ gã geɖewo le nye ŋkɔ me, eye nyee nye amesi na ŋusẽ wo be woazu xɔxɔ ƒe domenyilawo kple agbe mavɔ ƒe domenyilawo eye mexɔ wo eye mena mavɔ ƒe mɔxexedzi ma le dzidzi yeye la me eye mede dzesi wo katã kple ŋugbedodo ƒe gbɔgbɔ kɔkɔe ma, ŋugbedodo ƒe dzesi ma si womate ŋu agbã o. Eye azɔ ɣeyiɣi la ɖo be woaxɔ nunɔamesi si woƒle la bliboe, amesiwo katã wɔm Aƒetɔ tso dzi si yɔ fũ kple xɔse me kple nyanya be Yahweh fɔm tso ku me be nyemagaku o. Azɔ ye nye woƒe xɔxɔ blibo ƒe ɣeyiɣiwo afisi woaxɔ woƒe mɔxexedzi si woxɔ tso gbɔnye le Kristo ƒe dzidzi yeye me le mia me ŋutikɔkɔe ƒe mɔkpɔkpɔ la bliboe!

Kpɔ ɖa! Mise nye gbe eye miase nye nyawo gɔme eye mianya be miaƒe etsɔme ŋutikɔkɔetɔ la menye ƒe geɖewo enye esi gbɔna o, alo womedo ɖoɖo ɖe eŋu be wòava le Yubili siwo gbɔna me o, ke boŋ ele mia dzi nye lɔlɔ̃tɔwo eye kpata le mavɔ ƒe aɖabaƒoƒo aɖe me la woatrɔ mi katã eye tɔtrɔ ƒe ɣeyiɣiwo ɖo azɔ. Miakpɔ mia ɖokuiwo kpata be mietrɔ zu ŋutikɔkɔe mavɔ, eye mado awu fafã siwo womate ŋu aƒo ɖi o la na mi. Kpata miakpɔ mia ɖokuiwo mitsitre ɖe Yahweh ƒe fiazikpui xɔ me, le Kekeli Fofo la ƒe atsyã kple ŋutikɔkɔe mavɔ me, eye miakpɔ gome le eƒe nɔnɔme mavɔ me tegbee elabena Mawu gbagbe la ƒe viwo mienye, amesiwo katã woɖe tso Adam me eye woxɔ wo tso anyigba dzĩ la me.

Eye nye avuléʋu si wokɔ ɖe anyigba dzi le Golgota ye kpɔ miaƒe xɔxɔ ta zi ɖeka, eye tegbee esi metsɔ nye agbe na kple nyawo be "Wovu enu!" Eye nye Kristo Yesu, nyee nye Aƒetɔwo ƒe Aƒetɔ kple Fiawo ƒe Fia si fɔ tso ku me eye wòdzo yi dzi tegbee elabena Yahweh fɔm tso ku me be nyemagaku o! Nyee nye Adam mamlɛtɔ, eye nyee nye amegbetɔvi si xɔ amegbetɔvi siwo yɔ nye ŋkɔ la eye wòwɔ wo Mawu vi mavɔ siwo anɔ agbe tegbee le ŋutikɔkɔe mavɔ kple atsyã mavɔ me eye tegbee Yahweh akpɔ ŋutikɔkɔe le wo me, eye woawo hã le Yahweh me elabena le nye, Yeshua HaMashiach me la, blibodede katã le tegbee, eye Yahweh anye nu katã le nu katã me eye tegbee míazɔ abe nuwɔwɔ yeye ɖeka kple ame yeye ɖeka ene. Eye mi, nye amesiwo woyɔ, wotia eye miewɔ nuteƒe la, woxɔ mi tegbee, eye miele miaƒe agbe vavãwo gɔme se ge elabena miaƒe agbewo li tegbee. Eya ta mise egɔme be mieku, eye miaƒe agbewo ɣla ɖe kplim, Kristo Yesu, le Yahweh me. Ne nye, Kristo Yesu, amesi nye miaƒe agbe la, dze la, ekema miawo hã miadze kplim le ŋutikɔkɔe me. Mienya be miaƒe ame vavãwo ɣla ɖe nye, Kristo Yesu me oa?

Eya ta nukata miedze anyi nye tiatiawo, nye nuteƒewɔlawo, nye Hame kple nye Ŋugbetɔ gɔ̃ hã? Mele vavam na mi eye kpɔ ɖa, mele aƒe tum na mi. Eye maƒo mi nu ƒu kpata na ɖokuinye. Eye esi anyigba la le tɔtrɔm ɖe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ kple aʋawɔwɔ me la, dziƒowo akpɔ dzidzɔ ɖe miaƒe anyinɔnɔ ŋu eye azãɖuɖu gã aɖe anɔ anyi ne Mawu viwo kpe kple woƒe Aƒetɔwo ƒe Aƒetɔ kple Fiawo ƒe Fia tegbee le ŋutikɔkɔe mavɔ me eye amesiame akpɔ agbe yeye. Eye mama maganɔ amesiwo woxɔ to Alẽvi la ƒe ʋu me kple wo Fofo kple woƒe Mawu dome gbeɖe o.

Elabena azɔ ye nye ɣeyiɣi si Yahweh tia be yeadze eɖokui ɖeɖe fia bliboe gɔme na eƒe nuwɔwɔwo eye eɖokui ɖeɖe fia bliboe dzea egɔme tso Mawu ƒe aƒe la me, eye wòle eɖokui ɖeɖe fia ge bliboe na eƒe tiatiawo kple nuteƒewɔlawo, gbãtɔwo gɔ̃ hã, elabena amesiwo meƒo nu ƒu na ɖokuinye le kpẽ mamlɛtɔ ƒe ɖiɖi me la ye nye gbãtɔwo tso ame kukuwo dome. Eye azɔ ɣeyiɣi la ɖo be woanya bliboe, abe alesi nye kple Fofonye mienya mi katã bliboe ene. Eye nenema meɖe gbeƒã nye dzadza eye meɖo aɖaŋu na mi nye nuteƒewɔlawo be miayi edzi adzra mia ɖokuiwo ɖo ɖe nye dzadza ŋu, elabena azɔ ye nye ɣeyiɣiawo esi ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ dzea egɔme le Mawu ƒe aƒe la me. Eye kpata, adzɔ.

Kpɔ ɖa! Migavɔ̃ ahom siwo gbɔna siwo mia dometɔ geɖe kpɔna be wole nu ƒom ƒu o: aʋawɔwɔ ƒe ahomwo, afɔku siwo le aƒe tum ƒe ahomwo, dzɔdzɔmefɔku siwo le dzɔdzɔ ge godoo ƒe ahomwo. Mise egɔme be naneke mawɔ nuvevi mi le mɔ aɖeke nu o, togbɔ be ame geɖewo le tetekpɔwo kple gbɔgbɔmeʋa siwo woɖu dzi bliboe le nye, Kristo Yesu me hã. Kpɔ ɖa! Nye dzadza ƒe ɣeyiɣi na nye nuteƒewɔlawo ɖo eye esi vidzikuwo metsina o la, kpata woaƒo wo nu ƒu eye woahé wo tso ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ si menye wo tɔ o la me.

Mele mi yɔm be miava miaƒe aƒe me le dziƒowo afisi miaƒe fetuwo le mɔ kpɔm le.

Mele mi yɔm be miava aƒeme ava tɔ ɖe miaƒe Aƒetɔ la ŋkume, be miaxɔ nusiwo wowɔ le ŋutilã me la.

Nyee nye Yeshua HaMashiach.

Nyee nye ŋugbetɔsrɔ̃ si le aƒe tum be maƒo nye ŋugbetɔ nu ƒu elabena srɔ̃ɖegbe la ɖo vɔ.

Mele vavam na mi, nye lɔlɔ̃tɔwo, eye ɣeyiɣi la ɖo azɔ.

Nyee nye amesi na ŋusẽ mi be miazu xɔxɔ ƒe domenyilawo kple agbe mavɔ ƒe domenyilawo.

Mele vavam na mi be mahé mi ayi dziƒowo ƒe dedienɔnɔ me eye ɣeyiɣiawo ɖo azɔ.

Nye Yeshua HaMashiach medɔ nya siawo to nye subɔla dzi, eye to nye subɔla dzi wodɔ nya siawo.

Minɔ dzadzraɖo me eye migagblẽ miaƒe nɔƒewo ɖi o eye miyi edzi anɔ mɔ kpɔm nam. Miganɔ xɔsemanɔmee o, ke boŋ minɔ ŋudzɔ eye miase egɔme be nye dzadza ƒe dzesiwo le xexeame katã, eye kpata miatɔ ɖe NYEE NYE gã la ƒe ŋkume mavɔ la me!
