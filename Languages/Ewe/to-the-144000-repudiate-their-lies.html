Ðasefo Eve: Hame La
Le Yohanes Nyaɖeɖefia La Ƒe Dɔwɔwɔwo Me
June 2, 2024

Na 144,000 La: Migbe Woƒe Aʋatsokakawo

Mido to ɖe nye gbe ŋu eye miese nye nunya, elabena nunya le ɣli dom le mɔwo dzi: miese nye gbea? Nyee nye Yeshua HaMashiach, Yahweh ƒe vi, ame si va le ŋutilã me eye wònye Mawu ƒe alẽvi si tsɔa xexeame ƒe nu vɔ̃ dzoa. Miese egɔme be xexe si me miele la menye nɔnɔmetata aʋatso si miaƒe Jesuit nufialawo ta be yewoaflu mi la o. Wofia Jesuit ƒe aʋatsowo mi dometɔ geɖe, mlesrɔ̃ nu tsitretsi ɖe nusɔsrɔ̃ ŋu gɔ̃ hã. Wotrɔ aʋa sia si wowɔ ɖe nyateƒe ŋu be wòatrɔ amegbetɔwo ƒe tamesusuwo be woabɔbɔ ɖe Satana te eye wòdze agbagba be yeaɖe amegbetɔ ƒe tiatiawɔwɔ ƒe ablɔɖe ɖa eye yeaxɔe be amegbetɔ nadze eƒe nufiafiawo yome eŋumabumabui.

Womate ŋu ase ŋutinya vavãtɔ gɔme tso Jesuit xexemenunyalawo ƒe aʋatsowo me o, elabena aʋatsokalawo wonye siwo le aʋatso dzram le aʋatso gã me. Ŋɔŋlɔa me kɔ le nya la ŋu, eye Romatɔwo gblɔ nyateƒe la kɔtɛ be: Elabena Mawu ƒe dziku dze tso dziƒo ɖe amewo ƒe mawumavɔ̃mavɔ̃ kple madzɔmadzɔnyenye katã dzi, ame siwo xe mɔ na nyateƒe la le madzɔmadzɔnyenye me.

Miena nya mawo ɖo miaƒe dzi mea? Amewo tsɔa mawumavɔ̃mavɔ̃ kple madzɔmadzɔnyenye xea mɔ na nyateƒe la koŋ, eye Yahweh ƒe dziku le ame siwo wɔa esia la ŋu. Eya ta woƒe aʋatsowo wɔ miaƒe hehenanawo wozu aʋatso.

Wowɔ esia be woadzra mɔ ɖo na aʋatso gã si gbɔna, eye be woaɖe asi le Satana ƒe fiaɖuƒe ŋu be wòaɖu fia le aʋatso kple aʋatsokaka gã me. Aʋatso siwo wogblɔ na mi hena hehenana la wɔa dɔ be wòagba ŋku na amehawo le nyateƒe la ŋu: Amewo metso xexe bubu me o, mawudɔla siwo dze anyi siwo yina dzo ta la me be woabla wo ahame wo tegbee ko wonye. Migaxɔ woƒe aʋatsowo dzi se o eye miƒu du va gbɔnye, Kristo Yesu, elabena nyateƒe blibo si ana miavo la le menye. Megblɔ nya siawo na mi be woagaflu mi kple ame siwo akpe ɖe Satana ŋu axɔ lã wɔadã ƒe dzesi si gbɔna la o.

Wosea ŋutinya gɔme to nyateƒe siwo woɖo anyi le Ŋɔŋlɔawo kple agbalẽ siwo menye Biblia me tɔ o, 1 Henok, Yasher, Yubilewo, kple bubuawo me, tso gɔmedzedzea me dzi wɔwɔ me. Elabena ame mawumavɔ̃la siawo kple aʋatso siwo wozãna tsɔ tsyɔa nu nyateƒe la dzi la nye aʋa gã la ƒe akpa aɖe ko, dzidzimeviwo ƒe aʋa si le Yahweh kple Satana dome si le edzi yim va se ɖe aɖabaƒoƒo sia me gɔ̃ hã, gake nye avulédɔwo ɖu edzi hena tegbee. Elabena Yahweh ho aʋa ɖe da xoxoa ŋu ɖe eƒe aglãdzedze ta, tso amegbetɔ ƒe nuwɔwɔ ƒe gɔmedzedzea me ke esime Satana dze agbagba be yeatsrɔ̃ Adam kple Xawa. Yahweh gblɔ na da la le eƒe gbeƒãɖeɖea me be: Matsɔ cẽ maɖo wò kple nyɔnu la dome, kple wò dzidzimevi kple eƒe dzidzimevi dome: agba ta na wò, eye nàɖu afɔkpodzi nɛ. Eye ale dzidzimeviwo ƒe aʋawo yi edzi tso ɣemaɣi.

Le Eden Bɔa me la, Satana te ŋu gblẽ Adam ƒe dzidzimeviwo, eye wòbu fɔ Adam ɖe nu vɔ̃ ƒe ŋusẽ kple gbegblẽ te. (Le nu sia me Mawu vi la dze, be wòagblẽ abosam ƒe dɔwɔwɔwo.) Elabena Satana zã eƒe nyui kple vɔ̃ sɔsrɔ̃ gbegblẽ la tsɔ gblẽ amegbetɔwo keŋkeŋ, eye na ame siwo ɖoa to nye gbe la, wogblẽ gbegblẽ ma le menye, Kristo Yesu, ame si me mienye nuwɔwɔ yeye si womate ŋu agblẽ gbeɖe o. Womate ŋu agblẽ nye dzidzimeviwo o, elabena Yahweh wɔe be womate ŋu agblẽe o, eye to dzidzi yeyea me la, metsɔ ŋugbedodo ƒe gbɔgbɔ kɔkɔe ma gɔ̃ hã tre dzesi nye tiatia kple nuteƒewɔlawo!

Yohanes gblɔ nyateƒe mavɔ la le eƒe lɛta gbãtɔ me be: Ame sia ame si wodzi tso Mawu me la mewɔa nu vɔ̃ o: elabena eƒe dzidzimevi le eme, eye mate ŋu awɔ nu vɔ̃ o, elabena wodzi i tso Mawu me.

Le ɣeyiɣi kpui aɖe megbe le Eden ƒe anyidzedze, afi si Adam wɔ nu vɔ̃ le hege tso blibonyenye me yi gbegblẽ me la, dzɔlawo ɖi va Hermon Toa dzi le Yared ƒe ŋkekeawo me. Eye wo katã wobla wo nɔewo kple fiƒode siwo wo nɔewo wɔ, woƒe atamkaka makɔmakɔ si dzi woto bla wo ɖokuiwo tegbee ɖe nu vɔ̃ kple tohehe mavɔ ŋu gɔ̃ hã. Wowɔ esia be wo katã woaɖe srɔ̃ le ame siwo katã wotia la dome, eye to esia me wotrɔ woƒe dzɔdzɔme ƒe ɖoɖoa.

Wova amegbetɔwo gbɔ hetsɔ woƒe mawudɔla dzeanyiwo ƒe sidzedze vɔ̃ɖi la na, elabena wokpe ɖe Satana ƒe aglãdzedzea ŋu.

Wofia amewo be woawɔ yiwo, kple hɛwo, kple akpoxɔnuwo, kple akɔtakpoxɔnuwo, eye wona wonya anyigba dzi gawo kple wo wɔwɔ ƒe aɖaŋu, kple alɔnugegawo, kple atsyãɖonuwo, kple atike siwo wotsɔ wɔa aɖaŋudɔwoe, kple aɖabawo dodo atsyã, kple kpe xɔasi ƒomevi katã, kple amadede ƒe atike katã. Wofia dzosasawo, kple kewo kpakpa, dzosasawo gbãgbã, ɣletiviwo ŋuti nunya, ɣletiviwo ƒe ƒuƒoƒowo, alilikpowo ŋuti nunya, anyigba ƒe dzesiwo, ɣe ƒe dzesiwo, kple dzinu ƒe mɔzɔzɔ.

Eya ta dzɔla dzeanyiwo ɖe wo srɔ̃wo eye wowɔ ahasi kpli wo, eye wofɔ fu hedzi dzotso aʋadzi gãwo na wo. Eye le ɣeyiɣi aɖe megbe la, dzidzimevi makɔmakɔ siawo mi nu siwo katã le anyigba dzi, kple nu siwo katã wona amegbetɔwo, eye mlɔeba womi amegbetɔwo geɖe ale si woate ŋui. Elabena ʋunolawo kple amedulawo wonye, eye wowɔ vɔ̃ gã aɖe le Yahweh ŋkume, elabena ŋutilã ƒe agbe le ʋu la me eye wowɔ amegbetɔ ɖe Yahweh ƒe nɔnɔme nu. Wotsi hedzi ɖe edzi eye mlɔeba woɖo woƒe fiaɖuƒewo anyi ɖe anyigba bliboa dzi. Kpɔ ɖa! Woƒe xɔ gãwo ƒe aƒedowo gakpɔtɔ le anyigba dzi abe ɖasefo ɖe wo ŋu ene. Woɖu anyigba dzi le ɣeyiɣi mawo me, eye wowɔ wo hena amegbetɔwo tsɔtsrɔ̃. Eye ale wowɔ ŋunyɔnu ƒomevi katã, elabena ŋɔŋlɔ kɔkɔeawo gblɔ ale si wowɔe gɔ̃ hã.

Abe ɖe mesɔ gbɔ be amegbetɔwo nadzro ŋutilã tutɔwo me, awɔ ahasi kple mawudɔla dzeanyiwo, eye woadzi vi gãwo ene o, le dzɔlawo ta la, wo katã wowɔ nu vɔ̃ ɖe xeviwo, kple lãwo, kple lã siwo tana, kple tɔmelãwo ŋu. Tso nuwɔwɔ ƒe gɔmedzedzea me la, Yahweh ɖo agbe ƒomevi ɖe sia ɖe ɖe eƒomevi nu. Gake dzɔla vɔ̃ɖiwo gblẽ nuwɔwɔ la me, wotsɔ lã ƒomevi ɖeka kpe kple lã ƒomevi aɖe si sɔ kplii gɔ̃ hã be woade dzɔdzɔmeŋusẽ ƒe liɣiliɣi eme eye woatrɔ Yahweh ƒe nuwɔwɔ gbãtɔ si nye blibonyenye si wɔ ɖeka la. Wogbugbɔ wɔ esia, wotsɔ lã siwo sɔ kple wo nɔewo ƒomevi ɖeka kpe kple lã siwo sɔ kple wo nɔewo ƒomevi bubu va se ɖe esime wokpɔ nu si wodi la. Eye ale xexe si nɔ anyi ɣemaɣi la, gblẽ kple ŋunyɔnu vɔ̃ɖi ƒomevi katã siwo wɔ nu vɔ̃ gã aɖe ɖe Yahweh, amegbetɔwo, kple nuwɔwɔ katã ŋu. Miese egɔme be, ame dzeanyiwo di xexe si nye woawo ŋutɔ tɔ, xexe si me viviti le si to vovo na Yahweh keŋkeŋ gɔ̃ hã, eye woanyi ema tututu dome le dzo ta si Yahweh ŋutɔ dzra ɖo ɖi na wo la me.

Eye ale Noa ƒe ŋkekeawo va eye xexe si nɔ anyi ɣemaɣi, si tso tsi me hetsi tsi me la, tsi ɖɔ ɖe edzi eye wòtsrɔ̃. Elabena gogodo gã la ƒe dzɔtsoƒewo gbã, si he ŋutasesẽ gã aɖe vɛ esime tsi sesẽwo ƒe ŋusẽ gã la gbã anyigba la, eye dziƒo ƒe fesrewo ʋu, si na tsi siwo tso yamenutome la dzi (kple anyigba te) tsyɔ xexe bliboa dzi hetsrɔ̃e.

Eye ale agbalẽŋlɔla Henok ƒo nu tso dzotso aʋadzi siawo ƒe dzɔdzɔme ŋu ale, elabena Yahweh gblɔe ale: Eye azɔ, dzotso aʋadziwo, siwo wodzi tso gbɔgbɔwo kple ŋutilã me la, woayɔ wo be gbɔgbɔ vɔ̃wo le anyigba dzi, eye woƒe nɔƒe anye anyigba dzi. Gbɔgbɔ vɔ̃wo do tso woƒe ŋutilãwo me; elabena wodzi wo tso amewo me, dzɔla kɔkɔeawo gbɔe woƒe gɔmedzedze kple gɔmedzedze gbãtɔ tso; woanye gbɔgbɔ vɔ̃wo le anyigba dzi, eye woayɔ wo be gbɔgbɔ vɔ̃wo. Ke dziƒo gbɔgbɔwo ya, dziƒoe woƒe nɔƒe anye, gake anyigba dzi gbɔgbɔ siwo wodzi ɖe anyigba dzi ya, anyigba dzie woƒe nɔƒe anye. Eye dzotso aʋadziwo ƒe gbɔgbɔwo wɔa fu, wɔa ŋutasesẽ, tsrɔ̃a nu, dzea ame dzi, wɔa aʋa, eye wowɔa tsɔtsrɔ̃ le anyigba dzi, eye wohea kuxiwo vɛ: womeɖua nu o, gake dɔ kple tsikɔ wua wo, eye wohea nuvɔ̃wo vɛ. Eye gbɔgbɔ siawo atso ɖe amegbetɔviwo kple nyɔnuwo ŋu, elabena wotso wo me. Miese egɔme be gbɔgbɔ vɔ̃ siawo ƒe akpa asieke le ewo me le aʋakɔ gã la me le Noa ƒe kukuɖeɖe kple gbedodoɖa ta, eye amegbetɔwo kple woƒe akpa ɖeka le ewo me ko ye wɔ nu tso ɣemaɣi. Ema le tɔtrɔ ge esime woaʋu aʋakɔ gã la eye woaɖe wo katã asi.

Eya ta wotsrɔ̃ anyigba la, kple dzɔlawo ƒe ŋunyɔnuwo katã. Wode kɔsɔkɔsɔ dzɔlawo ŋutɔwo le woƒe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ vovovowo me hena dzidzime blaadre, be woaɖe asi le wo ŋu hena ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ le ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ gã la ƒe ɣeyiɣi me. (Elabena ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ gã la le gɔme dze ge kpuie.) Gake Noa kpɔ amenuveve le Yahweh ŋkume elabena ame dzɔdzɔee wònye, eye Yahweh na eƒe ƒomea tsi agbe luʋɔ enyi sɔŋ, kpakple nu gbagbe bubu geɖewo le Aɖaka la me. Noa dzɔ eɖokui kple eƒe ƒomea ŋu tso dzɔdzɔmeŋusẽwo gbegblẽ me hafi tsiɖɔɖɔa va. Gake via Xam kple eƒe dzidzimevi vɔ̃ɖiwo agbe Noa kple Yahweh ŋutɔ (ame si wɔ wo heɖe wo) mlɔeba, eye woatsɔ wo ɖokuiwo akpe ɖe Satana ƒe aglãdzedzea ŋu.

Mose Gbãtɔ 6 ƒe nyawo va eme to Noa ƒe dzidzimeviwo, Xam ƒe dzidzime gɔ̃ hã, ƒe nu vɔ̃wo me. Dzotso aʋadziwo nɔ anyigba dzi le ŋkeke mawo me: eye le ema hã megbe. "Ŋkeke mawo" ye nye ŋkeke siwo tso Yared dzi va se ɖe Noa dzi, eye "dzotso aʋadziwo le anyigba dzi" ye nye dzɔlawo kple amegbetɔwo vinyɔnuwo ƒe dzidzimeviwo hafi tsiɖɔɖɔa va. "Le ema megbe" ye nye le Noa ƒe ŋkekeawo megbe, esime mawudɔla dzeanyiwo ƒe nunya gagblẽ amegbetɔwo ƒe dzɔdzɔmeŋusẽwo ake. Mele biabiam tso mia si be migagaxɔ Setitɔwo ƒe nufiafia vodada ƒe aʋatsokaka dzi se o eye miaxɔ ŋɔŋlɔ kɔkɔea ƒe nyawo dzi ase, elabena nya siawo nye nyateƒe.

Esime woma anyigba la ɖe Noa viwo (Sem, Xam, kple Yafet) dome to dzodzodada me la, Xam ƒe gome ye nye be wòanyi anyigba siwo le anyiehe la dome, eye ale wonye etɔ. Gake Kanaan di Sem ƒe domenyinu ƒe akpa aɖe vevie eye wòʋu yi anyigba siwo menye etɔ be wòanɔ o dzi. Elabena Kanaan ye Noa ƒo fi de. Elabena Kanaan gblẽ eƒe domenyinu ɖi heɖo Tiro du la anyi ɖe nu si va zu Foiniketɔwo ƒe ƒuta dzi eye wòkplɔ ame yi Sidon, si me Yezebel dzɔ tso. Ewɔ esia be yeanɔ teƒe si te ɖe Hermon Toa ŋu, afi si dzɔlawo ɖi va kple afi si wotrɔ nye nɔnɔme le.

Kanaan kpɔ nu si dzɔlawo gblẽ ɖi hafi Noa ƒe tsiɖɔɖɔa va, elabena wodi be yewoata yewoƒe aglãdzedzea ta le tsiɖɔɖɔa megbe gɔ̃ hã, elabena wokpa nu geɖe ɖe kpewo kple kpewo me. Kanaan zã woƒe mawudɔla dzeanyiwo ƒe nunya le dzosasa gãwo me, eye Kanaantɔwo wɔ kɔnu vɔ̃ɖi geɖewo, elabena wotsɔ woawo ŋutɔwo viwo gɔ̃ hã sa vɔe. Wovɔ̃ɖi ŋutɔ eye wokaka ale gbegbe be wòte ŋu tsɔ eƒe ŋunyɔnu yeyewo de dɔ Kanaan nyigba la dzi, afi si Satana wɔ dɔ le le eƒe dzidadawo ta.

Eye ale tsiɖɔɖɔa megbe dzotso aʋadziwo ƒe towo, Kanaantɔwo ƒe to ŋunyɔtɔwo gɔ̃ hã, do tso Kanaan ƒe dzidadawo kple vodadawo kple dzosasawo me. Hititɔwo, kple Hivitɔwo, kple Perizitɔwo, kple Girgasitɔwo, kple Amoritɔwo, kple Yebusitɔwo va anyigba si Kanaan fi le Sem si la dzi. Woawoe nye ame dziŋɔwo, Refaimtɔwo gɔ̃ hã. To geɖewo nɔ anyi wu ale si woŋlɔe ɖi, gake ame dziŋɔ siawo nɔ lalam le ŋugbedodonyigbaa dzi le agbagbadzedze be yewoawu ame siwo kpɔ Kristo ƒe dzidzimea ta, mia tɔgbui Israel-viwo kple fofowo siwo nye Hebritɔwo gɔ̃ hã.

Yosua ge ɖe ŋugbedodonyigbaa dzi be yeaxɔ nu si wona Sem to dzodzodada me kple anyigba si nye Israel tɔ to Yahweh ŋutɔ ƒe ŋugbedodo me. Gake Israel-viwo mewɔ Yahweh ƒe se siwo nye be woagagblẽ gbe vɔ̃awo dometɔ aɖeke ɖe agbe o la dzi bliboe o, elabena wonye ame vɔ̃ɖiwo eye womenye amegbetɔwo o, eye woƒe nuwɔnawo vɔ̃ɖi, eye wowɔ vɔ̃ geɖe le Yahweh ŋkume, elabena alea woƒe dzɔdzɔme nɔ. Eye ale Yahweh gblɔ na Israel abe ale si woŋlɔe ɖe ŋɔŋlɔa me ene be: Gake ne mienya anyigba la dzi nɔlawo le mia ŋkume o la, ekema ava me be ame siwo miagblẽ ɖi le wo dome la, woanye ŋù le miaƒe ŋkuwo me, kple ŋù le miaƒe axawo dzi, eye woawɔ fu mi le anyigba si dzi mienɔna la dzi. Eye ale wònɔ.

Ŋutinya ɖee fia le akpa aɖe be ŋunyɔnu siawo, gbe vɔ̃ siwo Satana ŋutɔ ƒã gɔ̃ hã, ƒe dzidziɖedzi kple ʋuʋu, elabena wonɔ lu la dome tso blema ke. Woɖo sãnyigba geɖewo anyi to woƒe mɔ̃ɖaŋununya kple mawudɔla dzeanyiwo ƒe nunya zazã me; xɔtula gãwo wonye, dzosasalawo wonye, ŋusẽ siwo menye dzɔdzɔmetɔ o le wo dometɔ geɖe si, eye wodo ƒome kple wo fofo Abosam, eye wogakpɔa gome le eƒe nɔnɔmewo kple nudzodzrowo me gɔ̃ hã. Gbe vɔ̃ siawo ge ɖe hadome ƒe akpa ɖe sia ɖe me, eye menye wo katãe nye dzotso aʋadziwo o. Ame geɖe dzena abe amegbetɔwo ene, negbe ɖe wonye nuwɔwɔ siwo wotsaka siwo wotrɔ tegbee tso Yahweh ƒe ɖoɖowo me le nuwɔwɔ me eye womaxɔ wo ɖe agbe o. Mivɔ̃ wo o, elabena ame si le mia me la lolo wu ame si le xexeame.

Gbe vɔ̃ siawo di be yewoaxɔ nu si nye Mawu viwo tɔ to domenyinyi kple ŋugbedodo me. Womate ŋu awɔ ɖeka kpli wo o, elabena wotso wo fofo Abosam gbɔ, eye wo fofo ƒe nudzodzrowo ye woawɔ. Amewula wònye tso gɔmedzedzea me, eye ne egblɔ aʋatso la, eya ŋutɔ tɔ gblɔm wòle, elabena aʋatsokala wònye eye eyae nye fofoa. Woadze agbagba godoo agblẽ nu, aƒo ɖi nu, atsrɔ̃ nu, awu ame, aha nu, aha nu, eye woawɔ woƒe nu vɔ̃ɖitɔ gãtɔ ɖe nu kɔkɔe ɖe sia ɖe si ŋu wodi be yewoawɔ la ŋu. Ŋutifafa aɖeke mele wo si o, eye woƒe dzɔdzɔme gblẽ keŋkeŋ. Wogblẽ xexeame, eye woaɖo woƒe fiaɖuƒe anyi gaƒoƒo ɖeka esime drakɔ la aɖu fia le anyigba dzi. Gake wodi be yewoagaɖo yewoƒe sikaɣi la anyi ake, gake sikaɣi aɖeke manɔ wo si o negbe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ sesẽ ko, eye womasi o.

Gbe vɔ̃ vɔ̃ɖi siawo gblẽ sidzedze, nusɔsrɔ̃, ŋutinya, subɔsubɔha, dzɔdzɔmeŋutinunya, eye woyi edzi gblẽ nu. Wokaka mawudɔla dzeanyiwo ƒe mɔ̃ɖaŋununya eye nu yeye aɖeke mele ɣea te o. Esi ɣeyiɣiawo de la, wotsyɔ aʋatso anyigba la dzi keŋkeŋ, eye xexe bliboa mlɔa anyi le vɔ̃ɖinyenye me. To woƒe kekeli me la, Jesuit gbe vɔ̃wo gblẽ hadome keŋkeŋ eye wokplɔ amegbetɔwo yi aʋatso ƒe atsiaƒu aɖe me, afi si mɔ ɖeka kolia si dzi woato asi ye nye dzimetɔtrɔ kple gbegbe tso nu vɔ̃ kple aʋatso katã me. (Elabena Roma wɔ Islam hã; srɔ̃ nu be nànya.) Woƒe mawudɔla dzeanyiwo ƒe nunya kple aʋatsowo tsi tre ɖe ŋɔŋlɔawo ƒe ŋutinya ŋu eye ɖe eteƒe la, wonaa gli gbɔlo siwo gbaa ŋku na amewo ƒe tamesusuwo kple dziwo. Eya ta mido go le wo dome eye mianɔ vovo.
