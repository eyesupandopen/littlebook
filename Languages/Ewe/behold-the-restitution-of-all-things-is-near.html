Kpeɖodzi Eve: Israel

Siamlɔm 9, 2024

Kpɔ ɖa! Nuwo Katã Ɖɔɖɔɖo Gogo!

Tso Yeshua HaMashiach Gbɔ:

Ɖɔɖɔɖo ƒe ɣeyiɣiwo le aƒe tum ɖe anyigba la dzi, gake gbã la, ele be ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ gã la ƒe ɣeyiɣiwo kple nuwuɣi nava eme. Eye nye, Kristo Yesu, madrɔ̃ ʋɔnu anyigba la elabena anyigba la kple eƒe blibonyenye nye tɔnye, eye meƒle anyigba la kple nye avuléʋu le Kalvari. Le nyateƒe si matrɔ o me la, metsɔ nye ʋu maɖifɔ si wokɔ ɖi ɖe amegbetɔkpekpe ƒe nuvɔ̃wo ta la ƒe dropi ɖe sia ɖe na. Na ame siwo lɔ̃a nye ŋutifafa kple agbe mavɔ ƒe nunana la, ŋutifafa kple agbe mavɔ anɔ anyi, eye dzɔdzɔenyenye ƒe mɔwo aɖu dzi. Na ame siwo gbe nye nunana la, wotia ku, ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃, kple tohehe mavɔ ƒe mɔwo, elabena xexeame le nu yim eye tiatia bubu aɖeke meli o. Eye ale woatsɔ woƒe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ la, eye woaɖe nuvɔ̃, nya vɔ̃, tamesusu vɔ̃, kple nuwɔna vɔ̃ ɖe sia ɖe ɖe go ale be woana akɔnta. Gake ame siwo gbe nye xɔxɔ la mate ŋu anɔ te gbeɖe o, eye le woawo ŋutɔ ƒe lɔlɔ̃nu me la, wotia dzo ƒe ta si wodzra ɖo ɖi na abosam kple eƒe dɔlawo, eye nu sia mehiã o, gake ame ɖe sia ɖe vo atia. Nyee nye ame si gbɔna ʋɔnu drɔ̃ ge kple aʋa wɔ ge, eye Yahweh ƒe mɔwo aɖu anyigba dzi le dzɔdzɔenyenye me le nye fiaɖuƒe si gbɔna me. Na wò nye Hame, kple nye Ŋugbetɔsrɔ̃ xɔasi la gɔ̃ hã, woakɔ mi ayi dziƒowo, eye miaxɔ miaƒe domenyinu, eye miaxɔ miaƒe nɔƒewo le ɖoɖo siwo medzra ɖo ɖi na mi la dome, miaƒe nɔƒe le Yakob dome gɔ̃ hã elabena mi ye nye Mawu ƒe Israel.

Elabena le menye, Kristo Yesu me la, Yudatɔ alo Trɔ̃subɔla aɖeke meli o ke boŋ nuwɔwɔ yeye, eye nuwɔwɔ ma azɔ abe ame yeye ɖeka ene. Kpɔ ɖa! Mianye ame yeye, eye mianye nuwɔwɔ yeye, nyemena gbɔgbɔ kɔkɔe ƒe mɔxɔnu mi oa? Mawu gbagbe la ƒe nutrenu, si womate ŋu agbã o la gɔ̃ hã? Mia dometɔ kae ate ŋu agblẽ miaƒe ŋutilãmeviwo ƒe dzidzi me? Mimate ŋui o elabena miaƒe dzidzimefianuwo tsoa dzidzime yi dzidzime eye ɖeviwo nɔa anyi ɖaa. Eye ale wòle, womate ŋu agblẽ Yahweh ƒe ku si wowɔ ɖe Mawu ƒe viwo me la o, elabena manya wɔ be woaho mi le nye asiwo me o. Miexlẽe oa? Elabena wogadzodzi mi, menye tso ku si gblẽna me o, ke boŋ tso ku si megblẽna o me, to Mawu ƒe nya si le agbe, eye wònɔa anyi tegbee la dzi. Vinyewo, ɖe nye nyawo wɔ nuku akpaa? Ɖe mieɖe mɔ gbe aʋatso aɖewo tra mi tso tamesusu ɖeka le Kristo me gbɔa? Naneke meli ate ŋu aɖe Yahweh ƒe ku la ɖa ne wowɔe ɖe ememe vɔ o, elabena Mawu ƒe nunanawo kple yɔyɔ la le dzimetɔtrɔ manɔmee. Eya ta nu ka ta miele ɖi kem le miaƒe xɔxɔ ŋu? Togbɔ be miaƒe ŋutilã ana miawɔ nuvɔ̃, eye matsɔe ake mi eye makɔ mia ŋu ne mieʋu miaƒe nuvɔ̃wo me le dzimetɔtrɔ me hã la, naneke meli ate ŋu aɖe Mawu ƒe viwo ɖa le Mawu ƒe ƒomea me o. Na ame siwo nɔa agbe madzɔmadzɔ la, woƒe dɔwɔwɔwo afia dzo, eye woatso tsitretsitsi amama kple ŋukpe, eye na ame siwo ƒe dɔwɔwɔwo le te abe dzɔdzɔetɔwo ene la axɔ fetu gã, gake Mawu ƒe viwo axɔ xɔxɔ mavɔ. Ne Kristo ƒe gbɔgbɔ mele ame aɖeke me o la, menye etɔ o.

Na ame siwo nɔa agbe gbegblẽwo kple ame siwo wɔa wo ɖokuiwo abe ɖe wole agbe nɔm na Kristo le ŋkɔ me ko ene la adze anyi ne Hame vavã la dzo. Elabena ame si ƒãa nu ɖe ŋutilã me la, aŋe gbegblẽ tso ŋutilã me. Eye na ame siwo dze agbagba be yewoawɔm Aƒetɔ tso dzi xɔsemanɔmee me eye woyi edzi le nuvɔ̃ me la, woagblẽ wo ɖi na Yakob ƒe xaxa hena ŋutilã ƒe tsɔtsrɔ̃ eye ahiã be woayɔ Aƒetɔ la ƒe ŋkɔ va se ɖe nuwuɣi, eye ale wòanɔ. Ame siwo ble wo ɖokuiwo va se ɖe woƒe gbɔgbɔ mlɔeba dzi eye womeku le Kristo me o eye xɔxɔ ƒe ƒomedodo aɖeke menɔ wo kplim kpɔ o la ado go woƒe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃. Miebui be Yahweh si nye dzimedzrola la nye bometsilaa? Miebui be nye, Kristo Yesu, nyemedoa amegbetɔwo ƒe dzi kpɔna hena xɔse kple kaka ɖe dzinye oa? Gake ale wòanɔ, eye magblɔ na ame geɖe siwo yɔam be Aƒetɔ be: Midzo le gbɔnye, mi nuvɔ̃wɔlawo, nyemenya mi kpɔ o.

Gake na wò nye Hame, nye yɔyɔ kple nuteƒewɔla kple tiatia: Luʋɔ ɖe sia ɖe si kpɔ xɔxɔ la gɔ̃ hã zu ŋugbedodo siwo Yahweh do na Fofoawo la dome nyila. Elabena woŋlɔe ɖi be Trɔ̃subɔlawo nanye domenyila hatɔwo, kple ŋutilã ɖeka ma ke tɔwo, kple eƒe ŋugbedodo le Kristo me ƒe gomekpɔlawo to nyanyui la me, eye metsɔ wo do ɖe Amiti dzɔdzɔe la me. Eye ame siwo katã wowɔ yeyee le menye la, woagbugbɔ wo aɖo te bliboe ɖe blibonyenye kple dzɔdzɔenyenye kple kɔkɔenyenye vavã me. Nyemedo awu fafãwo ƒe ŋugbe na mi o, nye vi suewo? Eye meɖo miaƒe domenyinu ɖe towo dome, elabena mi ye nye Mawu ƒe Israel si meƒle tso anyigba dzi.

Dzudzɔ dzidzimeviwo ƒe tɔtrɔwo ŋu bubu le Adam ƒe dzidzime me, elabena Adam ƒe ʋu gblẽ le nuvɔ̃ me! Abe ale si woŋlɔe ɖi ene: wowɔ ame gbãtɔ Adam wòzu luʋɔ gbagbe, eye wowɔ Adam mlɔetɔ wòzu gbɔgbɔ si dea agbe ame me. Miexlẽe oa? Womanye Israel katã, siwo tso Israel o. Elabena ŋugbedodo la ƒe viwo ye nye Abraham ƒe dzidzimeviwo to xɔse me, eye abe ale si woŋlɔe ɖi ene la, Dzɔdzɔetɔ la anɔ agbe to xɔse me. Eye le menye, Kristo Yesu me la, nuwo katã wu enu elabena Dzata la anɔ ɖeka bliboe kple Alẽvi la eye wode dzesi mi bliboe kple nye, Kristo Yesu miaƒe Aƒetɔ. Kpɔ ɖa! Gake na mi ame siwo vɔ̃a nye Ŋkɔ la, dzɔdzɔenyenye ƒe vi la ado! Nyee nye Dzɔdzɔenyenye ƒe vi la, Mawu Dziƒotɔ, Yahweh, ƒe vi dzidzi ɖeka la gɔ̃ hã, elabena aɖeke meli abe eya ene o, eye nyee nye ame si le afi sia be maxɔ nye hame la tso fukpekpe me, nye ŋugbetɔsrɔ̃ gɔ̃ hã ayi eƒe aƒe me le dziƒowo, eye wòanɔ gbɔnye tegbee. Elabena ale wòanɔ, mado ƒome kple miaƒe ƒome vavã la elabena woka Israel hlẽ ɖe duta eye le Mawu ƒe Israel me la woaƒo wo nu ƒu, be womagaka wo hlẽ gbeɖe o, elabena mi ye nye Mawu ƒe Israel!

Eya ta maxɔ nye Hame la ayi dziƒowo, nye nuteƒewɔlawo, eye mianɔ gbɔnye tegbee. Elabena wodo mi ŋugbe na srɔ̃ŋutsu ɖeka: Yeshua HaMashiach, eye to nye gbɔgbɔ me mewɔ mi ɖetugbui dzadzɛ eye mekɔ nuteƒewɔlawo ŋu ale be woate ŋu anɔ te dzɔdzɔe le nye avuléʋu me. Ne wokɔ mi la, woaxe fe na mi ɖe miaƒe nuteƒewɔwɔ ta eye miatsɔ fiakuku geɖewo adzoe, togbɔ be ame aɖewo abu nu hã. Woaxe fe na mi wòagbɔ miaƒe tamesusu gãtɔwo ŋu, elabena ŋku mekpɔe o eye to mese o eye mege ɖe amegbetɔ ƒe dzi me nu siwo Yahweh dzra ɖo ɖi na ame siwo lɔ̃nɛ la o. Eye nuteƒewɔlawo, nye Ŋugbetɔsrɔ̃ gɔ̃ hã, anɔ gbɔnye tegbee, eye woaɖu fia abe fiawo kple nunɔlawo ene tegbee, elabena nuwuɣi aɖeke mele nye Fiaɖuƒe la ŋu o.

Eye ale nye dɔla ayi dzi afi si mianɔ gbɔnye le dziƒowo eye wòade dzesi ame 12,000 tso to ɖe sia ɖe me. Miexlẽ nye Nya siwo mena Yohanes Nyaɖela la oa? Eye mekpɔ dɔla bubu wòle dzi yim tso ɣedzeƒe, eye Mawu gbagbe la ƒe nutrenu le esi: eye wòdo ɣli kple gbe gã aɖe yɔ dɔla ene siwo wona mɔ be woagblẽ nu le anyigba kple atsiaƒu ŋu la, gblɔ be, migagblẽ nu le anyigba, alo atsiaƒu alo atiwo ŋu o, va se ɖe esime míade dzesi mía Mawu ƒe subɔlawo le woƒe ŋgonuwo. Miekpɔ nya yi dzi la oa? Dziƒo dɔla kɔkɔewo ɖi va anyigba dzi, elabena woƒe aƒe le dziƒowo. Be dɔla kɔkɔe aɖe nayi dzi la, ele dzi yim yi dziƒowo. Dɔla siwo dze anyi ye yia dzi vaa anyigba dzi tso aʋali la me, ɖe miede bubu nye nya kɔkɔe la ƒe Nyawo ŋu oa?

Eye woade dzesi ame 12,000 tso to sia to si woŋlɔ ɖi me: Yuda, Ruben, Gad, Aser, Naftali, Manase, Simeon, Levi, Isakar, Zebulon, Yosef, Benyamin. Eye ale nye ame 144,000 la axɔ Mawu gbagbe la ƒe nutrenu le woƒe ŋgonuwo. Eye woatso kplim le Zion To dzi, eye woadzi ha yeye aɖe le fiazikpui la ŋgɔ, kple lã ene la, kple hamemetsitsiwo ŋgɔ: eye ame aɖeke mate ŋu asrɔ̃ ha ma o negbe ame akpe alafa ɖeka blaeve vɔ ene la, siwo woƒle tso anyigba dzi. Ame siawo ye nye ame siwo meƒo ɖi kple nyɔnuwo o; elabena ɖetugbuiwo wonye. Ame siawo ye nye ame siwo dzea Alẽvi la yome le afi sia afi si wòyina. Elabena woƒle ame siawo tso amewo dome, be woanye kutsetse gbãtɔwo na Mawu kple Alẽvi la. Eye womekpɔ aʋatso aɖeke le woƒe nu me o: elabena fɔ aɖeke mele wo ŋu le Mawu ƒe fiazikpui la ŋgɔ o.

Yahweh ye tia ame 144,000 la hafi woɖo anyigba gɔme anyi hena ɣeyiɣi sia. Wotu wo le fukpekpe me, eye ŋutete kple ŋusẽ tɔxɛwo anɔ wo si si matsɔ ado awu yeye siwo mana wo la na wo, elabena wotɔ miaƒe awuwo na mi koŋ eye miaƒe dɔwɔwɔwo kple nɔnɔmewo anɔ eme. Woakɔ wo le nuteƒewɔlawo ƒe nuƒoƒoƒu me gake woɖe wo ɖi na nye taɖodzinuwo, Yahweh kple Yeshua HaMashiach ƒe taɖodzinuwo gɔ̃ hã. Wovaa xaxa gã la me be woakplɔ Israel ayi dedienɔƒe. Gake gbã la wokɔ wo be woaɖe Kristo elabena wonye eƒe Ŋugbetɔsrɔ̃, ame siwo woyɔ, wotia, wowɔ nuteƒe, eye wonye nyateƒetɔwo. Ame siwo gɔ̃ hã Kristo wɔ kɔkɔe abe ɖetugbui ene le eƒe avuléʋu me. Eye wò nye nye ŋugbetɔsrɔ̃ ye asubɔ Yahweh ƒe taɖodzinuwo le nye sedede nu le xaxa gãtɔwo ƒe ɣeyiɣiwo me.

Elabena mi ye nye kutsetse gbãtɔ siwo woƒo ƒu tso nuŋeŋe ƒe agblewo me, elabena nyee nye nuŋeŋe ƒe Aƒetɔ, eye azɔ ɣeyiɣi de be maƒo mi nu ƒu ɖe nye ava me ale be miaxɔ miaƒe dɔwɔwɔwo ƒe fetu. Elabena ame siwo ƒo lu nu ƒu la akpɔ geɖe eye ame siwo ƒo gbe ƒu la abu nu ne tsro la fia dzo le dzo la me, gake woaxɔ wo. Eye ale nye dɔwɔlawo kple dukɔ ava woƒe domenyinu gbɔ le menye, Yeshua HaMashiach, elabena nyee nye Aƒetɔwo ƒe Aƒetɔ kple Fiawo ƒe Fia eye womazi kpi dzinye o eye womaxe mɔ nam o.

Womeŋlɔe ɖi oa? Ale Aƒetɔ MAWU gblɔe nye esi; Kpɔ ɖa, maxɔ Israel viwo tso trɔ̃subɔlawo dome, afi sia afi si woyi, eye maƒo wo nu ƒu tso akpa sia akpa, eye makplɔ wo ava woawo ŋutɔ ƒe anyigba dzi: Eye mawɔ wo dukɔ ɖeka le anyigba la dzi le Israel ƒe towo dzi; eye fia ɖeka anye fia na wo katã: eye womaganye dukɔ eve o, eye womagama wo ɖe fiaɖuƒe eve me gbeɖe o: Womagakpɔ ɖi kple woƒe trɔ̃wo o, alo kple woƒe nu nyɔŋuwo alo kple woƒe dzidadawo dometɔ aɖeke o: ke boŋ maɖe wo tso woƒe nɔƒe siwo katã wowɔ nuvɔ̃ le la me, eye makɔ wo ŋu: ale woanye nye dukɔ, eye manye woƒe Mawu.

Nye Yeshua HaMashiach ye dɔ Nya siawo to nye dɔla dzi, eye to nye dɔla dzi ye wodɔ Nya siawo! Kpɔ ɖa! Nyee nye ame si gbɔna le alilikpowo me, eya ta midzra mia ɖokuiwo ɖo, nye ɖetugbui nunyalawo.

Tso Yahweh Mawu Gã Mavɔ La Gbɔ:

Mekpɔ miaƒe vɔvɔ̃, oo mi ame siwo dze anyi, elabena azɔ miaƒe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ gbɔna ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ gã si ŋugbe wodo le blema gɔ̃ hã, kple ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ gã la ƒe ɣeyiɣi siwo me, Yahweh Mawu gã mavɔ si gblɔ miaƒe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ ɖi le ɣeyiɣi siwo va yi me eye dziɖegbe adre ɖi woƒe gbewo eye ale woase Mawu ŋusẽkatãtɔ la ƒe gbe! Eye woaƒo nye taɖodzinuwo nu ƒu, nye ɖoɖowo na ɣeyiɣiwo gɔ̃ hã ava eme le vinye Yesu Kristo me. Elabena le kutsetse gbãtɔwo me la, nuŋeŋe ƒe fetuwo vana na dɔwɔla siwo Yeshua HaMashiach dɔ ɖa eye azɔ woaƒo nye lu nu ƒu ɖe nye ava me eye mama mlɔetɔ le anyigba dzi adze egɔme eye Yakob ƒe xaxa le afi sia.

Kpɔ ɖa! Mama dziƒowo le dzi eye maʋuʋu anyigba le te, eye maƒo xɔxɔ ƒe domenyila siwo susɔ la nu ƒu tso anyigba dzi, eye woava xɔxɔ me, eye woayɔ Aƒetɔ la ƒe ŋkɔ va se ɖe nuwuɣi, eye woasubɔm le nye gbedoxɔ me eye woaxɔ ameha gã kple triakɔ aɖe tso Daniel ƒe kwasiɖa blaadrelia me. Nye, Yahweh gɔ̃ hã, madze anyigba la me eye maɖe towo ɖa le wo nɔƒewo eye xexeame katã anya be El Shaddai tso le dzi kɔkɔƒe eye ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ gã la adze anyigba la dzi azɔ ne woƒo nye Nuteƒewɔlawo nu ƒu elabena dzidadawo awu enu, Yakob ƒe dzidada kple Israel ƒe aƒe la ƒe nuvɔ̃wo gɔ̃ hã.

Dzɔdzɔenyenye ƒe dziɖuɖu ava wo teƒe, nye vi dzidzi ɖeka, Yeshua HaMashiach, si ŋu mekpɔa ŋudzedze ɖo ɣesiaɣi la ƒe dziɖuɖu dzɔdzɔe gɔ̃ hã. Eya ye atsɔ gayotí aƒo dukɔwo eye anye dzɔdzɔenyenye ƒe dziɖuɖu, afi si Dzata la amlɔ anyi kple alẽvi la. Eye ale wòanye nunɔlagã tegbee le Melkizedek ƒe ɖoɖo nu eye Aƒetɔwo ƒe Aƒetɔ kple Fiawo ƒe Fia aƒo ƒu bliboe le nuwuɣi mavɔ me eye eƒe dziɖuɖu anɔ anyi tegbee, Yeshua HaMashiach gɔ̃ hã elabena eya ke ye nye Yesu Kristo hã!

Miedzra mia ɖokuiwo ɖo mi ame siwo le anyigba dzi le madzɔmadzɔnyenye me elabena medɔ vinye be wòadrɔ̃ nye ʋɔnu eye wòagbã ame siwo dze anyi la ƒe dziɖuɖu katã gudugudu, elabena woƒe ɣeyiɣiwo de eye ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ adze anyigba la dzi kpekpekpe. Elabena miedro dziku gã aɖe ɖi ɖe dzikugbea ŋu eye dzikugbea va mia dzi, oo mi ame dzodzrowo, elabena mieƒo ɖi mia ɖokuiwo le gbegblẽ blibo me, eye ŋutifafa aɖeke mele mia si ɖe miaƒe mɔ nyɔŋu kple fɔɖiwo ta o. Gake hafi miayi miaƒe domenyinu mavɔ afi si dzo blɔtɔ xɔdzo wu la ami mi la, miasubɔ nye taɖodzinuwo gbã, elabena nye ketɔwo gɔ̃ hã ɖoa to nye gbe.

Eya ta ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ adze egɔme tso Mawu ƒe aƒe me eye madɔ vinye Kristo Yesu Israel ƒe Mesia si fɔ tso ku me kple xexeame ƒe xɔla gɔ̃ hã, eye awɔ nu siwo katã meɖo eye medo ŋugbe na Fofoawo kple viwo la eye nu katã anye ẽ kple amen ne ŋugbedodowo me vava va eme.

Elabena wodzra dziƒowo ɖo ɖi na Vinyewo, ame siwo katã meɖo ɖi na nye taɖodzinuwo hafi nuwɔwɔ dze egɔme gɔ̃ hã. Elabena menya mia dometɔ ɖe sia ɖe le mavɔmavɔ siwo va yi me, eye metsɔ mi ɖo ɣaɣla aɖe si meɣla ɖe nye dzi me si meɖe fia le ɣeyiɣi nyuitɔ dzi ko le nye xɔxɔ ƒe ɖoɖo mavɔ la me. Naneke meli ate ŋu adzɔ si nyemenya hafi wòadzɔ o, eye Nyee, Mawu Ŋusẽkatãtɔ la, yɔa nu siwo meli o abe ɖe woli xoxo ene. Oo, mi ame siwo dze anyi, miafia dzo bliboe le miaƒe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ si gbɔna me, eye miezu bometsilawo be miasusu be miate ŋu atsɔ aɖaŋu alé Yahweh, gake NYEE NYE AME SI léa dadalawo le ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ me, elabena wonye nunyalawo le woawo ŋutɔ ƒe adegbeƒoƒowo me. Elabena mefia ɣaɣla dzodzrowo mi ko, elabena menya be miade nu siwo katã nye tɔnye la asi. Aleke miezu bometsilawoe nye esi oo mi ame siwo dze anyi eye mienye nunyalawo o. Nu siwo katã miewɔ ye nye mia ŋutɔ miaƒe fuwɔame kple tsɔtsrɔ̃ madzudzɔ le dzo xɔdzo wu la me, eye miaƒu tsi le dzo kple dzokpe me, elabena ale ta gã la fia dzo tegbee, eye maɖo nutrenu la, eye woaŋlɔ mi be bliboe, eye miagava susu me o.

Gake dzɔgbeve sia menye mia tɔ o, nye tiatiawo, nye nuteƒewɔlawo, ame siwo katã woyɔna ɖe nye ŋkɔ me gɔ̃ hã. Mia tɔ ye nye etsɔme si nye dzidzɔ mavɔ, dzidzɔ mavɔ, dzidzɔ mavɔ eye ŋkeke aɖeke mawu enu o eye ŋkeke vɔ̃ aɖeke manɔ anyi gbeɖe na ame siwo ge ɖe paradiso me o. Kpɔ ɖa! Maɖɔ nuwo katã ɖo eye mawɔ wo nanyo wu nye blibonyenye gbãtɔ hafi gbegblẽ dze anyi gɔ̃ hã, elabena mawɔ dziƒo yeye kple anyigba yeye si me dzɔdzɔenyenye le eye naneke si nye vɔ̃ alo ƒo ɖi mage ɖe eme o. Elabena avuwo, kple dzoɖalawo, kple gbolowɔlawo, kple amewulawo, kple trɔ̃subɔlawo, kple ame sia ame si lɔ̃a aʋatso hewɔnɛ la le xexe, eye wo katã ƒe gome anɔ dzo ta ma si me woƒe nɔvi mekunɛ o la me.

Eya ta naneke meli miavɔ̃ na le miaƒe etsɔme o nye yɔyɔ, tiatia, kple nuteƒewɔlawo, eye miaɖu fia kple Yeshua HaMashiach tegbee eye nuwuɣi aɖeke mele eƒe fiaɖuƒe alo dziɖuɖu ŋu o. Eye nye Mawu gã mavɔ la, Yahweh ŋutɔ gɔ̃ hã awɔ nye nɔƒe ɖe amegbetɔwo dome eye ŋutifafa kple blibonyenye si sɔ anɔ nye nuwɔwɔ katã dzi eye matutu aɖatsi katã ɖa le woƒe ŋkuwo me eye ame aɖeke magatso gbeɖe si aɖe fu na mi kura o, elabena nye, Yahweh, Mawu gã mavɔ la gɔ̃ hã, metsɔ nye ŋutɔ nye ŋkɔ ka atam le nu siawo ŋu elabena nyemate ŋu atsɔ gãtɔ aɖeke aka atam o. Gake na mi ame siwo vɔ̃a nye ŋkɔ la, Dzɔdzɔenyenye ƒe Vi la ado kple dɔyɔyɔ le eƒe aʋalawo te; eye miado go, eye miatsi abe nyivi siwo le kpo me ene.

Nye Yahweh, Mawu Gã mavɔ la, medɔ nye Nyawo to nye dɔla dzi, eye to nye dɔla dzi ye wodɔ nye Nyawo.

NYEE NYE NYEE NYE Gã La eye bubu aɖeke meli o!
