Ahuhɔ̃e La Da Aʋatso!

Ahuhɔ̃e, ahuhɔ̃e le gli ŋu, gblɔ xexe si me mɔ̃wo bɔ ɖo si le ame katã ƒe susu hem la ŋu nya nam! To aʋatso siwo wogbugbɔ gblɔna edziedzi me, kple aʋatsonɔnɔmewo siwo nu metsina o la me, wotrɔa woƒe susuwo le aʋatso gã me kple modzakaɖeɖe ƒe kɔnuwo ale be woble alẽawo to susu siwo woƒe tamesusuwo lé ɖe asi la me! Nenema kee, ŋutilã ƒe nudzodzrowo, kple ŋkuwo ƒe nudzodzrowo kple agbe me dada si wodoa dzo ɖe eme le sidzedze aʋatso si wona hena hehe si woyɔna be hehe afi sia afi si wodzraa woƒe aʋatsowo le aɖaŋu me la, blaa wo. To esia me la, wonya to na wo hefaa akɔ na wo kple gbeɖiɖi siwo wogbugbɔna edziedzi ale be wotrɔa dadawo kple bometsilawo ƒe susu heɖua aƒetɔ ɖe wo dzi. Eye le ɣeyiɣi aɖe megbe la, wokpɔe be to ɖeviwo ƒe ha siwo trɔa susu me la, amegbetɔ ƒe nɔnɔme trɔna ɖe enu eye wotsi eƒe dzo me!

Ke hã nenemae xexe si me nèle la le, O Israel, xexe si mlɔ anyi keŋkeŋ le vɔ̃ɖinyenye me, xexe si woɖe aboyoe hebla keŋkeŋ to aʋatso ƒe avɔ si wona be woatsɔ aɖo mɔ na amehawo ɖe woƒe amewuwu ƒe ŋutinya siwo wonana ɣesiaɣi fifia hena Adam tsɔtsrɔ̃ la me. Ahuhɔ̃ewo, ahuhɔ̃ewo le afi sia afi, gake le ahuhɔ̃e ƒe nukpɔkpɔ me la, nyateƒe mele afi ma o, elabena ŋku siwo kpɔa eŋu la mekpɔa nyateƒe o elabena woƒe ahuhɔ̃ewo nye aʋatso be woalɔ̃ aʋatso nyateƒe si ame dzedzeariwo ƒe mɔ̃ɖaŋununyawo wɔ si woɖo be woatsɔ awɔ amegbetɔ kluvi na woƒe aʋatsowo ale be Adam naku tegbee.

Ahuhɔ̃e yibɔwo, ahuhɔ̃ewo le afi sia afi eye woɖo wo ɖe afi sia afi si ŋkuwo ate ŋu atsa le, ale be ahuhɔ̃ewo nagblɔ aʋatso ɖeka siwo wɔ ɖeka to vɔ̃ɖinyenye ƒe ɖoɖo me, ale be nuvɔ̃ kple ku na modzaka amegbetɔ dzɔdzɔe eye dzotowo kple dzotowo geɖe siwo wotsɔ bla abe nunya kple dzidzedzekpɔkpɔ ene gake wohea ku kple tsɔtsrɔ̃ vɛ elabena nyateƒe aɖeke mele aʋatsonɔnɔme siwo wonana la me o. Ahuhɔ̃e yibɔwo, ahuhɔ̃ewo kple asi si wolé ɖe asi afi si nɔnɔme ɖe nɔnɔme yome tua tsukuku kple aʋatso gã ɖe anyigba la dzi, afi si womema nyateƒe tso aʋatso gbɔ o, si me ame siwo katã kpɔa dzidzɔ ɖe madzɔmadzɔnyenye ŋu la masi le o ke boŋ woaku tegbee le tsɔtsrɔ̃ mavɔ ƒe dzo me, tohe si wotsa wo ɖo tegbee eye afi aɖeke mele si woate ŋu asi ayi alo aɣla wo ɖokui ɖo si woate ŋu aƒo asa na ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ o.

O, se woaƒe hadzidzi! Ðo to nàse xevi siwo dzia ha la ƒe gbe gbogbo aɖewo wole ha dzim ɖekae, le ɖekawɔwɔ me kple aʋatsonɔnɔme siwo wɔ ɖeka si woƒe ahuhɔ̃e yibɔwo nana ɣesiaɣi. Wodzia ha te ɖe nyateƒe xeviwo ƒe hawo ŋu ŋutɔ gake le nyateƒe me la, wo tɔ nye aʋatso ha si gblɔa aʋatso ale be woƒe gbewo nawɔ ɖeka kple aʋatso anukwareɖiɖi le woƒe aʋatsokakawo me si wodi be yewoaɣla to esia me. Ke hã aʋatso xevi siawo mekpɔa woawo ŋutɔ ƒe hawo o ke boŋ wosrɔ̃a nyateƒe xeviwo ƒe hawo hegbugbɔa wo gblɔna evɔ womewlɔa ha siwo wodzina la me nyawo alo sea wo gɔme o, eye ale nɔnɔme siwo woɖena fiana le ahuhɔ̃e yibɔ siwo nye mɔ̃wo dzi la wɔa ɖeka ale be aʋatsowo gbugbɔna eye amehawo ƒe lãme kua akpa to esia me. Aʋatso nukpɔkpɔwo kple aʋatso nyateƒe si me woxɔ anyigba bliboa le ame dzedzeariwo ƒe aʋatsowo me, aʋatso nyateƒe si va anyigba dzi be wòadzi ɖe edzi ko ne meɖe nye mɔxela ɖa ne meƒo ame nuteƒewɔla siwo le Kristo me nu ƒu ɖe dziƒowo ƒe dedienɔnɔ me kpoyi eye womele mɔ kpɔm na o fifia.

Eya ta, se Yeshua HaMashiach ƒe nyawo eye nàse xexe si me nèle kple aʋatso si woɖe mɔ ɖo be wòalé anyigba la ɖe eƒe aʋatso asilélé me la gɔme. Aʋatso si wona le ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ me afi si woɖe mɔ na ame dzedzeariwo be woaɖu dzi, eye eya ta elabena amegbetɔ dzɔdzɔe di be yeawɔ ɖeka kple Satanawo le woƒe aglãdzedze me hena madzɔmadzɔnyenye ƒe fetu, be wòakpɔ dzidzɔ le madzɔmadzɔnyenye me hena eya ŋutɔ ƒe tsɔtsrɔ̃, eye ale woatsɔ amegbetɔ dzɔdzɔe ade asi na eƒe futɔwo fifia, ke hã ame gbogbo aɖewo akpɔ ɖeɖe to klɔla ƒe dzo me. Eye mama bubu kple blibo adzɔ le Yakob ƒe xaxaɣi, mama si me woaɖe Israel ƒe ame susɔeawo tso Esau ƒe kluvinyenye me ahaɖe wo asi ayi nye ƒe akpe ɖeka fiaɖuƒe si woaɖo anyigba dzi kpuie la me!

Ke hã, amegbetɔ dzɔdzɔe lɔ̃a ame dzedzeariwo ƒe aʋatso nyateƒe afi si wodi be yewoaɣla Yahweh (si ɣaɣla manya wɔ o), ke hã nenemae aʋatso gã si woɖe fia le anyigba dzi afi si ame siwo katã gbe Yesu Nazaret tɔ, Yeshua HaMashiach, la zu kluviwo to esia me. Eya ta, se Yeshua HaMashiach ƒe Nyawo, be nàse xexe si me nèle kple aʋatso gã si bla anyigba la le eƒe asilélé me la gɔme. Se egɔme, O Israel, be nye, Yeshua HaMashiach si nye Kristo Yesu hã la, nyee nye ame si aɖe wò ŋkuagbãnviviti ɖa ne netrɔ wò dzi le toɖoɖo blibo me ɖe nye gbe ƒe ɖiɖi ŋu, si wogblɔ le nuteƒewɔla siwo nɔa agbe le xɔse me le Yahweh ƒe Vi la me ƒe nuteƒewɔwɔ nuyiwo dzi, elabena ame dzɔdzɔe la anɔ agbe le xɔse me!

Nu kae nye esi nɔ anyi kpɔ? Ema, si anɔ anyi: eye nu kae nye esi wowɔ? Esi woagawɔ ake: eye nu yeye aɖeke mele ɣea te o. Ðe nu aɖe le anyi, si ŋu ame aɖe agblɔ be, Kpɔ ɖa, yeyee wònyea? Enɔ anyi xoxo le blemaɣi siwo do ŋgɔ na mí. Ɣeyiɣi gbãtɔ sia ƒe ŋkuɖodzinya aɖeke mele anyi o, eye ŋkuɖodzinya aɖeke manɔ anyi na ɣeyiɣi mlɔetɔ si ava la o, kple ame siwo ava le emegbe. Le nyateƒe me, le nyateƒe me, ɣeyiɣi aɖe ava si me womagaɖo ŋku ame dzedzeariwo dzi o, alo woava susu me o eye woƒe aʋatso nya ɣaɣlawo nu ayi tegbee ne wotsɔ wo ƒu gbe ɖe dzo me.

Eya ta, se blema aʋatso siwo ame dzedzeariwo na amegbetɔwo le xexe si nɔ anyi do ŋgɔ na Noa ƒe tsiɖɔɖɔ gã la gɔme, aʋatso mawo kee wogbugbɔ bla hezã tsɔ tsrɔ̃ amegbetɔwo ake. Eya ta se egɔme, ame dzedzeariwo tsɔa woƒe mɔ̃ɖaŋununyawo kple aʋatsokakawo kple aʋatso dzesiwo kple nukunu siwo wozã tsɔ ble amegbetɔwo hetsrɔ̃ wo le Yared ƒe ŋkekeawo me la vɛ. Eye anyigba atrɔ ava abe ale si wònɔ le Noa ƒe ŋkekeawo me ene eye nenema kee Yakob ƒe xaxa anɔ, ɣeyiɣi aɖe le anyigba dzi si Yahweh ƒe futɔwo aɖu dzi. Dzalẽ gã xoxoa si wotsa ɖe anyigba dzi fifia hena ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃, eye woaɖe ŋunyɔnu geɖewo ɖe asi le amegbetɔwo ƒe xexeame; abe ale si Yahweh gblɔe be yeakplɔ dzɔtsuwo vɛ be woawɔ yeƒe dziku dzi ene.

Elabena wotsɔa wo ɖokuiwo wɔa manyamanya le esia ŋu, be to Mawu ƒe nya la me la, dziƒowo nɔ anyi tso blema, eye anyigba tso tsi me, eye wòle tsi me, si me xexe si nɔ anyi ɣemaɣi la, tsi ɖɔ ɖe edzi sesĩe, hetsrɔ̃. Ke dziƒo kple anyigba siwo le anyi fifia la, to Nya ma ke me wodzra wo ɖo, wodzra wo ɖo hena dzo ɖe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ ŋkeke la, kple ame mawumavɔ̃lawo ƒe tsɔtsrɔ̃ ŋu.

Nublanuitɔe la, ame gbogbo aɖewo le anyigba dzi tsɔa wo ɖokuiwo wɔa manyamanya be xexe si nɔ anyi ɣemaɣi la, Yahweh ɖɔ tsi ɖe edzi sesĩe hetsrɔ̃ le Nefilimwo kple woƒe ŋunyɔnu geɖeawo ta; eye le ame dzedzeariwo ƒe alakpadada be yewonye mawuwo ta esi wogblẽ amegbetɔwo ƒe mɔwo le anyigba dzi kple woƒe nya ɣaɣla dzodzrowo siwo wofia amegbetɔwo hena eya ŋutɔ ƒe tsɔtsrɔ̃. Eya ta se nyateƒe la, eye nàse nye Nyawo gɔme, eye nàse Yeshua HaMashiach ƒe gbe ale be nàse nyateƒe la gɔme eye ale be woƒe mɔ̃ɖaŋununyawo naƒo mɔ̃ na wò o eye be lã wɔadã la ƒe dzesi natrɔ wò nàzu ŋunyɔnu si wodzra ɖo ɖe tohehe ƒe dzo me tegbee o.

Elabena woƒe mɔ̃ɖaŋununyawo nye esi wowɔ kpɔ, eye wòle anyi fifia, eye wòava bliboe ake hena mlɔeba ɣeyiɣi mlɔetɔ. Godoo la, woaɖe woƒe mɔ̃ɖaŋununyawo afia bliboe esi wole woƒe lã wɔadã ƒe ɖoɖo mlɔetɔ ɖem fia, kple aʋatso dzesiwo kple nukunu siwo katã wona ame siwo tsrɔ̃na la. Eye ale dzalẽ la afɔ aɖu anyigba dzi le aʋatso gã me to alẽvi si si dzo eve le la dzi.

Eye ame dzedzeariwo aɖe wo ɖokuiwo afia abe nu gbagbe dɔmenyotɔwo tso xexe siwo le xlã ƒom le aʋatso me le woƒe aʋatso xexemenuŋununnya si nye xexe siwo le xlã ƒom le aʋatso me ɣesiaɣi si wozã le blemaɣiwo me la me. Se egɔme be le anyigba dzi la, dziƒowo kple yamenutowo kple dziƒowo ƒe dziƒo gɔ̃ hã le afi si Yahweh bɔbɔ nɔ eƒe fiazikpui dzi, eye le nyateƒe me, le nyateƒe me la, Yahweh ɖua fia ɖe eƒe nuwɔwɔwo katã dzi si eya ɖeka wɔ le gɔmedzedzea me le eya ŋutɔ ƒe lɔlɔ̃nu ƒe aɖaŋuɖoɖo megbe eye hena eya ŋutɔ ƒe tegbee tameɖoɖowo.

Eya ta, srɔ̃ nu be nàse wò xexea gɔme abe ale si wòle ŋutɔŋutɔ ene eye nàse egɔme be ɣletiviwo nye ɣletivi tsatsala siwo wona hena dzesiwo kple ɣeyiɣiwo eye womenye xexe bubuwo siwo me agbenɔnɔ ƒomevi bubuwo le o, eya ta, gbe ame dzedzeariwo ƒe aʋatsowo. Ame tutɔ siawo siwo woyɔna be amedzrowo ye nye ame siwo dze aglã ɖe Yahweh ŋu tso gɔmedzedzea me eye wonye xexe bubu me tɔwo le gɔmesesea me be woƒe dzɔtsoƒe le dziƒowo le ŋkeke si dzi Yahweh ŋutɔ wɔ wo le dzɔdzɔenyenye blibo me. Ke hã, le woƒe aglãdzedzea me la, wodze anyi tso woƒe blibonyenye gbãtɔ me, eye wotrɔ wo ɖokuiwo tegbee, eye womasi o. Ke hã amegbetɔ dzɔdzɔe lɔ̃a aʋatso siawo ale be wòate ŋu awɔ eƒe ŋutilã ʋẽa ƒe nudzodzrowo dzi le madzɔmadzɔnyenye kple madzɔmadzɔnyenye katã me, eye ame siwo katã zɔa tsɔtsrɔ̃ ƒe mɔ gbadza sia dzi la adze dzo mavɔ me to esia me.

Eye nenemae woado ahava, awɔ abe ɖe wotsɔ ɖe le amegbetɔwo ƒe nyawo me ene eye woaɖe wo ɖokuiwo afia bliboe ne woɖe mɔxela ɖa esi woƒo nye Hamea nu ƒu ɖe gbɔnye le alilikpowo me. Woado ahava agblɔ be yewova be yewoakpɔ amegbetɔwo ta tso amedzidzedze me eye yewoaɖe amegbetɔwo tso aʋawɔwɔ me eye woava kple aʋatso geɖe siwo wobla nyuie eye woƒe gbe sena nyuie. Ke hã, dzo ewoawo ƒe susu le ɖeka eye woatsɔ woƒe ŋusẽ kple acɛ afia lã wɔadã la! Eye dzo ewoawo ye nye ame siwo alé fu gbolo la, eye woawɔe aƒedoe kple amama, eye woaɖu eƒe lã, eye woatsɔ dzo afiae le woƒe xexemeʋa etɔ̃lia si le kuku ge ɖe anyigba la dzi kpuie la me. Ke hã woado ahava kple woƒe mɔ̃ɖaŋununyawo le asi me, gake aʋatso kuxi gbɔkpɔnuwo kple aʋatso mɔwo kple aʋatsokaka siwo hea tegbee ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ kple fɔbuame vɛ na ame siwo katã wɔ ɖeka kple woƒe aglãdzedzea ko wotsɔna vɛ.

Ke hã, nenemae woafɔ, lã wɔadã si tso atsiaƒu me kple lã wɔadã si tso anyigba dzi, eye woafɔ abe alẽvi si si dzo eve le si ƒoa nu abe dzalẽ ene, Dzalẽ la ƒe Alẽvi si Esau tia na eɖokui be wòaɖu anyigba dzi le madzɔmadzɔnyenye me. Dziɖuɖu aɖe si me Ashkenaz ɖua fia ɖe dukɔwo dzi sesĩe to Noahide dzi to lã wɔadã si tso atsiaƒu me dzi eye lã wɔadã si tso anyigba dzi ɖua fia ɖe anyigba kple xexeame dzi to Esau ƒe Talmud dzi le aʋatso gã me esi wòtre mɔ̃ na Yakob.

Se egɔme be lã wɔadã si tso atsiaƒu me la ɖe eɖokui ɖa tso Babilon Ɣaɣla gbɔ ale be wòaɖe eɖokui ɖa tso eƒe nuvɔ̃ siwo sɔ gbɔ la gbɔ, eye woagblẽ eƒe ŋkɔ eye awɔ abe ɖe wole nutsotso wɔm ɖe eŋu ene. Gake se egɔme be lã wɔadã si tso atsiaƒu me kple lã wɔadã si tso anyigba dzi la tso ŋunyɔnu ʋukɔ ɖeka me eye wowɔa dɔ asi le asi me, lã wɔadã eve, be woasubɔ aha na dzalẽ la ƒe nudzodzo tameɖoɖowo nava eme. Lã wɔadã si tso atsiaƒu me la afɔ le eƒe ɣeyiɣi me eye wòafɔ azu woƒe aʋawɔla si ŋugbe wodo, woƒe Mahdi si lalam wole fifia. Eye wòaɣla eƒe nɔnɔme ŋutɔŋutɔ kple eƒe va yi kple eƒe nuvɔ̃wo, togbɔ be agbagbadzedze mawo nye dzodzro hã elabena nuvɔ̃ sia nuvɔ̃ si wòwɔ kpɔ la, woŋlɔe ɖi bliboe le dziƒowo. Togbɔ be, able ame siwo dzea eyome to eƒe ŋugbedodo dzodzrowo me.

Eye esi wole tsitretsim tso aʋa ƒe afi me la, woawɔ ɖeka kple dzalẽ la kple ame dzedzeari siwo tɔ wonye xoxo la le gaglãgbe, eye woaɖe afɔ kaba aɖo wo ɖokuiwo anyi le woƒe fiaɖuƒe mlɔetɔ me, si ƒe gɔmeɖoanyi woɖo anyi xoxo, hafi woatsrɔ̃ Babilon Ɣaɣla to madzɔmadzɔnyenye ƒe nya ɣaɣla la dzi. Ke hã, woadi be yewoatu nu si nɔ anyi xoxo la esi ame dzedzeariwo le didim be yewoaɖo yewoƒe sikaɣi yeyea anyi, sikaɣi si womakpɔ gbeɖe o ke boŋ ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ sesẽ boŋ. Togbɔ be ele alea hã, woafɔ le ŋusẽ gã me eye lã wɔadã ɖeka afia asi evelia eye woaɖi ɖase le wo nɔewo ŋu eye woagblɔ busunya gã geɖewo eye woahe aʋatso dzesiwo kple nukunu geɖewo vɛ.

Togbɔ be ele alea hã, nenemae woafɔ abe ale si woŋlɔe ɖi ene. Eye Alẽvi si si dzo eve le la awɔ dɔ asi le asi me kple dzalẽ la eye woatsaka nuwo katã awɔ ɖeka le woƒe habɔbɔ yeyea me. Woatsaka woƒe ganyawo ƒe ɖoɖowo, woatsaka woƒe dziɖuɖuwo, woabla woƒe habɔbɔ yeyea me nuwo katã ɖekae eye woatsaka wo ɖokuiwo awɔ ɖeka le woƒe ɖoɖo yeyea me. Woatsaka woƒe blema busu nya ɣaɣla subɔsubɔhawo awɔ subɔsubɔha yeye aɖe si adze abe wo Fofowo siwo subɔa woƒe katsa yibɔwo ƒe subɔsubɔha ene, ke hã woatsaka woƒe subɔsubɔhawo awɔ aʋatso subɔsubɔha bubu si wo Fofowo menya o gake woƒe katsawo akpɔtɔ anɔ anyi. (Ke hã, malée ɖe eƒe fofowo ƒe Mawu ŋu o, alo nyɔnuwo ƒe nudzodzrowo ŋu o, alo atsɔ ɖe le Mawu aɖeke me o: elabena ado eɖokui ɖe dzi wu wo katã).

Se nusi woawɔ gɔme, O Israel, elabena woawɔ aʋa kple ame kɔkɔewo eye woaɖu wo dzi, eye amesiame si ayɔ Yesu Nazaretɔ si hã nye Yeshua HaMashiach ƒe ŋkɔ vaseɖe nuwuwu la, woaxɔe ɖe agbe, elabena nenemae wodo ŋugbee eye woŋlɔe ɖi. Woatsɔ woƒe nuvlowɔwɔwo aɖo amesiwo yɔa Yesu ƒe ŋkɔ la dzi eye Barabas ade Babilon Ɣaɣla la ƒe ame kɔkɔewo asi, elabena wo dometɔ geɖe tsɔ woƒe Sɔlemexɔwo ɖe adzɔgbe na lã wɔadã si tso anyigba me, amesi meva haɖe o le ŋɔŋlɔ sia ɣiɣi. Eye wòtsɔ eɖokui wɔ woƒe Aƒetɔe eye wòdzra Bibliawo le eya ŋutɔ ƒe ŋkɔ me, gake elɔ̃ ɖe edzi bɔbɔe be yemenye Kristotɔ o, togbɔ be ame geɖewo xɔe se le bometsitsi me be enye Kristotɔ hã.

Eye esi anyigba la le gbegblẽm ɖe edzi eye wòdze aʋa gã, dɔvɔ̃, dɔwuame, madzɔmadzɔnyenye kple ku me la, woatsɔ woƒe kuxi siwo woawo ŋutɔwo he vɛ la ƒe fɔbuabuwo katã aɖo Kristo ƒe nuwo dzi eye woagblɔ be amedzrowo (aliens) ye kɔ amesiwo ƒo ƒu le Kristo me la dzoe, evɔ le nyateƒe me la, manya ƒo wo nu ƒu ɖe Yahweh ƒe fiazikpui xɔ la me na ŋugbetɔsrɔ̃ la ƒe srɔ̃ɖeɖe kple eƒe ŋugbetɔ la be woaxɔ woƒe domenyinu eye woakafu wo tegbee.
