Baba Na Ame Siwo Yɔa Viviti Be Kekeli Kple Kekeli Be Viviti!

Mise miaƒe Aƒetɔ kple xɔla ƒe nyawo, nye Hame, kple nye Ŋugbetɔsrɔ̃ gɔ̃ hã, elabena nyee nye ame si naa nunya nunyalawo, be nunyala siwo le mia dome la nagadze nunya geɖe wu. Eye nyee nye gbededala si fona kɔkɔe ƒe dzo la, eya ta mise nye nyawo mi katã, eye midze klo na nye yɔyɔ hena agbe mavɔ eye midze nunya yome elabena nunya ye nye nu vevitɔ, eya ta midi nunya. Eya ta miva gbɔnye kple dzi faa kple abɔ ʋuʋu eye midze klo eye miyi edzi mitsɔm, Yesu Nazaretɔ, miaƒe Aƒetɔ mavɔ elabena megale ame siwo katã nye agbe mavɔ ƒe domenyilawo yɔm, togbɔ be ame aɖewo ƒe to ku hã.

Gake migbe xexe sia me nunya si mezu naneke o la, elabena xexeame ƒe nunya tso xexeame ƒe fiavi siwo katã woabla kpuie hena ƒe akpe ɖeka le aʋli la me le fuwɔwɔ kple tohehe me ɖe woƒe mɔwo ta la gbɔ. Elabena le kɔsɔkɔsɔ kpekpe siwo me woabla wo ɖo kpuie me gɔ̃ hã la, woase woƒe etsɔme gɔme, eye woaƒo dzodzodzoe ahatrɔ kpakpakpa hena ƒe akpe ɖeka abe woƒe mavɔmavɔ si woɖo ɖi le dzodze kple dzokpe ʋeʋẽ ƒe ta si le dzo dzim ɖaa la me nyrɔna hefia dzo le blɔtɔ dzo xɔdzo wu la me ene elabena nenemae Yawe dzrae ɖo eye nenemae woƒe domenyinu le wo lalam.

Elabena nenemae woƒe dzi ƒe didi na Mawu gbagbe la ƒe viwo le, eya ta Yawe atrɔ woƒe dzi ƒe didi la adze woawo ŋutɔwo ƒe akɔta, eye nenemae woafi dzo tegbee ɖe mavɔmavɔwo ƒe mavɔmavɔ me. Elabena vevietɔ ame siwo dze anyi la axɔ woawo ŋutɔwo ƒe mɔwo elabena woawoe nye nuvɔ̃, dzidada, vodada, kple sedzimawɔmawɔ fofowo eye woagbugbɔ woƒe ŋunyɔnuwɔwɔwo ana wo tegbee. Eye woanya ahahe fe blibo ɖe nuvɔ̃ ɖe sia ɖe si wowɔ kpɔ la ta zi gbɔ zi geɖe eye woƒe tohehe anɔ anyi, elabena nenemae wokpɔe le woƒe dziwo me eye woƒe nuwɔwɔwo nye ŋunyɔnu tegbee kple mavɔmavɔ me. Elabena nenemae Yawe ƒe mɔwo le, mɔ ka nue ame drɔ̃a ʋɔnu ame bubu ɖo la, nenema kee woadrɔ̃ ʋɔnu wo godoo eye nenemae ame siwo katã tia woawo ŋutɔwo ƒe tsɔtsrɔ̃ ɖe agbe mavɔ teƒe le nye Yesu Nazaretɔ me la ƒe dzogbese kple tohehe le elabena nyee nye Mawu ƒe Alẽvi la.

Elabena ame sia ame si nye bometsila si adze aglã ɖe nuwɔwɔ ƒe ɖoɖo ŋu, kple Yawe si wɔ agbe abe avɔ si woƒo kpe ɖekae ene ƒe dzɔdzɔme ɖoɖowo gɔ̃ hã ŋu la, akpɔ gome le woƒe tohehe me. Aglãdzedze ɖe agbe ƒe ɖoɖowo ŋu ye nye be woahe ku kple tsɔtsrɔ̃ avae na ame bubuwo, eye vɔ̃ gã kple nu gbegblẽ gã aɖe ye ame siwo katã dzea aglã le mɔ sia nu eye wodzea woawo ŋutɔwo ƒe nudzodzrowo yome hena ame bubuwo ƒe gbegblẽ la wɔna. Dzidzɔkpɔkpɔ le ame si dze anyi la ƒe aglãdzedze me ye nye be woawɔ ɖeka kple woƒe mɔwo eye ame siwo katã wɔa esia la akpɔ gome le woƒe domenyinu me tegbee le dzo mavɔwo me, eye tiatia bubu aɖeke meli o.

Elabena tso blema kple tso gɔmedzedzea me la, wona tiatia ƒe ablɔɖe amegbetɔwo eye Xawa ble Adam wòxɔe le da si blee le eƒe aɖaŋudzedze me la gbɔ. Eya ta Adam kple Xawa tia ku abe nuvɔ̃ ƒe emetsonuwo ene, elabena wowɔ amewo pɛpɛpɛ abe mawudɔlawo ene, kple tameɖoɖo be woayi edzi anɔ kɔkɔe kple dzɔdzɔe, eye ku, si tsrɔ̃a nu sia nu la, mate ŋu alé wo o.

Eye nenemae nyui kple vɔ̃ nyanya gblẽ ame siwo katã ɖui tso ɣemaɣi la. Eye nukuwo ƒe aʋa la de asi ɣli dodo kple dɔmedzoe me tso Mose Ƒe Agbalẽ Gbãtɔ kple nyɔnu la ƒe nuku si ŋugbe wodo la me, le tsɔtsrɔ̃ gã aɖe me, eye le aʋa sia me la, wometsi naneke ɖi tso eƒe emetsonuwo me o. Va de asi na egbe sia la, nuwɔwɔ bliboa katã le veve sem eye wòle hũ ɖem le vevesese me fifia gɔ̃ hã elabena woaɖe nuwɔwɔ la hã tso gbegblẽ ƒe kluvinyenye me ayi Mawu viwo ƒe ŋutikɔkɔe ƒe ablɔɖe me, eye ɖeɖe la mehe ɖe megbe o! Gake miese egɔme vinyewo be va de asi na egbe sia la, woɖu nukuwo ƒe aʋa la dzi bliboe le nye, Kristo Yesu me, Elabena wogagbugbɔ mi dzi tso nuku si megblẽna o me eye le Mawu ƒe alẽvi la me la, wowɔ avuléle ɖe amegbetɔwo ƒe nuvɔ̃wo ta zi ɖeka ɖe ɣeyiɣi mavɔ me. Eye to Yawe ƒe ŋusẽ si womate ŋu adzidze o me la, woɖu ku dzi tegbee le nye tsitretsitsi tso ame kukuwo dome me, elabena manya wɔ be ku nalém ɖe te o elabena nyemewɔ nuvɔ̃ o eye nuvɔ̃ ƒe fetue nye ku.

Gake mexe asi si womate ŋu abu eŋu o ɖe miaƒe ɖeɖe kple ablɔɖe ta, eye Mawu gbagbe la viwo akpɔ wo ɖokuiwo le ɣeyiɣi aɖe ko me wole tsitre ɖe Mawu ƒe fiazikpui xɔ me wodo agbe mavɔ tegbee. Nenemae wòle eye nenemae wòanɔ, be ame sia ame si ayɔ Aƒetɔ la ƒe ŋkɔ la, woaxɔe ɖe agbe tegbee kple mavɔmavɔ me, ne woyi edzi wɔ nuteƒe le nye ŋkɔ yɔyɔ me va se ɖe nuwuwu. Elabena ame siwo dze anyi la nye futɔ siwo dzi woɖu, eye fifia tsɔtsrɔ̃ blibo ƒe ɣeyiɣiwo va woƒe fiaɖuƒe dzi kple tohehe blibo esi wokɔ Alẽvi la ƒe dɔmedzoe kple Mawu ƒe dɔmedzoe ɖe anyi le ŋkeke siwo gbɔna me. Elabena Aƒetɔ la ƒe ŋkeke la ɖo, eya ta miɖo to nye gbe hafi woaʋu Aɖaka la ƒe ʋɔtru.

Mise nye nyawo eye miese nye nuƒo gɔme. Elabena mele nu siwo le edzi yim fifia ŋu ƒom kple aʋatso gã aɖe gɔ̃ hã si able ame tiatiawo gɔ̃ hã ne wotia be yewoahe to ɖe nye gbe nu. Fifia maƒo nu tso gbeha siwo di be yewoawɔ abe ɖe yewomele kpo siwo me nye nu xeɖotɔ dzra wo ɖo me o la ŋu. Gbehawo ƒe tɔtrɔ kple ɖoɖo siwo kplɔa tsɔtsrɔ̃ yia ŋgɔ gbe sia gbe hekplɔa anyigba la yia aʋa blibo, kple fukpekpe blibo gbɔ gɔ̃ hã, elabena nenemae wona wo be woawɔe eye Mawu dee woƒe dzi me be woawɔ eƒe lɔlɔ̃nu elabena Yawe ƒe futɔwo gɔ̃ hã ɖoa to eƒe gbe.

Hawo mlɔa woawo ŋutɔwo ƒe fĩ me, wodea ke le ba me, eye womedzea eme na wo gbeɖe o, gake woami nu sia nu si woate ŋui esi wotsɔ woƒe ŋɔtiwo le ke dem ɖe anyigba elabena anyigba dzi tɔwo wonye, eye wonye ŋkuagbãtɔwo na nu bubuwo katã negbe woƒe dɔme ƒe dzodzro kple wo fofo ƒe nudzodzrowo ko woawɔ. Elabena nenemae anyigba la dzi kplɔla siwo li fifia le, eye wodoa ɣli le vevesese me esi wole woƒe amedzɔdzɔawo ƒom. Elabena naneke mate ŋu adze ha siawo ŋu bliboe o, eye woƒe nuɖuɖuwo gblẽ wo bliboe tso ememe, elabena wo katã wonye makɔmakɔwo eye wonye gbe vɔ̃ɖi vavãwo eye nenemae woƒe dzɔdzɔme le.

Lã vɔ̃ɖi siawo nye ati gbegblẽwo eye womekpea kutsetse o, woku zi eve, eye woho wo kple woƒe ke. Agbe mavɔ manɔ wo si gbeɖe o, eye woƒe mɔwo ye nye ku mavɔ kple tsɔtsrɔ̃ ƒe mɔwo, tsɔtsrɔ̃ mavɔ gɔ̃ hã le wo Fofowo ƒe domenyinu me le dzokpe blɔtɔ gã ma me. Wodzea amedzi le dzɔdzɔme nu, eye wodea wo ɖokuiwo ŋu gbã hedi sitsoƒe esi wole ɣli dom be yewonye amedzɔdzɔawo, eye wotsɔa fuléle si wowɔna ɖe dukɔwo ŋu la fiaa ame bubuwo gɔ̃ hã. Wodoa ɣli le vevesese me esi wole ame siwo wodi be yewoatsrɔ̃ la ƒom ale be woadze abe ɖe wotso afia na wo le woƒe mɔ vɔ̃ɖiwo me ene.

Elabena womate ŋu akpɔ nublanui na lã wɔadã siawo o elabena woƒe mɔwo ye nye tsɔtsrɔ̃ ƒe mɔwo eye ame siawo awɔ wo Fofo abosam ƒe nudzodzrowo elabena eƒe nudzodzrowo ye nye woƒe dzidzɔkpɔkpɔ eye nenemae woƒe dzi dzea emee. Baba na ame siwo ɖua vɔ̃ɖinyenye ƒe azã eye woyɔa kekeli be viviti kple viviti be kekeli. Elabena woƒe mɔwo ye nye tsɔtsrɔ̃ ƒe mɔwo, eye womasi le dzo mavɔwo me gbeɖe o. Miese egɔme vinyewo be le ɣeyiɣi aɖe ko me fifia la, woaɖe hawo le kpo siwo me nuxeɖotɔ la dzra wo ɖo la me. Elabena ne woɖe nuxeɖotɔ la ɖa la, ha siawo siwo dze abe aƒemelãwo kple ame siwo mewɔa nu vevi ame o siwo wɔa wo ɖokuiwo abe Mawu ƒe tiatiawo ene la azu gbehawo enumake ne woɖe wo le nuxeɖotɔwo ƒe asiléle si womate ŋu agbã o me, elabena nenemae woƒe dzɔdzɔme vavãtɔ le.

Woadze anyigba geɖewo dzi eye woami nu sia nu si wokpɔ. Woaɖu ame bubuwo ƒe ku kple tsɔtsrɔ̃, elabena nenemae woƒe dzɔdzɔme le. Wotsa le teƒeteƒewo, eye woƒe ɖoɖowo kple hadome ɖoɖowo le wo si, elabena Yawe naa nunya kple ɖoɖo ƒe seƒe aɖe hawo gɔ̃ hã, elabena Yawe ƒe mɔwo le dzɔdzɔe eye wole dzɔdzɔe. Ha siawo mesea veve ɖe nu ŋu o, seselelãme aɖeke mele wo si o, eye kuxi aɖeke mele wo si be woaɖu ame kukuwo gɔ̃ hã be wòasɔ kple woƒe nuɖuɖu ƒe dzodzro o, eye woaɖu ame gbagbewo gɔ̃ hã ne esɔ kple woƒe tameɖoɖowo. Kpɔ ɖa gaƒoƒoa de fifia afi si woaɖe mɔ na hawo le eye ne wowɔe la, woaɖu anyigba la, woami anyigba la ƒe ami, woatsrɔ̃ ŋutifafa bliboe tso anyigba dzi eye woaɖu nu ahami le aʋa blibo ƒe ʋukɔkɔɖi me. Elabena miaƒe modzakaɖela vɔ̃ɖiwo gɔ̃ hã dzina woƒe mɔwo, eye woƒe mɔwo menye ŋaɣi o.

Wowɔ gbeha siawo hena tsɔtsrɔ̃ kple anyigba la ƒe fĩ mi ami hena anyigba la kɔkɔ hena Yawe ƒe tameɖoɖowo, emegbe woabla wo ahatsɔ wo aƒu gbe ɖe dzo mavɔwo me tegbee. Elabena woƒe nuvɔ̃wo lolo, eye woƒe nuvɔ̃wo sɔ gbɔ. Gake miese egɔme be wodzra ha siawo ɖo ɖe woƒe kpo me hena ɣeyiɣi siwo woɖo eye ame geɖewo wɔa wo ɖokuiwo abe ɖe woɖo wo kɔkɔe zãnuwo hena woƒe fiaɖuƒe si le tsitrem ene. Eye fifia ye nye ɣeyiɣi si woaɖe wo asi, eye fifia woaɖe woƒe dzɔdzɔme vavãtɔ si wotsyɔ nu edzi le awu siwo dze abe mawusosroɖa tɔwo ene me la afia, eye Saturn ƒe kpe dzẽ yibɔ la le woƒe ɣletivi la dome eye wòle ŋkuwo dzi gɔ̃ hã. Wodia mɔ be yewoaɖe yewo ɖokuiwo ɖa elabena wotia yewo ɖokuiwo be yewonye Mawu ŋutɔ tɔ. Gake ame aɖeke mevaa Yawe gbɔ o negbe to nye, Yesu Nazaretɔ, ame si wo Fofo gbe eye ame siwo tsɔm Aƒetɔ la ye nye Mawu ƒe Israel vavãtɔ eye woagbugbɔ wo awɔ bliboe le nye me abe ame yeye ɖeka ene, eɖanye Yudeatɔ alo Trɔ̃subɔla o, eɖanye kluvi alo ablɔɖevi o. Woaɖe Edomtɔwo ƒe gbe vɔ̃ɖi siawo afia bliboe ɖe aʋatsokaka siwo wonye ta eye woaɖe wo ɖa bliboe le anyigba la kple anyigba bliboa dzi le ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ gã kple tohehe gã me.

Gake hafi woaɖe wo ɖa la, woaɖe mɔ na woƒe ʋu ƒe dzodzro esi woɖe woƒe dzɔdzɔme vavãtɔ fia be ame katã nakpɔ, elabena fifia wotsyɔa wo ɖokuiwo dzi le subɔsubɔhawo ƒe atsyɔ̃ɖoɖowo me. Woɖua gbeɖuɖɔ, woɖua ame kukuwo, wodzea ame gbagbewo dzi hemi wo elabena woƒe ʋu ƒe dzodzro lolo eye womate ŋu aɖi ƒo na wo o! Eye le ɣeyiɣi siawo katã me la, wowɔa wo ɖokuiwo abe kɔkɔetɔwo ene, wowɔa wo ɖokuiwo abe anyigba la dzi tɔwo ene eye wonye domenyila gomekpɔtɔwo, eye wowɔa wo ɖokuiwo abe ɖe yewomenye amedzelawo o ene elabena woƒe dzɔdzɔme nɔƒee nye gbedzi ke menye ŋugbedodonyigba la o. Eye nenemae ha ƒe dzɔdzɔme le elabena ne wogbugbɔ wo yi gbedzi ko la, woƒe gbedzi dzɔdzɔme vavãtɔ dzena gaglãa. Gake, wowɔa wo ɖokuiwo abe ɖe wole ame siwo wodi be yewoaɖu dzi la ƒe amenyenye me ene eye woaho wo ɖa bliboe le anyigba la dzi eye ke alo alɔ aɖeke mele wo si o elabena nenemae Yawe do ŋugbee. Eya ta miese egɔme be ha siawo dzea abe ŋugbedodonyigba la ƒe kplɔla dɔmenyotɔwo ene evɔ le nyateƒe me la, wonye lã wɔadã siwo kpɔa dzidzɔ ɖe ametsɔtsrɔ̃ ŋu eye woadi bliboe be yewoatsrɔ̃ nye, Kristo Yesu ƒe nu sia nu, eye woade Anyigba la dzi tɔ siwo dzea woƒe Talmud ƒe ɖoɖowo yome yi tsɔtsrɔ̃ me gɔ̃ hã asi eye gake anyigba la ƒe ame susɔeawo ava gbɔnye, Yeshua HaMashiach, elabena maxɔ Yakob ɖe agbe kokoko!

Baba na ame siwo yɔa viviti be kekeli kple kekeli be viviti! Ame siwo dzea Yawe, kple Yeshua HaMashiach dzi to Mawu Gbagbe la viwo dzi dzedze me gɔ̃ hã. Elabena esi wowɔe na nɔvinye suetɔwo dometɔ ɖeka la, wowɔe nam, eye hlãbiabia nye tɔnye, Aƒetɔ mavɔ lae gblɔe, eye maxe fe. Eya ta miʋu miaƒe ŋkuwo, vinyewo, eye migbe ame siwo kɔa Remfan ƒe ɣletivi ɖe woƒe aflaga dzi! Elabena ne miede ha kpli wo la, efia be mianɔ Molok ƒe agbadɔ me eye miadze klo ɖe woƒe Bafomet ŋkume! Nyemegblɔ na mi be miabu lã wɔadã la ƒe xexlẽme ŋu o, eye woɖe xexlẽme ma fia kple dada le nɔnɔme ma si wowɔ na wo ɖokuiwo be woasubɔ woƒe ɣletivi mawu la me, elabena woƒe kpe dzẽ la le woƒe ɣletivi la dome! Miese lododo sia gɔme, elabena nu teƒe ade kplɔa ame yia dzogoe etɔ̃ ade siwo wɔa dzogoe ade eye woƒe kpe dzẽ la nɔa eme, eya ta miabu lã wɔadã la ƒe xexlẽme ŋu eye midzudzɔ Edomtɔwo ƒe aʋatsokakawo dzi xɔxɔ se elabena wosubɔa ame siwo dze anyi la eye woƒe mawue nye dzoka la.

Eye tso Noa ƒe ŋkekeawo me, le tsiɖɔɖɔa megbe gɔ̃ hã la, woxe mɔ na Nefilimwo ƒe gbɔgbɔ siwo me ŋutilã mele o la ƒe akpa asieke le ewo me le aʋli la me, elabena Noa do gbe ɖa ɖe tɔgbuiyɔvi siwo wodze dzi eye woɖu wo dzi la ta. Eye nenemae Yawe de bubu Noa ƒe biabia ŋu hebla ame vɔ̃ɖitɔwo kple ame siwo ƒo ɖi wu, eye wodzra woƒe xexlẽme ƒe akpa asieke le ewo me ɖo ɖe aʋli la me tso ɣemaɣi. Mise nye nya eye mise nye nya nyuie, Vinyewo, eye miese miaƒe Aƒetɔ ƒe gbe gɔme. Le nuteƒewɔlawo ƒe ƒuƒoƒo le nye Hame kple nye Ŋugbetɔsrɔ̃ gɔ̃ hã gbɔnye le alilikpowo me la, woaɖe nuxeɖotɔ la ɖa, eye woaʋu aʋli la bliboe.

Womagaɖo te vɔ̃ɖinyenye o, elabena Yawe gaɖo te Satana ƒe fiaɖuƒe blibo la ale be woate ŋu axɔ ame siwo nye agbe mavɔ ƒe ɖeɖekpɔkpɔ ƒe domenyilawo ɖe agbe, ame siwo tsɔm woƒe Aƒetɔ le nye amenuveve kple nublanuikpɔkpɔ ƒe ɣeyiɣiwo me gɔ̃ hã. Esi amegbetɔwo gbe nye fifia ta la, womagaɖo te vɔ̃ɖinyenye tso amegbetɔwo gbɔ o. Miese egɔme be woaɖe wo katã asi, gbɔgbɔ siawo ƒe ameha gã aɖe gɔ̃ hã, ameha gã aɖe ŋutɔ si aɖu anyigba la dzi bliboe kple vɔ̃ɖinyenye si womaxe mɔ na o kple vɔ̃ɖinyenye si womate ŋu agblɔ o. Elabena agbalẽŋlɔla Henok ŋlɔ nu tso esiawo ŋu be: Eye dzotɔwo ƒe gbɔgbɔwo wɔa fu ame, wɔa ameteteɖeanyi, tsrɔ̃a nu, dzea amedzi, wɔa aʋa, eye wowɔa tsɔtsrɔ̃dɔ le anyigba dzi, eye wohea kuxiwo vɛ: womeɖua nu o, gake dɔ kple tsikɔ wua wo, eye wohea nukikliwo vɛ. Eye gbɔgbɔ siawo atso ɖe amegbetɔviwo kple nyɔnuwo ŋu, elabena wotso wo me.

Miexlẽe le nye nya kɔkɔe la me afi si mezɔ mɔ to Gadarenetɔwo ƒe nuto me o? Elabena menya gbɔgbɔwo do goe le ame eve siwo me wonɔ la me, eye nenemae meɖe mɔ na gbɔgbɔ vɔ̃ siawo be woage ɖe haha aɖe si le teƒe ma la me. Miese egɔme be wole Dzoka la ƒe fiaɖuƒe blibo la ɖe ge asi hena ɣeyiɣi kpui aɖe, be woanya ame siwo woɖo ɖi na agbe mavɔ la ayi nye abɔ siwo xɔa ame ɖe agbe la me! Elabena woaɖe gbɔgbɔ siawo siwo wode gaxɔ me la asi be woawɔ ɖeka kple Satana ƒe fiaɖuƒe bliboe le anyigba dzi. Eye woage ɖe hawo me ake. Elabena wowɔa aʋa kple amewo eye woaɖu amewo dzi elabena womate ŋu aɖi ƒo na woƒe nudzodzrowo gbeɖe o eye gbɔgbɔ siawo ye nye ame siwo doa ŋusẽ hawo eye wowɔa dɔ aduadu kple hawo eye gbeha siawo ye nye woƒe ʋu.

Menye woawo ko o ke ame siwo katã axɔ woƒe lã wɔadã ƒe dzesi la hã azu ʋu siwo lɔ̃ faa na gbɔgbɔ siawo siwo awɔ aʋa kple amegbetɔwo ake. Elabena woagami dzɔdzɔme me amegbetɔ ake le ɣeyiɣi mamlɛ siawo me, elabena nenemae woɖoe be wòadzɔ le nuwuwu ɣeyiɣi siawo me. Eye nuwuwu la dze egɔme xoxo eye wòle vavam bliboe kpuie ŋutɔ esi Yakob ƒe xaxa dze anyigba la dzi. Vidzidzi ƒe vevesesewo te tu kpuie ŋutɔ fifia be womedzudzɔna o eye Yawe wɔ dzesi geɖewo le anyigba dzi kple dzesi kple nukunu geɖewo le yame kple dziƒowo kple anyigba dzi, gake ame geɖewo gatsɔa xɔsemanɔmee ƒe susu ɖea nuxlɔ̃ame siawo ɖa.

Kpɔ ɖa! Le esi teƒe be amehawo nava dzimetɔtrɔ me la, nudzodzoeha siwo xɔ ame geɖewo kple dukɔ geɖewo dzi le anyigba bliboa dzi la gɔ̃ hã doa modzaka na wo. Wokoa alɔgbɔnu hekoa nu le nuxlɔ̃ame siwo Yawe na anyigba la ŋu elabena Mawu mavɔ la atso le eƒe nɔƒe eye wòaɖe eɖokui afia eye ame siwo dze anyi la ƒoa dzodzodzoe elabena woƒe ɣeyiɣiwo de, eye vɔvɔ̃ mavɔ lé wo. Elabena mi ame siwo dze anyi la mibui be miate ŋu atsɔ aɖaŋudzedze alé Mawu mavɔ la, evɔ nu ma tɔgbi manya wɔ o. Elabena ɣeyiɣi ka mee zãnu siwo me bubu mele o la tso be yewoawɔ aʋa ahalé zeŋmela la le aɖaŋudzedze me? Gake nenemae ame siwo dze anyi la ƒe tsukuku kple dada le, eye womasi le woƒe kpo si le dzo dzim ɖaa la me o.

Eya ta miese ɣeyiɣi siwo me miele la gɔme eye minɔ nye, nye Hame, kple nye Ŋugbetɔsrɔ̃ gɔ̃ hã me! Elabena miaƒe ɖeɖekpɔkpɔ blibo kple domenyinu blibo mehe ɖe megbe o, eye womagblẽ mi ɖi le hawo dome o elabena miexɔ nye ŋkɔ dzi se le amenuveve kple nublanuikpɔkpɔ ƒe ŋkeke siawo me, eye nenemae amenuveve kple nublanuikpɔkpɔ mavɔ anɔ mia si tegbee. Eye mia nɔviwo awɔ ɖeka kpli mi le mavɔmavɔ me kple nye Fiaɖuƒe si gbɔna si nye dzɔdzɔenyenye me, ame siwo katã medzra ɖo ɖe nye dzedze ŋu o gɔ̃ hã.

Elabena nyee nye klɔla si fona kɔkɔe ƒe dzo la be maklɔ Yawe ƒe kesinɔnuwo ŋu, aɖe gbegblẽawo ɖa, eye nyee nye ame si bɔbɔ nɔ anyi abe gbededala si lé akpɔkplɔ ɖe asi hefona dzokakawo eye wòxatsaa Yawe ƒe ga xɔasiwo hekua wo be wòahe ŋusẽ mavɔ kple ŋusẽ si nu metsina o vɛ. Ameha gã aɖe, ameha gãwo gɔ̃ hã, ava gbɔnye to Yakob ƒe xaxa me, eye woakpɔ fetu gã aɖe, eye wonye ɖeɖekpɔkpɔ mavɔ ƒe domenyilawo eye woayi dzidzɔ mavɔ me eye woaxɔ Israel ɖe agbe! Eya ta migavɔ̃ o vinyewo, eye migatsi dzi ɖe miaƒe lɔlɔ̃tɔwo ŋu o eye mixɔ nye Kristo Yesu dzi se, elabena mate ŋu axɔ ame siwo katã yɔa nye ŋkɔ la ɖe agbe bliboe, eye womate ŋu ahe ame aɖeke si woɖo ɖi na agbe mavɔ la le nye asi me o, eye maxɔ ame geɖewo tso dzo mavɔwo me, ahe wo tso dzo la me gɔ̃ hã.

Nye Yeshua HaMashiach ye dɔ nye subɔla wòtsɔ nya siawo ɖo ɖa, eye to nye subɔla dzi ye woɖo nya siawo ɖa!

Nyee nye ame si naa nunya nunyalawo, be nunyala nagadze nunya geɖe wu.

Nyee nye Mawu ƒe Alẽvi si gbɔna abe Yuda Ƒomea ƒe Dzata ene.

Nyemewɔ nuvɔ̃ o, gake mexe asi si womate ŋu abu eŋu o ɖe miaƒe ɖeɖe kple ablɔɖe ta.

Nyee nye ame si bɔbɔ nɔ anyi abe gbededala si xatsaa Yawe ƒe kesinɔnuwo ƒe ga xɔasiwo hekua wo ene.

Mate ŋu axɔ ame siwo katã yɔa nye ŋkɔ la ɖe agbe bliboe, eye womate ŋu ahe ame aɖeke si woɖo ɖi na agbe mavɔ la le nye asi me o.

Minɔ ŋudzɔ ɖe nye dzedze ŋu, elabena ɣeyiɣiawo de. Ɣeyiɣi si susɔ la wonae be wòasubɔ Yawe ƒe tameɖoɖowo ko vinyewo, eya ta migaɖe mɔ woafa akɔ na mi miadɔ alɔ̃ ake o, miŋe aɖaba aƒu nye nunya nyawo kple nuxlɔ̃ame geɖewo gɔ̃ hã dzi.

Nunyala kpɔa dɔvɔ̃ la eye wòɣlaa eɖokui, ke bometsilawo ya yia edzi kokoko, eye wokpea fu. Nyemeyɔ mi ɖe Yawe ƒe nunya si nɔa anyi ɖaa la me oa?

Eye nyee nye ame si aɣla mi ɖe afi si miate ŋu aɣla mia ɖokuiwo ɖo gbeɖe o, elabena maɣla mi ɖe Yawe ƒe fiazikpui xɔ me le dziƒowo ƒe dziƒo gɔ̃ hã afi si miage ɖe miaƒe Aƒetɔ ƒe dzidzɔkpɔkpɔ me eye dziƒowo ƒe kplɔ̃ɖoxɔwo aɖu azã na mi katã, elabena kpuie wu ale si miesusu la miatsɔ mia ŋutɔwo ƒe ŋkuwo akpɔ gãnyenye, ŋutikɔkɔe mavɔ si le mia lalam le miaƒe domenyinu me. Miyi edzi kokoko le nye lɔlɔ̃ me, elabena Fofonye lɔ̃ mi ŋutɔ eye mienye nye ŋku me vi, ame siwo yɔyɔ be nɔvinye ŋu mekpea ŋu nam o.

Nye Yeshua HaMashiach meƒo nu heɖo nya siawo ɖa eye nenemae woŋlɔ wo ɖi eye woɖe Nyanyui Mavɔ la fia le eƒe ɣeyiɣi me.
