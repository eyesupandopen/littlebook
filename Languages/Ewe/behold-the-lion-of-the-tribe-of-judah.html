Kpeɖodzi Eve: Israel
Vevẽ Kple Vivi La
Adeɛmekpɔxe 17, 2024

Kpɔ ɖa! Yuda Ƒomea Ƒe Dzata La!

Ʋu to wòwo, eye na maʋu ŋku wòwo, O Israel, eye nyɔ tso wò ŋkuagbãnvui me eye nàse wò Mesia vavã la ƒe gbe, si nye Yuda Ƒomea Ƒe Dzata la, elabena nenema menye. Eya ta se Yeshua HaMashiach ƒe nyawo gɔme, eya ame si gbɔ wò nya le, O Israel, elabena nenema menye.

Vavã, vavã, nɔviŋyewo, mise nye nyawo ne miase ɣeyiɣi siwo me miele la gɔme eye miase miaƒe yɔyɔ ƒe mɔkpɔkpɔ si le trɔtrɔm gbɔna ŋugbedodonyigba la dzi la gɔme, elabena nyee nye miaƒe yɔyɔ ƒe mɔkpɔkpɔ, eye mele mi yɔm, O Israel, be miase nu si gbɔna la gɔme kple dziku si woaɖe asi le le ɣeyiɣi siawo siwo me miele la hã me. Eye nyee nye Yuda Ƒomea Ƒe Dzata eye le dzɔdzɔenyenye me la, meva be madrɔ̃ ʋɔnu eye mawɔ aʋa, eya ta se nye Nyawo ƒe vevienyenye gɔme, eya ame si gbɔ wò nya le la ƒe Nyawo, elabena nenemae Yahweh wɔm be manye.

Elabena nyee nye nukikli kpe ma, nukikli kpe gã ma si dzi ame maɖixɔsewo katã aklia nu le, elabena ame dzɔdzɔewo maganɔ agbe ɖe Mose nu o ke boŋ ame dzɔdzɔewo anɔ agbe le xɔse me! Elabena Mose le wò Mesia vavã la me, eya ame si lé se si dzi Israel mate ŋu awɔ ɖo o la me ɖe asi pɛpɛpɛ, kple Adam ƒe dzidzimeviwo katã hã mate ŋu awɔ edzi o le nu vɔ̃ si le miaƒe ŋutilã gbegblẽa me ta. Eye nyee nye ame si xe Mose ƒe fe la esime metsɔ ɖokuinye na Yahweh abe Mawu ƒe alẽvi ene le avulédɔ mavɔ me elabena nu vɔ̃ wɔlawo ƒe fe menye tɔnye be maxe o, gake le nye lɔlɔ̃ na Yahweh, kple nye lɔlɔ̃ na amegbetɔƒomea ta la, mexe miaƒe fewo katã. Mexe fuwɔame gãwo, vevesese, kple ku ƒe fewo katã eye Yahweh xɔ nye vɔsa la tegbee.

Eya ta se egɔme, wole asi ɖe ge le Alẽvi la ƒe dziku la dzi kpuie eye wole dzadzraɖo ɖi be woaku kpẽawo, eye kpɔ ɖa, Mawu ƒe dziku ƒe agbawo le aƒe tum godoo eye wole wò ʋɔtru nu, eya ta, se nye gbe be wò kple viwòwo miate ŋu anɔ agbe eye miasi le mɔ̃ si Barabbas le ɖoɖom na nye dukɔ la nu, ʋɔdã la ƒe alẽvi si Esau tia be wòanye yewoƒe Moshiach, ame si hã nye gbedoxɔtula lã wɔadã. Eye wòado abe alẽvi si dzo eve le eŋu ene, eye wòva be yeama anyigbawo ɖe edzi tso nɔla vavã siwo wonya dzoe le woƒe ametsɔtsrɔ̃ me la gbɔ, be yeamae na aƒemadzinɔla siwo tso Esau gbɔ siwo tsɔ aʋatsonyawo do ɖe ŋgɔ, kple amedzidzela siwo ʋɔdã la dɔ ɖa siwo nye Askenaz ƒe dzidzimeviwo ke menye Yakob tɔwo o gake wotsɔ wo ɖokuiwo kpe ɖe Esau ŋu kple woƒe Talmud mɔnuwo.

Eye nyee nye ame si Yahweh dɔ ɖa abe Mawu ƒe alẽvi ene, abe ŋutifafa nubabla deblibo ene, abe miaƒe Ŋutitoto deblibo ene eye wònye vɔsa mamlɛtɔ na amegbetɔƒomea ƒe nu vɔ̃wo zi ɖeka, na amewo katã, eye tegbee eye vɔsa aɖeke megale nu vɔ̃wo ta o. Eye meva tɔnyewo gbɔ le lɔlɔ̃ kple ŋutifafa me, ame siwo gbe nye vɔsa la gɔ̃ hã, gake wodɔm abe Ŋutitoto deblibo ene le toɖoɖo Yahweh ƒe lɔlɔ̃nu me, eye nyee nye Mawu ƒe alẽvi, si Yahweh na pɛpɛpɛ ɖe miaƒe nu vɔ̃wo ta, eya ta se nye gbe O Israel eye nàse egɔme be nyemagava anyigba dzi abe Mawu ƒe alẽvi ene o, eye woaɖe ŋutifafa ɖa le anyigba dzi le ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ si va la me. Ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ si woaɖe asi le le Mawu ƒe aƒe la dzi gbã elabena ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ dzea egɔme tso Mawu ƒe aƒe la me.

Elabena woakɔ nuteƒewɔla siwo le Kristo me la ayi dziƒowo, eye woagblẽ xuxoewo ɖi na klẽlawo ƒe dzo hena ŋutilã la tsɔtsrɔ̃, ale be woate ŋu axɔ gbɔgbɔ la ɖe agbe gake abe dzo me ene. Eye ameha gãwo ava Yesu Nazaret tɔ la gbɔ tso dukɔwo kple ƒomewo kple gbegbɔgblɔwo katã me eye makɔ wo ŋu le dzɔdzɔenyenye me eye maxɔ wo ɖe ɖokuinye gbɔ eye magawɔ wo yeyee le dzɔdzɔenyenye kple kɔkɔenyenye vavã me eye woƒe fetu la, womate ŋu atsɔe asɔ kple fukpekpe siwo me woato la o, elabena woƒe fetu ƒe kpekpeme aƒo amegbetɔwo ƒe mɔkpɔkpɔwo ta sãsãsã eye woanɔ agbe tegbee le ŋutifafa deblibo kple kɔkɔenyenye kple dzɔdzɔenyenye vavã me.

Elabena woŋlɔe ɖi le wo ŋu eye nenemae wòanɔ: Ame siawoe nye ame siwo tso xaxa gã la me va, eye wonya woƒe awutewui didiwo, eye wona woƒe awutewui didiwo fu ɣi le Alẽvi la ƒe ʋu la me. Eya ta, wole Mawu ƒe fiazikpui la ŋkume, eye wosubɔnɛ zã kple keli le eƒe Gbedoxɔ me, eye ame si bɔbɔ nɔ fiazikpui la dzi la anɔ wo dome. Dɔ magawui wo o, tsikɔ magawui wo o, ɣe magaʋã wo o, alo dzoxɔxɔ aɖeke o. Elabena Alẽvi la, si le fiazikpui la titina la, aɖu wo dzi, eye wòakplɔ wo ayi agbetsidzɔƒewo, eye Mawu atutu aɖatsiwo katã ɖa le woƒe ŋkuwo me.

Eya ta se egɔme be nyee nye ame si wu nye dɔwɔwɔwo nu le trɔ̃subɔlawo dome eye nye amenuveve kple nublanuikpɔkpɔ ƒe ɣeyiɣiwo nu va yi, togbɔ be amenuveve kple nublanuikpɔkpɔ le gbɔnye tegbee hã, eye Yahweh ŋutɔ gblɔ be yeakpɔ nublanui na ame si yeakpɔ nublanui na, eya ta amenuveve kple nublanuikpɔkpɔ nɔa anyi tegbee vavã. Vavã, vavã, meyi trɔ̃subɔlawo gbɔ abe aboyome si tɔnyewo gbe ene ame siwo Esau kplɔ yi kluvinyenye me na eƒe kɔnu gbegblẽ siwo me wodi be yewoagblẽ Yahweh ƒe Nya mavɔ kple tegbee tɔwo dome le, si gbegblẽ mate ŋu awɔ o. Gake wowɔ woƒe dzixɔse vɔ̃ɖiwo le woƒe agbalẽŋlɔla siwo tsɔ ame vɔ̃ɖiwo ƒe kɔnuwo de agbalẽ si wosusu be enye kɔkɔe la me, gake aleke agbalẽ si me busunyawo le la awɔ anye kɔkɔe? Vavã, vavã, baba na ame siwo yɔa viviti be kekeli eye woyɔa kekeli be viviti, elabena woƒe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ mehe ɖe megbe o!

Eye nye ɣeyiɣiwo le trɔ̃subɔlawo dome nu va yi elabena wodze nu vɔ̃ mawo ke siwo me mia Fofowo dze esime wogbe Mawu ƒe alẽvi si Yahweh dɔ ɖe wo gbɔ si medze na wo o abe ŋutifafa nubabla mavɔ na dukɔwo ene, ame siwo me ameha gã aɖe va xɔxɔ me. Eye azɔ ame siwo anye ne wozu ŋugbedodoviwo le Mawu ƒe Israel dome la do wo ɖokuiwo ɖe dzi ɖe alɔ dzɔdzɔeawo ŋu, le Amiti dzɔdzɔea gɔ̃ hã ŋu si ŋu wotsɔ wo do ɖo esime wolã alɔ dzɔdzɔeawo ɖa le dzimaɖitsitsi ma ke me.

Gake woɖe wo tso gbe amiti me, ati si mɔkpɔkpɔ alo ŋugbedodo aɖeke mele eŋu o eye ame siwo va gbɔnye hetsɔm woƒe dzɔdzɔenyenye ƒe Aƒetɔ mavɔ le ŋutifafa, amenuveve, kple nublanuikpɔkpɔ ƒe ɣeyiɣiwo me la, woxɔ wo azɔ de Mawu ƒe Israel me eye wokpɔ gome blibo le ŋugbedodo siwo wowɔ na Fofoawo me eye ame siawo dometɔ geɖe asubɔm tegbee abe nye ŋutilã ene eye matsɔ wo na ɖokuinye abe nye ŋugbetɔ ene eye made dzesi nye ame 144,000 siwo kɔ wo ɖokuiwo ŋu le nye avuléʋu la me kɔkɔe abe ɖetugbui siwo gbolo gã la meƒo ɖi o ene. Eye to trɔ̃subɔlawo ƒe xɔxɔ me la, woyra dukɔwo eye le nyateƒe me la, to siwo bu la dometɔ siwo ɖo to nye gbe la va xɔxɔ me le woƒe Aƒetɔ me eye woanɔ agbe tegbee le towo dome le Mawu ƒe Israel me! Eya ta se egɔme, maxɔ Adam ƒe dzidzimeviwo dometɔ aɖewo ɖe agbe tso afi sia afi si wotsa le le anyigba dzi le eƒe tohehe ƒe ŋkekewo me.

Elabena Yahweh wɔm agbenagbɔgbɔe, eye nyemeganyaa ŋutilã la o. Vavã, vavã, nyee nye ame si lã gbe amiti la ɖa azɔ le woƒe dzimaɖitsitsi ta eye le esi wodze nye nubabla, nye Nubabla Yeyea gɔ̃ hã dzi ta. Eye eya ta elabena wòle be Aƒetɔ ɖeka nanɔ wo si, nyee, Kristo Yesu Aƒetɔwo ƒe Aƒetɔ kple Fiawo ƒe Fia eye nye aƒetɔnyenye me koe woƒe mɔkpɔkpɔ le. Gake wotsɔ mɔkpɔkpɔ dzodzro na wo ɖokuiwo le Barabbas me eye wotsɔ woƒe mɔkpɔkpɔwo ɖo aƒetɔ aʋatsonɔtɔ aɖe si dze woƒe Hameawo dzi kple ŋutilãme kesinɔnuwo ƒe mɔkpɔkpɔ dzodzrowo me, Xɔla aʋatsonɔtɔ aɖe si do ŋugbe be yeaxɔ Babilon Ɣaɣla la gake ewɔa dɔ le ɣaɣla me hena Babilon Ɣaɣla la tsɔtsrɔ̃. Alakpatɔ ma ke si nye lã wɔadã si tso anyigba me si afɔ abe Esau ƒe Moshiach aʋatsonɔtɔ ene, ʋɔdã la ƒe alẽvi si afɔ si dzo eve le eŋu. Eye ale nye Laodikea Hame si me woge ɖo la da mɔ eye wole lɔlɔ̃m ɖe woawo ŋutɔ ƒe ku dzi azɔ, elabena Barabbas hea ku vɛ ko, eye wòatsrɔ̃ gbolo gã ma, Babilon Ɣaɣla la, si ƒe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ va le eƒe vɔ̃ɖinyenye kple gbegblẽ blibo si wòtsɔ gblẽ anyigba la le eƒe ahasiwɔwɔ me la ta.

Eya ta wotia ame si he woƒe tsɔtsrɔ̃ vɛ la na wo ɖokuiwo le aglãdzedze me abe woƒe xɔla aʋatsonɔtɔ ene, elabena Satana ƒe Ƒuƒoƒo la nye alakpatɔwo siwo katã wɔa alakpa. Eya ta srɔ̃ nu, O Israel, nu si tututu Barabbas le ge aɖo la elabena edzra wo de wo futɔwo si me, eye wòawɔ eya ŋutɔ ƒe ta me abi aʋatso tɔ eye wòaɣla eɖokui ɖe anyigba me tso afɔku siwo le dzedze ge la me. Eye wòafɔ le aʋatso gã me abe Moshiach aʋatsonɔtɔ ene na Israel elabena woawɔ woƒe aʋa, woƒe aʋa etɔ̃lia gɔ̃ hã, eye wòanye etɔ̃awo dometɔ si nyo o wu. Nenema ke, elabena nenema woŋlɔe ɖi eye nenema woawɔe: Eye mekpɔ lã wɔadã aɖe wòdo tso atsiaƒu me, ta adre kple dzo ewo le eŋu, eye fiafiakuku ewo le eƒe dzowo dzi, eye busuŋkɔ le eƒe ta dzi. Eye lã wɔadã si mekpɔ la le abe lãklẽ ene, eye eƒe afɔwo le abe gbedzi ene, eye eƒe nu le abe Dzata ƒe nu ene: eye ʋɔdã la tsɔ eƒe ŋusẽ kple eƒe fiazikpui, kple ŋusẽ gã nɛ. Eye mekpɔ eƒe tawo dometɔ ɖeka abe ɖe woxɔ abi ku tɔ ene, gake eƒe ku bi la ku, eye xexeame katã ƒo nu kpe hekplɔ lã wɔadã la ɖo.

Eye ale wòle be nya siwo le ŋɔŋlɔawo me la ɖe eƒe aʋatsowɔwɔ fia, Nya siwo metsɔ na Yohanes Nyaɖela la le yubile geɖe siawo siwo va yi me. Eye adze abe Barabbas axɔ ta me abi ene, gake aka aʋatso aheble xexeame si axɔ eƒe aʋatso dzesiwo kple nukunuwo dzi ase bliboe elabena nenema wonae be wòahe ame siwo katã kpɔa dzidzɔ le madzɔmadzɔnyenye me la ava eɖokui gbɔ le aʋatso gã me, elabena eyae nye woƒe gbegblẽla kple tsrɔ̃la. Eye le eƒe asiwo me la, ehe da ƒe aɖuɖu vɛ, lã wɔadã ƒe abui ma gɔ̃ hã, eye woatsaka ga kple tsu togbɔ be womaku ɖe wo nɔewo ŋu o hã, gake ale ʋɔdã la ƒe alẽvi la afɔ. Eye wòafɔ ɖe ame dzedzea ƒe lã wɔadã ƒe ɖoɖo mamlɛtɔ dzi, si anye woƒe fiaɖuƒe mamlɛtɔ si aɖu anyigba dzi tso ŋugbedodonyigba la dzi. Gake mafɔ eɖeka o, ke boŋ afɔ kple lã wɔadã si tso atsiaƒu me la, eye woaɖu woƒe fiawo ƒe nubabla siwo tsɔ woƒe ŋusẽ kple acɛ blibo na ʋɔdã la dzi elabena wo katã wosubɔa ʋɔdã la eye eya ŋue wotsɔ wo ɖokuiwo na bliboe elabena eyae nye wo Fofo, eya ame si ƒã eƒe gbe vɔ̃ɖiwo ɖe Yahweh ƒe luwo dome, eye maho wo ɖa keŋkeŋ atsɔ wo aƒu gbe ɖe dzo me.

Eye ale ʋɔdã la ƒe alẽvi la afɔ le aʋatso blibo me eye wòaɖe aʋatso dzesi kple nukunu geɖewo afia atsɔ aɖo kpe eƒe aʋatso nyagblɔɖiwo dzi, eye wòagblɔ adegbe kple busunya geɖewo le eɖokui ŋu gɔ̃ hã agblɔ be ye ŋutɔ yenye Mawu. Eye lã wɔadã ɖeka afia asi evelia eye woaɖu dzi ɖekae, le ʋɔdã la ƒe ɖoɖowo nu si wòatsɔ aɖu eƒe fiaɖuƒe vɔ̃ɖi la dzi ɣeyiɣi kpui aɖe. Eye woawɔ nu siwo katã ku ɖe Kristotɔnyenye ŋu la be wòanye se dzi dada, eye woaxe mɔ ɖe nuŋɔŋlɔ kɔkɔeawo nu, eye wotsɔe de wo asi be woawɔ aʋa kple kɔkɔeawo ahaɖu wo dzi, eye ale woawɔe. Gake woaƒo xexeame ƒe subɔsubɔhawo nu ƒu ale be wo katã woasubɔ woƒe kpe yibɔawo elabena le Satana ƒe afɔ nu la, wowɔ ɖeka le aglãdzedze mamlɛtɔ me eye womasi le nye asi sesẽ la nu o, miaƒe Mesia mavɔ la, Yeshua HaMashiach ye gblɔe!

Gake le woƒe fuléle na nu siwo katã ku ɖe Kristo Yesu ŋu ta la, woana xɔxɔ nava ameha gã aɖe siwo woawu la dzi, ame siwo ayɔ Yesu Nazaret tɔ la ƒe ŋkɔ va se ɖe woƒe gbɔgbɔ mamlɛtɔ me eye mado ŋusẽ wo ahana mɔkpɔkpɔ gã wo, eye woava gbɔnye bliboe, ame siwo katã nye xɔxɔ mavɔ nyialawo gɔ̃ hã, eye ame aɖeke mate ŋu ahe wo le nye asiwo me o eye ame siwo katã ƒe ŋkɔwo woŋlɔ ɖe alẽvi la ƒe agbegbalẽ la me la akpɔ xɔxɔ. Eya ta mègatsi dzimaɖi ɖe wò lɔlɔ̃tɔ siwo dze abe wobu ene la ŋu o, elabena wo dometɔ geɖe le nye asi sesẽwo me eye makplɔ wo ava xɔxɔ me le Kristo Yesu me eye gbegbɔgblɔ ka ke me woyɔ nye ŋkɔ gã la le la mase woƒe gbe!

Gake, ale wòanɔ be alẽvi aʋatsonɔtɔ si dzo eve le eŋu la afɔ aɖu xexeame dzi tso ŋugbedodonyigba si Esau di be yeatsɔ awɔ ye tɔ la dzi, gake eƒe nudidiwo nye aʋatso eye Yakob ƒe ŋgɔgbedzinyenye li ke tegbee, ŋgɔgbedzinyenye ma ke si Esau dzra ɖe abolo kakɛ aɖe ta. Elabena Esau tsɔ eɖokui kpe ɖe ame dzedzeawo ŋu eye wòtsɔ eɖokui kpe ɖe Askenaz ŋu ame siwo tsɔ Kanaanitɔ vɔ̃ɖiwo ƒe kɔnuwo kple nuwɔnawo na wo ɖokuiwo gake woka aʋatso hegblɔna be yewotso Israel esime womenye nenema o. Elabena Kanaan ye di ame dzedzeawo ƒe mɔwo vevie le tsiɖɔɖɔ gã la megbe, ale wòtrɔ eƒe dzidzimeviwo tegbee yi fɔbuame mavɔ me elabena ame siwo katã trɔa wo ɖokuiwo ƒe lãmetsiwo tso Mawu ƒe nɔnɔme si me wowɔ wo ɖo la me la, tsɔtsɔke aɖeke mele wo si o elabena esiawoe nye Satana ƒe mɔ siwo Esau de eƒe Talmud busutɔ la me le akpa aɖe si me eƒe aʋatso dzɔ tso.

Eye ale Askenaz, si tso Yafet me la, kpe ɖe Esau ŋu le eƒe domenyinu ƒe aʋatsonyawo me, si me wodi be yewoafi Yakob ƒe domenyinu le ŋugbedodoviwo si. Gake, agbagbadzedze mawo nye dzodzro eye womali ke o elabena nyee nye ame si Yahweh tsɔ nuwo katã de nye asi sesẽ kple ŋutetewo me. Eya ta se egɔme be nyee nye ame si nyi nu siwo katã ƒe ŋugbe wodo na Yakob la dome eye nyee nye wò Mesia vavã la, eye wò fetuwo le nye asi me O Israel eye wò tsɔtsrɔ̃ mavɔ le Barabbas ƒe asi me, eya ta mègakpe ɖe eŋu o!

Eya ta se egɔme be ŋugbedodo ƒe fetu siwo wowɔ na blema Fofoawo la le nye asi me sesĩe eye ame aɖeke mate ŋu afi nu si nye tɔnye le mɔ dzɔdzɔe nu o elabena nenemae nye ŋusẽ si NYEE NYE gã la nam eye matsɔ domenyinu sia ana ŋugbedodoviwo, ame dzɔdzɔe siwo nɔa agbe le xɔse me le Yahweh ƒe Vi la me, ame si lɔ̃ wo hetsɔ eɖokui na ɖe ŋugbedodoviwo ƒe xɔxɔ mavɔ ta, eya ta O Israel xɔm abe wò Mesia ene be wò kple viwòwo miate ŋu anɔ agbe ahakpɔ dzidzedze le mɔ la dzi. Eye madzɔ ŋuwò ahakpɔ tawò be nàte ŋu ava wò domenyinu ƒe gome blibo la gbɔ le nye ƒe akpe ɖeka fiaɖuƒe si le wò ʋɔtru nu la me. Eya ta se nye yɔyɔ, O Israel! Elabena mele mi yɔm be miatsɔ mia ɖokuiwo abɔbɔ ɖe nye asi sesẽ la te be miawo hã miazu ŋugbedodoviwo elabena wolé Mose ɖe te bliboe to xɔse si le dzinye me, Yeshua HaMashiach, eya ta, dzudzɔ le nye dɔwɔwɔwo me eye nàdzudzɔ tso wò ŋutɔ tɔwo me.

Eya ta Askenaz wɔ ɖeka kple Esau le eƒe aʋatsokakawo me, si me wodi be yewoafi Yakob ƒe domenyinu le ŋugbedodoviwo si.
