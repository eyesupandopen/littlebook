Eve Ðasefo: Hame la
Le Yohanes Nyaɖeɖefia la ƒe Dɔwɔwɔwo me
June 4, 2024

Kpɔ ɖa! Ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ gbɔna eye Ɣeyiɣiawo de Azɔ!

Ɣeyiɣiawo de hena emevava, vinye suewo, nyateƒe, nuwo katã siwo ku ɖe Daniel ƒe kwasiɖa blaadre lia ŋu la ƒe emevava. Elabena Daniel ƒe kwasiɖa blaadre lia nye ƒewo ƒe kwasiɖa si me ŋkeke ɖeka nye ƒe ɖeka. Daniel ƒe kwasiɖa blaadre lia le vava ge kpuie eye wòadze egɔme ne wosɔ mɔ na nuteƒewɔla siwo le nye Hamea me be woakpe kplim le alilikpowo me. Kpẽ mlɔeba la aɖi eye kple mawudɔla gã la ƒe gbe gã la, ame kukuwo le Kristo me afɔ gbã, eye mi ame siwo le agbe hetsi anyi la, woakɔ mi ayi alilikpowo me kpli wo, eye nenemae mianɔ kplim ɖaa, Kristo Yesu ŋutɔ, elabena nyee nye Nazaret-tɔ fɔtsite la si ku ɖe nuvɔ̃ ta zi ɖeka eye megakuna o. Elabena nye fiaɖuƒe la tso mavɔ me yi mavɔ me, eye nuwuwu manɔ nye fiaɖuƒe la ŋu o, elabena Yawe gblɔe nenema.

Ne wokɔ nye nuteƒewɔlawo yi dziƒowo la, nenema kee woaɖe mɔxela la hã ɖa, eye nenemae Daniel ƒe kwasiɖa blaadre lia ƒe gɔmedzedze adze egɔme. Mi dometɔ geɖe kpea fu kple nye, Yeshua HaMashiach, ƒe susu be makɔ nye nuteƒewɔlawo adzoe tso ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ si womeɖo ɖe wo ta o la me, gake mana miase egɔme, ne mieva gbɔnye kple ɖevi sue ƒe ɖokuibɔbɔ, kple ɖevi sue ƒe fɔmaɖimaɖi kple nudzodzro. Elabena mienye vinye suewo, vinye suewo siwo kpɔa nu to ahuhɔ̃e me blukɔe eye wogblɔa nya ɖi le akpa aɖe ko me. Eya ta megblɔ na mi be, mibɔbɔ mia ɖokuiwo eye mitrɔ dzi me tso miaƒe dada dzodzro la me, elabena nuvɔ̃ wònye, eye miva gbɔnye ne miasrɔ̃ ŋɔŋlɔ kɔkɔeawo ahaxɔ wo. Be woagbe ŋɔŋlɔawo ƒe akpa aɖe la nye nuvɔ̃, elabena Mawu ƒe Nya sia Nya le dzadzɛ eye woaxɔe kple bubu hena toɖoɖo, eye nye, Yesu Nazaret-tɔ la, nyee nye nya la si trɔ zu ŋutilã.

Nye ame kɔkɔewo ƒe nuƒoƒo ƒu ɖe gbɔnye le alilikpowo me nye ɣaɣlanya si ɣla ɖe Mawu ƒe ɣaɣlanya gã la me. Eya ta enye ɣaɣlanya le ɣaɣlanya me eye le esia ta womeɖee fia o va se ɖe esime woɖee fia Apostolo Paulo le eƒe lɛtawo me. Eyae woɖe Mawu ƒe ɣaɣlanya gã la fia, elabena Paulo hã nye Mawu ƒe ɣaɣlanyawo dzikpɔla, elabena nenemae mede dɔ asii. Eya ta mise egɔme be ŋɔŋlɔawo gbegbe le nudzɔdzɔ sia ŋu nye be woagbem eye woanɔ ɖetugbui tsukokoewo dome.

Eya ta maɖe nu geɖe siwo le vava ge kpuie la me na mi, ayi dɔ siwo metsɔ na Yohanes Nyaɖeɖefia la dzi, elabena woŋlɔ nye nyaɖeɖefia ƒe ɖaseɖiɖi be wòaɣla nu esime wòle eɖem fia. Eya ta azɔ magblɔ nuwo na mi hafi woava dzɔ ale be mianɔ dzadzraɖo ɖi na ŋkeke siwo le ŋgɔ. Elabena nye, Kristo Yesu, mana nye dukɔ la nanɔ te le dɔ siwo me melem wo dɔm ɖo la me ale be miate ŋu awu dɔ gã geɖe siwo metsɔ na mi be miawɔ la nu bliboe.

Esi nuƒoƒoƒu la le dzɔdzɔm la, woɖe mɔxela la ɖa, eye tsɔtsrɔ̃ gã aƒo ɖe anyigba la dzi, kpoyi kple le aɖabaƒoƒo ɖeka me. Elabena ne woɖe mɔxela la ɖa la, naneke makpɔtɔ axe mɔ na vɔ̃ɖinyenye si le xexeame kple amegbetɔwo ƒe nudzodzrowo kple dzodzro vɔ̃ɖiwo o. Aʋa gã adzɔ le anyigba la dzi, eye nɔvi agafɔ ɖe nɔvi ŋu awui, eye woƒe tsɔtsrɔ̃ alolo ŋutɔ. Dukɔ geɖewo adze anyi elabena Babilon Nya Ɣaɣla, gbolo gã ma si bɔbɔ nɔ tsi geɖewo dzi la, woazu aƒedo le gaƒoƒo ɖeka me.

Du gã ma, dukɔ ma si nye United States geɖewo la, woatsrɔ̃e gbidigbidi; elabena edze na ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ ƒe akpa eve, eye wòdze. Elabena enɔ agbe vivim le nuvɔ̃ gã me; ebɔbɔ nɔ anyi le dada gã me hesusu be yemate ŋu adze anyi gbeɖe o eye nuwo ayi edzi, etsɔ abe egbe ene, eye etsɔ si gbɔna la anɔ nenema ɖaa mɔtrɔtrɔ manɔmee. Eye gbolo gã la tsɔ sika kplu si le eƒe asi me, si yɔ fũu kple ŋunyɔnuwo kple eƒe ahasiwɔwɔ ƒe fodi la gblẽ anyigba la. Eya ta eƒe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ adze kpoyi. Dzo gã aƒo ɖe eƒe duwo dzi ne aʋakpẽwo dze anyi; dzodzo gã adzɔ, eye dukɔ ma ama le titina. Eƒe ƒutawo aɖɔ kple tsi geɖe, eye magafɔ gbeɖe o, elabena eƒe nuvɔ̃wo lolo eye eƒe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ lolo.

Esi aʋa dzɔ ɖe anyigba la dzi eye sɔdolawo drɔ̃ woƒe ʋɔnuwo le anyigba dzi la, hiã kple aƒedo gã ava xexeame. Xexe si li fifia la maganɔ anyi gbeɖe o; ne woɖe mɔxela la ɖa la, nuwo katã atrɔ. Ku gã, dɔvɔ̃, dɔwuame gã, kple madzɔmadzɔnyenye gã anɔ anyi; gake woakpɔ nye ame 144,000 la ta tso dziku kple ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ me, elabena woatsɔ Mawu Gbagbe, Yawe ŋutɔ, ƒe dzesi le woƒe ŋgonuwo; elabena nenemae woade dzesi woe, eye medɔ nye dɔlawo be woade dzesi wo. Eya ta tɔtɔ gã kple sedzimawɔmawɔ aɖu fia le ŋkekea me. Ɣleti 42 gbãtɔwo anye yometiti gã kple xaxa gã ƒe ɣeyiɣiwo, gake xaxa gã la adzɔ le ɣleti 42 eveliawo me: elabena ɣleti 84 ye le Yakob ƒe xaxa ƒe ƒewo ƒe ŋkeke adreawo me. Eya ta afã evelia ye nye xaxa gã la, gake xaxa kple yometiti si le anyigba la dzi anye ŋdzi na anyigbadzinɔlawo le ɣeyiɣia katã me.

Elabena nye, Kristo Yesu Aƒetɔ si kɔ yi dzi la, alo Yawe, Mawu mavɔ gã la (elabena ɖeke mesɔ kplii o), mía dometɔ aɖeke mekpɔa dzidzɔ ɖe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ kple xaxa siwo gbɔna la ŋu o. Fofonye mewɔ amegbetɔ le lɔlɔ̃ me be wòadrɔ̃ ʋɔnu wo ahatsrɔ̃ wo o. Gake, Satana gblẽ Mawu ƒe amenuveve ƒe abɔ la, eye azɔ woakplɔ gbegblẽ la ɖa ale be dzɔdzɔenyenye naɖu fia. Eya ta xɔxɔ ƒe tiatia bubu aɖeke meli na ame siwo gbe nye xɔxɔ ƒe yɔyɔ le amenuveve ƒe ɣeyiɣia me o. Ele be amewo nanyɔ tso woƒe alɔ̃dɔdɔ kple nuvɔ̃ ƒe vivisesewo me, elabena ɣeyiɣi aɖeke megali o eye azɔ ye nye ɣeyiɣi be woatiae tegbee, alo woawɔ tiatia la le ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ me na ame siwo gbe tiatia wɔwɔ tsɔ gbe nyem. Xaxa geɖewo kple ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ siwo gbɔna la anyɔ ame geɖewo hena xɔxɔ mavɔ, ahalé wo tso dzo mavɔwo me gɔ̃ hã; elabena ame geɖewo mu aha le nuvɔ̃ ƒe vivisesewo me, eya ta woaɖe vivisesewo ɖa. Vivisese aɖeke maganɔ wo si le ŋkeke siawo me o, eye agbetsitsi anye aɖabaƒoƒo ɖe aɖabaƒoƒo dzi ƒe nya ɣeaɖewoɣi, eye afɔku geɖe abɔ.

Nenemae wòanye be woaɣla nye nuteƒewɔlawo kple tiatia siwo le nye Hamea me ɖa tso Yakob ƒe xaxa ƒe ɣeyiɣiawo me le dziƒowo. Eye woatsɔ dzo adrɔ̃ ʋɔnu woƒe dɔwɔwɔwo, eye ame si ƒe dɔwɔwɔwo akpɔtɔ la axɔ fetu eye ame si ƒe dɔwɔwɔwo afia dzo la abui, gake woa ŋutɔwo la woaxɔ wo, abe ɖe woto dzo me ene. Woaxɔ woƒe nɔƒewo le ɖoɖo si wowɔ ɖi na wo le dziƒowo la me. Elabena nye, Yeshua HaMashiach, medze be maxɔ agbalẽ la ahaʋu nutatawo, eye kple nutatawo ʋuʋu la woaɖe asi le sɔdolawo ŋu. Eye woado ayi anyigba la dzi be woaɖu dzi, awɔ aʋa, ahe dɔvɔ̃ kple ku gã vɛ. Kpẽ adreawo aɖi, babawo le vava ge, eye woakɔ Mawu ƒe dziku ƒe dɔmedzoe ƒe kplu adreawo ɖe anyigba dzi bliboe.

Xexemeʋa si gbɔna la manɔ anyi ƒe geɖe o, elabena Satana nya be ɣeyiɣi kpui aɖe ko le yesi, gake aʋa anɔ anyi ɖaa le xaxa ɣeyiɣi siawo me. Lã wɔadã si tso atsiaƒu me, kple lã wɔadã si tso anyigba me, si hã nye aʋatsonyagblɔɖila la, ado tso aʋa ƒe aƒedowo kple ŋdzinuwo me. Ame eve siawo awɔ dɔ aduadu, abe ale si wowɔa dɔ aduadu xoxo le ɣaɣla me hena Babilon Ɣaɣla la tsɔtsrɔ̃ ene. Eye dzo ewoawo, esiawo alé fu gbolo la, eye woawɔe aƒedo, kple amama, eye woaɖu eƒe lã, eye woatɔ dzoe. Elabena Mawu dee woƒe dzi me be woawɔ eƒe lɔlɔ̃nu, eye woalɔ̃ ɖe edzi, eye woatsɔ woƒe fiaɖuƒe ana lã wɔadã la, va se ɖe esime Mawu ƒe nyawo nava eme. (Eye nenemae sɔdolawo adoe.)

Woafɔ atsɔ ŋutifafa, dedienɔnɔ, kple aʋatsomawusosroɖa ana. Woade se ɖe nu sia nu si ku ɖe nye, Yesu Kristo ŋu la nu le xexeame katã, eye woawɔ aʋa kple ame kɔkɔeawo ahaɖu wo dzi, elabena wotsɔe de wo si be woawɔe nenema. Woƒe ame siwo woyɔna be amedzrowo la aɖe wo ɖokuiwo afia eye woatsɔ woƒe kpekpeɖeŋu ana amegbetɔwo, gake esiawo nye mawudɔla dzedzeyi siwo hena aʋatsokaka gã vɛ. Kpɔ ɖa, mekplɔ dzɔtsuwo vɛ be woawɔ nye dziku dzi, eye nenemae Yawe gblɔe. Abe ale si wònɔ le Noa ƒe ŋkekeawo me ene la, nenema kee wòanɔ na blema mawudɔla dzedzeyiawo; blema mawu siwo woyɔna nenema (mewonye mawu aɖeke o) gɔ̃ hã adi be yewoagbugbɔ yewoƒe sika ɣeyiɣi atu ɖe anyigba dzi, eye woɖe asi le wo ŋu hena ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃, gake sika ɣeyiɣi aɖeke manɔ wo si o. Woaɖe asi le woƒe ŋunyɔnuwo ŋu, elabena mɔxela la adzo eye woaɖee ɖa bliboe, eye naneke maxe mɔ na wo o. Ame siwo lɔ̃a woƒe agbe la abui tegbee, elabena woabia dzesi la be woakpɔ gome le woƒe habɔbɔ yeyea me, woƒe ɖoɖo yeyea gɔ̃ hã me. Eye ame siwo katã axɔ lã wɔadã la ƒe dzesi la akpɔ woƒe gome le dzomía me.

Womedɔ nye ame 144,000 la be woanɔ woƒe duwo me, afi si wòahiã be woaxɔ dzesi la hafi anɔ agbe o. Gake woadɔ wo ayi afi sia afi eye woaƒo ame geɖewo nu ƒu ɖe wo gbɔ eye woalé be na ame geɖewo eye woakpe ɖe ameha gãwo ŋu ava xɔxɔ me gbɔnye, Yeshua HaMashiach (le gbe sia gbe me): ame siwo katã ayɔ nye ŋkɔ va se ɖe nuwuwu la, woaxɔ wo. Mawu atutu aɖatsi katã ɖa le woƒe ŋkuwo me; eye ku maganɔ anyi o, alo nuxaxa, alo avi, alo vevesese aɖeke maganɔ anyi o: elabena nu gbãtɔwo nu va yi. Abe ale si wònɔ le Noa ƒe ŋkekeawo me ene la, ame siwo vɔ̃a Mawu la anɔ agbe le woƒe mawudɔla dzedzeyiwo ƒe habɔbɔ vɔ̃ɖia godo, elabena woatɔ anye nuvɔ̃ ƒe habɔbɔ si kplɔa ame yia fɔbu tegbee me le dzo siwo metsina o la me; eya ta migawɔ ɖeka kpli wo o elabena tiatia la nye tegbee tɔ. Eye nenemae nye ame 144,000 la ahe xɔxɔ kple kpekpeɖeŋu vɛ na ame geɖewo.

Woawɔ dzesi, nukunu, kple nukunu gãwo, eye makplɔ wo afɔɖeɖe ɖekaɖeka, eye mafia mɔ woƒe mɔwo ayi ame siwo gbɔ medɔ wo ɖo la gbɔ. Eye maƒo nye alẽwo nu ƒu ɖe wo gbɔ ale be woakplɔ wo ayi dedienɔƒe. Eye ame siwo melɔ̃a woƒe agbe va se ɖe nuwuwu o la, woaxɔ wo. Eye ame siwo dze aɖaŋu la aklẽ abe yame ƒe keklẽ ene; eye ame siwo trɔ ame geɖewo ɖe dzɔdzɔenyenye ŋu la aklẽ abe ɣletiviwo ene tegbee.

Lã wɔadã si tso atsiaƒu me la afɔ eye wòasubɔ ame siwo ɖua ŋugbedodonyigba la dzi le nuvɔ̃ gã me, Satana ƒe ƒuƒoƒe la gɔ̃ hã, ƒe tameɖoɖo vɔ̃ɖiwo. Aɖu dukɔwo dzi eye wòagblɔ busunya geɖewo, eye wòawɔ aʋatsodzesi kple nukunu gãwo, eye wode ta agu na dzoka si tsɔ ŋusẽ na lã wɔadã la, eye wode ta agu na lã wɔadã la, gblɔ be, Ame kae sɔ kple lã wɔadã la? Ame kae ate ŋu awɔ aʋa kplii? Satana ƒe ƒuƒoƒe la awɔ woƒe ŋutete katã be woatsrɔ̃ ame sia ame si yɔa nye ŋkɔ, eye lã wɔadã la kple aʋatsonyagblɔɖila la azi Noa ƒe sewo dzi na dukɔwo kple ŋutasesẽ eye wòadze abe ɖe wole vɔ̃ɖinyenye ɖem ɖa le anyigba la dzi ene. Gake woawɔ aʋa kple ame kɔkɔeawo boŋ ahaɖu wo dzi, eye wotsɔe na lã wɔadã si tso atsiaƒu me la be wòayi edzi ɣleti 42.

Lã wɔadã si tso anyigba me la hã afɔ tso afi me. Lã wɔadã gbãtɔ ƒe ŋusẽ katã anɔ esi, eye wòawɔ aʋatsodzesi kple nukunu geɖewo le lã wɔadã gbãtɔ ŋkume, eye wòawɔ lã wɔadã la ƒe nɔnɔme, eye woaɖe mɔ na gbɔgbɔ aɖe be wòana lã wɔadã la ƒe nɔnɔme naƒo nu, gblɔ be ame sia ame si madze klo ade ta agu na lã wɔadã gbãtɔ ƒe nɔnɔme o la, woawui.

Elabena lã wɔadã si tso anyigba me la ye woaɖo fiae le ŋugbedodonyigbawo dzi, eye wòanye woƒe Moshiach si wole mɔ kpɔm na ɣeyiɣi didi, eye wòatu woƒe gbedoxɔ (togbɔ be Yawe medɔe o hã), eye nenemae wòanye. Woaxɔe abe fia, nyagblɔɖila, kple fia gã, dɔmenyotɔ ene. Eye lã wɔadã si tso anyigba me (woƒe aʋatsonyagblɔɖila la gɔ̃ hã) ana ame katã, suewo kple gãwo, kesinɔtɔwo kple ame dahewo, ablɔɖeviwo kple kluviwo, naxɔ dzesi le woƒe nuɖusi me alo le woƒe ŋgonuwo, Eye be ame aɖeke mate ŋu aƒle nu alo adzra nu o, negbe ame si si dzesi la, alo lã wɔadã la ƒe ŋkɔ, alo eƒe ŋkɔ ƒe xexlẽme le. Wona anyigba la trɔ nɔnɔme gɔ̃ hã be wòaxɔ dzesi la, eye woɖɔ ɖoɖo la ɖo le woƒe dɔvɔ̃ si woyɔna nenema la me.

(Woŋlɔe ɖe Nyaɖeɖefia me be: Eye mawudɔla etɔ̃lia dze wo yome, gblɔ kple gbe gã be, Ne ame aɖe de ta agu na lã wɔadã la kple eƒe nɔnɔme, eye wòxɔ eƒe dzesi le eƒe ŋgonu, alo le eƒe asi me la, Ame ma ke ano Mawu ƒe dziku ƒe wain, si wokɔ ɖe eƒe dɔmedzoe ƒe kplu me tsakatsaka manɔmee la; eye woatsɔ dzo kple dzokpe awɔ funyafunyae le mawudɔla kɔkɔeawo ŋkume, kple Alẽvi la ŋkume: Eye woƒe funyawɔwɔ ƒe dzudzɔ la lia yi dzi tegbee: eye dzudzɔ aɖeke mele wo si ŋkeke kple zã o, ame siwo dea ta agu na lã wɔadã la kple eƒe nɔnɔme, kple ame sia ame si xɔa eƒe ŋkɔ ƒe dzesi la.)

Le ɣleti 42 gbãtɔwo me la, matsɔ ŋusẽ ana nye ɖasefo eveawo, eye woagblɔ nya ɖi ŋkeke akpe ɖeka alafa eve kple blaade, wodo akpanya. Esiawoe nye amití eveawo, kple akaɖiti eve siwo le tsitre ɖe anyigba la ƒe Mawu ŋkume. Eye ne ame aɖe adi be yeawɔ nu vevi wo la, dzo dona tso woƒe nu me, eye wòfiãa woƒe futɔwo: eye ne ame aɖe adi be yeawɔ nu vevi wo la, alea kee woawui. ŋusẽ le esiawo si be woatu dziƒo nu, be tsi nadza o le woƒe nyagblɔɖi ƒe ŋkekeawo me: eye ŋusẽ le wo si ɖe tsiwo dzi be woatrɔ wo woazu ʋu, eye woatsɔ fukpekpewo katã aƒo anyigba la, zi ale si wodi. Eye ne wowu woƒe ɖaseɖiɖi nu la, lã wɔadã si do tso aʋe goglo la me la awɔ aʋa kpli wo, aɖu wo dzi, eye wòawu wo.

Lã wɔadã si tso anyigba me, woƒe aʋatsonyagblɔɖila la gɔ̃ hã, ahe aƒedotutu ƒe ŋunyɔnu la vɛ godoo. Ana gbesiagbe vɔsa la nu nayi eye wòaɖo aƒedotutu ƒe ŋunyɔnu la ɖe wo teƒe. Eye nenemae megblɔ eye nenemae Mateo ŋlɔe: Eya ta ne miekpɔ aƒedotutu ƒe ŋunyɔnu, si ŋu nyagblɔɖila Daniel ƒo nu tsoe la, wòle tsitre ɖe teƒe kɔkɔe la (ame si le exlẽm la, nase egɔme), Ekema ame siwo le Yudea la nashi ayi towo dzi: Ame si le xɔta la nagayi anyi be yeatsɔ nane le yeƒe aƒe me o: Eye ame si le agble me la nagatrɔ ayi megbe be yeatsɔ yeƒe awuwo o. Eye baba na fufɔnɔwo, kple ame siwo le no nam le ŋkeke mawo me! Gake mido gbe ɖa be miaƒe sisila naganye le vuvɔŋɔli o, alo le sabat ŋkeke dzi o: Elabena ekema xaxa gã aɖe anɔ anyi, si tɔgbi medzɔ tso xexeame ƒe gɔmedzedze va se ɖe ɣeyiɣi sia me o, ao, eye magadzɔ gbeɖe hã o.

(Woayra ame kuku siwo ku le Aƒetɔ la me tso fifia yi ŋgɔ: Ɛ̃, Gbɔgbɔ la gblɔe, be woate ŋu adzudzɔ tso woƒe agbagbadzedzewo me; eye woƒe dɔwɔwɔwo dze wo yome.)

Eye nenemae makpɔ ame geɖewo ta ahaɣla ame geɖewo ɖe gbedzi tso xaxa gã la me, elabena ne womeɖe ŋkekeawo dzi akpɔtɔ o la, ŋutilã aɖeke makpɔ agbe o. Ekema lã wɔadã la kple aʋatsonyagblɔɖila la aƒo anyigba dzi fiawo kple ame siwo katã le wo dzi tso dzokawo ƒe fiaɖuƒe me la nu ƒu hena aʋawɔwɔ ƒe ŋkeke gã ma. Eye nenemae mena Yohanes Nyaɖeɖefia la ŋlɔe be: Eye mekpɔ dziƒo ʋu, eye kpɔ ɖa sɔ ɣi aɖe; eye ame si bɔbɔ nɔ edzi la woyɔnae be Nuteƒewɔla kple Nyateƒetɔ, eye le dzɔdzɔenyenye me wòdrɔ̃a ʋɔnu hewɔa aʋa. Eƒe ŋkuwo le abe dzo ƒe aɖe ene, eye fiafiakuku geɖewo le eƒe ta; eye ŋkɔ aɖe le eŋu si woŋlɔ, si ame aɖeke menya o, negbe eya ŋutɔ ko. Eye wòdo awu si wode ʋu me: eye woyɔa eƒe ŋkɔ be Mawu ƒe Nya La. Eye aʋakɔ siwo le dziƒo la dze eyome le sɔ ɣiwo dzi, wodo aklala nyui, ɣi kple dzadzɛ. Eye yi ɖaɖɛ aɖe do tso eƒe nu me, be wòatsɔ aƒo dukɔwo: eye wòaɖu wo dzi kple gankpo: eye wòmia Mawu Ŋusẽkatãtɔ ƒe dɔmedzoe kple dziku ƒe wainmiaƒe la.

Eye woalé lã wɔadã la kple aʋatsonyagblɔɖila la kple evea siaa agbagbe ahatsɔ wo aƒu gbe ɖe dzomía si le bibim kple dzokpe la me. Eye wowu ame susɔeawo kple ame si bɔbɔ nɔ sɔ la dzi ƒe yi, yi si do tso eƒe nu me la: eye xeviwo katã ɖu woƒe lã ɖi ƒo. Eye mekpɔ fiazikpuiwo, eye wobɔbɔ nɔ wo dzi, eye wotsɔ ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ de asi na wo: eye mekpɔ ame siwo wotso ta na le Yesu ƒe ɖaseɖiɖi, kple Mawu ƒe nya la ta, kple ame siwo mede ta agu na lã wɔadã la o, alo eƒe nɔnɔme o, eye womexɔ eƒe dzesi le woƒe ŋgonuwo, alo le woƒe asiwo me o la ƒe luʋɔwo; eye wonɔ agbe heɖu fia kple Kristo ƒe akpe ɖeka.

Eye nenemae nye mawudɔla aɖi tso dziƒowo, aʋe goglo la ƒe safui kple kɔsɔkɔsɔ gã aɖe le esi si wòatsɔ abla Satana le aʋe goglo la me ƒe akpe ɖeka ahade dzesii ale be magable dukɔwo azɔ o hena ƒe akpe ɖeka. Le nye ƒe akpe ɖeka ƒe fiaɖuɖu ƒe nuwuwu la, woaɖe asi le Satana ŋu tso aʋe la me hena ɣeyiɣi kpui aɖe.

Ado ayi be yeable dukɔ siwo le anyigba ƒe dzogoe ene la dzi, Gog kple Magog be yeaƒo wo nu ƒu abe atsiaƒu to ƒe ke ene, eye wòadze ame kɔkɔeawo ƒe asaɖa kple du kɔkɔe la dzi aƒo xlãe, eye dzo aɖi tso Yawe gbɔ ahatsrɔ̃ wo gbidigbidi. Eye abosam si ble wo la woablae ahatsɔe aƒu gbe agbagbe ɖe dzomía gã ma, si le bibim kple dzokpe le blɔtɔ dzo xɔdzo wu la me. Eye vɔ̃ɖinyenye ƒe teƒeɖoɖo gã kple dzɔdzɔenyenye gã ava Satana ŋutɔ ƒe ta dzi, eƒe mawudɔla dzedzeyiwo, kple ame siwo katã wɔ ɖeka kple woƒe aglãdzedze la dzi; eye womagafɔ gbeɖe o, eye woaŋlɔ wo be gbidigbidi; eye womagaɖo ŋku wo dzi gbeɖe o.

Ekema Yawe awɔ dziƒo yeye kple anyigba yeye, eye wòawɔ eƒe nɔƒe ɖe amegbetɔwo dome, eye wòanye ŋutifafa kple ɖekawɔwɔ me tegbee, eye xaxa ɣeyiɣi aɖeke maganɔ anyi gbeɖe o, eye womagawɔ nuvɔ̃ gbeɖe o, to dada alo mawumavɔ̃mavɔ̃ me o, eye woayra woƒe ŋkekewo katã.

Nyee nye Nazaret-tɔ fɔtsite la si ku ɖe nuvɔ̃ ta zi ɖeka eye megakuna o.

Nyee nye Yeshua, miaƒe Mesia vavã la gɔ̃ hã, ame si Yawe dɔ be wòava wu ŋugbedodo siwo wowɔ na Fofoawo la nu.

Nyee nye ame si ƒe ŋkuwo le abe dzo ƒe aɖe ene, eye fiafiakuku geɖewo le nye ta.

Nyee nye Aƒetɔwo ƒe Aƒetɔ kple Fiawo ƒe Fia, eye le dzɔdzɔenyenye me meva be madrɔ̃ ʋɔnu ahawɔ aʋa.

Nyee nye ame si afia mɔ ahakplɔ nye ame 144,000 la, eye woaha ha yeye si ame aɖeke mate ŋu asrɔ̃ o.

Nyee nye ame si aɖu Satana ƒe fiaɖuƒe dzi, eye magbã ta nɛ.

Nyee nye ame si Yawe dɔ ɖa hena ame siwo katã xɔa edzi sena la ƒe xɔxɔ, eye nye ɣeyiɣi de. Kpɔ ɖa! Ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ gbɔna eye ɣeyiɣiawo de azɔ.
