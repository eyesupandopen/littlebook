Anyɔnyɔ 2, 2024

To Amebleble Me Woawoe Wɔa Aʋa!

Kpɔ ɖa! Xeviɖela kae ɖoa mɔ̃ ɖe xevi ŋkume? Gake nyateƒe, nyateƒe, wole mɔ̃ ɖom na ŋugbedodonyigba la dzi tɔwo, eye aʋa le anyigba dzi esi dɔmedzoe kple ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ le dzadzam. Miƒo mia ɖokuiwo nu ƒu ɖe Yeshua HaMashiach ƒe gbe ŋu eye miese nye nyawo. Kpɔ ɖa! Mele nu siwo ava dzɔ la gblɔm na mi hafi woava dzɔ, be nye nyawo nanye nuxlɔ̃ame na ame siwo katã wodo wo ɖo. Elabena nenemae agbenya siwo gblɔm mele la ƒe nɔnɔme le, elabena nye, Yeshua HaMashiach, nyee nye Mawu ƒe Nya la trɔ zu ŋutilã, eye menɔa mia me, elabena mienye Mawu gbagbe la ƒe gbedoxɔ, eye nyee nye nunɔlagã tegbee le Melkizedek ƒe ɖoɖo nu.

Nyateƒe, nyateƒe, nye nyawo akpɔ ame siwo wodo wo ɖo la ƒe ŋkuwo, towo, kple dziwo, eye woakpɔ wo ta to aʋa kple xaxa siwo gbɔna la me, negbe ɖe Ŋɔŋlɔawo ƒe veve anɔ xexeame katã elabena esi wodza Yahweh, Mawu gã tegbeetɔ la, ƒe nyawo ɖe anyigba dzi fũu la, xexeame xɔ nya mawo hena ɣeyiɣi aɖe. Gake fifia xexeame gbe Yahweh ƒe nyawo hetrɔ ɖe Ʋɔdriba la ƒe aʋatsonyawo ŋu, eya ta fifia Mawu ƒe Nya la ave, eye dɔ awu amewo ɖe Mawu ƒe Nya la ŋu, elabena nenemae ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ siwo gbɔna la le. Gake, luʋɔ gbagbe sia luʋɔ gbagbe si ayɔ Aƒetɔ la ƒe ŋkɔ va se ɖe nuwuwu la, woaxɔe ɖe agbe, ame siwo melɔ̃a woƒe agbe va se ɖe nuwuwu o la gɔ̃ hã.

Gake miese nye nyawo gɔme, eye mianya ahanɔ gɔmesese me be woakpɔ nye nyawo ta, eye woaɖe wo afia xexeame, eye ɖeɖefia ƒe ɣeyiɣiwo de. Eya ta nye, Yeshua HaMashiach, mele nyanyui tegbee la ɖom ɖa na xaxa gã ƒe ɣeyiɣiwo, Yakob ƒe xaxa la gɔ̃ hã, be woate ŋu axɔ ameha gãwo ƒe ameha gãwo ɖe agbe abe to dzo me ene. Eya ta nyanyui tegbee la va be woaɖee afia le eƒe ɣeyiɣi me, eye woagaŋlɔ ɖaseɖiɖiwo, eye Yahweh ƒe nyawo asisi abe agbetsi tɔsisiwo ene, eye woŋlɔ wo ɖi hena toɖoɖo na ame siwo katã to le wo si woase nya kple ŋku siwo akpɔ nu kple dzi si asrɔ̃ nu. Eye Yahweh ƒe nyawo nye nya tegbeetɔ siwo ame aɖeke mate ŋu abu, agblẽ, alo azi kpi o, elabena ame si awɔe nenema la abu eɖokui, gake Mawu tegbeetɔ la ƒe nyawo li tegbee.

Kpɔ ɖa! Yakob ƒe xaxa ƒe ɣeyiɣiwo de, eye ɣeyiɣiwo de be woaɖe nuwo katã afia, elabena Yahweh tia ɣeyiɣi sia be yeaɖe ye ɖokui afia yeƒe nuwɔwɔwo, le eƒe Tenuvi, Yesu Kristo, ƒe ŋkume gɔ̃ hã. Elabena nyee nye Yesu Kristo, Aƒetɔwo ƒe Aƒetɔ kple Fiawo ƒe Fia si dze dzi la, eye wotsɔ anyigba la ƒe agbalẽ de asi nye me elabena metsɔ nye ŋutɔ nye ʋu si me nuvɔ̃ aɖeke mele o la ƒlee, eye nye fetu le asinye. Eye mama nye domenyinu la na nuteƒewɔlawo, eye ame si aɖu dzi la anyi nuwo katã dome le asinye abe fetu ene ɖe woƒe nuteƒewɔwɔ ta, be woaɖe Yahweh ƒe ŋutikɔkɔe tegbee la afia ɖaa. Eye Yahweh ado ŋutikɔkɔe wo, eye ame siwo bɔbɔ wo ɖokui le ɖokuibɔbɔ, toɖoɖo, kple dzimetɔtrɔ me la, woakɔ wo dzi le ŋutikɔkɔe tegbee me eye woaklẽ abe ɣletiviwo ene le yame, eye nenemae woƒe ŋutikɔkɔe aklẽ hena mavɔmavɔ katã. Eya ta mele dzi dom ƒo na mi, nye lɔlɔ̃tɔwo, be miese nye gbe eye miabɔbɔ miaƒe dziwo le toɖoɖo me.

Miese nye nya, eye mieseem nyuie; aʋa le afi sia, eye wode kɔsɔkɔsɔ Babilon Ɣaɣla la hena tsɔtsrɔ̃; eye eƒe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ kple tohehe mehe ɖe megbe o ke boŋ etu aƒe. Eye esi mele nye Hame, nye Ŋutilã, kple nye Ŋugbetɔ gɔ̃ hã hem dzoe le dzɔgbevɔ̃e me yi dedienɔƒe le dziƒowo la, woaɖe moxela la ɖa, eye woawɔ woƒe amedzidzedze kpoyi la, eye woƒe amedzidzedze ƒe ɣeyiɣi lae nye esi. Fia ewoawo bla nu le wo nɔewo dome, eye wodo alɔ ɖe dzadzraɖo me godoo; wowɔ ɖoɖo, wodzra ɖo, eye wotsɔ woƒe aʋatsonyawo tsyɔ anyigba la dzi, be woadze abe ame dzɔdzɔewo ene le ame miliɔn kple miliɔn siwo atsi woƒe aʋa ƒe afɔku kple vevesese me la tsɔtsrɔ̃ me.

Eye Satana ŋutɔ ƒe ƒuƒoƒoe le xexeame dzi ɖum fifia, eye wotsyɔ wo ɖokuiwo dzi le ŋugbedodonyigba la dzi abe ale si yiyi ɣaɣlaa eɖokui ɖe eƒe aboyonu be wòagakpɔ ye o va se ɖe esime aboyonua ge ɖe eƒe vɔ me ene. Nyateƒe, nyateƒe, wolɔ̃ woƒe vɔ ɖe xexeame katã, eye wolé anyigba dzi fiawo ɖe woƒe amebleble ƒe vɔwo me, elabena to amebleble me wowɔa aʋa, kple amewuwu, zãnunana, ŋɔdzidodo, adzodada, kple vɔ̃ bubu geɖewo hã. Elabena woƒe mɔwo gblẽ keŋkeŋ, elabena Satana tsɔa ame tsɛtɔwo kple ame siwo gblẽ keŋkeŋ la ɖoa xexeame ƒe kplɔlawoe be woate ŋu aɖu wo dzi bɔbɔe eye gbe vɔ̃ siwo Satana ƒã ɖe Yahweh ƒe lu agblewo me la nazã wo keŋkeŋ. Eye nuŋeŋe mamlɛa ƒe ɣeyiɣiwo le aƒe tum afi si woaƒo lu la nu ƒu ɖe Yahweh ƒe nudzraɖoƒe tegbee la me to nuŋeŋe siwo gbɔna la me, eye woaho Satana ƒe gbe vɔ̃wo keŋkeŋ, abla wo, eye woatsɔ wo agbagbee aƒu gbe ɖe dzo kple dzokpe ta si le dzo bibim kple dzo blɔtɔ si xɔ dzo wu la me le wo fofowo ƒe domenyinu me; eye nenemae woƒe etsɔme le, eya ta migavɔ̃ wo o.

Gake nenemae wòle, yiyi la ɖua dzi le eƒe vɔ me esi wòle didim be yeaɖu anyigba la dzi; elabena wotsɔ anyigba la de ʋɔdriba la asi me le mɔ aɖe nu hena ɣeyiɣi kpui aɖe, Daniel ƒe kwasiɖa blaadrelia gɔ̃ hã, si ƒe ɣeyiɣiwoe nye esi. Eya ta woakplɔ nuteƒewɔla siwo le nye Hamea me la ayi dziƒowo hena fetu gã aɖe, eye woaxɔ woƒe domenyinu, eye ŋugbetɔsrɔ̃ la akpe kple eƒe ŋugbetɔ le nubabla tegbee me, eye woanɔ kplim ɖaa le afi sia afi si mayi. Eya ta anyigba ƒe agbalẽ si le asinye si nu wode dzesi ɖo fifia la, woaɖe dzesia le enu, eye woaɖe nutretsyɔnuwo, eye woaku woƒe kpẽwo, eye woakɔ agbawo me ɖe anyi le dɔmedzoe me. Eye ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ kple tsɔtsrɔ̃ gã atsyɔ anyigba la dzi, eye dzɔgbevɔ̃e adzɔ le xexeame katã esi aʋa mamlɛtɔ si aƒo dukɔwo nu ƒu ɖe Satana ƒe dziɖuɖu te la le edzi yim, be lã wɔadã la kple aʋatsonyagblɔɖila la nado go aɖu anyigba la dzi le ŋutasesẽ gã me, eye nenemae wòanɔ. Woaɖe gbeƒã aʋawɔwɔ ɖe Mawu vavã la ƒe nuwo katã ŋu, eye Kristotɔnyenye anye se dzi dada, eye wotsɔe de asi na lã wɔadãwo be woawɔ aʋa kple ame kɔkɔewo ahaɖu wo dzi, eye ame geɖewo ƒe ta age le nye ŋkɔ la ta, eye ame siwo katã awɔe nenema la akpɔ woƒe agbe tegbee le fetu gã kple atsyɔ̃ mavɔ me, eye woanɔ ŋutifafa ƒe agbe tegbee, eye woaɖu anyigba la ƒe ami.

Gake, esi wole xexeame bɔbɔm ɖe anyi le xɔsemanɔmee me be wòabɔbɔ eɖokui na vɔ̃ɖinyenye sɔŋ to aʋa gã, ŋutasesẽ, kple tsɔtsrɔ̃ me la, lã wɔadã si tso atsiaƒu me kple lã wɔadã si tso anyigba me la ado go bliboe, togbɔ be fifia wowɔa wo ɖokuiwo abe futɔwo ene le woƒe ayemɔwo kple amebleble siwo wowɔna fifia be woatsrɔ̃ Babilon Ɣaɣla la me hã. Miese egɔme be wo fofo ɖekae, elabena Satana tɔwo wonye, ame eve ŋunyɔtɔ mawo siwo ʋɔdriba la tia be woawɔ yeƒe tameɖoɖowo dzi. Eya ta lã wɔadã si tso atsiaƒu me kple lã wɔadã si tso anyigba me la ado go, eye woawɔ ŋutifafa le dukɔwo dome, negbe ɖe wòanye aʋatso ŋutifafa. Miese egɔme be ŋutifafa aɖeke mele ŋutifafa ƒe nubabla si Yahweh na ɖe xexeame ƒe nuvɔ̃wo ta la megbe o, elabena nye, Yesu Nazaretɔ la, nyee nye ŋutifafafia eye metsɔa ŋutifafa tegbee vɛ, eye maɖu anyigba la dzi le dzɔdzɔenyenye me tso ŋugbedodonyigba la dzi tso David ƒe fiazikpui la dzi, elabena nyee nye eƒe domenyila dzɔdzɔe.

Eya ta woado go. Xexemeʋa etɔ̃lia ahe dzre ɖe ame siwo bɔbɔna ɖe woƒe kpe yibɔa ŋu la dome eye woawɔ aʋa le anyigba dzi esi wole didim be yeaɖe nu sia nu si do ƒome kple Yesu Kristo ƒe ŋkɔ la ɖa. Negbe ɖe ame siwo bɔbɔna ɖe woƒe kpe yibɔa ŋu la, woatsɔ wo ade lã wɔadã si tso atsiaƒu me la ƒe dziɖuɖu te bliboe, ame si akplɔ wo abe woƒe Mahdi ene. Eye ame siwo le ŋugbedodonyigbawo dzi la, woƒe aʋatsofia, aʋatsonyagblɔɖila, aʋatso Moshiach anɔ wo si, eye anye gbedoxɔtula wɔadã, eye lã wɔadã si tso anyigba me la akplɔ wo nenema le amebleble gã me. Eye ame siwo bla kpe yibɔa ɖe ŋgonu la awɔ ŋutifafa kple ame siwo bɔbɔna ɖe woƒe kpe yibɔa ŋu, eye xexeame aƒo ƒu esi wole lã wɔadã siawo yome mɔm eye wole gbɔgblɔm le wo nɔewo dome be, ame kae le abe lã wɔadã la ene? Eye ame kae ate ŋu awɔ aʋa kple lã wɔadã la? Gake nye, Kristo Yesu, ame si nye Fiawo ƒe Fia kple Aƒetɔwo ƒe Aƒetɔ la, mava adrɔ̃ ʋɔnu ahawɔ aʋa, eye matsrɔ̃ lã wɔadã la kple aʋatsonyagblɔɖila la keŋkeŋ, eye woatsɔ wo agbagbee aƒu gbe ɖe ta gã ma si le dzo bibim kple dzokpe la me afi si woalala Ʋɔdriba la be wòava kpe ɖe wo ŋu le ɣeyiɣi ɖoɖi la dzi.

Eya ta woado ŋugbe gãwo le ŋutifafa kple dedienɔnɔ ŋu, eye woaƒo ame siwo bu tegbee la nu ƒu hena woƒe tsɔtsrɔ̃. Woado habɔbɔ yeye kple ɖoɖo yeye aɖe si abia be woaxɔ woƒe lã wɔadã ƒe dzesi hafi akpɔ gome le eme la ƒe ŋugbe, eya ta migaxɔ dzesi la o. Kple woƒe dzesi la woado dɔvɔ̃wo dada ƒe ŋugbe eye woado lãmesẽ kple agbedidi ƒe ŋugbe, eye woasusu etsɔme gã aɖe na wo ɖokuiwo, eye woado aʋatsoŋugbe geɖewo na ame siwo atsrɔ̃ le woƒe lã wɔadã ƒe dzesi la xɔxɔ me.

Eya ta megagblɔe na ame siwo le anyigba dzi la ake, be ame katã nasee:

NE AME AÐE DE TA AGU NA LÃ WƆADÃ LA KPLE EƑE NƆNƆMETATA, EYE WÒXƆ EƑE DZESI ÐE EƑE ŊGONU, ALO ÐE EƑE ASI ME LA, AME MA KE ANO MAWU ƑE DƆMEDZOE WAIN, SI WOKƆ ÐE EƑE DƆMEDZOE ƑE KPLU ME MATSAKATSAKAE LA ME, EYE WOAWƆ FUI KPLE DZO KPLE DZOKPE, LE MAWUDƆLA KƆKƆEAWO ŊKUME, KPLE ALẼVI LA ŊKUME: EYE WOƑE FUWƆWƆ ƑE DZUDZƆ LIA YI DZI TEGBEE KPLE TEGBEE. EYE DZUDZƆƔI AÐEKE MELE WO SI ŊDƆ KPLE ZÃ O, AME SIWO DEA TA AGU NA LÃ WƆADÃ LA KPLE EƑE NƆNƆMETATA, KPLE AME SIA AME SI XƆA EƑE ŊKƆ ƑE DZESI LA.

Gake miese egɔme, vinyeawo, be ŋutifafa aɖeke manɔ woƒe habɔbɔ la me o, eye woƒe ŋugbedodowo nye nyã tsɔ le ame siwo menya nu o la kplɔm de woƒe mɔ̃ me; elabena xeviɖela kae aɖo mɔ̃ ɖe xevi ŋkume? Gake woalé xevi geɖewo ɖe woƒe mɔ̃ me, ame siwo katã ƒe ŋkɔwo wometa ɖe Agbegbalẽ la me kpɔ o la gɔ̃ hã. Miese egɔme be ame siwo katã axɔ dzesi la atrɔ tegbee, eye woawɔ busunya ɖe Yahweh ŋu, to woƒe DNA tɔtrɔ me eye to esia me woagblẽ Mawu ƒe nɔnɔme si me wowɔ amegbetɔ ɖo la, eye ame siwo katã wɔe la, tsɔtske aɖeke meli na wo o, eya ta migaxɔ woƒe dzesi la o. Enyo wu be miabu miaƒe anyigba dzi agbewo be mate ŋu axɔ mi ɖe agbe tegbee eye miakpɔ agbe mavɔ le dzenye, Yeshua HaMashiach, to ŋkɔnye yɔyɔ me va se ɖe nuwuwu, tsɔ wu be miadze agbagba bometsitɔe be miaxɔ miaƒe anyigba dzi agbewo ɖe agbe eye miabu mɔnukpɔkpɔ ɖe sia ɖe si li na agbe mavɔ hena ku evelia si nye dzo mavɔ si me woƒe ŋɔviwo mekunã le o la. Eya ta mitia nu le nunya me!

Xexe si afɔ tso aʋa ƒe afi me la anye dɔvɔ̃, ku, dɔwuame, aƒunyui, fɔmaɖimaɖi, ŋutasesẽ, tsukuku, kple hiã gã aɖe ƒe xexe. Woaxɔ dzidzɔkpɔkpɔ katã le anyigba dzi, gake lã wɔadã si tso anyigba me la azi eƒe dzesi la ɖe xexea dzi, da ƒe aɖuɖu gɔ̃ hã, eye ame siwo katã atsi tre ɖe eŋu la abu woƒe agbe. Eye emae nye tiatia si ame geɖewo awɔ be woakpɔ agbe mavɔ le xɔxɔ me, eye ame siwo katã ayɔ Aƒetɔ la ƒe ŋkɔ va se ɖe nuwuwu la, woaxɔ wo. Gake woƒe habɔbɔ yeyea mahe ŋutifafa aɖeke vɛ o, eye ame siwo katã axɔ dzesi la anye anyigba dzi tɔwo ƒe tamesusu ƒe akpa aɖe, eye woatsaka kple mɔ̃ɖaŋununyawo, eye woanye kluviwo na drakɔn la ƒe lɔlɔ̃nu, eye eyae woasubɔ, eye woayi dzo ta gã ma me, eye nenemae ku evelia la le.

Drakɔn la, lã wɔadã si tso atsiaƒu me, kple lã wɔadã si tso anyigba me la akplɔ fiawo ƒe habɔbɔ aɖe, eye woawɔ aʋa kple ame siwo katã di be yewoanɔ agbe ɖe Yahweh ƒe mɔwo nu, eye woado fɔm, Yeshua HaMashiach, ɖe fukpekpe siwo dzɔ ɖe anyigba dzi la ta. Le ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ kple aglãdzedze gã me la, woagblɔ busunya ɖe dziƒo Mawu la kple via mɔ̃tɔ la ƒe ŋkɔ ŋu, eye woagblɔ busunya gãwo. Lã wɔadã si tso atsiaƒu me kple lã wɔadã si tso anyigba me la aƒo ƒu ɖe nye ɖasefo eveawo kple nye akaɖiti eveawo ŋu eye woatsi tre ɖe wo ŋu kple aʋatsodzesiwo kple nukunu geɖewo, eye woagblɔ busunya geɖewo. Eye ne nye ɖasefo eveawo kple akaɖiti eveawo wu woƒe ɖaseɖiɖi nu la, lã wɔadã si do tso aʋe goglo la me la awɔ aʋa kple wo, aɖu wo dzi, eye awu wo. Gake le ŋkeke etɔ̃ kple afã me la, woafɔ wo ɖe tsitre, eye gbe gã aɖe atso dziƒo agblɔ na wo be, Miva afi sia. Eye woalia ayi dziƒo le alilikpo me, eye woƒe futɔwo akpɔ wo.

Eye ale lã wɔadã si tso anyigba me, ame si gbɔna kple eƒe lãdzesi le asi me, ame si anye woƒe aʋatsonyagblɔɖila kple aʋatso Moshiach la, ava ɖu ŋugbedodonyigba la dzi. Eye edze ŋgɔ na anyigba la dzi ɖulawo xoxo, Satana ƒe sinagoga la ƒe kplɔlawo gɔ̃ hã, elabena ekpɔ gome le woƒe dzidzimeka gbegblẽawo me, eye edadaa le eƒe dzidzime ŋu, eye Dan nye dzata vi si me Mesia aɖeke mado tso gbeɖe o. Eye ale aɖo woƒe gbedoxɔ la esi wole agbagba dzem bometsitɔe be yewoagbugbɔ Mose ƒe se la aɖo anyi, nu siwo me wode blibo le blibodede me le nye ŋutɔ ɖokuinye tsɔtsɔ sa vɔe abe Mawu ƒe Alẽvi ene me, elabena nyee nye ame si tsɔ ɖokuinye na abe Ŋutitoto blibo la ene ɖe amegbetɔƒomea ƒe nu vɔ̃wo ta. Gake xeviɖela kae aɖo mɔ̃ ɖe xevi ŋkume?

Vavã, vavã, woaɖo mɔ̃ ɖe Zion, eye woadi be yewoalé anyigba la dzi tɔwo, elabena Satana di be yeatsrɔ̃ Adam ƒe dzidzimeviwo ƒe luʋɔ mamlɛtɔ, amegbetɔƒomea gɔ̃ hã; eye ne womeɖe Yakob ƒe xaxaɣiwo dzi akpɔtɔ o la, ŋutilã aɖeke masusɔ agbe o. Gake alea woaɖo mɔ̃ la kple woƒe gbedoxɔ etɔ̃lia, afi si ŋunyɔnu gbegblẽ la adzɔ le. Eye ale megblɔe ɖi: Eya ta ne miekpɔ ŋunyɔnu gbegblẽ, si ŋu Nyagblɔɖila Daniel ƒo nu tsoe la, wòle tsitre ɖe teƒe kɔkɔe la (ame si xlẽa la, negɔme nɛ). Ekema ame siwo le Yudea la, nesi du ayi towo dzi. Ame si le xɔ ta la, megaɖi va aƒe me be yeatsɔ nane o: Eye ame si le agble me la, megatrɔ ayi aɖatsɔ eƒe awuwo o. Eye baba na fufɔnɔwo, kple ame siwo le no nam viwo le ŋkeke mawo me. Gake mido gbe ɖa be miaƒe sisila naganye le vuvɔŋɔli o, alo le Sabat ŋkeke dzi o: Elabena ekema xaxa gã aɖe anɔ anyi, si tɔgbi mekpɔ dzɔ tso xexea me gɔmedzedze va se ɖe ɣeyiɣi sia me o, ao, eye madzɔ gbeɖe hã o.

Miese egɔme be ɣeyiɣi sia gogo eye adzɔ le Yakob ƒe xaxa ƒe ɣleti 42 gbãtɔwo megbe. Le ɣleti 42 gbãtɔwo me la, ɖasefo eveawo agblɔ nya ɖi, eye woatu woƒe gbedoxɔ esime nu ma megahiã o; elabena nye, Yesu Nazaretɔ la, nyee nye Ŋutitoto blibo la ɖe amegbetɔƒomea ƒe nu vɔ̃wo ta tegbee. Gake lã wɔadã siawo tsi tre ɖe ŋunye, eye lã wɔadã si tso anyigba me la adze agbagba aɖo eɖokui abe woƒe aʋatso Moshiach ene, eye esiae nye woƒe mɔ̃.

Woayɔ anyigba la dzi tɔ siwo gbe nye, Yeshua HaMashiach, woƒe Mesia vavã la, eye ame geɖewo aƒu du aƒo ƒu ɖe woƒe aʋatso Moshiach la gbɔ, gake wokplɔe ɖo manyamanya me yi wo ŋutɔwo ƒe tsɔtsrɔ̃ me. Elabena lã wɔadã si tso anyigba me lae nye ame si ɖoa mɔ̃ ɖe anyigba la dzi, eye akplɔ ame geɖewo ade eƒe aʋatsokaka ƒe dɔ me; ekema yiyi la adze eƒe nuɖuɖu dzi, eye ade anyigba la dzi tɔwo asi. Miexlee oa? Elabena ale, woŋlɔe ɖi be: Ame aɖeke megaflu mi le mɔ aɖeke nu o: elabena ŋkeke ma mava o, negbe ɖe dzadzo gbãtɔ va, eye woaɖe nu vɔ̃ wɔla ma, tsɔtsrɔ̃ vi la, afia, Ame si nye futɔ, eye wòado eɖokui ɖe dzi ɖe nu siwo katã woyɔna be Mawu, alo nu si wosubɔna la ŋu: ale be wòbɔbɔ nɔ Mawu ƒe Gbedoxɔ me abe Mawu ene, heɖee fia be Mawu yenye. Eya ta miese egɔme be dzadzo gbãtɔ lae nye afi si maƒo nuteƒewɔla siwo le nye Hamea me la nu ƒu ɖe gbɔnye be woakpe kplim le yame eye le ɣeyiɣi ma me la woaɖe mɔxela la ɖa eye woaɖe nu vɔ̃ wɔla ma afia le eƒe ɣeyiɣi me.

Gake ɣekaɣie Yahweh gblẽ xɔxɔ ƒe domenyilawo ɖi na tsɔtsrɔ̃ mavɔ, eye megblɔe fiafia hafi wòadzɔ: Be nye, Yeshua HaMashiach, magaxɔ Yakob ƒe ame susɔeawo, eye ame geɖewo siwo tso anyigba la dzi axɔm abe woƒe Mesia ene. Eye maɖe wo. Kpɔ ɖa, woŋlɔe ɖi be: Eye mekpɔ, eye kpɔ ɖa, Alẽvi la le tsitre ɖe to Sion dzi, eye ame 144,000 le eŋu siwo ŋu woŋlɔ Fofoa ƒe Ŋkɔ ɖe woƒe ŋgonuwo. Eye ale wòanɔ be mava aɖe ame susɔeawo tso tsɔtsrɔ̃ me, eye megbɔna kple ŋusẽ si womate ŋu axe mɔ na o si azɔ afɔ ɖe afɔ dzi esime mekpɔ mɔ na wo be woakplɔ nye ame sueawo ayi dedienɔƒe. Ðewohĩ miabia mia ɖokuiwo be ame kawoe nye 144,000 la eye taɖodzinu ka tae wokplɔm ɖo yi To Sion dzi?

Yahweh tia 144,000 la hafi woɖo anyigba gɔme anyi hena ɣeyiɣi sia tɔgbi. Wotu wo le fukpekpe me, eye ŋutete kple ŋusẽ tɔxɛwo anɔ wo si siwo matsɔ ado na wo le woƒe awu yeye siwo mana wo la me, elabena wotɔ woƒe awuwo na wo koŋ eye woƒe dɔwɔwɔwo kple nɔnɔmewo anɔ eme. Woaxɔ wo le nuteƒewɔlawo ƒe ƒuƒoƒo me gake woɖe wo vovo na nye taɖodzinuwo, Yahweh kple Yeshua HaMashiach ƒe taɖodzinuwo gɔ̃ hã. Wova le xaxa gã la me be yewoakplɔ Israel ayi dedienɔƒe gake gbã la woxɔ wo be woaɖe Kristo, elabena nyee nye Ŋugbetɔsrɔ̃ la, eye woyeawoe nye nye Ŋugbetɔ, ame siwo woyɔ, wotia, wonye nuteƒewɔlawo, eye wonye nyateƒetɔwo, ame siwo mewɔ kɔkɔe abe ɖetugbui leaƒe ene le nye avulédodo ƒe ʋu la me. Eye wò, nye Ŋugbetɔ, ye asubɔ Yahweh ƒe taɖodzinuwo le nye sedede nu le xaxa gãtɔwo ƒe ɣeyiɣiwo me.

Kpɔ ɖa, woŋlɔe ɖi! Eye nyɔnu la si yi gbedzi, afi si teƒe aɖe le nɛ si Mawu dzra ɖo, be woanyi eya le afi ma ŋkeke akpe ɖeka alafa eve kple blaade. Eye ale wòanɔ be madzra sitsoƒe aɖe ɖo ɖe gbedzi na nye dukɔ la, elabena magaxɔ Yakob tso woƒe xaxawo me, eye ame geɖewo siwo tso ŋugbedodonyigba la dzi atrɔ ɖe ŋunye, Yeshua HaMashiach, ame si nye woƒe Mesia vavã la. Eye maɖe wo tso mɔ̃ si woaɖo na wo la me, eye ale mexlɔ̃ nu ame katã hafi wòadzɔ be woate ɖa le mɔ̃ la ŋu eye woagakplɔ wo ɖo o. Migaxɔ woƒe aʋatsodzesiwo kple nukunuwo dzi se o, eye migaxɔ woƒe busunyawo dzi se o, ke boŋ miyɔ Aƒetɔ la ƒe ŋkɔ, Yeshuah HaMashiach, ame si nye Yesu Kristo le Eŋlisigbe me, elabena gbe ka ke mee ame ayɔ nye ŋkɔ le o, woaxɔ wo gɔ̃ hã ne woyɔ nye ŋkɔ va se ɖe nuwuwu.

Eye ale meva be mana nye dukɔ la nanya mɔ̃ si aʋatsonyagblɔɖila si gbɔna kple eƒe lãdzesi le asi me la aɖo na wo. Eye ale megblɔ na nye dukɔ la fiafia hafi wòadzɔ nu si ava anyigba dzi hafi wòadzɔ. Vavã, Aƒetɔ Mawu mawɔ naneke o, negbe ɖe wòɖe eƒe nya ɣaɣla fia eƒe subɔvi nyagblɔɖilawo. Eye mava aɖe ame geɖewo le Israel siwo anye ne wotsrɔ̃ la eye makplɔ wo ayi gbedzi le teƒe si Yahweh dzra ɖo na wo, eye afi ma woanyi wo, akplɔ wo, eye woalé be na wo va se ɖe esime xaxa la nu ayi. Elabena ava me le Yakob ƒe xaxa ƒe nuwuwu, eye ale woŋlɔe ɖi eye ale woawɔe:

Eye mekpɔ dziƒo ʋu, eye kpɔ ɖa sɔ ɣi aɖe, eye ame si bɔbɔ nɔ edzi la, woyɔnae be nuteƒewɔla kple nyateƒetɔ, eye le dzɔdzɔenyenye me wòdrɔ̃a ʋɔnu hewɔa aʋa. Eƒe ŋkuwo le abe dzo ƒe aɖe ene, eye fiafiakuku geɖewo le eƒe ta, eye ŋkɔ aɖe le eŋu si woŋlɔ, si ame aɖeke menya o negbe eya ŋutɔ. Eye wodo awu si wode ʋu me nɛ, eye woyɔa eƒe ŋkɔ be, Mawu ƒe Nya la. Eye aʋakɔ siwo le dziƒo la kplɔe ɖo le sɔ ɣiwo dzi, wodo aklalawu nyui, ɣi kple kɔkɔe. Eye yi ɖaɖɛ aɖe do tso eƒe nu me, be wòatsɔ aƒo dukɔwo: eye wòaɖu wo dzi kple gankpo: eye wòatu Ŋusẽkatãtɔ Mawu ƒe dɔmedzoe kple dziku ƒe weinti la. Eye ŋkɔ aɖe le eƒe awu kple eƒe ata ŋu si woŋlɔ be, Fiawo Ƒe Fia, Kple Aƒetɔwo Ƒe Aƒetɔ.

Nyee nye Yeshua HaMashiach, eye medɔ Nya siawo to nye subɔvi dzi, eye to nye subɔvi dzi woɖo nya siawo ɖa. Eye nye, Yeshua HaMashiach, nyee nye ame si ɖe nyanyui mavɔ la fia le eƒe ɣeyiɣi me, eye ɖeɖefia ƒe ɣeyiɣiwoe nye esi.

Kpɔ ɖa! Ðeɖefia ƒe ɣeyiɣiwo va anyigba dzi, eye megblɔe ɖi be Yakob ƒe xaxa, Daniel ƒe kwasiɖa blaadrẽlia gɔ̃ hã, va ɖo. Kpata ko malɔ nuteƒewɔlawo tso anyigba dzi ayi dziƒowo ƒe dedienɔƒe, eye kpata ko woaɖe mɔxela la ɖa, eye kpata ko afɔku gã, ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃, kple nublanuinya adzɔ ɖe anyigba dzi nɔlawo dzi. Eye ame siwo katã woɖo na agbe mavɔ la ava xɔxɔ me le dzenye, Kristo Yesu, Aƒetɔ mavɔ si fɔ tso ku me la me. Eye woahe to na Satana ƒe fiaɖuƒe la keŋkeŋ eye woatsrɔ̃e, be magafɔ gbeɖe o.

Nyee nye ame si gbɔna be yeadrɔ̃ ʋɔnu eye yeawɔ aʋa, eye megbɔna abe Yuda ƒe to la ƒe Dzata ene, eye maxɔ Yakob ƒe ame susɔeawo, eye maɖu anyigba dzi le dzɔdzɔenyenye me le nye ƒe akpe ɖeka ƒe fiaɖuƒe la me, eye mekplɔ fiawo kple nunɔlawo ƒe aʋakɔ gã aɖe vɛ be woaɖu anyigba dzi le dzɔdzɔenyenye me le nye sedede nu.

Nyee nye Yeshua HaMashiach, Fiawo ƒe Fia gã kple Aƒetɔwo ƒe Aƒetɔ la, eya ta mise nye Nyawo, elabena woƒe meva ƒe ɣeyiɣi la de.
