Kpeɖodzi Eve: Israel
Kpeɖeŋu
Anyɔnyɔ 24, 2025

Na Nuteƒewɔlawo le Kristo me: Midzra Mia Ðokuiwo Ðo Hena Ƒoƒoƒu, Miaƒe Ŋugbetɔsrɔ̃ Le Aƒe Tum!

Mise, mise Yeshua HaMashiach ƒe Nya la, eye mibɔbɔ miaƒe dziwo le toɖoɖo me na nye Nyawo ƒe gbe! Eye nenemae wòle, be nye dukɔa me tɔ geɖewo trɔ ɖe ɖusime eye geɖewo hã trɔ ɖe miame, wote ɖa tso Kristo Yesu woƒe Aƒetɔ gbɔ gɔ̃ hã, esime mele vavam be maƒo mi nu ƒu ɖe ɖokuinye gbɔ ne mianɔ nuteƒe wɔm na nye gbe le toɖoɖo nye Nyawo katã me. Elabena, nyateƒe, nyateƒe, nyee nye miaƒe Aƒetɔ, Fia, Nunɔlagã, kple Mesia vavã la! Ɛ̃, mele mi katã yɔm ɖe nye abɔ lɔlɔ̃tɔwo me be woaƒo mi nu ƒu ɖe gbɔnye kpoyi kple le mɔ si mienɔ mɔ kpɔm na o nu fifia, kpakple ame siwo katã lala le toɖoɖo me le xɔse blibo me, eye vevietɔ ame siwo katã dze anyi ɖe aʋatso sia si Heylel Ben Shachar ɖo be wòable ame fafawo ƒe dzi, eye wòagbã miaƒe mɔkpɔkpɔ dzɔdzɔe na ɖeɖekpɔkpɔ dzɔdzɔe kple blibo la, miaƒe ɖeɖekpɔkpɔ blibo si aɖi ɖe nuteƒewɔlawo dzi le Kristo me, kpoyi kple le mɔ si mienɔ mɔ kpɔm na o nu fifia!

Aʋatsonya siwo aʋatsonyagblɔɖila siwo ɖoa ŋkekewo gblɔna, wogblɔa aʋatso le Yesu Kristo ƒe ŋkɔ me gɔ̃ hã, wogblɔna be yewole nu ƒom ɖe nunye! Gake womeƒoa nye Nyawo o, wometsea nye kuwo hã o, eye womenyenam hã o, eye magblɔ na ame siwo katã gblɔa aʋatsonya siawo le ŋkeke gã ma dzi be: “Midzo le gbɔnye mi nuvɔ̃wɔlawo, nyemenya mi kpɔ o!” Eye nenemae wòle, be nuteƒewɔla geɖewo le Kristo me siwo nye nye hame nye ŋutilã, kple nye ŋugbetɔ gɔ̃ hã bɔbɔ nɔ anyi le nuxaxa me fifia, esime woƒe Ŋugbetɔsrɔ̃ le aƒe tum abe ale si woɖe gbeƒãe, gblɔe ɖi, eye wodo ŋugbee ene! Gake mia dometɔ geɖewo bɔbɔ nɔ anyi kpoo fifia abe ɖe woate ŋu atso nuteƒewɔlawo ƒe ƒuƒoƒo gã la le Kristo me nu, alo Heylel Ben Shachar nate ŋu ate fli ɖe enu gɔ̃ hã ene, elabena nyateƒe, nyateƒe, eyae ɖo nuteƒewɔlawo ƒe dzedze mɔ le Kristo me ƒe ɣeyiɣi le aʋatso me eye nye nuteƒewɔla geɖewo ɖu eƒe aʋatsonyawo kple ŋukeklẽ abe ɖe aʋatsonya siawo tso Kristo Yesu ŋutɔ gbɔ ene! Mise nye Nyawo nye hame nye ŋutilã kple nye ŋugbetɔ gɔ̃ hã! Mimenya nye gbe oa? Mimenya nye Nyawo oa? Elabena yod ɖeka, alo yod ƒe ŋù ɖeka gɔ̃ hã mava yi o va se ɖe esime nuwo katã nava eme PƐPƐƐPƐ abe ale si woŋlɔe eye wodo ŋugbee ene.

Gake nenemae wòle, be mia dometɔ geɖewo bɔbɔ nɔ anyi le blanuiléle me, eye miaƒe dzi gbã le miaƒe toomaɖomaɖo me, ale gbegbe be miebɔbɔ nɔ anyi le blanuiléle me le fewuɖulawo kple alɔmeɖelawo ƒe nyawo te, ame siwo ƒe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ aɖi ɖe wo dzi kpoyi kple le mɔ si womesusu o nu fifia eye ame siawo dometɔ geɖe, geɖe adze ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ mavɔ me, eye womana bubu wo be woayɔ Yesu ƒe ŋkɔ va se ɖe woƒe nuwuwu o, elabena xɔxɔ to xɔse ta ku me nye mɔnukpɔkpɔ kple bubu si wonaa ame siwo nye agbe mavɔ kple agbe tegbee ƒe domenyilawo siwo axɔ fetu gã ɖe woƒe tsitrete ta, eye maxɔ wo ɖe ɖokuinye gbɔ tegbee kple yi ɖe mavɔmavɔ me le nye lɔlɔ̃ na wo me, gake nye Ŋugbetɔ nuteƒewɔla ye ava nye ame si ŋu ame katã akpɔ dzidzɔ ɖo le ŋkeke ma dzi! Eya ta, minɔ tsitre sesĩe nye Ŋugbetɔ eye mitsɔ dze de mia ɖokuiwo na nye Nyawo, Nya Nyui Mavɔ La ƒe Nya siwo metsɔ na xexeame be wòadzra mi nye lɔlɔ̃tɔwo ɖo, eya ta mibla nye dɔdɔ kple subɔla ƒe ɖaseɖiɖi ƒe Nyawo kplikpaa ɖe miaƒe dziwo ŋu, kple nyanya be Nya dzadzɛ siawo tso Yahweh gbɔ tẽ, le Kristo Yesu ŋkume, eye meɖea wo ɖe go le gɔmesese me le nye sedede nu esime meɖea wo fiana bliboe kple pɛpɛpɛ ɖe nye dɔdɔ ƒe dzi me, tso afi si meƒã wo ɖo gɔ̃ hã, si wodzra ɖo be woaɖe wo ɖe go le nye sedede nu, eye nyateƒe wona nye Nyawo le nyateƒe kple pɛpɛpɛnyenye katã me. Selah.

Nyateƒe, nyateƒe, abe ale si wòle ene: Heylel Ben Shachar, le fuléle gã me, le didim vevie be yeagbã ame siwo katã ƒe xɔse woate ŋu agbã to eƒe aʋatsonyawo kple aʋatsodadawo me la ƒe xɔse, ame siwo katã ƒe xɔse mele gɔmeɖoanyi kpe vavã ma, si nye Kristo Yesu dzi bliboe o, ke boŋ woƒo wo ɖokuiwo ɖe toomaɖomaɖo me ɖe nuvɔ̃wɔlawo ƒe aʋatsonyawo ŋu, ame siwo menya Kristo Yesu o eye womesubɔam kura o, ke boŋ aʋatsokala Heylel Ben Shachar. Eye to woƒe aʋatsonyawo kple madzɔmadzɔnyenye ƒe kuwo me la, mianya wo; gake nye nuteƒewɔla geɖewo ƒe towo gale ƒoƒom si me miedina be woatsɔ aʋatsonyawo kple nya fafa aʋatsotɔwo aƒo wo, aʋatsonya siwo Kristo Yesu medɔ o, alo da asi ɖe edzi o, eye nyemadɔ aʋatsonya ɖeka pɛ hã gbeɖe be wòatsi tre ɖe nye Ŋɔŋlɔ kɔkɔewo ŋu o, eye nyemagblɔ nya ɖeka pɛ hã si tsi tre ɖe ŋɔŋlɔ siwo meɖo anyi siwo mena la ŋu o, elabena nyateƒe, nyateƒe, nyee nye YHVH ƒe Nya la, NYE SI MELE Gã la si trɔ zu ŋutilã, eye wowɔm agbenagbɔgbɔe, eye nyematsi tre ɖe ɖokuinye ŋu kura o, togbɔ be woda aʋatso hã. Eya ta, mede se na mi le ŋusẽ katã me be mialé nye Nyawo ɖe miaƒe dziwo me kplikpaa kple adzɔgbeɖeɖe yeye si wotsɔ dze de me be miaɖe mia ɖokuiwo tso miaƒe nuteƒemawɔmawɔ me, be mi katã siwo xɔ aʋatsonya siawo dzi se la miate ŋu atrɔ dzi me ahavu wo me nam, be makɔ mia ŋu bliboe le nye avuléʋu la me eye be woadzra mi ɖo bliboe na nye dzedze si le mia dzi kpoyi kple le mɔ si mienɔ mɔ kpɔm na o nu fifia, esime xexeame susɔea le megbe xam tso Kristo Yesu, ame si aɖe wo bliboe la gbɔ. Esime wole megbe xam le adzɔgbeɖeɖe blibo me na aʋatso xɔsetakula si woɖo eye wona be wòade dzesi ame siwo akpɔ xɔxɔ to klẽla ƒe dzo me la ƒe nɔnɔme, elabena esiae nye aʋa si woawɔ kple kɔkɔewo si wole vava aɖe asi le eŋu bliboe hena toomaɖola siwo gale kluvinyenye me na nuvɔ̃ la ƒe xɔxɔ ƒe gɔmedzedze, be ŋutilã la nadze anyi hena tsɔtsrɔ̃, be nyateƒe woate ŋu aɖe gbɔgbɔ la, Selah.

Nyateƒe nyateƒe, nyee nye Kristo Yesu, Yeshua HaMashiach gɔ̃ hã, eye meva be mafa abi siwo le toomaɖolawo ŋu le nye nuteƒewɔlawo dome, ame siwo katã aɖe mɔ woatrɔ wo tso nye Nya Nyui Mavɔ La ƒe Nyawo ŋu, Nya siwo nye apostolowo kple nyagblɔɖilawo na le amenuveve ƒe ɣeyiɣi sia ƒe gɔmedzedze, Nya vavã siwo ɖe nye ŋaɣinyawo fia le wo ɖeɖefia ƒe ɣeyiɣiwo me, Nya siwo le nyateƒe egbea abe ale si wonɔ esime mena wo zi gbãtɔ ɖe ame siwo nɔ anyi la ƒe towo me ene, gake vevietɔ nye nusrɔ̃la siwo me nye apostolowo do tso: Mikpɔ nyuie: minɔ ŋudzɔ, eye mido gbe ɖa: elabena mimenya ɣeyiɣi la o.

Gake mia dometɔ geɖe ɖe mɔ aʋatsonya siwo tsi tre ɖe nye ŋugbedodo Nya vavãwo ŋu, nya siwo woɖo be woagbã miaƒe xɔse to mɔkpɔkpɔ aʋatsotɔwo tutuɖo ɖe ŋkeke si nyemena o dzi, mɔkpɔkpɔ siwo wogbãna kple tameɖoɖo be woatsɔ mɔkpɔkpɔ siwo meva eme o anyrɔ mi to ame siwo aɖe fu na mi la dzi, ame siwo katã vaa mia gbɔ le anukwaremaɖimaɖi gã me hegblɔa aʋatso siwo tsi tre ɖe ŋɔŋlɔa ŋu, aʋatsonya siwo ame siwo ƒoa nu tsi tre ɖe nye Nyawo ŋu gblɔna, ame siwo gblɔa nya ɖi le aʋatso me le nye ŋkɔ me gɔ̃ hã.

Gake, ame geɖewo le nye hame nye ŋutilã kple nye ŋugbetɔ gɔ̃ hã dome gakpɔtɔ le mɔ ɖem na aʋatsokplɔla siawo be woagbã woƒe mɔkpɔkpɔwo be woƒe Ŋugbetɔsrɔ̃ le aƒe tum le ŋkeke si woawo ŋutɔwo tso nya me ɖo dzi, aʋatsonyagblɔɖi si tsi tre ɖe nu si YHVH, NYE SI MELE Gã la, kple Kristo Yesu gɔ̃ hã ɖe gbeƒãe xoxo le Adonai-YHVH ƒe sedede nu elabena mewɔa ame si dɔm la ƒe lɔlɔ̃nu ɣesiaɣi. Elabena nyee nye Nya la si trɔ zu ŋutilã si le mia dome nyateƒe eye le mia me gɔ̃ hã to viyenye ƒe gbɔgbɔ kɔkɔe ma me, si yɔa Xexeame ƒe kekeliwo bliboe siwo wodo ŋusẽ tso dziƒo! Ke hã mia dometɔ geɖe tiae le woawo ŋutɔwo ƒe lɔlɔ̃nu me be yewoanɔ anyi le viviti me eye yewoŋe aɖaba aƒu Nya siwo atsi vɔ̃ɖitɔ la ƒe aŋutrɔ dzo tɔwo gɔ̃ hã dzi, si mia dometɔ geɖe lɔ̃a bometsitsitɔe. Nyateƒe, nyateƒe, nyee nye ame si bɔbɔ nɔ anyi kple yameʋuʋu le asi me eye wòle kɔkɔedzo la dom, be nyateƒe nyateƒe, woadzra mi katã ɖo na miaƒe Aƒetɔ ƒe dzedze, elabena mele vavam na mi nye Lɔlɔ̃tɔwo, eye ame kae li si ate ŋu axe mɔ nam? Mebia, eye megabia ake, MEGBE BE AME KA!

Gake, nenemae wòle. Be nye Ŋugbetɔ bɔbɔ nɔ anyi le blanuiléle me le tɔtɔ me, eye wòku ɖe ame siwo nɔa nu ƒom kpoo kple ame siwo nɔa nu lĩlĩm la ƒe nyawo ŋu! Eye eya ta mede se na mi be miagbe aʋatsonya siawo, ame siwo katã atsɔ woƒe xɔse kple kakaɖedzi ada ɖe aʋatso afakakala siwo nɔa nu ƒom kpoo kple ame siwo nɔa nu lĩlĩm eye woƒoa nu tsi tre ɖe nye agbe Nyawo ŋu la dzi, elabena megblɔe wòkɔ le ŋɔŋlɔa me, eye womate ŋu agbã ŋɔŋlɔa o: Be ame aɖeke le anyigba dzi menya ŋkeke, ɣeyiɣi, alo gaƒoƒo si me nuteƒewɔlawo ƒe ƒuƒoƒo gã la na Kristo adzɔ kpoyi kple le mɔ si womesusu o nu fifia o. Eye mede se na mi katã be mialé miaƒe dziwo ƒe mɔkpɔkpɔ kple tamesusuwo ɖe miaƒe Dzɔdzɔenyenye ƒe Aƒetɔ Mavɔ si le aƒe tum na mi nye Lɔlɔ̃tɔwo la ŋu bliboe, nye ɖetugbi tsitsi nyanuwo katã siwo kpa miaƒe akaɖikawo, eye wotsɔ nye gbɔgbɔ ƒe ami dzadzɛ la yɔ woƒe akaɖiwo me, be nyateƒe nyateƒe woadzra mi ɖo na miaƒe Ŋugbetɔsrɔ̃ ƒe dzedze eye be woadzra mi ɖo bliboe eye mianɔ lalam le dzadzraɖo me kple miaƒe amedzrowo. Ame siwo katã woyɔ, wotia, eye wokpe ɖe Ŋugbetɔsrɔ̃ la ƒe srɔ̃ɖeɖe gã ma kple eƒe ŋugbetɔ la me, ŋkeke ma tututu si le vavam ɖe mia dzi kpoyi kple le mɔ si mienɔ mɔ kpɔm na o nu fifia.

Eya ta nu ka tae nye Ŋugbetɔ bɔbɔ nɔ anyi le blanuiléle me le nuxaxa me abe ɖe miaƒe Ŋugbetɔsrɔ̃ gbe mi eye wògblẽ mi ɖi mienɔ tsitre ɖe mɔ to gbegbe, gbegbe kple dzi gbã le miaƒe mɔkpɔkpɔwo me ene? Ao, nye nuteƒewɔlawo nu siawo menye mia tɔ o, ke boŋ woatsɔ wo ana le ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ me na nuteƒemaɖolawo, kple ame siwo katã gbe woƒe Dzɔdzɔenyenye ƒe Aƒetɔ Mavɔ ƒe amekpekpe be woadzra wo ɖokuiwo ɖo le nye sewo nu, le lɔlɔ̃ toɖoɖo woƒe Aƒetɔ ƒe gbe kple mɔfiamewo katã me!

Eya ta, meva be maɖo ŋku Nya siawo siwo wona le blemaɣiyiwo me na Ŋugbedodoviwo, Israel le ŋutilã nu le wo ɖeɖefia ƒe ɣeyiɣiwo me dzi na mi zi ɖeka hã, kple Nya siwo li va se ɖe egbea nye lɔlɔ̃tɔwo, eya ta, mise miaƒe Aƒetɔ ƒe Nyawo eye mixɔ nye mɔfiame le fafafa, ɖokuibɔbɔ, dzi bɔbɔ, kple tamesusu ƒe dzadzraɖo katã me. Mise eye Miɖo To: Eye ne miesusu le miaƒe dzi me be, Aleke míawɔ anya nya si Aƒetɔ la megblɔ o? Ne Nyagblɔɖila aɖe ƒo nu le Aƒetɔ la ƒe Ŋkɔ me, ne nya la medzɔ o alo meva eme o, eyae nye nya si Aƒetɔ la megblɔ o, ke boŋ Nyagblɔɖila la gblɔe le dada me: eya ta migavɔ̃e o.

Gake, mia dometɔ geɖe ŋlɔ Nya siawo siwo wona abe liƒowo kple ametakpɔnuwo tso amegãxi dzeadãwo siwo ƒe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ mahe ɖe megbe o la ŋu be. Eya ta, mitsɔ ame sia ame si gblɔna be yeƒoa nu na Kristo Yesu, ame si hã nye Yeshua HaMashiach ƒe nyawo sɔ kple nye Nya siwo meɖo anyi eye mekpɔ wo ta le ŋɔŋlɔ kɔkɔewo me vevie, Nya kɔkɔe siwo Adonai-YHVH, NYE SI MELE Gã la na le blemaɣiyiwo me, kple Nya kɔkɔe siwo Adonai-YHVH kple Kristo Yesu, ame si hã nye Yeshua HaMashiach na le fifi ɣeyiɣiwo me.

Eya ta, nye Lɔlɔ̃tɔwo, mise miaƒe Dzɔdzɔenyenye ƒe Aƒetɔ mavɔ ƒe Nyawo, Nya siwo nye asiwo na esime meɖe wo fia ɖe nye dɔdɔ kple subɔla sẽŋu la ƒe dzi me eye to nye asiwo kple lɔlɔ̃nu me wova ɖe Nya siawo fia le gɔmesese me tso nye dɔdɔ kple subɔla sẽŋu la ƒe dzi me afi si nye, Kristo Yesu mena akɔfanya siawo, nya fafawo be woafa akɔ na nuteƒewɔlawo le Kristo me, nye ɖetugbi tsitsi nyanu siwo dzra wo ɖokuiwo ɖo to Nya siawo siwo wona be woaɖe wo ɖe go ɖe anyigba dzi na nuteƒewɔlawo le Kristo me le toɖoɖo katã me dzi, ale be woadzra mi katã ɖo eye mianɔ lalam le mɔ to kple miaƒe akaɖiwo yɔyɔ, eye wokpa miaƒe akaɖikawo, mi katã siwo tsɔ nye dzo eye mieda dzo miaƒe akaɖiwo le dzɔdzɔenyenye toɖoɖo nye Nyawo me, elabena mele aƒe tum na mi nye Lɔlɔ̃tɔwo.

Eye nenemae wòle, Ŋugbetɔsrɔ̃ la le aƒe tum na eƒe Ŋugbetɔ eye viviti titi aɖo anyigba dzi kpoyi fifia, elabena nyemama nye ŋutikɔkɔe kple ame bubu o, eye esime viviti ƒe yibɔɔ tsyɔ anyigba dzi la, nye dzedze ƒe ŋutikɔkɔe kple keklẽ aklẽ anyigba dzi abe ale si dzadzi dzonu dzona tso ɣedzeƒe heyia ɣetoɖoƒe ene, eye kpoyi kple le mɔ si womesusu o nu fifia nye Hame, nye Ŋutilã, kple nye Ŋugbetɔ gɔ̃ hã akpɔ wo ɖokuiwo wole tsitre ɖe dziƒowo ƒe dziƒo, wole tsitre ɖe YHVH, NYE SI MELE Gã la ƒe fiazikpui xɔ me gɔ̃ hã, ɣeyiɣi aɖe si Adonal-YHVH lala tso mavɔmavɔ siwo va yi me, ɣeyiɣi aɖe si didi ŋutɔ si miate ŋu ase gɔmee le ɣeyiɣi sia me o. Eye wotsyɔ mi katã dzi le eƒe ŋusẽ kple lɔlɔ̃ kple kekeli ƒe dzi nukutu la me, afi si wòkpɔ mia ta le le eƒe ŋaɣinya gãtɔ kekeake me gɔ̃ hã Yahweh, NYE SI MELE Gã la ƒe Ŋaɣinya Gã la, eye nyateƒe nyateƒe wodzra dziƒowo ɖo eye wokpɔ dzidzɔ xoxo ɖe miaƒe vava kpoyi fifia ŋu, hafi mieva ɖo gɔ̃ hã. Elabena mawudɔla kɔkɔewo kple dziƒo nu gbagbe wɔnukuwo gɔ̃ hã le mɔ kpɔm na miaƒe dzedze kple anyinɔnɔ kple dzidzɔ nye Lɔlɔ̃tɔwo.

Eya ta, miɖe dzi ɖi na miaƒe tamesusuwo kple dziwo bliboe le nye lɔlɔ̃ na mi nye viwo me, eye migaɖe mɔ aʋatsonya siwo aʋatsonyagblɔɖila siwo nɔa nu ƒom kpoo kple ame siwo nɔa nu lĩlĩm eye woɖoa ŋkekewo na nye Galilea srɔ̃ɖeɖe la agbã miaƒe xɔse o, alo awɔ mi blanui le dzi me abe ɖe magbe nye Ŋugbetɔ dzeani si dzra eɖokui ɖo le lɔlɔ̃ toɖoɖo eƒe Ŋugbetɔsrɔ̃ ƒe gbe me ene o. Eye eya ta mede se na mi nye lɔlɔ̃tɔwo be miatsɔ miaƒe dzi, luʋɔ, tamesusu, kple ŋusẽ blibo aɖo miaƒe Dzɔdzɔenyenye ƒe Aƒetɔ Mavɔ ƒe Nyawo ŋu, nye ŋɔŋlɔ kɔkɔe ƒe Nya siwo meɖe fia le agbalẽ vi ʋuʋu ma si meʋu le nye dɔdɔ kple subɔla ƒe asiwo me la me, elabena ɖeɖefia ta 10 ƒe agbalẽ vi la me nyawo kple nya nyui mavɔ la ƒe akpa aɖe le dzadzi dzonu adreawo ƒe gbeɖiɖiwo me, eye Nya siawo ye wona be woakplɔ nye dukɔa ato Yakob ƒe xaxa ƒe nuŋeŋewo me Daniel ƒe kwasiɖa blaadrelia gɔ̃ hã. Nya siwo wotre nu ɖo eye woɣla le YHVH, NYE SI MELE Gã la ƒe sedede nu, elabena nyateƒe, nyateƒe, dzadzi dzonu adreawo ƒe gbeɖiɖiwo kple gbe ƒoa nu, eye dzadzi dzonu adre siawo ye nye ɖeɖefia ƒe agbalẽa ƒe dɔwɔwɔ, Daniel ƒe kwasiɖa blaadrelia gɔ̃ hã si ahe Heylel Ben Shachar ƒe fiaɖuƒe ƒe tsɔtsrɔ̃ blibo kple keŋkeŋ vɛ tegbee, eye woaɖu anyigba dzi le Dzɔdzɔenyenye me, dzɔdzɔenyenye si me nu vɔ̃ɖi aɖeke mage ɖe nye ƒe akpe ɖeka fiaɖuƒe la me o, eya ta miɖe dzi ɖi le mɔkpɔkpɔ gã me le mɔkpɔkpɔ kple ŋugbedodo mavɔ ƒe Nya siawo me nye Lɔlɔ̃tɔwo.

Nyee nye Kristo Yesu, eye meva be matsɔ tohehe, mokananya, kple lɔlɔ̃ ƒe Nya siawo ava na toɖolawo, kple toomaɖolawo be nyateƒe, nyateƒe, miadze nye gbɔgbɔ ƒe yi la ƒe toomaɖolawo ƒe akpa dzi o, ale be woabu mi be miedze be woaƒo mi nu ƒu kple nuteƒewɔlawo kpoyi kple le mɔ si womesusu o nu fifia. Elabena le aɖabaƒoƒo mamlɛ siawo me gɔ̃ hã hafi ɣli gã ma aɖi, eye hafi kpẽ gã ma aɖi sesĩe kple le mɔ si womesusu o nu fifia, megale ʋuʋum eye mena ʋuʋu gã aɖe va nye dukɔa dzi, ale be mate ŋu aʋuʋu nuteƒemaɖolawo be woazu nuteƒewɔlawo le dzɔdzɔenyenye toɖoɖo me, alo adze anyi ɖe klẽla ƒe dzo me le woƒe toomaɖomaɖo me, tututu hafi kairos ƒe ɣeyiɣi ma nado go ɣeyiɣi si nuwo dzɔna ɖe wo nɔewo yome si le vavam aɖi ɖe mi katã dzi kpoyi kple le mɔ si womesusu o nu fifia. Esime xexeame le konyi fam na woƒe ɖela aʋatsotɔ, xɔsetakula aʋatsotɔ si nyemedɔ o, gake Barabbas boŋ ye tsɔe na, gake ame geɖewo le xɔsetakula aʋatsotɔ sia yome fifia, wotra wo mɔ tso Kristo Yesu ame si hã nye Yeshua HaMashiach ƒe gbe gbɔ, Selah.

Eye nenemae wòle, be medzraa ame siwo woagblẽ ɖi bliboe na klẽla ƒe dzo la ɖo, ame siwo katã woana mɔnukpɔkpɔe be woayɔ Yesu Kristo ƒe ŋkɔ va se ɖe woƒe nuwuwu, elabena ame siwo katã melɔ̃a woƒe agbe va se ɖe nuwuwu le mɔ sia nu o la akpɔ xɔxɔ mavɔ si nyateƒe nyateƒe, mesɔ kple fukpekpe si woakpe le nye ŋkɔ ta o.

Midzra mia ɖokuiwo ɖo nye Hame nye Ŋutilã kple nye Ŋugbetɔ gɔ̃ hã, midzra mia ɖokuiwo ɖo le nye Nya kɔkɔewo kple Nya Nyui Mavɔ La ƒe ŋɔŋlɔwo nu eye milalam nye lɔlɔ̃tɔwo elabena mele vavam na mi eye miaƒe mɔkpɔkpɔwo magbã o. Eya ta, mixɔ nye mokananya kple tohehe eye miɖo to nye Nyawo kple nuxlɔ̃amewo elabena mele vavam na mi nye Lɔlɔ̃tɔwo, eye nyemagblẽ nuteƒewɔlawo le Kristo me ɖi na klẽla ƒe dzo, alo na nuvɔ̃wɔlawo ƒe ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ si menye mia tɔ o la gbeɖe o.

Midzra mia ɖokuiwo ɖo nye Lɔlɔ̃tɔwo, le toɖoɖo kple mɔkpɔkpɔ katã me, eye minyrɔ mia ɖokuiwo ɖe Nya Nyui Mavɔ La ƒe Nyawo me, eye milé wo ɖe miaƒe tamesusuwo kple dziwo me eye miese ŋɔŋlɔa ƒe kesinɔnu si metsɔ na xexeame be wòakpɔ mia ta le ɖeɖefia ƒe ɣeyiɣi siawo me gɔme, ɖeɖefia si medze egɔme kple kpẽ dzadzraɖo geɖe le nye alẽha vi la me siwo ɖi nuteƒewɔwɔtɔe, kpẽ siwo dzra mɔa ɖo na nye dɔdɔ kple subɔla si nye dɔwɔnu tiatia na eƒe Aƒetɔ Mavɔ, Kristo Yesu gɔ̃ hã, ame si hã nye Yeshua HaMashiach, afi si meɖea nye Nyawo fiana ɖe nye dɔwɔnu si woɖe ɖe vovo si lɔ̃ ɖe edzi eye wòtsɔ eɖokui na bliboe na tameɖoɖo siwo ta medɔe la me, eye to nye sedede kple ŋusẽ me eƒe asibidɛwo kpɔa safuiwo be woaɖe Nya kɔkɔe siawo siwo metsɔ de eme be wòaɖe wo ɖe go ɖe anyigba dzi zi gbãtɔ la afia, Nya siwo woɖo be woakpɔ ame siwo katã woɖo wo ɖo la ƒe ŋkuwo, towo, kple dziwo, Selah.

Eya ta, mise nye gbe ƒe ɖiɖi le gbeƒãɖeɖe kɔkɔe sia si wona be wòahe nuteƒewɔlawo ava nye sedede gbɔ me, eye be wòaɖo mi katã ɖe toɖoɖo kpẽ ma ƒe gbeɖiɖi me, be woaƒo mi nu ƒu ɖe gbɔnye le nye sedede nu.
