Yahweh ƒe Alẽvi La Ƒe Ʋu

Miva miaƒe Aƒetɔ mavɔ la gbɔ ne miase nye Nyawo, nye ƒometɔwo, eye mibɔbɔ miaƒe dziwo le toɖoɖo me ɖe Yeshua HaMashiach ƒe Nyawo ŋu. Elabena nye, Yeshua HaMashiach, mawɔ nunyala le mia dome, O Israel, wòagadze nunya wu ne mieɖo to nye mɔfiamewo be miate ŋu axɔ nunya kple sidzedze geɖe wu elabena nunya kple sidzedze anye miaƒe ɣeyiɣiwo ƒe liƒo kple xɔxɔ ƒe ŋusẽ, Aƒetɔ la vɔvɔ̃ ye nye miaƒe kesinɔnu.

Eya ta, miese ɣeyiɣi siwo me mienɔna la gɔme be miate ŋu azɔ kple pɛpɛpɛnyenye eye be nye xɔxɔ ƒe tameɖoɖowo nava eme bliboe le mia amesiwo woyɔ ɖe nye ŋkɔ nu la me, wò hã O Israel, togbɔ be mienyam fifia o, eye miezɔna ɖe nye mɔwo nu o elabena Esau kple Ashkenaz ye le dzi ɖum le Israel, esime wogblɔe wòdze ƒãa be tsitsitɔ asubɔ ɖevitɔ eye gome aɖeke mele Esau alo Ashkenaz si ɖe Yakob ƒe ŋgɔgbedzinyenye ƒe domenyinu la ŋu o. Ke hã, Yakob gbe Yeshua HaMashiach eye metrɔ ɖe dukɔwo ŋu hena ɣeyiɣi aɖe be mahe nusi Israel meɖo to be wòawɔ bliboe o la ava eme be tso Israel me woayra dukɔwo eye be woaxɔ to siwo bu la ƒe ame susɔeawo ɖe agbe.

Ke hã, ne mietrɔ miaƒe dziwo le toɖoɖo me ɖe miaƒe Mesia vavã la ƒe mɔfiame Nyawo ƒe gbeɖiɖi ŋu la, woagbugbɔ mia ɖo te abe dukɔ tɔxɛ aɖe na Yahweh ene, togbɔ be mia fofowo trɔ le Yahweh ŋu ɣeyiɣi didi aɖee nye esi va yi hã. Ɛ̃, wotrɔ le Yahweh ŋu esi wogbem, Yesu Nazaret-tɔ, amesi hã nye Yeshua HaMashiach, le ŋkekeawo me esime Pontio Pilato ɖu Yudea dzi esime woƒe nunɔla gãwo kple Sanhedrin tsɔm de asi na nuvɔ̃mewɔlawo be woawɔ nusiwo se la gbe na wo tẽe be woagawɔ o, ke hã le woƒe fuléle nam kple Yahweh ƒe nuwo katã ta la, wowɔe kple dzitsitsi kple ŋutasesẽ gã aɖe. Eye nenemae wowɔ Adam ƒe xɔxɔ to eƒe futɔwo ƒe asi me, be amesiame si xɔ edzi se eye wòyɔ Yesu Nazaret-tɔ si hã nye Yeshua HaMashiach ƒe ŋkɔ la, woaxɔe ɖe agbe bliboe.

Eye nenemae wòle elabena nyee nye Yahweh ƒe alẽvi la, aleke woate ŋu awɔ avuléle ɖe amegbetɔ ƒe nuvɔ̃wo ta gbeɖe negbe ɖe wokɔ nye ʋu si me nuvɔ̃ mele o la ɖi le vɔsa me be woaxe fe si xɔ asi ŋutɔŋutɔ. Miese egɔme be togbɔ be dzɔdzɔmemegbetɔ ƒoa adegbe le dada gã me be ye ŋutɔ ye wɔ ye ɖokui eye ye ŋutɔ ye te ŋu kpɔa ye ɖokui dzi le nuwo katã me hã la, dzɔdzɔmemegbetɔ dze anyi ɖe madzɔmadzɔnyenye kple vlodoɖo ƒe nɔnɔme vɔ̃ɖi kple nublanui si dze nɛ pɛpɛpɛ la me.

Ke hã, le nye amenuveve kple nublanuikpɔkpɔ kple ɖokuinye tsɔtsɔ sa vɔe le Golgota dzi esime metsɔ nye ɖokui fia Yahweh abe Mawu ƒe alẽvi la ene esime metsɔ nye ɖokui na le vevesese gã me esime nye ʋu ƒe drop mamlɛtɔ kɔ ɖe Golgota dzi, va se ɖe nye gbɔgbɔ mamlɛtɔ dzi esime megblɔ nyawo be vɔ. Eye woɖim, eye mefɔ tso ame kukuwo dome le ŋkeke etɔ̃lia dzi le ŋɔŋlɔawo nu; abe alesi Yahweh do ŋugbe be yeawɔe ene, elabena manya wɔ be ku nalé ʋu si me nuvɔ̃ mele o la ɖe te o. Eye Yahweh fɔm ɖe tsitre afisi, le ɣeyiɣi kpui aɖe megbe la, mege ɖe kɔkɔeƒewo ƒe kɔkɔeƒe le dziƒowo ŋutɔ, afisi metsɔ nye ɖokui fia abe nunɔlagã tegbee na Yahweh, NYE SI MELE gã la, amesi ƒe vɔsa wòxɔ tegbee. Esi miekpɔe be nunɔlagã gã aɖe le mia si, amesi ge ɖe dziƒo, Yesu, Yahweh ƒe vi la, mina míalé miaƒe xɔse me ɖe asi sesĩe. Elabena nunɔlagã aɖe mele mia si o, O Israel, amesi mate ŋu ase veve ɖe miaƒe gbɔdzɔgbɔdzɔwo ta o, ke boŋ wotee kpɔ le nuwo katã me abe miawo ke ene, ke hã nuvɔ̃ aɖeke mele eŋu o.

Eya ta mebia be, O Israel, nukae vɔ? Eya ta meɖo eŋu tẽe be: Nye nubabla yeye, si nye nubabla mavɔ, nye Nubabla Yeye la gɔ̃ hã! Elabena woɖoa kpe nubabla dzi ne amewo ku: elabena ŋusẽ aɖeke mele eŋu o zi alesi amesi wɔe la le agbe; eye kloe nuwo katã kɔna kple ʋu le se la nu, eye ne womekɔ ʋu ɖi o la, nuvɔ̃tsɔtsɔke aɖeke meli o. Nenema ke abe Nubabla Xoxoa me ene esime Mose gblɔ se ɖesiaɖe na dukɔ la, le Se la nu, etsɔ nyiviwo kple gbɔ̃wo ƒe ʋu, kple tsi kple aklalã dzĩ kple hisop, eye wòhlẽe ɖe agbalẽa kple dukɔ la katã dzi, hegblɔ be, Esiae nye nubabla la ƒe ʋu, si Mawu ɖo na mi.

Ke hã, tsã ƒe nubabla la, Mose ƒe nubabla gbãtɔ la gɔ̃ hã, womeɖoe ɖe Yahweh ƒe amegbetɔ ƒe xɔxɔ ƒe tameɖoɖo ƒe nuwuwu ŋu o, eye womeɖoe be Israel naƒo ƒu le dada me, aɖo kɔma vovovowo ɖe mama mahiãwo kple amewuwu ƒe mamawo me o. Ke boŋ woɖoe be Israel nanye xɔxɔ ƒe dzesi na trɔ̃subɔlawo be dukɔwo nakpɔe be Mawu le Israel eye be dukɔwo nakɔ ɖe Israel me be woasrɔ̃ nu tso Yahweh ŋu abe alesi woŋlɔe ɖi le blemaɣeyiɣiwo me ene.

Eya ta, miese Mose ƒe ɣeyiɣi kpui aɖe ƒe nɔnɔme abe alesi Israel wɔe le ɣeyiɣi siwo do ŋgɔ na nye Nubabla Yeye la me gɔme eye miese egɔme be Mose le agbe bliboe le miaƒe Mesia vavã la me, Mawu ƒe kɔkɔetɔ si nɔ agbe pɛpɛpɛ le Mose nu eye wòtsɔ eƒe agbe na zi ɖeka, na amesiame, eye tegbee be yeaxe Mose ƒe fe ɖe mia ta. Abe alesi woŋlɔe ɖi ene, eya ta, miese nye Nubabla Yeye la gɔme, O Israel: Elabena esi Se la nye nu nyui siwo gbɔna la ƒe vɔvɔli, ke menye nuwo ŋutɔ ƒe nɔnɔme o ta la, mate ŋu atsɔ vɔsa mawo, siwo wosana ƒe sia ƒe ɖaa la, akɔ amesiwo vaa egbɔ la ŋuti gbeɖe o. Elabena ne menye nenema o la, ɖe womadzudzɔ wo tsɔtsɔ sa vɔe oa, elabena amesiwo vaa egbɔ la, ne wokɔ wo ŋuti vɔ la, nuvɔ̃ ƒe dzitsinya aɖeke maganɔ wo si o? Ke le vɔsa mawo me la, wogaɖoa ŋku nuvɔ̃wo dzi ƒe sia ƒe.

Elabena manya wɔ be nyitsuwo kple gbɔ̃wo ƒe ʋu naɖe nuvɔ̃wo ɖa o. Eye nenemae wòle nye dukɔmetɔwo, be Mose ate ŋu akɔ nuvɔ̃wo ŋuti ɣeyiɣi kpui aɖe ko, ke hã nuvɔ̃ geɖewo kpɔtɔ nɔ Mose te afisi amesiwo wɔ nu mawo la, woɖe wo ɖa le dukɔ la me tegbee le woƒe nuvɔ̃wo ta. Eya ta, Mose nye nu siwo gbɔna la ƒe vɔvɔli, nu siwo va eme bliboe le nye ɖokuinye tsɔtsɔ sa vɔe me, elabena nye ʋu si me nuvɔ̃ mele o la xe fe ɖe nuvɔ̃wo katã ta, zi ɖeka na amesiame kple tegbee, ale be amesiwo katã tso Adam me la ate ŋu akpɔ xɔxɔ to eme. Ke hã na amesiwo xɔ lã wɔadã la ƒe dzesi la, womakpɔ tsɔtsɔke aɖeke gbeɖe o.

Eye nenemae woŋlɔ ɖi tso Yeshua HaMashiach ŋu, be to nye agbenɔnɔ kple toɖoɖo me la, woate ŋu agbugbɔ Adam aɖo te na Elohim, eƒe Wɔla mavɔ kple dzɔdzɔetɔ la. Eya ta, ne eva xexeame la, egblɔ be, vɔsa kple nunana mèdi o: ke èdzra ŋutilã aɖe ɖo ɖi nam. Numevɔsawo, kple nuvɔ̃vɔsawo, medze ŋuwò o. Ekema megblɔ be, Kpɔ ɖa, meva (Le agbalẽa ƒe gɔmedzedze woŋlɔe ɖi tso ŋunye) be mawɔ wò lɔlɔ̃nu, O Mawu. Le etame, esi wògblɔ be, Vɔsa kple nunana, kple numevɔsawo, kple nuvɔ̃vɔsawo mèdi o, eye womeve wò hã o (siwo wotsɔna saa vɔe le Se la nu) Ekema wògblɔ be, Kpɔ ɖa, meva be mawɔ wò lɔlɔ̃nu, O Mawu, eɖe gbãtɔ ɖa, be wòaɖo evelia anyi. Lɔlɔ̃nu si me wokɔ mía ŋuti le, to Yesu Kristo ƒe ŋutilã tsɔtsɔ sa vɔe zi ɖeka me. Eye Levi ƒe Nunɔla ɖesiaɖe tsi tre gbesiagbe le subɔsubɔm, eye wòtsɔa vɔsa ƒomevi ɖeka sana enuenu, si mate ŋu aɖe nuvɔ̃wo ɖa gbeɖe o: Ke ame sia, esi wòtsɔ vɔsa ɖeka sa ɖe nuvɔ̃wo ta vɔ la, ebɔbɔ nɔ Mawu ƒe ɖusime tegbee, Eye tso ɣemaɣi la, ele lalam, va se ɖe esime woatsɔ eƒe futɔwo awɔ eƒe afɔtiɖoƒee. Elabena to vɔsa ɖeka me la, ekɔ amesiwo ŋuti wokɔ la ŋuti tegbee.

Elabena Yahweh hã ɖi ɖase na mí: elabena esi wògblɔ do ŋgɔ vɔ la, Esiae nye Nubabla si mawɔ kpli wo le ŋkeke mawo megbe, Elohim gblɔ, Matsɔ nye sewo ade woƒe dzi me, eye maŋlɔ wo ɖe woƒe tamesusuwo me. Eye nyemagaɖo ŋku woƒe nuvɔ̃wo kple woƒe madzɔmadzɔnyenyewo dzi o. Azɔ afisi nu siawo tsɔtsɔke le la, vɔsa aɖeke megali ɖe nuvɔ̃ ta o. Eye miele dukɔa ƒe manyamanya ƒe ɣeyiɣi aɖe me afisi mia Fofowo gbe amesi nye Yahweh ŋutɔ ƒe Ŋutitoto Alẽvi deblibo la xoxoxo, afisi Yahweh lɔ̃ xexeame ale gbegbe be wòtsɔ eƒe Tenuvi la na be amesiame si xɔ Yeshua HaMashiach ƒe ŋkɔ dzi se, Yesu Kristo gɔ̃ hã, ame ma akpɔ xɔxɔ bliboe. Eye eya ta meyɔ mi be miatrɔ tso miaƒe manyamanya kple ŋkuagbãnviviti me, O Israel, to Esau ƒe nunya si nye busunya si le eƒe Talmud fodi la me gbegbe kple eŋu matsɔmatsɔ bliboe me eye miatrɔ ava Yahweh ƒe Nya dzadzɛwo gbɔ, Nya siwo wodzi ɖe Byzantiatɔwo ƒe Nuŋɔŋlɔwo me, Textus Receptus gɔ̃ hã abe alesi wodzi ɖe Stephens ƒe nuŋɔŋlɔa me ene.

Eya ta, miese nusi Yeshua HaMashiach gbegbe tegbee fia na wò Israel gɔme be miate ŋu ase gbegbe ma ƒe vevienyenye gɔme. Elabena ne miewɔ nuvɔ̃ le mia ɖokui si megbe esi miexɔ nyateƒe ma hekpɔe dze sii vɔ la, vɔsa aɖeke megakpɔtɔ ɖe nuvɔ̃wo ta o, ke boŋ ʋɔnudɔdrɔ̃ ƒe mɔkpɔkpɔ vɔvɔ̃tɔ, kple dzo sesẽ, si afiã futɔwo. Amesi do vlo Mose ƒe Se la, ku nublanuimakpɔmakpɔe le ɖasefo eve, alo etɔ̃ te: Tohehe sesẽ ka gbegbe mienya be wòadze na amesi, si tia Mawu ƒe Vi la ɖe afɔ te, eye wòbua Nubabla Yeye la ƒe ʋu be enye nu makɔmakɔ. Eya ta, mixɔ nye vɔsa la eye miʋu miaƒe nuvɔ̃wo me nam be matsɔe ake mi elabena menye nuteƒewɔla kple dzɔdzɔetɔ be matsɔ miaƒe nuvɔ̃wo ake mi be woakɔ mia ŋuti le nye avuléʋu la me eye miʋu miaƒe vodadawo me na mia nɔewo.

Eya ta, meva mia gbɔ le nye nublanuikpɔkpɔ kple amenuveve me be makplɔ mi adzoe tso miaƒe ŋkuagbãnviviti me, O Israel, be miate ŋu akpɔ xɔxɔ to miaƒe toɖoɖo nye gbe me eye be miate ŋu adzo tso mia Fofowo ƒe gbegbe si woŋlɔ ɖi tegbee le miaƒe Talmud fodi la me kple miaƒe subɔsubɔha fodi si Esau wɔ na miaƒe tsɔtsrɔ̃ la me, ke hã si ŋu mialé ɖe abe ɖe Yahweh ŋutɔ ye na eƒe nunya ene, ke menye Satana o. O Israel, miese nye xɔxɔ gbe eye mixɔ nye lɔlɔ̃ na mi eye miva gbɔnye kple miaƒe dzi blibo ʋuʋu ɖi hedzra ɖo be miaxɔe be woatsɔ ŋutikɔkɔe kple nyuiwɔwɔ katã ayɔ mi.

Mena miese tsɔtsɔke blibo kple gbugbɔgaɖote si nye avuléʋu hena vɛ na amesiwo katã xɔm abe woƒe Aƒetɔ xɔla ene, kple abe woƒe Mesia vavã hã ene la gɔme, elabena nenema menye. Eye abe alesi wowɔ Adam, womɛ, eye wowɔe nuvɔ̃manɔmee kple blibonyenye me va se ɖe ŋkeke si dzi wòwɔ nuvɔ̃ le eɖokui si dzi ene la, nenema kee Adam ƒe nuvɔ̃ ƒe fexexe bia ʋu si me nuvɔ̃ mele o, nudidi si manya wɔ na amesiwo katã le anyigba dzi le gbegblẽ me be woana. Eye le Yahweh ƒe lɔlɔ̃ me la, ena blibonyenye kple ʋu si me nuvɔ̃ mele o ma le eƒe Tenuvi si wotsɔ sa vɔe ɖe Adam ƒe nuvɔ̃wo ta la ƒe ŋutilã me, eya ta vɔsa aɖeke megakpɔtɔ ɖe nuvɔ̃ ta o.

Nyee nye amesi tsɔ eɖokui na bliboe ɖe Adam si dze anyi la ƒe nuvɔ̃wo ta.

Nyee nye amesi tsɔ eɖokui na bliboe le avuléle mavɔ me be amesiwo katã tsɔ wo ɖokui na xɔxɔ mavɔ la, woakɔ wo ŋuti to eme.

Nyee nye amesi nye miaƒe Nunɔlagã tegbee le Melkizedek ƒe Ðoɖo nu, amesi tsɔ eɖokui na abe Ŋutitoto deblibo la ene zi ɖeka, na amesiame, kple tegbee.

Nyee nye Yeshua HaMashiach, amesi nye miaƒe Mesia vavã la eye nye dziɖuɖu nu metsina gbeɖe o.

Nyee nye Kristo Yesu, eye medɔ nye Nyawo to nye dɔla kple subɔla dzi eye to nye dɔla kple subɔla dzi ye wodɔ nye Nyawo ɖa.

Eya ta, miese egɔme be nuwo katã ƒe emevava va ɖo, O Israel, eye mele mi yɔm na nye ɖokui to Nyanyui Mavɔ si wona le nyanya ƒe ɣeyiɣi nyuiwo me la dzi eye nyanya ƒe ɣeyiɣiwo ye nye esia. Elabena tso gɔmedzedzea me Yahweh gblɔ na da la le abɔa me le ɣeyiɣi siwo me nuvɔ̃ ge ɖe xexeame la me be, Matsɔ ketiketi aɖo wò kple nyɔnu la dome eye aɖo wò dzidzimevi kple eƒe dzidzimevi dome, àwɔ abi eƒe afɔkpodzi, eye wòagbã ta na wò.

Eye nenema le ɣeyiɣi aɖe megbe la, David, esime wònye alẽkplɔvi dzaa aɖe, amesi va zu Israel Fia hafi fiaɖuƒea ma; amesi hã va zu ŋutsu si dze Yahweh ŋutɔ ƒe dzi ŋu, ʋli Israel ta eye wòwu Filistitɔ dzɔtsu ma, Goliat tso Gat si nye gbe vɔ̃ɖi si dzɔ tso Refaimwo me! Eya ta David alẽkplɔvi dzaa la yi aʋa kple Goliat tso Gat abe alẽkplɔla ene, kple atikplɔ, kotoku kple akakpo, yi aʋa kple aʋawɔla dzɔtsu aɖe, ke David yi aʋa le Yahweh, amesi Filistitɔwo do vlo la ƒe ŋkɔ me, eye David tsi tre be yeawɔ aʋa kple Israel ƒe futɔwo be Elohim ƒe aʋakɔa naɖu dzi.

Esi kpe si tso David ƒe akakpo me na dzɔtsu makɔmakɔ sia dze anyi le mlɔmlɔm la, David he dzɔtsu sia ŋutɔ ƒe yi hekpa ta nɛ. Eye abe alesi woŋlɔe ɖi le blema ene: Eye David tsɔ Filistitɔ la ƒe ta, hetsɔe va Yerusalem, ke etsɔ eƒe aʋawɔnuwo de eƒe agbadɔ me. Eya ta David ɖi Goliat tso Gat ƒe taƒu ɖe Yerusalem du ƒe gliwo godo. Ke hã, le nye ɖokuinye tsɔtsɔ sa vɔe abe Mawu ƒe alẽvi la ene me la, wokplɔm yi du ƒe gliwo godo eye afima le Golgota ye wohe m ɖe atitsoga ŋu le, taƒu ƒe teƒe la gɔ̃ hã. Eye esi wohem ɖe atitsoga ŋu la, woklãm ɖe ati krekre ma ŋu afisi wowɔ abi nye afɔkpodzi kple woƒe gatãwo esime woklãm ɖe ati krekre ma ŋu. Eye nenemae wokpɔ dziɖuɖu na Adam afima le Kalvari afisi wowɔ abi nye afɔkpodzi le afisi wogbã Satana ƒe ta hã le esime nye ʋu si he dziɖuɖu ɖe da la ƒe dzidzimevi dzi tegbee la kɔ ɖi. Eya ta, le mɔ ma ke nu abe alesi alẽkplɔvi aɖe ɖu Satana ŋutɔ ƒe dzidzimevi dzi ene la, nenema kee Alẽkplɔlagã la ɖu Satana dzi tegbee le nye lɔlɔ̃ ƒe vɔsa si mewɔ ɖe Adam ƒe nuvɔ̃wo ta la me.

Ke hã, esime meku le vevesese gã me abe nuvɔ̃wɔla tsɛ aɖe ene (nuvɔ̃wɔwɔ si me fɔ aɖeke mele ŋunye o) le du kɔkɔe ma ƒe agbowo godo la, nye ʋu si me nuvɔ̃ mele o la kɔ tso nye ŋutilã me afima le amegbetɔ nuvɔ̃wɔla si tso anyigba dzĩ me la ƒe xɔxɔ blibo ƒe nɔnɔme blibo me. Eye esi nye ʋu de anyigba dzĩ la me le taƒu ƒe teƒe afima le kalvari la, wogbã da la ƒe ta tegbee, eye le nuwo katã ƒe nuwuwu la, woabla dzoka xoxo ma ŋutɔ eye woatsɔe aƒu gbe tegbee ɖe tohehe ƒe dzo siwo me woƒe nɔvi mekunɛ o la me, elabena nenemae nye eƒe domenyinu.

Eye nyee nye amesi gbɔna be yeatsrɔ̃ futɔwo ƒe fiaɖuƒe gbidigbidi be woabla dzoka la ahatre enu ɖe aʋu me hena ƒe akpe ɖeka be woaɖo nye dziɖuɖu dzɔdzɔe la anyi ɖe anyigba dzi. Le eƒe nuwuwu la, woaɖe asi le eŋu hena eƒe ɣeyiɣi kpui aɖe afisi wòaƒo eƒe aglãdzedze mamlɛtɔ nu ƒu le. Eye ne wodze kɔkɔetɔwo ƒe asaɖa dzi la, dzo aɖi tso Yahweh ŋutɔ gbɔ ahatsrɔ̃ wo gbidigbidi ɣeyiɣi si me woatsɔ Satana aƒu gbe ɖe dzo mavɔ si le dzodzom tegbee kple dzokpe fodi me.

Eya ta, mena miese egɔme, O Israel, be Yeshua HaMashiach amesi nye Yesu Kristo le Eŋlisigbe me la ƒe vɔsa xe Mose ƒe tohehe bliboe ale be miate ŋu ase nye vɔsa ƒe blibonyenye kple blibonyenye si nye avuléʋu si wokɔ ɖi hena ɖe Adam ƒe nuvɔ̃wo ta la gɔme. Miese egɔme bliboe, O Israel, be ne ɖe amesiwo dze anyi la se Yahweh ƒe ŋaɣinya la gɔme la, anye ne womahe miaƒe Ŋutikɔkɔe ƒe Aƒetɔ la ɖe atitsoga ŋu gbeɖe o! Elabena to nye atitsogaŋuheku si me da la wɔ abi nye afɔkpodzi ɣeyiɣi kpui aɖe me la, wogbã da la ƒe ta tegbee fifia, abi si me wòmahaya tsoe gbeɖe o gɔ̃ hã.

Eya ta, O Israel, miese nye Nyawo eye miese nye lɔlɔ̃ na mi gɔme eye be mele mi yɔm le ɣeyiɣi mamlɛ sia me. Eya ta miese nye nuƒo gɔme elabena woŋlɔe ɖi be: Ŋutifafa ƒe Mawu si gbugbɔ Yeshua HaMashiach, alẽwo ƒe kplɔla gã la, tso ame kukuwo dome, to nubabla mavɔ ƒe ʋu la me, na miade blibo le dɔ nyuiwo katã me, be miawɔ eƒe lɔlɔ̃nu, awɔ nusi dze eŋu le mia me to Yesu Kristo dzi, amesi tɔ ŋutikɔkɔe nanye yi ɖe ebedzi, Amen.
