Mi bai lukautim meri bilong mi na bodi bilong mi; long wanem husat man i bin heitim mit bilong em yet, nogat, em i save givim kaikai na lukautim gut? Lukluk, putim yau, lukim ol sisen, na save olsem ol taim i kamap pinis na de bai kamap wantu tasol olsem bikpela san i kamap wantu long skai. Ol i no inap pasim mi, na mi no inap stop. Maski stilman bai traim, em bai singaut olsem laion; tasol em i laion i no gat tit tasol. Em i no inap pasim mi, na ol i no inap pasim mi long kisim samting bilong mi, long wanem mi baim pinis graun na moa yet ol manmeri bilong mi long blut bilong mi yet i no gat sin. Na nem bilong mi i sanap antap long olgeta samting, Papa bilong mi tasol i winim mi; na ol i no inap pasim mi, o stopim mi.

Mi kam, na mi no inap pasim maus bilong mi, na kros bilong mi i hat tru long Sikret Babilon, yes, Sikret Babilon husat i save salim ol liklik pikinini olsem kago i go long han bilong ol man nogut, ol bai stop. I mobeta ol i pasim bikpela ston long nek bilong ol, na tromoi ol i go long solwara; tasol ol i makim ol bilong paia i stap oltaim, na jasmen bai kamap klostu, long wanem kros bilong Pikinini Sipsip i redi long karamapim olgeta hap bilong graun na trabel bilong Jekop bai kamap taim ol i tekewe man bilong pasim rot.

Sanap strong, ol manmeri bilong mi, na sanap strong long mi wantaim wanpela bel na wanpela tingting. Lusim ol samting bilong graun yu bin mangalim, na putim mi, Bikpela Jisas Krais, olsem laik bilong yu; long wanem mi strongpela taua bilong yu, ston bilong hait, na wasman long baksait. Husat bai kam pait long yu na inap sanap long strongpela han bilong mi? Ol manmeri mi makim na i stap gut long mi i stap long han bilong mi, na i no gat wanpela inap kisim yu long mi. Yu bilong mi, na yu seif long mi; so malolo long mi long ol hatwok bilong yu yet, long wanem taim bilong bekim bai kamap wantu. Bekim bilong jasmen long ol man nogut i stap hia, na bekim bilong ol manmeri i stap gut long mi i stap long ol manmeri mi makim, long wanem mi bai givim pe long ol wantaim samting bilong ol, long wanem mi mekim ol i kamap inap.

O, yu dregon i no gat tit na i samting nating; yu snek bilong bipo: taim bilong yu i kamap pinis na jasmen i stap long yu. Maski yu bikmaus na ting yu bai win, mi bai kisim olgeta samting yu bin bungim long han bilong yu i save stil, na ol man i bihainim yu long mangal nogut bilong ol bai kisim hap bilong ol long raunwara paia, we olgeta bai kisim pe inap long wok bilong ol planti taim moa. Bikpela pe nogut bai kamap komfota bilong yu, na smok nogut bilong paia salfa bai stap oltaim long nus bilong yu, na yu bai baim inap oltaim oltaim. Na smok bilong pen bilong yu bai i go antap oltaim, na olgeta husat i bihainim yu stat long liklik i go inap long bikpela bai kisim samting bilong ol na pe wantaim yu long waitpela paia i hat tru i gat blupela kala em bai karamapim yu oltaim taim ol i pasim yu na tromoi yu i go long pe nogut bilong yu. Bai i gat bikpela krai na kaikai tit. Na yu ting yu inap sanap pait long Yawe na Krais bilong em, tasol yu bai kamap samting nating tasol na kisim sem i stap oltaim long pen bilong yu i no save pinis, na oltaim ol bai pasim yu i go hait. Na yu no inap bagarapim ol samting mi wokim gen, na ol bai lusim tingting tru long yu, na yu no inap kam long tingting bilong ol.

So ol manmeri bilong mi, meri bilong mi i stap gut long mi em mi laikim tru, wanem samting i stap bilong yu long pret long en? Kros bilong mi na kros bilong Papa bilong yu i no bilong yu na i no inap kam long yu, na yu no inap stap long bagarap bilong namba tu dai. So no ken pret long wanem mi bai bekim stret long ol birua bilong yu, long wanem bekim em i samting bilong mi, Bikpela i tok! Yu no save olsem yu bai jasim ol ensel? Yu no save olsem yu bai jasim graun? So mekim yu yet i kamap klin long mi, long wanem blut bilong mi i baim sin tasol bai mekim yu inap long sanap long ai bilong mi long glori. Pasim tok bilong mi long bel bilong yu, na holim ol rait bilong mi long poret bilong yu, na malolo long ol tok bilong mi bilong bipo; long wanem ol i laip long yu, na laip bilong yu i hait long mi, na mi bai kamapim!

Tasol yu, o, Misteri Babilon, bai bagarap long wanpela aua tasol.

Bikpela paia bai kam long yu; bikpela guria bai pundaunim yu; ol taua bilong yu bai pundaun; ol samting bilong yu bai bagarap na pinis, na ol bikpela weip bai karamapim nambis bilong yu, na mi bai givim pe long yu inap long ol wok bilong yu, na bikpela jasmen i stap long yu. Long wanem bipo yu bin stap olsem lait bilong hop na lait long graun long sotpela taim, tasol yu bin makim mangal, pasin bilong tingim yu yet, na pasin korap olsem poroman bilong yu, o, bikpela pamuk meri! Yu sanap long sen, wetim las jasmen bilong yu, em bai klia long olgeta manmeri long lukim, na bikpela pret bai kam long planti man.

Lukim! Long wanem yu bin givim baksait long neiba bilong yu, na i no gat pret long Bikpela long bel bilong yu. Yu bin tok, Mi no wido meri, tasol yu wido na aipas na yaupas long ai bilong mi.
