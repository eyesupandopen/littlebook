¡Wiñay Tatitumaru pacienciaampi suyapki uka iyawsirinakajaru!

Wiñay Tatituman arunakap ist'apxam, Yeshua HaMashiach, ukhamaraki nayan ukar jutapxam chuymanakamampi katuqañataki ukhamaraki amparajat churäwij suyt'añataki, chiqpachansa jichhaw pacha. Uka suyt'at pacha, kunapachatix alaxpachat munañampi, arcángel arupampi ukhamaraki Diosan trompetapampi (uka trompetax ist'asiniwa) jallq'anipkä ukhaxa, Criston jiwatanakax jan tukuskir jaktapxani, ukhamaraki jumanakax jakaskapki ukhamaraki qhiparapki ukanakax qinayanakar apkatatäpxani, ukanwa jan jiwiri isthapt'atäpxäta, ukhamat wiñay Tatitumampi wiñayataki jakapxäta.

Nayax Tatitunakat Tatitutwa, Reyinakat Reyitwa, ukhamaraki nayaw aka uraq wilaxtampi alata. Chiqpachansa, chiqpachansa, jaqin juchanakapat chanip phuqhawayta, jaqix Yahweh ukampi sumachasiñapataki, kunatix naya, Nazaretankir Jesusax, sumankañ aruskipäwimawa, ukanwa jaqinakax Diosampi sumachasipxi, ukatwa Pablompi Corintionakar qillqayta: Jichhax Criston arup apirinakäpxtwa: Diosax jiwas taypi achikt'askaspa ukhamawa, Criston lantiw mayipxsma, Diosampi sumachasipxam. Jupax jan juchanïkän ukaruw jiwas layku juchar tukuyäna, jiwasax jupan Diosan chiqapa kankañapar tukuñataki.

Ukatwa, wawanakaja, Diosan chiqapa kankañapar tukuwayxsma Cristo tuqi jumanakan qhant'añ suyt'äwi, uka wiñay wawa kankañ ajayu tuqi, ukanwa ABBA, Awki sasaw art'asipxta, ukhamatwa Diosan wiñay wawanakapjam wasitat yuritapxta ukhamaraki uka qullana ajayu arsuwimpi sellatäpxta. Amuyapxam, Yahweh-n sellopax janiw p'akitäkaspati, janirakiw mayjt'ayatäkaspati, ukhamaraki taqi uka sellatanakax wiñayatakiw imatäpxi, ukhamat amuyapxam kunatix Yahweh-x qillqki ukhamaraki arski ukax janiw khitis mayjt'aykaspati. Ukhamax ¿khitis amparajat apxaruspasma? ¿Khitis jach'a wiñay Diosar atipaspa, Yahweh, Qhananakan Awkipa? Ukhamax ¿kunatsa llakisipxta ukhamaraki nayax jaquqanipxäma sasax axsarapxta? Ukampis, ¿khitisa jumanakat wawanakapar jaquqanispa, ukax Tatituman ukhamaraki Qhispiyiriman nayraqatapan jisk'a kaspas ukham, Nazaretankir Jesusan nayraqatapanxa.

Chiqpachansa, chiqpachansa, Diosan churañanakapampi jawt'äwipampix janiw arrepentisiñampïkiti, ukhamaraki khitinakatix wiñay jakañ churatäpki Jesusar Tatitut arsusin, Diosax jiwatanakat jaktaykiti sasin iyawsasax wiñay jakañ katuqapxi ukhamaraki Diosan chiqapa kankañapar tukupxi Cristo Jesus Tatitupan. Jichhax maynix jan iyawsäwini chuymampi uka arsuwi lurachi, nayax jakiri Diosan sellop jark'aqtwa, ukhamat nayan ukar sum apaniñataki, ukhamat juchanakapat sum arrepentisipxañapataki, inas jathajax qala uraqir jalqantchi. Jichhax maynix Criston ajayup jan utjkchi, janiw jupankkiti. Nayaw jaqinakan chuymanakap yant'irïta ukhamaraki yant'irïta, ukhamat nayan q'umachäwi ninajan q'umachatäpxañapataki, kunatix nayaw q'umachäwi nina phichhantirïta, nayaw nina phichhantirïta, ukhamat nayan, Cristo Jesusan, phuqhat herenciamar puripxañamataki, ukhamax nayan lurañanakar llamp'u chuymampiw uñjapxam ukhamaraki jumanakan yurit wawa kankañ ajayun aytäwipar jan jark'apxamti, ukaw chiqapa kankañ ukhamaraki chiqpach qullana kankañ apani. Ukhamatwa wawanakaja, ajayu tuqi sarnaqapxam, aycha munañanaka jan phuqhapxañamataki.

Ukampis, waljaniw jumanakat chuymanakam inamay llakisiyapxta, Diosan sellopax jaqin p'inqa lurañapampi p'akitäspawa sasa. ¿Munatanakaja, jiskt'apsmawa? ¿El Shaddai-x nayraqat amuyupampi armatati naya, Nazaretankir Jesusar, qhispiyäwi qhipat wasitat juchar puripkasp uka juchaninak qhispiyañataki khitankit uka? ¿Yahweh, jach'a wiñay Diosax jan iwxt'ir munki, tunka waranqa kuti tunka waranqa tronop muyuntkchisa, ukhamaraki taqi kunsa munañapan iwxt'äwiparjam lurki, janiw q'añuchat Adan-an jan phuqhat kankañap amuykiti, taqi wila uraqit yurit jaqinaka, khitinakatix Adan-an wilapat katuqapki ukanaka?

Janiw wawanakaja, janiw Yahweh-x aka yänak jan amuykaspati ukhamaraki taqi tuqitwa jumanakatak wakichawayi ukhamaraki taqi thakhinak churawayi, ukhamat phuqhat qhispiyäwimax jumanakar puriniñapataki munatanakaja. Ukhamax iyawsäwi kamachi tuqi sarnaqapxam ukhamaraki nayan ajayuj tuqi jumanakan yuritampi ukhamaraki Cristo Jesusan jakañ ajayuparjam sarnaqapxam. Kunatix Cristo Jesusanxa, circuncisión ukax janiw kunatakis wakiskiti, janirakiw jan circuncidatäñasa, jan ukasti machaq lurañawa. Ukhamaraki khitinakatix aka kamachirjam sarnaqapki, sumankañaw jupanakan utjani, ukhamaraki khuyapt'ayasiña, ukhamaraki Diosan Israelapan. ¿Janiw amuyapktati chiqap jaqix iyawsäwimpiw jakani? Ukhamax amuyapxam, janiw khitis iyawsirinakajat jichhax phuqhatäkiti, janiwa, phuqhat qhispiyäwipkama. Ukhamaraki taqi qhispiyatanakax uka qullana ajayu arsuwimpi sellatäpki ukanakax taqiniw juchar puripxani, yatisasa jan yatisasa, uru pachanxa. Ukatwa, sapüru wilaxtampi q'umachasipxam. Nayan ukar jutapxam ukhamaraki nayampi sumankañam nina phichhantapxam ukhamaraki ch'amanchapxam, munatanakaja. Sapüru juchanakam nayar arsupxam, ukhamaraki llamp'u arupampi llamp'u chuymampiw uñjapxam kunapachatix nayax jumanakar chiqapa kankañat juchañchañatak jutkä ukhaxa. Nayax jumanakar thaqhawayxsmawa ukhamaraki nayraqatamankxtwa ch'amanchasa ukhamaraki thakhinakam q'umachasa, ukhamat uka chiqapa ukhamaraki k'ullk'u thakhi sarnaqapxañamataki, uka thakhi sarnaqañataki jawthapitapxta. Ukhamaraki uka phuqhat qhispiyäwi uru suyt'askakipxam, ukanwa Yahweh-n nayraqatapan phuqhat sayt'apxäta, uka chiqapa kankañampi isthapt'ata, ukax nayaw phuqhat churapxäma, kunatix naya, Cristo Jesusaw, q'uma, jan q'añun, jan ch'ixin, jan sip'un, jan ukax kunjam isimpi isthapt'apxäma.

Ukatwa, amuyapxam jisk'anakaja, taripäwix uraqir puriniwa, uka jach'a taripäwi nayra profetanakampi nayratpach yatiyata, uka phuqhasiñ pachax jichhaw purini. Uraqinx usunakas janiw sayt'kiti, taripäwix akankxiwa ukhamaraki qhispiyatanakajax phuqhat qhispiyäwipar puripxani. Ukhamatwa yänakax jutani, uhu tuqi. Sapa uhux nayrat sipan juk'amp jach'a ukhamaraki juk'amp ch'amani t'unjäwipampi, kunapachatix akatjamat qhispiyäwir apatäpkäta ukhamaraki akatjamat Jamasa Babiloniax janiw utjkxiti. Sapa uhux taripäwi apani ukhamaraki sapa uhux nayrat sipan juk'amp ch'amampi ukhamaraki juk'amp jach'at q'asasi. Ukhamaw nayan taripäwinakaja ukhamaraki iwxt'äwinakajax uraqitaki, kunatix nayax Juda tribun leonapjamaw juttha ukhamaraki chiqapa kankañampiw taripañataki ukhamaraki ch'axwañataki juttha.

Ukhamatwa, qhipa t'unjäwi janir purinkipan, nayan munañajampiw lurasini, Criston ikisipki uka iyawsirinakajax p'iyanikapat jaktayatäpxani ukhamaraki jakaskapki ukhamaraki qhiparapki uka iyawsirinakajax jupanakampi tantachatäpxani ukhamaraki alaxpachan alaxpachapar sarapxäta, ukanwa taqini uka jach'a Criston taripäwi tronop nayraqatan sayt'apxäta, ukhamat taqini janchin lurat yänakat katuqapxañamataki. Taqi khitinakatix nayan Bema-jar jak'achasiñatak apatäpki ukanakax janiw juchanakapat taripatäñatak jutpkiti, kunatix jumanakax juchan jaqin urupan juchanak pampachäwi katuqapxta, aka urun juchan jaqix uraq apnaqki uka apnaqäwix tukuyxiwa. Kunatix uraqix nayankiwa, Cristo Jesusankiwa, ukhamaraki phuqhat kankañapa, ukhamaraki nayax jak'achasiskthwa ukhamaraki ch'amampiw herenciaj q'iwsirinakat apaqä ukhamaraki phuqhat taripäwipax janiw qhiparkiti. Munatanakaja, amuyapxam, jumanakax jiwäwit wiñay jakañaruw nayan, Cristo Jesus Tatituman, pasapxta. Ukhamaraki nayaw juchat phuqhat q'umachawayxsma wilaxtampija, kunatix nayaw phuqhat Pascua-mätwa, khititix Yahweh jach'a wiñay Diosar luqtasiwayki, mä kuti, taqitaki, ukhamaraki wiñayataki jaqin juchanakapataki: ukatwa, juchanakatakix janiw luqtäwix utjkxiti.

Ukatwa, Diosan wawanakapax nayan taripäwi tronojar jak'achasipxani, ukhamat nayax lurañanakap taripañajataki, ukhamat taqini katuqapxañapataki kunatix phuqhapki ukataki, askïkpan jan ukax ñanqhäkpan. Ukhamatwa taqini qhispiyat jakañanakaman luqtäwinakap uraqin apanipxäta, wiñay Tatitumar luqtañataki. Kunjamatix qillqatäki, ukhamaw lurasini, kunjamatix qillqatäki: Janiw khitis yaqha cimiento uskukaspati, uskutäki ukat sipanxa, ukax Jesucristowa. Ukhamaraki maynix aka cimiento patxan lurchi, quri, qullqi, suma qalanaka, lawa, pastu, jan ukax ch'illiwa, Sapa maynin lurañapax qhananchatäniwa: kunatix uruw qhananchani, kunatix ninampiw qhananchatäni: ukhamaraki ninaw sapa maynin lurañap yant'ani kunjamäkisa. Maynin lurañapatix, luratapax, qhiparchi, jupax chanip katuqaniwa. Maynin lurañapatix nakhantchi, jupax chhaqhayaniwa, ukampis jupax qhispiyatäniwa: ukampis ninampis qhispiyatäkaspa ukhamawa.

Ukhamaw munatanakaja, nayax phuqhat chanip churapxäma nayar luqtatamata ukhamaraki nayan taripäwi ninajampi ch'illiwjam nakhantat yänakatxa chhaqhayapxäta ukhamaraki nayan taripäwi ninajar sayt'apki uka yänakatxa, nayax phuqhañamatak chursma uka lurañanakatxa, jach'a chanipaw jumanakankani. Kunatix qillqatawa: Nayrax janiw uñjkiti, jinchus janiw ist'kiti, janirakiw jaqin chuymapar mantkiti, kunatix Diosax jupar munapki ukanakatak wakichki ukanaka. Ukhamaw herenciaman ukhamaraki chanipaman kankañapa, uraqin janiw mä jaqis phuqhat amuykiti Diosan wiñay wawanakapan herenciapan jach'a kankañap ukhamaraki qhant'äwipa, khitinakatix Criston ajayuparjam wasitat yuritapki, ukanwa Diosan wawanakapjam wiñayataki wasitat yuritapxta.

Ukhamax amuyapxam munatanakaja, phuqhat qhispiyäwiman urupax amuyapkt ukat sipan juk'amp jak'ankiwa, ukhamaraki jichha segundon jumanakar t'ijtani ukhamaraki uka urux jank'akiw phuqhasini, suyt'apkt ukat sipan jank'aki. Amuyapxam, janiw iwxt'añatakix pachax utjkxiti, janiw achikt'asiñatakix pachax utjkxiti, janiw Jamasa Babiloniampi aruskipañatakix pachax utjkxiti, kunatix taripäwipax qhipa taripäwiwa ukhamaraki janiw mayjt'ayatäkaspati. Janiwa, arrepentisiñ pachapax inamayaw chhaqhayata, ukhamaraki profetanakajan ukhamaraki apostolonakajan arup phuqhat jan ist'kiti, chuymapan sasa, nayan arunakajar sawkasisin “Nayax reinajamaw qunt'atäsktha ukhamaraki janiw viudäktti, qharürux jichhürjamäniwa ukhamaraki jichhürux masürjamawa.”

Ukampis mä horanxa t'unjäwipax phuqhat jupar purini, ukhamaraki janiw wasitat jaktkaniti. Ukampis mä nayra jach'a marka lanti, Yahweh-n thakhinakap yäqir ukhamaraki jaqinakax Nazaretankir Jesusar arkir ukhamaraki jaqi masipar munañ jan p'inqasir marka lanti, mä ch'usa uraqiw qhiparani, ukanwa Jamasa Babiloniax mä horan ch'usar tukuyatäni, kunatix jupar taripki uka Tatitu Diosax ch'amaniwa. Ukhamaraki uka t'unjäwix juk'amp jak'achasinkipan munatanakaja, ukhamarakiw phuqhat qhispiyäwima, qhispiyäwi, chanipa, ukhamaraki wiñay kusisiñax jak'achasini, ukhamax juchan jaqinakan taripäwip jak'achasinkipan jan llakisipxamti, ukax janiw jumanakatakïkiti, juchanakamat phuqhat mistupxstaxa.

Ukhamatwa uka nayra ch'ipxtäwix jak'achasini, ukanwa mä segundon Yahweh-n trono utapan sayt'at jikxatasipxäta, ukanwa trononakamax k'achachatäki ukhamaraki ukanwa wiñay reyinaka ukhamaraki sacerdotenakajam qunt'atäpxta, wiñay Tatituman herenciap tuqi, ukanwa markanakar fierro thujrumpi apnaqapxäta, kunatix nayaruw churatäxi, ukhamaraki nayaw atipjirinakar churä. ¿Khitinakas atipjirinakajaxa? Khitinakatix juchat phuqhat mistupki, phuqhat arrepentisisin ukhamaraki juchanakap jan munasin. Ukhamaraki naya Cristo Jesusaw supayan k'arinakapar phuqhat ukhamaraki wiñayataki atipjawaytha, juparux mä ch'usa ukhamaraki phuqhat atipjat uñisirjam qhiparaya, ukatakix wiñay t'aqhisiñ ninanakax phichhantatawa ukhamaraki katuqañatak wakichatawa, ukhamaw herenciapaxa ukhamaraki ukhamaw wakisipxi.

Ukatwa, nayax mä jach'a thakhi wakichawaytha, taqi khitinakatix nayan ukar phuqhat jutapki ukanakataki, supayan k'arinakapar phuqhat atipjapxañapataki, kunjamatix nayax lurkayäta ukhamaraki. Ukhamax nayan ukar phuqhat jutapxam, ukhamat nayax uka chiqapa ukhamaraki k'ullk'u thakhin phuqhat qhispiyäwimar irpañajataki. Aruj ist'apxam, khitinakatix thakhin lluskhantasipki ukhamaraki tinkupki ukanaka, kunatix chiqap jaqix paqallq kutiw tinku, ukhamaraki wasitat jaktasi, ukhamax sayt'apxam Oh' Diosan jaqinakapa ukhamaraki nayan ukar phuqhat jutapxam, ukhamat nayax chanipamar wasitat mayachañajataki, wilaxtampij q'umachasa, ukhamat nayan chiqapa kankañajampi phuqhat isthapt'at sayt'apxañamataki. Amuyapxam, atipjirinakajax taqi kunwa herenciapxani ukhamaraki janiw kuna askis jupanakar jark'atäkaniti. Ukhamaraki atipjirinakajax, wiñayatakiw qhant'apxani, alaxpachan qhant'äwipjama: ukhamaraki jumanakax waljanir chiqapa kankañar kutt'ayapki ukanakax, warawaranakjamaw qhant'apxäta, wiñayat wiñayataki. Ukhamax jichhax aka yatisa wawanakaja, jichhaw aka pachan llakinakap jaytañ pacha ukhamaraki nayan samart'añ pacha ukhamaraki wilaxtampijan mä virgenjam q'uma qhiparañ pacha.

Ukatwa, munatanakaja, jak'achasinkir t'unjäwi nayraqatan sayt'apxam, kunatix uka axsarankay uru jak'achasinkir uñjapxtawa, ukhamaraki nayaw llamp'u chuymaninakan nayranakap jist'arawaytha, ukhamat uraqiman chiqpach kankañap uñjapxañamataki ukhamaraki janiw jumanakatakïki uka jach'a k'ari amuyur tinkupxañamataki. Amuyapxam, naya, Yeshua HaMashiach, juykhunakar uñjayirïtwa, ukatwa, nayan ukar jutapxam, ukhamat nayranakamar qulla jawiqañajataki ukhamaraki aka ñanqha uraqi, kawkhantix jakapki uka, chiqpachapjam mä juk'a uñjapxañamataki. Ukhamax juchan jaqinakan taripäwipar jan axsarapxamti, kunatix uka taripäwix janiw jumanakatakïkiti munatanakaja, janiwa, taripäwix janiw qhispiyatanakatakïkiti, kunatix jumanakax Cordero-n colerapat ukhamaraki Diosan colerapat qhispiyatäpxtawa, ukatwa qhispiyäwiman sayt'apxam ukhamaraki imatäpxam munatanakaja, kunatix urux jak'ankiwa ukhamaraki sapa segundon juk'amp jak'achasinkipxtam.

Jïsa, uka phuqhat qhispiyäwiman pachapax jumanakar purinxiwa munatanakaja ukhamaraki uka wiñay pachax jak'ankxiwa ukhamaraki sapa segundon juk'amp jak'achasinkipxtam. Ajayunakamar ch'amanchawaytwa, alaxpachan chimpunak ukhamaraki uraqin chimpunak uñacht'ayawaytwa, alaxpachan muspharkañanak lurawaytwa ukhamaraki juchan jaqinakar taripäwin t'unjäwinak antutawaytwa, ukhamaraki ikisipki ukanakar nart'ayañataki, ukhamat jichhaw Taqi Ch'amani Diosan wawanakapan phuqhat qhispiyäwipatak pachax sasin amuyapxañapataki, nayan noviaj ukhamaraki nayan janchija.

Jan jupanakamp chikanchasipxamti jan iyawsäwi thakinakapanxa, kunattix jupanakax alaxpachan uñacht'ayat walja unanchänaka ukhamarak muspharkañanaka, kawkïrinakatix Yahweh jupan amparapamp chimpukatäki ukanak katuqañ ukhamarak qhanañchañ munapxi. ¿Génesis panka armt'apxtati munatanakaxa? Akham qillqatawa: Diosax sänwa, Alaxpach firmamento ukan qhananakax utjapan, uru arumamp jaljañataki, unanchänakataki, tiemponakataki, urunakataki ukhamarak maranakataki. Alaxpach firmamento ukan qhananakäpan uraqir qhanañchañataki: ukhamaw lurasïna. Ukhamasti, amuyapxam Yahweh jupaw taqi kunxar munañani ukhamarak janiw kunas luratapanxa utjkiti jan jupan munañapampi, ukhamarak jan jupan yatitapampi. Uka layku, jan jupanakamp chikanchasipxamti jan iyawsäwi amuyunakapanxa, jaqinakax jichhax alaxpach apnaqapxi ukhamarak munañapar sarnaqayapxi sasina, jaqinakax Diosar atipjañ yant'apkchisa, ch'amachasitanakapax inamayakiwa.

Ukampis, tinkutanakax unanchänakax imantañ yant'apxi ukhamarak Yahweh jupan alaxpachan uchat walja yatiyäwinaka, novion noviapamp jaqichasiñapataki. Ukhamasti Diosan qhapaq kankañapan chuymacht'asipxam, yatisa kunte tinkutanakax lurañ munañ katuqapki ukax wiñay Diosan wiñay amtanakapak phuqhi, ukhamaw thakinakapaxa, uñisirinakapas arup ist'apxiwa. Ukampis jan pantjasipxamti Yahweh jupaw taqi kunxar munañani ukhamarak janiw kunas utjkiti jan jupan khuyapayasiñapampi, munañapampi, ukhamarak qhipa wiñay taripäwipampi jan arrepentisirinakxaru ukhamarak tinkutanakxaru. Uka nayra yatiyat jach'a taripäwis jichhax uraqir ch'amamp purinkaskiwa.

Janiwa, janiw jark'atäkiti janirakiw imantatäkiti, taqi uraqiw uñstäwixat yatini, tinkutanakax ch'axwäwimp ukhamarak k'ari alienígena mantäwimp uñstäwix imantañ munapkchisa. Amuyapxam janiw k'ari muyuri uraqinakat alienígenanakax utjkiti, jan ukasti wiñayataki alaxpachat alqatat tinkutanakawa, jupanakax k'arisiñ munapxi kunattix taqiniw k'arisirinakäpxi, sallqjañataki ukhamarak thakhit irpañatakiw jutapxi.

Amuyapxam uñstäwixax janiw payïr jutäwixamp kikpakïkiti, ukax Diosan jach'a imantat amuyupan imantatawa, nayax, Yeshua HaMashiach, apóstol Pablo jupar uñacht'ayta. Uñstäwixanwa, chiqpach arrepentimientomp nayar jutapki uka iyawsirinakarux nayamp apaqä, alaxpach imantat chiqar irpañataki, uraqin taripäwipax pasañapkama, taripäwi tiempox taqi uraqiruw purini, juk'ampis tinkutanakaru.

Amuyapxam payïr jutäwixanxa, qhispiyatanakaxampi, 144,000 ukanakampi, ukhamarak taqi alaxpach ejercitomp juttha, Supayan reinop taqpach tukjañataki, uka tukjäwitxa reinopax janiw wiñayas wasitat sayt'käniti. Supayax chinutäniwa ukhamarak waranqa maratak manqhapachar jaquntatäniwa, markanakar jan juk'amp sallqjañapataki, jisk'a tiempotak antutatäñapkama, ukanxa jach'a cadenamp chinutaw qhiparani, t'aqhisiñan ukhamarak jach'a axsaräwin herenciapat nina ukhamarak azufre qutanxa, ukatxa janiw wiñayas qhispkäniti. Jisk'a tiempop qhipatxa, uka wiñay nakhari azufre qutar taqpach jaquntatäniwa, ukax juk'amp lup'i azul ninamp nakhari. Ukatxa jach'a q'illu tronon taripäwipaw utjani, Yahweh jupax machaq alaxpacha ukhamarak machaq uraqi janir lurkipanxa, taqi kun machaqar tukuyañataki wiñay ukhamarak phuqhat q'umachäwinxa.

Ukham qillqatawa ukhamarakiw jank'ak phuqhasini, alaxpachat jach'a arumpi, arcángel arupampi ukhamarak Diosan trompetapampiw saraqanï, uka jach'a trompeta phust'äwix janiw qhiparkiti, ukax khuyapayasiñ tiempoxan qhipa trompetapawa, uraqix jank'ak ukhamarak jach'a taripäwipar uñkataskipanxa. Ajllitanakaxarux pusi uraq k'uchut tantachä, taqi khitinakatix Criston ikipki ukanakaru, jïsa, taqi khitinakatix Criston ikipki ukanakaru, uka qullan arsuwi ajayump chimpuntata. Jan sayt'as usuyasiñanakax uraqiruw usuyasiñ churani, khitinakatix ikipki ukanakaru, ukampis jupanakarux janiw jiwäwix mä segundos katxarukäniti. Ukatxa jumanakax jakaskapki ukhamarak qhiparapki ukanakax jupanakamp chikaw alaxpachar apaqatäpxäta, qinayanakan nayamp jikisiñataki, ukhamarak mä nayr ch'ipxtäwinxa uñjapxätawa.

Jïsa, nayranakamampiw jach'a wiñay Diosan wiñay qhapaq kankañap uñjapxäta, Qhananakan Awkipa, khititix jumanakan utjäwinakam munaski ukhamarak aka pachataki wiñayataki wakichaski, kunattix jumanakax wiñayaw Yahweh jupan chuymapankapxta. Nayaruw uñjapxita, wiñay ukhamarak wiñay Qhispiyirima, khititix t'aqhisiñan taqpach churasiyäta, jumanakax Qhananakan Awkipan nayraqatapan jakapxañamataki, khititix uraqin qhananakap wawanakachki. Jïsa, nayranakamampiw uñjapxita, taqi jumanakax qhapaq Tatitumar iyawsapki ukanakaxa, khitirutix janir nayranakamamp uñjapkta ukanakaxa.

Nayranakamampiw phuqhat qhapaq kankañax uñjapxäta, kunte Yahweh jupax herenciaxan churkitu, ukatxa atipjirinakaxamp phuqhat jaljañäni, ukhamarak taqi khitinakatix nayar jutapki ukanakarux nina yant'äwir sayt'iri luräwinakampiw palljä. Chiqansa, chiqansa, nayranakamampiw alaxpach ejercit uñjapxäta, jupanakat waljaniw wiñay qhispiyäwi herederonakar yanapt'añatak khitata. Phuqhat q'umachäwin sayt'apxäta, alaxpach phuqhat kankañan machaq luratäpxäta, ukhamarak wiñay tukuyäwimax kunte apaniki ukhamarak qullan jaqinakan herenciaxan qhapaq kankañapan qamir kankañapax jumanakatak kamsañs muni ukanakat muspharapxätawa.

Amuyapxam munatanakaxa, (taqi khitinakatix jan jakañanakap tukuykam munapki ukanakasa) jumanakas juchanakamat chiqpach arrepentimientomp mistupkta ukanakaxa: Wiñay tukuyäwimax wiñay qalltäwimawa, ukanxa wiñay jakañaman nayrïr segundopan sayt'apxäta, janiw kunas wasitat q'añuchkätamti. Qullan phuqhat kankañan machaq luratäpxätawa, janiw wiñayas wasitat q'añuchatäpxätati, jan q'añuchat machaq luratäpxätawa.

Wiñayatakiw Yahweh tatitur yupaychapxäta, wiñayatakiw nayamp chikäpxäta kawkirus sarkä ukanxa. T'axu laq'un manq'antatapa ukhamarak Supayan lunthatatapa taqpach kutt'ayapxäma. Luräwinakamat ukhamarak munasiñ irnaqäwimat phuqhat palljatäpxäta, kunte sutixar uñacht'ayapkta, qullan jaqinakar yanapt'apxatamat ukhamarak yanapt'askapxatamatxa. Uraqin lik'ipat manq'apxäni waranqa mara apnaqäwixanxa, ukanxa nayamp chikaw reynakax ukhamarak sacerdotenakaxjam apnaqapxäta, wiñayatakiw chiqapa apnaqäwix utjani.

Nayaw juchanakamat perdonasiñ wilaxtampi taqpach q'umachapxsma.

Nayaw phuqhat Päsawaxätwa, khititix Yahweh jach'a wiñay Diosar churasiyäta.

Wiñay qhispiyäwi arunakax jutiri t'aqhisiñanakan imatäniwa, ukhamarak khitinakarutix khitatäki ukanakan nayranakapa, jinchunakapa, ukhamarak chuymanakap jikxatani, kunattix naya, Yeshua HaMashiach, Jesucristo, aka yänakxa Yahweh jupan phuqhat munañapampi ukhamarak ch'amapampiw qhanañchtha, khititix tronop sapa yuqapamp jaljki, ch'amani amparaxampi lurat yänakax wiñayataki luriripar kutt'ayatäñapataki, khititix wiñayataki bendicitäki, jïsa ukhamarak Amén.
